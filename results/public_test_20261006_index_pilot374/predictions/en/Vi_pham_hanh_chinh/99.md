                                                  DECREE

[1]

     REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE LAWS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC ASSETS; SPENDING AND COUNTERFEITING; NATIONAL RESERVES; AND STATE GRAINS

  Decree No. 63/2019/ND-CP dated July 11, 2019 of the Government on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; practice of frugality and prevention of waste; national reserves; State treasuries; effective from September 01, 2019; amended and supplemented by:

  Decree No. 102/2021/ND-CP dated November 16, 2021 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Decrees on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of tax, invoice; customs; insurance business, lottery business; management and use of public property; practice of thrift, anti-wastefulness; national reserve; State Treasury; accounting, independent audit; effective from January 01, 2022.

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Law on Management and Use of Public Property dated June 21, 2017;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Practice of Thrift, Anti-Wastefulness dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Law on National Reserve dated November 20, 2012;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates a Decree on the sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; practice of thrift and prevention of waste; national reserves; and State treasuries.[2]

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, sanctioning forms, sanctioning levels, remedial measures, competence to make records of administrative violations and competence to sanction administrative violations in the following fields:

  a) Management and use of public property at agencies, organizations and units; property of projects using state capital; property with established all-people ownership and property of infrastructure constructed with state investment and management;

  b) Prudent management and anti-wastefulness;

  c) National reserves;

  d) State treasury.

  2. Administrative violations related to the fields of management and use of public property; prudent management and anti-wastefulness; national reserves; state treasury in accounting, construction, investment planning, price appraisal, auction and other regulations not specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be sanctioned according to relevant laws.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; practice of frugality against waste; national reserves; state treasuries.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies;

  b) People's armed forces units;

  c) Public service units;

  d) Communist Party of Vietnam agencies;

  dd) Political-social organizations, socio-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, other organizations established in accordance with the law on associations;

  e) Other relevant enterprises, organizations and individuals.

  3. Persons competent to impose penalties and organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of penalties for administrative violations under this Decree.

  4. In cases where officials, civil servants, or public employees commit violations while on duty, such violations shall not be subject to administrative penalties under this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with the law on officials, civil servants, and public employees.

  State agencies that commit violations while performing state management tasks shall not be subject to administrative penalties under this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws.

  Article 3. Statute of limitations for administrative penalties [3]

  1. The statute of limitations for administrative penalties in the fields of public asset management and use, frugal practices and anti-wastefulness, national reserves, and state treasuries under this Decree is 01 year. The statute of limitations for administrative penalties for public assets such as houses, land, and infrastructure is 02 years.

  2. The time for calculating the statute of limitations for administrative penalties in the fields of public asset management and use is as follows:

  a) For completed administrative violations, the statute of limitations shall be calculated from the time of termination of the violation;

  b) For ongoing administrative violations, the statute of limitations shall be calculated from the time of detection of the violation.

  c) Administrative violations against regulations on investment in and procurement of public assets in Article 6 of this Decree shall be identified as completed administrative violations in cases where investment in and procurement of assets have been completed and handed over to agencies, organizations and units for management and use; identified as ongoing administrative violations in other cases;

  d) Administrative violations against regulations on renting assets in Article 7 of this Decree shall be identified as completed administrative violations in cases where assets have been rented and put into use; identified as ongoing administrative violations in other cases;

  dd) Administrative violations against regulations on occupation of offices and public service establishments in Article 11 of this Decree shall be identified as follows:

  - Administrative violations against regulations on organization and individuals using adjacent land to move land boundary markers to expand land area or construct structures encroaching on the land area of offices and public service establishments of agencies, organizations and units shall be identified as completed administrative violations in cases where land area expansion or construction of encroaching structures have been completed; identified as ongoing administrative violations in other cases.

  - Administrative violations against regulations on organization and individuals using houses and land of offices and public service establishments of agencies, organizations and units shall be identified as ongoing administrative violations.

  e) Administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public assets being committed are those specified in Articles 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25 and 26 of this Decree.

  3. Identification of ongoing administrative violations and completed administrative violations for calculation of the statute of limitations for administrative violations in the field of practicing thrift and combating waste shall be defined as follows:

  a) Administrative violations specified in Clause 2 Article 31 of this Decree shall be identified as completed administrative violations in cases where procurement of assets have been completed and handed over to agencies, organizations and units for management and use; identified as ongoing administrative violations in other cases;

  b) Administrative violations specified in Clause 1 Article 32 of this Decree shall be identified as administrative violations that have ended in case of completion of spending of funds from the fund according to the contents of misuse of funds; identified as administrative violations that are being committed in the remaining cases;

  c) Administrative violations specified in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be identified as administrative violations that have ended in case of completion of spending of funds from the fund not according to standards, regulations, exceeding the norm, unit price according to law; identified as administrative violations that are being committed in the remaining cases;

  d) Administrative violations specified in Clause 1 Article 33 of this Decree shall be identified as administrative violations that have ended in case of illegal obstruction of the implementation of scientific research and application, implementation of projects using recycled resources has ended; identified as administrative violations that are being committed in the remaining cases;

  dd) Administrative violations specified in Clause 2 Article 33 of this Decree shall be identified as administrative violations that have ended in case of illegal exploitation and use of resources not according to planning, plan, process has ended; identified as administrative violations that are being committed in the remaining cases;

  e) Administrative violations specified in Clause 1 Article 35 of this Decree shall be identified as administrative violations that have ended in case of waste in management of working offices, public service establishments has ended; identified as administrative violations that are being committed in the remaining cases;

  g) Administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Clause 3 Article 36 of this Decree on procurement of assets, materials in a one-member state-owned limited liability company shall be identified as administrative violations that have ended in case of completion of asset procurement; identified as administrative violations that are being committed in the remaining cases;

  h) Administrative violations in the field of saving that are being committed are administrative violations specified in Clause 1 Article 31, Clause 1, Clause 4 Article 36 of this Decree; administrative violations on management, use of capital in a one-member state-owned limited liability company specified in Clause 2 Article 36 of this Decree; administrative violations in management, use of assets, materials specified in Clause 2, Clause 3 Article 36 of this Decree.

  4. Identification of administrative violations that are being committed, administrative violations that have ended to calculate the statute of limitations for administrative violations in the field of national reserves is specified as follows:

  a) The administrative violations identified as being committed are the administrative violations specified in Article 41, Article 42, Article 47, Points b and c, Clause 3, Article 48, Article 49 of this Decree;

  b) Apart from the administrative violations specified in Point a of this Clause, the remaining administrative violations are identified as having been completed.

  5. The time when administrative violations in the field of State Treasury are identified as having been completed is specified as follows:

  a) For the administrative violations specified in Article 54, Article 57, Article 58, Article 59, Article 60 of this Decree: the time of completion is the time of submission of dossiers and documents at the State Treasury;

  b) For the administrative violations specified in Article 55, Article 56, Article 61 of this Decree: the time of completion is the time when the State Treasury receives the conclusion of the criminal procedure agency on the violation case without signs of a crime.

  Article 4. Forms of sanctioning administrative violations and remedial measures

  1. Main sanctioning forms:

  a) The main sanctioning forms specified in this Decree include: Warning and fine;

  b) The maximum fine in the field of management and use of public property; national reserves; State treasury is 50,000,000 VND for individuals and 100,000,000 VND for organizations; the maximum fine in the field of saving practice and anti-wastefulness is 100,000,000 VND for individuals and 200,000,000 VND for organizations.

  2. Additional sanctioning forms:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall also be subject to the additional sanctioning form of confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit administrative violations.

  3. Remedial measures in the field of management and use of public property:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall also be subject to one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Forced to return the amount corresponding to the value of the property;

  b) Forced to return the amount caused by the commission of administrative violations;

  c) Forced to return the property, in case the property has been changed due to the commission of administrative violations, the property must be restored to its original state; in case the property cannot be restored to its original state, it must be paid in cash or returned in other property with equivalent functional value and use value to the original property;

  d) Enforced demolition of structures built on the area encroaching upon the head office or public service establishment;

  dd) Enforced destruction of false or falsified declarations on acts of violation against regulations on access to and use of public asset data;

  e) Enforced adjustment, supplementation of data, information, and declarations on public assets.

  4. Remedial measures in the field of frugal practice and anti-wastefulness:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall be subject to one or several remedial measures, such as:

  a) Withdrawal of funds from the state budget for misuse of funds from the state budget in contravention of the fund’s principles issued by the competent authority;

  b) Enforced return of funds from the state budget for illegal gains from the misuse of funds from the state budget in contravention of the fund’s operating regulations and financial mechanisms issued by the competent authority.

  5. Remedial measures in the field of national reserves:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Forcible return of illegal profits earned from violations against regulations on purchase, sale, preservation, distribution, and relief; violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods; violations against regulations on management of capital and fees in national reserve operations; violations against regulations on business, pledge, mortgage, lease, and exploitation of national reserve assets;

  b) Forcible restoration of the initial state of facilities, technical equipment, and warehouses for preservation of national reserve goods;

  c) Forcible return of national reserve goods damaged due to infringement or sabotage; violations against regulations on distribution and relief of national reserve goods;

  d) Forcible withdrawal of national reserve goods that have been distributed in violation of regulations;

  dd) Forcible restoration of the original state of assets in cases of illegal business, pledge, mortgage, lease, or exploitation of assets that are warehouses for preservation of national reserve goods.

  6. Remedial measures in the field of State treasuries:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory withdrawal of funds spent on preparing payment dossiers and documents for work volumes that have not been performed; work volumes that are in progress but not eligible for payment, and amounts paid in excess of the contract or contract appendix value;

  b) Compulsory withdrawal of funds spent on preparing payment dossiers and documents in violation of regulations and amounts spent in violation of regulations due to incorrect preparation of payment dossiers and documents compared to the contents of original payment dossiers and documents at the unit, but not to the extent of criminal liability investigation;

  c) Compulsory withdrawal of all funds spent on using forged payment dossiers and documents for state budget expenditure, but not to the extent of criminal liability investigation;

  d) Compulsory completion of payment dossiers and documents in accordance with regulations for acts of violating regulations on payment of state budget expenditures;

  dd) Compulsory execution of procedures for making commitment to expenditure before requesting the State Treasury to make payment or reimbursement of state budget, or compulsory re-execution of procedures for making commitment to expenditure for acts of violating procedures for making commitment to expenditure;

  e) Compulsory execution of procedures for making advance payment for acts of violating procedures and time limits for advance payment of state budget;

  g) Compulsory freezing of accounts or closing of accounts for acts of violating regulations on account registration and use at the State Treasury; compulsory freezing of accounts for acts of preparing forged payment dossiers and documents for account registration procedures at the State Treasury, but not to the extent of criminal liability investigation.

  7.[4] Organizations and individuals that are sanctioned for administrative violations under this Decree are not allowed to use state budget funds or funds originating from the state budget to pay fines and remedy consequences caused by their violations, except for the cases specified in Clause 8 Article 37 of the Government’s Decree No. 10/2021/ND-CP dated February 9, 2021 on management of construction investment costs.

  Article 5. Application of fine levels[5]

  1. The fine levels specified in Section 1, Section 2, Section 3 and Section 4 Chapter II, Section 1 Chapter III, Section 1 Chapter IV, Section 1 and Section 2 Chapter V of this Decree are the fine levels prescribed for administrative violations of organizations; for the same administrative violation, the fine level for an individual shall be 1/2 of the fine level for an organization (except for the provisions in Article 17, Article 23 and Article 27 of this Decree).

  2. When imposing a fine, the specific fine level for an administrative violation is the average of the fine level specified for that violation. If there are mitigating circumstances, each circumstance shall reduce the average fine level by 10%, but the fine level for that violation shall not be reduced below the minimum fine level; if there are aggravating circumstances, each aggravating circumstance shall increase the average fine level by 10%, but the fine level for that violation shall not exceed the maximum fine level.

  3. When determining the fine level for an administrative violation that has both aggravating and mitigating circumstances, the aggravating circumstances shall be reduced according to the principle that one mitigating circumstance shall reduce one aggravating circumstance.

  Chapter II

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, FORMS OF SANCTION, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE PROVISIONS AND FINES FOR MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY AT OFFICES, ORGANIZATIONS AND UNIT

  Article 6. Administrative violations against the provisions on investment and procurement of public property

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of making investment or purchasing assets without the decision of a competent authority or person, according to the following fines:

  a) Between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of making investment or purchasing assets such as machinery, equipment, other assets (excluding office buildings, public service establishments, cars) with total purchase value of less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of making investment or purchasing assets such as machinery, equipment, other assets (excluding office buildings, public service establishments, cars) with total purchase value of VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) Between VND 20,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 in case of making investment or purchasing assets such as office buildings, public service establishments, cars.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of failing to make centralized procurement of assets listed in the centralized procurement list according to the law on management and use of public assets.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for the act of making investment or purchasing assets beyond the standards and quotas prescribed by a competent State authority, according to the following fines:

  a) Between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of making investment or purchasing assets with a value of less than VND 50,000,000/asset unit;

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of making investment or purchasing assets with a value of between VND 50,000,000 and VND 100,000,000/asset unit;

  c) Between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 in case of investment or purchase of assets with a value exceeding VND 100,000,000/asset unit or more.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of the amount corresponding to the value of assets invested or purchased exceeding the standards and norms for the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. The determination of the excess value compared to the standards and norms as the basis for sanctioning is defined as follows:

  a) In case of investment or purchase of assets such as cars, machinery, equipment, and other assets exceeding the standards and norms in terms of quantity: The excess value/asset unit is determined by the unit price of such asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or asset purchase contract;

  b) In case of investment or purchase of assets such as cars, machinery, equipment, and other assets correct in terms of quantity but exceeding the standards and norms in terms of price: The excess value is determined by the value of the asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or asset purchase contract minus (-) the value of the asset according to the standards and norms prescribed by the competent State agency;

  c) In case of investment or purchase of working offices or public service establishments exceeding the standards and norms: The excess value is determined by the difference between the actual area minus (-) the area used according to the standards and norms multiplied by (x) the average purchase price according to the invoice (or according to the asset purchase contract);

  d) The persons with sanctioning competence specified in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as the basis for sanctioning according to the law on handling of administrative violations and shall be responsible for such determination.

  Article 7. Administrative violations against regulations on renting out property

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the following violations against regulations on renting out property in case the property rental contract is valued at less than VND 100,000,000:

  a) Renting out property without a decision on property rental issued by a competent authority or person;

  b) Renting out property beyond the standards or quotas prescribed by a competent State authority;

  c) Selecting a property rental service provider in a manner or order not prescribed by law on management and use of public property and relevant laws.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation against regulations on renting out property specified in Clause 1 of this Article in case the property rental contract is valued at VND 100,000,000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of the amount of property rented beyond the standards or quotas prescribed in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The value of the lease contract serving as the basis for sanctioning shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case the lease contract specifies the contract value, the sanctioning value shall be the value specified in the contract;

  b) In case the lease contract is not made or the contract is made but the value is not determined according to the provisions of Clause a of this Point, the sanctioning value shall be determined by the lease price of the property of the same type or with equivalent standards and techniques on the market at the time of the violation multiplied by (x) the lease period from the start of the lease to the time of the administrative sanctioning decision.

  The person with sanctioning competence specified in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the sanctioning value according to the law on handling of administrative violations and shall be responsible for such determination.

  Article 8. Administrative violations against regulations on the transfer and use of public property, destruction or intentional damage to public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of transferring public property beyond the standards and quotas issued by competent agencies or persons (not the correct subjects, exceeding the area, quantity, or price) at the following levels:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of transferring or using property with a value of less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of transferring or using property with a value of from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000;

  c) Between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 in case of transfer or use of property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the act of transferring or using public property for improper purposes (using public property for purposes other than those for which the property was invested, equipped or purchased; using the office building or public service establishment as a residence or for personal purposes; using the car for commuting from home to work for a position without a standard; using the car for work purposes for a position without a standard for use without the approval of a competent authority; using the car for personal purposes; using machinery, equipment or other property for personal purposes) at the following fines:

  a) Between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of transferring or using machinery, equipment or other property (excluding the office building, public service establishment and car) with original value according to the accounting book less than VND 100,000,000/1 unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property valued at less than VND 100,000,000);

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of transferring or using machinery, equipment or other property (excluding the office building, public service establishment and car) with original value according to the accounting book equal to or more than VND 100,000,000/1 unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more);

  c) Between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 in case of transferring or using the office building, public service establishment and car.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for the act of destroying or intentionally damaging public property but not to the extent of criminal liability investigation at the following fines:

  a) Between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of intentionally damaging machinery, equipment or other property with original value according to the accounting book less than VND 100,000,000/1 unit of property;

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of intentionally damaging machinery, equipment or other property with original value according to the accounting book equal to or more than VND 100,000,000/1 unit of property;

  c) Between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 in case of intentionally damaging property which is the working office, public service operation establishment, or automobile.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of the amount corresponding to the value of property exceeding the standard or norm for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the initial state of property which has been changed due to the administrative violation; in case of failure to restore the initial state of property, the property must be returned in cash or property with equivalent functional value and use value to the initial property for the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. The determination of the excess value over the standard or norm is as follows:

  a) In case of delivery or use of property which is an automobile, machine, equipment, or other property exceeding the standard or norm in quantity: The excess value is determined by multiplying the quantity of excess property by (x) the original price of the property on the accounting book; in case the original price of the property is not tracked on the accounting book, it shall be determined by the new purchase price of the property of the same type or having equivalent technical standards and functional use on the market at the time of the violation;

  b) In case of delivery or use of property which is an automobile, machine, equipment, or other property in quantity but exceeding the price level over the standard or norm: The excess value is determined by multiplying the quantity of excess property by (x) (the difference between the original price of the property on the accounting book minus (-) the property value according to the standard or norm prescribed by the competent State agency);

  c) In case of property which is the working office or public service operation establishment: The excess value is determined by the difference between the actual area minus (-) the area used according to the standard or norm multiplied by (x) the average unit price according to the accounting book; in case the original price of the property is not tracked on the accounting book, the unit price shall be calculated according to the investment quota of the house or work with equivalent technical standards promulgated by the Ministry of Construction at the time of the violation;

  d) Persons competent to impose penalties specified in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the value of the excess as the basis for imposing penalties in accordance with the law on handling of administrative violations and shall be responsible for such determination.

  Article 9. Administrative violations against regulations on lending of public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for lending or using public property in contravention of regulations (lending public property to other organizations or individuals without a loan contract or with a loan contract, regardless of the loan duration) at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of lending property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of lending property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 50,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 in case of lending property which is a working office, a public service establishment or a car.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Organizations that commit violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of borrowed property. In case the property has been changed due to administrative violations, the property must be restored to its original state; in case the property cannot be restored to its original state, the property must be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and use value to the original property;

  b) Enforced payment of the amount corresponding to the property rental during the borrowing period to the State budget. The determination of the property rental amount for the basis of sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 10. Administrative violations against regulations on exchange and donation of public property in contravention of regulations

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of exchanging public property in contravention of regulations (using public property of an organization to exchange for property of another organization or individual without permission of a competent authority or person) at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case the property used for exchange has a value of less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case the property used for exchange has a value of VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case the property used for exchange is an office building, a public service facility, or a car.

  2. A fine of from VND 20,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of donating public property in contravention of regulations (using public property as a gift in contravention of the gift regulations promulgated by a competent State authority).

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of property or equivalent monetary payment for the value of property exchanged or gifted for the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 11. Administrative violations against regulations on occupation of offices and public service establishments

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of occupying an office or public service establishment (an organization or individual using adjacent land to move land boundary markers to expand the land area or construct structures encroaching on the land area of the office or public service establishment of an agency, organization or unit; an organization or individual arbitrarily using houses and land of the office or public service establishment of an agency, organization or unit).

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Organizations and individuals committing the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of the office of the working establishment and the public service operation establishment and return of the office of the working establishment and the public service operation establishment that is occupied;

  b) Enforced demolition of the construction works built on the occupied area;

  c) Enforced payment to the State budget of the amount corresponding to the amount of property rental during the occupation period. The determination of the amount of property rental for the basis of sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 12. Acts of appropriation of public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of appropriation of public property that has not reached the level of criminal liability examination (keeping or using public property without permission of the competent State agency) at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 for public property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 for public property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of appropriation of the office of the working establishment, the public service operation establishment, the car.

  2. Additional penalties: confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures: Organizations and individuals that commit the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Forced restoration of the initial state of property that has been altered due to administrative violations in order to return it to the organization; in case the initial state of property cannot be restored, compensation in kind or property with equivalent utility and use value shall be made;

  b) Forced payment to the State budget of the amount corresponding to the property rental during the period of appropriation. The determination of the property rental amount shall be based on Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 13. Administrative violations against regulations on use of public property for business, lease, joint venture, or alliance

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the use of public property for business, lease, joint venture, or alliance without a decision approving the project of a competent authority or person in accordance with the law on management and use of public property at the time of committing the violation, at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of using property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of using property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of using property as working office, public service operation facility, or automobile.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the violation against regulations on determination of property value for business, lease, joint venture, or alliance purposes at the following levels:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 for establishing a property valuation council or property appraisal council with incorrect composition;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 for selecting an appraisal organization to determine the value of property used for business, lease, joint venture, or alliance purposes in contravention of the order prescribed by law.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for using public property for business, lease, joint venture, or alliance purposes contrary to the approved project, thereby affecting the performance of functions and tasks assigned by the State, at the following levels:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of using property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of using property as automobile; property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of using property as working office, public service operation facility, or automobile.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of the property that has been altered due to the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article; in case the initial state of the property cannot be restored, the property shall be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and use value to the initial property;

  b) Enforced return of illegal profits obtained from the commission of the acts specified in Clauses 1 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Administrative violations against regulations on failure to perform maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law

  1. An organization that has been granted by a competent authority the estimate for maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law but fails to perform maintenance and repair of the property, leading to damage to the property, shall be sanctioned according to the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 in case the property is valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case the property is valued at between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 in case the property is valued at VND 100,000,000 or more and is a working office, a public service establishment or a car that is not subject to criminal liability examination.

  2. The amount of money to be returned corresponding to the value of damaged property shall be determined as follows:

  a) For damaged property which cannot be repaired, the amount of money to be returned shall be determined corresponding to the new purchase price of the property of the same type or having equivalent standards and functions on the market multiplied by the remaining quality ratio at the time of determining the value;

  b) For damaged property which can be repaired, the amount of money to be returned is the cost of repairing such property;

  c) The head of the organization shall contact the financial authority under his/her management to determine the amount of money to be returned; on this basis, the head of the organization shall decide the amount of money to be returned and take responsibility for his/her decision.

  Article 15. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of public property

  1. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare plans for reorganization and handling of public property in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to declare and prepare plans for reorganization and handling of public property within the prescribed time limit;

  c) Failure to report to competent State agencies on the recovery, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of assets when the conditions are met as prescribed by law (except for cases where public assets have expired their use as prescribed by law but are still usable and agencies, organizations, units or enterprises still have the need to use them, the heads of agencies, organizations, units shall decide on the appropriate handling form).

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed on organizations for causing damage or loss of assets during the waiting period for handling.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for handling of assets without decisions of competent agencies or persons at the following levels:

  a) Between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of sale, transfer, liquidation or destruction of assets valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of sale, transfer, liquidation or destruction of assets valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) Between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 in case of sale, transfer, liquidation of assets which are working offices, public service establishments or cars.

  4. The act of declaring assets not in the list or current status of assets to be handled (leading to decisions on handling of assets not in accordance with regulations) shall be sanctioned as follows:

  a) A warning shall be imposed in case of declaration of assets valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed in case of declaration of property valued at between VND 50,000,000 and under VND 100,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed in case of declaration of property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  d) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of declaration of property being the office building, public service establishment or car.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of property which has been changed due to the acts specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the initial state of property, the property shall be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and value to the initial property;

  b) Enforced payment of the amount corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged property for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  6. The amount of money to be returned corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged property specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article shall be determined as follows:

  a) For property which is lost or damaged and cannot be restored, the amount of money to be returned shall be determined corresponding to the new purchase price of the property of the same type or having equivalent standards and utility on the market at the time of the violation.

  Market prices may be based on quotations of market suppliers that have been posted, announced on the market or from official information disclosed by suppliers via the Internet.

  b) For damaged assets that can be repaired, the amount of money to be returned is the cost of repairing such assets.

  Article 16. Administrative violations against regulations on organization of public asset disposal

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Past the prescribed time limit but still failing to implement the disposal plan approved by competent agencies or persons.

  The time limit shall be determined according to the disposal time limit prescribed in the asset disposal decision. In case the asset disposal decision does not specify a time limit, it shall comply with relevant laws.

  In case the disposal plan is approved by a competent authority and the disposal is extended, the prescribed time limit shall be determined according to the time limit after extension.

  b) Failing to hand over assets with transfer or recovery decisions to the receiving agency within the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Establishing a price-setting council without proper competence as prescribed by law;

  b) Establishing a price-setting council with improper composition as prescribed by law;

  c) Selecting a price assessment organization or a professional auction organization to handle property without proper forms and procedures as prescribed by law;

  d) Failing to fully notify the public about the auction of public property as prescribed by law on management and use of public property (failing to post notices about the auction of public property at specified locations; failing to publicly notify on mass media or failing to notify in time for the auction of public property as prescribed by law on auction of property; failing to fully post notices or publicly notify information as prescribed by law on auction of property).

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to establish a price-setting council as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to publicly notify the auction of public property as prescribed in Point d, Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. A fine shall be imposed for failing to conduct price assessment as a basis for determining the starting price when handling property that is a working office, a public service operation facility, or other property attached to land in the form of sale; or failing to conduct auction of property in cases where auction is required by law:

  a) Between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case the property is valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) Between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 in case the property is a working office, a public service establishment or a car.

  Article 17. Administrative violations against regulations on access to and use of data on public property

  A fine shall be imposed on organizations and individuals that commit violations against regulations on the login and use of data on public property as follows:

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Filing a report on declaration of assets for data entry into the national database of public property without proper legal documents and not conforming to the actual asset status of the unit;

  b) To delegate data entry for subordinate units without the written consent of the Ministry of Finance;

  c) To enter and approve data on public property inconsistent with the declaration reports of organizations assigned to manage and use public property.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) To erase, delete or modify the declaration report on property, thereby distorting the data on public property compared to the actual state of the property;

  b) To access, intrude or try to intrude, sabotage or alter data in the national database on public property and the software program structure;

  c) To exploit information on public property in the national database on public property of agencies, organizations and units outside the scope of management;

  d) To use data on public property in the national database on public property for personal purposes without permission from the agency competent to manage such database (using information stored in the database for purposes other than those prescribed by law on management and exploitation of the software for management and registration of public property without permission from the agency competent to manage such database).

  3. Remedial measures

  a) Enforced cancellation of false or forged declarations;

  b) Enforced correction of data to match actual property status of the unit;

  c) Enforced preparation of additional declarations when property-related operations change;

  d) Enforced correction of false or misleading information;

  dd) Enforced return of illegal profits from administrative violations.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PROPERTY OF STATE CAPITAL PROJECTS

  Article 18. Administrative violations against regulations on property provision of state capital projects

  1. Organizations that purchase property without decisions of competent authorities or persons in accordance with Clause 1 Article 6 of this Decree shall be sanctioned.

  2. To impose penalties on organizations that fail to carry out centralized procurement of assets listed in the centralized procurement list in accordance with the law, in accordance with Clause 2 Article 6 of this Decree.

  3. To impose penalties on organizations that purchase assets exceeding standards or quotas in accordance with Clause 3 Article 6 of this Decree.

  The determination of the value of assets exceeding standards or quotas shall comply with Clause 5 Article 6 of this Decree.

  4. To impose penalties on organizations that violate regulations on leasing assets for management of projects using state capital in accordance with Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 19. Administrative violations against regulations on transfer and use of assets of projects using state capital

  1. To impose penalties on organizations that transfer or use assets of projects using state capital exceeding standards or quotas or for improper purposes in accordance with Article 8 of this Decree.

  2. To impose penalties on organizations that lend assets of projects using state capital in contravention of regulations in accordance with Article 9 of this Decree.

  3. To impose penalties on organizations that exchange, gift or present assets of projects using state capital in contravention of regulations in accordance with Article 10 of this Decree.

  4. To impose penalties on organizations and individuals that commit acts of occupying working offices or public service establishments of project management boards using state capital in accordance with Article 11 of this Decree.

  5. To impose penalties on organizations and individuals that commit acts of misappropriating assets of projects using state capital in accordance with Article 12 of this Decree.

  6. To impose penalties on organizations that commit acts of violating regulations on use of assets of projects using state capital for production, business services, leasing, joint venture, or alliance in accordance with Article 13 of this Decree.

  7. To impose penalties on organizations that fail to perform maintenance or repair of assets of projects using state capital in accordance with law in accordance with Article 14 of this Decree.

  Article 20. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of assets of projects upon project completion or when no longer needed

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to conduct inventory or report to competent state agencies for handling of assets within the prescribed time limit;

  b) Failing to report to competent state agencies for establishment of all-people ownership of assets transferred to the Government of Vietnam by ODA experts, consultants, and supervisors in accordance with regulations.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Letting the property be damaged or lost during the waiting period for handling;

  b) Demolishing or changing the structure, parts or components of the property.

  3. Organizations that sell, transfer, liquidate or destroy property without the decision of competent authorities or persons shall be sanctioned according to Clause 3 Article 15 of this Decree.

  4. Organizations that declare property not in the list or current status shall be sanctioned according to Clause 4 Article 15 of this Decree.

  5. Organizations that violate regulations on organization of property handling that have been approved by competent authorities or persons shall be sanctioned according to Article 16 of this Decree.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Organizations that commit violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of property that has been changed due to administrative violations; in case the initial state of property cannot be restored, the property must be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and use value to the initial property;

  b) Enforced return of the amount corresponding to the value of lost or damaged property. The determination of the amount to be returned corresponding to the value of lost or damaged property shall comply with the provisions of Clause 6 Article 15 of this Decree.

  Section 3. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS, LEVELS OF SANCTION IN MANAGEMENT AND HANDLING OF PROPERTY ESTABLISHED WITH THE RIGHT OF FULL POPULATION OWNERSHIP, FORMS OF SANCTION AND REMEDIAL MEASURES

  Article 21. Violations of regulations on establishment of the right of full population ownership over property, preservation and transfer of property established with the right of full population ownership

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to report to competent agencies and persons for establishment of the right of full population ownership over property as prescribed;

  b) Transfer of property to functional agencies for handling beyond the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) To cover the damage or loss of property during the waiting period for handling;

  b) To dismantle, replace the structure, parts and components of the property.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Organizations that commit the violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) To be forced to restore the initial state of the property that has been changed due to the administrative violation; in case it is impossible to restore the initial state of the property, it must be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and use value to the initial property;

  b) To be forced to pay the amount corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged property. The determination of the amount to be paid corresponds to the value of the lost or damaged property shall comply with the provisions of Clause 6 Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on handling of property under the regime of establishing the people's ownership

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for the following acts:

  a) Failure to report to competent agencies or persons who prepare plans for handling of property established with the right of universal ownership;

  b) Failure to report to competent agencies or persons who prepare plans for handling of property established with the right of universal ownership within the time limit prescribed by law.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the following acts:

  a) Failure to prepare plans for handling of property established with the right of universal ownership;

  b) Failure to prepare plans for handling of property established with the right of universal ownership within the time limit prescribed by law.

  3. Organizations and individuals that commit violations against regulations on organization of handling of property established with the right of universal ownership that have been approved by competent agencies or persons shall be sanctioned in accordance with Article 16 of this Decree.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on login and use of data on property established with the right of universal ownership
  Organizations and individuals that commit violations against regulations on login and use of data on property established with the right of universal ownership shall be sanctioned in accordance with Article 17 of this Decree.

  Section 4. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR MANAGEMENT AND USE OF INFRASTRUCTURE ASSETS

  Article 24. Violations in exploitation of infrastructure assets

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of leasing the right to exploit or transfer the right to exploit infrastructure assets for a fixed term without a decision approving the project of the competent authority or person in accordance with the law on management and use of public assets.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the violation of regulations on determination of the lease price of the right to exploit or transfer the right to exploit infrastructure assets for a fixed term at the following levels:

  a) Between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 for the act of establishing a pricing council with incorrect composition or incorrect competence;

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 for the act of selecting a pricing assessment organization to determine the lease price of the right to exploit or transfer the right to exploit infrastructure assets for a fixed term in a manner inconsistent with the form and order prescribed by law.

  3. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of leasing the right to exploit or transfer the right to exploit infrastructure assets for a fixed term in a manner inconsistent with the approved project.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcibly return the amount of money obtained from the commission of the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 25. Violations in handling of infrastructure assets

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to declare and formulate a plan for handling of assets in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to report to the competent State agency for the recovery, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of assets when the conditions are met in accordance with law (except for cases where the public assets have expired their use period in accordance with law but the agencies, organizations, units or enterprises still have the need to use them, the head of the agency, organization, unit shall decide on the appropriate form of handling);

  c) Failing to report to the competent State agency for handling of assets in case of loss or damage.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Letting the assets deteriorate or be lost during the waiting period for handling;

  b) Failing to declare the list and current status of the assets to be handled, leading to decisions on handling of the assets in contravention of regulations.

  3. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of handling the assets without a decision of a competent authority or person.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of the amount corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged assets for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article. The determination of the amount to be returned corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged assets shall comply with Clause 6 Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 26. Violations against regulations on organization of handling of infrastructure assets

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to implement the handling plan approved by a competent authority or person within the prescribed time limit;

  b) Failing to hand over the assets subject to the decision on transfer or revocation to the receiving agency within the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Establishing a price determination council or a property destruction council without proper competence or composition as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to fully notify the public of the auction of public property as prescribed by law on property auction (failing to post notices of property auction at specified locations; failing to publicly notify on mass media or failing to notify in time for property auction as prescribed by law on property auction; failing to post notices or publicly notify with insufficient information as prescribed by law on property auction).

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to establish a price determination council or a property destruction council as prescribed by law;

  b) Selecting a price assessment organization or a professional auction organization to handle property without proper forms or procedures as prescribed by law;

  c) Failing to publicly notify the auction of public property as prescribed in Point b Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to conduct price assessment as a basis for determining the starting price when handling property in the form of sale.

  Article 27. Violations against regulations on access to and use of data on infrastructure assets

  Organizations and individuals that commit violations against regulations on access to and use of data on infrastructure assets shall be sanctioned in accordance with Article 17 of this Decree.

  Section 5. AUTHORITY TO MAKE RECORDS OF AND SANCTION ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC ASSETS

  Article 28. Authority to make records of administrative violations in the field of management and use of public assets

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations in the field of management and use of public assets include:

  a) Persons competent to sanction administrative violations in the field of management and use of public assets as prescribed in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree;

  b) Public employees assigned to inspect the observance of laws on management and use of public assets.

  2. Persons competent to make records as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall issue sanctioning decisions within their competence or transfer them to competent authorities as prescribed in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree for sanctioning.

  Article 29. The competence of the President of the People’s Committee to impose penalties

  1. The President of the People’s Committee of a district shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscate the material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  d) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The President of the People’s Committee of a province shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) A fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  d) Application of remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 30. Authority to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Inspector and persons assigned to perform specialized inspection tasks in the field of management and use of public property while on duty have the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations with a value not exceeding VND 1,000,000;

  d) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chief inspectors of provincial-level inspectorates and heads of specialized inspection teams at provincial-level inspectorates shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and instrumentalities of administrative violations valued at not more than VND 100,000,000;

  d) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspection teams at ministerial-level inspectorates shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) A fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means of administrative violations valued at not more than VND 140,000,000;

  d) Application of remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and means of administrative violations;

  d) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter III

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, SANCTIONING FORMS, SANCTION LEVELS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF SPARE AND WASTE PREVENTION

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE LAWS ON SPARE AND WASTE PREVENTION IN THE FIELD OF SPARE AND WASTE PREVENTION

  Article 31. Administrative violations against the laws on spare and waste prevention in the use of communication facilities, electricity, water, gasoline, oil, books, newspapers, office supplies, organization of conferences, seminars, expenses for reception of guests, business trips at home and abroad with the State budget; purchase of equipment for the operation of medical examination and treatment establishments provided by the State budget but not yet subject to criminal liability investigation

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the use of communication facilities, electricity, water, gasoline, oil, books, newspapers, office supplies, organization of conferences, seminars, expenses for reception of guests, business trips at home and abroad with the State budget exceeding the standards and quotas issued by competent authorities.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the purchase of equipment for the operation of medical examination and treatment establishments provided by the State budget not in accordance with the approved list by competent authorities at the following sanction levels:

  a) Between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of purchase of equipment with total value not in accordance with the list in a single purchase of less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of purchase of equipment with total value not in accordance with the list in a single purchase of between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000;

  c) Between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 in case of purchasing equipment with total value of equipment not listed in the list in a single purchase of VND 100,000,000 or more.

  3. Medical facilities that purchase equipment for operation of medical facilities provided by the State budget exceeding standards and quotas shall be sanctioned according to Article 6 of this Decree.

  Article 32. Violations of regulations of law on practice of frugality and prevention of waste in management and use of state financial funds outside the budget[10]

  1. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for the following acts:

  a) Using state financial funds outside the budget[11] for purposes other than those prescribed by the competent authority;

  b) Using state financial funds outside the budget[12] in contravention of the operating regulations and financial mechanisms of the fund issued by the competent authority.

  2. A fine of between VND 50,000,000 and VND 60,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of waste in use of investment capital in contravention of standards and regulations, exceeding quotas and unit prices as prescribed by law.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Withdrawn and returned to the State financial fund outside the budget[13] funds for misuse of funds for acts specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Forced to return to the State financial fund outside the budget[14] the illegal profits obtained for acts specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article.

  1. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 for acts of illegally obstructing the implementation of scientific research and application, implementation of projects using recycled resources.

  2. A fine of from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 for acts of exploiting and using natural resources not in accordance with the approved planning, plan and process by competent State agencies.

  Acts of violation of regulations of law on practice of frugality and prevention of waste in investment in construction of projects using the State budget shall comply with the provisions of Decree No. 139/2017/ND-CP dated November 27, 2017 of the Government on sanctioning administrative violations in investment and construction activities; exploitation, processing and trading of minerals as building materials, production and trading of building materials; management of technical infrastructure works; real estate trading, housing development, management and use of houses and offices; relevant documents and amendments (if any).

  Article 35. Acts of violation of regulations of law on practice of frugality and prevention of waste in management and use of working offices, public service establishments of organizations using the State budget funds but not yet to the level of criminal liability examination

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation against regulations on management of working offices and public service establishments of organizations using state budget funds, causing waste.

  2. For the violation against regulations on standards and norms of use of working offices and public service establishments of organizations using state budget funds, causing waste, the violation shall be sanctioned according to Article 8 of this Decree.

  Article 36. Violations against regulations of law on practice of frugality and prevention of waste in management and use of public capital and property in one-member limited liability companies with state capital but not yet to the level of criminal liability investigation

  1. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation against regulations on allocation and management and use of funds in one-member limited liability companies with state capital, not in accordance with the purpose, exceeding the norm, standard and regime issued by competent state agencies.

  2. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 40,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation against regulations on management and use of capital in one-member limited liability companies with state capital, causing waste and the violation of procurement, management and use of property and supplies in one-member limited liability companies with state capital, not in accordance with regulations of law on management of public finance and property, management of investment and construction.

  3. A fine of between VND 40,000,000 and 70,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation of procurement, management and use of fixed assets, supplies and other property in one-member limited liability companies with state capital, exceeding the norm, standard and regime issued by competent state agencies.

  4. A fine of between VND 70,000,000 and 100,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation of management and use of expenditure items in one-member limited liability companies with state capital, not in accordance with the norm, unit price, standard and regime prescribed by competent state agencies.

  Section 2. COMPETENCE TO MAKE RECORDS AND SANCTION ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF PRACTICE OF FRUGALITY AND PREVENTION OF WASTE

  Article 37. Competence to make records of administrative violations in the field of practicing frugality and combating waste

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations in the field of practicing frugality and combating waste include:

  a) Persons competent to impose administrative penalties in the field of practicing frugality and combating waste as prescribed in Article 38 and Article 39 of this Decree;

  b) Public employees assigned to inspect the observance of laws on practicing frugality and combating waste.

  2. Persons competent to make records as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall have to issue decisions on imposition of penalties within their competence or transfer to competent authorities as prescribed in Article 38 and Article 39 of this Decree to issue decisions on imposition of penalties.

  Article 38. Penalties imposed by Chairpersons of People’s Committees

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 200,000,000;

  b) Apply the remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 39. The sanctioning power of the Inspectorate

  1. The Chief Inspector of the Department and the equivalent titles assigned by the Government to perform specialized inspection functions, the head of the specialized inspection team at the Department level has the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 140,000,000;

  b) Impose remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Chief Inspectors of the Ministry shall have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 200,000,000;

  b) Impose remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter IV

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, SANCTIONING FORMS, SANCTION LEVELS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF NATIONAL STORAGE

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF NATIONAL RESERVE

  Article 40. Violations against regulations on purchase and sale of national reserve goods

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to notify or publicly post information on subjects eligible for purchase and sale; methods of purchase and sale; purchase and sale prices; purchase and sale time limits; quantities, types, specifications, standards and quality of national reserve goods as prescribed by competent State agencies.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for:

  Failure to comply with regulations on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods issued by competent State agencies.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to purchase national reserve goods when all conditions prescribed by decisions of competent State agencies are met;

  b) Purchase or sale of national reserve goods without written consent of competent State agencies;

  c) Buying or selling national reserve goods after the time limit for buying or selling national reserve goods has expired;

  d) Buying or selling national reserve goods in a manner inconsistent with the quantity, type, specifications, standards and quality approved by a competent State agency;

  dd) Failing to comply with regulations on methods of buying or selling national reserve goods issued by a competent State agency.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of arbitrarily changing the price of buying or selling national reserve goods for the purpose of making a profit.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of illegal profits made from the commission of the acts specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 41. Violations against regulations on management of national reserve goods

  1. A fine of between VND 300,000 and VND 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to keep records on the quality, quantity and types of national reserve goods during the preservation period;

  b) Failure to make periodic or ad-hoc reports on the preservation of national reserve goods.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 shall be imposed for the following violations:

  a) Failure to comply with regulations of the competent State agency on the transfer of national reserve goods;

  b) Failure to comply with regulations of the competent State agency on the transfer of national reserve goods.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced withdrawal of national reserve goods that have been transferred in contravention of regulations for the violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 42. Violations against regulations on the preservation of national reserve goods

  1. A fine for failure to comply with or non-compliance with national technical regulations or technical requirements for temporary preservation of national reserve goods issued by competent State agencies;

  Failure to comply with regulations on the time limit for preservation of national reserve goods; failure to preserve national reserve goods at the designated location as prescribed by competent State agencies shall be subject to the following fines:

  a) Between VND 300,000 and 500,000 in case of no damage;

  b) Between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at between VND 50,000,000 and under VND 100,000,000.

  2. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstructing the storage, preservation and circulation of national reserve goods at the following levels:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) Between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  3. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 for hiring organizations or individuals that are not qualified to preserve national reserve goods valued at under VND 100,000,000.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of illegal profits earned from the acts specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 43. Acts of infringing upon, sabotaging material and technical facilities, national reserve goods and national reserve warehouses but not to the extent of criminal liability examination

  1. A fine of from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 for illegally infringing upon material and technical facilities, national reserve goods and national reserve warehouses.

  2. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 for sabotaging material and technical facilities, national reserve goods and national reserve warehouses.

  3. A fine for acts of infringing upon, sabotaging national reserve goods at the following fines:

  a) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 in case of not causing damage to national reserve goods;

  b) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 in case of causing damage to national reserve goods with a value of less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 in case of causing damage to national reserve goods with a value of between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000.

  4. Additional penalties

  Confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of material and technical facilities and warehouses for national reserve goods in case of the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of national reserve goods damaged due to the acts of infringement and sabotage of national reserve goods specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 44. Violations against regulations on allocation and relief of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstructing the allocation, relief or performance of other tasks related to national reserves according to the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) Between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at between VND 50,000,000 and under VND 100,000,000.

  2. A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out procedures for allocation and relief of national reserve goods of value under VND 100,000,000 within the prescribed time limit.

  3. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for the improper use of national reserve goods; the improper allocation or relief of national reserve goods according to the decision of a competent State agency; the improper allocation or relief of national reserve goods in terms of types, quantity, quality, standards and specifications according to the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) From VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000.

  4. A fine for the act of causing loss of national reserve goods for distribution, relief or performance of another task shall be imposed at the following levels:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of national reserve goods valued at from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000.

  5. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of distributing national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 to the wrong recipients according to the decision of the competent State agency.

  6. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of delaying or postponing the distribution or relief of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 according to the regulations of the competent State agency.

  7. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of delaying or postponing the distribution of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 according to the decision of the competent State agency, causing damage to national reserve goods.

  8. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of illegal profits earned from the commission of the violations specified in Clauses 3, 5 and 6 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of lost, misused or improperly allocated national reserve goods for the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  c) Enforced return of damaged, defective or unsuitable national reserve goods for the violations specified in Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 45. Violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstructing the import, export and transport of national reserve goods at the following levels:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with procedures for import and export of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 as prescribed;

  b) Delivering goods in incorrect quantities, types, specifications, standards or quality for national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 as decided by a competent State agency.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 in incorrect quantities, types, specifications, quality, time or place as decided by a competent State agency.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 without a decision of a competent State agency.

  5. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of illegal profits earned from the commission of administrative violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;

  b) Enforced withdrawal of national reserve goods issued in contravention of regulations for the acts specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 46. Violations against regulations on receipt of national reserve goods but not reaching the level of criminal liability investigation

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Receiving national reserve goods at a time or place other than that specified in the decision of a competent State agency;

  b) Failure to comply with or improper compliance with regulations on procedures and order for receipt of national reserve goods.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to receive national reserve goods (national reserve goods are exported for relief or for performance of another task by a competent authority) when the goods have fully met the conditions prescribed by the competent State authority.

  Article 47. Acts of illegal interference in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and VND 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of illegal interference in national reserve activities in the following cases:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and VND 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate the material evidences and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 48. Violations against regulations on management of capital and fees in national reserve operations

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to report the use of money from national reserve capital for purchase of national reserve goods as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Making payment without a contract for purchase or sale of national reserve goods;

  b) Making payment when national reserve goods are not in the correct quantity or are not fully accompanied by invoices and vouchers;

  c) Making payment without a written record of contract liquidation or a list of vouchers for warehouse receipt with the signature of the head of the unit in cases of direct purchase without bidding;

  d) Making payment without approval of the head of the national reserve unit.

  d) Using the fees for import, export, and preservation of national reserve goods in a manner inconsistent with the contents or exceeding the prescribed quotas.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Using national reserve capital for purposes other than those prescribed;

  b) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on management of money withdrawn from national reserve capital;

  c) Failing to pay the remaining amount after the purchase of national reserve goods is completed as prescribed by the competent State agency.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of illegal profits obtained from the commission of the acts prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 49. Acts of violation against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, and exploitation of assets belonging to national reserve

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of national reserve assets (excluding national reserve goods storage warehouses) at the following levels:

  a) From VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case the asset is valued at less than VND 70,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case the asset is valued at from VND 70,000,000 to less than VND 150,000,000;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case the asset is valued at from VND 150,000,000 to less than VND 300,000,000;

  d) From VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 in case the asset is valued at from VND 300,000,000 to less than VND 500,000,000;

  dd) From VND 30,000,000 to VND 40,000,000 in case the asset is valued at from VND 500,000,000 or more.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of national reserve goods storage warehouses at the following levels:

  a) From VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 in case the national reserve goods storage warehouse is valued at less than VND 500,000,000;

  b) Between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 in case of national reserve goods storage with a value of VND 500,000,000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the property to its original state for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of illegal profits earned from the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. COMPETENCE TO MAKE RECORDS OF AND ENFORCE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE NATIONAL RESERVE

  Article 50. Competence to make records of administrative violations against the national reserve

  a) Persons competent to impose administrative penalties for violations against the national reserve as prescribed in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree;

  b) Public servants assigned to check the observance of the law on national reserves.

  2. Persons competent to make records as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall have to issue sanctioning decisions within their competence or transfer them to competent authorities as prescribed in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree to issue sanctioning decisions.

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose fines up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and means of administrative violations with a value not exceeding VND 100,000,000;

  2. The Chairperson of the People’s Committee of a province shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscate the material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  d) Apply the remedial measures prescribed in Clause 5, Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 52. The competence of inspectors to impose penalties

Heads of specialized inspection teams established by the Director General of the State Reserve Administration shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and means of administrative violations valued at not more than VND 100,000,000;

  d) Apply remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5, Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspection teams at the Ministry level shall perform the function of specialized inspection in the field of national reserves and have the power to:

  a) A warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means of administrative violations valued at not more than VND 140,000,000;

  d) Application of remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry has the power to:

  a) Issue a warning;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and means of administrative violations;

  d) Apply the remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5, Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 53. Authority to impose penalties of the Director of the State Reserve Department of the region, the Director General of the General Department of State Reserve

  1. The Director of the State Reserve Department of the region shall perform the function of specialized inspection in the field of national reserve and has the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  c) Confiscate the material evidences and means of administrative violations with a value not exceeding VND 140,000,000;

  d) Apply the remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5, Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The Director General of the General Department of State Reserve shall perform the function of specialized inspection in the field of national reserve and has the power to:

  a) A warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  d) Application of remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5, Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter V

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, PENALTIES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF STATE ALCOHOL

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON, PENALTIES FOR STATE BUDGET EXPENDITURES

  Article 54. Violations against regulations on state budget expenditures included in the state budget estimate shall be imposed by competent authorities.

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures on contents and works not included in the estimate approved by the competent authority;

  b) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures exceeding the estimate or capital plan for the year assigned by the competent authority or exceeding the estimate of expenditures approved (for works not concluded under contracts);

  c) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures different from the estimate assigned by the competent authority (expenditures on wrong estimate sources; expenditures on wrong purposes, subjects and contents compared to the assigned estimate); expenditures on wrong investment capital sources or expenditures on wrong project lists approved by the competent authority.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 6,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures on payment for work volumes not yet performed;

  b) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures on payment for work volumes in progress and not yet eligible for payment;

  c) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures on payment exceeding the contract value or contract appendix.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recovery of expenditures on preparation of payment dossiers and vouchers for work not yet performed; work in progress, which is not eligible for payment, and expenditures on payment exceeding the value of the contract or contract appendix as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 55. Acts of preparing dossiers and vouchers in violation of regulations and preparing dossiers and vouchers inconsistent with original dossiers and vouchers at the unit but not subject to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for signing a contract before the capital plan is approved by a competent authority (except for construction works under urgent orders).

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 6,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing dossiers and vouchers for payment to the State Treasury for expenditures that do not comply with the standards for titles and users;

  b) Preparing dossiers and vouchers for payment to the State Treasury for expenditures that exceed the spending limits in terms of quantity and value;

  c) Preparing dossiers and vouchers for payment to the State Treasury for expenditures that violate regulations (expenditures that do not meet the conditions and principles of the spending regime).

  In case an organization commits acts of purchasing or leasing state property exceeding the standards and quotas prescribed by competent state agencies, it shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of Articles 5 and 6 of this Decree.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of preparing dossiers and documents sent to the State Treasury in violation of the contents of the dossiers, invoices and original documents at the unit, but not to the extent of criminal liability examination.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced withdrawal of funds spent on preparing dossiers and documents in violation of regulations and funds spent on preparing dossiers and documents in violation of the contents of the original documents at the unit, but not to the extent of criminal liability examination, as prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 56. Acts of preparing forged dossiers and documents for state budget expenditure but not to the extent of criminal liability examination

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of preparing forged dossiers and documents sent to the State Treasury for payment of regular expenditures, public service expenditures, regular expenditures, expenditures on national target programs, expenditures on target programs for use of public service funds (excluding expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of physical facilities from regular state budget expenditures and retained fees under regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of VND 500,000,000 or more).

  2. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of preparing forged dossiers and documents sent to the State Treasury for payment of investment capital from state budget capital and investment capital from the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of physical facilities from regular state budget expenditures and retained fees under regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of VND 500,000,000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forcible withdrawal of all amounts spent on the use of forged dossiers and documents for the State budget but not yet to the extent of criminal liability examination as prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 57. Violations of the regime of payment of State budget expenditures

  1. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of making dossiers and documents requesting the State Treasury to transfer money for payment not in the name or account number of the beneficiary unit specified in the contract or contract appendix.

  2. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of making dossiers and documents requesting the payment of State budget expenditures not in accordance with the payment clauses specified in the contract or contract appendix between the unit making the estimate or the investor and the supplier of goods or services, including one of the following acts:

  a) Error in contract value;

  b) Error in payment time limit;

  c) Error in payment method;

  d) Error in payment ratio (including advance payment);

  d) Violation of other payment clauses specified in the contract and the contract appendix.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced completion of the dossier and documents in accordance with regulations for the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 58. Violations of procedures for control of expenditure commitments

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to send the State budget expenditure commitment to the State Treasury for expenditures falling within the scope of expenditure commitment as prescribed;

  b) Sending the request for State budget expenditure commitment to the State Treasury past the prescribed time limit for expenditures falling within the scope of expenditure commitment as prescribed;

  c) Sending the request for expenditure commitment exceeding the annual investment capital plan, the remaining balance of the annual estimate that is permitted to be used, or exceeding the value of the contract that is permitted to be committed to.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced to make commitment procedures before requesting the State Treasury to make payment and expenditure on the State budget for the acts specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced to redo commitment procedures for the acts specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 59. Violations of procedures and time limits for advance payment of the State budget

  1. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Making advance payment procedures after the last deadline for advance payment for regular expenditures without contracts for goods or services or expenditures not sent to the State Treasury as prescribed;

  b) Failing to make advance payment procedures for investment expenditures from the State budget and investment sources from the State budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, and expansion of physical facilities from regular expenditures of the State budget and fees left over according to regulations for regular expenditures with total investment over VND 500,000,000 paid in installments corresponding to the completed contract volume.

  2. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Not to carry out procedures for full payment of advance for regular expenditures under contracts for purchase and sale of goods and services under the regime specified in the final payment of the contract;

  b) Not to carry out procedures for full payment of advance for investment expenditures under state budget funds and investment funds from state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, and expansion of physical facilities from regular expenditures of state budget and fees retained under the regime specified for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500,000,000 when the payment value (including advance and payment for completed volume) reaches 80% of the contract value;

  c) Not to carry out or carry out procedures for payment of advance for compensation, support, and relocation expenditures after the time limit for payment of advance capital as prescribed.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced to carry out procedures for payment of advance for the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATION AND PENALTIES FOR ACCOUNT REGISTRATION AND USE AT THE STATE TREASURY

  Article 60. Violations of regulations on account registration and use at the State Treasury

  1. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for violations of regulations on account registration and use at the State Treasury.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced bank account freezing or closure for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 61. Acts of making false dossiers and documents for procedures for registration of account use at the State Treasury but not to the extent of criminal liability examination

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of making false dossiers and documents for procedures for registration of account use at the State Treasury.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced bank account freezing for the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 3. COMPETENCE TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE LAW ON THE STATE TREASURY

  Article 62. Competence to make records of administrative violations against the law on the State Treasury

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations in the field of State treasuries include:

  a) Persons competent to impose administrative penalties in the field of control of expenditure of the State budget through State treasuries as prescribed in Article 63 and Article 64 of this Decree;

  b) Public employees assigned to control expenditure of the State budget through State treasuries.

  c) Inspectors and persons assigned to perform specialized inspection tasks while on duty.

  2. Persons competent to make records as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall have to issue decisions on imposition of penalties within their competence or transfer to competent authorities as prescribed in Article 63 and Article 64 of this Decree to issue decisions on imposition of penalties.

  Article 63. Penalties imposed by inspectors

  1. Chief inspectors of the Finance Service, heads of specialized inspection teams of the Finance Service, heads of specialized inspection teams of the State Treasury shall be appointed by the Director of the State Treasury of the province and the Director General of the State Treasury shall appoint the head of the specialized inspection team of the State Treasury and have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  b) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Finance has the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. The Head of the specialized inspection team of the Ministry of Finance has the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  b) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 64. The power to impose penalties of the Director General of the State Treasury; Director of the State Treasury of provinces and centrally-run cities

  1. Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and centrally-run cities have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  b) Impose the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. General Directors of State Treasuries have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b) Impose the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter VI

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS[24]

  Article 65. Effect

  1. This Decree takes effect from September 1, 2019.

  2. Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 of the Government on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of management and use of state property; practice of thrift and prevention of waste; national reserves; state treasury and Decree No. 58/2015/ND-CP dated June 16, 2015 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 of the Government on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of management and use of state property; practice of thrift and prevention of waste; national reserves; state treasury shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  Article 66. Transitional provisions

  For administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; practice of thrift and prevention of waste; national reserves; state treasury that occurred before the effective date of this Decree but were only detected later or are under consideration or settlement, the provisions on sanctioning specified in this Decree shall apply if this Decree does not specify legal liability or specifies lighter legal liability for the violating individuals and organizations.

  Article 67. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Ministry of Finance shall guide the implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Governmental agencies, People’s Committees at all levels shall guide the implementation and inspect the implementation of sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; practice of thrift and prevention of waste; national reserves; state treasury specified in this Decree.

  3. The State’s functional agencies, organizations and individuals shall, upon detecting administrative violations in the management and use of public property; the practice of frugality and anti-wastefulness; the national reserve; the State treasury, transfer the dossiers (if any) or notify the persons competent to impose administrative penalties prescribed in this Decree for consideration and handling.

  In case the administrative violations in the management and use of public property fall under the cases of recovery of public property as prescribed by law on management and use of public property, the persons competent to impose administrative penalties prescribed in this Decree shall, after issuing the decisions on administrative penalties, notify the State agencies competent according to law on management and use of public property to issue decisions on recovery of property as prescribed. The handling of property after recovery shall comply with law on management and use of public property.

  4. The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of Government-attached agencies, chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels and relevant organizations and individuals shall be responsible for implementing this Decree.
