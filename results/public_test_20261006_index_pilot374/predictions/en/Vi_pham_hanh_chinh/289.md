                                             DECREE
           REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE TOURISM SECTOR
Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;
Pursuant to the Law on Tourism dated June 14, 2005;
Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002, and the Ordinance on Amendments to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated April 2, 2008;
At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
The Government promulgates the Decree on Penalties for Administrative Violations in the Tourism Sector, Chapter 1.
                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, sanctioning forms, sanctioning levels, sanctioning competence, sanctioning procedures and remedial measures in the tourism sector.
2. Administrative violations in the tourism sector are violations of regulations of the law on state management in the tourism sector committed intentionally or unintentionally by individuals or organizations, which are not crimes and must be sanctioned administratively according to law.
3. Administrative violations in the tourism sector specified in this Decree include:
a) Violations of regulations on tour business, tourism guidance, and tourist transport by car;
b) Violations of regulations on tourism accommodation business;
c) Violations of regulations on tourism promotion;
d) Violations of regulations on other tourism activities.
4. Other administrative violations in the field of tourism and tourism service business not specified in this Decree shall be handled in accordance with other Government Decrees on penalties for administrative violations in relevant state management fields.
Article 2. Regulated entities
Vietnamese individuals and organizations and foreign individuals and organizations (collectively referred to as entities) that commit administrative violations in the field of tourism in Vietnam.
Article 3. Penalties and remedial measures
1. For each administrative violation, the violating entity shall incur one of the following penalties:
a) Warning;
b) A fine.
2. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating entity may also incur one or more of the following additional penalties:
a) Deprive of the right to use for a fixed period the license for international travel business, tourist guide card, and tour guide certificate;
b) Confiscate the exhibits and means used for administrative violations.
3. Apart from the principal penalties and additional penalties prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individuals and organizations may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:
a) Forced to supplement the necessary interior, amenities, means, and equipment to ensure the health, safety of life, and property of tourists;
b) Forced to strictly comply with the regulations on preparation and storage of dossiers and documents as prescribed by law;

c) Forced to return to tourists or deposit with the state fund the illegally collected property of tourists;
d) Forced to pay the required deposit in full as prescribed;
dd) Forced to strictly comply with the regulations on reporting as prescribed by law;
e) Enforced to affix the car signs meeting the tourist service standards as prescribed by law;
g) Enforced to supplement the equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages of managers and service staff corresponding to the standards of each type and class of accommodation service establishments as prescribed by law;
h) Enforced to affix the signs of tourist accommodation establishments in accordance with the ranking decisions of competent regulatory agencies;
i) Enforced to remove and destroy publications, signs, signs, items and equipment that violate the law;
k) Enforced to dismantle and destroy promotional items, terminate promotional programs for some violations of regulations on tourism promotion activities;
l) Enforced to take measures to remedy environmental pollution caused by violations of regulations on environmental protection;
m) Enforced to implement decisions of competent agencies and persons regarding violations of regulations on obstructing inspection, examination and sanctioning of administrative violations.
Chapter 2.
         ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES
Section 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON TRAVEL AGENCY, TOURISM GUIDANCE AND TRAVEL CAR BUSINESS
Article 4. Violations against regulations on notification of travel agency operations A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
1. Failure to notify in writing the date of commencement of travel agency operations to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the province or centrally-run city where the enterprise is headquartered within 15 days from the date on which the enterprise officially commences operations.
2. Failure to notify in writing the change of legal representative of the enterprise, change of enterprise form, change of enterprise name, change of location of the headquarters of the travel agency to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the province or centrally-run city where the enterprise is headquartered within 15 days from the date on which the change occurs.
3. Failure to notify in writing the change of person in charge of international travel agency operations to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 30 days from the date on which the enterprise changes the person in charge of travel agency operations.
4. Failure to notify in writing the establishment and date of commencement of operation of the branch or representative office of the travel agency to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the province or centrally-run city where the enterprise is headquartered and the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the province or centrally-run city where the branch or representative office is located within 45 days from the date on which the branch or representative office is established and officially operates.
5. Failure to notify in writing the change of head, name or location of the branch or representative office of the travel agency to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the province or centrally-run city where the enterprise is headquartered and the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the province or centrally-run city where the branch or representative office is located within 10 days from the date on which the change occurs.
6. Failure to notify the General Department of Tourism in writing within 7 days from the date of losing the license for international tour business.
7. Failure to promptly notify the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of accidents, risks or incidents that may occur to tourists.
Article 5. Violations against regulations on tour business
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Using a person who has worked in the field of tour business for less than 3 years as the operator of domestic tour business;
b) Using a person who has worked in the field of tour business for less than 4 years as the operator of international tour business;
c) Using a person who has worked in the field of tour business for less than 3 years as the operator of domestic tour business;

d) Using a person who has worked in the field of tour business for less than 4 years as the operator of international tour business;
d) The tour contract lacks one of the contents as prescribed by law.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to provide a written tour program for tourists or representatives of tourist groups;
b) Failing to enter into a written tour contract with tourists or representatives of tourists as prescribed;
c) Failing to settle legitimate requests or proposals of tourists as prescribed by law;
d) Using tour guides to carry out the tour program without a written contract with the tour guide or a contract lacking the basic contents as prescribed;
dd) Failing to provide information related to the tour program when the tour agency request is made;
e) Failing to provide clear, public and honest information on the quantity and prices of tourist services to tourists;
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) The operator of a tour business uses a certificate of working time which is not consistent with reality to operate the tour business;
b) Fails to implement or fails to implement in a proper manner the procedures for making and storing dossiers and documents as prescribed by law;
c) Uses a guide with an expired card to guide tourists.
4. A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Uses equipment and facilities which do not ensure the health, safety of life and property of tourists as prescribed by law, except for the cases specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of this Decree;
b) Fails to implement the reporting regime to the General Department of Tourism and the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism as prescribed;
c) Uses a guide without a card to guide tourists.
5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Conducting international tour business without at least three international tour guides;
b) Failing to purchase insurance for Vietnamese tourists going abroad during the implementation of the tour program as prescribed;
c) Changing the tour program, standards and services that have been signed without the consent of tourists or their representatives;
6. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to ensure the sufficient amount of deposit for international tour business as prescribed;
b) Erasing or modifying contents in the license for international tour business;
c) Collecting money outside the contract or other acts of illegally collecting profits from tourists;
d) Not managing the operation and business of branches, representative offices and affiliated units of enterprises in accordance with law;
dd) Not managing tourists according to signed contracts and tourism programs;
e) Falsely declaring contents in dossiers for application for issuance or renewal of licenses for international tour business;
g) Failing to carry out procedures for renewal of licenses for international tour business in accordance with law;
h) Using names of enterprises, transaction names or abbreviations inconsistent with names registered with competent regulatory agencies for business operations;
7. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 25,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Using foreigners as tour guides in Vietnam;
b) Conducting tour business after having been notified of temporary suspension, termination or having been suspended by competent regulatory agencies.

8. A fine of between VND 25,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of operating a tour business without complying with the contents of the international tour business license.
9. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Allowing other organizations or individuals to use the international tour business license for operating a tour business;
b) Operating an international tour business without an international tour business license.
10. Additional penalties:
a) The right to use the international tour business license shall be suspended for up to 12 months for the violations specified in Points a and c Clause 5; Points a, d and dd Clause 6 and Point a Clause 7 of this Article;
b) The right to use the international tour business license shall be suspended for between 12 and 24 months for the violations specified in Clause 8 and Point a Clause 9 of this Article;
c) The exhibits of administrative violations shall be confiscated for the violations specified in Point a Clause 3 and Point e Clause 6 of this Article.
11. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced to supplement sufficient means and equipment to ensure the health, safety of life and property of tourists for the violations specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced to strictly comply with the regulations on preparation and storage of dossiers and documents in accordance with law for the violations specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;
c) Enforced to strictly comply with the regulations on reporting in accordance with law for the violations specified in Point b Clause 4 of this Article;
d) Enforced to pay the required deposit for the violations specified in Point a Clause 6 of this Article;
dd) Enforced to return to tourists or deposit with the state fund the illegally collected property of tourists for the violations specified in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 6 of this Article.
Article 6. Violations against regulations on travel agency business
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to notify in writing the time of commencement of operation of the travel agency to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 15 days from the date of commencement of operation of the travel agency;
b) Failure to notify in writing the change of the legal representative, the change of the name and address of the travel agency to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 15 days from the date of the change;
c) Failure to clearly, publicly and honestly inform the quantity and price of the tourist services of the travel agency provider to the tourist;
d) Operation at an address other than the one registered with the competent authority.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Erasing, altering or copying the contents of the tourist program of the travel agency provider;
b) Selling the tourist program at a price higher than the price of the travel agency provider;
c) Operating as a travel agency without having registered for the travel agency business;
d) Providing travel agency services without a written travel agency contract with a travel business enterprise.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Providing travel agency services for an enterprise without an international travel business license to sell travel programs to international tourists and Vietnamese tourists going abroad;
b) Continuing to provide travel agency services after the competent regulatory authority has revoked the certificate of registration for travel agency services.
4. Remedial measures:
Forcible refund of tourist fees or contribution to the state fund of the amounts collected in contravention of regulations for the acts specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 7. Violations against regulations on tourism guidance
1. A warning or a fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed on a tour guide or tour guide who fails to wear a badge or certificate while practicing his/her profession.

2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Giving incorrect or incomplete explanations of the introduction at tourist spots or tourist areas;
b) Failing to carry the tour program or assignment papers of the tour business while providing guidance;
c) Failing to guide tourists to comply with Vietnamese law, regulations and rules of the destination and respect local customs and practices;
d) Failing to provide sufficient information on the tour schedule and program to the representative of the tourist group or tourist.
3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to have an employment contract with the tour business;
b) Failing to disseminate and guide tourists on measures to ensure the safety of their lives, health and property while implementing the tour program;
c) Permitting other persons to use tourist guide cards or tour guide certificates to practice their profession;
d) Permitting other persons to use their tourist guide cards or tour guide certificates to practice their profession;
dd) Permitting the use of expired tourist guide cards;
e) Falsely declaring documents in the application for issuance, renewal or re-issuance of tourist guide cards or tour guide certificates.
4. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Erasing or altering the contents of tourist guide cards or tour guide certificates for the purpose of practicing their profession;
b) Using domestic tourist guide cards to guide foreign tourists;
c) Giving tours in tourist areas or tourist spots without a tour guide certificate.
5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Providing tour guides without holding a tourist guide card as prescribed;
b) Using a fake tourist guide card or a fake tour guide certificate to provide services;
c) Arbitrarily changing the tour program or reducing the standards and services of tourists;
d) Misleadingly introducing the cultural, historical, national and human values of Vietnam, thereby adversely affecting the image, traditions, ethics and fine customs of the Vietnamese nation;
dd) Taking advantage of the tour guiding activities to provide information that affects the security, order and social safety;
e) Taking tourists to prohibited areas;
g) Collecting money outside the contract or other acts of illegally profiting from tourists.
6. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed on foreign nationals who provide tour guiding services in Vietnam.
7. Additional penalties:
a) The tourist guide card shall be suspended for up to 12 months for the violations specified in Point d Clause 2; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article;
b) The tourist guide card shall be suspended for between 12 and 24 months for the violations specified in Point c Clause 3; Points a and b Clause 4 or recidivism of the violations specified in Points a, b and c Clause 2; Points a and b Clause 3; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article;
c) The tour guide certificate shall be suspended for between 12 and 24 months for the violations specified in Point c Clause 3; Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
d) The exhibits used for administrative violations shall be confiscated for the violations specified in Points d, dd and e Clause 3; Point b Clause 5 of this Article.
8. Remedial measures:
Forcible return of tourist properties or transfer to the state fund of properties illegally collected from tourists for the violations specified in Points c and g Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 8. Violations against regulations on tourist transport cars

1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with less than 09 seats without ensuring interior, facilities and equipment as prescribed for tourist transport.
2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with 09 seats to less than 24 seats without ensuring interior, facilities and equipment as prescribed for tourist transport.
3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with 24 seats or more without ensuring interior, facilities and equipment as prescribed for tourist transport cars.
4. A fine of between VND 4,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using a special-purpose car (caravan) or a two-storey car without ensuring interior, facilities and equipment as prescribed for tourist transport cars.
5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Changing the transport schedule without the consent of tourists;
b) Failing to affix the car’s signboard meeting the tourist service standards as prescribed by law;
c) Using drivers of tourist transport vehicles without tourist training certificates;
d) Using staff serving on tourist transport vehicles without tourist training certificates.
6. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to properly report to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Department of Transport as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to re-apply for tourist vehicle license plates as prescribed by law;
c) Failing to purchase passenger insurance for tourists on transport vehicles.
7. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Using fake tourist vehicle license plates for business operations;
b) Acceptance of tourist transport cars.
8. Additional penalties:
Confiscation of tourist transport cars with fake tourist service standards for the violation specified in Point a, Clause 7 of this Article.
9. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced addition of sufficient interior, amenities and equipment of tourist transport cars to ensure the health, safety of life and property of tourists for the violation specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced installation of tourist transport cars with tourist service standards according to law for the violation specified in Point c, Clause 5 of this Article;
c) Enforced implementation of reporting regimes according to regulations for the violation specified in Point a, Clause 6 of this Article.
Article 9. Violations against regulations on establishment and operation of branches and representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam
Violations against regulations on establishment and operation of branches and representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam shall be handled according to Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 13; Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 14 of the Government's Decree No. 06/2008/ND-CP of January 16, 2008 on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of trade.
SECTION 2. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON TOURISM ACCOMMODATION
Article 10. Violations against regulations on tourism accommodation
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to notify in writing the time of commencement of operation of tourism accommodation establishments to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the locality where the tourism accommodation establishments are located within 15 days from the date on which the tourism accommodation establishments officially start operating;
b) Failure to notify in writing the change of the head of the tourism accommodation establishments or the name of the tourism accommodation establishments to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 15 days from the date on which the change occurs;

c) Failure to notify in writing the application of surcharges on telecommunications charges in tourism accommodation establishments that have been ranked to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Department of Information and Communications within 15 days from the date on which the surcharges are applied.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to comply with regulations on nameplates and classification of tourist accommodation establishments;
b) Failure to affix classification nameplates after being classified by the General Department of Tourism and the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
c) Failure to promulgate and postulate internal regulations of tourist accommodation establishments;
d) Failure to provide clear and public information on quantity, quality and postulate prices of services and goods for tourists;
dd) Failure to hang decisions on recognition of classification of tourist accommodation establishments in conspicuous positions at the reception desk.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to comply with or improper compliance with regulations on preparation and storage of dossiers and documents on business activities of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
b) Failure to provide sufficient number of sleeping rooms and area according to standards corresponding to each type and classification of accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
c) Failure to ensure standards for parking and internal traffic, reception area as prescribed by law;
d) Failure to ensure quantity and standards of restaurant, bar as prescribed by law;
dd) Failure to ensure standards of kitchen area as prescribed by law;
e) Failure to ensure quantity and standards of conference, seminar, meeting room as prescribed by law;
g) Failure to ensure standards of other equipment and facilities as prescribed by law;
h) Failure to ensure standards of service as prescribed by law;
i) Failure to ensure standards of professional qualifications, foreign languages of managers and service staff as prescribed by law.
4. A fine of from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Using equipment and facilities that do not ensure the health, safety of life and property of tourists in accordance with law;
b) Failing to properly report to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism in accordance with law;
c) Failing to supplement equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages of managers and service staff corresponding to the ranking standards at the request of the tourism management authority.
5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to register with the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism for assessment and ranking of tourist accommodation establishments after three months from the date of official operation of tourist accommodation establishments;
b) Failing to register with the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism for re-assessment and re-acknowledgment of the ranking of tourist accommodation establishments before three months from the date of expiration of the ranking of tourist accommodation establishments;
c) Using star images attached to tourist accommodation establishments or on publications, billboards, signs, items and equipment used at tourist accommodation establishments when the ranking has not been recognized or is not consistent with the recognized ranking of the accommodation establishments.
6. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Using names of tourist accommodation establishments, transaction names, or abbreviations that are inconsistent with the names of tourist accommodation establishments registered with competent regulatory authorities for business operations;
b) Collecting money outside contracts or other acts of illicit profit collection from tourists;
c) Charging service fees in violation of regulations.
7. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 25,000,000 shall be imposed for tourist accommodation business operations after being notified of suspension, termination, or having their business operations suspended by competent regulatory authorities.
8. The provisions from Clause 1 to Clause 6 of this Article shall also apply to guesthouses and boarding houses of ministries, sectors, and localities that operate tourist accommodation establishments.

9. Additional penalties:
Confiscation of material evidence of administrative violations for violations specified in Point a Clause 4 and Clause 8 of this Article.
10. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced to supplement all equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications, foreign languages of managers, service staff corresponding to standards of each type, class of tourist accommodation establishments according to regulations of law for violations at points b, c, d, dd, e, g, h and i clause 3; point c clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced to supplement all means, equipment to ensure health, safety of life, property of tourists for violations at point a clause 4 of this Article;
c) Enforced to return tourists or put into state funds property illegally collected from tourists for violations at points b and c clause 6 of this Article;
d) Enforced to remove, cancel publications, billboards, signs, items, equipment for violations at point a clause 2 and point c clause 5 of this Article;
dd) Enforced to put signs of tourist accommodation establishments according to decisions of ranking of competent state agencies for violations at point b clause 2 of this Article.
Article 11. Violations against regulations on trade in goods, conditional business services in tourist accommodation establishments
A fine of from 3,000,000 VND to 5,000,000 VND for failure to register in writing with competent state agencies when trading in goods, conditional business services in tourist accommodation establishments that have been ranked.
SECTION 3. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON TOURISM PROMOTION
Article 12. Violations against regulations on tourism promotion activities
1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) The content of the tourism product being promoted or advertised is not consistent with the national tourism database that has been published;
b) The tourism promotion program that has been informed and advertised is not implemented or is not implemented correctly;
c) The tourism product being promoted or advertised is not consistent with its actual content and quality;
d) The tourism product being promoted or advertised uses a title or common symbol that is not defined by the General Department of Tourism.
2. A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of using tourism promotion activities to harm the legitimate rights and interests of other organizations or individuals.
3. Remedial measures:
Forcible dismantling and destruction of tourism-related promotional items, termination of promotional programs for violations specified in Points a, c and d, Clause 1 of this Article.
SECTION 4. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON OTHER TOURISM ACTIVITIES
Article 13. Violations against regulations on tourism resources and tourism development planning A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for exploiting tourism resources in violation of law in tourist areas, tourist spots or areas designated for tourism development.
Article 14. Violations against regulations on tourism environmental protection
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to post environmental protection regulations in visible locations in tourist accommodations, tourist areas, tourist spots or tourist transport vehicles.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to provide public sanitation facilities to meet environmental sanitation requirements in tourist accommodations, tourist areas, tourist spots or tourist transport vehicles;
b) Failure to classify waste in accordance with regulations in tourist accommodations, tourist areas, tourist spots or tourist transport vehicles;
c) Failure to arrange officials to monitor and manage the environmental protection of tourism facilities corresponding to each type and class of tourism accommodation facility in accordance with law.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

a) Failure to collect and treat waste in accordance with regulations in tourism accommodation facilities, tourist areas, tourist spots and tourist transport vehicles in accordance with regulations;
b) Damage to the living environment of wild animals in areas with tourism resources;
c) Cutting, breaking branches or committing other acts causing damage to green trees and vegetation in areas with tourism resources.
4. A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Failure to make annual reports on environmental protection of tourism in accordance with law;
b) Failure to arrange collection and treatment equipment to meet the requirements for environmental hygiene in tourism accommodation facilities, tourist transport vehicles, tourist areas and tourist spots in accordance with regulations.
5. Additional penalties:
Confiscate the material evidences and means used to commit administrative violations against the violations specified in Points b and c Clause 3 of this Article.
6. Remedial measures:
Enforce the remedial measures for environmental pollution caused by the violations specified in Points a and b Clause 2; Points a Clause 3 and b Clause 4 of this Article.
Article 15. Violations against regulations on management and business operations in tourist areas and tourist spots A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
1. Do not promulgate regulations on management of tourist areas and tourist spots;
2. Promulgating regulations of tourist areas and tourist spots contrary to law;
3. Violation of regulations of tourist areas and tourist spots.
Article 16. Sanctions for obstructing inspection, examination and sanctioning of administrative violations
1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for refusing to receive inspection or examination decisions or decisions on enforcement of decisions on sanctioning of administrative violations.
2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to make declarations or making inaccurate or late declarations at the request of officials on duty or competent regulatory agencies;
b) Failing to provide or providing insufficient information and documents related to inspection, examination and sanctioning of administrative violations by officials on duty or competent regulatory agencies;
c) Obstructing the work of inspection teams or persons assigned to inspect or examine;
d) Making threatening, insulting or offensive remarks or actions against persons on duty to inspect, examine or handle violations.
3. A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Delaying or evading the implementation of administrative decisions or inspection decisions of competent persons or agencies;
b) Unilaterally removing seals on exhibits or means of violation or unilaterally altering the scene of administrative violations;
c) Dispersing, altering or substituting exhibits under inspection, inspection or temporary custody;
d) Assaulting persons on duty.
4. Additional penalties:
Confiscation of exhibits and means used for administrative violations for violations specified in Points b and c, Clause 3 of this Article.
5. Remedial measures:
Enforced implementation of decisions of competent agencies or persons for violations specified in Clause 1; Points a and b, Clause 2 and Point a, Clause 3 of this Article.
Chapter 3.
                     JURISDICTION TO ENFORCE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES
Article 17. Competence to sanction administrative violations of chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

The presidents of the People’s Committees at all levels shall have the power to impose penalties within their competence as prescribed in Clauses 4 and 5 of Article 1 of the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on handling of administrative violations of 2008, Article 30 of the Ordinance on handling of administrative violations of 2002, which has been amended by the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on handling of administrative violations of 2007, for administrative violations specified in this Decree within the management area of the localities.
Article 18. Competence to sanction administrative violations of the inspection of Culture, Sports and Tourism
1. Cultural, Sports and Tourism Inspector who is on duty and has competence:
a) A warning;
b) A fine of up to VND 500,000;
c) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;
d) Apply remedial measures as prescribed in Points a, b, d, dd, e, g, h, i, k, l and m Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
dd) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
2. Chief Inspector of the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall have the competence to:
a) Issue warnings;
b) Impose a fine of up to VND 30,000,000;
c) Suspend the right to use licenses and practice certificates under their competence;
d) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations;
d) Apply the remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
3. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall have the competence to:
a) Issue warnings;
b) Impose a fine up to the maximum level of the tourism sector;
c) Suspend the right to use the license or certificate of practice within their competence;
d) Confiscate the material evidences and means used for administrative violations;
dd) Apply the remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
Article 19. Competence to sanction administrative violations of People’s Public Security, Border Guard and Coast Guard
The People's Public Security, Border Guard and Coast Guard shall have the power to impose penalties within their competence as prescribed in Clauses 6, 7 and 8 Article 1 of the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on handling of administrative violations of 2008 and Article 37 of the Ordinance on handling of administrative violations of 2002 for administrative violations in the field of tourism directly related to the field of their management as prescribed in this Decree.
Article 20. Competence of inspectors of other sectors to impose penalties
Within the scope of state management competence prescribed by the Government, inspectors and chief inspectors of other inspection agencies have the competence to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of tourism specified in this Decree, which fall under the state management of their respective sectors.
Article 21. Promulgation of forms of records and decisions used in administrative sanctioning This Decree promulgates forms of records and decisions used in administrative sanctioning in the field of tourism.
Chapter 4.
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 22. Effect
1. This Decree takes effect from April 30, 2012.
2. This Decree replaces the Government's Decree No. 149/2007/ND-CP dated October 9, 2007 on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of tourism.
Article 23. Implementation responsibilities
1. The Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall organize the implementation of this Decree.

2. Ministers, heads of ministerial agencies, heads of Governmental agencies and Presidents of People's Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall implement this Decree.
