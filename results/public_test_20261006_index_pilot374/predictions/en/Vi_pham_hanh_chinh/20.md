                                                  DECREE

                 REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE SECTOR OF MARITIME TRANSPORT

  Decree No. 142/2017/ND-CP dated December 1, 2017 of the Government on sanctioning of administrative violations in the maritime sector, which came into force on February 1, 2018, was amended and supplemented by:

  1. Decree No. 123/2021/ND-CP dated December 28, 2021 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Decrees on administrative penalties in the maritime sector; land and railway transport, effective from January 1, 2022.

  2. Decree No. 80/2026/ND-CP dated March 19, 2026 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 142/2017/ND-CP dated December 11, 2017 of the Government on administrative penalties in the maritime sector, amended by Decree No. 123/2021/ND-CP dated December 28, 2021 of the Government and Decree No. 139/2021/ND-CP dated December 31, 2021 of the Government on administrative penalties in the inland waterway transport sector, effective from May 15, 2026.

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Vietnamese Maritime Code dated November 25, 2015;

  At the request of the Minister of Transport;

  The Government promulgates the Decree on administrative penalties in the maritime sector[1].

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations; completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations; forms of sanction, sanction levels, and remedial measures for each administrative violation; sanctioning competence, specific fine levels according to titles, and competence to make records on administrative violations in the maritime sector; the implementation of forms of sanctioning administrative violations and remedial measures.

  2. Administrative violations in the maritime sector specified in this Decree include:

  a) Violations against regulations on construction, management and operation of maritime infrastructure;

  b) Violations against regulations on construction, management and operation of dry ports;

  c) Violations against regulations on operation of ships and boats;

  d) Violations against regulations on registration, deregistration of ships and boats and crew arrangement; use of professional certificates and crew books;

  dd) Violations against regulations on maritime bill of lading;

  e) Violations against regulations on maritime transport, multimodal transport, maritime transport support services and maritime safety assurance services;

  g) Violations against regulations on conditions for operation of shipbuilding, refitting and repair establishments and ship dismantling activities;

  h) Violations against regulations on safety of containers;

  i) Violations against regulations on maritime search and rescue, salvage of sunken property and maritime signaling;

  k) Violations against regulations on training of crew members;

  l) Violations against regulations on prevention of marine pollution;

  m) Violations against regulations on maritime labor; declaration of maritime labor accidents;

  n) Violations against regulations on prevention and control of natural disasters in the maritime sector;

  o) Violations against regulations on prevention and control of infectious diseases in the field of goods

  p)[4] Violation of regulations on state management, inspection, examination and sanctioning of administrative violations in the maritime sector.

  q)[5] Violation of regulations on posting of information on prices and surcharges in the maritime sector.

  3.[6] Acts of violation prescribed in Points c, d, dd, e, i and l Clause 2 of this Article, if committed outside the port waters and not prescribed in the Decree on sanctioning administrative violations against the waters, islands and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, shall also be sanctioned according to this Decree.

  4. Other administrative violations related to the maritime sector not specified in this Decree shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of the Decrees on sanctioning administrative violations in related sectors. Persons competent to sanction specified in Articles 60, 61, 62 and 63 of this Decree shall, upon detecting the administrative violations specified in this Clause, be sanctioned according to their competence.

  5. The handling of administrative violations in the maritime sector shall comply with this Decree and relevant provisions of the law on handling of administrative violations.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the maritime sector; persons competent to make records and persons competent to impose penalties for administrative violations in the maritime sector.

  2. Foreign individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations within the territorial waters, the contiguous zone, the exclusive economic zone and the continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or on ships bearing the Vietnamese flag shall be sanctioned for administrative violations as prescribed in this Decree and relevant provisions of Vietnamese law on handling of administrative violations, except otherwise provided for by international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  2a.[8] Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) Economic organizations established in accordance with law;

  b) Social organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-professional organizations, socio-political-professional organizations;

  c) Non-business service units and other organizations as prescribed by law.

  2b.[9] Business households must register their businesses in accordance with law and households that commit administrative violations specified in this Decree shall be sanctioned as individuals.

  3. Based on the nature and severity of the violations specified in Clause 2 Article 1 of this Decree, competent persons shall determine the specific sanctioning of organizations and individuals for administrative violations as prescribed by law.

  Article 3. Statute of limitations for sanctioning of administrative violations[10]

  The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the maritime sector is 01 year; for administrative violations related to port construction, dry docks, maritime works, environmental protection, price management and overseas labor management, the statute of limitations for sanctioning is 02 years.

  Article 3a. Completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations[11]

  1. Completed administrative violations include the following administrative violations:

  a) Violations against regulations on cargo stacking at points a, 1, 2 and 3, Article 16 of this Decree. The time of termination of the violation is calculated from the time of detection of the act of loading cargo exceeding the permitted gross tonnage (cargo volume) of the vessel;

  b) Violations detected through technical and professional equipment. The time of termination of the violation is calculated from the time the technical and professional equipment records the violation;

  c) Administrative violations prescribed in this Decree that are not covered by points a and b of this clause but have been completed before the time the competent person detects the violation. In cases where there are no documents or evidence to accurately determine the time of termination of the violation, these violations shall still be considered ongoing within the statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations.

  2. Administrative violations prescribed in this Decree that are not covered by clause 1 of this Article are ongoing administrative violations.

  Article 4. Forms of sanctioning administrative violations and remedial measures

  1. Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the maritime sector shall incur one of the following principal penalties for each violation:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fines.

  2. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual may also incur one or more of the following additional penalties:

  a) Suspension of the right to use the practice license or certificate for a fixed period;

  b) Suspension of operation for a fixed period;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for administrative violations, including: certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents not issued by competent authorities, and means used for administrative violations.

  3. Apart from the penalties specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the maritime sector shall also incur the remedial measures specified in Chapter II of this Decree, including:

  a) Enforced to change or modify the name of the port, offshore oil and gas port, port terminal, wharf, floating dock, water area or water zone in accordance with the naming principles;

  Enforced to arrange sufficient port security officers in accordance with regulations;

  Enforced to have a port security plan approved in accordance with regulations;

  Enforced to conduct inspections of the quality of port infrastructure in a timely manner or after collisions or impacts affecting the structure of the maritime works;

  b) Enforced to arrange and install life-saving equipment of ships in accordance with regulations;

  Enforced to fully establish maritime signals in accordance with regulations or establish appropriate maritime signals in accordance with regulations;

  Enforced to move the seabed, floating docks or fishing and aquaculture equipment out of the designated positions in the port waters;

  Enforced to demolish illegal construction works and parts thereof;

  c) Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been changed;

  d) Enforced remediation of the dredging supervision system on the dredging equipment in the construction site must ensure readiness and continuous operation;

  dd) Enforced installation of the dredging supervision system on the dredging equipment in the construction site;

  Enforced to have or implement or implement correctly the maritime safety plan approved by the competent authority;

  e) Enforced to lower the part of the cargo exceeding the permitted load of the truck in case the truck has not yet left the loading area;

  g) Enforced to build the maritime works protection plan or enforce the organization to implement the approved maritime works protection plan;

  Enforced to build the natural disaster response plan according to regulations;

  h) Enforced to change or repair the name of the dry port according to the name announced by the competent authority;

  i) Enforced declaration of additional and corrected information;

  ii) Enforced payment of maritime charges and other related fees as prescribed;

  iii) Enforced reimbursement of costs for salvage of sunken assets as prescribed;

  k) Enforced relocation of obstacles on the quay to the designated location;

  Enforced relocation from the operating zone in accordance with the ship's operating level;

  l) Enforced disembarkment of the number of passengers or cargo exceeding the prescribed amount;

  Enforced departure from the location;

  Enforced restoration of the original state of the signal location or structures if the location is moved or the signal structures are affected;

  m) Enforced to take safety measures for the transport of oversized or overweight goods;

  Enforced to carry out procedures for registration of the Vietnamese flag as prescribed;

  Enforced to carry out procedures for cancellation of ship registration as prescribed;

  n) Enforced to fully and accurately declare information on disembarkation and embarkation and assign titles to crew members via electronic means into the crew management database of the Vietnam Maritime and Waterway Administration as prescribed;

  o) Enforced to assign maritime pilot vessels in accordance with the certificate of professional competence of the maritime pilot vessel or the certificate of operation area of the maritime pilot vessel;

  Enforced to fully and promptly provide pilot services in the mandatory maritime pilot area or on the assigned pilot route;

  Enforced to use or arrange sufficient means for picking up and dropping off the pilot as prescribed or use the means for picking up and dropping off the pilot in a manner that ensures safety conditions;

  Enforced to arrange sufficient number of pilot vessels or minimum means as prescribed;

  p) To forcibly export the number of maritime signal beacons imported from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam;

  q) To forcibly arrange rest time for crew members as prescribed;

  p) To forcibly provide provisions and food for crew members working on board;

  p) To forcibly provide funds for crew members returning home;

  p) To fulfill the shipowner's responsibility for crew members who suffer work-related accidents or occupational diseases;

  p) To buy compulsory insurance for crew members working on board;

  p) To forcibly arrange crew members returning home within the prescribed time limit;

  r) To forcibly pay for the cost of requesting expertise, inspection, measurement and analysis of environmental samples in case of violations leading to oil spills or environmental pollution according to the current standard and unit price;

  Enforced to take measures to remedy environmental pollution and report on the remedied consequences of violations;

  Enforced to pay compensation for damage caused by oil pollution in accordance with regulations within the time limit specified by the competent person in the decision on sanctioning administrative violations;

  s) Enforced to post or post correctly the list of organizations and individuals that have signed contracts for collection and treatment of waste from ships at seaports and the price of collection and treatment of waste in accordance with regulations;

  Enforced to report on the management of collection and treatment of waste from ships at seaports in accordance with regulations;

  Enforced to declare or declare correctly to the Maritime Port Authority the waste in accordance with regulations;

  Enforced to take measures to remedy environmental pollution from waste that has been spilled, causing dissemination and leakage during the collection of waste from ships;

  Enforced to take measures to remedy regulations on prevention of pollution caused by ships;

  Enforced to take measures to remedy environmental pollution and violations of regulations on management and control of ballast water and ballast sediment of ships;

  t) Enforced to re-conclude labor contracts of crew members in accordance with labor permits;

  Enforced to leave the ship;

  Enforced to correct information on the position and status of the ship in case of natural disasters;

  Enforced to carry out medical examination and treatment;

  Enforced to return to the ship or medical quarantine area;

  Enforced to carry out medical quarantine, compulsory medical quarantine and medical treatment for persons, ships and goods carrying infectious disease agents.

  u) Enforced to return certificates, certifications, permits, written permissions, certificates and documents that have been erased or altered to the competent authorities or persons that have issued them.

  4. For administrative violations prescribed with the sanction level according to the volume of the ship, the total volume (GT) is the volume measured according to the 1969 International Convention on Measuring the Volume of Ships, which is recorded in the certificate issued by the inspection authority.

  In case the certificate of a vessel does not specify the total displacement, the total displacement shall be converted as follows:

  a) Seagoing ships and inland waterway vessels: 1.5 tons of deadweight shall be equivalent to 1 GT;

  b) Salar: 1 ton of total deadweight shall be equivalent to 1 GT;

  c) Tugger, pusher, passenger ship (including hydro-aircraft) and floating crane: 1 HP shall be equivalent to 0.5 GT; 1 KW shall be equivalent to 0.7 GT; 1 ton of lifting capacity of a crane on board a vessel shall be equivalent to 6 GT;

  d) Passenger ship without specified engine power: 1 passenger seat shall be equivalent to 0.67 GT; 1 berth shall be equivalent to 4 GT;

  dd) In case the vessel is a combined tug, combined pusher or combined side-by-side vessel: It shall be equivalent to the total displacement of the entire vessel including the sal, the head of the tug or the head of the pusher;

  e) The conversion specified in Points a, b, c, d and dd Clause 4 of this Article shall be chosen according to the method with the largest total displacement;

  g) Units of engine power: The main engine power of a vessel shall be calculated according to HP, CV or KW; the fraction of less than 1 HP or 1 KW shall be rounded to 1 HP, 1 CV or 1 KW.

  5. [15] For administrative violations related to ships and boats without information to calculate the total displacement of the ship as prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article, the method of determining the gross tonnage and capacity of the ship for application of penalties is as follows:

  a) The gross tonnage of the ship is denoted by T (ton) and calculated by the formula T = A x K, where:

  - A is the value of the main deck length, measured from the bow to the stern of the ship, multiplied by the value of the deck edge width at the middle of the ship, multiplied by the value of the side height, measured from the bottom to the middle of the deck, calculated by the formula A = L x B x D, where:

  L (m): Main deck length, measured from the bow to the stern of the ship;

  B (m): Deck edge width, measured at the middle of the ship;

  D (m): Side height, measured from the bottom to the middle of the deck;

  - K is the coefficient corresponding to the value of A and applied as follows:

  For the value of A from 4.55 m3 to 18.76 m3, the coefficient K = 0.26;

  For the value of A from 18.76 m3 to 49.80 m3, the coefficient K = 0.29;

  For the value of A from 49.80 m3 to 387.20 m3, the coefficient K = 0.35;

  For the value of A from 387.20 m3 to 1,119.80 m3, the coefficient K = 0.51;

  If the value of A is greater than 1,119.80 m3, the coefficient K = 0.57.

  b) If the vessel is not a cargo or passenger vessel, the form and level of sanction shall be determined based on the total power of the main engine installed on the vessel. If there is no basis for determining the power of the main engine installed on the vessel, and no label is available, the sanction level shall be determined based on the power of the 50-horsepower engine;

  c) In case the total deadweight is not determined according to the provisions of Point a, Clause 5 of this Article, the competent person shall request the ship inspection authority to determine the total deadweight of the vessel as the basis for issuing the decision on sanctioning administrative violations;

  d) For ships and boats such as floating docks or other floating structures on which construction equipment such as excavators and suction dredgers are installed for dredging waterways, mineral extraction, and cargo loading and unloading, if there is insufficient basis to determine the total tonnage or capacity of the ships and boats, the determination shall be as follows:

  A vessel with a length of up to 10 meters or a width of up to 4 meters shall be considered as a vessel with a gross tonnage of 5 to 15 tons;

  A vessel with a length of more than 10 meters or a width of more than 4 meters shall be considered as having a gross tonnage of more than 15 tons;

  The length of a vessel shall be calculated from the outermost point of the vessel’s bow to the outermost point of the vessel’s rudder or from the two outermost points of the vessel where the largest cross-section is located on the deck;

  The width of a vessel shall be calculated from one side of the deck to the other side of the deck at the location with the largest size.

  Article 4a. Enforcement of penalties and remedial measures[16]

  1. The enforcement of penalties and remedial measures prescribed in this Decree shall comply with the Law on Administrative Violations and guiding documents.

  2. Violating individuals and organizations shall cooperate with relevant organizations and individuals (if any) in implementing remedial measures specified in the administrative violation sanctioning decisions in accordance with law and shall bear all costs for the implementation of such remedial measures.

  3. For remedial measures of compulsory return of certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents, or papers that have been erased or altered, violating individuals and organizations shall implement the remedial measures of compulsory return of certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents, or papers that have been erased or altered in accordance with Article 85 of the Law on Administrative Violations.

  The person competent to issue decisions on remedial measures shall transfer the certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents, or papers that have been erased or altered to the competent authority or person that issued such certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents, or papers.

  4. For the remedy measures of compulsory payment of maritime charges and other related fees as prescribed:

  a) Persons competent to impose administrative penalties and violating individuals and organizations shall contact the maritime or inland waterway regulatory authority where the vessel last departs to determine the maritime charges and other related fees as prescribed.

  b) Violating individuals and organizations shall notify the results of the remedy measures to the persons competent to impose administrative penalties.

  Article 5. Principles for determining the fine amount

  The fine amount for each administrative violation specified in this Decree is the fine amount applicable to individuals; in case of the same administrative violation, the fine amount for organizations shall be twice that for individuals.

  Chapter II

      ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES AND REMEDY MEASURES
                                         QUOTA

  Section 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON CONSTRUCTION, MANAGEMENT AND UTILIZATION OF MARITIME INFRASTRUCTURE

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on announcement of opening of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, port terminals, wharves[18]

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for falsifying information in application for announcement of opening of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, port terminals, wharves.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for using certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents that have been erased or altered to falsify their contents or not issued by competent authorities but not yet to the extent of criminal prosecution in application for announcement of opening of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, port terminals, wharves.

  3. Additional penalties: Confiscation of certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents not issued by competent authorities for violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Remedial measures: Enforced return of certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents that have been erased or altered to falsify their contents to the competent authorities that have issued such certificates, certifications, licenses, permits, documents for violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on naming, renaming of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, port terminals, wharves, floating docks, water areas, water zones[19]

  1. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 shall be imposed for naming or renaming a port, offshore oil and gas port, port terminal, wharf, floating dock, water area or water zone in a manner inconsistent with the naming principles or failing to notify in writing the naming or renaming of the port, offshore oil and gas port, port terminal, wharf, floating dock, water area or water zone to the maritime regulatory authority in accordance with regulations.

  2. Remedial measures: Enforced change or modification of the name of a port, offshore oil and gas port, port terminal, wharf, floating dock, water area or water zone in a manner consistent with the naming principles in accordance with regulations for the act of naming or renaming a port, offshore oil and gas port, port terminal, wharf, floating dock, water area or water zone in a manner inconsistent with the naming principles in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 9.[20] (annulled)

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on maritime safety, maritime security and order in port operations

  1. A warning shall be imposed for entering or leaving the port land or boarding a vessel without following instructions of a competent authority or person.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for entering or leaving the port land or boarding a vessel without permission of a competent authority or person.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Violation of regulations on warning signs for ships to dock safely in accordance with regulations;

  b) Failure to arrange persons to fasten or loosen ropes for ships and boats as prescribed;

  c) Failure to notify the port authority of the plan for scheduling ships and boats to enter or exit the port as prescribed;

  d) Leaving objects on the quay or occupying space above the quay, thereby obstructing ships and boats from docking or departing from the quay or obstructing other activities at the port.

  4. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Using laborers without work permits or practice certificates as prescribed;

  b) Failure to promptly notify the port authority of incidents and accidents related to maritime safety, maritime security and environmental pollution at the port as prescribed;

  c) Failure to provide the port authority with periodic water depth data of the area in front of the quay as prescribed;

  d) Failure to conduct periodic surveys to publish maritime notices on water depth of the area in front of the quay and other water areas and waters as prescribed;

  d) Light equipment at the wharf is inoperative or operates in contravention of technical regulations.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Permitting a vessel to enter the port or moor in the port waters without permission of the maritime port authority;

  b) Unilaterally loading or unloading cargo when the vessel has not completed the procedures for entering the port as prescribed;

  c) The anti-collision buffer system or the vessel mooring system of the wharf is insufficient or fails to ensure safety for the vessel mooring;

  d) Failing to obtain or complete the annual certification of the port’s compliance as prescribed or failing to fully implement the approved port security plan as prescribed;

  dd) Failing to provide or provide in a timely and accurate manner maritime security information to competent authorities; failing to organize drills or practice exercises for connecting maritime security information as prescribed;

  e) [21] Arranging port security officials in contravention of regulations;

  g) Arranging piers and floating docks for ships to enter and exit without ensuring the time according to the plan of the maritime port authority or without ensuring the conditions as prescribed;

  h) Failing to equip lighting equipment at the pier as prescribed.

  i) [22] Failing to fully store dossiers for the management, operation and maintenance of the maritime works;

  k) [23] Failing to conduct inspection or control of the means, equipment for loading and unloading, vehicles and specialized machinery within the port gate without undergoing inspection as prescribed.

  6. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 60,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Operating the port contrary to its purpose as announced by the competent authority;

  b) Allowing ships to dock at the pier when the pier has not been permitted to be put into operation;

  c) Failing to have a maritime port security plan approved as prescribed;

  d) Failure to conduct quality inspection of port infrastructure structures within the prescribed time limit or after collision or impact affecting the structure of the maritime works;

  dd) Failure to strictly comply with relevant technical regulations in quality inspection of port infrastructure structures.

  7. [25] Additional penalties:

  a) Suspension of reception of international ships in the port for 01 to 03 months for the violation specified at Points d and e Clause 5 and Point c Clause 6 of this Article;

  b) Suspension of reception of ships in the port for 01 to 03 months for the violation specified at Point d Clause 6 of this Article.

  8. [26] Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory arrangement of sufficient port security officers in accordance with regulations for the violation specified at Point e Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory formulation of a port security plan approved in accordance with regulations for the violation specified at Point c Clause 6 of this Article;

  c) Enforced to conduct quality inspection of port infrastructure structures within the prescribed time limit or after collision or impact affecting the structure of the maritime works for the violation specified in Point d Clause 6 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on loading, unloading, warehousing and stacking of goods

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of loading, unloading and warehousing of goods in contravention of regulations.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of stacking goods on the wharf exceeding the permitted load.

  3. Remedial measures: Enforced to remove the part of goods stacked exceeding the permitted load for the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on ensuring maritime safety and environmental protection when constructing new or renovating and upgrading port facilities or when constructing and installing other works and equipment affecting maritime safety[27]

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Failure to have rescue equipment or rescue equipment not conforming to regulations;

  b) Failure to notify the Port Authority of the construction of other works in the port waters;

  c) Setting up insufficient or unsuitable nautical signals according to regulations or nautical signals that are not operational or are located in the wrong area of the construction site;

  d) Work ships and ships serving the construction of works mooring outside the permitted limits, obstructing the navigation of ships in the waterway;

  dd) Failure to record the results of supervision of the dredging and transport of dredged materials or failure to keep the construction log according to regulations; failure to submit periodic reports to the Port Authority and the Vietnam Maritime and Waterway Administration[28] on the situation and results of the dredging of the waterway, water area and water zone in the port waters combined with the collection of products according to regulations;

  e)[29] Carrying out aquaculture in the port waters without permission from the competent authority or in the wrong location or at the wrong time.

  g) Setting up the bottom in the port waters and waterway.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Construction in the wrong location as permitted;

  b) Construction beyond the time limit specified in the construction permit of the competent authority as prescribed;

  c) Using specialized equipment for surveying, dredging, setting up marine signals and carrying out other activities in the port waters without the approval of the port authority as prescribed;

  d) Failing to clean up and remove obstacles arising during construction after the works are completed;

  dd) Failing to fully implement the maritime safety plan approved by the competent authority;

  e) Creating obstacles in the port waters and territorial waters of Vietnam affecting maritime activities;

  g) Installing dredging supervision systems on vessels participating in the transport of dredging materials during dredging activities in an inappropriate manner as prescribed; dredging supervision systems failing to meet minimum technical specifications or being out of operation or operating in an unstable manner as prescribed;

  h) Failing to supervise the construction and transport of dredging materials as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Failure to establish maritime signals as prescribed;

  b) Failure to install the dredging monitoring system as prescribed on the vessel participating in the transport of sludge in the dredging, maintenance of the waterway, water area or water zone;

  c) Failure to implement or improper implementation of the maritime safety plan[31] approved by the competent authority.

  4. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of organizing the construction of a work not falling under the cases specified in Point a, Clause 5 of this Article without the permission or written approval of the competent authority as prescribed.

  5. A fine of between VND 40,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Organizing the construction of a work that requires the preparation of a technical-economic report or construction investment project without the permission or written approval of the competent authority as prescribed;

  b) Constructing a work not in accordance with the approved design;

  c) Failure to have a maritime safety plan[32] as prescribed.

  6. A fine of between VND 50,000,000 and VND 60,000,000 shall be imposed for construction of works not in accordance with the approved planning.

  7. A fine of between VND 80,000,000 and VND 100,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) Construction of works without permission of competent authorities and causing accidents;

  b) Construction of works in wrong positions as permitted and causing accidents;

  c) Failure to have or implement the maritime safety plan approved by competent authorities, leading to accidents.

  8. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory installation of life-saving equipment or installation of appropriate life-saving equipment as prescribed for the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory installation of adequate maritime signals as prescribed or installation of appropriate maritime signals as prescribed for the violation specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) [35] Enforced relocation of bottom trawlers, floating ladders or fishing and aquaculture vessels in the port waters to the wrong positions as prescribed for the violations specified in Point e and Point g Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) [36] Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been changed for the violations specified in Point e Clause 2 of this Article;

  dd) Enforced demolition of illegal construction works or parts thereof for the violations specified in Point a Clauses 2, 4, 5, 6 and Points a and b Clause 7 of this Article;

  e) Enforced remediation of the dredging monitoring system on the dredging equipment used in construction to ensure readiness and continuous operation for the violations specified in Point g Clause 2 of this Article;

  g) Enforced installation of the dredging monitoring system on the dredging equipment used in construction for the violations specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;

  h) Enforced compliance with the maritime safety plan [37] approved by the competent authority for the violations specified in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 7; or the maritime safety plan and the traffic safety plan approved by the competent authority for the violations specified in Point c Clause 3 and Point c Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on fire and explosion prevention and control at seaports

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for each of the following violations:

  a) No regulations board, warning board or warning sign at the place where fire and explosion are likely to occur as prescribed;

  a) Enforced medical examination and treatment for the acts specified in Point a Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return to the ship or quarantine area for the acts specified in Point a Clause 5 of this Article;

  c) Enforced medical quarantine, medical treatment for persons, ships, goods carrying infectious disease agents specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 58m. Violations of regulations when conducting inspection, examination, and sanctioning of administrative violations [103]

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of threatening, insulting, or offending the honor of persons on duty, but not yet to the level of criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to make reports, declarations or making false or late reports and declarations at the request of the official on duty or the competent regulatory authority;

  b) Delaying, evading, failing to provide or providing incomplete or late information and documents related to the inspection, examination and sanctioning of administrative violations by the official on duty or the competent regulatory authority;

  c) Creating difficulties, failing to cooperate or obstructing the work of inspection teams or persons assigned to inspect, examine and perform duties in the maritime sector;

  d) Failing to appoint a competent representative as prescribed by law to participate in the announcement of inspection or examination decisions or failing to appoint a competent representative to work with the inspection team.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of arbitrarily removing seals on documents, exhibits, means, warehouses, equipment that are being sealed; temporarily seizing or dispersing documents, exhibits of violations, or arbitrarily altering the scene of administrative violations in the maritime sector.

  4. A fine of between VND 50,000,000 and VND 80,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Delaying, evading the implementation of inspection or examination decisions in the maritime sector by the competent official or regulatory authority;

  b) Failure to comply with and fully implement the contents and requirements in the conclusion of the inspection or examination in the maritime sector of the competent regulatory authority.

  Article 58n. Inspection and handling of administrative violations on the electronic environment [104]

  1. When the information of the documents includes: the original of the ship registration certificate or the certified copy of the ship registration certificate and the valid certificate of the credit institution (for the case of the ship being mortgaged), the types of certificates and documents of the ship according to law, the professional capability certificate and professional certificate have been integrated and updated in the electronic identity card, electronic identification account on the National Identification Application, in the database managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction, the inspection shall be carried out through the information in the electronic identity card, electronic identification account on the National Identification Application, the database; the inspection of the information of the documents in the electronic identity card, electronic identification account on the National Identification Application, the database is as valid as the inspection of the original of the document.

  2. Temporary retention or deprivation of the right to use the documents

  a) When temporarily retaining or depriving the right to use the documents, the competent person shall carry out the temporary retention or deprivation of the right to use the documents on the electronic environment, update the information on the temporary retention or deprivation of the right to use the documents on the system of the database for handling administrative violations and synchronize with the National Identification Application, the database managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction so that the violators, the vessel owners (for the documents of the vessel owners) can know and comply with the provisions of law on maritime traffic order and safety, serving the competent functional forces to inspect and handle violations;

  b) The documents that have information on temporary retention or deprivation of the right to use on the National Identification Application, the database managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction shall be returned by the competent person to the person who is sanctioned according to regulations; the system of the database for handling administrative violations shall synchronize the information with the National Identification Application, the database managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction to remove the information on the temporary retention or deprivation of the right to use the documents;

  c) The person who is competent to sanction shall search for the electronic receipt of the collected fine for administrative violations sent by the public service portal system for printing, storing the administrative sanction file and serving as the basis for returning the temporarily retained or deprived documents, removing the information on the temporary retention or deprivation of the right to use on the National Identification Application, the database managed by the Ministry of Public Security according to the provisions of Point b of this Clause;

  d) The types of minutes and decisions of the competent person on the temporary retention or deprivation of the right to use, return of the documents shall comply with the provisions of law and be made and sent electronically on the National Identification Application, the application, other electronic information systems when the technical requirements are met.

  3. Competent organizations and individuals shall be responsible for safeguarding personal information and data of violators in accordance with law.

  4. Handling of administrative violations on the Internet for the acts specified in this Decree shall be carried out when the infrastructure, technical and information conditions are ensured in accordance with law on handling of administrative violations.

  Chapter III

    POWER TO MAKE RECORDS OF ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AND POWER TO IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Section 1. POWER TO MAKE RECORDS OF ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 59. Power to make records of administrative violations[105]

  The person competent to impose penalties for administrative violations and public servants, employees, members of the People’s Army and People’s Public Security forces on duty, who are under the management of the person competent to impose penalties for administrative violations specified in Articles 60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 65 and 66 of this Decree, who are on duty and discover administrative violations in the maritime sector, shall promptly prevent such violations and make records of administrative violations in accordance with law. For administrative violations that occur on seagoing ships or inland waterway vessels, the ship captain or the driver of the inland waterway vessel shall organize the making of records and immediately transfer them to the person competent to impose penalties for administrative violations when the seagoing ship or inland waterway vessel[106] arrives at the port.

  Chapter IV

                                        IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS[115]

  Article 67. Guidelines on collection, payment, and management of refundable amounts of organizations and individuals

  The Ministry of Finance shall assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with the Ministry of Transport in providing specific guidance on collection, payment and management of the refundable amount of organizations and individuals involved in administrative violations specified in this Decree.

  Article 68. Transitional provisions

  For administrative violations in the maritime sector that occurred before the effective date of this Decree but were detected and handled after the effective date of this Decree, the provisions on penalties shall apply according to the principle of favoring the violators.

  Article 69. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on February 1, 2018 and replaces Decree No. 93/2013/ND-CP dated August 20, 2013 of the Government on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of maritime and inland waterway transport.

  Article 70. Implementation organization

  1. The Minister of Transport shall organize and guide the implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall implement this Decree.
