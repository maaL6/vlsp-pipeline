                                                    DECREE:

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- Administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention and combat of some social evils.

  Administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention and combat of some social evils prescribed in this Decree include administrative violations in circulation and business of films, video discs, music discs; sale and rental of publications; cultural activities and cultural services in public places; advertising and writing, placing of signs; prevention and combat of prostitution, drug abuse and gambling.

  Article 2.- Struggle against administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention and combat of some social evils.

  All violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention and combat of social evils must be promptly detected and immediately suspended. The sanctioning must be carried out quickly and fairly; all consequences caused by administrative violations must be remedied in accordance with law.

  Article 3.- Persons subject to administrative sanctioning.

  Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention and combat of some social evils shall be sanctioned in the forms and levels prescribed in this Decree.

  The heads of State agencies and organizations, State officials and employees that commit violations prescribed in this Decree shall, in addition to administrative sanctioning, also be disciplined in accordance with law.

  Article 4.- Application of sanctioning forms and measures.

  1. When imposing a fine, the specific fine for a particular administrative violation shall be the average fine of the fine range prescribed for that violation; if the violation has mitigating circumstances, the fine may be reduced but not below the minimum fine of the fine range; if the violation has aggravating circumstances, the fine may be increased but not above the maximum fine of the fine range.

  The mitigating and aggravating circumstances shall be applied in accordance with the provisions of Article 7 and Article 8 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Additional penalties and other measures must be applied together with the main penalty if this Decree provides for additional penalties and application of other measures against administrative violations in order to thoroughly handle violations, eliminate causes and conditions for continued violations and remedy all consequences caused by administrative violations.

  All films, video discs, music discs, books, newspapers, photographs, calendars, and other cultural products containing obscene, pornographic, or violent content shall be confiscated and destroyed.

  Article 5.- Handling of violations by persons competent to sanction administrative violations.

  + If the person has no authority or exceeds his/her authority, depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation, he/she shall be disciplined or criminally prosecuted; if material damage is caused, he/she shall be compensated according to law.

  a/ Reproducing music discs without permission to circulate with 100 copies or more;

  b/ Reproducing music discs without a license for reproduction.

  a/ Reproducing music discs which have been decided to be prohibited from circulation or withdrawn;

  SECTION 2. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN PUBLIC CULTURAL AND CULTURAL SERVICE ACTIVITIES, PENALTIES

  Article 13.- Violations of the norms of civilized life in public cultural and cultural service activities.

  1. A fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Organizing cultural activities and cultural services in public places after 24 hours and before 5 hours, except for the cases specified in Point a, Clause 2, Article 15 of this Decree;

  b/ Organizing performances, music and dance shows, karaoke, film screenings, video tape screenings, which cause excessive noise;

  c/ Drinking alcohol in dance halls, public dance places, karaoke rooms, and other public cultural and cultural service establishments.

  2. A fine of between VND 150,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for the use of suggestive methods in dance halls, karaoke places, and other public cultural and cultural service establishments.

  Article 14.- Violations against regulations on conditions of public cultural and cultural service establishments.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to ensure adequate lighting in dance halls, public dance places, and karaoke rooms.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Failure to ensure sufficient area of dance halls, public dance places, karaoke rooms as prescribed;

  b. Failure to install glass or install glass but the whole karaoke room cannot be clearly seen from outside;

  c. Hanging, displaying paintings, photographs, calendars or other objects of pornographic, obscene, violent nature at dance halls, public dance places, karaoke operation points or other public cultural and service operation places.

  3. Additional penalties and measures for violations in this Article:

  Confiscation and destruction of paintings, photographs, calendars or other objects in case of violation prescribed in Point c Clause 2.

  c. Extinguishing lights at a dance hall, public dance hall, or karaoke bar while it is operating.

  SECTION 3: ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST SOCIAL EVILS, FORMS AND LEVELS OF SANCTION

  Article 18.- Sanctioning of acts of buying and selling sex, harboring and brokering sex.

  1. Persons committing acts of buying and selling sex shall be sanctioned in the form and level prescribed in Article 4 of the Government's Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  2. For persons who regularly commit prostitution acts but are not regularly prosecuted, the President of the People's Committee of the commune where such person resides shall consider and decide the application of the educational measure in the commune, ward or town.

  3. For persons who regularly commit prostitution acts and have been repeatedly educated by the local government and people but refuse to reform, the President of the People's Committee of the commune where such person resides shall prepare a dossier for the President of the People's Committee of the district to request the President of the People's Committee of the province to send such person to a medical institution; if the person is a minor, he/she shall be sent to a reform school.

  4. The procedures for the application of the educational measure in the commune, ward or town, and the sending to a reform school or medical institution shall comply with the law on handling of administrative violations.

  5. A fine of between 500,000 and 1,500,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of prostitution brokerage but not regularly and not yet reaching the level of criminal liability examination.

  6. A fine of between 1,500,000 and 5,000,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of prostitution brokerage with regular nature but not yet reaching the level of criminal liability examination.

  7. A fine of between 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of prostitution trafficking but not yet reaching the level of criminal liability examination.

  Article 19.- Sanctioning of acts of using, producing, storing, buying, selling and transporting drugs.

  1. Persons who use drugs in any form shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 Article 5 of the Government's Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  For persons who use drugs but are not addicted, the President of the People's Committee of the commune where they reside shall consider and decide the application of educational measures in the commune, ward or township.

  2. For drug addicts who have been repeatedly educated by local authorities and people but refuse to reform, the President of the People's Committee of the commune where such person resides shall prepare a dossier for the President of the People's Committee of the district to request the President of the People's Committee of the province to send him/her to a rehabilitation center; if the person is a minor, he/she shall be sent to a reform school.

  3. Procedures for applying educational measures in communes, wards, townships, sending to reform schools, medical facilities shall comply with the law on handling of administrative violations.

  4. The producers, stockpilers, buyers, sellers and transporters of narcotic substances who have not committed any crime shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Clause 3, Article 5 of the Government's Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  5. Additional penalties and other measures for violations in this Article:

  Confiscate the exhibits and means of violation specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 4.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts, which are not subject to criminal prosecution:

  a/ Accepting money, property or loans at a casino or other gambling den;

  b/ Acting as a security guard at a casino or other gambling den or concealing gambling activities;

  c. Making lottery tickets.

  4. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following gambling activities, which are not yet subject to criminal prosecution:

  a. Enticing, soliciting or gathering others to gamble;

  b. Using one's house or other location to store or exchange money for gambling;

  c. Setting up gambling machines, electronic games or organizing any form of betting to gamble;

  5. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following lottery ticket activities, which are not yet subject to criminal prosecution within wards, communes, townships, districts, districts, provincial capitals or cities under provinces:

  6. Persons organizing the making of questions within the scope of inter-district, inter-county, inter-prefecture, inter-provincial or inter-provincial examination, or persons organizing the making of questions in large quantities or having been administratively handled but still committing violations shall be examined for penal liability under Article 200 of the Penal Code.

  b. Confiscation and destruction of questionnaires specified in Point c Clause 3.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for using persons without certificates of professional and technical qualifications in massage and physical therapy to practice massage.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for transferring certificates of professional and technical qualifications in massage to other establishments or using certificates of other establishments for massage services.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for organizing massage services without certificates of professional and technical qualifications in massage.

  5. Additional penalties and other measures for violations in this Article:

  Depriving the right to use certificates of professional and technical qualifications in massage and physical therapy, certificates of professional and technical qualifications in massage for the violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 3.

  Article 22.- Violations against regulations on prevention and combat of social evils in accommodation establishments and restaurants.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to number rooms of accommodation establishments and restaurants in accordance with the registered plans;

  b) Displaying or exhibiting obscene, pornographic or violent pictures and photos at accommodation establishments and restaurants;

  c) Failure to register guests at their establishments;

  d) Failure to declare temporary stay of guests at accommodation establishments;

  dd) Locking or securing doors of dining rooms and drinking rooms of accommodation establishments and restaurants when guests are present;

  e) Using dim lights or insufficient lighting in dining rooms, drinking rooms, recreational rooms of accommodation establishments and restaurants;

  g) Using suggestive serving methods at accommodation establishments and restaurants;

  h. Failure to register the list of guests and staff with competent police authorities.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using guests and staff working at accommodation establishments and restaurants without signing labor contracts.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Circulating music discs that have been decided to be banned from circulation or withdrawn at accommodation establishments and restaurants;

  b. Circulating music discs with obscene, pornographic or violent content at accommodation establishments and restaurants.

  4. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Circulating films, video discs, performances of stage works, music or dance works that have been decided to be banned from circulation or withdrawn at accommodation establishments and restaurants;

  b. Circulating films, video discs, performances of stage works, music or dance works with obscene, pornographic or violent content at accommodation establishments and restaurants.

  5. Additional penalties and other measures for violations prescribed in this Article:

  a/ Confiscation of video tapes, audio tapes, video discs and music discs in case of violation prescribed in Point a Clause 3 or Point a Clause 4;

  b/ Confiscation and destruction of paintings, photographs, video tapes, audio tapes, video discs and music discs in case of violation prescribed in Point b Clause 1, Point b Clause 3 or Point b Clause 4;

  c/ Stripping of the right to use business licenses in case of violation prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4;

  Stripping of the right to use practice licenses for up to 6 months in case of violation prescribed in Clause 4.

  6. In case of violation committed by state-owned accommodation establishments or restaurants, in addition to being fined and having their exhibits of violation confiscated, the persons in charge of such establishments or restaurants shall also be disciplined in accordance with law.

  Article 23.- Facilitation, covering up, solicitation and concealment of social evils.

  1. A fine of from 5,000,000 to 10,000,000 dong shall be imposed on directors of hotels, owners of hotels, guesthouses, restaurants, boarding houses, guesthouses and massage establishments who cover up or are irresponsible for the occurrence of prostitution, drug abuse or gambling in establishments under their management.

  Besides the above-mentioned fine, the violating establishments may be deprived of the right to use their business licenses; if they are State employees, they shall also be disciplined.

  2. The direct supervisors of persons committing acts of prostitution, drug abuse, gambling, who cover up or fail to discipline the violators in a timely manner; persons who are responsible for fighting against social evils, who condone or cover up or fail to discipline in a timely manner the occurrence of these acts in the areas under their management, shall be disciplined according to the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2, Article 8 of the Government's Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  3. The directors of hotels, owners of hotels, guest houses, restaurants, boarding houses, guesthouses, massage establishments or any other person who organizes gambling or gambling dens, harbors, entices or leads prostitutes or organizations using drugs in any form, except for the cases specified in Clauses 4, 5 and 6, Article 18; Clause 4, Article 19; Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5, Article 20 of this Decree, shall be examined for penal liability according to Articles 200, 202 and 203 of the Penal Code; in cases of enticing or harboring minors in prostitution, drug abuse or gambling, they shall be examined for penal liability according to Article 148 of the Penal Code.

  SECTION 4: ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ADVERTISING AND THE FORMS AND LEVELS OF FINES

  Article 24.- Violations of regulations on advertising procedures.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to 20,000 dong shall be imposed for each advertisement made with posters, separate sheets or folded sheets without permits.

  2. A fine of between 100,000 dong and 300,000 dong shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising banners without permits;

  b. To draw and display billboards and signs for clients who have not been granted advertising licenses.

  3. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Failing to write the advertising license number, advertising duration or name of the advertising service provider on the billboard or sign;

  b. Advertising products, goods or introducing the activities of establishments without the certification, registration or approval of the specialized State management agency as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 1,500,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Advertising medicinal materials, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, medical instruments and medical activities that have not been approved by the competent health authority;

  b. Advertising with billboards and signs past the time limit specified in the license;

  c. Advertising on publications or on means of transport without a license.

  5. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of transferring advertising service business licenses or advertising execution licenses to other organizations or individuals or using licenses of other organizations or individuals for advertising service business or advertising execution.

  6. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising service business without a license;

  b/ Advertising cooperation activities with foreign individuals or organizations without a business cooperation contract;

  c/ Advertising without a license at cultural and artistic activity venues;

  d/ Hanging, erecting or placing billboards without a license.

  7. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed on foreign individuals or organizations that conduct advertising service business in Vietnam or execute advertising without signing contracts with Vietnamese advertising service providers.

  8. Additional penalties and other measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a. Deprive of the right to use the license in case of violation of the provisions of Clause 5;

  b. Enforce the dismantling of banners, posters, leaflets, folded papers or billboards and signs in case of violation of the provisions of Clause 1; Point a, Clause 2; Points b and c, Clause 4; Point d, Clause 6; Clause 7.

  Article 25.- Violations of regulations on forms and methods of advertising.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to 50,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of using sounds exceeding the prescribed volume for advertising from 23 hours to 5 hours.

  2. A fine of between 300,000 dong and 1,000,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of advertising on billboards and signs having shapes, colors and forms of presentation similar to traffic signals and public signs.

  3. A fine of between 1,000,000 dong and 2,000,000 dong/1 m2 shall be imposed for each of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising on billboards and signs exceeding the area (m2) prescribed in the license;

  b/ Advertising on radio and television stations exceeding the permitted number of times in a day or exceeding the permitted duration (%)

  c. Advertising on printed newspapers, radio and television stations exceeds the permitted number of days in one period;

  d. Advertising on printed newspapers exceeds the permitted area (%).

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Altering the form of the advertisement board or signboard compared to the sample specified in the permit;

  b. Advertising without clearly stating the name, type of goods, products or activities permitted to be advertised, only stating the generic brand or company name;

  c. Advertising on printed newspapers, radio and television stations violates the minimum distance (5 days) between two advertising periods.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Advertising on the front page of daily, weekly or monthly newspapers or on the cover of a magazine, special issue or supplement;

  b. Trading goods advertised for goods and products prohibited from advertising.

  c. Advertising intermingled with news items, articles or interspersed with news programs, special programs on radio and television, except for live foreign radio and television programs.

  6. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of producing goods advertised for goods and products prohibited from advertising.

  7. A fine of between VND 40,000,000 and 50,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of using the national flag, national anthem, national emblem, portraits of leaders, party flags, and national anthems as backgrounds for advertising presentation.

  8. Additional penalties and other measures for the violations specified in this Article:

  a. Confiscation of goods in case of violation specified in Point b, Clause 5 and Clause 6;

  b. Forcible dismantling of billboards in case of violation specified in Clause 2, Point a, Clause 3, Points a and b, Clause 4 and Clause 7.

  Article 26.- Violations against regulations on advertising locations, positions and scope.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to VND 10,000 shall be imposed for each poster, leaflet or folded paper advertisement placed in a location not specified in the license.

  2. A fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 shall be imposed for a banner advertisement placed in a location not specified in the license.

  3. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Hanging, erecting or placing a sign or board advertisement in a location not specified in the license.

  b) Hanging, erecting or placing a sign or board advertisement within the protection zone of a traffic work, thereby affecting the traffic order and safety.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for an advertisement placed in a prohibited location.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the provision of advertising services beyond the scope of practice or the area of operation specified in the license.

  6. Additional penalties and other measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a. Deprive of the right to use the advertising service business license in case of recidivism under Clause 5;

  b. Enforce the dismantling of banners, posters, leaflets, folded papers or boards and signs in case of violation of the provisions of Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4.

  Article 27.- Violations of the provisions on advertising contents.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for the act of using pictures, photographs, posters, leaflets, folded papers, tarpaulins, carts or boxes for advertising prohibited goods and products.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Offering and introducing prohibited goods and products;

  b/ Advertising prohibited goods and products on signs;

  c/ Advertising contents to the press, publications or artistic works without the permission to publish, distribute, disseminate or perform.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of modifying or falsifying the contents of approved billboards or signs in the permit.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Making false or misleading advertisements about the quality of goods that have been registered;

  b/ Making advertisements that reduce the prestige or quality of goods of other organizations or individuals.

  5. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising goods or products that are prohibited from being advertised on billboards, signs, on means of transport or in public cultural places;

  b/ Advertising goods or products that are prohibited from being advertised on radio, television, publications, video discs or audio discs.

  6. Additional penalties and other measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a. Confiscation of goods and products in case of violation of the provisions in Clause 1, Point a, Clause 2;

  b. Forcible dismantling of billboards in case of violation of the provisions in Point b, Clause 2; Clause 3; Clause 4; Point a, Clause 5;

  c. Forcible compensation for actual damage but not exceeding VND 1,000,000 in case of violation of the provisions in Point b, Clause 4; if the damage exceeds VND 1,000,000, compensation must be settled according to civil procedure procedures.

  Article 28.- Violations of regulations on writing and placing signs.

  1. A fine of from VND 50,000 to VND 150,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Hanging or placing signs not attached to the headquarters of agencies, organizations, hotels, restaurants, shops, and shops with signs;

  b. Failing to fully write or abbreviate the Vietnamese name on the sign.

  2. A fine of from VND 150,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a. Incomplete display of contents as prescribed on the signboard;

  b. Failure to use Vietnamese names and letters on the signboard of Vietnamese agencies, organizations, enterprises and companies, but only use foreign names and letters;

  c. Display of foreign names and letters on the signboard with brighter colors and lights than Vietnamese names and letters;

  d. The signboard of economic organizations with short names, international transaction names, foreign names, which are displayed above Vietnamese names or larger than Vietnamese names;

  dd. The signboard with advertising contents.

  3. Additional penalties and other measures for violations in this Article:

  Forcible dismantling of the signboard in case of violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1; Clause 2.

  Chapter 3:

                       JURISDICTION AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 29.- Jurisdiction to sanction administrative violations of People’s Committees at all levels.

  Chairmen of People’s Committees at all levels have the power to sanction according to the jurisdiction prescribed in Articles 26, 27 and 28 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations within the localities under their management for the violations specified in this Decree.

  Article 30.- Jurisdiction to sanction administrative violations of specialized inspection agencies of Culture and Information.

  Specialized inspection forces of Culture and Information have the power to sanction according to the jurisdiction prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for violations in cultural activities and cultural services specified in this Decree.

  Article 31.- Jurisdiction to sanction administrative violations of police agencies.

  People’s police forces have the power to sanction according to the jurisdiction prescribed in Article 29 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for social evils and violations in cultural activities and cultural services related to social order and safety under their jurisdiction as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 32.- Jurisdiction to sanction administrative violations of market management agencies.

  The market surveillance forces have the power to impose penalties within their competence as prescribed in Article 33 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against violations in the business of film, video discs, music discs, books, newspapers, pictures, calendars and the business of accommodation establishments and restaurants as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 33.- The competence of the Border Guard and Customs in imposing administrative penalties.

  The Border Guard and Customs forces have the power to impose penalties within their competence as prescribed in Article 29 and Article 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against violations in the export and import of film, video discs, music discs, books, newspapers, pictures, calendars as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 34.- The competence of the specialized medical inspection authorities in imposing administrative penalties.

  The specialized medical inspection authorities have the power to impose penalties within their competence as prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against violations in massage services, pharmaceutical, medicinal herb, cosmetic, medical instrument advertising and other medical activities not approved by competent health authorities as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 35.- The competence of the specialized labor inspection authorities in imposing administrative penalties.

  The specialized labor inspection authorities have the power to impose penalties within their competence as prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against violations in the employment of labor without signing labor contracts as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 36.- Collection and payment of fines.

  Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services, and social evils prevention and combat and are fined must pay the fine at the designated place; in case of failure to pay the fine within the prescribed time limit, they shall be enforced to pay the fine.

  It is strictly forbidden for the person imposing the fine to collect the fine on the spot.

  Article 37.- Confiscation and disposal of material evidences and means of administrative violations.

  When applying the confiscation of material evidences and means of administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services, and social evils prevention and combat, the person competent to impose the fine must strictly follow the procedures prescribed in Articles 51 and 52 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the following regulations:

  a/ For material evidences of administrative violations being films, video discs, music discs, publications without content violations and means of violation, the person issuing the confiscation decision must transfer them to the finance agency of the same level for the organization of the price assessment council (in which the representative of the Culture and Information Agency is a mandatory member) and auction.

  b/ For material evidences of administrative violations being unlabeled video discs, or labeled with false labels, films, video discs, music discs, publications not permitted to be circulated, the person issuing the confiscation decision must transfer them to the Culture and Information Agency of the same level for assessment; in case films, video discs, music discs, publications after assessment conclude that they do not have content violations and can be circulated, they shall be handed over to the finance agency of the same level for the organization of the price assessment council and auction according to the provisions of Point a of this Article; in case films, video discs, music discs, publications after assessment conclude that they have obscene, pornographic content, incite violence, the Culture and Information Agency shall carry out the disposal according to the provisions of Article 38 of this Decree.

  c/ For films, video discs, music discs, publications having decisions on circulation ban, recall, the person issuing the confiscation decision must transfer them to the Culture and Information Agency for handling according to competence.

  d/ All cases of transfer and disposal of material evidences and means of administrative violations prescribed in Points a, b, and c of this Article must be made into a record with the written confirmation of the concerned parties.

  Article 38.- Destruction of material evidences of administrative violations.

  1. In case the material evidences are films, video discs, music discs or publications clearly containing obscene, pornographic or violent contents, the person who issues the sanctioning decision shall decide on the destruction according to his/her competence or transfer them to the competent authority for destruction.

  2. In case the material evidences are films, video discs, music discs or publications containing signs of obscene, pornographic or violent contents, the competent sanctioning authority shall transfer them to the competent Culture and Information authority for assessment; if after assessment the material evidences are concluded to contain obscene, pornographic or violent contents, the Culture and Information authority shall organize the destruction.

  In case the material evidences are films, video discs, music discs or publications which, after assessment, are concluded to contain no obscene, pornographic or violent contents, they shall be transferred to the finance authority of the same level for organization of the price assessment and auction council according to the provisions of Article 37 of this Decree.

  3. When destroying films, video discs, music discs or publications containing obscene, pornographic or violent contents, the authority applying this measure shall establish a handling council composed of representatives of the Culture and Information authority, the police authority, the finance authority and the People's Procuracy of the same level; in case of necessity, representatives of concerned authorities and organizations may be invited to witness the destruction.

  The destruction must be made in writing with the certification of the members of the handling council.

  4. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall provide specific guidance on the procedures for destruction prescribed in this Article.

  Article 39.- Complaints and denunciations.

  1. Citizens have the right to denounce to competent State agencies the administrative violations of organizations and individuals in cultural activities and cultural services and social evils prevention and combat as prescribed in this Decree or to denounce violations of persons competent to impose administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree.

  2. Organizations and individuals that are administratively sanctioned or their legal representatives have the right to lodge complaints with the persons who have issued the sanctioning decisions within 10 days from the date of receipt of the decisions.

  3. The order and procedures for lodging complaints and denunciations and the competence to settle complaints and denunciations of administrative violations in cultural activities and cultural services and social evils prevention and combat shall be subject to the provisions of Articles 87, 88 and 90 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 40.- This Decree takes effect from February 1, 1996.

  The previous provisions contrary to this Decree are all annulled.

  Article 41.- The Minister of Culture and Information and the Minister of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall, within the scope of their functions and tasks, be responsible for guiding the organization of the implementation of this Decree.

  Article 42.- The ministers of the ministries, the heads of the ministerial agencies, the agencies attached to the Government, and the presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall be responsible for the performance of this task.
