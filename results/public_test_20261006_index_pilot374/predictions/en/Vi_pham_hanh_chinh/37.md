     AMENDMENTS AND SUPPLEMENTS TO THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 106/2025/ND-CP DATED MAY 15, 2025, ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE LAWS ON FIRE PREVENTION, FIRE EXTINGUISHING AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE AND RESCUE

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Government No. 63/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13, amended by the Law No. 54/2014/QH13, the Law No. 18/2017/QH14, the Law No. 67/2020/QH14, the Law No. 09/2022/QH15, the Law No. 11/2022/QH15, the Law No. 56/2024/QH15, and the Law No. 88/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting and Rescue No. 55/2024/QH15;

  At the request of the Minister of Public Security;

  The Government promulgates the Decree amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 106/2025/ND-CP dated May 15, 2025, on penalties for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting and rescue.

  Article 1. Amending and supplementing Point i Clause 2 Article 2 as follows:

  “i) The organization assigned to directly manage the establishment and organize activities within the scope of one establishment in accordance with the law on fire prevention and firefighting and rescue;”.

  1. To add Clause 6

  6. Organizations and individuals that repeatedly commit administrative violations against the same violation in a fire prevention and firefighting management facility shall not be sanctioned for each administrative violation but shall be sanctioned for one administrative violation with aggravating circumstances.

  2. To add Clause 7:

“7. The State shall ensure the implementation of the Law on Social Security in the field of social security for persons with disabilities, in accordance with the Law on Social Security in the field of social security for persons with disabilities.”

  “7.   3. To add Clause 8:

“8. The State shall ensure the implementation of the Law on Social Security in the field of social security for persons with disabilities, in accordance with the Law on Social Security in the field of social security for persons with disabilities.”

  Article 3. Amendments to Clause 4 Article 5:

  4. The time of termination of the violation shall be determined as follows:

  a) The time of termination of the violation for the prescription of sanctioning administrative violations for the violation specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 Article 6 of this Decree shall be counted from the date on which the competent agency or unit issues a written notice of the results of fire prevention and firefighting, rescue and relief training and retraining as prescribed;

  b) The time of termination of the violation for the prescription of sanctioning administrative violations for the violation specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 Article 18 of this Decree shall be counted from the date on which the specialized management agency issues a written document approving the fire prevention and firefighting design as prescribed;

  c) The time of termination of the violation for the prescription of sanctioning administrative violations for the violation specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 Article 18 of this Decree shall be counted from the date on which the specialized management agency issues a written document approving the fire prevention and firefighting acceptance test results as prescribed or the date on which the operation is terminated;

  d) The time of termination of the violation for the prescription of sanctioning administrative violations for the violation specified in Point a and Point b Clause 9 Article 20 of this Decree for the construction works not subject to fire prevention and firefighting design approval shall be counted from the date on which the investor signs the written record of acceptance of the completed construction item or construction work for use as prescribed.

  Article 23. Implementation clauses

  1. This Decree takes effect from April 20, 2026.

  2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of Government-attached agencies, presidents of provincial-level People’s Committees, and relevant agencies, organizations, and individuals shall implement this Decree.

  Article 24. Transitional clauses

  1. For administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting and rescue that occurred and ended before the effective date of this Decree but were detected before the statute of limitations for sanctioning expired or are under consideration and settlement when this Decree takes effect, the Government’s Decree on sanctioning of administrative violations in force at the time of commission of the violation shall apply.

  For administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting and rescue that occurred before the effective date of this Decree but are still ongoing when this Decree takes effect, this Decree shall apply.

  2. For administrative sanctioning decisions that were issued or fully implemented before the effective date of this Decree but are still filed by the sanctioned organizations or individuals, the regulations on administrative sanctioning at the time of issuance of the sanctioning decision shall apply.
