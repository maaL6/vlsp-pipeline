                                                    DECREE

    REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE SOCIAL PROTECTION AND CHILDREN’S RIGHTS AND WELFARE

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Government No. 63/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13, amended by the Law No. 54/2014/QH13, the Law No. 67/2020/QH14, the Law No. 56/2024/QH15 and the Law No. 88/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Children No. 102/2016/QH13, amended by the Law No. 28/2018/QH14, the Law No. 59/2024/QH15 and the Law No. 81/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on the Elderly No. 39/2009/QH12, amended by the Law No. 41/2024/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Persons with Disabilities No. 51/2010/QH12, amended by the Law No. 32/2013/QH13;

  Pursuant to the Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking No. 53/2024/QH15;

  At the request of the Minister of Health;

  The Government promulgates the Decree on administrative penalties for violations in the fields of social protection, assistance and children.

  Chapter I

                                                GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations; forms of sanction, sanction levels, and remedial measures for each administrative violation; sanctioned entities; sanctioning competence, specific fine levels according to titles, and competence to make records of administrative violations; the implementation of forms of sanctioning administrative violations and remedial measures in the fields of social protection, social assistance, and children.

  2. Administrative violations in the fields of social protection, social assistance, and children not specified in this Decree shall be subject to the provisions on sanctioning administrative violations in relevant state management fields.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations; foreign individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the fields of social protection, social assistance, and children within the territorial waters, territorial sea, adjacent sea, exclusive economic zone, and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam; on aircraft bearing the Vietnamese nationality, ships bearing the Vietnamese flag, except for cases where international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory provide otherwise, including:

  a) Individuals that commit administrative violations;

  b) Organizations that commit administrative violations as legal entities according to civil law provisions or other organizations established according to law, including: social assistance facilities, elderly care facilities, disability care facilities, victim support facilities, child protection service providers; religious facilities; policy payment service providers; socio-political-occupational organizations, social organizations, socio-occupational organizations; enterprises, cooperatives, production and business establishments; cultural, recreational, entertainment, and sports facilities; medical examination and treatment facilities; educational facilities; vocational education facilities, and other organizations.

  2. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations.

  3. Persons competent to impose administrative penalties.

  4. Agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 3. Statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties

  1. The statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties in the field of social protection, assistance and children is 01 year.

  2. The determination of completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations for calculating the statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties shall comply with Clause 1 Article 8 of the Government’s Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP dated December 23, 2021, detailing a number of articles and measures for the implementation of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations, amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  Article 4. Implementation of forms of administrative penalties

  1. The implementation of decisions on imposing administrative penalties in the field of social protection, assistance and children shall comply with the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13, amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 56/2024/QH15 and Law No. 88/2025/QH15 (hereinafter referred to as the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations) and guiding documents.

  2. The imposition of administrative penalties in the field of social protection, assistance and children on the internet shall comply with Chapter IIIa of the Government’s Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP, amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  3. During the consideration and handling of violations, during the implementation of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations for violations specified in Clause 2 Article 8, Article 9, Article 11, Clause 1 Article 12, Point a Clause 2 Article 19, Clause 2 Article 20, Clause 2 Article 22, Clause 1 Article 23, Clause 2 Article 24, Clause 1 Article 25, Clause 1 Article 29, Clause 1 Article 33, Clause 1 Article 36, Point a, Point b Clause 1 Article 37 of this Decree, if the violation is found to show signs of a crime, the competent person handling the case or the person issuing the decision on sanctioning administrative violations shall immediately transfer the violation dossier to a competent agency for criminal proceedings to handle according to Article 62 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations. If the competent agency for criminal proceedings does not investigate criminal liability, the violation dossier shall be transferred to the person competent to sanction administrative violations according to Clause 3 Article 62 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations to sanction administrative violations according to this Decree.

  Article 5. Sanctioning forms, remedial measures; implementation of sanctioning forms of administrative violations, remedial measures

  1. Main sanctioning forms:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fines.

  2. Additional sanctioning forms:

  a) Confiscation of material evidences of administrative violations;

  b) Suspension of part or all of business activities related to the violation for a period of 01 to 03 months;

  c) Suspension of operation for a fixed period of 03 to 06 months.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Remedial measures specified in Points a, dd, e and i Clause 1 Article 28 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  b) Compulsory cessation of care and/or nurturing;

  c) Compulsory withdrawal, deletion and removal of information, images and dossiers of human trafficking victims and persons identified as victims;

  d) Compulsory apology upon request;

  dd) Compulsory repayment of illegally collected money;

  e) Compulsory payment of all medical examination and treatment costs;

  g) Enforced repair, supplementation, and provision of material facilities and personnel in accordance with regulations;

  h) Enforced payment of benefits and policies in accordance with regulations;

  i) Enforced repayment of the amount of relief money or goods due to commission of violations;

  k) Enforced return of disability certificates;

  l) Enforced full performance of responsibilities of councils or full performance of methods for determining disability levels and re-determining disability levels;

  m) Enforced determination and re-determination of disability levels;

  n) Enforced payment of all costs for purchase of books, notebooks, and learning materials for children;

  o) Enforced supplementation of programs, textbooks, and teacher teams suitable for vocational education for disabled persons;

  p) Enforced issuance of diplomas and certificates;

  q) Enforced withdrawal and destruction of products, publications, toys, printed newspapers and publications;

  r) Enforced removal of news articles on electronic newspapers, electronic magazines, printed newspapers, printed books and electronic publications;

  s) Enforced termination of provision of substitute care and provision of appropriate substitute care for children;

  t) Enforced relocation of service establishments, production establishments, warehouses of hazardous goods, establishments at risk of direct fire or explosion, establishments providing child protection services, educational, medical, cultural establishments, children’s recreational and entertainment spots;

  u) Enforced dismantling of works and equipment;

  v) Enforced timely implementation of safety measures;

  x) Enforced removal of information and services harmful to children; removal of fake services and applications that distort and infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of children on the internet;

  y) Enforced collection, deletion, and removal of information on children’s private lives and personal secrets.

  4. The implementation of the forms of sanctioning and remedial measures specified in this Decree shall comply with the provisions of Section 2 and Section 3 Chapter III of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations, Articles 17a, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, and Article 41 of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP, amended and supplemented by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  Article 6. Monetary fines and authority to impose monetary fines

  1. The maximum monetary fine in the field of social protection, assistance, and children shall be 50,000,000 VND for individuals and 100,000,000 VND for organizations.

  2. The monetary fines specified in Chapter II of this Decree are the fines applicable to administrative violations of individuals, except for Articles 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 34, Clause 1, Clause 2 Article 35, and Clause 2 Article 37 of this Decree. For the same administrative violation, the monetary fine for organizations shall be twice as much as the monetary fine for individuals.

  3. The authority to sanction administrative violations of a title specified in Articles 39, 40, 41, 42, 43, 44, and 45 of this Decree is the authority to impose a monetary fine for an administrative violation of an individual; in case of imposing a monetary fine, the authority to impose a monetary fine for an organization shall be twice as much as the authority to impose a monetary fine for an individual with the same violation.

  Chapter II

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, SANCTIONING FORMS, SANCTIONING LEVELS, AND REMEDIAL MEASURES

  Section 1. VIOLATIONS, SANCTIONING FORMS, SANCTIONING LEVELS AND MEASURES TO PREVENT ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE SOCIAL PROTECTION AND ASSISTANCE DOMAIN

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on receipt of social assistance, social pension benefits, and support for human trafficking victims

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed on an individual who commits acts of fraud or deception in order to receive emergency social assistance but the amount is not sufficient to be subject to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed on an individual who commits acts of fraud or deception in order to receive one of the following support policies: cultural education, vocational training, or initial hardship allowance but the amount is not sufficient to be subject to criminal prosecution.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts:

  a) Falsely declaring in order to receive monthly social allowance but the amount is not sufficient to be subject to criminal prosecution;

  b) Falsely declaring in order to receive monthly social pension benefits but the amount is not sufficient to be subject to criminal prosecution;

  c) Falsely declaring in order to receive monthly support for care and maintenance but the amount is not sufficient to be subject to criminal prosecution;

  d) Making false declarations in order to be admitted to social assistance, child protection, elderly care, disability care, or victim support facilities.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of illegal profits earned from commission of violations prescribed in Clause 1, Clause 2, Points a, b, and c, Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced cessation of social assistance, child protection, elderly care, disability care, or victim support services for commission of violations prescribed in Point d, Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on assistance to human trafficking victims or victims in the process of identification

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following acts:

  a) Obstructing the reception of human trafficking victims or victims in the process of identification;

  b) Obstructing the provision of assistance to human trafficking victims or victims in the process of identification;

  c) Insult, denigrate, or discriminate against victims of human trafficking, persons in the process of being identified as victims.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for threatening victims of human trafficking, persons in the process of being identified as victims, their relatives, witnesses, whistleblowers, informants, denouncers, declarants, or persons who obstruct the acts specified in Article 3 of the Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking but are not subject to criminal prosecution.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for disclosing information on the name, age, phone number, social media account, address, place of residence, workplace, hometown, form of trafficking, health, image, and file of victims of human trafficking, persons in the process of being identified as victims without their consent but are not subject to criminal prosecution, except for cases where disclosure of information is necessary to protect the life or health of the disclosed person or otherwise provided for by law.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced withdrawal, deletion, or removal of information, images, and files of victims of human trafficking, persons in the process of being identified as victims for violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced apology to victims of human trafficking, persons in the process of being identified as victims of human trafficking, their relatives, witnesses, whistleblowers, informants, denouncers, declarants, or persons who obstruct the acts specified in Clauses 1, 2, and 3 of this Article upon request.

  Article 9. Violations of regulations on care and upbringing responsibilities of persons who take charge of caring for and raising social protection beneficiaries, the elderly, persons with disabilities, and children in the community

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for a person who takes charge of caring for and raising a social protection beneficiary, the elderly, a person with disabilities, or a child in the community but is not subject to criminal prosecution for committing one of the following acts:

  a) Enforced fasting; forced drinking; denial or restriction of personal hygiene;

  b) Forced to live in a toxic or dangerous environment;

  c) Insult, abuse, offend, torture, discriminate against the social protection subjects, the elderly, the disabled and the children.

  2. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed on the person in charge of receiving and caring for the subjects who commits one of the following acts but not yet to the level of criminal liability examination:

  a) Taking advantage of the receiving and caring for to make profit;

  b) Forcing the subjects to be cared for to work in the heavy, toxic or dangerous jobs.

  3. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed on the person in charge of receiving and caring for the subjects who commits one of the following acts but not yet to the level of criminal liability examination:

  a) Enticing or persuading the subjects to be cared for to commit acts of law violations;

  b) Forcing the recipients of care or nursing to commit acts of law violations.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Forcing the recipients of care or nursing to return the illegal profits earned from the commission of the acts of law violations specified in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article;

  b) Forcing the recipients of care or nursing to pay all medical examination and treatment costs incurred due to the commission of the acts of law violations specified in Clause 1, Point b Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on the operation of social assistance establishments, elderly care establishments, disability care establishments, victim support establishments and child protection service providers (hereinafter referred to as “Establishments”)

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed on an Establishment that fails to keep sufficient dossiers and papers of the recipients of care or nursing as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed on an Establishment that commits one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to report on the operation of the Establishment in a timely manner as prescribed by law;

  b) Failure to provide or provide inadequate quality of one of the items used for daily personal needs, including: blankets, curtains, mats, summer clothes, winter clothes, underwear, face towels, shoes, slippers, toothbrushes, and common medicines; monthly personal hygiene items for women, books and study materials for students, and other items as prescribed by law.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed on establishments that commit one of the following acts:

  a) Using funds and facilities of the establishment for improper purposes;

  b) Charging for services contrary to law;

  c) Failing to ensure one of the conditions regarding environment, health, facilities, and human resources;

  d) Failing to comply with the service provision process issued by competent regulatory agencies.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of illegal profits earned from the commission of the violation specified in Point a, Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced repayment of illegally collected amounts to the violators for the violations specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;

  c) Enforced repair, supplementation, and provision of material and human resources in accordance with regulations for the violations specified in Point c Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on the responsibility of payment service providers

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Monthly payment of social subsidies, monthly support for care and maintenance, monthly social pension, and burial expenses (collectively referred to as the policy regime) which are insufficient for the beneficiaries according to law regulations but not yet reaching the level of criminal liability investigation;

  b) Payment of the policy regime not on time to the beneficiaries according to law regulations but not yet reaching the level of criminal liability investigation.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to pay the policy regime to the beneficiaries but not yet reaching the level of criminal liability investigation;

  b) Paying out benefits and policies to the wrong beneficiaries but not to the extent of criminal liability.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forcible payment of benefits and policies in accordance with regulations to the wrong beneficiaries for the violations specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on management of relief money and goods

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts but not to the extent of criminal liability:

  a) Causing damage or loss of relief money and goods, except for force majeure events such as natural disasters and fires;

  b) Using or distributing relief money and goods for improper purposes or to the wrong beneficiaries;

  c) Swapping relief goods.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced repayment of the amount or compensation for the value of the relief goods damaged or lost due to the commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of the illegal profits obtained from the commission of the violation specified in Point b and Point c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for the users of the relief goods whose health is affected due to the commission of the violation specified in Point a and Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on prohibited acts against persons with disabilities

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Discriminating against or treating persons with disabilities differently;

  b) Obstructing the right to marry or the right to legally raise children of persons with disabilities;

  c) Prevent disabled persons from living independently and integrating into the community;

  d) Prevent disabled persons from participating equally in social activities;

  dd) Prevent disabled persons from exercising their right to access information technology.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to fulfill the responsibility to raise and care for disabled persons as prescribed by law.

  3. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of the image, personal information, and condition of disabled persons, organizations of disabled persons, or organizations for disabled persons for personal gain or committing acts of law violations but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  4. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for enticing, persuading, or forcing disabled persons to commit acts of law violations but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of illegal profits obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced apology when requested for the violation specified in Point a, Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced to provide care for disabled persons in accordance with law.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on health care responsibilities for disabled persons of medical examination and treatment establishments

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to provide advice on measures to prevent and detect disability early.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to identify congenital disabilities of newborns in order to take timely measures for appropriate treatment, rehabilitation and functional recovery.

  3. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to renovate or upgrade facilities for medical examination and treatment to ensure accessibility for disabled persons when renovating or repairing medical examination and treatment establishments.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on education responsibilities for disabled persons of educational establishments

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to ensure the conditions for teaching and learning suitable for persons with disabilities as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to provide learning aids and materials specifically for persons with disabilities as prescribed by law;

  c) Failing to allow persons with disabilities to be exempted from or have their learning content and educational activities reduced to the extent that their abilities cannot meet the requirements;

  d) Refusing to admit persons with disabilities to school at an older age as prescribed by law;

  dd) Setting admission conditions that restrict persons with disabilities, except for certain cases as prescribed by law;

  e) Failing to prioritize the admission of persons with disabilities as prescribed by law;

  g) Obstructing the learning rights of persons with disabilities.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to exempt or reduce tuition fees, training costs, and other contributions as prescribed by the law on education;

  b) Failure to renovate or upgrade teaching and learning facilities to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities when renovating or repairing educational institutions.

  Article 16. Violations against regulations on vocational education activities of vocational education institutions for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to provide job counseling for persons with disabilities as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to maintain conditions for vocational education activities as prescribed for persons with disabilities after conducting training activities for 06 months or more;

  b) Failure to provide programs, curricula, or teacher teams suitable for vocational education activities for persons with disabilities;

  c) Failure to ensure training forms and duration suitable for vocational education activities for persons with disabilities;

  d) Not to issue diplomas or certificates when disabled persons are eligible for such diplomas or certificates as prescribed by law.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced to supplement programs, curricula and teachers suitable for vocational education for disabled persons for the violation specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced to issue diplomas or certificates for the violation specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 17. Violations against regulations on participation in traffic for disabled persons and elderly persons

  A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  1. Failing to prioritize selling tickets to disabled persons and elderly persons.

  2. Failing to assist and arrange convenient seats for disabled persons and elderly persons as prescribed by law.

  3. Refusing to transport disabled persons or refusing to transport appropriate support equipment and devices of disabled persons using public transport.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on information and communication for disabled persons

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed on enterprises producing and distributing information and communication products and services, including hardware, software and digital information content, that violate one of the mandatory regulations on applying standards and technologies to support disabled persons in accessing and using information and communication products and services.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed on acts of applying policies on preferential lending at preferential interest rates and other support for research, manufacturing, production and provision of services and means to support disabled persons in accessing information and communication technologies inappropriately.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of illegal profits earned from committing the violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on determination of disability severity

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to fully fulfill the responsibilities of the disability assessment council;

  b) Failing to fully apply the methods for determining disability levels and re-determining disability levels.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Taking advantage of the disability assessment to seek personal benefits but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  b) Refusing to determine disability levels, re-determine disability levels, issue disability certificates, or re-issue disability certificates;

  c) Deceiving in the determination of disability levels, re-determination of disability levels, issuance of disability certificates, or re-issuance of disability certificates.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced full fulfillment of the council's responsibilities for the violation specified in Point a, Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced to fully apply the method of determining the degree of disability, re-determining the degree of disability for the violation specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced to return the illegal profits obtained from the violation specified in Point a and Point c Clause 2 of this Article;

  d) Enforced to determine, re-determine the degree of disability for the violation specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article;

  dd) Enforced to return the disability certificate for the violation specified in Point c Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on rights and obligations of the elderly

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Obstructing the elderly from living together with their children or grandchildren or living separately;

  b) Obstructing the elderly from exercising their rights to property ownership, rights to participate in cultural, educational, physical, sports, recreational, tourism, rest activities and other lawful rights;

  c) Not exempting contributions to social activities for the elderly;

  d) Not prioritizing the elderly in receiving money, in-kind aid, health care and housing to overcome initial difficulties when the elderly face difficulties due to natural disasters or other force majeure risks;

  dd) Not paying the state's support for the elderly as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts: forcing the elderly to fast; forcing the elderly to drink; not allowing or restricting personal hygiene; forcing the elderly to live in a toxic or dangerous environment or treating the elderly in other bad ways but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Forced apology when requested for the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Forced repayment of illegally collected amounts to the elderly for the violation specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Forced payment of the prescribed benefits and policies for the violation specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for the elderly affected by health due to the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on the obligation to care for and provide for the elderly

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to perform the obligation to care for and provide for the elderly as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to fully perform the commitment under the signed service contract to care for the elderly as prescribed by law;

  c) Taking advantage of the care for and provision for the elderly to seek personal benefits but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of illegal profits obtained from the commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS, SANCTIONS, SANCTION LEVELS AND OTHER MEASURES FOR RESTORING THE EFFECT OF ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST CHILDREN

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on child care and upbringing

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Parents or child caregivers fail to fully perform their duties and responsibilities in caring for and raising children, except for cases where children are temporarily separated or replaced as per law;

  b) Parents or child caregivers neglect to care for and raise children, fail to perform their duties and responsibilities in caring for and raising children, or abandon children to live on their own, sever emotional and material ties with children, or force children to live separately from their families, except for cases where children are temporarily separated or replaced as per law.

  2. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 25,000,000 shall be imposed on parents or child caregivers who intentionally abandon children but the acts do not reach the level of criminal liability.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on prohibition of violence against children

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations but the acts do not reach the level of criminal liability:

  a) Enforced fasting; forced to fast; deprivation of personal hygiene;

  b) Forced to live in a toxic or dangerous environment or other forms of treatment worse than that of children;

  c) Causing mental damage, insulting dignity and honor, insulting, cursing, threatening, isolating children, affecting their development;

  d) Isolation, expulsion or use of punitive measures to teach children, causing physical and mental damage to children;

  dd) Frequently threatening children with images, sounds, animals, objects that cause fear and mental damage to children.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article, causing injuries or health damage to children but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  b) Enforced destruction of items harmful to children's health for violations specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 24. Violations against regulations on prohibition of abuse and exploitation of children

  1. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts: forcing children to do domestic work beyond their capacity or beyond the time limit, affecting their study, play, entertainment, and adversely affecting their development.

  2. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts, which are not subject to criminal prosecution:

  a) Organizing or forcing children to beg for food;

  b) Renting or lending children or using children to beg for food;

  c) Leading, soliciting, inciting, enticing, persuading, exploiting, or forcing children to act as intermediaries in child exploitation activities;

  d) Leading, soliciting, inciting, enticing, persuading, exploiting, or forcing children to work against the law.

  3. A fine of between VND 40,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of exploiting children’s images or personal information to create content that harms their physical or mental development or for personal gain, which are not subject to criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of illegal profits earned from commission of violations prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of commission of violations prescribed in Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article, causing injuries or health damage to children but not reaching the level of criminal liability examination.

  Article 25. Violations against regulations on prohibition of use, solicitation, incitement, provocation, exploitation, enticement, coercion of children to commit acts of law violations, insult to honor and dignity of others

  1. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts but not reaching the level of criminal liability examination:

  a) Using children to commit acts of law violations, insulting to honor and dignity of others;

  b) Soliciting children to commit acts of law violations, insulting to honor and dignity of others;

  c) Inciting, provoking children to commit acts of law violations, insulting to honor and dignity of others;

  d) Exploiting, enticing or tricking children into committing acts of law violations, insulting the dignity and honor of others;

  dd) Forcing children to commit acts of law violations, insulting the dignity and honor of others.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Forcible payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children for the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article, which cause injuries or health damage to children but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  Article 26. Violations against regulations on children's right to health care

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to fulfill the responsibility to ensure a diet suitable for the physical and mental development of children according to their age;

  b) Failing to fulfill or inadequately fulfilling the responsibility to provide initial health care, disease prevention, counseling and support for children in reproductive health care and sexual health care.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to provide or obstructing pregnant women from accessing medical services for prenatal screening and prevention of congenital diseases;

  b) Failing to comply with decisions, measures and regulations of competent agencies and individuals to ensure the safety of children's lives and health;

  c) Practicing harmful customs and habits that affect children's health;

  d) Failing to prioritize medical examination and treatment for children.

  Article 27. Violations against regulations on children's right to education

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Destroying children's books, notebooks and learning materials;

  b) Intentionally failing to fulfill the obligation to contribute to the education of children as prescribed by law;

  c) Failing to ensure the time and conditions for the education of children.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for obstructing the education of children.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for enticing, persuading or forcing children to drop out of school.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcible payment of all costs for the purchase of books, notebooks and learning materials for children for the violation specified in Point a, Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 28. Violations against regulations on the right of children to access information and participate in social activities; the right to express opinions; the right to preserve and promote their identity; the right to play and entertain themselves and the prohibition of stigmatizing and discriminating against children

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Preventing children from participating in social activities appropriate to their age, maturity, needs and capacity, except for cases of the best interests of the children;

  b) Preventing children from expressing their opinions, wishes and participating in children-related issues;

  c) Preventing the preservation and promotion of identity;

  d) Preventing the enjoyment of leisure, participation in cultural, artistic, physical and sports activities and tourism activities in accordance with law.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Forcing or obstructing children from expressing their opinions, wishes and participating in children-related issues;

  b) Disrespecting, failing to listen to, consider, respond to or explain children’s opinions and wishes;

  c) Failing to receive, resolve or respond to children’s opinions, proposals and wishes, or failing to respond to children’s voice and wish representatives in accordance with regulations;

  d) Failure to disclose or disclose inaccurate information on learning and training plans, feeding regimes and contributions as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of denigrating or discriminating against children because of their personal characteristics, family circumstances, gender, ethnicity, nationality, beliefs or religion.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Forced apology upon request for violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Forced correction of false or misleading information for violations specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 29. Violations against regulations on support and intervention for children in special circumstances, abused children or children at risk of exploitation, violence, abandonment and other abuse

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts without criminal prosecution:

  a) Failure to notify, provide or conceal information on children at risk of exploitation, violence and other abuse to competent agencies and individuals;

  b) Preventing the provision of information on children at risk of exploitation, violence and other abuse to competent agencies and individuals.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to provide or conceal information on abused children to competent agencies and individuals without being subject to criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to notify the place receiving information on child abuse;

  c) Preventing the provision of information on abused children to competent agencies and individuals;

  d) Failing to provide information and cooperate in verifying the authenticity of abuse acts, unsafe conditions and the level of risk of harm to children when requested by competent agencies and individuals;

  dd) Failing to keep information confidential, notify or report acts of child abuse.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to provide adequate and timely support and intervention for children who are abused or at risk of violence, exploitation, abandonment, or children in special circumstances;

  b) Refusal to provide support, intervention, and substitute care for children who are abused or at risk of violence, exploitation, abandonment, or children in special circumstances.

  Article 30. Violations against regulations on prohibition of selling addictive substances, other stimulants, or unsafe food to children or allowing children to use them; violations against regulations on provision of child-safe and child-friendly products and services without criminal prosecution

  1. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Providing unsafe products or services that harm children;

  b) Selling addictive substances, other stimulants, or unsafe food that is harmful to children;

  c) Allowing children to use addictive substances, other stimulants, or unsafe food that is harmful to children.

  2. Additional penalties:

  a) Confiscation of material evidences of administrative violations for violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Suspension of one or all business activities related to violations for a period of 01 to 03 months for violations prescribed in Point b Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory destruction of goods and items for violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children for violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article that cause injuries or health damage to children but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  c) Compulsory return of the amount equal to the value of material evidences of administrative violations that have been sold, dispersed or destroyed in contravention of law for violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 31. Violations against regulations on provision of cultural, information and communication products and services with content affecting healthy development of children

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Providing Internet services and other services with content that affects the healthy development of children;

  b) Failing to note that children are not allowed to use publications, toys if the publications, toys contain content inappropriate for children; failing to warn about the presence of content inappropriate for children in digital content products on the Internet space;

  c) Failing to inform the age of children who are not allowed to watch, listen to radio, television, art, film and performance programs if the programs contain content inappropriate for children;

  d) Failing to comply with requirements for information on publications for children.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for one of the following acts of producing, publishing, copying, distributing, operating, disseminating, possessing, transporting, storing, trading in publications, toys, games, digital content on the Internet space and other products serving children but containing content that affects the healthy development of children, at one of the following levels:

  a) From VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 for a violation involving 01 product to less than 10 products;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 for a violation involving 10 products to less than 50 products;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 for a violation involving 50 products to less than 100 products;

  d) From VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 for violations involving 100 to less than 200 products;

  dd) From VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 for violations involving 200 to less than 500 products;

  e) From VND 30,000,000 to VND 40,000,000 for violations involving 500 to less than 1,000 products;

  g) From VND 40,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 for violations involving 1,000 or more products.

  3. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with or improper compliance with regulations on warnings of inappropriate content for children on radio, television, press and publication programs.

  4. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of material evidences of administrative violations for violations specified in Points b, d, Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced removal of news articles on electronic newspapers, electronic magazines, newspapers, printed newspapers, electronic publications for the violations specified in Point b, Point d Clause 1 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced withdrawal and destruction of products, publications, toys, printed newspapers and publications for the violations specified in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Enforced apology upon request for the violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  d) Enforced return of the amount equal to the value of the exhibits of administrative violations that have been sold, dispersed, destroyed in contravention of law for the violations specified in Point b, Point d Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 32. Violations against regulations on prohibition of disclosure of information on private life, personal secrets of children

  1. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for the disclosure of one of the following information without the consent of the parents, caregivers, guardians of the child and the child from 07 years of age or older, including: name, age; personal identification characteristics; information on health status and private life recorded in medical records; personal image; information on family members, caregivers of the child; personal property; phone number; personal postal address; address, information on place of residence, hometown; address, information on school, class, academic performance and friends of the child; information on services provided to the child.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology upon request for the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced withdrawal, deletion, or removal of information on children’s private lives and personal secrets in case of violation of the provisions in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 33. Violations of the provisions on the prohibition of taking advantage of the provision of child replacement care to harm children; taking advantage of the State’s policies and regulations and the support and assistance of organizations and individuals for children for personal gain

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts, which are not subject to criminal prosecution:

  a) Taking advantage of the provision of child replacement care to commit acts of physical, emotional, psychological, or moral harm to children through violence, exploitation, abandonment, neglect, and other forms of harm;

  b) Taking advantage of the State’s policies and regulations and the support and assistance of organizations and individuals for children for personal gain.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of violation of the provisions in Point a, Clause 1 of this Article, which causes physical or health harm to children but is not subject to criminal prosecution;

  b) Enforced return of illegal profits obtained from the commission of the violation specified in Point b, Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced termination of provision of substitute care, and change of form of substitute care for children in accordance with the violations specified in Point a, Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 34. Violations against regulations on prohibition of setting up establishments providing services, establishments producing goods, warehouses storing hazardous goods, which are at risk of directly causing fire or explosion near establishments providing child protection services, educational, medical, cultural establishments, children’s recreational and entertainment points or vice versa

  1. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Building works, installing equipment of establishments providing services, establishments producing goods, warehouses storing hazardous goods, which are at risk of directly causing fire or explosion near establishments providing child protection services, educational, medical, cultural establishments, children’s recreational and entertainment points;

  b) Building works, installing equipment of establishments providing child protection services, educational, medical, cultural establishments, children’s recreational and entertainment points near establishments providing services, establishments producing goods, warehouses storing hazardous goods, which are at risk of directly causing fire or explosion.

  2. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Building establishments providing services, establishments producing goods, warehouses storing hazardous goods, which are at risk of directly causing fire or explosion near establishments providing child protection services, educational, medical, cultural establishments, children’s recreational and entertainment points;

  b) Building establishments providing child protection services, educational, medical, cultural establishments, children’s recreational and entertainment points near establishments providing services, establishments producing goods, warehouses storing hazardous goods, which are at risk of directly causing fire or explosion.

  3. Additional penalties:

  Suspension of operation for a fixed period of 03 to 06 months for one of the violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory dismantling of works and equipment for the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory relocation of service establishments, production establishments, warehouses of toxic goods, hazardous goods, establishments providing services for child protection, educational, medical, cultural establishments, children’s recreational and entertainment spots due to improper placement within the scope as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 35. Violations against regulations on establishments providing child protection services and management and use of infrastructure for children

  1. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed on establishments providing child protection services operating outside the scope of their registered operation.

  2. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed on establishments providing child protection services operating without having been granted operation registration or without registering their operation.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Occupying infrastructure for learning, recreation, entertainment and child protection services;

  b) Using infrastructure for learning, recreation, entertainment and child protection services for improper purposes or in contravention of law.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of works and equipment for the violation specified in Point a Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state for the violation specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 36. Violations against regulations on failure to ensure safety, leading to accidents and injuries to children

  1. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to warn or alert children about dangerous, toxic, flammable, explosive, electric shock, falling, and other risks leading to accidents and injuries, but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  b) Failure to fully and promptly comply with safety measures for production, business, and circulation of transport means, leading to accidents and injuries to children, but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced timely implementation of safety measures for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article, causing injuries or health damage to children, but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  Article 37. Violations against the responsibility to protect children on the internet

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the violations committed when providing services on the internet:

  a) Providing or sharing information or services harmful to children, false, toxic, pornographic, violent, inciting suicide, distorting, threatening, insulting the honor and dignity, or infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of children, but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  b) Not to warn or remove information or services harmful to children, false, toxic, pornographic, violent, inciting suicide, distorting, threatening, insulting the honor and dignity, infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of children but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  c) Not to use measures and tools to ensure the safety of information on the private life of children; not to send warning messages about risks when children provide or change information on the private life of children;

  d) Not to fulfill requests for deletion or removal of information on the private life of children when parents, caregivers, guardians of children or competent agencies, organizations and individuals request it;

  dd) Not to organize the receipt of information, assessment and classification according to the safety level for children sent by agencies, organizations, individuals and children;

  e) Not to publish lists of online information networks, services and products according to the safety level for children;

  g) Not to detect and eliminate images, documents and information inappropriate for children, affecting the healthy development of children;

  h) Not to guide the use of information and communication technology services and equipment to protect children;

  i) Not to have measures to protect persons accessing information and using services who are children.

  2. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed on online gaming service providers that fail to have tools to control gaming time and protect children from gaming abuse and addiction. Online gaming service providers that fail to apply measures to limit gaming time for children shall be administratively sanctioned as prescribed in the Government’s Decree on administrative sanctions in the fields of post, telecommunications, radio frequency, information technology and electronic transactions.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of illegal profits earned from the commission of the violations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of commission of the violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, which cause injuries or health damage to children but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced removal of information and services harmful to children; removal of fake services and applications that distort and infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of children on the network (including the deletion of data and applications on servers or application stores; domain name closure or other technical measures to prevent user access) in case of commission of the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Enforced deletion of information on children’s private lives in case of commission of the violations prescribed in Point d Clause 1 of this Article.

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 5,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences of administrative violations valued at not more than VND 10,000,000.

  2. Heads of mobile police units at the company level shall have the power to:
