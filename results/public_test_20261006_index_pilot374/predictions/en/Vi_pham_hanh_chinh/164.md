                                                   DECREE

     REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE LAWS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC ASSETS; SPENDING AND COUNTERFEITING; NATIONAL RESERVES; AND STATE GRAINS

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Government dated June 19, 2015; and the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Law on Management and Use of Public Property dated June 21, 2017;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Thrifty Practices and Anti-Wastefulness dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Law on National Reserves dated November 20, 2012;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates the Decree on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrifty practices and anti-wastefulness; national reserves; and state treasuries.

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, sanctioning forms, sanctioning levels, remedial measures, competence to make records of administrative violations and sanctioning administrative violations in the following fields:

  a) Management and use of public property at agencies, organizations and units; property of projects using state capital; property with established all-people ownership and property of infrastructure constructed with state investment and management;

  b) Prudent management and anti-wastefulness;

  c) National reserves;

  d) State treasury.

  2. Administrative violations related to the fields of management and use of public property; prudent management and anti-wastefulness; national reserves; state treasury in accounting, construction, investment planning, price appraisal, auction and other regulations not specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be sanctioned according to relevant laws.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; practice of frugality against waste; national reserves; state treasuries.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies;

  b) Units of the People's Armed Forces;

  c) Public service units;

  d) Agencies of the Communist Party of Vietnam;

  dd) Political-social organizations, socio-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, other organizations established in accordance with the law on associations;

  e) Other relevant enterprises, organizations and individuals.

  3. Persons competent to impose penalties and organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of penalties for administrative violations under this Decree.

  4. In cases where officials, civil servants or public employees commit violations while on duty and such violations are related to their assigned duties under legal documents or administrative documents issued by competent authorities or persons, they shall not be subject to administrative penalties under this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with the law on officials, civil servants and public employees.

  State agencies that commit violations related to their assigned state management tasks shall not be subject to administrative penalties under this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws.

  Article 3. Statute of limitations for administrative penalties

  The statute of limitations for administrative penalties in the fields of management and use of public property; practice of frugality and prevention of waste; national reserves; and state treasuries under this Decree is 1 year. The statute of limitations for administrative penalties for violations involving public property such as houses, land and infrastructure structures invested and managed by the State is 2 years.

  Article 4. Forms of administrative penalties and remedial measures

  1. Principal forms of penalties:

  a) Principal forms of penalties under this Decree include: Warning and fine;

  b) The maximum fine in the field of management and use of public property; national reserves; state treasuries is 50,000,000 VND for individuals and 100,000,000 VND for organizations; the maximum fine in the field of saving practice and anti-wastefulness is 100,000,000 VND for individuals and 200,000,000 VND for organizations.

  2. Additional sanctioning forms:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall also be subject to the additional sanctioning form of confiscation of the exhibits and means used to commit the administrative violation.

  3. Remedial measures in the field of management and use of public property:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall also be subject to one or a number of remedial measures such as:

  a) Forced to return the amount corresponding to the value of the property;

  b) Forced to return the amount caused by the commission of the administrative violation;

  c) Forced to return the property, in case the property has been changed due to the commission of the administrative violation, the property must be restored to its original state; in case the property cannot be restored to its original state, it must be paid in cash or returned in other property with equivalent functional value and use value to the original property;

  d) Enforced demolition of structures built on the area encroaching upon the head office or public service establishment;

  dd) Enforced destruction of false or falsified declarations on acts of violation against regulations on access to and use of public asset data;

  e) Enforced adjustment, supplementation of data, information, and declarations on public assets.

  4. Remedial measures in the field of frugal practice and anti-wastefulness:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall be subject to one or several remedial measures, such as:

  a) Withdrawal of funds from the state budget for misuse of funds from the state budget, in contravention of the fund’s principles issued by the competent authority;

  b) Enforced return of funds from the state budget for illegal gains, in contravention of the fund’s operating regulations and financial mechanisms issued by the competent authority.

  5. Remedial measures in the field of national reserves:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Forcible return of illegal profits earned from violations against regulations on purchase, sale, preservation, distribution, and relief; violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods; violations against regulations on management of capital and fees in national reserve operations; violations against regulations on business, pledge, mortgage, lease, and exploitation of national reserve assets;

  b) Forcible restoration of the initial state of facilities, technical equipment, and warehouses for preservation of national reserve goods;

  c) Forcible return of national reserve goods damaged due to infringement or sabotage; violations against regulations on distribution and relief of national reserve goods;

  d) Forcible withdrawal of national reserve goods that have been distributed in violation of regulations;

  dd) Forcible restoration of the original state of assets in cases of illegal business, pledge, mortgage, lease, or exploitation of assets that are warehouses for preservation of national reserve goods.

  6. Remedial measures in the field of state treasuries:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual shall also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory recovery of amounts spent on preparing payment dossiers and documents for work volumes that have not been performed; work volumes that are in progress but not eligible for payment, and amounts paid in excess of the contract value or contract appendix;

  b) Compulsory recovery of amounts spent on preparing payment dossiers and documents in violation of regulations and amounts spent in violation of the contents of original payment dossiers and documents at the unit but not to the extent of criminal liability investigation;

  c) Compulsory recovery of all amounts spent on using forged payment dossiers and documents for state budget expenditure but not to the extent of criminal liability investigation;

  d) Compulsory completion of payment dossiers and documents in accordance with regulations for acts of violating regulations on payment of state budget expenditures;

  dd) Compulsory execution of procedures for commitment to expenditure before requesting the State Treasury to make payment or reimbursement of state budget, or compulsory re-execution of procedures for commitment to expenditure for acts of violating procedures for commitment to expenditure control;

  e) Compulsory execution of procedures for advance payment for acts of violating procedures and time limits for advance payment of state budget;

  g) Compulsory freezing of accounts or closing of accounts for acts of violating regulations on account registration and use at the State Treasury; compulsory freezing of accounts for acts of preparing forged payment dossiers and documents for account registration procedures at the State Treasury but not to the extent of criminal liability investigation.

  7. Organizations and individuals that are sanctioned for administrative violations under this Decree are not allowed to use state budget money or money originating from the state budget to pay fines and remedy consequences caused by their violations. In case of sanctioning an organization, after complying with the sanctioning decision, the sanctioned organization shall identify individuals who are at fault for causing the administrative violation to determine legal liability, including the return of fines and remedy consequences caused by their violations corresponding to the severity of the violation of such individuals according to regulations of law and the organization's regulations on management and use of public property.

  Article 5. Application of fines

  The fines specified in Section 1, Section 2, Section 3 and Section 4 Chapter II, Section 1 Chapter III, Section 1 Chapter IV, Section 1 and Section 2 Chapter V of this Decree are the fines prescribed for administrative violations of organizations; for the same administrative violation, the fine for an individual shall be half of the fine for an organization (except for the provisions in Article 17, Article 23 and Article 27 of this Decree).

  Chapter II

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, FORMS OF SANCTION, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE PROVISIONS AND FINES FOR MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY AT OFFICES, ORGANIZATIONS AND UNIT

  Article 6. Administrative violations against the provisions on investment and procurement of public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of making investment or procurement of property without the decision of a competent authority or person at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of investment or procurement of property being machinery, equipment or other property (excluding offices, public service establishments and cars) with a total purchase value of less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of investment or purchase of property such as machinery, equipment, other property (excluding office buildings, public service establishments, cars) with total purchase value of VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 20,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 in case of investment or purchase of property such as office buildings, public service establishments, cars.

  2. A fine of VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out centralized procurement of property listed in the centralized procurement list as prescribed by law on management and use of public property.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for investment or purchase of property exceeding standards or quotas as prescribed by competent regulatory agencies at the following levels:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of investment or purchase of property with a value of less than VND 50,000,000/property unit;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of investment or purchase of property with a value of from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000/property unit;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of investment or purchase of property with a value of from VND 100,000,000/property unit or more.

  4. Remedial measures:

  For the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article, the amount corresponding to the value of the investment or purchase of assets exceeding the standards or norms shall be compulsorily returned.

  5. The determination of the excess value as the basis for sanctioning is as follows:

  a) In case of investment or purchase of assets such as cars, machinery, equipment, or other assets exceeding the standards or norms in terms of quantity, the excess value per asset unit shall be determined by the unit price of such asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or purchase contract;

  b) In case of investment or purchase of assets such as cars, machinery, equipment, or other assets that are correct in terms of quantity but exceed the standards or norms in terms of price, the excess value shall be determined by the asset value according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or purchase contract minus the asset value according to the standards or norms prescribed by the competent regulatory authority;

  c) In case of investment or purchase of office buildings or public service establishments exceeding the standards or norms, the excess value shall be determined by the difference between the actual area minus the area used according to the standards or norms multiplied by the average purchase price stated on the invoice (or purchase contract);

  d) The persons competent to sanction as prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as the basis for sanctioning according to the law on handling of administrative violations and shall be responsible for such determination.

  Article 7. Administrative violations against regulations on asset leasing

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation against regulations on asset leasing in case the asset lease contract is valued at less than VND 100,000,000 for the following acts:

  a) Renting property without a decision on property rental from a competent authority or person;

  b) Renting property beyond the standards or quotas prescribed by a competent authority;

  c) Selecting property rental service providers in violation of the procedures prescribed by law on management and use of public property and relevant laws.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation against regulations on property rental specified in Clause 1 of this Article in case the property rental contract is valued at VND 100,000,000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of the amount of property rented beyond the standards or quotas prescribed in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The value of the property rental contract shall serve as the basis for imposing penalties as follows:

  a) In case the property rental contract specifies the contract value, the value specified in the contract shall serve as the basis for imposing penalties;

  b) In case of renting out property without concluding a contract or concluding a contract but lacking sufficient information to determine the value as prescribed in Clause a of this Point, the value used as the basis for sanctioning shall be determined by the rental price of property of the same type or having equivalent standards and techniques on the market at the time of the violation multiplied by (x) the rental period of the property from the beginning of the rental to the time of the decision on sanctioning.

  The person with sanctioning competence prescribed in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the value used as the basis for sanctioning in accordance with the law on handling of administrative violations and shall bear the responsibility for such determination.

  Article 8. Administrative violations against regulations on the transfer and use of public property, destruction or intentional damage to public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of transferring public property beyond the standards and quotas issued by competent agencies or persons (not conforming to the target, exceeding the area, quantity or price) at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of transferring or using property with a value of less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of transferring or using property with a value of from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of transferring or using property with a value of from VND 100,000,000 or more.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the act of transferring or using public property for improper purposes (using public property for purposes other than the intended use of the property invested, equipped or purchased; using the office building or public service establishment as a residence or for personal purposes; using cars for commuting from residence to workplace for positions without standards; using cars for work purposes for positions without standards for car use without approval from competent authorities; transferring or using cars for personal purposes; using machinery, equipment and other property for personal purposes) at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of transfer or use of property which is machinery, equipment, other property (excluding office buildings, public service establishments, cars) with original cost according to accounting books under VND 100,000,000/1 unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property with value under VND 100,000,000);

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of transfer or use of property which is machinery, equipment, other property (excluding office buildings, public service establishments, cars) with original cost according to accounting books of VND 100,000,000 or more/1 unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property with value of VND 100,000,000 or more);

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of transfer or use of property which is office buildings, public service establishments, cars.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for acts of destroying or intentionally damaging public property but not to the extent of criminal liability examination according to the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of intentionally damaging property which is machinery, equipment, other property with original cost according to accounting books under VND 100,000,000/1 unit of property;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of intentionally damaging property which is machinery, equipment, other property with original cost according to accounting books of VND 100,000,000 or more/1 unit of property;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of intentionally damaging property which is office buildings, public service establishments, cars.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of the amount corresponding to the value of the property exceeding the standard or norm for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the initial condition of the property that has been changed due to the administrative violation; in case the initial condition of the property cannot be restored, the property must be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and use value to the initial property for the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. The determination of the value exceeding the standard or norm is as follows:

  a) In case of delivery or use of property such as cars, machinery, equipment, and other property exceeding the standard or norm in quantity: The excess value is determined by multiplying the quantity of the property exceeding the standard or norm by (x) the original price of the property on the accounting book; in case the original price of the property is not tracked on the accounting book, it shall be determined by the new purchase price of the property of the same type or with equivalent technical standards and utility on the market at the time of the violation;

  b) In case of delivery or use of property such as cars, machinery, equipment, and other property in quantity but exceeding the price level compared to the standard or norm: The excess value is determined by multiplying the quantity of the property exceeding the standard or norm by (x) (the difference between the original price of the property on the accounting book minus (-) the value of the property according to the standard or norm prescribed by the competent state agency);

  c) In case of property such as office buildings or public service establishments: The excess value is determined by the difference between the actual area minus (-) the area used according to the standard or norm multiplied by (x) the average unit price according to the accounting book; in case the unit price is not tracked on the accounting book, it shall be determined according to the investment quota of the building or structure with equivalent technical standards promulgated by the Ministry of Construction at the time of the violation;

  d) The competent person prescribed in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as the basis for sanctioning according to the law on handling of administrative violations and shall be responsible for such determination.

  Article 9. Administrative violations against regulations on lending of public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for lending or using public property in contravention of regulations (lending public property to other organizations or individuals without a loan contract or with a loan contract, regardless of the loan duration) at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of lending property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of lending property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 50,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 in case of lending property which is an office building, a public service establishment or a car.

  2. Remedial measures:

  An organization that commits the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Forcible return of the lent property. If the property has been changed due to the administrative violation, the property must be restored to its original state; if the property cannot be restored to its original state, the property must be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and use value to the original property;

  b) Forcible payment of the amount corresponding to the property rental fee during the lending period to the state budget. The determination of the property rental fee for the purpose of imposing penalties shall comply with Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 10. Administrative violations against regulations on exchange and donation of public property in contravention of regulations

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of exchanging public property in contravention of regulations (using public property of an organization to exchange for property of another organization or individual without permission of a competent authority or person) at the following levels:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case the property used for exchange is valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case the property used for exchange is valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case the property used for exchange is a working office, a public service establishment or a car.

  2. A fine of from VND 20,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of donating public property in contravention of regulations (using public property to make a gift in contravention of the gift regulation promulgated by a competent state authority).

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of property or payment of an equivalent amount in cash for the property that has been exchanged or donated in contravention of regulations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 11. Administrative violations against regulations on occupation of offices and non-profit operation establishments

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of occupying an office or non-profit operation establishment (an organization or individual that is using adjacent land to move the land parcel boundary markers to expand the land area or to build structures that encroach upon the land area of the office or non-profit operation establishment of an agency, organization or unit; an organization or individual that arbitrarily uses houses and land of the office or non-profit operation establishment of an agency, organization or unit).

  2. Additional penalties:

  The exhibits and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be confiscated.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Organizations and individuals that commit the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Forced restoration of the initial state of the office or non-profit operation establishment and return of the occupied office or non-profit operation establishment;

  b) Forced demolition of structures built on the encroached land area;

  c) Forcible payment of the amount corresponding to the rental of the property during the period of occupation to the state budget. The determination of the rental amount shall serve as the basis for sanctioning in accordance with Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 12. Acts of appropriation of public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for acts of appropriation of public property which have not reached the level of criminal liability (keeping or using public property without permission of a competent state agency) at the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 for public property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 for public property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of appropriation of working offices, public service establishments, and cars.

  2. Additional sanctioning form: Confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures: Organizations and individuals that commit the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of property that has been altered due to administrative violations to be returned to the organization; in case the initial state of property cannot be restored, compensation in kind or property with equivalent utility and use value to the initial property shall be made;

  b) Enforced payment to the state budget of the amount corresponding to the property rental during the period of appropriation. The determination of the property rental amount to serve as the basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 13. Administrative violations against regulations on use of public property for business purposes, lease, joint venture, or association

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the use of public property for business purposes, lease, joint venture, or association without a decision approving the project of the competent authority or person according to the law on management and use of public property at the time of committing the violation, at the following levels:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of using property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of using property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) From VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case of using property as the office building, public service facility, or motor vehicle.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the violation against regulations on determination of property value for business purposes, lease, joint venture, or association at the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 for establishing a price council or property valuation council without the correct composition;

  b) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for selecting a valuation organization to determine the value of property used for business, lease, joint venture or association without following the order prescribed by law.

  3. A fine for using public property for business, lease, joint venture or association for purposes other than those approved by competent authorities or persons, affecting the performance of functions and tasks assigned by the State, shall be imposed at the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 in case of using property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 in case of using property which is a car; property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 20,000,000 in case of using property which is an office building, public service establishment or car.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of property which has been changed due to the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the initial state of property, the property shall be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and value to the initial property;

  b) Forcibly return the illegal profits earned from the acts specified in Clauses 1 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Administrative violations against regulations on failure to perform maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law

  1. An organization that has been granted by a competent authority to estimate maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law but fails to perform maintenance and repair of property, leading to damage to property, shall be sanctioned according to the following levels of penalties:

  a) A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 in case the property is valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case the property is valued at between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 in case the property is valued at VND 100,000,000 or more and is a working office, a public service establishment, or a car that is not subject to criminal liability examination.

  2. The amount of money to be returned corresponds to the value of damaged property as follows:

  a) For damaged property that cannot be repaired, the amount of money to be returned corresponds to the new purchase price of the property of the same type or having equivalent standards and functions on the market multiplied by the remaining quality ratio at the time of determining the value;

  b) For damaged property that can be repaired, the amount of money to be returned is the cost of repairing such property;

  c) The head of the organization shall contact the financial agency under his/her management to determine the amount of money to be returned; on that basis, the head of the organization shall decide the amount of money to be returned and take responsibility for his/her decision.

  Article 15. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of public property

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare plans for reorganization and handling of public property in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to declare and prepare plans for reorganization and handling of public property within the prescribed time limit;

  c) Failing to report to the competent state agency for the recovery, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of property when the conditions are met in accordance with law (except for cases where public property has expired its use period in accordance with law but is still usable and the agency, organization, unit or enterprise still has the need to use it, the head of the agency, organization, unit shall decide on the appropriate form of handling).

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed on organizations for causing damage or loss of property during the waiting period for handling.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for the act of handling property without a decision of a competent authority or person at the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed in case of selling, transferring, liquidating or destroying property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of selling, transferring, liquidating or destroying property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed in case of selling, transferring or liquidating property which is a working office, a public service establishment or a car.

  4. The act of declaring property not in the list or current status of property to be handled (leading to a decision on handling property not in accordance with regulations) shall be sanctioned as follows:

  a) A warning shall be imposed in case of declaring property valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed in case of declaring property valued at between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed in case of declaring property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  d) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of declaration of property which is a working office, a public service establishment or a car.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of property which has been changed due to the acts specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the initial state of property, the property must be returned in cash or property with equivalent utility and use value to the initial property;

  b) Enforced payment of the amount corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged property for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  6. The amount of money to be returned corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged property specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article is defined as follows:

  a) For property which is lost or damaged and cannot be restored, the amount of money to be returned shall be determined as equivalent to the new purchase price of the property of the same type or having equivalent standards and utility on the market at the time of the violation.

  The market price may be based on the price quotes of market suppliers which have been posted or announced on the market or from official information published by suppliers which are accessed via the Internet.

  b) For property which is damaged and can be restored, the amount of money to be returned is the cost of repairing the property.

  Article 16. Administrative violations against regulations on organization of public asset disposal

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to implement the disposal plan approved by competent agencies or persons within the prescribed time limit.

  The time limit shall be determined according to the disposal time limit specified in the asset disposal decision. If the disposal decision does not specify a time limit, the relevant law shall apply.   2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of sabotaging material and technical facilities, warehouses for preservation of national reserve goods.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for acts of infringing upon or sabotaging national reserve goods at the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage to national reserve goods;

  b) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods with a value of less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods with a value of between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000.

  4. Additional penalties

  Confiscate the material evidences and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state of the material and technical facilities and the warehouse for preservation of national reserve goods for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of national reserve goods damaged due to the acts of infringement or sabotage of national reserve goods specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 44. Violations against regulations on allocation and relief of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstructing the allocation, relief or performance of another task related to national reserve goods at the following levels:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 in case of no damage;

  b) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  2. A warning or a fine of VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out procedures for allocation and relief of national reserve goods valued at under VND 100,000,000 within the prescribed time limit.

  3. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for using national reserve goods for improper purposes; allocating and providing relief of national reserve goods to the wrong recipients according to decisions of competent state agencies; allocating and providing relief of national reserve goods without ensuring types, quantities, quality, standards and specifications according to the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 in case of no damage;

  b) From VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at under VND 50,000,000;

  c) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  4. A fine shall be imposed for losing national reserve goods for allocation, relief or performance of other tasks according to the following fines:

  a) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case of national reserve goods valued at under VND 50,000,000;

  b) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 in case of national reserve goods valued between VND 50,000,000 and under VND 100,000,000.

  5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 for distributing national reserve goods valued under VND 100,000,000 to the wrong recipients as decided by the competent authority.

  6. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 for delaying or delaying the allocation and relief of national reserve goods valued under VND 100,000,000 as prescribed by the competent authority.

  7. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 for delaying or delaying the distribution of national reserve goods valued under VND 100,000,000 as decided by the competent authority, causing damage to national reserve goods.

  8. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of material evidence and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of illegal profits obtained from the commission of the violations specified in Clauses 3, 5 and 6 of this Article;

  b) Forcible return of lost national reserve goods, misused national reserve goods or wrong allocation of national reserve goods for the acts specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  c) Forcible return of damaged, defective or unsuitable national reserve goods or insufficient national reserve goods for the acts specified in Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 45. Violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstructing the import, export and transport of national reserve goods at the following levels:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to carry out or improper carrying out of procedures for import and export of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 as prescribed;

  b) Delivery of goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 in violation of the decision of a competent authority regarding quantity, type, specifications, standards and quality.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 in violation of the decision of a competent authority regarding type, specifications, quantity, quality, time and place.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 without the decision of a competent authority.

  5. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory return of illegal profits earned from the commission of administrative violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;

  b) Enforced withdrawal of national reserve goods that have been issued in contravention of regulations for acts specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 46. Violations of regulations on receipt of national reserve goods but not reaching the level of criminal liability investigation

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Receiving national reserve goods not at the correct time and place as decided by the competent state agency;

  b) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on procedures and order for receipt of national reserve goods.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of failing to receive national reserve goods (national reserve goods issued for relief or for the performance of another task by a competent agency) when the goods have fully met the conditions as prescribed by the competent state agency.

  Article 47. Acts of illegally interfering in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for acts of illegally interfering in national reserve activities at the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 in case of no damage;

  b) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at between VND 50,000,000 and less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 48. Violations against regulations on management of capital and fees in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for failure to report the use of money from national reserve capital for purchase of national reserve goods as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Payment when there is no contract for purchase or sale of national reserve goods;

  b) Payment when national reserve goods are not in the correct quantity or there are not enough invoices and vouchers;

  c) Payment when there is no record of contract liquidation or list of documents for warehouse receipt with the signature of the head of the unit for cases of direct purchase without bidding;

  d) Payment when the head of the national reserve unit does not approve the payment;

  dd) Use of import, export, and storage fees for national reserve goods in violation of regulations or exceeding the prescribed limit.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Using national reserve capital for purposes other than those prescribed;

  b) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on management of funds withdrawn from national reserve capital;

  c) Failure to pay the remaining amount after the purchase of national reserve goods is completed as prescribed by the competent regulatory authority.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcible return of illegal profits earned from the commission of the acts specified in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 49. Violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease and exploitation of national reserve assets

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease and exploitation of national reserve assets (excluding the national reserve goods storage facility) at the following levels:

  a) From VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 in case the asset is valued at less than VND 70,000,000;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 in case the asset is valued at between VND 70,000,000 and less than VND 150,000,000;

  c) From VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 in case the asset is valued at between VND 150,000,000 and less than VND 300,000,000;

  d) Between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 in case the property is valued between VND 300,000,000 and under VND 500,000,000;

  dd) Between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 in case the property is valued at VND 500,000,000 or more.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the acts of trading, pledging, mortgaging, leasing or illegally exploiting the property which is a national reserve goods storage facility at the following levels:

  a) Between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 in case the national reserve goods storage facility is valued at under VND 500,000,000;

  b) Between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 in case the national reserve goods storage facility is valued at VND 500,000,000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the property to its original state in case of the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of the illegal profits earned from the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. COMPETENCE TO DRAW UP RECORDS AND ENFORCE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES IN THE FIELD OF NATIONAL STORAGE

  Article 50. Competence to make records of administrative violations in the field of national reserves

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations in the field of national reserves include:

  a) Persons competent to impose administrative penalties for violations in the field of national reserves specified in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree;

  b) Public servants assigned to check the observance of the law on national reserves.

  2. The person competent to make the record specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall have to issue a sanctioning decision within his/her competence or transfer it to the competent authority specified in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree to issue a sanctioning decision.

  Article 51. The competence of the President of the People’s Committee to impose penalties

  1. The presidents of the district-level People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) A warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of the material evidences and means used for the administrative violation, the value of which does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Application of the remedial measures specified in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The Chairperson of the provincial-level People’s Committee has the power to:

  a) Issue a warning;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscate the material evidences and means used for the administrative violation;

  d) Apply the remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5, Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 52. Authority to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Inspector and person assigned to perform specialized inspection tasks in the field of national reserves while on duty have the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscate the material evidences and means of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine level prescribed in Point b of this Clause.

  2. Chief Inspector of the Department, Head of the Inspectorate of the Department, specialized inspector in the field of national reserves, Head of the specialized inspectorate established by the Director General of the State Reserve Department, and Head of the specialized inspectorate established by the Director of the Regional State Reserve Department have the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) A fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means of administrative violations with a value not exceeding the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Application of remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspection teams at the Ministry level shall perform the specialized inspection functions in the field of national reserves and have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and means of administrative violations with a value not exceeding the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Apply remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscate the material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  d) Apply the remedial measures prescribed in Clause 5, Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 53. The power to impose penalties of the Director of the Regional State Reserve Department, the Director General of the State Reserve General Department

  1. The Director of the Regional State Reserve Department shall perform the function of specialized inspection in the field of national reserves and have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) A fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and instrumentalities of administrative violations, the value of which does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Application of remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The Director General of the State Resources General Department shall perform the function of specialized inspection in the field of national reserves and has the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and instrumentalities of administrative violations;

  d) Apply remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter V

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, SANCTIONING FORMS, SANCTIONING LEVELS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF STATE CAPITAL ALLOCATION

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE BUDGET EXPENDITURES AND SANCTIONING LEVELS

  Article 54. Violations against regulations on state budget expenditures to be included in state budget estimates shall be sanctioned by competent authorities

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures on items and works not included in the approved estimate;

  b) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures exceeding the estimate or capital plan for the year assigned by competent authorities or exceeding the approved estimate of expenditures (for works not subject to contracts);

  c) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures that are inconsistent with the estimate assigned by competent authorities (expenditures on incorrect estimate sources; expenditures on incorrect purposes, subjects or contents compared to the assigned estimate); expenditures on incorrect investment capital sources or expenditures on incorrect project lists approved by competent authorities.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 6,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of work volumes that have not been performed;

  b) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of work volumes that are in progress but not yet eligible for payment;

  c) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for payment exceeding the contract value or contract appendix.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced withdrawal of the amounts paid for work volumes that have not been performed; work volumes that are in progress but not yet eligible for payment and the amounts paid exceeding the contract value or contract appendix as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 55. Violations of regulations on preparation of dossiers and documents and preparation of dossiers and documents inconsistent with original dossiers and documents at the unit but not yet subject to criminal liability investigation

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for signing a contract before the capital plan is approved by the competent authority (except for construction works under urgent orders).

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 6,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures that do not meet the standards on titles and users;

  b) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures that exceed the spending limits in terms of quantity and value;

  c) Preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury for expenditures that do not comply with regulations (expenditures that do not meet the conditions and principles of the expenditure regime).

  In case an organization commits acts of purchasing or leasing state property that exceed the standards and limits prescribed by competent state agencies, it shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of Article 5 and Article 6 of this Decree.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of preparing dossiers and documents to be sent to the State Treasury that do not conform to the contents of the dossiers, invoices and original documents at the unit but the amount is not sufficient for criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced withdrawal of expenditures made from the preparation of dossiers and documents that do not comply with regulations and expenditures made from the preparation of dossiers and documents that do not conform to the contents of the original documents at the unit but the amount is not sufficient for criminal prosecution according to the provisions of Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 56. Acts of making false dossiers and documents to spend state budget but not yet to the level of criminal liability examination

  1. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for making false dossiers and documents sent to the State Treasury for payment of regular expenditures, public service expenditures with regular nature, expenditures on national target programs, expenditures on target programs for use of public service funds (excluding expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of physical facilities from regular expenditures of state budget and funds left according to regulations on regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500,000,000).

  2. A fine of from VND 30,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for making false dossiers and documents sent to the State Treasury for payment of investment capital from state budget and investment capital from state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of physical facilities from regular expenditures of state budget and funds left according to regulations on regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500,000,000.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recovery of all expenditures made from using false dossiers and documents to spend state budget but not yet to the level of criminal liability examination according to regulations in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 57. Acts of violating regulations on payment of state budget expenditures

  1. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for making dossiers and documents requesting the State Treasury to transfer money for payment not in the name or account number of the beneficiary unit specified in the contract or contract appendix.

  2. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for making dossiers and documents requesting payment of state budget expenditures not in accordance with payment clauses specified in the contract or contract appendix between the estimate unit or investor and the supplier of goods or services, including one of the following acts:

  a) Errors in contract value;

  b) Errors in payment deadlines;

  c) Errors in payment methods;

  d) Errors in payment ratios (including advance payments);

  dd) Errors in other payment clauses specified in the contract and the contract appendix.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced completion of dossiers and documents in accordance with regulations for the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 58. Acts of violating procedures for control of commitment to expenditure

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to send the state budget expenditure commitment to the State Treasury for expenditures falling within the scope of expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  b) Sending the request for state budget expenditure commitment to the State Treasury late beyond the prescribed time limit for expenditures falling within the scope of expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  c) Sending the request for expenditure commitment exceeding the annual investment capital plan, the remaining balance of the annual estimate that is permitted to be used, or exceeding the value of the contract that is permitted to be committed to.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced to carry out the expenditure commitment procedures before requesting the State Treasury to make payment or disbursement of the state budget for the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced to carry out the expenditure commitment procedures again for the violation specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 59. Violations against procedures and time limits for advance payment of the state budget

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to make procedures for advance payment after the last deadline for advance payment for regular expenditures without contracts for goods or services or expenditures not subject to deposit of contracts for goods or services to the State Treasury as prescribed;

  b) Failing to make procedures for advance payment for investment expenditures from state budget and investment sources from state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, and expansion of physical facilities from regular expenditures of state budget and retained fees under regulations for regular expenditures with total investment over VND 500,000,000 paid in installments corresponding to the completed contract volume.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to make procedures for full advance payment for regular expenditures with contracts for goods or services under regulations for the last payment of the contract;

  b) Failing to make procedures for full advance payment for investment expenditures from state budget and investment sources from state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, and expansion of physical facilities from regular expenditures of state budget and retained fees under regulations for regular expenditures with total investment over VND 500,000,000 when the payment value (including advance payment and payment for completed volume) reaches 80% of the contract value;

  c) Failing to make or making procedures for advance payment for compensation, support, and relocation expenditures after the prescribed deadline for advance payment.

  3. Remedial measures:

  For acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, the payment of advance payment is mandatory.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATION AND PENALTIES FOR REGISTRATION AND USE OF ACCOUNTS AT THE STATE TREASURY

  Article 60. Violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury.

  2. Remedial measures:

  The account shall be frozen or closed for acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 61. Violations of regulations on making false dossiers and documents for registration of use of accounts at the State Treasury but not yet to the level of criminal prosecution

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for making false dossiers and documents sent to the State Treasury for registration of use of accounts.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced bank account freezing for the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 3. COMPETENCE TO IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF STATE TREASURERIES

  Article 62. Competence to make records of administrative violations in the field of state treasuries

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations in the field of state treasuries include:

  a) Persons competent to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of state budget expenditure control through state treasuries specified in Articles 63 and 64 of this Decree;

  b) Public employees assigned to control state budget expenditure through state treasuries.

  2. Persons competent to make records specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall have to issue penalty-imposing decisions within their competence or transfer them to competent authorities specified in Articles 63 and 64 of this Decree for penalty-imposing decisions.

  Article 63. Authority to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Chief inspectors of the Finance Service, heads of specialized inspection teams of the Finance Service, heads of specialized inspection teams of the State Treasury shall be appointed by the Director of the State Treasury of the province and the Director of the specialized inspection team of the State Treasury shall be appointed by the General Director of the State Treasury and have the authority to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  b) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 6, Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chief inspectors of the Ministry of Finance shall have the authority to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 6, Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspection teams of the Ministry of Finance shall have the authority to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 250,000,000;

  b) The application of the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 64. The power to impose penalties of the Director General of the State Treasury; Director of the provincial/municipal State Treasury

  1. The Director of the provincial/municipal State Treasury has the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  b) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The Director General of the State Treasury has the power to:

  a) Impose a fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b) Apply the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter VI

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 65. Effect

  1. This Decree takes effect from September 01, 2019.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of management and use of state property; practice of thrift and anti-wastefulness; national reserves; state treasury and the Government’s Decree No. 58/2015/ND-CP dated June 16, 2015 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of management and use of state property; practice of thrift and anti-wastefulness; national reserves; state treasury shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  Article 66. Transitional provisions

  For administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; practice of thrift and anti-wastefulness; national reserves; state treasury that occurred before the effective date of this Decree but were only detected later or are under consideration and settlement, the provisions on sanctioning specified in this Decree shall apply if this Decree does not specify legal liability or specifies lighter legal liability for the violating individuals and organizations.

  Article 67. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Ministry of Finance shall provide guidelines for the implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies and People’s Committees at all levels shall provide guidelines for and inspect the implementation of the administration of public property; the practice of frugality and the prevention of waste; the national reserve; and the state treasury as prescribed in this Decree.

  3. State agencies, organizations and individuals shall, upon detecting acts of administrative violations in the field of administration of public property; the practice of frugality and the prevention of waste; the national reserve; and the state treasury, transfer dossiers (if any) or notify competent persons to handle them.

  In cases where acts of administrative violations in the field of administration of public property are subject to the recovery of public property as prescribed by law on administration of public property, competent persons to handle administrative violations as prescribed in this Decree shall, after issuing decisions on administrative violations, notify competent state agencies as prescribed by law on administration of public property to issue decisions on the recovery of property as prescribed. The handling of property after recovery shall comply with law on administration of public property.

  4. Ministers, heads of ministerial agencies, heads of Governmental agencies, Presidents of People’s Committees at all levels and relevant organizations and individuals shall implement this Decree.
