ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF EDUCATION

                                                  THE GOVERNMENT

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;
  Pursuant to the December 2, 1998 Law on Education;
  Pursuant to the July 2, 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;
  At the request of the Minister of Education and Training,

                                                  HEREBY PROMULGATES:

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations in the field of education (vocational training not falling under the scope of this Decree), forms of sanction, sanction levels, sanctioning competence, sanctioning procedures and remedial measures.

  2. Administrative violations in the field of education are acts of violating regulations on state management in the field of education committed intentionally or unintentionally by individuals or organizations, which are not crimes and must be sanctioned for administrative violations according to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and this Decree.

  3. Administrative violations in the field of education specified in other Government’s Decrees (hereinafter referred to as relevant Decrees) shall be handled according to the provisions of those Decrees.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree and relevant Decrees.

  2. Foreign individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education in Vietnam shall be sanctioned like Vietnamese individuals and organizations according to the provisions of this Decree and relevant Decrees, except for cases where international treaties signed or acceded by the Socialist Republic of Vietnam provide otherwise.

  3. Minors committing administrative violations in the field of education shall be handled according to the provisions of Clause 1 and Clause 3 Article 7 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 3. Principles of sanctioning

  1. All administrative violations in the field of education must be promptly detected and immediately suspended.

  The handling of administrative violations in the field of education must be swift, fair and thorough; all consequences caused by administrative violations must be remedied in accordance with law.

  2. Individuals and organizations shall only be sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education when they commit violations prescribed in this Decree and other Government Decrees on sanctioning of administrative violations.

  3. An administrative violation in the field of education shall only be sanctioned once.

  An individual or organization that commits multiple administrative violations in the field of education shall be sanctioned for each violation.

  Multiple individuals or organizations that commit the same administrative violation in the field of education shall be sanctioned for each violation.

  4. The sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of education must be based on the nature and severity of the violation, the personal circumstances of the violator and mitigating or aggravating circumstances to decide on the appropriate form and measures.

  5. Administrative violations in the field of education shall not be sanctioned in cases of emergencies, legitimate defense, unforeseen events or administrative violations committed by individuals suffering from mental illness or other diseases that have caused them to lose their ability to recognize or control their behavior.

  Article 4. Exemptions and aggravating circumstances

  Exemptions and aggravating circumstances shall be applied to administrative violations committed under Chapter II of this Decree in accordance with Articles 8 and 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 5. Statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations

  1. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education is one year from the date of commission of the administrative violation; for administrative violations in the field of education related to finance, intellectual property, construction, environment, housing, land, publishing, export, import, entry, exit or administrative violations of smuggling, production and trade in counterfeit goods, the statute of limitations is two years; if the above-mentioned time limit is exceeded, the violation shall not be sanctioned, but remedial measures prescribed in Clause 3 Article 7 of this Decree shall still be applied.

  2. For individuals who are prosecuted or have a decision to bring a case to trial according to criminal procedures but later have a decision to terminate the investigation or terminate the case and the violation shows signs of administrative violations in the field of education, they shall be sanctioned for administrative violations; within three days from the date of issuance of the decision to terminate the investigation or terminate the case, the person who has issued the decision must send the decision to the person with the power to sanction; in this case, the statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations is three months from the date on which the competent person receives the decision to terminate and the violation file.

  3. Within the time limit prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, if an individual or organization commits a new administrative violation in the field of education or deliberately evades or obstructs the sanctioning, the statute of limitations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article shall not apply. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations shall be recalculated from the date of commission of the new administrative violation or from the date on which the act of deliberately evading or obstructing the sanctioning is terminated.

  Article 6. Time limit for being considered as not having been sanctioned for administrative violations

  An individual or organization that is sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education, if, within one year from the date on which the sanctioning decision is fully served or from the date on which the sanctioning decision expires, does not commit any further violations, shall be considered as having not committed any administrative violations in the field of education.

  Article 7. Sanctioning forms and remedial measures

  1. For each administrative violation in the field of education, the violating individual or organization shall incur one of the following principal sanctioning forms:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fines.

  When applying the fine sanctioning form, the specific fine amount for a particular administrative violation shall be the average of the fine amount range prescribed for that violation. If the violation has mitigating circumstances as prescribed in Article 8 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, the fine amount may be reduced below the average but not below the minimum of the fine amount range; if the violation has aggravating circumstances as prescribed in Article 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, the fine amount may be increased above the average but not exceed the maximum of the fine amount range.

  2. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individual or organization in the field of education may incur one or the following additional sanctioning forms:

  a) Deprivation of the right to use decisions on establishment, licenses, or practice certificates for a fixed or indefinite period for individuals or organizations that commit serious violations of regulations on use of decisions, licenses, or practice certificates;

  b) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  c) Deportation if the violator is a foreigner.

  3. Apart from the sanctioning forms prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the field of education may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Forced restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to administrative violations at the request of competent agencies;

  b) Forced suspension or termination of teaching and educational activities;

  c) Forced cancellation of results of examination subjects or re-grading of examination papers;

  d) Forced provision of teaching, practical guidance, and additional internships with sufficient number of classes, contents, and programs in accordance with regulations; supply of materials and equipment in accordance with the approved plan;

  dd) Forced cancellation of incorrect decisions contrary to regulations of competent agencies;

  e) Enforced restoration of learning rights, lawful benefits, and correct assessment results of learners, ensuring the rights of the persons using diplomas and certificates;

  g) Enforced repayment of the amount of money collected from learners and all costs incurred for the repayment caused by administrative violations;

  h) Enforced revocation of illegally issued diplomas and certificates;

  i) Enforced removal from Vietnam or re-export of textbooks, educational materials, and equipment illegally imported due to administrative violations.

  Chapter II

    ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF EDUCATION, FORMS OF SANCTION AND SANCTION LEVELS

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on establishment of educational establishments and organization of educational activities

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of losing the establishment decision, license, or practice certificate without reporting to the competent authority.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts: erasing, modifying, supplementing contents, buying, selling, transferring, renting, leasing, borrowing, lending decisions on establishment, licenses, practice certificates in contravention of law.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for acts of establishing educational establishments illegally according to the following fines:

  a) Between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 for acts of establishing preschool, general education establishments;

  b) Between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 for acts of establishing vocational secondary education establishments, continuing education centers, foreign language centers, computer centers;

  c) Between VND 20,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 for college education establishments;

  d) Between VND 60,000,000 and VND 100,000,000 for university, postgraduate education establishments.

  4. A fine shall be imposed for acts of opening independent classes to teach preschool, general education, vocational secondary education, university entrance preparation, university entrance exam preparation, college, university and postgraduate education programs without permission of competent authorities or acts of granting permission without competence according to the following fines:

  a) Between VND 200,000 and VND 500,000 for preschool classes;

  b) From VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 for general education classes;

  c) From VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 for vocational secondary education classes, university entrance preparation classes, and university entrance exam preparation classes;

  d) From VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 for university and postgraduate education classes.

  6. Additional penalties and remedial measures for the acts specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscation of material evidence for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory return of the collected amount to learners and bearing all costs for refunding the collected amount for the acts specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on educational establishment operations

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the acts of failing to comply with decisions of competent authorities on suspension or dissolution of operations; operating beyond the time limit specified in the permit; and stopping operations without permission from competent authorities, causing damage to learners, according to the following fines:

  a) From VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 for preschool and general education institutions;

  b) From VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 for vocational secondary education institutions, non-formal education centers, foreign language centers and computer centers;

  c) From VND 15,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 for college education institutions;

  d) From VND 40,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 for university and postgraduate education institutions.

  2. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for training and issuing certificates in contravention of law.

  3. A fine of from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Training and issuing diplomas of the national education system beyond the assigned competence;

  b) Training in subjects and majors beyond the assigned competence.

  4. Remedial measures for violations prescribed in this Article:

  a) Compulsory return of collected money to learners and bearing all costs for refunding for violations specified in Clause 1 and revocation of illegally issued diplomas and certificates for violations specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory restoration of lost learning rights of learners caused by violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article under decisions of competent authorities.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on programs, contents and education plans

  1. Warning or fine for teaching insufficient number of classes or knowledge contents (converted into number of classes) of subjects according to regulations of competent authorities for general education, vocational secondary education, colleges, universities and postgraduate education at the following fines:

  a) Warning for violations of less than 5 classes per class in a school year;

  b) Fine of from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 for violations of 5 to 10 classes per class in a school year;

  c) Fine of from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 for violations of 11 to 15 classes per class in a school year;

  d) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for violations involving 16 to 20 lessons per class in a school year;

  dd) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 for violations involving 21 lessons or more per class in a school year.

  2. A fine for teaching or disseminating contents not included in the curriculum, textbooks or teaching programs for the purpose of distorting educational contents (but not to the extent of criminal prosecution) shall be imposed at the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for the first violation;

  b) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for the second violation or subsequent violations.

  3. Additional penalties for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscation of material evidence for violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Expulsion if the violating individual is a foreigner for violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  c) The license or certificate of practice shall be suspended for a fixed period if the violation is committed for the first time or indefinitely if the violation is repeated for the license or certificate of practice.

  4. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) The violator shall be forced to stop teaching or educational activities for the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The violator shall be forced to provide additional lessons and contents for the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on subjects, standards, quotas and enrollment authority

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 200,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for concealing or assisting in concealing enrollment dossiers in order to be admitted.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the violation of enrolling students in the wrong subjects or standards according to regulations of competent authorities with the following fines:

  a) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for the violation of enrolling less than 3 students in the wrong subjects;

  b) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for recruiting 3 to 5 students;

  c) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 for recruiting 6 to 10 students;

  d) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for recruiting 11 to 15 students;

  dd) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for recruiting 16 students or more.

  3. A warning or a fine for recruiting students for vocational secondary education beyond the prescribed quota, recruiting the wrong subjects or violating the prescribed standards shall be imposed at the following levels:

  a) A warning for recruiting less than 5% of the prescribed quota;

  b) A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for recruiting between 5% and 10% of the prescribed quota or recruiting less than 10 students who do not meet the prescribed subjects or standards;

  c) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for recruiting between 10% and 15% of the prescribed quota or recruiting between 10 and 20 students who do not meet the prescribed subjects or standards;

  d) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for recruiting more than 15% to 20% of the allocated enrollment quota or recruiting 21 to 30 learners who are not eligible or do not meet the criteria;

  dd) A fine of between VND 6,000,000 and 10,000,000 for recruiting more than 20% of the allocated enrollment quota or recruiting 31 or more learners who are not eligible or do not meet the criteria.

  4. A fine for recruiting for college, university and postgraduate education exceeding the allocated enrollment quota, recruiting ineligible or non-compliant learners according to regulations of competent authorities with the following fines:

  a) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for recruiting less than 5% of the allocated enrollment quota;

  b) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for recruiting between 5% and 10% of the allocated enrollment quota or recruiting less than 10 learners who are not eligible or do not meet the criteria;

  c) A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for recruiting between 10% and 15% of the allocated enrollment quota or recruiting 10 to 20 learners who are not eligible or do not meet the criteria;

  d) A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 40,000,000 for recruiting between 15% and 20% of the allocated enrollment quota or recruiting 21 to 30 learners who are not eligible or do not meet the criteria;

  dd) A fine of between VND 40,000,000 and 60,000,000 for recruiting more than 20% of the allocated enrollment quota or recruiting 31 or more learners who are not eligible or do not meet the criteria.

  5. A fine shall be imposed for the act of recruiting for training and awarding diplomas of the national education system beyond the assigned competence at the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of notifying recruitment (by any form) without permission of a competent authority;

  b) A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of receiving applications, organizing examinations or recruitment tests, or summoning candidates without assignment by a competent authority.

  6. A fine shall be imposed for the act of violating regulations of the State on recruitment for overseas training or training at foreign-invested educational establishments operating in Vietnam under the general education program, vocational secondary school, college, university, or postgraduate education program at the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for recruiting 1 to 5 learners;

  b) A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for recruiting 6 to 10 learners;

  c) A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for recruiting 11 to 15 learners;

  d) A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for recruiting 16 to 20 learners;

  d) Between VND 40,000,000 and VND 50,000,000 for the violation of recruiting 21 to 25 learners;

  e) Between VND 50,000,000 and VND 60,000,000 for the violation of recruiting 26 learners or more.

  7. Additional penalties for the violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscation of material evidence for the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Suspension of the right to use the practice license or certificate for a fixed period if the violation is committed for the first time and indefinitely if the violation is repeated for the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 6 of this Article.

  8. Remedial measures for the violations specified in this Article:

  a) Enforced cancellation of the incorrect decisions made due to the acts specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of the amounts of money collected from learners and bearing all costs for the return for the acts mentioned in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on entrance and graduation examinations of the national education system

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of disturbing or threatening to use force to prevent exam supervisors, exam observers, exam scorers or exam assistants from performing their duties.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 4,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of using force to prevent exam supervisors, exam observers, exam scorers or exam assistants from performing their duties.

  3. A fine of between VND 4,000,000 and 6,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Taking the exam on behalf of another person or taking the exam together with another person to assist him/her;

  b) Illegally transferring documents, information or items into the examination room for the examinee;

  c) Revealing the secret of the exam paper number.

  4. A fine of between VND 6,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of swapping exam papers or assisting another person to swap exam papers of the examinee in contravention of regulations of the competent authority.

  5. A fine of between VND 1,500,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations against the examination regulations:

  a) Issuing a decision on establishing the examination board or assigning tasks to examiners contrary to the regulations applicable to that examination;

  b) Losing or damaging the examination paper to the extent that it cannot be graded;

  c) Grading the entrance or graduation examination paper incorrectly according to the answer key and mark scale;

  d) Preparing a mark sheet that is inconsistent with the examination results.

  6. A fine of between VND 4,000,000 and VND 6,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of adding or modifying the contents of the examinee's examination paper in contravention of regulations.

  7. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of revealing the examination secret or losing the examination paper (but not to the extent of criminal prosecution).

  8. Additional penalties for violations prescribed in this Article:

  Confiscate the exhibits and means used to commit the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to the violations specified in Clause 4, Point d Clause 5 and Clause 6 of this Article;

  b) Enforced cancellation of the examination results of the violator (if the violator is a candidate) for the violations specified in Clauses 1 and 2 and of the beneficiaries for the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article;

  c) Enforced re-grading of the examination for the violations specified in Point c Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on assessment of learning outcomes of learners in general education institutions, vocational secondary schools and universities

  1. Warning or a fine for failure to fully comply with regulations of competent authorities on examination grading, assessment and ranking of learning outcomes that affect the results of subjects, semesters or academic years of learners shall be imposed at the following levels:

  a) Warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for violations against regulations on general education;

  b) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for violations in vocational secondary education;

  c) A fine of between VND 600,000 and 1,000,000 for violations in higher education.

  2. Remedial measures for violations prescribed in this Article:

  Enforced restoration of learning assessment results and learning classification in accordance with the actual situation of learners for violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on management, issuance and use of diplomas and certificates of the national education system

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to perform the responsibility of issuing diplomas and certificates or certifying the validity of issued diplomas and certificates;

  b) Issuing diplomas and certificates with incorrect contents, thereby obstructing users.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of using an illegitimate diploma or certificate.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to prepare or preparing an incomplete application as a basis for the issuance and management of diplomas or certificates;

  b) Providing a false certification or falsifying the application, leading to the issuance of diplomas or certificates to ineligible persons.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Issuing a decision or requesting a competent authority to issue a certificate illegally;

  b) Destroying or falsifying the application or documents used as a basis for the issuance or certification of the legitimacy of the certificate (but not to the extent of criminal prosecution).

  5. A fine of between VND 4,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Issuing decisions or requesting competent authorities to issue illegal diplomas;

  b) Destroying or falsifying dossiers and documents used as the basis for issuing or certifying the validity of diplomas (but not to the extent of criminal prosecution).

  6. Additional penalties for violations prescribed in this Article:

  Depriving the right to use practice licenses or certificates for a fixed term if the violation is committed for the second time and indefinitely if the violation is repeated for the violations prescribed in Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures for violations prescribed in this Article:

  a) Revoking diplomas and certificates for violations prescribed in Point b Clause 1, Clause 2, Point b Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 and Point a Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Compelling the restoration of legitimate rights and interests of users of diplomas and certificates for violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on use of teachers

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the use of teachers who do not meet the prescribed standards at the following fines:

  a) A warning for the use of preschool teachers who do not meet the prescribed standards;

  b) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 for the use of general education teachers who do not meet the prescribed standards;

  c) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 4,000,000 for the use of vocational high school teachers, non-formal education centers, foreign language centers, computer centers who do not meet the prescribed standards;

  d) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 for the use of college lecturers who do not meet the prescribed standards;

  dd) A fine of between VND 11,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 for the use of university and postgraduate lecturers who do not meet the prescribed standards.

  2. Remedial measures for the violations prescribed in this Article:

  Forcible suspension of teaching and educational activities for teachers who do not meet the prescribed standards and are used in the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 16. Violations against regulations on ensuring the proportion of native teachers and lecturers in educational establishments

  A fine shall be imposed for the violation of regulations of competent agencies on maintaining the proportion of native teachers and lecturers to the total number of teachers and lecturers in educational establishments at the following levels:

  a) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for violations at all levels of general education;

  b) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for violations at vocational secondary schools, non-formal education centers, foreign language centers, computer centers;

  c) A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 for the violation at a college training establishment;

  d) A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 for violations at undergraduate and postgraduate training establishments.

  Article 17. Violations against regulations on management of learners' dossiers

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for irresponsible management leading to the revision of transcripts, mark sheets or other documents related to the assessment of learners' learning results in contravention of regulations, causing consequences, with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for 1 to 2 violations committed by learners;

  b) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for 3 to 5 violations committed by learners;

  c) A fine of between VND 600,000 and 1,000,000 for 6 violations or more.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for failure to prepare or incomplete preparation of learner management dossiers in accordance with the Charter of the school.

  3. Remedial measures for violations prescribed in this Article:

  Forcible restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on use of textbooks, educational materials and provision and use of educational equipment

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 for issuing decisions contrary to regulations on purchase, receipt of gifts, use of textbooks, educational materials and educational equipment that do not ensure quality, causing adverse effects on teaching and learning.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of transporting and distributing textbooks, teaching materials and educational equipment contrary to the supply plan prescribed by competent authorities.

  3. Remedial measures for the violations prescribed in this Article:

  a) Forcibly exporting from Vietnam or re-exporting textbooks, teaching materials and educational equipment illegally imported due to the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Forcibly restoring the initial plan that has been changed due to the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on tuition fees, charges and other revenues from learners

  These violations shall be handled according to the provisions of Chapter II of the Government's Decree No. 106/2003/ND-CP of September 23, 2003 on the sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of charges and fees.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on compulsory expulsion

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for the act of deciding to impose compulsory expulsion on a learner contrary to regulations, with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for 1 to 2 learners;

  b) A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for 3 to 5 learners;

  c) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for 6 to 10 learners;

  d) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 for 11 learners or more.

  2. Remedial measures for violations prescribed in this Article:

  Enforced cancellation of incorrect decisions and restoration of learners' right to study for the acts prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on universal education

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 50,000 and 100,000 for obstructing students of all levels and grades from attending school.

  2. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of inciting students to skip school or drop out of school at all levels and educational stages.

  Chapter III

                                     DISPENSE AND PROCEDURES

  Article 22. Competence to impose administrative penalties in the field of education of the presidents of the People’s Committees at all levels

  1. The presidents of the People’s Committees of communes, wards and townships have the power to impose penalties as prescribed in Article 28 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their localities and management competence.

  2. The presidents of the People’s Committees of districts, townships and provincial cities have the power to impose penalties as prescribed in Article 29 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their localities and management competence.

  3. The presidents of the People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities have the power to impose penalties as prescribed in Article 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their localities and management competence.

  Article 23. Competence to impose administrative penalties of the Education Inspectorate

  1. Education inspectors on duty have the power to impose penalties as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against administrative violations in the field of education specified in Chapter II of this Decree.

  2. Directors of Departments of Education and Training of provinces and centrally-run cities have the power to impose penalties as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against administrative violations in the field of education specified in Chapter II of this Decree.

  3. Directors of the Ministry of Education and Training have the power to impose penalties as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations against administrative violations in the field of education specified in Chapter II of this Decree.

  Article 24. Authorization to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of education

  In case the persons competent to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of education specified in Article 22 and Clauses 2 and 3, Article 23 of this Decree are absent, authorization shall be granted in accordance with Article 14 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 25. Principles for determining the competence to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of education

  1. Presidents of People's Committees at all levels have the power to handle administrative violations in the field of education in their localities according to the competence specified in Articles 28, 29 and 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Education inspectors have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of education as prescribed in Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  In case the violation exceeds the competence of the Chief Inspector of the Department of Education and Training, the violation file shall be transferred to the President of the provincial People's Committee for handling according to his/her competence.

  3. In case an individual or organization commits multiple administrative violations at the same time, including violations in the field of education, the competence to impose penalties shall be determined according to the principles specified in Clause 3, Article 42 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  4. The competence to expel foreign individuals who commit administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with the provisions of Clause 1, Article 17 of the Government's Decree No. 21/2001/ND-CP of May 28, 2001, detailing the implementation of the Ordinance on Entry, Exit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam.

  5. In case a violation of the law on education is considered to show signs of a crime, the file shall be immediately transferred to the competent authority for settlement according to the provisions of Article 62 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 26. Procedures for imposing penalties for administrative violations in the field of education

  1. The procedures for making records and decisions on penalties shall comply with the provisions of Articles 19, 20 and 21 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The imposition of fines and the payment of fines shall comply with the procedures specified in Articles 24 and 25 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. The procedures for deprivation of the right to use decisions on establishment, licenses and practice certificates shall comply with the provisions of Article 59 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 11 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  4. Procedures for confiscation and disposal of material evidences and means used in administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with Article 60 and Article 61 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 31 of Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP dated November 14, 2003 of the Government detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 27. Compliance with decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education

  1. Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with the sanctioning decisions within 10 days from the date on which they are assigned the sanctioning decisions, except for cases specified in Article 65 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. The time limit shall be calculated as prescribed in Article 9 of Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP dated November 14, 2003 of the Government detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the field of education and fail to voluntarily comply with the decisions shall be subject to compulsory enforcement. Individuals and organizations subject to compulsory enforcement shall bear all costs for the organization of enforcement measures. Procedures for compulsory enforcement shall be prescribed in Articles 66 and 67 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                                 COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 28. Complaints, denunciations and settlement of complaints and denunciations

  1. Individuals, organizations or their lawful representatives have the right to lodge complaints against sanctioning decisions of competent persons specified in Articles 22, 23 and 24 of this Decree. Procedures for lodging complaints and settling complaints shall comply with Article 118 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. Competence and time limits for lodging complaints and settling complaints shall comply with the Law on Complaints and Denunciations. The lodging of complaints against decisions on sanctioning administrative violations shall not suspend the implementation of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education.

  2. Individuals have the right to denounce acts of lawlessness in handling administrative violations in the field of education by persons competent to impose penalties to competent state agencies. Procedures for complaints, denunciations and settlement of complaints and denunciations shall comply with Article 118 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 29. Handling of violations by persons competent to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of education

  A person competent to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of education who harasses, condones, covers up, fails to handle or fails to handle in a timely manner, improperly or exceeds his/her competence shall be handled in accordance with Article 121 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 30. Handling of violations by persons who are sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education

  A person who is sanctioned for an administrative violation in the field of education who commits acts of resisting persons on duty, delaying or evading compliance or commits other violations shall be handled in accordance with Article 122 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter V

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 31. Effect

  This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

  To annul the provisions on penalties for administrative violations in the field of education specified in Decree No. 18/2001/ND-CP of May 4, 2001, on the establishment and operation of foreign cultural and educational establishments in Vietnam and other previous regulations which are contrary to this Decree.

  Article 32. Responsibilities for guidance and implementation

  The Minister of Education and Training shall, within the scope of his/her functions, tasks and powers, provide guidance and organize the implementation of this Decree.

  The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies, the heads of the agencies attached to the Government, and the presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall implement this Decree.
