                                                   DECREE

     REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE LAWS ON DRYING AND TREATMENT OF WATERS

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;

  Pursuant to the Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting dated June 29, 2001;

  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002 and the Ordinance on Amendments to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated April 2, 2008;

  At the request of the Minister of Public Security;

  The Government promulgates the Decree on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting.

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, principles of sanctioning, sanctioning forms, sanctioning levels, remedial measures, sanctioning competence, sanctioning procedures, complaints, denunciations and settlement of complaints and denunciations about administrative sanctioning in the field of fire prevention and firefighting.

  2. Administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting or related to the field of fire prevention and firefighting not prescribed in this Decree but prescribed in other Government’s decrees on administrative sanctioning shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of those decrees.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting on the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall be sanctioned according to this Decree and other relevant provisions of law on administrative sanctioning.

  Article 3. Principles of sanctioning administrative violations

  The principles of sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting shall comply with Article 3 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 4. Forms of sanctioning administrative violations

  1. For each administrative violation in the fire prevention and firefighting sector prescribed in this Decree, the violating individual or organization shall incur one of the following principal penalties:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine.

  2. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the individual or organization that commits the administrative violation in the fire prevention and firefighting sector may also incur one or more of the following additional penalties:

  a) Deprivation of the right to use the practice license or certificate;

  b) Confiscation of the exhibits and means used for the administrative violation.

  3. Apart from the principal penalties and the additional penalties prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the individual or organization that commits the administrative violation in the fire prevention and firefighting sector may also incur one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced implementation of measures to remedy the environmental pollution caused by the administrative violation;

  b) Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to administrative violations or forced dismantling of illegal works or construction works;

  c) Enforced remediation of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations;

  d) Enforced reduction of the volume or quantity of hazardous substances and goods according to the prescribed norms;

  dd) Enforced relocation of hazardous substances and goods due to administrative violations to warehouses or locations as prescribed;

  e) Other remedial measures prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  4. Persons committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting that cause damage to organizations and individuals, besides being sanctioned for administrative violations as prescribed in Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article, shall also pay compensation for damage as prescribed by civil law.

  5. Foreigners committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting on the territory of Vietnam may be deported from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Deportation may be either the main sanction or an additional sanction. The competence, order and procedures for applying the sanction of deportation shall comply with the current law on deportation sanctions according to administrative procedures.

  Chapter II

                      ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AND SANCTIONS

  Article 5. Violations in promulgation, dissemination and organization of implementation of regulations and internal rules on fire prevention and firefighting

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to fully comply with internal rules and regulations on fire prevention and firefighting;

  b) Making internal rules, fire safety orders, warning signs, prohibitive signs, guidance signs on fire prevention and firefighting ineffective or faded, making it impossible to clearly see the guidance words and symbols;

  c) Equipping internal rules, fire safety orders, warning signs, prohibitive signs, guidance signs on fire prevention and firefighting in a non-compliant manner.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to arrange and post fire safety orders, warning signs, prohibitive signs, guidance signs on fire prevention and firefighting in accordance with regulations;

  b) Failing to comply with the fire prevention and firefighting regulations;

  c) Failing to disseminate the fire prevention and firefighting regulations to persons under their management;

  d) Issuing fire prevention and firefighting regulations that are incomplete or inappropriate to the fire and explosion hazards of the establishment.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of failing to arrange and post fire prevention and firefighting regulations as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of failing to issue or issuing fire prevention and firefighting regulations and regulations contrary to the State's legal documents.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to the administrative violation specified in Point b, Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced issuance, dissemination and posting of fire prevention and firefighting regulations in accordance with regulations for the acts specified in Point a, Point c, Point d, Clause 2, Clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 6. Violations against regulations on fire safety inspection

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to present dossiers and documents for fire safety inspection.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to fully or on time fulfill fire safety recommendations made in writing by competent authorities;

  b) Failure to appoint responsible persons to participate in fire safety inspection teams as prescribed;

  c) Failure to organize fire safety inspection as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to implement written guidance and direction on fire safety issued by competent authorities;

  b) Failing to implement fire prevention and firefighting recommendations made in writing by competent authorities;

  c) Obstructing inspection and examination activities in the field of fire prevention and firefighting of competent authorities.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for the following acts: after a facility is approved and tested for fire prevention and firefighting, the head of the facility fails to notify and sign a commitment in writing that the facility has fully met the requirements and maintained conditions to ensure fire safety during the use of the building, work and production and business activities in accordance with regulations.

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on fire safety management dossiers

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and VND 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to conduct statistics and report on fire prevention and firefighting activities in accordance with regulations;

  b) Failing to provide sufficient documents in the dossier for management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting activities in accordance with regulations;

  c) Failing to update information on changes related to fire prevention and firefighting activities in the dossier for management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting activities of the facility.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to prepare management and monitoring dossiers on fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in management, storage and use of dangerous fire and explosion substances and goods

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to keep records and dossiers on monitoring and management of dangerous fire and explosion substances and goods as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for storage, arrangement and placement of dangerous fire and explosion substances and goods beyond the quantity or mass or arranged without proper distance or in groups of dangerous fire and explosion substances and goods as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for use of equipment and means for storing dangerous fire and explosion substances and goods without a certificate of inspection or without ensuring fire and safety conditions as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for illegal storage of dangerous fire and explosion substances and goods.

  5. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for illegal use of dangerous fire and explosion substances and goods.

  6. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of dangerous goods and articles in respect of the acts specified in Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced construction and establishment of dossiers and books for monitoring and management of dangerous goods and articles in respect of the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced preservation, arrangement, reduction of quantity and mass of dangerous goods and articles in respect of the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Enforced relocation of dangerous goods and articles to warehouses or locations in respect of the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in production and trade of dangerous goods and articles

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to take measures or use means to prevent static charge accumulation in accordance with regulations;

  b) Failure to install natural ventilation or forced ventilation equipment as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to install equipment for detecting and handling leaks of hazardous substances and goods to the surrounding environment as prescribed;

  b) Failure to have a plan or equipment for handling incidents of ruptured tanks, storage equipment, pipelines for storing, containing and transporting oil, petroleum products and other flammable liquids;

  c) Mixing, distilling, loading hazardous substances and goods to the wrong place or mixing, distilling, loading hazardous substances and goods to storage equipment of wrong type or incompatible with hazardous substances and goods.

  3. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for manufacturing, trading, mixing, distilling, loading hazardous substances and goods without a permit as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 25,000,000 shall be imposed for manufacturing, trading hazardous substances and goods on the list of prohibited goods.

  5. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of dangerous goods and articles for fire and explosion in the acts specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Forcible remedial measures against the acts specified in Point a and Point b, Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on the transportation of dangerous goods and articles for fire and explosion

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Damaging or tearing up the permit for the transportation of dangerous goods and articles for fire and explosion;

  b) Failing to carry the permit for the transportation of dangerous goods and articles for fire and explosion as prescribed;

  c) Failing to remove the dangerous goods and articles for fire and explosion emblem from the means of transportation after the dangerous goods and articles for fire and explosion have been moved from the means of transportation;

  d) Losing the permit for the transportation of dangerous goods or articles liable to fire or explosion but having promptly notified the competent authority.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arranging dangerous goods or articles liable to fire or explosion on the means of transportation in contravention of regulations;

  b) Stopping or parking the means of transportation of dangerous goods or articles liable to fire or explosion in places not permitted in accordance with regulations.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to maintain all conditions for fire safety and firefighting when using the motorized means of transportation for the transportation of dangerous goods or articles liable to fire or explosion throughout the period of transportation permitted in accordance with regulations;

  b) Transporting other goods together with dangerous goods or articles liable to fire or explosion on the same means of transportation without permission in accordance with regulations;

  c) Transporting persons not having the duty on the means of transportation of dangerous goods or articles liable to fire or explosion.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Transporting dangerous goods and/or articles beyond the quantity, mass and/or type specified in the permit;

  b) Failing to affix the dangerous goods and/or articles transport mark on the transport vehicle as prescribed;

  c) Failing to comply with the fire safety conditions or failing to follow the guidance of the competent operator when loading, unloading, pumping, transferring dangerous goods and/or articles out of the transport vehicle as prescribed;

  d) Loading, unloading, pumping, transferring dangerous goods and/or articles at a location that does not meet the fire safety conditions as prescribed;

  dd) Loading, unloading, pumping, transferring dangerous goods and/or articles on the way to another vehicle without permission of the competent authority;

  e) Erasing the dangerous goods and/or articles transport permit;

  g) Losing the dangerous goods and/or articles transport permit without notifying the competent authority.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Transporting dangerous goods without a permit as prescribed;

  b) Using a fake permit to transport dangerous goods.

  6. Additional penalties:

  a) The permit to transport dangerous goods shall be revoked for the act specified in Point e Clause 4 of this Article;

  b) The dangerous goods shall be confiscated for the act specified in Point b Clause 1, Point a and Point b Clause 5 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) The dangerous goods shall be reorganized as prescribed for the act specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced reduction in quantity, volume, and types of hazardous substances and goods according to the provisions in Point a, Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Enforced relocation of hazardous substances and goods to warehouses or locations according to the provisions for the acts specified in Point a and Point b, Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in the use of fire and heat sources, fire and heat-generating equipment and tools

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arranging cooking or worship places without ensuring fire safety according to regulations;

  b) Carrying matches, lighters, mobile phones, electronic devices, or fire and heat-generating equipment and tools to prohibited places.

  2. A warning or a fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for the use of fire and heat sources, fire and heat-generating equipment and tools without ensuring the prescribed fire safety distance.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Using fire sources, mobile phones, electronic devices or other fire and heat-generating devices in places where they are prohibited;

  b) Soldering or cutting metals without fire safety measures as prescribed.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on fire safety in design, installation, management and use of electricity

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of not providing fire safety regulations in electricity use at establishments.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of using electrical equipment not in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to maintain the operation of emergency lighting systems;

  b) Changing the design or the structure and main parameters of electrical systems and electrical equipment without the approval of competent persons or agencies;

  c) Installation and use of electrical wires, cables, or electrical devices, protective or electrical consumption devices that do not ensure fire safety;

  d) Use of electrical devices that overload the design;

  dd) Use of electrical devices in places where it is prohibited.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Use of electrical devices that do not meet fire and explosion requirements as prescribed in dangerous fire and explosion environments;

  b) Lack of backup power supply as prescribed.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of not designing and installing electrical systems to meet fire safety and rescue requirements as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remediation of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on fire safety in design, construction, inspection and maintenance of lightning protection systems

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to keep records on lightning protection system monitoring as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to conduct periodic inspection of lightning protection system as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to remedy errors and damage that cause the lightning protection system to lose its effectiveness.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to install lightning protection system for buildings and structures subject to installation of lightning protection system as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remediation of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in investment and construction

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to take measures and means to ensure fire safety during construction.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to design fire prevention and firefighting systems for works that are required to have fire prevention and firefighting design.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for putting houses or works into use without ensuring one of the fire safety conditions for works that are not required to undergo fire prevention and firefighting approval according to regulations.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Construction or installation not in accordance with fire prevention and firefighting design approved by competent authorities;

  b) Failure to submit dossiers for re-approval when renovating, expanding or changing the use of houses or works during construction or use according to regulations.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Organization of construction of works subject to fire safety approval when there is no “Fire safety approval certificate” as prescribed;

  b) Manufacturing or modification of motorized transport means with special requirements for fire safety assurance which has not been approved by competent authorities.

  6. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for bringing houses or works into operation or use without conducting fire safety acceptance tests as prescribed.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced remedial measures to ensure fire safety for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced construction and installation of fire safety items according to the approved fire safety designs for the acts specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Enforced remedial measures to address unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations or forced organizations to conduct fire safety acceptance tests by state management agencies for the acts specified in Clause 6 of this Article.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on fire safety and fire prevention distances

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arranging or arranging materials and goods without ensuring the safe distance for fire prevention and fighting and fire prevention as prescribed;

  b) Failing to organize industrial sanitation, leading to the creation of an environment that is dangerous for fire and explosion.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of building fire walls, fire partitions or making fire doors and other fire prevention solutions that fail to meet the requirements as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Making ceilings, floors, partitions, roofs or gathering flammable materials in places not permitted as prescribed;

  b) Building houses in the forest or near the forest without ensuring the safety against fire spread as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to completely remove flammable substances in the corridor of the safety line of the pipeline, gas pipeline and petroleum products;

  b) Construction of works that violate the fire separation distance.

  5. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to build fire walls, fire partitions, fire doors and fire prevention measures as prescribed;

  b) Destruction of the fire prevention effect of fire walls, fire partitions, fire doors and fire prevention measures.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to the administrative violation specified in Point a, Clause 1, Point b, Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced dismantling of the illegal construction works for the acts specified in Point b, Clause 3, Point b, Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Enforced remedial action for the unsafe condition caused by the administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Point a Clause 3, Point a Clause 4, Point a Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 16. Penalties for violations against regulations on emergency exits in fire prevention and firefighting

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for the act of designing emergency exit doors that are not opened in the direction of escape, or installing mirrors in emergency staircases.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arranging, placing supplies, goods, means of transport and other items that obstruct the escape route;

  b) Removing, dismantling or damaging emergency lighting equipment, warning signs, guidance signs on the escape route;

  c) Failing to install warning signs, guidance signs on the escape route.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Incomplete rescue equipment and tools as prescribed;

  b) Incomplete ventilation and smoke exhaust equipment as prescribed for the exit;

  c) Incomplete lighting equipment for the exit or insufficient brightness as prescribed or ineffective;

  d) Incomplete exit doors, exits, or staircases in terms of quantity, area, width, or compliance with regulations.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for locking, jamming, or blocking exit doors.

  5. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and VND 25,000,000 shall be imposed for making exit doors ineffective.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedial measures for unsafe conditions caused by the administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 of this Article.

  Article 17. Violations against fire fighting plans of establishments

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to manage fire fighting plans as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Developing a fire fighting plan that fails to satisfy requirements as prescribed;

  b) Failing to disseminate fire fighting plans as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit fire fighting plans for approval as prescribed;

  b) Failing to participate in fire fighting plan practice as prescribed.

  c) Not to supplement or amend the fire fighting plan as prescribed;

  d) Not to fully practice the fire fighting plan in the approved fire fighting plan.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to formulate a fire fighting plan as prescribed;

  b) Failing to organize the practice of the fire fighting plan as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedial measures for the unsafe situation caused by the administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 18. Acts of violation against fire alarm information

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for the act of letting the fire alarm information equipment or devices fail or become ineffective.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to have fire alarm information equipment or devices as prescribed;

  b) Delaying or failing to report the fire in a timely manner; or reporting the fire incompletely.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Giving a false fire alarm;

  b) Failing to report the fire or obstructing or hindering the fire alarm information;

  c) The information service provider fails to promptly fix the malfunction of the fire alarm information receiving equipment of the fire prevention and firefighting police agency upon written request.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedial measures for the initial situation that has been changed due to the administrative violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on equipment, maintenance and use of fire prevention and firefighting equipment

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Moving or changing the position of installation of fire prevention and firefighting equipment according to the design approved by a competent authority;

  b) Obstructing the fire prevention and firefighting equipment.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to inspect and maintain the fire prevention and firefighting system and equipment periodically as prescribed;

  b) Incomplete or unsynchronized fire prevention and firefighting equipment as prescribed;

  c) Failure to equip common fire-fighting equipment for motor vehicles with 4 or more seats, motor vehicles used for transporting fire and explosion hazards as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Equipping, installing, or using fire prevention and firefighting equipment that has not been tested for quality, type, and model as prescribed;

  b) Equipping fire prevention and firefighting equipment that is not suitable for the fire and explosion hazards of the establishment as prescribed;

  c) Using fire-fighting equipment in a permanent fire-fighting position for other purposes;

  d) Failing to store enough fire-fighting water as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to equip common fire-fighting equipment for houses and works as prescribed;

  b) Destroying, damaging or rendering ineffective fire-fighting equipment.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to equip or install fire alarm and fire-fighting systems as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced remedial of unsafe conditions caused by the administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced remedial of initial conditions that have been changed due to the administrative violations specified in Point b Clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 20. Violations against fire-fighting regulations

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and VND 200,000 shall be imposed for entering the fire-fighting area without permission of competent persons.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for failing to rescue people, rescue property or fight fire in a timely manner.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to prepare forces, means, water sources and other conditions for firefighting as prescribed;

  b) Failing to arrange traffic routes, positions for access to buildings, works and other gaps for fire fighting vehicles and other fire fighting machinery to operate as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to obey orders of fire fighting commanders;

  b) Failing to obey orders of competent persons to participate in firefighting;

  c) Obstructing the operation of fire fighting forces and means;

  d) Refusing to participate in the remedial measures for the fire when requested by competent persons;

  dd) Failing to protect the fire scene as prescribed.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to organize the evacuation, rescue of persons or firefighting;

  b) Taking advantage of firefighting to harm the health, lawful property of citizens and the property of the State.

  Forcible remedial measures for the unsafe situation caused by the administrative violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to organize the dissemination of laws and knowledge on fire prevention and fighting as prescribed;

  b) Using persons as local firefighting forces, persons operating 4 or more-seater motor vehicles, persons operating motor vehicles transporting fire and explosion hazardous substances or goods without undergoing fire prevention and fighting training or without having obtained a certificate of having undergone fire prevention and fighting training as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to organize fire prevention and fighting training or refresher courses as prescribed.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcible restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to the administrative violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  a) Failure to ensure the number of personnel and the duration of a shift or watch in terms of fire safety;

  b) Failure to use fire fighting equipment at the facility.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  b) Failure to manage or maintain the operation of a fire fighting team at the facility or a specialized fire fighting team in accordance with regulations.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to participate in fire fighting activities when required by competent persons.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to establish a local or specialized fire prevention and firefighting team as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedial measures for the unsafe situation caused by the administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on production and business of fire prevention and firefighting equipment and fire prevention and firefighting design

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed on a fire prevention and firefighting design unit that is not legally qualified or lacks professional and technical competence as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed on an individual or organization that operates a fire prevention and firefighting equipment and device business without sufficient material facilities, professional and technical competence, or has not been trained or provided with fire prevention and firefighting professional training as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed on the conversion of fire fighting cars, fire fighting ships or boats without permission of competent authorities.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 shall be imposed on a construction or installation unit of fire prevention and firefighting facilities that is not legally qualified or lacks professional and technical competence as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedial measures for the unsafe conditions caused by the administrative violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 24. Violations against the regulations on mandatory fire and explosion insurance

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of conducting business in mandatory fire and explosion insurance without taking measures to prevent and limit losses for the mandatory fire and explosion insurance participants as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) A facility that is subject to mandatory fire and explosion insurance purchase but fails to purchase it as prescribed;

  b) Purchasing mandatory fire and explosion insurance in contravention of the rules and the premium rates for mandatory fire and explosion insurance issued by the Ministry of Finance;

  c) A facility that is subject to mandatory fire and explosion insurance purchase but fails to separate the mandatory fire and explosion insurance contract from the comprehensive insurance contract as prescribed.

  d) Not to deduct the compulsory fire and explosion insurance premium for contribution to fire prevention and firefighting activities as prescribed.

  Article 25. Acts of causing fire and explosion at households

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for unintentional violation of fire safety regulations, causing fire and explosion without causing damage or causing damage of less than VND 25,000,000.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentional violation of fire safety regulations, causing fire and explosion, causing damage of between VND 25,000,000 and 50,000,000.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentional violation of fire safety regulations, causing fire and explosion, causing damage of over VND 50,000,000 but not being investigated for penal liability.

  Article 26. Sanctioning individuals and organizations for causing fire and explosion

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for violation of fire safety regulations, causing fire and explosion, causing damage of less than VND 2,000,000.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentional violation of fire safety regulations, causing fire and explosion, causing damage of between VND 2,000,000 and less than VND 10,000,000.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentional violation of fire safety regulations, causing fire or explosion and damage of between VND 10,000,000 and under VND 25,000,000.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentional fire or explosion causing damage of between VND 25,000,000 and 50,000,000.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentional fire or explosion due to irresponsibility, but not reaching the level of criminal prosecution.

  6. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentional fire or explosion causing damage of over VND 50,000,000 without criminal prosecution.

  Article 27. Deportation

  Foreigners committing administrative violations prescribed in Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 18, 20 and 21 of this Decree may be deported from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam depending on the severity of their violations.

  Chapter III

                             JURISDICTION TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 28. Competence to sanction administrative violations of the People’s Public Security

  1. The People’s Public Security soldiers on duty shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 200,000.

  2. The Station Chief or Team Leader of the person specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 500,000.

  3. The Chief of the commune, ward or township People’s Public Security (hereinafter referred to as the commune level) shall have the power to:

  a) A warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 2,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Compulsory restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to the administrative violation.

  4. The Chief of Police of the district, district-level town, provincial city (hereinafter referred to as district-level) has the power to:

  a) Issue a warning;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 10,000,000;

  c) Suspend the right to use the professional practice license or certificate under his/her jurisdiction;

  d) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Apply the remedial measures prescribed in this Decree.

  5. The Head of the Fire Prevention and Fighting, Rescue and Safety Police Department, the Head of the Police Department for Social Order Management, the Head of the Police Department for Land and Railway Traffic, the Head of the Police Department for Waterway Traffic, the Head of the Police Department for Environmental Crime Prevention and Control, the Head of the Police Department for Investigation of Social Order Crimes, the Provincial Police Department and the Head of the Police Department for Fire Prevention and Fighting of the District Police Department under the Provincial Police Department shall, within the scope of their functions, tasks and powers, have the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 10,000,000;

  c) Suspend the license or certificate of practice within their competence;

  d) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Apply the remedial measures prescribed in this Decree.

  6. Directors of provincial-level police offices (hereinafter referred to as provincial-level police authorities) and directors of provincial fire departments have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Suspend the license or certificate of practice within their competence;

  d) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Apply the remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  e) Directors of provincial-level police authorities have the power to decide to apply the expulsion form of sanction according to the delegation of the Minister of Public Security.

  7. The Director of the Police Department of Fire Prevention and Rescue, the Director of the Police Department of Social Order, the Director of the Police Department of Road and Railway Traffic, the Director of the Police Department of Waterway Traffic, the Director of the Police Department of Environmental Crime Prevention and Control, and the Director of the Police Department of Investigation of Social Order within their respective functions, tasks and powers have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine up to the maximum level prescribed in Point a, Clause 2, Article 14 of the 2008 Ordinance on amendments to certain articles of the Ordinance on handling of administrative violations;

  c) Deprivation of the right to use the license or certificate of practice within the competence;

  d) Confiscation of goods and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Application of the remedial measures prescribed in this Decree.

  8. The Minister of Public Security shall decide to apply the form of expulsion.

  Article 29. Competence to impose penalties for administrative violations of the Presidents of the People’s Committees at all levels

  1. The Presidents of the People’s Committees of commune-level People’s Committees have the right to:

  a) A warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 2,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Compulsory restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to the administrative violation.

  2. The President of the district-level People’s Committee has the power to:

  a) Issue a warning;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations;

  d) Deprive the right to use the license or certificate of practice within their competence;

  dd) Apply the remedial measures prescribed in this Decree.

  3. The President of the People’s Committee of the province has the power to:

  a) Issue a warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to the maximum level prescribed in Point a, Clause 2, Article 14 of the 2008 Ordinance on amendments to a number of articles of the Ordinance on handling of administrative violations;

  c) Confiscate goods and means used for administrative violations;

  d) Deprive the right to use the license or certificate of practice within their competence;

  dd) Apply the remedial measures prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 30. Competence to impose administrative penalties of other agencies

  Apart from persons competent to impose penalties specified in Article 28 and Article 29 of this Decree, persons competent to impose penalties as prescribed in the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance on Amendments to the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, while performing their functions and tasks within their management areas and fields, may impose penalties as prescribed by law upon detecting administrative violations specified in this Decree.

  Article 31. Authorization to impose administrative penalties in the field of fire prevention and firefighting

  Persons competent to impose administrative penalties specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 Article 28; Clauses 1, 2 and 3 Article 29 of this Decree may authorize their deputies to exercise the competence to impose administrative penalties. The authorization must be made in writing. The authorized deputy shall be responsible for his/her decision to impose administrative penalties before the head and before the law.

  Article 32. Principles for determining competence to impose administrative penalties

  1. Competence to impose administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree is the competence to apply to an administrative violation. In case of fine, the competence to impose penalties shall be determined based on the maximum fine level specified for each specific violation, and in case of administrative violations under the competence of many persons, the first person to receive the violation shall impose the penalty.

  2. In case of imposing penalties on one person for committing multiple administrative violations, the competence to impose penalties shall be determined according to the following principles:

  a) If the forms and levels of penalties specified for each violation are all under the competence of the person imposing penalties, the competence to impose penalties shall still be under that person;

  b) If the form or level of sanction prescribed for one of the acts exceeds the sanctioning authority of the sanctioning person, that person shall transfer the violation to the competent authority for sanctioning.

  3. In case the competent sanctioning person of the People’s Public Security is handling a person or organization that commits a fire prevention and fighting violation under this Decree and discovers that such person or organization also commits an administrative violation prescribed in other Government’s Decrees, he/she may impose an administrative sanction for such act.

  Chapter IV

                         ORDER AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 33. Order and procedures for sanctioning administrative violations

  1. When detecting an administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and fighting, the competent sanctioning person shall order the immediate suspension of the violation act.

  2. The order and procedures for sanctioning administrative violations shall be carried out as follows:

  a) For administrative violations, if the sanctioning form is a warning or a fine of up to VND 200,000, the competent sanctioning person shall issue a sanctioning decision on the spot according to the simple procedures prescribed in Article 54 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the Ordinance on Amendments to a Number of Articles of the 2008 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  b) For administrative violations that are sanctioned with a fine of over VND 200,000, the person competent to impose the sanction shall make a record of the administrative violation in accordance with Article 55 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the Ordinance on Amendments to the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and issue a sanctioning decision in accordance with Article 56 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations; if the violation exceeds the sanctioning competence of the person making the record, he/she shall send the record to the person competent to impose the sanction to issue a sanctioning decision in accordance with regulations.

  c) The form for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting shall be uniformly applied according to the form prescribed in the field of security, order and social safety.

  3. When applying the fine, the specific fine level for an administrative violation is the average of the fine level for that violation prescribed in this Decree; in case of a violation with mitigating circumstances, the fine level may be reduced, but not below the minimum fine level; in case of a violation with aggravating circumstances, the fine level may be increased, but not exceed the maximum fine level prescribed. When imposing a fine, the fine level and specific fine level must be announced to the fined person.

  4. A minor aged between 16 and under 18 who commits an administrative violation and is sanctioned with a fine shall not be fined more than half of the fine level for an adult; if he/she has no money to pay the fine, his/her parents or guardian shall pay the fine on his/her behalf.

  Article 34. Application of measures to prevent administrative violations and ensure the sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting

  In case of timely prevention of administrative violations or to ensure the sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting, measures such as temporary detention of persons; temporary detention of material evidences and means of administrative violations; search of persons; search of transport means and objects; search of places where material evidences and means of administrative violations are hidden shall be applied in accordance with the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the Ordinance on Amendments to the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 35. Collection of fines for administrative violations

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting shall pay the fine on time at the State Treasury specified in the sanctioning decision, except for cases where the fine has been paid on the spot in accordance with Article 54 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the Ordinance on Amendments to the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and receive a receipt for the collected fine. The collection of fines must use receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance in accordance with regulations.

  2. In remote areas, on rivers and seas, where travel is difficult or outside working hours, individuals and organizations that are sanctioned may pay fines to the persons with sanctioning competence. The persons with sanctioning competence shall collect the fines on the spot and deposit them into the State Treasury in accordance with Clause 3, Article 58 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. The sanctioned persons may not pay the fines if they do not have the fine collection receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance in accordance with regulations.

  Article 36. Compliance with decisions on sanctioning of administrative violations

  1. Individuals and organizations that are sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting shall comply with the sanctioning decisions within 10 days from the date on which they are assigned the sanctioning decisions. If the individuals and organizations that are sanctioned do not voluntarily comply with the sanctioning decisions after the above-mentioned time limit, they shall be enforced to comply with the decisions in accordance with law.

  2. Individuals that are sanctioned for a fine of 500,000 VND or more may be granted a deferment of the fine decision in accordance with Article 65 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. When it is deemed necessary, the persons with sanctioning competence or the sanctioning agencies may publicly notify the administrative violations and sanctioning decisions to the agencies, organizations or local governments where the individuals commit the violations or reside and to the superior agencies directly managing or local governments where the violating organizations register their operations.

  Article 37. Deprivation of the right to use practice licenses and certificates

  The deprivation of the right to use practice licenses and certificates shall only be carried out for violations specified in this Decree and other Government Decrees on sanctioning of administrative violations related to fire prevention and firefighting and must comply with the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance on Amendments to the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 38. Transfer of dossiers of violations showing signs of a crime for criminal prosecution In cases where a violation is considered for sanctioning, if the violation shows signs of a crime or if a sanctioning decision has been issued, but the violation shows signs of a crime and the time limit for criminal prosecution has not expired, the dossier of the violation showing signs of a crime shall be transferred to the competent criminal procedure agency in accordance with Article 62 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 39. Complaints and denunciations

  1. Individuals and organizations that are sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting or their legal representatives have the right to lodge complaints against decisions on sanctioning administrative violations and decisions on application of measures to prevent and ensure handling of administrative violations by competent persons.

  All individuals have the right to denounce acts of violation of law on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting to competent state agencies.

  2. Complaints and denunciations and their settlement shall comply with the law on complaints and denunciations.

  3. Filing lawsuits against decisions on sanctioning administrative violations and decisions on application of measures to prevent and ensure handling of administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting shall comply with the law on administrative procedures.

  Chapter V

                                            IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 40. Effect

  This Decree takes effect from August 5, 2012 and replaces Decree No. 123/2005/ND-CP dated October 5, 2005 on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting.

  Article 41. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Minister of Public Security shall be responsible for organizing the implementation and guiding, inspecting and urging the implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of Government-attached agencies, Presidents of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities and relevant organizations and individuals shall be responsible for implementing this Decree.
