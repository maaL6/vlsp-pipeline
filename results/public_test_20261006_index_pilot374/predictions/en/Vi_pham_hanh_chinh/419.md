                Hanoi, June 26, 2003                                            DECREE




                                                   NUMBER 74/2003/ND-CP

     ON ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST THE LAW ON ELECTRICITY

                                                   THE GOVERNMENT

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;
  Pursuant to the July 2, 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;
  At the request of the Minister of Industry,

                                                  HEREBY PROMULGATES:

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  Administrative violations in the electricity sector are acts of intentionally or unintentionally violating regulations of law on state management in the electricity sector and electricity use which are not crimes and must be sanctioned administratively according to law, including:

  1. Violation of regulations on electricity operation licenses.

  2. Violations against regulations on construction, production, transmission and distribution of electricity.

  3. Violations against regulations on electricity supply.

  4. Violations against regulations on electricity use.

  5. Violation of regulations on protection of electrical works and safety corridors of power grids.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the electricity sector as prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  2. Foreign individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the electricity sector within the territory, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, except for cases where international treaties signed or acceded to by the Socialist Republic of Vietnam provide otherwise.

  3. Minors that commit administrative violations in the electricity sector shall be sanctioned according to Article 7 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 3. Principles for sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector

  1. The sanctioning of administrative violations in the electricity sector shall be carried out by persons competent as prescribed in Chapter III of this Decree in strict accordance with law.

  2. All administrative violations in the electricity sector must be promptly detected and immediately suspended. The sanctioning must be carried out swiftly, fairly and thoroughly; all consequences caused by administrative violations in the electricity sector must be remedied in strict accordance with law.

  3. One administrative violation in the electricity sector shall be sanctioned only once. A person that commits multiple administrative violations in the electricity sector shall be sanctioned for each violation. If multiple persons commit the same administrative violation in the electricity sector, each violator shall be sanctioned.

  4. The sanctioning of administrative violations in the electricity sector must be based on the nature and severity of the violation, personal circumstances and mitigating or aggravating circumstances to decide on the appropriate form and sanctioning measures. Mitigating and aggravating circumstances shall be applied according to Article 8 and Article 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  5. Administrative violations in the electricity sector shall not be sanctioned in cases of emergencies, legitimate defense, unforeseen events or administrative violations committed while suffering from mental illness or other diseases that cause loss of consciousness or the ability to control one's actions.

  Article 4. Statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector and the time limit for being considered not having been sanctioned for administrative violations in the electricity sector

  1. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector is one year from the date of commission of the administrative violation in the electricity sector; if the above-mentioned time limit is exceeded, the sanction shall not be imposed, but measures prescribed in Clause 3 Article 5 of this Decree shall still be applied to remedy the consequences caused by the administrative violation in the electricity sector.

  2. For individuals who are prosecuted or have a decision to bring the case to trial according to criminal procedures but later have a decision to terminate the investigation or terminate the case by competent authorities, if the violation shows signs of administrative violations in the electricity sector, they shall be sanctioned administratively; in this case, the statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector is three months from the date the competent sanctioning authority receives the termination decision and the violation dossier.

  3. Within the time limit prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, if individuals or organizations commit new administrative violations in the electricity sector or deliberately evade or obstruct the sanctioning, the statute of limitations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article shall not apply. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector shall be recalculated from the time of commission of the new administrative violation or the time of termination of the act of evasion or obstruction of sanctioning.

  4. Individuals or organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the electricity sector, if they have not committed a new violation within one year from the date of completion of the sanctioning decision or from the date of expiration of the statute of limitations for executing the sanctioning decision, shall be considered not having been sanctioned for administrative violations in the electricity sector.

  Article 5. Forms of sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector

  1. The main sanctioning form: for each administrative violation in the electricity sector, the violating individual or organization shall have to bear one of the following main sanctioning forms:

  a) Warning: applied to individuals and organizations that commit minor administrative violations for the first time, with mitigating circumstances, or for all administrative violations committed by minors aged 14 to under 16. Warning shall be decided in writing;

  b) Fine: based on the nature and severity of the violation, the fine level shall be decided within the fine range specified in this Decree for each violation. The specific fine for a violation in the electricity sector is the average of the fine range for that violation; if the violation has mitigating circumstances, the fine may be reduced but not below the minimum fine level; if the violation has aggravating circumstances, the fine may be increased but not above the maximum fine level.

  2. Additional penalties: depending on the nature and severity of the violation, individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the electricity sector may also be subject to one or more of the following additional penalties:

  a) Suspension of the electricity operation license for a fixed period;

  b) Confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit administrative violations in the electricity sector.

  3. Apart from the main penalties and additional penalties specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations that commit administrative violations in the electricity sector may also be forced to restore the initial state altered by administrative violations in the electricity sector or forced to dismantle illegal construction works.
  Chapter 2:
     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE ELECTRICITY SECTOR, PENALTIES AND PENALTY LEVELS

  Article 6. Violations against regulations on electricity operation licenses

  1. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using an electricity operation license that has expired.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Renting, lending or borrowing electricity operation licenses;

  b) Amending the contents of electricity operation licenses.

  3. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Conducting electricity operation without an electricity operation license;

  b) Conducting electricity operation after being suspended or having its electricity operation license revoked by a competent authority.

  4. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individual or organization shall be stripped of the right to use the electricity operation license for a period of six months to one year in case of violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 7. Violations of regulations on construction, production, transmission and distribution of electricity

  1. A fine of between VND 300,000 and VND 700,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Cutting, pruning or demolishing works in contravention of law when constructing, operating or repairing the electricity grid;

  b) Failing to restore the original state of works that have been changed due to the construction or repair of electricity works.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Violating regulations on ensuring safety for people and property during the renovation, upgrading, repair and construction of electricity works;

  b) Violating procedures and regulations in the production, transmission and distribution of electricity, causing safety hazards to people or damage to property.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Installing a power line or power station without permission from a competent authority;

  b) Violating the law on electricity investment and development;

  c) Obstructing organizations and individuals from performing repair and construction of electrical works.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of producing, transmitting or distributing electricity without the permission of a competent authority.

  5. Apart from the main penalties for the violations specified in this Article, the violating individuals and organizations in the electricity sector may also be forced to restore the original state that has been changed due to the violation of the provisions specified in Point b Clause 1 and Point a Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on electricity supply

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Inspecting organizations and individuals using electricity without carrying professional cards or without inspection decisions of electricity grid management agencies and units;

  b) Disconnecting or cutting electricity without proper contents as notified.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to make a handover acceptance record for the electricity user according to regulations after installing, replacing, repairing or adjusting the electricity metering system;

  b) Failing to send a person to inspect the meter after five days from the date on which the electricity seller receives the electricity buyer's request for meter inspection;

  c) Failing to notify and publicly post at electricity trading locations the regulations of law on rights and obligations of electricity buyers and sellers, on conditions for signing electricity trading contracts, on electricity prices and on procedures for stopping electricity supply;

  d) Failing to sign an electricity trading contract after ten days from the date on which the electricity buyer's household has ensured the conditions as prescribed and agreed upon the clauses in the draft contract.

  3. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Without work tickets or task assignment tickets and professional cards, arbitrarily perform one of the following tasks: repairing, connecting equipment, power lines, and power stations of the electricity industry or of organizations and individuals using electricity; removing, installing, and adjusting meters on the power grid;

  b) Failing to handle incidents two hours after receiving notifications from electricity buyers about power grid incidents managed by electricity sellers;

  c) Obstructing officials from inspecting and supervising electricity trading.

  4. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily using electricity works of buyers to supply electricity to other electricity users;

  b) Cutting off electricity supply to organizations and individuals not subject to the restriction on electricity use in case of electricity shortage, except for cases where there is a risk of serious incidents that endanger the safety of people and equipment.

  5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to sign electricity trading contracts within ten days after the electricity buyers have satisfied the conditions as prescribed and agreed upon the terms in the draft contracts;

  b) Cutting off electricity without notification according to regulations on order and procedures for stopping electricity supply.

  6. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Delaying repair of the power grid after an incident without a valid reason;

  b) Using electricity metering equipment that has not been inspected and sealed by a competent authority according to regulations or intentionally using a system with a metering error exceeding regulations.

  7. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of abusing their profession to harass electricity-using organizations and individuals for personal gain.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on electricity use

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for obstructing a competent person from inspecting electricity use.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 700,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) To install, cut, repair or replace electrical equipment and electrical works of the electricity supplier without authorization;

  b) To move or falsify the electricity metering system (including seals and wiring diagrams), electrical equipment and electrical works of the electricity supplier without authorization;

  c) To cause faults in the electricity system of the electricity supplier without authorization;

  d) To use equipment causing interference to damage or falsify data transmission equipment, measuring equipment and protection equipment of the electricity system.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for the act of stealing electricity in any form for the purpose of daily life and consumption:

  a) A fine of between VND 200,000 and VND 400,000 shall be imposed for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of less than 100 kWh;

  b) A fine of between VND 300,000 and VND 700,000 shall be imposed for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of between 100 kWh and less than 200 kWh;

  c) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of between 200 kWh and less than 300 kWh;

  d) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 3,000,000 for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of between 300 kWh and under 400 kWh;

  dd) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of between 400 kWh and under 500 kWh.

  4. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Unilaterally turning on electricity when the electricity-using works of the electricity buyer have not been commissioned or handed over or are in the period of being stopped from being supplied with electricity due to violations of regulations on electricity use;

  b) Using additional electricity sources without permission of the electricity seller other than the permitted electricity sources in case of using electricity for production, business or service;

  c) Unilaterally selling electricity to other organizations or individuals without the consent of the electricity seller;

  d) Using electricity for fishing, rat trapping, anti-theft, property protection, crops or other acts violating regulations on safety in electricity use.

  5. A fine for the act of stealing electricity in any form for other purposes than those prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article:

  a) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of less than 100 kWh;

  b) A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and 10,000,000 for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of between 100 kWh and less than 200 kWh;

  c) A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of between 200 kWh and less than 300 kWh;

  d) A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 20,000,000 for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of between 300 kWh and less than 400 kWh;

  dd) A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 for the act of stealing electricity with a quantity of between 400 kWh and less than 500 kWh.

  6. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individuals and organizations shall also have their exhibits and means used for the violation confiscated for the violations specified in Clause 2, Clause 3 and Clause 5 of this Article.

  7. Apart from the main and additional penalties for the violations specified in this Article, the violating individuals and organizations in the electricity sector may also be forced to restore the initial state that has been changed due to the violations specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on protection of safety of electrical works and safety corridors of the power grid

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 shall be imposed for climbing on or entering the power station without any duty.

  2. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for any of the following acts:

  a) Letting fly kites, balloons, playing remote-controlled aircraft or other flying objects within the protection zone of the power structure or the safety net corridor; forcing livestock, vehicles or tools onto the power poles or the fence of the power station;

  b) Installing antennas, clotheslines, billboards, advertising lights or other objects within the protection zone of the power structure, within the safety net corridor or at positions where falling or dropping of these objects may cause safety hazards to the power lines or other parts of the power structure, endangering human lives;

  c) Pruning trees, planting trees or trimming branches in violation of regulations within the protection zone of the power structure or within the safety net corridor;

  d) Pruning trees or trimming branches outside the protection zone of the power structure or outside the safety net corridor without taking safety measures to prevent trees from touching the power lines or other parts of the power structure.

  3. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 700,000 shall be imposed for acts of violating the safety of the foundation, fence of the power station, power poles or other structures and parts of the power structure.

  4. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following acts:

  a) Failure to install warning signs, signal lights, and hazard zone indications at the electrical works as prescribed or failure to install markers or signs along underground cables in land;

  b) Stacking or storing flammable and explosive substances outside the electrical works' protective zone or outside the power grid safety corridor, violating regulations on fire and explosion safety, thereby affecting the safety of the electrical works;

  c) Building tents, shops, houses, or other structures that affect drainage, block ventilation doors, or obstruct access to the power station.

  5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Stacking or storing flammable and explosive substances within the electrical works' protective zone or within the power grid safety corridor;

  b) Burning forests, slash-and-burn fields, or other substances, violating regulations on electrical works safety and power grid safety corridor protection;

  c) Using parts or structures of the electrical works for other purposes without permission from the competent electrical works management unit;

  d) Using tools or equipment with heights that violate the safe distance as prescribed by the law on high-voltage power grid safety protection;

  d) Removal, erasure, covering up, causing damage to the signs of prohibition, signs of warning, safety signals of electricity at the electricity poles, within the protection zone of the electricity works or the safety corridor of the power grid, power station, power plant;

  e) Causing damage to the electricity poles, power lines, insulating porcelain, lightning rods, grounding wires, power cables and other parts and structures of the electricity works with voltage of less than 1 kV.

  6. A fine of from 3,000,000 VND to 6,000,000 VND for the act of causing damage to the electricity poles, power lines, insulating porcelain, lightning rods, grounding wires, power cables and other parts and structures of the electricity works with voltage of 1 kV to 110 kV.

  7. A fine of from 4,000,000 VND to 8,000,000 VND for the act of causing damage to the electricity poles, power lines, insulating porcelain, lightning rods, grounding wires, power lines, power cables and other parts and structures of the electricity works with voltage of more than 110 kV.

  8. A fine of from 5,000,000 VND to 10,000,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a) Building, upgrading, renovating houses, making tents, shops or other works violating the regulations on safety of the electricity works and the safety corridor of the high-voltage power grid;

  b) Failing to ensure the safety conditions for the houses, construction works left in the safety corridor of the high-voltage power grid according to regulations;

  c) Carrying out works in the ground or dredging the riverbed, lake in the safety corridor of the underground power cable when there is no agreement with the electricity works management agency;

  d) Discharging waste and wastewater containing corrosive substances in the safety corridor of the underground cable line;

  dd) Setting off mines in violation of regulations on safety of power works and safety corridor of the power grid;

  e) Failing to ensure the safety distance as prescribed by law when designing, constructing or repairing power works;

  g) Using electricity as a direct protection means in violation of regulations permitted by competent authorities.

  9. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for digging pits, driving piles, mooring ships or other acts of violating the safety corridor of the underground cable line which has been marked with signs or signals.

  10. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for constructing harbors, houses and other works within the safety zone of power plants or the safety corridor of the underground cable line which has been marked with signs or signals.

  11. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individuals and organizations shall also have their exhibits and means used for the violation confiscated for the acts specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Point b Clause 4, Point a, Point d and Point e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7, Point c Clause 8, Clause 9 and Clause 10 of this Article.

  12. Apart from the main and additional penalties for the violations specified in this Article, the violating individuals and organizations in the electricity sector may also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of illegal construction works for administrative violations in the electricity sector specified in Point b Clause 2, Point c Clause 4, Point c Clause 5, Point a and Point e Clause 8 and Clause 10 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to violations of the provisions in Point c Clause 2, Clause 3, Point c Clause 4, Point c, Point dd and Point e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7 and Clause 10 of this Article.

  Chapter 3:

    JURISDICTION AND PROCEDURES FOR HANDLING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ELECTRICITY

  Article 11. Competence of chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels to impose penalties

  The presidents of the People’s Committees of all levels shall have the competence to sanction administrative violations in the electricity sector that occur in their localities under their management, as follows:

  1. The Chairperson of the People's Committee of the commune has the power to:

  a) A warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for administrative violations in the electricity sector valued at up to VND 500,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to administrative violations in the electricity sector.

  2. The President of the district-level People’s Committee has the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 20,000,000;

  c) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  d) Enforce the restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to administrative violations in the electricity sector.

  d) Enforced dismantling of illegal or unauthorized construction works that violate the law on electricity activities.

  3. The President of the provincial-level People’s Committee has the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Suspend the electricity operation license issued by the provincial-level People’s Committee;

  d) Confiscate the exhibits and means used for administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  dd) Enforced restoration of the initial state altered by administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  e) Enforced dismantling of illegal or unauthorized construction works that violate the law on electricity activities.

  Article 12. Power of electricity inspectors to impose penalties

  The Electricity Inspectorate of the Ministry of Industry has the authority to impose administrative penalties for administrative violations in the electricity sector nationwide. The Electricity Inspectorate of the Department of Industry of a province or centrally-run city has the authority to impose administrative penalties for administrative violations in the electricity sector that occur within its local jurisdiction. The authority of the Electricity Inspectorate to impose administrative penalties for administrative violations in the electricity sector is specified as follows:

  1. The electricity inspector on duty has the right to:

  a) A warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 200,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit administrative violations in the electricity sector valued at up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the initial state that has been altered due to administrative violations in the electricity sector.

  2. The Chief Inspector of the Electricity Department of the Industry Department has the right to:

  a) A warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 20,000,000;

  c) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  d) Compulsory restoration of the initial state that has been changed due to administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  dd) Compulsory dismantling of illegal or unauthorized construction works that violate the provisions of law in the electricity sector.

  3. The Chief Inspector of the Electricity Department of the Ministry of Industry has the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Deprive the right to use the electricity operation license granted by the Ministry of Industry and the People’s Committee of the province or centrally-run city;

  d) Confiscate the exhibits and means used for administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  dd) Enforce the restoration of the initial state altered by administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  e) Enforce the dismantling of illegal or unauthorized construction works that violate the provisions of law in the electricity sector.

  Article 13. The sanctioning power of other forces

  Apart from the persons specified in Articles 11 and 12 of this Decree, the following competent persons, upon detecting administrative violations in the electricity sector specified in this Decree within their management scope and localities, have the power to impose sanctions as follows:

  1. People’s police soldiers on duty shall have the power to sanction administrative violations in the electricity sector specified in:

  a) Point a, Clause 1, Article 7;

  b) Point c Clause 3 Article 8;

  c) Clause 1, Clause 3 and Clause 5 Article 9;

  d) Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, Point b and Point c Clause 4, Point a, Point b, Point c, Point dd and Point e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7, Point dd and Point g Clause 8, Clause 9 and Clause 10 Article 10.

  2. Construction inspectors shall have the competence to sanction administrative violations in the electricity sector specified at:

  a) Point a Clause 2 Article 7;

  b) Clause 3, Point a and Point c Clause 8 and Clause 10 Article 10.

  3. Environmental inspectors shall have the competence to sanction administrative violations in the electricity sector specified at Point d Clause 8 Article 10.

  Article 14. Authorization and principles of determination of competence to sanction administrative violations in the electricity sector

  1. The authorization to impose administrative penalties in the electricity sector shall comply with Article 41 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The principles for determining the competence to impose administrative penalties in the electricity sector shall comply with Article 42 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 15. Procedures for imposing administrative penalties in the electricity sector

  1. The procedures and order for imposing administrative penalties in the electricity sector shall comply with the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Documents related to the imposition of administrative penalties in the electricity sector must be fully kept at the sanctioning agency. The record of administrative violation shall be made according to the form prescribed in this Decree, except for cases handled under the simplified procedures prescribed in Article 54 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. Individuals and organizations that are fined must pay the fine within the prescribed time and at the place specified in the sanctioning decision, except for cases where the fine has been paid on the spot as prescribed in Article 54 and Article 58 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and receive the fine receipt. The collection of fines must use receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance as prescribed by current regulations.

  The Ministry of Finance shall assume the prime responsibility and coordinate with the Ministry of Industry in guiding the collection and use of fines for administrative violations in the electricity sector.

  4. The procedures for revoking the right to use the electricity operation license shall comply with Article 59 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  When applying the confiscation of material evidences and means of violation, the competent persons shall strictly comply with the provisions of Article 60 and Article 61 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 16. Execution of decisions on sanctioning and enforcement of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector

  1. Individuals and organizations that are sanctioned for administrative violations in the electricity sector under this Decree shall strictly comply with the sanctioning decisions within ten days from the date on which they are assigned the sanctioning decisions, unless otherwise provided by law. If the sanctioned individuals or organizations do not voluntarily comply with the sanctioning decisions, they shall be enforced to comply with the decisions under Article 66 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. When applying measures to enforce the decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector, the competent agencies and persons shall strictly comply with the procedures for enforcement under Article 66 and Article 67 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                          RESOLUTION OF COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 17. Complaints, denunciations and resolution of complaints and denunciations against decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the electricity sector

  1. Individuals and organizations that are sanctioned for administrative violations in the electricity sector or their lawful representatives have the right to lodge complaints or file lawsuits against the sanctioning decisions against them in accordance with law.

  2. All citizens have the right to denounce to competent state agencies administrative violations committed by individuals and organizations in the electricity sector and denounce violations committed by persons competent to impose administrative penalties in the electricity sector.

  3. Competence, procedures, order and time limits for handling complaints, denunciations and administrative lawsuits shall comply with the Law on Complaints and Denunciations and the Ordinance on Procedures for Handling Administrative Cases (amended) dated January 5, 1999.

  Article 18. Handling of violations by persons competent to impose penalties and persons sanctioned for administrative violations in the electricity sector

  1. Persons competent to impose administrative penalties in the electricity sector who commit acts of harassment, condoning or covering up violations, failing to handle or failing to handle them in a timely and adequate manner, exceeding their competence, or illegally appropriating or using money, material evidence or means of violation shall, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, be disciplined or investigated for penal liability, and if causing material damage, must pay compensation according to law.

  2. Persons sanctioned for administrative violations in the electricity sector who commit acts of opposing persons on duty, delaying or evading compliance with sanctioning decisions or other violations shall, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, be sanctioned for administrative violations according to the Government's regulations on sanctioning administrative violations in the security and order sector or investigated for penal liability, and if causing material damage, must pay compensation according to law.

  Chapter V

                                                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 19. Effect

  This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette. All regulations on penalties for administrative violations in the electricity sector that are contrary to this Decree are hereby annulled.

  Article 20. Implementation

  The Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, and Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall implement this Decree.
