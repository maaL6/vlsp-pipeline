  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Government dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Law on Plant Protection and Quarantine dated November 25, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Law on Veterinary Medicine dated June 19, 2015;

  At the proposal of the Minister of Agriculture and Rural Development; The Government promulgates the Decree amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 31/2016/ND-CP dated May 6, 2016 on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of plant varieties, plant protection and quarantine; the Government’s Decree No. 90/2017/ND-CP dated July 31, 2017 on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of veterinary medicine.

  1. Article 2 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  Article 2. Entities subject to administrative penalties

  1. Organizations and individuals that commit administrative violations related to plant varieties, plant protection and quarantine on the territory of Vietnam.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies that commit violations which are not within their assigned state management tasks;

  b) Economic organizations established under the Law on Enterprises, including private enterprises, joint stock companies, limited liability companies, partnerships and enterprise dependents (branches, representative offices);

  c) Economic organizations established under the Law on Cooperatives, including cooperatives, cooperative federations;

  d) Organizations established under the Law on Investment, including domestic investors, foreign investors and organizations with foreign investment; representative offices and branches of foreign traders in Vietnam; representative offices of foreign trade promotion organizations in Vietnam;

  dd) Social organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-political-occupational organizations, socio-occupational organizations;

  e) Public service providers and other organizations as prescribed by law.

  3. Households and individual businesses that violate the provisions of this Decree shall be sanctioned as individuals that violate the provisions of this Decree.”

  Article 25. Violations against regulations on trade in plant protection products

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations against regulations on trade in plant protection products:

  a) Trading in plant protection products together with other goods such as food, foodstuffs, beverages, animal feed, aqua feed, medicines, veterinary medicines;

  b) Trading in expired plant protection products; plant protection products of unsatisfactory quality or not conforming to relevant technical regulations, valued at less than VND 5,000,000;

  c) Trading in plant protection products when the certificate of eligibility for trading in plant protection products has expired;

  d) Failing to maintain all conditions for trading in plant protection products as prescribed in Article 63 of the Law on Plant Protection and Quarantine during business operations.

  c) Trading in plant protection products without a certificate of eligibility for trading in plant protection products;

  d) Trading in plant protection products not on the list of plant protection products permitted in Vietnam with a value of less than VND 5,000,000.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations of trading in plant protection products:

  d) Trading in plant protection products used for fumigation disinfection for persons without a certificate of practice for handling objects subject to plant quarantine or organizations without a certificate of practice for handling objects subject to plant quarantine;

  d) Providing incorrect instructions to buyers of plant protection products not in accordance with the contents on the label of the plant protection product;

  e) Trading in plant protection products during the suspension of trading activities, deprivation of the right to use or revocation of the certificate of eligibility for trading in plant protection products;

  g) Trading in plant protection products not on the List of plant protection products permitted for use in Vietnam with a value of between VND 5,000,000 and under VND 15,000,000.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations of trading in plant protection products:

  a) Trading expired plant protection drugs; drugs not guaranteed in quality and not conforming to corresponding technical regulations, valued at from VND 100,000,000 to under VND 200,000,000;

  a) Deprivation of the right to use the certificate of eligibility for trading plant protection drugs for 01 to 03 months for the violations specified in Point b, Point c Clause 4; Point b, Point c Clause 5 and Point b, Point c Clause 6 of this Article.
