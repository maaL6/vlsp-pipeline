                                                               CIRCULAR

   REGULATING THE USE OF LAND FOR CULTURAL, MEDICAL, EDUCATION AND SPORTS FACILITIES

  Pursuant to the Law on Land dated November 29, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 21/2013/ND-CP dated March 4, 2013 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment;

  At the request of the Director General of the General Department of Land Administration and the Director of the Department of Legal Affairs, the Minister of Natural Resources and Environment promulgates the Circular on land use norms for construction of cultural facilities, medical facilities, educational and training facilities and sports facilities.

  Article 1. The Circular on land use norms for construction of cultural facilities, medical facilities, educational and training facilities and sports facilities is issued together with this Circular.

  Article 2. This Circular takes effect from March 30, 2017.

  Article 3. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Governmental agencies, People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities, heads of units affiliated to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment and relevant organizations and individuals shall implement this Circular.

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular prescribes land use norms for construction of cultural facilities, medical facilities, educational and training facilities, sports facilities and cultural works on land classified as community living land (excluding cultural facilities, medical facilities, educational and training facilities and sports facilities managed by the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Public Security).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  These norms apply to state management agencies, specialized agencies in natural resources and environment, relevant organizations and individuals in preparation, appraisal and approval of land use planning and plans at provincial and district levels; appraisal of land use needs when allocating land, leasing land, repurposing land for construction of new or expansion of land use of cultural, medical, educational and training facilities, sports facilities and cultural works on land classified as community living land.

  Article 3. Interpretation of terms

  1. Land use norm per work: means the area of land required for a work to fully utilize its functions and use the land effectively for a specific purpose.

  2. Land use norm per capita: means the area of land calculated in m2 per person for a person to fully utilize his/her abilities in a field and use the land effectively for a specific purpose. The land use norm per capita is prescribed according to administrative divisions of commune, district and province. The land use norm per capita is calculated according to the total population of each administrative division.

  3. Social cultural center; village cultural house; village hall, village office, village club: means cultural works of the type of land for community living as prescribed in Circular No. 28/2014/TT-BTNMT dated June 02, 2014 of the Minister of Natural Resources and Environment on statistics, land inventory and mapping of current land use.

  Chapter III

                                          APPLICATION OF LAND USE QUOTAS

  Article 8. Land use quotas according to works shall specify the minimum and maximum land area for a specific type of work corresponding to each administrative level; in case of works of a higher administrative level on the same administrative level, the land area corresponding to the work shall be added to the land area of the work of the higher administrative level.

  Article 9. Land use quotas per capita shall specify the minimum and maximum land area for a person in a specific area corresponding to each administrative level; in case of works of a higher administrative level on the same administrative level, the land area corresponding to the work shall be added to the land area of the work of the higher administrative level. Land use quotas per capita shall be used to determine the total land area of a type of land for each administrative unit.

  Article 10. Depending on the conditions of each locality, one or more works may be built, but the total land area of each type of work in the locality shall not exceed the maximum land area determined according to the population scale of the locality.

  Article 11. In case a unit of administrative level has a low population scale and the total land area of a type of land calculated per capita of that unit of administrative level is too small, the land area of the work shall be allowed to be applied as the minimum land area of the land use quota according to the work.

  Article 12. During the implementation, if there are difficulties and problems, agencies, organizations and individuals shall promptly report them to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment for consideration and settlement.
