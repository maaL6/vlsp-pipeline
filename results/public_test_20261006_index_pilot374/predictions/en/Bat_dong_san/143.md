  Article 2. Based on this Resolution, the People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall:

  1. Organize the adjustment of land use planning until 2020 and the final land use plan (2016 - 2020), including the review and adjustment of planning of sectors, fields and localities in the province that use land to be consistent with the adjustment of land use planning until 2020 and the final land use plan (2016 - 2020) of the province; the organization of the adjustment of land use planning and plan must be strictly consistent with the requirements for socio-economic development, ensuring national defense and security in the province.

  2. Determine the boundaries and publicly disclose the area of rice cultivation land, protective forest land, and special-use forest land that needs strict protection; regulate the allocation of resources, first of all, the state budget, to ensure benefits between areas with conditions for industrial, urban and service development and areas that protect rice cultivation land and forest land; have appropriate policies and measures to reduce production costs, increase income, so that rice growers can produce with confidence; increase investment in social infrastructure and technical infrastructure, ensuring consistency and synchronicity between areas.

  3. Continue to direct the preparation and approval of the adjustment of land use planning until 2020 and the annual land use plan at the district level according to land law regulations as the basis for land expropriation, land allocation, land lease, and land repurposing to ensure consistency with the adjustment of land use planning and plan of the province.

  4. Take responsibility before the Government and the Prime Minister for repurposing forest land for other purposes, minimize the repurposing of forest land, and review and inspect the current use of protective forest land, special-use forest land, and production forest land that is natural forest before repurposing land for construction projects and projects as prescribed; for construction projects and projects that use protective forest land, special-use forest land, and production forest land that is natural forest, which must be reported to the Government and the Prime Minister, send reports to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment to assume the prime responsibility and coordinate with the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development to appraise and submit to the Prime Minister before deciding on the repurposing of land as prescribed.

  The People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall direct, inspect, and take responsibility before the Government and the Prime Minister for the efficient and effective use of land in compliance with the law.

  5. Strengthen the work of propagating land law so that land users fully understand the law, use land for the right purpose, efficiently, economically, protect the environment, and achieve sustainable development.

  6. Land management and use must comply with land use planning and plans, especially for land repurposed for economic development and economic restructuring towards service and urban development to increase budget revenue; promote land use auction. Encourage the efficient and economical use of land, address the problem of abandoned land that has been allocated and leased. Organize the management and supervision of land funds allocated for urban development, economic zones, industrial parks, industrial clusters, non-agricultural production and business land in order to use land funds efficiently and effectively, address the imbalance in land use structure between residential land and land for construction of technical infrastructure, social infrastructure and environmental protection requirements.

  7. Strengthen inspection and examination of land management and use to prevent and promptly handle violations in the implementation of land use planning and plans; strictly refuse to handle land allocation, land lease, land repurposing, land expropriation in cases not included in land use planning and plans; inspect and handle cases of land allocation and land lease but not used or used for improper purposes according to land law regulations.

  8. Strengthen investigation and assessment of land quality and potential; assess land degradation and pollution; improve the land information database system; train human resources; research and apply high science and technology in land management, improve the quality of forecasting, preparation, appraisal, management, organization of implementation and supervision of land use planning and plans.

  9. Strengthen forecasting, information and communication to enable local authorities, sectors and people to proactively respond to the impact of climate change.

  10. Annually, the People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall send reports on the implementation of land use planning and plans to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment for consolidation and submission to the Government for submission to the National Assembly as prescribed.

  Article 3. The Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment shall assume the prime responsibility and coordinate with relevant agencies in directing and inspecting the conversion of protective forests, special-use forests, and production forests into natural forests according to approved planning and law provisions.

  Article 4. The President of the People’s Committee of Dak Nong province and heads of relevant ministries and sectors shall be responsible for implementing this Resolution.
