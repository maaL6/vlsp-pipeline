                                                         DECISION

    ISSUED IS THE PLAN FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PRIME MINISTER'S DECREE NO. 11/CT-TTG DATED APRIL 23, 2019 ON SOME SOLUTIONS TO PROMOTE STABLE AND STRONG DEVELOPMENT OF THE REAL ESTATE MARKET

                                                   THE MINISTER OF CONSTRUCTION

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 81/2017/ND-CP dated July 17, 2017, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Construction;

  Pursuant to the Prime Minister’s Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019, on a number of solutions to promote the stable and healthy development of the real estate market;

  At the request of the Director of the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management,

                                                         DECIDES:

  Article 1. To promulgate, together with this Decision, the Plan for implementation of the Prime Minister’s Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019, on a number of solutions to promote the stable and healthy development of the real estate market.

  Article 2. This Decision takes effect from the date of signing.

  Article 3. The Chief of the Office of the Ministry of Construction, the Director of the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management, the heads of the agencies and units assigned to perform the tasks shall be responsible for implementing this Decision.

                                                          PLAN

     IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PRIME MINISTER'S DIRECTIVE NO. 11/CT-TTG DATED APRIL 23, 2019 ON SOME SOLUTIONS TO PROMOTE STABLE AND STRONG DEVELOPMENT OF THE REAL ESTATE MARKET
      (Annexed to Decision No. 398/QD-BXD dated May 22, 2019 of the Minister of Construction)

  I. Objectives

  - Implement Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019 of the Prime Minister on a number of solutions to promote the stable and healthy development of the real estate market (hereinafter referred to as Directive No. 11/CT-TTg) in a synchronized, timely and effective manner.

  - Strengthen the work of improving the institutional framework for housing management and real estate market in a timely and appropriate manner to the actual situation; effectively implement social housing programs: provide housing for persons with meritorious service to the revolution; provide housing for poor households in rural areas, central flood-prone areas, and the Mekong Delta; provide housing for low-income urban residents, industrial park workers, and students; and enhance the guidance on inspection and supervision of housing management and real estate market.

  II. Contents of the plan

  1. Regarding the tasks of completing the Law on amendments to the Law on Construction, the Law on Housing, and the Law on Real Estate Business:

  1.1. Study and improve the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Construction.

  - Leading unit: Department of Construction Operations;

  - Coordinating unit: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant Departments and Divisions;

  - Deadline: Presented to the Government in July 2019; finalized for presentation to the National Assembly in October 2019.

  1.2. Study and finalize the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Housing regarding housing support policies for officials, civil servants and public employees facing housing difficulties under Resolution No. 26-NQ/TW of the XIIth Central Executive Committee;

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant Departments and Divisions;

  - Deadline: Expected in 2020 (after the Politburo approves the housing project for officials, civil servants and public employees in the political system and issues a resolution on this issue).

  1.3. Study and finalize the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Real Estate Business

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant Departments;

  - Deadline: According to the legislative program of the National Assembly.

  2. For the task of researching and promulgating amendments to regulations and standards for construction of apartment buildings, tourist apartments (condotel), tourist villas (resort villa), office buildings with short-term accommodation (office tel), boarding houses and rental rooms.

  - Leading unit: Department of Science, Technology and Environment;

  - Implementing unit: National Institute of Architecture;

  - Deadline: Completed in 2019.

  3. For the task of researching and proposing the promulgation of legal documents on regulations on management and operation of office buildings with short-term accommodation (office tel).

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Department of Legal Affairs and related units;

  - Deadline: Q3/2019.

  4. For the task of urging localities to complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  4.1. Urging localities to complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Information Center and Institute of Construction Economics;

  - Deadline: Regular task.

  4.2. Coordinate with ministries and branches in the process of collecting relevant information to improve the housing and real estate market information system

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: General Statistics Office - Ministry of Planning and Investment;

  - Completion time: Routine task.

  5. For the task of researching, amending policies and laws and accelerating the development of social housing

  5.1. Research, amend and supplement Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing;

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Department of Legal Affairs and related units;

  - Time limit: after the Law on amendments to the Law on Housing is passed by the National Assembly.

  5.2. To urge and guide localities to focus on promoting the development of social housing in accordance with the Prime Minister's directive in the Directive No. 03/CT-TTg dated January 25, 2017.

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Local Department of Construction and related units;

  - Time limit: monthly.

  5.3. To inspect and review urban land planning and exploitation and use for development of social housing in some key localities, synthesize the situation and report to the Prime Minister.

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Local Department of Construction and related units;

  - Deadline: September 2019.

  6. To coordinate with ministries, sectors and local authorities in strengthening inspection and requesting project developers of urban areas and housing development projects to implement construction of technical infrastructure, social infrastructure and fire prevention and firefighting systems in projects and connect with common infrastructure of the area before handing over houses to people according to regulations of construction law, housing law and relevant laws.

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Construction Inspectorate, Department of Urban Development and related units;

  - Deadline: Within 2019.

  7. To regularly monitor and synthesize the situation periodically or irregularly to report to the Government and the Prime Minister on the development of the real estate market in order to take timely measures to stabilize the market when there are abnormal signs or major fluctuations.

  - Leading unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market;

  - Coordinating unit: Institute of Construction Economics and related units;

  - Time limit for completion: quarterly and ad-hoc as required.

  III. Implementation organization

  1. Assignment of tasks:

  The Minister of Construction shall lead, direct and generally manage the implementation of the Plan on a number of solutions to promote the stable and healthy development of the real estate market;

  The Deputy Ministers shall, according to their working fields, be assigned to direct units to implement the contents of the Plan;

  The heads of units affiliated to the Ministry shall, based on the implementation plan of the Ministry, organize the implementation of assigned tasks, ensuring timeliness and effectiveness; clearly assign tasks to leaders and specialists responsible for implementation of each specific task of the unit; focus on directing, intensifying inspection and supervision of the progress and results of implementation of the Plan; report on the implementation of the Plan according to assigned functions and tasks to the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management for consolidation and reporting to the Ministry leadership.

  The Director of the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management shall take charge and coordinate with the Ministry Office in monitoring, consolidating and reporting on the implementation results of the Plan; promptly report to the Ministry leadership for handling difficulties and obstacles in the implementation process.

  2. Implementation funding

  The Planning and Finance, Science and Technology and Environment Departments shall balance and allocate funding for the implementation of tasks according to the estimates of the units assigned to perform the tasks from the annual sources of economic and scientific and technological services and other lawful funding sources as prescribed.

  The units assigned to perform the tasks shall prepare estimates for implementation and send them to the Planning and Finance Department and the Science and Technology and Environment Department for reporting to the Ministry leadership for consideration and approval.

  During the implementation of the Plan, if it is necessary to amend or supplement specific tasks, the units shall proactively propose and report to the Minister for consideration and decision.
