                                                    CUNG DIENT

   ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GOVERNOR’S DECISION NO. 25/2023/QD-TTG DATED OCTOBER 3, 2023 ON REDUCING LAND RENTS FOR 2023

                                 THE GENERAL DIRECTOR OF THE GENERAL DIRECTORATE OF TAXES

                        - Provincial/municipal Tax Departments;
                        - The divisions and units affiliated to the General Department of Taxation.
  On October 3, 2023, the Prime Minister issued Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg on reduction of land rent in 2023. To promptly implement the contents of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister, the General Department of Taxation proposes the Director of Taxation Department of provinces and centrally-run cities to direct the Taxation Branches and Sub-branches to urgently carry out the following tasks based on the contents of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister:

  1. Tax authorities at all levels shall widely and thoroughly disseminate the main contents of the policy on reduction of land rent as prescribed in Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg on reduction of land rent in 2023 to organizations, units, enterprises, households and individuals currently being directly leased land by the State under land lease decisions or land lease contracts or certificates of land use rights, ownership of houses and other property on land of competent state authorities in the form of annual land rent payment, specifically:

  - At each tax management area, tax authorities shall actively cooperate with press agencies, radio stations, television stations, branches/representative offices of VCCI, etc. to organize the dissemination of contents of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg.

  - Develop guidelines for implementation of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg, publish guidelines on the website of the Tax Department and send guidelines via email to organizations, units, enterprises, households and individuals that are directly leased land by the State under land lease decisions or land lease contracts or certificates of land use rights, ownership of houses and other property on land of competent state agencies in the form of annual payments.

  - Information dissemination must be regular and continuous in many forms, ensuring that taxpayers can access and clearly understand the scope, subjects, time limits, procedures, and benefits of implementing the land rent reduction policy, so that taxpayers can correctly and promptly implement the state's support policies and overcome financial difficulties.

  2. Processing of applications for reduction of land rent according to the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg.

  Based on the 2023 land rent reduction application of the land lessee according to Article 4 of the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg submitted by the taxpayer, the tax authority shall determine the amount of land rent to be reduced and issue a decision on reduction of land rent according to the law on collection of land rent. At the same time, it shall compile the situation of land rent reduction according to the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg in the province/city, update information on land rent reduction of the taxpayer, the amount of land rent to be paid in 2023 and the equivalent late payment amount (if any) on the system. In case the land lessee has paid land rent for 2023 and after the competent authority determines and decides to reduce the land rent, there is an excess amount of land rent, the excess amount shall be deducted from the land rent of the next period or the following year according to the law on tax administration and other relevant laws. In case there is no next period of land rent payment, the excess amount shall be offset or refunded according to the law on tax administration and other relevant laws.

  3. The head of the tax authority shall assign relevant departments within the tax authority to update, search, review, inspect applications, and urge implementation according to law. In case of detecting cases where taxpayers do not meet the conditions for land rent reduction according to regulations, they shall notify the taxpayers to pay according to the current law on land rent (not according to the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg).

  4. The Tax Department shall compile a report on the implementation of the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg (according to the attached appendix/form), specifically: implementation situation, amount of land rent reduced, number of applications, number of applications approved for reduction, number of outstanding applications (if any) and reasons for non-resolution of reduction, problems and proposals (if any) to the General Department of Taxation before April 30, 2024 at the address of the General Department of Taxation, No. 123 Lo Duc, Hai Ba Trung, Hanoi, and send a digital copy via email: nbngoc01@gdt.gov.vn.

  The General Department of Taxation requests the heads of Tax Departments to strictly implement. In case of arising practical problems, please promptly report to the General Department of Taxation for guidance./.
