                                                      DIRECTIVE

     ON SOME SOLUTIONS TO PROMOTE THE REAL ESTATE MARKET TO BE DEVELOPED SAFELY, STRENGTHENED AND STABLE

  In recent times, the world’s political and economic situation has been complex, unstable and unpredictable, affecting Vietnam’s economy, which is highly open. In this context, the Government has issued many directives to stabilize the macroeconomy, control inflation, maintain major balances, and help the economy recover and achieve a relatively high growth rate compared to other countries in the region and the world.

  On October 11, 2021, the Government issued Resolution No. 128/NQ-CP on safe and flexible adaptation and effective control of the COVID-19 epidemic; Resolution No. 01/NQ-CP dated January 8, 2022 on the main tasks and solutions for implementing the socio-economic development plan and the state budget estimate for 2022; Resolution No. 02/NQ-CP dated January 10, 2022 on the main tasks and solutions for improving the business environment and enhancing national competitiveness in 2022; Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP dated January 30, 2022 on the Resolution on the socio-economic recovery and development program and the implementation of Resolution No. 43/2022/QH15 of the National Assembly on fiscal and monetary policies to support the program. In particular, the Central Executive Committee of the XIIIth Party Congress issued Resolution No. 18-NQ/TW on continuing to innovate and improve institutions and policies, enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of land management and use, and create incentives for our country to become a high-income developed country.

  The active and dedicated implementation of the Party's and State's resolutions, legal regulations, and the Government's direction, management, and policies on the real estate market by all levels, sectors, localities, and business communities has brought many positive changes to the socio-economic situation, ensuring social security and macroeconomic stability.

  The real estate market plays a very important role in maintaining macroeconomic stability, controlling inflation, ensuring major balances, and promoting growth. However, apart from the positive results, the development of the real estate sector has also revealed many shortcomings and limitations, which are not yet truly sustainable and still pose many risks, such as: an unreasonable structure of real estate goods, high prices of real estate products, especially housing prices, compared to people's incomes, and an inconsistent, incomplete, unreliable, and untransparent database of real estate market information.

  Some existing problems and inadequacies that need to be addressed are: the legal system related to the real estate market has some contents that have not yet met practical needs and have not been supplemented or amended in a timely manner; the development of real estate projects in some localities does not follow planning and plans and is not suitable for market needs; the order and procedures for investment are still stipulated in many different legal documents and through many steps, so the time for investment procedures is prolonged; the regulation on real estate transactions not required to go through the floor has created a basis for the formation of “phantom projects” and fraud cases against customers, on the other hand, the state also faces many difficulties in managing information on the real estate market, and at the same time, there is a situation of tax collection failure and failure to collect taxes for the state budget; the state management is not strict, not timely, and does not meet the management requirements....

  To overcome the above problems and inadequacies and continue to promote the safe, healthy and sustainable development of the real estate market in the future, the Prime Minister requests:

  I. SOME GENERAL POINTS OF VIEW AND SOLUTIONS

  1. Review legal regulations to solve both immediate and long-term problems in the direction of building healthy, appropriate and effective policy tools to ensure the interconnection, safety and certainty between the capital market and the real estate market, diversify the capital source for the real estate market; promote decentralization and delegation to various levels, thoroughly study and propose pilot mechanisms and policies to create new incentives and impetus for market development.

  2. Persistently, firmly and resolutely develop a safe, healthy and sustainable real estate ecosystem; do not excessively tighten credit, but increase inspection and supervision, do not relax state management; do not criminalize civil and economic relations; actively detect, prevent and strictly handle violations in accordance with law, pay attention to tax evasion in real estate business, do not let it collapse, protect those who do the right thing, protect and harmonize the interests of the state, people and enterprises.

  3. Harmonize, rationally and effectively combine fiscal policies and monetary policies, implement flexible, safe and prudent monetary policies, reasonable fiscal expansion policies, focus on ensuring effectiveness, strictly manage prices and markets; do not manage policies “by surprise”, do not abruptly switch from “loosening” to strict control, or vice versa.

  4. Ministries, sectors and localities should focus on synchronizing the implementation of tasks and solutions in the Government’s Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP on the program for economic and social recovery and development.

  5. Focus on implementing solutions to attract investment, promote production and business, create many jobs in many fields and localities; stabilize the macroeconomy, control inflation, and ensure major economic balances.

  6. To make specific and accurate assessment of the situation and supply and demand for real estate market development based on respect for market laws and competition laws, play the management role of the state, policy instruments to intervene and control when necessary in a proper, timely and effective manner, prevent market manipulation, hoarding, price gouging, etc.

  7. To identify market segments and needs, such as industrial real estate, worker housing, low-income housing, to have appropriate policies to promote the market, focus on social security, create opportunities for low-income people, “not sacrificing social progress and equity for the sake of economic growth”.

  8. To develop strategic infrastructure to create new development space, new development motivation, promote the formation of new industries, services, urban areas.

  9. To strengthen inspection, examination, audit to resolve difficulties, handle violations to protect the market, protect property, protect people, not to legitimize violations but to objectively assess, find the cause, have a mechanism to resolve practical difficulties.

  10. To do well the communication work, have appropriate and effective forms, promote education, training of financial knowledge, legal knowledge for the people, in schools and by other forms.

  II. SOME SPECIFIC TASKS AND SOLUTIONS

  1. Ministry of Construction

  a) To urgently study and perfect the legal system related to real estate to ensure synchronicity, resolve difficulties and obstacles, create conditions for the stable and healthy development of the real estate market, build the real estate market information system linked to land information, perfect the legal basis and strengthen the implementation of cashless payment in real estate transactions. Accordingly, request to submit to the National Assembly for comments and approval of the Law on Housing (amended), the Law on Real Estate Business (amended) to create a legal framework for real estate business, enhance the effectiveness of real estate market management; urgently amend and supplement Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing, Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP dated April 01, 2021 of the Government on amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing to be consistent with the Law on Housing.

  b) To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with relevant ministries and sectors in, researching and proposing regulations on procedures for implementation of housing and urban area projects.

  c) To closely monitor the real estate market; to submit quarterly reports to the Prime Minister on real estate business and real estate market nationwide and propose solutions for stable and healthy development of the real estate market.

  d) To inspect and examine the implementation of laws on real estate business, real estate trading floors and real estate brokerage; to coordinate with provincial People’s Committees in inspecting and reviewing housing and real estate business projects to propose competent regulatory authorities to revoke, suspend, temporarily halt, adjust, transfer or transfer real estate business projects in accordance with law.

  dd) To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with relevant ministries and sectors in, establishing task forces to direct, guide and urge localities to implement the Government’s Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP on the socio-economic recovery and development program for policies on support for development of social housing, housing for industrial park workers and renovation of old apartment buildings.

  e) To direct, guide and coordinate with localities in reviewing and supplementing planning, allocating land funds for development of social housing and housing for workers in accordance with law, strictly implementing the 20% land fund allocation in housing and urban area projects and ensuring the demand for social housing and housing for workers in the area.

  g) To direct, guide and coordinate with localities in reviewing, establishing and publishing the list of investors in social housing, housing for industrial park workers and renovation of old apartment buildings that are eligible for 2% interest rate support packages through the commercial banking system.

  h) To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with relevant ministries and sectors in, researching and proposing policies to promote development of social housing and housing for industrial park workers.

  i) To complete and publicly disclose the information system on housing and real estate market.

  2. Ministry of Finance

  a) Urgently amend Decree No. 153/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020 of the Government on offering and trading of individual corporate bonds in the domestic market and offering corporate bonds to the international market.

  b) Control capital raising activities of real estate businesses on the stock market to avoid speculation, manipulation and price inflation.

  c) Coordinate with the State Bank of Vietnam in inspecting and supervising compliance with laws on issuance, investment and provision of corporate bond services, especially issuance of individual bonds of real estate businesses, credit institutions related to real estate businesses, businesses with large issuance volumes and high interest rates, businesses with losses, and businesses issuing without collateral.

  d) Direct and guide the capital raising activities (including bond issuance) of real estate businesses on the stock market in accordance with law; create conditions and not obstruct businesses (with sufficient capacity, good and healthy business results...) to raise capital for recovery and development.

  dd) Study and improve tax policies suitable to the reality of real estate in order to encourage effective use of houses and land, limit speculation in houses and land, ensure reasonable and stable revenue for the state budget, in accordance with Vietnam's socio-economic conditions and international practices.

  3. Ministry of Planning and Investment

  a) Direct, urge and guide localities, propose solutions to overcome difficulties and obstacles, resolutely reduce unnecessary administrative procedures in investment procedures, approval of investment policies, investment decisions and issuance of investment registration certificates for housing and real estate projects to support and increase supply for the market.

  b) To monitor and promote the attraction of foreign investment in real estate business.

  4. Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment

  a) Coordinate with the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Justice in studying, reviewing and amending the current legal provisions related to the auction of land use rights (laws on auction, land laws, tax administration laws) to ensure consistency and suitability to the actual situation in localities.

  b) To inspect, examine and direct localities to prevent and strictly handle violations in land use right auction activities in accordance with law.

  c) To check and review the planning and land use plan indicators to ensure that they are consistent with the land planning of the respective industries and sectors, ensuring harmony and reasonableness, avoiding overlap and litigation.

  5. The Ministry of Public Security shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate closely and effectively with the Ministry of Finance, the State Bank of Vietnam and relevant agencies in enhancing the grasp of macroeconomic situation; assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Construction and the Ministry of Finance in strictly handling the activities of real estate trading floors and real estate brokerage activities that violate law, tax evasion, and the provision of inaccurate information that negatively impacts the activities of the financial, credit and real estate markets.

  6. The Ministry of Information and Communications shall direct press agencies to provide timely, official, accurate and complete information on the real estate market, stock market and bond market; and strictly handle acts of providing inaccurate information that negatively affect the operation of the financial, credit and real estate markets.

  7. The State Bank of Vietnam

  Strategically credit management in the real estate sector. Direct credit institutions to strictly control the quality of credit and lending in the real estate sector in accordance with law; urgently consider and prioritize lending to social housing, worker housing and affordable commercial housing projects with high efficiency and ability to repay debts.

  8. People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities

  a) To promptly organize the formulation, appraisal and approval of urban and rural construction planning, land use planning and plans; to publicly disclose information on construction planning, lists and schedules of implementation of infrastructure development projects and real estate projects, especially large projects, and the merger, establishment and upgrading of administrative units in localities, and to prevent the spread of rumors and speculation to push up prices for illegal profit-making.

  b) To promptly develop the housing development program for the period from now to 2025 and 2030 and the 5-year and annual housing development plans in accordance with the Law on Housing, which clearly define the list of housing projects as the basis for approving the housing investment policies in the locality.

  c) Review and compile a list of housing and real estate projects in the area; specifically assess the reasons and causes for projects that have been approved for investment but have not been implemented or are delayed in implementation. On this basis, focus on resolving difficulties and legal issues regarding procedures for preparing investment in housing projects to increase the supply for the market.

  d) Publicly publish the list of projects, land fund, procedures for housing and real estate development in the area to attract investment; create favorable conditions for domestic and foreign investors to easily access and participate in investment in housing and real estate.

  (d) Focusing on directing and organizing the land allocation and selection of investors for real estate projects and new housing projects to increase the supply for the market.

  e) Promote the auction of land use rights; bidding for projects involving land use to select investors to help increase the supply of commercial housing projects for the real estate market.

  g) Implement solutions to promote the development of social housing and worker housing in the area:

  - Review and supplement planning, allocate land fund for development of social housing and worker housing in accordance with regulations, strictly implement the 20% land fund allocation in housing projects and urban areas and ensure the demand for development of social housing and worker housing in the area.

  - Focus on implementation of Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on the program for economic and social recovery and development, which includes the urgent preparation of project lists, review of land fund, completion of legal procedures, selection of investors to implement social housing and worker housing projects in industrial parks, renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings in the area.

  - Review eligible entities and conditions on this basis to prepare and publish the list of investors of social housing and worker housing projects, renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings that are eligible for interest rate support packages of 2% through the commercial banking system in accordance with the Decree on interest rate support from the state budget for loans of enterprises, cooperatives and business households.

  - Coordinate with the provincial Social Policy Bank in reviewing and summarizing the needs of individual customers and households that borrow to buy or lease social housing and worker housing; build new or renovate and repair houses in accordance with Article 16 of Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP, Clause 10 Article 1 of Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP and relevant documents, ensuring accuracy and reflection of actual needs of the people.

  h) Urgently complete the inspection and assessment of quality of remaining old apartment buildings in the area to identify apartment buildings that must be demolished; promptly prepare and approve plans and lists of projects for renovation and reconstruction of apartment buildings in accordance with Decree No. 69/2021/ND-CP.

  i) Control, detect, prevent and strictly handle violations in land use right auction activities in accordance with law.

  k) Take measures to manage and prevent the division, “lotting and sale of land plots” in areas where investment is not permitted and lacks infrastructure systems; strengthen control of real estate trading floors, real estate brokerage organizations and individuals; rectify acts of barter trading, “price inflating” causing disruption of real estate market information.

  l) Review projects that use land but are delayed in implementation, left idle, misused, or waste land; and coordinate with relevant agencies to consider and guide handling according to competence and regulations of land law and other relevant laws to bring land funds into effective use and guide development in accordance with actual social needs.

  m) Closely monitor, promptly grasp information and developments of the market, and take timely measures to stabilize the market and prevent price surges and real estate "bubbles" in the area.

  n) Strengthen inspection and examination, and strictly handle violations of real estate business law, land law, construction law, and relevant laws (if any).

  9. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of Government-attached agencies, and chairpersons of People's Committees of provinces and centrally affiliated cities shall, within the scope of their assigned functions and tasks, thoroughly understand and strictly implement this Directive; and submit 06-month and annual reports to the Ministry of Construction for consolidation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  10. The Ministry of Construction shall take charge and coordinate with the Government Office and relevant agencies in guiding, urging, and inspecting the implementation of this Directive, and consolidating and reporting to the Prime Minister.
