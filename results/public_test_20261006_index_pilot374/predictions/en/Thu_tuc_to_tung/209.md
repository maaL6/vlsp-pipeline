                                         PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAFOOT VESSELS

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam of 1992, amended by Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10;
  Pursuant to Resolution No. 11/2007/QH12 on the 2008 Law and Ordinance Construction Program;
  The National Assembly Standing Committee promulgates the Ordinance on procedures for arrest of seafoots.

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  This Order provides for the competence, order and procedures for the arrest of ships to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints, the application of temporary emergency measures, the enforcement of civil judgments, the performance of judicial assistance and the competence, order and procedures for the release of arrested ships.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Order applies to Vietnamese agencies, organizations and individuals and foreign agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the settlement of maritime complaints, the application of temporary emergency measures, the enforcement of civil judgments and the mutual legal assistance in the exercise of the right to arrest seagoing vessels.

  Article 3. Competence to decide on the arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The People’s Court of a province or centrally-run city (hereinafter referred to as the provincial People’s Court) where a port is located, which is competent to decide the arrest of a seagoing vessel that is subject to a request for arrest for the settlement of maritime complaints, enforcement of civil judgments, or performance of judicial mandates of foreign courts.

  In case a port has multiple docks located in different provinces or centrally-run cities, the People’s Court of the province where the dock is located and the port where the seagoing vessel is requested to be seized shall have the competence to decide on the seizure of such seagoing vessel.

  2. The People’s Court which is handling the civil case, the People’s Court of the province where the arbitration council has accepted the dispute, has the competence to decide to apply the emergency measure of temporary arrest of the seagoing vessel.

  3. The Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court shall consider and decide which court has the competence to decide the arrest of a seagoing vessel in case of dispute over jurisdiction between provincial People’s Courts.

  Article 4. Responsibility for incorrect requests for arrest of seagoing vessels

  1. The person who makes a request for arrest of a seagoing vessel shall be responsible to the law for his/her request. In case the request for arrest of a seagoing vessel is incorrect and causes damage, the person who makes the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel shall have to compensate for the damage.

  2. All damage caused by the consequences of incorrect requests for arrest of seagoing vessels shall be settled by the parties through agreement. In case of failure to reach agreement and a dispute arises, the parties may request the court or arbitration to settle the dispute according to the law.

  3. If the court issues a decision to arrest a seagoing vessel incorrectly due to an incorrect request for arrest of the seagoing vessel or an incorrect seagoing vessel is requested for arrest and causes damage, the court shall have to compensate for the damage according to the law.

  Article 5. Financial security measures for requests for arrest of seagoing vessels

  1. The person who makes a request for arrest of a seagoing vessel shall have to take financial security measures, except for the cases specified in Points c and d, Clause 1, Article 44 of this Ordinance, in one or both of the following forms:

  a) Submit to the court a guarantee document in the form of property of a bank or other credit institution or of an individual, agency or other organization;

  b) To send a sum of money, precious metals, precious stones or valuable papers under the decision on the financial security measure of the Court to the blocked account at the bank where the Court of competent jurisdiction to decide the arrest of the seagoing vessel has its head office within 48 hours from the time of receiving such decision.

  In case the financial security measure for the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel is implemented on a weekend or a public holiday, the secured property shall be temporarily kept at the Court; the Court shall only receive the sum of money or valuable papers and seal and preserve them. On the next working day, the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing vessel shall immediately send such property to the bank under the supervision of the Court.

  2. The value of the financial security shall be determined by the Court to be equivalent to the loss or damage that may arise as a result of the incorrect request for arrest of the seagoing vessel.

  3. When deciding to release the seagoing vessel that is being arrested, the Judge shall consider the financial security measure for the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article, unless otherwise agreed by the parties. Depending on each specific case, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To keep the financial security measure for the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel if the Judge considers that the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel is incorrect and the value of the financial security may be sufficient or insufficient to compensate for the damage;

  b) To return a part of the value of the financial security for the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel if the Judge considers that the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel is incorrect and the value of the financial security exceeds the liability to compensate for the damage;

  c) To return the entire value of the financial security for the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel if the Judge considers that the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel is correct.

  Article 6. Fee for arrest of the seagoing vessel

  1. The applicant for the arrest of a seagoing vessel shall pay the fee as prescribed by law.

  2. The fee for the arrest of a seagoing vessel to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints, the application of temporary emergency measures, the enforcement of civil judgments, and the performance of judicial mandates of foreign courts shall be paid to the court competent to decide the arrest of the seagoing vessel as prescribed in Article 3 of this Ordinance within 48 hours from the time the court requests the payment of the fee.

  Article 7. Supervision of the observance of law in the arrest of seagoing vessels.

  The People's Procuracy shall supervise the observance of law in the arrest of seagoing vessels; exercise the rights to make recommendations as prescribed by law to ensure the timely and lawful arrest of seagoing vessels.

  Article 8. Documents and evidence attached to the application for the arrest of a seagoing vessel or the written request for the release of a seagoing vessel being arrested.

  1. The application for the arrest of a seagoing vessel or the written request for the release of a seagoing vessel being arrested must be accompanied by documents and evidence proving that the request for the arrest of the seagoing vessel or the release of the seagoing vessel being arrested is well-founded and lawful.

  2. In case documents and evidence proving the request for the arrest of a seagoing vessel or the release of a seagoing vessel being arrested are in a foreign language, they must be enclosed with Vietnamese translations certified as lawful under Vietnamese law. Documents and papers prepared, issued, or certified by competent foreign authorities under foreign law must be consularly legalized, unless exempted under the provisions of an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a member.

  Article 9. Enforcement of decisions on the arrest of seagoing vessels and decisions on the release of seagoing vessels being arrested.

  1. Immediately after issuing a decision to arrest a seagoing vessel or a decision to release a seagoing vessel that is being arrested, the Chief Justice of the Court shall assign a judicial officer to deliver the decision as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  2. Within twelve hours from the time of assignment, the judicial officer shall go to the port to deliver two copies of the decision to the Director of the Maritime Port Authority or the Director of the inland port (hereinafter referred to as the port director) where the seagoing vessel is operating. The port director shall implement the decision to arrest the seagoing vessel or the decision to release the seagoing vessel that is being arrested in accordance with law and hand over one copy to the ship captain for implementation.

  In case the judicial officer cannot reach the port within the above-mentioned time limit, the decision may be sent via fax or email in accordance with law.

  3. The maritime, security, quarantine, customs, tax, culture and information, fire and explosion prevention and control, environmental protection and other state management agencies shall coordinate in implementing the decision to arrest the seagoing vessel upon request of the port director and shall be subject to the port director's coordination in implementing the decision to arrest the seagoing vessel.

  Vietnam Coast Guard shall coordinate in implementing the decision to arrest the seagoing vessel upon request of the port director.

  4. During the period the seagoing vessel is being arrested, the ship owner, the ship charterer and the ship operator shall maintain the operation of the seagoing vessel.

  5. The Government shall prescribe the implementation of the Court's decision prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, and the handling of the seagoing vessel in case the ship owner abandons the ship or auctions the seagoing vessel that is being arrested.

  Article 10. Notification of the implementation of the decision to arrest the seagoing vessel or the decision to release the seagoing vessel that is being arrested

  1. The Director of the Port Authority shall notify in writing the Court, the Vietnam Maritime Administration and the relevant state-managed agencies at the port of the implementation of the decision to arrest the seagoing vessel or the decision to release the arrested seagoing vessel; in case of arrest of the seagoing vessel for enforcement of judgment, the notification shall be made immediately to the civil judgment enforcement agency for the latter to carry out the civil judgment enforcement.

  2. The captain shall notify the ship owner, the ship charterer, the ship operator and the persons with related interests of the arrest or release of the seagoing vessel.

  Chapter II

    PROCEDURES FOR ARRESTING THE SEAGOING VESSELS, RELEASING THE ARRESTED SEAGOING VESSELS AND KEEPING THE ARRESTED SEAGOING VESSELS FOR ENSURING THE settlement of maritime complaints

  Article 11. Maritime complaints giving rise to the right to request the arrest of seagoing vessels

  A maritime complaint giving rise to the right to request the arrest of a seagoing vessel is a complaint about:

  1. Wages, repatriation expenses, social insurance contributions and other amounts payable to the crew members, officers and other crew members of the seagoing vessel;

  2. Compensation for loss of life, injuries and other health damage directly related to the operation of the seagoing vessel;

  3. Loading charges, marine security charges, port charges, port charges, other port charges and fees;

  4. Salvage fees;

  5. Losses and damage to property outside the contract directly related to the operation of the seagoing vessel;

  6. Damage or threat of damage to the environment, the coast or related interests caused by the seagoing vessel; measures taken to prevent, limit or eliminate such damage; compensation for such damage; costs for reasonable measures actually taken or to be taken to restore the environment; losses incurred or likely to be incurred by third parties related to such damage; losses, costs or damages similar to those specified in this clause;

  7. Costs related to the raising, moving, salvaging, destruction or neutralization of the sunken, stranded or abandoned seagoing vessel, including any objects on or in the seagoing vessel and costs or expenses related to the preservation of the abandoned seagoing vessel and costs for the crew of the seagoing vessel;

  8. Agreements related to the use or hire of the seagoing vessel, even if specified in the vessel hire contract or otherwise;

  9. Agreements related to the transport of goods or passengers on the seagoing vessel, even if specified in the vessel hire contract or otherwise;

  10. Losses or damage related to goods, including luggage transported on the seagoing vessel;

  11. General losses;

  12. Pilotage;

  13. Use of marine fuel;

  14. Goods, materials, food, fuel, equipment (including electronics) supplied or services provided for the operation, management, preservation and maintenance of ships;

  15. New construction, conversion, restoration, repair or equipping of ships;

  16. Payment made on behalf of the ship owner;

  17. Insurance premium paid by the ship owner or the person in the name of the ship owner or the shipless ship lessee;

  18. Commission, brokerage or agency fees related to ships paid by the ship owner, the shipless ship lessee or the authorized person;

  19. Disputes over ownership of seagoing vessels;

  20. Disputes between co-owners of seagoing vessels over use of seagoing vessels or income from seagoing vessels;

  21. Mortgage of seagoing vessels;

  22. Disputes arising from contracts for sale of seagoing vessels.

  Article 12. Right to request the arrest of seagoing vessels to secure the settlement of maritime complaints

  A person with a maritime complaint specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance has the right to request the competent court specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide the arrest of seagoing vessels to secure the settlement of maritime complaints.

  Article 13. Conditions for the arrest of seagoing vessels to secure the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. When there is a request for the arrest of seagoing vessels to secure the settlement of maritime complaints specified in Article 12 of this Ordinance, the court shall decide the arrest of seagoing vessels in the following cases:

  a) The ship owner is responsible for the maritime claim at the time the maritime claim arises and remains the ship owner at the time the ship is seized;

  b) The bareboat charterer is responsible for the maritime claim at the time the maritime claim arises and remains the bareboat charterer or ship owner at the time the ship is seized;

  c) The maritime claim is based on the mortgage of the ship;

  d) The maritime claim is related to the ownership or possession of the ship;

  dd) The maritime claim is secured by a maritime lien on the ship.

  2. The seizure of the ship may also be carried out against one or more other ships owned by the person responsible for the maritime claim and at the time the maritime claim arises, such person is:

  a) The owner of the ship related to the maritime claim;

  b) The bareboat charterer, the fixed-term charterer or the voyage charterer of the ship related to the maritime claim.

  3. The provisions of Clause 2 of this Article shall not apply to maritime complaints related to the ownership of seagoing vessels.

  Article 14. Time limit for seizing seagoing vessels to secure the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. The maximum time limit for seizing seagoing vessels to secure the settlement of maritime complaints is thirty days from the date of seizure of the seagoing vessel.

  2. If the applicant for seizure of the seagoing vessel files a lawsuit at the Court or requests arbitration to settle the dispute within the time limit for the seizure of the seagoing vessel to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint, the time limit for the seizure of the seagoing vessel to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint shall terminate when the Court issues a decision on the application or non-application of the measure of emergency temporary seizure of the seagoing vessel.

  Article 15. Application for seizure of seagoing vessels to secure the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. The applicant for seizure of the seagoing vessel to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint shall make an application.

  2. The application for seizure of the seagoing vessel to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of making the application;

  b) Name of the court receiving the request;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel subject to arrest; port where the seagoing vessel is operating;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship charterer or operator, in case the ship charterer or operator is required to pay;

  h) Specific maritime complaint giving rise to the right to request the arrest of the seagoing vessel and the maximum value of such maritime complaint;

  i) Estimated loss or damage that may arise as a result of the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel.

  3. In case the applicant for the arrest of the seagoing vessel does not know exactly all the contents specified in Points dd, e and g Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall record what he/she knows related to those issues.

  Article 16. Sending the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for securing the settlement of maritime complaints and the enclosed documents and evidences The applicant for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for securing the settlement of maritime complaints shall send the application and the enclosed documents and evidences to the competent court to decide the arrest of the seagoing vessel specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 17. Receipt of the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for securing the settlement of maritime complaints

  Right after receiving the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for securing the settlement of maritime complaints and the enclosed documents and evidences, the competent court to decide the arrest of the seagoing vessel specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record the receipt of the application in the register. The Chief Justice shall immediately assign a Judge to settle the application.

  Article 18. Consideration of the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for securing the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. Within forty-eight hours from the time of receiving the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences, the Judge shall consider the application and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To accept the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for securing the settlement of maritime complaints if he/she deems that the conditions for issuing the decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel are met, and requests the applicant to take the financial security for the arrest of the seagoing vessel and the arrest fee;

  b) To return the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for securing the settlement of maritime complaints if he/she deems that the conditions for issuing the decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel are not met or the settlement of the application is beyond the jurisdiction of that court.

  2. In case of decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing vessel to secure settlement of maritime complaints, the Court must issue or send immediately such decision together with the request and the enclosed documents and evidences to the requester.

  Article 19. Complaints and settlement of complaints about the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing vessel to secure settlement of maritime complaints

  1. Within 24 hours from the time of receipt of the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing vessel to secure settlement of maritime complaints, the requester has the right to lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court against such decision.

  2. Within 24 hours from the time of receipt of the written complaint against the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing vessel to secure settlement of maritime complaints and the enclosed documents and evidences, the Chief Justice of the Court must issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing vessel to secure settlement of maritime complaints;

  b) To annul the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing vessel to secure settlement of maritime complaints and to receive back the request and the enclosed documents and evidences for the purpose of accepting the request for arrest of the seagoing vessel.

  3. The decision of the Chief Justice of the Court on settlement of complaints is the final decision.

  Article 20. Decision on arrest of a seagoing vessel to secure settlement of maritime complaints

  1. The judge shall immediately issue a decision to seize the seagoing vessel to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint when the applicant for the seizure of the seagoing vessel presents receipts and documents proving that he/she has taken the financial security measures for the request for the seizure of the seagoing vessel as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 5 and has paid the fee for the seizure of the seagoing vessel as prescribed in Article 6 of this Ordinance.

  2. The decision to seize the seagoing vessel to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the court issuing the decision;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the applicant for the seizure of the seagoing vessel;

  d) Maritime complaint giving rise to the right to request the court to seize the seagoing vessel;

  dd) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel subject to seizure; port where the seagoing vessel is engaged in maritime activities;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the shipless vessel lessee or operator;

  i) The Court's assessment and legal grounds for accepting the application;

  k) The Court's decisions.

  3. The decision to seize the ship to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint shall take effect immediately, even in the case of complaints or petitions.

  4. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision to seize the ship to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint to the Port Authority Director for implementation as prescribed in Article 9 of this Ordinance; immediately send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or immediately send such decision to the person requesting the seizure of the ship; immediately send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seized ship has foreign elements.

  Article 21. Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints and petitions regarding the decision to seize the ship to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint

  1. The ship captain, ship owner, shipless vessel lessee or operator may lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court regarding the decision to seize the ship to secure the settlement of the maritime complaint. The time limit for filing a complaint is forty-eight hours from the time the ship captain receives the Court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may propose in writing to the Chief Justice of the Court to decide on the arrest of the seagoing vessel to ensure the settlement of the maritime complaint. The time limit for the proposal is forty-eight hours from the time the Procuracy of the same level receives the Court's decision.

  2. Within 48 hours from the date of receipt of the complaint or petition against the decision to arrest the seagoing vessel, the Chief Justice of the Court shall consider and settle the case and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Keep the decision to arrest the seagoing vessel in order to ensure the settlement of the maritime complaint;

  b) Cancel the decision to arrest the ship to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints;

  3. The decision to settle complaints and petitions of the Chief Justice of the Court is the final decision.

  Article 22. Basis for releasing a detained ship to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. A seagoing vessel which is seized to secure the settlement of a maritime complaint shall be immediately released when any of the following grounds is satisfied:

  a) After the ship owner, ship lessee or ship operator takes measures to replace or fully pay the debt;

  b) The obligation of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator, which has been guaranteed by another person or has a commitment letter from a reputable insurance organization. The Ministry of Finance shall notify the list of reputable insurance organizations;

  c) At the request of the person who requested the ship arrest;

  d) The decision on ship arrest has been annulled;

  dd) The time limit for ship arrest under the court decision has expired.

  2. The substitute security measures shall be agreed upon by the parties. In case of no agreement between the parties on the level and form of substitute security, the court shall decide the level and form of substitute security, but not exceeding the value of the arrested ship or the asset obligation which is the basis for the ship arrest in case the asset obligation is smaller than the value of the ship.

  Article 23. Request for release of the arrested ship as security for settlement of maritime complaints

  1. When one of the grounds specified in Points a, b and c, Clause 1, Article 22 of this Ordinance is met, the ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator, the captain, the person who requested the ship arrest and other relevant persons may request the release of the arrested ship. The request for release of the ship must be made in writing.

  2. The written request for release of the arrested ship must contain the following main contents:

  a) Date, month and year of the written request;

  b) Name of the court receiving the written request;

  c) Name and address of the person requesting the release of the detained seagoing vessel;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the detained seagoing vessel; port where the detained seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  dd) Number, date, month and year of the decision on detention of the seagoing vessel and the court that issued such decision;

  e) Reason for requesting the release of the detained seagoing vessel;

  g) Guarantee of the person requesting the release of the detained seagoing vessel.

  Article 24. Sending the written request for release of the detained seagoing vessel to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints

  The applicant for the release of the detained ship shall send a written request and documents and evidences to the court that has issued the decision on the detention of the ship.

  Article 25. Decision on the release of the detained ship for the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. Immediately after receiving the written request for the release of the detained ship for the settlement of maritime complaints, the Chief Justice shall assign a judge to decide the release of the ship.

  2. Within 24 hours from the time of receiving the written request for the release of the detained ship for the settlement of maritime complaints and the attached documents and evidences, if there are grounds, the judge assigned to decide the release of the ship shall consider and issue a decision on the release of the ship. If the request is not accepted due to lack of grounds, the judge shall notify the applicant in writing, clearly stating the reasons for the rejection of the request for the release of the ship.

  3. The decision on the release of the detained ship for the settlement of maritime complaints shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the court that issues the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the court to issue the decision on the release of the detained ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seized seagoing vessel, port where the seized seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship charterer or operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for releasing the seized seagoing vessel;

  i) Court decisions.

  4. The decision to release the seized seagoing vessel to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints shall take effect immediately.

  5. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision to release the seized seagoing vessel to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints to the Director of the Port Authority for implementation according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; immediately send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or immediately send such decision to the person requesting the release of the seized seagoing vessel; immediately send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seized seagoing vessel is released with foreign elements.

  Article 26. Re-arrest of seagoing vessels for security for settlement of maritime complaints

  1. A seagoing vessel which has been released or secured by a substitute security measure for settlement of a maritime complaint shall not be re-arrested on the basis of the same maritime complaint, except in the following cases:

  a) The total value of the substitute security is still insufficient to satisfy the obligation to provide property if the total value of the security is lower than the value of the released seagoing vessel;

  b) The guarantor fails to perform or is unable to perform in whole or in part the obligation to provide property which he/she has guaranteed on behalf of the ship owner, the ship charterer or the ship operator;

  c) The release of the seagoing vessel or the cancellation of the substitute security measure is carried out at the request of the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing vessel on the basis of legitimate reasons;

  d) The person requesting the arrest of the seagoing vessel is unable to prevent the release of the seagoing vessel or the cancellation of the security measure despite the application of necessary measures.

  2. A seagoing vessel shall not be considered as having been released if the release of the seagoing vessel is not decided by a competent court or the seagoing vessel escapes from the place of arrest, except for the cases specified in Points d and dd Clause 1 Article 22 of this Ordinance.

  3. The procedures for re-arrest of a seagoing vessel for security for settlement of a maritime complaint shall comply with the procedures for arrest of a seagoing vessel for security for settlement of a maritime complaint prescribed in this Chapter.

  Chapter III

              PROCEDURES FOR APPLICATION AND CANCELLATION OF THE MEASURE OF TEMPORARY HOLDING OF SEAFOOTERS

  Article 27. The measure of temporary holding of seafooters

  The measure of temporary holding of seafooters is a measure applied in the course of settlement of civil cases as prescribed in Clause 13, Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Code.

  Article 28. Right to request application of the measure of temporary holding of seafooters

  In the course of settlement of civil cases, the parties involved or their lawful representatives may request the court which is settling such cases to apply the measure of temporary holding of seafooters to temporarily settle the urgent requests of the parties involved, protect evidence, preserve the existing state to avoid irreparable damage or ensure the enforcement of judgments when one of the cases prescribed in Article 29 of this Ordinance arises.

  Article 29. Conditions for application of the measure of temporary holding of seafooters

  When a request for holding of seafooters is made as prescribed in Article 28 of this Ordinance, the court shall decide to apply the measure of temporary holding of seafooters in the following cases:

  1. The seagoing vessel is being seized to ensure the settlement of the maritime complaint, and the person requesting the seizure of the seagoing vessel has initiated a civil lawsuit at the court;

  2. The shipowner is a person with property obligations in the case being settled and is still the shipowner at the time of applying the emergency measure of temporary seizure of the seagoing vessel;

  3. The shipless vessel lessee, the fixed-term vessel lessee, the voyage vessel lessee or the vessel operator is a person with property obligations in the civil case arising from the maritime complaint specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance and is still the shipless vessel lessee, the fixed-term vessel lessee, the voyage vessel lessee, the vessel operator or the shipowner at the time of applying the emergency measure of temporary seizure of the seagoing vessel;

  4. The dispute is being settled in the case arising on the basis of the mortgage of the seagoing vessel;

  5. The dispute is being settled in the case related to the ownership or possession of the seagoing vessel.

  Article 30. Application for application of the emergency measure of temporary seizure of the seagoing vessel

  1. The person requesting the application of the emergency measure of temporary seizure of the seagoing vessel must make an application.

  2. The application for application of the emergency measure of temporary seizure of the seagoing vessel contains the following main contents:

  a) Date, month and year of filing the request;

  b) Name of the court receiving the request;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel to be arrested; port where the seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship charterer or operator in case of demanding the ship charterer or operator to pay;

  h) Specific dispute being settled in the case and maximum value of such dispute;

  i) Reasons for requesting the application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel;

  k) Estimated losses or damage that may arise due to the incorrect request for the application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel;

  l) Guarantees of the applicant for the application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel;

  m) Number, date, month and year, and contents of the decision of the Court on the detention of the seagoing vessel to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints (if any).

  3. In case the applicant for the application of the emergency measure does not know exactly all the contents specified in Points dd, e and g Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall record what he/she knows related to those issues.

  Article 31. Sending the application for the application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences The applicant for the application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel shall send the application and the enclosed documents and evidences to the Court that is handling the case.

  Article 32. Receipt of the application for the application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel

  1. Immediately after receiving the application for the application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences, the Court that is handling the case shall record the receipt of the application in the receipt book.

  2. In cases where the court is handling a case and has the competence to decide on the arrest of a seagoing vessel as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 3 of this Ordinance, the application and accompanying documents and evidence shall be handled as follows:

  a) Immediately hand over the application and accompanying documents and evidence to the judge assigned to handle the case if the court receives the application and accompanying documents and evidence before opening the court session;

  b) Immediately hand over the application and accompanying documents and evidence to the trial council that is handling the case if the court receives the application and accompanying documents and evidence during the trial of the case.

  Article 33. Consideration of applications for application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of seagoing vessels

  1. Within three days from the date of receipt of the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel and the accompanying documents and evidence, the judge shall consider the application and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Approve the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel if he/she finds that the conditions for issuing a decision on application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel are fully met, and at the same time request the applicant to provide financial security for the application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel and the seagoing vessel arrest fee within the time limit set by the court without applying the time limit prescribed in Articles 5 and 6 of this Ordinance. When the applicant for application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel presents receipts and documents proving that he/she has provided financial security and paid the seagoing vessel arrest fee, the judge shall immediately issue a decision on application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel;

  b) Reject the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel if he/she finds that the conditions for issuing a decision on application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel are not fully met.

  2. After receiving the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary arrest of a seagoing vessel and the accompanying documents and evidence, the trial council shall immediately consider the application and issue one of the decisions prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. In case of decision to return the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel, the Court shall issue or send immediately such decision together with the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences to the applicant; the Trial Council shall publicly announce the decision to return the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel at the court session and record such announcement in the court session minutes.

  Article 34. Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints and petitions regarding the decision to return the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel

  1. Within three working days from the date of receipt of the decision to return the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel, the applicant has the right to lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court against such decision.

  2. Within three working days from the date of receipt of the written complaint against the decision to return the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences, the Chief Justice of the Court shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to return the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel;

  b) To annul the decision to return the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel and to take back the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences for the purpose of accepting the application for detention of the seagoing vessel.

  3. After receiving the public announcement of the decision to return the application for application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel at the court session, the applicant has the right to lodge a complaint, and the Procuracy has the right to petition the Trial Council against such decision and record such petition in the court session minutes. The Trial Council shall settle the complaint or petition immediately and issue one of the decisions specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The decision of the Chief Justice on settlement of the complaint or the decision of the Trial Council on settlement of the complaint or petition shall be the final decision.

  Article 35. Decision on application of emergency measures to temporarily seize a seagoing vessel

  1. A decision on application of emergency measures to temporarily seize a seagoing vessel must contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the court issuing the decision;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting application of emergency measures to temporarily seize the seagoing vessel;

  d) Reason for requesting application of emergency measures to temporarily seize the seagoing vessel;

  dd) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seized seagoing vessel; port where the seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the shipless vessel lessee or operator;

  i) The Court's assessment and legal grounds for accepting the application;

  k) The Court's decisions.

  2. The decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaints or petitions.

  3. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel to the Port Authority Director for implementation as prescribed in Article 9 of this Ordinance; immediately send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or immediately send such decision to the person requesting the application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel; immediately send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing vessel is detained by a foreign entity.

  Article 36. Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints and petitions regarding the decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel

  1. The ship captain, ship owner, shipless vessel lessee or operator may lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court regarding the decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel. The time limit for lodging a complaint is three working days from the date on which the ship captain receives the Court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may propose in writing to the Chief Justice of the Court to decide on the application of the measure of emergency temporary detention of a seagoing vessel. The time limit for the proposal is three working days from the date on which the Procuracy of the same level receives the Court's decision.

  2. Within three working days from the date of receipt of the complaint or petition against the decision to apply the measure of temporary emergency arrest of a seagoing vessel, the Chief Justice of the Court shall consider and settle the case and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Keep the decision to apply the measure of temporary emergency detention of the seagoing vessel;

  b) Cancel the decision to apply the measure of temporary emergency detention of a seagoing vessel.

  3. At the court session, the handling of complaints and petitions shall fall under the jurisdiction of the Trial Council. The Trial Council shall consider, handle and issue one of the decisions specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The decision to settle complaints or petitions of the Chief Justice or the Trial Council is the final decision.

  Article 37. Grounds for annulment of decisions on application of emergency measures to temporarily seize seagoing vessels

  The decision to apply the measure of temporary emergency detention of a ship is annulled when the conditions for application of the measure of temporary emergency detention are no longer met or at the request of the person requesting the application of the measure of temporary emergency detention of the ship.

  Article 38. Request for cancellation of decision on application of emergency measures of temporary detention of seagoing vessels

  1. The following persons may request in writing the cancellation of decision on application of emergency measures of temporary detention of seagoing vessels:

  a) The person requesting the application of emergency measures of temporary detention of seagoing vessels;

  b) The master of the vessel, the lessee of the vessel, the operator of the vessel, the captain and other relevant persons when the conditions for application of emergency measures of temporary detention of seagoing vessels no longer exist.

  2. The written request for cancellation of emergency measures of temporary detention of seagoing vessels shall contain the following main contents:

  a) The date of making the request;

  b) The name of the Court receiving the request;

  c) The name and address of the person requesting the cancellation of decision on application of emergency measures of temporary detention of seagoing vessels;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seized seagoing vessel; port where the seized seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  dd) Number, date, month and year of the decision on application of the measure of temporary emergency seizure of the seagoing vessel and the Court that has issued such decision;

  e) Reason for requesting the cancellation of the decision on application of the measure of temporary emergency seizure of the seagoing vessel.

  Article 39. Sending a written request for cancellation of the decision on application of the measure of temporary emergency seizure of the seagoing vessel

  The person requesting the cancellation of the decision on application of the measure of temporary emergency seizure of the seagoing vessel shall send a written request and enclosed documents and evidences to the Court that has issued the decision on application of the measure of temporary emergency seizure of the seagoing vessel.

  Article 40. Handling of the written request for cancellation of the decision on application of the measure of temporary emergency seizure of the seagoing vessel

  In case the Court handling the case receives a written request for cancellation of the decision on application of the measure of temporary emergency seizure of the seagoing vessel, the written request shall be handled as follows:

  1. Immediately hand over to the Judge assigned to handle the case if the Court receives the written request before opening the court session;

  2. Immediately hand over to the Trial Council currently hearing the case the written request received by the Court within the time limit for hearing the case.

  Article 41. Decision to annul the decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel

  1. Immediately after receiving the written request to annul the decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences, the assigned judge or the Trial Council currently hearing the case shall consider and issue a decision to annul the decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel if it deems that there are sufficient grounds. In case of not accepting the request due to insufficient grounds, the judge or the Trial Council shall notify in writing the requesting party of the reasons for not accepting the request.

  2. The decision to annul the decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of a seagoing vessel shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue the decision to annul the decision to apply the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel being detained; port where the detained seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  d) The number, date, month and year of the decision on application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel and the Court that has issued such decision;

  e) The name, address and nationality of the master of the seagoing vessel;

  g) The name, address and nationality of the seagoing vessel lessee or operator;

  h) The name, address and nationality of the captain of the seagoing vessel;

  i) The reason for cancellation of the decision on application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel;

  k) The Court’s decisions.

  3. The decision on cancellation of the decision on application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel shall take effect immediately.

  4. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision on cancellation of the decision on application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel to the Director of the Port Authority for implementation as prescribed in Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision immediately to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision immediately to the person requesting the cancellation of the decision on application of the emergency measure of temporary detention of the seagoing vessel; send such decision immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing vessel is detained and its owner is a foreigner.

  Article 42. Application of the emergency measure of temporary seizure of a seagoing vessel in case of arbitration settlement of a dispute

  During the arbitration settlement of a dispute, the parties or their lawful representatives may request the competent court specified in Clause 2 Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide on the application of the emergency measure of temporary seizure of a seagoing vessel. The procedures for deciding on the application of the emergency measure of temporary seizure of a seagoing vessel shall comply with the provisions of this Chapter.

  Chapter IV

              PROCEDURES FOR SEIZURE AND RELEASE OF A SEAGOING VESSEL SUBJECT TO JUDGMENT ENFORCEMENT

  Article 43. Right to request seizure of a seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement

  Through the competent civil judgment enforcement agency, the judgment debtor under the law on civil judgment enforcement of Vietnam may request the competent court specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide on the seizure of a seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement.

  Article 44. Conditions for seizure of a seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement

  1. When a request for seizure of a seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement is made as prescribed in Article 43 of this Ordinance, the court shall decide on the seizure of the seagoing vessel in the following cases:

  a) The ship owner is the judgment debtor and is still the ship owner at the time of the ship arrest;

  b) The shipless vessel lessee, the fixed-term vessel lessee, the voyage vessel lessee or the vessel operator is the judgment debtor in the civil case arising from the maritime complaint specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance and is still the shipless vessel lessee, the fixed-term vessel lessee, the voyage vessel lessee, the vessel operator or the ship owner at the time of the ship arrest;

  c) The judgment debt obligation is secured by the mortgage of the ship;

  d) The judgment debt obligation is the return of the ship to the judgment creditor.

  2. The court shall decide to arrest the ship for judgment enforcement only when the civil judgment enforcement agency is unable to apply the asset seizure measure or other enforcement measures for judgment enforcement, except for the cases specified in Points c and d, Clause 1 of this Article or the judgment debtor is overseas and has no other assets in Vietnam.

  Article 45. Application for arrest of the ship for judgment enforcement

  1. The applicant for arrest of the ship for judgment enforcement shall make an application enclosed with a copy of the court’s judgment or decision or a copy of the arbitration’s decision.

  2. The application for arrest of the ship for judgment enforcement shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date, month and year of filing the request;

  b) Name of the civil judgment enforcement agency receiving the request;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel to be arrested, port where the seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the bareboat charterer, operator in case of demanding the bareboat charterer, operator to pay;

  h) Obligations regarding assets subject to enforcement according to the court’s judgment or arbitration decision;

  i) Reasons for requesting the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  k) Estimated losses or damage that may arise due to the incorrect request for the arrest of the seagoing vessel.

  3. In case the applicant for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement does not know exactly all the contents specified in Points dd, e and g Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall state what he/she knows related to those issues.

  Article 46. Sending the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement and the enclosed documents and evidences The applicant for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement shall send the application and the enclosed documents and evidences to the competent civil judgment enforcement agency for transfer to the competent court specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 47. Receipt of the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement

  1. Immediately after receiving the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement and the enclosed documents and evidences, the civil judgment enforcement agency shall record the receipt in the register and send the application and the enclosed documents and evidences in writing to the competent court for decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance. The written transfer of the application must clearly state the reason for the inability to apply the asset seizure measure or other enforcement measures for judgment enforcement.

  2. Immediately after receiving the written transfer of the application from the civil judgment enforcement agency enclosed with the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel and the documents and evidences, the competent court for decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record the receipt in the register. The Chief Justice of the court shall immediately assign a judge to handle the application.

  Article 48. Consideration of the application for the arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement

  1. Within 48 hours from the time of receipt of the documents specified in Clause 2, Article 47 of this Ordinance, the judge shall consider the application and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To accept the application for arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement if he/she deems that the conditions for issuing the decision on arrest of the seagoing vessel are met, and to request the applicant to provide financial security for the application for arrest of the seagoing vessel and the seagoing vessel arrest fee, except in cases where the financial security is not required;

  b) To return the application for arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement if he/she deems that the conditions for issuing the decision on arrest of the seagoing vessel are not met or the application is beyond the jurisdiction of the court.

  2. In case of issuing a decision to return the application for arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement, the court shall send the decision to the competent civil judgment enforcement agency; or immediately send the decision together with the application and the enclosed documents and evidence to the applicant.

  Article 49. Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints and petitions regarding the decision to return the application for arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement

  1. Within 24 hours from the time of receipt of the decision to return the application for arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement, the applicant has the right to lodge a complaint, and the competent civil judgment enforcement agency has the right to petition the Chief Judge of the court regarding such decision. The complaint or petition must be made in writing.

  2. Within 24 hours from the time of receipt of the written complaint or petition regarding the decision to return the application for arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement and the enclosed documents and evidence, the Chief Judge of the court shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to return the application for arrest of the seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement;

  b) Cancel the decision on return of the request for maritime vessel arrest for judgment enforcement and receive the request and documents and evidences attached thereto for the acceptance of the maritime vessel arrest.

  3. The decision on settlement of complaints and petitions of the Chief Justice is the final decision.

  Article 50. Decision on maritime vessel arrest for judgment enforcement

  1. The judge shall immediately issue a decision on maritime vessel arrest for judgment enforcement when the applicant presents receipts and documents proving that he/she has taken the financial security measures for the maritime vessel arrest request specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 5, except for the case where the financial security measures are not required and the maritime vessel arrest fee specified in Article 6 of this Ordinance has been paid.

  2. The decision on maritime vessel arrest for judgment enforcement shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the court issuing the decision;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting the maritime vessel arrest for judgment enforcement;

  d) Reason for request for arrest of the ship for enforcement of judgment;

  dd) Name of the competent civil judgment enforcement agency;

  e) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the ship requested for arrest, port where the ship is engaged in navigation;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  i) Name, address and nationality of the bareboat charterer, operator;

  k) Judgment of the Court and legal grounds for accepting the request;

  l) Court decisions.

  3. The decision to seize a ship for enforcement of judgment shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaints or petitions.

  4. The court shall deliver two copies of the decision to seize a ship for enforcement of judgment to the Director of the Port Authority for enforcement as prescribed in Article 9 of this Ordinance; immediately send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or immediately send such decision to the person requesting the seizure of the ship for enforcement of judgment, the competent civil judgment enforcement agency; immediately send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seized ship has foreign elements.

  Article 51. Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints and petitions regarding the decision to seize a ship for enforcement of judgment

  1. The ship owner, ship lessee, ship operator and the captain may lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the court regarding the decision to seize a ship for enforcement of judgment. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty-eight hours from the time the captain receives the court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may lodge a written petition with the Chief Justice of the court regarding the decision to seize a ship for enforcement of judgment. The time limit for lodging a petition is forty-eight hours from the time the Procuracy of the same level receives the court's decision.

  2. Within forty-eight hours from the time of receiving the written complaint or petition regarding the decision to seize a ship for enforcement of judgment, the Chief Justice of the court shall consider and settle the complaint or petition and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to seize a ship for enforcement of judgment;

  b) To annul the decision to seize a ship for enforcement of judgment.

  3. The decision to settle complaints and petitions of the Chief Justice of the Court is the final decision.

  Article 52. Grounds for releasing a seized seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement

  1. A seized seagoing vessel shall be immediately released when one of the following grounds is satisfied:

  a) The shipowner, shipcharterer or ship operator, who is the judgment debtor, has taken substitute security measures or has fully fulfilled the judgment enforcement obligation;

  b) The obligation regarding the shipowner’s or shipcharterer’s or ship operator’s property has been guaranteed by another person, or the commitment letter of a reputable insurance organization;

  c) At the request of the person who requested the seizure of the seagoing vessel.

  2. Substitute security measures shall be agreed upon by the parties. In case of no agreement between the parties on the level and form of substitute security measures, the Court shall decide the level and form of substitute security measures, but not exceeding the value of the seized seagoing vessel or the property obligation that is the basis for the seizure of the seagoing vessel in case the property obligation is smaller than the value of the seagoing vessel.

  Article 53. Request for release of a seized seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement

  1. When there is one of the grounds specified in Clause 1 Article 52 of this Ordinance, the ship owner, shipless owner, ship operator, captain and other relevant persons shall have to request in writing the release of the seized ship for judgment enforcement.

  2. The written request for release of the seized ship shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of request;

  b) Name of the court receiving the request;

  c) Name and address of the person requesting the release of the seized ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seized ship; port where the seized ship is operating;

  dd) Number, date and year of the decision on the seizure of the ship and the court that issued such decision;

  e) Reason for requesting the release of the seized ship.

  Article 54. Sending written requests for release of seized ships for judgment enforcement

  The applicant for the release of a seized ship to enforce the judgment must send a written request and documents and evidence to the court that issued the decision to seize the ship.

  Article 55. Decision to release a seized ship for enforcement of judgment

  1. Immediately after receiving the written request for release of the seized ship to enforce the judgment, the Chief Justice shall assign a judge to handle the release of the seized ship.

  2. Within 24 hours from the time of receipt of the written request for release of the seized ship to enforce the judgment and the enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider and issue a decision on release of the seized ship if he/she finds grounds for it. If he/she does not accept the request for release of the seized ship because there are no grounds for it, the judge shall notify the requester in writing, clearly stating the reason for not accepting the request for release of the seized ship.

  3. The decision to release a seized ship for enforcement of the judgment shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) The name of the court that issued the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the court to issue a decision to release the seized seagoing vessel;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the released seized seagoing vessel; port where the seized seagoing vessel is released;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship charterer or operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for releasing the seized seagoing vessel;

  i) Court decisions.

  4. The decision to release the seized seagoing vessel for judgment enforcement shall take effect immediately.

  5. The court shall send two copies of the decision on release of the seized seagoing vessel to the Director of the Port Authority for execution in accordance with Article 9 of this Ordinance; immediately send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or immediately send such decision to the person requesting the release of the seized seagoing vessel for execution of the judgment, the competent civil judgment enforcement agency; immediately send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seized seagoing vessel is released with foreign elements.

  Chapter V

   PROCEDURES FOR SEIZING A SEAGOING VESSEL AND RELEASING IT FOR EXECUTION OF THE JUDGMENT

  Section 1. HIRING A FOREIGN COURT TO SEIZE A SEAGOING VESSEL

  Article 56. Hiring a foreign court to seize a seagoing vessel

  1. During the settlement of a lawsuit at a court or the settlement of a dispute at an arbitration tribunal, if there is a request for the seizure of a seagoing vessel, the competent court of Vietnam specified in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall hire a competent foreign court to seize the seagoing vessel.

  2. The Vietnamese court shall hire a foreign court to seize the seagoing vessel in accordance with Vietnamese law and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a member or according to the principle of reciprocity.

  Article 57. The written request for hiring a foreign court The written request for hiring a foreign court for the seizure of a seagoing vessel contains the following main contents:

  1. Date, month, year and place of making the judicial request;

  2. Name and address of the Vietnamese court making the judicial request;

  3. Name and address of the foreign court making the judicial request;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel subject to the request for arrest; port where the seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  5. Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  6. Name, address and nationality of the ship charterer or operator;

  7. Reason for the judicial request for the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  8. Time limit for the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  9. Persons responsible for losses or damage caused by the request for the arrest of a seagoing vessel being incorrect.

  Article 58. Procedures for judicial request for the arrest of a seagoing vessel

  1. A competent Vietnamese court that makes a judicial request for the arrest of a seagoing vessel by a competent foreign court shall prepare a request and send it to the Vietnamese Ministry of Justice. The request shall contain the following documents:

  a) A written request for judicial assistance for the arrest of a seagoing vessel by a competent Vietnamese court;

  b) A written request for judicial assistance for the arrest of a seagoing vessel;

  c) Other papers as requested by the competent foreign court.

  2. Within ten working days after receiving the request for judicial assistance for the arrest of a seagoing vessel, the Ministry of Justice shall record it in the register of judicial requests, check the validity of the request and transfer it to the competent foreign court in accordance with the provisions of the international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the foreign country are parties or through diplomatic channels. If the request is invalid, the Ministry of Justice shall return it to the court that has prepared the request and clearly state the reason.

  3. Within five working days after receiving a written notice of the result of the judicial assistance from the competent foreign court, the Ministry of Justice shall transfer that notice to the competent Vietnamese court that has sent the request for judicial assistance for the arrest of a seagoing vessel.

  SECTION 2. EXECUTION OF JUDICIAL ASSISTANCE FOR THE ARREST OF SEAFOOT VESSELS OF FOREIGN COURTS

  Article 59. Principles of judicial assistance for the arrest of seafoots

  1. Judicial assistance for the arrest of seafoots between Vietnamese courts and foreign courts shall be carried out on the principles of respect for the independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of States, non-interference in each other’s internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit, in accordance with international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory and Vietnamese law.

  2. In cases where the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and a foreign country have not signed or acceded to an international treaty providing for judicial assistance for the arrest of seafoots, judicial assistance for the arrest of seafoots may be accepted by a Vietnamese court on the principle of reciprocity, but not contrary to Vietnamese law, international law and international practice.

  Article 60. Principles of execution of judicial mandates for the arrest of seafoots

  1. Vietnamese courts shall execute judicial mandates of foreign courts for the arrest of seafoots in accordance with international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory or on the principle of reciprocity.

  2. Vietnamese courts shall not accept the execution of judicial mandates of foreign courts for the arrest of seafoots in the following cases:

  a) The execution of judicial mandates for the arrest of seafoots infringes upon Vietnam’s sovereignty or threatens Vietnam’s security;

  b) The execution of judicial mandates for the arrest of seagoing vessels is not within the jurisdiction of Vietnamese courts.

  Article 61. Procedures for judicial entrustment of the arrest of seagoing vessels

  1. The judicial request of a foreign court for the arrest of a seagoing vessel by a Vietnamese court must be made in writing and sent to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam in accordance with the provisions of the international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory or the provisions of Vietnamese law.

  2. The Ministry of Justice of Vietnam shall receive the written judicial request for the arrest of a seagoing vessel and immediately transfer it to the competent Vietnamese court for decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 62. Written judicial authorization for the arrest of a seagoing vessel

  The judicial mandate document on the arrest of a seagoing vessel contains the following main contents:

  1. Date, month, year and place of making the written judicial mandate;

  2. Name and address of the foreign court that has entrusted the judicial process;

  3. Name and address of the Vietnamese court that performs the judicial mandate;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel subject to the request for arrest; port where the seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  5. Name, address and nationality of the master of the vessel;

  6. Name, address and nationality of the vessel charterer or operator;

  7. Reason for the judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  8. Time limit for the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  9. Person responsible for losses or damage caused by the incorrect request for arrest of the seagoing vessel.

  Article 63. Receipt of the written judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel

  1. Immediately after receiving the written judicial mandate for the arrest of a seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences, the Vietnamese Ministry of Justice shall record it in the register and send a written judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences to the competent court for decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  2. Immediately after receiving the written judicial mandate for the arrest of a seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences, the competent court for decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record it in the register of received applications. The Chief Justice shall immediately assign a judge to handle the written judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel.

  Article 64. Consideration of the written judicial mandate for the arrest of a seagoing vessel

  1. Within forty-eight hours from the time of receiving the written judicial mandate for the arrest of a seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences, the judge shall consider and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To accept the written judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel if he/she deems that the principle of mutual legal assistance and the principle of execution of the judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel are observed;

  b) To return the written judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel if he/she deems that the principle of mutual legal assistance or the principle of execution of the judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel is violated or that the handling of the written judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel is beyond the jurisdiction of that court.

  2. In case of decision to return the written judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel, the court shall immediately send such decision together with the written judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel and the enclosed documents and evidences to the Vietnamese Ministry of Justice for notification to the foreign court.

  Article 65. Decision on arrest of a seagoing vessel under judicial mandate of a foreign court

  1. A judge shall issue a decision to arrest a seagoing vessel under the judicial mandate of a foreign court immediately after the applicant for the arrest of the seagoing vessel has presented receipts and documents proving that he/she has taken the financial security measures for the request for the arrest of the seagoing vessel specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 5 and paid the fee for the arrest of the seagoing vessel specified in Article 6 of this Ordinance in Vietnam, except otherwise provided for by an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing vessel under the judicial mandate of a foreign court must contain the following principal details:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Vietnamese court issuing the decision;

  c) Name of the foreign court that has issued the judicial mandate;

  d) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing vessel under the judicial mandate of the foreign court;

  dd) Reason for the request for the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  e) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel to be arrested; port where the seagoing vessel is engaged in navigation;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship captain;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  i) Name, address and nationality of the ship charterer or operator;

  k) The Court’s assessment and legal grounds for accepting the judicial mandate for the arrest of the seagoing vessel;

  l) The Court’s decisions.

  3. The decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel under the judicial mandate of a foreign court shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaints or petitions.

  4. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel under the judicial mandate of a foreign court to the Director of the Port Authority for implementation as prescribed in Article 9 of this Ordinance; and immediately send such decision to the Ministry of Justice and the Procuracy of the same level.

  Article 66. Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints and petitions regarding the decision on the arrest of the seagoing vessel under the judicial mandate of a foreign court

  1. The ship captain, owner, charterer or operator may lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court regarding the decision to arrest the ship under the judicial mandate of a foreign court. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty-eight hours from the time the ship captain receives the court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may lodge a written request with the Chief Justice of the Court regarding the decision to arrest the ship under the judicial mandate of a foreign court. The time limit for lodging a request is forty-eight hours from the time the Procuracy of the same level receives the court's decision.

  2. Within forty-eight hours from the time of receiving the written complaint or request specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Chief Justice of the Court shall consider and settle the complaint or request and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to arrest the ship under the judicial mandate of a foreign court.

  b) To annul the decision to arrest the ship under the judicial mandate of a foreign court.

  3. The decision of the Chief Justice of the Court on settlement of the complaint or request is the final decision.

  Article 67. Grounds for releasing a ship under judicial mandate A ship under judicial mandate shall be immediately released when one of the following grounds is met:

  1. The decision to arrest the ship is annulled.

  2. The time limit for the detention of the ship under the decision of the Court has expired;

  3. At the request of the foreign Court that has entrusted the detention of the ship;

  Article 68. Request for release of the ship under judicial custody In the case specified in Clause 3, Article 67 of this Ordinance, the foreign Court must have a written request for release of the ship. The written request for release of the ship under judicial custody contains the following main contents:

  1. Date, month, year and place of making the request;

  2. Name and address of the foreign Court requesting the release of the ship;

  3. Name of the Vietnamese Court receiving the request;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the ship under detention; port where the detained ship is operating;

  5. Number, date, month, year of the decision on detention of the ship and the Court that has issued such decision;

  6. Reasons for requesting the release of a seized ship.

  Article 69. Sending a written request for the release of a seized ship under judicial custody A foreign court shall send a written request for the release of a seized ship under judicial custody and the enclosed documents and evidence to the Vietnamese Ministry of Justice for transfer to the court that issued the decision to seize the ship.

  Article 70. Decision on the release of a seized ship under judicial custody

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for the release of a seized ship under judicial custody, the Chief Justice shall assign a judge to handle the release of the seized ship under judicial custody.

  2. Within 24 hours from the date of receipt of the written request for the release of the seized ship under judicial custody and the enclosed documents and evidence, the assigned judge shall consider and issue a decision on the release of the seized ship if there are grounds for doing so. If the request is rejected due to lack of grounds, the judge shall notify the requester in writing, clearly stating the reasons for rejecting the request for the release of the seized ship under judicial custody.

  3. A decision on the release of a seized ship under judicial custody must contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Vietnamese court that issued the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the court to issue a decision to release the seized seagoing vessel;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, deadweight and other characteristics of the released seagoing vessel; port where the seized seagoing vessel is released;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship charterer or operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for releasing the seized seagoing vessel;

  i) Court decisions.

  4. The decision to release the seized seagoing vessel under the judicial mandate of a foreign court shall take effect immediately.

  5. The court shall deliver two copies of the decision on release of the seized ship under the judicial custody of the foreign court to the port authority director for implementation in accordance with Article 9 of this Ordinance; and immediately send such decision to the Ministry of Justice and the Procuracy of the same level.

  This Ordinance takes effect on July 1, 2009.

  Article 72. Implementation guidance

  The Government, the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuracy shall, within the scope of their functions and tasks, guide the implementation of this Ordinance.
