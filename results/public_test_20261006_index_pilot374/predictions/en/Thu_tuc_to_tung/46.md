                                                     DECREE

     DETAILED REGULATIONS ON ORDER AND PROCEDURES FOR PREPARING AND PAYING THE COURT COSTS

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Government dated February 18, 2025;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on amendments to the Law on Securities, the Law on Accounting, the Law on Independent Audit, the Law on State Budget, the Law on Management and Use of Public Property, the Law on Tax Administration, the Law on Personal Income Tax, the Law on National Reserve, and the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated November 29, 2024;

  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Legal Costs dated December 11, 2024;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates the Decree detailing the order and procedures for preparing estimates and paying legal costs.

  Chapter I

                                                 GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Decree elaborates the order and procedures for making estimates and paying out funds for payment of legal costs.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Decree applies to competent agencies conducting legal proceedings and agencies, organizations and individuals involved in making estimates and paying out funds for payment of legal costs.

  Article 3. Making estimates, allocating and settling funds for payment of legal costs

  The making of estimates, allocation of estimates and settlement of funds for payment of legal costs shall comply with the law on state budget, the law on accounting and relevant laws. This Decree provides additional guidance on the following matters:

  1. Making estimates

  Every year, at the time of making state budget estimates, based on the actual payment of legal costs incurred in the previous year and the estimated payment in the current year, the competent agency conducting legal proceedings and relevant agencies shall make estimates of funds for payment of legal costs according to the Law on Legal Costs, and then compile them into their own estimates and send them to their superior management agencies for consideration and compilation into the first-level estimate of their units, which shall then be sent to the finance agency of the same level for submission to the competent authority according to the law on state budget.

  2. Budget allocation

  After the Prime Minister or the People’s Committee assigns the budget estimate, the first-level budget estimate unit at the central and local levels shall allocate and assign the budget estimate to the budget-issuing units and subordinate budget units in accordance with the law on state budget. The budget estimate for regular expenditures for payment of legal costs shall be allocated to the fund portion not subject to autonomy of the agency or unit.

  3. Final settlement

  The agencies and units shall be responsible for final settlement of funds for payment of legal costs in accordance with this Decree and compile them in the annual budget settlement reports of the agencies and units in accordance with the law on final settlement of regular state budget expenditures, the law on accounting, and other relevant guiding documents and legal provisions.

  Chapter II

    REGULATIONS ON PROCEDURES FOR PREPAYMENT AND PAYMENT OF LEGAL COSTS BETWEEN COMPETENT PROCURING AGENCIES AND PROCURING ORGANIZATIONS, INDIVIDUALS

  Article 4. Procedures for payment of on-site consideration and assessment costs; on-site consideration costs

  1. After the on-site consideration and assessment or on-site consideration is completed, the person invited to participate in on-site consideration and assessment, the person invited to participate in on-site consideration, the person conducting on-site consideration shall send 01 set of application for payment to the competent agency conducting legal proceedings by one of the following forms: via postal services, in person, or online on the electronic platform if the infrastructure and technical conditions are met.

  2. An application for payment of expenses for persons invited to conduct on-site examination and appraisal, persons invited to conduct on-site examination, persons conducting on-site examination includes:

  a) An application for payment made using Form No. 01 enclosed with this Decree.

  b) Documents enclosed with the application for payment, which are the originals of invoices and legal documents related to the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred when invited to conduct on-site examination and appraisal, persons conducting on-site examination or persons conducting on-site examination.

  3. If an application for payment is incomplete as prescribed, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority shall notify the applicant for payment and clearly state the reason for supplementing the application as prescribed. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application, the competent procedural authority shall make payment of expenses.

  4. The competent procedural authority shall pay expenses for use of services and other expenses (if any) under contracts, invoices and receipts for service charges as prescribed by law.

  Article 5. Procedures for advance payment and payment of property valuation expenses

  1. In case of need for advance payment of property valuation expenses, within 05 working days from the date of receipt of the request for property valuation from the competent procedural authority, the Property Valuation Council shall send 01 set of application for advance payment to the competent procedural authority by one of the following forms: via postal services, in person or online on the electronic platform if the infrastructure and technical conditions for the advance payment procedure are met.

  2. An application for advance payment of property valuation expenses includes:

  a) Application for advance payment of asset valuation costs using Form No. 02 enclosed with this Decree.

  b) Table of total cost of asset valuation and calculation basis of asset valuation costs.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete application for advance payment of asset valuation costs, the competent procedural authority shall make the advance payment. The maximum advance payment amount shall not exceed the asset valuation costs calculated by the asset valuation council and submitted in the application for advance payment as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the asset valuation result, the asset valuation council shall send 01 set of application for payment of asset valuation costs to the competent procedural authority by post, in person or online on the electronic platform if the infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures are met.

  5. Application for payment of asset valuation costs shall include:

  a) Application for payment using Form No. 01 enclosed with this Decree.

  b) Documents attached to the application for payment, which are the written request for valuation; the original invoices and legal documents related to the necessary expenses, which are consistent with the expenses specified in Articles 21, 22, 23 and 24 of the Ordinance on Procedural Costs Arising During Valuation.

  6. In case the application for payment of asset valuation costs is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority shall notify the asset valuation council and clearly state the reason for supplementing the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for payment of valuation costs, the competent procedural authority shall pay the costs to the property valuation council as prescribed.

  In case the advance payment for the price assessment is insufficient, the competent procedural authority shall pay the remaining amount.

  In case the advance payment exceeds the valuation cost, the asset valuation council shall refund the difference within 02 working days from the date of submission of the application for payment of valuation cost.

  Article 6. Procedures for advance payment and payment of assessment costs

  1. Procedures for advance payment for assessment costs

  a) In civil and administrative procedures

  The procedures for advance payment of assessment costs shall comply with Article 38 of the Ordinance on Costs of Legal Proceedings.

  b) In criminal proceedings, the procedure for advance payment of assessment costs shall comply with Article 44 of the Ordinance on Procedural Costs.

  Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of the decision to request the assessment from the competent procedural authority, the assessment organization or individual shall send 01 set of application for advance payment of assessment costs to the competent procedural authority by one of the following forms: via postal services, in person or online on the electronic environment if the infrastructure and technical conditions for the advance payment procedure are met.

  2. The application for advance payment of assessment costs includes:

  a) The written request for advance payment made using Form No. 02 enclosed with this Decree.

  b) The table of total cost of assessment and the basis for calculating the cost of assessment.

  3. Time limit for payment of advance payment and level of advance payment of assessment costs

  a) In civil and administrative procedures: The time limit for payment of advance payment of assessment costs shall comply with Article 38 of the Ordinance on Procedural Costs.

  b) In criminal procedures: The time limit for payment of advance payment of assessment costs shall comply with the notice of request for advance payment of the assessment organization or individual specified in Clause 2 Article 44 of the Ordinance on Procedural Costs.

  c) The maximum level of advance payment shall not exceed the cost of assessment calculated by the assessment organization or individual and submitted in the application for advance payment as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Within 10 days from the date on which the assessment results are available, the assessment organization or individual shall send 01 set of application for payment of assessment costs to the competent agency conducting legal proceedings by one of the following methods: via postal services, in person or online on the electronic platform if the infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures are met.

  5. The application for payment of assessment costs includes:

  a) The payment request form Form No. 01 issued together with this Decree.

  b) Documents attached to the payment request form, which are the originals of relevant invoices and legal documents to prove necessary and reasonable expenses incurred during the assessment process.

  6. In case the application for payment of assessment costs is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the date on which the application is received, the competent agency conducting legal proceedings shall notify the applicant and clearly state the reason for supplementing the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date on which the complete and valid application for payment of assessment costs is received, the competent agency conducting legal proceedings shall pay the assessment costs to the assessment organization or individual as prescribed.

  In case the advance payment for assessment costs is insufficient, the competent agency conducting legal proceedings shall pay the remaining amount.

  In case the advance payment exceeds the assessment costs, the assessment organization or individual shall return the excess amount within 02 working days from the date on which the application for payment of assessment costs is sent.

  Article 7. Procedures for payment of costs to the jury

  1. Within 10 days after the end of the court session, the jury shall send 01 set of application for payment of costs to the jury to the court by one of the following forms: by post, in person or online on the electronic platform if the infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures are met.

  2. The application for payment of costs to the jury includes:

  a) The written request for payment made using Form No. 01 enclosed with this Decree;

  b) Documents enclosed with the written request for payment, including the list of time spent on attending the court session and studying the jury’s application, certified by the presiding judge; the original invoices and legal documents related to the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred as prescribed in Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 Article 46 of the Ordinance on Procedural Costs.

  3. If the application for payment of costs to the jury is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days after receiving the application, the court shall notify the jury of this and clearly state the reason for supplementing the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days after receiving a complete and valid application for payment of costs to the jury, the court shall pay the costs to the jury as prescribed.

  Article 8. Payment of costs to lawyers, legal assistants and people’s defense counsel in cases where defense counsel is appointed in criminal proceedings

  1. Procedures for payment of expenses for lawyers and legal assistants in case of appointment of defense counsel shall comply with the law on lawyers and legal assistance.

  2. Procedures for payment of expenses for people’s defense counsel in case of appointment of defense counsel shall comply with the following procedures:

  a) An application for payment of expenses for people’s defense counsel shall include:

  An application form for payment made using Form No. 01 enclosed with this Decree.

  Documents enclosed with the application form for payment are a list of working hours of the people’s defense counsel certified by the competent procedural authority or the person conducting the proceedings; the original invoices and legal documents related to the necessary and reasonable expenses specified in Points b, c, d and dd Clause 2 Article 49 of the Ordinance on Procedural Expenses.

  b) After the completion of each stage of the proceedings (investigation, prosecution, trial), the people’s defense counsel shall send an application for payment to the competent procedural authority requesting the appointment of a defense counsel as a people’s defense counsel by one of the following methods: by post, in person or online on the electronic platform if the infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures are met.

  c) If the application for payment of expenses for people’s defense counsel is incomplete or invalid, within 3 days from the date of receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority shall notify the people’s defense counsel and clearly state the reason for supplementing the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for payment of expenses for people’s defense counsel, the competent procedural authority shall pay expenses for the people’s defense counsel as prescribed.

  Article 9. Payment of expenses for witnesses, observers, interpreters and translators

  1. After the completion of witness, observer, interpreter or translation work, the witness, observer, interpreter or translator shall send the application for payment to the competent procedural authority by post, in person or online on the electronic platform if the infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures are met.

  2. An application for payment of expenses for a witness, observer, interpreter or translator shall include:

  a) An application for payment of expenses for a witness, observer, interpreter or translator made using Form No. 01 enclosed with this Decree;

  b) Documents attached to the application for payment, which are the originals of relevant invoices and legal documents to prove necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by the witness, observer, interpreter or translator when performing the witness, observation, interpretation or translation work as prescribed.

  3. If an application for payment of expenses for a witness, observer, interpreter or translator is incomplete or invalid, the competent procedural authority shall notify the witness, observer, interpreter or translator of this within 3 days from the date of receipt of the application and clearly state the reason for supplementing the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for payment, the competent procedural authority shall pay expenses for the witness, observer, interpreter or translator.

  Article 10. Payment of expenses for overseas judicial missions

  1. Procedures for payment of costs of judicial delegation abroad in civil, administrative and criminal procedures shall comply with the law on judicial cooperation.

  2. Procedures for payment of costs of service of process and notification of procedural documents abroad in civil and administrative procedures through overseas representative offices of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall comply with procedures prescribed by the Supreme People’s Court in coordination with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  Article 11. Payment of costs of participation in court sessions and sessions for settlement of cases and lawsuits

  1. After the court session or session for settlement of cases and lawsuits is concluded, persons specified in Clause 1, Article 69 of the Ordinance on Costs of Procedures shall submit a request for payment to the competent authority conducting the procedure by one of the following forms: via postal services, in person or online on the electronic environment if they meet the conditions regarding infrastructure and technology for payment procedures.

  2. A request for payment of costs of participation in court sessions and sessions for settlement of cases and lawsuits shall include:

  a) A written request for payment of costs of participation in court sessions and sessions using Form No. 01 issued together with this Decree.

  b) Documents enclosed with the written request for payment, which are the originals of invoices and legal documents related to the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by the participant in the court session or session for settlement of cases and lawsuits when attending the court session or session for settlement of cases and lawsuits.

  3. In case the request for payment of costs of participation in court sessions and sessions for settlement of cases and lawsuits is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the request, the competent authority conducting the procedure shall notify the participant in the court session or session and clearly state the reason for supplementing the request as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the complete and valid application for payment, the competent procedural authority shall pay the expenses to the court participant or meeting participant in accordance with regulations.

  Chapter III

                                            IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 12. Effect

  1. This Decree takes effect from the date of its signing.

  2. In case the referenced documents in this Decree are amended, supplemented or replaced, the amended, supplemented or replaced documents shall prevail.

  3. The Government’s Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP dated August 14, 2014 detailing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Costs of Expertise and Valuation; Costs of Witnesses and Interpreters in Legal Proceedings (Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP) shall cease to be effective from the date this Decree takes effect.

  4. In cases where the application for payment of costs of expertise, valuation, costs of witnesses and interpreters in legal proceedings is being processed according to the provisions of Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP before the effective date of this Decree, the application shall continue to be processed according to the provisions of Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP. The competent procedural authorities, agencies, organizations and individuals conducting legal proceedings shall bear the full responsibility for the completeness and validity of the application, the order and procedures for payment of expenses in accordance with this Clause, ensuring compliance with law.

  5. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of Government-attached agencies, and presidents of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.
