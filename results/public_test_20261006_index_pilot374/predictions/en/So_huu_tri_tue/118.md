                                                    DECREE
    DETAILED PROVISIONS AND GUIDELINES FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE CIVIL CODE ON COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS
                                                   THE GOVERNMENT
   Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;
   Pursuant to the Civil Code of June 14, 2005;
   Pursuant to the Law on Intellectual Property dated November 29, 2005;
   Pursuant to the proposal of the Minister of Culture and Information,

DECREE
   Chapter 1:
                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS
   Article 1. Scope
   This Decree elaborates and guides the implementation of a number of articles of the Civil Code and the Intellectual Property Law on copyright and related rights.
   Article 2. Regulated entities
   This Decree applies to Vietnamese organizations and individuals; foreign organizations and individuals engaged in activities related to copyright and related rights.
   Article 3. Protection of copyright and related rights
   1. Protection of copyright means protection of the rights of authors to literary, artistic and scientific works as prescribed in Article 738 of the Civil Code and Articles 18, 19 and 20 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Protection of related rights means protection of the rights of performers to performances; the rights of phonogram and video recording producers to phonograms and video recordings; the rights of broadcasting organizations to broadcasting programs and satellite signals carrying encrypted programs as prescribed in Articles 745, 746, 747 and 748 of the Civil Code and Articles 29, 30 and 31 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   Article 4. Interpretation of terms
   In this Decree, the following terms are construed as follows:
   1. A posthumous work means a work first published after the author's death.
   2. An anonymous work means a work without the author's name (real or pen name) on it when it is published.
   3. The original work means a work existing in a material form on which the creation of the work is first fixed.
   4. A copy of a work means a direct or indirect copy of a part or the whole of the work. A photograph of a work is also a copy of the work.
   5. Fixation means the expression in writing, other characters, lines, shapes, layout, colors, sounds, images or the reproduction of sounds and images in a certain material form, from which they can be recognized, copied or communicated.
   6. A recording means the fixation of sounds and images of a performance or other sounds and images, or the fixation of the reproduction of sounds and images not in the form of fixation attached to a film work or other audiovisual work.

   7. A copy of a recording means a direct or indirect copy of a part or the whole of the recording.
   8. The public performance, phonogram or videogram is the delivery of copies of the public performance, phonogram or videogram to the public with the consent of the relevant right holder.
   9. Rebroadcasting means the simultaneous transmission of a broadcast program by a broadcasting organization to another broadcasting organization. The reception of a broadcast is also considered as retransmission.
   10. A satellite signal carrying a coded program means a signal carrying a program transmitted via a satellite in a form in which the audio or visual characteristics, or both, have been altered in order to prevent the unauthorized reception of the program.
   Article 5. The State's policy on copyright and related rights The State's policy on copyright and related rights specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Intellectual Property Law includes:
   1. Financial support for the purchase of copyright for state agencies and organizations tasked with disseminating works, performances, phonograms, videograms, broadcast programs of ideological, scientific and artistic value for the public interest, in order to contribute to socio-economic development.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Planning and Investment and relevant agencies in guiding the formulation of financial plans (creation of sources, funds), and mechanisms for the purchase of copyright.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall approve the list of works eligible for copyright purchase support for central agencies and organizations; the provincial People's Committees shall approve the list of works eligible for copyright purchase support for local agencies and organizations.
   2. Prioritize investment in, and training and retraining of officials, civil servants and public employees engaged in the management and enforcement of copyright and related rights protection from the central to the local level.
   To focus on training and retraining the team of officials to perform the task of self-protecting rights in the activities of collective representation of copyright and related rights.
   3. Prioritize scientific research topics related to law, mechanisms, policies, and application of scientific and technological measures and technologies for copyright and related rights protection.
   4. To enhance the education of knowledge on copyright and related rights in the system of schools at all levels. The Ministry of Education and Training shall take charge and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information in including the teaching of copyright and related rights in the curriculum of universities, colleges and vocational high schools.
   Article 6. Content and responsibilities for state management of copyright and related rights
  1. The Government shall unify the state management of copyright and related rights.
   2. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall be responsible to the Government for the state management of copyright and related rights, and shall have the following tasks and powers:
   a. Formulate and direct the implementation of strategies, laws, mechanisms and policies on protection of copyright and related rights;
   b. Promulgate, organize and guide the implementation of legal documents on copyright and related rights in accordance with law;
   c. To take measures to protect the legitimate rights of the State, organizations and individuals in the field of copyright and related rights protection;
   d. To manage copyright over works (including computer programs and data collections) and related rights over performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasting programs owned by the State in accordance with law;
   dd. To provide regulations on the provision, cooperation, commissioning and guarantee of copyright over works and related rights over performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasting programs;

   e. To develop and manage the copyright and related rights apparatus; organize the training and retraining of officials on copyright and related rights;
   g. To manage the activities of organizations representing collective copyright and related rights;
   h. To grant, re-grant, change and cancel the Certificate of Copyright Registration, Certificate of Related Rights Registration and other procedures related to the Certificate of Copyright Registration and the Certificate of Related Rights Registration;
   i. To establish and manage the National Register of Copyright and Related Rights;
   k. To publish and distribute the Official Gazette on copyright and related rights;
   l. To organize and direct the education, propaganda, dissemination of knowledge, laws, mechanisms, policies and information and statistics on copyright and related rights;
   m. To organize and manage the assessment of copyright and related rights;
   n. To inspect, examine and handle violations of laws on copyright and related rights; to settle complaints and denunciations about copyright and related rights;
   o. To cooperate internationally in copyright and related rights.
   3. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall take charge and coordinate with ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies, People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities (hereinafter referred to as provincial People’s Committees) in the state management of copyright and related rights.
   The Copyright Office of Literature and Art is an agency of the Ministry of Culture and Information assisting the Minister of Culture and Information in performing the state management of copyright and related rights. The Minister of Culture and Information shall specify the functions, tasks and powers of the Copyright Office of Literature and Art in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall coordinate with the Ministry of Science and Technology in formulating general policies, laws on intellectual property protection, consolidating general information on intellectual property, implementing general international cooperation projects on intellectual property and other general tasks under the direction of the Government.
   4. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and provincial People’s Committees shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information in the state management of copyright and related rights.
   Article 7. State management competence of provincial People’s Committees
   1. Provincial People’s Committees shall have the following tasks and powers:
   a/ To organize the guidance on the implementation of the laws on copyright and related rights in their localities;
   b/ To issue documents within their competence to guide and direct the implementation of the laws and policies on copyright and related rights in accordance with the characteristics and situation of their localities;
   c/ To organize activities for the protection of copyright and related rights in their localities; to take measures to protect the legitimate rights and interests of the State, organizations and individuals in copyright and related rights;
   d/ To inspect, examine and handle within their competence complaints, denunciations and violations of the laws on copyright and related rights in their localities;
   e/ To guide and receive applications for registration of copyright and related rights within their competence and in accordance with the law;
   f/ To coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information, relevant ministries, branches and provincial People’s Committees in the protection of copyright and related rights.
   2. The Department of Culture and Information shall assist the provincial People’s Committees in performing the state management functions of copyright and related rights.
   The presidents of the provincial People’s Committees shall specify the functions, tasks and powers of the Department of Culture and Information, the district-level People’s Committees and the commune-level People’s Committees in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   Chapter II

                                                 COPYRIGHT
   Article 8. Authors
   1. An author is a person who directly creates a part or the whole of a literary, artistic or scientific work, including:
   a/ A Vietnamese individual whose work is protected by copyright;
   b/ A foreign individual whose work is created and expressed in a certain material form in Vietnam;
   c/ A foreign individual whose work is first published in Vietnam;
   d/ A foreign individual whose work is protected in Vietnam under an international copyright treaty to which Vietnam is a signatory.
   2. Organizations and individuals that provide assistance, comments or materials for others to create works shall not be recognized as authors.
   Article 9. Works expressed in other characters
   A work expressed in other characters specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a work expressed in symbols instead of written characters such as Braille for the visually impaired, shorthand symbols and other similar symbols which the recipients can copy in various forms.
   Article 10. Lectures, speeches and other talks
   A lecture, speech or other talk specified in Point b Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a work expressed in spoken language and must be fixed in a certain material form.
   Article 11.- Press works
   The press works specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property include the following genres: news reports, flash reports, narratives, interviews, reflections, investigations, commentaries, editorials, specialized articles, press notes and other press genres for publication or broadcast on print, audio, video, electronic or other media.
   Article 12. Musical works
   A musical work specified in Point d Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a work expressed in musical notes in a musical score or other musical characters, with or without lyrics, independent of performance or non-performance.
   Article 13. Theatrical works
   A stage work specified in Point dd Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a work of the performance art form, including drama (spoken drama, musical drama, opera, silent drama), circus, dance, puppetry and other forms of stage works.
   Article 14: Film works
   A film work and a work created by the same method specified in Point e Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a work composed of a series of successive images creating a motion effect with or without sound, displayed on a certain material and can be distributed and communicated to the public by technical devices and technologies, including film, documentary, science film, animation and other similar forms.
   Article 15: Sculptural and applied fine art works
   1. A sculptural work specified in Point g Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a work expressed by lines, colors, shapes, layouts such as: painting, graphics, sculpture, installation art and similar forms of expression, existing in a unique copy. For graphics, it can be expressed up to the 50th version, numbered and signed by the author.
   2. An applied fine art work specified in Point g Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a work expressed by lines, colors, shapes, layouts with useful functions that can be attached to a useful object, mass-produced by hand or machine such as: emblems; handicrafts; forms of expression on products, product packaging.
   Article 16: Photographic works
   A photographic work specified in Point h Clause 1 Article 14 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a work expressing the image of the objective world on a luminous material or on a medium where the image is created or can be created by any technical method (chemical, electronic or other method);
   Static images taken from a film or similar work shall not be considered as photographic works but as part of that film or similar work.
   Article 17. Architectural works
   An architectural work specified in Point i, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law is a design drawing in any form that expresses a creative idea of a house, construction work, or spatial planning (construction planning) that has been built or not yet built. Architectural works include design drawings of floor plans, facades, sections, perspectives, and creative ideas of houses, construction works, architectural complexes, spatial organization, and landscape architecture of a region, city, urban system, urban functional area, or rural residential area.
   A model or scale drawing of a house, construction work, or spatial planning is considered an independent architectural work.
   Article 18. Plans, diagrams, maps, and drawings
   A plan, diagram, map, or drawing specified in Point k, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law includes a diagram, diagram, map, or drawing related to topography and types of scientific and architectural works.
   Article 19. Copyright on film and stage works
   1. A film or stage work created by a collective of authors shall be considered a collective work. The persons participating in the creation of a film or stage work specified in Clause 1, Article 21 of the Intellectual Property Law shall enjoy personal rights to their creative parts as prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, and 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law. The producer, director, and scriptwriter may agree on the exercise of the rights to name the film work specified in Clause 1, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law and the revision of the script of the film work specified in Clause 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Organizations and individuals that invest in financial and material and technical resources for the production of film works or staging of theatrical works specified in Clause 2 Article 21 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be owners of the rights specified in Clause 3 Article 19 and Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Organizations and individuals that invest in financial and material and technical resources for the production of film works or staging of theatrical works may agree on the exercise of the rights specified in Clause 3 Article 19 and Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the obligations specified in Clause 3 Article 21 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Article 20: Use of folk literary and artistic works
   1. Folk literary and artistic works specified in Points a, b and c Clause 1 Article 23 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be protected regardless of their fixed form.
   2. The use of folk literary and artistic works specified in Clause 2 Article 23 of the Law on Intellectual Property refers to the research, collection and introduction of the authentic value of folk literary and artistic works.
   3. Users of folk literary and artistic works specified in Clause 2 of this Article shall agree on the remuneration for folk literary and artistic works keepers and shall enjoy copyright over their research, collection and introduction.
   4. The citation of the origin of folk literary and artistic works specified in Clause 2 Article 23 of the Law on Intellectual Property refers to the indication of the geographical name of the community of residents where the folk literary and artistic works are formed.
   Article 21: Objects not falling within the scope of copyright protection

   1. News of current events that are purely reported as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 15 of the Law on Intellectual Property refers to daily news reports that are merely informative and not creative.
   2. Administrative documents prescribed in Clause 2 Article 15 of the Law on Intellectual Property include documents of state agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-political-occupational organizations, social organizations, socio-occupational organizations, economic organizations, people’s armed units and other organizations as prescribed by law.
   Article 22. Personal rights
   1. The right to name a work as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property does not apply to works translated from one language to another.
   2. The right to publish a work or permit another person to publish a work as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property refers to the distribution of a work to the public in sufficient copies to meet the reasonable needs of the public depending on the nature of the work, which is carried out by the author or copyright owner or by another individual or organization with the consent of the author or copyright owner.
   The publication of a work does not include the performance of a theatrical, film or musical work; the public reading of a literary work; the broadcasting of a literary or artistic work; the display of a sculptural work; or the construction of a work from an architectural work.
   3. The right to protect the integrity of a work and prevent others from modifying or mutilating it as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property refers to the prevention of others from modifying or mutilating the work, except where the author has agreed.
   4. The author of a computer program and the investor producing the computer program may agree on the naming and development of the computer program.
   Article 23. Property rights
   1. The right to perform a work in public as prescribed in Point b Clause 1 Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be exclusively performed by the copyright owner or permitted by the copyright owner to another person to perform the work in person or via audio or video recording programs or any technical means accessible to the public.
   For the purposes of this Clause, performing a work in public includes performing the work anywhere other than at home.
   2. The right to make copies as prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be the exclusive right of the copyright owner to make or permit another person to make copies of the work by any means or in any form, including the regular or temporary storage of the work in electronic form.
   3. The right to distribute the original or copy of a work as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be the exclusive right of the copyright owner to make or permit another person to make copies of the work by any form or technical means accessible to the public for sale, lease or other forms of transfer of the original or copy of the work.
   For sculptural works and photographic works, the right to distribute also includes the right to display and exhibit the work in public.
   4. The right to communicate the work to the public via wired, wireless, electronic information network or any other technical means as prescribed in Point dd Clause 1 Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be the exclusive right of the copyright owner or permitted by the copyright owner to another person to communicate the work or copy of the work to the public accessible at a location and time chosen by the copyright owner.
   5. The right to lease the original or copy of a motion picture or computer program as prescribed in Point e Clause 1 Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be exclusively performed by the copyright owner or permitted by the copyright owner to another person to lease the work for a fixed term.

   The right to lease a computer program shall not be applied when the program is not the main object of lease, such as a computer program attached to the normal operation of a type of transport vehicle or other technical equipment and machinery.
   Article 24. Reasonable quotation and import of copies of works
   1. Reasonable quotation of a work without infringing upon the author's intention for commentary or illustration in one's own work specified in Point b Clause 1 Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law must satisfy the following conditions:
   a/ The quoted part is only for the purpose of introducing, commenting or clarifying the issue mentioned in one's own work;
   b/ The quantity and substance of the quoted part from the work used for quotation do not cause harm to the copyright of the work used for quotation; and are consistent with the nature and characteristics of the type of work used for quotation.
   2. The import of copies of other people's works for exclusive use specified in Point k Clause 1 Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law applies only to the case of importing no more than one copy.
   3. The use of works in the cases specified in Clause 1 Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law does not apply to the reproduction of architectural works, sculptural works and computer programs.
   Article 25. Reproduction of works
   1. Reproduction of the provisions in Point a, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall apply to cases of scientific research and teaching by individuals not for commercial purposes.
   2. Copying a work for archiving in a library for research purposes specified in Point dd Clause 1 Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law means copying no more than one copy.
   3. Libraries shall not copy and distribute copies of works to the public, including digital copies thereof.

Article 26. Duration of copyright protection
   1. The term of protection of property rights and personal rights specified in Clause 3 Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property for the copyright of a work is 50 years from the date of first publication of the work.
   2. The term of protection of property rights and personal rights specified in Clause 3 Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property for photographic works and applied fine art works specified in Point a Clause 2 Article 27 of the Law on Intellectual Property is fifty years from the date of first publication of the work. Within the term of fifty years, if the work has not been published, the term of protection is fifty years from the date of fixation of the work.
   3. Since the Intellectual Property Law came into force on July 1, 2006, the duration of protection of photographic works and applied art works shall be calculated as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.
   Article 27. Copyright owners
   The copyright holders specified in Article 36 of the Intellectual Property Law include:
   1. Vietnamese organizations and individuals;
   2. Foreign organizations and individuals whose works are created and expressed in a certain material form in Vietnam;
   3. Foreign organizations and individuals whose works are first published in Vietnam;
   4. Foreign organizations and individuals whose works are protected in Vietnam under the International Copyright Treaty to which Vietnam is a signatory.
   Article 28: Copyright owners of anonymous works
   1. The anonymous works specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law are state-owned.
   2. In case the anonymous works are managed by organizations and individuals, these organizations and individuals shall enjoy the rights of the copyright owners.

   3. When the true identity of the copyright owners is determined, the ownership rights shall belong to these copyright owners, from the date on which the identity of the copyright owners is determined.
   Article 29. Use of state-owned works
  1. Organizations and individuals using state-owned works specified in Points a and b, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law, except for the cases specified in Clauses 2 and 3, Article 28 of this Decree, shall have to perform the following obligations:
   a/ To apply for permission to use;
   b/ To pay royalties, remuneration, and other material benefits;
   c/ To submit a copy of the work within thirty days from the date of its dissemination and circulation.
   2. Organizations and individuals performing the obligations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall do so at the Copyright Office of Literature and Art.
   3. The Copyright Office of Literature and Art shall be responsible for receiving the transfer of copyright in any form from organizations and individuals specified in Point c, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law in accordance with law.
   4. The Ministry of Culture and Information and the Ministry of Finance shall define the financial management regime specified in Point b, Clause 1 of this Article.
   Article 30. Use of works in the public domain
   1. Organizations and individuals that use works in the public domain specified in Article 43 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall respect personal rights specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 4 Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   The copyright holders of works in the public domain shall not enjoy the right to publish specified in Clause 3 Article 19 and property rights specified in Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   2. State agencies, organizations and individuals that have relevant rights and obligations when detecting acts of infringement of personal rights specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 4 Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property against works that have expired their protection period may request the infringers to stop the infringement, apologize, publicly correct, and compensate for damages; they may lodge complaints, denounce, and request competent state agencies to handle the infringement. Depending on the nature and severity of the infringement, the infringers may be handled according to administrative, civil or criminal laws.
   Political, social and professional organizations, social and professional organizations, and organizations representing collective copyright and related rights may request competent state agencies to protect personal rights of works of their members that have expired their protection period.
   Chapter III
                                               RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 31. Rights of performers
   1. Direct copying of performances fixed in sound recordings or video recordings as prescribed in Point b, Clause 3, Article 29 of the Intellectual Property Law means making other copies from such sound recordings or video recordings.
   2. Indirect copying of performances fixed in sound recordings or video recordings as prescribed in Point b, Clause 3, Article 29 of the Intellectual Property Law means making other copies not from such sound recordings or video recordings, such as copying from electronic information networks, broadcasting programs, related postal and telecommunications network services and other similar forms.
   3. Dissemination of unrecorded performances to the public in other ways as prescribed in Point c, Clause 3, Article 29 of the Intellectual Property Law means disseminating unrecorded performances to the public by any technical means other than broadcasting.
   Article 32. Use of related rights without permission or payment of royalties or remuneration Cases of use of related rights not for commercial purposes do not require permission or payment of royalties or remuneration, including:

   1. Making a copy for scientific research purposes as prescribed in Point a, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Making a copy for teaching purposes, except for performances, sound recordings, video recordings or broadcasting programs that have been published for teaching purposes as prescribed in Point b, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   Article 33. Reasonable quotation
   Reasonable quotation for the purpose of providing information specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of the Law on Intellectual Property is the use of excerpts for the purpose of purely providing information, which must comply with the following conditions:
   1. The quoted part is only for the purpose of introducing, commenting on or clarifying the issue in the provision of information;
   2. The quantity and substance of the excerpt from the performance, phonogram, video recording, broadcast program is not detrimental to the rights of the performer, the rights of the phonogram, video recording producer, the rights of the broadcasting organization to the performance, phonogram, video recording, broadcast program used for quotation; is consistent with the nature and characteristics of the performance, phonogram, video recording, broadcast program used for quotation.
   Article 34: Temporary copies
   The temporary copy of the broadcasting organization enjoying the broadcasting rights specified in Point d Clause 1 Article 32 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a time-limited copy, made by the broadcasting organization using its own equipment, for the purpose of serving the immediate broadcast of the broadcasting organization itself. In special cases, such copies may be stored in official archives.
   Article 35: Use of phonograms, video recordings
   1. Direct use of published phonograms, video recordings for commercial purposes to perform sponsored, advertised, or monetized broadcast programs in any form specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 33 of the Law on Intellectual Property is the use of such phonograms, video recordings by the broadcasting organization for broadcasting via radio or terrestrial, including satellite, digital media. Indirect use of published phonograms, video recordings for commercial purposes to perform sponsored, advertised, or monetized broadcast programs in any form specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 33 of the Law on Intellectual Property is the reception, replay of broadcast programs; the transmission of programs in the digital environment.
   2. Use of published phonograms, video recordings in business, commercial activities specified in Point b Clause 1 Article 33 of the Law on Intellectual Property is the use of published phonograms, video recordings by organizations, individuals directly or indirectly for use in restaurants, hotels, shops, supermarkets; karaoke, postal, telecommunications, digital service establishments; in tourism, aviation, public transport and other business, commercial activities.
   3. The remuneration of performers in case of phonogram or videogram recording used as prescribed in Article 33 of the Law on Intellectual Property depends on the agreement between the performer and the phonogram or videogram recording producer when performing the phonogram or videogram recording program.
   The ratio of division of remuneration, other material benefits agreed upon by the right holders or collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights.
   Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights may entrust a collective representative organization of copyright and related rights to collect and distribute remuneration, other material benefits. The entrusted collective representative organization of copyright and related rights shall receive a certain fee as agreed.
   Article 36: Owner of broadcasting program
   The owner of a broadcasting program specified in Clause 3, Article 44 of the Law on Intellectual Property is a broadcasting organization that invests its financial and technical facilities in broadcasting.

   When using works, phonogram or videogram recordings to produce broadcasting programs, broadcasting organizations must fulfill obligations to copyright owners, related rights owners in accordance with law.
   Chapter IV
                     CERTIFICATION OF COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS REGISTRATION
   Article 37. Registration of copyright and related rights

   1. Authors, copyright owners, and related rights owners under Article 50 of the Intellectual Property Law may directly or authorize other organizations or individuals to submit applications for registration of copyright and related rights at the Copyright Office of Literature and Art or at the Department of Culture and Information of the place where the authors, copyright owners, or related rights owners reside or have their offices.
   2. Foreign individuals and legal entities whose works, performances, phonograms, videograms, or broadcasts are protected under copyright and related rights under Clause 2 Article 13 and Article 17 of the Intellectual Property Law may directly or authorize copyright and related rights consulting and service organizations to submit applications for registration of copyright and related rights at the Copyright Office of Literature and Art or at the Department of Culture and Information of the place where the authors, copyright owners, or related rights owners reside or have their offices.
   Article 38. Copies of works registered for copyright and copies of performances, phonograms, videograms, or broadcasts registered for related rights
   1. The Copyright Office of Literature and Art shall keep a copy of the work registered for copyright and a copy of the performance, phonogram, videogram, or broadcast registered for related rights under Point b Clause 2 Article 50 of the Intellectual Property Law after issuing the Certificate of Copyright Registration or the Certificate of Related Rights Registration.
   2. Copies of works registered for copyright under Point b Clause 2 Article 50 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be replaced by three-dimensional photographs for works with unique characteristics such as paintings, sculptures, monuments, reliefs, and grand paintings attached to architectural works; or works that are too large or bulky.
   Article 39. Competence to issue Certificates of Copyright Registration and Certificates of Related Rights Registration
   1. The Copyright Office of Literature and Art shall have the competence to issue, re-issue, change, or cancel the validity of the Certificate of Copyright Registration or the Certificate of Related Rights Registration under Clauses 1 and 2 Article 51 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   a. The author, copyright owner or related rights owner who wishes to re-issue or replace the copyright registration certificate or the related rights registration certificate shall submit an application stating the reason and the application dossier in accordance with Article 50 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   b. The Copyright Office of Literature and Art shall re-issue the copyright registration certificate or the related rights registration certificate in case the copyright registration certificate or the related rights registration certificate is lost; replace the copyright registration certificate or the related rights registration certificate in case it is torn, damaged or the copyright owner or the related rights owner changes;
   c. The Copyright Office of Literature and Art shall cancel the copyright registration certificate or the related rights registration certificate in case the person who has been granted the copyright registration certificate or the related rights registration certificate is not the author or the copyright owner and in cases where the registered works, performances, phonograms, videograms or broadcasting programs are not subject to protection according to law.
   2. The Department of Culture and Information shall, after receiving the application for registration, re-issue or replacement of the copyright registration certificate or the related rights registration certificate in accordance with law, transfer it to the Copyright Office of Literature and Art for consideration and settlement within its competence.
   The Department of Culture and Information shall transfer it to the applicant immediately after receiving the consideration and settlement result from the Copyright Office of Literature and Art.

   3. Organizations and individuals shall pay fees and charges when carrying out procedures related to copyright and related rights for the Copyright Office of Literature and Art in accordance with law.
   4. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall promulgate the application forms for registration, the copyright registration certificate and the related rights registration certificate.
   Article 40: Effect of copyright registration certificates and related rights registration certificates The copyright registration certificates issued by the Vietnam Copyright Protection Agency, the Vietnam Copyright Protection Agency, the Copyright Office, the Copyright Office of Literature and Art before the effective date of the Civil Code and the Intellectual Property Law shall remain in effect.
   Chapter V
      COLLECTIVE REPRESENTATIVE ORGANIZATIONS, COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS CONSULTING AND SERVICE PROVIDING
   Article 41. Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights

   1. Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 56 of the Intellectual Property Law must comply with the following conditions when operating:
   a/ Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights must be authorized by authors or copyright owners or related rights owners;
   b/ Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights must be authorized to represent authors or copyright owners or related rights owners in managing a specific right or group of rights;
   c/ The collection and distribution of royalties, remuneration and other material benefits arising from the exploitation of rights or groups of rights prescribed in the charter of operation of collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights.
   2. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall provide guidelines on the division of royalties, remuneration and other material benefits in cases where authors or copyright owners or related rights owners have not authorized collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights.
   3. In case of works, phonograms, videograms, broadcasting programs related to rights and interests of many collective representative organizations authorized to represent different rights, groups of rights, the parties may agree that an organization will negotiate licensing, collection and distribution of fees on their behalf, and report to the Ministry of Culture.
   - Information before implementation.
   4. Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights shall report and provide information to the Copyright Office of Literature and Art every six months, annually or ad hoc on their activities.
   Article 42. Copyright and related rights consulting and services
   1. A copyright and related rights consultancy or service organization shall be established in accordance with Clause 1 Article 57 of the Intellectual Property Law when its head and individuals providing copyright and related rights consultancy or services fully satisfy the following conditions:
   a. To be a Vietnamese citizen with full civil act capacity;
   b. Permanent residence in Vietnam;
   c. Hold a bachelor’s degree in law.
   2. To report and provide information to the Copyright Office of Literature and Art every six months, annually or ad hoc on consulting activities and copyright and related rights services.
   Chapter VI
                                 PROTECTION OF COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 43: Right to self-protection

   1. Applying technological measures to prevent acts of infringement of copyright and related rights specified in Point a, Clause 1, Article 198 of the Intellectual Property Law, which means that right holders attach information on management of rights to original works or copies of works, phonograms, videograms, broadcasting programs; and include information on management of rights together with the communication of works to the public for identification of works, authors, rights holders, information on terms and conditions of use of works, and all data, codes, or symbols that represent such information for protection of copyright and related rights. At the same time, right holders may apply technological measures to protect information on management of rights and prevent acts of accessing works and illegally exploiting their ownership rights as prescribed by law.
   2. Right holders may apply other measures specified in Clause 1, Article 198 of the Intellectual Property Law to protect their rights and legitimate interests.
   Article 44: Right to initiate civil lawsuits for copyright and related rights
   1. The following right holders have the right to initiate lawsuits at competent courts for protection of their rights and legitimate interests related to copyright and related rights:
   a. Authors;
   b. Copyright owners and related rights holders;
   c. Legally entitled heirs of authors or copyright owners and related rights holders;
   d. Individuals and organizations to whom rights are transferred by copyright owners and related rights holders;
   dd. Individuals and organizations that use works under contracts;
   e. Performers;
   g. Phonogram and video recording producers;
   h. Broadcasting organizations;
   i. Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights that are entrusted with rights;
   k. Other right holders as prescribed by law.
   2. State agencies and relevant organizations within the scope of their tasks and powers may initiate civil lawsuits to request the court to protect public interests and the State's interests in the field of copyright and related rights.
   Article 45. Complaints and denunciations related to copyright and related rights registration
  1. The following persons have the right to file complaints and denunciations:
   a. Authors, copyright owners, related rights owners, authorized organizations and individuals may lodge complaints about the issuance, re-issuance, change or revocation of copyright registration certificates and related rights registration certificates.
   b. Any third party may denounce the issuance of copyright registration certificates and related rights registration certificates.
   2. The procedures for filing complaints and denunciations are as follows:
   a. A complainant about the registration of copyright or related rights must submit a written complaint to the Copyright Office of Literature and Art. The complaint must specify the date of filing the complaint; the complainant’s name and address; the name and address of the agency, organization or individual complained against; the content and reasons for the complaint and the complainant’s request. The complaint must be signed by the complainant. The complaint must be accompanied by documents such as the Certificate of Copyright Registration, the Certificate of Related Rights Registration, or the decision to revoke the validity, the written refusal to grant, re-grant or change the Certificate of Copyright Registration, the Certificate of Related Rights Registration and other relevant documents and evidence.
   b. A denouncer must submit a written complaint to a competent State agency. The complaint must specify the date of filing the complaint; the denouncer’s name and address; the name and address of the agency, organization or individual denounced against; the content and reasons for the complaint and the denouncer’s request. The complaint must be signed by the denouncer. The complaint must be accompanied by relevant documents and evidence.

   3. Within the time limit prescribed by the Law on Complaints and Denunciations, the Copyright Office of Literature and Art, the Ministry of Culture and Information or a competent State agency must respond in writing to the complainant or denouncer.
   4. In case of disagreement with the response of the Copyright Office of Literature and Art, the Ministry of Culture and Information or a competent State agency, the complainant or denouncer may lodge a complaint with a competent authority as prescribed by law.
   Chapter VII
                                                              IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
   Article 46. Transition period
  1. Copyright and related rights protected under the law in force before the Intellectual Property Law comes into force shall continue to be protected under the Intellectual Property Law if the protection period is still valid on the date of entry into force of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Applications for registration of copyright and related rights submitted to competent authorities before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law shall continue to be processed according to the provisions of legal documents in force at the time of submission.
   3. All acts of infringement of copyright or related rights or breach of contracts made before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law shall be handled according to the provisions of the current law in force at the time of the infringement.
   Article 47. Effect
   This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette. This Decree replaces the Government's Decree No. 76-CP of November 29, 1996, guiding the implementation of a number of provisions on copyright in the Civil Code.
   Article 48. Implementation responsibilities
   1. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of Government-attached agencies, presidents of provincial-level People's Committees, agencies, organizations and individuals with relevant rights and obligations shall be responsible for the implementation of this Decree.
   2. The Minister of Culture and Information shall be responsible for guiding the implementation of this Decree.
