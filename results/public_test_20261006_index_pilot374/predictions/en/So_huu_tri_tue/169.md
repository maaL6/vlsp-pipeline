                                                   CIRCULAR

   THE MINISTRY OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND ENVIRONMENT

No. 3055-TT/SHCN

of December 31, 1996

ON GUIDELINES FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THE REGULATIONS ON PROCEDURES FOR GRANTING INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS AND OTHER PROCEDURES IN THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 63/CP OF OCTOBER 24, 1996, ON INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY

The Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment, in accordance with the Government’s Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996, on industrial property, hereby provides guidelines for implementation of the regulations on procedures for granting industrial property rights and other procedures.

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996, detailing industrial property; the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment issues this Circular to specify and guide the procedures for making, filing and examining applications for protection titles, procedures for approving and registering contracts for transfer of industrial property rights, procedures for considering applications for voluntary licenses, procedures for modifying and extending the validity of protection titles, procedures for handling applications for international registration of inventions and utility solutions under the PCT Treaty and applications for international registration of trademarks under the Madrid Treaty and procedures for granting licenses for industrial property representation.

  Chapter I

                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. Terms

  1.1. The terms in this Circular are understood as follows:

  - "Decree" refers to the Government's Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996, detailing industrial property;

  - "Application" refers to an application for a protection title;

  - "Invention application", "Utility solution application", "Industrial design application", "Trademark application", "Appellation of origin application" refer to applications for the grant of invention patents, utility solution patents, industrial design patents, certificates of registration of trademarks and certificates of rights to use appellation of origin of goods;

  - "International application" means an application for international registration of an invention or utility solution filed under the PCT Treaty;

  - "International registration application" means an application for international registration of a trademark filed under the Madrid Agreement on International Registration of Trademarks;

  - "Trademark" means "goods mark" as defined in Article 2 of the Decree;

  - "Holder of the document" means an individual, legal entity or organization that has made, issued or signed the document acknowledging its validity.

  1.2. Other terms shall be construed in accordance with the Decree.

  2. Document authentication

  2.1. Signature authentication

  During the performance of procedures related to the establishment, maintenance, extension, enforcement, transfer, etc. of industrial property rights as prescribed in this Circular, the signature of the person whose name is on the papers or documents in transaction with competent authorities must be authenticated as being that of the person whose name is on the document. In case the signatory is a representative of the holder of the document, the signature must be authenticated as being that of the person who is competent to represent the holder of the document, as prescribed below:

  (i) For subjects with valid seals, the signature certification shall be made by affixing the seal of the subject to the signature;

  (ii) For Vietnamese subjects without valid seals, the signature certification must be made at the State notary office or the local government agency where the subject resides or has its head office;

  (iii) For foreign subjects without valid seals, the signature certification must be made at the notary office or the equivalent competent agency.

  2.2. Certification of copies

  a. Any document that is a copy in any form must be certified as a copy from the original according to the provisions in the following paragraph b) before it can be used as an official document in the process of carrying out procedures related to industrial property at competent agencies.

  b. A document is recognized as a copy from the original if the copy bears a copy certification from one of the following agencies: (i) the notary office, (ii) the People’s Committee or a competent agency, (iii) the State agency or social organization that made the original document; and, if the copy has multiple pages, each page must be certified or the pages must be stapled together.

  2.3. Certification of translations

  a. Any Vietnamese translation of documents must be certified as a literal translation from the original according to the provisions in the following paragraph b) before it can be used as an official document in the process of carrying out procedures related to industrial property before competent agencies.

  b. The translation may be certified in one of the following ways: (i) by notarization; (ii) by the original document’s owner; (iii) by all parties to the contract or agreement (if the original document is a contract or agreement); (iv) by the competent authority that uses the translation in the course of relevant procedures.

  3. The person on behalf of the subject matter shall carry out the procedures for industrial property.

  3.1. Only the persons specified in Points 3.2 and 3.3 below are entitled to act on behalf of the applicant in filing, supplementing, and correcting documents of the application; receiving and responding to the opinions of the Industrial Property Office regarding the application; deciding to continue or suspend the protection application process; receiving the protection title; maintaining, modifying, and extending the validity of the protection title, as well as other industrial property procedures before the Industrial Property Office and competent authorities.

  The Industrial Property Office may only transact with the above-mentioned persons and such transactions shall be regarded as official transactions with the entities.

3.2. For entities that have the right to personally submit applications and relevant procedures specified in Clauses 2 and 3.a) of Article 15 of the Decree, the following persons are permitted to act on behalf of entities to perform the tasks specified in Point 3.1 above:

  (i) The individual or his/her legal representative (for the individual as the subject);

  (ii) The legal representative of the entity; the individual who is a member of the entity and is authorized by the legal representative of the entity to act as its representative; the head of the representative office or branch of the entity, authorized by the legal representative of the entity to act as its representative (for entities that are legal persons or other entities);

  (iii) The head of the overseas representative office of a foreign entity, authorized by such entity to act as its representative; the representative of a Vietnamese enterprise wholly owned by a foreign entity, authorized by such entity to act as its representative;

  (iv) The person satisfying one of the conditions specified in paragraphs (i), (ii), (iii) above is one of the individuals or belongs to one of the legal entities or other entities - if the entity comprises multiple individuals, legal entities, other entities and if that person is authorized by all the individuals, legal entities, other entities to act as representative.

  3.3. For entities that are only permitted to submit applications and related procedures through industrial property representative service providers specified in Clause 3.b. Article 15 of the Decree, and for other entities that carry out the above-mentioned procedures through industrial property representative service providers, only persons who are granted industrial property representative cards by industrial property representative service providers and have authorization from the entities may carry out the tasks specified in Point 3.1 above.

  4. Delegation of procedures for industrial property

  4.1. Any authorization for the performance of industrial property procedures must be made in writing (authorization document), which must include the following contents:

  (i) Full name, full address, telephone number, fax number (if any) of the authorizing party; (ii) Full name, full address, telephone number, fax number (if any) of the authorized party; (iii) Scope of authorization (the tasks that the authorized party performs in the name of the authorizing party); (iv) Duration of authorization; (v) Place of preparation and date of preparation of the authorization document; (vi) Signature of the person preparing the authorization document (certified according to regulations on signature certification).

  4.2. The authorized party must be an individual or organization permitted to carry out the industrial property procedures specified in Points 3.2 (ii), (iii), (iv), and 3.3 of this Circular.

  4.3. Any change in the scope of authorization and termination of authorization before the expiration date must be notified in writing to the Industrial Property Office.

  4.4. If the authorization document covers a range of procedures that are independent of each other, the authorized party may submit a copy of the authorization document, provided that the original authorization document has been submitted to the Industrial Property Office and the number and date of submission of the application containing the original authorization document are indicated.

  Chapter 2:

                                                APPLICATIONS AND HANDLING OF APPLICATIONS

  5. General requirements for applications

  5.1. An application must comply with the provisions of Clause 2 Article 11 of the Decree and the general requirements for form specified in Point 5.2 below.

  5.2. An application must comply with the following general requirements for form:

  (i) Each application may only request the grant of one protection title, and the type of protection title requested must be appropriate to the industrial property subject matter stated in the application;

  (ii) All documents in the application must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents that may be presented in other languages as prescribed in Point 5.3 below;

  (iii) All documents in the application must be presented vertically on a white A4-sized paper (210mm x 297mm), with margins of 20mm on each side, except for documents added to the application for necessary reasons to supplement or illustrate, which are not intended to be included in the application and may therefore be presented differently;

  (iv) If a document must be prepared using a form, that form must be used by filling in the appropriate sections.

  (v) Each document must include the required number of copies; if a document comprises multiple pages, the page number must be written in Arabic numerals in the center of each page;

  (vi) Documents must be typed or printed with a fade-resistant ink, clearly, cleanly, without erasing or altering.

  5.3. The following documents may be made in a language other than Vietnamese but must be translated into Vietnamese:

  (i) Power of attorney (if any);

  (ii) Documents certifying the right to submit applications if the applicant is entitled to submit applications on behalf of another person (Inheritance Certificate, Certificate or Agreement on transfer of the right to submit applications, including the transferred application; Work contract or Labor contract...);

  (iii) Transfer of priority rights (if the application includes a request for priority rights and such rights are received from another person);

  (iv) Documents related to proving the grounds for entitlement to priority rights (first application, exhibition certificate...);

  (v) Original documents or copies of original documents that the applicant includes in the application to supplement the application.

  6. Requirements for the application for an invention/utility solution

  In addition to the general requirements specified in Point 5 of this Circular, the application for an invention/utility solution must satisfy the requirements specified in this Point.

  6.1. The application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application for grant of an invention/utility solution patent, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, in 3 copies;

  (ii) A description of the invention/utility solution (hereinafter referred to as the Description), in 3 copies;

  (iii) A request for protection, in 3 copies;

  (iv) Drawings, diagrams, calculations... (if any) to further clarify the nature of the technical solution stated in the Description, in 3 copies;

  (v) A summary of the invention/utility solution, in 3 copies;

  (vi) Documents certifying the right to file the application if the applicant is entitled to the right to file the application of another person (Inheritance Certificate, Certificate or Agreement on exclusive right to file the application; Employment contract or Labor contract...), including 1 copy;

  (vii) Power of attorney (if needed), including 1 copy;

  (viii) Copy of the first application or document certifying the exhibition if the application requests the right to priority according to international treaties, including 1 copy;

  (ix) Receipt of payment of application fee and publication fee, including 1 copy.

  6.2. The documents specified in point 6.1 above must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of filing the application:

  (i) Vietnamese version of documents 6.1 (ii), 6.1 (iii) and 6.1 (v), if the application already contains the English/French/Russian version of these documents;

  (ii) Original of document 6.1 (vii) if the application already contains a copy;

  (iii) Document 6.1 (viii), including the Vietnamese translation.

  6.3. The Description must fully disclose the nature of the technical solution to be protected. The Description must contain sufficient information so that any person with an average level of knowledge in the relevant technical field can implement the solution.

  The Description must clearly disclose the novelty, inventive step (if the subject matter to be protected is an invention) and applicability of the technical solution to be protected.

  The Description must include the following contents:

  (i) International Patent Classification Index (according to the Strasbourg Convention), (ii) Name of the technical solution, (iii) Field in which the technical solution is used or related, (iv) Technical condition in the said field at the time of filing (prior art technical solutions), (v) Nature of the technical solution, (vi) Brief description of the enclosed drawings (if any), (vii) Example of implementation of the technical solution, (viii) Benefits that can be achieved (effectiveness of the technical solution).

  6.4. Protection claims shall define the scope (volume) of protection for the invention/utilitarian solution. Protection claims must be presented concisely, clearly and in accordance with the description and drawings, in which the new features of the technical solution to be protected must be clearly disclosed.

  6.5. The invention/utilitarian solution summary for brief disclosure of the nature of the invention/utilitarian solution. The summary must disclose the main contents of the technical solution for information purposes.

  6.6. Requirements for form and content The Description, Drawings, Protection Claims, Invention/utilitarian solution summary and other documents of the Invention/utilitarian solution application shall be prescribed by the Industrial Property Office.

  7. Requirements for Industrial Design Applications

  In addition to the general requirements specified in Point 5 of this Circular, the industrial design application must satisfy the requirements specified in this Point.

  7.1. The application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application for an industrial design exclusive right, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) 3 copies of the industrial design description;

  (iii) A set of photographs or a set of drawings of industrial designs, comprising 6 sets; (iv) A document certifying the right to file a legal application if the applicant is entitled to the right to file an application of another person (Inheritance Certificate; Certificate or Agreement on Transfer of Right to File Application; Assignment Contract or Labor Contract), comprising 1 copy;

  (v) A document certifying the ownership of the mark if the industrial design contains the mark, including 1 copy;

  (vi) Power of attorney (if any);

  (vii) A copy of the first application or document certifying the exhibition if the application requests the priority rights under an international treaty, including 1 copy;

  (viii) Copies of the fee for filing the application and the fee for publication of the application, including 1 copy.

  7.2. The documents mentioned in Point 7.1 above must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of filing the application:

  (i) The Vietnamese version of Document 7.1(ii), if the application already contains an English/French/Russian version of such document;

  (ii) Document 7.1(v);

  (iii) The original of Document 7.1(vi), if the application already contains a copy;

  (iv) Document 7.1(vii), including the Vietnamese translation.

  7.3. The description of the industrial design must fully and clearly present the nature of the industrial design and must be consistent with the set of photographs or drawings and include the following contents:

  (i) The name of the industrial design; (ii) The international classification index of the industrial design (according to the Locarno Convention); (iii) The field of use of the product bearing the industrial design; (iv) The known similar industrial designs; (v) The list of photographs or drawings; (vi) The nature of the industrial design, in which the basic design features of the industrial design to be protected must be clearly stated, which are different from the known similar industrial designs.

  7.4. The photograph or drawing must fully show the nature of the industrial design as described in order to determine the scope (volume) of protection of such industrial design.

  The photograph or drawing must be clear and sharp, and must not confuse other products with the product bearing the industrial design in question.

  All photographs or drawings must be in the same scale. The size of each photograph must not be smaller than 90mm x 120mm and must not be larger than 210mm x 297mm.

  7.5. Requirements for the Description and the set of photographs or drawings of the industrial design are prescribed by the Industrial Property Office.

  8. Requirements for the trademark application

  In addition to the general requirements prescribed in point 5 of this Circular, the trademark application must satisfy the requirements specified in this point.

  8.1 The application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application for the grant of a Certificate of Trademark Registration, on which the trademark sample is affixed, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, in 3 copies;

  (ii) Regulations on use of the mark if the mark in question is a collective mark, including 1 copy;

  (iii) Mark samples, including 15 copies;

  (iv) Copies of documents certifying lawful business rights (business license or business registration certificate, etc.), including 1 copy;

  (v) Documents certifying lawful right to file an application, if the applicant is entitled to the right to file an application of another person (inheritance certificate, certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application, including the application already filed; contract for assignment of work or labor contract, etc.), including 1 copy;

  (vi) Power of attorney (if any);

  (vii) Copies of the first application or exhibition certificate if the application includes a request for priority rights under an international treaty, including 1 copy;

  (viii) Documents certifying origin, awards, medals, if the mark contains such information, including 1 copy;

  (ix) Licenses of competent authorities, if the mark contains symbols, proper names, etc. specified in Point g, Clause 2, Article 6 of the Decree, including 1 copy;

  (x) A copy of the fee payment receipt, including 1 copy.

  8.2. The above-mentioned documents must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of filing the application:

  (i) Original of document 8.1 (vi), if a copy is already included in the application;

  (ii) Document 8.1 (vii), including the Vietnamese translation.

  8.3. The description of the mark in the application must clarify the distinctive capacity of the mark, specifying each constituent element of the mark and the overall meaning of the mark. If the mark contains words not in Vietnamese, the pronunciation (transliteration into Vietnamese) must be clearly stated, and if the words have meaning, their meaning must be translated into Vietnamese.

  If the requested protected words or phrases are presented in the form of illustrations as distinctive elements of the mark, the form of these illustrations must be described.

  If the mark contains non-Arabic or non-Latin numerals, they must be translated into Arabic numerals.

  If the mark consists of several separate parts used simultaneously on a product, the position of each part of the mark on the product or packaging must be clearly stated.

  8.4. The list of products and services bearing the mark in the application must be consistent with or of the same type as the products and services permitted for trade as stated in the business license or business registration certificate and must be grouped according to the International Classification of Products and Services (under the Nice Agreement).

  8.5. The mark sample affixed to the application and other mark samples must be clearly presented with a size not exceeding 80mm x 80mm and a distance between the nearest two points not less than 15mm.

  If color protection is requested, the mark sample must be presented in the color to be protected.

  If color protection is not requested, all mark samples must be presented in black and white.

  9. Requirements for the Origin designation application.

  In addition to the general requirements specified in point 5 of this Circular, the Origin designation application must satisfy the requirements specified in this point.

  9.1. The application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application for issuance of the Certificate of Right to Use the Origin Designation of Goods, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, in 3 copies;

  (ii) A copy of the document certifying the lawful business rights (business license, business registration certificate, etc.), 1 copy;

  (iii) A description of the quality characteristics of the product bearing the name of the goods origin, including the certification of the competent State agency, 1 copy;

  (iv) The certification of the competent agency that the product manufactured or traded by the applicant has specific characteristics and quality and is produced in the territory corresponding to the name of the goods origin (conforming to the description in document (iii)), 1 copy;

  (v) A copy of the title of protection of the name of the goods origin granted by the country of origin, or the document of the country of origin certifying the rights of the applicant to use the protected name of the goods origin in the country of origin (if the name of the goods origin originates from a foreign country), 1 copy;

  (vi) A map describing the territory corresponding to the name of the goods origin, including the location of the applicant's production and business, 1 copy;

  (vii) A power of attorney (if necessary), 1 copy;

  (viii) The receipt of the application fee, 1 copy.

  If the applicant only requests the issuance of the Certificate of Rights to Use the Name of the Goods Origin for a name of the goods origin that has been previously registered, the documents (iii) and (vi) are not required in the application. If the name of the goods origin originates from a foreign country, the documents (ii), (iii) and (vi) are not required in the application.

  9.2. The documents must be submitted simultaneously. The original of document 9.1 (vii) may be submitted within 3 months from the date of submission of the application if a copy is already included in the application.

  9.3. The competent authority certifying the specific nature of the product bearing the origin name and certifying that the product produced by the applicant has such specific nature shall be the central or local authority managing the quality of goods where the origin name is located.

  10. Filing of application

  An application may be submitted to the Industrial Property Office or to any other location for receipt of applications set up by the Industrial Property Office. An application may also be sent in an insured manner by post to the above-mentioned locations for receipt of applications.

  11. Receipt of applications

  11.1. Upon receipt of an application, the Industrial Property Office shall perform the following tasks:

  (i) Re-check the list of documents stated in the Declaration;

  (ii) Seal the date of the application and the declaration;

  (iii) Record discrepancies between the list of documents in the application and the actual number of documents in the application;

  (iv) Preliminarily check the application to conclude whether the application is received or not according to point 11.2 below;

  (v) Send to the applicant an application sealed with the date of receipt, the application number and the result of the list of documents check, with the full name and signature of the officer receiving the application; (The above application replaces the receipt paper).

  11.2. The Industrial Property Office shall not receive an application if it finds that the application has one of the following deficiencies:

  (i) The application is missing one of the following mandatory documents: application, which must include the trademark form and the list of products and services (for trademark applications), the origin name and type of goods (for origin name applications), the description of the invention/utilitarian solution, the protection request (for invention/utilitarian solution applications), the description of the industrial design and the set of photographs and drawings of the industrial design (for industrial design applications), the authorization document (for applications requiring authorization documents), the receipt of fees;

  (ii) The form of protection (the type of protection title requested) is not suitable for the industrial property subject matter stated in the application;

  (iii) The application is signed or has been seriously erased or altered.

  11.3. In case the application is not received, the Industrial Property Office must notify the applicant of the reason for not receiving the application. For applications submitted by post, the Industrial Property Office must notify in writing within 15 days from the date of receipt of the application; The Industrial Property Office is not required to return the application documents to the applicant, but must refund the fees paid according to the application that is not received after deducting the costs for the return of such payment.

  12. Processing of applications received

  After receiving an application, it shall be processed as follows:

  A set of documents including necessary documents shall be separated to preserve the initial state of the application (hereinafter referred to as "application dossier");

  The remaining documents shall be combined into a set of documents for testing in accordance with this Circular.

  13. Form testing

  13.1. After being processed in accordance with Point 12 of this Circular, the application shall be tested for form in accordance with the provisions of this Point.

  13.2. An application shall be considered invalid if it has one of the following deficiencies:

  (i) The application is made in a language other than Vietnamese, except for the case specified in Point 5.3 of this Circular;

  (ii) The application contains insufficient information about the applicant (for inventions, utility solutions, industrial designs), the applicant does not sign the application, or the signature is not certified, or the information about the representative is erased.

  (iii) There are grounds to assert that the applicant does not have the right to file the application;

  (iv) The application is filed contrary to the provisions of Article 15 of the Decree;

  (v) The description, abstract, application for protection of the invention, utility solution, industrial design description is made in English/French/Russian, and the applicant fails to add the Vietnamese version within the time limit specified in Points 6.2 and 7.2 of this Circular;

  (vi) The authorization document is only a copy without the original within the time limit specified in Points 6.2, 7.2, 8.2 and 9.2 of this Circular;

  (vii) The application contains deficiencies specified in Point 13.3 below, which affect the validity of the application, and the applicant fails to correct them or the correction is not satisfactory despite the request of the Industrial Property Office;

  (viii) The subject matter specified in the application is not protected by the State according to the provisions of Clause 4 Article 4, Clause 3 Article 5, Clause 2 Article 6 and Clause 2 Article 7 of the Decree;

  13.3. Handling of deficiencies of the application during the formal examination stage

  If the application contains the following deficiencies, the Industrial Property Office shall notify the applicant and within 2 months from the date of notification, the applicant shall remedy such deficiencies:

  (i) The number of one of the mandatory documents is insufficient;

  (ii) The application does not satisfy the requirement of uniformity;

  (iii) The application does not satisfy the requirement of presentation form;

  (iv) The application for registration of a mark does not clearly state the type of mark to be registered, lacks the description of the mark, or the list of products is not properly grouped or incorrectly grouped;

  (v) Information on the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased;

  (vi) The application fee is not fully paid.

  13.4. The applicant may proactively amend the documents in the application but must not expand the scope (volume) of protection and must not alter the nature of the industrial property subject matter stated in the application and must pay the fee as prescribed. If the amendment expands the scope (volume) of protection or alters the nature of the subject matter, the applicant shall file a new application and all procedures shall be re-proceded from the beginning.

  13.5. Determination of the valid date of application

  The valid date of application shall be determined as follows:

  (i) For applications without the deficiencies specified in Point 13.2 above, the valid date of application is the date of receipt of the application at the Industrial Property Office stated in the receipt mark on the application form;

  (ii) For applications with deficiencies specified in Point 13.3 above, and deficiencies that have been rectified within the corresponding time limit, the valid date of application is the date on which the application arrives at the Industrial Property Office as recorded in the receipt mark. If the deficiencies are rectified later than that time limit, the valid date of application is the date on which the deficiencies are fully rectified so that the application becomes valid.

  13.6. Determination of priority date

  The priority date of the application shall be determined as follows:

  If the application does not request priority, the priority date is the date of submission of the valid application;

  If the application contains a request for priority rights, the priority date shall be the date stated in such request and approved by the Industrial Property Office.

  13.7. Results of form examination shall be notified by the Industrial Property Office to the applicant as follows:

  (i) If the application is considered valid, the Industrial Property Office shall send the applicant a notice of acceptance of the application, which shall clearly state the name and address of the applicant; the name of the industrial property representative service organization (if the application is filed through such organization); the name of the subject matter stated in the application, the date of filing of the valid application, the priority date; the application number; the remaining deficiencies which the applicant must continue to remedy and the time limit for such remedy. If the applicant fails to remedy the deficiencies within this time limit, the application will not be further considered;

  (ii) If the application is considered invalid, the Industrial Property Office shall send the applicant a notice of rejection of the application, which shall clearly state the name and address of the applicant, the name of the industrial property representative service organization (if the application is filed through such organization); the date of receipt of the application by the Industrial Property Office, the name of the subject matter stated in the application; the reason for rejection of the application (the reason for the application being considered invalid);

  (iii) If the application has the deficiencies stated in point 13.3 above, the Industrial Property Office shall send the applicant a notice of the results of the form examination, which shall clearly state the name and address of the applicant, the name of the industrial property representative service organization (if the application is filed through such organization), the date of receipt of the application by the Industrial Property Office; the name of the subject matter stated in the application, the deficiencies which must be remedied and set a time limit for the applicant to remedy the deficiencies.

  13.8. The time limit for form examination is 3 months from the date of receipt of the application by the Industrial Property Office indicated on the receipt stamp; for applications with late-filing documents as prescribed in points 6.2, 7.2, 8.2, 9.2, the time limit for form examination is 3 months from the date of full submission of such documents. Before the expiration of the above time limit, the Industrial Property Office must complete the form examination and notify the applicant as prescribed in point 13.7 above.

  14. Publication of valid applications

  14.1. All invention applications, utility solutions and industrial design applications which have been accepted and considered valid shall be published by the Industrial Property Office in the Industrial Property Gazette within the following time limits:

  a. Invention applications and utility solutions shall be published in the 19th month from the priority date, except for the cases prescribed in points b), c), d) below;

  b. In case of an early publication request, the application shall be published within 1 month from the date on which the Industrial Property Office receives the early publication request or later than the date specified in the request;

  c. In case of an invention or utility solution application, if there is a written request for examination of the contents submitted before the date on which such application is accepted, the application shall be published within 1 month from the date on which such application is accepted;

  d. In case of an invention or utility solution application, if there is a written request for examination of the contents submitted after the date on which such application is accepted but before the expiration of 18 months from the priority date, the application shall be published within 1 month from the date on which the Industrial Property Office receives the request for examination of the contents;

  e. International applications and industrial design applications shall be published in the second month from the date on which the application is accepted, except for the case specified in Point b above.

  14.2. Information related to a valid application published in the Official Gazette includes: all information about the valid application stated in the application acceptance notice, except for information on remaining deficiencies; a summary of the invention or utility solution enclosed with drawings if necessary; one or several drawings or photographs of the industrial design.

  14.3. Anyone may access more detailed information about the nature of the subject matter stated in the application published in the Industrial Property Office’s Official Gazette, or request the Industrial Property Office to provide such information, and the requester shall pay the information provision fee as prescribed.

  15. Request for examination of the contents of an invention or utility solution.

  15.1. Within 42 months from the priority date of the invention application, or 36 months from the priority date of the utility solution application, the applicant or any third party may request the Industrial Property Office to examine the contents of the corresponding invention or utility solution.

  An applicant for examination of the contents of an invention or utility solution must pay fees as prescribed.

  15.2. The applicant shall request examination of the invention or utility solution after the application is published in the Industrial Property Gazette within 1 month from the date of receipt of the written request for examination and shall be notified of this to the applicant.

  The request for examination of the contents of the invention or utility solution submitted before the publication of the application shall be published together with the corresponding application under Point 14.1c) and d) of this Circular.

  15.3. Except for cases where the contents of the request are written by the applicant himself/herself in the application, the request for examination of contents of an invention or utility solution must be made in writing, clearly stating the name and address of the applicant; the number of the application and the date of filing of the application for examination of contents of the invention or utility solution; the name and address of the applicant, the name of the invention or utility solution, and must be accompanied by a receipt or document certifying the payment of the examination fee.

  16. Examination of contents of application

  16.1. The examination of contents of the application shall be carried out by the Industrial Property Office in accordance with the provisions of this Point for:

  (i) All applications for industrial marks, designs and designations of origin if such applications have been validly accepted and the applicant has paid the examination fee as prescribed, and (ii) All applications for international registration, and (iii) Inventions, utility solutions, including international applications, which have been validly accepted and have been submitted to the Industrial Property Office within the time limit prescribed in Point 15 of this Circular.

  16.2. The purpose of examining the contents of the application is to assess the protection potential of the subject matter specified in the application according to the protection standards and determine the corresponding scope (volume) of protection.

  16.3. Within the time limit for examination of contents of applications, the Industrial Property Office shall send the notice of examination results to the applicant and the person requesting examination of contents as follows:

  a/ If the industrial property subject matter does not satisfy the protection criteria, the notice of examination results must clearly state the reason for the intention to refuse to grant the protection title and set a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to give his/her comments;

  b/ If the industrial property subject matter satisfies the protection criteria but the scope (volume) of protection must be narrowed or the application has deficiencies, the notice of examination results must clearly state this and set a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to give his/her comments or remedy the deficiencies;

  c/ If the subject matter satisfies the protection criteria, including the case stated in point b above, the notice of examination results must request the applicant to pay the fee for publication of the protection title, the registration fee and the fee for grant of the protection title and the fee for maintenance of validity of the first year (for inventions and utility solutions).

  16.4. Within the time limit for examination of contents, the applicant may actively amend and supplement the documents in the application and pay the fee as prescribed.

  The Industrial Property Office may request the applicant to amend and supplement the documents within a set time limit. If the applicant fails to amend and supplement the documents as requested by the Industrial Property Office without a valid reason, the Office shall consider withdrawing the application.

  The amendment and supplement shall not change the nature of the subject matter and shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection stated in the application.

  16.5. The time limit for examination of contents of applications is (i) 18 months for invention applications and 9 months for utility solution applications from the date of receipt of the request for examination of contents, if the request is filed after the date of publication of the application or from the date of publication of the application if the request for examination of contents is filed before the date of publication of the application; (ii) 9 months for industrial design applications and trademark applications from the date of signing the notice of acceptance of the valid application; (iii) 6 months for origin name applications from the date of signing the notice of acceptance of the valid application.

  If, during the examination of the contents of the application, the applicant or the Industrial Property Office, at his/her initiative or at the latter's request, makes corrections or additions to the application, the examination period may be extended by the time required for such corrections or additions.

  Before the expiration of the examination period, the Industrial Property Office shall notify the applicant and the applicant of the examination results in accordance with Point 16.3 above.

  Chapter 3

                              INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS TRANSFER

  17. Contract for transfer of industrial property rights

  17.1. A contract for transfer of industrial property rights means a contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property objects or a contract for transfer of the right to use industrial property objects.

  17.2. A contract for transfer of ownership of an invention, utility solution, industrial design or trademark must include the following main contents:

  - Full name and address of the transferring party and the receiving party;

  - The transfer base (the protection title granted to the transferring party or transferred to the transferring party);

  - The transfer object (the entire ownership right to the entire volume of protection of the industrial property object or a part of the volume of protection of the trademark - a part of the list of goods and services);

  - The transfer price;

  - The rights and obligations of each party, including corresponding obligations and not contrary to the provisions of Article 40 of the Decree;

  - Conditions for amendment, termination, and invalidation of the contract;

  - Method of handling complaints and disputes;

  - Date and place of signing;

  - Signature of the parties or authorized representatives of the parties, enclosed with full name, position of the signer, and signature certification.

  17.3. A license agreement shall include the following:

  - Full names and addresses of the licensor and licensee;

  - Basis for license transfer (the protection title granted to the licensor; or the exclusive license agreement);

  - License scope, including:

  - License form (exclusive/non-exclusive);

  - License subject matter, defined by the scope of use rights (belonging to the protected acts of use) and the scope of industrial property subject matter (belonging to the volume of protection of industrial property subject matter);

  - Territorial scope (belonging to the territory of Vietnam);

  - Duration (belonging to the duration of protection of industrial property subject matter).

  For a secondary license, the scope of the license must fall within the scope of the license of the corresponding exclusive license contract;

  - License price;

  - Rights and obligations of each party, including corresponding obligations and not contrary to the provisions of Article 40 of the Decree;

  - Conditions for amendment, termination, and invalidation of the contract;

  - Method of handling complaints and disputes;

  - Date and place of signing;

  - Signature of the parties or authorized representatives of the parties, enclosed with full name, position of the signatory, and signature certification.

  17.4. The license contract must not contain unreasonable clauses restricting the rights of the license recipient, especially clauses restricting the rights of the license grantor to the corresponding industrial property object or not aiming to protect such rights, such as:

  - Clauses directly or indirectly restricting the export of products manufactured under licenses to territories other than those where the license-giving party is the owner of the corresponding industrial property rights or has the exclusive right to import the corresponding industrial property objects;

  - Clauses compelling the license-accepting party to purchase all or a certain proportion of the raw materials, components or equipment of the license-giving party or of a person designated by the license-giving party without the purpose of ensuring the quality of goods manufactured by the license-accepting party;

  - Clauses prohibiting the license-accepting party from improving the industrial property object (except for the mark), compelling the license-accepting party to transfer free of charge to the license-giving party the improvements created by the license-accepting party or the right to file an application for industrial property protection, the industrial property right to such improvements;

  - Clauses prohibiting the license-accepting party from suing on the validity of the industrial property right, or on the license transfer right of the license-giving party.

  17.5. If the transfer of industrial property rights is part of another contract, the content on the transfer of industrial property rights must be made into a separate part from the rest of the contract and must comply with the provisions of this point.

  18. Contracts on transfer of industrial property rights must be approved and registered.

  18.1. According to Clause 5 Article 38 and Clause 5 Article 62 of the Decree, all contracts on transfer of ownership or transfer of use rights of industrial property objects in the following cases must be approved by the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment before proceeding with the registration procedures of the contract under Article 42 of the Decree and according to Point 20 of this Circular:

  (i) One of the participating parties (the license-giving party or the license-accepting party) is a state organization or a state-owned joint venture, while the other party is a non-state individual or organization;

  (ii) The transferring party is an individual, legal entity or other entity of Vietnam and the receiving party is an individual or foreign organization.

  18.2. All contracts on transfer of ownership or use rights of industrial property objects - including contracts that have gone through the approval procedures - must be registered according to Article 42 of the Decree and Point 20 of this Circular.

  19. Procedures for approval of contracts on transfer of ownership of industrial property

  19.1. The application for approval of a contract (hereinafter referred to as the application) shall include the following documents:

  (i) An application for approval of a contract on transfer of ownership of industrial property, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, in 3 copies, in which the applicant must be a State-owned organization or a joint venture organization with State capital contribution if the contract falls under Case 18.1 (i); or a Vietnamese party if the contract falls under Case 18.1 (ii);

  (ii) 2 original copies or 2 copies of the contract, including the appendix (if any); if the contract is made in a language other than Vietnamese, it must be accompanied by a Vietnamese translation;

  (iii) The original copy of the protection title (for the case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects), or a copy of the corresponding protection title (for the case of transfer of use rights of industrial property objects), if the contract to be approved is a secondary contract, it must be accompanied by a copy of the Certificate of registration of the exclusive contract on the corresponding secondary title;

  (iv) The written consent of the co-owners on the transfer of rights if the corresponding industrial property rights are co-owned; or if the above-mentioned agreement cannot be reached, there must be a written explanation of the reasons for the disagreement of the remaining co-owners;

  (v) Business license of the recipient in case of transfer of ownership or use rights of the trademark;

  (vi) Evidence of payment of approval fee for the contract;

  (vii) Power of attorney (if any).

  19.2. The application for approval shall be submitted to the Industrial Property Office in accordance with the provisions on submission of applications (point 10 of this Circular).

  The time limit for submission of the application for approval of the contract is 60 days from the date of signing the contract. The above time limit may be extended if the applicant proves that the delay is justified.

  19.3. The Industrial Property Office shall receive the application for approval in accordance with the provisions on receipt of applications at point 11 of this Circular, with appropriate amendments, where the documents specified in point 11.2.(i) are: Declaration; contract on transfer of industrial property rights; evidence of payment of fees and power of attorney.

  19.4. The Industrial Property Office shall consider the application for approval within 2 months from the date of receipt of the application, in accordance with the following provisions:

  a. In case the application for approval is valid and the contents of the contract comply with regulations, the Industrial Property Office shall report the results of consideration of the application for approval and request the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to issue a decision approving the contract within 15 days from the date of receipt of the report of the Industrial Property Office.

  b. In case the application contains deficiencies which can be remedied (other than the cases specified in Section c below), the Industrial Property Office shall notify the applicant for approval of the remedial measures within a reasonable time limit.

  The time limit for the applicant to approve the remedial measures shall not be counted in the time limit for consideration of the application.

  c. In case the application is invalid for the following reasons, the Industrial Property Office shall request the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to refuse to approve the contract on transfer of industrial property rights:

  (i) The applicant fails to remedy the deficiencies within the time limit notified by the Industrial Property Office;

  (ii) The applicant is not the person specified in Point 19.1(i) above;

  (iii) The transferring party is not the owner of the protection title (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects); or is not the owner of the protection title and is not the person who is granted the exclusive license and is permitted to transfer the secondary license for the corresponding industrial property object (in case of transfer of use rights of industrial property objects);

  (iv) The receiving party does not have a business license for goods/services corresponding to the corresponding trademark registration certificate (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property rights for trademarks);

  (v) The corresponding industrial property rights have expired; or the industrial property object is involved in a dispute;

  (vi) There are grounds to believe that the transfer will infringe upon the industrial property rights of a third party;

  (vii) The contract contains contents that are not in accordance with the regulations on conditions for restriction on transfer, or/and lacks mandatory contents, as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree and Points 17.2, 17.3, 17.4 of this Circular;

  (viii) The contract contains no price or transfer price clause or the price is outside the minimum and maximum levels as prescribed;

  (ix) The contract is not fully signed by the delivering party and the receiving party and/or the signature is not legally certified;

  (x) The Contract signatory is not competent to sign.

  19.5. Before requesting the rejection of approval of the Contract, the Industrial Property Office shall notify the results of consideration of the approval dossier, the proposed rejection, the reasons for rejection and set a suitable time limit for the applicant to give his/her opinion. If after the set time limit the applicant does not have any objection or the objection is not reasonable, the Industrial Property Office shall officially request the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to reject the approval of the Contract.

  20. Procedures for registration of industrial property transfer contracts 20.1. An application for registration of a contract (hereinafter referred to as the registration application) must include the following documents:

  (i) An application for registration of an industrial property transfer contract, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, comprising 02 copies;

  (ii) 2 original copies or 2 copies of the transfer contract, including the appendix (if any); if the contract is made in a language other than Vietnamese, it must be accompanied by a Vietnamese translation of the contract;

  (iii) The original copy of the protection title (for the case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects); or the corresponding copy of the protection title (for the case of transfer of use rights of industrial property objects), if the contract needs to be registered as a secondary license contract, it must be accompanied by the certificate of registration of the exclusive license contract on the corresponding secondary;

  (iv) The written consent of the co-owners on the transfer of rights if the corresponding industrial property rights are co-owned; or if the above-mentioned agreement is not reached, there must be a written explanation of the reasons for the disagreement of the remaining co-owners;

  (v) The business license of the recipient in case of transfer of ownership or transfer of use rights of a trademark;

  (vi) The decision approving the contract of the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment (for the case of contracts that must be approved);

  (vii) The receipt of the fee for contract registration;

  (viii) The authorization (if necessary);

  In case where both the approval procedures and the registration procedures are required, the documents already submitted in the approval dossier shall be considered as documents of the registration dossier.

  20.2. The application shall be submitted and received in accordance with the regulations applicable to the approval application (points 19.2 and 19.3 of this Circular).

  20.3. The Industrial Property Office shall consider the application within 2 months from the date of receipt of the application, except for the approved contract, which shall be considered within 15 days.

  a. In case the application is valid and the contract’s contents comply with regulations, the Industrial Property Office shall issue a decision to grant the certificate of registration of the contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property objects, and the protection title in case of transfer of the trademark for a part of the product and service list; or the certificate of registration of the license contract and carry out the following procedures:

  (i) Record in the National Register of Industrial Property and the Register of Contracts for Transfer of Ownership of Industrial Property Objects or the Register of License Contracts for each corresponding case;

  (ii) Record in the protection title (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects) and grant the protection title to the person to whom the ownership of the trademark is transferred for a part of the product and service list;

  (iii) Seal 2 copies of the contract and give 1 copy to the applicant, and keep 1 copy;

  (iv) Grant the applicant a copy of the certificate of registration of the contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property objects or the certificate of registration of the license contract;

  (v) Publish the decision to grant the certificate of registration in the Industrial Property Gazette.

  b. In case the application contains deficiencies that can be remedied (other than the cases specified in Section c below), the Industrial Property Office shall notify the applicant and request him/her to remedy the deficiencies within a reasonable time limit.

  The time limit for the applicant to remedy the deficiencies of the application shall not be counted in the time limit for consideration of the application as prescribed.

  c. In case the application is invalid for the following reasons, the Industrial Property Office shall refuse to register the contract:

  (i) The applicant fails to remedy the deficiencies within the time limit set by the Industrial Property Office or the remedy is not satisfactory;

  (ii) The applicant is neither the transferring party nor the receiving party of the transfer contract and is not the authorized industrial property representative as prescribed;

  (iii) The transferring party is not the owner of the protection title (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects); or is not the owner of the protection title and must also be the person who is granted the exclusive license and is permitted to transfer the secondary license for the corresponding industrial property object (in case of license transfer);

  (iv) The receiving party does not have a business license for goods/services corresponding to the corresponding Certificate of Trademark Registration (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property rights to a trademark);

  (v) The industrial property right is no longer valid within the protection period; or is in dispute;

  (vi) There are grounds to assert that the transfer of industrial property rights will infringe upon the industrial property rights of a third party;

  (vii) The contract contains contents that are inconsistent with the provisions on conditions for restriction on transfer as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree, or/and lacks mandatory contents as prescribed in Points 17.2, 17.3, and 17.4 of this Circular;

  (viii) The contract lacks full signatures of the transferring party and the receiving party and/or the signatures are not legally certified;

  (ix) The person signing the contract lacks signing competence;

  (x) There is no Decision approving the contract (for contracts that are mandatory to approve).

  d) Before officially refusing to register the contract, the Industrial Property Office shall notify the results of consideration of the registration dossier, the intention to refuse, the reasons for refusal, and set a suitable time limit for the applicant to provide comments. After the set time limit, if the applicant does not provide any comments or provide unreasonable comments, the Industrial Property Office shall issue a notice of refusal to register the contract on transfer of industrial property rights, clearly stating the reasons.

  21. Consideration of applications for non-voluntary license issuance

  21.1. An application for non-voluntary license issuance shall include the following documents:

  (i) An application for a non-voluntary license, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office;

  (ii) Documents proving the special significance of the invention, utility solution, industrial design for national security, defense, public health protection, environmental protection and documents proving that the invention, utility solution, industrial design is not used by the industrial property owner (or the person who is fully transferred the right to use such objects) without a justifiable reason or used to an extent that fails to meet the requirements for national security, defense, public health protection, environmental protection;

  (iii) Documents proving the applicant’s capacity to use the invention, utility solution, industrial design and the conditions that the applicant considers reasonable but are not accepted by the industrial property owner (or the person who is fully transferred the right to use the industrial property object) without a justifiable reason;

  (iv) Receipts of payment of the fee for the application for a non-voluntary license.

  (v) Power of attorney (if any).

  21.2. The application for a non-voluntary license shall be submitted to the Industrial Property Office.

  21.3. After receiving the application for a non-voluntary license, the Industrial Property Office shall consider the application according to the provisions of Clause 5 Article 51 of the Decree. The procedures for consideration of the application for a non-voluntary license are similar to those for approval of the contract on transfer of industrial property rights (point 19 of this Circular).

  Chapter 4:

      PROCESSING OF INTERNATIONAL APPLICATIONS FOR INVENTIONS/UTILITARIAN SOLUTIONS AND TRADEMARKS

  22. Procedures before the Industrial Property Office

  The provisions on the submission of applications and the performance of other procedures before the Industrial Property Office specified in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 15 of the Decree and Point 3 of this Circular shall also apply to the performance of procedures before the Industrial Property Office for international applications for inventions, utilitarian solutions and trademarks specified in this Chapter.

  23. Processing of international applications for inventions/utilitarian solutions under the PCT Treaty

  23.1 Receiving authority

  The authority competent to receive international applications in Vietnam is the Industrial Property Office.

  The Industrial Property Office shall:

  (i) Receive international applications originating from Vietnam;

  (ii) Collect fees and transfer corresponding fees to the International Office and the International Search Agency in accordance with the Treaty;

  (iii) Check whether the fees specified are paid on time;

  (iv) Check and process the Vietnamese-origin International Application in accordance with the Treaty;

  (v) Identify the applicant: if the applicant of the Application is subject to national secrecy, the following procedures shall not be carried out and the fees shall be refunded to the applicant except for the deposit fee and the fee for the International Application copy;

  (vi) Send a copy of the Vietnamese-origin International Application to the International Office and a copy to the International Search Agency;

  (vii) Send and receive letters from the applicant and the international agencies.

  23.2. Language

  The Vietnamese-origin International Application submitted to the Industrial Property Department must be made in English or Russian. Each application shall be made in 3 copies.

  In case of insufficient copies, the Industrial Property Office will make additional copies to meet the required number and the applicant must pay the fee for making copies of the international application.

  23.3. International search office and preliminary examination office.

  For international units of Vietnamese origin, the competent international search offices and preliminary examination offices are the patent offices of Austria, the Federal Republic of Russia, Sweden and the European Patent Office.

  23.4. International application with Vietnam designation

  If the international application includes Vietnam designation, the Industrial Property Office will be the designated office. In this case, to enter the national phase, within 21 months from the date of priority, the applicant must submit to the Industrial Property Office:

  (i) An application for grant of the patent/ utility solution, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, in 3 copies;

  (ii) A copy of the international application, in 3 copies (in case the applicant requests to enter the national phase before the date of international publication);

  (iii) A Vietnamese translation of the international application (including: Description, Protection request (original, amendment and explanation according to Article 19 of the PCT Treaty), Summary, Illustration notes), in 3 copies;

  (iv) National fees.

  23.5. International application selecting Vietnam

  If the International Preliminary Examination Request selects Vietnam, the Industrial Property Office shall be the selected agency. In this case, and if the selection of Vietnam is made within 19 months from the priority date, to enter the national stage, within 31 months from the priority date, the applicant shall pay to the Industrial Property Office:

  (i) An application for grant of the patent/ utility solution title, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, in 3 copies;

  (ii) The Vietnamese translation of the International Application (including: the Description, the Protection Request (the original, the amendment and the explanation according to Article 19 of the PCT Treaty), the Abstract, the Illustration Notes), in 3 copies;

  (iii) The Vietnamese translation of the Appendices of the International Preliminary Examination Report, in 3 copies;

  (iv) National fees.

  23.6. Documents for claiming priority

  To be entitled to priority, an international applicant shall submit to the International Office the necessary documents under Rule 17.1(a) of the Treaty Implementation Regulations; and submit to the Industrial Property Office 3 copies of Vietnamese translations of such documents within the time limits specified in Points 23.4 and 23.5 above.

  23.7. To amend and supplement documents in the national phase

  In accordance with Rule 51 bis of the PCT Treaty Implementation Regulations, the applicant shall submit the Power of Attorney and the Transfer of Application Rights in the International Phase (if any) within 24 months from the date of priority for the international application designated for Vietnam and 34 months for the international application selecting Vietnam.

  In accordance with Article 28 and Article 41 of the PCT Treaty, during the national phase, the applicant may amend or supplement the documents of the application as prescribed in Point 16.4 of this Circular.

  Supplementary and amended documents submitted by applicants to the Industrial Property Office must be made in Vietnamese and in three copies.

  23.8. Time limit for the start of the national phase.

  The time limit for commencement of processing of an international application designated for Vietnam or selected for Vietnam in the national stage shall be counted from the first day of the 22nd month from the priority date, if Vietnam is the designated country, or from the 32nd month from the priority date, if Vietnam is the selected country and the selection has been made before the expiration of 19 months from the priority date, if the applicant does not request entry into the national stage earlier than the above-mentioned time limits.

  23.9. International Request for Examination

  An international application shall be examined for form and content according to the procedures prescribed for a national application.

  23.10. An international application is considered withdrawn

  Apart from cases of withdrawal as prescribed in the PCT Treaty and the Treaty’s Implementation Regulations, if the national fee is not paid to the Industrial Property Office or if the Vietnamese translation is not available after the time limit specified in Points 23.4 and 23.5 above, the international application designated by Vietnam shall be considered withdrawn.

  23.11. Fees

  Vietnamese applicants shall pay fees according to the rates and procedures prescribed in the Regulation on Implementation of the PCT Treaty and the regulations of the inter-ministerial committee of Finance, Science, Technology and Environment.

  24. Filing and submission of applications for international registration of Vietnamese-origin trademarks abroad under the Madrid Agreement

  24.1. Any individual, legal entity or other entity may file an application for international registration of a mark under the Madrid Agreement, provided that such mark has been registered in Vietnam.

  24.2. Registration application

  24.3. The application for international registration of a mark must be made in French according to the form provided free of charge by the Industrial Property Office, by filling in the sections reserved for the applicant (except the sections reserved for the Industrial Property Office and the International Office) and must be enclosed with the mark forms. The application must specify the countries of the Madrid Convention which the applicant wishes the mark to be protected. The applicant must preliminarily calculate the total amount of fees payable to the International Office according to the fee schedule printed on the form. If the applicant is certain that the calculated amount of fees is correct or after being notified by the Industrial Property Office of the exact amount of fees payable, the applicant must pay that amount to the International Office. In addition, the applicant must also pay an additional fee according to regulations to the Industrial Property Office.

  24.3. The receiving agency

  An application for international registration of a mark shall be submitted to the International Office through the Industrial Property Office.

  The date on which the Industrial Property Office receives the application shall be considered the date of receipt of the application at the International Office if the International Office receives the application within 2 months from that date.

  24.4. Sending the application to the International Office

  After the application is submitted to the International Office, all transactions between the applicant and the International Office must go through the Industrial Property Office, including the modification of documents, restriction of the product list, and transfer of registered rights.

  25. Processing of applications for international registration of a mark designated for Vietnam

  25.1. After receiving the notification of the International Office of an application for international registration of a mark designated for Vietnam, the Industrial Property Office shall examine the contents of the application as for an application for mark registration submitted directly to the Industrial Property Office. Within 12 months from the date on which the mark is registered internationally, the Industrial Property Office must issue a conclusion on the protectability of the mark. If the mark is not protectable or is partially rejected, the Industrial Property Office must notify the applicant in writing through the International Office within the above-mentioned time limit, clearly stating the reasons for the rejection.

  If within the above-mentioned time limit, the Industrial Property Office does not issue a refusal notice, the mark shall be accepted for protection in Vietnam.

  25.2. Within 3 months from the date of the industrial property office's notice of refusal, the applicant may lodge a complaint against the industrial property office's decision. The complaint and settlement procedures shall be the same as those for the application for registration of a mark submitted directly to the industrial property office. The industrial property office shall notify the applicant and the international office of the result of the complaint settlement.

  25.3. The mark accepted for protection in Vietnam under the Madrid Agreement shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette. The scope (volume) of protection shall be certified according to the contents of the mark registration recorded by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) and certified by the Industrial Property Office.

  Chapter 5:

   AMENDMENTS TO PROTECTING TEXTS; EFFECT OF PROTECTING TEXTS ON INVENTIONS AND SOLUTIONS; EXTENSION OF EFFECT OF PROTECTING TEXTS ON INDUSTRIAL DESIGN, TRADEMARKS AND NAMES OF GOODS.

  26. Amendment to the protection title

  26.1. The holder of a protection title shall have to notify the Industrial Property Office in writing of any change to the name or address of the holder of the protection title.

  26.2. The holder of the protection title may request the Industrial Property Office to narrow the scope (volume) of protection of industrial design and trademark band, removing one or some solutions of industrial design, modifying some details of trademark but not fundamentally changing the trademark; reducing some products in the list of products and services specified in the trademark protection title.

  26.3. To amend the above-mentioned contents, the holder of the protection title must submit an application for amendment of the protection title to the Industrial Property Office. The application for amendment shall be made according to the form prescribed by the Industrial Property Office and must be enclosed with: (i) the original copy of the protection title; (ii) documents certifying the amendment of the name and address of the holder of the protection title; (iii) two sets of photographs or drawings of the industrial design pattern to be removed; (iv) 10 samples of the amended trademark; (v) receipts of fees for amendment of the protection title; (vi) authorization papers if necessary.

  26.4. The Industrial Property Office shall consider the application for amendment of the protection title within 2 months from the date of receipt of the application. If the application is deemed valid, the amendment does not increase the scope (volume) of protection or change the nature of the protected subject matter, the Industrial Property Office shall amend the protection title, register and announce the change in the Industrial Property Gazette. In the opposite case, the Industrial Property Office shall notify the applicant of the refusal of amendment, clearly stating the reasons.

  27. Maintenance of validity

  To maintain the validity of the protection title of an invention or utility solution, the holder of the protection title shall pay maintenance fees within 6 months before the expiration date of the protection title. The maintenance fee may be paid later than the above-mentioned time limit, but not later than 6 months from the date of expiration of the protection title, and the holder of the protection title shall pay an additional 10% of the fee for each month of late payment.

  28. Extension of validity

  28.1. To extend the validity, within 6 months before the expiration date of the protection title, the holder of the protection title shall submit an application for extension to the Industrial Property Office.

  The application for extension may be submitted later than the above-mentioned time limit, but not later than 6 months from the date of expiration of the protection title, and the applicant for extension shall pay an extension fee plus 10% of the extension fee for each month of late payment.

  28.2. The application for extension of validity of the protection title shall include the following documents:

  (i) An application for extension of validity of the protected title, made according to the form issued by the Industrial Property Office, comprising 2 copies;

  (ii) The original protected title;

  (iii) Evidence of payment of the extension fee;

  (iv) A power of attorney (if any);

  28.3. The Industrial Property Office shall consider the application for extension within 2 months from the date of receipt of the application. The Industrial Property Office shall issue an extension decision, record it in the protected title, the register and publish it in the Industrial Property Gazette if the application does not fall into any of the following cases:

  - The application for extension is invalid or submitted in violation of the prescribed procedures;

  - There are grounds to believe that the owner of the certificate of registration of the mark, the owner of the certificate of the right to use the origin name of goods, does not use the mark or the origin name of goods or does not use such objects continuously for the last 5 years before the expiration date of the protected title without any reasonable reason;

  - The applicant for extension is not the owner of the mark, the origin name of goods or the corresponding industrial design protected title.

  If the application falls into one of the above cases, the Industrial Property Department shall issue a notice of refusal to grant the extension, clearly stating the reasons.

  Chapter VI

                                       PROCEDURES FOR GRANTING REPRESENTATION LICENSES

  29. Application for grant of representation license.

  29.1. The application for grant of representation license shall be considered by the Industrial Property Department at the points 29.2 and 29.3 below. The applicant for grant of representation license shall pay the fee as prescribed.

  29.2. The application for grant of certificate of industrial property representation service organization shall include:

  - An application for grant of certificate of industrial property representation service organization, which includes a list of industrial property representatives of the organization.

  - A copy of the operating charter and business registration certificate;

  - A copy of the decision on appointment to a leadership position of an organization or a written authorization to represent the organization signed by the head of the organization of one of the members of the list of applicants above;

  - The fee schedule for industrial property representation services of the organization after completing the registration procedures as prescribed in the regulations on the management of fees and charges;

  - The receipt of the fee paid for the application for the representation license.

  The application for the issuance of the certificate of industrial property representation service organization must be submitted together with the application for the issuance of the industrial property representative card of the individuals on the list of applicants above.

  29.3. The application for the issuance of the industrial property representative card includes:

  - An application for the issuance of the industrial property representative card, which includes the certificate of the People's Committee of the ward, commune or town about the permanent address of the applicant;

  - A copy of the university degree certificate;

  - A copy of the certificate of completion of a regular industrial property training course; or a certificate of the duration and job performance as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 58 of the Decree;

  - Copies of certificates of passing the current examination on Vietnamese industrial property law issued by the Industrial Property Office;

  - Copies of recruitment decisions of industrial property representative service organizations;

  - Evidence of payment of application fees.

  30. Consideration of application for issuance of representative licenses

  Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application for issuance of representative licenses, the Industrial Property Office shall consider and decide to issue or refuse to issue the representative licenses. If refusing, the Industrial Property Office shall clearly notify the applicant of the reason. If accepting, and if the applicant has paid the representative license fee, the Industrial Property Office shall carry out the following procedures:

  - Issue the representative license;

  - Record the issuance of the representative license in the industrial property representative register;

  - Announce the issuance of the representative license in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.

  Chapter VII

                                         FINAL PROVISIONS

  31. Complaints

  If the applicant disagrees with the Decision on handling of the application, the approval and registration of the contract for transfer of industrial property rights, or the consideration of the application for voluntary license, extension of protection title, or issuance of the representative license, he/she may lodge a complaint or bring a lawsuit according to the procedures prescribed in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 Article 27 of the Decree.

  32. Handling of applications submitted from July 1, 1996 to the effective date of this Circular.

  Applications submitted from July 1, 1996 to the effective date of this Circular may use the forms prescribed in Circular No. 1134/SC of October 17, 1991 of the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment for the implementation of Decree No. 84/HDBT of March 20, 1990 of the Council of Ministers, and shall be handled according to this Circular. The time limit for handling of such applications shall be extended by the time from the date of submission of the application to the effective date of this Circular.

  33. Forms and Regulations on examination of applications

  The Director of the Department of Industrial Property shall issue the necessary forms related to applications and the approval and registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights, and issue the Regulations on examination of applications.

  34. Implementation

  This Circular replaces the following documents of the State Science and Technology Committee, the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment:

  - Chapters II, III, IV of Circular No. 1134/SC of October 17, 1991, guiding the implementation of Decree No. 84/HDBT of March 20, 1990 of the Council of Ministers,

  - Circular No. 437/SC of March 19, 1993, guiding the registration of goods marks,

  - Circular No. 163/TT-SHCN of April 15, 1994, guiding the implementation of regulations on the approval and registration of license contracts,

  - Circular No. 238/TT-SHCN of May 2, 1994, guiding the submission and handling of international applications for patent protection of inventions/ utility solutions under the patent cooperation agreement in Vietnam.

  - Decision No. 199/QD of December 21, 1992, promulgating regulations on industrial property representatives.

  This Circular takes effect 15 days after its signing.
