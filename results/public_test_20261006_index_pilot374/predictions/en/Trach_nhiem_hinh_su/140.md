                                                 DECISION

    FOR THE MINISTER OF PUBLIC SECURITY

No. 315/2003/QD-BCA(V19)

May 21, 2003

ON THE PUBLICATION OF THE LIST OF LEGAL DOCUMENTS THAT HAVE BEEN ABANDONED

                                         THE MINISTER OF PUBLIC SECURITY

  Pursuant to the Law on Promulgation of Legislative Documents dated November 12, 1996 and the Law on Amendments to the Law on Promulgation of Legislative Documents dated December 16, 2002;
  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 101/CP of October 23, 1997, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on promulgation of legal documents, and the Prime Minister's Decision No. 355/TTg of May 28, 1997, on the establishment of the Government's Steering Committee for the general review and systematization of legal documents, and the Decree No. 37/1998/ND-CP of June 9, 1998, on the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;
  At the request of the Director of the Department of Legal Affairs,

                                                 DECISION:

  Article 1. The list of legal documents promulgated by the Ministry of Public Security in the field of security and order, which are annulled by December 31, 2002, is hereby announced. This list comprises 15 documents (attached).

  Article 2. This Decision takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

  Article 3.- The General Directors, Heads of units attached to the Minister, Directors of the Police Departments of provinces and centrally-run cities shall be responsible for the implementation of this Decision.
