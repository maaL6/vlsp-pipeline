                                                       THE LAW

                                           DRUG PREVENTION AND CONTROL

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam;
  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on Prevention and Combat of Drug Abuse.

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Law provides for drug prevention and combat; management of persons illegally using narcotic substances; drug rehabilitation; responsibilities of individuals, families, agencies and organizations in drug prevention and combat; state management and international cooperation in drug prevention and combat.

  Article 2. Interpretation of terms

  In this Law, the following terms are construed as follows:

  1. Narcotic substances are addictive and psychotropic substances specified in the list of narcotic substances promulgated by the Government.

  2. Addictive substances are stimulant or depressant substances that easily cause addiction in users.

  3. Psychotropic substances are stimulant or depressant substances or hallucinogenic substances that, if used repeatedly, may lead to addiction in users.

  4. Precursors are chemicals indispensable in the process of preparation and production of narcotic substances specified in the list of precursors promulgated by the Government.

  5. Veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances or precursors to veterinary drugs containing substances specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  6. Plants containing narcotic substances are opium, coca, cannabis plants and other plants containing narcotic substances as prescribed by the Government.

  7. Drug prevention and combat means the prevention, suppression and combat against drug-related crimes and evils; control of lawful activities related to drugs.

  8. Drug-related evils means the illegal use of narcotic substances, drug addiction and drug-related violations that have not reached the level of or are not subject to criminal prosecution.

  9. Control of lawful activities related to drugs means the permission, monitoring, inspection and supervision of lawful activities related to drugs and the prevention and suppression of the misuse of such activities for other purposes.

  10. Illegal drug users are persons who use narcotic substances without permission of competent persons or agencies and test positive for narcotic substances in their bodies.

  11. Narcotic substance testing in the body means the application of professional techniques to determine narcotic substances in the body through urine, blood or other biological samples.

  12. Drug addicts are persons who use narcotic substances, addictive drugs, psychotropic drugs and are dependent on these substances.

  13. Drug rehabilitation means the process of providing medical, psychological and social support to drug addicts to help them stop using drugs, addictive substances, psychotropic substances, recover physically and mentally, raise their awareness and change their behavior to stop the illegal use of these substances.

  14. Drug rehabilitation centers are establishments established to fully implement the rehabilitation process as prescribed by this Law, including public drug rehabilitation centers and voluntary drug rehabilitation centers.

  Article 3. State policies on drug prevention and combat

  1. Implement measures for drug prevention and combat in a synchronized manner; combine with HIV/AIDS prevention and combat and other social evils.

  2. Strengthen the propaganda and education activities on drug prevention and combat; encourage organizations and individuals to participate in the propaganda and education on drug prevention and combat.

  3. Prioritize resources for drug prevention and combat in ethnic minority and mountainous regions, remote areas, islands, border areas and areas with complex drug issues.

  4. Officials and soldiers of the specialized agencies for drug crime prevention and combat, and drug rehabilitation workers in public drug rehabilitation centers shall enjoy preferential regimes and policies suitable to the nature of their tasks and areas of operation as prescribed by the Government.

  5. Protect and support individuals, families, agencies and organizations participating in drug prevention and combat.

  6. Strictly manage persons illegally using narcotic substances and drug addicts; encourage drug addicts to voluntarily quit drugs and receive treatment for addiction to opium-based drugs using substitutes; encourage individuals, families, agencies and organizations to participate in and support drug rehabilitation and post-rehabilitation management.

  7. Ensure funding for compulsory drug rehabilitation; support funding for voluntary drug rehabilitation and post-rehabilitation management.

  8. Domestic and foreign organizations and individuals investing in drug rehabilitation, post-rehabilitation management and drug rehabilitation prevention and control shall be exempted or granted reductions in land rent and corporate income tax in accordance with law.

  9. Encourage scientific research, technological development and application of high technology in drug prevention and control.

  10. Commend individuals, organizations and agencies that achieve outstanding results in drug prevention and control.

  Article 4. Financial sources for drug prevention and control

  1. State budget.

  2. Funding, aid, investment and donations from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals.

  3. Payment by families and drug addicts.

  4. Other lawful sources of finance.

  Article 5. Prohibited acts

  1. Planting drugs, guidance on planting drugs.

  2. Research, assessment, testing, examination, production, storage, transportation, preservation, storage, trading, distribution, processing, exchange, export, import, temporary import, re-export, temporary export, re-import, transit of drugs, precursors, addictive drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursor drugs, medicinal raw materials which are addictive active ingredients, psychotropic active ingredients, medicinal precursors, veterinary drugs containing drugs, precursors.

  3. Appropriation of drugs, precursors, addictive drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursor drugs, medicinal raw materials which are addictive active ingredients, psychotropic active ingredients, medicinal precursors, veterinary drugs containing drugs, precursors.

  4. Receipt, management, control, custody, allocation, preservation of drugs, precursors in contravention of law; allowing drug users, addictive drugs or psychotropic drugs in contravention of law.

  5. Use, illegal use of drugs; coercion, inducement of others to use drugs; tolerance, support for illegal use of drugs.

  6. Manufacturing, storing, transporting, buying and selling means and instruments used for the illegal production or use of narcotic substances.

  7. Opposing or obstructing the narcotic substance testing in the body, determination of the narcotic addiction status, management of the illegal users of narcotic substances, narcotic rehabilitation, post-narcotic rehabilitation management.

  8. Retaliation or obstruction of the persons performing the narcotic prevention and combat tasks, the persons participating in the narcotic prevention and combat.

  9. Exploiting the position, power and profession to violate the law on narcotic prevention and combat.

  10. Providing guidance on the illegal production or use of narcotic substances; advertising and marketing of narcotic substances.

  11. Stigmatizing the illegal users of narcotic substances, the narcotic rehabilitation patients, the post-narcotic rehabilitation patients.

  12. Other acts prohibited by the relevant laws on narcotic substances.

  Chapter II

                                  RESPONSIBILITY FOR PREVENTION AND CONTROL OF DRUGS

  Article 6. Responsibilities of individuals and families

  1. To propagate and educate family members and relatives about the harm of drugs and comply with the law on drug prevention and control; manage and prevent family members from violating the law on drug prevention and control.

  2. To strictly follow the instructions of competent persons on the use of narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursors, veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances and precursors.

  3. Cooperate with competent authorities in the fight against crime and drug-related evils; participate in supporting voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community, drug rehabilitation at drug rehabilitation centers and treatment of addiction to opium-based drugs with replacement drugs; monitor and assist drug rehabilitation individuals in reintegration into the community; prevent and combat drug relapse.

  4. To provide timely information on crimes, drug-related evils and cultivation of plants containing narcotic substances to police authorities or competent state agencies; to participate in the removal of plants containing narcotic substances organized by local governments.

  Article 7. Responsibilities of state agencies

  1. To organize the prevention and combat of drugs in agencies and units; to prevent and stop cadres, civil servants, public employees, and workers under their management and cadres and soldiers of the People's Armed Forces from violating laws on drug prevention and combat; to propagate and mobilize the people to detect, denounce, and fight against drug-related crimes and evils.

  2. To implement the State’s policies and guidelines on socio-economic planning and development to replace the cultivation of narcotic-containing plants in the areas where narcotic-containing plants are eradicated.

  Article 8. Responsibilities of educational institutions

  1. To organize the implementation of the education program on drug prevention and combat; to disseminate and educate students, pupils and trainees on the law on drug prevention and combat; to strictly manage and prevent students, pupils and trainees from violating the law on drug prevention and combat.

  2. To cooperate with families, agencies, organizations and local authorities in managing and educating students, pupils and trainees on drug prevention and combat.

  3. To cooperate with competent agencies, organizations and individuals in organizing drug tests in the body when necessary to detect students, pupils and trainees from using narcotic substances illegally.

  Article 9. Responsibilities of press agencies

  Press agencies shall cooperate with relevant agencies and organizations in disseminating the State’s policies, guidelines and laws on drug prevention and combat.

  Article 10. Responsibilities of the Vietnam Fatherland Front, its member organizations and other organizations

  1. To organize and cooperate with competent authorities in propagating and disseminating knowledge and laws on drug prevention and combat among the people; mobilizing the people to participate in drug prevention and combat and implement drug prevention and combat movements.

  2. To prevent and stop members of its organization and all citizens from violating laws on drug prevention and combat.

  3. To cooperate with local governments at all levels and competent authorities in mobilizing drug addicts to undergo drug rehabilitation, treatment for addiction to opium-based drugs with replacement drugs; participate in moral persuasion, education, vocational training, job search and assistance for drug addicts after rehabilitation to reintegrate into the community; prevent and combat drug relapse.

  Article 11. Specialized agencies for drug crime prevention and combat

  1. Specialized agencies for drug crime prevention and combat include:

  a) Specialized agencies for drug crime prevention and combat under the People’s Public Security;

  b) Specialized agencies for drug crime prevention and combat under the Vietnam Border Guard, Coast Guard and Customs.

  2. Specialized agencies for drug crime prevention and combat under the People’s Public Security, within the scope of their tasks and powers, shall assume the prime responsibility for, and cooperate with relevant agencies and organizations in, carrying out activities to prevent, stop and combat drug crimes.

  3. Specialized agencies for drug crime prevention and combat affiliated to the Border Guard, the Vietnam Coast Guard, and the Customs shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, take charge and coordinate with the police authorities and other relevant agencies and organizations in carrying out activities for prevention, prevention, and combat against drug crimes in the areas or localities under their management and control.

  4. In the same localities, when detecting acts of law violations related to the tasks and powers of multiple agencies, the agencies that detect such acts first shall handle them according to their competence as prescribed by law; in cases where the cases are beyond their competence, they shall transfer the dossiers, persons, and material evidences of law violations to competent agencies for handling.

  5. The Government shall prescribe the coordination among specialized agencies for drug crime prevention and combat.

  Chapter III

                  CONTROL OF LEGAL ACTIVITIES RELATED TO DRUGS

  Article 12. Legal activities related to drugs

  1. Legal activities related to drugs are activities permitted by competent state management agencies, including:

  a) Research, assessment, testing, examination, and production of narcotic substances (excluding cultivation of plants containing narcotic substances), precursors, addictive drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursor drugs, raw materials for drugs that are addictive active ingredients, psychotropic active ingredients, precursors used for drugs, and veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances or precursors;

  b) Transport, preservation, storage, purchase, sale, distribution, use, processing, exchange of narcotic substances, precursors, addictive drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursor drugs, raw materials for drugs which are addictive substances, psychotropic substances, precursors used for drugs and veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances, precursors;

  c) Import, export, temporary import, re-export, temporary export, re-import, transit of narcotic substances, precursors, addictive drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursor drugs, raw materials for drugs which are addictive substances, psychotropic substances, precursors used for drugs and veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances, precursors.

  2. Legitimate activities related to drugs prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall be strictly controlled in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  Article 13. Control of research, assessment, production of narcotic substances, precursors

  1. Research, assessment, production of narcotic substances (excluding cultivation of plants containing narcotic substances) and precursors must be monitored, inspected and supervised by competent state agencies.

  2. The Government shall prescribe competent agencies to grant permission, procedures for granting permission, monitoring, inspection and supervision prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 14. Control of transport of narcotic substances, precursors

  1. Agencies, organizations and individuals that have been permitted by competent state agencies may transport narcotic substances, precursors; when transporting, they must be packed and sealed in accordance with regulations of competent agencies; take responsibility for quantity, quality, safety measures, prevent loss during transport and be subject to monitoring, inspection and supervision by competent state agencies.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 15. Control of activities related to narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursors, and raw materials used as addictive or psychotropic active ingredients or precursors used as drugs

  The control of activities related to narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursors, and raw materials used as addictive or psychotropic active ingredients or precursors used as drugs shall comply with the law on pharmaceuticals, except for the activities specified in Point b, Clauses 1 and 2, Article 17 of this Law.

  Article 16. Control of veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances or precursors

  The control of veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances or precursors shall comply with the Government’s regulations.

  Article 17. Control of import, export, temporary import, re-export, temporary export, re-import, and transit of narcotic substances, precursors, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursors, raw materials used as addictive or psychotropic active ingredients or precursors used as drugs

  1. The following activities must be permitted by competent authorities:

  a) Import, export, temporary import, re-export, temporary export, re-import of narcotic substances or precursors;

  b) Temporary importation, re-exportation, temporary exportation, re-importation of narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursors, raw materials for drugs which are addictive substances, psychotropic substances, precursors used for drugs.

  2. The transit of narcotic substances, precursors, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursors, raw materials for drugs which are addictive substances, psychotropic substances, precursors used for drugs through the territory of Vietnam must strictly follow the route specified in the transit permit. The agencies and organizations that carry out the transit must carry out procedures and be under the control of the customs authorities and competent authorities of Vietnam.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 18. Preparation of dossiers on lawful activities related to drugs

  The agencies, organizations and individuals that carry out the activities specified in Clause 1 Article 12 of this Law shall prepare dossiers and make reports in accordance with the Government's regulations.

  Article 19. Control of lawful activities related to drugs for national defense and security purposes

  The research, assessment, production, transportation, storage, preservation, use, import, export, temporary importation, re-exportation, temporary exportation, re-importation, transit of narcotic substances, precursors, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursors, raw materials for drugs which are addictive substances, psychotropic substances, precursors used for drugs, veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances, precursors for national defense and security purposes shall comply with the Government's regulations.

  Article 20. Control of narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursors for first aid, emergency treatment during international travel, treatment of diseases for entry, exit, transit persons

  1. The carrying of narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, and precursor drugs for first aid and emergency treatment on ships, aircraft, trains, cars, or other transport vehicles during international voyages or tourism shall not be considered as carrying of export, import, or transit goods through the territory of Vietnam.

  The commanders and operators of transport vehicles shall declare to the Vietnamese customs authorities the quantity of drugs used and take appropriate safety measures to prevent the misuse or illegal transportation of such drugs, and shall be subject to the control of competent Vietnamese authorities.

  2. The carrying of narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, and precursor drugs for treatment of diseases by the entrants, exiters, or transit persons through the territory of Vietnam shall comply with the Government’s regulations and be subject to the control of competent Vietnamese authorities.

  Article 21. Handling of narcotic substances, precursors, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursor drugs, and drug-making materials which are addictive or psychotropic substances, precursors used as drugs, veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances, or precursors seized in violation cases Narcotic substances, precursors, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, precursor drugs, and drug-making materials which are addictive or psychotropic substances, precursors used as drugs, veterinary drugs containing narcotic substances, or precursors seized in criminal cases or administrative violation cases shall be handled in accordance with the law on criminal procedure and the law on handling of administrative violations.

  Chapter IV

                          MANAGEMENT OF DRUG USERS

  Article 22. Drug testing in the body

  1. Drug testing in the body shall be conducted on persons in the following cases:

  a) Persons who are detected using drugs illegally;

  b) Persons whose agencies or competent persons have grounds to believe that they have committed acts of using drugs illegally;

  c) Persons who are using drugs illegally and are under management;

  d) Persons who are under administrative education measures in communes, wards or townships due to acts of using drugs illegally; undergoing drug rehabilitation; undergoing treatment for addiction to opium-based drugs with replacement drugs; or under post-rehabilitation management.

  2. Heads of drug rehabilitation establishments and establishments providing treatment for addiction to opium-based drugs with replacement drugs; persons competent to impose administrative penalties for acts of using drugs illegally shall conduct drug tests within their competence or request specialized agencies or persons to test for drugs in the body for cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  In case of positive test results, the results shall be immediately sent to the President of the People’s Committee of the commune where the person with positive test results resides, except for cases where the person is undergoing compulsory drug rehabilitation.

  3. The State shall ensure funding for drug tests in the body for cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 23. Management of persons who use drugs illegally

  1. Management of illegal drug users is a preventive measure to help illegal drug users not to continue illegally using drugs and prevent their law-breaking acts.

  Management of illegal drug users is not an administrative handling measure.

  2. The time limit for management of illegal drug users is 01 year from the date on which the President of the commune-level People's Committee issues the management decision.

  3. The management of illegal drug users includes:

  a) Counseling, encouragement, education and assistance to illegal drug users so that they do not continue illegally using drugs;

  b) Drug testing in the body;

  c) Preventing illegal drug users from committing acts of disrupting social order and safety.

  4. Within 03 working days from the date on which the positive test result of a local resident is received, the President of the commune-level People's Committee shall issue a decision and organize the management of the local illegal drug user.

  5. Within the management period, the President of the People’s Committee of commune shall issue a decision to stop managing the illegal drug user in the following cases:

  a) The illegal drug user is identified as a drug addict;

  b) The illegal drug user is subject to the administrative handling measure of education at the commune, ward or township due to the act of illegally using drugs;

  c) The illegal drug user is subject to the administrative handling measure of being sent to a compulsory education institution or reform school;

  d) The illegal drug user must serve a prison sentence;

  dd) The illegal drug user on the management list dies or is declared missing by the Court.

  6. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 24. Responsibilities of the illegal drug user

  1. To provide sufficient and accurate information about their illegal use of narcotic substances to the commune-level police of their place of residence.

  2. To comply with the management of the commune-level People’s Committee.

  Article 25. Responsibilities of families, agencies, organizations and communities in managing persons illegally using narcotic substances

  1. Families of persons illegally using narcotic substances shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) To manage and educate persons illegally using narcotic substances; to prevent acts of illegal use of narcotic substances;

  b) To provide information about acts of illegal use of narcotic substances of persons illegally using narcotic substances to the commune-level police of the place where such persons reside;

  c) To prevent persons illegally using narcotic substances from committing acts of causing disorder and social safety;

  d) To cooperate with competent agencies in taking persons illegally using narcotic substances for narcotic substance testing in their bodies.

  2. Agencies and organizations where drug users work, and communities where drug users live, shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Encourage, assist and educate drug users; prevent acts of illegal drug use;

  b) Cooperate with families and competent agencies in taking drug users for drug tests.

  Article 26. Making lists of drug users

  1. Commune-level police shall assist People’s Committees at the same level in making lists of drug users residing in their localities.

  2. When a drug user changes his/her residence, the commune-level police of the locality where he/she moves to shall notify the commune-level police of the locality where he/she moves from within 05 working days from the date of his/her move-out to include him/her in the list and continue to manage him/her.

  3. Commune-level police shall assist People’s Committees at the same level in removing drug users from the list in the following cases:

  a) The drug user has not committed any act of illegal drug use during the management period specified in Clause 2 Article 23 of this Law;

  b) Illegal users of narcotic substances fall under the category of persons subject to suspension of management as prescribed in Clause 5, Article 23 of this Law;

  c) Illegal users of narcotic substances move to reside in other localities.

  Chapter V

                                              CAI NHIEN MA TUY

  Article 27. Determination of drug addiction status

  1. The determination of drug addiction status shall be made for persons falling into the following cases:

  a) Persons illegally using drugs who are under management and are detected using drugs;

  b) Persons illegally using drugs who have no stable place of residence;

  c) Persons under administrative education measures in communes, wards or townships due to acts of illegal drug use or within 01 year from the date of completion of administrative education measures in communes, wards or townships due to acts of illegal drug use who are detected using drugs;

  d) Persons under post-drug rehabilitation management who are detected using drugs;

  dd) Persons voluntarily determining their drug addiction status.

  2. The commune-level police authority of the place where a person falling into the cases specified in Points a, b, c and d, Clause 1 of this Article is detected shall prepare a dossier requesting a competent medical facility to determine the drug addiction status.

  3. In case the district-level police or provincial-level police directly detect or during the investigation and acceptance of cases of law violations detect cases specified in Points a, b, c and d Clause 1 of this Article, the police agency in charge of acceptance shall prepare a dossier requesting a competent medical facility to determine the addict status.

  4. Upon obtaining results of determination of addict status, the medical facility shall send the results to the requesting agency and the person determined to have addict status.

  5. The person requested to determine addict status shall have the following rights and responsibilities:

  a) Be guaranteed dignity and honor; assisted in travel, accommodation, treatment of withdrawal syndrome and accompanying diseases during the period of determination of addict status;

  b) Comply with the internal regulations of the facility determining addict status; honestly declare to medical staff on the drug use fee and signs of drug use;

  c) Persons aged 18 years or older or parents, guardians or legal representatives of persons aged between 12 and 18 years specified in Points a, b, c and dd Clause 1 of this Article upon receiving results of determination of addict status shall register for voluntary drug rehabilitation as prescribed in Article 28 of this Law or register for treatment of opium-based drugs with replacement drugs in case of addiction to opium-based drugs with the People’s Committee of the commune where they reside.

  6. The Government shall prescribe medical facilities qualified to determine addict status; dossiers, procedures for determination of addict status.

  7. The Minister of Health shall prescribe diagnostic criteria and professional procedures for determination of addict status.

  8. The State shall ensure funding for determination of drug addiction status for cases specified in Points a, b, c and d, Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 28. Drug rehabilitation measures

  1. Drug rehabilitation measures include:

  a) Voluntary drug rehabilitation;

  b) Mandatory drug rehabilitation.

  2. Voluntary drug rehabilitation measures shall be implemented at home, community or drug rehabilitation facilities; mandatory drug rehabilitation measures shall be implemented at public drug rehabilitation facilities.

  Article 29. Drug rehabilitation process

  1. The drug rehabilitation process includes the following stages:

  a) Reception and classification;

  b) Treatment for seizure, detoxification, mental disorders and other diseases;

  c) Education, counseling, behavior and personality rehabilitation;

  d) Therapeutic work and vocational training;

  dd) Preparation for reintegration into the community.

  2. Compulsory drug rehabilitation must ensure all stages specified in Clause 1 of this Article; voluntary drug rehabilitation must ensure completion of all 3 stages specified in Points a, b and c, Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 30. Voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and in the community

  1. Voluntary drug rehabilitation at home or community means that a drug addict voluntarily undergoes rehabilitation at home or community with the professional assistance of organizations and individuals providing drug rehabilitation services, the cooperation and assistance of family and community, and under the management of the commune-level People’s Committee.

  2. The duration of voluntary drug rehabilitation at home or community is from 06 months to 12 months.

  3. A person who voluntarily undergoes drug rehabilitation at home or community shall be assisted with funding upon completion of at least 03 stages specified in Points a, b and c Clause 1 Article 29 of this Law.

  4. A person who voluntarily undergoes drug rehabilitation at home or community shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Comply with and fully implement regulations on voluntary drug rehabilitation and follow the guidance of professional agencies;

  b) Pay expenses related to drug rehabilitation as prescribed.

  5. The President of the commune-level People’s Committee shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Receive and register for voluntary drug rehabilitation at home or community;

  b) Provide guidance and management of voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community;

  c) Issue certificates of completion of voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community.

  6. Chairpersons of district-level People’s Committees shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Assign tasks to public service units under their jurisdiction to provide voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community;

  b) Receive and publish lists of eligible organizations and individuals to provide voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community;

  c) Notify the People’s Committees of commune-level People’s Committees of lists of organizations and individuals providing voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community;

  d) Allocate funds to support voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community;

  dd) Direct, guide and inspect voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community.

  7. Narcotics rehabilitation centers, organizations and individuals that are eligible to provide one or more rehabilitation activities according to the narcotic rehabilitation process specified in Clause 1 Article 29 of this Law may provide voluntary narcotic rehabilitation services at home and in the community and shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Receive and organize the provision of voluntary narcotic rehabilitation services at home and in the community;

  b) Strictly follow the professional process as prescribed by competent authorities;

  c) Within 05 working days from the date on which the narcotic rehabilitation user uses the service or voluntarily terminates the use of the service or completes the service, notify the People’s Committee of the commune where the person registers for voluntary narcotic rehabilitation at home and in the community.

  8. Organizations and individuals that are eligible may register for the provision of voluntary narcotic rehabilitation services at home and in the community with the President of the district-level People’s Committee.

  9. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 31. Voluntary narcotic rehabilitation at narcotic rehabilitation centers

  1. The duration of voluntary narcotic rehabilitation at narcotic rehabilitation centers is from 06 months to 12 months.

  2. Voluntary drug addicts at drug rehabilitation centers shall be assisted in funding when they complete at least 03 stages specified in Points a, b and c Clause 1 Article 29 of this Law.

  3. Voluntary drug addicts at drug rehabilitation centers shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Strictly and fully comply with regulations on voluntary drug rehabilitation and follow guidance of specialized agencies;

  b) Pay fees related to drug rehabilitation as prescribed.

  In case voluntary drug addicts at public drug rehabilitation centers have difficult circumstances, they may be considered for reduction or exemption of fees to be paid.

  4. Drug rehabilitation centers shall have the responsibility to issue certificates of completion of voluntary drug rehabilitation to drug addicts.

  5. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 32. Subjects subject to administrative handling measures of being sent to compulsory drug rehabilitation centers

  A person addicted to drugs aged 18 or older shall be subject to the administrative measure of being sent to a compulsory rehabilitation facility in accordance with the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations if he/she falls into one of the following cases:

  1. Failing to register, fail to comply with or voluntarily terminate drug rehabilitation;

  2. During the period of voluntary drug rehabilitation, the person is found to have illegally used drugs;

  3. The person addicted to opium-based drugs fails to register, fail to comply with or voluntarily terminate treatment for opium-based drug addiction with replacement drugs or his/her treatment for opium-based drug addiction with replacement drugs is terminated due to violation of regulations on drug addiction treatment;

  4. Relapses during the period of post-rehabilitation management.

  Article 33. Drug rehabilitation for persons aged between 12 and 18

  1. A person addicted to drugs aged between 12 and 18 shall be sent to a compulsory rehabilitation facility if he/she falls into one of the following cases:

  a) Failing to register, fail to comply with or voluntarily terminate drug rehabilitation;

  b) During the period of voluntary drug rehabilitation, the person is found to have illegally used drugs;

  c) The person is a drug addict using opium-based drugs without registration, fails to carry out or voluntarily terminates the treatment of opium-based drugs with replacement drugs, or has his/her treatment of opium-based drugs with replacement drugs terminated due to violation of regulations on drug rehabilitation.

  2. Drug addicts aged from 12 to under 18 years of age who are sent to compulsory rehabilitation facilities shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Comply with regulations on compulsory drug rehabilitation, internal regulations, and regulations, and be under the management, education, and treatment of the compulsory rehabilitation facility;

  b) Participate in treatment, medical treatment, education, counseling, cultural and vocational education, therapeutic labor, and activities for behavioral and personality recovery.

  3. The period of compulsory drug rehabilitation for drug addicts aged from 12 to under 18 years of age shall be from 06 months to 12 months.

  4. The sending of drug addicts aged from 12 to under 18 years of age to compulsory rehabilitation facilities shall be decided by the district-level People’s Court and is not an administrative handling measure.

  5. The Standing Committee of the National Assembly shall prescribe the order and procedures for the People’s Court to consider and decide on the sending of drug addicts aged from 12 to under 18 years of age to compulsory rehabilitation facilities.

  Article 34. Preparation of dossiers for proposal of compulsory rehabilitation centers for drug addicts aged from 12 to under 18 years

  1. The preparation of dossiers for proposal of compulsory rehabilitation centers for drug addicts aged from 12 to under 18 years shall be carried out as follows:

  a) The President of the People’s Committee of the commune where the person resides or commits a violation in case his/her residence is not identified shall prepare the dossier for proposal of compulsory rehabilitation centers;

  b) In case the drug addict is discovered by the district-level police or provincial-level police or in the course of investigation and acceptance of the violation cases, the person aged from 12 to under 18 years is subject to compulsory rehabilitation centers, the police agency in charge of the case shall verify, collect documents and prepare the dossier for proposal of compulsory rehabilitation centers for the person;

  c) The dossier for proposal shall include the record of violation, summary of the person’s background, documents proving the person’s current drug addiction, written statement of the drug addict or his/her legal representative, opinions of his/her parents, guardian or legal representative;

  d) The commune-level police shall assist the President of the People’s Committee of the same level in collecting documents and preparing the dossier for proposal as prescribed in Point a, Clause 1 of this Article.

  2. The agency or person preparing the dossier for proposal as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall be responsible for the legality of the dossier for proposal. After completing the preparation of the dossier for proposal, the agency or person preparing the dossier shall notify in writing the person proposed to be subject to the measure of compulsory rehabilitation centers, his/her parents, guardian or legal representative of the preparation of the dossier. These persons have the right to read the dossier and give their opinions on the preparation of the dossier within 03 working days from the date of receiving the notice.

  3. Within 01 working day from the date of expiration of the deadline for reading the dossier, the agency or person who has prepared the dossier shall send the dossier to the Head of the Department of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of the district where the person proposed is residing or commits a violation in case his/her residence is not identified. Within 02 working days from the date of receiving the dossier, the Head of the Department of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of the district shall decide to transfer the dossier for proposal to the People’s Court of the district for decision on compulsory rehabilitation centers. In case the dossier is incomplete, the Head of the Department of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of the district shall transfer it back to the agency or person who has prepared the dossier for supplementation, the deadline for supplementation is 02 working days from the date of receiving the dossier. Within 02 working days from the date of receiving the supplemented dossier, the Head of the Department of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of the district shall decide to transfer the dossier for proposal to the People’s Court of the district for decision on compulsory rehabilitation centers.

  4. An application for the district-level People’s Court to consider and decide on the compulsory rehabilitation facility admission shall include:

  a) An application for the compulsory rehabilitation facility admission specified in Point c, Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) A written request for consideration of the compulsory rehabilitation facility admission from the head of the district-level Department of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs.

  The head of the Department of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall state his/her opinion on the protection of children’s rights in the written request.

  5. An application for the compulsory rehabilitation facility admission of a drug addict aged between 12 and under 18 years of age must be recorded in the register and stored in accordance with the law on storage.

  6. The Government shall elaborate on the preparation of applications for the compulsory rehabilitation facility admission of drug addicts aged between 12 and under 18 years of age; and the drug rehabilitation regime applicable to drug addicts aged between 12 and under 18 years of age.

  Article 35. Public drug rehabilitation facilities

  1. A public drug rehabilitation facility shall be established by the President of the provincial-level People’s Committee.

  2. Public drug rehabilitation centers must arrange the following areas:

  a) Temporary accommodation area for persons whose drug addiction status is determined and persons who, during the period of preparing dossiers for application of administrative handling measures, are sent to compulsory rehabilitation centers;

  b) Compulsory drug rehabilitation area;

  c) Voluntary drug rehabilitation area;

  d) Rehabilitation area for persons aged from 12 to under 18;

  dd) Rehabilitation area for persons with infectious diseases of Group A and Group B as prescribed by the Law on Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases;

  e) Rehabilitation area for persons who commit acts of disrupting order or violating regulations of drug rehabilitation centers.

  3. In the areas specified in Clause 2 of this Article, separate areas for men and women must be arranged. Persons whose gender differs from their biological sex as stated in their dossiers and records shall be managed in separate rooms in the area according to the biological sex of the learners.

  4. Activities of public drug rehabilitation establishments include:

  a) Receiving and organizing drug rehabilitation for drug addicts who are brought to compulsory rehabilitation establishments and voluntary drug addicts;

  b) Determining the drug addiction status;

  c) Receiving, managing, educating, counseling, treating withdrawal syndrome, mental disorders and other diseases for persons who are in the process of preparing dossiers for being brought to compulsory rehabilitation establishments.

  5. Public drug rehabilitation establishments have the following rights:

  a) Receiving drug addicts for voluntary drug rehabilitation in accordance with the capacity of the rehabilitation establishments;

  b) The head of a public drug rehabilitation establishment may decide to apply appropriate measures to manage, educate and treat drug addicts.

  6. Public drug rehabilitation establishments have the following responsibilities:

  a) Comply with regulations on determination of drug addiction status, drug rehabilitation process, and management of persons undergoing the process of preparing dossiers for admission to compulsory rehabilitation centers;

  b) Respect the lives, health, honor, dignity, and property of drug addicts, persons undergoing the process of determining drug addiction status, and persons undergoing the process of preparing dossiers for admission to compulsory rehabilitation centers;

  c) Ensure the rights of drug addicts during the rehabilitation period and issue certificates of completion of compulsory drug rehabilitation for persons aged from 12 to under 18;

  d) Heads of public drug rehabilitation centers shall be responsible for ensuring the operation conditions of drug rehabilitation centers; publicly posting drug rehabilitation costs at rehabilitation centers in accordance with law;

  dd) Prevent and combat smuggling of drugs into rehabilitation centers;

  e) Within 05 working days from the date on which a drug addict voluntarily receives services or voluntarily terminates the use of services or completes the drug rehabilitation process, the person shall notify the People’s Committee of the commune where the person registers for voluntary drug rehabilitation.

  7. The Government shall stipulate conditions for material facilities, equipment, and personnel of public drug rehabilitation centers and management regimes at public drug rehabilitation centers.

  Article 36. Voluntary drug rehabilitation centers

  1. Voluntary drug rehabilitation centers shall be established by individuals or organizations providing drug rehabilitation services. The Government shall define conditions and competence, and procedures for granting and revoking licenses for voluntary drug rehabilitation centers.

  2. Voluntary drug rehabilitation centers shall have the following rights:

  a) Receive drug addicts for voluntary rehabilitation in accordance with their capacity;

  b) Be entitled to collect expenses related to drug rehabilitation;

  c) Be entitled to preferential regimes as prescribed by law.

  3. Voluntary drug rehabilitation centers shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Comply with the drug rehabilitation process;

  b) Respect the lives, health, honor, dignity and property of drug addicts;

  c) Ensure the rights of drug addicts aged from 12 to under 18 during the drug rehabilitation period;

  d) The head of a drug rehabilitation facility shall be responsible for ensuring the operation conditions of the drug rehabilitation facility; publicly posting the costs related to drug rehabilitation at the facility in accordance with law;

  dd) Prevent and combat the smuggling of drugs into the facility;

  e) Arrange separate areas or rooms when receiving subjects aged from 12 to under 18, people with infectious diseases of group A and group B according to the Law on Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases, women; people with differences between their physical and gender as recorded in their dossiers and records managed in separate rooms in areas according to the gender of the learners;

  g) Within 05 working days from the date on which the drug addict is received or voluntarily terminates the use of services or completes the drug rehabilitation process, he/she must notify the People’s Committee of the commune where he/she registers for voluntary drug rehabilitation.

  Article 37. Application of drug rehabilitation measures to Vietnamese persons expelled by foreign countries due to illegal drug use or drug addiction; foreign drug addicts living in Vietnam

  1. Vietnamese persons expelled by foreign countries to Vietnam due to illegal drug use or drug addiction upon their return to Vietnam must have their drug addiction status determined. If they are determined to be addicted to drugs, they must undergo drug rehabilitation measures in accordance with this Law.

  2. Foreign drug addicts living in Vietnam shall register for voluntary drug rehabilitation at a drug rehabilitation facility in accordance with this Law and pay all costs related to drug rehabilitation. If they fail to undergo voluntary drug rehabilitation, they shall be handled in accordance with law.

  Article 38. Drug rehabilitation for persons in custody, prisoners, inmates of compulsory education establishments, students of reform schools

  1. The State shall apply appropriate drug rehabilitation measures to persons in custody, prisoners, inmates of compulsory education establishments, students of reform schools who are drug addicts.

  2. Superintendents of detention camps, superintendents of temporary detention camps, heads of temporary detention houses, directors of compulsory education establishments, principals of reform schools shall closely cooperate with local medical facilities in implementing the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 39. Exemption from serving the remaining time in the decision to send to compulsory rehabilitation establishments for persons aged 14 to under 18 who are undergoing compulsory drug rehabilitation

  A person aged 14 to under 18 who is undergoing compulsory drug rehabilitation and commits a crime before or during the period of serving the decision on compulsory drug rehabilitation and is sentenced to imprisonment by the Court but is not given a suspended sentence shall be exempted from serving the remaining time in the decision to send to compulsory rehabilitation establishments.

  Article 40. Post-rehabilitation management at the place of residence

  1. Persons who have voluntarily completed drug rehabilitation, persons who have completed treatment for addiction to opium-based drugs with replacement drugs, persons aged 12 to under 18 who have fully served the decision to send to compulsory rehabilitation establishments shall be subject to post-rehabilitation management for 1 year from the date of completion of rehabilitation or serving the decision.

  2. Persons who have fully served the decision to apply administrative measures to send to compulsory rehabilitation establishments shall be subject to post-rehabilitation management for 2 years from the date of serving the decision.

  3. Contents of post-treatment management include:

  a) Making a list of persons to be managed post-treatment;

  b) Counseling, assistance, prevention and combat of drug addiction;

  c) Monitoring, detecting and preventing illegal use of drugs.

  4. Social support during post-treatment management includes:

  a) Cultural education support for persons aged from 12 to under 18;

  b) Vocational education support, loan, job search and participation in social activities for community integration.

  5. The President of the People’s Committee of commune shall issue decisions and organize post-treatment management and social support.

  6. The Government shall define the dossiers, procedures, management regimes and support policies for persons under management after drug rehabilitation.

  Article 41. Harm reduction measures for illegal drug users and drug addicts

  1. Harm reduction measures for illegal drug users and drug addicts are measures to reduce the harm related to the illegal use of drugs, which causes harm to themselves, their families and the community.

  2. Harm reduction measures for illegal drug users and drug addicts include:

  a) Treatment of addiction to opium-based drugs with replacement drugs;

  b) Other measures as prescribed by the Government.

  Article 42. Responsibilities of families of drug addicts and the community

  1. Families of drug addicts shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) To assist drug addicts in the process of drug rehabilitation, post-rehabilitation and community integration;

  b) To cooperate with competent agencies and persons in preparing dossiers for application of compulsory drug rehabilitation measures; cooperate with relevant agencies, organizations and units in assisting drug addicts in the process of drug rehabilitation, post-rehabilitation and community integration.

  2. The communities where drug addicts reside shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) To motivate and assist drug addicts;

  b) To cooperate with relevant agencies, organizations and units in assisting drug addicts in the process of drug rehabilitation, post-rehabilitation and community integration.

  Article 43. Compilation of lists of drug addicts and persons under post-rehabilitation management

  1. The commune-level police shall assist the People’s Committees of the same level in compiling lists of drug addicts and persons under post-rehabilitation management residing in their localities.

  2. When drug addicts or persons under post-rehabilitation management change their place of residence, the commune-level police of the locality where they move to shall have the responsibility to notify the commune-level police of the locality where they move from within 05 working days from the date of their move-out, in order to include them in the list and cooperate in management.

  3. The commune-level police shall assist the People’s Committees of the same level in removing persons addicted to drugs or persons under post-treatment management from the list of drug addicts and persons under post-treatment management in the following cases:

  a) Within the post-treatment management period prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 Article 40 of this Law, such persons are not found to have illegally used drugs;

  b) Drug addicts or persons under post-treatment management must serve prison sentences;

  c) Drug addicts or persons under post-treatment management move to reside in other localities;

  d) Drug addicts or persons under post-treatment management die or are declared missing by the Court.

  Chapter VI

                            STATE MANAGEMENT OF DRUG PREVENTION AND CONTROL

  Article 44. Content of state management of drug prevention and control

  1. To formulate and organize the implementation of strategies, principles, policies and plans on drug prevention and combat.

  2. To promulgate and organize the implementation of legal documents on drug prevention and combat.

  3. To organize the establishment of apparatuses and training and retraining of personnel engaged in drug prevention and combat.

  4. To control lawful activities related to drugs.

  5. To carry out drug rehabilitation and post-rehabilitation management.

  6. To organize the drug prevention and combat.

  7. To carry out state statistics on drug prevention and combat.

  8. To organize the research and application of scientific and technological advances in drug prevention and combat.

  9. To organize the propaganda and education on drug prevention and combat.

  10. To cooperate internationally in drug prevention and combat.

  11. To commend, inspect, investigate, settle complaints and denunciations and handle violations of the law on drug prevention and combat.

  Article 45. Responsibilities for state management of drug prevention and combat

  1. The Government shall unify the state management of drug prevention and combat.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall be the focal agency to assist the Government in carrying out the state management of drug prevention and combat.

  3. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies and Governmental agencies shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, have to implement and cooperate with relevant agencies and organizations in drug prevention and combat.

  4. People’s Committees at all levels shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, carry out the state management of drug prevention and combat in their localities.

  Article 46. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Public Security

  1. To promulgate, within its competence, or submit to competent state agencies for promulgation, legal documents on drug prevention and combat.

  2. To assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with relevant agencies and organizations in drug prevention and combat; organize the receipt and handling of drug-related crime information; control lawful activities related to drugs within its competence.

  3. To organize the assessment of narcotic substances and precursors.

  4. To provide training and retraining for persons engaged in investigation and combat against drug-related crimes.

  5. To guide the compilation of lists of persons illegally using narcotic substances, drug addicts, and persons under post-rehabilitation management.

  6. To assume the prime responsibility for conducting state statistics on drug prevention and combat; managing information on drug-related crimes, persons illegally using narcotic substances, drug addicts, persons under post-rehabilitation management, and results of control of lawful activities related to drugs.

  7. To cooperate internationally in drug prevention and combat.

  Article 47. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs

  1. To promulgate, within its competence, or submit to competent regulatory bodies for promulgation, legal documents on drug prevention and combat.

  2. To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with relevant agencies, organizations and local governments in, guiding the organization of voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community, drug rehabilitation at drug rehabilitation facilities and post-rehabilitation management.

  3. To guide the establishment, dissolution and operation of public drug rehabilitation facilities.

  4. To train and retrain persons working in drug rehabilitation and post-rehabilitation management.

  5. To guide and direct the organization of the dissemination and education on drug prevention and combat in the system of vocational education and training establishments.

  6. To cooperate internationally in drug rehabilitation.

  7. To compile statistics on drug addicts undergoing voluntary drug rehabilitation at home and community, and drug rehabilitation at drug rehabilitation facilities.

  Article 48. Responsibilities of the Ministry of National Defense

  1. To promulgate, within its competence, or submit to competent regulatory bodies for promulgation, legal documents on drug prevention and combat.

  2. To organize the implementation of the drug prevention and combat plan in the land, sea border areas, the maritime border areas, the exclusive economic zones and the continental shelves.

  3. To direct the Border Guard and the Vietnam Coast Guard to assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with relevant agencies, organizations and functional forces in, receiving and handling information on drug-related crimes, detecting, preventing and combating drug-related violations, and controlling lawful activities related to drugs in the areas specified in Clause 2 of this Article in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  4. To provide training and retraining for the drug prevention and combat specialists of the Vietnam Border Guard and the Vietnam Coast Guard.

  5. To compile statistics on drug addicts in the detention camps, temporary detention camps and the custody houses under the management of the Ministry of National Defense.

  Article 49. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Health

  1. To promulgate, within its competence, or submit to competent regulatory bodies for promulgation, legal documents on drug prevention and combat.

  2. To conduct research on drugs and methods of drug rehabilitation; issue and revoke licenses for circulation of drugs and methods of drug rehabilitation; provide support for rehabilitation personnel and medical technical personnel for drug rehabilitation.

  3. To compile statistics on persons undergoing treatment for addiction to opium-based drugs using replacement drugs.

  Article 50. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Finance

  To direct the Customs authority to carry out drug prevention and control activities in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  Chapter VII

                             INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION IN DRUG PREVENTION AND CONTROL

  Article 51. Principles of international cooperation in drug prevention and control

  1. The State of Vietnam shall implement international treaties on drug prevention and control and other relevant international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a member on the principles of respect for independence, sovereignty, territorial integrity and mutual benefit; cooperate with foreign countries, international organizations, foreign organizations and individuals in drug prevention and control activities.

  2. On the basis of international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory, this Law and other relevant provisions of law, and signed international agreements, competent authorities of Vietnam shall implement cooperation programs on drug prevention and combat with relevant authorities of other countries, international organizations, foreign organizations and individuals.

  Article 52. International cooperation policy on drug prevention and combat

  The Vietnamese State shall implement multilateral and bilateral international cooperation policies in the field of drug prevention and combat on the principle of respect for the independence and sovereignty of other countries; encourage international organizations, foreign organizations and individuals to cooperate with Vietnamese agencies and organizations in mutual support in terms of material facilities, strengthening legal capacity, information, technology for training and medicine for drug prevention and combat activities.

  Article 53. Transfer of controlled goods

  The Vietnamese State shall implement requests for transfer of controlled goods on the basis of international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory in order to detect and investigate criminal liability of drug offenders. The decision to apply and implement this measure shall be made in accordance with agreements between competent authorities of Vietnam and relevant countries.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 54. Effect

  1. This Law takes effect on January 1, 2022.

  2. The Law on Drug Prevention and Control No. 23/2000/QH10, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 16/2008/QH12, shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Law.

  Article 55. Transitional provisions

  1. From the effective date of this Law:

  a) Persons who are undergoing voluntary drug rehabilitation at home, community or rehabilitation facility shall continue to undergo voluntary drug rehabilitation until the end of the period of registration as prescribed by the Law on Drug Prevention and Control No. 23/2000/QH10, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 16/2008/QH12, and shall be managed after drug rehabilitation as prescribed by this Law;

  b) Persons who are under management after drug rehabilitation shall continue to comply with the decision on management after drug rehabilitation until the end of the period of management as prescribed by the Law on Drug Prevention and Control No. 23/2000/QH10, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 16/2008/QH12. In case a person who is under management after drug rehabilitation reaches the age of 18 from the effective date of this Law and relapses, administrative measures of sending him/her to a compulsory rehabilitation facility shall be applied;

  c) Persons who are complying with the decision on compulsory drug rehabilitation at community as prescribed by the Law on Drug Prevention and Control No. 23/2000/QH10, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 16/2008/QH12, may register for voluntary drug rehabilitation as prescribed by this Law. The period of compliance with the decision on compulsory drug rehabilitation at community shall be counted into the period of voluntary drug rehabilitation;

  d) Persons who are subject to the obligation to be sent to a compulsory rehabilitation facility as prescribed by the Law on Drug Prevention and Control No. 23/2000/QH10, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 16/2008/QH12, and are in the process of preparing dossiers for being sent to a compulsory rehabilitation facility, shall undergo drug rehabilitation as prescribed by this Law;

  d) Licenses related to lawful drug-related activities that have been issued shall continue to be used until the expiration date inscribed on the licenses.

  2. Within 02 years from the effective date of this Law, compulsory drug rehabilitation establishments established in accordance with the Law on Drug Prevention and Combat No. 23/2000/QH10, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 16/2008/QH12, must fully satisfy the conditions of public drug rehabilitation establishments in accordance with this Law.

  Other drug rehabilitation establishments in accordance with the Law on Drug Prevention and Combat No. 23/2000/QH10, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 16/2008/QH12, that have been granted operating licenses before the effective date of this Law may continue to operate until the expiration date of the licenses.

  This Law was passed by the XIV National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 11th session on March 30, 2021.
