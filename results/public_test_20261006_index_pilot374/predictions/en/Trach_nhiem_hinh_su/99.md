                                             CIRCULAR

ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE LAW ON THE FAMILY

                               GUIDELINES FOR EXECUTION OF SENTENCES OF EXILE

  Pursuant to the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments dated June 17, 2010;

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 77/2009/ND-CP dated September 15, 2009, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 15/2008/ND-CP dated February 4, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 104/2008/ND-CP dated September 16, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of National Defense;

  In order to harmonize the implementation of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments on the execution of sentences of exile, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall provide the following unified guidelines:

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  This Circular provides guidance on procedures for enforcement of expulsion penalties; handling cases where persons subject to expulsion penalties flee, fall ill or die while awaiting exit; handling of receipt of corpses, remains or ashes of persons subject to expulsion penalties who die; and funding for enforcement of expulsion penalties.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to persons subject to deportation sentences according to court judgments and decisions (hereinafter referred to as “deported persons”) and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the enforcement of deportation sentences.

  Article 3. Principles for enforcement of expulsion penalties

  1. Ensure the State's political, legal, foreign affairs and humanitarian policies.

  2. The execution of the expulsion penalty must ensure safety, proper identification, proper time, proper procedures and proper jurisdiction in accordance with Vietnamese law, international law and practice.

  3. To ensure the lawful rights and interests of the deported persons; strictly prohibit any act of infringing upon the life, health, dignity and lawful property of the deported persons.

  Article 4. Agencies executing expulsion judgments

  1. Criminal enforcement agencies of provincial police offices where the People’s Courts or Military Courts issue expulsion judgment execution decisions shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with relevant agencies in, executing expulsion judgments according to the Court’s judgment execution decisions.

  2. Criminal enforcement agencies of provincial police offices where prisons of the Ministry of Public Security, prisons of the Ministry of National Defense, or prisons of military zones are located shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with relevant agencies in, executing expulsion judgments against persons who have fully served their prison sentences with the additional penalty of expulsion.

  Article 5. Funds for judgment execution

  Funds for executing expulsion judgments shall be paid from the state budget in the annual budget estimates of the criminal enforcement agencies of provincial police offices and the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security. Expenses for purchasing exit visas for deported persons shall comply with Article 102 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments.

  Chapter II

                          ORDER AND PROCEDURES FOR EXECUTING EXPULSION JUDGMENTS

  Article 6. Notification of expulsion judgment execution

  1. Within 03 (three) working days from the date of receipt of the court’s decision on execution of the deportation sentence in the cases specified in Clause 1 Article 96 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments or from the date of receipt of the prison director’s notice on the completion of the prisoner’s sentence of deportation, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, diplomatic missions, consular offices, other authorized agencies performing consular functions of the country where the deported person is a citizen residing in Vietnam or the representative office of the international organization where the person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed the person’s entry into Vietnam.

  2. In case the deported person is granted amnesty or exemption from serving the prison sentence, the prison director shall notify in writing the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the prison is located within 24 (twenty-four) hours from the date of receipt of the amnesty or exemption from serving the prison sentence, to immediately notify the agencies specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 7. Deportation judgment enforcement dossier

  1. Within 03 (three) working days from the date of receipt of the court’s judgment enforcement decision or from the date of receipt of the deported person, the person granted amnesty or exemption from serving the prison sentence, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall prepare a deportation judgment enforcement dossier.

  2. The deportation judgment enforcement dossier (hereinafter referred to as the enforcement dossier) must include all documents and papers specified in Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments. The certificate of completion of other penalties or other obligations specified in Point c Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments includes: certificate of completion of prison sentence, amnesty, exemption from serving the prison sentence, exemption or reduction of fine, decision on termination or suspension of judgment enforcement, certificate or certification of competent judgment enforcement agency on judgment enforcement results or results of performance of other obligations.

  3. When handing over the deported person to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the prison is located, the prison director shall hand over a copy of the judgment enforcement decision, certificate of completion of prison sentence, certificate of amnesty, certificate of exemption from serving the prison sentence, and documents proving performance of other obligations (if any) to prepare the deportation judgment enforcement dossier.

  Article 8. Designation of accommodation location

  1. Within 03 (three) working days from the date of preparing the judgment execution dossier, the head of the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall issue a decision to designate a place of residence for the person to be deported while awaiting exit. The designated place of residence must have a clear address and ensure convenience for management and supervision. The use of criminal detention cells, detention cells of detention houses, detention camps or detention houses of prisons for managing the person to be deported while awaiting exit is prohibited.

  2. In case the person to be deported falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 Article 99 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Execution, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall issue a decision and organize the deportation of the person to the accommodation facility of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as the accommodation facility).

  3. In case the deported person or their relatives or legal representatives request to stay at a lawful temporary residence within another province or city, they must submit an application to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared for consideration and decision. The application must clearly state the applicant's full name, address, relationship with the deported person, address of the place of residence, commitment to comply with all requirements of the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority, abide by laws and regulations of the local government where the place of residence is located. The application must be translated into Vietnamese, certified by the diplomatic mission, consular mission or other authorized agency of the country of which the deported person is a citizen or the international organization where the person works with its head office in Vietnam, and the People's Committee of the commune or the agency or organization where the deported person stays.

  4. Within 02 (two) working days from the date of issuing the decision to designate a place of residence, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall notify the court that has issued the judgment execution decision, the procuracy of the same level and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.

  Article 9. Management of deported persons

  1. The criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall assume the prime responsibility and coordinate with functional agencies and local governments where the deported person stays to manage the deported person.

  2. Within 03 (three) working days from the date of issuing the decision to place the deported person in the accommodation facility or the decision to designate a place of residence in another province or city, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall send a notice together with a copy of the judgment execution judgment or decision to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the accommodation facility is located or the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the deported person stays to coordinate in management and assume the prime responsibility for carrying out procedures for settlement when the deported person absconds or dies as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 99 and Article 100 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Execution.

  Article 10. Settlement of cases where persons subject to deportation penalties abscond

  1. During the stay awaiting exit, if the deported person absconds, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the accommodation facility is located or the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the designated accommodation location is located shall immediately organize the pursuit and notify the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement file is prepared; if the pursuit fails, within 07 (seven) days from the date on which the deported person absconds, a wanted notice shall be issued in accordance with law.

  2. In case the deported person absconds is recaptured or surrenders, the agency that recaptures or receives the surrender must make a record, immediately notify the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement file is prepared to receive and bring the person to the designated accommodation facility, and notify the agency that issued the wanted notice to issue a wanted notice in accordance with regulations.

  3. In case the deported person absconds on the way to the accommodation facility or the exit location, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority that organizes the escort shall be responsible for organizing the pursuit, issuing a wanted notice, and issuing a wanted notice.

  4. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement file is prepared shall immediately notify the Procuracy of the same level, the Court that issued the deportation judgment, the criminal judgment enforcement management agency under the Ministry of Public Security, and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular about the deported person's absconding or surrender.

  Article 11. Handling cases where deported persons fall ill or die

  1. During the stay awaiting exit at the accommodation facility, the deported person may receive medical examination and treatment in accordance with the Government's regulations for deported persons, foreigners who have completed their prison sentences and are staying at the accommodation facility to wait for exit procedures. In case the deported person is seriously ill and must go to the State medical facility for treatment, the accommodation facility or designated accommodation location must immediately notify the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the accommodation facility or designated accommodation location is located to notify the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.

  2. In case the deported person is receiving emergency treatment or is seriously ill and unable to move, and has the certification of a provincial hospital or higher, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority that manages the deported person at the designated accommodation location or the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the accommodation facility is located must notify the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement file is prepared to make a written notification to the Court that issued the judgment enforcement decision to consider and issue a decision to extend the period of forced departure from Vietnam.

  3. In case the deported person dies during his/her stay, the accommodation establishment or designated accommodation place shall immediately notify the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office where the accommodation establishment or designated accommodation place is located, the investigation agency and the competent Procuracy to determine the cause of death. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours from the time the deported person dies, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office where the deported person dies shall notify the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office where the judgment enforcement file is made to immediately notify in writing or by telegraph the court that has issued the judgment enforcement decision, and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular. After completing the procedures for determining the cause of death and obtaining permission from the competent agency, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office where the accommodation establishment or designated accommodation place is located shall be responsible for organizing the burial.

  In case the deported person dies on the way to the accommodation establishment or place of exit, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office where the deportation is organized shall take charge and cooperate with the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office, the investigation agency and the competent Procuracy where the deported person dies to carry out the procedures for settlement as prescribed in Clause 4, Article 99 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  4. Expenses for burial of the deported person:

  a) Expenses for burial of the deported person at the accommodation establishment shall comply with the Government's regulations on food, accommodation, clothing, daily life, medical examination and treatment, and burial expenses for deported persons during their stay at the accommodation establishment;

  b) Expenses for burial of the deported person during his/her stay at another designated accommodation place shall be applied similarly to those for the deported person who dies at the accommodation establishment, except for cases where his/her relatives or legal representative pay for it.

  Article 12. Settlement of receipt of the corpse, remains or ashes of the deported person

  1. In case the deported person dies and his/her relatives or legal representative request to receive the corpse for burial, within 48 (forty-eight) hours from the time the deported person dies, a written request must be sent to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office where the judgment enforcement file is made for consideration and decision.

  2. The written request must clearly state the full name, address of the corpse recipient, his/her relationship with the deported person, the burial place, commitment to ensure security, order, environmental hygiene and self-payment of expenses. The written request must be certified by the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which the deported person is a citizen or the representative office of the international organization where the deported person works and must be translated into Vietnamese. If the requester is a Vietnamese citizen, the written request must be certified by the People's Committee of the commune where he/she resides. In case of receiving the corpse for burial in the territory of Vietnam, the written request must be approved by the People's Committee of the commune where the corpse is buried.

  3. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours from the receipt of the written request, the head of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall decide on the receipt of the corpse and notify in writing the applicant, the court that has issued the judgment enforcement decision, and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular on the receipt or non-receipt of the corpse when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of the corpse will affect the security, order, and environmental hygiene.

  4. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours from the notification of the receipt of the corpse, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority and the applicant shall carry out the handover and receipt of the corpse, money, and lawful property of the deported person (if any), and make a record signed by the handover and receipt parties and the witness; if the applicant fails to come to receive the corpse within this time limit, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall be responsible for organizing the burial.

  5. The receipt of the remains of the deported person shall only be permitted after 3 (three) years from the date of burial. In case of cremation, the receipt of ashes may be permitted from the date of cremation completion. The applicant for the receipt of the remains or ashes shall submit a written request to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the deported person is buried for consideration and decision; the written request must contain all the details specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Within 7 (seven) working days from the receipt of the written request, the head of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall decide on the receipt of the remains or ashes and notify in writing the applicant, the court that has issued the judgment enforcement decision, and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular on the receipt or non-receipt of the remains or ashes when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of the remains or ashes will affect the security, order, and environmental hygiene.

  6. The preservation and transportation of the corpse, remains, or ashes of the deported person shall be the responsibility of the applicant, the diplomatic representative mission, the consular mission of the country of which the deported person is a citizen, or the international organization representative mission where the deported person works, and must comply with the law on medical treatment; environmental hygiene; prevention and control of infectious diseases and medical quarantine at the border, and other regulations of Vietnamese law.

  Article 13. Permission for exit

  1. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall check the passport or a document valid as a substitute for the passport of the deported person. If the passport does not have a visa or the visa has expired, the immigration management authority shall be requested to issue an exit visa according to regulations. In case of emergency, the visa must be issued at the border gate within 12 (twelve) hours before the deported person arrives at the border gate.

  2. In case the deported person does not have a passport or a document that is equivalent to a passport, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security to request the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which the deported person is a citizen or the international organization representative office where the deported person works to issue necessary documents for the visa application for exit.

  In case the request has been made but the agency or organization has not yet settled or failed to meet the request, but due to urgent reasons, the deportation must be carried out immediately or the deported person has already exceeded the time limit for leaving Vietnam as prescribed by law, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall request the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security to issue a document that is equivalent to a passport and request the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to notify in writing the country of which the deported person is a citizen to settle the procedures for reception and entry. In case the country of which the deported person is a citizen refuses to receive or allow entry, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall take charge and coordinate with the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security and the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority to settle the case through diplomatic channels or carry out deportation according to international practice.

  3. In case the deported person must serve another judgment or has not yet fully served the fine or other civil obligations under the case of temporary exit as prescribed by Vietnamese law, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the deportation judgment enforcement dossier is made shall immediately make a written report to the court that has issued the judgment enforcement decision to issue a decision on extension of the time limit for leaving Vietnam; coordinate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to notify and request the diplomatic mission, consular office, or other authorized agency to perform the consular functions of the country of which the deported person is a citizen with its head office in Vietnam or the international organization representative office where the deported person works, the organization that has guaranteed the deported person's entry into Vietnam to settle the case, or contact the family of the deported person, the agency where the deported person works to provide financial support for the fine and other civil obligations; at the same time, notify the competent civil judgment enforcement agency to carry out judgment enforcement procedures or request the competent court to consider exemption or reduction of judgment enforcement obligations for the amount collected to the state budget as prescribed by the Law on Civil Judgment Enforcement. When there is a decision or document of the civil judgment enforcement agency on the termination of judgment enforcement for the deported person, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall carry out procedures to force the deported person to leave Vietnam.

  Article 14. Enforced exit from Vietnam

  1. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the deportation judgment enforcement dossier is made shall decide on the exit time, but it must not exceed the time limit specified in the court's judgment enforcement decision or the time limit prescribed by law.

  2. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office that has prepared the judgment enforcement dossier shall purchase an exit ticket for the deported person in case the deported person pays for it himself/herself or the diplomatic mission or consular mission of the country of which the deported person is a citizen or the international organization’s representative office where the deported person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed the deported person’s entry into Vietnam shall cover the cost of repatriating the deported person.

  In case the budget must be used to pay for the ticket for the plane, car, train or ship for the deported person to leave Vietnam as prescribed in Article 102 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the Ministry of Public Security shall decide to use the budget and purchase the ticket for the deported person to leave Vietnam.

  3. At least 24 hours before the time of exit, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office that has prepared the judgment enforcement dossier shall notify the deported person, the competent immigration management agency, the Border Gate Department of the Border Guard Command and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the time and place of exit for coordination in deportation. In case the deported person is deported by land to a country that shares a land border with Vietnam, the exit time must not be at night, except for special cases.

  In case the deported person has lawful money or property deposited at the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office or the accommodation facility, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office or the accommodation facility shall return such money or property to the deported person 24 hours before the time of exit. The return of money or property must be recorded in a written record, signed by the sender and receiver and the witness.

  4. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office that has prepared the judgment enforcement dossier shall organize the escort of the deported person to the exit location; coordinate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security, the Border Gate Department of the Border Guard Command to carry out the procedures for forcing the deported person to leave Vietnam. The deportation at the exit location must be recorded in a written record, signed by the deported person, the representatives of the agencies participating in the deportation, the diplomatic mission or consular mission of the country of which the deported person is a citizen or the international organization’s representative office where the deported person works.

  5. In case the deported person does not voluntarily comply with the deportation or refuses to exit Vietnam, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office that has prepared the judgment enforcement dossier shall escort the deported person to the border gate of the country of which the deported person is a citizen; in case the deportation is escorted by air, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office shall notify and agree with the airline on the measures to ensure security and safety during the escort as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 17 of the Government’s Decree No. 81/2010/ND-CP on security of civil aviation and strictly comply with the international treaties on civil aviation security to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  Chapter III

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 15. Effect

  This Circular takes effect from January 1, 2012.

  Article 16. Implementation

  Competent agencies of the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, within the scope of their functions, tasks and powers, organize the implementation and provide guidance and inspection on the implementation of this Circular.

  In case of any problems arising during the implementation of this Circular, concerned agencies, units and localities shall report them to the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for timely guidance.
