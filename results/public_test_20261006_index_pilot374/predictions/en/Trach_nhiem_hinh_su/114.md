                                                  RESOLUTION

     SUPPLEMENTING SOME GUIDELINES OF DECISION NO. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP OF OCTOBER 2, 2007 AND DECISION NO. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP OF OCTOBER 2, 2007 OF THE JUDGMENT COUNCIL OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE’S COURT

                           THE JUDGES' COUNCIL OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of People's Courts;
  In order to apply correctly and uniformly the provisions of the Penal Code and the Criminal Procedure Code;
  After obtaining the unanimous opinion of the Chief Procurator of the Supreme People's Procuracy and the Minister of Justice,

                                                  HEREBY DECIDES:

  Article 1. To supplement Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Judges' Council of the Supreme People's Court on guidance on application of a number of provisions of the Penal Code on prescription for execution of judgments, exemption from serving penalties, and reduction of the duration of serving penalties:

  1. To supplement guidance on the reduction of the duration of serving prison sentences at Point b Sub-Item 3.2 Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Judges' Council of the Supreme People's Court as follows:

  b) For persons sentenced to imprisonment of 30 years or less, each time the duration of serving the prison sentence may be reduced by three months to three years. The reduction of three years must be granted to persons who have made outstanding achievements in labor, reform, and strictly observed the rules and regulations of detention.

  In case a person serving a prison sentence is eligible for reduction of his/her prison sentence and the remaining prison sentence he/she must serve is less than 3 months, the reduction in prison sentence may be equal to the remaining prison sentence, but he/she must serve at least half of the sentence as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 58 of the Criminal Code.

  Example 1: Nguyen Van A was sentenced to 5 years of imprisonment. He has served 4 years and 10 months of imprisonment and is eligible for reduction of his/her prison sentence as guided in Sub-item 3.1, Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP. In this case, A may be reduced by 2 months of his/her remaining prison sentence.

  Example 2: Tran Van T was sentenced to 3 months of imprisonment. He has served 1 month of his sentence and is eligible for reduction of his/her prison sentence as guided in Sub-item 3.1, Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP. In this case, T may be reduced by 1 month and 15 days of his/her remaining prison sentence, because according to Clause 3, Article 58 of the Criminal Code, T must serve at least half of the sentence (i.e. 1 month and 15 days of imprisonment).

  2. To supplement Section 1a of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on guidance on the combination of life imprisonment or death sentence imposed by another legally effective judgment of the defendant as follows:

  In case the defendant is prosecuted for one or several crimes under the jurisdiction of the district-level People’s Court and, upon accepting the case, the district-level People’s Court finds that the defendant has been sentenced to life imprisonment or death sentence in another legally effective judgment, the district-level People’s Court shall report to the provincial People’s Court for the latter to coordinate with the provincial People’s Procuracy to withdraw the case file for prosecution and trial at the provincial level.

  Article 2. To supplement Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on guidance on the implementation of a number of provisions in Part V “Execution of court judgments and decisions” of the Criminal Procedure Code

  1. To supplement Section 10, Part IV of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on decisions of the Council on exemption and reduction as follows:

  10. On the basis of consideration of the file and opinions of the Procuracy representative, the Council on exemption and reduction may:

  a) Accepting the entire request for exemption or reduction of the sentence duration or shortening of the probation period of the suspended sentence;

  b) Accepting a part of the request for exemption or reduction of the sentence duration or shortening of the probation period of the suspended sentence;

  c) Not accepting the request for exemption or reduction of the sentence duration or shortening of the probation period of the suspended sentence;

  d) For the person proposed for sentence reduction, if, after the entire request for sentence reduction is accepted, the remaining sentence duration is not more than 1 month, the Sentence Reduction Council may decide to reduce the remaining sentence entirely.

  2. To supplement Section 13 of Part IV of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on the effectiveness of decisions on exemption or reduction of the sentence duration or shortening of the probation period of the suspended sentence as follows:

  13. The Court’s decision on exemption or reduction of the sentence duration or shortening of the probation period of the suspended sentence may be protested by the Procuracy of the same level or the Procuracy of the superior level according to appellate procedures. The time limit for protest of the Procuracy of the same level is seven days, and that of the Procuracy of the superior level is fifteen days, from the date on which the Court issues the decision. The Court’s decision on exemption or reduction of the sentence duration or shortening of the probation period of the suspended sentence takes effect from the date on which the time limit for protest according to appellate procedures expires. In case the person serving a prison sentence has his/her sentence duration reduced to the same duration as the remaining prison sentence, the Court’s decision on reduction of the prison sentence duration shall take effect immediately, although such decision may be protested by the Procuracy according to appellate procedures.

  3. To supplement Section 3a on guidance on the time of starting to calculate the non-custodial sentence duration in Part III of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court as follows:

  The time of starting to calculate the non-custodial sentence duration is the date on which the agency or organization assigned to supervise and educate the convicted person receives the judgment execution decision and a copy of the judgment. The time of starting to calculate the non-custodial sentence duration must be stated in the judgment decision.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was sentenced to 1 year of non-custodial reform for the crime of stealing property as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 138 of the Criminal Code. In this case, the decision section of the judgment must clearly state: “…to sentence Nguyen Van A to 1 year of non-custodial reform for the crime of stealing property; the non-custodial reform period shall be calculated from the date on which the agency or organization assigned to supervise and educate the convicted person receives the judgment execution decision and a copy of the judgment”.

  Article 3. Effect

  1. This Resolution was passed by the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on September 23, 2010 and takes effect 45 days after the date of its signing.

  2. If any problems arise during the implementation of this Resolution, the Supreme People’s Court should be informed for timely explanation or additional guidance.
