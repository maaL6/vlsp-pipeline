                      AMENDMENTS AND SUPPLEMENTS TO THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE CODE

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam amended by Resolution No. 203/2025/QH15;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on amendments to the Criminal Procedure Code No. 101/2015/QH13, which has been amended by Law No. 02/2021/QH15, Law No. 34/2024/QH15 and Law No. 59/2024/QH15.

  2. To amend and supplement Article 20 as follows:

  Article 20. Responsibilities for exercising the right to prosecute and supervising the observance of law in criminal proceedings The Procuracy shall exercise the right to prosecute and supervise the observance of law in criminal proceedings, decide on the charge, detect violations of law in order to ensure that all criminal acts, offenders, criminal legal persons and violations of law are detected and handled promptly and strictly, the initiation of prosecution, investigation, prosecution, trial and enforcement of judgments shall be carried out in accordance with the law, the right person, the right crime, the right law, the offenders and criminal legal persons shall not be let go, and the innocent shall not be wrongfully accused.

  The Chief Procurator of the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall define the competence of the People’s Procuracies at each level in the exercise of the right to prosecution and supervision of the observance of law in criminal proceedings according to this Code.”.

  31. To amend and supplement Article 268 as follows:

  Article 268. Jurisdiction of courts

  1. Regional People’s Courts shall have jurisdiction to try first-instance criminal cases of less serious crimes, serious crimes, very serious crimes and extremely serious crimes, for which the maximum penalty prescribed by the Penal Code is 20 years of imprisonment.

  2. Provincial People’s Courts shall have jurisdiction to try first-instance:

  a) Criminal cases of crimes not under the jurisdiction of regional People’s Courts;

  b) Criminal cases under the jurisdiction of regional People’s Courts but involving many complicated circumstances, involving many levels and sectors, and attracting special attention of public opinion; cases which, if handled, will affect politics and foreign affairs; cases in which the offenders are key leaders at provincial level or higher, persons holding prominent positions in religion or having high prestige among ethnic minorities.

  The Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court shall assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with the Chief Procurator of the Supreme People’s Procuracy in elaborating this point.

  3. Regional military courts shall have jurisdiction to try first-instance criminal cases of less serious crimes, serious crimes and very serious crimes, except for crimes and cases:

  a) Crimes of violation of national security;

  b) Crimes of sabotaging peace, anti-humanity and war crimes;

  c) Crimes specified in Articles 123, 125, 126, 227, 277, 278, 279, 280, 282, 283, 284, 286, 287, 288, 337, 368, 369, 370, 371, 399 and 400 of the Penal Code;

  d) Crimes committed outside the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam;

  dd) Criminal cases involving defendants, victims, involved parties in foreign countries or property related to the case in foreign countries.

  4. The military court of the military zone shall try the following cases at first instance:

  a) Criminal cases involving crimes not under the jurisdiction of the military court of the region;

  b) Criminal cases under the jurisdiction of the military court of the region but having many complicated circumstances which are difficult to assess, have inconsistent nature of the case or are related to many levels and many sectors; cases involving defendants who are judges, prosecutors, investigators, key leaders at provincial level, regiment commanders and higher, persons with religious status or high prestige among ethnic minorities.”.
