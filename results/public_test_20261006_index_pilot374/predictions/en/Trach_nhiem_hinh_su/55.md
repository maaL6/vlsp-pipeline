                                                  RESOLUTION

     GUIDELINES FOR APPLICATION OF SOME PROVISIONS OF THE PENAL CODE IN TRIAL OF CORRUPTION CRIMES AND OTHER OFFICE-RELATED CRIMES

                            THE JUDGES’ COUNCIL OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE’S COURT

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of People’s Courts dated November 24, 2014;

  To apply correctly and uniformly some provisions of the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13, amended and supplemented by Law No. 12/2017/QH14, in trial of corruption crimes and other office-related crimes;

  After consulting the Chief Procurator of the Supreme People’s Procuracy and the Minister of Justice.

                                                 HEREBY DECIDES:

  Article 1. Scope of application

  This Resolution provides guidelines for application of some provisions of the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13, amended and supplemented by Law No. 12/2017/QH14 (hereinafter referred to as the Criminal Code) on corruption crimes and other office-related crimes; determines the value of the appropriated property and damage caused by corruption crimes and other office-related crimes; and handles items and money directly related to the crimes.

  Article 2. On some terms

  1. “Authorities and organizations” specified in Clause 1 Article 352 of the Criminal Code includes authorities, organizations, state units and enterprises, non-state organizations.

  2. “State authorities, organizations and units” are authorities, organizations and units specified in Clause 9 Article 3 of the Anti-Corruption Law, including: state authorities, political organizations, socio-political organizations, people’s armed units, public service units, state-owned enterprises and other organizations and units established by the State, invested in with material facilities, allocated with all or part of operating funds, directly managed by the State or participated in management to serve the general and essential development needs of the State and society.

  3. “Non-state enterprises and organizations” specified in Articles 353, 354, 364 and 365 of the Criminal Code are enterprises and organizations not falling under the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. “Person with position” specified in Clause 2 Article 352 of the Criminal Code is a person specified in Clause 2 Article 3 of the Anti-Corruption Law.

  5. “By another form” specified in Clause 2 Article 352 of the Criminal Code means a case where the person is not appointed, elected, recruited or contracted but is assigned to perform tasks and has powers while performing those tasks.

  Example: A person appointed by a competent state authority to directly participate in the Covid-19 disease prevention and control checkpoint.

  6. “Proactively declare before being discovered” specified in Clause 7 Article 364 and Clause 6 Article 365 of the Criminal Code means a case where the act of committing bribery or bribery brokerage has not yet been detected by a competent authority or denounced by anyone but the briber or bribe broker has voluntarily declared the entire bribery or bribery brokerage act that he/she has committed.

  7. “Taking initiative to return at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property” means that the offender has taken initiative to return at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property after committing the crime. Taking initiative to return at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property also means that the offender has influenced their parents, spouse, children, siblings, or other relatives to return at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property, or not objecting to such return.

  For cases where the same person is prosecuted for multiple crimes, including embezzlement, bribery, and other crimes, but has taken initiative to return at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property, such return shall also be considered as taking initiative to return at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property, unless there is clear evidence that the property originates from another crime.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled VND 4,000,000,000 and abused his position to appropriate VND 2,000,000,000. After A was prosecuted, his wife transferred her real estate, which was her private property, to return VND 3,000,000,000 on his behalf. This act shall be considered as taking initiative to return at least three-quarters of the embezzled property.

  8. “Actively cooperating with competent authorities in detecting, investigating, and handling crimes” means that after committing corruption or other position-related crimes, the offender has taken initiative to provide information, documents, and evidence that are significant for detecting, investigating, and handling crimes related to the crimes they are charged with (e.g., accurately indicating the hiding place of important evidence to help competent authorities recover it; reporting and accurately indicating the hiding place of accomplices; reporting crimes and new offenders related to the crimes they are charged with...). Apart from the above cases, other cases may be considered as “actively cooperating with competent authorities in detecting, investigating, and handling crimes” but must be clearly stated in the court’s judgment.

  9. “Making great contributions” means that the offender has helped procedural authorities detect, apprehend, investigate, and handle crimes unrelated to the crimes they are charged with; saved others in life-threatening situations or saved state-owned, collective, or individual property worth VND 100,000,000 or more from natural disasters, epidemics, fires, or other force majeure events; or made inventions, patents, or innovations of great value confirmed by competent state authorities. Apart from the above cases, other cases may be considered as “making great contributions” but must be clearly stated in the court’s judgment.

  Article 3. Certain circumstances as signs of a crime

  1. “Having been disciplined for this act but still committing the violation” specified in Point a Clause 1 Articles 353, 354, 355 and 358 of the Criminal Code means that the offender has been disciplined for a similar act before but the time limit for being considered not yet disciplined has not expired as per the law.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was disciplined by a warning for embezzling VND 500,000 but 06 months later, A embezzled VND 1,500,000.

  In case the offender has been disciplined by a competent agency or organization and then the procedural authority has prosecuted him/her for the same act, the previous disciplinary action shall not be considered as “having been disciplined for this act but still committing the violation”.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was disciplined by a competent authority for embezzlement of property, but later A was considered for prosecution for the same act, the circumstance “having been disciplined for this act but still committing the violation” shall not be applied to A.

  2. “Having been convicted of one of the crimes specified in Section 1 of this Chapter but not having his/her criminal record erased and still committing the violation” specified in Clause 1 Articles 353, 354 and 355 of the Criminal Code means that the offender has been convicted once for one or more crimes specified in Section 1 of Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, but his/her criminal record has not been erased and he/she commits one of the acts listed in Clause 1 of one of Articles 353, 354 and 355 of the Criminal Code.

  In case the offender has other prior convictions, these prior convictions shall be used to determine recidivism and dangerous recidivism, except for cases where all prior convictions have been used to determine the criminal elements in previous convictions.

  Example: Before committing the act of receiving a bribe of VND 1,500,000, A had two prior convictions, in which the first one, A was convicted of embezzlement of property with the amount of VND 5,000,000; the second one, A was convicted of abuse of position and power to appropriate property with the amount of VND 1,000,000. In this case, the second prior conviction shall be used to determine the criminal elements of the crime of embezzlement of property. As for the first prior conviction, since it has been used to determine the criminal elements of abuse of position and power to appropriate property (with the amount of VND 1,000,000), it shall not be used to determine recidivism.

  3. “Other material benefits” specified in Articles 354, 358, 364 and 366 of the Criminal Code means material benefits that are not property specified in Article 105 of the Civil Code.

  For example: Bribery by providing funding for overseas study, travel, etc.

  4. “Non-material benefits” specified in Point b Clause 1 Articles 354, 358, 364, 365 and 366 of the Criminal Code are benefits that are not material benefits.

  For example: Bribery by awarding or proposing to award titles or awards; electing, appointing or appointing to positions; raising exam scores; promising to allow graduation, study, competition or performance abroad; sexual bribery, etc.

  5. “Abuse of position and power” specified in Clause 1 Article 355 of the Criminal Code means using beyond the assigned powers, responsibilities or tasks or performing tasks and powers that are not assigned or assigned in that field.

  For example: Nguyen Van A is the Deputy Chairman of the People’s Committee of the province. A is only assigned to handle the cultural and social sector, not assigned to handle the land management sector but A still issues a decision to expropriate land of Company X to assign to Company Y (which is A’s family company). In this case, A’s act has exceeded the assigned responsibilities and tasks.

  6. “Abuse of position and power” specified in Clause 1 Article 356 of the Criminal Code means relying on the assigned position and power to act contrary to, not act or act incorrectly according to the law.

  For example: Nguyen Van A is the Director of the Natural Resources and Environment Department of B district who has committed acts of misconduct, submitted to the leaders of the People’s Committee of B district to sign the decision to grant certificates of land use rights to land parcels that do not meet the conditions for granting land use rights certificates according to the land law.

  7. “Personal gain” specified in Clause 1 Articles 356, 357 and 359 of the Criminal Code means that the offender has abused his/her position and power or exceeded his/her power to achieve material or non-material benefits for himself/herself or for other agencies, organizations or individuals.

  8. “Other personal engines” specified in Clause 1 of Articles 356, 357 and 359 of the Criminal Code means cases where the offender has abused his/her position, powers or exceeded his/her powers to assert, consolidate or enhance his/her status, prestige and power in an improper manner.

  9. Damage caused by the criminal acts specified in Articles 353, 354, 355 and 358 of the Criminal Code means actual damage that is causally related to the criminal acts and does not include money, property or other material benefits that the offender has appropriated or will appropriate.

  Example: Nguyen Van A, the warehouse manager of Company B, embezzles 200,000,000 VND of medicine for epidemic prevention. Due to the lack of medicine, the consequence is that all the poultry worth 10,000,000,000 VND of Company B dies. In this case, the amount of money A appropriated is 200,000,000 VND and the actual damage caused by A’s criminal act is 10,000,000,000 VND.

  10. “Work secrets” specified in Article 361 and Article 362 of the Criminal Code means work information expressed in any form (e.g. documents, draft documents, speeches, images…) which the agencies and organizations specified are not allowed to disclose to others and which information does not fall into the list of state secrets.

  Article 4. On some circumstances for setting the penalty framework

  1. The circumstance of “using cunning tricks” or “using dangerous tricks” specified in Clause 2 of Articles 353, 354, 355, 364 and 365 of the Criminal Code:

  a) “Using cunning tricks” means cases where the offender uses sophisticated deceptive tricks or uses high technology to commit the criminal act, conceal the crime, shift the blame to others or the offender commits acts of destroying evidence, making it difficult to detect, investigate and handle the crime.

  b) “Using dangerous tricks” means cases where the offender uses tricks that may endanger the lives or health of others or may cause other serious consequences to appropriate property or conceal the crime.

  2. The circumstance of “committing the crime 02 times or more” specified in Clause 2 of Articles 353, 354, 355, 356, 357, 358, 361, 364, 365 and 366 of the Criminal Code means that the offender has committed one of the acts specified in these Articles 02 times or more and each act constitutes a crime, but has not yet been examined for penal liability and the statute of limitations for examination of penal liability has not expired.

  Example: On August 15, 2018, Nguyen Van A embezzled VND 10,000,000. On September 25, 2019, A embezzled VND 20,000,000. A’s criminal acts have not yet been handled and the statute of limitations for examination of penal liability has not expired. In this case, A is examined for penal liability for embezzlement of property and the circumstance of “committing the crime 02 times or more” is applied according to Point c Clause 2 Article 353 of the Criminal Code.

  3. The circumstance of “negatively affecting the lives of cadres, public employees, civil servants and workers in agencies and organizations” specified in Point g Clause 2 Article 353 of the Criminal Code means one of the following cases:

  a) Causing loss or reduction of regular income, lawful additional income of cadres, public employees, civil servants and workers;

  b) Causing complaints, denunciations, loss of solidarity and trust within agencies and organizations, affecting the morale of cadres, public employees, civil servants and workers.

  4. The circumstance of “causing negative impact on security, order and social safety” specified in Clause 3 of Articles 353, 355 of the Criminal Code means one of the following cases:

  a) Causing mass complaints, protests, disturbances for enemy forces to take advantage of, drag and incite against the government, distort the Party’s guidelines, policies and laws of the State;

  b) Causing panic, fear or anger among the people;

  c) Creating difficulties in the implementation of the Party’s guidelines and the State’s policies and laws.

  For example: Nguyen Van A embezzles money for support for the poor in economic development, causing difficulties for the People’s Committee of commune B in implementing the poverty alleviation policy in the locality.

  Article 5. Principles of handling corruption crimes and other position-related crimes

  1. The handling of corruption crimes and other position-related crimes must be strict and fully comply with the principles of the criminal law and the criminal procedure law.

  2. During the proceedings, the offender who embezzles property or receives bribes and has proactively returned at least three-quarters of the embezzled or received bribes and has actively cooperated with the competent authorities in detecting, investigating and handling the crime or making great contributions shall not be subject to the highest level of the penalty scale prescribed for the prosecuted or tried offender.

  3. Consider applying the policy of special leniency to exempt from penalties as prescribed in Article 59 of the Criminal Code for cases where the offender has at least 02 mitigating circumstances prescribed in Clause 1 Article 51 of the Criminal Code or the offender is a first-time accomplice in the case but plays a minor role if falling into one of the following cases:

  a) The offender acts not for personal gain or other personal motives but only for the purpose of innovation and breaking through for the cause of socio-economic development and national defense;

  b) The offender is a dependent (such as a subordinate to a superior, a salaried employee, or a person performing tasks under the direction of a superior), is not fully aware of their criminal act, acts not for personal gain or other personal motives, and does not benefit from it; has proactively reported before being discovered, actively cooperated with the competent authority, and effectively contributed to the investigation and handling of the crime;

  c) The offender has proactively declared before being discovered, actively cooperated with competent authorities, contributed to limiting damage, proactively returned all appropriated property, fully remedied all consequences and fully compensated for all damage caused by him/her;

  d) The offender, after being discovered, has honestly declared, repented and actively returned all appropriated property, fully remedied all consequences and fully compensated for all damage caused by him/her.

  Article 6. Consideration of criminal liability and decision on penalty in case of separation of cases

  In case a case is separated for settlement in different stages (separated into multiple cases), the consideration of criminal liability and decision on penalty shall be carried out as follows:

  1. The offender’s previous conviction in a previous case shall not be considered as a basis for determining “committing the crime 02 times or more”, except for cases where the criminal acts considered and handled in different stages are independent of each other;

  2. The offender’s previous conviction in a previous case shall not be considered as having a bad personal record to prevent him/her from serving a suspended sentence, if he/she meets other conditions for serving a suspended sentence;

  3. The decision on penalty in each judgment or decision must ensure that the total penalty of the judgments does not exceed the highest level of the penalty bracket corresponding to the total value of appropriated property, of the bribe or damage caused by the crime.

  Example: Nguyen Van A committed abuse of power in the performance of his/her official duties. Since only the damage of property worth VND 100,000,000 was proven at the initial stage, the procedural authority decided to separate the case into 02 cases, handling A first for the act of causing damage worth VND 100,000,000. However, at the second stage, the procedural authority proved that A’s act also caused damage to property worth VND 350,000,000. At the first stage, the Court applied Clause 2 Article 357 of the Criminal Code to sentence A to 05 years of imprisonment for the crime of abuse of power in the performance of his/her official duties. When handling the case at the second stage, A was prosecuted again under Clause 2 Article 357 of the Criminal Code. To ensure that the total penalty of both judgments does not exceed the highest level of this clause, the Court could only decide to sentence A to no more than 05 years of imprisonment.

  Article 7. Criminal prosecution in case of using property obtained through corruption to commit one of the money laundering acts

  In case of using property obtained through corruption to commit one of the money laundering acts specified in Article 324 of the Criminal Code, in addition to being criminally prosecuted for corresponding crimes specified in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, the offender shall also be criminally prosecuted for money laundering as prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzles VND 5,000,000, then uses this money to invest in real estate to conceal the illegal origin of the embezzled money. In this case, A shall be criminally prosecuted for embezzlement of property as prescribed in Article 353 and money laundering as prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code.

  Article 8. Criminal prosecution in case of committing the same act repeatedly as prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code

  1. In case a person commits the same act repeatedly as prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, but each time the value of the appropriated property, bribe, or property damage is below the minimum level prescribed for criminal prosecution and does not fall into one of the other cases for criminal prosecution, and at the same time, in these acts, there has been no disciplinary action taken and the disciplinary action statute of limitations has not expired, if the total value of the appropriated property, bribe, or damage is equal to or higher than the minimum level prescribed for criminal prosecution as prescribed in the Criminal Code, the person who repeatedly commits the same act shall be criminally prosecuted for the corresponding crime based on the total value of the appropriated property, bribe, or damage.

  Example: On August 15, 2019, Nguyen Van A received VND 1,500,000 as a bribe; on September 15, 2019, A continued to receive VND 1,500,000 as a bribe; on December 30, 2019, A again received VND 1,500,000 as a bribe, which was discovered by a competent authority. These acts of A have not been disciplined and the disciplinary action statute of limitations has not expired. In this case, the total amount of bribe received by A is determined to be VND 4,500,000, so A shall be criminally prosecuted as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 354 of the Criminal Code.

  2. In case a person commits the same act repeatedly as prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, each time satisfying the elements of a crime and the total value of the appropriated property, bribe or damaged property of the violations falls within the enhanced penalty bracket, if the violations have not yet been subject to criminal prosecution and the time limit for criminal prosecution has not expired, in addition to being subject to the penalty bracket corresponding to the total value of the appropriated property, bribe or damaged property, they shall also be subject to the aggravating circumstances or circumstances increasing criminal liability as follows:

  a) If the total value of the appropriated property, bribe or damaged property falls under Clause 2 of the corresponding article, the offender shall be additionally subject to the aggravating circumstance of “committing the crime 02 times or more”.

  Example: On August 15, 2019, Nguyen Van A received a bribe of VND 50,000,000; on June 30, 2020, A continued to receive a bribe of VND 100,000,000 which was detected by a competent authority. In this case, A is subject to 02 aggravating circumstances for sentencing: “bribe is money, property or other material benefits worth from VND 100,000,000 to under VND 500,000,000” and “committing the crime 02 times or more” as prescribed in Point c and Point dd Clause 2 Article 354 of the Criminal Code.

  b) If the total value of the appropriated property or received bribe or damaged property falls within another enhanced penalty bracket, the offender shall be additionally subject to the aggravating circumstance of “committing the crime 02 times or more”.

  Example: On March 11, 2019, Nguyen Van A received a bribe of VND 400,000,000; on July 30, 2020, A continued to receive a bribe of VND 200,000,000 which was detected by a competent authority. In this case, A is subject to the aggravating circumstance of “bribe is money, property or other material benefits worth from VND 500,000,000 to under VND 1,000,000,000” as prescribed in Point a Clause 3 Article 354 and the aggravating circumstance of “committing the crime 02 times or more” as prescribed in Point g Clause 1 Article 52 of the Criminal Code.

  Article 9. Criminal prosecution in cases where the offender appropriates property, receives bribes and causes property damage

  In case the offender both embezzles property and receives bribes, and also causes property damage, the value of the embezzled property, the bribes, and the value of the property damage shall fall under the same penalty bracket, the offender shall be examined for penal liability according to the corresponding points of that penalty bracket. In case the value of the embezzled property, the bribes, and the value of the property damage fall under different penalty brackets, the offender shall be examined for penal liability according to the higher penalty bracket.

  Article 10. Determination of the value of the embezzled property and damage caused by corruption crimes or other crimes against positions

  1. The value of the embezzled property used as a basis for criminal handling shall be determined at the time of committing the crime. In case the crime is committed continuously or for a long period, the value of the embezzled property shall be determined at the time of ending the crime. In case the crime is in progress but is detected and stopped, the value of the embezzled property shall be determined at the time of detecting and stopping the crime.

  2. Damage to property used as a basis for criminal handling shall be determined according to the following cases:

  a) In case the damage occurs at the time of committing the crime, the damage shall be determined at this time; in case the crime is committed continuously or for a long period, the damage shall be determined at the time of detecting and stopping the crime or at the time of ending the crime.

  b) In case the damage occurs or continues after the crime is committed or after the crime is ended, the damage shall be determined at the time of stopping the damage.

  c) In case the damage cannot be determined according to the guidance in Point a and Point b, Clause 2 of this Article, the damage shall be determined at the time of initiating the case.

  3. The consideration of civil liability shall comply with the provisions of civil law.

  Article 11. Handling of objects and money directly related to crimes

  1. After accepting a case, the court shall consider applying measures of confiscation of property and freezing of accounts directly related to the commission of the crime if such property has not been subject to enforcement measures during the investigation and prosecution stages.

  2. The court shall consider and decide on confiscation for state budget, confiscation for destruction or forced return and compensation to the owner or lawful manager in accordance with law of property directly related to crimes of corruption and other crimes against positions, including:

  a) Tools and means used for committing the crime;

  b) Money and property appropriated;

  c) Bribery;

  d) Damage caused by the crime;

  dd) Unlawful gains from the crime;

  e) Profits, interest, and dividends arising from property obtained through commission of crimes;

  g) Other property as prescribed by law.

  3. Property belonging to cases of confiscation for state budget which is no longer available at the time of settlement of the case shall be confiscated by the Court at the value of property according to the appraisal conclusion of the competent authority.

  4. In case property originating from corruption crimes or other crimes related to positions has been incorporated into the common property block, the Court shall only confiscate or force the return to the owner or lawful manager for the portion of property originating from the crime. Profits earned from this common property block shall also be divided according to the ratio to confiscate or return to the owner or lawful manager in accordance with the law.

  Article 12. Effect

  1. This Resolution was passed by the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on December 30, 2020 and takes effect from February 15, 2021.

  2. In case the offender has been tried before the effective date of this Resolution in accordance with previous regulations and guidance and the judgment has taken legal effect, this Resolution shall not be used as a basis for protest according to cassation or reopening procedures.
