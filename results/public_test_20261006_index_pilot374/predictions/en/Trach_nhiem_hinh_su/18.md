                Law No. 101/2025/QH15                                      Hanoi, November 26, 2025


                                                      THE LAW

                             TRANSFER OF CONVICTED PERSONS TO PRISON

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam amended by Resolution No. 203/2025/QH15;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on Transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  This Law provides for the principles, competence, conditions, order and procedures for the transfer of persons serving prison sentences between Vietnam and foreign countries; and responsibilities of Vietnamese state agencies in the transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Law applies to Vietnamese agencies, organizations and individuals, and foreign agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the transfer of persons serving prison sentences between Vietnam and foreign countries.

  Article 3. Interpretation of terms

  In this Law, the following terms are construed as follows:

  1. Transfer of persons serving prison sentences means the transfer of persons serving prison sentences between Vietnam and foreign countries, who wish to be repatriated and accept the transfer to continue serving their prison sentences, based on the provisions of domestic law, international treaties or the principle of reciprocity.

  2. A person serving a prison sentence is a person sentenced to a fixed-term imprisonment or life imprisonment and is serving the sentence at a prison.

  3. Transferring country means a country that can transfer or has transferred a person serving a prison sentence in its country to the receiving country.

  4. Receiving country means a country that can receive or has received a person serving a prison sentence for the purpose of continuing the execution of the prison sentence.

  5. Conversion of prison sentence means the decision of the competent People’s Court of Vietnam to convert a prison sentence in a judgment or decision of a competent foreign court against a person serving a prison sentence who is received in Vietnam in accordance with the Criminal Code and other provisions of Vietnamese law.

  Article 4. Application of law and principles of transfer of a person serving a prison sentence

  1. The transfer of a person serving a prison sentence shall comply with this Law; where this Law does not provide for such a transfer, the provisions of the law on criminal procedure, the law on execution of criminal judgments and other relevant provisions of Vietnamese law shall apply.

  2. The transfer of a person serving a prison sentence shall be carried out on the basis of the following principles:

  a) Respect for independence, sovereignty, territorial integrity, non-interference in each other’s internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit; in accordance with the Constitution and laws of Vietnam and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory;

  b) Where Vietnam and the foreign country are not signatories to the same international treaty on transfer of a person serving a prison sentence, the transfer of a person serving a prison sentence shall be carried out on the basis of the principle of reciprocity but not contrary to Vietnamese law, in accordance with international law and international practice;

  c) To respect and protect the lawful rights and interests of persons serving prison sentences.

  Article 5. Central authorities of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam on transfer of persons serving prison sentences

  1. The Ministry of Public Security is the central authority of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam on transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  2. The central authority of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam on transfer of persons serving prison sentences shall be responsible for acting as a focal point for exchanging information and documents related to the transfer of persons serving prison sentences; making, sending and receiving requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences; presiding over and coordinating with ministries, sectors and local authorities in considering and settling requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences; monitoring and urging the transfer of persons serving prison sentences; performing other duties and powers in accordance with the law on transfer of persons serving prison sentences and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  Article 6. Application of the principle of reciprocity in the transfer of persons serving prison sentences

  1. The application of the principle of reciprocity in the transfer of persons serving prison sentences shall be implemented when the following conditions are fully met:

  a) Compliance with the principle specified in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  b) The competent authority of the foreign country has made a written commitment to fulfill Vietnam’s requests in similar cases. In case Vietnam is the requesting country, the Ministry of Public Security shall make a written commitment to apply the principle of reciprocity;

  c) In accordance with the practice and cooperation needs on the transfer of persons serving prison sentences between Vietnam and foreign countries.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall, based on the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article, decide on the application of the principle of reciprocity in the transfer of persons serving prison sentences. Where necessary, the Ministry of Public Security shall seek the opinions of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and relevant agencies before making a decision.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 7. Time for withdrawal of the request for transfer

  1. A person serving a prison sentence or his/her lawful representative shall only have the right to withdraw the request for transfer before the decision to receive the person serving a prison sentence or the decision to transfer the person serving a prison sentence of the competent People’s Court of Vietnam takes effect.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 8. Language in the transfer of persons serving prison sentences

  1. Where Vietnam and a foreign country are both members of an international treaty on the transfer of persons serving prison sentences, the language in the transfer of persons serving prison sentences shall be the language specified in that international treaty.

  2. Where Vietnam and the foreign country are not both members of an international treaty on extradition of persons serving imprisonment sentences, the application and documents requesting the extradition of persons serving imprisonment sentences must be enclosed with translations into the language of the receiving country or another language accepted by the receiving country.

  3. The agency making the application for the extradition of persons serving imprisonment sentences must translate the application and documents into the language specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 9. Exemption from consular legalization The application for the extradition of persons serving imprisonment sentences is exempted from consular legalization if it is signed and sealed by the competent person or agency of the foreign country, except otherwise provided for by the international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a member.

  Article 10. Costs of extradition of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. Where Vietnam is the extraditing country, Vietnam shall pay the costs incurred in the extradition of persons serving imprisonment sentences up to the time of handover of the person serving imprisonment sentence, unless otherwise agreed.

  2. Where Vietnam is the receiving country, Vietnam shall pay the costs incurred in the extradition of persons serving imprisonment sentences from the time of receiving the person serving imprisonment sentence, unless otherwise agreed.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 11. Funding for the transfer of persons serving prison sentences

  1. Funding for the transfer of persons serving prison sentences shall be provided by the state budget in accordance with the law on the state budget and other relevant laws.

  The persons serving prison sentences, their lawful representatives, and other agencies, organizations, and individuals may voluntarily contribute to or support the full or partial cost of their daily living, travel, and other expenses of the transferred persons serving prison sentences in accordance with the law.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 12. Transit of persons serving prison sentences

  1. The transit of persons serving prison sentences through the territory of Vietnam must be notified in writing to the Ministry of Public Security for handling. The consent or refusal to allow the transit of persons serving prison sentences shall comply with the law.

  2. In case of permission for transit, the country requesting the transit of the person serving prison sentence shall be responsible for managing such person during the transit period in Vietnam and bear all transit expenses in accordance with Vietnamese law, unless otherwise agreed.

  3. In case of air transport and emergency landing on the territory of Vietnam, the person serving prison sentence may be managed in accordance with Vietnamese law. The management of the person serving prison sentence and related expenses shall comply with Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. In case Vietnam requests foreign countries to permit transit of persons serving prison sentences, the Ministry of Public Security shall contact foreign countries to carry out procedures for permission for transit.

  5. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 13. Responsibilities of state agencies for transfer of persons serving prison sentences

  1. The Government shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) To unify the state management of transfer of persons serving prison sentences;

  b) To report to the National Assembly on the transfer of persons serving prison sentences in the annual report on judgment enforcement.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall be responsible to the Government for the state management of transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  The Minister of Public Security shall assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court and the Chief Procurator of the Supreme People’s Procuracy in regulating the application of science and technology and digital transformation in the transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  3. The Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall cooperate with the Ministry of Public Security in exercising the State management over the transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  People's courts at all levels and People's Procuracies at all levels shall perform tasks on transfer of persons serving prison sentences in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  4. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies and other relevant agencies shall, within the scope of their functions and tasks, perform tasks on transfer of persons serving prison sentences as prescribed by this Law and other relevant laws; cooperate with the Ministry of Public Security in exercising state management over transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  Chapter II

              RECEIVING FOREIGN CONVICTED PERSONS FOR VIETNAM

  Article 14. Competence to decide on the reception of persons serving prison sentences abroad in Vietnam

  The People's Court of the region where the last permanent residence of a person serving a prison sentence abroad in Vietnam is located shall request the competent authority to decide on the reception of such person in Vietnam. If the last permanent residence of the person is not identified in Vietnam or the person is not permanently residing in Vietnam, the People's Court of the region 1 - Hanoi shall decide on the reception of such person in Vietnam.

  Article 15. Conditions for receiving persons serving prison sentences abroad in Vietnam

  1. A person serving a prison sentence in another country may be received in Vietnam to continue serving the prison sentence if the following conditions are met:

  a) He/she is a Vietnamese citizen;

  b) The act of committing the crime for which he/she was convicted in the transferring country also constitutes a crime under the Vietnamese criminal law at the time of filing the request for transfer of the person serving the prison sentence;

  c) At the time of receiving the request for transfer, the period of remaining prison sentence must be at least 01 year; in special cases, this period may be less than 01 year;

  d) The judgment or decision against him/her has taken legal effect and there are no procedural procedures awaiting handling;

  dd) There is the consent of Vietnam in case the competent authority of the transferring country files the request for transfer or the consent of the transferring country in case the Ministry of Public Security files the request for transfer;

  e) There is the consent of the person serving the prison sentence; in case the person serving the prison sentence is a minor or unable to express consent to the transfer due to age, health condition and mental state, the consent of his/her legal representative is required.

  2. The Government shall elaborate on the special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 16. Cases of refusal to receive persons serving prison sentences abroad in Vietnam

  1. The competent People’s Court shall decide to refuse to receive persons serving prison sentences abroad in Vietnam if they fall into one of the following cases:

  a) Not conforming to the principles specified in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  b) Failing to satisfy one of the conditions specified in Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law;

  c) May prejudice sovereignty, national defense, security, social order and safety, public health and interests of the State, lawful rights and interests of agencies, organizations and individuals in Vietnam;

  d) Exceeding the capacity to meet the necessary requirements for the continued enforcement of judgments in Vietnam of the persons serving prison sentences.

  2. The Government shall elaborate Point d Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 17. Application for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad in Vietnam

  1. An application for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence abroad to Vietnam shall include the following documents:

  a) A written request for transfer made by the Ministry of Public Security or a competent authority of the transferring country specified in Clause 1 Article 18 of this Law;

  b) Documents specified in Clause 2 Article 18 of this Law.

  2. An application for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence abroad to Vietnam shall be made in 03 sets, unless otherwise agreed. The language used to make the application shall comply with Article 8 of this Law.

  Article 18. Written request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence abroad to Vietnam and enclosed documents

  1. A written request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence abroad to Vietnam must include the following details:

  a) Date, month, year and place of making the request;

  b) Grounds for requesting the transfer of the person serving a prison sentence;

  c) Names and addresses of competent agencies of Vietnam and the transferring country;

  d) Information on identification, nationality, last permanent residence in Vietnam (if any) and the place where the person serving the prison sentence is serving the sentence in the transferring country, legal grounds for the person serving the prison sentence to be eligible for transfer;

  dd) Other necessary information (if any).

  2. The written request for transfer of the person serving the prison sentence shall be enclosed with the following documents:

  a) Application for transfer or documents showing the desire for transfer or consent to the transfer of the person serving the prison sentence or his/her legal representative;

  b) Documents proving the Vietnamese nationality of the person serving the prison sentence who is requested for transfer according to Vietnamese law;

  c) Copies of the judgment or decision of the court of the transferring country regarding the person serving the prison sentence who is requested for transfer;

  d) Documents on the execution of additional penalties (if any).

  d) The law of the transferring country applicable to the determination of the elements constituting the crime and the crime name, the provisions on penalties, the statute of limitations for the execution of penalties for such crime of the judgment;

  e) The written consent of the competent authority of the transferring country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a request for transfer or the written consent to consider the request for transfer of the Ministry of Public Security in case the competent authority of the transferring country makes a request for transfer;

  g) Documents describing the personal characteristics, fingerprints and facial photos of the person serving the prison sentence proposed for transfer;

  h) Written documents and documents certifying the time the person serving the prison sentence proposed for transfer has served the prison sentence, the amnesty, special amnesty, exemption, reduction of sentence in the transferring country and the remaining time to serve the prison sentence;

  i) Documents related to the health status, mental state, medical records of the person serving the prison sentence proposed for transfer and recommendations (if any);

  k) The international treaty on the transfer of persons serving prison sentences between Vietnam and the transferring country (if any);

  l) Other necessary documents (if any).

  3. The Government shall elaborate Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Receipt of requests for transfer from persons serving prison sentences abroad

  1. Vietnamese citizens serving their sentences abroad or their legal representatives may submit applications for transfer to Vietnam to continue serving their sentences at one of the following agencies:

  a) Competent authorities of the transferring country;

  b) The Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country;

  c) Ministry of Public Security.

  2. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer, the Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country shall transfer the request to the Ministry of Public Security. If it falls under the special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country shall transfer the request to the Ministry of Public Security within 5 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer. Based on the request for transfer, the Ministry of Public Security shall consider and prepare a request for transfer as prescribed in Article 20 of this Law.

  The transfer of applications for transfer to Vietnam from Vietnamese representative missions in the transferring countries to the Ministry of Public Security may be carried out electronically in accordance with law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 20. Preparation of request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam

  1. Requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam shall be prepared by the Ministry of Public Security or competent authorities of the transferring country.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer, the Ministry of Public Security shall consider and decide on the preparation of the request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam based on the information and documents provided by the competent authorities of the transferring country, and send it directly or through diplomatic channels to the competent authorities of the transferring country.

  In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country receives a request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam from the Ministry of Public Security, it shall immediately forward it to the competent authorities of the transferring country and notify the Ministry of Public Security in writing.

  3. In case the competent authorities of the transferring country prepare a request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam, it shall be sent directly or through diplomatic channels to the Ministry of Public Security. In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country receives a request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam from the competent authorities of the transferring country, it shall immediately forward it to the Ministry of Public Security for handling in accordance with this Law.

  4. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 21. Receipt, examination and transfer of dossiers on request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam

  1. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences and enclosed documents from the competent authorities of the transferring country or from the date of receipt of the documents specified in Clause 2 Article 18 of this Law from the competent authorities of the transferring country, the Ministry of Public Security shall examine the dossiers in accordance with Articles 17 and 18 of this Law. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for examination of the dossiers shall be 7 days.

  The Ministry of Public Security may request competent agencies of the transferring country to provide additional information and documents. After 60 days from the date of sending the written request for additional information and documents without receiving such information and documents, the Ministry of Public Security shall return the dossier to the competent agency of the transferring country and clearly state the reason. If it falls under the special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for providing additional information and documents is 20 days.

  2. Where the request for transfer complies with the provisions of Article 17 and Article 18 of this Law, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately transfer 01 set of dossiers to the competent regional People’s Court for consideration and decision, and simultaneously transfer 01 set of copies of the dossiers to the competent regional People’s Procuracy.

  The transfer of documents, records and dossiers may be carried out electronically in accordance with law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 22. Processing of dossiers on request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam

  1. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete application for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence abroad to Vietnam, the competent regional People’s Court shall accept the application and notify the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy of the same level in writing. If it falls under the special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for accepting the application shall be 5 days.

  Within the time limit for preparing consideration of requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences, if the request dossiers are incomplete or contain unclear or contradictory points, the competent regional People’s Court shall request the Ministry of Public Security to request the competent authority of the transferring country to provide additional information and documents. The time limit for providing additional information and documents is 20 days from the date of the Ministry of Public Security’s written request.

  2. Within 30 days from the date of acceptance, depending on each case, the competent People’s Court of the region shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision to consider request for transfer of person serving prison sentence when all conditions prescribed in this Law are met. This decision must be sent to the proposed transferee, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any);

  b) Decision to suspend consideration of request for transfer of person serving prison sentence as prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article and return the application to the Ministry of Public Security.

  If it is a special case prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for issuing one of the decisions in this Clause is 10 days.

  The decisions in this Clause must be immediately transferred to the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy of the same level.

  3. Regional People’s Court shall decide to suspend consideration of request for transfer of person serving prison sentence when one of the following cases arises:

  a) It is beyond its jurisdiction;

  b) Competent authority of the transferring country withdraws the request for transfer;

  c) The person serving prison sentence or his/her lawful representative withdraws the application for transfer;

  d) The competent authority of the transferring country shall not add information or documents within the time limit specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  dd) Other reasons why the request for transfer cannot be considered.

  4. Within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision to consider the request for transfer, the competent regional People’s Court shall hold a preliminary hearing to consider the request for transfer. If it falls under the special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for holding the hearing to consider the request for transfer is 10 days.

  Article 23. Preliminary hearing to consider the request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence abroad to Vietnam

  1. The preliminary hearing to consider the request for transfer shall be conducted by 1 judge, with the participation of 1 procurator of the People’s Procuracy of the same level, and shall follow the following order:

  a) The judge shall present the contents of the request for transfer and state his/her opinion on the legal basis for the transfer;

  b) The procurator shall express the People’s Procuracy’s opinion on the transfer;

  c) The lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer shall present his/her opinion (if any).

  2. Based on the results of the first-instance hearing, the judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision to receive the person serving the prison sentence and clearly state the offense and the duration of the prison sentence that the person must continue to serve in Vietnam. In this case, the judge shall consider and decide on the conversion of the prison sentence (if any); the conversion of the prison sentence shall comply with Article 25 of this Law;

  b) Decision to refuse to receive the person serving the prison sentence and clearly state the reason.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of issuance of the decision to receive or refuse to receive the person serving the prison sentence, the competent regional People’s Court shall send the decision to the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the People’s Procuracy of the province, the Ministry of Public Security, the proposed transferee, the lawyer or their lawful representative (if any). If it falls under the special case specified in Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for the competent regional People’s Court to send the decision to receive or refuse to receive is 05 days.

  Article 24. Appellate hearing to consider the decision to receive or refuse to receive

  1. The proposed transferee or their lawful representative has the right to appeal within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision to receive or refuse to receive of the competent regional People’s Court. The People’s Procuracy of the same level has the right to protest within 15 days, the People’s Procuracy of the province has the right to protest within 30 days from the date on which the competent regional People’s Court issues the decision to receive or refuse to receive. If it falls under the special case specified in Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for appeal and the time limit for protest of the People’s Procuracy of the same level is 07 days, the time limit for protest of the People’s Procuracy of the province is 15 days.

  The appeal must be notified in writing by the competent regional People’s Court to the People’s Procuracy of the same level and persons related to the appeal within 05 days from the date on which the appeal time limit expires.

  The People’s Procuracy must send the protest decision to the first-instance court, the proposed transferee or their lawful representative within 02 days from the date on which the protest decision is issued.

  The competent regional People’s Court shall send the dossier and the appeal or protest to the competent provincial People’s Court within 07 days from the expiration of the appeal or protest time limit.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the application and the appeal or protest, the provincial People’s Court shall open a hearing to consider the decision on receipt or refusal of receipt of the first-instance court that is appealed or protested. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for the provincial People’s Court to open a hearing shall be 07 days.

  3. The appellate hearing shall be conducted by 1 judge, with the participation of 1 Procurator of the People’s Procuracy of the same level, and shall follow the following order:

  a) The judge presents a summary of the dossier requesting the transfer of a person serving a prison sentence abroad to Vietnam, the decision to accept or refuse to accept, and the content of the appeal or protest;

  b) The judge asks the lawful representative of the person requested for transfer whether the appeal has been changed, supplemented or withdrawn; if so, the judge requests the procurator to present his/her opinion on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the appeal.

  The judge asks the procurator whether the protest has been changed, supplemented or withdrawn; if so, the judge requests the lawyer or the lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer to present his/her opinion on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the protest;

  c) The procurator, lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer shall give his/her opinion on the contents of the appeal or protest; the procurator shall give his/her opinion on the settlement of the appeal or protest.

  4. Based on the results of the appellate session, the judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Not accepting appeals and protests and upholding the first-instance decisions;

  b) Amending the first-instance decisions;

  c) Annulment of the first-instance decisions and transfer of the dossiers to the competent regional People’s Court for reconsideration;

  d) Annulment of the first-instance decisions and suspension of consideration of the request for transfer as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 22 of this Law;

  dd) Suspension of consideration of the appellate requests.

  5. The decisions on receipt or refusal of receipt shall take legal effect including:

  a) The decisions of the first-instance courts not subject to appeal or protest which take effect from the expiration of the time limit for appeal or protest;

  b) The decisions of the appellate courts which take effect from the date of issuance and are final.

  The decision of the appellate court must be immediately sent to the first-instance court, the first-instance People's Procuracy, the People's Procuracy of the same level, the person requested for transfer, the lawyer or the lawful representative of such person (if any).

  Article 25. Conversion of imprisonment

  1. In case the imprisonment penalty in the judgment or decision of the transferring country's court is not in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Code and other provisions of Vietnamese law, it must be converted accordingly.

  2. The conversion of imprisonment penalty must be based on the circumstances of the case stated in the judgment or decision of the transferring country's court.

  3. The converted imprisonment penalty must not be stricter than the imprisonment penalty that the transferring country's court has imposed in terms of nature and duration. The time already served in the transferring country's prison shall be deducted from the time served in Vietnam's prison.

  4. The person serving the prison sentence who is received in Vietnam shall not be sentenced for the second time for the crime specified in the judgment or decision of the transferring country's court.

  5. The Minister of Public Security shall assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with the Chief Justice of the Supreme People's Court and the Chief Procurator of the Supreme People's Procuracy in elaborating this Article.

  Article 26. Execution of the decision on reception of the person serving the prison sentence

  1. Within 10 days from the effective date of the decision on receiving the person serving a prison sentence issued by the People’s Court, the Chief Justice of the People’s Court of the region that has issued the first-instance decision shall issue a decision on execution of the decision on receiving. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for issuing the decision on execution of the decision on receiving is 5 days. The decision on execution of the decision on receiving takes effect from the date of signing and promulgation.

  2. The decision on execution of the decision on receiving must be sent to the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the transferring country and the transferred person or their lawful representative.

  3. The Ministry of Public Security shall notify in writing the competent authority of the transferring country and the organization executing the receiving. The receiving of the person serving a prison sentence must be carried out within 45 days from the effective date of the decision on execution of the decision on receiving, unless otherwise agreed. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for receiving the person serving a prison sentence is 15 days.

  4. The Government shall elaborate Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 27. Cancellation of the decision on receiving the person serving a prison sentence, the decision on execution of the decision on receiving the person serving a prison sentence

  1. The decision on receiving the person serving a prison sentence that has not taken legal effect shall be canceled in the following cases:

  a) The person serving a prison sentence or their lawful representative withdraws the application for transfer or expresses their unwillingness to transfer in writing;

  b) The transferring country withdraws the request for transfer;

  c) The person serving the prison sentence dies or absconds from the country of transfer;

  d) Other cases where the reception of the person serving the prison sentence cannot be carried out.

  2. The decision on reception of the person serving the prison sentence, which has taken legal effect, shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) The cases specified in Points b, c and d, Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) The decision on execution of the decision on reception of the person serving the prison sentence is annulled.

  3. The decision on execution of the decision on reception of the person serving the prison sentence shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) The cases specified in Point a, Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The person transferred by the competent authority of the country of transfer is not the person specified in the decision on reception;

  c) The competent authority of the transferring country fails to hand over the transferred person at the agreed time and place without a valid reason or fails to carry out the reception within the time limit specified in Clause 3 Article 26 of this Law.

  4. Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Ministry of Public Security, the President of the competent People’s Court shall annul the decision on reception of the person serving a prison sentence specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article; annul the decision on execution of the decision on reception of the person serving a prison sentence specified in Clause 3 of this Article. These decisions take effect from the date of signing and must be immediately sent to the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the transferring country, the person serving the prison sentence or their lawful representative.

  5. The consideration of the request for transfer of the person serving a prison sentence in the cases specified in Point a and Point b Clause 1, Point c Clause 3 of this Article shall only be carried out at least 03 years after the effective date of the decision on annulment of the decision on reception of the person serving a prison sentence, the decision on execution of the decision on reception of the person serving a prison sentence.

  Article 28. Reception of the transferred person

  1. The Ministry of Public Security shall organize the reception of the transferred person at the agreed time and place in writing with the competent authority of the transferring country.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 29. Continued serving of prison sentence in Vietnam

  1. The continued serving of prison sentence in Vietnam of the transferred person shall comply with the provisions of Vietnamese law.

  2. Upon receiving a notice of decision on pardon, general pardon, exemption or reduction of penalty, conditional early release or other judicial decisions of the transferring country regarding a person serving a prison sentence who is received in Vietnam, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately send such notice to the competent authority for consideration and decision in accordance with relevant laws.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 30. Notification of the person serving a prison sentence who is received in Vietnam's compliance with the sentence

  The Ministry of Public Security shall immediately notify in writing the competent authority of the transferring country of the person serving a prison sentence who is received in Vietnam's compliance with the sentence in one of the following cases:

  1. The person serving a prison sentence is temporarily suspended from serving the prison sentence, is exempted or granted a reduction in the prison sentence, granted a general pardon, pardon, conditional early release or is subject to criminal prosecution for another crime;

  2. The person serving a prison sentence has fully served the prison sentence;

  3. The person serving a prison sentence absconds from the detention facility;

  4. The person serving a prison sentence dies before fully serving the prison sentence;

  5. Competent authorities of the transferring country shall request information on the enforcement of judgments of the person received in Vietnam.

  Chapter III

     TRANSFER OF CONVICTED PERSONS SENTENCED TO IMPRISONMENT IN VIETNAM TO FOREIGN COUNTRIES

  Article 31. Competence to decide on transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  The People's Court of the region where the foreigner is serving his/her sentence shall have the competence to decide on the transfer of the foreigner serving his/her sentence in Vietnam to the foreign country.

  Article 32. Conditions for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. A person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam may be extradited to a foreign country for continued serving of the prison sentence if the following conditions are met:

  a) A citizen of the receiving country or a person permitted to reside indefinitely in the receiving country or agreed to be received by the receiving country;

  b) The crime committed by the person who has been convicted in Vietnam also constitutes a crime according to the law of the receiving country at the time of making the request for transfer of the person serving the prison sentence;

  c) At the time of receiving the request for transfer, the convict must have served at least 01 year of imprisonment; in special cases, this period may be less than 01 year;

  d) Fully satisfy the conditions specified in Points d and e Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law;

  (d) Having fully performed the civil responsibilities in the additional sentence of fine, confiscation of property and other legal responsibilities as prescribed by law or having been assisted by the receiving country, other agencies, organizations or individuals in performing these legal responsibilities or having been assured by the receiving country that the person serving the prison sentence will perform these legal responsibilities after being transferred to the receiving country;

  e) The consent of Vietnam in case the competent authority of the receiving country makes a request for transfer or the consent of the receiving country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a request for transfer.

  2. The Government shall elaborate on the special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 33. Cases of refusal to transfer persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  The competent People’s Court of Vietnam shall decide to refuse the transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country if the person falls into one of the following cases:

  1. Not conforming to the principles specified in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  2. Failing to satisfy one of the conditions specified in Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law;

  3. Threatening the sovereignty, national defense, security, social order and safety, public health and interests of the State, legitimate rights and interests of agencies, organizations and individuals in Vietnam;

  4. There are grounds to believe that the transferred person is at risk of torture, retaliation, persecution, abuse, discrimination, cruel and inhuman punishment, humiliation in the receiving country.

  Article 34. Application for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country

  1. An application for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country shall include the following documents:

  a) A written request for transfer from the Ministry of Public Security or a competent authority of the receiving country specified in Clause 1 Article 35 of this Law;

  b) Documents enclosed as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 35 of this Law.

  2. An application for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country shall be made in 03 sets, unless otherwise agreed. The language used to make the application shall comply with Article 8 of this Law.

  Article 35. Written request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country and enclosed documents

  1. A written request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country must contain the following information:

  a) Date, month, year and place of making the request;

  b) Grounds for requesting the transfer of the person serving a prison sentence;

  c) Names and addresses of competent agencies of Vietnam and the receiving country;

  d) Information for identification of the person’s identity, nationality, permanent residence in the receiving country (if any) and the place where he/she is serving a prison sentence in Vietnam, legal grounds for the person’s eligibility for transfer;

  dd) Other necessary information (if any).

  2. The written request for transfer of the person serving the prison sentence shall be enclosed with the following documents:

  a) An application for transfer or a document showing the desire for transfer or consent to the transfer of the person serving the prison sentence or his/her lawful representative;

  b) Documents proving the nationality of the receiving country or the fact that the person is permitted to reside indefinitely in the receiving country (if any);

  c) A copy of the judgment or decision of the People’s Court of Vietnam against the person serving the prison sentence for whom the transfer is requested;

  d) A document on the enforcement of additional penalties (if any);

  dd) The law of the receiving country applicable to the determination of the elements constituting the crime and the offense, and the provisions on penalties and the statute of limitations for the enforcement of penalties for such offense in the judgment;

  e) A document showing the consent of the competent authority of the receiving country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a request for transfer or a document showing the consent to consider the transfer request of the Ministry of Public Security in case the competent authority of the receiving country makes a request for transfer;

  g) Documents specified in Points g, h, i, k and l, Clause 2, Article 18 of this Law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 36. Receipt of requests for transfer from persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam

  1. Foreigners serving prison sentences in Vietnam or their lawful representatives may send requests for transfer to foreign countries for continued serving of prison sentences to one of the following agencies:

  a) The Ministry of Public Security;

  b) Competent agencies of the receiving countries;

  c) Representative offices of the receiving countries in Vietnam.

  2. Upon receipt of a request for transfer or documents showing the desire for transfer from a person serving a prison sentence or his/her lawful representative, the Ministry of Public Security shall receive and consider making a request for transfer in accordance with Article 37 of this Law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 37. Preparation of request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. Requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries shall be prepared by the Ministry of Public Security or competent authorities of the receiving countries.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer, the Ministry of Public Security shall consider and decide on the preparation of the request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries based on the information and documents provided by the competent authorities of the receiving countries and send it directly or through diplomatic channels to the competent authorities of the receiving countries.

  In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Vietnamese representative mission in the receiving country receives a request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries from the Ministry of Public Security, it shall immediately forward it to the competent authorities of the receiving country and notify the Ministry of Public Security in writing.

  3. In case the competent authorities of the receiving countries prepare requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries, they shall send them directly or through diplomatic channels to the Ministry of Public Security. In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Vietnamese representative mission in the receiving country receives a request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries from the competent authorities of the receiving countries, it shall immediately forward it to the Ministry of Public Security for handling in accordance with this Law.

  4. In case a foreigner serving a prison sentence at a detention facility of the Ministry of National Defense is to be transferred to a detention facility of the Ministry of Public Security for serving the sentence before the transfer procedures are carried out, he/she shall be transferred to such facility.

  5. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 38. Receipt, examination and transfer of dossiers on request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer of the person serving the prison sentence and the enclosed documents from the competent authority of the receiving country or from the date of receipt of the documents specified in Clause 2 Article 35 of this Law from the competent authority of the receiving country, the Ministry of Public Security shall inspect the dossier in accordance with Articles 34 and 35 of this Law. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the dossier inspection period is 7 days.

  The Ministry of Public Security may request the competent authority of the receiving country to supplement information and documents. After 60 days from the date of sending the written request for supplementation of information and documents without receiving the supplementary information and documents, the Ministry of Public Security shall return the dossier to the competent authority of the receiving country and clearly state the reason. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the supplementation of information and documents period is 20 days.

  2. In case the dossier of request for transfer complies with the provisions of Articles 34 and 35 of this Law, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately transfer 01 set of dossier to the competent regional People’s Court for consideration and decision, and at the same time, immediately transfer 01 set of copy of the dossier to the competent regional People’s Procuracy.

  The transfer of documents, papers and dossiers may be carried out in electronic environment in accordance with the law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 39. Acceptance of dossiers of request for transfer of the person serving the prison sentence in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the complete dossier of request for transfer of the person serving the prison sentence in Vietnam to foreign countries, the competent regional People’s Court shall accept and notify in writing the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy of the same level. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the dossier acceptance period is 5 days.

  Within the period of preparing for consideration of the request for transfer of the person serving the prison sentence, if the dossier of request for transfer is incomplete in information and documents or has unclear points or contradictions, the competent regional People’s Court shall request the Ministry of Public Security to request the competent authority of the receiving country to supplement information and documents. The supplementation of information and documents period is 20 days from the date of the Ministry of Public Security’s written request.

  2. Within 30 days from the date of acceptance, the competent regional People’s Court shall, depending on the case, issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision to consider the request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence when all conditions prescribed in this Law are met. This decision must be sent to the person requesting the transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any);

  b) Decision to suspend consideration of the request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence as prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article and return the dossier to the Ministry of Public Security.

  If it is a special case prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for issuing one of the decisions in this Clause is 10 days.

  The decisions in this Clause must be immediately transferred to the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy of the same level.

  3. The competent regional People’s Court shall decide to suspend consideration of the request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence when one of the following cases arises:

  a) It is beyond its competence;

  b) The competent authority of the receiving country withdraws the request for transfer;

  c) The person serving a prison sentence or his/her legal representative withdraws the request for transfer;

  d) The competent authority of the receiving country fails to provide additional information or documents within the time limit specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  dd) Other reasons why the request for transfer cannot be considered.

  4. Within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision to consider the request for transfer, the competent regional People’s Court shall hold a preliminary meeting to consider the request for transfer. If the case falls under the special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for holding the meeting to consider the request for transfer shall be 10 days.

  Article 40. Preliminary meeting to consider the request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country

  1. The preliminary meeting to consider the request for transfer shall be conducted by 1 judge, with the participation of 1 Procurator of the People’s Procuracy of the same level, and shall follow the following order:

  a) The judge presents the contents of the request for transfer and expresses his/her opinion on the legal basis for the transfer;

  b) The Procurator expresses the People’s Procuracy’s opinion on the transfer;

  c) The proposed person shall present his/her opinion (if any);

  d) The lawyer or the lawful representative of the proposed person shall present his/her opinion (if any).

  2. Based on the results of the first-instance hearing, the judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision to transfer the person serving a prison sentence;

  b) Decision to refuse to transfer the person serving a prison sentence and clearly state the reason.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of issuance of the decision to transfer or refuse to transfer the person serving a prison sentence, the competent regional People’s Court shall send the decision to the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the People’s Procuracy of the province, the Ministry of Public Security, the proposed person, the lawyer or the lawful representative of the person (if any). If it falls under the special case specified in Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for the competent regional People’s Court to send the decision to transfer or refuse to transfer is 5 days.

  Article 41. Appellate hearing to consider the decision to transfer or refuse to transfer

  1. The proposed person or the lawful representative of the person shall have the right to appeal within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision to transfer or refuse to transfer of the competent regional People’s Court. The People’s Procuracy of the same level shall have the right to protest within 15 days, the People’s Procuracy of the province shall have the right to protest within 30 days from the date on which the competent regional People’s Court issues the decision to transfer or refuse to transfer. If it falls under the special case specified in Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for appeal and the time limit for protest of the People’s Procuracy of the same level shall be 7 days, and the time limit for protest of the People’s Procuracy of the province shall be 15 days.

  2. Within 05 days from the expiration of the time limit for appeal, the competent People's Court of the region shall notify in writing the People's Procuracy of the same level and persons concerned with the appeal.

  The People's Procuracy shall send the decision on protest to the court of first instance, the person requested for transfer or his/her lawful representative within 02 days from the date of issuance of the decision on protest.

  The competent People's Court of the region shall send the dossier and the appeal or protest to the competent People's Court of the province within 07 days from the expiration of the time limit for appeal or protest.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the dossier and the appeal or protest, the competent People's Court of the province shall open a session for review of the decision on transfer or the decision on refusal of transfer of the court of first instance, which is appealed or protested. If it is a special case prescribed in Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for opening a session for review of the decision on transfer or the decision on refusal of transfer of the court of first instance, which is appealed or protested, shall be 07 days.

  3. The session for review of the decision on transfer or the decision on refusal of transfer of the court of first instance, which is appealed or protested, shall be conducted by 1 judge, with the participation of 1 procurator of the People's Procuracy of the same level, and shall be conducted in the following order:

  a) The judge shall present a summary of the contents of the dossier requesting the transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country, the decision on transfer or the decision on refusal of transfer, and the contents of the appeal or protest;

  b) The judge shall ask the person requested for transfer or his/her lawful representative whether he/she has changed, supplemented or withdrawn the appeal; if so, the judge shall request the procurator to present his/her opinion on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the appeal.

  The judge shall ask the procurator whether he/she has changed, supplemented or withdrawn the protest; if so, the judge shall request the person requested for transfer, the lawyer or the lawful representative of the person requested for transfer to present his/her opinion on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the protest.

  c) The procurator, the proposed transferor, the lawyer, and the lawful representative of the proposed transferor shall express their opinions on the contents of the appeal or protest; the procurator shall express the People’s Procuracy’s viewpoint on the handling of the appeal or protest.

  4. Based on the results of the appellate hearing, the judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To reject the appeal or protest and uphold the first-instance decision;

  b) To amend the first-instance decision;

  c) To annul the first-instance decision and transfer the dossier to the competent regional People’s Court for reconsideration;

  d) To annul the first-instance decision and suspend the consideration of the transfer request as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 39 of this Law;

  dd) To suspend the appellate hearing.

  5. The decision on transfer or the decision on refusal of transfer shall take legal effect including:

  a) The decision of the first-instance court not subject to appeal or protest shall take effect from the expiration of the time limit for appeal or protest;

  b) The decision of the appellate court shall take effect from the date of issuance and be the final decision.

  The decision of the appellate court shall be immediately sent to the first-instance court, the first-instance procuracy, the people's procuracy of the same level, the person proposed for transfer, the lawyer or the lawful representative of such person (if any).

  Article 42. Execution of the decision on transfer of a person serving a prison sentence

  1. Within 10 days from the effective date of the decision on transfer of a person serving a prison sentence issued by the people's court, the Chief Justice of the regional people's court that has issued the first-instance decision shall issue a decision on execution of the transfer decision. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for issuing the decision on execution of the transfer decision is 5 days. The decision on execution of the transfer decision shall take effect from the date of signing and issuance.

  2. The decision on execution of the transfer decision shall be sent to the people's procuracy of the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the receiving country, the transferred person or the lawful representative of such person.

  3. The Ministry of Public Security shall notify in writing the competent authority of the receiving country and the organization executing the transfer. The transfer of a person serving a prison sentence shall be carried out within 45 days from the effective date of the decision on execution of the transfer decision, unless otherwise agreed. If it is a special case specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for transferring a person serving a prison sentence is 15 days.

  4. The Government shall elaborate Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 43. Cancellation of decisions on transfer of persons serving prison sentences and decisions on execution of decisions on transfer of persons serving prison sentences

  1. A decision on transfer of a person serving a prison sentence that has not taken legal effect shall be canceled in the following cases:

  a) The person serving the prison sentence or their lawful representative withdraws the application for transfer or expresses their unwillingness to transfer in writing;

  b) The receiving country withdraws the request for transfer;

  c) The person serving the prison sentence dies or absconds from Vietnam;

  d) Other cases where the transfer of the person serving the prison sentence cannot be carried out.

  2. A decision on transfer of a person serving a prison sentence that has taken legal effect shall be canceled in the following cases:

  a) The cases specified in Points b, c and d, Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Decision on execution of decision on transfer of person serving prison sentence is annulled.

  3. Decision on execution of decision on transfer of person serving prison sentence is annulled in the following cases:

  a) In the case specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The transferred person is not a person specified in the decision on transfer of person serving prison sentence;

  c) The competent authority of the receiving country fails to receive the transferred person at the agreed time and place without justifiable reasons or the transfer is not carried out within the time limit specified in Clause 3 Article 42 of this Law.

  4. Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Ministry of Public Security, the President of the competent People’s Court shall annul the decision on transfer of person serving prison sentence specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article; annul the decision on execution of decision on transfer of person serving prison sentence specified in Clause 3 of this Article. These decisions take effect from the date of signing and must be immediately sent to the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the receiving country, the person serving the prison sentence or their lawful representative.

  5. The consideration of the request for transfer of person serving prison sentence in the cases specified in Point a and Point b Clause 1, Point c Clause 3 of this Article shall only be carried out at least 03 years after the date on which the decision on annulment of decision on transfer of person serving prison sentence, the decision on execution of decision on transfer of person serving prison sentence takes legal effect.

  Article 44. Transfer of transferred person

  1. The Ministry of Public Security shall organize the handover of the transferred person at the time and place agreed upon in writing with the competent authority of the receiving country.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 45. Reconsideration of judgments against transferred persons

  Judgments against persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam who are transferred to foreign countries may only be reconsidered by the People’s Courts of Vietnam according to the procedures of cassation and reopening according to the provisions of the law on criminal procedure.

  Article 46. Exchange of information on the execution of judgments of transferred persons

  1. After the person serving a prison sentence is transferred to the receiving country, the Ministry of Public Security shall request the competent authority of the receiving country to provide information on the execution of the judgment of the transferred person as required for state management.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall immediately notify in writing the competent authority of the receiving country when:

  a) The person serving a prison sentence is granted a reduction in the duration of the prison sentence, amnesty, special amnesty, early release on condition or is investigated for criminal liability for another crime;

  b) The People’s Court of Vietnam shall review the judgment against the transferred person according to the cassation or reopening procedures and the legal consequences of the cassation or reopening.

  Chapter IV

                                             IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 47. Effect

  1. This Law takes effect on July 1, 2026.

  2. The Law on Mutual Legal Assistance No. 08/2007/QH12, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 81/2025/QH15, shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Law, except for the transitional provisions specified in Article 48 of this Law, Article 45 of the Law on Extradition, Clause 1 of Article 42 of the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters and Clause 1 of Article 38 of the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance in Civil Matters.

  Article 48. Transitional provisions

  Requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences received by the Ministry of Public Security before the effective date of this Law shall continue to be considered and resolved in accordance with the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance No. 08/2007/QH12, which has been amended and supplemented by Law No. 81/2025/QH15.
