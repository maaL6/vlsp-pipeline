                                                  RESOLUTION

                                      ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE LAW ON LAWYERS

                      THE SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM

     Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam of 1992, amended and supplemented by Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10 of December 25, 2001 of the Xth National Assembly, the 10th session;

                                                  RESOLVES:

   1. The Law on Lawyers, passed by the XIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, the 9th session on June 29, 2006, takes effect from January 1, 2007.

  2. The Government shall review the documents detailing and guiding the implementation of the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers, cancel them and promulgate new documents for the implementation of this Law.

  3. From the effective date of the Law on Lawyers:

  a) Persons who have been granted the Law Practice Certificate under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers may continue to practice law as prescribed by this Law;

  b) Persons who are law apprentices under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers may continue to practice law as apprentices as prescribed by this Law; the time spent as law apprentices shall be counted as the time spent as law apprentices as prescribed by this Law and the Law Apprentice Card shall be invalid;

  c) The Law Card granted by the Bar Association under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers shall be valid until a new one is issued; the national bar association shall guide the replacement of the Law Card;

  d) Within six months, law offices and law partnership companies that have been granted the operation registration papers according to the 2001 Law on Lawyers must carry out the procedures for conversion into law offices or law partnership companies according to this Law.

  The Ministry of Justice shall guide the conversion of law offices and law partnership companies that have been granted the operation registration papers according to the 2001 Law on Lawyers;

  dd) Within six months, individuals and organizations that are providing legal services according to the 1999 Enterprise Law and continue to provide legal services must have all the conditions for law practice and must convert their practice organization forms according to this Law; if they fail to convert, they must cease their operations.

  The Ministry of Justice shall guide the conversion of individuals and organizations that are providing legal services according to the 1999 Enterprise Law;

  e) Foreign lawyers who have been granted the practice licenses in Vietnam; branches of foreign law-practicing organizations, foreign law firms, and foreign-Vietnam law partnership companies that have been granted the establishment licenses according to the Government's Decree No. 87/2003/ND-CP of July 22, 2003 on the practice of foreign lawyers and foreign lawyers in Vietnam may continue to practice according to this Law;

  g) Bar associations established according to the 2001 Law on Lawyers do not have to carry out the procedures for re-establishment;

  h) Before the national bar association is established, the Ministry of Justice shall conduct the examination of law practice probation results, and the bar associations shall issue the lawyer cards.

  4. The Government shall specify the provision of financial assistance from the state budget to mountainous provinces that are particularly difficult to develop their lawyer teams and ensure the operation of bar associations.

  This Resolution was passed by the XIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 9th session on June 29, 2006.
