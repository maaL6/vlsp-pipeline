                                                  DECREE

    AMENDMENTS TO THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE NO. 14/2013/ND-CP OF FEBRUARY 5, 2013 AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE NO. 07/2007/ND-CP OF JANUARY 12, 2007 ON SPECIFIC PROVISIONS AND GUIDELINES FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE LAW ON LEGAL AID

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;

  Pursuant to the Law on Legal Aid dated June 29, 2006;

  At the request of the Minister of Justice, the Government promulgates the Decree amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 14/2013/ND-CP dated February 5, 2013 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 07/2007/ND-CP dated January 12, 2007 detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid.

  Article 1. Amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 14/2013/ND-CP dated February 5, 2013 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 07/2007/ND-CP dated January 12, 2007 detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid as follows:

  1. Clause 7 Article 1 is amended as follows:

  1. A person holding a bachelor’s degree in law, who has graduated from a law professional training course and is working at a state legal aid center, who is sent to attend a legal aid professional training course; if he/she passes the examination of the legal aid professional training course, shall be granted a certificate of legal aid professional training by the Judicial Academy of the Ministry of Justice in accordance with law.

  A person who has been a lawyer in accordance with the Law on Lawyers or is exempted from a law professional training course in accordance with the Law on Lawyers, who is working at a state legal aid center, who is sent to take an examination of legal aid professional training; if he/she passes the examination of the legal aid professional training course, shall be granted a certificate of legal aid professional training by the Judicial Academy of the Ministry of Justice in accordance with law.

  2. The Minister of Justice shall prescribe the program and duration of legal aid professional training courses for legal aid professionals; promulgate the Regulations on organization, examination and grant of certificates of legal aid professional training courses.

  2. Clause 8 Article 1 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  Article 25. Professional titles of legal aid workers

  1. Legal aid workers shall have professional titles of public employees as prescribed by law.

  2. The Minister of Home Affairs shall promulgate the list, codes and classification of professional titles of legal aid workers.

  3. The Minister of Justice shall promulgate professional standards of professional titles of legal aid workers after reaching agreement with the Minister of Home Affairs.

  3. Clause 9 Article 1 is amended as follows:

  1. Legal assistants shall be paid salaries according to the salary table of professional titles of public employees as prescribed by law.

  3. Legal assistants shall be paid allowances for professional responsibilities equal to 25% of their current salaries plus allowances for leadership positions and allowances for exceeding the salary bracket (if any). When participating in legal proceedings, legal assistants shall be entitled to allowances for legal aid cases equal to 40% of the allowances applicable to lawyers providing legal aid at the State's request as prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article. When performing extrajudicial representation or mediation, legal assistants shall be entitled to allowances for legal aid cases equal to 20% of the allowances applicable to lawyers providing legal aid at the State's request.

  4. Clause 12 of Article 1 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  “2. Collaborators shall be entitled to allowances according to the legal aid cases they have handled, based on the number of hours of legal counseling and other forms of legal aid. The allowance per working session (1/2 working day) shall apply to legal aid cases handled by collaborators in the form of participation in legal proceedings or representation outside legal proceedings.

  The allowance per legal aid case for collaborators and lawyers providing legal aid at the State’s request shall be based on the quality of legal aid content, the duration of legal aid provision, the complexity of the legal aid case, the form of legal aid results, and the form of legal aid.

  In cases of legal aid provision through participation in legal proceedings, the allowance paid to lawyers providing legal aid at the State’s request shall be 500,000 VND per working session or allocated on a case-by-case basis with a minimum equal to 3 months’ basic salary and a maximum equal to 10 months’ basic salary (depending on the nature and content of the case). The Ministry of Justice shall specify the nature and content of cases, the method of calculating sessions and time for allocating expenses or paying allowances to lawyers per session or per working time.

  The duration of legal aid provision includes the time spent on studying case files and preparing documents for legal aid provision, the time spent meeting and contacting legal aid beneficiaries or their relatives, the time spent verifying legal aid cases, and the time spent working at agencies, organizations, and individuals related to the legal aid case. The basis for determining the working time of collaborators and lawyers providing legal aid at the State’s request is the working time certified by relevant agencies, organizations, and individuals.”

  Article 2. Effect

  This Decree takes effect from November 10, 2015.

  Article 3. Implementation responsibilities

  The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies, the heads of the agencies attached to the Government, and the presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall implement this Decree.
