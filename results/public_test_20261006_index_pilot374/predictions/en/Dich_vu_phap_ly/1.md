                                                        THE LAW

                                                LEGAL ASSISTANCE

  Law on Legal Aid No. 11/2017/QH14 dated June 20, 2017 of the National Assembly, effective from January 01, 2018, is amended and supplemented by:

  1. The Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking No. 53/2024/QH15 dated November 28, 2024 of the National Assembly, effective from July 1, 2025;

  2. The Law on Juvenile Justice No. 59/2024/QH15 dated November 30, 2024 of the National Assembly, effective from January 1, 2026;

  3. The Law No. 05/2026/QH16 dated April 23, 2026 of the National Assembly amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid, effective from January 1, 2027.

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on Legal Aid[1].

  Chapter I

                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Law provides for legal aid providers; legal aid organizations; legal aid providers; legal aid activities; and responsibilities of agencies, organizations and individuals for legal aid activities.

  Article 2. Legal aid

  Legal aid means the provision of free legal services to legal aid providers in legal aid cases in accordance with this Law, which contributes to ensuring human rights and citizens' rights to access justice and equality before the law.

  Article 3. Principles of legal aid activities

  1. Compliance with laws and rules of legal aid profession.

  2. Timeliness, independence, honesty and respect for objective truth.

  3. Best protection of lawful rights and interests of legal aid providers.

  4. No material benefits or other benefits shall be collected from the legal aid recipients.

  Article 4. The State’s policy on legal aid

  1. Legal aid is the responsibility of the State.

  2. The State shall have policies to ensure the right to legal aid in accordance with socio-economic conditions.

  3. The State shall have policies to improve the quality of legal aid and attract resources for legal aid.

  4. The State shall support, encourage, recognize and honor agencies, organizations and individuals that participate in and contribute to legal aid activities.

  Article 5. Financial resources for legal aid

  1. Financial resources for legal aid include state budget; contributions and sponsorship from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals and other lawful sources.

  2. State budget funds shall be allocated in the annual state budget estimate of the agency implementing the management of legal aid as prescribed by law on state budget, public investment, science, technology and innovation, digital transformation, digital technology industry and relevant specialized laws.

  For localities that have not yet balanced their budgets, budget allocation shall be prioritized from the annual budget balance supplement to support the handling of complex and typical legal aid cases.

  3. Funds for legal aid activities of voluntary organizations shall be self-assured by such organizations.

  Article 6. Prohibited acts in legal aid activities

  1. Legal aid organizations and legal aid providers are strictly prohibited from committing the following acts:

  a) Violating the honor, dignity, lawful rights and interests of legal aid beneficiaries; discriminating against legal aid beneficiaries;

  b) Receiving or demanding any amount of money, material benefits or other benefits from legal aid beneficiaries; harassing legal aid beneficiaries;

  c) Disclosing information on legal aid cases or legal aid beneficiaries, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the legal aid beneficiaries or otherwise provided for by law;

  d) Refuse or fail to continue providing legal aid, except for cases specified in this Law and regulations of law on proceedings;

  dd) Abuse legal aid activities for personal gain, infringing upon national defense and security, causing social disorder and safety, and adversely affecting social ethics;

  e) Incite or incite legal aid recipients to provide false information and documents, file complaints, denunciations, or lawsuits in violation of law.

  2. Legal aid recipients, agencies, organizations, and individuals involved in legal aid activities are strictly prohibited from committing the following acts:

  a) Infringing upon the health, life, honor, and dignity of legal aid providers and the reputation of legal aid organizations;

  b) Intentionally providing false information and documents about legal aid cases;

  c) Threatening, obstructing, or illegally interfering in legal aid activities; causing disturbances, disorder, or seriously violating regulations of legal aid providers.

  Chapter II

   LEGAL ASISTED PERSONS, THEIR RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS

  Article 7. Legal assisted persons[3]

  1. Persons who have rendered services to the revolution; parents, spouses and children of martyrs and persons who have raised martyrs.

  2. Persons belonging to poor households or near-poor households.

  3. Persons infected with the chemical warfare agent dioxin.

  4. Persons with disabilities.

  5. Children.

  6. Persons of ethnic minorities residing in areas with particularly difficult socio-economic conditions; persons of ethnic minorities with very small populations.

  7. Monthly social assistance beneficiaries are elderly persons, persons who are victims of violence in family violence cases, and persons infected with HIV.

  8. Low-income workers are persons who are accused or victimized.

  9. Persons facing sudden difficulties due to natural disasters or fires shall receive legal assistance in cases directly related to the remediation of consequences arising from these events.

  10. Persons aged between 16 and under 18 years of age are persons who are denounced, persons who are recommended for prosecution, persons who are detained in emergencies, persons who are accused or victimized, persons who are witnesses, persons who are serving diversion measures, and prisoners as prescribed by the Law on Juvenile Justice.

  11. Victims, persons currently being identified as victims of human trafficking, and persons under 18 years of age accompanying them are prescribed by the Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking.

  12. Individuals who suffer damage but do not hire persons to protect their lawful rights and interests as prescribed by the Law on State Compensation Liability.

  13. Foreigners under international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a member are prescribed to receive legal assistance.

  14. Other persons receiving legal assistance as prescribed by law.

  Article 8. Rights of legal aid recipients

  1. To receive legal aid without paying any fees, material benefits or other benefits.

  2. To request legal aid on their own or through relatives, competent procedural bodies, procedural bodies, competent persons, other agencies, organizations or individuals.

  3. To be informed of their rights to legal aid, the order and procedures for legal aid when they arrive at legal aid organizations and relevant state agencies.

  4. To request confidentiality of legal aid matters.

  5. To choose a legal aid organization and legal aid provider in the local area from the published list; to request a change of legal aid provider when the legal aid provider falls into one of the cases specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 25 of this Law.

  6. To change or withdraw their legal aid request.

  7. To be compensated for damages according to law.

  8. Complaints and denunciations about legal aid shall comply with this Law and other relevant laws.

  Article 9. Obligations of legal aid recipients

  1. Provide documents proving that they are legal aid recipients.

  2. Cooperate with, and provide timely and complete information, documents and evidence related to legal aid cases, and take responsibility for the accuracy of such information, documents and evidence.

  3. Respect legal aid organizations, legal aid providers, and other agencies, organizations and individuals involved in legal aid cases.

  4. Do not request other legal aid organizations to provide legal aid for the same case that is being accepted and handled by a legal aid organization.

  5. Comply with laws on legal aid and internal regulations of legal aid providers.

  Chapter III

   ORGANIZATIONS PROVIDING LEGAL AID

  Article 10. Organizations providing legal aid

  1. Organizations providing legal aid include State Legal Aid Centers and organizations participating in legal aid.

  2. Departments of Justice shall publish lists of local organizations providing legal aid, post them on the websites of the Departments of Justice and send them to the Ministry of Justice for compilation and posting on the National Legal Portal.

  Article 11. State Legal Aid Centers

  1. State Legal Aid Centers are public service units affiliated to the Departments of Justice, established by provincial-level People’s Committees, having legal personality, seals, head offices and separate accounts.

  2. State Legal Aid Centers may have branches.

  Branches are dependent units of State Legal Aid Centers. State Legal Aid Centers shall be responsible for all activities of branches. Based on socio-economic conditions and legal aid needs in localities, the presidents of provincial-level People’s Committees shall decide on establishment of branches of State Legal Aid Centers.

  3. The Government shall define the organization and operation of the State Legal Aid Center and its branches; the order and procedures for establishment, dissolution and merger of branches of the State Legal Aid Center.

  Article 12. Organizations participating in legal aid

  1. Organizations participating in legal aid include organizations signing legal aid contracts and organizations registering to participate in legal aid.

  2. Organizations signing legal aid contracts include law-practicing organizations and legal consultancy organizations signing legal aid contracts with the provincial/municipal Justice Services in accordance with this Law.

  3. Organizations registering to participate in legal aid include law-practicing organizations and legal consultancy organizations registering to participate in legal aid in accordance with this Law.

  Article 13. Rights and obligations of legal aid-providing organizations

  1. Legal aid-providing organizations have the following rights and obligations:

  a) Provide legal aid;

  b) To request relevant agencies and organizations to cooperate in providing information and documents on legal aid cases;

  c) To compensate for damage caused by the fault of persons affiliated to their organizations while providing legal aid;

  d) To implement the regime of statistics, reporting and communication on legal aid;

  dd) To settle complaints in accordance with Clause 2 Article 45 of this Law;

  e) To make proposals to competent state agencies on issues related to settlement of legal aid cases.

  2. State legal aid centers have the following rights and obligations:

  a) Rights and obligations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) To perform other tasks authorized or requested by competent state agencies in charge of legal aid.

  3. Organizations that sign contracts for legal aid shall have the following rights and obligations:

  a) Rights and obligations as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) To receive remuneration and expenses for legal aid cases as prescribed by the Government;

  c) Other rights and obligations as prescribed in the legal aid contracts.

  4. Organizations that register for legal aid shall have the following rights and obligations:

  a) Rights and obligations as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) To provide legal aid as per the registration details.

  Article 14. Legal aid contracts

  1. Legal aid contracts shall be signed between the Department of Justice and law-practicing organizations, legal consultancy organizations and between the State Legal Aid Centers and lawyers, legal aid collaborators on legal aid implementation in accordance with the law on civil matters.

  2. Based on legal aid requests and local practice, the Department of Justice shall select and sign legal aid contracts with organizations that meet the following conditions:

  a) Having a field of operation registered in accordance with the field of legal aid as prescribed by this Law;

  b) Being a law-practicing organization or a legal consultancy organization with at least 01 legal consultant with at least 02 years of legal consultancy experience or 01 lawyer working regularly at the organization;

  c) Having facilities suitable for legal aid activities;

  d) Not being subject to the enforcement of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in law-practicing or legal consultancy activities.

  3. The State Legal Aid Centers shall, based on legal aid needs and legal aid resources available in the locality, select and sign legal aid contracts with lawyers that meet the following conditions:

  a) Not being subject to the enforcement of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in law-practicing activities;

  b) Not prohibited from practicing or restricted in professional activities according to decisions of competent authorities;

  c) Not being investigated for penal liability;

  d) Obtain written consent of the law-practicing organization where the lawyer works or the agency or organization with which the lawyer has signed labor contracts.

  4. The State legal aid center shall select and sign contracts for legal aid with legal aid collaborators according to Article 24 of this Law.

  5. Organizations and individuals that have committed the prohibited acts specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Law shall not be allowed to select and sign contracts for legal aid for at least 2 years from the date of conclusion of the violation.

  6. The Minister of Justice shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 15. Registration for legal aid

  1. Law-practicing organizations and voluntary legal counseling organizations that provide legal aid with their own resources may register for legal aid as follows:

  a) The law-practicing organization is eligible as prescribed in Points a, c and d, Clause 2, Article 14 of this Law;

  b) The legal consultancy organization is eligible as prescribed in Points a, c and d, Clause 2, Article 14 of this Law and has at least 01 legal consultant with 02 years of legal consultancy experience or 01 lawyer working regularly at the organization.

  2. The law-practicing organization or the legal consultancy organization that participates in legal aid shall register the scope, form, field and subjects of legal aid with the Department of Justice that has issued the Certificate of operation registration.

  3. The Minister of Justice shall prescribe the order and procedures for registration to participate in legal aid.

  Article 16. Termination of legal aid provision by legal aid-providing organizations

  1. The organization that signs a legal aid provision contract shall terminate the legal aid provision when falling into one of the following cases:

  a) No longer satisfying one of the conditions for signing the legal aid provision contract as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 14 of this Law;

  b) Terminating the legal aid provision contract;

  c) Providing legal aid that causes serious consequences;

  d) Ceasing operations as prescribed by law.

  2. Organizations that register for legal aid shall cease providing legal aid when falling into one of the following cases:

  a) No longer satisfying one of the conditions for registration for legal aid as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law;

  b) Voluntarily ceasing participation in legal aid after notifying the Department of Justice where they registered for legal aid in writing;

  c) Failing to provide legal aid for 2 consecutive years, except for cases of objective reasons;

  d) Providing legal aid that causes serious consequences;

  dd) Ceasing operations as prescribed by law.

  3. Upon termination of legal aid, the legal aid-organizing organization shall notify the Department of Justice in writing of the termination of legal aid and transfer the unfinished case file to the legal aid-organizing organization assigned by the Department of Justice for further implementation.

  Chapter IV

      LEGAL AID PROVIDERS, THEIR RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS

  Article 17. Legal aid providers

  1. Legal aid providers include:

  a) Legal aid assistants;

  b) Lawyers providing legal aid under contracts with state legal aid centers; lawyers providing legal aid under assignment of legal aid-organizing organizations;

  c) Legal consultants with at least 02 years of legal consultation experience working at legal aid-organizing organizations;

  d) Legal aid collaborators.

  2. The Department of Justice shall publish the list of legal aid providers in the locality, post it on the website of the Department of Justice and send it to the Ministry of Justice for compilation and posting on the National Legal Portal[8].

  Article 18. Rights and obligations of legal aid providers

  1. Legal aid providers have the following rights and obligations:

  a) To provide legal aid;

  b) To be guaranteed to provide legal aid independently, without being threatened, obstructed, harassed or illegally interfered;

  c) To refuse or discontinue providing legal aid in the cases specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 Article 25 and Clause 1 Article 37 of this Law and according to the law on proceedings;

  d) To be provided with allowances and professional training in legal aid skills;

  d) Ensure the quality of legal aid cases;

  e) Comply with the principles of legal aid;

  g) Strictly observe the internal regulations of the legal aid provider;

  h) Compensate or refund a sum of money to the legal aid provider that has paid the victim for damage caused by their fault during legal aid as prescribed by law.

  2. Legal aid providers have the following rights and obligations:

  a) The rights and obligations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enroll in training courses to improve knowledge and skills in legal aid;

  c) Perform other tasks as assigned;

  d) Enjoy the regimes, policies and incentives[9] as prescribed.

  3. Lawyers and legal aid collaborators who sign contracts for legal aid with the State Legal Aid Centers shall enjoy remuneration and expenses for legal aid cases as prescribed.

  4. The Government shall elaborate Point d Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 19. Legal aid assistants[10]

  Vietnamese citizens who are employees of the State Legal Aid Centers and satisfy the following criteria may become legal aid assistants:

  1. Possess good moral qualities;

  2. Have a bachelor’s degree in law or higher;

  3. Have been trained as lawyers or are exempt from legal professional training;

  4. Having completed the probation period for law practice or legal aid, except for cases where they have been legal assistants;

  5. Having sufficient health to perform legal aid.

  Article 20. Legal aid probation

  1. Employees of the State Legal Aid Centers who have certificates of completion of law practice training or are exempted from law practice training as prescribed by the Law on Lawyers may undergo legal aid probation at the State Legal Aid Centers.

  The legal aid probation period is 12 months. The State Legal Aid Centers shall assign legal assistants to guide legal aid probationers and certify the legal aid probation. Legal assistants guiding probationers must have at least 3 years of experience as legal assistants. At the same time, one legal assistant may not guide more than 2 probationers.

  2. Legal aid probationers may assist legal assistants in professional activities but may not represent, defend, or protect the lawful rights and interests of legal aid recipients at court hearings; may not sign legal consultation documents.

  Legal aid probationers may meet with legal aid recipients and other involved parties in legal aid cases with the consent of such parties, together with legal assistants, to study case files, collect documents, objects, and details related to the case, and engage in other professional activities. Legal assistants guiding probationers shall supervise and take responsibility for the activities of legal aid probationers prescribed in this Clause.

  3. Persons who are exempted from or have their probation period for law practice reduced as prescribed by the Law on Lawyers may have their legal aid probation period exempted from or reduced.

  4. The Minister of Justice shall elaborate on the probation, examination of the results of legal aid probation and the Certificate of examination of the results of legal aid probation.

  Article 21. Appointment, issuance of cards and re-issuance of cards of legal aiders[11]

  1. The President of the People’s Committee of a province shall consider appointing and issuing cards of legal aiders to persons who meet the criteria for legal aiders specified in Article 19 of this Law and are not in any of the following cases:

  a) Are subject to disciplinary action; are subject to administrative measures of compulsory detoxification or compulsory education;

  b) Are being investigated for penal liability; have been convicted of serious crimes intentionally, very serious crimes intentionally or especially serious crimes intentionally, including cases where their criminal record has been erased; have been convicted but their criminal record has not been erased for very serious crimes unintentionally, especially serious crimes unintentionally or less serious crimes intentionally.

  2. The President of the People’s Committee of a province shall consider appointing and issuing cards of legal aiders to persons who have been dismissed or whose cards of legal aiders have been revoked under the cases specified in Points a, dd or g Clause 1 Article 22 of this Law when they meet the criteria for legal aiders specified in Article 19 of this Law and the reasons for dismissal or revocation of cards are no longer applicable.

  3. The President of the People’s Committee of a province shall re-issue cards of legal aiders in case the cards are lost or damaged.

  4. The Government shall elaborate on this Article; specify the order and procedures for appointment, issuance of cards and re-issuance of cards of legal aiders.

  Article 22. Dismissal and revocation of legal aid cards[12]

  1. A legal aid provider shall be dismissed and his/her legal aid card revoked if he/she falls into any of the following cases:

  a) No longer meets the legal aid provider’s qualifications prescribed in Article 19 of this Law;

  b) Fails to participate in legal proceedings for 12 consecutive months, except for objective reasons;

  c) Is disciplined by termination of employment;

  d) Is disciplined by warning or more for committing acts prescribed in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Law;

  dd) Is prohibited from practicing legal aid under a competent authority’s decision;

  e) Is convicted and the judgment has taken legal effect;

  g) Being subject to administrative measures of being sent to a compulsory rehabilitation center or compulsory education center.

  2. Legal assistants shall be automatically dismissed and their legal assistant cards shall be automatically invalidated in the following cases:

  a) Being transferred to another job; quitting or being laid off;

  b) Retiring.

  3. The presidents of the provincial-level People’s Committees shall decide on the dismissal and revocation of legal assistant cards.

  4. The Government shall elaborate this Article and provide for the procedures for dismissal and revocation of legal assistant cards.

  Article 23. [13] (annulled)

  Article 24. Legal collaborators [14]

  1. The following persons may become legal aid collaborators:

   a) Retired or voluntarily retired or resigned due to objective reasons, having full civil act capacity, good moral qualities, sufficient health and willing to provide legal aid, including former legal aid collaborators, judges, judicial inspectors, procurators, procuratorial inspectors, investigators, bailiffs, civil judgment enforcement inspectors, persons holding the rank or grade of chief specialist or equivalent or higher working in legal affairs; persons holding the rank or grade of specialist or equivalent working in legal affairs for 05 years or more at agencies of the Communist Party of Vietnam, the State, the Vietnam Fatherland Front and its member organizations; former legal aid collaborators;

  b) Officials holding the rank III and equivalent working in legal affairs for 05 years or more or officials holding the rank I, II and equivalent working in legal affairs and having the written consent of their workplaces.

  2. Directors of Departments of Justice shall consider and issue legal aid collaborators cards to persons who meet the criteria and conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article and do not fall into any of the following cases:

  a) Are being investigated for penal liability; have been convicted of serious crimes intentionally, very serious crimes intentionally, especially serious crimes intentionally, including cases where their criminal record has been erased; have been convicted but their criminal record has not been erased for very serious crimes unintentionally, especially serious crimes unintentionally or less serious crimes intentionally;

  b) Are subject to administrative measures of compulsory detoxification or compulsory education; are subject to disciplinary action.

  3. Directors of State Legal Aid Centers shall sign contracts for legal aid with persons who are issued legal aid collaborators cards to provide legal counseling and act as non-adversarial representatives in localities.

  4. Legal aid collaborators cards shall be revoked when the legal aid collaborators fall into any of the following cases:

  a) No longer meet the criteria and conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Fail to provide legal aid services for 2 consecutive years, except for objective reasons;

  c) Commit acts specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Law or have been administratively sanctioned in the legal aid sector but have not been revoked their legal aid worker cards and continue to commit violations;

  d) The legal aid contract is terminated or the legal aid worker fails to sign a legal aid contract with the State Legal Aid Center within 30 days from the date of receiving the contract signing request from the Center without a valid reason;

  dd) The legal aid worker is a public employee who is disciplined by being forced to resign;

  e) Subject to administrative measures of being sent to a compulsory rehabilitation center or compulsory education facility;

  g) Convicted and the judgment has taken legal effect.

  5. The Director of the Department of Justice shall decide to revoke the legal aid worker card; re-issuance of the legal aid worker card in case of loss or damage.

  6. The Government shall elaborate this Article; and provide for the order and procedures for issuance, re-issuance and revocation of legal aid collaborators cards.

  Article 25. Cases where legal aid cannot be provided or must be refused

  1. A legal aid provider shall not be allowed to provide legal aid in the following cases:

  a) Committing prohibited acts specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Law, except for cases where the violator has fully served the violation and is allowed to provide legal aid as prescribed by this Law;

  b) Having their legal aid collaborators cards, legal aid collaborators cards, lawyer practice certificates or legal advisors cards revoked;

  c) Cases where they are not allowed to participate in legal proceedings as prescribed by law on legal proceedings.

  2. A legal aid provider shall refuse to provide legal aid in the following cases:

  a) Having provided or providing legal aid to legal aid recipients who are parties with conflicting interests in the same case, except for cases where the parties have reached other agreements on legal counseling or out-of-court representation in civil matters;

  b) There are grounds to believe that the legal aid provider may be unobjective in providing legal aid;

  c) There are reasons to show that the legal aid case cannot be handled effectively, affecting the legitimate rights and interests of the legal aid recipient.

  3. The legal aid provider shall notify in writing and clearly state the reasons to the legal aid recipient and appoint another person to provide legal aid in the cases specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Chapter V

                SCOPE, DOMAIN, FORMS AND ACTIVITIES OF LEGAL AID

  Article 26. Scope of legal aid

  1. The State legal aid center shall provide legal aid in one of the following cases:

  a) The legal aid recipient is residing in the locality;

  b) Legal aid cases arising in the locality;

  c) Legal aid cases requested by the competent legal aid agency at the central level;

  d) Cases transferred under Clause 3 Article 35 of this Law.

  2. Organizations signing legal aid contracts shall provide legal aid within the scope of the contract.

  3. Organizations registering for legal aid shall provide legal aid within the scope of their registration.

  Article 27. Fields and forms of legal aid

  1. Legal aid shall be provided in legal fields, except for business and trade.

  2. Forms of legal aid include:

  a) To participate in legal proceedings;

  b) To provide legal counseling;

  c) To act as a non-judicial representative.

  Article 28. Places of reception of legal aid recipients

  1. Legal aid organizations shall arrange places of reception of legal aid recipients at their headquarters or other places outside their headquarters to ensure that the presentation of requests is easy and convenient.

  2. The reception schedule and regulations for reception of legal aid recipients shall be posted at the headquarters of legal aid organizations.

  Article 29. (amended)

  Article 30. Requests for and acceptance of legal aid cases

  1. When requesting legal aid, the applicant must submit the application to the legal aid provider.

  2. A legal aid request shall only be accepted when it involves a specific case directly related to the legal rights and interests of the legal aid recipient and complies with the provisions of this Law.

  3. The legal aid provider shall refuse to accept the request and clearly notify the reasons in writing to the applicant in one of the following cases:

  a) The legal aid request does not comply with the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The legal aid request contains illegal content;

  c) The legal aid recipient has died;

  d) The case is being accepted and resolved by another legal aid provider;

  dd) The legal aid request has been resolved and no new content arises.

  4. In case the applicant cannot provide a complete dossier but needs legal aid immediately because the case is about to expire the time limit for initiating a lawsuit, the date of trial is approaching, the case is handled by a competent authority, or the competent authority transfers the request for legal aid to a legal aid organization or to avoid causing damage to the lawful rights and interests of the legal aid recipient, the person receiving the request shall report to the head of the legal aid organization and immediately accept the request, and at the same time guide the legal aid applicant to supplement necessary papers and documents.

  5. The Minister of Justice shall elaborate this Article; and specify the procedures for accepting, immediately accepting, and refusing to accept legal aid cases.

  Article 31. Participation in legal proceedings

  1. Legal aid assistants and lawyers providing legal aid shall participate in legal proceedings as defense persons or persons protecting the lawful rights and interests of legal aid recipients in accordance with this Law and the law on legal proceedings.

  2. When a legal aid recipient requests the assignment of a legal aid provider to participate in legal proceedings, within 3 working days from the date of acceptance of the case, the legal aid organization shall be responsible for assigning a legal aid provider.

  In case the legal aid recipient is a person who is arrested or detained and requests the assignment of a legal aid provider, within 12 hours from the time of acceptance, the legal aid organization shall be responsible for assigning a legal aid provider.

  3. Within 12 hours from the time of receiving the legal aid request of the arrested or detained person or within 24 hours from the time of receiving the legal aid request of the suspect, defendant, or victim who is a legal aid recipient in accordance with the law on legal proceedings, the competent authority or person shall be responsible for notifying the local state legal aid center. Immediately after receiving the notification from the competent authority or person, the state legal aid center shall be responsible for accepting the case in accordance with Clause 4 Article 30 of this Law and assigning a legal aid provider to participate in legal proceedings.

  4. The assignment of a person to participate in legal proceedings must be made in writing and sent to the legal aid recipient and the relevant competent authority.

  Article 32. Legal counseling[18]

  1. Legal aid providers shall provide legal counseling to legal aid beneficiaries by giving guidance, offering opinions, and assisting in drafting documents related to disputes, complaints, or legal issues; guiding and assisting parties in mediation, negotiation, and reaching agreement on the resolution of cases.

  2. The Minister of Justice shall elaborate this Article and specify procedures for legal counseling.

  Article 33. Extrajudicial representation[19]

  1. Legal aid providers, lawyers, and legal aid collaborators shall act as extrajudicial representatives for legal aid beneficiaries before competent state agencies.

  2. The Minister of Justice shall elaborate this Article and specify procedures for extrajudicial representation.

  Article 34. Coordination in verification of legal aid cases[20]

  1. Where it is necessary to verify circumstances or events related to a legal aid case in another locality, the legal aid organization that has accepted the case shall request the legal aid organization in the locality where verification is required to cooperate in the verification. Requests for verification must be made in writing, clearly stating the matters to be verified and the time limit for response.

  2. The Minister of Justice shall elaborate this Article; and specify the order and procedures for cooperation in verifying legal aid cases.

  Article 35. Transfer of legal aid requests and cases[21]

  1. Where a legal aid request does not satisfy the conditions specified in Points a and b, Clause 1, Article 26 of this Law, the State Legal Aid Center shall transfer the legal aid request to a competent State Legal Aid Center and notify the requester thereof.

  2. Where the legal aid organization lacks resources to provide legal aid, it shall transfer the legal aid request to the State Legal Aid Center in the locality and notify the requester thereof.

  3. The State Legal Aid Center that is handling a legal aid case may transfer the case to another State Legal Aid Center in the locality when the case has been transferred to a competent procedural authority or the procedural authority in the locality for settlement, and notify the legal aid recipient thereof.

  Article 36. Recommendations in legal aid activities[23]

  1. During the provision of legal aid, the legal aid organization shall make a written recommendation to the competent state agency regarding issues related to the settlement of the legal aid case. The receiving agency shall, within the scope of its tasks and powers, respond in writing as prescribed by law.

  2. Where the receiving agency fails to respond as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article, the legal aid organization may recommend its superior agency to consider and settle the matter.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article; and specify the order and procedures for making requests in legal aid activities.

  Article 37. Discontinuation of legal aid cases

  1. A legal aid case shall be discontinued in one of the following cases:

  a) The case must be refused as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 30 of this Law;

  b) The legal aid recipient commits one of the prohibited acts prescribed in Clause 2 Article 6 of this Law;

  c) The legal aid recipient withdraws the legal aid request.

  2. In case of discontinuation of legal aid, the legal aid provider or legal aid provider shall notify the legal aid recipient in writing and clearly state the reason.

  3. In case of a legal aid case that is being carried out and the legal aid recipient no longer meets the requirements prescribed in Article 7 of this Law, the case shall be continued until the end.

  Article 38. Legal aid case files [24]

  When providing legal aid, the legal aid provider and the legal aid provider shall be responsible for preparing legal aid case files in accordance with the regulations of the Minister of Justice.

  Article 39. Archiving of legal aid case files

  1. Within 30 days after the case is concluded, the legal aid provider shall hand over the legal aid case file to the legal aid provider.

  2. The papers and documents in the legal aid case file shall be counted, numbered, arranged in the order of day, month and year, and archived in accordance with the law.

  3. The electronic file of each legal aid case shall be digitized, updated in the legal aid case management system and stored in the legal aid database.

  Chapter VI

          RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE AUTHORITIES AND ORGANIZATIONS FOR LEGAL AID ACTIVITIES

  Article 40. State management of legal aid

  1. The Government shall unify the state management of legal aid.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall act as the focal point agency to assist the Government in the state management of legal aid and shall have the following tasks and powers:

  a) To take charge of the formulation, promulgation or submission to competent state agencies for promulgation of legal documents on legal aid;

  b) To formulate, promulgate or submit to competent agencies for promulgation the strategy and plan for development of legal aid and organize the implementation thereof;

  c) To promulgate regulations guiding the professional practice, assessment of quality and effectiveness of legal aid cases; forms of documents in legal aid activities; criteria for identifying complex, typical, quality and successful legal aid cases; to implement the regime of reporting and statistics on legal aid;

  d) To guide, organize and monitor the implementation of legal documents on legal aid;

  dd) To organize training, retraining and professional skills training for legal aid providers;

  e) Organize communication about legal aid[26];

  g) [27] Inspect the implementation of legal aid activities; commend, discipline and handle violations in legal aid activities;

  h) Receive support and contributions from organizations and individuals for legal aid activities;

  i) Carry out international cooperation in legal aid;

  k) [28] Apply information technology and digital technology and implement digital transformation in legal aid activities; build, manage, operate and regularly upgrade the legal aid information system and database; connect, integrate and share legal aid data on the National Legal Portal.

  3. [29] Ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in the state management and organization of legal aid; create, integrate, share, exploit and use data through connection and interconnection with the legal aid database on the National Legal Portal.

  4. [30] Provincial People’s Committees shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, state manage legal aid in their localities, ensure working conditions of the State Legal Aid Centers; coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in creating, integrating, sharing, exploiting and using data through connection and interconnection with the legal aid database on the National Legal Portal.

  5. [31] Commune-level People’s Committees shall coordinate with the State Legal Aid Centers in the implementation of legal aid activities in their localities.

  6.[32] The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 41. Responsibilities of agencies related to legal aid in legal proceedings[33]

  1. Within the scope of their tasks and powers, competent legal proceeding agencies and legal proceeding conducting agencies shall coordinate and organize the implementation of inter-sectoral mechanisms and direct legal aid at People’s Courts and criminal investigations; create conditions for legal aid recipients to enjoy legal aid rights and for legal aid providers to participate in legal proceedings in accordance with law.

  2. The Minister of Justice, the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court, the Procurator General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy, the Minister of National Defense, the Minister of Public Security, and the Minister of Finance shall coordinate in developing inter-sectoral councils on legal aid in legal proceedings at the central and provincial levels.

  3. Agencies related to legal aid in legal proceedings shall coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in establishing, integrating, sharing, exploiting, and using data through connection and interconnection with legal aid databases on the National Law Portal.

  Article 42. Responsibilities of the Vietnam Fatherland Front, its member organizations, and relevant state agencies[34]

  1. The Vietnam Fatherland Front and its member organizations shall, within the scope of their functions and tasks, supervise the implementation of policies and laws on legal aid; coordinate with relevant state agencies and organizations in legal aid in accordance with law.

  2. During the handling and settlement of cases involving citizens, if the citizen is eligible for legal aid, the state agency shall explain the right to legal aid and refer them to the legal aid provider.

  Article 43. Responsibilities of socio-professional organizations of lawyers

  1. Vietnam Bar Federation shall coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in managing and supervising the provision of legal aid by law-practicing organizations and lawyers in accordance with this Law.

  2. Provincial-level bar associations shall coordinate with provincial-level Justice Services in managing and supervising the provision of legal aid by law-practicing organizations and lawyers in accordance with this Law.

  Article 44. Responsibilities of managing organizations of legal consultancy organizations Managing organizations of legal consultancy organizations shall coordinate with competent regulatory agencies in charge of legal aid in managing and supervising the provision of legal aid by legal consultancy organizations and legal consultants in accordance with this Law.

  Chapter VII

                            RESOLUTION OF COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND REPORTS

  Article 45. Resolution of complaints and denunciations

  1. Persons receiving legal aid have the right to lodge complaints against the following acts of legal aid-providing organizations and persons when they have grounds to believe that such acts are illegal and infringe upon their lawful rights and interests:

  a) Refusing to accept legal aid cases;

  b) Failing to provide legal aid;

  c) Providing legal aid in violation of law;

  d) Changing legal aid providers in violation of law.

  2. The head of a legal aid provider shall be responsible for handling complaints about the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article. If the complainant disagrees with the decision on handling the complaint of the head of the legal aid provider or the complaint is not resolved within the prescribed time limit, the complainant may lodge a complaint with the Director of the Department of Justice.

  If the complainant disagrees with the decision on handling the complaint of the Director of the Department of Justice or the complaint is not resolved within the prescribed time limit, the complainant may file a lawsuit with the court.

  The Government shall elaborate this Clause and specify the procedures for handling complaints about legal aid.

  3. Complaints and lawsuits about legal aid that do not fall under the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall comply with the law on complaints and other relevant laws.

  4. Individuals have the right to denounce acts of violation of this Law to competent state agencies. Denunciation and settlement of denunciation shall comply with the law on denunciation.

  Article 46. Dispute settlement

  1. In case of dispute between legal aid recipients and legal aid providers, legal consultants, legal aid collaborators, legal aid centers, or legal consultancy organizations related to the provision of legal aid, dispute settlement shall comply with the law on civil matters.

  2. In case of dispute between legal aid recipients and lawyers or law-practicing organizations related to the provision of legal aid, dispute settlement shall comply with the law on lawyers and other relevant laws.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 47. Entry into force

  1. This Law takes effect on January 01, 2018.

  2. The Law on Legal Aid No. 69/2006/QH11 shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Law.

  Article 48. Transitional provisions

  1. From the effective date of this Law, persons appointed as legal aiders under the Law on Legal Aid No. 69/2006/QH11 shall continue to operate under this Law; after 5 years from the effective date of this Law, persons appointed as legal aiders without a certificate of completion of law professional training shall have their legal aid cards revoked.

  2. Within 1 year from the effective date of this Law, legal aid organizations and legal aid participants under the Law on Legal Aid No. 69/2006/QH11 shall comply with this Law; in case of failure to comply with this Law, they shall terminate their participation in legal aid and transfer the dossiers of ongoing cases to the State Legal Aid Centers where they registered for further implementation.

  3. Legal aid cases currently being handled under the Law on Legal Aid No. 69/2006/QH11 but not yet concluded by the effective date of this Law shall continue to be handled under the Law on Legal Aid No. 69/2006/QH11 until the cases are concluded.

  4. Within 1 year from the effective date of this Law, provincial People’s Committees shall review the branches of the State Legal Aid Centers established in their localities and, based on legal aid needs, legal aid resources and operational effectiveness, report to the Ministry of Justice for the maintenance, merger or dissolution of the branches of the State Legal Aid Centers.
