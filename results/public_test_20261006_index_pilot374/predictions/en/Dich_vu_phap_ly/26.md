                                            CIRCULAR
GUIDELINES FOR CALCULATING THE TIME BASED ON REAL WORK SHIFTS AND CHARGING FOR LEGAL ASSISTANCE
Pursuant to the Law on Legal Aid dated June 20, 2017;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017, detailing a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 96/2017/ND-CP dated August 16, 2017, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Justice;
At the request of the Director of the Department of Legal Aid;
The Minister of Justice promulgates a Circular guiding the calculation of time according to actual working sessions and the allocation of expenses for legal aid cases.
Chapter I
                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope and subjects of application
1. This Circular provides guidance on calculation of actual working hours and assignment of legal aid cases as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid cases in the form of participation in legal proceedings or representation outside legal proceedings.
2. This Circular applies to the following subjects:
a) Legal aid workers;
b) Lawyers providing legal aid;
c) Organizations signing contracts for legal aid;
d) State legal aid centers (hereinafter referred to as “centers”), branches of centers (hereinafter referred to as “branches”), provincial-level Justice Departments and other relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Article 2. Implementation principles
1. Ensure compliance with the law on remuneration, allowances and procedures for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid cases, and organizations signing legal aid contracts.
2. Ensure the legality and reasonableness of declaration of time and work as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid cases.
3. When declaring the time and work of legal aid cases, the declarant shall be responsible for the authenticity of the declaration.
4. When certifying the time and work of legal aid providers, the certifier shall be responsible for the authenticity of the certification.
Chapter II
               CALCULATION OF TIME BASED ON REAL WORKING SHIFTS
Article 3. Time based on real working shifts
1. Time based on real working shifts (1/2 working day) shall apply to legal aid cases in the form of participation in legal proceedings or non-legal proceedings representation selected by legal aid providers as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid cases.
2. A practical working session shall be calculated on the basis of 04 working hours. In case the legal aid provider works less than 04 hours, the calculation shall be as follows:
a) ½ working day if the working hours are less than 3 hours;
b) If the number of working hours is odd and equal to 03 or more, it shall be counted as 1 working session.

3. When applying the method of calculating working time according to actual working shifts, legal aid providers must record the tasks and time spent on these tasks in the Actual Time-keeping Table (Form TP-TGPL-01) issued together with this Circular.
Article 4. Time limit for participation in criminal proceedings

1. The basis for calculating the time limit for legal aid in criminal proceedings shall include:
a) Participating in questioning of the accused or taking statements of the accused, victim, witness, or involved party in a criminal case;
b) Participating in confrontation, identification, voice recognition, examination of the scene, examination of the corpse, examination of traces on the body, experimental investigation and other procedural tasks at the request of the procedural authority;
c) Working with procedural conductors and procedural-conducting agencies;
d) To verify, collect and evaluate documents, objects and evidence related to the case according to the procedural stages;
dd) To work with the accused at the detention room of the border guard station, detention house, detention camp or prison;
e) To meet with the accused at large or their relatives; victims or their relatives; witnesses, involved parties in criminal cases and other participants in the proceedings;
g) To study dossiers, make copies and prepare documents at the procedural bodies or at the centers, branches and organizations signing contracts for legal aid;
h) To prepare arguments for defense and protection;
i) To participate in court sessions. In case the legal aid provider comes to participate in the court session but the court session is postponed without the legal aid provider's request and without prior notice, the time the legal aid provider works with the procedural body or the procedural body on matters related to the postponement of the court session shall be counted;
k) To perform tasks related to the procedures for appealing, protesting the first-instance judgment, the second-instance judgment, the procedures for cassation or reopening;
l) To perform other reasonable tasks according to the law to serve the defense and protection.
2. The remuneration and allowances for legal aid providers for performing the tasks specified in Points e, g, h and l of Clause 1 of this Article shall not exceed the number of sessions of these tasks at the corresponding charge level specified in Appendix No. 01 enclosed with this Circular.
3. The working time of legal aid providers must be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) The working time for performing the activities specified in Point a, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the procedural conductors or the procedural agencies;
b) The working time for performing the activities specified in Points b and c, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the procedural conductors or the procedural agencies or by the persons working directly with the legal aid providers;
c) The working time for performing the activities specified in Point d, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the agencies, organizations or individuals with whom the legal aid providers work directly;
d) The working time for performing the activities specified in Point dd, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the accused persons or the procedural agencies or the officers of the Border Guard Posts, the Detention Centers, the Pre-trial Detention Centers or the Prisons;
dd) The working time for performing the activities specified in Point e, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the persons working directly with the legal aid providers;
e) The working time for performing the activities specified in Point g, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the procedural conductors or the legal aid providers who have signed contracts with the Centers, Branches or Organizations.
g) The time for carrying out the activities specified in Points h, k, l Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Center, Branch or organization signing the contract for legal aid;
h) The time for carrying out the activities specified in Point i, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or the Court Clerk.
Article 5. Time limit for participation in civil proceedings

1. The basis for calculating the time limit for legal aid in civil proceedings shall include:
a) Working with legal aid recipients and relevant persons to identify the legal relationship in dispute; assessing conditions for filing a lawsuit, counterclaim, independent claim; providing counseling and guidance on writing lawsuits, applications for temporary emergency measures, counterclaims, independent claims, and providing evidence to the court; guiding the preparation of lawsuits or other tasks in the lawsuit filing and case acceptance stage;
b) Working with legal aid recipients, involved parties, relevant agencies, organizations and individuals on the origin, content of disputes, evidences proving or other tasks in the preparation for trial stage;
c) Participate in the meeting to examine the submission, access and disclosure of evidence; verify, collect and assess necessary documents, objects and evidence;
d) Participating in taking statements of involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons; participating in appraisal and valuation of property and performing other procedural tasks at the request of the procedural bodies;
(d) Participating in mediation as prescribed by law;
e) Working with procedural conductors and procedural-conducting agencies according to the stages of the proceedings;
g) Meeting with litigants, witnesses and other participants in the proceedings on other relevant matters;
h) Studying dossiers, making copies and preparing documents at procedural-conducting agencies or at the Center, Branches and organizations signing contracts for legal aid;
i) Preparing arguments for defense;
k) Attending court sessions or civil case settlement meetings. In case the legal aid provider attends a court session but the court session is postponed without the legal aid provider’s request and without prior notice, the time the legal aid provider works with the procedural-conducting agency or procedural-conducting person on matters related to the postponement of the court session shall be counted;
l) Performing tasks related to procedures for appealing or protesting first-instance judgments, second-instance judgments, cassation or reopening procedures;
m) Performing other reasonable tasks according to law provisions to serve the defense.
2. Remuneration and allowances for legal aid providers when performing tasks specified in Points g, h, i and m, Clause 1 of this Article shall be at most equal to the number of sessions of these tasks according to the corresponding case charge level in Appendix No. 02 attached hereto.
3. The working time of the legal aid provider must be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) The working time of the activities specified in Points a and b, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the person directly working with the legal aid provider or the Center, Branch; organization signing the legal aid contract;
b) The working time of the participation in the meeting to examine the submission, access, disclosure of evidence specified in Point c, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the person assigned to handle the case; the working time of the verification, collection, assessment of necessary documents, objects, evidence specified in Point c, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the agency, organization, individual where the legal aid provider goes to verify, collect evidence;
c) The working time of the participation in the taking of statements of litigants, witnesses, other relevant persons specified in Point d, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by these persons or the procedural officer, the procedural agency; the working time of the participation in the appraisal, valuation of property and other procedural work at the request of the procedural agency specified in Point d, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the agency, organization, individual where the property is appraised or valued or the procedural agency, the procedural officer;
d) The working time of the activities specified in Points dd, kk, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or the Court Clerk, the Mediation Clerk;
dd) The working time of the activities specified in Point e, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the procedural agency, the procedural officer;
e) The working time of the activities specified in Point g, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the person directly working with the legal aid provider;
g) The working time of the activities specified in Point h, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the person assigned to handle the case or the Center, Branch, organization signing the legal aid contract;
h) The time for carrying out the activities specified in Points i, l, m Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Center, Branch or organization signing the contract for legal aid.
Article 6. Time for participation in administrative proceedings
1. The basis for calculating the time for providing legal aid for administrative proceedings shall include:

a) Participation in activities specified in Points a, b, c, d, e, g, h, i, k, l, m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) Participation in dialogue.
2. Remuneration and allowances for legal aid providers when performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i, m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular shall be at most equal to the number of sessions for performing these tasks according to the charge level for the corresponding case specified in Appendix No. 03 attached hereto.
3. The working time of legal aid providers shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) The time for carrying out the activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals specified in Clause 3 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) The time for performing the activities specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the judge or the dialogue secretary.
Article 7. Time for participating in extrajudicial representation
1. The basis for calculating the time for providing legal aid for extrajudicial representation cases includes:
a) Meeting and contacting the legal aid recipients and their relatives; witnesses;
b) Studying dossiers and preparing documents for representation;
c) Verifying and collecting documents, objects, evidence, and details related to representation;
d) Working with relevant agencies, organizations, and individuals;
dd) Participating in representation before competent agencies, organizations, and individuals.
2. The working time of the legal aid provider must be certified by the agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) The working time of the activities specified in Points a, d and dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the person working with the legal aid provider or the relevant agency, organization or individual or the agency, organization or individual competent to settle the case;
b) The working time of the activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Center or the Branch or the organization signing the legal aid contract.
Article 8. Methods of determining working time in some special cases
1. In case 02 or more legal aid providers provide legal aid for 01 legal aid recipient in the same case according to the law, the working time serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the legal aid case shall be the actual working time of each legal aid provider, but the total working time shall not exceed 30 working sessions/1 case for the case involving procedural participation or 20 working sessions/1 case for the case involving non-procedural representation.
2. In case the procedural authority suspends the case temporarily:
a) The working time serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the legal aid case shall be the actual working time up to the time of temporary suspension of the case;
b) When the case is continued to be settled, the working time serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the legal aid case shall be the actual working time of the subsequent tasks from the time the case is continued to be settled to the time the case is concluded.
3. In case of replacement or change of the legal aid provider
a) The time serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the legal aid provided to the replaced or changed person shall be the actual time they have provided legal aid up to the time of replacement or change;
b) The time serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the legal aid provided to the appointed replacement shall be the actual time they have performed subsequent tasks from the time of appointment. If the appointed replacement chooses the cost-sharing form, the remuneration and allowances for both persons (the replacement and the replaced or changed person) shall not exceed the corresponding cost-sharing level specified in Appendix No. 01, Appendix No. 02 and Appendix No. 03 attached hereto.
4. In case of providing legal aid to the denounced person, the person proposed to be prosecuted, or the accused at the time of arrest or detention, or the victim according to the law on procedures, the time and certification of the time shall be as follows:
a) The time for verification, collection, and evaluation of necessary documents, objects, and evidence related thereto, certified by the agency, organization, or individual where the legal aid provider is based;
b) The time for taking statements from the denounced person, the person proposed to be prosecuted, the victim, or other relevant persons; participating in confrontation activities, identification, and voice recognition of the denounced person or the person proposed to be prosecuted together with the investigator or procurator certified by the investigator or procurator;
c) The time for working with the accused at the detention room of the border guard station, detention house, or detention camp, certified by the accused or the border guard station officer, detention house officer, detention camp officer, or the procedural authority;
d) The time for meeting with the denounced person, the person proposed to be prosecuted, or their relatives; the victim or their relatives; or other relevant persons certified by these persons, but not exceeding the number of sessions for performing this task in the cost-sharing form specified in Appendix No. 01 attached hereto according to the level of less serious crimes.
Chapter III
                                      DEPUTY CHIEF JUSTICE
Article 9. Assignment of expenses for legal aid cases

1. The assignment of expenses for legal aid cases shall apply only to legal aid cases in the form of participation in legal proceedings, which are selected in writing by the legal aid provider immediately after being assigned and remain unchanged throughout the process of performing the legal aid case.
2. The complexity, procedural requirements and contents of each specific case to determine the chargeable amount of expenses for the case as follows:
a) Apply the provisions on classification of crimes in Clause 2 Article 1 of the Law on amendments to the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 dated June 20, 2017 to determine the chargeable amount for criminal proceedings.
The classification of the crime must be applied immediately at the time of selecting the form of assignment of the case and based on one of the documents issued by the procedural authority: Decision to prosecute the defendant, Investigation conclusion, Death sentence, Sentence. In case there are multiple documents with the same crime but different penalty ranges, the document with the higher penalty range shall be applied.
b) Apply the provisions of Clause 3 Article 337 of the Civil Procedure Code and Article 5 of the Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the fixed charge for legal aid cases in civil proceedings.
c) Apply the provisions of Clause 12 Article 3 of the Law on Administrative Procedures and Article 6 of the Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for identifying complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the chargeable amount for cases involving administrative proceedings.
3. When performing the charge for legal aid services, the legal aid provider shall perform the following tasks:
a) For criminal proceedings: perform the tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 4 of this Circular.
Details of the tasks to be performed and the corresponding maximum charge (converted to working hours) for each case are shown in Appendix No. 01 attached hereto. If the number of working hours specified in Appendix No. 01 is lower than 3 basic salaries, the charge for the case shall be equal to 3 basic salaries.
b) For civil proceedings; administrative proceedings: perform the tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 5 and Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.
Details of the tasks to be performed and the corresponding maximum charge (converted to working hours) for each case are shown in Appendix No. 02 and Appendix No. 03 attached hereto. If the number of working hours specified in Appendix No. 02 and Appendix No. 03 is lower than 3 basic salaries, the charge for the case shall be equal to 3 basic salaries.
4. When performing the charge for legal aid services, the legal aid provider shall list the tasks and certify the tasks performed in the Task List (Form TP-TGPL-02) attached hereto.
5. In case a legal aid case is handled through multiple procedural stages specified in the appendices attached hereto, the charge for the case shall be applied according to the stages but the maximum charge shall not exceed 10 basic salaries.
Article 10. Charge for legal aid services in some special cases
1. In case 02 or more legal aid providers provide legal aid for 01 legal aid recipient in the same case, the actual work of each provider shall be based on the maximum work charge equal to 130% of the corresponding charge shown in the appendices enclosed with this Circular but not exceeding 10 basic salaries.

Example 01: A is prosecuted for a crime with a penalty bracket of a very serious crime. The Director of the Legal Aid Center appoints 02 persons to provide legal aid to A from the investigation stage to the first-instance trial stage. Based on the actual work done by the 02 persons providing legal aid, the maximum chargeable cost of the case is: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number of sessions exceeds 10 basic salaries, the chargeable cost of the case is only 10 basic salaries.
2. In case of providing legal aid to 01 legal aid recipient who is prosecuted for 02 or more offenses in the same case, the chargeable amount for the legal aid provider shall be as follows:
a) If two offenses have penalty scales of two different types of crimes, based on actual work performed, the maximum charge for the case shall be 130% of the charge applicable to the case with the penalty scale of the higher type of crime specified in Appendix No. 01 attached hereto but not exceeding 10 basic salaries.
Example 02: A is prosecuted for 2 offenses: 1 offense with a penalty bracket of serious, 1 offense with a penalty bracket of very serious. The legal assistant provides legal aid to A from the investigation stage to the first-instance trial stage. Based on the actual work done, the maximum chargeable cost of the case is: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number of sessions exceeds 10 basic salaries, the chargeable cost of the case is only 10 basic salaries.
b) If two offenses have the same penalty bracket, based on the actual work done, the maximum chargeable amount for the case shall be 130% of the chargeable amount applicable to that case, which is shown in Appendix No. 01 attached hereto, but not exceeding 10 basic salaries.

Example 03: B is prosecuted for two offenses, both of which have the same penalty bracket for very serious offenses. The legal assistant provides legal assistance to B from the investigation stage to the first-instance trial stage. Based on the actual work done, the maximum chargeable amount for the case is not more than: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number of sessions exceeds 10 basic salaries, the chargeable amount for the case is only 10 basic salaries.
3. In case the procedural body temporarily suspends the case
a) The charge for the case of the legal aid provider shall be based on the actual work that the legal aid provider has performed up to the date of temporary suspension;
b) When the case is continued to be settled, the charge for the case of the legal aid provider shall be based on the work that the legal aid provider has performed from the date of continued settlement to the date of case completion.
4. In case of replacement or change of the legal aid provider
a) The charge for the case of the replaced or changed person shall be based on the actual work that the replaced or changed person has performed legal aid up to the date of replacement or change;
b) The charge for the case of the appointed replacement person shall be based on the actual work that this person has performed subsequent works from the date of appointment.
If the appointed replacement person chooses the form of time according to actual working sessions, the remuneration and allowances for both persons (the replacement person and the replaced or changed person) shall not exceed 30 working sessions/1 case according to the provisions of Clause 1 Article 13 of the Government’s Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 detailing a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid.
Chapter IV
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 11. Transitional provisions
For activities arising before the effective date of this Circular, the provisions of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice guiding the calculation of time for implementation and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice amending and supplementing a number of articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice guiding the calculation of time for implementation and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid cases shall continue to apply.
Article 12. Effect
1. This Circular takes effect from July 15, 2021.
2. This Circular replaces Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice guiding the calculation of time for implementation and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice amending and supplementing a number of articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice guiding the calculation of time for implementation and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid cases.
