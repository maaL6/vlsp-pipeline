                                                    DECREE

    GOVERNMENT

No. 88/2003/ND-CP

of July 30, 2003

ON THE ORGANIZATION, ACTIVITIES AND MANAGEMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

                                                    THE GOVERNMENT

  Pursuant to the Decree No. 102/SL/L004 of May 20, 1957 promulgating the Law on the right to form associations; and the Civil Code of October 28, 1995;
  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;
  At the request of the Minister of Home Affairs,

                                                    DECREE

  Chapter 1:

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for the organization, operation and state management of associations.

  2. This Decree does not apply to the following organizations:

  a) The Vietnam Fatherland Front, the Vietnam General Confederation of Labor, the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, the Vietnam Peasants’ Union, the Vietnam War Veterans Union, the Vietnam Women’s Union;

  b) Religious organizations.

  Article 2. Associations

  1. Associations defined in this Decree are understood as voluntary organizations of citizens, Vietnamese organizations of the same profession, interest and gender, sharing the common purpose of uniting and rallying members, regularly operating without profit motives in order to protect the lawful rights and interests of their members; supporting each other in effective operation, contributing to the socio-economic development of the country, and being organized and operated in accordance with this Decree and other relevant legal documents.

  2. Associations may have different names: Union, General Association, Federation, Association, Club with legal personality and other names as prescribed by law (hereinafter referred to as associations).

  3. Scope of operation of the association (by territory) includes:

  a) National or inter-provincial scope of operation;

  b) Provincial scope of operation;

  c) District scope of operation;

  d) Commune scope of operation.

  Article 3. Establishment of associations and principles of operation of associations

  1. Establishment of associations must be approved by competent regulatory agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  2. Associations shall be organized and operate according to the association charter approved by competent regulatory agencies and in compliance with law; associations shall organize and operate according to the principles of voluntariness, self-governance, self-financing and shall be accountable to law.

  Article 4. Responsibilities of state agencies towards associations

  1. State agencies shall, within the scope of their functions and tasks, create favorable conditions for associations to operate in accordance with their charters and effectively.

  2. Associations recognized as socio-political organizations, socio-political professional organizations, or associations with activities related to the tasks of the State shall be supported by the State budget according to regulations of the Prime Minister.

  Article 5. Legal personality, seals, names and symbols of associations

  1. Associations shall have legal personality, their own seals and accounts.

  2. Associations shall be entitled to choose their names and symbols according to regulations of law.

  Chapter II

                                    CONDITIONS AND PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHING ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 6. Conditions for establishment of associations

  1. The association must have objectives that are not contrary to law; and its name and main field of operation must not be identical to that of an association that has been legally established in the same territory.

  2. The association must have a charter.

  3. The association must have a headquarters.

  4. The association must have enough registered members.

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall prescribe the number of registered members of an association.

  Article 7. The founding committee

  1. The founding committee must be established by the founders of an association. The founding committee must be recognized by the regulatory authority in charge of the sector or field in which the association is expected to operate.

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall specify the number of members of the association establishment mobilization committee.

  2. After being recognized, the association establishment mobilization committee shall perform the following tasks:

  a) Mobilize citizens and organizations to join the association and complete the application for permission to establish the association;

  b) The head of the association establishment mobilization committee shall send the application for permission to establish the association to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 8. Application for permission to establish the association

  1. Application for permission to establish the association.

  2. Draft charter.

  3. Expected operational direction.

  4. List of persons in the Association establishment promotion board recognized by competent state agencies.

  5. Curriculum vitae of the head of the Association establishment promotion board certified by competent agencies.

  6. Documents defining the Association’s headquarters and assets.

  Article 9. Main contents of the Association’s charter

  1. Name of the Association.

  2. Motto, purpose, field and scope of operation of the Association.

  3. Tasks and powers of the Association.

  4. Principles of organization and operation.

  5. Procedures for joining and leaving the association.

  6. Membership criteria.

  7. Rights and obligations of members.

  8. Structure, organization, election and dismissal procedures; duties and powers of the leadership board, the inspection board and other leadership positions of the association.

  9. Assets, finances and methods of managing assets and finances of the association.

  10. Conditions for dissolution and settlement of assets and finances.

  11. Commendation and handling of violations.

  12. Procedures for amendment and supplementation of the charter.

  13. Effect.

  Article 10. Responsibilities of competent state agencies in granting permission to establish associations upon receipt of application for permission to establish associations

  Competent state agencies prescribed in Article 15 of this Decree shall have to issue receipt papers upon receipt of application for permission to establish associations. If the application is complete and lawful, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application, the competent state agency shall have to reply to the association establishment mobilization committee; in case of refusal, it shall have to reply in writing and clearly state the reason.

  Article 11. Time limit for holding the association establishment congress

  1. Within 90 days from the date on which the decision to grant permission to establish associations takes effect, the association establishment mobilization committee shall have to hold the congress.

  2. If the above-mentioned time limit is not observed, the association establishment mobilization committee shall send a written request to the state agency that has granted permission to establish associations for extension. The extension period shall not exceed 30 days. If the congress is not held within the extended period, the decision to grant permission to establish associations shall become invalid.

  Article 12. Main contents of the association establishment congress

  1. Announcement of the decision to grant permission to establish associations.

  2. Discuss and vote on the Charter.

  3. Elect the leadership board and the examination board according to the regulations of the Charter.

  4. Approve the program of the association's operation.

  5. Approve the resolution of the Congress.

  Article 13. Reporting on the results of the Congress

  Within 30 days after the Congress, the leadership board of the association shall send the documents of the Congress to the state agency that has decided to permit the establishment of the association, including:

  1. The Charter and the minutes of the approval of the Charter;

  2. The minutes of the election of the leadership board and the examination board (with the list attached) and the biographies of the leaders of the association;

  3. Programs of the association;

  4. Resolutions of the Congress.

  Article 14. Approval of the association’s charter and the effectiveness of the association’s charter

  1. The competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall decide to approve the association’s charter when the association’s charter has been passed by the Congress. If the provisions of the association’s charter are contrary to law, the competent state agency shall refuse to approve and request the association to amend the charter.

  2. The association’s charter shall take effect from the date on which the competent state agency decides to approve it.

  Article 15. Competent state agencies shall permit the establishment, division, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the association’s charter

  1. The Minister of Home Affairs shall permit the establishment, division, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the charter for associations operating nationwide or inter-provincially.

  2. The President of the People’s Committee of the province shall permit the establishment, division, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the charter for associations operating within the province.

  Chapter 3:

                                                    MEMBERS

  Article 16. Conditions for becoming a member

  Citizens and organizations of Vietnam that meet the criteria for membership as prescribed in the Charter and voluntarily apply for membership may become members of the association.

  The membership rights and procedures shall be prescribed in the Charter of the association.

  Article 17. Rights and obligations of members

  The rights and obligations of members are prescribed in the Charter of the association.

  Article 18. Associated members and honorary members

  1. Joint venture enterprises and enterprises with 100% foreign investment (hereinafter referred to as enterprises with foreign elements) operating in Vietnam, which contribute to the development of the association and approve the association’s charter, may be recognized as associate members by the association of economic organizations.

  Associate members may participate in the association’s activities and attend its congress. Associate members shall not vote for or run for the association’s leadership board and shall not vote on association’s issues.

  The procedures for admission of associate members shall be prescribed by the association’s charter.

  2. Vietnamese citizens and organizations that are not eligible to become members of the association but contribute to the association may be recognized by the association as associate members or honorary members. The rights and obligations of associate members and honorary members shall be prescribed by the association’s charter.

  Chapter IV

                          ORGANIZATION, ACTIVITIES, RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF ASSOCIATION

  Article 19. Term congress and extraordinary congress

  1. The association’s highest leadership body is the general congress or the representative congress.

  2. The term of the Congress shall be prescribed by the Charter of the association but not more than 5 years.

  3. The extraordinary Congress shall be convened when at least 2/3 (two-thirds) of the total number of members of the Executive Board or at least 1/2 (one-half) of the total number of official members request it.

  Article 20. Main contents to be decided at the Congress

  1. The direction of the association's operation.

  2. The election of the association's leadership board and examination board.

  3. The change of the association's name and amendment of the Charter (if any).

  4. The joining of the Federation of Associations of the same field of operation.

  5. The division, separation, merger, consolidation and dissolution of the association.

  6. Finances of the association.

  Article 21. Voting principles at the congress

  1. The congress may vote by raising hands or by secret ballot. The voting form shall be decided by the congress.

  2. The voting on decisions of the congress must be approved by more than half (1/2) of the officially present delegates.

  Article 22. Rights of the association

  1. To propagate the purpose of the association.

  2. To represent the members in internal and external relations related to the functions and tasks of the association.

  3. To protect the lawful rights and interests of the association and its members.

  4. To organize and coordinate the activities of the members for the common interests of the association; to mediate disputes within the association.

  5. To disseminate and train the knowledge for the members; to provide necessary information for the members according to the provisions of law.

  6. To provide consultancy and criticism on the issues within the scope of the association's activities at the request of organizations and individuals.

  7. To participate in the opinions on the legal documents related to the association's activities according to the provisions of law. To make recommendations to the competent state agencies on the issues related to the development of the association and the field of the association's activities.

  8. To coordinate with the relevant agencies and organizations to perform the tasks of the association.

  9. To raise the association's funds based on the association fees of the members and the sources of income from business and service activities according to the provisions of law to cover the association's operating expenses.

  10. To receive legitimate sources of funding from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals according to the provisions of law.

  11. Associations operating nationwide or inter-provincially may join as members of international and regional associations according to the provisions of the Government's Decree No. 20/2002/ND-CP dated February 20, 2002 on the signing and implementation of international agreements of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government, the socio-political organizations, the social organizations and the socio-professional organizations of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  Article 23. Obligations of the association

  1. The association’s activities must comply with the approved charter.

  2. The association’s field of operation shall be subject to the state management of the state management agency of that field.

  3. Before organizing the congress, the 30-day term, the association’s leadership board must submit a written report to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree and the ministry managing the field of operation of the association.

  4. The establishment of the association’s representative office in another locality must be approved by the People’s Committee of the province where the representative office is located and reported in writing to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  5. When changing the association’s president, vice-president, general secretary, changing the association’s headquarters, amending or supplementing the charter, the association must report to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  6. The establishment of the association’s legal entities must comply with the law and be reported to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  7. Annually, the association must report on the organization and operation of the association to the competent state agency and the state management agency of the field of operation of the association, by December 1st of each year.

  8. To comply with the guidance, inspection and supervision of competent state agencies in the observance of law.

  9. The list of members, branch offices, affiliated units, financial documents of the association, meeting minutes of the association’s leadership board shall be compiled into a dossier and kept at the association’s headquarters.

  10. Funds collected under Clauses 9 and 10 Article 22 of this Decree shall be used for association activities as prescribed in the association’s charter and shall not be distributed to members.

  11. The use of association funds must comply with law provisions. Annually, the association must submit a financial settlement report as prescribed by the State and send it to the financial agency of the same level.

  Chapter V

                             ASSOCIATION DIVISION, MERGER, MERGE, AND DISOLUTION

  Article 24. Division, merger, merge, and dissolution

  1. Depending on the association’s requirements and operational capacity, the association’s leadership board shall request the competent state agency prescribed in Article 15 of this Decree to permit the association to be divided, merged, merged, or dissolved. The division, merger, merge, and dissolution of the association shall comply with law provisions.

  2. The association shall be dissolved in the following cases:

  a) On its own initiative;

  b) At the decision of the competent authority specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 25. Association dissolution

  The association shall be dissolved in the following cases:

  1. The duration of operation has expired;

  2. At the request of more than half of the total number of official members;

  3. The objectives have been achieved.

  Article 26. Responsibilities of the leadership board of a dissolved association

  1. The leadership board shall send the following documents to the competent authority prescribed in Article 15 of this Decree:

  a) An application for dissolution of the association;

  b) A resolution on dissolution of the association;

  c) A list of assets and finances;

  d) An estimate of the method of disposal of assets and finances and the time limit for payment of debts.

  2. The leadership board shall notify the time limit for payment of debts (if any) to relevant organizations and individuals in accordance with the law, in 5 consecutive central newspapers for associations operating nationwide and inter-provincially; and in local newspapers for associations operating within a province.

  Article 27. Decision on dissolution of an association

  The competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall decide to dissolve the association 15 days after the end of the debt payment and liquidation of assets and finances specified in the association's notice when applying for dissolution without any complaint.

  The association shall cease its operation from the date on which the decision of the competent state agency dissolving the association takes effect.

  Article 28. Association dissolution

  The association shall be dissolved according to the decision of the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree in the following cases:

  1. The association has not operated continuously for 12 months;

  2. There is a resolution of the congress on dissolution but the association's leadership fails to comply with it;

  3. The association's operation seriously violates the law.

  Article 29. Responsibilities of the competent state agency when the association is dissolved

  Upon the dissolution of a society, the competent state agency prescribed in Article 15 of this Decree shall:

  a) Issue a decision on the dissolution of the society;

  b) Notify the decision on the dissolution of the society on the mass media.

  Article 30. Settlement of property and finances upon the dissolution, merger, consolidation, division or separation of a society

  1. Upon the self-dissolution, dissolution of a society, its property shall be settled as follows:

  a) Property and finances sponsored by domestic and foreign organizations; property and finances supported by the State, if the society has fully fulfilled its property obligations and paid its debts, the remaining property and balance of finances shall be decided by the competent state agency;

  b) For property and finances of the society that it has acquired on its own, if the society has fully fulfilled its property obligations and paid its debts after its dissolution, the remaining property and balance of finances shall be decided by the society in accordance with the provisions of its charter.

  2. Settlement of property and finances of a society upon its merger:

  a) After the merger, the old associations shall cease to exist, and the new associations shall enjoy the lawful rights and benefits and assume responsibility for the outstanding debts and service contracts of the old associations;

  b) The assets and finances of the merged associations shall not be divided or transferred but shall be transferred entirely to the new associations.

  3. Settlement of assets and finances of associations upon merger:

  a) The assets and finances of the associations that are merged into other associations shall be transferred to the merging associations;

  b) The merging associations shall enjoy the lawful rights and benefits regarding the existing assets and finances of the merged associations, assume responsibility for the outstanding debts regarding the assets and finances and the service contracts that are being executed.

  4. Settlement of assets and finances of associations upon division or separation:

  a) After the division, the associations that are divided shall cease to operate, and the rights and obligations regarding the assets and finances shall be transferred to the new associations according to the decision on division;

  b) After the separation, the associations shall exercise their rights and obligations regarding their assets and finances in accordance with the operational purposes of such associations.

  Article 31. Right to lodge complaints

  In case a society is dissolved without its consent, it may lodge a complaint according to law. During the waiting period for complaint settlement, the society shall not operate.

  Chapter 6:

                                      STATE MANAGEMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 32. State management of associations

  1. To draft or promulgate legal documents on associations.

  2. To guide ministries, sectors, local authorities, associations and citizens in implementing the law on associations.

  3. Permit the establishment, division, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the association’s charter in accordance with Article 15 of this Decree.

  4. To provide professional guidance to officials and civil servants in charge of managing associations.

  5. To propagate and disseminate laws on associations.

  6. To inspect and examine the observance of laws on associations; to examine the implementation of association charters for associations.

  7. To manage the signing of international cooperation agreements on associations in accordance with law.

  8. To settle complaints and denunciations and handle violations of laws on associations.

  9. To compile and report on the organization, operation and management of associations.

  The Ministry of Home Affairs shall assist the Government in maintaining the unified state management of associations nationwide.

  Article 33. State management tasks of ministries and ministerial-level agencies over associations operating in the fields under their management nationwide

  1. To submit written applications to competent state agencies under Article 15 of this Decree for permission to establish, divide, merge, consolidate, dissolve, approve association charters, and recognize founding committees.

  2. To guide and facilitate associations in participating in activities under the management of the Ministry in accordance with law; to organize the collection of opinions of associations for improvement of state management regulations on the industry/field.

  3. To inspect the implementation of state management regulations on the industry/field by associations, and to handle or propose competent state agencies to handle violations in accordance with law.

  Article 34. State management tasks of provincial People’s Committees for associations operating within the province

  1. To exercise the competence under Clause 2 Article 15 of this Decree and to exercise state management over the organization and operation of associations.

  2. To inspect and examine the observance of law on associations.

  3. To settle complaints and denunciations and handle violations of law on associations.

  4. To consider providing support to associations operating within the locality.

  5. Consider and permit local associations operating within the local area to receive sponsorship from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals in accordance with law.

  6. Direct the Departments, Boards, sectors, People’s Committees of districts and communes in managing associations.

  7. Annually, compile and report to the Ministry of Home Affairs on the situation of association organization, operation and management in local areas.

  Chapter VII

                                    PROMOTION AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 35. Promotion

  1. Associations that make many contributions to socio-economic development shall be promoted in accordance with the State’s regulations.

  2. Association members who make many achievements shall be promoted in accordance with the association’s regulations and the State’s regulations.

  Article 36. Handling of violations

  1. Any person who violates the right to establish associations or abuses the name of associations to engage in illegal activities shall, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, be disciplined, administratively sanctioned, or criminally prosecuted according to law; in case of material damage, compensation shall be made according to law.

  2. Any person who abuses his/her position or power to permit the establishment of associations contrary to this Decree shall, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, be disciplined, administratively sanctioned, or criminally prosecuted according to law; in case of material damage, compensation shall be made according to law.

  3. The leadership of associations or representatives of associations who deliberately prolong the term of term conventions as prescribed by association charters or fail to comply with regulations on obligations of associations shall, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, be handled according to law.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 37. Effect

  1. This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

  2. This Decree replaces Decree No. 258/TTG of June 14, 1957 of the Prime Minister on the detailed implementation of the Law No. 102/SL/L004 of May 20, 1957 on the right to establish associations.

  3. Associations which were permitted to be established by the Ministry of the Interior and the provincial administrative commissions under the 1957 Decree No. 102/SL/L004 and the Chairman of the Council of Ministers (now the Prime Minister) or the Minister, the Head of the Government's Organization and Personnel Committee (now the Minister of the Interior) or the President of the provincial or centrally-run city People's Committee may be permitted to be established under the 1989 Directive No. 01/CT on the management, organization and operation of mass associations without having to re-apply for establishment.

  4. Associations of economic organizations may be established under the 1989 Decision No. 38/HDBT of the Council of Ministers (now the Government) on economic linkages in production, circulation and services without having to re-apply for establishment.

  Article 38. Implementation

  The Minister of the Interior shall guide the implementation of this Decree.

  The Ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies and the agencies attached to the Government, the Presidents of the provincial and centrally-run city People's Committees shall be responsible for the implementation of this Decree.
