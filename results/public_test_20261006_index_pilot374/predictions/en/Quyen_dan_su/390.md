                                               CIRCULAR

     ON THE ISSUE OF LEGAL IDENTITY CARDS

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 38-CP of June 4, 1993, on the functions, tasks, powers and organization of the Ministry of Justice;
  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 37/1998/ND-CP of June 9, 1998, on the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;
  To meet the citizens' request for issuance of judicial record cards;
  The Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Public Security shall issue the following regulations on the issuance of judicial record cards:

  I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. Judicial record form A judicial record form is a form issued by a competent state agency specified in Point 5, Section I of this Joint Circular to a person who requests it to confirm whether or not he/she has a criminal record.

  The Judicial Record Form is Form No. 01/TP-LLTP, which is issued together with this Joint Circular.

  2. Principles for confirming the presence or absence of criminal records

  A person shall be considered to have a criminal record only if there is a legally effective criminal judgment of the court that has convicted him/her of a crime and his/her criminal record has not been erased according to law.

  3. Right to request issuance of judicial record cards Vietnamese citizens have the right to request competent state agencies to issue judicial record cards for use in necessary cases as prescribed by law.

  Foreigners also have the right to request competent state agencies of Vietnam specified in point 5 section I of this Joint Circular to issue judicial record cards to confirm whether or not they have criminal records during their stay in Vietnam.

  The applicant for issuance of judicial record cards may authorize another person to act on his/her behalf in carrying out procedures for issuance of judicial record cards.

  4. Cases in which judicial record cards are not issued The following cases are not eligible for issuance of judicial record cards:

  a/ Request for issuance of judicial record cards for other persons without legitimate authorization;

  b/ Persons who are defendants in criminal cases.

  5. Competence to issue judicial record cards The issuance of judicial record cards is within the competence of provincial/municipal Justice Services (hereinafter referred to as “provincial/municipal Justice Services”).

  Provincial/municipal Justice Services shall coordinate with provincial/municipal police authorities (hereinafter referred to as “provincial/municipal police authorities”) and, where necessary, with courts in verifying the judicial records of involved parties.

  6. Fees

  The applicant for the issuance of a judicial record must pay a fee as prescribed by law.

  II. PROCEDURES AND ORDER OF GRANTING OF LEGAL PERSONALITY CERTIFICATES

  1. Procedures for submission and receipt of applications for issuance of judicial record certificates

  a. Application for issuance of judicial record card The applicant for issuance of judicial record card must make an application using Form No. 02/TP-LLTP issued together with this Joint Circular; the application for issuance of judicial record card must be fully, clearly and accurately filled out.

  The application must be accompanied by a copy of the ID card and a copy of the household registration book or a valid document proving the applicant’s permanent residence; for foreigners, a copy of the passport and a copy of the certificate of permanent or temporary residence in Vietnam.

  In case of authorization to carry out procedures for request for issuance of judicial record, a written authorization must be certified by the People’s Committee of commune, ward or town where the authorizing person or authorized person resides; if the authorizing person is a Vietnamese citizen residing abroad, the written authorization must be certified by the diplomatic mission or consular mission of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam abroad; if the authorizing person is a foreigner who has left Vietnam, the written authorization must be certified by the competent authority of the country of which he/she is a citizen or permanent resident and must be consularly legalized according to the regulations of Vietnamese law.

  The application for issuance of a judicial record shall be made in 2 sets.

  b. Submission of application for issuance of judicial record card The applicant for issuance of judicial record card who is a Vietnamese citizen residing in Vietnam shall submit the application to the Department of Justice of the province where he/she permanently resides; if he/she resides abroad, he/she shall submit the application to the Department of Justice of the province where he/she permanently resides before his/her departure.

  A foreigner residing in Vietnam who applies for a judicial record certificate shall submit his/her application to the Department of Justice of the province where he/she resides; if he/she has left Vietnam, he/she shall submit his/her application to the Department of Justice of the province where he/she last resided before leaving Vietnam.

  c. Receipt of application

  Upon receiving the application, the Department of Justice shall check the contents of the application and the validity of the enclosed documents; if there are any deficiencies or unclear points, the applicant shall be requested to supplement or clarify them.

  If the application is deemed valid, the Department of Justice shall receive and collect the fee and issue a date of result return to the applicant.

  In case the Department of Justice deems that the case is not under its jurisdiction, it shall immediately guide the involved parties to submit the application at the correct address.

  The receipt of application must be recorded in the register of issuance of judicial record cards.

  2. Procedures for searching and verifying criminal records

  a. Search the police agency’s dossier Within 3 days from the date of receipt of a valid dossier, the Department of Justice shall send a judicial record verification form No. 03/TP-LLTP issued together with this Joint Circular and 01 dossier to the provincial police agency for search.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the judicial record certificate, the provincial police authority shall conduct the search and verification of the applicant’s judicial record and send the results of the judicial record verification (on the back of the judicial record certificate) to the provincial Justice Department. In complex cases where the search of the Police Records Department of the Ministry of Public Security is required, this time limit shall be extended by no more than 10 days; the provincial police authority shall be responsible for contacting the Police Records Department of the Ministry of Public Security.

  b. Search the court’s dossier

  In case the records of the police agency show that there are suspicious circumstances that the involved person has a criminal record, but there are not enough grounds to conclude or the part of the record about the involved person’s prior conviction has unclear or incomplete details, the Justice Department shall contact the court that has tried the case involving the involved person to check the criminal record, in order to clarify whether the involved person has a criminal record or not.

  The Court’s record search (if any) must be conducted immediately after the date of receipt of the notification of the result of the judicial record verification by the provincial police; the record search period for the record kept at the Court shall not exceed 7 days.

  3. Preparation and issuance of judicial record cards Within 3 days from the date on which the police and court (if any) have completed the search of the applicant’s dossier, the provincial/municipal Justice Department shall prepare and issue judicial record cards to the applicant.

  a. For a person with no prior convictions (no criminal record), the judicial record form must clearly state "No prior convictions" in the "Prior convictions" section and have the columns (1), (2), (3), (4) and (5) crossed out. If the person has prior convictions, the offense, applicable law, principal penalty, additional penalties (if any), and the date of the legally effective judgment must be clearly stated; if there are multiple prior convictions, the order of the prior convictions must be stated in chronological order.

  In case the involved person has a criminal record but meets the conditions for automatic or conditional erasure of criminal record as prescribed by the criminal law, the Department of Justice shall guide the involved person to apply for the competent People’s Court to erase the criminal record as prescribed by the criminal procedure law.

  b. Grant of judicial record cards There is only one original of a judicial record card, and it is not permitted to be copied. Depending on the purpose for which the judicial record card is requested, the provincial-level Justice Department shall grant the applicant the necessary number of judicial record cards.

  4. Preservation of judicial record dossiers Judicial record dossiers include applications for judicial record cards, documents enclosed by applicants, and judicial record verification cards containing the results of the search of dossiers of police authorities and the results of the search of dossiers of courts (if any).

  Judicial record dossiers must be preserved and stored at the provincial-level Justice Department in accordance with the law on preservation.

  III. ORGANIZATION OF IMPLEMENTATION

  1. The Minister of Justice and the Minister of Public Security shall have to direct and guide their respective agencies in the implementation of this Joint Circular.

  2. Provincial-level Justice Departments and police authorities shall assign competent officials to perform judicial record work; for provinces and cities with many requests for judicial record cards, they shall assign full-time officials to perform this work.

  3. Every 6 months and annually, the provincial-level Justice Department shall report to the Ministry of Justice, and the provincial-level police authority shall report to the Ministry of Public Security on the implementation of this Joint Circular.

  4. This Joint Circular takes effect 15 days after its signing.

  During the implementation, if there are any problems, the Director of the Department of Justice and the Director of the Provincial Police Department shall report to the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Public Security for consideration and settlement.
