                                                   DECREE

                                     REGULATIONS ON FAMILY WORK

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;

  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated June 9, 2000;

  Pursuant to the Law on Gender Equality dated November 29, 2006;

  Pursuant to the Law on Prevention and Control of Domestic Violence dated November 21, 2007;

  At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;

  The Government promulgates the Decree on family affairs.

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Decree provides for family work; responsibilities of agencies and organizations in carrying out family work.

  Family work is the activities of agencies and organizations aimed at building a warm, equal, progressive, happy and sustainable family.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Decree applies to agencies and organizations in carrying out family work.

  Article 3. State management agencies in charge of family work

  1. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall be responsible for the state management of family work nationwide.

  2. Ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in carrying out the state management of family work.

  3. People’s Committees at all levels shall exercise the state management over family affairs in their localities.

  Chapter II

                                      FAMILY WORK CONTENT

  Article 4. Formulation and implementation of policies and laws on family work

  To formulate and implement policies and laws on family; equality in family; prevention and combat of family violence and social evils that penetrate into family.

  Article 5. Planning, training and retraining of cadres working in family work

  1. To formulate and implement planning of cadres working in family work at all levels.

  2. To train and retrain cadres working in family work to improve their capacity; to compile documents on family work.

  3. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Ministry of Home Affairs shall elaborate Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 6. Propagating, mobilizing and disseminating knowledge on family work

  1. To propagate and disseminate the Party’s guidelines, policies and laws of the State on family, equality in family, prevention and combat of family violence and prevention and control of social evils that penetrate into family, and implementation of family planning.

  2. To develop documents and disseminate knowledge on family, mobilize people to eliminate outdated customs and practices on marriage and family; inherit, preserve and promote the good traditional cultural values of family and clan, which are associated with building advanced family values in a developing society.

  Article 7. Organizations and agencies providing public services in the field of family

  1. The provision of public services in the field of family shall be carried out by public service units and enterprises.

  2. Conditions, procedures, competence for establishment, reorganization and dissolution of public service units and enterprises providing public services in the field of family shall comply with the Government’s Decree No. 55/2012/ND-CP dated June 28, 2012 on establishment, reorganization and dissolution of public service units, the Enterprise Law and relevant legal provisions.

  Article 8. Activities supporting family building

  1. To provide pre-marriage counseling and education; provide knowledge and skills for building and organizing family life; appropriately integrate family life education into curricula at all levels; support the maintenance and preservation of good traditional family values.

  2. To mediate family conflicts and disputes and protect the lawful rights and interests of family members.

  3. To develop and guide the replication of happy and sustainable family models.

  4. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Education and Training, the Ministry of Justice and relevant ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall elaborate Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Celebration of Vietnam Family Day

  Every year, Vietnam Family Day (June 28) shall be celebrated to promote the responsibility of leaders of all sectors, levels, mass organizations, social organizations and families to build warm, equal, progressive, happy and sustainable families; to encourage and promptly commend organizations, individuals and families that have outstanding achievements in family work.

  Article 10. Inspection, examination and handling of violations against laws on family work

  Organize inspection, examination and handling of violations against laws on family work in accordance with law.

  Article 11. Scientific research and development of family databases and family work

  1. Organize investigation, survey and scientific research activities to serve the development of policies and laws on family work.

  2. Develop family databases and family work databases.

  3. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall elaborate Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. International cooperation

  To cooperate with other countries, international organizations and non-governmental organizations in developing policies, management experience, scientific research, exchanging experts, providing technical and financial support in the field of family according to law.

  Article 13. Initial and final reports, information and reporting regime

  1. People’s Committees at all levels shall organize initial and final reports and evaluate the implementation of family work activities in their localities and report to superior authorities.

  2. Annually, before December 15, relevant ministries and branches and People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall send reports on the implementation of family work to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for synthesis and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  Chapter III

                                             IMPLEMENTING ORGANIZATION

  Article 14. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism

  1. To formulate and implement policies and laws on family.

  2. To provide guidance and organize training and retraining to enhance the capacity of family affairs cadres.

  3. To provide guidance and propagate, mobilize and disseminate policies, laws and knowledge on family affairs.

  4. To develop and guide the replication of family models.

  5. To exercise state management over public service-providing units in the family affairs sector.

  6. To provide guidance and organize the celebration of Vietnam Family Day.

  7. To cooperate internationally and conduct scientific research on family affairs and family affairs according to law.

  8. Develop databases on families and family work.

  9. Inspect, examine and handle violations against regulations on family work.

  10. Periodically report on the implementation of family work to the Prime Minister.

  Article 15. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Planning and Investment

  Preside over and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in guiding and integrating policies on family work into strategies, planning and plans for socio-economic development.

  Article 16. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Finance

  Preside over and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and relevant ministries and branches in allocating funds for family work according to the current state budget allocation; guide and inspect relevant ministries, branches and localities in allocating and using funds for family work.

  Article 17. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Justice

  1. To assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and relevant agencies in formulating policies and laws on family affairs within the scope of assigned functions and tasks.

  2. To provide guidance on mediation of family conflicts and disputes in accordance with law.

  Article 18. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Education and Training

  To assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Vietnam Women’s Union in integrating family education into the education programs of all levels.

  Article 19. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Home Affairs

  To coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in providing guidance on planning and strengthening the cadre team for family affairs at all levels.

  Article 20. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs

  To assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and relevant ministries and agencies in formulating policies and laws on protection, care and education of children in families, care and support of elderly people in families, and prevention and combat of social evils that penetrate into families within the scope of assigned functions and tasks.

  Article 21. Responsibilities of ministries and ministerial agencies

  1. To assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in implementing the contents related to family work within the scope, functions, tasks and powers assigned.

  2. To promulgate and submit to competent authorities for promulgation policies, laws, programs and schemes related to family work; to guide, inspect, examine, make preliminary and final assessments of the implementation of policies, laws, programs and schemes related to family work under their management.

  3. To annually make assessments of the implementation of tasks related to family work under state management by ministries and branches, and send them to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for synthesis and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  Article 22. Responsibilities of People’s Committees at all levels

  1. To organize and inspect the implementation of policies and laws on family work in localities.

  2. To formulate and implement family work programs and plans in conjunction with local socio-economic development programs.

  3. To allocate budgets for family work in accordance with regulations of law on state budget.

  Article 23. Participation in the state management of family affairs of the women’s unions at all levels

  The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Governmental agencies and People’s Committees at all levels shall ensure that the Women’s Union at the same level participates in the state management of family affairs in accordance with law and Articles 4 and 6, Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of Article 8 and Article 9 of this Decree.

  Article 24. Participation in family work

  The Central Committee of the Vietnam Fatherland Front and its member organizations are requested to participate in the dissemination, popularization and education of policies and laws, and to inspect and supervise the implementation of policies and laws on family affairs in accordance with law.

  Article 25. Funds for family work

  1. Funds for family work activities of ministries, branches and People’s Committees at all levels shall be allocated from the state budget in accordance with law. The preparation of estimates and allocation of funds for family work activities shall comply with the Law on State Budget and relevant documents.

  2. Funds raised by domestic and foreign organizations and individuals for family affairs shall be managed and used in accordance with law.

  Chapter 4.

                                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 26. Effect

  This Decree takes effect as of February 18, 2013.

  Article 27. Implementation responsibilities

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies, chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities, and relevant agencies and organizations shall implement this Decree.
