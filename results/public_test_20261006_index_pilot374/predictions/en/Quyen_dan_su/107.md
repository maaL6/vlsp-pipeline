                                                   CIRCULAR

   RULES ON JURISDICTION, ORDER, PROCEDURES AND MEASURES FOR PROTECTION OF THE MIND, HEALTH, PROPERTY, DIGNITY AND PERSONALITY OF THE PLAINTIFFS AGAINST THE ACT OF SCAM AND CHARGE

  Pursuant to the Law on Complaints dated June 12, 2018;

  Pursuant to the Law on Anti-Corruption dated November 20, 2018;

  Pursuant to the Law on Thrifty Practices and Anti-Wastefulness dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 31/2019/ND-CP dated April 10, 2019 detailing a number of articles and measures for the organization of the Law on Complaints;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP dated February 25, 2019 on reporting and settlement of complaints in the People’s Public Security;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 01/2018/ND-CP dated August 06, 2018 defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;

  At the request of the Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Public Security;

  The Minister of Public Security promulgates a Circular defining the competence, order, procedures and measures for protection of the life, health, property, honor and dignity of whistleblowers on acts of corruption and wastefulness.

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides the competence, procedures, and measures to protect the life, health, property, honor, and dignity of whistleblowers on corruption acts specified in the Law on Prevention and Combat of Corruption and violations of the Law on Thrifty Practices and Anti-Wastefulness (hereinafter referred to as whistleblowers on corruption and wastefulness acts).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Protected persons specified in Clause 3 of this Circular.

  2. Persons settling whistleblowers on corruption and wastefulness acts.

  3. Police authorities at all levels; agencies, organizations, units, and individuals responsible for implementing measures to protect the life, health, property, honor, and dignity of protected persons under Clause 1 of Article 3 of this Circular.

  Article 3. Protected persons, scope, content of protection, and grounds for applying protection measures

  1. Protected persons include: whistleblowers on corruption and wastefulness acts; spouses, biological parents, adoptive parents, biological children, and adopted children of whistleblowers on corruption and wastefulness acts (hereinafter referred to as protected persons). Protected persons living, working, or studying abroad are not subject to this Circular.

  2. Scope and content of protection of protected persons include: protection of life, health, property, honor, and dignity. Protected property refers to property owned by protected persons. Property of protected persons abroad is not subject to this Circular.

  3. Grounds for applying protective measures: When there are grounds to believe that the life, health, property, honor, or dignity of the person specified in Clause 1 of this Article is being infringed upon or at immediate risk of being infringed upon due to the denunciation of acts of corruption or waste.

  Article 4. Rights and obligations of protected persons

  The rights and obligations of protected persons shall comply with Article 48 of the Law on Denunciation. Persons competent to settle denunciations and police authorities competent to apply protective measures shall not be liable for consequences arising to the life, health, property, honor, or dignity of protected persons in cases where protected persons request termination or refuse application of protective measures or fail to comply with obligations specified in Clause 2 Article 48 of the Law on Denunciation.

  Article 5. Responsibilities for protection of protected persons

  1. Police authorities at all levels where protected persons reside or have property shall assume the prime responsibility for and coordinate with relevant agencies, organizations, units, and individuals in protecting the life, health, property, honor, and dignity of protected persons; decide to mobilize and utilize forces, means, tools, measures, and request relevant agencies, organizations, units, and individuals to coordinate in protection. Relevant agencies, organizations, units, and individuals shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate as requested by police authorities at all levels.

  2. Persons competent to settle denunciations upon receiving requests for application of protective measures to the life, health, property, honor, or dignity of denunciators of acts of corruption or waste and finding such requests well-founded or credible, or during the settlement of denunciations, persons competent to settle denunciations finding grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular, shall promptly request competent police authorities to apply protective measures as follows:

  a) Persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 6, 7, 8 Article 13; Clause 4 Article 14; Clause 4 Article 15; Article 16; Clauses 1 and 3 Article 17; and Article 20 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly request competent police authorities to apply protective measures to the Director of Police of provinces or centrally-run cities (collectively referred to as provincial-level police directors) and simultaneously send such requests to the Minister of Public Security for direction. Upon receiving such requests, provincial-level police directors shall consider and decide to apply protective measures or direct relevant police units to issue protective measure decisions;

  b) Persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 3, 4, 5 Article 13; Clauses 2, 3 Article 14; Clauses 2, 3 Article 15; Clause 2 Article 17 and Articles 18, 19 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly request or request the application of protective measures to Directors of provincial-level police authorities. Upon receiving requests for protection, Directors of provincial-level police authorities shall consider and decide to apply protective measures or direct police authorities of relevant affiliated units to issue decisions on application of protective measures;

  c) Persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 2 Article 13; Clause 1 Article 14; Clause 1 Article 15 and Article 21 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly request or request the application of protective measures to Chief Police Officers of districts, townships, provincial capitals, cities under central government (collectively referred to as district-level Chief Police Officers). Upon receiving requests for protection, Chief Police Officers of districts shall consider and decide to apply protective measures or direct Chief Police Officers of wards, chiefs of police stations, chiefs of police posts, chiefs of police stations of townships, chiefs of police stations of communes under the civil police force (collectively referred to as commune-level Chief Police Officers) to decide to apply protective measures;

  d) Persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 1 Article 13 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly request or request the application of protective measures to Chief Police Officers of communes. Upon receiving requests for protection, Chief Police Officers of communes shall consider and decide to apply protective measures;

  dd) In case of emergencies where there is a basis to determine that the life, health, property, honor and dignity of protected persons may be immediately harmed after denunciation of acts of corruption or waste; persons competent to settle denunciations shall promptly request or request the nearest police authorities or police authorities where the life, health, property, honor and dignity of protected persons may be harmed to apply necessary protective measures;

  e) In case protected persons are prisoners in detention camps; pupils in compulsory education establishments; students in reform schools: persons competent to settle denunciations shall promptly request or request the application of protective measures to superintendents of detention camps, directors of compulsory education establishments, principals of reform schools.

  3. Upon receiving the proposal or request of a competent person to resolve the denunciation, the written proposal for application of protection measures by the denouncer regarding corrupt or wasteful acts or the written proposal of a competent agency or unit; if the proposal or request is considered well-founded, within the scope of their tasks and powers, within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the proposal or request, the head of the competent police authority shall issue a decision on application of protection measures; preside over and coordinate with relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals in protecting the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the protected person. In case the case is complex and requires examination and verification at multiple locations, the time limit for issuing a decision on application of protection measures may be extended but not more than 05 working days.

  In case it is beyond the competence, the head of the competent police authority shall promptly transfer it to the competent police authority for handling according to regulations and notify in writing the competent person to resolve the denunciation, the denouncer or the competent agency or unit of the transfer of the proposal or request.

  4. In case the proposal or request of a competent person to resolve the denunciation and the denouncer regarding corrupt or wasteful acts is unfounded or it is considered unnecessary to apply protection measures, within 03 working days, the head of the competent police authority shall notify in writing and clearly state the reasons to the competent agency or unit, the denouncer or send a written notice to the denouncer for explanation to the denouncer.

  Article 6. Written request for decision on application of protection measures

  1. The written proposal for application of protection measures by the denouncer regarding corrupt or wasteful acts shall comply with the provisions of Clause 2 Article 50 of the Law on Denunciation.

  2. The written request for decision on application of protection measures by the competent person to resolve the denunciation sent to the competent police authority shall include the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the written proposal or request;

  b) Name of the competent police authority requested to decide on application of protection measures;

  c) Full name, position, agency or unit of the person competent to settle the denunciation;

  d) Grounds and reasons for requesting the application of protective measures;

  d) Date of issuance of the decision to accept the denunciation; time limit for settling the denunciation;

  e) Full name, address and telephone number of the protected person; contents of the request for protection.

  3. When sending the written request, the person competent to settle the denunciation must enclose the decision to accept the denunciation and the written request for application of protective measures of the denunciant regarding the act of corruption or waste as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 50 of the Law on Denunciation (original) and other documents related to the application of protective measures (if any).

  Article 7. Competence to decide on the application of protective measures of the person competent to settle the denunciation in the People’s Public Security

  1. The person competent to settle the denunciation prescribed in Clause 1 Article 5 of the Government’s Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP dated February 25, 2019 on denunciation and settlement of denunciation in the People’s Public Security upon receiving the written request for application of protective measures for the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denunciant regarding the act of corruption or waste and deeming the request for protection to be well-founded and authentic or finding grounds as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular during the settlement of the denunciation shall promptly decide to apply protective measures to the protected person whose address or place of property is within the jurisdictional area.

  In case the protected person’s address or place of property is outside the jurisdictional area, the person competent to settle the denunciation prescribed in Clause 1 Article 5 of the Government’s Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall report to the Head of the district-level Public Security for handling according to their competence.

  2. The person competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 2 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and waste and considering the protection request to be well-founded and authentic or during the settlement of the denunciation finds that there are grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular shall promptly decide to apply protective measures to the protected person whose address of residence or place of property is within the jurisdictional area or direct the concerned commune-level police chief to decide to apply protective measures (except for the case where the denounced person is the commune-level police chief).

  In case the protected person’s address of residence or place of property is outside the jurisdictional area, the person competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 2 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall request the concerned district-level police chief to decide to apply protective measures, and at the same time report to the director of the provincial police department for monitoring and direction.

  3. The person competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 3 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and waste and considering the protection request to be well-founded and authentic or during the settlement of the denunciation, the person competent to settle denunciations finds that there are grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular shall promptly request the concerned district-level police chief to decide to apply protective measures.

  In case the protected person is a prisoner at a detention camp, a student at a compulsory education facility, or a student at a reform school: Upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and waste and considering the protection request to be well-founded and authentic or during the settlement of the denunciation, the director of the detention camp, the director of the compulsory education facility, or the principal of the reform school shall promptly decide to apply protective measures.

  4. The competent persons to settle denunciations specified in Clause 4 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and waste and considering the protection request to be well-founded and authentic or during the settlement of the denunciation finding grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular shall promptly decide to apply protective measures to the protected person whose address of residence or place of property is within the jurisdictional area or direct the relevant district-level police chief to decide to apply protective measures. The provincial police inspectorate shall be responsible for advising the director of the provincial police department to direct a deputy district-level police chief or another competent unit or individual to decide to apply protective measures in case the denounced person is the district-level police chief.

  In case the protected person’s address of residence or place of property is outside the jurisdictional area, the competent person to settle denunciations specified in Clause 4 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall request the relevant director of the provincial police department to decide to apply protective measures.

  5. The competent persons to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 5 and 6 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and waste and considering the protection request to be well-founded and authentic or during the settlement of the denunciation, the denunciation-settling person finds grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular shall promptly request the relevant director of the provincial police department to decide to apply protective measures.

  In case the protected person is a cadre, soldier, prisoner at a detention camp, a cadre, soldier, student at a compulsory education facility, a cadre, soldier, student at a reform school and the denounced person is a warden, deputy warden of a detention camp, director, deputy director of a compulsory education facility, principal, deputy principal of a reform school: The Director of the Police Department of Management of Detention Camps, Compulsory Education Facilities, Reform Schools upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and waste and considering the protection request to be well-founded and authentic or during the settlement of the denunciation finding grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular shall promptly decide to apply protective measures.

  The Inspectorate of the Ministry of Public Security shall be responsible for advising the Minister of Public Security to direct the Deputy Director - Deputy Secretary of the Party Committee of the provincial police department or another competent unit or individual to decide to apply protective measures in case the denounced person is the director of the provincial police department.

  Article 8. Procedures for protection

  1. The order and procedures for protection of the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the whistleblower about acts of corruption and waste shall comply with the provisions in Section 2, Chapter VI of the Law on Whistleblowing.

  2. In case the competent person who has resolved the whistleblower’s complaint has issued a conclusion on the contents of the complaint or a decision to suspend the resolution of the complaint, he/she shall immediately notify the competent police authority to decide to terminate the application of the protection measure. The decision to terminate the application of the protection measure shall be sent to the protected person, the competent person who has resolved the whistleblower’s complaint, and other relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals.

  3. After the time when the competent person who has resolved the whistleblower’s complaint has issued a conclusion on the contents of the complaint or a decision to suspend the resolution of the complaint, the competent police authority shall decide to terminate the application of the protection measure. If the competent person who has resolved the whistleblower’s complaint continues to receive a written request from the whistleblower about acts of corruption and waste and considers that there are grounds and authenticity to determine that the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the protected person are further infringed upon or threatened with infringement, he/she shall forward the written request to the competent police authority for handling in accordance with the law and notify the whistleblower in writing.

  Article 9. Application dossier of protection measures

  The application dossier of protection measures shall comply with the provisions in Article 55 of the Law on Whistleblowing and the provisions on the archiving of documents formed and disseminated in the People’s Public Security (the dossier group on anti-corruption).

  Article 10. Protection measures

  Based on the actual situation, the competent police authority may apply one or a number of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the protected person in accordance with the provisions in Article 58 of the Law on Whistleblowing.

  Article 11. Funding for the application of protection measures

  The funds for implementation of protective measures shall be provided from the State budget. The police units and local authorities shall prepare a total estimate of funds for implementation of protective measures and submit it to the Ministry of Public Security (via the Department of Planning and Finance) for consolidation and submission to the Ministry of Finance as prescribed.

  Article 12. Effect, transitional provisions and implementation responsibilities

  1. This Circular takes effect from February 15, 2021.

  2. Any provisions in this Circular that are amended, supplemented or replaced shall also be adjusted and implemented according to the amended, supplemented or replaced provisions.

  3. The heads of police units affiliated to the Ministry, the directors of police of provinces and centrally-run cities, and relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals shall implement this Circular.

  4. The Inspectorate of the Ministry of Public Security shall assume the prime responsibility for monitoring, inspecting, consolidating and reporting on the implementation of this Circular; the heads of police units affiliated to the Ministry shall submit their units' advisory and consolidation reports; the heads of local police shall submit reports to the Inspectorates of local police for monitoring, inspection, consolidation and reporting on the implementation of this Circular. If any problems arise during the implementation, the police units and local authorities, agencies, organizations, units and individuals shall report them to the Ministry of Public Security (via the Inspectorate of the Ministry of Public Security) for consolidation and submission to the Ministry's leadership for consideration and resolution.
