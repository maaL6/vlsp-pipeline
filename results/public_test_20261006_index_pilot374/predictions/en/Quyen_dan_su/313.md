                                                   CIRCULAR

                GUIDELINES FOR CARE FOR THE HEALTH OF THE ELDERLY

  Pursuant to Articles 14, 15 and 16 of the Ordinance on the elderly No. 23/2000/PL/UBTVQH of April 28, 2000, the health sector is assigned to perform its tasks.

Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 30/2002/ND-CP of March 26, 2002, which provides for and guides the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on the elderly, and the Government's Decree No. 120/2003/ND-CP of October 20, 2003, which amends Article 9 of the Decree No. 30/2002/ND-CP of October 20, 2003, and the Government's Decree No. 49/2003/ND-CP of May 15, 2003, which provides for the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Health guides the implementation of the health care for the elderly as follows:

  I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. The elderly persons entitled to health care under this Circular are citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, overseas Vietnamese and foreign elderly persons who have lived and worked in Vietnam for 60 years or more. Old age is often accompanied by weakness and illness; about 95% of the elderly have diseases. On average, an elderly person has 2,69 diseases, mainly chronic and non-communicable diseases. The deepest wish of the elderly is to live a healthy life, receive health care and be examined and treated when they fall ill. This is a legitimate request of the elderly and requires the attention of all sectors, levels and the whole society.

  2. The elderly shall receive primary health care and be given priority in examination and treatment at health facilities.

  3. People's Committees at all levels shall be responsible for ensuring the health care regime for the elderly in their localities. The health sector shall be responsible for professional and technical aspects of health care for the elderly.

  II. ORGANIZATION OF MANAGEMENT OF HEALTH AND PRIMARY HEALTH CARE FOR THE ELDERLY

  People's Committees and People's Councils at all levels shall be responsible for directing the health sector to cooperate with relevant sectors in developing and implementing plans for health care for the elderly in their localities according to the following requirements:

  1. To organize the dissemination of contents on physical training, health improvement and disease prevention, especially common diseases among the elderly, so that the elderly can prevent diseases themselves.

  2. To organize health check-ups to prepare health records for the elderly. Periodic health check-ups shall be conducted once a year, and a network of family doctors and home health care services for the elderly shall be gradually organized.

  3. To organize various forms of senior citizen clubs such as health and wellness clubs, outdoor health clubs. Depending on the conditions and requirements of senior citizens, each locality may organize clubs of people with chronic diseases such as diabetes, asthma, hypertension, cardiovascular diseases, kidney and urinary diseases, cataracts, etc.

  4. To prioritize investment in community-based rehabilitation programs for disabled senior citizens to prevent and recover sequelae due to injuries, accidents or cerebrovascular diseases, chronic diseases, occupational diseases, etc.

  5. To organize volunteer networks to provide home-based health care for senior citizens, especially the poor and lonely people with no place to rely on. Encourage retired medical officials to volunteer or participate in training for volunteers on necessary knowledge in health care for senior citizens such as: self-care activities in daily life, especially for patients with sequelae after cardiovascular diseases, accidents, social communication activities and self-medication.

  III. DOCTORS' EXAMINATION AND TREATMENT OF SENIOR CITIZENS

  1. To organize a network of examination and treatment of senior citizens:

  a. The Institute of Geriatrics shall have the responsibility:

  - To organize research on application of scientific and technological advancements in the three fields of clinical geriatrics, basic geriatrics, social geriatrics, and propose measures to improve the quality of health care for senior citizens in Vietnam.

  - To direct professional and technical aspects of health care for senior citizens nationwide. To organize inspection of implementation of professional processes and standards, and equipment for lower-level geriatric facilities to ensure rehabilitation after diseases of senior citizens.

  - To provide specialized treatment for geriatric diseases.

  - To cooperate with Hanoi Medical University and other medical training institutions in providing undergraduate and postgraduate education in geriatrics. To compile textbooks and specialized training books and disseminate knowledge on elderly health care.

  b. First and second-class general hospitals: To establish geriatric departments and have separate examination rooms for elderly patients at the examination departments.

  c. Specialized hospitals (except pediatric specialized hospitals): To organize examination of elderly patients at examination departments; when elderly patients need to be hospitalized, hospitals must provide courteous reception and attentive care, and not allow patients to be put in the same beds.

  d. District, district-level, provincial cities and district-level cities medical centers: To have a number of beds reserved for elderly patients at clinical departments and organize examination of elderly patients at examination departments.

  dd. Rehabilitation nursing hospitals must be further built and reinforced according to the provisions of the Decision No. 966/BYT dated May 30, 1999 of the Minister of Health on guidelines for the operation of rehabilitation nursing hospitals; reserve an appropriate proportion of beds for caring for and rehabilitating elderly patients. In conjunction with the Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs Department, implement the nursing regime for elderly people who have rendered services to the country.

  e. Commune, ward and township health stations are responsible for managing health and providing primary health care for elderly people in their localities. In cases where elderly people are disabled, lonely, have no place to rely on and fall ill but do not come for examination and treatment at the prescribed places, the head of the commune health station shall appoint a medical officer to examine and treat the elderly person at their place of residence or report to the local People's Committee to organize the transportation of the patient to the examination and treatment facility.

  2. Treatment work.

  - The examination and treatment of elderly patients shall be carried out according to the professional and technical chain. In case of emergency, the patients shall be transferred to the nearest medical facilities.

  - The examination and treatment facilities shall give priority to the examination of elderly patients (after the emergency cases).

  - The chronic disease management for elderly patients shall be organized step by step according to the programs and projects in order to detect and handle the abnormal developments in time. There should be a plan for prevention and treatment of chronic diseases.

  - The rehabilitation of elderly patients after acute treatment at hospitals shall be enhanced and the home treatment shall be guided.

  - The traditional medicine and modern medicine shall be combined, and the non-drug treatment methods shall be developed, especially at the grassroots medical facilities for elderly patients.

  IV. EXPENSE FOR INITIAL HEALTH CHECKING AND TREATMENT OF ELDERLY PATIENTS

  1. The expenses for health check-up to make the health records for elderly patients, organize the periodic health check-up and the initial health care activities for elderly patients shall be borne by the local governments.

  2. The expenses for examination and treatment:

  a. Persons with compulsory health insurance cards or voluntary health insurance cards shall be paid for medical examination and treatment by the social insurance (BHYT) as prescribed by law on health insurance.

  b. Persons eligible for the poor medical examination and treatment regime shall be paid for medical examination and treatment by the poor medical examination and treatment fund as prescribed in the Prime Minister's Decision No. 139/2002/QD-TTg of 15/10/2002 on medical examination and treatment for the poor and the Joint Circular No. 14/2002/Tr-LT-BYT-BTC of December 16, 2002 of the Ministry of Health and the Ministry of Finance guiding the implementation of the above-mentioned Decision.

  c. Persons aged 90 and over shall be entitled to health insurance regimes as prescribed in the Circular No. 24/2003 of June 6, 2003 of the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs guiding the implementation of the Government's Decree No. 120/2003/ND-CP of October 20, 2003.

  3. Persons without health insurance cards as prescribed in Point 2 above shall pay for medical examination and treatment themselves.

  4. Domestic and foreign organizations and individuals and families of the elderly are encouraged to purchase health insurance cards for the elderly.

  5. Localities should set up health care funds for the elderly to support the health care and medical examination and treatment for the elderly.

  V. IMPLEMENTATION

  1. At the provincial, district and commune levels, health care committees for the elderly shall be set up, headed by the leaders of the administration, with the health sector as the permanent member, with the participation of the finance sector, the labor, war invalids and social affairs sector, relevant sectors, representatives of the elderly associations, the Red Cross, the Fatherland Front, the farmers' associations and the women's federations to ensure and maintain the health care activities of the elderly in the locality. In order to unify the health care activities, the health care committees for the elderly should be integrated into the local primary health care committees.

  2. Departments of Health of provinces and centrally-run cities shall advise People’s Committees of provinces in developing plans for elderly health care throughout provinces in accordance with this Circular and direct district-level and commune-level health authorities to participate in elderly health care within their localities.

  3. Department of Treatment shall coordinate with functional Departments and Bureaus, the Inspectorate of the Ministry of Health, the Institute of Gerontology and relevant units in guiding, directing, inspecting and supervising the implementation of this Circular.

  4. Health education and communication centers shall coordinate with the Institute of Gerontology in disseminating knowledge on elderly health care.

  5. This Circular takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette. If any problems arise during the implementation, local units and authorities shall report them to the Ministry of Health (Department of Treatment) for consideration and resolution or amendment.
