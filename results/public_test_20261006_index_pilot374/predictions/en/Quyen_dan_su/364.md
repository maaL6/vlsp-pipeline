                                                    DIRECTIVE

                   ON THE ORGANIZATION OF THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE 2000 LAW ON MARRIAGE AND FAMILY

  The 2000 Marriage and Family Law was passed by the Xth National Assembly at its 7th session on June 9, 2000, and came into force on January 1, 2001.

  In order to promptly implement the Law on Marriage and Family and the Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of June 9, 2000 of the National Assembly on the implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family in 2000, the Prime Minister hereby gives the following instructions:

  I. TO CONDUCT REVIEW OF CURRENT LEGAL DOCUMENTS ON MARRIAGE AND FAMILY OR TO PROMULGATE NEW DOCUMENTS ON MARRIAGE AND FAMILY

  1. From now until the end of 2000, the Ministry of Justice shall take charge of and complete the review of current legal documents on marriage and family in order to propose the Government and other competent agencies to amend, supplement or annul documents related to marriage and family which are no longer in conformity with the new Law on Marriage and Family.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall take charge of and cooperate with relevant agencies and organizations in preparing sub-law documents to be submitted to the Government or the Standing Committee of the National Assembly for promulgation in order to guide the implementation of the Law, especially the issues related to the right to use land for cultivation, residential land and housing related to marriage and family relations; marriage and adoption registration in remote areas, civil status registration of marriage and family with foreign elements, including civil status registration of marriage and family with foreign elements in border areas with neighboring countries; and sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of marriage and family. The above-mentioned tasks must be completed in the second quarter of 2001.

  3. The Ethnic and Mountainous Regions Committee shall take charge of preparing the proposal for the Government to promulgate in the first quarter of 2001 a Decree on the application of the Law on Marriage and Family to ethnic minorities, measures for handling actual adoption in ethnic minority areas, together with a list of outdated customs and practices on marriage and family which are not allowed to be applied and a list of good customs and practices on marriage and family which are encouraged to be developed.

  II. TO PROMOTE, PUBLICIZE AND EDUCATE ON THE LAW ON MARRIAGE AND FAMILY

  1. The promotion and publicization of the Law on Marriage and Family must be carried out regularly and widely among cadres, civil servants, public employees, soldiers of the People's Armed Forces and the people, especially in communes, wards, townships, residential clusters, agencies, organizations, enterprises, schools and families.

  The promotion and publicization must aim to make everyone clearly understand the basic principles and contents of the Law, especially the new contents amended and supplemented and the Resolution of the National Assembly on the settlement of actual marriage situations, so that they can voluntarily and actively comply with, raise their awareness and concern for their personal responsibility for building families, making each family a true family of each individual, making the Vietnamese society a great family of all Vietnamese people working together to build an independent, free, prosperous and happy country.

  From the third quarter of 2000, ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and People’s Committees at all levels shall formulate and organize the implementation of plans for popularizing, propagating and educating the Law on Marriage and Family in their agencies, units and localities; consider this a key task of the law popularization, propaganda and education work in the last six months of 2000 and the whole year 2001, and at the same time, innovate the forms and measures of law popularization and education on marriage and family in order to deepen these activities and avoid the law popularization and education work being merely popular, superficial and ineffective.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall coordinate with the Central Committee of the Vietnam Fatherland Front, the Central Committee of the Vietnam Women’s Union, the Vietnam General Confederation of Labor, the Central Committee of the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, the Vietnam Peasants’ Union, the Vietnam War Veterans Union, the Ministry of Culture and Information, the Vietnam Committee for Protection and Care of Children, the Population and Family Planning Committee, the Vietnam Lawyers Association, the central and local press, radio and television agencies in popularizing and propagating the Law on Marriage and Family and the National Assembly’s Resolution on the implementation of this Law; direct and guide the intensification of legal counseling on marriage and family, encourage the establishment of various types of family clubs where people can participate in activities according to gender, age, occupation or interests in order to exchange experiences and learn from each other the necessary things in the responsibilities of being a wife, husband, father, mother, grandfather, grandmother... in order to avoid from the beginning the awkwardness and unfortunate things in the truly important mission of family life.

  The Government’s Council for Coordination of Law Popularization and Education shall be responsible for determining the contents and programs for popularizing and propagating the Law on Marriage and Family and the National Assembly’s Resolution on the implementation of this Law; coordinate with relevant agencies and organizations in compiling outlines and training materials suitable for each type of target audience and train reporters to popularize and propagate the Law on Marriage and Family.

  3. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall coordinate with the Central Ideological and Cultural Committee and the Ministry of Justice in directing the mass media to have plans to give priority to the popularization and propaganda of the Law on Marriage and Family from now until the end of 2000 and 2001; guide the formulation and implementation of community living rules related to the provisions of the Law on Marriage and Family.

  4. The People’s Daily, Voice of Vietnam Radio, Vietnam Television and other mass media at the central and local levels shall regularly have programs and sections to introduce and explain the contents and new points of the Law on Marriage and Family.

  5. The Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for coordinating with the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy in organizing in-depth training on the Law on Marriage and Family for judges, procurators, court clerks, lay judges, civil status officers and other judicial personnel to ensure the uniform application and supervision of the Law on Marriage and Family.

  6. The Ministry of Education and Training shall assume the prime responsibility for coordinating with the Ministry of Justice in directing the review of the system of textbooks and teaching materials of universities and colleges and the materials on civic education related to the Law on Marriage and Family in vocational high schools, secondary schools and primary schools to make plans for timely supplementation and amendment in accordance with the Law on Marriage and Family; organize training for teachers teaching law and civic education on the Law on Marriage and Family.

  7. The Ethnic and Mountainous Committee shall assume the prime responsibility for coordinating with the Ministry of Justice in making plans for the dissemination and propaganda of the Law on Marriage and Family among ethnic minorities.

  8. The Ministry of Finance shall assume the prime responsibility for coordinating with the Ministry of Justice in making plans for guiding the budget balance, ensuring the necessary funding for the implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family and the Resolution on the implementation of the Law of the National Assembly from the preparation of guiding documents for the implementation of this Law, organizing the registration of marriage and adoption in remote areas, organizing training classes and widespread dissemination and propaganda among cadres, soldiers and the people.

  9. The presidents of the People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall direct local agencies to make specific plans for the settlement of actual marriage situations in their localities according to the National Assembly’s Resolution on the implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family, creating favorable conditions for marriage registration, including on-site registration, to resolve this situation definitively in 2001 - 2002.

  The Prime Minister requests ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies, presidents of the People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities within the scope of their management responsibilities to make specific plans for the implementation of this Directive.

  The Ministry of Justice and the Government Office shall be responsible for monitoring and urging the implementation of this Directive and summarizing and reporting the results of implementation to the Prime Minister.
