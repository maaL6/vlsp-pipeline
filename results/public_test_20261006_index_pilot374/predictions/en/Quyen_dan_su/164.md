                                           CIRCULAR
   GUIDELINES ON VISA GRANT, STAYING PERMIT GRANT, ENTRY PERMIT GRANT AND RESOLUTION OF RESIDENCE ISSUES FOR FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM
Pursuant to the Law on Entry, Exit, Transit, and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 106/2014/ND-CP dated November 17, 2014, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;
At the request of the Director General of the General Department of Security, the Minister of Public Security promulgates a Circular guiding the issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits and permanent residence permits for foreigners in Vietnam.
Chapter I
                                       GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope of regulation
This Circular provides guidance on the issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits, and permanent residence permits for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Public Security.
Article 2. Regulated entities
This Circular applies to foreigners entering and residing in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Chapter II
 PROCEDURES AND JURISDICTION FOR RESOLVING APPLICATIONS FOR STAYING PERMIT AND STAYING CARD, RESOLVING STAYING PROBLEM
Article 3. Procedures and competence to settle visa application
1. Agencies, organizations and individuals that guarantee foreign nationals entering Vietnam under Clause 1 Article 16 of the Law on Entry, Exit, Transit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as the Law) shall directly submit written requests to the Immigration Department.
2. Written requests for visas shall be made using the forms issued by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 5, 2015, which provides forms related to entry, exit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam, including:
a) Form NA2 for use by agencies and organizations;
b) Form NA3 for use by individuals.
3. Processing of visa requests:
a) The Immigration Department shall consider, process, and respond to requests for visas made by agencies, organizations, and individuals in accordance with Clause 3 Article 16 of the Law;
b) In cases of visa requests made at border checkpoints, the Immigration Department shall consider and process them in accordance with Clause 5 Article 16 of the Law.
Article 4. Procedures and competence for processing applications for temporary residence cards
1. Foreigners eligible for temporary residence cards in accordance with Clause 2 Article 36 of the Law must apply for such cards through agencies, organizations, or individuals that have invited or guaranteed them directly at the Immigration Department or the Immigration Division of the Public Security Office of the province or centrally-affiliated city where the inviting or guaranteeing agency or organization is headquartered or where the individual invited or guaranteed resides.
2. Application for a temporary residence card as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law. The written application for a temporary residence card specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law shall be made using the form issued by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 5, 2015, which provides forms of documents related to entry, exit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam, including:

a) Form NA6 and NA8 for use by agencies and organizations;
b) Form NA7 and NA8 for individuals.
3. Grant of temporary residence cards
Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of complete dossiers, the Immigration Department or the Immigration Division of the provincial-level police office shall consider issuing temporary residence cards.
4. The duration of a temporary residence card shall be appropriate to the purpose of entry and the request of the agency, organization or individual. The temporary residence card shall have a duration of 01 year to 05 years but shorter than the duration of the passport by at least 30 days.
Article 5. Permanent settlement
1. Procedures and competence for permanent residence settlement shall comply with Article 41 and Article 42 of the Law.
2. Determination of the duration of temporary residence in Vietnam for foreigners applying for permanent residence:
a) Foreigners who have continuously resided in Vietnam for 03 years or more according to Clause 3 Article 40 of the Law shall be determined based on the entry and exit verification stamps issued at border checkpoints, with a total duration of temporary residence in Vietnam of 03 years or more within the last 04 years as of the date of submission of the application for permanent residence.
b) Papers proving continuous temporary residence in Vietnam before 2000 according to Point b Clause 1 Article 42 of the Law shall include one of the following papers: temporary residence certificate; temporary residence register; explanation of the duration of temporary residence in Vietnam, certified by the commune, ward or township police office where the stateless person temporarily resides.
3. Places of submission of applications for permanent residence:
a) Persons applying for permanent residence according to Clause 1 and Clause 2 Article 39 of the Law shall submit their applications to the Immigration Department.
b) Persons applying for permanent residence according to Clause 3 and Clause 4 Article 39 of the Law shall submit their applications to the Immigration Office of the police office of the province or centrally-run city where they apply for permanent residence.
Chapter III
 PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE AND REISSUANCE OF ENTRY AND EXIT PERMIT FOR STATELESS PERSONS RESIDING IN VIETNAM
Article 6. Entry and exit permits
1. International travel documents prescribed in Point i, Clause 1, Article 44 of the Law are entry and exit permits issued by the Immigration Department to stateless persons residing in Vietnam who wish to exit Vietnam (using Form NC13 enclosed with this Circular).
2. Entry and exit permits have a validity period of 3 years; in case of loss or damage, re-issuance of entry and exit permits shall be considered.
Article 7. Procedures for issuance and re-issuance of entry and exit permits
1. Applicants for issuance and re-issuance of entry and exit permits shall submit 1 set of application dossiers to the Immigration Department or the Immigration Department of the police office of the province or centrally-affiliated city where they reside. The application dossier includes:
a) Application for issuance and re-issuance of entry and exit permits (using Form NC14 enclosed with this Circular);
b) Photocopy of permanent residence card enclosed with the original for comparison for persons who have been issued permanent residence cards;
c) Written explanation of the duration of temporary residence in Vietnam, certified by the commune, ward or township police office where the stateless person temporarily resides for persons who have not been granted permanent residence;
d) The issued entry and exit permit, in case of re-issue application due to damage to the entry and exit permit;
dd) The lost report, in case of re-issue application due to loss of the entry and exit permit (no certification from the competent authority of the place of loss is required).
2. Processing of entry and exit permit issuance and re-issuance:
a) In case of submission of application at the Immigration Department:

Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of the complete application, the Immigration Department shall consider issuing or re-issuing the entry and exit permit.
b) In case of submission of application at the Immigration Management Office of the provincial-level police authority:
Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the complete application, the Immigration Management Office of the provincial-level police authority shall consider and transfer the application for issuance or re-issuance of the entry and exit permit to the Immigration Department. The Immigration Department shall consider issuing or re-issuing the entry and exit permit within 02 working days.
Chapter IV
                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 8. Transitional provisions
1. Entry and exit permits that are valid before the effective date of this Circular may be used until the expiry of their validity period.
2. Applications for visas, temporary residence cards; issuance and re-issuance of entry and exit permits; and permanent residence applications submitted before the effective date of this Circular but not yet processed shall be considered and processed by the immigration authorities in accordance with this Circular.
Article 9. Effect
1. This Circular takes effect from August 20, 2015.
2. This Circular replaces Circular No. 45/2011/TT-BCA dated June 29, 2011 of the Minister of Public Security guiding the issuance of entry and exit permits to foreigners residing in Vietnam without passports.
Article 10. Implementation responsibilities
1. The General Department of Security shall assume the prime responsibility for guiding the police units of localities and other relevant units in implementing this Circular.
2. The General Directors, Heads of units affiliated to the Ministry, Directors of the police and fire departments of provinces and centrally-run cities shall implement this Circular. If any problems arise during the implementation of this Circular, the police units and localities shall report them to the Ministry (via the Immigration Department - General Department of Security) for timely guidance.
