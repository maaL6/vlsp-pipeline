                                                   DECREE

   GOVERNMENT

No. 77/2001/ND-CP

Details of marriage registration according to Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of the National Assembly on the implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family

                                                   THE GOVERNMENT

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Government dated September 30, 1992;
  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated June 9, 2000;
  Pursuant to the Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of June 9, 2000 of the National Assembly on the implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family;
  To strengthen the state management in the field of marriage and family, ensure the lawful rights and interests of citizens, and eliminate the situation of unregistered marriage, encourage the establishment of legal marital relationships; At the request of the Minister of Justice,

                                                   DECREE

  Chapter 1:

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree elaborates on marriage registration for:

  a) Couples who were married before January 3, 1987, the date on which the 1986 Law on Marriage and Family came into force, but have not yet registered their marriage;

  b) Couples who have been living together as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001, the date on which the 2000 Law on Marriage and Family came into force, but have not yet registered their marriage.

  2. The provisions of Clause 1 of this Article include cases where one party is a Vietnamese citizen and the other is a citizen of a neighboring country with which Vietnam shares a border, who have settled and lived in Vietnam for a long time; and cases where one party is a Vietnamese citizen and the other is a stateless person residing in Vietnam, or both parties are stateless persons residing in Vietnam.

  A stateless person residing in Vietnam referred to in this Clause means a person who does not have Vietnamese nationality and is not recognized by other countries as having that nationality, who is settling and living in Vietnam for a long time.

  Article 2. Encouragement of marriage registration and marriage registration obligations

  1. The State shall encourage and create favorable conditions for the registration of marriage of couples who were married before January 3, 1987, but have not yet registered their marriage. The registration of marriage of these couples is not time-limited.

  2. If a man and a woman have been living together as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001 and have all the conditions for marriage as prescribed by the 2000 Law on Marriage and Family, they are obliged to register their marriage. If they fail to register their marriage after January 1, 2003, the law will not recognize them as husband and wife.

  Article 3. Recognition of the effective date of marriage

  The marital relationship of the persons who register their marriage as prescribed in this Decree shall be recognized from the date on which the parties establish the husband and wife relationship or live together as husband and wife in reality. The effective date of marriage recognition must be clearly recorded in the marriage register and the marriage certificate as guided by the Ministry of Justice.

  Article 4. Exemption from marriage registration fee

  The marriage registration for the cases prescribed in this Decree is exempt from the fee.

  Chapter II

                                        MARriage REGISTRATION PROCEDURES

  Article 5. Jurisdiction and procedures for marriage registration

  1. The People’s Committees of communes, wards or townships (hereinafter referred to as the People’s Committees of commune level) where the permanent residence of one of the two parties is registered, shall carry out the marriage registration.

  In case both parties do not have permanent residence but have registered temporary residence for a fixed period according to the law on residence registration, the People’s Committee of commune level where one of the parties has registered temporary residence for a fixed period shall carry out the marriage registration.

  2. When registering for marriage, the parties only need to make a marriage registration declaration and present their ID cards or valid documents as substitutes.

  In the marriage registration declaration, the parties shall add the date of establishment of the husband-wife relationship or cohabitation as husband and wife in reality. In case the husband and wife cannot agree on the date of establishment of the husband-wife relationship or cohabitation as husband and wife in reality, the date and month shall be calculated as follows:

  If the month is determined but the date is not determined, the date of the next month shall be taken;

  If the year is determined but the date and month are not determined, the date of January 1 of the next year shall be taken.

  Article 6. Settlement of marriage registration for husband-wife relationship established before January 3, 1987

  In case both husband and wife have permanent residence or temporary residence for a fixed period at the place of marriage registration, the People’s Committee shall carry out the marriage registration immediately after receiving the marriage registration declaration.

  In case either of the parties has not permanently or temporarily resided at the place of marriage registration, but the People's Committee is aware of their marital status, the marriage registration shall be processed immediately. When there is uncertainty whether the parties have violated the one-wife, one-husband relationship, the People's Committee shall request them to make a written assurance and obtain the certification of at least two witnesses to the contents of the assurance. The witnesses shall be responsible to the law for the accuracy of their testimony.

  Article 7. Processing of marriage registration for cases where a man and a woman have lived together as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001

  In case either of the parties has not had a permanent or temporary residence permit at the place of marriage registration, the marriage registration declaration must be certified by the agency or unit where the person works (for officials, civil servants, and members of the armed forces) or the People's Committee of the commune where the person has had a permanent or temporary residence permit. If both parties have permanently or temporarily resided at the place of marriage registration, the above certification is not required.

  After receiving the marriage registration declaration, the People's Committee shall check and, if the parties are eligible for marriage as prescribed in Article 9 of the 2000 Law on Marriage and Family, immediately register the marriage. When there is uncertainty whether either of the parties or both parties are eligible for marriage, the People's Committee shall request them to make a written assurance and obtain the certification of at least two witnesses to the contents of the assurance. The witnesses shall be responsible to the law for the accuracy of their testimony.

  Article 8. Marriage registration location

  To create convenience for the parties to marry, the marriage registration may be carried out at the office of the People's Committee or at the hamlet, village, or residential group according to the plan of the People's Committee of the commune.

  Article 9. Recognition of common children of husband and wife

  Children born before the date of marriage registration of the cases prescribed in Article 1 of this Decree and recognized by their parents shall also be recognized as common children of husband and wife.

  If the father's name is still left blank in the birth registration book and birth certificate of the child, the People's Committee of the commune where the birth was registered shall, based on the marriage certificate of the parents, add the father's name to the birth registration book and birth certificate of the child, and simultaneously remove the note "illegitimate child" in the birth registration book.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 10. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Justice The Ministry of Justice shall assist the Government in directing, guiding and inspecting the implementation of this Decree.

  Article 11. Responsibilities of People's Committees at all levels

  1. The People's Committees of the provinces and districts shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, guide, direct and inspect the People's Committees of the communes in the implementation of this Decree.

  2. The People's Committees of the communes shall:

  a) Promote, mobilize, review and compile lists of cases requiring marriage registration as prescribed in this Decree;

  b) To organize timely, convenient and accurate marriage registration for the cases specified in this Decree;

  c) To accurately and timely confirm the marital status of the parties to the marriage, if requested;

  d) To ensure the balance of funding from the state budget according to current regulations for the marriage registration activities specified in this Decree.

  Article 12. Effect

  1. This Decree takes effect 15 days after its signing.

  2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of Government-attached agencies, and Presidents of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall be responsible for implementing this Decree.
