                                                        THE LAW

                                           MARRIAGE AND FAMILY

  The family is the cell of the society. A good family leads to a good society, and a good society leads to a better family. In the socialist family, the wife and husband should be equal, love and help each other to advance, actively participate in the cause of building socialism and defending the Fatherland, and together raise their children to be useful citizens of the society.
  Inheriting and developing the 1959 Law on Marriage and Family, in order to continue building and consolidating the socialist family, preserving and promoting the fine customs and practices of the nation, eliminating the outdated customs and the remnants of the feudal marriage and family regime, and resisting the influence of the capitalist marriage and family regime;

Pursuant to Articles 64 and 65 of the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam;
  This Law provides for the marriage and family regime.

  Chapter 1:

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.

1. The term “person” in this Law means a natural person or a legal person.

2. The term “organization” in this Law means a legal entity.

  The State shall ensure the genuine observance of the regime of voluntary, progressive, monogamous marriage and equality between husband and wife, aiming to build democratic, harmonious, happy and sustainable families.

  Marriages between Vietnamese citizens of different ethnicities and religions, between religious persons and non-religious persons shall be respected and protected.

  Article 2.- The State shall have to provide the necessary conditions for the development of the economy and society, the protection of the environment and the preservation of the natural resources of the country.

  The husband and wife have the obligation to have a planned child.

  Parents have the obligation to raise their children to become useful citizens for society.

  Children have the obligation to respect, care for and raise their parents.

  Article 3.- The State shall have to provide the necessary conditions for the development of the economy and society, the protection of the environment and the preservation of the natural resources of the country.

  The State and society shall protect mothers and children and assist mothers in performing their noble functions.

  Article 4.- The State shall have to provide the necessary conditions for the development of the economy and society, the protection of the environment and the preservation of the natural resources of the country.

  Prohibition of child marriage, forced marriage, hindrance to voluntary and progressive marriage, claims of property in marriage; prohibition of forced divorce.

  It is forbidden for a person who is married to marry or live with another person.

  Prohibits the abuse and mistreatment of parents, spouses, and children.

  Chapter 2:

                                                     MARRIAGE

  Article 5.- The State shall have to provide the necessary conditions for the development of the economy and society, the protection of the environment and the preservation of the natural resources of the country.

  Marriage is permitted for men aged 20 or older and women aged 18 or older.

  Article 6.

1. The State shall ensure the implementation of the Law on Social Security in the field of social security for persons with disabilities.

2. The State shall provide support for the implementation of the Law on Social Security in the field of social security for persons with disabilities.

3. The State shall provide support for the implementation of the Law on Social Security in the field of social security for persons with disabilities.

  Marriage is decided by the free will of the man and woman, neither of whom may be forced or obstructed by the other.

  Article 7.- The State shall have to provide the necessary conditions for the development of the economy and society, the protection of the environment and the preservation of the natural resources of the country.

  Marriage is prohibited in the following cases:

  a) Having a spouse;

  b) Suffering from mental illness and unable to recognize their own acts; suffering from venereal disease;

  c) Between people of the same direct blood line; between siblings of the same parents, same father and different mother or same mother and different father; between other people with the same surname within three generations;

  d) Between adoptive parents and adopted children.

  Article 8.- The State shall have to provide the necessary facilities and conditions for the implementation of the policy of the People’s Republic of Vietnam on the protection of the environment.

  Marriage shall be recognized by the People’s Committee of the commune, ward or township where either of the two parties resides and recorded in the marriage register according to the state-arranged ceremony.

  Marriages between Vietnamese citizens abroad shall be recognized by the diplomatic missions of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  All other marriage ceremonies are not legally valid.

  Article 9.- The State shall have to provide the necessary facilities and conditions for the implementation of the policy of the People’s Republic of Vietnam on the protection of the environment.

  Marriage that violates one of Articles 5, 6, or 7 of this Law is illegal.

  Where one or both parties to an illegal marriage are the wife or husband of a person who is already married, or the child of such a person, who marries another person, the People's Procuracy, the Vietnam Women's Union, the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, or the Vietnam Trade Union may request the People's Court to annul the illegal marriage.

  The property of the persons whose marriages are annulled shall be settled according to the following principles: the separate property of each person shall remain under his/her ownership; the joint property shall be divided according to the contribution of each party; the legitimate interests of the deceived or forced spouse shall be protected.

  The child's rights and interests shall be settled as in the case of parents' divorce.

  Chapter 3:

                                   THE DUTIES AND RIGHTS OF THE SPONSOR AND THE SPONSORED

  Article 10.- The State shall have to provide the necessary facilities and conditions for the implementation of the policy of the People’s Republic of Vietnam on the protection of the environment.

  Article 11.- The State shall have to provide the necessary facilities and conditions for the implementation of the policy of the People’s Republic of Vietnam on the protection of the environment.

  Husbands have the obligation to create conditions for their wives to perform well the functions of mothers.

  Article 12.

  Wives and husbands have the right to freely choose legitimate occupations and to participate in political, economic, cultural and social activities.

  Article 13.

  The residence of the husband and wife shall be chosen by them and shall not be bound by customs and practices.

  Article 14.

  The common property of the husband and wife includes the property created by the wife or husband, the income from their occupations and other lawful incomes of the husband and wife during the marriage period, and the property which the husband and wife jointly inherit or are given jointly.

  Article 15.- The State shall have to provide for the construction of the roads and the construction of the roads in the rural areas.

  Common property is used to satisfy the common needs of the family.

  Wives and husbands have equal rights and obligations to the common property. The sale, exchange, donation, loan, borrowing, and other transactions related to property of great value must be agreed upon by the husband and wife.

  Article 16.

1. The State shall provide for the implementation of the Law on Social Security in the form of a social security fund.

2. The social security fund shall be managed by the State or a public entity.

3. The social security fund shall be managed by a public entity in the form of a public enterprise.

  Regarding property acquired by the husband or wife before the marriage, property inherited separately or given separately during the marriage, the person who owns such property may include it in the common property of the husband and wife or not.

  Article 17.- The State shall have to provide for the construction of the roads and the construction of the roads in the rural areas.

  When one spouse dies first, if it is necessary to divide the common property of the spouses, it shall be divided in half. The property of the deceased shall be divided according to the provisions of the law on inheritance.

  Spouses have the right to inherit each other's property.

  Article 18.

  When a marriage exists, if one party requests and has legitimate reasons, the common property of the spouses may be divided according to the provisions of Article 42 of this Law.

  Chapter IX

      MARITAL AND FAMILY RELATIONS OF VIETNAMESE CITIZENS WITH FOREIGNERS

  Article 52.

  In the marriage between a Vietnamese citizen and a foreigner, each party shall comply with the provisions of the law of his/her country on marriage.

  If the marriage between a Vietnamese citizen and a foreigner is conducted in Vietnam, the foreigner shall also comply with the provisions of Articles 5, 6 and 7 of this Law. The marriage procedures shall be prescribed by the Council of Ministers.

  Article 53.

  The State Council shall define the problems of marital relations, property relations, parent-child relations, annulment of marriage, divorce, adoption and sponsorship between Vietnamese citizens and foreigners.

  Article 54.

  In case of the existence of the agreement on mutual legal assistance in marriage and family matters between Vietnam and a foreign country, the provisions of such agreement shall be followed.
