                                                  DECISION

    PROMULGATING TEMPORARY REGULATIONS ON THE ACCEPTANCE OF FOREIGNERS' ORPHANED, ABANDONED AND MARRIED OFFSPRING BY VIETNAMESE ORPHANS IN CHILDREN'S HOUSES MANAGED BY THE LABOR - TRADE UNION AND SOCIAL SECTOR

                                           THE MINISTRY PRESIDENTS COUNCIL

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Council of Ministers dated July 4, 1981;
  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated December 29, 1986;
  Pursuant to the Law on Protection, Care and Education of Children dated August 12, 1991;
  At the request of the Minister of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs,

                                                  DECISION

  Article 1. To promulgate the temporary regulations on the adoption of Vietnamese orphans, abandoned children and disabled children in the care of the labor, war invalids and social affairs sector, which are managed by the foreigner, together with this Decision.

  Article 2. This Decision takes effect from the date of signing. All previous regulations contrary to this Decision are annulled.

  Article 3. The Ministers, the heads of the State Committee, the heads of the agencies attached to the Council of Ministers, the Presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall be responsible for the implementation of this Decision.

                                                    REGULATIONS

     FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE:

Foreigners receiving foster children as Vietnamese children are being mistreated, abandoned, and neglected at child-rearing institutions, managed by the labor and trade union sector and the social sector.
        (Enacted together with Decision No. 145-HDBT of April 29, 1992 of the Council of Ministers)

  Chapter 1:

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- The adoption of Vietnamese orphans, abandoned children or disabled children by foreigners must be based on the affection between the adopter and the adopted child in the parent-child relationship, ensuring that the child is fully raised, cared for and educated.

  Article 2.- Foreigners who adopt children must be at least 20 years older than the adopted children.

  Article 3.- The adoptive children mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation are persons staying at the child-rearing establishments under the management of the Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs sector, including:

  - Children aged 15 and under, or those with disabilities, may be over 15.

  - Adopted children may be over 15 years of age in cases where the adopters are old and lonely.

  Article 4.- Adoption shall be based on the voluntary consent of the adopters, parents, sponsors or directors of the child-rearing establishments. If the child is 9 years of age or older, the consent of the child is also required.

  Article 5.- The People's Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities where child-rearing establishments are located as mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation shall consider and grant certificates of adoption of Vietnamese children by foreigners within 2 months from the date of receipt of complete dossiers. In case of refusal, the reasons shall be clearly explained to the adopters.

  Chapter II

                           CONDITIONS FOR FOREIGNERS TO ADOPT CHILDREN

  Article 6.- Foreigners who wish to adopt Vietnamese children mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation shall send to the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs the following documents:

  1. Permission for adoption granted by competent State agencies in the country of which the applicant is a citizen or permanent resident (the permission must clearly state the legitimate purpose of the adoption application);

  2. Certificates from competent foreign State agencies confirming the applicant's health status, mental and material capacity to adequately care for, educate and develop the adopted child physically, intellectually and morally;

  3. The adoption application shall be sent to the People’s Committee of the province or centrally-run city where the child-rearing establishment is located, clearly stating the reasons for the adoption, the applicant’s full name, age and address (if the applicant is married, the application must bear the signatures of both spouses), the full name, age and address of the child to be adopted, and other requirements.

  In case the full name, age and address of the child to be adopted are unknown, the foreign applicant may send an application expressing his/her desire for adoption and request the Vietnam Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs to select and introduce such child.

  The above-mentioned documents in foreign languages must be translated into Vietnamese and certified by a Vietnamese notary office.

  Article 7. The documents specified in Points 1 and 2 of Article 6 shall be valid for 6 months from the date of certification by the competent State agencies of the host country.

  Article 8. After being notified of the acceptance of the adoption application or having been legally authorized by the adopter, the applicant must come to Vietnam to carry out the following procedures with the Vietnam Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs:

  1. Present the permit for the child to enter the country of the adopter;

  2. Make a commitment to register the child’s full name at the local social organization (a non-governmental organization sponsored by the State) where the adopter resides;

  3. Make an annual commitment to send a notice with a photo of the adopted child until the child reaches the age of 18 years old, using the prescribed form, to the Vietnam Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs.

  4. To take care of the adopted child; to help the adopted child recover from illness or undergo rehabilitation to improve his/her disability if he/she is a disabled child.

  5. To provide the adopted child with education or vocational training.

  6. To pay all expenses related to the adoption procedures.

  Chapter 3:

                                            IMPLEMENTING ORGANIZATION

  Article 9.- The adoption of a child by a person shall be carried out when the following papers are available:

  1. The certificate of the People's Committee of a province or centrally-run city on the granting of a foreigner to adopt a Vietnamese child;

  2. The record of the handover of the child to be adopted by a foreigner;

  3. The commitments specified in Article 8 of this Regulation;

  4. The passport of the foreigner who adopts the child;

  5. In case the authorized person comes to receive the adopted child, he/she must have a legal authorization of the adopter.

  Article 10.- The Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall set up a dedicated working unit with the task of monitoring Vietnamese children adopted by foreigners according to the temporary regulations of this Decision.

  Article 11.- The Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall coordinate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Ministry of Justice in guiding the implementation of these temporary regulations.

  Article 12.- This Provisional Regulation takes effect from the date of its promulgation.
