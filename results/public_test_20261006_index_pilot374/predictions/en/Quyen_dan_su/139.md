                                                   CIRCULAR

  GUIDELINES FOR GRANTING STAY PERMIT, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE PERMIT, AND TEMPORARY STAY PERMIT TO FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM UNDER THE JURISDICTION OF THE MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS

  Pursuant to the Law on Entry, Exit, Transit, and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;

  Pursuant to the Law on promulgation of legal documents dated June 3, 2008;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 58/2013/ND-CP dated June 11, 2013 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP dated August 6, 2015 on coordination mechanisms between ministries, ministerial agencies, provincial-level People’s Committees in managing entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam;

  The Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgates the Circular guiding procedures for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards and extension of temporary residence for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  Chapter I

                                              GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  This Circular provides guidance on the issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence permits, and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs as prescribed in Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, and Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry, Exit, Transit, and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as the Law on Entry and Exit).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to foreigners under the management of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs who enter and reside in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.

  Chapter II

              JURISDICTION AND PROCEDURES FOR GRANTING STAY PERMIT, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE PERMIT, AND STAY PERMIT STICKS

  Article 3. Competence of relevant agencies in issuing visas, extending temporary residence permits, and issuing temporary residence cards

  1. The Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the Consular Department) shall receive and process applications for visas or extension of temporary residence permits for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit.

  2. The State Reception Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the State Reception Department) shall receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit (excluding members of foreign consulates in Ho Chi Minh City and their spouses, children under 18 years of age, and accompanying servants for the term of such members).

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City):

  a) Receive and process applications for visas and temporary residence extension for foreigners specified in Clause 1, Clause 2, and Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit in provinces and cities from Quang Nam province and Da Nang city to the south.

  b) Receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners who are members of foreign consulates in Ho Chi Minh City and their spouses, children under 18 years of age, and accompanying servants for the term of such members.

  4. Vietnamese representative missions abroad:

  a) Receive applications for visa issuance or temporary residence extension as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Circular, passports or documents valid as substitutes for passports (hereinafter referred to as passports), and visa application forms (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 5, 2015 of the Ministry of Public Security, which provides forms of documents related to entry, exit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam - hereinafter referred to as Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of foreigners specified in Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, and Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit to transfer information to the Consulate Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for processing according to their competence.

  b) Issue visas to foreigners specified in Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, and Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit based on notifications from the Consulate Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  Article 4. Procedures for processing applications for or notifications of visa issuance

  1. Agencies and organizations that invite or guarantee foreign nationals to enter Vietnam under Clause 2 and Clause 3 Article 15 of the Law on Immigration shall directly submit application forms for visa issuance to the Consulate General or the Department of Foreign Affairs.
  HO CHI MINH CITY

  The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (or another agency as prescribed by the sending country) of the country where a foreigner falls under the provisions of Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit may send the application for visa together with the passport and visa application form (using the NA1 form enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the foreigner to the Vietnamese representative mission in the host country or the concurrent mission.

  The overseas representative office of Vietnam shall, within 01 working day from the date of receipt of all documents, forward information to the Consular Department or the Foreign Affairs Department of Ho Chi Minh City for processing visa applications/requests within its competence.

  2. Application for visa, including:

  a) A written or diplomatic note notifying/asking for the issuance of a visa, which clearly states: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and code, date of issuance and expiration of the passport, occupation, position, purpose of entry, visa duration, visa type and place of receipt of the visa of the foreigner.

  b) In case a foreigner is a visitor to a member of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, in addition to the documents specified in Point a, Clause 2 of this Article, a copy of the identity card of the relevant representative mission member must be added.

  c) In case a foreigner is a contract employee working at a foreign representative office in Vietnam, in addition to the documents specified in Point a, Clause 2 of this Article, a valid employment contract is required.

  d) In case a foreigner is subject to the increase of the number of employees of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, in addition to the documents specified in Point a, Clause 2 of this Article, the diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the country of dispatch notifying the increase of the number of employees shall be added.

  3. Handling of visa application notifications/requests:

  a) The Consulate General or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as the Immigration Department) and the Vietnamese representative mission in the foreign country of the issuance of visas to foreigners subject to the provisions of Clause 1 and Clause 2, Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit within 01 working day from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application.

  b) The Consulate General shall send a written request to the Immigration Department, the State Reception Office and the relevant unit within 01 working day from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for the issuance of a visa to a foreigner subject to the provisions of Clause 3, Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit. After receiving the written reply from the State Reception Office and the relevant unit, and the Immigration Department has no opinion, the Consulate General shall reply to the requesting agency/organization and notify the Vietnamese representative mission in the foreign country of the issuance of a visa to the foreigner.

  c) The Consulate General or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify in writing the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security within 01 working day from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for the issuance of a visa to a foreigner subject to the provisions of Clause 4, Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit.

  The Consulate General or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall reply to the requesting agency/organization and notify the Vietnamese representative mission in the foreign country of the issuance of a visa to the foreigner within 02 working days from the date of notification to the Immigration Department if the latter has no opinion.

  d) The Consulate General or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the Vietnamese representative mission in the foreign country of the issuance of a visa to the foreigner including the following information: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and serial number, date of issuance and expiration of the passport, occupation, position, purpose of entry, visa duration, type of visa, place of receipt of the visa by the foreigner, and other information (if any).

  4. Issuance of visas:

  a) The overseas Vietnamese representative mission shall issue visas to foreigners within 01 working day from the date of receipt of the notification as prescribed in Point d Clause 3 of this Article, the passport and the visa application form (using the NA1 form enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the foreigner.

  b) In case the foreigner is approved for visa issuance at the border gate, the receiving agency specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall notify the visa-issuing agency/organization of the foreigner’s visa issuance at the border gate.

  5. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director General of the Consular Department to decide the reciprocation with the foreign party on the time limit for processing the application for visa issuance, as well as the components of the application for visa issuance, in accordance with the Law on Entry and Exit.

  Article 5. Procedures for processing applications for extension of temporary residence

  1. Foreigners who have been issued with visa marked NG1, NG2 and NG4, upon entering Vietnam, if they wish to extend their temporary residence, must go through the agency/organization inviting or guaranteeing the foreigner’s entry into Vietnam to submit the application for extension of temporary residence to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  2. Application for extension of temporary residence:

  a) A written or diplomatic note notifying or requesting the extension of temporary residence, which clearly states: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and mark, date of issuance and expiration of the passport, occupation, position, purpose of entry, requested extension of temporary residence and the request for issuance of a new visa (if any) of the foreigner.

  b) The foreigner’s passport, which is valid for more than 30 days compared to the requested extension of temporary residence.

  c) Application for visa issuance or extension of temporary residence (using Form NA5 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA), certified and stamped by the inviting or guaranteeing agency or organization of the foreigner.

  d) For foreigners entering Vietnam to visit members of foreign representative missions in Vietnam, a copy of the identity card of such member issued by the State General Office of Reception or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall be enclosed.

  e) For foreigners who are contract workers working at foreign representative missions in Vietnam, a copy of the valid labor contract shall be enclosed.

  3. Processing of applications for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the Consulate General or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider extending temporary residence and issue a new visa (if any) to the foreigner.

  b) The extended temporary residence period and the new visa (if any) shall comply with the purpose of entry and the application of the inviting or guaranteeing agency or organization and shall have a duration of no more than 12 months, which is at least 30 days shorter than the foreigner’s passport validity period.

  c) For persons specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article, the extended temporary residence period and the new visa (if any) shall comply with Point b Clause 3 of this Article and the validity period of the identity card of the foreign representative mission member in Vietnam issued by the State General Office of Reception or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  4. Foreigners who have entered Vietnam under the visa exemption regime specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit shall be considered for extension of temporary residence by the Consulate General or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City, with each extension period not exceeding the previously granted temporary residence period, based on the application submitted as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, unless otherwise provided for by an international treaty to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  5. The Director of the Consular Department shall decide on the extension of temporary residence and the issuance of new visas (if necessary) for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit who have entered Vietnam with an SQ visa.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director of the Consular Department to decide on the reciprocation of the extension of temporary residence for foreigners in accordance with the Law on Entry and Exit.

  Article 6. Procedures for processing applications for temporary residence cards

  1. The agency inviting or guaranteeing the foreigner specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit shall submit an application for a temporary residence card to the competent agency specified in Article 3 of this Circular.

  2. An application for a temporary residence card shall include:

  a) A diplomatic note requesting the issuance of a temporary residence card of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam, clearly stating the following information: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and serial number, occupation and position of the applicant (refer to the NA6 form enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA);

  For cases of replacement or addition of personnel of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam, the diplomatic note requesting the issuance of a temporary residence card must clearly state the full name and position of the person being replaced or added;

  b) An application form for a temporary residence card (using the NA8 form enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA), certified by a competent person and stamped by the foreign representative mission in Vietnam;

  c) Passport of foreigner still valid;

  d) In case foreigner is subject to increase of staff of foreign representative mission in Vietnam, besides documents specified in Points a, b and c Clause 1 of this Article, diplomatic note of Ministry of Foreign Affairs of sending country notifying increase of staff is required.

  3. Processing of issuance of temporary residence card:

  Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of complete and valid application according to Clause 2 of this Article, State Reception Bureau or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider issuing temporary residence card to foreigner.

  4. Temporary residence card shall bear the code NG3, have a validity period of not more than 05 years and shorter than validity period of passport by at least 30 days.

  Temporary residence card which has expired shall be considered for issuance of new temporary residence card at the request of foreign representative mission in Vietnam.

  5. State Reception Bureau and Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall transfer list of persons granted temporary residence card to immigration authority according to Clause 2 Article 7 of Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP of the Government on coordination between ministries, ministerial agencies, People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities in management of entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam.

  6. Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize Director of State Reception Bureau to decide reciprocation with foreign parties on time limit for processing of application for temporary residence card and components of application for temporary residence card, in accordance with Law on Entry and Exit.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 7. Transitional provisions

  1. Visa, temporary residence certificate and temporary residence card that are still valid before the effective date of this Circular may be used until the expiration date of the visa, temporary residence certificate or temporary residence card.

  2. Applications for visa, temporary residence card or temporary residence extension submitted before the effective date of this Circular but not yet processed shall be processed by the competent authority of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in accordance with this Circular.

  Article 8. Implementation

  1. The Consular Department shall:

  a) Guide foreign Vietnamese representative missions and relevant agencies in the issuance of visas and temporary residence extension in accordance with this Circular.

  b) Annually report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the visa issuance and temporary residence extension under this Circular.

  2. The State Reception Bureau shall:

  a) Guide foreign representative missions in Vietnam and relevant agencies on the procedures for issuing temporary residence cards under this Circular.

  b) Annually report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the temporary residence card issuance under this Circular.

  3. The Ho Chi Minh City Foreign Affairs Department shall report monthly, quarterly and annually on the visa issuance and temporary residence extension to the Consular Department; and on the temporary residence card issuance to the State Reception Bureau.

  4. Foreign representative missions in Vietnam shall:

  a) Issue visas under the notice of the Consular Department or the Ho Chi Minh City Foreign Affairs Department.

  b) Report monthly, quarterly and annually on the visa issuance to foreigners under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for the Consular Department.

  Article 9. Effect

  This Circular takes effect from September 1, 2016.

  Article 10. Implementation responsibilities

  Director of the Consular Department, Director of the State Reception Department, Director of the Ho Chi Minh City Foreign Affairs Service, heads of Vietnamese representative missions abroad and units affiliated to the Ministry shall be responsible for implementing this Circular. In case of any difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular, concerned agencies shall report to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (via the Consular Department for visa issuance and temporary residence extension procedures; via the State Reception Department for temporary residence card issuance procedures) for timely guidance./.
