                                                      DIRECTIVE

     ON A NUMBER OF SOLUTIONS TO SUPPORT THE SCHOOL’S DEVELOPMENT OF SAFE, HEALTHY AND RESILIENT INDUSTRIES

  Over the past period, the world's political and economic situation has been characterized by complex, unstable and unpredictable changes, affecting Vietnam's economy with a very large opening. In this context, the Government has issued many directives aimed at stabilizing macroeconomics, controlling inflation, ensuring major balances, helping the economy to recover and achieve relatively high growth rates compared to other countries in the region and in the world.

  On October 11, 2021, the Government promulgated Resolution No. 128/NQ-CP on safe and flexible adaptation to and effective control of COVID-19; Resolution No. 01/NQ-CP dated January 8, 2022 on major tasks and solutions for implementation of the socio-economic development plan and state budget estimate for 2022; Resolution No. 02/NQ-CP dated January 10, 2022 on major tasks and solutions for improvement of business environment and enhancement of national competitiveness in 2022; Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP dated January 30, 2022 on Resolution on socio-economic recovery and development program and implementation of Resolution No. 43/2022/QH15 of the National Assembly on fiscal and monetary policies supporting the Program. In particular, the 13th Central Committee of the Communist Party promulgated Resolution No. 18-NQ/TW on further reform, improvement of institutions and policies, enhancement of the effectiveness and efficiency of land management and use, and promotion of Vietnam’s development into a high-income developed country.

  The active and committed implementation of resolutions of the Communist Party and the State, regulations of law, and direction, management, solutions, and policies of the Government regarding the real estate market by authorities, sectors, localities, and enterprises has led to positive changes in socio-economic situations, ensured social security, and maintained macroeconomic stability.

  The real estate market plays a very important role in maintaining macroeconomic stability, controlling inflation, ensuring major balance, and promoting growth. However, despite the positive results, the development of the real estate sector has also revealed many problems, limitations, and shortcomings, and is not truly sustainable, with many risks remaining, such as: the structure of real estate goods is unreasonable, real estate product prices, especially housing prices, are still quite high compared to people's incomes, the information database on the real estate market is not synchronized, complete, reliable, or transparent.

  Some problems and shortcomings to be addressed include: The legal system related to real estate market has some contents that have not yet met practical needs and have not been promptly amended or supplemented; the development of real estate projects in some localities does not follow the planning and plan and is not suitable for the market demand; the investment procedures are prescribed in different legal documents through many steps, resulting in a prolonged time for implementing investment procedures; regulations on mandatory real estate transactions via the platform have created the basis for the formation of "ghost projects" and customer fraud cases, while the state also faces many difficulties in managing information about the real estate market, leading to tax evasion and loss of revenue for the state budget; the state management is not strict, timely, or satisfactory...

  In order to address the above-mentioned problems and shortcomings and continue to promote the safe, healthy, and sustainable development of the real estate market in the future, the Prime Minister requests:

  I. A NUMBER OF GENERAL CONCERNS AND SOLUTIONS

  1. Review the provisions of law to resolve immediate and long-term issues in the direction of building a healthy, appropriate, and effective policy tool to ensure interconnection, safety, and certainty between capital markets and the real estate market, diversifying capital sources for the real estate market; promoting decentralization and delegation at all levels, conducting thorough research, proposing pilot policies to create new momentum and force for market development.

  2. Persistently, firmly, and decisively develop a safe, healthy, and sustainable real estate ecosystem; do not tighten credit unreasonably, but strengthen inspection and supervision, do not relax state management; do not criminalize economic - civil relations; proactively detect, prevent, and strictly handle violations in accordance with the law, paying attention to acts of tax evasion in real estate business, not allowing collapse, protecting those who do the right thing, protecting and harmonizing the interests of the state, the people, and enterprises.

  3. Harmonize, reasonably and effectively combine fiscal policies and monetary policies, implement monetary policies in a flexible, safe and cautious manner, expand fiscal policies in a reasonable manner, focus on ensuring effectiveness, strictly manage prices and markets; do not implement policies abruptly, do not suddenly change from “loosening” to strict control or vice versa.

  4. Ministries, sectors and localities shall focus on synchronously implementing solutions prescribed in Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on socio-economic rehabilitation and development program.

  5. Focus on implementing solutions to attract investment, promote production and business activities, create more jobs in various fields and localities; stabilize macroeconomics, control inflation, ensure major balance of the economy.

  6. Specifically and accurately assess the situation and supply-demand relationship to develop real estate market on the basis of respecting market laws and parallel competition laws, promote the regulatory role of the State, policy tools to intervene and control when necessary in a proper, timely, effective manner, prevent manipulation of the market, hoarding and price increase...

  7. Identify market segments, demands such as industrial real estate, worker housing, low-income housing to have appropriate policies to promote the market, focus on social security, create opportunities for people with low incomes, “not sacrifice progress and social equality for mere economic growth”.

  8. Develop strategic infrastructure to create new development space, new development driving force, promote the formation of new industries, services and urban areas.

  9. Strengthen inspection, examination and audit to resolve difficulties, handle violations to protect the market, protect assets, protect people, do not legalize violations but objectively evaluate, understand causes, have mechanisms to resolve practical difficulties.

  10. Do well in communication work, have appropriate forms, effectively promote education and training in financial knowledge and legal knowledge for the people, in schools and by other means.

  II. SOME SPECIALIZED TASKS AND SOLUTIONS

  1. Ministry of Construction

  a) Expeditiously study and complete the legal system related to real estate sector to ensure consistency, resolve difficulties and obstacles, enable the real estate market to develop stably and healthily, build a real estate market information system associated with land information, improve legal basis and increase implementation of non-cash payment in real estate transactions. Accordingly, propose to submit to National Assembly for opinions and approve Law on Housing (amended), Law on Real Estate Business (amended) to create legal framework for real estate business activities, enhance effectiveness of real estate market management; expedit amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing, Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP dated April 01, 2021 of the Government on amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing in accordance with Law on Housing.

  b) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries and central authorities in studying and proposing to promulgate regulations guiding the process of implementation of housing projects and urban areas.

  c) Regularly monitor the situation and development of the real estate market; submit quarterly reports on real estate business and real estate market nationwide to the Prime Minister and propose solutions for the real estate market to develop stably and healthily.

  d) Inspect and examine the implementation of law on real estate business, real estate transaction center operation, real estate brokerage; cooperate with provincial People’s Committees in organizing inspection and review of housing projects and real estate business to propose to competent state agencies to revoke, suspend, temporarily stop or adjust, convert, transfer real estate business projects as prescribed by law.

  dd) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in establishing working groups to direct, guide, and urge local governments to implement Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on the program for socio-economic rehabilitation and development regarding policies supporting social housing development, worker housing in industrial parks, and renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings.

  e) Direct, guide, and cooperate with local governments in reviewing and supplementing planning and allocating land fund for social housing and worker housing development in accordance with regulations of law, strictly complying with regulations on allocation of 20% land fund in housing projects and urban areas, ensuring demand for social housing and worker housing development in their areas.

  g) Direct, guide, and cooperate with local governments in reviewing, preparing, and announcing list of investors in social housing, worker housing, and renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings eligible for loan packages with 2% interest via commercial banking system.

  h) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in researching and proposing policies to promote development of social housing and worker housing in industrial parks.

  i) Improve and implement public information systems on housing and real estate market.

  2. Ministry of Finance

  a) Urgently amend Decree No. 153/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020 of the Government on sale and transaction of private corporate bonds on domestic market and sale of corporate bonds on international market.

  b) Control capital mobilization activities of real estate enterprises on stock market to avoid speculation, manipulation, and price manipulation.

  c) Cooperate with the State Bank of Vietnam in inspecting and supervising compliance with laws on issuance, investment, and provision of services regarding corporate bonds, especially issuance of separate bonds of real estate enterprises, credit institutions related to real estate enterprises, enterprises with large issuance volume, high interest rates, loss-making enterprises, and issuing enterprises having no collateral.

  d) Direct and guide implementation and capital mobilization activities (including bond issuance) of real estate trading enterprises on the securities market in accordance with law; create conditions for, and not obstruct, enterprises (with sufficient capacity, good and healthy business results, etc.) to mobilize capital to support recovery and development.

  dd) Research and improve tax policies suitable for reality regarding real estate in order to encourage effective use of houses and land, contribute to limiting real estate speculation, ensure reasonable and stable revenues for the state budget, conform to socio-economic conditions of Vietnam and international practices.

  3. Ministry of Planning and Investment

  a) Direct, urge, and guide localities, propose solutions to difficulties and obstacles, resolutely reduce unnecessary administrative procedures in investment procedures, approval of investment guidelines, investment decisions, and issuance of certificates of investment registration for housing and real estate projects to support and increase supply for the market.

  b) Monitor and promote attracting foreign investment in the real estate business sector.

  4. Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment

  a) Cooperate with the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Justice in researching, reviewing, and amending current regulations of law related to land use rights auction (law on auction, law on land, law on tax administration) to ensure consistency and conformity with actual situation in localities.

  b) Inspect and inspect, and direct local governments to prevent and strictly handle violations in land use rights auction activities in accordance with law.

  c) Inspect and review indicators of land use planning and plans to ensure appropriate land use planning for various industries, professions and fields in a harmonious and rational manner, avoiding overlapping and disputes.

  5. Ministry of Public Security Take charge and cooperate closely with the Ministry of Finance, State Bank of Vietnam and relevant agencies in enhancing macroeconomics monitoring; take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Construction and the Ministry of Finance in strictly handling illegal real estate trading floors and real estate brokerage activities, tax evasion, providing inaccurate information, causing negative impacts on financial, credit and real estate market activities.

  6. Ministry of Information and Communications Directs news and press agencies to enhance provision of timely, official, accurate and adequate information on real estate market, securities market and bond market; strictly handle acts of providing inaccurate information causing negative impacts on financial, credit and real estate market activities.

  7. State Bank of Vietnam

  Strictly monitor credit in real estate sector. Direct credit institutions to strictly control credit quality and grant loans in real estate sector in accordance with regulations of law; promptly consider prioritizing loans for social housing, worker housing, affordable commercial housing with high efficiency and capable of being repaid.

  8. People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities

  a) Expeditiously organize the formulation, appraisal and approval of urban and rural construction planning, land use planning and plans; publicly disclose information on construction planning, lists and progress of infrastructure development projects and real estate projects, especially large-scale ones, as well as the merger, establishment or upgrading of local administrative units, thereby preventing rumors and speculation to push up prices for illegal profit-seeking purposes.

  b) Expeditiously develop housing development programs for the period from now to 2025 and 2030 and five-year and annual housing development plans in accordance with the Law on Housing, which clearly define lists of housing projects serving as the basis for approving investment guidelines of housing projects in the area.

  c) Review and produce lists of local housing and real estate projects; specifically assess reasons and causes for projects approved for investment guidelines but not yet implemented or delayed implementation. On this basis, focus on resolving legal and procedural difficulties in preparing investment for housing projects to increase supply for the market.

  d) Organize public disclosure of lists of projects, land fund, procedures for housing and real estate development in the area to attract investment; facilitate domestic and foreign investors to easily access and participate in housing and real estate investment.

  dd) Focus on directing and organizing implementation of land allocation, selection of project developers for new real estate projects and housing projects to increase supply for the market.

  e) Promote implementation of land use right auctions; bidding for land-based projects to select investors to increase supply of commercial housing projects for the real estate market.

  g) Implement solutions to promote development of social housing and worker housing in the area:

  - Review and supplement planning, allocate land fund for social housing and worker housing development in accordance with the law, strictly implement regulations on land fund allocation of 20% in housing projects and urban areas, and ensure demand for social housing and worker housing development in the area.

  - Focus on implementing Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on socio-economic recovery and development program, which urgently produce lists of projects, review land fund, complete legal procedures, select project developers for implementation of social housing, worker housing in industrial parks, renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings in the area.

  - Review eligible entities and conditions to produce and publish list of investors in social housing, worker housing, renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings eligible for concessional loans at 2% via commercial bank system according to Decree on interest support from state budget for loans granted by enterprises, cooperatives, business households.

  - Cooperate with Vietnam Bank for Social Policies in reviewing and consolidating demand of individuals, households borrowing money to purchase, lease-purchase social housing, housing for workers; build, renovate, repair houses according to Article 16 of Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP, Clause 10 Article 1 of Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP and relevant documents to ensure accuracy and reflect actual demand of people.

  h) Expeditiously complete quality inspection and assessment of remaining old apartment buildings in the area to determine which apartment buildings must be deconstructed; promptly produce and approve plans, lists of apartment building renovation and reconstruction projects according to Decree No. 69/2021/ND-CP.

  i) Control, detect, prevent, and strictly handle violations against regulations on land use right auctions.

  k) Adopt measures to manage and prevent separation, “zoning division, land sale” in areas where investment is not permitted or infrastructure is insufficient; strengthen control of real estate trading floors, organizations, individuals engaging in real estate brokerage; rectify direct trading, “pushing prices” causing disorder in real estate market information.

  l) Review projects using land but delayed implementation, left unused, used for wrong purposes, wasted land; cooperate with relevant agencies in considering, guiding handling within their competence and according to land laws, other relevant law provisions to effectively use land fund, develop in a manner suitable for actual demand of society.

  m) Closely monitor and promptly grasp information and market developments, and take timely measures to stabilize the market and prevent property price spikes and real estate bubbles in their provinces.

  n) Intensify inspection and examination, and strictly handle violations against regulations on real estate trading, land, construction, and relevant laws (if any).

  9. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, and Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall, within their assigned functions and tasks, thoroughly understand and strictly implement this Directive; and submit biannual and annual reports to the Ministry of Construction for consolidation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  10. The Ministry of Construction shall take charge and cooperate with the Government Office and relevant agencies in guiding, expediting, and inspecting the implementation of this Directive, and consolidate and report to the Prime Minister.
