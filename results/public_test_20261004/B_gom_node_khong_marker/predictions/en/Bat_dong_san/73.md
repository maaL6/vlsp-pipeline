                                                    TELECOMMUNICATION

   ON IMPLEMENTATION OF DECISION NO. 25/2023/QD-TTG DATED OCTOBER 3, 2023 OF THE PRIME MINISTER ON REDUCTION IN LAND RENTS FOR 2023.

                                 THE TAX AGENCY GENERAL THE TAX AGENCY GENERAL

                        - Tax Departments of provinces and central-affiliated cities;
                        - Departments and Units under the Tax Agency.
  On October 03, 2023, The Prime Minister issued Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg on reduction of land rent in 2023. To promptly implement the provisions of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister, the Tax Agency requests the Director of Tax Departments of provinces and central-affiliated cities to direct Tax Departments and Sub-Departments to promptly carry out the following tasks based on the provisions of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister:

  1. Tax authorities at all levels shall organize extensive and adequate communication about the policy on reduction of land rent as prescribed in Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg on reduction of land rent in 2023 to organizations, units, enterprises, households and individuals that are directly leased land by the State under the Land Lease Decision or Land Lease Contract or Certificate of land use rights, ownership of houses and other property on land issued by competent state agencies in form of annual land rent payment, specifically:

  - In each tax management area, tax authorities shall actively cooperate with press agencies, radio stations, television stations, branches/representative offices of Vietnam Customs, etc. in organizing communication and dissemination of the contents of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg.

  - Develop documents guiding the implementation of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg, publish these documents on the website of the Tax Agency and send them via email to organizations, units, enterprises, households and individuals that are directly leased land by the State under the Land Lease Decision or Land Lease Contract or Certificate of land use rights, ownership of houses and other property on land issued by competent state agencies in form of annual land rent payment.

  - Communication information must be provided regularly and continuously in various forms to ensure taxpayers can access and fully understand the scope, eligible taxpayers, deadline, procedures, and benefits when implementing policies on land rent reduction to enable taxpayers to comply with and promptly implement state support policies and resolve financial difficulties for taxpayers.

  2. Process applications for land rent reduction according to Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister.

  Based on the 2023 land rent application submitted by the land lessee according to Article 4 of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister, the tax authority shall determine the amount of land rent eligible for reduction and issue a decision on land rent reduction as prescribed by law on collection of land rents. At the same time, consolidate the situation of land rent reduction under Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg within provinces/cities, update information on land rent reduction of taxpayers, the amount of land rent payable in 2023, and adjust the corresponding late payment interest (if any) on the system. In case the land lessee has paid the land rent in 2023 but there is an excess amount after the competent authority determines and decides the land rent reduction, the excess amount shall be deducted from the land rent of the subsequent period or the next year according to regulations of law on tax administration and other relevant laws. In case there is no subsequent period for land rent payment, make offsetting or refund the excess amount according to regulations of law on tax administration and other relevant laws.

  3. The head of the tax authority shall assign relevant departments within the tax authority to update, search, review, and inspect the dossiers and expedite implementation as prescribed by law. In case it is detected that the taxpayer is ineligible for land rent reduction as prescribed, notify the taxpayer to pay according to the current law on land rent (not according to Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister).

  4. The Tax Department shall consolidate and submit a report on the implementation of Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg (according to the appendix/form enclosed therewith), specifically: the implementation status, the amount of land rent reduction, the number of applications for reduction, the number of applications already reduced, the number of pending applications (if any) and the reasons for failure to reduce them, difficulties and proposals (if any) to the Department of Policy - General Tax Department before April 30, 2024 at the address of the General Tax Department, 123 Lò Đúc, Hai Bà Trưng, Hà Nội, and send the soft copy via email: nbngoc01@gmail.com.

  The General Tax Department requests the Directors of the Tax Departments to seriously implement this Decision. In case of any actual difficulty arising, promptly report it to the General Tax Department for guidance on resolution./.
