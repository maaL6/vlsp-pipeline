                                                    DECREE
    ELABORATION AND GUIDANCE ON IMPLEMENTATION OF CERTAIN ARTICLES OF THE CIVIL CODE AND THE LAW ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY REGARDING COPYRIGHTS AND RELATED RIGHTS
                                                   GOV
   Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
   Pursuant to the Civil Code of June 14, 2005;
   Pursuant to the Intellectual Property Law dated November 29, 2005;
   At the proposal of the Minister of Culture and Information,

DECREES:
   Chapter 1.
                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS
   Article 1. Scope of regulation
   This Decree provides detailed regulations and guidelines for a number of articles of the Civil Code and the Intellectual Property Law regarding copyright and related rights.
   Article 2: Subjects of application
   This Decree applies to Vietnamese organizations and individuals; foreign organizations and individuals engaged in activities related to copyright and related rights.
   Article 3: Protection of copyright and related rights
   1. Copyright protection means the protection of authors' rights to literary, artistic and scientific works as defined in Article 738 of the Civil Code and Articles 18, 19 and 20 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Related rights protection means the protection of rights of performers to their performances; rights of producers of audio and video recordings to such recordings; rights of broadcasting organizations to broadcasts and encrypted program-carrying satellite signals prescribed in Articles 745, 746, 747 and 748 of the Civil Code and Articles 29, 30 and 31 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   Article 4.- Interpretation of terms
   In this Decree, the terms below are construed as follows:
   1. “Dead work” means a work first published after its author has died.
   2. “Anonymous work” means a work not bearing the name of its author (real name or pen name) when it is published.
   3. “Original work” means a work existing in a material form in which the creation of the work is first fixed.
   4. “Copy of a work” means a direct or indirect copy of part or the whole of a work. A photocopy of a work is also a copy of the work.
   5. “Fixation” means the expression in writing, other characters, lines, three-dimensional figures, layout, colors, sounds, images or the reproduction of sounds and images in a certain material form so that they can be recognized, copied or transmitted from such a fixed form.
   6. “Audio and video recording” means a fixation of sounds and images of a performance or other sounds and images, or the reproduction of sounds and images not in the form of a fixation associated with cinematographic works or other audiovisual works.

   7. “Copy of an audio and video recording” means a direct or indirect copy of part or the whole of an audio and video recording.
   8. Public disclosure of fixed performances or phonograms and video recordings means the making available to the public of copies of fixed performances or phonograms and video recordings with the consent of related right holders.
   9. Re-broadcasting means simultaneous broadcasting of a broadcast by one broadcasting organization from another broadcasting organization. Re-broadcasting is also re-transmission.
   10. Encrypted program-carrying satellite signals mean signals carrying programs transmitted via satellites in such a form that their sound or visual characteristics or both have been altered for the purpose of preventing illegal reception of the programs.
   Article 5. State policies on copyright and related rights The State policies on copyright and related rights stipulated in Clauses 2, 3 and 4, Article 8 of the Intellectual Property Law include:
   1. Financial support for purchase of copyrights by State agencies and organizations tasked with disseminating works, performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasts of ideological, scientific and artistic value in service of the public interest, thereby contributing to socio-economic development.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Planning and Investment and relevant agencies in, guiding the formulation of financial plans (sources and funds) and mechanisms for the purchase of copyrights.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall approve the list of works eligible for purchase of copyrights by central agencies and organizations; provincial-level People’s Committees shall approve the list of works eligible for purchase of copyrights by local agencies and organizations.
   2. Priority investment in, training and fostering officials and public employees engaged in the management and enforcement of copyright and related rights protection work from the central to local levels.
   To train and foster a contingent of officials performing the task of protecting their rights in collective representation of copyright and related rights.
   3. To prioritize scientific research topics related to law, mechanisms and policies, and apply scientific-technical and technological measures to protect copyright and related rights.
   4. To enhance the education of knowledge about copyright and related rights in educational systems at all levels. The Ministry of Education and Training shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information in, including the contents of copyright and related rights teaching in the university, college and vocational secondary education programs.
   Article 6.- Contents and responsibilities for State management of copyright and related rights
  1. The Government shall perform the unified State management of copyright and related rights.
   2. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall be answerable before the Government for performing the State management of copyright and related rights, having the following tasks and powers:
   a/ To formulate and direct the implementation of strategies, laws, mechanisms and policies on protection of copyright and related rights;
   b/ To promulgate, organize and guide the implementation of legal documents on copyright and related rights according to law provisions;
   c/ To apply measures to protect lawful rights of the State, organizations and individuals in the domain of copyright and related rights protection;
   d/ To manage copyright to works (including computer programs and data collections) and related rights to performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasts under the State's ownership as prescribed by law;
   e/ To provide for the provision, cooperation, ordering and assurance of copyright to works and related rights to performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasts;

   f/ To set up and manage the copyright and related rights apparatus; to train and foster officials in copyright and related rights;
   g/ To manage activities of organizations representing collective rights of copyright and related rights;
   h/ To grant, re-grant, change and cancel certificates of copyright registration, certificates of related right registration and other procedures related to these certificates;
   i/ To make and manage the national register of copyright and related rights;
   j/ To publish and issue Official Gazette on copyright and related rights;
   1. To organize and direct activities of education, propagation and dissemination of knowledge about, and mechanisms and policies on copyright and related rights, and information and statistics on copyright and related rights;
   2. To organize and manage activities of assessment of copyright and related rights;
   3. To inspect, examine and handle violations of the law on copyright and related rights; to settle complaints and denunciations about copyright and related rights;
   4. To carry out international cooperation on copyright and related rights.
   3. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall be responsible before the Government for assuming the prime responsibility for, and coordinating with ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and provincial-level People’s Committees (below referred collectively to as provincial-level People’s Committees) in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   The Copyright Office of Vietnam is an agency of the Ministry of Culture and Information, assisting the Minister of Culture and Information in performing the function of state management of copyright and related rights. The Minister of Culture and Information shall specify the functions, tasks and powers of the Copyright Office of Vietnam in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall coordinate with the Ministry of Science and Technology in formulating general policies and laws on intellectual property protection, compiling general information on intellectual property, undertaking joint international cooperation projects on intellectual property and performing other general tasks under the direction of the Government.
   4. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and provincial-level People’s Committees shall, within the scope of their respective tasks and powers, coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   Article 7.- The State management competence of the provincial People’s Committees
   1. The provincial People’s Committees have the following tasks and powers:
   a/ To organize the guidance of the implementation of the provisions of law on copyright and related rights in their localities;
   b/ To promulgate documents according to their competence to guide and direct the implementation of the provisions of law and policies on copyright and related rights suitable to the local conditions;
   c/ To organize activities to protect copyright and related rights in their localities; to apply measures to protect the legitimate rights and interests of the State, organizations and individuals regarding copyright and related rights;
   d/ To inspect, examine and handle according to their competence complaints, denunciations and violations of the provisions of law on copyright and related rights in their localities;
   e/ To guide and receive applications for registration of copyright and related rights according to their competence and the provisions of law;
   f/ To coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information and concerned ministries, branches and provincial-level People’s Committees in the protection of copyright and related rights.
   2. The provincial/municipal Culture and Information Services shall assist the provincial-level People’s Committees in performing the function of State management over copyright and related rights.
   The presidents of the provincial-level People’s Committees shall specify the functions, tasks and powers of the provincial/municipal Culture and Information Services and district-and commune-level People’s Committees in performing the function of State management over copyright and related rights.
   Chapter II

                                                 COPYRIGHT
   Article 8.- Authors
   1. Authors are those who personally create parts or the whole of literary, artistic and scientific works, including:
   a/ Vietnamese individuals whose works are protected by copyright;
   b/ Foreign individuals whose works are created and expressed in a certain material form in Vietnam;
   c/ Foreign individuals whose works are first published in Vietnam;
   d/ Foreign individuals whose works are protected in Vietnam under international agreements on copyright to which Vietnam is a contracting party.
   2. Organizations and individuals who support, give opinions or supply materials to others for creation of works shall not be recognized as authors.
   Article 9.- Works expressed in other characters
   "Work presented in other forms specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means a work presented in symbols instead of letters such as Braille for the blind, shorthand symbols and similar symbols which can be copied by different forms by users.
   Article 10. Lectures, speeches and other presentations
   Lectures, speeches and other presentations specified at Point b, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law are works expressed in spoken language and fixed in a certain material form.
   Article 11.- Press works
   Press works specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law include categories such as reportage, fast news, news report, interview, comment, investigation, editorial, special report, press comment and other categories for publication or broadcast on print, radio, television, online media or other means.
   Article 12.- Musical works
   Musical works specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in musical notes in musical pieces or other musical symbols with or without lyrics, regardless of whether they are performed or not.
   Article 13. Theatrical works
   Theatrical works specified at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works of various art forms of performance, including drama (spoken drama, musical and dance drama, opera, silent drama), circus, dance, pantomime and other types of theatrical works.
   Article 14: Cinematographic works
   " cinematographic works and works created by a process similar to that specified at Point f, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works composed of a series of consecutive images creating a moving effect accompanied or not accompanied by sound, expressed on a certain material and which can be distributed and communicated to the public by technical devices and technologies, including the following types: feature films, documentaries, scientific films, animated films and other similar types.
   Article 15.- Plastic art works and applied fine arts works
   1. Plastic works specified at Point g, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in lines, colors, three-dimensional forms and layouts such as paintings, graphics, sculptures, installation art and similar forms, existing in the form of original copies. Particularly for graphic works, they may be expressed up to the 50th version, which are numbered and signed by authors.
   2. Applied fine art works specified at Point g, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in lines, colors, three-dimensional forms and layouts with useful features which can be attached to useful objects and are mass-produced by hand or by machines, such as emblems; handicrafts; forms expressed on products or product packages.
   Article 16: Photographic works
   "Photographic works specified at Point h, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works showing images of the objective world on photosensitive materials or on media from which such images are created or can be created by any technical method (chemical, electronic or other methods);
   Still images taken from a cinematographic work or similar to cinematography are not considered photographic works but are part of such cinematographic work.
   Article 17.- Architectural works
   Architectural works specified at Point i, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means design drawings in any form expressing creative ideas on houses, construction works and space planning (construction planning) which have been or have not yet been built. Architectural works include design drawings on ground plans, top plans, cross-sectional views and perspective views expressing creative ideas on houses, works, architectural work complexes, space organization, landscape architecture of a region, an urban area, an urban system, an urban functional zone or a rural residential area.
   The model and plan for a house, construction work or space planning shall be considered as an independent architectural work.
   Article 18.- Schematics, plans, maps and drawings
   Sketches, plans, maps and drawings specified at Point k, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law include sketches, plans, maps and drawings related to topography, scientific works and architectural works.
   Article 19.- Copyright in cinematographic works and theatrical works
   1. Cinematographic and theatrical works shall be created by a collective of authors. Persons who participate in the creation of cinematographic and theatrical works specified in Clause 1, Article 21 of the Intellectual Property Law shall enjoy the moral rights to their creations according to Clauses 1, 2 and 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law. Producers, directors and screenwriters may reach agreement on the exercise of the right to title cinematographic works specified in Clause 1, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law and the revision of scripts of cinematographic works specified in Clause 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Organizations and individuals that invest financial resources and material and technical facilities in the production of cinematographic works or staging of theatrical works specified in Clause 2, Article 21 of the Intellectual Property Law are holders of the rights specified in Clause 3, Article 19 and Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   Organizations and individuals that invest financial resources and material and technical facilities in the production of cinematographic works or staging of theatrical works may reach agreements on the exercise of the rights specified in Clause 3, Article 19 and Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law and obligations specified in Clause 3, Article 21 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   Article 20: Use of folklore literature and art works
   1. Folklore literature and art works specified at Points a, b and c, Clause 1, Article 23 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be protected regardless of their fixation.
   2. The use of folklore literature and art works specified in Clause 2, Article 23 of the Intellectual Property Law means the study and introduction of the authentic value of such folklore literature and art works.
   3. Users of folklore literature and art works specified in Clause 2 of this Article shall reach agreements on the payment of remuneration to the keeper of such folklore literature and art works and shall enjoy the copyright to their study and introduction.
   4. Reference to the origin of a folklore literature and art work specified in Clause 2, Article 23 of the Intellectual Property Law means the indication of the place name of the community where such folklore literature and art work is formed.
   Article 21: Subjects not covered by copyright protection

   1. News of the day, which are purely for information provision, as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 15 of the Intellectual Property Law, are daily news briefs which are merely informative and not creative.
   2. Administrative documents prescribed in Clause 2, Article 15 of the Intellectual Property Law include documents of State agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-politico-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, economic organizations, people’s armed forces units and other organizations as prescribed by law.
   Article 22. Moral rights
   1. The right to name works prescribed in Clause 1, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law shall not apply to works translated from one language into another.
   2. The right to publish or authorize others to publish works prescribed in Clause 3, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law means the distribution of works to the public in sufficient copies to meet reasonable public demand, depending on the nature of the work, by authors or copyright holders or by other individuals or organizations with the consent of the author or copyright holder.
   The publication of a work does not include the performance of a theatrical, cinematographic or musical work; reading a literary work before the public; broadcasting a literary or artistic work; displaying a plastic work; building a work from an architectural work.
   3. The right to protect the integrity of a work and prevent others from modifying or editing it prescribed in Clause 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law means the non-permissibility for others to modify or edit a work unless otherwise agreed by the author.
   4. Authors of computer programs and investors in computer program production may agree on the naming and development of computer programs.
   Article 23.- Property rights
   1. The right to perform works in public as prescribed at Point b, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be exercised exclusively by copyright holders or permitted by them for direct performance of works or through audio and video recordings or any technical means accessible to the public.
   In this clause, the public performance of a work includes the performance of the work anywhere except at home.
   2. The reproduction right prescribed at Point c, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law is the right exercised exclusively by copyright holders or permitted by them for others to make copies of works by whatever means or forms, including the regular or temporary storage of works in electronic form.
   3. The distribution right of originals or copies of works prescribed at Point d, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law is the right exercised exclusively by copyright holders or permitted by them for others to use by whatever technical means or forms accessible to the public for sale, lease or other transfer of originals or copies of works.
   For plastic works and photographic works, the distribution right also covers the display and exhibition before the public.
   4. The right to communicate works to the public by wire, wireless means, electronic information networks or any other technical means prescribed at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law is the exclusive right exercised by copyright holders or permitted by them for others to put works or copies of works to the public which can be accessed by the public at places and times they themselves choose.
   5. The right to lease cinematographic originals or copies, computer programs prescribed at Point e, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be exercised exclusively by copyright holders or permitted by them for others to use under a definite term.

   1. The right to lease computer programs shall not apply when such programs are not themselves the main objects of lease as computer programs associated with the normal operation of means of transport as well as other technical machines and equipment.
   Article 24.- Reasonable quoting and importation of copies of works
   1. Reasonable quoting of works without misrepresenting the authors’ opinions for comments or illustrations in one’s own works, as provided for at Point b, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law, must comply with the following conditions:
   a/ The quoted parts aim only to introduce, comment or clarify matters mentioned in one’s own works;
   b/ The quantity and actual nature of the quoted parts from the works used for quoting do not cause prejudice to the copyright on the works used for quoting; and are compatible with the nature and characteristics of the type of work used for quoting.
   2. Importation of copies of works of others for personal use, as provided for at Point k, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law, shall apply only to cases of importing not more than one copy.
   3. The use of works in the cases specified in Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law shall not apply to the reproduction of architectural works, plastic works and computer programs.
   Article 25.- Reproducing works
   1. Self-copying one copy specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law applies to cases of scientific research and teaching by individuals not for commercial purposes.
   2. Reproduction of works for archival in libraries for research purposes specified at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law means reproduction of not more than one copy.
   3. The library must not reproduce and distribute copies of the work to the public, including digital copies.

Article 26. Term of copyright protection
   1. The term of protection of property rights and personal rights specified in Clause 3, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law for posthumous works shall be fifty years from the date such works are first published.
   2. The term of protection of property rights and personal rights specified in Clause 3, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law for photographic works and works of applied fine art specified at Point a, Clause 2, Article 27 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be fifty years from the first publication of such works. Within fifty years, if the works have not yet been published, the term of protection shall be fifty years from the date such works are fixed.
   3. From the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law on July 1, 2006, the term of protection for photographic works and works of applied fine art shall be calculated according to the provisions in Clause 2 of this Article.
   Article 27.- Copyright holders
   Copyright owners specified in Article 36 of the Intellectual Property Law include:
   1. Vietnamese organizations and individuals;
   2. Foreign organizations and individuals having works created and expressed in certain material forms in Vietnam;
   3. Foreign organizations and individuals having works first published in Vietnam;
   4. Foreign organizations and individuals having works protected in Vietnam under international treaties on copyright to which Vietnam is a contracting party.
   Article 28. Copyright owners of anonymous works
   1. Anonymous works specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law are owned by the State.
   2. In case anonymous works are managed by organizations or individuals, such organizations or individuals shall enjoy the rights of their owners.

   3. When the actual owner of a work is identified, the ownership right shall belong to such owner as from the date the owner's identity is identified.
   Article 29.- Use of works under the State ownership
  1. Organizations and individuals that use works under the State ownership as stipulated in Points a and b, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law, except for cases specified in Clauses 2 and 3, Article 28 of this Decree, shall have to perform the following obligations:
   a/ To obtain permission for use;
   b/ To pay royalties, remunerations or other material benefits;
   c/ To submit one copy of the work within thirty days after its dissemination and circulation.
   2. Organizations and individuals that perform the obligations stipulated in Clause 1 of this Article shall do so at the Copyright Department of Culture and Art.
   3. The Copyright Department of Culture and Art shall be responsible for receiving copyrights in whatever form from organizations and individuals defined in Point c, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law according to law provisions.
   4. The Ministry of Culture and Information and the Ministry of Finance shall stipulate the financial management regime stipulated in Point b, Clause 1 of this Article.
   Article 30.- Use of works belonging to the public
   1. Organizations and individuals that use works belonging to the public defined in Article 43 of the Intellectual Property Law must respect the moral rights provided for in Clauses 1, 2 and 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   The copyright holders of works belonging to the public shall not enjoy the right to publish as provided for in Clause 3, Article 19 and the economic rights provided for in Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. State agencies, organizations and individuals with related rights and obligations, when detecting acts of infringement upon moral rights provided for in Clauses 1, 2 and 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law, on works whose term of protection has expired, may request the persons committing such acts to stop their acts of infringement, make public apologies or corrections and pay compensations for damage caused; may lodge complaints, denunciations or petitions to competent state agencies for handling. Depending on the nature and seriousness of the infringement, the organizations and individuals committing acts of infringement may be handled according to administrative, civil or criminal law provisions.
   Political-social-professional organizations, socio-professional organizations and organizations representing the collective rights of copyright and related rights may request competent state agencies to protect moral rights to works of members whose term of protection has expired.
   Chapter III
                                               RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 31. Rights of performers
   1. Direct reproduction of a performance fixed on audio or video recording as prescribed in Point b Clause 3 Article 29 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the creation of other copies from such audio or video recording.
   2. Indirect reproduction of a performance fixed on audio or video recording as prescribed in Point b Clause 3 Article 29 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the creation of other copies not from such audio or video recording, such as copying from an electronic information network, broadcast, relevant telecommunications postal network services and similar forms.
   3. Transmission to the public by other means of an unfixed performance as prescribed in Point c Clause 3 Article 29 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the dissemination of an unfixed performance to the public by any technical means other than broadcasting.
   Article 32. Use of related rights without having to obtain permission or pay royalties or remunerations For cases of use of related rights for non-commercial purposes, there is no need to obtain permission or pay royalties or remunerations, including:

   1. Making one copy for scientific research purposes of an individual as prescribed in Point a Clause 1 Article 32 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   2. Making one copy for teaching purposes, except for cases where the performance, audio or video recording or broadcast has been published for teaching purposes as prescribed in Point b Clause 1 Article 32 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Article 33. Reasonable quoting
   Reasonable quotation for the purpose of providing information specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law means the use of quotations for the sole purpose of reporting news, which must satisfy the following conditions:
   1. Quotations are provided only for the purpose of introducing, commenting or clarifying issues in the provision of information;
   2. The quantity and authenticity of excerpts from performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts which are not prejudicial to the rights of performers, producers of phonograms or video recordings or broadcasting organizations to their performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts used for excerpting shall be appropriate to the nature and characteristics of such performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts used for excerpting.
   Article 34. Temporary copies
   The provisional copy of a broadcasting organization entitled to the broadcasting right stipulated in Point d, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law means a fixed copy made by such organization with its own means and equipment for the purpose of serving the immediate subsequent broadcast of such organization. In special cases, such copy may be stored at an official archive center.
   Article 35.- Use of phonograms and video recordings
   1. Direct use of published audio and video recordings for commercial purposes in order to make broadcasts which are sponsored, advertised or charged in whatever form specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Intellectual Property Law means the use by broadcasting organizations of such audio and video recordings for broadcasting via wire or wireless means, including also transmission via satellites or digital environment. Indirect use of published audio and video recordings for commercial purposes to make broadcasts which are sponsored, advertised or charged in whatever form specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Intellectual Property Law means relaying or rebroadcasting broadcasted programs; uploading programs in digital environment onto airwaves.
   2. Use of published audio and video recordings in business and commercial activities specified at Point b, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Intellectual Property Law means the direct or indirect use by organizations and individuals of such published audio and video recordings for use in restaurants, hotels, shops, supermarkets; karaoke service establishments, postal and telecommunications service providers, digital environment; tourism, aviation, public transport and other business and commercial activities.
   3. The performance remuneration of a performer in case the phonogram or video recording is used as prescribed in Article 33 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall depend on the agreement between the performer and the producer of such phonogram or video recording when performing the phonogram or video recording program.
   The ratio of royalty, remuneration and other material benefits shall be agreed upon by copyright holders or copyright and related rights collective rights organizations.
   Collective representative organizations may authorize another collective representative organization to collect and distribute royalties, remunerations and other material benefits. Authorized collective representative organizations shall be entitled to a fee as agreed upon.
   Article 36.- Owners of broadcasting programs
   Owners of broadcasting programs specified in Clause 3, Article 44 of the Intellectual Property Law means broadcasting organizations that invest their financial and material-technical facilities in broadcasting.

   When using works, phonograms or video recordings to produce broadcasts, broadcasting organizations shall fulfill obligations toward copyright owners and related rights owners in accordance with law.
   Chapter IV
                     CERTIFICATES OF COPYRIGHT REGISTRATION AND RELATED RIGHTS REGISTRATION
   Article 37. Copyright and related rights registration

   1. Authors, copyright owners and related right owners as defined in Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property may directly or authorize other organizations and individuals to file applications for copyright and related rights registration with the Copyright Office of Literature and Art or with the provincial-level Culture and Information Services of localities where they reside or are headquartered.
   2. Foreign individuals and juridical persons having works, performances, phonograms, videograms or broadcasts whose copyright and related rights are protected as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 13 and Article 17 of the Law on Intellectual Property may directly or authorize copyright and related rights consultancy and service organizations to file applications for copyright and related rights registration with the Copyright Office of Literature and Art or with the provincial-level Culture and Information Services of localities where they reside or are headquartered.
   Article 38. Copies of registered copyright works, copies of fixation of performances, phonograms, videograms or broadcasts registered for related rights
   1. The Copyright Office of Literature and Art shall keep one copy of a work registered for copyright and one copy of the fixation of a related right subject matter as prescribed at Point b, Clause 2, Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property after granting a copyright registration certificate or a related right registration certificate.
   2. A copy of a work registered for copyright as prescribed at Point b, Clause 2, Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property may be replaced by a three-dimensional space photograph for a work with particular characteristics such as paintings, statues, monuments, bas-reliefs or grand murals associated with architectural works; or a work of too large size or bulky shape.
   Article 39. Competence to grant copyright registration certificates and related right registration certificates
   1. The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall be competent to grant, re-grant, renew and cancel validity of copyright registration certificates and related right registration certificates specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 51 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   a) Authors, copyright owners and related right owners who wish to apply for re-grant or renewal of copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates shall submit applications stating reasons and documents specified in Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   b) The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall re-grant copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates in case they are lost; renew copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates in case they are torn, damaged or the owners of copyrights or related rights change;
   c) The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall cancel copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates in case it is determined that holders of these certificates are not authors or owners or works, performances, audio and video recordings or broadcasts have been registered but are not eligible for protection as prescribed by law.
   2. Departments of Culture and Information shall, after receiving applications for registration, re-grant, renewal of copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates as prescribed by law, transfer them to the Copyright Office of Vietnam for consideration and settlement within their competence.
   Departments of Culture and Information shall be responsible for transferring applications to organizations and individuals immediately after receiving results of consideration and settlement from the Copyright Office of Vietnam.

   3. Organizations and individuals shall pay fees and charges when carrying out procedures related to copyright and related rights to the Copyright Office of Vietnam as prescribed by law.
   4. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall issue forms of registration applications, certificates of copyright registration, certificates of related right registration.
   Article 40: Validity of certificates of copyright registration, certificates of related right registration Copyright certificates issued by Vietnam Copyright Protection Company, Vietnam Copyright Protection Agency, Copyright Office of Vietnam, Literature and Art Copyright Office before the effective date of the Civil Code and Intellectual Property Law shall continue to be valid.
   Chapter 5:
      COLLECTIVE REPRESENTATIVE ORGANIZATIONS, ORGANIZATIONS PROVIDING COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS CONSULTANCY AND SERVICES
   Article 41: Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights

   1. Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 56 of the Intellectual Property Law must satisfy the following conditions:
   a/ They must be authorized by authors or owners of copyright or related rights;
   b/ They may represent authors or owners of copyright or related rights in managing one right or a group of specific rights.
   c/ The collection and distribution of royalties, remunerations and other material benefits arising from the exploitation of rights or groups of rights specified in the operation charter of the collective representative organization.
   2. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall guide the distribution of royalties, remunerations and other material benefits in cases where authors or copyright holders or related rights holders have not yet authorized their representatives.
   3. In case a work, phonogram or video recording or broadcast is related to rights and interests of many organizations acting as collective representatives authorized to represent different rights or groups of rights, the parties may agree to let one organization negotiate on their behalf for use license, collect and divide money and report to the Ministry of Culture.
   - Information before implementation.
   4. Collective representative organizations must send reports and information to the Copyright Office of Vietnam on a six-monthly, annual or irregular basis about their activities.
   Article 42. Copyright and related rights consultancy service providers
   1. A copyright and related rights consultancy or service organization may be established under Clause 1 Article 57 of the Law on Intellectual Property when its head and individuals providing copyright and related rights consultancy and services fully satisfy the following conditions:
   a/ Being a Vietnamese citizen with full civil act capacity;
   b/ To permanently reside in Vietnam;
   c/ To have graduated from a university with a degree in law.
   2. To submit biannual, annual or irregular reports on and information about their consultancy and service activities related to copyright and related rights to the Copyright Office of Vietnam.
   Chapter VI
                                 PROTECTION OF COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 43. Right to self-protection

   1. Application of technological measures to prevent acts of infringement upon copyright and related rights specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 198 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the right holders' putting up management information associated with the original or copy of a work, phonogram, video recording or broadcast; putting up management information appearing together with the communication of the work to the public in order to identify the work, its author, the owner of rights, information on the term and conditions for use of the work and all data or codes or symbols expressing such information in order to protect copyright and related rights. At the same time, the right holders may apply technological measures to protect the management information and prevent acts of accessing the work and/or illegally exploiting their ownership rights under law.
   2. The right holders may apply other measures specified in Clause 1, Article 198 of the Law on Intellectual Property to protect their legitimate rights and interests.
   Article 44.- Right to initiate civil lawsuits on copyright and related rights
   1. The following right holders shall have the right to initiate lawsuits at competent courts in order to protect their lawful rights and interests related to copyright and related rights:
   a/ Authors;
   b/ Owners of copyright and related rights;
   c/ Lawful heirs of authors or owners of copyright and related rights;
   d/ Individuals and organizations that are assigned the rights of owners of copyright and related rights;
   e/ Individuals and organizations that use works under contracts;
   f/ Performers;
   g. Producers of audio and video recordings;
   h. Broadcasting organizations;
   i. Authorized collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights;
   k. Other rights holders as prescribed by law.
   2. State agencies and relevant organizations shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, have the right to initiate civil lawsuits to request Courts to protect public interests and State interests in the domain of copyright and related rights.
   Article 45. Complaints and denunciations related to registration of copyright and related rights
  1. The following persons shall have the right to file complaints and denunciations:
   a/ Authors, copyright owners, related right owners, authorized organizations and individuals shall have the right to complain about the grant, re-grant, change or cancellation of copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates.
   b/ Any third party may lodge a denunciation of the grant of a copyright registration certificate or a related right registration certificate.
   2. The procedures for lodging a complaint or denunciation are as follows:
   a/ A complainant about the registration of copyright or related rights shall file an application to the Copyright Office of Literature and Art. Such application must clearly state the date of complaint; the name and address of the complainant; the name and address of the complained agency/organization/individual; the content and reason for the complaint and the complainant's request. The complaint application must be signed by the complainant. Enclosed with the complaint application are documents such as the copyright registration certificate, related right registration certificate, or decision on invalidation, written refusal to grant, re-grant or change of the copyright registration certificate, related right registration certificate and other relevant documents and evidence.
   b/ A denouncer shall file an application to a competent State agency. Such application must clearly state the date of denunciation; the name and address of the denouncer; the name and address of the denounced agency/organization/individual; the content and reason for the denunciation and the denouncer's request. The denunciation application must be signed by the denouncer. Enclosed with the denunciation application are relevant documents and evidence.

   3. Within the time limit prescribed by the Law on Complaints and Denunciations, the Copyright Office of Literature and Art, the Ministry of Culture and Information or a competent State agency shall reply in writing the complainant or denouncer.
   4. In case disagreeing with the reply of the Copyright Office of Literature and Art, the Ministry of Culture and Information or a competent State agency, the complainant or denouncer may lodge his/her complaint or denunciation to the competent level according to law.
   Chapter VII
                                                              IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
   Article 46.- Transition period
  1. Copyright and related rights shall be protected under legal documents which take effect before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law if they are still in the term of protection on the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law, they shall continue to be protected under the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Applications for registration of copyright and related rights filed with competent authorities before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law shall continue to be processed in accordance with the legal documents effective at the time of filing.
   3. Any act of infringement of copyright or related rights or breach of a contract made before the effective date of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be handled in accordance with the applicable law at the time such act is committed.
   Article 47.- Implementation effect
   This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO. This Decree replaces the Government's Decree No. 76/CP of November 29, 1996 guiding the implementation of a number of provisions on copyright in the Civil Code.
   Article 48.- Implementation responsibility
   1. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies, chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees and agencies, organizations and individuals having related rights and obligations shall implement this Decree.
   2. The Minister of Culture and Information shall guide the implementation of this Decree and organize its implementation./.
