                                                  DECREE

     AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 106/2025/ND-CP DATED MAY 15, 2025 ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF FIRE PREVENTION, FIRE FIGHTING AND INJURY AND WRECKAGE REWIND

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization No. 63/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 18/2017/QH14, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 09/2022/QH15, Law No. 11/2022/QH15, Law No. 56/2024/QH15, and Law No. 88/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to Law on Fire Prevention, Fighting and Rescue No. 55/2024/QH15;

  At the request of the Minister of Public Security;

  The Government promulgates Decree amending some Articles of Decree No. 106/2025/ND-CP dated May 15, 2025 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention, fighting and rescue.

  Article 1. Amending Point i Clause 2 Article 2 as follows:

  i) Organizations assigned to directly manage facilities and organizations operating within a facility in accordance with fire prevention, firefighting and rescue laws;".

  Article 2. Addition of Clauses 6, 7 and 8 after Clause 5 Article 3 as follows:

  1. Addition of Clause 6

  “6. Organizations and individuals committing multiple administrative violations of the same violation in a facility subject to fire prevention and firefighting management shall not be sanctioned for each administrative violation but only one administrative violation with aggravating circumstances.”.

  2. Addition of Clause 7

  “7. Organizations operating within a facility, organizations and individuals renting, borrowing or staying in houses shall be sanctioned for administrative violations prescribed herein corresponding to responsibilities of organizations operating within facilities, organizations and individuals renting, borrowing or staying in houses according to fire prevention, firefighting and rescue laws.”.

  3. Addition of Clause 8

  “8. The handling of administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting and rescue under this Decree by electronic means shall comply with Article 28a and Article 28b of the Government’s Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP dated December 23, 2021 elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations (amended by the Government’s Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP dated July 1, 2025).”.

  Article 3. Amendments to Clause 4 Article 5 as follows:

  “4. The time of termination of violations is determined as follows:

  a) The time of termination of violations for calculation of the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 6 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which competent authorities issue written notices of training and refresher training in fire prevention, firefighting, rescue and salvation as prescribed;

  b) The time of termination of violations for calculation of the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 18 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which specialized management agencies issue written appraisal of fire prevention and firefighting designs as prescribed;

  c) The time of termination of violations for calculation of the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 18 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which specialized management agencies issue written approval of fire prevention and firefighting commissioning results as prescribed or terminate operation;

  d) The time of termination of violations for calculation of the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Points a and b Clause 9 Article 20 of this Decree for works not subject to appraisal of fire prevention and firefighting design shall be calculated from the date on which project owners sign records of completion commissioning of work items or construction works for use as prescribed.”.

  Article 23. Implementation clauses

  1. This Decree comes into force as of April 20, 2026.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and cities, and relevant agencies, organizations, and individuals shall implement this Decree.

  Article 24. Transitional provisions

  1. For administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention, firefighting, and rescue occurring and ending before the effective date of this Decree but detected and the prescriptive period has not expired or are being considered and handled after this Decree takes effect, the Government’s Decree on penalties for administrative violations in force at the time of commission of such violations shall apply.

  For administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention, firefighting, and rescue occurring before the effective date of this Decree but still ongoing after this Decree takes effect, this Decree shall apply.

  2. For decisions on imposition of administrative penalties already issued or completely executed before the effective date of this Decree, if the sanctioned entities file complaints, regulations on imposition of administrative penalties at the time of issuance of the decision on imposition of administrative penalties shall apply.
