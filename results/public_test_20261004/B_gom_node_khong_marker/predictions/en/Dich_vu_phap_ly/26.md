                                            CIRCULAR
GUIDING TIME CALCULATION BY WORKSHOP AND BUDGET FOR LEGAL ASSISTANCE
Pursuant to the Law on Legal Assistance dated June 20, 2017;
Pursuant to Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 of the Government elaborating certain articles of the Law on Legal Aid;
Pursuant to Decree No. 96/2017/ND-CP dated August 16, 2017 of the Government defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Justice;
At the request of Director of Department of Legal Aid;
The Minister of Justice promulgates Circular providing guidelines for calculation of working hours and incurring of legal aid expenditure.
Chapter I
                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope and regulated entities
1. This Circular provides guidelines on calculation of working hours and legal aid case fees as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for participation in legal proceedings or extrajudicial representation.
2. This Circular applies to the following entities:
a) Legal aid assistants;
b) Lawyers providing legal aid services;
c) Organizations signing contracts for legal aid services;
d) State legal aid centers (hereinafter referred to as “centers”) and their branches (hereinafter referred to as “branches”); Departments of Justice and other relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Article 2. Principles
1. Ensure compliance with the law on remuneration, allowances and procedures for payment of remuneration and allowances for the execution of legal aid cases by persons providing legal aid services or organizations signing contracts for the provision of legal aid services.
2. Ensure the legality and rationality in declaring time and work as a basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the execution of legal aid cases.
3. When declaring the time and work for the execution of legal aid cases, the declarant shall be responsible for the truthfulness of the declaration.
4. When verifying the time and work of the person providing legal aid services, the verifier shall be responsible for the truthfulness of the verification.
Chapter II
               CALCULATION OF TIME BASED ON WORK HOURS
Article 3. Time based on working hours
1. Time based on working hours (half a working day) shall apply to legal aid cases in the form of participation in proceedings or extra-procedural representation selected by persons providing legal aid services as a basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the execution of cases.
2. One working day shall be counted on the basis of 4 working hours. In case a legal aid worker works less than 4 hours, the number of working days shall be calculated as follows:
a) The remaining time shall be counted as half of a working day if the number of hours is not divisible by 3;
b) It is considered as 01 working day if the number of hours of work is odd and at least 03 hours.

3. When applying the method of calculating working hours, legal aid-providing persons shall record their tasks and time of performing such tasks in the Schedule of actual working hours (Form TP-TGPL-01) enclosed with this Circular.
Article 4. Time limit for criminal proceedings

1. The time limit for legal aid in criminal proceedings shall be determined as follows:
a) Participate in interrogation of suspects or take statements from accused persons, crime victims, witnesses and litigants in criminal cases;
b) Participate in confrontation, voice identification and recognition, scene examination, autopsy, inspection of traces on the body, experimental investigation, and other activities to participate in legal proceedings as required by the presiding authorities;
c) Working with proceeding officers and agencies;
d) Verify, collect and assess relevant documents, items and evidence according to stages of legal proceedings;
dd) Work with persons facing charges in detention cells of border guard stations, custody houses, temporary detention camps or prisons;
e) Meet with persons facing charges on bail or their relatives; crime victims or their relatives; witnesses, litigants in criminal cases and other participants in legal proceedings;
g) Study files, make copies and prepare documents at procedural authorities or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts for provision of legal aid;
h) Prepare defense arguments;
i) Attend court sessions. If a legal aid provider attends a court session but the trial is postponed not at the request of the legal aid provider and no notice is given, the time during which the legal aid provider works with the procedural authority or person regarding matters related to the postponement of the court session shall be included;
k) Perform tasks related to procedures for appeal against first-instance judgments, appellate judgments, cassation or reopening procedures;
l) Perform other reasonable tasks as per the law to serve the purpose of defense and protection.
2. Remuneration and allowances for legal aid-providing persons performing tasks specified in Points e, g, h, l Clause 1 of this Article shall not exceed the number of sessions of such tasks according to the corresponding case assignment in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith.
3. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons must be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours of activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or proceeding agencies;
b) Working hours of activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or proceeding agencies or persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
c) Working hours of activities specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where legal aid-providing persons are directly working;
d) Working hours of activities specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by accused persons or proceeding agencies or officers of border guard stations, custody centers, detention camps or prisons;
dd) Working hours of activities specified in Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
e) Working hours of activities specified in Point g Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts on provision of legal aid services;
g) The time of performing activities specified in Points h, k and l Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Center or its Branch or organization signing a legal aid contract;
h) The time of performance of activities specified in Point i Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or Court clerk.
Article 5. Time limit for participation in civil procedures

1. The time limit for provision of legal aid in a civil case shall be determined on the basis of:
a) Work with legal aid beneficiaries and relevant persons to determine the legal relationship of the dispute; assess conditions for initiating lawsuits, counter-claims, independent claims; provide guidance on drafting petitions for initiation of lawsuits, petitions for application of provisional urgent measures, counter-claims, independent claims, and evidence for the court; guide preparation of lawsuit files or other tasks during the stage of lawsuit initiation and acceptance of cases;
b) Work with legal aid beneficiaries, litigants and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals on the origin and content of disputes, evidence to prove or other tasks in the stage of trial preparation;
c) Attend meetings to inspect the submission, access, and disclosure of evidence; verify, collect, and assess necessary documents, items, and evidence;
d) Participate in taking statements from involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons; participate in activities of property appraisal and valuation and perform other procedural tasks at the request of the presiding agency;
dd) Participate in mediation as prescribed by law;
e) Working with proceeding officers and agencies in various stages of legal proceedings;
g) Meeting with litigants, witnesses, and other participants in legal proceedings regarding other relevant matters;
h) Studying documents, photocopying, and preparing materials at procedural agencies or centers, branches, or organizations signing contracts for legal aid services;
i) Preparing defense arguments;
k) Attending court sessions or meetings to resolve civil matters. In cases where legal aid-providing individuals attend court sessions but such sessions are postponed not at the request of the legal aid-providing individuals and without prior notice, the period during which the legal aid-providing individuals work with procedural agencies and proceeding officers on matters related to the postponement of court sessions shall be included;
l) Performing tasks related to procedures for appealing against first-instance judgments, appellate judgments, cassation procedures, or reopening procedures;
m) Performing other reasonable tasks as prescribed by law to serve the protection.
2. Remuneration and allowances for legal aid-providing individuals performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i, and m Clause 1 of this Article shall not exceed the number of sessions of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 02 enclosed herewith.
3. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours for activities specified in Points a and b Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts to provide legal aid;
b) Working hours for participation in meetings for inspection of the handover, access and disclosure of evidences specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons assigned to settle lawsuits; working hours for verification, collection and assessment of documents, objects and necessary evidences related to such activities specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where legal aid-providing persons conduct verification or collection of evidences;
c) Working hours for participation in taking statements of involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by these persons or presiding officers or procedural-conducting authorities; working hours for participation in appraisal and valuation of assets and performance of other activities to participate in legal proceedings at the request of presiding authorities specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where assets are appraised or valued or presiding officers or procedural-conducting persons;
d) Working hours for activities specified in Points dd and k Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by judges or court clerks or mediators’ meeting clerks;
dd) Working hours for activities specified in Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by presiding officers or procedural-conducting persons;
e) Working hours for activities specified in Point g Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
g) Working hours for activities specified in Point h Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons assigned to settle lawsuits or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts to provide legal aid.
h) The time for performing activities specified in Points i, l, and m Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Center or its Branch or organization signing the legal aid contract.
Article 6. Time for participating in administrative proceedings
1. The basis for calculating the time for providing legal aid for cases participating in administrative proceedings includes:

a) Participating in activities as prescribed in Points a, b, c, d, e, g, h, i, k, l, and m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) Participating in dialogues.
2. The remuneration and allowances for legal aid-providing persons when performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i, and m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular shall not exceed the number of sessions of performance of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 03 enclosed with this Circular.
3. The working hours of legal aid-providing persons must be certified by agencies, organizations, or individuals as follows:
a) The time for performing activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations, or individuals specified in Clause 3 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) The time for performing activities specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the judge or the secretary of the dialogue session.
Article 7. Time for participation in extrajudicial representation
1. The basis for calculating the time for providing legal aid for extrajudicial representation cases includes:
a) Meeting and contacting legally-aided persons, their relatives, witnesses;
b) Studying dossiers and preparing documents for the representation;
c) Verifying, collecting documents, items, evidence and circumstances related to the representation;
d) Working with relevant agencies, organizations and individuals;
dd) Participating in the representation before competent agencies, organizations and individuals that resolve the cases.
2. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons must be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours for activities specified in Points a, d and dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons working with legal aid-providing persons or relevant agencies, organizations or individuals or competent agencies, organizations or individuals settling legal aid-related cases;
b) Working hours for activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by centers, branches or organizations signing contracts to provide legal aid.
Article 8. Determination of working hours in some special cases
1. In case two or more legal aid-providing persons provide legal aid for one legal aid recipient in the same case as prescribed by law, the working hours serving as basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling legal aid-related cases are the actual working hours of each legal aid-providing person but the total working hours must not exceed 30 working sessions/case for cases involving participation in legal proceedings or 20 working sessions/case for cases involving extrajudicial representation.
2. In case procedural authorities suspend lawsuits
a) Working hours serving as basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling legal aid-related cases are the actual working hours from the time of suspension of the lawsuit;
b) When the lawsuit is resumed, working hours serving as basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling legal aid-related cases are the actual working hours of subsequent tasks from the time when the lawsuit resumes until the end of the case.
3. In case of replacement or change of legal aid-providing persons
a) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the replacement or changed person is the actual period during which they provided legal aid until the time of replacement or change;
b) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the person designated to replace the person is the actual period during which they performed subsequent tasks after being designated to replace the person. If the designated person chooses the method of incurring expenses for the case, the remuneration and allowances for both the replacement and the replaced or changed person shall not exceed the corresponding expenses prescribed in Annexes No. 01, No. 02 and No. 03 enclosed with this Circular.
4. In case of providing legal aid for a person denounced, recommended for prosecution or charged at the time of arrest or temporary detention of another person or crime victim according to the provisions of procedural law, the time and time confirmation shall be as follows:
a) The time of verification, collection and assessment of necessary documents, items and evidence related by agencies, organizations and individuals where the legal aid-providing person goes to verify and collect evidence for confirmation;
b) The time of participating in taking statements from denounced, recommended for prosecution or crime victims or other relevant persons; participating in activities of confrontation, identification and recognition of voices of denounced or recommended persons of charge together with investigators or procurators as confirmed by investigators or procurators;
c) The time of working with the charged person in the detention room of the border guard station, custody house or detention camp as confirmed by the charged person or officials of the border guard station, custody house or detention camp or procedure-conducting agency;
d) The time of meeting denounced, recommended persons or their relatives; crime victims or their relatives; other relevant persons as confirmed by these persons but must not exceed the number of sessions of performing this work under the method of incurring expenses for the cases prescribed in Annex No. 01 enclosed with this Circular according to the level of less serious crimes.
Chapter III
                                      WORKING BLOCK
Article 9. Case-based payment

1. The case-based payment shall be made in the form of participation in legal proceedings by the legal aid-providing person after being assigned and not changed during the process of providing legal aid.
2. Complexity, procedural requirements and contents of each specific case shall be used to determine the level of reimbursement for case-related expenses as follows:
a) Apply regulations on classification of crimes in Clause 2 Article 1 of the Law on amendments to the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 dated June 20, 2017 to determine the level of expenditure on criminal proceedings.
The classification of crimes must be applied immediately upon selecting the form of charging and based on one of the documents issued by the presiding agency: the decision to charge the suspect, the investigation conclusion, the indictment, the forensic report, or the judgment. In cases where multiple documents contain different crime titles under different penalty brackets, the document with the higher penalty bracket shall prevail.
b) Apply Clause 3 Article 337 of the Civil Procedure Code and Article 5 of Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the level of case allocation for civil proceeding cases.
c) Apply Clause 12 Article 3 of the Law on Administrative Procedures and Article 6 of Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the level of charging for cases of participation in administrative procedures.
3. When incurring expenses for a case, the legal aid-providing person shall perform the following tasks:
a) For criminal proceedings: perform the tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 4 of this Circular.
Details of tasks to be performed and maximum amounts thereof (converted by working session) for each case are provided in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith. In case the number of converted sessions for incurring expenses for the case prescribed in Appendix No. 01 is lower than 03 times the basic salary, the amount of expenses for the case shall be equal to 03 times the basic salary.
b) For civil or administrative proceedings: perform the tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 5 and Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.
Details of tasks to be performed and maximum amounts thereof (converted by working session) for each case are provided in Appendix No. 02 and Appendix No. 03 enclosed herewith. In case the number of converted sessions for incurring expenses for the case prescribed in Appendix No. 02 or Appendix No. 03 is lower than 03 times the basic salary, the amount of expenses for the case shall be equal to 03 times the basic salary.
4. When incurring expenses for a case, the legal aid-providing person shall record the tasks and confirm the performed tasks in the Work List (Form TP-TGPL-02) enclosed herewith.
5. In case a legal aid case is carried out through multiple stages of legal proceedings as prescribed in the Appendices enclosed herewith, the amount of expenses for the case shall be applied according to the stages but must not exceed 10 times the basic salary.
Article 10. Incurring expenses for cases in special cases
1. In case 2 persons providing legal aid for 1 person in the same case, depending on the actual work of each person, the maximum amount of the case shall be 130% of the corresponding amount specified in the Appendix enclosed herewith but must not exceed 10 times the basic salary.

Example 1: A is prosecuted for a crime with a penalty bracket of very serious crime, the director of the legal aid center assigns 2 persons to provide legal aid for A from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work done by 2 persons providing legal aid, the maximum amount of the case shall be: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, the case shall be paid for 10 times the basic salary.
2. In case of providing legal aid for 1 person who is prosecuted for 2 or more crimes in the same case, the amount of the case shall be paid as follows:
a) If 2 crimes have different penalty brackets, based on the actual work done, the maximum amount of the case shall be 130% of the corresponding amount specified in the Appendix enclosed herewith but must not exceed 10 times the basic salary.
Example 2: A is prosecuted for 2 crimes: 1 crime with a penalty bracket of serious crime and 1 crime with a penalty bracket of very serious crime. The legal aid worker provides legal aid for A from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work done, the maximum amount of the case shall be: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, the case shall be paid for 10 times the basic salary.
b) If two offences have the same penalty bracket, based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of the budget shall be 130% of the maximum amount of the budget for such case prescribed in Appendix No. 01 enclosed with this Circular but not exceeding 10 times the basic salary. Example 03: B is prosecuted for 02 offences with the same penalty bracket of very serious offence. The legal assistant provided legal assistance to B from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of the budget shall not exceed: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since the number of sessions exceeds 10 times the basic salary, the budget shall be 10 times the basic salary.
3. In case the presiding agency suspends the case
a) The budget for the legal assistant shall be allocated based on the actual work performed by the legal assistant up to the time of suspension;
b) When the case continues to be resolved, the budget for the legal assistant shall be allocated based on the work performed by the legal assistant from the time of resumption of resolution until the end of the case.
4. In case of replacement or change of legal assistant
a) The budget for the person replaced or changed shall be allocated based on the actual work performed by the person replaced or changed up to the time of replacement or change;
b) The budget for the person appointed as a replacement shall be allocated based on the actual work performed by the person appointed as a replacement after being appointed as a replacement.
If the person appointed as a replacement chooses the working period by session, the remuneration and allowance for both persons (the replacement and the person replaced or changed) shall not exceed 30 working sessions/case according to Clause 1 Article 13 of the Government's Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 elaborating certain articles of the Law on Legal Assistance.
Chapter IV
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 11. Transitional provisions
For activities arising before the effective date of this Circular, regulations of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases shall continue to apply.
Article 12. Entry into force
1. This Circular comes into force from July 15, 2021.
2. This Circular replaces Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases.
