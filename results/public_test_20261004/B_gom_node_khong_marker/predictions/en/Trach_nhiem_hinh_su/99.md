                                             JOINT CIRCULAR

                               GUIDANCE ON EXECUTION OF PENALTIES FOR DELAYED VIOLATION

  Pursuant to the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments dated June 17, 2010;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 77/2009/ND-CP of September 15, 2009, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 15/2008/ND-CP of February 4, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 104/2008/ND-CP of September 16, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Defense;

  In order to uniformly implement provisions of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments regarding execution of deportation sentences, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall jointly provide guidance as follows:

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidelines for procedures for enforcing deportation sentences; settlement of cases where persons subject to deportation sentences abscond, fall ill or die during the period pending their exit; settlement of receipt of corpses, remains and ashes of persons subject to deportation sentences who die; funding for enforcement of deportation sentences.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to persons subject to deportation sentences according to court judgments or decisions (hereinafter referred to as “deported persons”) and agencies, organizations and individuals related to enforcement of deportation sentences.

  Article 3. Principles of enforcement of deportation sentences

  1. Ensure political, legal, foreign affairs and humanitarian policy requirements of the State.

  2. The enforcement of deportation sentences must ensure safety, correct persons, correct time limit, procedures and jurisdiction in accordance with Vietnamese law and international practices.

  3. Ensure lawful rights and interests of deportees; prohibit all acts of infringing upon the life, health, dignity and lawful property of deportees.

  Article 4. Agencies enforcing deportation sentences

  1. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the People’s Court or Military Court issues the decision on execution of the expulsion penalty and where the headquarter of the criminal enforcement agency is located shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in organizing the execution of the expulsion penalty according to the judgment execution decision of the Court.

  2. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the prison affiliated to the Ministry of Public Security or the Ministry of National Defense or military zone is located shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in organizing the execution of the deportation sentence for persons who have completely served their imprisonment sentences and had the additional penalty of deportation.

  Article 5. Funds for judgment enforcement

  The funding for enforcement of the expulsion sentence shall be covered by the state budget in the annual budget estimates of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority and the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security. The cost of purchasing tickets for exit vehicles to take the expelled person back to the country shall comply with Article 102 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  Chapter II

                          PROCEDURES FOR EXECUTION OF PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON EXPORT

  Article 6. Notice of enforcement of the decision on expulsion

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the decision on execution of the expulsion sentence from the Court in the case specified in Clause 1 Article 96 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments or from the date of receipt of the notification from the Superintendent of the prison regarding the time for the convict to completely serve the additional penalty of expulsion, the execution agency of the provincial police must notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, diplomatic missions, consular offices, and other agencies authorized to perform consular functions of the country where the expelled person is a citizen, located in Vietnam, or the representative office of the international organization for which that person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed his/her entry into Vietnam.

  2. In cases where the convict has the additional penalty of expulsion and is granted amnesty or exemption from serving the imprisonment sentence, within 24 hours from the receipt of the decision on amnesty or exemption from serving the imprisonment sentence, the Superintendent of the prison must notify in writing the execution agency of the provincial police where the prison is located so that it can immediately notify the authorities specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 7. Documents for execution of expulsion sentence

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the judgment execution decision from the Court or from the date of receiving the person who has completely served the imprisonment sentence, the person who is granted amnesty or exemption from serving the additional penalty of expulsion, the execution agency of the provincial police must prepare documents for execution of the expulsion sentence.

  2. The documents for execution of the expulsion sentence (hereinafter referred to as judgment execution documents) must include all the papers and documents specified in Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments. The certificate of completion of other penalties or other obligations specified in Point c Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments includes: certificate of completion of imprisonment, amnesty, exemption from serving the imprisonment sentence, exemption or reduction of fines, decision on termination or suspension of judgment execution, certificate or confirmation from the competent execution agency regarding the results of judgment execution or the results of fulfilling other obligations.

  3. Upon handing over a person who has completely served his/her prison sentence, or a person who is granted special amnesty or exemption from serving an additional penalty of expulsion to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security office where the prison is located, the superintendent of the prison shall hand over together with a copy of the judgment/decision on execution of the prison sentence, certificate of completion of the prison sentence, certificate of special amnesty or exemption from serving the prison sentence and papers evidencing the performance of other obligations (if any) for the preparation of a dossier for execution of the expulsion penalty.

  Article 8. Designation of accommodation

  1. Within 03 working days after the preparation of a judgment execution dossier, the head of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security office where the dossier is prepared shall issue a decision to designate an accommodation place for the expelled person pending his/her exit. The designated accommodation place must have a clear address to ensure convenience for management and supervision. The detention room or custody cell of the detention center or custody house of the prison shall not be used to manage the expelled person pending his/her exit.

  2. In case the expelled person falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2, Article 99 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security office where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall issue a decision to take the expelled person to the accommodation establishment of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as “the accommodation establishment”).

  3. In case the person subject to expulsion or his/her relatives or legal representative request permission for him/her to stay in a lawful temporary residence in another province or city, he/she shall send a written request to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared for consideration and decision. The written request must clearly state the full name and address of the requester, the relationship with the person subject to expulsion, the address of the place of residence, and the commitment to comply with all requirements of the criminal enforcement agency of the province, abide by the law and regulations of the local authority of the place of residence. The written request must be translated into Vietnamese and certified by the diplomatic mission, consular office or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the person subject to expulsion is a citizen, or the representative office of the international organization where such person works, which is headquartered in Vietnam, and the People’s Committee of the commune or agency or organization where the person subject to expulsion comes to reside.

  4. Within 02 working days from the date of issuance of the decision on designation of the place of residence, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall notify the Court that issued the judgment execution decision, the Procuracy at the same level and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.

  Article 9. Management of persons subject to expulsion

  1. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies and local authorities of the province where the person subject to expulsion resides in managing such person.

  2. Within 03 working days from the date of issuance of the decision on admission to the accommodation establishment or the decision on designation of the place of residence in another province or city, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall send a notice enclosed with a copy of the judgment or decision to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment is located or the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the person subject to expulsion resides for cooperation in management and taking charge of carrying out procedures for settlement when the person subject to expulsion absconds or dies as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 99 and Article 100 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  Article 10. Settlement of cases where persons serving expulsion sentences abscond

  1. During their stay pending exit, if the expelled person absconds, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment is located or the province where the place of accommodation is located shall immediately organize a pursuit and notify such to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared; in case of unsuccessful pursuit, within 7 days from the date the expelled person absconds, a pursuit warrant must be issued in accordance with law.

  2. In case the absconding expelled person is recaptured or surrenders, the arresting or receiving agency shall make a record and immediately notify such to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared for receipt and transfer of the person to the accommodation establishment and notify such to the agency that has issued the pursuit warrant for issuance of a decision on termination of pursuit as prescribed.

  3. In case the expelled person absconds on escort to the accommodation establishment or the place of exit, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the escort is organized shall organize the pursuit, issue a pursuit warrant and terminate the pursuit.

  4. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall immediately notify such to the same-level Procuracy, the Court that issued the decision on execution of the expulsion sentence, the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular of the expulsion sentence server’s absconding, recapture or surrender.

  Article 11. Settlement of cases where the person serving the expulsion sentence falls ill or dies

  1. During their stay pending exit at the accommodation establishment, the expelled person may receive medical examination and treatment in accordance with the Government’s regulations applicable to persons serving the expulsion sentence and foreigners who have completely served imprisonment sentences and are staying at the accommodation establishment pending completion of exit procedures. In case the expelled person falls seriously ill and must be treated at a state health facility, the accommodation establishment or the designated place of accommodation shall immediately notify such to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment or the designated place of accommodation is located for notification to the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.

  2. In case the expelled person is undergoing emergency treatment or suffers a serious illness and cannot travel and has confirmation from a hospital of the province or higher level, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office managing the expelled person at the accommodation place shall designate, and the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the accommodation establishment is located shall notify such to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office which has prepared the judgment execution dossier for making a written notice to the court that has issued the judgment execution decision for consideration and issuance of a decision to extend the time limit for compulsory departure from the Vietnamese territory.

  3. During the stay period, if the expelled person dies, the designated accommodation establishment or place shall immediately report it to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the accommodation establishment or place is located, the investigation agency and the competent Procuracy for determination of the cause of death. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours after the expelled person dies, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the expelled person dies shall notify it to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office which has prepared the judgment execution dossier for immediate written or telegraphic notice to the court that has issued the judgment execution decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular. After completing the procedures for determining the cause of death and obtaining permission from the competent agency, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the accommodation establishment or place is located shall organize the burial.

  In case the expelled person dies on the way to the accommodation establishment or the place of exit, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office organizing the escort shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office, the investigation agency and the competent Procuracy where the expelled person dies in, carrying out the settlement procedures as prescribed in Clause 4, Article 99 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments.

  4. Burial expenses for the deceased expelled person:

  a/ Burial expenses for the deceased expelled person at the accommodation establishment shall comply with the Government's regulations on regimes of food, accommodation, clothing, daily-life activities, medical examination and treatment and burial expenses for persons serving expulsion penalty during their stay at the accommodation establishment;

  b/ Expenses for burial of an expelled person who dies during his/her stay at another accommodation establishment shall be applied similarly to those incurred by a deceased expelled person at the accommodation establishment, unless such expenses are paid by his/her relatives or lawful representative.

  Article 12.- Handling of applications for receipt of the dead body, remains and ashes of an expelled person

  1. In case an expelled person dies and his/her relatives or lawful representative request the receipt of his/her dead body for burial, within 48 (forty eight) hours after the expulsion person dies, there must be an application sent to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department which has compiled the judgment execution dossier for consideration and decision.

  2. The application must clearly state the full name and address of the applicant for receipt of the dead body, the relationship with the expelled person, the place of burial, the commitment to ensure security, order and environmental sanitation and the payment of expenses. The application must be certified by the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen or the representative office of the international organization where he/she works and must be translated into Vietnamese. If the applicant is a Vietnamese citizen, the application must also be certified by the commune-level People’s Committee of the locality where he/she resides. In case of receipt of the dead body for burial in the Vietnamese territory, the application must be approved by the commune-level People’s Committee of the locality where the burial takes place.

  3. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours after receiving the application, the head of the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department shall decide to permit the receipt of the dead body and notify in writing the applicant, the court that has issued the judgment execution decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the permission or non-permission to receive the dead body when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of the dead body affects security, order and environmental sanitation.

  4. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours after notifying the permission to receive the dead body, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department and the applicant shall hand over and receive the dead body, money and lawful property of the expelled person (if any) and make a written record bearing the signatures of the deliverer, the receiver and the witness; past this time limit, if the applicant fails to come and receive the dead body, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department shall organize the burial.

  5. The receipt of exhumed remains shall be granted only after 3 years from the date of burial. In case of cremation, the receipt of ashes may be granted after the completion of the cremation. The person requesting the receipt of exhumed remains or ashes must send a written request to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office where the burial takes place for consideration and decision. The written request must contain all the contents specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Within 7 working days after receiving the written request, the head of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office shall decide to grant the receipt of exhumed remains or ashes and notify in writing the applicant, the court that issued the judgment enforcement decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the grant or refusal of the receipt of exhumed remains or ashes when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of exhumed remains or ashes affects security, order and environmental sanitation.

  6. The preservation and transportation of the exhumed remains, ashes or bones of the expelled person shall be carried out by the applicant or the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which such person is a citizen, or the representative office of the international organization where such person works, and shall comply with the provisions of law on medical treatment; environmental sanitation; prevention and control of infectious diseases and border quarantine and other provisions of Vietnamese law.

  Article 13. Exit permit

  1. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police office where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall examine the passport or a passport substitute paper of the expelled person. In case the passport does not contain a visa or the visa has expired, the applicant shall request the immigration management agency to issue an exit visa as prescribed. In case of emergency, the visa must be issued at the border gate. The written request must be sent at least 12 (twelve) hours before the expelled person arrives at the border gate.

  2. In case the person subject to expulsion does not have a passport or a document of equivalent value, the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and immigration authorities of the Ministry of Public Security to request the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which the person is a citizen or the representative office of the international organization where the person works to issue necessary documents for exit visa procedures.

  In case the requested agency or organization has not yet resolved or failed to resolve the request, but there is an urgent need to expel the person immediately or the time limit for leaving the territory of Vietnam has expired as prescribed by law, the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall request the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security to issue exit documents and request the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to notify in writing the country of which the expelled person is a citizen for handling the reception and entry procedures. In case the country of which the expelled person is a citizen refuses to receive or allow entry, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall take charge and cooperate with the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security and the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority in resolving the matter through diplomatic channels or expelling the person according to international practices.

  3. Where the person subject to expulsion is required to serve another sentence or has not yet completed the payment of a fine or fulfillment of another civil obligation falling within the cases of temporary exit suspension as prescribed by Vietnamese law, the criminal enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security authority that has prepared the expulsion judgment execution file shall immediately send a written report to the Court that issued the judgment execution decision to request such Court to issue a decision on extension of the period for leaving the territory of Vietnam; and shall coordinate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in notifying and requesting the diplomatic mission, consular office, or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen, located in Vietnam, or the representative office of the international organization where such person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed his/her return to Vietnam, to provide support for funding the payment of the fine or fulfillment of other civil obligations; and concurrently notify the competent civil judgment enforcement agency to carry out judgment enforcement procedures or to request the competent Court to consider granting exemption from or reduction of the judgment execution obligation regarding amounts payable to the state budget in accordance with the Law on Civil Judgment Enforcement. Upon receipt of a decision or document from the civil judgment enforcement agency on the termination of judgment enforcement for the expelled person, the criminal enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security authority shall carry out procedures to compel the expelled person to leave the territory of Vietnam.

  Article 14. Compulsory departure from the territory of Vietnam

  1. The criminal enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security authority that has prepared the judgment execution file shall decide on the period of exit, but must not exceed the time limit specified in the Court’s judgment execution decision or the time limit prescribed by law.

  2. The criminal enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security authority that has prepared the judgment execution file shall purchase exit tickets for the expelled person in cases where the expelled person bears the costs themselves, or the diplomatic mission, consular office of the country of which such person holds citizenship, or the representative office of the international organization where such person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed his/her return to Vietnam, to cover the costs of bringing the expelled person back to his/her country.

  In case of using budget to pay for air tickets, cars, trains or sea ferries for repatriation of persons who are expelled from Vietnam as prescribed in Article 102 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments, the criminal judgment execution management agency of the Ministry of Public Security shall decide to use budget and purchase tickets for means of transport for repatriation of persons who are expelled from Vietnam.

  3. At least 24 (twenty-four) hours before the date of exit, the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is made shall notify the person who is expelled, the competent immigration management agency, the Border Guard Command’s Department of Checkpoints and agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the time and place of exit for coordination in expulsion. In case of expulsion by land road to a country sharing a land border with Vietnam, the time of exit must not be at night, except for special cases.

  In case the person who is expelled has money or property deposited at the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office or accommodation establishment, at least 24 (twenty-four) hours before the date of exit, the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office or accommodation establishment shall return such money or property to him/her. The return of money or property must be recorded in writing, signed by the delivering and receiving parties and witnesses.

  4. The criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is made shall escort the person who is expelled to the place of exit; coordinate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security, the Border Guard Command’s Department of Checkpoints in carrying out procedures for forcing the person who is expelled to leave the territory of Vietnam. The expulsion at the place of exit must be recorded in writing, signed by the person who is expelled, representatives of agencies participating in the expulsion, diplomatic missions, consular offices of the country of nationality of the expeller or representative office of international organization where he/she works.

  5. In case the person subject to expulsion fails to voluntarily comply with the expulsion decision or refuses to leave Vietnam, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall escort him/her to the border checkpoint of the country of which he/she is a citizen; in case of escorting by air, the criminal enforcement agency of the province shall notify and reach agreement with the air-freight company on security measures during the escorting as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 17 of Decree No. 81/2010/ND-CP of the Government on civil aviation security and strictly comply with international agreements on civil aviation security to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  Chapter III

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 15. Effect

  This Circular takes effect on January 1, 2012.

  Article 16. Implementation

  Agencies of the Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of National Defense and Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, within their functions, tasks and powers, organize implementation, guide and inspect implementation of this Circular.

  During the implementation of this Circular, if any problems arise, agencies, units and localities should report them to the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense or the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for timely guidance.-
