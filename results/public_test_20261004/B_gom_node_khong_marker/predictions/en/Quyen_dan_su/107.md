                                                   CIRCULAR

   ON AUTHORITY, ORDER, PROCEDURES AND MEASURES FOR PROTECTION OF THE INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, HEALTH, PROPERTY, DIGNITY AND PERSONAL RIGHTS OF DENUNCIATORS OF CORRUPTION AND MISPRACTICE

  Pursuant to the Law on Denunciation dated June 12, 2018;

  Pursuant to the Law on Anti-Corruption dated November 20, 2018;

  Pursuant to the Law on Practice of Thrift and Prevention of Wastefulness dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to Decree No. 31/2019/ND-CP dated April 10, 2019 of the Government elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Denunciation;

  Pursuant to Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP dated February 25, 2019 of the Government on denunciation and settlement of denunciation in the People’s Public Security;

  Pursuant to Decree No. 01/2018/ND-CP dated August 6, 2018 of the Government on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;

  At the request of Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Public Security;

  The Minister of Public Security promulgates a Circular on jurisdiction, procedures and measures for protection of life, health, property, honor and dignity of denouncers of corrupt acts and wastefulness.

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides for the competence, order and procedures for, and measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of denunciators of corrupt practices prescribed in the Anti-Corruption Law and acts of wastefulness prescribed in the Law on Practice of Thrift and Anti-Wastfulness (hereinafter referred to as denunciators of corrupt practices and wastefulness).

  Article 2. Subjects of application

  1. Protected persons specified in Clause 3 of this Circular.

  2. Persons settling denunciations of corrupt practices and wastefulness.

  3. Police offices at all levels; agencies, organizations, units and individuals responsible for taking measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of protected persons specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Circular.

  Article 3. Protected persons, scope and contents of protection and grounds for application of protective measures

  1. Protected persons include: Denunciators of corrupt practices and wastefulness; spouses, natural parents, adoptive parents, natural children and adopted children of denunciators of corrupt practices and wastefulness (hereinafter referred to as protected persons). Protected persons who are living, working or studying abroad are not regulated by this Circular.

  2. Scope and contents of protection of protected persons include: Protection of life, health, property, honor and dignity. Protected property is property under the ownership of protected persons. Property of protected persons in foreign countries is not regulated by this Circular.

  3. Grounds for application of protective measures: When there are grounds to believe that the life, health, property, honor or dignity of the person specified in Clause 1 of this Article is being harmed or is in imminent danger of harm due to denunciations of corrupt or wasteful acts.

  Article 4. Rights and obligations of protected persons

  The rights and obligations of protected persons shall comply with Article 48 of the Law on Denunciation. The competent denunciation-settling authority and the competent police office applying protective measures shall not be held responsible for consequences that may arise from the life, health, property, honor or dignity of the protected person in cases where the protected person requests termination or refusal to apply protective measures or fails to comply with the obligations prescribed in Clause 2, Article 48 of the Law on Denunciation.

  Article 5. Responsibilities for protecting protected persons

  1. Police offices at all levels where protected persons live or have their property shall take charge and coordinate with relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals in protecting the life, health, property, honor and dignity of protected persons; decide to mobilize and use forces, means, tools, measures and other matters proposed or requested by relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals to coordinate in protection. Relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate as requested or required by police offices at all levels.

  2. When receiving a request for application of protective measures to the life, health, property, honor or dignity of a denouncer of corrupt or wasteful acts, and finding that the protection request is well-grounded, credible or when, during the process of handling the denunciation, the competent denunciation-settling authority finds grounds specified in Clause 3, Article 3 of this Circular, it shall promptly request or request the competent police office to apply protective measures as follows:

  a) The persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 6, 7 and 8 of Article 13; Clause 4 of Article 14; Clause 4 of Article 15; Article 16; Clauses 1 and 3 of Article 17 and Article 20 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the directors of the police departments of provinces or centrally run cities (hereinafter referred collectively to as provincial-level Police Departments) concerned, and at the same time send them to the Minister of Public Security for direction. Upon receiving a proposal or request for protection, the director of the provincial-level Police Department shall consider deciding to apply protective measures or direct the relevant affiliated police units to issue a decision to apply protective measures;

  b) The persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of Article 13; Clauses 2 and 3 of Article 14; Clauses 2 and 3 of Article 15; Clause 2 of Article 17 and Article 18 and Article 19 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the directors of the provincial-level Police Departments. Upon receiving a proposal or request for protection, the director of the provincial-level Police Department shall consider deciding to apply protective measures or direct the relevant affiliated police units to issue a decision to apply protective measures;

  c) The persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 2 of Article 13; Clause 1 of Article 14; Clause 1 of Article 15 and Article 21 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the chiefs of the police offices of urban districts, rural districts, towns or provincial cities or centrally run cities (hereinafter referred collectively to as district-level Police Departments). Upon receiving a proposal or request for protection, the chief of the district-level Police Department shall consider deciding to apply protective measures or direct the chiefs of the police offices of wards, stations, police posts, town police stations or communes under the payroll of the People's Public Security Force (hereinafter referred collectively to as commune-level Police Departments) concerned to decide to apply protective measures;

  d) The person competent to resolve denunciations as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 13 of the Law on Denunciations shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the Chief of the commune-level police authority. Upon receiving the proposal or request for protection, the Chief of the commune-level police authority shall consider and decide on the application of protective measures;

  dd) In urgent cases where there are grounds to determine that the life, health, property, honor, or dignity of the protected person may be immediately infringed upon after the denunciation of corrupt or wasteful acts is made; the person competent to resolve denunciations shall immediately propose or request the nearest police authority or the police authority where the infringement of the life, health, property, honor, or dignity of the protected person may occur to apply necessary protective measures;

  e) Where the protected person is a prisoner at a prison or a reeducatee at a correctional institution or a student at a reformatory school: The person competent to resolve denunciations shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the superintendent of the prison, the director of the correctional institution, or the principal of the reformatory school.

  3. Upon receiving the proposal or request from the person competent to resolve denunciations, the written proposal for protective measures from the denouncer regarding corrupt or wasteful acts, or the written request from a competent agency or unit; if finding that the proposal or request is well-grounded, within the scope of their duties and powers, and within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the proposal or request, the Head of the competent police authority shall issue a decision to apply protective measures; and shall take charge and coordinate with relevant agencies, organizations, units, and individuals in protecting the life, health, property, honor, or dignity of the protected person. In complex cases requiring inspection and verification at multiple locations, the time limit for issuing a decision to apply protective measures may be longer but shall not exceed 05 working days.

  In cases outside their jurisdiction, the Head of the competent police authority shall promptly transfer the case to the competent police authority for handling as prescribed and notify in writing the person competent to resolve denunciations, the denouncer, or the competent agency or unit of the transfer of the proposal or request.

  4. In case the request or petition of the person competent to resolve the denunciation and the denouncer about corrupt and wasteful acts is groundless or deemed unnecessary to apply protective measures, within 3 working days, the head of the police authority at all levels shall send a written notice clearly stating the reason to the competent agency/unit, the denouncer or send such notice to the denunciation resolver for explanation.

  Article 6. Written request or petition for decision on application of protective measures

  1. The written request for application of protective measures by the denouncer about corrupt and wasteful acts shall comply with Clause 2 Article 50 of the Law on Denunciation.

  2. The written request or petition of the person competent to resolve the denunciation sent to the police authority at all levels competent to decide on application of protective measures shall include the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the written request or petition;

  b) Name of the competent police authority requested to decide on application of protective measures;

  c) Full name, position, agency, unit of the person competent to resolve the denunciation;

  d) Grounds and reasons for requesting or petitioning the application of protective measures;

  d) Date of issuance of the decision to accept the denunciation; time limit for settlement of the denunciation;

  e) Full name, address, and phone number of the protected person; contents of the protection request.

  3. When submitting a written request or petition, the person competent to resolve denunciations must enclose the decision to accept the denunciation, the petition for application of protective measures by the denouncer regarding corrupt or wasteful acts as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 50 of the Law on Denunciations (original) and other documents related to the application of protective measures (if any).

  Article 7. Authority to decide on application of protective measures by persons competent to resolve denunciations within the People’s Public Security

  1. The person competent to resolve denunciations specified in Clause 1 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP dated February 25, 2019 of the Government on denunciations and resolution thereof within the People’s Public Security shall, upon receiving a written request for application of protective measures for the life, health, property, honor, and dignity of the denouncer regarding corrupt or wasteful acts, and if the protection request is deemed well-grounded, credible, or if there are grounds as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular during the process of resolving the denunciation, promptly decide on the application of protective measures to the protected person whose place of residence or property is located within their jurisdiction.

  In cases where the protected person has a place of residence or property outside the jurisdiction, the person competent to resolve denunciations specified in Clause 1 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall report to the Head of the district-level police authority for handling according to their jurisdiction.

  2. The person competent to resolve denunciations as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall, upon receiving a written request for application of protective measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding corrupt or wasteful acts, if finding that the protection request is well-grounded, credible or during the process of resolving the denunciation, there are grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular, shall promptly decide to apply protective measures to the protected person whose place of residence or property is within their jurisdiction or direct the Head of the relevant commune-level police authority to decide on the application of protective measures (except where the denounced person is the Head of the commune-level police authority).

  In cases where the protected person’s place of residence or property is outside the jurisdiction, the person competent to resolve denunciations as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall request the Head of the relevant district-level police authority to decide on the application of protective measures, and concurrently report to the Director of the provincial police authority for monitoring and direction.

  3. The person competent to resolve denunciations as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall, upon receiving a written request for application of protective measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding corrupt or wasteful acts, if finding that the protection request is well-grounded, credible or during the process of resolving the denunciation, there are grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular, shall promptly request the Head of the relevant district-level police authority to decide on the application of protective measures.

  In case protected persons are inmates of prisons or employees of reformatories or students of reformatories: Upon receiving written requests for application of measures to protect life, health, property, honor and dignity of denouncers against corrupt and wasteful acts, and when deeming that such protection requests are well-grounded, credible or when finding grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular during the process of handling denunciations, superintendents of prisons, directors of reformatories shall promptly decide on the application of protective measures.

  4. Persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 4 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall, upon receiving written requests for application of measures to protect life, health, property, honor and dignity of denouncers against corrupt and wasteful acts, and when deeming that such protection requests are well-grounded, credible or when finding grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular during the process of handling denunciations, shall promptly decide on the application of protective measures to protected persons having addresses of places of residence or places of property within their jurisdiction or shall direct heads of relevant district-level police offices to make decisions on the application of protective measures. Provincial-level police authorities shall advise directors of provincial police departments to direct deputy heads of district-level police offices or other competent units or individuals to make decisions on the application of protective measures in case denounced persons are heads of district-level police offices.

  In case protected persons have addresses of places of residence or places of property outside their jurisdiction, persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 4 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall request directors of relevant provincial police departments to make decisions on the application of protective measures.

  5. The persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 5 and 6 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall, upon receiving written requests for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of denunciators against corrupt and wasteful acts, if deeming that such protection requests are well-grounded, credible or during the process of settlement of denunciations, if finding grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular, shall promptly request directors of concerned provincial-level police departments to decide on the application of protective measures.

  In case protected persons are officials, soldiers, inmates in prisons, officials, soldiers, inmates in compulsory education establishments, officials, soldiers, students in reformatories and denounced persons are superintendents, deputy superintendents of prisons, directors, deputy directors of compulsory education establishments, principals, deputy principals of reformatories; directors of Police Departments for Management of Prisons, Compulsory Education Establishments and Reform Schools, upon receiving written requests for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of denunciators against corrupt and wasteful acts, if deeming that such protection requests are well-grounded, credible or during the process of settlement of denunciations, if finding grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular, shall promptly decide on the application of protective measures.

  Inspectors of the Ministry of Public Security shall advise the Minister of Public Security to direct deputy directors, deputy secretaries of Party Committees of provincial-level police departments or other competent units and individuals to decide on the application of protective measures in case denounced persons are directors of provincial-level police departments.

  Article 8. Procedures for protection

  1. Procedures for protecting the life, health, property, honor and dignity of denunciators against corrupt and wasteful acts shall comply with Section 2 Chapter VI of the Law on Denunciation.

  2. In case a person competent to settle denunciations has made a conclusion on the contents of the denunciation or decided to terminate the settlement of the denunciation, he/she shall immediately notify such to the competent police authority for decision on termination of application of protective measures. The decision on termination of application of protective measures shall be sent to the protected person, the person competent to settle the denunciation and other agencies, organizations, units and individuals involved.

  3. After the person competent to settle denunciations makes a conclusion on the contents of the denunciation or decides to terminate the settlement of the denunciation, if the competent person continues to receive written requests from denunciators regarding acts of corruption and wastefulness and finds there are grounds and authenticity to determine that the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the protected person continue to be infringed upon or threatened, he/she shall transfer the documents to the competent police authority for handling in accordance with law and notify in writing the denunciator thereof.

  Article 9. Dossiers on application of protective measures

  Dossiers on application of protective measures shall comply with Article 55 of the Law on Denunciations and regulations on document archiving and dissemination in the People's Public Security (group of dossiers on prevention and combat of corruption).

  Article 10. Protective measures

  Based on practical situations, competent police authorities may apply one or several protective measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of protected persons as prescribed in Article 58 of the Law on Denunciations.

  Article 11. Funding for assurance of application of protective measures

  Funding for application of protective measures shall be ensured from the State budget. Police units and localities shall prepare estimates of funding for application of protective measures and incorporate them into annual security expenditure estimates and send them to the Ministry of Public Security (via the Department of Planning and Finance) for consolidation and submission to the Ministry of Finance in accordance with regulations.

  Article 12. Effect, transitional provisions and implementation responsibilities

  1. This Circular comes into force from February 15, 2021.

  2. When regulations referred to in this Circular are amended or replaced, the referenced contents shall be adjusted accordingly.

  3. Heads of police authorities affiliated to ministries, directors of police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities and relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals shall implement this Circular.

  4. Inspectorate of Ministry of Public Security shall take charge of monitoring, inspecting, consolidating and reporting the implementation of this Circular; Heads of police authorities affiliated to ministries shall assign their advisory and consolidation units, heads of local police authorities shall assign local police inspectorates to take charge of monitoring, inspecting, consolidating and reporting the implementation of this Circular. Difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry of Public Security (via Inspectorate of Ministry of Public Security) for consolidation and reporting to the Ministry’s leadership for consideration and settlement.
