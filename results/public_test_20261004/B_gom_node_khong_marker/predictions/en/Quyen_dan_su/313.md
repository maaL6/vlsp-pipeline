                                                   CIRCULAR

                GUIDANCE ON HEALTHCARE FOR THE AGELESS

  Pursuant to Articles 14, 15 and 16 of the Ordinance No. 23/2000/PL-UBTVQH of April 28, 2000 on the elderly, which assigns tasks to the health sector; pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 30/2002/ND-CP of March 26, 2002, defining and guiding a number of articles of the Ordinance on the Elderly; pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 120/2003/ND-CP of January 20, 2003 amending Article 9 of Decree No. 30/2002/ND-CP of January 20, 2003; and pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 49/2003/ND-CP of May 15, 2003 defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Health, The Ministry of Health shall provide guidelines for elderly care work as follows:

  I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. The elderly who receive health care under this Circular are citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or Vietnamese elderly residing abroad and foreign elderly during their stay in Vietnam from 60 years old. Old age is often accompanied by poor health and diseases; the elderly suffer from diseases accounting for about 95%. On average, an elderly person suffers from 2.69 diseases, mainly chronic and non-transmissible diseases, so the deepest aspiration of the elderly is to live a healthy life, receive health care and medical examination and treatment when they are sick. This is a legitimate requirement of the elderly, which requires the attention of all sectors, all levels and the entire society.

  2. The elderly receive initial health care and have priority in medical examination and treatment at medical establishments.

  3. The People’s Committees at all levels shall ensure the health care regime for the elderly in their localities. The health sector shall be responsible for professional and technical matters in health care for the elderly.

  II. ORGANIZATION OF INITIAL HEALTH MANAGEMENT AND HEALTH CARE FOR THE ELDERLY

  The People’s Committees and People’s Councils at all levels shall direct the health sector to coordinate with relevant sectors in formulating plans and organizing the implementation of plans on health care for the elderly in their localities according to the following requirements:

  1. Organize the propagation and dissemination of contents on physical training, health enhancement and disease prevention, especially common diseases among the elderly, so that the elderly can prevent themselves.

  2. Organize health checkups to make health records for the elderly. The periodic health checkup shall be carried out once a year, gradually organize the network of family doctors and home-based health care services for the elderly.

  3. To organize various forms of senior citizen clubs such as health promoting clubs and outdoor health clubs. Depending on conditions and requirements of seniors, each locality may organize clubs for persons suffering from chronic diseases such as diabetes, asthma, hypertension, heart disease, kidney and urinary tract diseases, cataract, etc.

  4. To prioritize investment in community-based functional rehabilitation programs for disabled seniors to prevent and rehabilitate sequelae caused by trauma or accidents or cerebrovascular complications, chronic diseases, occupational diseases, etc.

  5. To organize a network of volunteers to provide home-based healthcare for the elderly, especially poor and lonely people who have no one to rely on. To encourage retired health workers to volunteer or train volunteers in necessary knowledge about healthcare for the elderly, such as self-care activities in personal living, especially for those suffering from sequelae after heart disease, accident, social communication and self-care activities.

  III. HEALTHCARE AND MEDICAL TREATMENT FOR THE ELDERLY

  1. To organize a network of healthcare and medical treatment for the elderly:

  a/ The Institute of Geriatrics shall:

  - Study and apply scientific and technical advances in three fields: clinical geriatrics, basic geriatrics and social geriatrics, and propose measures to raise the quality of healthcare for Vietnamese the elderly.

  - Implement professional technical direction in healthcare for the elderly nationwide. Organize inspections of the implementation of professional processes and standards and equipment for lower-level geriatric establishments to ensure training and rehabilitation of the functions after diseases of the elderly.

  - To be a line for deep treatment of geriatric medicine.

  - To coordinate with the Medical University of Hanoi and other training schools for health workers in providing undergraduate and postgraduate training in geriatrics. To compile specialized training syllabi and books and popularize knowledge on health care for the elderly.

  b/ General hospitals of class I and class II: To set up geriatric departments and have separate examination rooms for elderly patients at medical examination departments.

  c/ Specialized hospitals (except for specialized hospitals for children): To organize medical examination for the elderly at medical examination departments; when elderly patients need to be admitted for inpatient treatment, hospitals shall receive them in an orderly manner, attend to them with all due care, and not let them lie in the same bed as other patients.

  d/ Medical centers of urban districts, rural districts, provincial towns and cities: To have some beds for elderly patients at clinical departments and organize medical examination for the elderly at medical examination departments.

  e/ Hospitals for functional rehabilitation must continue to be built and consolidated under the provisions of the Minister of Health's Decision No. 966/BYT of May 30, 1999, guiding the contents of operation of hospitals for functional rehabilitation; to reserve an appropriate proportion of beds for caring for and rehabilitating the elderly. To coordinate with the Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs Service in implementing the regime of convalescence for the elderly who have made meritorious services to the country.

  f/ Medical stations of communes, wards or district towns shall manage the health of the elderly in their localities and provide initial health care for them. Where the elderly are disabled, lonely and helpless, and sick but do not come for medical examination and treatment at prescribed places, the heads of the commune-level medical stations shall appoint health workers to conduct medical examination and treatment at the places where the elderly live or report to the local People's Committees to organize the bringing of the sick to medical examination and treatment establishments.

  2. Treatment work.

  - The medical examination and treatment for older patients shall be carried out by specialized technical departments; in case of emergency, they shall be transferred to the nearest health establishments.

  - Medical examination and treatment establishments must give priority to medical examination for older patients (after emergency treatment).

  - Step by step, organize the management of chronic diseases among older people according to programs and projects so as to detect and handle them in time when there are unusual developments. Plans on disease prevention and treatment for chronic diseases must be made.

  - Intensify the rehabilitation work for older patients after acute treatment at hospitals and guide their continued treatment at home.

  - Combine traditional medicine with modern medicine, develop non-pharmaceutical treatment methods especially at grassroots-level health services for older patients.

  IV. FUNDS FOR THE ORGANIZATION OF INITIAL HEALTH CARE AND MEDICAL EXAMINATION AND TREATMENT FOR OLDER PEOPLE

  1. Funds for health checkups for the purpose of keeping health records for older people, organizing periodic health checkups and initial health care activities for older people shall be borne by localities.

  2. Funds for medical examination and treatment:

  a/ Persons with compulsory health insurance cards or voluntary health insurance cards shall be covered by social insurance for medical examination and treatment according to the provisions of law on health insurance.

  b/ Persons eligible for the regime of medical examination and treatment for the poor shall be covered by the fund for medical examination and treatment for the poor for medical examination and treatment expenses according to the Prime Minister's Decision No. 139/2002/QD-TTg of January 15, 2002 on medical examination and treatment for the poor and Joint Circular No. 14/2002/Tr-LT-BYT-BTC of December 16, 2002, of the Ministry of Health and the Ministry of Finance, guiding the implementation of the above-said Decision.

  c/ Elderly persons aged 90 or more shall enjoy the health insurance regime as prescribed in the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs' Circular No. 24/2003 of January 6, 2003, guiding the implementation of the Government's Decree No. 120/2003/ND-CP of January 20, 2003.

  3. Persons who have no health insurance card as prescribed at Point 2 above shall have to pay by themselves expenses for medical examination and treatment.

  4. Domestic and foreign organizations and individuals as well as families of elderly persons are encouraged to buy health insurance cards for them.

  5. Localities should set up funds for care of the health of the elderly to support the care of their health and medical examination and treatment.

  V. ORGANIZATION OF IMPLEMENTATION

  1. At provincial, district and communal levels, the care of the health of the elderly shall be organized by the health authorities as the head of the care board, with the participation of the finance, labor, war invalids and social affairs, related sectors and representatives of the older persons association, the Red Cross Society, the Fatherland Front, the Peasants Association and the Women Union, to ensure and maintain the health care activities of the elderly in localities. In order to unify the health care activities, the care boards for the health of the elderly should be incorporated into the local initial health care boards.

  2. Provincial Departments of Health shall advise provincial People’s Committees on developing plans for health care for older persons throughout provinces in accordance with this Circular and direct district-level health authorities and commune-level health authorities to participate in health care for older persons within their areas.

  3. Department of Treatment shall cooperate with relevant departments, functional departments, Inspectorate of Ministry of Health, Geriatrics Institute and relevant units in providing guidance, directing, inspecting and examining implementation of this Circular.

  4. Health communication education centers shall cooperate with Geriatric Institute in disseminating knowledge about health care for older persons.

  5. This Circular comes into force 15 days after its publication. Difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry of Health (Department of Treatment) for consideration and settlement or amendment.
