                                                    DIRECTIVE

                   ON THE ORGANIZATION OF THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW IN 2000

  The Marriage and Family Law of 2000 was passed on June 9, 2000 by the Xth National Assembly at its 7th session and took effect on January 1, 2001.

  To promptly implement the Marriage and Family Law and Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of June 9, 2000 of the National Assembly on implementation of the 2000 Marriage and Family Law, the Prime Minister hereby issues the following instructions:

  I. TO CONDUCT A REVIEW OF THE APPLICABLE LAWSUITS OR PROMULGATE NEW LAWSUITS GUIDING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW

  1. From now till the end of 2000, the Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for reviewing the applicable lawbooks on marriage and family in order to propose the Government and other competent agencies to amend or annul those documents related to marriage and family which are no longer compatible with the new Marriage and Family Law.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with concerned agencies and organizations in, preparing draft laws to submit to the Government or the National Assembly Standing Committee for promulgation as guidelines for the implementation of the provisions of the Law, especially the land use right for farming, residential land and houses related to marriage and family relations; marriage registration, adoption registration in remote areas, civil status registration of marriage and family involving foreign elements, including civil status registration of marriage and family involving foreign elements in border areas with neighboring countries; and sanction administrative violations in the field of marriage and family. The above-said tasks must be completed by the second quarter of 2001.

  3. The Committee for Ethnic Minorities and Mountainous Areas shall assume the prime responsibility for preparing a proposal to be submitted to the Government for promulgation in the first quarter of 2001, defining the application of the Marriage and Family Law to ethnic minority groups, plans for handling actual adoption cases in ethnic minority areas, together with a list of backward customs and practices on marriage and family not allowed to apply and a list of good customs and practices on marriage and family to be promoted.

  II. TO PROVIDE, TEACH AND EDUCATE THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW

  1. The dissemination and propagation of the Marriage and Family Law must be carried out regularly, thoroughly and extensively among officials, public employees, officers and soldiers of the people’s armed forces and among the people, especially in communes, wards, townships, population clusters, agencies, organizations, enterprises, schools and families.

  The dissemination and propagation must aim to make everyone clearly understand the principles and basic contents of the Law, especially the newly amended and supplemented contents and the Resolution of the National Assembly on the settlement of the actual marriage situation so as to make people voluntarily and actively abide by it, raise their awareness and concern about their personal responsibility to take care of family building, making each family truly become a happy home of each individual, making Vietnam society truly become a big family of all Vietnamese people working together to build an independent, free, prosperous and happy country.

  Right from the third quarter of 2000, ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and People’s Committees at all levels must work out and organize the implementation of plans on dissemination, propagation and education of the Marriage and Family Law within their agencies, units and localities; regard this as a key task of law dissemination, propagation and education work in the last six months of 2000 and the whole year 2001, and at the same time renew forms and measures of law propagation and education on marriage and family so as to make such activities more thorough, avoiding the form of law propagation and education which is more about the surface, with low effectiveness.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall have to coordinate with the Central Committee of the Vietnam Fatherland Front, the Central Committee of the Vietnam Women’s Union, the Vietnam General Confederation of Labor, the Central Committee of the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, the Vietnam Peasants’ Association, the Vietnam War Veterans’ Association, the Ministry of Culture and Information, the Vietnam Committee for Child Protection and Care, the Committee for Population and Family Planning, the Vietnam Lawyers’ Association, the central and local mass media agencies in organizing the extensive dissemination and propagation of the Marriage and Family Law and the National Assembly’s Resolution on the implementation of this Law; direct and guide the intensification of legal counseling activities on marriage and family, encourage the establishment of various types of family clubs where people can participate in activities according to gender, age, profession or interest to exchange experiences and learn from one another about necessary matters in their responsibilities as wives, husbands, fathers, mothers, grandparents… so that from the very beginning they can avoid misgivings and regrettable things not worth doing in the truly important mission in family life.

  The Government’s Council for Coordination in Law Dissemination and Education shall have to determine the contents and programs for disseminating and propagating the Marriage and Family Law and the National Assembly’s Resolution on the implementation of this Law; coordinate with concerned agencies and organizations in compiling training curricula and materials suitable to each type of target group and train rapporteurs for disseminating and propagating the Marriage and Family Law.

  3. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall coordinate with the Central Committee’s Committee for Ideological and Cultural Affairs and the Ministry of Justice in directing the mass media to plan to give priority to the dissemination and propagation of the Marriage and Family Law from now till the end of 2000 and 2001; guide the formulation and implementation of community activity rules related to the provisions of the Marriage and Family Law.

  4. The People’s Daily, Radio Voice of Vietnam, Vietnam Television Station and other mass media in the central and local areas regularly have programs and special sections introducing and explaining the contents and new points of the Marriage and Family Law.

  5. The Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy in, organizing intensive training courses on the Marriage and Family Law for judges, procurators, court clerks, people’s jurors, civil status workers and other judicial personnel to ensure uniform application and supervision of the implementation of the Marriage and Family Law.

  6. The Ministry of Education and Training shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in, directing the review of the curriculum system and teaching materials of universities, colleges and citizen education documents related to the Marriage and Family Law in vocational secondary schools, general secondary schools and elementary schools in order to work out plans for timely supplementation and amendment thereof in conformity with the Marriage and Family Law; organize training courses for teachers who teach law and citizen education on the Marriage and Family Law.

  7. The Committee for Ethnic Minorities and Mountainous Areas shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in, working out plans to popularize and propagate the Marriage and Family Law among ethnic minority people.

  8. The Ministry of Finance shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in, working out plans to guide budget balancing, ensuring necessary funding sources for the implementation of the Marriage and Family Law and the Resolution of the National Assembly on implementation of the Law, from the elaboration of documents guiding the implementation of this Law, organization of marriage registration and adoption registration in remote areas, organization of training courses and campaigns of extensive dissemination and propagation among cadres, soldiers and people.

  9. Presidents of provincial/municipal People’s Committees shall direct local agencies to work out specific plans to deal with the actual marriage situation in their localities under the Resolution of the National Assembly on implementation of the Marriage and Family Law, creating favorable conditions for marriage registration, including on-site registration, so as to completely deal with this situation in 2001-2002.

  The Prime Minister requests ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and presidents of provincial/municipal People’s Committees under their respective management responsibilities to work out specific plans to implement this Directive.

  The Ministry of Justice and Government Office shall monitor and expedite the implementation of this Directive and submit consolidated reports to the Prime Minister on the results of implementation.
