                                                        LAW

                                           HUSBAND AND WIFE

  The family is the cell of society. A good family makes a good society and a good society makes a good family. In a socialist family, wives and husbands are equal, love and help each other to make progress, actively participate in the cause of building socialism and defending the Fatherland, and together raise their children to become citizens useful to the society.
  To inherit and develop the Marriage and Family Law of 1959 in order to further build and consolidate the socialist families, preserve and promote the fine customs and practices of the nation, get rid of backward practices and the remnants of the anti-people marriage and family regime, and resist the influence of the bourgeois marriage and family regime; Based on Articles 64 and 65 of the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam;
  This Law provides for the marriage and family regime.

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1

  The State shall ensure genuine voluntary and progressive marriage, monogamy, equality between husband and wife, in order to build democratic, harmonious, happy and sustainable families.

  Marriage between Vietnamese citizens of different ethnicities and religions, or between religious and non-religious people shall be respected and protected.

  Article 2

  The husband and wife have the obligation to plan their child birth.

  Parents have the obligation to raise their children to become citizens useful to society.

  Children have the obligation to respect, care for and support their parents.

  Article 3

  The State and society shall protect mothers and children, and assist mothers in well performing their noble motherhood functions.

  Article 4

  Prohibiting underage marriage, forced marriage, obstructing voluntary marriage, progress and property claims in marriage proposals; prohibiting forced divorce.

  Prohibiting married persons from marrying or cohabiting with other persons.

  To prohibit the ill-treatment and persecution of fathers, mothers, wives or children.

  Chapter II

                                                     MARRIAGE

  Article 5

  Men must be twenty years of age or older and women must be eighteen years of age or older to get married.

  Article 6

  Marriage shall be decided voluntarily by men and women; neither party may coerce the other party, nor anyone may force or obstruct the marriage.

  Article 7

  To prohibit marriage in the following cases:

  a/ Being married;

  b/ Suffering from mental diseases which render him/her incapable of perceiving his/her acts; suffering from venereal diseases;

  c/ Among persons of the same direct blood line; among siblings of the same father or mother or of different fathers or mothers; among persons of different generations who are related by blood;

  d) Between adoptive parents and adopted children.

  Article 8

  The marriage shall be recognized and recorded in the marriage register by the People's Committee of the commune, ward or township where either of the partners resides according to the procedures prescribed by the State.

  The marriage between Vietnamese citizens living abroad shall be recognized by diplomatic missions of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  All other marriage ceremonies are not legally valid.

  Article 9

  The marriage that violates one of Articles 5, 6 and 7 of this Law is illegal.

  One or both parties of a marriage which has been lawfully contracted but later dissolved by the other party, or the spouse or child of a person who is married, and the wife, husband or child of such person, or the People's Procuracy, the Vietnam Women's Union, the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union or Vietnam Trade Union may request the People's Court to cancel such illegal marriage.

  The property of persons whose marriage is annulled shall be handled according to the principle that their personal property still belongs to them; common property shall be divided based on each party's contribution; the legitimate interests of the party who is deceived or forced into marriage shall be protected.

  The children's rights shall be settled as in the case of parents' divorce.

  Chapter III

                                   OBLIGATIONS AND RIGHTS OF HUSBAND AND WIFE

  Article 10.-

  Wives and husbands have equal obligations and rights in all aspects of their family life.

  Article 11

  Spouses have the obligation to be faithful to each other, love, respect, care for and help each other to make progress and together carry out planned childbirth.

  A husband shall create conditions for his wife to well perform the mother’s function.

  Article 12

  Wives and husbands have the right to choose legitimate occupations and take part in political, economic, cultural and social activities.

  Article 13

  The residence of husband and wife shall be selected by themselves and not bound by customs and practices.

  Article 14

  Common property of husband and wife includes property created by either spouse, income from their profession and other lawful incomes during the marriage period, and property jointly inherited or given to them.

  Article 15.-

  Common property is used to ensure common family needs.

  The husband and wife have equal rights and obligations to the common property. The purchase, sale, exchange, loan, pledge and other transactions related to the property of a value of big must be agreed upon by the husband and wife.

  Article 16

  For property acquired by a spouse before marriage or separately inherited or given during the marriage period, the person who owns such property may or may not include it in the common property of husband and wife.

  Article 17

  When a spouse dies first, if it is necessary to divide their common property, such property shall be divided into two halves. The portion of the deceased’s property shall be divided in accordance with the law on inheritance.

  Husband and wife have the right to inherit each other's property.

  Article 18

  When marriage exists, if one party makes a request and has a plausible reason, the common property of husband and wife may be divided according to Article 42 of this Law.

  Chapter IV

                               OBLIGATIONS AND RIGHTS OF PARENTS AND CHILDREN

  Article 19

  Parents have the obligation to love, nurture and educate their children, and to care for their physical, intellectual and moral development.

  Parents must not discriminate among their children.

  Parents must set good examples for their children in all aspects and closely coordinate with schools and social organizations in educating their children.

  Article 20

  Parents have the obligation to rear their adult children who have no working capacity to support themselves.

  Article 21.-

  Children have equal obligations and rights in the family.

  Children have the obligation to respect, care for and support their parents and listen to their advice.

  Article 22.-

  Adult children who still live with their parents have the right to choose their occupations and participate in political, economic, cultural and social activities.

  Article 23

  A dog may have its own property.

  Those who are 16 years of age or older and still live with their parents have the obligation to take care of the family's common life; if they have personal incomes, they must contribute to the family's needs.

  Article 24.-

  Parents represent their minor children before law.

  Parents managing property of minor children.

  Article 25.-

  Parents shall pay compensation for damage caused by illegal acts of their under-16 children. In case parents have no means while their children have personal property, the property of the children shall be used for compensation.

  Children aged 16 or older shall pay compensation with their own property for damage caused by their illegal acts. If they have no property, their parents shall pay compensation.

  Article 26

  A father or mother who is sanctioned for one of the crimes of infringing upon the body or dignity of a minor child, seriously ill-treating or persecuting a minor child may be decided by the People's Court not to let him/her look after and educate the child, manage the property of the child or represent the child for between one and five years. For such person who has been reformed, the People's Court may shorten this term.

  The above-mentioned fathers and mothers shall still have the obligation to contribute the costs of raising their children.

  Article 27

  Grandparents have the obligation to rear and educate their minor grandchildren in cases where the children have no parents left. Adult grandchildren have the obligation to rear their grandparents in cases where the grandparents have no children left. Brothers and sisters have the obligation to support one another in cases where they have no parents left.

  Chapter 5.

                                        DETERMINING PARENTS

  Article 28.-

  Children born during the marriage period or conceived by the wife during such period are common children of husband and wife.

  In case of a request for re-determination of this matter, there must be other evidence.

  Article 29.-

  A person declared to be the father or mother of a child may request to have that child determined not to be his/her offspring.

  A person who is not declared to be the father or mother of a child may apply for recognition of that child as his/her offspring.

  Article 30.-

  The recognition and recording in the birth register of an out-of-wedlock child by the People's Committee of the commune, ward or township where the child resides.

  Article 31.-

  A person who is not married may claim his/her parents even if they have died.

  The mother, father or patron has the right to request the determination of parents for an illegitimate child.

  The People’s Procuracy, Vietnam Women’s Union, Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union and Vietnam Trade Union have the right to request determination of parents for an illegitimate child.

  Article 32.-

  A child who is adopted by his/her parents or recognized by the People’s Court as parent has all rights and obligations like a natural child.

  Article 33.-

  Disputes over child adoption and parent recognition shall be settled by the People’s Court of the place of permanent residence of the child.

  Chapter 6.

                                                CHILD CARE

  Article 34

  The adoption aims to establish a close bond between the adopter and the adopted child in the parent-child relationship, ensuring that the minor adopted child is well nurtured, cared for, and educated.

  Between adopters and adopted children, there are the obligations and rights of parents and children prescribed in Articles 19 thru 25 of this Law.

  Article 35.-

  Adoptees must be aged 15 or younger. In case adoptees are war invalids or disabled persons or adoptees who are old, weak and lonely persons, their ages may be over 15 years old.

  The adoptive parent must be at least 20 years older than the adopted child.

  Article 36.-

  The adoption must be agreed upon by both parents of the adoptive couple and by the natural parents or tutors of the adopted minor child. If the child is aged 9 years or older, his/her consent is also required.

  Article 37.-

  The adoption shall be recognized and recorded in the civil status book by the People's Committee of the commune, ward or township where the adopter or adoptee resides.

  Article 38.-

  The State and society encourage the adoption of orphans.

  The adopted children of martyrs shall still enjoy all rights and benefits of the martyrs.

  Article 39

  The adoption may terminate when the adoptive parent or the adopted child or both of them commit serious acts of infringing upon the body and dignity of each other or other acts which make the affection between the adoptive parent and the adopted child no longer exist.

  The termination of child adoption shall be decided by the People's Court at the request of the adopted child or the adopter. In case the adopted child is a minor, his/her natural parents or legal guardian, the People's Procuracy, Vietnam Women's Union, Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union and Vietnam Trade Union have the right to request the termination of child adoption.

  Chapter VII

                                                     LY HÔN

  Article 40.-

  When a spouse or both spouses apply for divorce, the People's Court shall conduct investigation and mediation.

  In case both husband and wife apply for divorce, if the reconciliation fails and it is well established that the two parties are really willing to divorce, the People's Court shall recognize the voluntary divorce.

  In case a spouse requests a divorce and the reconciliation fails, the People's Court shall conduct the trial. If it deems that the situation is serious, their common life cannot be prolonged and the purpose of marriage cannot be achieved, the People's Court shall grant the divorce.

  Article 41

  In case a wife is pregnant, her husband may apply for divorce only after she has given birth for one year.

  This restriction shall not apply to the wife's application for divorce.

  Article 42

  Upon their divorce, the division of property shall be agreed upon by the two parties and recognized by the People's Court. If the two parties fail to reach agreement, the People's Court shall make decision.

  The division of property upon divorce must, in principle, comply with the following regulations:

  a/ Private property of one party shall still belong to that party;

  b/ The common property of husband and wife shall be divided into two parts, but with consideration given to the property situation, the specific condition of the family and each party’s contribution;

  c/ In case the couple’s personal property is unidentified while they are still living together in the family, the wife or husband shall be entitled to a part of the family’s common property, based on his/her contribution to the maintenance and development of the common property as well as to the family life. Labor in the family shall be regarded as productive labor;

  d/ When dividing property, the interests of the wife and minor children must be protected, as well as the legitimate interests of production and profession.

  Article 43

  When divorcing, if the poorer spouse requests alimony, the richer spouse shall pay alimony according to his/her capability.

  The amount and time of allowances shall be agreed upon by the two parties. If they fail to reach agreement, the People's Court shall decide.

  When circumstances change, the person receiving support or the person paying support may request a change in the level or duration of support. If the supporting person marries another person, they are no longer entitled to support.

  Article 44. Transfer of title to a foreign State

  A divorced couple still have all their obligations and rights toward their common children.

  Article 45.-

  Upon divorce, the custody of minor children must be based on their interests in all aspects. In principle, breastfed children shall be placed under the care of their mothers-in-law.

  The person who fails to rear his/her child has the obligation and right to visit and care for the child and contribute the expenses for raising and educating the child. If he/she delays or shirks making such contribution, the People's Court shall decide to deduct such expenses from his/her income or force him/her to pay such expenses.

  For the benefit of the child, where necessary, the custody arrangement or the level of contribution to the costs of raising and educating the child may be changed.

  Chapter VIII

                                                SALVAGE REGIMES

  Article 46

  The tutorship shall be provided in cases where it is necessary to ensure the care for, education of, and protection of the rights and interests of minors whose parents have died or are alive but cannot perform such duties.

  Article 47

  Parents may appoint their tutors for their minor children. If parents cannot appoint a tutor, their relatives may appoint a tutor for such person. The appointment of tutors shall be recognized by the People's Committees of communes, wards or townships.

  In case parents or relatives cannot appoint a patron, the State agency with functions or social organization shall act as patron for their minor children.

  Article 48

  Citizens acting as patrons must be aged 21 or older, have good moral qualifications and practical conditions to act as patrons.

  Article 49

  Citizens or organizations acting as patrons shall have the following tasks and powers:

  a) To care for and educate the patronized person;

  b) To manage the assets of the patron;

  c/ Representing the legal patron and protecting his/her interests.

  Article 50.-

  Citizens acting as patrons shall be subject to the supervision of the People's Committees of communes, wards or townships in terms of patronage work.

  The relatives of the patron, the People's Procuracy, the Vietnam Women's Union, the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union and the Vietnam Trade Union have the right to request the People's Committee of commune, ward or township to replace the patron if he/she fails to fulfill his/her duty or causes serious damage to the interests of the patron.

  Citizens acting as patrons may request the replacement of themselves if they no longer meet the conditions for being patrons.

  Article 51

  The tutorship shall terminate when the minor is returned to his/her parents or adopted by another person or reaches full 18 years of age.

  Chapter IX

      THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN VIETNAMESE CITIZENS AND FOREIGNERS

  Article 52

  In the marriage between Vietnamese citizens and foreigners, each party shall comply with the provisions of their respective countries' laws on marriage.

  If a marriage between a Vietnamese citizen and a foreigner is conducted in Vietnam, the foreigner shall also comply with the provisions of Articles 5, 6 and 7 of this Law. The marriage procedures shall be stipulated by the Council of Ministers.

  Article 53

  Matters concerning the husband and wife relations, property relations, parent and child relations, marriage annulment, divorce, adoption and patronage between Vietnamese citizens and foreigners shall be stipulated by the State Council.

  Article 54

  In cases where there are legal and judicial assistance agreements on marriage and family between Vietnam and foreign countries, such agreements shall prevail.

  Chapter 10.

                                          FINAL PROVISIONS

  Article 55.-

  For ethnic minority people, the State Council shall, based on this Law and specific circumstances, make appropriate regulations.

  Article 56.-

  The Council of Ministers, the Supreme People’s Court and the Prosecutor General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall, within their respective functions, guide the implementation of this Law.

  The Vietnam Fatherland Front, Vietnam Women’s Union, Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, and Vietnam Trade Union shall educate and mobilize the people to strictly implement this Law.

  Article 57.-

  This Law replaces the Marriage and Family Law of 1959.

  This Law was passed on December 29, 1986, by the National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 12th session, the 7th session.
