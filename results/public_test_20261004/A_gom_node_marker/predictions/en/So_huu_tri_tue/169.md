                                                   CIRCULAR

   OF THE MINISTRY OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND ENVIRONMENT No. 3055-TT/SHCN of December 31, 1996, Guiding the Implementation of Provisions on Procedures for Establishing Industrial Property Rights and a Number of Other Procedures in Decree No. 63-CP of October 24, 1996 of the Government, detailing industrial property

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996 detailing industrial property; The Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment hereby promulgates this Circular in order to specify and guide the procedures for making, filing and examining applications for protection titles, the procedures for approving and registering industrial property right transfer contracts, the procedures for considering applications for non-voluntary licenses, the procedures for modifying and extending the validity of protection titles, the procedures for processing international applications for invention or utility solution registration under the PCT Convention and international applications for trademark registration under the Madrid Agreement and the procedures for granting industrial property representation permits.

  Chapter I

                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. Terms

  1.1. Terms used in this Circular are construed as follows:

  - "Decree" is used to refer to the Government's Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996 detailing industrial property;

  - "Application" is used to refer to an application for a protection title;

  - "Invention application", "Utility solution application", "Industrial design application", "Trademark application", "Appellation origin registration application" are used respectively to refer to an application for an invention patent, an application for a utility solution patent, an application for an industrial design patent, an application for a trademark registration certificate and an application for a certificate of registered appellation origin right;

  - "International Application" means an international application for registration of an invention or utility solution filed under the PCT Convention;

  - "International Application" means an international application for registration of a trademark filed under the Madrid Agreement on International Registration of Marks;

  - "Mark" means "Trade Mark" as defined in Article 2 of the Decree;

  - "Document holder" means an individual, juridical person or organization that has created, issued or signed to recognize the validity of a document.

  1.2. Other terms are construed according to the Decree.

  2. Document certification

  2.1. Signature certification

  During the process of carrying out procedures related to the establishment, maintenance, extension, enforcement and transfer of industrial property rights specified in this Circular, signatures of persons standing for documents and transactions with competent authorities must be certified as being those of the persons standing for such documents and, where the signatories are representatives of the persons standing for documents, must be certified as being those having the authority to represent the persons standing for documents, as follows:

  (i) For entities having lawful seals, the authentication of signatures shall be effected by affixing the entity’s seal on the signatures;

  (ii) For Vietnamese entities not having lawful seals, the authentication of signatures must be effected at State Notary Public offices or local administrative agencies of localities where such entities reside or are headquartered;

  (iii) For foreign entities not having lawful seals, the authentication of signatures must be effected at notary public offices or equivalent competent agencies.

  2.2. Authentication of copies

  a/ All documents being copies made in whatever way must be authenticated as reproductions from originals according to the provisions in Item b below before they can be used as official documents in the course of carrying out related procedures concerning industrial property at competent agencies.

  b/ Documents recognized as reproductions from originals if their copies bear certification of reproduction from one of the following agencies: (i) Notary Public office; (ii) People's Committee or competent agency; (iii) State agency or social organization which has made the original document; and, if the copy has many pages, each page or the pages must be certified as being adjacent.

  2.3. Authentication of translations

  a/ All documents translated into Vietnamese must be authenticated as being made verbatim from the originals according to Item b below before they can be used as official documents in the course of carrying out related procedures concerning industrial property at competent agencies.

  b/ The certification of a translation may be effected in one of the following modes: (i) notarization; (ii) certification by the original document holder himself/herself; (iii) certification by all parties to the contract or agreement (if the original document is a contract or agreement); (iv) recognition by the agency competent to use such translation in the course of carrying out related procedures.

  3. Persons acting as owners shall carry out procedures related to industrial property

  3.1. Only persons defined in Points 3.2 and 3.3 below may act as applicants for protection titles, supplement or modify documents of applications; receive and answer opinions of the National Office of Industrial Property related to applications; decide to continue or terminate the process of protection request; receive protection titles; maintain, modify and extend validity of protection titles as well as other procedures on industrial property before the National Office of Industrial Property and competent agencies.

  The National Office of Industrial Property shall be permitted to conduct transactions with the above-mentioned persons and such transactions shall be considered official ones with the subjects. 3.2. For subjects who have the right to directly carry out the filing of Applications and related procedures prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 a/ Article 15 of the Decree, the following persons shall be permitted to act on behalf of the subjects to carry out the jobs mentioned in Point 3.1 above:

  (i) The individual or their legal representative (if the entity is an individual);

  (ii) The legal representative of the entity; an individual being a member of the entity authorized by the legal representative of the entity to represent; the head of the representative office or branch of the entity authorized by the legal representative of the entity to represent (if the entity is a juridical person or another entity);

  (iii) Heads of representative offices in Vietnam of foreign entities authorized by such entities; persons represented under a proxy agreement of enterprises established in Vietnam with 100% investment capital of foreign entities authorized by such entities;

  (iv) The person who satisfies one of the conditions stated in paragraphs (i), (ii) and (iii) above is one of the individuals or one of the juridical persons or other subjects - if the subject includes many individuals, juridical persons or other subjects and if such person is authorized by all individuals, juridical persons or other subjects.

  3.3. For subjects who are only permitted to carry out the filing of Applications and related procedures through the Industrial Property Representation Service Organization specified in Clause 3.b, Article 15 of the Decree as well as for all other subjects who carry out the above-mentioned procedures through the Industrial Property Representation Service Organization, only those who are granted industrial property representative cards by the Industrial Property Representation Service Organization with authorization letters of new subjects shall be permitted to carry out the jobs mentioned in Point 3.1 above.

  4. Authorization for carrying out procedures on industrial property

  4.1. All authorization for carrying out procedures on industrial property must be expressed in writing (authorization letter) which must include the following contents:

  (i) Name (full name), full address, telephone number, fax number (if any) of the Authorizing Party; (ii) Name (full name), full address, telephone number, fax number (if any) of the Authorized Party; (iii) Scope of authorization (jobs that the Authorized Party performs in the name of the Authorizing Party); (iv) Term of authorization; (v) Place and date of making of the Authorization Letter; (vi) Signature of the Authorizing Party (certified according to regulations on signature certification).

  4.2. The Authorized Party must be an individual or organization permitted to carry out procedures on industrial property specified at Points 3.2 (ii), (iii), (iv) and 3.3 of this Circular.

  4.3. Any change in the scope of authorization and premature termination of authorization must be notified in writing to the National Office of Industrial Property.

  4.4. If the scope of authorization of a power of attorney includes several independent procedures, the authorized party may submit copies of the power of attorney, provided that the original copy of the power of attorney has been submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property and the number and date of submission of the dossier containing the original copy of the power of attorney are indicated.

  Chapter II

                                                APPLICATIONS AND PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS

  5. General requirements for Applications

  5.1. The application must ensure the uniformity prescribed in Clause 2, Article 11 of the Decree and meet the general requirements on form prescribed at Point 5.2 below.

  5.2. The application must meet the general requirements in terms of form as follows:

  (i) Each Application may request the grant of only one Title of protection which must be appropriate to the subject matter of industrial property stated in the Application;

  (ii) All documents of the Application must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents which may be presented in other languages as prescribed in Point 5.3 below;

  (iii) All documents of an Application must be presented horizontally on a blank sheet of paper of size A4 (210mm x 297mm), with margins of 20mm on each side, except for documents added to the Application for necessary purposes of supplementation or illustration which are not intended to be included in the Application and may be presented differently;

  (iv) If a document type requires a specific form, such form must be used by filling in appropriate designated spaces;

  (v) Each document type must contain the required number of copies; if a document type contains multiple pages, the page number must be written in Arabic numerals at the beginning of each page;

  (vi) Documents must be typed or printed with an ink that is difficult to fade, clear, clean, non-erasable, and non-modifiable.

  5.3. The following documents may be made in another language other than Vietnamese but must be translated into Vietnamese:

  (i) Authorization letter (if any);

  (ii) Documents certifying the lawful right to file an application if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application, including the transfer of the previously filed Application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.);

  (iii) Certificate of priority right (if the Application requests the priority right and such right is inherited from another person);

  (iv) Documents related to proving the grounds for priority right (first application, exhibition certificate, etc.);

  (v) Original documents or copies thereof which the applicant has included in the Application to support it.

  6. Requirements on an invention/utility solution application

  Apart from general requirements prescribed in Point 5 of this Circular, an invention/utility solution application must satisfy the requirements prescribed in this Point.

  6.1. An application shall include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration for grant of a patent for an invention/utility solution, made according to the form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 3 copies;

  (ii) Description of the invention/utility solution (hereinafter referred to as the Description), in 3 copies;

  (iii) Protection request, in 3 copies;

  (iv) Drawings, schematics, calculation sheets, etc. (if necessary) to further clarify the nature of the technical solutions stated in the Description, including 3 copies;

  (v) Summary of invention/utility solution, comprising 3 copies;

  (vi) Documents certifying the lawful right to file an application if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate or agreement on assignment of the right to file an application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.), 1 copy;

  (vii) A power of attorney (if necessary), one copy;

  (viii) A copy of the first application or document certifying the exhibition if the application requests priority rights under an international treaty, one copy;

  (ix) The fee and charge receipts for filing the application and the charge for announcement of the application, 1 copy each.

  6.2. The documents mentioned in Point 6.1 above must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of filing the Application:

  (i) Vietnamese versions of documents 6.1(ii), 6.1(iii) and 6.1(v) if such documents are available in English/French/Russian versions in the Application;

  (ii) Original copies of documents 6.1 (vii) if the Application contains copies;

  (iii) Documents 6.1 (viii), including Vietnamese translations thereof.

  6.3. The Description must fully disclose the nature of the technical solution to be protected. The Description must contain all information sufficient to enable any person with average qualifications in the relevant technical field to implement such solution.

  The Description must clarify the novelty, creativity (if the subject matter is an invention) and applicability of the technical solution to be protected.

  The Description must include the following contents:

  (i) International classification indicators of inventions (according to the Strasbourg Agreement), (ii) Name of the technical solution, (iii) Field in which the technical solution is used or related to, (iv) Technical status of the technical solution in the above-mentioned field at the time of filing the application (known technical solutions), (v) Nature of the technical solution, (vi) Brief description of accompanying drawings (if any), (vii) Example of implementation of the technical solution, (viii) Possible benefits (effectiveness of the technical solution).

  6.4. Protection request aims to determine the scope (volume) of protection for inventions/utility solutions. The protection request must be presented in a concise and clear manner, in accordance with the description and drawings, clearly indicating the new features of the technical solution to be protected.

  6.5. Summary of the invention/utility solution for brief disclosure of the nature of the invention/utility solution. The summary must disclose the main contents of the nature of the technical solution for information purposes.

  6.6. Requirements on form and contents of Description, Drawings, Protection Request, Summary of Invention/Utility Solution and other documents of the Application for invention/utility solution shall be specified by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  7. Requirements on Applications for industrial designs

  Apart from general requirements prescribed in Point 5 of this Circular, an application for industrial design must also satisfy the requirements prescribed in this Point.

  7.1. An application must include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration for grant of a patent for industrial design, made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, 3 copies;

  (ii) Description of industrial design, 3 copies;

  (iii) Set of photos or set of drawings of the industrial design, 6 sets;

(iv) Document certifying the legitimate filing right if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance; certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application; assignment contract or labor contract), 1 copy;

  (v) Document certifying the ownership of marks if the industrial design contains marks, 1 copy;

  (vi) The power of attorney (if necessary);

  (vii) A copy of the first application or documents certifying the display at the exhibition if the application requests the priority right under an international treaty, including 1 copy;

  (viii) Documents proving payment of fees for filing and publication of the application, including 1 copy.

  7.2. The documents mentioned in Point 7.1 above must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of submission of the Application:

  (i) Vietnamese version of document 7.1 (ii), if the Application already contains an English/French/Russian version of such document;

  (ii) Document 7.1 (v);

  (iii) Original copy of document 7.1 (vi), if the Application already contains a copy;

  (iv) Document 7.1 (vii), including its Vietnamese translation.

  7.3. The description of an industrial design must fully and clearly present the nature of such industrial design and must be consistent with the set of photos or drawings, including the following contents:

  (i) Name of industrial design, (ii) International classification index of industrial designs (according to the Locarno Agreement), (iii) Field of use of products bearing the industrial design, (iv) Known similar industrial designs, (v) List of photos or drawings, (vi) Nature of the industrial design, in which the basic design characteristics of the industrial design requested for protection must be clearly stated, which are different from known similar industrial designs.

  7.4. The set of photos or drawings must fully express the nature of the industrial design as described in order to determine the scope (volume) of protection of such industrial design.

  The photos or drawings must be clear and sharp, and must not mix with other products of the industrial design protected.

  All photos/drawings must be taken under the same scale. Size of each photo shall not be smaller than 90 mm x 120 mm and not larger than 210 mm x 297 mm.

  7.5. Requirements for the Description and the set of photos/drawings of industrial designs shall be prescribed by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  8. Requirements for mark applications

  Apart from the general requirements prescribed in Point 5 of this Circular, a mark application shall satisfy the requirements prescribed in this Point.

  8.1 An application shall include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration for grant of a certificate of registered mark affixed with a sample mark made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, 3 copies;

  (ii) Regulations on use of the mark if the protected mark is a collective mark, 1 copy;

  (iii) Sample marks, 15 copies;

  (iv) Copies of documents certifying lawful business rights (business licenses or certificates of business registration, etc.), 1 copy;

  (v) Documents certifying lawful filing rights if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate of transfer of the right to file an application or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application, including the previously filed application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.), 1 copy;

  (vi) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  (vii) Copy of the first application or Certificate of exhibition if the application requests priority rights under an international treaty, 1 copy;

  (viii) Documents certifying the origin, prizes, medals, if the mark contains such information, including 1 copy;

  (ix) License of a competent agency, if the mark uses symbols, proper name, etc. prescribed at Point g, Clause 2, Article 6 of the Decree, including 1 copy;

  (x) Receipt for payment of fee for filing the application, including 1 copy.

  8.2. The above documents must be filed simultaneously. Particularly, the following documents may be filed within 3 months from the date of filing the Application:

  (i) Original copy of document 8.1 (vi), if the Application already has a copy;

  (ii) Document 8.1 (vii), including the Vietnamese translation.

  8.3. The description of the mark in the Declaration must clarify the distinguishability of the mark, specifying each constituent element of the mark and the overall meaning of the mark. If the mark contains words other than Vietnamese, the Vietnamese pronunciation must be clearly written; if the words have meanings, their meanings must be translated into Vietnamese.

  If the protected words or phrases are presented graphically as distinctive elements of the mark, the graphic form of such words or phrases must be described.

  If a mark contains numerals other than Arabic numerals or Lamaean numerals, it must be translated into Arabic numerals.

  If a mark consists of several separate parts used simultaneously on one product, the position of each part of such mark on the product or its packaging must be clearly indicated.

  8.4. The list of products and services bearing marks in the Declaration must be appropriate or of the same type as those permitted for business activities as stated in the Business License or Certificate of Business Registration and must be grouped according to the International Classification of Products and Services (according to the Nixon Agreement).

  8.5. The sample of a mark included in the Declaration as well as other samples of marks must be presented clearly with size not exceeding 80mm x 80mm and the distance between two nearest points not smaller than 15mm.

  If a color protection request is made, the sample of the mark must be presented in the exact color to be protected.

  If no color protection request is made, all samples of marks must be presented in black and white.

  9. Requirements for Origin Appellations.

  In addition to the general requirements specified at Point 5 of this Circular, an Origin Appellation must meet the requirements stated in this Point.

  9.1. The application must include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration for issuance of certificate of rights to use goods origin appellations, made according to a form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, 3 copies;

  (ii) Copies of documents certifying lawful business rights (business licenses, business registration certificates, etc.), 1 copy;

  (iii) Presentation on quality characteristics of products bearing goods origin appellations, with certification of competent state agencies, 1 copy;

  (iv) Certification of competent agencies that products manufactured or commercially traded by applicants have particular characteristics and quality and are manufactured in territories corresponding to such goods origin appellations (consistent with presentation in document (iii)), 1 copy;

  (v) Copies of titles of protection of goods origin appellations granted by the countries of origin, or documents of the countries of origin certifying the right of the applicants to use goods origin appellations currently under protection in the countries of origin (if the goods origin appellations originate from foreign countries), 1 copy;

  (vi) Description map of the territory corresponding to the goods origin appellation, including instructions on location of production and business of the applicant, 1 copy;

  (vii) Authorization letter (if necessary), 1 copy;

  (viii) The fee receipt shall be one copy.

  If the applicant requests only the certificate of rights to use a goods origin appellation already registered, documents (iii) and (vi) are not required in the Application. If the goods origin appellation is of foreign origin, documents (ii), (iii) and (vi) are not required in the Application.

  9.2. Documents to be submitted simultaneously. The original of document 9.1 (vii) may be submitted within 3 months from the date of application if copies are available in the Application.

  9.3. The central or local agencies managing the quality of goods with the goods origin appellations shall certify the particular characteristics of products bearing such appellations and certify that the products manufactured by the applicants bear such particular characteristics.

  10. Submission of Applications

  Applications may be submitted at the National Office of Industrial Property or at any other places designated by the National Office of Industrial Property. Applications may also be sent by post under security to the above-mentioned places.

  11. Receipt of Applications

  11.1. Upon receiving an Application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall perform the following tasks:

  (i) Re-examination of the list of documents stated in the Declaration;

  (ii) Affixing a seal to certify the date of receipt of the Application and the Declaration;

  (iii) Recording differences between the list of documents stated in the Declaration and the number of documents actually included in the Application;

  (iv) Preliminary examination of the Application for conclusion on whether or not the Application is accepted according to Point 11.2 below;

  (v) Sending to the Applicant a Declaration bearing the seal to certify the date of receipt of the Application, the number of the Application and the results of the examination of the list of documents, with the signature and name of the officer receiving the Application; (the above-mentioned Declaration replaces the Receipt of Application).

  11.2. The National Office of Industrial Property shall not accept an Application if it finds that the Application has one of the following defects:

  (i) The Application lacks one of the following mandatory documents: the Declaration, which must contain the sample mark and the list of products or services (for marks applications), the appellation of origin of goods and the type of goods (for appellation of origin applications), the Description of invention/utility solution, the Protection request (for inventions/utility solutions), the Description of industrial design and the set of photos and drawings of the industrial design (for industrial design applications), the Power of attorney (for applications requiring a Power of attorney), and the fee payment document;

  (ii) The form of protection (type of the requested protection title to be issued) is not appropriate for the subject matter of industrial property stated in the Application;

  (iii) The Declaration is not signed or has been erased or seriously altered.

  11.3. In case the Application is rejected, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the Applicant of the reason for rejection. For an Application submitted by post, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify in writing within 15 days from the date on which the Application arrives; the National Office of Industrial Property is not required to return documents of the Application to the Applicant but must refund the fees paid according to the Application rejected after deducting the costs for sending and returning the money.

  12. Processing of received Applications

  After receiving, the Application shall be processed as follows:

  A set of documents including necessary documents is separated to preserve the initial state of the Application (called "Application dossier set");

  Remaining documents are combined into a set of documents used for examination according to this Circular.

  13. Formal examination

  13.1. After being processed according to Point 12 of this Circular, the Application shall be formally examined according to this Point.

  13.2. The application shall be considered invalid if it contains one of the following defects:

  (i) The application is made in a language other than Vietnamese, except for the case specified in Point 5.3 of this Circular;

  (ii) The declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author (for inventions, utility solutions or industrial designs), about the applicant, the applicant fails to sign or the signature is not certified, and information about the representative has been erased.

  (iii) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the right to file the application;

  (iv) The application is filed against the provisions of Article 15 of the Decree;

  (v) The description, summary, protection request for invention, utility solution, or description of industrial design is made in English/French/Russian but the applicant fails to supplement the Vietnamese version within the time limit prescribed in Points 6.2 and 7.2 of this Circular;

  (vi) The power of attorney is only a copy without the original supplemented within the time limit prescribed in Points 6.2, 7.2, 8.2 and 9.2 of this Circular;

  (vii) The application contains the defects mentioned in Point 13.3 below which affect the validity of the application and although the Industrial Property Office has requested correction, the applicant still fails to correct them or the correction is unsatisfactory;

  (viii) The subject matters stated in the Application are not protected by the State as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 4, Clause 3 Article 5, Clause 2 Article 6 and Clause 2 Article 7 of the Decree;

  13.3. Handling of deficiencies in the Application during the stage of examination of form

  If the Application has the following deficiencies, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify such to the Applicant and within 2 months from the date of notification, the Applicant must correct such deficiencies:

  (i) Not enough quantity of one of the mandatory documents;

  (ii) The Application does not satisfy the uniformity requirement;

  (iii) The Application fails to meet the requirements on presentation form;

  (iv) The mark application does not clearly state the type of mark registered, lacks description of the mark, or the list of products is not classified or classified incorrectly;

  (v) Information about the Applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased;

  (vi) Failure to fully pay the fee for filing the application.

  13.4. The applicant may take initiative in amending and supplementing documents in the Application but must not expand the scope (volume) of protection and must not change the nature of the industrial property subject matter stated in the Application and must pay a fee as prescribed. If the amendment expands the scope (volume) of protection or changes the nature of the subject matter, the applicant must file a new Application and all procedures must be carried out again from the beginning.

  13.5. Determination of the date of filing a valid application

  The date of filing a valid application shall be determined as follows:

  (i) For an Application without the faults specified at Point 13.2 above, the date of filing a valid application is the date on which the Application is submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property and recorded in the receipt seal on the Declaration;

  (ii) For an Application with the faults specified at Point 13.3 above and the faults have been corrected within the corresponding time limit, the date of filing a valid application is the date on which the Application is submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property and recorded in the receipt seal. If the faults are corrected later than this time limit, the date of filing a valid application is the date on which the faults are completely corrected so that the Application becomes valid.

  13.6. Determination of the priority date

  The priority date of an Application shall be determined as follows:

  If the Application does not contain a request for priority right, the priority date shall be the date of filing of the valid Application;

  If the Application contains a request for priority right, the priority date shall be the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  13.7. The results of examination of the form of an Application shall be notified to the Applicant by the National Office of Industrial Property as follows:

  (i) If the Application is considered valid, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send to the Applicant a Notice of acceptance of the Application, clearly stating the name and address of the Applicant; the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the Application is filed through such organization); the name of the subject matter stated in the Application, the date of filing of the valid Application, the priority date; the serial number of the Application; remaining defects which the Applicant must continue to correct and the time limit for correcting such defects. Past this time limit, if the Applicant fails to correct the defects, the Application will not be further considered;

  (ii) If the Application is considered invalid, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send to the Applicant a Notice of refusal to accept the Application, clearly stating the name and address of the Applicant, the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the Application is filed through such organization); the date of filing of the Application to the National Office of Industrial Property, the name of the subject matter stated in the Application; the reason for refusal to accept the Application (the reason why the Application is considered invalid);

  (iii) If the Application still contains defects as stated at Point 13.3 above, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send to the Applicant a Notice of results of examination of the form of the Application, clearly stating the name and address of the Applicant, the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the Application is filed through such organization), the date of filing of the Application to the National Office of Industrial Property; the name of the subject matter stated in the Application, the defects to be corrected and the time limit for the Applicant to correct the defects.

  13.8. The time limit for examination of form shall be 3 months from the date the Application is received by the National Office of Industrial Property as indicated in the receipt seal; for applications with late documents as prescribed at Points 6.2, 7.2, 8.2 and 9.2, the time limit for examination of form shall be 3 months from the date on which such documents are supplemented. Before the above-mentioned time limit expires, the National Office of Industrial Property must complete the examination of form and notify the applicant according to Point 13.7 above.

  14. Publication of valid Applications

  14.1. All inventions, utility solutions and industrial designs accepted as valid shall be published by the National Office of Industrial Property in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within the following time limits:

  a/ Inventions and utility solutions shall be published in the 19th month from the priority date, except for cases specified at Points b, c and d Below;

  b/ In case of early publication request, an application shall be published within 1 month from the date the National Office of Industrial Property receives the early publication request or within the later time limit stated in the request;

  c/ For inventions and utility solutions, if there is a written request for examination of contents submitted before the acceptance of these applications as valid, an application shall be published within 1 month after it is accepted as valid;

  d/ For inventions and utility solutions, if there is a written request for examination of contents submitted after the acceptance of these applications as valid but before the expiration of the 18-month time limit from the priority date, an application shall be published within 1 month after the National Office of Industrial Property receives the request for examination of contents;

  e/ International applications and industrial design applications shall be published in the second month from the date of acceptance of valid applications, except for cases specified at Point b Above.

  14.2. Information related to a valid Application published in the Official Gazette includes all information about the valid Application stated in the Notification of acceptance of the Application, except for information on errors that must be corrected; an invention or utility solution summary enclosed with drawings if necessary; one or more drawings or photos of industrial designs.

  14.3. Everyone may have access to more detailed information about the nature of the subject matter stated in the Application published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property, or request the National Office of Industrial Property to provide such information and shall pay fees for providing information as prescribed.

  15. Request for examination of the contents of inventions or utility solutions.

  15.1. Within 42 months from the priority date of the invention application, or 36 months from the priority date of the utility solution application, the applicant or any third party may request the National Office of Industrial Property to conduct examination of the contents of the respective invention or utility solution.

  The person requesting examination of the contents of an invention or utility solution shall pay a fee as prescribed.

  15.2. A request for examination of the contents of an invention or utility solution after the application is published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property shall be submitted within 1 month from the date of receipt of the written request for examination and notified to the applicant.

  Requests for examination of the contents of inventions or utility solutions submitted before the application is published shall be published together with the corresponding application according to Point 14.1c) and d) of this Circular.

  15.3. Except for cases where the examination request is made by the applicant in the declaration form, the request for examination of the contents of an invention or utility solution must be made in writing, clearly stating the name and address of the requester; the number and submission date of the invention or utility solution application requiring examination of its contents; the name and address of the applicant, the name of the invention or utility solution, and must be accompanied by a receipt or document confirming the payment of the examination fee.

  16. Examination of contents of the Application

  16.1. The contents of an Application shall be examined by the National Office of Industrial Property under the provisions of this Point for:

  (i) All applications for marks, industrial designs and appellations of origin which have been accepted as valid and the applicants have paid the fee for content examination according to regulations; and (ii) All international applications; and (iii) All inventions, utility solutions, including international applications, which have been accepted as valid and the content examination requests have been filed with the National Office of Industrial Property within the time limit specified in Clause 15 of this Circular.

  16.2. The purpose of examination of contents of an Application is to assess the protection capacity of the subject matter stated in the Application according to the protection criteria and determine the scope (volume) of protection corresponding thereto.

  16.3. Within the time limit for examination of contents of an Application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send a notice of results of examination of contents to the applicant and the person requesting examination of contents as follows:

  a/ If the industrial property object fails to meet the protection criteria, the notice of examination results must clearly state the reasons for the intended refusal to grant the title of protection and fix a time limit of two months from the date of notification for the applicant to give his/her opinion;

  b/ If the industrial property object satisfies the protection criteria but the scope (volume) of protection has to be narrowed or the Application contains deficiencies, the Notice on the results of the content examination must clearly state this and fix a time limit of two months from the date of notification for the applicant to give his/her opinions or correct the deficiencies;

  c/ If the subject matter satisfies the protection criteria, even in the case stated at Point b above, the notice of test results shall require the applicant to pay the fee for publication of the title of protection, the registration fee and the title of protection and the fee for maintenance of validity for the first year (for inventions and utility solutions).

  16.4. During the time limit for examination of contents, the applicant may take initiative in amending and supplementing documents in the Application and must pay a fee according to regulations.

  The National Office of Industrial Property may request the applicant within a given time limit to amend and supplement documents. If the applicant fails to make such amendment and supplementation at the request of the National Office of Industrial Property without plausible reason, the application shall be considered withdrawn.

  The amendments shall not alter the nature of the subject matter and shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection stated in the Application.

  16.5. Time limit for examination of contents of an Application is (i) 18 months for an invention application or 9 months for a utility solution application from the date of receipt of a request for examination of contents, if such request is filed after the date of publication of the Application or from the date of publication of the Application if the request for examination of contents is filed before the date of publication of the Application; (ii) 9 months for an industrial design application or a trademark application from the date of signing of a notice of acceptance of the valid application; (iii) 6 months for an application for appellation of origin from the date of signing of a notice of acceptance of the valid application.

  If during the process of examining the contents of the Application, the Applicant takes initiative or at the request of the National Office of Industrial Property to amend or supplement documents, the time limit for examination of contents may be extended by the duration reserved for the purpose of amending or supplementing documents.

  Before the deadline for content examination, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of results of content examination to the applicant and the person requesting examination under Point 16.3 above.

  Chapter III

                              TRANSFER OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

  17. Contracts for transfer of industrial property rights

  17.1. Industrial property ownership transfer contract means a contract on transfer of ownership of industrial property objects or a contract on transfer of the right to use industrial property objects.

  17.2. A contract on transfer of ownership of an invention, utility solution, industrial design or mark must contain the following principal details:

  - Full names and addresses of the Transmitting Party and Receiving Party;

  - Basis for transfer (certificate of protection granted to the Transferee or transferred to the Transferee);

  - The transferee (being the entire ownership right over the entire quantity of protection of industrial property object or over a part of the quantity of protection of mark - a part of the list of goods and services);

  - Transfer price;

  - Rights and obligations of each party, including corresponding obligations not contrary to Article 40 of the Decree;

  - Conditions for modification, termination or invalidation of contracts;

  - Methods of settling complaints and disputes;

  - Date and place of signing;

  - Signatures of the Parties or their authorized representatives, with full names and positions of the signatories and certification of signatures.

  17.3. A contract on licensing of an invention, utility solution, industrial design or mark ("license contract") must contain the following contents:

  - Full name and address of the Licensor and the Licensee;

  - Grounds for licensing (the protection title granted to the Licensor; or the exclusive license contract);

  - Scope of licensing, including:

  Type of license (exclusive/non-exclusive);

  License object determined by use right limit (belonging to protected use acts) and industrial property ownership object limit (belonging to protection volume of industrial property object);

  Territorial limit (belonging to Vietnam territory);

  Time limit (belonging to time limit for protection of industrial property object).

  For secondarily licensed object, the scope of license must be within the scope of license of corresponding exclusive license contract;

  - License price;

  - Rights and obligations of each party, including corresponding obligations not contrary to Article 40 of the Decree;

  - Conditions for modification, termination or invalidation of contract;

  - How to settle complaints and disputes;

  - Date and place of signing;

  - The signatures of the parties or their authorized representatives, with their full names and positions, and the certification of the signatures.

  17.4. License contracts must not contain unreasonable restrictions on the licensee's rights, especially those not arising from the licensor's rights to the corresponding industrial property object or not aimed at protecting such rights, such as:

  - Provisions directly or indirectly restricting the export of products manufactured under licenses to territories other than those where the license transfer parties are holders of the corresponding industrial property rights or have exclusive right to import the corresponding industrial property objects;

  - The provisions forcing the licensee to buy all or a certain percentage of raw materials, components or equipment supplied by the licensor or by the person designated by the licensor for the purpose of ensuring the quality of goods produced by the licensee;

  - To prohibit licensees to improve industrial property objects (except marks) and force licensees to transfer free of charge improvements they have created or the right to file applications for protection of industrial property and industrial property rights to such improvements;

  - Provisions prohibiting licensees from filing lawsuits on the validity of industrial property rights or on the right to transfer licenses of licensors.

  17.5. If the transfer of industrial property rights is part of another Contract, the contents of the transfer of industrial property rights must be made into a separate part from the remaining parts of the Contract and must comply with the provisions in this Point.

  18. Industrial property right transfer contracts shall be approved and registered.

  18.1. Under Clause 5, Article 38 and Clause 5, Article 62 of the Decree, all contracts for transfer of ownership or transfer of the right to use industrial property subject matter falling into the following cases must be approved by the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment before carrying out the procedures for registration of the Contract according to Article 42 of the Decree and Point 20 of this Circular:

  (i) One of the parties (the Transferee or the Receiving Party) is a State organization or has joint venture capital contributed by the State while the other party is an individual or organization not belonging to the State;

  (ii) The Transferee is an individual, juridical person or another entity of Vietnam and the Receiving Party is a foreign individual or organization.

  18.2. All contracts for transfer of ownership or transfer of the right to use industrial property subject matter, including those already carried out the approval procedures, must be registered under Article 42 of the Decree and Point 20 of this Circular.

  19. Procedures for approving industrial property right transfer contracts

  19.1. A dossier for approval of a Contract (hereinafter referred to as the Approval Dossier) shall include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration requesting approval for an industrial property right transfer contract, made according to the form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 3 originals, in which the applicant must be a State organization or an organization jointly invested by the State if the Contract falls under case 18.1(i); or the Vietnamese party if the Contract falls under case 18.1(ii);

  (ii) 2 originals or 2 copies of the Contract, including the Appendix (if any); if the Contract is made in another language other than Vietnamese, it must be enclosed with a Vietnamese translation;

  (iii) Original copy of the protection title (for cases of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects), or a copy of the protection title corresponding thereto (for cases of transfer of the right to use industrial property objects), if the Contract to be approved is a secondarily made one, it must be enclosed with a copy of the certificate of registration of the corresponding secondarily made exclusive contract;

  (iv) Written consent of co-owners regarding the transfer of rights if the corresponding industrial property rights are under joint ownership; or if the above-mentioned agreement cannot be reached, there must be a written explanation for the reason for the dissent of the remaining co-owners;

  (v) Business license of the Receiving party in case of transfer of ownership or transfer of the right to use a mark;

  (vi) Receipt of payment of fees for approval of the Contract;

  (vii) Power of attorney (if necessary).

  19.2. The dossier of approval shall be submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property as prescribed in the submission of applications (point 10 of this Circular).

  The time limit for submission of an application for approval for a Contract shall be 60 days from the date of signing of the Contract. This time limit may be extended if the applicant can prove that the delay is justifiable.

  19.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall receive the application for approval as prescribed in Point 11 of this Circular, with appropriate amendments, including the documents specified in Point 11.2(i): Declaration; Contract for transfer of industrial property rights; Fee payment document and Power of attorney.

  19.4. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the application for approval within 02 months from the date of receipt of the application, as follows:

  a/ In case the application for approval is valid and the contents of the Contract comply with regulations, the National Office of Industrial Property shall report the results of considering the application for approval and propose the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to issue a decision approving the Contract within 15 days from the date of receipt of the report of the National Office of Industrial Property.

  b/ In case the application has errors which can be corrected (except for cases specified in Clause c below), the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant of industrial property rights to correct such errors within an appropriate time limit.

  The time limit for the applicant of the application for approval to correct errors in the application shall not be included in the time limit for considering the application.

  c/ In case the application for approval is invalid for the following reasons, the National Office of Industrial Property shall propose the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to refuse to approve the Contract for transfer of industrial property rights:

  (i) The applicant fails to correct errors within the time limit notified by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  (ii) The applicant for approval is not the person specified in Point 19.1(i) above;

  (iii) The transferor is not the owner of the protection title (in case of transfer of ownership right to industrial property object); or is not the owner of the protection title and also not the licensee who has been licensed to hold the exclusive right and is permitted to hold the secondary right to the corresponding industrial property object (in case of transfer of the right to use industrial property object);

  (iv) The transferee does not have a business license for the appropriate goods/service with the corresponding certificate of registered mark (in case of transfer of industrial property right to mark);

  (v) The corresponding industrial property right is no longer within the term of protection; or the industrial property object is being in dispute;

  (vi) There are grounds to affirm that the transfer will infringe upon the industrial property right of a third party;

  (vii) The contract contains contents which are not in compliance with regulations on conditions restricting the transfer, or/and lacks mandatory contents as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree and Points 17.2, 17.3, 17.4 of this Circular;

  (viii) The contract contains no clause on price or transfer price which is outside the minimum and maximum limits as prescribed;

  (ix) The contract does not contain full signatures of the Transferor and the Transferee and/or the signature is not legally certified;

  (x) The signatory of the Contract is not competent to sign it.

  19.5. Before requesting the refusal to approve the Contract, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the results of the examination of the dossier of approval, the intended refusal, the reason for refusal and fix a proper time limit for the applicant to give his/her opinion. If past such fixed time limit the applicant does not give any opinion opposing or his/her opposing opinions are unjustifiable, the National Office of Industrial Property shall officially request the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to refuse to approve the Contract.

  20. Procedures for registration of Contracts for transfer of industrial property rights 20.1. A dossier of application for registration of a Contract (hereinafter referred to as registration dossier) must comprise the following documents:

  (i) Declaration for registration of a Contract for transfer of industrial property rights, made according to the form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 02 copies;

  (ii) 02 originals or 02 copies of the Contract for transfer, including the Appendix (if any); if the Contract is made in another language other than Vietnamese, the Vietnamese translation of the Contract must be enclosed;

  (iii) Original copy of the Protection Title (for cases of transfer of the right to own industrial property objects); or copy of the Protection Title corresponding thereto (for cases of transfer of the right to use industrial property objects), if the Contract to be registered is a secondarily licensed Contract, the Certificate of registration of the secondarily licensed exclusive license must be enclosed;

  (iv) Written consent of the co-owners regarding the transfer of the right, if the corresponding industrial property right is under joint ownership; or if the above-mentioned agreement is not reached, there must be a written explanation for the reason for the non-consensus of the remaining number of co-owners;

  (v) Business License of the Receiving Party in case of transfer of the right to own or the right to use a mark;

  (vi) Decision approving the Contract of the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment (for cases where the Contract is subject to mandatory approval);

  (vii) Receipt for payment of fees for registration of the Contract;

  (viii) Authorization letter (if necessary);

  In case both approval and registration procedures are required, documents submitted in the Approval dossier shall be considered as documents of the Registration dossier.

  20.2. The Registration dossier shall be submitted and received as prescribed as for the Approval dossier (Points 19.2 and 19.3 of this Circular).

  20.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the Registration dossier within 2 months from the date of receipt of the dossier, except for the case of an approved Contract, in which the above time limit is 15 days.

  a/ If the Registration dossier is valid and the contents of the Contract comply with regulations, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a Decision on grant of Certificate of registration of Contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property objects and Protection Title in case of transfer of a trademark for part of the List of products and services; or a Certificate of registration of License Contract and carry out the following procedures:

  (i) Recording in the National Register of Industrial Property and Register of Contracts for Transfer of Ownership of Industrial Property Objects or Register of License Contracts for each respective case;

  (ii) Recording in the Title of protection (for cases of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects) and granting the Title of protection to the transferee of the trademark right for a part of the List of products or services;

  (iii) Stamping the registration seal on 2 copies of the Contract and giving one copy to the applicant for registration and keeping one copy;

  (iv) Granting to the applicant for registration one copy of the Certificate of registration of contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property object or the Certificate of registration of license contract;

  (v) Publishing the Decision on grant of the Certificate of registration in the Industrial Property Gazette.

  b/ In case the application file has errors which can be corrected (except for cases specified in paragraph c below), the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify such to the applicant for registration and request him/her to correct the errors within an appropriate time limit.

  The time limit for the applicant for registration to correct errors in the application file shall not be included in the time limit for examination of the application file under regulations.

  c/ In case the application file is invalid for the following reasons, the National Office of Industrial Property shall refuse to register the contract:

  (i) The applicant for registration fails to correct errors within the time limit set by the National Office of Industrial Property or fails to make satisfactory correction;

  (ii) The applicant for registration is neither the Transmitting Party nor the Receiving Party of the Transfer Contract and is not an Industrial Property Representative authorized as prescribed;

  (iii) The Transmitting Party is neither the owner of the Protection Title (in case of transfer of industrial property rights); nor the owner of the Protection Title and is also the licensee of the exclusive right and the transferee of the secondary right to the corresponding industrial property object (in case of license transfer);

  (iv) The Receiving Party does not have a business license for the appropriate goods/service in accordance with the corresponding trademark registration certificate (in case of transfer of industrial property rights to marks);

  (v) The industrial property rights are no longer within the term of protection or are being disputed;

  (vi) There are grounds to confirm that the transfer of industrial property rights will infringe upon the industrial property rights of a third party;

  (vii) The contract contains contents which are not in compliance with regulations on conditions restricting the transfer as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree, or/or does not contain mandatory contents as prescribed in Points 17.2, 17.3 and 17.4 of this Circular;

  (viii) The contract does not bear the full signatures of both the Transmitting Party and the Receiving Party and/or the signature is not legally certified;

  (ix) The signatory of the Contract is not competent to sign;

  (x) There is no decision approving the contract (for cases where the contract must be approved).

  d) Before officially refusing to register the contract, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant of the results of the examination of the registration dossier, the intended refusal and the reason for refusal, and shall fix a proper time-limit for the applicant to give its opinion. Past this fixed time-limit, if the applicant does not express any objection or gives unjustified opinions, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to register the industrial property right transfer contract, clearly stating the reason.

  21. Examination of applications for issuance of non-voluntary licenses

  21.1. An application for issuance of a non-voluntary license shall include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration for issuance of a non-voluntary license, made according to the form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  (ii) Documents evidencing the special significance of the invention, utility solution or industrial design for national security, defense, public health protection and environmental protection, and relevant documents evidencing that the invention, utility solution or industrial design has not been used by the industrial property owner (or the person wholly licensed to use such object) without plausible reasons or at a level which fails to meet the needs of national security, defense, public health protection and environmental protection;

  (iii) Documents evidencing the capacity to use the invention, utility solution or industrial design of the applicant and conditions which the applicant considers reasonable but have not been accepted by the industrial property owner (or the person wholly licensed to use the industrial property object) without plausible reasons;

  (iv) Voucher for payment of fee for application for issuance of non-voluntary license.

  (v) Authorization letter (if necessary).

  21.2. The dossier of application for non-voluntary license shall be submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property.

  21.3. After receiving the dossier of application for non-voluntary license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the dossier according to the provisions in Clause 5, Article 51 of the Decree. The procedures for considering the dossier of application for non-voluntary license are similar to those for approving the contract on transfer of industrial property rights (Point 19 of this Circular).

  Chapter 4:

      HANDLING OF INTERNATIONAL APPLICATIONS FOR INVENTIONS/UTILITATIVE SOLUTIONS REGISTERED AS NATIONAL MARKS

  22. Procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property

  The regulations on submission of applications and other relevant procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property as stated in Clauses 2 and 3, Article 15 of the Decree and Point 3 of this Circular shall also apply to the procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property for international applications for inventions, utility solutions and marks mentioned in this Chapter.

  23. Handling of international applications for inventions/utility solutions under the PCT Convention

  23.1 Receipt of international applications

  The Vietnam-based authority competent to receive international applications is the National Office of Industrial Property.

  The National Office of Industrial Property shall:

  (i) Receive Vietnamese origin international applications;

  (ii) Collect fees and transfer them to the International Bureau and the International Searching Agency in accordance with the provisions of the Treaty;

  (iii) Check whether the prescribed fees are paid on time;

  (iv) Examine and process Vietnamese origin international applications in accordance with the provisions of the Treaty;

  (v) Identify the subject matter of the protection application: if the subject matter of the protection application falls under the category of national secret, the following procedures shall not be carried out and the fees shall be refunded to the applicant, excluding the fee for sending and copying of international applications;

  (vi) To send one copy (the dossier) of the international application of Vietnamese origin to the International Office and one copy (the reference) to the international reference agency;

  (vii) To send and receive letters from the applicant and from the international agencies.

  23.2. Language

  The international application of Vietnamese origin filed with the National Office of Industrial Property must be made in English or Russian. Each application shall be made in 3 copies.

  In case the prescribed number of copies is not enough, the National Office of Industrial Property shall make additional copies for the necessary number of copies and the applicant shall pay a fee for making copies of the international application.

  23.3. The international reference agency and the preliminary examination agency.

  For the international units of Vietnamese origin, the competent international reference agency and the preliminary examination agency are the patent offices of Australia, Austria, the Russian Federation, Switzerland and the European Patent Office.

  23.4. The international application with Vietnam designation

  If Vietnam is designated in an international Application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall be the designated agency. In this case, to enter the national phase, within 21 months from the priority date, the applicant must submit to the National Office of Industrial Property:

  (i) An application form for a patent/utility solution, made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 3 copies;

  (ii) A copy of the international application, in 3 copies (if the applicant requests entry into the national phase before the international publication);

  (iii) The Vietnamese translation of the international application (including: Description, Protection Request (original submitted; amended and explained version under Article 19 of the PCT Treaty), Summary, and Illustrations with notes, in 3 copies;

  (iv) National fees.

  23.5. International Applications with selection of Vietnam

  If Vietnam is selected in an international application for preliminary examination, the National Office of Industrial Property shall be the selected agency. In this case, if the selection of Vietnam is carried out within 19 months from the priority date, to enter the national phase, within 31 months from the priority date, the applicant must submit to the National Office of Industrial Property:

  (i) An application form for a patent/utility solution, made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 3 copies;

  (ii) Vietnamese translations of the International Application (including: Description, Protection Request (original submitted; amended and explained according to Article 19 of the PCT Treaty), Summary, and Notes on drawings), 3 copies;

  (iii) Vietnamese translations of the Appendixes to the Preliminary Examination Report, 3 copies;

  (iv) National fees.

  23.6. Documents for claiming priority right

  In order to claim priority right, the applicant must submit to the International Office the necessary documents according to Rule 17.1(a) of the Regulations on Implementation of the Treaty; and submit to the National Office 3 Vietnamese translations of such documents within the time limit specified in Points 23.4 and 23.5 above.

  23.7. Modification and supplementation of documents in the National Phase

  In accordance with Rule 51 bis of the Regulations on Implementation of the PCT Treaty, the applicant must submit the Power of attorney, the Transfer Form of the filing right in the International Phase (if any) within 24 months from the priority date for an application designating Vietnam and 34 months for an application selecting Vietnam.

  In accordance with Article 28 and Article 41 of the PCT Treaty, in the National Phase, the applicant may modify and supplement documents of the Application as prescribed in Point 16.4 of this Circular.

  Additional and amended documents submitted by applicants to the National Office of Industrial Property must be made in Vietnamese and in three copies.

  23.8. Starting time of the National Phase.

  Time limit for processing an international application with Vietnam designated or selected in the National Phase shall be counted from the first day of the 22nd month after the priority date, if Vietnam is designated, or from the 32nd month after the priority date, if Vietnam is selected, provided that such selection has been made before the expiration of the 19-month period after the priority date, if the applicant does not request entry into the National Phase earlier than the above-mentioned time limits.

  23.9. Examination of international applications

  International applications shall be examined both in form and substance according to procedures applicable to national applications.

  23.10. International applications considered as withdrawn

  Apart from cases considered as withdrawn under the provisions of the PCT Treaty and the Implementing Regulations of the Treaty, in cases where the national fees are not paid to the National Office of Industrial Property or there is no Vietnamese translation after the expiration of the time limits prescribed at Points 23.4 and 23.5 respectively mentioned above, international applications with Vietnam designated shall be considered as withdrawn.

  23.11. Fees

  International applicants of Vietnamese origin shall pay fees at the levels and according to the procedures defined in the Regulation on implementation of the PCT Agreement and the Joint Circular of the Ministry of Finance, Science and Technology and Environment.

  24. Making and filing international applications for marks of Vietnamese origin abroad under the Madrid Agreement

  24.1. Any individual, legal entity or other subject having the right to file an international application for a mark under the Madrid Agreement, provided that such mark has been registered in Vietnam.

  24.2. Registration application

  An international application for a mark must be made in French according to the form provided free of charge by the National Office of Industrial Property, by filling in the special sections reserved for applicants (except for the sections reserved for the National Office of Industrial Property and the International Office), and must be enclosed with the mark samples. The application must specify the countries of the Madrid Agreement for which the applicant wishes to have the mark protected. The applicant shall preliminarily calculate the total amount of fees payable to the International Office according to the fee schedule printed on the application form. If the applicant is sure that the calculated amount of fee is correct or after receiving the National Office of Industrial Property's notification of the exact amount of fee payable, the applicant shall pay such fee to the International Office. In addition, the applicant shall also pay an additional fee prescribed by law to the National Office of Industrial Property.

  24.3. Receipt of applications

  Applications for international registration of marks shall be filed with the International Office through the National Office of Industrial Property.

  The date on which the National Office of Industrial Property receives the application shall be considered as the date of receipt of the application at the International Office if the International Office receives the application within two months from that date.

  24.4. Filing of Applications to International Office

  After an Application is filed with the International Office, all transactions between the Applicant and the International Office shall be conducted through the National Office of Industrial Property, including amendments to documents, restriction of the product list, transfer of registered rights.

  25. Processing of international mark registration applications with designated Vietnam

  25.1. After receiving a Notice from the International Office of an application for international registration of a mark with designated Vietnam, the National Office of Industrial Property shall examine the contents of such Application as for marks filed directly with the National Office of Industrial Property. Within 12 months after a mark is internationally registered, the National Office of Industrial Property shall make a conclusion on the possibility of protection of the mark. If the mark has no possibility of protection or is partially rejected, within such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify in writing the Applicant through the International Office, clearly stating the reason for rejection.

  If within such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property does not issue a notice of rejection, the mark shall be accepted for protection in Vietnam.

  25.2. Within 3 months after the National Office of Industrial Property issues a notice of rejection, the Applicant may lodge a complaint about the Decision of the National Office of Industrial Property. The procedures for lodging complaints and settling complaints are the same as those applicable to marks filed directly with the National Office of Industrial Property. The results of complaint settlement shall be notified by the National Office of Industrial Property to the Applicant and to the International Office.

  25.3. Marks accepted for protection in Vietnam under the Madrid Agreement shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property. The scope (volume) of protection shall be confirmed according to the contents of such registered mark as recorded by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) and certified by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  Chapter 5:

   AMENDMENT OF PROTECTION TITLES; PRESERVATION OF THE EFFECTIVENESS OF PROTECTION TITLES, INVENTIVE SOLUTIONS AND PROVISIONS; EXTENSION OF THE EFFECTIVENESS OF PROTECTION TITLES OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGNS, MARKS AND APPellations.

  26. Amendments to protection titles

  26.1. The owner of a protection title shall have the obligation to notify in writing the National Office of Industrial Property of all changes in the name and address of the owner of the protection title.

  26.2. The owner of a protection title may request the National Office of Industrial Property to narrow the scope (volume) of protection of an industrial design or mark, to remove one or more schemes of the industrial design, to modify details of the mark which, however, does not fundamentally alter the mark; to reduce a number of products on the list of products and services stated in the protection title.

  26.3. In order to amend the above-mentioned contents, the owner of a protection title shall file an application for amendment of the protection title with the National Office of Industrial Property. The application for amendment shall be made according to the form set by the National Office of Industrial Property and shall be enclosed with: (i) The original copy of the protection title; (ii) Documents certifying the amendment of the name and address of the owner of the protection title; (iii) Two sets of photos or drawings of the scheme of the industrial design to be removed; (iv) Ten samples of the modified mark; (v) Receipt for payment of fee for amendment of the protection title; (vi) A power of attorney, if necessary.

  26.4. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider an application for amendment of a title of protection within two months from the date of receipt of such application. If the application is found to be valid and the proposed amendment does not increase the scope (volume) of protection or does not change the nature of the protected subject matter, the National Office of Industrial Property shall carry out the amendment of the title of protection, register the new title and publish such change in the Official Industrial Property Gazette. In the opposite case, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant of the refusal to approve the amendment, clearly stating the reason.

  27. Maintenance of validity

  In order to maintain the validity of an invention or utility solution title, its owner shall pay a validity maintenance fee within six months before the expiry of the term of validity. The validity maintenance fee may be paid after the above-mentioned time limit but not more than six months from the expiry of the previous term of validity and the owner shall additionally pay 10% of the fee for each month of late payment.

  28. Extension of validity

  28.1. In order to have the validity of a title of protection extended, the owner shall file an application for extension with the National Office of Industrial Property within six months before the expiry of the term of validity of the title of protection.

  An application for extension may be filed after the above-mentioned time limit but not more than six months from the expiry of the term of validity of the title of protection and the applicant shall pay an extension fee plus 10% of the fee for each month of late payment.

  28.2. A dossier of application for extension of validity of a title of protection shall include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration for extension of validity of a title of protection, made according to a form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 2 copies;

  (ii) Original copy of the protection title;

  (iii) Receipt for payment of renewal fee;

  (iv) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  28.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider an application for renewal within 2 months from the date of receipt of such application. The National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision on renewal, record it in the protection title, register it and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property if the application does not fall into the following cases:

  - The application for renewal is invalid or is filed improperly according to regulations;

  - There are grounds to believe that the owner of the certificate of registered mark, the owner of the certificate of registered appellation of origin completely does not use the mark or the appellation of origin of goods or does not use such subject matter continuously for the last five years before the expiration date of the protection title without a plausible reason;

  - The applicant for renewal is not the owner of the protection title of the corresponding mark, appellation of origin of goods or industrial design.

  If the application falls into one of the above cases, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to renew with reasons stated.

  Chapter 6.

                                       PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE OF REPRESENTATION LICENSES

  29. Application for a representative license.

  29.1. The representative license shall be granted by the National Office of Industrial Property on the basis of examination of dossiers of application for the representative license as prescribed in Points 29.2 and 29.3 below. The applicant for a representative license shall pay a fee according to regulations.

  29.2. An application for a certificate of industrial property representation service organization shall include:

  - Application for a certificate of industrial property representation service organization, which includes a proposal on the list of industrial property representatives of the organization.

  - A copy of the operation charter and business registration certificate;

  - The copy of the decision on appointment to the leading position of the Organization or the written authorization for representation of the Organization, signed by the head of the Organization, by one of the members in the above-mentioned list of requesters;

  - The list of charges for industrial property representation services of an organization after having carried out registration procedures in accordance with regulations on charge and fee management;

  - Charge receipts for application for a representative license.

  The dossier of application for a certificate of industrial property representation service organization must be submitted together with the dossier of application for the industrial property representative card of individuals on the above-mentioned list.

  29.3. A dossier of application for an industrial property representative card comprises:

  - An application for the industrial property representative card, which contains certification by the People's Committee of the ward, commune or township of the applicant's permanent residence address;

  - A copy of the university degree;

  - A copy of the certificate of completion of a formal training course in industrial property; or a written certification of the duration and job performed as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 58 of the Decree;

  - A copy of the certificate of passing an examination on the current law on industrial property issued by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  - Copy of decision on recruitment of industrial property representation service organization;

  - Receipt of fee payment.

  30. Consideration of dossiers of application for representative license.

  Within one month after receiving a dossier of application for representative license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider issuing or refusing to issue a representative license. In case of refusal, the National Office of Industrial Property shall clearly state the reason therefor to the applicant. In case of acceptance and the applicant has paid the fee for issuance of representative license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall carry out the following procedures:

  - Issuing a representative license;

  - Recording the issuance of representative license in the register of industrial property representatives;

  - Publicizing the issuance of representative license in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property.

  Chapter VII

                                         FINAL PROVISIONS

  31. Complaints

  If disagreeing with a decision related to the processing of an Application as well as the approval and registration of an industrial property right transfer contract, consideration of an application for issuance of non-voluntary license, extension of protection title or grant of a representation permit, the applicant may lodge a complaint or a lawsuit according to the order and procedures specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4, Article 27 of the Decree.

  32. To process applications filed from July 1, 1996 to the effective date of this Circular.

  Applications filed from July 1st, 1996 to the effective date of this Circular may use the forms prescribed in Circular No. 1134/SC of October 17th, 1991 of the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment, guiding the implementation of Decree No. 84/HDBT of March 20th, 1990 of the Council of Ministers, and shall be processed according to this Circular. The time limit for processing the above-mentioned applications shall be extended by the period from the date of filing of the applications to the effective date of this Circular.

  33. Document forms and Regulations on testing of applications

  The Director of the National Office of Industrial Property shall be responsible for promulgating necessary forms related to Applications and related to the approval and registration of industrial property right transfer contracts, and promulgating the Regulations on examination of Applications.

  34. Implementation

  This Circular replaces the following documents of the State Committee for Science and Technology, the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment:

  - Chapters II, III and IV of Circular No. 1134/SC of October 17, 1991 guiding the implementation of Decree No. 84/HDBT of March 20, 1990 of the Council of Ministers,

  - Circular No. 437/SC of March 19, 1993 providing guidance on the registration of trademarks,

  - Circular No. 163/TT-SHCN of April 15, 1994 guiding the implementation of the regulations on approval and registration of license contracts,

  - Circular No. 238-TT-SHCN of May 2, 1994 guiding the filing and processing of international applications for protection of inventions/utility solutions under the Patent Cooperation Treaty in Vietnam,

  - Decision No. 199/QD of December 21, 1992 promulgating regulations on industrial property representation.

  This Circular takes effect 15 days after its signing.
