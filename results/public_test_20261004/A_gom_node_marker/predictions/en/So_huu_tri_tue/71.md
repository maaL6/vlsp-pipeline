                                                  CIRCULAR

  AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF CIRCULAR NO. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN OF FEBRUARY 14, 2007 GUIDING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF DECREE NO. 103/2006/ND-CP DETAILING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE LAW ON INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY, AMENDED AND SUPPLEMENTED BY CIRCULAR NO. 13/2010/TT-BKHCN OF JULY 30, 2010 AND CIRCULAR NO. 18/2011/TT-BKHCN OF JULY 22, 2011

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 28/2008/ND-CP of March 14, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Science and Technology;

  Pursuant to the Law on Intellectual Property dated November 29, 2005 and Law No. 36/2009/QH12 dated June 19, 2009, amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property dated November 29, 2005 (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Intellectual Property”);

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP of September 22, 2006, detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property and Decree No. 122/2010/ND-CP of December 31, 2010, amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP;

  The Minister of Science and Technology amends and supplements a number of provisions of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN dated February 14, 2007, guiding the implementation of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP, detailing a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, which is amended and supplemented under the Circular No. 13/2010/TT-BKHCN dated July 30, 2010 and the Circular No. 18/2011/TT-BKHCN dated July 22, 2011 (hereinafter referred to as “Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN”):

  Article 1. Amending and supplementing a number of provisions of Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN

  1. To amend Point 1.1 of Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “1.1 Industrial property rights arising or established on the grounds specified in Clause 3, Article 6 of the Law on Intellectual Property, Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 6 of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP of September 22, 2006 of the Government detailing and guiding a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property concerning industrial property, as amended by Decree No. 122/2010/ND-CP, and as specifically provided for at this Point.”

  2. To amend Point 7.1.b (iii) and add Point 7.1.b (iv) of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “(iii) Map of geographical area (if the registered mark is a certification mark for geographical origin of products or a collective mark or certification mark containing a place name or other sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties);

  (iv) Written permission from the People’s Committee of the province/centrally affiliated city to register the mark as prescribed in Point 37.7.a of this Circular (if the registered mark is a collective mark or certification mark containing a place name or other sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties).”

  3. To amend and supplement point 13.8 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “13.8 Time limit for formal examination of applications

  a) The time limit for formal examination of an application is 01 month from the date of filing the application as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.

  b) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice according to Point 13.6.a of this Circular, the period during which the applicant responds to the notice shall not be included in the time limit for formal examination. This period shall be understood as follows:

  (i) The period from the date of issuance of the notice to the date on which the applicant responds to the notice; or (ii) The time limit stated in the notice (including the extended time limit as prescribed) in case the applicant does not respond to the notice.

  c) In case the applicant proactively requests amendments to the application or responds to the notice issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam prescribed in Point 13.6.a of this Circular, the time limit for formal examination shall be extended by 10 days as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.

  d) Before the end of the time limit prescribed in Points 13.8.a, 13.8.b or 13.8.c above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must complete the formal examination of the application and notify the results to the applicant according to Point 13.6 or Point 13.7 of this Circular.”

  4. To amend Point 15.6.d, to add Points 15.6.dd and 15.6.e of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “d) Before issuing a notice of intended issuance of a protection title as prescribed in Point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall verify the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Points 25.7, 35.9 and 39.10 of this Circular.

  dd) The notification prescribed in Point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular shall be made for the following applications:

  (i) The application does not fall into the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

  (ii) The application has the earliest submission date or priority date among invention registration applications falling under the case specified in Clause 1 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

  (iii) The application has the earliest submission date or priority date among applications for registration of industrial designs falling within the cases specified in Clause 1, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

  (iv) The application has the earliest submission date or priority date among applications for registration of marks falling within the cases specified in Clause 2, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

  (v) Application under agreement specified in Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.

  e) Applications not falling under the cases specified in Point 15.6.d of this Circular shall be processed as follows:

  (i) Being rejected to grant a title of protection because the application does not satisfy the rule of first filing, if the earliest date of filing or priority date is granted a title of protection; or (ii) Being considered as an application with the earliest date of filing or priority date and handled according to the provisions at Point 15.6 above, if all applications with the earlier date of filing or priority date are rejected to be granted a title of protection or withdrawn, being considered as withdrawn."

  5. To amend and supplement point 15.7.a (iii) of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  "(iii) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions or the applicant has corrected deficiencies and obtained satisfactory results or provided a legitimate explanation within the time limit prescribed at Points 15.7.a(i) and (ii) above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of the content appraisal results, which includes:

  - For applications falling under the cases specified at Point 15.6.d of this Circular: A notice of intended issuance of the protection title and a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the protection title, the fee for announcement of the decision on issuance of the protection title, the registration fee and the fee for maintenance of validity for the first year for the invention. The applicant may request an extension of the above-mentioned time limit according to the provisions of Point 9.2 of this Circular.

  - For applications not falling under the cases specified at Point 15.6.d of this Circular: A notice of continued processing of the application according to Point 15.6.e of this Circular."

  6. To amend and supplement point 15.8 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “15.8 Time limit for substantive examination of applications

  a) The time limit for substantive examination of an application shall comply with Clause 2 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.

  b) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice according to Point 15.7.a(i) and (ii) of this Circular, the period during which the applicant responds to the notice shall not be included in the time limit for formal examination. This period is understood as follows:

  (i) The period from the date of issuance of the notice to the date on which the applicant responds to the notice; or (ii) The time limit stated in the notice (including the extended time limit as prescribed) in case the applicant does not respond to the notice.

  c) In case the applicant proactively requests amendment or supplementation of the application or responds to the notice issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as prescribed in Point 15.7.a(i) and (ii) of this Circular, the time limit for substantive examination shall be extended accordingly corresponding to the time limit for processing the request for amendment or supplementation of the application or explanation of the applicant as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:

  (i) Up to 6 months for inventions;

  (ii) Up to 3 months for marks;

  (iii) Up to 2 months and 10 days for industrial designs;

  (iv) For GIs, not more than 02 months."

  7. To amend and supplement point 25.7 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “25.7 Inspection of the first-to-file principle for inventions

  For invention registration applications already concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing notices of intended issuance of invention or utility solution patents according to Point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall inspect to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:

  a) In order to inspect the first-to-file principle, at least the following compulsory sources must be consulted (but not limited to these sources): All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (by the inspection time) with the same classification index with the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application being appraised - up to the third classification index and with an earlier submission date or priority date than the submission date or priority date of the application being appraised (if the application is entitled to priority rights) that has not yet been published or with a later publication date than the submission date or priority date (if the application is entitled to priority rights) of the application being appraised.

  b) The consultation is to find cases where there are many applications (including the application being appraised) registering inventions that are identical or similar to each other and determine the application with the earliest submission date or priority date.

  c) If there are many applications falling under the case specified in Point 25.1.b above, the invention patent or utility solution patent may only be granted to the invention in the valid application with the earliest submission date or priority date among the applications satisfying the conditions for the grant of protection titles.

  d) Among the applications falling under the case specified in Point 25.7.b above, if there are many applications with the same earliest submission date or priority date, the invention patent or utility solution patent may only be granted to the invention of a single application among those applications as agreed upon by all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all corresponding subjects of such applications will be rejected from the grant of protection titles."

  8. To amend and supplement point 35.9 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  "35.9 Examination of the first-to-file principle for industrial designs

  For industrial design registration applications already concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing the notice of intended issuance of an industrial design patent according to the provisions in Point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:

  a) To examine the first-to-file principle, it is required to search information in the compulsory sources specified in Point 35.4.b (iv) of this Circular.

  b) The search is to find cases where there are many applications (including those being appraised) registering industrial designs of product parts and/or products that are identical or insignificantly different from each other and determine the application with the earliest submission date or priority date.

  c) If there are many applications falling into the case specified in Point 35.9.b above, the industrial design patent may only be granted to the industrial design in the valid application with the earliest submission date or priority date among the applications satisfying the conditions for the grant of a protection title.

  d) Among the applications falling into the case specified in Point 35.9.b, if there are many applications with the same earliest submission date or priority date, the industrial design patent may only be granted to the industrial design of a single application among such applications under the agreement of all applicants; if no agreement can be reached, all corresponding subjects of such applications will be rejected from the grant of protection titles."

  9. To amend and supplement point 37.7 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “37.7 Requirements for documents permitting registration of collective marks or certification marks containing geographical names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam

  a) Documents permitting registration of collective marks or certification marks containing geographical names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties shall be issued by the following competent authorities:

  (i) People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities where there are geographical areas corresponding to geographical names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties (in case geographical areas belong to one province);

  (ii) All People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities where there are geographical areas corresponding to geographical names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties (in case geographical areas belong to multiple provinces).

  b) Maps of geographical areas corresponding to geographical names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties must fully present information to the extent that such geographical areas can be accurately identified and must be certified by the competent authorities mentioned in Point 37.7.a above.”

  10. To add the following point 37.8 to the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN:

  “37.8 Criteria for determining place names and other signs indicating geographical origins of products

  a) Signs indicating the geographical origin of products are signs used for local products and have the meaning to indicate the geographical origin of products (indicating that the products originate from such local areas).

  Signs indicating the geographical origin of products are usually place names but can also be symbolic signs of local areas (images of typical things of local areas, such as symbols, maps, flags, badges, scenic views, special works of local areas, etc.) or any other signs.

  Place names may be current names or historical names, official names or folk names of a geographical area (determined according to administrative boundaries or geographical methods).

  b) A place name or symbolic sign of a local area used for ordinary products (not specialties) may or may not have the meaning to indicate the geographical origin of products, depending on the products and the actual use of the place name or symbolic sign of the local area.

  c) The place name or symbolic sign of a local area has the meaning to indicate the geographical origin of products in the following cases:

  (i) Used for local specialties (specialized products with reputation due to certain characteristics, produced in local areas);

  (ii) Used for plants, domestic animals and products processed from plants and domestic animals of local areas;

  (iii) Used for products extracted from natural materials (coal, iron, steel, aluminum, cement, stone, salt, wood, etc.) in local areas;

  (iv) Used for products of local industries developing in local areas;

  (v) Other cases determined according to products and actual use of local place names or symbolic signs of local areas for products.

  d) Local place names or symbolic signs of local areas have no meaning to indicate geographical origins of products in the following cases:

  (i) Have been used with common mark functions and widely recognized, i.e., achieving the meaning to indicate commercial origin (distinguishability) and losing the meaning to describe geographical origin, e.g.: Hanoi beer, Saigon beer;

  (ii) The corresponding local area cannot be the place where the product is manufactured, e.g.: Arctic cigarettes...

  Place names or symbolic signs of local areas which have no meaning to indicate geographical origins of products may be protected as common marks without permission of local authorities.

  dd) Local place names or symbolic signs of local areas under general geographical knowledge known by many people (e.g.: names of provinces, cities, scenic spots) used for local common products (including products with business advantages but not yet having reputation or quality characteristics) used by many local business entities for their goods and services with the meaning to describe manufacturing location (but not having enough grounds to be classified in categories (c) and (d) above) will be objects not eligible for protection.

  However, such local place names and/or signboards may still be used as an auxiliary element in forming the common marks of organizations or individuals in the respective localities, provided that such place names are excluded from the scope of protection (not protected separately) and no permission from the local authorities is required."

  11. To amend and supplement point 39.10 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “39.10 Examination of the first-to-file principle applicable to marks

  For mark registration applications which have been concluded as satisfying the protection conditions, before issuing notices of intended grant of mark registration certificates according to the provisions in point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in clauses 2 and 3 of Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:

  a/ To examine the first-to-file principle, it is required to search all mark registration applications received by the National Office of Intellectual Property (by the time of examination), which have an earlier filing date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority right) than the filing date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority right) of the application currently being appraised.

  b/ The search is conducted to find cases where there are many applications (including the application currently being appraised) filed by different persons for marks identical or confusingly similar to each other for identical or similar products or services, or many applications filed by the same person for identical marks for identical products or services; and to determine the application with the earliest filing date or priority date.

  c/ If there are many applications falling into the case specified at Point 39.10.b above, the mark registration certificate shall be granted only to the mark in the valid application with the earliest filing date or priority date among the applications satisfying the conditions for the grant of the protection title.

  d/ Among the applications falling into the case specified at Point 39.10.b above, if there are many applications with the same earliest filing date or priority date, the mark registration certificate shall be granted only to the mark of a single application out of such applications under the agreement of all applicants; if no agreement can be reached, all corresponding objects of such applications shall be rejected from the grant of the protection title."

  Article 2. Effect

  This Circular takes effect 45 days after its signing./.
