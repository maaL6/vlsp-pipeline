                                                    DECREE
    ONELINE GUIDANCE AND ELABORATION OF A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE CIVIL CODE ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY LAW REGARDING COPYRIGHTS AND RELATED RIGHTS
                                                   THE GOVERNMENT
   Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated December 25, 2001;
   Pursuant to the Civil Code dated June 14, 2005;
   Pursuant to the Intellectual Property Law dated November 29, 2005;
   At the proposal of the Minister of Culture and Information,

DECREES:
   Chapter I
                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS
   Article 1: Scope of regulation
   This Decree details and guides a number of articles of the Civil Code and the Law on Intellectual Property regarding copyright and related rights.
   Article 2: Subjects of application
   This Decree applies to Vietnamese organizations and individuals; foreign organizations and individuals having activities related to copyright and related rights.
   Article 3: Protection of copyright and related rights
   1. Copyright protection means the protection of the author's rights to literary, artistic and scientific works as prescribed in Article 738 of the Civil Code and Articles 18, 19 and 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   2. Related rights protection means the protection of the performer's rights to his performance; the rights of producers of audio and video recordings to their audio and video recordings; and the rights of broadcasting organizations to broadcasts and encrypted program-carrying satellite signals as prescribed in Articles 745, 746, 747 and 748 of the Civil Code and Articles 29, 30 and 31 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Article 4: Interpretation of terms
   In this Decree, the terms below are construed as follows:
   1. “Dead work” means a work first published after its author has died.
   2. “Anonymous work” means a work which does not bear the name of its author (real name or pen name) when it is published.
   3. “Original work” means a work existing in a material form in which the creation of the work is first fixed.
   4. “Work copy” means a direct or indirect copy of part or the whole of a work. A photocopy of a work is also a copy of the work.
   5. “Fixation” means the expression in writing, other characters, lines, three-dimensional figures, layout, colors, sounds, images or the reproduction of sounds and images in a certain material form so that they can be recognized, copied or transmitted.
   6. “Audio and video recording” means a fixation of sounds and images of a performance or other sounds and images, or the fixation of the reproduction of sounds and images not in the form of a fixation associated with cinematographic works or other audiovisual works.

   7. “Copy of audio and video recording” means a direct or indirect copy of part or the whole of an audio and video recording.
   8. Public disclosure of fixed performances or phonograms and video recordings means the making available to the public of copies of fixed performances or phonograms and video recordings with the consent of related right holders.
   9. Re-broadcasting means simultaneous broadcasting of a broadcast by one broadcasting organization from another broadcasting organization. Re-broadcasting is also re-broadcasting.
   10. Encrypted program-carrying satellite signals mean signals carrying programs transmitted via satellites in such a form that their sound or visual characteristics or both have been altered for the purpose of preventing illegal reception of the programs.
   Article 5. State policies on copyright and related rights The State policies on copyright and related rights specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4, Article 8 of the Law on Intellectual Property include:
   1. Financial support for purchase of copyrights by State agencies and organizations tasked with disseminating works, performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasts of ideological, scientific and artistic value in service of the public interest, thereby contributing to socio-economic development.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Planning and Investment and relevant agencies in, guiding the formulation of financial plans (sources and funds) and mechanisms for the purchase of copyrights.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall approve lists of works eligible for copyright purchase support for central agencies and organizations; provincial-level People’s Committees shall approve lists of works eligible for copyright purchase support for local agencies and organizations.
   2. Priority investment in, training and fostering officials and public employees engaged in the management and enforcement of copyright and related rights protection from the central to local levels.
   To train and foster the contingent of officials performing the task of self-protecting rights in collective representation of copyright and related rights.
   3. To prioritize scientific research topics related to law, mechanisms and policies, and apply scientific-technical and technological measures to protect copyright and related rights.
   4. To enhance the education of knowledge about copyright and related rights in educational systems at all levels. The Ministry of Education and Training shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information in, including the contents of copyright and related rights teaching in the university, college and vocational secondary education programs.
   Article 6. Contents and responsibilities for state management of copyright and related rights
  1. The Government shall perform the unified state management of copyright and related rights.
   2. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall be responsible before the Government for performing the state management of copyright and related rights, having the following tasks and powers:
   a/ To formulate and direct the implementation of strategies, laws, mechanisms and policies on protection of copyright and related rights;
   b/ To promulgate, organize and guide the implementation of legal documents on copyright and related rights in accordance with law;
   c/ To apply measures to protect the lawful rights of the State, organizations and individuals in the domain of copyright and related rights protection;
   d/ To manage copyright to works (including computer programs and data collections) and related rights to performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasts under the State's ownership in accordance with law;
   dd. To stipulate the provision, cooperation, ordering and assurance of copyright to works and related rights to performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasts;

   e/ To build and manage the copyright and related rights apparatus; to train and foster officials in copyright and related rights;
   g. To manage activities of organizations representing copyright and related rights collectives;
   h/ Granting, re-granting, changing and canceling copyright registration certificates, related rights registration certificates and other procedures related to copyright registration certificates and related rights registration certificates;
   i. Compiling and managing the National Register of Copyright and Related Rights;
   k. Publishing and distributing Official Gazette on copyrights and related rights;
   l. To organize and direct activities of education, propagation and dissemination of knowledge about, and mechanisms and policies on copyright and related rights, and information and statistics on copyright and related rights;
   m. To organize and manage activities of assessment of copyright and related rights;
   n. To inspect, examine and handle violations of the law on copyright and related rights; to settle complaints and denunciations about copyright and related rights;
   o. To implement international cooperation on copyright and related rights.
   3. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall be responsible before the Government for taking charge and coordinating with ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and provincial-level People's Committees (below referred to as provincial-level People's Committees) in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   The Copyright Office of Vietnam is an agency of the Ministry of Culture and Information, assisting the Minister of Culture and Information in performing the function of state management of copyright and related rights. The Minister of Culture and Information shall specify the functions, tasks and powers of the Copyright Office of Vietnam in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall coordinate with the Ministry of Science and Technology in formulating general policies and laws on intellectual property protection, consolidating general information on intellectual property, implementing joint international cooperation projects on intellectual property, and performing other general tasks under the direction of the Government.
   4. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and provincial-level People's Committees shall, within the scope of their respective tasks and powers, coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   Article 7: State management competence of provincial People’s Committees
   1. The provincial People's Committee has the following tasks and powers:
   a/ To organize the guidance of implementation of law provisions on copyright and related rights in localities;
   b/ To promulgate documents according to its competence to guide and direct the implementation of law provisions and policies on copyright and related rights suitable to local conditions;
   c/ To organize activities to protect copyright and related rights in localities; to take measures to protect the legitimate rights and interests of the State, organizations and individuals in relation to copyright and related rights;
   d/ To inspect, examine and handle according to its competence complaints, denunciations and violations of law provisions on copyright and related rights in localities;
   e/ To guide and receive applications for registration of copyright and related rights according to its competence and law provisions;
   f/ To coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information and concerned ministries, sectors and provincial-level People's Committees in activities to protect copyright and related rights.
   2. The Department of Culture and Information shall assist the provincial People’s Committee in performing state management of copyright and related rights.
   The President of the provincial People’s Committee shall specify the functions, tasks and powers of the Department of Culture and Information and the district-and commune-level People’s Committees in performing state management of copyright and related rights.
   Chapter II

                                                 COPYRIGHT
   Article 8: Author
   1. Authors are persons who directly create part or all of literary, artistic and scientific works, including:
   a/ Vietnamese individuals whose works are protected by copyright;
   b/ Foreign individuals whose works are created and expressed in certain material forms in Vietnam;
   c/ Foreign individuals whose works are first published in Vietnam;
   d/ Foreign individuals whose works are protected in Vietnam under international agreements on copyright to which Vietnam is a contracting party.
   2. Organizations and individuals who support, give opinions or supply materials for others to create works shall not be recognized as authors.
   Article 9: Works expressed in other characters
   Works expressed in other characters specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in symbols instead of written language such as Braille for the blind, shorthand symbols and similar symbols which can be copied by different forms by users.
   Article 10: Lectures, speeches and other presentations
   Lectures, speeches and other presentations specified at Point b, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in spoken language and fixed in a certain material form.
   Article 11: Press works
   Press works specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law include categories such as reportage, fast news, news report, interview, comment, editorial, feature, press note and other categories of press works intended for publication or broadcast on print, radio, television, online media or other media.
   Article 12: Musical works
   Musical works specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in musical notes in musical compositions or other musical symbols with or without lyrics, regardless of whether they are performed or not.
   Article 13: Theatrical works
   Theatrical works specified at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works of different art forms, including drama (spoken drama, musical and dance drama, opera, silent drama), circus, dance, puppetry and other types of theatrical works.
   Article 14: Cinematographic works
   Cinematographic works and works created by a method similar to that specified at Point e, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works composed of a series of images forming a moving effect accompanied or not accompanied by sound, presented on a certain material and can be distributed and communicated to the public by technical devices and technologies, including film, documentary, science fiction, animation and other similar types.
   Article 15: Plastic art works and applied art works
   1. Plastic art works specified at Point g, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works presented by lines, colors, shapes and layouts such as painting, graphics, sculpture, installation art and similar forms, existing in the form of original copies. Especially for graphics, it may be presented up to the 50th version, numbered and bearing the author's signature.
   2. Applied art works specified at Point g, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works presented by lines, colors, shapes and layouts with useful features that can be attached to useful objects, mass-produced by hand or by machine such as symbols; handicrafts; forms presented on products and product packages.
   Article 16: Photographic works
   Photographic works specified at Point h, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works presenting images of the objective world on photosensitive materials or on media from which images are created or can be created by any technical methods (chemical, electronic or other methods);
   Still images taken from a cinematographic work or similar to cinematography are not considered photographic works but are part of such cinematographic work.
   Article 17: Architectural works
   Architectural works specified in Point i Clause 1 Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law are design drawings in any form expressing creative ideas about houses, construction works and space planning (construction planning) which have been or have not been built. Architectural works include design drawings of ground plans, top plans, cross-sections, perspective views expressing creative ideas about houses, works, architectural complexes, space organization, landscape architecture of an area, an urban area, urban system, urban functional zones, rural residential areas.
   Models and floor plans of houses, construction works or space planning are considered independent architectural works.
   Article 18: Sketches, diagrams, maps and drawings
   Sketches, diagrams, maps and drawings specified in Point k Clause 1 Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law include sketches, diagrams, maps and drawings related to topography, scientific and architectural works.
   Article 19: Copyright on cinematographic works and theatrical works
   1. Cinematographic works and theatrical works are created by a collective of authors. Persons participating in the creation of cinematographic works and theatrical works specified in Clause 1 Article 21 of the Intellectual Property Law shall enjoy moral rights to their creations according to Clauses 1, 2 and 4 Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law. Producers, directors and screenwriters may reach agreements on the exercise of the right to name cinematographic works specified in Clause 1 Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law and the revision of scripts of cinematographic works specified in Clause 4 Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Organizations and individuals that invest in financial and material-technical facilities to produce cinematographic works or stage productions specified in Clause 2 Article 21 of the Law on Intellectual Property are holders of rights specified in Clause 3 Article 19 and Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Organizations and individuals that invest in financial and material-technical facilities to produce cinematographic works or stage productions may reach agreements on the exercise of the rights specified in Clause 3 Article 19 and Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property and obligations specified in Clause 3 Article 21 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Article 20: Use of folklore literature and art
   1. Folklore literature and art works specified at Points a, b and c Clause 1 Article 23 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be protected regardless of their fixation.
   2. The use of folklore literature and art works specified in Clause 2 Article 23 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the study and introduction of the authentic value of such folklore literature and art works.
   3. Users of folklore literature and art works specified in Clause 2 of this Article must reach agreements on payment of remuneration to the custodian of such folklore literature and art works and enjoy the copyright over their study and introduction.
   4. Reference to the origin of a folklore literature and art work specified in Clause 2 Article 23 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the indication of the locality where such folklore literature and art work is formed.
   Article 21: Subjects not covered by copyright protection

   1. News of the day, which are purely for information provision as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 15 of the Intellectual Property Law, are daily news briefs which are merely for information provision and not creative.
   2. Administrative documents prescribed in Clause 2, Article 15 of the Intellectual Property Law include documents of regulatory agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, economic organizations, people’s armed forces units and other organizations as prescribed by law.
   Article 22: Moral rights
   1. The right to name works prescribed in Clause 1, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law does not apply to works translated from one language into another.
   2. The right to publish or permit others to publish works prescribed in Clause 3, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law means the distribution of works to the public in sufficient copies to meet reasonable public demand, depending on the nature of the work, by authors or copyright holders or by other individuals or organizations with the consent of the author or copyright holder.
   Publishing a work does not include performing a theatrical, cinematographic or musical work; reading a literary work before the public; broadcasting a literary or artistic work; displaying a plastic work; building a work from an architectural work.
   3. The right to protect the integrity of a work and prevent others from modifying or editing it prescribed in Clause 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law means the act of preventing others from modifying or editing a work unless otherwise agreed by the author.
   4. Authors of computer programs and investors in computer program production may agree on naming and development of computer programs.
   Article 23.- Economic rights
   1. The right to perform works in public as prescribed at Point b, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be exercised exclusively by copyright holders or permitted by such holders to directly perform or through audio and video recordings or any technical means accessible to the public.
   In this clause, performance of a work before the public includes performance of the work anywhere except at home.
   2. The reproduction right prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the right of copyright holders to exclusively perform or authorize others to perform the act of making copies of works by any means or form, including regular or temporary storage of works in electronic form.
   3. The right to distribute originals or reproductions of works specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law means the right of copyright holders to exercise or authorize others to exercise in any technical form or mode accessible to the public for sale, lease or other transfer of the original or reproduction of works.
   For plastic art works and photographic works, distribution rights also include public display and exhibition.
   4. The right to communicate a work to the public by wire, wireless means, electronic information networks or any other technical means specified at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law means the exclusive right of the copyright owner or his licensee to communicate the work or a copy of the work to the public which is accessible at a place and time selected by the owner or licensee.
   5. The right to lease originals or reproductions of cinematographic works or computer programs specified at Point f, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be exercised exclusively by copyright holders or permitted by such holders for a definite term.

   The right to lease shall not apply to computer programs which are not themselves the main object of lease such as computer programs associated with the normal operation of means of transport as well as other technical machines and equipment.
   Article 24: Reasonable quotation and importation of copies of works
   1. Reasonable quotation of a work without misrepresenting the author's intention for comments or illustrations in one's own work as provided at Point b, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law must comply with the following conditions:
   a/ The quoted part is intended solely to introduce, comment on or clarify matters mentioned in one's own work;
   b/ The quantity and actual nature of the quoted part from the work used for quotation do not cause prejudice to the copyright to the work used for quotation; and are appropriate to the nature and characteristics of the type of work used for quotation.
   2. Importation of copies of another person's work for personal use as provided at Point k, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law shall apply only to cases of importing not more than one copy.
   3. The use of a work in the cases specified in Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law does not apply to the reproduction of architectural works, plastic art works or computer programs.
   Article 25: Reproducing works
   1. The reproduction of one copy prescribed at Point a, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law applies to scientific research and teaching activities of individuals not for commercial purposes.
   2. Reproducing works for archival in libraries for research purposes prescribed at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law means the reproduction of not more than one copy.
   3. Libraries shall not reproduce and distribute copies of works to the public, including digital copies. Article 26: Term of copyright protection
   1. The term of protection of property rights and moral rights prescribed in Clause 3, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law for posthumous works is fifty years from the first publication of such works.
   2. The term of protection of property rights and moral rights prescribed in Clause 3, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law for photographic works and works of applied fine art prescribed at Point a, Clause 2, Article 27 of the Intellectual Property Law is fifty years from the first publication of such works. If such works have not yet been published, the term of protection shall be fifty years from the date on which such works are fixed.
   3. From the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law, which is July 1, 2006, the term of protection of photographic works and works of applied fine art shall be calculated according to the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article.
   Article 27: Copyright owners
   Copyright owners defined in Article 36 of the Intellectual Property Law include:
   1. Vietnamese organizations and individuals;
   2. Foreign organizations and individuals whose works are created and expressed in a certain material form in Vietnam;
   3. Foreign organizations and individuals whose works are first published in Vietnam;
   4. Foreign organizations and individuals whose works are protected in Vietnam under international treaties on copyright to which Vietnam is a signatory.
   Article 28: Copyright owners of anonymous works
   1. Anonymous works specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be owned by the State.
   2. In case an anonymous work is managed by an organization or individual, such organization or individual shall have the rights of the owner.

   3. When the actual owner of a work is identified, the ownership shall belong to that owner from the date the owner's identity is identified.
   Article 29.- Use of works under the State ownership
  1. When using works under the State ownership as stipulated in Points a and b, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law, except for cases specified in Clauses 2 and 3, Article 28 of this Decree, organizations and individuals shall have to perform the following obligations:
   a/ To obtain permission for use;
   b/ To pay royalties, remunerations or other material benefits;
   c/ To submit a copy of the work within thirty days after its dissemination and circulation.
   2. Organizations and individuals shall perform the obligations stipulated in Clause 1 of this Article at the Copyright Office for Literature and Art.
   3. The Copyright Office for Literature and Art shall be responsible for receiving copyrights in whatever form from organizations and individuals stipulated in Point c, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law according to law provisions.
   4. The Ministry of Culture and Information and the Ministry of Finance shall stipulate the financial management regime stipulated in Point b, Clause 1 of this Article.
   Article 30: Use of works belonging to the public
   1. Organizations and individuals using works belonging to the public specified in Article 43 of the Law on Intellectual Property must respect the moral rights prescribed in Clauses 1, 2 and 4, Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Owners of copyrights to works belonging to the public shall not be entitled to the right to publish as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 19 and economic rights as prescribed in Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   2. When detecting acts of infringement of moral rights prescribed in Clauses 1, 2 and 4, Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property to works whose term of protection has expired, state agencies, organizations and individuals with relevant rights and obligations may request persons committing such acts to stop such acts, publicly apologize, correct errors and pay damages; may lodge complaints, denunciations or petitions for competent state agencies to take actions. Depending on the nature and seriousness of the infringements, organizations and individuals committing such acts may be handled according to administrative, civil or criminal laws.
   Socio-political-professional organizations, socio-professional organizations and organizations representing collective rights of copyright holders may request competent state agencies to protect moral rights to works of members whose term of protection has expired.
   Chapter 3
                                               RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 31.- Rights of performers
   1. “Direct reproduction of a performance fixed on audio or video recording according to Point b Clause 3 Article 29 of the Law on Intellectual Property” means the creation of other copies from such audio or video recording.
   2. Indirect reproduction of a performance fixed on audio or video recording according to Point b Clause 3 Article 29 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the creation of other copies not from such audio or video recording, including reproduction from electronic information networks, broadcasts, relevant telecommunications postal network services and similar forms.
   3. “transmission to the public of an unfixed performance prescribed at Point c, Clause 3, Article 29 of the Intellectual Property Law” means dissemination of an unfixed performance to the public by any technical means other than broadcasting.
   Article 32.- Use of related rights without having to ask for permission or pay royalties or remunerations For cases of use of related rights not for commercial purposes, there is no need to ask for permission or pay royalties or remunerations, including:

   1. Making one copy for personal scientific research purposes prescribed at Point a, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Making one copy for teaching purpose, except for published performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts for teaching purposes specified in Point b Clause 1 Article 32 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Article 33.- Reasonable quotation
   Reasonable quotation for the purpose of providing information specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law means the use of quotations for the sole purpose of reporting news, which must be consistent with the following conditions:
   1. The quoted part is only intended to introduce, comment or clarify issues in the provision of information;
   2. The quantity and substance of the quoted part from a performance, phonogram, video recording or broadcast are not prejudicial to the rights of the performer, the rights of the producer of the phonogram or video recording, or the rights of the broadcasting organization to the performance, phonogram, video recording or broadcast used for quoting; and consistent with the nature and characteristics of the performance, phonogram, video recording or broadcast used for quoting.
   Article 34: Provisional copies
   Provisional copies of broadcasting organizations entitled to broadcasting rights as prescribed in Point d, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law means fixed copies made by such organizations using their own means and equipment for the purpose of serving their immediate subsequent broadcasts. In special cases, such copies may be stored at official archives.
   Article 35: Use of phonograms and video recordings
   1. Direct use of published phonograms or video recordings for commercial purposes in order to make broadcasts which are sponsored, advertised or charged in whatever form specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Intellectual Property Law means the use by broadcasting organizations of such phonograms or video recordings for broadcasting via wireless or wired means, including also transmission through satellites or digital environment. Indirect use of published phonograms or video recordings for commercial purposes in order to make broadcasts which are sponsored, advertised or charged in whatever form specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Intellectual Property Law means the rebroadcasting or re-transmission of broadcast programs; or the uploading of programs into the digital environment for broadcasting.
   2. Use of published audio and video recordings in business and commercial activities specified at Point b, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the direct or indirect use by organizations and individuals of published audio and video recordings for use in restaurants, hotels, shops, supermarkets; karaoke establishments, post offices, telecommunications service providers, digital environment; tourism, aviation, public transport and other business and commercial activities.
   3. The payment of remuneration to performers in cases where audio and video recordings are used as prescribed in Article 33 of the Law on Intellectual Property depends on the agreement between the performer and the producer of such audio and video recordings when performing audio and video recording programs.
   The ratio of distribution of royalties, remuneration and other material benefits shall be agreed upon by rights holders or copyright and related rights collective representative organizations.
   Copyright and related rights collective representative organizations may entrust one copyright and related rights collective representative organization to collect and distribute royalties, remuneration and other material benefits. Entrusted copyright and related rights collective representative organizations shall enjoy a certain fee under agreement.
   Article 36: Owners of broadcasts
   Owners of broadcasts specified in Clause 3, Article 44 of the Law on Intellectual Property means broadcasting organizations that invest their financial resources and material and technical facilities in broadcasting.

   When using works, audio and video recordings to produce broadcasts, broadcasting organizations must fulfill obligations to copyright holders and related right holders in accordance with law.
   Chapter 4:
                     CERTIFICATE OF COPYRIGHT REGISTRATION AND RELATED RIGHTS REGISTRATION
   Article 37: Copyright and related rights registration

   1. Authors, copyright owners and related right owners as defined in Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property may directly or authorize other organizations and individuals to file applications for copyright and related rights registration with the Copyright Office of Literature and Art or file applications at the provincial-level Culture and Information Services of the localities where they reside or are headquartered.
   2. Foreign individuals and juridical persons having works, performances, phonograms, videograms or broadcasts whose copyright and related rights are protected as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 13 and Article 17 of the Law on Intellectual Property may directly or authorize copyright and related rights consultancy and service organizations to file applications for copyright and related rights registration with the Copyright Office of Literature and Art or the provincial-level Culture and Information Services of the localities where they reside or are headquartered.
   Article 38: Copies of registered copyright works, copies of fixed recordings of performances, phonograms, videograms or broadcasts registered for related rights
   1. The Copyright Office of Literature and Art shall keep one copy of a work registered for copyright and one copy of a fixed recording of a related right subject matter specified at Point b, Clause 2, Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property after granting a certificate of copyright registration or a certificate of related right registration.
   2. A copy of a work registered for copyright as prescribed in Point b Clause 2 Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property may be replaced by a three-dimensional space photograph for works with particular characteristics such as paintings, statues, monuments, bas-reliefs or grand murals associated with architectural works; or works of excessively large size and bulky shape.
   Article 39: Authority to grant Certificates of Copyright Registration, Certificates of Related Rights Registration
   1. The Copyright Office of Vietnam has the authority to grant, re-grant, renew and cancel validity of Certificates of Copyright Registration, Certificates of Related Rights Registration specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 51 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   a. Authors, copyright owners and related rights owners who wish to apply for re-grant or renewal of Certificates of Copyright Registration or Certificates of Related Rights Registration shall submit applications clearly stating reasons and documents specified in Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   b. The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall re-grant Certificates of Copyright Registration or Certificates of Related Rights Registration in case where these certificates are lost; renew Certificates of Copyright Registration or Certificates of Related Rights Registration in case where these certificates are torn, damaged or the owners of copyright or related rights change;
   c. The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall cancel Certificates of Copyright Registration or Certificates of Related Rights Registration in case where it is determined that the holders of these certificates are not authors or owners of copyright or related rights, or in case where the registered works, performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts are not eligible for protection as prescribed by law.
   2. The Department of Culture and Information shall, after receiving the application for registration, re-grant or change of a copyright registration certificate or related rights registration certificate as prescribed by law, transfer it to the Copyright Office of Vietnam for consideration and settlement according to its competence.
   The Department of Culture and Information shall send it to the applicant immediately after receiving the result of consideration and settlement from the Copyright Office of Vietnam.

   3. Organizations and individuals shall pay fees and charges when carrying out procedures related to copyright and related rights to the Copyright Office of Vietnam as prescribed by law.
   4. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall issue forms of applications for registration, copyright registration certificates and related rights registration certificates.
   Article 40: Validity of copyright registration certificates and related rights registration certificates Copyright registration certificates issued by the Copyright Protection Company of Vietnam, the Copyright Protection Agency of Vietnam, the Copyright Office of Vietnam and the Copyright Office of Literature and Art of Vietnam before the effective date of the Civil Code and the Law on Intellectual Property shall continue to be valid.
   Chapter 5:
      COLLECTIVE REPRESENTATIVE ORGANIZATIONS, ORGANIZATIONS PROVIDING COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS CONSULTANCY AND SERVICES
   Article 41. Collective representative organizations

   1. Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 56 of the Law on Intellectual Property must satisfy the following conditions when operating:
   a) The representative organization of the collective of copyright and related rights must be authorized by the author or owner of copyright and related rights;
   b) The collective representative organization is authorized to represent the author or owner of copyright or related rights in the management of a specific right or group of rights;
   c/ The collection and distribution of royalties, remunerations and other material benefits arising from the exploitation of rights or groups of rights specified in the operation charter of the collective representative organization.
   2. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall guide the distribution of royalties, remunerations and other material benefits in cases where authors or copyright holders or related rights holders have not yet authorized their representatives.
   3. In case a work, phonogram or video recording or broadcast is related to rights and interests of many organizations acting as collective representatives authorized to represent different rights or groups of rights, the parties may agree to let one organization negotiate on their behalf for use license, collect and divide money and report to the Ministry of Culture.
   - Information before implementation.
   4. Collective representative organizations must send reports and information to the Copyright Office of Vietnam on a six-monthly, annual or irregular basis about their activities.
   Article 42. Copyright and related rights consultancy service providers
   1. A copyright and related rights consultancy or service organization may be established under Clause 1 Article 57 of the Law on Intellectual Property when its head and individuals providing copyright and related rights consultancy and services fully satisfy the following conditions:
   a/ Being a Vietnamese citizen with full civil act capacity;
   b/ Having their permanent residence in Vietnam;
   c/ Having a university degree in law.
   2. To submit biannual, annual or irregular reports on copyright and related rights consultancy and services to the Copyright Office of Vietnam.
   Chapter 6.
                                 PROTECTION OF COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 43.- Right to self-defense

   1. Application of technological measures to prevent acts of infringement of copyright and related rights specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 198 of the Intellectual Property Law means the right holders' putting up management information on the original or copies of works, phonograms, videograms or broadcasts; putting up management information on the works together with their dissemination to the public in order to identify the work, its author, the owner of rights, information on the term and conditions for use of the work and all data or codes or symbols expressing such information in order to protect copyright and related rights. At the same time, rights holders may apply technological measures to protect management information and prevent acts of accessing or illegally exploiting their ownership rights in accordance with law.
   2. Rights holders may apply other measures specified in Clause 1 Article 198 of the Law on Intellectual Property to protect their rights and legitimate interests.
   Article 44. Right to initiate civil lawsuits on copyright and related rights
   1. The following rights holders have the right to initiate lawsuits at competent Courts to protect their lawful rights and interests related to copyrights and related rights:
   a/ Author;
   b/ Owners of copyright and related rights;
   c/ The lawful heir of the author or the owner of copyright or related rights;
   d. Individuals and organizations that are transferred the rights of copyright holders or related rights holders;
   dd. Individuals and organizations that use works under contracts;
   e. Performers;
   g. Producers of audio and video recordings;
   h. Broadcasting organizations;
   i. Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights that are authorized;
   k. Other rights holders as prescribed by law.
   2. State agencies and relevant organizations within their tasks and powers may initiate civil lawsuits to request Courts to protect public interests and State interests in the field of copyright and related rights.
   Article 45. Complaints and denunciations related to copyright and related rights registration
  1. The following persons are entitled to file complaints or denunciations:
   a/ Authors, copyright owners, related right owners, authorized organizations and individuals may complain about the grant, re-grant, change or cancellation of copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates.
   b/ Any third party may denounce the grant of copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates.
   2. Procedures for filing complaints or denunciations are prescribed as follows:
   a/ Persons filing complaints about copyright and related right registration must send written complaints to the Copyright Office of Literature and Art. Such a written complaint must clearly state the date of complaint; name and address of the complainant; name and address of the complained agency/organization/individual; content and reason(s) for complaint and the complainant's request. The written complaint must bear the signature of the complainant. Enclosed with the written complaint shall be documents such as the copyright registration certificate, related right registration certificate, or decision on invalidation, written refusal to grant, re-grant or change of the copyright registration certificate or related right registration certificate and other relevant documents and evidence.
   b/ Persons filing denunciations must send written denunciations to competent State agencies. Such a written denunciation must clearly state the date of denunciation; name and address of the denouncer; name and address of the denounced agency/organization/individual; content and reason(s) for denunciation and the denouncer's request. The written denunciation must bear the signature of the denouncer. Enclosed with the written denunciation shall be relevant documents and evidence.

   3. Within the time limit prescribed by the Law on Complaints and Denunciations, the Copyright Office of Literature and Art, the Ministry of Culture and Information or competent State agencies shall reply in writing the complainants or denouncers.
   4. In case of disagreement with the reply of the Copyright Office of Vietnam, the Ministry of Culture and Information or competent state agencies, the complainant or denouncer may lodge a complaint or denunciation to the competent authority as prescribed by law.
   Chapter 7
                                                              IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
   Article 46: Transition period
  1. Copyright and related rights shall be protected in accordance with the effective legal documents before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law; if the copyright and related rights are still under protection on the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law, they shall continue to be protected in accordance with the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Applications for registration of copyright and related rights filed with competent authorities before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law shall continue to be processed in accordance with the effective legal documents at the time of filing.
   3. All acts of infringement of copyright and related rights or breach of contracts made before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law shall be handled in accordance with the applicable laws at the time of occurrence of such acts.
   Article 47: Entry into force
   This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO. This Decree replaces the Government's Decree No. 76/CP of November 29, 1996, guiding the implementation of a number of provisions on copyright in the Civil Code.
   Article 48.- Implementation responsibility
   1. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies, presidents of provincial/municipal People's Committees and agencies, organizations and individuals with related rights and obligations shall implement this Decree.
   2. The Minister of Culture and Information shall guide the implementation of this Decree and organize the implementation thereof.
