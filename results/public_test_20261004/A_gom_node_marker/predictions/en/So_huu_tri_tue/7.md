                                            CIRCULAR
  ELABORATING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES AND PROVIDING IMPLEMENTATION MEASURES OF THE LAW ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, DECREE NO. 65/2023/ND-CP DATED AUGUST 23, 2023 OF THE GOVERNMENT ELABORATING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES AND PROVIDING IMPLEMENTATION MEASURES OF THE LAW ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY REGARDING INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY, PROTECTION OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS, RIGHTS TO PLANT VARIETIES, AND STATE MANAGEMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, AMENDED BY DECREE NO. 15/2026/ND-CP DATED JANUARY 14, 2026, DECREE NO. 33/2026/ND-CP DATED JANUARY 21, 2026, AND DECREE NO. 100/2026/ND-CP DATED MARCH 31, 2026
Pursuant to the Law on Intellectual Property No. 50/2005/QH11 amended by Law No. 36/2009/QH12, Law No. 42/2019/QH14, Law No. 07/2022/QH15, Law No. 93/2025/QH15 and Law No. 131/2025/QH15; Pursuant to Decree No. 55/2025/ND-CP of the Government on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Science and Technology;
Pursuant to Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP of the Government elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP;
At the request of the Director General of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and the Director of the Department of Legal Affairs;
The Minister of Science and Technology promulgates a Circular elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP dated August 23, 2023 of the Government elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP dated January 14, 2026, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP dated January 21, 2026, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP dated March 31, 2026.
Chapter I
                                    GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
1. This Circular elaborates on Clause 2 Article 67, Clause 3 Article 74, Clause 4 Article 89, Clause 4 Article 94, Clause 8 Article 95, Clause 7 Article 96, Clause 4 Article 97, Clause 8 Article 100, Clause 1 Article 108, Clause 6 Article 109, Clause 3 Article 112a, Clause 4 Article 113, Clause 4 Article 114, Clause 5 Article 115, Clause 4 Article 116, Clause 7 Article 119a, Clause 3 Article 120, Clause 2 Article 150, Clause 3 Article 155 of the Law on Intellectual Property; Clause 5 Article 14a, Clause 2 Article 48, Points a and c Clause 4 Article 62, Points a and c Clause 4 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. Guidelines for implementation of certain contents of the Law on Intellectual Property and Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP:
a) Authorization to carry out procedures related to industrial property rights in accordance with Clause 4 Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Industrial property gazette, national register of industrial property, industrial property representatives;
c) Assurance of industrial property information.
Article 2. Regulated entities
Organizations and individuals carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights, complaint procedures, and complaint resolution related to industrial property procedures, organizations and individuals related to assurance of industrial property information, organizations and individuals related to training in industrial property laws, training in industrial property assessment operations, and other relevant organizations and individuals.
Article 3. Interpretation of terms
In this Circular, the terms below are construed as follows:
1. "Applicant" performing procedures related to industrial property rights (hereinafter referred to as "applicant") means an organization or individual filing an application for registration of an invention, layout design, industrial design, mark, geographical indication, filing an international application, submitting a request for extension or maintenance of validity of a protection title, termination or invalidation of a protection title, amendment of a protection title, or submitting an application for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract.
2. "Procedures related to industrial property rights" in this Circular include procedures for registration of establishment of rights to inventions, industrial designs, layout designs, marks and geographical indications, procedures for maintenance and extension of validity of protection titles, procedures for termination and invalidation of protection titles, procedures for amendment of protection titles, and procedures for transfer of industrial property rights. Requests arising from procedures related to industrial property rights include opposition to applications, amendments, additions, recognition of changes in applicants, division, withdrawal, conversion of applications, and requests for rapid content appraisal, which are part of the procedures specified in this clause.
3. "Complainant" means an organization or individual making a complaint related to industrial property procedures prescribed in Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
4. "Law on Intellectual Property" means the Law on Intellectual Property No. 50/2005/QH11, amended by Law No. 36/2009/QH12, Law No. 42/2019/QH14, Law No. 07/2022/QH15, Law No. 93/2025/QH15 and Law No. 131/2025/QH15.
5. “Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP” refers to Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP of the Government of Vietnam on elaboration of certain articles and implementation measures of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights, and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP.
6. “Paris Convention” refers to the Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property, adopted in 1883, amended in 1967 and 1979.
7. “PCT Treaty” refers to the Patent Cooperation Treaty, adopted in 1970, amended in 1979, 1984, and 2001.
8. “Budapest Treaty” refers to the Budapest Treaty on International Recognition of the Deposit of Microorganisms for the Purposes of Patent Procedure, adopted in 1977, amended in 1980.
9. “Madrid Agreement” refers to the Madrid Agreement on International Registration of Marks, adopted in 1891, amended in 1979.
10. “Madrid Protocol” refers to the Protocol relating to the Madrid Agreement, adopted in 1989, amended in 2006 and 2007.
11. “Hague Agreement” refers to the Hague Agreement on International Registration of Industrial Designs, adopted in 1999.
12. “PCT Application” refers to an invention registration application filed under the PCT Treaty.
13. “PCT application with designation or selection of Vietnam” means a PCT application filed with any member of the PCT Agreement, including Vietnam, in which Vietnam is designated or selected.
14. “PCT application at national stage” means a PCT application with designation or selection of Vietnam filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
15. “PCT application of Vietnamese origin” means a PCT application filed from Vietnam, which contains a protection request with respect to any member of the PCT Agreement, including Vietnam.
16. “Madrid application” means an international mark registration application filed under the Madrid Agreement or the Madrid Protocol.
17. “Madrid application of Vietnamese origin” means a Madrid application for protection of a mark filed from Vietnam with respect to other members of the Madrid Agreement or the Madrid Protocol.
18. “Madrid application with designation of Vietnam” means a Madrid application for protection of a mark filed from Vietnam with respect to other members of the Madrid Agreement or the Madrid Protocol.
19. “La Hay application” means an industrial design international registration application filed under the Hague Agreement.
20. “La Hay application with designation of Vietnam” means a La Hay application for protection of an industrial design filed from Vietnam with respect to any member of the Hague Agreement, including Vietnam.
21. “La Hay application originating from Vietnam” means a La Hay application filed from Vietnam, which includes a request for industrial design protection in any member of the Hague Agreement, including Vietnam.
22. “International office” means an International Office of the World Intellectual Property Organization.
Article 4. Procedures for processing applications for registration of inventions, industrial designs, marks, geographical indications and layout designs
1. Applications for registration of inventions, industrial designs, marks and geographical indications shall be received and processed in the following order, except for cases where there are separate provisions applicable to each type of application according to this Circular and relevant legal documents:
a) Receipt as prescribed in Articles 13, 47, 70 and 95 of this Circular;
b) Formal examination as prescribed in Articles 14, 48, 71 and 96 of this Circular;

c) Publication as prescribed in Articles 15, 49, 72 and 97 of this Circular, except for confidential invention registration applications that must not be published according to Article 49 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
d) Content examination as prescribed in Articles 17, 50, 73 and 98 of this Circular. For invention registration applications, content examination shall be carried out upon request;
d) In case a third party has an objection to the application, such objection shall be handled in accordance with Articles 27, 59, 82 and 103 of this Circular;
e) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on grant or refusal to grant a protection title based on the results of the content appraisal of the application. The decision on grant of a protection title shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette;
g) During the processing of the application, the applicant may amend, supplement, withdraw, split or convert the application as prescribed, provided that corresponding fees and charges are paid.
2. An arrangement design registration application shall be received, formally appraised and published in accordance with Articles 40, 41 and 42 of this Circular. During the processing of the application, the applicant may amend, supplement or withdraw the application as prescribed, provided that corresponding fees and charges are paid. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on grant or refusal to grant a protection title based on the results of the formal appraisal of the arrangement design registration application. The decision on grant of a protection title shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.
Article 5. Calculation of time limits in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaint procedures
1. Time limits in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaint procedures shall be calculated in accordance with Article 15 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. The period or fixed time limit (including extended periods as prescribed) for the applicant, complainant or related party to respond to the notification, amend or supplement documents as prescribed shall not be included in the time limit for processing the respective application or request as prescribed, including:
a) The period from the date of issuance of the notification until the date the applicant, complainant or related party responds to the notification; or the fixed time limit stated in the notification, in cases where the applicant, complainant or related party does not respond to the notification;
b) The time from the date of filing the application to the date on which the applicant submits or the complainant or related party submits supplementary documents as prescribed in this Circular; or the period for the applicant, complainant or related party to submit supplementary documents as prescribed in this Circular in case the applicant, complainant or related party fails to submit supplementary documents.
Article 6. Method of filing applications for implementation of procedures related to industrial property rights and filing complaints
1. Applicants and complainants may file applications for implementation of procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints by themselves or through their lawful representatives in Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 89, Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
2. Legal representatives of individuals as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article and Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property are determined as follows:
a) For Vietnamese individuals, foreign individuals permanently residing or having production and business establishments in Vietnam, legal representatives include: individuals acting as representatives at law or under authorization; industrial property representation service providers acting under authorization;
b) For foreign individuals not permanently residing in Vietnam or not having production and business establishments in Vietnam, legal representatives are industrial property representation service providers acting under authorization.
3. Legal representatives of organizations as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article and Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property are determined as follows:
a) For Vietnamese organizations, legal representatives include: representative offices or branches in Vietnam; industrial property representation service providers acting under authorization;
b) For foreign organizations having production and business establishments in Vietnam, their lawful representatives include: representative offices or branches in Vietnam or wholly foreign-invested organizations in Vietnam of such organizations established under investment laws; industrial property representation service organizations acting under authorization;
c) For foreign organizations not having production and business establishments in Vietnam, their lawful representatives are industrial property representation service organizations acting under authorization.
4. Vietnamese individuals and foreigners permanently residing in Vietnam shall submit applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights by themselves and file complaints according to Clause 1 Article 89 and Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property by signing their names as individuals on declarations.
5. Vietnamese organizations and foreign organizations having production and business establishments in Vietnam shall submit applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights by themselves and file complaints according to Clause 1 Article 89 and Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property through their legal representatives or persons belonging to organizations authorized by their legal representatives.
6. When carrying out relevant procedures, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may only conduct transactions with applicants, complainants or lawful representatives of applicants or complainants according to Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article. The conduct of transactions with the above-mentioned subjects is collectively referred to as transactions with applicants and complainants.
Article 7. Application submission forms
1. Applications for procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints shall be submitted in paper or electronic form via the electronic application submission system in accordance with Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Circular.
2. Electronic applications specified in Clause 1 of this Article are documents created or digitalized from physical documents and accurately and completely reflect the contents and format of physical documents, with electronic authentication as prescribed by the law on electronic transactions. Applications for procedures related to industrial property rights, electronic complaints shall be submitted via the electronic application submission system and have legal validity equivalent to that of applications for procedures related to industrial property rights and written complaints.
3. Applications shall be received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam at the One-stop Division of the Ministry of Science and Technology.
Article 8. Authorization in procedures related to industrial property rights and procedures for filing complaints
1. The authorization of representation, including re-authorization and the performance of authorization of representation in procedures related to industrial property rights (hereinafter referred to as "authorization") must comply with the authorization laws prescribed by the Civil Code, Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property, and the guidelines provided herein.
Applicants and complainants may change their authorized representatives. The change of an authorized representative terminates the authorization relationship between the applicant or complainant and the authorized person. The termination of authorization with the authorized person and the change of the new authorized representative must be declared in writing by the applicant or complainant (in the authorization document or a separate document).
The authorized person may re-authorize another person in accordance with the Civil Code, and the re-authorized person must meet the requirements for lawful representation as prescribed in Article 6 of this Circular. When the re-authorized organization or individual conducts transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving complaints must submit the initial authorization document, except where such document has already been submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. The time when a written authorization is recognized in a transaction with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or an administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving the complaint is the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving the complaint receives a valid written authorization. In case of change of the authorized representative or re-authorization or revision of information related to changes in the authorization scope, premature termination of authorization, change of the address of the authorized party, this time is the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving the complaint receives corresponding valid documents.
3. In case the written authorization is submitted later than the submission date but before the date on which the application is accepted as valid or received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving the complaint shall contact the applicant or complainant who claims to represent the applicant or complainant (in the declaration or complaint form) to carry out the procedures for formal examination to conclude whether the application is valid or invalid, accepted or not accepted, including conclusions on the legality of the representative status.
4. All transactions of any authorized party within the authorization scope at any time are considered transactions on behalf of the applicant or complainant, giving rise to rights and obligations of the applicant or complainant. In case of change of the authorized representative or re-authorization, the new authorized representative or re-authorized person shall continue the representation and all issues arising from the previous authorization with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving the complaint.
5. If the authorization document has a scope of authorization including multiple independent procedures and the original copy of the authorization document has been submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the administrative organization assigned by the Minister of Science and Technology to resolve the complaint shall, when carrying out subsequent procedures, submit copies of the authorization document with precise instructions on the number of the application containing the original copy of such authorization document in the declaration or documents related to subsequent procedures.

6. In cases where the authorization document clearly states that the authorization terminates upon either party’s written declaration of termination or upon receipt of another authorization document, such authorization document shall be considered having provisions on the term as prescribed at Point c, Clause 2, Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. In cases where authorization is granted to an organization or individual not permitted to act as a representative or authorization is granted to multiple organizations or individuals, including organizations or individuals not permitted to act as representatives, the application shall be considered invalid.
8. The authorization regulations in this Article shall also be applied accordingly to the implementation of administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP and the lodging of complaints regarding the results of resolving such administrative procedures.
Article 9. Responsibilities of applicants, complainants, and representatives
1. Applicants, complainants, and representatives of applicants and complainants shall ensure the honesty of information and documents provided to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and the administrative organization assigned by the Minister of Science and Technology to resolve complaints in accordance with the following regulations:
a) All transaction documents must be certified by the applicant, complainant, or representative of the applicant or complainant with their signatures and seals of the organization (if any). Where the law stipulates that documents must be notarized or authenticated, such requirements shall be complied with.
b) Any Vietnamese translation of documents made in languages other than Vietnamese must be accompanied by a declaration from the applicant or complainant or their representative that it is an exact translation from the original document, unless the Vietnamese translation has been notarized to confirm its accuracy;
c) If the representative of the applicant or complainant is an industrial property representation service provider, the representative must possess a certificate of qualification for industrial property representation services.
2. The applicant or complainant shall be responsible for all consequences and obligations arising from the transactions conducted by the representative of the applicant with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned by the Minister of Science and Technology to resolve complaints as prescribed by law.
3. The representative of the applicant or complainant shall be held accountable before the applicant or complainant as prescribed by law.
Article 10. Industrial property fees and charges
1. Applicants shall carry out procedures related to industrial property rights and other relevant procedures as prescribed in this Circular, and industrial property service users shall pay fees and charges in accordance with regulations of the Minister of Finance and other service charges as prescribed.
2. Fees and charges shall be collected as follows:
a) Applicants shall pay fees and charges to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as prescribed;
b) When collecting fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue receipts as documents for payment of fees and charges, which clearly state the collected fees and charges, and keep them in the application dossier to serve the appraisal of the application;
c) In case fees and charges are paid by post or directly transferred to the account of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the collection of fees and charges shall be determined through documents proving the payment of fees and charges in the application.
Chapter II
                               PROCESSING OF INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Section 1. INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 11. Invention registration applications
1. An invention registration application includes the following documents:
a) The invention registration declaration, made according to Form No. 01 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Description of the invention;
c) Summary of the invention;
d) Authorization document (if the application is filed by a representative);
dd) Documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits such rights from another person;
e) Documents proving priority rights if priority rights are claimed, including:

e1) Copies of the first application or applications submitted by the first receiving agency;

e2) Document on assignment of priority rights if such rights are inherited from another person;
g) Presentation documents on the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource for inventions directly created based on the genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource;
h) Other documents supporting the application (if any).
2. An application for registration of a confidential invention shall include the following documents:
a) Documents specified in clause 1 of this Article bearing a confidentiality seal as prescribed by law on protection of state secrets;
b) Written certification that the subject matter of the application is a state secret as prescribed by law on protection of state secrets.
Article 12. Requirements for documents in an invention registration application
1. Documents in an invention registration application must meet corresponding specific requirements as prescribed in clauses 2 to 12 of this Article and general requirements regarding format as follows:
a) Documents must be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm), with four sides provided with margins, each margin being 20 mm wide, using the Times New Roman font, with font size not smaller than 13, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b) For documents consisting of multiple pages, each page must bear the page number in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with an ink type that does not fade, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. Documents must bear signatures and seals; if the documents consist of at least 2 pages, each page must bear a signature or a seal affixed by the applicant or the issuing authority (if any), except for digitally signed electronic documents. Before receiving the application, if any insignificant spelling errors are detected, the applicant may correct them and must affix signatures (and seals, if any) at the correction positions; the signatures and seals must not obscure the contents of the documents.
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically under the electronic filing system must be in the “.pdf” format; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; the applicant may use a digital signature to directly sign on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as color photos in the case of submission via the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not local languages, rare words, or self-created terms). Symbols, measurement units, electronic fonts, and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Points d, dd, e, and h Clause 1 Article 11 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) Documents of the application must ensure that all mandatory information is included and that the information is consistent.
2. Documents in the application must indicate that the subject matter of protection is a product or process in accordance with Clause 12 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The invention registration application must ensure consistency as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. A group of inventions with a close technical relationship aimed at implementing a single common creative intention as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property if falling into the following cases:
a) One object is used to create (manufacture, manufacture, or prepare) the other object;
b) One object is used to perform the other object;
c) One subject used for use of the other subject;
d) Subjects of the same type and with the same function to ensure the same result.
4. The invention registration declaration must be filled in with all information as required at appropriate places in the Declaration, in which the applicant shall specify the classification index of the technical solution to be protected according to the International Classification Table of Inventions (according to the latest Paris Convention on International Classification of Inventions) published in the Industrial Property Gazette (with full classification, including parts, classes, sub-classes and groups (main groups or sub-groups)). If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify and the applicant shall pay a classification fee as prescribed.
5. The summary of an invention is used to briefly describe (not exceeding 150 words) the invention to be protected. The summary must disclose the main contents of the nature of the technical solution and is only provided for information. The summary may contain drawings and characteristic formulas. All drawings and characteristic formulas (if any) are presented in half a page of A4 and must be clear. The summary must be presented as a separate document.
6. The description of an invention includes the description and protection request (protection scope) of the invention and must comply with Clauses 7, 8, 9 and 10 of this Article. The description of an invention may include drawings (if necessary) to illustrate the invention.
7. The description section of an invention description must fully and clearly disclose the nature of the technical solution of the invention to the extent that it is based on which persons with average knowledge in the field of technology can implement such invention; clarify the novelty, inventive step, and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requires the issuance of an invention patent); clarify the novelty and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requires the issuance of a utility solution patent). If the application contains drawings, the description must be consistent with these drawings.
The description must include the following contents presented in the following order:
a) Name of the invention: briefly present one or more subject matters of the protection request (hereinafter referred to as "subject matters"); the name of the invention must be concise, accurate, and not promotional or advertising;
b) Field of use of the invention: the field in which the subject matters are used or related. If the invention is used or related to multiple fields, all of these fields must be indicated. The fields mentioned above must be consistent with the results of the invention classification;
c) Technical status of the invention: the technical status in the field of use of the invention at the time of filing the application (similar subject matters already known (if any)). If there is no information on the technical status of the invention, this must be clearly stated;
d) Technical nature of the invention: the nature of the subject matter to be protected, clearly stating the following contents: technical issues to be solved (the purpose of the invention), specifying the purpose that the invention is intended to achieve or the task (issue) that the invention is intended to solve (for example, to overcome disadvantages and limitations of the technical solutions already indicated in the technical state of the invention). The purpose or task of the invention must be presented in an objective and specific manner, not for expansion or advertisement; technical signs (characteristics) that constitute the object requesting protection, namely technical signs (characteristics) that form a solution to achieve the purpose of the invention (called fundamental technical signs) and must indicate new technical signs (characteristics) compared to similar known technical solutions; benefits (effects) that may be achieved compared to the technical state (if any), this content can be described as a separate part, as prescribed at Point h of this Clause;
dd) Brief description of accompanying drawings (if any);
e) Detailed description of methods of implementing the invention: a detailed description of one or more methods of implementing the invention such that persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field can implement the invention. Persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field are those who have common practical skills and know general and popular knowledge in the corresponding technical field;
g) Example of implementation of the invention (if any): indicating one or more specific methods of implementing the invention. If the invention is characterized by quantitative signs, it is necessary to indicate the specific values of such signs; if the invention cannot be characterized by quantitative signs, it is necessary to indicate the definite state of such signs. In addition, there must be specific results related to the functions and purposes that the corresponding object allows to achieve;
h) Benefits (efficiency) that may be achieved (if any and not stated in the technical nature section of the invention): may be expressed as increased productivity, quality, accuracy or efficiency; saved energy consumption and materials; simplified or created convenience when processing, operating, managing or using; overcome environmental pollution, etc. If the benefits (efficiency) that may be achieved are derived from statistical results from experimental data, the applicant must provide necessary conditions and methods for such experiments;
i) Illustrations (if any) must be numbered sequentially and grouped together on separate pages dedicated to illustrations and placed at the end of the description. If there are reference signs on the illustrations to serve the description, these reference signs must be used consistently in all documents of the application.
8. The protection request must be presented briefly, clearly and in conformity with the description and illustrations on separate pages, including points of the protection request, which must clarify new signs of the subject matter of the protection request and comply with the following regulations:
a) The protection request must be fully illustrated by the description, including basic technical signs needed and sufficient to identify the subject matter, achieve the proposed purpose and distinguish the subject matter of the protection request from known subjects;
b) Technical signs in each point of the protection request must be clear, accurate and accepted in the corresponding technical field; terms used in the protection request must be clear and consistent with terms used in the description;
c) The protection request must not refer to the description and illustrations, except for cases where it refers to parts that cannot be accurately described in words, such as the nucleotide sequence and amino acid sequence, interference diagrams, state diagrams, etc.;
d) If the application contains drawings illustrating the subject matter of the protection request, the signs mentioned in the protection request may be accompanied by reference numbers, which must be enclosed in parentheses. These reference numbers shall not be considered as limiting the scope of protection;
dd) The protection request point should (but is not required) be divided into two parts: "the restricted part" and "the distinctive part", where: "the restricted part" includes the name of the subject matter and its signs identical to those of the nearest known subject matter, and is connected to "the distinctive part" by the phrase "distinctive in..." or "characteristic in..." or similar words; "the distinctive part" includes the distinctive signs of the subject matter compared to the nearest known subject matter, and these signs combined with the signs of the "restricted part" constitute the subject matter of the protection request;
e) A protection request may include one or more points. Where a multi-point protection request is used, it may express a subject matter to be protected, with the first point (referred to as the independent point) and subsequent points (referred to as dependent points) used to specify the independent point (referred to as dependent point); or express a group of protected subject matters, with several independent points, each independent point expressing a protected subject matter within that group, and each independent point may have dependent points. Each protection request point shall only address one protected subject matter and must be expressed in a single sentence;
g) Points of the protection request must be numbered consecutively in Arabic numerals and end with a period;
h) Multi-point protection requests used to express a group of subject matters must satisfy the following requirements: independent points, expressing distinct subjects, not referencing other protection request points, except where such references allow for avoiding complete repetition of the content of other points; dependent points must be expressed after the independent points from which they depend.
9. For an invention registration application related to pharmaceuticals, in addition to the general requirements for the invention description specified in Clause 7 of this Article, the description must also state the results of clinical tests and/or pharmacological effects of the pharmaceutical, including at least the following information:
a) Substances/mixes used;
b) The method(s) used in the test;
c) Information about test results;
d) The correlation between the pharmacological effects obtained from the test and the actual application of pharmaceutical products in diagnosis and treatment.
10. Regarding an invention registration application related to biological technology, apart from meeting the general requirements for the invention description specified in Clause 7 of this Article, it must also meet the following requirements:
a) Regarding an invention registration application concerning a gene sequence or a part of a gene sequence, the description must include a list of gene sequences according to the standards of the World Intellectual Property Organization for presenting lists of nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences in the invention registration application. The list of sequences shall be presented separately at the end of the description;
b) Include electronic information carriers (e.g., floppy disks, optical discs, etc.) readable by common electronic devices, which contain the nucleotide sequence and amino acid sequence identical to the list of sequences stated in the description in case of requirement of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) Specifically regarding inventions concerning or related to biological materials, if they cannot be described or fully described to the extent that persons with average knowledge of biological technology can implement them, the invention registration application must meet the following conditions: c1) Biological material samples have been deposited at the biological material storage agency no later than the date of filing the application for registration of such biological materials; c2) The description clearly states necessary information about the properties of biological materials that the applicant may obtain; c3) The declaration clearly states the biological material storage agency, the storage number of the biological material sample deposited by the storage agency, and documents confirming these information are submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date, or no later than the date of filing the earliest request for disclosure (if any), whichever is earlier, except for cases where the deposit is carried out in accordance with the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Treaty;
d) In case the applicant is not the biological material depositor, the name and address of the depositor and documents certifying the lawful use of biological materials must be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date or no later than the submission date of the earliest request (if any), whichever comes first, except for cases where the deposit is carried out in accordance with the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Agreement.
11. For inventions related to biological materials specified at Point c, Clause 10 of this Article, the deposit of biological material samples at the competent authority as prescribed at Point c1, Clause 10 of this Article must meet the following requirements:
a) The biological material depository is an agency in Vietnam or abroad on the list of international depository agencies under the Budapest Treaty or recognized by a competent authority in Vietnam regarding the function of storing biological materials;
b) For biological material samples deposited at an overseas depository, if it is deemed necessary to clarify the nature of the subject matter requested for protection or to satisfy a third party's request for access to such subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may proceed as follows:

b1) Request the applicant to deposit additional biological material samples at a Vietnamese competent depository in case the biological material samples are not deposited at an international depository under the Budapest Treaty;

b2) Request the biological material depository to provide samples in case the biological material samples are deposited at an international depository under the Budapest Treaty.
12. For invention registration applications directly created based on genetic resources or traditional knowledge about genetic resources, where the applicant does not submit explanatory documents on the origin of the genetic resources or traditional knowledge about genetic resources that the author of the invention or the applicant has accessed according to Point g, Clause 1, Article 11 of this Circular due to the inability to determine the origin of the genetic resources or traditional knowledge about genetic resources, such fact must be clearly stated and the applicant shall take responsibility for the truthfulness of such statement.
Article 13. Receipt of invention registration applications
1. An invention registration application shall be received when it fully satisfies the following conditions:
a) It contains all required documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Description, specifying the protection request;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. A confidential invention registration application shall be received when it fully satisfies the following conditions:
a) It contains all required documents as prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article and documents prescribed in Point b Clause 2 Article 11 of this Circular;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
3. In case an application fully satisfies the conditions prescribed in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and verify the submission date and issue a number to such application and return it to the applicant with an application receipt stating information about the submission date, application number, and results of the verification of the list of documents, clearly indicating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For electronic applications, upon receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue an application receipt to the applicant, which includes information on the submission date, application number, and examination results of the list of documents in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application submission system.
4. In cases where the application does not meet the conditions specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the application will be rejected for receipt (if the application is submitted by post, or if it is an electronic application submitted through the electronic application submission system, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam will send a notification of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reasons for rejection). For applications submitted by post that are rejected for receipt, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return the application documents to the applicant, except for the original submitted for comparison.
5. Regarding documents proving priority rights, the applicant may submit additional documents within 3 months from the date of submission as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
6. For secret invention registration applications, the receipt of the application according to Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article must comply with the provisions of law on protection of state secrets.
Section 2. APPLICATION FORMS OF INVENTION REGISTRATION
Article 14. Format appraisal of invention registration applications
1. Format appraisal of an invention registration application according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the examination of compliance with regulations on format of the application, thereby concluding whether the application is considered valid.
2. An invention registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:

a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Article 86 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed against regulations in Articles 89 and 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not eligible for protection as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 and Article 59 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) There are grounds to confirm that the author of the invention does not satisfy the requirements prescribed in Clause 1 Article 122 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed in Article 10 of this Circular (including cases where the fees for filing the application, publication of the application, and content appraisal fees have not been fully paid, except for the information search fee for appraisal purposes and the content appraisal fee if no request for content appraisal is made in the application);
e) The application does not meet the formal requirements prescribed in Article 12 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding presentation; failing to classify or incorrectly classifying the invention without paying classification fees; lacking translations of documents proving priority rights (if required), or translations of documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not properly certified; the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author, applicant, representative, or signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; for a split application, the explanatory text about the subject matter of protection in the split application that is included in the original application is missing; documents in the secret invention registration application have not yet been sealed with confidentiality stamps as prescribed, etc.;
g) There is no valid written authorization (if the application is filed through a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 02 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming the lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (documents proving inheritance rights according to civil laws, documents proving the transfer of application submission rights; assignment contracts or labor contracts, etc.); documents showing pharmaceutical test results on humans, animals or plants mentioned in the description (when the subject matters of protection requests are pharmaceuticals for humans, animals or plants); etc.
If the prescribed time limit mentioned above expires but the applicant fails to submit additional documents satisfying requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application.
4. Notification of the results of the formal examination shall be made as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notification of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the protection title, reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application (including priority claims) and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide opinions or correct deficiencies.
b) If the prescribed time limit specified at Point a of this Clause expires but the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to meet requirements or does not have any objection or has an objection but it is unconvincing, within 15 working days from the expiration of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the protection title and send it to the applicant.
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant has made satisfactory corrections to deficiencies and provided a legitimate explanation within the prescribed time limit, the application shall be considered valid and proceed to content disclosure and appraisal as per regulation (if the application requests content appraisal). In case the request for priority rights is rejected, the application shall still be considered valid and proceed to content disclosure as per regulation.
5. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of invention registration applications to be transferred for publication, specifying the application number, filing date and date of transfer for publication of each application.
6. The filing date of an invention application to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in accordance with Clause 3 Article 13 hereof.
In case an application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative working hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date specified in this clause shall be the next working day.
7. The priority date of an invention registration application shall be determined as follows:
a/ If an application does not request priority right or although it requests priority right, it is not approved by the National Office of Industrial Property, such application shall be regarded as having no priority date;
b) If the application requests priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the National Office of Intellectual Property;
c) The priority date determined according to the request for priority rights on the basis of the first application(s) submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
8. In case the applicant requests to enjoy priority right but at the time of filing the invention registration application there is no document proving the priority right, the examination of the application shall start from the time the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the supplementary documents or end within 03 months as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 13 of this Circular.
Article 15. Publication of invention registration applications
1. An invention registration application which is formally valid shall be published within the time limit prescribed in Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Contents of publication of an invention registration application in the Industrial Property Official Gazette include the following information: applicant, representative (if any); application number, filing date, priority date (if any); name and nationality of the inventor; information about the origin of the application (application conversion, application splitting, initial application number of split/conversion application, etc.); summary of the invention; drawings enclosed with the summary (if any); content appraisal request date (if any); early disclosure request date (if any); international classification of the invention; and other information (if any).
3. The description of the invention shall be published on the website of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 3. CONTENT ANALYSIS OF INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 16. Request for content analysis of invention registration applications
1. Applicants may request content analysis of invention registration applications according to regulations in Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following regulations:
a) Submit written request for substantive examination of the invention registration application using Form No. 2 in Appendix I of this Circular or the declaration of the invention registration application (if such request is made upon filing the application) or Form No. 13 in Appendix I of this Circular in case of fast-track substantive examination;
b) The time limit for submission of a request for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall comply with Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property; a fast-track request for substantive examination of an invention registration application must be submitted immediately upon filing the application as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) The applicant must pay the lookup fee and substantive examination fee as prescribed. In case the applicant fails to fully pay the fees and charges mentioned above, the request for substantive examination shall be considered invalid and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam will not conduct the substantive examination.
A request for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall be submitted after the date the application is published in the Industrial Property Gazette in the second month from the date of receipt of the request. A request for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall be submitted before the date of publication of the application.
2. A third party has the right to request substantive examination of an invention registration application in accordance with Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following regulations:
a) Submit a request for substantive examination of the invention registration application using Form No. 2 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) The time limit for submission of a request for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall comply with Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) The requester for substantive examination of an invention registration application must pay the lookup fee and substantive examination fee as prescribed;
d) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the request for content appraisal within 2 months from the date of receipt of such request;
dd) In case the request for content appraisal is invalid, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request for content appraisal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to correct errors. If the applicant fails to correct errors within the imposed time limit or fails to meet the requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the rejection of content appraisal.
e) In case the request for content appraisal is valid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct content appraisal in accordance with Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Circular and notify the results of content appraisal to the applicant.
Article 17. Content appraisal of invention registration applications
1. Content appraisal of an invention registration application as prescribed in Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the protection eligibility of the technical solution stated in the application according to the protection conditions and determination of the corresponding protection scope according to the provisions of this Article.
2. Assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles for an invention registration application includes:
a) Assessment of the conformity of the subject matter stated in the invention registration application and the type of protection title according to the provisions of Article 19 of this Circular;
b) Assessment of the subject matter stated in the invention registration application according to each protection condition as prescribed in Articles 20, 21 and 22 of this Circular. The assessment shall be carried out sequentially according to each point stated in the protection request;
c) Assess the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 23 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the ability to grant protection titles according to Point b Clause 2 of this Article shall be considered complete when the subject matter of the invention stated in the application has been assessed with all protection conditions and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter does not meet or meets the protection conditions, specifically:
a) A reason is found to conclude that the subject matter does not meet one or more or all of the protection conditions; or b) No reason is found to conclude that the subject matter does not meet at least one protection condition.
4. Based on the results of the assessment of the ability to grant protection titles as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding scope of protection.
5. Before issuing a notice of content appraisal results for the intended issuance of an invention/utility solution title according to Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 25 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall check the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 23 of this Circular.
6. The notice of content appraisal results for the intended issuance of an invention/utility solution title as prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 25 of this Circular shall be issued for the following invention registration applications:
a) Applications not falling under the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Applications with the earliest submission date or priority date among invention registration applications falling under the cases specified in Clause 1 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Application as agreed in Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. An invention registration application not falling into the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article shall be processed as follows:
a) The grant of an invention/utility solution patent shall be rejected if it fails to satisfy the first-to-file rule, provided that the application with the earliest filing date or priority date is granted an invention/utility solution patent; or b) The grant of an invention/utility solution patent shall be considered as the case specified in clause 6 of this article if all other applications for registration of inventions with earlier filing dates or priority dates are rejected from being granted invention/utility solution patents or withdrawn or deemed to have been withdrawn.
8. During the process of content appraisal, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations for the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify the deficiencies.
9. During the process of content appraisal of an application, depending on the complexity of assessing the conditions for protection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may consult independent expert consultants or the Council for Patent Appraisal Consultation.
The Council for Patent Appraisal Consultancy shall be established and operate on the basis of the principles prescribed in the decision of the Director of the National Office of Intellectual Property.
Independent consultants shall be selected from the List of industrial property consultancy experts compiled by the National Office of Intellectual Property and other sources (in case no suitable expert is available in such list). The applicant may request to attend the meeting of the Appraisal Council or with independent consultants to clarify information stated in the application for the appraisal by the Council or the consultant.
Article 18. Minimum information sources
1. In order to assess the novelty and creativity of technical solutions stated in an application, information must be searched from the following compulsory sources (but not limited to these sources):
a) All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam having the same classification index with the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal, up to the third sub-classification index and with priority dates or submission dates earlier than those of the application currently undergoing appraisal, except for applications that have been or will not be published (when assessing novelty) or except for unpublished applications up to the priority date or submission date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (when assessing creativity);
b) Invention registration applications or invention protection titles published by other organizations or countries within 25 years until the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights) stored in the invention database at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and other information sources prescribed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, with the search scope specified in point a of this clause;
c) Confidential invention protection titles issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam up to the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal.
Where necessary and possible, the search may be extended to the national science and technology database and other accessible information sources, including Internet sources.
2. The purpose of information search is to find technical solutions with similar or identical nature to the technical solutions stated in the application. In this Clause:
a) Two technical solutions are considered identical when they have all basic signs (characteristics) being identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
b) Two technical solutions are considered similar when they have most of their basic signs (characteristics) being identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
c) “Contrasting technical solution” means a technical solution identical or most similar to the one stated in the application;
d) “Contrasting documents” means documents describing the contrasting technical solutions or evidence proving that the contrasting technical solutions have been publicly disclosed.
3. The search results must be presented in the search report, which must clearly state the field searched, the scope of search, and the search results within that scope (statistics on found contrasting technical solutions, specifying identical signs, names of contrasting documents, page numbers, line numbers, origin of documents, and date of publication of corresponding documents), and must name the person making the report (the searcher).
Article 19. Assessment of the conformity of subject matters stated in invention registration applications
1. Subject matters stated in invention registration applications shall be considered unconformable with the type of invention protection title requested by the applicant for issuance (invention patent or utility solution patent) if such subject matters are not technical solutions, specifically products or processes.
2. Technical solutions (subject matters protected as inventions) as stipulated in Clause 1 of this Article are a necessary and sufficient combination of information about technical methods and/or technical means (application of natural laws) aimed at solving a specific task (problem). Technical solutions may take one of the following forms:
a) Physical products, such as tools, machinery, equipment, components, electrical circuits, etc., expressed by a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by technical signs (features) regarding structure, which has functions (utilities) as a means to meet certain needs of humans; or products in the form of substances (including simple substances, compounds and mixtures of substances), such as materials, materials, foods, pharmaceuticals, etc., expressed by a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by technical signs (features) regarding the presence, proportion and state of elements, which have functions (utilities) as a means to meet certain needs of humans; or biological materials, such as genes, genetically modified plants/animals, etc., expressed by a collection of information about a product containing genetic information altered under human influence, capable of self-reproduction;
b) Processes (technological processes; methods of diagnosis, prediction, inspection, processing, etc.) expressed by a collection of information identifying the method of conducting a specific process or task, characterized by signs (features) regarding the order, conditions, participating components, measures and means for performing operations to achieve a certain purpose.
3. Subjects mentioned in the application shall not be considered as technical solutions in the following cases:
a) Subjects mentioned in the application are just ideas or intentions, just stating (posing) issues but not being ways to solve issues, failing to answer questions “by what means” or “by what means";
b) Issues (tasks) set to be solved are not technical issues and cannot be solved by technical means;
c) Natural products, not created products of humans.
Article 20. Assessment of novelty

1. In order to assess the novelty of technical solutions stated in the application, it is necessary to compare fundamental signs (characteristics) of such technical solutions with signs of control technical solutions found during the information lookup according to the provisions of Article 18 of this Circular, in which:
a) Fundamental technical signs of a technical solution may be characteristics of the physical object’s structure (details, groups of details, connections, etc.) or composition of substances (components (presence, proportion), states of elements, etc.) together with other fundamental technical signs to form a necessary and sufficient set for determining the nature (content) of the subject matter.
The above-mentioned fundamental technical signs may be presented in the form of technical functions of an element in the structure or composition of the product (called functional signs), provided that such presentation is sufficient for a person with average knowledge in the field to easily understand the technical means or method to perform such function under normal conditions without requiring creativity. The functions and utility of the subject matter requested for protection are not fundamental technical signs but may be the purpose or achieved results of such subject matter;
b) Fundamental technical signs of the technical solutions stated in the application, in protection titles shall be presented in the invention protection request, description or drawings;
c) Fundamental technical signs of the technical solutions stated in other documents shall be presented and detected according to the description documents or actual forms of such technical solutions.
2. In order not to be considered as losing novelty for inventions already published in the exceptions prescribed in clauses 3 and 4 Article 60 of the Law on Intellectual Property, applicants must submit documents related to the publication to prove eligibility for benefiting from exceptions. The above-mentioned documents must be submitted together with the application or additionally according to regulations on amendments to applications.
3. Corresponding to one point of the protection request, the technical solution stated in the application shall be considered new compared to the world's technical state if:
a) No control technical solution is found during the information search; or b) A control technical solution is found but the technical solution stated in the application has at least one fundamental technical sign not present in the control technical solution (and such sign is called a different fundamental sign).
Article 21. Assessment of creativity level
1. In order to assess the level of creativity of the technical solution stated in the application, it is required to search for information in compulsory sources (but not limited to such search as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 18 of this Circular).
2. The assessment of the level of creativity of the technical solution stated in the application shall be carried out by evaluating the fundamental distinctive signs mentioned in the protection request in order to make the following conclusion:
a) Whether or not the fundamental distinctive signs have been considered to be disclosed in the compulsory information sources; and b) Whether or not the collection of fundamental distinctive technical signs has been considered obvious to persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field.
For a protection request point, the technical solution shall be considered creative if the inclusion of the fundamental distinctive technical signs in the collection of fundamental technical signs of the technical solution is the result of creative activities and not the obvious result of common knowledge in the corresponding technical field.
3. In the following cases (but not limited to these cases), for a protection request point, the technical solution shall be considered not creative:
a) The collection of fundamental distinctive technical signs is obviously obvious (any person with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field knows that in order to perform the defined function or achieve the defined purpose, it is necessary to use such a collection of signs, and conversely, when using such a collection of signs, it is necessary to achieve the corresponding purpose or perform the corresponding function);
b) The collection of fundamental distinctive technical signs has been uniformly or similarly disclosed in one or several known technical solutions in the compulsory information sources. In which case, two signs are considered uniform if they have the same nature; two signs are considered similar if they have similar natures, the same purpose and the same way of achieving the basic purpose;
c) Technical solutions are simple combination of known technical solutions with functions, purposes and effects also being simple combination of functions, purposes and effects of each known technical solution.
Article 22. Assessment of industrial applicability

1. Technical solutions stated in applications shall be considered “feasible” according to regulations in Article 62 of the Law on Intellectual Property if:
a) Information about nature of solutions together with instructions about necessary technical conditions are presented clearly and sufficiently to the extent that persons with average knowledge about corresponding technical fields can create, manufacture or use, exploit or implement such solutions;
b) The creation, manufacture, use, exploitation or implementation of the above-said solutions is repeated with identical results and similar to results stated in invention descriptions.
2. Technical solutions shall be considered incapable of industrial application in the following cases:
a) Nature of subject matters or instructions for subject matter implementation are contrary to fundamental principles of science (for example, not complying with the principle of energy conservation, etc.);
b) Subject matters include elements and components that have no technical connection or cannot be connected (joined, bound, dependent, etc.) to each other;
c) The subject contains internal contradictions;
d) The subject matters of the instructions may be given only a limited number of times (cannot be repeated);
dd) To be able to implement the solution, the person must have special skills which cannot be transferred or only given to others;
e) The results obtained from the inconsistent implementation of the project are not consistent;
g) The results obtained are different from those stated in the description;
h) Having no or lacking the most important instructions for solution;
i) Other cases with legitimate reasons.
Article 23. Evaluation of the first-to-file principle
Regarding an invention registration application which has been concluded to meet the protection conditions, before issuing a notice of content appraisal results and intending to grant an invention patent or utility solution patent as prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 25 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file rule prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:
1. In order to assess the first-to-file rule, at least the following compulsory sources must be consulted (but not limited to these sources): All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (by the time of examination) with the same classification index with the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal - up to the third class index and with an earlier submission date or priority date than the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is entitled to priority rights) that has not yet been published or with a later publication date than the submission date or priority date (if the application is entitled to priority rights) of the application currently undergoing appraisal.
2. The consultation is to find cases where there are multiple applications (including the application currently undergoing appraisal) registering inventions that are identical or similar to each other and determine the application with the earliest submission date or priority date.
3. If there are multiple applications falling under the case specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the invention patent or utility solution patent may only be granted to the invention in the valid application with the earliest submission date or priority date among the applications meeting the conditions for the grant of a protection title.
4. Among the applications falling under the case specified in Clause 2 of this Article, if there are multiple applications with the same earliest submission date or priority date, the invention patent or utility solution patent may only be granted to a single application among them according to the agreement of all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all such applications shall be rejected from the grant of a protection title.
Article 24. Use of information lookup results and content appraisal results of foreign invention registration applications
1. During the process of content appraisal of an invention registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may refer to the information lookup results and content appraisal results of the corresponding application filed overseas.
2. The lookup results and content appraisal results mentioned in Clause 1 of this Article include one of the following documents:
a) Lookup report, appraisal report or notice of appraisal results;
b) Patent application or protection title.

3. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of content appraisal results, the applicant may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to use the content appraisal results of an invention registration application filed overseas to assess the protection capacity if the following conditions are satisfied:
a) The content appraisal results specified in the documents mentioned in Clause 2 of this Article must be issued by national or regional intellectual property offices selected as preliminary lookup and appraisal authorities under the PCT Agreement;
b) At least one protection request point assessed as meeting the protection conditions is included in the content appraisal results of the invention registration application filed overseas mentioned above.
c) Points of protection request in the application filed in Vietnam initially or after amendment must be identical to points of protection requested which are assessed as satisfying the protection conditions in the content appraisal results of the invention registration application filed overseas mentioned above;
d) The applicant shall submit the following documents to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam: request for use of content appraisal results of foreign country using Form No. 12 in Appendix I of this Circular; copies of appraisal results; Vietnamese translations of appraisal results, points of protection requested which are assessed by the foreign invention agency as satisfying the protection conditions and Vietnamese translations, documents cited in the application processing results of the foreign invention agency upon request; description of amendments, detailed presentation of amended and supplemented contents compared with the initial description submitted (if any amendments are made); and fees as prescribed.
4. In case the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article are satisfied, the request for use of content appraisal results of foreign country is accepted and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of content appraisal results within 8 months from the date of receipt of the request from the applicant. All procedures for amendment and supplementation of the invention registration application within the above-mentioned 8-month period if it makes one of the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article no longer satisfied, the invention registration application shall be appraised according to normal procedures.
5. In case one of the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article is not satisfied, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for use of content appraisal results of foreign country and the invention registration application shall be appraised according to normal procedures.
Article 25. Issuance and refusal to issue invention/utility solution patents
1. An invention registration application shall be refused to issue an invention patent or utility solution patent in cases specified at Points a, b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clauses 1a and 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. An invention registration application shall be granted an invention patent or utility solution patent when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For invention registration applications falling into the cases specified in clause 1 or clause 2 of this article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at points b, c, d and dd of clause 1, clauses 1a and 1b of Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of the protection title and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of the protection title, the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title, the validity maintenance fee and the fee for use of the first-year protection title;
b) If the subject matter stated in the invention registration application does not satisfy the protection conditions or the application falls into the cases specified at points b, c, d and dd of clause 1, clauses 1a and 1b of Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions but the application has deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the protection title, all reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application, and may provide guidance on amendments to the scope (quantity) of protection and set a time limit of 03 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide opinions opposing or correcting deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or does not provide opinions opposing within the time limit specified at point b of this clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the protection title;
d) In case the applicant submits corrections or objections within the time limit specified in Point b of this Clause, within 4 months from the date of receipt of the correction or objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the examination as follows:

d1) If the correction is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;

d2) If the correction is satisfactory or the objection is justifiable given the content examination results and/or the satisfactory amendment to the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content examination results, stating the intended grant of the protection title and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of the protection title, the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title, the validity maintenance fee and the fee for use of the first-year protection title;
dd) In case the applicant fails to fully pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of the protection title, or the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title within the time limit specified in Points a and d2 of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;
e) In case the applicant fully and timely pays all fees and charges within the time limit specified in Points a and d2 of this Clause, within 1 month from the date of full payment of fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant the protection title. If the applicant has fully paid the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of the protection title, or the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title but fails to pay the validity maintenance fee and the fee for use of the first-year protection title within the prescribed time limit, such protection title shall still be granted but its validity shall be immediately terminated after the grant.
4. The decision on issuance of an invention patent or utility solution patent shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance. The published contents include the information specified in the corresponding decision (including the decision number and issuance date) and the contents specified in Clause 2 Article 15 of this Circular.
The full description of the invention patent or utility solution patent shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5. An invention patent or utility solution patent made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued in electronic form and paper form (if the applicant requests a paper copy). In case industrial property rights to an invention are under joint ownership, the invention patent or utility solution patent shall only be issued to the first person in the list of co-applicants.
A copy of an invention patent made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests such copy (specified in the invention registration declaration) for other co-owners.
Section 4. PROCESSING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 26. Processing of requests for rapid content appraisal of invention registration applications
1. Requests for rapid content appraisal of invention registration applications shall be received after the applicant has fully paid fees and charges as prescribed and has submitted the following documents:
a) Request form for rapid content appraisal, made according to Form No. 13 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the invention has been commercially exploited according to Point b Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP within 12 months prior to the filing date;
2. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall receive and process requests for expedited substantive examination of all documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article as follows:
a) If the request for expedited substantive examination fails to satisfy the requirements prescribed in Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall notify the applicant of the results of the examination, clearly stating the reasons for the intended refusal or deficiencies, and impose a time limit of 10 working days from the notification date for the applicant to provide feedback or correct errors;
b) Upon expiration of the time limit prescribed in Point a of this Clause, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate corrections, or does not provide any objection or provides an unjustifiable objection, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall notify the applicant of the refusal to accept the request for expedited substantive examination and continue processing the corresponding invention registration application in accordance with regulations;
c) In cases where the request for expedited substantive examination satisfies the requirements prescribed in Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the request shall be published together with the invention registration application and shall undergo substantive examination in accordance with this Circular within the time limit prescribed in Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, except where the invention registration application is determined to be invalid under Article 14 of this Circular.
Article 27. Handling of objections to inventions registration applications
1. Within 6 months from the date of publication of an invention registration application or within 3 months from the date of publication of an invention registration application in case the invention registration application is subject to rapid content appraisal according to Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, any third party has the right to oppose the grant of a protection title for such invention registration application and must pay fees and charges.
The objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese enclosed with documents or sources of information to prove it. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. In cases where the objection to the invention registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process the objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response;
b) After receiving the applicant's feedback within the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of the feedback and impose a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify the issues subject to the objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) Based on the information, evidence, arguments provided by the parties, the dialogue results (if any) as prescribed in Points a, b, and c of this Clause and the documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the objector of the results of processing the objection together with the results of content appraisal of the corresponding application, except for the case specified at Point dd of this Clause.
dd) In case an invention registration application has been withdrawn or is considered to have been withdrawn according to Article 116 and Clause 3 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the opposition holder of the termination of processing of the objection within 01 month from the date of notification of acceptance of withdrawal of the application or from the date of expiry of the time limit for requesting content appraisal of the invention registration application specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. In case the objection of the opposition holder relates to the right to register inventions, the objection shall be processed as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the opposition holder to file a lawsuit with a competent Court according to regulations of law on civil procedures and impose a 02-month time limit from the date of notification for the opposition holder to send a copy of the notice of acceptance of the lawsuit by the Court;
b) Upon expiry of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if the opposition holder fails to send a copy of the notice of acceptance of the lawsuit, the objection shall be considered withdrawn and the invention registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection.
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the notice of acceptance of the lawsuit by the Court within the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the invention registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the Court, except for the case specified at Point dd Clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment/decision of the Court.
4. The time limit for the applicant to respond to the objection and the time limit for the opposition holder to respond to the applicant's opinion according to Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal of the application.
Article 28. Amendment to invention registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention/good solution patent, applicants may take initiative or based on the appraisal results notification to amend and supplement information and documents in their invention registration applications.
2. The request for amendment to an invention registration application shall satisfy the following regulations:
a) In case of amendment to the application based on the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam's appraisal results notification, the request for amendment shall be made in writing in response to such notification, clearly stating the content of the amendment. In case the applicant takes initiative in amending the application, the request for amendment shall be made using Form No. 10 in Appendix I of this Circular, clearly stating the content of the amendment;
b) In case of amendment related to the description or summary, the applicant must submit part or all of the amended description or summary together with a detailed presentation of the amended content. The amendment to the description and summary of the invention registration application must not expand the scope (volume) already disclosed in the initial description and must not change the nature of the subject matter of the invention stated in the application;
c) In case of change of industrial property representative, the applicant must submit a document declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representative and change of the new industrial property representative as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular;
d) In case of amendment to name, address or country code of the applicant, the applicant must submit certification documents (original or certified true copy) or legal documents (certified true copy) proving the change (decision on name or address change; certificate of enterprise registration which records the change of name and address, etc.), except for cases where the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may proactively extract and use information already available in databases publicly disclosed by database owners. In case information cannot be extracted or is not fully and accurately extracted, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to individuals or organizations to supplement application components and clearly state reasons. The time for requesting and supplementing applications shall not be included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
dd) In case of amendment to other information besides points b, c and d of this clause, documents proving the lawful amendment or commitment of the applicant are required;
e) In case of amendment to information under points c, d and dd of this clause, the applicant may request amendment to the same content related to multiple invention registration applications in one declaration of amendment request;
g) The applicant must pay an fee for appraisal of the request for amendment to each content with each invention registration application and a fee for disclosure of information about amendments to the application if the amended content must be disclosed according to point a of clause 4 of this Article, except for cases where the amendments are made to correct errors caused by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
3. In case the applicant proactively requests amendment to the contents mentioned in points b, c and d of clause 2 of this Article before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the protection title due to the invalidity of the application in terms of form, such request shall be processed during the process of appraising the application and the results of processing this request (acceptance or refusal to accept the request for amendment) shall be stated in the notice of appraisal results or the results of processing the invention registration application.
4. Requests for amendment not related to the description and abstract submitted after the application has been published shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment to an invention registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:

a1) Issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendment to the application;

a2) Publish the amended content on the Official Gazette of Industrial Property;
b) If the request for amendment to the application does not satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opposing opinions;
c) If the prescribed time limit expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, no opposing opinions, or provides inadequate opposing opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application.
5. Requests for amendments to descriptions or summaries submitted after the application has been published shall be processed as follows:
a) In case the applicant proactively amends the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice recording the request for amendment and publish the amended content on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
b) Requests for amendments shall be processed during the process of appraising the application, and the results of processing these requests (acceptance or refusal to accept the request for amendments) shall be stated in the notice of appraisal results or the results of processing the invention registration application;
c) In case the request for amendment to the application relates to the nature of the invention stated in the description submitted after the notice of intended grant of the patent for the invention/utility solution is issued, the invention registration application must be re-appraised with the re-appraisal period as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, and the applicant must pay fees as prescribed.
Article 29. Recording changes of applicants for invention registration
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention/good solution patent, the applicant may request the recognition of changes in the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, or according to the decision of a competent authority.
2. The request for recognition of changes in the applicant must meet the following requirements:
a) The request for recognition of changes in the applicant shall be made using Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of recognition of changes in the applicant due to transfer of the application, the applicant must submit documents showing the transfer of the invention registration application (original or certified copy), including the name and address of the transferring party and the transferee; the number of transferred applications or information sufficient to identify such applications;
c) In case of recognition of changes in the applicant due to inheritance, inheritance, or according to the decision of a competent authority, the applicant must submit documents proving the inheritance or inheritance of assets upon consolidation, merger, division, separation of legal entities, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entities under the same ownership, business format conversion, or according to the decision of the Court or other competent authorities, or inheritance due to the abandonment of the registration right by another applicant;
d) The applicant must pay the fee for appraisal of the request for recognition of changes in the applicant and the fee for disclosure of information on recognition of changes in the applicant;
dd) Requests for recognition of changes due to transfer of multiple applications from the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that the appraisal fee is paid according to the corresponding regulations for the number of applications requested for recognition of transfer.
3. Requests for recognition of changes in the applicant shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for change of applicant satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of change of applicant and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property (except where the amendment or supplementation of the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the protection title due to invalidation of the application in terms of form);
b) If the request for change of applicant does not satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize the change of applicant and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection, or provides unjustifiable objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to recognize the change of applicant.
Article 30. Splitting of invention registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to grant or refuses to grant an invention/good utility solution patent, the applicant may split one or more technical solutions in the submitted invention registration application (initial application) into one or more new invention registration applications (split applications). The splitting of an application must comply with the following regulations:
a) The request for splitting of an application must be expressed in writing, clearly indicating the number of the initial application and providing an explanation of the subject matter of the protection request in the split application and the changes in the split application compared to the initial application;
b) The applicant must submit a new invention registration declaration for the split technical solution and a description, summary, and authorization document (if the application is filed through a representative);
c) The applicant must pay the application fee and all fees and charges for procedures performed for the split application, excluding those already performed in the initial application, without having to repeat them in the split application and without having to pay the examination fee for priority right requests;
d) The subject matters of the protection request in the separate application must be those included in the initial application and must differ from the subject matters of the protection request in the initial application after separation;
dd) The separate application shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection beyond the content already disclosed in the description and shall not alter the nature of the subject matter stated in the initial application.
3. The separate application shall bear a new application number and shall have the filing date of the initial invention registration application and (the) priority dates of the initial invention registration application (if any).
4. The separate application shall be appraised for form and shall continue to be processed according to the unfinished procedures for the initial application. The separate application shall be published as prescribed.
5. The initial application (after separation) shall continue to be processed according to the procedures being carried out if there is no amendment to the initial application when submitting the separate application or shall be processed according to the procedures for amendment of applications as prescribed in Article 28 of this Circular if there is an amendment to the initial application when submitting the separate application.
Article 31. Conversion of invention registration applications
1. Before the National Office of Intellectual Property issues or refuses to issue an invention patent or utility solution patent, the applicant may convert the request for issuance of an invention patent into a request for issuance of a utility solution patent or vice versa for the whole or part of the invention registration application.
2. A request for conversion of an invention registration application shall satisfy the following provisions:
a) The request for conversion of an application (regarding the initial application) must be made in writing, clearly stating the request for conversion;
b) The applicant shall submit a Declaration which specifies the number of the initial application, the fees for submission of the application for conversion as prescribed, and the fees and charges, and a written authorization (if the application is submitted through a representative);
c) In case of partial conversion of an invention registration application, the applicant shall carry out procedures for application separation as prescribed in Article 30 of this Circular before requesting the conversion of the application.
3. After receiving a valid request for application conversion, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the conversion application according to corresponding regulations but shall not re-carry out the procedures already carried out for the application before the request for conversion is received.
The conversion application shall have a new application number and the filing date shall be the same as that of the initial application or the priority dates of the initial application.
4. In case of a request for conversion from an application for issuance of a utility solution patent to an application for issuance of an invention patent filed after the notice of content appraisal results indicating the intended issuance of a protection title is issued, the application shall be re-appraised and the applicant shall pay fees and charges as prescribed.
5. The initial application (after being converted) shall be considered as withdrawn at the time of submitting the request for application conversion, except for cases where only a part of the registration application is converted as prescribed in Point c Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 32. Withdrawal of inventions registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention/utility solution patent, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the application.
2. The withdrawal of the application shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative in writing. In case the application is filed by a representative, the letter of attorney must specify authorization for withdrawal of the application or enclose a receipt specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 02 months from the date on which the declaration of withdrawal of the application is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal as follows:
a) If the request for withdrawal meets the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the request for withdrawal and terminate the processing of the application. The withdrawn application cannot be re-registered but can be used as a basis for requesting priority right.
b) If the request for withdrawal does not meet the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for withdrawal and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to remedy deficiencies.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are insufficient, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for withdrawal.
Article 33. Re-examination of applications
1. An application shall be re-examined in the following cases:
a) Having a written opinion of a third party opposing the intended issuance of an invention patent in the notice of content appraisal results together with legitimate evidence proving that the opposition is due to force majeure events or objective obstacles, resulting in the inability to oppose the application within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The applicant requests amendments related to the nature of the invention stated in the application after the National Office of Intellectual Property has notified the content appraisal results, which state the intended issuance of a protection title according to the provisions of Point c Clause 5 Article 28 of this Circular;
c) The invention registration application is requested to be converted in the case specified in Clause 4 Article 31 of this Circular.
2. The re-appraisal of the application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-appraisal of an invention registration application shall comply with the provisions of Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complex cases involving multiple facts requiring verification or expert opinions, the re-appraisal time limit may be extended but must not exceed the initial appraisal time limit prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The re-appraisal contents shall comply with the corresponding provisions in Section 3 Chapter II of this Circular;
c) For each case specified at Point a Clause 1 of this Article, the re-appraisal of the invention registration application may only be carried out once by a third party.
3. After the re-examination results are available, an invention registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For cases specified at Point a, Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-examination results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing according to regulations of this Circular;
b) For cases specified at Points b and c, Clause 1 of this Article, processing shall comply with Article 25 of this Circular.
Section 5. PCT APPLICATIONS AND PROCESSING OF PCT APPLICATIONS
Article 34. PCT Applications

1. PCT applications include PCT applications originating from Vietnam and national PCT applications.
2. For PCT applications originating from Vietnam, applicants may submit them through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or directly to the International Bureau. Applications submitted directly to the International Bureau must be made in the language prescribed in the PCT Agreement and must meet the requirements regarding form and content prescribed in the PCT Agreement.
Applications submitted through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in English, each application shall be made into 01 copy and must meet the requirements regarding form and content prescribed in the PCT Agreement and the applicant must pay the fee for preliminary examination of form, fees and charges according to regulations of the Regulation on implementation of the PCT Agreement and the law on fees and charges of member countries designated in the PCT application.
3. For an application with designation or selection of Vietnam to enter the national phase, the applicant shall submit it to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 31 months from the priority date (if the application requests priority rights) or from the date of filing the international application, the following documents are required:
a) An invention registration declaration, made according to Form No. 01 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) A copy of the international application (in case the applicant requests entry into the national phase before the international publication);
c) Vietnamese translation of the description and summary in the international application (publication or original submitted initially if the application has not yet been published and the amended version and explanatory document of the amendments, if the international application is amended under Article 19 and/or Article 34.2(b) of the PCT Treaty);
d) A written authorization (in case the application is filed through a representative).
4. The international application with designation or selection of Vietnam to enter the national phase shall have the submission date as the international application submission date.
Article 35. Processing of PCT applications originating from Vietnam filed through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam
1. After receiving a PCT application originating from Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Collect fees for preliminary examination of application format;
b) Examine conformity of submission of applications for security control in case of inventions according to Article 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 14 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) Notify fees as prescribed to enable applicants to transfer them to International Office and international search agency according to provisions of PCT Agreement;
d) Examine and process applications according to provisions of PCT Agreement;
dd) Transfer applications to International Office and international search agency in case applications meet preliminary requirements regarding format and fees as per national laws are submitted within time limit and subjects of protection requested in applications do not fall under state secrets;
e) Issue notice of refusal to perform subsequent tasks in case applications fail to satisfy security control requirements in case of inventions according to Article 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 14 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. After PCT Applications originating from Vietnam have been transferred by the National Office of Intellectual Property to International Office, all transactions related to the applications shall be directly conducted by the applicants with International Office or competent agencies of member countries of PCT Agreement as designated in the applications according to provisions of PCT Agreement.
Article 36. Processing of PCT Applications at national stage
A national application for priority shall be processed as follows:
1. The request for priority right in the national application for priority shall be processed in accordance with the PCT treaty and the Regulations on implementation of the PCT treaty. To enjoy the priority right, the applicant must:
a) Reaffirm the request for priority right in the declaration;
b) Pay the fee for examination of the request for priority right;
c) Submit the Vietnamese translation of the documents submitted to the International Office at the request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and necessary documents according to Rule 17.1(a) of the Regulations on implementation of the PCT treaty.
2. The applicant may amend or supplement documents in the application. The amendment or supplementation of documents in the application must comply with the following regulations:
a) Articles 28 and 41 of the PCT treaty, Rules 52.1(b) and 78.1(b) of the Regulations on implementation of the PCT treaty, Article 115 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 28 of this Circular;
b) The written authorization or transfer of the right to file an application in the international stage (if any) must be submitted within 34 months from the priority date (if the application requests the priority right) or from the date of filing the international application;

c) Amendments and supplements submitted by applicants to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese.
3. The starting time for processing a national stage PCT application is the first day of the 32nd month from the priority date (if the application requests priority rights) or from the date of filing the international application. If the applicant submits a written request for early processing of the national stage PCT application, the PCT application shall be processed ahead of schedule in accordance with this Clause in accordance with Article 23.2 of the PCT Agreement.
4. A PCT application at the national stage shall undergo formal examination and substantive examination according to procedures applicable to applications for invention registration filed under the national filing format and shall be published within 01 month from the date on which the application is considered valid, provided that such publication does not occur earlier than the date on which the application is published internationally under the PCT Agreement, unless the applicant requests early publication.
Chapter III
                         PROCESSING OF REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS FOR ARCHITECTURAL PLANNING
Section 1. REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS FOR ARCHITECTURAL PLANNING
Article 37. Registration applications for architectural planning
An application for registration of architectural planning includes the following documents:
1. Declaration for layout design registration, made using Form No. 6 under Appendix I hereto.
2. Set of photos or set of layout design drawings (if the application is submitted in paper form, submit 04 sets).
3. Semiconductor integrated circuit production designs, including 04 designs, if the layout design has been commercially exploited.
4. Description of integrated circuits produced according to layout designs, including information on functions and structure of semiconductor integrated circuits produced according to layout designs to clarify the nature of the layout designs.
5. Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative).
6. Documents proving the registration right, if the applicant inherits such right from another person.
7. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 38. Requirements for documents in an application for layout design registration
1. Documents of an application for layout design registration must meet specific requirements as prescribed in Clauses 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 of this Article and general requirements regarding format as follows:
a) Documents must be presented in vertical orientation (drawings, diagrams and tables may be presented horizontally) on one sheet of paper with A4 size (210mm x 297mm), including four margins, each margin is 20mm wide, using Times New Roman font, font size not smaller than 13, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b) Documents consisting of multiple pages must have page numbers written in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with opaque ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents consist of at least 2 pages, it is required to have signatures on each page or append a seal on every two adjacent pages of the applicant/issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents signed digitally. Before receiving the application, if detecting insignificant spelling errors, the applicant may correct them and must append signatures (and seals, if any) at the corrected positions; the signatures and seals must not obscure the contents of the documents;
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system must be formatted as “.pdf”; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; individuals signing documents of the application for layout design registration may use digital signatures to directly sign on the electronic documents or directly sign on the physical documents and scan the physical documents in color mode in case of submission through the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not local languages, rare words, or self-created words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts, and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers used for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 5, 6, and 7 Article 37 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) Documents of the application must ensure that all mandatory information and consistent information are included.
2. The layout design registration application shall ensure consistency as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The declaration for layout design registration must be fully completed with information as required in appropriate places in the declaration. In case the applicant requests to keep information confidential, the documents to be kept confidential must be clearly stated in the declaration.
4. The set of photos and drawings of layout design must fully show the spatial structure of components and their interconnections in semi-conducting closed circuits so that based on such set of photos and drawings, a unique layout design can be identified and determined. For the above-mentioned purposes, the set of photos and drawings of layout design must satisfy the conditions specified in clauses 5, 6 and 7 of this Article.
5. The set of photos and drawings of layout design must include at least one of the following three types of documents:
a) Layout drawings made by CAD for each integrated circuit layer;
b) Drawings or photos of photolithically carved masks used for producing each integrated circuit layer;
c) Photographs of each layer of layout design shown in the integrated circuit.
6. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the applicant shall submit a set of photographs and drawings of the layout design enclosed with supporting documents being objects carrying electronic data of part or all of such set of photographs and drawings.
7. Form of set of photographs and drawings of the layout design:
a) The set of photographs and drawings of the layout design must include (a collection) of photographs and drawings separated for each layer of layout design, accompanied by symbols of each layer, size of the integrated circuit and magnification;
b) All photographs and drawings of the layout design must be on the same scale. For paper documents, each photograph and drawing of the layout design must be magnified at least 20 times the size in the integrated circuit so that the eye can see the basic circuit design;
c) Each photograph and drawing of the layout design may be presented on an A4-sized paper sheet or a larger paper sheet, provided that it is folded into an A4-sized paper sheet;
d) The photographs and drawings of the layout design must be clear and sharp.
8. Samples of semi-conducting integrated circuits produced according to layout designs to be submitted must be the whole or part of the integrated circuits corresponding to the layout designs stated in the applications. If the integrated circuits produced according to layout designs are part inseparable from another product, the products enclosed with the applications must be accompanied with documents indicating accurately the integrated circuit parts produced according to such layout designs. In case the layout design has been commercially exploited anywhere in the world before the application submission date, the applicant must submit samples of the first commercially exploited one.
9. Description of a semi-conducting integrated circuit manufactured under a layout design shall include the following detailed information about such semi-conducting integrated circuit which is required to be protected:
a) Name/code: is a collection of letters and/or numbers used to distinguish this integrated circuit from other integrated circuits when put on the market;
b) Description of basic functions of the integrated circuit (e.g., memory or logic function or another function);
c) Description of basic structure of the integrated circuit (e.g., bipolar, MOS, BiMOS, optoelectronic or another structure);
d) Description of technology for manufacturing the integrated circuit (e.g., TTL, DTL, ECL, ITL, CMOS, NMOS, PMOS or another technology);
dd) Description of main characteristics distinguishing it from other semi-conducting integrated circuits on the market at the time of filing the application or at the time of first commercial exploitation in the world, whichever is earlier.
10. Documents requesting confidentiality protection shall be separated into a package of confidential documents and may be submitted in the following forms:
a) Microfilm or similar form for documents showing the size of the design on computer drawings;
b) Electronic data;
c) Drawings or photos of parts that are not visible, provided that the characteristics of the layout design are basically visible.
The maximum level of confidentiality for a layout design that has not been commercially exploited is 50% of the surface area of each layer; for a layout design that has been commercially exploited, it is 2 layers out of 5 layers from top to bottom.

For an electronic application, if the application is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall return to the applicant a receipt which includes information about the submission date, application number, and results of the inspection of the list of documents of the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application submission system.

In case an application fails to satisfy all conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, it shall be rejected if it is submitted by post or electronically through the electronic application submission system, and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reason for rejection). Section 2. FORMAL ANALYSIS OF REGISTERED PLANNING DESIGN APPLICATIONS
Article 41. Formal analysis of registered planning design applications
1. After being received, a registered planning design application shall undergo formal analysis as prescribed in Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property to assess the validity of the application and no substantive analysis shall be conducted.
2. A registered planning design application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the registration right as prescribed in Article 86 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed against regulations in Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not eligible for protection as prescribed in Articles 8 and 69 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) There are grounds to confirm that the author of the layout design fails to satisfy the requirements specified in Clause 1, Article 122 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed in Article 10 of this Circular (including cases where the fees for filing the application, publication of the application, and appraising the application have not been fully paid);
e) The application does not meet the formal requirements prescribed in Article 38 of this Circular (the application contains errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding the presentation; translation of documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not properly certified as prescribed, the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author, the applicant, the representative (if any), there is no signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.
g) There is no valid authorization document (if the application is filed through a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information and evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 01 month from the date of notification by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming legal registration rights, if the applicant inherits the right to file the application from another person (documents proving inheritance rights, documents proving the transfer of the right to file the application; assignment contracts or labor contracts), etc. After the above-mentioned time limit, if the applicant fails to submit additional documents to meet the requirements, the application will continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application.
4. Notification of results of appraisal of layout design registration applications shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notification of the formal examination results to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the Certificate of registered layout design, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies in the application and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinions or correct deficiencies;

b) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or provides unsatisfactory corrections within the time limit specified in Point a of this Clause or fails to provide opposing opinions or provides unsatisfactory opposing opinions, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the Certificate of registered layout design and send it to the applicant;
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant has provided satisfactory corrections or satisfactory explanations within the prescribed time limit, within 01 month from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant the Certificate of registered layout design.
5. The submission date of a layout design registration application is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam according to Clause 2 Article 40 of this Circular.
In case the application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative hour or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date as prescribed in this Clause is the next working day.
6. The Certificate of registered layout design, made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, shall be issued in both electronic and paper form (in case the applicant requests a paper copy). In case the industrial property rights to a layout design are under joint ownership, the Certificate of registered layout design shall only be issued to the first person in the list of joint applicants.
A copy of the Certificate of registered layout design issued according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests a copy (specified in the declaration for registration of layout design) for other co-owners.
Article 42. Disclosure of applications for registration of layout designs
1. Applications for registration of layout designs shall be disclosed as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. From the date on which the application is disclosed, all persons may access detailed information about the nature of the layout design stated in the disclosed application, except for information that is kept confidential as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 38 and Article 39 of this Circular.
3. For confidential information about layout designs prescribed in Clause 3 Article 38 and Article 39 of this Circular, only agencies competent to carry out procedures for invalidation of protection titles or procedures for handling acts of infringement upon rights to new layout designs are permitted to access such information.

The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is responsible for actively extracting and using information available in databases announced by database managing agencies. 6. In case the applicant makes amendments to the application based on the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam's notice of appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall accept or refuse to accept the applicant's request for amendments to the application in the notice of appraisal results or the corresponding decision during the processing of such layout design registration application.
7. In case of requesting amendments to the application, including amendments made by the applicant and amendments to the application based on notification of appraisal results, such request shall be made in the form as prescribed in Point a Clause 2 of this Article. The handling of such request shall comply with Clause 5 of this Article.
8. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue the certificate of registration of layout design, the applicant may request the recognition of changes to the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, or according to the decision of a competent authority.

Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection, or provides unjustifiable objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept change of applicant.

Article 44. Withdrawal of layout design registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to grant or refuses to grant a certificate of registered layout design, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the layout design registration application.
2. The withdrawal of a layout design registration application must be made by the applicant or their authorized representative through a written declaration. For an application filed through a representative, the authorization document must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or enclose a subpoena specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 02 months from the receipt of a declaration of withdrawal of the layout design registration application, the withdrawal request shall be processed as follows:

b) In case the withdrawal request fails to satisfy the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or has corrected errors but the correction is not satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request.

Article 45. Applications for registration of industrial designs
An application for registration of an industrial design shall include the following documents:
1. Declaration for registration of industrial design, made using Form No. 3 in Appendix I of this Circular.
2. Documents identifying the industrial design to be protected are sets of photos or drawings of the industrial design (4 sets of photos or drawings shall be submitted if the application is filed in paper form).
3. Description of the industrial design.
4. Authorization document (if the application is filed by a representative).
5. Documents proving registration rights, if the applicant inherits such rights from another person.
6. Documents proving priority rights, if there is a request for priority right, including:
a) Copy of the first application or applications received by the first receiving agency;
b) Document transferring the priority right, if such right is inherited from another person.
7. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 46. Requirements on documents in an application for registration of industrial designs
1. Documents of an industrial design registration application must meet corresponding specific requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article and general requirements on form as prescribed below:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm) with 20 mm margins on all sides, using the Times New Roman font size of 13 pt or larger, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b/ Each document other than a set of photos or drawings of industrial designs shall be numbered in Arabic numerals for each page thereof;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with fade-resistant ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents have 2 pages or more, each page must bear a signature or a seal affixed by the applicant or the issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if minor spelling errors are found, the applicant may correct them and must affix a signature (and seal, if applicable) at the correction location. The signature and seal must not obscure the content of the documents.
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system must be in the “.pdf” format, except for sets of photos, drawings of industrial designs, and documents that may be submitted in common image file formats (such as “.jpg” or “.png”, etc.); the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; the individual signing the documents of the industrial design registration application may use a digital signature to sign directly on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as a color photo in the case of submission via the application.
VNeID;

d) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not local languages, rare words, or self-created terms). Symbols, measurement units, electronic fonts, and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and transaction papers between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents under Clauses 4, 5, 6, and 7 Article 45 of this Circular that may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese when requested;
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure complete and consistent mandatory information.
2. The industrial design registration application must ensure consistency as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 5 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In cases where an application requests protection for an entire product or a part of a product accompanied by one or more variations of such industrial design, the variations of an industrial design must not be significantly different from the basic variation and must not be significantly different from each other.
Where products are understood as follows:
a) Physical products are tangible objects existing in the form of physical matter such as objects, tools, equipment, and means, produced by industrial or handicraft methods, with clear structures and functions;
b) Non-physical products are objects not existing in the form of tangible matter, created through projecting or displaying a design on a surface or into the environment (including air), with practical functions to operate, interact, or perform specific tasks;
c) Part of a product is the upper part of the product that the applicant requests protection, including detachable parts and non-detachable parts;
d) The detachable part of the product is the product component capable of independent circulation used for assembly or composition of the product if it is registered independently, also collectively referred to as product, excluding specific regulations.
3. The declaration for registration of industrial design must be fully filled out with information as required in appropriate places and meet the following requirements:
a) In the section "Name of industrial design", the name of the product bearing the industrial design must be written in brief using common words, not advertising, containing commercial signs, annotations, or instructions;
b) In the section "International classification of industrial designs", the international classification index of the industrial design requiring protection must be specified according to the International Classification of Industrial Designs (according to the Locarno Agreement on International Classification of Industrial Designs). If the applicant fails to classify or incorrectly classifies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam will classify and the applicant must pay classification fees as prescribed;
c) In the section "Field of use of industrial design", the specific field of use of the product bearing the industrial design must be clearly stated, specifying the purpose, utility, and function of the product;
d) In the section "Type of industrial design", one of the two sections "Industrial design of the entire product" or "Industrial design of a part of the product" must be selected. In case of an industrial design of a part of the product, the method of expression of the protection request shown on the set of photos or drawings must be clearly stated.
4. The set of photos or drawings of the industrial design must meet the following requirements:
a) The set of photos or drawings of the industrial design must fully express the design characteristics of the industrial design requiring protection to the extent that the person with average knowledge in the relevant field can determine such industrial design. A person with average knowledge in the relevant field as prescribed at this point is a person who has common practical skills and knows general and popular knowledge in the relevant field;
b) The set of photos or drawings must present the subject matter of the protection request in a uniform and accurate manner. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the set of photos or drawings must be printed or affixed on A4 paper without frames;
c) The photos or drawings must be clear and sharp; the background color of the photos or drawings must be consistent and contrast with the color of the product with the industrial design; on the photos or drawings, only the product with the industrial design requested for protection shall be presented (not accompanied by other products), except for the cases specified in Points k and l of this Clause; do not contain technical drawing instructions or explanations about the industrial design, except for concise and necessary instructions to indicate cross-sections or enlarged views;
d) The set of photos or drawings must clearly show the protected parts and unprotected parts on the product in one of the following ways:

d1) The protected parts are shown by continuous lines while the unprotected parts are shown by interrupted lines; d2) The protected parts are shown in contrasting colors with the unprotected parts;
dd) The photos or drawings must present the industrial design at the same scale. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the shape representing the product with the industrial design must have an size of no larger than 190mm x 277mm and the largest dimension must not be smaller than 120mm;
e) The photos or drawings must present the industrial design at the same orientation and be numbered sequentially: three-dimensional perspective of the industrial design, projections of the industrial design from the front, back, right, left, top and bottom; the projections must be presented in frontal view;
g) Photos or projections identical or symmetrical with existing photos or projections, photos or projections of the bottom surface of the product with thickness and weight too large, photos or projections of the product with thickness too thin, photos or projections of the surface with thickness too thin of the industrial design, photos or projections where the protected parts are invisible and not required to be included in the application, provided that these must be clearly stated in the list of photos and drawings under the description;
h) For an industrial design of the whole product or part of a product which can be developed in the form of a plane (e.g., containers, packages, etc.), projections of such industrial design may be replaced by photos or drawings of the industrial design in the developed state;
i) Depending on the complexity of the industrial design, it may be necessary to provide additional photos and perspective drawings from different angles, cross-sections, enlarged views, separate details or parts of the product, or illustrative photos or drawings showing the installation and use of components on the complete product in order to clarify the nature and design characteristics of the industrial design (which are not used to establish industrial property rights to the industrial design of the component or part);
k) For the industrial design of the whole product, photos or drawings of each component or part of the product may be provided but only for illustrative purposes and not used to establish industrial property rights to the industrial design of the component or part;
l) The photos or drawings must show the industrial design in the selected state of use; photos or drawings showing other states may be provided to clarify the nature of the industrial design. In case the non-physical product's industrial design has a shape change or displacement, this effect may be shown by a series of sequential images, each image in the series showing a successive state of the change process;
m) For an application with multiple schemes, the basic scheme must be presented first. Each scheme of the industrial design must be presented in a sufficient number of photos and drawings as prescribed in this clause; the photos and drawings must be numbered to show the order of the corresponding scheme and the order of such photos and drawings in such scheme;
n) For a set of products, there must be a perspective drawing of the entire set of products and a set of photos and drawings of each product in the set according to the provisions of this clause.
5. An industrial design description must include the following contents:
a) List all basic design features expressing the nature of the industrial design and conforming to design features shown in the set of photos or drawings as follows:

a1) Design features of the industrial design requiring protection shall be listed, including: three-dimensional features, lines, correlation between three-dimensional features and/or lines, color features (if any), shape, layout, proportion, display order and visual effects of graphic elements on non-physical products;

a2) For products with different usage states (e.g., products with a lid or can be folded, etc.), list basic design features of the product in different states;

a3) If the industrial design consists of multiple schemes, specify distinctive design features of each scheme compared to the basic scheme;

a4) If the industrial design is a set of products, list basic design features of each product in the set;
b) Closest similar industrial design (if any): clearly state whether there is a similar industrial design, if yes, specify the industrial design that is least different from the industrial design of the same type of product stated in the application, which has been widely known before the application submission date or the priority date (if the application requests priority rights), specifying the source of information disclosing the closest similar industrial design;
c) List photos or drawings: List sequentially photograms, perspective drawings, projections, cross-sections, etc. of the industrial design, conforming to the serial number written on the photos or drawings.

Section 2. APPLICATION FORMS OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGN REGISTRATION
Article 48. Format appraisal of industrial design registration applications
1. Format appraisal of an industrial design registration application according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means examination of compliance with regulations on form of the application, thereby making a conclusion on whether the application is considered valid or invalid.
2. An industrial design registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Article 86 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed against regulations in Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not eligible for protection as prescribed in Articles 8 and 64 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) There are grounds to confirm that the author of the industrial design fails to satisfy the requirements prescribed in Clause 1, Article 122 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed in Article 10 of this Circular (including cases where the fees for filing the application, publication of the application, examination of the application, and information lookup for examination have not been fully paid);
e) The application fails to meet the formal requirements prescribed in Article 46 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding presentation; failing to classify or incorrectly classifying the industrial design without paying classification fees; lacking translations of documents proving priority rights (if required), or translations of documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent, erased, or not properly certified; the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author, applicant, representative, or signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.
g) There is no valid authorization document (if the application is filed through a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information and evidence) to suspect the authenticity of information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 02 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming the lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (documents proving inheritance rights, documents proving the transfer of application submission rights; assignment contracts or labor contracts, etc.). If the applicant fails to submit supplementary documents satisfying requirements by the above-mentioned deadline, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents in the application.

4. Notification of the results of the formal examination shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notification of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant an industrial design patent, reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application (including the priority right request) and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide opinions or correct deficiencies;
b) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to meet requirements after the time limit prescribed at Point a of this Clause expires or does not have any objection or has an objection but it is unconvincing, within 15 working days from the expiration of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant an industrial design patent and send it to the applicant;
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant has corrected deficiencies to meet requirements or has an unconvincing objection within the prescribed time limit, the application shall be considered valid in terms of form and be transferred for publication and substantive appraisal as prescribed. In case the priority right request is rejected, the application shall still be considered valid and be transferred for publication as prescribed.
5. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of industrial design registration applications that have been transferred for publication, clearly stating the application number, filing date, and date of transfer of publication for each application.
6. The filing date of an industrial design registration application is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in accordance with Clause 2 Article 47 of this Circular.
In case the application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative working hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the filing date as prescribed in this Clause shall be the subsequent working day.
7. The priority date of an industrial design registration application shall be determined as follows:
a) If the application does not contain a request for priority right or although it contains a request for priority right, it is not approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, it shall be considered that the application has no priority date;
b) If the application contains a request for priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) The determination of the priority date according to the request for priority right based on the first application submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
8. In case the applicant requests priority right but, at the time of filing the industrial design registration application, there is no document proving the priority right, the examination of the application form shall begin from the time the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the supplementary documents or end the 3-month period as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 47 of this Circular.
Article 49. Publication of industrial design registration applications
1. A valid industrial design registration application shall be published as follows:
a) The application shall be published within 01 month from the date on which it is considered valid if the applicant does not request late publication or requests late publication but the application is considered valid after the deadline for requesting late publication;
b) In case the applicant requests late publication and the application is considered valid before the deadline for requesting late publication, the application shall be published in the following month of the end date of the deadline for requesting late publication.
2. Contents of an industrial design registration application published in the Industrial Property Gazette include the following information: Applicant, representative (if any); Application number, filing date, priority date (if any); name and nationality of the author of the industrial design; information about the origin of the application (split application, initial application number of split application, etc.); set of photos or drawings of the industrial design; number of schemes/products requested for protection; international classification of the industrial design; and other information (if any).
Section 3. CONTENT APPRAISAL OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGN REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 50. Content appraisal of industrial design registration applications
1. Content appraisal of industrial design registration applications according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application according to the conditions for protection and determination of the corresponding scope of protection according to this Article.
2. Assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles in industrial design registration applications includes:
a) Assessment of the conformity of subject matters stated in industrial design registration applications with the requirements for grant of industrial design patents according to Article 52 of this Circular;
b) Assessment of subject matters stated in industrial design registration applications according to each protection condition as prescribed in Articles 54, 55 and 56 of this Circular. The assessment shall be carried out separately for each product type (if the application refers to a set of products); in case it refers to multiple solutions, each solution shall be assessed separately, starting from the basic solution (the first solution stated in the application);
c) Assessment of the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 57 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles under Point b Clause 2 of this Article shall be completed after the industrial design subject matter stated in the application has been assessed with respect to all protection conditions and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter fails to satisfy or satisfies the protection conditions, specifically:
a) There is a reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy one or several or all protection conditions; or b) There is no reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Before issuing a notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of an industrial design patent according to Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 58 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 57 of this Circular.
5. Based on the results of the assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding scope of protection.
6. Notification of content appraisal results for the intended grant of industrial design patents specified in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 58 of this Circular shall be made for the following applications for registration of industrial designs:
a) Applications not falling into the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Applications with the earliest submission date or priority date among all applications for registration of industrial designs falling into the cases specified in Clause 1 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Applications under agreements specified in Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. Applications for registration of industrial designs not falling into the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article shall be handled as follows:
a) They shall be rejected from the grant of industrial design patents because they fail to satisfy the first-to-file rule if they have the earliest submission date or priority date among all applications for registration of industrial designs granted industrial design patents; or b) They shall be considered applications with the earliest submission date or priority date and handled as the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article if all other applications for registration of industrial designs with earlier submission dates or priority dates are rejected from the grant of industrial design patents, withdrawn or considered withdrawn.
8. During the process of content appraisal of an application, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations about the contents of the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application in order to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to remedy the situation.
Article 51. Minimum information sources
1. Information lookup purpose is to search in the mandatory information sources for industrial designs identical or similar to those stated in the application. Mandatory information sources used in the process of content appraisal of an application include the following documents:
a) Industrial design registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam with an application publication date earlier than the submission date or priority date of the application currently being appraised (if the application enjoys priority rights);
b) Industrial design registration applications and protection titles of industrial designs published by other organizations or countries within 25 years before the application submission date or priority date of the application currently being appraised (if the application enjoys priority rights), which are stored in the existing database of industrial designs at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) Other information related to industrial designs collected and stored by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
d) Industrial design registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, international industrial design registration applications with designation of Vietnam and with submission dates or priority dates (if the applications enjoy priority rights) earlier or on the same day as the submission date or priority date of the application currently being appraised (used to check the first-to-submit principle prescribed in Article 57 of this Circular).
2. Where necessary and possible, the information lookup may be expanded beyond the mandatory information sources specified in Clause 1 of this Article, including national databases of inventions and marks and other accessible information sources.

3. The lookup results must be presented in the lookup report, clearly stating the lookup field, scope, and search results within that scope (statistics and identification of found control industrial designs, information origin, and publication date of corresponding information) and the full name of the person preparing the report (the searcher).
In which, "control industrial design" means an industrial design identical or similar to the industrial design stated in the application, which is compared with the industrial design stated in the application when evaluating novelty and creativity.
Article 52. Assessment of conformity of subject matters stated in applications for registration of industrial designs
1. Subject matters stated in an application for registration of an industrial design shall be deemed to be unconformable with the industrial design patent if such subject matters are not the external appearance of the whole product or part of the product.
External appearance of the whole product or part of the product means a collection of design features (shapes, lines, colors or combination thereof) of the whole product or part of the product which can be seen during the utilization of the product. The utilization of the product means putting such products into use according to their properties and utilities, excluding maintenance, repair or servicing of the products. For an industrial design of a separate part registered independently, the visibility shall be assessed on products assembled from such part.
2. Subject matters stated in an application as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article include:
a/ External appearance of the whole product or part of the product which is required by technical characteristics of the product;
b/ External appearance of civil or industrial construction works other than external appearance of modules or separate units which can be used independently or assembled together to form construction works such as shops, kiosks, mobile houses or similar products.
Article 53. Assessment of distinctiveness of industrial designs The assessment of distinctiveness between two industrial designs shall comply with the following provisions:
1. Two industrial designs shall be regarded as identical when they are applied to products of the same type and have the same collection of fundamental and non-fundamental design features.
2. Two industrial designs shall be considered not significantly different from each other when they are used for products of the same type and have the same collection of fundamental design features.
3. Two industrial designs shall be considered similar to each other when they are used for products of the same type and have at least one fundamental design feature which is identical or not significantly different from each other.
4. Two similar industrial designs shall be considered the closest to each other when they have the maximum number of fundamental design features which are identical or not significantly different from each other compared with all other similar industrial designs.
5. Two industrial designs shall be considered significantly different from each other when they are used for products of different types or products of the same type but have at least one different fundamental design feature.
6. Design features of an industrial design shall be understood as follows:
a) Design features of an industrial design are elements expressed in form of lines, three-dimensional figures, colors, position correlation or size correlation of the protection requirement on the overall product, shape, layout arrangement, proportion correlation, display order and visual effect of graphic elements on non-physical products so that when combined with other features (signs), they form such industrial design;
b) Fundamental design features are design features which can be easily recognized/remembered, necessary and sufficient to determine the nature of the industrial design and distinguish the industrial design from another industrial design used for products of the same type.
Products of the same type are products with identical or similar uses or functions. Products assembled from multiple parts and parts used for assembly into such products are products of different types.
c) The following elements shall not be considered as fundamental design features of an industrial design:

c1) Shapes and lines determined by the technical function of the product (e.g., the flat shape of a data recording disc determined by the relative movement between the disc and the disc reader, etc.);

c2) Elements whose presence in the collection of signs is insufficient to cause aesthetic impression (the appearance of the product remains unchanged when such elements are present or absent; e.g., a change in a familiar shape or line but such change is insufficient to be recognized, therefore the changed shape or line is still only recognized as the old one);

c3) Materials used for manufacturing the product;

c4) Information displayed inside non-physical products (such as documents, data, etc.); signs affixed or stuck on the product, etc., for the purpose of providing information or instructions on the origin, characteristics, composition, uses, usage method, etc. of such product; e.g., information on goods labels (such as manufacturers, commercial indications, origin, barcodes, etc.), trademarks, geographical indications, etc.;

c5) Size of the product, except for changes in decorative sizes of fabric samples and similar materials;

c6) Invisible design features during the utilization of the product’s functions.

For an industrial design of a detachable part registered independently, the determination of invisible design features must be based on the product assembled from such part;

c7) Other elements that fail to meet the conditions specified at Point b of this Clause.
Article 54. Assessment of novelty
1. To assess the novelty of an industrial design stated in an application, it is necessary to compare the set of fundamental design features of such industrial design with the set of fundamental design features of each control industrial design found during the information lookup process.
2. An industrial design stated in an application shall be considered new if:
a) The industrial design is not found in the mandatory information sources; or b) Although the industrial design is found in the mandatory information sources, it is considered significantly different from the industrial designs in the application; or c) The industrial design is the one mentioned in the application that is published or disclosed and falls under the cases specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 65 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 55. Assessment of creativity
1. To assess the creativity of the industrial design mentioned in the application, it is necessary to compare the set of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial design with the set of fundamental design characteristics of the industrial designs found during the information lookup.
2. The industrial design mentioned in the application shall be considered creative if it does not fall into the following cases:
a) The industrial design is simply a combination of known design characteristics (design characteristics that have been publicly disclosed and arranged or assembled in a simple manner such as replacement, change of position, increase or decrease of quantity, etc.);
b) The industrial design is a copy/mockup of part or all of the natural appearance of trees, fruits, animals, etc., or the appearance of geometric shapes (circle, ellipse, triangle, square, rectangle, regular polygon, prism with cross-section being one of the above shapes, etc.) that are known;
c) The industrial design is a simple copy/mockup of part or all of the appearance of products or works that are well-known or recognized in Vietnam or the world;
d) The industrial design is a mockup of an industrial design in another field if such mockup has been actually recognized (for example: toy that is a mockup of a car, motorcycle, etc.).
Article 56. Assessment of industrial applicability
1. An industrial design stated in an application shall be deemed to have industrial applicability if, based on information about such industrial design presented in the application, a person with average knowledge in the relevant field could use it as a model to create a batch of products with the external appearance or part of the external appearance being such industrial design, by manufacturing physical products by industrial or handicraft methods or by uniformly recreating non-physical products.
2. In the following cases, subject matters stated in applications shall be deemed not to have industrial applicability:
a) Subject matters stated in applications are the appearance of the whole product or part of the product in an undefined existence state (products in forms, liquids, interfaces with images displayed randomly, etc.);
b) The whole product or part of the product can only have the appearance like the subject matter stated in the application by using special skills or the whole product or part of the product cannot be repeatedly manufactured with the appearance like the subject matter stated in the application;
c) Other cases with legitimate reasons.
Article 57. Assessment of the first-to-file principle
For industrial designs whose registration applications have been concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing content appraisal results and intending to grant industrial design patents according to the provisions of Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 58 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property under the following regulations:
1. In order to check the first-to-file principle, information must be searched in the compulsory sources specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 51 of this Circular.
2. The search is to find industrial design registration applications for products of the same type identical or insignificantly different from each other or to find industrial design registration applications for products containing parts with industrial designs identical or insignificantly different from the registered industrial designs or to find industrial design registration applications for products including parts with external appearance identical or insignificantly different from the registered industrial designs and determine the application with the earliest filing date or priority date.
3. If there are many applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, an industrial design patent may only be granted to a valid application with the earliest filing date or priority date among applications satisfying the conditions for grant of an industrial design patent.
4. Among applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article for registration of products of the same type, if there are many applications with the same earliest filing date or priority date, an industrial design patent may only be granted to one application out of such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all such applications shall be rejected from grant of industrial design patents.
Article 58. Grant and refusal to grant industrial design patents
1. An industrial design registration application shall be refused to be granted an industrial design patent if it falls into the cases specified at Points a, b, c, d and dd, Clause 1 and Clause 1b, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. An industrial design registration application shall be granted an industrial design patent when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. Industrial design registration applications falling into the cases specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall be subject to the following tasks by the National Office of Intellectual Property:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clause 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the protection title and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on issuance of the protection title, and the registration fee for the decision on issuance of the protection title;
b) If the subject matter stated in the industrial design registration application does not satisfy the protection conditions or the application falls into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clause 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to issue the protection title, all reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application, and may provide guidance on amendments to the scope (volume) of protection and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to provide objections or correct deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or does not provide objections within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the protection title;
d) In case the applicant submits corrections or objections within the time limit specified in Point b of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the correction or objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the examination as follows:

d1) If the correction is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;

d2) If the correction is satisfactory or the objection is justifiable given the content examination results and/or the correction is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content examination results, stating the intended grant of the protection title and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for disclosure of the decision on the grant of the protection title, and the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title;
dd) If the applicant fails to fully pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for disclosure of the decision on the grant of the protection title, and the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title within the time limit specified in Points a and d2 of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;
e) If the applicant fully and promptly pays all fees and charges within the time limit specified in Points a and d2 of this Clause, within 01 month from the date of full payment of fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant the protection title.
4. The decision on the grant of an industrial design patent shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision. The published contents include information stated in the corresponding decision (including the decision number and issuance date) and other contents as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 49 of this Circular.
5. Industrial design patents made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued as electronic copies and paper copies (if the applicant requests a paper copy). In case industrial design rights are under joint ownership, an industrial design patent shall only be granted to the first person on the list of co-applicants.
A copy of an industrial design patent made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests a copy (specified in the declaration for registration of industrial design) for other co-owners.
Section 4. PROCESSING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO INDUSTRIAL DESIGN REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 59. Processing of objections to industrial design registration applications
1. Within 3 months from the date on which an industrial design registration application is published, any third party has the right to object to the grant of an industrial design patent for such industrial design registration application and must pay fees and charges.
The objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese enclosed with documents or sources of information for verification. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. If the objection to an industrial design registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process the objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 1 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to respond in writing.
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of such feedback and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide written responses to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to feedback if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) Based on information, evidence, arguments provided by the parties, the dialogue results (if any) as prescribed in points a, b, and c of this clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the feedback and notify the objector of the results of processing the feedback together with the content appraisal results corresponding to the application, excluding the case specified in point dd of this clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of the suspension of processing the feedback regarding the withdrawal of the industrial design registration application according to Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 1 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification of acceptance of withdrawal of the application.
3. In cases where the objector's feedback is related to the right to register industrial designs, the feedback shall be processed as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the objector to file a lawsuit petition to a competent court in accordance with civil procedure law and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to submit a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit;
b) Upon expiration of the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if the objector fails to submit a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit, the objector's feedback shall be considered withdrawn, and the industrial design registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no feedback;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in Point a of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the industrial design registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the court, except for the case specified in Point dd Clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the court.
4. The time limit for the applicant to provide feedback on objections and the time limit for the objector to respond to the applicant's feedback as prescribed in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal of the application.
Article 60. Amendment to industrial design registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may proactively amend or supplement documents in the industrial design registration application or based on the notification of appraisal results.
2. Requests for amendment to industrial design registration applications include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment to the application by the applicant, the request for amendment must be made using Form No. 10 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of amendment to the application based on the notification of appraisal results of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the amendment shall be made in a written response clearly stating the amended contents;
c) The applicant may request amendment with the same content related to multiple industrial design registration applications in one declaration or one written request for amendment;
d) In case of amendments to photos, drawings or descriptions, applicants shall submit sets of drawings and photos (4 sets for paper applications) or amended descriptions enclosed with detailed presentation of amendments to original documents submitted;
dd) In case of amendments to name, address or country code of the applicant, the applicant shall submit certification documents (original copies or certified true copies) or legal documents (certified true copies) proving the changes (decision on change of name, address; certificate of enterprise registration recording changes in name, address, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for actively extracting and using information available in databases announced by database management agencies to replace application components for handling administrative procedures. In case information cannot be extracted or is not fully and accurately extracted, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to individuals and organizations to supplement application components and clearly state reasons. Time for requesting and supplementing applications shall not be included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
e) In case of amendments to industrial property representatives, the applicant shall submit a document declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representative and change of the new industrial property representative as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular;
3. Applicants shall pay an appraisal fee for amendment requests for each amendment and a disclosure fee for amendments to the application if amendments must be disclosed as prescribed in Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except for amendments to correct errors made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Amendments to industrial design registration applications shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection already disclosed in photos, drawings and descriptions stated in the applications and shall not change the nature of the industrial design subject matter stated in the applications.
5. In case of amendment to application by applicant, the request for amendment shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment to application satisfies regulations in clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendment to application (except for the case where the amendment to application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant an industrial design patent due to invalidity of the application).
The amended information shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette if the request for amendment to information related to the application that is valid in terms of form includes name, nationality, address of the author of the industrial design, set of photos or drawings of the industrial design;
b) If the request for amendment to application does not satisfy regulations in clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment to application, clearly stating reasons and setting a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objection. If the prescribed time limit expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements, does not provide objection or provides unjustified objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment to application;
c) In case the request for amendment to application relates to the nature of the industrial design stated in the set of photos, drawings or description submitted after receiving the notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of an industrial design patent, the application shall be re-appraised and the applicant must pay fees as prescribed.
6. In case the applicant makes amendments to the application based on the notice of content appraisal results issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall accept or refuse to accept the request for amendment to the application in the notice of content appraisal results or corresponding decision during the processing of the application for registration of such industrial design.
7. In case of requesting amendment to the application, including amendments made by the applicant and amendments to the application based on notification of appraisal results, such request shall be made in the form as prescribed in Point a Clause 2 of this Article. The handling of such request shall comply with Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 61. Recording of changes of applicants for industrial design registration
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may request recording of changes of the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, or according to decision of a competent authority.
2. A request for recording of changes of the applicant must satisfy the following regulations:
a) The request for recording of changes of the applicant shall be made using Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to transfer of the application, the applicant must submit documents showing the transfer of the industrial design registration application (original or certified copy), including name and address of the transferring party and the transferee; number of transferred application or information sufficient to identify the application;
c) In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to inheritance, inheritance or according to decision of a competent authority, the applicant must submit documents proving related to inheritance or inheritance of assets upon consolidation, merger, division, separation of legal entities, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entities of the same owner, business format conversion or according to decision of the Court or other competent authorities or inheritance due to abandonment of the right to register by another applicant;
d) The applicant must pay an appraisal fee for the request for recording of changes of the applicant and a fee for disclosure of information on recording of changes of the applicant;
dd) The request for recording of changes due to transfer of many applications from the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that an appraisal fee is paid according to regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for recording of transfer.
3. The request for recording of changes in the applicant shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for recording of changes in the applicant meets the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of recording of changes in the applicant and publish it in the Industrial Property Gazette (except for cases where the amendment or supplementation of the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the industrial design patent due to invalidation of the application in terms of form);
b) If the request for recording of changes in the applicant does not meet the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to record changes in the applicant and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements, provides no objection, or provides unjustifiable objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to record changes in the applicant.
Article 62. Splitting of industrial design registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to grant or refuses to grant an industrial design patent, the applicant may take the initiative or based on the notification of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam of the appraisal results, split one or several industrial designs in the submitted industrial design registration application (initial application) into one or more new industrial design registration applications (split applications).
2. The applicant must submit a new declaration for the split industrial design. The split application shall have a new application number and the filing date shall be the same as that of the initial industrial design registration application or the priority dates of the initial industrial design registration application (if any).
The applicant shall pay an application fee and all fees and charges for procedures performed for the separate application outside the initial application which are not required to be repeated in the separate application and is not required to pay the charge for examination of the request for priority rights, except for cases where the application separation must be performed because the initial application does not meet the requirement of uniformity of the application.
3. For the initial application, the applicant shall perform the amendment and supplementation of the application according to the provisions of Article 60 of this Circular.
4. Separate application and initial application shall be processed as follows:
a) Separate application shall be appraised in terms of form and continue to be processed according to the unfinished procedures for the initial application. Separate application shall be published according to regulations.

b) Initial application shall continue to be processed according to the provisions of Article 60 of this Circular.
Article 63. Withdrawal of industrial design registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue the industrial design patent, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the industrial design registration application.
2. The withdrawal of the industrial design registration application shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative through a written declaration. For applications filed through representatives, the authorization document must clearly state the authorization for application withdrawal or must be enclosed with a command specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 02 months from the date of receipt of the declaration on withdrawal of the industrial design registration application or the withdrawal request, as follows:
a) In case the withdrawal request satisfies the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the withdrawal request and terminate the processing of the application. The withdrawn industrial design registration application cannot be re-registered but can only be used as a basis for requesting priority rights.
b) In case the withdrawal request does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a 01-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements after correction, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the withdrawal request.
Article 64. Re-valuation of industrial design registration applications
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the re-valuation of an industrial design registration application in the following cases:
a) There is a written opinion of a third party opposing the intended grant of an industrial design patent in the notice of content evaluation results, together with legitimate evidence proving that the opposition arises due to a force majeure event or an objective obstacle, resulting in the inability to oppose the application within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The applicant requests amendments to the application after notification of content evaluation results has been issued, indicating the intended grant of a protection title according to the provisions of point c clause 5 Article 60 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of an application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of an industrial design registration application shall comply with Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complex cases involving multiple facts requiring verification or expert opinions, the time limit for re-examination may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial examination prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The contents of re-examination shall comply with corresponding provisions in Section 3 Chapter IV of this Circular;
c) For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of an industrial design registration application shall only be carried out once by a third party.
3. After obtaining the results of re-examination, the industrial design registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For the case specified at Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-examination results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application in accordance with this Circular;
b) For the case specified at Point b Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application in accordance with Article 58 of this Circular.
Section 5. LAHART APPLICATIONS AND HANDLING OF LAHART APPLICATIONS
Article 65. LAHART APPLICATIONS

1. LAHART application includes LAHART application with Vietnam designation and LAHART application of Vietnamese origin.
2. For LAHART application of Vietnamese origin, applicant may submit application via the National Office of Intellectual Property or directly to International Office. Application submitted to International Office must be made in language according to provisions of the Hague Agreement and must satisfy requirements on form and content specified in the Hague Agreement.
3. LAHART application submitted via the National Office of Intellectual Property must be made in English (each application is made into 02 copies for paper application) and must satisfy requirements on form and content specified in the Hague Agreement and applicant must pay international application transfer fee, fees and charges according to provisions of the Hague Agreement and law on fees and charges of member countries designated.

4. For LAHART application with Vietnam designation, the submission date is the international registration date.
Article 66. Handling of LAHART applications of Vietnamese origin submitted via the National Office of Intellectual Property
1. In case LAHART application of Vietnamese origin is submitted via the National Office of Intellectual Property, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Collect international application transfer fee;
b) Notify the fee payable by the applicant directly to the International Office in accordance with the Hague Agreement within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the application;
c) Conduct preliminary examination of the form of the application within 12 working days from the date of receipt of the application;
d) In case the application contains errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of such errors and impose a time limit of 10 working days from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify them;
dd) Transfer the Hague Application originating from Vietnam to the International Office within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application.
2. The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the Hague Application originating from Vietnam shall be considered as the date of filing the international registration of industrial designs in case the International Office receives such application within 1 month from the date specified on the receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
3. After the Hague Application originating from Vietnam has been filed with the International Office, all transactions related to the application shall be conducted directly by the applicant with the International Office or the competent authority of the member countries of the Hague Agreement designated in the application in accordance with the Hague Agreement.
Article 67. Processing of Hague Applications with Vietnamese Designation
After receiving the notification from the International Office, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the Hague Application with Vietnamese designation as follows:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct content appraisal of an application like that for registration of industrial designs filed under the national filing format, except for cases specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 9 of this Article. Within 6 months from the date on which the application is published by the International Office, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conclude on the possibility of protection of the industrial design in the application.
2. In case the industrial design in the application satisfies the protection conditions as prescribed by Vietnamese law and the application has no deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Before the expiration of the 6-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to accept the protection of the internationally registered industrial design in the application, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of Internationally Registered Industrial Designs) and send it to the International Office for announcement of acceptance of protection for the internationally registered industrial design according to the form of the International Office;
b) Publish the decision in the Industrial Property Gazette within 1 month from the date of issuance of the decision.
3. In case the industrial design does not satisfy the protection conditions or the application has deficiencies (lack of photos/drawings making the set of photos/drawings fail to fully disclose the design features of the industrial design or the internationally registered industrial design fails to satisfy the declarations of Vietnam or there are information that need to be verified, etc.), before the expiration of the 6-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal according to the form of the International Office, clearly stating the reasons for refusal and send such notice to the International Office.
4. In case an industrial design is registered but fails to satisfy the protection conditions or the application contains deficiencies (lack of photos/drawings which make the set of photos/drawings fail to fully disclose the design features of the industrial design or the international registration fails to satisfy the declaration of Vietnam or information needs to be verified, etc.), before the end of the 6-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Issue a notice of rejection for the industrial design that fails to satisfy the protection conditions or has deficiencies according to the form of the International Office, clearly stating the reasons for rejection and send such notice to the International Office;
b) Issue a decision to accept protection for the industrial designs that satisfy the protection conditions and have no deficiencies, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part on Industrial Designs Registered Internationally) and send it to the International Office.

The Notification of acceptance of protection for the industrial design registered internationally shall be made according to the form of the International Office, clearly stating the industrial design accepted for protection;
c) Publish the decision in the Industrial Property Gazette within 1 month from the date of issuance of the decision.

5. Within 3 months from the date the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the rejection notice as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article, the applicant may correct deficiencies or protest against the rejection opinion of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The correction of deficiencies or protest against the rejection opinion stated in the notice shall be carried out according to the procedures applicable to applications for registration of industrial designs filed under the national filing form, including regulations on the method of filing applications.
In case the Hague Application is planned to be rejected due to failure to meet the requirements on the uniformity of the application as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 5, Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the applicant may remedy the above-mentioned deficiencies by requesting the separation of one or several industrial designs in the international registration mentioned above into one or more new applications. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall separate the application and issue decisions and notices for the new application independently from the original application.
6. In case the applicant sufficiently corrects errors and/or has a legitimate objection within the time limit specified in Clause 5 of this Article, within 02 months, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Issue a decision to accept protection of internationally registered industrial designs for industrial designs satisfying the protection conditions, record them in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of International Industrial Designs Registered Internally), and send it to the International Office for announcement of acceptance of protection of internationally registered industrial designs after refusal using the form of the International Office, specifying the industrial design accepted for protection;
b) Publish the decision in the Industrial Property Gazette within 01 month from the date of issuance.
7. In case the applicant fails to correct errors or insufficiently corrects errors, does not have an objection or has an illegitimate objection to the industrial designs notified of refusal within the time limit specified in Clause 5 of this Article, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to protect the internationally registered industrial designs.
8. In case the applicant fails to submit documents proving priority rights within the 03-month time limit from the date on which the International Office announces the application with designated Vietnam, or submits documents proving priority rights but they are not approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the application shall be considered as having no request for priority right.
9. Procedures for filing a complaint and resolving a complaint against decisions specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article shall be carried out like those applicable to applications for registration of industrial designs filed under national procedures. In case some or all industrial designs are refused in the decisions to refuse to accept protection after receiving the results of complaint resolution, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send the International Office for announcement of acceptance of protection after refusal using the form of the International Office, specifying the industrial designs accepted for protection.
10. In case a third party has an opinion about the application, if Vietnam is designated before the date of issuance of the decision to approve the protection, such opinion shall be regarded as a reference source for the processing of the application.
11. The contents to be disclosed under Point b Clause 2, Point c Clause 4 and Point b Clause 6 of this Article include basic information specified in the corresponding decision (including decision number and date) and other information specified in Clause 2 Article 49 of this Circular.
Chapter V
                            PROCESSING OF REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS FOR MARKS
Section 1. REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS FOR MARKS
Article 68. Registration applications for marks
A registration application for a mark shall include the following documents:
1. Declaration for mark registration, made according to Form No. 04 in Appendix I of this Circular.
2. Samples of marks enclosed therewith (5 samples of marks shall be enclosed for paper applications).
3. Regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks if the registered marks are collective marks or certification marks.
4. Authorization document (if the application is filed by a representative).
5. Documents proving the mark registration right if the applicant inherits the registration right from another person.
6. Documents proving the permission to register collective marks or certification marks containing geographical locations or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. Documents proving the priority right if the applicant requests the priority right, including:

a) A copy of the first application or the first applications received by the receiving agency;
b) The document transferring the priority right if such right is inherited from another person.
8. Documents proving the right to use an individual’s image for registration of a mark, including:
a) A copy of personal identification documents containing the applicant's image in case the applicant uses his/her own image;
b) A document permitting the use of the individual's image in accordance with civil law regulations in case the applicant uses another individual's image and a copy of such person's personal identification documents containing his/her image;
c) The applicant is not required to submit copies of personal identification documents as prescribed in Points a and b of this Clause in case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam has access to such information from the National Population Database.
9. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 69. Requirements on documents in applications for mark registration
1. Documents in applications for mark registration must meet corresponding requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of this article and general requirements regarding format as follows:
a) Documents must be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm); except for geographical map documents which may be presented on one side of an A3 paper sheet (420 mm x 297 mm) with four 20 mm wide margins on all sides, using the Times New Roman font, with font size not smaller than 13, except for supporting documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b) Documents with multiple pages shall be numbered in Arabic numerals.
c) Documents must be typed or printed using an ink type that is difficult to fade and clear, presented in a clean manner without erasing or modifying. Documents must bear signatures and seals. If documents have 2 pages or more, signatures of each page or seal of the applicant or the issuing authority (if any) must be affixed on every two adjacent pages, except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if insignificant spelling errors are found, the applicant may correct them and affix signatures (and seals, if any) at the correction locations. The corrected content must not be obscured by the signatures and seals;
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically under the electronic application submission system must be in “.pdf” format; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the paper document as per regulation; the individual signing the application’s documents may use a digital signature to directly sign on the electronic document or directly sign on the paper document and scan it as a color photo in case of submission via the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not local languages, rare words, or self-created terms). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts, and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers related to transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 4, 5, 7, 8, and 9 Article 68 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure complete and consistent mandatory information.
2. The mark registration application must ensure consistency as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 6 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The mark registration declaration must be fully filled out with information as requested in appropriate spaces of the declaration and meet the following requirements:
a) In the section "Non-traditional marks", if the applicant requests registration of a sound mark or a three-dimensional mark, the corresponding box must be marked;
b) In the section "Mark samples", there must be samples according to regulations on mark samples in Clause 4 of this Article;
c) In the section "Description of the mark", it is required to specify each constituent element of the mark and the overall meaning of the mark (if any) and describe the following contents: c1) If the mark contains words in another language other than Vietnamese, the Vietnamese pronunciation must be clearly written; if these words have meanings, they must be translated into Vietnamese; c2) If the mark contains numbers other than Arabic numerals or Roman numerals, they must be translated into Arabic numerals; c3) If the mark is a shape or contains a shape element, the content and meaning of the shape element must be clearly stated; c4) If the mark shows colors and there is a request for protection of color marks, this request must be clearly stated and the name of the color shown on the mark must be specified;

c5) If the mark is a sound, it is required to fully, in detail and completely describe the sound sign used as the mark (the sound of what musical instrument, with or without lyrics, etc.). This description may be included in the attached application form;
d) In the section "Exclusion elements": The applicant may exclude one or several elements of the mark sample without requesting protection of these exclusion elements separately;
dd) In the section "Applicant": In case the applicant is a foreign individual or organization, the name and address must be written in Latin letters in the international form of the original name, without translation or transcription into Vietnamese;
e) In the Section "List and classification of goods and services bearing marks", the applicant must classify them in accordance with the International Classification of Goods and Services under the Nice Agreement published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Gazette. If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify them and the applicant must pay the classification fee as prescribed.
4. The mark sample must meet the following requirements:
a) The mark sample must be clearly presented with the size of each component in the sample not exceeding 80mm and not smaller than 8mm, and the entire sample must be presented within the mark sample frame measuring 80mm x 80mm printed on the declaration form;
b) For three-dimensional marks, the mark sample must be accompanied by a photograph or drawing showing the perspective view and may also be accompanied by a descriptive model in the projection form;
c) For marks requiring color protection, the mark sample must be presented in the exact colors required for protection. If no color protection is required, the mark sample must be presented in black and white;
d) For sound marks, the mark sample must be an MP3 audio file with a capacity not exceeding 5 MB and a graphic representation of the sound, specifically a 5-line musical staff.
The 5-line musical staff must be clear, accurate, complete, and consistent with the audio file, which may include notes, clefs, beats, rhythms, and musical notes, etc.
5. Regulations on use of collective marks shall include the following main contents:
a/ Information on marks and goods or services bearing marks;
b/ Name, address, grounds for establishment and operation of collective organizations being mark owners when protection titles are granted;
c/ Criteria for becoming members of collective organizations;
d/ List of members of collective organizations;
e/ Conditions for using marks. For collective marks containing geographical origin indications, conditions for using collective marks must include geographical origin conditions as well as characteristics of goods or services;
f/ Measures to handle acts of violating regulations on use of marks.
6. Regulations on use of certification marks must contain the following principal contents:
a/ Information on marks and goods or services certified by marks;
b) The organization is the owner of the mark when the protection title is granted;
c) Conditions for using the mark. For certification marks containing signs indicating geographical origins, conditions for using certification marks must include conditions on geographical origins and characteristics of goods or services;
d) Characteristics of goods or services certified by the mark;
dd) Methods of evaluating characteristics of goods or services and methods of controlling the use of the mark;
e) Expenses paid by mark users for certification and protection of the mark, if any.
7. Documents proving permission for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing place names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property, including written permission for use in accordance with Point a and maps of corresponding geographical areas in accordance with Point b of this Clause.
a) Written permission for use of place names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing such elements, issued by the following competent authorities:

a1) People’s Committees of provinces or central-affiliated cities where there are geographical areas corresponding to place names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties (in case geographical areas belong to one province); a2) All People’s Committees of provinces or central-affiliated cities where there are geographical areas corresponding to place names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties (in case geographical areas belong to multiple provinces).
b) The map of the geographical area corresponding to another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties must fully present information which can be used to accurately determine such geographical area and must be certified by the competent authority specified in Point a of this Clause.
8. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date of notification. After the above time limit, if the applicant fails to submit supplementary documents satisfying the requirements, the application will continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application. Documents specified in this Clause may include the following documents:
a) Documents proving the applicant’s status:

a1) Certificate of enterprise registration, contract or other documents certifying the production of products and provision of services of the applicant as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a2) Agreement or written confirmation that the manufacturer does not use the mark and does not object to the registration of the mark of the person conducting commercial activities of the manufacturer as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a3) Decision or establishment license, organizational charter which may indicate information about the function and competence to manage collective marks, quality certification marks, geographical origin marking of goods and services as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a4) Agreement, certificate of enterprise registration, documents related to the mark registration of co-owners as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a5) Documents confirming that the applicant has received the mark registration right from another person as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a6) Agreement or written consent of the mark owner confirming the mark registration right of the representative or agent as prescribed in Clause 7 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 6septies of the Paris Convention.

a7) Documents proving that the applicant is an individual residing in Vietnam and is currently valid.
b) Documents proving the representative status of the applicant:

Original letter of attorney of the applicant; documents confirming that the applicant is the legal representative of the mark registration organization or the person authorized by such organization; documents confirming that the authorized person of the applicant meets the requirements to act as the representative of the applicant as prescribed in Article 6 of this Circular;
c) Documents proving the right to use or registration of marks containing special signs as prescribed in Clauses 2, 4 and 7 Article 73, Point p Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property; documents proving that the use of commercial indications, origin indications, prizes, medals or distinctive symbols of a certain product does not mislead or deceive consumers;
d) Documents proving priority rights;
dd) Documents proving the control and certification function of the certification mark registration organization;
e) Necessary information to clarify or confirm contents stated in regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks or other application documents;
g) Other appropriate documents to clarify the authenticity of information in the application.
Article 70. Receipt of applications for mark registration
1. An application for mark registration shall be received when it fully satisfies the following conditions:
a) It contains all minimum documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Mark samples enclosed (5 samples are required for paper applications);

a) Regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks for marks registered as collective marks or certification marks; a4) Documents proving the permission to register collective marks or certification marks containing geographical locations or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. In case an application meets all conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and verify the submission date and application number of such application and issue an application receipt to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number, and results of the verification of the list of application documents, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For applications submitted via the electronic application system, if the application is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue an application receipt to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number, and results of the verification of the list of application documents, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application system.
3. In case an application does not meet all conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall reject it (if the application is submitted by post or electronically via the electronic application system, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reasons for rejection). For applications submitted by post that are rejected, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return the application documents to the applicant, except for the original submitted for comparison.
4. Regarding documents proving the grounds for priority right, the applicant may submit additional documents within 3 months from the date of submission as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Section 2. VALIDATION OF APPLICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF MARKS
Article 71. Formal examination of applications for registration of marks
1. Formal examination of a mark registration application according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means an examination of compliance with regulations on form of the application in order to make a conclusion on whether the application is valid or not.
Formal examination of a certification mark or collective mark registration application does not include evaluation of specific characteristics of goods or services certified by the mark, methods for evaluating characteristics of goods or services and methods for controlling the use of the mark, costs paid by the mark user for certification and protection of the mark (if any), and conditions for using the mark, and measures for handling violations against regulations on use of the mark prescribed in regulations on use of such mark.
2. A mark registration application shall be considered invalid if it falls into one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the registration right as prescribed in Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed in contravention of Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is not protected by the State as prescribed in Articles 8 and 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
d) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed;
dd) The application fails to meet the requirements regarding form as prescribed in Article 69 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding the quantity of one of the mandatory documents; failing to meet the requirements regarding the presentation form; the declaration does not clearly state the registered mark, lacks the trademark description; fails to classify goods or services bearing the mark, or incorrectly classified and the applicant fails to pay classification fees; lacks translations of priority right documentation (if required), translations of registration documentation if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not certified as prescribed, the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the applicant, the representative, signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.
e) There is no valid authorization document (if the application is submitted through a representative).
3. Notification of the results of the formal examination shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the results of the formal examination, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the trademark registration certificate, the reasons for refusal or errors of the application (including the request for priority rights) and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinion or correct errors.

b) If the deadline specified at Point a of this Clause expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to make satisfactory corrections or does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection, within 15 working days from the expiration of the above-mentioned deadline, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the trademark registration certificate and send it to the applicant.
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article (or the applicant has made satisfactory corrections to deficiencies or provided a legitimate explanation), the application shall be determined as valid and published and subject to content appraisal in accordance with regulations. In case the request for priority rights is rejected, the application shall still be considered valid and be transferred for publication in accordance with regulations.
4. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of trademark registration applications that are transferred for publication, specifying the application number, filing date and date of transfer for publication of each application.
5. The filing date of a mark registration application is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam according to Clause 2 Article 70 hereof.
In case an application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative working hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date specified in this clause shall be the next working day.
6. The priority date of a mark registration application shall be determined as follows:
a/ If an application does not request priority right or although it requests priority right, it is not approved by the National Office of Industrial Property, such application shall be regarded as having no priority date;
b) If the application requests priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the National Office of Intellectual Property;
c) The priority date determined according to the request for priority rights on the basis of the first application(s) submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
7. In case an applicant requests to enjoy priority right but at the time of filing a mark registration application there is no document proving the priority right, the examination of the application shall start from the time the National Office of Industrial Property receives the supplementary documents or end within 3 months as prescribed in Clause 4, Article 70 of this Circular.
Article 72. Disclosure and publication of mark registration applications
1. Mark registration applications shall be disclosed immediately after they are received as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 70 of this Circular.
2. Valid mark registration applications shall be published under Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
3. Contents of a published mark registration application in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property include information about the applicant, the representative (if any), the application number, the filing date, the priority date (if any); information about the mark sample and the list of goods and services bearing the mark; international classification of goods and services; information about the origin of the application (application conversion, application splitting, original application number of the split application); and other information (if any).
Regulations on use of collective marks and regulations on use of certification marks shall be published on the website of the National Office of Industrial Property.
Section 3. EVALUATION OF CONTENTS OF MARK REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 73. Evaluation of contents of mark registration applications
1. Content appraisal of a mark registration application according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application according to protection conditions and determination of the corresponding scope of protection according to this Article.
2. Assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection title of a mark registration application includes:
a) Assessment of the conformity of the subject matter stated in the mark registration application with the requirements for grant of the certificate of mark registration as prescribed in Article 75 of this Circular;
b) Assessment of the subject matter stated in the mark registration application according to protection conditions as prescribed in Articles 76, 77 and 78 of this Circular. The assessment shall be carried out separately for each sign for each good or service in the list of goods and services;
c) Assessment of the first-to-file principle as prescribed in Article 79 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection title according to Point b Clause 2 of this Article shall be completed after the subject matter stated in the application has been assessed according to all protection conditions and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter fails to satisfy or satisfies the protection conditions, specifically:
a) There is a reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy one or several or all protection conditions; or

b) There is no reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Before issuing a notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of a certificate of mark registration as prescribed in Points a, d2, dd and g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine the principle of first-to-file according to Article 79 of this Circular.
5. Based on the results of assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding scope of intended protection.
6. The notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of a certificate of mark registration as prescribed in Points a, d2, dd and g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular shall be issued for the following applications for mark registration:
a) Applications not falling into the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Applications with the earliest submission date or priority date among applications for mark registration falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Applications under agreement specified in Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. Applications for mark registration not falling into the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article shall be handled as follows:
a) Applications for mark registration shall be rejected because they fail to satisfy the principle of first-to-file if they have the earliest submission date or priority date; or b) Applications for mark registration shall be considered as having the earliest submission date or priority date and handled as the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article if all other applications for mark registration with earlier submission dates or priority dates are rejected, withdrawn or considered as withdrawn.
8. During the process of substantive examination of an application, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations for the contents of the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify the deficiencies.
Article 74. Minimum information sources
1. In order to assess the likelihood of confusion of signs stated in a mark registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall, at least, conduct a search in the following minimum information sources:
a) Mark registration applications filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam with submission dates or priority dates earlier than or on the same day as the application is being examined, and international mark registration applications designating Vietnam which have been notified by the International Office of Vietnam of submission dates or priority dates earlier than or on the same day as the application is being examined for identical or similar goods or services;
b) Protected or recognized protected marks that are still valid in Vietnam and used for identical, similar or related goods or services;
c) Protected marks whose validity has expired within the last 3 years, except for marks whose validity has expired due to non-use as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property, and used for identical or similar goods or services;
d) Geographical indications currently under protection in Vietnam;
dd) Well-known marks already recognized according to regulations;
e) Place names, symbols or other signs indicating geographical origins of goods and services; geographical names, quality marks, inspection marks, warranty marks of international organizations; national flags, national emblems, national anthems of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and foreign countries, international anthem; flags, names, symbols of state agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations of Vietnam and the world; names and images of leaders, national heroes, names and images of Vietnamese and foreign celebrities, etc. collected and preserved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. Where necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may consult with sources of information other than those specified in Clause 1 of this Article, such as trade names, industrial designs, plant varieties that have been or are being protected in Vietnam; names and images of characters and symbols in works under copyright protection, etc.
Article 75. Assessment of the conformity of subject matters stated in applications for registration of marks The assessment of the conformity between subject matters stated in an application for registration of a mark and the request for grant of a certificate of registered mark shall be carried out as follows:

1. Under Clause 1, Article 72 of the Law on Intellectual Property, signs registered as marks must be visible signs in the form of letters, numbers, words, drawings, images, including three-dimensional figures or a combination thereof, expressed in one or several certain colors or sound signals expressed in graphics (specifically, 5-line music staves).
2. The following signs shall not be protected as marks:
a) Signs are color but not combined with signs in form of letters (signs in form of written words or numbers), signs in form of images or not presented as signs in form of letters or signs in form of images; or sound signs cannot be presented in graphic form (specifically, in form of 5-line music staves);
b) Signs which are subject to the non-protection as marks according to Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Signs contrary to social ethics and public order, harmful to national defense and security according to Article 8 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 76. Assessment of distinguishability of signs stated in applications
1. The assess of the distinguishability of letters as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be carried out as follows:
Except for exceptions prescribed in Clause 5 of this Article, the following letters shall be considered indistinguishable:
a) Characters belonging to languages which Vietnamese consumers have common knowledge cannot recognize or remember (cannot read, understand or remember), such as characters not derived from Latin: Arabic, Slavic, Sanskrit, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, etc.; unless the characters belong to the above-mentioned languages accompanied by other components that constitute an overall distinguishable structure or are presented in graphic form or in other special forms;
b) Characters derived from Latin but consisting of only one letter or only numbers or although consisting of two letters, they cannot be read into one syllable (for Vietnamese) or do not constitute a word for other common languages (English, French) - even when accompanied by numbers, except for cases where these signs are presented in graphic form or in other special forms;
c) A collection of too many letters or words making it impossible to recognize and remember as a sequence of too many characters not arranged in a definite order or rule or a document or a paragraph of a document;
d) Latin characters that are words with meanings which have been widely used and common in Vietnam in relevant fields but are no longer distinguishable;
dd) A word or a collection of words commonly used in Vietnam as names of goods or services;
e) A word or a collection of words describing the main goods or services bearing the trademark as a sign indicating time, location, method of production, type, quantity, quality, characteristics, components, utility, value of goods or services;
g) A word or a collection of words describing the geographical origin of goods or services;
h) A word or a collection of words describing legal form and business field of the trademark owner;
i) Signs consisting of letters falling under Points e, g, h, i, k, l, m, o, p Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. The assessment of distinguishability of graphic signs (including visual signs, drawings, shapes, etc.) according to Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be carried out as follows: Except for exceptions specified in Clause 5 of this Article, graphic signs shall be considered indistinguishable if:
a) Graphic signs are common shapes such as circles, ellipses, triangles, quadrilaterals, etc., or simple drawings; drawings or images are only used as backgrounds or decorative lines of products or product packages;
b) Signs are too complicated for consumers to easily recognize or remember their characteristics, including a large number of images and lines combined or superimposed on each other;
c) Drawings, images, symbols, typical signs, common shapes of goods or parts thereof, common shapes of packages or containers of goods that have been used regularly and widely recognized;
d) Signs depicting the main characteristics of goods or services bearing a mark, such as location, production method, type, quantity, quality, properties, components, uses, value, or other characteristics of goods or services bearing the mark, or signs that significantly increase the value of goods;
dd) Drawings and images describing the geographical origin of goods or services;
e) Signs that are identical or insignificantly different from industrial designs already or currently protected by others;
g) Signs falling under the cases specified in Points e, g, h, i, l, m, and p Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The ability to distinguish sounds is assessed as follows:
Except for exceptions prescribed in Clause 5 of this Article, sounds shall be considered indistinguishable if:
a) Common sounds used for goods or services or in production and business related to goods or services and become familiar to relevant public;
b) Sounds with too many melodies, timbres or too long which cannot be recognized and remembered;
c) Sounds too short and simple, including only 1 or 2 musical notes;
d) Sounds carrying contents describing main goods or services bearing marks as signs indicating time, location, production method, type, quantity, quality, nature, composition, utility, value or other characteristics of goods or services bearing marks;
dd) Sounds carrying contents describing geographical origin of goods or services;
e) Sounds having meanings describing legal form, business sector;
4. In case a sound mark contains language contents, such language contents shall be evaluated for distinguishability similar to evaluation of distinguishability of letter signs according to Clause 1 of this Article.
5. Exceptions applicable when evaluating distinguishability of letter signs, graphic signs and sound signs:
a) Signs falling into cases specified in Points a, b, c, e, and g Clause 1, Points a, b, d, and dd Clause 2, Points d and dd Clause 3 of this Article that have been used with the function of mark and are widely recognized by consumers as having achieved the distinguishability for related goods or services before the filing date. In order to be eligible for application of exceptions prescribed in this clause, applicants must provide evidence of widespread use of such signs with the function of mark (number of relevant consumers aware of it, starting time of use, scope and level of use before the filing date, sales volume from sale of goods or provision of services, etc.) and thus signs having the distinguishability for related goods or services of the applicant. In this case, signs must be continuously used and widely spread in lawful production, business, commercial activities, advertising, marketing in forms as shown in the registration application.
b) Signs specified in Point g Clause 1, Point dd Clause 2, Point dd Clause 3 of this Article shall be registered as certification marks or collective marks containing signs indicating geographical origins of goods or services.
c) Signs specified in Point g Clause 1, Point dd Clause 2, Point dd Clause 3 of this Article shall be registered as certification marks indicating geographical origins of goods or services or collective marks.
6. The assessment of the distinguishability of signs combined between characters and images (hereinafter referred to as “combined signs”) shall be carried out as follows:
A combined sign is considered having the distinguishability when the combination of characters and images forms an overall distinct sign, specifically:
a) Both characters and images have the distinguishability and form an overall distinct sign;
b) The strong component of a mark (an element strongly affecting the consumer’s perception, causing attention and impression about the mark upon hearing or seeing) is a character or image sign with the distinguishability, although the remaining components have little or no distinguishability;
c) In case a combination of signs including both characters and images is indistinguishable or has little distinguishable ability but the unique combination creates a distinct impression, such overall combination shall still be considered distinguishable;
d) A combination of signs including both characters and images is indistinguishable or has little distinguishable ability but the overall combination achieves distinguishable ability through use according to Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 77. Criteria for determining place names and other signs indicating geographical origins of goods and services

The determination of place names and other signs indicating geographical origins of goods and services according to Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 and Points dd and dd1 Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall comply with the following criteria:
1. Signs indicating geographical origins of goods and services are signs used for local goods and services and have the meaning of guiding geographical origins of goods and services (indicating that goods and services originate from such local areas).
Signs indicating geographical origins of goods and services are place names or symbolic signs of local areas (images of typical things of local areas, such as symbols, maps, flags, badges, scenic views, special works of local areas, etc.) or may be any other signs. Place names may be current names or historical names or official names or folk names of a geographical area being used to replace current place names or widely known place names (determined according to administrative boundaries or geographical methods).
2. A place name or symbolic sign of a local area used for ordinary goods and services (not specialties) may or may not have the meaning of guiding geographical origins of goods and services, depending on goods and services and actual use of the place name or symbolic sign of the local area.
3. A place name or symbolic sign of a local area has the meaning of guiding geographical origins of goods and services in the following cases:
a) Used for local specialties (specialized products with reputation due to certain characteristics and manufactured locally);
b) Used for local characteristic plants and animals and products processed from such plants and animals;
c) Used for products extracted from natural materials (coal, iron, steel, aluminum, cement, stone, salt, wood, etc.) in the locality;
d) Used for products of local developed industries;
dd) Used for specific services (services with reputation associated with certain characteristics in the locality);
e) Other cases determined according to products and actual use of local place names and symbolic signs for products.
4. Local place names and symbolic signs have no guiding meaning for geographical origins of goods or services in the following cases:
a) Have been used as common marks and widely recognized, i.e., achieving the guiding meaning of commercial origin (distinguishability) and losing the descriptive meaning of geographical origin, for example: Hanoi beer, Saigon beer;
b) The corresponding locality cannot be the place where goods are produced, e.g., Arctic cigarettes, etc. Place names and distinctive signs of a locality that have no meaning to indicate the geographical origin of goods or services may be protected as common marks without permission from local authorities.
5. Place names and distinctive signs of a locality which are part of common geographical knowledge and known by many people (e.g., names of provinces, cities, scenic spots) used for local common goods or services (including products with business advantages but not yet having reputation or quality characteristics) used by many local business entities for their goods or services to describe the place of production (but there is not enough basis to classify them in the cases specified in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article) are signs that are not protected as marks but can be used as an auxiliary element to form common marks of organizations or individuals in the corresponding localities, provided that such place names are excluded from the protection scope (not separately protected) and do not require permission from local authorities.
6. Signs indicating the geographical origin of Vietnam prescribed at Point dd1 Clause 1 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property are understood as signs consisting of letters with meanings indicating the name of Vietnam, including "Việt" or "Việt Nam", the corresponding English letters "Viet", "Vietnam", or "Viet Nam".
Article 78. Assessment of the likelihood of confusion of signs stated in applications
1. In case the sign stated in the application is a letter or a graphic symbol, the assessment of the similarity to the point of confusion with another mark for registration shall be carried out as follows:
a) In order to assess whether the sign requesting registration stated in the application is identical or similar to the point of confusion with another mark (hereinafter referred to as “comparison mark”), it is necessary to compare the pronunciation and meaning (regarding signs in the form of letters), the structure and method of presentation (regarding both signs in the form of letters and figures according to Points b and c of this Clause), and at the same time, to compare goods or services bearing the sign with those bearing the comparison mark according to Clause 3 of this Article;
b) Sign identical to the comparison mark: A sign shall be considered identical to the comparison mark if it is identical to the comparison mark in terms of structure and method of presentation;
c) Sign similar to the point of confusion with the comparison mark if:

c1) The sign is similar to the comparison mark in terms of structure or pronunciation or meaning or form of presentation to the point of confusion, making consumers mistakenly believe that these two subjects are one or that one subject is a variant of the other or that these two subjects have the same origin in commerce;

c2) The sign is merely an transcription or translation from the comparison mark if the comparison mark is a well-known mark.
2. In case the sign stated in the application is a sound sign, the assessment of the similarity to the point of confusion with another mark shall be carried out as follows:
a) In order to assess whether the sign requesting registration stated in the application is identical or similar to the point of confusion with the comparison mark, it is necessary to compare the sound elements (length, structure, components of sound; melody, rhythm, pattern, etc.) and language elements (if any) according to Points b and c of this Clause, and at the same time, to compare goods or services bearing the sign with those bearing the comparison mark according to Clause 3 of this Article;
b) Sign identical to the comparison mark: A sign shall be considered identical to the comparison mark if it is identical to the comparison mark in terms of sound elements and language elements;
c) A sign shall be considered similar to the point of confusion with a control mark if:

c1) The sign is similar to the control mark in terms of sound elements or language elements (pronunciation) or meaning to the point where consumers may mistakenly believe that these two subjects are one or that one subject is a variant of the other or that these two subjects have the same origin;

c2) The sign is just the pronunciation of the control mark if the control mark is a well-known mark.
3. The similarity between goods and services shall be assessed as follows:
a) Two goods or two services shall be considered identical (of the same type) when they have the following characteristics:

a1) They have the same nature (components, structure, etc.) and the same function and use purpose; or

a2) They have similar natures and the same function and use purpose;
b) Two goods or two services shall be considered similar if they fall into one of the following cases:

b1) They are similar in nature and are marketed through the same commercial channel (distributed by the same method, sold together or next to each other, in the same store, relevant public/related consumer, etc.);

b2) They are similar in function and use purpose and are marketed through the same commercial channel (distributed by the same method, sold together or next to each other, in the same store, relevant public/related consumer, etc.);
c) One good and one service shall be considered similar if they fall into one of the following cases:

c1) There is a relationship between them in terms of nature (the good or service or parts of this good or service are composed of the other good or service); or

c2) There is a relationship between them in terms of function (to complete the function of this good or service, the other good or service must be used or they are often used together); or

c3) There is a close relationship between them in terms of mode of performance (this good or service is the result of using or exploiting the other good or service, etc.).
4. Signs shall be considered identical or similar to the point of confusion with control marks for identical or similar goods in the following cases:
a) Signs identical with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of goods or services bearing the control mark;
b) Signs similar to the point of confusion with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of goods or services bearing the control mark;
c) Signs identical or similar to the control mark being a well-known mark and goods or services bearing signs not identical or similar to those of goods or services bearing such mark, but the use of such signs as marks may mislead consumers into believing that there is a relationship between goods or services bearing such signs and the owner of the well-known mark, which may actually reduce the distinguishability of the well-known mark or damage the reputation of the well-known mark.
5. The appraisal of other points of confusion of signs shall comply with Article 73 and Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following specific regulations:
a) Signs considered as causing confusion as to the origin of goods or services in the following cases:

a1) Signs identical or confusingly similar to the name or symbol of a country or territory (national flag, national emblem, national name, place name, local name, local symbol, etc.) which causes the misperception that the goods or services bearing such signs originate from such country or territory but actually originate from another country or territory;

a2) Signs identical or similar to geographical indications currently under protection if the use of such signs may mislead consumers as to the geographical origin of goods; signs identical to geographical indications or containing geographical indications or being translated or transcribed from geographical indications currently under protection for wines and spirits if the signs requesting registration as marks for wines or spirits do not originate from the geographical areas bearing such geographical indications;

a3) Signs identical or similar to trade names of other persons that have been lawfully used for the same type of goods or services and may cause consumers to misperceive that the goods or services bearing such signs are produced or provided by the persons with such trade names;

a4) Signs identical or similar to real names, aliases, pen names or images of Vietnamese or foreign leaders, national heroes or famous personalities; signs identical or similar to names or images of characters or typical images of works widely known if the use of such signs may mislead consumers as to the misperception that the goods or services bearing such signs are produced or provided by the owners of such works;

a5) Signs identical or insignificantly different from industrial designs of other persons that have been and are protected on the basis of applications for registration of industrial designs with submission dates or priority dates earlier than those of applications for registration of marks.
b) In the following cases, signs shall be considered likely to cause confusion or misunderstanding as to the nature and value of goods or services:

b1) Signs which are words, drawings, images, symbols, sounds, etc. and which create misleading impressions about the features and utility of goods or services, such as signs identical with or similar to marks or other signs widely used to the extent that they are considered associated with a function or utility of a certain type of goods or service, causing consumers to mistakenly believe that goods or services bearing such signs also have such features or utilities;

b2) Signs which are words, images, sounds and which create misleading impressions about the composition or structure of goods or services, such as descriptions of other goods or services related to goods or services bearing such signs, creating misleading impressions that goods or services bearing such signs are made from or of the same nature as the described goods or services.
Article 79. Assessment of the first-to-file principle
For mark registration applications already concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing content appraisal results and intending to grant mark registration certificates according to Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examinations to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:
1. To examine the first-to-file principle, it is required to search all mark registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (by the time of examination) with an application submission date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority rights) earlier or on the same day as the examination of the current application.
2. The search is to find cases where there are many applications (including the currently examined application) filed by different persons for marks identical or confusingly similar to each other for identical or similar goods or services, or many applications filed by the same person for identical marks for identical goods or services; and determine the application with the earliest application submission date or priority date.
3. If there are many applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the certificate of mark registration shall be granted only to the mark in the valid application with the earliest submission date or priority date among the applications satisfying the conditions for grant of the certificate of mark registration.
4. Among the applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, if there are many applications with the same earliest submission date or priority date, the certificate of mark registration shall be granted only to the mark of a single application among such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all corresponding subjects of such applications shall be rejected from the grant of the certificate of mark registration.
Article 80. Grant and refusal to grant certificates of mark registration
1. Mark registration applications shall be refused the grant of certificates of mark registration in the cases specified at Points a, b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Mark registration applications shall be granted certificates of mark registration when they satisfy the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. Mark registration applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article shall be subject to the following tasks by the National Office of Intellectual Property:
a/ If the subject matter stated in an application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of a certificate of mark registration and fixing a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay a fee for the grant of a certificate of mark registration, a disclosure fee for the decision on grant of a certificate of mark registration and a registration fee for the decision on grant of a certificate of mark registration;
b) If the subject matters of the application for protection do not satisfy the protection conditions or fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1 Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, or if they satisfy the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the trademark registration certificate, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies in the application, and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide their opinion;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or does not provide any objection within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the trademark registration certificate;
d) In case the applicant corrects deficiencies or provides an objection within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 50 days from the receipt of the document on correction of deficiencies or the objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct the content appraisal as follows:

d1) If the correction is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the trademark registration certificate;

d2) If the applicant has satisfactorily corrected deficiencies or provided a justifiable objection to all or part of the content appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended grant of the trademark registration certificate to the part or all of the application that satisfies the protection conditions, and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for grant of the trademark registration certificate, the disclosure fee for the decision on grant, and the registration fee for the decision on grant of the trademark registration certificate;
dd) If the subject matters of the protection request stated in the application include goods or services not eligible for protection or have elements in the mark sample not separately protected (and the applicant fails to exclude them by themselves or exclude them improperly), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of the Certificate of Mark Registration for the eligible parts, fees and charges for the grant of the Certificate of Mark Registration and other fees and charges as prescribed that the applicant must pay in case of agreement with all content appraisal results of the application, reasons for refusal to grant for the remaining parts, non-protection elements (if any), and at the same time impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide written opinions;
e) In case the applicant does not provide opinions or does not provide written consent and fully pays the fees and charges within the time limit specified at Point dd of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the Certificate of Mark Registration;
g) In case the applicant submits written opinions within the time limit specified in Point dd of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the document, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall proceed as follows:

g1) If the applicant has a justifiable objection to the entire appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the trademark registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the trademark registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the record registration fee for the decision on issuance of the trademark registration certificate;

g2) If the applicant has an objection but it is unjustified or provides partial justification for the appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the trademark registration certificate for the part meeting the protection conditions, the reasons for refusal to issue for the remaining part or non-protection elements (if any), and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the trademark registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the record registration fee for the decision on issuance of the trademark registration certificate;

h) In case the applicant fails to fully pay the fees and charges within the time limit specified in Points a, d2, g1, g2 of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the trademark registration certificate;
i) In case the applicant fully and timely pays the fees and charges as prescribed in Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 of this Clause and submits a written consent to all appraisal results for the case specified in Point dd of this Clause, within 1 month from the date of full payment of fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to issue the trademark registration certificate with the part meeting the protection conditions, non-protection elements (if any), refuse to issue for the part not meeting the protection conditions (if any), clearly stating the reasons for the refused contents (if any).
4. The decision on grant of a certificate of mark registration shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance. Published contents include information in the decision on grant of a certificate of mark registration (including the decision number and issuance date) and the contents specified in Clause 3 Article 72 of this Circular.
Regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks shall be published on the web portal of the National Office of Intellectual Property.
5. A certificate of mark registration made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP may be issued in electronic form and paper form (if the applicant requests a paper copy). In case industrial property rights to a mark are under joint ownership, the certificate of mark registration may only be granted to the first person on the list of joint applicants.
A copy of the certificate of mark registration made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP may be issued by the National Office of Intellectual Property if the applicant requests a copy (specified in the declaration for mark registration) for other co-owners.
Section 4. PROCESSING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO MARK REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS AND RECORDING OF POPULAR MARKS
Article 81. Processing of requests for rapid content appraisal of mark registration applications
1. Requests for rapid content appraisal of mark registration applications shall be received when the following documents are fully provided and the applicant pays the fee and charge for such rapid content appraisal request:
a) Request form for rapid content appraisal, made according to Form No. 14 specified in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the registered mark falls under one of the cases specified in Point a Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
2. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall receive and process requests for fast content appraisal of all documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article as follows:
a) In case the request for fast content appraisal fails to meet the requirements specified in Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, within 15 working days from the receipt of the request, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall notify the refusal to accept the request for fast content appraisal and continue to process the related mark registration application as prescribed;
b) In case the request for fast content appraisal meets the requirements specified in Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the request for fast content appraisal shall be published at the same time as the mark registration application and the content appraisal as prescribed in this Circular within the time limit specified in Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, except where the mark registration application is determined to be invalid according to Article 71 of this Circular or the application falls under the cases specified in Points b and c Clause 4 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Article 82. Handling of objections to mark registration applications
1. Within 3 months from the date on which the mark registration application is published, any third party has the right to object to the grant of the Certificate of Mark Registration and must pay fees and charges. The objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese enclosed with documents or citing sources of information to prove it. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. In case the objection to the mark registration application meets the requirements specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall receive and handle the objection as prescribed below, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 01 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of the objection and impose a time limit of 01 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response. The objection shall be considered in the process of substantive examination if:

a1) The mark and goods or services in the application are identical to those provided by the objector or have grounds to conclude that they are similar enough to cause confusion or not similar enough to cause confusion with the mark and goods or services provided by the objector;

a2) Other cases where there are clear grounds to conclude that the objection is unjustified;
b) After receiving the feedback from the applicant within the time limit specified in point a clause 2 of this Article, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of the feedback and impose a time limit of 01 month from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to the objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) Based on information, evidence, arguments provided by the parties, the results of the dialogue (if any) as prescribed in points a, b, and c of this clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall handle the objection and notify the results of handling the objection together with the results of substantive examination of the corresponding application to the objector, except for the case specified in point dd of this clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the termination of the handling of the objection to the objector regarding the withdrawal of the mark registration application according to Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 01 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification of acceptance of the withdrawal of the application.
3. In case the objection relates to the registration right, the objection shall be handled as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the objector to file a lawsuit petition to a competent Court according to regulations of law on civil procedures and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the objector to send copies of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit, except for the following cases where the objection is handled during the content appraisal:

a1) There are clear grounds to determine that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 7 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a2) The objection to the right to file a mark registration application with signs being or containing signs being place names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a3) The objection to the right to file an application without accompanying documents or sources of information cited for proof or with accompanying documents or sources of information cited but the documents or sources of information are not sufficient to determine that the applicant does not have registration rights;
b) Upon expiration of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if the objector fails to send copies of the notice of acceptance of the lawsuit or the objection, such copies shall be considered withdrawn, and the mark registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives copies of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the mark registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the Court, except for the case specified at Point dd Clause 2 of this Article. The application processing shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the Court.
4. The time limit for the applicant to respond to the objection and the time limit for the objector to respond to the applicant's opinion as prescribed in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal of the application.
Article 83. Amendment to mark registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of trademark registration, the applicant may take initiative or based on the notice of appraisal results to amend or supplement documents in the application for trademark registration.
2. The request for amendment or supplementation of an application for trademark registration shall include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment or supplementation of the application by the applicant, the request for amendment or supplementation must be made using Form No. 10 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of amendment or supplementation of the application based on the notice of appraisal results from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the amendment or supplementation shall be made in writing with the content of amendment or supplementation clearly stated;
c) The applicant may request amendment or supplementation with the same content related to multiple applications for trademark registration in one declaration or one written request for amendment or supplementation;
d) In case of amendment or supplementation related to the trademark sample, the amended trademark samples (5 samples in case of paper application) or the list of goods and services must be submitted together with the detailed presentation of the amended content compared to the initial submitted documents. In case the applicant takes initiative in amendment or supplementation without protecting the exclusion elements in the trademark sample, the applicant must clearly state them in the declaration of amendment;

dd) In case of change to name, address or country code of the applicant, the applicant must submit confirmation documents (original copies or certified true copies) or legal documents (certified true copies) proving the change (decision on change to name, address; certificate of enterprise registration which records the change to name, address, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for actively extracting and using information available in databases announced by database management agencies to replace components of application for handling administrative procedures. In case where information cannot be extracted or the extracted information is insufficient or inaccurate, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to individuals or organizations to supplement application components and clearly state reasons. The time limit for requesting and supplementing applications shall not be included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
e) In case of change to industrial property representative, the applicant must submit a document declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representative and change to the new industrial property representative as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. The applicant must pay an appraisal fee for the request for amendment to each amended content and a disclosure fee for the amended content of the application if such amended content must be disclosed as prescribed in Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except for cases where the amendments are made to correct errors made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Requests for amendment to mark registration applications shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection already disclosed in the mark samples and the list of goods and services stated in the applications and shall not change the nature of the subject matters of the registration requests stated in the applications.
5. In case of amendment to the application by the applicant, the request for amendment shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment to the application satisfies the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice accepting the request for amendment to the application (except where the amendment is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant a certificate of registered mark due to invalidation of the application).
The amended content shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette in case the request for amendment to information related to the application that is valid in terms of form includes the sample of the mark and the list of goods and services enclosed;
b) If the request for amendment to the application does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice intending to refuse the request for amendment to the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the set time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection or inappropriate objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice refusing the request for amendment to the application;
c) In cases where the request for amendment to the application relates to the sample of the mark, the list of goods and services bearing the mark, changes to information of the applicant (in cases of collective mark registration, certification mark registration), regulations on use of collective marks, or regulations on use of certification marks, which are submitted after receiving a notice intending to grant a certificate of registered mark, the mark registration application must be re-examined and the applicant must pay fees as prescribed.
6. In cases where the applicant makes amendments to the application based on the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam's notice of appraisal results, the acceptance or refusal to accept the applicant's request for amendment to the application shall be carried out by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the notice of appraisal results or the corresponding decision during the processing of the mark registration application.
7. In case an applicant requests amendment to his/her application including amendments made by him/her and amendments to the application based on notification of content appraisal results, such request shall be made in the form specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article. The handling of such request shall comply with regulations in Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 84. Recording of changes of applicants for mark registration
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of mark registration, an applicant may request recording of changes of the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, or according to decision of a competent authority.
2. A request for recording of changes of the applicant for mark registration must satisfy the following requirements:
a) The request for recording of changes of the applicant shall be made using Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to transfer of the application, the applicant must submit documents showing the transfer of the mark registration application (original or certified true copy), including name and address of the transferring party and the receiving party; number of transferred application or information sufficient to identify the application. Documents with multiple pages must bear signatures of the parties or have overlapping seals on every two adjacent pages.

The receiving party must satisfy conditions applicable to persons having registration rights as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to inheritance, succession, or according to decision of a competent authority, the applicant must submit documents proving related to inheritance or succession of assets upon consolidation, merger, division, separation of legal entities, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entities under the same ownership, business format conversion, or according to decision of a court or other competent authorities, or inheritance due to abandonment of registration rights by another applicant;
d) The applicant shall pay fees and charges as prescribed;
dd) An application for recognition of changes due to transfer of multiple applications from the same applicant may be filed in the same declaration, provided that an appraisal fee is paid according to regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for recognition of transfer.
3. An application for recognition of changes of the applicant shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for recognition of changes of the applicant satisfies the provisions of clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of recognition of changes of the applicant and publish it on the Industrial Property Official Gazette (except for cases where the request for recognition of changes of the applicant is filed before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the protection title due to invalidation of the application in terms of form);
b) If the request for recognition of changes of the applicant does not satisfy the provisions of clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize changes of the applicant and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection, or provides unjustifiable objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to recognize changes of the applicant;
c) In case of a request for recognition of changes due to transfer of a trademark registration application filed after a notice of intended issuance of the trademark registration certificate is issued, the trademark registration application must be re-appraised and the transferred content must be published. The applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee and a publication fee as prescribed.
Article 85. Splitting of trademark registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a trademark registration certificate, the applicant may take the initiative or based on the appraisal results notification, split part of the list of goods and services in the submitted trademark registration application (initial application) into one or more new trademark registration applications (split applications).
2. For a split-up application, the applicant shall submit a declaration of registration of new mark to the list of goods and services split up. The split-up application shall bear a new serial number and the filing date of the initial mark registration application or (priority dates) of the initial mark registration application (if any). The applicant shall pay the filing fee and all charges and fees for procedures performed for the split-up application other than the procedures already performed in the initial application without having to perform again in the split-up application and is not required to pay the charge for examination of the request for priority right.
3. For an initial application, the applicant shall carry out the procedures for amendment and supplementation of the application as prescribed in Article 83 of this Circular.
4. Split-up applications and initial applications shall be processed as follows:
a/ Split-up applications shall be appraised for form and further processed according to the unfinished procedures for initial applications. Split-up applications shall be published under regulations;
b/ Initial applications shall be further processed under Article 83 of this Circular.
Article 86. Withdrawal of mark registration applications
1. Before the National Office of Industrial Property decides to grant or refuses to grant a certificate of mark registration, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the mark registration application.
2. The withdrawal of a mark registration application shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative through a written declaration. For an application filed through a representative, the power of attorney must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or enclosed with a command specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 02 months from the date of receipt of the declaration on withdrawal of the mark registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal request as follows:
a) In case the withdrawal request satisfies the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the withdrawal request and simultaneously terminate the processing of the application. The withdrawn mark registration application cannot be re-registered but can only be used as a basis for requesting priority rights;

b) In case the withdrawal request does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a 01-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements after correction, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request within 15 working days from the expiration of such time limit.
Article 87. Re-examination of mark registration applications
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct the re-examination of mark registration applications in the following cases:
a) The re-examination of a mark registration application is conducted due to a written objection from a third party against the intended grant of a certificate of mark registration in the notice of content examination results, together with legitimate evidence proving that the objection arises from a force majeure event or an objective obstacle, resulting in the inability to object to the application within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The re-examination of an application is conducted due to a request for amendment or supplementation of the application, or recognition of changes to the applicant after notification of content examination results, which indicates the intended grant of a protection title according to the provisions of point c clause 5 Article 83 and point c clause 3 Article 84 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of a mark registration application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of a mark registration application shall comply with Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complicated cases involving multiple facts requiring verification or expert opinions, the time limit for re-examination may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial examination prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The contents of the re-examination shall comply with corresponding provisions in Section 3 Chapter V of this Circular;
c) For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of the mark registration application shall only be carried out once by a third party.
3. After obtaining the results of the re-examination, the mark registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For the case specified at Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the results of the re-examination, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application in accordance with this Circular;
b) For the case specified at Point b Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application in accordance with Article 80 of this Circular.
Article 88. Recognition of well-known marks
1. The right to a well-known mark shall be protected and belong to its owner without registration procedures. The mark owner may use documents specified in Clause 2 of this Article to prove his ownership of the mark and to prove that the mark satisfies the conditions for being considered well-known.
2. Documents proving the ownership of a mark and proving the popularity of the mark may include information on the scope, scale, level and continuity of use of the mark, including an introduction on the origin, history and period of continuous use of the mark; the number of countries where the mark has been registered or recognized as a well-known mark; the list of goods and services bearing the mark; the territorial scope over which the mark is circulated, sales volume of products or service provision; the quantity of goods and services bearing the mark that have been manufactured and sold; the value of assets of the mark, the price of transfer or licensing of the mark, the investment capital contribution value of the mark; investment and costs for advertising and marketing of the mark, including participation in national and international exhibitions; infringement cases, disputes and decisions and judgments of courts or competent authorities; survey data on consumers’ knowledge of the mark through purchase, use and advertising and marketing; rankings and assessments of the reputation of the mark by national and international organizations and mass media; prizes and medals obtained by the mark; assessment results of intellectual property assessment organizations.
3. In case a well-known mark is recognized, leading to a decision to handle an infringement upon rights to such well-known mark according to Point d Clause 1 Article 129 of the Law on Intellectual Property or leading to a decision not to protect another mark according to Point i Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property, such well-known mark shall be recorded in the List of well-known marks kept at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam for use as reference information in the work of establishing and protecting intellectual property rights.
Section 5. MARRAKECH APPLICATIONS AND HANDLING OF MARRAKECH APPLICATIONS
Article 89. MARRAKECH APPLICATIONS

1. A MARRAKECH application includes a Vietnamese origin MARRAKECH application and a Vietnamese designated MARRAKECH application.
2. For a Vietnamese origin MARRAKECH application, the applicant must submit it through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and pay fees for carrying out procedures for international registration of marks of Vietnamese origin.
3. A Vietnamese origin MARRAKECH application includes the following documents:
a) Declaration for international registration of a mark of Vietnamese origin, made according to Form No. 7 in Vietnamese in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) MM2 declaration, made according to the form of the International Bureau in English or French (2 declarations shall be submitted in paper applications);
c) Mark samples identical to the marks in the application submitted in Vietnam (base application) or certificate of mark registration (registration application) (2 mark samples shall be submitted in paper applications);
d) The application form MM18 made in English if the application is filed through the United States (2 application forms shall be submitted in case of paper application);
dd) A written authorization made in Vietnamese (if the application is filed through a representative);
e) Other relevant documents (if any).
4. An application form for Madrid origin filed in Vietnam must meet the requirements in terms of format and content as prescribed. The applicant must fully and accurately fill in the information in the declaration, and agree with the information stated in the base or registered facility.
5. For an application form for Madrid origin filed through a representative in Vietnam, the international registration date or the later appointment date (in case of later appointment) shall be considered as the submission date in Vietnam and the application shall be eligible for the corresponding priority date (if any).
Article 90. Processing of applications for Madrid origin filed in Vietnam and related requirements
1. After receiving an application form for Madrid origin filed in Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall appraise it to determine whether it meets the requirements specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 89 of this Circular and carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the application has errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant to correct such errors. If the applicant fails to correct such errors within 3 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification, the application shall be considered as withdrawn;
b) In case the application is flawless or has flaws but the applicant has repaired them to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify fees and charges that the applicant must pay directly to the International Office, sign and send the application to the International Office within 15 working days from the date of notification of fees and charges;
c) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the Madrid application originating from Vietnam shall be considered as the international registration date of such application in case the International Office receives the application within 02 months from the date written on the receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the application is not complete for sending to the International Office within the above time limit, the date of receipt of the application at the International Office shall be considered as the international registration date.
2. After the Madrid application originating from Vietnam is submitted to the International Office, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant and continue processing (cooperating with the applicant if necessary) notifications and requests from the International Office or other matters related to the application (if any).
3. Requests after the Madrid application originating from Vietnam is granted with international registration number as follows (expansion of protection territory), change of name and address of owner of international registration, limitation of goods and service list, extension of validity of international registration, designation of representative, change of representative, recognition of transfer of international registration, etc. can be made directly to the International Office or through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case of submission through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the applicant needs to submit the following documents and pay corresponding appraisal fee for relevant request:
a) Application form for request using Form No. 08 in Vietnamese in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Corresponding application form of the International Office (02 applications in case of paper application);
c) Power of attorney in Vietnamese (in case the request is submitted through the representative);
d) Other relevant documents (if any).
4. After receiving the requests specified in Clause 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the application has errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant to correct such errors. If the applicant fails to correct such errors within 03 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification, the request shall be considered as withdrawn;

b) In case the application has no errors or errors have been corrected by the applicant and the application is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify fees to the applicant for direct payment to the International Bureau, sign the request and transfer the request to the International Bureau within 10 working days from the date of issuance of the fee notification;
c) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the subsequent request shall be considered as the subsequent date of international registration if the International Bureau receives such request within 02 months from the date written on the receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the request is not completed before being sent to the International Bureau within the above-mentioned time limit, the date on which the request is received at the International Bureau shall be considered as the subsequent date of international registration.
5. In case of an international registration extension request submitted through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the applicant must submit it within 06 months and not later than 01 month from the expiration date of the international registration. In case of an international registration validity extension during the grace period, the application shall be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 01 month from the expiration date of the grace period.
Article 91. Processing of Madrid-based applications with designated Vietnam
1. After receiving the notification of the International Bureau of the Madrid-based application with designated Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct content appraisal of the application like that of a mark registration application submitted under the national procedure, except for cases specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 12 of this Article. Within 12 months from the date on which the International Bureau issues the notification, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conclude the protectionability of the mark.
2. In case a mark satisfies the protection conditions prescribed by Vietnamese law, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Before the expiration of the 12-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to accept protection for the internationally registered mark in Vietnam, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of Internationally Registered Marks), and send it to the International Office declaring protection;
b) Publish the decision in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance.
The scope (volume) of protection is determined according to the contents requested in the international registration of the mark already recorded by the International Office and accepted by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
3. In case a mark does not satisfy the protection conditions for part or all of goods or services, or a mark satisfies the protection conditions but has deficiencies in its international registration (lack of regulations on use of collective marks, certification marks, lack of photos or drawings showing the three-dimensional perspective of the mark, application submission rights, list of vague goods and services, etc.), before the expiration of the 12-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of provisional refusal, clearly stating the contents (part or all of the list of goods and services subject to the refusal) and the reasons for the provisional refusal, and send such notice to the International Office.
4. In case a mark contains an exclusion element which the applicant has not stated in the application or has stated but incorrectly, before the expiration of the 12-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of provisional refusal and send such notice to the International Office.
5. Within 3 months from the date the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notice of provisional refusal specified in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article, the applicant may correct deficiencies or oppose the intended refusal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
The correction of errors or objection to the intended refusal shall be carried out according to the procedures applicable to mark registration applications filed under the national filing format, including regulations on the application submission method.
6. For marks temporarily refused under Clause 3 of this Article, where the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article expires and the applicant has corrected errors as required and/or provided a legitimate objection to the notice of temporary refusal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Issue a decision to accept protection for internationally registered marks in Vietnam with a protection scope (volume) corresponding to goods or services meeting the protection conditions; record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of Internationally Registered Marks); and send it to the International Office for Protection Declaration after the notice of temporary refusal;
b) Publish the decision in the Industrial Property Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance.
7. For marks temporarily refused under Clause 3 of this Article, where the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article expires and the applicant fails to correct errors as required, provides no objection, or provides an illegitimate objection to the notice of temporary refusal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Where the mark does not meet the protection conditions for a part of the list of goods or services, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply the procedures specified in Clause 6 of this Article to the goods or services meeting the protection conditions (goods or services not mentioned in the notice of temporary refusal);
b) Where the mark does not meet the protection conditions for the entire list of goods or services, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse acceptance of protection for internationally registered marks in Vietnam and send it to the International Office for Confirmation of Refusal of the entire list;
c) In case there are grounds to determine that the mark satisfies the protection conditions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article to the protected goods or services.
8. For marks temporarily rejected under Clause 4 of this Article, after the expiration of the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the applicant does not have any objection or agrees with the contents stated in the notice of temporary rejection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article to the exclusion elements stated in the notice of temporary rejection;
b) In case the applicant has a justifiable objection to all or part of the contents stated in the notice of temporary rejection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article to the corresponding exclusion elements;
c) In case the applicant has an unjustifiable objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Points a and b Clause 7 of this Article.
9. In case the international registration for a mark registered in Vietnam is rejected before the decision on acceptance or refusal of protection is issued, or the international registration is not extended or invalidated, the entire list of goods and services and the international registration application shall be considered as withdrawn.
10. Procedures for filing and settling complaints about decisions prescribed in Point a Clause 2, Clauses 6, 7 and 8 of this Article shall be carried out as for applications for registration of marks filed under the national procedure if there are grounds to believe that such decisions are issued inconsistently with regulations of law on contents and issuance order. Results of complaint settlement shall be notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to applicants. In case after receiving results of complaint settlement, part or all of the list of goods or services subject to refusal in the refusal decisions is accepted for protection or there is a change in the exclusion elements (not separately protected), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send to the International Office the next decision affecting the protection of marks according to the form of the International Office regarding corresponding contents.
11. From the date of international registration of a mark accepted for protection in Vietnam, at the request of the mark owner, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a certificate of registered international mark protected in Vietnam, provided that the applicant pays fees and charges as prescribed.
12. From the date on which the Madrid application is published by the International Office in the Official Gazette before the date of issuance of the decision to accept protection, or 12 months from the date on which the International Office notifies the appointment of Vietnam, whichever comes first, if the third party has opinions about the Madrid application with appointment of Vietnam, these opinions shall be regarded as reference information sources during the processing of the application.
13. Contents of publication prescribed in Point b Clause 2, Point b Clause 6 of this Article include basic information specified in the corresponding decision (including decision number and date) and other information prescribed in Clause 3 Article 72 of this Circular.
Article 92. Conversion of international registration of marks into applications filed under the national procedure
1. In case of international registration of a mark in Vietnam by an individual or organization being a member of the Madrid Protocol which is invalidated according to Article 6 of the Madrid Protocol, such individual or organization may file an application for conversion to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to register protection for such mark for part or all of goods or services on the list of goods and services recorded in the international registration of the mark but invalidated according to Article 9 quinquies of the Madrid Protocol. An application for conversion of a mark shall be considered valid if it satisfies the following conditions:
a) The application is filed within 3 months from the date of recording in the international register of the corresponding international registration which is invalidated;
b) The list of goods and services subject to the application for conversion of the mark has never been subject to a decision on refusal, termination or cancellation in Vietnam;
c) The application is made using Form No. 9 in Appendix I of this Circular (in which the list of goods and services in Vietnamese in the conversion application must be smaller than or equal to the list of goods and services invalidated in the corresponding international registration);
d) The application satisfies all other requirements regarding form for mark registration applications as prescribed by Vietnamese law;
dd) The applicant fully pays fees and charges as prescribed for mark registration applications filed under the national filing form; in cases specified at Point b Clause 2 of this Article, the applicant is not required to pay the application disclosure fee and the lookup fee for application appraisal.
The application for conversion of a mark shall have the filing date recorded as the date of international registration or the later date designated (in case of later designation in Vietnam). In case of international registration enjoying priority rights under an international treaty, the application for conversion of a mark shall have the corresponding priority right recorded, unless there are grounds to reject it.
2. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall appraise the application for conversion of a mark according to regulations on conversion conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article and the following principles:
a) Regarding the form elements already accepted by the International Office in the corresponding international registration, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall not conduct re-examination, except for cases where the application has deficiencies (such as lack of regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks, or photos or drawings showing the three-dimensional perspective of marks, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the mark registration certificate in case the application fails to meet the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article;
b) Regarding applications for mark registration converted from international registrations already accepted for protection in Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall not conduct content re-examination. In cases where the application meets the conversion conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures to decide the grant of the protection title, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property, and publish the decision in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property as for applications submitted under the national procedure;
c) For valid applications for mark registration conversion not falling under the case specified at Point b of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures to determine the validity of the application, publish the application, conduct content examination, and follow subsequent procedures as for applications for mark registration submitted under the national procedure.
Chapter VI
                          PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATIONS
Section 1. APPLICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATIONS
Article 93. Applications for registration of geographical indications
An application for registration of a geographical indication shall include the following documents:
1. Declaration for registration of a GIP, made using Form No. 05 in Appendix I of this Circular.
2. Description of peculiar characteristics and quality, reputation of products bearing GIP and elements of natural conditions creating such peculiar characteristics and quality, reputation (hereinafter referred to as “description of peculiar characteristics”).
3. Map of geographical area corresponding to the GIP.
4. Sample of GIP in case the GIP is not a word (05 samples in case the application is submitted in paper form).
5. Documents proving that the GIP is being protected in the country where such GIP is located if it is a foreign GIP.
6. Presentation documents on use conditions and method of presentation of GIP to ensure the ability to distinguish between GIPs in case the registered GIP is homonym GIP.
7. Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative).
8. Other documents supplementing the application (if any).
Article 94. Requirements of applications for geographical indication registration
1. Documents included in an application for geographical indication registration must meet specific requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of this Article and general requirements regarding format as follows:
a) Documents shall be presented in horizontal orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in vertical orientation) on one sheet of paper of A4 size (210mm x 297mm); maps of geographical areas may be presented on one sheet of paper of A3 size (420mm x 297mm) with four margins of 20mm each, using Times New Roman font, font size not smaller than 13, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b) Documents containing multiple pages shall have page numbers written in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents shall be typed or printed using opaque ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. Documents shall bear signatures and seals; if the documents contain at least 2 pages, signatures of the applicant or the issuing authority (if any) shall be affixed on every two adjacent pages, except for electronic documents bearing digital signatures. Before receiving the application, if any spelling mistake is found, the applicant may correct it and affix signature (and seal, if any) at the corrected position; the correction must not obscure the contents of the documents;

d) Documents included in the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system shall be in the “.pdf” format; name of the electronic document shall correspond to name of the document type as per regulation; individuals signing documents included in the application for geographical indication registration may use digital signatures to directly sign on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan the physical document in color mode in case of submission through VneID application;
d) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not use local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 7 and 8 Article 93 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese when requested;
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure full and consistent mandatory information.
2. The geographical indication registration application must ensure consistency as prescribed in Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. Each application may register only one geographical indication for a product determined by its nature, characteristics and relationship to a geographical area, regardless of its form of existence or processing level.
3. The geographical indication registration declaration must be fully filled out with information as required at appropriate places in the declaration.
4. The geographical indication sample (for cases of geographical indications that are not words) must be presented clearly with each sample not larger than 80mm x 80mm and not smaller than 20mm x 20mm.
5. The description of peculiar characteristics must include the following contents:
a) The detailed content description, including the following main information:

a1) Information on geographical indications and products bearing geographical indications;

a2) Characteristics and specific quality of products bearing geographical indications determined by geographical conditions - determined by perceptible, qualitative, physical, chemical and biological quantitative indicators. These indicators must be identifiable and verifiable by technical means or experts according to sampling standards and testing methods; and/or

a3) Reputation of products bearing geographical indications determined by geographical conditions through widespread knowledge about such products and selection of such products among relevant consumers, which can be verified; and

a4) Geographical area;

a5) Geographical conditions creating characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications, including climatic, hydrological, geological, topographical and ecological factors and other natural conditions; skills and techniques of producers, including traditional production processes of local areas (including one, several or all stages from raw material production and processing to product creation and may also include product packaging if such packaging affects the characteristics, quality and reputation of products), where such processes are elements creating and maintaining characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications, including clear and detailed information that can be verified (if there is any secret or technical secret not yet disclosed or widely known outside of local areas in the above-mentioned information, the applicant has the right to refuse to provide detailed information about such secrets or techniques if he/she does not commit to keep such information confidential at his/her request); and

a6) Organic relationship between characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications mentioned in points a2 and a3 of this clause with geographical conditions mentioned in point a5 of this clause;

a7) Information on the mechanism for self-inspection of characteristics and specific quality of products;
b) Summary section, including summary of specific characteristics, quality, reputation of products, geographical conditions contributing to specific characteristics, quality, and reputation of products, and organic relationship between specific characteristics, quality, and reputation of products bearing GIs and geographical conditions.
6. Description of specific characteristics according to Clause 5 of this Article must be accompanied by documents proving that information on characteristics, quality/reputation is well-grounded and accurate (test results, research, investigation, etc.). Characteristics and quality mentioned in the description must be distinctive characteristics and quality of products of all manufacturers in the corresponding geographical area (determined based on collection, statistics, analysis, and synthesis of relevant information and data from all manufacturers in the geographical area or using appropriate sampling methods to ensure representation of all manufacturers in the geographical area). The applicant shall be responsible before the law for the accuracy of information stated in the description of specific characteristics and attached documents.

The description of specific characteristics is considered well-grounded and accurate when physical, chemical, biological indicators of products bearing GIs are analyzed and tested at recognized testing organizations in accordance with laws on technical standards and regulations and other relevant laws related to the recognition scope, including corresponding tests.
In case physical, chemical, biological indicators of products cannot be carried out at recognized testing organizations due to unmet technical conditions (no standardized testing methods available, no recognized testing organizations suitable for the type or specific characteristics of products, etc.), applicants may use test results from unrecognized organizations or other analysis methods but must provide documents proving sampling methods, analysis/testing methods, implementation conditions, scientific and practical basis of the methods, assessment of reliability of results, and other relevant documents to ensure scientific, objective, and honest results.
7. The map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication must fully present information to the extent that it can accurately identify the geographical area with natural conditions sufficient to create the particular characteristics, quality and reputation of the product. For Vietnamese geographical indications, the map must be certified by the People’s Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government where the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication is located and may be submitted together with a description document on the geographical area.
8. Presentation documents on the conditions for use and method of presentation of geographical indications to ensure the ability to distinguish between geographical indications (in case of homonym geographical indications) must clearly indicate the method of presenting and providing information about geographical indications on products (product packaging, business facilities, transaction documents) in actual production and business activities of products bearing geographical indications to clearly determine the geographical origin of the real products and ensure consumers are not confused that products bearing such geographical indications originate from other geographical areas, for example, specifying the geographical origin as the name of the country/province/city next to the geographical indication or using other identification signs accompanied by the geographical indication such as images, logos/iconography or other rules for labeling goods, etc.
Article 95. Receipt of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. An application for registration of a geographical indication shall be received when it fully satisfies the following conditions:
a) It contains all required documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Description of peculiar characteristics;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. In cases where an application fully satisfies the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and certify the submission date and the application number of such application and issue a receipt to the applicant, which includes information on the submission date, the application number, and the results of the examination of the list of documents of the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For electronic applications, upon receipt of an application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall return to the applicant an application receipt stating information on the submission date, application number, and examination results of the list of documents in which full name and signature of the receiving official are specified through the electronic application submission system.
3. In case an application fails to meet all conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, it shall be rejected from receipt (if the application is submitted by post or the electronic application is submitted through the electronic application submission system, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reason for rejection). For applications rejected from receipt by post, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return documents of the application except for the original submitted for comparison.
Section 2. APPLICATION FOR REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICES
Article 96. Format appraisal of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Format appraisal of an application for registration of a geographical indication according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the inspection of compliance with regulations on format of the application, thereby making a conclusion on whether the application is considered valid or invalid.
2. An application for registration of a geographical indication shall be considered invalid if it falls into one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the registration right as prescribed in Article 88 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is submitted against regulations in Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not protected by the State as prescribed in Articles 8 and 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed (including cases where the fee for filing the application, the charge for publication of the application, the charge for examination of the application, and the charge for information lookup for examination)
dd) The application does not meet the requirements regarding the format prescribed in Article 94 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding the quantity of one of the mandatory documents; failing to meet the requirements regarding the presentation format; the declaration does not clearly state the product bearing the geographical indication; the information about the applicant or the organization managing the geographical indication in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not certified as prescribed; the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the applicant, the organization managing the geographical indication, the representative, or the signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.
e) There is no valid authorization document (if the application is filed through a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date of notification issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. After the above time limit, if the applicant fails to submit additional documents to meet the requirements, the application will continue to be examined based on the documents included in the application.
4. Notification of the results of the formal examination shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notification of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to issue the Certificate of geographical indication registration, the reason for refusal or errors in the application, and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinion or correct errors;
b) If the applicant fails to correct errors or unsatisfactory corrections or does not have any objection or provides unsatisfactory objection within 15 working days from the end of the time limit prescribed in Point a of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on refusal to grant the certificate of geographical indication registration and send it to the applicant;
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article (or the applicant has made satisfactory corrections or provided a satisfactory explanation), the application shall be determined as valid and transferred for publication and content appraisal as prescribed.
5. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of geographical indication registration applications that are transferred for publication, clearly stating the application number, submission date, and date of transfer for publication of each application.
6. The date of submission of a geographical indication registration application is determined as follows:
a) The application submission date is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam according to Clause 2 Article 95 of this Circular. In case the application is submitted through the electronic application system after the administrative hour or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the application submission date according to this Point is the next working day;
b) For a geographical indication submitted under international requests, the application submission date is the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives an official written request from the party participating in the negotiation of the international treaty, including a brief description of the particular characteristics.
Article 97. Publication of geographical indication registration applications
1. A valid geographical indication registration application shall be published according to Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
2. Contents of disclosure of geographical indication registration applications in the Industrial Property Official Gazette include information on applicants, geographical indication management organizations, representatives (if any); application number and submission date; information on geographical indications, products bearing geographical indications, geographical areas, summary specified at Point b, Clause 5, Article 94 of this Circular and other information (if any).
3. Description of peculiar characteristics shall be disclosed on the web portal of the National Office of Intellectual Property.
Section 3. EVALUATION OF CONTENTS OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 98. Content appraisal of geographical indication registration applications
1. Content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application according to protection conditions and determination of the corresponding protection scope according to provisions of this Article.
2. Assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection title of a geographical indication registration application includes:
a) Assessment of the conformity of the subject matter stated in the geographical indication registration application with the requirements for grant of a certificate of geographical indication registration according to Article 100 of this Circular;
b) Assessment of the subject matter stated in the geographical indication registration application according to protection conditions specified in Article 101 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for a title of protection shall be completed after assessing the conformity of the subject matter stated in the application and the subject matter stated in the application with all the protection conditions and having sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter is or is not conformable, does not satisfy or satisfies the protection conditions, specifically:

a) There are reasons to conclude that the subject matter does not satisfy one or more than one protection condition; or b) There are no reasons to conclude that the subject matter does not satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Based on the results of the assessment of the eligibility for a title of protection as prescribed in clauses 2 and 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding scope of protection.
5. During the process of substantive examination of an application, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations about the contents of the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application in order to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to remedy the deficiencies.
Article 99. Minimum information sources
The minimum information sources used in the substantive examination of applications for geographical indication registration include:
1. Marks currently protected in Vietnam for products identical or similar to products bearing geographical indications, with the starting date of protection earlier than the submission date of the geographical indication registration application, including marks protected under international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory.
2. Marks in mark registration applications with an earlier submission date or priority date in Vietnam for products identical or similar to products bearing geographical indications.
3. Marks recognized by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as well-known marks.
4. Geographical indications currently under protection in Vietnam for products identical to those bearing such geographical indications.
Article 100. Assessment of conformity of subject matters stated in applications for registration of geographical indications Subject matters stated in applications for registration of geographical indications shall be deemed unconformable with the type of geographical indication protection title if such subject matters are not visible signs used to indicate the geographical origin of products from specific regions, localities, territories or countries according to Clause 22 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 101. Assessment of conditions for protection of geographical indications
1. The assessment of geographical indications according to protection conditions shall comply with the following regulations:
a) Subject matters stated in applications shall satisfy the conditions specified in Article 79 of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically, they must fully demonstrate the following conditions: a1) There exists a geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication stated in the application; a2) Products originate from the geographical area mentioned above; a3) Such products have particular characteristics and quality and/or reputation determined by the geographical conditions of the geographical area mentioned above according to Article 82 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Subject matters stated in applications do not fall into the cases specified in Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In which case, a geographical indication is deemed misleading consumers about the actual geographical origin of products bearing such geographical indication according to Clause 4 Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property if such geographical indication is identical or similar to the point of confusion with a known sign being the geographical origin of goods or services, causing relevant consumers in the territory of Vietnam to misunderstand the actual origin of goods or services;
c) The assessment of conditions for protection of geographical indications as prescribed in Points a and b of this Clause shall be carried out on the basis of information provided by applicants and information found from the minimum information sources prescribed in Article 99 of this Circular;
d) Depending on the complexity of assessing the protection conditions specified at Point a of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may consult independent consulting experts, the Geographical Indication Consultation Council or conduct additional surveys.
The Geographical Indication Consultation Council shall be established and operate on the basis of the principles prescribed in decisions of the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Independent consulting experts shall be selected from the List of industrial property consulting experts compiled by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and other sources (in cases where no suitable experts are available in such List).
2. During the process of assessing the conditions for protection of geographical indications, if a mark identical or similar to a geographical indication is found, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the owner of the mark to obtain their opinion as prescribed in Article 112 or Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. During the process of assessing conditions for protection of a geographical indication, if there are grounds to determine that the geographical indication in the application is homonymic with a protected geographical indication as prescribed in Clause 22a Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property or homonymic with a geographical indication in another accepted application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant and request the applicant to submit an explanatory document on the conditions for use and the method of presentation of the geographical indication to ensure the distinguishability between geographical indications. At the same time, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the owner of the protected geographical indication and request information on the method of presentation of the geographical indication to serve as a basis for considering and assessing the distinguishability between homonymic geographical indications. In case two or more geographical indications in the submitted applications are determined to be homonymic with each other, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request all applicants to submit explanatory documents on the conditions for use and the method of presentation of the geographical indication for consideration and decision on protection.
When necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall work with all applicants and owners of homonymic geographical indications to clarify the conditions for use and the method of presentation of the geographical indication before deciding to grant a protection title.
Article 102. Grant and refusal to grant certificates of registered geographical indications
1. A certificate of registered geographical indication shall not be granted to a geographical indication registration application in the cases specified at Points a, b, and dd Clause 1 Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. A certificate of registered geographical indication shall be granted to a geographical indication registration application when it meets the requirements prescribed in Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For a geographical indication registration application falling under the cases specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of a geographical indication registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the registration fee for decision on issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate;
b) If the subject matter stated in the application fails to satisfy the protection conditions or falls into the cases specified at Points b and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or meets the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to issue the geographical indication registration certificate, reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to provide opinions;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or does not have any objection within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the geographical indication registration certificate;
d) In case the applicant corrects deficiencies or provides opinions within the time limit specified in point b of this clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the correction document or opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the content appraisal as follows:

d1) If the applicant has sufficiently corrected deficiencies or provided justifiable explanations for the content appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the registration fee for the decision on issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate;

d2) If the applicant fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies or provides unsatisfactory opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the geographical indication registration certificate;
dd) If the applicant submits all fees and charges within the time limit specified in points a and d1 of this clause, within 1 month from the date of submission of all fees and charges as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to issue the geographical indication registration certificate;
e) If the applicant fails to submit all fees and charges within the time limit specified in point a and d1 of this clause, within 15 working days from the date of expiration of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the geographical indication registration certificate.
4. The decision on issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision. The published contents include information in the decision on issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate (including the decision number and issuance date) and the contents specified in Clause 2 Article 97 of this Circular.
The description of peculiar characteristics shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5. The certificate of geographical indication registration made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued in electronic and paper forms (if the applicant requests a paper copy).
A copy of the certificate of geographical indication registration made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests such copy.

Section 4. PROCESSING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO THE REGISTRATION FORGE OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICES
Article 103. Processing of objections to applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Within 03 months from the date on which an application for registration of a geographical indication is published, any third party has the right to object to the grant of a protection title for such application and must pay fees and charges.
An objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese, enclosed with documents or sources of information for verification. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. Where the objection to the application for registration of a geographical indication satisfies the provisions of clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process the objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 01 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 01 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response;
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of such feedback and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to feedback if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) Based on information, evidence, arguments provided by the parties, the dialogue results (if any) as prescribed in points a, b, and c of this clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the feedback and notify the objector of the results of processing the feedback together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application, excluding the case specified in point dd of this clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of the suspension of processing the feedback regarding the withdrawal of the geographical indication registration application according to Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 1 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification of acceptance of application withdrawal.
3. In cases where the objector's feedback is related to the right to register geographical indications, the objector's feedback shall be processed as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the objector to file a lawsuit petition to a competent court in accordance with civil procedure law and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to send a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit;
b) Upon expiration of the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if the objector fails to send a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit, the objector's feedback shall be considered withdrawn, and the geographical indication registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no feedback;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the geographical indication registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall continue immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the court.
4. The time limit for the applicant to provide opinions on objection and the time limit for the objector to respond to the applicant's opinions as prescribed in points a and b clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal of the application.
Article 104. Amendment to geographical indication registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to grant or refuses to grant a certificate of geographical indication registration, the applicant may take the initiative or based on the notification of appraisal results to amend or supplement documents in the geographical indication registration application.
2. Requests for amendment to geographical indication registration applications include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment to the application by the applicant, the request for amendment must be made using Form No. 10 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of amendment to the application based on the notification of appraisal results of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the amendment shall be made in writing with responses clearly stating the amended contents;
c) The applicant may request amendment to the same content related to multiple geographical indication registration applications in one declaration or one written request for amendment;
d) In case of amendments to descriptions of peculiar characteristics or maps of geographical areas, applicants shall submit amended descriptions of peculiar characteristics and maps of geographical areas;

dd) In case of amendments to names or country codes of applicants or geographical indications-managing organizations, applicants shall submit confirmation documents (original copies or certified true copies) or legal documents (certified true copies) proving the changes (decisions on name or address change; certificates of enterprise registration recording the changes in name or address, etc.) unless the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam can take advantage of information already available in databases publicly disclosed by database owners. In case information cannot be taken advantage of or is not fully and accurately available, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send written requests for individuals and organizations to supplement documents and provide explanations. The time limit for requesting and supplementing documents is not included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
e) In case of change of industrial property representatives, applicants shall submit documents declaring termination of authorization for old industrial property representatives and change of new industrial property representatives as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. Applicants shall pay an appraisal fee for each amendment and a disclosure fee for amendments to the application if such amendments must be disclosed as prescribed in Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except for amendments to correct errors made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Amendments to geographical indication registration applications must not expand the protection scope (volume) stated in the applications and must not change the nature of geographical indication subject matters stated in the applications.
5. In case amendments to applications are made by applicants, the amendments shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment to the application satisfies the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice accepting the request for amendment to the application (except in cases where the amendment is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the protection title due to invalidation of the application).
The amended content shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette in case the request for amendment to information related to the application that is valid in terms of form includes the description of specific characteristics and products bearing geographical indications;
b) If the request for amendment to the application does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice intending to refuse the request for amendment to the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opposing opinions. Upon expiration of the set time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, no opposing opinions are provided, or provides inappropriate opposing opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice refusing the request for amendment to the application;
c) In case the request for amendment to the application relates to the description of specific characteristics or geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication filed after the notice intends to grant the Certificate of geographical indication registration is issued, the application for geographical indication registration must be re-examined and the applicant must pay fees as prescribed.
6. In case the applicant makes amendments to the application based on the notice of appraisal results issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the acceptance or refusal to accept the applicant's request for amendment to the application shall be carried out by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the notice of appraisal results or the corresponding decision during the processing of such application for geographical indication registration.
7. In case an applicant requests amendment to his/her application including amendments made by him/herself and amendments to the application based on notification of appraisal results, such request shall be made in the form of a declaration as prescribed at Point a, Clause 2 of this Article. The handling of such request shall comply with Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 105. Recording of changes of applicants for registration of geographical indications
1. Before the National Office of Industrial Property issues or refuses to issue a certificate of registration of a geographical indication, the applicant may request recording of changes of the applicant due to inheritance or pursuant to a decision of a competent authority.
2. A request for recording of changes of an applicant for registration of a geographical indication must satisfy the following requirements:
a/ In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to inheritance or pursuant to a decision of a competent authority, the applicant shall submit a declaration, made according to Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular, and documents proving the inheritance of assets, rights and obligations arising from consolidation, merger, division, separation or other organizational changes of the applicant, or pursuant to a decision of a court or another competent authority.
b/ The applicant shall pay fees and charges as prescribed.
3. Requests for recording of changes of applicants shall be handled as follows:

a/ If a request for recording of changes of an applicant satisfies the requirements specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of recording of changes of the applicant and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property (except for cases where the application is amended before the National Office of Industrial Property publishes the application or refuses to grant a protection title because the application is invalid in terms of form).
b) If the request for change of applicant fails to satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize the change of the applicant and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. If the prescribed time limit expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements, does not provide objections, or provides unjustified objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize the change of the applicant.
Article 106. Withdrawal of geographical indication registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam decides to grant or refuses to grant a certificate of geographical indication registration, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the application.
2. The withdrawal of a geographical indication registration application must be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative through a written declaration. For an application filed through a representative, the authorization document must clearly state the authorization for application withdrawal or enclose a command specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 02 months from the receipt of the declaration of withdrawal of the geographical indication registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal request as follows:
a) In case the withdrawal request satisfies the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal and terminate the processing of the application and record the withdrawal in the application file. The withdrawn geographical indication registration application cannot be re-registered.
b) In case the withdrawal request fails to satisfy the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors;
If the prescribed time limit expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request.
Article 107. Re-examination of applications for geographical indications registration
1. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall conduct re-examination of an application for geographical indication registration in the following cases:
a) Re-examination of an application for geographical indication registration due to a written objection from a third party against the intended grant of a certificate of geographical indication registration in the notice of content appraisal results, together with legitimate evidence proving that the objection is due to force majeure events or objective obstacles, making it impossible to object within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Re-examination of an application due to a request for amendment or supplementation after notification of content appraisal results stating the intended grant of a certificate of geographical indication registration as prescribed at Point c, Clause 5, Article 104 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of an application for geographical indication registration shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of an application for geographical indication registration shall comply with the provisions of Clause 3, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complex cases involving multiple facts requiring verification or expert opinions, the time limit for re-examination may be extended but must not exceed the initial appraisal time limit prescribed in Clause 2, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The contents of re-examination shall comply with the corresponding provisions of Articles 98 through 101 of this Circular.
c) For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of a geographical indication registration application shall be carried out only once for the third person.
3. After obtaining the results of the re-examination, the geographical indication registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For the cases specified at Point a, Clause 1 of this Article, based on the results of the re-examination, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application according to the provisions of this Circular;
b) For the cases specified at Point b, Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application according to the provisions of Article 102 of this Circular.
Chapter VII
REQUESTS FOR PROMULGATION AND PROCESSING OF REQUESTS FOR PROMULGATION OF REGISTRATION FOR PROTECTION AND INTERNATIONAL REGISTRATION

Article 108. Maintenance of validity of invention/good solution patents
1. A dossier requesting maintenance of validity of an invention/good solution patent includes the following documents:
a) Declaration, made according to Form No. 01 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
2. Request for maintenance of validity of an invention/utility solution patent and fees for assessment of validity maintenance request, validity maintenance fee, fees for use of invention/utility solution patent, registration fee and publication fee shall be paid to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam before the end of the validity period. This request may be filed after the above-mentioned time limit but not more than 6 months from the end of the validity period and the protection certificate holder must pay fees for each month of delay as prescribed by law on fees and charges.
3. A request for maintenance of validity of an invention/utility solution patent shall be considered invalid if it falls into one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article or the documents are invalid;
b) The applicant fails to pay all fees and charges as prescribed;
c) The validity maintenance request is filed beyond the time limit prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;
4. Applications for maintenance of validity shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of valid application, within 01 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice on maintenance of validity of the protection title, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance of the notice;
b) In case of invalid application, within 01 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to maintain validity, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections;
c) If the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, no objection or inappropriate objection by the end of the time limit prescribed at Point b of this Clause, within 01 month from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on refusal to maintain validity.
d) In addition to the cases specified at Point c of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on refusal to maintain validity in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired according to regulations at the time of submission of the request for maintenance of validity; d2) The protection title has been cancelled at the time of submission of the application or during the processing of the application.
5. In case the applicant submits a written withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity, within 01 month from the date of receipt of the written withdrawal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal as follows:
a) In case the written withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity is valid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice accepting the withdrawal and terminating the processing of the request for maintenance of validity. The withdrawn request for maintenance of validity cannot be restored.
b) In case the written withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the request and set a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedy deficiencies but still fails to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity.
Article 109. Extension of validity of industrial design patents, marks registration certificates
1. An industrial design patent may be extended up to 02 times, each time for 05 years. In case an industrial design is protected by multiple schemes, the patent may be extended for all or some of them, including the basic scheme.
2. A mark registration certificate may be extended multiple times, each time for 10 years, for the entire or part of the list of goods or services.
3. In one application, the validity of one or more industrial design patents or one or more mark registration certificates may be extended.
An application for extension of validity includes the following documents:
a) Declaration, made according to Form No. 02 in Appendix II of this Circular;

b) Original copy of the industrial design patent or mark registration certificate (if the protection title is issued in paper form and it is required to record the extension in the protection title);
c) A written authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative). In case of extension of validity for certain solutions or parts of the list of goods and services, information on authorization for extension with corresponding scope is required.
4. Request for extension of validity of an industrial design patent, a certificate of registered mark and fees for examination of the request for extension of validity, validity extension fee of protection title, use fee of protection title, registration fee and fee for publication of decision on extension of validity of protection title shall be submitted to the National Office of Intellectual Property within 06 months until the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark expires. The request for extension may be submitted after the above time limit but not more than 06 months from the date the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark expires, provided that the protection title holder pays a fee for each month of delay in accordance with the law on fees and charges.
5. An application for extension of validity shall be considered invalid if it falls into one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all the documents specified in Clause 3 of this Article or the documents are invalid;
b) The applicant fails to pay all fees and charges as prescribed;
c) The request for extension of validity is submitted beyond the time limit specified in Clause 4 of this Article;
d) There is insufficient information confirming that the protection title holder authorizes the extension of validity for certain solutions or parts of the list of goods and services (if the application is submitted by a representative).
6. Applications for extension of validity shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of valid application, within 01 month from the date on which the application is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to extend validity, record it in the Industrial Design Patent, or the Mark Registration Certificate (if requested), register it and publish the decision on extension of validity in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance;
b) In case of invalid application, within 01 month from the date on which the application is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to extend validity, clearly stating reasons and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance for the applicant to correct errors or provide objection;
c) If the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction, no objection or inappropriate objection by the end of the time limit specified in Point b of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse extension of validity within 01 month from the end of the time limit mentioned above.
d) In addition to cases specified in Point c of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse extension of validity in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired at the time of submission of the request for extension of validity; d2) The protection title was cancelled at the time of submission of the application or during the processing of the application.
7. In case the applicant submits a written withdrawal of the request for extension of validity, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of the withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 108 of this Circular.
8. After completing the procedures for extension, if the owner of the Industrial Design Patent or the Mark Registration Certificate requests the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to record the decision on extension of validity in the Industrial Design Patent or the Mark Registration Certificate, the procedures for amendment to the Industrial Design Patent or the Mark Registration Certificate must be carried out and fees and charges must be paid as prescribed.
Article 110. Amendment of protection titles
1. Owners of protection titles, organizations and individuals exercising the right to register geographical indications, and organizations managing geographical indications may request amendments to the following information in protection titles, provided that they pay a fee for appraisal of requests for amendments to protection titles, a registration fee and a fee for publication of decisions on recording of amendments to protection titles:
a/ Changes in the names and addresses of protection title holders; names, addresses and nationalities of authors of inventions, industrial designs or layout designs;
b/ Changes in protection title holders (transfer of ownership due to inheritance, acquisition, merger, division, separation, consolidation, joint venture, association or establishment of new legal persons under the same ownership, business form conversion or under decisions of courts or other competent agencies);
c/ Amendments to descriptions of geographical indications or geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications, regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks. In this case, protection title holders shall additionally pay a fee for content re-appraisal.
2. Protection title holders may request narrowing of the scope of protection according to Clause 3, Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, provided that they pay a fee for appraisal of requests for amendments to protection titles, a registration fee and a fee for publication of decisions on amendments to protection titles in the following cases:
a/ Requesting reduction of one or more independent or dependent points included in the protection request stated in an invention patent or utility solution patent;
b/ Requesting removal of one or more industrial design schemes or products included in a set of products stated in an industrial design patent;
c/ Requesting reduction of one or more goods or services or group of goods or services included in the list of goods or services stated in a mark registration certificate or removing minor details being elements subject to exclusion (not protected separately) without affecting the distinguishability of marks stated in mark registration certificates.
3. Application for amendments to the provisions in clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration requesting amendments, made according to Form No. 03 in Appendix II of this Circular, clearly stating the request for amendments. A declaration requesting amendments may request amendments to multiple protection titles if they have the same amended content, provided that the applicant pays fees as prescribed for each protection title;
b) Original copy of the protection title in case the protection title is issued in paper form;
c) Documents certifying the change of name or address (original or certified true copy); decision on change of name or address; certificate of enterprise registration recording the change of name or address; other legal documents proving the change of name or address (original or certified true copy) if the content of the request for amendments is name or address;
d) Documents proving the transfer of ownership as prescribed at Point b Clause 1 of this Article (original or certified true copy) if the request for amendments is the change of owner of the protection title;
dd) Photocopies or drawings of the industrial design which have been amended if the request for amendments is the amendment to the protection title of the industrial design (5 sets if the application is submitted in paper form); description of geographical indications, map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication which has been amended if the request for amendments is the amendment to the protection title of the geographical indication (2 copies if the application is submitted in paper form); regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks which have been amended if the request for amendments is the amendment to the protection title of the collective mark or certification mark (2 copies if the application is submitted in paper form); sample of mark if the request for amendments is the amendment to the sample of mark as prescribed at Point c Clause 2 of this Article (5 samples if the application is submitted in paper form);
e) Document explaining in detail the content of amendments;
g) A written authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative). In case of amendment to the protection title as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, information on authorization for narrowing the scope of protection must be provided.
4. An application for amendment to a protection title shall be considered invalid if it falls into one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all the documents specified in Clause 3 of this Article or the documents are invalid;
b) The request for narrowing the scope of protection does not fall within the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article;
c) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed;
d) There is insufficient information confirming that the holder of the protection title authorizes the request for narrowing the scope of protection (if the application is submitted by a representative).
5. Applications for amendment to protection titles shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of valid applications, within 02 months from the date of receipt of the application or from the date of expiration of the re-examination period specified at Point d of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to amend the protection title, record it in the protection title, register it and publish the decision on amendment in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision.
b) In case the application is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to request for amendments, clearly stating reasons and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections.
c) If the prescribed time limit specified in Point b of this Clause expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate corrections, no objection or inappropriate objection, within 01 month from the expiration of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the request for amendments to the protection title;

d) For requests for amendments specified in Point c Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, procedures for re-appraisal of the corresponding applications shall be carried out in accordance with Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) In case the protection title has been terminated or expired at the time of submission of the application as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the request for amendments to the protection title, except where the content of the request for amendments aims to record accurate information before the termination of the protection title. In case the protection title has been invalidated at the time of submission of the application or during the processing of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the request for amendments to the protection title;
e) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the request for amendments to the protection title in case it receives a written request from a competent authority for suspension of processing to implement relevant decisions and procedures as prescribed by law or documents regarding the industrial property rights subject matter under the protection title being used as collateral. The request for amendments shall continue to be processed when the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives documents proving that the relevant decisions and procedures have been terminated.
6. In case the applicant submits a written request for withdrawal of the application for amendments to the protection title, within 01 month from the receipt of such a request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal request as follows:
a) In case of valid withdrawal request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal request and terminate processing of the request for amendment to protection title. The withdrawn request for amendment to protection title shall not be re-accepted.
b) In case of invalid withdrawal request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the applicant fails to correct errors by the imposed time limit or fails to meet requirements after correction, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept withdrawal request.
7. In case of detecting errors in a protection title, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall, at its discretion or upon request of the person detecting errors, revoke the protection title and reissue the protection title with amended information. Within 2 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam revokes the original protection title, it shall reissue the protection title recording correct information and send it to the owner of the protection title.
The owner of the protection title must pay a fee for appraisal of request for amendment to protection title as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, registration fee and fee for announcement of amended information if the protection title has errors that have been published and such errors are caused by the owner of the protection title, except for errors caused by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Article 111. Termination of validity of protection titles
1. Organizations and individuals requesting termination of validity of protection titles according to Clause 4 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property must pay a fee for requesting termination of validity of protection titles, registration fee and fee for announcement of decision on termination of validity of protection titles.
2. The validity of a certificate of registered mark shall be terminated when the mark is not used by its owner or his/her licensee within five consecutive years before the request for validity termination is made without plausible reasons as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property. A mark shall not be considered to be used if the act of using it is merely formal and does not reflect actual commercial exploitation of goods or services bearing such mark, as in the following cases:
a) The use of the mark is only for business preparation, research or testing of goods or services bearing the mark but no commercial transaction arises or there is no clear evidence of actual implementation of bringing such goods or services to market;
b) The use of the mark is only conducted within the internal operation of the applicant and is not intended to supply goods or services bearing the mark to the market;
c) The use of the mark is only shown in documents, reports, designs, web pages (website), advertising media or other information media without being associated with goods or services bearing the mark and plans to bring them to market or have brought them to market.
3. In a request for validity termination, only one protection title may be requested for termination of its validity.
A request for validity termination of a protection title shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration requesting validity termination of a protection title, made according to Form No. 4 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b) Evidence (if any);
c) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
d) Explanation of the reason for the application (specify the number of the protection title, the reason and legal grounds, and the content proposed to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title), and relevant documents.
4. Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application for termination of validity, if the application has formal errors (e.g., the authorization document is invalid; fees and charges are not fully paid as prescribed; necessary information is missing to determine the scope of the request, etc.), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct the errors.
If the applicant does not have a document correcting errors or has a document correcting errors but fails to meet the requirements within the above-mentioned time limit, within 1 month from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to consider the application for termination of validity of the protection title.
5. In case a third party requests termination of validity of a protection title, the application for termination of validity of the protection title shall be processed within 5 months from the date of submission of the application for termination of validity of the protection title; for complex cases or where the owner of the protection title has a different opinion from the applicant for termination of validity, the processing time limit may be extended but must not exceed 7 months from the date of receipt of the application for termination of validity of the protection title.
The application for termination of validity of a protection title shall be processed as follows:
a) Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request or from the date on which the application for termination of validity is completed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify in writing the opinion of the third party to the owner of the protection title, setting a 1-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the owner of the protection title to provide their opinion;
b) After receiving feedback from the protection title holder within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the request for termination of validity and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the requestor to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) In cases where information, documents, or evidence provided by a third party or the protection title holder is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the third party or the protection title holder to submit additional documents or information to verify or clarify such information within 1 month from the date of notification by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
d) Based on the review of information, evidence, arguments, and documents provided by the parties, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title or issue a notice of refusal to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title;
dd) The decision to terminate the validity of the protection title shall be recorded in the National Register of Industrial Property and published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance.
6. In case the protection title holder declares relinquishment of industrial property rights as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property, within 1 month from the receipt of the declaration, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall handle the matter as follows:
a) Issue a decision to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title or issue a notice of intended refusal to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title, setting a 1-month time limit for the protection title holder to provide feedback;
b) If the above-mentioned time limit expires without any feedback from the protection title holder or with inappropriate feedback, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title.
7. In case the requestor for termination of validity withdraws the written request for termination of validity of the protection title, within 1 month from the receipt of the withdrawal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal as follows:
a) In case of withdrawal of valid request for termination of validity, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of the request and terminate the processing of the request for termination of validity of the protection title. The withdrawn request for termination of validity of the protection title cannot be re-validated;
b) In case of withdrawal of invalid request for termination of validity, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the request and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements after correction, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to withdraw the request and continue to consider the request for termination of validity of the protection title.
8. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall terminate the processing of the entire or partial request for termination of validity of a protection title in cases where such protection title is subject to a request for termination of validity which has been entirely or partially invalidated under the decision of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (or under a legally effective judgment/decision of the Court).
Article 112. Invalidation of protection titles

1. Organizations and individuals requesting invalidation of protection titles as stipulated in Clause 4, Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property must pay the request fee, the fee for appraisal of the request for invalidation of protection titles, the registration fee, and the fee for publication of the decision to invalidate the protection titles.
2. An invention/utility solution shall be invalidated because the invention patent granted beyond the scope disclosed in the initial description of the invention registration application according to Point dd, Clause 2, Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property in cases where, compared with the initial description and for a person with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field, the description of the invention contains changes in content and such changes result in information not directly and clearly derived from the initial description of the application, specifically as follows:
a) During the amendment and supplementation of the application, the applicant inserts technical signs or technical signs that cannot be directly and clearly determined from the initial description;
b) Addition of information (including: information on purpose, effectiveness, etc.) that cannot be directly and clearly determined from the initial description (including drawings) and/or the initial protection request to clearly disclose the invention or fully disclose the protection request;
c) The added content in the description is technical signs related to size specifications obtained by measuring size specifications on drawings;
d) Inserting details or components not mentioned in the initial description which lead to special effects and/or benefits not present in the initial application;
dd) Adding effects and/or benefits (benefits) that an average person with knowledge in the relevant technical field cannot determine from the initial application;
e) Changing technical signs of the protection request but these changed technical signs are not directly and clearly disclosed or determined from the initial description;
g) Adding new contents by changing undefined contents into specific and definite contents;
h) Combining separate technical signs of the initial application into a new technical sign while the relationship between these technical signs is not disclosed in the initial application;
i) Changing one or more technical signs in the description to make such technical signs different from those stated in the initial description;
k) Removing a technical sign from the protection request point where such technical sign is necessary for the protected subject matter to achieve the set objective and/or the removal of such technical sign changes other technical signs.
3. A mark registration certificate shall be invalidated due to the mark registration applicant’s malicious intent as prescribed at Point a, Clause 1, Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property if falling into one of the following cases:
a) The mark registration applicant registers a large number of marks identical or similar to the point of indistinguishability from other marks currently used by others in Vietnam for identical or similar goods or services where such registration exceeds normal business capacity and there is no evidence showing the actual intention to use these marks in production and business activities; or b) At the time of filing the application, the registered mark is identical or similar to the point of indistinguishability from a mark recognized by relevant consumers in Vietnam as a sign indicating the commercial origin of identical or similar goods or services of another person or a mark that is identical or similar to the point of indistinguishability from a well-known mark in other countries; and the registration aims to take advantage of the reputation of such mark to earn profits or is mainly aimed at reselling, licensing, or transferring the registration right to the holder of marks specified in this clause or aims to prevent the market entry of the holder of marks specified in this clause to limit competition or acts contrary to other fair trade practices.
4. In a request for invalidation, only one protection title may be requested for invalidation. A request for invalidation of a protection title includes the following documents:
a) Declaration requesting invalidation of the protection title, made according to Form No. 5 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b) Evidence (if any);
c) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
d) Explanation of the reason for the request (specifying the number of the title, the reason, legal grounds, and the content of the request to partially or entirely invalidate the protection title) and relevant documents;
5. Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request for invalidation, if the application has formal errors (e.g., the authorization document is invalid; fees and charges are not fully paid as prescribed; necessary information is missing to determine the scope of the request, etc.), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct the errors.
If the applicant does not have a document correcting errors or has a document correcting errors but fails to meet the requirements within the above-mentioned time limit, within 1 month from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to consider the request for invalidation of the protection title.
6. The request for invalidation of a protection title shall be processed within 12 months for requests for invalidation of an invention patent/utility solution and 5 months for requests for invalidation of an industrial design patent, a certificate of registered layout design, a certificate of registered mark, or a certificate of registered geographical indication, from the date of submission of the request for invalidation; for complex cases, the processing time may be extended by up to 3 more months. A request for invalidation of a protection title shall be processed as follows:
a) Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request or from the date the request for correction meets the requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify in writing the opinion of a third party to the owner of the protection title, setting a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the owner of the protection title to provide their opinion;
b) After receiving feedback from the owner of the protection title within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the applicant for invalidation and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to respond in writing to such feedback;
c) In cases where information, documents, or evidence provided by a third party or the owner of the protection title is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the third party or the owner of the protection title to submit additional documents or information for verification or clarification of the information within 1 month from the date of notification by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. Where necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may organize direct consultation between the third party and the owner of the protection title to clarify related issues;
d) Based on the review of information, evidence, arguments, and documents provided by the parties, the results of direct consultation, and the appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to partially or entirely invalidate the protection title or notify refusal to partially or entirely invalidate the protection title.
dd) The decision to invalidate the protection title shall be recorded in the National Register of Industrial Property and published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision.
7. In cases where the applicant for invalidation submits a written withdrawal of the request for invalidation of the protection title, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of withdrawal of requests for termination of validity as prescribed in Clause 7 Article 111 of this Circular.
8. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall terminate the processing of the entire or partial request for invalidation of the protection title in cases where the protection title is subject to an invalidation request that has been entirely or partially invalidated under the decision of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (or under a legally effective judgment or decision of a competent court).
9. Regulations in clauses 2 and 3 of this Article shall also be applied to the processing of applications for registration of marks.
Article 113. Termination or invalidation of international registrations
1. Applications for termination or invalidation of international registrations of marks shall be processed as follows:
a) For applications for termination or invalidation of international registrations of marks under the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol filed by a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the content of such application to the mark owner via the International Office, specifying a time limit of 03 months from the date of notification for the mark owner's opinion;
b) The international registration of a mark may be terminated or invalidated for part or all of the list of goods or services;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision on termination or invalidation of international registration of a mark for part or all of the list of goods or services and such decision is no longer subject to administrative complaints or lawsuits, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of termination or invalidation of international registration of the mark using the form of the International Office, clearly stating the list of goods or services to be terminated or invalidated, and send such notice to the International Office;
d) Other relevant regulations in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular regarding the processing of applications for termination or invalidation of protection titles of marks based on mark registration applications filed under the national filing method prescribed in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular shall also apply to the processing of applications for termination or invalidation of international registrations of marks.
2. Applications for termination or invalidation of international registrations of industrial designs shall be processed as follows:

a) For an application for termination or invalidation of international registration of industrial designs under the Hague Agreement filed by a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the owner of the industrial design of the contents of such application for termination or invalidation via the International Office, setting a time limit of 3 months from the date of notification for the owner to provide their opinion;
b) The international registration of an industrial design may be invalidated for some or all of the industrial designs in such registration;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision on invalidation of international registration of some or all of the industrial designs and such decision is no longer subject to administrative complaints or lawsuits, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of invalidation of international registration of industrial designs using the form provided by the International Office, specifying the industrial designs subject to invalidation, and send this notice to the International Office;
d) Other relevant provisions in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular regarding the processing of applications for termination or invalidation of protection titles of industrial designs granted based on national registration applications submitted in accordance with the national filing procedure shall apply to applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of industrial designs.
Chapter VIII
   REGISTRATION OF CONTRACTS FOR TRANSFER OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Article 114. Application files for registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights
1. The owner of a protection title (the assignor) or the industrial property right transferee may file an application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract with the National Office of Industrial Property to request the latter to record the industrial property right assignment, provided that the fee for dossier appraisal and lookup (in case of mark assignment), the fee for grant of certificate of registered mark (in case of partial assignment of goods or service bearing a mark), the registration fee and the fee for announcement of decision on recording of industrial property right assignment are paid.
2. An application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract shall include the following documents:
a/ Declaration for registration of industrial property right assignment contract, made according to Form No. 6 in Annex II of this Circular;
b/ Contract (original or certified copy as prescribed); if the contract is made in another language than Vietnamese, it must be enclosed with a Vietnamese translation; if the contract has many pages, each page must bear the signatures of the parties or a seal on every two adjoining pages;
c/ Original protection title in case the protection title is granted in paper form;
d/ Written consent of co-owners on the industrial property right assignment, if the corresponding industrial property right is under joint ownership; or mortgagee in case the industrial property right is being used as mortgaged asset;
e/ Power of attorney (if the application is filed through a representative);
e) For applications for registration of contracts on transfer of collective marks or certification marks, apart from the above documents, the following documents are required: e1) Regulations on use of collective marks or certification marks of the transferee party as prescribed in Article 105 of the Law on Intellectual Property; e2) Documents evidencing the right to file an application of the transferee party to certification marks or collective marks as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4, Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In this case, apart from fees and charges applicable to applications for registration of industrial property rights transfer contracts as prescribed, the applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee.
3. Each application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract shall be recorded for only one step of assignment. In case an industrial property object is assigned in more than one steps, each step of assignment must be registered with a separate application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract.
Article 115. Procedures for processing applications for registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights
1. An application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all the documents specified in Clause 2 Article 114 of this Circular or contains invalid documents, including: The contract does not have both parties’ signatures (and seals, if any); name and address of the transferor in the contract do not match the corresponding information in the protection title, authorization document, and declaration; name and address of the transferee in the contract do not match the name and address stated in the authorization document and declaration;

The contract is not certified as valid; the transfer contract lacks required contents or contains contents inconsistent with regulations on conditions restricting transfer of industrial property rights prescribed in Articles 139 and 140 of the Law on Intellectual Property, etc.;
b) The applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge as prescribed;
c/ The assignor is not the owner of the protection title;
d/ There are grounds to affirm that the assignment of industrial property rights infringes upon the industrial property rights of a third party.
2. In case the application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract is valid, within 02 months after receiving such application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall perform the following jobs:
a/ To issue a decision to record the industrial property right assignment;
b/ To record in the protection title the new owner; in case of partial assignment of the list of goods or services bearing protected marks, to grant a certificate of mark registration to the assignee and determine the scope of the list of goods or services recorded in the original protection title for the assigned part;
c/ To record the industrial property right assignment in the National Register of Industrial Property;
d/ To publish the decision to record the industrial property right assignment in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days after issuing such decision.
3. In case the application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract is invalid, within 02 months after receiving such application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of intended refusal to register the contract, clearly stating the application's deficiencies, and fix a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct the deficiencies or to express opinions opposing the intended refusal to register the contract.
4. In case the applicant fails to correct errors or corrects errors but fails to meet requirements, does not have any objection or has an objection but it is not accurate about the intended refusal to register the contract within the time limit specified in Clause 3 of this Article, within 2 months from the end date of the above time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to register the contract.
5. In case after the receipt of the application for registration of the industrial property right transfer contract, the parties have disputes related to the contract or there is a document from a competent agency or organization requesting the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to suspend the processing to implement relevant decisions and procedures as prescribed by law or documents on the subject matter of industrial property rights under the protection title is being used as collateral, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the consideration of the application until it receives documents proving that the dispute has been resolved or documents proving that the relevant decisions and procedures have been terminated, then the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue to consider the application as prescribed.
6. In case the protection title is subject of the industrial property right transfer contract which has been terminated, invalidated or expired as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to register the industrial property right transfer contract.
7. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to record the transfer or refuses to register the industrial property right transfer contract, if one of the contracting parties wishes to withdraw the application for registration of the industrial property right transfer contract, it must reach agreement with the other party on the withdrawal of the submitted application for registration of the industrial property right transfer contract, except for the withdrawal of the application due to irreparable errors at the request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Within 1 month from the receipt of the request for withdrawal of the application for registration of the transfer contract, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall handle the withdrawal request as follows:
a) In case of a valid withdrawal request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal request and terminate the processing of the transfer contract registration request. The withdrawn transfer contract registration request cannot be re-registered;
b) In case the application for withdrawal of the application is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the application and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are insufficient, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal of the application.
Chapter IX
 COMPLAINTS AND RESOLUTION OF COMPLAINTS RELATED TO INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY PROCEDURES
Article 116. Persons having the right to complain, subjects of complaint, statute of limitations for filing a complaint and persons settling complaints
1. Persons with complaint rights are organizations and individuals specified in Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Decisions and notifications subject to complaints specified in Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property are official decisions and notifications issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or organizations authorized or delegated by People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities to carry out procedures related to industrial property according to corresponding provisions of this Circular and Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, including the following decisions and notifications:
a) Notification of rejection of application;
b) Notification of acceptance or rejection of request for amendment to application, conversion of application, change of applicant, withdrawal of application;
c) Decision on rejection of grant of protection title; Decision on grant of protection title;
d) Decision on rejection of protection of internationally registered industrial design; Decision on rejection of protection of internationally registered mark; Decision on acceptance of protection of internationally registered industrial design; Decision on acceptance of protection of internationally registered mark;
dd) Notification of maintenance of validity of protection title; decision on rejection of maintenance of validity of protection title;
e) Decision on extension of validity of protection title; decision on rejection of extension of validity of protection title;
g) Decision on amendment to protection title; decision on rejection of request for amendment to protection title;
h) Decision on partial or total termination or cancellation of validity of protection title; notification of rejection of partial or total termination or cancellation of validity of protection title;
i) Decisions and notices on handling of applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of marks; decisions and notices on handling of applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of industrial designs;
k) Decisions on recognition of transfer of industrial property rights, decisions rejecting registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights; decisions on recognition of transfer of rights to use subject matters of industrial property rights, decisions rejecting registration of contracts for transfer of rights to use subject matters of industrial property rights;
l) Decisions, notices and other acts which satisfy the conditions for being subjects of complaints according to regulations of law on complaints.
Notices of information and requests for completion of dossiers which are not administrative decisions shall not be subjects of complaints, such as notices of appraisal results, notices of deficiencies, requests for amendment and supplementation of documents, intended refusals, notices of provisional refusal to protect internationally registered marks, notices of refusal to protect internationally registered industrial designs.
3. The following contents shall not be accepted in the course of complaint settlement:
a) Requests for amendment and supplementation of applications for establishment of industrial property rights which are subjects of complained decisions and notices (such as requests for amendment of invention descriptions (including protection requests); requests for amendment of sets of photos or sets of drawings and descriptions of industrial designs; requests for amendment of mark samples and lists of goods and services bearing marks; requests for amendment of product names bearing GIs and descriptions of specific properties of products bearing GIs and maps of geographical areas corresponding to GIs for applications for registration of GIs), except otherwise provided by international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory;
b) The fact that existed during the appraisal of the industrial property registration application but could only be known by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and relevant organizations or individuals after the decision on grant/rejection of the protection title was issued (hereinafter referred to as "new fact") due to objective reasons, except for cases where this fact is provided by a third party in accordance with Point b Clause 6 Article 119 of this Circular.
4. The complaint must be filed within the statute of limitations prescribed in Articles 9 and 33 of the Law on Complaints.
5. The person competent to resolve first-time complaints is the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam; the person competent to resolve second-time complaints is the Minister of Science and Technology (hereinafter referred to as "complaint resolver") for procedures related to industrial property rights specified in this Circular. For procedures related to industrial property rights specified in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the persons competent to resolve first-time and second-time complaints shall be determined in accordance with the law on complaints.
Article 117. Complaint applications
1. Complaint applications shall comply with Clause 1 Article 3 of Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP dated October 19, 2020 elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Complaints (hereinafter referred to as "Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP"), which fully specifies information prescribed in Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and documents related to the content of the complaint as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 8 of the Law on Complaints.

Article 8 of the Law on Complaints, specifically as follows:
a) Copies of the decision or notice subject to the complaint and copies of the industrial property registration application are the objects of such decision or notice (in case of the second-time complainant); or documents providing guidance on the above-mentioned documents;
b) Copies of the decision resolving the first-time complaint (for second-time complaints);
c) Evidence (evidence or material evidence) used to prove and clarify the complaint argument. Evidence may be submitted additionally within 01 month from the date of filing the complaint.
2. In case the complaint is filed through a lawful representative in Vietnam as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property, the application must be enclosed with a written authorization or a copy of the written authorization if it falls under the case specified in Clause 5 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. A complaint can only be filed against one decision or official notification related to industrial property procedures.
Article 118. Withdrawal of complaints and suspension of complaint settlement
1. The complainant may withdraw the complaint according to Article 10 of the Law on Complaints. If the withdrawal of the complaint is done under the authorization of the complainant, the authorization for withdrawal of the complaint must be clearly stated in the written authorization. The withdrawn application shall be considered not submitted. The complainant shall not be refunded the complaint and the service provision fees for complaint settlement already paid, except for cases where the complaint is withdrawn before the notice of acceptance or rejection of the application is issued.
2. The person settling the complaint shall issue a decision to suspend the complaint settlement in the following cases:
a) The complainant withdraws the complaint;
b) The protection title, international registration of marks, international registration of industrial designs in Vietnam has been invalidated or the validity of the international registration of marks has expired according to relevant international treaties.
Article 119. Order and procedures for resolution of complaints
1. The acceptance of a complaint shall comply with Article 27 of the Law on Complaints and Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically as follows:
a) Within 10 working days from the date of receipt of the complaint, the person in charge of resolving the complaint must:

a1) Issue a notice of refusal to resolve the complaint if the complaint falls under one of the cases specified at Point b of this Clause, clearly stating the reasons for refusal; or

a2) Issue a notice of acceptance of the complaint if the complaint does not fall under the cases specified at Point b of this Clause, specifying the date of acceptance, and determine the lookup fee and/or appraisal fee for the case where re-appraisal is required to serve the resolution of the complaint corresponding to the content of the complaint (if any), and impose a deadline of 01 month for the complainant to pay the fee;
b) The complaint shall not be accepted for resolution in one of the following cases: b1) The subject matter of the complaint is not an official decision or notice prescribed in Clause 2 Article 116 of this Circular; b2) The decision, notice, or act complained about does not directly relate to the lawful rights and interests of the complainant; b3) The complaint is not filed in accordance with Clause 2 Article 117 of this Circular; b4) The complaint is filed beyond the prescriptive period, except for the cases specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 15 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP; b5) The complaint has already been resolved by a second complaint resolution decision; b6) The complaint has been accepted by the Court or resolved by a judgment or decision of the Court, except for the case where there is a decision to terminate the resolution of the administrative case by the Court; b7) The complainant continues to file a complaint after the 30-day time limit from the date of the decision to terminate the resolution of the complaint as prescribed in Article 10 and Clause 8 Article 11 of the Law on Complaints; b8) The complaint does not specify illegal elements of the decision, notice, or act complained about and only requests amendments to the complaint that is the subject matter of such decision or notice; b9) The complaint is against a notice, administrative decision, or act related to an industrial property subject matter classified as state secret.
c) If the complainant fails to pay the appraisal fee for the case requiring re-appraisal to serve the resolution of the complaint according to the notice of acceptance for complaint resolution prescribed at Point a2 of this Clause, the complaint shall be resolved based on the documents included in the application.

2. The time limit for resolving complaints shall comply with Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Articles 28 and 37 of the Law on Complaints.
3. To verify the content of the complaint, the person resolving the complaint shall seek opinions from persons with relevant rights and obligations regarding the complaint that has been accepted under Article 21 of Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP (hereinafter referred to as "relevant parties") as follows:
a) The complaint resolver shall notify in writing the content of the complaint to the related party and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of notification for the latter to provide its opinion (if any);
b) The related party may provide information or evidence to support its argument within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, and the complaint resolver shall be responsible for reviewing such information and evidence when resolving the complaint;
c) The complaint resolver shall notify in writing the content of the related party's opinion and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the complainant to provide feedback on the related party's opinion;
d) If, upon the expiration of the imposed time limit, one party does not provide an opinion, the complaint application shall be resolved based on the documents included in the application, including documents expressing the other party's opinions.
4. During the resolution of the first-time complaint for complex cases, the first-time complaint resolver may proactively conduct or, at the request of the complainant, conduct re-appraisal as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property. During the re-appraisal process, the first-time complaint resolver may consult with independent experts or the Advisory Council in accordance with the following regulations:
a) The first-time complaint resolver may consult with independent consulting experts or the Advisory Council during the re-appraisal process, depending on the complexity of the content subject to re-appraisal. The Advisory Council consists of a chairman and members. Independent consulting experts and the Advisory Council shall advise the complaint resolver on technical issues, legal issues of the content subject to re-appraisal, and the proposed resolution.
Independent consulting experts, the Chairman and members of the Advisory Council are persons with appropriate professional qualifications selected from the List of Industrial Property Consulting Experts and other sources (in cases where no suitable experts are available on such list).
List of industrial property counseling experts shall be compiled and published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in cooperation with the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in handling complaints.
b) The Counseling Council shall be organized and operate according to the following principles: b1) The Counseling Council is established under the decision of the first complaint resolver; b2) The Counseling Council shall work in the form of meetings, collective discussions and voting by majority; b3) Parties involved in the complaint case, parties with related rights and obligations may be invited to participate in the meeting of the Counseling Council to clarify the details of the case.
c) The following persons are not allowed to participate in the Counseling Council and act as independent counseling experts in the complaint case: c1) The complained person (the person who issued the complained decision or notice); c2) The person who appraised the application for establishment of industrial property rights related to the complained decision or notice; c3) The person whose rights and interests are directly related to the complaint case; c4) The person who may not be objective in the complaint case if there are grounds to determine such. The persons mentioned in this point shall provide explanations, clarifications and information related to the work they have performed within the content of the complaint.
d) Opinions of independent counseling experts, opinions of the President and members of the Counseling Council and working results of the Counseling Council must be presented in writing.
5. The first complaint resolver shall organize a dialogue session as prescribed in Article 30 of the Law on Complaints. Independent counseling experts and members of the Counseling Council regarding re-appraisal during the first complaint resolution (if any) may be invited to attend the dialogue session.
6. The person competent to resolve the first complaint shall issue a complaint resolution decision as follows:
a) Based on the results of re-examination of the complained decision or notice, the first complaint resolver shall issue a complaint resolution decision which must contain the contents specified in Clause 2, Article 31 of the Law on Complaints;

b) In case the complainant is not the applicant or the requester for establishment of industrial property rights or registration of an industrial property right transfer contract subject to the decision or notice complained about and provides new details which may affect the conclusion on complaint settlement, the re-examination shall be carried out for the contents related to such new details. Based on the results of such re-examination, the person competent to settle the first-time complaint shall issue a decision on complaint settlement as prescribed at Point a of this Clause.
7. The provisions on first-time complaint settlement in this Circular shall also apply to the second-time complaint settlement, except for the provisions related to consultation with experts in the re-examination of application contents prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article. For complicated complaint cases, if necessary, the person settling the second-time complaint shall establish an Advisory Council under the guidance prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article to consult opinions during the complaint settlement process.
8. In case the complaint settlement results lead to the amendment, supplementation or partial or total cancellation of an administrative decision, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall resume the process of examination of relevant applications to carry out the remaining procedures in accordance with the complaint settlement results.
Article 120. Publication of decisions on complaint settlement
Decisions on complaint settlement shall be published on the web portal of the complaint settlement authority within 15 working days and in the Industrial Property Gazette within 01 month from the date of issuance of the decision.
Article 121. Effect of complained decisions, notices and decisions on complaint settlement
1. Composed decisions and notices shall remain effective during the complaint settlement period, except for cases where they are suspended according to the written decision of the complaint settler as prescribed in Article 35 of the Law on Complaints or at the request of the Court in accordance with the law on proceedings.
2. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, organizations authorized or delegated by People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall immediately implement legally effective decision on complaint resolution as prescribed in Article 44 of the Law on Complaints, specifically as follows:
a) Decision on first complaint resolution issued by Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, head of organization authorized or delegated by People’s Committee of province or central-affiliated city to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be effective after 30 days from the date of signing if the complainant does not file second complaint; for remote areas where travel is difficult, this period may be extended but must not exceed 45 days;
b) Decision on second complaint resolution issued by Minister of Science and Technology or Chairperson of People’s Committee of province or central-affiliated city shall be effective after 30 days from the date of signing; for remote areas where travel is difficult, this period may be extended but must not exceed 45 days.
3. The receipt of second complaint shall be notified to the person resolving the first complaint within 10 working days from the date of receipt. In this case, the decision on first complaint resolution shall not be legally effective. The decision and notification complained shall remain effective until the decision on second complaint resolution takes legal effect.
4. The second complainant may immediately notify the person resolving the first complaint of the filing of such complaint to ensure their rights and interests.
Article 122. Measures to limit arising complaints
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, an organization authorized or delegated by the People’s Committee of a province or central-affiliated city to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, shall be responsible for reviewing and re-examining issued decisions and notices; if detecting signs of violation, promptly correct, rectify, and prevent arising complaints, except where such decisions or notices are currently subject to complaints.
2. During the resolution of complaints, the person resolving complaints shall encourage and facilitate the disputing parties to reach reconciliation as per the law.
Chapter X
   INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY PUBLICATION, NATIONAL REGISTRATION BOOK ON INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY
Article 123. Industrial property publication
1. Industrial property publication means an official publication published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, providing public information on the establishment, change, termination, or invalidation of industrial property rights and other relevant information required to be publicly disclosed in accordance with the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, and this Circular.

2. Industrial property publication shall be published in print or electronic form.
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for:
a) Retain 1 copy of each issue of Official Gazette for each year for printed Official Gazette. The retention period is permanent;
b) Manage database of electronic Official Gazette including electronic copies of published issues of Official Gazette and information and attributes of documents published in Official Gazette.
4. Contents of industrial property Official Gazette include information to be published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam when organizations and individuals carry out procedures related to industrial property rights specified in the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP and this Circular.
5. Geographical indications accepted for protection under international treaties shall be published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial property Official Gazette within 30 days from the effective date of relevant international treaties. Necessary information related to the above-mentioned geographical indications shall be published according to Clause 2 Article 11 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP including: name of geographical indication; name of product bearing the geographical indication; summary of particular characteristics of the product bearing the geographical indication; protection area of the geographical indication and other information (if any).
Article 124. National Register of Industrial Property and Industrial Property Representatives
1. National Register of Industrial Property is official and public database of the State, fully presenting information about legal status of established industrial property rights. National Register of Industrial Property includes:
a) National Register of Inventions;
b) National Register of Utility Solutions;
c) National Register of Industrial Designs;
d) National Register of Semiconductor Integrated Circuit Layout Designs;
dd) National Register of Marks;
e) National Register of Geographical Indications.
2. For industrial property objects registered under national procedures, the National Register specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall include sections corresponding to each protection title, and each section shall comprise:
a) Information on the protection title: number, issuance date; name of protected subject matter, scope (quantity) of protection, validity period; name and address of the owner of the protection title/geographical indication registrant, geographical indication management organization, name and nationality of the author of the invention, layout design or industrial design;
b) Information on the application for the protection title (number, submission date, priority date of the application, name of the industrial property representation service provider (if any));
c) Any amendment related to information on amendments to the protection title, validity status of the protection title (validity maintenance, extension, termination, cancellation); transfer of ownership or use rights of industrial property objects; number of re-issuance times, re-issuance date, issuance of copies, number of copies (for any co-owner), copy issuance date, change of industrial property representation service provider (if any), etc.
3. For trademarks and industrial designs accepted for protection under international registration procedures, in the Parts of International Trademarks and Industrial Designs in the National Registerbooks corresponding to Points c and dd Clause 1 of this Article, the following sections shall be included:
a) Information on the protection status: decision number, issuance date or protection acceptance date; name of protected subject matter, protection scope, validity period; name and address of the owner of the protection title, name and nationality of the author of the industrial design;
b) Information on the application for the protection title (application number, submission date, priority date of the application, name of the industrial property representation service organization (if any));
4. For geographical indications protected under international treaties, the Part of International Geographical Indications in the National Registerbook specified at Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following sections:
a) Information on the protection status: name of the international treaty, effective date of the international treaty or date of acceptance of protection of the geographical indication under the international treaty; protected geographical indication, protection scope; name and address of the organization managing the geographical indication;
b) Information on the dossiers and documents related to the geographical indication requesting protection (international treaty, international agreement on protection, database related to the geographical indication requesting protection (if any));
c) Any amendment related to the legal status of the protected geographical indication: validity status, protection scope, transfer of management rights, etc.
5. The National Registerbook of Industrial Property Representatives shall have sections corresponding to each industrial property representation service organization, specifically:
a) Information on industrial property representation service organizations (full names, transaction names, addresses, name recording and deletion, and amendments to information on industrial property representation service organizations);
b) Information on lists of industrial property representatives of organizations (full names, permanent addresses, and practicing certificate numbers of each member in the list);
c) Information on changes to lists of industrial property representatives (issuance, re-issuance, revocation of practicing certificates, deletion, etc.).
6. National registries mentioned in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 of this Article shall be established and maintained by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in paper or electronic form. Any person may search the electronic registry (if available) or request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue copies or excerpts from the registry, provided that they pay fees for the issuance of copies or excerpts from the registry.
Article 125. Changes to information in national registries of industrial property
1. Owners of protection titles, organizations and individuals permitted by the State to exercise the right to register geographical indications, and organizations managing geographical indications may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to record changes to industrial property representation service organizations of protection title holders, provided that they pay fees for the appraisal of the request for recording, and registration fees and publication fees for decisions on recording changes to information on industrial property representation service organizations as prescribed.
2. Applications for changes to information on industrial property representation service organizations in national registries of industrial property include the following documents:
a) Declaration made using Form No. 03 in Appendix II of this Circular, clearly stating the request for recording of changes. A declaration may request recording of changes to information on industrial property representation service organizations of multiple protection titles if the same information is changed, provided that the applicant pays fees as prescribed for each protection title;
b) Authorization document.
3. An application for change of information about an industrial property representation service organization in the National Register shall be considered invalid if it falls into one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 2 of this Article or contains invalid documents;
b) The applicant fails to pay all fees and charges as prescribed.
4. Applications for recording changes in information about industrial property representation service organizations in the National Register shall be processed as follows:
a) In case the application for recording changes is valid, within 02 months from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on recording changes in the national register of industrial property, register and publish such changes in the Industrial Property Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision;
b) In case the application for recording changes is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to record the changes, clearly stating the reasons therefor and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections;
c) If the prescribed time limit specified at Point b of this Clause expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate corrections, no objection or inappropriate objection, within 02 months from the expiration of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision refusing to record the changes in the industrial property representation service organization.
d) In addition to the cases specified in Point c of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to recognize changes to industrial property representation service providers in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired according to regulations at the time of application submission; d2) The protection title was invalidated at the time of application submission or during the processing of the application.
5. Where the applicant requests withdrawal of the application for recognition of changes to information on industrial property representation service providers in the National Register, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of withdrawal of applications for amendments to protection titles as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 110 of this Circular.
Chapter XI
  TRAINING IN INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY LAW AND TRAINING IN INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY ASSESSMENT PROFESSIONS

Article 126. Industrial property law training program
1. Duration and time of training: at least 400 hours of lectures on legal knowledge and skills regarding industrial property (excluding basic law lectures for learners without bachelor's or master's degrees in Law) and not exceeding 12 months.
2. Contents of the training program and guidelines for implementation are provided in Appendix III of this Circular.
Article 127. Recognition of industrial property law training institutions The recognition of industrial property law training institutions shall be carried out as follows:
1. Training institutions wishing to be recognized as eligible to organize training courses on industrial property law in accordance with Clause 4 Article 62 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall submit their applications for recognition to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. An application for recognition as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a) A written request for recognition of the training institution as prescribed using Form No. 01 under Appendix III hereof;
b) Documents proving that the organization meets the criteria and conditions for recognition (conditions regarding facilities, equipment; information about officials in the payroll who have taught at the undergraduate level in intellectual property for 5 years or more; list of information about instructors participating in the training program; approved standard documents for use consistent with the contents specified in the structure of the training program on industrial property law).
3. Applications for recognition of training institutions shall be processed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 15 working days from the date of receipt in the following order:
a) In case of valid applications, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to recognize the training institution, record it in the List of institutions eligible to provide training courses on industrial property law, and publish it on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision;
b) In case of invalid applications, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicants of the deficiencies in the applications and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicants to correct such deficiencies;
c) In case the applicants fail to correct deficiencies or fail to meet requirements after being notified, within 15 working days from the end of the time limit specified in Point b of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse recognition of the training institution, clearly stating the reasons for refusal.
4. List of recognized training institutions under this Article shall be posted and updated on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Article 128. Deletion of names of training institutions in industrial property law The deletion of names of training institutions in industrial property law from the List of eligible training institutions in industrial property law shall be carried out as follows:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall delete names of training institutions no longer satisfying eligibility for training in industrial property law in the following cases:
a) The training institution is temporarily suspended, shut down, or ceases to exist as a legal entity;
b) The training institution no longer satisfies the criteria and conditions specified in Clause 2 Article 62 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) The training institution fails to organize training according to the prescribed industrial property law training program specified in Article 126 of this Circular.
2. For the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to delete the name from the List of training institutions in industrial property law and publish information about the deletion on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision.
Article 129. Industrial property assessment training programs
1. Duration and time of training: at least 400 hours of lectures on legal knowledge, professional skills, and industrial property assessment methods shall not exceed 12 months.
2. Contents of the training program and guidelines for implementation are provided in Appendix III of this Circular.
Article 130. Recognition of industrial property assessment training institutions The recognition of industrial property assessment training institutions shall be carried out as follows:
1. Training institutions wishing to be recognized as eligible to organize industrial property assessment training courses under Clause 2 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall submit application for recognition to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. An application for recognition as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a) Written request for recognition of industrial property assessment training institution using Form No. 02 in Appendix III of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the organization meets the criteria and conditions for recognition (material facilities and equipment; information on officials in the payroll who have participated in industrial property assessment; list of information about lecturers participating in the training program; approved standard documents for use, consistent with the contents specified in the structure of the Industrial property assessment training program).
3. Applications for recognition of industrial property assessment training institutions shall be processed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 15 working days from the date of receipt in the following order:
a) In case of valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to recognize the industrial property assessment training institution, record it in the List of institutions eligible for industrial property assessment training and publish it on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision;
b) In case of invalid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of deficiencies in the application and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to correct such deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies, within 15 working days from the end of the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse recognition of the industrial property assessment training institution, clearly stating the reasons for refusal.
4. The list of recognized training institutions under this Article shall be published and updated on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Article 131. Deletion of names of industrial property assessment training institutions The deletion of names of industrial property assessment training institutions from the List of institutions eligible for industrial property assessment training shall be carried out as follows:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall delete names of training institutions no longer satisfying the conditions for industrial property assessment training in the following cases:
a) The training institution is temporarily suspended, shut down, or ceases to exist as a legal entity;
b) The training institution no longer satisfies the criteria and conditions specified in Clause 2 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) The training institution fails to organize the training according to the industrial property assessment training program as prescribed in Article 129 of this Circular.
2. For the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to remove the name from the list of industrial property assessment training institutions and publish information on the removal of the name of the training institution on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision.
Chapter XII
                    ASSURANCE OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY INFORMATION
Article 132. Measures to assure information on industrial property rights The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall take the following measures to assure information on industrial property rights to serve the exploitation of industrial property information and the exploitation of industrial property rights:
1. Maintain the information technology infrastructure to serve the assurance of information on industrial property rights.
2. Develop and manage the database on industrial property and the system for management of geographical indications as prescribed in Articles 9b and 9c of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
3. Carry out activities to disseminate information on industrial property rights, including providing information on industrial property rights according to the titles issued, disseminating laws on the scope of rights of protection title holders, providing training and refresher courses on necessary knowledge and skills for organizations and individuals on the establishment, exploitation and management of industrial property rights and respect for industrial property rights.
Article 133. Development and management of databases on intellectual property right transactions prices and databases on protection of intellectual property rights
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall:

1. Develop, manage operation, maintain and develop databases on intellectual property right transaction prices in accordance with Article 9d of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. Develop and manage databases on protection of intellectual property rights in accordance with Article 9e of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Article 134. Access to and use of information in industrial property databases All organizations and individuals have the right to access and use information in industrial property databases in two forms:
1. Searching and finding information in databases publicly available at information lookup facilities open to the public or published on the Internet.
2. Using information lookup and analysis services or information and document supply services provided by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, provided that fees are paid as prescribed.
Article 135. Information lookup and analysis services and information and document supply services The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall provide information lookup and analysis services and information and document supply services for organizations and individuals that need them as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 134 of this Circular.
Article 136. Assurance of industrial property information in localities
1. Depending on their conditions and capabilities, local industrial property authorities may establish and manage industrial property databases to ensure industrial property information for research, application, development of production, business activities and protection of industrial property rights in localities.
2. Industrial property authorities in provinces and centrally affiliated cities shall be responsible for and have the right to conduct activities to assure industrial property information as prescribed in this Circular.
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for making statistics of information in the database on industrial property to serve state management of industrial property in provinces and centrally affiliated cities on a periodic basis (at least once every 6 months).
Article 137. Issuance of copies of documents and certification of first applications for priority right
1. Any relevant organization or individual has the right to request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue copies of documents issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or copies from master registers made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or copies of documents made by such organization or individual and submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam during the process of establishment of industrial property rights. Requesters for issuance of copies must pay document reproduction costs.
2. Applicants have the right to request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to provide certified copies of industrial property registration applications submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to apply for priority right (including applications rejected as invalid or withdrawn by applicants), provided that they pay document reproduction costs.
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall provide copy issuance and certification services for organizations and individuals upon request as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.
Chapter XIII
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 138. Implementation provisions
1. This Circular comes into force as of April 01, 2026.
2. Circular No. 23/2023/TT-BKHCN dated November 30, 2023 of the Minister of Science and Technology on elaboration of the Law on Intellectual Property and measures for implementation of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP dated August 23, 2023 of the Government on elaboration of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights, and state management of intellectual property related to procedures for establishment of industrial property rights and assurance of industrial property information shall cease to be effective from the date this Circular takes effect.
Article 139. Transitional provisions
Applications in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints submitted before the effective date of this Circular shall be processed in accordance with regulations of law effective at the time of application submission, except for the following cases:
1. Regulations in Clause 4 Article 14, Clause 4 Article 41, Clause 4 Article 48, Clause 3 Article 71 and Clause 4 Article 96 of this Circular on notification of results of formal examination applicable to applications for registration of inventions, layout designs, industrial designs, marks and geographical indications where applications are rejected as invalid.

2. Regulations in Article 76 and Article 78 of this Circular shall apply to applications for registration of audio marks which have not yet been notified of content appraisal results./.
