                                                  DECREE

    AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF DECREE NO. 14/2013/ND-CP OF FEBRUARY 5, 2013 BY THE GOVERNMENT AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF DECREE NO. 07/2007/ND-CP OF JANUARY 12, 2007 BY THE GOVERNMENT DETAILING AND GUIDING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE LAW ON LEGAL ASSISTANCE

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated December 25, 2001;

  Pursuant to the Law on Legal Aid dated June 29, 2006;

  At the request of the Minister of Justice, The Government promulgates a Decree amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 14/2013/ND-CP dated February 5, 2013 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 07/2007/ND-CP dated January 12, 2007 detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid.

  Article 1. Amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 14/2013/ND-CP dated February 5, 2013 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 07/2007/ND-CP dated January 12, 2007 detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid as follows:

  1. Clause 7 Article 1 is amended as follows:

  “1. Persons who have obtained bachelor of law degrees, graduated from lawyer training courses, and are working in state legal aid centers, and are appointed to participate in legal aid professional training courses; if they pass the examination of the legal aid professional training course, they shall be granted certificates of completion of the legal aid professional training course by the Judicial Academy or the Ministry of Justice in accordance with the law.

  Persons who were once lawyers under the Law on Lawyers or were exempted from lawyer training courses under the Law on Lawyers, and are working in state legal aid centers, and are appointed to take the examination for the legal aid professional training course; if they pass the examination of the legal aid professional training course, they shall be granted certificates of completion of the legal aid professional training course by the Judicial Academy or the Ministry of Justice in accordance with the law.

  2. The Minister of Justice shall stipulate programs and time for legal aid professional training courses for legal aid professionals; promulgate a Regulation on organization, examination and issuance of certificates of completion of legal aid professional training courses.”

  2. Clause 8 Article 1 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  “Article 25. Professional titles of legal aid professionals

  1. Legal aid professionals shall have professional titles of public employees as prescribed by law.

  2. The Minister of Home Affairs shall promulgate a list, code and classification of professional titles of legal aid professionals.

  3. The Minister of Justice shall promulgate professional standards for professional titles of legal aid professionals after reaching agreement with the Minister of Home Affairs.”

  3. Clause 9 Article 1 is amended as follows:

  “1. Legal aid professionals shall be paid salaries according to the salary table of public officials as prescribed by law.

  3. Legal aid professionals shall receive a responsibility-based allowance equal to 25% of their current salary plus a leadership allowance and an over-the-bracket allowance (if any). When participating in legal proceedings, legal aid professionals shall receive case handling allowances equal to 40% of the allowance applicable to lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State as prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article. When representing outside legal proceedings or conducting mediation, legal aid professionals shall receive case handling allowances equal to 20% of the allowance applicable to lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State.”

  4. Clause 12 Article 1 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  “2. An associate shall be entitled to allowance for each legal aid case he/she has handled based on the number of hours of legal counseling and other forms of legal aid provided. The working-hour allowance (half a working day) shall be applied to legal aid cases handled by an associate through participation in legal proceedings or extrajudicial representation.

  The level of allowance for an associate or lawyer providing legal aid at the request of the State shall be determined based on the quality of legal aid services provided, the duration of legal aid services provided, the complexity of the legal aid case, the form of presentation of legal aid results, and the form of legal aid provided.

  In the case of providing legal aid through participation in legal proceedings, the level of allowance paid to the lawyer providing legal aid at the request of the State shall be VND 500,000 per working hour or allocated as a lump sum for the case with a minimum of 3 months' basic salary and a maximum of 10 months' basic salary (depending on the nature and content of the case). The Ministry of Justice shall specify the nature and content of the case, the method of calculation of working hours, and the time basis for allocation or payment of allowances for lawyers according to working hours or working days.

  The period of providing legal aid includes the time for studying the case file and preparing documents for the provision of legal aid, the time for meeting and contacting the legal aid recipient or their relatives, the time for verifying the legal aid case, and the time spent working at agencies, organizations, or individuals related to the legal aid case. The basis for determining the working period of an associate or lawyer providing legal aid at the request of the State is the working period confirmed by the relevant agency, organization, or individual.”

  Article 2. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on November 10, 2015.

  Article 3. Implementation responsibilities

  Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall implement this Decree./.
