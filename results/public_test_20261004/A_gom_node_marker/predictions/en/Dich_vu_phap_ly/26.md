                                            CIRCULAR
GUIDANCE ON TIME CALCULATION BY WORKSHOP AND BUDGETING OF LEGAL ASSISTANCE
Pursuant to the Law on Legal Aid dated June 20, 2017;
Pursuant to Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 of the Government elaborating certain articles of the Law on Legal Aid;
Pursuant to Decree No. 96/2017/ND-CP dated August 16, 2017 of the Government defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Justice;
At the request of Director of Department of Legal Aid;
Minister of Justice promulgates Circular providing guidelines for calculation of working hours and incurring of legal aid expenditure.
Chapter I
                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope and regulated entities
1. This Circular provides guidelines on calculation of working hours and legal aid case expenditure as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for participation in legal proceedings or extrajudicial representation.
2. This Circular applies to the following entities:
a) Legal aid assistants;
b) Lawyers providing legal aid services;
c) Organizations signing contracts for legal aid services;
d) State legal aid centers (hereinafter referred to as “centers”) and their branches (hereinafter referred to as “branches”); Departments of Justice and other relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Article 2. Principles
1. Ensure compliance with regulations of law on remuneration, allowances and procedures for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid provision cases to persons providing legal aid or organizations signing contracts for legal aid.
2. Ensure legitimacy and rationality in declaring time and work as a basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid provision cases.
3. When declaring time and work for legal aid provision cases, declarers shall be responsible for the truthfulness of their declaration.
4. When verifying time and work of persons providing legal aid, verifiers shall be responsible for the truthfulness of their verification.
Chapter II
               METHOD OF CALCULATION OF TIME BASED ON WORK HOURS
Article 3. Time based on work hours
1. Time based on work hours (half a working day) shall apply to legal aid cases in the form of participation in proceedings or extra-procedural representation selected by persons providing legal aid as a basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for case handling.
2. A working session shall be calculated on the basis of 04 working hours. In case the legal aid-providing person works less than 04 hours, it shall be calculated as follows:
a) It shall be counted as half of a working session if the incomplete hours are less than 03 hours;
b) It shall be counted as one working session if the incomplete hours are at least 03 hours.

3. When applying the method for calculating time according to actual working sessions, legal aid-providing persons must record jobs and time of performing these jobs in the Schedule of Actual Time (Form TP-TGPL-01) promulgated together with this Circular.
Article 4. Time for participation in criminal proceedings

1. The basis for calculation of time for providing legal aid for criminal proceedings is:
a) Participation in interrogation of suspects or taking statements of accused persons, crime victims, witnesses and litigants in criminal cases;
b) Participation in confrontation, identification, recognition of voices, examination of scenes, autopsy, examination of traces on bodies, experimental investigation and performance of other tasks for participation in legal proceedings at the request of presiding agencies;
c) Working with presiding officers and presiding agencies;
d) Verify, collect and assess relevant documents, items and evidence according to stages of legal proceedings;
dd) Work with persons facing charges in detention cells of border guard stations, detention centers or prisons;
e) Meet persons facing charges on bail or their relatives; crime victims or their relatives; witnesses, litigants in criminal cases and other participants in legal proceedings;
g) Study files, make copies and prepare documents at presiding authorities or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts for provision of legal aid;
h) Prepare defense arguments;
i) Attend court sessions. If a legal aid provider attends a court session but the trial is postponed not at the request of the legal aid provider and no notice is given, the time during which the legal aid provider works with presiding authorities or proceeding officers on matters related to the postponement of the court session shall be included in such time;
k) Perform tasks related to procedures for appeal against first-instance judgments, appellate judgments, cassation or reopening procedures;
l) Perform other reasonable tasks as prescribed by law to serve the defense and protection.
2. Remuneration and allowances for legal aid-providing persons performing tasks specified in Points e, g, h, l Clause 1 of this Article shall not exceed the number of sessions of such tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith.
3. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons must be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours for activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or proceeding agencies;
b) Working hours for activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or proceeding agencies or persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
c) Working hours for activities specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where legal aid-providing persons are directly working;
d) Working hours for activities specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by accused persons or proceeding agencies or officers of border guard stations, custody houses, detention camps or prisons;
dd) Working hours for activities specified in Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
e) Working hours for activities specified in Point g Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts for legal aid provision;
g) The time of performing activities specified in Points h, k and l Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Center or its Branch or organization signing a legal aid contract;
h) The time of performance of activities specified in Point i Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or Court clerk.
Article 5. Time limit for participation in civil procedures

1. The time limit for provision of legal aid in a civil case shall be determined on the basis of:
a) Work with legal aid beneficiaries and relevant persons to determine the legal relationship of the dispute; assess conditions for initiating lawsuits, counter-claims, independent claims; provide guidance on drafting petitions for initiation of lawsuits, petitions for application of provisional urgent measures, counter-claims, independent claims, and evidence for the court; guide preparation of lawsuit files or other tasks during the stage of lawsuit initiation and acceptance of cases;
b) Work with legal aid beneficiaries, litigants and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals on the origin and content of disputes, evidence to prove or other tasks in the stage of trial preparation;
c) Attend meetings to inspect the submission, access, and disclosure of evidence; verify, collect, and assess necessary documents, items, and evidence;
d) Participate in taking statements from involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons; participate in activities of property appraisal and valuation and perform other procedural tasks at the request of the presiding agency;
dd) Participate in mediation as prescribed by law;
e) Working with proceeding officers and agencies in various stages of legal proceedings;
g) Meeting with litigants, witnesses, and other participants in legal proceedings regarding other relevant matters;
h) Studying documents, photocopying, and preparing materials at procedural authorities or centers, branches, or organizations signing contracts for legal aid services;
i) Preparing defense arguments;
k) Attending court sessions or meetings to resolve civil matters. In cases where legal aid-providing individuals attend court sessions but such sessions are postponed not at the request of the legal aid-providing individuals and without prior notice, the period during which the legal aid-providing individuals work with procedural authorities and proceeding officers on matters related to the postponement of court sessions shall be included in the time;
l) Performing tasks related to procedures for appeal against first-instance judgments, appellate judgments, cassation procedures, or reopening procedures;
m) Performing other reasonable tasks as prescribed by law to serve the protection.
2. The remuneration and allowances for legal aid-providing individuals performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i, and m Clause 1 of this Article must not exceed the number of sessions of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 02 enclosed herewith.
3. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons must be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours for activities specified in Points a and b Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts to provide legal aid;
b) Working hours for participation in meetings for inspection of the handover, access and disclosure of evidences specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons assigned to settle lawsuits; working hours for verification, collection and assessment of documents, objects and necessary evidences related to this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where legal aid-providing persons conduct verification and collection of evidences;
c) Working hours for participation in taking statements of involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by these persons or by proceeding officers or proceeding authorities; working hours for participation in appraisal and valuation of assets and performance of other procedural activities at the request of proceeding authorities specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where assets are appraised or valued or by proceeding officers or proceeding authorities;
d) Working hours for activities specified in Points dd and k Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by judges or Court clerks or mediators’ meetings clerks;
dd) Working hours for activities specified in Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding authorities or proceeding officers;
e) Working hours for activities specified in Point g Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
g) Working hours for activities specified in Point h Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons assigned to settle lawsuits or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts to provide legal aid;
h) The time of performing activities specified in Points i, l and m Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the center or its branches or organizations signing legal aid contracts.
Article 6. Time for participation in administrative proceedings
1. The basis for calculating the time of providing legal aid for cases of participation in administrative proceedings includes:

a) Participate in activities specified in Points a, b, c, d, e, g, h, i, k, l, and m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) Participate in dialogues.
2. The remuneration and allowances for persons providing legal aid services when performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i and m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular shall not exceed the number of sessions of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 03 enclosed herewith.
3. The working time of a legal aid-providing person must be certified by an agency, organization or individual as follows:
a) The time for performing activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations and individuals specified in Clause 3 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) The time for performing activities specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or the Dialogue Secretary.
Article 7. Time for participation in extrajudicial representation
1. The basis for calculating the time for providing legal aid for extrajudicial representation cases includes:
a) Meeting and contacting legal aid beneficiaries, their relatives, witnesses;
b) Studying documents and preparing materials for representing;
c) Verifying, collecting documents, items, evidence, and circumstances related to the representation;
d) Working with relevant agencies, organizations, and individuals;
dd) Participating in representation before competent agencies, organizations, and individuals handling the case.
2. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons must be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours for activities specified in Points a, d and dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons working with legal aid-providing persons or relevant agencies, organizations or individuals or competent agencies, organizations or individuals settling legal aid-related cases;
b) Working hours for activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by centers, branches or organizations signing contracts to provide legal aid.
Article 8. Determination of working hours in some special cases
1. In case at least 2 legal aid-providing persons provide legal aid for 1 legal aid recipient in the same legal aid-related case as prescribed by law, the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling the legal aid-related case is the actual working hours of each legal aid-providing person but the total working hours must not exceed 30 working sessions/case for cases involving participation in legal proceedings or 20 working sessions/case for extrajudicial representation cases.
2. In case procedural authorities suspend lawsuits
a) The basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling the legal aid-related case is the actual working hours from the time of suspension of the lawsuit;
b) When the lawsuit resumes settlement, the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling the legal aid-related case is the actual working hours of subsequent tasks from the time when the lawsuit resumes settlement until the end of the case.
3. In case of replacement or change of legal aid-providing persons
a) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the case handling for the replaced or changed person is the actual period during which they provided legal aid until the time they were replaced or changed;
b) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the replacement is the actual period during which the replacement performs subsequent tasks after being appointed. If the replacement chooses to allocate expenses for the case, the remuneration and allowances for both the replacement and the replaced person shall not exceed the allocated expenses for the case prescribed in Appendix No. 01, Appendix No. 02, and Appendix No. 03 enclosed with this Circular.
4. In case of providing legal aid for persons denounced or facing requisitions for charges or facing criminal charges at the time of arrest or temporary detainment of suspects or crime victims as prescribed by procedural law, the time and confirmation of time shall be as follows:
a) The time for verification, collection and assessment of necessary documents, items and evidence related to the case, which are verified and collected by agencies, organizations or individuals where the legal aid-providing person comes from;
b) Attend the process of taking statements from persons denounced or facing requisitions for charges, crime victims or other relevant persons; participate in denunciation activities, voice identification and recognition activities with investigators or procurators as confirmed by investigators or procurators;
c) Working time with the accused in the detention room of the Border Guard Station, Detention Center or Temporary Detention Camp confirmed by the accused or officials of the Border Guard Station, Detention Center or Temporary Detention Camp or the presiding agency;
d) Time of meeting with persons denounced or facing requisitions for charges or their relatives; crime victims or their relatives; other relevant persons as confirmed by these persons but not exceeding the number of sessions of this work under the form of assignment of case expenditure in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith according to the level of less serious crimes.
Chapter III
                                      CASE EXPENDITURE ASSIGNMENT
Article 9. Case expenditure assignment

1. The form of case expenditure assignment shall only apply to legal aid cases in the form of participation in legal proceedings selected in writing by legal aid-providing persons immediately after being assigned and remain unchanged throughout the process of performing legal aid cases.
2. Complexity, procedural requirements and contents of each specific case to determine the level of case expenditure assignment are as follows:
a) Apply regulations on classification of crimes in Clause 2 Article 1 of the Law on amendments to the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 dated June 20, 2017 to determine the level of case expenditure assignment for criminal proceeding participation cases.
The classification of crimes must be applied at the time of selecting the form of case expenditure assignment and based on one of the documents issued by the presiding agency: Decision to charge the suspect, Investigation conclusion, Indictment, Memorial, Judgment. In case there are multiple documents with different penalty frames, the document with the higher penalty frame shall be applied.
b) Apply regulations in Clause 3 Article 337 of the Civil Procedure Code and regulations in Article 5 of the Justice Minister's Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 prescribing criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the level of case expenditure assignment for civil proceeding participation cases.
c) Apply Clause 12 Article 3 of the Law on Administrative Procedures and Article 6 of Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the level of charging for cases of participation in administrative procedures.
3. When making a case assignment, the legal aid provider shall perform the following tasks:
a) For criminal proceedings: perform tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 4 of this Circular.
Details of tasks to be performed and maximum amounts thereof (converted by working session) for each case are specified in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith. In case the number of converted sessions for case allocation is lower than the three basic pay levels, the allocated amount shall equal the sum of these three levels.
b) For civil proceeding or administrative proceeding cases: perform tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 5 and Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.
Details of tasks to be performed and maximum amounts thereof (converted by working session) for each case are specified in Appendix No. 02 and Appendix No. 03 enclosed herewith. In case the number of converted sessions for case allocation is lower than 03 times the basic salary, the allocated amount shall equal 03 times the basic salary.
4. When incurring expenses for case-based provision of legal aid services, legal aid-providing persons shall list jobs and certify the performed jobs in the Job List (Form TP-TGPL-02 enclosed herewith).
5. In case a legal aid case is provided with legal aid services in multiple stages as specified in the Appendix enclosed herewith, the level of case allocation shall be applied to each stage but must not exceed 10 times the base salary.
Article 10. Case-based allocation in some special cases
1. In case 2 persons providing legal aid for 1 person are provided with legal aid for the same case, based on the actual work of each person, the maximum case-based allocation is 130% of the corresponding allocation specified in the Appendix enclosed herewith but not exceeding 10 times the basic salary.

Example 1: A is prosecuted for a crime with a penalty bracket of very serious crime, the Director of Legal Aid Center assigns 2 persons to provide legal aid for A from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work done by 2 persons providing legal aid, the maximum case-based allocation is: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, the case-based allocation is only 10 times the basic salary.
2. In case of providing legal aid for 1 person who is prosecuted for 2 or more crimes in the same case, the case-based allocation for the person providing legal aid shall be as follows:
a) If 2 crimes have different penalty brackets, based on the actual work done, the maximum case-based allocation is 130% of the corresponding allocation specified in the Appendix enclosed herewith but not exceeding 10 times the basic salary.
Example 2: A is prosecuted for 2 crimes: 1 crime with a penalty bracket of serious crime and 1 crime with a penalty bracket of very serious crime. The legal aid worker provides legal aid for A from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work done, the maximum case-based allocation is: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, the case-based allocation is only 10 times the basic salary.
b) If two charges have the same penalty bracket, based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of the charge is 130% of the maximum amount of the charge for such case specified in Appendix No. 01 enclosed with this Circular but not exceeding 10 times the basic salary. Example 03: B is prosecuted for 02 charges with the same penalty bracket of very serious crime. The legal assistant provides legal assistance to B from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of the charge is not exceeding: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, the charge shall be allocated for 10 times the basic salary.
3. In case the presiding agency suspends the case
a) The charge of the legal aid provider is based on the actual work performed by the legal aid provider until the suspension time;
b) When the case continues to be resolved, the charge of the legal aid provider is based on the work performed by the legal aid provider from the time of resumption of the resolution to the end of the case.
4. In case of replacement or change of legal aid provider
a) The charge of the person replaced or changed is based on the actual work performed by the person replaced or changed until the time of replacement or change;
b) The charge of the person appointed as replacement is based on the actual work performed by the person appointed as replacement.
If the person appointed as replacement chooses the working time by session, the remuneration and allowance for both persons (the replacement and the person replaced or changed) shall not exceed 30 working sessions/case according to Clause 1 Article 13 of the Government's Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 elaborating on certain articles of the Law on Legal Aid.
Chapter IV
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 11. Transitional provisions
For activities arising before the effective date of this Circular, regulations of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of costs for legal aid-related cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of costs for legal aid-related cases shall continue to apply.
Article 12. Entry into force
1. This Circular comes into force from July 15, 2021.
2. This Circular replaces Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of costs for legal aid-related cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of costs for legal aid-related cases.
