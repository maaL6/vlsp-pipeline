                                                   DECREE

     ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF FIRE PREVENTION AND FIRE FIGHTING

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated December 25, 2001;

  Pursuant to the Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting dated June 29, 2001;

  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 02, 2002 and the Ordinance on Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated April 02, 2008;

  At the request of the Minister of Public Security;

  The Government promulgates a Decree prescribing penalties for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting;

  Chapter 1.

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, principles of sanctioning, forms of sanctioning, levels of sanction, remedial measures, competence to sanction, order and procedures for sanctioning, complaints, denunciations and settlement of complaints and denunciations on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting.

  2. Administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting or related to the field of fire prevention and fighting which have not been prescribed in this Decree but have been prescribed in other Government's decrees on sanctioning administrative violations shall be sanctioned according to regulations in such decrees.

  Article 2. Subjects of application

  Individuals and organizations committing acts of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and fighting on the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall be sanctioned according to provisions of this Decree and other relevant law provisions on sanctioning administrative violations.

  Article 3. Principles of sanctioning administrative violations

  Principles of sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting shall comply with provisions in Article 3 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 4. Forms of sanctioning administrative violations

  1. For each administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting prescribed in this Decree, violating individuals or organizations must be subject to one of the following main forms of sanction:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fine.

  2. Depending on the nature and severity of the violations, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting may also be subject to one or more of the following additional forms of sanction:

  a) Depriving the right to use licenses, practicing certificates;

  b) Confiscating material evidences and means used for administrative violations.

  3. In addition to the main forms of sanction, the additional forms of sanction prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Forced to implement measures to remedy the environmental pollution caused by administrative violations;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violations or enforced dismantling of illegally constructed works or structures;

  c) Enforced rectification of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations;

  d) Enforced reduction in quantity of substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards according to prescribed norms;

  dd) Enforced relocation of substances with fire and explosion hazards caused by administrative violations to storage facilities or locations as prescribed;

  e) Other remedial measures prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  4. Persons who commit acts of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting causing damage to organizations and individuals shall, in addition to being administratively sanctioned under Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article, pay damages in accordance with civil law.

  5. Foreigners who commit acts of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting within the territory of Vietnam may be expelled from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Deportation may be applied as a principal or additional sanction. The authority, procedures for applying the sanction of deportation shall comply with current laws on deportation under administrative procedures.

  Chapter 2.

                      ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES

  Article 5. Violations against regulations on promulgation, dissemination and implementation of fire prevention and firefighting regulations and internal rules

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to comply with fire prevention and firefighting regulations and internal rules;

  b) Damaging or making fire prevention and firefighting regulations, internal rules, signs, warning signs, or instructions unclear or illegible.

  c) Failing to provide fire prevention and firefighting regulations, internal rules, signs, warning signs, or instructions in conformity with regulations.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to post fire prevention and firefighting regulations, internal rules, signs, warning signs, or instructions as prescribed;

  b) Failing to comply with fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations;

  c) Failing to disseminate fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations to persons under their management;

  d) Promulgating fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations which do not contain sufficient contents or are not suitable for the nature of fire and explosion hazards of facilities.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to arrange or post fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to promulgate or promulgating fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations in contravention of legal normative documents of the State.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original state altered due to administrative violations specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory promulgation, dissemination and posting of fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations in accordance with regulations applicable to the violations specified in Points a, c and d Clause 2 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 6. Violations against regulations on fire safety inspection

  1. A fine of from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to present documents serving fire safety inspection.

  2. A fine of from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to implement or implement recommendations on fire prevention and firefighting at the prescribed time limit as requested in writing by competent authorities;

  b) Failure to appoint responsible persons to join the fire safety inspection team as prescribed;

  c) Failure to organize fire safety inspection as prescribed.

  3. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to comply with written instructions and direction on fire prevention and firefighting issued by competent authorities;

  b) Failing to comply with fire prevention and firefighting recommendations issued in writing by competent authorities;

  c) Obstructing inspection and examination of fire prevention and firefighting activities by competent authorities.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to issue written notification and commitment on satisfaction of requirements and maintenance of conditions for ensuring fire safety during use of houses, structures, and production and business activities after a facility is approved and commissioned for fire prevention and firefighting purposes.

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on records for management of fire prevention and firefighting safety

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to make statistics and submit reports on fire prevention and firefighting activities as prescribed;

  b) Failing to have sufficient documents prescribed in records for management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting activities;

  c) Failing to update information about changes related to fire prevention and firefighting activities in records for management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting activities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to prepare documents on management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in management, preservation and use of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failing to keep records and documents on monitoring and management of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for storing, arranging or organizing substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards in excess of the prescribed quantity or weight, failing to maintain the prescribed distance or group separation between them.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using equipment and devices containing or holding substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards without inspection certificates or failing to satisfy fire safety requirements as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for illegally storing substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for illegally using substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards.

  6. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of substances and fire/explosion hazards for the acts specified in Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Forcible compilation and establishment of records and books for monitoring and managing substances and fire/explosion hazards according to regulations for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  b) Forcible preservation, arrangement, reduction of quantity and volume of substances and fire/explosion hazards according to regulations for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Forcible transfer of substances and fire/explosion hazards to warehouses or locations according to regulations for the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in production and trading of substances and fire/explosion hazards

  1. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to take measures and use equipment to prevent electrostatic accumulation as prescribed;

  b) Failing to adopt natural ventilation or forced ventilation equipment as prescribed.

  2. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to install detection and treatment equipment for leakage of substances and explosives around the environment as prescribed;

  b) Failing to have plans or equipment for handling incidents of rupture of tanks, equipment and pipelines containing petroleum products, petroleum products and other flammable liquids;

  c) Charging or filling explosives or dangerous goods not at the prescribed place or charging or filling explosives or dangerous goods into equipment of improper type or incompatible with explosives or dangerous goods.

  3. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for manufacturing, trading, charging or filling explosives or dangerous goods without license as prescribed.

  4. A fine of from VND 20,000,000 to VND 25,000,000 shall be imposed for manufacturing, trading in substances and/or explosives on the list of substances and explosives banned from trading.

  5. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards for violations specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Forcible remedy of environmental pollution as prescribed for violations specified at Point a and Point b Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on transport of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Damaging or fraying the license for transport of substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards;

  b) Failing to carry the license for transport when transporting substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards as prescribed;

  c) Failing to remove the logo of substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards affixed on the transport vehicle after the substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards have been removed from the transport vehicle;

  d) Losing the license for transport of substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards but having promptly reported such to competent authorities.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Arranging flammable and explosive substances on transport vehicles against regulations;

  b/ Stopping or parking the means of transport of inflammable and/or explosive substances or goods at places where such transport is not permitted as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to maintain adequate fire safety conditions when using motorized transport vehicles for transporting substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards during the permitted transport period as prescribed;

  b) Transporting other goods together with substances or inflammable and explosive goods on the same means of transport without permission as prescribed;

  c) Transporting unauthorized persons on means carrying flammable and/or explosive substances or goods.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Transporting hazardous substances or goods in quantities, volumes or categories exceeding those specified in the transport permit;

  b) Failing to post the symbol of transport of hazardous substances or goods on the means of transport as prescribed;

  c) Failing to comply with fire safety requirements or failing to follow instructions of competent persons when unloading, pumping or transferring hazardous substances or goods from the means of transport as prescribed;

  d) Unloading, pumping or transferring hazardous substances or goods at locations where fire safety conditions are not satisfied as prescribed;

  dd) Unloading, pumping or transferring hazardous substances or goods while they are being transported to another vehicle without permission of a competent authority;

  e) Erasing or altering the transport permit;

  g) Losing the transport permit but failing to report it to the competent authority.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Transporting flammable and explosive substances or goods without a license for transport of flammable and explosive substances or goods as prescribed;

  b) Forging or using a forged license for transport of flammable and explosive substances or goods.

  6. Additional penalties:

  a) Suspend the license for transport of flammable and explosive substances or goods if the violation prescribed in Point e Clause 4 of this Article is committed;

  b) Confiscate the flammable and explosive substances or goods if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 1, Point a, Point b Clause 5 of this Article is committed.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced rearrangement of flammable and explosive substances or goods if the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 2 of this Article is committed;

  b) Enforced reduction in quantity, weight or type of flammable and explosive substances or goods as prescribed in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Enforced relocation of flammable and explosive substances or goods to storage facilities or locations as prescribed for the acts specified in Points a and b Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting during use of fire sources, heat sources, devices and instruments that generate fire or heat

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to arrange cooking or worship places in a manner that ensures fire safety and firefighting requirements as prescribed;

  b) Bringing matches, lighters, mobile phones, electronic devices or devices and instruments that generate fire or heat to prohibited areas.

  2. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for using fire sources, heat sources, devices and instruments that generate fire or heat without maintaining a safe distance from fire sources, heat sources, devices and instruments that generate fire or heat as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Using fire sources, mobile phones, electronic devices or other fire and heat generating devices in prohibited areas;

  b) Welding or cutting metals without taking measures to ensure fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in electrical design, installation, management and use

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to provide regulations on fire safety during electricity use at the facility.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for using electrical equipment not in compliance with instructions of the manufacturer.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to maintain regular operation of the emergency lighting system;

  b) Changing the design or changing the structure or main specifications of the electrical system or electrical equipment without approval of a competent person or authority;

  c) Installing and using electrical conductors, power cables or switching, protection or consumption equipment not ensuring fire safety;

  d) Using electrical equipment in excess of design load;

  dd) Using electrical equipment at prohibited places.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Using electrical equipment not meeting fire safety requirements in a fire and explosion hazard environment;

  b) Failing to have backup power sources as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to design and install electrical systems serving fire prevention, firefighting and rescue requirements as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced rectification of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on fire safety during design, construction, inspection and maintenance of lightning protection systems

  1. A fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to prepare records on monitoring of lightning protection systems as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out periodic inspections of lightning protection systems as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to rectify faults or damage that cause the loss of effectiveness of lightning protection systems.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to install lightning protection systems at houses and structures required to install such systems as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced rectification of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in investment and construction

  1. A fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to adopt measures and equipment to ensure fire safety during construction.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to design fire protection systems for structures requiring fire protection design.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for putting structures into use without satisfying one of fire safety requirements if the structures are not required to undergo fire safety approval process as per regulation.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to comply with fire safety design approved by competent authorities;

  b) Failure to submit documents for re-approval when renovating, expanding or changing the uses of the structures during construction and use as per regulation.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Organizing construction of works subject to fire prevention and firefighting approval without obtaining the "Certificate of Fire Prevention and Fighting Approval" as prescribed;

  b) Manufacturing or modifying motor vehicles with special requirements for fire safety assurance without approval by competent authorities.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for putting houses or works into operation or use without organizing fire prevention and firefighting commissioning as prescribed.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory remedial measures for ensuring fire safety in case of violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory construction and installation of fire protection works according to approved fire protection designs in case of violations specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Mandatory organization of remedial measures for unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations or mandatory organization of fire prevention and firefighting commissioning by state management agencies in case of violations specified in Clause 6 of this Article.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on fire safety distance

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Arranging and arranging materials and goods not maintaining a safe fire separation distance as prescribed;

  b) Failing to organize industrial hygiene resulting in the possibility of creating a fire and explosion-hazardous environment.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for building fire walls, fire partitions or fire doors and other fire prevention solutions against regulations.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Placing combustible materials on ceilings, floors, walls, or in storage areas where such placement is prohibited;

  b) Building houses in forests or near forests against regulations on preventing spread of fire.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to clean flammable substances within safety corridors of pipelines for petroleum, gas and petroleum products;

  b) Construction of works violating fire separation distance.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to build fire walls, fire partitions, fire doors and fire prevention solutions as prescribed;

  b) Loss of fire prevention effect of fire walls, fire partitions, fire doors and fire prevention solutions.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of original condition altered by administrative violations specified in Point a Clause 1, Point b Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory dismantling of illegally constructed works if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 3 or Point b Clause 4 of this Article is committed;

  c) Enforced rectification of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Point a Clause 3, Point a Clause 4, and Point a Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 16. Penalties for violations against regulations on escape in fire prevention and firefighting

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for designing exit doors not opening in the direction of escape, installing mirrors in the staircases.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Arranging or placing supplies, goods, vehicles, and other items that obstruct the escape route;

  b) Removing, detaching, or damaging emergency lighting equipment, signs, or wayfinding devices on the escape route;

  c) Failing to install signs or wayfinding devices on the escape route.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to provide adequate rescue equipment and devices as prescribed;

  b) Failing to provide smoke extraction and ventilation equipment for the emergency exit;

  c) Failing to provide emergency lighting on the emergency exit or providing insufficient lighting or ineffective lighting;

  d) Designing or building the number of, area, width of, or design of the emergency exit or staircase not in compliance with regulations.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for closing, squeezing, or blocking the emergency exit.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 15,000,000 to VND 25,000,000 shall be imposed for damaging the effectiveness of the emergency exit.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory remedial measures for the unsafe conditions caused by the administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 17. Violations against fire prevention and firefighting plan of facilities

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to manage fire prevention and firefighting plan as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Preparing fire prevention and firefighting plan which fails to meet requirements as prescribed;

  b) Failure to disseminate fire prevention and firefighting plan as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to submit fire prevention and firefighting plan for approval as prescribed;

  b) Failure to participate in practice of fire prevention and firefighting plan as prescribed;

  c) Failure to add or adjust fire prevention and firefighting plans as prescribed;

  d) Failure to practice all fire scenarios in approved fire prevention and firefighting plans.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to develop fire prevention and firefighting plans as prescribed;

  b) Failure to organize practice of fire prevention and firefighting plans as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory remedial measures for unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on fire alarm information

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for failure to maintain intact fire alarm devices or failure to keep them operational.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to have fire alarm devices or fire alarm signals as prescribed;

  b) Delayed or untimely fire alarm signals; inadequate fire alarm signals.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Falsifying fire alarm signals;

  b) Failure to give fire alarm signals or obstruction of fire alarm signals;

  c) Failure to promptly repair faults of fire alarm information reception devices of Fire and Rescue Police authorities upon receipt of written requests.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Forcible remedy of initial state changed due to administrative violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on installation, maintenance and use of fire prevention and firefighting equipment

  1. A fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Moving or changing the position of the fire prevention and firefighting equipment installed according to the design approved by a competent authority;

  b) Blocking the fire prevention and firefighting equipment.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to inspect and maintain the fire prevention and firefighting system and equipment on a regular basis as prescribed;

  b) Failing to provide adequate or synchronized fire and rescue equipment as prescribed;

  c) Failing to provide common fire and rescue equipment for motor vehicles with 4 seats or more, or specialized motor vehicles used for transporting flammable and explosive goods as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Providing, installing, or using fire and rescue equipment that has not been inspected for quality, type, or specifications as prescribed;

  b) Providing fire and rescue equipment that is inappropriate for the fire and explosion hazards of the establishment as prescribed;

  c) Using fire and rescue equipment at the fire station for other purposes;

  d) Failing to store sufficient water for firefighting as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to provide common fire extinguishers for houses and works as prescribed;

  b) Losing, damaging or rendering ineffective fire prevention and fighting equipment.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to provide or install fire alarm and firefighting system as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Forced to overcome unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Forced to overcome initial changes due to administrative violations specified at point b clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and fighting

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of entering the fire prevention and fighting area without permission of competent persons.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to promptly rescue people or property or carry out firefighting.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to adequately prepare forces, equipment, water sources and other conditions for firefighting as prescribed;

  b) Failure to arrange traffic routes and access positions for fire trucks and other motorized firefighting vehicles as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to comply with orders of the fire commander;

  b) Failure to comply with the order for mobilization to participate in firefighting issued by a competent person;

  c) Obstructing the operation of fire and firefighting forces and equipment;

  d) Do not participate in remedying consequences of fire upon request of competent persons;

  dd) Failing to protect the scene of the fire as prescribed.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to organize rescue, firefighting or fire prevention;

  b) Taking advantage of fire prevention and firefighting to harm the health or property of citizens and state property.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on dissemination of fire prevention and firefighting laws, knowledge, learning, training and education

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for damaging fire prevention and firefighting banners, posters, billboards, propaganda pictures or photos.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to organize dissemination of fire prevention and firefighting laws and knowledge as prescribed;

  b) Employing firefighters at grassroots level, operators of motor vehicles with 4 seats or more, operators of motor vehicles transporting flammable and explosive goods who have not undergone training courses in fire prevention and firefighting or have not been granted certificates of completion of training courses in fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to organize training courses in fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory restoration of the original condition which has been altered by the administrative violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on establishment, organization and management of grassroots fire prevention and firefighting forces and specialized fire prevention and firefighting forces

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to ensure the number of personnel and the duration of each shift in fire prevention and firefighting;

  b) The grassroots fire department fails to proficiently use the fire-fighting equipment installed at the grassroots level.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for failure to organize the shift rotation at the grassroots level as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Establishing a grassroots or specialized fire department but failing to meet requirements as prescribed;

  b) Failure to manage or maintain the operation of the grassroots or specialized fire department as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to participate in fire prevention and firefighting activities when requested by competent persons.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to establish grassroots or specialized fire prevention and firefighting forces as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory remedial measures for the unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on manufacturing and trading of fire and rescue equipment and fire prevention and firefighting design

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed upon the fire prevention and firefighting design unit that fails to satisfy eligibility requirements regarding legal entity and professional qualifications as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed upon the individual or organization that manufactures and trades in fire and rescue equipment when they fail to meet eligibility requirements regarding facilities, professional qualifications and training in fire prevention as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for modifying a fire truck or fireboat without permission from a competent authority.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed upon the unit that installs fire protection works without satisfying eligibility requirements regarding legal entity and professional qualifications as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedial measures for unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 24. Violations against regulations on compulsory fire insurance

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to take preventive measures or limiting damage to policyholders under regulations on compulsory fire insurance.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to purchase compulsory fire insurance as prescribed;

  b) Purchasing compulsory fire insurance against regulations or premium rates of compulsory fire insurance promulgated by the Ministry of Finance;

  c) Failing to separate the compulsory fire insurance contract in the package insurance contract as prescribed;

  d) Failing to deduct compulsory fire insurance premiums for funding firefighting activities as prescribed.

  Article 25. Acts leading to house fires

  1. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire not causing damage or causing damage assessed at < VND 25,000,000.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire causing damage assessed at from VND 25,000,000 to VND 50,000,000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire causing damage assessed at > VND 50,000,000 but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 26. Penalties imposed upon individuals and organizations for house fires

  1. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire causing damage assessed at < VND 2,000,000.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire causing damage assessed at from VND 2,000,000 to VND 10,000,000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations, thereby causing a fire or explosion resulting in a loss of property assessed at from VND 10,000,000 to under VND 25,000,000.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally causing a fire or explosion resulting in a loss of property assessed at from VND 25,000,000 to under VND 50,000,000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for irresponsibly causing a fire or explosion but not to the extent of being liable to criminal prosecution.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally causing a fire or explosion resulting in a loss of property assessed at over VND 50,000,000 without being liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 27. Deportation

  Foreigners who commit administrative violations prescribed in Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 18, 20 and 21 of this Decree may, depending on the severity of their violations, be subject to deportation from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  Chapter 3.

                             AUTHORITY TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES

  Article 28. Power to impose administrative penalties of People’s Public Security Forces

  1. Soldiers on duty of People’s Public Security Forces shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose fines of up to VND 200,000.

  2. Heads of police stations and leaders of competent officers specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose fines of up to VND 500,000.

  3. Chiefs of commune-level police authorities (hereinafter referred to as “commune-level police authorities”) shall have the power to:

  a/ Warning;

  b/ Fines of up to VND 2,000,000;

  c/ Confiscation of material evidence and means used in administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;

  d/ Forcible restoration of the initial state altered due to administrative violations.

  4. Chiefs of police offices of urban districts, rural districts, towns or provincial cities (hereinafter referred collectively to as district-level police offices) shall have the right:

  a/ To issue warnings;

  b/ To impose fines of up to VND 10,000,000;

  c/ To strip off the right to use licenses or professional practice certificates under their jurisdiction;

  d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  5. Heads of Fire and Rescue Police Divisions, Heads of Police Divisions for Administrative Management of Social Order, Heads of Road and Rail Traffic Police Divisions, Heads of Waterway Police Divisions, Heads of Police Divisions for Prevention and Control of Environmental Crimes, Heads of Police Divisions for Investigation into Social Order-related Crimes, provincial-level Police Departments and Heads of Fire and Rescue Police Divisions of district level under the provincial-level Fire and Rescue Police Department shall, within the ambit of their functions, tasks and powers, have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 10,000,000.

  c) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;

  d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  6. Directors of Police Departments of provinces and central-affiliated cities (hereinafter referred to as “provincial level”) and Directors of Fire Prevention and Fighting Police Departments have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 30,000,000.

  c) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;

  d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  e) Directors of provincial police authorities have the power to decide on the imposition of the deportation penalty under the delegation of the Minister of Public Security.

  7. Directors of the Fire and Rescue Police Department, the Police Department for Administrative Management of Social Order, the Road and Railway Traffic Police Department, the Waterway Police Department, the Environmental Crime Prevention and Control Police Department, and the Investigation Police Department for Social Order Crimes shall, within their functions, tasks and powers, have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose fines up to the maximum level prescribed at Point a, Clause 2, Article 14 of the Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the 2008 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  c) Deprive of the right to use licenses or professional practice certificates falling under their jurisdiction;

  d) Confiscate goods or means used for committing administrative violations;

  e) Apply remedies specified in this Decree.

  8. The Minister of Public Security shall decide to apply the sanctioning form of expulsion.

  Article 29. Competence to sanction administrative violations of chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

  1. Chairpersons of commune-level People’s Committees have the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 2,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations worth up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original condition altered by the administrative violation.

  2. Chairpersons of People’s Committees of districts have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;

  dd) Apply remedial measures prescribed in this Decree.

  3. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees have the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose fines up to the maximum level prescribed at Point a Clause 2 Article 14 of the Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the 2008 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  c) Confiscate goods and means used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;

  dd) Apply remedial measures prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 30. Authority to impose administrative penalties of other agencies

  In addition to persons having authority to impose penalties specified in Articles 28 and 29 of this Decree, persons having authority to impose penalties according to the provisions of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, when performing their functions and tasks within the areas under their management, if detecting administrative violations prescribed in this Decree, shall have the right to impose penalties as prescribed by law.

  Article 31. Authorization for imposition of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting

  Persons having authority to impose administrative penalties specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 of Article 28; Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of Article 29 of this Decree may authorize their deputies to exercise their authority to impose administrative penalties. The authorization must be made in writing. Authorized deputies shall be responsible before their heads and before law for decisions to impose administrative penalties.

  Article 32. Principles for determination of authority to impose administrative penalties

  1. The authority to impose administrative penalties under this Decree is the authority applicable to an administrative violation. In case of imposing a fine, the authority to impose the penalty shall be determined based on the maximum level of the fine bracket prescribed for each specific violation. In case an administrative violation falls under the authority to impose penalties of more than one person, the penalty shall be imposed by the first person to receive the case.

  2. In case of imposing a fine on a person who commits multiple administrative violations, the authority to impose the penalty shall be determined according to the following principles:

  a) If the form and level of penalty prescribed for each violation fall under the authority of the person imposing the penalty, such authority shall still belong to that person.

  b) If the form and level of sanction prescribed for one of acts of violation fall beyond the competence of the sanctioning person, he/she shall have to transfer the violation case to the competent sanctioning level.

  3. In cases where the persons with sanctioning competence of the People's Public Security are handling individuals or organizations committing violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting under this Decree but detect that such individuals or organizations also commit administrative violations prescribed in other Government's Decrees, they may sanction such administrative violations.

  Chapter 4.

                         ORDER AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 33. Order and procedures for sanctioning administrative violations

  1. Upon detecting administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting, the persons with sanctioning competence must issue orders to stop immediately the acts of violation.

  2. The order and procedures for sanctioning administrative violations are as follows:

  a) For administrative violations which are sanctioned by warning or fines of up to VND 200,000, the persons with sanctioning competence shall issue decisions to sanction on the spot according to simple procedures prescribed in Article 54 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  b/ For administrative violations which are sanctioned with fines of over VND 200,000, the persons with sanctioning competence shall have to make records on such administrative violations according to the provisions in Article 55 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and issue sanctioning decisions according to the provisions in Article 56 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations; if the violation falls beyond the sanctioning competence of the record maker, he/she shall have to send the record to the person with sanctioning competence for issuing a sanctioning decision according to regulations.

  c) The forms used for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting shall be applied uniformly according to the forms prescribed in the field of security, social order and safety.

  3. When imposing a fine, the specific fine level for an administrative violation is the average level of the fine bracket for such act prescribed in this Decree; in case of an aggravating or extenuating circumstance, the fine level may be reduced but not reduced below the minimum level of the fine bracket; in case of an aggravating circumstance, the fine level may be increased but not increased above the maximum level of the fine bracket already prescribed. When imposing a fine, the offender must be informed of the fine bracket and the specific fine level.

  4. For a minor from full 16 to under 18 years of age who commits an administrative violation and is subject to a fine, the fine level must not exceed half of the fine level applicable to a adult; in case he/she has no money for payment, his/her parents or guardian shall pay instead.

  Article 34. Application of measures to prevent administrative violations and ensure the imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting

  Where it is necessary to promptly prevent administrative violations or ensure the imposition of penalties for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting, measures such as temporary detention of persons; temporary seizure of exhibits and means used in administrative violations; body search; search of transport vehicles and objects; search of places where exhibits and means used in administrative violations are hidden shall be applied in accordance with the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 35. Collection of fines for administrative violations

  1. Individuals and organizations fined for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting must pay fines within the time limit stated in the State Treasury specified in the sanctioning decision, except for cases where fines have been paid on the spot as prescribed in Article 54 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and receipt of fine receipts. The collection of fines must use receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance in accordance with regulations.

  2. In remote areas, rivers, seas, areas where travel is difficult or outside working hours, sanctioned individuals and organizations may pay fines to competent sanctioning persons. Competent sanctioning persons shall collect fines on the spot and remit them to the State Treasury in accordance with Clause 3, Article 58 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. Sanctioned persons may not pay fines until they receive fine receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance in accordance with regulations.

  Article 36. Execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting must execute sanctioning decisions within 10 days from the date of receipt of sanctioning decisions. Past the above time limit, sanctioned individuals and organizations that fail to voluntarily execute sanctioning decisions shall be forced to execute them in accordance with law.

  2. Individuals subject to fines of VND 500,000 or more may postpone the execution of sanctioning decisions according to the provisions in Article 65 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. When it is deemed necessary, persons with sanctioning competence or agencies conducting sanctioning of individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting may publicly notify the administrative violations and sanctioning decisions to agencies, organizations or local administrations where the violating individuals work or reside and to the directly superior agencies managing or local administrations where the violating organizations register their operations.

  Article 37. Deprivation of the right to use licenses, professional practice certificates

  The deprivation of the right to use licenses, professional practice certificates shall be effected only for violations prescribed in this Decree and other Government's decrees on sanctioning of administrative violations related to fire prevention and fighting and must comply with the provisions of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 38. Transferring dossiers of violation cases with criminal signs for penal liability examination In case of considering violation cases for deciding on sanctions, if deeming that the violation acts show signs of crimes or in case of having issued sanctioning decisions, if later detecting that the violation acts show signs of crimes while the statute of limitations for penal liability examination has not expired, the dossiers of violation cases with criminal signs must be transferred to the competent criminal procedure-conducting agencies according to the provisions in Article 62 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 39. Complaints, denunciations

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting or their lawful representatives may lodge complaints about sanctioning decisions, decisions on application of measures to prevent and ensure handling of administrative violations of competent persons.

  All individuals have the right to denounce illegal acts against regulations on administrative penalties for violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting to competent state agencies.

  2. Complaints, denunciations and settlement thereof shall comply with regulations of law on complaints and denunciations.

  3. The initiation of lawsuits against decisions on administrative penalties, decisions on application of preventive measures and assurance of administrative penalties for violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting shall comply with regulations of law on administrative procedures.

  Chapter 5.

                                            IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 40. Implementation effect

  This Decree takes effect from August 05, 2012 and replaces Decree No. 123/2005/ND-CP dated October 05, 2005 prescribing administrative penalties for violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting.

  Article 41. Implementation responsibility

  1. The Minister of Public Security shall organize implementation, provide guidance, inspect and expedite implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities and relevant organizations and individuals shall implement this Decree./.
