                                                   DECREE

     OF THE GOVERNMENT NO. 74/2003/ND-CP OF JUNE 26, 2003, ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ELECTRICITY

                                                   THE GOVERNMENT

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization of December 25, 2001;
  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations of July 2, 2002;
  At the request of the Minister of Industry,

                                                  DECREE:

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  Administrative violations in the field of electricity means intentional or unintentional violations of regulations of law on state management in the field of electricity operation and use which are not crimes and must be administratively sanctioned as prescribed by law, including:

  1. Violations of regulations on electricity operation permit.

  2. Violations of regulations on construction, production, transmission and distribution of electricity.

  3. Violations of regulations on power supply.

  4. Violations of regulations on electricity use.

  5. Violations of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grid.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  2. Foreign individuals and organizations committing acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity within the territory, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, except otherwise provided for by international agreements which the State of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam has signed or acceded to.

  3. Minors committing acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned under the provisions of Article 7 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 3. Principles for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. The sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity must be carried out by competent persons prescribed in Chapter III of this Decree in strict compliance with law.

  2. All administrative violations in the field of electricity must be detected in time and stopped immediately. The sanctioning must be carried out quickly, fairly and thoroughly; all consequences caused by administrative violations in the field of electricity must be remedied in strict compliance with law.

  3. An act of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned only once. A person who commits many acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned for each act of violation. Many persons jointly committing an act of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall each be sanctioned.

  4. The sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity must be based on the nature and seriousness of the violations, personal records and extenuating and aggravating circumstances in order to decide appropriate sanctioning forms and measures. Extenuating and aggravating circumstances shall apply according to the provisions of Articles 8 and 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  5. No administrative penalties shall be imposed in the field of electricity in cases of emergency, legitimate self-defense, unexpected events or administrative violations while suffering from mental illness or other diseases that deprive them of the ability to perceive or control their acts.

  Article 4. Statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity and period during which no administrative penalty is imposed

  1. The statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity is one year from the date on which the administrative violation is committed; if the above-mentioned statute of limitations expires, no administrative penalty shall be imposed but the remedial measures prescribed in Clause 3 Article 5 of this Decree shall still be applied to remedy the consequences caused by the administrative violation against regulations on electricity.

  2. For individuals who are prosecuted or brought to trial according to criminal procedures but later decided to suspend investigation or suspend the case by competent authorities, if their acts show signs of administrative violations against regulations on electricity, they shall be administratively sanctioned; in this case, the statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity is three months from the date on which the person competent to impose penalties receives the suspension decision and the dossier of the violation.

  3. Within the time limit specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, if the violating individual or organization commits a new administrative violation against regulations on electricity or deliberately evades or obstructs the imposition of penalties, the statute of limitations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply. The statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity shall be recalculated from the time of committing the new administrative violation or the time of terminating the act of evading or obstructing the imposition of penalties.

  4. Individuals and organizations that are administratively sanctioned for violations against regulations on electricity, if they do not commit new violations within one year from the date of completely serving the sanctioning decision or from the date of expiry of the statute of limitations for executing the sanctioning decision, shall be considered as having not been administratively sanctioned for violations against regulations on electricity.

  Article 5. Forms of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity

  1. Primary forms of sanction: For each administrative violation in the field of electricity, violating individuals or organizations must be subject to one of the following primary forms of sanction:

  a) Warning: applied to individuals and organizations committing minor, first-time administrative violations with extenuating factors or to all administrative violations committed by minors aged between full 14 years and under 16 years. Warning shall be decided in writing;

  b) Fine: based on the nature and severity of the violation, the fine level in the fine bracket prescribed in this Decree shall be decided for each violation. The specific fine level for an administrative violation in the field of electricity is the average level of the fine bracket for such act; if the violation has extenuating factors, the fine level may be reduced but not lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation has aggravating factors, the fine level may be increased but not higher than the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  2. Additional forms of sanction: Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be subject to one or more of the following additional forms of sanction:

  a) Depriving the right to use for a definite period of time the electricity operation permit;

  b) Confiscating material evidences and means used for committing administrative violations in the field of electricity.

  3. In addition to the primary forms of sanction and additional forms of sanction prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be forced to restore the initial state altered due to their administrative violations in the field of electricity or forced to dismantle illegally constructed works.
  Chapter 2:
     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON ELECTRICITY, PENALTIES AND FINES FOR THESE VIOLATIONS

  Article 6. Violations against regulations on electricity operation permits

  1. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using an expired electricity operation permit.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Leasing or lending the electricity operation permit;

  b) Modifying the contents of the electricity operation permit by oneself.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Carrying out electricity activities without obtaining an electricity operation permit;

  b) Engaging in electricity activities after being suspended or deprived of the right to use the license for electricity activities by competent authorities.

  4. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individual or organization may also have their right to use the license for electricity activities deprived from six months to one year in case of committing the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on construction, production, transmission and distribution of electricity

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 700,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Felling trees, pruning trees or demolishing works against regulations when building, operating or repairing power grid;

  b) Failure to restore the original state of relevant works which have been changed due to the construction or repair of electrical works.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Committing violations against regulations on safety assurance for people and property during renovation, upgrading, repair and construction of electrical works;

  b) Violating procedures and regulations on electricity production, transmission and distribution, causing safety hazards to people or damage to property.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Arbitrarily installing power lines and substations serving the purpose of power distribution beyond the scope permitted by a competent authority;

  b) Committing violations against laws on investment in electricity development;

  c) Obstructing organizations and individuals from performing repair and construction tasks of electrical works.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for carrying out electricity production, transmission and distribution without obtaining a competent authority’s inspection and certification of eligibility for such activities.

  5. Aside from the main forms of sanction against violations specified in this Article, violating individuals and organizations in the field of electricity may also be compelled to restore the original state which has been altered due to their violations prescribed in Point b Clause 1 and Point a Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on electricity supply

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Inspecting organizations or individuals using electricity but not wearing professional cards or having inspection decisions of the grid management agency or unit;

  b/ Carrying out electricity connection or disconnection not in compliance with the notified contents.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to make records on acceptance of handover to the electricity user under regulation after installing, replacing, repairing or adjusting the electricity metering system;

  b/ Failing to send persons to check the meter within five days after the electricity seller receives the request of the electricity buyer for checking the meter;

  c/ Failing to notify and post up at public places of electricity trading the provisions of law on rights and obligations of electricity buyers and sellers, conditions for signing electricity sale and purchase contracts, electricity prices and order and procedures for stopping power supply;

  d/ Failing to sign electricity sale and purchase contracts within ten days after the domestic electricity buyer has ensured all conditions as prescribed and agreed upon the articles and clauses in the draft contract.

  3. A fine of between 500,000 VND and 1,000,000 VND shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to have work tickets or duty assignment tickets and professional cards but arbitrarily carrying out one of the following jobs: repairing, connecting equipment, lines, power stations of the electricity sector or of organizations and individuals using electricity; removing, installing, adjusting meters on the power grid;

  b) Failing to handle the incident after two hours from the receipt of the notice of the electricity purchaser about the grid incident managed by the electricity seller;

  c) Obstructing the official to conduct inspection of the electricity sale.

  4. A fine of between 1,000,000 VND and 2,000,000 VND shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily using the electricity works of the purchaser to supply electricity to other organizations and individuals using electricity;

  b) Cutting off the electricity supply of organizations and individuals not subject to the regulation on restriction of use in case of lack of electricity, except for cases where there is a risk of causing serious and unsafe incidents for people and equipment.

  5. A fine of between 2,000,000 VND and 5,000,000 VND shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to sign the electricity trading contract within ten days after the electricity purchaser has ensured all conditions prescribed by law and agreed on the terms of the draft contract;

  b) Cutting off power without notice in accordance with regulations on procedures for stopping power supply.

  6. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Delaying repair of the grid after an incident occurs without a legitimate reason;

  b) Using electricity metering equipment that has not been inspected and sealed by a competent authority as prescribed, or intentionally using electricity metering systems with an error exceeding the prescribed limit.

  7. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of abusing their profession to harass organizations and individuals using electricity for self-seeking purposes.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on electricity use

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for act of obstructing persons competent to inspect the use of electricity.

  2. A fine of between 300,000 VND and 700,000 VND shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Arbitrarily closing, cutting, repairing or replacing electrical equipment and works of the electricity seller;

  b) Arbitrarily moving or falsifying the electricity metering system (including sealing and wiring diagrams), electrical equipment and works of the electricity seller;

  c) Unintentionally causing faults in the electricity system of the electricity seller;

  d) Using equipment that causes interference to damage or falsify data transmission, measurement and protection equipment of the electricity system.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for stealing electricity in any form for daily life or consumption:

  a) A fine of between 200,000 VND and 400,000 VND shall be imposed for stealing electricity with quantity of under 100 kWh;

  b) A fine of between 300,000 VND and 700,000 VND shall be imposed for stealing electricity with quantity of from 100 kWh to less than 200 kWh.

  c) A fine of between 1,000,000 dong and 2,000,000 dong shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with volume of between 200 kWh and under 300 kWh;

  d) A fine of between 2,000,000 dong and 3,000,000 dong shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with volume of between 300 kWh and under 400 kWh;

  dd) A fine of between 3,000,000 dong and 5,000,000 dong shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with volume of between 400 kWh and under 500 kWh.

  4. A fine of between 3,000,000 dong and 5,000,000 dong shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily closing power supply when the electricity project of the electricity purchaser has not been tested and handed over or when being suspended from power supply due to violations of regulations on use of electricity;

  b) Using other power sources without permission of the electricity seller in case of using electricity for production, business and service provision;

  c) Arbitrarily selling electricity to other organizations and individuals without consent of the electricity seller;

  d) Using electricity for fishing, trapping rats, preventing theft, protecting assets, crops or other acts violating regulations on safety in use of electricity.

  5. To impose a fine on acts of electricity theft in any form for purposes other than those specified in Clause 3 of this Article:

  a) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with quantity of under 100 kWh;

  b) A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with quantity of from 100 kWh to less than 200 kWh;

  c) A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with quantity of from 200 kWh to less than 300 kWh;

  d) A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with quantity of from 300 kWh to less than 400 kWh;

  dd) A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with quantity of from 400 kWh to less than 500 kWh.

  6. Depending on nature and seriousness of violations, violating individuals or organizations are also liable to have material evidences and means used for committing violations confiscated for violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 5 of this Article.

  7. In addition to main forms of sanction and additional sanction against violations specified in this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be forced to restore the initial state which has been changed due to their violations specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grid

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 50,000 to VND 150,000 shall be imposed for climbing on power poles or entering the substation without permission.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 300,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Flying kites, balloons, remote-controlled toy planes or other flying objects within the safety perimeter of the electrical work or the safety corridor of the power grid; tying livestock, vehicles or tools to power poles or walls of the substation;

  b) Installing antennas, antennas, advertising boards or other objects within the safety perimeter of the electrical work, the safety corridor of the power grid or at positions where they may cause unsafe conditions for the power lines or other parts of the electrical work upon falling or dropping, threatening human life;

  c) Felling trees, planting trees or pruning branches inconsistently with applicable regulations within the safety perimeter of the electrical work or the safety corridor of the power grid;

  d) Felling trees or pruning branches outside the safety perimeter of the electrical work or outside the safety corridor of the power grid without taking any safety measures to prevent them from hitting the power lines or other parts of the electrical work.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 700,000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on foundation, walls and fences of the substation, power poles or other structural elements and parts of the electrical work.

  4. A fine of between 1,000,000 dong and 2,000,000 dong shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to place warning signs, signal lights or danger indication at the location of the electrical works as prescribed or failing to place landmarks or signs along the underground cable;

  b) Placing or storing flammable and/or explosive substances outside the protection perimeter of the electrical works or outside the safety corridor of the power grid in violation of regulations on fire and explosion safety, thus affecting the safety of the electrical works;

  c) Setting up booths, shops or other structures which affect the water drainage, block the air vents, obstruct the access to the substations.

  5. A fine of between 2,000,000 dong and 4,000,000 dong shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Placing or storing flammable and/or explosive substances within the protection perimeter of the electrical works or within the safety corridor of the power grid;

  b) Firing forests, burning fields or other substances in violation of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety corridors of the power grid;

  c) Taking advantage of the electrical works’ components and structures for other purposes without permission from the competent electricity work management unit;

  d) Using equipment and devices that violate the safety distance as prescribed by laws on high-voltage grid safety protection;

  dd) Removing, erasing or covering the warning signs, safety signals at the poles, within the safety perimeter of the electrical works or the safety perimeter of the high-voltage grid, power station, power plant;

  e) Damaging the poles, wires, insulation, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, power cables and other parts and structures of the electrical works with voltage of less than 1 kV.

  6. A fine of between 3,000,000 dong and 6,000,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of damaging the poles, wires, insulation, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, power cables and other parts and structures of the electrical works with voltage of between 1 kV and 110 kV.

  7. A fine of between 4,000,000 dong and 8,000,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of damaging the poles, wires, insulation, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, power cables and other parts and structures of the electrical works with voltage of more than 110 kV.

  8. A fine of between 5,000,000 dong and 10,000,000 dong shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Building new houses, upgrading, renovating houses, setting up booths, shops or other works in violation of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety perimeter of high-voltage grid;

  b) Failing to ensure safety conditions for houses, lawful construction works left in the safety perimeter of high-voltage grid as prescribed;

  c) Constructing works in underground or dredging river and lake beds within safety protection corridors of underground power transmission lines without agreement with power work authority;

  d) Discharging waste and wastewater containing corrosive substances within safety protection corridors of underground power transmission lines;

  dd) Mining in violation of regulations on safety protection of power works and safety protection corridors of power grid;

  e) Failing to maintain safe distance as prescribed by law when designing, building and repairing power works;

  g) Using electricity as direct protective means against unauthorized use as permitted by competent authorities.

  9. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for digging pits, driving piles, anchoring ships or other acts violating safety protection corridors of underground power transmission lines where warning signs have been installed.

  10. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for constructing wharves, buildings and other works within the protection corridor of power plants or safety protection corridors of underground power transmission lines where warning signs have been installed.

  11. Depending on nature and severity of violations, violating individuals and organizations may also have exhibits and means used for violations confiscated in respect of violations specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Point b Clause 4, Points a, d and e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7, Point c Clause 8, Clause 9 and Clause 10 of this Article.

  12. Apart from the main and additional penalties for violations specified in this Article, violating individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of illegally constructed works in case of administrative violations in the field of electricity specified in Point b Clause 2, Point c Clause 4, Point c Clause 5, Point a and Point e Clause 8 and Clause 10 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the initial state altered due to violations specified in Point c Clause 2, Clause 3, Point c Clause 4, Points c, dd and e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7 and Clause 10 of this Article.

  Chapter 3:

    AUTHORITY AND PROCEDURES FOR PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ELECTRICITY

  Article 11. Authority to impose penalties of Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

  Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels have authority to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of electricity occurring within their local areas under their management, specifically as follows:

  1. Chairpersons of People’s Committees of communes have the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and equipment used for committing administrative violations in the electricity sector and assessed at up to VND 500,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the electricity sector.

  2. Chairpersons of People’s Committees of districts have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 20,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and equipment used for committing administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity;

  dd) Enforced dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity.

  3. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose fines of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Suspend the license for electricity operation issued by the People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government;

  d) Confiscate exhibits and equipment used for committing administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  dd) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity;

  e) Enforced dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity.

  Article 12. Power inspection authority

  Electricity inspectors of the Ministry of Industry have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity across the country. Electricity inspectors of provincial Departments of Industry have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity occurring within their provinces and cities. The power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity of electricity inspectors is specified as follows:

  1. The electricity inspector on duty has the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 200,000;

  c) Confiscating exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the electricity sector with a value of up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity.

  2. Chief Inspector of Electricity Department of the Ministry of Industry has the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 20,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and equipment used for committing administrative violations against regulations on electricity;

  d) Enforce the restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation against regulations on electricity;

  dd) Enforce the dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity.

  3. Chief Inspector of Electricity Department of the Ministry of Industry has the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) A fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Suspension of the license for electricity operation issued by the Ministry of Industry and People’s Committees of provinces or centrally-affiliated cities;

  d) Confiscation of exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  dd) Forcible restoration of the initial state altered due to administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  e) Forcible dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works violating regulations of law on electricity operation.

  Article 13. Authority to sanction of other forces

  Apart from the persons defined in Articles 11 and 12 of this Decree, the following competent persons, when detecting acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity prescribed in this Decree, shall have authority to sanction, specifically as follows:

  1. The people’s police officers who are on duty shall have authority to sanction administrative violations in the field of electricity prescribed in:

  a) Point a Clause 1 Article 7;

  b) Point c Clause 3 Article 8;

  c) Clauses 1, 3 and 5 Article 9;

  d) Clauses 1, 2, 3, Points b and c Clause 4, Points a, b, c, dd and e Clause 5, Clauses 6, 7, Points dd and g Clause 8, Clauses 9 and 10 Article 10.

  2. Construction inspectors have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity as prescribed in:

  a) Point a Clause 2 Article 7;

  b) Clauses 3, Points a and c Clause 8 and Clause 10 Article 10.

  3. Environmental inspectors have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity as prescribed in Point d Clause 8 Article 10.

  Article 14. Authorization and principles for determining the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity

  1. The authorization of the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity shall comply with the provisions of Article 41 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The principles for determining the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity shall comply with the provisions of Article 42 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 15. Procedures for imposing penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity

  1. Procedures and order for imposing penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity shall comply with the provisions of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Documents related to the imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity must be fully kept at the imposing agency. The record of administrative violation shall be made according to the form prescribed in this Decree, except for cases where simple procedures are applied as prescribed in Article 54 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. Individuals or organizations that are fined must pay fines within the time limit and at the place specified in the penalty imposition decision, except for cases where fines have been paid on the spot as prescribed in Articles 54 and 58 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and they receive receipts for fine payment. The receipt for fine payment must use receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance under current regulations.

  The Ministry of Finance shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Industry in guiding the collection and use of fines for administrative violations against regulations on electricity.

  4. Procedures for stripping the right to use electricity operation licenses must comply with Article 59 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  When applying the form of confiscating material evidences and means used in violation, the persons with sanctioning competence must strictly comply with the provisions of Articles 60 and 61 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 16.- Execution of sanctioning decisions and coercive execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity under this Decree shall have to strictly execute the sanctioning decisions within ten days after being handed over the sanctioning decisions, except otherwise provided for by law. If sanctioned individuals or organizations fail to voluntarily execute the sanctioning decisions, they shall be coerced to execute them according to Article 66 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. When applying measures to coerce the execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity, competent agencies and persons must abide by the coercion order and procedures prescribed in Articles 66 and 67 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                          SETTLEMENT OF COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 17.- Complaints, denunciations and settlement of complaints and denunciations against decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity or their lawful representatives have the right to lodge complaints or lawsuits against the sanctioning of such individuals and organizations according to law provisions.

  2. Every citizen has the right to lodge a denunciation with competent state agencies about administrative violations in the field of electricity committed by individuals and organizations and denunciations about violations committed by persons competent to sanction administrative violations in the field of electricity.

  3. The competence, procedures, order and time limits for settling complaints, denunciations and administrative lawsuits shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Complaints and Denunciations and the Ordinance on Procedures for Settlement of Administrative Cases (amended) of January 5, 1999.

  Article 18. Handling of violations committed by persons having power to impose penalties and persons subject to administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity

  1. Persons who have power to impose administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity but commit acts of harassment, tolerance or covering up of violations, failure to handle or improper handling, handling beyond their competence, illegal appropriation or use of money, exhibits and means used in violation shall, depending on nature and severity of their violations, be disciplined or examined for penal liability; if causing material damage, they must pay compensation therefor according to law provisions.

  2. If a person who is sanctioned for an administrative violation in the field of electricity commits acts of resisting law enforcers, delaying or evading the execution of sanctioning decisions or other violations, he/she shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of his/her violation, be administratively sanctioned under the Government’s regulations on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of security and order or examined for penal liability; if causing material damage, he/she must pay compensation therefor according to law provisions.

  Chapter 5.

                                                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 19. Effect

  This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO. All previous regulations on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity which are contrary to this Decree are now annulled.

  Article 20. Implementation

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.
