                                                   DECREE

       OF THE GOVERNMENT NO. 49/2005/ND-CP OF APRIL 11, 2005, ON SANCTIONING OF ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF EDUCATION

                                                  THE GOVERNMENT

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
  Pursuant to the December 2, 1998 Law on Education;
  Pursuant to the July 2, 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;
  At the proposal of the Minister of Education and Training,

                                                  DECREE:

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations in the field of education (profession training not within the scope of regulation of this Decree), forms of sanction, levels of sanction, competence to sanction, procedures for sanction and remedies.

  2. Administrative violations in the field of education are those acts of violating regulations on state management in the field of education committed by individuals or organizations intentionally or unintentionally but not being crimes and according to the provisions of Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and this Decree must be sanctioned for administrative violations.

  3. Those administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in other Government’s decrees (hereinafter referred to as relevant decrees) shall apply the provisions of such decrees.

  Article 2. Subjects of application

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education shall all be sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree and relevant decrees.

  2. Foreign individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education in Vietnam shall all be sanctioned like Vietnamese individuals and organizations according to the provisions of this Decree and relevant decrees, except otherwise provided for by international agreements which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam has signed or acceded to.

  3. Individuals who are minors committing administrative violations in the field of education shall be handled according to the provisions of Clause 1 and Clause 3, Article 7 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 3. Principles of sanction

  1. All administrative violations in the field of education must be detected in time and stopped immediately.

  The handling of administrative violations in the field of education must be carried out quickly, fairly and thoroughly; all consequences caused by administrative violations must be remedied in accordance with law.

  2. Individuals and organizations shall be administratively sanctioned for violations in the field of education only when they commit violations prescribed in this Decree and other relevant Government’s decrees on administrative sanctions.

  3. An administrative violation in the field of education shall be administratively sanctioned only once.

  A person or an organization committing multiple administrative violations in the field of education shall be sanctioned for each violation.

  If many persons or organizations commit the same administrative violation in the field of education, each violating person or organization shall be sanctioned.

  4. The sanction against administrative violations in the field of education must be based on the nature and seriousness of the violation, personal record of the violator and extenuating and aggravating circumstances to decide appropriate handling forms and measures.

  5. No administrative sanctions shall be imposed on violations in the field of education in cases of emergency, legitimate self-defense, unexpected events or administrative violations committed by individuals suffering from mental diseases or other diseases that have caused them to lose the ability to perceive or control their acts.

  Article 4. Extenuating and aggravating circumstances

  Extenuating and aggravating circumstances applied to sanctioning administrative violations prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree shall comply with the provisions of Articles 8 and 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 5. Statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations

  1. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education is one year from the date the administrative violation is committed; for administrative violations in the field of education related to finance, intellectual property, construction, environment, housing, land, publication, export, import, exit, entry or administrative violations being acts of smuggling, production, trading of fake goods, the statute of limitations is two years; if past the above time limit, no sanction shall be imposed but the consequence-overcoming measures prescribed in Clause 3, Article 7 of this Decree shall still apply.

  2. For individuals who are sued, prosecuted or have decisions to bring the cases to trial according to criminal procedures but later there are decisions to suspend the investigation or suspend the cases, if their violations show signs of administrative violations in the field of education, they shall be administratively sanctioned; within three days after issuing the decision to suspend the investigation or suspend the case, the person who has issued the decision must send the decision to the person with sanctioning competence; in this case, the statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations is three months from the date the competent person receives the suspension decision and the dossier of the violation.

  3. Within the time limits prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, if individuals or organizations commit new administrative violations in the field of education or deliberately evade or obstruct the sanctioning, the statute of limitations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations shall be recalculated from the time of committing new administrative violations or from the time of terminating acts of deliberately evading or obstructing the sanctioning.

  Article 6. Time limit for being considered as having not yet been sanctioned for administrative violations

  Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education, if after one year from the date of completely serving the sanctioning decisions or from the date of expiry of the effect of the sanctioning decisions, do not relapse into violations, they shall be considered as having not yet been sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education.

  Article 7. Sanctioning forms and remedies

  1. For each act of administrative violation in the field of education, violating individuals and organizations must bear one of the following principal sanctioning forms:

  a/ Warnings;

  b/ Fines.

  When applying the fine form, the specific fine level for an act of administrative violation is the average level of the fine bracket prescribed for such act. If the violation involves extenuating circumstances as prescribed in Article 8 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, the fine level may be reduced to below the average level but not lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation involves aggravating circumstances as prescribed in Article 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, the fine level may be increased above the average level but not exceeding the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  2. Depending on the nature and severity of violations, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education may be subject to one or more of the following additional penalties:

  a) Depriving the right to use decisions on establishment, licenses, or professional practice certificates for a definite or indefinite period, applicable to individuals or organizations committing serious violations regarding the use of decisions, licenses, or professional practice certificates;

  b) Confiscating exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations;

  c) Expelling violators who are foreigners.

  3. In addition to the penalties prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory restoration of the initial state altered due to administrative violations at the request of competent authorities;

  b) Compulsory suspension or termination of teaching or educational activities;

  c) Compulsory cancellation of examination results or re-marking of examination papers;

  d) Enforced provision of adequate number of lessons, contents and programs as prescribed; provision of materials and equipment according to approved plans;

  dd) Enforced cancellation of decisions against regulations of competent authorities;

  e) Enforced restoration of learners’ right to study, legitimate benefits and correct assessment results, ensuring rights of certificate holders;

  g) Enforced return of collected amounts to learners and payment of all return fees incurred due to administrative violations;

  h) Enforced revocation of illegally issued certificates;

  i) Enforced removal from Vietnam or re-export of illegally imported textbooks, documents and educational equipment due to administrative violations.

  Chapter 2:

    ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF EDUCATION, PENALTIES AND FINES

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on establishment and operation of educational institutions

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to report the loss of decision on establishment, license or practicing certificate to competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for erasing, altering, modifying, buying, selling, transferring, leasing, borrowing or lending the decision on establishment, license or practicing certificate in contravention of regulations of law.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal establishment of educational institutions:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for illegally establishing an early childhood education institution or general education institution;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for illegally establishing a vocational secondary education institution, continuing education center, foreign language center or computer center;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for illegally establishing a college;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 60.000.000 to VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed for illegally establishing a university or postgraduate institution.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for opening independent classes to teach preschool education programs, general education programs, professional secondary education programs, undergraduate preparatory courses, university entrance examination preparation courses, college and university training courses, and postgraduate training courses without obtaining permission from competent authorities or giving permission beyond their competence:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if such violation involves preschool education programs;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if such violation involves general education programs;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed if such violation involves professional secondary education programs, undergraduate preparatory courses, or university entrance examination preparation courses;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if such violation involves university training courses or postgraduate training courses.

  6. Additional penalties and remedial measures applicable to the violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscation of exhibits, for the violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory return of collected amounts to learners and payment of all return fees incurred by the entity committing the violation prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on operation of educational institutions

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for failing to comply with a competent authority’s decision on suspension or dissolution of the training course; operating beyond the time limit specified in the license; or suspending operation without permission from a competent authority, causing damage to learners:

  a) VND 3,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 for early childhood education and general education institutions;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 for vocational training institutions, continuing education centers, foreign language centers and computer centers;

  c) VND 15,000,000 - VND 30,000,000 for college training institutions;

  d) VND 40,000,000 - VND 60,000,000 for higher education institutions and postgraduate training institutions.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for providing training and issuing certificates against regulations of law.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 for one of the following acts of violation:

  a) Training and issuing degrees of national education system beyond the assigned authority;

  b) Training in fields and specializations beyond the assigned authority.

  4. Remedies for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Forced to return to learners the collected amounts and bear all costs of refunding for the violations prescribed in clause 1 and revoke degrees and certificates issued illegally for the violations prescribed in clauses 2 and 3 of this Article;

  b) Forced to restore the lost right to learn of learners due to the violations prescribed in clause 1 of this Article under the decision of competent authorities.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on programs, contents and plans for education

  1. A warning or fine shall be imposed for the acts of teaching insufficient number of lessons or knowledge content (calculated by number of lessons) of subjects as prescribed by competent authorities for general education, vocational secondary education, college, university and post-graduate education with the following fines:

  a) Warning shall be imposed for violations of less than 5 lessons per class in a school year;

  b) A fine of from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for committing 5 - 10 violations per class per year;

  c) A fine of from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for committing 11 - 15 violations per class per year;

  d) A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for committing 16 - 20 violations per class per year;

  dd) A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for committing 21 violations or more per class per year.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of teaching or disseminating contents not prescribed in the prescribed curriculum or textbook in order to distort educational contents (but not liable to criminal prosecution):

  a) A fine of from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for the first violation;

  b) A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the second violation or later;

  3. Additional penalties for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscate exhibits of violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Deportation if violating individuals are foreigners for acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Suspension of practicing certificate or license if first-time violations or re-commitment of violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Mandatory suspension of teaching and educational activities of violating individuals for acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory addition of teaching sections and contents for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on eligible entities, eligibility criteria, eligibility indicators and enrollment authority

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 300.000 shall be imposed for providing false information or assisting in providing false information in application for admission to ensure successful enrollment.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for admitting students to general education level or grade at inappropriate levels or with unqualified conditions as prescribed by competent authorities:

  a) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for the violation of admitting less than 3 students;

  b) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the violations of wrong recruitment from 3 to 5 students;

  c) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation of wrong recruitment from 6 to 10 students;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 11 - 15 students;

  dd) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation of wrong recruitment from 16 students or more.

  3. A warning or fine shall be imposed for enrolling students in vocational secondary education beyond the prescribed number of students, enrolling ineligible students or failing to meet the prescribed standards as prescribed by competent authorities.

  a) A warning shall be imposed if the enrollment rate exceeds the target enrollment rate by less than 5%;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by 5% - 10% or less or fail to enroll 10 learners who do not meet eligibility requirements;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by 10% - 15% or less or fail to enroll 10 learners who do not meet eligibility requirements;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by 15% - 20% or less or fail to enroll 21 - 30 learners who do not meet eligibility requirements;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 6.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by more than 20% or enrolling 31 learners or more who do not meet eligibility requirements.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for enrolling learners for college, university and postgraduate training programs in excess of the target enrollment volume, learners who do not meet eligibility requirements as prescribed by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if learners exceed the target enrollment volume by less than 5%;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if learners exceed the target enrollment volume by 5% - 10% or less or fail to enroll 10 learners who do not meet eligibility requirements;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if learners exceed the target enrollment volume by 10% - 15% or less or fail to enroll 10 learners who do not meet eligibility requirements;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners whose enrollment exceeds the target volume by 15% - 20%, or enrolling 21 - 30 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners whose enrollment exceeds the target volume by more than 20%, or enrolling 31 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements.

  5. The following fines shall be imposed for enrolling learners for training and issuance of diplomas in the national education system beyond the assigned authority:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for announcing enrolment (in any form) before obtaining permission from a competent authority;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for receiving applications, organizing entrance exams or consults, or summoning examinees before obtaining permission from a competent authority.

  6. The following fines shall be imposed for violations against regulations of the State on enrolment for overseas training or training at educational institutions with foreign elements operating within the territory of Vietnam according to general education, vocational education and training, college, university, postgraduate programs:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if 1 - 5 learners are enrolled improperly;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if 6 - 10 learners are enrolled improperly.

  c) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 11 - 15 learners improperly;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 16 - 20 learners improperly;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 21 - 25 learners improperly;

  e) A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 26 learners or more improperly.

  7. Additional penalties for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscation of exhibits shall be imposed for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Suspension of practicing licenses/certificates if the violation is committed for the first time and indefinite suspension if the violation is repeated for violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 6 of this Article.

  8. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Enforced cancellation of false decisions due to acts specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of collected amounts to learners and payment of all return fees for acts mentioned in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on entrance exams to educational institutions and graduation exams for issuance of diplomas of national education system

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for disturbing or threatening to use force to obstruct examiners, invigilators, marking officers and other service providers from performing their duties.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for using force to obstruct examiners, invigilators, marking officers and other service providers from performing their duties.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 4.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Taking an exam instead of another person or taking an exam with the purpose of assisting another person;

  b) Illegally transferring documents, information or equipment into the examination room for examinees;

  c) Disclosing secrets of test papers.

  4. A fine of from VND 6,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of swapping test papers or abetting other persons to swap test papers of examinees in contravention of regulations of competent authorities.

  5. A fine of from VND 1,500,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations against regulations on examination:

  a) Issuing decisions on establishment of apparatus and assignment of tasks to officials in contravention of regulations applicable to such examination;

  b) Losing or damaging test papers to the extent that such test papers cannot be graded;

  c) Grading entrance exams or graduation exams inconsistently with answers or score ranges;

  d) Making grade sheets unconformable with grading results of test papers.

  6. A fine of from VND 4,000,000 to VND 6,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of writing or modifying contents of test papers of examinees in contravention of regulations.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for disclosing or losing examination papers (but not liable to criminal prosecution).

  8. Additional penalties for violations specified in this Article:

  Confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit violations specified in Clauses 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original state altered by the violation specified in Clause 4, Point d Clause 5 and Clause 6 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory cancellation of examination results of the violator (if the violator is an examinee) for violations specified in Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3, Clause 4, Clause 6 and Clause 7 of this Article;

  c) Mandatory re-marking of examination papers for violations specified in Point c Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on assessment of learners’ performance in general education schools, vocational secondary education schools and higher education institutions

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent authorities on marking, assessment and grading of learners’ performance which affects learners’ performance in a subject, term or school year as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed if the violation is committed in the general education;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if the violation is committed in the professional secondary education;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 600.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation is committed in higher education.

  2. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article :

  The entity committing the violation in Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to restore learners’ actual performance in assessment and grading.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on management, issuance and use of diplomas/certificates of national education system

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to fulfill the responsibility to issue or certify copies of or to certify the legitimacy of issued diplomas/certificates;

  b) Issuing diplomas/certificates with false contents thus causing obstacles to their users.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for using illegally issued diplomas/certificates.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to prepare or inadequately preparing documents as the basis for issuance and management of diplomas/certificates;

  b) Providing false certification or falsifying documents leading to the issuance of diplomas/certificates to ineligible persons.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Issuing decisions or requesting competent authorities to issue certificates illegally;

  b) Destroy or falsify documents used as the basis for issuance or certification of legitimacy of certificates (but not liable to criminal prosecution).

  5. A fine ranging from VND 4.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Issuing decisions or requesting competent authorities to issue decisions on issuance of diplomas illegally;

  b) Destroying or falsifying documents used as the basis for issuance or certification of legitimacy of diplomas (but not liable to criminal prosecution).

  6. Additional penalties for violations specified in this Article:

  Suspend licenses/practicing certificates if the violation is committed twice or more, or suspend them indefinitely if the violation is repeated, applicable to the violations specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Revoke diplomas/certificates, applicable to the violations specified in Point b Clause 1, Clause 2, Point b Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 and Point a Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of lawful rights and benefits of certificate holders for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on using teachers

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for using unqualified teachers as prescribed:

  a) A warning shall be imposed for using unqualified preschool teachers;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for using unqualified general education teachers;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for using unqualified teachers of vocational secondary schools, continuing education centers, foreign language centers or computer centers;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for using unqualified college lecturers;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 11.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for using unqualified university lecturers or postgraduate lecturers.

  2. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Mandatory suspension of teaching and education activities for teachers who fail to meet the prescribed standards mentioned in the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 16. Violations against regulations on ensuring the proportion of full-time teachers in educational institutions

  The following fines shall be imposed for violations against regulations of competent authorities on ensuring the proportion of full-time teachers in total number of teachers in educational institutions:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the violation involving general education level or grade;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the violation involving vocational secondary education institutions, continuing education centers, foreign language centers, or computer centers;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for the violation involving college-level training institutions;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the violation involving university-level training institutions or postgraduate training institutions.

  Article 17. Violations against regulations on management of learners’ dossiers

  1. A warning or fine shall be imposed for irresponsibility in management leading to the alteration of school books, score sheets or other documents related to assessment of learners’ performance against regulations, causing the following penalties:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for violations from 1 to 2 learners;

  b) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for violations with from 3 to 5 learners;

  c/ VND 600,000 - VND 1,000,000 for a violation involving 6 or more cases.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to prepare or incompletely preparing the learner management dossier as prescribed in the school charter.

  3. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced restoration of the original condition which has been altered by the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on use of textbooks, educational materials and provision and use of educational equipment

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for issuing a decision against regulations on purchase, receipt of gifts, textbooks, educational materials and educational equipment that fail to meet quality requirements or cause adverse impacts on teaching and learning activities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for transporting and distributing textbooks, educational materials, or educational equipment against the plan for provision prescribed by a competent authority.

  3. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Enforced bringing out of Vietnamese territory or re-export of illegally imported textbooks, documents and educational equipment due to the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original plan altered due to the violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on tuition fees, charges and other amounts collected from learners

  To apply the provisions of Chapter II of the Government's Decree No. 106/2003/ND-CP of September 23, 2003, prescribing the sanctioning of administrative violations in the domain of fees and charges.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on forms of disciplinary actions for compulsory withdrawal from learning

  1. A warning or fine shall be imposed for making a decision to discipline learners to withdraw from learning against regulations, as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if 1 - 2 learners are involved in the violation;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if 3 - 5 learners are involved in the violation;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed if 6 - 10 learners are involved in the violation;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed if 11 learners or more are involved in the violation.

  2. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced cancellation of illegal decisions and restoration of learners’ right to learn for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations of regulations on education popularization

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 50,000 and 100,000 shall be imposed for obstruction of students’ attendance at schools at all levels and educational levels.

  2. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for inciting students to not attend school or drop out of school at all levels and educational levels.

  Chapter 3:

                                     AUTHORITY AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTIONING

  Article 22. Authority to sanction administrative violations in the field of education of the Chairmen of People’s Committees at all levels

  1. The chairmen of the People’s Committees of communes, wards or townships have authority to sanction as prescribed in Article 28 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their localities and under their management.

  2. The chairmen of the People’s Committees of rural districts, urban districts, towns or provincial cities have authority to sanction as prescribed in Article 29 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their localities and under their management.

  3. The President of the People's Committee of a province or centrally run city may sanction according to the provisions in Article 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within his/her jurisdiction.

  Article 23.- Competence of the Education Inspectorate to sanction administrative violations

  1. An on-duty education inspector may sanction according to the provisions in Clause 1, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  2. The Chief Inspector of the provincial/municipal Service of Education and Training may sanction according to the provisions in Clause 2, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  3. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Education and Training may sanction according to the provisions in Clause 3, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  Article 24.- Authorization for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education

  In case the person competent to sanction administrative violations in the field of education as defined in Article 22 and Clauses 2 and 3, Article 23 of this Decree is absent, the authorization shall comply with the provisions in Article 14 of the Government’s Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 25.- Principles for determining competence to sanction administrative violations in the field of education

  1. The chairmen of the People’s Committees at all levels shall have the right to handle administrative violations in the field of education in their localities according to competence prescribed in Articles 28, 29 and 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The education inspectors shall have the right to sanction administrative violations in the field of education according to the provisions of Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  In case where a violation falls beyond the competence of the Chief Inspector of the provincial-level Education and Training Department, the violation dossier shall be transferred to the chairperson of the provincial-level People’s Committee for handling according to his/her competence.

  3. In case an individual or organization commits simultaneously many acts of administrative violation, including violations in the field of education, the sanctioning competence shall be determined according to the principle prescribed in Clause 3, Article 42 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  4. The competence to expel individuals who are foreigners and commit administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with the provisions of Clause 1, Article 17 of Decree No. 21/2001/ND-CP of May 28, 2001 of the Government detailing the implementation of the Ordinance on Entry, Exit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam.

  5. In case where a violation against the law on education is considered as having signs of crime, the dossier must be immediately transferred to the competent agency for settlement according to the provisions of Article 62 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 26.- Procedures for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education

  1. The procedures for making records and decisions on sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Articles 19, 20 and 21 of Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 of the Government detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The fine and payment of fines must comply with the procedures prescribed in Articles 24 and 25 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. Procedures for stripping the right to use decisions on establishment, licenses and professional practice certificates shall comply with Article 59 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 11 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  4. The procedures for confiscating and handling material evidences and means used in administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with Articles 60 and 61 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 31 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 27. Execution of decisions on administrative penalties in the field of education

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education must abide by sanctioning decisions within 10 days after being given such decisions, except for cases already prescribed in Article 65 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. The time limit is calculated as prescribed in Article 9 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education but failing to voluntarily comply shall be forced to implement them. Individuals and organizations subject to enforcement must bear all expenses for the implementation of enforcement measures. The procedures for enforcement are prescribed in Articles 66 and 67 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                                 COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 28. Complaints and denunciations and settlement thereof

  1. Individuals, organizations or their lawful representatives may lodge complaints about sanctioning decisions of competent persons specified in Articles 22, 23 and 24 of this Decree. The procedures for lodging and settling complaints shall comply with Article 118 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. The competence and time limit for lodging and settling complaints shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Complaints and Denunciations. The lodging of complaints about decisions on sanctioning administrative violations does not suspend the execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education.

  2. Individuals have the right to denounce illegal acts in handling administrative violations in the field of education by competent sanctioning authorities to competent state agencies. The procedures for lodging complaints and denunciations and settling them shall comply with Article 118 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 29. Handling of violations committed by persons having competence to impose administrative penalties for violations in the field of education

  Persons who have competence to sanction administrative violations in the field of education but harass, tolerate, cover up, fail to handle or handle not in time, improperly, handle beyond prescribed competence shall be handled according to the provisions of Article 121 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 30. Handling of violations committed by persons subject to administrative penalties for violations against regulations on education

  Persons who are sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education if committing acts of opposing officials on duty, delaying or shirking the execution or committing other violations shall be handled according to the provisions of Article 122 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter 5.

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 31. Effect

  This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO.

  To annul the regulations on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Decree No. 18/2001/ND-CP of May 4, 2001, and other regulations previously in contravention of this Decree.

  Article 32. Responsibility for guidance and implementation

  The Minister of Education and Training shall, within the ambit of his/her functions, tasks and powers, guide and organize the implementation of this Decree.

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial/municipal People's Committees shall implement this Decree.
