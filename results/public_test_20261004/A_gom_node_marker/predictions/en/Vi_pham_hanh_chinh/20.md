                                                  DECREE

                 ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MARITIME

  Decree No. 142/2017/ND-CP dated December 1, 2017 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime, which takes effect from February 1, 2018, is amended and supplemented by:

  1. Decree No. 123/2021/ND-CP dated December 28, 2021 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decrees on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime; road traffic and railway transport, which comes into force from January 01, 2022.

  2. Decree No. 80/2026/ND-CP dated March 19, 2026 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 142/2017/ND-CP dated December 11, 2017 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime, amended by Decree No. 123/2021/ND-CP dated December 28, 2021 of the Government and Decree No. 139/2021/ND-CP dated December 31, 2021 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on inland waterway transport, which comes into force from May 15, 2026.

  Pursuant to the Law on Government organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on actions against administrative violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Vietnam Maritime Code dated November 25, 2015;

  At the request of the Minister of Transport;

  The Government promulgates Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime [1].

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1.[2] This Decree provides for administrative violations; completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations; penalties, fines, remedial measures applicable to each administrative violation; power to impose penalties, specific fines imposed by title holders, and power to issue administrative offense notices; implementation of penalties and remedial measures.

  2.[3] Administrative violations against regulations on maritime prescribed in this Decree include:

  a) Violations against regulations on construction, management and operation of maritime infrastructure;

  b) Violations against regulations on construction, management and operation of ICDs;

  c) Violations against regulations on ship operation;

  d) Violations against regulations on registration, deregistration of ships, crew member assignment; use of certificates of competency and crew member books;

  dd) Violations against regulations on maritime pilots;

  e) Violations against regulations on maritime transport business, multimodal transport, maritime transport support services and provision of maritime safety services;

  g) Violations against regulations on eligibility requirements for establishment of facilities building, modifying and repairing ships and vessels and ship-breaking activities;

  h/ Violations of the regulations on safety of the Comte-no;

  i) Violations against regulations on maritime search and rescue operations; salvage of sunken property and maritime signals;

  k) Violations against regulations on training and training of crew members;

  l) Violations of regulations on prevention of marine pollution;

  m) Violations against regulations on maritime labor; declaration of maritime occupational accidents;

  n) Violations against regulations on prevention and control of natural disasters in maritime sector;

  o) Violations against regulations on prevention and control of infectious diseases in the maritime sector.

  p)[4] Violations against regulations on state management, inspection, examination, and imposition of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on maritime.

  q)[5] Violations against regulations on posting information about prices and surcharges in the maritime sector.

  3.[6] Violations specified in Points c, d, dd, e, i, and l Clause 2 of this Article if they occur outside the port waters but are not prescribed in the Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime, islands, and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall also be sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree.

  4. Other administrative violations related to the maritime sector that are not prescribed in this Decree shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of Decrees on penalties for administrative violations in relevant sectors. The persons having power to impose penalties specified in Articles 60, 61, 62, and 63 of this Decree when detecting administrative violations specified in this Clause shall be sanctioned according to their competence.

  5. The handling of administrative violations against regulations on maritime shall comply with the provisions of this Decree and relevant regulations of law on handling of administrative violations.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations committing administrative violations against regulations on maritime; persons having power to make records and persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime.

  2. Foreign organizations and individuals committing administrative violations within the territory, [7] contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone or continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or on board of ships flying the Vietnamese flag shall be administratively sanctioned according to this Decree and relevant provisions of Vietnamese law on handling of administrative violations, unless otherwise provided for by international agreements to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  2a.[8] Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) Economic organizations established under regulations of law;

  b) Social organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-professional organizations, socio-professional-political organizations;

  c) Public service providers and other organizations as prescribed by law.

  2b.[9] Household businesses must register their business activities according to regulations of law and households committing administrative violations specified in this Decree shall be sanctioned like individuals.

  3. Depending on nature and severity of violations specified in Clause 2 Article 1 hereof, competent persons shall determine specific penalties imposed on organizations and individuals for administrative violations according to regulations of law.

  Article 3. Prescriptive period for imposition of administrative penalties [10]

  The prescriptive period for administrative penalties in the maritime sector is 01 year; however, the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations against regulations on construction of seaports, ICDs, maritime works, environmental protection, price management and overseas labor management shall be 02 years.

  Article 3a. Completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations [11]

  1. Completed administrative violations include the following:

  a) Violations against regulations on loading cargoes at Point a Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3 Article 16 hereof. The time of termination of the violation is the time when the violation of overloading the vehicle is detected;

  b) Violations detected by professional technical equipment and devices. The time of termination of the violation is the time when the violation is recorded by the professional technical equipment and devices;

  c) Administrative violations prescribed herein that do not fall under the cases specified in Points a and b of this Clause but have been completely committed before the competent person detects the violation. In case there are no documents or evidence to accurately determine the time of termination of the violation, these violations shall still be considered within the prescriptive period for administrative penalties.

  2. Ongoing administrative violations prescribed herein that do not fall under the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 4. Penalties and remedial measures

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the maritime sector shall be subject to one of the following main penalties for each violation:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fines.

  2. [12] Depending on the nature and severity of the violations, the violating individuals or organizations may also be subject to one or more of the following additional penalties:

  a) Suspension of practicing licenses/certificates;

  b) Suspension of operations;

  c) [13] Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations, including: certificates, written certifications, licenses, written permission documents, certificates, and papers not issued by competent authorities, and vehicles used for committing administrative violations.

  3. [14] Apart from the penalties prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the maritime sector must also implement remedial measures as prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree, including:

  a) Enforced renaming of ports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves, quays, floating terminals, port waters or sea areas in accordance with regulations on naming principles;

  Enforced assignment of adequate number of port security officers as per regulation;

  Enforced implementation of approved port security plans as per regulation;

  Enforced inspection of quality of port facilities within prescribed time limit or after accidents or collisions affecting maritime structures;

  b) Enforced arrangement and installation of life-saving equipment of ships as per regulation;

  Enforced establishment of adequate number of aids to navigation as per regulation or appropriate ones as per regulation;

  Enforced relocation of cages, rafts or fishing and aquaculture vessels from their prescribed positions within port waters;

  Enforced demolition of illegal works or parts thereof;

  c) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered;

  d) Enforced rectification of dredging monitoring system on vehicles involved in transport and discharge of dredged materials during construction to ensure readiness and continuity;

  dd) Enforced installation of dredging monitoring system on vehicles involved in transport and discharge of dredged materials during construction;

  Enforced implementation or proper implementation of maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities;

  e) Enforced unloading of goods stacked beyond the permissible payload of the vehicle if the vehicle has not left the stacking area;

  g) Enforced development of marine structure protection plan or enforced implementation of approved one;

  Enforced development of natural disaster response plan as prescribed;

  h) Enforced change or correction of the name of the wharf according to the name announced by a competent authority;

  Enforced provision of additional and corrected information;

  i) Enforced payment of maritime charges, fees and other relevant amounts as prescribed;

  Enforced reimbursement of costs of salvaging sunken property as prescribed;

  k) Enforced removal of obstacles on the wharf surface to the prescribed locations;

  Enforced relocation out of operational zones in conformity with the ship class;

  l) Enforced discharge of excess number of persons or cargoes from ships or vessels;

  Enforced departure of ships from their positions;

  Enforced restoration of the original state of signal positions and structures if the positions are moved or the signal structures are affected;

  m) Enforced implementation of safety measures for transport of oversized and overweight goods;

  Enforced completion of procedures for registration with Vietnamese nationality flag as per regulation;

  Enforced completion of procedures for deregistration of ships as prescribed;

  n) Enforced provision of adequate and accurate information on embarkation and disembarkation and assignment of crew member titles by electronic means to the Crew Member Management Database of the Vietnam Maritime and Waterway Administration as prescribed;

  o) Enforced arrangement of maritime pilots to guide ships in accordance with the certificate of maritime pilotage competency or certificate of maritime pilotage area;

  Compulsory provision of adequate and timely pilotage services in compulsory maritime pilotage areas or on assigned ship navigation routes;

  Forcing the use or arranging sufficient pilotage aids as prescribed or using pilotage aids to ensure safety conditions;

  Enforced arrangement of sufficient number of pilots or minimum number of aircraft as prescribed;

  p) Enforced removal from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam of imported marine flares;

  q) Enforced arrangement of rest periods for seafarers as per regulation;

  Enforced provision of essential goods, food and water for seafarers working on board;

  Enforced provision of funds for repatriation of seafarers;

  Enforced fulfillment of shipowners’ responsibilities for seafarers suffering occupational accidents or occupational diseases;

  Enforced purchase of compulsory insurance for seafarers working on board;

  Enforced arrangement for repatriation of seafarers within prescribed time limit;

  r) Enforced payment of costs of solicitation for assessment, inspection, measurement and analysis of environmental samples in case of violations leading to oil spills or environmental pollution exceeding applicable norms and unit prices;

  Enforced implementation of measures to remedy environmental pollution and reporting on results of remediation of consequences of violations;

  Enforced compensation for damage caused by oil pollution in accordance with regulations within the time limit prescribed in the decision on administrative penalties imposed by competent authorities;

  s) Enforced posting or proper posting of the list of organizations and individuals that have signed contracts for waste collection and treatment services from ships at seaports and the prices of waste collection and treatment services as prescribed;

  Enforced reporting on the management of waste collection and treatment from ships at seaports as prescribed;

  Enforced declaration or declaration of waste in accordance with regulations by maritime port authorities;

  Enforced implementation of measures to remedy environmental pollution caused by spilled waste, dispersion, and leakage during the process of collecting waste from ships;

  Enforced implementation of measures to remedy regulations on prevention of pollution caused by ships;

  Enforced implementation of measures to remedy environmental pollution and violations against regulations on management and control of dredge water and dredge sediment of ships;

  t) Enforced re-signing of crew member employment contracts in accordance with employment licenses;

  Enforced departure of crew members from ships;

  Enforced correction of information on the location and condition of operating ships when natural disasters occur;

  Enforced inspection and medical treatment;

  Enforced return to the ship or medical quarantine area;

  Enforced medical quarantine, enforced medical quarantine, and medical treatment for persons, ships, and goods carrying infectious agents.

  u) Enforced return of certificates, written confirmation, licenses, written permission, certificates, and documents that are erased, altered, or falsified by competent authorities or persons issuing such documents.

  4. For administrative violations, penalties shall be imposed based on the ship’s capacity, total capacity (GT), which is measured in accordance with the International Convention on Measurement of Ship Capacity 1969, and recorded in certificates issued by registry authorities.

  In case the certificate of the ship does not specify the CT, the total capacity of the ship shall be converted as follows:

  a) Self-propelled seagoing ships and inland waterway vehicles: 1.5 tons of gross tonnage shall be equal to 01 GT;

  b) Barges: 01 tonne of GV shall be equal to 01 GT;

  c) Tugboats, towing boats, passenger ships (including aircrafts) and offshore cranes: 01 horsepower (HP, CV) shall be equal to 0.5 GT; 01 kW shall be equal to 0.7 GT; 01 tonne of lifting capacity of the crane on board the ship shall be equal to 06 GT;

  d) Passenger ships without specifying engine capacity: 01 passenger seat shall be equal to 0.67 GT; 01 bed shall be equal to 04 GT;

  dd) In case the ship is a towboat, towing boat or sideboard boat: It shall be equal to the total capacity of the whole fleet including barges, towing or towing heads;

  e) The conversion prescribed in Points a, b, c, d and dd Clause 4 of this Article shall be carried out by selecting the conversion method with the largest total capacity;

  g) Unit of engine capacity: The main engine capacity of the ship shall be calculated according to HP, CV or KW; the fractional part below 01 HP or 01 CV or 01 kW shall be rounded up to 01 HP, 01 CV or 01 kW.

  5.[15] For administrative violations related to ships and vessels without information for calculating total ship capacity conversion specified in Clause 4 of this Article, the method for determining gross tonnage and ship capacity for imposing penalties is as follows:

  a) Gross tonnage of ships and vessels is symbolized by T (tonnes) and calculated according to formula T = A x K, where:

  - A is the value of the measurement of the main deck length, measured from the bow to the stern of the ship or vessel multiplied by the measurement of the middle deck width at the middle of the ship or vessel multiplied by the measurement of the hull height, measured from the bottom to the top of the middle of the ship or vessel, calculated according to formula A = L x B x D, where:

  L (m): Main deck length measured from the bow to the stern of the ship or vessel;

  B (m): Width of the middle of the ship or vessel;

  D (m): Height of the hull measured from the bottom to the top of the middle of the ship or vessel;

  - K is the coefficient corresponding to the value of A and applied as follows:

  The value of A from 4.55 m3 to 18.76 m3, the coefficient K = 0.26;

  If the volume of A is from over 18.76 m3 to 49.80 m3, coefficient K = 0.29;

  If the volume of A is from over 49.80 m3 to 387.20 m3, coefficient K = 0.35;

  If the volume of A is from over 387.20 m3 to 1.119.80 m3, coefficient K = 0.51;

  If the volume of A is over 1.119.80 m3, coefficient K = 0.57.

  b) If the ship is not a cargo or passenger ship, the form and level of sanction shall be applied based on the total capacity of the main engine installed on the ship. In case there is no basis for determining the capacity of the main engine installed on the ship, or the ship has no name tag, the sanction level shall be applied according to the capacity of the engine of 50 horsepower;

  c) In case it is impossible to determine the gross tonnage as prescribed in point a clause 5 of this Article, the competent person shall request the registry to determine the gross tonnage of the ship to serve as a basis for issuing a decision on administrative sanction;

  d) For ships such as floating terminals or other floating structures where dredging and suction equipment is installed for dredging channels, mineral exploration, and cargo handling, if there is insufficient basis to determine the gross tonnage or the capacity of the ship, it shall be determined as follows:

  The ship with the maximum length of up to 10 meters or the maximum width of up to 4 meters shall be determined as the ship with the gross tonnage of from 5 tons to 15 tons;

  A ship with a length greater than 10 m or a width greater than 4 m shall be considered as having a gross tonnage of more than 15 metric tons;

  The length of a ship is measured from the outer edge of its bow to the outer edge of its stern or from two outer edges of the largest cross-section on the deck.

  The width of a ship is measured from one side to the other at the largest size.

  Article 4a. Enforcement of penalties and remedial measures [16]

  1. The enforcement of penalties and remedial measures prescribed in this Decree shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations and guiding documents.

  2. Violators are responsible for cooperating with relevant organizations and individuals (if any) to implement remedial measures specified in decisions on administrative penalties in accordance with the law and bear all costs incurred from the implementation of such remedial measures.

  3. For remedial measures involving the mandatory return of certificates, confirmation letters, licenses, written permission, certificates, or papers that have been erased, altered, or falsified, violators are responsible for implementing the remedial measure of mandatory return of such certificates, confirmation letters, licenses, written permission, certificates, or papers in accordance with Article 85 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Persons competent to issue decisions on the implementation of remedial measures shall transfer erased, altered, or falsified certificates, confirmation letters, licenses, written permission, certificates, or papers to the issuing authorities or persons.

  4. Regarding the remedial measure of forcible payment of maritime charges and fees and other relevant amounts as prescribed:

  a) Persons having authority to impose administrative penalties and violating individuals or organizations shall contact the regulatory agencies managing maritime or inland waterways where the last ship departs in order to determine the maritime charges and fees and other relevant amounts payable as prescribed.

  b) Violating individuals or organizations shall notify the results of implementation of the remedial measures to persons having authority to impose administrative penalties.

  Article 5. Principles for determination of fines

  The fine level applicable to each administrative violation prescribed in this Decree is the fine level applicable to individuals; in case of the same administrative violation, the fine level applicable to an organization is twice as much as that applicable to an individual.

  Article 6.[17] (annulled)

  Chapter II

      ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
                                         QUERY

  Section 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON CONSTRUCTION, MANAGEMENT AND OPERATION OF MARITIME INFRASTRUCTURES

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on declaration of opening of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves and quays [18]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for providing false information in the application for declaration of opening of a seaport, offshore oil and gas port, wharf or quay.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for using a certificate, written confirmation, license, written permission, certificate or document that is erased, altered or falsified or not issued by a competent authority but not liable to criminal prosecution in the application for declaration of opening of a seaport, offshore oil and gas port, wharf or quay.

  3. Additional penalties: Confiscation of certificates, written confirmations, licenses, written permissions, certificates or documents that are not issued by competent authorities if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  4. Remedial measures: Enforced return of erased, altered or falsified certificates, written confirmations, licenses, written permissions, certificates or documents to issuing authorities or persons thereof if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on naming and renaming of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves, quays, floating terminals, zones and waters [19]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 30,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for naming or renaming a port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or sea area inconsistently with the principles of naming as prescribed, or failing to notify in writing the maritime authorities of the naming or renaming of the port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or sea area.

  2. Remedial measures: Enforced change or correction of the name of the port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or sea area to conform with the principles of naming as prescribed if the act of naming or renaming the port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or sea area inconsistently with the principles of naming as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article is committed.

  Article 9.[20] (annulled)

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on assurance of maritime safety, maritime security and order during operation of ports

  1. A warning shall be imposed for failure to comply with instructions of competent authorities or persons when entering or leaving the port land area or boarding a ship.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to obtain permission from competent authorities or persons when entering or leaving the port land area or boarding a ship.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Committing violations against regulations on warning signs for safe landing of ships as prescribed;

  b) Failing to assign personnel to tie or untie mooring lines as prescribed;

  c) Failing to notify the maritime port authority of the plan for the vessel’s arrival and departure from the port as prescribed;

  d) Placing objects on the wharf or occupying the space above the wharf, thereby obstructing the vessel's approach or departure from the wharf or hindering other activities at the port.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Employing employees who do not have practicing licenses or certificates as prescribed;

  b) Failing to promptly notify the maritime port authority of incidents or accidents related to maritime safety, maritime security and environmental pollution at the port as prescribed;

  c) Failing to provide the maritime port authority with periodic data on the depth of the waters in front of the wharf as prescribed;

  d) Failing to carry out periodic surveys to publish notices on the depth of the waters in front of the wharf and other zones as prescribed;

  dd) The lighting equipment at the wharf is not working or does not work in accordance with technical regulations as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Allowing ships to enter the port or anchor at the port waters without obtaining permission from the port authority;

  b) Deliberately unloading cargoes when the ship has not yet completed the procedures for entering the port as prescribed;

  c) The anti-collision cushion system and mooring fittings of the wharf are inadequate or unsafe for the ship’s anchorage;

  d) Failure to have or failure to carry out annual procedures for certification of the port certificate of compliance as prescribed or failure to fully implement the approved port security plan as prescribed;

  dd) Failure to provide or provision of maritime security information by the deadline to competent authorities; failure to organize drills or practice maritime security information connection as prescribed;

  e)[21] Failure to appoint port security officers as prescribed;

  g) Failure to arrange wharf or floating terminal for ships and vessels to enter or leave within the time limit according to the plan for ship mobilization of the maritime port office or failure to meet requirements as prescribed;

  h) Failure to install lighting equipment at the wharf as prescribed.

  i) [22] Failure to adequately store dossiers serving the management, operation and maintenance of maritime works;

  k) Failure to carry out inspection or operate loading and unloading equipment, vehicles, or specialized machinery within the port facility without undergoing inspection as per regulation.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 30,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Operating a port against its declared functions;

  b) Let ships and vessels dock at the wharf when the wharf has not been permitted to be put into operation or use;

  c) Failure to have approved port security plans as prescribed;

  d) Failing to carry out inspection of quality of port infrastructure within the prescribed time limit or after an incident involving collision or impact that affects the structure of maritime works;

  dd) Failing to comply with relevant technical regulations on inspection of quality of port infrastructure.

  7. [25] Additional penalties:

  a) Suspend the receipt of ships operating on international routes into the port for 1 to 3 months in case of commission of the violation specified in Points d and e Clause 5 and Point c Clause 6 of this Article;

  b) Suspend the receipt of ships into the port for 1 to 3 months in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 6 of this Article.

  8. [26] Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced assignment of sufficient port security officers as prescribed in case of commission of the violation specified in Point e Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced preparation of approved port security plans as prescribed in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 6 of this Article;

  c) Enforced inspection of quality of port infrastructure within the prescribed time limit or after an incident that causes damage to maritime structures in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 6 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on loading, unloading, storage and handling of goods

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for loading, unloading and storing goods in violation of regulations.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for loading cargoes on a wharf beyond the permissible limit.

  3. Remedial measures: Enforced removal of goods exceeding the permissible weight limit in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on safety assurance for maritime and environmental protection during construction of new ports or renovation and upgrading of existing ports, or during construction and installation of other works and equipment affecting maritime safety [27]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) There is no life-saving equipment or the life-saving equipment is not suitable as prescribed;

  b) Failing to notify the port authority of the construction of other works in the port waters;

  c) Failure to adequately install or properly install navigational aids as prescribed, or failure to operate or mislead navigational aids in areas where works are being constructed;

  d) Construction ships and ships serving construction work anchoring outside of permitted areas, causing obstruction to maritime traffic on navigational channels;

  dd) Failure to record supervision results of dredging and transport of dredged materials or failure to keep construction logbook as prescribed; failure to submit periodic reports on progress and results of dredging navigational channels, waters, and areas within port waters in combination with full utilization of products as prescribed to Vietnam Maritime Administration and Vietnam Inland Waterway Administration[28];

  e) [29] Conducting aquaculture in port waters without obtaining permission from a competent authority or at a location or at a time not permitted by the competent authority.

  g) Making anchor in port waters or navigational channels.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Executing works not in accordance with the licensed location;

  b) Executing construction beyond the time limit specified in the construction permit of the competent authority as prescribed;

  c) Using specialized equipment and devices for surveying, dredging channels, establishing navigational aids, and conducting other activities within the port waters without obtaining the approval of the maritime port authority as prescribed;

  d) Failing to remove or discharge obstacles arising during the construction process after the project is completed;

  dd) Failing to fully implement the maritime safety plan approved by the competent authority;

  e) Creating obstacles within the port waters and territorial waters of Vietnam that affect maritime operations;

  g) Installing a dredging monitoring system on the vehicle used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during dredging activities inconsistently with regulations; the dredging monitoring system fails to meet minimum technical specifications, is not in a ready state for operation, operates intermittently or stably as prescribed;

  h) Failing to organize supervision of construction and discharge of dredged materials as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to establish navigational aids as prescribed;

  b) Failure to install dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged material during dredging or maintenance of navigational channels, waters, and areas;

  c) Failure to implement or improper implementation of maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for organizing construction of works not falling under the cases specified in Point a Clause 5 of this Article without obtaining a license or written approval from a competent authority as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Organizing construction of works subject to preparation of economic-technical reports on construction of works or preparation of investment projects for construction of works without obtaining a license or written approval from a competent authority as prescribed;

  b) Constructing works against the appraised and approved design;

  c) Failure to prepare maritime safety plans as prescribed.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 50,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 shall be imposed for constructing works against the approved planning.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 80,000,000 to VND 100,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Executing construction works without permission of competent authorities and causing accidents;

  b) Executing construction works at locations other than the permitted ones and causing accidents;

  c) Failure to have or failure to comply with maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities leading to accidents.

  8. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory provision of life-saving equipment or appropriate life-saving equipment as prescribed if the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is committed;

  b)[34] Mandatory installation of adequate aids to navigation as prescribed or installation of appropriate aids to navigation as prescribed if the violation prescribed in Point c Clause 1 of this Article is committed;

  c) Enforced relocation of docks, floating rafts or fishing and aquaculture facilities to the wrong locations in the port waters for violations specified in Points e and g Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered for violations specified in Point e Clause 2 of this Article;

  dd) Enforced demolition of illegal works or parts thereof for violations specified in Point a Clause 2, Clause 4, Clause 5, Clause 6 and Points a and b Clause 7 of this Article;

  e) Enforced rectification of dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during construction to ensure readiness and continuity for violations specified in Point g Clause 2 of this Article;

  g) Enforced installation of dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during construction for violations specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;

  h) Enforced implementation of maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities for violations specified in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 7; implementation or proper implementation of maritime safety plans and traffic safety plans approved by competent authorities for violations specified in Point c Clause 3 and Point c Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on prevention and control of fire and explosion at seaports

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to have internal regulations, warning signs or instructions at fire and explosion-prone areas as prescribed;

  b) Using specialized firefighting equipment and devices for wrong purposes.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Using firefighting equipment and devices against regulations or not in operational readiness or expired as prescribed;

  b) Failing to place firefighting equipment and devices at the prescribed places or failing to arrange firefighting equipment and devices suitable for goods being transported or unloaded.

  b) Failing to comply with medical quarantine, enforcing medical quarantine, or providing medical treatment for persons, vessels, goods, and other subjects subject to quarantine carrying infectious agents as prescribed or upon request of competent authorities.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory medical examination and treatment in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory return to the ship or the quarantine area in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 5 of this Article;

  c) Mandatory enforced medical quarantine, enforced medical quarantine, and medical treatment for persons, vessels, goods carrying infectious agents as prescribed in Point b Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 58m. Violations against regulations on inspection, investigation, and imposition of administrative penalties [103]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for threatening, insulting, or hurting the honor of an official on duty but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for committing any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to report or declare or reporting and declaring not truthfully or on time as requested by law enforcement officers or competent authorities;

  b) Delaying, avoiding, failing to provide or providing insufficiently or improperly information and documents related to inspection, examination, and sanctioning of administrative violations of law enforcement officers or competent authorities;

  c) Causing difficulties, failing to cooperate or obstructing the work of inspection teams, inspection teams, or persons assigned to inspect, examine, and perform official duties in the maritime sector;

  d) Failing to appoint a competent representative as prescribed by law to participate in the announcement of inspection decision or failing to appoint a competent representative to work with the inspection team.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for arbitrarily removing seals on documents, exhibits, means, warehouses, equipment, or facilities involved in violations that are being sealed; temporarily seizing or dispersing documents and exhibits involved in violations; or arbitrarily altering the scene of an administrative violation in the maritime sector.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 80.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Delaying or avoiding the execution of inspection decisions in the maritime sector of competent authorities or law enforcement officers;

  b) Failing to properly or fully comply with the conclusions of inspection or examination in the maritime sector of competent authorities.

  Article 58n. Online inspection and handling of administrative violations [104]

  1. When information of documents including: original copies of ship registration certificates or certified true copies of ship registration certificates and valid confirmation letters of credit institutions (for mortgaged ships), certificates and other documents of ships as prescribed by law, professional certificates and certificates have been integrated and updated in electronic identification, electronic identification accounts on the National Identification Application, databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction, the inspection shall be carried out through information in electronic identification, electronic identification accounts on the National Identification Application, databases; the inspection of information of documents in electronic identification, electronic identification accounts on the National Identification Application, databases has the same validity as the inspection of originals of such documents.

  2. Temporary seizure or suspension of the right to use documents

  a) When temporarily seizing or suspending the right to use documents, competent persons shall update information on temporary seizure or suspension of the right to use such documents on the system for processing administrative violations and synchronize with the National Identification Application, databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction so that violators and ship owners (regarding documents of ship owners) know and comply with regulations of law on maritime order and safety, and serve competent authorities to inspect and handle violations;

  b) Where information on documents subject to temporary seizure or suspension of use rights is available on the National Identification Application or databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security or the Ministry of Construction, the competent person shall return such documents to the violating entity as prescribed; the administrative violation handling database system shall synchronize information with the National Identification Application and databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security or the Ministry of Construction for removal of information on temporary seizure or suspension of use rights;

  c) The person having authority to impose penalties shall access the electronic receipt of fine collected via the public service portal system to print and retain it in the administrative violation handling record and as a basis for returning documents subject to temporary seizure or suspension of use rights, removing information on temporary seizure or suspension of use rights from the National Identification Application and databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security as prescribed in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Records and decisions issued by competent persons regarding temporary seizure or suspension of use rights and return of documents shall comply with legal regulations and be made and sent electronically via the National Identification Application, other applications, or electronic information systems when technical conditions are met.

  3. Organizations and individuals having authority to inspect and handle administrative violations shall be responsible for ensuring confidentiality of personal information and data of violators in accordance with the law.

  4. Online handling of administrative violations prescribed in this Decree shall be carried out when infrastructure, technical and information conditions are satisfied in accordance with the law on handling of administrative violations.

  Chapter III

  Section 1. POWERS TO RECORD ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 59. Authority to make records of administrative violations [105]

  Persons having authority to impose penalties for administrative violations and officials, public employees, and persons of the People's Army and People's Public Security forces who are performing their duties under the management of persons having authority to impose penalties for administrative violations prescribed in Articles 60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 65, and 66 of this Decree and detect administrative violations against regulations on maritime within their jurisdiction shall promptly prevent such violations and make records of administrative violations as per regulation. For administrative violations committed on board a ship or inland waterway vehicle, the master or operator of the inland waterway vehicle shall be responsible for making records of the violation and immediately transferring them to the person having authority to impose penalties for administrative violations when the ship or inland waterway vehicle arrives at the port.

  Chapter IV

                                        IMPLEMENTATION CLAUSES [115]

  Article 67. Guidelines on collection, transfer and management of remittances

  The Ministry of Finance shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Transport in providing specific guidelines on collection, transfer and management of remittances from organizations and individuals involved in administrative violations prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 68. Transitional provisions

  For administrative violations against regulations on maritime committed before the effective date of this Decree but detected and handled after the effective date of this Decree, the provisions on penalties shall be applied in a manner that is beneficial to the violating organizations and individuals.

  Article 69. Entry into force

  This Decree comes into force as of February 01, 2018 and replaces the Government’s Decree No. 93/2013/ND-CP dated August 20, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime transport and inland waterway transport.

  Article 70. Implementation

  1. The Minister of Transport shall organize and provide guidelines for implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall implement this Decree./.
