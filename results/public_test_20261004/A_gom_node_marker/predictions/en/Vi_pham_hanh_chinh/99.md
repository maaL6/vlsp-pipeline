                                                  DECREE

     ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY; THRIFTY PRACTICE AND WASTE PREVENTION; NATIONAL RESERVES; STATE TREASURY

  The Government’s Decree No. 63/2019/ND-CP dated July 11, 2019 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and waste prevention; national reserves; state treasury shall take effect from September 1, 2019 and be amended by:

  Decree No. 102/2021/ND-CP dated November 16, 2021 of the Government on amendments to some Articles of Decrees on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on tax and invoices; customs; insurance business and lottery business; management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury; independent accounting and audit; comes into force from January 01, 2022.

  Pursuant to Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to Law on Actions against administrative violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to Law on Management and Use of Public Property dated June 21, 2017;

  Pursuant to Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to Law on Thrift Practice and Wastefulness Combat dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to Law on National Reserve dated November 20, 2012;

  At the request of Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury.[2]

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, remedial measures, power to record administrative violations and power to impose penalties for administrative violations in the following fields:

  a) Management and use of public property at agencies, organizations and units; projects funded by state budget; property under public ownership and infrastructure invested and managed by the State;

  b) Thrift practice and wastefulness combat;

  c) National reserve;

  d) State Treasury.

  2. Administrative violations related to management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury in terms of accounting, construction, investment planning, valuation, auction and other regulations not specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be sanctioned according to relevant laws.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrift practice against wastefulness; national reserve; state treasury.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies;

  b) Units of the People’s Armed Forces;

  c) Public service providers;

  d) Agencies of the Communist Party of Vietnam;

  dd) Socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, and other organizations established under the law on associations;

  e) Other relevant enterprises, organizations, and individuals.

  3. Persons having power to impose penalties and organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree.

  4. In case officials or public employees commit violations while performing their official duties, if such violations are within their assigned official duties prescribed in legislative documents or administrative documents issued by competent authorities or persons, they shall not be administratively sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with regulations of law on officials, public employees.

  State agencies committing violations within their assigned state management tasks shall not be administratively sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws.

  Article 3. Prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties [3]

  1. The prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury prescribed in this Decree is 01 year. For administrative violations involving public property being houses, land and infrastructure assets invested and managed by the State, the prescriptive period is 02 years.

  2. The time for calculation of the prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property is prescribed as follows:

  a) For completed administrative violations, the prescriptive period shall be calculated from the time the violation ends;

  b) For ongoing administrative violations, the prescriptive period shall be calculated from the time the violation is detected;

  c) Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property under Article 6 hereof shall be considered completed if investment and procurement of property have been finished and property has been handed over to agencies, organizations, or units for management and use; or administrative violations under consideration shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  d) Administrative violations against regulations on leasing property under Article 7 hereof shall be considered completed if property has been leased out and put into use; or administrative violations under consideration shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  dd) Administrative violations against regulations on encroaching on working offices or public service facilities under Article 11 hereof shall be considered as follows:

  - The act of an organization or individual that is using adjacent land and arbitrarily relocating the boundary of a land parcel to expand the land area or build an encroaching work into the space of the land area of the working office or public service facility of an agency, organization, or unit shall be considered an administrative violation that has been completed if the land area has been expanded or the encroaching work has been built out; or an administrative violation under consideration shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases.

  - The act of an organization or individual that arbitrarily uses a house or land belonging to the working office or public service facility of an agency, organization, or unit shall be considered an administrative violation under consideration.

  e) Administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property that are being committed shall be those prescribed in Articles 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, and 26 hereof.

  3. Determination of ongoing administrative violations and completed administrative violations for calculation of prescriptive periods of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness control shall be regulated as follows:

  a) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 31 hereof shall be considered completed if property procurement and handover to agencies, organizations, units for management and use have been implemented; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  b) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 32 hereof shall be considered completed if expenditures from fundings are made for purposes other than those of the fundings; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  c) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 hereof shall be considered completed if expenditures from fundings are made against standards, regulations, norms, unit prices as prescribed by law; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  d) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 33 hereof shall be considered completed if illegal obstruction of research, application of science, technology, implementation of projects using recycled resources has been terminated; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  dd) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 33 hereof shall be considered completed if exploitation and utilization of resources do not conform to planning, plans, procedures have been terminated; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  e) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 35 hereof shall be considered completed if wastefulness in management of working offices, public service facilities has been terminated; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  g) The violations against regulations on procurement of assets and supplies in a single-state-owned limited liability company under Clause 2 and Clause 3 Article 36 hereof shall be considered administrative violations that have been completely committed if the procurement is completed; or administrative violations that are being committed in the remaining cases;

  h) Administrative violations in the field of savings being committed are violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 31, Clauses 1 and 4 Article 36 of this Decree; violations regarding management and use of capital in single-member limited liability companies with state capital prescribed in Clause 2 Article 36 of this Decree; violations in management and use of assets and supplies prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 36 of this Decree.

  4. The determination of ongoing administrative violations and completed administrative violations for calculation of the statute of limitations for imposing penalties for administrative violations against regulations on national reserves shall be carried out as follows:

  a) Administrative violations currently being committed are those prescribed in Articles 41, 42, 47, Points b and c Clause 3 Article 48, and Article 49 of this Decree;

  b) Except for administrative violations prescribed in Point a of this Clause, other administrative violations shall be considered as completed ones.

  5. The time when administrative violations in the field of State treasury are considered completed is specified as follows:

  a) For administrative violations prescribed in Articles 54, 57, 58, 59 and 60 of this Decree, the end time is the time of submission of dossiers and vouchers at the State Treasury;

  b) Regarding administrative violations prescribed in Articles 55, 56 and 61 of this Decree: The end time shall be the time when the State Treasury receives the conclusion of the criminal proceeding agency on the violation case which shows no sign of a crime.

  Article 4. Penalties and remedial measures

  1. Primary penalties:

  a) The primary penalties prescribed in this Decree include warnings and fines;

  b) The maximum fine for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property, national reserves, and state treasury shall be VND 50,000,000 for an individual and VND 100,000,000 for an organization; the maximum fine for administrative violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat shall be VND 100,000,000 for an individual and VND 200,000,000 for an organization.

  2. Additional penalties:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating entity may also incur additional penalties such as confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit the violation.

  3. Remedial measures for violations against regulations on management and use of public property:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating entity may also be subject to one or more remedial measures, including:

  a) Enforced payment of an amount equivalent to the value of the property;

  b) Enforced payment of an amount incurred from committing administrative violations;

  c) Enforced return of the property. In case the property has been altered due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original condition. If the property cannot be restored to its original condition, it must be replaced with another property of equal utility and use value;

  d) Enforced demolition of the construction work on the encroached area of the working office or public service facility;

  dd) Enforced cancellation of false or fraudulent declarations in case of commission of the administrative violation in login and use of data on public property;

  e) Enforced adjustment and supplementation of data, information, and additional declarations on public property.

  4. Remedial measures in the field of thrift practice and wastefulness combat:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual may also be subject to one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Revoke and remit to state budget funds used for purposes other than those prescribed by competent authorities in case of using state budget funds for purposes or guidelines other than those prescribed by competent authorities;

  b) Enforced return of illicit revenues generated by using state budget funds for purposes other than those prescribed by competent authorities in case of using state budget funds for purposes or guidelines other than those prescribed by competent authorities.

  5. Remedial measures in national reserves:

  Depending on nature and severity of violations, violating organizations and individuals may also be subject to one or several remedial measures as follows:

  a) Enforced return of illicit revenues generated by committing violations against regulations on purchase, sale, preservation, distribution, and assistance; violations against regulations on import and export of national reserves; violations against regulations on capital management and fees in national reserve activities; violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, and extraction of assets included in national reserves;

  b) Enforced restoration of original conditions of facilities, physical-technical infrastructure, and storage of national reserves;

  c) Enforced refund of damaged national reserves caused by acts of infringement or destruction; violations against regulations on distribution and assistance of national reserves;

  d) Enforced recall of national reserves that have been issued or distributed inconsistently with applicable regulations;

  dd) Compulsory restoration of property to its original state in case of illegal trading, pledge, lease or exploitation of property being national reserve storehouses.

  6. Remedies in the field of State treasury:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of violations, violators are also subject to one or several remedies as follows:

  a) Compulsory revocation of expenses incurred from the preparation of documents and vouchers for payment for the unfinished work; unfinished work, payment not eligible and paid amount exceeding contract value or contract appendix;

  b) Compulsory revocation of expenses incurred from the preparation of documents and vouchers against regulations and expenses incurred from the preparation of documents and vouchers against the contents on the original documents and vouchers at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Compulsory revocation of all expenses incurred from the use of fake documents and vouchers for payment of the State budget but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Compulsory completion of documents and vouchers according to regulations in case of violations against regulations on payment of expenses of the State budget;

  dd) Compulsory completion of procedures for commitment before requesting the State Treasury to pay or remit the State budget or compulsory re-completion of procedures for commitment in case of violations against procedures for control of commitment;

  e) Forced to carry out procedures for payment of advances in case of violations against procedures and time limit for advance payment of state budget;

  g) Forcible freezing or closing of accounts in case of violations against regulations on registration and use of accounts at State Treasuries; forcible freezing of accounts in case of making false dossiers and papers to register the use of accounts at State Treasuries but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  7.[4] Organizations and individuals subject to administrative penalties under this Decree are not allowed to use state budget or state budget-derived funds for paying fines and remedying consequences caused by their violations, except for the case specified in Clause 8 Article 37 of the Government’s Decree No. 10/2021/ND-CP dated February 9, 2021 on management of construction investment costs.

  Article 5. Application of fines [5]

  1. The fines prescribed in Section 1, Section 2, Section 3 and Section 4 of Chapter II, Section 1 of Chapter III, Section 1 of Chapter IV, Section 1 and Section 2 of Chapter V of this Decree are the fine levels applicable to administrative violations committed by organizations; for the same administrative violation, the fine level imposed on an individual shall be half of that imposed on an organization (except for the provisions of Articles 17, 23 and 27 of this Decree).

  2. When imposing fines, the specific fine level for an administrative violation is the average level of the fine bracket prescribed for such act. If there are extenuating circumstances, each extenuating circumstance shall reduce the fine level by 10% of the average level of the fine bracket but the fine level imposed on such act must not be reduced below the minimum level of the fine bracket; if there are aggravating circumstances, each aggravating circumstance shall increase the fine level by 10% of the average level of the fine bracket but the fine level imposed on such act must not exceed the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  3. When determining the fine for an administrative violation involving both aggravating and extenuating circumstances, the aggravating circumstance shall be reduced according to the principle that one extenuating circumstance is reduced by one aggravating circumstance.

  Chapter II

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINE RATES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR DAMAGE CAUSED BY ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS AND FINE RATES IN MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY AT AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND EMPLOYEES

  Article 6. Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of investing or procuring property without a decision issued by a competent authority or person, as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the property invested or procured is machinery, equipment, or other property (excluding office buildings, public service facilities, and automobiles) with a total purchase value of less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the property invested or procured is machinery, equipment, or other property (excluding office buildings, public service facilities, and automobiles) with a total purchase value of VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing or purchasing assets that are working offices, public service facilities, or cars.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to make centralized procurement of assets on the list of assets subject to centralized procurement as prescribed by laws on management and use of public assets.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for investing or purchasing assets beyond the limits set by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds VND 50.000.000/01 asset unit;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000/01 asset unit;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds VND 100.000.000/01 asset unit or more.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory payment of an amount equivalent to the value of assets invested or purchased beyond the limits imposed for the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the value in excess of the standard or limit as the basis for sanctioning is prescribed as follows:

  a) In case of investment and procurement of assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment and other assets in excess of the standard or limit in terms of quantity: The excess value per unit of asset shall be determined by the unit price of such asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or contract for asset procurement;

  b) In case of investment and procurement of assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment and other assets in excess of the standard or limit in terms of quantity but exceeding the price level: The excess value shall be determined by the value of the asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or contract for asset procurement minus (-) the value of the asset according to the standard or limit prescribed by the competent authority;

  c) In case of investment and procurement of working offices or public service facilities in excess of the standard or limit: The excess value shall be determined by the difference between the actual area minus (-) the area used according to the standard or limit multiplied by (x) the average purchase unit price according to the invoice (or contract for asset procurement);

  d) Persons having sanctioning competence prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as the basis for sanctioning according to the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 7. Administrative violations against regulations on leasing of assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on leasing of assets in case the value of the asset lease contract is less than VND 100.000.000 for the following acts:

  a) Committing an act of leasing assets without a decision on asset lease issued by a competent agency or person;

  b) The act of leasing property in excess of the standards and limits prescribed by competent state authorities;

  c) The act of selecting service providers for property lease not in accordance with the forms and procedures prescribed by law on management and use of public property and relevant laws.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on property lease as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article if the value of the property lease contract is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory return of the amount of rent in excess of the standards and limits for the acts specified in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The value of the property lease contract serving as the basis for imposing penalties shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case the property lease contract specifies the contract value, the basis for imposing penalties shall be the value specified in the contract;

  b) In case the property is leased without entering into a contract or with an unsubstantiated contract, the value serving as the basis for imposing penalties shall be determined by multiplying the value of the leased property of the same type or having equivalent standards and technical requirements on the market at the time of commission of the violation by the duration of leasing property calculated from the start date of leasing to the date of issuance of the decision on administrative penalty.

  Persons having power to impose penalties prescribed in Article 29 and Article 30 hereof shall determine the value serving as the basis for imposing penalties according to regulations of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 8. Administrative violations against regulations on allocation and use of public property, destruction or intentional damage to public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of allocating public property beyond the standards and norms issued by competent authorities or persons (incorrect subjects, area, quantity, or price) with the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case where the value of allocated or used property is under VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case where the value of allocated or used property is from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case where the value of allocated or used property is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the act of allocating or using public property for wrongful purposes (using public property for wrongful purposes or functions of invested, equipped, or purchased property; using working offices or public service facilities as residential houses or for personal purposes; using cars to transport from residence to workplace for unqualified positions; using cars for business trips for unqualified positions not approved by competent authorities; allocating or using cars for personal purposes; using machinery, equipment, or other property for personal purposes) with the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case where the value of allocated or used property that is machinery, equipment, or other property (except for working offices, public service facilities, and cars) has an original cost recorded in accounting books of less than VND 100.000.000 per unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property valued at less than VND 100.000.000);

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of assignment or use of assets being machinery, equipment and other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles) with a book value of VND 100.000.000 or more per asset unit (hereinafter referred to as “assets with a value of VND 100.000.000 or more”);

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of assignment or use of assets being office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for destruction or intentional damage to public property which is not liable to criminal prosecution:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentional damage to assets being machinery, equipment and other assets with a book value of less than VND 100.000.000 per asset unit;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentional damage to assets being machinery, equipment and other assets with a book value of VND 100.000.000 or more per asset unit;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentional damage to assets being office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory payment of an amount corresponding to the value of assets exceeding the norms and limits for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state of property altered by administrative violations; in case of failure to restore the original state of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value shall be provided for the violation prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the value exceeding norms and limits as follows:

  a) In case of delivery and use of property being automobiles, machinery, equipment, or other property in excess of the prescribed quantity: The excess value is determined by multiplying the excess quantity by the original cost of the property recorded in the accounting book; in case the original cost of the property is not recorded in the accounting book, it is determined by the purchase price of the same type of property or a property meeting technical standards and having similar use value on the market at the time of commission of the violation;

  b) In case of delivery and use of property being automobiles, machinery, equipment, or other property in accordance with the prescribed quantity but exceeding the prescribed price: The excess value is determined by multiplying the excess quantity by (the difference between the original cost of the property recorded in the accounting book minus (-) the value of the property according to the prescribed norms and limits stipulated by the competent authority);

  c) In case of property being working offices or public service facilities: The excess value is determined by subtracting (-) the area used according to the prescribed norms and limits from the actual area recorded in the accounting book; in case the area is not recorded in the accounting book, the unit price is calculated based on the investment rate of houses or works with equivalent technical standards issued by the Ministry of Construction applicable at the time of commission of the violation;

  d) Persons having sanctioning competence prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as the basis for sanctioning in accordance with the law on handling of administrative violations and shall be responsible for such determination.

  Article 9. Administrative violations against regulations on lending of public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for lending or using public property in contravention of regulations (lending or using public property to other organizations or individuals regardless of whether there is a loan contract or not and regardless of the loan term):

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the lent property is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the lent property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed if the lent property is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Organizations committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of lent property. In case where the property has been changed due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original state; in case where it cannot be restored to its original state, it must be returned in cash or property with similar functions and use value to the initial property;

  b) Enforced payment to the state budget of an amount corresponding to the rent of property during the lending period. The determination of the rent of property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 10. Administrative violations against regulations on public property exchange and gifting

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of exchanging public property in contravention of regulations (using public property of an organization to exchange for property of another organization or individual without permission from a competent authority or person) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed where the property used for exchange is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed where the property used for exchange is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed where the property used for exchange is the head office, public service facility, or car.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of gifting public property in contravention of regulations (using public property as gifts in violation of Regulations on gift gifting promulgated by a competent authority).

  3. Remedial measures:

  Compulsory return of property or refund of money equivalent to the value of property exchanged or gifted in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 11. Administrative violations against regulations on encroaching on working offices or public service facilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for encroaching on a working office or public service facility (an organization/individual that is using adjacent land and relocates the boundary of the land parcel to expand the land area or builds an encroaching work into the land area of the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit; or an organization/individual that uses the house/land belonging to the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit without permission).

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  The violator that commits any of the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also take the remedial measures as follows:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of the working office or public service facility and return of the encroached working office or public service facility;

  b) Mandatory demolition of the work built on the encroached area;

  c) Enforced payment to state budget of an amount equivalent to the rent for property during the period of encroachment. The determination of the rent for property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 12. Acts of appropriating public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed on acts of appropriating public property which are not serious enough to be examined for penal liability (possession or use of public property without permission of competent state agencies) according to the following fine levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of public property is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of public property is VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of appropriation of working offices, public service facilities and cars.

  2. Additional sanctioning forms: Confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit acts specified in clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures: Organizations and individuals that commit violations specified in clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state of property altered by administrative violations to return it to its original condition; in case of failure to restore the original state of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value must be provided;

  b) Enforced payment to the State budget of an amount corresponding to the rent of property during the period of appropriation. The determination of the rent of property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with Clause 4 Article 7 hereof.

  Article 13. Administrative violations against regulations on using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture, or association

  1. A fine shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture, or association without a decision approving the Scheme issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with regulations of law on management and use of public property at the time of commission of the violation, as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is a working office, public service facility, or car.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on determining the value of property used for business purposes, leasing, joint venture, or association, as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to have the prescribed composition of members when establishing the Valuation Council or the Valuation Appraisal Council;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to follow procedures for selecting a valuation organization to determine the value of assets used for business, lease, joint venture or association purposes.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business, lease, joint venture or association purposes in a manner that is contrary to the scheme approved by a competent authority or person and affects the performance of functions and tasks assigned by the State:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is a car or the property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is an office building, a public service facility or a car.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of the property altered by the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, the property must be returned in cash or with similar functions and use value;

  b) Enforced transfer of illegal profits earned from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Administrative violations against regulations on failure to maintain or repair property as prescribed by law

  1. Organizations assigned by competent authorities to prepare estimates for maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law but failing to maintain or repair property resulting in damage shall be sanctioned as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of damaged property is less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of damaged property is from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of damaged property is VND 100.000.000 or more and the property is an office building, public service facility, or automobile and not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Determination of the amount of money to be returned corresponding to the value of damaged property:

  a) For irreparable damage, the amount of money to be returned shall be determined based on the purchase price of a new property of the same type or with equivalent standards and use of the market multiplied by the remaining quality percentage at the time of valuation;

  b) In case of damaged assets that can be repaired, the amount to be returned is the repair cost;

  c) The head of the organization shall assign the financial agency under their management to act as the focal point for determining the amount to be returned; based on this determination, the head of the organization shall decide the amount to be returned and take responsibility for his/her decision.

  Article 15. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare or prepare plans for disposition of public property in accordance with law;

  b) Declaring or preparing plans for disposition of public property beyond the prescribed time limit;

  c) Failing to report to competent state agencies for repossession, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of property when conditions are met in accordance with law (except where public property has expired in accordance with law but remains usable and agencies, organizations, units or enterprises still have the need to use it, in which case the head of the agency/organization/unit shall decide on an appropriate disposal method).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on organizations that damage or lose property while awaiting disposal.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for handling assets without a decision of a competent authority or person:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 in case of selling, transferring, liquidating or destroying assets valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the property is worth VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 in case of selling, transferring or liquidating assets being working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. The following penalties shall be imposed for declaring incorrect information about the list of assets and their current conditions (which may lead to a decision on handling of assets against regulations):

  a) A warning shall be imposed in case of declaring assets valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring assets worth VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring assets worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring as assets working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state of property altered by the acts specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original state of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value must be provided;

  b) Enforced return of an amount equivalent to the value of the lost or damaged property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  6. The amount of money to be refunded corresponding to the value of lost or damaged property specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case of lost or damaged assets that cannot be repaired, the amount to be returned shall be determined according to the price of new assets of the same type or with similar standards and use at the time when the violation occurs.

  Market prices may be based on quoted prices of suppliers posted or announced on the market or from official information disclosed by suppliers via the Internet.

  b/ For damaged assets which can be repaired, the amount to be returned is the repair cost thereof.

  Article 16. Administrative violations against regulations on organization of handling of public property

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to implement the approved plan for handling waste within the prescribed time limit.

  The time limit for a violation is determined by the handling time limit specified in the decision on handling of assets. If the decision on handling of assets does not specify a specific time limit, relevant laws shall apply.

  In case the plan for extending the disposal of assets is approved by a competent authority, the time limit shall be determined according to the extended time limit.

  b) Failing to hand over assets subject to transfer or recovery decisions to receiving agencies within the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Establishing the Pricing Council ultra vires as prescribed by law;

  b) Establishing the Pricing Council with improper composition as prescribed by law;

  c) Selecting a valuation organization or a professional auction organization to handle property in an improper form or order as prescribed by law;

  d) Failing to fully notify the public of the auction of public property as prescribed by law on management and use of public property (failing to post up information about the auction of public property at the prescribed places; failing to publicly notify it on mass media or failing to make sufficient notifications about the auction of public property as prescribed by law on property auction; failing to fully post up or publicly notify information as prescribed by law on property auction).

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to establish the Pricing Council as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to publicly notify the auction of public property as prescribed in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. A fine shall be imposed for failing to carry out valuation as a basis for determining the reserve price when handling property being working offices, public service facilities, or other property affixed to land through sale; failing to conduct the auction of property in cases where such auction is required by law:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the property is less than VND 100.000.000.

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case where the property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case where the property is a working office, public service facility or car.

  Article 17. Administrative violations against regulations on entry and use of data on public property

  The following fines shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on entry and use of data on public property:

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing declaration reports on property for entry of data into the National Public Property Database against legal documents or in a manner inconsistent with the current status of the property of the unit;

  b) Implementing decentralized data entry for subordinate units without a written agreement from the Ministry of Finance;

  c) Entering or approving data on public property inconsistently with the declaration report of the organization assigned to manage and use the property.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Erasing, deleting or altering asset declaration reports in order to falsify data on public property against the actual state of property;

  b) Accessing, intruding or attempting to intrude, destroy or alter data in the National Public Property Database or software program structure;

  c) Exploiting information on public property in the National Public Property Database by agencies, organizations or units not under their management;

  d) Using data on public property in the National Public Property Database for personal purposes without permission granted by the competent authority managing such database (using information stored in the database for purposes other than those prescribed by laws on management and exploitation of the Public Asset Registration Management Software without permission granted by the competent authority managing such database).

  3. Remedial measures

  a) Mandatory cancellation of false or fraudulent declaration reports;

  b) Mandatory correction of data to ensure consistency with the actual state of property of the unit;

  c) Enforced preparation of additional reports on assets declared not yet prepared when there are changes in asset operations;

  d) Enforced correction of false or misleading information;

  dd) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by the administrative violations.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Forced to restore the original state of assets changed due to administrative violations; in case of failure to restore the original state of assets, must be returned in cash or assets with functions and use value equivalent to the initial assets;

  b) Forced to pay an amount corresponding to the value of assets lost or damaged. The determination of the amount to be paid corresponding to the value of assets lost or damaged shall comply with provisions in clause 6 Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on logging in and using data on assets under public ownership
  Organizations and individuals committing violations against regulations on logging in and using data on assets under public ownership shall be sanctioned in accordance with Article 17 hereof.

  Section 4. VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF INFRASTRUCTURAL ASSETS

  Article 24. Violations against regulations on exploitation of infrastructure assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for leasing out or transferring the right to extract or use infrastructure assets for a definite term without obtaining a decision approving the scheme issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with regulations of law on management and use of public property.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory return of the proceeds obtained from the commission of the violation prescribed in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 25. Violations against regulations on disposal of infrastructure assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare plans for handling assets in accordance with regulations of law;

  b) Failing to report to competent authorities for recovery, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of assets when conditions are met as prescribed by law (except where public assets have expired according to regulations of law but agencies, organizations, units or enterprises still require them for use, in which case heads of agencies, organizations, units or enterprises shall decide on appropriate disposal methods);

  c) Failing to report to competent authorities for disposal of assets that are lost or destroyed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  b) Declaring incorrect asset lists or current conditions of assets proposed for disposal, resulting in decisions on disposal of assets being inconsistent with regulations.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to obtain decision of competent authority or person before disposing of assets.

  4. Remedial measures:

The determination of the amount to be returned as the value of the lost or damaged property shall comply with regulations in Clause 6 Article 15 hereof.

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF NATIONAL RESERVES

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON NATIONAL RESERVES AND FINES FOR THESE VIOLATIONS

  Article 40. Violations against regulations on purchase and sale of national reserves

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to notify or publicly post information on entities engaging in purchase and sale; methods of purchase and sale; selling prices; time limit for purchase and sale; quantity, categories, specifications, standards, and quality of national reserve goods as prescribed by competent regulatory authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  Failure to comply with regulations issued by competent regulatory authorities on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to purchase national reserve goods when all conditions have been met according to decisions issued by competent regulatory authorities;

  b) Purchase or sale of national reserve goods without written consent of competent regulatory authorities;

  c) Purchase or sale of national reserve goods after the time limit for purchase or sale of national reserve goods has expired;

  d) Purchase or sale of national reserve goods not in accordance with quantity, categories, specifications, standards, and quality approved by competent regulatory authorities.

  dd) Failing to comply with regulations issued by competent state authorities on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for arbitrarily changing the prices of national reserve goods for self-seeking purposes.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Compulsory disgorgement of illegal gains from commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 1, Clause 2 or Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 41. Violations against regulations on management of national reserve goods

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to open books for monitoring quality, quantity and categories of national reserve goods during storage;

  b) Failing to submit periodic or irregular reports on storage of national reserve goods.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for violations against regulations on transfer of national reserve goods:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent authorities on transfer of national reserve goods;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent authorities on transfer of national reserve goods.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Compulsory recall of transferred national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  Article 42. Violations against regulations on preservation of national reserve goods

  1. A fine shall be imposed for failure to comply with national technical regulations or temporary technical requirements for preservation of national reserve goods issued by competent authorities;

  Failure to comply with regulations on time limit for preservation of national reserve goods; preservation of national reserve goods at locations other than those specified by competent authorities shall be fined as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 if damage is caused by national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 if damage is caused by national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  2. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of storage, preservation and circulation of national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 if damage is caused by national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 if damage is caused by national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for hiring an organization/individual ineligible to preserve national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100,000,000.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from illegal activities to state budget in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 43. Acts of trespass or destruction of material-technical facilities, storage of national reserves which are not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for trespassing into material-technical facilities or storage of national reserves.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for destruction of material-technical facilities or storage of national reserves.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for acts of trespass or destruction of national reserves:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused to national reserves;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if less than VND 50,000,000 is damaged to national reserves;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  4. Additional penalties

  Confiscation of exhibits and equipment used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of the material and technical facilities or the storage of national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed;

  b) Mandatory return of damaged national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article is committed.

  Article 44. Violations against regulations on provision and assistance of national reserve goods

  1. Warnings or fines shall be imposed for obstruction of provision, assistance or performance of other tasks related to national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if the loss incurred is caused by the violation and the value of the national reserve goods is less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if the loss incurred is caused by the violation and the value of the national reserve goods is from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000;

  2. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out procedures for issuance or provision of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 within the prescribed time limit.

  3. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for using national reserve goods for wrong purposes; providing national reserve goods to wrong entities according to decisions of competent authorities; providing national reserve goods not satisfying requirements regarding categories, quantity, quality, standards, and specifications as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if the loss incurred is caused by the violation and the value of the national reserve goods is less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if the loss incurred is caused by the violation and the value of the national reserve goods is from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for loss of national reserve goods used for distribution, relief or performance of other tasks:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if the value of the national reserve goods is less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the value of the national reserve goods is from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for distributing national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 to ineligible entities according to a competent authority’s decision.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for delayed or untimely provision of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 in accordance with regulations of competent authorities.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for delaying or procrastinating the distribution of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 under decisions of competent authorities, thus causing damage to national reserve goods.

  8. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clauses 3, 5 and 6 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of lost national reserve goods, goods used for wrong purposes or goods issued to wrong recipients in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  c) Enforced return of damaged, inferior or devalued national reserve goods and insufficient quantity of national reserve goods in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 45. Violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of the import, export and transportation of national reserve goods with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 2,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to implement or improperly implementing procedures for import and export of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 as prescribed;

  b) Failing to deliver goods in the correct quantity, type, specifications, standards and quality of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 under decisions of competent state agencies.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with the classification, specifications, quantity, quality, time and place of receipt or delivery of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 under a decision of a competent authority.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 without a decision issued by a competent authority.

  5. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing the violations prescribed in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;

  b) Enforced recall of national reserve goods that have been issued against regulations if the violation prescribed in Clause 2, Clause 3 or Clause 4 of this Article is committed.

  Article 46. Violations against regulations on receipt of national reserve goods which are not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Receiving national reserve goods at a location or within a time limit other than those decided by the competent authority;

  b) Failing to comply with regulations on procedures for receiving national reserve goods or failing to comply with such procedures.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to receive national reserve goods (national reserve goods issued for aid or for performance of other tasks of the competent authority) when all conditions prescribed by the competent authority are satisfied.

  Article 47. Illegal intervention in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or fine shall be imposed for illegal intervention in national reserve activities as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if damage is caused by national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if damage is caused by national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000.

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 48. Violations against regulations on management of capital and charges in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to report on the use of funds derived from national reserve capital for purchase of national reserve products as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Payment made without a contract for purchase of national reserve products;

  b) Payment made when the quantity of national reserve products is incorrect or payment made without sufficient invoices and documents;

  c) Payment made without a record of contract liquidation or a list of documents recorded in the warehouse with signature of the unit’s head in case of direct purchase not through bidding;

  d) Payment made without approval of the unit’s head;

  dd) Use of import fees, export fees, and preservation fees for national reserve products inconsistently with the prescribed contents or exceeding the prescribed limits.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Using national reserve capital for wrong purposes;

  b) Failing to comply with regulations on management of money derived from national reserve capital;

  c) Failing to remit the remaining amount after completing the purchase of national reserve goods as prescribed by competent authorities.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory remittance of illegal profits earned from committing the violations specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 49. Violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, and exploitation of assets included in national reserves

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, or exploitation of assets included in national reserves (except for storage of national reserve goods):

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of the asset is less than VND 70.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at from VND 70.000.000 to under VND 150.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at from VND 150.000.000 to under VND 300.000.000;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at from VND 300.000.000 to under VND 500.000.000;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at ≥ VND 500.000.000.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of property being national reserve storage:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed in case the value of national reserve storage is less than VND 500.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed in case the value of national reserve storage is VND 500.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of property to its original conditions in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced transfer of illegal benefits obtained from the commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. AUTHORITY TO RECORD ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AND IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON NATIONAL RESERVES

  Article 50. Authority to record administrative violations against regulations on national reserves

  1. Persons having authority to record administrative violations against regulations on national reserves include:

  a) Persons having authority to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on national reserves as prescribed in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree;

  b) Officials assigned to inspect compliance with laws on national reserves.

  2. Persons having authority to record administrative violations as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall be responsible for issuing decisions to impose penalties within their jurisdiction or transferring them to competent authorities as prescribed in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree.

  Article 51. Power to impose penalties of Chairpersons of People’s Committees

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 50.000.000;

  c) [15] Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed VND 100.000.000;

  d) Enforce remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 52. Power to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Inspectors and persons assigned to conduct specialized inspections of national reserves while on duty have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 500.000;

Chief Inspectors of provincial Departments, heads of provincial inspectorates performing specialized inspections in the field of national reserves, heads of specialized inspection teams established by the Director General of the State Reserves Administration and heads of specialized inspection teams established by Directors of Regional State Reserves Departments shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 50.000.000;

  c) [17] Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed VND 100.000.000;

  d) Enforce remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of ministerial-level specialized inspection teams performing specialized inspections in the field of national reserves shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed VND 140.000.000;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry has the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 53. Power to impose penalties of Directors of Regional State Reserves Departments and Director General of State Reserves Administration

  1. Director of Regional State Reserve Department shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  c) [19] Confiscate the exhibits and instrumentalities for committing administrative violation which are worth no more than VND 140.000.000;

  d) Enforce the remedial measures specified in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Director General of State Reserve Administration shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Apply remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter V

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE DEPOSITS

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE DEPOSITS

  Article 54. Administrative violations against regulations on state budget expenditures approved by competent authorities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment for contents or tasks not included in the budget estimates approved by competent authorities;

  b) Prepare documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses in excess of the annual funding estimates or plans allocated by competent authorities or in excess of the approved budget estimates (for non-contractual work);

  c) Prepare documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses not in accordance with the estimates allocated by competent authorities (payment of expenses from sources other than those specified in the estimates; payment of expenses for purposes or beneficiaries other than those specified in the estimates; payment of expenses from sources other than those specified in the investment capital sources; payment of expenses for projects other than those specified in the list of projects approved by competent authorities).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Prepare documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses for the amount of work that has not been performed;

  b) Prepare documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses for the amount of work that is being performed but is incomplete or ineligible for payment;

  c) Prepare documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses exceeding the contract value or the contract appendix.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of expenses paid for the amount of work that has not been performed; the amount of work that is being performed but is incomplete or ineligible for payment; and the amount of expenses paid in excess of the contract value or the contract appendix as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 55. Acts of making documents and records in contravention of regulations and acts of making documents and records different from original ones at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for signing contracts before obtaining capital plans approved by competent authorities (except for construction works under emergency orders).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Making documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenses not in conformity with standards regarding title and beneficiaries;

  b) Making documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenses exceeding the limits regarding quantity or value;

  c) Making documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenses in contravention of regulations (payment not satisfying conditions and rules of regulations).

  In case an organization commits the act of purchasing or leasing state assets beyond the standards and limits prescribed by competent authorities, it shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of Articles 5 and 6 of this Decree.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for making documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury different from contents on the original documents, invoices and records at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of funds for the purposes of falsification of documents and records as prescribed, and for the purposes of falsification of documents and records in comparison with their original contents at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 56. Acts of making false documents and records for payment of state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1.[20] A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for making false documents and records and sending them to the State Treasury for payment of regular and recurrent expenditures, recurrent expenditures on national target programs or recurrent expenditures on professional programs (excluding expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of physical foundations from recurrent expenditures on the state budget and retained fees according to regulations for recurrent expenditures with a total investment of VND 500.000.000 or more).

  2.[21] A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for making false documents and records and sending them to the State Treasury for payment of investment capital from the state budget and investment capital from the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of physical foundations from recurrent expenditures on the state budget and retained fees according to regulations for recurrent expenditures with a total investment of VND 500.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of funds for the purposes of falsification of documents and records as prescribed, and for the purposes of falsification of documents and records in comparison with their original contents at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article.

  Article 57. Violations against regulations on payment of state budget expenditures

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records requesting the State Treasury to transfer payments to an account holder other than the one specified in the contract or appendix of the contract.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records requesting payment of state budget expenditures not in accordance with payment terms specified in the contract or appendix of the contract between the estimate unit or investor and the goods or service provider, including any of the following violations:

  a) Incorrect contract value;

  b) Incorrect payment term;

  c) Incorrect payment method;

  d) Incorrect payment percentage (including advance payment);

  dd) Incorrect other payment terms specified in the contract and appendix of the contract.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced completion of documents and vouchers in accordance with regulations for violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 58. Violations against procedures for controlling expenditure commitments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit expenditure commitments to the State Treasury for expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  b) Submitting requests for expenditure commitments to the State Treasury beyond the prescribed deadline for expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  c) Submitting requests for expenditure commitments exceeding the annual investment capital plan, the balance of the approved annual budget or the value of contracts for which commitments are permitted.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) The entity committing the violation in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to carry out expenditure commitment procedures before requesting the State Treasury to make payment or disburse funds for the state budget.

  b) Enforced re-performance of commitment payment procedures for the acts specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 59. Violations against procedures and time limits for advance payment of state budget

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Performing procedures for advance payment after the final deadline for advance payment for regular expenditures without goods or service purchase contracts or expenditures not required to submit goods or service purchase contracts to the State Treasury as prescribed;

  b) Failing to perform procedures for advance payment for investment capital expenditures funded by the state budget and investment capital expenditures funded by the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of material facilities using regular expenditures from the state budget and retained fees according to regulations for regular expenditures with total investment exceeding VND 500.000.000 through multiple payments of completed volume under contracts.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to perform procedures for full advance payment for regular expenditures with goods or service purchase contracts according to regulations in the final payment of the contract;

  b) Failing to perform procedures for full advance payment for investment capital expenditures funded by the state budget and investment capital expenditures funded by the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of material facilities using regular expenditures from the state budget and retained fees according to regulations for regular expenditures with total investment exceeding VND 500.000.000 when the value of the payment (including advance payment and payment of completed volume) reaches 80% of the contract value;

  c) Failing to make or to carry out procedures for making advance payments on compensation, support and resettlement expenses after the time limit for making advance payments has expired as prescribed.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced adoption of advance payment procedures for violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR REGISTRATION AND USE OF FUNDS AT STATE BANK

  Article 60. Violations against regulations on registration and use of accounts at State Treasuries

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on registration and use of accounts at State Treasuries.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced freezing or closing of accounts in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 61. Acts of making fake dossiers and papers to carry out procedures for registration of use of accounts at State Treasuries but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for acts of making fake dossiers and papers to be sent to the State Treasury to carry out procedures for registration of use of accounts.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Forcible freezing of accounts, applicable to violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 3. AUTHORITY TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF STATE TREASURY

  Article 62. Authority to make records of administrative violations in the field of State treasury

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations in the field of State treasury include:

  a) Persons competent to impose administrative penalties for violations in the field of control of state budget expenditures through the State Treasury as prescribed in Articles 63 and 64 of this Decree;

  b) Officials assigned to control state budget expenditures through state treasuries.

  c) Inspectors and persons assigned to perform specialized inspection tasks who are on duty.

  2. Persons competent to issue records as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall be responsible for issuing sanctioning decisions within their competence or transferring to competent authorities specified in Articles 63 and 64 of this Decree to issue sanctioning decisions.

  Article 63. Authority to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Chief Inspectors of Departments of Finance, heads of specialized inspectorate teams of Departments of Finance, heads of specialized inspectorate teams of State Treasuries established by Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and heads of specialized inspectorate teams of State Treasuries established by General Directors of State Treasuries have the authority to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 50.000.000;

  b) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chief Inspectors of the Ministry of Finance have the authority to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  b) Take remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspection teams established by the Ministry of Finance have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  b) Take remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 64. Power to impose penalties of Director General of State Treasury; Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and central-affiliated cities

  1. Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and central-affiliated cities have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  b) Applying remedial measures prescribed in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The General Director of State Treasury has the power to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  b) Apply remedial measures prescribed in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 65. Entry into force

  1. This Decree comes into force as of September 01, 2019.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury and the Government’s Decree No. 58/2015/ND-CP dated June 16, 2015 on amendments to the Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury shall cease to be effective from the effective date hereof.

  Article 66. Transitional provisions

  For administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury committed before the effective date hereof but discovered afterwards or being considered and handled, regulations on penalties prescribed herein shall apply if this Decree does not prescribe legal liability or impose lighter legal liability on violating individuals or organizations.

  Article 67. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Ministry of Finance shall provide guidelines for implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies, People’s Committees at all levels shall be responsible for providing guidelines for implementation and inspecting the imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury prescribed herein.

  3. When detecting administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury, competent authorities of the State, organizations and individuals shall transfer dossiers (if any) or notify them to persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations prescribed herein for consideration and handling.

  Where administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property fall under cases requiring recovery of public property in accordance with the law on management and use of public property, the persons competent to impose penalties for administrative violations prescribed in this Decree shall, after issuing decisions on imposition of administrative penalties, notify the competent state agencies in accordance with the law on management and use of public property to issue decisions on recovery of property as prescribed. The handling of recovered property shall comply with the law on management and use of public property.

  4. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels and relevant organizations and individuals are responsible for implementing this Decree./.
