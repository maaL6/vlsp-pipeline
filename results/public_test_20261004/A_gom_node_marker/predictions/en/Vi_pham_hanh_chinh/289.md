                                             DECREE
           REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF TOURISM
Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
Pursuant to the Law on Tourism dated June 14, 2005;
Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 02, 2002, and the Ordinance on Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated April 02, 2008;
At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
The Government promulgates the Decree prescribing penalties for administrative violations in the field of tourism, Chapter 1.
                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, authority to impose penalties, procedures for imposition of penalties and remedial measures in tourism.
2. Administrative violations in tourism are violations against regulations of law on state management in tourism committed by individuals or organizations intentionally or unintentionally and not considered crimes and subject to administrative penalties as per the law.
3. Administrative violations in tourism prescribed in this Decree include:
a) Violations against regulations on tour operator business, tour guide business, car business;
b) Violations against regulations on tourist accommodation business;
c) Violations against regulations on tourism promotion;
d) Violations against other regulations on tourism.
4. Other administrative violations in tourism and tourist service provision not prescribed in this Decree shall be sanctioned according to other Government’s Decrees on sanctioning of administrative violations in relevant state management sectors.
Article 2. Regulated entities
Vietnamese organizations and individuals, foreign organizations and individuals (hereinafter referred to as “individuals and organizations”) committing administrative violations in tourism within the territory of Vietnam.
Article 3. Sanctioning forms and remedial measures
1. For each administrative violation, violating individuals or organizations must face one of the following sanctioning forms:
a) Warnings;
b) Fines.
2. Depending on nature and severity of the violations, violating individuals and organizations may also be subject to one or more of the following additional sanctioning forms:
a) Suspension of international tour operator licenses, tour guide badges, and narrator certificates;
b) Confiscation of exhibits and vehicles used for administrative violations.
3. In addition to primary and additional penalties prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individual or organization may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:
a) Mandatory provision of adequate amenities, facilities, equipment, and devices to ensure health, life safety, and property of tourists;
b) Mandatory compliance with regulations on documentation and record-keeping as prescribed by law;

c) Mandatory return of illegally collected property to tourists or confiscation of such property;
d) Mandatory payment of deposit as prescribed;
dd) Mandatory compliance with reporting regulations as prescribed by law;
e) Enforced installation of signboards of standard tourist service cars as prescribed by law;
g) Enforced provision of adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages of managers and staff corresponding to standards of each type and class of accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
h) Enforced installation of signboards of tourist accommodation establishments in accordance with classification decisions issued by competent authorities;
i) Enforced removal of publications, advertising boards, signboards, items and equipment violating regulations of law;
k) Enforced dismantling and destruction of tourist propaganda and advertising items, termination of advertising programs for certain violations against regulations on tourism promotion activities;
l) Enforced application of measures to remedy environmental pollution caused by violations against regulations on environmental protection;
m) Enforced implementation of decisions of competent agencies and persons for acts of obstructing inspection and examination and imposing administrative penalties.
Chapter 2.
         ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES AND FINES
SECTION 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON TRAVEL BUSINESS, GUIDED TOURISM, AND AUTOMOBILE RENTAL FOR PASSENGER TRANSPORT
Article 4. Violations against regulations on notification of travel business operation A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
1. Failure to notify in writing the time of commencing travel business operations to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the province or centrally-affiliated city where the headquarters of the enterprise is located within 15 days from the date on which the enterprise officially commences business operations.
2. Failure to notify in writing the change of the legal representative of the enterprise, change of the type of enterprise, change of the name of the enterprise, or change of the location of the headquarters of the travel business enterprise to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the headquarters of the enterprise is located within 15 days from the date of such change.
3. Failure to notify in writing the change of the operator of international travel business operations to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 30 days from the date on which the enterprise changes the operator of international travel business operations.
4. Failure to notify in writing the establishment and commencement date of the branch/representative office of the travel business enterprise to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the headquarters of the enterprise is located and the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the branch/representative office is located within 45 days from the date on which the branch/representative office is established and officially commences operations.
5. Failure to notify in writing the change of the head, name or location of the branch/representative office of the travel business enterprise to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the headquarters of the enterprise is located and the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the branch/representative office is located within 10 days from the date of such change.
6. Failing to notify in writing the General Department of Tourism within 7 days from the date of losing international tour business license.
7. Failing to promptly notify the Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism of accidents or risks or incidents that may occur to tourists.
Article 5. Violations against regulations on tour business
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for at least 03 years to operate domestic tour business;
b) Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for at least 04 years to operate international tour business;
c) Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for at least 03 years to operate domestic tour business;

d) Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for at least 04 years to operate international tour business;
dd) The tour contract does not contain one of the contents as prescribed by law.
2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to provide written tour programs for tourists or representatives of tourist groups;
b) Failing to enter into tour contracts with tourists or their representatives in accordance with regulations;
c) Failing to settle legitimate requests and recommendations from tourists in accordance with law;
d) Using tour guides to carry out tour programs without entering into written contracts with tour guides or without including basic contents as prescribed;
dd) Failing to provide guidance or information related to tour programs upon request by tour operator;
e) Failing to clearly, publicly and honestly inform tourists about the number and prices of tourism services.
3. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) The tour operator uses false documents to operate tour business;
b) Failure to comply with regulations on making and keeping records as prescribed by law;
c) Using expired tour guide’s badge to guide tourists.
4. A fine of from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using vehicles, equipment or devices that fail to ensure health, safety and property of tourists as prescribed by law, except for cases specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 hereof;
b) Failure to comply with regulations on reporting to Vietnam National Administration of Tourism, Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
c) Using people who do not have tour guide’s badges to guide tourists.
5. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Providing international tour services without at least 3 international tour guides;
b) Failing to purchase insurance for Vietnamese tourists traveling abroad during the period of tourism program as prescribed;
c) Changing the tourism program, standards or services without consent of tourists or their representatives;
6. A fine of from VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to ensure sufficient deposit for international tour business as prescribed;
b) Erasing or modifying contents in license for international tour business;
c) Collecting money outside of contract or other acts of collecting illicit profits from tourists;
d) Failing to manage business activities of branches, representative offices and affiliates in accordance with law;
dd) Failing to manage tourists under signed travel contracts or programs;
e) Declaring contents in application for issuance or replacement of international tour operator license dishonestly;
g) Failing to follow procedures for replacement of international tour operator license as prescribed by law;
h) Using wrong names of enterprises, transactions or abbreviations registered with competent authorities for business purposes;
7. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Employing foreigners to act as tour guides in Vietnam;
b) Engaging in tour operator business after having issued a notice of suspension, termination or being suspended by a competent authority.

8. A fine ranging from VND 25,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for providing tour operator services against the international tour operator license.
9. A fine ranging from VND 30,000,000 to VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Allowing other organizations or individuals to use the international tour operator license to provide tour operator services;
b) Providing international tour operator services without an international tour operator license.
10. Additional penalties:
a) Suspension of the international tour operator license for 12 months in case of commission of the violation specified in Points a and c Clause 5; Points a, d and dd Clause 6 and Point a Clause 7 of this Article;
b) Suspension of the international tour operator license for 12 - 24 months in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 8 and Point a Clause 9 of this Article;
c) Confiscation of exhibits involved in administrative violations in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 3 and Point e Clause 6 of this Article.
11. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate means and equipment to ensure health, life safety, and property of tourists in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced compliance with regulations on preparation and retention of documents as prescribed by law in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;
c) Enforced compliance with reporting regulations as prescribed by law in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 4 of this Article;
d) Enforced payment of full amount of deposit for violations under Point a Clause 6 of this Article;
dd) Enforced return of illegally collected amounts to tourists or transfer them to state budget in case of commission of the violation in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 6 of this Article.
Article 6. Violations against regulations on tour operator business
1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:
a) Failing to notify in writing the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the date on which travel agency business commences within 15 days from the date on which the travel agency commences business activities;
b) Failing to notify in writing the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of changes to legal representatives, name or address of the travel agency within 15 days from the date of such changes;
c) Failing to clearly, publicly and honestly inform the number and prices of tourist services provided by the travel agency to tourists;
d) Operating at an address other than the registered one with competent authorities.
2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Erasing, altering or falsifying contents of tour program of the travel agency;
b) Selling tour program at a price higher than that charged by the travel agency;
c) Operating as a travel agency without registration of travel agency business;
d) Acting as travel agent without having signed travel agency contract with the travel enterprise.
3. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Acting as travel agent for enterprises that have no license to provide international travel services to sell tour programs to international tourists and Vietnamese tourists traveling abroad;
b) Continuing to act as travel agent after being revoked by competent authority.
4. Remedial measures:
Enforced return of revenues collected in violation of regulations to tourists or state budget if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 2 of this Article is committed.
Article 7. Violations against regulations on tourism guiding
1. A warning or a fine of from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed upon a tour guide or a tour narrator who fails to wear badge or certificate while working.

2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Providing inaccurate or inadequate information about the points or attractions;
b) Failing to carry tour programs and documents issued by tour operator companies while providing tour guiding services;
c) Failing to guide tourists to comply with Vietnamese laws, internal rules and regulations of the places where they visit, and respect local customs and practices;
d) Failing to provide adequate information about tour itineraries and programs to representatives of tourist groups and tourists.
3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Failing to enter into employment contracts with tour operator companies;
b) Failing to disseminate and guide tourists on measures to ensure their life safety, health and property during tour programs;
c) Let other persons use their tour guide cards or interpreter certificates to practice their profession;
d) Using tour guide’s cards or other persons’ certificates of tour guide to practice;
dd) Using expired tour guide’s cards;
e) Declare untruthfully documents and papers in the application for issuance, replacement or re-issuance of tour guide’s cards or interpreters’ certificates.
4. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Erasing and modifying contents of tour guide’s cards or interpreters’ certificates for practice;
b) Using domestic tour guide cards to guide foreign tourists.
c) Giving a tour at tourist attractions without a tour guide certificate.
5. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Providing tour guide services without holding a tour guide badge as prescribed;
b) Using fake tour guide badges or certificates of tour guide to provide tour guide services;
c) Arbitrarily changing tour programs, reducing standards and services provided to tourists;
d) Giving false information about cultural, historical, national and people-related values of Vietnam, causing negative impacts on the image, traditions, ethics and fine customs of Vietnam;
dd) Taking advantage of tour guiding activities to give presentations or provide information that affects social security, order and safety;
e) Leading tourists to prohibited areas;
g) Collecting money outside the contract or committing other acts of illegal benefits from tourists.
6. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed upon a foreigner who provides tour guiding services in Vietnam.
7. Additional penalties:
a) Revoke the tour guide badge for 12 months in case of commission of the violation in Point d Clause 2; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article;
b) Revoke the tour guide badge for 12 - 24 months in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 3; Points a and b Clause 4 or repetition of the violation specified in Points a, b and c Clause 2; Points a and b Clause 3; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article;
c) Revoke the speaker’s certificate for 12 to 24 months in case of commission of the violation specified at Point c Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
d) Confiscate exhibits used for commission of administrative violations in respect of violations prescribed in Points d, dd and e Clause 3; Point b Clause 5 of this Article.
8. Remedial measures:
Enforced return of illegally collected amounts to tourists or confiscation of such amounts to the state budget in case of commission of the violation in Point c or g Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 8. Violations against regulations on tourist transport business

1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for using an under-9 passenger car without adequate interiors, amenities or equipment as prescribed to transport tourists.
2. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for using an automobile with a capacity of 9 seats to less than 24 seats but the interior, amenities and equipment thereof are not up to standard for transporting tourists.
3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for using a 24-seat or more automobile without adequate interior, amenities and equipment as prescribed for tourist transport vehicles.
4. A fine ranging from VND 4,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using a caravan or a two-storey car with inadequate interior and equipment as prescribed for tourist transport.
5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Changing the transport schedule without the consent of tourists;
b) Failing to install signboards of standard tourist service automobiles as prescribed by law;
c) Using drivers of cars to transport tourists without certificates of training in tourism operations;
d) Employing drivers on tourist buses who do not have certificates of training in tourism operations.
6. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to comply with regulations on reporting to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism or the Department of Transport as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to carry out procedures for re-granting of standard tourist vehicle license plates in accordance with law;
c/ Not buying passenger insurance for tourists by means of transport.
7. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Using fake signboards of standard tourist service automobiles to conduct business activities;
b) Falsely identifying as tourist transport vehicles.
8. Additional penalties:
Confiscating the badge of the fake tourist transport vehicle in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 7 of this Article.
9. Remedial measures:
a) Mandatory provision of adequate interior, amenities and equipment of tourist transport vehicles to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;
b) Mandatory installation of the badge of the tourist transport vehicle in accordance with regulations of law in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 5 of this Article;
c) Mandatory submission of reports as prescribed in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 6 of this Article.
Article 9. Violations against regulations on establishment and operation of branches and representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam
Violations of regulations on establishment and operation of branches or representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam shall be sanctioned according to Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 13; Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 14 of the Government’s Decree No. 06/2008/ND-CP dated January 16, 2008.
Item 2. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON TOURIST ACCOMMODATION BUSINESS
Article 10. Violations of regulations on tourist accommodation business
1. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to notify in writing the start date of tourist accommodation business to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments of localities where tourist accommodation establishments are based within 15 days after such establishments officially open;
b) Failure to notify in writing the change of heads of tourist accommodation establishments or names of tourist accommodation establishments to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments within 15 days after such changes;

c) Failure to notify in writing the application of telecommunication charges in classified tourist accommodation establishments to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments or provincial-level Information and Communications Departments within 15 days after such application is made.
2. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to comply with regulations on name boards and classification of tourist accommodation establishments;
b) Failing to put up name boards after being classified by the General Department of Tourism, the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
c) Failing to promulgate and post up internal rules and regulations of tourist accommodation establishments;
d) Failing to clearly and publicly inform tourists about the quantity, quality and price of services and goods offered;
dd) Failing to put up decisions on recognition of tourist accommodation establishment classification at visible positions at reception desks.
3. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 7.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to comply or improperly complying with regulations on making and keeping records and documents on business activities of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to have sufficient number of and adequate sleeping rooms in accordance with standards applicable to each type and class of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
c) Failure to meet standards regarding parking and internal traffic, reception hall area as per the law;
d) Failure to meet number and standards of restaurants and bars as per the law;
dd) Failure to meet standards regarding kitchen area as per the law;
e) Failure to meet number and standards of conference rooms, seminar rooms, meeting rooms as per the law;
g) Failure to meet standards regarding other equipment and amenities as per the law;
h) Failure to meet standards regarding services as per the law;
i) Failure to meet standards regarding professional qualifications and foreign languages of managers and staff as per the law.
4. A fine ranging from VND 7.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:
a) Using means and equipment that fail to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to comply with regulations on reporting to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism as prescribed by law;
c) Failing to provide additional equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages for managers and staff in accordance with the classification standards at the request of the tourism authority.
5. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to register with the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism for appraisal and classification of tourist accommodation establishments after 03 months from the date on which such establishments officially commence business operations;
b) Failing to register with the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism for appraisal and reclassification of tourist accommodation establishments before 03 months from the date on which the recognition of the classification of tourist accommodation establishments expires;
c) Using the star image affixed to the tourist accommodation establishment or on publications, advertising boards, signboards, items and equipment used at the tourist accommodation establishment when it has not been recognized or is not in accordance with the recognized classification of the tourist accommodation establishment.
6. A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using names of tourist accommodation establishments, transaction names or abbreviated names not conformable with names of tourist accommodation establishments registered with competent authority for business purposes;
b) Collecting money outside of contracts or other acts of illegally profiting from tourists;
c) Collecting service charges in contravention of regulations.
7. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed for engaging in tourist accommodation business after having the operation suspended, terminated or stopped by competent authority.
8. Regulations from Clause 1 through Clause 6 of this Article shall also apply to guest houses and hostels of ministries, central authorities and local governments that engage in tourist accommodation business.

9. Additional penalties:
Confiscation of exhibits of administrative violations in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 4 or Clause 8 of this Article.
10. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages of managers and staff corresponding to standards applicable to each category and class of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law, for violations specified in Points b, c, d, dd, e, g, h and i Clause 3; Point c Clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced provision of adequate means and equipment ensuring health, life safety and property of tourists, for violations specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
c) Enforced return of illegally collected property to tourists or confiscation thereof, for violations specified in Points b and c Clause 6 of this Article;
d) Enforced removal and destruction of publications, advertising boards, signboards, items and equipment, for violations specified in Point a Clause 2 and Point c Clause 5 of this Article;
dd) Enforced installation of signs indicating classes of tourist accommodation establishments in accordance with classification decisions issued by competent authorities, for violations specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 11. Violations against regulations on sale of goods and conditional business services in tourist accommodation establishments
A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to register with competent authorities when providing conditional business services in classified tourist accommodation establishments.
SECTION 3. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON TOURISM INFORMATION
Article 12. Violations against regulations on tourism promotion
1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) The contents of tourist product communication and promotion are not consistent with the published national tourism database;
b) Failure to implement or improper implementation of tourism promotion programs;
c) Promotion of tourist products whose contents and quality are not satisfactory;
d) Use of tourist product communication and promotion products without or with incorrect general title and logo regulated by the General Department of Tourism.
2. A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of tourism promotion activities to harm legitimate rights and interests of other organizations and individuals.
3. Remedial measures:
Enforced dismantling and destruction of tourist propaganda and promotion items, termination of promotion programs, for violations prescribed in Points a, c and d Clause 1 of this Article.
SECTION 4. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON OTHER TOURIST ACTIVITIES
Article 13. Violations of regulations on tourism resources and tourism development planning A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for exploiting tourism resources against regulations in tourist areas, tourist attractions or areas which have been planned for tourism development.
Article 14. Violations of regulations on protection of the environment in tourism
1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to post the environmental protection rules at places easily visible in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions or on tourist transport means.
2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to have public sanitation works meeting environmental sanitation requirements in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions or on tourist transport means as prescribed;
b) Failure to classify garbage as prescribed in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions or on tourist transport means.
c) Failing to assign officials to monitor and manage environmental protection of tourism in accordance with regulations of law.
3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

a) Failing to collect and treat waste as prescribed at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions and tourist transport vehicles;
b) Causing damage to the living environment of wild animals in areas where tourism resources are available;
c) Cutting branches or committing other acts causing damage to trees and vegetation in areas where tourism resources are available.
4. A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to submit annual reports on environmental protection of tourism as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to arrange collection and treatment equipment meeting environmental sanitation requirements at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist transport vehicles and tourist areas.
5. Additional penalties:
Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b or Point c Clause 3 of this Article.
6. Remedial measures:
Mandatory implementation of remedial measures for environmental pollution caused by the violation specified in Point a or Point b Clause 2; Point a Clause 3 and Point b Clause 4 of this Article.
Article 15. Violations against regulations on management and business operations in tourist areas and tourist attractions A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
1. Failing to promulgate internal regulations or rules on management of tourist areas or tourist attractions;
2. Promulgating internal regulations or rules on management of tourist areas or tourist attractions inconsistently with applicable law provisions;
3. Committing violations against regulations of tourist areas or tourist attractions.
Article 16. Penalties for obstruction of inspection and examination and administrative penalties
1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for refusing to accept inspection decisions or enforcement decisions on penalties for administrative violations.
2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 7.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Failing to declare or providing false or untimely declarations at the request of law enforcement officers or competent authorities;
b) Failing to provide or providing insufficient information or documents related to the inspection, examination, and imposition of penalties for administrative violations by law enforcement officers or competent authorities;
c) Obstructing the work of inspection teams or persons assigned to carry out inspection tasks;
d) Using words or actions that threaten, insult, or harm the honor of law enforcement officers or persons carrying out inspection tasks.
3. A fine ranging from VND 7.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Delaying or avoiding the execution of administrative decisions and inspection decisions of competent persons or agencies;
b) Arbitrarily removing seals on exhibits and means used in commission of violations or arbitrarily altering scenes of administrative violations;
c) Dispersing, altering or swapping exhibits under examination, inspection or temporary seizure;
d) Attacking people on duty.
4. Additional penalties:
Confiscating exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations, applicable to the violations prescribed in Points b and c Clause 3 of this Article.
5. Remedial measures:
Compulsory execution of decisions of competent authorities and persons, applicable to the violations specified in Clause 1; Points a and b Clause 2 and Point a Clause 3 of this Article.
Chapter 3.
                     AUTHORITY TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES
Article 17. Authority to impose administrative penalties of Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels have the authority to impose penalties within their jurisdiction as prescribed in Clauses 4 and 5 Article 1 of the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2008, Article 30 of the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2002 amended by the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2007 for administrative violations specified in this Decree within the management scope of local governments.
Article 18. Authority to impose administrative penalties of inspectors of Culture, Sports and Tourism
1. Inspectors of Culture, Sports and Tourism who are on duty have the authority to:
a) Issue warnings;
b) Impose fines of up to VND 500,000;
c) Confiscate material evidence and means used for committing administrative violations with a value of up to VND 2,000,000;
d) Apply remedial measures prescribed in Points a, b, d, dd, e, g, h, i, k, l and m Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
dd) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
2. Chief Inspector of Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism has the jurisdiction:
a) Issue warnings;
b) Impose a fine of up to VND 30,000,000;
c) Revoke the right to use licenses or practicing certificates within his/her jurisdiction;
d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for committing administrative violations;
dd) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Implementing other rights according to regulations of law on handling administrative violations.
3. Chief Inspector of Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism has the jurisdiction:
a) Imposing penalties in the form of warnings;
b) Imposing fines up to maximum limits applicable to tourism sector;
c) Revoking licenses or practicing certificates within their jurisdiction;
d) Seizing exhibits and vehicles used for committing administrative violations;
dd) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Exercise of other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
Article 19. Authority to sanction administrative violations of People’s Public Security, Border Guard and Coast Guard
People’s Public Security, Border Guard and Coast Guard have authority to sanction according to their competence specified in Clauses 6, 7 and 8 Article 1 of the Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the 2008 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 37 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for administrative violations in tourism directly related to their management fields prescribed in this Decree.
Article 20. Authority to sanction of inspectors of other sectors
Within the scope of state management authority prescribed by the Government, inspectors and Chief Inspectors of inspectors of other sectors have authority to sanction administrative violations in tourism prescribed in this Decree under the state management fields of their sectors.
Article 21. Issuance of forms of records and decisions for use in sanctioning administrative violations Issuing together with this Decree the forms of records and decisions for use in sanctioning administrative violations in tourism.
Chapter 4.
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 22. Effect
1. This Decree takes effect on April 30, 2012.
2. This Decree replaces the Government’s Decree No. 149/2007/ND-CP of October 9, 2007 on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of tourism.
Article 23. Implementation responsibility
1. The Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall implement this Decree.

2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.
