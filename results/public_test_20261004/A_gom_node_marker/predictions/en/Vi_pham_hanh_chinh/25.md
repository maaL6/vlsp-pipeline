                                                    DECREE

    REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN SOCIAL SUPPORT AND CHILDCARE FIELD

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization No. 63/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 56/2024/QH15 and Law No. 88/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to Law on Children No. 102/2016/QH13 amended by Law No. 28/2018/QH14, Law No. 59/2024/QH15 and Law No. 81/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to Law on Elderly People No. 39/2009/QH12 amended by Law No. 41/2024/QH15;

  Pursuant to Law on Persons with Disabilities No. 51/2010/QH12 amended by Law No. 32/2013/QH13;

  Pursuant to Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking No. 53/2024/QH15;

  At the request of the Minister of Health;

  The Government promulgates Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children.

  Chapter I

                                                GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations; penalties, fines, remedial measures for each administrative violation; entities subject to penalties; authority to impose penalties, specific fines by title and authority to make records of administrative violations; implementation of administrative penalties and remedial measures in social protection and children affairs.

  2. Administrative violations in social protection and children affairs not prescribed in this Decree shall be governed by regulations on penalties for administrative violations in relevant state management sectors.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations; foreign individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in social protection and children affairs within the territory, territorial waters, contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam; on aircraft bearing Vietnamese nationality or seagoing ships flying the flag of Vietnam, unless otherwise provided by international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory, including:

  a) Individuals committing administrative violations;

  b) Organizations committing administrative violations that are juridical persons as prescribed by civil law or other organizations established under law, including: types of social assistance establishments, elderly care facilities, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, victim support facilities, child protection service providers; religious establishments; policy payment service providers; socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations; enterprises, cooperatives, production and business establishments; cultural, entertainment, sports establishments; medical examination and treatment establishments; education establishments; vocational education and training institutions and other organizations.

  2. Persons having authority to make records of administrative violations.

  3. Persons having authority to impose penalties for administrative violations.

  4. Agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of penalties for administrative violations as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 3. Prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations

  1. The prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations in the field of social protection and children is 01 year.

  2. The determination of whether an administrative violation has ended or is being committed for the purpose of calculating the prescriptive period shall comply with Clause 1 Article 8 of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP dated December 23, 2021 of the Government on elaboration of certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  Article 4. Enforcement of penalties for administrative violations

  1. The enforcement of decisions on imposition of penalties for administrative violations in the field of social protection and children shall comply with the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 56/2024/QH15 and Law No. 88/2025/QH15 (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Handling of Administrative Violations”) and guiding documents.

  2. The imposition of penalties for administrative violations in the field of social protection and children online shall comply with Chapter IIIa of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  3. During the process of considering and handling violations, during the implementation of decisions on administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 8, Article 9, Article 11, Clause 1 Article 12, Point a Clause 2 Article 19, Clause 2 Article 20, Clause 2 Article 22, Clause 1 Article 23, Clause 2 Article 24, Clause 1 Article 25, Clause 1 Article 29, Clause 1 Article 33, Clause 1 Article 36, Points a and b Clause 1 Article 37 of this Decree, if detecting signs of crimes in the violations, the competent persons who are handling the cases or the persons issuing decisions on administrative penalties must immediately transfer the violation files to the competent authorities for criminal proceedings for handling according to Article 62 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations. In case the competent authorities for criminal proceedings do not conduct criminal prosecution, the violation files shall be transferred to the persons with power to impose administrative penalties according to Clause 3 Article 62 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations for imposing administrative penalties according to regulations of this Decree.

  Article 5. Penalties, remedial measures; implementation of penalties and remedial measures

  1. Principal penalties:

  a) Warnings;

  b) Fines.

  2. Additional penalties:

  a) Confiscation of exhibits of administrative violations;

  b) Suspension of part or all of the business activities related to the violations for 1 to 3 months;

  c) Suspend operation for a fixed period of 03 to 06 months.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Remedial measures prescribed in Points a, dd, e and i Clause 1 Article 28 of the Law on Actions against administrative violations;

  b) Enforced cessation of care and nurturing;

  c) Enforced removal, deletion or removal of information, images and documents of human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  d) Compulsory apology upon request;

  dd) Enforced return of illegal collected amounts;

  e) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs;

  g) Enforced repair and supplementation of facilities and personnel as per regulation;

  h) Compulsory payment of benefits as prescribed;

  i) Enforced return of aid money or goods obtained from committing the violation;

  k) Enforced return of disability certificates;

  l) Enforced fulfillment of responsibilities of the council or enforced implementation of methods for determining and re-determining the level of disability;

  m) Enforced determination and re-determination of disability level;

  n) Enforced payment of all expenses for books, notebooks and learning supplies for children;

  o) Enforced provision of adequate programs, syllabi and number of teachers in conformity with vocational education for persons with disabilities;

  p) Enforced issuance of diplomas/certificates;

  q) Enforced recall and destruction of products, publications, toys, newspapers, and published works;

  r) Enforced removal of news articles from online newspapers, magazines, radio, television, and electronic publications;

  s) Enforced termination of substitute care or change to appropriate substitute care;

  t) Enforced relocation of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods or goods with fire/explosion risk, child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and entertainment venues for children;

  u) Enforced dismantling of structures and equipment;

  v) Enforced timely implementation of safety measures;

  x) Enforced removal of information and services harmful to children; removal of counterfeit services and applications that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of children on the internet;

  y) Enforced revocation, deletion or removal of information on private lives and personal secrets of children.

  4. The implementation of penalties and remedial measures prescribed in this Decree shall comply with Section 2 and Section 3 Chapter III of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations, Articles 17a, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23 and Article 41 of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  Article 6. Fines and authority to impose fines

  1. The maximum fine for a violation against regulations on social protection and children affairs imposed upon an individual is VND 50.000.000, and upon an organization is VND 100.000.000.

  2. The fines prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree are applied to administrative violations committed by individuals, except for Articles 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 34, Clause 1, Clause 2 Article 35 and Clause 2 Article 37 of this Decree. The fine imposed upon an organization for the same administrative violation shall be twice as much as the fine imposed upon an individual.

  3. The authority to impose administrative penalties of a title prescribed in Articles 39, 40, 41, 42, 43, 44 and 45 of this Decree is the authority to impose penalties for administrative violations committed by individuals; in case of fines, the authority to impose penalties for organizations shall be twice as much as the authority to impose penalties for individuals committing the same violation.

  Chapter II

     VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON SOCIAL SUPPORT AND ASSISTANCE

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on declaration of eligibility for social support and pension benefits and victim assistance

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed on an individual committing the act of fraudulently claiming emergency social support but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed on an individual committing the act of fraudulently claiming victim status to receive one of the following support policies: education or vocational training or allowance for initial difficulties but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed on:

  a) Making false declarations to receive monthly social allowances but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Making false declarations to receive monthly pension benefits but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Making false declarations to receive monthly allowance for care and nurture but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Making false declarations in order to be admitted to social support facilities, child protection service providers, elderly care facilities, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, and human trafficking victim support facilities.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing the violations specified in Clause 1, Clause 2, Point a, Point b, Point c Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced cessation of care and support at social assistance facilities, child protection service providers, elderly care facilities, facilities for persons with disabilities, and human trafficking victim support facilities, in respect of violations under Point d Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on support for human trafficking victims and persons undergoing victim identification

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Obstructing the reception of human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  b) Obstructing the provision of support to human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  c) Insulting, stigmatizing, or discriminating against victims of human trafficking or persons undergoing victim identification.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for threatening victims of human trafficking, persons undergoing victim identification, their relatives, witnesses, informants, denouncers, or individuals preventing violations prescribed in Article 3 of the Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking without facing criminal prosecution.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for disclosing information about the name, age, phone number, social media account, address, place of residence, workplace, native place, form of trafficking, health condition, image, and personal documents of a victim of human trafficking or a person undergoing victim identification without their consent and without facing criminal prosecution, except where such disclosure is necessary to protect the life or health of the disclosed individual or where otherwise provided by law.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory revocation, deletion, or removal of information, images, and documents of victims of human trafficking or persons undergoing victim identification in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory apology for the acts specified in Clauses 1, 2, and 3 of this Article when requested, for victims of human trafficking, persons undergoing victim identification, their relatives, witnesses, informants, denouncers, or individuals preventing violations.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on care and support responsibilities of persons providing care for social protection beneficiaries, elderly persons, persons with disabilities, and children in the community

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed upon a person providing care for a beneficiary of community-based care and support who does not face criminal prosecution.

  a) Forcing someone to abstain from eating or drinking; preventing or restricting personal hygiene;

  b) Forcing someone to live in a toxic and dangerous environment;

  c) Insulting, mistreating, hurting, persecuting, or discriminating against social protection beneficiaries, older persons, persons with disabilities, and children.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on an individual who accepts care and support but commits one of the following acts which, however, is not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) Taking advantage of the care and support to seek personal benefits;

  b) Forcing the person receiving care and support to engage in arduous, hazardous, or dangerous labor.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed on an individual who accepts care and support but commits one of the following acts which, however, is not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) Enticing or inducing the person receiving care and support to commit illegal acts;

  b) Enforced provision of care for the violator.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing the violations specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of medical examination and treatment costs incurred by the affected person due to the violations specified in Clause 1, Point b Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on operation of social support facilities, facilities providing care for older persons, facilities providing care for persons with disabilities, victim support facilities, and child protection service providers (hereinafter referred to as “Facilities”)

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed upon a Facility failing to adequately store documents of persons receiving care as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed upon a Facility committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit periodic reports on the operation of the Facility as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to provide or providing inadequate personal items for daily living activities, including: blankets, mosquito nets, mats, summer clothing, winter clothing, undergarments, towels, shoes, toilet brushes, and common medicines; monthly personal hygiene items for female individuals, books, study supplies for students, and other items as prescribed by law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on the establishment committing one of the following violations:

  a) Using funding or facilities for wrong purposes;

  b) Collecting service fees against the law;

  c) Failing to meet environmental, health, facility, or personnel requirements;

  d) Failing to comply with service provision procedures issued by competent authorities.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal profits earned from committing the violation in Point a Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of illegal collected amounts to beneficiaries for committing the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;

  c) Enforced repair and supplementation and assurance of physical foundations and personnel as per regulation for committing the violation prescribed in Point c Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on responsibilities of service providers

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Making payments of monthly social allowances, monthly care and nurturing expenses, monthly social pension, burial expense support (hereinafter referred to as “policy benefits”) which are insufficient for eligible beneficiaries as prescribed by law but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Making payments of policy benefits beyond the deadline for eligible beneficiaries as prescribed by law but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to make payments of policy benefits for eligible beneficiaries but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Making payments to ineligible beneficiaries but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Compulsory payment of allowances to eligible beneficiaries for violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on management of aid money and supplies

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations, if not liable to criminal prosecution:

  a) Damaging or losing aid money or supplies, except force majeure events such as natural disasters or fires;

  b) Using or distributing aid money or supplies for wrongful purposes or to ineligible beneficiaries;

  c) Swapping aid supplies.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of the amount or compensation for the value of aid products that are damaged or lost due to the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by beneficiaries whose health is affected due to violations specified in Points a and c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on prohibited acts for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) To stigmatize and discriminate against persons with disabilities;

  b) Obstructing the marriage and child adoption rights of persons with disabilities;

  c) Obstructing persons with disabilities from living independently and integrating into the community;

  d) Obstructing persons with disabilities from participating in social activities on an equal footing;

  dd) Obstructing persons with disabilities from exercising their right to access information technology.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to fulfill responsibilities to nurture and care for persons with disabilities as prescribed by law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of the image, personal information, condition of persons with disabilities, organizations of persons with disabilities, or organizations for persons with disabilities for self-seeking purposes, or committing violations of the law but not to the extent of being liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for enticing, persuading, or forcing persons with disabilities to commit violations of the law but not to the extent of being liable to criminal prosecution.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory return of illegal profits obtained from committing the violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced fulfillment of responsibilities to nurture and care for persons with disabilities in accordance with law.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on responsibility for healthcare of persons with disabilities at medical examination and treatment establishments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to provide counseling on measures to prevent and detect disabilities early.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to identify congenital disabilities in newborns so as to promptly take appropriate treatment, rehabilitation and functional recovery measures.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to renovate or upgrade facilities serving medical examination and treatment to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities upon renovation and repair of medical examination and treatment establishments.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on education of persons with disabilities at educational institutions

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to ensure teaching and learning conditions suitable for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to provide learning support means and materials specifically for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  c) Failing to allow persons with disabilities to be exempted from or reduced the number of subjects or contents and educational activities which they cannot handle;

  d) Refusing persons with disabilities to enroll at higher ages in accordance with law;

  dd) Imposing enrolment requirements that exclude persons with disabilities, except for certain cases prescribed by law;

  e) Failing to give priority to enrolment for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  g) Obstructing the right to learn of persons with disabilities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed on:

a) The entity committing the violation in Point a) of this Clause;

  a) Failing to provide exemption or reduction of tuition fees, training costs and other contributions in accordance with the law on education;

  b) Failing to renovate and upgrade teaching and learning facilities to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities when renovating and repairing educational institutions.

  Article 16. Violations against regulations on vocational education and training activities of vocational education and training institutions for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to provide employment counseling for persons with disabilities in accordance with law.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to maintain conditions for vocational education and training activities for persons with disabilities after organizing training activities for 6 months or more;

  b) Failing to provide adequate programs, syllabi or qualified teachers suitable for vocational education and training activities for persons with disabilities;

  c) Failing to ensure appropriate training forms and duration suitable for vocational education and training activities for persons with disabilities;

  d) Failing to issue diplomas or certificates when persons with disabilities are eligible for issuance of diplomas or certificates as prescribed by law.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced provision of adequate vocational education and training programs, syllabi, and qualified teachers for persons with disabilities in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced issuance of degrees or certificates in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 17. Violations against regulations on traffic participation for persons with disabilities and older persons

  A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  1. The sale of tickets to persons with disabilities and the elderly shall not be prioritized.

  2. Persons who are responsible as per the law but fail to assist or arrange seats suitable for persons with disabilities and the elderly.

  3. Refusal to transport persons with disabilities or refusal to transport appropriate assistive devices and equipment for persons with disabilities by public means.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on information and communication for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed upon an enterprise manufacturing or distributing information and communication products, including hardware, software, and digital content, that violates one of the regulations on mandatory application of standards and technologies to support persons with disabilities in accessing and using information and communication products.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to apply preferential lending policies with preferential interest rates and other support policies for research, development, production, and provision of services and assistive devices for persons with disabilities to access information technology and communication.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on determination of disability level

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to fully exercise the responsibilities of the Council for determination of disability level;

  b) Failing to fully apply methods for determining and re-determining the disability level.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Taking advantage of determination of disability level for self-seeking purposes but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Refusing to determine or re-determine the disability level, issue or reissue a certificate of disability;

  c) Committing fraud in determination of disability level, re-determination of disability level, issuance or reissuance of a certificate of disability.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory fulfillment of responsibilities of the Council for the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced full implementation of methods for determining and re-determining the level of disability in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced transfer of the illegal profit earned from committing the violations specified in Points a and c Clause 2 of this Article;

  d) Enforced determination and re-determination of the level of disability in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article;

  dd) Enforced return of the certificate of disability in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on rights and obligations of older persons

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Obstructing an older person who lives with their children or grandchildren or lives alone;

  b) Obstructing an older person from exercising their right to own property, participate in cultural, educational, sports, recreational, tourism, rest activities, or other lawful rights;

  c/ Not exempting the contributions to social activities for the elderly;

  d) Do not prioritize older persons receiving money, aid, healthcare, and accommodation to overcome initial difficulties when older persons face difficulties due to natural disasters or other force majeure events;

  dd) Failing to pay support amounts provided by the State for older persons as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts: forcing to abstain from eating or drinking; preventing or restricting personal hygiene; forcing to live in a toxic and dangerous environment or committing other forms of maltreatment against older persons which, however, is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of illegal collected amounts to the elderly for violations under Point c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced payment of benefits as prescribed in case of the violations specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by older persons whose health is affected as a result of commission of the violation in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on care for older persons

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to fulfill their obligations to care for and support older persons in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to fully implement commitments under service contracts for caring for older persons in accordance with law;

  c) Taking advantage of caring for and supporting older persons for personal gain but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 2. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON CHILDREN

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on care for children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) The parent or caregiver fails to fulfill their obligations and responsibilities in caring for the child, except where the child is placed under temporary separation or adoption as prescribed by law;

  b) The parent or caregiver fails to care for the child, fails to fulfill their obligations and responsibilities in caring for the child, or abandons the child to fend for themselves, breaks the emotional and material bond with the child, or forces the child to live separately from the family, except where the child is placed under temporary separation or adoption as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed on the parent or caregiver who intentionally abandons a child but not to the extent of criminal liability.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on prohibition of violence against children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations which, however, is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability:

  a) Forcing children to abstain from eating or drinking; preventing or restricting personal hygiene;

  b) Forcing children to live in a toxic and dangerous environment or subjected to other forms of ill-treatment;

  c) Causing mental damage, hurting dignity, insulting, scolding, threatening, isolating children and affecting their development;

  d) Isolating, repelling or using punitive measures to teach children causing physical and mental damage to children;

  dd) Regularly threatening children with images, sounds, animals and objects, making them scared and suffering mental damage.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Forced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children if the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article causes injury or health damage to children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Forced destruction of articles harmful to children’s health if the violation prescribed in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article is committed.

  Article 24. Violations against regulations on child exploitation

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations: forcing children to do housework beyond their capacity or beyond the prescribed time limit, affecting their study, entertainment and recreation, adversely affecting their development.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations which, however, is not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) Organizing or forcing children to beg;

  b) Leasing out, lending out or using children to beg;

  c) Leading, inciting, enticing, persuading, inducing, abusing or forcing children to act as intermediaries in child exploitation activities;

  d) Leading, inciting, enticing, persuading, inducing, abusing or forcing children to work against the law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of children’s images or personal information to create content that negatively affects their physical and mental development or for the purpose of seeking profit, if not serious enough for criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of committing violations specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article which cause injury or damage to their health but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 25. Violations against regulations on prohibiting using, inciting, persuading, abusing, enticing, coercing children to commit violations of law or hurting others’ honor and dignity

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for any one of the following acts which, however, is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability:

  a) Using children to commit violations of law, or to hurt the dignity and honor of others;

  b) Inciting children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of others;

  c) Inciting or encouraging children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of others;

  d) Taking advantage of, enticing or deceiving children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of other persons;

  dd) Forcing children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of other persons.

  2. Remedies:

  Forced payment of all expenses for medical examination and treatment for children in case of violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article which cause injury or damage to their health but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 26. Violations against regulations on the right to health care of children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to ensure appropriate nutrition suitable for physical and mental development of children at each age group;

  b) Failing to perform or partially performing the initial health care, disease prevention, counseling and support for children in reproductive and sexual health care.

  2. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to provide or obstruct pregnant women from accessing healthcare services to receive counseling on prenatal screening and prevention of congenital diseases for children;

  b) Failing to comply with decisions, measures, and regulations of competent authorities and individuals to ensure the life safety and health of children;

  c) Applying harmful customs and practices that affect children’s health;

  d) Failing to prioritize medical examination and treatment for children.

  Article 27. Violations against regulations on the right to education of children

  1. A fine of from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Destroying books, notebooks, and school supplies of children;

  b) Intentionally failing to contribute to children’s learning as prescribed by law;

  c) Failing to ensure time and conditions for learning for children.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for obstructing children’s attendance at school.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for inciting, enticing or forcing children to drop out of school.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory payment of all expenses for books, notebooks and learning materials for children in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 28. Violations against regulations on children’s right to access information and participate in social activities; right to express opinions; right to preserve and promote their identity; right to play and entertain themselves; and prohibition of discrimination against children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Obstructing children from participating in social activities suitable for their age, maturity level, needs and capacity, except where it is in the best interests of the children;

  b) Obstructing children from expressing opinions, aspirations and participating in matters concerning children;

  c) Obstructing the right to preserve and promote identity;

  d) Obstructing the right to enjoy, relax, participate in cultural, artistic, physical training, sports and tourism activities as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Forcing or repressing children when they express opinions, aspirations and participate in matters concerning children;

  b) Failing to respect, listen to, consider, respond to and explain opinions and aspirations of children;

  c) Failing to receive, process and respond to opinions, recommendations and aspirations of children, or failing to establish a representative body for the voices and aspirations of children as prescribed by law;

  d) Failing to disclose or to accurately disclose information on study and training plans, care regimes and contributions as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for stigmatizing or discriminating against children due to their personal characteristics, family circumstances, gender, ethnicity, nationality, belief or religion.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced correction of false or misleading information in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 29. Violations against regulations on support and intervention for children in special circumstances, children who are victims of abuse or children at risk of exploitation, violence, abandonment, and other forms of abuse

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for any one of the following acts, which shall not be examined for penal liability:

  a) Failing to notify or provide information about children at risk of exploitation, violence and other forms of abuse to competent authorities or individuals;

  b) Obstructing the provision of information about children at risk of exploitation, violence and other forms of abuse to competent authorities or individuals.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to provide or concealing information about a child victim of abuse for competent authorities or individuals without facing criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to notify the place where information on child abuse is received;

  c) Obstructing the provision of information about a child victim of abuse to competent authorities or individuals;

  d) Failing to provide information and cooperate in conducting verification of the truthfulness of the abuse, the state of insecurity, and the extent of potential harm to children when requested by competent authorities or individuals;

  dd) Failing to maintain confidentiality of information, or failing to report or denounce acts of child abuse.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to adequately and promptly provide support or intervention in cases where children are harmed or at risk of violence, exploitation, abandonment, or have special circumstances;

  b) Refusing to provide support, intervention, or alternative care in cases where children are harmed or at risk of violence, exploitation, abandonment, or have special circumstances.

  Article 30. Violations against regulations on prohibiting sale of addictive substances, other stimulants, unsafe foods, or foods harmful to children; violations against regulations on providing safe and child-friendly products and services without criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Providing unsafe products or services that cause harm to children;

  b) Selling addictive substances, other stimulants, unsafe foods, or foods harmful to children;

  c) Allowing children to use addictive substances, other stimulants, unsafe foods, or foods harmful to children.

  2. Additional penalties:

  a) Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations for violations specified in clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Suspend part or all of business activities related to violations for 1 to 3 months for violations specified in point b clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced destruction of goods and items for violations specified in clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children for violations specified in clause 1 of this Article that cause injury or damage to children’s health but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced return of an amount equal to the value of exhibits of administrative violations that have been illegally sold, dispersed or destroyed for violations specified in clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 31. Violations against regulations on provision of cultural products, information and communication services that affect healthy development of children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Providing internet services and other services with contents affecting healthy development of children;

  b) Failing to note that children are not allowed to use on publications, products, toys if publications, products, toys have contents inappropriate for children; failing to warn about contents inappropriate for children in digital content products on cyberspace;

  c) Failing to inform about age of children who are not allowed to watch, listen to radio programs, television programs, art, cinema and performances if there are contents inappropriate for children;

  d) Failing to comply with requirements for recording information on publications for children.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for one of the acts of producing, publishing, copying, circulating, operating, distributing, owning, transporting, storing, trading in publications, toys, games, digital content on cyberspace and other products serving children but having contents affecting healthy development of children, by one of the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed when the violation involves 01 - 10 products;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed when the violation involves 10 - 50 products;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed when the violation involves 50 - 100 products;

  d) VND 15,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 when from 100 products to less than 200 products are illegally exported;

  dd) VND 20,000,000 - VND 30,000,000 for a violation involving from 200 products to under 500 products;

  e) VND 30,000,000 - VND 40,000,000 when the violation involves from 500 products to less than 1,000 products;

  g) VND 40,000,000 - VND 50,000,000 for a violation involving ≥ 1,000 products.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to issue or issuing inappropriate warnings about children’s content on radio, television programs, press and publications.

  4. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations for violations prescribed in Points b and d Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced removal of articles from online newspapers, magazines, radio and television, and electronic publications, for violations specified in Points b and d Clause 1 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced recall and destruction of products, publications, toys, printed newspapers, and other electronic publications, for violations specified in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Enforced apology upon request, for violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  d) Enforced payment of an amount equal to the value of exhibits of administrative violations that have been unlawfully sold, dispersed or destroyed, for violations specified in Points b and d Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 32. Violations against regulations on prohibiting disclosure of information about private life and personal secrets of children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for disclosing or publicizing any of the following information without consent of parents, caregivers, guardians and children aged 7 years or older, including: name, age; personal identification characteristics; health conditions and private life recorded in medical records; personal images; information about family members, caregivers; personal property; phone number; personal postal address; address, information about place of residence, native place; address, information about school, class, study results and friends; information about services provided for children.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology upon request, for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced removal, deletion or removal of information on private life and personal secrets of children in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 33. Violations against regulations on prevention of abuse of child adoption to harm children; abuse of benefits and policies of the State and assistance provided by organizations and individuals for children for self-seeking purposes

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations which, however, is not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) Taking advantage of child adoption to cause physical, emotional, psychological, honorable or dignity-related harms to children in the forms of violence, exploitation, abandonment, neglect and other forms of harm;

  b) Taking advantage of benefits and policies of the State and assistance provided by organizations and individuals for children for self-seeking purposes.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) The entity committing the violation in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to pay all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by children if such violation causes bodily injury, emotional distress, psychological damage, or loss of honor or dignity to children but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  b) The violating entity is compelled to return the illegal profits earned from committing the violation in Point b Clause 1 of this Article.

  c) Enforced termination of substitute care or change to appropriate substitute care for the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 34. Violations against regulations on construction of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods storage facilities with fire and explosion risks near child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, children’s entertainment facilities, or vice versa

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Building works or installing equipment of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods storage facilities with fire and explosion risks near child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, children’s entertainment facilities;

  b) Building works or installing equipment of child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, children’s entertainment facilities near service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods storage facilities with fire and explosion risks.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Building service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods storage facilities with fire and explosion risks near child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, children’s entertainment facilities;

  b) Building child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, children’s entertainment facilities near service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods storage facilities with fire and explosion risks.

  3. Additional penalties:

  Suspend operation for a fixed period of 03 – 06 months if any of the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of works or equipment if the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article is committed;

  b) Enforced relocation of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods or goods with risk of fire or explosion, child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and entertainment venues if they are located outside the permissible area as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 35. Violations against regulations on child protection service providers and management and use of infrastructure for children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed upon a child protection service provider operating beyond the registered scope.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed upon a child protection service provider that operates without registration or with an unregistered scope.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Appropriating infrastructure serving learning, entertainment, recreation and child protection services;

  b) Using infrastructure serving learning, entertainment, recreation and child protection services for purposes other than those prescribed by law or against regulations of law.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory dismantling of works and equipment if the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 3 of this Article is committed;

  b) Mandatory restoration of original condition if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 3 of this Article is committed.

  Article 36. Violations against regulations on failure to ensure safety leading to accidents or injuries to children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for committing any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to inform or warn of dangerous, toxic, flammable, explosive, electric shock, fall, and other risks leading to accidents or injuries for children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to fully and promptly comply with safety measures for manufacturing, trading, and transporting means of transport leading to accidents or injuries for children but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory prompt implementation of safety measures in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by children in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article that causes injury or damage to their health but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 37. Violations against regulations on responsibility for protecting children online

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations while conducting business or providing services online:

  a) Providing or sharing information or services harmful to children, or information or services that are fake, toxic, pornographic, violent, suicide incitement, distorted, threatening, or infringing upon honor, dignity, or legitimate rights and interests of children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to issue warnings or remove information or services harmful to children, or information or services that are fake, toxic, pornographic, violent, suicidal, distorted, threatening, or infringing upon the honor, dignity, or legitimate rights and interests of children, but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Failing to implement measures and tools to ensure the safety of information on private life secrets of children; failing to send warning messages about risks when children provide or change their private life secrets;

  d) Failing to comply with requests for removal or deletion of information on private life secrets of children when such requests are made by parents, caregivers, guardians, or competent agencies, organizations, or individuals responsible for child protection;

  dd) Failing to organize the receipt of information and assessment and classification of the level of safety for children sent by agencies, organizations, individuals, and children;

  e) Failing to publish a list of online information networks, services, and products classified by their level of safety for children;

  g) Failing to detect and remove images, documents, and information inappropriate for children or affecting their healthy development;

  h) Failing to provide guidance on the use of information technology services and devices to protect children;

  i) Failing to implement measures to protect children who access information or use services.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on online video game service providers that fail to install tools for controlling time and protecting children from excessive or addictive use of video games. Online video game service providers that fail to implement measures to limit playing time of G1 games for children shall incur administrative penalties in accordance with the Government’s Decree on administrative penalties for violations against regulations on postal services, telecommunications, radio frequencies, information technology and electronic transactions.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory return of illegal profits obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by children in case of committing the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article that causes injury or damage to their health but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  c) Mandatory removal of information and services harmful to children; removal of counterfeit and infringing services and applications that violate the lawful rights and interests of children on the internet (including data deletion, application removal from servers or application stores; domain name closure or other technical measures to prevent users from accessing them) in case of committing the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Mandatory deletion or removal of personal privacy information of children in case of committing the violation prescribed in Point d Clause 1 of this Article.

  Chapter IV

                                                               IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 47. Entry into force

  1. This Decree comes into force from May 16, 2026.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 130/2021/ND-CP dated December 30, 2021 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  Article 48. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Minister of Health shall provide guidelines, inspect, expedite and organize implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities, relevant organizations and individuals shall implement this Decree.
