                                                   DECREE

     ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY; IMPLEMENTATION OF EXEMPTIONS AND PROTECTION FROM PENALTIES; NATIONAL RESERVATION; STATE DEPOSIT

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015; and the Law on Actions against administrative violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Law on Management and Use of Public Property dated June 21, 2017;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Thrift Practice and Anti-Wasteness dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Law on National Reserves dated November 20, 2012;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates a Decree prescribing penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and anti-wasteness; national reserves; state treasury.

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, remedial measures, power to record administrative violations and power to impose penalties for administrative violations in the following fields:

  a) Management and use of public property at agencies, organizations and units; state-owned project property; property under establishment of public ownership and infrastructure property invested and managed by the State;

  b) Thrift practice and wastefulness combat;

  c) National reserve;

  d) State treasury.

  2. Administrative violations related to management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury in terms of accounting, construction, investment planning, valuation, auction and other regulations not specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be sanctioned according to relevant laws.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrift practice against wastefulness; national reserve; state treasury.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies;

  b) Units of the people’s armed forces;

  c) Public service providers;

  d) Agencies of the Communist Party of Vietnam;

  dd) Socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, and other organizations established under the law on associations;

  e) Other relevant enterprises, organizations, and individuals.

  3. Persons having power to impose penalties and organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree.

  4. In case officials or public employees commit violations while performing their official duties, if such violations are within their assigned official duties prescribed in legislative documents or administrative documents issued by competent authorities or persons, they shall not be administratively sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree but shall be handled according to regulations of law on officials, public employees.

  State agencies committing violations within their assigned state management tasks shall not be administratively sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree but shall be handled according to relevant laws.

  Article 3. Prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties

  The prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury prescribed in this Decree is 01 year. Regarding administrative violations involving public property being houses, land and infrastructure assets invested and managed by the State, the prescriptive period is 02 years.

  Article 4. Penalties and remedial measures

  1. Principal penalties:

  a) Principal penalties prescribed in this Decree include: Warnings and fines;

  b) The maximum fine for violations against regulations on management and use of public property, national reserves or state treasury shall be VND 50,000,000 if the violation is committed by an individual or VND 100,000,000 if it is committed by an organization; the maximum fine for violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat shall be VND 100,000,000 if the violation is committed by an individual or VND 200,000,000 if it is committed by an organization.

  2. Additional penalties:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violator may also be subject to the additional penalty of confiscation of exhibits and/or instrumentalities used for committing the administrative violation.

  3. Remedial measures in respect of violations against regulations on management and use of public property:

  Depending on the nature, severity and extent of the violation, the violator may also be subject to one or several remedial measures as follows:

  a) Mandatory payment of an amount equivalent to the value of the property;

  b) Mandatory payment of an amount incurred due to commission of administrative violation;

  c) Mandatory return of the property; in case where the property has been altered due to the administrative violation, the original condition of the property must be restored; in case where the original condition of the property cannot be restored, the violator shall pay with money or other property having similar functions and use value to the original property;

  d) Enforced demolition of construction works built on encroached areas of working offices or public service facilities;

  dd) Enforced cancellation of false or fraudulent declarations in case of commission of the violation against regulations on use of data on public property;

  e) Enforced adjustment and supplementation of data, information, and reports on additional declaration of public property.

  4. Remedies in the field of thrift practice and wastefulness combat:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, violating organizations or individuals may be subject to one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Revoke and transfer to state budget funds used for purposes other than those prescribed by competent authorities if funds used for purposes other than those prescribed by competent authorities are used;

  b) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by using funds originating from state budget to state budget if such funds are used inconsistently with operating regulations and financial mechanisms of the funds issued by competent authorities.

  5. Remedial measures in the field of national reserves:

  Depending on the nature and severity of violations, violating organizations or individuals may also be subject to one or several remedial measures as follows:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing violations against regulations on purchase, sale, preservation, distribution, and assistance; violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods; violations against regulations on management of capital and fees in national reserve activities; violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, and exploitation of assets under national reserve;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state of facilities, physical and technical equipment, and storage of national reserve goods;

  c) Enforced return of damaged national reserve goods caused by acts of infringement or destruction; violations against regulations on distribution and assistance of national reserve goods;

  d) Enforced recall of national reserve goods that have been issued or distributed inconsistently with regulations;

  dd) Enforced restoration of the original state of assets if the violation involves illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, or exploitation of assets that are storage facilities for national reserve goods.

  6. Remedial measures in the field of state treasury:

  Depending on the nature and severity of violations, violating organizations may also be subject to one or several remedial measures as follows:

  a) Enforced revocation of payments made for the unfinished work; unfinished work not eligible for payment and paid amounts exceeding the contract value or contract appendix;

  b) Enforced revocation of payments made for improper documents and improper payments made from improper documents compared to original documents at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced revocation of all payments made from fake documents used for state budget payments but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Enforced completion of documents as per the law in case of violations against regulations on payment of state budget expenditures;

  dd) Enforced commitment to advance payments before requesting State Treasury to pay state budget or enforced re-commitment in case of violations against procedures for controlling commitment payments;

  e) Enforced procedures for advance payments in case of violations against procedures and time limit for advance payments of state budget;

  g) Enforced freezing or closing accounts in case of violations against procedures for registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury; enforced freezing of accounts in case of making fake documents to register the use of accounts at the State Treasury but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  7. Organizations and individuals subject to administrative penalties under this Decree are not allowed to use state budget money or money originating from the state budget for paying fines and remedying consequences caused by their violations. In case of sanctioning organizations, after serving the sanctioning decisions, sanctioned organizations shall identify individuals at fault for committing administrative violations in order to determine legal responsibilities, including the return of fines and remedying consequences caused by their violations corresponding to the degree of violation of such individuals according to the provisions of law and Regulations on management and use of public property of the organizations.

  Article 5. Application of fine levels

  The fine levels prescribed in Section 1, Section 2, Section 3 and Section 4 Chapter II, Section 1 Chapter III, Section 1 Chapter IV, Section 1 and Section 2 Chapter V of this Decree are the fine levels prescribed for administrative violations committed by organizations; for the same administrative violation, the fine level imposed on an individual is half of that imposed on an organization (except for the provisions of Articles 17, 23 and 27 of this Decree).

  Chapter II

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, SANCTIONING FORMS, LEVELS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS AND SANCTION LEVELS IN MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY AT AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND EMPLOYEES

  Article 6. Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property

  1. To impose a fine on the act of investing or procuring property without decision of competent agencies or persons, with the following fine levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing in or purchasing assets that are machinery, equipment and other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles) with a total value of less than VND 100.000.000 at one time;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing in or purchasing assets that are machinery, equipment and other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles) with a total value of VND 100.000.000 or more at one time;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing in or purchasing assets that are office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to make centralized procurement of assets on the list of assets subject to centralized procurement as prescribed by laws on management and use of public assets.

  3. A fine shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets exceeding the limits prescribed by competent authorities as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing in or purchasing assets valued at less than VND 50.000.000 per asset unit;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing in or purchasing assets valued at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000 per asset unit;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing in or purchasing assets valued at VND 100.000.000 per asset unit or more.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of an amount corresponding to the value of assets invested or purchased in excess of the prescribed standards and norms for the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the value exceeding the prescribed standards and norms as a basis for sanctioning is prescribed as follows:

  a) In case of investing or purchasing assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment and other assets in excess of the prescribed quantity: The excess value per unit of asset is determined by the unit price of such asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or contract for asset purchase;

  b) In case of investing or purchasing assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment and other assets in excess of the prescribed quantity but at a price higher than the prescribed price: The excess value is determined by the value of the asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or contract for asset purchase minus (-) the value of the asset according to the prescribed standards and norms stipulated by competent state agencies;

  c) In case of investing or purchasing working offices or public service facilities in excess of the prescribed standards and norms: The excess value is determined by the difference between the actual area minus (-) the area used according to the prescribed standards and norms multiplied by (x) the average purchase unit price according to the invoice (or contract for asset purchase);

  d) Persons having sanctioning competence prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as a basis for sanctioning according to the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 7. Administrative violations against regulations on leasing assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on leasing property in case value of lease contract is less than VND 100.000.000 in the following cases:

  a) Committing act of leasing property without decision on leasing property issued by competent authority or person;

  b) Committing act of leasing property beyond standards and norms prescribed by competent authority;

  c) Committing act of selecting service providers for property lease not in accordance with form and procedures prescribed by laws on management and use of public property and relevant laws.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on leasing property prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article in case value of lease contract is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory return of amount leased in excess of standards and norms if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  4. Value of lease contracts serving as basis for imposing penalties shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case the property lease contract specifies the contract value, the basis for sanction shall be the contract value specified in the contract;

  b) In case of leasing property without making a contract or with making a contract but not having enough information to determine the value as prescribed in Clause a of this Point, the value used as the basis for sanction shall be determined by the leased value of property of the same type or with equivalent standards and techniques on the market at the time of committing the violation and (x) the duration of leasing property from the beginning of leasing to the time of issuing the decision on administrative sanction.

  The person competent to impose sanctions prescribed in Article 29, Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the value used as the basis for sanction according to the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 8. Administrative violations against regulations on allocation and use of public property, destruction or intentional damage to public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of allocating public property beyond the standards and norms issued by competent agencies or persons (incorrect subjects, area, quantity, price), subject to the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the allocated and used property is valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the allocated and used property is valued at from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case the allocated and used property is valued at VND 100.000.000 or more.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of misusing or using public property for improper purposes (using public property for purposes or uses other than those intended; using working offices or public service facilities as residential houses or for personal use; using cars to transport from residence to workplace for unqualified positions; using cars for business trips by unqualified positions without approval by competent authorities; using cars for personal use; using machinery, equipment, or other property for personal use) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of using property that is machinery, equipment, or other property (excluding working offices, public service facilities, and cars) with an original cost recorded in accounting books of less than VND 100.000.000 per unit of property (hereinafter referred to as “property valued at less than VND 100.000.000”);

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of using property that is machinery, equipment, or other property (excluding working offices, public service facilities, and cars) with an original cost recorded in accounting books of VND 100.000.000 or more per unit of property (hereinafter referred to as “property valued at VND 100.000.000 or more”);

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of using property that is working offices, public service facilities, or cars.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of destroying or intentionally damaging public property if not liable to criminal prosecution:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging property that is machinery, equipment, or other property with an original cost recorded in accounting books of less than VND 100.000.000 per unit of property;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging property that is machinery, equipment, or other property with an original cost recorded in accounting books of VND 100.000.000 or more per unit of property;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentional damage to property that is working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) The violating entity is compelled to return an amount equal to the value of property in excess of the prescribed limits and norms in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) The violating entity is compelled to restore the original condition of the property altered by the administrative violation; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, it shall be returned as money or property with similar functions and use value to the original property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the value in excess of the prescribed limits and norms:

  a) In case of allocation or use of property that is cars, machinery, equipment or other property in excess of the prescribed quantity: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the excess quantity by the book value of the property; in case the book value of the property does not record the book value, the purchase price of the new property of the same type or one that has equivalent technical standards and uses on the market at the time of commission of the violation shall be used;

  b) In case of allocation or use of property that is cars, machinery, equipment or other property in excess of the prescribed quantity but in excess of the prescribed price: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the excess quantity by (the difference between the book value of the property minus (-) the value of the property according to the prescribed limits and norms prescribed by a competent authority);

  c) In case of property that is working offices or public service facilities: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the actual area minus (-) the area used according to the prescribed limits and norms by the average unit price recorded in the accounting books; in case the book value does not record the unit price, the unit price shall be determined according to the investment rate of houses or works with equivalent technical standards issued by the Ministry of Construction applicable at the time of commission of the violation;

  d) Persons having authority to impose penalties specified in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the value exceeding as the basis for imposing penalties according to regulations of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 9. Administrative violations against regulations on lending public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of lending or using public property in contravention of regulations (lending public property to other organizations or individuals regardless of whether there is a loan contract or not, regardless of the loan term) with the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the lent property is valued at less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the lent property is valued at VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed in case the lent property is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Organizations committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of borrowed property. In case where the property has been changed due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original state; in case where the original state cannot be restored, it must be returned in cash or property with similar functions and use value to the original property;

  b) Enforced payment to the state budget of an amount corresponding to the rent of property during the lending period. The determination of the rent of property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 10. Administrative violations against regulations on exchange and gifting of public property inconsistently with regulations

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of exchanging public property inconsistently with regulations (using public property of one organization to exchange for property of another organization or individual without permission from a competent authority or person) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property used for exchange is valued at less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property used for exchange is valued at VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property used for exchange is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of gifting public property inconsistently with regulations (using public property as gifts in violation of the Regulation on gift-giving issued by a competent authority).

  3. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory return of property or monetary payment equivalent to the value of property exchanged or donated in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 11. Administrative violations against regulations on encroachment upon working offices or public service facilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for encroaching upon a working office or public service facility (an organization/individual that is using adjacent land and has moved the boundary of a land parcel to expand the land area or built an encroaching work into the land area of the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit; or has used a house or land belonging to the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit without permission).

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Organizations and individuals that commit the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state of the office building or public service facility and return of the office building or public service facility that is encroached upon;

  b) Enforced demolition of the construction work on the encroached area;

  c) Enforced payment to the state budget of an amount corresponding to the rent paid for the property during the period of encroachment. The determination of the rent paid for the property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 12. Acts of appropriating public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of appropriating public property which is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability (holding or using public property without permission of competent state agencies) in the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the value of public property of less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the value of public property of VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of appropriating office buildings, public service facilities, or cars.

  2. Additional penalties: Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures: Organizations and individuals committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original state of property altered by the administrative violation in order to return it to its original condition; in case of failure to restore the original state of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value to the original property is required;

  b) Mandatory payment to the state budget of an amount corresponding to the rent of property during the period of appropriation. The determination of the rent of property as a basis for penalty imposition shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 13. Administrative violations against regulations on using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture, or association

  1. A fine shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture, or association without a decision approving the Scheme issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with the law on management and use of public property at the time of commission of the violation, and imposed in the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) VND 15,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the assets are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on determination of value of assets for business purposes, lease, joint venture or association purposes:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to have a prescribed number of members in a Council for valuation or a Council for appraisal of property prices;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to follow procedures for selecting a valuation organization for determining the value of assets used for business purposes, lease, joint venture or association purposes.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes against the approved scheme by competent authorities or persons, thereby affecting the performance of functions and tasks assigned by the State:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if the assets are valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of using property being automobiles or property assessed at ≥ VND 100,000,000;

  c) VND 15,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the assets are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of original conditions of property altered by the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article; in case of failure to restore original conditions of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value shall be provided;

  b) Enforced transfer of illegal benefits obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Administrative violations against regulations on failure to maintain or repair property as per the law

  1. Organizations assigned maintenance or repair estimates by competent authorities as per the law but failing to maintain or repair property resulting in damage shall incur the following penalties:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 if the value of damaged property is less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of damaged property is from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 if the value of damaged property is VND 100.000.000 or more and the property is an office building, public service facility, or car and not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Determination of the amount to be refunded corresponding to the value of damaged assets is prescribed as follows:

  a) For irreparable damage, the refundable amount shall be determined by multiplying the purchase price of a new asset of the same type or with equivalent standards and use by the market price multiplied by the remaining quality percentage at the time of valuation;

  b) For reparable damage, the refundable amount shall be the cost of repair of such asset;

  c) The head of the organization shall assign the financial agency under its management to act as the focal point for determining the refundable amount; on the basis of which, the head of the organization shall decide the refundable amount and take responsibility for his/her decision.

  Article 15. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare plans for disposition of public property in accordance with law;

  b) Declaring and preparing plans for disposition of public property beyond the prescribed time limit;

  c) Failing to report to competent state authorities for repossession, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of assets when conditions are met in accordance with law (except where public assets have expired according to law regulations but remain usable and agencies, organizations, units or enterprises still wish to use them, in which case heads of agencies, organizations, units shall decide on appropriate disposal methods).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on an organization for damaging or losing assets pending disposal.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for handling assets without a decision of a competent authority or person:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 in case of selling, transferring, liquidating or destroying assets valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the property is worth VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 in case of selling, transferring or liquidating assets being working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. The following penalties shall be imposed for declaring incorrect information about the list of assets and their current conditions (which may lead to a decision on handling of assets against regulations):

  a) A warning shall be imposed in case of declaring assets valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring assets worth VND 50.000.000 - VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring assets worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring assets that are working offices, public service facilities, or cars.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) The violator is compelled to restore the original condition of the asset that has been changed by the violation prescribed in Clause 2 or Clause 3 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the asset, the violator must refund the money or return an asset with similar functions and use value to the original one;

  b) The violator is compelled to refund an amount equivalent to the value of the lost or damaged asset in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  6. Determination of the refundable amount equivalent to the value of the lost or damaged asset prescribed in Point b Clause 5 of this Article:

  a) In case the lost or damaged asset cannot be repaired, the refundable amount shall be determined based on the purchase price of a new asset of the same type or standard with similar functions and use value on the market at the time when the violation occurs.

  Market prices may be based on quotes of suppliers posted or announced on the market or from official information disclosed by suppliers via the Internet.

  b) For damaged assets that can be repaired, the amount to be refunded is the repair cost.

  Article 16. Administrative violations against regulations on organization of handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to implement the disposal plan approved by a competent authority or person after the prescribed time limit has expired.

  The expiration date shall be determined according to the disposal time limit specified in the decision on disposal of property. In case the disposal time limit is not specified in the decision on disposal of property, relevant laws shall apply. In case the disposal plan is approved by a competent authority to extend the disposal time limit, the new time limit shall be determined according to the extended time limit.

  b) Failure to hand over property subject to a transfer or recovery decision to the receiving agency within the prescribed time limit.

  a) Failure to purchase national reserves when all conditions have been met according to decisions of competent authorities;

  b) Trading national reserve goods without written consent of competent authority;

  c) Trading national reserve goods when trading deadline expires;

  d) Trading national reserve goods in quantities, categories, specifications, standards or quality not approved by competent authority;

  dd) Failure to comply with regulations on trading methods for each type of national reserve goods issued by competent authority.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for arbitrarily changing prices of national reserve goods for self-seeking purposes.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory return of illegal revenues generated by committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 41. Violations against regulations on management of national reserve goods

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to open books for monitoring quality, quantity and categories of national reserve goods during preservation;

  b) Failing to submit periodic or ad hoc reports on preservation of national reserve goods.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for the violation involving the transfer of national reserve goods:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for committing the violation involving failure to comply with regulations of competent authorities on transfer of national reserve goods;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to comply with regulations of competent authorities on transfer of national reserve goods.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recall of transferred national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  Article 42. Violations against regulations on preservation of national reserve goods

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for failure to comply with national technical regulations or temporary technical requirements for preservation of national reserve goods issued by competent authorities; failure to comply with regulations on time limit for preservation of national reserve goods; or failure to preserve national reserve goods at locations specified by competent authorities:

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if damage is caused and the value of the damaged national reserve goods is less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if damage is caused and the value of the damaged national reserve goods is from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  2. Warnings or fines shall be imposed for obstruction of storage, preservation and circulation of national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if damage is caused and the value of the damaged national reserve goods is less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for hiring an organization or individual ineligible to preserve national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100,000,000.

  4. Remedial measures:

  The violating entity is compelled to return the illegal profit earned from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 through 3 of this Article.

  Article 43. Acts of infringing upon or destroying material-technical facilities and storage of national reserve goods which are not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for illegally entering a material-technical facility or storage of national reserve goods.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for destroying a material-technical facility or storage of national reserve goods.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for acts of infringing upon or destroying national reserve goods:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused to national reserve goods;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if the damage caused to national reserve goods is assessed at < VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if the damage caused to national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  4. Additional penalties

  Confiscation of exhibits and equipment used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of the material and technical facilities or the storage of national reserve goods in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory return of damaged national reserve goods in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 44. Violations against regulations on distribution and provision of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of the distribution or provision of national reserve goods or performance of other tasks related to national reserve goods by the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if the damage caused by the violation is caused by the national reserve goods with a value of less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if the damage caused by the violation is caused by the national reserve goods with a value of from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to follow procedures for distribution or provision of national reserve goods with a value of less than VND 100,000,000 within the prescribed time limit.

  3. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for using national reserve goods for wrong purposes; distributing or providing national reserve goods to wrong entities according to decisions of competent authorities; distributing or providing national reserve goods not satisfying requirements regarding categories, quantity, quality, standards, and specifications by the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for damage caused by national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for damage caused by national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for loss of national reserve goods used for distribution, relief or performance of other tasks:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of national reserve goods is assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to distribute national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100.000.000 to the right entities according to decisions issued by competent authorities.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for delayed or postponed distribution or provision of national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100.000.000 as prescribed by competent authorities.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for delayed or postponed distribution of national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100.000.000 as prescribed by competent authorities, causing damage to national reserve goods.

  8. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of exhibits and vehicles used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory return of illegal profits obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clauses 3, 5 and 6 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory refund of national reserve goods that are lost, improperly used or issued to wrong recipients, applicable to the violations prescribed in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  c) Mandatory refund of national reserve goods that are damaged, of poor quality or insufficient quantity, applicable to the violation prescribed in Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 45. Violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods

  1. Warnings or fines shall be imposed for obstruction of the import, export and transport of national reserve goods.

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if the loss incurred is caused by the shipment of national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if the loss incurred is caused by the shipment of national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000;

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to comply with procedures for entry and exit of national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100,000,000 as prescribed;

  b) Delivery of goods not in conformity with quantity, category, specifications, standards, or quality requirements of national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100,000,000 as decided by competent authorities.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with decisions of competent authorities on type, specifications, quantity, quality, time, or place of entry or exit of national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100,000,000.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to obtain decision of competent authorities when entering or exiting national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100,000,000.

  5. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of exhibits and equipment used for committing the violations in Clause 1 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory return of illegal profits earned from committing the administrative violations prescribed in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory recall of national reserve goods that have been issued or allocated against regulations if the violation prescribed in Clause 2, 3 or 4 of this Article is committed.

  Article 46. Violations against regulations on receipt of national reserve goods but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Receiving national reserve goods at a location or within a time limit other than those decided by a competent authority;

  b) Failing to implement or improperly implementing regulations on procedures and order for receipt of national reserve goods.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to receive national reserve goods (national reserve goods exported for aid or for performance of other tasks by competent authorities) when all conditions have been fully met in accordance with regulations of competent authorities.

  Article 47. Illegal intervention in national reserve activities

  1. Warnings or fines shall be imposed for illegal intervention in national reserve activities as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 48. Violations against regulations on management of capital and charges in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to submit reports on use of capital from national reserve funds for purchase of national reserve products as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Payment made without a contract for purchase of national reserve products;

  b) Payment made when the quantity of national reserve products is incorrect or payment made without sufficient invoices and documents;

  c) Payment made without a record of contract liquidation or a list of documents recorded in the warehouse with signature of the unit head in case of direct purchase without bidding;

  d) Payment made without approval of the head of national reserve unit.

  dd) Using fees for import, export and preservation of national reserve goods in contravention of prescribed contents or exceeding prescribed limits.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Using national reserve funds for purposes other than those prescribed;

  b) Failing to comply with regulations on management of funds derived from national reserve funds;

  c) Failing to remit the remaining amount after having completely purchased national reserve goods as prescribed by competent authorities.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory remittance of illegal profits earned from committing the violations specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 49. Violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease and exploitation of assets included in national reserves

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets included in national reserve (except from storage of national reserve):

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the assets is less than VND 70.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the assets is from VND 70.000.000 to less than VND 150.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the assets is from VND 150.000.000 to less than VND 300.000.000;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the assets is from VND 300.000.000 to less than VND 500.000.000;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the assets is ≥ VND 500.000.000.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of storage of national reserve:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the storage of national reserve is less than VND 500.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed if the national reserve store is worth VND 500.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) The violator is compelled to restore the property to its original condition in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The violating entity is compelled to return illegal benefits obtained from the violation specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. POWERS TO MAKE RECORDS AND IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON NATIONAL RESERVE STOCK

  Article 50. Powers to make records and impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on national reserve stock

  1. Persons having the power to make records of administrative violations against regulations on national reserve stock include:

  a) Persons having the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on national reserve stock prescribed in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree;

  b) Officials assigned to inspect compliance with laws on national reserves.

  2. Persons competent to issue records as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall be responsible for issuing sanction decisions within their competence or transferring to competent authorities specified in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree to issue sanction decisions.

  Article 51. Sanctioning competence of Chairpersons of People’s Committees

  1. Chairpersons of district-level People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose fines of up to VND 50.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine level prescribed at Point b of this Clause;

  d) Apply remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Enforce remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 52. Power to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Inspectors and persons assigned to conduct specialized inspections of national reserves while on duty have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violation worth less than the fine specified in Point b this Clause.

  2. Chief Inspectors of provincial Departments, heads of inspectorates at provincial level performing specialized inspections in the field of national reserves, heads of specialized inspection teams established by the Director General of State Reserves shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violation worth less than the fine specified in Point b this Clause;

  d) Enforce remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 hereof.

  3. Heads of specialized inspection teams at ministerial level performing specialized inspections in the field of national reserves shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. Chief Inspector of the Ministry has the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 53. Authority to impose penalties of Director of Regional State Reserve Department, Director General of State Reserve Administration

  1. Director of Regional State Reserve Department shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Director General of State Reserve Administration shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter V

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR DAMAGE CAUSED BY ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE STATE ARMED FORCES SECTOR

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE SPENDING IN THE STATE BUDGETS

  Article 54. Administrative violations against regulations on state spending in the state budget allocated by competent authorities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for contents or tasks not included in the estimates approved by competent authorities;

  b) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for expenditures exceeding the annual funding estimates or plans assigned by competent authorities or exceeding the estimated expenditures approved (for tasks not performed under contracts);

  c) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for expenditures not conformable with the estimates assigned by competent authorities (expenditures not conformable with the estimated funding sources; expenditures not for the right purposes or for the right entities or contents as per the estimates); expenditures not conformable with the investment funding sources, expenditures not conformable with the list of projects approved by competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for the workload that has not been performed;

  b) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for the workload that is being performed but not yet completed or eligible for payment;

  c) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment exceeding the value of the contract or appendix of the contract.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of payments made for the unfinished work; unfinished work not eligible for payment and paid amounts exceeding the contract value or appendix according to Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 55. Acts of making documents and records against regulations and making documents and records different from original ones at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for signing a contract before obtaining capital plans approved by competent authorities (except for construction works under emergency orders).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Making documents and records sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenditures not in compliance with title and eligible entities;

  b) Making documents and records sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenditures exceeding the limits of quantity and value;

  c) Making documents and records sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenditures against regulations (expenditures not meeting conditions and rules of expenditure regime).

  In case an organization commits the act of purchasing or leasing state assets beyond the standards and norms prescribed by competent state agencies, it shall be sanctioned in accordance with Article 5 and Article 6 of this Decree.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of preparing documents and records sent to the State Treasury which are incorrect with respect to the contents on the original documents, invoices and records at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of funds for expenses incurred due to preparation of false documents and records as prescribed, and expenses incurred due to preparation of false documents and records which are incorrect with respect to the contents on the original documents, invoices and records at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 56. Acts of preparing false documents and records for payment of state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of preparing false documents and records sent to the State Treasury for payment of regular expenses, professional expenses of a recurring nature, national target programs, or target programs using professional funding (excluding expenses for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of material facilities funded by regular state budget expenditures and retained fees under regulations for regular expenditures with total investment exceeding VND 500.000.000).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of preparing false documents and records sent to the State Treasury for payment of investment capital funded by state budget and investment capital from state budget for implementation of target programs or expenses for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of material facilities funded by regular state budget expenditures and retained fees under regulations for regular expenditures with total investment exceeding VND 500.000.000.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recovery of all amounts paid using forged documents and records for state budget expenditures, if not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article.

  Article 57. Violations against regulations on payment of state budget expenditures

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records requesting State Treasury to transfer payments to wrong names or account numbers of beneficiary units specified in contracts or contract appendices.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records requesting payment of state budget expenditures inconsistently with payment terms specified in contracts or contract appendices between the unit in charge of cost estimation or investor and suppliers of goods or services, including one of the following violations:

  a) Incorrect contract value;

  b) Incorrect payment term;

  c) Incorrect payment method;

  d) Incorrect payment percentage (including advance payment);

  dd) Incorrect payment terms specified in the contract and its appendices.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced completion of documents and records as prescribed for the violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 58. Violations against procedures for control of expenditure commitments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit expenditure commitments to the State Treasury for expenditures subject to commitment requirements as prescribed;

  b) Submitting requests for expenditure commitments to the State Treasury beyond the prescribed deadline for expenditures subject to commitment requirements as prescribed;

  c) Submitting requests for expenditure commitments exceeding the annual investment capital plan, the balance of the approved annual budget or the value of contracts for which expenditure commitments are permitted.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced adoption of commitment procedures before requesting the State Treasury to pay or reimburse the state budget for the acts specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced adoption of commitment procedures for the acts specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 59. Violations against regulations on procedures and time limit for advance payment of state budget

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Adopting procedures for advance payment after the final deadline for advance payment for regular expenditures without contracts for goods or service purchase or without sending contracts for goods or service purchase to the State Treasury as prescribed;

  b) Failing to adopt procedures for advance payment for investment expenditures funded by the state budget and investment capital from the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrade and expansion of facilities from regular expenditures of the state budget and retained fees according to regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500.000.000 through multiple payments of completed volume under contracts.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to follow procedures for settling all advances for regular expenditures under contracts for sale of goods or services in accordance with regulations in the final settlement of the contract;

  b) Failure to follow procedures for settling all advances for investment expenditures from state budget capital and state budget capital for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of material facilities from regular expenditures of state budget capital and retained fees in accordance with regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500,000,000 when the payment value (including advances and settled volume) reaches 80% of the contract value;

  c) Failure to follow or follow procedures for settling advances for compensation, support and resettlement expenditures after the prescribed deadline for settling advances.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forcible completion of procedures for settling advances for the acts specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS AND FINES FOR REGISTRATION AND USE OF ACCOUNTS AT THE STATE BANK

  Article 60. Violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Bank

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for committing violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Bank.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Forcible freezing or closing of accounts for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 61. Acts of making fake dossiers and papers to carry out procedures for registration of account use at State Treasuries but not seriously enough to be examined for penal liability

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 shall be imposed on acts of making fake dossiers and papers to send to State Treasuries to carry out procedures for registration of account use.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Forcible freezing of accounts for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 3. COMPETENCE TO SANCTION ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF STATE TREASURY

  Article 62. Competence to make records of administrative violations in the field of State Treasury

  1. Persons having authority to make records of administrative violations in the field of state treasury include:

  a) Persons having authority to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of control of spending of state budget through state treasuries as prescribed in Article 63 and Article 64 of this Decree;

  b) Officials assigned to control spending of state budget through state treasuries.

  2. Persons having authority to make records as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall be responsible for issuing decisions on penalties within their jurisdiction or transferring to competent authorities as prescribed in Article 63 and Article 64 of this Decree to issue decisions on penalties.

  Article 63. Authority to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Chief Inspectors of Departments of Finance, heads of specialized inspectorate teams of Departments of Finance, heads of specialized inspectorate teams of State Treasuries established by Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and heads of specialized inspectorate teams of State Treasuries established by General Directors of State Treasuries have the right to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 50,000,000;

  b) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chief Inspectors of the Ministry of Finance have the power to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 100.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspectorate teams of the Ministry of Finance have the power to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 250.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 64. Power to impose penalties of Director General of State Treasury; Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and central-affiliated cities

  1. Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and central-affiliated cities have the power to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  b) Applying remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The Director of State Treasury has the right to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b) Applying remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter VI

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 65. Effect

  1. This Decree comes into force as of September 01, 2019.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury and the Government’s Decree No. 58/2015/ND-CP dated June 16, 2015 on amendments to the Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  Article 66. Transitional provisions

  For administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury committed before the effective date of this Decree but detected afterwards or being considered and handled, the provisions of this Decree on penalties shall apply if this Decree does not prescribe legal liability or impose lighter legal liability on violating individuals or organizations.

  Article 67. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Ministry of Finance shall provide guidelines for implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies, People’s Committees at all levels shall be responsible for providing guidelines for implementation and inspecting the imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury prescribed in this Decree.

  3. When detecting administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury, competent authorities of the State, organizations and individuals shall transfer the dossiers (if any) or notify them to persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations prescribed in this Decree for consideration and handling.

  Where administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property fall under cases requiring recovery of public property in accordance with the law on management and use of public property, the persons competent to impose administrative penalties prescribed in this Decree shall, after issuing decisions on imposition of administrative penalties, notify the competent state agencies in accordance with the law on management and use of public property to issue decisions on recovery of property as prescribed. The handling of recovered property shall comply with the law on management and use of public property.

  4. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels and relevant organizations and individuals are responsible for implementing this Decree./.
