                                                    DECREE

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- Administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of a number of social evils.

  Administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of a number of social evils prescribed in this Decree include administrative violations in circulation and trading of films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs; selling and leasing publications; cultural activities and cultural services at public places; advertising and writing, putting up signboards; prevention of prostitution, drug abuse and gambling.

  Article 2.- Combating administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of a number of social evils.

  All acts of violation in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of social evils must be detected in time and stopped immediately. The sanctioning must be conducted quickly and fairly; all consequences caused by administrative violations must be remedied according to law.

  Article 3.- Subjects to be handled for administrative violations.

  Individuals and organizations that commit acts of administrative violation in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of a number of social evils shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in this Decree.

  Heads of State agencies and organizations, State officials and employees who commit acts of violation prescribed in this Decree shall, apart from being administratively sanctioned, also be disciplined according to law provisions.

  Article 4.- Application of sanctioning forms and measures.

  1. When imposing a fine on an administrative violation, the specific fine level shall be the average level of the fine bracket prescribed for such violation; if the violation involves extenuating circumstances, the fine level may be reduced but not lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation involves aggravating circumstances, the fine level may be increased but not higher than the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  The extenuating and aggravating circumstances shall be applied according to the provisions of Article 7 and Article 8 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Additional penalties and other measures shall be applied in conjunction with primary penalties if this Decree provides for additional penalties and application of other measures to administrative violations in order to thoroughly handle violations, eliminate causes and conditions for continued violations and remedy all consequences caused by administrative violations.

  All films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, books, newspapers, pictures, calendars and other cultural products with indecent, pornographic or violent contents must be confiscated and destroyed.

  Article 5.- Handling of violations committed by persons competent to sanction administrative violations.

  + Those who have improper authority or impose sanctions beyond their competence shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, be disciplined or examined for penal liability; if material damage is caused, they must pay compensation according to law provisions.   Item 3: ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS OF SOCIAL ECONOMICS, FORMS AND LEVELS OF SANCTION

  Article 18.- Sanctioning acts of buying sex, selling sex, brothel keeping and prostitution brokerage.

  1. Persons who commit acts of buying sex, selling sex, brothel keeping and prostitution brokerage shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Article 4 of Decree No. 53-CP of June 28, 1994 of the Government.

  For persons who commit acts of buying and/or selling sex but not on a regular basis, the presidents of the commune-level People’s Committees of the localities where such persons reside shall consider and decide to apply the measure of education at the communes, wards or townships.

  2. For persons who have committed acts of regular prostitution and have been repeatedly educated by the local authorities and people but still refuse to reform themselves, the presidents of the commune-level People’s Committees of the localities where such persons reside shall compile dossiers for the presidents of the district-level People’s Committees to propose the presidents of the provincial-level People’s Committees to send them to medical treatment establishments; if they are minors, they shall be sent to reformatories.

  3. The procedures for applying the measure of education at communes, wards or townships, sending to reformatories or medical treatment establishments shall comply with the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations.

  4. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 for acts of prostitution brokerage but not on a regular basis which, however, is not serious enough to warrant examination for penal liability.

  5. A fine of between VND 1,500,000 and 5,000,000 for acts of prostitution brokerage on a regular basis which, however, is not serious enough to warrant examination for penal liability.

  6. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for acts of prostitution promotion which, however, is not serious enough to warrant examination for penal liability.

  Article 19.- Sanctioning acts of using, producing, storing, trading in and transporting narcotics.

  1. Persons who commit acts of using narcotics in all forms shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5 of Decree No. 53-CP of June 28, 1994 of the Government.

  For persons who have committed acts of drug use but not yet reached the level of addiction, the presidents of the commune-level People’s Committees of the localities where such persons reside shall consider and decide on the application of the measure of education at communes, wards or district towns.

  2. For drug addicts who have been educated by the local administration and people many times but still refuse to reform themselves, the presidents of the commune-level People’s Committees of the localities where such persons reside shall compile dossiers for the presidents of the district-level People’s Committees to propose the presidents of the provincial-level People’s Committees to send them to medical treatment establishments; if they are minors, they shall be sent to reformatories.

  3. The procedures for applying the measure of education at communes, wards or district towns, sending to reformatories or medical treatment establishments shall comply with the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations.

  4. Persons who illegally produce, store, trade in or transport narcotics but not yet liable to criminal prosecution shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels prescribed in Clause 3, Article 5 of the Government’s Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  5. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  Confiscation of material evidences and means used in the commission of violations specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 4.

  a/ Organizing gambling activities;

  b/ To organize the production and distribution of the sheets and other publications for the making of the sheets;

  c/ Organizing the network of distributors.

  6. Those who organize gambling within the inter-district, district-level town, provincial city or inter-provincial area or organize gambling in a large quantity or have been administratively handled but still commit violations shall be examined for penal liability according to Article 200 of the Penal Code.

  7. Additional penalties and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  a/ Confiscating money gained from violations, material evidences and means used in the violations, for cases of violation specified in Clause 2; Point a, Clause 3; Points c and d, Clause 4; Clause 5;

  b/ To confiscate and destroy poems prescribed at Point c, Clause 3.

  Article 21.- Violations of regulations on the prevention and combat of social evils in massage services.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for any of the following acts:

  a/ Leasing or lending out certificates and professional technical diplomas in massage and physiotherapy;

  b/ Practicing massage without having certificates and professional technical diplomas in massage and physiotherapy or using certificates and diplomas of other persons.

  2. A fine of 500,000 to 1,000,000 VND for acts of employing persons who have no certificates and professional technical diplomas in massage and physiotherapy to practice massage.

  3. A fine of 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 VND for acts of transferring certificates of qualification and conditions for massage practice to other establishments or using certificates of other establishments to provide massage services.

  4. A fine of 5,000,000 to 10,000,000 VND for acts of providing massage services without certificates of qualification and conditions for massage practice.

  5. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations specified in this Article:

  Stripping off the right to use certificates and professional technical diplomas in massage and physiotherapy, certificates of qualification and conditions for massage practice, for violations specified in Clauses 1 and 3.

  Article 22.- Violations of regulations on prevention and combat of social evils in operation of accommodation establishments and restaurants.

  1. A fine of from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to number rooms of accommodation establishments or restaurants in accordance with the registered plan;

  b) Displaying pornographic pictures and photos or provocative images at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  c) Failing to register accommodation of guests at their establishments;

  d) Failing to declare temporary residence as prescribed for guests staying at accommodation establishments;

  dd) Closing the door to the dining room or bar area of accommodation establishments or restaurants when they are occupied by guests;

  e) Using dim lights or insufficient lighting in dining rooms, bar areas, entertainment areas of accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  g) Using erotic service methods at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  h. Failing to register the list of waiters and staff members with competent police authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for employing waiters or staff members working at accommodation establishments that are restaurants without entering into labor contracts.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a. Circulating music tapes and discs which have been banned or withdrawn under decisions issued by accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  b. Circulating music tapes and discs containing pornographic or violent contents at accommodation establishments or restaurants.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a. Circulating films, video tapes and discs, or performing theatrical works, musical works or dance works which have been banned or withdrawn under decisions issued by accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  b. Circulating films, video tapes and discs, or performing theatrical works, musical works or dance works containing pornographic or violent contents at accommodation establishments or restaurants.

  5. Additional forms of sanction and other measures against violations in this Article:

  a/ Confiscation of films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, for violations specified at Point a, Clause 3; Point a, Clause 4;

  b/ Confiscation and destruction of pictures, photos, films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, for violations specified at Point b, Clause 1; Point b, Clause 3; Point b, Clause 4;

  c/ Deprivation of the right to use business licenses, for violations specified in Clauses 3 and 4.

  Deprivation of the right to practice for up to six months, for performers who commit violations specified in Clause 4.

  6. For state accommodation establishments or restaurants, apart from being fined and having their evidences confiscated, the persons in charge of such establishments or restaurants shall also be disciplined according to law provisions.

  Article 23.- Tolerance, cover-up, enticing and harboring of social evils.

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for directors of hotels, owners of hotels, guest houses, restaurants, inns, motels, massage establishments who cover-up or due to irresponsibility let acts of prostitution, drug abuse, gambling occur in establishments under their management.

  Apart from the above-mentioned fines, the violating establishment may be deprived of the right to use its business license; if it is a State official, he/she shall also be disciplined.

  2. The immediate superior of the person who commits acts of buying and selling sex, using narcotics or gambling but covers up or fails to promptly discipline the violator; or the person who is tasked with directly combating social evils but tolerates, covers up or fails to promptly handle so as to let such acts occur in the area under his/her management shall be disciplined according to the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2, Article 8 of the Government's Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  3. Directors of hotels, owners of hotels, guest houses, restaurants, inns, motels, massage establishments or anyone else who organizes gambling, facilitates gambling, provides accommodation for, entices or guides prostitutes or uses narcotic substances in any form other than cases specified in Clauses 4, 5 and 6 Article 18; Clause 4 Article 19; Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 Article 20 of this Decree shall face criminal prosecution according to Articles 200, 202 and 203 of the Criminal Code; enticing or accommodating minors for prostitution, drug use or gambling shall face criminal prosecution according to Article 148 of the Criminal Code.

  SECTION 4: ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ADVERTISING AND WRITING AND PUBLISHING ADVERTISEMENTS, FORMS AND LEVELS OF SANCTION

  Article 24.- Violations of the regulations on procedures for asking for permission to advertise.

  1. A caution or a fine of up to 20,000 VND shall be imposed on each advertisement in posters, loose-leaf papers or folded sheets without permit.

  2. A fine of between 100,000 VND and 300,000 VND shall be imposed on one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising with no permit;

  b/ Drawing and displaying advertisement boards or billboards without obtaining permission from advertisers.

  3. A fine of 500,000 to 1,500,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to write on advertisement boards or billboards the number of the advertisement permit, the time limit for advertisement or name of the owner of the advertisement service;

  b/ Advertising products or goods or introducing activities of establishments without certification, registration or approval by specialized State management agencies as prescribed.

  4. A fine of 1,500,000 to 5,000,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising medicinal materials, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, medical instruments and medical activities without approval by competent health agencies;

  b/ Advertising with advertisement boards or billboards beyond the time limit prescribed in the permit;

  c/ Advertising on publications or means of transport without permit.

  5. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for trading in the license for providing advertising services or license for conducting advertisements for another organization or individual or using the license of another organization or individual to provide advertising services or conduct advertisements.

  6. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Providing advertising services without license;

  b) Providing cooperative advertising services with foreign organizations or individuals without a business cooperation contract;

  c) Conducting advertisements without license at places of cultural and artistic activities;

  d) Laying up advertisement boards without license.

  7. A fine of from VND 30,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed on foreign organizations or individuals providing advertising services in Vietnam or conducting advertisements without signing contracts with Vietnamese advertisers.

  8. Additional penalties and measures applicable to the violations specified in this Article:

  a/ Depriving the right to use licenses in case of violations specified in Clause 5;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of barricades, barriers, loose sheets, folded sheets or advertising boards and signs, for violations specified in Clause 1; Point a, Clause 2; Points b and c, Clause 4; Point d, Clause 6; Clause 7.

  Article 25.- Violations of regulations on forms and methods of advertising.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to VND 50,000 for using the prescribed loudness level beyond the limit for advertising from 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for act of advertising on boards, billboards with appearance, color and form similar to traffic signals, public signboards.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for each square meter of area on which the limit is exceeded once, once a day or 1% in excess of the prescribed limit;

  a/ Advertising on billboards or advertising boards in excess of the prescribed area (m2) stated in the license;

  b/ Advertising on radio and television stations in excess of the permitted number of times or the permitted time (%).

  c/ Advertising on printed newspapers, radio and television stations for more days than allowed in a campaign;

  d/ Advertising on printed newspapers for more area (%) than allowed.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Modifying forms of advertising boards and signs compared with the set form in licenses;

  b/ Advertising goods or products not clearly named or activities permitted to be advertised but only general brand names or company names are mentioned;

  c/ Advertising on printed newspapers, radio and television stations in violation of the minimum distance (5 days) between two advertising campaigns.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising on front pages of daily newspapers, weekly newspapers or monthly newspapers or on the front page of magazines, special issues or supplements;

  b/ Trading in goods or products banned from advertisement.

  c/ Advertisements interspersed in news items or articles or interspersed in news programs and special programs on radio and television stations, except for live broadcast of foreign programs.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 30,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for producing goods which are subject to prohibited advertisement.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for using the national flag, national anthem, national emblem, portrait of a leader, Party emblem or international anthem as background for advertisement presentation.

  8. Additional forms of sanction and other measures against acts of violation prescribed in this Article:

  a/ Confiscating goods in case of violations specified at Point b, Clause 5 and Clause 6;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of billboards and advertising boards, for violations specified in Clause 2; Point a, Clause 3; Points a and b, Clause 4 and Clause 7.

  Article 26.- Violations of regulations on places and areas for advertising.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to VND 10,000 shall be imposed for each advertisement on posters, loose leaflets or folded leaflets not placed at the prescribed places in the license.

  2. A fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 shall be imposed for advertisement on tape not placed at the prescribed places in the license.

  3. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Placing, erecting or setting up advertisement boards or signs not at the places prescribed in the license.

  b/ Placing, erecting or setting up advertisement boards or signs within the protection perimeter of traffic works, thus affecting traffic order and safety.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for act of advertisement at places banned from advertisement.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for act of providing advertisement services beyond the scope of practice or area of operation prescribed in the license.

  6. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations specified in this Article:

  a/ Depriving the right to use the license for providing advertising services in case of recidivism of violation of Clause 5;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of barricades, posters, leaflets, folded sheets or advertisement boards and signs, for violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4.

  Article 27.- Violations of regulations on contents of advertisements.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of using pictures, photos, posters, loose sheets, folded sheets, umbrellas, trolleys, goods boxes to advertise goods or products which are banned from advertisement.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ To offer goods or products banned from advertisement;

  b/ Advertising goods and products which are banned from advertising on signboards;

  c/ Advertising contents of press, publications or artistic works which have not yet been licensed for publication, distribution, dissemination or performance.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for act of modifying and falsifying contents of approved advertisement boards and signs in licenses.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Making untruthful or unquality advertisement on registered goods;

  b/ Making advertisement with contents which reduce prestige and quality of goods of other organizations and individuals.

  5. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising goods and products which are banned from advertisement on advertisement boards and signs, means of transport or at places of public cultural activities;

  b/ Advertising goods and products which are banned from advertisement on radio and television, publications, video tapes and disks, music tapes and disks.

  6. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures against violations in this Article:

  a/ Confiscating goods or products in case of violations specified in Clause 1, Point a, Clause 2;

  b/ Forcing the dismantlement of advertising boards and signs in case of violations specified at Point b, Clause 2; Clause 3; Clause 4; Point a, Clause 5;

  c/ Forcing compensation for actual damage not exceeding VND 1,000,000 in case of violations specified at Point b, Clause 4; if the damage exceeds VND 1,000,000, the compensation must be settled according to civil proceedings.

  Article 28.- Violations of regulations on writing and putting up signboards.

  1. A fine of between VND 50,000 and VND 150,000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts:

  a/ Putting up signboards not attached to head offices of agencies, organizations, hotels, restaurants, shops or stalls with signboards;

  b/ Failing to write down or to write in Vietnamese the full name on the signboard.

  2. A fine of between VND 150,000 and VND 500,000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to adequately display the contents prescribed on the signboards;

  b/ Using names and/or scripts in Vietnamese on the signboards of agencies, organizations, enterprises or companies of Vietnam while using names and/or scripts in foreign languages;

  c/ Displaying names and/or scripts in foreign languages on the signboards with more prominent colors and/or lights than those of Vietnamese names and/or scripts;

  d/ Signboards of economic organizations bearing abbreviated names, international transaction names or names in foreign languages which are written in Vietnamese above or bigger than the Vietnamese ones;

  e/ Signboards bearing advertisement contents.

  3. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations specified in this Article:

  Forcible dismantlement of signboards, for violations specified at Point a, Clause 1 and Clause 2.

  Chapter III

                       COMPETENCE AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 29.- Competence of the People's Committees at all levels to sanction administrative violations.

  The presidents of the People's Committees at all levels shall have the right to sanction according to competence prescribed in Articles 26, 27 and 28 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations within the territory under their management for the violations prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 30.- Competence of the specialized cultural-information inspection bodies to sanction administrative violations.

  The specialized cultural-information inspection force shall have the right to sanction according to competence prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for the violations in cultural activities and cultural services prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 31.- Competence of the police offices to sanction administrative violations.

  The people's police force shall have the right to sanction according to competence prescribed in Article 29 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for the social evils and acts of violation in cultural activities and cultural services related to social order and safety under their jurisdiction prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 32.- Competence of the market control offices to sanction administrative violations.

  The market management force may sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 33 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in the business of films, video tapes and disks, audio tapes and disks, books, newspapers, pictures, photos, calendars and business activities of accommodation establishments, restaurants and eateries prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 33.- Competence of the Border Guard and Customs forces to sanction administrative violations.

  The border guard and customs forces may sanction according to their competence prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in the management of the export and import of films, video tapes and disks, audio tapes and disks, books, newspapers, pictures, photos, calendars prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 34.- Competence of the specialized medical inspectorate to sanction administrative violations.

  The specialized medical inspectorate may sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in the massage service, advertisement of pharmaceuticals, medicinal materials, cosmetics, medical instruments and medical activities not yet approved by competent medical bodies prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 35.- Competence of the labor inspectorate to sanction administrative violations.

  The specialized labor inspectorate may sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of using labor without signing labor contracts prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 36.- Collection and payment of fines.

  Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services, and social evil prevention and combat shall pay fines at prescribed places; if failing to pay fines within the prescribed time limit, they shall be forced to do so.

  The fine collector is prohibited from collecting fines on the spot.

  Article 37.- Confiscation and handling of material evidences and means used in the commission of administrative violations.

  When applying the sanctioning form of confiscating material evidences and means used in administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and social evils prevention and combat, the persons with sanctioning competence shall have to strictly observe the procedures prescribed in Articles 51 and 52 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the following regulations:

  a/ For material evidences of administrative violations being films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs or publications with no violation in their contents and means used for commission of violations, the persons who issue the confiscation decisions shall have to transfer them to finance agencies of the same level for organizing valuation councils (in which representatives of culture and information agencies are compulsory members) and auctioning them.

  b/ For exhibits of administrative violations being unlabeled video tapes or tape recordings, films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, publications not yet permitted for circulation, the persons who issue the confiscation decisions shall have to transfer them to the Culture and Information Offices of the same level for assessment; in cases where the films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, publications are assessed as having no violation in their contents and can be circulated, they shall be handed over to the Finance Office of the same level for organization of valuation councils and auction according to the provisions at Point a of this Article; in cases where the films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, publications are assessed as having pornographic contents, In case of provocation of violence, the Culture and Information Agency shall carry out destruction as prescribed in Article 38 of this Decree.

  c/ For films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, publications which have been banned from circulation or withdrawn from sale, the person who has issued the confiscation decision shall transfer them to the Culture and Information Service for handling according to its competence.

  d/ All cases of transfer and handling of material evidences and means used in the commission of administrative violations specified at Points a, b and c of this Article must be recorded in writing with certification by concerned parties.

  Article 38.- Destruction of material evidences of administrative violations.

  1. For material evidence being films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs or publications with clearly indecent, pornographic contents or contents that incite violence, the person who issues the sanctioning decision shall decide on their destruction within his/her competence or transfer them to a competent agency for destruction.

  2. For material evidences being films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs or publications with pornographic or violent contents, the persons with sanctioning competence shall transfer them to the competent cultural and information agencies for organization of expertise; if after the expertise, the conclusions state that the material evidences have pornographic, violent or provocative contents, the said cultural and information agencies shall organize the destruction thereof.

  In case films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs or publications are assessed as having no pornographic, obscene or violent contents, they shall be transferred to finance agencies of the same level for organization of valuation council and auction under Article 37 of this Decree.

  3. When destroying films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, publications with indecent or pornographic contents or inciting violence, the agencies applying this measure shall set up a handling council composed of representatives of the Culture and Information Agency, the Public Security Service, the Finance Agency and the People's Procuracy of the same level; in case of necessity, representatives of concerned agencies and organizations may be invited to witness the destruction.

  The destruction must be recorded in writing with certification by members of the disposal council.

  4. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall provide specific guidance on procedures for invalidation specified in this Article.

  Article 39.- Complaints and denunciations.

  1. Citizens have the right to denounce to competent State agencies administrative violations committed by organizations and individuals in cultural activities, cultural services and social evils prevention and combat as prescribed in this Decree or denounce violations committed by persons with competence to sanction administrative violations as prescribed in this Decree.

  2. Organizations and individuals sanctioned for administrative violations or their lawful representatives have the right to lodge complaints with persons who have issued sanctioning decisions within 10 days after receiving such decisions.

  3. The order of and procedures for lodging complaints and denunciations and competence to settle complaints and denunciations about administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and social evils prevention and combat shall comply with the provisions in Articles 87, 88 and 90 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 40.- This Decree takes effect on February 1st, 1996.

  All previous provisions which are contrary to this Decree are now annulled.

  Article 41.- The Minister of Culture and Information, the Minister of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall, within the ambit of their respective functions and tasks, have to guide the implementation of this Decree.

  Article 42.- The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies, the agencies attached to the Government, the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government shall have to perform this task.
