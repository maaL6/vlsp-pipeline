                                                     DECREE

  AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF DECREE NO. 31/2016/ND-CP DATED MAY 06, 2016 OF THE GOVERNMENT ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF PLANT BREEDS, PROTECTION AND IMPORT OF ORGANIC PRODUCTS; DECREE NO. 90/2017/ND-CP DATED JULY 31, 2017 OF THE GOVERNMENT ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ANIMAL Husbandry

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to Law on Plant Protection and Quarantine dated November 25, 2013;

  Pursuant to Law on Animal Health dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to Law on Fisheries dated November 21, 2017;

  At the request of the Minister of Agriculture and Rural Development; The Government promulgates Decree amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 31/2016/ND-CP dated May 06, 2016 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on plant varieties, plant protection and quarantine; Decree No. 90/2017/ND-CP dated July 31, 2017 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on animal health.

  Article 1. Amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 31/2016/ND-CP dated May 06, 2016 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on plant varieties, plant protection and quarantine:

  1. Article 2 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  Article 2. Subjects of administrative penalties

  1. Organizations and individuals committing administrative violations related to plant varieties, plant protection and quarantine in the territory of Vietnam.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies committing violations not falling within their assigned state management tasks;

  b) Economic organizations established under the Law on Enterprises, including: Private enterprises, joint-stock companies, limited liability companies, partnerships, and dependent units of enterprises (branches and representative offices);

  c) Economic organizations established under the Law on Cooperatives, including: Cooperatives and cooperative unions;

  d) Organizations established under the Law on Investment, including: Domestic investors, foreign investors, and foreign-invested economic organizations; representative offices and branches of foreign traders in Vietnam; representative offices of foreign trade promotion organizations in Vietnam;

  dd) Social organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-politico-professional organizations, and socio-professional organizations;

  e) Public service providers and other organizations as prescribed by law.

  3. Households and individual business households that violate the provisions of this Decree shall be sanctioned like individuals."

  “Article 25. Violations against regulations on trading of plant protection drugs

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations against regulations on trading of plant protection drugs:

  a) Trading plant protection drugs together with other goods such as food, foodstuff, beverages, animal feed, aqua feed, medical drugs or veterinary drugs;

  b) Trading expired plant protection drugs; trading in plant protection drugs which fail to satisfy quality requirements or are unconformable with corresponding technical regulations and assessed at < VND 5,000,000;

  c) Trading plant protection drugs after the certificate of eligibility for trading of plant protection drugs has expired;

  d) Failure to maintain adequate conditions for trading plant protection drugs as prescribed in Article 63 of the Law on Plant Protection and Quarantine during business activities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations against regulations on trading of plant protection drugs:

  a) Trading expired plant protection drugs; trading in plant protection drugs which fail to satisfy quality requirements or are unconformable with corresponding technical regulations and assessed at from VND 5,000,000 to under VND 15,000,000;

  b) Trading in plant protection drugs on the List of plant protection drugs banned from use in Vietnam, with a quantity of less than 3 kg (or 3 liters) of finished drugs;

  c) Trading in plant protection drugs without a Certificate of eligibility for trading in plant protection drugs;

  d) Trading in plant protection drugs not on the List of plant protection drugs permitted for use in Vietnam, assessed at VND 5,000,000 or less.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations of trading in plant protection drugs:

  a) Trading in plant protection drugs which have expired; drugs which fail to ensure quality or are unconformable with corresponding technical regulations, assessed at between VND 15,000,000 and under VND 30,000,000;

  b) Trading in plant protection drugs on the List of plant protection drugs banned from use in Vietnam, with a quantity of from 3 kg (or 3 liters) to under 5 kg (or 5 liters) of finished drugs;

  c) Trading in plant protection drugs in glass ampoules;

  d) Selling plant protection drugs used for steam disinfection of humans without practicing cards for handling of plant quarantine objects or organizations without practicing cards for handling of plant quarantine objects;

  dd) Instructing the wrong contents on labels of plant protection drugs to buyers;

  e) Trading in plant protection drugs during suspension of trading, revocation of the right to use or withdrawal of certificates of eligibility for trading in plant protection drugs;

  g) Trading in plant protection drugs not included in the List of plant protection drugs permitted for use in Vietnam with a value of from VND 5,000,000 to under VND 15,000,000.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations of trading in plant protection drugs:

  a) Trading in plant protection drugs beyond their expiry dates; drugs which fail to ensure quality or are not conformable with corresponding technical regulations with a value of between VND 30,000,000 and under VND 50,000,000;

  b) Trading in plant protection drugs on the List of plant protection drugs banned from use in Vietnam with a quantity of between 5 kg (or 5 liters) and under 10 kg (or 10 liters) of finished drugs;

  c) Trading in plant protection drugs not included in the List of plant protection drugs permitted for use in Vietnam with a value of between VND 15,000,000 and under VND 30,000,000.

  5. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations of trading in plant protection drugs:

  a) Trading in plant protection drugs which have expired use duration or fail to satisfy quality requirements or do not comply with corresponding technical regulations, assessed at from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000;

  b) Trading in plant protection drugs on the List of plant protection drugs banned from use in Vietnam, with an amount of from 10 kg (or 10 liters) to under 20 kg (or 20 liters) of finished drugs;

  c) Trading in plant protection drugs not on the List of plant protection drugs permitted for use in Vietnam, assessed at from VND 30,000,000 to under VND 50,000,000.

  6. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 40,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations of trading in plant protection drugs:

  a) Trading in plant protection drugs which have expired use duration or fail to satisfy quality requirements or do not comply with corresponding technical regulations, assessed at from VND 100,000,000 to under VND 200,000,000;

  b) Trading in plant protection drugs on the List of plant protection drugs banned from use in Vietnam, with an amount of from 20 kg (or 20 liters) to under 30 kg (or 30 liters) of finished drugs;

  c) Trading in plant protection drugs not on the List of plant protection drugs permitted for use in Vietnam, assessed at from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  7. A fine of between VND 40,000,000 and 45,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations of trading in plant protection drugs:

  a) Trading in expired pesticides; pesticides of which quality is not assured or which fail to satisfy corresponding technical regulations, assessed at from VND 200,000,000;

  b) Trading in plant protection drugs on the List of plant protection drugs banned from use in Vietnam, with an amount of from 30 kg (or 30 liters) to under 50 kg (or 50 liters) of finished drugs;

  c) Trading in plant protection drugs not on the List of plant protection drugs permitted for use in Vietnam, assessed at from VND 100,000,000 to under VND 200,000,000.

  8. A fine ranging from VND 45,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Trading in plant protection drugs on the List of plant protection drugs banned from use in Vietnam, with an amount of from 50 kg (or 50 liters) of finished drugs or more, in case where the presiding agency issues a decision not to institute criminal proceedings, a decision to dismiss the decision to institute criminal proceedings, a decision to terminate investigation or a decision to dismiss the case;

  b) Trading in plant protection drugs not on the List of plant protection drugs permitted for use in Vietnam, assessed at from VND 200,000,000, or more, or gaining illicit profits of from VND 100,000,000, or more, except for cases of import licenses prescribed in Clause 2 Article 67 of the Law on Plant Protection and Quarantine, when the presiding agency issues a decision not to institute criminal proceedings, a decision to dismiss the decision to institute criminal proceedings, a decision to terminate investigation or a decision to dismiss the case.

  9. Additional penalties

  a) Suspend the certificate of eligibility to trade in plant protection drugs for 1 to 3 months, for violations specified in Points b and c Clause 4; Points b and c Clause 5 and Points b and c Clause 6 of this Article.

  b) Suspend the certificate of eligibility for trading in plant protection drugs for 03 - 06 months if the violation prescribed in Point b or Point c Clause 7 or Clause 8 of this Article is committed.

  10. Remedial measures

  a) Enforced recall and return of drugs to manufacturers or distributors for destruction or recycling if drugs have expired, are unqualified or fail to meet corresponding technical regulations as prescribed in Point b Clause 1, Point a Clause 2, Point a Clause 3, Point a Clause 4, Point a Clause 5, Point a Clause 6 and Point a Clause 7 of this Article;

  b) Enforced destruction of plant protection drugs on the List of plant protection drugs banned from use in Vietnam, plant protection drugs not on the List of plant protection drugs permitted for use in Vietnam, and plant protection drugs in glass ampoules if the violation prescribed in Point b or Point d Clause 2; Point b, Point c or Point g Clause 3; Point b or Point c Clause 4; Point b or Point c Clause 5; Point b or Point c Clause 6; Point b or Point c Clause 7 or Clause 8 of this Article is committed.”

  Article 4. Entry into force

  Article 5. Transitional provisions

  For administrative violations committed before the effective date of this Decree but detected afterwards or being considered and handled, regulations beneficial to violating individuals and organizations shall apply.

  Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall be responsible for guiding and implementing this Decree./.
