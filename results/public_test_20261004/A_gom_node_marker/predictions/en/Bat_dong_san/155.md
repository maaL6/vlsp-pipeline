                                                               CIRCULAR

   REGULATING THE LEVEL OF APPLICATION OF LAND FOR CONSTRUCTION OF AFFAIRS, MEDICAL FACILITIES, EDUCATION AND TRAINING FACILITIES, SPORTS FACILITIES

  Pursuant to the Land Law dated November 29, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 21/2013/ND-CP dated March 4, 2013 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment;

  At the request of Director General of Department of Land Administration and Director of Department of Legal Affairs, Minister of Natural Resources and Environment promulgates Circular on land use norms for construction of cultural centers, medical facilities, educational and training facilities, and sports facilities.

  Article 1. Promulgated together with this Circular are land use norms for construction of cultural centers, medical facilities, educational and training facilities, and sports facilities.

  Article 2. This Circular comes into force from March 30, 2017.

  Article 3. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies, People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities, Heads of units affiliated to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment and relevant organizations and individuals shall be responsible for implementing this Circular./.

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides for land use rates applicable to land for construction of cultural facilities, health facilities, education and training facilities, sports facilities, and cultural works classified as community land (excluding cultural facilities, health facilities, education and training facilities, and sports facilities managed by the Ministry of National Defense or the Ministry of Public Security).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This rate applies to regulatory authorities, specialized agencies in charge of natural resources and environment, relevant organizations and individuals involved in preparation, appraisal, and approval of provincial and district-level land use planning and plans; appraisal of land use demand upon land allocation, land lease, and land repurposing for construction or expansion of the scale of use of land for cultural, health, education and training, sports, and cultural works classified as community land.

  Article 3. Term interpretation

  1. “Land use quota by work” means the land area necessary for a work to fully utilize its capacity and to be used effectively for a specific purpose.

  2. “Land use quota per capita” means the land area calculated in square meters for each person to fully utilize their capacity in one field and to be used effectively for a specific purpose. The land use quota per capita is regulated according to administrative units of commune, district or province. The land use quota per capita is calculated according to total population of each administrative unit.

  3. “Communal cultural centers; village cultural houses; village meeting rooms, head offices and clubs” means cultural works classified as community land specified in Circular No. 28/2014/TT-BTNMT dated June 2, 2014 of the Minister of Natural Resources and Environment on land statistics, land inventorying and land use mapping.

  Chapter III

                                          APPLICATION OF LAND USE QUOTAS

  Article 8. Land use quotas according to works specify minimum and maximum land area for a specific work corresponding to each administrative level; in case there is a work of superior level at the administrative level, the land type corresponding to such work shall be added with the land area of the work of the superior level.

  Article 9. Land use quota per capita specify minimum and maximum land area for a person in each specific area and corresponding to each administrative level; in case there is a work of superior level at the administrative level, the land type corresponding to such work shall be added with the land area of the work of the superior level. Land use quota per capita is used to determine total area of a land type according to each administrative unit.

  Article 10. Depending on conditions of each local area, one or more than one work may be built but total land area of each type of work in the local area must not exceed the maximum area determined according to population scale of the local area.

  Article 11. In case an administrative unit has low population scale but total land area of a land type calculated per capita of such administrative unit is too small, it is allowed to apply the land area of the work as the land area of the minimum land area of the land use quota according to work.

  Article 12. During the implementation process, if difficulties arise, agencies, organizations and individuals shall promptly report them to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment for consideration and settlement./.
