                                                      DIRECTIVE

     ON A NUMBER OF MEASURES TO SUPPORT THE SCHOOL FOR SECURITY, SAFE AND STABLE DEVELOPMENT

  During the period, the world's political and economic situation has been subject to many complex, unstable and unpredictable changes, affecting Vietnam's economy with a very large opening. In this context, the Government has issued many directives aimed at stabilizing macroeconomics, controlling inflation, ensuring major balance, helping the economy recover and achieve relatively high growth compared to other countries in the region and around the world.

  On October 11, 2021, the Government promulgated Resolution No. 128/NQ-CP on safe and flexible adaptation to and effective control of COVID-19; Resolution No. 01/NQ-CP dated January 8, 2022 on major tasks and solutions for implementation of the socio-economic development plan and state budget estimate for 2022; Resolution No. 02/NQ-CP dated January 10, 2022 on major tasks and solutions for improvement of business environment and enhancement of national competitiveness in 2022; Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP dated January 30, 2022 on Resolution on socio-economic recovery and development program and implementation of Resolution No. 43/2022/QH15 of the National Assembly on fiscal and monetary policies supporting the Program. In particular, the 13th Central Committee of the Communist Party promulgated Resolution No. 18-NQ/TW on further reform, improvement of institutions and policies, enhancement of the effectiveness and efficiency of land management and use, and promotion of Vietnam’s development into a high-income developed country.

  The active implementation by authorities, sectors, local governments, and enterprises of the resolutions of the Communist Party and the State, as well as the regulations of law and the direction, management, solutions, and policies of the Government regarding the real estate market has led to positive changes in socio-economic situations, ensuring social security and macroeconomic stability.

  The real estate market plays a very important role in maintaining macroeconomic stability, controlling inflation, ensuring major balance, and promoting growth. However, despite the positive results, the development of the real estate sector has also revealed many problems, limitations, and shortcomings, and is not truly sustainable, with many risks remaining, such as: the structure of real estate goods is unreasonable, real estate product prices, especially housing prices, are still quite high compared to people's incomes, the information database on the real estate market is not synchronized, complete, reliable, or transparent.

  Some problems and difficulties to be solved include: The legal system related to real estate market has some contents which have not yet met practical needs and have not been added or amended in time; development of real estate projects in some localities does not follow the planning and plan, and is not suitable for market demand; investment procedures are prescribed in many different legal documents through many steps, thus prolonging the time for implementation of investment procedures; regulations on mandatory real estate transactions via the platform have created the basis for the formation of "ghost projects" and customer fraud cases, while the state also faces many difficulties in managing information about the real estate market, and at the same time, there is a situation of tax evasion and loss to the state budget; state management is not strict, not timely, and not well meet the management requirements.

  In order to overcome the above-mentioned problems and difficulties and continue to promote the safe, healthy and sustainable development of the real estate market in the coming period, the Prime Minister requests:

  I. A NUMBER OF GENERAL POINTS AND SOLUTIONS

  1. To review the provisions of law to resolve immediate and long-term issues in the direction of building healthy, appropriate and effective policy tools to ensure interconnection, safety and certainty between capital markets and the real estate market, diversifying capital sources for the real estate market; to promote decentralization and delegation to all levels, conduct in-depth research and propose pilot policies to create new driving forces and impulses for market development.

  2. To persistently, firmly and decisively develop a safe, healthy and sustainable real estate ecosystem; not to tighten credit unreasonably but to strengthen inspection and supervision, not to relax state management; not to criminalize economic - civil relations; to proactively detect, prevent and strictly handle violations in accordance with the law, paying attention to acts of tax evasion in real estate business, not to let any breakdown occur, protect those who do the right thing, protect and harmonize the interests of the State, the people and enterprises.

  3. Harmonize, reasonably and effectively combine fiscal policies and monetary policies, implement monetary policies in a flexible, safe and cautious manner, expand fiscal policies in a reasonable manner, focus on ensuring effectiveness, strictly manage prices and markets; do not implement “impulsive” policies, do not suddenly change from “loosening” to strict control or vice versa.

  4. Ministries, sectors and local governments shall focus on synchronizing implementation of tasks prescribed in Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on socio-economic recovery and development program.

  5. Focus on implementing solutions to attract investment, promote production and business activities, create more jobs in various fields and localities; stabilize macroeconomics, control inflation, ensure major balance of the economy.

  6. Specifically and accurately assess the situation and supply-demand to develop real estate market on the basis of respecting market laws, parallel competition laws, promoting the regulatory role of the State, policy tools to intervene and control when necessary in a proper, timely, effective manner, prevent manipulation of the market, hoarding, price hikes...

  7. Identify market segments, demands such as industrial real estate, worker housing, low-income housing to have appropriate policies to promote the market, focus on social security, create opportunities for people with low incomes, “not sacrifice progress and social equality for pursuing simple economic growth”.

  8. Develop strategic infrastructure to create new development space, new development dynamics, promote the formation of new industries, services, and urban areas.

  9. Strengthen inspection, examination, audit to resolve difficulties, handle violations to protect the market, protect assets, protect people, do not legalize violations but objectively evaluate, understand causes, have mechanisms to resolve practical difficulties.

  10. To effectively carry out communication work in appropriate forms, promote education and training in financial knowledge and legal knowledge for the people, in schools and through other forms.

  II. SOME SPECIALIZED TASKS AND KEY ISSUES

  1. Ministry of Construction

  a) Expeditiously study and complete the legal system related to real estate sector to ensure consistency, resolve difficulties and obstacles, create conditions for the real estate market to develop stably and healthily, build the real estate market information system associated with land information, improve legal basis and enhance the implementation of non-cash payment in real estate transactions. Accordingly, propose the National Assembly to give opinions and adopt the Law on Housing (amended) and the Law on Real Estate Business (amended) to create a legal framework for real estate business activities, enhance the effectiveness of real estate market management; promptly amend and supplement Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing, Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP dated April 01, 2021 of the Government on amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing in accordance with the Law on Housing.

  b) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries and central authorities in studying and proposing the promulgation of regulations guiding the implementation process of housing projects and urban areas.

  c) Regularly monitor the situation and development of the real estate market; submit quarterly reports on the real estate business and real estate market nationwide to the Prime Minister and propose solutions for the real estate market to develop stably and healthily.

  d) Inspect and examine compliance with regulations on real estate trading, real estate transaction platforms, and real estate brokerage; cooperate with provincial People’s Committees in organizing inspections and reviews of housing and real estate business projects to propose to competent authorities the revocation, suspension, temporary suspension, adjustment, conversion, or transfer of real estate business projects as per the law.

  dd) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in establishing working groups to direct, guide, and expedite local governments in implementing Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on the program for socio-economic recovery and development regarding policies supporting the development of social housing, worker housing in industrial parks, and renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings.

  e) Direct, guide, and cooperate with local governments in reviewing and supplementing planning and allocating land fund for social housing and worker housing development in accordance with the law, strictly complying with regulations on allocation of 20% land fund in housing projects and urban areas, ensuring the demand for social housing and worker housing development in their areas.

  g) Direct, guide, and cooperate with local governments in reviewing, preparing, and announcing lists of investors in social housing, worker housing, and renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings that are granted loans with 2% interest through commercial banking system.

  h) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in researching and proposing policies to promote the development of social housing and worker housing in industrial parks.

  i) Improve and implement the public information system on housing and real estate market.

  2. Ministry of Finance

  a) Urgently amend Decree No. 153/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020 of the Government on sale and transaction of private corporate bonds on domestic market and sale of corporate bonds on international market.

  b) Control capital mobilization activities of real estate businesses on the securities market to avoid speculation, manipulation and price manipulation.

  c) Cooperate with State Bank of Vietnam in inspecting and supervising compliance with regulations on issuance, investment and provision of services regarding corporate bonds, especially issuance of separate bonds of real estate businesses, credit institutions related to real estate businesses, enterprises with large issuance volume, high interest rates, loss-making enterprises, issuing enterprises without collateral.

  d) Direct and guide implementation of capital mobilization activities (including issuance of bonds) of real estate businesses on the securities market in accordance with law; create conditions for, not obstruct enterprises (with sufficient capacity, good and healthy business results) to mobilize capital to support recovery and development.

  dd) Research and improve tax policies suitable for reality regarding real estate in order to encourage effective use of houses and land, contribute to limiting speculation on houses and land, ensure reasonable and stable revenues for state budget, conform to socio-economic conditions of Vietnam and international practices.

  3. Ministry of Planning and Investment

  a) Direct, urge and guide localities, propose solutions to difficulties and obstacles, resolutely reduce unnecessary administrative procedures in investment procedures, approval of investment guidelines, investment decisions and issuance of certificates of investment registration of housing and real estate projects to support and increase supply for the market.

  b) Monitor and promote attraction of foreign investment in real estate business sector.

  4. Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment

  a/ To coordinate with the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Justice in studying, reviewing and amending current law provisions related to land use rights auction (law on auction, law on land and law on tax administration) to ensure uniformity and compatibility with local actual conditions.

  b) Inspect and inspect, and direct local governments to prevent and strictly handle violations in land use rights auction activities in accordance with law.

  c) Inspect and review indicators of land use planning and plans to ensure appropriate land use planning for various industries, professions and fields in a harmonious and rational manner, avoiding overlapping and disputes.

  5. Ministry of Public Security Take charge and cooperate closely with the Ministry of Finance, State Bank of Vietnam and relevant agencies in enhancing macroeconomics monitoring; take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Construction and the Ministry of Finance in strictly handling illegal real estate trading floors and real estate brokerage activities, tax evasion, providing inaccurate information, causing negative impacts on financial, credit and real estate market activities.

  6. Ministry of Information and Communications Directs news and press agencies to enhance provision of timely, official, accurate and adequate information on real estate market, securities market and bond market; strictly handle acts of providing inaccurate information causing negative impacts on financial, credit and real estate market activities.

  7. State Bank of Vietnam

  Strictly monitor credit in real estate sector. Direct credit institutions to strictly control credit quality and grant loans in real estate sector in accordance with regulations of law; promptly consider prioritizing loans for social housing, worker housing, affordable commercial housing with high efficiency and capable of being repaid.

  8. People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities

  a) Expeditiously organize the formulation, appraisal and approval of urban and rural construction planning, land use planning and plans; implement transparency in information on construction planning, lists and progress of infrastructure development projects and real estate projects, especially large-scale projects, and consolidation, establishment and upgrading of local administrative units, thereby preventing rumors and speculation to push up prices for illegal profit purposes.

  b) Expeditiously develop the housing development program from now until 2025 and 2030 and the 5-year and annual housing development plans according to the Law on Housing, which clearly defines the list of housing projects as the basis for approving investment guidelines for housing projects in the area.

  c) Review and formulate the list of housing and real estate projects in the area; specifically assess reasons for projects approved for investment guidelines but not yet implemented or delayed implementation. Based on this, focus on resolving legal and procedural difficulties in preparing investment for housing projects to increase supply for the market.

  d) Organize the disclosure of the list of projects, land fund, procedures for development of housing and real estate in the area to attract investment; facilitate domestic and foreign investors to easily access and participate in housing and real estate investment.

  dd) Focus on directing and organizing the land allocation and selection of project developers for new real estate projects and housing projects to increase supply for the market.

  e) Promote the auction of land use rights; bid for land-based projects to select investors to increase supply of commercial housing projects for the real estate market.

  g) Implement solutions to promote social housing and worker housing development in the area.

  - Review and add land planning and arrangement for land for social housing and worker housing in accordance with regulations of law, strictly comply with regulations on land reserved for 20% of housing projects, urban areas, and ensure demand for social housing and worker housing in the area.

  - Focus on implementation of Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on socio-economic recovery and development program, which requires to urgently make list of projects, review land fund, complete legal procedures, select investors to implement social housing, worker housing in industrial parks, renovate and reconstruct old apartment buildings in the area.

  - Review eligible entities and conditions to make and publish list of investors in social housing, worker housing, renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings eligible for loan package with interest rate of 2% via commercial bank system according to Decree on interest support from state budget for loans granted by enterprises, cooperatives, business households.

  - Cooperate with Vietnam Bank for Social Policies in reviewing and consolidating demand of individuals, households borrowing to purchase, lease-purchase social housing, worker housing; build new or renovate, repair houses according to Article 16 of Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP, Clause 10 Article 1 of Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP and relevant documents, ensuring accuracy and reflecting actual demand of people.

  h) Urgently complete quality inspection and assessment of remaining old apartment buildings in the area to determine apartment buildings to be deconstructed; promptly prepare and approve plans and lists of apartment building renovation and reconstruction projects according to Decree No. 69/2021/ND-CP.

  i) Control, detect, prevent, and strictly handle violations in land use right auction activities in accordance with the law.

  k) Take measures to manage and prevent the division, "land division and sale on the land" in areas where investment is not permitted or where infrastructure systems are lacking; strengthen control of activities of real estate trading floors, organizations and individuals engaging in real estate brokerage; rectify the practices of direct trading and "price manipulation" causing disorder in real estate market information.

  l) Review projects that use land but are implemented late, left unused, or used for wrong purposes, resulting in land wastefulness; based on this, cooperate with relevant agencies in considering and guiding handling according to their competence and regulations of law on land and other relevant laws to effectively use land fund, and develop in a manner suitable to actual social demand.

  m) Closely monitor, promptly grasp information and developments of the market, and take timely measures to stabilize the market and prevent real estate price spikes and "real estate bubbles" in the area.

  n) Strengthen inspection and examination, and strictly handle violations against regulations of law on real estate business, land, construction and other relevant laws (if any).

  9. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People's Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall, within their assigned functions and tasks, thoroughly understand and strictly organize implementation of this Directive; and submit reports to the Ministry of Construction every 6 months and annually for consolidation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  10. The Ministry of Construction shall take charge and cooperate with the Government Office and relevant agencies in guiding, urging and inspecting the implementation of this Directive, and consolidate and report to the Prime Minister.
