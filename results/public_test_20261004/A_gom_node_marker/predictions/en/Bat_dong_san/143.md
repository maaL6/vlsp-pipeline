  Article 2. Pursuant to this Resolution, the People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall:

  1. Organize adjustment of land use planning by 2020 and final land use plan (2016-2020), including review and adjustment of planning of sectors, fields and local areas in the province using land to align with adjustment of land use planning by 2020 and final land use plan (2016-2020) of the province; organization of adjustment of land use planning and plan must strictly align with requirements for socio-economic development, national defense and security assurance in the province.

  2. Determine boundaries and publicize area of rice cultivation land, protective forests and reserve forests that need to be strictly protected; regulate allocation of resources, first of all state budget resources to ensure benefits between areas eligible for industrial, urban and service development and areas protecting rice cultivation land and forest land; adopt appropriate policies and measures to reduce production costs, increase incomes and enable rice farmers to have confidence in production; increase investment in social infrastructure and technical infrastructure to ensure consistency and synchronization among areas.

  3. Continue to direct preparation and approval of adjustment of land use planning by 2020 and annual district-level land use plans according to land laws as a basis for land repossession, land allocation, land lease and permission for land repurposing to ensure alignment with adjustment of land use planning and plan of the province.

  4. Take responsibility before the Government and Prime Minister for repurposing forest land to other purposes, limiting the repurposing of forest land and reviewing and inspecting the current use of protection forests, dedicated forests and natural production forests before repurposing land for construction works and projects as per regulation; in respect of construction works and projects that use protection forests, dedicated forests and natural production forests and are required to report to the Government and Prime Minister, send reports to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment to cooperate with the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development in appraising and submitting to the Prime Minister before deciding on land repurposing as per regulation.

  The People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall direct, inspect and take responsibility before the Government and Prime Minister for using land efficiently and effectively and complying with regulations of law.

  5. Intensify the dissemination of land laws to enable land users to grasp regulations of law, use land for right purposes, efficiently and effectively, protect environment and achieve sustainable development.

  6. Manage and use land according to land use planning and plans especially for areas where land will be repurposed for economic development and restructuring towards service and urban development to increase revenue for the budget; promote the auction of land use rights. Encourage efficient and effective use of land and remedy the abandonment of allocated and leased land. Strictly manage and supervise land fund for urban development, economic zones, industrial parks, industrial clusters, land for non-agricultural production and business activities in order to use land fund efficiently and effectively and remedy the imbalance in land use between residential land and land for technical infrastructure and social infrastructure and environmental protection requirements.

  7. Intensify inspection and examination of management and use of land to prevent and promptly handle violations in implementation of land use planning and plans; resolutely refuse to allocate, lease or repurpose land or expropriate land if such activities are not included in land use planning and plans; examine and handle cases where land has been allocated or leased but not used or used for wrong purposes as prescribed by land laws.

  8. Intensify investigation and assessment of land quality and potential; assess land degradation and pollution; improve the land information database system; train human resources; research and apply high technology and science to land management, improve the quality of land use planning, preparation, appraisal, management, organization of implementation and supervision.

  9. Intensify forecasting, information provision and communication to enable local authorities, sectors and people to proactively respond to climate change impacts.

  10. Annually, the People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall send reports on results of implementation of land use planning and plans to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment for consolidation and reporting to the Government for submission to the National Assembly as prescribed.

  Article 3. The Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in directing and inspecting the conversion of protection forests, reserve forests and production forests into natural forests in accordance with approved planning and law provisions.

  Article 4. The President of the People’s Committee of Dak Nong province and heads of relevant ministries and sectors shall implement this Resolution./.
