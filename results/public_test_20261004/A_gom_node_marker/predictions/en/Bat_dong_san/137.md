                                                          PLAN

     IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PRIME MINISTER’S DIRECTIVE No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019 on measures to promote the stable and healthy development of the real estate market.
      (Issued together with the Minister of Construction’s Decision No. 398/QD-BXD dated May 22, 2019)

  I. Objectives

  - To implement in a synchronous, timely and effective manner the Prime Minister’s Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019 on measures to promote the stable and healthy development of the real estate market (hereinafter referred to as “Directive No. 11/CT-TTg”).

  - To strengthen the improvement of housing and real estate market management institutions in a timely and appropriate manner according to the actual situation; to effectively implement social housing programs: support for houses for people with meritorious services to the revolution; support for houses for poor households in rural areas, flood-prone areas in central Vietnam, regularly flooded areas of the Mekong Delta; houses for low-income people in urban areas, houses for workers in industrial parks, and houses for students, etc.; to strengthen the guidance on inspection and examination of housing and real estate market management.

  II. Plan contents

  1. Regarding the task of completing the Law on amendments to the Law on Construction, Law on Housing, Law on Real Estate Business:

  1.1. Research and finalize the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Construction.

  - Presiding authority: Department of Construction Management;

  - Coordinating authority: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Deadline for completion: Submit to the Government in July 2019; finalize and submit to the National Assembly in October 2019.

  1.2. Research and finalize draft Law on amendments to some Articles of Housing Law regarding support policies for housing for officials, public servants and employees having housing difficulties according to Resolution No. 26-NQ/TW of the XIIth Central Committee;

  - Presiding authority: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating authority: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Deadline for completion: Expected in 2020 (after the Politburo approves housing scheme for officials, public servants and employees in political system and issues resolution on this issue).

  1.3. Research and finalize draft Law on amendments to some Articles of Law on real estate business

  - Presiding authority: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating authority: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Deadline for completion: According to the National Assembly’s program on law development.

  2. Research and promulgate amendments to construction standards and regulations applicable to apartment buildings, condominium buildings, resort villas, officetels, guest houses, and dormitories.

  - Presiding authority: Department of Science, Technology and Environment;

  - Executing authority: National Architecture Institute;

  - Deadline for completion: Complete in 2019.

  3. Research and propose promulgation of legislative documents on regulations on management and operation of officetels.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant units;

  - Deadline for completion: 3rd quarter of 2019.

  4. Urging local governments to complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  4.1. Urging local governments to complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: Information Center, Construction Economics Institute;

  - Deadline for completion: Regular tasks.

  4.2. Cooperation with ministries in collecting relevant information to improve housing and real estate market information system

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Cooperating unit: General Department of Statistics - Ministry of Planning and Investment;

  - Deadline for completion: Regular task.

  5. Research, amendment to policies and laws, and promotion of social housing development

  5.1. Research, amendment to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing;

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Cooperating unit: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant units;

  - Deadline for completion: after the Law on amendments to the Law on Housing is approved by the National Assembly.

  5.2. Urging and guiding local governments to promote social housing development in accordance with the Prime Minister’s Directive No. 03/CT-TTg dated January 25, 2017

  - Presiding authority: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating authority: Local Departments of Construction and relevant authorities;

  - Deadline for completion: monthly routine tasks.

  5.3. Inspecting and reviewing planning and use of urban land reserved for social housing development in key areas, consolidating situation and reporting to the Prime Minister

  - Presiding authority: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating authority: Local Departments of Construction and relevant authorities;

  - Deadline for completion: September 2019.

  6. Tasks of cooperating with ministries, central authorities and local governments in enhancing inspection and requiring investors in urban area projects and housing development projects to synchronously build technical infrastructures, social infrastructures, fire prevention and firefighting systems in projects and connect with general infrastructure of the area before transferring houses to people as prescribed by construction laws, housing laws and relevant laws.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Cooperating units: Construction Inspectorate, Department of Urban Development and relevant units;

  - Deadline for completion: 2019.

  7. Tasks of regularly monitoring and consolidating situation on a periodic or irregular basis to report to the Government and Prime Minister on developments of real estate market in order to promptly take measures to stabilize market when having signs of abnormal or large fluctuations.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Cooperating units: Institute of Construction Economics and relevant units;

  - Completion time: quarterly and irregular as requested.

  III. Implementation

  1. Task assignment:

  The Minister of Construction shall take charge, direct and manage the implementation of the Plan on several solutions to promote the development of the real estate market in a stable and healthy manner;

  The Ministers shall, within their respective fields, assign and direct units to implement the contents of the Plan;

  Heads of units under the Ministry shall, based on the implementation plan of the Ministry, organize the implementation of assigned tasks in a timely and effective manner; clearly assign tasks to leaders and specialists responsible for each specific task of the unit; focus on directing, strengthening inspection and supervision of progress and results of implementation of the Plan; report on the implementation of the Plan according to their assigned functions and tasks to the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management for consolidation and reporting to the Ministry's leadership.

  Director of the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry Office in monitoring, consolidating and reporting on the results of implementation of the Plan; promptly report to the Ministry's leadership on difficulties arising during the implementation process.

  2. Funding for implementation

  The Departments of Planning and Finance, Science, Technology and Environment shall balance and allocate funding for performing tasks according to the estimates of units assigned to perform tasks from annual sources of economic and scientific and technological services and other lawful funding sources as prescribed by law.

  Assigned units shall prepare estimates for performance and send them to the Department of Planning and Finance, the Department of Science, Technology and Environment for reporting to the leadership of the Ministry for consideration and approval.

  During the implementation of the Plan, in case it is necessary to amend or supplement specific tasks, units shall take the initiative in proposing and reporting to the Minister for consideration and decision./.
