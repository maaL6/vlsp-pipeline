                                                   ORDINANCE

                                         PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING VESSELS

  Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10;
  Pursuant to Resolution No. 11/2007/QH12 on the 2008 law and ordinance-making program;
  The National Assembly’s Standing Committee promulgates the Ordinance on procedures for arrest of seagoing vessels.

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Ordinance provides for the authority and procedures for arresting seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims, application of provisional emergency measures, enforcement of civil judgments, provision of judicial assistance and the authority and procedures for releasing arrested seagoing ships.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Ordinance applies to Vietnamese agencies, organizations and individuals and foreign agencies, organizations and individuals related to the settlement of maritime claims, application of provisional emergency measures and enforcement of civil judgments and provision of judicial assistance giving rise to the right to arrest seagoing ships.

  Article 3. Authority to decide on arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The People’s Court of a province or central-affiliated city (hereinafter referred to as “the provincial People’s Court”) where there is a port or inland waterway port (hereinafter referred to as “port”) where the seagoing ship requested to be arrested is operating shall have authority to decide on arrest of such seagoing ship to ensure the settlement of maritime claims, enforcement of civil judgments and execution of judicial entrustment of a foreign Court.

  In case a port has multiple wharves in different provinces or central-affiliated cities, the provincial People’s Court where the wharf where the seagoing ship requested to be arrested is operating is located shall have authority to decide on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  2. The People’s Court which is settling a civil lawsuit, the provincial People’s Court where the arbitration council accepts the dispute shall have authority to decide on application of provisional emergency measures to arrest seagoing ships.

  3. The Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court shall consider and decide on the competent court to make decision on arrest of seagoing ships in case there is dispute over jurisdiction among provincial-level People’s Courts.

  Article 4. Responsibilities for improper requests for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Persons who request the arrest of seagoing ships shall be responsible before law for their requests. In case the improper request for arrest of seagoing ships causes damage, the requester must pay damages.

  2. All damage caused by the consequence of improper request for arrest of seagoing ships shall be settled by the parties themselves. In case they fail to reach agreement and there is a dispute, they may request the court or arbitrator to settle it according to law provisions.

  3. If the court makes a decision on improper arrest of seagoing ship due to the reason of the improper request for arrest of seagoing ship or the seagoing ship has an improper request for arrest and causes damage, the court must pay damages according to law provisions.

  Article 5. Financial security measures for requests for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Persons who request the arrest of seagoing ships shall take financial security measures, except for cases specified at Points c and d, Clause 1, Article 44 of this Ordinance, in one or both of the following forms:

  a/ To submit to the court guarantee documents in form of assets of banks or other credit institutions or of individuals, agencies or organizations;

  b/ To remit a sum of money, precious metal, gemstones or valuable papers according to the court's decision on forcible application of financial security measures into a safe account at the bank where the competent court is headquartered within forty eight hours after receiving such decision.

  In case of application of financial security measures for the request for arrest of a seagoing ship on the last weekend day or public holiday, the security asset shall be temporarily kept at the court; the court shall receive the sum of money or valuable papers and seal and preserve them. On the following working day, the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship shall immediately remit such asset into a bank under the court's supervision.

  2. The value of financial security determined by the court is equivalent to the loss or damage which may arise as a consequence of an improper request for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. When deciding to release a seagoing ship which is being arrested, the judge shall consider the application of financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clause 1 of this Article, unless otherwise agreed upon by the parties. On a case-by-case basis, the judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To maintain the application of financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship if deeming that the request for arrest of the seagoing ship is improper and the value of financial security is sufficient or insufficient to compensate for the damage;

  b/ To return part of the value of financial security for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship if deeming that the request for arrest of the seagoing ship is improper and the value of financial security exceeds the liability to compensate for the damage;

  c/ To return the whole value of financial security for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship if deeming that the request for arrest of the seagoing ship is proper.

  Article 6. Fees for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The seagoing ship arrest requester shall pay a fee as prescribed by law.

  2. Fees for seagoing ship arrest to secure the settlement of maritime claims, application of provisional urgent measures, enforcement of civil judgments and performance of judicial entrustment of foreign Courts shall be paid to competent Courts which decide on seagoing ship arrest specified in Article 3 of this Ordinance within forty eight hours after the Court requests payment of fees.

  Article 7.- Supervising the observance of law in seagoing ship arrest.

  The People's Procuracy shall supervise the observance of law in seagoing ship arrest; exercise the proposing rights according to law provisions to ensure the timely and lawful arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 8.- Documents and evidence enclosed with the written request for seagoing ship arrest or the written request for release of seagoing ship being arrested

  1. Enclosed with the written request for seagoing ship arrest or the written request for release of seagoing ship being arrested must be documents and evidence proving that the request for seagoing ship arrest or release of seagoing ship being arrested is well grounded and lawful.

  2. In case documents and evidence proving the request for seagoing ship arrest or release of seagoing ship being arrested are in foreign languages, they must be enclosed with their Vietnamese translations which have been lawfully certified according to Vietnam's law provisions. For documents and papers made, granted or certified by competent foreign agencies under foreign laws, they must be legalized by consular offices, except for cases of exemption therefrom according to provisions of international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party.

  Article 9.- Enforcement of decisions on seagoing ship arrest or release of seagoing ship being arrested

  1. Immediately after issuing a decision to arrest or release a seagoing ship under arrest, the court president shall assign a court official to execute the delivery of the decision as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  2. Within twelve hours from the time of assignment, the court official shall arrive at the port and deliver two copies of the decision to the director of the maritime port office or the director of the inland waterway port (hereinafter referred to as the port authority) where the seagoing ship is operating. The director of the port authority shall execute the decision to arrest or release the seagoing ship under arrest in accordance with law and hand over one copy to the captain for execution.

  In case the court official cannot reach the port within the above-mentioned time limit, the decision may be sent by fax or email in accordance with law.

  3. Maritime, security, quarantine, customs, tax, culture and information, fire prevention and control, environmental protection and other state management agencies shall coordinate in executing the decision to arrest the seagoing ship upon request of the director of the port authority and submit to the direction of the director of the port authority in coordinating the execution of the decision to arrest the seagoing ship.

  Vietnam Coast Guard shall coordinate in executing the decision to arrest the seagoing ship upon request of the director of the port authority.

  4. During the period when the seagoing ship is arrested, the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator shall maintain the operation of the seagoing ship.

  5. The Government shall stipulate the execution of the court decisions prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article and the handling of seagoing ships in cases where the ship owner abandons or puts the seagoing ship up for auction while it is under arrest.

  Article 10. Notification of the execution of decisions to arrest or release seagoing ships under arrest

  1. The Director of the port authority shall notify in writing the Court, Vietnam Maritime Administration and relevant state management agencies at the port of the execution of the decision to arrest or release a seagoing ship being arrested; in case of arrest for enforcement of civil judgment, immediately notify the civil judgment enforcement agency so that it can enforce the civil judgment.

  2. The captain shall notify the ship owner, the ship hirer, the ship operator and other persons with related interests of the arrest or release of the seagoing ship.

  Chapter 2.

    PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS, RELEASE OF SEAGOING SHIPS BEING ARRESTED AND ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS AGAIN TO ENSURE THE SETTLEMENT OF MARITIME CLAIMS

  Article 11. Maritime claims giving rise to the right to request arrest of seagoing ships

  Maritime claims giving rise to the right to request arrest of seagoing ships are claims about:

  1. Salaries, repatriation expenses, social insurance premiums and other amounts payable to crew members, officers and other crew members on board a seagoing ship;

  2. Compensation for loss of life, injury and other health damage directly related to the operation of a seagoing ship;

  3. Freight charges, maritime security charges, pilotage charges, port charges and other port charges;

  4. Charges for rescue of seagoing ships;

  5. Losses and damage to property outside of contract directly related to operation of seagoing ships;

  6. Damage or threat of damage caused by seagoing ships to environment, shore or relevant interests; measures applied to prevent, limit or remove this damage; compensation for this damage; costs of reasonable practical measures applied or applied to restore environment; loss incurred or likely to be incurred by third parties related to this damage; damage, costs or losses similar to those specified in this clause;

  7. Costs related to raising, moving, salvaging, destroying or rendering harmless wrecked, stranded or abandoned seagoing ships, including any objects on board and costs or expenses related to preservation of abandoned seagoing ships and costs for crew members of seagoing ships;

  8. Agreements related to use or hiring of seagoing ships, even if they are specified in the ship hire contracts or in other forms;

  9. Agreements related to transport of goods or passengers on seagoing ships, even if they are specified in the ship hire contracts or in other forms;

  10. Losses or damage related to goods, including luggage transported on seagoing ships;

  11. General damage;

  12. Towing of seagoing ships;

  13. Use of maritime pilots;

  14. Goods, materials, foodstuffs, fuels and equipment (including machinery and equipment) supplied or services provided for the operation, management, preservation and maintenance of seagoing ships;

  15. Building, modification, repair or provision of seagoing ships;

  16. Payment made on behalf of ship owners;

  17. Insurance premiums paid by ship owners or persons acting on behalf of ship owners or persons hiring bareboat charters;

  18. Charges for commissions or brokerage fees related to seagoing ships which must be paid by ship owners, persons hiring bareboat charters or authorized persons;

  19. Disputes over the ownership of seagoing ships;

  20. Disputes among co-owners of seagoing ships over the use of their seagoing ships or over incomes generated by their seagoing ships;

  21. Seagoing ship mortgage;

  22. Disputes arising from contracts for purchase and sale of seagoing ships.

  Article 12. Right to request arrest of seagoing ships in order to secure settlement of maritime claims

  Persons having maritime claims defined in Article 11 of this Ordinance may request competent courts defined in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide on the arrest of seagoing ships in order to secure settlement of maritime claims.

  Article 13. Conditions for arrest of seagoing ships in order to secure settlement of maritime claims

  1. When there is a request for arrest of seagoing ships in order to secure settlement of maritime claims defined in Article 12 of this Ordinance, the court shall decide on the arrest of seagoing ships in the following cases:

  a) The ship owner is responsible for maritime claims at the time when such claims arise and remains the ship owner at the time of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  b) The bareboat hirer is responsible for maritime claims at the time when such claims arise and remains the bareboat hirer or the ship owner at the time of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  c) Such maritime claim is made on the basis of mortgage of the seagoing ship;

  d) Such maritime claim is related to the ownership or possession of the seagoing ship;

  dd) Such maritime claim is secured by a maritime lien related to the seagoing ship.

  2. The arrest of a seagoing ship shall also be carried out against one or more seagoing ships under the ownership of the person who is responsible for maritime claims and at the time when such claims arise, he/she is:

  a) Owner of the seagoing ship related to the arising of maritime claims;

  b) Bareboat hirer, time chartered vessel hirer or voyage chartered vessel hirer of the seagoing ship related to the arising of maritime claims.

  3. The provisions of clause 2 of this Article shall not apply to maritime claims related to the ownership of seagoing ships.

  Article 14. Time limit for seizing a seagoing ship to secure settlement of maritime claims.

  1. The maximum time limit for seizing a seagoing ship to secure settlement of a maritime claim is thirty days from the date of seizure of the seagoing ship.

  2. Within the time limit for seizing a seagoing ship to secure settlement of a maritime claim, if the person requesting the seizure of the seagoing ship initiates a lawsuit at court or requests arbitration to settle the dispute and continues to request the seizure of the seagoing ship, the time limit for seizing the seagoing ship to secure settlement of the maritime claim shall terminate when the court issues a decision on the application or non-application of provisional urgent measures to seize the seagoing ship.

  Article 15. Application for seizure of seagoing ship to secure settlement of maritime claims

  1. The person requesting the seizure of a seagoing ship to secure settlement of a maritime claim shall file an application.

  2. An application for seizure of a seagoing ship to secure settlement of a maritime claim shall contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of filing of the application;

  b) Name of the requesting Court;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting arrest of the seagoing ship;

  d) Name, nationality, seagoing ship identification number as prescribed by International Maritime Organization (IMO), tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested to be arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or ship operator in case the request for payment is made by the hirer or operator;

  h) Specific maritime claims giving rise to the right to request arrest of the seagoing ship and the maximum value of such maritime claim;

  i) Expected loss or damage that may arise due to the request for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. In case the seagoing ship arresting requester does not know accurately and fully the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write down what he/she knows related to such matters.

  Article 16. Sending applications for seagoing ship arrest for security of settlement of maritime claims and enclosed documents and evidence The seagoing ship arresting requester must send his/her application and enclosed documents and evidence to the competent court which decides on seagoing ship arrest as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 17. Receipt of applications for seagoing ship arrest for security of settlement of maritime claims

  Immediately after receiving an application for seagoing ship arrest for security of settlement of maritime claims and enclosed documents and evidence, the competent court which decides on seagoing ship arrest as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record it in the book of receipt. The court president shall immediately assign a judge to settle the application.

  Article 18. Consideration of applications for seagoing ship arrest for security of settlement of maritime claims

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving an application for seagoing ship arrest and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider the application and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To accept the application for seagoing ship arrest for security of settlement of maritime claims if finding that there are sufficient conditions for issuing a decision on seagoing ship arrest, requesting the applicant to apply financial measures to support the request for seagoing ship arrest and seagoing ship arrest charge;

  b/ To return the application for seagoing ship arrest for security of settlement of maritime claims if finding that there are insufficient conditions for issuing a decision on seagoing ship arrest or that the handling of the application falls beyond the jurisdiction of such court.

  2. In case of decision to return the request for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims, the Court shall issue or immediately send such decision together with the request and enclosed documents and evidence to the requester.

  Article 19. Complaints and settlement of complaints against decisions to return requests for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims

  1. Within twenty-four hours after receiving the decision to return the request for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims, the requester may lodge a written complaint with the Court president about such decision.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving the written complaint about the decision to return the request for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims and enclosed documents and evidence, the Court president shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision to return the request for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims;

  b/ To cancel the decision to return the request for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims and receive back the request and enclosed documents and evidence for handling the request for arrest of seagoing ships.

  3. The complaint-settling decision of the Court president is final.

  Article 20. Decisions to arrest seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims

  1. The judge shall immediately issue a decision to arrest the seagoing ship to secure the settlement of the maritime claim when the seagoing ship arrest requester produces the receipts and documents proving that he/she has taken financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, and has paid the seagoing ship arrest charge specified in Article 6 of this Ordinance.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing ship to secure the settlement of a maritime claim shall contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance of the decision;

  b/ Name of the decision-issuing court;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the seagoing ship arrest requester;

  d/ Maritime claim giving rise to the right to request the court to arrest the seagoing ship;

  e/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested to be arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating on maritime waters;

  f/ Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the hirer and operator;

  i) The Court’s findings and legal grounds for accepting the petition;

  k) Court decisions

  3. Decisions to arrest seagoing ships for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of maritime claims shall take effect immediately, even in cases where complaints or petitions are lodged.

  4. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision to arrest a seagoing ship to the port authority for enforcement under Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; and send it to the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship or immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship is arrested involving foreign elements.

  Article 21. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints and recommendations about decisions to arrest seagoing ships for ensuring settlement of maritime complaints

  1. The captain, owner of a bareboat, hirer or operator of a bareboat shall have the right to lodge a written complaint with the court president about the decision to arrest the ship for the purpose of settling the maritime complaint. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty eight hours from the moment the captain receives the court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level shall be entitled to file written recommendations with the Chief Judge of the Court on the decision to arrest the seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime claims. The time limit for filing recommendations is forty eight hours from the time the Procuracy of the same level receives the decision of the Court.

  2. Within forty eight hours after receiving the written complaint or recommendation about the decision to arrest the seagoing ship, the Chief Judge of the Court shall consider settling it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision to arrest the seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime claims;

  b/ To cancel the decision to arrest the seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime claims;

  3. The decision to settle the complaint or recommendation by the Chief Judge of the Court is final.

  Article 22. Grounds for releasing seagoing ships being arrested in order to ensure the settlement of maritime claims

  1. Seagoing ships being arrested in order to ensure the settlement of maritime claims shall be immediately released when there is one of the following grounds:

  a/ After the ship owner, the ship hirer or the ship operator has taken measures to secure the replacement or fully pay the debt;

  b) The obligations regarding the assets of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator who has another person to guarantee the performance of the obligation or have a written commitment from a reputable insurance organization. The Ministry of Finance shall notify the list of reputable insurance organizations;

  c) At the request of the person who has requested the arrest of the seagoing ship;

  d) The decision on arrest of the seagoing ship has been annulled;

  dd) The time limit for arrest of the seagoing ship under the court's decision has expired.

  2. The substitute security measure shall be agreed upon by the parties. In case there is no agreement between the parties on the level and form of the substitute security measure, the court shall decide on the level and form of the substitute security measure but it must not exceed the value of the arrested seagoing ship or the property obligation which is the basis for the arrest of the seagoing ship in case the property obligation is smaller than the value of the seagoing ship.

  Article 23. Request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested to secure the settlement of maritime claims

  1. When there is one of the grounds prescribed at Points a, b and c, Clause 1, Article 22 of this Ordinance, the ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator, the captain, the person who has requested the arrest of the seagoing ship and other relevant persons may request the release of the seagoing ship being arrested. The request for release of the seagoing ship must be made in writing.

  2. A written request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of making of the written request;

  b) Name of the requesting Court;

  c) Name and address of the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating on maritime waters;

  dd) Number and date of the decision to arrest the seagoing ship and the issuing Court;

  e) Reason for requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  g) Guarantee of the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested.

  Article 24. Sending written requests for the release of seagoing ships being arrested to secure settlement of maritime claims

  The applicant for release of the seagoing ship being arrested to ensure the settlement of maritime claims shall send the written request and enclosed documents and evidence to the Court that has issued the decision on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  Article 25. Decision on release of seagoing ship being arrested to ensure the settlement of maritime claims

  1. Immediately after receiving the written request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested to ensure the settlement of maritime claims, the Chief Justice of the Court shall assign a Judge to settle the release of the seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving the written request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested to ensure the settlement of maritime claims and enclosed documents and evidence, if finding there are grounds, the Judge assigned to settle the release of the seagoing ship shall consider and issue a decision on release of the seagoing ship. In case of refusal without grounds, the Judge shall notify in writing the applicant for release of the seagoing ship thereof, clearly stating the reasons for refusal.

  3. A decision on release of the seagoing ship being arrested to ensure the settlement of maritime claims shall contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance;

  b/ Name of the issuing court;

  c/ Legal grounds for the court to issue a decision on release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested, and the port where the seagoing ship is arrested and operating on maritime waters;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or ship operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i) Decisions of the Court.

  4. The decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested to secure the settlement of maritime claims shall take effect immediately.

  5. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested to secure the settlement of maritime claims to the port authority director for enforcement in accordance with Article 9 of this Ordinance; promptly send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or promptly send such decision to the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested; and promptly send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship being arrested is released involving foreign elements.

  Article 26. Arrest of seagoing ships for settlement of maritime claims

  1. A seagoing ship seized to secure settlement of a maritime claim which has been released or has been secured by an alternative measure shall not be seized again on the same maritime claim, except for the following cases:

  a) The total value of collateral provided is not sufficient to satisfy the obligation in case the total value of collateral provided is smaller than the value of the ship to be released;

  b) The guarantor performs the property obligation on behalf of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator fails to perform or cannot partially or fully perform the guaranteed property obligation;

  c/ The release of the ship or the cancellation of the alternative security measure shall be effected at the request of the seagoing ship arrest applicant on the grounds of plausible reasons;

  d/ The person requesting arrest of a seagoing ship cannot prevent the release of the ship or the cancellation of security measures, though necessary measures have already been applied.

  2. Not to be considered as seagoing ships to be released if the release of such ships is not decided by competent Courts or if they escape from their places of arrest, except for cases specified at Points d and dd, Clause 1, Article 22 of this Ordinance.

  3. Procedures for arresting a seagoing ship to ensure settlement of maritime claims shall comply with procedures for arresting seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime claims prescribed in this Chapter.

  Chapter 3.

              PROCEDURES FOR APPLICATION AND CANCELLATION OF THE MEASURE OF IMPOSEMENT OF TEMPORARY ARREST ON SEAGOING VESSELS

  Article 27. The measure of temporary arrest on seagoing vessels in emergency cases

  The measure of temporary arrest on seagoing vessels in emergency cases is a measure applied during the process of settlement of civil lawsuits as prescribed in Clause 13, Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Code.

  Article 28. Right to request application of the measure of temporary arrest on seagoing vessels in emergency cases

  During the process of settlement of civil lawsuits, involved parties and their lawful representatives may request the Courts which are settling such lawsuits to apply the measure of temporary arrest on seagoing vessels in emergency cases to temporarily settle urgent requests of involved parties, protect evidences, preserve the existing conditions to avoid irrecoverable damage or ensure judgment enforcement in one of the cases specified in Article 29 of this Ordinance.

  Article 29. Conditions for application of the measure of temporary arrest on seagoing vessels in emergency cases

  When there is a request for arrest of seagoing vessels as prescribed in Article 28 of this Ordinance, the Court shall decide to apply the measure of temporary arrest on seagoing vessels in emergency cases in the following cases:

  1. The seagoing ship is being arrested to secure the settlement of a maritime claim which has been brought to court by the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship;

  2. The ship owner is the person with property-related obligations in the case being settled and remains the ship owner at the time of application of the provisional emergency measure of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  3. The bareboat hirer, the time charter hirer, the voyage charterers or ship operators are persons with property-related obligations in civil cases arising from maritime claims specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance and remain the bareboat hirers, time charter hirers, voyage charterers or ship operators or ship owners at the time of application of the provisional emergency measure of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  4. Disputes are being settled in cases arising on the basis of the mortgage of such seagoing ship;

  5. Disputes are being settled in cases related to the ownership or possession right of such seagoing ship.

  Article 30. Applications for application of the provisional emergency measure of arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Persons requesting the application of the provisional emergency measure of arrest of seagoing ships shall file applications therefor.

  2. An application for application of the provisional emergency measure of arrest of seagoing ship must contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of the application;

  b) Name of the Court receiving the application;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting arrest of the seagoing ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested to be arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the hirer or operator in case the hirer or operator demands payment;

  h) Specific disputes being resolved in the lawsuit and their maximum value;

  i) Reasons for requesting application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships;

  k) Expected loss or damage that may arise due to improper request for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships;

  l) Commitment of the requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships;

  m) Number, date and contents of the decision of the Court to arrest seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime claims (if any).

  3. In case the requester for application of provisional urgent measures does not know accurately and fully the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write down what he/she knows related to such matters.

  Article 31. Submission of applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence The requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships must submit an application and enclosed documents and evidence to the Court which is handling the case.

  Article 32. Receipt of applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Immediately after receiving an application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the Court which is handling the case shall record it in the register of receipt of application.

  2. In case the Court is settling a case and has competence to decide to arrest a seagoing ship as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance, the application and enclosed documents and evidence shall be handled as follows:

  a/ To immediately hand them over to the Judge assigned to settle the case if the Court receives the application and enclosed documents and evidence before opening the court hearing;

  b/ To immediately hand them over to the trial panel which is hearing the case if the Court receives the application and enclosed documents and evidence during the time of hearing the case.

  Article 33. Consideration of applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Within three days after receiving an application for application of provisional urgent measure to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the Judge shall consider the application and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To accept the application for application of provisional urgent measure to arrest a seagoing ship if seeing that conditions are fully met for issuing a decision to apply such measure, and at the same time request the applicant to provide financial security for the application and pay the seagoing ship arrest charge within the time limit set by the Court without applying the time limits prescribed in Articles 5 and 6 of this Ordinance. When the applicant for application of provisional urgent measure to arrest a seagoing ship produces receipts and vouchers evidencing that he/she has provided financial security and paid the seagoing ship arrest charge, the Judge shall immediately issue a decision to apply such measure;

  b/ To return the application for application of provisional urgent measure to arrest a seagoing ship if seeing that conditions are not fully met for issuing a decision to apply such measure.

  2. After receiving the application for application of provisional urgent measure to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the trial panel shall immediately consider it and issue one of the decisions prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. In case of decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the Court shall issue or immediately send such decision together with the application and enclosed documents and evidence to the applicant; the Trial panel shall publicly notify the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship at the Court session and record such notification in the minutes of the Court session.

  Article 34. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints and recommendations about decisions to return applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Within three working days after receiving the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the applicant may lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court about such decision.

  2. Within three working days after receiving the written complaint about the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the Chief Justice of the Court shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  b) To cancel the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and receive back the application and enclosed documents and evidence for acceptance of the application for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. After receiving the public notice of the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship at the Court session, the applicant may lodge a complaint, the Procuracy may make a recommendation to the Trial panel about such decision and record it in the minutes of the Court session. The Trial panel shall promptly settle the complaint or recommendation and issue one of the decisions specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The decision to resolve the complaint of the Chief Judge, the decision to resolve the complaint and recommendation of the Trial Panel shall be final.

  Article 35. Decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. A decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship must contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance of the decision;

  b/ Name of the decision-issuing court;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  d/ Reason for requesting application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  e/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating on sea;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the hirer or operator of the bareboat;

  i) Judgments of the Court and legal grounds for acceptance of the application;

  k) Decisions of the Court.

  2. The decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall take effect immediately even in case of complaint or petition.

  3. The Court shall hand over two copies of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship to the port authority director for enforcement as prescribed in Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision to the requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship; send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the arrested seagoing ship has foreign elements.

  Article 36. Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints and petitions about decisions to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. The master, ship owner, ship hirer and ship operator may lodge their complaints in writing to the court president about the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship. The time limit for lodging complaints is three working days after the master receives the court's decision.

  The procuracy of the same level may make its written proposal to the court president about the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship. The time limit for making such proposal is three working days after the procuracy of the same level receives the court's decision.

  2. Within three working days after receiving the written complaint or proposal about the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the court president shall consider settling them and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  b/ To cancel the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship.

  3. At the court hearing, the settlement of complaints and proposals shall fall under the jurisdiction of the trial panel. The trial panel shall consider settling them and issue one of the decisions specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The decision to settle complaints and proposals by the court president or the trial panel is final.

  Article 37. Grounds for canceling the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship

  Decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall be annulled when conditions for application of provisional urgent measures no longer exist or at the request of persons requesting application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships.

  Article 38. Request for annulment of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. The following persons have the right to make written requests for annulment of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships:

  a/ Persons requesting application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships;

  b/ Owners, ship lessees, ship operators, masters and other persons concerned when conditions for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships no longer exist.

  2. A written request for annulment of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships must contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of making the written request;

  b/ Name of the court receiving the written request;

  c) Name and address of the person requesting cancellation of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating on maritime waters;

  dd) Number, date of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e) Reason for requesting cancellation of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship.

  Article 39. Sending written requests for cancellation of decisions to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  The person requesting cancellation of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall send a written request enclosed with accompanying documents and evidence to the court that has issued such decision.

  Article 40. Processing written requests for cancellation of decisions to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  In case the court that is handling a case receives a written request for cancellation of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, such written request shall be processed as follows:

  1. To immediately transfer to the Judge assigned to resolve the case if the Court receives the written request before opening the hearing;

  2. To immediately transfer to the Trial panel in charge of the case the Court receives the written request during the trial.

  Article 41. Decision on cancellation of decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Immediately after receiving the written request for cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships and accompanying documents and evidence, the Judge assigned or the Trial panel in charge of the case must consider issuing a decision on cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships if there are sufficient grounds. In case of refusal due to lack of grounds, the Judge or the Trial panel must notify in writing the requester of the reason for refusal.

  2. The decision on cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue the decision on cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating on maritime services;

  dd) Number, date of the decision to apply provisional emergency measures to arrest the seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  i) Reason for annulment of the decision to apply provisional emergency measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  k) Decisions of the court.

  3. The decision to annul the decision to apply provisional emergency measures to arrest the seagoing ship shall take effect immediately.

  4. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision on cancellation of the decision to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships to the port authority director for enforcement as prescribed in Article 9 of this Ordinance; immediately send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision to the requester for cancellation of the decision to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships; and immediately send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship is released after being arrested involving foreign elements.

  Article 42. Application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships in case the arbitration settles a dispute

  During the arbitration settlement of a dispute, the involved parties and their lawful representatives may request the competent Court specified in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships. Procedures for deciding to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships comply with the provisions of this Chapter.

  Chapter 4.

              PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS AND RELEASE OF SEAGOING SHIPS WHICH ARE ARRESTED FOR ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS

  Article 43. Right to request arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  Through the competent civil judgment-enforcing agency, the judgment creditor under Vietnamese law on civil judgment enforcement has the right to request the competent Court specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide to arrest seagoing ships for judgment enforcement.

  Article 44. Conditions for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. When there is a request for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement as prescribed in Article 43 of this Ordinance, the Court shall decide to arrest the seagoing ship in the following cases:

  a/ The ship owner is the judgment debtor regarding assets and remains the ship owner at the time of arrest;

  b/ The hirer of bareboat, time charter boat, voyage charterer or ship operator is the judgment debtor regarding assets in a civil case arising from a maritime claim specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance and remains the hirer of bareboat, time charter boat, voyage charterer or ship operator or the ship owner at the time of arrest;

  c/ The obligation to enforce the judgment regarding assets is secured by the mortgage of such seagoing ship;

  d/ The obligation to enforce the judgment is the return of such seagoing ship to the judgment creditor.

  2. The Court shall decide to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement only when the civil judgment enforcement agency cannot apply the measure of distraint of assets or other coercive measures to enforce the judgment, except for cases specified at Points c and d, Clause 1 of this Article or the judgment debtor is overseas and has no other assets in Vietnam.

  Article 45. Applications for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. A person requesting the arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement shall make an application enclosed with a copy of the judgment or decision of the Court or a copy of the decision of the Arbitrator.

  2. An application for arrest of a seagoing ship for enforcement of a judgment shall contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of making the application;

  b/ Name of the civil judgment-enforcing agency receiving the application;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the applicant for arrest of the seagoing ship;

  d/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be arrested and the port where it is operating on sea;

  e/ Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  f/ Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g/ Name, address and nationality of the hirer or operator in case the hirer or operator demands payment;

  h) The obligation to provide assets for enforcement of a judgment according to the court’s judgment or decision or the arbitral award;

  i) Reasons for request for arrest of seagoing ships;

  k) Expected loss or damage that may arise due to improper request for arrest of seagoing ships.

  3. In case the requester for arrest of seagoing ships for enforcement of a judgment does not know accurately and fully the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write down what he/she knows related to such issues.

  Article 46. Sending applications for arrest of seagoing ships for enforcement of judgments and enclosed documents and evidence The requester for arrest of seagoing ships for enforcement of a judgment shall send an application and enclosed documents and evidence to the civil judgment-enforcing agency competent to enforce the judgment in order to transfer them to the competent court prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 47. Receipt of applications for arrest of seagoing ships for enforcement of judgments

  1. Immediately after receiving the application for arrest of seagoing ships for enforcement of a judgment and enclosed documents and evidence, the civil judgment-enforcing agency shall record it in the register of received applications and issue a written transfer of the application and enclosed documents and evidence to the competent court for decision on arrest of seagoing ships prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance. The written transfer of the application must clearly state the reason for the failure to apply the property distraint measure or other coercive measures for enforcement of the judgment.

  2. Immediately after receiving the written transfer of the application from the civil judgment-enforcing agency together with the application for arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence, the competent court for decision on arrest of seagoing ships prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record it in the register of received applications. The court president shall immediately assign a judge to settle the application.

  Article 48. Consideration of petitions for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving the documents specified in Clause 2, Article 47 of this Ordinance, the Judge shall consider the petition and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To accept the petition for arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement if he/she finds that all conditions for issuing a decision to arrest the seagoing ship are met, request the petitioner to take financial security measures for the arrest of the seagoing ship and pay the arrest charge, except for cases where the application of financial security measures is unnecessary;

  b/ To return the petition for arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement if he/she finds that the conditions for issuing a decision to arrest the seagoing ship are not fully met or the handling of the petition does not fall under the jurisdiction of such Court.

  2. In case of returning the petition for arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement, the Court shall send the decision to the competent civil judgment enforcement agency; issue or immediately send such decision together with the petition and enclosed documents and evidence to the petitioner.

  Article 49. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints and recommendations about decisions to return petitions for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. Within twenty four hours after receiving the decision to return the petition for arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement, the petitioner may lodge a complaint or the competent civil judgment enforcement agency may make a recommendation to the Chief Justice of the Court about such decision. Complaints and recommendations must be made in writing.

  2. Within twenty four hours after receiving the written complaint or recommendation about the decision to return the petition for arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement and enclosed documents and evidence, the Chief Justice of the Court shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision to return the written request for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement;

  b/ To cancel the decision to return the written request for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement and receive back the request together with enclosed documents and evidence for handling the arrest of seagoing ships.

  3. The Chief Justice's decision on settlement of complaints and petitions is final.

  Article 50. Decision to arrest seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. The judge shall immediately issue a decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement when the requester produces vouchers and/or documents evidencing his/her application of financial security measures for the request for arrest of seagoing ship specified in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, except for cases where financial security measures are not required and the seagoing ship arrest fee has been paid as prescribed in Article 6 of this Ordinance.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement must contain the following principal details:

  a/ Date of issuance of the decision;

  b/ Name of the court issuing the decision;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting arrest of the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  d) Reason for request for arrest of the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  dd) Name of civil judgment enforcement authority competent to enforce the judgment;

  e) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest and the port where it is operating;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  i) Name, address and nationality of the hirer and operator;

  k) Judgments of the Court and legal grounds for acceptance of the petition;

  l) Decisions of Courts.

  3. The decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaint or petition.

  4. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement to the port authority director for enforcement under Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision immediately to the same-level Procuracy; issue or send such decision immediately to the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement and the competent civil judgment enforcement agency; and send such decision immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship to be arrested involves foreign elements.

  Article 51. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints, recommendations about decisions to arrest seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. The ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator and the captain of the ship may lodge a written complaint with the court president about the decision to arrest the sea-going ship for judgment execution. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty eight hours from the moment the captain receives the court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may make written proposal to the Chief Justice of the Court on the decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement. The time limit for making such proposal is forty eight hours after the Procuracy of the same level receives the Court's decision.

  2. Within forty eight hours after receiving a written complaint or petition against a decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement, the court president shall consider and settle it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  b) To cancel the decision to arrest the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement.

  3. The decision to settle complaints and petitions by the court president is final.

  Article 52. Grounds for releasing seagoing ships being arrested for judgment enforcement

  1. A seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall be released immediately when there is one of the following grounds:

  a/ After the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator who is the judgment debtor has taken substitute security measures or has fulfilled his/her obligation to enforce the judgment;

  b/ The property obligation of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator has been guaranteed by another person for fulfillment, or there is a written commitment from a reputable insurance organization;

  c/ At the request of the person who has requested the arrest of the seagoing ship.

  2. Substitute security measures shall be agreed upon by the parties. In case the parties fail to reach agreement on the level and form of substitute security measures, the court shall decide on such level and form but they must not exceed the value of the arrested seagoing ship or the property obligation which is the ground for arrest of the seagoing ship in case the property obligation is smaller than the value of the seagoing ship.

  Article 53. Request for release of seagoing ships being arrested for judgment enforcement

  1. When there is one of the grounds prescribed in Clause 1, Article 52 of this Ordinance, the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator, the captain and other relevant persons must make a written request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement.

  2. A written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested shall contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of making the request;

  b/ Name of the court receiving the request;

  c/ Name and address of the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship being arrested operates on sea;

  e/ Serial number and date of the decision arresting the seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e) Reasons for request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested.

  Article 54. Sending written requests for release of seagoing ships being arrested for judgment enforcement

  The petitioner for release of a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall send documents and accompanying evidence to the court that has issued the decision on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  Article 55. Decision to release seagoing ships being arrested for enforcement of judgments

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement, the court president shall assign a judge to settle the release of such seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a written request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider and issue a decision to release the seagoing ship if finding there are grounds. In case of refusal without grounds, the judge shall notify in writing the requester of such refusal, clearly stating the reason for the refusal.

  3. A decision to release a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision on release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being released; port where the seagoing ship is released;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i) Decisions of the Court.

  4. The decision to release a seagoing ship being arrested for enforcement of a judgment shall take effect immediately.

  5. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision to release a seagoing ship being arrested for enforcement of a judgment to the Director of the Port Department for enforcement in accordance with Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision immediately to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision immediately to the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested for enforcement of a judgment and the competent civil judgment-enforcing agency; and send such decision immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship is released involving foreign elements.

  Chapter 5.

   PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS AND RELEASE OF SEAGOING SHIPS BEING ARRESTED FOR LEGAL ASSISTANCE

  Section 1. JUDICIAL CONSULTATION WITH FOREIGN COURTS FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS

  Article 56. Legal assistance with foreign courts for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. In the course of settlement of a lawsuit at a court or a dispute at an arbitrator, if there is a request for seagoing ship custody, the Vietnamese competent court specified in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall provide legal assistance to a competent foreign court for arrest of seagoing ship.

  2. A Vietnamese court shall provide legal assistance to a foreign court for arrest of seagoing ship in accordance with Vietnamese law and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party or on the principle of reciprocity.

  Article 57. Judicial entrustment documents A judicial entrustment document on arrest of a seagoing ship shall contain the following principal contents:

  1. Date and place of making the judicial entrustment document;

  2. Name and address of the Vietnamese Court entrusting the judicial assistance;

  3. Name and address of the foreign Court executing the judicial entrustment;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  5. Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  6. Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  7. Reason for the judicial entrustment of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  8. Time limit for arrest of seagoing ships;

  9. Persons responsible for damage or loss caused due to improper requests for arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 58. Procedures for judicial entrustment of arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The competent Vietnamese Court making judicial entrustment to the competent foreign Court for arrest of seagoing ships shall make an entrustment dossier and send it to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam. An entrustment dossier must contain the following documents:

  a/ A written request of the competent Vietnamese Court for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships;

  b/ A written entrustment for arrest of seagoing ships;

  c/ Other papers at the request of the competent Court of the entrusted country.

  2. Within ten working days after receiving the dossier of judicial entrustment for arrest of seagoing ships, the Ministry of Justice shall record in the judicial entrustment book, check the validity of the dossier and transfer it to the competent foreign Court under the provisions of the international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the foreign country are members or through the diplomatic channel. In case the dossier is invalid, the Ministry of Justice shall return it to the Court that has made the dossier and clearly state the reason therefor.

  3. Within five working days after receiving a written notice of the results of execution of judicial request from a competent foreign Court, the Ministry of Justice shall forward such notice to the Vietnamese Court which has sent the request for arrest of seagoing ships.

  Section 2. EXECUTION OF JUDICIAL REQUESTS FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS FROM FOREIGN COURTS

  Article 59. Principles of mutual legal assistance in arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Mutual legal assistance in arrest of seagoing ships between Vietnamese Courts and foreign Courts shall be executed on the principles of respect for independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of each country, non-interference in internal affairs of each other, equality and mutual benefit, compliance with international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory, and compliance with Vietnamese law.

  2. In case the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and a foreign country have not yet signed or acceded to an international treaty containing provisions on mutual legal assistance in arrest of seagoing ships, mutual legal assistance in arrest of seagoing ships may be accepted by Vietnamese Courts on the principle of reciprocity, but must not contravene Vietnamese law, international law and international practice.

  Article 60. Principles of execution of judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Vietnamese Courts shall execute judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships from foreign Courts under provisions of international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory or on the principle of reciprocity.

  2. Vietnamese Courts shall not accept execution of judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships from foreign Courts in the following cases:

  a/ Performance of judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships which infringe upon Vietnam’s sovereignty or threaten Vietnam’s security;

  b/ Performance of judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships beyond the jurisdiction of Vietnamese Courts.

  Article 61. Procedures for judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The foreign Court’s judicial request for arrest of seagoing ship to be sent to the Vietnamese Court shall be made in writing and sent to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam under the provisions of the international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party or Vietnamese law.

  2. Upon receiving the written judicial request for arrest of seagoing ship, the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam shall immediately forward it to the Vietnamese Court competent to decide on arrest of seagoing ship specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 62. Written judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships

  A written judicial request for arrest of seagoing ship must contain the following principal contents:

  1. Date, month, year and place of making the written judicial request;

  2. Name and address of the foreign judicial requesting authority;

  3. Name and address of the Vietnamese judicial requesting authority;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel requested for arrest; port where the seagoing vessel is operating;

  5. Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  6. Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  7. Reason for the request for judicial assistance in seagoing vessel arrest;

  8. Time limit for seagoing vessel arrest;

  9. Person responsible for damage caused by improper request for seagoing vessel arrest.

  Article 63. Receipt of legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Upon receiving a written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence, the Vietnamese Ministry of Justice shall record it in the book and send a written notification of such request together with enclosed documents and evidence to the competent Court which shall decide on the arrest of seagoing ships specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  2. Upon receiving a written request for judicial assistance in arresting a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the competent court to decide on arrest of seagoing ships specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record it in the book of receipt. The court president shall immediately assign a judge to settle the written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ship.

  Article 64. Examination of legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving a written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider issuing one of the following decisions:

  a) Accept a written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships if it is deemed to ensure the principles of mutual legal assistance and execution of requests for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships;

  b) Return the legal mandate document on arrest of seagoing ships if it is deemed to violate the principles of mutual legal assistance or the principles for execution of legal mandates on arrest of seagoing ships or the settlement of legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships does not fall within the jurisdiction of such Court.

  2. In case of decision to return a letter of request for arrest of seagoing ships, the Court shall immediately send such decision together with the letter of request for arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam for notification to the foreign Court.

  Article 65. Decision to arrest a seagoing ship under judicial request of a foreign Court

  1. The judge shall issue a decision to arrest a seagoing ship under judicial request of a foreign Court immediately after the seagoing ship arrest requester has presented the bill and documentary evidence proving that he/she has taken financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, and has paid the fee for seagoing ship arrest specified in Article 6 of this Ordinance in Vietnam, except where otherwise provided by an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing ship under judicial request of a foreign Court must contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance of the decision;

  b/ Name of the Vietnamese Court issuing the decision;

  c/ Name of the foreign Court making the judicial request;

  d/ Name, address and nationality of the seagoing ship arrest requester under the foreign Court's judicial request;

  e/ Reason for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship;

  e) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the ship is operating on sea;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  i) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  k) Judgments of the Court and legal grounds for accepting the written request for judicial assistance in seagoing ship arrest;

  l) Decisions of the Court.

  3. The decision to arrest a seagoing ship under judicial assistance of a foreign Court shall take effect immediately even if there are complaints or petitions.

  4. The Court shall hand over two copies of the decision to arrest a seagoing ship under judicial assistance of a foreign Court to the port authority director for execution according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; and send such decision to the Ministry of Justice and the Procuracy of the same level.

  Article 66. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints and recommendations about decisions on arrest of seagoing ships under judicial requests of foreign Courts

  1. The master, ship owner, ship hirer or ship operator may lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court about the decision to arrest a seagoing ship under a judicial request of a foreign Court. The time limit for lodging a complaint is 48 hours from the moment the master receives the Court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may file a written recommendation with the Chief Justice of the Court about the decision to arrest a seagoing ship under a judicial request of a foreign Court. The time limit for filing a recommendation is 48 hours from the moment the Procuracy of the same level receives the Court's decision.

  2. Within 48 hours after receiving the written complaint or recommendation specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Chief Justice of the Court shall consider settling it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision to arrest the seagoing ship under a judicial request of a foreign Court.

  b/ To cancel the decision to arrest the seagoing ship under a judicial request of a foreign Court.

  3. The decision to settle the complaint or recommendation of the Chief Justice of the Court is final.

  Article 67. Grounds for release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial requests A seagoing ship being arrested under a judicial request shall be immediately released when there is one of the following grounds:

  1. Decision on arrest of seagoing ship is annulled.

  2. Time limit for arrest of seagoing ship under court decision expires;

  3. At request of foreign court which has entrusted to arrest seagoing ship;

  Article 68. Request for release of seagoing ship being arrested under judicial entrustment In case specified in Clause 3, Article 67 of this Ordinance, foreign court must make written request for release of seagoing ship. Written request for release of seagoing ship being arrested under judicial entrustment must contain the following principal contents:

  1. Date and place of making request;

  2. Name and address of foreign court requesting release of seagoing ship being arrested;

  3. Name of Vietnamese court receiving written request;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of seagoing ship being arrested; port where seagoing ship being arrested operates maritime traffic;

  5. Serial number and date of the decision to arrest the seagoing ship and the Court issuing such decision;

  6. Reasons for requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested.

  Article 69. Sending written requests for the release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandates Foreign Courts shall send written requests for the release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandates together with enclosed documents and evidence to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam for the latter to forward them to the Court issuing the decision to arrest such seagoing ships.

  Article 70. Decisions on the release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandates

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for the release of a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate, the Chief Justice of the Court shall assign a Judge to settle the matter of the release of such seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving the written request for the release of a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate and enclosed documents and evidence, the assigned Judge shall consider and issue a decision to release the seagoing ship if he/she finds there are grounds for such decision. In case of refusal for no grounds, the Judge shall notify in writing the requester of the refusal, clearly stating the reason(s) for the refusal.

  3. A decision to release a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate must contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Vietnamese Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be released; port where the seagoing ship being arrested is released;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or ship operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for releasing the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i) Decisions of the Court.

  4. Decisions on release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial requests of foreign Courts shall take immediate effect.

  5. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision on release of seagoing ship being arrested under judicial request of a foreign Court to the port authority for implementation as prescribed in Article 9 of this Ordinance and immediately send such decision to the Ministry of Justice and the Procuracy of the same level.

  Chapter 6.

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 71. Implementation effect

  This Ordinance takes effect on July 1, 2009.

  Article 72. Implementation guidance

  The Government, the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall, within their respective functions and tasks, guide the implementation of this Ordinance.
