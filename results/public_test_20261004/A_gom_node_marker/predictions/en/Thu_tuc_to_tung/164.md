              Law No. 13/2012/QH13                           Hanoi, June 20, 2012



                                                       LAW

                                              JUDICIAL ASSESSMENT

  Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on Judicial Expertise.

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Law provides for judicial experts; judicial assessment organizations; subject-matter judicial experts and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations; judicial assessment activities; judicial assessment costs, benefits and policies in judicial assessment activities and responsibilities of regulatory agencies for judicial assessment organizations and activities.

  Article 2. Interpretation of terms

  In this Law, the terms below are construed as follows:

  1. Judicial assessment means a judicial expert uses his/her knowledge, scientific, technical and professional means and methods to make professional conclusions on matters related to investigation, prosecution, adjudication and enforcement of criminal judgments, settlement of civil cases and administrative cases at the request of the presiding agency or person or at the request of the assessment requester according to regulations of this Law.

  2. Assessment requesters include presiding agencies and persons.

  3. Assessment requesters are persons who have the right to request judicial assessment themselves after requesting the presiding agency or person to request judicial assessment but their requests are rejected. Persons who have the right to request judicial assessment themselves include involved parties in civil cases and administrative cases, civil plaintiffs, civil defendants, persons with relevant interests and obligations in criminal cases or their lawful representatives, except for cases where the request for judicial assessment is related to determination of criminal liability of suspects or defendants.

  4. Judicial assessment organizations and individuals include judicial experts, subject-matter judicial experts, public judicial assessment organizations, non-public judicial assessment organizations and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations.

  5. Judicial assessors include public judicial assessors and subject-matter judicial assessors.

  6. Public judicial assessor is a person who meets the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law and is appointed by a competent authority to conduct judicial assessment.

  7. Subject-matter judicial assessor is a person who meets the criteria specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 Article 18 and Article 20 of this Law and is solicited or requested for judicial assessment.

  8. Subject-matter judicial assessment organization is an agency or organization that meets the criteria specified in Article 19 and Article 20 of this Law and is solicited or requested for judicial assessment.

  Article 3. Principles of conducting judicial assessment

  1. Compliance with laws and professional standards.

  2. Honesty, accuracy, objectivity, impartiality, and timeliness.

  3. Only making professional conclusions on issues within the scope of request.

  4. To take legal responsibility for assessment conclusions.

  Article 4. Responsibilities of individuals and organizations for judicial assessment activities

  1. Individuals and organizations solicited or requested to conduct judicial assessment shall be responsible for receiving and conducting judicial assessment in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  2. Other individuals and organizations shall be responsible for enabling judicial assessors to conduct judicial assessment in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  Article 5. State policies on judicial assessment activities

  1. The State shall invest in and develop public judicial assessment system in fields where there is a large and regular demand for judicial assessment to meet requirements of proceedings; adopt preferential policies to enable non-public judicial assessment organizations to develop.

  2. The State shall adopt policies to prioritize training and professional development for judicial assessors.

  Article 6. Prohibited acts

  1. Refusing to make judicial assessment conclusions without a legitimate reason.

  2. Intentionally making false judicial assessment conclusions.

  3. Intentionally prolonging the time for conducting judicial assessment.

  4. Abusing the conduct of judicial assessment for personal gain.

  5. Disclosing information secrets known while conducting judicial assessment.

  6. Inciting or forcing judicial assessors to make false judicial assessment conclusions.

  7. Interfering with or obstructing judicial assessors’ conduct of judicial assessment.

  Chapter II

                                           JUDICIAL EXPERTISE CONSULTANT

  Article 7. Criteria for appointment of judicial experts

  1. A Vietnamese citizen residing in Vietnam who satisfies the following criteria may be considered and appointed as a judicial expert:

  a) Having good health and moral qualities;

  b) Holding a university degree or higher and having at least 5 years of professional experience in the field of training.

  In cases where the person proposed to be appointed as a forensic examiner, forensic psychiatric examiner, or criminal technique examiner has directly assisted in expert examination activities at a forensic examination organization, forensic psychiatric examination organization, or criminal technique examination organization, the period of professional experience must be at least 3 years;

  c) For the person proposed to be appointed as a judicial expert in the fields of forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry, and criminal techniques, he/she must have a certificate of training or refresher training in expertise.

  2. Persons falling into one of the following cases shall not be appointed as judicial experts:

  a) Lacks civil act capacity or has limited civil act capacity;

  b) Being examined for penal liability; having been convicted but not yet had his/her conviction against unintentional crime or less serious intentional crime expunged; having been convicted of serious crime, very serious crime or extremely serious intentional crime;

  c) Being subject to administrative handling measure of education at commune, ward or township, being sent to compulsory rehabilitation center or compulsory educational institution.

  3. Ministers and Heads of ministerial-level agencies shall elaborate on clause 1 of this Article for judicial experts in fields under their management after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  Article 8. Dossiers of request for appointment of judicial experts

  1. Written request for appointment of judicial expert.

  2. Copy of university degree or higher in the field proposed for appointment.

  3. Curriculum vitae and judicial record card.

  4. Written certification of the actual period of professional practice of the agency or organization where the proposed appointee works.

  5. Certificate of training or refresher course in judicial assessment skills for the proposed appointee as a judicial assessor in forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry and criminal techniques.

  6. Other documents proving that the proposed appointee meets the criteria prescribed by the Minister or Head of the ministerial-level agency competent to manage the field of assessment.

  Article 9. Authority and procedures for appointment of judicial assessors

  1. The Minister of Health shall appoint forensic examiners and forensic psychiatrists working at central authorities.

  The Minister of Public Security shall appoint criminal technique assessors working at central authorities.

  Ministers and Heads of ministerial-level agencies shall appoint judicial assessors working in other fields at central authorities under their management.

  Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally affiliated cities (hereinafter referred to as provincial level) shall appoint local judicial assessors.

  2. The Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Public Security shall select persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law to propose the Minister of Health to appoint forensic examiners under their management.

  The Ministry of National Defense shall select persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law to propose the Minister of Public Security to appoint criminal technical experts under their management.

  Heads of units affiliated to ministries or ministerial agencies assigned to manage judicial assessment activities shall select persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law to propose ministers or heads of ministerial agencies to appoint judicial experts in the field of assessment under their management.

  Heads of specialized agencies affiliated to provincial People’s Committees managing the field of judicial assessment shall take charge and cooperate with directors of Departments of Justice in selecting persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law, receive applications from persons applying for appointment as judicial experts specified in Article 8 of this Law, and propose Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees to appoint judicial experts in their provinces.

  Within 20 days from the date of receipt of valid applications, ministers, heads of ministerial agencies, and chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees shall decide on the appointment of judicial experts. In case of refusal, they must notify in writing to the applicants and provide explanation.

  3. Ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People’s Committees shall prepare and publish lists of judicial experts on their web portals, and concurrently send them to the Ministry of Justice to compile a general list of judicial experts.

  Article 10. Removal of judicial experts

  1. Cases subject to removal of judicial experts:

  a) Failing to meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law;

  b) Falling into one of the cases specified in Clause 2 Article 7 of this Law;

  c) Being subject to disciplinary measures at the level of reprimand or more severe, or administrative penalties for intentional violations of laws on judicial assessment;

  d) Committing one of the acts specified in Article 6 of this Law;

  dd) At the request of the judicial assessor who is a civil servant, public employee, army officer, people’s police officer, professional serviceman, or national defense worker and has decided to resign for retirement or early retirement.

  2. The application for dismissal of a judicial assessor includes:

  a) A written request for dismissal of the judicial assessor from the agency or organization that proposed the appointment;

  b) Documents and papers proving that the judicial assessor falls into one of the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. The Minister of Public Security and the Minister of National Defense shall consider proposing the Minister of Health to dismiss forensic examiners under their management.

  The Minister of National Defense shall consider proposing the Minister of Public Security to dismiss criminal technical experts under their management.

  Ministers and Heads of ministerial agencies shall dismiss judicial experts operating at central authorities in areas under their management at the request of Heads of units affiliated to ministries or ministerial agencies assigned to manage judicial expertise activities.

  Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees shall dismiss local judicial experts at the request of heads of specialized agencies of People’s Committees after heads of specialized agencies reach agreement with Directors of Departments of Justice.

  4. Within 10 days from the date on which the valid application is received, Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees shall consider deciding to dismiss judicial experts and adjust the list of judicial experts on the web portals of ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People’s Committees, and concurrently send it to the Ministry of Justice for adjustment to the general list of judicial experts.

  Article 11. Rights and obligations of judicial experts

  1. Conduct judicial expertise as per orders or requests of ordering parties or requesting parties or as per assignments by agencies or organizations receiving such orders or requests.

  2. Refuse to conduct judicial expertise if contents subject to expertise exceed their professional capacity; subjects of expertise or relevant documents provided are insufficient or invalid for making expert conclusions; time limit for conducting judicial expertise is not sufficient or there are other legitimate reasons. In case of refusal to conduct judicial expertise, within 5 working days from the date on which decisions on orders or requests for judicial expertise are received, notify ordering parties or requesting parties in writing and clearly state reasons.

  3. Participate in training courses on judicial expertise and legal knowledge.

  4. Establish a judicial expertise office when fully satisfying the conditions specified in Article 15 of this Law.

  5. Establish and join a private judicial expert association in accordance with the law on associations.

  6. Enjoy benefits and policies prescribed by this Law and other relevant laws.

  7. Exercise rights and perform obligations as prescribed in Article 23 and Clause 1 Article 34 of this Law.

  Chapter III

                                       JUDICIAL EXPERTISE ORGANIZATIONS

  Section 1. PUBLIC JUDICIAL EXPERTISE ORGANIZATIONS

  Article 12. Public judicial assessment organizations

  1. Public judicial assessment organizations shall be established by competent state authorities in the fields of forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry and criminal techniques.

  Where necessary, Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies and Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees shall consider deciding on establishment or propose establishment of public judicial assessment organizations in other fields after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  2. Public judicial assessment organizations in the field of forensic medicine include:

  a) National Forensic Institute affiliated to the Ministry of Health;

  b) Provincial Forensic Centers;

  c) Military Forensic Institute affiliated to the Ministry of National Defense;

  d) Forensic Assessment Center affiliated to Institute of Criminal Science, the Ministry of Public Security.

  3. Public forensic psychiatric assessment organizations include:

  a) Central Forensic Psychiatry Institute affiliated to the Ministry of Health;

  b) Regional Forensic Psychiatry Centers affiliated to the Ministry of Health.

  Based on the requirements for forensic psychiatric assessment of legal proceedings and the actual conditions of regions and zones nationwide, the Minister of Health shall consider and decide the establishment of regional forensic psychiatry centers after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  4. Public forensic criminalistics assessment organizations include:

  a) Institute of Criminal Science affiliated to the Ministry of Public Security;

  b) Department of Forensic Criminalistics affiliated to provincial police authorities;

  c) Department of Forensic Criminalistics Assessment affiliated to the Ministry of National Defense.

  5. Based on local needs and practical conditions, the Department of Criminal Techniques affiliated to the provincial police authority shall have forensic examiner(s) perform forensic examination of death.

  6. Public judicial assessment organizations shall have their own seals and accounts as prescribed by law.

  7. The Government shall elaborate on the functions, tasks, organizational structure, working regime of public judicial assessment organizations specified in this Article.

  Article 13. Assurance of material facilities for public judicial assessment organizations

  1. Public judicial assessment organizations shall be assured by the State of material facilities, funding, equipment, means and other necessary conditions for the performance of judicial assessment.

  2. Funding for operation of public judicial assessment organizations shall be ensured from the state budget and other sources as prescribed by law.

  3. The Ministry of Health shall provide regulations on material facility, equipment and means requirements for public judicial assessment organizations in the fields of forensic medicine and forensic psychiatry.

  The Ministry of Public Security shall provide regulations on material facility, equipment and means requirements for public judicial assessment organizations in the field of criminal techniques.

  Section 2. ESTABLISHMENT OF PRIVATE JUDICIAL EXPERTISE FACILITIES

  Article 14. Judicial expertise offices

  1. Judicial expertise office means a private judicial expertise organization established in the fields of finance, banking, construction, antiques, relics and copyright.

  2. A judicial expertise office established by one judicial expert shall be organized and operate as a sole proprietorship. A judicial expertise office established by two or more judicial experts shall be organized and operate as a partnership.

  The legal representative of a judicial expertise office is its head. The head of a judicial expertise office must be a judicial expert.

  Article 15. Conditions for establishment of judicial expertise offices

  1. A judicial expert may establish a judicial expertise office when fully satisfying the following conditions:

  a) Having worked as a judicial expert for at least 5 years in the field proposed for establishment of the office;

  b) The establishment plan is made in accordance with Point d Clause 2 Article 16 hereof.

  2. Officials, public officials, public employees, army officers, people’s police officers, professional servicemen, national defense workers are not allowed to establish judicial assessment offices.

  Article 16. Licensing of establishment of judicial assessment offices

  1. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees where head offices are located shall consider and decide on licensing of establishment of judicial assessment offices at request of directors of Departments of Justice.

  2. Judicial assessors applying for establishment of judicial assessment offices shall submit application for establishment to Departments of Justice. Application consists of:

  a) Application for establishment;

  b) Copies of decisions on appointment of judicial assessors;

  c) Draft regulations on organization and operation of judicial assessment offices;

  d) The plan for establishment of a judicial assessment office must clearly state its establishment purposes; expected name, personnel and location of head office; conditions regarding facilities, equipment and means for judicial assessment in accordance with regulations of ministries or ministerial agencies specialized in the field of assessment and implementation plan.

  3. Within 30 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application, the Director of the Department of Justice shall review and appraise the application for establishment of a judicial assessment office, reach agreement with the head of the specialized agency under the management of the provincial People’s Committee in charge of judicial assessment, and submit it to the President of the provincial People’s Committee for consideration and decision.

  Within 15 days from the date on which the Department of Justice submits the application, the President of People’s Committee of province shall consider and decide to permit the establishment of the judicial assessment office. In case of refusal, a written notice stating reasons must be provided. The applicant may file a complaint or lawsuit according to regulations of law.

  Article 17. Registration of operation of judicial assessment offices

  1. Within 01 year from the date on which Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees issue decision permitting establishment, judicial assessment offices shall register their operation at Departments of Justice.

  1 year after a provincial-level People’s Committee president issues a decision permitting establishment of a judicial assessment office but such office fails to register its operation, the decision permitting establishment of the judicial assessment office shall cease to be effective.

  2. The judicial assessment office shall submit an application for operation registration to the Department of Justice. The application includes:

  a) Application for operation registration;

  b) Regulations on organization and operation of the judicial assessment office;

  c) Documents proving eligibility to ensure operation of the judicial assessment office under the establishment scheme specified in Point d Clause 2 Article 16 hereof;

  d) Copies of decisions permitting establishment of judicial assessment offices.

  3. Within 30 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application, the Department of Justice shall take charge and cooperate with the specialized agency of the provincial People’s Committee in charge of judicial assessment in inspecting the satisfaction of conditions specified in the establishment plan under Point d Clause 2 Article 16 of this Law and issue an operation registration certificate; in case of refusal, a written notification stating the reasons must be provided, and at the same time reported to the President of the provincial People’s Committee for consideration and decision on revocation of the establishment permission decision. The person subject to refusal has the right to file a complaint or lawsuit according to law.

  4. The judicial assessment office shall commence its operation from the date of issuance of the certificate of registration.

  Chapter IV

   JURIDICAL CONSULTANCY CLIENTS, JURIDICAL CONSULTANCY ORGANIZATIONS

  Article 18. Case-specific judicial assessors

  1. Vietnamese citizens permanently residing in Vietnam who meet the following criteria may be selected as subject-matter judicial experts:

  a) Have good health and moral qualities;

  b) Hold at least a university degree and have at least 5 years of practical experience in the field of training.

  2. In cases where a person does not hold a university degree but has in-depth knowledge and extensive practical experience in the field to be expertized, he/she may be selected as a subject-matter judicial expert.

  3. Subject-matter judicial experts shall conduct expert examinations upon request or order in accordance with this Law. Subject-matter judicial experts have the rights and obligations prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7 Article 11 of this Law.

  Article 19. Subject-matter judicial expertise service providers

  1. A subject-matter judicial expertise service provider must satisfy the following conditions:

  a) Have legal entity status;

  b) Having professional activities appropriate to the contents solicited or requested for judicial assessment;

  c) Having conditions regarding professional officers and physical foundations to ensure the performance of judicial assessment.

  2. Ad hoc judicial assessment organizations shall conduct judicial assessment at the request of the ordering party in accordance with this Law. The head of such organization shall be responsible for receiving and assigning persons to conduct judicial assessment.

  3. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies, and specialized agencies of provincial People’s Committees shall conduct judicial assessment only at the request of the ordering party.

  Article 20. Preparation and publication of lists of ad hoc judicial assessors and ad hoc judicial assessment organizations

  1. The Ministry of Construction, the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Information and Communications, the Ministry of Planning and Investment, the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment, the Ministry of Transport, the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, Vietnam State Bank, other ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People’s Committees shall select, prepare, and publish annually lists of ad hoc judicial assessors and ad hoc judicial assessment organizations in the fields under their management to meet the requirements of judicial assessment in legal proceedings.

  The list, enclosed with information on the judicial assessment specialization, experience, and capacity of the ad hoc judicial assessor or ad hoc judicial assessment organization, shall be posted on the web portal of the ministry, ministerial agency, or provincial People’s Committee and concurrently sent to the Ministry of Justice for compilation of a general list.

  2. In special cases, the ordering party may solicit a qualified individual or specialized organization not included in the published list to conduct judicial assessment, provided that the reason must be clearly stated.

  Upon request of presiding authorities, presiding officers, ministries, ministerial agencies and specialized agencies of provincial People’s Committees managing fields of judicial assessment, they shall be responsible for introducing individuals or organizations eligible to conduct judicial assessment outside of the published list.

  Chapter V

                                     JUDICIAL ASSESSMENT ACTIVITIES

  Article 21. Rights and obligations of ordering parties

  1. Ordering parties have the rights:

  a) To order individuals or organizations specified in clause 4 Article 2 of this Law to conduct judicial assessment;

  b) To request individuals or organizations specified in point a clause 1 of this Article to provide judicial assessment conclusions in accordance with the requested contents and within the prescribed time limit;

  c) To request individuals or organizations conducting judicial assessment to explain their judicial assessment conclusions.

  2. The ordering party shall have the following obligations:

  a) Select an organization or individual to conduct judicial assessment in accordance with the nature and requirements of the case subject to judicial assessment;

  b) Issue a decision on ordering judicial assessment in writing;

  c) Provide information and documents related to the subject matter of judicial assessment at the request of the organization or individual conducting judicial assessment;

  d) Advance judicial assessment costs when ordering judicial assessment; pay promptly and fully judicial assessment costs to the organization or individual conducting judicial assessment upon receipt of the judicial assessment conclusion;

  dd) Ensure the safety of the judicial assessor during the process of conducting judicial assessment or when participating in proceedings as a judicial assessor.

  Article 22. Rights and obligations of persons requesting judicial assessment

  1. Persons requesting judicial assessment may send written requests to procedural-conducting agencies or persons to order judicial assessment. If the procedural-conducting agency or person does not accept the request, it must notify such in writing within 7 days. Upon expiry of such time limit or from the date of receipt of the notice of refusal to order judicial assessment, the requesting party may independently request judicial assessment.

  2. The requesting party shall have the rights:

  a) Request the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider to provide the judicial assessment conclusion within the time limit agreed upon and in accordance with the contents requested;

  b) Request the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider to explain the judicial assessment conclusion;

  c) Request the Court to summon the judicial assessor who has conducted the judicial assessment to participate in the hearing to explain and present the judicial assessment conclusion;

  d) Request the presiding agency or presiding officer to order a re-assessment; request an additional assessment as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 29 of this Law.

  3. The requesting party shall have the obligations:

  a) Provide information and documents related to the subject matter of judicial assessment at the request of the judicial assessor and take responsibility for the accuracy of the information and documents provided;

  b) Pay an advance on judicial assessment costs when requesting a judicial assessment; pay promptly and fully the judicial assessment costs to the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider when receiving the judicial assessment conclusion.

  4. The person requesting judicial assessment may only exercise the right to request judicial assessment before the Court issues a decision to bring the case to first-instance trial.

  Article 23. Rights and obligations of judicial assessors when conducting judicial assessment

  1. Judicial assessors have the rights:

  a) To select necessary and appropriate methods for conducting judicial assessment according to the contents requested for assessment;

  b) To use results of additional experiments or tests or professional conclusions conducted by other individuals or organizations to serve the judicial assessment;

  c) To independently make judicial assessment conclusions.

  2. Judicial assessors have the obligations:

  a) To comply with the principles of conducting judicial assessment;

  b) Conduct assessment in accordance with the contents of the request for assessment;

  c) Conduct and provide assessment conclusions within the prescribed time limit; in cases where additional time is necessary for conducting assessment, promptly notify the ordering party or requesting party;

  d) Prepare an assessment file;

  dd) Preserve the specimens of assessment and documents related to the assessment case;

  e) Not to notify the results of assessment to others, unless it is agreed in writing by the ordering party or requesting party;

  g) Take personal responsibility for the assessment conclusions issued. In cases where a false conclusion is intentionally made, causing damage to individuals or organizations, compensation or indemnity must be provided in accordance with the law.

  3. In addition to the rights and obligations specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, judicial assessors have other rights and obligations as prescribed by the law on proceedings.

  Article 24. Rights and obligations of organizations solicited or requested to conduct judicial assessment

  1. Organizations solicited or requested for judicial assessment shall have the rights:

  a) To request persons soliciting or requesting judicial assessment to provide information and documents necessary for the judicial assessment;

  b) To refuse to conduct judicial assessment if they do not have sufficient conditions necessary for conducting the judicial assessment;

  c) To receive advances on judicial assessment expenses upon receiving solicitation or requests for judicial assessment; to be paid in a timely and full manner for judicial assessment expenses when returning judicial assessment results.

  2. Organizations solicited or requested for judicial assessment shall have the obligations:

  a) To receive and assign persons with professional qualifications appropriate to the contents of the solicitation or request for judicial assessment within their organizations to conduct the judicial assessment and take responsibility for such persons’ professional qualifications; to assign persons responsible for coordinating the conduct of the judicial assessment in cases where multiple persons are required to conduct the judicial assessment;

  b) To ensure equipment, facilities and other necessary conditions for conducting the judicial assessment;

  c) To pay compensation for damage caused by the judicial assessor assigned by them who intentionally made a false conclusion, causing damage to individuals or organizations;

  d) Notify the ordering party or requesting party in writing within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the decision on ordering or requesting assessment, clearly stating the reasons in case of refusal to accept the order or request for assessment.

  Article 25. Request for judicial assessment

  1. The ordering party shall issue a decision on ordering judicial assessment in writing and send it together with the subject matter of assessment and relevant documents and items (if any) related to the individual or organization conducting the assessment.

  2. A decision to request expertise must contain the following contents:

  a) Name of the requesting authority; full name of the person competent to request the assessment;

  b) Name of the organization; full name of the person requested for expertise;

  c) Name and characteristics of the subject matter to be appraised;

  d) Name of relevant documents or comparison samples enclosed (if any);

  d) Contents of the request for judicial assessment;

  e) Date and time of the request for judicial assessment and deadline for the provision of the judicial assessment conclusion.

  3. In case of requesting an additional or repeated judicial assessment, the decision to request the judicial assessment must clearly state that it is an additional or repeated judicial assessment.

  Article 26. Request for judicial assessment in civil cases, administrative cases and criminal cases

  1. The person requesting the judicial assessment shall send a written request for judicial assessment together with the subject matter of the judicial assessment, relevant documents and objects (if any) and copies of papers proving that he/she is involved party in the civil case, administrative case, civil plaintiff, civil defendant, person with related rights and obligations in the criminal case or his/her lawful representative to the individual or organization conducting the judicial assessment.

  2. A written request for judicial assessment must contain the following contents:

  a) Name of the organization or full name of the person requesting the judicial assessment;

  b) Contents of the request for judicial assessment;

  c) Name and characteristics of the object of assessment;

  d) Name of relevant documents or comparison samples enclosed (if any);

  dd) Date of request for expertise and time limit for issuing the expertise conclusion;

  e) Signature and full name of the requesting person.

  Article 27. Delivery of application and subject matter of solicitation or request for expertise

  1. The dossier and subject matter of the request for assessment shall be delivered directly or sent to the individual or organization conducting the assessment by post.

  2. The direct handover and receipt of dossiers and objects subject to solicitation or request for expertise must be recorded in writing. A record of handover and receipt must contain the following contents:

  a) Time and place of handover and receipt of assessment files;

  b) Full name of representative of the delivering party and the receiving party;

  c) Decision on solicitation or written request for judicial assessment; subject matter to be assessed; relevant documents and items;

  d) Method of preservation of subject matter to be assessed, relevant documents and items upon delivery and receipt;

  dd) Condition of subject matter to be assessed, relevant documents and items upon delivery and receipt;

  e) Signature of representative of the delivering party and the receiving party.

  3. Sending dossiers and subject matters of solicitation or request for judicial assessment by post must be carried out in the form of service with number. Individuals and organizations receiving dossiers sent by service with number shall be responsible for preserving them and, upon opening the seals, shall prepare records as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. For the delivery and receipt of forensic subject matters, forensic psychiatric subject matters being human subjects, the ordering party or requesting party shall take charge and cooperate with the individual or organization requested to conduct judicial assessment in managing the subject matter during the process of conducting judicial assessment.

  5. Upon completion of the judicial assessment, the individual or organization conducting the judicial assessment shall return the subject matter to the ordering party or requesting party, unless otherwise provided by law.

  The ordering party or requesting party shall return the object of assessment as prescribed by law.

  The handover and receipt of the object of assessment after the assessment is completed shall comply with Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 28. Individual assessment, collective assessment

  1. Individual assessment means an assessment conducted by one person. Collective assessment means an assessment conducted by two or more persons.

  2. In the case of individual assessment, the assessor shall conduct the assessment, sign the assessment conclusion, and bear personal responsibility for such assessment conclusion.

  3. In the case of collective assessment in a specialized field, the assessors shall jointly conduct the assessment, sign the joint assessment conclusion, and bear joint responsibility for such assessment conclusion; if having different opinions, the assessors shall record their opinions in the assessment conclusion and take responsibility for such opinions.

  In the case of collective assessment in different specialized fields, each assessor shall conduct the assessment within their specialized field and bear personal responsibility for such assessment conclusion.

  Article 29. Additional assessment, re-assessment

  1. Additional assessment shall be conducted in case the contents of the initial assessment conclusion are unclear or incomplete, or when new issues arise related to the facts of the case or matter previously concluded by assessment. The solicitation and request for additional assessment shall be carried out as for the first-time assessment.

  2. Re-assessment shall be conducted in cases where there are grounds to believe that the initial assessment conclusion is inaccurate, or in the cases specified in Clause 2 Article 30 of this Law.

  3. The ordering party shall decide on the solicitation of re-assessment either on their own initiative or upon the request of the requesting party. If the ordering party does not accept the request for re-assessment, they must notify the requesting party in writing, clearly stating the reasons.

  Article 30. Assessment councils

  1. In cases where there is a difference between the initial assessment conclusion and the re-assessment conclusion regarding the same assessment content, the re-assessment shall be decided by the ordering party. The second re-assessment must be conducted by an assessment council.

  Ministers and Heads of ministerial agencies managing the fields subject to assessment shall decide on the establishment of councils for conducting the second re-assessment. An assessment council shall consist of at least three members who have high expertise and prestige in the field subject to assessment. The assessment council shall operate under the collective assessment mechanism prescribed in Clause 3 Article 28 of this Law.

  2. In special cases, the Procurator General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy or the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court shall decide on the re-assessment after obtaining conclusions from the assessment council.

  Article 31. Written records of the process of judicial assessment

  1. The judicial assessor shall promptly, fully and truthfully document the entire process and results of the judicial assessment in writing.

  2. The written record of the judicial assessment process must be kept in the judicial assessment file.

  Article 32. Judicial assessment conclusion

  1. A judicial assessment conclusion is a written remark or evaluation by the judicial assessor on the subject matter of the judicial assessment according to the contents of the order or request for judicial assessment. A judicial assessment conclusion must contain the following contents:

  a) Full name of the judicial assessor; organization conducting the judicial assessment;

  b) Name of the procedure-conducting agency; full name of the procedure-conducting person issuing the order for judicial assessment; number of the written order for judicial assessment or full name of the person requesting judicial assessment;

  c) Information identifying the subject matter of the judicial assessment;

  d) Time of receipt of the written order or request for judicial assessment;

  dd) Contents of the request for expertise;

  e) Method of conducting the expertise;

  g) Conclusion on the object of expertise;

  h) Time and place for conducting and completing the expertise.

  2. In case of soliciting or requesting an individual to conduct the expertise, the signature of the person conducting the expertise must be authenticated in accordance with the law on authentication.

  In case of soliciting or requesting an organization to conduct the expertise, the head of the organization must sign and affix a seal on the conclusion of the expertise, and the solicited or requested organization shall be responsible for the conclusion of the expertise.

  In case the Expertise Council specified in Clause 1 Article 30 of this Law conducts the expertise, the person who decides the establishment of the Council must sign and affix a seal on the conclusion of the expertise and shall be responsible for the legal status of the Expertise Council.

  3. In case the expertise is conducted before the decision to institute criminal proceedings is issued, according to the procedures prescribed by this Law, the procedural authority may use such conclusion as a judicial expertise conclusion.

  Article 33. Judicial assessment file

  1. A judicial assessment file prepared by the judicial assessor includes:

  a) Decision on ordering or requesting the judicial assessment and attached documents (if any);

  b) Record of handover and receipt of the file and subject matter of the order or request for judicial assessment;

  c) Written record of the process of conducting the judicial assessment;

  d) Judicial assessment photos (if any);

  dd) Previous judicial assessment conclusions or results of tests or experiments conducted by other persons (if any);

  e) Other documents related to the judicial assessment (if any);

  g) Conclude judicial assessment.

  2. Judicial assessment files must be prepared in a uniform form. The Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of Health and ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy in uniformly prescribing the form of judicial assessment files.

  3. Individuals and organizations conducting judicial expertise shall be responsible for preserving and storing their expertise records in accordance with archival laws.

  4. Judicial assessment files shall be presented upon request of presiding authorities or presiding officers competent to resolve criminal, administrative cases and civil matters.

  Article 34. Cases ineligible for judicial assessment

  1. Persons falling into one of the following cases shall not be permitted to conduct judicial assessment:

  a) They fall into one of the cases in which they must refuse to participate in legal proceedings or be changed as prescribed by procedural law;

  b) To be re-assessed on the same contents of a case or matter which they have assessed, unless otherwise provided for by law.

  2. An organization shall not conduct judicial assessment in one of the following cases:

  a) It has interests and obligations related to the case as prescribed by procedural law;

  b) There are clear grounds to believe that such organization may not be objective or impartial during the judicial assessment.

  Article 35. Judicial assessment assistance

  1. A request for judicial assessment conducted by a foreign individual or organization shall only be made if the subject matter of the judicial assessment is located overseas or the professional capability, equipment and facilities of the domestic judicial assessor do not meet the requirements of the judicial assessment.

  2. The judicial assessor shall be responsible for receiving and conducting the judicial assessment at the request of the competent authority of the foreign proceeding state.

  3. Procedures and costs for judicial assessment assistance between Vietnam and foreign countries shall comply with the law on judicial assistance.

  Chapter VI

     COSTS OF JUDICIAL ASSESSMENT, REGULATIONS AND POLICIES IN JUDICIAL ASSESSMENT ACTIVITIES

  Article 36. Costs of judicial assessment The ordering party and the requesting party shall pay costs of judicial assessment to the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider in accordance with regulations of law on costs of judicial assessment.

  Article 37. Benefits for judicial assessors and participants in judicial assessment

  1. A judicial assessor, a subject-matter judicial assessor, an assistant to a judicial assessor who is receiving salary from state budget, or a person assigned by a competent authority to be present and perform tasks during the period of judicial assessment in cases of autopsy, post-mortem examination, or exhumation shall receive remuneration as a subject-matter judicial assessor.

  2. In addition to the remuneration as a subject-matter judicial assessor prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article, a full-time judicial assessor working for a public judicial assessment organization shall be entitled to a professional allowance and other allowances.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 38. Policies applicable to judicial assessment activities

  1. Public judicial assessment organizations shall be entitled to preferential policies in accordance with regulations of the Government.

  2. Judicial experts, judicial assessment organizations and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations that make active contributions to judicial assessment activities shall be honored and rewarded in accordance with the law on emulation and commendation.

  3. Ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Councils shall, based on their actual capabilities and conditions as well as competence, prescribe other regimes and policies to attract experts and competent organizations to participate in judicial assessment activities.

  Chapter VII

     RESPONSIBILITIES OF STATE AGENCIES FOR LEGAL ASSESSMENT ORGANIZATIONS AND ACTIVITIES

  Article 39. State administrative body for judicial assessment

  1. The Government shall perform the unified state management of judicial assessment.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall assist the Government in performing the unified state management of judicial assessment.

  3. The Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense, the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Construction, the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment, the Ministry of Transport, the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, Vietnam State Bank and other ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall perform state management and take responsibility before the Government for organization and operation of judicial assessment in fields under their management; and cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in unified state management of judicial assessment.

  4. The provincial People’s Committees shall, within their tasks and powers, perform state management of judicial assessment in their localities.

  Article 40. Tasks and powers of the Ministry of Justice

  1. Promulgate or request competent regulatory authorities to promulgate legislative documents on judicial assessment and provide guidance on the implementation thereof.

  Take charge of developing and submitting to the Prime Minister comprehensive strategies, planning, and plans for judicial assessment development; cooperate with ministries and ministerial agencies in developing strategies, planning, and plans for judicial assessment development by sector.

  2. Provide written opinions on establishment of public judicial assessment organizations under the authority to make decisions of ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People’s Committees; where necessary, propose ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People’s Committees to consider and establish public judicial assessment organizations to meet the requirements of judicial assessment in legal proceedings.

  3. Develop programs for training in legal knowledge for judicial assessors; cooperate with ministries and ministerial agencies in organizing professional training and legal knowledge training for judicial assessors.

  4. Collect, compile, and publish a general list of judicial assessment organizations and individuals on the web portal of the Ministry of Justice.

  5. Encourage the performance of state management of judicial assessment by ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People’s Committees; request ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People’s Committees to report on the organization and operation of judicial assessment; and report to the Government on the organization and operation of judicial assessment nationwide.

  6. Take charge or request relevant ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Committees to organize inspection of judicial assessment organization and operation.

  7. Exercise state management of international cooperation in judicial assessment.

  Article 41. Tasks and powers of ministries and ministerial agencies in charge of professional management of judicial assessment

  1. Promulgate or request competent regulatory authorities to promulgate legislative documents on judicial assessment in the fields under their management and provide guidance on implementation thereof.

  2. Promulgate judicial assessment regulations or provide guidance on application of professional regulations for judicial assessment activities at the request and specific characteristics of the fields under their management.

  3. Take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in deciding establishment, consolidation and strengthening public judicial assessment organizations under their management as prescribed by this Law.

  4. Appoint and dismiss judicial assessors within their competence; prepare and publish lists of subject-matter judicial assessors and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations specified in Clause 1 Article 20 of this Law.

  5. Ensure funding, assessment equipment, facilities and other necessary material conditions for public judicial assessment organizations under their management to meet assigned tasks and responsibilities.

  6. Annually assess the quality of operation of judicial assessment organizations and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations and judicial assessors in the fields under their management.

  7. Provide for conditions regarding material facilities, equipment and means for judicial assessment by judicial assessment offices in the fields under their management.

  8. Develop and organize the implementation of programs and plans on training in judicial assessment skills and legal knowledge for a contingent of public judicial assessors in the fields under their management.

  9. Inspect, examine and settle complaints and denunciations about organization and operation of judicial assessment in the fields under their management; cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in inspection and examination of organization and operation of judicial assessment as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 40 of this Law.

  10. Enter into international cooperation on judicial assessment in the fields under their management.

  11. Annually review organization and operation of judicial assessment in the fields under their management and send reports to the Ministry of Justice for consolidation and reporting to the Government.

  Article 42. Tasks and powers of the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of National Defense

  In addition to the tasks and powers specified in Article 41 of this Law, the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of National Defense have the following tasks and powers:

  1. The Ministry of Health has the following tasks and powers:

  a) To perform state management of forensic examination and forensic psychiatric examination;

  b) To promulgate professional standards in the fields of forensic examination and forensic psychiatric examination;

  c) To specify standards for forensic examiners and forensic psychiatric examiners;

  d) To provide training and refresher courses and issue certificates of competency in forensic examination and forensic psychiatric examination as prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security has the following tasks and powers:

  a) To perform state management of criminal technical examination;

  b) To promulgate professional standards in the field of criminal technical examination;

  c) To specify standards for criminal technical assessors;

  d) To provide training and refresher courses in and issue certificates of professional competence in criminal technical assessment as prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law;

  dd) To conduct annual statistics on solicitation and evaluation of assessment performance and use of judicial assessment conclusions within investigation authorities under its management;

  e) To guide investigation authorities under its management to apply regulations of law on solicitation of assessment, evaluation and use of judicial assessment conclusions;

  g) To ensure funding and provide guidance on payment of judicial assessment expenses within investigation authorities under its management;

  h) To annually review and send reports to the Ministry of Justice on the situation of solicitation and evaluation of assessment performance and use of judicial assessment conclusions within investigation authorities under its management.

  3. The Ministry of National Defense shall have the duties and powers specified in Points dd, e, g and h Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 43. Duties and powers of provincial People’s Committees

  1. Provincial People’s Committees have the following tasks and powers:

  a) Establish public judicial assessment organizations; decide to permit the establishment of judicial assessment offices; prepare and publish lists of local judicial assessment organizations and ad hoc judicial assessment organizations;

  b) Appoint and dismiss judicial assessors within their competence; prepare and publish lists of local judicial assessors;

  c) Ensure funding, operational facilities, material bases, and other necessary conditions for local public judicial assessment organizations;

  d) Organize professional training and legal knowledge education for local judicial assessors;

  dd) Annually assess the organization and quality of judicial assessment in their localities; ensure the quantity and quality of judicial assessors and ad hoc judicial assessors to promptly meet the requirements of judicial assessment in local proceedings;

  e) Inspect, examine, and resolve complaints and denunciations about judicial assessment within their competence; cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in inspecting and examining the organization and operation of judicial assessment as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 40 of this Law;

  g) Report to the Ministry of Justice on the organization and operation of judicial assessment in their localities, and concurrently send reports to relevant ministries and ministerial agencies to perform state management tasks prescribed in Articles 40, 41, and 42 of this Law.

  2. The Department of Justice shall be responsible for assisting the provincial People’s Committee in performing state management over judicial assessment in their province; taking charge and cooperating with specialized agencies in assisting the provincial People’s Committee in managing the operation of the judicial assessment office.

  Specialized agencies of the provincial People’s Committee shall assist the People’s Committee at the same level in performing state management over specialized fields of judicial assessment, and shall be held accountable to the provincial People’s Committee for organizing and conducting judicial assessment within their scope of management; and shall cooperate with the Department of Justice in assisting the People’s Committee at the same level in performing state management over local judicial assessment.

  Article 44. Responsibilities of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuracy

  1. To guide the application of laws on ordering judicial assessment and evaluation and use of judicial assessment conclusions in the system of People's Courts and People's Procuracies.

  2. To implement the statistical regime on ordering and evaluating the performance of judicial assessment and use of judicial assessment conclusions in the system of People's Courts and People's Procuracies, and to report to the National Assembly in their annual reports.

  3. To cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in implementing the statistical regime and reporting on ordering and evaluating the performance of judicial assessment and use of judicial assessment conclusions.

  4. To ensure funding and provide guidance on payment of judicial assessment costs in the system of People's Courts and People's Procuracies.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 45. Effect

  1. This Law takes effect on January 01, 2013.

  2. The Judicial Expertise Ordinance No. 24/2004/PL-UBTVQH11 ceases to be effective from the effective date of this Law.

  3. From the effective date of this Law, if the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code, Civil Procedure Code and Administrative Procedure Law on judicial expertise contain different contents from this Law, the provisions of this Law shall apply.

  Article 46. Detailed regulations and guidance for implementation

  The Government, Supreme People’s Court and Supreme People’s Procuracy shall detail and guide the implementation of articles and clauses assigned in this Law.

  This Law was passed on June 20, 2012 by the XIIIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 3rd session.
