                                                     DECREE

     ELABORATION OF THE ORDER AND PROCEDURES FOR PREPARING AND PAYING PROCEDURAL EXPENSES

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated February 18, 2025;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on amendments to the Law on Securities, the Law on Accounting, the Law on Independent Audit, the Law on State Budget, the Law on Management and Use of Public Property, the Law on Tax Administration, the Law on Personal Income Tax, the Law on National Reserves, and the Law on Actions against Administrative Violations dated November 29, 2024;

  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Court Costs dated December 11, 2024;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates a Decree elaborating on procedures for estimation and payment of court costs.

  Chapter I

                                                 GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Decree provides for procedures for estimation and payment of procedural expenses.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Decree applies to competent procedural authorities and agencies, organizations and individuals involved in estimation and payment of procedural expenses.

  Article 3. Estimation, allocation and settlement of procedural expense

  The estimation, allocation and settlement of procedural expense shall comply with regulations of law on state budget, accounting laws and relevant laws. This Decree provides the following guidelines:

  1. Estimation

  Every year, at the time of preparing the state budget estimate, based on the actual paid procedural expenses of the previous year and the estimated implementation of the current year, and the plan for implementation of procedural activities of the planned year, competent procedural authorities and relevant agencies are responsible for making an estimate of procedural expenses in accordance with the Ordinance on Procedural Expenses, consolidating it into their own estimates, and submitting it to the superior management agency for consideration and consolidation into the first-level estimate of the unit, which is then sent to the finance agency at the same level for submission to the competent authority in accordance with the law on state budget.

  2. Allocation of estimates

  After being assigned budget estimates by the Prime Minister or People’s Committees, first-level estimate-making units at central and local levels shall allocate and assign budget estimates to budget-dependent using units and subordinate budget units in accordance with state budget laws. In particular, allocation and assignment of recurrent expenditures for payment of procedural costs shall be included in funding not subject to the autonomy regime of agencies and units.

  3. Settlement

  Agencies and units shall settle funding for payment of procedural costs in accordance with this Decree and consolidate them in their annual budget settlement reports in accordance with laws on settlement of recurrent expenditures of the state budget, accounting laws, guiding documents, and other relevant laws.

  Chapter II

    PROVISIONS ON PROCEDURAL COSTS PAYMENT BY AGENCIES WITH PROCEDURAL AUTHORITY FOR AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND INDIVIDUALS EXECUTING LEGAL PROCEEDINGS

  Article 4. Procedures for payment of on-site consideration and appraisal costs; on-site consideration costs

  1. After completing on-site consideration and appraisal, on-site consideration, persons invited to participate in on-site consideration and appraisal, persons invited to participate in on-site consideration, and persons conducting on-site consideration shall submit a set of application for payment to the agency with procedural authority by one of the following methods: by post, in person, or online via electronic means if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technology are met.

  2. Application for payment of expenses for persons invited to participate in on-site assessment, on-site consideration, or on-site examination includes:

  a) Payment request form made using Form No. 01 enclosed herewith.

  b) Documents attached to the payment request form are the original invoices and relevant legal documents to prove necessary and reasonable expenses incurred when being invited to participate in on-site assessment, on-site consideration, or on-site examination.

  3. In case the application for payment is incomplete as prescribed, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority must notify the payer and provide an explanation for supplementation of the application as prescribed. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the complete and valid application, the competent procedural authority shall pay the expenses.

  4. The competent procedural authority shall pay service fees and other expenses (if any) under contracts, invoices, and service charge receipts as prescribed by law.

  Article 5. Procedures for advance payment and payment of property valuation expenses

  1. In case it is necessary to advance property valuation expenses, within 05 working days from the date of receipt of the request for property valuation from the competent procedural authority, the Property Valuation Council shall send an application for advance payment to the competent procedural authority by one of the methods: postal service, direct submission, or online submission if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technology for advance payment procedures are met.

  2. The application for advance payment of property valuation expenses includes:

  a) The written request for advance on property valuation expenses made using Form No. 02 enclosed herewith.

  b) The table for calculating total property valuation expenses and the basis for calculating property valuation expenses.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the complete application for advance from the Property Valuation Council, the competent procedural authority shall be responsible for making the advance. The maximum advance shall not exceed the property valuation expense calculated by the Property Valuation Council and sent in the application for advance as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Within 10 days from the date of obtaining the valuation results, the Property Valuation Council shall send an application for payment of valuation expenses to the competent procedural authority by one of the methods: via postal services, in person or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technical requirements for payment procedures are met.

  5. The application for payment of valuation expenses includes:

  a) The written request for payment made using Form No. 01 enclosed herewith.

  b) Documents attached to the written request for payment are the written request for valuation; the original copies of relevant invoices and vouchers to prove necessary expenses and expenses determined under Articles 21, 22, 23 and 24 of the Ordinance on Expenses for Procedural Activities arising during the process of valuation.

  6. In case the application for payment of valuation expenses is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority must notify the Property Valuation Council and clearly state the reasons for supplementing the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the request for payment of full and valid valuation expense dossiers, competent procedural authorities shall pay the valuation expense to the property valuation council in accordance with regulations.

  In case the paid advance on valuation expense is insufficient, competent procedural authorities shall pay the remaining amount.

  In case the paid advance exceeds the valuation expense, the property valuation council must refund the difference within 2 working days from the date of sending the request for payment of valuation expense.

  Article 6. Procedures for advance and payment of assessment expense

  1. Procedures for advance of assessment expense

  a) In civil procedures and administrative procedures

  Procedures for advance of assessment expense shall comply with Article 38 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  b) In criminal procedures Procedures for advance of assessment expense shall comply with Article 44 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  Within 5 working days from the date on which decision on request for expertise is received from competent procedural authorities, entities shall submit 1 set of application for advance payment of expertise costs to competent procedural authorities by post or in person or online if infrastructure and technical requirements for advance payment are met.

  2. Application for advance payment of expertise costs includes:

  a) Application form for advance payment using Form No. 02 attached hereto.

  b) Table of calculation of total costs of expertise and basis for calculation of costs of expertise.

  3. Deadline for payment and level of advance payment of expertise costs

  a) In civil and administrative procedures: Deadline for advance payment of expertise costs shall comply with Article 38 of the Ordinance on Procedure Costs.

  b) In criminal procedures: Deadline for advance payment of expertise costs shall comply with notice of request for advance payment of expertise costs issued by entities conducting expertise as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 44 of the Ordinance on Procedure Costs.

  c) Maximum level of advance payment shall not exceed expertise costs calculated and submitted by entities conducting expertise in application for advance payment as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Within 10 days from the date of obtaining the appraisal results, the appraising organization or individual shall submit one set of application for payment of valuation expense to the competent procedural authority by one of the following methods: via postal service, in person, or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technical requirements for payment procedures are met.

  5. The application for payment of valuation expense includes:

  a) A written request for payment made using Form No. 1 enclosed with this Decree.

  b) Documents attached to the written request for payment are the original invoices and relevant legal documents to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred during the appraisal process.

  6. In case the application for payment of valuation expense is incomplete or invalid, within 3 days from the receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority must notify the requesting organization or individual and clearly state the reasons for supplementation as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the receipt of the complete and valid application for payment of valuation expense, the competent procedural authority shall pay the valuation expense to the appraising organization or individual as prescribed.

  In case the paid advance on valuation expense is insufficient, the competent procedural authority shall pay the remaining amount.

  In case the paid advance exceeds the valuation expense, the appraising organization or individual must refund the difference within 2 working days from the date of submission of the application for payment of valuation expense.

  Article 7. Procedures for payment of expenses for lay assessors

  1. Within 10 days from the end of the trial, the lay assessor shall send an application for payment of expenses to the Court by one of the methods via postal services, in person or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technical requirements for payment procedures are met.

  2. The application for payment of expenses for the lay assessor includes:

  a) An application form for payment made using Form No. 01 enclosed with this Decree.

  b) Documents attached to the application form for payment include the schedule of time of participation in the trial, the time of study of the dossier of the lay assessor certified by the presiding judge; the original invoices and legal documents related to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses that have arisen as prescribed in Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 Article 46 of the Ordinance on Procedural Expenses.

  3. In case the application for payment of expenses for the lay assessor is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the receipt of the application, the Court must notify the lay assessor and clearly state the reason for supplementing the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the receipt of the complete and valid application for payment of expenses for the lay assessor, the Court shall pay the expenses for the lay assessor as prescribed.

  Article 8. Payment of expenses for lawyers, legal assistants and people’s defense counsels in cases where defense counsels are appointed in criminal proceedings

  1. Procedures for paying expenses for lawyers and legal aid professionals in case of designation of defense counsels shall comply with regulations of law on lawyers and legal aid.

  2. Procedures for paying expenses for people’s advocates in case of designation of defense counsels shall be carried out as follows:

  a) A dossier requesting payment of expenses for people’s advocates includes:

  A written request for payment made using Form No. 01 enclosed herewith.

  Documents attached to the written request for payment are the schedule of working time of the people’s advocate certified by the competent procedural authority or proceeding officer; the original invoices and relevant legal documents to prove necessary and reasonable expenses incurred as prescribed in Points b, c, d and dd Clause 2 Article 49 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  b) After completing each stage of legal proceedings (investigation, prosecution and trial), the people’s advocate shall send the dossier requesting payment to the competent procedural authority to request designation of a defense counsel being a people’s advocate by one of the methods via postal services, submission in person or online on the electronic environment if meeting the infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures.

  c) In case the dossier requesting payment of expenses for people’s advocates is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the receipt of the dossier, the competent procedural authority must notify the people’s advocate and clearly state the reasons for supplementation of the dossier as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the receipt of the complete and valid dossier requesting payment of expenses for people’s advocates, the competent procedural authority shall pay expenses for people’s advocates as prescribed.

  Article 9. Payment of expenses for witnesses, interpreters and translators

  1. After completing their tasks as witnesses, interpreters or translators, witnesses, interpreters or translators shall submit payment requests to competent procedural authorities by post, in person or online if infrastructure and technical conditions are satisfied.

  2. A payment request includes:

  a) A written request for payment of expenses for witnesses, interpreters and translators made using Form No. 01 enclosed herewith.

  b) Documents attached to the written request are relevant invoices and documents to prove necessary and reasonable expenses paid by witnesses, interpreters and translators when performing their tasks as per the law.

  3. If the payment request is inadequate or invalid, competent procedural authorities must inform witnesses, interpreters and translators within 03 days from the date on which they receive the request and provide justification for supplementation as per the law.

  Competent procedural authorities shall pay expenses for witnesses, interpreters and translators within 10 days from the date on which they receive adequate and valid payment requests.

  Article 10. Payment of overseas judicial entrustment expenses

  1. Procedures for payment of costs of overseas judicial entrustment in civil procedures, administrative procedures and criminal procedures shall comply with regulations of law on legal assistance.

  2. Procedures for payment of costs of service or notification abroad in civil procedures and administrative procedures through overseas representative missions of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall comply with procedures stipulated by the Supreme People’s Court in cooperation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  Article 11. Payment of costs of participation in court sessions or meetings for settlement of cases or lawsuits

  1. After the end of the court session or meeting for settlement of the case or lawsuit, the persons specified in Clause 1, Article 69 of the Ordinance on Procedure Costs shall send the payment request dossier to the competent procedural authority in one of the following forms: by post, in person or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technology for payment procedures are met.

  2. The payment request dossier includes:

  a) A written request for payment of costs of participation in the court session or meeting, made using Form No. 01 enclosed herewith.

  b) Documents attached to the payment request dossier are the original copies of relevant invoices and documents to prove necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by the participants in the court session or meeting that have been paid when they attended the court session or meeting for settlement of the case or lawsuit.

  3. In case the payment request dossier is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the receipt of the dossier, the competent procedural authority must notify the participant in the court session or meeting and clearly state the reason for supplementation as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid payment request, the competent procedural authority shall pay the court participants' expenses as prescribed.

  Chapter III

                                            IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 12. Entry into force

  1. This Decree comes into force from the date of its signing.

  2. In case documents referred to in this Decree are amended or replaced, such amendments or replacements shall prevail.

  3. The Government’s Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP dated August 14, 2014 on elaboration of the Ordinance on Expenses for Assessment and Valuation; Expenses for Witnesses and Interpreters in Legal Proceedings (Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP) ceases to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  4. For cases where payment requests for assessment expense, valuation expense, expense for witness or interpreter in legal proceedings are being processed according to regulations in Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP before the effective date of this Decree, such payment requests shall continue to comply with Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP. Competent procedural authorities, agencies, organizations and individuals performing legal proceedings shall take full responsibility for the completeness and validity of payment requests and procedures for payment of expenses as prescribed in this Clause, ensuring compliance with law.

  5. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, and Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall be responsible for implementing this Decree.
