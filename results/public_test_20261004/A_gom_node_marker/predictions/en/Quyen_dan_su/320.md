                                                    DECREE

    OF THE GOVERNMENT NO. 88/2003/ND-CP OF JULY 30, 2003, PROVIDING FOR THE ORGANIZATION, OPERATION AND MANAGEMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

                                                    THE GOVERNMENT

  Pursuant to the Ordinance No. 102/SL-L004 of May 20, 1957 promulgating the Law on Right to Establish Associations; and the Civil Code of October 28, 1995;
  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Government of December 25, 2001;
  At the request of the Minister of Home Affairs,

                                                    DECREE:

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for organization, operation and state management of associations.

  2. This Decree does not apply to organizations:

  a) Vietnam Fatherland Front, Vietnam General Confederation of Labor, Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, Vietnam Peasants' Association, Vietnam War Veterans' Association, Vietnam Women's Union;

  b) Religious organizations.

  Article 2. Associations

  1. Associations specified in this Decree are understood as voluntary organizations of Vietnamese citizens and organizations in the same industry, occupation, interest and gender, sharing the same purpose of gathering and uniting members, regularly operating, not for self-seeking purposes, aiming to protect lawful rights and interests of members; supporting each other in effective operation, contributing to the national socio-economic development, organized and operating under this Decree and other relevant legal documents.

  2. Associations have different names: unions, general associations, federations, associations, clubs with legal entity status and other names as prescribed by law (hereinafter referred to as “associations”).

  3. Scope of operation of associations (territorially) includes:

  a/ Associations operating nationwide or inter-provincially;

  b/ Associations operating within provinces or centrally run cities (hereinafter referred to as provinces);

  c/ Associations operating within rural districts, urban districts, provincial capitals or towns (hereinafter referred to as districts);

  d/ Associations operating within communes, wards or district towns (hereinafter referred to as communes).

  Article 3. Establishment of associations and principles for their organization and operation

  1. The establishment of an association must be permitted by a competent state agency defined in Article 15 of this Decree.

  2. An association shall be organized and operate under its charter approved by a competent state agency and not contrary to law; it shall be organized and operate on the principles of voluntariness, self-governance, self-financing and take responsibility before law.

  Article 4. Responsibilities of regulatory agencies for associations

  1. Regulatory agencies shall, within their functions and tasks, facilitate the operation of associations in accordance with their charters and with an effective manner.

  2. Associations recognized as socio-political organizations or socio-political-professional organizations, and associations engaged in activities associated with the State's tasks shall be supported by the State budget in accordance with regulations of the Prime Minister.

  Article 5. Legal entity status, seals, name and symbol of associations

  1. Associations have legal entity status, seals and separate accounts.

  2. Associations may select their names and symbols in accordance with law.

  Chapter 2:

                                    CONDITIONS AND PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 6. Conditions for establishment of associations

  1. Having operation purposes not in contravention of law; having names and main operation fields not identical to those of associations lawfully established before in the same territory.

  2. Having a charter.

  3. Having a head office.

  4. Having enough registered members.

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall stipulate the number of registered members of an association.

  Article 7. Association establishment campaigning board

  1. To establish an association, its founders must set up an association establishment campaigning board. The association establishment campaigning board is recognized by the State management agency in charge of the sector or domain in which the association is expected to operate.

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall regulate the number of members of the Campaign Board.

  2. After being recognized, the Campaign Board shall perform the following tasks:

  a) To mobilize citizens and organizations to join the association, and complete the dossier of application for establishment;

  b) The head of the Campaign Board shall submit the dossier of application for establishment to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 8. Dossiers of application for establishment

  1. The written application for establishment.

  2. Draft Charter.

  3. Proposed operation orientation.

  4. List of members of the Campaign Board recognized by competent authorities.

  5. Personal background of the head of the Campaign Board certified by competent authorities.

  6. Documents determining the headquarters and assets of the association.

  Article 9. Main contents of the Association Charter

  1. Name of the association.

  2. Objectives, scope and domain of operation of the association.

  3. Tasks and powers of the association.

  4. Principles of organization and operation.

  5. Formalities for joining and leaving the association.

  6. Criteria for membership.

  7. Rights and obligations of members.

  8. Structure, organization, formalities for election and dismissal; tasks and powers of the leadership board, inspection board and other leading positions of the association.

  9. Assets, finance and methods of managing assets and finance of the association.

  10. Conditions for dissolution and settlement of assets and finance.

  11. Commendation and handling of violations.

  12. Formalities for amendment and supplementing the Association Charter.

  13. Effect.

  Article 10. Responsibilities of competent state agencies permitting association establishment upon receiving dossiers of application for association establishment

  Competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall issue receipts when receiving dossiers of application for association establishment. If the dossiers are complete and lawful, within 60 days after receiving such dossiers, competent state agencies shall reply to associations establishment campaigning boards; in case of refusal, they must issue written replies clearly stating the reasons therefor.

  Article 11. Time limit for holding associations establishment congresses

  1. Within 90 days after decisions permitting association establishment take effect, associations establishment campaigning boards shall hold congresses.

  2. Past the above time limit, if no congress is held, associations establishment campaigning boards shall send written requests for extension to state agencies which have decided on association establishment permission. The extension duration must not exceed 30 days. Past the extended time limit, if no congress is held, decisions permitting association establishment shall cease to be effective.

  Article 12. Principal contents of associations establishment congresses

  1. Announcement of decisions permitting association establishment.

  2. Discussion and voting on the Charter.

  3. Election of the Leadership Board and the Inspection Board in accordance with the Society’s Charter.

  4. Approval of the Society's operational program.

  5. Approval of the Congress's resolution.

  Article 13. Report on the results of the Congress

  Within 30 days from the date of the Congress, the Society's Leadership Board shall send the Congress's documents to the State agency that has decided to permit the establishment of the Society, including:

  1. The Society's Charter and minutes of approval of the Society's Charter;

  2. Minutes of election of the Leadership Board and the Inspection Board (with the list attached) and the personal background of the head of the Society;

  3. Operation program of the association;

  4. Resolution of the congress.

  Article 14. Approval of the association charter and effect of the association charter

  1. Competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall decide to approve the association charter when it has been approved by the congress. In case the association charter is contrary to law, competent state agencies shall refuse to approve it and request the association to amend it.

  2. The association charter takes effect on the date the competent state agency decides to approve it.

  Article 15. State agencies competent to permit establishment; division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution and approval of association charters

  1. The Minister of Home Affairs shall permit establishment; division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution and approval of charters for associations operating nationwide or inter-provincially.

  2. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees shall permit establishment; division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution and approval of charters for associations operating within provinces.

  Chapter 3.

                                                    MEMBERS

  Article 16. Conditions for becoming a member

  Vietnamese citizens and organizations who meet the criteria to become a member as prescribed in the society charter and voluntarily apply to join the society may become members of the society.

  The competence and procedures for admitting members shall be prescribed in the society charter.

  Article 17. Rights and obligations of members

  Rights and obligations of members shall be prescribed in the society charter.

  Article 18. Associate members and honorary members

  1. Joint-venture enterprises and wholly foreign-invested enterprises (hereinafter referred to as foreign-invested enterprises) operating in Vietnam that contribute to the development of the association and agree with its Charter shall be considered for recognition as associate members by the association of economic organizations.

  Associate members may participate in activities and attend the association’s congresses. Associate members shall not participate in election or nomination of the association’s leadership board and shall not vote on issues of the association.

  Procedures for admission of associate members shall be prescribed in the association’s Charter.

  2. Vietnamese citizens and organizations that are unable to become an association member but contribute to the association may be recognized as associate members or honorary members by the association. Rights and obligations of associate members and honorary members shall be prescribed in the association’s Charter.

  Chapter 4

                          ORGANIZATION, OPERATION, RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE ASSOCIATION

  Article 19. Term-based congress and extraordinary congress

  1. The highest leading body of the association is the general meeting or the congress of delegates.

  2. The term of a congress shall be prescribed in the society charter but must not exceed 5 years.

  3. An extraordinary congress may be convened when at least two-thirds (two thirds) of the total number of members of the Executive Committee or at least half (one-half) of the total number of official members request it.

  Article 20. Principal matters to be decided at a congress

  1. Operation orientation of the society.

  2. Election of the leadership and inspection boards.

  3. Renaming of the society, amendment of the society charter (if any).

  4. Joining other societies operating in the same field.

  5. Division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution of the society.

  6. Finance of the association.

  Article 21. Voting rules at a congress

  1. A congress may vote by show of hands or secret ballot. The form of voting shall be decided by the congress.

  2. The approval of decisions at a congress must be voted for by more than half of the present official delegates.

  Article 22. Rights of an association

  1. To propagate its goals.

  2. To represent its members in internal and external relations related to its functions and tasks.

  3. To protect the legitimate rights and interests of the association and its members.

  4. To organize and coordinate activities among its members for the common interests of the association; to reconcile disputes within the association.

  5. To disseminate and train knowledge for its members; to provide necessary information for its members in accordance with law.

  6. To advise and critically examine issues falling within the scope of operation of the association at the request of organizations and individuals.

  7. To participate in opinions on legal documents related to the contents of the association's operation in accordance with law. To propose to competent state agencies matters related to the development of the association and the fields in which the association operates.

  8. To coordinate with relevant agencies and organizations in performing the tasks of the association.

  9. To raise funds for the association on the basis of membership fees of its members and revenues from business and service activities in accordance with law to cover its operating expenses.

  10. To receive lawful financial supports from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals in accordance with law.

  11. An association with a nationwide or inter-provincial scope of operation may join as a member of international and regional associations under Decree No. 20/2002/ND-CP of February 20, 2002 of the Government on conclusion and implementation of international agreements by provinces and centrally-run cities, socio-political organizations, social organizations and socio-professional organizations of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  Article 23.- Obligations of associations

  1. The association's activities must comply with its approved charter.

  2. The association shall be subject to state management by the state management agency in charge of the sector or domain in which it operates.

  3. 30 days before organizing a term congress, the leadership board of the society shall report in writing to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree and the ministry managing the sector or domain in which the society operates.

  4. The establishment of a representative office of an association in another locality must be permitted by the provincial-level People’s Committee of the locality where such office is located and reported in writing to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  5. When changing the President, Vice President or General Secretary of the association, changing its head office, amending and supplementing its Charter, the association shall report to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  6. The establishment of legal entities under associations must comply with regulations of law and be reported to competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  7. Annually, associations shall report on their organization and operation to competent state agencies and state management agencies of sectors or fields in which they operate, no later than December 1 every year.

  8. To abide by the guidance, inspection and examination of competent state agencies on law observance.

  9. The list of members, branches, affiliated units, financial documents of the association, minutes of meetings of the leadership board shall be made into dossiers and kept at the association head office.

  10. The revenues as prescribed in Clauses 9 and 10, Article 22 of this Decree must be used for association activities according to the association charter and not distributed to members.

  11. The use of the association’s revenues must comply with law provisions. Every year, the association must make financial statements according to the State’s regulations and send them to finance agencies of the same level.

  Chapter 5

                             DIVISION, SEPARATION; MERGER; CONSOLIDATION AND DISOLUTION OF ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 24. Division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution

  1. Depending on the requirements and operation capability of the association, the leadership board shall request the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree to permit the division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution of the association. The division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution of the association shall comply with law provisions.

  2. A society may dissolve in the following cases:

  a) It dissolves by itself;

  b) It is dissolved under a decision of a competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 25. Self-dissolution of societies

  A society may dissolve itself in the following cases:

  1. Upon expiry of its operation duration;

  2. At the request of more than half of its official members;

  3. Its objectives have been achieved.

  Article 26. Responsibilities of the leadership board when the association is dissolved

  1. Send to competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree the following documents:

  a) Application for dissolution;

  b) Resolution on dissolution;

  c) Statement of assets and finance;

  d) Proposed method of disposal of assets and finance and deadline for debt repayment.

  2. Notify the deadline for debt repayment (if any) to relevant organizations and individuals as prescribed by law on more than 5 consecutive issues of central newspapers for associations operating nationwide or inter-provincially; notify local authorities for associations operating within provinces.

  Article 27. Decision on dissolution

  Competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall decide on the dissolution of a society after 15 days from the date of expiry of the debt payment and liquidation of assets and finance stated in the society's notice when applying for dissolution without complaint.

  The society shall terminate its operation as from the effective date of the competent state agency's decision on dissolution of the society.

  Article 28. Dissolved societies

  A society shall be dissolved under the decision of the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree in the following cases:

  1. It fails to operate for 12 consecutive months;

  2. When there is a congress resolution on dissolution but the society's leadership board does not comply with it;

  3. Its activities seriously violate law.

  Article 29. Responsibilities of competent state agencies upon dissolution of societies

  When the association is dissolved, the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall:

  a/ To decide on the dissolution of the association;

  b/ Announcing the decision to dissolve the association on the mass media.

  Article 30. Settlement of assets and finance upon dissolution, consolidation, merger or division or separation of associations

  1. When a society dissolves or is dissolved, its assets shall be handled as follows:

  a/ Assets and finance donated by domestic and foreign organizations; assets and finance supported by the State, for which the association has fulfilled all property obligations and paid all debts, the remaining assets and financial balance shall be decided by competent state agencies;

  b/ For self-generated assets and financial resources of the association which have been fully used for the performance of obligations regarding assets and payment of debts after the association is dissolved, the remaining assets and financial balance shall be decided by the association according to its Charter.

  2. To settle assets and finance of the association when it is consolidated:

  a) After the merger of associations into new associations, old associations shall cease to exist and the new associations shall enjoy lawful rights and interests and be responsible for unpaid debts and service contracts currently being executed by old associations;

  b) Assets and finance of merged associations shall not be divided or transferred but transferred entirely to the new associations.

  3. Settlement of assets and finance of associations upon merger:

  a) When an association merges with another association, its assets and finance shall be transferred to the merging association;

  b) The merging association shall enjoy lawful rights and interests regarding existing assets and finance of the merging association and be responsible for unpaid debts regarding assets and finance and service contracts currently being executed.

  4. Settlement of assets and finance of associations upon division or separation:

  a) After a division of an association, the divided association shall cease to operate and its rights and obligations regarding assets and finance shall be transferred to the new association according to the decision on division of the association;

  b) After a separation of an association, the separated associations shall exercise their rights and obligations regarding assets and finance in accordance with operation purposes of such associations.

  Article 31. Right to complain

  In case a society is dissolved without its consent, it has the right to lodge a complaint as prescribed by law. During the waiting for the settlement of the complaint, the society shall not be allowed to operate.

  Chapter 6

                                      STATE MANAGEMENT OF SOCIETY

  Article 32. State management of society

  1. To make proposals or promulgate legal documents on society.

  2. To guide ministries, sectors, localities, societies and citizens in implementing the law on society.

  3. To permit the establishment, division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of society charters as prescribed in Article 15 of this Decree.

  4. To provide professional guidance for officials and public employees engaged in the management of associations.

  5. To disseminate the association law.

  6. To inspect and examine the observance of the association law; to inspect the implementation of the association charters by associations.

  7. To manage the conclusion of international cooperation on associations under law.

  8. To settle complaints and denunciations and handle violations of the association law.

  9. To summarize reports on the organization, operation and management of associations.

  The Ministry of Home Affairs shall assist the Government in performing the unified state management over associations operating in the fields under the management of ministries nationwide.

  Article 33. State management tasks of ministries and ministerial-level agencies regarding associations operating in the fields under their management nationwide

  1. Sign written agreements with competent state authorities under Article 15 hereof on establishment; division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution and approval of society charters; recognition of society establishment advocacy boards.

  2. Guide and enable societies to participate in activities within sectors and fields under management of ministries as per the law; organize collection of opinions from societies for completion of regulations on state management of sectors and fields.

  3. Inspect implementation of regulations on state management of sectors and fields applicable to societies, handle or propose competent state authorities to handle violations as per the law.

  Article 34. State management tasks of provincial People’s Committees for societies operating within provinces

  1. Exercise jurisdiction under Clause 2 Article 15 hereof and perform state management of organization and operation of societies.

  2. Inspect compliance with regulations on societies.

  3. Resolve complaints, denunciations, and handle violations against regulations on societies.

  4. Consider providing support for societies operating within local areas.

  5. Consider and permit local associations to receive donations from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals in accordance with law.

  6. To direct provincial/municipal departments, sectors and People’s Committees of districts and communes in managing associations.

  7. Annually, make a consolidated report on the organization, operation and management of associations in their localities to the Ministry of Home Affairs.

  Chapter VII

                                    COMMENDATION AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 35. Commendation

  1. A society that makes many contributions to socio-economic development shall be commended and/or rewarded according to the State's regulations.

  2. Members who have made many achievements shall be commended and/or rewarded according to regulations of the association and the State.

  Article 36. Handling of violations

  1. Those who violate the right to establish associations or abuse the association name to conduct illegal activities shall, depending on the nature and severity of their violations, be disciplined, administratively sanctioned or examined for penal liability in accordance with law; if causing material damage, they must pay compensation therefor in accordance with law.

  2. Those who abuse their positions and powers to permit the establishment of associations in contravention of this Decree shall, depending on the nature and severity of their violations, be disciplined, administratively sanctioned or examined for penal liability in accordance with law; if causing material damage, they must pay compensation therefor in accordance with law.

  3. If the leadership board or representative of the association deliberately prolongs the term of the congress prescribed by its charter or fails to comply with regulations on obligations of associations, they shall be handled according to the nature and seriousness of their violations as prescribed by law.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 37. Effect

  1. This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO.

  2. This Decree replaces Decree No. 258-TTg of June 14, 1957 of the Prime Minister on the detailed implementation of Law No. 102-SL-L004 of May 20, 1957 on the right to establish associations.

  3. Associations established under the previous Ordinance No. 102-SL-L004 of May 20, 1957 by the Ministry of Home Affairs and the provincial executive committees are permitted to be re-established under Directive No. 01/CT of January 5, 1989 by the Chairman of the Council of Ministers (now the Prime Minister) or the Minister or the Head of the Organization and Personnel Committee of the Government (now the Minister of Home Affairs), the President of the People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government.

  4. The association of economic organizations established under Decision No. 38-HDBT of April 10, 1989 of the Council of Ministers (now the Government) on economic association in production, circulation and services is not required to apply for re-establishment.

  Article 38. Implementation organization

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall guide the implementation of this Decree.

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies, the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government shall implement this Decree.
