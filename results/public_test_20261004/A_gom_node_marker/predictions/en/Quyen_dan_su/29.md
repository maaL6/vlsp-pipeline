                                                  CIRCULAR

  GUIDELINES ON PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE OF VISA, PERMANENT RESIDENCE EXTENSION AND PERMANENT RESIDENCE CARD FOR FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM UNDER THE JURISDICTION OF THE MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS

  Circular No. 04/2016/TT-BNG dated June 30, 2016 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs providing guidelines on procedures for issuance of visa, extension of permanent residence and issuance of permanent residence card for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which takes effect from September 1, 2016, is amended by:

  Circular No. 04/2025/TT-BNG dated June 13, 2025 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 04/2016/TT-BNG dated June 30, 2016 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs providing guidelines for procedures for issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Decision No. 2985/2007/QD-BNG dated November 29, 2007 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgating the Regulation on organization and operation of the Fund for protection of overseas Vietnamese citizens and legal entities, which takes effect from July 13, 2025.

  Pursuant to Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;

  Pursuant to Law on promulgation of legislative documents dated June 3, 2008;

  Pursuant to Decree No. 58/2013/ND-CP dated June 11, 2013 of the Government defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP dated August 6, 2015 of the Government on mechanism for cooperation among ministries, ministerial agencies and People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities in management of entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam;

  The Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgates the Circular providing guidelines for procedures for issuance of visas, issuance of temporary residence cards and extension of temporary residence for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs [1].

  Chapter I

                                             GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidelines on issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs as prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of Law on entry, exit, transit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Entry and Exit”).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to foreigners under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs entering and residing in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations, and individuals.

  Chapter II

              COMPETENCE AND PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE OF VISAS, EXTENSION OF TEMPORARY RESIDENCE, AND ISSUANCE OF TEMPORARY RESIDENCE CARDS

  Article 3. Competence of relevant agencies in issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards

  1. Consular Department, Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as “Consular Department”) shall receive and process applications/notification of visa issuance, extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of Law on Entry and Exit.

  2. The Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs, Ministry of Foreign Affairs [3] shall receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration (excluding members of foreign consular missions based in Da Nang City southward and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants).

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City, Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as “the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City”):

  a) Receive and process applications for visas or notices of visa issuance and extension for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration in provinces and cities from Da Nang City southward.

  b) Receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners who are members of foreign consular missions in provinces and cities from Da Nang City southward and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants.

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions:

  a) Receive applications for visas according to Clause 2 Article 4 of this Circular, passports or passport substitute documents (hereinafter referred to as “passport”), application forms for visas (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 of the Ministry of Public Security on forms related to entry, exit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam - hereinafter referred to as “Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA”) of foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration to transfer information to the Department of Consular Affairs or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for processing within their jurisdiction.

  b) Issue visas to foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration according to notices of the Department of Consular Affairs or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  Article 4. Procedures for processing visa application/notification

  1. Agencies and organizations that invite or guarantee foreigners to enter Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3, Article 15 of the Law on Immigration shall directly send visa application notifications/petition to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs.
  Ho Chi Minh City.

  The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (or other agencies as prescribed by the sending country) of the country where the foreigner is from who falls under the provisions of Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 8 of the Law on Immigration may send visa application dossiers together with passports and visa application declarations (using form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the relevant foreigners to the Vietnamese representative missions in the host countries or the concurrent countries.

  The overseas Vietnamese representative mission shall, within 1 working day after receiving all the above-mentioned papers, forward information to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for processing the visa application/notification within its competence.

  2. A visa application consists of:

  a) A written document or diplomatic note on visa application/notification, which clearly states: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and symbol, date of issue - expiry date of the passport, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa duration, visa type and visa recipient of the foreigner.

  b) In case the foreigner is a member visiting a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, apart from the documents prescribed in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an additional copy of the identity card of the relevant member is required.

  c) In case a foreigner is a contractual employee working at a Vietnamese representative mission, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, 1 copy of the valid employment contract is required.

  d) In case a foreigner is subject to recruitment by a Vietnamese representative mission, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, a diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country notifying the recruitment is required.

  3. Settlement of visa application:

  a) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as the Immigration Department) and the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit within 1 working day from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier.

  b)[5] (annulled)

  c) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify in writing the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security within 1 working day from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier requesting visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit.

  The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall reply to the visa-issuing agency or organization and concurrently notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to foreigners after 2 working days from the notification to the Immigration Department if no opinion is given.

  d) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to foreigners including the following information: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and code, date of issue and expiry date of the passport, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa validity period, visa type, visa recipient of the foreigner and other information (if any).

  4. Issuing visas:

  a) Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall issue visas to foreigners within 01 working day from receipt of notices as prescribed in Point d Clause 3 of this Article, passports and visa application forms (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of foreigners.

  b) In case a foreigner is granted a visa at a border checkpoint, the receiving authority specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall notify the issuing authority or organization of the grant of the visa to the foreigner.

  5. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director General of Consular Department to decide reciprocal treatment with foreign countries regarding time limit for processing dossiers and components of dossiers applying for issuance of visas in accordance with Immigration Law.

  Article 5. Procedures for processing applications for extension of temporary residence

  1. Foreigners who have been issued with visas of types NG1, NG2 and NG4 and wish to extend their temporary residence after entering Vietnam shall, upon request, send notice/dossier of application for extension of temporary residence to Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City through agencies or organizations that invite and guarantee foreigners to enter Vietnam.

  2. Application for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Written notice or diplomatic note on application for extension of temporary residence, specifying full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and symbol, issuance date and expiry date, occupation, position, purpose of entry, duration of application for extension of temporary residence and need for issuance of new visa (if any) of the foreigner.

  b) The passport of the foreigner is still valid for more than 30 days compared to the duration of application for extension of temporary residence.

  c) The application form for issuance of visa or extension of temporary residence (using Form NA5 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) containing certification and seal of the agency or organization that invites or guarantees the foreigner.

  d) For a foreigner who visits a member of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, an copy of identity card of this member issued by the Department of State Protocol and Interpretation[6] or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City is required.

  e) For a foreigner who is a contractual employee working at a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, a copy of the valid employment contract is required.

  3. Processing applications for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Within 05 working days from the date on which the complete and valid application is received as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider extending the temporary residence and issue a new visa (if necessary) for the foreigner.

  b) Duration of temporary residence that is extended and duration of new visa that is issued (if any) must be appropriate to the purpose of entry and request of the agency or organization that invites or guarantees the foreigner and must not exceed 12 months, at least 30 days less than the duration of the foreigner’s passport.

  c) For the persons specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article, the duration of temporary residence that is extended and the duration of new visa that is issued (if any) must comply with Point b Clause 3 of this Article and the duration of identity card of the member of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam issued by the Department of State Protocol and Interpretation[7] or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  4. [8] Foreigners who have been granted visa-free entry to Vietnam and fall under the categories specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration shall be considered for extension of temporary residence and issuance of visas one or multiple times by the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City on the basis of the application submitted according to Clause 2 of this Article, unless otherwise provided by international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  5. The Director General of the Consular Department shall decide on the extension of temporary residence and issuance of new visas (if necessary) for foreigners falling under the categories specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration who have entered Vietnam with SQ visas.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director General of the Consular Department to decide on reciprocal agreements with foreign parties regarding the extension of temporary residence for foreigners in accordance with the Law on Immigration.

  Article 6. Procedures for processing applications for temporary residence cards

  1. Agencies that invite and guarantee foreigners as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration shall submit applications for temporary residence cards to competent agencies prescribed in Article 3 of this Circular.

  2. An application for a temporary residence card includes:

  a) A diplomatic note requesting the issuance of a temporary residence card from a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, specifying the applicant’s full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and issue date, occupation, and position (refer to Form NA6 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA);

  In case of replacement or addition of staff members of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, the diplomatic note requesting the issuance of a temporary residence card must specify the full name and title of the person replaced or added;

  b) Application for temporary residence card (using form NA8 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) bearing certification of competent persons and seal of Vietnam-based foreign representative missions;

  c) Valid passport of the foreigner;

  d) In case the foreigner is subject to increase in headcount of Vietnam-based foreign representative mission, apart from documents specified under Points a, b, and c Clause 1 of this Article, diplomatic note of Ministry of Foreign Affairs of host country must be added.

  3. Temporary residence card issuance:

  Within 5 working days from the date on which adequate application is received as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, Department of State Protocol and Interpretation or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider issuing temporary residence card to the foreigner.

  4.[10] The temporary residence card shall bear symbol NG3, have term of at most 5 years and shorter than remaining term of the passport by at least 30 days.

  Expired temporary residence card shall be considered for issuance of new temporary residence card at request of Vietnam-based foreign representative mission.

  5. Department of State Protocol and Interpretation and Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall transfer list of persons issued with temporary residence cards to immigration authorities according to Clause 2 Article 7 of Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP of the Government on cooperation between ministries, ministerial agencies, People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities in management of entry, exit, transit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize Director of State Protocol and External Interpretation Department to decide reciprocal treatment with foreign parties regarding processing time limit and components of application for temporary residence card in accordance with Immigration Law.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 7. Transitional provisions

  1. Validity of visas, certificates of temporary residence, temporary residence cards issued before the effective date hereof shall be valid until expiry of issued visas, certificates of temporary residence, temporary residence cards.

  2. Application for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, extension of temporary residence submitted before the effective date hereof that have not been processed shall be processed by competent authorities affiliated to Ministry of Foreign Affairs in accordance with this Circular.

  Article 8. Implementation

  1. Consular Department is responsible for:

  a) Overseas Vietnamese representative missions and relevant agencies shall grant visas and extend temporary residence in accordance with this Circular.

  b) Annually, report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the issuance of visas and extension of temporary residence in accordance with this Circular.

  2. The Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs [13] shall be responsible for:

  a) Guiding overseas Vietnamese representative missions and relevant agencies on procedures for issuance of temporary residence cards in accordance with this Circular.

  b) Annually, report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the issuance of temporary residence cards in accordance with this Circular.

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall be responsible for monthly, quarterly, and annual reports on visa issuance and extension of temporary residence to the Consular Department; and on issuance of temporary residence cards to the Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs [14].

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall be responsible for:

  a) Issuing visas according to notification of the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  b) Monthly, quarterly and annual reports on issuance of visas for foreigners under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall be sent to Department of Consular.

  Article 9. Entry into force [15]

  This Circular comes into force from September 1, 2016.

  Article 10. Responsibility for implementation

  Director of Department of Consular, Director of Department of State Protocol and Interpretation [16], Director of Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City and Heads of Vietnam representative missions in foreign countries and units affiliated to the Ministry are responsible for implementing this Circular. Any difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to Ministry of Foreign Affairs (via Department of Consular for visa issuance and extension procedures; via Department of State Protocol and Interpretation [17] for temporary residence card issuance procedures) for timely guidance./.
