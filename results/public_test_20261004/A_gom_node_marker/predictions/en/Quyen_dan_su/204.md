                                                   DECREE

                                     PROVISIONS ON FAMILY WORK

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;

  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated June 9, 2000;

  Pursuant to the Law on Gender Equality dated November 29, 2006;

  Pursuant to the Law on Prevention and Combat of Domestic Violence dated November 21, 2007;

  At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;

  The Government promulgates a Decree on family affairs,

  Chapter 1.

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Decree provides for family affairs; responsibilities of agencies and organizations in implementing family affairs.

  Family affairs are activities of agencies and organizations aimed at building prosperous, equal, progressive, happy and sustainable families.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Decree applies to agencies and organizations in implementing family affairs.

  Article 3. Agencies managing state affairs on family affairs

  1. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall be responsible before the Government for performing state management of family affairs nationwide.

  2. Ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall, within their tasks and powers, cooperate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in performing state management of family affairs.

  3. People’s Committees at all levels shall perform state management of family affairs in their localities.

  Chapter 2.

                                      FAMILY WORK

  Article 4. Formulation and implementation of policies and laws on family affairs

  Formulate and implement policies and laws on families; equality in families; prevention and combat against domestic violence and social evils invading families.

  Article 5. Planning, training and fostering of officials engaged in family affairs

  1. Formulate and implement planning for officials engaged in family affairs at all levels.

  2. Provide training and fostering to improve capacity of officials engaged in family affairs; compile documents on family affairs.

  3. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Ministry of Home Affairs shall elaborate Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 6. Propagating, mobilizing and disseminating knowledge about family affairs

  1. Propagate and disseminate the Party’s guidelines, State policies and laws on families, equality in families, prevention and combat against domestic violence, and prevention and control of social evils invading families, and family planning.

  2. Develop materials and disseminate knowledge about families, mobilize people to eliminate backward customs and practices on marriage and family; inherit, preserve and promote good traditional cultural values of families and clans associated with building advanced values of families in a developing society.

  Article 7. Organizations and agencies providing public services in the field of family

  1. The provision of public services in the field of family shall be carried out by public service providers and enterprises.

  2. Conditions, procedures and competence for establishment, reorganization and dissolution of public service providers and enterprises providing public services in the field of family shall comply with the provisions of Decree No. 55/2012/ND-CP dated June 28, 2012 of the Government on establishment, reorganization and dissolution of public service providers, the Law on Enterprises and relevant laws.

  Article 8. Family building support activities

  1. Provide pre-marriage counseling and education; provide knowledge and skills for building and organizing family life; appropriately integrate contents of family life education into educational programs at all levels; support the maintenance and preservation of good traditional values of families.

  2. Resolve family conflicts and disputes and protect the legitimate rights and interests of family members.

  3. Develop and guide the replication of models of happy and sustainable families.

  4. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Education and Training, the Ministry of Justice and relevant ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall elaborate Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Organization of Commemorating Vietnam Family Day

  Every year, organize the Commemorating Vietnam Family Day (June 28) to emphasize the responsibility of leaders of sectors, levels, mass organizations, social organizations and families for caring about building prosperous, equal, progressive, happy and sustainable families; encourage and promptly commend individuals, families with outstanding achievements in family work.

  Article 10. Inspection, examination and handling of violations against regulations on family work

  Organize activities of inspection, examination and handling of violations against regulations on family work as prescribed by law.

  Article 11. Scientific research and database development on families and family work

  1. Organize activities of investigation, survey and scientific research serving the formulation of policies and laws on family work.

  2. Develop a database on families and family work.

  3. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall elaborate Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. International cooperation

  Cooperation with other countries, international organizations, non-governmental organizations in developing policies, management experience, scientific research, expert exchange, technical and financial support in the field of family as prescribed by law.

  Article 13. Preliminary review, final review, information and reporting regime

  1. People’s Committees at all levels shall organize preliminary review, final review, assessment of implementation of activities related to family affairs in their localities, and report to superior authorities.

  2. Annually, before December 15, relevant ministries and central authorities, People’s Committees of provinces and centrally affiliated cities shall send reports on results of implementation of family affairs to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for consolidation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  Chapter 3.

                                             IMPLEMENTATION ORGANIZATION

  Article 14. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism

  1. Develop and implement policies and laws on families.

  2. Provide guidance and organize training and advanced training for family affairs officials.

  3. Provide guidance and propagate, mobilize, and disseminate policies, laws, and knowledge about family affairs.

  4. Develop and provide guidance on replicating family models.

  5. Perform state management of public service providers in the field of families.

  6. Provide guidance and organize the Commemoration of Vietnam’s Family Day.

  7. Conduct international cooperation and scientific research on families and family affairs as prescribed by law.

  8. Develop database on families and family affairs.

  9. Inspect, examine and handle violations against regulations on family affairs as prescribed by law.

  10. Conduct preliminary review, final review and periodic reports to the Prime Minister on implementation of family affairs.

  Article 15. Responsibilities of Ministry of Planning and Investment

  Take charge and cooperate with Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in providing guidelines for and integrating policies on family affairs into strategies, planning and plans for socio-economic development.

  Article 16. Responsibilities of Ministry of Finance

  Take charge and cooperate with Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and relevant ministries and central authorities in allocating funding for implementation of family affairs according to current regulations on state budget decentralization; provide guidelines and inspect relevant ministries, central authorities and local governments in allocation and use of funding for implementation of family affairs.

  Article 17. Responsibilities of Ministry of Justice

  1. To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and concerned agencies in, formulating policies and laws on family work within the ambit of its assigned functions and tasks.

  2. To guide the reconciliation of family conflicts and disputes in accordance with law.

  Article 18. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Education and Training

  To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Vietnam Women’s Union in, integrating contents of education on families into educational programs at all levels.

  Article 19. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Home Affairs

  To coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in guiding the implementation of the planning and strengthening of the contingent of officials engaged in family work at all levels.

  Article 20. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs

  To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and concerned ministries and agencies in, formulating policies and laws on protection, care for and education of children in families, care for and support of elderly people in families and prevention and combat of social evils invading families according to its assigned functions and tasks.

  Article 21. Responsibilities of ministries and ministerial agencies

  1. Take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in implementing contents related to family affairs within their assigned scope, functions, tasks and powers.

  2. Promulgate and submit to competent authorities for promulgation policies, laws, programs, and projects related to family affairs; provide guidance, inspect, examine, conduct preliminary and final reviews of implementation of policies, laws, programs, and projects under management responsibility for family affairs.

  3. Annually, review and summarize the performance of tasks related to family affairs under the state management domain, which are carried out by ministries and central authorities, and send them to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for consolidation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  Article 22. Responsibilities of People’s Committees at all levels

  1. Organize and inspect the implementation of policies and laws on family affairs in localities.

  2. Develop and implement family work programs and plans in conjunction with local socio-economic development programs.

  3. Allocate budget for family affairs in accordance with regulations of law on state budget.

  Article 23. Participation in state management of family affairs by the Women’s Union at all levels

  The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Governmental agencies and People’s Committees at all levels shall ensure that the Women’s Union at the same level participates in state management of family affairs in accordance with law and as prescribed in Articles 4, 6, Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of Article 8 and Article 9 of this Decree.

  Article 24. Participation in implementation of family affairs

  The Central Committee of the Vietnam Fatherland Front and its member organizations are requested to participate in propagating, disseminating and educating about policies and laws, inspecting and supervising the implementation of policies and laws on family affairs in accordance with law.

  Article 25. Funding for implementation of family affairs

  1. Funding for activities related to family affairs of ministries, sectors and People’s Committees at all levels is allocated from the state budget in accordance with law. The estimation and allocation of funding for family affairs shall comply with the Law on State Budget and relevant documents.

  2. Funding mobilized by domestic and foreign organizations and individuals for family affairs shall be managed and used in accordance with law.

  Chapter 4.

                                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 26. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on February 18, 2013.

  Article 27. Implementation responsibilities

  Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies, chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees and relevant agencies and organizations shall implement this Decree.
