                                                   DECREE

   OF THE GOVERNMENT NO. 77/2001/ND-CP OF OCTOBER 22, 2001 DETAILING THE REGISTRATION OF MARRIAGE ACCORDING TO THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY’S RESOLUTION NO. 35/2000/QH10 ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW

                                                   THE GOVERNMENT

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization of September 30, 1992;
  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family of June 9, 2000;
  Pursuant to the National Assembly's Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of June 9, 2000 on implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family;
  In order to strengthen the State management in the field of marriage and family, ensure the legitimate rights and interests of citizens, and move towards eliminating the non-registered marriage situation, and encourage the establishment of lawful marriage relations; At the proposal of the Minister of Justice,

                                                   DECREE:

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  1. This Decree details marriage registration for:

  a/ Cases of husband and wife relationship established before January 3, 1987, when the 1986 Marriage and Family Law took legal effect but marriage has not yet been registered;

  b/ Cases of man and woman living together as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001, when the 2000 Marriage and Family Law took legal effect but marriage has not yet been registered.

  2. The provisions in Clause 1 of this Article include cases where one party is a Vietnamese citizen and the other party is a citizen of a neighbor country bordering on Vietnam and has lived stably and for a long time in Vietnam; cases where one party is a Vietnamese citizen and the other party is a stateless person residing in Vietnam or both parties are stateless persons residing in Vietnam.

  Stateless persons residing in Vietnam mentioned in this Clause means those who have no Vietnamese nationality and are not recognized by other countries as having such nationality and are living stably and for a long time in Vietnam.

  Article 2. Encouragement of marriage registration and marriage registration obligation

  1. For cases of husband and wife relationship established before January 3, 1987 but marriage has not yet been registered, the State encourages and creates favorable conditions for marriage registration. Marriage registration for these cases is not restricted in terms of time.

  2. A man and a woman who cohabit as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001 and meet all conditions for marriage as prescribed in the 2000 Marriage and Family Law shall have the obligation to register their marriage. After January 1, 2003, if they fail to register their marriage, they will not be recognized by law as husband and wife.

  Article 3. Recognition of the effective date of marriage

  The marriage relationship between persons who register their marriage under this Decree is recognized from the date on which the partners establish the husband and wife relationship or cohabit as husband and wife in reality. The effective date of marriage must be clearly inscribed in the marriage registration book and marriage certificate according to the guidance of the Ministry of Justice.

  Article 4. Exemption from marriage registration fee

  The marriage registration for cases specified in this Decree is exempted from fees.

  Chapter 2:

                                        MARRIAGE REGISTRATION PROCEDURES

  Article 5. Competence and procedures for marriage registration

  1. The People's Committee of commune, ward or township (below referred to as commune-level People's Committee) where either partner registers his/her permanent residence shall perform the marriage registration.

  In case both partners have no permanent residence but register for temporary residence with a definite term according to regulations of law on household registration, the commune-level People's Committee where either partner registers for temporary residence with a definite term shall perform the marriage registration.

  2. Upon marriage registration, the partners are only required to make an application form for marriage registration and produce their identity cards or valid substitutes.

  In the application form for marriage registration, the partners shall write the date of establishment of their husband and wife relationship or cohabitation as husband and wife in reality. In case the husband and wife cannot determine the date of establishment of their husband and wife relationship or cohabitation as husband and wife in reality, the date shall be calculated as follows:

  If the month can be determined but the day cannot be determined, the first day of the next month shall be taken;

  If the year can be determined but the day and month cannot be determined, the first day of January of the next year shall be taken.

  Article 6. Settlement of marriage registration for husband and wife relationship established before January 3, 1987

  In case both husband and wife permanently or temporarily reside at the place of marriage registration, the People's Committee shall perform the marriage registration right after receiving the application form for marriage registration.

  In case either of the two partners does not permanently or temporarily reside at the place where their marriage is registered but the People's Committee knows well about their marital status, the marriage registration shall be processed immediately. When there are unclear circumstances as to whether the partners have committed a violation against monogamy, the People's Committee shall request them to make a written commitment which is certified by at least two witnesses. The witnesses shall take responsibility before law for the accuracy of their testimonies.

  Article 7. Marriage registration in case a man and a woman cohabit as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001

  In case either of the two partners has no permanent residence registration or temporary residence registration at the place where their marriage is registered, the marriage registration form must be certified by the agency or unit where he/she works (for officials, public employees and persons serving in the armed forces) or the People's Committee of the commune where his/her permanent residence or temporary residence is registered. If both partners permanently or temporarily reside at the place where their marriage is registered, the above certification is not required.

  After receiving the marriage registration form, the People's Committee shall check it and process the marriage registration if both partners meet all conditions prescribed in Article 9 of the 2000 Law on Marriage and Family. When there are unclear circumstances as to whether one or both partners meet all conditions prescribed in Article 9 of the 2000 Law on Marriage and Family, the People's Committee shall request them to make a written commitment which is certified by at least two witnesses. The witnesses shall take responsibility before law for the accuracy of their testimonies.

  Article 8. Places of marriage registration

  To create favorable conditions for the marriage partners, the marriage registration may be carried out at the office of the People's Committee or at a village, hamlet or residential quarter according to the plan of the People's Committee of the commune.

  Article 9. Recognition of common children of husband and wife

  Children born before the date of marriage registration for cases specified in Article 1 of this Decree and recognized by their parents are also common children of husband and wife.

  If the birth register and birth certificate of a child still leave blank regarding his/her father, the People's Committee of commune where the birth is registered shall, based on the marriage certificate of the parents, record the father in the birth register and birth certificate of the child, and at the same time cross out the note "illegitimate child" in the birth register.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 10. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Justice The Ministry of Justice shall be responsible for assisting the Government in directing, guiding and inspecting the implementation of this Decree.

  Article 11. Responsibilities of People's Committees at all levels

  1. Provincial-level People's Committees and district-level People's Committees shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, guide, direct, inspect and urge commune-level People's Committees to implement this Decree.

  2. Commune-level People's Committees shall:

  a/ To propagate, mobilize, review and make lists of cases subject to marriage registration under this Decree;

  b/ To organize timely, convenient and accurate marriage registration for cases specified in this Decree;

  c/ To accurately and promptly certify the marital status of the marriage partners, if requested;

  d/ To ensure balance of funds from the state budget under current regulations for marriage registration activities mentioned in this Decree.

  Article 12.- Implementation effect

  1. This Decree takes effect 15 days after its signing.

  2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial/municipal People's Committees shall implement this Decree.
