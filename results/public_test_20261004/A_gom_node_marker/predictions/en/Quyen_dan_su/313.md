                                                   CIRCULAR

                GUIDANCE ON HEALTHCARE FOR THE AGELESS

  Pursuant to Articles 14, 15 and 16 of the Ordinance No. 23/2000/PL-UBTVQH of April 28, 2000 on the elderly, which assigns tasks to the health sector; pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 30/2002/ND-CP of March 26, 2002, defining and guiding a number of articles of the Ordinance on the Elderly; pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 120/2003/ND-CP of January 20, 2003 amending Article 9 of Decree No. 30/2002/ND-CP of January 20, 2003; and pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 49/2003/ND-CP of May 15, 2003 defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Health, The Ministry of Health shall provide guidelines for elderly care work as follows:

  I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. The elderly receiving health care under this Circular are citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or Vietnamese elderly residing abroad and foreign elderly during their stay in Vietnam from 60 years old. Old age is often accompanied by poor health and diseases; the elderly suffer from diseases accounting for about 95%. On average, an elderly person suffers from 2.69 diseases, mainly chronic and non-transmissible diseases, so the deepest aspiration of the elderly is to live healthy lives, receive health care, and have medical examination and treatment when they are sick. This is a legitimate requirement of the elderly, requiring the attention of all sectors, all levels and the entire society.

  2. The elderly shall receive initial health care and be given priority to medical examination and treatment at medical establishments.

  3. People’s Committees at all levels shall ensure the health care regime for the elderly in their localities. The health sector shall be responsible for professional and technical matters in health care for the elderly.

  II. ORGANIZATION OF MANAGEMENT AND HEALTH CARE FOR THE ELDERLY

  People’s Committees and People’s Councils at all levels shall direct the health sector to coordinate with relevant sectors in formulating plans and organizing the implementation of plans on health care for the elderly in their localities according to the following requirements:

  1. To organize the propagation and dissemination of contents on physical training, health enhancement and disease prevention, especially common diseases among the elderly, so that the elderly can prevent themselves.

  2. To organize health checkups to make health records for the elderly. Periodic health checkups shall be carried out once a year, gradually organize the network of family doctors and home-based health care services for the elderly.

  3. To organize various forms of senior citizen clubs such as health promoting clubs and outdoor health clubs. Depending on conditions and requirements of seniors, each locality may organize clubs for persons suffering from chronic diseases such as diabetes, asthma, hypertension, heart disease, kidney and urinary tract diseases, cataract, etc.

  4. To prioritize investment in community-based functional rehabilitation programs for disabled seniors to prevent and rehabilitate sequelae caused by trauma, accidents or cerebrovascular complications, chronic diseases, occupational diseases, etc.

  5. To organize a network of volunteers to provide home-based healthcare for the elderly, especially poor and lonely people who have no one to rely on. To encourage retired health workers to volunteer or train volunteers in necessary knowledge about healthcare for the elderly, such as self-care activities in personal living, especially for those suffering from sequelae after heart disease, accident, social communication and self-care.

  III. HEALTHCARE AND MEDICAL TREATMENT FOR THE ELDERLY

  1. To organize a network of healthcare and medical treatment for the elderly:

  a/ The Institute of Geriatrics shall:

  - Organize research and application of scientific and technical advances in three fields: clinical geriatrics, basic geriatrics and social geriatrics, and propose measures to improve the quality of healthcare for Vietnamese the elderly.

  - Implement professional technical direction in healthcare for the elderly nationwide. Organize inspection of the implementation of professional processes, standards and equipment for lower-level geriatric establishments to ensure training and rehabilitation of post-disease functions for the elderly.

  - To provide specialized treatment on geriatric medicine.

  - To coordinate with the Medical University of Hanoi and other training schools for health workers in providing undergraduate and postgraduate training in geriatrics. To compile specialized training textbooks and disseminate knowledge about health care for the elderly.

  b. General hospitals of class I and class II: To establish geriatric departments and have separate examination rooms for the elderly patients at the medical examination departments.

  c. Specialized hospitals (except for specialized hospitals for children): To organize medical examination for the elderly at the medical examination departments; when the elderly patients need to be treated in the inpatient department, the hospitals must organize the reception and attend to them in a thorough manner, not let the patients lie in the same bed.

  d. Medical centers of urban districts, rural districts, towns and provincial cities: To have some beds for the elderly patients at the clinical departments and organize medical examination for the elderly patients at the medical examination departments.

  e. Hospitals for functional rehabilitation must continue to be built and consolidated according to the provisions of Decision No. 966/BYT of May 30, 1999 of the Minister of Health on guidelines for operation of hospitals for functional rehabilitation; to reserve an appropriate proportion of beds for caring for and rehabilitating the elderly patients. To coordinate with the Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs sector in implementing the regime of convalescence for the elderly who have made meritorious services to the country.

  f. The commune, ward or township health stations shall manage the health of the elderly people in their localities and provide initial health care for them. Where the elderly people are disabled, lonely and helpless and sick but do not come to the prescribed places for medical examination and treatment, the heads of the commune-level health stations shall appoint health workers to conduct medical examination and treatment at the places where the elderly people live or report to the local People's Committees to organize the bringing of the sick people to medical examination and treatment establishments.

  2. Treatment work.

  - The examination and treatment for older patients shall be carried out by specialized medical teams; in case of emergency, they shall be transferred to the nearest health establishment.

  - Medical establishments must prioritize examinations for older patients (after emergency care).

  - Gradually organize management of chronic diseases for older persons according to programs and projects so as to detect and handle them in time when abnormal developments occur. Plans for disease prevention and treatment for chronic diseases are required.

  - Intensify rehabilitation work for older patients after acute treatment at hospitals and guide their continued treatment at home.

  - Combine traditional medicine with modern medicine, develop non-pharmaceutical treatment methods especially at grassroots health facilities for older patients.

  IV. FUNDS FOR INITIAL HEALTH CARE AND EXAMINATION AND TREATMENT OF OLDER PERSONS

  1. Funds for health checkups for making health monitoring books for older persons, organizing periodic health checkups and initial health care activities for older persons shall be borne by local governments.

  2. Funds for examination and treatment:

  a/ Persons with compulsory health insurance cards or voluntary health insurance cards shall be covered by social insurance for medical examination and treatment according to the provisions of law on health insurance.

  b/ Persons eligible for the regime of medical examination and treatment for the poor shall be covered by the fund for medical examination and treatment for the poor for medical examination and treatment expenses according to the Prime Minister's Decision No. 139/2002/QD-TTg of January 15, 2002 on medical examination and treatment for the poor and Joint Circular No. 14/2002/Tr-LT-BYT-BTC of December 16, 2002, between the Ministry of Health and the Ministry of Finance, guiding the implementation of the above-said Decision.

  c/ Elderly persons aged 90 years or older shall enjoy the health insurance regime as prescribed in the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs' Circular No. 24/2003 of January 6, 2003, guiding the implementation of the Government's Decree No. 120/2003/ND-CP of January 20, 2003.

  3. Persons who have no health insurance card as prescribed at Point 2 above shall have to pay by themselves expenses for medical examination and treatment.

  4. Domestic and foreign organizations and individuals as well as families of elderly persons are encouraged to buy health insurance cards for them.

  5. Local governments should set up funds for care of the health of the elderly to support the care of their health and medical examination and treatment.

  V. ORGANIZATION OF IMPLEMENTATION

  l/ At provincial, district and communal levels, the care of the health of the elderly shall be organized by the provincial-level government authorities in charge of health as heads of the boards, the health sector in charge of health as standing members, together with the finance, labor, war invalids and social affairs sectors, relevant sectors and representatives of the elderly associations, the Red Cross Society, the Fatherland Front, the Peasants' Association and the Women's Union, to ensure and maintain the health care activities for the elderly in localities. To unify the health care activities, the boards for care of the health of the elderly should be incorporated into the local initial health care boards.

  2. Departments of Health of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall advise People’s Committees of provinces on developing plans for health care for older persons throughout their provinces according to this Circular and direct district-level health authorities and commune-level health authorities to participate in health care for older persons within their localities.

  3. Department of Treatment shall cooperate with relevant departments, functional departments, Inspectorate of Ministry of Health, Geriatrics Institute and relevant units in providing guidance, directing, inspecting and examining implementation of this Circular.

  4. Health communication and education centers shall cooperate with Geriatrics Institute in disseminating knowledge about health care for older persons.

  5. This Circular takes effect 15 days after its publication in official gazette. Difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry of Health (Department of Treatment) for consideration and settlement or amendment and supplementation as appropriate.
