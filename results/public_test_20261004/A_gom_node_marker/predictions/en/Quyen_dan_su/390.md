                                               JOINT CIRCULAR

     OF THE MINISTRY OF JUSTICE - THE MINISTRY OF PUBLIC SECURITY No. 07/1999/TTLT-BTP-BCA of February 8, 1999, Regulating the Issuance of Legal Aid Certificates

  Pursuant to Decree No. 38-CP of June 4, 1993 of the Government on the functions, tasks, powers and organizational apparatus of the Ministry of Justice;
  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 37/1998/ND-CP of June 9, 1998, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational apparatus of the Ministry of Public Security;
  In order to meet citizens' demands for issuance of judicial record cards;
  The Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Public Security shall stipulate the issuance of judicial record cards as follows:

  I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. Judicial record card A judicial record card is a card issued by a competent state agency defined at Point 5, Section I of this Joint Circular to a requester in order to certify whether or not he/she has a previous conviction.

  The form of judicial record card is Form No. 01/TP-LLTP, promulgated together with this Joint Circular.

  2. Principles for certification of having or not having a previous conviction

  A person shall be considered having a previous conviction only when he/she has been sentenced by a court with legal effect for committing a crime and his/her criminal record has not yet been expunged under law.

  3. Right to request issuance of criminal record certificates Vietnamese citizens may request competent state agencies to issue criminal record certificates for use in necessary cases as prescribed by law.

  Foreigners may also request competent state agencies of Vietnam specified at Point 5, Section I of this Joint Circular to issue criminal record certificates to certify whether or not they have any previous criminal conviction during their stay in Vietnam.

  Persons requesting the issuance of criminal record certificates may authorize other persons to carry out procedures for requesting the issuance of criminal record certificates on their behalf.

  4. Non-issuance of criminal record certificates The following cases shall not be issued with criminal record certificates:

  a/ Applications for issuance of criminal record certificates are made by unauthorized persons;

  b/ Persons who are currently accused or defendants in criminal cases.

  5. Competence to issue criminal record certificates The issuance of criminal record certificates shall fall under the jurisdiction of provincial/municipal Justice Services (below referred to as provincial/municipal Justice Services).

  Provincial/municipal Justice Services shall coordinate with provincial-level Police Departments (below referred to as provincial Police Departments) and, when necessary, with courts in verifying the judicial records of involved parties.

  6. Fees

  An applicant for a judicial record card shall pay a fee as prescribed by law.

  II. PROCEDURES AND ORDER FOR ISSUANCE OF LEGAL RECORD

  1. Procedures for submission and receipt of applications for issuance of criminal record certificates

  a) Application for issuance of criminal record certificates The applicant for issuance of a criminal record certificate shall file an application according to Form No. 02/TP-LLTTP promulgated together with this Joint Circular; the application for issuance of a criminal record certificate must be fully, clearly and accurately declared.

  Enclosed with the application shall be copies of identity cards and household registration books or other valid documents evidencing the places of residence of the applicants; for foreigners, copies of their passports and certificates of permanent or temporary residence in Vietnam.

  In case of authorization for carrying out procedures to request issuance of judicial record cards, there must also be a written authorization certified by the People's Committee of the commune, ward or township where the authorizing person or authorized person resides; if the authorizing person is a Vietnamese citizen residing abroad, the written authorization must be certified by the diplomatic mission or consular office of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam in such country; if the authorizing person is a foreigner who has left Vietnam, the written authorization must be certified by the competent authority of the country of which he/she is a citizen or a country of permanent residence and must be legalized by consular procedures in accordance with Vietnamese law.

  The application for issuance of a criminal record certificate shall be made in 2 sets.

  b/ Submission of applications for issuance of judicial record cards Applicants for issuance of judicial record cards who are Vietnamese citizens residing in the country shall submit their applications at provincial-level Justice Departments of localities where they permanently reside; if they reside abroad, their applications shall be submitted to the provincial-level Justice Department of the last locality where they resided before leaving Vietnam.

  Applicants for issuance of judicial record cards who are foreigners residing in Vietnam shall submit their applications at provincial-level Justice Departments of localities where they reside; if they have left Vietnam, their applications shall be submitted to the provincial-level Justice Department of the last locality where they resided before leaving Vietnam.

  c/ Receipt of applications

  Upon receipt of an application, the provincial-level Justice Department shall check the contents declared in the application and the validity of enclosed papers; if any details are missing or unclear, it shall request the applicant to supplement or clarify them.

  If finding that the application is valid, the provincial-level Justice Department shall receive it, collect fee and issue to the applicant a notice of the date of result notification.

  In case it finds that the case does not fall under its handling competence, the provincial-level Justice Department shall immediately guide the involved party in submitting the application according to the correct address.

  The receipt of an application must be recorded in the Judicial Record Card-issuing Register.

  2. Procedures for searching and verifying judicial records

  a. Searching for records of police authorities Within 3 days from the date on which adequate and valid records are received, Departments of Justice shall send criminal record verification forms using Form No. 03/TP-LLTP enclosed with this Joint Circular and 1 set of records to provincial police authorities for searching.

  Within 10 days from the date on which criminal record verification forms are received, provincial police authorities shall search and verify criminal records of involved parties and send Criminal record verification results (on the back of criminal record verification forms) to Departments of Justice. In case of complicated cases requiring search of records of Police Operational Records Department affiliated to Ministry of Public Security, this time limit may be extended for another 10 days at most; provincial police authorities shall contact Police Operational Records Department.

  b. Searching for records of courts

  In case details of involved parties' previous convictions are suspected through search of records of police authorities but there is not enough basis to make conclusion or the part about their previous convictions is unclear and inadequate, Departments of Justice shall contact courts that have adjudicated lawsuits related to involved parties to search records of previous convictions in order to clarify whether involved parties have been convicted or not.

  Search of records of courts (if any) must be conducted immediately after receiving criminal record verification results of provincial police authorities; time limit for searching records of previous convictions at courts shall not exceed 7 days.

  3. Making and issuing criminal record certificates Within 3 days from the date on which search results of records of police authorities and courts (if any) are available, Departments of Justice shall issue criminal record certificates to involved parties.

  a/ How to make judicial record cards If the involved parties have no previous criminal conviction (not yet been sentenced or having been sentenced but their criminal records have been expunged), they shall write clearly "No previous criminal conviction" in the column of "previous criminal conviction" and cross the columns (1), (2), (3), (4) and (5) of the judicial record card; if the involved parties have previous criminal convictions, they shall clearly write down the crimes, applicable legal provisions, principal and additional penalties (if any) and the date the judgment was pronounced and took legal effect; if there are many previous criminal convictions, they shall be listed in the order of time.

  In cases where the involved parties have been sentenced but meet conditions for automatic expungement of criminal records or conditional expungement of criminal records as prescribed by the penal legislation, the provincial/municipal Justice Services shall guide them in carrying out procedures to request competent People's Courts to expunge their criminal records according to the provisions of the criminal procedure legislation.

  b/ Issuance of judicial record cards A judicial record card is only one original and not permitted to be copied. Depending on the purpose of requesting issuance of judicial record cards by the involved parties, the provincial/municipal Justice Services shall issue the necessary number of judicial record cards to the involved parties.

  4. Archiving of judicial record dossiers Judicial record dossiers include applications for issuance of judicial record cards, the involved parties' enclosed papers and judicial record cards with the results of dossier search by the police offices or courts (if any).

  Judicial record dossiers must be archived and preserved at the provincial/municipal Justice Services under the law on archives.

  III. IMPLEMENTATION ORGANIZATION

  1. The Minister of Justice and the Minister of Public Security shall have to direct and guide their respective branches in implementing this Joint Circular.

  2. The provincial/municipal Justice Services and Public Security Services shall appoint professional officers to do the judicial record work; provinces and cities with many requests for issuance of judicial record cards shall appoint officers to do this work exclusively.

  3. Every 6 months and annually, Departments of Justice shall report to Ministry of Justice; provincial police authorities shall report to Ministry of Public Security on implementation of this Joint Circular.

  4. This Joint Circular comes into force 15 days after its signing.

  Difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to Ministry of Justice or Ministry of Public Security for consideration and settlement.
