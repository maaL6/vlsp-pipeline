                                                   CIRCULAR

  GUIDELINES ON ISSUANCE OF VISAS, PERMANENT RESIDENCE PERMITS AND PERMANENT RESIDENCE CARDS FOR FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM UNDER THE MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS’S JURISDICTION

  Pursuant to Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;

  Pursuant to the Law on promulgation of legal documents dated June 03, 2008;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 58/2013/ND-CP dated June 11, 2013 defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP dated August 06, 2015 defining the mechanism for cooperation between ministries, ministerial-level agencies and People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities in management of entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam;

  The Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgates the Circular guiding procedures for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards and extension of temporary residence for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  Chapter I

                                              GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidelines on issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs as prescribed in Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, Clause 4 Article 8 of Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Entry and Exit”).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to foreigners under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs entering and residing in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.

  Chapter II

              COMPETENCE AND PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE OF VISAS, EXTENSION OF TEMPORARY RESIDENCE, AND ISSUANCE OF TEMPORARY RESIDENCE CARDS

  Article 3. Competence of relevant agencies in issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards

  1. Consular Department, Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as “Consular Department”) shall receive and process applications/notification of issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, Clause 4 Article 8 of Law on Entry and Exit.

  2. The State Protocol Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the State Protocol Department) shall receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration (except for members of foreign consular missions in Ho Chi Minh City and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants).

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City):

  a) Receive and process applications for visas and extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration in provinces and cities from Quang Nam and Da Nang southward.

  b) Receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners who are members of foreign consular missions in Ho Chi Minh City and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants;

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions:

  a) Receive applications for visa issuance according to Clause 2 Article 4 of this Circular, passports or passport substitute documents (hereinafter referred to as passports), and application forms for visa issuance (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 5, 2015 of the Ministry of Public Security on forms related to entry, exit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam - hereinafter referred to as Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration to transfer information to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for processing within their jurisdiction.

  b) Issue visas to foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration according to notices of the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  Article 4. Procedures for processing visa application

  1. Agencies and organizations that invite or guarantee foreigners to Vietnam as prescribed in Clause 2, Clause 3 Article 15 of Law on Immigration shall directly send visa application to Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs.
  Ho Chi Minh City.

  Ministry of Foreign Affairs (or other agencies as prescribed by sending country) of country where foreigner under Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, Clause 4 Article 8 of Law on Immigration may send visa application together with passport and visa application form (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of relevant foreigners to Vietnamese representative missions in host country or concurrent country.

  Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall inform Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City within 1 working day from the date on which they receive adequate documents to process visa application within their competence.

  2. Application for visa includes:

  a) Written document or diplomatic note requesting visa, specifying: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, number and signature of passport, date of issue - expiry date of passport, occupation, position, entry purpose, duration of visa, type of visa and visa recipient of the foreigner.

  b) In case the foreigner is a member visiting a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, apart from documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an additional copy of ID card of the member of the relevant representative mission is required.

  c) In case a foreigner who is a contractual employee working at a Vietnamese representative mission, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, 1 copy of the valid employment contract is required.

  d) In case a foreigner is subject to recruitment by a Vietnamese representative mission, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, a diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country notifying the recruitment is required.

  3. Settlement of visa application:

  a) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as the Immigration Department) and the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit within 1 working day from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier.

  b) The Consular Department shall send a written request for visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit to the Immigration Department, the State Protocol Department and relevant units within 1 working day from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier. After receiving a complete reply from the State Protocol Department and relevant units, if the Immigration Department does not have any opinion, the Consular Department shall reply to the requesting agency or organization and notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to the foreigners.

  c) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security within 1 working day from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier requesting visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit.

  The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall reply to the requesting agency or organization and notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to the foreigners after 2 working days from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier, if the Immigration Department does not have any opinion.

  d) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to the foreigner, including the following information: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and code, date of issue and expiry date of the passport, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa duration, visa type, visa recipient of the foreigner, and other information (if necessary).

  4. Implementation of visa issuance:

  a) The overseas Vietnamese representative mission shall issue a visa to the foreigner within 01 working day from the receipt of the notification as prescribed in Point d Clause 3 of this Article, the passport and the declaration for visa issuance (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the foreigner.

  b) In case the foreigner is approved for visa issuance at the border checkpoint, the receiving agency specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall notify the agency or organization that notifies/presents the visa application about the foreigner's acceptance of the visa at the border checkpoint.

  5. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director of the Consular Department to decide the reciprocal treatment with the foreign party regarding the processing time and contents of the application for visa issuance in accordance with the Law on Immigration.

  Article 5. Procedures for processing applications for extension of temporary residence

  1. Foreigners who have been issued visas with codes NG1, NG2 and NG4, if they wish to extend their temporary residence after entering Vietnam, must send their application for extension of temporary residence to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City through the agency or organization that invites or guarantees the foreigner’s entry into Vietnam.

  2. Application for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Written notice or diplomatic note of extension of temporary residence, specifying: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, number and symbol of passport, date of issue - expiry date of passport, occupation, position, entry purposes, duration of application for extension of temporary residence and need for issuance of new visa (if any) of the foreigner.

  b) Passport of the foreigner is still valid for more than 30 days compared to time of application for extension of temporary residence.

  c) Declaration for issuance of visa, application for extension of temporary residence (using form NA5 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA), certified and stamped by agencies, organizations inviting and sponsoring the foreigner.

  d) For foreigners entering Vietnam to visit members of foreign representative missions in Vietnam, attach 1 copy of identity card issued by the State Protocol Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  e) For foreigners who are contract workers working at foreign representative missions in Vietnam, attach 1 copy of valid employment contract.

  3. Processing applications for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Within 5 working days from the date on which adequate documents specified in Clause 2 of this Article are received, the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider extending temporary residence and issuing new visas (if necessary) for the foreigners.

  b) Duration of temporary residence extended and duration of new visas issued (if any) must be appropriate to entry purposes and request of agencies, organizations inviting and sponsoring and must not exceed 12 months, at least 30 days less than the duration of passport of the foreigners.

  c) For persons specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article, the extension of temporary residence duration and issuance of new visas (if any) shall comply with Point b Clause 3 of this Article and the period of identity verification by members of foreign representative missions in Vietnam issued by the State Protocol Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  4. Foreigners who have entered Vietnam under visa exemption and are specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration may be considered for one or more times extension of their temporary residence duration by the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City on the basis of the application submitted according to Clause 2 of this Article, unless otherwise provided by international agreements to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  5. The Director General of the Consular Department shall decide the extension of temporary residence duration and issuance of new visas (if necessary) for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration who have entered Vietnam under SQ visas.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director General of the Consular Department to decide the reciprocal extension of temporary residence duration for foreigners in accordance with the Law on Immigration.

  Article 6. Procedures for processing applications for temporary residence cards

  1. Agencies that invite and guarantee foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration shall submit applications for temporary residence cards to competent agencies specified in Article 3 of this Circular.

  2. An application for a temporary residence card includes:

  a) A diplomatic note requesting the issuance of a temporary residence card from the foreign representative mission in Vietnam, specifying the applicant’s full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and symbol, occupation and position (refer to Form NA6 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA);

  In case of replacement or addition of new staff members of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, the diplomatic note requesting the issuance of temporary residence card must specify full name and title of the person who is replaced or added to the staff;

  b) Declaration requesting the issuance of temporary residence card (using form NA8 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA), bearing certification of competent persons and seal of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam;

  c) Valid passport of the foreigner;

  d) In case the foreigner is subject to increase in staff of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam, apart from documents specified in Points a, b, c Clause 1 of this Article, the diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country must be additionally sent to inform about the increase in staff.

  3. Temporary residence card issuance:

  Within 05 working days from the date on which the satisfactory application is received as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the Department of State Protocol or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider issuing the temporary residence card to the foreigner.

  4. The temporary residence card has the code NG3, valid for no more than 05 years and shorter than the duration of the passport by at least 30 days.

  The expired temporary residence card shall be considered for issuance of a new one at the request of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam.

  5. The State Protocol Department and the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall transfer the list of persons granted temporary residence cards to the immigration management agency in accordance with Clause 2 Article 7 of Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP of the Government on the mechanism for cooperation among ministries, ministerial agencies, and People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities in managing the entry, exit, transit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director of the State Protocol Department to decide the reciprocal application with foreign parties regarding the processing time limit and the contents of applications for temporary residence cards in accordance with the Law on Entry and Exit.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 7. Transitional provisions

  1. Visas, certificates of temporary residence, and temporary residence cards that are valid before the effective date of this Circular may continue to be used until their expiration dates.

  2. Applications for visas, temporary residence cards, or extension of temporary residence that have been submitted before the effective date of this Circular but have not yet been processed shall be processed by the competent authority of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in accordance with this Circular.

  Article 8. Implementation

  1. The Consular Department shall:

  a) Guide overseas Vietnamese representative missions and relevant agencies in granting visas and extending temporary residence according to this Circular.

  b) Annually report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the situation of visa issuance and extension of temporary residence according to this Circular.

  2. The State Protocol Department shall:

  a) Guide foreign representative missions in Vietnam and relevant agencies in procedures for issuance of temporary residence cards according to this Circular.

  b) Annually report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the situation of issuance of temporary residence cards according to this Circular.

  3. The Department of External Relations of Ho Chi Minh City shall monthly, quarterly and annually report on the issuance of visas and extension of temporary residence to the Consular Department; and on the issuance of temporary residence cards to the State Protocol Department.

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall:

  a) Issuing visas according to notices of Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  b) Monthly, quarterly and annual reports on issuance of visas for foreigners under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall be sent to Consular Department.

  Article 9. Effect

  This Circular takes effect from September 01, 2016.

  Article 10. Responsibility for implementation

  Director General of Consular Department, Director General of State Protocol Department, Director of Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City and Heads of Vietnam representative offices located overseas and units affiliated to Ministry are responsible for implementation of this Circular. Any problems arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to Ministry of Foreign Affairs (via Consular Department in case of procedures for issuance of visas and extension of temporary residence; via State Protocol Department in case of procedures for issuance of temporary residence cards) for timely guidance./.
