                                                    PROCLAMATION

                   ON THE ORGANIZATION OF THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW OF 2000

  The Marriage and Family Law of 2000 was passed on June 9, 2000 by the Xth National Assembly at its 7th session and takes effect as from January 1, 2001.

  In order to promptly implement the Marriage and Family Law and Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of June 9, 2000 of the National Assembly on implementation of the 2000 Marriage and Family Law, the Prime Minister hereby issues the following Directive:

  I. TO REVIEW THE LAW ON MARRIAGE AND FAMILY AND PROVIDE GUIDANCE ON IMPLEMENTATION OF THE LAW

  1. From now till the end of 2000, the Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for reviewing the current marriage and family legislation in order to propose the Government and other competent agencies to amend or annul documents related to marriage and family which are no longer compatible with the new Marriage and Family Law.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with concerned agencies and organizations in, preparing draft laws to submit to the Government or the National Assembly Standing Committee for promulgation in order to guide the implementation of the provisions of the Law, especially the land use right for farming, residential and housing related to marriage and family relations; marriage registration, adoption registration in remote areas, civil status registration of marriage and family involving foreign elements, including civil status registration of marriage and family involving foreign elements in border areas with neighboring countries; and sanction administrative violations in the field of marriage and family. The above-said tasks must be completed by the second quarter of 2001.

  3. The Committee for Ethnic Minorities and Mountainous Areas shall assume the prime responsibility for preparing a proposal to be submitted to the Government for promulgation in the first quarter of 2001, defining the application of the Marriage and Family Law to ethnic minority groups, plans for handling actual adoption cases in ethnic minority areas, together with a list of backward customs and practices on marriage and family not allowed to apply and a list of good customs and practices on marriage and family to be promoted.

  II. COMMUNICATION, PUBLICIZATION AND EDUCATION OF THE LAW ON MARRIAGE AND FAMILY

  1. The communication and publicization of the Law on Marriage and Family must be carried out regularly, in-depth and extensively among officials, public employees, officers and soldiers of the people’s armed forces and among the people, especially in communes, wards, townships, population clusters, agencies, organizations, enterprises, schools and families.

  The communication and publicization must aim to make everyone clearly understand the principles and basic contents of the Law, especially the newly amended and supplemented contents and the Resolution of the National Assembly on the settlement of the actual marriage situation so as to make everyone voluntarily and actively comply with it, raise their awareness and concern about personal responsibility for family building, making each family truly become a happy home of each individual, making Vietnam society truly become a big family of all Vietnamese people who work together to build an independent, free, prosperous and happy country.

  From the third quarter of 2000, ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and People’s Committees at all levels shall have to work out and organize the implementation of plans on communication, publicization and education of the Law on Marriage and Family within their agencies, units and localities; regard this as a key task of the communication, publicization and education of law for the last six months of 2000 and the whole year 2001, and at the same time renew forms and measures of communication and education of the law on marriage and family so as to make such activities more in-depth, avoiding the communication and education of law in a movement, heavy on the surface and with low efficiency.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall coordinate with the Central Committee of the Vietnam Fatherland Front, the Central Committee of the Vietnam Women’s Union, the Vietnam General Confederation of Labor, the Central Committee of the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, the Vietnam Peasants’ Association, the Vietnam War Veterans’ Association, the Ministry of Culture and Information, the Vietnam Committee for Child Protection and Care, the Committee for Population and Family Planning, the Vietnam Lawyers’ Association, the central and local mass media agencies in disseminating and popularizing the Marriage and Family Law and the Resolution of the National Assembly on the implementation of this Law; direct and guide the intensification of legal counseling activities on marriage and family, encourage the establishment of various types of family clubs where people can participate in activities according to gender, age, profession or interest to exchange experiences and learn from each other about necessary matters in their responsibilities as wives, husbands, fathers, mothers, grandparents… so that from the beginning they can avoid misunderstandings and regrettable things not worth doing in the real important mission in family life.

  The Government’s Council for Coordination in Law Dissemination and Education shall determine the contents and programs for disseminating and popularizing the Marriage and Family Law and the Resolution of the National Assembly on the implementation of this Law; coordinate with concerned agencies and organizations in compiling training plans and materials suitable for each type of target audience and train rapporteurs for disseminating and popularizing the Marriage and Family Law.

  3. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall coordinate with the Central Committee for Ideological and Cultural Affairs and the Ministry of Justice in directing the mass media to plan to prioritize the dissemination and popularization of the Marriage and Family Law from now till the end of 2000 and 2001; guide the formulation and implementation of community activity rules related to the provisions of the Marriage and Family Law.

  4. The People’s Daily, Radio Voice of Vietnam, Vietnam Television and other mass media in central and local areas regularly have programs and special sections introducing and explaining the contents and new points of the Law on Marriage and Family.

  5. The Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy in, organizing intensive training courses on the Law on Marriage and Family for judges, procurators, court clerks, people’s jurors, civil status workers and other judicial officials to ensure uniform application and supervision of the implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family.

  6. The Ministry of Education and Training shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in, directing the review of the system of teaching textbooks and materials of universities, colleges and documents on civic education related to the Law on Marriage and Family in vocational secondary schools, general secondary schools and elementary schools in order to work out plans for timely supplementation and amendment thereof in conformity with the Law on Marriage and Family; organize training courses for teachers who teach law and civic education on the Law on Marriage and Family.

  7. The Committee for Ethnic Minorities and Mountainous Areas shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in, working out plans to popularize and propagate the Law on Marriage and Family among ethnic minority people.

  8. The Ministry of Finance shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in, working out plans to guide budget balancing, ensure necessary funding sources for the implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family and the Resolution of the National Assembly on implementation of the Law, from the elaboration of documents guiding the implementation of this Law, organization of marriage registration and adoption registration in remote and deep-lying areas, organization of training courses and campaigns for wide dissemination and propagation among cadres, soldiers and people.

  9. The Presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall direct local agencies to work out specific plans to deal with the actual marriage situation in their localities according to the Resolution of the National Assembly on implementation of the Marriage and Family Law, create favorable conditions for marriage registration, including on-site registration, so as to completely deal with this situation in 2001-2002.

  The Prime Minister requests the ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities, within the scope of their respective management responsibilities, to work out specific plans to implement this Directive.

  The Ministry of Justice and the Government Office shall have to monitor and urge the implementation of this Directive and summarize the results of implementation for reporting to the Prime Minister.
