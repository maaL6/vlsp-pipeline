                                           CIRCULAR
   GUIDELINES ON ISSUANCE OF STAY PERMITS, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE PERMITS, PERMITS FOR EXIT AND ENTRY, AND RESIDENCE SOLUTIONS FOR FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM
Pursuant to Law on Entry, Exit, Transit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;
Pursuant to Decree No. 106/2014/ND-CP dated November 17, 2014 of the Government on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;
At the request of the Director General of General Department of Security, the Minister of Public Security promulgates a Circular providing guidelines for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits and permanent residence for foreigners in Vietnam.
Chapter I
                                       GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
This Circular provides guidelines on issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits, and permanent residence for foreigners in Vietnam within the competence of the Ministry of Public Security.
Article 2. Regulated entities
This Circular applies to foreigners entering and residing in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations, and individuals.
Chapter II
 PROCEDURES AND COMPETENCE TO HANDLE APPLICATIONS FOR VISAS, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE CARDS, AND PERMANENT RESIDENCE
Article 3. Procedures and competence to handle applications for visas
1. Agencies, organizations, and individuals that guarantee foreigners’ entry into Vietnam according to Clause 1 Article 16 of the Law on Entry, Exit, Transit, and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “the Law”) shall submit application at Immigration Department.
2. The visa application form is made according to the form issued by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 and includes:
a) Form NA2 used for agencies and organizations;
b) Form NA3 used for individuals.
3. Visa application processing:
a) Immigration Department shall consider, process and respond to agencies, organizations and individuals applying for visas as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 16 of the Law;
b) In case of visa application at border checkpoint, Immigration Department shall consider, process and respond to visa application as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 16 of the Law.
Article 4. Procedures and authority for processing applications for temporary residence card
1. Foreigners eligible for temporary residence cards as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 36 of the Law must apply through agencies, organizations or individuals that have directly invited or guaranteed them to carry out procedures at Immigration Department or Immigration Division of police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities where agencies, organizations invite or guarantee their headquarters are located or where individuals invite or guarantee them to reside.
2. Application for temporary residence card as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law. The application form for temporary residence card prescribed in Point a Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law shall be made using the form promulgated by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 on forms related to entry, exit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam, including:

a) Form NA6 and NA8 used for agencies and organizations;
b) Form NA7 and NA8 used for individuals.
3. Issuance of temporary residence cards
Within 05 working days from the date on which the complete application is received, Immigration Department or Immigration Division of police authority of province or central-affiliated city shall consider issuing temporary residence cards.
4. Duration of temporary residence cards shall be issued in accordance with entry purposes and request of agencies, organizations and individuals. Duration of temporary residence cards shall range from 01 year to 05 years but at least 30 days shorter than duration of passports.
Article 5. Permanent residence settlement
1. Procedures and competence for permanent residence settlement shall comply with Article 41 and Article 42 of the Law.
2. Determination of period of temporary residence in Vietnam for foreigners applying for permanent residence:
a) Foreigners who have been temporarily residing in Vietnam for 03 years or more according to Clause 3 Article 40 of the Law shall be determined on the basis of entry and exit check stamps issued at border checkpoints with total temporary residence duration in Vietnam of 03 years or more in the 04 years prior to the date of application submission.
b) Documents proving continuous temporary residence in Vietnam before 2000 according to Point b Clause 1 Article 42 of the Law include one of the following documents: certificate of temporary residence; temporary residence registration book; written explanation of the period of temporary residence in Vietnam certified by police of commune, ward or town where the stateless person temporarily resides.
3. Location of application submission for permanent residence:
a) Applicants for permanent residence according to Clause 1, Clause 2 Article 39 of the Law shall submit their applications at Immigration Department.
b) Applicants for permanent residence according to Clause 3, Clause 4 Article 39 of the Law shall submit their applications at Immigration Division of Police of province or central-affiliated city where they apply for permanent residence.
Chapter III
 PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE AND REISSUANCE OF PERMITS FOR NON-NATIONAL RESIDERS IN VIETNAM
Article 6. Entry and exit permits
1. International travel documents specified in Point i Clause 1 Article 44 of the Law are entry and exit permits issued by Immigration Department to stateless persons residing in Vietnam who wish to leave Vietnam (using form NC13 enclosed with this Circular).
2. Entry and exit permits shall be valid for 3 years; entry and exit permits that are lost or damaged may be re-issued.
Article 7. Procedures for issuance and reissuance of entry and exit permits
1. Applicants for issuance or reissuance of entry and exit permits shall submit 1 application at Immigration Department or Immigration Division of police authority of province or central-affiliated city where they reside. Applications include:
a) Application forms for issuance or reissuance of entry and exit permits (using form NC14 enclosed with this Circular);
b) Photocopies of permanent residence cards enclosed with original copies for verification purposes if holders of permanent residence cards have been issued with such cards;
c) Written explanations regarding period of temporary residence in Vietnam, certified by police authorities of communes, wards or townships where stateless persons temporarily reside if they have not been permitted to permanently reside;
d) Issued entry and exit permits, in case of application for re-issuance due to damaged entry and exit permits;
dd) Report on lost documents, in case of application for re-issuance due to lost entry and exit permits (no need for certification by competent authorities where the documents are lost).
2. Handling of issuance and re-issuance of entry and exit permits:
a) In case of submission of applications at Immigration Department:


                                                                                                       2

Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of complete applications, Immigration Department shall consider issuing or re-issuing entry and exit permits.
b) In case of submission of applications at Immigration Divisions of police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities:
Within 3 working days from the date of receipt of complete applications, Immigration Divisions of police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall consider and transfer applications for issuance or re-issuance of entry and exit permits to Immigration Department. Immigration Department shall consider issuing or re-issuing entry and exit permits within 2 working days.
Chapter IV
                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 8. Transitional provisions
1. Entry and exit permits which are still valid before the effective date of this Circular may be used until their expiry dates.
2. Applications for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards; issuance or reissuance of entry and exit permits; permanent residence settlement submitted before the effective date of this Circular but not yet settled shall be considered and settled by immigration authorities according to regulations in this Circular.
Article 9. Effect
1. This Circular takes effect on August 20, 2015.
2. This Circular replaces Circular No. 45/2011/TT-BCA dated June 29, 2011 of the Minister of Public Security providing guidelines for issuance of entry and exit permits for foreigners permanently residing in Vietnam who do not have passports.
Article 10. Implementation responsibilities
1. The General Department of Security shall take charge of guiding police units, local authorities and other relevant units to implement this Circular.
2. Directors General of General Departments, Heads of units affiliated to Ministries, Directors of Police Departments, Fire and Rescue Police Departments of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall be responsible for implementing this Circular. Any problems arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry (via the Immigration Department - General Department of Security) for timely guidance./.
