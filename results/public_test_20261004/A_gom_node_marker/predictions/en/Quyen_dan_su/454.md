                                                  DECISION

    PROMULGATING A PROVISIONAL REGULATION ON THE RECEIPT BY FOREIGNERS OF ADOPTED VIETNAMESE CHILDREN WHO ARE DEAD-BORN, ABANDONED OR ABDUCTED IN THE PROVINCES BY THE EMPLOYMENT - VETERANS AND SOCIAL WORKS MANAGEMENT SECTORS

                                           THE MINISTER-CHAIRMAN'S COUNCIL

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Council of Ministers dated July 4, 1981;
  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated December 29, 1986;
  Pursuant to the Law on Protection, Care for and Education of Children dated August 12, 1991;
  At the request of the Minister of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs;

                                                  DECISION

  Article 1.- To promulgate a provisional regulation on the adoption of Vietnamese orphans, abandoned children and disabled children by foreigners in the care establishments under the management of the labor, war invalids and social affairs sector, together with this Decision.

  Article 2.- This Decision takes effect as from its signing date. All previous regulations contrary to this Decision are now annulled.

  Article 3.- The ministers, the heads of the State Committees, the heads of the agencies attached to the Council of Ministers, the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government shall have to implement this Decision.


                                                                       Phan Văn Khải

                                                                            (Signed)



                                                    PROVISIONS

     FOR THE TIME BEING AWAITED TO REGARD FOREIGNERS AS ADOPTORS OF VIETNAMESE ORPHANES WHO ARE DEAD-BORED, ABANDONED OR DISABLED IN THE CARE AND NURTURE FACILITIES MANAGED BY THE VETERANS AND SOCIAL WORKS DEPARTMENT
        (Promulgated together with the Council of Ministers' Decision No. 145-HDBT of April 29, 1992)

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- The adoption of Vietnamese orphaned, abandoned or disabled children by foreigners must be based on the feelings between the adoptive parents and the adopted children in the relationship between father and daughter, ensuring that the children are properly nurtured, looked after and educated.

  Article 2.- Foreigners who adopt children must be at least 20 years older than the adopted children.

  Article 3.- The adoptees mentioned in Article 1 of this Article are those living in the care and nurture establishments managed by the labor, war invalids and social work sector, including:

  - Children aged from 15 years or younger, for disabled cases, they may be over 15 years old.

  The adopted child may be over 15 years old if the adopter is an old, weak and lonely person.

  Article 4.- The adoption of a child shall be based on the voluntary principle of the adopter, his/her parents, tutor or director of the child-rearing establishment. If the child is 9 years old or older, his/her consent is also required.

  Article 5.- The People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government where there is the child-rearing establishment mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation shall consider and grant a certificate of recognition of the foreigner's adoption of a Vietnamese child within two months after receiving the complete dossier. In case of refusal, the applicant shall be informed of the reason.

  Chapter II

                           CONDITIONS FOR FOREIGNERS TO ADOPT CHILDREN

  Article 6.- Foreigners who apply for Vietnamese children as adoptive ones mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation shall send to the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs the following papers:

  1. The written permission for the adoption issued by the competent State agency of the country of which he/she is a national or of which he/she resides (the written permission must clearly state the legitimate purpose of the application for adoption);

  2. The certificate of the competent State agency of the foreign country certifying the health condition, mental and material capability to ensure the nurturing, education and healthy physical, intellectual and moral development of the adopted child;

  3. The application for adoption shall be sent to the People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government where there is the child-rearing establishment, clearly stating the reason for the application, the applicant's name, age and address (if the applicant has a spouse, both must sign) and the name, age and address of the child whom he/she wishes to adopt, and other requirements.

  In case the name, age and address of the child whom he/she wishes to adopt are not yet known, the foreign applicant may send an application expressing his/her aspiration for adoption and request the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of Vietnam to select and recommend a child.

  The above-mentioned papers written in foreign languages must be translated into Vietnamese and certified by a Vietnamese notary public.

  Article 7.- The papers prescribed at Points 1 and 2 of Article 6 shall remain valid for six months after they are certified by the competent State agencies of the host country.

  Article 8.- After being notified of the acceptance of the application for adoption or after being authorized by the adopter, the applicant shall have to come to Vietnam and carry out the following procedures with the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs:

  1. To produce the permit for the child to enter the country of the adopter;

  2. To undertake to record the child's name as adopted at the local social organization (non-governmental organization sponsored by the State) where the adopter resides;

  3. To undertake to annually notify the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of Vietnam of the child's photograph enclosed with the notice of adoption until the adopter's son or daughter reaches full eighteen years of age according to the set form.

  4. To undertake to nurture the adopted children in a proper manner; to assist them in health rehabilitation or correction to improve their disabled conditions if they are disabled children.

  5. To undertake to provide education or vocational training for the adopted children.

  6. To bear all expenses related to the adoption procedures.

  Chapter III

                                            IMPLEMENTATION ORGANIZATION

  Article 9.- The adoption of a child shall be effected when the following papers are available:

  1. The certificate of the People’s Committee of a province or centrally-affiliated city permitting a foreigner to adopt a Vietnamese child;

  2. The record of transfer of a child to be adopted by a foreigner.

  3. The commitments of Article 8 of this Regulation.

  4. Passports of foreigners who adopt children.

  5. The person authorized to receive adopted children must have a lawful authorization letter from the adoptive parent.

  Article 10.- The Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall set up a specialized working section to monitor Vietnamese children adopted by foreigners under this Decision’s Provision.

  Article 11.- The Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall coordinate with the Ministry for Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Justice in guiding the implementation of this Provision.

  Article 12. These provisional regulations shall take effect from the date of their promulgation.
