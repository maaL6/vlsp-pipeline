                                             JOINT CIRCULAR

                               GUIDANCE ON EXECUTION OF EXPULSION SENTENCE

  Pursuant to the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments dated June 17, 2010;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 77/2009/ND-CP dated September 15, 2009, defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 15/2008/ND-CP dated February 4, 2008, defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 104/2008/ND-CP dated September 16, 2008, defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of National Defense;

  In order to uniformly implement the provisions of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments regarding execution of expulsion sentence, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall provide the following guidance:

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidelines for procedures for enforcing deportation sentences; handling cases where persons subject to deportation sentences abscond, fall ill, or die during the period pending their exit; handling of receipt of corpses, remains, and ashes of persons subject to deportation sentences who die; funding for enforcement of deportation sentences.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to persons subject to deportation sentences according to court judgments or decisions (hereinafter referred to as “deported persons”) and agencies, organizations, and individuals involved in enforcement of deportation sentences.

  Article 3. Principles of enforcement of deportation sentences

  1. Ensure political, legal, foreign affairs, and humanitarian policy requirements of the State.

  2. The enforcement of deportation sentences must ensure safety, correct persons, correct time limit, procedures, and jurisdiction in accordance with Vietnamese law and international practices.

  3. Ensure the lawful rights and interests of deportees; prohibit all acts of infringing upon the life, health, dignity, and lawful property of deportees.

  Article 4. Agencies enforcing deportation sentences

  1. The criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the People’s Court or Military Court issues the decision on enforcement of the deportation sentence and where the headquarters of the criminal enforcement agency is located shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in organizing the enforcement of the deportation sentence according to the court’s judgment enforcement decision.

  2. The criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where there is a prison affiliated to the Ministry of Public Security, a prison affiliated to the Ministry of National Defense, or a prison affiliated to a military zone shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in organizing the enforcement of the deportation sentence for persons who have completely served their imprisonment sentences and are subject to the additional penalty of deportation.

  Article 5. Funds for judgment enforcement

  Funds for enforcement of the deportation sentence shall be covered by the state budget in the annual budget estimates of the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority and the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security. Expenses for purchasing exit tickets to bring the deportees back to their country shall comply with Article 102 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  Chapter II

                          PROCEDURES FOR ENFORCING DEPORTATION SENTENCES

  Article 6. Notices of enforcement of deportation sentences

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the decision on execution of the expulsion sentence from the Court in the case specified in Clause 1 Article 96 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments or from the date of receipt of the notification from the Superintendent of the prison regarding the time for the convict to completely serve the additional penalty of expulsion, the execution agency of the provincial police must notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, diplomatic missions, consular offices, and other agencies authorized to perform consular functions of the country where the expelled person is a citizen, located in Vietnam, or the representative office of the international organization for which that person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed his/her entry into Vietnam.

  2. In cases where the convict has the additional penalty of expulsion and is granted amnesty or exemption from serving the imprisonment sentence, within 24 hours from the receipt of the decision on amnesty or exemption from serving the imprisonment sentence, the Superintendent of the prison must notify in writing the execution agency of the provincial police where the prison is located so that it can immediately notify the authorities specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 7. Documents for execution of expulsion sentence

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the judgment execution decision from the Court or from the date of receiving the person who has completely served the imprisonment sentence, the person who is granted amnesty or exemption from serving the additional penalty of expulsion, the execution agency of the provincial police must prepare documents for the execution of the expulsion sentence.

  2. The documents for the execution of the expulsion sentence (hereinafter referred to as judgment execution documents) must include all the papers and documents specified in Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments. The certificate of completion of other penalties or other obligations specified in Point c Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments includes: certificate of completion of imprisonment sentence, amnesty, exemption from serving the imprisonment sentence, exemption or reduction of fines, decision on termination or suspension of judgment execution, certificate or confirmation from the competent execution agency regarding the results of judgment execution or the results of fulfilling other obligations.

  3. Upon handing over a person who has completely served his/her prison sentence, or a person who is granted special amnesty or exemption from serving a sentence with deportation penalty to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Police Department where the prison is located, the superintendent of the prison shall hand over a copy of the judgment/decision on execution of the prison sentence, certificate of completion of the prison sentence, certificate of special amnesty or exemption from serving the prison sentence and documents proving the performance of other obligations (if any) for the preparation of a dossier for deportation penalty execution.

  Article 8. Designation of accommodation

  1. Within 03 working days after the preparation of a judgment enforcement dossier, the head of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Police Department where the dossier is prepared shall issue a decision to designate the place of accommodation for the person subject to deportation pending his/her exit. The designated accommodation must have a clear address to ensure convenience for management and supervision. The detention cell or custody cell of the detention center or custody house of the prison shall not be used to manage the person subject to deportation pending his/her exit.

  2. In case the deportee falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2, Article 99 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Police Department where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall issue a decision to take the deportee to the accommodation establishment of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as “the accommodation establishment”).

  3. In case the person subject to expulsion or his/her relatives or legal representative request permission for temporary residence in a lawful place of stay in another province or city, a written request must be sent to the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared for consideration and decision. The written request must clearly state the full name and address of the requester, the relationship with the person subject to expulsion, the address of the place of residence, and a commitment to comply with all requirements of the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority, and to abide by the law and regulations of the local authority of the place of residence. The request must be translated into Vietnamese and certified by the diplomatic mission, consular office, or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen, or by the representative office of the international organization where such person works, which has its headquarters in Vietnam, and by the People’s Committee of the commune or agency or organization where the expelled person resides.

  4. Within 2 working days from the date of issuance of the decision designating the place of residence, the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall notify the Court that issued the judgment execution decision, the same-level Procuracy, and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.

  Article 9. Management of persons subject to expulsion

  1. The criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies and local authorities of the province or city where the person subject to expulsion resides in managing such person.

  2. Within 3 working days from the date of issuance of the decision to place the person in a detention center or the decision to designate a place of residence in another province or city, the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall send a notice enclosed with a copy of the judgment or decision to the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the detention center is located or where the person subject to expulsion resides for cooperation in management and the initiation of procedures for handling the case in case the person absconds or dies as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 99 and Article 100 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  Article 10. Settlement of cases where persons subject to expulsion abscond

  1. During their stay pending exit, if the expelled person absconds, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment is located or the province where the place of accommodation is located shall immediately organize the pursuit and notify such to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared; in case of no result of pursuit, within 7 days from the date the expelled person absconds, a pursuit warrant must be issued under law.

  2. In case of recapture of a person who has been extradited or surrendered, the agency that captures or receives him/her shall make a record and immediately notify it to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is filed for receiving and taking him/her to a detention center and notify it to the agency that has issued the pursuit warrant for issuing a decision on termination of pursuit as prescribed.

  3. Where the expelled person absconds on escort to the accommodation establishment or the exit point, the criminal enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security authority organizing the escort shall take charge of pursuit, issue a pursuit warrant, and terminate the pursuit.

  4. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall immediately notify the same-level Procuracy, the Court that issued the decision on deportation, the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular of the deportation victim’s escape, his/her arrest or surrender.

  Article 11. Settlement of cases where persons subject to expulsion are sick or deceased

  1. During their stay pending exit at accommodation establishments, expelled persons may receive medical examination and treatment according to regulations of the Government for persons who are subject to expulsion sentences or foreigners who have completely served imprisonment sentences and are staying at accommodation establishments pending completion of exit procedures. In case expelled persons suffer from serious diseases and must be treated at state medical establishments, designated accommodation establishments or places of stay shall immediately notify such to criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial-level police departments in localities where they are located or designated places of stay so that these agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular can make notification.

  2. In case expelled persons are receiving emergency treatment or suffering from serious diseases and unable to move, with certification by hospitals of provinces or higher level, criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial-level police departments in localities where expelled persons are staying shall designate, and criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial-level police departments in localities where accommodation establishments are located shall notify criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial-level police departments in which judgment enforcement dossiers are prepared thereof so that these agencies can issue written notices to the courts that have issued judgment enforcement decisions for consideration and decision on extension of time limit for forced departure from Vietnam’s territory.

  3. During their stay, if expelled persons die, designated accommodation establishments or places of stay shall immediately notify such to criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial-level police departments in localities where they are located or designated places of stay, investigation agencies and competent procuracies for determination of causes of death. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours after expelled persons die, criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial-level police departments in localities where these persons die shall notify criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial-level police departments in which judgment enforcement dossiers are prepared thereof so that these agencies can immediately issue written notices or telegram to the courts that have issued judgment enforcement decisions and agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular. After completing the procedures for determining causes of death and obtaining permission from competent agencies, criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial-level police departments in localities where accommodation establishments or designated places of stay are located shall organize burial.

  In case an expelled person dies on the way to the accommodation establishment or the place of exit, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the escorting is organized shall take charge and cooperate with the criminal enforcement agency of the province, investigation agency and competent Procuracy of the locality where the expelled person dies in carrying out procedures for settlement as prescribed in Clause 4, Article 99 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  4. Expenses for burial of the deceased expelled person:

  a/Expenses for burial of the deceased expelled person at the accommodation establishment shall comply with the Government’s regulations on regime of food, accommodation, clothing, daily-life activities, medical examination and treatment, and expenses for burial of persons serving expulsion penalty during their stay at the accommodation establishment;

  b/Expenses for burial of the deceased expelled person during his/her stay at another designated accommodation establishment shall be applied similarly to those for burial of the deceased expelled person at the accommodation establishment, except for cases where his/her relatives or lawful representative pays such expenses by themselves.

  Article 12. Settlement of receipt of corpses, remains and ashes of expelled persons

  1. In case a deceased expelled person’s relative or lawful representative requests receipt of his/her corpse for burial, within 48 (forty eight) hours after the deceased person dies, a written request must be sent to the criminal enforcement agency of the province which has compiled the judgment enforcement dossier for consideration and decision.

  2. The written request must clearly state the full name and address of the requester, the relationship between the requester and the deceased expelled person, the burial place, the commitment to ensure security, order and environmental sanitation and the payment of expenses. The written request must be certified by the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which the deceased person is a citizen or the representative office of the international organization where he/she works and must be translated into Vietnamese. If the requester is a Vietnamese citizen, the written request must be certified by the People’s Committee of the commune where he/she resides. In case of receipt of a corpse for burial in the Vietnamese territory, the consent of the People’s Committee of the commune where the burial takes place is required.

  3. Within 24 (twenty four) hours after receiving the application, the head of the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office shall decide to permit receipt of the corpse and notify in writing the applicant, the court that has issued the judgment execution decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the permission or non-permission to receive the corpse when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of the corpse affects security, order and environmental sanitation.

  4. Within 24 (twenty four) hours after notifying the permission to receive the corpse, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office and the applicant must hand over and receive the corpse, money and lawful property of the deported person (if any) and make a record bearing signatures of the handover and receipt parties and witnesses; past this time limit, if the applicant fails to come to receive the corpse, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office shall organize the burial.

  5. The receipt of exhumation remains of the deported person may be permitted only after 03 (three) years from the date of burial. In case of cremation, the receipt of ashes may be permitted after the completion of the cremation. The requester for receipt of exhumation remains or ashes must send an application to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the deportation victim is buried for consideration and decision; the application must contain all the contents specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Within 07 (seven) working days after receiving the application, the head of the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office shall decide to permit receipt of exhumation remains or ashes and notify in writing the applicant, the court that has issued the judgment execution decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the permission or non-permission to receive exhumation remains or ashes when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of exhumation remains or ashes affects security, order and environmental sanitation.

  6. The repatriation of the repatriated person’s corpse, remains and ashes shall be preserved and transported by the person who makes the request or the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which he/she is a citizen, or the representative office of the international organization where he/she works, and shall comply with the provisions of law on medical treatment, environmental hygiene, prevention and control of infectious diseases and border quarantine and other provisions of Vietnamese law.

  Article 13. Exit permit

  1. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority that prepares the judgment enforcement dossier shall examine the passport or a document of equivalent value to the passport of the repatriated person. In case the passport does not contain a visa or the visa has expired, the immigration management agency shall issue a visa in accordance with regulations. In case of emergency, the visa must be issued at the border checkpoint, and the written request must be sent at least 12 (twelve) hours before the repatriated person arrives at the border checkpoint.

  2. In case the repatriated person does not have a passport or a document of equivalent value to the passport, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall notify the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security in writing to request the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which the repatriated person is a citizen, or the representative office of the international organization where the repatriated person works, to issue necessary documents for exit visa procedures.

  In case the requested agency or organization has not yet resolved or has not responded to the request, but there is an urgent need to repatriate immediately or the time limit for leaving the territory of Vietnam has expired as prescribed by law, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall request the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security to issue a valid exit document and request the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to notify in writing the country of which the repatriated person is a citizen for handling the reception and entry procedures. In case the country of which the repatriated person is a citizen refuses to receive or allow entry, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall take charge and cooperate with the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security and the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority in resolving the matter through diplomatic channels or repatriating the person according to international practices.

  3. Where the expelled person is required to serve another sentence or has not yet completed the fine or other civil obligations under the case of exit suspension as prescribed by Vietnamese law, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the expulsion judgment enforcement record is prepared shall immediately send a report to the Court that issued the judgment enforcement decision to extend the period for leaving the territory of Vietnam; cooperate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in notifying and requesting the diplomatic mission, consular office, or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen and headquartered in Vietnam, or the representative office of the international organization where the person works, or the agency or organization that guaranteed his/her entry into Vietnam to settle or contact the family of the expelled person or the agency where the expelled person works to support the payment of fines and other civil obligations; and concurrently notify the competent civil judgment enforcement agency to carry out judgment enforcement procedures or to request the competent Court to consider exempting or reducing the judgment enforcement obligation for amounts payable to the state budget in accordance with the Law on Civil Judgment Enforcement. When there is a decision or document from the civil judgment enforcement agency on the completion of judgment enforcement for the expelled person, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall carry out procedures to compel the expelled person to leave the territory of Vietnam.

  Article 14. Compulsory departure from the territory of Vietnam

  1. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement record is prepared shall decide the time limit for exit but must not exceed the time limit specified in the Court's judgment enforcement decision or the time limit prescribed by law.

  2. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement record is prepared shall purchase exit tickets for the expelled person if the expelled person bears the cost or the diplomatic mission, consular office of the country of which the person is a citizen or the representative office of the international organization where the person works or the agency or organization that guaranteed his/her entry into Vietnam to cover the costs of repatriation.

  In case of using budget to pay for air tickets, cars, trains or sea ferries for repatriation of persons who are expelled from Vietnam as prescribed in Article 102 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments, the criminal judgment execution management agency of the Ministry of Public Security shall decide to use budget and purchase tickets for means of transport to take the persons who are expelled out of the territory of Vietnam.

  3. At least 24 (twenty-four) hours before the date of exit, the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is made shall notify the person who is expelled, the competent immigration management agency, the Border Guard Command’s Department of Checkpoints and agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the time and place of exit for coordination in expulsion. In case of expulsion by land road to a country sharing a land border with Vietnam, the time of exit must not be at night, except for special cases.

  In case the person who is expelled has money or property deposited at the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office or accommodation establishment, at least 24 (twenty-four) hours before the date of exit, the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office or accommodation establishment shall return such money or property to him/her. The return of money or property must be recorded in writing with signatures of the delivering and receiving parties and witnesses.

  4. The criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is made shall escort the person who is expelled to the place of exit; coordinate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security, the Border Guard Command’s Department of Checkpoints in carrying out procedures to force the person who is expelled out of the territory of Vietnam. The expulsion at the place of exit must be recorded in writing with signatures of the person who is expelled, representatives of agencies participating in the expulsion, diplomatic missions, consular offices of the country of nationality of the expeller or representative office of international organization where he/she works.

  5. In case the person subject to expulsion fails to voluntarily comply with the expulsion decision or refuses to leave Vietnam, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall escort the expelled person to the border checkpoint of the country of which he/she is a citizen; in case of escorting by air, the criminal enforcement agency of the province shall notify and reach agreement with the air-freight company on security measures during the escorting as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 17 of Decree No. 81/2010/ND-CP of the Government on civil aviation security and comply with international agreements on civil aviation security to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  Chapter III

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 15. Effect

  This Circular takes effect on January 1, 2012.

  Article 16. Implementation organization

  The competent agencies of the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, within their functions, tasks and powers, organize implementation, guide and inspect the implementation of this Circular.

  Any problems arising in the course of implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for timely guidance./.
