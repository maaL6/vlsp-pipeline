                      AMENDMENTS TO A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE CODE

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, as amended by Resolution No. 203/2025/QH15;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on amendments to the Criminal Procedure Code No. 101/2015/QH13, as amended by Law No. 02/2021/QH15, Law No. 34/2024/QH15 and Law No. 59/2024/QH15.

  2. To amend and supplement Article 20 as follows:

  “Article 20. Responsibility to exercise prosecution rights and supervise legal compliance in criminal proceedings The Procuracy shall exercise prosecution rights and supervise legal compliance in criminal proceedings, decide on charges, detect violations of laws to ensure that all criminal acts, offenders, juridical persons committing crimes, and violations of laws are detected and handled promptly and strictly; that charges, investigations, prosecution, adjudication, and judgment enforcement are applied to the right persons, for the right crimes, and in accordance with the law; that no crime or offender, juridical person committing a crime is left unpunished, and innocent people are not wrongfully convicted.

  The Prosecutor General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall provide for the jurisdiction of the People’s Procuracies at each level over the exercise of prosecution rights and supervision of legal compliance in criminal proceedings as prescribed by this Code.”.

  1. Regional People’s Courts shall conduct first-instance trials over criminal lawsuits involving less serious crimes, serious crimes, very serious crimes and extremely serious crimes with the maximum penalty of up to 20 years in prison as prescribed by the Criminal Code.

  b) Criminal lawsuits within the jurisdiction of regional People’s Courts but involve complicated circumstances, are related to multiple levels and sectors, or receive special public concern; lawsuits that, upon settlement, affect politics or foreign affairs; lawsuits where offenders are key leaders at provincial level or higher, religious dignitaries or persons of high prestige in ethnic minority groups.

  3. Regional Military Courts shall conduct first-instance trials over criminal lawsuits involving less serious crimes, serious crimes and very serious crimes, except for the following crimes and lawsuits:

  b) Crimes of undermining peace, opposing humanity and war crimes;

  c) Crimes prescribed in Articles 123, 125, 126, 227, 277, 278, 279, 280, 282, 283, 284, 286, 287, 288, 337, 368, 369, 370, 371, 399 and 400 of the Criminal Code;

  d) Crimes committed outside the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam;

  a) Criminal cases which do not fall within the jurisdiction of regional military courts;

  41. Amendments to Article 382:

  “Article 382. Jurisdiction to conduct cassation procedure

  1. The Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court shall conduct cassation review of judgments/decisions which have taken legal effect and are appealed against by the Appellate Court of the Supreme People’s Court, provincial People’s Courts or Central Military Court through a panel of five judges.

  2. The General Panel of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court shall conduct cassation review of judgments/decisions which have taken legal effect as prescribed in clause 1 of this Article but are complicated or judgments/decisions which have been reviewed by the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court through a panel of five judges but fail to reach an agreement when voting on the settlement of the case.

  When the General Panel of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court conducts cassation review, at least two-thirds of its members must attend the trial, which is chaired by the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court. A decision of the General Panel of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court must be approved by more than half of its members; otherwise, the trial shall be adjourned.
