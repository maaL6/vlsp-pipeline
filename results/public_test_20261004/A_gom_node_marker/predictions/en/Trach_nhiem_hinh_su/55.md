                                                  DECISION

     GUIDELINES FOR APPLICATION OF A NUMBER OF PROVISIONS OF THE CRIMINAL CODE IN THE JUDGMENT OF CORRUPTION AND OTHER OFFENCES AGAINST REGULATIONS ON OFFICE

                            COUNCIL OF JUSTICES OF SUPREME PEOPLE’S COURT

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of People’s Court dated November 24, 2014;

  In order to apply correctly and consistently a number of provisions of the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 amended by the Law No. 12/2017/QH14 in the judgment of corruption and other offences against regulations on office;

  After obtaining opinions from the Procurator General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy and the Minister of Justice.

                                                 DECISION:

  Article 1. Scope

  This Resolution provides guidelines for application of a number of provisions of the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 amended by the Law No. 12/2017/QH14 (hereinafter referred to as “the Criminal Code”) on corruption and other offences against regulations on office; determination of value of property appropriated, damage caused by corruption and other offences against regulations on office; handling of items and money directly related to crimes.

  Article 2. Definitions

  1. “Agencies and organizations” as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 352 of the Criminal Code include state agencies, organizations, units, enterprises, and non-state organizations.

  2. “State agencies, organizations, and units” are those specified in Clause 9 Article 3 of the Law on Anti-Corruption, including: state agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, people’s armed forces units, public service providers, state enterprises, and other organizations or units established or invested in by the State with full or partial funding for their operations, directly managed or under the State's management to serve the overall development needs and essential interests of the State and society.

  3. “Enterprises and non-state organizations” as prescribed in Articles 353, 354, 364, and 365 of the Criminal Code are those not specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. “Persons holding positions” as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 352 of the Criminal Code are those specified in Clause 2 Article 3 of the Law on Anti-Corruption.

  5. “Carried out through another form” as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 352 of the Criminal Code refers to cases where the act is not due to appointment, election, recruitment, or contract but is assigned to carry out tasks and has authority while performing such tasks.

  Example: A person appointed by a competent state agency to attend a checkpoint for Covid-19 prevention and control.

  6. “Voluntary declaration before detection” as prescribed in Clause 7 Article 364 and Clause 6 Article 365 of the Criminal Code refers to cases where the corrupting act has not yet been detected or reported by anyone but the corruptor or briber has voluntarily declared all acts of corruption committed.

  7. “Voluntarily returning at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property” means cases where offenders voluntarily return at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property after committing the offenses. It is also considered to be voluntarily returning at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property in cases where offenders have influenced their parents, spouses, children, siblings, or other relatives to return or do not object to them returning at least three-quarters of the property they embezzled or were bribed.

  For cases within the same case where a person is criminally prosecuted for multiple crimes, including embezzlement, bribery, and other crimes, but has voluntarily returned property equivalent to at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property, it is also considered to be voluntarily returning at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property, except where there are clear grounds to determine that the property originated from another crime.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled VND 4.000.000.000 and abused his position and power to appropriate VND 2.000.000.000. After A was charged, A’s wife transferred the house and land, which were his personal property, to replace A in returning VND 3.000.000.000, it is considered to be voluntarily returning at least three-quarters of the embezzled property.

  8. “Actively cooperating with competent authorities in detecting, investigating, and handling crimes” means that after committing corrupt or other crimes involving positions, offenders voluntarily provide information, documents, and evidence that is significant for detecting, investigating, and handling crimes related to the crimes for which they are charged (such as: correctly identifying the location where important exhibits are hidden to assist competent authorities in recovering such exhibits; providing accurate information and identifying the whereabouts of other accomplices; providing information about new crimes and offenders involved in the crimes for which they are charged...). In addition to the above cases, other cases may be determined as “actively cooperating with competent authorities in detecting, investigating, and handling crimes,” but the Court must clearly state this in the judgment.

  9. “Great meritorious contribution” means cases where offenders have assisted procedural authorities in detecting, arresting, investigating or handling crimes unrelated to the crimes for which they are charged; rescued other people in dangerous situations or rescued property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more of the State, collectives or individuals during natural disasters, epidemics, fires or other force majeure events; or made inventions, discoveries or innovations with significant value as confirmed by competent state authorities. Apart from the above-mentioned cases, other cases may be determined as “great meritorious contributions” but the Courts must clearly state them in their judgments.

  Article 3. Regarding some circumstances as signs of guilt determination

  1. “Having been disciplined for this act but committing violations again” prescribed in Point a Clause 1 Articles 353, 354, 355 and 358 of the Criminal Code is the case where the offender has previously been disciplined for the corresponding act but the time limit for being considered not yet having been disciplined under law has not expired.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was given a warning for embezzlement of VND 500,000 but 6 months later, A committed embezzlement of VND 1,500,000.

  In case an offender who has been disciplined by a competent authority or organization is examined for penal liability by a procedural authority for the same act, the previous disciplinary action shall not be considered “having been disciplined for this act but committing violations again”.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was disciplined by a competent authority for embezzlement of property but then A is examined for prosecution for the same act, the circumstance “having been disciplined for this act but committing violations again” shall not apply to A.

  2. “Having been convicted of one of the crimes prescribed in Section 1 of this Chapter but not yet having had the criminal records remitted, and committing violations again” as prescribed in Clause 1 of Articles 353, 354 and 355 of the Criminal Code means that the offender has previously been convicted once for one or more of the crimes prescribed in Section 1 of Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, but has not yet had his/her criminal record remitted, and then commits one of the acts prescribed in Clause 1 of one of Articles 353, 354 and 355 of the Criminal Code.

  In case the offender has other previous convictions besides the above-mentioned previous convictions, such previous convictions shall be used to determine whether he/she relapses into crime or commit dangerous recidivism, except for cases where all previous convictions have been used to determine the signs of re-offence in previous convictions.

  Example: Before committing the act of taking bribes amounting to VND 1,500,000, A had two previous convictions, in which the first previous conviction, A was convicted of the crime of embezzlement with a stolen amount of VND 5,000,000; the second previous conviction, A was convicted of the crime of abuse of position or power for property appropriation with a stolen amount of VND 1,000,000. In this case, the second previous conviction is used to determine the sign of re-offence for the crime of embezzlement. For the first previous conviction, since it has already been used to determine the sign of re-offence of abuse of position or power for property appropriation (with a stolen amount of VND 1,000,000), it is not allowed to continue using it to determine recidivism.

  3. “Other material interests” as prescribed in Articles 354, 358, 364 and 366 of the Criminal Code means material interests other than assets prescribed in Article 105 of the Civil Code.

  Example: Taking bribes by sponsoring study abroad or travel expenses, etc.

  4. “Non-material interests” as prescribed in Point b Clause 1 of Articles 354, 358, 364, 365 and 366 of the Criminal Code means non-material interests.

  Example: Bribery by offering or proposing rewards, titles, prizes; appointing, electing, assigning positions; increasing test scores; promising graduation, study abroad, competitive exams, performance in foreign countries; sexual bribery, etc.

  5. “Abusing position or power” as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 355 of the Criminal Code means using beyond the assigned authority, responsibilities, or duties, or failing to be assigned, assigned duties, or powers in such fields but still performing them.

  Example: Nguyen Van A is the Deputy Chairperson of the People’s Committee of a province. A is only assigned to manage cultural and social affairs and not to manage land, but A still issues a decision to expropriate land from Company X to assign to Company Y (owned by A’s family). In this case, A’s actions exceed his assigned authority and responsibilities.

  6. “Abusing position or power” as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 356 of the Criminal Code means relying on the assigned position or power to act against or fail to act against or improperly act against the law.

  Example: Nguyen Van A, the Head of the Department of Natural Resources and Environment of District B, committed an act against official duties, submitted to the leadership of the People’s Committee of District B to sign a decision to issue a certificate of land use rights for a plot of land ineligible for issuance of a certificate of land use rights under the provisions of land law.

  7. “Profit” as prescribed in Clause 1 Articles 356, 357, and 359 of the Criminal Code refers to cases where the offender has abused his/her position or power or misused his/her power to gain unjust material or non-material benefits for himself/herself or for other agencies, organizations, or individuals.

  8. “Other personal motives” as prescribed in Clause 1 Articles 356, 357, and 359 of the Criminal Code refers to cases where the offender has abused his/her position or power or misused his/her power to affirm, consolidate, or enhance his/her status, prestige, or power in an unjust manner.

  9. Damage caused by criminal acts prescribed in Articles 353, 354, 355 and 358 of the Criminal Code means actual damage that occurs and is causally related to the criminal act, excluding money, property or other material benefits that the offender has appropriated or will appropriate.

  Example: Nguyen Van A, the warehouse keeper of Company B, embezzled 200,000,000 VND worth of medicine for epidemic prevention. Due to lack of medicine, all poultry of Company B, valued at 10,000,000,000 VND died. In this case, the amount embezzled by A is 200,000,000 VND and the actual damage caused by A’s criminal act is 10,000,000,000 VND.

  10. “Work secrets” as prescribed in Article 361 and Article 362 of the Criminal Code means work-related information presented in any form (e.g., documents, drafts, speeches, images, etc.) that agencies or organizations have stipulated not to disclose to others and are not included in the list of state secrets.

  Article 4. Some circumstances determining the penalty bracket

  1. Circumstances of “using deceitful tricks” or “using dangerous tricks” prescribed in Clause 2 of Articles 353, 354, 355, 364 and 365 of the Criminal Code:

  a) “Using deceitful tricks” means cases where offenders use sophisticated deception techniques, high technology to commit criminal acts, conceal crimes, shift blame to others or destroy evidences, causing difficulties for the detection, investigation and handling of crimes.

  b) “Using dangerous tricks” means cases where offenders use tricks that may endanger the lives and health of others or cause serious consequences to appropriate property or conceal crimes.

  2. The circumstance of “committing the crime more than once” as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 353, 354, 355, 356, 357, 358, 361, 364, 365 and 366 of the Criminal Code refers to the case where the offender has committed one or more than one of the acts specified in these Articles twice or more, each of which constitutes a crime, but has not yet been criminally prosecuted and the statute of limitations for criminal prosecution has not expired.

  Example: On August 15, 2018, Nguyen Van A embezzled 10.000.000 VND. On September 25, 2019, A embezzled another 20.000.000 VND. A’s criminal acts have not yet been criminally handled and the statute of limitations for criminal prosecution has not expired. In this case, A is criminally prosecuted for embezzlement and the circumstance of “committing the crime more than once” shall be applied according to Point c Clause 2 Article 353 of the Criminal Code.

  3. The circumstance of “causing adverse effects on the lives of officials, public employees, and employees in agencies and organizations” as prescribed in Point g Clause 2 Article 353 of the Criminal Code refers to one of the following cases:

  a) Losing or reducing regular income, lawful additional income of officials, public employees, and employees;

  b) Causing grievances, denunciations, loss of unity, loss of trust within agencies and organizations, affecting the morale of officials, public employees, and employees.

  4. The circumstance of “causing adverse effects on social security, order, and safety” as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 353 and Article 355 of the Criminal Code refers to one of the following cases:

  a) Causing large-scale lawsuits, protests, causing disturbances for hostile forces to take advantage of, entice, incite against the government, distort the Party’s line, policies, and laws of the State;

  b) Causing panic, fear, or anger among the people;

  c) Obstructing the implementation of the Party's line and the State's policies and laws.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled poverty alleviation funds for economic development, causing the People's Committee of commune B to face difficulties in implementing local policies on hunger elimination and poverty reduction.

  Article 5. Principles for handling corruption crimes and other position-related crimes

  1. The handling of corruption crimes and other position-related crimes must strictly comply with and thoroughly adhere to the principles of criminal law and criminal procedure law.

  2. During the process of prosecution, if the offender has actively returned at least three-quarters of the embezzled property or bribes received and cooperated actively with competent authorities in detecting, investigating, handling the crime or making meritorious contributions, the highest level of the penalty bracket applicable to the offender shall not be applied.

  3. Consider applying the special amnesty policy of exemption from penalties as prescribed in Article 59 of the Criminal Code to offenders who have at least two mitigating circumstances specified in Clause 1, Article 51 of the Criminal Code, or first-time offenders who are accomplices but play an insignificant role in one of the following cases:

  a) The offender commits the crime not for self-seeking motives or other personal motives but for the purpose of innovation and daring to break through for the socio-economic development cause and national defense;

  b) The offender is a person dependent (such as a subordinate to a superior, an employee receiving salary, or a person performing tasks under the direction of the superior), unaware of his/her criminal acts, not for self-seeking motives or other personal motives, and does not benefit; has actively declared before being detected, actively cooperated with competent authorities, and effectively contributed to the investigation and handling of the crime;

  c) The offender has proactively declared before detection, actively cooperated with competent authorities, contributed to limiting damage, proactively returned all appropriated property, has fully remedied consequences, and has compensated for all damage caused;

  d) The offender, after being detected, has sincerely declared, shown repentance, proactively returned all appropriated property, has fully remedied consequences, and has compensated for all damage caused.

  Article 6. Consideration of criminal liability and decision on penalties in case of case splitting

  In case a case is split for resolution at different stages (split into multiple cases), the consideration of criminal liability and decision on penalties shall be carried out as follows:

  1. It is not considered that the offender was convicted in the previous case as a ground for determining "committing the crime more than once," except where the criminal acts are considered and handled criminally at different stages are independent of each other;

  2. It is not considered that the offender was convicted in the previous case as having a poor personal record, thus not being eligible for suspended sentence, if they meet all other conditions for being eligible for suspended sentence;

  3. The decision on penalties in each judgment or decision must ensure that when aggregated, the penalties from multiple judgments do not exceed the highest level of the corresponding penalty bracket corresponding to the total value of property appropriated, or the bribe, or the damage caused by the crime.

  Example: Nguyen Van A committed acts of abuse of power in the performance of his duty. At first stage, it was only possible to prove that A caused damage to assets valued at VND 100.000.000, so the procedural authority decided to separate into 02 lawsuits and handle A first for the act of causing damage of VND 100.000.000. However, later in stage 2, the procedural authority proved that A's acts also caused damage to assets valued at VND 350.000.000. In stage 1, the Court applied clause 2 Article 357 of the Criminal Code to convict A of 05 years' imprisonment for abuse of power in the performance of his duty. When hearing the lawsuit in stage 2, A continued to be prosecuted under clause 2 Article 357 of the Criminal Code, so as to ensure that when summing up the penalties of both judgments, they would not exceed the highest level of this clause, the Court could only decide a penalty of no more than 05 years' imprisonment for A.

  Article 7. Criminal prosecution in case where the asset user has acquired assets through corruption to commit one of the money laundering acts

  In case where the asset user has acquired assets through corruption to commit one of the money laundering acts prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code, apart from being examined for penal liability for corresponding crimes prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, the offender shall also be examined for penal liability for money laundering crime according to Article 324 of the Criminal Code.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled VND 5.000.000.000, then used this money to invest in real estate business to conceal the illegal origin of the embezzled money. In this case, A is examined for penal liability for the crime of property embezzlement prescribed in Article 353 and the money laundering crime prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code.

  Article 8. Criminal prosecution in case of repeated commission of the same act prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code

  1. In case a person commits multiple times the same act prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, but the value of property appropriated or bribes given or property damage caused each time is below the minimum value prescribed for criminal prosecution and does not fall into other cases subject to criminal prosecution, and at the same time, such person has never been disciplined and the statute of limitations for disciplinary action has not expired, if the total value of property appropriated or bribes given or property damage caused by each time is equal to or above the minimum value prescribed for criminal prosecution under the provisions of the Criminal Code, then the person who commits multiple times the same violation shall be criminally prosecuted for the corresponding crime according to the total value of property appropriated, bribes given or property damaged.

  Example: On August 15, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of VND 1.500.000; on September 15, 2019, A continued to accept a bribe of VND 1.500.000; on December 30, 2019, A again accepted a bribe of VND 1.500.000, which was detected by a competent authority. These acts of A have not yet been disciplined and the statute of limitations for disciplinary action has not expired. In this case, the total amount of bribes accepted by A is determined to be VND 4.500.000, so A is criminally prosecuted under Clause 1 Article 354 of the Criminal Code.

  2. In case a person commits multiple times the same act prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, where each time constitutes a crime and the total value of property appropriated or bribes given or property damaged caused by each time falls within the aggravated penalty bracket, if the acts have not yet been criminally prosecuted and the statute of limitations for criminal prosecution has not expired, then in addition to being subject to the corresponding penalty bracket for the total value of property appropriated, bribes given or property damaged, they shall also be subject to the aggravating circumstance of "heavy fine bracket" or "aggravating circumstance of criminal liability as follows:"

  a) If the total value of property appropriated, bribes or damaged property is subject to the penalty in Clause 2 of the corresponding article, the offender shall be additionally subject to the aggravating circumstance of “committing the crime more than once”.

  Example: On August 15, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of VND 50.000.000; on June 30, 2020, A continued to accept a bribe of VND 100.000.000 and was detected by competent authorities. In this case, A shall be subject to 2 aggravating circumstances: “The bribe is money, property or other material benefits valued at from VND 100.000.000 to under VND 500.000.000” and “Committing the crime more than once” as prescribed in Points c and dd Clause 2 Article 354 of the Criminal Code.

  b) If the total value of property appropriated or bribes or damaged property is subject to the penalty in another category of aggravation, the offender shall be additionally subject to the aggravating circumstance of “committing the crime more than once”.

  Example: On March 11, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of VND 400.000.000; on July 30, 2020, A continued to accept a bribe of VND 200.000.000 and was detected by competent authorities. In this case, A shall be subject to the aggravating circumstance of “The bribe is money, property or other material benefits valued at from VND 500.000.000 to under VND 1.000.000.000” as prescribed in Point a Clause 3 Article 354 and the aggravating circumstance of “Committing the crime more than once” as prescribed in Point g Clause 1 Article 52 of the Criminal Code.

  Article 9. Criminal prosecution in case offenders appropriated property or accepted bribes while causing damage to property

  Where the offender appropriates property or accepts a bribe and causes damage to property, the value of the appropriated property, the bribe, and the damaged property shall be subject to the same penalty bracket. The offender shall be criminally prosecuted according to the corresponding points of this penalty bracket. If the value of the appropriated property, the bribe, and the damaged property falls under different penalty brackets, the offender shall be criminally prosecuted according to the higher penalty bracket.

  Article 10. Determination of the value of property appropriated or the damage caused by corruption-related crimes or other position-related crimes

  1. The value of property appropriated as a basis for criminal prosecution is determined at the time of commission of the crime. In cases where the crime is committed continuously or over an extended period, the value of property appropriated shall be determined at the time of completion of the crime. In cases where the crime is detected or prevented during the commission of the crime, the value of property appropriated shall be determined at the time when the crime is detected or prevented.

  2. The property damage serving as a basis for criminal prosecution shall be determined on a case-by-case basis as follows:

  a) If the damage occurs at the time of commission of the crime, the damage shall be determined at that time; if the crime is committed continuously or over an extended period, the damage shall be determined at the time the crime is detected or prevented or at the time the crime ends.

  b) If the damage occurs or continues after the crime is committed or after the crime ends, the damage shall be determined at the time the damage is prevented.

  c) If the damage cannot be determined in accordance with the guidance in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article, the damage shall be determined at the time the case is filed.

  3. The consideration of civil liability shall comply with the provisions of civil law.

  Article 11. Handling of items and money directly related to crimes

  1. After accepting a case, the Court shall consider immediately applying the measure of distraint of property or freezing of accounts directly related to criminal acts if such property has not yet been subject to enforcement measures during the stage of investigation and prosecution.

  2. The Court shall consider and decide on confiscation for state budget, destruction, return or compensation to lawful owners or managers in accordance with law for property directly related to corruption crimes and other position-related crimes, including:

  a) Instruments and means used for committing crimes;

  b) Money and property appropriated;

  c) Bribes;

  d) Damage caused by criminal acts;

  dd) Illegal gains from criminal acts;

  e) Profits, yields or profits generated from assets acquired as a result of commission of the crime;

  g) Other assets as prescribed by law.

  3. If the assets subject to confiscation into the state budget no longer exist at the time of resolution of the lawsuit, the Court shall decide on the confiscation of the asset value according to the valuation conclusion of the competent authority.

  4. In case assets originate from corruption crimes or other position-related crimes and are incorporated into a common asset group, the Court shall only confiscate or return them to their lawful owners or managers for the portion originating from crimes. The profits generated from this common asset group shall be divided in proportion to be confiscated or returned to the lawful owners or managers in accordance with law.

  Article 12. Effect

  1. This Resolution was adopted by the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on December 30, 2020 and comes into force as of February 15, 2021.

  2. Where the offender was tried before the effective date of this Resolution in accordance with previous regulations and guidance and the judgment has taken legal effect, this Resolution shall not be used as a basis for lodging protests according to cassation or reopening procedures.
