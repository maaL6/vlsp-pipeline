                                                  RESOLUTION

                                      ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF LAW ON LAWYERS

                      THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY OF THE SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM

     Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10 of December 25, 2001 of the Xth National Assembly, the 10th session;

                                                  DECISION:

   1. The Law on Lawyers was passed on June 29, 2006 by the XIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 9th session and took effect on January 1, 2007.

  2. The Government shall review documents detailing and guiding the implementation of the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers in order to annul them or promulgate new documents for the implementation of this Law.

  3. From the effective date of the Law on Lawyers,

  a/ Persons who have been granted lawyer's practice certificates under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers may continue practicing law under this Law;

  b/ A person who is a law-practicing probationer under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers may continue his/her probationary practice of law under this Law; the probationary period shall be counted into the probationary period of law practice under this Law and the probationer's card shall no longer be valid;

  c/ Lawyer’s cards granted by bar associations under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers shall remain valid until they are replaced; the national lawyers’ organization shall guide the replacement of lawyer’s cards;

  d/ Within six months, law-practicing offices or law partnership companies granted operation registration papers under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers shall carry out procedures for conversion into law-practicing offices or law partnership companies under this Law.

  The Ministry of Justice shall guide the conversion of lawyer's offices and law partnership companies which have been granted operation registration papers under the provisions of the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers;

  dd) Within six months, individuals and organizations that are providing legal services under the provisions of the 1999 Law on Enterprises and continue to provide legal services must fully satisfy the conditions for practicing law and must change their organizational form of practice in accordance with this Law; if they fail to make such a change, they must terminate their operation.

  The Ministry of Justice shall guide the conversion for individuals and organizations providing legal services in accordance with the Law on Enterprises 1999;

  e) Foreign lawyers who have been granted practice licenses in Vietnam; branches of foreign law-practicing organizations, foreign law firms, foreign law partnership companies and Vietnamese law firms which have been granted establishment licenses under Decree No. 87/2003/ND-CP of July 22, 2003 of the Government on law practice by foreign law-practicing organizations and foreign lawyers in Vietnam may continue their practice under this Law;

  g/ Bar associations established under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers are not required to carry out the procedures for re-establishment;

  h/ During the period in which the National Association of Lawyers has not been set up, the Ministry of Justice shall organize the examination of law-practicing probation results and the Bar Associations shall grant lawyer’s cards.

  4. The Government shall specify the provision of funding from the state budget for provinces in mountainous areas with extremely difficult conditions to develop a contingent of lawyers and ensure the operation of the Bar Association.

  This Resolution was adopted on June 29, 2006 by the XIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 9th session.
