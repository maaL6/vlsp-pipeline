                                                  DECREE

    AMENDMENTS TO A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 14/2013/ND-CP OF FEBRUARY 5, 2013 AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 07/2007/ND-CP OF JANUARY 12, 2007 DETAILING AND GUIDING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE LAW ON LEGAL ASSISTANCE

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;

  Pursuant to the Law on Legal Aid dated June 29, 2006;

  At the proposal of the Minister of Justice, The Government promulgates a Decree amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government's Decree No. 14/2013/ND-CP dated February 5, 2013 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government's Decree No. 07/2007/ND-CP dated January 12, 2007 detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid.

  Article 1. To amend and supplement a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 14/2013/ND-CP of February 5, 2013 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 07/2007/ND-CP of January 12, 2007 detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid as follows:

  1. Clause 7 Article 1 is amended as follows:

  1. Persons who have law bachelor degrees, graduated from lawyer training courses and are working in state legal aid centers, and are appointed to attend professional re-training courses on legal aid services; if they pass the examination of professional re-training courses on legal aid services, they shall be granted certificates of professional re-training in legal aid services by the Judicial Academy or the Ministry of Justice according to law provisions.

  Persons who used to be lawyers under the Lawyer Law or were exempted from lawyer training courses under the Lawyer Law and are working in State legal aid centers may be appointed to attend examinations on professional re-training in legal aid services; if they pass the examinations on professional re-training in legal aid services, they shall be granted certificates of professional re-training in legal aid services by the Judicial Academy or the Ministry of Justice under law.

  2. The Minister of Justice shall provide for programs and time of training courses in legal aid for professional titles of legal aid professionals; promulgate Regulation on organization, examination and issuance of certificates of training courses in legal aid."

  2. Clause 8 Article 1 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  Article 25. Professional titles of legal aid professionals

  1. Legal aid professionals have professional titles as public employees as prescribed by law.

  2. The Minister of Home Affairs shall promulgate a list of professional titles and their codes and classification of legal aid professionals.

  3. The Minister of Justice shall promulgate professional standards for legal aid titles after reaching agreement with the Minister of Home Affairs."

  3. Clause 9 Article 1 is amended as follows:

  1. Legal aid professionals shall be paid salaries according to the salary table of public employees in accordance with law.

  3. Legal aid professionals shall receive a responsibility-based allowance equal to 25% of their current salary plus seniority allowance and allowances beyond the bracket (if any). When participating in legal proceedings, legal aid professionals shall receive case handling allowance equal to 40% of the allowance applicable to lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State as prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article. When representing outside legal proceedings or conducting mediation, legal aid professionals shall receive case handling allowance equal to 20% of the allowance applicable to lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State.”

  4. Clause 12 Article 1 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  “2. A collaborator shall be entitled to allowance for each legal aid case he/she has handled based on the number of hours of legal counseling and other forms of legal aid provided. The working-hour allowance (half a working day) shall be applied to legal aid cases handled by collaborators in the form of participation in proceedings or extra-procedural representation.

  The level of allowance for legal aid collaborators and lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State is based on the quality of legal aid services provided, the duration of legal aid services provided, the complexity of legal aid cases, the form of legal aid results, and the form of legal aid services provided.

  In case of providing legal aid in the form of participation in legal proceedings, the allowance paid to lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State shall be VND 500,000 per working session or allocated as a lump sum for each case with a minimum amount equal to 3 months' basic salary and a maximum amount equal to 10 months' basic salary (depending on the nature and content of the case). The Ministry of Justice shall specify the nature and content of cases, the method of calculating working sessions, and the time period used as a basis for allocating or paying allowances to lawyers according to working sessions or working periods.

  The time of providing legal aid includes the time of studying the dossiers of cases and preparing documents for the provision of legal aid, the time of meeting and contacting legal aid beneficiaries or their relatives, the time of verifying legal aid cases, and the time of working at agencies, organizations or individuals related to legal aid cases. The basis for determining the working time of collaborators and lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State is the time of working with certification by relevant agencies, organizations or individuals."

  Article 2. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on November 10, 2015.

  Article 3. Implementation responsibilities

  Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.-
