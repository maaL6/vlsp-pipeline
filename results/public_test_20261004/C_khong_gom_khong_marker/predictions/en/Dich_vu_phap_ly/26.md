                                            CIRCULAR
GUIDANCE ON TIME CALCULATION BY WORKSHOP AND BUDGETING OF LEGAL ASSISTANCE
Pursuant to the Law on Legal Aid dated June 20, 2017;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 on elaboration of the Law on Legal Aid;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 96/2017/ND-CP dated August 16, 2017 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Justice;
At the request of the Director of Legal Aid Department;
The Minister of Justice promulgates a Circular providing guidelines for calculation of working hours and allocation of legal aid expenses.
Chapter I
                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope and regulated entities
1. This Circular provides guidelines for calculating working hours and legal aid case fees as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for participation in legal proceedings or extrajudicial representation.
2. This Circular applies to the following entities:
a) Legal aid professionals;
b) Lawyers providing legal aid;
c) Signing contracts for legal aid services;
d) State legal aid centers (hereinafter referred to as centers) and their branches (hereinafter referred to as branches); Departments of Justice and other relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Article 2. Implementation principles
1. Ensure compliance with regulations of law on remuneration, allowances and procedures for payment of remuneration and allowances for case handling for persons providing legal aid or organizations signing contracts for provision of legal aid.
2. Ensure legality and rationality in declaring time and work as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid-related cases.
3. When declaring the time and work of performing legal aid cases, declarers shall be responsible for the authenticity of their declarations.
4. When certifying the time and work of the legal aid-providing person, the certifier shall be responsible for the authenticity of the certification.
Chapter II
               HOW TO CALCULATE TIME BASED ON WORK HOURS
Article 3. Working hours
1. The time of half a working day shall be applied to legal aid cases in the form of participation in proceedings or extra-procedural representation selected by the legal aid-providing person as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for case handling.
2. One working day shall be counted on the basis of 4 working hours. In case a legal aid worker works less than 4 hours, the number of working days shall be calculated as follows:
a) The remaining time shall be counted as half of a working day if the number of hours is not divisible by 3;
b) It is considered as 01 working day if the number of hours of work is odd and at least 03 hours.

3. When applying the method of calculating working hours, legal aid-providing persons shall record their tasks and time of performing such tasks in the Schedule of actual working hours (Form TP-TGPL-01) enclosed with this Circular.
Article 4. Time limit for criminal proceedings

1. The time limit for legal aid in criminal proceedings shall be determined as follows:
a) Participate in interrogation of suspects or take statements from accused persons, crime victims, witnesses and litigants in criminal cases;
b) Participate in confrontation, voice identification and recognition, scene examination, autopsy, inspection of traces on the body, experimental investigation, and other activities to participate in legal proceedings as required by the presiding authorities;
c) Working with proceeding officers and agencies;
d) Verify, collect and assess relevant documents, items and evidence according to stages of legal proceedings;
dd) Working with persons facing charges in detention cells of border guard stations, detention houses, temporary custody centers or prisons;
e) Meet suspects on bail or their relatives; crime victims or their relatives; witnesses, litigants in criminal cases and other participants in legal proceedings;
g) Research documents, make copies and prepare documents at the presiding agency or at the center, branch or organization signing the legal aid contract;
h) Prepare arguments for defense and protection;
i) Attend the Court session. In case where the legal aid-providing person attends the Court session but the trial is postponed not at the request of the legal aid-providing person and without prior notice, the time during which the legal aid-providing person works with the presiding agency or presiding officer on matters related to the postponement of the Court session shall be counted;
k) Perform tasks related to procedures for appeal against first-instance judgments, appellate judgments, cassation or reopening procedures;
l) Perform other reasonable tasks as prescribed by law to serve the defense and protection.
2. The remuneration and allowances for persons providing legal aid services when performing the tasks specified in Points e, g, h, l Clause 1 of this Article shall not exceed the number of sessions of performance of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 01 enclosed with this Circular.
3. The working time of a legal aid-providing person must be certified by an agency, organization or individual as follows:
a) The time of performance of activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the proceeding officer or the proceeding agency;
b) The time of performing activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or agencies conducting proceedings or persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
c) The time of performing activities specified in point d clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where the persons providing legal aid work directly;
d) The time of performing activities specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be confirmed by the accused or the presiding agency or officers of the Border Guard Station, the detention center, the custody center or the prison;
dd) The time of performing activities specified in Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons working directly with legal aid-providing persons;
e) The time of performing activities specified in Point g Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the proceeding officer or the center, branch or organization signing the legal aid contract;
g) The time of performing activities specified in Points h, k and l Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Center or its Branch or organization signing a legal aid contract;
h) The time of performance of activities specified in Point i Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or Court clerk.
Article 5. Time limit for participation in civil procedures

1. The time limit for provision of legal aid in a civil case shall be determined on the basis of:
a) Work with legal aid beneficiaries and relevant persons to determine the legal relationship of the dispute; assess conditions for initiating lawsuits, counter-claims, independent claims; provide guidance on drafting petitions for initiation of lawsuits, petitions for application of provisional urgent measures, counter-claims, independent claims, and evidence for the court; guide preparation of lawsuit files or other tasks during the stage of lawsuit initiation and acceptance of cases;
b) Work with legal aid beneficiaries, litigants and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals on the origin and content of disputes, evidence to prove or other tasks in the stage of trial preparation;
c) Attend meetings to inspect the submission, access, and disclosure of evidence; verify, collect, and assess necessary documents, items, and evidence;
d) Participate in taking statements from involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons; participate in activities of property appraisal and valuation and perform other procedural tasks at the request of the presiding agency;
dd) Participate in mediation as prescribed by law;
e) Working with proceeding officers and agencies in various stages of legal proceedings;
g) Meet with involved parties, witnesses and other participants in legal proceedings regarding other relevant matters;
h) Research documents, make copies and prepare documents at the presiding agency or at centers, branches or organizations signing contracts for legal aid services;
i) Prepare arguments to defend;
k) Attend the Court session or meeting to resolve civil matters. In case where the legal aid-providing person attends the Court session but the postponement of the trial is not at the request of the legal aid-providing person and is not notified in advance, the time for which the legal aid-providing person works with the presiding agency or presiding officer on matters related to the postponement of the Court session shall be counted;
l) Perform tasks related to procedures for appeal against first-instance judgments, appellate judgments, cassation or reopening procedures;
m) Perform other reasonable tasks as prescribed by law to serve the protection.
2. The remuneration and allowances for persons providing legal aid services when performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i, and m Clause 1 of this Article shall not exceed the number of sessions of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 02 enclosed with this Circular.
3. The working time of a legal aid-providing person must be certified by an agency, organization or individual as follows:
a) The time of performing activities specified in Points a and b Clause 1 of this Article shall be confirmed by persons working directly with legal aid-providing persons or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts for providing legal aid;
b) The time of participation in the meeting for examination of the handover, access and disclosure of evidence specified at Point c, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the person assigned to resolve the case; the time of verification, collection and assessment of necessary documents, items and evidence related to the case specified at Point c, Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the agency, organization or individual where the legal aid provider comes to verify and collect evidence;
c) The time for the involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons to give statements as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by such persons or presiding officers or procedural authorities; the time for the appraisal and valuation of property and performance of other activities of legal proceedings at the request of presiding authorities as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where the property is appraised or valued or presiding officers or proceeding officers;
d) The time of conducting activities specified in Points dd and k Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or Court clerk or conciliation meeting clerk;
dd) The time of performance of activities specified in Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the procedural authority or person;
e) The time of performing activities specified in point g clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons working directly with legal aid-providing persons;
g) The time of performing activities specified in point h clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the person assigned to resolve the case or the center, branch or organization signing a legal aid contract;
h) The time of performing activities specified in Points i, l and m Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the center or its branches or organizations signing legal aid contracts.
Article 6. Time for participation in administrative proceedings
1. The basis for calculating the time of providing legal aid for cases of participation in administrative proceedings includes:

a) Participate in activities specified in Points a, b, c, d, e, g, h, i, k, l, and m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) Participate in dialogues.
2. The remuneration and allowances for persons providing legal aid services when performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i and m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular shall not exceed the number of sessions of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 03 enclosed herewith.
3. The working time of a legal aid-providing person must be certified by an agency, organization or individual as follows:
a) The time for performing activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations and individuals specified in Clause 3 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) The time of conducting activities specified in point b clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the judge or the dialogue secretary.
Article 7. Time of participation in extrajudicial representation
1. The basis for calculating the time of providing legal aid for extrajudicial representation cases includes:
a) Meeting and contacting legal aid beneficiaries and their relatives; witnesses;
b) Study dossiers and prepare documents for representation;
c) Verify and collect documents, items, evidence, and facts related to the representation;
d) Working with relevant agencies, organizations and individuals;
dd) Representing before competent agencies, organizations or individuals in charge of the case.
2. The working time of a legal aid-providing person must be certified by an agency, organization or individual as follows:
a) The time of performing activities specified in Points a, d and dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons working with legal aid-providing persons or relevant agencies, organizations and individuals or competent agencies, organizations and individuals handling the cases;
b) The time of performing activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the center or its branch or organization signing a legal aid contract.
Article 8. Method of determining time in some special cases
1. In case 2 or more persons providing legal aid for 1 person in the same case as prescribed by law, the period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling the legal aid case shall be the actual period of each person providing legal aid but the total period must not exceed 30 working sessions/case for cases involving participation in legal proceedings or 20 working sessions/case for cases involving extrajudicial representation.
2. In case the presiding agency suspends the lawsuit
a) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid-related cases is the actual period from the date of completion until the date of suspension of the case;
b) When the lawsuit is further resolved, the time for which the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for legal aid-related cases is used shall be the actual time for performing subsequent tasks from the time when the lawsuit is further resolved to the time when the case is closed.
3. In case of replacement or change of legal aid-providing persons
a) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the case handling for the replaced or changed person is the actual period during which they provided legal aid until the time they were replaced or changed;
b) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the replacement is the actual period during which the replacement performs subsequent tasks after being appointed. If the replacement chooses to allocate expenses for the case, the remuneration and allowances for both the replacement and the replaced person shall not exceed the allocated expenses for the case prescribed in Appendix No. 01, Appendix No. 02, and Appendix No. 03 enclosed with this Circular.
4. In case of providing legal aid for persons denounced or facing requisitions for charges or facing criminal charges at the time of arrest or temporary detainment of suspects or crime victims as prescribed by procedural law, the time and confirmation of time shall be as follows:
a) The time for verification, collection and assessment of necessary documents, items and evidence related to the case, which are verified and collected by agencies, organizations or individuals where the legal aid-providing person comes from;
b) Attend the process of taking statements from persons denounced or facing requisitions for charges, crime victims or other relevant persons; participate in denunciation activities, voice identification and recognition activities with investigators or procurators as confirmed by investigators or procurators;
c) Working time with the accused in the detention room of the Border Guard Station, Detention Center or Temporary Detention Camp confirmed by the accused or officials of the Border Guard Station, Detention Center or Temporary Detention Camp or the presiding agency;
d) The time of meeting with the accused or the person recommended for prosecution or their relatives; the victim or the victim’s relatives; other relevant persons as confirmed by these persons, but not exceeding the number of sessions of performing this work under the form of assignment of case expenditure in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith, depending on the seriousness of the crime.
Chapter III
                                      WORKING BLOCK
Article 9. Case-based payment

1. The case-based payment shall be made in the form of participation in legal proceedings by the legal aid-providing person after being assigned and not changed during the process of providing legal aid.
2. Complexity, procedural requirements and contents of each specific case shall be used to determine the level of reimbursement for case-related expenses as follows:
a) Apply regulations on classification of crimes in Clause 2 Article 1 of the Law on amendments to the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 dated June 20, 2017 to determine the level of expenditure on criminal proceedings.
The classification of crimes must be applied immediately upon selecting the form of charging and based on one of the documents issued by the presiding agency: the decision to charge the suspect, the investigation conclusion, the indictment, the forensic report, or the judgment. In cases where multiple documents contain different crime titles under different penalty brackets, the document with the higher penalty bracket shall prevail.
b) Apply Clause 3 Article 337 of the Civil Procedure Code and Article 5 of Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the level of case allocation for civil proceeding cases.
c) Apply Clause 12 Article 3 of the Law on Administrative Procedures and Article 6 of Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the level of charging for cases of participation in administrative procedures.
3. When making a case assignment, the legal aid provider shall perform the following tasks:
a) For criminal proceedings: perform tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 4 of this Circular.
Details of tasks to be performed and maximum amounts thereof (converted by working session) for each case are specified in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith. In case the number of converted sessions for case allocation is lower than the three basic pay levels, the allocated amount shall equal the sum of these three levels.
b) For civil proceeding or administrative proceeding cases: perform tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 5 and Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.
Details of tasks to be performed and maximum amounts thereof (converted by working session) for each case are specified in Appendix No. 02 and Appendix No. 03 enclosed herewith. In case the number of converted sessions for case allocation is lower than 03 times the basic salary, the allocated amount shall equal 03 times the basic salary.
4. When incurring expenses for case-based provision of legal aid services, legal aid-providing persons shall list jobs and certify the performed jobs in the Job List (Form TP-TGPL-02 enclosed herewith).
5. In case a legal aid case is provided with legal aid services in multiple stages as specified in the Appendix enclosed herewith, the level of case allocation shall be applied to each stage but must not exceed 10 times the base salary.
Article 10. Case-specific contract in some special cases
1. In case 2 or more persons providing legal aid for 1 person in the same case, depending on the actual tasks of each person, the maximum amount of the case shall be 130% of the corresponding amount specified in the Appendices enclosed with this Circular but must not exceed 10 times the basic salary.

Example 1: A is prosecuted for a crime with a penalty bracket of very serious crimes. The Director of Legal Aid Center assigns 2 persons to provide legal aid for A from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work done by the 2 persons providing legal aid, the maximum amount of budget for case assignment shall be: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, only 10 times the basic salary can be allocated for case assignment.
2. In case of providing legal aid for a legally-aided person who is prosecuted for two or more crimes in the same case, the level of charging for the legal aid provided shall be as follows:
a) If two offences have different penalty frames, based on the actual tasks performed, the maximum rate of budget allocation for cases shall be 130% of the rate applicable to cases with higher penalty frames as prescribed in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith but must not exceed 10 times the basic salary.
Example 2: A is prosecuted for 02 crimes: 01 crime with a penalty bracket of serious offense and 01 crime with a penalty bracket of very serious offense. The legal assistant provides legal aid for A from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of case assignment is: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, only 10 times the basic salary can be assigned.
b) If two offences have the same penalty bracket, based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of the budget shall be 130% of the amount applied to such case as prescribed in Appendix 1 enclosed with this Circular but not exceeding 10 times the basic salary. Example 3: B is prosecuted for two offences with the same penalty bracket of very serious offence. The legal assistant provided legal assistance for B from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of the budget shall not exceed: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since the number of sessions exceeds 10 times the basic salary, the budget shall be allocated for 10 times the basic salary.
3. In case the presiding agency suspends the lawsuit
a) The contract for the case of the legal aid provider is based on the actual work that the legal aid provider has performed up to the time of suspension;
b) When the case is further resolved, the settlement of the matter by the legal aid provider shall be based on the work performed by the legal aid provider from the time of further resolution until the end of the case.
4. In case of replacement or change of legal aid-providing persons
a) The contract for the case of the replaced or changed person is based on the actual work that the replaced or changed person has provided legal aid until the time of replacement or change;
b) The contract for the replacement shall be based on the actual work performed by the replacement after being appointed.
If the person designated to replace the person is selected by working session, the remuneration and allowance for both persons (the replacement and the replaced person) shall not exceed 30 working sessions/one case as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 13 of the Government's Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 elaborating on certain articles of the Law on Legal Aid.
Chapter IV
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 11. Transition clauses
For activities arising before the effective date of this Circular, regulations of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time of performance and payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time of performance and payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases shall continue to apply.
Article 12. Effect
1. This Circular comes into force as of July 15, 2021.
2. This Circular replaces Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases.
