                                                                     MINISTER OF PUBLIC SECURITY
Phạm Hồng Hà



                                                          PLAN

     IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GOVERNMENT PRIME MINISTER’S DIRECTIVE No. 11/CT-TTG DATED APRIL 23, 2019 ON A NUMBER OF MEASURES TO SUPPORT THE NON-INDUSTRIAL SCHOOLS IN THE REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND REHABILITATION
      (Enforced together with Decision No. 398/QD-BXD dated May 22, 2019 of the Minister of Construction)

  I. OBJECTIVES

  - Synchronous, timely and effective implementation of Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019 of the Prime Minister on solutions to promote the development of the real estate market in a stable and healthy manner (hereinafter referred to as Directive No. 11/CT-TTg).

  - Intensify the improvement of housing and real estate market management institutions in a timely manner and in conformity with practical situations; effectively implement social housing programs: support for housing for people with meritorious services to the revolution; support for housing for poor households in rural areas, flood-prone areas in central Vietnam, regularly flooded areas of the Mekong Delta; housing for low-income people in urban areas, housing for workers in industrial parks, and housing for students, etc.; intensify the guidance on inspection and examination of housing and real estate market management.

  II. PLAN DETAILS

  1. Regarding the task of completing projects of the Law on Amendments to the Law on Construction, the Law on Housing and the Law on Real Estate Business:

  1.1. To study and finalize the draft Law Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Construction Law.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Construction Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Legal Department and related departments;

  - Completion deadline: Submit to the Government in July 2019; finalize and submit to the National Assembly in October 2019.

  1.2. Study and finalize the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Housing regarding housing support policies for officials, public servants and employees facing housing difficulties according to Resolution No. 26-NQ/TW of the XIIth Central Committee;

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Legal Department and related departments;

  - Completion time limit: Expected in 2020 (after the Politburo approves the housing project for officials, public employees and civil servants in the political system and issues a Resolution on this issue).

  1.3. Study and finalize the draft Law on amendments to several articles of the Law on real estate business

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Legal Department and related departments;

  - Time limit for completion: Under the program on law-making of the National Assembly.

  2. Research and promulgate amendments to construction regulations and standards applicable to apartment buildings, condominium buildings, resort villas, officetels, guest houses, and other lodging structures.

  - The focal unit: Department of Science, Technology and Environment;

  - Implementing unit: National Architecture Institute;

  - Completion time limit: to be completed in 2019.

  3. Research and propose promulgation of legislative documents on regulations on management and operation of office-temporary accommodation (officetel) type works

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Legal Department and related units;

  - Completion time: 3rd quarter of 2019.

  4. For the task of urging local governments to complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  4.1. Promptly complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: Information Center, Construction Economics Institute;

  - Completion time limit: Regular tasks.

  4.2. Cooperate with ministries and central authorities in collecting relevant information to improve housing and real estate market information system

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The General Department of Statistics - the Ministry of Planning and Investment;

  - Completion time limit: Regular tasks.

  5. Research and amend policies and laws and expedite social housing development

  5.1. Research and amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing;

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Legal Department and related units;

  - Completion time limit: after the Law on amendments to the Law on Housing is passed by the National Assembly.

  5.2. Urge and guide local governments to focus on promoting social housing development in accordance with the Prime Minister’s Directive No. 03/CT-TTg dated January 25, 2017

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The local construction service and relevant units;

  - Time limit for completion: monthly regular work.

  5.3. Inspect and review planning and use of urban land for social housing development in key areas, consolidate situation and report to Prime Minister

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The local construction service and relevant units;

  - Completion deadline: September 2019.

  6. Regarding the task of cooperating with ministries, central authorities and local governments in enhancing inspection and requiring project developers of urban area projects and housing development projects to synchronously build technical infrastructures, social infrastructures, fire prevention and firefighting systems in their projects and connect them with general infrastructure of the area before transferring houses to people according to construction laws, housing laws and relevant law provisions.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: Construction Inspectorate, Urban Development Department and relevant units;

  - Completion time limit: in 2019.

  7. Regularly monitor and summarize the situation on a periodic or ad hoc basis to report to the Government and Prime Minister on developments of the real estate market in order to take timely measures to stabilize the market when there are signs of abnormal conditions or large fluctuations.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Construction Economics Institute and related units;

  - Time limit for completion: quarterly and irregularly at request.

  III. Implementation

  1. Task assignment:

  The Minister of Construction shall take charge, direct and manage the implementation of the Plan on solutions to promote the development of the real estate market in a stable and healthy manner;

  Departments in charge shall assign and direct units to implement the Plan;

  Heads of units affiliated to the Ministry shall, based on the Ministry’s implementation plan, organize the performance of assigned tasks in a timely and effective manner; clearly assign tasks to leaders and specialists responsible for each specific task of their units; focus on directing, strengthen inspection and supervision of progress and results of implementation of the Plan; report on the implementation of the Plan according to their assigned functions and tasks to the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management for consolidation and reporting to the Minister.

  Director of Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management shall take charge and cooperate with Ministry Office in monitoring, consolidating, reporting on implementation results of the Plan; promptly report difficulties to the Minister for resolution.

  2. Funding for implementation

  Ministries of Planning and Finance, Science, Technology and Environment shall balance and allocate funding for performance of tasks according to the estimates of units assigned to perform tasks from annual sources of economic and scientific and technological services and other lawful funding sources in accordance with current regulations.

  Tasked units shall prepare and submit estimates of implementation to the Department of Planning and Finance, the Department of Science, Technology and Environment for reporting to the Minister for consideration and approval.

  During the implementation of the Plan, if it is necessary to amend or supplement specific tasks, units shall proactively propose and report them to the Minister for consideration and decision./.
