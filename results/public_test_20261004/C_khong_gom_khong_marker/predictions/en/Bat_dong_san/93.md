                                                      DIRECTIVE

     ON A NUMBER OF SOLUTIONS TO SUPPORT THE SCHOOL’S DEVELOPMENT OF SAFE, HEALTHY AND RESILIENT INDUSTRIES

  Over the past period, the world's political and economic situation has been characterized by complex, unstable and unpredictable changes, affecting Vietnam's economy with a very large opening. In this context, the Government has issued many directives aimed at stabilizing macroeconomics, controlling inflation, ensuring major balances, helping the economy to recover and achieve relatively high growth rates compared to other countries in the region and in the world.

  On October 11, 2021, the Government promulgated Resolution No. 128/NQ-CP on safe and flexible adaptation to and effective control of COVID-19; Resolution No. 01/NQ-CP dated January 8, 2022 on major tasks and solutions for implementation of the socio-economic development plan and state budget estimate for 2022; Resolution No. 02/NQ-CP dated January 10, 2022 on major tasks and solutions for improvement of business environment and enhancement of national competitiveness in 2022; Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP dated January 30, 2022 on Resolution on socio-economic recovery and development program and implementation of Resolution No. 43/2022/QH15 of the National Assembly on fiscal and monetary policies supporting the Program. In particular, the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam’s XIIIth Congress promulgated Resolution No. 18-NQ-TW on further reform, improvement of institutions and policies, enhancement of the effectiveness and efficiency of land management and use, and promotion of Vietnam’s transformation into a high-income developed country.

  The active and effective implementation of resolutions of the Party and the State, regulations of law, direction, management, solutions and policies of the Government on real estate market by local authorities, sectors, industries and enterprises has helped to make positive changes in socio-economic situation, ensure social security and macroeconomic stability.

  The real estate market plays a very important role in maintaining macroeconomic stability, controlling inflation, ensuring major balance and promoting growth. However, apart from positive results, the development of the real estate sector also exposes many problems, limitations and disadvantages, which are not truly sustainable and pose many risks, such as: the real estate product structure is unreasonable, the prices of real estate products, especially housing prices, are still quite high compared to people's incomes, the information database on the real estate market is not synchronized, insufficient, untrustworthy and opaque...

  Some problems to be solved include: The legal system related to real estate market has some contents which have not yet met practical needs and have not been amended or added in time; development of real estate projects in some localities does not follow the planning and plan and is not suitable for market demand; investment procedures are prescribed in many different legal documents through many steps, thus delaying the implementation of investment procedures; regulations on real estate transactions not required through the platform have created the basis for the formation of "ghost projects" and customer fraud cases, while the state also faces many difficulties in managing information about the real estate market, at the same time, there is a situation of tax revenue loss and loss to the state budget; the state management is not strict, not timely, and not well meet the management requirements...

  In order to remedy the above-mentioned problems and shortcomings and further promote the safe, healthy and sustainable development of the real estate market in the future, the Prime Minister requests:

  I. A NUMBER OF GENERAL POINTS AND SOLUTIONS

  1. Review regulations of law to resolve immediate and long-term issues in direction of building healthy, appropriate and effective policy instruments to ensure interconnection, safety and certainty between capital markets and real estate market, diversify capital sources for real estate market; promote decentralization and delegation at all levels, conduct thorough research and propose pilot policies to create new driving force for market development.

  2. Persistently, firmly and decisively develop a safe, healthy and sustainable real estate ecosystem; do not tighten credit irrationally but strengthen inspection and supervision, do not relax state management; do not criminalize economic - civil relations; proactively detect, prevent and strictly handle violations in accordance with law, pay attention to tax evasion in real estate business, do not let any collapse occur, protect those who do the right thing, protect and harmonize interests of the State, people and enterprises.

  3. Harmonize, reasonably and effectively combine fiscal policies and monetary policies, implement monetary policies in a flexible, safe and cautious manner, expand fiscal policies in a reasonable and effective manner, focus on ensuring efficiency, strictly manage prices and markets; do not implement “impulsive” policies, do not suddenly change from “loosening” to strict control or vice versa.

  4. Ministries, central authorities and local governments shall focus on implementing tasks prescribed in Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on socio-economic recovery and development program.

  5. To focus on implementing solutions to attract investment, promote production and business activities, create more jobs in various fields and localities; stabilize macroeconomics, control inflation, and ensure major balances of the economy.

  6. Conduct specific and accurate assessment of the situation and supply-demand to develop the real estate market on the basis of respecting the market laws and parallel competition laws, promoting the regulatory management role, and using policy tools to intervene and control when necessary in a proper, timely, and effective manner, avoiding manipulation of the market, hoarding goods, and price hikes.

  7. Identify market segments and demands such as industrial real estate, worker housing, low-income housing in order to develop appropriate policies to promote the market, focus on social security, create opportunities for low-income people, "not sacrifice social progress and equality for mere economic growth".

  8. Develop strategic infrastructure to create new development space and new development drivers, promote the formation of new industries, services and urban areas.

  9. Intensify inspection, examination and audit to resolve difficulties and handle violations in order to protect the market, assets and human lives, to assess violations in an objective manner, find out causes and mechanisms for resolving practical difficulties.

  10. To do a good job in communication with appropriate forms of effect, promote education and training in financial knowledge and legal knowledge for the people, in schools and other forms.

  II. A NUMBER OF SPECIALIZED TASKS AND SOLUTIONS FOR CRITICAL ISSUES

  1. Ministry of Construction

  a) Expeditiously study and improve the legal system related to real estate to ensure consistency, resolve difficulties and obstacles, enable the real estate market to develop stably and healthily, build a real estate market information system associated with land information, improve legal basis and increase implementation of non-cash payment in real estate transactions. Accordingly, propose the National Assembly for opinions and approval of the Law on Housing (amended) and the Law on Real Estate Business (amended) to create a legal framework for real estate business activities, enhance the effectiveness of real estate market management; promptly amend and supplement Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing, Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP dated April 01, 2021 of the Government on amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing in accordance with the Law on Housing.

  b) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in researching and proposing regulations on guidelines for implementation of housing and urban area projects.

  c) Regularly monitor the real estate market and quarterly report to the Prime Minister on the real estate business and real estate market nationwide and propose solutions for the stable and healthy development of the real estate market.

  d) Inspect and examine compliance with regulations on real estate trading, real estate transaction platform operation, and real estate brokerage; cooperate with provincial People’s Committees in organizing inspection and review of housing and real estate business projects to propose competent authorities to revoke, suspend, temporarily suspend, adjust, convert, or transfer real estate business projects as per the law.

  dd) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in establishing a working group to direct, guide, and urge local governments to implement Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on the socio-economic rehabilitation and development program regarding policies supporting social housing development, worker housing in industrial parks, and renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings.

  e) Direct, guide, and cooperate with local governments in reviewing and supplementing land use planning and allocating land fund for social housing and worker housing development in accordance with regulations of law; strictly adhere to the provision of 20% land fund in housing projects and urban areas; ensure demand for social housing and worker housing development in their areas.

  g) Direct, guide, and cooperate with local governments in reviewing, preparing, and publishing lists of investors in social housing, worker housing, and apartment renovation projects that are eligible for 2% interest rate support via commercial bank system.

  h) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in researching and proposing policies to promote development of social housing and housing for workers in industrial parks.

  i) Improve and publicize housing and real estate market information systems.

  2. Ministry of Finance

  a) Expeditiously amend Decree No. 153/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020 of the Government on offering and trading in separate corporate bonds on domestic market and offering of corporate bonds to international markets.

  b) Control capital mobilization activities of real estate businesses on the securities market to avoid speculation, manipulation and price manipulation.

  c) Cooperate with State Bank of Vietnam in inspecting and supervising compliance with regulations on issuance, investment and provision of services related to corporate bonds, especially issuance of separate bonds of real estate enterprises, credit institutions related to real estate enterprises, enterprises with large issuance volume, high interest rates, loss-making enterprises, issuing enterprises without collateral.

  d) Direct and guide capital mobilization (including bond issuance) of real estate businesses on the securities market in accordance with law; enable and not obstruct enterprises (with sufficient capacity, good and healthy business results) to mobilize capital for recovery and development.

  dd) Research and improve tax policies in conformity with reality for real estate to encourage effective use of houses and land, reduce speculation on houses and land, ensure reasonable and stable revenues for state budget, conform to socio-economic conditions of Vietnam and international practices.

  3. Ministry of Planning and Investment

  a) Direct and urge local governments to propose solutions for resolving difficulties and obstacles, and resolutely reduce unnecessary administrative procedures in investment procedures, approval of investment policies, investment decisions, and issuance of certificates of investment registration for housing and real estate projects to support and increase market supply.

  b) Monitor and promote the attraction of foreign investment in the real estate business.

  4. Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment

  a/ To coordinate with the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Justice in studying, reviewing and amending current law provisions related to land use rights auction (law on auction, law on land and law on tax administration) to ensure uniformity and compatibility with local actual conditions.

  b) Inspect and inspect, and direct local governments to prevent and strictly handle violations in land use rights auction activities in accordance with law.

  c) Inspect and review indicators of land use planning and plans to ensure appropriate land use planning for various industries, professions and fields in a harmonious and rational manner, avoiding overlapping and disputes.

  5. Ministry of Public Security Take charge and cooperate closely with the Ministry of Finance, State Bank of Vietnam and relevant agencies in enhancing macroeconomics monitoring; take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Construction and the Ministry of Finance in strictly handling illegal real estate trading floors and real estate brokerage activities, tax evasion, providing inaccurate information, causing negative impacts on financial, credit and real estate market activities.

  6. Ministry of Information and Communications Directs news and press agencies to enhance provision of timely, official, accurate and adequate information on real estate market, securities market and bond market; strictly handle acts of providing inaccurate information causing negative impacts on financial, credit and real estate market activities.

  7. State Bank of Vietnam

  Strictly monitor credit in real estate sector. Direct credit institutions to strictly control credit quality and grant loans in real estate sector in accordance with regulations of law; promptly consider prioritizing loans for social housing, worker housing, affordable commercial housing with high efficiency and capable of being repaid.

  8. People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities

  a) Expeditiously organize the formulation, appraisal and approval of urban and rural construction planning, land use planning and plans; publicly disclose information on construction planning, lists and progress of infrastructure development projects and real estate projects, especially large-scale ones, as well as the merger, establishment or upgrading of local administrative units, thereby preventing rumors and speculation to push up prices for illegal profit-seeking purposes.

  b) Expeditiously develop housing development programs for the period from now to 2025 and 2030, as well as five-year and annual housing development plans in accordance with the Law on Housing, which clearly define lists of housing projects serving as the basis for approving investment guidelines of housing projects in the area.

  c) Review and produce list of housing and real estate projects in the area; specifically assess reasons for projects approved for investment guidelines but not yet implemented or delayed implementation. On this basis, focus on resolving legal and procedural difficulties in preparation for investment in housing projects to increase supply for the market.

  d) Organize public disclosure of lists of projects, land funds, procedures for housing and real estate development in the area to attract investment; facilitate domestic and foreign investors to easily access and participate in housing and real estate investment.

  dd) Direct and organize land allocation and selection of project developers for new real estate projects and housing projects to increase market supply.

  e) Promote the implementation of land use right auctions; bidding for projects using land to select investors to increase supply of commercial housing projects for the real estate market.

  g) Implement solutions to promote social housing and worker housing development in the area:

  - Review and supplement land use planning and allocate land fund for social housing and worker housing development in accordance with regulations of law; strictly implement the provision of 20% land fund in housing projects and urban areas; ensure demand for social housing and worker housing development in the area.

  - Focus on implementation of Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on socio-economic rehabilitation and development program, in which expediently make list of projects, review land fund, complete legal procedures, select investors to implement social housing, worker housing in industrial parks, renovate and reconstruct old apartment buildings in the area.

  - Review eligible entities and conditions to produce and publish list of investors in social housing, worker housing, renovation of old apartment buildings eligible for loan package with 2% interest via commercial bank system according to Decree on interest support from state budget for loans granted by enterprises, cooperatives, business households.

  - Cooperate with Vietnam Bank for Social Policies in reviewing and consolidating demand of customers who are individuals or households borrowing to purchase or lease-purchase social housing, worker housing; building new houses or renovating, repairing houses according to Article 16 of Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP, Clause 10 Article 1 of Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP and relevant documents, ensuring accuracy and reflecting actual demand of people.

  h) Expeditiously complete quality assessment of remaining old apartment buildings in the area to identify apartment buildings requiring demolition; promptly prepare and approve plans and lists of apartment building renovation and reconstruction projects according to Decree No. 69/2021/ND-CP.

  i) Control, detect, prevent and strictly handle violations in land use rights auction activities in accordance with law.

  k) Take measures to manage and prevent the division of land, "land division and sale on the premise" in areas where investment is not permitted or where infrastructure is lacking; strengthen control over activities of real estate trading floors, organizations and individuals engaging in real estate brokerage; rectify acts of direct trading and "pushing up prices" causing disorder in real estate market information.

  l) Review projects that use land but are implemented late, left uncultivated or used for wrong purposes, causing land wastefulness; on the basis of which, cooperate with relevant agencies in considering and guiding handling according to their competence and regulations of law on land and other relevant laws to bring land fund into effective use, develop in a way that is suitable for actual demand of society.

  m) Closely monitor and promptly grasp information and market developments, and take timely measures to stabilize the market and prevent property price spikes and real estate bubbles in the area.

  n) Intensify inspection and examination and concurrently take strict actions against violations of real estate business laws, land laws, construction laws and relevant laws (if any).

  9. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall, within their assigned functions and tasks, thoroughly grasp and strictly organize implementation of this Directive; concurrently, submit reports to the Ministry of Construction every 6 months and annually for consolidation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  10. The Ministry of Construction shall take charge and cooperate with the Government Office and relevant agencies in guiding, urging and inspecting the implementation of this Directive, and consolidate and report to the Prime Minister.
