                                                    COMMUNICATION

   ON IMPLEMENTATION OF DECISION NO. 25/2023/QD-TTG DATED OCTOBER 03, 2023 OF THE PRIME MINISTER ON REDUCTION IN LAND RENTS FOR 2023.

                                 THE DIRECTOR GENERAL OF THE ELECTRICITY TAX OFFICE

                        - Tax Departments of provinces and centrally-run cities;
                        - Departments and Units under the General Department of Taxation.
  On October 3, 2023, the Prime Minister issued Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg on reduction of land rents for 2023. In order to promptly implement the contents specified in the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg, the General Department of Taxation requests the Directors of Tax Departments of provinces and central-affiliated cities to direct Tax Departments and Sub-Departments to promptly carry out the following tasks based on the contents specified in the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg:

  1. Tax authorities at all levels shall widely and thoroughly disseminate the content of the policy on land rent reduction as prescribed in Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg on land rent reduction for 2023 to organizations, units, enterprises, households, and individuals that are directly leased land by the State under the land lease decision or land lease contract or certificate of land use rights and ownership of houses and other property on land issued by competent state agencies in form of annual land rent payment, specifically:

  - In each tax area, tax authorities shall proactively cooperate with press agencies, radio and television stations, branches/representative offices of Vietnams Customs, etc. in organizing communication and dissemination of the contents of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg.

  - Develop documents guiding implementation of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg, post these documents on the website of the Department of Taxation, and send them via email to organizations, units, enterprises, households, and individuals directly leased land by the State under the Land Lease Decision or Land Lease Contract or Certificate of land use rights, ownership of houses and other property on land issued by competent regulatory authorities in form of annual payment.

  - Information dissemination must be regular and continuous in various forms, ensuring taxpayers have access to and fully understand the scope, beneficiaries, time limits, procedures, and benefits when implementing policies on land rent reduction so that taxpayers can properly and promptly implement state support policies and resolve financial difficulties for taxpayers.

  2. Process applications for land rent reduction in accordance with Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg.

  Based on the application for land rent reduction in 2023 submitted by the land lessee according to Article 4 of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister, the tax authority shall determine the amount of land rent eligible for reduction and issue a decision on land rent reduction as prescribed by law on collection of land rents. At the same time, it shall consolidate the situation of land rent reduction under Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg within its province or city, update information on land rent reduction provided by taxpayers, the amount of land rent payable in 2023, and adjust the corresponding late payment interest (if any) on the system. In case the land lessee has paid the land rent in 2023 but there is an excess amount after the competent authority determines and decides the land rent reduction, such excess amount shall be deducted from the land rent payable for the next period or the next year in accordance with tax administration laws and other relevant laws. In case there is no subsequent period for land rent payment, the excess amount shall be offset or refunded in accordance with tax administration laws and other relevant laws.

  3. Heads of tax authorities shall assign relevant departments within their tax authorities to update, search, review and inspect dossiers, and expedite implementation in accordance with law. Where taxpayers are found ineligible for land rent reduction as prescribed, they shall be notified to pay land rent in accordance with applicable regulations on land rent (excluding Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister).

  4. The Tax Department shall consolidate and submit a report on the implementation of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister (according to the appendix/form enclosed therewith), specifically: the implementation status, the amount of land rent reduction, the number of applications for reduction, the number of applications already reduced, the number of pending applications (if any) and the reasons for failure to reduce them, difficulties and proposals (if any) to the Department of Policy - General Tax Department before April 30, 2024 at the address of the General Tax Department, 123 Lò Đúc, Hai Bà Trưng, Hà Nội, and send the soft copy via email: nbngoc01@gdt.gov.vn.

  The General Department of Taxation requests the Directors of Tax Departments to strictly implement this Circular. Any practical problems arising should be promptly reported to the General Department of Taxation for guidance on resolution./.
