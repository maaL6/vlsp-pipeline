  Article 2. Pursuant to this Resolution, the People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall be responsible for:

  1. To organize adjustment of land use planning to 2020 and final land use plan (2016-2020), including review and adjustment of planning of sectors, fields and localities in province using land for agreement with adjustment of land use planning to 2020 and final land use plan (2016-2020) of province; organization of adjustment of land use planning and plan must strictly meet requirements for socio-economic development, ensure national defense and security in province.

  2. Define boundaries and publicize areas of rice cultivation land, protective forests and special-use forests that need to be strictly protected; regulate allocation of resources, first of all, state budget resources to ensure benefits between areas eligible for industrial development, urban development and service provision and areas protecting rice cultivation land and forest land; adopt appropriate policies and measures to reduce production costs, increase incomes and enable rice farmers to have confidence in production; increase investment in social infrastructure and technical infrastructure to ensure uniformity and synchronization among areas.

  3. To continue to direct the formulation and approval of land use planning adjustments up to 2020 and annual district-level land use plans in accordance with land laws as a basis for land expropriation, land allocation, land lease or permission for land repurposing to ensure consistency with provincial land use planning and plans.

  4. Take responsibility before the Government and Prime Minister for repurposing forest land to other purposes, limiting the repurposing of forest land and reviewing and inspecting the current use of protection forests, dedicated forests and natural production forests before repurposing land for implementation of works and projects as prescribed; for works and projects using protection forests, dedicated forests or natural production forests that must be reported to the Government or Prime Minister, send reports to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment to take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development in appraising and submitting them to the Prime Minister before deciding on land repurposing as prescribed.

  The People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall direct, inspect and take responsibility before the Government and Prime Minister for thrifty and effective use of land in compliance with regulations of law.

  5. Intensify the dissemination of land laws so that land users can fully grasp the provisions of law, use land for the right purpose, in a thrifty and effective manner, protect the environment and achieve sustainable development.

  6. Manage and use land in accordance with land use planning and plans, especially for areas where land use purpose is to be repurposed for economic development and economic restructuring towards service and urban development to increase revenue for the budget; promote land use right auctions. Encourage thrifty and efficient use of land and remedy the abandonment of allocated and leased land. Strictly manage and supervise land fund for urban development, economic zones, industrial parks, industrial clusters, land for production and business purposes other than agriculture in order to use the land fund thriftily and efficiently and remedy the imbalance in land use structure between residential land and land for construction of technical infrastructure and social infrastructure and environmental protection requirements.

  7. Intensify inspection and examination of land management and use to prevent and promptly handle violations in implementation of land use planning and plans; resolutely refuse to allocate or lease out land, repurpose land or expropriate land for cases not included in land use planning and plans; examine and handle cases where land has been allocated or leased out but not used or used for wrong purposes according to land laws.

  8. Intensify investigation and assessment of land quality and potential; assess land degradation and pollution; improve the land database system; train human resources; study and apply high technology and science to land management, improve the quality of land use prediction, formulation, appraisal, management, organization of implementation and supervision of land use planning and plans.

  9. Enhance forecasting, information and communication to enable local authorities, sectors and people to proactively respond to climate change impacts.

  10. Annually, the People’s Committee of Dak Nong province shall send reports on results of implementation of land use planning and plans to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment for consolidation and reporting to the Government for submission to the National Assembly as prescribed.

  Article 3. The Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in directing and inspecting the conversion of protection forests, special-use forests and production forests into natural forests in accordance with approved planning and laws.

  Article 4. Chairpersons of the People’s Committees of Dak Lak province and heads of relevant ministries and sectors are responsible for implementing this Resolution./.
