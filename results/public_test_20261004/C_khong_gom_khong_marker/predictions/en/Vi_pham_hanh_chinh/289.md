                                             DECREE
           REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF TOURISM
Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
Pursuant to the Law on Tourism dated June 14, 2005;
Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002, and the Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated April 2, 2008;
At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
The Government promulgates a Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on tourism, Chapter 1.
                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, authority to impose penalties, procedures for imposing penalties and remedial measures in tourism sector.
2. Administrative violations in the field of tourism means acts of violating law provisions on state management in the field of tourism committed intentionally or unintentionally by individuals or organizations but not constituting crimes and subject to administrative sanctions under law.
3. Administrative violations against regulations on tourism prescribed in this Decree include:
a/ Violations of regulations on tour business, tourist guide business and automobile business for tourist transport;
b) Violations against regulations on tourism accommodation business;
c) Violations against regulations on tourism promotion;
d) Violations of other regulations on tourism activities.
4. Other administrative violations in the field of tourism and tourist service provision not prescribed in this Decree shall be handled according to other Decrees of the Government on handling of administrative violations in related state management fields.
Article 2. Regulated entities
Vietnamese organizations and individuals and foreign organizations and individuals (hereinafter referred to as “individuals and organizations”) committing administrative violations in the field of tourism within the territory of Vietnam.
Article 3. Forms of sanctions and remedies
1. For each administrative violation, the violating individual or organization shall be subject to one of the following penalties:
a) Warning;
b) Fine.
2. Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, violating individuals or organizations may also be subject to one or more of the following additional sanctioning forms:
a/ Depriving the right to use for a definite term of international tour business licenses, tourist guide badges and/or interpreters’ certificates;
b) Confiscating material evidence and means used for administrative violations.
3. In addition to the main forms of sanction and additional sanctions prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, the violating individuals or organizations may also be subject to one or more of the following remedies:
a) Enforced provision of adequate amenities, facilities, means, and equipment to ensure health, life safety, and property of tourists;
b) Enforced compliance with regulations on preparation and retention of documents as prescribed by law;

c/ Compulsory return to tourists or confiscation to the State funds of illegally collected assets;
d) Enforced full payment of deposit as per regulation;
dd) Enforced compliance with reporting regulations as prescribed by law;
e) Enforced installation of signboards on cars used for tourist services as prescribed by law;
g) Enforced provision of adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign language skills of managers and attendants corresponding to standards applicable to each type and class of accommodation establishment in accordance with regulations of law;
h) Enforced installation of signs indicating tourist accommodation establishment classes in accordance with the classification decision issued by a competent authority;
i) Enforced removal of advertisements, signs, equipment and devices in violation of regulations of law;
k/ Forced dismantlement and destruction of tourist propaganda and promotion articles, termination of tourism promotion programs, for a number of acts of violating regulations on tourism promotion activities;
l) Forced to take measures to overcome the environmental pollution caused by acts of violating regulations on environmental protection;
m) Enforced implementation of competent authorities and persons’ decisions on obstruction of inspection and examination activities and imposition of administrative penalties for violations.
Chapter II
         ADMINISTRATIVE OFFENCES, PENALTIES AND FINES
Section 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON HAND-TO-HAND TRANSPORT, TOURISM GUIDANCE AND AUTOMOBILE TRAFFIC FOR PASSENGER TRANSPORT
Article 4. Violations against regulations on notification of tour operator business activities A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
1. Failure to send written notification of the commencement date of tour operator business to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments of the localities where tour operator enterprises are headquartered within 15 days after such enterprises officially commence their tour operator business.
2. Failure to notify in writing the changes of legal representatives of enterprises, changes of enterprise types, changes of enterprise names or changes of locations of headquarters of tour operator enterprises to the Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism where such enterprises are headquartered within 15 days after the changes take place.
3. Failure to notify in writing the change of operator of international tour operator business to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 30 days from the date on which the enterprise changes the operator of international tour operator business.
4. Failure to notify in writing the establishment and commencement date of a branch or representative office of an tour operator enterprise to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism where such enterprise is headquartered and the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism where such branch or representative office is located within 45 days from the date on which such branch or representative office is established and commences operation.
5. Failure to notify in writing the changes of heads, names or locations of branches or representative offices of tour operator enterprises to the Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism where such enterprises are headquartered or where their branches or representative offices are located within 10 days from the date of such changes.
6. Failing to notify in writing the General Department of Tourism within 7 days after losing the license for international tour business.
7. Failure to promptly notify the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of accidents or risks or incidents that may occur to tourists.
Article 5. Violations against regulations on tour operator business
1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:
a) Using persons who have not worked in the tour operator sector for three years as managers of domestic tour business activities;
b) Employing managers of international tour operator business who have worked in the field for less than four years;
c/ The person who manages domestic tour business does not have at least three years of experience in the field of tour business;

d/ The person who manages international tour business activities has not worked in this field for four years;
dd) The tour contract lacks one of the contents prescribed by law.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) There is no written tour program for tourists or representatives of tourist groups;
b) There is no written tour operator contract with tourists or representatives of tourists as prescribed;
c) Failing to resolve legitimate requests and recommendations of tourists in accordance with the law;
d) Using tour guides to carry out tour programs without having written contracts with tour guides or contracts which do not contain all basic contents as prescribed;
dd) Do not provide or guide information related to tour programs at the request of travel agency clients;
e) Failure to clearly and publicly disclose information on the quantity and prices of tourist services for tourists;
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Persons managing tour operator business use documents certifying working hours not in line with reality to manage tour operator business;
b) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on preparation and retention of documents as prescribed by law;
c) Using tour guides who use expired cards to guide tourists.
4. A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Using means and equipment that fail to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists as prescribed by law, except for cases specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of this Decree;
b) Failing to properly submit reports to the General Department of Tourism and Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism as prescribed;
c/ Employing persons who do not possess the tour guide’s card to guide tourists.
5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a/ Conducting international tour business without at least three international tour guides;
b) Not to buy insurance for Vietnamese tourists going abroad during the period of tourism program as prescribed;
c/ Changing tourist programs, standards or services already signed without the consent of tourists or their representatives;
6. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failure to ensure sufficient collateral for international tour operator business as prescribed;
b/ Erasing and modifying contents of international tour business licenses;
c) Collecting money outside of contract or other acts of illicit gain from tourists;
d) Failing to manage operations and business of branches, representative offices, and affiliated entities of enterprises as prescribed by law;
dd) Failing to manage tourists under signed contracts or tour programs;
e) Declaring untruthful contents in applications for issuance or change of international tour operator licenses;
g) Failing to carry out procedures for changing international tour operator licenses as prescribed by law;
h) Using names of enterprises, transaction names or abbreviations not registered with competent state agencies for business activities;
7. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 25,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Employing foreigners as tour guides in Vietnam;
b) Tour operator business activities after having been notified of suspension, termination or operation suspension by competent authorities.

8. A fine of from VND 25,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with international tour operator license.
9. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 40,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) To permit other organizations and individuals to use international tour operator licenses for tour operator activities;
b/ Conducting international tour business without obtaining an international tour business license.
10. Additional penalties:
a) Revoke the license to operate international tour operator business for up to 12 months in case of commission of the violation specified at Points a and c Clause 5; Points a, d and dd Clause 6 and Point a Clause 7 of this Article;
b) Depriving the right to use international tour operator licenses for between 12 and 24 months, for violations specified in Clause 8 and Point a Clause 9 of this Article;
c) Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations for violations prescribed in point a clause 3 and point e clause 6 of this Article.
11. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate means and equipment to ensure health, life safety, and property of tourists in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced compliance with regulations on preparation and retention of documents as prescribed by law in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;
c) Enforced compliance with reporting regulations as prescribed by law in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 4 of this Article;
d) Enforced payment of full amount of deposit for violations under Point a Clause 6 of this Article;
dd) Enforced return of illegally collected amounts to tourists or transfer them to state budget in case of commission of the violation in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 6 of this Article.
Article 6. Violations against regulations on tour operator business
1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:
a) Failure to notify in writing the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism of the commencement date of tour operator business within 15 days from the date on which the tour operator commences its business;
b) Failure to notify in writing the changes of legal representatives or names and addresses of tour operator within 15 days from the date of change;
c) Failing to clearly and publicly disclose or truthfully disclose the quantity and prices of tourist services provided by tour operator agents to tourists;
d) Operating at a location other than the registered one with competent authorities.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Erasing, modifying or copying the tour program of the tour operator in a misleading manner;
b/ Selling tourist programs at prices higher than those charged by tour operator;
c) Travel agency activities without travel agency business registration;
d) Travel agency business without a written travel agency contract with the travel agency business.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Acting as an agent for tour operator companies that do not have licenses to operate international tour business to sell tour programs to international tourists and Vietnamese tourists traveling abroad;
b) Continue to operate as travel agency after being revoked by competent authority.
4. Remedial measures:
Enforced refund to tourists or transfer to state budget of revenues collected in contravention of regulations for violations prescribed in Point b Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 7. Violations against regulations on tourist attractions
1. A warning or a fine of from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed on tour guides and docents who fail to wear cards or certificates while working.

2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) The tour guide’s presentation is incorrect or inadequate in terms of contents of the tourist attraction or tourist area;
b) Failing to carry tour programs and documents on assignment issued by tour operator companies while providing tour guiding services;
c) Failing to guide tourists to comply with Vietnamese laws, internal rules and regulations of places where they visit for sightseeing and tourism activities, and respect local customs and practices;
d) Failing to fully provide information on tour itinerary and tour program for representatives of tourist groups and tourists.
3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Not having a labor contract with the tour operator;
b) Failing to disseminate and instruct tourists on measures to ensure their life safety, health and property during the tour program;
c) Let other persons use their tour guide cards or interpreter certificates to practice their profession;
d) Using tour guide’s cards or other persons’ certificates of tour guide to practice;
dd) Using expired tour guide’s cards;
e) Declare untruthfully documents and papers in the application for issuance, replacement or re-issuance of tour guide’s cards or interpreters’ certificates.
4. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Erasing and modifying contents of tour guide’s cards or interpreters’ certificates for practice;
b) Using domestic tour guide cards to guide foreign tourists.
c) Giving a tour at tourist attractions without a tour guide certificate.
5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Guiding tourists without a tour guide badge as prescribed;
b) Using fake tour guide cards or fake speaker certificates to practice;
c/ Arbitrarily changing tourist programs, reducing standards and services for tourists;
d) Misleading cultural, historical, national and human values of Vietnam, adversely affecting the image, traditions, ethics and fine customs of Vietnam;
dd) Taking advantage of tour guiding activities to provide explanations or information that affects security, order and social safety;
e) Bringing tourists to prohibited areas;
g) Collecting money outside of contract or other illegal benefits from tourists.
6. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed upon a foreigner who provides tour guiding services in Vietnam.
7. Additional penalties:
a) Revoke the tour guide badge for 12 months in case of commission of the violation in Point d Clause 2; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article;
b) Revoke the tour guide badge for 12 - 24 months in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 3; Points a and b Clause 4 or repetition of the violation specified in Points a, b and c Clause 2; Points a and b Clause 3; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article;
c) Revoke the speaker’s certificate for 12 to 24 months in case of commission of the violation specified at Point c Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
d) Confiscate exhibits used for commission of administrative violations in respect of violations prescribed in Points d, dd and e Clause 3; Point b Clause 5 of this Article.
8. Remedial measures:
Enforced return of illegally collected amounts to tourists or confiscation of such amounts to the state budget in case of commission of the violation in Point c or g Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 8. Violations against regulations on tourist transport business

1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for using an under-9 passenger car without adequate interiors, amenities or equipment as prescribed to transport tourists.
2. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for using an automobile with a capacity of 9 seats to less than 24 seats but the interior, amenities and equipment thereof are not up to standard for transporting tourists.
3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for using a 24-seat or more automobile without adequate interior, amenities and equipment as prescribed for tourist transport vehicles.
4. A fine ranging from VND 4,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using a caravan or a two-storey car with inadequate interior and equipment as prescribed for tourist transport.
5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Changing the transport schedule without the consent of tourists;
b) Failing to install signboards of standard tourist service automobiles as prescribed by law;
c) Using drivers of cars to transport tourists without certificates of training in tourism operations;
d) Employing drivers on tourist buses who do not have certificates of training in tourism operations.
6. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to comply with regulations on reporting to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism or the Department of Transport as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to carry out procedures for re-granting of standard tourist vehicle license plates in accordance with law;
c/ Not buying passenger insurance for tourists by means of transport.
7. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Using fake signboards of standard tourist service automobiles to conduct business activities;
b) Faking tourist transport vehicles.
8. Additional penalties:
Confiscating fake signboards of tourist service standard cars for violations prescribed in Point a Clause 7 of this Article.
9. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate interior, amenities and equipment for tourist transport vehicles to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists in case of violations under Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced installation of a signboard indicating that the vehicle meets standards for tourist services as prescribed by law, applicable to the violations specified in Point c Clause 5 of this Article;
c) Enforced submission of reports as prescribed in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 6 of this Article.
Article 9. Violations against regulations on establishment and operation of branches and representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam
Violations of regulations on establishment and operation of branches or representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam shall be sanctioned according to Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 13; Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 14 of the Government’s Decree No. 06/2008/ND-CP dated January 16, 2008 prescribing sanctions against administrative violations in the field of trade.
Section 2. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON TRADE IN TOURIST ACCOMMODATIONS
Article 10. Violations against regulations on tourist accommodation business
1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:
a) Failure to notify in writing the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the tourist accommodation establishment is located of the commencement date of business operation within 15 days from the date on which the tourist accommodation establishment officially commences its business operation;
b) Failure to notify the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism in writing of changes to the head of the tourist accommodation establishment or the name of the establishment within 15 days from the date of such change;

c) Failure to notify in writing the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism or the Department of Information and Communications within 15 days from the date of implementation of the telecommunication surcharge rate applied at the rated tourist accommodation establishment.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts of violation:
a) Failing to comply with regulations on name and classification of tourist accommodation establishments;
b) Failure to install the classification sign after it has been classified by the General Department of Tourism, the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
c) Failing to promulgate and post internal regulations or rules of tourist accommodation establishments;
d/ Failure to clearly and publicly inform tourists of the quantity, quality and price of services and goods;
dd) Do not hang decision on recognition of tourist accommodation establishment class at a visible position at reception desk.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on preparation and retention of records and documents on business activities of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
b) The number of sleeping rooms or area of sleeping rooms does not meet corresponding standards for each type and class of accommodation establishment as prescribed by law;
c) Failure to meet standards of parking and internal traffic, reception hall area as prescribed by law;
d) Failure to ensure the number and standards of restaurants and bars as prescribed by law;
dd) Failing to meet the standards of kitchens as prescribed by law;
e) Failure to ensure the number and standards of conference rooms and seminar rooms as prescribed by law;
g) Failing to ensure standards of equipment and other conveniences as prescribed by law;
h) Failing to meet service standards as prescribed by law;
i) Failing to ensure professional qualifications and foreign language proficiency of managers and service staff in accordance with law.
4. A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Using means and equipment that do not ensure health, life safety, and property of tourists as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to comply with regulations on reporting to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism as prescribed by law;
c) Failure to provide adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign language skills of managers and staff in conformity with the ranking standards as required by tourism authorities.
5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to register with the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments for appraisal and ranking of tourist accommodation establishments after three months from the date on which such establishments officially commence business activities;
b) Failing to register with the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments for appraisal and re-certification of tourist accommodation establishment classes three months before the expiry of the certification of tourist accommodation establishment classes;
c) Using star images affixed to tourist accommodation establishments or on publications, advertising boards, signboards, equipment and devices used at tourist accommodation establishments when such establishments have not been granted recognition of classes or with incorrect classes.
6. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Using names of tourist accommodation establishments, transaction names or abbreviated names not conformable with names of tourist accommodation establishments registered with competent authorities for business activities;
b) Collecting money outside of contract or other acts of illicit gain from tourists;
c) Collecting service charges in contravention of regulations.
7. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed for providing tourist accommodation services after being subject to a decision on temporary suspension or termination of operation or suspension of operation of the tourist accommodation establishment by a competent authority.
8. Regulations from clause 1 through clause 6 of this Article shall also apply to guest houses and hostels of ministries, central authorities, and local governments that conduct business in tourist accommodation establishments.

9. Additional penalties:
Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations for violations prescribed in Point a Clause 4 and Clause 8 of this Article.
10. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign language skills of managers and attendants corresponding to standards applicable to each category and class of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law, for violations specified in Points b, c, d, dd, e, g, h and i Clause 3; Point c Clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced provision of adequate means and equipment to ensure health, life safety, and property of tourists in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
c) Enforced return of illegally collected amounts to tourists or transfer them to state budget in case of commission of the violation in Point b or Point c Clause 6 of this Article;
d) Enforced destruction of publications, billboards, signboards, equipment and devices in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 2 or Point c Clause 5 of this Article;
dd) Enforced installation of the classification signboard in accordance with the decision on classification issued by a competent authority, for violations specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 11. Violations against regulations on sale of goods and provision of conditional services in tourist accommodation establishments
A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to register in writing with competent authorities when providing conditional services or goods in a rated tourist accommodation establishment.
Section 3. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON TRAVEL ADVISORSHIP
Article 12. Violations against regulations on tourism promotion
1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:
a) Contents of tourist propaganda and promotion products do not conform to the announced national tourism database;
b) Failing to implement or improperly implementing tourism promotion programs that have been informed and advertised;
c) Promoting tourism products not in line with actual contents and quality;
d) Using products for tourism communication and promotion without or with incorrect general title and symbol prescribed by the General Department of Tourism.
2. A fine ranging from VND 7.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of tourism promotion activities to harm lawful rights and interests of other organizations or individuals.
3. Remedial measures:
Enforced dismantling and destruction of tourist propaganda and promotion items, termination of tourism promotion programs, for violations specified in Points a, c and d Clause 1 of this Article.
Section 4. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON OTHER TOURISM ACTIVITIES
Article 13. Violations against regulations on tourism resources and tourism development planning A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for exploiting tourism resources in a manner that is contrary to law within a tourist area or tourist attraction or a tourist development planning area.
Article 14. Violations against regulations on tourism environmental protection
1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to post environmental protection regulations at easily visible places in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions or on tourist transport means.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) There are no public sanitation works meeting environmental sanitation maintenance requirements in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, and tourist transport means as prescribed;
b) Failing to classify waste in accordance with regulations at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, and on tourist transport vehicles;
c) Do not assign officers to monitor and manage tourism environmental protection work corresponding to each type and class of tourist accommodation establishments in accordance with law.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:

a) Failure to collect and treat waste in accordance with regulations at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, and tourist transport vehicles as prescribed;
b/ Infringing upon the living environment of wild animals in areas where tourist resources exist;
c) Cutting down, breaking branches or committing other acts that damage trees and vegetation in tourist resources areas.
4. A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to submit annual reports on tourism environmental protection work as prescribed by law;
b) Failure to arrange waste collection and treatment facilities and equipment meeting environmental sanitation requirements at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist transport vehicles, and tourist areas or attractions.
5. Additional penalties:
Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations in case of commission of the violations specified in Points b and c Clause 3 of this Article.
6. Remedial measures:
Forcible application of measures to overcome environmental pollution caused by violations at Points a and b, Clause 2; Point a, Clause 3 and Point b, Clause 4 of this Article.
Article 15. Violations against regulations on management and business activities in tourist areas and tourist attractions A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
1. Do not promulgate regulations on management of tourist areas and tourist attractions;
2. Promulgate rules and regulations of tourist areas or tourist attractions in contravention of law;
3. Violations of regulations on tourist areas or tourist attractions.
Article 16. Penalties for obstruction of inspection and examination and administrative penalties
1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for refusing to accept inspection decisions or enforcement decisions on execution of administrative sanctioning decisions.
2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to declare or declaring not truthfully or within the prescribed time limit at the request of law enforcement officers or competent state management agencies;
b) Failing to provide or insufficiently providing information and documents related to inspection, examination, and sanctioning of administrative violations by law enforcement officers and competent authorities;
c) Obstructing the work of inspection or examination teams or persons assigned to conduct inspection or examination;
d) Using words or actions to threaten, insult, or harm the honor of the person on duty.
3. A fine of between VND 7,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Delaying or avoiding the execution of administrative decisions and inspection decisions of competent persons or agencies;
b) Arbitrarily removing the seals on exhibits and means of violation or arbitrarily altering the scene of administrative violation;
c) Dispersing, altering or swapping exhibits under inspection, examination or temporary seizure;
d) Attacking people on duty.
4. Additional penalties:
Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations in respect of violations prescribed in Points b and c Clause 3 of this Article.
5. Remedial measures:
Enforced implementation of competent authorities’ and individuals’ decisions regarding violations under Clause 1; Points a and b Clause 2 and Point a Clause 3 of this Article.
Chapter III
                     POWER TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES
Article 17. Authority to impose administrative penalties of Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

Presidents of People’s Committees at all levels shall have the power to impose penalties within their competence prescribed in Clauses 4 and 5 Article 1 of the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2008, Article 30 of the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2002 amended by the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2007 for administrative violations specified in this Decree within the management scope of local governments.
Article 18. Authority to impose penalties for administrative violations of inspectors of Culture, Sports and Tourism
1. Inspectors of Culture, Sports and Tourism who are on duty have the jurisdiction:
a) Warning;
b) A fine of up to VND 500,000;
c) Confiscating material evidence and means used for committing administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;
d) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Points a, b, d, dd, e, g, h, i, k, l and m Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
dd) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
2. Chief Inspector of the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism has the jurisdiction:
a) Warning;
b) A fine of up to VND 30,000,000;
c) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;
d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for administrative violations;
dd) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
3. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism has the authority:
a) Warning;
b) A fine of up to the maximum amount in the tourism sector;
c) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;
d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for administrative violations;
dd) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
Article 19. Power to impose penalties of People’s Public Security forces, Border Guard forces and Coast Guard forces
The People's Public Security, Border Guard and Coast Guard have the right to impose penalties within their competence prescribed in Clauses 6, 7 and 8 Article 1 of the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2008 and Article 37 of the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2002 for administrative violations in the field of tourism directly related to their management as prescribed in this Decree.
Article 20.- Sanctioning competence of inspectors of other branches
Within the scope of state management prescribed by the Government, inspectors and chief inspectors of other inspection agencies shall have the authority to impose penalties for administrative violations in the tourism sector prescribed in this Decree under their respective state management.
Article 21. Issuance of record forms and decision forms for use in administrative penalties Issued together with this Decree are record forms and decision forms for use in administrative penalties for violations against regulations on tourism.
Chapter IV
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 22. Effect
1. This Decree takes effect on April 30, 2012.
2. This Decree replaces the Government's Decree No. 149/2007/ND-CP of October 9, 2007 on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of tourism.
Article 23. Implementation responsibilities
1. The Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall be responsible for organizing the implementation of this Decree.

2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.-
