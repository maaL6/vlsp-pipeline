                                                   DECREE

     PROVISIONS ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY; IMPLEMENTATION OF EXPENDITURES AND ANTI-SUBSIDIES; NATIONAL RESERVES; STATE DEPOSITS

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015; and the Law on Actions against administrative violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Law on Management and Use of Public Property dated June 21, 2017;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on thrift practice and wastefulness combat dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the National Reserves Law dated November 20, 2012;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates a Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury.

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, remedial measures, authority to record administrative violations and authority to impose penalties for administrative violations in the following fields:

  a) Manage and use public property at agencies, organizations and units; state-owned project property; property under establishment of public ownership and infrastructure property invested in and managed by the State;

  b) Practicing thrift and combating wastefulness;

  c) National reserve;

  d) State Treasury.

  2. Administrative violations related to management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury in terms of accounting, construction, investment planning, valuation, auction and other regulations not specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be sanctioned according to relevant law provisions.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrift practice against wastefulness; national reserve; state treasury.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) Regulatory agencies;

  b) Units of the People’s Armed Forces;

  c) Public service providers;

  d) The Communist Party of Vietnam;

  dd) Socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations and other organizations established under the law on associations;

  e) Other relevant enterprises, organizations and individuals.

  3. Persons having authority to impose penalties and organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree.

  4. If an official or public employee commits an administrative violation while performing his/her official duties and such violation is included in his/her assigned official duties prescribed in a legislative document or an administrative document issued by a competent authority or person, he/she shall not be administratively sanctioned according to this Decree but handled according to regulations of law on officials, public employees.

  State agencies committing violations within their state management tasks shall not be administratively sanctioned in accordance with this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws.

  Article 3. Prescriptive periods for administrative violations

  The prescriptive period for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury prescribed in this Decree is 01 year. For administrative violations involving public property being houses, land and infrastructure assets invested and managed by the State, the prescriptive period shall be 02 years.

  Article 4. Forms of administrative penalties and remedial measures

  1. Main forms of sanction:

  a) The main forms of sanction prescribed in this Decree include: Warning and fine;

  b) The maximum fine for violations against regulations on management and use of public property, national reserves or state treasury shall be VND 50,000,000 if the violation is committed by an individual or VND 100,000,000 if the violation is committed by an organization; the maximum fine for violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat shall be VND 100,000,000 if the violation is committed by an individual or VND 200,000,000 if the violation is committed by an organization.

  2. Additional penalties:

  Depending on the nature and severity of violations, violating organizations or individuals may also be subject to additional penalties in the form of confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit administrative violations.

  3. Remedial measures in the field of management and use of public property:

  Depending on the acts, nature and seriousness of violations, violating organizations or individuals shall also be subject to one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Enforced return of an amount equivalent to the value of the property;

  b) Enforced payment of amounts incurred from administrative violations;

  c) Enforced return of property; in case where the property has been changed due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original state; in case where the original state of the property cannot be restored, it must be compensated in cash or other property with similar functions and use value;

  d) Enforced demolition of construction works built on encroached areas of working offices or public service facilities;

  dd) Enforced cancellation of false or fraudulent declarations in case of commission of the violation against regulations on use of data on public property;

  e) Enforced adjustment and supplementation of data, information, and reports on additional declaration of public property.

  4. Remedies in the field of thrift practice and wastefulness combat:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, violating organizations or individuals may also be subject to one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Revoke and transfer to state budget funds used for purposes other than those prescribed by competent authorities if funds used for purposes other than those prescribed by competent authorities are used;

  b) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by using funds originating from state budget to state budget if such funds are used inconsistently with operating regulations and financial mechanisms of the funds issued by competent authorities.

  5. Remedial measures in the field of national reserves:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, violating organizations or individuals may also be subject to one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing violations against regulations on purchase, sale, preservation, distribution and assistance; violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods; violations against regulations on management of capital and fees in national reserve activities; violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease and extraction of assets included in national reserves;

  b) Enforced restoration of the initial state of facilities, technical and physical conditions, and storage of national reserve goods;

  c) Enforced return of damaged national reserve goods caused by acts of infringement or destruction; violations against regulations on provision and assistance of national reserve goods;

  d) Enforced recall of national reserve products which have been issued against regulations;

  dd) Compulsory restoration of the original state of assets in case of illegal trading, pledge, lease or exploitation of assets being national reserve warehouses.

  6. Remedial measures in the field of state treasury:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of the violations, the violating organizations may be subject to one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Enforced revocation of payments made for the unfinished work; unfinished work not eligible for payment and paid amounts exceeding the contract value or appendix;

  b) Enforced recovery of funds for amounts already spent on the preparation of documents and records in violation of regulations and amounts spent on the preparation of documents and records not consistent with the contents of the original documents and records at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced recovery of all amounts spent on the use of fake documents and records for state budget expenditures, if not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Enforced completion of documents and vouchers in accordance with regulations on payment of state budget expenditures;

  dd) Forced to follow procedures for commitment before requesting the State Treasury to pay or transfer the state budget or forced to follow procedures for commitment again in case of violation of procedures for control of commitment spending;

  e) Forced to carry out procedures for payment of advances in case of violations against procedures and time limit for advance payment of state budget;

  g) Forcible freezing or closing of accounts in case of violations against regulations on registration and use of accounts at State Treasuries; forcible freezing of accounts in case of making false dossiers and papers to register the use of accounts at State Treasuries but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  7. Organizations and individuals subject to administrative penalties under this Decree are not allowed to use state budget or state budget-derived funds for paying fines and remedying consequences caused by their violations. In case of imposing penalties on organizations, after serving the penalty decisions, the sanctioned organizations shall identify individuals at fault for committing administrative violations to determine legal responsibilities, including the return of fines and remedying consequences caused by their violations corresponding to the degree of violation of such individuals in accordance with law and Regulations on management and use of public property of the organizations.

  Article 5. Application of fines

  The fines prescribed in Items 1, 2, 3 and 4 of Chapter II, Item 1 of Chapter III, Item 1 of Chapter IV, Items 1 and 2 of Chapter V of this Decree are the fines for administrative violations committed by organizations; for the same administrative violation, the fine imposed on an individual shall be half of that imposed on an organization (except for the provisions of Articles 17, 23 and 27 of this Decree).

  Chapter II

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT AND USE

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS AND FINES FOR ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC PROPERTY AT AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND EMPLOYEES

  Article 6. Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets without a decision of a competent authority or person:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if investing in or purchasing assets being machinery, equipment, or other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a total purchase value of less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 in case of investing or purchasing assets being machinery, equipment and other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a total purchase value of VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) VND 20,000,000 - VND 50,000,000 in case of investing or purchasing assets being working offices, public service facilities, and cars.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to conduct centralized procurement of assets on the list of assets subject to centralized procurement in accordance with laws on management and use of public assets.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets beyond the standards and limits prescribed by competent authorities:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if the value of assets invested or purchased is less than VND 50,000,000 per asset;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds VND 50,000,000 but is less than VND 100,000,000 per asset;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the invested or purchased assets are valued at ≥ VND 100,000,000 per asset.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of an amount equivalent to the value of the assets invested or purchased in excess of the prescribed standards and limits for the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. The determination of the value in excess of the standard or limit as a basis for sanction shall be prescribed as follows:

  a) In case of investing in or purchasing assets being automobiles, machinery, equipment and other assets in excess of the prescribed quantity: The excess value per unit of asset is determined by the unit price of such asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or the asset purchase contract;

  b) In case of investment or procurement of assets being automobiles, machinery, equipment and other assets in quantity but exceeding the price level compared to standards and norms: The excess value is determined by subtracting (-) the asset value according to standards and norms prescribed by competent state agencies from the purchase value recorded on the asset procurement invoice or contract;

  c) In case of investment or procurement of working offices or public service facilities in excess of standards and norms: The excess value is determined by subtracting (-) the actual area from the area used according to standards and norms multiplied by (x) the average purchase unit price according to invoices (or procurement contracts);

  d) The persons having authority to impose penalties specified in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the value exceeding as the basis for imposing penalties according to regulations of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 7. Administrative violations against regulations on leasing property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on leasing property if value of lease contract is less than VND 100.000.000, in the following cases:

  a) The act of leasing property without a decision on property lease issued by a competent authority or person;

  b) The act of renting property in excess of the standards and limits prescribed by competent state authorities;

  c) Acts of selecting property lease service providers in improper forms and procedures as prescribed by laws on management and use of public property and relevant laws.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for committing a violation against regulations on property lease specified in Clause 1 of this Article if the value of the property lease contract is VND 100,000,000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of the amount of rent collected in excess of the prescribed standards and norms for the acts specified at Point b, Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The value of the property lease contract used as a basis for imposing penalties shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case the property lease contract specifies the value of the contract, the basis for sanction shall be the value specified in the contract;

  b) In case of leasing property without entering into a contract or with insufficient information to determine the value as prescribed in Clause a of this Point, the value used as a basis for imposing penalties shall be determined by the lease price of similar property or property with equivalent standards and technical specifications on the market at the time of committing the violation.

  Persons having authority to impose penalties specified in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the value serving as a basis for imposing penalties according to regulations of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 8. Administrative violations against regulations on allocation and use of public property, destruction or intentional damage to public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of allocating public property in excess of the standards and norms issued by competent authorities or persons (incorrectly allocated to the wrong entities, exceeding the area, quantity, or price):

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 in case of delivery and use of assets valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the property is valued at from over VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the property is valued at over VND 100,000,000.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of misusing public property (using public property for purposes or functions other than those intended; using working offices or service facilities as residential houses or for personal use; using cars to transport from residence to workplace for unqualified positions; using cars for business trips by unqualified positions without approval by competent authorities; using cars for personal use; using machinery, equipment, and other property for personal use):

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 in case of delivery and use of assets being machinery, equipment, or other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a book value of less than VND 100,000,000 per asset unit (hereinafter referred to as “assets valued at less than VND 100,000,000”);

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 in case of delivery and use of assets being machinery, equipment, or other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a book value of VND 100,000,000 or more per asset unit (hereinafter referred to as “assets with a value of VND 100,000,000 or more”);

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 in case of delivery and use of assets being working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for destroying or intentionally damaging public property if not liable to criminal prosecution:

a) A fine of 1,000 - 10,000 VND;

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 in case of intentional damage to assets being machinery, equipment or other assets with book value of less than VND 100,000,000 per asset unit;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentional damage to assets being machinery, equipment or other assets with a book value of VND 100.000.000 or more per asset unit;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the property is a working office, public service facility or car.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced payment of an amount equal to the value of assets in excess of the prescribed standards and norms for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original status of property altered by administrative violations; in case of failure to restore the original status of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value to the original property shall be provided for the violations prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. The value in excess of the prescribed standards and norms shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case of delivery and use of assets being automobiles, machinery, equipment or other assets in excess of the prescribed quantity: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the excess quantity by the original cost of the asset recorded in the accounting book; in case the original cost of the asset is not recorded in the accounting book, it shall be determined according to the purchase price of a new asset of the same type or an asset meeting the prescribed technical standards and having similar uses on the market at the time of commission of the violation;

  b) In case of delivery and use of assets being automobiles, machinery, equipment or other assets in quantity but exceeding the price level compared with standards and norms: The excess value is determined by multiplying the excess quantity by (x) (the difference between the original cost of the asset on the accounting book minus (-) the value of the asset according to standards and norms prescribed by competent state agencies);

  c) In case the asset is a working office or public service facility: The excess value shall be determined by subtracting (-) the actual area from the area used according to standards and norms multiplied by (x) the average unit price recorded in the accounting book; in case it is not recorded in the accounting book, the unit price shall be calculated according to the investment rate of a house or work with equivalent technical standards issued by the Ministry of Construction applicable at the time of commission of the violation;

  d) The persons having authority to impose penalties specified in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the value exceeding as the basis for imposing penalties according to regulations of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 9. Administrative violations against regulations on lending public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for lending or using public property in contravention of regulations (lending or using public property to other organizations or individuals regardless of whether there is a loan contract or not and regardless of the loan term):

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 in case of lending assets valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 in case of lending assets valued at ≥ VND 100,000,000;

  c) VND 50,000,000 - VND 60,000,000 in case of lending assets being working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  2. Remedial measures:

  An organization committing the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the remedial measures as follows:

  a) Enforced return of borrowed property. In case where the property has been changed due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original state; in case where the original state cannot be restored, it must be returned in cash or property with similar functions and use value to the original property;

  b) Enforced transfer of the amount corresponding to the asset rental during the loan period to state budget. The determination of the asset rental for the purpose of determining the fine shall comply with Clause 4 Article 7 hereof.

  Article 10. Administrative violations against regulations on public property transfer and gifting

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of exchanging public property in contravention of regulations (using public property of an organization to exchange for property of another organization or individual without permission from a competent authority or person):

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if the property used for exchange is valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the property used for exchange is valued at ≥ VND 100,000,000;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the property to be exchanged is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for gifting public property in contravention of regulations (using public property as gifts in violation of Regulations on gift gifting promulgated by competent authorities).

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of property or monetary compensation for the value of property exchanged or given in case of commission of the violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 11. Administrative violations against regulations on encroaching on working offices and public service facilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for encroaching on the working office or public service facility (an organization/individual that is using adjacent land and relocates the boundary of a plot to expand the land area or builds an encroaching work within the land area of the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit; or an organization/individual that uses the house/land belonging to the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit without permission).

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Organizations and individuals committing violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the remedial measures as follows:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state of the office or public service facility and return of the office or public service facility that is encroached upon;

  b) Enforced demolition of construction works built on encroached land;

  c) Enforced transfer of the amount corresponding to the rent for the property during the period of encroachment into the state budget. The determination of the amount of rent for the property used as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 12. Acts of expropriating public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of appropriating public property which is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability (holding or using public property without permission of competent state agencies):

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 for public property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 for public property assessed at ≥ VND 100,000,000;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the property is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. Additional penalties: Confiscate the exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures: Organizations and individuals committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state of property altered by administrative violations in order to return it to the organization; in case of failure to restore the original state of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value must be provided;

  b) Enforced transfer of the amount corresponding to the property rent during the period of appropriation to state budget. The determination of the property rent as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with Clause 4 Article 7 hereof.

  Article 13. Administrative violations against regulations on use of public property for business, lease, joint venture, or association purposes

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes without a decision on approval of the Scheme issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with the law on management and use of public property at the time of commission of the violation:

a) A fine equal to 100% of the value of property used for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes;

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if the assets are valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the property is valued at ≥ VND 100,000,000;

  c) VND 15,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the assets are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on determination of value of assets for business purposes, lease, joint venture or association purposes:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to have a prescribed number of members in a Council for valuation or a Council for appraisal of property prices;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to follow procedures for selecting a valuation organization for determining the value of assets used for business purposes, lease, joint venture or association purposes.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes against the approved scheme by competent authorities or persons, thereby affecting the performance of functions and tasks assigned by the State:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if the assets are valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of using property being automobiles or property assessed at ≥ VND 100,000,000;

  c) VND 15,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the assets are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state of property altered by the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original state of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value must be provided;

  b) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from illegal activities to government budget, applicable to the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Administrative violations against regulations on failure to maintain and repair property as prescribed by law

  1. Organizations assigned by competent authorities to estimate the maintenance and repair of assets in accordance with law but fail to maintain or repair the assets, leading to damage, shall be sanctioned at the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more and is a working office, public service facility or car but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. The amount of money to be refunded corresponding to the value of damaged property shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case of irreparable damage to assets, the amount to be refunded shall be determined by multiplying the price of a new asset of the same type or with standards and uses similar to those on the market by the remaining quality percentage at the time of value determination;

  b/ For damaged assets which can be repaired, the amount to be returned is the repair cost;

  c/ The head of the financial agency under his/her management shall act as the focal point for determining the payable amount, and on the basis of this amount, the head of the organization shall decide the payable amount and take responsibility for his/her decision.

  Article 15. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare plans for disposition of public property in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to declare and prepare plans for disposition of public property within the prescribed time limit;

  c) Failing to report to competent state authorities for repossession, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of assets when conditions are met in accordance with law (except where public assets have expired according to law regulations but remain usable and agencies, organizations, units or enterprises still wish to use them, in which case heads of agencies, organizations, units shall decide on appropriate disposal methods).

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed on organizations for damaging or losing assets while waiting for handling.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for handling assets without a decision of a competent authority or person:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 in case of selling, transferring, liquidating or destroying assets valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the property is worth VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 in case of selling, transferring or liquidating assets being working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. The following penalties shall be imposed for declaring incorrect information about the list of assets and their current conditions (which may lead to a decision on handling of assets against regulations):

  a) A warning shall be imposed in case of declaring assets valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring assets worth VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring assets worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring as assets working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state of property altered by the acts specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original state of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value must be provided;

  b) Enforced return of an amount equivalent to the value of the lost or damaged property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  6. The amount of money to be refunded corresponding to the value of lost or damaged property specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case of lost or damaged assets that cannot be repaired, the amount to be returned shall be determined according to the price of new assets of the same type or with similar standards and use at the time when the violation occurs.

  Market prices may be based on quoted prices of suppliers posted or announced on the market or from official information disclosed by suppliers via the Internet.

  b/ For damaged assets which can be repaired, the amount to be returned is the repair cost thereof.

  Article 16. Administrative violations against regulations on organization of handling of public property

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to implement the approved plan for handling waste within the prescribed time limit.

  The time limit for handling an asset shall be determined according to the time limit specified in the decision on handling of the asset. If the time limit is not specified in the decision on handling of the asset, relevant laws shall apply. If the plan for extending the handling of the asset is approved by a competent authority, the time limit shall be determined according to the extended time limit.

  b) Failing to hand over assets subject to transfer or recovery decisions to receiving agencies within the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Establishing the Pricing Council ultra vires as prescribed by law;

  b) Establishing the Pricing Council with improper composition as prescribed by law;

  c) Selecting a valuation organization or a professional auction organization to handle assets in a manner that does not comply with the prescribed form and procedures;

  d) Failing to fully notify the public of the auction of public property in accordance with the law on management and use of public property (failing to post up information about the auction of public property at prescribed locations; failing to make public announcements on mass media or making public announcements but not for a sufficient period of time as required by the law on property auction; failing to adequately disclose information as required by the law on property auction).

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to establish a Pricing Council in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to publicly notify the sale of public property as prescribed in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  4.   a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for loss of national reserve goods used for distribution, relief or performance of other tasks:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if the value of the national reserve goods is less than VND 50,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the value of the national reserve goods is from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for distributing national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 to ineligible entities according to a competent authority’s decision.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for delayed or untimely provision of national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100.000.000 in accordance with regulations of competent authorities.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for delaying or procrastinating the distribution of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 under decisions of competent authorities, thus causing damage to national reserve goods.

  8. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clauses 3, 5 and 6 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of lost national reserve goods, goods used for wrong purposes or goods issued to wrong recipients in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  c) Enforced return of damaged, inferior or devalued national reserve goods and insufficient quantity of national reserve goods in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 45. Violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of the import, export and transportation of national reserve goods with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 2,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to implement or improperly implementing procedures for import and export of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 as prescribed;

  b) Failing to deliver goods in the correct quantity, type, specifications, standards and quality of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 under decisions of competent state agencies.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 not in conformity with the type, specifications, quantity, quality, time and place specified in the decision of a competent authority.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 without a decision issued by a competent authority.

  5. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing administrative violations prescribed in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;

  b) Enforced recall of national reserve products that have been issued against regulations if the violation prescribed in Clause 2, Clause 3 or Clause 4 of this Article is committed.

  Article 46. Violations against regulations on receipt of national reserve goods but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 2,000,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Receiving national reserve goods not on schedule or at locations specified in decisions of competent authorities;

  b) Failing to implement or improperly implementing regulations on procedures and order for receipt of national reserve goods.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to receive national reserve goods (national reserve goods exported for aid or execution of other tasks by competent authorities) when all conditions have been met in accordance with regulations of competent authorities.

  Article 47. Illegal intervention in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or fine shall be imposed for illegal intervention in national reserve activities, with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 48. Violations against regulations on management of capital and fees in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to report on the use of funds derived from the national reserve capital for purchase of national reserve products as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Payment shall be made when there is no contract for purchase and sale of national reserve goods;

  b) Make payment when national reserve goods are not in quantity or have insufficient invoices and documents;

  c) Make payment without a contract liquidation record or receipt of goods from warehouse with signature of the unit’s head in case of direct purchase not through bidding;

  d) Payment shall be made when the Head of the national reserve unit does not approve it;

  dd) Using import fees, export fees and storage fees for national reserve goods in contravention of prescribed contents or exceeding prescribed limits.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Using national reserve capital for wrong purposes;

  b) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on management of money derived from national reserve funds;

  c) Failing to pay the remaining amount after completing the purchase of national reserve goods in accordance with regulations of competent state agencies.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of profits earned from illegal activities to government budget in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 49. Violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease and extraction of assets included in national reserves

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets included in national reserves (except from storage of national reserve products):

  a) VND 3,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if the value of the property is less than VND 70,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the value of the property is VND 70,000,000 - VND 150,000,000;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the value of the property is from VND 150,000,000 to under VND 300,000,000;

  d) VND 20,000,000 - VND 30,000,000 if the value of the property is from VND 300,000,000 to under VND 500,000,000;

  dd) VND 30,000,000 - VND 40,000,000 if the property is worth VND 500,000,000 or more.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets being national reserve warehouses:

  a) VND 20,000,000 - VND 30,000,000 if the value of the national reserve warehouse is less than VND 500,000,000;

  b) VND 30,000,000 - VND 40,000,000 if the storage facility is used to store national reserve goods assessed at ≥ VND 500,000,000.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of property to its original conditions, for the acts specified in clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from illegal activities to government budget, applicable to the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. AUTHORITY TO MAKE RECORD AND IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF NATIONAL RESERVATION

  Article 50. Authority to make records of administrative violations against regulations on national reserves

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations in the field of national reserves include:

  a) Persons having authority to impose administrative penalties for violations against regulations on national reserves prescribed in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree;

  b) Officials assigned to inspect compliance with laws on national reserves.

  2. Persons competent to make records specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall issue sanctioning decisions according to their competence or transfer to competent authorities specified in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree to issue sanctioning decisions.

  Article 51. Authority to impose penalties of Chairpersons of People’s Committees

  1. The chairpersons of the district-level People’s Committees have the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The President of the People’s Committee of a province shall have the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscating exhibits and means used in administrative violations;

  d) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 52. Authority to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Inspectors and persons assigned to perform specialized inspections in the field of national reserves who are on duty shall have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscating exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause.

  2. Chief Inspectors of provincial Departments, heads of inspectorates at provincial level shall perform their functions as specialized inspectors in the field of national reserves, heads of specialized inspectorates established by decisions of Director General of State Reserves Administration and heads of specialized inspectorates established by decisions of Directors of Regional State Reserves Departments shall have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of ministerial-level specialized inspection teams performing the function of specialized inspection in the field of national reserves shall have the following powers:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry has the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscating exhibits and means used in administrative violations;

  d) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 53. Authority to impose penalties of Director of Regional State Reserve Department, Director General of State Reserve Administration

  1. Director of Regional State Reserve Department shall perform specialized inspection in national reserve field and have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Director General of State Reserve Administration shall perform the specialized inspection in the field of national reserve and has the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  c) Confiscating exhibits and means used in administrative violations;

  d) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter V

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN THE DOMESTIC CUSTODY SECTOR

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS OF STATE FINANCE

  Article 54. Violations against regulations on state budget expenditures included in state budget estimates assigned by competent authorities

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a/ To compile dossiers and vouchers for submission to the State Treasury for payment of contents and tasks not included in the estimates approved by competent authorities;

  b/ To compile dossiers and vouchers for submission to the State Treasury for disbursement of amounts in excess of the annual capital estimates or plans allocated by competent authorities or the approved expenditure estimates (for non-contracted jobs);

  c) Prepare documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for disbursement of expenditures not in accordance with the estimates allocated by competent authorities (expenditures not from the approved budget; expenditures not for the right purposes or for the right entities or contents as per the approved budget); expenditures not from the investment capital or expenditures not on the list of projects approved by competent authorities.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 6,000,000 for one of the following acts of violation:

  a) Prepare documents and send them to the State Treasury for payment for the amount of work not yet done;

  b) Prepare documents and send them to the State Treasury for payment for unfinished work or work not eligible for payment;

  c/ Compiling dossiers and documents for submission to the State Treasury for payment of an amount exceeding the contract value or the contract appendix.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of amounts paid for the unfinished work; unfinished work not eligible for payment and paid amounts exceeding contract value or appendix under Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 55. Acts of preparing documents and records in violation of regulations and acts of preparing documents and records inconsistently with original documents and records at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of signing contracts before having capital plans approved by competent authorities (except for construction works under emergency orders).

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 6,000,000 for one of the following acts of violation:

  a) Prepare documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for disbursement of expenditures not in compliance with criteria regarding title and eligible beneficiaries;

  b/ To compile dossiers and vouchers for submission to the State Treasury for disbursement of expenditures in excess of the prescribed levels in quantity or value;

  c/ Compiling dossiers and vouchers for sending to the State Treasury for disbursement of expenses in violation of regulations (expenses not meeting conditions and rules of the regime).

  In case an organization buys or leases state assets beyond the standards and norms prescribed by competent state agencies, it shall be sanctioned in accordance with Articles 5 and 6 of this Decree.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records sent to the State Treasury which do not accurately reflect contents of the original documents or invoices at the unit, if not liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of expenses incurred from preparation of documents and records against regulations and expenses incurred from preparation of documents and records not consistent with the original documents and records at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 56. Acts of falsifying documents and records to spend state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing false documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of regular and professional expenses, recurrent programs, or recurrent programs using professional funding (excluding expenses for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrade, expansion of facilities funded by regular state budget expenditures and retained fees under regulations for regular use with total investment exceeding VND 500.000.000).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing false documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of capital for investment funded by state budget and state budget-funded investment in execution of target programs or for expenditure on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of material facilities using regular state budget expenditures and retained fees under regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500.000.000.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recovery of all amounts spent on the use of fake documents and records for state budget expenditures, which, however, is not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article.

  Article 57. Violations against regulations on payment of state budget expenditures

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records requesting the State Treasury to transfer funds to an account of a recipient other than the correct name or account number specified in the contract or appendix thereto.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records requesting payment of state budget expenditures in a manner inconsistent with payment terms specified in contracts or appendixes to contracts between the estimate unit or project investor and goods or service providers, including any of the following violations:

  a) Return the contract value;

  b) Returning the payment deadline;

  c) Returning payment methods;

  d) Returning the payment rate (including advance payment);

  dd) Errors in other payment terms specified in the contract and its appendices.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced completion of documents and records in accordance with regulations for the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 58. Violations against procedures for controlling expenditure on commitments

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to send commitment on state budget expenditures to State Treasuries for expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  b) Send the request for commitment of state budget expenditures to the State Treasury after the prescribed deadline for expenditures under the scope of commitment as prescribed;

  c) Send a request for commitment of expenditure in excess of the annual investment capital plan, the balance of the annual estimate which is still permitted for use or exceeding the value of the contract which is still permitted for commitment of expenditure.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced adoption of advance payment procedures before requesting the State Treasury to make payments or disburse funds for state budget in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced re-performance of expenditure commitment procedures for the acts specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 59. Violations against procedures and time limits for payment of advances to state budget

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Carry out procedures for making advance payments after the final deadline for making advance payments for regular expenditures not covered by goods or service purchase contracts or expenditures not required to be sent to the State Treasury under regulations;

  b) Do not carry out procedures for making advance payments on investment capital expenditures from state budget and investment capital from state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities from regular state budget expenditures and retained fees according to regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500,000,000 through payment of completed volume under contracts.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 4,000,000 for one of the following acts of violation:

  a) Failing to carry out procedures for settling all advances for regular expenses and expenses covered by goods or service purchase contracts under the regime prescribed in the final settlement of the contract;

  b) Failure to carry out procedures for settling the full payment of advances for investment capital amounts derived from state budget and investment capital sources from state budget for implementation of target programs or for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities using regular budget expenditures and retained fees according to regulations on regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500,000,000 when the value of payments (including advances and payments for the completed volume) reaches 80% of the contract value;

  c) Failing to make or to carry out procedures for making advance payments on compensation, support and resettlement expenses after the time limit for making advance payments has expired as prescribed.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced adoption of advance payment procedures for violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR REGISTRATION AND USE OF FUNDS AT STATE BANK

  Article 60. Violations against regulations on registration and use of accounts at State Treasuries

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on registration and use of accounts at State Treasuries.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced freezing or closing of accounts in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 61. Acts of making fake dossiers and papers to carry out procedures for registration of use of accounts at State Treasuries but not seriously enough to be examined for penal liability

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing false documents and sending them to the State Treasury to apply for account use registration.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced freezing of accounts in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 3. AUTHORITY TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES FOR OFFENCES AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE DEPOSITS

  Article 62. Authority to make records of administrative violations against regulations on state treasury

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations in the field of state treasury include:

  a) Persons having authority to impose administrative penalties for violations against regulations on control of state budget expenditures through state treasuries prescribed in Article 63 and Article 64 hereof;

  b) Officials assigned to control state budget expenditures through state treasuries.

  2. Persons having authority to make records as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall be responsible for making decisions on penalties within their jurisdiction or transferring to competent authorities specified in Articles 63 and 64 of this Decree to issue decisions on penalties.

  Article 63. Authority to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Chief Inspectors of Departments of Finance, heads of specialized inspectorates of Departments of Finance, heads of specialized inspectorates of State Treasuries established by Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and heads of specialized inspectorates of State Treasuries established by General Directors of State Treasuries have the power to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 50,000,000;

  b) Applying remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Finance has the right to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b) Applying remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspection teams of the Ministry of Finance have the right to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 250,000,000;

  b) Applying remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 64. Authority to impose penalties of Director General of State Treasury; Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and central-affiliated cities

  1. Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and centrally-affiliated cities have the right to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 70,000,000;

  b) Applying remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The Director of State Treasury has the right to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b) Applying remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter VI

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 65. Effect

  1. This Decree comes into force as of September 01, 2019.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury and the Government’s Decree No. 58/2015/ND-CP dated June 16, 2015 on amendments to the Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury shall cease to be effective from the effective date hereof.

  Article 66. Transition clauses

  Regarding administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury committed before the effective date hereof but discovered afterwards or being considered and handled, regulations on penalties hereof shall be applied if this Decree does not prescribe legal liabilities or impose lighter legal liabilities on violating individuals and organizations.

  Article 67. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Ministry of Finance shall provide guidance on implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies, and People’s Committees at all levels shall be responsible for providing guidance on and inspecting the implementation of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; and state treasury as prescribed in this Decree.

  3. When detecting administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury, competent authorities shall transfer dossiers (if any) or notify them to persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations specified herein for consideration and handling.

  In case of administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property which are subject to recovery of public property as prescribed by law on management and use of public property, the person competent to impose administrative penalties specified in this Decree shall, after issuing a decision on imposition of administrative penalties, notify the competent state agency in accordance with law on management and use of public property to issue a decision on recovery of property as prescribed. The handling of recovered property shall comply with law on management and use of public property.

  4. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels and relevant organizations and individuals shall be responsible for implementing this Decree./.
