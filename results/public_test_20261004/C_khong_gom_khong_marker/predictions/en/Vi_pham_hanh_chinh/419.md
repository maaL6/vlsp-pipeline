                                                   DECREE

     THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE No. 74/2003/ND-CP OF JUNE 26, 2003, ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ELECTRONICS

                                                   GOV

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002;
  At the proposal of the Minister of Industry,

                                                  DECREE

  Chapter 1.

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  “Administrative violation in the field of electricity means an act of intentionally or unintentionally violating regulations of law on state management in the field of electricity and power use which is not a crime and must be administratively sanctioned as prescribed by law, including:

  1. Violations of regulations on electricity operation permits.

  2. Violations against regulations on electricity construction, production, transmission and distribution.

  3. Violations against regulations on power supply.

  4. Violations against regulations on electricity use.

  5. Violations of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grids.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity specified in Chapter II of this Decree.

  2. Foreign organizations and individuals committing administrative violations in the field of electricity within the territory, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, unless otherwise provided by international agreements which the State of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam has signed or acceded to.

  3. Minors who commit acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned under the provisions of Article 7 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 3. Principles of sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. The imposition of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity shall be carried out by competent persons specified in Chapter III hereof in accordance with regulations of law.

  2. All administrative violations in the field of electricity must be detected in time and stopped immediately. The sanctioning must be conducted quickly, fairly and thoroughly; all consequences caused by administrative violations in the field of electricity must be remedied in accordance with law.

  3. A single administrative violation in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned once. A person who commits multiple administrative violations in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned for each violation. If many persons jointly commit a single administrative violation in the field of electricity, each violator shall be sanctioned.

  4. The sanction against administrative violations in the field of electricity must be based on the nature and seriousness of the violation, personal record and extenuating and aggravating circumstances to decide appropriate forms and measures of sanction. Extenuating and aggravating circumstances shall comply with the provisions of Article 8, Article 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  5. No administrative penalties shall be imposed for violations against regulations on electricity in cases of emergency, legitimate self-defense, unexpected events or administrative violations committed while suffering from mental illness or other diseases that deprive one of the ability to perceive or control one's acts.

  Article 4. Prescriptive periods for administrative penalties in the field of electricity and the period during which an administrative violation is considered not yet penalized in the field of electricity

  1. The prescriptive period for sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity is one year from the date the administrative violation in the field of electricity is committed; past this time limit, no sanction shall be imposed but the remedial measures prescribed in Clause 3, Article 5 of this Decree shall still be applied to overcome the consequences caused by the administrative violation in the field of electricity.

  2. Individuals who are prosecuted or brought to trial according to criminal procedures but later have their investigations or cases terminated by competent authorities and their violations show signs of administrative violations in the field of electricity shall be administratively sanctioned; in this case, the statute of limitations for administrative sanctions against violations in the field of electricity is three months from the date on which persons with sanctioning competence receive the termination decisions and violation records.

  3. During the time limit specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, if the violating individual or organization commits a new administrative violation in the electricity sector or deliberately avoids or obstructs the imposition of penalties, the prescriptive period prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply. The prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations in the electricity sector shall be recalculated from the time of committing a new administrative violation or the time of terminating the act of avoiding or obstructing the imposition of penalties.

  4. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity, if more than one year after completely serving the sanctioning decisions or expiry of the statute of limitations for execution of sanctioning decisions, do not relapse into violations, they shall be considered as having never been sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity.

  Article 5. Forms of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity

  1. Main forms of sanction: For each administrative violation in the field of electricity, violating individuals and organizations must be subject to one of the following main forms of sanction:

  a/ Warning shall apply to individuals or organizations committing minor and first-time administrative violations with extenuating circumstances or to all administrative violations committed by minors aged between full 14 years and under 16 years. Warning shall be decided in writing;

  b) Fines: depending on the nature and severity of each violation, fines shall be imposed within the fine bracket prescribed herein. The specific fine for an administrative violation in the electricity sector is the average level of the fine bracket for such violation; if the violation involves extenuating factors, the fine may be reduced but must not be lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation involves aggravating factors, the fine may be increased but must not be higher than the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  2. Additional penalties: Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individual or organization may also be subject to one or more of the following additional penalties:

  a) Suspend the license to operate electricity for a fixed period;

  b) Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the field of electricity.

  3. Apart from the main forms of sanction and additional sanctions prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be forced to restore the initial state altered due to their administrative violations in the field of electricity or forced to dismantle illegally constructed works.
  Chapter II
     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ELECTRONICS, PENALTIES AND FINES

  Article 6. Violations of regulations on electricity operation license

  1. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using expired electricity operation license.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Lease or lend electricity operation permits;

  b) To modify by themselves the contents inscribed in their electricity operation permits.

  3. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Power activities without power operation licenses;

  b) Electricity activities when they have been suspended or deprived of the right to use electricity operation licenses by competent authorities.

  4. Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, violating individuals or organizations may also have their electricity operation licenses withdrawn for between six months and one year in case of violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on electricity generation, production, transmission and distribution

  1. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 700,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Felling trees, pruning trees or demolishing works against regulations of law when building, operating and repairing power grids;

  b) Failure to restore the original state of related works altered due to the construction or repair of electrical works.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Violations against regulations on safety assurance for people and property during renovation, upgrade, repair, and construction of electrical works;

  b) Violating procedures and regulations on electricity production, transmission and distribution, causing safety hazards to people or damage to property.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily installing power lines and substations serving the distribution of electricity beyond the scope permitted by a competent authority;

  b) Violations of laws on electricity development investment;

  c) Obstructing organizations and individuals from performing the task of repairing and constructing electrical works.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for manufacturing, transmitting or distributing electricity without obtaining a certificate of eligibility from a competent authority.

  5. Apart from the main forms of sanction against acts of violation specified in this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be forced to restore the initial state which has been altered due to their violations prescribed at Point b, Clause 1 and Point a, Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on power supply

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Inspect organizations and individuals using electricity without professional cards or without inspection decisions of grid management agencies or units;

  b) Failing to follow the notified contents when carrying out power on/off.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to prepare records of commissioning and handover to the electricity user in accordance with regulations after installation, replacement, repair or adjustment of electricity metering system;

  b) Failing to appoint inspectors to inspect the meter after 05 days from the date on which the electricity seller receives the request for inspection of the meter from the electricity buyer;

  c) Failing to notify and publicly post up at electricity trading places the provisions of law on rights and obligations of parties buying and selling electricity, conditions for signing electricity sale and purchase contracts, electricity prices and procedures for stopping power supply;

  d/ Failing to sign the electricity sale and purchase contract within ten days after the buyer of domestic electricity has ensured all conditions prescribed in the contract and agreed terms in the draft contract.

  3. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Without working tickets or work assignment tickets and professional cards, arbitrarily carrying out one of the following tasks: repairing, connecting equipment, lines, power stations of the electricity sector or of organizations or individuals using electricity; removing, installing, and adjusting meters on the power grid;

  b) Failure to take actions against the fault within 2 hours from the moment in which the electricity purchaser receives the notice of the grid fault managed by the electricity seller;

  c) Obstructing the performance of inspection and supervision of electricity trading.

  4. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Arbitrarily using electricity works of the purchaser to supply electricity to other organizations and individuals using electricity;

  b) Shutting down power supply of organizations and individuals not required to be limited in use during power outage, except for cases where there is a risk of causing serious and unsafe incidents for people and equipment.

  5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to sign the electricity trading contract within ten days after the electricity purchaser has ensured all conditions prescribed by law and agreed on the terms of the draft contract;

  b) Cutting off power without notice in accordance with regulations on procedures for stopping power supply.

  6. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Delaying repair of the grid after an incident occurs without a legitimate reason;

  b) Using electricity metering equipment that has not been inspected and sealed by a competent authority as prescribed, or intentionally using electricity metering systems with an error exceeding the prescribed limit.

  7. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of abusing their profession to harass organizations and individuals using electricity for self-seeking purposes.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on electricity use

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for act of obstructing persons competent to inspect the use of electricity.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 700,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily installing, cutting, repairing or replacing electrical equipment and works of the electricity seller;

  b) Arbitrarily moving or falsifying electricity metering systems (including seals and wiring diagrams), electrical equipment, and electrical works of the electricity seller;

  c) Unintentionally causing a fault in the electricity system of the electricity seller;

  d) Using interfering devices to damage or falsify data transmission, measurement and protection equipment of power systems.

  3. To impose a fine on acts of stealing electricity in any form to serve daily-life activities and consumption;

  a) A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 400,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft with a quantity of < 100 kWh;

  b) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 700,000 shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with a quantity of between 100 kWh and under 200 kWh.

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft in the amount of from 200 kWh to under 300 kWh;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft in the amount of from 300 kWh to under 400 kWh;

  dd) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with a quantity of between 400 kWh and under 500 kWh.

  4. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily turning on electricity supply when the electricity project of the electricity purchaser has not been commissioned or is under suspension due to violations against regulations on electricity use;

  b) Use other power sources without permission of the electricity seller if the permitted power source is not available in case of using electricity for production, business or services;

  c) Delivering electricity to other organizations or individuals without the consent of the seller;

  d) Using electricity for fishing, trapping rats, preventing theft, protecting property, crops or other acts violating regulations on safety in using electricity.

  5. To impose a fine on act of stealing electricity in any form to serve purposes other than those specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft of < 100 kWh;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft in the amount of from 100 kWh to under 200 kWh;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft in the amount of from 200 kWh to under 300 kWh;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft in the amount of from 300 kWh to under 400 kWh;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft in the amount of from 400 kWh to under 500 kWh.

  6. Depending on the nature and severity of their violations, violating individuals or organizations may also have the exhibits and means used for committing violations in the cases specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 5 of this Article confiscated.

  7. In addition to the main forms of sanction and additional sanctions for violations prescribed in this Article, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be forced to restore the initial state altered due to their violations prescribed in Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grids

  1. A caution or a fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 shall be imposed for acts of climbing on power poles or entering power stations without duty.

  2. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Flying kites or balloons or remotely controlling aircrafts or other flying objects within the safety perimeter of the electrical works or the safety perimeter of the power lines; tying animals, vehicles and tools to power poles or walls of power stations;

  b) Installing antennas, lightning rods, signboards, advertising lights or other items within the protection perimeter of the electrical works, in the safety protection corridor of the power grid or at positions where they may cause unsafe conditions for the lines or other parts of the electrical works upon falling, threatening human lives;

  c) Cutting trees, planting trees or pruning branches against regulations within the protection perimeter of power works or in the safety protection corridor of power grid;

  d) Cutting trees or trimming branches outside the perimeter of the electrical work or outside the safety corridor of the power grid without taking measures to prevent the trees from hitting the lines or other parts of the electrical work.

  3. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 700,000 shall be imposed for acts of violating safety of foundation, walls and fences of power station, power pole or other structures and parts of electrical works.

  4. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to place warning signs, signal lights and danger indication at the location of the electrical works as prescribed or failure to place markers or signs along the underground cable;

  b) Arranging or storing flammable and/or explosive substances outside of the safety perimeter of the electrical works or outside of the safety corridor of the power grid in a manner that violates regulations on fire and explosion safety and thus affects the safety of the electrical works;

  c/ Setting up booths, shops or other constructions which affect water drainage, block air vents and obstruct access to power stations.

  5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 4,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Arrange and store flammable and explosive substances within the safety perimeter of the electrical works or in the safety corridor of the power grid;

  b) Firing forests, fields or other substances in violation of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grid;

  c) Abusing components and structures of electrical works for other purposes without permission from the competent electricity work management unit;

  d) Using equipment and devices with height violating safety distance as prescribed by laws on safety protection of high-voltage power lines;

  dd) Removing, erasing or covering the safety signs and signals on power poles within the protection perimeter of the electrical works or the safety protection corridor of the power grid, substations and power plants;

  e) Damaging power poles, power lines, insulated glass, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, power cables and other parts and structures of electrical works with voltage of less than 1 kV.

  6. A fine of between 3,000,000 dong and 6,000,000 dong shall be imposed for acts of damaging power poles, power lines, insulated glass, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, power cables and other parts and structures of electrical works with voltage of between 1 kV and 110 kV.

  7. A fine of between 4,000,000 dong and 8,000,000 dong shall be imposed for acts of damaging power poles, power lines, insulated glass, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, braided wires, power cables and other parts and structures of electrical works with voltage of over 110 kV.

  8. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Building new houses, upgrading and renovating houses, setting up booths, shops or other works in violation of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of high-voltage power grid;

  b) Failure to ensure safety conditions for houses and lawful construction works left in the high-voltage grid safety protection corridor as prescribed;

  c) Constructing works in underground or dredging river and lake beds within safety protection corridors of underground power cables without agreement with power work authority;

  d) Discharge waste and wastewater containing corrosive substances in the safety protection corridor of underground power cables;

  dd) Mining in violation of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grid;

  e) Failing to maintain a safe distance as prescribed by law when designing, building, or repairing electrical works;

  g) Using electricity as a means of direct protection against unauthorized use as permitted by competent authorities.

  9. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for acts of digging holes, setting piles, anchoring ships or other acts of violating protection corridors of underground power cables which have been put up with signboards or signals.

  10. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for building a wharf, constructing a building or other works within the safety perimeter of a power plant or the cable protection corridor where warning signs have been installed or are expected to be installed.

  11. Depending on the nature and severity of their violations, violating individuals or organizations may also have the exhibits and means used for committing violations confiscated, applicable to the acts specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Point b Clause 4, Points a, d and e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7, Point c Clause 8, Clause 9 and Clause 10 of this Article.

  12. In addition to the main forms of sanction and additional sanctions for violations prescribed in this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of illegally constructed works in case of administrative violations against regulations on electricity specified in Point b Clause 2, Point c Clause 4, Point c Clause 5, Point a and Point e Clause 8 and Clause 10 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered due to violations under Point c Clause 2, Clause 3, Point c Clause 4, Points c, dd and e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7 and Clause 10 of this Article.

  Chapter III

    POWER OF ADMINISTRATION AND PROCEDURES FOR PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ELECTRONICS

  Article 11.- Sanctioning competence of the Presidents of the People’s Committees at all levels

  Presidents of People’s Committees at all levels shall have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity occurring within their areas under their management, and in particular as follows:

  1. The President of the commune-level People’s Committee has the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscating exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the electricity sector valued at up to VND 500,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity.

  2. The chairpersons of the district-level People’s Committees have the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 20,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity;

  dd) Enforced dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity activities.

  3. The President of the People’s Committee of a province shall have the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Revoke the right to use electricity operation license issued by People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities;

  d) Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  dd) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity;

  e) Enforced dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity.

  Article 12. Power inspection authority

  Electricity inspectors of the Ministry of Industry have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity across the country. Electricity inspectors of provincial Departments of Industry have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity occurring within their provinces and cities. The power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity of electricity inspectors is specified as follows:

  1. The electricity inspector on duty has the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 200,000;

  c) Confiscating exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the electricity sector with a value of up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity.

  2. Chief Inspector of Electricity Department of the Provincial-level Industry Department shall have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 20,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity;

  dd) Enforced dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity activities.

  3. Chief Inspector of Electricity Inspectorate of Ministry of Industry has the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c/ Depriving the right to use the electricity operation permit granted by the Ministry of Industry and People’s Committees of provinces or centrally-run cities;

  d) Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  dd) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity;

  e) Enforced dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity.

  Article 13. Authority to impose penalties of other forces

  Apart from the persons specified in Articles 11 and 12 of this Decree, the following competent persons, when detecting acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity prescribed in this Decree under their respective management fields and areas, shall have the right to impose penalties, specifically as follows:

  1. People’s police officers on duty have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity as prescribed in:

  a) Point a Clause 1 Article 7;

  b) Point c Clause 3 Article 8;

  c) Clause 1, Clause 3 and Clause 5 of Article 9;

  d) Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, Point b and Point c Clause 4, Points a, b, c, dd and e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7, Points dd and g Clause 8, Clause 9 and Clause 10 Article 10.

  2. Construction inspectors have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity as prescribed in:

  a) Point a Clause 2 Article 7;

  b) Clause 3, Points a and c Clause 8 and Clause 10 Article 10.

  3. Environmental inspectors have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity specified in Point d Clause 8 Article 10.

  Article 14. Authorization and principles for determining the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity

  1. The authorization for sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity shall comply with Article 41 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The principles for determining the competence to sanction administrative violations in the field of electricity shall comply with Article 42 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 15. Procedures for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. Procedures and order for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity shall comply with the provisions of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Documents related to the sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity must be fully kept at the sanctioning agency. The administrative violation record shall be made according to the form prescribed in this Decree, except for cases handled under simple procedures prescribed in Article 54 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. Individuals and organizations that are fined must pay fines on time and at the places stated in the sanctioning decisions, except for cases where fines have been paid on the spot as prescribed in Articles 54 and 58 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and they are entitled to receive fine receipts. The collection of fines must use receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance according to current regulations.

  The Ministry of Finance shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Industry in guiding the collection and use of fines for administrative violations against regulations on electricity.

  4. Procedures for stripping the right to use electricity operation licenses must comply with Article 59 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  When applying the sanctioning form of confiscating material evidences and means used in the commission of violations, the persons with sanctioning competence shall have to strictly observe the provisions of Article 60 and Article 61 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 16. Execution of decisions on penalties and enforcement of decisions on penalties for administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity under this Decree shall strictly abide by sanctioning decisions within ten days after being handed over with sanctioning decisions, except otherwise provided for by law. If sanctioned individuals and organizations fail to voluntarily abide by sanctioning decisions, they shall be forced to execute according to Article 66 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. When applying coercive measures to enforce the execution of decisions on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity, competent agencies and persons must comply with the coercive procedures prescribed in Articles 66 and 67 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                          HANDLING OF COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND VIOLATIONS

  Article 17. Complaints, denunciations and handling of complaints, denunciations against decisions on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity or their lawful representatives have the right to lodge complaints or lawsuits against the sanctioning of such individuals and organizations according to law provisions.

  2. Every citizen has the right to lodge a denunciation with competent state agencies about administrative violations in the field of electricity committed by individuals and organizations and denunciations about violations committed by persons competent to sanction administrative violations in the field of electricity.

  3. The competence, procedures, order and time limits for settling complaints, denunciations and administrative lawsuits shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Complaints and Denunciations and the Ordinance on Procedures for Settlement of Administrative Cases (amended) of January 5, 1999.

  Article 18. Handling of violations committed by persons having power to impose penalties and persons subject to administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity

  1. Persons who have power to impose administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity but commit acts of harassment, tolerance or covering up of violations, failure to handle or improper handling, handling beyond their competence, illegal appropriation or use of money, exhibits and means used in violation shall, depending on nature and severity of their violations, be disciplined or examined for penal liability; if causing material damage, they must pay compensation therefor according to law provisions.

  2. If a person who is sanctioned for an administrative violation in the field of electricity commits acts of resisting law enforcers, delaying or evading the execution of sanctioning decisions or other violations, he/she shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of his/her violation, be administratively sanctioned under the Government’s regulations on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of security and order or examined for penal liability; if causing material damage, he/she must pay compensation therefor according to law provisions.

  Chapter 5.

                                                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 19. Effect

  This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO. All previous regulations on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity which are contrary to this Decree are now annulled.

  Article 20. Implementation

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.
