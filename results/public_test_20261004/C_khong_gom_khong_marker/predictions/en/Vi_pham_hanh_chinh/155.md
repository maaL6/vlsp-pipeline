  Article 5. Transitional provisions

  For administrative violations committed before the effective date of this Decree but detected afterwards or being considered and handled, regulations beneficial to violating individuals and organizations shall apply.

  Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall be responsible for guiding and implementing this Decree./.
