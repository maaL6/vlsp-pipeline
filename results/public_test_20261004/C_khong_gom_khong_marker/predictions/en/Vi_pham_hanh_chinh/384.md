                                                   DECREE

       THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE No. 49/2005/ND-CP OF APRIL 11, 2005, ON SANCTIONING OF ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF EDUCATION

                                                  GOV

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
  Pursuant to the December 2, 1998 Law on Education;
  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002;
  At the request of the Minister of Education and Training,

                                                  DECREE

  Chapter 1.

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations in the field of education (profession training not regulated by this Decree), forms of sanction, levels of sanction, authority to sanction, procedures for sanction and remedies.

  2. Administrative violations in the field of education are acts of violating the regulations on state management in the field of education deliberately or unintentionally committed by individuals or organizations, which are not crimes and must be administratively sanctioned under the provisions of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and this Decree.

  3. The administrative violations in education prescribed in other Government’s decrees (hereinafter referred to as relevant decrees) shall be applied with provisions of such decrees.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree and relevant decrees.

  2. Foreign individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education in Vietnam shall be sanctioned like Vietnamese individuals and organizations according to provisions of this Decree and relevant decrees, unless otherwise provided by international agreements which Vietnam has signed or acceded to.

  3. If an individual who is a minor commits an administrative violation in the field of education, he/she shall be handled according to the provisions of Clauses 1 and 3, Article 7 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 3. Principles of sanction

  1. All administrative violations in the field of education must be detected in time and stopped immediately.

  The handling of administrative violations in the field of education must be carried out quickly, fairly and thoroughly; all consequences caused by administrative violations must be remedied in accordance with law.

  2. Individuals and organizations shall be administratively sanctioned for violations in the field of education only when they commit violations prescribed in this Decree and other relevant Government’s decrees on administrative sanctions.

  3. An administrative violation in the field of education shall be administratively sanctioned only once.

  A person or an organization committing multiple administrative violations in the field of education shall be sanctioned for each violation.

  If many persons or organizations commit the same administrative violation in the field of education, each violating person or organization shall be sanctioned.

  4. The sanction against administrative violations in the field of education must be based on the nature and seriousness of the violation, personal record of the violator and extenuating and aggravating circumstances to decide appropriate handling forms and measures.

  5. Administrative violations in the field of education shall not be sanctioned in cases of emergency, legitimate self-defense, unexpected events or administrative violations committed by individuals suffering from mental illness or other diseases that have caused them to lose the ability to perceive or control their acts.

  Article 4. Extenuating and aggravating circumstances

  The extenuating and aggravating circumstances applicable to the sanctioning of administrative violations prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree shall comply with the provisions of Articles 8 and 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 5. Prescriptive periods for administrative violations

  1. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education is one year from the date the administrative violation is committed; for administrative violations in the field of education related to finance, intellectual property, construction, environment, housing, land, publication, export, import, exit and entry or administrative violations being acts of smuggling, production and/or trading of fake goods, the statute of limitations shall be two years; past the above time limit, no sanction shall be imposed but the consequence-overcoming measures prescribed in Clause 3, Article 7 of this Decree shall still apply.

  2. For individuals who are prosecuted or brought to trial according to criminal procedures but later have their investigations or cases terminated and their violations show signs of administrative violations in the field of education, they shall be administratively sanctioned; within three days from the date of issuing the decision on investigation termination or case termination, the person who has issued such decision must send it to the person with sanctioning authority; in this case, the statute of limitations for administrative violation sanction is three months from the date the competent person receives the decision on termination and the dossier of the violation.

  3. Within the time limit specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, if individuals or organizations commit new administrative violations in the field of education or deliberately evade or obstruct the sanctioning, the statute of limitations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations shall be recalculated from the time of committing new administrative violations or from the time of terminating acts of deliberately evading or obstructing the sanctioning.

  Article 6. Time limit for being considered as not yet administratively sanctioned

  Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education shall be considered as having never been sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education if they do not relapse within one year after completely serving the sanctioning decisions or the decisions take effect.

  Article 7. Penalties and remedial measures

  1. For each administrative violation in the field of education, the violating individual or organization shall be subject to one of the following main forms of sanction:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fine.

  When applying a fine, the specific fine level for an administrative violation is the average level of the fine bracket prescribed for such act. If the violation involves extenuating circumstances as prescribed in Article 8 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, the fine level may be lowered below the average level but must not be lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation involves aggravating circumstances as prescribed in Article 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, the fine level may be raised above the average level but must not exceed the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  2. Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education may be subject to one or more of the following additional sanctioning forms:

  a) Revoke the right to use decisions on establishment, licenses or practicing certificates for a definite or indefinite period of time, applicable to individuals or organizations that commit serious violations of regulations on the use of decisions, licenses or practicing certificates;

  b) Confiscating material evidence and means used for administrative violations;

  c/ Deportation if the violator is a foreigner.

  3. In addition to the penalties prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, violating individuals or organizations in the field of education may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state altered by the administrative violations at the request of competent authorities;

  b) Enforced suspension or termination of teaching and education activities;

  c) Enforced cancellation of test results or re-marking of test papers;

  d) Enforced provision of adequate number of lectures, tutorials, and practice sessions according to regulations; provision of materials and equipment according to approved plans;

  dd) Compulsory cancellation of decisions which are wrong or contrary to regulations of competent authorities;

  e) Enforced restoration of learners’ right to learn, legitimate benefits and correct assessment results, and assurance of rights of certificate/certificate holders;

  g) Enforced return of collected amounts to learners and incur all return fees caused by administrative violations;

  h) Enforced revocation of illegally issued diplomas/certificates;

  i) Enforced bringing out of Vietnamese territory or re-export of illegally imported textbooks, documents and educational equipment due to administrative violations.

  Chapter II

    ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON EDUCATION, PENALTIES AND FINES

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on establishment of educational institutions and organization of educational activities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for losing the decision on establishment, license or practicing certificate but failing to report it to a competent authority.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for erasing, altering or adding contents of, buying, selling, transferring, leasing, borrowing or lending decisions on establishment, licenses or practicing certificates in violation of regulations of law.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for illegally establishing educational institutions:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for establishing an early childhood education institution or a general education institution;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for establishing a vocational secondary education training institution, continuing education center, foreign language center or computer center;

  c) VND 20,000,000 - VND 40,000,000 for college training institutions;

  d) VND 60,000,000 - VND 100,000,000 for higher education institutions.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for opening independent classes to teach preschool education programs, general education programs, professional secondary education programs, undergraduate preparatory courses, university entrance examination preparation courses, college and university training courses, or postgraduate training courses without permission from a competent authority, or imposing permission on such activities beyond their competence:

  a) VND 200,000 - VND 500,000 for early childhood education classes;

  b) VND 500,000 - VND 1,000,000 for general education class;

  c) VND 1,000,000 - VND 2,000,000 for vocational training classes, preparatory courses for university entrance exams;

  d) VND 2,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 for undergraduate or postgraduate training programs.

  6. Additional penalties and remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscate exhibits for acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of collected amounts to learners and incur all return fees for the acts specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on operation of educational institutions

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for failing to comply with a competent authority’s decision on suspension or dissolution of the training course; operating beyond the time limit specified in the license; or suspending operation without permission from a competent authority, causing damage to learners:

  a) VND 3,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 for early childhood education and general education institutions;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 for vocational training institutions, continuing education centers, foreign language centers and computer centers;

  c) VND 15,000,000 - VND 30,000,000 for college training institutions;

  d) VND 40,000,000 - VND 60,000,000 for higher education institutions and postgraduate training institutions.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for providing training and issuing certificates against regulations of law.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 for one of the following acts of violation:

  a) Training and issuing degrees of the national education system beyond the assigned authority;

  b) Providing training in fields and specializations beyond their assigned authority.

  4. Remedial measures for violations under this Article:

  a) Enforced return of collected amounts to learners and incurrence of all return fees for violations prescribed in Clause 1 and enforced revocation of illegally issued diplomas/certificates for violations prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of learners’ lost learning rights caused by violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article according to decisions of competent authorities.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on educational programs, contents and plans

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for failing to teach enough number of lessons or knowledge (calculated by number of lessons) of a subject as prescribed by competent authorities in general education, vocational education and training, college, university and postgraduate education at the following levels:

  a) A warning shall be imposed for committing less than 5 violations in a class within a school year;

  b/ A fine of VND 200,000 - VND 500,000 for committing violations in 5 - 10 teaching periods of a class in a school year;

  c/ A fine of VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for a violation involving 11 to 15 periods of a class in a school year;

  d) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for violations from 16 to 20 periods of a class in a school year;

  dd) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for violations from 21 periods or more in a class in a school year.

  2. To impose a fine on act of teaching or disseminating contents not in program, textbook and/or teaching material prescribed for purpose of distorting educational contents (but not to the extent of being examined for penal liability) shall be imposed with the following fine level:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for the first violation;

  b) VND 1,000,000 - VND 2,000,000 for each subsequent violation.

  3. Additional penalties for violations under this Article:

  a) Confiscate exhibits for violations specified in clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Deportation if the violating individual is a foreigner for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Revoke the license for a fixed period in case of first-time violation or indefinitely in case of repeated violation in respect of the violations prescribed in clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Remedial measures for violations under this Article:

  a) Enforced suspension of teaching and educational activities of violating individuals in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced addition of teaching details and missing contents in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on eligible candidates, eligibility criteria, enrollment targets and authority

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 200,000 and VND 300,000 for acts of making false declaration or abetting the making of false declaration in enrolment dossiers in order to be enrolled.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for admitting students to general education level or grade at inappropriate levels or with unqualified conditions as prescribed by competent authorities:

  a) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for the violation of admitting less than 3 students;

  b) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for the violations of wrong recruitment from 3 to 5 students;

  c) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation of wrong recruitment from 6 to 10 students;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 11 - 15 students;

  dd) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the violation of wrong recruitment from 16 students or more.

  3. A warning or fine shall be imposed for enrolling students in vocational secondary education beyond the prescribed number of students, enrolling ineligible students or failing to meet the prescribed standards as prescribed by competent authorities.

  a) A warning shall be imposed if the enrollment rate exceeds the target enrollment rate by less than 5%;

  b) A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for the violation of enrolment exceeding from 5% to less than 10% of the target enrollment quota assigned or enrolling less than 10 learners not in eligible subjects or eligible criteria;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting learners who do not meet eligibility criteria or target enrollment of 10% - 15% or admitting 10 - 20 learners.

  d) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting learners whose number exceeds the target capacity by 15% - 20%, or admitting 21 - 30 learners who fail to meet eligibility criteria;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 6.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting more than 20% above the target number of enrolled learners or admitting 31 learners or more who fail to meet eligibility criteria.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for enrolling students in college, university or postgraduate training programs in excess of prescribed quotas, ineligible students or failing to meet prescribed standards as regulated by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the violation involving enrolment of less than 5% of the target enrolment;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by 5% - 10% or fail to enroll 10 learners at all if they are ineligible;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting learners who do not meet eligibility criteria or target enrollment rate of 10% - 15% or admitting 10 - 20 learners.

  d) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting learners whose number is 15% - 20% higher than the target or admitting 21 - 30 learners who fail to meet eligibility criteria;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting more than 20% above the target number of enrolled learners or admitting 31 learners or more who fail to meet eligibility criteria.

  5. The following fines shall be imposed for enrolling students for training and issuance of diplomas of national education system beyond the assigned authority:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for making enrollment announcements in any form before obtaining permission from a competent authority;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for collecting applications, organizing exams or enrolling examinees without being assigned by a competent authority.

  6. The following fines shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations of the State on enrolment for overseas training or training at educational institutions with foreign elements operating in the territory of Vietnam according to general education, vocational education and training, college, university or postgraduate programs:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 1 - 5 learners;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 6 - 10 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 11 - 15 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 16 - 20 learners at variance with regulations;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 21 - 25 learners at variance with regulations;

  e) A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed for admitting 26 or more learners with incorrect qualifications.

  7. Additional penalties for violations under this Article:

  a) Confiscate exhibits of violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Suspend practicing licenses or certificates for a fixed period in case of first-time violations and impose indefinite suspension if the violations prescribed in Clauses 3, 4 and 6 of this Article are repeated.

  8. Remedial measures for violations under this Article:

  a) Enforced cancellation of wrongful decisions due to acts specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of collected amounts to learners and incur all return fees for the acts specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on entrance examinations to educational institutions and graduation examinations for issuance of diplomas in national education system

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for disturbing or threatening to use force to obstruct examiners, invigilators, and other officials in charge of examination administration from performing their duties.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for using force to obstruct exam administrators, invigilators, and examiners in performing their duties.

  3. A fine of between VND 4,000,000 and 6,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) To take part in a contest or to participate in a contest as a supporting person;

  b) Illegally transferring documents, information and equipment to examination rooms for examinees;

  c/ Disclosing secrets of test papers.

  4. A fine of between VND 6,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of swapping test papers or abetting other persons to swap test papers of examinees in contravention of regulations of competent agencies.

  5. A fine of between VND 1,500,000 and 3,000,000 for any violation of the following postgraduate regulations:

  a) Issuing decisions on establishment of apparatuses and assignment of tasks to officials in contravention of regulations applicable to such examination;

  b) Making the test papers lost or damaged to the extent that they cannot be graded;

  c) Marking entrance exams or graduation exams not in accordance with the answers and score range;

  d) Make a table of incorrect scores compared to the marking results of the exam.

  6. A fine of from VND 4,000,000 to VND 6,000,000 shall be imposed for writing or modifying test scripts of examinees in contravention of regulations.

  7. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for act of disclosing secrets or losing examination papers (but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability).

  8. Additional penalties for violations under this Article:

  Confiscate exhibits and means used to commit violations prescribed in Clauses 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures for violations under this Article:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the violations prescribed in Clause 4, Point d Clause 5 and Clause 6 of this Article;

  b) Enforced cancellation of test results of the violator (if the violator is a candidate) for the acts specified in Clauses 1 and 2 and of the beneficiary of the candidates for the acts specified in Clauses 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article;

  c) Enforced correction of examination scripts in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on assessment of learners’ performance in general education institutions, vocational education and training institutions and higher education institutions

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for failure to fully comply with regulations of competent authorities on marking, assessment and grading of learning outcomes, thereby affecting learners’ performance in courses, semesters or years, as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for committing the violation in the general education;

  b) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for violations in vocational education and training institutions;

  c) A fine of between VND 600,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for violations in higher education.

  2. Remedies for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced restoration of assessment and grading results to reflect actual learning performance of learners in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on management, issuance and use of diplomas/certificates of national education system

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Failing to issue or certify copies of or to certify the legitimacy of issued diplomas/certificates;

  b/ Granting diplomas or certificates with contents which cause obstacles to users.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of using illegal diplomas or certificates.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to prepare or inadequately preparing documents as a basis for issuance and management of diplomas/certificates;

  b) Fabricate or falsify documents to grant degrees or certificates to ineligible persons.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Decide or request competent authorities to issue decisions on issuance of certificates illegally;

  b/ Destroying or falsifying dossiers and documents used as grounds for granting or certifying the validity of certificates (but not to the extent of being examined for penal liability).

  5. A fine of between VND 4,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Decide or request competent authorities to issue decisions on issuance of diplomas illegally;

  b/ Destroying or falsifying dossiers and documents used as grounds for the grant of titles or certification of their validity (but not to the extent of being examined for penal liability).

  6. Additional penalties for violations under this Article:

  The license or practicing certificate shall be suspended for a definite period in case of second violations and permanently in case of repeated violations prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Revocation of diplomas/certificates in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 1, Clause 2, Point b Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 and Point a Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of rights and legitimate interests of certificate holders in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on using teachers

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for using unqualified teachers as prescribed:

  a) A warning shall be imposed for employing unqualified preschool teachers;

  b) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of using general education teachers who are not qualified;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for employing unqualified teachers of vocational secondary schools, continuing education centers, foreign language centers or computer centers.

  d) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for employing unqualified lecturers;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 11.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for employing unqualified lecturers in undergraduate or postgraduate programs.

  2. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced suspension of teaching and education activities for teachers who fail to meet the prescribed standards while working as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 16. Violations against regulations on teacher and lecturer ratio in educational institutions

  The following fines shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations of competent authorities on ensuring the ratio of full-time teachers and lecturers to total number of teachers and lecturers in educational institutions:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations in formal education levels or grades;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing the violation in a vocational secondary education institution, continuing education center, foreign language center or computer center;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for committing the violation at a college;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for committing the violation at a university or postgraduate training institution.

  Article 17. Violations against regulations on management of learners’ dossiers

  1. A warning or fine shall be imposed for irresponsibility in management leading to the alteration of school books, score sheets or other documents related to assessment of learners’ performance against regulations, causing the following penalties:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for violations from 1 to 2 learners;

  b) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for violations with from 3 to 5 learners;

  c/ VND 600,000 - VND 1,000,000 for a violation involving 6 or more cases.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to prepare or incompletely preparing the learner management dossier as prescribed in the school charter.

  3. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced restoration of the original condition which has been altered by the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on use of textbooks, educational materials and provision and use of educational equipment

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for issuing a decision against regulations on purchase, receipt of gifts, textbooks, educational materials and educational equipment that fail to meet quality requirements or cause adverse impacts on teaching and learning activities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for transporting and distributing textbooks, educational materials, or educational equipment against the plan for provision prescribed by a competent authority.

  3. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Enforced bringing out of Vietnamese territory or re-export of illegally imported textbooks, documents and educational equipment due to the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original plan altered due to the violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on tuition fees, charges and other amounts collected from learners

  To apply the provisions of Chapter II of the Government's Decree No. 106/2003/ND-CP of September 23, 2003, prescribing the sanctioning of administrative violations in the domain of fees and charges.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on forms of disciplinary action of expulsion

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed on the act of deciding to discipline learners to leave school against regulations, with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for violations involving between 1 and 2 learners;

  b) A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for violations involving from 3 to 5 learners;

  c) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for violations involving from 6 to 10 learners;

  d) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for violations involving 11 learners or more.

  2. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced cancellation of incorrect decisions and restoration of learners’ right to learn for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on education for all

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 50,000 and VND 100,000 shall be imposed for obstruction of students at all levels and levels of education.

  2. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of inciting not to go to school or drop out of school for students at all levels and levels of education.

  Chapter III

                                     POWER AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTION

  Article 22. Authority to impose administrative penalties for violations against regulations on education of Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

  1. The presidents of the People’s Committees of communes, wards or townships shall have the right to sanction according to the provisions in Article 28 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their respective localities and under their management.

  2. The presidents of the People's Committees of rural districts, urban districts, provincial capitals and towns shall have the right to sanction according to the provisions in Article 29 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their respective localities and under their management.

  3. The presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall have the right to sanction according to the provisions in Article 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their respective localities and under their management.

  Article 23. Authority to impose penalties for administrative violations of inspectors of education

  1. Inspectors on duty in the educational sector shall have the right to impose penalties according to the provisions of Clause 1, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for administrative violations against regulations on education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  2. The Chief Inspectors of the provincial/municipal Services of Education and Training shall have the right to sanction according to the provisions in Clause 2, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  3. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Education and Training has the right to sanction according to the provisions in Clause 3, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  Article 24. Authorization to impose administrative penalties for violations against regulations on education

  In case where the persons competent to sanction administrative violations in the field of education specified in Article 22 and Clauses 2 and 3, Article 23 of this Decree are absent, the authorization shall comply with the provisions of Article 14 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 25. Principles for determining authority to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on education

  1. The chairpersons of the People’s Committees at all levels shall have the right to handle administrative violations in the field of education in their localities according to their competence prescribed in Articles 28, 29 and 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The education inspectors have the right to sanction administrative violations in the field of education according to the provisions in Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  In case of violations beyond the jurisdiction of Chief Inspectors of Departments of Education and Training, violation dossiers shall be transferred to Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees for handling according to their jurisdiction.

  3. In case an individual or organization commits at the same time many acts of administrative violation, including violations in the field of education, the sanctioning competence shall be determined according to the principle prescribed in Clause 3, Article 42 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  4. The competence to expel individuals who are foreigners and commit administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with the provisions of Clause 1, Article 17 of Decree No. 21/2001/ND-CP of May 28, 2001 of the Government detailing the implementation of the Ordinance on Entry, Exit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam.

  5. In cases where violations of the law on education are deemed to show signs of crime, the dossiers must be immediately transferred to competent agencies for settlement according to Article 62 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 26. Procedures for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education

  1. The procedures for making records and decisions on sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Articles 19, 20 and 21 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The fine and payment of fines must comply with the procedures prescribed in Articles 24 and 25 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. Procedures for stripping the right to use decisions on establishment, licenses and professional practice certificates shall comply with Article 59 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 11 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  4. The procedures for confiscating and handling material evidences and means used in administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with Articles 60 and 61 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 31 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 27. Execution of decisions on administrative penalties in the field of education

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education must abide by sanctioning decisions within 10 days after being given such decisions, except for cases already prescribed in Article 65 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. The time limit is calculated as prescribed in Article 9 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education but failing to voluntarily comply shall be forced to implement them. Individuals and organizations subject to enforcement must bear all expenses for the implementation of enforcement measures. The procedures for enforcement are prescribed in Articles 66 and 67 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                                 COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 28. Complaints and denunciations and settlement thereof

  1. Individuals, organizations or their lawful representatives may lodge complaints about sanctioning decisions of competent persons specified in Articles 22, 23 and 24 of this Decree. The procedures for lodging and settling complaints shall comply with Article 118 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. The competence and time limit for lodging and settling complaints shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Complaints and Denunciations. The lodging of complaints about decisions on sanctioning administrative violations does not suspend the execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education.

  2. Individuals have the right to denounce illegal acts in handling administrative violations in the field of education by competent sanctioning authorities to competent state agencies. The procedures for lodging complaints and denunciations and settling them shall comply with Article 118 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 29. Handling of violations committed by persons having competence to impose administrative penalties for violations in the field of education

  Persons who have competence to sanction administrative violations in the field of education but harass, tolerate, cover up, fail to handle or handle not in time, improperly, handle beyond prescribed competence shall be handled according to the provisions of Article 121 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 30. Handling of violations committed by persons subject to administrative penalties for violations against regulations on education

  Persons who are sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education if committing acts of opposing officials on duty, delaying or shirking the execution or committing other violations shall be handled according to the provisions of Article 122 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter 5.

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 31. Effect

  This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO.

  To annul the regulations on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Decree No. 18/2001/ND-CP of May 4, 2001, and other regulations previously in contravention of this Decree.

  Article 32. Responsibility for guidance and implementation

  The Minister of Education and Training shall, within the ambit of his/her functions, tasks and powers, guide and organize the implementation of this Decree.

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial/municipal People's Committees shall implement this Decree.
