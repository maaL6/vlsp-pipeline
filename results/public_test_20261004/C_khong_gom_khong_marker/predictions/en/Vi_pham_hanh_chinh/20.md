                                                  DECREE

                 REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE SEAFARING SECTOR

  Decree No. 142/2017/ND-CP dated December 1, 2017 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime, which takes effect from February 1, 2018, is amended and supplemented by:

  1. The Government’s Decree No. 123/2021/ND-CP dated December 28, 2021 on amendments to a number of articles of Decrees on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime, road traffic and railway transport shall come into force from January 01, 2022.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 80/2026/ND-CP dated March 19, 2026 on amendments to the Government’s Decree No. 142/2017/ND-CP dated December 11, 2017 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime, amended by the Government’s Decree No. 123/2021/ND-CP dated December 28, 2021 and the Government’s Decree No. 139/2021/ND-CP dated December 31, 2021 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on inland waterway transport, comes into force from May 15, 2026.

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Vietnam Maritime Code dated November 25, 2015;

  At the request of the Minister of Transport;

  The Government promulgates Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime[1].

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. [2] This Decree provides for administrative violations; completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations; penalties, fines, remedial measures applicable to each administrative violation; authority to impose penalties and specific fines imposed by title holders; authority to issue administrative offense notices; implementation of penalties and remedial measures.

  2. [3] Administrative violations against regulations on maritime prescribed herein include:

  a) Violations against regulations on construction, management and operation of maritime infrastructure;

  b) Violations against regulations on construction and management of operation of an ICD;

  c) Violations against regulations on operation of ships and vessels;

  d) Violations against regulations on registration and deregistration of ships and vessels and crew member assignment; use of certificates of competency and crew member books;

  dd) Violations against regulations on maritime pilots;

  e) Violations against regulations on maritime transport business, multimodal transport, maritime transport support services and provision of maritime safety services;

  g) Violations against regulations on eligibility requirements for establishment of facilities building, modifying and repairing ships and vessels and ship-breaking activities;

  h/ Violations of the regulations on safety of the Comte-no;

  i) Violations against regulations on maritime search and rescue operations; salvage of sunken property and maritime signals;

  k) Violations against regulations on training and training of crew members;

  l) Violations of regulations on prevention of marine pollution;

  m) Violations against regulations on maritime labor; declaration of maritime occupational accidents;

  n) Violations against regulations on prevention and control of natural disasters in maritime sector;

  o) Violations against regulations on prevention and control of infectious diseases in the field of goods

  p) Violations against regulations on state management, inspection and handling of administrative violations in maritime sector.

  q) [5] Violations against regulations on posting of information about prices and surcharges in the maritime sector.

  3. [6] Violations specified in Points c, d, dd, e, i and l Clause 2 of this Article if they occur outside the port waters but have not been prescribed in the Decree on penalties for administrative violations in the sea areas, islands and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall also be sanctioned according to provisions of this Decree.

  4. Other administrative violations against regulations on maritime not prescribed in this Decree shall be sanctioned according to regulations of other Decrees on administrative sanctions against related violations. Persons having power to impose sanctions specified in Articles 60, 61, 62 and 63 of this Decree when detecting administrative violations specified in this Clause shall have the power to impose sanctions.

  5. Handling of administrative violations against regulations on maritime shall comply with this Decree and relevant regulations of law on handling of administrative violations.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the maritime sector; persons competent to issue offense notices and persons competent to impose penalties for administrative violations in the maritime sector.

  2. Foreign organizations and individuals committing administrative violations within the territory, contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone or continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or on board a Vietnamese-flagged ship shall be administratively sanctioned in accordance with this Decree and relevant provisions of Vietnamese law on handling of administrative violations, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  2a. [8] Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) Economic organizations established in accordance with law;

  b) Social organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-professional organizations, and socio-professional-political organizations;

  c) Public service providers and other organizations as prescribed by law.

  2b. [9] Household businesses must register their business as prescribed by law, and households committing administrative violations prescribed in this Decree shall be sanctioned for such violations like individuals.

  3. Based on the nature and severity of the violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 1 of this Decree, the competent person shall determine the specific penalties for organizations and individuals to impose administrative penalties as prescribed by law.

  Article 3. Prescriptive periods of administrative penalties [10]

  The prescriptive period for administrative penalties in the maritime sector is 01 year; however, for administrative violations against regulations on construction of seaports, ICDs, maritime works, environmental protection, price management and overseas labor management, the prescriptive period shall be 02 years.

  Article 3a. Completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations [11]

  1. Completed administrative violations include the following administrative violations:

  a) Violations against regulations on loading of goods prescribed in Point a Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 Article 16 hereof. The time of termination of violations shall be determined from the time when the overloading of goods is detected;

  b) Acts detected through professional technical means and equipment. The time of termination of violations shall be calculated from the time professional technical means and equipment record the violations;

  c) Administrative violations prescribed in this Decree that do not fall under the cases specified at Points a and b of this Clause but have been completely carried out before the competent person discovers them. In cases where there is no document or evidence to accurately determine the time when the violation ends, these violations are still determined to be within the statute of limitations for administrative penalties.

  2. Administrative violations specified in this Decree that do not fall under the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article are ongoing administrative violations.

  Article 4. Forms of administrative penalties and remedial measures

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the maritime sector shall be subject to one of the following main penalties for each violation:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fine.

  2. [12] Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual may also be subject to one or more of the following additional penalties:

  a) Revoke the practicing license or certificate for a definite period of time;

  b) Suspend operation for a definite period of time;

  c) [13] Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations, including: certificates, written certifications, licenses, written permission, certificates, papers not issued by competent authorities, and vehicles used for committing administrative violations.

  3. [14] Apart from the penalties prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations against regulations on maritime shall also implement remedial measures as prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree, including:

  a) Enforced renaming of ports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves, wharf bridges, floating terminals, waters, and areas in accordance with naming principles;

  Enforced adequate arrangement of port security officers as per regulation;

  Enforced implementation of approved port security plans as per regulation;

  Enforced inspection of quality of port infrastructure within prescribed time limit or after accidents and collisions affecting maritime structures;

  b) Enforced arrangement and installation of life-saving equipment on board ships as per regulation;

  Enforced establishment of adequate and appropriate aids to navigation as per regulation;

  Enforced relocation of cages, cages or fishing and aquaculture facilities to the wrong locations in the port waters;

  Forcible demolition of illegal works or parts thereof;

  c) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered;

  d) Enforced improvement of dredging monitoring system on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during construction to ensure readiness and continuity;

  dd) Enforced installation of dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during construction;

  Compulsory adoption or implementation of or proper implementation of maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities;

  e) Enforced unloading of goods stacked beyond the permissible payload of the vehicle if the vehicle has not left the loading area;

  g) Enforced development of a plan for protection of maritime works or enforced implementation of approved plan for protection of maritime works;

  Enforced formulation of natural disaster response plans as per regulation;

  h) Enforced change or correction of names of ICDs to conform with those announced by competent authorities;

  Compulsory additional and correction of information;

  i) Enforced payment of maritime charges and fees and other relevant amounts as prescribed;

  Enforced return of expenses for salvage of sunken property as per regulation;

  k) Enforced relocation of obstacles on the port waterway to the prescribed locations;

  Forced to relocate to areas of operation appropriate to ship classes;

  l) Forced to disembark passengers or unload goods carried on board the ship in excess of the prescribed limit;

  Forcing ships to leave their positions;

  Enforced restoration of the original state of the signal position and structure of the work if the position is moved or the structure of the signal work is affected;

  m) Enforced implementation of safety measures for transport of oversized and overweight goods;

  Enforced completion of procedures for registration with Vietnamese nationality flag as per regulation;

  Enforced completion of procedures for deregistration of ships as prescribed;

  n) Enforced provision of adequate and accurate information on embarkation and disembarkation and assignment of crew member titles by electronic means to the Crew Member Management Database of the Vietnam Maritime and Waterway Administration as prescribed;

  o) Enforced arrangement of maritime pilots to guide ships in accordance with the certificate of maritime pilotage competency or certificate of maritime pilotage area;

  Compulsory provision of adequate and timely pilotage services in compulsory maritime pilotage areas or on assigned ship navigation routes;

  Forcing the use or arranging sufficient pilotage aids as prescribed or using pilotage aids to ensure safety conditions;

  Enforced arrangement of sufficient number of pilots or minimum number of aircraft as prescribed;

  p) Enforced removal from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam of imported maritime flares;

  q) Enforced arrangement of rest periods for crew members as prescribed;

  Forced to supply essential supplies and foodstuffs for crew members working on board;

  Forcing provision of funds for repatriation of crew members;

  Forcing ship owners to fulfill their responsibilities for crew members suffering from occupational accidents and occupational diseases;

  Forcing the purchase of compulsory insurance for seafarers working on board ships;

  Forced to arrange repatriation of seafarers within prescribed time;

  r) Enforced payment of expenses for solicitation of assessment, inspection, measurement and analysis of environmental samples in case of violations leading to oil spills or environmental pollution in accordance with applicable norms and unit prices;

  To force the implementation of measures to overcome the environmental pollution and report the results of overcoming consequences of violations;

  Compulsory compensation for damage caused by oil pollution in accordance with regulations within the time limit set by the person competent to impose penalties in the decision on administrative penalties;

  s) Enforced posting or proper posting of the list of organizations and individuals that have signed contracts for waste collection and treatment services from ships at seaports and the prices of waste collection and treatment services as prescribed;

  Enforced reporting on the management of waste collection and treatment from ships at seaports as prescribed;

  Enforced provision of accurate or correct declarations of waste by the port authority as per regulation;

  Enforced implementation of measures to control environmental pollution caused by spilled waste, dispersion and leakage during the collection of waste from ships;

  Forcing the implementation of measures to overcome the provisions preventing pollution caused by ships;

  Forced to implement measures to overcome environmental pollution and violations of regulations on management and control of bilge water and bilge sludge of ships;

  t) Enforced re-signing of crew member employment contracts in accordance with employment licenses;

  Forcing seafarers to leave the ship;

  Enforced correction of information on the location and condition of operating ships in case of natural disasters;

  Enforced medical examination and treatment;

  Enforced return to the ship or medical quarantine area;

  Enforced medical isolation, enforced medical isolation, and medical treatment for persons, ships, and goods carrying infectious agents.

  u) Enforced return of certificates, written confirmation, licenses, written permission, certificates and papers which are erased or falsified by the competent authorities or persons who have issued them.

  4. For administrative violations, the fines shall be imposed according to the ship’s capacity or total capacity (GT) which is the capacity measured in accordance with the International Convention on Measurement of Ship’s Capacity 1969 and specified in the certificate issued by the registry.

  In case the ship’s certificate does not specify CT, the total capacity of the ship shall be calculated as follows:

  a) Self-propelled seagoing ships and inland waterway vessels: 1.5 tons of tonnage shall be calculated as 01 GT;

  b) Sail: 01 tonne of gross tonnage is equal to 01 GT;

  c) Tugboats, towing boats, passenger ships (including aircrafts) and floating cranes: 1 horsepower (HP or CV) is equal to 0.5 GT; 1 kW is equal to 0.7 GT; 1 tonne of lifting capacity of a crane on board a ship is equal to 6 GT;

  d) Passenger ships not specified with engine capacity: 1 passenger seat = 0,67 GT; 1 sleeping berth = 4 GT;

  dd) In case of a tow or towing group or a side-by-side group: The total capacity of the whole group including barges, towing units or towing units shall be used for calculation;

  e) The conversion specified in Points a, b, c, d and dd Clause 4 of this Article shall be carried out by selecting the method with the largest total capacity;

  g) Engine capacity unit: The main engine capacity of a ship shall be expressed in HP, CV or kW; the fractional part under 1 HP or 1 CV shall be rounded up to 1 HP, 1 CV or 1 kW.

  5. [15] For administrative violations related to ships and vessels without information for calculating total ship capacity specified in Clause 4 of this Article, the method for determining gross tonnage and ship capacity for imposing penalties is as follows:

  a) The gross tonnage of a ship is denoted by T (ten) and calculated according to the formula T = A x K, where:

  - A is the value of the length of the main deck, measured from the bow to the stern of a passenger ship, multiplied by the breadth of the middle of the ship's deck and the height of the ship's side, measured from the bottom to the top of the middle of the ship's deck, calculated according to the formula A = L x B x D, where:

  L (m) - Main deck length from bow to stern;

  B (m) - Width of the side margin at the middle of the ship;

  D (m) = height of the ship’s side from the bottom to the top of the deck;

  - K is the coefficient corresponding to value A and applied as follows:

  If the value of A is between 4.55 m3 and 18.76 m3, the coefficient K = 0.26;

  If the volume of A is between over 18.76 m3 and 49.80 m3, the coefficient K = 0.29;

  If the volume of A is between over 49.80 m3 and 387.20 m3, the coefficient K = 0.35;

  If the volume of A is between over 387.20 m3 and 1.119.80 m3, the coefficient K = 0.51;

  If the value of A is over 1,119.80 m3, the coefficient K = 0.57.

  b) If the ship is not a cargo or passenger ship, the applicable penalty shall be determined based on the total main engine capacity installed on the ship. In case it is impossible to determine the main engine capacity of the ship or the ship does not have a label, the applicable penalty shall be the same as that for engines with a capacity of 50 horsepower;

  c) In case the gross tonnage of the ship is not determined as prescribed in Point a Clause 5 of this Article, the competent person shall request the registry to determine the gross tonnage of the ship to serve as a basis for issuing an administrative penalty decision;

  d) For ships and vessels such as floating terminals or other floating structures where dredging and pumping equipment is installed for dredging channels, mineral exploitation, and cargo handling, if there are insufficient grounds to determine the gross tonnage or capacity of the ship or vessel, the determination shall be carried out as follows:

  Ships with a maximum length of 10 m or width of 4 m shall be determined as ships with a gross tonnage of between 5 and 15 metric tons;

  A ship of more than 10 m in length or more than 4 m in width shall be considered as having a gross tonnage of more than 15 metric tons;

  Length of a ship means the distance from the outer edge of its bow to the outer edge of its stern or from two outer edges of the ship where the largest cross-section on the deck is located;

  The width of a ship is measured from one side to the other at its widest point.

  Article 4a. Enforcement of penalties and remedial measures [16]

  1. The implementation of penalties and remedial measures prescribed in this Decree shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations and guiding documents.

  2. Violating individuals and organizations shall cooperate with relevant organizations and individuals (if any) in implementing remedial measures specified in the decision on administrative penalties as prescribed by law and bear all costs for the implementation of such remedial measures.

  3. In case of enforced return of a certificate, written confirmation, license, written permission, or document that is erased, altered, or falsified in content, the violating individual or organization shall be responsible for implementing the enforced return of such certificate, written confirmation, license, written permission, or document as prescribed in Article 85 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  The competent person shall issue a decision to enforce the remedial measure of transferring the certificate, confirmation, license, written permission, certificate, or document that has been erased, altered, or falsified in content to the agency or person that issued such certificate, confirmation, license, certificate, or document.

  4. For the remedial measure of forcible payment of maritime charges and fees and other relevant amounts as prescribed:

  a) Persons having authority to impose administrative penalties and violating individuals or organizations shall contact the state management agency in charge of inland waterway shipping where the ship departs from to determine maritime charges, fees and other relevant amounts payable as prescribed.

  b) Violating individuals and organizations shall notify the results of implementation of remedial measures to persons having authority to sanction administrative violations.

  Article 5. Principles for determining fines

  The fine for each administrative violation prescribed in this Decree shall be imposed on an individual; in case of the same administrative violation, the fine imposed on an organization shall be twice as much as that imposed on an individual.

  Article 6.[17] (annulled)

  Chapter II

      ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
                                         RESULTS

  Section 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON CONSTRUCTION, MANAGEMENT AND EXPLOITATION OF MARITIME INFRASTRUCTURE

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on announcement of opening of offshore ports, oil and gas ports, wharves and quays [18]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for providing false information in the application for announcement of the opening of offshore ports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves or quays.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for using a certificate, written confirmation, license, written permission, certificate or document that is erased, altered or falsified in content or not issued by a competent authority, if not liable to criminal prosecution, in an application for announcement of the opening of offshore port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf or jetty.

  3. Additional penalties: Confiscate the certificate, written confirmation, license, written permission, certificate or document that is not issued by a competent authority in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Remedial measures: Enforced return of certificates, written confirmation, licenses, written permission, certificates and documents that are erased or falsified to competent authorities or persons that issued such certificates, written confirmation, licenses, written permission, certificates and documents in case of commission of the violation in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on naming and renaming of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves, landing stages, floating terminals, port waters, and territorial waters [19]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 30,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for naming or renaming a port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or territorial waters inconsistently with the principles of naming as prescribed or failure to notify in writing the maritime authorities of the naming or renaming of the port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or territorial waters as prescribed.

  2. Remedial measures: Enforced renaming of ports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves, quays, floating terminals, waters or zones in a manner compliant with regulations on naming principles if the names of these facilities are not compliant with regulations on naming principles as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 9.[20] (annulled)

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on maritime safety assurance, maritime security and order during operation of ports

  1. A warning shall be imposed for entering or leaving the port land area or boarding a ship or boat against the guidance of a competent authority or person.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of entering or leaving port land areas or boarding ships or vessels without permission of competent agencies or persons.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Violations against regulations on warning signs for safe landing of ships as prescribed;

  b) Failing to assign persons to tie or untie the mooring lines as prescribed;

  c) Failing to notify the port authority of the ship's itinerary as prescribed;

  d) Placing objects on the wharf or occupying the space above the wharf, thus obstructing the landing and departure of ships or other activities at the port.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Employing employees who do not have practicing licenses or certificates as prescribed;

  b) Failure to promptly notify the port authority of incidents or accidents related to maritime safety, maritime security and environmental pollution at the port as prescribed;

  c) Failure to regularly provide the port authority with data on depth of waters in front of the wharf as prescribed;

  d) Failure to carry out periodic surveys for announcing maritime notices on the depth of waters in the areas in front of the wharf and other waters as prescribed;

  dd) Lighting equipment at the wharf is not working or does not work in accordance with technical regulations as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) To load or moor vessels in port waters without permission of the port authority;

  b) Unlawfully loading or unloading goods when the ship has not yet completed its procedures for port entry as prescribed;

  c) The anti-collision cushion system and ship mooring fittings of the wharf are inadequate or unsafe for ships to anchor;

  d) Failing to obtain or failing to carry out annual procedures for certification of the port’s certificate of compliance as prescribed, or failing to fully implement the approved port security plan as prescribed;

  dd) Failing to provide or providing maritime security information to competent authorities in a timely and accurate manner; failing to organize exercises or practice in connection with maritime security information as prescribed;

  e) Arranging port security officers against regulations;

  g) Failure to arrange wharf or floating terminal for ships and vessels to enter or leave within the time limit according to the plan for ship mobilization of the maritime port office or failure to meet requirements as prescribed;

  h) Failure to install lighting equipment at the wharf as prescribed.

  i) [22] Failure to adequately store dossiers serving the management, operation and maintenance of maritime works;

  k) Failure to carry out inspection or operate loading and unloading equipment, vehicles, or specialized machinery within the port facility without undergoing inspection as per regulation.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 30,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Operating a port against its declared functions;

  b) Let ships and vessels dock at the wharf when the wharf has not been permitted to be put into operation or use;

  c) Failure to have approved port security plans as prescribed;

  d) Failure to carry out inspection of quality of port infrastructure within the prescribed time limit or after an incident involving collision or impact that affects the structure of maritime works;

  dd) Failing to comply with relevant technical regulations in quality inspection of port infrastructure.

  7. [25] Additional penalties:

  a) Suspend the receipt of ships and vessels operating on international routes to the port for 1 to 3 months in case of commission of the violation specified at Point d or Point e Clause 5 or Point c Clause 6 of this Article;

  b) Suspend the receipt of ships and vessels into the port from 1 to 3 months for violations prescribed in Point d Clause 6 of this Article.

  8. [26] Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced adequate allocation of port security officers as prescribed in case of commission of the violation in Point e Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced development of approved port security plan in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 6 of this Article;

  c) Enforced inspection of quality of port infrastructure within the prescribed time limit or after an incident that causes damage to maritime structures in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 6 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on loading, unloading, storage and handling of goods

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for loading, unloading and storing goods in violation of regulations.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for loading cargoes on a wharf beyond the permissible limit.

  3. Remedial measures: Enforced removal of goods exceeding the permissible weight limit in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on safety assurance for maritime and environmental protection during construction of new ports or renovation and upgrading of existing ports, or during construction and installation of other works and equipment affecting maritime safety [27]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) There is no life-saving equipment or the life-saving equipment is not suitable as prescribed;

  b) Failing to notify the port authority of the construction of other works in the port waters;

  c) Failure to adequately install or properly install navigational aids as prescribed, or failure to operate or mislead navigational aids in areas where works are being constructed;

  d) Construction ships and ships serving construction work anchoring outside of permitted areas, causing obstruction to maritime traffic on navigational channels;

  dd) Failure to record supervision results of dredging and transport of dredged materials or failure to keep construction logbook as prescribed; failure to submit periodic reports on progress and results of dredging navigational channels, waters, and areas within port waters in combination with full utilization of products as prescribed to Vietnam Maritime Administration and Vietnam Inland Waterway Administration[28];

  e) [29] Conducting aquaculture in port waters without obtaining permission from a competent authority or at a location or at an inappropriate time as per the granted permission.

  g) Making anchor in port waters or navigational channels.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Executing works not in accordance with the licensed location;

  b) Executing construction beyond the time limit specified in the construction permit of the competent authority as prescribed;

  c) Using specialized equipment and devices to survey and dredge navigational channels, establish maritime aids to navigation, and carry out other activities within the port waters without obtaining the approval of the port authority as prescribed;

  d) Failure to remove or clear obstacles arising during construction after the project is completed;

  dd) Failure to fully implement maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities;

  e) Creating obstacles in the port waters and territorial waters of Vietnam, affecting maritime activities;

  g) Installing dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials in dredging activities inconsistently with regulations; dredging monitoring systems that fail to meet minimum technical specifications or are not in operational readiness or operate intermittently and stably as prescribed;

  h) Failing to organize supervision of dredging and dredged material dumping as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to establish navigational aids as prescribed;

  b) Failure to install dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged material during dredging or maintenance of navigational channels, waters, or areas;

  c) Failing to implement or improperly implementing maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for organizing construction of works other than those specified in Point a Clause 5 of this Article without obtaining a license or written approval from a competent authority as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 40,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Organize construction of works subject to preparation of economic-technical reports on construction of works or preparation of investment projects for construction of works without obtaining licenses or written approvals from competent authorities as prescribed;

  b) Building works not in accordance with the approved design;

  c) Failure to have maritime safety plans as prescribed.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 50,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 shall be imposed for constructing a work against the approved planning.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 80,000,000 to VND 100,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Executing works without permission of competent authorities leading to accidents;

  b) Executing construction work at an unauthorized location and causing accidents;

  c) Failure to have or failure to comply with maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities, leading to accidents.

  8. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced provision of life-saving equipment or appropriate life-saving equipment as prescribed in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) [34] Enforced provision of adequate and appropriate aids to navigation as prescribed in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) [35] Enforced relocation of cages, cages or fishing and aquaculture facilities to the wrong locations in the port waters for committing the violation prescribed in Point e or Point g Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) [36] Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered in case of commission of the violation specified in Point e Clause 2 of this Article;

  dd) Enforced demolition of illegal works or parts thereof in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 2, Clause 4, Clause 5, Clause 6 and Points a and b Clause 7 of this Article;

  e) Enforced rectification of dredging monitoring system on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during construction must ensure readiness and continuity in case of commission of the violation specified in Point g Clause 2 of this Article;

  g) Enforced installation of dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during construction in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;

  h) Enforced adoption of maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities in case of commission of the violation in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 7; implementation or proper implementation of maritime safety plans and traffic safety plans approved by competent authorities in case of commission of the violation in Point c Clause 3 and Point c Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on fire and explosion prevention and control at seaports

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) There is no internal rule board, warning signs or instructions at places prone to fire and explosion as prescribed;

  b) Using specialized fire fighting equipment and devices for wrong purposes.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Fires are not properly installed or are not in a ready state or have expired according to regulations;

  b) Failing to place fire and explosion prevention and control equipment at prescribed places or failing to arrange them in a manner appropriate to the type of goods being transported or unloaded.

  c) Obstructing or hindering the work of inspection teams, inspection teams, or persons assigned to conduct inspection and perform duties in the maritime sector;

  d) Failing to appoint a competent representative as prescribed by law to participate in the announcement of inspection results or failing to appoint a competent representative to work with the inspection team.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for removing seals on documents, exhibits, means, warehouses or equipment in violation of regulations; temporarily seizing or dispersing documents and exhibits involved in administrative violations; or arbitrarily altering the scene of an administrative violation against regulations on maritime.

  4. A fine of between VND 50,000,000 and 80,000,000 for one of the following acts of violation:

  a) Delaying or avoiding the execution of inspection decisions by competent authorities or persons in the maritime sector;

  b) Failing to properly and fully comply with contents and requirements in the inspection conclusion of the competent authority in the maritime sector.

  Article 58n. Online inspection and handling of administrative violations [104]

  1. When verifying information of documents including: original copies of ship registration certificates or certified true copies of ship registration certificates and valid confirmation letters of credit institutions (if ships are mortgaged), certificates and other documents of ships as per the law, professional qualification certificates that have been integrated and updated in electronic identification cards, electronic identification accounts on the National Identification Application, databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction, verification shall be carried out via information on electronic identification cards, electronic identification accounts on the National Identification Application, databases; verification of information on documents included in electronic identification cards, electronic identification accounts on the National Identification Application, databases shall be equivalent to verification of original copies of such documents.

  2. Temporary seizure or deprivation of the right to use documents

  a) When temporarily seizing or revoking the right to use documents, competent persons shall update information on such temporary seizure or revocation of the right to use documents on the system for handling administrative violations and synchronize it with the National Identification Application and databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction so that violators and ship owners (regarding documents belonging to the ship owners) are informed thereof and comply with regulations of law on maritime order and safety, and serve competent authorities in inspecting and handling violations;

  b) In case information on the documents subject to temporary seizure or suspension of use is available on the National Identification Application or databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security or the Ministry of Construction, competent persons shall return such documents to sanctioned persons as prescribed; the database system for handling administrative violations shall synchronize information with the National Identification Application and databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security or the Ministry of Construction in order to remove information on temporary seizure or suspension of use of such documents;

  c) The person having authority to impose penalties shall access the electronic receipt of fine collected from administrative violations sent by the public service portal system for printing and archiving the administrative penalty record and as a basis for returning the temporarily seized or suspended documents or removing information about temporary seizure or suspension of use on the National Identification Application or database provided by the Ministry of Public Security according to the provisions of point b of this clause;

  d) Records and decisions of competent persons on temporary seizure or deprivation of the right to use or return of documents shall be made in accordance with law and sent by electronic means via the National Identification Application, other applications or electronic information systems when technical conditions are met.

  3. Organizations and individuals having the power to inspect and handle administrative violations shall be responsible for ensuring confidentiality of personal information and data of violators in accordance with law.

  4. Online handling of administrative violations prescribed in this Decree shall be carried out when infrastructure, technical and information conditions are satisfied in accordance with regulations of law on handling of administrative violations.

  Section 1. AUTHORITY TO RECORD ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 59. Authority to make records of administrative violations

  Persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations and officials, public employees, and persons of the People's Army or People's Public Security forces who are performing their duties within the management of persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations prescribed in Articles 60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 65, and 66 of this Decree and detect administrative violations against regulations on maritime within their jurisdiction must promptly prevent such violations and make records of administrative violations as per regulation.   Chapter IV

                                        IMPLEMENTATION CLAUSES [115]

  Article 67. Guidelines on collection, transfer and management of remittances

  The Ministry of Finance shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Transport in providing specific guidelines on collection, transfer, and management of the remittance of amounts to be returned to the state by organizations and individuals involved in administrative violations prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 68. Transition clauses

  For administrative violations in maritime sector committed before the effective date of this Decree but detected and handled after the effective date of this Decree, regulations on penalties shall be applied in a manner beneficial to violating organizations and individuals.

  Article 69. Effect

  This Decree comes into force as of February 1, 2018 and replaces the Government’s Decree No. 93/2013/ND-CP dated August 20, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime transport and inland waterway transport.

  Article 70. Implementation

  1. The Minister of Transport shall be responsible for organizing and guiding the implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall be responsible for implementing this Decree./.
