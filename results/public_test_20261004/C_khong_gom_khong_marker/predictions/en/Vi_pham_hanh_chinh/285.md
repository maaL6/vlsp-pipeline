                                                   DECREE

     REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF FIRE PREVENTION AND FIRE FIGHTING

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;

  Pursuant to the Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting dated June 29, 2001;

  Pursuant to the July 2, 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the April 2, 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  At the request of the Minister of Public Security;

  The Government promulgates a Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting,

  Chapter 1.

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, principles of sanctioning, forms of sanctioning, sanctioning levels, remedial measures, competence to sanction, procedures for sanctioning, complaints, denunciations and settlement of complaints and denunciations about sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting.

  2. Administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting or related to fire prevention and firefighting not prescribed in this Decree but prescribed in other Government’s decrees on administrative penalties shall be administratively sanctioned according to provisions of such decrees.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting within the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall be sanctioned in accordance with this Decree and other relevant provisions of law on administrative sanctions.

  Article 3. Principles of sanctioning administrative violations

  The principles for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting shall comply with Article 3 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 4. Forms of administrative penalties

  1. For each administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting prescribed in this Decree, the violating individual or organization must be subject to one of the following main forms of sanction:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fine.

  2. Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting may also be subject to one or more of the following additional sanctioning forms:

  a) Revoke the right to use licenses and practicing certificates;

  b) Confiscating material evidence and means used for administrative violations.

  3. In addition to the main forms of sanction, supplementary forms prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a/ Forced application of measures to overcome environmental pollution caused by administrative violations;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violations or enforced dismantlement of illegally constructed works or structures;

  c) Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations;

  d) Enforced reduction of quantity of substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards according to prescribed norms;

  dd) Enforced relocation of hazardous substances from fire and explosion risks caused by administrative violations to storage facilities or locations as prescribed;

  e) Other remedial measures prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  4. Persons who commit acts of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and fighting, causing damage to organizations or individuals, shall, in addition to being administratively sanctioned under Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article, pay damages under the civil law.

  5. Foreigners who commit acts of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and fighting on the territory of Vietnam may be expelled from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. The expulsion may be applied as a principal or additional sanction. The competence, order and procedures for application of the sanction of expulsion shall comply with the current law provisions on sanctioning of expulsion according to administrative procedures.

  Chapter II

                      ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES

  Article 5. Violations against regulations on promulgation, dissemination and implementation of fire prevention and firefighting regulations

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Failing to fully comply with fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations;

  b) Making ineffective or making fire prevention and firefighting rules, orders, signs, warning signs, or direction signs unclear or illegible.

  c) Fire safety equipment and signage are not conformable with regulations.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to arrange or post fire prevention and firefighting orders, signs, no-entry signs, and instructions as prescribed;

  b) Failing to comply with fire safety rules and regulations;

  c) Failing to disseminate fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations among persons under their management;

  d) Promulgate fire prevention and firefighting regulations and rules with insufficient contents or not suitable to the nature of fire and explosion hazards of facilities.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to arrange or post fire prevention and firefighting regulations as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to promulgate or promulgating regulations, internal rules on fire prevention and fighting in contravention of the State’s legal documents.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violations prescribed in Point b Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced promulgation, dissemination and posting of fire prevention and firefighting regulations in accordance with regulations for the violations specified in Points a, c and d Clause 2 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 6. Violations against regulations on fire safety inspection

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to present dossiers and documents serving for inspection of safety of fire prevention and fighting.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to fully or punctually implement fire prevention and firefighting recommendations as requested in writing by competent authorities;

  b) Failing to appoint responsible persons to join the fire safety inspection team as prescribed;

  c) Failing to organize fire safety inspections as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to organize implementation of documents guiding and directing fire prevention and firefighting issued by competent authorities;

  b) Failing to implement fire prevention and firefighting recommendations as requested in writing by competent authorities;

  c) Obstructing inspection and examination of fire prevention and firefighting in the field by competent authorities.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for failure of the facility’s head to issue a written notice and commitment on satisfaction of all requirements and maintenance of fire safety conditions during use of the house, work, or production/ business activities after the facility is approved and commissioned.

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on fire safety management dossiers

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Failing to make statistics and reports on fire prevention and firefighting work as prescribed;

  b) The application for management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting activities does not contain sufficient documents as prescribed;

  c) Failing to update information on changes related to fire prevention and firefighting in the records of management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting activities at the facility.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to prepare fire prevention and firefighting management and monitoring records as prescribed.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in management, preservation and use of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to have books, records for monitoring and managing substances, goods with fire and explosion hazards as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for storing, arranging or positioning flammable and explosive substances or goods in excess of the prescribed quantity or weight, or failing to maintain the prescribed distance or group separation between them.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using devices or containers containing or holding flammable and explosive substances without a certificate of inspection or without satisfying fire safety requirements.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for act of illegally storing inflammable and/or explosive materials or goods.

  5. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for act of illegally using substances, goods dangerous to fire and explosion.

  6. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate substances and goods dangerous to fire and explosion for acts specified in Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced preparation and establishment of documents and records for monitoring and managing hazardous substances and goods related to fire and explosion in accordance with regulations for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  b) Enforced preservation, arrangement, reduction of quantity and volume of fire and explosion hazards in accordance with regulations for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Enforced transfer of flammable and explosive materials to storage facilities or designated locations in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in production and trading of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to take measures and equipment to prevent electrostatic accumulation as prescribed;

  b) There is no natural ventilation or forced ventilation equipment as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to install devices for detecting and treating leaks of substances or dangerous goods with fire and explosion hazards in the surrounding environment as prescribed;

  b) There is no plan or equipment for handling incidents of tank rupture, or equipment and pipelines containing, carrying and transporting petroleum, petroleum products and other flammable liquids;

  c) Failing to place or load inflammable and explosive materials or dangerous goods at prescribed places or placing or loading inflammable and explosive materials or dangerous goods in devices of improper types or incompatible with such materials or goods.

  3. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of manufacturing, trading, mixing, filling substances, goods dangerous to fire and explosion without licenses as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 25,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of manufacturing, trading in substances, goods dangerous to fire and explosion on the list banned from business.

  5. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate substances and goods dangerous to fire and explosion for acts specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remediation of environmental pollution in accordance with regulations for violations specified in Point a and Point b Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on transportation of flammable and explosive substances

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Damaging or disintegrating the license for transport of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards;

  b) Failing to carry the transport permit when transporting inflammable and explosive substances or goods as prescribed;

  c) Do not remove or detach fire and explosion hazard symbols on transport means when the hazardous materials or goods have been removed from the transport means;

  d) Losing the license for transport of substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards but having promptly reported such to competent authorities.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Arranging flammable and explosive substances on transport vehicles against regulations;

  b/ Stopping or parking the means of transport of inflammable and/or explosive substances or goods at places where such transport is not permitted as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to maintain adequate fire safety conditions when using motorized transport vehicles for transporting substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards during the permitted transport period as prescribed;

  b) Transporting other goods together with substances or inflammable and explosive goods on the same means of transport without permission as prescribed;

  c) Transporting unauthorized persons on means carrying flammable and/or explosive substances or goods.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Transporting flammable and explosive substances and goods in excess of the quantity, weight and type specified in the license;

  b) Failing to post on transport means the symbols of dangerous goods in case of fire and explosion as prescribed;

  c) Failing to comply with fire safety conditions or failing to follow instructions of competent persons when loading, unloading, pumping, transferring flammable and explosive substances or goods from transport vehicles as prescribed;

  d) Loading, unloading, pumping, and transporting flammable and explosive substances or goods to locations where fire prevention and firefighting conditions are not satisfied as prescribed;

  dd) Loading, unloading, pumping or transferring flammable and explosive substances or goods on the transport route onto other vehicles without permission of competent authorities;

  e) Repair and erase licenses for transport of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards;

  g) Losing the license for transport of substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards without notifying the competent authority.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Transporting flammable and explosive substances or goods without a license for transport of flammable and explosive substances or goods as prescribed;

  b) Falsify or use falsified licenses to transport substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards.

  6. Additional penalties:

  a) Revoke the license to transport substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards in case of commission of the violation specified at Point e Clause 4 of this Article;

  b) Confiscate flammable and explosive substances and goods for acts specified in Point b Clause 1, Points a and b Clause 5 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced rearrangement of hazardous substances and goods with fire and explosion risks in accordance with regulations for the acts specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced reduction in quantity, weight and type of fire and explosion hazards as prescribed in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Enforced transfer of flammable and explosive materials to storage facilities or designated locations in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a or Point b Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in use of fire sources, heat sources, fire-producing equipment and devices

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Failure to arrange cooking and worship places in a manner that ensures fire safety as per regulation;

  b) Lighting candles, turning on fire, using mobile phones, electronic devices or devices and tools that generate fire or heat in prohibited places.

  2. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for using fire sources, heat sources, equipment and devices that generate fire or heat without maintaining a safe distance in terms of fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Using fire sources, mobile phones, electronic devices or other devices that generate fire or heat in places where such activities are banned;

  b) Welding and cutting of metals without taking fire prevention and firefighting measures as prescribed.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in design, installation, management and use of electricity

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to provide regulations on fire prevention and firefighting safety in electricity use at establishments.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of using electrical equipment not in compliance with the manufacturer’s instructions.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to maintain regular operation of the emergency lighting system;

  b) Changing the design or changing the main structure and specifications of electrical systems and electrical equipment without approval by competent persons or authorities;

  c) Installing and using electrical conductors, power cables or switching, protection or consumption equipment not ensuring fire safety;

  d) Using electrical equipment in excess of the design;

  dd) Using electrical equipment in places where it is banned.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Using electrical equipment that fails to meet fire and explosion prevention requirements in a fire and explosion hazard environment;

  b) No reserve power source as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to design and install electrical systems serving fire prevention, firefighting and rescue purposes as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on fire safety in design, construction, inspection and maintenance of lightning protection system

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to have dossiers for monitoring lightning protection system as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to periodically inspect lightning protection system as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to remedy faults or damage causing ineffectiveness of lightning protection system.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to install lightning protection systems at houses and works required to install such systems as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in investment and construction

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to take measures and means to ensure safety in fire prevention and fighting during construction of works.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to design fire prevention and fighting system for works subject to having design on fire prevention and fighting.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to satisfy one of fire safety requirements when putting a house or work into use if such house or work is not required to undergo fire safety approval.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Construction and installation not in accordance with fire prevention and firefighting design approved by competent authorities;

  b) Failing to submit applications for re-examination when renovating, expanding or changing the use of houses and works during construction and use as prescribed.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Organize construction of works subject to fire prevention and firefighting approval without obtaining a "Certificate of Fire Prevention and Fighting Approval" as prescribed;

  b) Designing or modifying motor vehicles with special requirements for fire safety assurance which have not been approved by competent authorities.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for putting houses or works into operation or use before organizing commissioning of fire prevention and firefighting systems as prescribed.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced improvement of fire safety conditions for violations under Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced construction and installation of fire protection works in accordance with approved fire protection design for the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Enforced rectification of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations or enforced acceptance testing for fire prevention and firefighting by state management agencies in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 6 of this Article.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on fire safety and fire prevention distance

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Arrange and organize supplies and goods not maintaining a safe fire safety distance as prescribed;

  b/ Failing to organize industrial hygiene which may lead to the creation of an environment with fire and explosion hazards.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for building fire-rated walls, fire-rated partitions or fire-rated doors and other fire prevention measures against regulations.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Removing the floor, ceiling, partition, roof or gathering flammable materials at places where such activities are not permitted as prescribed;

  b) Building houses in or near forests without ensuring fire prevention and fighting safety as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to clean flammable substances located within safety corridors of pipelines for petroleum, gas and petroleum products;

  b) Construct works violating fire separation distance requirements.

  5. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to build fire-rated walls, fire-rated partitions, fire-rated doors and fire prevention solutions as prescribed;

  b) Destroying fireproof walls, fireproof partitions, fireproof doors and fire prevention measures.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 and Point b Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced dismantling of illegally constructed works in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 3 and Point b Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Point a Clause 3, Point a Clause 4, Point a Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 16. Penalties for violations against regulations on fire and rescue

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for act of designing exit doors not opening in the direction of escape, installing mirrors in the staircases of the exit.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Arrange and place supplies, goods, means of transport and other items obstructing the escape route;

  b) Removing or damaging emergency lighting equipment, warning signs, and signage on the exit routes;

  c) Failing to install warning signs or instructions for escape on the exit routes.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to have adequate rescue equipment and instruments as prescribed;

  b) There is no ventilation and smoke exhaust equipment as prescribed for the emergency exit;

  c) There is no emergency lighting on the exit or there is but the brightness is not sufficient as prescribed or it is ineffective;

  d) Designing and building escape doors, escape routes, and/or staircases with insufficient quantity, area, width, or in contravention of regulations.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for closing or blocking the emergency exit.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 15,000,000 to VND 25,000,000 shall be imposed for disengaging the means of egress.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 17. Violations against fire safety plans of facilities

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to manage fire prevention plans as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Developing fire prevention and firefighting plans that fail to meet requirements as prescribed;

  b) Failure to disseminate fire safety plans as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to submit fire prevention and firefighting plan as prescribed;

  b) Failing to participate in fire prevention and firefighting training as prescribed;

  c) Failing to supplement or adjust fire prevention and firefighting plans as prescribed;

  d) Failing to practice all fire-fighting scenarios in the approved fire-fighting plan.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to develop fire prevention and firefighting plans as prescribed;

  b) Failing to organize fire prevention and firefighting internship as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 18. Violations against fire alarm information

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for act of leaving means, information devices of fire alarm broken or useless.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to have fire alarm signals and devices as prescribed;

  b) Slow or untimely fire alarm; insufficient fire alarm.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Fake fire alarm;

  b) Failing to report fire or obstructing or hindering the reporting of fire;

  c) The information service provider fails to promptly repair the fire alarm receiving device of the Fire and Rescue Police when there is a written request.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of initial changes caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on fire and explosion prevention and control equipment, storage and use

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Move or change the installation position of fire and firefighting equipment according to the design approved by a competent authority;

  b) Blocking fire and fighting equipment.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to carry out periodic inspection and maintenance of fire prevention and firefighting systems and equipment as prescribed;

  b) Furnishing of fire and firefighting equipment is inadequate or inconsistent with regulations;

  c) Failure to provide common fire safety equipment for motor vehicles with 4 seats or more and motor vehicles used exclusively for transporting flammable and explosive goods as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Equipping, installing, or using fire and explosion prevention and fighting equipment that has not been inspected for quality, type, or design in accordance with regulations;

  b) Fires and explosions are not suitable for the nature of the facility as prescribed;

  c) Using fire extinguishers at fire stations for other purposes;

  d) Failing to adequately store firefighting water as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failure to provide common fire safety equipment for houses and works as prescribed;

  b) Destroying or damaging fire and rescue equipment.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to install fire alarm and firefighting systems as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced rectification of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced remedy of initial changes caused by administrative violations specified in Point b Clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 20. Violations against fire safety regulations

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of entering fire prevention and firefighting areas without permission of competent persons.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to promptly carry out the rescue of people, rescue of property or firefighting.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to adequately prepare forces, means, water sources and other conditions for firefighting as prescribed;

  b) Failure to arrange traffic routes and access positions for fire trucks and other motorized firefighting vehicles as per regulation.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to comply with the orders of the fire commander;

  b) Failing to comply with the order for firefighting of competent persons;

  c) Obstructing the operation of fire and firefighting forces and means;

  d) Do not participate in remedying consequences of fire upon request of competent persons;

  dd) Failing to protect the scene of the fire as prescribed.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to organize rescue, firefighting or fire prevention;

  b) Taking advantage of fire prevention and firefighting to harm the health or property of citizens and state property.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on dissemination of fire prevention and firefighting laws, knowledge, learning, training and education

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of damaging fire prevention and fighting banners, slogans, billboards, posters, propaganda pictures and posters.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to organize the dissemination of fire prevention and firefighting laws and knowledge as prescribed;

  b) Using firefighters of grassroots fire department, operators of motor vehicles with 4 seats or more, operators of motor vehicles transporting hazardous materials and goods prone to fire and explosion but have not been trained in fire prevention and firefighting or have not been issued with certificate of training in fire prevention and firefighting as per regulation.

  3. A fine ranging from 1.000.000 VND to 2.000.000 VND shall be imposed for failing to organize fire prevention and firefighting training and refresher training as prescribed.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced restoration of the original condition which has been altered by the administrative violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on establishment, organization and management of grassroots fire prevention and firefighting forces and specialized fire prevention and firefighting forces

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Failure to ensure the number of personnel and the duration of each shift in terms of fire safety;

  b) The grassroots fire department fails to proficiently use the fire and rescue equipment installed at the grassroots level.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to organize the duty division at the establishment as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Establishing grassroots or specialized fire prevention and firefighting teams that fail to meet requirements as prescribed;

  b) Failing to manage or maintain the operation of grassroots or specialized fire prevention and firefighting teams as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to participate in fire prevention and firefighting activities when requested by competent persons.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to establish grassroots or specialized fire prevention and firefighting forces as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on production and trading of fire and explosion prevention and fighting means and equipment and fire prevention and fighting design

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed on a fire prevention and firefighting design unit that fails to have legal entity status or professional technical capacity as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed upon an individual or organization that provides fire and explosion protection equipment and devices but fails to satisfy technical requirements or has not been trained in fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of modifying a fire truck or fireboat without permission of a competent authority.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for failure of fire prevention and firefighting installation and equipment installation entities to have legal entity status or professional technical capabilities as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 24. Violations against regulations on compulsory fire insurance

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to take measures to prevent or limit loss of members of compulsory fire insurance policy as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) The entity is required to purchase compulsory fire insurance but fails to do so as prescribed;

  b) Buying compulsory fire insurance against regulations or the premium for compulsory fire insurance issued by the Ministry of Finance;

  c/ The establishments subject to compulsory purchase of fire insurance shall not separate the compulsory fire insurance contract in their comprehensive insurance contracts as prescribed;

  d) Failing to deduct and pay compulsory fire insurance premiums for funding firefighting activities as prescribed.

  Article 25. Acts causing fire or explosion at households

  1. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations which leads to a fire or explosion but causes less than VND 25,000,000 in damage.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations, thereby causing a fire or explosion resulting in losses of from VND 25.000.000 to VND 50.000.000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations which leads to a fire or explosion causing damage assessed at > VND 50.000.000 but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 26. Penalties imposed upon individuals and organizations for causing fires or explosions

  1. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for committing violations against fire safety regulations that lead to a fire or explosion causing damage of less than VND 2,000,000.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations that leads to a fire or explosion causing damage assessed at from VND 2.000.000 to under VND 10.000.000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations which leads to a fire or explosion causing damage of from VND 10,000,000 to under VND 25,000,000.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for causing a fire or explosion unintentionally, thereby causing damage assessed at from VND 25.000.000 to VND 50.000.000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for causing a fire or explosion through irresponsibility but not to the extent of being liable to criminal prosecution.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for unintentionally causing a fire or explosion resulting in damages exceeding VND 50.000.000 without being liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 27. Deportation

  Foreigners who commit administrative violations prescribed in Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 18, 20 and 21 of this Decree may, depending on the seriousness of their violations, be subject to deportation from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  Chapter III

                             POWER TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES

  Article 28. Power to impose penalties of People’s Public Security forces

  1. Soldiers of the People’s Public Security on duty shall have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 200,000.

  2. Heads of stations and leaders of the competent persons specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall have the following powers:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 500,000.

  3. The Chief of the commune, ward or township Police (hereinafter referred to as commune-level) has the right:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 2,000,000.

  c/ Confiscating material evidence and means used in administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original condition which has been altered by the administrative violation.

  4. Heads of police offices of urban districts, rural districts, towns and provincial cities (hereinafter referred to as district-level police offices) shall have the following powers:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 10,000,000.

  c) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;

  d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  5. Heads of Fire and Rescue Police Divisions, Heads of Police Divisions for Administrative Management of Social Order, Heads of Road and Rail Traffic Police Divisions, Heads of Waterway Police Divisions, Heads of Police Divisions for Prevention and Control of Environmental Crimes, Heads of Police Divisions for Investigation into Social Order-related Crimes, provincial-level Police Departments and Heads of Fire and Rescue Police Divisions of district level under the provincial-level Fire and Rescue Police Department shall, within the ambit of their functions, tasks and powers, have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 10,000,000.

  c) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;

  d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  6. Directors of Police Departments of provinces and central-affiliated cities (hereinafter referred to as “provincial level”) and Directors of Fire Prevention and Fighting Police Departments have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 30,000,000.

  c) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;

  d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for administrative violations;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  e) Directors of provincial police authorities have the power to decide on the imposition of the deportation penalty under the delegation of the Minister of Public Security.

  7. Directors of the Fire and Rescue Police Department, the Police Department for Administrative Management of Social Order, the Road and Railway Traffic Police Department, the Waterway Police Department, the Environmental Crime Prevention and Control Police Department, and the Investigation Police Department for Social Order Crimes shall, within their functions, tasks and powers, have the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b/ A fine of up to the maximum level prescribed at Point a, Clause 2, Article 14 of the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  c) Revoke licenses and practicing certificates within their competence;

  d) Confiscate goods and means used for committing administrative violations;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  8. The Minister of Public Security shall decide to impose the penalty of deportation.

  Article 29. Authority to impose administrative penalties of Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

  1. The President of the commune-level People’s Committee has the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 2,000,000.

  c/ Confiscating material evidence and means used in administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original condition which has been altered by the administrative violation.

  2. The chairpersons of the district-level People’s Committees have the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Confiscating material evidence and means used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Revoke licenses or practicing certificates within their competence;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  3. The President of the People’s Committee of a province shall have the right to:

  a) Warning;

  b/ A fine of up to the maximum level prescribed at Point a, Clause 2, Article 14 of the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  c/ Confiscating goods and means used in administrative violations;

  d) Revoke licenses or practicing certificates within their competence;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  Article 30. Authority to impose administrative penalties of other agencies

  Apart from persons with sanctioning competence prescribed in Articles 28 and 29 of this Decree, persons with sanctioning competence as prescribed in the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, when performing their functions and tasks within the areas and domains under their management and detecting administrative violations prescribed in this Decree, shall have the right to impose sanctions according to law provisions.

  Article 31. Authorization for imposition of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting

  Persons having authority to impose administrative penalties prescribed in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 Article 28; Clauses 1, 2 and 3 Article 29 of this Decree may authorize their deputies to exercise their authority to impose administrative penalties. The authorization must be made in writing. Authorized deputies shall be responsible for their decisions on imposition of administrative penalties before their heads and before law.

  Article 32. Principles for determining authority to impose administrative penalties

  1. The authority to impose administrative penalties under this Decree is the authority applicable to a specific administrative violation. In case of fines, the authority to impose penalties shall be determined based on the maximum fine bracket prescribed for each specific violation. If an administrative violation falls within the authority to impose penalties of multiple persons, the penalty shall be imposed by the first person to receive and handle the violation.

  2. In case of sanctioning a person for committing many administrative violations, the sanctioning competence shall be determined according to the following principle:

  a) If the sanctioning form and level prescribed for each act are under the authority of the sanctioning person, the sanctioning authority shall still belong to such person;

  b/ If the sanctioning form and level prescribed for one of acts of violation exceeds the competence of the sanctioning person, he/she shall have to transfer the violation case to the competent sanctioning level.

  3. In case a person with sanctioning competence of the People's Public Security is handling an individual or organization committing violations against fire prevention and fighting under this Decree but discovers that such individual or organization also commits administrative violations prescribed in other Government's Decrees, he/she may sanction such administrative violations.

  Chapter IV

                         PROCEDURES FOR IMPOSING ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES

  Article 33. Procedures for sanctioning administrative violations

  1. Upon detecting an administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting, the person with sanctioning competence shall immediately order the cessation of the violation.

  2. Procedures for sanctioning administrative violations shall be carried out as follows:

  a/ For administrative violations which are sanctioned by warning or a fine of up to VND 200,000, the persons with sanctioning competence shall issue on the spot decisions to sanction them according to simple procedures prescribed in Article 54 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  b/ For administrative violations which are sanctioned with fines of over VND 200,000, the persons with sanctioning competence shall have to make records on such administrative violations according to the provisions in Article 55 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and issue sanctioning decisions according to the provisions in Article 56 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations; if the violation falls beyond the sanctioning competence of the record maker, he/she shall have to send the record to the person with sanctioning competence for issuing a sanctioning decision according to regulations.

  c) The forms used for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting shall be applied uniformly according to the forms prescribed in the field of security, social order and safety.

  3. When imposing a fine, the specific fine level for an administrative violation shall be the average level of the fine bracket applicable to such act prescribed in this Decree; in case the violation involves extenuating circumstances, the fine level may be reduced but not reduced below the minimum level of the fine bracket; in case the violation involves aggravating circumstances, the fine level may be increased but not increased above the maximum level of the fine bracket already prescribed. When imposing a fine, the offender must be informed of the fine bracket and the specific fine level.

  4. For a minor from full 16 to under 18 years of age who commits an administrative violation and is subject to a fine, the fine level must not exceed half of the fine level applicable to a adult; in case he/she has no money for payment, his/her parents or guardian shall pay instead.

  Article 34. Application of measures to prevent administrative violations and ensure the imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting

  In case of timely prevention of administrative violations or assurance of sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting, apply measures to temporarily detain persons; temporarily detain material evidences and means used for committing administrative violations; search persons; search transport means and objects; search places where material evidences and means used for committing administrative violations are hidden according to the provisions of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 35. Collection of fines for administrative violations

  1. Individuals and organizations fined for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting shall pay fines within the time limit at State Treasuries stated in sanctioning decisions, except for cases where fines have been paid on the spot as prescribed in Article 54 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and are entitled to receive fine receipts. The collection of fines must use receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance under regulations.

  2. In remote areas, on rivers or seas, where travel is difficult or outside working hours, sanctioned individuals and organizations may pay fines to persons with sanctioning competence. Persons with sanctioning competence shall collect fines on the spot and remit them to the State Treasury under Clause 3, Article 58 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. Sanctioned persons may delay paying fines if they do not have fine collection receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance under regulations.

  Article 36. Execution of decisions on administrative penalties

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting must comply with sanctioning decisions within 10 days from the date on which they are issued with sanctioning decisions. If individuals and organizations sanctioned fail to voluntarily comply with sanctioning decisions after the above time limit, they shall be forced to comply with sanctioning decisions as prescribed by law.

  2. Individuals who are sanctioned with fines of VND 500,000 or more may postpone the execution of the sanctioning decisions according to the provisions in Article 65 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. When it is deemed necessary, the person having authority to impose penalties or the agency imposing penalties on individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting may publicly notify the violation or decision on imposition of penalties to the agency, organization or local government where the violating individual works or resides, and to the superior agency directly managing or the local government where the violating organization registers its operations.

  Article 37. Revocation of the right to use licenses and practicing certificates

  The revocation of the right to use licenses and practicing certificates shall be effected only for violations prescribed in this Decree and other Government's decrees on sanctioning administrative violations related to fire prevention and fighting and must comply with the provisions of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 38.- Transferring dossiers on violation cases with criminal signs for penal liability examination In case of considering a violation case for deciding on sanctioning, if deeming that the violation act shows signs of a crime or in case where a sanctioning decision has been issued but later detecting that the violation act shows signs of a crime and the statute of limitations for penal liability examination has not yet expired, the dossier on the violation case with criminal signs must be transferred to the competent criminal proceedings-conducting agency according to the provisions of Article 62 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 39. Complaints and denunciations

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting or their lawful representatives have the right to lodge complaints about decisions on sanctioning administrative violations, decisions on application of preventive measures and assurance of handling of administrative violations of competent persons.

  All individuals have the right to denounce illegal acts against regulations on administrative penalties for violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting to competent state agencies.

  2. Complaints and denunciations shall be made and complaints and denunciations shall be settled in accordance with regulations of law on complaints and denunciations.

  3. The initiation of lawsuits against decisions on administrative penalties and decisions on application of preventive measures for ensuring administrative penalties in the field of fire prevention and firefighting shall comply with the provisions of law on administrative procedures.

  Chapter 5.

                                            IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 40. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on August 5, 2012 and replaces Decree No. 123/2005/ND-CP of October 5, 2005 prescribing administrative sanctions against violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting.

  Article 41. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Minister of Public Security shall organize implementation, provide guidance, inspect and urge implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities and relevant organizations and individuals shall be responsible for implementing this Decree./.
