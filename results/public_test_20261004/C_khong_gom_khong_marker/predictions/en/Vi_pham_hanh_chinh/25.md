                                                    DECREE

    REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN SOCIAL SUPPORT AND CHILDCARE FIELD

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization No. 63/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 56/2024/QH15 and Law No. 88/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Children No. 102/2016/QH13 amended by Law No. 28/2018/QH14, Law No. 59/2024/QH15 and Law No. 81/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to Law on Older Persons No. 39/2009/QH12 amended by Law No. 41/2024/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Persons with Disabilities No. 51/2010/QH12 amended by Law No. 32/2013/QH13;

  Pursuant to the Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking No. 53/2024/QH15;

  At the request of the Minister of Health;

  The Government promulgates a Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children affairs.

  Chapter I

                                                GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations; penalties, fines, remedial measures applicable to each administrative violation; entities subject to penalties; authority to impose penalties and specific fines depending on title and authority to issue offense notices; implementation of administrative penalties and remedial measures in social protection and children affairs.

  2. Administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children not specified in this Decree shall be handled according to regulations on administrative penalties for relevant state management sectors.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese organizations and individuals; foreign organizations and individuals committing administrative violations in the field of social protection and children within the territory, territorial waters, contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam; on aircraft bearing Vietnamese nationality or seagoing ships flying the flag of Vietnam, except otherwise provided for by international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory, including:

  a) Individuals committing administrative violations;

  b) Organizations committing administrative violations are juridical persons as prescribed by civil law or other organizations established under law, including: types of social assistance establishments, elderly care facilities, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, victim support facilities, child protection service providers; religious establishments; policy payment service providers; socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations; enterprises, cooperatives, production and business establishments; cultural, entertainment, sports establishments; medical examination and treatment establishments; educational establishments; vocational education and training institutions, and other organizations.

  2. Persons having authority to make records of administrative violations.

  3. Persons having authority to impose administrative penalties.

  4. Agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 3. Prescriptive periods for administrative violations

  1. The prescriptive period for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children is 01 year.

  2. The determination of whether an administrative violation has ended or is ongoing for the purpose of calculating the prescriptive period for imposing penalties shall comply with Clause 1 Article 8 of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP dated December 23, 2021 of the Government on elaboration of certain articles and implementation measures of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  Article 4. Execution of administrative penalties

  1. The execution of decisions on administrative penalties for violations against regulations on social protection and children shall comply with the Law on Handling Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 56/2024/QH15 and Law No. 88/2025/QH15 (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Handling of Administrative Violations”) and guiding documents.

  2. Administrative penalties for violations against regulations on social protection and children’s affairs in the online environment shall be imposed in accordance with Chapter IIIa of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  3. During the process of considering and handling violations, during the implementation of decisions on administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 8, Article 9, Article 11, Clause 1 Article 12, Point a Clause 2 Article 19, Clause 2 Article 20, Clause 2 Article 22, Clause 1 Article 23, Clause 2 Article 24, Clause 1 Article 25, Clause 1 Article 29, Clause 1 Article 33, Clause 1 Article 36, Points a and b Clause 1 Article 37 of this Decree, if the violation is detected as having criminal signs, the competent person who is handling the case or the person issuing the decision on administrative penalties must immediately transfer the violation dossier to the agency with criminal proceeding jurisdiction for handling according to the provisions of Article 62 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations. In case the agency with criminal proceeding jurisdiction does not conduct criminal prosecution, the violation dossier shall be transferred to the person with authority to impose administrative penalties according to the provisions of Clause 3 Article 62 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations for imposition of administrative penalties according to the provisions of this Decree.

  Article 5. Penalties and remedial measures; implementation of administrative penalties and remedial measures

  1. Main forms of sanction:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fine.

  2. Additional penalties:

  a) Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations;

  b) Suspend part or all of the business activities related to the violation for a period of 1 to 3 months;

  c) Suspend operation for a fixed period of 03 to 06 months.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Remedial measures prescribed in Points a, dd, e and i Clause 1 Article 28 of the Law on Actions against administrative violations;

  b) Enforced cessation of care and nurturing;

  c) Enforced removal, deletion or removal of information, images and documents of human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  d) Compulsory apology upon request;

  dd) Enforced return of illegal collected amounts;

  e) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs;

  g) Enforced repair and supplementation of facilities and personnel as per regulation;

  h) Compulsory payment of benefits as prescribed;

  i) Enforced return of aid money or goods obtained from committing the violation;

  k) Enforced return of disability certificates;

  l) Enforced fulfillment of responsibilities of the council or enforced implementation of methods for determining and re-determining the level of disability;

  m) Enforced determination and re-determination of disability level;

  n) Enforced payment of all expenses for books, notebooks and learning supplies for children;

  o) Enforced provision of adequate programs, syllabi and number of teachers in conformity with vocational education for persons with disabilities;

  p) Enforced issuance of degrees and certificates;

  q) Enforced recall and destruction of products, publications, toys, newspapers, and other publications;

  r) Enforced removal of news articles from online newspapers, magazines, radio and television, and electronic publications;

  s) Enforced termination of substitute care or change to more appropriate substitute care;

  t) Enforced relocation of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods or goods with risk of fire and explosion, child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and recreational facilities for children;

  u) Enforced dismantling of works and equipment;

  v) Enforced timely implementation of safety assurance measures;

  x) Enforced removal of information and services harmful to children; enforced removal of fake, distorted services and applications infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of children on the internet;

  y) Enforced revocation, deletion or removal of information on private lives and personal secrets of children.

  4. The implementation of penalties and remedial measures prescribed in this Decree shall comply with Section 2 and Section 3 Chapter III of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations and Articles 17a, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, and Article 41 of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  Article 6. Fines and authority to impose fines

  1. The maximum fine for a violation against regulations on social protection and children affairs imposed upon an individual is VND 50.000.000; that imposed upon an organization is VND 100.000.000.

  2. The fine levels prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree are applicable to administrative violations committed by individuals, except for Articles 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 34, Clause 1, Clause 2 Article 35 and Clause 2 Article 37 of this Decree. For the same administrative violation, the fine level imposed on an organization shall be twice as much as that imposed on an individual.

  3. The authority to impose penalties for administrative violations of a title prescribed in Article 39, Article 40, Article 41, Article 42, Article 43, Article 44 and Article 45 of this Decree is the authority applicable to an administrative violation committed by an individual; in case of fines, the authority to impose penalties for administrative violations on organizations is twice as much as the authority to impose penalties on individuals committing the same violation.

  Chapter II

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON SOCIAL SECURITY

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on claiming social assistance policies, social pension benefits, and support for human trafficking victims

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed on an individual for committing fraudulent acts in order to receive emergency social assistance but not to the extent of being liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for committing fraudulent victim fabrication and engaging in human trafficking for one of the following support policies: formal education, vocational training or allowance for initial difficulties, if not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Making false declarations to receive monthly social allowances but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Making false declarations to receive monthly social pension allowances but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Making false declarations to receive monthly care and nurturing funding support but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Making false declarations in order to be admitted to social support facilities, child protection service providers, elderly care facilities, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, and human trafficking victim support facilities.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing the violations specified in Clause 1, Clause 2, Point a, Point b, Point c Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced cessation of care and support at social assistance facilities, child protection service providers, elderly care facilities, facilities for persons with disabilities, and human trafficking victim support facilities, in respect of violations under Point d Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on support for human trafficking victims and persons undergoing victim identification

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Obstructing the reception of human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  b) Obstructing the provision of support to human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  c) Insult, stigmatize, or discriminate against victims of human trafficking or persons undergoing victim identification.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for threatening a human trafficking victim, a person undergoing victim identification, their relative, witness, denouncer, informant, whistleblower, or person preventing human trafficking acts as prescribed in Article 3 of the Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking, if not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for disclosing information about a human trafficking victim’s name, age, phone number, social media accounts, address, place of residence, workplace, native place, form of human trafficking, health condition, image, and personal documents, or a person undergoing victim identification without their consent, if not liable to criminal prosecution, except where such disclosure is necessary to protect the disclosed individual’s life or health or as otherwise provided by law.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced revocation, deletion or removal of information, images and documents of human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced apology for human trafficking victims, persons undergoing victim identification, their relatives, witnesses, denouncers, informants, denunciators, denouncers, or individuals preventing violations as requested in case of violations under Clauses 1, 2, and 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on responsibilities for care and nurturing of social protection beneficiaries, older persons, persons with disabilities, and children in the community

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on an individual who takes in and cares for a person receiving community-based care but is not criminally prosecuted for such act.

  a) Forcing them to abstain from eating or drinking; preventing or restricting their personal hygiene;

  b) Being kept alive in a toxic and dangerous environment;

  c) To abuse, ill-treat, hurt, persecute or discriminate against social protection beneficiaries, older persons, persons with disabilities and children.

  2. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed on persons who stand to receive care and nurture but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability as follows:

  a) Taking advantage of care and nurturing to seek personal benefits;

  b) Compulsory provision of care and support for persons who work in arduous, hazardous or dangerous jobs.

  3. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 40,000,000 shall be imposed on persons who stand to receive care and nurture but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability as follows:

  a) Entice or induce recipients of care and nurture to commit violations of law;

  b) Compulsory provision of care and support to violators.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by the persons whose health is affected due to the violations prescribed in Clause 1, Point b Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on operation of social support facilities, facilities for care of the elderly, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, victim support facilities, and child protection service providers (hereinafter referred to as “Facilities”)

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed on a storage facility failing to adequately store all documents and records of persons receiving for nurture or care as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed on establishments committing one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to submit periodic reports on the operation of the facility as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to provide or providing low-quality items for personal daily activities, including: blankets, mosquito nets, mats, summer clothing, winter clothing, undergarments, towels, shoes, toilet brushes, common medicines; monthly personal hygiene items for female individuals, books, study supplies for students, and other items as prescribed by law.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed on establishments committing one of the following acts:

  a) Misusing funding and facilities of the facility;

  b) Collecting service fees in violation of regulations of law;

  c/ Failing to satisfy one of environmental, medical, material and personnel conditions;

  d) Failing to comply with service provision procedures promulgated by competent authorities.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Point a Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of illegal collected amounts to entities in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;

  c) Enforced repair, supplementation, and assurance of physical and human resources as per regulation in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on responsibilities of service providers

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Monthly social allowances, monthly care and nurturing expenses, monthly social pension, burial expense support (referred to as policy regime) provided to the beneficiaries are insufficient according to regulations of law but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Spending on policies for beneficiaries beyond the prescribed time limit as per the law but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to pay policy benefits to beneficiaries but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Spending on policies for ineligible beneficiaries but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forcible payment of policy benefits to eligible entities for violations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on management of aid money and supplies

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations which, however, is not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) to damage or lose money or aid, except force majeure events such as natural disasters or fires;

  b) Using and distributing aid money or goods for wrong purposes or to wrong beneficiaries;

  c/ Swapping aid items.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of the amount or compensation for the value of aid products that are damaged or lost due to the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by beneficiaries whose health is affected due to violations specified in Points a and c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on prohibited acts for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) To stigmatize and discriminate against persons with disabilities;

  b) Obstructing the marriage and child adoption rights of persons with disabilities;

  c) Obstructing persons with disabilities from living independently and integrating into the community;

  d) Impeding persons with disabilities from participating on an equal basis in social activities;

  dd) Obstructing persons with disabilities from exercising their right to access information technology.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to fulfill their responsibilities to nurture and care for persons with disabilities as prescribed by law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of the image, personal information, or condition of persons with disabilities or organizations of persons with disabilities or organizations for persons with disabilities for self-seeking purposes, or committing other violations of law which, however, are not serious enough for criminal prosecution.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for enticing, persuading or forcing persons with disabilities to commit violations of law but not to the extent of being liable to criminal prosecution.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced fulfillment of responsibilities to nurture and care for persons with disabilities in accordance with law.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on health care responsibilities for persons with disabilities of medical examination and treatment establishments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to provide counseling on measures to prevent and detect disabilities early.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to identify congenital disabilities in newborns so as to promptly take appropriate treatment and rehabilitation measures.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to renovate or upgrade medical facilities to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities when renovating or repairing medical facilities.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on educational responsibilities of educational institutions for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to ensure teaching and learning conditions suitable for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to provide learning support facilities and materials specifically for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  c) Do not allow persons with disabilities to be exempted from or reduced in the number of subjects or contents and educational activities which they cannot handle;

  d) Refuse to admit persons with disabilities at an older age in accordance with law;

  dd) Impose conditions on enrolment that restrict persons with disabilities, except for certain cases as prescribed by law;

  e) Failing to prioritize enrollment for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  g) Obstructing the right to learn of persons with disabilities.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to provide exemption or reduction of tuition fees, training costs and other contributions in accordance with the law on education;

  b) Failure to renovate or upgrade teaching and learning facilities in a manner that ensures accessibility for persons with disabilities when renovating or repairing educational institutions.

  Article 16. Violations against regulations on vocational education and training activities of vocational education and training institutions for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to provide employment counseling for persons with disabilities in accordance with law.

  2. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to maintain conditions for vocational education and training activities as prescribed for persons with disabilities after organizing training activities for at least 6 months;

  b) Failing to provide adequate programs, syllabi or qualified teachers for vocational education and training for persons with disabilities;

  c) Failing to ensure appropriate training forms and time for vocational education and training for persons with disabilities;

  d) Failing to issue diplomas or certificates when persons with disabilities are eligible for issuance of diplomas or certificates as prescribed by law.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced provision of adequate vocational education and training programs, syllabi, and qualified teachers for persons with disabilities in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced issuance of degrees or certificates in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 17. Violations against regulations on traffic participation for persons with disabilities and older persons

  A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  1. The sale of tickets to persons with disabilities and the elderly shall not be prioritized.

  2. Persons who are responsible as per the law but fail to assist or arrange seats suitable for persons with disabilities and the elderly.

  3. Refusal to transport persons with disabilities or refusal to transport appropriate assistive devices and equipment for persons with disabilities by public means of transportation.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on information and communication for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on enterprises manufacturing and distributing information and communication products, including hardware, software, and digital content, for committing one of the violations against regulations on mandatory application of standards and technologies to support persons with disabilities in accessing and using information and communication products.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to apply eligible beneficiaries of concessional loan policies and other support policies for research, development, manufacturing, provision of services and assistive devices for persons with disabilities to access information technology and communication.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on determination of disability level

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to fully exercise their responsibilities as the Council for determination of disability level;

  b) Failing to adequately implement methods for determining and re-determining the level of disability.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Taking advantage of determination of disability level for self-seeking purposes but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Refuse to determine or re-determine the level of disability, issue a certificate of disability, reissue a certificate of disability;

  c) Committing fraud in determining or re-determining the level of disability, issuing a certificate of disability, or reissuing a certificate of disability.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced fulfillment of responsibilities of the council in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced full implementation of methods for determining and re-determining the level of disability in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Points a and c Clause 2 of this Article;

  d) Enforced determination and re-determination of the level of disability in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article;

  dd) Enforced return of disability certificates in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on rights and obligations of older persons

  1. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Obstructing older persons who live with their children or grandchildren or live separately;

  b) Obstructing older persons from exercising their rights to own property, participate in cultural, educational, physical training, sports, entertainment, tourism, rest and other lawful rights;

  c/ Not exempting the contributions to social activities for the elderly;

  d) Do not prioritize older persons receiving money, aid, healthcare, and accommodation to overcome initial difficulties when older persons face difficulties due to natural disasters or other force majeure events;

  dd) Failing to pay support amounts provided by the State for older persons as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts: forcing to abstain from eating or drinking; preventing or restricting personal hygiene; forcing to live in a toxic and dangerous environment or committing other forms of maltreatment against older persons which, however, is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of illegal collected amounts to the elderly for violations under Point c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced payment of benefits as prescribed in case of the violations specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by older persons whose health is affected as a result of commission of the violation in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on care for older persons

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to fulfill their obligations to care for and support older persons in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to fully implement commitments under service contracts for caring for older persons in accordance with law;

  c) Taking advantage of caring for and supporting older persons for personal gain but not to the extent of criminal prosecution.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 2. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON CHILDREN

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on care for children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Parents and caregivers fail to fulfill their obligations and responsibilities in caring for and raising children, except for cases where they are temporarily isolated from the children or the children are placed under another caregiver as prescribed by law;

  b) Parents or caregivers do not care for, nurture, or fulfill their obligations and responsibilities in caring for, nurturing, or abandoning children to live on their own, severing emotional and material ties with children, or forcing children to not live with their families, except in cases where children must be temporarily isolated or have substitute caregivers as per the law.

  2. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 25,000,000 shall be imposed on fathers, mothers or children who intentionally abandon their children but not seriously enough to be examined for penal liability.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on violence against children

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for any violation which, however, is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability:

  a) Forcing them to abstain from eating or drinking; preventing or restricting their personal hygiene;

  b) Keeping children alive in a toxic or dangerous environment or subjected to other forms of ill-treatment;

  c) Causing mental damage, infringing upon dignity and honor, insulting, abusing, threatening, isolating, affecting children’s development;

  d/ To isolate, repulse or use measures to punish children in order to harm their physical and mental health;

  dd) Regularly threatening children with images, sounds, animals or objects, causing them fear and mental harm.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of committing the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article which causes injury or damage to children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Enforced destruction of articles harmful to children’s health in case of commission of the violation specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 24. Violations against regulations on child exploitation

  1. A fine of 20,000,000 to 30,000,000 VND for one of acts of forcing children to do housework beyond their capacity, beyond time, affecting their study, entertainment and recreation, adversely affecting their development.

  2. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 40,000,000 for any one of the following acts which, however, is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability:

  a/ Organizing and forcing children to beg;

  b/ Leasing or lending children or using them for begging;

  c) Leading, inciting, persuading, enticing, inducing, provoking, abusing, forcing to act as intermediary in child exploitation transactions;

  d) Leading, inciting, persuading, enticing, inducing, provoking, abusing, or forcing children to work against the law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of children’s images or personal information to create content that causes harm to their physical and mental development or for the purpose of exploitation, if not liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of committing violations specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article which cause injury or damage to their health but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 25. Violations against regulations on prohibiting using, inciting, persuading, abusing, enticing, coercing children to commit violations of law or hurting others’ honor and dignity

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for any one of the following acts which, however, is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability:

  a) Using children to commit violations of law, or to hurt the dignity and honor of others;

  b) Inciting children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of others;

  c) Inciting or encouraging children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of others;

  d) Taking advantage of, enticing or deceiving children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of others;

  dd) Forcing children to commit acts of law violation, hurting honor and dignity of other people.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Forced to pay all expenses for medical examination and treatment for children for violations specified in clause 1 of this Article which cause injury or damage to children's health but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability.

  Article 26. Violations against regulations on children’s right to health care

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to ensure appropriate nutrition suitable for children’s physical and mental development according to each age group;

  b/ Failing to perform or partially performing the initial health care, disease prevention, counseling and support for children in reproductive and sexual health care.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to provide or obstructing pregnant women from accessing healthcare services for counseling on prenatal screening and prevention of congenital diseases in children;

  b) Failing to comply with decisions, measures and regulations of competent agencies or individuals to ensure the safety of children’s lives and health;

  c/ Applying customs and practices which are harmful to children’s health;

  d) Failing to prioritize medical examination and treatment for children.

  Article 27. Violations against regulations on children’s right to education

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Destroying books, notebooks and school supplies of children;

  b) Intentionally failing to fulfill the obligation to contribute to children’s learning in accordance with the law;

  c/ Failing to ensure time and study conditions for children.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for obstructing children’s attendance at school.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of enticing, dragging, forcing children to drop out of school.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced payment of all expenses for books, notebooks and learning supplies for children in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 28. Violations against regulations on children’s right to access information and participate in social activities; right to express opinions; right to preserve and promote their identity; right to play and entertain themselves; prohibition of stigmatization and discrimination against children

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Obstructing children from participating in social activities appropriate to their age, maturity level, needs and capacity, except where such obstruction is in the best interests of the children;

  b/ Obstructing children to express their opinions, aspirations and participate in matters concerning children;

  c) Obstructing the right to preserve and promote identity;

  d) Obstructing the right to enjoy, relax, participate in cultural, artistic, physical training, sports and tourism activities as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Forcing and repressing children when they express opinions, aspirations and participate in matters concerning children;

  b) Failing to respect, listen to, consider, respond to, or explain children’s opinions and aspirations;

  c) Do not receive, process, or respond to opinions, recommendations, and aspirations of children, or represent the voices of children as prescribed;

  d) Failing to disclose or to accurately disclose information on study and training plans, care regimes and contributions as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for stigmatizing or discriminating against children due to their personal characteristics, family circumstances, gender, ethnicity, nationality, belief or religion.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced correction of false or misleading information in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 29. Violations against regulations on support and intervention for children in special circumstances, children who are victims of abuse or children at risk of exploitation, violence, abandonment, and other forms of abuse

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for any one of the following acts, which shall not be examined for penal liability:

  a) Failing to notify or provide information about children at risk of exploitation, violence and other forms of abuse to competent authorities or individuals;

  b) Impede the provision of information on children at risk of exploitation, violence and other forms of abuse to competent authorities and individuals.

  2. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to provide or concealing information on children who are victims of abuse for competent authorities or individuals without facing criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to notify the receiving authority of the act of child abuse;

  c) Impede the provision of information on the child victim to competent authorities and individuals;

  d) Failing to provide information and cooperate in conducting verification of the authenticity of acts of infringement, the state of insecurity, and the extent of the risk of causing harm to children when requested by competent authorities or individuals;

  dd) Failing to keep confidential information, notices and denunciations of acts of child abuse.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to adequately and promptly provide support or intervention in cases where children are subjected to abuse, face the risk of violence, exploitation, abandonment, or have exceptional circumstances;

  b) Refuse to provide support, intervene, or provide alternative care for children who are victims of abuse or at risk of violence, exploitation, abandonment, and children with special circumstances.

  Article 30. Violations against regulations on sale of addictive substances, other stimulants, unsafe foods to children or use of these substances by children; violations against regulations on provision of safe and friendly products and services for children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Providing unsafe products or services that cause harm to children;

  b) Selling addictive substances, other stimulants, unsafe foods, and foods harmful to children;

  c/ Letting children use addictive substances, other stimulants, unsafe and harmful foods for them.

  2. Additional penalties:

  a) Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations for violations prescribed in clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Suspend part or all of the business operation related to the violation for a period of between 01 and 03 months if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 1 of this Article is committed.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced destruction of goods and items in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of committing the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article which causes injury or damage to children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced transfer of an amount equal to the value of exhibits of administrative violations which have been unlawfully sold, liquidated, hidden or destroyed in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 31. Violations against regulations on provision of cultural, information and communication products and services with contents affecting children’s healthy development

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Provide internet services and other services with contents affecting children’s healthy development;

  b) Do not provide notes on children’s refusal to use publications, products or toys if such publications, products or toys contain inappropriate content for children; do not provide warnings about inappropriate content for children in digital products on the internet;

  c/ Failing to notify children of their age to refrain from watching or listening to radio, television programs, art and cinematographic works and performances if they contain contents inappropriate for children;

  d) Failing to comply with requests for recording information on publications intended for children.

  2. To impose a fine on one of acts of producing, publishing, copying, circulating, operating, distributing, owning, transporting, storing or trading in publications, toys, games and digital contents on cyberspace and other products serving children with contents affecting their healthy development, at one of the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for committing the violation with respect to 01 - 10 products;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for committing the violation with respect to 10 - 50 products;

  c) A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for committing the violation with between 50 and under 100 products;

  d) VND 15,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 when from 100 products to less than 200 products are illegally exported;

  dd) VND 20,000,000 - VND 30,000,000 for a violation involving from 200 products to under 500 products;

  e) VND 30,000,000 - VND 40,000,000 when the violation involves from 500 products to less than 1,000 products;

  g) VND 40,000,000 - VND 50,000,000 for a violation involving ≥ 1,000 products.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to issue or issuing inappropriate warnings about children’s content on radio, television programs, press and publications.

  4. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations for violations prescribed in Points b and d Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced removal of news articles from online newspapers, magazines, radio and television, and electronic publications, in respect of the violations prescribed in Points b and d Clause 1 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced recall and destruction of products, publications, toys, newspapers, and other printed materials in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;

  d) Enforced transfer of an amount equal to the value of exhibits of administrative violations which have been unlawfully sold, dispersed or destroyed in case of commission of the violations specified in Points b and d Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 32. Violations against regulations on disclosure of information about children’s private lives and personal secrets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for disclosing or revealing any of the following information without consent from the child’s parents, caregiver or guardian and the child aged 7 years or older, including: name, age; personal identification characteristics; health conditions and private life recorded in medical records; personal images; family members, caregivers; personal property; phone number; personal postal address; residential address, native place; school, class, study results and friends; services provided for the child.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced revocation, deletion or removal of information on private life and personal secrets of children in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 33. Violations against regulations on prevention of abuse of child adoption to harm children; abuse of policies and regulations of the State and assistance provided by organizations and individuals for children for self-seeking purposes

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for any one of the following acts which, however, is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability:

  a) Taking advantage of child replacement care to commit acts of causing physical, emotional, psychological, honorable or dignity-related harms to children in the forms of violence, exploitation, abandonment, neglect and other forms of harm;

  b) Taking advantage of the State’s regimes and policies and the support and assistance provided by organizations and individuals for children to seek personal benefits.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of committing the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article which causes injury or damage to children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Point b Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced termination of substitute care or change to appropriate substitute care for the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 34. Violations against regulations on construction of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage facilities containing hazardous goods or those in danger of fire or explosion near child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and places of entertainment for children, or vice versa

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Construct works and install equipment of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods with risk of direct fire or explosion near child protection service providers, education institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and entertainment venues for children;

  b) Construct works and install equipment of child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and entertainment venues located near service providers, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods or goods with fire and explosion risks.

  2. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Establish service facilities, manufacturing facilities, and storage facilities for hazardous goods or goods with a risk of direct fire or explosion near child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and children’s entertainment venues;

  b) Establish child protection service facilities, education, medical, cultural, and entertainment facilities near service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods or goods with fire and explosion risks.

  3. Additional penalties:

  Suspend operation for a fixed period of 03 to 06 months in case of commission of any violation specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of works and equipment in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced relocation of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods or goods with fire and explosion risks, child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and entertainment venues due to incorrect placement as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 35. Violations against regulations on child protection service providers and management and use of infrastructure for children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on an infant care service provider operating beyond the registered scope of operation.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on an infant care service provider that operates before obtaining registration or fails to register for operation.

  3. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Appropriating infrastructure for learning, entertainment, recreation and child protection services;

  b) Using infrastructure for learning, entertainment, recreation and child protection services in a wrongful or unlawful manner.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of works and equipment in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of original condition in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 36. Violations against regulations on failure to ensure safety leading to accidents or injuries for children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to notify or warn children of dangerous, toxic, flammable, explosive, electric shock, falling, tripping, and other risks leading to accidents or injuries but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to fully and promptly implement measures to ensure safety in production, business and circulation of means of transport leading to accidents or injuries to children but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced prompt implementation of safety measures for violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article which cause injury or damage to their health but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 37. Violations against regulations on child protection in cyberspace

  1. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for any violation in trading or providing services on network environment;

  a) Providing or sharing information or services harmful to children, or information or services that are fake, toxic, pornographic, violent, suicide incitement, distorted, threatening, offending honor and dignity, infringing upon legitimate rights and interests of children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to issue warnings or remove information or services harmful to children, or information or services that are fake, toxic, pornographic, violent, inciting suicide, distorted, threatening, offending honor and dignity, infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Do not use measures and tools to ensure safety of personal privacy information of children; do not send warning messages when children provide or change their personal privacy information;

  d) Failing to comply with requests for deletion or removal of information on private life secrets of children when such requests are made by parents, caregivers, guardians of children or competent agencies, organizations and individuals responsible for child protection;

  dd) Failing to organize the receipt of information and assessment and classification of safety levels for children sent by agencies, organizations, individuals and children;

  e) Failing to publish lists of online information networks, services and products according to the level of safety for children;

  g) Failing to detect and remove images, documents, or information that is inappropriate for children or affects their healthy development;

  h/ Failing to guide the use of information technology services and equipment for child protection;

  i) No measures to protect persons accessing information or using services who are children.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on online video game service providers that fail to install time control tools or child protection measures against excessive and compulsive use of video games. If online video game service providers fail to implement measures to limit the playing time of G1 games for children, they shall incur administrative penalties in accordance with the Government’s Decree on administrative penalties for violations against regulations on postal services, telecommunications, radio frequencies, information technology, and electronic transactions.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of committing the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article which causes injury or damage to children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced removal of information and services harmful to children; enforced removal of counterfeit, distorted services and applications infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of children on the internet (including data deletion, application removal from servers or application stores; domain name closure or implementation of other technical measures to prevent users' access) for violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Enforced removal of information on private life of children in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article.

  Chapter IV

                                                               IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 47. Effect

  1. This Decree comes into force as of May 16, 2026.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 130/2021/ND-CP dated December 30, 2021 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  Article 48. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Minister of Health shall guide, inspect, urge and organize the implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities, relevant organizations and individuals are responsible for implementing this Decree.
