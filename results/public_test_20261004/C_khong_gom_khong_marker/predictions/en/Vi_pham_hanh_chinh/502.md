                                                    DECREE

  Chapter 1.

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- Administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and the prevention and combat of a number of social evils.

  Administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of some social evils prescribed in this Decree include administrative violations in circulation, trading of films, video tapes and disks, music tapes and disks; selling and leasing publications; cultural activities and cultural services at public places; advertising and writing, putting up signboards; prevention of prostitution, drug abuse and gambling evils.

  Article 2.- To combat administrative violations in cultural activities and cultural services in order to prevent and combat a number of social evils.

  All violations in cultural activities, cultural services and social evils prevention and combat must be detected in time and stopped immediately. The sanctioning must be conducted quickly and fairly; all consequences caused by administrative violations must be remedied according to law.

  Article 3.- Subjects to be handled for administrative violations.

  Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of some social evils shall be sanctioned under forms and levels prescribed in this Decree.

  The heads of State agencies and organizations, State officials and employees who commit acts of violation prescribed in this Decree shall, in addition to administrative sanctions, be disciplined under law.

  Article 4.- Application of sanctioning forms and measures.

  1. When imposing a fine on an administrative violation, the specific fine level shall be the average level of the fine bracket prescribed for such violation; if the violation involves extenuating circumstances, the fine level may be reduced but not lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation involves aggravating circumstances, the fine level may be increased but not higher than the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  The extenuating and aggravating circumstances shall be applied according to the provisions of Article 7 and Article 8 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Additional penalties and other measures shall be applied in conjunction with primary penalties if this Decree provides for additional penalties and application of other measures to administrative violations in order to thoroughly handle violations, eliminate causes and conditions for continued violations and remedy all consequences caused by administrative violations.

  All films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, books, newspapers, pictures, calendars and other cultural products with indecent, pornographic or violent contents must be confiscated and destroyed.

  Article 5.- Handling of violations committed by persons competent to sanction administrative violations.

  + Those who have improper authority or impose sanctions beyond their competence shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, be disciplined or examined for penal liability; if material damage is caused, they must pay compensation according to law provisions.   Section 3. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS OF SOCIAL SECURITY, PENALTIES AND FINES

  Article 18.- Sanctions against acts of buying and selling sex, sheltering and/or acting as intermediary in prostitution.

  1. Those who commit acts of buying and selling sex shall be sanctioned in accordance with the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Article 4 of the Government’s Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  Persons who commit acts of buying and selling sex but not on a regular basis shall be considered by the presidents of the commune-level People’s Committees of the localities where such persons reside for decision to apply measures of education at communes, wards or townships.

  2. For persons who have repeatedly been educated by the local authorities and people but still refuse to reform themselves, the commune-level People’s Committee presidents of the localities where such persons reside shall compile dossiers thereon for the district-level People’s Committee presidents to propose the provincial-level People’s Committee presidents to send them to medical treatment establishments or reformatories, if they are minors.

  3. Procedures for applying the measure of education at communes, wards or townships, or sending violators to reformatories or medical treatment establishments shall comply with the law on handling of administrative violations.

  4. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 shall be imposed for act of brothel dealing but not regularly yet not serious enough to be examined for penal liability.

  5. A fine of between VND 1,500,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for act of brokering prostitution with regular nature but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability.

  6. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for act of prostitution which is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability.

  Article 19.- Sanctions against acts of using, producing, storing, trading in and transporting narcotics.

  1. Those who use narcotics in whatever form shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5 of the Government’s Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  Persons who use narcotics but not to the extent of addiction shall be considered and decided by the presidents of the commune-level People’s Committees of the localities where such persons reside to apply measures of education at communes, wards or townships.

  2. For drug addicts who have been educated by the local authorities and people many times but refuse to reform themselves, the presidents of the commune-level People’s Committees of the localities where such persons reside shall compile dossiers thereon for the presidents of the district-level People’s Committees to propose the provincial-level People’s Committees to send them to medical treatment establishments or reformatories if they are minors.

  3. Procedures for applying the measure of education at communes, wards or townships, or sending violators to reformatories or medical treatment establishments shall comply with the law on handling of administrative violations.

  4. Those who illegally produce, store, trade in or transport narcotics which is not serious enough to be examined for penal liability shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Clause 3, Article 5 of the Government’s Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  5. Additional penalties and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used for committing violations prescribed in Clauses 1, 2 and 4.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for any of the following acts:

  a/ Leasing or lending certificates and professional technical diplomas in massage and physiotherapy;

  b/ Practicing massage without a certificate or professional technical certificate in massage or physiotherapy or using another person's certificate or certificate.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for employing persons who do not possess certificates or professional/technical diplomas in massage or physiotherapy to provide massage services.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for transferring the certificate of eligibility for massage practice to another establishment or using the certificate of another establishment to provide massage services.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for providing massage services without obtaining a certificate of qualification and conditions for massage practice.

  5. Additional penalties and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  Revoke certificates and professional-technical certificates in massage, physiotherapy or certificates of qualification and conditions for massage practice in case of violations specified in Clauses 1 and 3.

  Article 22.- Violations of regulations on the prevention and combat of social evils in the operation of accommodation establishments and restaurants.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for any of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to number rooms of accommodation establishments and restaurants according to the registered plans;

  b/ Displaying pornographic and obscene pictures and/or photos or inciting violence at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  c/ Failing to register accommodation for their guests;

  d/ Failing to declare temporary residence as prescribed for guests staying in accommodation establishments;

  dd. Closing and locking doors inside dining rooms and drinking rooms of accommodation establishments and restaurants at the time when they are occupied by guests;

  e/ Using dim lights or insufficient lighting as prescribed in dining rooms, drinking rooms, entertainment and recreation rooms of accommodation establishments and restaurants;

  g/ Using erotic service methods at accommodation establishments and restaurants;

  h/ Failing to register the list of waiters and staff members with competent police offices.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for employing a person who works at a restaurant accommodation establishment but fails to enter into an employment contract.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Circulating music tapes and discs which have been banned from circulation or withdrawn under decisions issued at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  b/ Circulating music tapes and discs with pornographic or violent contents at accommodation establishments and restaurants.

  4. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Circulating films, video tapes and discs, performing theatrical works, musical works or dance works which have been banned from circulation or withdrawn under decisions issued at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  b/ Circulating films, video tapes and disks, performing theatrical, musical or dance works with pornographic or violent contents at accommodation establishments or restaurants.

  5. Additional penalties and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  a/ Confiscating films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, for violations specified at Point a, Clause 3; Point a, Clause 4;

  b/ Confiscating and destroying pictures, photos, films, video tapes and disks, music tapes and disks, for violations specified at Point b, Clause 1; Point b, Clause 3; Point b, Clause 4;

  c/ Depriving of the right to use business licenses in cases of violation specified in Clauses 3 and 4.

  Depriving the right to use the practicing license for up to 6 months, for performers who commit violations specified in Clause 4.

  6. For violations committed by state-owned accommodation establishments or restaurants, apart from fines and confiscation of exhibits of violations, persons in charge of such establishments or restaurants shall be disciplined according to law provisions.

  Article 23.- To tolerate, cover up, entice and shelter social evils.

  1. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed upon directors of hotels, owners of hotels, guest houses, restaurants, inns, motels, massage establishments for covering up or failing to prevent acts of prostitution, drug abuse, gambling occurring in their establishments.

  Apart from the above-mentioned fines, the violating establishment may be deprived of the right to use its business license; if it is a State official, he/she shall also be disciplined.

  2. The immediate superior of the person who commits acts of buying and selling sex, using narcotics or gambling but covers or fails to promptly discipline the offender; or the person who is tasked with directly combating social evils but tolerates, covers or fails to promptly handle such acts occurring in the area under his/her management shall be disciplined according to the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2, Article 8 of the Government’s Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  3. Directors of hotels, owners of hotels, guest houses, restaurants, inns, motels, massage establishments or anyone else who organizes gambling, facilitates gambling, provides accommodation for, entices or guides prostitutes or uses narcotic substances in any form other than cases specified in Clauses 4, 5 and 6 Article 18; Clause 4 Article 19; Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 Article 20 of this Decree shall face criminal prosecution according to Articles 200, 202 and 203 of the Criminal Code; enticing or accommodating minors for prostitution, drug use or gambling shall face criminal prosecution according to Article 148 of the Criminal Code.

  Section 4. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF PUBLICITY AND COMMERCIAL PROMOTION, FORMS AND LEVELS OF SANCTION

  Article 24.- Violations of regulations on procedures for requesting permission for advertising.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to VND 20,000 shall be imposed for each advertisement in posters, leaflets or folded sheets without permit.

  2. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising of banners without licenses;

  b/ To receive and display advertisement boards and signs which have not yet been granted to clients for advertising.

  3. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 for any of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to write the license number, advertising duration or name of the advertiser on the advertisement board or signboard;

  b/ Advertising products or goods or introducing activities of establishments without written certification, certification, registration or approval of specialized State management agencies as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 1,500,000 and 5,000,000 for any of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising medicinal materials, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, medical instruments and medical activities which have not yet been approved by competent health agencies;

  b/ Advertising with billboards and advertising boards beyond the prescribed time limit in the license;

  c/ Advertising on publications and means of transport without permit.

  5. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for act of transferring license of business in advertising service, license of conducting advertisement to other organizations or individuals or using license of other organizations or individuals to conduct advertising service.

  6. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Providing advertising services without licenses;

  b/ Carrying out advertising cooperation services with foreign individuals and/or organizations without business cooperation contracts;

  c/ Advertising without licenses at places of cultural and artistic activities;

  d/ To erect or place up advertisement boards or signs without permit.

  7. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 shall be imposed on foreign individuals or organizations that provide advertising services in Vietnam or conduct advertising activities without entering into contracts with Vietnamese advertisers.

  8. Additional penalties and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  a/ Depriving the right to use licenses in case of violations specified in Clause 5;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of barricades, barriers, loose sheets, folded sheets or advertising boards and signs, for violations specified in Clause 1; Point a, Clause 2; Points b and c, Clause 4; Point d, Clause 6; Clause 7.

  Article 25.- Violations of regulations on forms and methods of advertising.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to VND 50,000 for using the prescribed loudness level beyond the limit for advertising from 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for act of advertising on boards, billboards with appearance, color and form similar to traffic signals, public signboards.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for each square meter of area on which the limit is exceeded once, once a day or 1% in excess of the prescribed limit;

  a/ Advertising on billboards or advertising boards in excess of the prescribed area (m2) stated in the license;

  b/ Advertising on radio and television stations in excess of the permitted number of times or the permitted time (%).

  c/ Advertising on printed newspapers, radio and television stations for more days than permitted in a campaign;

  d/ Advertising on printed newspapers in excess of the permitted area (%).

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for any of the following acts:

  a/ To modify the form of the advertisement boards and signs according to the set form in the license;

  b/ Advertising goods or services not clearly identified, which are permitted to be advertised but only their general names or brand names are mentioned;

  c/ Advertising on print journalism, radio and television violates the minimum distance of 5 days between two advertising periods.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising on the front page of a daily, weekly or monthly newspaper or on the front page of a magazine or special issue;

  b/ Trading in goods or products banned from advertisement.

  c/ Advertisements interspersed in news items or articles or interspersed in news programs and special programs on radio and television stations, except for live broadcast of foreign programs.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 30,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for producing goods which are subject to prohibited advertisement.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for using the national flag, national anthem, national emblem, portrait of a leader, Party emblem or international anthem as background for advertisement presentation.

  8. Additional forms of sanction and other measures against acts of violation prescribed in this Article:

  a/ Confiscating goods in case of violations specified at Point b, Clause 5 and Clause 6;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of billboards and advertising boards, for violations specified in Clause 2; Point a, Clause 3; Points a and b, Clause 4 and Clause 7.

  Article 26.- Violations of regulations on places and areas for advertising.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to VND 10,000 for each advertisement on posters, loose-leaf or folded leaflets not placed at prescribed places in the permit.

  2. A fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 shall be imposed for advertisement with tape not in compliance with the prescribed place in the license.

  3. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ To erect or place advertisement boards or signs at locations or positions other than those prescribed in licenses.

  b/ Posting, erecting, placing advertising boards or signs within the protection perimeter of traffic works, thus affecting traffic order and safety.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for placing advertisements in prohibited areas.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of providing advertising services beyond the scope of practice, areas of operation specified in the license.

  6. Additional penalties and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  a/ Depriving the right to use the license for providing advertising services in case of recidivism of violation of Clause 5;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of barricades, posters, leaflets, folded sheets or advertisement boards and signs, for violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4.

  Article 27.- Violations of regulations on contents of advertisements.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of using pictures, photos, posters, loose sheets, folded sheets, umbrellas, trolleys, goods boxes to advertise goods or products which are banned from advertisement.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ To offer goods or products banned from advertisement;

  b/ Advertising goods and products which are banned from advertising on signboards;

  c/ Advertising contents of press, publications or artistic works which have not yet been licensed for publication, distribution, dissemination or performance.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for act of modifying to falsify contents of approved advertisement boards and signs in licenses.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Untruthful or unqualified advertisements of registered goods;

  b/ Advertisements which reduce the prestige and quality of goods of other organizations or individuals.

  5. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising goods or products which are banned from being advertised on billboards, advertising boards, means of transport or at places of public cultural activities;

  b/ Advertising goods and products banned from advertising on radio and television, publications, video tapes and disks, music tapes and disks.

  6. Additional penalties and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  a/ Confiscating goods and products in case of violations specified in Clause 1, Point a, Clause 2;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of billboards and advertising boards, for violations specified at Point b, Clause 2; Clause 3; Clause 4; Point a, Clause 5;

  c/ Forcible compensation for actual damage not exceeding VND 1,000,000 for violations specified at Point b, Clause 4; if the damage is over VND 1,000,000, the compensation must be settled according to civil proceedings.

  Article 28.- Violations of the regulations on writing and putting up signboards.

  1. A fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 for any of the following acts:

  a/ Posting up signs not affixed to head offices of agencies, organizations, hotels, restaurants, shops or stalls with signs;

  b/ Failing to fully inscribe or write in Vietnamese on the signboards.

  2. A fine of between VND 150,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to adequately display the contents prescribed on the signboard;

  b/ Failing to use Vietnamese names and/or characters on the signboards of agencies, organizations, enterprises or companies of Vietnam but using foreign names and/or characters;

  c/ The name and letters in a foreign language on the signboard are more brightly colored and illuminated than the Vietnamese name and letters;

  d/ The signboards of economic organizations bearing abbreviated names, international transaction names or foreign-language names which are written in Vietnamese above their names or bigger than the Vietnamese names;

  dd. The signboard shall be attached with advertisement contents.

  3. Additional penalties and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  Enforced dismantling of signboards in case of commission of the violation specified at Point a Clause 1; Clause 2.

  Chapter III

                       POWER AND PROCEDURES FOR IMPOSING ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES

  Article 29.- The competence of the People’s Committees at all levels to sanction administrative violations.

  The presidents of the People’s Committees at all levels shall have the right to sanction according to their competence prescribed in Articles 26, 27 and 28 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations within the localities under their management for violations prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 30.- The competence of specialized cultural and information inspection agencies to sanction administrative violations.

  The specialized inspection force of Culture and Information shall have the right to sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for violations in cultural activities and cultural services prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 31.- The competence of the police to sanction administrative violations.

  The People's Police Force shall have the right to impose penalties within its jurisdiction as prescribed in Article 29 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for social evils and violations in cultural activities and services related to social order and safety under its jurisdiction as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 32.- The competence of the market management agencies to sanction administrative violations.

  The market management force may sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 33 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in the business of films, video tapes and disks, music tapes and disks, books, newspapers, pictures, photos, calendars and business activities of accommodation establishments and restaurants prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 33.- The border guard and customs offices' competence to sanction administrative violations.

  The border guards and customs offices shall have the right to sanction according to their competence prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in the management of the export and import of films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, books, newspapers, pictures, photos and calendars prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 34.- The competence of the specialized medical inspectorate to sanction administrative violations.

  The specialized medical inspectorate has the right to sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in massage service, advertisement of pharmaceutical products, medicinal materials, cosmetics, medical instruments and medical activities not yet approved by competent medical agencies prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 35.- The competence of the specialized labor inspection agency to sanction administrative violations.

  Specialized labor inspectorates shall have the right to impose penalties within their competence as prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for acts of using labor without signing labor contracts as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 36.- Collection and payment of fines.

  Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services, and social evil prevention and combat shall pay fines at prescribed places; if failing to pay fines within the prescribed time limit, they shall be forced to do so.

  The fine collector is prohibited from collecting fines on the spot.

  Article 37.- Confiscation and handling of material evidences and means used in the commission of administrative violations.

  When applying the sanctioning form of confiscating material evidences and means used in administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and social evils prevention and combat, the persons with sanctioning competence shall have to strictly observe the procedures prescribed in Articles 51 and 52 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the following regulations:

  a/ For material evidences of administrative violations being films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs or publications with no violation in their contents and means used for commission of violations, the persons who issue the confiscation decisions shall have to transfer them to finance agencies of the same level for organizing valuation councils (in which representatives of culture and information agencies are compulsory members) and auctioning them.

  b/ For exhibits of administrative violations being unlabeled video tapes or video tapes with false labels, video tapes, audio tapes, music tapes and publications not yet permitted for circulation, the persons who issue the confiscation decisions shall have to transfer them to the Culture and Information Offices of the same level for assessment; in cases where the video tapes, audio tapes, music tapes and publications are assessed as having no violation in their contents and can be circulated, they shall be handed over to the Finance Office of the same level for organization of valuation councils and auction according to the provisions at Point a of this Article; in cases where the video tapes, audio tapes, music tapes and publications are assessed as having pornographic or violent contents, the Culture and Information Offices shall have to destroy them according to the provisions in Article 38 of this Decree.

  c/ For films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, publications which have been banned from circulation or withdrawn under decisions, the persons who have issued the decisions on confiscation shall have to transfer them to the Culture and Information Services for handling according to their competence.

  d/ All cases of transfer and handling of material evidences and means used in the commission of administrative violations specified at Points a, b and c of this Article must be recorded in writing with certification by concerned parties.

  Article 38.- Destruction of material evidences of administrative violations.

  1. For material evidence being films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs or publications with clearly indecent, pornographic contents or contents that incite violence, the person who issues the sanctioning decision shall decide on their destruction within his/her competence or transfer them to a competent agency for destruction.

  2. For material evidences being films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs or publications with pornographic or violent contents, the persons with sanctioning competence shall transfer them to the competent cultural and information agencies for organization of expertise; if after the expertise, the conclusions state that the material evidences have pornographic, violent or provocative contents, the said cultural and information agencies shall organize the destruction thereof.

  In case films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs or publications are assessed as having no pornographic, obscene or violent contents, they shall be transferred to finance agencies of the same level for organization of valuation council and auction under Article 37 of this Decree.

  3. When destroying films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, publications with indecent or pornographic contents or inciting violence, the agencies applying this measure shall set up a handling council composed of representatives of the Culture and Information Agency, the Public Security Service, the Finance Agency and the People's Procuracy of the same level; in case of necessity, representatives of concerned agencies and organizations may be invited to witness the destruction.

  The destruction must be recorded in writing with certification by members of the disposal council.

  4. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall provide specific guidance on procedures for invalidation specified in this Article.

  Article 39.- Complaints and denunciations.

  1. Citizens have the right to denounce to competent state agencies administrative violations committed by organizations and individuals in cultural activities, cultural services and social evil prevention and combat specified in this Decree or denounce violations committed by persons with power to sanction administrative violations specified in this Decree.

  2. Organizations and individuals subject to administrative penalties or their lawful representatives may lodge complaints with the persons who have issued the penalty decisions within 10 days from the date of receipt of such decisions.

  3. The order of complaint and denunciation procedures and competence to settle complaints and denunciations about violations of administrative regulations on cultural activities, cultural services and social evils shall comply with Articles 87, 88 and 90 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 40.- This Decree takes effect on February 1st, 1996.

  The previous regulations which are contrary to this Decree shall be annulled.

  Article 41.- The Minister of Culture and Information, the Minister of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall, within the ambit of their respective functions and tasks, have to guide the implementation of this Decree.

  Article 42.- The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies and agencies attached to the Government, the presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government shall have to perform this task.
