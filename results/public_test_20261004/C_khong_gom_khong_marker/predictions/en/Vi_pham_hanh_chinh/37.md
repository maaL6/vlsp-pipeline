                                                  DECREE

     AMENDMENTS TO A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 106/2025/ND-CP DATED MAY 15, 2025 ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON FIRE PREVENTION, FIRE FIGHTING AND INJURY AND WIDE RECOVERY

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization No. 63/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 18/2017/QH14, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 09/2022/QH15, Law No. 11/2022/QH15, Law No. 56/2024/QH15, and Law No. 88/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Fire Prevention, Fighting and Rescue No. 55/2024/QH15;

  At the request of the Minister of Public Security;

  The Government promulgates Decree on amendments to some Articles of Decree No. 106/2025/ND-CP dated May 15, 2025 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting and rescue.

  Article 1. Point i Clause 2 Article 2 is amended as follows:

  i) Organizations assigned to directly manage facilities and organizations operating within a facility in accordance with fire prevention, firefighting and rescue laws;".

  Article 2. Addition of Clauses 6, 7 and 8 after Clause 5 Article 3 as follows:

  1. Clause 6 is added

  “6. Organizations and individuals committing multiple administrative violations of the same violation in a facility subject to fire prevention and firefighting management shall not be sanctioned for each administrative violation but only one administrative violation with aggravating circumstances.”.

  2. Addition of Clause 7

  “7. Organizations operating within a facility, organizations and individuals renting, borrowing or staying in houses shall be sanctioned for administrative violations prescribed herein corresponding to responsibilities of organizations operating within facilities, organizations and individuals renting, borrowing or staying in houses according to fire prevention, firefighting and rescue laws.”.

  3. Addition of Clause 8

  “8. The handling of administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting and rescue under this Decree by electronic means shall comply with Article 28a and Article 28b of the Government’s Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP dated December 23, 2021 elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations (amended by the Government’s Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP dated July 1, 2025).”.

  Article 3. Amendments to Clause 4 Article 5 as follows:

  “4. The time of termination of an act of violation shall be determined as follows:

  a) The time of termination of the violation for calculation of the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 6 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which the competent authority issues a written notification of training and refresher training results in accordance with regulations;

  b) The time of termination of the violation for calculation of the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 18 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which the specialized authority issues a written appraisal of fire prevention and firefighting design as prescribed;

  c) The time of termination of the violation for calculation of the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 18 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which the specialized authority issues a written approval of fire prevention and firefighting commissioning results as per regulation or the date on which the operation terminates;

  d) The time of termination of the violation for calculation of the prescriptive period for sanctioning administrative violations prescribed in Points a and b Clause 9 Article 20 of this Decree for works not subject to fire prevention and firefighting design appraisal shall be calculated from the date on which the investor signs the record of acceptance testing and completion of work items or construction works for use as prescribed.”.

  Article 23. Implementation

  1. This Decree comes into force as of April 20, 2026.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and cities and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals shall be responsible for implementation of this Decree.

  Article 24. Transitional provisions

  1. For administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention, firefighting and rescue committed before the effective date of this Decree and discovered but the prescriptive period has not expired or are being considered and handled after this Decree takes effect, the Government’s Decree on penalties for administrative violations effective at the time of commission of such violations shall apply.

  For administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention, firefighting and rescue committed before the effective date of this Decree but still ongoing after the effective date of this Decree, regulations in this Decree shall apply.

  2. If organizations or individuals subject to administrative penalties still lodge complaints against decisions on imposition of administrative penalties issued or completely executed before the effective date hereof, regulations on imposition of administrative penalties at the time of issuance of such decisions shall apply.
