              Law No. 13/2012/QH13                           Hanoi, June 20, 2012



                                                       LAW

                                              LEGAL ASSESSMENT

  Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on Judicial Expertise.

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Law provides for judicial experts; judicial expertise service providers; subject-matter judicial experts and subject-matter judicial expertise service providers; judicial expertise activities; judicial expertise costs, benefits and policies in judicial expertise activities and responsibilities of regulatory agencies for judicial expertise service providers and activities.

  Article 2. Interpretation of terms

  In this Law, the terms below are construed as follows:

  1. Judicial expertise means a judicial expert uses knowledge, means, scientific, technical and professional methods to make professional conclusions on issues related to investigation, prosecution, adjudication and enforcement of criminal judgments, settlement of civil cases and administrative cases at the request of presiding agencies or persons or at the request of persons requesting judicial expertise in accordance with this Law.

  2. The person soliciting the assessment includes the procedural authority and the procedural person.

  3. The person requesting expertise is a person who has the right to request expertise himself/herself after having requested the procedural authority or the procedural person to solicit expertise but his/her request was rejected. Persons who have the right to request expertise themselves include involved parties in civil matters or administrative cases, civil plaintiffs, civil defendants, persons with related rights and obligations in criminal cases or their lawful representatives, except for cases where the request for expertise is related to the determination of criminal liability of suspects or defendants.

  4. Judicial expertise entities include judicial experts, subject-matter judicial experts, public judicial expertise service providers, non-public judicial expertise service providers and subject-matter judicial expertise service providers.

  5. Judicial experts include judicial experts and subject-matter judicial experts.

  6. Judicial expert officers are those who meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law and are appointed by competent authorities to conduct judicial expertise.

  7. Case-specific judicial assessors are those who meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of Article 18 and Article 20 of this Law and are solicited or requested for assessment.

  8. Ad hoc judicial assessment organization means an agency or organization that fully satisfies the criteria specified in Articles 19 and 20 of this Law and is solicited or requested to conduct judicial assessment.

  Article 3. Principles of judicial expertise

  1. Comply with laws and professional standards.

  2. Truthful, accurate, objective, unbiased and timely.

  3. To make professional conclusions only on issues within the scope of request.

  4. To take responsibility before law for assessment conclusions.

  Article 4. Responsibilities of individuals and organizations for judicial assessment activities

  1. Individuals and organizations solicited or requested to conduct judicial assessment shall be responsible for receiving and conducting judicial assessment in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  2. Other organizations and individuals shall be responsible for facilitating judicial assessors to carry out assessment in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  Article 5. State policies on judicial assessment activities

  1. The State shall invest in and develop a system of public judicial assessment organizations in fields with large and frequent demands for assessment to meet the requirements of legal proceedings; and adopt preferential policies to facilitate the development of non-public judicial assessment organizations.

  2. The State shall adopt policies to prioritize training and advanced training in professional skills for judicial assessors.

  Article 6. Prohibited acts

  1. Refusal to issue a judicial assessment conclusion without a legitimate reason.

  2. Intentionally making untruthful judicial assessment conclusions.

  3. Deliberately prolonging the time for judicial assessment.

  4. Taking advantage of judicial expertise for self-seeking purposes.

  5. Disclosing information they know when conducting judicial assessment.

  6. Persuading or forcing judicial assessors to make false assessment conclusions.

  7. Interfere with or obstruct the performance of judicial assessment by judicial assessors.

  Chapter II

                                           LEGAL ASSESSOR

  Article 7. Criteria for appointment of judicial assessors

  1. Vietnamese citizens permanently residing in Vietnam who fully meet the following criteria may be considered and appointed as judicial experts:

  a) Have good health and moral qualities;

  b) Hold a university degree or higher and have at least 5 years of professional experience in the training field.

  In case the person proposed for appointment as forensic examiner, forensic psychiatric examiner or criminal technique examiner has directly assisted in assessment activities at a forensic examination organization, forensic psychiatric examination organization or criminal technique examination organization, the period of actual professional experience shall be three years or more;

  c) Persons proposed for appointment as judicial experts in forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry and criminal techniques must possess certificates of training or refresher training in expertise skills.

  2. A person who falls into one of the following cases shall not be appointed as a judicial assessor:

  a) Have their civil act capacity lost or have their civil act capacity restricted;

  b) Being criminally prosecuted; having been convicted but not yet granted expungement of criminal records for unintentional crimes or less serious intentional crimes; having been convicted of serious crimes, very serious crimes, extremely serious crimes due to intentional acts;

  c/ Being subject to administrative handling measures of education at communes, wards or townships, being sent to compulsory detoxification centers or compulsory education establishments.

  3. Ministers and Heads of ministerial agencies shall elaborate clause 1 of this Article for judicial experts in fields under their management after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  Article 8. Application for appointment of judicial assessors

  1. Written request for appointment of judicial assessor.

  2. Copies of university degrees or higher in relevant fields proposed for appointment.

  3. Curriculum vitae and criminal record certificate.

  4. Written certification of the actual period of professional experience of the agency or organization where the person proposed for appointment works.

  5. Certificates of training or refresher training in judicial expertise for persons proposed to be appointed as judicial experts in forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry and criminal techniques.

  6. Other documents proving that the proposed person meets the prescribed standards as per regulations of the Minister or Head of the ministerial-level agency competent to manage the field of assessment.

  Article 9. Authority and procedures for appointment of judicial assessors

  1. The Minister of Health shall appoint forensic examiners and forensic psychiatrists working at central authorities.

  The Minister of Public Security shall appoint criminal forensic examiners working at central authorities.

  Ministers and Heads of ministerial agencies shall appoint judicial experts to operate in other fields at central authorities under their management.

  The Chairpersons of the People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities (hereinafter referred to as provincial level) shall appoint judicial assessors in their localities.

  2. The Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Public Security shall select qualified individuals as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 7 hereof to propose the Minister of Health to appoint forensic examiners under their control.

  The Ministry of National Defense shall select persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 hereof and propose the Minister of Public Security to appoint criminal forensic examiners under its control.

  Heads of units affiliated to ministries or ministerial agencies assigned to manage judicial assessment activities shall select persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law and propose Ministers or Heads of ministerial agencies to appoint public expert officers in the fields of assessment under their management.

  The head of the specialized agency under the management of the provincial People’s Committee shall take charge and cooperate with the Director of the Department of Justice in selecting a qualified person as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law, receive the application from the applicant for appointment as a judicial assessor as prescribed in Article 8 of this Law, and propose the President of the provincial People’s Committee to appoint the judicial assessor in the province.

  Within 20 days from the date of receipt of a valid application, the Minister, the Head of the ministerial-level agency, or the President of the provincial People's Committee shall decide to appoint a judicial assessor. In case of refusal, a written notification stating the reasons must be provided to the applicant.

  3. Ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Committees shall be responsible for preparing and publishing lists of judicial experts on their web portals, and concurrently sending them to the Ministry of Justice for compilation of a general list of judicial experts.

  Article 10. Removal of judicial experts

  1. Cases of dismissal of judicial experts:

  a) No longer satisfying the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law;

  b) They fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 Article 7 hereof;

  c) Being disciplined in the form of warning or more severe, or administratively sanctioned for intentional violation of regulations of law on judicial assessment;

  d) Committing one of the acts specified in Article 6 of this Law;

  dd) At the request of a judicial assessor who is a civil servant, public employee, army officer, people’s police officer, professional serviceman or national defense worker and has decided to resign from his/her job for retirement or discharge.

  2. The application for dismissal of a judicial assessor includes:

  a) The written request for dismissal of the judicial expert by the agency or organization that has proposed the appointment of such person;

  b) Documents and papers proving that the judicial assessor falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. The Minister of Public Security and the Minister of National Defense shall consider proposing the Minister of Health to dismiss forensic examiners under their management.

  The Minister of National Defense shall consider and propose the Minister of Public Security to dismiss criminal forensic examiners under their management.

  Ministers and Heads of ministerial agencies shall dismiss judicial experts working in central authorities in the fields under their management at the request of Heads of units affiliated to ministries or ministerial agencies assigned to manage judicial assessment activities.

  The Chairperson of the provincial People’s Committee shall dismiss local judicial experts at the request of the head of the specialized agency of the People’s Committee after the head of the specialized agency reaches agreement with the Director of the Department of Justice.

  4. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a valid application, the Minister, the Head of the ministerial agency, or the President of the provincial People’s Committee shall consider deciding to dismiss the judicial assessor and adjust the list of judicial assessors on the web portal of the ministry, the ministerial agency, or the provincial People’s Committee, and concurrently send it to the Ministry of Justice for adjustment of the general list of judicial assessors.

  Article 11. Rights and obligations of judicial assessors

  1. To conduct assessment at the request or order of the person soliciting or requesting assessment, or as assigned by the agency or organization solicited or requested.

  2. Refusal to conduct assessment in case the contents of assessment exceed the professional competence; the subject matters of assessment and relevant documents provided are insufficient or invalid for making assessment conclusions; or the time is not sufficient for conducting assessment or there are other legitimate reasons. In case of refusal to conduct assessment, within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the decision on solicitation or request for assessment, a written notification must be sent to the person soliciting or requesting assessment, clearly stating the reason.

  3. Participate in training courses on expertise skills and legal knowledge.

  4. Establish a judicial assessment office when fully satisfying the conditions specified in Article 15 of this Law.

  5. Establish or join a private judicial assessment association in accordance with the law on associations.

  6. Enjoy benefits and policies as prescribed in this Law and other relevant laws.

  7. Rights and obligations specified in Article 23 and Clause 1 Article 34 of this Law.

  Chapter III

                                       LEGAL ASSESSMENT ORGANIZATION

  Section 1. LEGAL ASSESSMENT INSTITUTIONS

  Article 12. Public judicial assessment organizations

  1. Public judicial assessment organizations established by competent state authorities in the fields of forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry and criminal techniques.

  Where necessary, Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies and Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees shall consider deciding on establishment or request competent authorities to establish public judicial assessment organizations in other fields after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  2. Public forensic examination institutions include:

  a) The National Institute of Forensic Medicine under the Ministry of Health;

  b) Provincial forensic center;

  c) Military Medical Examinations Institute affiliated to Ministry of National Defense;

  d) Forensic examination centers affiliated to Institute of Criminal Science, Ministry of Public Security.

  3. Public forensic psychiatric assessment organizations include:

  a) The Central Mental Medical Institute affiliated to the Ministry of Health;

  b) Regional forensic psychiatric centers under the Ministry of Health.

  Pursuant to the request for forensic psychiatric assessment of legal proceedings and the actual conditions of regions and zones nationwide, the Minister of Health shall consider and decide on the establishment of regional forensic psychiatric centers after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  4. Public forensic examination institutions in criminal technology include:

  a) Institute of Criminal Science affiliated to the Ministry of Public Security;

  b) Department of Criminal Techniques affiliated to provincial police authorities;

  c) The Criminal Technical Expertise Department under the Ministry of National Defense.

  5. Based on actual local needs and conditions, the Department of Criminal Techniques affiliated to the provincial police authority shall assign forensic examiner to conduct forensic examination of death.

  6. Public judicial assessment organizations shall have their own seals and accounts as prescribed by law.

  7. The Government shall elaborate on the functions, tasks, organizational structure and working regulations of public judicial assessment organizations specified in this Article.

  Article 13. Assurance of physical foundations for public judicial assessment organizations

  1. Public judicial assessment organizations shall be provided by the State with material facilities, funding, equipment, means and other necessary conditions for conducting judicial assessment.

  2. Funding for operation of public judicial assessment organizations shall be provided by state budget and other revenues as prescribed by law.

  3. The Ministry of Health shall provide regulations on conditions for material facilities, equipment and means used for judicial assessment in the fields of forensic medicine and forensic psychiatry.

  The Ministry of Public Security shall regulate conditions for material facilities, equipment and means used for judicial assessment in the field of criminal techniques.

  Section 2. FOREIGN LEGAL ASSESSMENT INSTITUTIONS

  Article 14. Judicial assessment offices

  1. Judicial assessment office means a private judicial assessment organization established in the fields of finance, banking, construction, antiques, relics and copyrights.

  2. A judicial assessment office established by 01 public expert officer shall be organized and operate as a sole proprietorship. A judicial assessment office established by 02 or more public expert officers shall be organized and operate as a partnership.

  The legal representative of a judicial assessment office is the head of such office. The head of a judicial assessment office must be a judicial assessor.

  Article 15. Conditions for establishment of judicial assessment offices

  1. A judicial assessor may establish a judicial assessment office when fully satisfying the following conditions:

  a) Have worked as judicial experts in the field proposed for establishment of the office for at least 5 years;

  b) The establishment project is in accordance with Point d Clause 2 Article 16 hereof.

  2. Officials, public officials, public employees, army officers, people’s police officers, professional servicemen, national defense workers are not allowed to establish judicial assessment offices.

  Article 16. Licensing establishment of judicial assessment offices

  1. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees of administrative divisions where headquarters of the proposed judicial assessment offices are located shall consider and decide to permit establishment of the proposed judicial assessment offices at the request of directors of Departments of Justice.

  2. The judicial assessor applying for establishment of a judicial assessment office shall submit an application to the Department of Justice. The application includes:

  a) The application for establishment;

  b) Copies of decisions on appointment of judicial experts;

  c) Draft Regulation on organization and operation of judicial assessment offices;

  d) The plan for establishment of a judicial assessment office must clearly state its establishment purposes; expected name, personnel and location of head office; conditions regarding facilities, equipment and means for judicial assessment in accordance with regulations of ministries or ministerial agencies specialized in the field of assessment and implementation plan.

  3. Within 30 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application, the Director of the Department of Justice shall review and appraise the application for establishment of a judicial assessment office, reach agreement with the head of the specialized agency under the management of the provincial People’s Committee in charge of judicial assessment, and submit it to the President of the provincial People’s Committee for consideration and decision.

  Within 15 days from the date on which the Department of Justice submits the application, the President of People’s Committee of province shall consider and decide to permit the establishment of the judicial assessment office. In case of refusal, a written notice stating reasons must be provided. The applicant may file a complaint or lawsuit according to regulations of law.

  Article 17. Registration of operation of judicial assessment offices

  1. Within 01 year from the date on which Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees issue decision permitting establishment, judicial assessment offices shall register their operation at Departments of Justice.

  1 year after a provincial-level People’s Committee president issues a decision permitting establishment of a judicial assessment office but such office fails to register its operation, the decision permitting establishment of the judicial assessment office shall cease to be effective.

  2. The judicial assessment office shall submit an application for operation registration to the Department of Justice. The application includes:

  a) Application for operation registration;

  b) Regulations on organization and operation of the judicial assessment office;

  c) Documents proving eligibility to ensure operation of the judicial assessment office under the establishment scheme specified in Point d Clause 2 Article 16 hereof;

  d) Copies of decisions permitting establishment of judicial assessment offices.

  3. Within 30 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application, the Department of Justice shall take charge and cooperate with the specialized agency of the provincial People’s Committee in charge of judicial assessment in inspecting the satisfaction of conditions specified in the establishment plan under Point d Clause 2 Article 16 of this Law and issue an operation registration certificate; in case of refusal, a written notification stating the reasons must be provided, and at the same time reported to the President of the provincial People’s Committee for consideration and decision on revocation of the establishment permission decision. The person subject to refusal has the right to file a complaint or lawsuit according to law.

  4. The judicial assessment office shall commence its operation from the date of issuance of the certificate of registration.

  Chapter IV

   JURIDICAL CONSULTANCY CLIENTS, JURIDICAL CONSULTANCY ORGANIZATIONS

  Article 18. Ad hoc judicial assessors

  1. Vietnamese citizens permanently residing in Vietnam who fully meet the following criteria may be selected as subject-matter judicial experts:

  a) Have good health and moral qualities;

  b) Hold a university degree or higher and have at least 5 years of professional experience in the training field.

  2. In cases where a person does not have a university degree but has in-depth knowledge and practical experience in the field to be assessed, he/she may be selected as a subject-matter judicial assessor.

  3. Ad hoc judicial assessors shall conduct assessment at request or order in accordance with this Law. Ad hoc judicial assessors have rights and obligations prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7 Article 11 of this Law.

  Article 19. Ad hoc judicial assessment organization

  1. A case-specific judicial assessment organization must fully satisfy the following conditions:

  a) Having legal entity status;

  b) having professional activities appropriate to the contents of the solicited assessment;

  c) Have qualified professional officers and facilities to ensure the performance of judicial assessment.

  2. Ad hoc judicial assessment organizations shall conduct judicial assessment at request or order in accordance with this Law. The head of such organization shall be responsible for receiving and assigning persons to conduct judicial assessment.

  3. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies and specialized agencies of provincial People’s Committees shall conduct judicial assessment at the request of the ordering party.

  Article 20. Preparation and publication of lists of subject-matter judicial experts and subject-matter expertise service providers

  1. The Ministry of Construction, the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Information and Communications, the Ministry of Planning and Investment, the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment, the Ministry of Transport, the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, the State Bank of Vietnam, other ministries and ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Committees shall select, prepare and publish annually lists of subject-matter judicial experts and subject-matter judicial expertise service providers in the fields under their management to meet the requirements for expertise in legal proceedings.

  The list, together with information on the expertise, experience and capacity of the subject-matter judicial assessor or the subject-matter judicial assessment organization, shall be posted on the web portal of the Ministry, ministerial-level agency or provincial-level People's Committee and sent to the Ministry of Justice for compilation of a general list.

  2. In special cases, the ordering party may order a qualified individual or organization not on the published list to conduct the assessment, but must clearly state the reason.

  At the request of the presiding authorities, presiding officers, ministries, ministerial agencies and specialized agencies of provincial People’s Committees managing the field of assessment shall introduce individuals or organizations eligible to conduct assessment outside the published list.

  Chapter V

                                     LEGAL ADVISORY ACTIVITIES

  Article 21. Rights and obligations of ordering parties

  1. The ordering party has the right to:

  a) Request individuals and organizations specified in Clause 4 Article 2 of this Law to conduct assessment;

  b) Request individuals and organizations specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article to return assessment conclusions in accordance with the requested contents and within the prescribed time limit;

  c) Request the individual or organization that has conducted judicial assessment to explain the assessment conclusion.

  2. The person soliciting the assessment shall have the obligations:

  a) Select an organization or individual to conduct assessment in accordance with the nature and requirements of the case subject to assessment;

  b) Issue a decision on solicitation of assessment in writing;

  c) Provide information and documents related to the subject matter of judicial expertise at the request of individuals or organizations conducting judicial expertise;

  d) Advance judicial assessment expense upon request for judicial assessment; pay in a timely and full manner judicial assessment expense to individuals or organizations conducting judicial assessment upon receipt of assessment conclusions;

  dd) Ensure safety for judicial assessors during the process of conducting assessment or when participating in proceedings as judicial assessors.

  Article 22. Rights and obligations of persons requesting judicial assessment

  1. The requesting party may send a written request to the presiding agency or person to solicit expertise. If the presiding agency or person rejects the request, it must notify in writing the requesting party within 7 days. Past this time limit or after receiving the notice of refusal, the requesting party may request expertise by themselves.

  2. The person requesting assessment has the right to:

  a) Request individuals and organizations to provide judicial expertise conclusions within the agreed time limit and in accordance with the requested contents;

  b) Request the individual or organization that has conducted judicial assessment to explain the assessment conclusion;

  c) Request the Court to summon the judicial expert who has conducted the assessment to participate in the hearing to explain and present the assessment conclusion;

  d) Request procedural authorities and persons to request re-assessment; request additional assessment as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 29 of this Law.

  3. The person requesting judicial expertise shall have the obligation to:

  a) Provide information and documents related to the subject matter of judicial expertise at the request of the judicial expert and take responsibility for the accuracy of the information and documents provided;

  b) Pay advance on judicial assessment expense upon request for judicial assessment; pay in a timely and full manner judicial assessment expenses to individuals or organizations conducting judicial assessment upon receipt of judicial assessment conclusions.

  4. The person requesting the assessment shall only be entitled to request the assessment before the Court issues a decision to bring the case to first-instance trial.

  Article 23. Rights and obligations of judicial assessors when conducting judicial assessment

  1. Judicial experts have the right to:

  a) Select appropriate and necessary methods for conducting assessment according to the contents of the assessment request;

  b) Using results of additional experiments or tests or professional conclusions made by other individuals or organizations to serve the assessment;

  c) To independently make the assessment conclusion.

  2. The judicial assessor has the obligation to:

  a) Comply with principles of judicial expertise;

  b) Conduct assessment in accordance with the contents of the request for assessment;

  c) Perform and return the assessment conclusion within the prescribed time limit; in cases where additional time is required for the assessment, promptly notify the ordering party or requesting party of such requirement;

  d) Prepare an assessment file;

  dd) Preserve the material evidence and documents related to the assessment case;

  e) Failing to notify the results of the assessment to other persons, except where such notification is agreed in writing by the person who has solicited or requested the assessment;

  g) To bear personal responsibility for the assessment conclusions they have issued. Where they intentionally issue false assessment conclusions causing damage to individuals or organizations, they shall also pay compensation or indemnity in accordance with law.

  3. In addition to the rights and obligations prescribed in clause 1 and clause 2 of this Article, judicial assessors have other rights and obligations as prescribed by law on legal proceedings.

  Article 24. Rights and obligations of organizations solicited or requested to conduct judicial assessment

  1. Organizations solicited or requested to conduct judicial expertise shall have the right to:

  a) Request the ordering party or requesting party to provide necessary information and documents for the assessment;

  b) Refuse to conduct assessment if there are not enough necessary conditions for conducting assessment;

  c) Receive advance payment of judicial assessment expense upon receiving a request for judicial assessment; receive timely and full payment of judicial assessment expense when returning assessment results.

  2. The organization solicited or requested to conduct judicial assessment shall have the obligations:

  a) Receive and assign persons with professional qualifications suitable to the contents of the solicited or requested assessment to conduct the assessment within their organization and take responsibility for such persons’ professional qualifications; assign persons responsible for coordinating the performance of the assessment in cases where multiple persons are required to perform the assessment;

  b) Ensure equipment, means and other necessary conditions for the performance of assessment;

  c) Pay compensation in case the person assigned to conduct assessment intentionally makes a wrong conclusion, causing damage to individuals or organizations;

  d) Notify the ordering party or requesting party in writing within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the decision on ordering or requesting assessment, clearly stating the reasons in case of refusal to accept the order or request for assessment.

  Article 25. Request for judicial assessment

  1. The ordering party shall issue a decision on ordering judicial assessment in writing and send it together with the subject matter of assessment and relevant documents and items (if any) related to the individual or organization conducting the assessment.

  2. A decision to request expertise must contain the following contents:

  a) Name of the requesting authority; full name of the person competent to request the assessment;

  b) Name of the organization; full name of the person requested for expertise;

  c) Name and characteristics of the subject matter to be appraised;

  d) Name of relevant documents or comparison samples enclosed (if any);

  dd) The contents of the request for expertise;

  e) Date and time of request for assessment and time limit for provision of assessment conclusions.

  3. In case of additional assessment or re-assessment, the decision on assessment solicitation must clearly state that it is an additional assessment or a re-assessment.

  Article 26. Request for judicial expertise in civil cases, administrative cases and criminal cases

  1. The requesting party shall send a written request for expertise, enclosed with the subject matter of expertise, relevant documents and items (if any), and copies of documents proving their status as involved parties in civil matters or administrative cases, civil plaintiffs, civil defendants, persons with related rights and obligations in criminal cases, or their lawful representatives to the individual or organization conducting the expertise.

  2. A written request for judicial assessment must contain the following contents:

  a) Name of the organization or full name of the person requesting the assessment;

  b) The contents of the request for expertise;

  c) Name and characteristics of the object of assessment;

  d) Name of relevant documents or comparison samples enclosed (if any);

  dd) Date of request for expertise and time limit for issuing the expertise conclusion;

  e) Signature and full name of the requesting person.

  Article 27. Delivery of application and subject matter of solicitation or request for expertise

  1. The dossier and subject matter of the request for assessment shall be delivered directly or sent to the individual or organization conducting the assessment by post.

  2. The direct handover and receipt of dossiers and objects subject to solicitation or request for expertise must be recorded in writing. A record of handover and receipt must contain the following contents:

  a) Time and place of handover and receipt of assessment files;

  b) Full name of the representative of the delivering party and the receiving party;

  c) Decision on solicitation or written request for assessment; subject matter to be assessed; relevant documents and items;

  d) Methods of preservation of objects of assessment, relevant documents and items upon delivery and receipt;

  dd) The condition of the object of assessment, relevant documents and items upon delivery and receipt;

  e) Signatures of representatives of the parties delivering and receiving objects of assessment.

  3. The sending of dossiers and objects of solicitation or request for assessment by post must be carried out in the form of service delivery with a number. Individuals and organizations receiving the dossiers sent by service delivery with a number shall be responsible for preserving them and, upon breaking the seal, must prepare a record as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. In case of delivery and receipt of subjects of forensic examination or forensic psychiatric examination involving human subjects, the ordering party shall take charge and cooperate with the individual or organization receiving the order in managing the subjects during the process of conducting the examination.

  5. Upon completion of the assessment, the assessing individual or organization shall return the subject matter of assessment to the ordering party or requesting party, unless otherwise provided by law.

  The person soliciting or requesting assessment shall be responsible for returning the subject matter of assessment in accordance with the law.

  The handover and receipt of objects after the completion of assessment shall comply with Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 28. Individual assessment, collective assessment

  1. Individual assessment means an assessment carried out by one person. Collective assessment means an assessment carried out by two or more persons.

  2. In case of personal assessment, the assessor shall conduct the assessment, sign the assessment conclusion and take personal responsibility for such assessment conclusion.

  3. In case of a collective assessment in a specialized field, the assessors shall jointly conduct the assessment, sign the general assessment conclusion and bear responsibility for such assessment conclusion; if having different opinions, the assessor shall record his/her opinion in the assessment conclusion and take responsibility for such opinion.

  In case of a collective assessment in different fields, each assessor shall perform the assessment within their field and take personal responsibility for the assessment conclusion.

  Article 29. Additional assessment and re-assessment

  1. Additional assessment shall be conducted in cases where the contents of the previous assessment conclusion are unclear or incomplete, or when new issues arise related to the facts of the case or matter previously concluded by assessment. The solicitation or request for additional assessment shall be carried out as for initial assessment.

  2. Re-assessment shall be carried out where there are grounds to believe that the initial assessment conclusion is inaccurate or in the case specified in Clause 2 Article 30 of this Law.

  3. The assessment requester shall decide on the re-assessment either by themselves or at the request of the assessment petitioner. If the assessment requester does not accept the re-assessment request, he/she must notify such in writing to the assessment petitioner and clearly state the reason therefor.

  Article 30. Appraisal council

  1. In case there is a difference between the first assessment conclusion and the re-assessment conclusion on the same assessment content, the second re-assessment shall be decided by the assessment requester. The second re-assessment must be carried out by an assessment council.

  The Minister or Head of the ministerial-level agency managing the field subject to assessment shall decide on the establishment of a Council for conducting a second assessment. The assessment council shall be composed of at least three members who have high expertise and prestige in the field subject to assessment. The assessment council shall operate under the collective assessment mechanism prescribed in Clause 3 Article 28 of this Law.

  2. In special cases, the Prosecutor General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy or Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court shall decide to conduct a re-expertise after the conclusion of the Expertise Council.

  Article 31. Written record of judicial assessment process

  1. The judicial assessor shall promptly, fully and truthfully document the entire process and results of the assessment in writing.

  2. The written record of the process of assessment shall be kept in the assessment file.

  Article 32. Judicial expertise conclusion

  1. Judicial expertise conclusion means a written remark or evaluation by a judicial expert on the object of judicial expertise according to the contents of the expertise solicitation or request. A judicial expertise conclusion must contain the following contents:

  a) Full name of the person conducting the assessment; organization conducting the assessment;

  b) Name of the procedural authority; full name of the person conducting the procedure requesting assessment; number of the written request for assessment or full name of the person requesting assessment;

  c) Information identifying the object of expertise;

  d) Time of receipt of written requests for expertise;

  dd) The contents of the request for expertise;

  e) Methods of conducting assessment;

  g) Concluding on the object of expertise;

  h) Time and place for conducting and completing the assessment.

  2. In case of soliciting or requesting an individual to conduct assessment, the signature of the assessor must be authenticated in accordance with laws on authentication.

  In case of soliciting or requesting an organization to conduct assessment, the head of such organization must sign and affix his/her seal on the assessment conclusion and the solicited or requested organization shall be responsible for the assessment conclusion.

  In case the assessment council specified in Clause 1 Article 30 of this Law conducts assessment, the person who decides to establish the council must sign and affix his/her seal on the assessment conclusion and take responsibility for the legal status of the assessment council.

  3. In case an assessment is conducted before a decision to institute criminal proceedings is issued and in accordance with the procedures prescribed by this Law, the presiding agency may use such conclusion as judicial assessment conclusion.

  Article 33. Judicial assessment file

  1. A judicial assessment file prepared by a judicial assessor includes:

  a) Decision on solicitation, written request for assessment and enclosed documents (if any);

  b) Record of handover and receipt of dossiers and objects subject to solicitation or request for expertise;

  c) Written record of the process of conducting the assessment;

  d) Appraisal photos (if any);

  dd) Previous expertise conclusions or results of testing and experimentation conducted by other persons (if any);

  e) Other documents related to the assessment (if any);

  g) Conclude judicial assessment.

  2. Judicial assessment files must be prepared in a uniform form. The Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of Health and ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy in uniformly prescribing the form of judicial assessment files.

  3. Individuals and organizations conducting judicial expertise shall be responsible for preserving and storing their expertise records in accordance with archival laws.

  4. Judicial assessment files shall be presented upon request of presiding authorities or presiding officers competent to resolve criminal, administrative cases and civil matters.

  Article 34. Cases ineligible for judicial assessment

  1. Persons falling into one of the following cases shall not be permitted to conduct judicial assessment:

  a) They fall into one of the cases in which they must refuse to participate in legal proceedings or be changed as prescribed by procedural law;

  b) To be re-assessed on the same contents of a case or matter which they have assessed, unless otherwise provided for by law.

  2. Organizations in one of the following cases shall not conduct judicial assessment:

  a) Have rights and obligations related to the lawsuit or case in accordance with the law on proceedings;

  b) There are clear grounds to believe that this organization may not be objective and impartial in conducting the assessment.

  Article 35. Judicial assistance in judicial assessment

  1. A request for judicial expertise shall be made to a foreign individual or organization only if the subject matter of the judicial expertise is located overseas or the professional capability and equipment of domestic judicial expertise entities are not satisfactory.

  2. Judicial expertise entities shall be responsible for receiving and conducting judicial expertise at the request of foreign procedural authorities.

  3. Procedures and costs of judicial assistance in judicial expertise between Vietnam and foreign countries shall comply with laws on judicial assistance.

  Chapter VI

     COSTS OF LEGAL ASSISTANCE, POLICIES AND REGULATIONS IN LEGAL ASSISTANCE

  Article 36. Judicial assessment expense The ordering party or requesting party shall pay judicial assessment expense to the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider in accordance with law on judicial assessment expense.

  Article 37. Benefits of judicial assessors and participants in judicial assessment

  1. A judicial assessor, a subject-matter judicial assessor, an assistant to a judicial assessor who is receiving a salary from the state budget, or a person assigned by a competent authority to perform tasks shall receive case-specific judicial assessment allowances during the period of performing such tasks in cases involving autopsy, post-mortem examination, or exhumation.

  2. In addition to case-specific allowance for judicial expertise as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article, full-time public expert officers under public expert bodies shall be entitled to professional allowances and other allowances.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 38. Policies applicable to judicial assessment activities

  1. Private judicial assessment organizations shall be eligible for preferential treatment policies as prescribed by the Government.

  2. Judicial experts, judicial assessment organizations and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations that make active contributions to judicial assessment activities shall be honored and rewarded in accordance with the law on emulation and commendation.

  3. Ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Councils shall, based on their actual capabilities and conditions as well as competence, prescribe other regimes and policies to attract experts and competent organizations to participate in judicial assessment activities.

  Chapter VII

     RESPONSIBILITIES OF STATE AGENCIES FOR LEGAL ASSESSMENT ORGANIZATIONS AND ACTIVITIES

  Article 39. State administrative body for judicial assessment

  1. The Government shall perform the unified state management of judicial assessment.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall assist the Government in performing the unified state management of judicial assessment.

  3. The Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense, the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Construction, the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment, the Ministry of Transport, the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, Vietnam State Bank and other ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall perform state management and take responsibility before the Government for organization and operation of judicial assessment in the fields under their management; and cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in unified state management of judicial assessment.

  4. Provincial People’s Committees shall, within their tasks and powers, perform state management of judicial assessment in their localities.

  Article 40. Tasks and powers of the Ministry of Justice

  1. Promulgate or submit to competent regulatory agencies for promulgation legal normative documents on judicial assessment and guide the implementation of such documents.

  Take charge of developing and submitting to the Prime Minister general strategies, planning and plans for judicial assessment development; cooperate with ministries and ministerial agencies in developing strategies, planning and plans for judicial assessment development by sector.

  2. Have written opinions on establishment of public judicial assessment organizations under the authority to decide of ministries, ministerial agencies managing specialized fields and provincial People’s Committees; in necessary cases, request ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Committees to consider and establish public judicial assessment organizations to meet requirements for judicial assessment of proceedings.

  3. Develop training programs for legal knowledge of judicial experts; cooperate with ministries and ministerial agencies in organizing professional training and legal knowledge training for judicial experts.

  4. Compile and publish a general list of judicial assessors on the Ministry of Justice’s website.

  5. To urge the performance of state management tasks on judicial assessment by ministries, ministerial-level agencies and provincial-level People’s Committees; request ministries, ministerial-level agencies and provincial-level People’s Committees to report on the organization and operation of judicial assessment; and report to the Government on the organization and operation of judicial assessment nationwide.

  6. Take charge or request relevant ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Committees to organize inspection of judicial assessment organization and activities.

  7. Perform state management of international cooperation in judicial assessment.

  Article 41. Tasks and powers of ministries and ministerial agencies in charge of professional management of judicial assessment

  1. Promulgate or submit to competent regulatory agencies for promulgation legal normative documents on judicial expertise in the field of expertise under their management and guide the implementation thereof.

  2. Promulgate regulations on judicial assessment or provide guidance on application of professional regulations for judicial assessment activities at the request and according to the characteristics of the fields of assessment under their management.

  3. Take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in deciding establishment, consolidation and strengthening public judicial assessment organizations under their management as prescribed by this Law.

  4. Appoint and dismiss judicial experts within their competence; prepare and publish lists of subject-matter judicial experts and subject-matter judicial expertise service providers specified in Clause 1 Article 20 of this Law.

  5. Ensure funding, assessment equipment and facilities, and other necessary material conditions for public judicial assessment organizations under their management to meet assigned tasks and requirements.

  6. Annually, assess the quality of operation of judicial assessment organizations and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations and judicial assessors in the fields under their management.

  7. To stipulate conditions on material foundations, assessment equipment and means of judicial assessment offices in domains under their management.

  8. Develop and organize implementation of programs and plans for training in judicial expertise and legal knowledge for a contingent of public expert officers in the fields under their management.

  9. Inspect and settle complaints and denunciations about organization and operation of judicial assessment in fields under their management; cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in inspection and supervision of organization and operation of judicial assessment as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 40 hereof.

  10. Implement international cooperation in judicial expertise in the fields under their management.

  11. Annually, make a review of organization and operation of judicial expertise in fields under their management and send report to the Ministry of Justice for consolidation and reporting to the Government.

  Article 42. Tasks and powers of the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of National Defense

  In addition to the tasks and powers specified in Article 41 of this Law, the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of National Defense have the following tasks and powers:

  1. The Ministry of Health has the following tasks and powers:

  a) State management of forensic medicine and forensic psychiatry;

  b) Promulgate professional regulations in the fields of forensic medicine and forensic psychiatry;

  c) To specify standards for forensic examiners and forensic psychiatrists;

  d) Provide training and refresher training in forensic examination and forensic psychiatric examination and issue certificates of competency in forensic examination and forensic psychiatric examination as prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 7 hereof.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security has the following tasks and powers:

  a) State management of criminal technical assessment;

  b) Promulgate professional regulations in the field of criminal technical assessment;

  c) To specify criteria for criminal forensic examiners;

  d) Provide training and refresher training in criminal technical assessment and issue certificates of competence in criminal technical assessment as prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 7 hereof;

  dd) Carry out annual statistics on solicitation and assessment of judicial expertise performance and use of judicial expertise conclusions within the system of investigation authorities under their management;

  e) Guide investigation authorities under their management to apply regulations of law on solicitation for judicial assessment and evaluation and use of judicial assessment conclusions;

  g) Ensure funding and provide guidance on payment of judicial assessment expenses within the system of investigation authorities under their management;

  h) Annually, review and send reports to the Ministry of Justice on the situation of ordering and evaluating judicial assessment and use of judicial assessment conclusions in investigation authorities under their management.

  3. The Ministry of National Defense shall have the duties and powers specified in Points dd, e, g and h Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 43.- Tasks and powers of the provincial-level People’s Committees

  1. The provincial People's Committees have the following tasks and powers:

  a) Establish public judicial assessment organizations; decide to permit establishment of judicial assessment offices; prepare and publish lists of local judicial assessment organizations and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations;

  b) Appoint and dismiss judicial experts within their competence; prepare and publish lists of local judicial experts;

  c) Ensure funding, operational facilities, physical infrastructure, and other necessary conditions for local public judicial assessment organizations;

  d) Provide training in professional skills and legal knowledge for local judicial experts;

  dd) Annually assess the organization and quality of judicial assessment in their localities; ensure the quantity and quality of public expert officers and subject-matter experts to promptly meet the quality requirements for judicial assessment in local proceedings;

  e) Inspect, examine and settle complaints and denunciations about judicial expertise within their competence; cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in inspecting and examining judicial expertise organization and activities as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 40 of this Law;

  g) Report to the Ministry of Justice on the organization and operation of judicial expertise in their province, and send it to relevant ministries and ministerial-level agencies for performing state management tasks prescribed in Articles 40, 41 and 42 of this Law.

  2. Departments of Justice shall be responsible for assisting provincial People’s Committees in performing state management of judicial assessment in their provinces and cities; taking charge and cooperating with specialized agencies in assisting provincial People’s Committees in managing operation of judicial assessment offices.

  Specialized agencies of provincial People’s Committees shall assist the People’s Committees at the same level in performing state management over specialized judicial assessment fields and be answerable to the provincial People’s Committees for organizing and conducting judicial assessment activities within their respective fields of management; and shall cooperate with Departments of Justice in assisting the People’s Committees at the same level in performing state management over local judicial assessment.

  Article 44. Responsibilities of the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy

  1. Guide the application of laws on solicitation for judicial assessment and evaluation and use of judicial assessment conclusions in the system of People’s Courts and People’s Procuracies.

  2. Implement statistical regulations on solicitation and assessment of judicial expertise performance and use of judicial expertise conclusions in the system of People’s Courts, People’s Procuracies, and report to the National Assembly in annual reports.

  3. Cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in implementing regulations on statistics and reporting on solicitation and assessment of judicial expertise performance and use of judicial expertise conclusions.

  4. Ensure funding and provide guidance on payment of judicial assessment expenses in the system of People’s Courts and People’s Procuracies.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 45. Effect

  1. This Law takes effect on January 1, 2013.

  2. The Judicial Assessment Ordinance No. 24/2004/PL-UBTVQH11 ceases to be effective as from the date this Law takes effect.

  3. From the effective date of this Law, regulations of the Criminal Procedure Code, Civil Procedure Code and Administrative Procedure Law on judicial assessment which are different from those in this Law shall be applied.

  Article 46. Implementation guidelines

  The Government, the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall elaborate and guide the implementation of articles and clauses as assigned in the Law.

  This Law was passed on June 20, 2012, by the XIIIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 3rd session.
