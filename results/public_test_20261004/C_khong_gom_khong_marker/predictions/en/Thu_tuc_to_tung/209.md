                                                   ORDINANCE

                                         PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING VESSELS

  Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10;
  Pursuant to Resolution No. 11/2007/QH12 on the program for elaboration of laws and ordinances in 2008;
  The National Assembly’s Standing Committee promulgates the Ordinance on Procedures for Arrest of Seagoing Ships.

  Chapter 1.

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Ordinance provides for the competence and procedures for arresting seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims, application of provisional emergency measures, enforcement of civil judgments, provision of judicial assistance and the competence and procedures for releasing arrested seagoing ships.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Ordinance applies to Vietnamese agencies, organizations and individuals and foreign agencies, organizations and individuals involved in settlement of maritime claims, application of provisional urgent measures, enforcement of civil judgments and judicial assistance giving rise to the right to arrest seagoing ships.

  Article 3. Authority to decide arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The People’s Court of a province or central-affiliated city (hereinafter referred to as “provincial People’s Court”) where there is a seaport or inland waterway port (hereinafter referred to as “port”) and the seagoing ship subject to arrest is operating shall have jurisdiction to decide to arrest such seagoing ship to ensure the settlement of maritime claims, enforcement of civil judgments and execution of judicial requests of foreign Courts.

  In case a port has multiple wharves in different provinces or centrally-affiliated cities, the provincial People’s Court of the administrative division where the wharf where the seagoing ship requested for arrest is located and is operating shall have the jurisdiction to decide the arrest of such seagoing ship.

  2. The People’s Court in charge of the civil lawsuit or the provincial-level People's Court of the place where the arbitration council accepts the dispute shall have the jurisdiction to decide on the application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships.

  3. The Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court shall consider and decide on the competent court to make a decision to arrest a seagoing ship in case there is a dispute over jurisdiction among provincial-level People’s Courts.

  Article 4. Responsibilities for failure to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Persons who make requests for arrest of seagoing ships shall be responsible before law for their requests. In case their requests for arrest of seagoing ships are improper and cause damage, they must pay damages.

  2. All damage caused by the consequence of improper request for arrest of seagoing ships shall be settled by the parties themselves. In case they fail to reach agreement and there is a dispute, they may request a court or an arbitrator to settle it according to law provisions.

  3. If a court issues a decision to arrest a seagoing ship not in accordance with the reasons for request for arrest of the seagoing ship or the seagoing ship has made a request for arrest but fails to be arrested, causing damage, it shall pay compensation as prescribed by law.

  Article 5. Financial security measures for requirements for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The seagoing ship arrest requester shall apply financial security measures, except for cases specified at Points c and d, Clause 1, Article 44 of this Ordinance, in either or both of the following forms:

  a/ To submit to the Court guarantee documents in form of assets of banks or other credit institutions or of other individuals, agencies and organizations;

  b/ To remit a sum of money, precious metals, gemstones or valuable papers under the court's decision to enforce financial security measures into a safe account at the bank where the competent court is headquartered for the time limit of at least forty eight hours after receiving such decision.

  In case of applying a security measure for the purpose of arresting a seagoing ship on the last weekend or holiday, the collateral shall be temporarily deposited at the Court; the Court shall receive the money or valuable papers and seal and preserve them. On the following working day, the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship shall immediately deposit such property in a bank under the supervision of the Court.

  2. The financial security value determined by the Court is equivalent to the loss or damage that may arise as a consequence of the improper request for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. When deciding to release a seagoing ship under arrest, the judge shall consider measures to secure funds for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clause 1 of this Article, unless otherwise agreed by the parties. On a case-by-case basis, the judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Maintain financial security measures for the request for arrest of a seagoing ship if it is deemed inappropriate and the value of the financial security may be sufficient or insufficient to compensate for damage;

  b) Returning part of the financial security to the request for arrest of the seagoing ship if deeming that such request is improper and the financial security exceeds the damage compensation liability;

  c/ Returning all financial security value to the seagoing ship arrest request if the latter is considered justifiable.

  Article 6. Fees for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Persons requesting arrest of seagoing ships shall pay charges according to law provisions

  2. Fees for arresting seagoing ships to secure the settlement of maritime claims, application of provisional emergency measures, enforcement of civil judgments and performance of judicial entrustment by foreign courts shall be paid to Courts competent to decide on arrest of seagoing ships specified in Article 3 of this Ordinance within forty eight hours after the Courts request payment of fees.

  Article 7.- Supervising the observance of law in the arrest of seagoing ships.

  The People’s Procuracy shall supervise the compliance with laws on arrest of seagoing ships; exercise the right to make recommendations as prescribed by law in order to ensure the timely and lawful arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 8. Documents and evidence accompanying a request for arrest of a seagoing ship or a written request for release of a seagoing ship under arrest

  1. The request for arrest of a seagoing ship or the written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested must be accompanied by documents and evidence proving that the request for arrest of the seagoing ship or release of the seagoing ship being arrested is well-grounded and lawful.

  2. In case documents and evidence proving the request for arrest or release of a seagoing ship are in foreign languages, they must be enclosed with their Vietnamese translations which have been lawfully certified under Vietnam’s law. Documents and papers made, issued or certified by foreign competent authorities under foreign laws must be consular legalized, except for cases of exemption as provided for under international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  Article 9. Execution of decisions on arrest and release of seagoing ships

  1. Immediately after issuing a decision to arrest or release a seagoing ship under arrest, the court president shall assign a court official to execute the delivery of the decision as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  2. Within 12 hours after being assigned, the court officer shall come to the port and hand over two copies of the decision to the director of the maritime port office or the director of the inland waterway port office (hereinafter referred to as the port authority) where the seagoing vessel is operating under arrest. The director of the port authority shall execute the decision on arrest or release of the seagoing vessel under arrest in accordance with law and hand over one copy to the captain for execution.

  In case the court officials cannot reach the port within the above-said time limit, the decision may be sent by fax or e-mail as prescribed by law.

  3. The maritime, security, quarantine, customs, tax, culture and information, fire prevention and firefighting, environmental protection and other state management agencies shall coordinate in implementing the decision to arrest a seagoing ship at the request of the port authority director and are subject to the port authority director's direction in coordinating the implementation of the decision to arrest the seagoing ship.

  The Vietnam Coast Guard shall coordinate with the port authority in implementing decisions to arrest seagoing ships upon request of the port authority.

  4. During the period in which the seagoing ship is arrested, the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator shall maintain the operation of the seagoing ship.

  5. The Government shall provide for implementation of Court decisions specified in Clause 2 of this Article and handling of seagoing ships in case their owners abandon or put them up for auction while being arrested.

  Article 10. Notification of the execution of decisions on arrest of seagoing ships and release of arrested seagoing ships

  1. Directors of port authorities shall notify in writing the Courts, Vietnam Maritime Administration and relevant state management agencies at ports of the execution of decisions on arrest or release of seagoing ships being arrested; in case of arrest of seagoing ships for enforcement of civil judgments, they shall immediately notify such to civil judgment enforcement agencies for enforcement of civil judgments.

  2. The captain shall notify the ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator and other relevant persons of the arrest or release of the ship.

  Chapter II

    PROCEDURES FOR ARREST AND RELEASE OF SEAGOING SHIPS INVOLVED IN ARREST AND RE-ARRESTED SEAGOING SHIPS TO ENSURE THE SETTLEMENT OF CLAUSES

  Article 11. Maritime claims giving rise to the right to request arrest of seagoing ships

  A maritime claim giving rise to the right to request arrest of a seagoing ship means a claim about:

  1. Salaries, repatriation expenses, social insurance premiums and other amounts payable to crew members, officers and other crew members on board a ship;

  2. Compensation for loss of life, injury and other damage to human health directly related to the operation of the seagoing ship;

  3. Freight charges, maritime security charges, pilotage charges, port charges and other port charges and fees;

  4. Salaries for rescue of seagoing ships;

  5. Losses and damage to property outside of the contract directly related to the operation of the seagoing ship;

  6. Damage caused or threatened to be caused by the seagoing ship to the environment, the shore or related interests; measures applied to prevent, limit or eliminate such damage; compensation for such damage; expenses for reasonable practical measures already applied or to be applied to restore the environment; loss which has been caused or may be caused to a third party related to such damage; damage, expenses or losses specified in this clause;

  7. Expenses related to the raising, moving, salvage, destruction or harmless treatment of sunken, stranded or abandoned seagoing ships, including any objects on board and expenses or losses related to the preservation of abandoned seagoing ships and expenses for crew members of the seagoing ships;

  8. Agreements related to the use or hiring of seagoing ships, which are stipulated in ship-hiring contracts or in other forms;

  9. Agreements related to the transport of goods or passengers on board a seagoing ship, even if they are stipulated in the ship hire contract or in other forms;

  10. Losses or damage related to goods, including luggage transported on board the ship;

  11. General damage;

  12. Leading a ship;

  13. Using maritime pilots;

  14. Goods, materials, foodstuffs, fuels and equipment (including machinery and equipment) supplied or services provided for the purpose of operating, managing, preserving and maintaining seagoing ships;

  15. Building, repairing, restoring, modifying or equipping seagoing ships;

  16. The payment shall be made on behalf of the ship owner;

  17. Insurance premiums shall be paid by the ship owner or his/her representative or hirer of a bareboat;

  18. Commission and brokerage or agent fees related to seagoing ships which must be paid by ship owners, bareboat charterers or authorized persons;

  19. Disputes over the ownership of seagoing ships;

  20. Disputes among co-owners of seagoing ships over the use of their seagoing ships or revenues generated from their seagoing ships;

  21. Mortgaging seagoing ships;

  22. Disputes arising from contracts for sale of seagoing ships.

  Article 12. Right to request arrest of seagoing ships for the purpose of ensuring settlement of maritime claims

  The persons who file maritime complaints specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance may request the competent courts specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide to arrest seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of their complaints.

  Article 13. Conditions for arresting seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims

  1. When there is a request for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime claims specified in Article 12 of this Ordinance, the Court shall decide to arrest seagoing ships in the following cases:

  a) The ship owner is responsible for maritime claims at the time when such claims arise and remains the ship owner at the time of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  b) The bareboat operator is responsible for maritime claims at the time of arising and remains a bareboat operator or ship owner at the time of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  c/ This maritime claim on the basis of the mortgage of such seagoing ship;

  d) This maritime claim is related to the ownership or possession of such seagoing ship;

  dd) This maritime claim is secured by one maritime lien related to such seagoing ship.

  2. The arrest of a seagoing ship shall also be carried out against one or more seagoing ships under the ownership of the person who is responsible for the maritime claim and at the time such maritime claim arises, he/she is:

  a) Owners of seagoing ships related to the arising of maritime claims;

  b) Passenger on a bareboat, or on a fixed-boat or voyage charter of a seagoing ship related to the arising of maritime claims.

  3. Regulations in Clause 2 of this Article shall not apply to maritime claims related to the ownership of seagoing ships.

  Article 14. Time limit for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime claims

  1. The time limit for arresting a seagoing ship to ensure the settlement of a maritime claim shall not exceed thirty days from the date of arrest of such ship.

  2. During the period in which a seagoing ship is seized to secure settlement of a maritime claim, if the person requesting the seizure of the seagoing ship initiates a lawsuit at court or requests arbitration to settle a dispute and later requests the seizure of the seagoing ship, the period of seizure of the seagoing ship to secure settlement of the maritime claim shall end when the court issues a decision to apply or not to apply provisional urgent measures to seize the seagoing ship.

  Article 15. Application for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime claims

  1. Persons requesting arrest of seagoing ships to secure the settlement of maritime claims shall make written requests.

  2. An application for arrest of a seagoing ship to secure the settlement of a maritime claim shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of the application;

  b) Name of the Court receiving the application;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the seagoing ship arrest requester;

  d) Name, nationality, and IMO number of the seagoing ship as prescribed by the International Maritime Organization, tonnage, and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g/ Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or operator in case the payment by the lessee or operator is required;

  h) Specific maritime claims giving rise to the right to request arrest of seagoing ships and the maximum value of such maritime claims;

  i) Expected loss or damage arising from the requirement to arrest a seagoing ship.

  3. In case the person requesting arrest of a seagoing ship fails to fully and accurately know the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write down what he/she knows related to such matters.

  Article 16.- Sending written requests for arrest of seagoing ships in order to secure the settlement of maritime claims and enclosed documents and evidence The person requesting the arrest of a seagoing ship in order to secure the settlement of a maritime claim shall have to send such request and enclosed documents and evidence to the competent Court which decides on the arrest of the seagoing ship as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 17. Receipt of written requests for arrest of seagoing ships to secure settlement of maritime claims

  Right after receiving a written request for arrest of a seagoing ship to ensure the settlement of a maritime claim and enclosed documents and evidence, the competent court shall decide to arrest the seagoing ship specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance and record it in the book of receipt of requests. The court president shall immediately assign a judge to settle the request.

  Article 18. Consideration of requests for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime claims

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving a written request for arrest of a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider the request and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Accept applications for arrest of seagoing ships to secure settlement of maritime claims if it deems that conditions for issuing a decision on arrest of seagoing ships are fully met, request applicants to apply financial security measures for requests for arrest of seagoing ships and arrest charges;

  b) Return the request for arrest of a seagoing ship to secure the settlement of the maritime claim if it finds that the conditions for issuing a decision on arrest of the seagoing ship are not fully met or the settlement of the request does not fall within the jurisdiction of such Court.

  2. In case of deciding to return the application for arrest of a seagoing ship to secure the settlement of a maritime claim, the Court shall issue or send such decision together with the application and enclosed documents and evidence to the requester.

  Article 19. Complaints and settlement of complaints about decisions to return requests for arrest of seagoing ships for guarantee of settlement of maritime complaints

  1. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a decision to return a request for arrest of a seagoing ship in order to secure the settlement of a maritime claim, the requester may lodge a written complaint with the court president about such decision.

  2. Within twenty four hours after receiving a written complaint about a decision to return a request for arrest of a seagoing ship for settlement of the maritime complaint and enclosed documents and evidence, the court president shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime claims;

  b) Cancel the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of the maritime claim and receive back the request and enclosed documents and evidence for acceptance of the request for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. The decision to resolve the complaint of the Chief Justice of the Court is final.

  Article 20. Decision to arrest seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime claims

  1. The judge shall immediately issue a decision to arrest the seagoing ship to secure the settlement of the maritime claim when the requester for arrest of the seagoing ship produces the receipts and/or documents evidencing that he/she has applied the financial security measure for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, and has paid the fee for arrest of seagoing ship as prescribed in Article 6 of this Ordinance.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing ship for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of a maritime claim shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the seagoing ship arrest requester;

  d) Maritime claims giving rise to the right to request the Court to arrest the seagoing ship;

  dd) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the hirer and operator;

  i) The Court’s findings and legal grounds for accepting the petition;

  k) Court decisions

  3. Decisions to arrest seagoing ships for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of maritime claims shall take effect immediately, even in cases where complaints or petitions are lodged.

  4. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision to arrest a seagoing ship to the port authority for enforcement under Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; and send it to the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship or immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship is arrested involving foreign elements.

  Article 21. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints and recommendations about decisions to arrest seagoing ships for ensuring settlement of maritime complaints

  1. The captain, owner of a bareboat, hirer or operator of a bareboat shall have the right to lodge a written complaint with the court president about the decision to arrest the ship for the purpose of settling the maritime complaint. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty eight hours from the moment the captain receives the court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may make written recommendations to the Chief Justice of the Court on the decision to arrest a seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime claims. The time limit for making recommendations is forty eight hours from the time the Procuracy of the same level receives the Court's decision.

  2. Within forty eight hours after receiving a written complaint or petition against a decision to arrest a seagoing ship, the court president shall consider and settle it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to arrest a seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime claims;

  b) Cancel decisions on arrest of seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime claims;

  3. The decision to resolve complaints and petitions of the Chief Justice of the Court is final.

  Article 22. Grounds for release of seagoing ships being arrested to ensure settlement of maritime claims

  1. Seagoing ships being arrested to secure the settlement of maritime claims shall be immediately released when there is one of the following grounds:

  a) After the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator has taken measures to secure the replacement or fully pay the debt;

  b/ The obligation to provide assets of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator who has been guaranteed by another person to perform the task or have a written commitment from a reputable insurance organization. The Ministry of Finance shall notify the list of reputable insurance organizations;

  c/ At the request of the person who has requested the arrest of the seagoing ship;

  d) Decision to arrest a sunk ship;

  dd) The time limit for arrest of seagoing ships under court decisions has expired.

  2. The security interest shall be agreed upon by the parties. In case the parties fail to reach agreement on the level and form of security interest, the court shall decide the level and form of security interest which must not exceed the value of the seagoing ship seized or the property obligation is the basis for seizing the seagoing ship in case the property obligation is smaller than the value of the seagoing ship.

  Article 23. Request for release of seagoing ships being arrested to ensure settlement of maritime claims

  1. When there is one of the grounds prescribed at Points a, b and c, Clause 1, Article 22 of this Ordinance, the ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator, the captain, the person who has requested the arrest of the sea-going ship and other concerned persons may request the release of the arrested sea-going ship. Requests for the release of sea-going ships must be made in writing.

  2. A written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of making the written request;

  b) Name of the Court receiving the written request;

  c) Name and address of the seagoing ship requester who is being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Number and date of the decision to arrest the seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e) Reasons for requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  g) The applicant’s certainty of the arrest of the seagoing ship.

  Article 24. Sending written requests for release of seagoing ships being arrested to secure settlement of maritime claims

  The seagoing ship release applicant who is being arrested to secure the settlement of a maritime claim shall send the written request and enclosed documents and evidence to the court that has issued the decision on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  Article 25. Decision to release seagoing ships being arrested to secure settlement of maritime claims

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested to ensure the settlement of a maritime claim, the court president shall assign a judge to settle the release of the seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a written request for release of a seagoing ship which is being arrested to ensure the settlement of a maritime claim and enclosed documents and evidence, if finding there are grounds, the judge assigned to settle the release of the seagoing ship shall consider and issue a decision on the release of the seagoing ship. In case of refusal without finding any ground, the judge shall notify in writing the requester of the refusal, clearly stating the reason for the refusal.

  3. A decision to release a seagoing ship being arrested to secure the settlement of a maritime claim shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested, and the port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or ship operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i) Decisions of Courts.

  4. Decisions to release seagoing ships under arrest for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of maritime claims shall take immediate effect.

  5. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested to the port authority director for implementation according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; and send it to the requester for the release of the seagoing ship being arrested or immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship is released with foreign elements.

  Article 26. Arrest of seagoing ships for settlement of maritime claims

  1. A seagoing ship seized to secure settlement of a maritime claim which has been released or has been secured by an alternative measure shall not be seized again on the same maritime claim, except for the following cases:

  a) The total value of collateral provided is not sufficient to satisfy the obligation in case the total value of collateral provided is smaller than the value of the ship to be released;

  b) The guarantor performs the property obligation on behalf of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator fails to perform or cannot partially or fully perform the guaranteed property obligation;

  c/ The release of the ship or the cancellation of the alternative security measure shall be effected at the request of the seagoing ship arrest applicant on the grounds of plausible reasons;

  d/ The person requesting arrest of a seagoing ship cannot prevent the release of the ship or the cancellation of security measures, though necessary measures have already been applied.

  2. Not to be considered as seagoing ships to be released if the release of such ships is not decided by competent Courts or if they escape from their places of arrest, except for cases specified at Points d and dd, Clause 1, Article 22 of this Ordinance.

  3. Procedures for arresting a seagoing ship to ensure settlement of maritime claims shall comply with procedures for arresting seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime claims prescribed in this Chapter.

  Chapter III

              PROCEDURES FOR IMPLEMENTATION AND CANCELLATION OF TEMPORARY DETENTION MEASURES

  Article 27. Provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  The provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships is a measure applied in the course of settlement of civil lawsuits as prescribed in Clause 13, Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Code.

  Article 28. Right to request application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  In the course of settlement of civil lawsuits, involved parties and their lawful representatives may request the courts which are settling such lawsuits to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships for temporary settlement of their urgent requests, protection of evidences, preservation of the existing conditions in order to avoid irreparable damage or to ensure judgment execution when falling into one of the cases specified in Article 29 of this Ordinance.

  Article 29. Conditions for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  When there is a request for arrest of a seagoing ship as prescribed in Article 28 of this Ordinance, the Court shall decide to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship in the following cases:

  1. The seagoing ship is being seized to secure the settlement of a maritime claim, and the person requesting the seizure of the seagoing ship has initiated a civil lawsuit at court;

  2. The ship owner is a person who has property-related obligations in the case and remains the ship owner at the time of application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  3. The bareboat charterers, the fixed-term charter party lessees, the voyage charterers or ship operators are persons who have property-related obligations in civil lawsuits arising from maritime claims specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance and remain bareboat charterers, fixed-term charter party lessees, voyage charterers, ship operators or ship owners at the time of application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships;

  4. Disputes being settled in the lawsuits arising on the basis of the mortgage of such seagoing ships;

  5. Disputes are being settled in lawsuits related to the ownership or possession of such seagoing ships.

  Article 30. Application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Persons requesting application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall make written requests.

  2. An application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of the application;

  b) Name of the Court receiving the application;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the seagoing ship arrest requester;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g/ Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or operator in case the lessee or operator demands payment;

  h) Specific disputes being resolved in the lawsuit and their maximum value;

  i) Reasons for requesting application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships;

  k) Expected loss or damage that may arise due to improper application of provisional urgent measures for arrest of seagoing ships;

  l) The assessment of the applicant for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  m) Number, date and contents of the court's decision to arrest a seagoing ship for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of a maritime claim (if any).

  3. If the petitioner for application of provisional emergency measures does not know accurately and fully the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write down what he/she knows related to such matters.

  Article 31. Sending applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence The applicant for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall send an application and enclosed documents and evidence to the court which is handling the case.

  Article 32. Receipt of applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the court handling the case shall record in the register the receipt of the request.

  2. In case a court is settling a case and has competence to decide the arrest of a seagoing ship specified in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance, the application and enclosed documents and evidence shall be handled as follows:

  a) Immediately hand over the petition and enclosed materials and evidences to the Judge assigned to settle the lawsuit before the Court opens the hearing;

  b) Immediately hand over to the trial panel in charge of the lawsuit if the Court receives the petition and accompanying documents and evidences during the trial.

  Article 33. Consideration of applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Within three days after receiving a written request for application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider the request and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To accept applications for application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships if seeing that all conditions for issuing a decision to apply such measure are met, and at the same time request the applicants to provide financial security for the application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships and pay the seagoing ship arrest charge within the time limit set by the court without applying the time limits prescribed in Articles 5 and 6 of this Ordinance. When the requesters for application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships produce receipts and/or documents evidencing that they have provided the financial security and paid the seagoing ship arrest charge, the judge shall immediately issue a decision to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships;

  b) Return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship if it is considered that conditions for issuing a decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship are not fully met.

  2. After receiving a written request for application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and vouchers, the trial panel shall immediately consider and make one of the decisions specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. In case of decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the Court shall issue or immediately send such decision together with the application and enclosed documents and evidence to the applicant; the trial panel shall publicly notify the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship at the court hearing and record such notification in the minutes of the court hearing.

  Article 34. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints, recommendations about decisions to return applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Within three working days after receiving a decision to return an application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the applicant may lodge a written complaint with the court president about such decision.

  2. Within three working days after receiving a written complaint about a decision to return an application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the court president shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  b) Cancel the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and receive the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship together with enclosed documents and evidence for acceptance of the application for arrest of a seagoing ship.

  3. After receiving the notice of disclosure of the decision to return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship at the court session, the requester may lodge a complaint and the Procuracy may make a recommendation to the trial panel against such decision which must be recorded in the minutes of the court session. The trial panel shall promptly settle the complaint or recommendation and issue one of the decisions specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The Chief Judge’s decision to resolve the complaint, the decision to resolve the complaint and recommendation of the Trial panel shall be final.

  Article 35. Decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. A decision to apply a provisional urgent measure of arrest of a seagoing ship must contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the applicant for application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships;

  d) Reasons for requesting application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships;

  dd) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the hirer and operator;

  i) The Court’s findings and legal grounds for accepting the petition;

  k) Decisions of Courts.

  2. Decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaints or petitions.

  3. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship to the port authority director for execution according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision immediately to the procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision immediately to the requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship; and send such decision immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the arrested seagoing ship involves foreign elements.

  Article 36. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints and recommendations about decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. The captain, owner, hirer or operator of a ship may lodge a written complaint with the court president about the decision to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of the ship. The time limit for lodging a complaint is 3 working days after the captain receives the court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level shall be entitled to make written proposal to the Chief Justice of the Court on the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship. The time limit for making such proposal is 3 working days after the Procuracy of the same level receives the Court's decision.

  2. Within three working days after receiving a written complaint or petition against a decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the court president shall consider settling it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships;

  b) Cancel the decision to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ships.

  3. At the hearing, the settlement of complaints and petitions shall fall under the jurisdiction of the Trial panel. The Trial panel shall consider settling them and issue one of the decisions specified in clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The decision to resolve complaints and petitions of the Chief Judge or the Trial panel is final.

  Article 37. Grounds for annulment of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  Decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall be annulled when conditions for application of provisional urgent measures no longer exist or at the request of persons requesting application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships.

  Article 38. Request for cancellation of decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship

  1. The following persons may request in writing to cancel the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship:

  a) The person requesting the application of the provisional urgent measure to arrest a seagoing ship;

  b) The ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator, the captain and other relevant persons when conditions for application of provisional emergency measures to arrest seagoing ships no longer exist.

  2. Written request for cancellation of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of making the written request;

  b) Name of the Court receiving the written request;

  c) Name and address of the person requesting cancellation of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Number and date of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e) Reasons for requesting cancellation of decision on application of provisional urgent measure of arrest of seagoing ship.

  Article 39. Sending written requests for cancellation of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  The requester for cancellation of a decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall send such written request and enclosed documents and evidence to the court that has issued the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest such seagoing ship.

  Article 40. Processing written requests for cancellation of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  In case the Court is handling a case and receives a written request for cancellation of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, such request shall be handled as follows:

  1. To immediately hand over the case to the Judge assigned to settle it if the Court receives a written request before opening the hearing;

  2. Immediately deliver to the trial panel in charge of the lawsuit the Court’s written request during the trial.

  Article 41. Decision on cancellation of decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Upon receiving a written request for cancellation of the decision to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of a seagoing ship and accompanying documents and evidence, the assigned judge or the trial panel shall consider issuing a decision to cancel the decision to apply the provisional urgent measure of arrest of the seagoing ship if there are sufficient grounds. In case of refusal due to insufficient grounds, the judge or the trial panel must notify in writing the requester of the reasons for refusal.

  2. A decision to cancel a decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision on annulment of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Number and date of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or operator;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  i) Reasons for annulment of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships;

  k) Decisions of Courts.

  3. The decision to cancel the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall take immediate effect.

  4. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision on cancellation of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship to the port authority director for execution according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision immediately to the procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision immediately to the requester for cancellation of the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship; and send such decision immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the arrested seagoing ship is released involving foreign elements.

  Article 42. Application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships in case arbitrators settle disputes

  During the process of dispute settlement by arbitrators, involved parties and their lawful representatives may request competent courts defined in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide on the application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships. The procedures for deciding on the application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall comply with the provisions of this Chapter.

  Chapter IV

              PROCEDURES FOR ARREST AND RELEASE OF SEAGOING VESSELS INVOLVED IN CRIME

  Article 43. Right to request arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  Through competent civil judgment enforcement agencies, judgment creditors under Vietnamese civil judgment enforcement laws shall be entitled to request competent Courts specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide to arrest seagoing ships for judgment enforcement.

  Article 44. Conditions for arresting seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. When there is a request for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment execution as prescribed in Article 43 of this Ordinance, the court shall decide to arrest seagoing ships in the following cases:

  a) The ship owner is the judgment debtor in terms of assets and remains the ship owner at the time of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  b/ The bareboat charterers, the fixed-term boat charterers, the voyage charterers or the ship operators are the judgment debtors for assets in civil lawsuits arising from maritime claims specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance and are still bareboat charterers, fixed-term boat charterers, voyage charterers, ship operators or ship owners at the time of arrest of the seagoing ships;

  c/ The obligation to execute the judgment on assets mortgaged with such seagoing ship;

  d/ Judgment execution obligation means the return of such seagoing ship to the judgment creditor.

  2. The court shall decide to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement only when the civil judgment enforcement agency cannot apply property distraint or other coercive measures for judgment enforcement, except for cases specified in Points c and d Clause 1 of this Article or the judgment debtor is overseas and does not have any other property in Vietnam.

  Article 45. Applications for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. The person requesting arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement shall make an application enclosed with a copy of the court’s judgment or decision or a copy of the arbitral award.

  2. An application for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of the application;

  b) Name of civil judgment enforcement authority receiving the application;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the seagoing ship arrest requester;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be arrested, port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g/ The name, address and nationality of the hirer or operator in case the hirer or operator demands payment;

  h) The obligation to execute assets under a court judgment or decision or an arbitral award;

  i) Reasons for request for arrest of seagoing ships;

  k) Expected loss or damage that may arise due to improper requirements for arrest of seagoing ships.

  3. In case the person requesting arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement does not know accurately and fully the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write down what he/she knows related to such matters.

  Article 46.- Sending applications for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment execution and enclosed documents and evidence The requesters for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment execution shall have to send their applications and enclosed documents and evidence to the competent civil judgment-executing bodies so that they may be forwarded to the competent courts defined in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 47. Receipt of written requests for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. Upon receiving a written request for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment execution and enclosed documents and evidence, the civil judgment-executing agency shall record in the book of receipt of the request and send a written transfer of such request and enclosed documents and evidence to the competent court for decision on arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance. The written transfer of the request must clearly state the reason for the failure to apply the measure of distraint of assets or other coercive measures for judgment execution.

  2. Upon receiving the written request for transfer of the civil judgment enforcement agency’s application enclosed with the request for arrest of the seagoing ship and documents and evidence, the competent court shall decide to arrest the seagoing ship specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance and record it in the application register. The court president shall immediately assign a judge to settle the application.

  Article 48. Consideration of requests for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving the documents specified in Clause 2, Article 47 of this Ordinance, the judge shall consider the application and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Accept a request for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement if it finds that all conditions for issuing a decision to arrest the seagoing ship are met, request the requester to take financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship and pay the seagoing ship arrest charge, except for cases where no financial security measure is required;

  b/ Returning the written request for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement if deeming that conditions for issuing a decision to arrest such ships are not fully met or the handling of the request does not fall under the jurisdiction of such Court.

  2. In case of decision to return the request for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement, the Court shall send such decision to the competent civil judgment-enforcing agency; issue or immediately send such decision together with the request and enclosed documents and evidence to the requester.

  Article 49. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints, recommendations about decisions to return requests for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a decision to return an application for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement, the requester may lodge a complaint or the competent civil judgment enforcement agency may file a recommendation to the court president on such decision. Complaints and recommendations must be made in writing.

  2. Within twenty four hours after receiving a written complaint or petition against a decision to return a request for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement and enclosed documents and evidences, the court president shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  b) Cancel the decision to return the request for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement and receive back the request and enclosed documents and evidence for acceptance of the arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. The Chief Judge’s decision on resolution of complaints and petitions shall be final.

  Article 50. Decision to arrest seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. The judge shall immediately issue a decision to arrest the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement when the requester produces vouchers and documents proving that he/she has taken financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, except for cases where financial security measures are not required and the seagoing ship arrest fee has been paid as prescribed in Article 6 of this Ordinance.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the person requesting arrest of the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  d) Reasons for requesting arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement;

  dd) Name of civil judgment-enforcing agency;

  e) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be arrested, port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  i) Name, address and nationality of the hirer and operator;

  k) The Court’s findings and legal grounds for accepting the petition;

  l) Decisions of Courts.

  3. The decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaint or petition.

  4. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement to the port authority director for enforcement under Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision immediately to the same-level Procuracy; issue or send such decision immediately to the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement and the competent civil judgment enforcement agency; and send such decision immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship to be arrested involves foreign elements.

  Article 51. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints, recommendations about decisions to arrest seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. The ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator and the captain of the ship may lodge a written complaint with the court president about the decision to arrest the sea-going ship for judgment execution. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty eight hours from the moment the captain receives the court's decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may make written proposal to the Chief Justice of the Court on the decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement. The time limit for making such proposal is forty eight hours after the Procuracy of the same level receives the Court's decision.

  2. Within forty eight hours after receiving a written complaint or petition against a decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement, the court president shall consider and settle it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  b) Cancel the decision to arrest the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement.

  3. The decision to resolve complaints and petitions of the Chief Justice of the Court is final.

  Article 52. Grounds for release of seagoing ships being arrested for enforcement of judgments

  1. A seagoing ship being arrested for enforcement of a judgment shall be immediately released when there is one of the following grounds:

  a) After the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator being the judgment debtor has taken measures to secure the replacement or has fulfilled the judgment execution obligation;

  b/ The obligation to provide assets of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator, which has been guaranteed by another person, shall be performed by the insurance organization with a good reputation;

  c/ At the request of the person who has requested the arrest of the seagoing ship.

  2. The security interest shall be agreed upon by the parties. In case the parties fail to reach agreement on the level and form of security interest, the court shall decide the level and form of security interest which must not exceed the value of the seagoing ship seized or the property obligation is the basis for seizing the seagoing ship in case the property obligation is smaller than the value of the seagoing ship.

  Article 53. Request for release of seagoing ships being arrested for enforcement of judgments

  1. When there is one of the grounds prescribed in Clause 1, Article 52 of this Ordinance, the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator, the captain and other persons concerned shall have to request in writing the release of the seagoing ship which is being arrested for judgment execution.

  2. A written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of making the written request;

  b) Name of the Court receiving the written request;

  c) Name and address of the seagoing ship requester who is being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Number and date of the decision to arrest the seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e) Reasons for request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested.

  Article 54. Sending written requests for release of seagoing ships being arrested for judgment enforcement

  The petitioner for release of a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall send documents and accompanying evidence to the court that has issued the decision on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  Article 55. Decision to release seagoing ships being arrested for enforcement of judgments

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement, the court president shall assign a judge to settle the release of such seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a written request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider and issue a decision to release the seagoing ship if finding there are grounds. In case of refusal without grounds, the judge shall notify in writing the requester of such refusal, clearly stating the reason for the refusal.

  3. A decision to release a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) The name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being released; the port where the seagoing ship is released;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or ship operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reasons for the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i) Decisions of Courts.

  4. The decision to release a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall take immediate effect.

  5. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement to the director of the port office for enforcement under Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision immediately to the procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision immediately to the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement and the competent civil judgment enforcement agency; and send such decision immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship is released with foreign elements.

  Chapter 5.

   PROCEDURES FOR ARREST AND RELEASE OF SEAGOING VESSELS ARRESTED FOR LEGAL PROTECTION

  Section 1. LEGAL PROTECTION FOR FOREIGN COURTS IN HOLDING SEAGOING VESSELS

  Article 56. Execution of legal mandates for foreign Courts to arrest seagoing ships

  1. In the course of settlement of a case at court or a dispute at arbitration, if there is a request for detention of a seagoing ship, the competent Vietnamese court defined in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall entrust the foreign competent court to arrest the seagoing ship.

  2. Vietnamese Courts shall request foreign Courts to arrest seagoing ships according to Vietnam’s law and treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory or on the reciprocity principle.

  Article 57. Legal mandate documents Legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships shall contain the following principal contents:

  1. Date and place of making of the written request for judicial entrustment;

  2. Name and address of the Vietnamese judicial requesting Court;

  3. Name and address of the foreign Court executing judicial requests;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  5. Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  6. Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or operator;

  7. Reasons for judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships;

  8. Time limit for arrest of seagoing ships;

  9. The person shall be responsible for damage or loss caused by improper requests for arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 58. Procedures for judicial entrustment of arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Vietnamese competent courts, which have been authorized by foreign competent courts to arrest seagoing ships, shall make and send to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam their request for judicial assistance. A request for judicial assistance must contain the following documents:

  a) A written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships from a competent Vietnamese court;

  b) Legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships;

  c/ Other papers at the request of the competent court of the entrusted country.

  2. Within ten working days after receiving a dossier of judicial request for arrest of a seagoing ship, the Ministry of Justice shall record in the judicial request book, check the validity of the dossier and transfer it to the competent Court of the foreign country under the provisions of an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the foreign country are members or through the diplomatic channel. In case the dossier is invalid, the Ministry of Justice shall return it to the Court which has compiled the dossier and clearly state the reason therefor.

  3. Within five working days after receiving a written notice of results of execution of judicial request from a competent foreign court, the Ministry of Justice shall forward such notice to the Vietnamese competent court which has sent the request for arrest of seagoing ships.

  Section 2. EXECUTION OF FOREIGN COURT’S JUDGMENT ON SEIZURE OF SEAGOING VESSELS

  Article 59. Principles of mutual legal assistance in arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Judicial assistance in the arrest of seagoing ships between Vietnamese courts and foreign courts shall be provided on the principle of respecting independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of each country, not interfering in each other's internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit, in conformity with international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party and Vietnamese law.

  2. In case the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and foreign countries have not yet signed or acceded to international treaties providing for mutual legal assistance in the arrest of seagoing ships, mutual legal assistance in the arrest of seagoing ships may be accepted by Vietnamese courts on the principle of reciprocity, but must not contravene Vietnamese law, international law and international practice.

  Article 60. Principles of judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Vietnamese Courts shall execute legal mandates of foreign Courts on arrest of seagoing ships in accordance with international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory or on the reciprocity principle.

  2. Vietnamese Courts shall refuse to accept legal mandates of foreign Courts on arrest of seagoing ships in the following cases:

  a/ The performance of legal mandates on arrest of seagoing ships which infringe upon Vietnam’s sovereignty or threaten Vietnam’s security;

  b) The performance of judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships does not fall under the jurisdiction of Vietnamese Courts.

  Article 61. Procedures for judicial entrustment of arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The foreign Court's judicial request for Vietnam's Court to arrest a seagoing ship must be made in writing and sent to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam under the provisions of an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party or Vietnamese law.

  2. The Ministry of Justice of Vietnam, upon receiving a written request for judicial assistance in arresting a seagoing ship, shall immediately forward it to the Vietnamese Court with competence to decide on the arrest of seagoing ships as stipulated in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 62. Legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships

  A judicial request for arrest of a seagoing ship shall contain the following principal contents:

  1. Date and place of making of the written request for judicial entrustment;

  2. Name and address of the foreign judicial requesting court;

  3. Name and address of Vietnamese courts executing judicial requests;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  5. Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  6. Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or operator;

  7. Reasons for judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships;

  8. Time limit for arrest of seagoing ships;

  9. The person shall be responsible for damage or loss caused by improper requests for arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 63. Receipt of legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Upon receiving a written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence, the Vietnamese Ministry of Justice shall record it in the book and send a written notification of such request together with enclosed documents and evidence to the competent Court which shall decide on the arrest of seagoing ships specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  2. Upon receiving a written request for judicial assistance in arresting a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the competent court to decide on arrest of seagoing ships specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record it in the book of receipt. The court president shall immediately assign a judge to settle the written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ship.

  Article 64. Examination of legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving a written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider issuing one of the following decisions:

  a) Accept a written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships if it is deemed to ensure the principles of mutual legal assistance and execution of requests for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships;

  b) Return the legal mandate document on arrest of seagoing ships if it is deemed to violate the principles of mutual legal assistance or the principles for execution of legal mandates on arrest of seagoing ships or the settlement of legal mandate documents on arrest of seagoing ships does not fall within the jurisdiction of such Court.

  2. In case of decision to return a letter of request for arrest of seagoing ships, the Court shall immediately send such decision together with the letter of request for arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam for notification to the foreign Court.

  Article 65. Decision to arrest a seagoing ship pursuant to a foreign Court's judicial request

  1. The judge shall issue a decision to arrest a seagoing ship under judicial mandate of a foreign Court immediately after the seagoing ship arrest requester has produced a bill or document evidencing that he/she has taken financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, and has paid the seagoing ship arrest fee specified in Article 6 of this Ordinance in Vietnam, except otherwise provided for by an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing ship pursuant to a judicial request of a foreign Court must contain the following principal details:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Vietnamese Court issuing the decision;

  c) Name of the foreign Court providing judicial assistance;

  d) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting the arrest of a seagoing ship pursuant to a judicial request of a foreign Court;

  dd) Reasons for request for arrest of seagoing ships;

  e) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the ship is operating;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  i) Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or operator;

  k) Judgments of the Court and legal grounds for acceptance of a written request for judicial entrustment on arrest of seagoing ships;

  l) Decisions of Courts.

  3. Decisions on arrest of seagoing ships under judicial requests of foreign Courts shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaints or petitions.

  4. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision on arrest of a seagoing ship under judicial mandate of a foreign Court to the port authority for execution according to Article 9 of this Ordinance, and send such decision to the Ministry of Justice and the Procuracy of the same level.

  Article 66. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints and recommendations about decisions on arrest of seagoing ships under judicial requests of foreign Courts

  1. The captain, owner of the ship, the ship hirer and the ship operator may lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court about the decision to arrest the seagoing ship under judicial mandate of the foreign Court. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty eight hours from the time the captain receives the decision of the Court.

  The Procuracy of the same level may make written recommendations to the Chief Justice of the Court on the decision to arrest a seagoing ship under judicial mandate of the foreign Court. The time limit for making recommendations is forty eight hours from the time the Procuracy of the same level receives the decision of the Court.

  2. Within forty eight hours after receiving a written complaint or petition specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the court president shall consider settling it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold decisions on arrest of seagoing ships under judicial requests of foreign Courts.

  b) To cancel decisions on arrest of seagoing ships under judicial requests of foreign Courts.

  3. The decision to resolve complaints and petitions of the Chief Justice of the Court is final.

  Article 67. Grounds for release of seagoing ships being seized under judicial requests A seagoing ship being seized under a judicial request shall be immediately released when there is one of the following grounds:

  1. Decision on arrest of seagoing ship shall be annulled

  2. The time limit for arrest of seagoing ships under court decisions has expired;

  3. At the request of the foreign Court, which has entrusted the arrest of a seagoing ship;

  Article 68.- Requests for release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandates In cases specified in Clause 3, Article 67 of this Ordinance, the foreign Court shall have to make a written request for the release of the seagoing ship. A written request for the release of a seagoing ship being arrested under a judicial mandate must contain the following principal details:

  1. Date and place of making the written request;

  2. Name and address of the foreign Court requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  3. Name of the Vietnamese Court receiving the written request;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  5. Serial number and date of the decision to arrest a seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  6. Reasons for requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested.

  Article 69. Sending written requests for release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandates Foreign Courts shall send written requests for release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandates and enclosed documents and evidence to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam for the latter to forward them to the Court that has issued the decision on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  Article 70. Decision on release of seagoing ships being seized under judicial requests

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate, the Chief Justice of the Court shall assign a judge to settle the release of such seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge assigned to settle the case shall consider issuing a decision to release the seagoing ship if finding there are grounds. In case of refusal for lack of grounds, the judge shall notify in writing the requester for release of the seagoing ship being arrested, clearly stating the reason for refusal.

  3. A decision to release a seagoing ship being seized under judicial mandate must contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Vietnamese Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be released; port where the seagoing ship is being arrested to be released;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship lessee or ship operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reasons for the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i) Decisions of Courts.

  4. Decisions on release of seagoing ships being seized under judicial requests of foreign Courts shall take immediate effect.

  5. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate of a foreign Court to the port authority director for execution according to Article 9 of this Ordinance, and send such decision to the Justice Ministry and the Procuracy of the same level.

  Chapter 6.

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 71. Effect

  This Ordinance takes effect on July 1, 2009.

  Article 72. Implementation guidelines

  The Government, the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall, within the ambit of their respective functions and tasks, guide the implementation of this Ordinance.
