                                                     DECREE

     ELABORATION OF THE ORDER AND PROCEDURES FOR PREPARING AND PAYING PROCEDURAL EXPENSES

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated February 18, 2025;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on amendments to the Law on Securities, the Law on Accounting, the Law on Independent Audit, the Law on State Budget, the Law on Management and Use of Public Property, the Law on Tax Administration, the Law on Personal Income Tax, the Law on National Reserves, and the Law on Actions against administrative violations dated November 29, 2024;

  Pursuant to the December 11, 2024 Ordinance on Procedural Expenses;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government shall promulgate a Decree elaborating on procedures for preparing and paying procedural expenses.

  Chapter I

                                                 GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Decree provides for procedures for preparing and paying procedural expenses.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Decree applies to competent procedural authorities and agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the estimation and payment of procedural expenses.

  Article 3. Estimation, allocation and settlement of funding for payment of procedural expenses

  The estimation, allocation and settlement of procedural expenses shall comply with regulations of law on state budget, accounting laws and relevant laws. This Decree provides the following guidelines:

  1. Make a cost estimate

  Every year, at the time of preparing the state budget estimate, based on the actual payment of procedural expenses incurred in the previous year and the estimated payment for the current year, the competent procedural authority and relevant agencies shall prepare an estimate of procedural expenses according to the provisions of the Ordinance on Procedural Expenses, incorporate it into their own estimates, and submit them to the superior management agency for review and consolidation into the first-level estimate sent to the finance authority at the same level for submission to the competent authority in accordance with the law on the state budget.

  2. Allocation of estimates

  After being assigned by the Prime Minister or People’s Committee, the central or local unit in charge of budget estimation shall allocate and assign the budget estimate to the units using the attached budget and the subordinate budget units according to regulations of law on state budget. In particular, for the allocation and assignment of recurrent expenditure estimates for payment of procedural costs, the allocated amount shall be included in the funding not subject to the autonomy regime of the agency/unit.

  3. Settlement

  Agencies and units shall be responsible for finalizing expenditures on payment of procedural costs in accordance with this Decree and consolidating them in their annual budget finalization reports in accordance with laws on finalization of recurrent state budget expenditures, accounting laws, guiding documents, and other relevant laws.

  Chapter II

    PROVISIONS ON PROCEDURES FOR ADJUSTMENT AND PAYMENT OF PROCEDURAL COSTS BETWEEN AGENCIES WITH JURISDICTION TO IMPLEMENT PROCEDURES AND AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND INDIVIDUALS EXECUTING PROCEDURAL ACTIVITIES

  Article 4. Procedures for payment of on-site inspection and appraisal expenses; on-site inspection expenses

  1. After completing on-site inspection and appraisal or on-site review, the person invited to participate in on-site inspection and appraisal, the person invited to participate in on-site review, or the person conducting the on-site review shall submit a set of payment request dossier to the competent procedural authority by one of the following methods: via postal service, in person, or online if the infrastructure and technical conditions are met.

  2. Application for payment of expenses for persons invited to participate in on-site assessment, persons invited to participate in on-site examination, and persons conducting on-site examination shall include:

  a) Payment request form using Form No. 1 enclosed herewith.

  b) The attached documents to the payment request are the original invoices and relevant legal documents to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred when being invited to participate in on-site or site-based review or consideration or when conducting on-site consideration.

  3. In case the payment request is incomplete as prescribed, within 03 days from the receipt of the request, the competent procedural authority shall notify the payer and provide an explanation for supplementation as prescribed. Within 10 days from the receipt of a complete and valid request, the competent procedural authority shall pay the expenses.

  4. Competent procedural authorities shall pay service fees and other costs (if any) under contracts, invoices, and service charge receipts in accordance with the law.

  Article 5. Procedures for advance payment and settlement of property valuation expenses

  1. In case of need to advance property valuation expense, within 5 working days from the date on which request for property valuation is received from competent procedural authorities, property valuation council shall send application for advance to competent procedural authorities in one of the methods via postal services, in person or online if meet infrastructure and technical requirements for advance procedures.

  2. The application for advance of property valuation expense includes:

  a) The written request for advance of property valuation expense made using Form No. 2 enclosed herewith.

  b) The table for calculating total costs of asset valuation and the basis for calculating costs of asset valuation.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete application for advance payment from the Property Pricing Council, the competent procedural authority shall make the advance payment. The maximum advance payment shall not exceed the property valuation cost calculated by the Property Pricing Council and included in the application for advance payment as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Within 10 days from the date of obtaining the valuation results, the Property Valuation Council shall submit a request for payment of valuation costs to the competent procedural authority by one of the following methods: via postal services, in person, or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technical requirements for payment procedures are met.

  5. The application for payment of valuation expense shall include:

  a) Payment request form using Form No. 1 enclosed herewith.

  b) Documents attached to the payment request are written requests for valuation; original copies of relevant invoices and legal documents to prove necessary expenses, consistent with the expenses determined in Articles 21, 22, 23, and 24 of the Ordinance on Expenses arising during the valuation process.

  6. In case the application for payment of valuation expense is incomplete or invalid, within 3 days from the receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority shall notify the Council of property valuation and provide an explanation for supplementation as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the application for payment of valuation expense in full and properly, the competent procedural authority shall pay the valuation expense to the property valuation council as prescribed.

  If the paid advance on valuation expense is insufficient, the competent procedural authority shall pay the deficit.

  In case the advance payment exceeds the valuation cost, the property valuation council shall refund the difference within 2 working days from the date of submission of the application for payment of valuation costs.

  Article 6. Procedures for advance payment and payment of assessment expense

  1. Procedures for advance payment of assessment expense

  a) In civil procedures and administrative procedures

  The procedures for advance payment of assessment expense shall comply with Article 38 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  b/ In criminal proceedings, procedures for advance payment of assessment expense shall comply with Article 44 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  Within 5 working days from the date on which decision on request for expertise is received from competent procedural authorities, entities shall submit application for advance of expertise costs to competent procedural authorities by post or in person or online if infrastructure and technical requirements for advance payment are met.

  2. The application for advance of assessment expense includes:

  a) The application form for advance shall be made using Form No. 2 enclosed herewith.

  b) The table for calculating total assessment expense and the basis for calculation of assessment expense.

  3. Time limit for payment and level of advance on assessment expense

  a/ In civil and administrative procedures: The time limit for payment of advance on assessment expense shall comply with Article 38 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  b/ In criminal proceedings: The time limit for payment of advance on assessment expense shall comply with the notice of advance request of the organization or person conducting assessment specified in Clause 2, Article 44 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  c) The maximum advance shall not exceed the assessment expense calculated by the assessing organization or individual and included in the application for advance as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Within 10 days from the date of obtaining the appraisal results, the appraiser shall submit a request for payment of valuation expense in writing to the competent procedural authority by post or in person or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technical requirements for payment procedures are met.

  5. The application for payment of assessment expense shall include:

  a) Payment request form using Form No. 1 enclosed herewith.

  b) The attached documents to the payment request are the original invoices and relevant legal documents to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred during the assessment process.

  6. In case the application for payment of assessment expense is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority shall notify the applicant and provide an explanation for supplementation as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for payment of assessment expense, the competent procedural authority shall pay the expense to the organization or individual conducting the assessment in accordance with regulations.

  In case the paid advance on assessment expense is insufficient, the competent procedural authority shall pay the deficit.

  In case the advance is higher than the assessment expense, the assessment organization or individual must refund the difference within 2 working days from the date of sending the application for payment of assessment expense.

  Article 7. Procedures for paying expenses for lay assessors

  1. Within 10 days from the end of a trial, the lay assessor shall send a payment request to the Court by one of the methods via postal services, in person or online if the conditions for infrastructure and technical requirements for payment procedures are met.

  2. The application for payment of expenses for the lay assessors shall include:

  a) Payment request form using Form No. 1 enclosed herewith.

  b) The enclosed documents of the payment request are the schedule of time for attending the Court session and the time for study of the dossier of the Lay assessor certified by the presiding judge; the originals of relevant invoices and legal documents to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred as prescribed in Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5, Article 46 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  3. If the application for payment of expenses for the lay assessor is incomplete and valid, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the application, the Court shall notify the lay assessor and clearly state the reason for supplementation as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for payment of expenses for the lay assessors, the Court shall pay the expenses for the lay assessors in accordance with regulations.

  Article 8. Payment of expenses for lawyers, legal aid professionals and people’s advocates in case of appointment of defense counsels in criminal proceedings

  1. Procedures for paying expenses for lawyers and legal aid professionals in case of appointment of defense counsels shall comply with regulations of law on lawyers and legal aid.

  2. Procedures for paying expenses for people’s advocate in case of appointment of advocate shall be carried out as follows:

  a) The application for payment of expenses for people’s advocate includes:

  Payment request form No. 1 enclosed herewith.

  The enclosed documents of the payment request are the schedule of working time of the people’s advocate with certification by the competent procedural authority and proceeding officers; the originals of relevant invoices and legal documents to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred as prescribed in Points b, c, d and dd Clause 2 Article 49 of the Ordinance on Procedural Expenses.

  b) After completing each stage of legal proceedings (investigation, prosecution, adjudication), the people’s advocate shall send the payment request to the competent procedural authority to request the appointment of a defense counsel who is a people’s advocate by any of the methods via postal services, in person or online on the electronic environment if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technology for payment procedures are met.

  c) In case the application for payment of expenses for people’s advocate is inadequate and invalid, within 03 days from the date on which the application is received, the competent procedural authority shall notify the people’s advocate and provide explanation for supplementation of the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for payment of expenses for a public defender, the competent procedural authority shall pay such expenses in accordance with regulations.

  Article 9. Payment for witnesses, interpreters and translators

  1. After completing the witnessing or translation, the witness, witness, interpreter, or translator shall submit a payment request to the competent procedural authority by post, in person, or online if infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures are met.

  2. The application for payment of expenses for witnesses, interpreters and translators shall include:

  a) A written request for payment of expenses for witnesses, interpreters and translators, made using Form No. 1 enclosed herewith.

  b) The attached documents to the payment request are the original invoices and relevant legal documents to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by witnesses, interpreters, translators who have paid their fees when witnessing or interpreting as prescribed.

  3. In case the application for payment of expenses for witnesses, interpreters or translators is incomplete and invalid, within 03 days from the date on which the application is received, the competent procedural authority shall notify the witnesses, interpreters or translators and provide explanation for supplementation as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid payment request, the competent procedural authority shall pay expenses for witnesses, interpreters, translators.

  Article 10. Payment of costs of overseas legal mandates

  1. Procedures for payment of costs of overseas judicial entrustment in civil procedures, administrative procedures and criminal procedures shall comply with regulations of law on judicial assistance.

  2. Procedures for payment of expenses for service and notification of procedural documents abroad in civil procedures and administrative procedures through overseas representative missions of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall comply with procedures stipulated by the Supreme People’s Court in cooperation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  Article 11. Payment of expenses for participation in court sessions and meetings for settlement of cases or lawsuits

  1. After the end of the trial or meeting for the resolution of the case, the persons specified in Clause 1 Article 69 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses shall send the payment request dossier to the competent procedural authority by one of the methods via postal services, in person or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technical facilities for payment procedures are met.

  2. The application for payment of expenses for participation in court sessions or meetings for resolution of cases shall include:

  a) A written request for payment of court hearing or meeting expenses made using Form No. 1 enclosed herewith.

  b) The enclosed documents are the original invoices and relevant legal documents to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by the participants in court or meeting for case resolution.

  3. In case the application for payment of expenses for participation in a Court session or meeting for resolution of a case is incomplete and invalid, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority shall notify the participants in the Court session or meeting and clearly state the reasons for supplementation as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid payment request, the competent procedural authority shall pay expenses for participants in court sessions or meetings as prescribed.

  Chapter III

                                            IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 12. Effect

  1. This Decree comes into force as of its signing date.

  2. In case documents referred to in this Decree are amended or replaced, such amendments or replacements shall apply.

  3. The Government’s Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP dated August 14, 2014 detailing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Assessment and Valuation Expenses; Expenses for Witnesses and Interpreters in Legal Proceedings (Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP) shall cease to be effective as from the effective date of this Decree.

  4. For cases where payment requests for assessment expense, valuation expense and expense for witness or interpreter in legal proceedings are being processed according to regulations of Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP before this Decree takes effect, such payment requests shall continue to be processed according to regulations of Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP. Competent procedural authorities, agencies, organizations and individuals shall take full responsibility for the completeness and validity of payment requests and procedures for payment of expenses as prescribed in this Clause and ensure compliance with law.

  5. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, and Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall be responsible for implementing this Decree.
