                                                   DECREE

                                     PROVISIONS ON FAMILY WORK

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;

  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated June 9, 2000;

  Pursuant to the Law on Gender Equality dated November 29, 2006;

  Pursuant to the Law on Prevention and Combat of Domestic Violence dated November 21, 2007;

  At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;

  The Government promulgates a Decree on family affairs,

  Chapter 1.

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Decree provides for family affairs; responsibilities of agencies and organizations in performing family affairs.

  Household work means activities of agencies and organizations aimed at building prosperous, equal, progressive, happy and sustainable families.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Decree applies to agencies and organizations in performing family work.

  Article 3. State management agencies in charge of family affairs

  1. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall be responsible before the Government for performing the state management of family affairs nationwide.

  2. Ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall, within the ambit of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in performing the state management of family affairs.

  3. People’s Committees at all levels shall perform state management of family affairs in their localities.

  Chapter II

                                      FAMILY WORK

  Article 4. Formulation and implementation of policies and laws on family affairs

  To formulate and implement policies and laws on families; equality in families; prevention and combat of domestic violence and social evils invading families.

  Article 5. Planning, training and fostering of cadres in family affairs

  1. To formulate and organize the implementation of plannings on the contingent of family affair officials at all levels.

  2. To train and foster the improvement of capacity of the contingent of officials in charge of family affairs; to compile documents on family affairs.

  3. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Ministry of Home Affairs shall elaborate Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 6. Disseminating and raising awareness of knowledge about family affairs

  1. To propagate and disseminate the Party’s policies and the State’s policies and laws on families, to promote equality in families, to prevent and combat domestic violence, and to prevent and stop social evils from invading families and to plan families.

  2. To develop documents and popularize knowledge about families, mobilize people to get rid of backward customs and practices on marriage and family; inherit, preserve and promote good traditional cultural values of families and clans associated with building advanced values of families in the developing society.

  Article 7. Organizations and agencies providing family-related public services

  1. The provision of public services in the family field shall be carried out by public service providers and enterprises.

  2. Conditions, procedures and competence for establishment, reorganization or dissolution of public service providers and enterprises providing public services in the family field shall comply with the Government’s Decree No. 55/2012/ND-CP dated June 28, 2012 on establishment, reorganization or dissolution of public service providers, the Law on Enterprises and relevant laws.

  Article 8. Family building support activities

  1. Provide premarital counseling and education; provide knowledge and skills for building and organizing family life; appropriately integrate contents of family life education into educational programs at all levels; support the maintenance and preservation of good traditional values of families.

  2. Settle family conflicts and disputes and protect lawful rights and interests of family members.

  3. Develop and guide the replication of models of happy and sustainable families.

  4. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Education and Training, the Ministry of Justice and relevant ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall elaborate Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Organization of the Commemoration Day of Vietnamese Families

  Every year, the Vietnamese Family Day (June 28) is commemorated to promote the responsibility of leaders of all sectors, all levels, mass organizations, social organizations and families to pay attention to building prosperous, equal, progressive, happy and sustainable families; encourage and promptly commend individuals, families and organizations with outstanding achievements in family work.

  Article 10.- Inspection and handling of violations of the legislation on family work

  Organize inspection and handling of violations against regulations on family work as prescribed by law.

  Article 11. Scientific research and development of database on families and family affairs

  1. To organize investigation, survey and scientific research activities in service of formulation of policies and laws on family work.

  2. Develop a database on families and family affairs.

  3. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall elaborate Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. International cooperation

  To cooperate with other countries, international organizations and non-governmental organizations in formulating policies, managing experience, conducting scientific research, exchanging experts, providing technical and financial support in the family field in accordance with law.

  Article 13. Preliminary and final reviews, information and reporting regimes

  1. People’s Committees at all levels shall organize preliminary and final reviews and assessments of implementation of family affairs in their localities and report to superior authorities.

  2. Before December 15 every year, relevant ministries and sectors, People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall send reports on results of family work to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for summarization and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  Chapter III

                                             IMPLEMENTATION ORGANIZATION

  Article 14. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism

  1. To formulate and implement policies and laws on families.

  2. To guide and organize training and fostering to raise capacity of family affair officials.

  3. To guide and propagate, mobilize and disseminate policies, laws and knowledge on family work.

  4. Develop and guide the replication of family models.

  5. State management of public service providers in the family field.

  6. To guide and organize the commemoration of Vietnamese Family Day.

  7. International cooperation and scientific research on families and family work in accordance with law.

  8. Develop database on families and family affairs.

  9. Inspect and handle violations against regulations on family affairs as prescribed by law.

  10. Conduct preliminary and final reviews and submit periodic reports on implementation of family affairs to the Prime Minister.

  Article 15. Responsibilities of Ministry of Planning and Investment

  Taking charge and cooperating with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in guiding and integrating policies on family affairs into socio-economic development strategies, planning and plans.

  Article 16. Responsibilities of Ministry of Finance

  To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and concerned ministries and branches in, allocating funds for family work according to the current decentralized state budget system; to guide and inspect concerned ministries, branches and localities in allocating and using funds for family work.

  Article 17. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Justice

  1. To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and concerned agencies in, formulating policies and laws on family work within their assigned functions and tasks.

  2. Guide the work of reconciliation of family conflicts and disputes in accordance with law.

  Article 18. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Education and Training

  To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in, integrating contents on education about family into educational programs at all levels.

  Article 19. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Home Affairs

  To coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in guiding the implementation of the planning and strengthening of the contingent of family affair officials at all levels.

  Article 20. Responsibilities of Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs

  Take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, relevant ministries and agencies in formulating policies and laws on protection, care for and education of children in families, care for and support of older persons in families, and prevention and control of social evils invading families within their assigned functions and tasks.

  Article 21. Responsibilities of ministries and ministerial agencies

  1. Take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in implementing contents related to family affairs within their assigned scope, functions, tasks and powers.

  2. Promulgate and submit to competent authorities for promulgation policies, laws, programs, and projects related to family affairs; provide guidance, inspect, examine, conduct preliminary and final reviews of implementation of policies, laws, programs, and projects under management responsibility for family affairs.

  3. Annually, review and summarize the performance of tasks related to family affairs under the state management domain, which are carried out by ministries and central authorities, and send them to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for consolidation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  Article 22. Responsibilities of People’s Committees at all levels

  1. Organize and inspect the implementation of policies and laws on family affairs in localities.

  2. Develop and implement family work programs and plans in conjunction with local socio-economic development programs.

  3. Allocate budget for family affairs in accordance with regulations of law on state budget.

  Article 23. Participation in state management of family affairs at all levels The Women’s Union

  The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and People’s Committees at all levels shall ensure that the Women’s Union at the same level participates in state management of family affairs in accordance with law and Article 4, Article 6, Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of Article 8 and Article 9 of this Decree.

  Article 24. Participation in family affairs

  Request the Central Committee of the Vietnam Fatherland Front and its member organizations to participate in propagating, disseminating and educating about policies and laws, inspecting and supervising the implementation of policies and laws on family affairs as prescribed by law.

  Article 25. Funding for family affairs

  1. Funds for family activities of ministries, sectors and People’s Committees at all levels shall be allocated from the state budget in accordance with law. The estimation and allocation of funds for family activities shall comply with the Law on State Budget and relevant documents.

  2. Funds mobilized by domestic and foreign organizations and individuals for family affairs shall be managed and used in accordance with law.

  Chapter IV

                                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 26. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on February 18, 2013.

  Article 27. Implementation responsibilities

  Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies, chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees and relevant agencies and organizations shall implement this Decree.-
