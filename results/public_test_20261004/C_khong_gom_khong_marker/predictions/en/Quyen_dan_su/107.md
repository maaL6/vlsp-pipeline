                                                   CIRCULAR

   PROVISIONS ON AUTHORITY, ORDER, PROCEDURES AND MEASURES FOR PROTECTION OF THE INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, HEALTH, PROPERTY, DIGNITY AND PERSONAL RIGHTS OF REPORTERS OF CORRUPTION AND EXPLOITATION

  Pursuant to the Law on Denunciation dated June 12, 2018;

  Pursuant to the Law on Anti-Corruption dated November 20, 2018;

  Pursuant to the Law on thrift practice and waste combat dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 31/2019/ND-CP dated April 10, 2019 on elaboration of certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Denunciation;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP dated February 25, 2019 on denunciation and settlement of denunciations in the People's Public Security;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 01/2018/ND-CP dated August 6, 2018 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;

  At the request of the Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Public Security;

  The Minister of Public Security promulgates a Circular on jurisdiction, procedures and measures for protection of life, health, property, honor and dignity of denouncers of corrupt practices and wastefulness.

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides for the jurisdiction, procedures and measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of denunciators of corrupt acts prescribed in the Anti-Corruption Law and acts of violation against thrift practice and wastefulness prescribed in the Law on Thrift Practice and Wastefulness (hereinafter referred to as denunciators of corrupt acts and wastefulness).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Protected persons specified in Clause 3 of this Circular.

  2. Persons settling denunciations of corrupt and wasteful acts.

  3. Police authorities at all levels; agencies, organizations, units and individuals shall take measures to protect the lives, health, property, honor and dignity of protected persons as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Circular.

  Article 3. Persons protected, scope and contents of protection and grounds for application of protective measures

  1. Protected persons include: Persons denouncing acts of corruption and wastefulness; spouses, natural parents, adoptive parents, natural children and adopted children of persons denouncing acts of corruption and wastefulness (hereinafter collectively referred to as protected persons). Protected persons who are living, working or studying abroad are not regulated by this Circular.

  2. Scope and contents of protection of protected persons include: Protection of life, health, property, honor and dignity. Protected property means property under the ownership of protected persons. Property of protected persons in foreign countries is not regulated by this Circular.

  3. Grounds for application of protective measures: When there are grounds to believe that the lives, health, property, honor or dignity of the persons specified in Clause 1 of this Article are being or are immediately in danger of being infringed upon due to denunciations of corrupt practices or wastefulness.

  Article 4. Rights and obligations of protected persons

  Rights and obligations of protected persons shall comply with Article 48 of the Law on Denunciation. Persons competent to settle denunciations and competent police authorities applying protective measures shall not be held liable for consequences to the life, health, property, honor, and dignity of protected persons in cases where protected persons voluntarily request termination or refusal of protective measures or fail to comply with obligations specified in Clause 2, Article 48 of the Law on Denunciation.

  Article 5. Responsibilities for protection of protected persons

  1. Police authorities at all levels where protected persons live or have property shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies, organizations, units, and individuals in protecting the life, health, property, honor, and dignity of protected persons; decide on the mobilization and use of forces, means, tools, measures, and other matters proposed or requested by relevant agencies, organizations, units, and individuals for cooperation in protection. Relevant agencies, organizations, units, and individuals shall, within their tasks and powers, cooperate as requested or required by police authorities at all levels.

  2. When receiving a request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer against corrupt and wasteful acts, and finding that such request is well-grounded, credible or when, during the process of handling the denunciation, the person competent to settle the denunciation finds grounds specified in Clause 3, Article 3 of this Circular, he/she shall promptly request or propose the competent police authority to apply such measures as follows:

  a) The persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 6, 7 and 8 of Article 13; Clause 4 of Article 14; Clause 4 of Article 15; Article 16; Clauses 1 and 3 of Article 17 and Article 20 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the directors of the police departments of provinces or centrally-run cities (hereinafter referred collectively to as provincial-level Police Departments) concerned, and at the same time send them to the Minister of Public Security for direction. Upon receiving a proposal or request for protection, the director of the provincial-level Police Department shall consider deciding on the application of protective measures or direct the concerned affiliated police units to issue decisions on the application of protective measures;

  b) The persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of Article 13; Clauses 2 and 3 of Article 14; Clauses 2 and 3 of Article 15; Clause 2 of Article 17 and Article 18 and Article 19 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the directors of provincial-level Public Security Departments. Upon receiving a proposal or request for protection, the director of the provincial-level Public Security Department shall consider deciding on the application of protective measures or direct the relevant affiliated police units to issue a decision on the application of protective measures;

  c) The persons competent to settle denunciations specified in Clauses 2, Article 13; Clause 1, Article 14; Clause 1, Article 15 and Article 21 of the Law on Denunciation shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the heads of police offices of urban districts, rural districts, towns, provincial cities and centrally-run cities (hereinafter referred collectively to as district-level police offices). Upon receiving a request for protection, the head of the district-level police office shall consider deciding on the application of protective measures or direct the heads of police wards, stations, police posts, town police offices or commune police offices of the People's Police Force (hereinafter referred collectively to as communal police offices) concerned to decide on the application of protective measures;

  d) The person competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 1, Article 13 of the Law on Denunciations shall promptly propose or request the application of protective measures to the Chief of the commune-level police office. Upon receiving a proposal or request for protection, the Chief of the commune-level police office shall consider and decide on the application of protective measures;

  dd) In case of emergency where there are grounds to determine that the life, health, property, honor or dignity of the protected person may be immediately infringed upon after denunciation of corrupt or wasteful acts is made; the person competent to settle the denunciation shall immediately propose or request the nearest police authority or the police authority where the act of infringing upon the life, health, property, honor or dignity of the protected person may occur to apply necessary protective measures;

  e) Where protected persons are inmates of prisons or re-education camps or students of reformatories: Competent persons entitled to handle denunciations shall promptly request or propose the application of protective measures to superintendents of prisons, directors of compulsory education establishments or principals of reformatories.

  3. Upon receiving requests or petitions from persons competent to resolve denunciations, written proposals for application of protective measures from denouncers regarding corrupt and wasteful acts, or written proposals from competent agencies or units; if considering that such requests or petitions are well-grounded, within their tasks and powers, within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the requests or petitions, heads of police authorities at all levels shall issue decisions on application of protective measures; take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies, organizations, units, and individuals in protecting the lives, health, property, honor, and dignity of protected persons. In cases where the case is complex and requires inspection and verification at multiple locations, the time limit for issuing a decision on application of protective measures may be longer but must not exceed 05 working days.

  In case of non-compliance with their jurisdiction, heads of police authorities at all levels shall immediately transfer the case to competent police authorities for handling as prescribed and notify in writing the person competent to resolve denunciations, the denouncer or the competent authority/unit of the transfer request.

  4. In case the request of the person competent to settle denunciations and the denouncer about corrupt or wasteful acts is groundless or deemed unnecessary to apply protective measures, within 3 working days, the head of police authority at all levels shall send a written notice clearly stating the reason to the competent agency/unit, the denouncer or send such notice to the denunciation solver for explanation.

  Article 6. Written requests and proposals for decisions on application of protective measures

  1. Written requests for application of protective measures by denouncers of corrupt or wasteful acts shall comply with Clause 2 Article 50 of the Law on Denunciation.

  2. Written requests or proposals of persons competent to resolve denunciations sent to police authorities at all levels competent to decide on protective measures shall include the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the written request or proposal;

  b) Name of competent police authority requesting or requesting decision on application of protective measures;

  c) Full name, position, agency or unit of the person competent to settle denunciations;

  d) Grounds and reasons for proposing and requesting application of protective measures;

  d) Date of issuance of the decision to accept the denunciation; time limit for settlement of the denunciation;

  e) Full name, address, phone number of the protected person; contents of the protection request.

  3. When sending a written request or petition, the person competent to settle denunciations shall send it together with the decision on acceptance of the denunciation, the petition for application of protective measures by the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and wastefulness as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 50 of the Law on Denunciations (original) and other documents related to the application of protective measures (if any).

  Article 7. Authority to decide on application of protective measures by persons competent to resolve denunciations in the People's Public Security

  1. The person competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 1 Article 5 of the Government’s Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP dated February 25, 2019 on denunciations and settlement thereof in the People's Public Security shall, upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor or dignity of the denouncer against corrupt or wasteful acts, if finding that the protection request is well-grounded, credible or there are grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular during the process of settlement of the denunciation, shall promptly decide to apply protective measures to the protected person whose place of residence or property is located within their jurisdiction.

  In case the protected person has a place of residence or property outside the jurisdiction, the person competent to resolve denunciations specified in Clause 1 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall report it to the Head of the district-level police authority for handling according to their jurisdiction.

  2. The person competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 2 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall, upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and wastefulness, and if deeming that the protection request is well-grounded, credible or there are grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular during the process of settlement of the denunciation, shall promptly decide to apply protective measures to the protected person with their place of residence or property within their jurisdiction or direct the Head of the relevant commune-level police authority to decide on the application of protective measures (except where the denounced person is the Head of the commune-level police authority).

  In case the protected person has a place of residence or property outside the jurisdiction, the competent person specified in Clause 2 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall request the Head of the relevant district-level police authority to decide on the application of protective measures and report to the Director of the provincial-level Police Department for monitoring and direction.

  3. The person competent to resolve denunciations specified in Clause 3 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall, upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and wastefulness, and if finding that the protection request is well-grounded, credible or during the process of resolving the denunciation, the person competent to resolve the denunciation finds grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular, shall promptly request the Head of the relevant district-level police authority to decide on the application of protective measures.

  In case the protected persons are inmates of prisons or reeducators of compulsory education establishments or students of reformatories: Upon receiving written requests for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of denunciators of corrupt and wasteful acts, and when deeming that such requests are well-grounded, credible or when finding grounds specified in Clause 3, Article 3 of this Circular during the process of denunciation settlement, superintendents of prisons, directors of compulsory education establishments or principals of reformatories shall promptly decide on the application of protective measures.

  4. The person competent to settle denunciations specified in Clause 4 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall, upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption and wastefulness, and if deeming that the protection request is well-grounded, credible or there are grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular during the process of settlement of the denunciation, shall promptly decide to apply protective measures to the protected person with their place of residence or property within their jurisdiction or direct the Head of the relevant district-level police authority to decide on the application of protective measures. The provincial police authority shall advise the Director of the province’s police department to direct a deputy head of the district-level police authority or another competent unit or individual to decide on the application of protective measures in case the denounced person is the Head of the district-level police authority.

  In case the protected person has a place of residence or property outside the jurisdiction, the competent person to resolve denunciations specified in Clause 4 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall request the relevant director of provincial police authority to decide on the application of protective measures.

  5. The person competent to resolve denunciations specified in Clauses 5 and 6 Article 5 of Decree No. 22/2019/ND-CP shall, upon receiving a written request for application of measures to protect the life, health, property, honor or dignity of the denouncer regarding acts of corruption or wastefulness, and if finding that the protection request is well-grounded, credible or during the process of resolving the denunciation, the denunciation resolver finds grounds specified in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Circular, shall promptly request the relevant director of the provincial police authority to decide on the application of protective measures.

  In case the protected persons are officials, soldiers, inmates in prisons, officials, soldiers, inmates in compulsory education establishments, officials, soldiers, students in reformatories and the denounced persons are superintendents, deputy superintendents of prisons, directors, deputy directors of compulsory education establishments, principals, deputy principals of reformatories: The Director of Police Department for Management of Prisons, Compulsory Education Establishments and Reform Schools shall, upon receiving written requests for application of measures to protect life, health, property, honor and dignity of denunciators regarding acts of corruption and wastefulness and deeming that the protection requests are well-grounded, authentic or during the process of denunciation settlement, there are grounds specified in Clause 3, Article 3 of this Circular, shall promptly decide on the application of protection measures.

  The Inspector of the Ministry of Public Security shall advise the Minister of Public Security to direct Deputy Directors and Deputy Secretaries of Party Committees of provincial-level Police Departments or other competent units and individuals to decide on protective measures in cases where the denounced persons are directors of provincial-level Police Departments.

  Article 8. Procedures for protection

  1. Procedures for protecting the life, health, property, honor and dignity of denunciators of corrupt or wasteful acts shall comply with Section 2 Chapter VI of the Law on Denunciation.

  2. In case a person competent to settle denunciations has made a conclusion on the contents of the denunciation or decided to terminate the settlement of the denunciation, he/she shall immediately notify such to the competent police authority for decision on termination of application of protective measures. The decision on termination of application of protective measures shall be sent to the protected person, the person competent to settle the denunciation and other relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals.

  3. After the person competent to resolve denunciations issues conclusions on the denunciation contents or decides to terminate the resolution of denunciations, the competent police authority shall decide to terminate the application of protective measures if the person competent to resolve denunciations continues to receive written requests from denunciators regarding acts of corruption and wastefulness, and if there are grounds and the denunciators' statements are considered credible, determining that the lives, health, property, honor, and dignity of protected persons continue to be infringed upon or threatened. The competent police authority shall forward the documents to the competent authorities for handling in accordance with the law and notify the denunciators in writing.

  Article 9. Application for protective measures

  The application dossier for the application of protective measures shall comply with Article 55 of the Law on Denunciation and regulations on archiving of documents forming the popularization in the People's Public Security (group of dossiers on prevention and combat of corruption).

  Article 10. Protective measures

  Based on the actual situation, competent police authorities may apply one or several measures to protect the life, health, property, honor and dignity of protected persons in accordance with Article 58 of the Law on Denunciation.

  Article 11. Funding for protection measures

  Funding for application of protective measures shall be covered by the State budget. Police authorities at all levels shall prepare and submit consolidated estimates of funding for application of protective measures to the Ministry of Public Security (via the Department of Planning and Finance) for consolidation and submission to the Ministry of Finance in accordance with regulations.

  Article 12. Effect, transitional provisions and implementation responsibilities

  1. This Circular comes into force from February 15, 2021.

  2. When regulations referred to in this Circular are amended or replaced, the referenced contents shall be adjusted accordingly and comply with the amended or replaced regulations.

  3. Heads of police authorities affiliated to ministries, directors of police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities, relevant agencies, organizations, units and individuals shall be responsible for implementing this Circular.

  4. The Inspectorate of the Ministry of Public Security shall take charge, monitor, inspect, consolidate and report on the implementation of this Circular; the Head of the police authority affiliated to the Ministry shall assign a coordinating unit, the Head of the local police authority shall assign the local police inspectorate to take charge, monitor, inspect, consolidate and report on the implementation of this Circular. Any difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry of Public Security (via the Inspectorate of the Ministry of Public Security) for consolidation and reporting to the leadership of the Ministry for consideration and resolution.
