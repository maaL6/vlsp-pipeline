                                                  CIRCULAR

  GUIDANCE ON ISSUANCE OF VISAS, PERMANENT RESIDENCE PERMITS AND PERMANENT RESIDENCE CARDS TO FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM UNDER THE EXPANSION OF THE MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS

  The Circular No. 04/2016/TT-BNG dated June 30, 2016 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs guiding procedures for issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence and issuance of temporary residence cards to foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, takes effect from September 1, 2016, is amended and supplemented by:

  Circular No. 04/2025/TT-BNG dated June 13, 2025 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs on amendments to Circular No. 04/2016/TT-BNG dated June 30, 2016 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs providing guidelines for procedures for issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Decision No. 2985/2007/QD-BNG dated November 29, 2007 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgating the Regulation on organization and operation of the Fund for protection of overseas Vietnamese citizens and legal entities, which takes effect from July 13, 2025.

  Pursuant to the Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;

  Pursuant to the Law on promulgation of legal documents dated June 3, 2008;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 58/2013/ND-CP dated June 11, 2013 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP dated August 6, 2015 on cooperation among ministries, ministerial agencies and People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities in management of entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam;

  The Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgates a Circular providing guidelines for procedures for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards and extension of temporary residence for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs [1].

  Chapter I

                                             GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidance on issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs as prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of Law on Entry, Exit, Transit, and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Immigration”).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to foreigners under the management of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs who enter and reside in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.

  Chapter II

              POWER TO GRANT VISA, STAY PERMITS AND LAST-IN-STAY CARD

  Article 3. Competence of relevant agencies in issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence and issuance of temporary residence cards

  1. The Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as “Consular Department”) shall receive and process applications for issuance of visas or extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration.

  2. The Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs Interpretation, Ministry of Foreign Affairs [3] shall receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration (excluding members of foreign consular missions based in Da Nang City southward and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants during their term).

  3. [4] The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City):

  a) Receive and process applications for issuance of visas or notices of extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration in provinces and cities from Da Nang City southward.

  b) Receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners who are members of foreign consular offices in provinces and cities from Da Nang City southward and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants during their term.

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions:

  a) Receive application for issuance of visa as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 4 hereof, passport or document with equivalent value to passports (hereinafter referred to as “passport”), application form for issuance of visa (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 of Ministry of Public Security on forms related to entry, exit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam - hereinafter referred to as “Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA”) of foreigners specified under Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of Law on Immigration for transfer to Department of Consular Affairs or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for handling.

  b) Issuing visas to foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration according to notices of Consular Department or Ho Chi Minh City Department of Foreign Affairs.

  Article 4. Procedures for processing application/notification of issuance of visas

  1. Agencies and organizations that invite or guarantee entry of foreigners into Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 15 of the Law on Immigration shall directly submit notice/dossier of application for visa issuance to Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs
  TP. HCM.

  The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (or other agencies as prescribed by the sending country) of the country where the foreigner is located, who falls under the provisions of Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration, may send the application for visa issuance, along with the passport and the declaration form for visa issuance (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the relevant foreigner to the Vietnamese representative mission in the host country or the concurrent country.

  Vietnam’s overseas representative missions shall, within 1 working day from the date on which they receive all documents mentioned above, forward them to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for processing and issuing visas within their jurisdiction.

  2. The application for issuance of a visa includes:

  a) A written notice or diplomatic note requesting visa issuance, specifying full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and symbol, issuance date and expiry date, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa duration, visa type and visa-accepting country of the foreigner.

  b) In case the foreigner is a visitor to a member of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an additional copy of the identity card of the relevant member of the representative mission is required.

  c) In case the foreigner is a contractual employee working at a Vietnamese foreign representative office, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an additional copy of the valid employment contract is required.

  d) In case the foreigner is subject to an increase in the staff of the overseas representative mission in Vietnam, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, it is required to supplement the diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country notifying the staff increase.

  3. Processing of visa application/notification:

  a) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as the Immigration Department) and overseas Vietnamese representative missions within 01 working day from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application for visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit.

  b)[5] (annulled)

  c) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify in writing the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security within one working day from the date of receipt of a valid application for issuance of visas to foreigners specified in Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration.

  The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall reply to the visa-requesting agency or organization and, concurrently, notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the issuance of visas to foreigners after 2 working days from the date of notification to the Immigration Department if it has no opinion.

  d/ The Consular Department or the Ho Chi Minh City Foreign Affairs Service’s notice to the overseas Vietnamese representative mission on the granting of visas to foreigners shall contain the following information: full name, date of birth, nationality, sex, number and symbol of the passport, date of issue and expiry of the passport, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa duration, visa type, place of receipt of visas by foreigners and other information (if any).

  4. Issuing visas:

  a) Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall issue visas to foreigners within 01 working day from receipt of notices as prescribed in Point d Clause 3 of this Article, passports and visa application forms (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of foreigners.

  b) In case a foreigner is granted a visa at a border checkpoint, the receiving authority specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall notify the issuing authority or organization of the grant of the visa to the foreigner.

  5. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director General of Consular Department to decide reciprocal treatment with foreign countries regarding time limit for processing dossiers and components of dossiers applying for issuance of visas in accordance with Immigration Law.

  Article 5. Procedures for processing applications for extension of temporary residence

  1. Foreigners who have been issued with visas of types NG1, NG2 and NG4 and wish to extend their temporary residence after entering Vietnam shall, upon request, send notice/dossier of application for extension of temporary residence to Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City through agencies or organizations that invite and guarantee foreigners to enter Vietnam.

  2. Application for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Written notice or diplomatic note on application for extension of temporary residence, specifying full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and symbol, issuance date and expiry date, occupation, position, purpose of entry, duration of application for extension of temporary residence and need for issuance of new visa (if any) of the foreigner.

  b) The passport of the foreigner is still valid for more than 30 days compared to the duration of application for extension of temporary residence.

  c) The application form for issuance of visa or extension of temporary residence (using Form NA5 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) containing confirmation and seal of the inviting or guaranteeing agency or organization.

  d) For foreigners entering Vietnam to visit members of foreign representative missions in Vietnam, a copy of their identity card issued by the Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs Interpretation [6] or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City is required.

  e) For a foreigner who is an employee under a labor contract and working at a Vietnamese foreign representative office, 1 copy of the valid labor contract is required.

  3. Processing applications for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider extending the temporary residence and issue a new visa (if necessary) for the foreigner.

  b/ The duration of temporary residence extension and the duration of the new visa granted (if any) shall be suitable to the purpose of entry and at the request of the inviting agency or organization and must not exceed 12 months, which is at least 30 days shorter than the passport duration of the foreigner.

  c) For persons specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article, their temporary residence duration shall be extended and new visas shall be issued (if any) in accordance with Point b Clause 3 of this Article and in conformity with the identity card validity period of members of foreign representative missions in Vietnam issued by the State Protocol Department and Foreign Affairs Interpreting Agency [7] or Ho Chi Minh City Department of Foreign Affairs.

  4. [8] Foreigners who have been granted visa-free entry to Vietnam and fall under the categories specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration may be considered by the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for one or multiple extensions of temporary residence and issuance of visas with each extension not exceeding the previously granted temporary residence duration, based on the application submitted in accordance with Clause 2 of this Article, unless otherwise provided by international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  5. The Director of Consular Department shall decide to extend temporary residence and issue new visas (if necessary) for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of Law on Immigration who have entered Vietnam with SQ visas.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director General of Consular Department to decide reciprocal treatment with foreign countries in extending temporary residence for foreigners in accordance with Law on Immigration.

  Article 6. Procedures for processing applications for temporary residence cards

  1. The agency that invites and guarantees foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration shall submit an application for issuance of temporary residence card to the competent authority specified in Article 3 of this Circular.

  2. Application for temporary residence card includes:

  a) The diplomatic note requesting the issuance of temporary residence card by the foreign representative mission in Vietnam, specifying the applicant’s full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and symbol, occupation and position (refer to Form NA6 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA);

  In case of replacement or addition of new staff members of a Vietnamese representative mission, the diplomatic note requesting the issuance of temporary residence cards must specify full name and title of the person replaced or added to the staff;

  b) The application form for issuance of temporary residence card (using Form NA8 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) bearing certification of the competent person and seal of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam;

  c) Validity of foreign passports;

  d) In case the foreigner is subject to an increase in the staff of the overseas representative mission in Vietnam, apart from the documents specified in Points a, b and c Clause 1 of this Article, it is required to supplement the diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country notifying the staff increase.

  3. Grant temporary residence cards:

  Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the State Protocol and Foreign Affairs Interpretation Department [9] or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider issuing a temporary residence card to the foreigner.

  4. [10] Temporary residence card with the symbol NG3, which is valid for no more than 5 years and shorter than the remaining validity of the passport by at least 30 days.

  A temporary residence card that has expired shall be considered for issuance of a new temporary residence card at the request of the foreign representative office in Vietnam.

  5. The Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs Interpretation and the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall transfer the list of persons granted temporary residence cards to immigration authorities in accordance with Clause 2 Article 7 of the Government’s Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP on cooperation between ministries, ministerial agencies, and People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities in management of entry, exit, transit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize Director of State Protocol and Foreign Interpretation Department to decide reciprocal treatment with foreign parties regarding processing time limit and contents of application for temporary residence card in accordance with Immigration Law.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 7. Transition clauses

  1. The visas, certificates of temporary residence and temporary residence cards which are issued before the effective date of this Circular may be used until their expiry dates.

  2. Applications for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards or extension of temporary residence submitted before the effective date of this Circular which have not been processed shall be processed by competent agencies of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in accordance with regulations laid down herein.

  Article 8. Implementation

  1. The Consular Department shall have to:

  a) Guide overseas Vietnamese representative missions and relevant agencies in issuing visas and extending temporary residence according to this Circular.

  b) Annually report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the issuance and extension of visas under this Circular.

  2. The Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs Interpretation shall be responsible for:

  a) Guide foreign representative missions in Vietnam and relevant agencies on procedures for issuance of temporary residence cards according to this Circular.

  b) Annually report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the issuance of temporary residence cards under this Circular.

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall send monthly, quarterly and annual reports on issuance of visas and extension of temporary residence to the Consular Department; issuance of temporary residence cards to the State Protocol Department and the Foreign Affairs Interpreter[14].

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall be responsible for:

  a) Issuing visas according to notices of Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  b/ To send monthly, quarterly and annual reports on visa granting to foreigners under the management of the Ministry for Foreign Affairs to the Consular Department.

  Article 9. Entry into force [15]

  This Circular takes effect on September 1, 2016.

  Article 10. Implementation responsibilities

  The Director of Consular Department, Director of State Protocol and Foreign Interpretation Department, Director of Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City and Heads of Vietnam representative missions in foreign countries and units affiliated to the Ministry are responsible for implementing this Circular. Any difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (via Consular Department for visa issuance and temporary residence extension procedures; via State Protocol and Foreign Interpretation Department for temporary residence card issuance procedures) for timely guidance./.
