                                                    DIRECTIVE

                   ON THE ORGANIZATION OF THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW IN 2000

  The Marriage and Family Law of 2000 was passed on June 9, 2000 by the Xth National Assembly at its 7th session and took effect on January 1, 2001.

  In order to promptly implement the Marriage and Family Law and Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of June 9, 2000 of the National Assembly on implementation of the 2000 Marriage and Family Law, the Prime Minister hereby issues the following Directive:

  I. ORGANIZING THE RENOVATION OF AND SUPPLEMENT OF CURRENT LEGAL DOCUMENTS OR THE PROMULGATION OF NEW DOCUMENTS ON GUIDANCE IN THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW

  1. From now till the end of 2000, the Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for and complete the review of current legal documents on marriage and family in order to propose the Government and other competent agencies to amend, supplement or annul those documents which are no longer compatible with the new Marriage and Family Law.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with concerned agencies and organizations in, preparing and submitting under-legislation documents to the Government or the National Assembly’s Standing Committee for promulgation in order to guide the implementation of the provisions of the Law, especially matters related to the right to use farming land, residential land and houses related to marriage and family relations; marriage registration, adoption registration in remote areas, civil status registration concerning marriage and family involving foreign elements, including civil status registration concerning marriage and family involving foreign elements in border areas with neighboring countries; and sanction administrative violations in the field of marriage and family. The above-said tasks must be completed by the second quarter of 2001.

  3. The Committee for Ethnic Minorities and Mountainous Areas shall assume the prime responsibility for preparing a proposal to be submitted to the Government in the first quarter of 2001 for promulgation of a Decree prescribing the application of the Marriage and Family Law to ethnic minority groups, plans on handling actual adoption cases in ethnic minority areas, enclosed with a list of backward customs and practices on marriage and family which must not be applied and a list of good customs and practices on marriage and family which are encouraged for promotion.

  II. PROVIDING, PROMULGATING AND EDUCATING ON THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW

  1. The dissemination and propagation of the Law on Marriage and Family must be carried out regularly, in-depth and extensively among cadres, civil servants, public employees, soldiers of the people’s armed forces and among the people, especially in communes, wards, townships, population clusters, agencies, organizations, enterprises, schools and families.

  The dissemination and propagation must be aimed at making everyone clearly understand the principles and basic contents of the Law, especially the newly amended and supplemented contents and the Resolution of the National Assembly on the settlement of the actual marriage situation, so as to voluntarily and actively comply with, raise the awareness and concern of each person for his/her personal responsibility to take care of family building, make each family truly become a happy family of each individual, and make Vietnam society truly become a big family of all Vietnamese people working together to build an independent, free, prosperous and happy country.

  From the third quarter of 2000, ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and People's Committees at all levels shall have to work out and organize the implementation of plans on dissemination, propagation and education of the Marriage and Family Law in their agencies, units and localities; regard this as a major task of law dissemination, propagation and education work for the last six months of 2000 and the whole year 2001, and at the same time renew forms and measures of law propagation and education on marriage and family so as to make such activities more intensive and avoid the form of law propagation and education which is more about movements, heavy on the surface and has low efficiency.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall coordinate with the Central Committee of the Vietnam Fatherland Front, the Central Committee of the Vietnam Women’s Union, the Vietnam General Confederation of Labor, the Central Committee of the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, the Vietnam Peasants' Association, the Vietnam War Veterans' Association, the Ministry of Culture and Information, the Vietnam Committee for Child Protection and Care, the Committee for Population and Family Planning, the Vietnam Lawyers Association, central and local press, radio and television agencies in disseminating and popularizing the Marriage and Family Law and the Resolution of the National Assembly on implementation of this Law; direct and guide the intensification of legal counseling activities on marriage and family, encourage the establishment of various types of family clubs where people can participate in activities according to gender, age, occupation or interest to exchange experiences and learn from each other about necessary things in their responsibilities as wives, husbands, fathers, mothers, grandparents, etc., so that from the beginning they can avoid confusion and regrettable things not worth doing in the truly important mission in their family life.

  The Government’s Council for Cooperation in Law Popularization and Education shall determine contents and programs of popularization and communication of the Law on Marriage and Family and the Resolution of the National Assembly on implementation of this Law; cooperate with relevant agencies and organizations in compiling training plans and materials suitable to each type of target group and train rapporteurs for popularization and communication of the Law on Marriage and Family.

  3. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall coordinate with the Central Committee for Ideological and Cultural Affairs and the Ministry of Justice in directing mass media to prioritize the dissemination and propagation of the Marriage and Family Law from now till the end of 2000 and 2001; guiding the formulation and implementation of community activity rules related to the provisions of the Marriage and Family Law.

  4. The People’s News Agency, Radio Voice of Vietnam, Vietnam Television Station and other mass media in central and local areas regularly have programs and special sections introducing and explaining the contents and new points of the Law on Marriage and Family.

  5. The Ministry of Justice shall take charge and cooperate with the Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy in organizing specialized training courses on the Law on Marriage and Family for judges, procurators, court clerks, people’s jurors, civil status officers and other judicial officials to ensure uniform application and supervision of the implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family.

  6. The Ministry of Education and Training shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in, directing the review of the curricula and teaching materials of universities, colleges and citizen education documents related to the Marriage and Family Law at vocational secondary schools, general secondary schools and elementary schools in order to work out plans on timely supplementation and amendment thereof in conformity with the Marriage and Family Law; organize training courses for teachers who teach law and citizen education on the Marriage and Family Law.

  7. The Committee for Ethnic Minorities and Mountainous Areas shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in developing plans to disseminate and raise awareness about the Law on Marriage and Family among ethnic minority people.

  8. The Ministry of Finance shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Justice in, making plans to guide budget balancing and ensure necessary funding sources for implementation of the Marriage and Family Law and the Resolution of the National Assembly on implementation of this Law through elaboration of documents guiding the implementation of this Law, organization of marriage registration and child adoption registration in remote areas, organization of training courses and campaigns for staff, officers and people.

  9. The presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall direct local agencies to work out specific plans on handling the actual marriage situation in their localities according to the National Assembly’s Resolution on implementation of the Marriage and Family Law, create favorable conditions for marriage registration, including on-site registration, so as to completely deal with this situation in 2001-2002.

  The Prime Minister requests Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, and Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities, within their respective responsibilities, to develop specific plans for implementation of this Directive.

  The Ministry of Justice and Government Office shall monitor and expedite the implementation of this Directive and submit consolidated reports to the Prime Minister on the results of implementation.
