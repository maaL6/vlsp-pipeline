                                           CIRCULAR
   GUIDANCE ON PROVISION OF VISAS, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE PERMITS, PERMITS FOR EXIT FROM VIETNAM AND RESIDENCE SOLUTIONS FOR FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM
Pursuant to the Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 106/2014/ND-CP dated November 17, 2014 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;
At the request of the Director General of Security Department, the Minister of Public Security promulgates a Circular guiding a number of contents on the grant of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits and permanent residence for foreigners in Vietnam.
Chapter I
                                       GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
This Circular provides guidance on issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits, and permanent residence for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Public Security.
Article 2. Regulated entities
This Circular applies to foreigners entering and residing in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Chapter II
 PROCEDURES AND AUTHORITY TO HANDLE APPLICATION FOR VISA, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE CARD, PERMANENT RESIDENCE
Article 3. Procedures and competence in processing visa application
1. Agencies, organizations and individuals that guarantee entry of foreigners into Vietnam according to Clause 1 Article 16 of Law on Entry, Exit, Transit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “Law”) shall submit application at Immigration Department.
2. The visa application form made by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 shall be used to apply for visas to Vietnam.
a) NA2 form used for agencies and organizations;
b) Form NA3 used for individuals.
3. Processing visa application:
a) Immigration Department shall consider, process and respond to agencies, organizations and individuals applying for issuance of visas in accordance with Clause 3 Article 16 of the Law;
b) In case of application for visa issuance at border checkpoint, Immigration Department shall consider and process according to Clause 5 Article 16 of the Law.
Article 4. Procedures and competence in processing applications for temporary residence cards
1. Foreigners eligible for issuance of temporary residence cards as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 36 of the Law shall, through agencies, organizations or individuals that have directly invited or guaranteed them to carry out procedures at Immigration Department or Immigration Division affiliated to police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities where agencies, organizations invite or guarantee their headquarters to be located or where individuals invite or guarantee them to reside.
2. Application for issuance of temporary residence card as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law. Written application for issuance of temporary residence card specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law using form promulgated by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 on forms related to entry, exit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam including:

                                                                                                    1

a) Forms NA6 and NA8 used for agencies and organizations;
b) Forms NA7 and NA8 are used for individuals.
3. Grant temporary residence cards
Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of a complete application, the Immigration Department or Immigration Division of the police authority of the province/centrally affiliated city shall consider issuing a temporary residence card.
4. Duration of temporary residence cards shall be issued in conformity with entry purposes and request of agencies, organizations and individuals. Duration of temporary residence cards is between 1 year and 5 years but shorter than duration of passports at least 30 days.
Article 5. Settlement of permanent residence
1. Procedures and competence for settlement of permanent residence shall comply with Articles 41 and 42 of the Law.
2. Determine the period of temporary residence in Vietnam for foreigners applying for permanent residence:
a) Foreigners who have been temporarily residing in Vietnam for 3 years or more as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 40 of the Law shall be determined on the basis of the entry and exit check stamps issued at border checkpoints with a total temporary residence period in Vietnam of 3 years or more in the last 4 years until the date of application for permanent residence.
b/ Documents evidencing the continuous temporary residence in Vietnam before 2000 as prescribed at Point b, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Law, including one of the following documents: certificate of temporary residence; temporary residence register; written explanation of the period of temporary residence in Vietnam, certified by the police office of the commune, ward or township where the stateless person temporarily resides.
3. The place for submission of applications for permanent residence:
a) The applicant for permanent residence as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 39 of the Law shall submit their application at Immigration Department.
b) The applicant for permanent residence as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 39 of the Law shall submit their application at Immigration Department of province/centrally affiliated city where they wish to apply for permanent residence.
Chapter III
 PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE AND REISSUANCE OF PERMITS FOR NON-CITIZENS RESIDING IN VIETNAM
Article 6. Entry and exit permits
1. International travel documents specified in Point i Clause 1 Article 44 of the Law are entry and exit permits issued by Immigration Department to stateless individuals residing in Vietnam who wish to leave Vietnam (using Form NC13 enclosed with this Circular).
2. A temporary entry and exit permit shall be valid for 3 years; in case of loss or damage, an entry and exit permit may be re-issued.
Article 7. Procedures for issuance and reissuance of entry and exit permits
1. Applicants for issuance or reissuance of entry and exit permits shall submit 01 application set at Immigration Department or Immigration Division of police authority of province/centrally affiliated city where they reside. The application set includes:
a) Declaration requesting issuance or reissuance of entry and exit permits (using form NC14 enclosed with this Circular);
b) Photocopies of permanent residence cards enclosed with originals for comparison with those already issued to the applicants;
c/ The applicant’s written explanation of the duration of his/her temporary residence in Vietnam, certified by the police office of the commune, ward or township where the applicant temporarily resides, for those whose permanent residence has not yet been permitted;
d) Issued entry and exit permits, in case of application for re-issuance due to damaged entry and exit permits;
dd) Lost notice, for cases of application for re-issuance of entry and exit permits due to loss (no confirmation by competent authorities where lost).
2. Handle issuance and reissuance of entry and exit permits:
a) In case of submission of application at Immigration Department:


                                                                                                       2

Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of a complete application, the Immigration Department shall consider issuing or reissuing an entry and exit permit.
b) In case of submission of application at Immigration Department of province/centrally affiliated city police:
Within 3 working days from the date of receipt of a complete application, the Immigration Department of the province/centrally affiliated city shall consider and transfer the application for issuance or reissuance of an entry/exit permit to the Immigration Department. The Immigration Department shall consider issuing or reissuing an entry/exit permit within 2 working days.
Chapter IV
                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 8. Transitional provisions
1. The entry and exit permits which are still valid before the effective date of this Circular may be used until their expiry dates.
2. Applications for issuance of visas and temporary residence cards; issuance and reissuance of entry and exit permits; permanent residence settlement submitted before the effective date of this Circular but not yet processed shall be considered and settled by immigration authorities in accordance with regulations herein.
Article 9. Effect
1. This Circular takes effect on August 20, 2015.
2. This Circular replaces the Circular No. 45/2011/TT-BCA dated June 29, 2011 of the Minister of Public Security guiding the issuance of entry and exit permits to foreigners permanently residing in Vietnam who do not have passports.
Article 10. Implementation responsibilities
1. The General Department of Security shall take charge and guide police units, local authorities, and other relevant units in organizing the implementation of this Circular.
2. The General Director of the General Department, Heads of units under the Ministry, Directors of Police Departments and Fire and Rescue Police Departments of provinces and centrally-affiliated cities shall be responsible for implementing this Circular. Any problems arising in the course of implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry (via the Immigration Department - Security Department) for timely guidance./.
