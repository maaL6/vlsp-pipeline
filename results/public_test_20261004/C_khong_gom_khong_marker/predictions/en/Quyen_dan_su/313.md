                GUIDANCE ON HEALTHCARE FOR THE AGELESS

  Pursuant to Articles 14, 15 and 16 of the Ordinance No. 23/2000/PL-UBTVQH of April 28, 2000 on the elderly, which assigns tasks to the health sector; pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 30/2002/ND-CP of March 26, 2002, defining and guiding a number of articles of the Ordinance on the Elderly; pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 120/2003/ND-CP of January 20, 2003 amending Article 9 of Decree No. 30/2002/ND-CP of January 20, 2003; pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 49/2003/ND-CP of May 15, 2003 defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Health guides the implementation of the work of health care for the elderly as follows:

  I. GENERAL PROVISIONS:

  1. The elderly receiving health care under this Circular are citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or Vietnamese elderly residing abroad and foreign elderly during their stay in Vietnam from 60 years old. Old age is often accompanied by poor health and diseases; the elderly suffer from diseases accounting for about 95%. On average, an elderly person suffers from 2.69 diseases, mainly chronic and non-transmissible diseases, so the deepest aspiration of the elderly is to live healthy, receive health care, and be examined and treated when sick or diseased. This is a legitimate requirement of the elderly, requiring the attention of all sectors, levels, and the entire society.

  2. Elderly persons shall receive initial health care and be given priority in medical examination and treatment at health facilities.

  3. People’s Committees at all levels shall take responsibility for ensuring health care for older persons in their localities. The health sector shall be responsible for professional and technical matters in caring for the health of older persons.

  II. ORGANIZATION OF MANAGEMENT AND CARE OF HEALTH FOR THE AGE GROUP

  The People’s Committees and People’s Councils at all levels shall direct the health sector to coordinate with relevant sectors in developing plans and organizing implementation of plans for health care for older persons in their localities according to the following requirements:

  1. To organize the dissemination of information on physical training, health promotion and disease prevention, especially common diseases among the elderly so that they can take measures to prevent themselves from getting sick.

  2. Organize health checkups to make health monitoring dossiers for older persons. Periodic health checkups shall be carried out once a year; gradually organize a network of family doctors and home-based healthcare services for older persons.

  3. To organize various forms of senior citizen clubs such as health promoting clubs and outdoor health clubs. Depending on conditions and requirements of seniors, each locality may organize clubs for persons suffering from chronic diseases such as diabetes, asthma, hypertension, heart disease, kidney and urinary tract diseases, cataract, etc.

  4. Prioritize investment in community-based rehabilitation programs for disabled older persons to prevent and rehabilitate sequelae from trauma, accidents, or cerebrovascular diseases, chronic diseases, occupational diseases, etc.

  5. To organize volunteer networks to provide home-based health care for older persons, especially poor and lonely persons who have no one to rely on. To encourage retired health workers to volunteer or train volunteers in necessary knowledge about health care for older persons, such as activities of self-care in personal activities, especially for persons suffering from sequelae of heart disease, accidents, social communication activities and self-care in health.

  III. MEDICAL EXAMINATION AND TREATMENT FOR THE AGELESS

  1. To organize a network of medical examination and treatment for the elderly:

  a/ The Gerontology Institute shall have the responsibility to:

  - To organize research on application of scientific and technical advances in three fields: clinical gerontology, basic gerontology and social gerontology, proposing measures to raise the quality of health care for Vietnamese elderly.

  - To implement professional technical guidance on health care for older persons nationwide. To organize inspection of implementation of professional procedures and standards and provision of equipment for lower-level geriatric establishments to ensure rehabilitation training after diseases of older persons.

  - It is a deep treatment line for geriatric pathology.

  - To coordinate with Hanoi Medical University and other medical training schools in providing undergraduate and postgraduate training courses in gerontology. To compile specialized training syllabi and books and popularize knowledge on health care for the elderly.

  b/ General hospitals of class I and class II: To set up geriatric departments with separate examination rooms for elderly patients at the medical examination departments.

  c/ Specialized hospitals (except for specialized hospitals for children): To organize medical examination for the elderly at their departments; when the elderly patients need to be treated in the inpatient department, the hospitals shall have to provide thorough reception and attentive care to them, so as not to let them lie in the same bed with other patients.

  d/ Medical centers of urban districts, rural districts, provincial towns and cities: having a number of beds for older patients in the clinical departments and organizing medical examination for older persons at the medical examination departments.

  e/ Restorative hospitals must be further built and consolidated according to the provisions of the Minister of Health’s Decision No. 966/BYT of May 30, 1999 on guidelines for operation of restorative hospitals; reserve an appropriate proportion of beds for care and functional rehabilitation of elderly patients. In conjunction with the Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs Service, implement the regime of convalescence for senior citizens with meritorious services to the country.

  e/ The commune-level medical stations shall manage the health of and provide first-aid care for local older persons. In cases where disabled or lonely older persons fall ill but fail to seek medical examination and treatment at prescribed places, the heads of the commune-level medical stations shall appoint medical workers to conduct medical examination and treatment at such persons' places or report to the local People's Committees for organizing the transport of the sick persons to medical examination and treatment establishments.

  2. Treatment work.

  - The examination and treatment of elderly patients shall be carried out by specialized medical teams; in case of emergency, they shall be transferred to the nearest health establishment.

  - Medical examination and treatment establishments shall give priority to examining older patients (after emergency care) before treating them.

  - To step by step organize the management of chronic diseases for the elderly under programs and projects so as to detect and handle in time when there are unusual developments. To have plans on prevention and treatment of chronic diseases for the affected persons.

  - To step up the functional rehabilitation for elderly patients after acute treatment at hospitals and guide their continued treatment at home.

  - To combine traditional medical treatment methods with modern medicine, develop non-pharmaceutical treatment methods especially at grassroots health stations for older patients.

  IV. FUNDS FOR PREVENTION AND TREATMENT OF HEALTH PROBLEMS OF THE AGELESS

  1. The local government shall be responsible for funding health checkups to make health monitoring books for older persons, organize periodic health checkups and perform initial health care activities for older persons.

  2. Funds for medical examination and treatment:

  a/ Persons with compulsory health insurance cards or voluntary health insurance cards shall be covered by social insurance for payment of medical examination and treatment expenses according to the provisions of law on health insurance.

  b/ Persons eligible for the regime of medical examination and treatment for the poor shall be paid by the fund for medical examination and treatment for the poor for medical examination and treatment expenses according to the Prime Minister's Decision No. 139/2002/QD-TTg of January 15, 2002 on medical examination and treatment for the poor and Joint Circular No. 14/2002/Tr-LT-BYT-BTC of December 16, 2002, of the Ministry of Health and the Ministry of Finance, guiding the implementation of the above-said Decision.

  c/ The elderly aged 90 or more shall enjoy the health insurance regime prescribed in the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs’ Circular No. 24/2003 of January 6, 2003 guiding the implementation of the Government’s Decree No. 120/2003/ND-CP of January 20, 2003.

  3. Those who do not have health insurance cards as prescribed in Point 2 above shall have to pay by themselves expenses for medical examination and treatment.

  4. To encourage domestic and foreign organizations and individuals, families of older persons to buy HI cards for older persons.

  5. Local governments shall establish health care funds for older persons to support their health care and medical examination and treatment.

  V. ORGANIZATION OF IMPLEMENTATION

  1. At provincial, district and communal levels, the health authorities shall organize the Senior Citizen Health Care Committees under the leadership of the heads of the local administrations, with the participation of the Finance, Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs sectors, relevant sectors and representatives of the Senior Citizen Association, Red Cross Society, Fatherland Front, Peasants’ Union and Women’s Union in order to ensure and maintain the health care activities for senior citizens in their localities. In order to unify the health care activities, the Senior Citizen Health Care Committees should be incorporated into the local initial health care committees.

  2. Departments of Health of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall advise People’s Committees of provinces on developing plans for health care for older persons throughout provinces in accordance with this Circular and direct district-level health authorities and commune-level health authorities to participate in health care for older persons within their localities.

  3. The Department of Treatment shall cooperate with relevant departments, functional departments, Inspectorate of Ministry of Health, Geriatrics Institute and relevant units in guiding, directing, inspecting and supervising the implementation of this Circular.

  4. The health education communication center shall cooperate with the Geriatrics Institute in disseminating knowledge about health care for older persons.

  5. This Circular takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette. Any problems arising in the course of implementation should be reported to the Ministry of Health (Department of Treatment) for consideration and settlement or amendment and supplementation accordingly.

The Health Education and Promotion Office shall coordinate with the Geriatrics Institute in organizing the dissemination of knowledge on health care for the elderly.
