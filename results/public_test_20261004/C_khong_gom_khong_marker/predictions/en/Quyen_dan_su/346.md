                                                   DECREE

   THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE No. 77/2001/ND-CP OF OCTOBER 22, 2001, DETAILING THE REGISTRATION OF MARRIAGE ACCORDING TO THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY’S RESOLUTION No. 35/2000/QH10 ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MARRIAGE AND FAMILY LAW

                                                   GOV

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization of September 30, 1992;
  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated June 9, 2000;
  Pursuant to the National Assembly's Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of June 9, 2000 on the implementation of the Marriage and Family Law;
  To strengthen state management in the field of marriage and family, ensure the legitimate rights and interests of citizens, and move towards eliminating the situation of unregistered marriage, and encourage the establishment of lawful marriage relations; At the proposal of the Minister of Justice,

                                                   DECREE

  Chapter 1.

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for marriage registration for:

  a/ Cases of husband and wife relationship established before January 3, 1987, the effective date of the 1986 Marriage and Family Law but not yet registered for marriage;

  b/ Cases of men and women living together as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001, when the 2000 Marriage and Family Law took legal effect, but marriage has not yet been registered.

  2. The provisions of clause 1 of this Article shall also apply to cases where one party is a Vietnamese citizen and the other party is a citizen of a neighbor country bordering Vietnam who has lived stably and for a long time in Vietnam; cases where one party is a Vietnamese citizen and the other party is a stateless person residing permanently in Vietnam or both parties are stateless persons residing permanently in Vietnam.

  A stateless person permanently residing in Vietnam mentioned in this Clause means a person who does not have Vietnamese nationality and is not recognized by other countries as having such nationality, and who is currently living and working stably and permanently in Vietnam.

  Article 2.- Encouragement of marriage registration and marriage registration obligation

  1. For cases of husband and wife relationship established before January 3, 1987 but marriage has not yet been registered, the State shall encourage and create favorable conditions for marriage registration. The time limit for marriage registration in these cases is not restricted.

  2. A man and a woman who cohabited as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001 and met all conditions for marriage under the 2000 Marriage and Family Law shall have the obligation to register their marriage. After January 1, 2003, if they fail to register their marriage, the law will not recognize them as husband and wife.

  Article 3. Recognition of marriage valid day

  Marriage relations of persons who register their marriage under this Decree shall be recognized from the date on which the partners establish the husband and wife relation or live together as husband and wife in reality. The date of recognition of marriage shall be clearly inscribed in the Marriage Register and Marriage Certificate according to guidance of the Ministry of Justice.

  Article 4.- Exemption from marriage registration fee

  The marriage registration for cases specified in this Decree is exempted from fee.

  Chapter II

                                        PROCEDURES FOR MARRIAGE REGISTRATION

  Article 5.- Competence and procedures for marriage registration

  1. The People’s Committee of commune, ward or township (hereinafter referred to as commune-level People’s Committee) where either partner registers permanent residence shall perform the marriage registration.

  In case both parties do not have permanent residence registration but have registered temporary residence for a definite period in accordance with the law on household registration, the commune-level People’s Committee of the place where either party registers temporary residence shall register their marriage.

  2. Upon marriage registration, the parties shall only have to fill in the marriage registration form and produce their identity cards or valid substitutes thereof.

  In the marriage registration declaration, the parties shall write the date of establishment of their husband and wife relationship or cohabitation as husband and wife in reality. In case the husband and wife cannot determine the date of establishment of their husband and wife relationship or cohabitation as husband and wife in reality, the date shall be calculated as follows:

  If a month is identified but the date cannot be determined, the first day of the next month shall be used.

  If a year is identified but the day and month are not, January 1 of the next year shall be used.

  Article 6.- Settlement of marriage registration for husband and wife relations established before January 3, 1987

  In case both husband and wife permanently or temporarily reside in the place where their marriage is registered, the People's Committee shall register their marriage right after receiving the application form for marriage registration.

  In cases where one of the partners does not permanently or temporarily reside at the place of their marriage registration, but the People's Committee is aware of their marital status, the marriage registration shall also be processed immediately. When there are unclear circumstances as to whether the partners have committed a monogamous relationship, the People's Committee shall request them to make a written commitment with certification by at least two witnesses regarding the contents of such commitment. The witnesses shall be legally responsible for the accuracy of their testimonies.

  Article 7.- Settlement of marriage registration for cases where a man and a woman cohabit as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001

  In case either of the two partners has no permanent residence registration or has a temporary residence registered at the place of marriage registration, the marriage registration declaration must be certified by the agency or unit where he/she is working (for officials, public employees and persons serving in the armed forces) or the People's Committee of the commune where his/her permanent residence or temporary residence is registered. If both partners permanently or temporarily reside at the place of marriage registration, the above certification is not required.

  After receiving a marriage registration declaration, the People's Committee shall check whether both partners are eligible for marriage as prescribed in Article 9 of the 2000 Marriage and Family Law. If there is any unclear circumstance that either or both partners are not eligible for marriage, the People's Committee shall request them to make a written commitment which is certified by at least two witnesses. The witnesses shall be responsible before law for the accuracy of their testimonies.

  Article 8. Places of marriage registration

  In order to create favorable conditions for marriage partners, marriage registration may be carried out at the office of the People's Committee or at a village, hamlet or residential quarter according to the plan of the commune-level People's Committee.

  Article 9. Recognition of common children of husband and wife

  Children born before the marriage registration date of cases specified in Article 1 of this Decree and recognized by their parents are also common children of husband and wife.

  If the father’s section in the birth register and birth certificate of a child remains blank, the People's Committee of the commune where the birth was registered shall, based on the marriage certificate of the parents, add the father’s section to the birth register and birth certificate of the child and cross out the note “out-of-wedlock child” in the birth register.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 10. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Justice The Ministry of Justice shall be responsible for assisting the Government in directing, guiding and inspecting the implementation of this Decree.

  Article 11. Responsibilities of People’s Committees at all levels

  1. Provincial People’s Committees and district-level People’s Committees shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, guide, direct, inspect and urge commune-level People’s Committees in implementing this Decree.

  2. The commune-level People’s Committees shall take responsibility to:

  a) Propagate, mobilize, review and make a list of cases requiring marriage registration in accordance with this Decree;

  b/ To organize timely, convenient and accurate marriage registration for cases specified in this Decree;

  c/ To accurately and promptly certify the marital status of the marriage partners, if so requested;

  d/ To ensure balance of funds from the state budget in accordance with current regulations for marriage registration activities mentioned in this Decree.

  Article 12. Effect

  1. This Decree takes effect 15 days after its signing.

  2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.-
