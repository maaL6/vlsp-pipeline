                                                    DECREE

    THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE No. 88/2003/ND-CP OF JULY 30, 2003, PROVIDING FOR THE ORGANIZATION, OPERATION AND MANAGEMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

                                                    GOV

  Pursuant to the Ordinance No. 102/SL-L004 of May 20, 1957 promulgating the Law on the Right to Establish Associations; and the Civil Code of October 28, 1995;
  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
  At the proposal of the Minister of Home Affairs,

                                                    DECREE

  Chapter 1.

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for organization, operation and state management of associations.

  2. This Decree does not apply to organizations:

  a) Vietnam Fatherland Front, Vietnam General Confederation of Labor, Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, Vietnam Peasants' Association, Vietnam War Veterans' Association, Vietnam Women's Union;

  b/ Church organizations.

  Article 2. Association

  1. The association specified in this Decree is understood as a voluntary organization of Vietnamese citizens and organizations in the same industry or occupation, with the same interests, gender, having the same purpose of gathering and uniting members, regularly operating, not for self-seeking purposes, to protect the lawful rights and interests of its members; to support each other in effective operation, contributing to the national socio-economic development, and to be organized and operate under this Decree and other relevant legal documents.

  2. Associations have various names: unions, general associations, federations, societies, clubs with legal person status and other names as prescribed by law (hereinafter referred to as associations).

  3. Scope of operation of associations (territorial) includes:

  a) The association has a nationwide or inter-provincial scope of operation;

  b/ The association operates within a province or centrally run city (below referred to as province);

  c/ The association operates within a district, town or provincial city (hereinafter referred collectively to as district);

  d/ The association operates within a commune, ward or township (hereinafter referred collectively to as commune).

  Article 3.- Establishment of associations and principles for organization and operation of associations

  1. The establishment of an association must be permitted by a competent state agency defined in Article 15 of this Decree.

  2. The association shall be organized and operate in accordance with its charter approved by a competent authority and not contrary to law; the association shall be organized and operate on the principles of voluntariness, self-governance, self-financing, and legal liability.

  Article 4. Responsibilities of regulatory agencies for associations

  1. State agencies shall, within the ambit of their functions and tasks, create favorable conditions for associations to operate in accordance with their charters in an effective manner.

  2. Societies recognized as socio-political organizations, socio-political professional organizations or associations engaged in activities associated with the State's tasks shall be supported by the State budget according to regulations of the Prime Minister.

  Article 5. Legal entity status, seals, name and symbols of associations

  1. The association has its own legal entity status, seal and account.

  2. The association shall select its name and symbol in accordance with law.

  Chapter II

                                    CONDITIONS AND PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 6. Conditions for establishment of associations

  1. Their purposes of operation are not contrary to law; their names and main fields of operation are not identical to those of associations already lawfully established in the same territorial area.

  2. Having a charter.

  3. Have head office.

  4. Have enough number of members registered to participate.

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall prescribe the number of members registered to join the association.

  Article 7. Campaign board

  1. To establish a society, its founders shall set up a campaign board for establishment of the society. The campaign board shall be recognized by the State management agency in charge of the sector or domain in which the society is expected to operate.

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall prescribe the number of members of the Campaign Board.

  2. After being recognized, the Campaign Board shall perform the following tasks:

  a) To mobilize citizens and organizations to join the association, complete dossiers of application for establishment of the association;

  b) The head of the campaign committee shall submit an application for establishment to a competent authority as prescribed in Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 8. Applications for establishment of associations

  1. Applications for association establishment.

  2. Draft Charter.

  3. Expected operation orientation.

  4. The list of members of the Campaign Board shall be recognized by competent state agencies.

  5. The curriculum vitae of the head of the campaign board, certified by a competent authority.

  6. Documents determining the headquarters and assets of the association.

  Article 9. Main contents of the society charter

  1. Name of the association.

  2. The purposes, scope and domains of operation of the association.

  3. Tasks and powers of associations.

  4. Organization and operation principles.

  5. The formalities for entering and leaving the association.

  6. Membership criteria.

  7. Rights and obligations of members.

  8. Structure, organization and procedures for election and dismissal; tasks and powers of the leadership board, inspection board and other leading positions of the association.

  9. Assets, finance and methods of managing assets and finance of the association.

  10. Conditions for liquidation and final settlement of assets and finance.

  11. Commendation and handling of violations.

  12. Procedures for amending and supplementing the Charter.

  13. Effect.

  Article 10. Responsibilities of competent state agencies permitting association establishment upon receiving dossiers of application for association establishment permission

  The competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall issue a receipt when receiving an application for establishment of a society. If the application is complete and lawful, within 60 days after receiving it, the competent state agency shall reply the Society establishment campaigning board; in case of refusal, it shall issue a written reply clearly stating the reason.

  Article 11. Time of holding a founding congress

  1. Within 90 days after a decision to permit the establishment of a society takes effect, the campaigning board shall hold a congress.

  2. Past the above time limit, if the congress is not held, the campaigning board shall send a written request for extension to the state agency that has decided to permit the association establishment. The extension duration must not exceed 30 days. Past this time limit, if the congress is not held, the decision permitting the association establishment shall cease to be effective.

  Article 12. Main contents of a founding congress

  1. Announcement of decision permitting establishment of associations.

  2. Discuss and vote on the Charter.

  3. To elect the leadership and inspection boards in accordance with the association charter.

  4. To adopt the association’s operation program.

  5. To adopt the congress’s resolution.

  Article 13. Report on congress results

  Within 30 days after a congress, the leadership board of a society shall send the congress documents to the state agency which has decided to permit the establishment of the society, including:

  1. The society charter and minutes of the society charter approval;

  2. The minutes of the election of the leadership and inspection boards (enclosed with the list) and the personal background of the head of the association;

  3. The operation of the association;

  4. Congress resolutions.

  Article 14.- Approval of the society charter and effect of the society charter

  1. Competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall decide to approve the association charter when it has been approved by the congress. In case the association charter is contrary to law, competent state agencies shall refuse to approve it and request the association to amend it.

  2. The society charter takes effect on the date on which it is approved by a competent state agency.

  Article 15.- The State agencies competent to permit the establishment, division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the society charters

  1. The Minister of Home Affairs shall permit the establishment, division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of charters of associations operating nationwide or inter-provincially.

  2. The President of the People’s Committee of a province shall permit the establishment, division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the Charter of associations operating within the province.

  Chapter III

                                                    MEMBER

  Article 16. Conditions for membership

  Vietnamese citizens and organizations who fully meet the membership criteria prescribed by the association charter and voluntarily apply to join the association may become its members.

  The competence and procedures for admitting members shall be prescribed in the association charter.

  Article 17. Rights and obligations of members

  Rights and obligations of members are prescribed in the society charter.

  Article 18. Associate members and honorary members

  1. Joint-venture enterprises and wholly foreign-owned enterprises (hereinafter referred to as foreign-invested enterprises) operating in Vietnam that contribute to the development of the association and agree with its Charter shall be considered for recognition as associate members by the association of economic organizations.

  The associate members may participate in activities and attend the congresses of the association. The associate members shall not participate in election and nomination of the leadership of the association, nor vote on issues of the association.

  The procedures for admitting members of an association shall be prescribed in the association charter.

  2. Vietnamese citizens and organizations that are not eligible to become members of associations but make contributions to such associations may be recognized by the associations as their associate members or honorary members. Rights and obligations of associate members and honorary members shall be prescribed in the associations charters.

  Chapter IV

                          ORGANIZATION, OPERATION, RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE ASSOCIATION

  Article 19. Term congresses and extraordinary congresses

  1. The supreme governing body of a society is its general meeting or congress of delegates.

  2. The term of a congress shall be prescribed in the association charter but must not exceed five years.

  3. An extraordinary congress shall be convened when at least two-thirds of the total number of Executive Committee members or at least half of the total number of official members so request.

  Article 20.- Principal contents of decisions at congresses

  1. Operation orientation of the association.

  2. Election of the leadership board and inspection board.

  3. Change of association name and amendment of the Charter (if any).

  4. Joining a union of associations in the same sector.

  5. Division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution of associations.

  6. Finance of the association.

  Article 21. Voting rules at a congress

  1. Congresses may vote by hand or secret ballot. The congresses shall decide on the form of voting.

  2. The adoption of a decision of a congress must be approved by more than half of the present official delegates.

  Article 22. Rights of associations

  1. To propagate the association’s purposes.

  2. Represent members in internal and external relations related to the functions and tasks of the association.

  3. Protect the rights and legitimate interests of the association and its members.

  4. Organize and coordinate activities among members for the common interests of the association; reconcile disputes within the association.

  5. To disseminate and train knowledge for members; to provide necessary information for members in accordance with law.

  6. To advise and critically comment on issues falling within the scope of operation of the association at the request of organizations and individuals.

  7. To contribute opinions to legal documents related to the association’s operation in accordance with law. To propose to competent state agencies matters related to the association’s development and operation.

  8. To coordinate with relevant agencies and organizations in performing the society’s tasks.

  9. To raise funds for the association on the basis of membership fees and revenues from business and service activities in accordance with law to cover their operating expenses.

  10. To receive lawful financial supports from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals in accordance with law.

  11. A national or inter-provincial association may join as a member of an international or regional association under the provisions of the Government’s Decree No. 20/2002/ND-CP of February 20, 2002 on conclusion and implementation of international agreements between provinces and centrally-run cities, socio-political organizations, social organizations and socio-professional organizations of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  Article 23.- Obligations of associations

  1. The association's activities must comply with its approved charter.

  2. The association shall be subject to state management by the state management agency in charge of the sector or domain in which it operates.

  3. 30 days before organizing a term congress, the leadership board of the society shall report in writing to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree and the ministry managing the sector or domain in which the society operates.

  4. The establishment of a representative office of an association in another locality must be permitted by the provincial-level People’s Committee of the locality where such office is located and reported in writing to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  5. When changing the President, Vice President or General Secretary of the association, changing its head office, amending and supplementing its Charter, the association shall report to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  6. The establishment of legal entities under associations must comply with regulations of law and be reported to competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  7. Annually, associations shall report on their organization and operation to competent state agencies and state management agencies of sectors or fields in which they operate, no later than December 1 every year.

  8. To abide by the guidance, inspection and examination of competent state agencies on law observance.

  9. The list of members, branches, affiliated units and financial documents of the association, minutes of meetings of the leadership board shall be made into dossiers and kept at the head office of the association.

  10. The revenues specified in Clauses 9 and 10 Article 22 of this Decree shall be used for association activities according to the association charter and not distributed to members.

  11. The use of the association’s funds must comply with law. Annually, the association shall make financial statements according to the State’s regulations and send them to the finance agency at the same level.

  Chapter 5.

                             DIVISION, COMBINATION, UNION AND DISUNION

  Article 24. Division, separation, merger, consolidation and dissolution

  1. Depending on the requirements and operational capacity of the association, the leadership board shall request the competent authority specified in Article 15 of this Decree to grant permission for the division, separation, merger, consolidation or dissolution of the association. The division, separation, merger, consolidation or dissolution of the association shall comply with regulations of law.

  2. The association shall be dissolved in the following cases:

  a) Self-dissolution;

  b) Request competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree to decide on dissolution.

  Article 25. Self-dissolution of associations

  The association shall dissolve in the following cases:

  1. Upon expiry of operation duration;

  2. At the request of more than half of the total number of official members;

  3. Completed objectives.

  Article 26.- Responsibilities of the leadership board of a society when it dissolves itself

  1. To send to competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree the following documents:

  a) Application for dissolution of the association;

  b) Resolution on dissolution of the association;

  c) Asset and financial statement;

  d) Proposed method of handling assets and finance and deadline for debt repayment.

  2. To notify the deadline for debt repayment (if any) to relevant organizations and individuals in accordance with law on 5 consecutive issues of a central newspaper for a national or an inter-provincial association; or a local newspaper for a provincial association.

  Article 27. Decision on dissolution of associations

  The competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall decide on the dissolution of the association after 15 days from the date of expiration of the debt payment and liquidation period stated in the association's application for dissolution without any complaint.

  The association shall terminate its operation as from the effective date of the decision of the competent state agency on its dissolution.

  Article 28.- Dissolved associations

  The association is dissolved under the decision of a competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree in the following cases:

  1. The association fails to operate for 12 consecutive months;

  2. When a congress resolution on dissolution is passed but the leadership board of the association fails to comply with it;

  3. The association’s activities seriously violate law.

  Article 29. Responsibilities of competent state agencies upon association dissolution

  When the association is dissolved, the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall:

  a/ To decide on the dissolution of the association;

  b/ Announcing the decision to dissolve the association on the mass media.

  Article 30. Settlement of assets and finance upon dissolution, consolidation, merger or division or separation of associations

  1. When a society dissolves or is dissolved, its assets shall be handled as follows:

  a/ Assets and finance donated by domestic and foreign organizations; assets and finance supported by the State, for which the association has fulfilled all property obligations and paid all debts, the remaining assets and financial balance shall be decided by competent state agencies;

  b/ For self-generated assets and financial resources of the association which have been fully used for the performance of obligations on assets and payment of debts after the association is dissolved, the remaining assets and financial balance shall be decided by the association under its Charter.

  2. To settle assets and finance of the association when it is consolidated:

  a) After the merger, the old associations shall cease to exist and the new association shall enjoy all lawful rights and benefits and be responsible for outstanding debts and service contracts currently being executed by the old associations;

  b/ The assets and finance of the merged associations shall not be divided or transferred but wholly transferred to the new association.

  3. To settle assets and finance of the association upon merger:

  a/ If a society is merged into another society, its assets and finance shall be transferred to the merging society;

  b) The merged association shall enjoy lawful rights and benefits regarding assets and finance of the merged associations, be responsible for unpaid debts regarding assets and finance and ongoing service contracts.

  4. To settle assets and finance of the association upon its division or separation:

  a/ After a society is divided, the divided society shall terminate its operation and its property and finance-related rights and obligations shall be transferred to the new society under the decision on division of the society;

  b/ After separating, associations shall exercise their property and finance-related rights and obligations in accordance with their operation purposes.

  Article 31. Right to complain

  In case a society is dissolved without its consent, it may lodge a complaint according to law. While waiting for the settlement of the complaint, the society shall not operate.

  Chapter 6.

                                      STATE MANAGEMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 32. State management of associations

  1. To formulate or promulgate legal documents on associations.

  2. To guide ministries, sectors, localities, associations and citizens in implementing the law on associations.

  3. To permit the establishment, division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the society charter as prescribed in Article 15 of this Decree.

  4. To provide professional guidance for officials and public employees engaged in the management of associations.

  5. Disseminating the law on associations.

  6. Inspect and examine the observance of law on associations; inspect the implementation of the association charters by associations.

  7. Manage the signing of international cooperation agreements on associations in accordance with law.

  8. Settle complaints and denunciations, handle violations of the law on associations.

  9. Consolidating reports on the organization, operation and management of the association.

  The Ministry of Home Affairs shall assist the Government in performing the unified state management of associations nationwide.

  Article 33. State management tasks of ministries and ministerial-level agencies for associations operating in the fields under their management nationwide

  1. Sign a written agreement with competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree on the permission for establishment, division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the society charter and recognition of the society establishment campaigning board.

  2. Guide and facilitate associations to participate in activities under the management of ministries as prescribed by law; organize consultation with associations to improve regulations on state management of the sectors or fields.

  3. Inspect the implementation of regulations on state management of the sector or industry for associations; handle violations or propose handling of violations by competent state authorities in accordance with law.

  Article 34.- State management tasks of provincial-level People’s Committees regarding associations operating within provinces

  1. Exercise of competence as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 15 hereof and state management of organization and operation of associations.

  2. Inspect and examine the observance of law on associations.

  3. Settle complaints and denunciations and handle violations of the law on associations.

  4. Consider supporting local associations.

  5. Consider and permit local associations to receive donations from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals in accordance with law.

  6. To direct provincial/municipal departments, sectors and People’s Committees of districts and communes in managing associations.

  7. Annually, make a consolidated report on the organization, operation and management of associations in their localities to the Ministry of Home Affairs.

  Chapter VII

                                    COMMENDATION AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 35. Commendation

  1. A society that makes many contributions to socio-economic development shall be commended and/or rewarded according to the State's regulations.

  2. Members who have made many achievements shall be commended and/or rewarded according to regulations of the association and the State.

  Article 36. Handling of violations

  1. Those who violate the right to establish associations or abuse the association name to conduct illegal activities shall, depending on the nature and severity of their violations, be disciplined, administratively sanctioned or examined for penal liability in accordance with law; if causing material damage, they must pay compensation therefor in accordance with law.

  2. Those who abuse their positions and powers to permit the establishment of associations in contravention of this Decree shall, depending on the nature and severity of their violations, be disciplined, administratively sanctioned or examined for penal liability in accordance with law; if causing material damage, they must pay compensation therefor in accordance with law.

  3. If the leadership board or representative of the association deliberately prolongs the term of the congress prescribed by its charter or fails to comply with regulations on obligations of associations, they shall be handled according to the nature and seriousness of their violations as prescribed by law.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 37. Effect

  1. This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO.

  2. This Decree replaces the Prime Minister's Decree No. 258-TTg of June 14, 1957, detailing the implementation of the Law No. 102/SL/L004 of May 20, 1957 on the right to establish associations.

  3. The associations were permitted to be re-founded under the Order No. 102/SL/L004 of May 20, 1957 by the Ministry of Home Affairs and the provincial executive committees, and under the Directive No. 01/CT of January 5, 1989 on the management, organization and activities of mass associations by the Chairman of the Council of Ministers (now the Prime Minister) or the Minister or the Head of the Organization and Personnel Committee of the Government (now the Minister of Home Affairs), the President of the People's Committee of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government, and are not required to apply for re-founding.

  4. The association of economic organizations established under the Decision No.38/HDBT of April 10, 1989 of the Council of Ministers (now the Government) on economic union in production, circulation and services shall not have to apply for re-establishment.

  Article 38. Implementation

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall provide guidelines for this Decree.

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies and government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.-
