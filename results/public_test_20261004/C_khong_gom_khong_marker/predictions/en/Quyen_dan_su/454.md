                                                  DECISION

    PROMULGATING PROVISIONS ON THE PERMANENT REINTATEMENT OF FOREIGNERS’ CHILDREN WHO ARE VIETNAMESE OR ABANDONED OR ABDUCTED IN ADOPTION CAMPOS BY EMPLOYMENT, VETERANS AND SOCIAL SECURITY DEPARTMENTS

                                           THE MINISTER’S COUNCIL

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Council of Ministers dated July 4, 1981;
  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated December 29, 1986;
  Pursuant to the Law on protection, care for and education of children of August 12, 1991;
  At the request of the Minister of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs,

                                                  DECISION

  Article 1.- To promulgate now provisional regulations on the adoption by foreigners of Vietnamese orphans, abandoned children and disabled children in the nurturing establishments under the management of the labor, war invalids and social affairs service, enclosed with this Decision.

  Article 2.- This Decision takes effect as from its signing. All previous provisions contrary to this Decision are now annulled.

  Article 3.- The ministers, the State Committee managers, the heads of the agencies attached to the Council of Ministers and the presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government shall have to implement this Decision.


                                                                       Phan Văn Khải

                                                                            (Signed)



                                                    PROVISIONS

     FOR THE TIME-being OF FOREIGNERS TO RECEIVE ADOPTED CHILDREN WHO ARE VIETNAMESE AND ABANDONED OR ABDUCTED BY THEYSELF AT THE ADOPTION PROVINCES, BY THEIR WORKING IN THE SOCIAL SECURITY AND SOCIAL PROTECTION FIELD
        (Promulgated together with Decision No. 145-HDBT of April 29, 1992 of the Council of Ministers)

  Chapter 1.

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- The adoption of Vietnamese orphans, abandoned children and disabled children by foreigners must be based on the affection between the adoptive parents and the adopted child in the relationship between father and daughter, ensuring that the child is properly nurtured, looked after and educated.

  Article 2.- A foreigner who adopts a child must be twenty years older than the adopted child.

  Article 3.- The adoptees mentioned in Article 1 of this Decree are persons living in the fostering establishments managed by the labor, war invalid and social affairs branch, including:

  - Children aged 15 years or younger, in cases of disability, may be over 15 years old.

  - The adopted child may be over 15 years old if the adoptive parent is an old, weak and lonely person.

  Article 4.- The adoption of children shall be based on the principle of voluntariness of the adoptive parents, the parents, the patron or the director of the child-rearing establishment. If the child is nine years old or older, his/her consent is also required.

  Article 5.- The People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government where there is the child-rearing establishment mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation shall have to consider and grant a certificate of recognition of the application for adoption of a Vietnamese child by a foreigner within two months after receiving the complete dossier. In case of refusal, the applicant shall be informed of the reason.

  Chapter 2:

                           CONDITIONS FOR FOREIGNERS TO RECEIVE ADOPTED CHILDREN

  Article 6.- Foreigners who apply for Vietnamese children mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation must send to the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs all the following papers:

  1. The adoption permission issued by a competent State agency of the country of which the applicant is a national or of which he/she resides (the permit must clearly state the legitimate purpose of the application for adoption);

  2. The certificate of the competent foreign State confirming the health conditions, mental and physical capacity to ensure the nurturing, education and healthy physical, intellectual and moral development of the adopted child;

  3. The application for adoption shall be sent to the People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government where there is the child-rearing establishment, clearly stating the reason for the application, the applicant's name, age and address (if the applicant has a spouse, both spouses' signatures are required) and the name, age and address of the child whom he/she wishes to adopt, and other requirements.

  In case of not knowing the name, age and address of the child to be adopted, the foreigner who asks for adoption may send an application stating his/her wish for adoption to the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of Vietnam for selection and introduction.

  The above-mentioned papers, which are written in foreign languages, must be translated into Vietnamese and certified by a Vietnamese notary public.

  Article 7.- The papers prescribed in Points 1 and 2 of Article 6 shall remain valid for 6 months after they are certified by the competent State bodies of the host country.

  Article 8.- After being notified of the acceptance of the adoption application or after being authorized by the adopter, the applicant shall have to come to Vietnam and carry out the following procedures at the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs:

  1. To present the permit to the child who enters the country of the adoptive parent;

  2. To undertake to name a child as adopted by a local social organization (a non-governmental organization sponsored by the State) where the adoptive parent resides;

  3. To make annual commitments to notify and send photos of adopted children together with their adoption applications to the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of Vietnam until the adopted children reach full 18 years old;

  4. To undertake to rear the adopted children in a proper manner; to assist them in health restoration or rehabilitation in order to improve their disabled conditions if they are disabled children.

  5. To undertake to provide their adopted children with cultural education or vocational training.

  6. To bear all expenses related to the adoption procedures.

  Chapter III

                                            IMPLEMENTATION ORGANIZATION

  Article 9.- The adoption of a child shall be effected when the following papers are available:

  1. The certificate of the People’s Committee of a province or centrally-affiliated city permitting a foreigner to adopt a Vietnamese child;

  2. The record of transfer of a child to be adopted by a foreigner.

  3. The commitments of Article 8 of this Regulation.

  4. Passports of foreigners who adopt children.

  5. The person authorized to receive adopted children must have a lawful authorization letter from the adoptive parent.

  Article 10.- The Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall set up a specialized working section to monitor Vietnamese children adopted by foreigners under this Decision’s Provision.

  Article 11.- The Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall coordinate with the Ministry for Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Justice in guiding the implementation of this Provision.

  Article 12. These provisional regulations shall take effect from the date of their promulgation.
