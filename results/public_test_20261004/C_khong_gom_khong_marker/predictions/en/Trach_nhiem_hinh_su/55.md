                                                  RESOLUTION

     GUIDANCE ON APPLICATION OF A NUMBER OF PROVISIONS OF THE CRIMINAL CODE IN THE JUDGMENT OF COMMITTING RAPE AND OTHER OFFENCES RELATING TO OFFENSES

                            JUDGES' COUNCIL OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of People's Courts dated November 24, 2014;

  To properly and uniformly apply a number of provisions of the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 amended by Law No. 12/2017/QH14 in the trial of corruption-related crimes and other position-related crimes;

  After obtaining opinions from the Prosecutor General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy and the Minister of Justice.

                                                 DECISION:

  Article 1. Scope

  This Resolution provides guidance on application of some regulations of the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 amended by Law No. 12/2017/QH14 (hereinafter referred to as “the Criminal Code”) on corruption-related crimes and other crimes involving positions; determination of value of property appropriated, damage caused by corruption-related crimes and other crimes involving positions; handling of items and money directly related to crimes.

  Article 2. Definitions

  1. “Agencies and organizations” as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 352 of the Criminal Code include state agencies, organizations, units and non-state enterprises and organizations.

  2. “State agencies, organizations and units” means agencies, organizations and units specified in Clause 9 Article 3 of the Law on Anti-Corruption, including: state agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, people’s armed forces units, public service providers, state enterprises and other organizations and units established or invested in by the State with their operation funding allocated in whole or in part by the State, directly managed or managed by the State to meet the needs of the common development and essential needs of the State and society.

  3. “Non-state enterprises and organizations” as prescribed in Articles 353, 354, 364 and 365 of the Criminal Code are enterprises and organizations not falling into the cases guided in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. “Persons holding positions” as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 352 of the Criminal Code are those specified in Clause 2 Article 3 of the Law on Anti-Corruption.

  5. “doing another form of crime” as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 352 of the Criminal Code is a case where the person is not appointed, elected, recruited or hired but is assigned to perform tasks and has authority while performing such tasks.

  Example: A person assigned by a competent authority to attend at the checkpoint for Covid-19 prevention and control.

  6. “Voluntary declaration before detection” as prescribed in Clause 7 Article 364 and Clause 6 Article 365 of the Criminal Code refers to cases where corrupting acts or bribery activities have not been detected or reported by competent authorities but the corruptors or bribery solicitors have voluntarily declared all corrupting or bribery activities they have committed.

  7. “Voluntarily returning at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property” means cases where the offender has voluntarily returned at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property after committing the crime. It is also considered voluntary return of at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property in cases where the offender, after committing the crime, influences parents, spouses, children, siblings, or other relatives to return or do not object to their return of at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property.

  In case a person is examined for penal liability in the same case for several crimes, including embezzlement, corruption and other crimes, but he/she has voluntarily returned at least three-fourths of the embezzled or bribed property, he/she shall also be considered as having voluntarily returned at least three-fourths of the embezzled or bribed property, except where there are clear grounds to determine that the property originated from another crime.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled VND 4.000.000.000 and abused his position to appropriate VND 2.000.000.000. After A was prosecuted, A’s wife transferred land and house as her personal property to repay VND 3.000.000.000 instead of A, she is considered to have voluntarily repaid at least three-quarters of the embezzled property.

  8. “actively cooperating with competent authorities in detecting, investigating and handling crimes” means that after committing corrupt or other crimes involving positions, the offenders have proactively provided information, documents and evidence that is significant for the detection, investigation and handling of crimes related to the crimes against which they are charged (such as: correctly identifying the place where important exhibits are hidden to assist competent authorities in recovering such exhibits; providing statements and correctly identifying the places where other accomplices are hiding; providing statements on crimes and new offenders involved in the crimes against which they are charged...). In addition to the above cases, other cases may be determined as “actively cooperating with competent authorities in detecting, investigating and handling crimes” but the Courts must clearly state this in their judgments.

  9. “Great meritorious contributions” means cases where offenders have assisted procedural authorities in detecting, arresting, investigating or handling crimes unrelated to the crimes for which they are charged; rescued other people in dangerous situations or rescued property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more of the State, collectives or individuals during natural disasters, epidemics, fires or other force majeure events; or made inventions, discoveries or innovations with significant value as confirmed by competent state authorities. Apart from the above-mentioned cases, other cases may be determined as “great meritorious contributions” but the Courts must clearly state them in their judgments.

  Article 3. Some circumstances as signs of conviction

  1. “Having been disciplined for this act but still committing violations” as prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of Articles 353, 354, 355 and 358 of the Criminal Code refers to cases where the offender has previously been disciplined for the corresponding act but the prescribed time limit for being considered not yet having been disciplined has not expired.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was disciplined in the form of a warning for embezzling VND 500,000 but 6 months later, A embezzled VND 1,500,000.

  In case a person who has been disciplined by a competent agency or organization commits the same act again and is then examined for penal liability by the procedural agency for this act, his/her previous disciplinary action shall not be regarded as having been taken for this act but repeated violation.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was disciplined by a competent authority for embezzlement, and later considered for prosecution for the same act of embezzlement. In this case, the circumstance "having been disciplined for this act but committing another violation" shall not apply to A.

  2. “Having been convicted of one of the crimes prescribed in Section 1 of this Chapter and having the conviction not yet expunged but committing violations again” as prescribed in Clause 1 of Articles 353, 354 and 355 of the Criminal Code means that the offender has previously been convicted once for one or more of the crimes prescribed in Section 1 of Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code and the conviction has not yet been expunged, but then commits one of the acts specified in Clause 1 of one of Articles 353, 354 and 355 of the Criminal Code.

  In case the offender has other previous convictions besides the one mentioned above, these previous convictions shall be used to determine whether he/she relapses into or threatens to relapse into the commission of the same offence, except where all previous convictions have been used to determine signs of a fixed crime in previous convictions.

  Example: Before committing the act of receiving a bribe amounting to VND 1.500.000, A had two previous convictions, in which the first conviction led to A being sentenced for embezzlement with an amount of VND 5.000.000; the second conviction led to A being sentenced for abuse of position or power for embezzlement with an amount of VND 1.000.000. In this case, the second conviction is used to determine the determinate sign for the embezzlement crime. For the first conviction, since it has already been used to determine the determinate sign of abuse of position or power for embezzlement (with an amount of VND 1.000.000), it is not allowed to continue using it to determine recidivism.

  3. “Other material benefits” prescribed in Articles 354, 358, 364 and 366 of the Criminal Code are material benefits other than assets prescribed in Article 105 of the Civil Code.

  Example: Bribery in the form of funding for studying abroad, traveling, etc.

  4. “Intangible benefits” as prescribed in Point b Clause 1 Articles 354, 358, 364, 365 and 366 of the Criminal Code are non-material benefits.

  Ex: Bribery in form of rewards, offer of rewards, titles, prizes; election, appointment, designation; raising of test scores; promises of graduation, study abroad, competition, performance abroad; sexual bribery, etc.

  5. “Abusing position or power” as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 355 of the Criminal Code means using beyond the assigned authority, responsibilities and duties or performing tasks or exercising powers not assigned or assigned to perform in such fields.

  Example: Nguyen Van A is the Vice President of the People’s Committee of a province. A is only assigned to manage social and cultural affairs but is not assigned to manage land, yet he issued a decision on land expropriation for Company X to hand over to Company Y (owned by A’s family). In this case, A’s actions exceed his assigned duties and responsibilities.

  6. “Abusing position or power” as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 356 of the Criminal Code means relying on assigned position or power to commit illegal acts, failing to commit or committing against the law.

  Example: Nguyen Van A is the Head of Department of Natural Resources and Environment of district B who has committed an act against regulations, and has submitted to the leadership of the People’s Committee of district B for signing a decision on issuance of certificate of land use rights for a land parcel which is ineligible for issuance of certificate of land use rights as prescribed by land law.

  7. “profits” as prescribed in clause 1 of Articles 356, 357 and 359 of the Criminal Code means cases where offenders have abused their positions or powers or misused their powers to gain unjust material or non-material benefits for themselves or other agencies, organizations or individuals.

  8. “Other personal motives” as prescribed in Clause 1 of Articles 356, 357 and 359 of the Criminal Code refers to cases where offenders abuse their positions or powers or abuse their authority to legitimately assert, consolidate or enhance their status, prestige or power.

  9. Damage caused by criminal acts prescribed in Articles 353, 354, 355 and 358 of the Criminal Code means actual damage that occurs and is causally related to the criminal act, excluding money, property or other material benefits that the offender has appropriated or will appropriate.

  Example: Nguyen Van A, as the warehouse keeper of Company B, embezzled a sum of 200.000.000 VND in epidemic prevention medicines. Due to lack of such medicines, all poultry products of Company B, valued at 10.000.000.000 VND, died. In this case, the amount embezzled by A is 200.000.000 VND and the actual loss caused by A’s criminal act is 10.000.000.000 VND.

  10. “work-related secrets” as prescribed in Article 361 and Article 362 of the Criminal Code are work-related information presented in any form (e.g., documents, drafts, speeches, images, etc.) that agencies or organizations have stipulated not to disclose to others and are not included in the list of state secrets.

  Article 4. Some factors determining the penalty bracket

  1. The circumstance of “using deceitful tricks” or “using dangerous tricks” as prescribed in Clause 2 of Articles 353, 354, 355, 364 and 365 of the Criminal Code;

  a) “Using deceitful tricks” refers to cases where offenders use sophisticated deception and high technology to commit crimes, conceal their crimes, shift blame onto others, or destroy evidence, thereby obstructing the detection, investigation, and handling of the crime.

  b) “Using dangerous tricks” means cases where offenders use tricks that may endanger the lives or health of others or cause other serious consequences to appropriate property or conceal crimes.

  2. The circumstance of “committing the crime more than once” prescribed in Clause 2 of Articles 353, 354, 355, 356, 357, 358, 361, 364, 365 and 366 of the Criminal Code refers to a case where the offender has committed one of the acts specified in these articles more than twice, each of which constitutes a separate crime, but has not yet been examined for penal liability and the statute of limitations for examination for penal liability has not expired.

  Example: On August 15, 2018, Nguyen Van A embezzled 10.000.000 VND. On September 25, 2019, A embezzled another 20.000.000 VND. The criminal acts of A have not yet been penalized and the prescriptive period for criminal prosecution has not expired. In this case, A is prosecuted for embezzlement and the aggravating circumstance of "committing the crime more than once" as prescribed in Point c Clause 2 Article 353 of the Criminal Code.

  3. The circumstance of “causing adverse effects on the lives of officials, public employees and employees in agencies or organizations” as prescribed in Point g Clause 2 Article 353 of the Criminal Code is one of the following cases:

  a) Cause loss or reduction of regular income and lawful additional income of officials, public employees and workers;

  b) Cause grievances and denunciations, causing disunity and loss of trust among staff members of agencies or organizations, affecting the morale of officials, public employees and workers.

  4. The circumstance of “causing bad influence on security, order and social safety” prescribed in Clause 3 Article 353, 355 of the Criminal Code is one of the following cases:

  a) Organize large-scale petitions, demonstrations or disturbances to enable hostile forces to take advantage of, entice and incite against the government, distort the Party's line, policies and laws of the State;

  b/ To cause confusion, fear or anger among the people;

  c/ Causing difficulties in implementation of the Party's line and the State's policies and laws.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled poverty alleviation funds, causing the People's Committee of commune B to face difficulties in implementing policies for hunger elimination and poverty reduction in the province.

  Article 5. Principles of handling corruption and other crimes involving positions

  1. The handling of corruption-related crimes and other crimes involving positions must be strict and in full compliance with the principles of criminal law and criminal procedure law.

  2. During the course of proceedings, if the offender has actively returned at least three-quarters of the property embezzled or bribed and cooperated actively with competent authorities in detecting, investigating and handling the crime or recorded meritorious contributions, the highest level of the penalty bracket applicable to the offender shall not be applied.

  3. Consider applying the special amnesty policy of exemption from penalties as prescribed in Article 59 of the Criminal Code to cases where offenders have at least 2 extenuating circumstances specified in Clause 1, Article 51 of the Criminal Code or offenders who commit crimes for the first time are accomplices but play insignificant roles in cases falling into one of the following cases:

  a) The offender commits the crime not for self-seeking motives or other personal motives but only to innovate and take risks for the socio-economic development and national defense;

  b) The offender is a person who has a dependent relationship (such as a subordinate to a superior, an employee receiving salary, or a person performing tasks under the direction of the superior), but is not fully aware of his/her criminal act, not for self-seeking motives or other personal motives, and does not benefit from it; has proactively declared the crime before it was detected, actively cooperated with competent authorities, and effectively contributed to the investigation and handling of the crime;

  c) The offender has proactively declared before being detected, actively cooperated with competent authorities, contributed to limiting damage, proactively returned all appropriated property, has fully remedied consequences and compensated for all damage caused by themselves;

  d) After being detected, the offender has made a sincere declaration of repentance, proactively returned all appropriated property, has fully remedied consequences and compensated for all damage caused.

  Article 6. Consideration of penal liability and decision on penalties in case of separation of lawsuits

  In case a lawsuit is separated for settlement in different stages (separated into multiple lawsuits), the consideration of penal liability and decision on penalties shall be carried out as follows:

  1. The fact that the offender has been convicted in a previous case shall not be used as a ground to determine that he/she has committed the crime more than once, except where the criminal acts are considered and handled in stages that are independent of each other;

  2. The fact that the offender has been convicted in a previous case is not considered as a ground for refusing to grant suspended sentence if he/she meets all other conditions for suspended sentence;

  3. The penalty imposed in each judgment or decision must be such that the total of penalties imposed in all judgments does not exceed the highest level of the corresponding penalty bracket corresponding to the total value of property appropriated, bribes paid, or damage caused by the crime.

  Example: Nguyen Van A committed acts of abuse of power in the performance of his duty. At first stage, it was only possible to prove that A caused property damage valued at VND 100,000,000, so the procedural authority decided to separate into 02 lawsuits and handle A first for the act of causing property damage of VND 100,000,000. However, later in stage 2, the procedural authority was able to prove that A's acts also caused property damage valued at VND 350,000,000. In stage 1, the Court applied clause 2 Article 357 of the Criminal Code to convict A of 05 years' imprisonment for abuse of power in the performance of his duty. When hearing the lawsuit in stage 2, A continued to be prosecuted under clause 2 Article 357 of the Criminal Code, so as to ensure that when summing up the penalties of both judgments, they would not exceed the highest level of this clause, the Court could only decide a penalty of no more than 05 years' imprisonment for A.

  Article 7. Criminal prosecution in case property users have property due to corruption and commit one of money laundering acts

  In case a property user commits one of the money laundering acts prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code due to corruption, apart from being examined for penal liability for the corresponding crime prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, the offender shall also be examined for penal liability for money laundering as prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled 5.000.000.000 VND and used the money to invest in real estate to conceal the illegal origin of the embezzled money. In this case, A is criminally prosecuted for the crime of embezzlement prescribed in Article 353 and money laundering prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code.

  Article 8. Criminal prosecution for repeated commission of the same act prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code

  1. In case a person commits many times the same act prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code but each time the value of property appropriated or bribed is below the minimum level prescribed for criminal prosecution and does not fall into one of other cases subject to criminal prosecution, and at the same time, such person has never been disciplined and the statute of limitations for disciplinary action has not expired, if the total value of property appropriated or bribed or the damage caused is equal to or above the minimum level for criminal prosecution as prescribed by the Criminal Code, then the person who commits many times the same violation shall be criminally prosecuted for the corresponding crime according to the total value of property appropriated, bribed or damaged.

  Example: On August 15, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of VND 1,500,000; on September 15, 2019, A continued to accept a bribe of VND 1,500,000; on December 30, 2019, A again accepted a bribe of VND 1,500,000 which was detected by competent authorities. These acts of A have not yet been disciplined and the statute of limitations for disciplinary action has not expired. In this case, the total amount of bribes accepted by A is determined to be VND 4,500,000, so A is prosecuted for criminal liability under Clause 1 Article 354 of the Criminal Code.

  2. In case a person commits the same act prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code more than once, each time having all constituent elements of a crime and total value of property embezzled or bribed or damaged property from each violation falling within the enhanced penalty bracket, if such violations have not yet been examined for penal liability and the statute of limitations for examination of penal liability has not expired, apart from being subject to the corresponding penalty bracket applicable to the total value of property embezzled or bribed or damaged, he/she shall also be subject to the aggravating circumstance of increased penal liability as follows:

  a) If the total value of property embezzled or bribed or damaged property is subject to the provisions of clause 2 of the corresponding article, the offender shall be additionally subject to the aggravating circumstance of “committing the crime more than once”.

  Example: On August 15, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of VND 50.000.000; on June 30, 2020, A again accepted a bribe of VND 100.000.000 which was detected by competent authorities. In this case, A is subject to two aggravating circumstances: “The bribe is money, assets or other material benefits valued at between VND 100.000.000 and under VND 500.000.000” and “Committing the crime more than once” as prescribed in Points c and dd Clause 2 Article 354 of the Criminal Code.

  b) If the total value of assets appropriated or bribes received or damage under other aggravated penalty frames, the offender shall be additionally subject to the aggravating circumstance of “committing the crime more than once.”

  Example: On March 11, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of VND 400.000.000; on July 30, 2020, A again accepted a bribe of VND 200.000.000 which was detected by competent authorities. In this case, A is subject to the aggravating circumstance of "bribes are money, assets or other material benefits valued at between VND 500.000.000 and under VND 1.000.000.000" as prescribed in point a clause 3 Article 354 and the aggravating circumstance of "committing the crime more than once" as prescribed in point g clause 1 Article 52 of the Criminal Code.

  Article 9. Criminal prosecution in case offenders appropriating property or accepting bribes while causing damage to property

  Where the offender appropriates property or receives a bribe and causes damage to property, the value of the appropriated property, the bribe, and the damaged property shall be subject to different penalty frameworks. The offender shall be criminally prosecuted according to the corresponding points of such penalty frameworks. Where the value of the appropriated property, the bribe, and the damaged property falls under different penalty frameworks, the offender shall be criminally prosecuted according to the higher penalty framework.

  Article 10. Determination of value of property expropriated and damage caused by corruption-related crimes and other position-related crimes

  1. The value of property appropriated as a basis for criminal handling shall be determined at the time of commission of the criminal act. In case the crime is committed continuously or over a long period, the value of property appropriated shall be determined at the time of completion of the criminal act. For cases where the criminal act is in progress but detected or prevented, the value of property appropriated shall be determined at the time when the criminal act is detected or prevented.

  2. Property damage used as a basis for criminal prosecution shall be determined on a case-by-case basis as follows:

  a) In case damage occurs at the time of commission of a crime, such damage shall be determined at this time; for a continuous and prolonged crime, the damage shall be determined at the time when the crime is detected or the crime ends.

  b) In case damage occurs or continues after the crime is committed or has ended, the damage shall be determined at the time when the damage is prevented.

  c) In case of failure to determine the damage as guided in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article, the damage shall be determined at the time of filing the lawsuit.

  3. The civil liability shall be considered in accordance with the civil law.

  Article 11. Handling of items and money directly related to crimes

  1. After accepting a case, the Court shall immediately consider applying the measure of distraint of assets or freezing of accounts directly related to the criminal act if such assets have not yet been subject to enforcement measures during the stage of investigation and prosecution.

  2. The Court shall consider and decide to confiscate and transfer to the state budget, destroy or force the return and compensation for the lawful owner or manager in accordance with law for assets directly related to corruption crimes and other position-related crimes, including:

  a) Tools and instruments used for the commission of the crime;

  b/ Money and assets appropriated;

  c/ Bribes;

  d) Damage caused by criminal acts;

  dd) Illegal gains from criminal acts;

  e) Profits, yields or profits generated from assets acquired through the commission of the crime;

  g) Other assets as prescribed by law.

  3. If the assets subject to confiscation into the state budget no longer exist at the time of resolution of the lawsuit, the Court shall decide on the confiscation of the asset value according to the valuation conclusion of the competent authority.

  4. In case assets originate from corruption crimes or other position-related crimes and are incorporated into a common asset group, the Court shall only confiscate or return them to their lawful owners or managers for the portion originating from crimes. The profits generated from this common asset group shall be divided in proportion to be confiscated or returned to the lawful owners or managers in accordance with law.

  Article 12. Effect

  1. This Resolution was adopted by the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on December 30, 2020 and comes into force as of February 15, 2021.

  2. Where the offender was tried before the effective date of this Resolution in accordance with previous regulations and guidance and the judgment has taken legal effect, this Resolution shall not be used as a basis for lodging protests according to cassation or reopening procedures.
