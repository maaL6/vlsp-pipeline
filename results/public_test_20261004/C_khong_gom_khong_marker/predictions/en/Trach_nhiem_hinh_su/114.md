                                                  RESOLUTION

     ADDITIONAL GUIDANCE OF RESOLUTION No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP DATED OCTOBER 2, 2007 AND RESOLUTION NO. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP DATED OCTOBER 2, 2007 OF THE COUNCIL OF JUDGES OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE’S COURT

                           JUDGES' COUNCIL OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of People's Courts;
  In order to properly and uniformly apply the provisions of the Penal Code and the Criminal Procedure Code;
  After reaching agreement with the Prosecutor General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy and the Minister of Justice,

                                                  DECISION:

  Article 1. To supplement Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Judge's Council of the Supreme People's Court guiding the application of a number of provisions of the Penal Code on statute of limitations for judgment execution, penalty exemption and reduction of penalty-serving duration

  1. To add guidance on the reduction of the term of imprisonment at Point b, Item 3.2, Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Judge's Council of the Supreme People's Court as follows:

  b/ A person sentenced to imprisonment for 30 years or less may have his/her sentence reduced from three months to three years each time. In case of a three-year sentence reduction, he/she must have made particularly outstanding achievements in labor, reform and strict observance of the internal rules and regulations on detention.

  In case a person serving imprisonment penalty is eligible for reduction of the remaining duration of imprisonment and the remaining imprisonment penalty is less than 3 months, the reduced duration of imprisonment may be equal to the remaining duration of imprisonment but he/she must serve half of the imposed penalty in accordance with Clause 3 Article 58 of the Penal Code.

  Example 1: Nguyen Van A was sentenced to 5 years in prison, and A has served 4 years and 10 months of the sentence and is eligible for a reduction in the sentence according to the guidelines in Section 3.1 of Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP. In this case, A may have the remaining sentence reduced (2 months).

  Example 2: Tran Van T was sentenced to 3 months in prison, but he has already served a third of the sentence (1 month) and meets all other conditions for reduction of the time served according to the guidance in Subsection 3.1 Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP. In this case, T can only be reduced by up to 1 month and 15 days because, as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 58 of the Penal Code, T must serve half of the imposed sentence (i.e., 1 month and 15 days).

  2. To add to Section 1a of the guidance on the combination of life imprisonment or death penalty which has been imposed on a defendant by another legally effective judgment in Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Judges' Council of the Supreme People's Court as follows:

  In case a defendant is prosecuted for one or several crimes within the jurisdiction of a district-level People's Court and, upon accepting the case, it is determined that the defendant has been sentenced to life imprisonment or death in another judgment which has taken legal effect, the district-level People's Court shall report such to the provincial-level People's Court so that the latter may reach agreement with the provincial-level People's Procuracy on withdrawing the case file for prosecution and trial at the provincial level.

  Article 2. Supplementing Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP dated October 02, 2007 of the Council of Judges of the Supreme People’s Court guiding a number of provisions in Part five "Execution of Courts' judgments and decisions" of the Criminal Procedure Code

  1. To add Section 10 to Part IV of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Judge's Council of the Supreme People's Court on decisions of the Council for consideration of exemption or reduction as follows:

  10. Based on the review of the dossier and opinions of the representative of the Procuracy, the Board for consideration for exemption or reduction has the right to:

  a) Accept all applications for exemption from or reduction of the length of time of serving suspended sentences;

  b) To accept part of the proposal on exemption from or reduction of the penalty execution term or shortening of the probation period of suspended sentences;

  c) Reject requests for exemption from or reduction of the length of time of serving the suspended sentence;

  d/ For persons proposed for consideration of commutation, if after their proposals are all accepted, the remaining penalty-serving duration is less than one month, the commutation council may decide to commute the entire remaining penalty.

  2. To add the following section 13 to Part IV of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Judge's Council of the Supreme People's Court on the effect of decisions on exemption from or reduction of the duration of serving the suspended sentence or shortening of the probation period:

  13. The court’s decision on exemption from or reduction of the duration of serving the penalty or shortening of the probation period of a suspended sentence may be protested against by the procuracy of the same level or the immediate superior procuracy according to appellate procedures. The time limit for protestation by the procuracy of the same level is seven days and that by the immediate superior procuracy is fifteen days as from the date the court issues the decision. The court’s decision on exemption from or reduction of the duration of serving the penalty or shortening of the probation period of a suspended sentence takes effect as from the date the time limit for protestation according to appellate procedures expires. In case the person who is serving the imprisonment penalty has his/her reduced term equal to the remaining term of imprisonment he/she has to serve, the court’s decision on reduction of the term of serving the imprisonment penalty shall be immediately executed even if such decision is protested against by the procuracy according to appellate procedures.

  3. To add Section 3a guiding on the time of starting to calculate the non-custodial reform period in Part III of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP dated October 2, 2007 of the Judge's Council of the Supreme People's Court as follows:

  The starting date of the non-custodial sentence is the date on which the agency or organization assigned to supervise and educate the convict receives the judgment enforcement decision and a copy of the judgment. The starting date of the non-custodial sentence must be declared in the judgment’s decision section.

  Example: Nguyen Van A is sentenced to 1 year of community sentence for theft under Clause 1 Article 138 of the Criminal Code. In this case, the judgment shall specify: “…sending Nguyen Van A to 1 year of community sentence for theft; the duration of community sentence starts from the date the supervising agency receives the judgment enforcement decision and copies of the judgment”.

  Article 3. Effect

  1. This Resolution was adopted by the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court on September 23, 2010 and takes effect forty five days after its signing.

  2. Difficulties arising during the implementation of this Decree should be promptly reported to the Supreme People’s Court for further clarification or guidance.
