                Law No. 101/2025/QH15                                      Hanoi, November 26, 2025


                                                      LAW

                             TRANSFER OF PERSONS SERVING IMPRISONMENT SENTENCES

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, as amended by Resolution No. 203/2025/QH15;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on Transfer of Persons Serving Prison Sentences.

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Law provides for principles, competence, conditions, procedures for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences between Vietnam and foreign countries; responsibilities of Vietnamese state agencies in transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Law applies to Vietnamese agencies, organizations and individuals and foreign agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences between Vietnam and foreign countries.

  Article 3. Interpretation of terms

  In this Law, the terms below are construed as follows:

  1. Transfer of sentenced person means an act whereby Vietnam and foreign countries transfer to each other sentenced persons who wish to be returned to their home country and agree to receive them for further serving their sentences on the basis of domestic laws, international treaties or the reciprocity principle.

  2. “Persons serving imprisonment sentences” refer to those who have been sentenced to a specific term of imprisonment or life imprisonment and are currently serving their sentences at detention centers.

  3. The transferring country is a country that can transfer or has transferred its current sentenced person to the receiving country.

  4. The receiving country means a country capable of or having received a person serving an imprisonment sentence for further execution of such sentence.

  5. “Conversion of imprisonment penalty” means a competent Vietnamese People’s Court decides to convert the imprisonment penalty in the judgment or decision of a competent foreign court which has been pronounced against a person serving an imprisonment sentence who is received back to Vietnam in accordance with the provisions of the Penal Code and other provisions of Vietnamese law.

  Article 4. Application of laws and principles for transfer of sentenced persons

  1. Transfer of sentenced persons shall comply with this Law; where this Law does not provide for such transfer, regulations of law on criminal procedure, criminal judgment enforcement and other relevant provisions of Vietnamese law shall apply.

  2. The transfer of sentenced persons shall be carried out on the following principles:

  a) Respect for independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity, non-interference in each other’s internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit; compliance with the Constitution, Vietnamese laws and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory;

  b) In case Vietnam and a foreign country are not parties to the same international treaty on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences, the transfer of such persons shall be carried out on the principle of reciprocity but must not contravene Vietnamese law, comply with international law and international practice;

  c) Respect and protect lawful rights and interests of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  Article 5. Central authority of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam for transfer of sentenced persons

  1. The Ministry of Public Security is the central authority of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam for the transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  2. The central authority of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam for transfer of current sentenced persons shall act as the focal point for exchange of information and documents related to transfer of current sentenced persons; prepare, send and receive requests for transfer of current sentenced persons; take charge and cooperate with ministries, sectors and local governments in considering and handling requests for transfer of current sentenced persons; monitor and urge the implementation of transfer of current sentenced persons; perform other tasks and powers as prescribed by law on transfer of current sentenced persons and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  Article 6. Application of the reciprocity principle in transfer of sentenced persons

  1. The application of the reciprocity principle in the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall be carried out when all of the following conditions are fully met:

  a) Comply with the principles specified in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  b) The competent authority of the foreign country makes a written commitment to comply with Vietnam’s request in a similar case. In case Vietnam is the requesting country, the Ministry of Public Security shall make a written commitment to apply the reciprocity principle;

  c) Conform to practical situations and cooperation demands for transfer of sentenced persons between Vietnam and foreign countries.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall, in accordance with Clause 1 of this Article, decide on the application of the reciprocity principle in the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences. Where necessary, the Ministry of Public Security shall seek opinions from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and relevant agencies before making a decision.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 7. Time limit for withdrawal of transfer proposal

  1. The convict or his/her lawful representative shall only have the right to withdraw their application for transfer before the decision on admission of the convict or the decision on transfer of the convict issued by the competent People’s Court of Vietnam takes effect.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 8. Language in transfer of sentenced persons

  1. In case Vietnam and a foreign country are both signatories to an international treaty on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences, the language used in the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall be the language prescribed in such international treaty.

  2. In case Vietnam and a foreign country are not parties to the same international treaty on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences, the application and documents requesting the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences must be enclosed with translations into the language of the receiving country or another language accepted by the receiving country.

  3. The authority preparing the request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence must translate the documents into the language specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 9. Exemption from consular legalization Application files for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall be exempt from consular legalization if they are signed and sealed by competent persons or authorities of foreign countries, unless otherwise provided by international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  Article 10. Expenditures on transfer of sentenced person

  1. In case Vietnam is a transferring country, Vietnam shall pay expenses for transfer of sentenced person arising until the time of handover of sentenced person, unless otherwise agreed.

  2. In case Vietnam is the receiving country and Vietnam incurs expenses for the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences arising from the time of reception of such persons, unless otherwise agreed.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 11. Funding for transfer of sentenced persons

  1. Funding for the transfer of persons serving prison sentences shall be provided by the state budget in accordance with the law on the state budget and other relevant laws.

  The convicts, their legal representatives, other agencies, organizations and individuals may voluntarily contribute or support all or part of living expenses, travel expenses and other expenses of the transferred convicts in accordance with law.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 12. Transit of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. The transit of persons serving imprisonment sentences through the territory of Vietnam must be notified in writing to the Ministry of Public Security for handling. The consent or refusal to transit persons serving imprisonment sentences shall comply with the law.

  2. In case of transit permission, the country requesting transit of a person serving an imprisonment sentence shall be responsible for managing such person during their transit through Vietnam and shall bear all transit costs in accordance with Vietnamese law, unless otherwise agreed upon.

  3. In case of air transport and unexpected landing on Vietnamese territory, the sentenced person may be subject to management in accordance with Vietnamese law. The management of the sentenced person and related costs shall comply with Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. In case Vietnam requests a foreign country to permit transit for a person serving an imprisonment sentence, the Ministry of Public Security shall contact the foreign country to carry out procedures for requesting transit permission.

  5. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 13. Responsibilities of state agencies for transfer of sentenced persons

  1. The Government shall have the following responsibilities:

  a) Unify state management of transfer of sentenced persons;

  b) Report to the National Assembly on the transfer of persons serving prison sentences in annual reports on judgment enforcement.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall be held accountable to the Government for state management of transfer of sentenced person.

  The Minister of Public Security shall take charge and cooperate with the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court and the Procurator General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy in applying science, technology and digital transformation to the transfer of sentenced persons.

  3. The Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall cooperate with the Ministry of Public Security in performing state management of transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  People’s Courts and People’s Procuracies at all levels shall perform the tasks of transferring persons serving imprisonment sentences in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  4. Ministries, ministerial agencies and other relevant agencies shall, within their jurisdiction, perform the tasks of transferring persons serving imprisonment sentences in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws; and cooperate with the Ministry of Public Security in performing state management of transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  Chapter II

              RECEPTION OF PERSONS SERVING IMPRISONMENT SENTENCES IN FOREIGN COUNTRIES TO VIETNAM

  Article 14. Authority to decide on the reception of persons serving their sentences in foreign countries back to Vietnam

  The regional People’s Court of the last place of residence in Vietnam of the overseas sentenced person proposed for transfer shall have the jurisdiction to decide on the reception of such person back to Vietnam. In case it is impossible to identify the last place of residence in Vietnam of the person proposed for transfer or the person proposed for transfer does not reside in Vietnam, the Regional People’s Court of Hanoi shall have the jurisdiction to decide on the reception of such person back to Vietnam.

  Article 15. Conditions for receiving persons serving their sentences in foreign countries back to Vietnam

  1. A person serving an overseas sentence may be repatriated to Vietnam for continued execution of his/her sentence when all of the following conditions are met:

  a) Being a Vietnamese citizen;

  b) The criminal acts for which he/she is sentenced in the transferring country also constitute a crime according to the criminal law of Vietnam at the time of making the request for transfer of the person serving the imprisonment sentence;

  c) At the time of receipt of the transfer request, the remaining sentence must be at least one year; in special cases, this period may be less than one year;

  d) The judgment or decision against such person has taken legal effect and no procedural proceedings are pending;

  dd) With the consent of Vietnam in case the competent authority of the transferring country makes a transfer request or with the consent of the transferring country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a transfer request;

  e) The consent of the sentenced person; where the sentenced person is a minor or incapable of expressing consent due to age, health conditions, or mental state, the consent must also be given by their lawful representative.

  2. The Government shall elaborate special cases specified in point c clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 16. Cases of refusal to receive persons serving their sentences in foreign countries back to Vietnam

  1. The competent People’s Court shall decide to refuse the reception of a person serving an overseas sentence back to Vietnam in one of the following cases:

  a) Not in compliance with the principles prescribed in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  b) Failing to satisfy one of the conditions specified in Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law;

  c) The interests of the State’s sovereignty, national defense and security, social order and safety, community health and benefits, lawful rights and interests of agencies, organizations and individuals in Vietnam may be harmed;

  d) Exceeding the capacity to meet necessary requirements for continued enforcement of judgments in Vietnam of the sentenced person.

  2. The Government shall elaborate Point d Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 17. Application for transfer of persons serving their sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam

  1. A dossier of request for transfer of a person serving an overseas sentence to Vietnam shall include the following documents:

  a) Written request for transfer of the Ministry of Public Security or competent authorities of the transferring country specified in Clause 1 Article 18 hereof;

  b) Documents specified in Clause 2 Article 18 hereof.

  2. A request for transfer of a person serving an overseas sentence to Vietnam shall be prepared in 3 sets, unless otherwise agreed. The language used for preparing the request shall comply with Article 8 of this Law.

  Article 18. Written requests for transfer of persons serving their sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam and enclosed documents

  1. A written request for transfer of a person serving an overseas sentence to Vietnam must contain the following contents:

  a) Date and place of making the document;

  b) Based on the request for transfer of the sentenced person;

  c/ Name and address of the competent authority of Vietnam and the transferring country;

  d) Information to identify identity, nationality, last place of residence in Vietnam (if any), and current place of serving sentence in the transferring country, based on legal grounds for determining eligibility for transfer;

  dd) Other necessary information (if any).

  2. The written request for transfer of the sentenced person shall be enclosed with the following documents:

  a) The transfer request or documents expressing the desire for transfer or consent to the transfer by the sentenced person or his/her lawful representative;

  b) Documents proving Vietnamese nationality of the convicts proposed for transfer in accordance with Vietnamese law;

  c) Copies of the court’s judgments or rulings of the transferring country for the person serving the imprisonment sentence proposed for transfer;

  d) Written document on additional penalties (if any);

  dd) The law of the transferring country shall apply to determine the constituent elements of the crime and the offence, the provisions on penalties, the statute of limitations for execution of penalties for such crime of the judgment;

  e) Written consent of the competent authority of the transferring country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a request for transfer or written consent to consider the transfer request of the Ministry of Public Security in case the competent authority of the transferring country makes a request for transfer;

  g) Documents describing identity traits, fingerprints, and facial photos of the sentenced person proposed for transfer;

  h) Documents and materials certifying the time period during which the sentenced person proposed for transfer has served his/her sentence, the general amnesty, special amnesty, exemption from or reduction of sentence in the transferring country and the remaining time period during which he/she must serve his/her sentence;

  i) Documents related to health conditions, mental status, medical records of the person serving the sentence proposed for transfer and recommendations (if any);

  k) International treaties on transfer of sentenced persons between Vietnam and the transferring country (if any);

  l) Other necessary documents (if any).

  3. The Government shall elaborate Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Receipt of transfer requests from persons serving imprisonment sentences abroad

  1. Vietnamese citizens who are serving their imprisonment sentences overseas or their lawful representatives may submit applications for transfer to Vietnam to continue serving their imprisonment sentences to one of the following agencies:

  a) Competent authorities of the transferring country;

  b) Vietnamese representative offices in the transferring countries;

  c) Ministry of Public Security.

  2. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the transfer request, the Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country shall forward the request to the Ministry of Public Security. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country must forward the request to the Ministry of Public Security within 5 days from the date of receipt of the transfer request. Based on the transfer request, the Ministry of Public Security shall consider and prepare a transfer request according to Article 20 of this Law.

  The transfer of applications for transfer to Vietnam from Vietnamese representative missions in the transferring countries to the Ministry of Public Security may be carried out online in accordance with law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 20. Request for transfer of persons serving their sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam

  1. The Ministry of Public Security or a competent authority of the transferring country shall request the transfer of the sentenced person from abroad to Vietnam.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer, and based on information and documents provided by the competent authority of the transferring country, the Ministry of Public Security shall consider issuing a decision to transfer the sentenced person abroad to Vietnam and send it directly or via diplomatic channels to the competent authority of the transferring country.

  In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or a Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country receives a request from the Ministry of Public Security for the transfer of a person serving an overseas sentence back to Vietnam, it shall immediately transfer the person to the competent authority of the transferring country and notify the Ministry of Public Security in writing.

  3. In case a competent authority of the transferring country makes a request for transfer of a person serving an overseas sentence to Vietnam, it shall be sent directly or via diplomatic channel to the Ministry of Public Security. In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or a Vietnamese representative office in the transferring country receives such a request, it shall immediately forward it to the Ministry of Public Security for handling in accordance with this Law.

  4. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 21. Receipt, examination and transfer of requests for transfer of persons serving their sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam

  1. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer of the sentenced person and accompanying documents from the competent authority of the transferring country or from the date of receipt of the documents specified in Clause 2 Article 18 of this Law from the competent authority of the transferring country, the Ministry of Public Security shall be responsible for examining the dossier in accordance with Articles 17 and 18 of this Law. In special cases specified at Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for dossier examination shall be 7 days.

  The Ministry of Public Security may request the competent authority of the transferring country to provide additional information and documents. After 60 days from the date of sending the written request for additional information and documents, if no additional information or documents are received, the Ministry of Public Security shall return the dossier to the competent authority of the transferring country, clearly stating the reason. In special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for providing additional information and documents is 20 days.

  2. In case the application for transfer meets the provisions of Article 17 and Article 18 of this Law, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately transfer 01 set of the application to the competent regional People’s Court for consideration and decision, and at the same time, immediately transfer a copy of the application to the competent regional People’s Procuracy.

  The transfer of documents, materials and dossiers may be carried out online in accordance with law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 22. Acceptance of requests for transfer of persons serving their sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam

  1. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete application for transfer of a person serving an overseas sentence to Vietnam, the competent regional People’s Court shall accept and notify in writing the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy at the same level. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for acceptance is 5 days.

  Within the time limit for consideration of requests for transfer of current imprisonment servers, if the request dossiers are incomplete in terms of information and documents or contain unclear points or contradictions, the competent regional People’s Courts shall propose the Ministry of Public Security to request the competent authorities of the transferring countries to supplement information and documents. The time limit for supplementation of information and documents is 20 days from the date of the Ministry of Public Security’s written request.

  2. Within 30 days from the date of acceptance, the competent regional People’s Court shall, on a case-by-case basis, issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision on consideration of request for transfer of sentenced person when all conditions specified in this Law are met. This decision must be sent to the person requested for transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any);

  b) Decide to terminate consideration of the request for transfer of the sentenced person as prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article and return the dossier to the Ministry of Public Security.

  If the case falls under the special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for issuing one of the decisions specified in this Clause shall be 10 days.

  Decisions under this Clause must be immediately sent to the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy at the same level.

  3. Regional People’s Courts shall decide to terminate consideration of requests for transfer of current imprisonment servers in one of the following cases:

  a) Not within their jurisdiction;

  b) The competent authority of the transferring country withdraws the transfer request;

  c) The convict or his/her lawful representative withdraws the application for transfer;

  d) The competent authority of the transferring country does not provide additional information and documents within the time limit specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  dd) Other reasons preventing the consideration of the transfer request.

  4. Within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision on consideration of the transfer request, the competent regional People’s Court shall hold a first-instance meeting to consider the transfer request. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for holding a meeting to consider the transfer request is 10 days.

  Article 23. First-instance court sessions for consideration of requests for transfer of persons serving their sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam

  1. The first-instance trial shall review the transfer request conducted by a single Judge, with the participation of a Procurator from the People’s Procuracy at the same level, and shall proceed as follows:

  a) The judge presents the contents of the request for transfer and states his/her opinions on the legal grounds of the transfer;

  b) Procurators express opinions of the People’s Procuracy on the transfer;

  c) The lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer presents opinions (if any).

  2. On the basis of the results of the first-instance trial, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision on admission of sentenced person and specifying crime, time limit for which admitted person must continue to serve sentence in Vietnam. In this case, Judge shall consider and decide conversion of imprisonment sentences (if any); conversion of imprisonment sentences shall comply with Article 25 hereof;

  b) Decision to refuse to receive persons serving imprisonment sentences, clearly stating reasons.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of issuance of the decision to receive or refuse to receive a person serving imprisonment sentence, the competent regional People’s Court shall send such decision to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the provincial People’s Procuracy, the Ministry of Public Security, the person proposed for transfer, and his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any). In special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for the competent regional People’s Court to send the decision to receive or refuse to receive is 5 days.

  Article 24. Appellate meetings for consideration of decisions on admission or refusal of admission

  1. The person proposed for transfer or his/her lawful representative may file an appeal within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision on acceptance or decision on refusal to accept of the competent regional People’s Court. The People’s Procuracy at the same level may file a protest within 15 days, and the provincial People’s Procuracy may file a protest within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision on acceptance or decision on refusal to accept of the competent regional People’s Court. In special cases specified in point c clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for filing an appeal or protest by the People’s Procuracy at the same level shall be 07 days, and the time limit for filing a protest by the provincial People’s Procuracy shall be 15 days.

  The appeal must be notified in writing by the competent regional People's Court to the People's Procuracy of the same level and persons related to the appeal within 05 days from the expiration date of the time limit for appeal.

  The People’s Procuracy shall send the decision to protest to the first-instance Court, the person proposed for transfer or his/her lawful representative within 02 days from the date of issuing the decision to protest.

  The competent regional People’s Court shall send the dossier and appeal to the competent provincial People’s Court within 07 days from the expiration of the time limit for appeal.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the application and appeal, the provincial People’s Court shall hold an appellate meeting to consider the decision on acceptance or rejection of the first-instance court’s decision appealed against. In special cases specified in point c clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for the provincial People’s Court to hold an appellate meeting is 7 days.

  3. The appellate trial shall be conducted by 1 Judge and attended by 1 procurator of the People’s Procuracy at the same level and shall follow the procedures below:

  a) The judge summarizes the contents of the application for transfer of a person serving an overseas sentence to Vietnam, the decision on admission or refusal of admission, and the contents of the appeal or protest;

  b) The Judge shall ask the lawful representative of the person subject to transfer about whether or not the person subject to transfer changes, supplements or withdraws his/her appeal; if so, the Judge shall request the procurator to present his/her opinions on such change, supplement or withdrawal of the appeal.

  The Judge shall ask the Procurator whether to change, supplement or withdraw the protest; if so, the Judge shall request the lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer to present opinions on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the protest;

  c) Procurators, lawyers and lawful representatives of persons proposed for transfer shall express opinions on contents of appeals; procurators shall express viewpoints of the People’s Procuracy on the resolution of appeals.

  4. On the basis of the results of the appellate meeting, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Reject the appeal and uphold the first-instance decision;

  b) To revise the first-instance decisions;

  c) Repeal the first-instance decision and transfer the case to a competent regional People’s Court for re-consideration;

  d) Repeal the first-instance decision and terminate the consideration of the transfer request in accordance with Clause 3 Article 22 of this Law;

  dd) Terminate the appellate review.

  5. Legally effective decisions on reception or refusal of reception include:

  a) The decision of the first-instance Court, which is not appealed against, shall take effect on the expiration of the time limit for appeal or protest;

  b) The decision of the appellate Court shall take effect from the date of issuance and is final.

  The decision of the appellate Court must be immediately sent to the first-instance Court, the first-instance Procuracy, the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the person requested for transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any).

  Article 25. Conversion of imprisonment sentences

  1. Where a prison sentence in a judgment or decision pronounced by the transferring country’s court against a person serving such sentence is inconsistent with the provisions of the Criminal Code and other provisions of Vietnamese law, it must be converted accordingly.

  2. The conversion of a prison sentence must be based on the circumstances of the case stated in the judgment or decision issued by the court of the transferring country.

  3. The converted imprisonment penalty shall not be more severe than the penalty in terms of nature and duration, which was imposed by the court of the transferring country. The period served in the transferring country shall be deducted from the period served in Vietnam.

  4. Persons serving imprisonment sentences who are received back to Vietnam shall not be subject to a second conviction for crimes as stated in court judgments or rulings of the transferring country.

  5. The Minister of Public Security shall take charge and cooperate with the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court and the Procurator General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy in elaborating this Article.

  Article 26. Execution of decisions on receipt of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. Within 10 days from the effective date of the decision on admission of the sentenced person issued by the People’s Court, the Chief Judge of the regional People’s Court that issued the first-instance decision shall issue a decision to execute the decision on admission. In special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for issuing the decision to execute the decision on admission is 5 days. The decision to execute the decision on admission takes effect from the date of its signing.

  2. The decision on execution of the decision on reception must be sent to the People's Procuracy at the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the transferring country and the transferee or their lawful representative.

  3. The Ministry of Public Security shall notify in writing the competent authorities of the transferring country and organize the reception. The reception of persons serving imprisonment sentences must be carried out within 45 days from the effective date of the decision on execution of the reception decision, unless otherwise agreed upon. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for reception of persons serving imprisonment sentences is 15 days.

  4. The Government shall elaborate clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 27. Cancellation of decisions on receipt of persons serving imprisonment sentences and decisions on execution of decisions on receipt of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. A decision to receive a person serving an imprisonment sentence that has not yet taken legal effect shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) The convict or his/her lawful representative withdraws the application for transfer or expresses his/her desire not to be transferred in writing;

  b) The transferring country withdraws its request for transfer;

  c) The convict dies or absconds from the transferring country;

  d) Other cases where it is impossible to receive the sentenced person.

  2. A decision to receive a person serving a prison sentence, which has taken legal effect, shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) Cases specified in Points b, c and d Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Decision on execution of decision on receipt of sentenced person is annulled.

  3. The decision on execution of the decision on receipt of a person serving an imprisonment sentence shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) Cases specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The person handed over by the competent authority of the transferring country is not included in the decision on reception;

  c) The competent authority of the transferring country fails to hand over the transferee at the agreed time and place without a legitimate reason or the receipt is not carried out within the time limit specified in Clause 3 Article 26 of this Law.

  4. Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Ministry of Public Security, the Chief Justice of the competent People’s Court shall cancel the decision on admission of the sentenced person as prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article; cancel the decision on execution of the decision on admission of the sentenced person as prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article. These decisions shall take effect from the date of their signing and must be immediately sent to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the transferring country, the sentenced person or his/her lawful representative.

  5. The transfer of sentenced person in the cases specified in point a and point b clause 1, point c clause 3 of this Article shall be considered at least 03 years after the decision to cancel the decision on admission of sentenced person, the decision on execution of decision on admission of sentenced person takes legal effect.

  Article 28. Reception of transferred persons

  1. The Ministry of Public Security shall arrange the reception of the transferred person at the time and place agreed in writing with the competent authority of the transferring country.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 29. Continued serving of prison sentences in Vietnam

  1. The continued serving of the sentence in Vietnam by the transferred person shall comply with Vietnamese law.

  2. Upon receiving a notification of the decision on special amnesty, general amnesty or exemption from or reduction of penalties, parole or other judicial decisions of the transferring country for a person currently serving imprisonment sentence who is received back to Vietnam, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately send such notification to the competent authority for consideration and decision in accordance with relevant laws.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 30. Notices of compliance with sentences of persons serving imprisonment sentences who are received back to Vietnam

  The Ministry of Public Security shall immediately notify in writing the competent authorities of the transferring country of the execution of the sentenced person who is received back to Vietnam in one of the following cases:

  1. Persons serving imprisonment sentences shall be granted suspension of sentence enforcement, exemption from or reduction of the length of time of serving their imprisonment sentences, amnesty, special amnesty, parole or conditional early release, or criminal prosecution for another crime;

  2. Persons who are serving imprisonment sentences and have completely served their sentences;

  3. The convicts who are on their prison sentences escape from the detention centers;

  4. The convict dies before completing his/her prison sentence;

  5. The competent authority of the transferring country shall request information on the compliance with the judgment by the person received into Vietnam.

  Chapter III

     TRANSFER OF PERSONS SERVING IMPRISONMENT SENTENCES IN VIETNAM TO FOREIGN COUNTRIES

  Article 31. Authority to decide on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  The regional People’s Court where the foreign national is serving his/her imprisonment sentence has the jurisdiction to decide the transfer of the person serving his/her imprisonment sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country.

  Article 32. Conditions for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. A person currently serving a sentence in Vietnam may be transferred to a foreign country for continued service of his/her sentence when all of the following conditions are met:

  a) Being a citizen of the host country or a person permitted to reside indefinitely in the host country or to be received by the host country;

  b) The criminal acts for which he/she is sentenced in Vietnam also constitute a crime under the law of the receiving country at the time of making the request for transfer of the person serving the imprisonment sentence;

  c) At the time of receipt of the transfer request, the remaining sentence must be at least one year; in special cases, this period may be less than one year;

  d) Satisfying all conditions specified in Points d and e Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law;

  dd) Have fulfilled civil liabilities in the additional judgments and penalties being fines, property confiscation and other legal responsibilities as prescribed by law or have been supported by other countries, agencies, organizations and individuals to perform these legal responsibilities or have been received by other countries to guarantee that the persons serving imprisonment sentences will perform these legal responsibilities after being transferred back to the receiving countries;

  e) With the consent of Vietnam in case the competent authority of the receiving country makes a request for transfer or with the consent of the receiving country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a request for transfer.

  2. The Government shall elaborate special cases specified in point c clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 33. Cases of refusal to transfer persons currently serving imprisonment sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  The competent Vietnamese People’s Court shall decide to refuse the transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country if one of the following cases occurs:

  1. Failing to comply with the principles prescribed in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  2. Failing to satisfy one of the conditions specified in Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law;

  3. The purposes are detrimental to national sovereignty, defense, security, social order and safety, community health, and the interests of the State, legitimate rights and benefits of agencies, organizations, and individuals in Vietnam;

  4. There are grounds to believe that the person transferred is at risk of torture, revenge, coercion, ill-treatment, discrimination, cruel and inhumane punishment or humiliation in the host country.

  Article 34. Dossiers of request for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. A dossier of request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country shall include the following documents:

  a) Written request for transfer of the Ministry of Public Security or competent authorities of the receiving country specified in Clause 1 Article 35 hereof;

  b) Documents specified in Clause 2 Article 35 hereof.

  2. A request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country shall be made in 3 sets, unless otherwise agreed. The language used for making the request shall comply with Article 8 of this Law.

  Article 35. Written requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries and enclosed documents

  1. A written request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country must contain the following contents:

  a) Date and place of making the document;

  b) Based on the request for transfer of the sentenced person;

  c/ Name and address of the competent authority of Vietnam and the receiving country;

  d) Information to identify personal identification, nationality, place of permanent residence in the receiving country (if any) and place where the convict is currently serving his/her sentence in Vietnam, legal grounds for the convict to be eligible for transfer;

  dd) Other necessary information (if any).

  2. The written request for transfer of the sentenced person shall be enclosed with the following documents:

  a) The transfer request or documents expressing the desire for transfer or consent to the transfer by the sentenced person or his/her lawful representative;

  b/ Documents evidencing the nationality of the receiving country or the person is permitted to reside indefinitely in the receiving country (if any);

  c) Copies of judgments or decisions of Vietnamese People’s Courts against persons serving imprisonment sentences proposed for transfer;

  d) Written document on additional penalties (if any);

  dd) The laws of the country to which the application is made to determine the constituent elements of the crime and the offence, the provisions on penalties, the statute of limitations for enforcement of penalties for such crime of the judgment;

  e) Written consent of competent authorities of the receiving country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a request for transfer or written consent to consider the transfer request of the Ministry of Public Security in case competent authorities of the receiving country make a request for transfer;

  g) Documents specified in points g, h, i, k and l clause 2 Article 18 of this Law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 36. Receipt of transfer requests from persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam

  1. Foreigners who are serving their imprisonment sentences in Vietnam or their lawful representatives may file applications for transfer to foreign countries for further serving their imprisonment sentences to one of the following agencies:

  a) Ministry of Public Security;

  b) Competent authorities of the host country;

  c/ The representative mission of the host country in Vietnam.

  2. Upon receiving a written request for transfer or documents expressing the desire to be transferred by the sentenced person or his/her lawful representative, the Ministry of Public Security shall receive and consider making a transfer request in accordance with Article 37 of this Law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 37. Making requests for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. Request for transfer of persons currently serving imprisonment sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries shall be made by the Ministry of Public Security or competent authorities of the receiving country.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer, the Ministry of Public Security shall review and decide to issue a request for the transfer of the sentenced person to the foreign country and send it directly or via diplomatic channels to the competent authority of the receiving country.

  In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or a Vietnamese representative mission in the receiving country receives a request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country from the Ministry of Public Security, it shall immediately transfer the person to the competent authority of the receiving country and notify the Ministry of Public Security in writing.

  3. In case competent authorities of the receiving country request transfer of a person serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country, such request shall be sent directly or via diplomatic channels to the Ministry of Public Security. If the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or Vietnamese representative mission in the receiving country receives the request from the competent authority of the receiving country, it shall immediately forward it to the Ministry of Public Security for handling in accordance with this Law.

  4. In case a foreigner is serving time at a detention facility of the Ministry of National Defense, he/she shall be transferred to a detention facility of the Ministry of Public Security for serving his/her sentence before carrying out procedures for transfer of the sentenced person.

  5. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 38. Receipt, examination and transfer of requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer of the sentenced person and accompanying documents from the competent authority of the receiving country or from the date of receipt of the documents specified in Clause 2 Article 35 of this Law from the competent authority of the receiving country, the Ministry of Public Security shall be responsible for examining the dossier in accordance with Articles 34 and 35 of this Law. In special cases specified at Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for dossier examination shall be 7 days.

  The Ministry of Public Security may request competent authorities of the receiving country to supplement information and documents. After 60 days from the date of sending a written request for supplementation of information and documents, if no supplementary information or documents are received, the Ministry of Public Security shall return the dossier to the competent authority of the receiving country, clearly stating the reason. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for supplementation of information and documents is 20 days.

  2. In case the application for transfer meets the provisions of Article 34 and Article 35 of this Law, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately transfer 01 set of the application to the competent regional People’s Court for consideration and decision, and at the same time, immediately transfer a copy of the application to the competent regional People’s Procuracy.

  The transfer of documents, materials and dossiers may be carried out online in accordance with law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 39. Acceptance of requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete application for transfer of a person serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country, the competent regional People’s Court shall accept and notify in writing the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy at the same level. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for accepting the application is 5 days.

  Within the time limit for consideration of requests for transfer of current imprisonment servers, if the request dossiers are incomplete in terms of information and documents or contain unclear points or contradictions, the competent regional People’s Courts shall request the Ministry of Public Security to request the competent authorities of the receiving country to supplement information and documents. The time limit for supplementation of information and documents is 20 days from the date on which the Ministry of Public Security issues a written request.

  2. Within 30 days from the date of acceptance, the competent regional People’s Court shall, on a case-by-case basis, issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision on consideration of request for transfer of sentenced person when all conditions specified in this Law are met. This decision must be sent to the person requested for transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any);

  b) Decide to terminate consideration of the request for transfer of the sentenced person as prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article and return the dossier to the Ministry of Public Security.

  If the case falls under the special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for issuing one of the decisions specified in this Clause shall be 10 days.

  Decisions under this Clause must be immediately sent to the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy at the same level.

  3. Regional People’s Courts shall decide to terminate consideration of requests for transfer of current imprisonment servers in one of the following cases:

  a) Not within their jurisdiction;

  b) The competent authority of the receiving country revokes the transfer request;

  c) The convict or his/her lawful representative withdraws the application for transfer;

  d) The competent authority of the receiving country does not provide additional information or documents within the time limit specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  dd) Other reasons preventing the consideration of the transfer request.

  4. Within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision on consideration of the transfer request, the competent regional People’s Court shall hold a first-instance meeting to consider the transfer request. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for holding a meeting to consider the transfer request is 10 days.

  Article 40. First-instance court sessions for consideration of requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. The first-instance trial shall review the transfer request conducted by a single Judge, with the participation of a Procurator from the People’s Procuracy at the same level, and shall proceed as follows:

  a) The judge presents the contents of the request for transfer and states his/her opinions on the legal grounds of the transfer;

  b) Procurators express opinions of the People’s Procuracy on the transfer;

  c) The person proposed for transfer presents his/her opinions (if any);

  d) The lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer presents opinions (if any).

  2. On the basis of the results of the first-instance trial, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision on transfer of sentenced person;

  b) Decision on refusal of transfer of sentenced person and reasons therefor.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of issuance of the decision to transfer or decision to refuse transfer of the sentenced person, the competent regional People’s Court shall send the decision to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the provincial People’s Procuracy, the Ministry of Public Security, the person proposed for transfer, and his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any). In special cases specified in point c clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for the competent regional People’s Court to send the decision to transfer or decision to refuse transfer is 5 days.

  Article 41. Appellate meetings for consideration of decisions on transfer or decisions on refusal to transfer

  1. The person proposed for transfer or his/her lawful representative may appeal within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision on transfer or decision on refusal of transfer issued by the competent regional People’s Court. The People’s Procuracy at the same level may appeal within 15 days and the provincial People’s Procuracy may appeal within 30 days from the date on which the competent regional People’s Court issues the decision on transfer or decision on refusal of transfer. In special cases specified in point c clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for appeal shall be 07 days and the time limit for appeal by the People’s Procuracy at the same level shall be 15 days.

  The appeal must be notified in writing by the competent regional People's Court to the People's Procuracy of the same level and persons related to the appeal within 05 days from the expiration date of the time limit for appeal.

  The People’s Procuracy shall send the decision to protest to the first-instance Court, the person proposed for transfer or his/her lawful representative within 02 days from the date of issuing the decision to protest.

  The competent regional People’s Court shall send the dossier and appeal to the competent provincial People’s Court within 07 days from the expiration of the time limit for appeal.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the application and appeal, the provincial People’s Court shall hold an appellate meeting to review the transfer decision or decision on refusal of transfer by the first-instance court that is appealed against. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for the provincial People’s Court to hold an appellate meeting is 7 days.

  3. The appellate trial shall be conducted by 1 Judge and attended by 1 procurator of the People’s Procuracy at the same level and shall follow the procedures below:

  a) The judge summarizes the contents of the request for transfer of the person serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country, the decision on transfer or the decision on refusal of transfer, and the contents of the appeal or protest;

  b) The Judge shall ask the person proposed for transfer or the lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer whether he/she changes, supplements or withdraws the appeal; if so, the Judge shall request the Procurator to present his/her opinions on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the appeal.

  The Judge shall ask the Procurator whether to change, supplement or withdraw the protest; if so, the Judge shall request the person proposed for transfer, lawyer and lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer to present their opinions on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the protest;

  c) Procurators, persons proposed for transfer, lawyers and lawful representatives of persons proposed for transfer shall express opinions on contents of appeals; procurators shall express viewpoints of the People’s Procuracy on the resolution of appeals.

  4. On the basis of the results of the appellate meeting, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Reject the appeal and uphold the first-instance decision;

  b) To revise the first-instance decisions;

  c) Repeal the first-instance decision and transfer the case to a competent regional People’s Court for re-consideration;

  d) Repeal the first-instance decision and terminate the consideration of the transfer request in accordance with Clause 3 Article 39 of this Law;

  dd) Terminate the appellate review.

  5. Legally effective decisions on transfer or decisions on refusal of transfer include:

  a) The decision of the first-instance Court, which is not appealed against, shall take effect on the expiration of the time limit for appeal or protest;

  b) The decision of the appellate Court shall take effect from the date of issuance and is final.

  The decision of the appellate Court must be immediately sent to the first-instance Court, the first-instance Procuracy, the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the person requested for transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any).

  Article 42. Execution of decisions on transfer of current imprisonment servers

  1. Within 10 days from the effective date of the decision to transfer the sentenced person of the People’s Court, the Chief Judge of the regional People’s Court that issued the first-instance decision shall issue a decision to execute the transfer decision. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for issuing the decision to execute the transfer decision is 5 days. The decision to execute the transfer decision takes effect from the date of its signing.

  2. The decision on execution of the transfer decision must be sent to the People's Procuracy at the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the receiving country and the transferee or their lawful representative.

  3. The Ministry of Public Security shall notify in writing the competent authorities of the receiving country and organize the transfer. The transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence must be carried out within 45 days from the date on which the decision to execute the transfer decision takes effect, unless otherwise agreed upon. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for transferring a person serving an imprisonment sentence is 15 days.

  4. The Government shall elaborate clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 43. Annulment of decisions on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences and decisions on execution of decisions on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. A decision to transfer a person serving an imprisonment sentence who has not yet taken legal effect shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) The convict or his/her lawful representative withdraws the application for transfer or expresses his/her desire not to be transferred in writing;

  b) The receiving country withdraws its request for transfer;

  c) The convicts who are currently serving their sentences die or escape from Vietnam;

  d) Other cases where the transfer of the sentenced person cannot be carried out.

  2. A decision to transfer a person serving a prison sentence, which has taken legal effect, shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) Cases specified in Points b, c and d Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Decision on execution of decision on transfer of sentenced person is annulled.

  3. The decision on execution of the transfer decision of the sentenced person shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) Cases specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The transferee is not a person included in the decision to transfer the sentenced person;

  c) The competent authority of the receiving country does not receive the transferred person at the agreed time and place without a legitimate reason or the transfer is not carried out within the time limit specified in Clause 3 Article 42 of this Law.

  4. Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Ministry of Public Security, the Chief Justice of the competent People’s Court shall cancel the decision on transfer of the sentenced person specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article; cancel the decision on execution of the decision on transfer of the sentenced person specified in Clause 3 of this Article. These decisions shall take effect from the date of their signing and must be immediately sent to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the receiving country, the sentenced person or his/her lawful representative.

  5. The transfer of sentenced person in the cases specified in point a and point b clause 1, point c clause 3 of this Article shall be considered at least 03 years after the decision to cancel the decision on transfer of sentenced person, the decision on execution of decision on transfer of sentenced person takes legal effect.

  Article 44. Handover of transferred persons

  1. The Ministry of Public Security shall organize the handover of the transferred person at the time and place agreed in writing with the competent authority of the receiving country.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 45. Re-consideration of judgments against transferred persons

  The judgments already pronounced against the convicts in Vietnam and transferred to foreign countries shall be reviewed by Vietnamese Peoples Courts according to cassation or reopening procedures according to the law on criminal procedure.

  Article 46. Exchange of information on execution of judgments by transferred persons

  1. After the convicts are transferred to their home countries, the Ministry of Public Security shall request competent authorities of the receiving countries to provide information on the compliance with the sentences of the transferred convicts at the request of state management.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall immediately notify in writing the competent authorities of the receiving country when:

  a) Persons currently serving imprisonment sentences who are granted exemption from or reduction of their sentence duration, amnesty, special amnesty, parole or conditional early release, or criminal prosecution for another crime;

  b) Vietnamese People’s Courts review judgments already passed against transferred persons according to cassation or reopening procedures and legal consequences of such procedures.

  Chapter IV

                                             IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 47. Effect

  1. This Law comes into force as of July 01, 2026.

  2. The Law on Mutual Legal Assistance No. 08/2007/QH12, as amended by Law No. 81/2025/QH15, ceases to be effective from the effective date of this Law, except for transitional provisions specified in Article 48 of this Law, Article 45 of the Law on Extradition, Clause 1 Article 42 of the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters and Clause 1 Article 38 of the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance in Civil Matters.

  Article 48. Transition clauses

  Requests for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences received by the Ministry of Public Security before the effective date of this Law shall continue to be considered and resolved in accordance with the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance No. 08/2007/QH12, as amended by Law No. 81/2025/QH15.
