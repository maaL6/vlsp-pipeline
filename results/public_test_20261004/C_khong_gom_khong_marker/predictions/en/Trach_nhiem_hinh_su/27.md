                      AMENDMENTS TO CRIMINAL PROCEDURE CODE

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, as amended by Resolution No. 203/2025/QH15;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on amendments to the Criminal Procedure Code No. 101/2015/QH13, as amended by Law No. 02/2021/QH15, Law No. 34/2024/QH15 and Law No. 59/2024/QH15.

  Article 20. Responsibility to exercise prosecution rights and supervise legal compliance in criminal proceedings The Procuracy shall exercise prosecution rights and supervise legal compliance in criminal proceedings, decide on charges, detect violations of law to ensure that all criminal acts, offenders, juridical persons committing crimes, and violations of law must be detected and handled promptly and strictly; that charges, investigations, prosecutions, adjudications, and judgments are imposed against the right persons for the right crimes in accordance with the law; that no criminals or offenders, juridical persons committing crimes escape detection; and that no innocent people are wrongfully convicted.

  The Prosecutor General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall provide for jurisdiction of People’s Procuracies at all levels over exercise of the right of prosecution and supervision of compliance with laws on criminal proceedings according to this Code.”.

  Article 268. Jurisdiction of Courts

  1. Regional People’s Courts shall conduct first-instance trials over criminal lawsuits involving less serious crimes, serious crimes, very serious crimes and extremely serious crimes for which the maximum penalty is up to 20 years' imprisonment as prescribed in the Criminal Code.

  2. Provincial-level People’s Courts shall conduct first-instance trials over lawsuits:

  a) Criminal cases falling beyond the jurisdiction of regional People’s Courts;

  b) Criminal cases within the jurisdiction of regional People’s Courts with many complicated details, related to multiple levels and sectors, and attracting special public attention; cases where the settlement affects politics or foreign affairs; cases where offenders are key leaders at provincial level or higher, religious dignitaries, or individuals of high prestige in ethnic minority groups.

  The Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court shall take charge and cooperate with the Procurator General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy in elaborating this point.

  3. Regional Military Courts shall conduct first-instance trials over criminal lawsuits involving less serious crimes, serious crimes and very serious crimes, except for crimes and lawsuits:

  a) Offences against the security of the State;

  b/ Crimes of undermining peace, opposing humanity and war crimes;

  c) The crimes prescribed in Articles 123, 125, 126, 227, 277, 278, 279, 280, 282, 283, 284, 286, 287, 288, 337, 368, 369, 370, 371, 399 and 400 of the Criminal Code;

  d) Committing crimes outside the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam;

  dd) The criminal case involves defendants, crime victims or litigants in foreign countries or property related to the case in foreign countries.

  4. The Military Courts of military zones shall conduct first-instance trials over the following lawsuits:

  a) Criminal cases falling beyond the jurisdiction of regional military courts;

  b) Criminal cases within the jurisdiction of regional military courts involving complex facts difficult to assess and determine, or cases related to multiple levels and sectors; cases where defendants are judges, prosecutors, investigators, key leading officials at provincial level, regiment heads and equivalent or higher, individuals holding religious dignities or having high prestige among ethnic minorities.”.

  41. Amendments to Article 382:

  Article 382. Jurisdiction over cassation review

  1. The Council of Judges of the Supreme People’s Court conducts cassation review through a trial panel composed of five judges for legally effective judgments/decisions of the Appellate Court of the Supreme People’s Court, provincial People’s Courts and Central Military Court which are appealed against.

  2. The General Assembly of Judges of the Supreme People’s Court conducts cassation review of judgments or decisions which have taken legal effect as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article but are complicated or judgments or decisions which have been reviewed by the Council of Judges of the Supreme People’s Court through a trial panel composed of five judges but they fail to reach agreement when voting on the decision to resolve the case.

  When the General Council of Judges of the Supreme People’s Court conducts cassation trials, at least two-thirds of its members must attend the trial, which is chaired by the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court. The decision of the General Council of Judges of the Supreme People’s Court must be approved by more than half of its members; otherwise, the trial shall be adjourned. Within 30 days from the date of the decision to adjourn the trial, the General Council of Judges must resume the trial.

  3. The Committee of Judges of the Central Military Court shall conduct cassation review of legally effective judgments/decisions of military courts of military zones or regional military courts which are appealed against.
