                                             JOINT CIRCULAR

                               GUIDANCE ON EXECUTION OF PENALTIES FOR DELAYED VIOLATION

  Pursuant to the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments dated June 17, 2010;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 77/2009/ND-CP of September 15, 2009, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 15/2008/ND-CP of February 4, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 104/2008/ND-CP of September 16, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Defense;

  In order to uniformly implement provisions of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments regarding execution of deportation sentences, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall jointly provide guidance as follows:

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidelines on procedures for enforcing deportation sentences; handling cases where persons subject to deportation sentences abscond, fall ill, or die during the period pending their exit; handling of receipt of corpses, remains, and ashes of persons subject to deportation sentences who die; funding for enforcement of deportation sentences.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to persons subject to expulsion under court judgments or decisions (hereinafter referred to as “expelled persons”) and agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the enforcement of expulsion sentences.

  Article 3. Principles of enforcement of the expulsion sentence

  1. Ensure political, legal, foreign affairs and humanitarian policy requirements of the State.

  2. The enforcement of the deportation penalty must ensure safety, the right persons, the right time, procedures and jurisdiction in accordance with Vietnamese law and international law and practices.

  3. Ensure the lawful rights and interests of persons subject to expulsion; strictly prohibit all acts infringing upon the life, health, dignity, and lawful property of persons subject to expulsion.

  Article 4. Agencies enforcing deportation sentences

  1. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the People’s Court or Military Court issues the decision on execution of the expulsion penalty and where the headquarter of the criminal enforcement agency is located shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in organizing the execution of the expulsion penalty according to the judgment execution decision of the Court.

  2. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the prison affiliated to the Ministry of Public Security or the Ministry of National Defense or military zone is located shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in organizing the execution of the deportation sentence for persons who have completely served their imprisonment sentences and had the additional penalty of deportation.

  Article 5. Funds for judgment enforcement

  The funding for enforcement of the expulsion sentence shall be covered by the state budget in the annual budget estimates of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority and the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security. The cost of purchasing tickets for exit vehicles to take the expelled person back to the country shall comply with Article 102 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  Chapter II

                          PROCEDURES FOR EXECUTION OF PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON EXPORT

  Article 6. Notice of enforcement of the decision on expulsion

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the decision on execution of the expulsion sentence from the Court in the case specified in Clause 1 Article 96 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments or from the date of receipt of the notification from the prison superintendent of the time for which the convict has completed serving the additional penalty of expulsion, the execution agency of the province-level Public Security must notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, diplomatic missions, consular offices and other agencies authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen and headquartered in Vietnam or the representative office of the international organization for which he/she works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed his/her entry into Vietnam.

  2. In case inmates subject to the additional penalty of expulsion are granted amnesty or exempt from serving their imprisonment sentences, within 24 (twenty-four) hours after receiving decisions on amnesty or exemption from serving imprisonment sentences, superintendents of prisons shall notify in writing the criminal judgment execution agencies of provincial-level Police Departments of localities where such prisons are based so that they can immediately notify such to the agencies specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 7. Dossiers on enforcement of the sanction of expulsion

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the judgment enforcement decision from the court or from the date of receiving the person who has completely served their prison sentence, the person who is granted amnesty or exemption from serving the sentence with deportation as an additional penalty, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police department shall prepare a dossier for enforcing the deportation penalty.

  2. The dossier for enforcement of an expulsion sentence (hereinafter referred to as judgment enforcement dossier) must include all documents and materials specified in Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement. A certificate of completion of other penalties or other obligations specified in Point c Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement includes: a certificate of completion of imprisonment penalty, amnesty, exemption from serving the imprisonment penalty, exemption from or reduction of fines, decision on termination or suspension of judgment enforcement, or written certification or confirmation from the competent judgment enforcement agency regarding the judgment enforcement results or the performance of other obligations.

  3. When handing over a person who has completely served his/her prison sentence, or a person who is granted special amnesty or exemption from serving an additional penalty of expulsion to the execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department of the locality where the prison is located, the superintendent of the prison shall hand over together with a copy of the judgment/decision on execution of the prison sentence, certificate of completion of the prison sentence, certificate of special amnesty or exemption from serving the prison sentence and papers evidencing the performance of other obligations (if any) for compilation of a dossier for execution of the expulsion penalty.

  Article 8. Designation of accommodation

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of preparation of the judgment enforcement dossier, the head of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police department where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall issue a decision to designate accommodation for the expelled person pending his/her exit. The designated accommodation must have a clear address and facilitate management and supervision. It is prohibited to use the criminal custody room or detention cell of the detention center or prison to manage the expelled person pending his/her exit.

  2. In case the person subject to expulsion falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2, Article 99 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security authority where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall issue a decision and organize the transfer of the expelled person to the accommodation establishment of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as “accommodation establishment”).

  3. In case the person subject to expulsion or his/her relatives or legal representative request permission for temporary residence in a lawful place of stay in another province or city, they must submit an application to the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared for consideration and decision. The application must clearly state the full name and address of the applicant, the relationship with the person subject to expulsion, the address of the place of temporary residence, and the commitment to comply with all requirements of the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority, to abide by the law and regulations of the local authority of the place of temporary residence. The application must be translated into Vietnamese and certified by the diplomatic mission, consular office, or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen, or by the representative office of the international organization where such person works, based in Vietnam, and by the People’s Committee of the commune or agency or organization where the expelled person will reside.

  4. Within 2 working days after issuing a decision to designate accommodation, the execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department where the judgment execution dossier is made shall notify such decision to the court that has issued the judgment execution decision, the procuracy of the same level and agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular.

  Article 9. Management of persons subject to expulsion

  1. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies and local authorities of the administrative division where the expelled person resides in managing the expelled person.

  2. Within 3 working days from the date of issuance of a decision to place the person in a accommodation establishment or a decision to designate a place of accommodation in another province or city, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall send a notice enclosed with a copy of the judgment or decision on judgment execution to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment is located or the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the expelled person will stay for cooperation in management and initiation of procedures for handling the case when the expelled person absconds or dies as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 99 and Article 100 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  Article 10. Settlement of cases where persons subject to expulsion abscond

  1. During their stay pending exit, if the expelled person absconds, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment is located or the province where the place of accommodation is located shall immediately organize the pursuit and notify such to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared; in case of no result of pursuit, within 7 days from the date the expelled person absconds, a pursuit warrant must be issued under law.

  2. In case of recapture of a person who has been extradited or surrendered, the agency that captures or receives him/her shall make a record and immediately notify it to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is filed for receiving and taking him/her to a detention center and notify it to the agency that has issued the pursuit warrant for issuing a decision on termination of pursuit as prescribed.

  3. Where the expelled person absconds on escort to the accommodation establishment or the exit point, the criminal enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security authority organizing the escort shall take charge of pursuit, issue a pursuit warrant, and terminate the pursuit.

  4. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall immediately notify the same-level Procuracy, the Court that issued the decision on deportation, the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular of the deportation victim’s escape, his/her arrest or surrender.

  Article 11. Settlement of cases where persons subject to expulsion are sick or deceased

  1. During their stay at accommodation establishments pending exit procedures, persons subject to expulsion may receive medical examination and treatment in accordance with regulations of the Government for persons serving expulsion sentences and foreigners who have completely served prison sentences and are staying at accommodation establishments pending exit procedures. Where persons subject to expulsion suffer from serious illnesses and must be treated at state medical facilities, accommodation establishments or places of stay designated shall promptly notify such to criminal judgment enforcement agencies of provincial police authorities where the accommodation establishments or places of stay are located so that these agencies may notify prescribed agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.

  2. In case the expelled person is undergoing emergency treatment or suffers from a serious illness and cannot travel, and there is confirmation from a hospital of the province or higher level, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the expelled person is staying shall designate, and the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment is located shall notify the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared to issue a written notice to the court that has issued the judgment execution decision for consideration and issuance of a decision to extend the period of compulsory departure from Vietnam.

  3. During the stay period, if the expelled person dies, the designated accommodation establishment or place must immediately notify such to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department of the locality where the establishment or place is located, the investigation agency and the competent Procuracy for determination of the cause of death. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours after the expelled person dies, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department of the locality where the expelled person dies shall notify such to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department of the locality which has compiled the judgment execution dossier for immediate notification in writing or by telegraph to the court that has issued the judgment execution decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular. After completing the procedures for determining the cause of death and obtaining permission from the competent agency, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department of the locality where the establishment or place is located shall organize the burial.

  Where the expelled person dies on the way to the detention center or the place of exit, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the escorting is organized shall take charge and cooperate with the criminal enforcement agency of the province, investigation agency and competent Procuracy of the place where the expelled person dies in carrying out procedures as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 99 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  4. Expenses for burial of a repatriated person who dies:

  a) Expenses for burial of persons who die in expulsion centers shall comply with regulations of the Government on regimes of meals, accommodation, clothing, daily-life activities, medical examination and treatment, and burial expenses for persons serving expulsion penalty during their stay at the accommodation establishments;

  b) The burial cost for a person who dies during their stay at another designated accommodation establishment shall be applied similarly to the burial cost for a person who dies at the accommodation establishment, unless his/her relatives or legal representative pays it.

  Article 12. Handling of receipt of corpses, remains and ashes of persons subject to expulsion

  1. In case the expelled person dies and his/her relatives or lawful representative request to receive his/her corpse for burial, within 48 (forty eight) hours after such person dies, there must be a written request sent to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department where the judgment execution dossier is made for consideration and decision.

  2. The application must clearly state the full name and address of the person receiving the deceased, their relationship with the expelled person, the burial place, and a commitment to ensure security, order, and environmental hygiene, and the applicant shall bear all costs. The application must be certified by the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen, or by the representative office of the international organization where such person works, and must be translated into Vietnamese. If the applicant is a Vietnamese citizen, certification by the People’s Committee of the commune where he/she resides is required. In cases where the deceased is received for burial in the territory of Vietnam, the consent of the People’s Committee of the commune where the burial takes place is required.

  3. Within 24 (twenty four) hours after receiving the application, the head of the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office shall decide to permit the receipt of the corpse and notify in writing the applicant, the court that has issued the judgment execution decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the permission or non-permission to receive the corpse when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of the corpse affects security, order and environmental sanitation.

  4. Within 24 (twenty four) hours after notifying the receipt of the corpse, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office and the requester shall hand over and receive the corpse, money and lawful property of the deported person (if any) and make a written record bearing signatures of the handover and receipt parties and witnesses; past this time limit, if the requester fails to come to receive the corpse, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office shall organize the burial.

  5. The receipt of human remains of an expelled person shall be granted only after 03 years from the date of burial. In case of cremation, the receipt of ashes may be granted after the completion of the cremation. The requester for receipt of human remains or ashes must submit a written request to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the burial takes place for consideration and decision. The written request must contain all the contents specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Within 7 working days after receiving a request, the head of the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level police office shall decide to accept or refuse to accept the remains and notify in writing the requester, the judgment execution court and agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the acceptance or refusal of remains when there are grounds to believe that the acceptance of remains affects security, order and environmental sanitation.

  6. The person who makes the request or the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which he/she is a citizen, or the representative office of the international organization where he/she works shall be responsible for preserving and transporting the body, remains and ashes of the expelled person in accordance with the law on medical treatment; environmental hygiene; prevention and control of infectious diseases and border quarantine and other regulations of Vietnamese law.

  Article 13. Exit permit

  1. The execution agency of the provincial police office which has compiled the judgment execution dossier shall check the passport or a paper with passport substitute value of the expelled person. In case the passport does not contain visa or the visa has expired, the immigration control agency shall issue a visa according to regulations. In case of emergency, the written request must be sent at least 12 (twelve) hours before the expelled person arrives at the border gate.

  2. In case the person subject to expulsion does not have a passport or a document of equivalent value, the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority shall notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security to request the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which that person is a national or the representative office of the international organization where that person works to issue necessary documents for exit visa procedures.

  In case where the requesting authority or organization has not yet processed or failed to meet the request, but there is a urgent need to expel the person immediately or the time limit for leaving the territory of Vietnam has expired as prescribed by law, the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police department shall request the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security of Vietnam to issue an exit document and request the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam to notify in writing the country of which the expelled person is a citizen for handling procedures for reception and entry. In case the country of which the expelled person holds nationality refuses to receive or permit entry, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam shall take charge and cooperate with the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security of Vietnam and the criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police department in resolving through diplomatic channels or carrying out expulsion in accordance with international practices.

  3. Where the person subject to expulsion is required to serve another sentence or has not yet completed the payment of a fine or fulfillment of another civil obligation under the case of exit suspension as prescribed by Vietnamese law, the criminal enforcement agency of the province-level police authority where the expulsion judgment record is prepared shall immediately send a report to the Court that issued the judgment execution decision to issue a decision on extension of the period for leaving the territory of Vietnam; and cooperate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in notifying and requesting the diplomatic mission, consular office, or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen and headquartered in Vietnam, or the representative office of the international organization for which the person works, or the organization that has guaranteed the person’s entry into Vietnam, to resolve or contact the family of the expelled person or the employer of the expelled person to provide support for funding for the payment of the fine or fulfillment of other civil obligations; and concurrently notify the competent civil judgment enforcement agency to carry out judgment enforcement procedures or to request the competent Court to consider granting exemption from or reduction of the judgment execution obligation regarding amounts payable to the state budget in accordance with the Law on Civil Judgment Enforcement. Upon receipt of a decision or written document from the civil judgment enforcement agency on the completion of judgment enforcement for the expelled person, the criminal enforcement agency of the province-level police authority shall carry out procedures to compel the expelled person to leave the territory of Vietnam.

  Article 14. Forced departure from Vietnamese territory

  1. The execution agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall decide on the time limit for exit but it must not exceed the time limit specified in the judgment execution decision of the Court or the time limit prescribed by law.

  2. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall purchase exit tickets for the expelled person in case the expelled person pays the cost by themselves or the diplomatic mission, consular office of the country of which the expelled person holds nationality or the representative office of the international organization where the expelled person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed the expulsion person’s return to Vietnam shall cover the cost of repatriation.

  In case of using budget to pay for air tickets, cars, trains or sea ferries for repatriation of persons expelled from Vietnam as prescribed in Article 102 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement, the criminal judgment enforcement management agency affiliated to the Ministry of Public Security shall decide to use budget and purchase tickets for repatriation vehicles.

  3. At least 24 (twenty-four) hours before the date of exit, the execution agency of the provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is made shall notify the person subject to expulsion, the competent immigration management agency, the Border Gate Department under the Border Guard High Command and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the time and place for exit for coordination in the expulsion. In case of expulsion by land road to a country sharing a land border with Vietnam, the time for exit must not be at night, except for special cases.

  In case the expelled person has money or lawful property deposited at the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level police department or accommodation establishment, 24 (twenty-four) hours before his/her departure, such money or property shall be returned to him/her. The return of money or property must be recorded in writing with signatures of the deliverer and the receiver as well as a witness.

  4. The criminal enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall escort the expelled person to the exit point; cooperate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, immigration authorities of the Ministry of Public Security, and the Border Guard Command’s Border Gate Department in carrying out procedures to compel the expelled person to leave Vietnamese territory. The execution of expulsion at the exit point must be recorded in writing, bearing the signatures of the expelled person, representatives of the agencies participating in the expulsion, diplomatic missions, consular offices of the country of nationality of the expeller, or representative offices of international organizations where the expelled person works.

  5. In case the person subject to expulsion fails to voluntarily comply with the expulsion decision or refuses to leave Vietnam, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall escort him/her to the border checkpoint of the country of which he/she is a citizen; in case of escorting by air, the criminal enforcement agency of the province shall notify and reach agreement with the air-freight company on security measures during the escorting as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 17 of Decree No. 81/2010/ND-CP of the Government on civil aviation security and strictly comply with international agreements on civil aviation security to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  Chapter III

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 15. Effect

  This Circular takes effect on January 1, 2012.

  Article 16. Implementation

  Agencies of the Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of National Defense and Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, within their functions, tasks and powers, organize implementation, guide and inspect implementation of this Circular.

  During the implementation of this Circular, if any problems arise, agencies, units and localities should report them to the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense or the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for timely guidance.-
