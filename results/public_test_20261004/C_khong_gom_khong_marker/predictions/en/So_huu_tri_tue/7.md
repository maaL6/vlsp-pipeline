                                            CIRCULAR
  ELABORATION OF CERTAIN ARTICLES AND PROVISIONS OF THE LAW ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, DECREE NO. 65/2023/ND-CP DATED AUGUST 23, 2023 OF THE GOVERNMENT ELABORATING CERTAIN ARTICLES AND PROVISIONS OF THE LAW ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY REGARDING INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY, PROTECTION OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS, RIGHTS TO PLANT VARIETIES, AND STATE MANAGEMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, AMENDED BY DECREE NO. 15/2026/ND-CP DATED JANUARY 14, 2026, DECREE NO. 33/2026/ND-CP DATED JANUARY 21, 2026, AND DECREE NO. 100/2026/ND-CP DATED MARCH 31, 2026
Pursuant to the Law on Intellectual Property No. 50/2005/QH11 amended by Law No. 36/2009/QH12, Law No. 42/2019/QH14, Law No. 07/2022/QH15, Law No. 93/2025/QH15 and Law No. 131/2025/QH15; Pursuant to Decree No. 55/2025/ND-CP of the Government on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Science and Technology;
Pursuant to Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP of the Government on elaboration of certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP;
At the request of the Director of the National Office of Intellectual Property and the Director of the Legal Department;
The Minister of Science and Technology promulgates a Circular elaborating on certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP dated August 23, 2023 of the Government elaborating on certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights, and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP dated January 14, 2026, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP dated January 21, 2026, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP dated March 31, 2026.
Chapter I
                                    GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
1. This Circular elaborates Clause 2 Article 67, Clause 3 Article 74, Clause 4 Article 89, Clause 4 Article 94, Clause 8 Article 95, Clause 7 Article 96, Clause 4 Article 97, Clause 8 Article 100, Clause 1 Article 108, Clause 6 Article 109, Clause 3 Article 112a, Clause 4 Article 113, Clause 4 Article 114, Clause 5 Article 115, Clause 4 Article 116, Clause 7 Article 119a, Clause 3 Article 120, Clause 2 Article 150, Clause 3 Article 155 of the Law on Intellectual Property; Clause 5 Article 14a, Clause 2 Article 48, Points a and c Clause 4 Article 62, Points a and c Clause 4 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. Guide the implementation of the following provisions of the Law on Intellectual Property and Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP:
a) Authorize the performance of procedures related to industrial property rights in accordance with Clause 4 Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Industrial property gazettes, national registers of industrial property, industrial property representatives;
c/ Assuring industrial property information.
Article 2. Regulated entities
Organizations and individuals shall carry out procedures related to industrial property rights, complaint procedures, and settlement of complaints related to industrial property procedures, organizations and individuals related to assurance of industrial property information, organizations and individuals related to training in industrial property law, training in industrial property assessment operations, and other relevant organizations and individuals.
Article 3. Interpretation of terms
In this Circular, the terms below are construed as follows:
1. “Applicant” performing procedures related to industrial property rights (hereinafter referred to as “applicant”) means an organization or individual filing an application for registration of an invention, layout design, industrial design, mark, geographical indication, filing an international application, submitting a request for extension or maintenance of validity of a protection title, termination or invalidation of a protection title, amendment to a protection title, or submitting an application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract.
2. Procedures related to industrial property rights in this Circular include procedures for registration of establishment of rights to inventions, industrial designs, layout designs, marks and geographical indications, procedures for maintenance and extension of validity of protection titles, procedures for termination or invalidation of protection titles, procedures for amendment of protection titles and procedures for transfer of industrial property rights. Requests arising from procedures related to industrial property rights include opposition to applications, amendments and additions, recognition of changes in applicants, application splitting, withdrawal of applications, conversion of applications, requests for fast content appraisal which are part of the procedures specified in this clause.
3. "Complainant" means an organization or individual that files a complaint related to industrial property procedures as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
4. “Intellectual Property Law” refers to the Intellectual Property Law No. 50/2005/QH11, amended by Law No. 36/2009/QH12, Law No. 42/2019/QH14, Law No. 07/2022/QH15, Law No. 93/2025/QH15 and Law No. 131/2025/QH15.
5. “Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP” refers to Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP of the Government, which provides detailed regulations and implementation measures for the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights, and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP.
6. "Paris Convention" means the Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property of 1883, amended in 1967 and 1979.
7. "PCT Treaty" means the Patent Cooperation Treaty of 1970, amended in 1979, 1984 and 2001.
8. "Budapest Treaty" means the Budapest Treaty on International Recognition of the Deposit of Microorganisms for the Purposes of Patent Procedure in 1977, amended in 1980.
9. "Madrid Agreement" means the Madrid Agreement on International Mark Registration of 1891, amended in 1979.
10. "Madrid Protocol" means a protocol related to the 1989 Madrid Agreement, amended in 2006 and 2007.
11. "The Hague Agreement" means the Hague Agreement on International Registration of Industrial Designs, 1999.
12. “PCT application” means an invention registration application filed under the PCT treaty.
13. “PCT application with designation or selection of Vietnam” means a PCT application filed with any member of the PCT treaty, including Vietnam, in which Vietnam is designated or selected.
14. “National stage PCT application” means a PCT application which is designated or selected to be filed in Vietnam and submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
15. "PCT application originating from Vietnam" means a PCT application filed from Vietnam, which includes a protection request in any member of the PCT Agreement, including Vietnam.
16. “Madrid application” means an international mark registration application filed pursuant to the Madrid Agreement or the Madrid Protocol.
17. "Madrid Application originating from Vietnam" means a Madrid Application for mark protection filed from Vietnam with respect to other members of the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol.
18. "Madrid Application with Vietnam designation" means a Madrid Application for protection of a mark originating from another member of the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol.
19. “La Hay formula” means an application for international registration of industrial designs filed under the Hague Agreement.
20. "La Hay formula with Vietnam designation" means a La Hay formula petition for industrial design protection in Vietnam, originating from any member of the Hague Agreement, including Vietnam.
21. "La Hay formula originating from Vietnam" means a La Hay formula submitted from Vietnam, which includes an application for industrial design protection in any member of the Hague Agreement, including Vietnam.
22. “International Office” means an International Office of the World Intellectual Property Organization.
Article 4. Procedures for processing applications for registration of inventions, industrial designs, marks, geographical indications and layout designs
1. Applications for registration of inventions, industrial designs, marks and geographical indications shall be received and processed in the following order, except for cases where there are separate regulations applicable to each type of application according to this Circular and relevant legal documents:
a) Receive as prescribed in Articles 13, 47, 70 and 95 of this Circular;
b) Appraise the form as prescribed in Articles 14, 48, 71 and 96 of this Circular;

c) Disclosure as prescribed in Articles 15, 49, 72 and 97 of this Circular, except for cases where confidential invention registration applications are not required to be disclosed as prescribed in Article 49 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
d) Content appraisal as prescribed in Articles 17, 50, 73 and 98 of this Circular. For invention registration applications, content appraisal shall be carried out upon request;
dd) In case a third party has an objection to the application, such objection shall be handled in accordance with Articles 27, 59, 82 and 103 of this Circular;
e) The National Office of Intellectual Property shall issue decisions to grant or refuse to grant protection titles based on the results of the content appraisal of applications. Decisions to grant protection titles shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property;
g) During the processing of the application, the applicant may amend, withdraw, split or convert the application as prescribed, provided that the corresponding fees and charges are paid.
2. An application for layout design registration shall be received, formally appraised and published according to Articles 40, 41 and 42 of this Circular. During the processing of an application, the applicant may amend or withdraw it as prescribed, provided that he/she pays corresponding fees and charges. The National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to grant or refuse to grant a protection title based on the results of the formal appraisal of the application for layout design registration. Decisions to grant protection titles shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property.
Article 5. Calculation of time limits in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaint procedures
1. The calculation of time limits in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaint procedures shall comply with Article 15 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. The time or fixed period (including the extension as prescribed) for the applicant, complainant or related party to respond to the notice, amend or supplement documents as prescribed shall not be included in the corresponding processing time limit or request as prescribed, including:
a) The period from the date of issuance of the notice to the date on which the applicant, complainant or related party responds to the notice; or the time limit specified in the notice, in case the applicant, complainant or related party does not respond to the notice;
b) The period from the date of filing to the date on which the applicant submits or the complainant or related party submits additional documents as prescribed in this Circular; or the period for the applicant, complainant or related party to submit additional documents as prescribed in this Circular in case they do not submit additional documents.
Article 6. Method of filing applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights and filing complaints
1. Applicants and complainants may submit applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights or complaints by themselves or through their lawful representatives in Vietnam in accordance with Clauses 1 and 2 Article 89, Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
2. Legal representatives of individuals as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article and Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be determined as follows:
a) For Vietnamese individuals and foreign individuals permanently residing in Vietnam or having production and business establishments in Vietnam, legal representatives include: individuals acting as legal representatives or authorized representatives; industrial property representation service providers acting under authorization;
b/ For a foreign individual who does not permanently reside in Vietnam or has no production and business establishment in Vietnam, his/her lawful representative is an industrial property representation service organization authorized by him/her.
3. Legal representatives of organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article and Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be determined as follows:
a) For Vietnamese organizations, legal representatives include: representative offices or branches in Vietnam; industrial property representation service providers under authorization;
b) For foreign organizations having production and business establishments in Vietnam, their lawful representatives include: representative offices or branches in Vietnam or wholly foreign-invested organizations in Vietnam of such organizations established under investment laws; industrial property representation service organizations under authorization;
c/ For foreign organizations having no production and business establishments in Vietnam, their lawful representative is an industrial property representation service organization under authorization.
4. Vietnamese individuals and foreign individuals permanently residing in Vietnam shall submit applications for procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 89 and Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property by signing their names on the declarations.
5. Vietnamese organizations and foreign organizations having production or business establishments in Vietnam shall submit applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights and lodging complaints according to Clause 1 Article 89 and Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property through their legal representatives or persons authorized by their legal representatives.
6. When carrying out relevant procedures, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may only conduct transactions with applicants, complainants or lawful representatives of applicants and complainants as prescribed in Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article. Transactions conducted with the aforementioned entities shall be collectively referred to as transactions with applicants and complainants.
Article 7. Format of application
1. Applications in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints shall be submitted in paper or electronic form via the electronic filing system in accordance with Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Circular.
2. “Electronic application” as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article means a document created or digitalized from a physical document and accurately and completely reflecting the content and format of the physical document, with electronic authentication in accordance with the law on electronic transactions. Applications for procedures related to industrial property rights, and electronic complaints shall be submitted via the electronic application submission system and have the same legal validity as applications for procedures related to industrial property rights and written complaints.
3. Applications shall be received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam at the One-stop Division of the Ministry of Science and Technology.
Article 8. Authorization in procedures related to industrial property rights and procedures for lodging complaints
1. The authorization of representation, including re-authorization and authorization of representation in procedures related to industrial property rights (hereinafter referred to as "authorization") must comply with the provisions of law on authorization of the Civil Code, Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property and guidance in this Circular.
The applicant or complainant may change their authorized representative. The change of the authorized representative terminates the authorization between the applicant/complainant and the authorized person. The termination of authorization with the authorized person and the change of the authorized representative must be declared in writing by the applicant/complainant (in the authorization document or a separate document).
The authorized person may authorize another person as prescribed by the Civil Code and the authorized person must meet the requirements for lawful representation as prescribed in Article 6 of this Circular. When the authorized organization or individual conducts transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving complaints must submit an initial authorization document, except where such document has already been submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. The time when a written authorization is recognized in a transaction with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or an administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving complaints is the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving complaints receives a valid written authorization. In case of change of the authorized representative or re-authorization or revision of information related to changes in authorization scope, premature termination of authorization, change of the address of the authorized party, this time is the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving complaints receives corresponding valid documents.
3. In case the written authorization is submitted after the deadline but before the date on which the application is accepted or processed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving complaints about transactions with the applicant or complainant (in the declaration or complaint) shall carry out the procedures for formal examination to determine whether the application is valid or invalid, accepted or rejected, including conclusions on the legality of the representative status.
4. All transactions of any authorized party within their authorization at any time shall be considered as transactions in the name of the applicant or complainant, giving rise to rights and obligations of the applicant or complainant. In case of change of the authorized representative or re-authorization, the new authorized representative or person re-authorized shall continue the representation with all issues arising from the previous transaction with the National Office of Intellectual Property or administrative organization assigned by the Minister of Science and Technology to resolve complaints.
5. If the written authorization has a scope of authorization including several independent procedures and the original copy of the written authorization has been submitted to the National Office of Intellectual Property, the administrative organization assigned by the Minister of Science and Technology to resolve the complaint shall submit copies of the written authorization with specific instructions to the number of the application containing the original copy of the written authorization in the declaration or documents of the subsequent procedures.

6. In case the written authorization clearly states that the authorization terminates when either party issues a written declaration on termination or when another written authorization is issued, such written authorization shall be considered having provisions on the term as prescribed at Point c, Clause 2, Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. In case of authorization to an organization or individual not permitted to act as a representative or authorization to multiple organizations and individuals, including organizations and individuals not permitted to act as representatives, the application shall be considered invalid.
8. The provisions on authorization in this Article shall also be applied accordingly to the implementation of administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP and the lodging of complaints about the results of settling such administrative procedures.
Article 9. Responsibilities of applicants, complainants and representatives
1. Applicants, complainants and representatives of applicants or complainants shall ensure the truthfulness of information and documents provided to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or assigned administrative organizations to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving complaints according to the following regulations:
a/ All transaction documents must be signed by the applicant or complainant or their representatives and affixed with the organization’s seal (if any). In cases where the law stipulates that a document must be notarized or authenticated, such stipulations shall apply.
b) Any Vietnamese translation of documents made in languages other than Vietnamese must be accompanied by a commitment from the applicant or complainant or their representative to ensure that the translation is an exact reproduction of the original document, except where the Vietnamese translation has been notarized as certified;
c/ In case the representative of the applicant or complainant is an industrial property representation service organization, the representative of such organization signing transaction documents must have a certificate of practice in industrial property representation services.
2. The applicant or complainant shall be responsible for all consequences and obligations arising from the representative of the applicant's performance in transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in resolving complaints in accordance with law.
3. Representatives of applicants and complainants shall be responsible to the applicants and complainants as per the law.
Article 10. Industrial property fees and charges
1. Applicants shall carry out procedures related to industrial property rights and other relevant procedures as prescribed in this Circular and users of industrial property services must pay fees and charges according to regulations of the Minister of Finance and other service charges as prescribed.
2. Fees and charges shall be collected as follows:
a) The applicant shall pay fees and charges to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as prescribed;
b) When collecting fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue receipts as documents for payment of fees and charges, clearly stating the collected fees and charges, and keep them in the application dossier to serve the appraisal of the application;
c) In case fees and charges are paid by post or directly transferred to the account of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the collection of fees and charges shall be determined through documents proving the payment of fees and charges in the application.
Chapter II
                               HANDLING OF INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Section 1. APPLICATION FOR INVENTION REGISTRATION
Article 11. Inventions registration applications
1. An invention registration application shall include the following documents:
a) An invention registration declaration, made using Form No. 1 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Description of the invention;
c) Summary of the invention;
d) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
dd) Documents proving the registration right, if the applicant inherits such right from another person;
e) Documents proving priority rights if priority rights are claimed, including:

e1) A copy of the application or the first applications submitted by the receiving authority; e2) The document on transfer of priority rights if such rights are inherited from another person;
g) Presentation documents on the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource for inventions directly created from such genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource;
h) Other documents supporting the application (if any).
2. An application for registration of a confidential invention shall comprise the following documents:
a) Documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall bear a seal of confidentiality as prescribed by law on protection of state secrets;
b) Written confirmation that the applicant is subject to state secrets in accordance with the law on protection of state secrets.
Article 12. Requirements on documents in an invention registration application
1. Documents of an invention registration application must satisfy respective specific requirements according to Clauses 2 thru 12 of this Article and meet the general requirements on form as prescribed below:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm) with 20 mm margins on all sides, using the Times New Roman font size of 13 pt or larger, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b/ For a document consisting of many pages, each page must be numbered in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with fade-resistant ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents have 2 pages or more, each page must bear a signature or a seal affixed by the applicant or the issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if minor spelling errors are found, the applicant may correct them and must affix a signature (and seal, if applicable) at the correction location. The signature and seal must not obscure the content of the documents.
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system must be in the “.pdf” format; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; the individual signing the documents of the invention registration application may use a digital signature to sign directly on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as color photos for the case of submission through the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not use local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers used for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Points d, dd, e, and h Clause 1 Article 11 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) Documents of the application must ensure that all mandatory information and consistent information are included.
2. Documents in the application must show that the subject matter of protection request is a product or process in accordance with Clause 12 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. An invention registration application shall ensure uniformity as prescribed in clauses 3 and 4 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. A group of inventions with a close technical relationship for the sole execution of a common creative intention according to clause 4 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be considered in the following cases:
a) One object used to create (manufacture, design, or prepare) another object;
b) One object used for the performance of another object;
c) One object is used for use of the other;
d) Subjects of the same type and with the same function to ensure the same result.
4. The invention registration declaration must be fully filled in with information as required at appropriate places in the Declaration, in which the applicant shall specify the classification index of the technical solution to be protected according to the International Classification of Inventions (according to the latest Paris Convention on International Classification of Inventions) published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property (with full classification, including parts, classes, sub-classes and groups (major groups or sub-groups)). If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify it and the applicant shall pay a classification fee as prescribed.
5. An invention summary shall be used to briefly describe (not exceeding 150 words) the invention subject to protection. The summary shall disclose principal contents of the technical solution and shall be intended solely for information purpose. The summary may contain drawings and specific formulas. All drawings and specific formulas (if any) shall be presented on half an A4 page and must be clear. The summary shall be presented as a separate document.
6. An invention description shall include an invention description and a protection request (protection scope) of the invention and must satisfy the provisions of clauses 7, 8, 9 and 10 of this article. The invention description may include drawings (if necessary) to illustrate the invention.
7. The description of an invention must fully and clearly disclose the nature of the technical solution of the invention to the extent that it can be implemented by a person with average knowledge in the art; must clarify the novelty, inventive step and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requires the grant of an invention patent); and must clarify the novelty and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requires the grant of a utility solution patent). If the application contains drawings, the description must be consistent with such drawings.
The description must include the following contents in the following order:
a) Name of invention: briefly express one or several subject matters of the protection request (hereinafter referred to as "subject matters"); the name of an invention must be concise, accurate and not promotional or advertising;
b/ Field of application: the field in which the subject matter is used or related to. If an invention is used or related to many fields, all such fields must be indicated. The above-mentioned fields must be appropriate to the results of the classification of the invention;
c) Technical status of the invention: the technical status in the field where the invention is used at the time of filing (similar objects are known (if any)). If there is no information on the technical status of the invention, this must be clearly stated;
d) Technical nature of the invention: the nature of the subject matter to be protected, clearly stating the following contents: technical issues to be solved (the purpose of the invention), specifying the purpose that the invention needs to achieve or the task (issue) that the invention needs to solve (for example, to overcome the disadvantages and limitations of the technical solutions already indicated in the technical state of the invention). The purpose or task of the invention must be presented in an objective and specific manner, not for expansion or advertisement; technical signs (characteristics) that constitute the object of protection, namely technical signs (characteristics) that form a solution to achieve the purpose of the invention (called fundamental technical signs) and must indicate new technical signs (characteristics) compared to similar known technical solutions; benefits (effects) that may be achieved compared to the technical state (if any), this content can be described as a separate part, as prescribed at Point h of this Clause;
dd) Brief description of accompanying drawings (if any);
e) Detailed description of the invention execution methods: describe one or several methods of executing the invention in such a way that persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field can execute the invention. Persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field are persons who have common practical skills and know general and popular knowledge in the corresponding technical field;
g) Example of implementation of invention (if any): Specify one or several specific methods of implementing the invention. If the invention is characterized by quantitative signs, specify the particular values of such signs; if not quantitative, specify the definite states of such signs. In addition, provide specific results related to the functions and purposes that the corresponding subject matter allows for achievement;
h) Benefits (efficiency) that may be achieved (if any and not stated in the technical nature section of the invention): may be expressed as increased productivity, quality, accuracy or efficiency; saved energy consumption and materials; simplified or created convenience when processing, operating, managing or using; overcome environmental pollution, etc. If the benefits (efficiency) that may be achieved are derived from statistical results from experimental data, the applicant must provide necessary conditions and methods for such experiments;
i) Illustrations (if any) shall be numbered and grouped together on separate pages dedicated to illustrations, placed at the end of the description. If there are symbols used for illustration, these symbols must be used consistently in all documents of the application.
8. The protection request must be presented in brief and clearly, in accordance with the description and drawings on separate pages, including points of the protection request, which must clarify new signs of the subject matter of the protection request and comply with the following regulations:
a/ The protection request must be fully described with technical specifications which are necessary and sufficient to identify the object, achieve the set goal and distinguish the object requested for protection from the known one;
b) Technical specifications in each protection request must be clear, accurate and accepted in the corresponding technical field; terms used in the protection request must be clear and consistent with those used in the description;
c/ The protection request shall not be cited to the description and drawings, except for cases where such parts cannot be described accurately in words, such as the nucleotide sequence and amino acid sequence, interference diagrams, state diagrams, etc.;
d/ If the application contains drawings illustrating the protection request, the signs stated in the protection request may be accompanied by indication numbers which must be enclosed in brackets. Such indication numbers shall not be regarded as limiting the scope of protection;
dd) The protection request point should (but is not required) be divided into two parts: "Restricted part" and "Distinct part", in which: "Restricted part" includes the name of the subject and signs of such subject identical to those of the nearest known subject, and is connected to "Distinct part" by the phrase "distinct in a place" or "characteristic in a place" or equivalent words; "Distinct part" includes distinctive signs of the subject compared to the nearest known subject, and these signs combined with signs of the "restricted part" constitute the protected subject;
e) The protection request may include one or more points. Where the protection request includes multiple points, it may be used to express a subject matter of protection, with the first point (referred to as the independent point) and subsequent points (referred to as dependent points) used to specify the independent point; or to express a group of protected subject matters, with several independent points, each representing a protected subject matter within the group, each of which may have dependent points. Each protection request point shall refer to only one protected subject matter and must be expressed in a single sentence;
g) The points of the protection request must be numbered in Arabic numerals with a dot at the end;
h/ A request for protection of many points used to express a group of objects must satisfy the following requirements: the independent points express separate objects and must not be cited to other protection-requested points, except where such citation allows avoiding the repetition of the entire contents of another point; dependent points must be expressed after the independent ones from which they depend.
9. Regarding an application for registration of a pharmaceutical-related invention, in addition to the general requirements on the description prescribed in Clause 7 of this Article, the description must specify the results of clinical tests and/or pharmacological effects of the pharmaceutical product, including at least the following information:
a) Used materials/compound;
b) The method(s) used for testing;
c) Information about test results;
d) The relationship between the pharmacological effects obtained in tests and the practical application of pharmaceuticals in disease prevention, diagnosis and treatment.
10. For an invention registration application related to biological technology, apart from meeting the general requirements for invention description specified in Clause 7 of this Article, it must also meet the following requirements:
a) Regarding an invention registration application concerning a genetic sequence or a part of a genetic sequence, the description must contain a list of genetic sequences presented according to the World Intellectual Property Organization's standards for presenting lists of nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences in invention registration applications. The sequence list shall be presented separately at the end of the description;
b) Include electronic information carriers (e.g., floppy disks, optical disks, etc.) readable by common electronic devices, which contain nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences identical to those in the list of sequences stated in the description in case of request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c/ For inventions on or related to biological materials, applications for registration of inventions must satisfy the following conditions if they cannot be described or adequately described for persons with average knowledge in the field of biotechnology to implement them: c1/ Biological material samples already deposited at agencies competent to store biological materials not later than the filing date of applications for registration of inventions related to such biological materials; c2/ The description clearly states necessary information about properties of biological materials which the applicant may have; c3/ The declaration clearly states the agency storing biological materials, the storage number of the biological material samples already deposited by the storing agency and documents certifying these information are filed with the National Office of Intellectual Property within 16 months from the priority date or not later than the filing date of early disclosure requests (if any), whichever is earlier, except for cases where the deposit is made under the regulations of the PCT Implementation Regulation;
d) In case the applicant is not the biological material depositor, the name and address of the depositor and documents certifying the lawful use of biological materials must be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date or no later than the earliest submission date of the application for early disclosure (if any), whichever is earlier, except where the deposit is made in accordance with the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Agreement.
11. For inventions relating to biological materials falling under Point c Clause 10 of this Article, the submission of biological material samples to competent authorities as prescribed in Point c1 Clause 10 of this Article must meet the following requirements:
a/ The biological material-keeping agency means a Vietnamese or foreign agency on the list of international biological material-keeping agencies under the Budapest Treaty or recognized by a competent Vietnamese agency for the function of biological material keeping;
b) For biological material samples deposited at a foreign depository, if it is deemed necessary to clarify the nature of the subject matter of the protection request or to meet the requirements of a third party regarding access to such subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may take the following actions:

b1) Request the applicant to deposit additional biological material samples at a competent depositor in Vietnam in cases where the biological material samples have not been deposited at an international competent depositor under the Budapest Treaty;

b2) Request the biological material sample depositor to provide samples in cases where the biological material samples have been deposited at an international competent depositor under the Budapest Treaty.
12. Regarding an application for registration of an invention directly created from a genetic resource or traditional knowledge about a genetic resource, where the applicant fails to submit explanatory documents on the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource that the author of the invention or the applicant has accessed in accordance with Point g Clause 1 Article 11 of this Circular due to the inability to determine the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource, such fact must be clearly stated and the applicant shall take responsibility for the truthfulness of such statement.
Article 13. Receipt of applications for registration of inventions
1. An invention registration application shall be accepted when it satisfies the following conditions:
a) Having all minimum documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Description, including protection request;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. A secret invention registration application shall be accepted when it satisfies the following conditions:
a) Have sufficient documents as prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article and documents prescribed in Point b Clause 2 Article 11 of this Circular;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
3. In case an application meets all conditions specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and verify the submission date and application number of such application and return a receipt to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number, and results of the verification of the list of documents included in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For electronic applications, upon receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue an application receipt to the applicant, which includes information on the submission date, the application number, and the results of the inspection of the list of documents in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application submission system.
4. In case an application fails to satisfy all conditions specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, it shall be rejected for receipt (if the application is submitted by post or via the electronic application system, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reasons for rejection). For applications rejected for receipt by post, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return documents of the application except for the original submitted for comparison.
5. Regarding documents proving priority rights, applicants may submit additional documents within 3 months from the date of application as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
6. For secret invention registration applications, the receipt of such applications in accordance with Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article must comply with the law on protection of state secrets.
Section 2. VERIFICATION OF APPLICATION FOR INVENTION REGISTRATION
Article 14. Format appraisal of invention registration applications
1. The formal examination of an invention registration application according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the inspection of compliance with regulations on form of the application in order to conclude whether the application is valid or not.
2. An invention registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:

a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Article 86 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed in contravention of Article 89 and Article 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to affirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not eligible for protection as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 and Article 59 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) There are grounds to confirm that the author of the invention fails to satisfy the provisions of Clause 1, Article 122 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed in Article 10 of this Circular (including cases where the application fee, application disclosure fee, application appraisal fee, and information lookup fee for appraisal are not fully paid, except for the information lookup fee for appraisal and the content appraisal fee if no content appraisal is requested in the application);
e) The application fails to meet the format requirements specified in Article 12 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements for presentation format; failing to classify or incorrectly classifying inventions without paying classification fees; lacking translations of documents proving priority rights (if required), or translations of documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not properly certified as prescribed; the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author, applicant, representative, or lacks the signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; for a split application, the explanatory text about the subject matter of protection in the split application that is included in the original application is missing; documents in the application for secret invention registration have not yet been sealed with the secret seal as prescribed, etc.;
g) There is no valid written authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming the lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (documents proving inheritance rights according to civil laws, documents proving the transfer of application submission rights; assignment contracts or labor contracts, etc.); documents showing pharmaceutical test results on humans, animals, or plants mentioned in the description (when the subject matters of protection requests are pharmaceuticals for humans, animals, or plants); etc.
In case the above-mentioned time limit expires but the applicant fails to submit supplementary documents satisfying the requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application.
4. Notification of the results of the formal assessment shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the protection title, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application (including priority claims), and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinion or correct deficiencies.
b/ If, upon the expiration of the time limit prescribed at Point a of this Clause, the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to make satisfactory correction thereof or does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection, within 15 working days after the expiration of such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision refusing the grant of the protection title and send it to the applicant.
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant has made satisfactory corrections to deficiencies and provided a legitimate explanation within the prescribed time limit, the application shall be considered valid and proceed to content disclosure and appraisal as per regulation (if the application requests content appraisal). In case the request for priority rights is rejected, the application shall still be considered valid and proceed to content disclosure as per regulation.
5. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of invention registration applications to be transferred for publication, specifying the application number, filing date and date of transfer for publication of each application.
6. The filing date of an invention application to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in accordance with Clause 3 Article 13 hereof.
In case an application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative working hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date specified in this clause shall be the next working day.
7. The priority date of an invention registration application shall be determined as follows:
a/ If an application does not request priority right or although it requests priority right, it is not approved by the National Office of Industrial Property, such application shall be regarded as having no priority date;
b) If the application requests priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the National Office of Intellectual Property;
c) The priority date determined according to the request for priority rights on the basis of the first application(s) submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
8. In case the applicant requests to enjoy priority right but at the time of filing the invention registration application there is no document proving the priority right, the examination of the application shall start from the time the National Office of Intellectual Property receives the supplementary documents or end after 3 months as prescribed in Clause 5, Article 13 of this Circular.
Article 15. Publication of applications for registration of inventions
1. An invention registration application shall be considered as valid in form and published within the time limit prescribed in Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Contents of disclosure of an invention registration application in the Industrial Property Official Gazette include the following information: applicant, representative (if any); application number, filing date, priority date (if any); name and nationality of the inventor; information on origin of the application (application conversion, application splitting, initial application number of split/conversion application, etc.); summary of the invention; drawings enclosed with the summary (if any); content appraisal request date (if any); early disclosure request date (if any); international classification of the invention; and other information (if any).
3. The invention description shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 3. CONTENT CONSIDERATION OF INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 16. Requirements for substantive examination of applications for registration of inventions
1. Applicants may request content appraisal of invention registration applications according to Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following regulations:
a) A written request for substantive examination of an invention registration application, made using Form No. 2 in Appendix I of this Circular or the invention registration application form (if such a request is made immediately upon filing the application), or Form No. 13 in Appendix I of this Circular in case of a fast-track request;
b) The time limit for submission of an application for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall comply with Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property; an application for substantive examination of an invention registration application must be submitted immediately upon submission of the application in accordance with Clause 3 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) The applicant shall pay a fee for searching and a content appraisal fee as prescribed. In case the applicant fails to fully pay the above-mentioned fees and charges, the request for content appraisal shall be considered invalid and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam will not conduct the content appraisal.
An application for content appraisal of an invention registration application shall be filed after the date on which such application is published in the Industrial Property Gazette in the second month from the date of receipt of the request. An application for content appraisal of an invention registration application filed before the date of publication of the application shall be published together with the corresponding application.
2. The third party may request content appraisal of an invention registration application according to Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following regulations:
a) Request content appraisal of the invention registration application using Form No. 2 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) The time limit for submission of an application for content appraisal of an invention registration application according to Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) The applicant for content appraisal of an invention registration application shall pay a fee for searching and a content appraisal fee as prescribed;
d) Request content appraisal of an invention registration application, which is notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to the applicant within 2 months from the date of receipt of such request;
dd) In case of invalid request for content appraisal, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request for content appraisal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to correct errors. If the applicant fails to correct errors within the imposed time limit or fails to meet requirements after correction, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the rejection of content appraisal of the application;
e) In case of a valid request for content appraisal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct content appraisal according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Circular and notify the appellant of the content appraisal results.
Article 17. Content appraisal of invention registration applications
1. Content appraisal of an invention registration application according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the protection eligibility of technical solutions stated in the application according to protection conditions and determination of corresponding protection scope according to provisions of this Article.
2. The assessment of the capability to grant protection titles for invention registration applications shall include:
a) Assess the conformity of subject matters stated in the invention registration application and the type of protection title as prescribed in Article 19 of this Circular;
b) Evaluate subject matters stated in invention registration applications according to each protection condition as prescribed in Articles 20, 21 and 22 of this Circular. The evaluation shall be carried out sequentially for each point stated in the protection request;
c) Assess the first-to-file principle as prescribed in Article 23 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the ability to grant a title of protection as stipulated in point b clause 2 of this Article shall be considered complete when the subject matter of the invention stated in the application has been assessed under all protection conditions and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter does not meet or meets the protection conditions, specifically:
a) A reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy one or several or all of the protection conditions; or b) No reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Based on the results of assessment of the ability to grant protection titles prescribed in clauses 2 and 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding expected protection scope.
5. Before issuing a notice of content appraisal results for issuance of an invention patent/utility solution as prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 25 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall verify the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 23 of this Circular.
6. Notification of content appraisal results for issuance of invention/utility solution patents specified at Points a and d2, Clause 3, Article 25 of this Circular shall be made for the following invention applications:
a) The application does not fall under the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application has the earliest submission date or priority date among invention registration applications falling under the case specified in Clause 1 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Application as agreed in Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. An invention registration application not falling into the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article shall be processed as follows:
a) The grant of an invention/utility solution patent shall be rejected if it fails to satisfy the first-to-file rule, provided that the application with the earliest filing date or priority date is granted an invention/utility solution patent; or b) The grant of an invention/utility solution patent shall be considered as the case specified in clause 6 of this article if all other applications for registration of inventions with earlier filing dates or priority dates are rejected from being granted invention/utility solution patents or withdrawn or deemed to have been withdrawn.
8. During the process of content appraisal, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations for the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify the deficiencies.
9. During the process of content appraisal of an application, depending on the complexity of assessing the conditions for protection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may consult independent expert consultants or the Council for Patent Appraisal Consultation.
The Council for Patent Appraisal Consultancy shall be established and operate on the basis of the principles prescribed in the decision of the Director of the National Office of Intellectual Property.
Independent consultants shall be selected from the List of industrial property consultancy experts compiled by the National Office of Intellectual Property and other sources (in case no suitable expert is available in such list). The applicant may request to attend the meeting of the Appraisal Council or with independent consultants to clarify information stated in the application for the appraisal by the Council or the consultant.
Article 18. Minimum information sources
1. In order to assess the novelty and creativity of technical solutions stated in applications, it is required to search information in the following compulsory sources (but not limited to the minimum number of sources).
a) All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall have the same classification index with the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal, up to the third sub-classification index and have a priority date or filing date earlier than the priority date or filing date of the application currently undergoing appraisal, except for applications that have been or will not be published (when assessing novelty) or applications that have not yet been published up to the priority date or filing date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (when assessing the inventive step);
b) Inventions or invention protection titles published by other organizations or countries within 25 years before the filing date or priority date of the application that is being examined (if the application enjoys priority rights) shall be kept in the invention database at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and other information sources prescribed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, with the search scope specified in point a of this clause;
c/ The invention protection titles granted by the National Office of Intellectual Property up to the filing date or priority date of the application are being examined.
In necessary and possible cases, the search may be extended to the national database on science and technology and other accessible information sources, including those on the Internet.
2. The purpose of information lookup is to find technical solutions with similar or identical nature to those in the application. In this clause:
a) Two technical solutions shall be considered identical when they have all basic signs (characteristics) identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
b) Two technical solutions shall be considered similar when they have most of their basic signs (characteristics) identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
c) “Controlled technical solution” means a technical solution identical or similar to the one stated in the application;
d) “Comparison documents” are documents describing the technical solution of comparison or evidence proving that the technical solution of comparison has been publicly disclosed.
3. The search results must be presented in the search report, specifying the field searched, the scope of search, and the results found within that scope (statistics on technical solutions used for comparison, specifying identical signs, names of comparison documents, page numbers, line numbers, origin of documents, and publication date of corresponding documents), and the name of the person preparing the report (the searcher).
Article 19. Assessment of conformity of subject matters stated in an invention registration application
1. Subject matters stated in an invention registration application shall be deemed incompatible with the type of invention protection title requested to be granted by the applicant (an invention patent or a utility solution patent) if such subject matters are not technical solutions, specifically products or processes.
2. Technical solutions (subjects of protection as inventions) defined in Clause 1 of this Article are a necessary and sufficient combination of information on technical methods and/or means (application of natural laws) for solving a given task (problem). Technical solutions may take one of the following forms:
a) Physical products, such as tools, machinery, equipment, components, electrical circuits, etc., expressed by a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by technical signs (features) regarding structure, which has functions (utilities) as a means to meet certain needs of humans; or products in the form of substances (including simple substances, compounds and mixtures of substances), such as materials, materials, foods, pharmaceuticals, etc., expressed by a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by technical signs (features) regarding the presence, proportion and state of elements, which have functions (utilities) as a means to meet certain needs of humans; or products in the form of biological materials, such as genes, genetically modified plants/animals, etc., expressed by a collection of information about a product containing genetic information altered under human influence and capable of self-reproduction;
b) Process (technological process; method of diagnosis, prediction, inspection, handling, etc.) expressed as a set of information defining the manner in which a specific process or task is carried out, characterized by signs (features) regarding the order, conditions, participants, measures and means for performing operations to achieve a certain purpose.
3. Subjects mentioned in the application shall not be considered as technical solutions in the following cases:
a) The subject matter stated in the application is only an idea or intention, stating only the issue but not the solution, failing to answer the question "by what means" or "by what means";
b) The issues (tasks) to be addressed are not technical issues and cannot be resolved by technical means;
c/ Natural products, not created by humans.
Article 20. Assessment of novelty

1. In order to evaluate the novelty of a technical solution stated in an application, it is required to compare fundamental signs (characteristics) of such technical solution with signs of control technical solutions found during the information lookup according to Article 18 of this Circular, in which:
a) Fundamental technical signs of a technical solution may be characteristics of the material object’s structure (details, groups of details, connections, etc.) or the composition of substances (components (presence, proportion), states of elements, etc.), together with other fundamental technical signs, forming a necessary and sufficient set to determine the nature (content) of the subject.
The above fundamental technical signs may be expressed in the form of technical functions of an element in the structure or composition of a product (called functional signs), provided that such expression is sufficient for persons with average knowledge in the field to easily understand the technical means or method of performing such function under normal conditions without requiring creativity. The functions and utility of the subject matter of protection request are not fundamental technical signs but may be the purpose or achieved result of such subject matter;
b) Basic technical specifications of the technical solution stated in the application or protection title, which are shown in the invention protection request, description or drawings;
c) Basic technical specifications of technical solutions stated in other documents shall be presented and detected according to the description documents or actual form of such technical solutions.
2. In order to avoid being considered as losing novelty of an invention which has been published in the exceptions specified in clauses 3 and 4 of article 60 of the Intellectual Property Law, the applicant shall submit documents related to the publication to prove that he/she is eligible for the exception. The above-mentioned documents must be submitted together with the application or additionally according to regulations on amendment and supplementation of applications.
3. A technical solution stated in an application shall be considered new compared with the world’s technical conditions if it is corresponding to a point of the protection request.
a) No technical solution for comparison is found during the information lookup; or b) A technical solution for comparison is found but there is at least one basic technical sign in the technical solution mentioned in the application that is not present in the technical solution for comparison (and such a sign is called a different basic sign).
Article 21. Assessment of creativity
1. To assess the creativity of technical solutions stated in the application, it is required to search information in compulsory sources (but not limited to the minimum sources as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 18 of this Circular).
2. The assessment of the level of creativity of a technical solution stated in an application shall be carried out by evaluating fundamental distinctive signs stated in the protection request in order to make a conclusion:
a) Whether other distinct fundamental signs are considered to have been disclosed in the mandatory minimum information source; and b) Whether a collection of other distinct fundamental technical signs is considered obvious to persons with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field.
In response to a protection request point, a technical solution shall be considered creative if the incorporation of other distinctive fundamental technical signs into the collection of fundamental technical signs of the technical solution is the result of creative activities and not the obvious result of common knowledge in the relevant technical field.
3. In the following cases (but not limited to these cases), for each protection request point, a technical solution shall be deemed uncreative:
a) Collecting other obviously different basic technical signs (anyone with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field knows that in order to perform a defined function or achieve a defined purpose, it is necessary to use such a collection of signs and vice versa when using such a collection of signs, it is necessary to achieve the corresponding purpose or perform the corresponding function);
b) Collect other distinct fundamental technical signs which have been expressed in a uniform or equivalent form in one or several known technical solutions from the mandatory minimum information sources. In this case, two signs shall be considered uniform if they have the same nature; two signs shall be considered equivalent if they have similar natures, the same purpose and the same way of achieving the basic purpose;
c) Technical solutions are simple combination of known technical solutions with functions, purposes and effects or simple combination of functions, purposes and effects of each known technical solution.
Article 22. Assessment of industrial applicability

1. Technical solutions stated in an application shall be considered “feasible” according to Article 62 of the Law on Intellectual Property if:
a) Information on the nature of the solution together with instructions on necessary technical conditions shall be presented clearly and sufficiently so that persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field can create, manufacture or use, exploit or implement such solution;
b) The creation, production, use, exploitation or implementation of the above solutions shall be repeated with the same results and different from the results stated in the invention description.
2. Technical solutions shall be considered incapable of industrial application in the following cases:
a) The nature of the subject or instructions for performing the subject is contrary to fundamental principles of science (e.g., failing to comply with the law of conservation of energy, etc.);
b) The subject includes elements and components that have no technical connection with each other or cannot be connected (joined, bound, dependent, etc.) to each other;
c) The subject contains internal contradictions;
d) The subject matters of the instructions may be given only a limited number of times (cannot be repeated);
dd) To be able to implement the solution, the person must have special skills which cannot be transferred or only given to others;
e) The results obtained from the inconsistent implementation of the project are not consistent;
g) The results obtained are different from those stated in the description;
h) Having no or lacking the most important instructions for solution;
i) Other cases with legitimate reasons.
Article 23. Evaluation of the first-to-file principle
For invention registration applications already concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing content appraisal results notices with the intended issuance of invention patents or utility solution patents as prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 25 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct inspections to ensure the first-to-file rule prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property under the following regulations:
1. In order to assess the first-to-file rule, at least the following compulsory sources must be consulted (but not limited to these sources): All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (up to the time of examination) with the same classification index as the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal - up to the third class index and with an earlier filing date or priority date than the filing date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is entitled to priority rights) that has not yet been published or with a later publication date than the filing date or priority date (if the application is entitled to priority rights) of the application currently undergoing appraisal.
2. The lookup is to find cases where there are many applications (including the application currently being appraised) for registration of inventions that are identical or similar to each other and determine the earliest date of filing or priority date of the application.
3. If there are many applications in the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, an invention patent or utility solution patent may be granted only to inventions in valid applications with the earliest submission date or priority date among applications satisfying the conditions for protection titles.
4. Among applications falling into the cases mentioned in Clause 2 of this Article, if there are many applications with the same filing date or the earliest priority date, an invention patent or a utility solution patent may be granted to only one application among them according to agreement of all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all such applications shall be rejected from the grant of protection titles.
Article 24. Use of information lookup results and content appraisal results of invention registration applications of foreign invention authorities
1. During the process of content appraisal of an invention registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may refer to information lookup results and content appraisal results of a corresponding application filed overseas.
2. Results of search and appraisal specified in Clause 1 of this Article include one of the following documents:
a) Reports on lookup, appraisal or notice of appraisal results;
b) The publication of the patent or protection title.

3. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of content appraisal results, applicants may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to use the content appraisal results of an invention application filed overseas to assess the protectability if the following conditions are satisfied:
a) The results of the content appraisal of documents specified in Clause 2 of this Article must be issued by national or regional intellectual property offices selected as preliminary lookup and appraisal agencies under the PCT Agreement;
b) In the content appraisal results of the above-mentioned invention registration application filed overseas, there is at least one protection request point assessed as satisfying the protection conditions;
c) Points of protection requested in the application filed in Vietnam initially or after amendment must be identical to points of protection assessed as satisfying the protection conditions in the content appraisal results of the invention registration application filed overseas mentioned above;
d) The applicant shall submit the following documents to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam: a request for use of foreign appraisal results made using Form No. 12 in Appendix I of this Circular; copies of appraisal results; Vietnamese translations of appraisal results and points of protection assessed by the foreign IP agency as meeting protection conditions; Vietnamese translations and cited documents in the processing results of the foreign IP agency upon request; a description of amendments, detailed presentation of amended and supplemented contents compared to the initial description submitted (if any); and fees as prescribed.
4. In case the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article are satisfied, the requirements for use of the foreign content appraisal results shall be accepted and the National Office of Intellectual Property shall issue a notice of content appraisal results within 8 months from the date of receipt of the applicant's request. All procedures for amendment or supplementation of an invention registration application within the above-said time limit if it causes one of the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article to no longer be satisfied, such invention registration application shall be appraised according to ordinary procedures.
5. In case one of the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article is not satisfied, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify refusal to use foreign results and the invention registration application shall be appraised according to normal procedures.
Article 25. Issuance and refusal to issue invention/good solution patents
1. An invention registration application shall be rejected for grant of an invention patent or utility solution patent in the cases specified at Points a, b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Clauses 1a and 1b, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. An invention registration application shall be granted an invention patent or utility solution patent when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For invention registration applications falling into the cases specified in clause 1 or clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clauses 1a and 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the title of protection and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the title of protection, the fee for publication of the decision on issuance of the title of protection, the registration fee for the decision on issuance of the title of protection, the validity maintenance fee and the fee for use of the title of protection for the first year;
b) If the subject matter stated in the invention registration application does not satisfy the protection conditions or the application falls into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clauses 1a and 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions but the application has deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the protection title, all reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application, providing guidance on amendments to the protection scope (volume) and setting a time limit of 3 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide objections or correct deficiencies;
c/ In case the applicant fails to correct errors or does not make any objection within the time limit prescribed at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days after the expiry of such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;
d) In case the applicant rectifies deficiencies or provides objections within the time limit specified in point b of this clause, within 4 months from the date of receipt of the rectification document or objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the examination as follows:

d1) If the rectification is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;

d2) If the rectification is satisfactory or the objection is justifiable given the content examination results and/or the amended application is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content examination results, stating the intended grant of the protection title and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of the protection title, the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title, the validity maintenance fee and the fee for use of the first-year protection title;
dd) If the applicant fails to fully pay the fee for issuance of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on issuance of a protection title or the registration fee for issuance of a protection title within the time limit specified at Points a and d2 of this Clause, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse the issuance of a protection title within 15 working days from the date of expiry of the corresponding time limit;
e/ If the applicant fully and punctually pays all fees and charges within the time limit specified at Points a and d2 of this Clause, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to grant a protection title within one month after the payment of such fees and charges. If within the prescribed time limit the applicant has paid all fees for the grant of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of a protection title, the registration fee for the decision on the grant of a protection title but fails to pay the fee for maintenance of validity and the fee for use of the protection title in the first year, such protection title shall still be granted but its validity shall be terminated immediately after the grant.
4. The decision on grant of an invention patent or utility solution patent shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance. Such publication shall include information stated in the corresponding decision (including the decision number and issuance date) and the contents specified in Clause 2 Article 15 of this Circular.
The full description of an invention patent or utility solution patent shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5. An invention patent or utility solution patent made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued in electronic and paper forms (if the applicant requests a paper copy). In case industrial property rights to an invention are under joint ownership, the invention patent or utility solution patent shall only be granted to the first person on the list of co-applicants.
An invention patent copy made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in case the applicant requests issuance of a copy (specified in the invention registration declaration) for other co-owners.
Section 4. HANDLING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO INVENTORY REGISTRATION
Article 26. Handling of requests for content appraisal of invention registration applications
1. An application for content appraisal of an invention registration application shall be accepted when the applicant has fully paid the prescribed fees and charges and has submitted the following documents:
a) Written request for rapid content appraisal, made using Form No. 13 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the invention has been commercially exploited according to Point b Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP within 12 months prior to the filing date;
2. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall receive and process requests for prompt content appraisal of documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article according to the following regulations:
a) In case of a request for fast content appraisal that fails to meet the requirements specified in Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the results of the fast content appraisal, clearly stating the reasons for the intended refusal or deficiencies and impose a time limit of 10 working days from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinions or correct deficiencies;
b) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or unsatisfactory corrections or does not have any objection or provides inadequate objection within 10 working days from the end of the time limit prescribed in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the rejection of the request for fast content appraisal and the corresponding invention registration application shall continue to be processed as prescribed;
c) In case of a request for rapid content appraisal meeting the provisions of Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, such request shall be announced at the same time as the announcement of the invention registration application and subject to content appraisal in accordance with this Circular within the time limit prescribed in Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, except where the invention registration application is determined to be invalid under Article 14 of this Circular.
Article 27. Handling of objections to invention registration applications
1. Within 6 months from the date of publication of an invention registration application or within 3 months from the date of publication of an invention registration application in cases where such application is subject to rapid content appraisal according to Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, any third party shall have the right to oppose the grant of a protection title for such application and must pay fees and charges.
Objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese accompanied by documents or sources of information to prove. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. In case an objection to an invention registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and handle such objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 1 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response;
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the objector and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) On the basis of information, evidence and arguments provided by the parties, results of the dialogue (if any) as prescribed in Points a, b and c of this Clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the result of processing the objection together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application to the objector, except for the case specified at Point dd of this Clause.
dd) In case an invention registration application has been withdrawn or is considered to have been withdrawn according to Article 116 and Clause 3 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the suspension of processing of objection opinions within 01 month from the date of notification of acceptance of withdrawal of the application or from the date of expiration of the time limit for requesting content appraisal of the invention registration application specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. In case opinions of objectors are related to the right to register inventions, such objector’s opinions shall be handled as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector to file a lawsuit petition to the competent Court in accordance with civil procedure law and impose a time limit of 02 months from the notification date for the objector to submit a copy of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit;
b) Upon expiry of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if the objector fails to submit copies of the notice of acceptance of the case or his/her objection, such copies shall be regarded as withdrawn and the invention registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection.
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the invention registration application pending the dispute resolution results of the Court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the Court.
4. The time limit for applicants to respond to objections and the time limit for objectors to respond to applicants’ opinions as prescribed in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal.
Article 28. Amendment to invention registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention/good solution patent, applicants may take initiative or based on notification of appraisal results to amend information and documents in their applications for registration of inventions.
2. The request for amendment to an invention registration application shall satisfy the following requirements:
a) In case of amendment to the application based on the notice of appraisal results of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the request for amendment shall be made in writing and clearly state the amendment. In case the applicant proactively amends the application, the request for amendment shall be made using Form No. 10 under Appendix I of this Circular and clearly state the amendment.
b) In case of amendments to the description or summary, the applicant shall submit part or all of the amended description and summary together with a detailed presentation of the amendments. The amendments to the description and summary of the invention registration application must not expand the scope (volume) already disclosed in the initial description and must not change the nature of the subject matter of the invention stated in the application;
c) In case of change of industrial property representatives, applicants shall submit documents declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representatives and change of new industrial property representatives as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular;
d) In case of change to name, address or country code of the applicant, the applicant must submit a confirmation document (original or certified true copy) or legal document (certified true copy) proving such change (decision on change to name or address; certificate of enterprise registration which records the change to name or address, etc.), except for cases where the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may take initiative in extracting and using information already available in databases publicly disclosed by database owners. In case the information cannot be extracted or is not fully and accurately extracted, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to the individual or organization to supplement documents and provide explanation. The time for requesting and supplementing documents shall not be included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
dd) In case of amendment to other information besides points b, c and d of this clause, documents proving lawful amendment or commitment of the applicant are required;
e) In case of amendments to information specified in points c, d and dd of this clause, applicants may request amendments to the same content related to multiple invention registration applications in a single declaration for amendments;
g) The applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee for each content of amendment and each invention registration application and a fee for disclosure of information about amendments to the application if such amendments must be disclosed under Point a Clause 4 of this Article, except where such amendments are made to correct errors made by the National Office of Industrial Property.
3. In case an applicant proactively requests amendment to the contents specified in Points b, c and d Clause 2 of this Article before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant a protection title due to the invalidity of the application, such request shall be processed during the process of application appraisal. The result of processing this request (acceptance or refusal to accept the amendment request) shall be stated in the notice of application appraisal results or the results of processing the invention registration application.
4. Requests for amendments or additions not related to the description and summary submitted after the application has been published shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment to an invention registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:

a1) Issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendment to the application;

a2) Publish the amended content in the Industrial Property Gazette;
b) If the request for amendment to the application does not satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide feedback;
c/ If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to correct errors or his/her correction is unsatisfactory, or does not or improperly object to the request for amendment or supplementation of the application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to accept such request.
5. Requests for amendments to the description and summary submitted after the application has been published shall be processed as follows:
a) In case the applicant proactively amends the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice acknowledging the request for amendment and publish the amended content on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
b) Requests for amendments or supplements shall be processed during the process of appraisal of applications and the results of processing such requests (acceptance or rejection of acceptance of requests for amendments or supplements) shall be stated in notices of results of appraisal or results of processing of such applications;
c) In case of a request for amendment to an application related to the nature of the invention stated in the description filed after the notice of intended issuance of the patent/good solution is issued, the invention registration application shall be re-examined within the time limit specified in Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the applicant must pay the prescribed fee.
Article 29. Recording of changes to applicants for invention registration
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention/good solution patent, applicants may request the recognition of changes in applicants on the basis of transfer, inheritance, adoption, or decision of competent authorities.
2. The request for recognition of changes to the applicant must meet the following requirements:
a) Request to record changes in applicants using Form No. 11 under Appendix I hereof;
b) In case of recording changes to applicants due to transfer of applications, applicants shall submit documents showing the transfer of invention registration applications (original copies or certified true copies), including name and address of the transferring parties and the transferee; number of transferred applications or information sufficient to identify such applications;
c) In case of recording change of applicant due to inheritance or inheritance or according to decision of competent authority, the applicant must submit documents proving related to inheritance or inheritance of assets upon consolidation, merger, division, separation of legal entity, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entity of same owner, business format conversion or according to decision of Court or other competent authority or inheritance due to other applicants giving up registration rights;
d) The applicant must pay an application appraisal fee for recording of changes to the applicant and a fee for disclosure of information on recording of changes to the applicant;
dd) The request for recognition of changes due to transfer of many applications from the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that an appraisal fee is paid according to regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for recognition of transfer.
3. The request for recognition of changes in applicants shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for change of applicant satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of change of applicant and publish it in the Industrial Property Gazette (except for cases where the amendment to the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the protection title due to invalidation of the application in terms of form);
b) If the request for change of applicant fails to satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize such change and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate corrections, no objection, or provides inadequate objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to recognize the change of the applicant.
Article 30. Splitting of invention applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a decision on issuance of an invention/good solution patent, the applicant may separate one or more technical solutions in the previously filed application for invention registration (initial application) into one or more new applications for invention registration (separated application). The separation of an application must comply with the following regulations:
a) The request for application splitting shall be made in writing, specifying the original application number and providing an explanation of the subject matter of protection requested in the split application and the changes in the split application compared to the original application;
b) The applicant must submit a new invention registration declaration for the separated technical solution and its description, summary, and authorization document (if the application is submitted through a representative);
c) The applicant shall pay the application fee and all fees and charges for procedures performed for the separate application outside of the procedures already performed in the initial application without having to repeat them in the separate application and is not required to pay the charge for appraisal of the request for priority right;
d) The protected subject matters in the separate application must be those included in the initial application and must differ from the protected subject matters in the initial application after separation;
dd) The separated application shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection beyond the content already disclosed in the description and shall not alter the nature of the subject matter stated in the initial application.
3. The separate application shall bear a new serial number and the filing date of the initial invention registration application and the priority dates of the initial invention registration application (if any).
4. The split-off application shall be appraised in terms of form and further processed according to procedures not yet completed with respect to the initial application. The split-off application must be published as prescribed.
5. The initial application (after being split) shall continue to be processed according to the procedures being processed if there is no change in the initial application when submitting the split application or shall be processed according to the procedures for amendment of applications as prescribed in Article 28 of this Circular if there is a change in the initial application when submitting the split application.
Article 31. Conversion of invention registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention patent or utility solution patent, applicants may convert their application for an invention patent into one for a utility solution patent or vice versa, as applicable, for the entire application or part thereof.
2. An invention registration application shall be eligible for conversion if it satisfies the following requirements:
a) The request for conversion of the application form (for the initial application) must be made in writing, clearly stating the conversion request;
b) The applicant must submit an application form specifying the initial application number and the filing fee for the conversion application as prescribed, along with the fee, charge, and a letter of authorization (if the application is submitted through a representative).
c) In case of partial conversion of an invention application, the applicant shall carry out procedures for application splitting as prescribed in Article 30 of this Circular before requesting the conversion.
3. After receiving a request for conversion of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application for conversion according to corresponding regulations but shall not repeat the procedures already carried out for the application before the conversion request is received.
The application form for conversion shall bear a new serial number and the submission date of the original application or the priority date(s) of the original application.
4. In case of a request for conversion from an application for issuance of a utility solution patent to an application for issuance of an invention patent filed after the notice of content appraisal results of intended protection title is issued, such application shall be re-appraised and the applicant shall pay fees and charges as prescribed.
5. The initial application (after conversion) shall be considered as withdrawn at the time of submission of the request for conversion of the application, except for cases where only a part of the registration application is converted in accordance with Point c Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 32. Withdrawal of applications for registration of inventions
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention/good solution patent, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the application for registration of the invention.
2. The withdrawal of an invention registration application shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative in writing. For applications filed through a representative, the written authorization must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or enclosed with the authorization letter must specify the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 2 months from the date of receipt of a declaration on withdrawal of an invention registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process such withdrawal as follows:
a) In case of withdrawal request satisfying Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal request and terminate processing of the corresponding application. The withdrawn invention registration application cannot be restored but can only be used as a basis for requesting priority right.
b) In case the withdrawal request fails to satisfy the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedges deficiencies but still fails to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal request rejection.
Article 33. Re-examination of applications for registration of inventions
1. An invention registration application shall be re-examined in the following cases:
a/ Having a written opinion of a third party opposing the intended grant of an invention patent in the notice of content appraisal results, together with legitimate evidence proving that the opposition is due to force majeure events or objective obstacles, thus making it impossible to oppose the application within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The applicant for amendment to an invention related to its nature stated in the application after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam has notified the content appraisal results, which indicate the intended grant of a protection title under Point c Clause 5 Article 28 of this Circular;
c) The invention registration application is requested to be converted in the case specified in Clause 4 Article 31 of this Circular.
2. The re-appraisal of an application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of an invention registration application according to Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complicated cases with many facts to be verified or expert opinions to be solicited, the time limit for re-appraisal may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial appraisal prescribed in Clause 2, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The appraisal contents shall be carried out in accordance with the corresponding provisions of Section 3 Chapter II of this Circular;
c) For each case specified at Point a, Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of an invention registration application shall be carried out only once for the third person.
3. After the results of the re-examination are available, the invention registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For cases specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-assessment results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue to handle them according to the provisions of this Circular;
b) For cases specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article, handle according to regulations in Article 25 hereof.
Section 5. APPLICATION FOR PROBATION AND PROCESSING OF APPLICATION FOR PROBATION
Article 34. Application for pardon

1. A PCT application includes a Vietnamese origin PCT application and a national-stage PCT application.
2. For a PCT application originating in Vietnam, the applicant may file it through the National Office of Intellectual Property or directly to the International Bureau. A PCT application filed directly to the International Bureau must be made in the language prescribed in the PCT Convention and must satisfy the formal and substantive requirements prescribed in the PCT Convention.
An application filed through the National Office of Intellectual Property must be made in English, each application shall be made in 1 copy and must satisfy the requirements on form and content specified in the PCT Convention and the applicant must pay an examination fee for preliminary form inspection, fees and charges as prescribed by the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Convention and the law on fees and charges of member countries designated in the PCT Application.
3. For a PCT application with designation or selection of Vietnam to enter the national phase, the applicant shall submit to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 31 months from the priority date (if the application requests priority rights) or from the date of submission of international applications the following documents:
a) An invention registration declaration, made according to Form No. 1 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) International application copies (if the applicant requests to be processed at the national level before the international publication date);
c) Vietnamese translation of the description and summary in the international application (the initial published version or original copy if the application has not yet been published, and the revised version and explanatory notes on the revised part, if the international application is amended under Article 19 and/or Article 34.2(b) of the PCT Treaty);
d) Authorization letter (if the application is submitted by a representative).
4. International applications with designation or/and selection of Vietnam shall have their submission date being the same as that of international applications.
Article 35. Processing of PCT applications originating in Vietnam filed through the National Office of Intellectual Property
1. After receiving a Vietnamese origin application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Collect fees for preliminary examination of the form of application;
b) Inspect the conformity of filing applications at request for security control regarding inventions according to Article 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 14 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) Notify fees as prescribed to allow applicants to transfer them to International Office and International Searching Agency in accordance with PCT treaty;
d) Inspect and process applications in accordance with the PCT treaty;
dd) Transfer the application to the International Office and the international search agency in case the application meets the preliminary requirements regarding form and fee as prescribed by national laws, is filed within the prescribed time limit, and the subject matter of protection requested in the application is not a state secret;
e) Issue a notice of refusal to perform subsequent tasks in case the application fails to meet security control requirements for inventions as prescribed in Article 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 14 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. After the Vietnamese application origin has been transferred by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to the International Office, all transactions related to the application shall be directly carried out by the applicant with the International Office or the competent authority of a member country of the PCT Convention designated in the application under the provisions of the PCT Convention.
Article 36. Processing of applications for PCT filing in the national phase
The application for national stage processing shall be processed as follows:
1. Request for priority right in the PCT Application at the national stage shall be handled in accordance with the PCT Treaty and the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Treaty. In order to enjoy the priority right, the applicant must:
a) Reaffirm the priority right in the declaration;
b) Pay fees for appraisal of priority right requests;
c/ Submitting Vietnamese versions of documents already submitted to the International Office at the request of the National Office of Intellectual Property and necessary documents according to Rule 17.1(a) of the Regulation on Implementation of the PCT Treaty.
2. The applicant may amend or supplement documents in the application. The amendment or supplementation of documents in the application must comply with the following regulations:
a) Article 28 and Article 41 of the PCT Treaty, Rules 52.1(b) and 78.1(b) of the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Treaty, Article 115 of the Intellectual Property Law and Article 28 of this Circular;
b) The written authorization or transfer of the right to file an international application (if any) must be submitted within 34 months from the priority date (if the application requests priority rights) or from the date of filing the international application;

c) Documents on amendments and supplements submitted by applicants to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese.
3. The starting time for processing a national stage PCT application is the first day of the thirty-second month from the priority date (if the application requests priority rights) or from the date of filing the international application. If the applicant submits a written request for early processing of the national stage PCT application, such application shall be processed ahead of schedule in accordance with the provisions of Article 23.2 of the PCT Treaty.
4. A national application submitted under the national filing form shall be subject to both formal and substantive examination according to procedures applicable to invention applications and published within one month from the date on which the application is considered valid, provided that such publication does not occur earlier than the international publication of the application under PCT treaty, unless otherwise requested by the applicant.
Chapter III
                         HANDLING OF APPLICATION FOR PLANNING
Section 1. APPLICATION FOR PLANNING REGISTRATION
Article 37. Applications for registration of layout designs
An application for layout design registration shall include the following documents:
1. Declaration for layout design registration, made using Form No. 6 under Appendix I attached hereto.
2. The set of photos or set of layout design drawings (if the application is submitted in paper form, 4 sets shall be submitted).
3. Semiconductor integrated circuit designs shall be produced in a layout design and consist of 4 designs if the layout design has been commercially exploited.
4. Description of an integrated circuit produced by layout design, including information on functions and structure of the semiconductor integrated circuit produced by layout design to clarify nature of the layout design.
5. A letter of authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative).
6. Documentary evidence of registration rights, if the applicant inherits such rights from another person.
7. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 38. Requirements for documents in an application for registration of layout design
1. Documents of an application for layout design registration must meet corresponding specific requirements prescribed in clauses 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 of this Article and general requirements on form as prescribed below:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm) with 20 mm margins on all sides, using the Times New Roman font size of 13 pt or larger, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b/ For a document consisting of many pages, each page must be numbered in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with fade-resistant ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents have 2 pages or more, each page must bear a signature or a seal of the applicant/issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if minor spelling errors are found, the applicant may correct them and must affix a signature (and seal, if any) at the correction location; the signature and seal must not obscure the content of the document.
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system must be in “.pdf” format; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; the applicant may use a digital signature to directly sign on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as color photos in the case of submission through the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not use local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers used for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 5, 6, and 7 Article 37 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) Documents of the application must ensure that all mandatory information and consistent information are included.
2. The layout design registration application shall ensure consistency as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The declaration for layout design registration must be fully completed with information as required in appropriate places in the declaration. In case the applicant requests to keep information confidential, the documents to be kept confidential must be clearly stated in the declaration.
4. The set of photos and drawings of layout design must fully show the spatial structure of components and their interconnections in semi-conducting closed circuits so that based on such set of photos and drawings, a unique layout design can be identified and determined. For the above-mentioned purposes, the set of photos and drawings of layout design must satisfy the conditions specified in clauses 5, 6 and 7 of this Article.
5. The set of photos and drawings of layout design must include at least one of the following three types of documents:
a) Layout drawings made by CAD for each integrated circuit layer;
b) Drawings or photos of photolithically carved masks used for producing each integrated circuit layer;
c) Photographs of each layer of layout design shown in the integrated circuit.
6. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the applicant shall submit a set of photos and a set of layout design drawings enclosed with supplementary documents being objects containing electronic data of part or all of such set of photos and layout design drawings.
7. Forms of set photos and set drawings of layout design:
a) The set of photos and drawings of layout design shall include separate photos and drawings for each class of layout design, with symbols for each class, size of integrated circuit and magnification;
b) All photos and drawings of layout design must be taken under the same scale. For paper documents, each photo or drawing of layout design must be a magnified copy at least 20 times the size of the integrated circuit so that the basic circuit can be seen with the naked eye;
c) Each photo or drawing of layout design may be presented on an A4 paper sheet or a larger paper sheet, provided that it is folded into an A4 size;
d) The layout photos and drawings must be clear and sharp.
8. The submitted semiconductor integrated circuit (IC) design must be the whole or a part of the IC corresponding to the layout design stated in the application. If the IC is an integral part of another product, the accompanying product must be accompanied by documents indicating the exact part of the IC designed for such purpose. In case the layout design has been commercially exploited anywhere in the world before the application submission date, the applicant must submit the first commercially exploited sample.
9. Description of a semi-conducting integrated circuit manufactured by layout design shall include the following detailed information about the semi-conducting integrated circuit manufactured by layout design to be protected:
a) Name/code: is a collection of letters and/or numbers used to distinguish this integrated circuit from other integrated circuits when being put on the market;
b) Describe basic functions of the integrated circuit (e.g., memory function, logic function, or other functions);
c) Description of fundamental structure of integrated circuit (e.g., bipolar or MOS, BiMOS, optoelectronic or other structures);
d) Description of technology for manufacturing integrated circuits (e.g. TTL, DTL, ECL, ILT, CMOS, NMOS, PMOS or other technologies);
dd) Describe main characteristics distinguishing from other integrated semiconductor circuits on the market at the time of filing or at the time of first commercial exploitation in the world, whichever is earlier.
10. Documents requesting confidentiality must be separated into a package of confidential documents and may be submitted in the following forms:
a) Microfilm or similar form of documents showing size of design on computer drawings;
b) Electronic data;
c) Drawings or photos of parts that are not visible, provided that the layout design’s characteristics are basically visible.
The maximum level of confidentiality for layout designs not yet commercially exploited shall be 50% of the surface area of each layer; for layout designs already commercially exploited, it shall be 2 layers out of 5 layers from top to bottom.
Article 39. Confidentiality of information in layout design registration applications The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and organizations and individuals involved in the process of receiving and processing layout design registration applications shall be obliged to keep confidential information in such applications that the applicant requests to be kept confidential as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 38 of this Circular.

Article 40. Receipt of applications for layout design registration

1. An application for registration of a layout design shall be received by the National Office of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of its receipt, unless otherwise agreed by the applicant.

Article 41. Format appraisal of layout design registration applications

c/ Having grounds to affirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly ineligible for protection under Articles 8 and 69 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

In case there are grounds (information and evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 1 month from the date of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming the lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (documents proving inheritance rights, documents proving the transfer of application submission rights; assignment contracts or labor contracts), etc. After the above-mentioned time limit, if the applicant fails to submit additional documents satisfying the requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents in the application.

a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the Certificate of registered layout design and the reasons for refusal or deficiencies in the application, and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinion or correct deficiencies.

c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant has sufficiently corrected errors or provided a legitimate explanation within the prescribed time limit, within 1 month from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant a layout design registration certificate.

In case an application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative working hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date specified in this clause shall be the next working day.

A copy of a certificate of registered layout design made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in case the applicant requests issuance of copies (specified in the declaration for registration of layout design) for other co-owners.

Article 45. Applications for registration of industrial designs
An application for registration of an industrial design shall include the following documents:
1. An application form for registration of industrial design, made according to Form No. 3 in Appendix I of this Circular.
2. Documents identifying an industrial design to be protected are a set of photos or drawings of the industrial design (4 sets of photos or drawings shall be submitted in paper applications).
3. Description of industrial design.
4. A letter of authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative).
5. Documentary evidence of registration rights, if the applicant inherits such rights from another person.
6. Documents proving priority rights if priority rights are claimed include:
a) A copy of the application or the first applications of the receiving authority;
b) The conveyance of priority right if such right is acquired from another person.
7. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 46. Requirements on documents in an application for registration of industrial designs
1. Documents of an industrial design registration application must meet corresponding specific requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article and general requirements on form as prescribed below:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm) with 20 mm margins on all sides, using the Times New Roman font size of 13 pt or larger, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b/ Each document other than a set of photos or drawings of industrial designs shall be numbered in Arabic numerals for each page thereof;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with fade-resistant ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents have 2 pages or more, each page must bear a signature or a seal affixed by the applicant or the issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if minor spelling errors are found, the applicant may correct them and must affix a signature (and seal, if applicable) at the correction location. The signature and seal must not obscure the content of the documents.
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system must be in the “.pdf” format, except for sets of photos, drawings of industrial designs, and documents that may be submitted in common image file formats (such as “.jpg” or “.png”, etc.); the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; the individual signing the documents of the industrial design registration application may use a digital signature to sign directly on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as a color photo in the case of submission via the application.

dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not use local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers used for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 4, 5, 6, and 7 Article 45 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure complete and consistent mandatory information.
2. An industrial design registration application shall ensure uniformity as prescribed in clauses 3 and 5 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In case an application for protection of an industrial design covers the whole product or part of a product accompanied by one or more variants of such industrial design, these variants must not be significantly different from the basic variant and each other.
In which, products are understood as follows:
a) Physical products are tangible objects such as articles, tools, equipment and means, which are produced by industrial or handicraft methods, have clear structures and functions;
b) “Non-physical product” means an object which does not exist in a tangible material form and is created by projecting or displaying a pattern on a surface or into the environment (including air), and has practical functions for operation, interaction, or performance of specific tasks;
c) Part of the product means the upper part of the product requested for protection by the applicant, including detachable and non-detachable parts;
d/ The detachable part of a product means a product component which can be independently circulated for assembly or combination into a product if it is registered independently and is also collectively called product, except for special regulations.
3. The industrial design registration declaration must be fully completed with information as required in appropriate places and meet the following requirements:
a) In the field "Name of industrial design", the name of the product bearing the industrial design must be written in brief common words, not for advertising purposes, without commercial signs, annotations or indications;
b) In the section "International classification of industrial designs", the applicant must specify the international classification of the industrial design for which protection is requested in accordance with the List of International Classification of Industrial Designs (according to the Locarno Agreement on International Classification of Industrial Designs). If the applicant fails to classify or incorrectly classify, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify and the applicant must pay a classification fee as prescribed;
c) In the field of application of the industrial design, specify the particular field of application of the product with such industrial design, clearly stating the purpose, utility and function of such product;
d) In the field "Type of industrial design", select one of the two fields "Industrial design of the whole product" or "Industrial design of a part of the product". In case of industrial design of a part of the product, clearly indicate the method of expression of the protection request shown in the set of photos and drawings.
4. The industrial design photographing and drawing department must satisfy the following requirements:
a) The industrial design photo and drawing must fully express the design characteristics of the industrial design to be protected to the extent that an average person with knowledge in the relevant field may identify such industrial design. An average person with knowledge in the relevant field as prescribed at this point is a person who has common practical skills and knows general and popular knowledge in the relevant field;
b) The set of photos or drawings must present the subject matter of the protection request in a uniform and accurate manner. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the set of photos or drawings must be printed or affixed on an unbound A4 sheet of paper;
c) The photos or drawings must be clear and sharp; the background color of the photos or drawings must be uniform and contrast with the color of the protected product; on the photos or drawings, only the product with the industrial design requested for protection shall be shown (not accompanied by other products), except for the cases specified in Points k and l of this Clause; they must not contain technical drawing instructions or explanations about the industrial design, except for brief and necessary instructions to indicate cross-sections or enlarged figures;
d) The photographing and drawing must clearly show the protected parts and unprotected parts of the product in one of the following ways:

d1) The protected area is represented by continuous lines while the unprotected area is represented by interrupted lines; d2) The protected area is represented by contrasting colors with the unprotected area;
dd) The photos and drawings must show the industrial design in the same scale. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the product shape with the industrial design shall not be larger than 190 mm x 277 mm and its largest side length shall not be smaller than 120 mm;
e) The photos and drawings must show the industrial design in the same orientation and be numbered sequentially as follows: three-dimensional perspective views of the industrial design, views of the industrial design from the front, back, right, left, top and bottom; the views must be frontal;
g) Photographs or diagrams identical or symmetrical with existing photographs or diagrams, photographs or diagrams of the bottom surface of products with large size and weight, photographs or diagrams of products with excessively thin thickness, photographs or diagrams of the surface with excessively thin thickness of industrial designs, photographs or diagrams of parts not visible in protection requests which are not required to be included in the application, provided that such parts must be clearly stated in the list of photographs and drawings included in the description;
h) For industrial designs of the whole product or part of the product which can be developed in a planar form (e.g. containers, packages, etc.), projections of such industrial designs may be replaced by photos or drawings of the industrial design in the developed state;
i) Depending on the complexity of the industrial design, additional photos or perspective drawings from different angles, cross-sections, enlarged views, separate details or parts of the product, photos or drawings illustrating the installation and use of components on the complete product in order to clarify the nature and design characteristics of the industrial design may be required (which are not used to establish industrial property rights to the industrial design of such components or parts);
k) For the industrial design of the whole product, photos or drawings of each part or component on the product may be provided but only for the purpose of illustration and not to establish industrial property rights to such industrial design of the part or component;
l) The photos or drawings must show the industrial design in the selected state of use; photos or drawings showing other states may be provided to clarify the nature of the industrial design. In case the non-physical product's industrial design has a change in shape or displacement, this effect can be shown by a series of sequential images, each image in the series shows a successive state of the change process;
m) For an application containing multiple plans, the basic plan must be presented first. Each plan of an industrial design must be presented in a set of photos and drawings as prescribed in this clause; the photos and drawings must be numbered to indicate the order of the corresponding plan and the order of such photos and drawings within that plan;
n) For a set of products, there must be a composite image of the entire set of products and a set of photos or drawings of each product in the set as prescribed in this Clause.
5. An industrial design description must include the following contents:
a) List all basic design features expressing the nature of the industrial design, suitable for the design features shown in the set of photos and drawings as follows:

a1) Design features of the industrial design to be protected shall be listed including: three-dimensional features, lines, correlation between three-dimensional features and/or lines, color features (if any), shape, layout, proportion, display order and visual effects of graphic elements on non-physical products;

a2) For products with different usage states (e.g., products with a lid or can be folded, etc.), list the basic design features of the product in different states;

a3) If the industrial design includes multiple variants, specify the distinctive design features of each variant compared to the basic variant;

a4) If the industrial design is a set of products, list the basic design features of each product in the set;
b) Closest similar industrial designs (if any): clearly state whether or not there is a similar industrial design; if there is, specify the industrial design that is least different from the industrial design of the same type of product stated in the application and which is widely known before the filing date or the priority date (if the application requests priority rights), specifying the source of information disclosing such closest similar industrial design;
c) List of photos or drawings: list three-dimensional photograms, projections, cross-sections, etc. of industrial designs in the order of their serial numbers.

1. The formal examination of an industrial design application according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the inspection of compliance with regulations on form of the application in order to conclude whether the application is valid or not.
2. An industrial design registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Article 86 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming the lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (documents proving inheritance rights, documents proving the transfer of application submission rights; assignment contracts or labor contracts, etc.). After the above-mentioned time limit, if the applicant fails to submit additional documents satisfying the requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents in the application.

c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant has made satisfactory corrections to deficiencies or provided legitimate reasons for rejection within the prescribed time limit, the application shall be considered valid in terms of form and shall be forwarded for publication and content appraisal as prescribed. In case the request for priority rights is rejected, the application shall still be considered valid and shall be forwarded for publication as prescribed.
5. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of industrial design registration applications that are transferred for publication, specifying the application number, filing date and date of transfer for publication of each application.

In case an application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative working hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date specified in this clause shall be the next working day.
7. The priority date of an industrial design registration application shall be determined as follows:
a/ If an application does not request priority right or although it requests priority right, it is not approved by the National Office of Industrial Property, such application shall be regarded as having no priority date;
b) If the application requests priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the National Office of Intellectual Property;
c) The priority date determined according to the request for priority rights on the basis of the first application(s) submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
8. In case an applicant requests to enjoy priority right but at the time of filing the application for registration of industrial design there is no document proving the priority right, the examination of the application shall start from the time the National Office of Industrial Property receives the supplementary documents or end after 3 months as prescribed in Clause 4, Article 47 of this Circular.

Contents of disclosure of an industrial design registration application in the Industrial Property Gazette include the following information: applicant, representative (if any); application number, filing date, priority date (if any); name and nationality of the author of the industrial design; information on origin of the application (split application, initial application number of split application, etc.); set of photos or drawings of the industrial design; number of schemes/products requested for protection; international classification of the industrial design; and other information (if any).

Article 50. Substantive examination of applications for registration of industrial designs
1. Content appraisal of an industrial design registration application according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application for the protection conditions and determination of the corresponding protection scope according to this Article.
2. The assessment of the ability to grant protection titles for industrial design registration applications includes:
a) Assess the conformity of the subject matter stated in the application for registration of industrial design with the requirements for issuance of an industrial design patent according to Article 52 of this Circular;
b) Evaluate subject matters stated in the industrial design registration application according to each protection condition as prescribed in Articles 54, 55 and 56 of this Circular. The evaluation shall be carried out for each product (if the application mentions a set of products); in case it mentions multiple solutions, evaluate each solution one by one, starting from the basic solution (the first solution stated in the application);
c) Assess the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 57 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for a title of protection under Point b Clause 2 of this Article shall be completed after the subject matter of the industrial design mentioned in the application has been assessed with all conditions for protection and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter does not meet or meets the protection conditions, specifically:
a) A reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy one or several or all of the protection conditions; or b) No reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Before issuing a notice of content appraisal results for issuance of an industrial design patent as prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 58 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 57 of this Circular.
5. Based on the results of assessment of the ability to grant protection titles prescribed in clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding expected protection scope.
6. Notification of content appraisal results for issuance of industrial design patents specified in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 58 of this Circular shall be made for the following applications for registration of industrial designs:
a) The application does not fall under the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application has the earliest submission date or priority date among applications for registration of industrial designs falling within the cases specified in Clause 1, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Application as agreed in Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. Applications for registration of industrial designs not falling into the cases specified in clause 6 of this article shall be processed as follows:
a) The grant of an industrial design patent shall be refused because the application does not satisfy the first-to-file rule if it has the earliest filing date or priority date; or

b) The grant of an industrial design patent shall be considered as for applications with the earliest filing date or priority date and treated as in the cases specified in clause 6 of this article if all other industrial design registration applications with earlier filing dates or priority dates are refused to be granted, withdrawn or deemed to be withdrawn.
8. During the process of content appraisal, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations for the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify the deficiencies.
Article 51. Minimum information sources
1. The purpose of information lookup is to search among the mandatory information sources for industrial designs identical or similar to those stated in the application. Mandatory information sources used during the content appraisal of the application include the following documents:
a) Industrial design registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and having an earlier publication date than the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights);
b) Applications for registration of industrial designs and protection titles of industrial designs filed by other organizations or countries within 25 years before the filing date or the priority date of the application being appraised (if the application is eligible for priority), which are kept in the existing database on industrial designs at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) Other information relating to industrial designs collected and stored by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
d) The industrial design registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, international industrial design registration applications with Vietnam designated as the country of origin and with an earlier submission date or priority date (if the application is eligible for priority rights), or with the same submission date or priority date as the application currently undergoing appraisal (for checking the first-to-file rule prescribed in Article 57 of this Circular).
2. Where necessary and possible, information may be searched more extensively than the minimum compulsory sources specified in Clause 1 of this Article, such as the national database on inventions and marks and other accessible sources of information.

3. The search results must be presented in the search report, specifying the field searched, the scope of search, and the search results within that scope (statistics and identification of industrial designs for comparison, information sources, and publication dates of corresponding information) and the full name of the person preparing the report (the searcher).
In which, “industrial design for comparison” means an industrial design identical or similar to the industrial design stated in the application and compared with the industrial design stated in the application when assessing the novelty and creativity.
Article 52. Assessment of conformity of subject matters of industrial design registration applications
1. Objects mentioned in an industrial design registration application shall be considered incompatible with the industrial design patent if such objects are not the external appearance of the whole product or a part of the product.
Exterior appearance of a whole product or part of a product means a collection of design features (shapes, lines, colors or combination thereof) of the whole product or part of the product which can be seen during the use of the product. In this context, use of the product means putting such products into use according to their functions and utility, excluding maintenance, repair or servicing of the products. For an industrial design of a detachable part registered independently, the visibility shall be assessed on the basis of the product assembled from such part.
2. The entities mentioned in the application as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article are:
a) The external appearance of the entire product or part of the product that is required by its technical characteristics;
b/ Exterior appearance of civil or industrial construction works, except for the exterior appearance of separate modules or units which may be used independently or assembled together to form construction works such as shops, kiosks, mobile houses or similar products.
Article 53. Assessment of distinctiveness of industrial designs The assessment of distinctiveness between two industrial designs shall comply with the following provisions:
1. Two industrial designs shall be regarded as identical when they are used for products of the same type and have the same collection of fundamental and non-fundamental design characteristics.
2. Two industrial designs shall be considered not significantly different from each other when they are used for products of the same type and have the same collection of basic design characteristics.
3. Two industrial designs shall be regarded as similar when they are used for products of the same type and have at least one basic design feature which is identical or insignificantly different from each other.
4. Two industrial designs among similar industrial designs shall be regarded as the closest to each other when they have the same number of basic design characteristics or are not significantly different from each other most compared with all other similar industrial designs.
5. Two industrial designs shall be considered significantly different from each other when they are used for products of different types or for products of the same type but have at least one basic design feature which is different.
6. Design characteristics of an industrial design shall be understood as follows:
a) Design characteristics of an industrial design means elements expressed in the form of lines, three-dimensional shapes, colors, position correlation or size correlation of the protection request on the overall product, shape, layout arrangement, proportion correlation, display order and visual effects of graphic elements on non-physical products so that when combined with other characteristics (signs), they constitute such industrial design;
b) Fundamental design features are those which can be easily recognized or remembered and which are necessary and sufficient to determine the nature of an industrial design and distinguish one industrial design from another for products of the same type.
Products of the same type are products with identical or similar uses or functions. Products assembled from different types of parts and parts used for assembly thereof are products of different types.
c) The following elements shall not be considered fundamental design features of an industrial design:

c1) Shapes and lines determined by the technical function of the product (e.g., the flat shape of a data recording disc determined by the relative movement between the disc and the disc reader, etc.);

c2) Elements whose presence in the set of signs is insufficient to cause aesthetic impression (the appearance of the product remains unchanged when such elements are present or absent; for example, a change in a familiar shape or line but such change is insufficient to be recognized, so the changed shape or line is still only recognized as the old one);

c3) Materials used for manufacturing the product;

c4) Information displayed inside non-physical products (such as texts, data, etc.); signs affixed or stuck on the product, etc., for the purpose of providing information or instructions on the origin, characteristics, composition, uses, usage method, etc. of such product; for example, information on goods labels (such as manufacturers, commercial indications, origin, barcodes, etc.), trademarks, geographical indications, etc.;

c5) Size of the product, except for changes in decorative sizes of fabric samples and similar materials;

c6) Invisible design features during the use of the product. For an industrial design of a detachable part registered independently, the determination of invisible design features must be considered on the product assembled from such part;

c7) Other elements that fail to meet the conditions specified at Point b of this Clause.
Article 54. Assessment of novelty
1. In order to evaluate the novelty of an industrial design stated in an application, it is required to compare a collection of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial design with a collection of fundamental design characteristics of each control industrial design found during the information search.
2. An industrial design stated in an application shall be regarded as new if:
a) The industrial design is not found in the mandatory information source; or b) Although the industrial design is found in the mandatory information source, the industrial design stated in the application is considered significantly different from the industrial designs mentioned in the mandatory information source; or c) The industrial design mentioned in the application is the same as the industrial design announced or disclosed and falls under the cases specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 65 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 55. Assessment of creativity
1. To evaluate the creativity of an industrial design mentioned in an application, it is required to compare a collection of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial design with a collection of fundamental design characteristics of control industrial designs found during the information lookup process.
2. Industrial designs stated in applications shall be deemed to be creative if they do not fall into the following cases:
a) Industrial design means a simple combination of known design features (publicly disclosed design features arranged or simply assembled together such as replacement, change of position, increase or decrease in quantity, etc.);
b) Industrial design means a form which is a copy or imitation of part or the whole of the inherent natural forms of trees, fruits, animals, etc., and the forms of known geometric figures (circle, ellipse, triangle, square, rectangle, regular polygon, prism with cross-section being one of the above-mentioned figures, etc.);
c) Industrial design means a mere reproduction or imitation of the whole or part of the appearance of products or works which are well known or recognized in Vietnam or in the world;
d) Industrial designs which simulate industrial designs in other fields if such simulation has been known in practice (e.g. toys simulating cars, motorcycles, etc.).
Article 56. Assessment of industrial applicability
1. An industrial design stated in an application shall be deemed to have industrial applicability if, based on information about such industrial design presented in the application, a person with average knowledge in the relevant field can use it as a model to create a batch of products with the external appearance or part of the external appearance being such industrial design by manufacturing physical products by industrial or handicraft methods or by uniformly recreating non-physical products.
2. In the following cases, the subject matter stated in the application shall be deemed incapable of industrial application:
a) The subject matter stated in the application is the form of the entire product or a part of the product with an undetermined existence status (products in solid forms, liquids, interfaces with images displayed randomly, etc.);
b) The whole product or part of the product having the shape of the object stated in the application can be created only with special skills, or it is impossible to repeatedly create the whole product or part of the product having the shape of the object stated in the application;
c) Other cases with other legitimate reasons.
Article 57. Evaluation of first-to-file principle
For applications for registration of industrial designs which have been concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing notices of content appraisal results with the intended grant of industrial design patents according to the provisions at Point a, and Point d2, Clause 3, Article 58 of this Circular, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:
1. To verify the first-to-file principle, information must be searched in the mandatory sources specified in Point d Clause 1 Article 51 of this Circular.
2. Searching is to find industrial design registration applications of products of the same type identical or insignificantly different from each other or to find industrial design registration applications of products containing parts with industrial designs identical or insignificantly different from registered industrial designs or to find industrial design registration applications of products including parts with external appearance identical or insignificantly different from registered industrial designs and determine the application submission date or the earliest priority date.
3. If there are many applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, an industrial design patent may only be granted to a valid application with the earliest filing date or priority date among applications satisfying the conditions for the grant of an industrial design patent.
4. Among applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article for registration of products of the same type, if there are many applications with the same filing date or the earliest priority date, an industrial design patent may only be granted to one application among them as agreed upon by all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all such applications shall be rejected from the grant of industrial design patents.
Article 58. Issuance and refusal to issue industrial design patents
1. An application for registration of an industrial design shall be rejected for grant of an industrial design patent in the cases specified at Points a, b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, and Clause 1b, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. An industrial design registration application shall be granted an industrial design patent when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For industrial design registration applications falling into cases specified in clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Clause 1b, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the title of protection and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the title of protection, the fee for publication of the decision on issuance of the title of protection, and the registration fee for the decision on issuance of the title of protection;
b) If the subject matter stated in the application for registration of industrial design does not satisfy the protection conditions or the application falls into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Clause 1b, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the protection title, all reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application, providing guidance on amendments to the protection scope (volume) and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide objections or correct deficiencies;
c/ In case the applicant fails to correct errors or does not make any objection within the time limit prescribed at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days after the expiry of such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;
d) In case the applicant rectifies deficiencies or provides objections within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the rectified document or the objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the examination as follows:

d1) If the rectification is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustifiable, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;

d2) If the rectification is satisfactory or the objection is justifiable and the application is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content examination results, stating the intended grant of the protection title and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of the protection title, and the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title;
dd) If the applicant fails to fully pay the fee for issuance of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on issuance of a protection title or the registration fee for issuance of a protection title within the time limit specified at Points a and d2 of this Clause, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse the issuance of a protection title within 15 working days from the date of expiry of the corresponding time limit;
e) If the applicant fully and punctually pays all fees and charges within the time limit specified at Points a and d2 of this Clause, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall issue a decision to grant a protection title within 1 month from the date of full payment of fees and charges.
4. The decision on grant of an industrial design patent shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance. Published contents shall include information stated in the corresponding decision (including decision number and date) and other contents as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 49 of this Circular.
5. Industrial design patents made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued as electronic documents and paper documents (if the applicant requests a paper document). In case industrial design rights are under joint ownership, an industrial design patent shall only be granted to the first person on the list of co-applicants.
A copy of an industrial design patent made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests issuance of a copy (specified in the declaration for registration of industrial designs) for other co-owners.
Section 4. HANDLING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO INDUSTRIAL DESIGN REGISTRATION
Article 59. Handling of objections to applications for registration of industrial designs
1. Within 3 months after an industrial design registration application is published, any third party may object to the grant of a patent for such industrial design registration application and shall pay fees and charges.
Objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese accompanied by documents or sources of information to prove. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. In case an objection to an industrial design registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process such objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 1 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response.
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the objector and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) On the basis of information, evidence and arguments provided by the parties, results of the dialogue (if any) as prescribed in Points a, b and c of this Clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the result of processing the objection together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application to the objector, except for the case specified at Point dd of this Clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the opposition of the applicant about the withdrawal of the application for registration of industrial design according to Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 01 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of the application.
3. If the objection of the objector is related to industrial design registration rights, such objection shall be handled as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector to file a lawsuit petition to the competent Court in accordance with civil procedure law and impose a time limit of 02 months from the notification date for the objector to submit a copy of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit;
b) Upon expiry of the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if the objector fails to submit copies of the notice of acceptance of the case or his/her objection, such objection shall be deemed withdrawn and the application for registration of industrial design shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the industrial design registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the Court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the Court.
4. The time limit for applicants to respond to objections and the time limit for objectors to respond to applicants’ opinions as prescribed in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal.
Article 60. Amendment to applications for registration of industrial designs
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may take initiative or based on notification of appraisal results to amend documents in the application for registration of industrial designs.
2. Requests for amendments to applications for registration of industrial designs shall include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment to application by applicant, request for amendment shall be made using Form No. 10 under Appendix I hereof;
b) In case of amendment to the application on the basis of notification of appraisal results from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, such amendment shall be made in writing with contents of amendment specified;
c) The applicant may request amendments to the same content related to multiple applications for registration of industrial designs in a single declaration or a single written request for amendments;
d) In case of amendments to photos, drawings or descriptions, applicants shall submit the amended photos/drawings (4 sets for paper applications) or amended descriptions enclosed with detailed explanations of the amendments compared to the original documents submitted;
dd) In case of change to name or address or country code of the applicant, the applicant must submit a certification document (original or certified true copy) or legal document (certified true copy) proving such change (decision on change of name or address; certificate of enterprise registration which records the change of name or address, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for actively extracting and using information available in databases announced by database management agencies to replace documents used for processing administrative procedures. In case where information cannot be extracted or is not fully and accurately extracted, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to individuals or organizations to supplement documents and clearly state reasons. The time limit for requesting and supplementing documents shall not be included in the time limit for processing administrative procedures;
e) In case of change of industrial property representatives, applicants shall submit documents declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representatives and change of new industrial property representatives as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular;
3. The applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee for each amendment and a fee for disclosure of amendments to the application if such amendments must be disclosed under Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except where such amendments are made to correct errors of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Any amendment to an industrial design registration application must not expand the scope (quantity) of protection already disclosed in the set of photos, drawings and description stated in the application and must not alter the nature of the subject matter of the industrial design stated in the application.
5. In case of amendment or supplementation of the application by the applicant, the request for amendment or supplementation shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment to the application satisfies the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendment to the application (except in cases where the amendment to the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant an industrial design patent due to invalidity of the application).
Amendments and supplements shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette if amendments or supplements to information related to a valid application in terms of form include the name, nationality and address of the author of the industrial design, set of photos or drawings of the industrial design;
b) If the request for amendment to the application fails to satisfy the requirements specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application, clearly stating the reasons therefor and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of such notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opposing opinions. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, no opposing opinions, or provides inadequate opposing opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application;
c) In case of requesting amendments to applications related to the nature of industrial designs included in sets of photos, drawings or descriptions filed after receiving notices of content appraisal results and intended issuance of industrial design patents, such applications shall be re-appraised and applicants shall pay fees as prescribed.
6. In case of amendment or supplementation of an application by the applicant on the basis of notification of appraisal results from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the acceptance or rejection of such amendment or supplementation shall be made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the notification of appraisal results or corresponding decision during the processing of the industrial design registration application.
7. In case the application is amended or supplemented by the applicant and supplemented on the basis of notification of appraisal results, such amendment or supplementation shall be made in the form of an application form as prescribed in Point a Clause 2 of this Article. The handling of amendments or supplements to the application in this case shall comply with the provisions of Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 61. Recording of changes to applicants for registration of industrial designs
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may request the recognition of changes in the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, or according to a decision of a competent authority.
2. The request for recognition of changes to the applicant must meet the following requirements:
a) Request to record changes in applicants using Form No. 11 under Appendix I hereof;
b) In case of recording changes to applicants due to transfer of applications, applicants shall submit documents showing transfer of industrial design registration applications (original copies or certified true copies), including name and address of the transferring party and the transferee; number of transferred applications or information sufficient to identify such applications;
c) In case of recording change of applicant due to inheritance or inheritance or according to decision of competent authority, the applicant must submit documents proving related to inheritance or inheritance of assets upon consolidation, merger, division, separation of legal entity, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entity of same owner, business format conversion or according to decision of Court or other competent authority or inheritance due to other applicants giving up registration rights;
d) The applicant must pay an appraisal fee for requesting recognition of changes to the applicant and a fee for announcing information on recognition of changes to the applicant;
dd) The request for recognition of changes due to transfer of many applications from the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that an appraisal fee is paid according to regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for recognition of transfer.
3. The request for recognition of changes in applicants shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for change of applicant satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of change of applicant and publish it in the Industrial Property Gazette (except for cases where the amendment or supplementation of the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant an industrial design patent due to invalidation of the application in terms of form);
b) If the request for change of applicant fails to satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize such change and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate corrections, no objection, or provides inadequate objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to recognize the change of the applicant.
Article 62. Splitting of applications for registration of industrial designs
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may take initiative or based on the notification of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam of the appraisal results, separate one or more than one industrial design in the previously filed application for registration of industrial designs (initial application) into a new application for registration of industrial designs (separated application).
2. The applicant shall submit a new declaration for registration of separated industrial designs. The separated application shall bear a new serial number and the submission date of the initial industrial design registration application or the priority dates of the initial industrial design registration application (if any).
The applicant shall pay the application fee and all fees and charges for procedures performed for the separate application outside the initial application which are not required to be repeated in the separate application and is not required to pay the charge for appraisal of the request for priority right, except for cases where the application separation must be performed because the initial application does not meet the requirement of application uniformity.
3. For the initial application, the applicant must amend and supplement the application according to Article 60 of this Circular.
4. The initial application and the separate application shall be processed as follows:
a/ The split-off application shall be appraised for form and further processed according to the unfinished procedures for the initial application. The split-off application shall be published under regulations.

b) The initial application shall continue to be processed in accordance with Article 60 of this Circular.
Article 63. Withdrawal of applications for registration of industrial designs
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may withdraw the application for registration of the industrial design.
2. The withdrawal of an industrial design registration application shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative in writing. For applications filed through a representative, the written authorization must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or be enclosed with a command specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 2 months from the date of receipt of the declaration on withdrawal of the industrial design registration application or request for withdrawal of the application, the applicant shall submit the following documents:
a) In case of withdrawal request satisfying the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal request and terminate the processing of the application. The withdrawn industrial design registration application cannot be re-registered but can be used as a basis for requesting priority rights.
b) In case the withdrawal request fails to satisfy the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedges deficiencies but still fails to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal request rejection.
Article 64. Re-examination of applications for registration of industrial designs
1. The National Office of Industrial Property shall carry out the re-examination of an industrial design registration application in the following cases:
a/ Having a written opinion of a third party opposing the intended grant of an industrial design patent in the notice of content appraisal results, together with legitimate evidence proving that the opposition is due to force majeure events or objective obstacles, thus making it impossible to oppose the application within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The applicant for amendment to the application after notification of the results of the content appraisal, which states the intended issuance of the title of protection as prescribed in Point c Clause 5 Article 60 of this Circular.
2. The re-appraisal of an application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of an industrial design registration application as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complicated cases with many facts to be verified or expert opinions to be solicited, the time limit for re-appraisal may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial appraisal prescribed in Clause 2, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The appraisal contents shall be carried out in accordance with the corresponding provisions of Section 3 Chapter IV of this Circular;
c) For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of an industrial design registration application shall be carried out only once for a third person.
3. After the results of the re-examination are available, the application for registration of industrial designs shall be processed as follows:
a) For cases specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-assessment results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue to handle them according to the provisions of this Circular;
b) For cases specified in point b clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application according to regulations in Article 58 of this Circular.
Section 5. LAWSUITS AND PROCESSING OF LAWSUITS
Article 65. La Haye letters

1. A La Haye letter includes a La Haye letter addressed to Vietnam and a La Haye letter originating in Vietnam.
2. For a La Hay formula application of Vietnamese origin, the applicant may file it through the National Office of Intellectual Property or directly to the International Bureau. An application filed with the International Bureau must be made in the language prescribed in the Hague Agreement and must satisfy the formal and substantive requirements prescribed in the Hague Agreement.
3. The Hague formula applications filed through the National Office of Intellectual Property must be made in English (each application shall be made into 02 copies for paper applications) and must satisfy the requirements on form and content specified in the Hague formula and the applicant must pay international application transfer fees, charges and fees under the Hague formula and the laws on charges and fees of designated member countries.

4. For a Hague-Addis Ababa Convention application with Vietnam's designation, the filing date shall be the international registration date.
Article 66. Processing of applications originating in Vietnam filed through the National Office of Intellectual Property
1. In case of an application for registration of a Vietnam-based La Hay domain name submitted through the National Office of Intellectual Property, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Collect international application transfer fees;
b) Notify the fee payable by the applicant directly to the International Office in accordance with the Hague Agreement within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the application;
c/ To conduct preliminary examination of the form of the application within 12 working days after receiving it;
d) In case the application contains errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of such errors and impose a time limit of 10 working days from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify them;
dd) Transfer the La Hay application originating in Vietnam to the International Office within 01 month from the date of receipt of the application.
2. The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the application originating in Vietnam shall be considered as the filing date for international registration of industrial designs if the international office receives such application within 1 month from the date specified in the receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
3. After the application for registration of a mark in the Hague system originated from Vietnam has been filed with an International Office, all transactions related to such application shall be conducted directly by the applicant with such International Office or the competent body of a country which is designated in the application under the Hague Agreement.
Article 67. Processing of La Hay application with Vietnam designation
After receiving the notification from the International Office, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall handle the Hague Application under the designation of Vietnam as prescribed as follows:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct a substantive examination of an application like that for registration of industrial designs filed under the national filing format, except for the cases specified in clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 9 of this Article. Within 6 months from the date on which the application is published by the International Bureau, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conclude on the possibility of protection of the industrial design stated in the application.
2. In case the industrial design in the application satisfies the protection conditions prescribed by Vietnamese law and the application has no flaws, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Before the expiration of the 6-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to accept protection for the internationally registered industrial design stated in the application, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of International Industrial Designs Registered), and send it to the International Office for protection declaration for the internationally registered industrial design using the form provided by the International Office;
b/ Publicizing the decision in the Industrial Property Gazette within one month after its issuance.
3. In case an industrial design fails to satisfy the protection conditions or the application contains deficiencies (such as insufficient photos/drawings which fail to fully disclose the design features of the industrial design, or international registration fails to satisfy Vietnam’s declaration, or information requires verification, etc.), before the end of the 6-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal using the form of the International Office, clearly stating the reasons for refusal, and send such notice to the International Office.
4. In case an industrial design is registered ineligible for protection or the application contains deficiencies with respect to certain industrial designs (lack of photos/drawings which cause the set of photos/drawings not fully disclose the design features of the industrial design or the international registration fails to satisfy the declaration of Vietnam or information needs to be verified, etc.), before the end of the 6-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Send a notice of refusal to International Office using the form provided by the International Office, specifying the reasons for refusal and send it to the International Office;
b) Issue a decision to grant protection for industrial designs which satisfy the protection conditions and are flawless, record them in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part on International Registration of Industrial Designs), and send them to the International Office of the Protection Declaration for International Registration of Industrial Designs, using the form of the International Office, clearly stating the industrial design granted protection;
c/ Publicizing the decision in the Industrial Property Gazette within one month after its issuance.

5. Within 03 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of rejection according to Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article, applicants may correct errors or oppose the decision. The correction of errors or opposition of the decision in the notice shall be carried out according to procedures similar to those applicable to applications for registration of industrial designs submitted under national regulations, including regulations on the method of application submission.
In case a La Hay application is planned to be rejected due to failure to meet the requirements for uniformity of the application as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 5 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the applicant may remedy such deficiencies by requesting the separation of one or more industrial designs in the international registration mentioned above into one or more new applications. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall separate the application and issue decisions and notices on the new application independently from the original application.
6. If the applicant successfully corrects deficiencies and/or has a legitimate objection within the time limit specified in Clause 5 of this Article, within 2 months, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Issue a decision to accept protection of internationally registered industrial designs for industrial designs satisfying the protection conditions, record them in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of Internationally Registered Industrial Designs), and send them to the International Office for announcement of acceptance of protection of internationally registered industrial designs after refusal using the form of the International Office, clearly stating the industrial design accepted for protection;
b/ Publicizing the decision in the Industrial Property Gazette within one month after its issuance.
7. In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies, does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection to the industrial designs notified for rejection within 15 working days from the end of the time limit prescribed in Clause 5 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to reject protection of such internationally registered industrial designs.
8. In case of expiration of the 3-month period from the date on which the International Office announces the Vietnam-based application, if the applicant fails to submit documents proving priority rights or submits documents proving priority rights but they are not approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the application shall be considered as having no request for priority right.
9. Procedures for filing and settling complaints about decisions prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article shall be carried out as those applicable to applications for registration of industrial designs filed according to the national form. In case after receiving the complaint settlement results, some or all of the industrial designs are rejected in the rejection decisions and accepted for protection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send to the International Office a notice of acceptance for protection after rejection, which is made according to the form of the International Office, clearly stating the industrial design accepted for protection.
10. In case a third party has an opinion on the Hague-Visby formula petition, which specifies Vietnam before the date of issuance of the decision to accept protection, such opinion shall be regarded as a source of reference information for the processing of the Hague-Visby formula petition specifying Vietnam.
11. The information to be disclosed as prescribed in Point b Clause 2, Point c Clause 4 and Point b Clause 6 of this Article includes basic information specified in the corresponding decision (including the decision number and date) and other information as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 49 of this Circular.
Chapter V
                            HANDLING OF REGISTRATION FORMS
Section 1. APPLICATION FOR REGISTRATION OF TRADE DESIGN
Article 68. Mark registration applications
An application for registration of a mark shall comprise the following documents:
1. The application form for registration of a mark shall be made using Form No. 4 in Appendix I to this Circular.
2. The sample mark enclosed therewith (5 samples of the mark are required for paper applications).
3. Regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks for registered marks being collective marks or certification marks.
4. A letter of authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative).
5. Documents proving the right to register marks if the applicant inherits the registration right of another person.
6. Documents proving the permission for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing geographical names or other signs indicating the geographical origin of local specialties of Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. Documents proving priority rights if priority rights are claimed, including:

a) A copy of the application or the first applications of the receiving authority;
b) The conveyance of priority right if such right is acquired from another person.
8. Documents proving the right to use an individual’s image for registration of a mark, including:
a) A copy of personal documents (with personal photos) in case the applicant uses their own photos;
b) Written permission for use of personal images in accordance with civil law regulations in case the applicant uses the image of another person and a copy of his/her identity document containing the personal image;
c) The applicant is not required to submit copies of identity documents as prescribed in Points a and b of this Clause if the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam can access such information from the National Population Database.
9. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 69. Requirements on documents in applications for registration of marks
1. Documents of a mark registration application must satisfy corresponding requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of this article and meet the general requirements on form as prescribed below:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except for drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm); for geographical area maps, documents may be presented on one side of an A3 paper sheet (420 mm x 297 mm) with four 20 mm wide margins on all sides, using the Times New Roman font, with the font size not smaller than 13, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not for inclusion in the application;
b/ For a document consisting of many pages, each page must be numbered in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with fade-resistant ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents have 2 pages or more, each page must bear a signature or a seal affixed by the applicant or the issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if minor spelling errors are found, the applicant may correct them and must affix a signature (and seal, if applicable) at the correction location. The signature and seal must not obscure the content of the documents.
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system must be in the “.pdf” format; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; the individual signing the documents of the application for trademark registration may use a digital signature to directly sign on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as a color photo in the case of submission through the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not use local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers used for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 4, 5, 7, 8, and 9 Article 68 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure complete and consistent mandatory information.
2. Mark registration applications must ensure uniformity as prescribed in clauses 3 and 6 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The application form for registration of a mark must be completed with all information requested in appropriate spaces in the application form and must satisfy the following requirements:
a) In the section "Non-traditional marks", if the applicant requests registration of a sound mark or a three-dimensional mark, the corresponding box must be marked;
b) In the section "Mark samples", there must be samples according to regulations on mark samples in Clause 4 of this Article;
c/ In the section "Description of mark", specify each constituent element of the mark and its general meaning (if any) and describe the following contents: c1/ If the mark contains words in a language other than Vietnamese, clearly write their Vietnamese pronunciation and if such words or phrases have meanings, they must be translated into Vietnamese; c2/ If the mark contains numerals other than Arabic numerals or Roman numerals, they must be translated into Arabic numerals; c3/ If the mark is a shape or contains a shape element, clearly state the content and meaning of this shape element; c4/ If the mark shows colors and there is a protection request for color mark, specify this request and name the colors shown on the mark;

c5) If the mark is a sound, it must be described in full, detailed and complete about the sound sign which is requested to be registered as a mark (the sound of what musical instrument, with or without lyrics, etc.). This description can be presented in the attached documents;
d) In the “Exclusion elements” section: The applicant may exclude one or more elements of the mark sample which are not separately protected by the applicant;
dd) In the field "Applicant": If the applicant is a foreign individual or organization, the name and address must be written in Latin letters according to the international form of the original name, not translated or transcribed into Vietnamese;
e) In the section "List and classification of goods and services bearing marks", the applicant must classify in accordance with the International Classification of Goods and Services under the Nice Agreement published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Gazette. If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify and the applicant must pay a classification fee as prescribed.
4. A mark sample must satisfy the following requirements:
a) The trademark sample must be clearly presented with the size of each component in the trademark not larger than 80 mm and not smaller than 8 mm, the overall trademark must be presented in the trademark mold with size of 80 mm x 80 mm printed on the declaration;
b) For a 3D mark, the mark sample must be accompanied by a photograph or drawing showing the perspective view and may also be accompanied by a descriptive model in the form of a projection;
c/ For a trademark with color protection requirements, its sample must be presented in the exact colors requested for protection. If no color protection is required, the trademark sample must be presented in black and white;
d/ For a sound trademark, the trademark sample must be an MP3 digital audio file with a size of not exceeding 5 MB and graphically represented in the form of a musical score with five lines.
The 5 musical notes must be clearly, accurately, completely and uniformly written in the audio file, including note symbols, rests, beats, rhythms, and musical notes, etc.
5. Regulations on use of collective marks shall contain the following principal contents:
a) Information about marks and goods or services bearing marks;
b/ Name, address and grounds for establishment and operation of the collective organization being the owner of the mark when the protection title is granted;
c/ Criteria for becoming a member of a collective organization;
d) List of members of the collective organization;
dd) Conditions for use of marks. For collective marks containing signs indicating geographical origins, conditions for use of collective marks must include conditions on geographical origins and characteristics of goods or services;
e) Measures to handle acts of violating the regulation on use of marks.
6. The regulations on use of a certification mark must contain the following principal contents:
a) Information about marks and goods or services certified by marks;
b/ The organization being the owner of a mark when the protection title is granted;
c/ Conditions for use of marks. For certification marks containing signs indicating geographical origins, conditions for use of certification marks must include conditions on geographical origins and characteristics of goods or services;
d) Characteristics of goods or services certified by marks;
dd) Methods of assessing characteristics of goods and services and methods of controlling the use of marks;
e) Expenses paid by mark users for certification and protection of marks, if any.
7. Documents proving the permission for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing place names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property, including written permission for use in accordance with Point a and maps of the corresponding geographical area in accordance with Point b of this Clause.
a/ Documents permitting the use of other place names or signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing such elements, issued by the following competent agencies:

a1) The People’s Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government where the geographical area corresponds to another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties (in case the geographical area belongs to one province);

a2) All the People’s Committees of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government where the geographical areas correspond to another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties (in case the geographical area belongs to many provinces).
b) The map of the geographical area corresponding to another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties must fully present information sufficient to accurately identify such geographical area and must be certified by the competent authority specified in point a of this clause.
8. In case there are grounds (information and evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date of notification. After the above time limit, if the applicant fails to submit supplementary documents satisfying requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application. Documents specified in this Clause may include the following documents:
a) Documents proving the applicant’s status:

a1) Certificate of enterprise registration, contract or other documents certifying the production of products or provision of services by the applicant as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a2) Agreement or written confirmation that the manufacturer does not use the mark and does not object to the registration of the mark by the person conducting commercial activities of the product of the manufacturer as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a3) Decision or establishment license, organizational charter which may indicate information about the function and competence to manage collective marks, quality certification marks, geographical origin marking marks for goods or services as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a4) Agreement, certificate of enterprise registration, documents related to the mark registration of co-owners as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a5) Documents confirming that the applicant has received the mark registration right from another person as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a6) Agreement or written consent of the mark owner confirming the mark registration right of the representative or agent as prescribed in Clause 7 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 6septies of the Paris Convention.

a7) Documents proving that the applicant is an individual residing in Vietnam and is currently valid.
b) Documents proving the representative status of the applicant: the original letter of attorney of the applicant; documents confirming that the applicant is the legal representative of the mark registration organization or the person authorized by such organization; documents confirming that the authorized person of the applicant meets the requirements to act as the representative of the applicant as prescribed in Article 6 of this Circular;
c) Documents proving the right to use or registration of marks containing special signs as prescribed in Clauses 2, 4 and 7 Article 73, Point p Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property; documents proving that the use of commercial indications, origin indications, prizes, medals or distinctive symbols of a certain product does not mislead or deceive consumers or is deceptive in nature;
d) Documents proving priority rights;
dd) Documents proving the control and certification function of the mark certification registration organization;
e) Necessary information to clarify or confirm contents stated in regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks or other application documents;
g) Other appropriate documents to clarify the authenticity of the information in the application.
Article 70. Receipt of applications for registration of marks
1. A mark registration application shall be accepted when it fully satisfies the following conditions:
a) Having all minimum documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Mark samples enclosed (enclosed with 05 samples of marks in case of paper application);

a3) Regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks for marks registered as collective marks or certification marks; a4) Documents proving the permission to register collective marks or certification marks containing geographical locations or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. In case an application fully satisfies the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and verify the submission date and application number of such application and issue a receipt to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number, and results of the verification of the list of documents included in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For applications submitted via the electronic application system, upon receipt of an application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue to the applicant an application receipt stating information about the submission date, application number, and examination results of the list of documents in which full name and signature of the receiving official are specified through the electronic application system.
3. In case an application fails to meet all conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Section, it shall be rejected for receipt (if the application is submitted by post or via electronic submission system, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, specifying the reasons for rejection). For applications rejected for receipt by post, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return documents of the application except for the original application submitted for comparison.
4. Regarding documents proving eligibility for priority right, applicants may submit additional documents within 3 months from the date of application as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Section 2. REGULATION ON APPLICATION FORMS FOR REGISTRATION OF TRADE DESIGNS
Article 71. Formal examination of mark registration applications
1. Formal examination of a mark registration application according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means an examination of compliance with regulations on form of the application in order to make a conclusion on whether the application is valid or not.
The formal examination of applications for registration of certification trademarks or collective trademarks shall not include evaluation of specific characteristics of goods or services certified by such trademarks, methods of evaluating characteristics of goods or services and methods of controlling the use of trademarks, costs to be paid by trademark users for certification and protection of trademarks (if any), conditions for using trademarks, and measures to handle violations against regulations on use of trademarks stated in regulations on use of such trademarks.
2. A mark registration application shall be considered invalid in any of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed in contravention of Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c/ Having grounds to affirm that the object stated in the application is an object not protected by the State as prescribed in Articles 8 and 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
d) The applicant fails to fully pay the fee and charge as prescribed;
dd) The application fails to meet the requirements regarding form as prescribed in Article 69 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding the quantity of one of the mandatory documents; failing to meet the requirements regarding the presentation format; the declaration does not clearly state the registered mark, lacks a description of the mark; fails to classify goods or services bearing the mark, or incorrectly classified and the applicant fails to pay classification fees; lacks a translation of the priority right documentation (if required), a translation of the registration documentation if the applicant inherits the registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not properly certified, the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the applicant, the representative, or does not include the signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.;
e) No valid written authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative).
3. Notification of the results of the formal assessment shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the trademark registration certificate, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application (including priority claims), and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinion or correct deficiencies.

b/ If, upon expiration of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to make satisfactory correction thereof or does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection, within 15 working days after such time limit expires, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of a certificate of registered mark and send it to the applicant.
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article (or the applicant has made satisfactory corrections to deficiencies or provided a legitimate explanation), the application shall be determined as valid and published and subject to content appraisal in accordance with regulations. In case the request for priority rights is rejected, the application shall still be considered valid and be transferred for publication in accordance with regulations.
4. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of trademark registration applications that are transferred for publication, specifying the application number, filing date and date of transfer for publication of each application.
5. The filing date of a mark registration application is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam according to Clause 2 Article 70 hereof.
In case an application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative working hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date specified in this clause shall be the next working day.
6. The priority date of a mark registration application shall be determined as follows:
a/ If an application does not request priority right or although it requests priority right, it is not approved by the National Office of Industrial Property, such application shall be regarded as having no priority date;
b) If the application requests priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the National Office of Intellectual Property;
c) The priority date determined according to the request for priority rights on the basis of the first application(s) submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
7. In case an applicant requests to enjoy priority right but at the time of filing a mark registration application there is no document proving the priority right, the examination of the application shall start from the time the National Office of Industrial Property receives the supplementary documents or end after 3 months as prescribed in Clause 4, Article 70 of this Circular.
Article 72. Disclosure of applications for registration of marks
1. Mark registration applications shall be disclosed immediately after they are received as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 70 of this Circular.
2. A valid mark registration application shall be published in accordance with Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
3. Contents of disclosure of a mark registration application in the Industrial Property Official Gazette shall include information on the applicant, the representative (if any), the application number, the filing date, the priority date (if any); information on the mark sample and the list of goods or services bearing the mark; international classification of goods or services; information on the origin of the application (application conversion, application splitting, the original application number of the split application); and other information (if any).
Regulations on use of collective marks and regulations on use of certification marks shall be published on the website of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 3. CONCLUSION OF INVENTORY OF APPLICATION FOR MARK REGISTRATION
Article 73. Substantive examination of applications for registration of marks
1. Content appraisal of a mark registration application according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application according to protection conditions and determination of the corresponding scope of protection according to provisions of this Article.
2. The assessment of the ability to grant protection titles for mark registration applications includes:
a) Assess the conformity of subject matters stated in the mark registration application with the requirements for issuance of a certificate of mark registration as prescribed in Article 75 of this Circular;
b) Evaluate subject matters stated in the mark registration application according to protection conditions specified in Articles 76, 77 and 78 of this Circular. The evaluation shall be carried out one component at a time for each sign for each goods or service stated in the list of goods and services;
c) Assess the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 79 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for a title of protection as prescribed in Point b Clause 2 of this Article shall be completed after the subject matter stated in the application has been assessed and all conditions for protection have been satisfied, with sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter fails to satisfy or satisfies the protection conditions, specifically:
a) Finding reasons to conclude that the subject does not satisfy one or several or all of the protection conditions; or

b) No reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Before issuing a notice of content appraisal results and planning to grant a certificate of mark registration as prescribed in Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 79 of this Circular.
5. Based on the results of assessment of the ability to grant protection titles prescribed in clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding expected protection scope.
6. Notification of content appraisal results with planned issuance of mark registration certificates as prescribed in Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular shall be made for the following mark registration applications:
a) The application does not fall under the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application has the earliest submission date or priority date among applications for registration of marks falling within the cases specified in Clause 2, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Application as agreed in Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. Mark registration applications not falling into the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article shall be processed as follows:
a/ Being rejected from grant of a mark registration certificate because the mark registration application fails to satisfy the first-to-file rule, if the mark registration certificate is granted to an application with the earliest filing date or priority date; or b/ Being regarded as having the earliest filing date or priority date and treated as in the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article, if all other mark registration applications with earlier filing dates or priority dates are rejected from grant of mark registration certificates, withdrawn or regarded as withdrawn.
8. During the process of content appraisal, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations for the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify the deficiencies.
Article 74. Minimum information sources
1. In order to assess the ability to cause confusion of signs stated in a mark registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct search in the following minimum information sources:
a) Mark registration applications already filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam with an earlier filing date or priority date or on the same day as the application or priority date of the application currently under appraisal and international mark registration applications designating Vietnam which have been notified by the International Office of Vietnam of an earlier filing date or priority date or on the same day as the filing date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal for identical or similar goods or services;
b) Markes which are protected or recognized for protection and still in effect in Vietnam and used for identical, similar or related goods or services;
c) The protected marks have expired within three years, except for cases where the marks are expired due to non-use as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property and are used for identical or similar goods or services;
d) Geographical indications currently under protection in Vietnam;
dd) Well-known marks already recognized according to regulations;
e) Place names, symbols or other signs indicating geographical origins of goods and services; geographical names, quality marks, inspection marks, warranty marks of international organizations; national flags, national emblems, national anthems of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and foreign countries, international anthem; flags, names, symbols of state agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations of Vietnam and the world; names and images of leaders, national heroes, names and images of Vietnamese and foreign celebrities, etc. collected and preserved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. Where necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may consult with sources of information other than those specified in Clause 1 of this Article, such as trade names, industrial designs, plant varieties that have been or are being protected in Vietnam; names and images of characters and symbols in works under copyright protection, etc.
Article 75. Assessment of the conformity of subject matters stated in applications for registration of marks The assessment of the conformity between subject matters stated in an application for registration of a mark and the request for grant of a certificate of registered mark shall be carried out as follows:

1. Under Clause 1, Article 72 of the Law on Intellectual Property, signs registered as marks must be visible signs in the form of letters, numbers, words, drawings, images, including three-dimensional figures or a combination thereof, expressed in one or several certain colors or sound signals expressed in graphics (specifically, 5-line music staves).
2. The following signs shall not be protected as marks:
a) Signs are color but not combined with signs in form of letters (signs in form of written words or numbers), signs in form of images or not presented as signs in form of letters or signs in form of images; or sound signs cannot be presented in graphic form (specifically, in form of 5-line music staves);
b) Signs which are subject to non-protection as marks according to Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c/ Signs contrary to social ethics and public order, harmful to national defense and security as prescribed in Article 8 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 76. Assessment of distinguishability of signs stated in applications
1. The ability of distinguishing a sign in the form of letters as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be assessed as follows:
Apart from the exceptions specified in Clause 5 of this Article, the following signs shall be considered indistinguishable:
a) Characters belonging to languages which Vietnamese consumers have common knowledge of which they cannot recognize or remember (cannot read, understand or remember), such as characters not derived from Latin: Arabic, Slavic, Sanskrit, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, etc.; unless the characters belong to the above-mentioned languages accompanied by other components that constitute a whole and can be distinguished or presented in graphic form or in other special forms;
b/ Latin characters which are composed of a single letter or number or which, although consisting of two letters, cannot be pronounced as one syllable (for Vietnamese) or do not constitute a word in other common languages (English, French), even when accompanied by numbers, except where such signs are presented in graphic form or in other special forms;
c/ A collection of too many letters or words which cannot be recognized and remembered as a series of characters not arranged in an orderly manner or a document or a passage of a document;
d) Letters of Latin origin which are words with meanings and meanings of these words have been widely used and common in Vietnam in the relevant fields to the extent of being indistinguishable;
dd) One or a collection of words used in Vietnam as common names of related goods or services;
e) One or a set of words describing the main goods or services bearing the trademark as a sign indicating time, place, method of production, type, quantity, quality, nature, composition, utility and value of the goods or services;
g) One or a set of words describing or indicating geographical origin of goods or services;
h) One or a set of words with descriptive meaning of legal form and business sector of the mark owner;
i) Signs of letters falling under Points e, g, h, i, k, l, m, o, and p Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. The ability to distinguish a graphic sign (including visual signs, drawings, shapes, etc.) as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be assessed as follows: Except for the exceptions specified in Clause 5 of this Article, a graphic sign shall be considered indistinguishable if:
a) Geometric signs are common shapes such as circles, ellipses, triangles, quadrilaterals, etc., or simple drawings; drawings and images may only be used as backgrounds or decorative lines of products or product packages;
b/ Signs of overly complicated figures which are not easy for consumers to recognize and remember such figures as having too many images or lines combined or superimposed on each other;
c) Drawings, images, symbols, signs, typical shapes of goods or parts thereof, typical shapes of packages or containers of goods which have been frequently used and widely recognized;
d) Signs in the form of shapes that describe the main goods or services bearing the mark, such as location, production method, type, quantity, quality, properties, components, utility, value, or other characteristics of the goods or services bearing the mark, or signs that significantly increase the value of the goods;
dd) Drawings and images describing the geographical origin of goods or services;
e) Signs which are identical or insignificantly different from industrial designs already or currently protected by others;
g) Geometric signs in the cases specified at Points e, g, h, i, l, m and p, Clause 2, Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The ability of a sound signal to be distinguished shall be assessed as follows:
Except for the exceptions specified in Clause 5 of this Article, a sound signal shall be considered indistinguishable if:
a/ Popular and common sounds used for goods or services or in production and business domains related to goods or services and already familiar to concerned public;
b) The sound has too many melodies or tones or is too long to be recognized and remembered;
c/ The short and simple musical notes, including one or two musical notes;
d) Sounds carrying the main contents of goods or services bearing a mark as indications on time, location, method of production, type, quantity, quality, nature, composition, utility, value or other characteristics of such goods or services;
dd) Sounds carrying descriptions and/or geographical origins of goods or services;
e) The sound has a meaningful description of legal form and business sector;
4. In case a sound mark contains linguistic content, such linguistic content shall be evaluated for distinguishability similar to evaluation of distinguishability of word marks according to Clause 1 of this Article.
5. Exceptions applicable to assessment of the distinguishability of signs in the form of letters, figures and sounds:
a) Signs falling into the cases specified in Points a, b, c, e and g Clause 1, Points a, b, d and dd Clause 2, Points d and dd Clause 3 of this Article which have been used with the function of mark and are widely recognized by consumers as having achieved the distinguishing capability for related goods or services before the filing date. In order to be entitled to apply the exceptions prescribed in this Clause, the applicant must provide evidence of the widespread use of such signs with the function of mark (the number of relevant consumers who know about it, the starting time of use, scope and level of use before the filing date, sales volume from the sale of goods or provision of services, etc.) and therefore the signs have the distinguishing capability for related goods or services of the applicant. In this case, the signs must be continuously used and widely spread in lawful production, business, commercial, advertising and marketing activities in the form shown in the application.
b) Signs mentioned in Point g Clause 1, Point dd Clause 2, and Point dd Clause 3 of this Article shall be registered as certification trademarks or collective trademarks containing signs indicating geographical origins of goods or services.
c) Signs mentioned in Point g Clause 1, Point dd Clause 2, and Point dd Clause 3 of this Article shall be registered as geographical origin certification marks of goods or services or collective marks.
6. The ability to distinguish between a combination of a word and a symbol (hereinafter referred to as “combination sign”) shall be assessed as follows:
A combined sign is considered distinguishable when the sign of letters and signs of figures combine into a distinguishable whole, specifically:
a) The letter and figure signs are distinguishable and form an overall distinguishable sign;
b) The strong component of a mark (an element that strongly affects the consumer's perception, causing attention and impression about the mark when heard or seen) is a sign in letters or figures that can be distinguished, even though the remaining components have little or no distinguishable ability;
c) In case a combination of signs including both written and graphic signs is indistinguishable or has little distinguishable ability but the unique combination of these signs creates a distinct impression, such overall combination shall still be considered distinguishable;
d) The combination sign consists of letters and images that are indistinguishable or have little distinguishable features but, overall, is distinguishable through use in accordance with Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 77. Criteria for determining geographical origins of goods and services indicated by place names or other signs

The identification of geographical origins of goods and services by using other place names and signs as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 and Points dd and dd1 Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be carried out according to the following criteria:
1. Sign indicating geographical origin of goods or services means a sign used for local goods or services and serving as an indication of the geographical origin of such goods or services (indicating that the goods or services originate from such locality).
Signs indicating the geographical origin of goods or services are local place names or symbolic signs (images of typical local objects such as symbols, maps, flags, badges, scenic views, and special works of local areas, etc.) or any other signs. Place names may be current names, historical names, official names, or folk names of a geographical area used to replace current place names or widely known place names (determined according to administrative boundaries or geographical methods).
2. A place name or a local symbol used for common goods and services (not specialties) may or may not indicate geographical origin of goods or services depending on goods or services and practical use of such place name or local symbol.
3. Place names and signboards of local areas which indicate geographical origins of goods or services in the following cases:
a) Used for local specialties (special products with certain characteristics and reputation, produced locally);
b) Used for local characteristic plants and animals and products processed from such plants and animals;
c) Used for products exploiting natural materials (coal, iron, steel, aluminum, cement, stone, salt, wood, etc.) in the local area;
d/ For products of local developing industries;
dd) Used for specific services (services with reputation associated with certain characteristics in the locality);
e) Other cases determined according to products and actual use of local place names and symbolic signs for products.
4. Place names and/or symbols of local areas have no meaning to indicate geographical origins of goods or services in the following cases:
a/ Having been used with the ordinary mark function and widely recognized, i.e., achieving the meaning of commercial origin indication (distinguishability) and losing the descriptive meaning of geographical origin, e.g.: Hanoi beer, Saigon beer;
b) The corresponding locality cannot be the place where goods are produced, e.g., Arctic tobacco, etc. Place names and distinctive signs of localities that have no meaning to indicate geographical origins of goods or services may be protected as common marks without permission from local authorities.
5. Place names and distinctive signs of local areas which are part of common geographical knowledge and known to many people (e.g. names of provinces, cities, scenic spots) used for ordinary goods or services of local areas (including products with business advantages but not yet having quality reputation or characteristics) used by many local business entities for their own goods or services as a means of describing production locations (but there is not enough ground to classify them in the cases specified in clauses 3 and 4 of this article) are signs which are not protected as marks but may be used as an auxiliary element to form ordinary marks of corresponding organizations or individuals in the local area, provided that such place names are excluded from the scope of protection (not protected separately) and no permission from local authorities is required.
6. Signs indicating Vietnamese geographical origin specified at Point dd1, Clause 1, Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property are understood as signs consisting of letters with meanings indicating the name of Vietnam, including “Việt” or “Việt Nam” (Vietnamese for “Vietnam”) or the corresponding English letters “Viet”, “Vietnam”, or “Viet Nam”.
Article 78. Assessment of the ability to cause confusion of signs stated in the application
1. In case the sign prescribed in the application is a word or image sign, the similarity to the extent of confusion between the sign requested for registration and another mark shall be assessed as follows:
a) In order to assess whether or not the signs of registration request stated in the application are identical or confusingly similar to another mark (hereinafter referred to as “comparative mark”), it is necessary to compare the pronunciation and meaning (for sign in the form of letters) and the structure and method of presentation of the sign (for both sign in the form of letters and figures according to Points b and c of this Clause), and at the same time, to compare goods or services bearing the sign with those bearing the comparative mark according to Clause 3 of this Article;
b) Sign identical to a control mark: a sign shall be considered identical to a control mark if it is identical to such mark in terms of its structure and manner of presentation;
c) A sign is considered similar to the point of confusion with a control mark if:

c1) The sign is similar to the control mark in terms of composition or /and pronunciation or /and meaning or /and form to the point of confusion that consumers may mistakenly believe that these two subjects are one or that this subject is a variant of that one or that these two subjects have the same origin in commerce;

c2) The sign is just an transcription or translation from the control mark if the control mark is a well-known mark.
2. In case the sign stated in the application is a sound sign, the similarity to the extent of confusion between the sign requesting registration and another mark shall be assessed as follows:
a) In order to assess whether the signs of registration requests stated in the application are identical or confusingly similar to the control mark, it is necessary to compare the sound elements (sound length, structure, composition; melody, rhythm, and cadence, etc.) and linguistic elements (if any) according to the provisions of Points b and c of this Clause, and at the same time, to compare goods or services bearing the signs with those bearing the control mark according to the provisions of Clause 3 of this Article;
b) Sign identical to a control mark: a sign shall be regarded as identical to a control mark if it is identical to such mark in terms of sound elements and linguistic elements;
c) A sign is considered similar to the point of confusion with a control mark if:

c1) The sign is similar to the control mark in terms of sound elements or language elements (pronunciation) or meaning to the point where consumers may mistakenly believe that these two subjects are one or that this subject is a variant of that other subject or that these two subjects have the same origin;

c2) The sign is just the pronunciation of the control mark if the control mark is a well-known mark.
3. The similarity of goods or services shall be assessed as follows:
a) Two goods or two services are considered identical (of the same type) when they have the following characteristics:

a1) They have the same nature (components, composition, etc.) and the same function and use purpose; or

a2) They have nearly similar nature and the same function and use purpose;
b) Two goods or two services are considered similar if they fall into one of the following cases:

b1) They are similar in nature and are marketed through the same commercial channel (distributed by the same method, sold together or next to each other, in the same type of shop, relevant public/potential consumers, etc.);

b2) They are similar in function and use purpose and are marketed through the same commercial channel (distributed by the same method, sold together or next to each other, in the same type of shop, relevant public/potential consumers, etc.);
c) A good and a service shall be regarded as similar if they fall into one of the following cases: c1) They are related to each other in terms of nature (the goods or service or parts thereof are made from the other goods or service); or c2) They are related to each other in terms of function (to complete the function of this good or service, the other good or service must be used or they are often used together); or c3) They are closely related to each other in terms of mode of performance (this good or service is the result of using or exploiting the other good or service, etc.).
4. Signs shall be considered identical or confusingly similar to a control mark used for identical or similar goods in the following cases:
a) Signs identical with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of the goods or services bearing the control mark;
b) Signs are similar to the extent of confusion with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of the control mark;
c/ Signs identical or similar to a control mark being a well-known mark are goods or services bearing signs which are neither identical nor similar to those of goods or services bearing such mark but the use of such signs as marks may mislead consumers into believing that there exists a relationship between goods or services bearing such signs and owners of well-known marks, thus possibly reducing the distinguishability of well-known marks or harming the reputation of well-known marks.
5. The appraisal of other capacity to cause confusion of signs shall comply with Article 73 and Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following specific regulations:
a) Signs considered as causing confusion as to the origin of goods or services in the following cases:

a1) Signs identical or confusingly similar to the name or symbol of a country or territory (national flag, national emblem, national name, place name, local name, local symbol, etc.) which causes the misperception that the goods or services bearing such signs originate from such country or territory but actually originate from another country or territory;

a2) Signs identical or similar to geographical indications currently under protection if the use of such signs may mislead consumers as to the geographical origin of goods; signs identical to geographical indications or containing geographical indications or being translated or transcribed from geographical indications currently under protection for wines and spirits if the signs requesting registration as marks for wines or spirits do not originate from the geographical areas bearing such geographical indications;

a3) Signs identical or similar to trade names of other persons that have been lawfully used for the same type of goods or services and may cause consumers to misperceive that the goods or services bearing such signs are produced or provided by the persons with such trade names;

a4) Signs identical or similar to real names, aliases, pen names or images of Vietnamese or foreign leaders, national heroes or famous personalities; signs identical or similar to names or images of characters or typical images of works widely known if the use of such signs may mislead consumers as to the misperception that the goods or services bearing such signs are produced or provided by the owners of such works;

a5) Signs identical or insignificantly different from industrial designs of other persons that have been and are protected on the basis of applications for registration of industrial designs with submission dates or priority dates earlier than those of applications for registration of marks.
b) Signs that are considered likely to cause confusion or misinterpretation of the nature and value of goods or services in the following cases:

b1) Signs that are words, drawings, images, symbols, sounds, etc. that create a misleading impression on the features and utility of goods or services, such as signs identical or similar to marks or other widely used signs to the extent that they are considered associated with a function or utility of a certain type of goods or service, causing consumers to mistakenly believe that goods or services bearing such signs also have such features or utilities;

b2) Signs that are words, images, sounds that create a misleading impression on the composition of goods or services, such as descriptions of other goods or services related to goods or services bearing such signs that create the misleading impression that goods or services bearing such signs are composed of or have the same nature as the described goods or services.
Article 79. Evaluation of the first-to-file rule
For mark registration applications which have been concluded to meet the protection conditions, before issuing notices of content appraisal results and planning to grant mark registration certificates under Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file rule prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:
1. In order to examine the first-to-file principle, it is required to search all trademark registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (by the time of examination) that have an earlier submission date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority rights) or the same submission date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority rights) as the application currently undergoing appraisal.
2. The lookup is to find cases where there are many applications (including applications currently being appraised) from different people registering marks that are identical or similar to the point of confusion with each other for identical or similar goods and services, or where there are many applications from the same person registering identical marks for identical goods and services; and to determine the earliest application submission date or priority date.
3. If there are many applications in the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, a certificate of registered mark shall be granted only to the mark in the valid application with the earliest submission date or priority date among the applications satisfying the conditions for grant of a certificate of registered mark.
4. Among applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, if there are many applications with the same earliest filing date or priority date, the certificate of mark registration shall be granted only to the mark of a single application among such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all relevant subject matters of such applications shall be rejected from the grant of certificates of mark registration.
Article 80. Grant and refusal to grant mark registration certificates
1. Applications for registration of marks shall be rejected for grant of mark registration certificates in the cases specified at Points a, b, c, d and e, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. A mark registration application shall be granted a mark registration certificate when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For mark registration applications falling into cases specified in clause 1 or clause 2 of this article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matters stated in the application satisfy the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of the trademark registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for grant of the trademark registration certificate, the disclosure fee for the decision on grant of the trademark registration certificate, and the registration fee for the decision on grant of the trademark registration certificate;
b) If the subject matters of the protection request stated in the application fail to satisfy the protection conditions or fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or satisfy the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the trademark registration certificate, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide its opinion;
c/ In case the applicant fails to correct errors or does not make any objection within the time limit prescribed at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days after the expiry of such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the mark registration certificate;
d) In case the applicant corrects deficiencies or provides objections within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the correction document or objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the content appraisal as follows:

d1) If the correction is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the certificate of mark registration;

d2) If the applicant has satisfactorily corrected deficiencies or provided justifiable objections to all or part of the content appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of the certificate of mark registration for the eligible part or the whole, and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for grant of the certificate of mark registration, the disclosure fee for the decision on grant, and the registration fee for the decision on grant of the certificate of mark registration;
dd) If the subject matters of the protection request stated in the application include goods or services not eligible for protection or have elements of a mark model not separately protected (and the applicant fails to exclude them by themselves or excludes them improperly), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of a certificate of registered mark for the eligible part, the fee for grant of the certificate of registered mark and fees and charges as prescribed that the applicant must pay in case of agreement with all content appraisal results of the application, reasons for refusal to grant for the remaining part, non-protected elements (if any), and at the same time set a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide written opinions;
e) In case the applicant does not give opinions or do not give written consent and fully pays the fee and charge within the time limit specified at Point dd of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of the above time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the certificate of registered mark;
g) In case the applicant submits a written opinion within the time limit specified at Point dd of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the document, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall proceed as follows:

g1) If the applicant has a justifiable objection to the entire appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the trademark registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the trademark registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the record registration fee for the decision on issuance of the trademark registration certificate;

g2) If the applicant has an objection but it is unjustifiable or provides a justifiable explanation for part of the appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the trademark registration certificate for the protected part, the reason for refusal to issue it for the remaining part or non-protection elements (if any), and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the trademark registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the record registration fee for the decision on issuance of the trademark registration certificate;

h) If the applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge within the time limit specified at Points a, d2, g1 and g2 of this Clause, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the certificate of registered mark within 15 working days after the expiry of the corresponding time limit;
i) If the applicant fully and on time pays all fees and charges prescribed in Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 of this Clause and obtains written consent to all appraisal results for the case specified at Point dd of this Clause, within 01 month from the date of full payment of fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant a certificate of registered mark with the part satisfying the protection conditions and the non-protection elements (if any), and refuse to grant the part failing to satisfy the protection conditions (if any), clearly stating the reasons for refusal (if any).
4. The decision on grant of a certificate of registered mark shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days after its issuance. Published contents shall include information in the decision on grant of a certificate of registered mark (including decision number and date) and contents specified in Clause 3, Article 72 of this Circular.
Regulations on use of collective marks and regulations on use of certification marks shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5. The certificate of trademark registration made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued in both electronic and paper forms (if the applicant requests a paper version). In case industrial property rights to a mark are under joint ownership, the certificate of trademark registration shall only be issued to the first person on the list of joint applicants.
A copy of a certificate of registered mark made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in case the applicant requests issuance of copies (specified in the mark registration declaration) for other co-owners.
Section 4. HANDLING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO REGISTRATION AND DECLARATION OF REGULAR MARKS
Article 81. Handling of requests for content appraisal of applications for registration of marks
1. An application for content assessment of a trademark registration application shall be accepted when the following documents are available and the applicant pays an application fee and charge for content assessment:
a) Written request for rapid content appraisal, made using Form No. 14 under Appendix I hereto;
b) Documents proving that the registered mark falls under one of the cases specified in Point a Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
2. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall receive and process requests for prompt content appraisal of documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article according to the following regulations:
a) In case of a request for fast content appraisal that fails to meet the requirements specified in Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, within 15 working days from the date of receipt of the request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the refusal to accept the request for fast content appraisal and shall continue processing the related mark registration application as prescribed;
b) In case of a request for rapid content appraisal meeting the provisions of Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, such request shall be announced at the same time as the announcement of the mark registration application and the content appraisal in accordance with this Circular within the time limit prescribed in Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, except where the mark registration application is determined to be invalid under Article 71 of this Circular or the application falls under the cases specified at Points b and c Clause 4 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Article 82. Handling of objections to mark registration applications
1. Within 3 months after a mark registration application is published, any third party shall have the right to oppose the grant of a certificate of mark registration and shall pay fees and charges. An opposing opinion must be made in writing in Vietnamese, enclosed with documents or quoting information sources for verification. Documents or information sources may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. In case an objection to a mark registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process such objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 01 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a deadline of 01 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response, except in the following cases where the objection may be handled during the content appraisal process:

a1) The mark and goods or services in the application are identical to the mark and goods or services provided by the objector or have sufficient grounds to conclude that the marked and goods or services are similar and misleading or not similar and misleading to the mark and goods or services provided by the objector;

a2) Other cases where there are sufficient grounds to conclude that the objection is unjustified;
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a clause 2 of this Article, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the feedback to the objector and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide written responses to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) On the basis of information, evidence and arguments provided by the parties, results of the dialogue (if any) as prescribed in Points a, b and c of this Clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the result of processing the objection together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application to the objector, except for the case specified at Point dd of this Clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the termination of processing of objections to the withdrawal of applications for mark registration as prescribed in Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 01 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of application.
3. In case of objection related to registration rights, such objection shall be handled as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector to file a lawsuit petition to a competent Court in accordance with civil procedure law and impose a time limit of 02 months from the notification date for the objector to submit copies of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit, excluding the following cases where the objection is handled during the content appraisal:

a1) There are clear grounds to determine that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 7 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a2) The objection concerns the right to file a trademark registration application for signs that are or contain signs indicating geographical names or other signs indicating the geographical origin of local specialties of Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a3) The objection concerns the right to file an application without accompanying documents or sources of information cited for proof, or with accompanying documents or sources of information cited but the documents or sources of information are insufficient to determine that the applicant does not have registration rights.
b) Upon expiry of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if the objector fails to send a copy of the notice of acceptance of the case or his/her objection, such copy shall be regarded as withdrawal and the mark registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the mark registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the Court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the Court.
4. The time limit for applicants to respond to objections and the time limit for objectors to respond to applicants’ opinions as prescribed in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal.
Article 83. Amendment to mark registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of mark registration, an applicant may take initiative or based on notification of appraisal results to amend documents in the application for mark registration.
2. Requests for amendments to applications for registration of marks shall include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment to application by applicant, request for amendment shall be made using Form No. 10 under Appendix I hereof;
b) In case of amendment to the application on the basis of notification of appraisal results from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, such amendment shall be made in writing with contents of amendment specified;
c) The applicant may request amendments to the same content related to multiple mark registration applications in a single declaration or a single written request for amendments;
d) In case of amendments to the mark sample, the amended sample must be submitted (5 samples are required for paper applications); in case of amendments to the list of goods or services, the amended list must be submitted together with a detailed presentation of the amendments and additions compared with the initial documents submitted. In case the applicant proactively amends or adds elements which are excluded from protection in the mark sample, the applicant must clearly state this in the declaration of amendments.

dd) In case of change to name, address or country code of the applicant, the applicant must submit a written confirmation (original or certified copy) or legal document (certified copy) proving such change (decision on change to name or address; certificate of enterprise registration which records the change to name or address, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for actively extracting and using information available in databases announced by database owners to replace documents included in application for handling administrative procedures. In case of failure to extract information or insufficient or inaccurate information, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to individuals or organizations to supplement documents and provide explanation. Time for requesting and supplementing documents is not included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
e) In case of change of industrial property representatives, applicants shall submit documents declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representatives and change of new industrial property representatives as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. The applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee for each amendment and a fee for disclosure of amendments to the application if such amendments must be disclosed under Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except where such amendments are made to correct errors of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Requests for amendments to mark registration applications must not expand the scope (volume) of protection already disclosed in the mark samples and the list of goods and services stated in the applications, and must not alter the nature of the subject matters of the registration requests stated in the applications.
5. In case of amendment or supplementation of the application by the applicant, the request for amendment or supplementation shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment to the application satisfies the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendment to the application (except in cases where the amendment to the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant a certificate of registered mark due to invalidation of the application).
The contents of amendments and supplements shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette in case amendments or supplements to information related to a valid application in terms of form, including sample marks and lists of goods and services enclosed therewith are requested;
b) If the request for amendment to the application fails to satisfy the requirements specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opposing opinions. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, no opposing opinions, or provides inadequate opposing opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application;
c) In case of requesting amendments to applications related to mark samples, lists of goods and services bearing marks, changes in information of applicants (in case of collective mark registration or certification mark registration), regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks which are filed after notice of intended grant of the certificate of mark registration is issued, mark registration applications shall be re-examined and applicants shall pay fees as prescribed.
6. In case of amendment or supplementation of an application by the applicant on the basis of notification of appraisal results from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the acceptance or rejection of such amendment or supplementation shall be made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the notification of appraisal results or corresponding decision during the processing of the mark registration application.
7. In case the applicant requests amendments to the application, including amendments made by the applicant and amendments to the application based on notification of content appraisal results, such request shall be made in the form of an application form as prescribed in Point a Clause 2 of this Article. The handling of such request shall comply with the provisions of Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 84. Recording of changes to mark registration applicants
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of trademark registration, an applicant may request the recognition of changes in the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, or according to a decision of a competent authority.
2. The request for recognition of changes in applicants for mark registration must satisfy the following requirements:
a) Request to record changes in applicants using Form No. 11 under Appendix I hereof;
b) In case of recording a change of application submitters due to transfer of applications, the applicants shall submit documents evidencing the transfer of mark registration applications (originals or certified true copies), which contain principal contents such as names and addresses of the transferor and transferee; number of transferred applications or information sufficient to identify such applications. Documents with multiple pages must bear signatures of the parties or overlapping seals.

The transferee must satisfy conditions applicable to persons with registration rights as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) In case of recording change of applicant due to inheritance or inheritance or according to decision of competent authority, the applicant must submit documents proving related to inheritance or inheritance of assets upon consolidation, merger, division, separation of legal entity, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entity of same owner, business format conversion or according to decision of Court or other competent authority or inheritance due to other applicants giving up registration rights;
d) The applicant must pay fees and charges as prescribed;
dd) The request for recognition of changes due to transfer of many applications from the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that an appraisal fee is paid according to regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for recognition of transfer.
3. The request for recognition of changes in applicants shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for change of applicant satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of change of applicant and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette (except for the case where the request for change of applicant is filed before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant a protection title due to invalidation of the application in terms of form);
b) If the request for change of applicant fails to satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize such change and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate corrections, no objection, or provides inadequate objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to recognize the change of the applicant;
c) In case of request for recording of changes due to transfer of mark registration applications filed after notice of intended grant of the certificate of mark registration is issued, mark registration applications shall be re-examined and contents of transfer shall be published. Applicants shall pay examination fees and publication fees as prescribed.
Article 85. Splitting of mark registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of mark registration, an applicant may take initiative or based on notification of appraisal results to separate part of the list of goods and services in the submitted application for mark registration (initial application) into one or more new applications for mark registration (separated applications).
2. For a split application, the applicant shall submit a declaration of registration of new mark for the list of goods or services split. The split application shall bear a new serial number and the filing date of the initial mark registration application or (priority dates) of the initial mark registration application (if any). The applicant shall pay the filing fee and all fees and charges for procedures performed for the split application other than those already performed in the initial application, which need not be repeated in the split application, and is not required to pay the charge for examination of requests for priority right.
3. For the initial application, the applicant must carry out procedures for amendment and supplementation of the application as prescribed in Article 83 of this Circular.
4. The initial application and the separate application shall be processed as follows:
a/ The split-off application shall be appraised in terms of form and further processed according to the unfinished procedures for the initial application. The split-off application must be published under regulations;
b) The initial application shall continue to be processed in accordance with Article 83 of this Circular.
Article 86. Withdrawal of applications for registration of marks
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam decides to grant or refuses to grant a certificate of mark registration, an applicant may declare withdrawal of his/her application for mark registration.
2. The withdrawal of a mark registration application shall be effected by the applicant or his or her authorized representative in writing. For an application filed through a representative, the power of attorney must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or be enclosed with a command specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 2 months from the date of receipt of a declaration on withdrawal of a trademark registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal request as follows:
a) In case of a withdrawal request meeting the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the withdrawal request and, at the same time, terminate the processing of the application. A withdrawn mark registration application cannot be re-registered but may be used as a basis for claiming priority rights;

b) In case the withdrawal request fails to satisfy the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedy deficiencies but still fails to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant in writing of refusal to accept the application for withdrawal within 15 working days from the expiration date of such time limit.
Article 87. Re-examination of applications for registration of marks
1. The re-examination of a mark registration application shall be carried out by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the following cases:
a/ Re-examination of a mark registration application in the presence of written opinions of a third party objecting to the intended grant of a certificate of mark registration, in the notice of content examination results together with legitimate evidence proving that the objection is due to force majeure events or objective obstacles, thus making it impossible to object to the application within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Re-examine the application due to a request for amendments or additions to the application and recording of changes in the applicant after notifying the content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the title of protection as prescribed at Point c, Clause 5, Article 83, and Point c, Clause 3, Article 84 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of a mark registration application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of a mark registration application as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complicated cases with many details to be verified or expert opinions to be solicited, the time limit for re-appraisal may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial appraisal prescribed in Clause 2, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The appraisal contents shall be carried out in accordance with the corresponding provisions of Section 3 Chapter V of this Circular;
c/ For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of a mark registration application shall be effected only once for a third person.
3. After the results of the re-examination are available, the mark registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For cases specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-assessment results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application according to the provisions of this Circular;
b) For cases specified in point b clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application according to regulations in Article 80 of this Circular.
Article 88. Recording of well-known marks
1. Rights to well-known marks shall be protected and belong to their owners without registration procedures. Mark owners may use the documents specified in Clause 2 of this Article to prove their ownership of the marks and to prove that the marks meet the conditions for being considered well-known.
2. Documents evidencing the ownership of a mark and proving its popularity may include information on the scope, scale, level and continuity of use of the mark, including descriptions of the origin, history and period of continuous use of the mark; the number of countries where the mark has been registered or recognized as a popular mark; the list of goods and services bearing the mark; the territorial scope within which the mark is circulated; sales volume of products or provision of services; the quantity of goods and services bearing the mark that have been produced and sold; the value of the asset of the mark, the price of transfer or licensing of the right to use the mark, the investment capital contribution value of the mark; investments and expenses for advertising and marketing of the mark, including participation in national and international exhibitions; infringement cases, disputes and decisions and judgments of courts or competent agencies; survey data on consumers' knowledge of the mark through purchase, use and advertising and marketing; rankings and assessments of the reputation of the mark by national and international organizations and mass media; prizes and medals won by the mark; assessment results of intellectual property assessment organizations.
3. In case a well-known mark is recognized, leading to a decision on handling of infringement upon rights to such well-known mark as prescribed at Point d, Clause 1, Article 129 of the Law on Intellectual Property or leading to a decision not to protect another mark as prescribed at Point i, Clause 2, Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property, such well-known mark shall be recorded in the List of well-known marks kept at the National Office of Intellectual Property for use as reference information in the work of establishment and protection of intellectual property rights.
Section 5. APPLICATION FOR ADMISSION TO THE MADRID PROGRAM AND PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS
Article 89. Madrid System

1. Madrid applications include those originating in Vietnam and those with Vietnamese designation.
2. For a Madrid application of Vietnamese origin, the applicant shall submit it through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and pay a fee for carrying out procedures for international registration of a Vietnamese-origin mark.
3. Madrid applications originating in Vietnam shall include the following documents:
a) The application form for registration of a Vietnamese origin mark under Form No. 7 in Vietnamese, which is enclosed with Appendix I of this Circular;
b) MM2 Declaration, made by International Office using English or French language (2 declarations in paper form);
c) The sample mark is identical to the mark in the application submitted in Vietnam (the base application) or the certificate of trademark registration (the base registration) (2 samples are required if the application is filed in paper form);
d) The application form MM18 made in English if the application is submitted in the United States (2 application forms shall be filed in case the application is submitted in paper form);
dd) A written authorization in Vietnamese (if the application is submitted by a representative);
e) Other relevant documents (if any).
4. A Madrid application originating from Vietnam must satisfy both the form and content requirements as prescribed. The applicant must fully, accurately, and correctly fill in the information in the declaration, and agree with the information stated in the application for establishment or registration of the establishment.
5. For a Madrid application with Vietnam designated as the country of international registration or the later designation (in case of later designation), the date of filing in Vietnam and the priority date of the application (if any) shall be the later one.
Article 90. Processing of applications originating in Vietnam and related requests
1. After receiving a Madrid application originating from Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall appraise whether the application satisfies the requirements prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 89 of this Circular and carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the application contains errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant to correct such errors. If the applicant fails to correct the errors within 3 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification, the application shall be considered withdrawn;
b) In case the application is flawless or has flaws but the applicant has repaired them to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify fees and charges that the applicant must pay directly to International Office, sign and send the application to International Office within 15 working days from the date of notification of fees and charges;
c) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the Madrid application shall be considered as the international registration date in case the International Office receives the application within 2 months from the date specified on the receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the application is not complete for submission to the International Office within the above time limit, the date on which the application is received at the International Office shall be considered as the international registration date.
2. After the Madrid application of Vietnamese origin is filed with the International Office, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant thereof and continue to process (cooperating with the applicant if necessary) notifications and requests from the International Office or other matters related to the application (if any).
3. Requests for the following actions after an application for international registration of a mark originating in Vietnam is granted, including designation (expansion of protection territory), change of name and address of the owner of the international registration, restriction of the list of goods or services, extension of validity of the international registration, designation of representative, change of representative, transfer of international registration, etc., may be made directly to the International Office or through the National Office of Industrial Property. In case of submission through the National Office of Industrial Property, the applicant shall submit the following documents and pay the corresponding appraisal fee for the relevant request:
a) The application form, made according to Form No. 8 in Vietnamese in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) The corresponding declaration, made using the form of International Office (2 declarations in case of paper application);
c) A written authorization in Vietnamese (if the application is submitted by a representative);
d) Other relevant documents (if any).
4. After receiving the requests specified in Clause 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) In case of any error in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant to correct it. If the applicant fails to correct the error within 3 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification, the application shall be considered as withdrawn;

b) In case the application is flawless or has flaws but the applicant has corrected them and satisfied the requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify fees to be paid directly by the applicant at the International Bureau, sign and send the application to the International Bureau within 10 working days from the date of notification of fees;
c) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the subsequent request for designation shall be considered as the subsequent date of international registration in case the International Office receives such request within 2 months from the date specified in the request receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the request is not completed before the above-mentioned time limit, the date on which the request is received at the International Office shall be considered as the subsequent date of international registration.
5. In case of application for extension of international registration through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the applicant shall submit it within 6 months before and after the expiration date of international registration. If the application for extension of validity of international registration during the grace period is submitted, it must be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 1 month before the expiration date of the grace period.
Article 91. Processing of applications for designation of Vietnam by Madrid
1. After receiving a notice of the International Office of Madrid on an application for registration of a mark with designation of Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct a substantive examination of such application as for applications for registration of marks filed under the national filing system, except for the cases specified in clauses 3, 4 and 12 of this article. Within 12 months from the date of issuance of the notice by the International Office of Madrid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conclude on the possibility of protection of the mark.
2. In case a mark satisfies the protection conditions prescribed by Vietnamese law, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a/ Before the expiration of the 12-month time limit specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to protect internationally registered marks in Vietnam, record it in the national register of industrial property (the Part of Internationally Registered Marks) and send it to the International Office for Protection Declaration;
b/ Publicizing the decision in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days after its issuance.
The scope of protection is determined according to the contents of the international mark registration application which has been recorded by the International Office and accepted by the National Office of Intellectual Property.
3. In case a mark fails to satisfy the protection conditions for part or the whole of goods or services, or a protected mark has international registration but contains deficiencies (lack of regulations on use of collective marks, certification marks, lack of photos or drawings showing three-dimensional appearance of the mark, right to file an application, list of vague goods and services, etc.), before the expiration of the 12-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of provisional refusal, clearly stating the contents (part or the whole of the list of goods and services subject to the refusal) and the reasons for provisional refusal, and send such notice to the International Office.
4. In case a mark contains an exclusion element which has not yet been stated by the applicant in the application or has been stated but not accurately, before the expiration of the 12-month time limit specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of provisional rejection and send it to the International Office.
5. Within 03 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notice of provisional rejection specified in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article, applicants may correct errors or provide opinions opposing the intended rejection of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
The correction of errors or objection to the intended refusal shall be effected according to the same procedures as for a mark registration application filed under the national filing form, including regulations on the method of filing applications.
6. For a mark subject to provisional refusal under Clause 3 of this Article, if the applicant has completed the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article and has corrected deficiencies and/or provided a valid objection to such provisional refusal notice, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Issue a decision to accept protection of an internationally registered mark in Vietnam with a protection scope (volume) corresponding to goods or services satisfying protection conditions; record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of International Mark Register); and send it to the International Office for Protection Declaration after the provisional refusal notice is issued;
b/ Publicizing the decision in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days after its issuance.
7. For a mark subject to provisional refusal under Clause 3 of this Article, if the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or unsatisfactory deficiencies, or does not have any objection or provides inadequate objections to such provisional refusal notice after the expiration of the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) In case a mark fails to satisfy the protection conditions for a part of the list of goods or services, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article to the goods or services satisfying the protection conditions (goods or services not mentioned in the notice of temporary refusal);
b) In case a mark fails to satisfy the protection conditions for the entire list of goods and services, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant protection for the internationally registered mark in Vietnam and send it to the International Office of Vietnam to confirm the refusal of all marks;
c) In case there are grounds to determine that a mark satisfies the protection conditions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article to goods or services satisfying the protection conditions.
8. For a mark temporarily rejected under Clause 4 of this Article, upon expiration of the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the applicant does not have any objection or agrees with the contents stated in the notice of temporary refusal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article to the exclusion elements as stated in the notice of temporary refusal;
b) Where the applicant has a justifiable objection to the entire or part of the contents stated in the notice of provisional refusal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply the procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article with corresponding exclusion elements;
c) In case the applicant has an unjustifiable objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply procedures prescribed in Points a and b Clause 7 of this Article.
9. In case the international registration or the extension of international registration is not granted before the date of issuance of the decision to accept or refuse to accept protection of a trademark registered internationally in Vietnam and the validity of the entire list of goods and services or the international registration application filed in Vietnam is considered as being withdrawn.
10. Procedures for filing and settling complaints about the decisions mentioned in Point a Clause 2, Clauses 6, 7 and 8 of this Article shall be carried out as for applications for registration of marks filed according to the national form if there are grounds to believe that these decisions are issued inconsistently with the provisions of law on the content and issuance order. The results of complaint settlement shall be notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to the applicant. In case after receiving the results of complaint settlement, part or all of the list of goods or services subject to refusal in the refusal decisions is accepted for protection or there is a change in the non-protection elements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send the International Office the next decision affecting the protection of marks according to the International Office's form regarding the corresponding contents.
11. From the date of registration of international mark protection accepted in Vietnam, at the request of the mark owner, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a certificate of protection of internationally registered mark in Vietnam on condition that the applicant pays fees and charges as prescribed.
12. From the date on which the Madrid application is published in the Official Gazette by the International Office until before the date of issuance of the decision to accept protection, or 12 months from the date on which the International Office notifies the application with designation of Vietnam, whichever comes first, if the third party has opinions about the Madrid application with designation of Vietnam, these opinions shall be regarded as reference information sources during the processing of the application.
13. The information to be disclosed as prescribed in Point b Clause 2 and Point b Clause 6 of this Article includes basic information specified in the corresponding decision (including the decision number and date) and other information as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 72 of this Circular.
Article 92. Conversion of international mark registration applications into applications filed under national forms
1. In case of international registration of a mark in Vietnam by an individual or organization being a member of the Madrid Protocol which is invalidated according to Article 6 of the Madrid Protocol, such individual or organization may file an application for conversion to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to register protection for such mark for part or all of goods or services on the list of goods and services already recorded in the international registration of the mark but invalidated according to Article 9 quinquies of the Madrid Protocol. An application for registration of a converted mark shall be considered valid if it satisfies the following conditions:
a) The application is submitted within 3 months from the date of recording in the international register of corresponding international registration that it is invalidated;
b) List of goods and services included in the trademark registration application for conversion has never been subject of a refusal decision, termination or cancellation in Vietnam;
c) The application is made using Form No. 9 in Appendix I of this Circular (in which the list of goods and services in Vietnamese in the conversion application must be smaller than or equal to the list of goods and services that are invalidated in the corresponding international registration);
d) The application satisfies all other formal requirements for a mark registration application in accordance with Vietnamese law;
dd) The applicant shall fully pay fees and charges as prescribed for a mark registration application filed under the national filing form; in the case specified at Point b, Clause 2 of this Article, the applicant is not required to pay the application publication fee or the lookup fee for application appraisal.
An application for registration of a converted mark shall be recorded as having been filed on the international registration date or the later designated date (in case of later designation) in Vietnam. In case of international registration enjoying priority rights under an international treaty, the application for registration of a converted mark shall be recorded with corresponding priority rights, unless there are grounds to reject such application.
2. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall appraise a mark conversion registration application in accordance with the provisions on conversion conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article and according to the following principles:
a) Regarding formal elements already accepted by the International Office in corresponding international registrations, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall not conduct re-examination, except for cases where the application contains deficiencies (e.g., lack of regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks, or photos or drawings showing three-dimensional perspective of marks, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant a mark registration certificate in case the application fails to meet the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article;
b) For a mark registration application converted from an international registration accepted for protection in Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall not conduct a re-examination of contents. Where the application satisfies the conversion conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures to issue a protection title, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property, and publish the decision in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property as for applications filed under the national filing system;
c) For valid mark registration applications not falling under Point b of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures for determining validity, publish the applications, appraise contents and follow subsequent procedures as applicable to mark registration applications filed in the national filing format.
Chapter VI
                          HANDLING OF GEOGRAPHICAL GUIDELINE REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Section 1. REGISTRATION FORMS OF GEOGRAPHICAL GUIDELINES
Article 93. Applications for registration of geographical indications
An application for registration of a GIP shall include the following documents:
1. Declaration for registration of GIs, made using Form No. 5 in Appendix I hereto.
2. Description of peculiar characteristics and quality, reputation of products bearing GIs and elements typical of natural conditions creating such peculiar characteristics and quality, reputation of those products (hereinafter referred to as description of peculiar characteristics).
3. The map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication.
4. Geographical indications in case of geographical indications that are not words (05 examples in case of applications submitted in paper form).
5. Documents proving that a geographical indication is being protected in the country of origin, if it is a foreign geographical indication.
6. Presentation documents on conditions for use and method of presentation of geographical indications to ensure the ability to distinguish between geographical indications in case registered geographical indications are homonym geographical indications.
7. Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative).
8. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 94. Requirements on applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Documents of a geographical indication registration application must meet corresponding specific requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of this article and general requirements on form as prescribed below:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except for drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm); for geographical area maps, documents may be presented on one side of an A3 paper sheet (420 mm x 297 mm) with four 20 mm wide margins on all sides, using the Times New Roman font, with the font size not smaller than 13, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not for inclusion in the application;
b/ For a document consisting of many pages, each page must be numbered in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with fade-resistant ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents have 2 pages or more, each page must bear a signature or a seal of the applicant/issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if minor spelling errors are found, the applicant may correct them and must affix a signature (and seal, if any) at the correction location; the signature and seal must not obscure the content of the document.

d) Documents of the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system must be in the “.pdf” format; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; the individual signing the documents of the geographical indication registration application may use a digital signature to sign directly on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as color photos for cases where the application is submitted through the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not use local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers used for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 7 and 8 Article 93 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure complete and consistent mandatory information.
2. A geographical indication registration application shall ensure uniformity as prescribed in Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. Each application may register one geographical indication for a product which is determined by its nature, characteristics and relation to a geographical area, regardless of its form of existence or processing level.
3. The declaration for GDS registration must be filled in with all information as requested in appropriate places in the Declaration.
4. The geographical indications form (for geographical indications other than words) must be clearly presented with each sample not larger than 80 mm x 80 mm and not smaller than 20 mm x 20 mm.
5. The description of peculiar characteristics must include the following contents:
a) The detailed content description, including the following main information:

a1) Information on geographical indications and products bearing geographical indications;

a2) Characteristics and specific quality of products bearing geographical indications determined by geographical conditions - determined by perceptible, qualitative, physical, chemical and biological quantitative indicators. These indicators must be identifiable and verifiable by technical means or experts according to sampling standards and testing methods; and/or

a3) Reputation of products bearing geographical indications determined by geographical conditions through widespread knowledge about such products and selection of such products among relevant consumers, which can be verified; and

a4) Geographical area;

a5) Geographical conditions creating characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications, including climatic, hydrological, geological, topographical and ecological factors and other natural conditions; skills and techniques of producers, including traditional production processes of local areas (including one, several or all stages from raw material production and processing to product creation and may also include product packaging if such packaging affects the characteristics, quality and reputation of products), where such processes are elements creating and maintaining characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications, including clear and detailed information that can be verified (if there is any secret or technical secret not yet disclosed or widely known outside of local areas in the above-mentioned information, the applicant has the right to refuse to provide detailed information about such secrets or techniques if he/she does not commit to keep such information confidential at his/her request); and

a6) Organic relationship between characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications mentioned in points a2 and a3 of this clause with geographical conditions mentioned in point a5 of this clause;

a7) Information on the mechanism for self-inspection of characteristics and specific quality of products;
b) Summary section, including summary of specific characteristics and quality, reputation of products, geographical conditions contributing to specific characteristics and quality, reputation of products, and organic relationship between specific characteristics and quality, reputation of products bearing GIs and geographical conditions.
6. The description of peculiar characteristics as prescribed in Clause 5 of this Article must be accompanied by documents proving that information on characteristics and quality/renown is well-grounded and accurate (test results, research, investigation, etc.). Characteristics and quality stated in the description must be characteristic characteristics and quality of products of all manufacturers in the corresponding geographical area (determined based on the collection, statistics, analysis, and synthesis of relevant information and data from all manufacturers in the geographical area or by using appropriate sampling methods to ensure representativeness for all manufacturers in the geographical area). The applicant shall be responsible before law for the accuracy of the information stated in the description of peculiar characteristics and accompanying documents.

The description of particular characteristics shall be considered well-grounded and accurate when the physical, chemical or biological indicators of products bearing a geographical indication have been analyzed or tested at recognized testing organizations in accordance with laws on technical standards and regulations and other relevant laws related to the recognition scope, including corresponding tests.
In case physical, chemical and biological indicators of products cannot be carried out at recognized testing organizations due to unmet technical conditions (no standardized testing methods or no recognized testing organizations suitable for the type or characteristics of products, etc.), applicants may use analysis and testing results from unrecognized organizations or other analysis methods but must provide documents proving sampling, analysis, testing, implementation conditions, scientific and practical basis of the method, assessment of reliability of results and other relevant documents to ensure scientific, objective and honest results.
7. The map of the geographical area corresponding to a GIs must fully show information which can be used to accurately identify the geographical area with natural conditions creating particular characteristics and quality and reputation of products. For Vietnamese GIs, the map must be certified by the People’s Committee of the province or centrally run city where the geographical area corresponding to the GI is located and may be enclosed with a description document on the geographical area.
8. Presentation documents on conditions for use and methods of presentation of geographical indications to ensure the ability to distinguish between geographical indications (in case of homonym geographical indications) must specify the method of presenting and indicating information about geographical indications on products (product packaging, business facilities, transaction documents) in actual production and business activities of products bearing geographical indications to clearly determine the geographical origin of the real products and ensure consumers are not confused that products bearing such geographical indications originate from other geographical areas, for example, fully specifying the geographical origin as the name of a country/province or city next to the geographical indication or using other identification signs accompanied by the geographical indication such as images, logos/iconography or other rules for labeling goods, etc.
Article 95. Receipt of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. A geographical indication registration application shall be accepted when it fully satisfies the following conditions:
a) Having all minimum documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Description of peculiar characteristics;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. In case an application fully satisfies the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and verify the submission date and application number of such application and issue a receipt to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number, and results of the verification of the list of documents included in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For electronic applications, upon receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue an application receipt to the applicant, which includes information on the submission date, the application number, and the results of the inspection of the list of documents in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application submission system.
3. In case an application fails to meet all conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, it shall be rejected for receipt (if the application is submitted by post or via electronic submission system, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reasons for rejection). For applications rejected for receipt by post, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return documents of the application except for the original submitted.
Section 2. VERIFICATION OF REGISTRATION FORMS OF GEOGRAPHICAL GUIDELINES
Article 96. Format appraisal of geographical indication registration applications
1. The formal examination of a geographical indication registration application under Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means an examination of compliance with regulations on form of the application in order to conclude whether the application is valid or not.
2. A geographical indication registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Article 88 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed in contravention of Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c/ Having grounds to affirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not protected by the State under Articles 8 and 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed (including cases where the fee for filing the application, the charge for publication of the application, the charge for examination of the application, and the charge for information lookup for examination are not fully paid);
dd) The application does not meet the requirements regarding the form specified in Article 94 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding the quantity of one of the mandatory documents; failing to meet the requirements regarding the presentation format; the declaration does not clearly state the product bearing the geographical indication; information about the applicant or the organization managing the geographical indication in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not certified as prescribed, the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the applicant, the organization managing the geographical indication, the representative, and there is no signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.;
e) No valid written authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information and evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. After the above time limit, if the applicant fails to submit supplementary documents satisfying the requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents in the application.
4. Notification of the results of the formal assessment shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the Certificate of geographical indication registration, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies in the application, and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinion or correct deficiencies;
b/ If, upon the expiration of the time limit prescribed at Point a of this Clause, the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to make satisfactory correction thereof or does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection, within 15 working days after the expiration of such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse to grant a geographical indication registration certificate and send it to the applicant;
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article (or the applicant has made satisfactory corrections to deficiencies or provided a legitimate explanation), the application shall be determined as valid and proceed with content disclosure and appraisal as prescribed.
5. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of geographical indication registration applications that are transferred for publication, specifying the application number, filing date and date of transfer for publication of each application.
6. The date of filing an application for registration of a geographical indication shall be determined as follows:
a) The submission date is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam according to Clause 2 Article 95 hereof. In case the application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative working hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date specified in this Point shall be the next working day;
b) For a GIP submitted under an international request, the submission date shall be the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the official written request from the party participating in the negotiation of the international treaty, including a brief description of the particular characteristics.
Article 97. Publication of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. A valid geographical indication registration application shall be published in accordance with Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
2. Contents of a geographical indication registration application disclosed in the Industrial Property Official Gazette include information on the applicant, the geographical indication management organization, the representative (if any); the application number and submission date; information on the geographical indication, products bearing the geographical indication, geographical area, summary specified at Point b, Clause 5, Article 94 of this Circular, and other information (if any).
3. Description of peculiar characteristics shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 3. CONTENT CONSULTATION OF REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS FOR GEOGRAPHICAL GUIDELINES
Article 98. Substantive examination of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application under Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application according to protection conditions and determination of the corresponding scope of protection under this Article.
2. The assessment of the ability to grant protection titles for geographical indication registration applications shall include:
a) Assess the conformity of the subject matters stated in the application for geographical indication registration with the requirements for issuance of a certificate of geographical indication registration as prescribed in Article 100 of this Circular;
b) Assess the subject matters stated in the geographical indication registration application according to the protection conditions specified in Article 101 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for a title of protection shall be completed after assessing the conformity of the subject matter stated in the application and the subject matter stated in the application with all the protection conditions and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter is or is not conformable, does not satisfy or satisfies the protection conditions, specifically:

a) A reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy one or several or all of the protection conditions; or b) No reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Based on the results of assessment of the ability to grant protection titles prescribed in clauses 2 and 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding expected protection scope.
5. During the process of content appraisal, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations for the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify the deficiencies.
Article 99. Minimum information sources
The minimum sources of information for content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application include:
1. Marks currently protected in Vietnam for products identical or similar to those bearing a geographical indication, with the protection starting date earlier than the date of filing the application for registration of such geographical indication, including marks protected under international treaties to which Vietnam is a contracting party.
2. Marks in applications for registration of marks with an earlier submission date or priority date in Vietnam for products identical or similar to those bearing a GIP.
3. Marks shall be recognized by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as well-known marks.
4. Geographical indications currently under protection in Vietnam for products identical to those with such geographical indications.
Article 100. Assessment of conformity of subject matters stated in geographical indication registration applications Subject matters stated in geographical indication registration applications shall be considered unconformable with a type of geographical indication protection title if such subject matters are not visible signs used to indicate the geographical origin of products originating from specific regions, localities, territories or countries according to Clause 22 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 101. Assessment of conditions for protection of geographical indications
1. The assessment of a GIP shall be carried out according to the following regulations:
a) The subject matters stated in the application satisfy the conditions specified in Article 79 of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically, they must fully prove the following conditions:

a1) There exists a geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication stated in the application;

a2) The product originates from the aforementioned geographical area;

a3) The product has specific characteristics and quality and/or reputation determined by the geographical conditions of the aforementioned geographical area according to the provisions of Article 82 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The subject matters stated in the application do not fall into the cases specified in Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In particular, a geographical indication is considered misleading consumers as to the true geographical origin of products bearing such geographical indication according to Clause 4 Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property if such geographical indication is identical or similar to the point of similarity with which it is possible to cause confusion with a sign currently known as a geographical indication of goods or services, causing relevant consumers in the territory of Vietnam to misunderstand the actual origin of goods or services;
c/ The assessment of conditions for protection of geographical indications under Points a and b of this Clause shall be based on information provided by applicants and on information found from the minimum information sources specified in Article 99 of this Circular;
d) Depending on the complexity of assessing the protection conditions specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may consult independent consulting experts, the Geographical Indication Consultation Council or conduct additional surveys.
The geographical indication consultancy council shall be established and operate on the basis of the principle prescribed in the decision of the Director of the National Office of Intellectual Property.
Independent consultants shall be selected from the List of industrial property consultancy experts compiled by the National Office of Intellectual Property and other sources (in case no suitable expert is available in such list).
2. In the course of assessment of conditions for protection of a geographical indication, if a mark identical or similar to such a geographical indication is found, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the mark owner thereof for its opinion as prescribed in Article 112 or Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. During the process of assessing conditions for protection of a geographical indication, if there are grounds to determine that the geographical indication in the application is homonymic with a protected geographical indication as prescribed in Clause 22a Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property or homonymic with a geographical indication in another accepted application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant and request the applicant to submit an explanatory document on the conditions for use and the method of presentation of the geographical indication to ensure the distinguishability between such geographical indications. At the same time, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the owner of the protected geographical indication and request information on the method of presentation of the geographical indication to serve as a basis for considering and assessing the distinguishability among homonymic geographical indications. In case two or more geographical indications in the submitted applications are determined to be homonymic with each other, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request all applicants to submit explanatory documents on the conditions for use and the method of presentation of the geographical indication to serve as a basis for deciding on protection.
In case of necessity, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall work with all applicants and owners of identical geographical indications to clarify conditions for use and presentation of geographical indications before deciding to grant protection titles.
Article 102. Issuance and refusal to issue certificates of geographical indications registration
1. An application for registration of a GI shall be rejected to grant a certificate of GI registration if it falls into the cases specified at Points a, b and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. A geographical indication registration application shall be granted a certificate of registered geographical indication when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For a geographical indication registration application falling into the cases specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matters stated in the application satisfy the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of a geographical indication registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the registration fee for decision on issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate;
b) If the subject matter stated in the application does not meet the protection conditions or falls under the cases specified at Points b and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, or meets the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the geographical indication registration certificate, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application, and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide their opinion;
c/ In case the applicant fails to correct errors or does not make any objection within the time limit prescribed at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days after the expiry of such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse granting a certificate of geographical indication registration;
d) In case the applicant corrects deficiencies or provides opinions within the time limit specified in point b of this clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the correction document or opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the content appraisal as follows:

d1) If the applicant has sufficiently corrected deficiencies or provided justifiable explanations for the content appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the registration fee for decision on issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate;

d2) If the applicant fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies or provides unsatisfactory opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the geographical indication registration certificate;
dd) If the applicant submits all and on time fees and charges prescribed in point a, d1 of this clause upon expiry of the time limit specified in point a, d1 of this clause, within 01 month from the date of submission of all fees and charges as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant a geographical indication registration certificate;
e) If the applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges within the time limit specified at Points a and d1 of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on refusal to grant a certificate of geographical indication registration within 15 working days from the date of expiry of the corresponding time limit.
4. The decision on grant of a geographical indication registration certificate shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance of such decision. Published contents shall include information in the decision on grant of a geographical indication registration certificate (including decision number and issuing date) and the contents specified in Clause 2, Article 97 of this Circular.
The description of peculiar characteristics shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5. The certificate of geographical indication registration made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued in both electronic and paper forms (if the applicant requests a paper version).
A copy of a geographical indication registration certificate made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests such copy and it is stated in the declaration for geographical indication registration.

Section 4. HANDLING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO REGISTRATION FORMS OF GEOGRAPHICAL GUIDELINES
Article 103. Handling of objections to applications for registration of geographical indications

1. An application for registration of a geographical indication shall be rejected if it is rejected by at least 50% of the members of the panel.

2. The panel shall consider the objections in the application and make recommendations on how to resolve them.
1. Within 3 months after a geographical indication registration application is published, any third party may object to the grant of a protection title for such application and shall pay fees and charges.
Objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese accompanied by documents or sources of information to prove. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. In case an objection to a geographical indication registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process such objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 1 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response;
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the objector and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) On the basis of information, evidence and arguments provided by the parties, results of the dialogue (if any) as prescribed in Points a, b and c of this Clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the result of processing the objection together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application to the objector, except for the case specified at Point dd of this Clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the opposition of the applicant about the withdrawal of the geographical indication registration application according to Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 01 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of the application.
3. In case of objections related to the right to register geographical indications, such objections shall be handled as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector to file a lawsuit petition to the competent Court in accordance with civil procedure law and impose a time limit of 02 months from the notification date for the objector to submit a copy of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit;
b) Upon expiry of the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if the objector fails to submit copies of the notice of acceptance of the case or his/her objection, such objection shall be deemed withdrawn and the geographical indication registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the geographical indication registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the Court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the Court.
4. The time limit for the applicant to respond to the objection and the time limit for the objector to respond to the applicant’s opinion as prescribed in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal of the application.
Article 104. Amendment to geographical indication registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of geographical indication registration, applicants may take the initiative or based on notification of appraisal results to amend and supplement documents in their applications for geographical indication registration.
2. Requests for amendments to geographical indication registration applications shall include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment to application by applicant, request for amendment shall be made using Form No. 10 under Appendix I hereof;
b) In case of amendment to the application on the basis of notification of appraisal results from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, such amendment shall be made in writing with contents of amendment specified;
c) The applicant may request amendments to the same content related to multiple geographical indications registered in one declaration or one written request for amendments;
d) In case of amendments to the description of peculiar characteristics or map of geographical area, the applicant must submit the amended description of peculiar characteristics and map of geographical area;

dd) In case of change to the name and address or country code of the applicant or the geographical indication-managing organization, the applicant shall submit a certification document (original or certified copy) or legal document (certified copy) proving such change (decision on change of name or address; certificate of enterprise registration which records the change of name or address, etc.), except for cases where the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may take initiative in extracting and using information already available in databases publicly disclosed by database managing agencies. In case the information cannot be extracted or is not fully and accurately extracted, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to the individual or organization to supplement documents and provide explanation. The time for requesting and supplementing documents shall not be included in the administrative procedure settlement time limit;
e) In case of change of industrial property representatives, applicants shall submit documents declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representatives and change of new industrial property representatives as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. The applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee for each amendment and a fee for disclosure of amendments to the application if such amendments must be disclosed under Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except where such amendments are made to correct errors of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Any amendment to a geographical indication registration application must not expand the scope (volume) of protection stated in such application and must not change the nature of the geographical indication subject matter stated in such application.
5. In case of amendment or supplementation of the application by the applicant, the request for amendment or supplementation shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment to the application satisfies the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendment to the application (except in cases where the amendment is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant a protection title due to invalidation of the application).
Amendments and supplements shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette in case amendments or supplements to information related to a valid application in terms of form are requested, including descriptions of particular characteristics and products bearing GIs;
b) If the request for amendment to the application fails to satisfy the requirements specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application, clearly stating the reasons therefor and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of such notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opposing opinions. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, no opposing opinions, or provides inadequate opposing opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment to the application;
c) In case of request for amendment to application related to description of particular characteristics and geographical area corresponding to GIs submitted after notice of intended issuance of certificate of registration of GI, the application for registration of GI shall be re-examined and applicant must pay fees as prescribed.
6. In case of amendment or supplementation of an application by the applicant on the basis of notification of appraisal results from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the acceptance or rejection of such amendment or supplementation shall be made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the notification of appraisal results or corresponding decision during processing of the geographical indication registration application.
7. In case the applicant requests amendments to the application, including amendments made by the applicant and amendments to the application based on notification of appraisal results, such request shall be made in the form of an application form as prescribed in Point a Clause 2 of this Article. The handling of such request shall comply with the provisions of Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 105. Recording of changes to applicants for registration of geographical indications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of registered geographical indication, the applicant may request recognition of changes in the applicant on the basis of inheritance or according to the decision of a competent authority.
2. An application for recognition of changes to the applicant for registration of a GIP shall satisfy the following requirements:
a) In case of recording changes to the applicant due to inheritance or inheritance or according to a decision of a competent authority, the applicant must submit a declaration made using Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular and documents proving the inheritance, inheritance of assets, rights and obligations arising from consolidation, merger, division, separation or other changes to the applicant's organization, or according to a decision of the Court or another competent authority.
b) The applicant shall pay fees and charges as prescribed.
3. The request for recognition of changes in applicants shall be processed as follows:

a) If the request for change of applicant satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of change of applicant and publish it in the Industrial Property Gazette (except for cases where the amendment or supplementation of the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the protection title due to invalidation of the application).
b) If the request for change of applicant fails to satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize such change and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate corrections, no objection, or provides inadequate objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to recognize the change of the applicant.
Article 106. Withdrawal of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam decides to grant or refuses to grant a certificate of registered geographical indication, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the application for registration of geographical indication.
2. The withdrawal of a geographical indication registration application shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative in writing. For an application filed through a representative, the written authorization must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or be enclosed with a command specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 2 months from the date of receipt of a declaration on withdrawal of a geographical indication registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal request as follows:
a) In case of withdrawal request satisfying the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal request and terminate the processing of the application, record the withdrawal in the application dossier. The withdrawn geographical indication registration application cannot be re-registered.
b) In case the withdrawal request fails to satisfy the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors;
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedges deficiencies but still fails to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal request rejection.
Article 107. Re-examination of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall carry out the re-examination of a geographical indication registration application in the following cases:
a/ Re-examination of a geographical indication registration application due to a written objection from a third party against the intended grant of a certificate of geographical indication registration in the notice of content examination results, together with legitimate evidence proving that the objection is due to force majeure events or objective obstacles, thus making it impossible to object to the application within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Re-appraise the application upon request for amendments or supplements to the application after notifying the results of the content appraisal stating the intended issuance of a geographical indication registration certificate as prescribed in Point c Clause 5 Article 104 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of a single-location registration application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of a geographical indication registration application shall comply with Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complicated cases with many facts to be verified or expert opinions to be solicited, the time limit for re-appraisal may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial appraisal prescribed in Clause 2, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The appraisal shall be carried out in accordance with the corresponding provisions of Articles 98 to 101 of this Circular;
c/ For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of a geographical indication registration application may be carried out only once for every third applicant.
3. After the results of the re-examination are available, a geographical indication registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For cases specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-assessment results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application according to the provisions of this Circular;
b) For cases specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application according to regulations in Article 102 of this Circular.
Chapter VII
REQUESTS AND HANDLING OF REQUESTS RELATED TO NATIONAL PROVOCATION OF ENGLISH DIPLOMAS

Article 108. Maintenance of validity of invention patents/utility solutions
1. An application for maintenance of validity of an invention patent/utility solution shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration, made according to Form No. 1 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
2. The request for maintenance of validity of an invention/utility solution patent and the fee for examination of the request for maintenance of validity, the validity maintenance fee, the fee for use of the invention/utility solution patent, the registration fee and the publication fee must be filed with the National Office of Intellectual Property before the end of the term of validity. This request may be filed after the above-said time limit but not more than 6 months after the end of the term of validity and the protection title holder shall pay a fee for each month of delay in accordance with the law on fees and charges.
3. Applications for maintenance of validity of invention patents/utility solutions shall be considered invalid in the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article or contains invalid documents;
b) The applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge as prescribed;
c) Request to maintain validity of submission not in a timely manner as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;
4. Applications for maintenance of validity shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of a valid application, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice on maintenance of validity of the protection title, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance of the notice;
b) In case of an unsatisfactory application, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to maintain the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide feedback;
c) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies within the time limit specified in point b of this clause, does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection, within 1 month from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to maintain validity.
d) In addition to the cases specified in point c of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to maintain validity in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired according to regulations at the time of submission of the application for validity maintenance; d2) The protection title was invalidated at the time of submission of the application or during the processing of the application.
5. In case the applicant submits a written request for withdrawal of the validity maintenance request, within 1 month from the receipt of such a document, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal of the validity maintenance request as follows:
a) In case of valid withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of the request and terminate the processing of the request for maintenance of validity. The withdrawn request for maintenance of validity cannot be restored.
b) In case the written request for withdrawal of validity is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the request and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are insufficient, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity.
Article 109. Extension of validity of industrial design patents, marks registration certificates
1. An industrial design patent shall be extended for a maximum of two consecutive times, five years each time. Where an industrial design is protected by multiple schemes, the patent may be extended to all or some of the schemes, including the basic scheme.
2. A certificate of trademark registration may be renewed for many consecutive times, 10 years for each time, for all or part of the list of goods and services.
3. In one application, an extension of validity may be requested for one or more than one industrial design patent; one or more than one mark registration certificate.
An application for extension of validity shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration, made according to Form No. 2 in Appendix II of this Circular;

b) Original copies of the industrial design patent, mark registration certificate (in case the protection title is issued in paper form and there is a request for recording of the extension thereof);
c) Authorization document (if the request is submitted through a representative). In case of extension for some plans or parts of the list of goods and services, information on authorization for extension with corresponding scope is required.
4. The request for extension of validity of an industrial design patent, a certificate of registered mark and the fee for examination of the request for validity extension, the validity extension fee of a protection title, the use fee of a protection title, the registration fee and the fee for publication of the decision on validity extension of a protection title shall be filed with the National Office of Intellectual Property within 6 months counting to the date of expiry of such industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark. A request for validity extension may be filed after the above-mentioned time limit but not more than 6 months after the expiry of the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark, provided that the protection title holder pays a fee for each month of delay in accordance with law on fees and charges.
5. A dossier of request for extension of validity shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 3 of this Article or contains invalid documents;
b) The applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge as prescribed;
c) Request for extension of validity of submission not within the prescribed time limit as specified in Clause 4 of this Article;
d) Lack of information confirming that the protection title holder authorizes only the extension for certain arrangements or parts of the list of goods and services (in case of submission by a representative).
6. Applications for extension of validity shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of a valid application, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on extension of validity, record it in the Industrial Design Patent, or the Mark Registration Certificate (if requested), register it and publish the decision on extension of validity in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance;
b) In case of an unsatisfactory application, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to extend validity, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide feedback;
c) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies within the time limit specified in point b of this clause, does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection, within 1 month from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the extension of validity.
d) In addition to the cases specified in point c of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse extension in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired according to regulations at the time of submission of the request for extension; d2) The protection title was cancelled at the time of submission of the application or during the processing of the application.
7. In case the applicant submits a written request for withdrawal of the validity extension, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of the request for withdrawal of maintenance of validity as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 108 of this Circular.
8. After procedures for extension have been completed, in case the owner of an industrial design patent or a certificate of registered mark requests the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to record the decision on extension of validity in the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark, he/she shall carry out procedures for amendment of the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark and pay fees and charges as prescribed.
Article 110. Amendment of titles of protection
1. Owners of protection titles, organizations and individuals exercising the right to register geographical indications, and organizations managing geographical indications may request amendments to the following information in protection titles, provided that they pay a fee for appraisal of requests for amendment to protection titles, a registration fee and a publication fee for the decision on recording of amendments to protection titles.
a) Changes to the name and address of the owner of the protection title; the name, address, and nationality of the author of the invention, industrial design, or layout design;
b) Changing the owner of a protection title (transferring ownership due to inheritance, acquisition, merger, division, separation, consolidation, joint venture, association, establishment of a new legal entity under the same ownership, changing business form or as decided by the Court or other competent authorities);
c/ Amending the description of the geographical indication or geographical area corresponding to such geographical indication, the regulations on use of collective mark or the regulations on use of certification mark. In this case, the protection title holder shall pay an additional fee for content appraisal.
2. The protection title holder may request narrowing of the scope of protection according to Clause 3, Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, provided that he/she pays a fee for examination of the request for amendment of the protection title, a fee for examination of the request for narrowing of the scope of protection, a registration fee and a fee for publication of the decision on amendment of the protection title in the following cases:
a/ Requesting the reduction of one or several independent or dependent points included in the protection request inscribed in the invention patent or utility solution patent;
b) Request the removal of one or several industrial design solutions, one or several products in the set of products in the industrial design patent.
c) Request to reduce one or several goods or services or group of goods or services on the list of goods and services written in the mark registration certificate or remove small details being elements subject to exclusion (not protected separately) without affecting the distinguishability of marks written in the mark registration certificate.
3. Application for amendments to the provisions in clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a/ Declaration requesting amendments, made according to Form No. 3 in Annex II of this Circular, clearly stating the request for amendments. One declaration requesting amendments may request amendments to many protection titles if they have the same content of amendment, provided that the requester pays fees according to regulations for each protection title;
b/ Original protection titles in case the protection titles are issued in paper form;
c) Documents certifying the change of name and address (original or certified true copy); decisions on change of name and address; certificates of enterprise registration which record the change of name and address; other legal documents proving the change of name and address (original or certified true copy), if the content requested for amendment is name and address;
d) Documents proving the transfer of ownership as prescribed in Point b Clause 1 of this Article (original copies or certified true copies) if the certificate holder wishes to change the holder of the protection title;
dd) Photographs or drawings of the industrial design modified if the request for modification of the title of protection of the industrial design is made (05 sets if the application is filed in paper form); description of geographical indications, map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication modified if the request for modification of the title of protection of the geographical indication is made (02 copies if the application is filed in paper form); regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks modified if the request for modification of the title of protection of the collective mark or certification mark is made (02 copies if the application is filed in paper form); sample of a mark, if the request for modification of the sample of a mark as prescribed at Point c, Clause 2 of this Article is made (05 samples if the application is filed in paper form);
e) Detailed presentation documents on amendments;
g/ The written authorization (if the request is filed through a representative). In case of amendment to the protection title under Clause 2 of this Article, there must be information on authorization for request for narrowing the scope of protection.
4. A dossier of request for amendment to a protection title shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 3 of this Article or contains invalid documents;
b) Request to narrow the scope of protection not falling within the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article;
c) The applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge as prescribed;
d) Lack of information confirming that the protection title holder authorizes the request for narrowing the scope of protection (in case the request is submitted through a representative).
5. Applications for amendments to protection titles shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of a valid application, within 2 months from the date of receipt of the application or from the date of expiration of the re-examination period specified at Point d of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to amend the protection title, record it in the protection title and register it, and publish such decision on the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance.
b) In case of invalid application dossiers, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment, clearly stating reasons and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opinions opposing such refusal.
c/ If, upon expiry of the time limit prescribed at Point b of this Clause, the applicant fails to correct errors or his/her correction is unsatisfactory, he/she does not raise any objection or raises unjustifiable objections, within one month after expiry of such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to reject the request for amendment of the protection title;

d) For amendments to the provisions specified in point c of clause 1 and clause 2 of this Article, procedures for re-appraisal of the corresponding application shall be carried out in accordance with Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) In case the protection title has been terminated or expired at the time of application submission as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to reject the request for amendment to the protection title, except where the amendment request aims to record accurate information before the termination of the protection title. In case the protection title has been invalidated at the time of application submission or during the processing of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to reject the request for amendment to the protection title;
e) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of requests for amendments to protection titles in cases where it receives written requests from competent authorities for suspension of processing to implement relevant decisions and procedures as prescribed by law or documents regarding the industrial property rights subject matter under the protection title being used as collateral. Requests for amendments shall continue to be processed after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives documents proving that the relevant decisions and procedures have been terminated.
6. In case an applicant submits a written request for withdrawal of the application dossier for amendment of a protection title, within 1 month after receiving such a request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal request as follows:
a) In case of a valid withdrawal request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance and terminate the processing of the request for amendment to the protection title. The withdrawn request for amendment to the protection title cannot be re-accepted.
b) In case of invalid request for withdrawal of application for amendments, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the request and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are insufficient, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal of the application.
7. In case of detecting any error in a protection title, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall, at its discretion or upon request of the person detecting the error, revoke the protection title and reissue the protection title with amended information. Within 2 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam revokes the original protection title, it shall reissue the protection title recording correct information and send it to the owner of the protection title.
The owner of a protection title shall pay an examination fee for requesting amendment to the protection title as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, registration fee and announcement fee for information about amendments if the protection title has a defect which is disclosed and caused by the fault of the owner of the protection title, except for the defect which is caused by the fault of the National Office of Intellectual Property.
Article 111. Termination of validity of protection titles
1. Organizations and individuals requesting termination of validity of protection titles under Clause 4 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall pay request fees, fees for appraisal of requests for termination of validity of protection titles, registration fees and fees for publication of decisions on termination of validity of protection titles.
2. A certificate of registered mark shall be invalidated because its owner or the licensee fails to use it for five consecutive years before requesting invalidation without a plausible reason as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property. A mark shall not be considered used if the act of using it is merely formal and does not reflect actual commercial exploitation of goods or services bearing such mark, as in the following cases:
a/ Use of a mark only for business preparation, research or testing of goods or services bearing such mark without yet giving rise to commercial transactions or without clear evidence of actual implementation for putting such goods or services on the market;
b) Use of a mark shall be made only within the internal operations of the applicant and is not intended to supply goods or services bearing such mark to the market;
c) Use of a mark shall be made only on documents, reports, designs, websites, advertising media or other information media and not associated with goods or services bearing the mark and plans to bring such goods or services to market or have brought to market.
3. In a request for termination of validity, only one protection title may be requested to be terminated.
An application for termination of validity of a protection title shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration requesting termination of validity of a protection title, made using Form No. 4 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b) Evidence (if any);
c) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
d) Explanation documents of the reasons for the request (specifying the number of protection title, reasons and legal grounds, contents proposed to terminate part or all of the validity of the protection title) and relevant documents.
4. Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request for termination of validity, if the application has formal errors (invalid authorization document; failure to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed; lack of necessary information to determine the scope of the request, etc.), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct the errors.
In case the applicant does not have a document to correct errors or has a document to correct errors but fails to meet requirements within the above time limit, within 01 month from the date of expiration of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to consider the request for termination of validity of the protection title.
5. In case a third party requests termination of validity of a protection title, the application for termination of validity of the protection title shall be processed within 05 months from the date of submission of the application; for complicated cases or where the owner of the protection title has a different opinion from the applicant for termination of validity, the processing time limit may be extended but must not exceed 07 months from the date of receipt of the application for termination of validity of the protection title.
An application for termination of validity of a protection title shall be handled as follows:
a) Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request or from the date on which the application for termination of satisfactory repair expires, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify in writing the opinion of a third party to the owner of the protection title, setting a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the protection title owner to provide an opinion;
b) After receiving feedback from the protection title holder within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the applicant for invalidation and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) In case information, documents and evidence provided by a third party or the owner of a protected title are considered unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the third party or the owner of the protected title to submit additional documents and information within 01 month from the date of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification;
d) Based on the information, evidence and arguments provided by the parties, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title or a notice of refusal to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title;
dd) The decision to terminate the validity of a protection title shall be recorded in the National Register of Industrial Property and published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance of such decision.
6. In case the owner of a protection title declares relinquishment of industrial property rights according to Clause 3 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property, within 1 month from the date of receipt of such declaration, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall handle it as follows:
a) Issue a decision to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title or notify the intended refusal to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title, specifying a time limit of 01 month for the owner of the protection title to provide feedback;
b/ Upon expiry of the above-said time limit, if the protection title holder does not give opinions or gives inadequate opinions, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice to refuse partial or total termination of validity of the protection title.
7. In case the applicant for termination of validity submits a written withdrawal of the request for termination of validity of the protection title, within 1 month from the receipt of such a document, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal as follows:
a) In case of withdrawal of valid request for termination of validity, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of request and terminate processing of the request for termination of validity of protection titles. The withdrawn request for termination of validity of protection title shall not be re-validated;
b) In case of withdrawal of an invalid request for termination of validity, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the request and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are insufficient, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal of the application and continue to consider the application for termination of validity of the protection title.
8. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall terminate the processing of the resolution of the entire or partial request for termination of validity of a protection title in cases where the protection title is subject to a request for termination of validity which has been entirely or partially terminated under a decision of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (or under a legally effective judgment or decision of the Court).
Article 112. Invalidation of protection titles

1. Organizations and individuals requesting cancellation of validity of protection titles under Clause 4 Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall pay request fees, fees for appraisal of requests for cancellation of validity of protection titles, registration fees and fees for announcement of decisions on cancellation of validity of protection titles.
2. The validity of an invention/utility solution patent shall be invalidated if the invention is granted a patent beyond the disclosure scope in the initial description of the invention registration application according to Point dd Clause 2 Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property in cases where, compared with the initial description and for persons with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field, the description of the invention contains changes in content and such changes lead to information not directly and clearly derived from the initial description of the application, specifically as follows:
a) During the amendment and supplementation of the application, the applicant inserts technical specifications or technical specifications that cannot be directly and clearly identified from the initial description;
b) Supplement information (including information on purpose, effectiveness, etc.) that cannot be directly and clearly determined from the initial description (including drawings) and/or initial protection request to clearly disclose the invention or fully disclose the protection request;
c) The added contents in the description are technical specifications related to size parameters obtained by measuring size parameters on drawings;
d) Including details or additional components not mentioned in the initial description of the application, leading to effects and/or special effects not present in the initial application;
dd) Adding to the description of effects and/or benefits which persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field cannot determine from the initial application;
e) Change of technical specifications of the protection request, which are not directly and clearly identified or disclosed from the initial description;
g) Introducing new contents by changing undefined contents into specific and definite ones;
h) Combining separate technical signs of the initial application into a new technical sign while the relationship between these technical signs is not disclosed in the initial application;
i) Changing one or more technical specifications in the description to make them different from those stated in the initial description;
k) Removing a technical indicator from the protection request point where such technical indicator is necessary for the protected subject to achieve the set objective and/or the removal of such technical indicator changes other technical indicators or (more than one) technical indicators.
3. A certificate of registered mark shall be invalidated if it is intentionally cancelled by the applicant for registration of the mark according to Point a Clause 1 Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property in one of the following cases:
a) The applicant registers a large number of marks which are identical or confusingly similar to marks currently used by others in Vietnam for identical or similar goods or services but such registration exceeds normal business capacity and there is no evidence showing that the applicant intends to actually use these marks in production and business activities; or

b) At the time of filing the application, the registered mark is identical or confusingly similar to a mark already recognized by relevant consumers in Vietnam as a sign indicating the commercial origin of identical or similar goods or services of another person or a mark which is identical or confusingly similar to a well-known mark in other countries; and such registration aims to take advantage of the reputation of such mark to make profits or is mainly intended to resell, license or transfer the registration right to the holder of marks mentioned in this clause or to prevent the market entry of the holder of marks mentioned in this clause in order to limit competition or acts contrary to other fair trade practices.
4. In a request for invalidation, only one protection title may be requested to be invalidated. A request for invalidation of a protection title shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration requesting cancellation of validity of protection title, made using Form No. 5 under Appendix II hereof;
b) Evidence (if any);
c) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
d) Explanation documents of the request (specifying the number of title, reason, legal grounds, and contents proposed for partial or full cancellation of the validity of the protection title) and relevant documents;
5. Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request for invalidation, if the application has formal deficiencies (e.g., the letter of authorization is invalid; fees and charges are not fully paid as prescribed; necessary information is missing to determine the scope of the request, etc.), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct the deficiencies.
In case the applicant does not have a document to correct errors or has a document to correct errors but fails to meet requirements within the above time limit, within 01 month from the date of expiration of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to consider the request for invalidation of the protection title.
6. Applications for invalidation of protection titles shall be processed within 12 months for applications for invalidation of invention/good solution patents and 5 months for applications for invalidation of industrial design patents, layout-design registration certificates, marks registration certificates or geographical indication registration certificates from the date of filing the application for invalidation; for complicated cases, this time limit may be extended by up to 3 months. Applications for invalidation of protection titles shall be processed as follows:
a) Within 01 month from the date of receipt of the request or from the date of the written request for cancellation of satisfactory repair, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify in writing the opinion of a third party to the owner of the title of protection, setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the owner of the title of protection to provide their opinion;
b) After receiving feedback from the holder of the protection title within the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the applicant for invalidation and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) In case information, documents and evidence provided by a third party or the owner of a protected title are unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the third party or the owner of the protected title to submit additional documents and information for verification or clarification within 01 month from the date of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. Where necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may organize direct consultation between the third party and the owner of the protected title to clarify relevant matters;
d) Based on the information, evidence, arguments and documents provided by the parties, the results of direct communication and appraisal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to partially or entirely invalidate the protection title or notify refusal to partially or entirely invalidate the protection title.
dd) The decision to cancel the validity of a protection title shall be recorded in the National Register of Industrial Property and published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance of such decision.
7. In case the applicant for invalidation submits a written request for withdrawal of the protection certificate, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of withdrawal of the request for termination of validity as prescribed in Clause 7 Article 111 of this Circular.
8. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall terminate the processing of the resolution of the entire or partial request for invalidation of a protection title in cases where the protection title is subject to an invalidation request and has been entirely or partially invalidated under a decision of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (or under a legally effective judgment/decision of a competent court).
9. The provisions of clauses 2 and 3 of this Article shall also apply to the processing of applications for registration of inventions and marks.
Article 113. Termination or invalidation of international registration

1. An international registration may be terminated or invalidated by a decision of the International Bureau of the World Intellectual Property Organization (hereinafter referred to as the WIPO) in accordance with the provisions of Article 114 of this Treaty.

2. The decision to terminate or invalidate an international registration shall be made available to all parties to the international registration.
1. An application for termination or invalidation of an international registration of a mark shall be processed as follows:
a) For an application for termination or invalidation of international registration of a mark under the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol filed by a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the contents of such application to the mark owner via the International Office, setting a time limit of 3 months from the notification date for the mark owner to provide its opinion;
b) The international registration of a mark may be terminated or invalidated for part or all of the goods or services list;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to terminate or invalidate the international registration of a mark for part or all of the goods and service list and such decision is no longer subject to administrative complaints or lawsuits, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of termination or invalidation of the international registration of the mark using the form provided by the International Office, specifying the goods and services subject to termination or invalidation, and send this notice to the International Office;
d) Other relevant provisions in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular on handling requests for termination or invalidation of protection titles based on mark registration applications filed under the national filing system prescribed in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular shall apply to the handling of requests for termination or invalidation of international mark registrations.
2. An application for termination or invalidation of international registration of an industrial design shall be processed as follows:

a) For an application for termination or invalidation of international registration of industrial designs filed by a third party under the Hague Agreement, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the content of such application to the owner of the industrial design via the International Office and impose a time limit of 3 months from the date of notification for the owner of the industrial design to provide their opinion;
b) International registration of industrial designs may be invalidated for some or all industrial designs in such registration;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to invalidate international registrations for some or all industrial designs and such decision is no longer subject to administrative complaints or lawsuits, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of invalidation of international registrations of industrial designs using the form of the International Office, specifying the industrial designs whose validity has been invalidated, and send this notice to the International Office;
d) Other relevant provisions in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular on handling requests for termination or invalidation of protection titles granted based on national registration applications submitted under the national filing form shall apply to requests for termination or invalidation of international registrations of industrial designs.
Chapter VIII
   REGISTRATION OF CONTRACTS FOR ASSIGNMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Article 114. Applications for registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights
1. The owner of a protection title (the assignor) or the industrial property right transferee may file an application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract with the National Office of Industrial Property to request the latter to record the industrial property right assignment, provided that the fee for dossier appraisal and lookup (in case of mark assignment), the fee for grant of certificate of registered mark (in case of partial assignment of the list of goods and services bearing marks), the registration fee and the fee for announcement of the decision on recording the industrial property right assignment are paid.
2. An application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration for registration of industrial property right transfer contract, made according to Form No. 6 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b/ Contract (original or certified true copy as prescribed); if the contract is made in a language other than Vietnamese, it must be enclosed with a Vietnamese version; if the contract has many pages, each page must bear the signatures of the parties or be appended with a seal on every two adjoining pages;
c/ Original protection titles in case the protection titles are issued in paper form;
d) Written consent of co-owners regarding the transfer of industrial property rights, if such industrial property rights are under joint ownership; or the mortgagee in cases where industrial property rights are being used as mortgaged assets;
dd) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
e) For applications for registration of contracts on transfer of collective marks or certification marks, apart from the above documents, the following documents are required: e1) Regulations on use of collective marks or certification marks of the transferee party as prescribed in Article 105 of the Law on Intellectual Property; e2) Documents evidencing the right to file an application of the transferee party to certification marks or collective marks as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4, Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In this case, apart from fees and charges applicable to applications for registration of industrial property rights transfer contracts as prescribed, the applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee.
3. Each application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract shall be recorded for only one step of assignment. In case an industrial property object is assigned in more than one steps, each step of assignment must be registered with a separate application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract.
Article 115. Procedures for processing applications for registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights
1. An application for registration of an industrial property right assignment contract shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all the documents specified in Clause 2 Article 114 of this Circular or contains invalid documents, including: The contract does not have both parties’ signatures (and seals, if any); name and address of the transferor in the contract do not match the corresponding information in the protection title, authorization document, and declaration; name and address of the transferee in the contract do not match the name and address stated in the authorization document and declaration;

The contract is not certified as valid; the transfer contract lacks required contents or contains contents inconsistent with regulations on conditions restricting transfer of industrial property rights prescribed in Articles 139 and 140 of the Law on Intellectual Property, etc.;
b) The applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge as prescribed;
c) The assignor is not the owner of the protection title;
d/ There are grounds to affirm that the transfer of industrial property rights infringes upon the industrial property rights of a third party.
2. In case of a valid application for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract, within 02 months after receiving such application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) Issue decisions on recording of transfer of industrial property rights;
b/ Recording in the title of protection the new owner; in case of partial transfer of the list of goods or services bearing a protected mark, granting a certificate of registration of mark to the transferee and defining the scope of the transferred part of the list of goods or services in the original title of protection;
c) Record the transfer of industrial property rights in the National Register of Industrial Property;
d/ Publicizing the decision on recording of the transfer of industrial property rights in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days after its issuance.
3. In case an application for registration of a transfer contract of industrial property rights is invalid, within 2 months after receiving such application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to register the contract, clearly stating the deficiencies in the application and fixing a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct such deficiencies or to provide opinions opposing the intended refusal to register the contract.
4. In case the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, does not have any objection or provides inadequate objection regarding the intended refusal to register the contract within the time limit specified in Clause 3 of this Article, within 2 months from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to register the contract.
5. In case after receiving the application for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract, the parties have a dispute related to the contract or there is a written request from a competent agency or organization for the National Office of Intellectual Property to suspend the processing of the application in order to carry out relevant decisions and procedures as prescribed by law or documents on the use of industrial property rights subject matter under protection titles as security assets, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall suspend the consideration of the application until it receives documents proving that the dispute has been resolved or documents proving that the relevant decisions and procedures have been terminated; after that, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall continue to consider the application according to regulations.
6. In case the protection title is subject of an industrial property right transfer contract which has been terminated or invalidated or its protection term expires as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse registration of such industrial property right transfer contract.
7. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to record the transfer or reject registration of an industrial property right transfer contract, if either party wishes to withdraw the application for registration of the industrial property right transfer contract, it must obtain the consent of the other party regarding the withdrawal of the submitted application, except in cases where the application is withdrawn due to irreparable deficiencies as requested by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Within 1 month from the date of receipt of a request for withdrawal of application dossiers for transfer contract registration, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process such request as follows:
a) In case of a valid withdrawal request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal request and terminate the processing of the transfer contract registration request. The withdrawn transfer contract registration request cannot be re-registered;
b) In case the application for withdrawal of the application is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the application and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are insufficient, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal of the application.
Chapter IX
 COMPLAINTS AND RESOLUTION OF COMPLAINTS RELATED TO INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY PROCEDURES
Article 116. Persons having the right to complain, subjects of complaint, statute of limitations for filing a complaint and persons settling complaints
1. Persons with complaint rights are organizations and individuals specified in Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Decisions and notifications subject to complaints specified in Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property are official decisions and notifications issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or organizations authorized or delegated by People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities to carry out procedures related to industrial property according to corresponding provisions of this Circular and Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, including the following decisions and notifications:
a) Notify the applicant of the refusal to accept the application;
b) Notify acceptance or rejection of requests for amendments to applications, conversion of applications, change of applicants, withdrawal of applications;
c/ Decision to refuse granting of protection titles; decision to grant protection titles;
d) Decision to refuse to grant protection for internationally registered industrial designs; Decision to refuse to grant protection for internationally registered marks; Decision to grant protection for internationally registered industrial designs; Decision to grant protection for internationally registered marks;
dd) Notice of maintenance of validity of protection titles; decisions to refuse to maintain validity of protection titles;
e) Decision on extension of validity of protection titles; decision on refusal to extend validity of protection titles;
g) Decision on amendments to protection titles; decision on refusal of requests for amendments to protection titles;
h) Decision to terminate or partially or entirely annul validity of a protection title; notice of refusal to terminate or annul validity of a protection title;
i) Decisions and notices on processing of applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of marks; decisions and notices on processing of applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of industrial designs;
k) Decision on recognition of transfer of industrial property rights, decision on refusal to register an industrial property right transfer contract; decision on recognition of transfer of ownership of industrial property object, decision on refusal to register a contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property object;
l) Decisions, notices and other acts that meet conditions are subjects of complaints as prescribed by law on complaints.
Notifications of information and requests for completion of dossiers which are not administrative decisions shall not be subject to complaints, such as notifications of appraisal results, notices of deficiencies, requests for amendment or supplementation of documents, proposed refusals, temporary notices of refusal to protect internationally registered marks, or notices of refusal to protect internationally registered industrial designs.
3. The following contents shall not be accepted in the course of complaint settlement:
a) Requests for amendments to applications for registration of establishment of industrial property rights subject to complained decisions or notices (such as requests for amendments to invention descriptions (including protection requests); requests for amendments to sets of photos or drawings and descriptions of industrial designs; requests for amendments to mark samples and lists of goods or services bearing marks; requests for amendments to product names bearing GIs and descriptions of specific properties of products bearing GIs and maps of geographical areas corresponding to GIs for GI registration applications), except where otherwise provided by international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory;
b/ An existing circumstance in the process of examination of an industrial property registration application which, for objective reasons, the National Office of Industrial Property and concerned organizations or individuals can only know after a decision on grant or refusal to grant a protection title is issued (hereinafter referred to as new circumstance), except for cases where such circumstance is presented by a third party under Point b. Clause 6, Article 119 of this Circular.
4. The complaint must be filed within the statute of limitations prescribed in Articles 9 and 33 of the Law on Complaints.
5. The person competent to resolve first-time complaints is the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam; the person competent to resolve second-time complaints is the Minister of Science and Technology (hereinafter referred to as “complaint resolver”) for procedures related to industrial property rights prescribed in this Circular. For procedures related to industrial property rights prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the persons competent to resolve first-time and second-time complaints shall be determined in accordance with the law on complaints.
Article 117. Complaint letters
1. The complaint shall be made in accordance with Clause 1 Article 3 of Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP dated October 19, 2020 on elaboration of certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Complaints (hereinafter referred to as “Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP”), which specifies information specified in Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and documents related to the content of the complaint as prescribed in Clause 2.

Article 8 of the Law on Complaints, specifically as follows:
a/ Copies of the complained decision or notice and copies of the industrial property registration application are subject matter of such decision or notice (in case of a second complainant); or documents guiding information on the above-said documents;
b) Copies of decisions on first-time complaint settlement (for second-time complaints);
c) Evidence (evidence or material evidence) used to prove and clarify the complaint argument. Evidence may be submitted within 1 month from the date of filing the complaint.
2. In case a complaint is filed by a lawful representative in Vietnam as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property, the applicant must enclose a letter of authorization or a copy of the letter of authorization if it falls under the case specified in Clause 5 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. A complaint may be lodged against one official decision or notice related to industrial property procedures.
Article 118. Withdrawal of complaints and termination of complaint settlement
1. The complainant may withdraw his/her complaint according to Article 10 of the Law on Complaints. If the withdrawal is made under the authorization of the complainant, such authorization must be clearly stated in the written authorization. The withdrawn complaint shall be considered as not submitted. The complainant shall not be refunded the complaint and the paid complaint settlement service provision fees, except for cases where the complaint is withdrawn before the date of notification of the acceptance or non-acceptance of the complaint.
2. The complaint resolver shall issue a decision to terminate the resolution of the complaint in the following cases:
a) The complainant withdraws his/her complaint;
b) Protection titles, international registration of marks or international registration of industrial designs in Vietnam which have been invalidated or international mark registrations which have become invalid according to relevant international treaties.
Article 119. Order and procedures for settling complaints
1. The acceptance of a complaint shall comply with Article 27 of the Law on Complaints and Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically as follows:
a) Within 10 working days from the date of receipt of the complaint application, the complaint resolver shall:

a1) Issue a notice of non-acceptance of complaint resolution if the application falls under one of the cases specified at Point b of this Clause, clearly stating the reason for refusal;

or

a2) Issue a notice of acceptance of complaint resolution if the application does not fall under the cases specified at Point b of this Clause, specifying the date of acceptance and determining the lookup fee and/or appraisal fee in case of re-appraisal to serve the resolution of the complaint corresponding to the content of the complaint (if any), and impose a time limit of 01 month for the complainant to pay the fee;
b) The complaint shall not be accepted for resolution in any of the following cases: b1) The subject matter of the complaint is not an official decision or notice prescribed in Clause 2 Article 116 of this Circular; b2) The decision, notice, or act complained about does not directly relate to the lawful rights and interests of the complainant; b3) The complaint is not filed in accordance with Clause 2 Article 117 of this Circular; b4) The complaint is filed beyond the prescriptive period, except for the cases specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 15 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP; b5) The complaint has already been resolved by a second complaint resolution decision; b6) The complaint has been accepted by the Court or resolved by a judgment or decision of the Court, except for the case where there is a decision to terminate the resolution of the administrative case by the Court; b7) The complainant continues to file a complaint after the 30-day time limit from the date of issuance of the decision to terminate the resolution of the complaint as prescribed in Article 10 and Clause 8 Article 11 of the Law on Complaints; b8) The complaint does not specify illegal elements of the decision, notice, or act complained about and only requests amendments to the complaint that is the subject matter of such decision or notice; b9) The complaint is against a notice, administrative decision, or act related to an industrial property subject matter classified as state secret.
c/ If the complainant fails to pay the appraisal fee for the case subject to re-appraisal in service of the complaint settlement according to the notice of complaint acceptance and settlement specified at Point a2 of this Clause, the complaint shall be settled on the basis of documents included in the dossier.

2. The time limit for complaint settlement shall comply with Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Articles 28 and 37 of the Law on Complaints.
3. To verify the content of the complaint, the complaint resolver shall seek opinions from persons with rights and obligations related to the received complaint according to Article 21 of Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP (hereinafter referred to as “relevant parties”) as follows:
a) The complaint resolver shall notify in writing the content of the complaint to the related party and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of notification for the latter to provide its opinion (if any);
b) The involved parties may provide information and evidence to support their arguments within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, and the complaint resolver shall be responsible for reviewing such information and evidence when resolving the complaint;
c) The complaint resolver shall notify in writing the contents of the related party's opinions and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the complainant to provide feedback on the related party's opinions;
d/ If at the end of the prescribed time limit one party has no opinion, the complaint shall be settled on the basis of documents in the application, including documents expressing opinions of the other party.
4. During the first complaint settlement for complicated complaint cases, the first complaint settler may, on its own initiative or at the request of the complainant, conduct a re-appraisal as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property. During the re-appraisal, the first complaint settler may consult an independent expert or an Advisory Council in accordance with the following regulations:
a) The first complaint resolver may consult an independent consulting expert or a Consulting Council during the re-appraisal process depending on the complexity of the content to be re-appraised. A Consulting Council shall be composed of a president and members. An independent consulting expert or a Consulting Council shall advise the complaint resolver on technical issues, legal issues related to the content to be re-appraised and the proposed solution.
Independent consulting experts, the chairman and members of the Consulting Council are persons with appropriate professional qualifications selected from the List of industrial property consulting experts and other sources (in case no appropriate expert is available in such list).
List of industrial property consultancy experts compiled by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and coordinated with an administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints, and published in the Industrial Property Gazette.
b/ The Advisory Council shall be organized and operate according to the following principles: b1/ The Advisory Council is set up under the decision of the first complaint solver; b2/ The Advisory Council works in the form of meetings, collective discussions and voting by majority; b3/ Involved parties in the complaint case and parties with related rights and obligations may be invited to attend the meeting of the Advisory Council to clarify the case details.
c/ The following persons shall not participate in the Advisory Council and not act as independent consultants in the complaint case: c1/ The complained person (the person who issued the complained decision or notice); c2/ The person who appraised the application for registration of industrial property rights related to the complained decision or notice; c3/ The person whose rights and interests are directly related to the complaint case; c4/ The person who may not be objective in the complaint case, if there are grounds to determine such. The persons mentioned at this point shall have to explain, give explanations and information related to the work they have done which is the subject matter of the complaint.
d) Opinions of independent consulting experts, opinions of the chairman and members of the Consulting Council and working results of the Consulting Council must be presented in writing.
5. The first complaint resolver shall organize a dialogue session in accordance with Article 30 of the Law on Complaints. Independent counseling experts and members of the Counseling Council for re-appraisal during the resolution of the initial complaint (if any) may be invited to attend the dialogue session.
6. The person competent to resolve first-time complaints shall issue a decision on resolution of the complaint as follows:
a) Based on the results of re-examination of the decision or notice subject to complaint, the person who first resolved the complaint shall issue a decision resolving the complaint, which must contain the contents specified in Clause 2 Article 31 of the Law on Complaints;

b) In case the complainant is not the applicant or the requester for establishment of industrial property rights or registration of an industrial property right transfer contract subject to the decision or notice complained about and provides new details which may affect the conclusion on complaint settlement, the contents related to such new details shall be re-examined. Based on the examination results, the person competent to first settle the complaint shall issue a complaint settlement decision under Point a of this Clause.
7. Regulations on first-time complaint settlement in this Circular shall also apply to second-time complaint settlement, except for regulations related to consultation with experts in re-appraisal of application contents specified in Clause 4 of this Article. For complicated complaint cases, if necessary, the person settling second-time complaints shall establish an Advisory Council under the guidance specified in Clause 4 of this Article to consult opinions during the complaint settlement process.
8. In case the complaint settlement results lead to the amendment or cancellation of part or all of an administrative decision, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall resume the process of appraisal of the related application for completion of remaining procedures in accordance with the complaint settlement results.
Article 120. Publication of complaint settlement decisions
The complaint settlement decision shall be published on the web portal of the complaint settlement agency within 15 working days and in the Industrial Property Gazette within one month from the date of issuance of the decision.
Article 121. Effect of decisions and notices complained about and decisions on resolution of complaints
1. The decision or notice complained about shall remain effective during the complaint settlement period, except for cases where the enforcement is suspended under the written decision of the complaint solver in accordance with Article 35 of the Law on Complaints or at the request of the Court in accordance with the law on procedures.
2. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and organizations authorized or delegated by People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall immediately execute legally effective decisions on complaint resolution in accordance with Article 44 of the Law on Complaints, specifically as follows:
a) The decision on first-time complaint settlement issued by the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the head of an organization authorized or delegated by the People’s Committee of a province or central-affiliated city to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall come into force after 30 days from the date of its signing if the complainant does not file a second complaint; for remote and rural areas where travel is difficult, this period may be extended but must not exceed 45 days;
b/ The second complaint settlement decision of the Minister of Science and Technology or the President of the People's Committee of a province or city directly under the Central Government takes legal effect after 30 days from the date of its signing; for remote and deep-lying areas where travel is difficult, this time limit may be extended but must not exceed 45 days.
3. The acceptance of a second complaint shall be notified to the first complaint solver within 10 working days from the date of acceptance of the application. In this case, the decision on first complaint settlement shall not take legal effect. The decision and notice complained about shall continue to take effect until the decision on second complaint settlement takes legal effect.
4. The second complainant may immediately notify the first complainant of the filing of such complaint to ensure his/her rights and interests.
Article 122. Measures to limit arising complaints
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and organizations authorized or delegated by People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be responsible for reviewing issued decisions and notices; if detecting signs of violation, promptly rectify them to avoid complaints, except where such decisions and notices are subject to complaints.
2. During the process of resolving complaints, the complaint resolver shall encourage and facilitate the disputing parties to reach reconciliation in accordance with the law.
Chapter X
   INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY DECLARATION, NATIONAL REGISTRATION BOOK ON INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY
Article 123. Industrial property gazettes
1. Industrial property gazette means an official publication issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam for public disclosure of information on establishment, change, termination or invalidation of industrial property rights and other relevant information required to be disclosed in accordance with the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP and this Circular.

2. Industrial property gazettes shall be published in the form of printed gazettes or electronic gazettes.
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for:
a) Retain 1 copy of each issued Official Gazette for each year for printed Official Gazette. The retention period is permanent;
b) Manage the database of the Official Gazette online, including electronic versions of published issues of the Official Gazette and information and attributes of documents published in the Official Gazette.
4. Contents of the Industrial Property Official Gazette include information to be published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam when organizations and individuals carry out procedures related to industrial property rights prescribed in the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, and this Circular.
5. Geographical indications accepted for protection under international treaties shall be announced by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Gazette within 30 days from the effective date of relevant international treaties. Necessary information related to the above-mentioned geographical indications shall be announced according to Clause 2 Article 11 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, including: name of the geographical indication; name of the product bearing the geographical indication; summary of specific characteristics of the product bearing the geographical indication; protected area of the geographical indication and other information (if any).
Article 124. National register of industrial property and industrial property representatives
1. National Register of Industrial Property means an official and public database of the State which fully shows information on the legal status of established industrial property rights. The national register of industrial property comprises the following types:
a) National invention register;
b) National register of utility solutions;
c) National register of industrial designs;
d) National register of layout designs of semiconductor integrated circuits;
dd) National register of marks;
e) National register of geographical indications.
2. For industrial property objects registered under national procedures, the National Register specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall comprise sections corresponding to each protection title, and each section shall comprise:
a) Information on protection titles: number and issuance date of the protection title; name of protected subject matter, scope (quantity) of protection, validity period; name and address of the owner of the protection title/Geographical indication registrant, geographical indication management organization, name and nationality of the author of the invention, layout design or industrial design;
b) Information on the application for issuance of a protection title (application number, submission date, priority date of the application, name of the industrial property representation service organization (if any));
c) Any amendment related to information on amendments to protection titles, validity of protection titles (validity maintenance, extension, termination, or cancellation); transfer of ownership or use rights to industrial property objects; number of re-granting times, date of re-granting, issuance of copies, number of copies (for co-owners), date of issuance of copies, changes to industrial property representation service providers (if any), etc.
3. For marks and industrial designs accepted for protection under international registration procedures, in the Part of International Marks and Part of International Industrial Designs in the respective National Registers prescribed at Points c and dd, Clause 1 of this Article, the following sections shall be included:
a) Information on the protection status: decision number and issuance date or acceptance date; name of protected subject matter, scope of protection, validity period; name and address of the owner of the protection title, name and nationality of the author of the industrial design;
b) Information on the application for issuance of a protection title (application number, submission date, priority date of the application, name of the industrial property representation service organization (if any));
4. For a GIP protected under an international treaty, the International Section in the National Register of GIs prescribed at Point f, Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following sections:
a) Information on protection status: name of international treaty, effective date of international treaty or date of acceptance for protection of geographical indications under international treaties; protected geographical indications, scope of protection; name and address of organization managing geographical indications;
b) Information on documents related to the geographical indication subject to protection request (international treaties, international agreements on protection, databases related to the geographical indication subject to protection request (if any));
c) Any amendment related to the legal status of the protected geographical indication: validity, protection scope, transfer of management rights, etc.
5. The National Register of Industrial Property Representatives shall include sections corresponding to each industrial property representation service organization, specifically:
a) Information on industrial property representation service organizations (full names, transaction names, addresses, name recording and deletion, and amendments to information on industrial property representation service organizations);
b) Information on the list of industrial property representatives of the organization (full name, permanent address, and practicing certificate number of each member in the list);
c) Information on changes to the list of industrial property representatives (issuance, re-issuance, revocation of practice certificates, removal from the list, etc.).
6. National registries specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article shall be established and kept by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in paper or electronic form. Any person may search the electronic register (if any) or request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue a copy or extract from the register, provided that they pay fees for the issuance of copies or extracts from the register.
Article 125. Change of information in the National Register of Industrial Property
1. The owner of a protection title, organization or individual permitted by the State to exercise the right to register geographical indications, and the organization managing geographical indications may request the National Office of Intellectual Property to record changes in industrial property representation service organizations of the protection title holders, provided that fees for examination of requests for recording, registration and publication of decisions on recording of changes in information on industrial property representation service organizations are paid according to regulations.
2. A dossier requesting change of information on an industrial property representation service organization in the National Register of Industrial Property shall include the following documents:
a/ Declaration, made according to Form No. 3 in Annex II of this Circular, clearly stating the request for recording of changes. One declaration may request recording of changes in information on industrial property representation service organizations for many protection titles if the same information is changed, provided that the requester pays fees according to regulations for each protection title;
b) Authorization document.
3. An application for change of information on an industrial property representation service organization in the National Register shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 2 of this Article or contains invalid documents;
b) The applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge as prescribed.
4. Applications for recording changes to information on industrial property representation service providers in the National Register shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of a valid application for change recording, within 2 months from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to record the change of industrial property representation service provider in the National Register of Industrial Property and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision;
b) In case of invalid application for recording changes, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to record changes, clearly stating reasons and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide feedback;
c) If the prescribed time limit specified in Point b of this Clause expires but the requester fails to correct errors or fails to make satisfactory correction of errors, does not have any objection or provides inadequate objection, within 2 months from the expiration date of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to recognize changes in industrial property representation service providers.
d) In addition to the cases specified in point c of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to recognize changes in industrial property representation service providers in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired according to regulations at the time of application submission; d2) The protection title was cancelled at the time of application submission or during the processing of the application.
5. In case an applicant requests withdrawal of application dossiers for recording changes in information on industrial property representation service providers in the National Register, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of withdrawal of dossiers of amendment of protection titles as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 110 of this Circular.
Chapter XI
  EDUCATION IN INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY LAW AND INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY ASSESSMENT

Article 126. Industrial property law training programs
1. Duration and time of training: at least 400 hours of lectures on legal knowledge and skills in industrial property (excluding basic law lectures for learners who do not have a bachelor or master degree in Law) and shall not exceed 12 months.
2. Training programs and guidelines are provided in Appendix III of this Circular.
Article 127. Recognition of industrial property training institutions The recognition of industrial property training institutions shall be carried out as follows:
1. Training institutions wishing to be recognized as eligible to organize training courses on industrial property law in accordance with Clause 4 Article 62 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall submit request for recognition to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. An application for recognition as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a) Written request for recognition of a training institution in industrial property law, made using Form No. 1 in Appendix III of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the organization meets the criteria and conditions for recognition (conditions regarding facilities, equipment; information on officials in the payroll who have taught at the university level in intellectual property for 5 years or more; list of information on lecturers participating in training programs; approved standard documents for use, consistent with the contents specified in the structure of the training program on industrial property law).
3. Applications for recognition of industrial property training institutions shall be processed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 15 working days from the date of receipt in the following order:
a) In case of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on recognition of the training institution in industrial property law, record it in the List of eligible institutions providing training in industrial property law and publish it on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision;
b) In case of an unsatisfactory application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of deficiencies in the application and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to correct such deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies, within 15 working days from the end of the time limit specified in point b of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on refusal to recognize the training institution in industrial property law, clearly stating the reasons for refusal.
4. List of training institutions recognized under this Article shall be posted and updated on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Article 128. Deletion of names of industrial property training institutions The deletion of names of industrial property training institutions from the List of institutions eligible to provide industrial property training shall be effected as follows:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall remove the name of a training institution that no longer satisfies the conditions for providing industrial property training in the following cases:
a) The training facility is suspended, shut down or dissolved;
b) The training institution no longer meets the criteria and conditions specified in Clause 2 Article 62 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c/ The training establishment fails to organize the training according to the prescribed training program on industrial property law specified in Article 126 of this Circular.
2. For the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to remove the name from the list of training institutions for industrial property laws and publish information on the removal of the name of the training institution on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision.
Article 129. Industrial property assessment training programs
1. Duration and time of training: at least 400 hours of lectures on legal knowledge, professional skills and methods for industrial property assessment shall be provided and must not exceed 12 months.
2. Training programs and guidelines are provided in Appendix III of this Circular.
Article 130. Recognition of industrial property assessment training institutions The recognition of industrial property assessment training institutions shall be carried out as follows:
1. Training institutions wishing to be recognized as eligible to organize industrial property assessment training courses in accordance with Clause 2 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall submit request for recognition to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. An application for recognition as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a) Written request for recognition of industrial property assessment training institution, made using Form No. 2 under Appendix III hereof;
b) Documents proving that the organization meets the criteria and conditions for recognition (material facilities and equipment; information on officials in the payroll who have participated in industrial property assessment; list of lecturer information participating in training programs; approved standard documents for use, consistent with the contents specified in the structure of the Industrial Property Assessment Training Program).
3. Applications for recognition of industrial property assessment training institutions shall be processed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 15 working days from the date of receipt in the following order:
a) In case of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on recognition of industrial property assessment training institution, record it in the List of institutions eligible to provide industrial property assessment training and publish it on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision;
b) In case of an unsatisfactory application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of deficiencies in the application and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to correct such deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies, within 15 working days from the end of the time limit specified in point b of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse recognition of industrial property assessment training institution, clearly stating the reasons for refusal.
4. List of training institutions recognized under this Article shall be posted and updated on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Article 131. Deletion of names of industrial property assessment training institutions The deletion of names of industrial property assessment training institutions from the List of institutions eligible to provide industrial property assessment training shall be carried out as follows:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall remove the name of a training institution if it no longer satisfies the conditions for industrial property assessment training in the following cases:
a) The training facility is suspended, shut down or dissolved;
b) The training facility no longer meets the criteria and conditions specified in Clause 2 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) The training institution fails to organize the training according to the industrial property assessment training program as prescribed in Article 129 of this Circular.
2. For the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to remove the name from the list of industrial property assessment training institutions and publish information on the removal of the name of the training institution on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision.
Chapter XII
                    PROTECTION OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY INFORMATION
Article 132. Measures to secure information on industrial property rights The National Office of Industrial Property shall take measures to secure information on industrial property rights for the purpose of exploitation of industrial property information and exercise of industrial property rights.
1. Maintain information technology infrastructure serving activities of assurance of information on industrial property rights.
2. Develop and manage databases on industrial property and information systems for management of geographical indications in accordance with Article 9b and Article 9c of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
3. To disseminate information on industrial property rights, including providing information on industrial property rights according to issued titles, disseminating laws on scope of rights of protection title holders, training and providing necessary knowledge and skills for organizations and individuals in establishing, exploiting and managing industrial property rights and respecting industrial property rights.
Article 133. Development and management of databases on intellectual property right transactions and databases on protection of intellectual property rights
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for:

1. Develop and manage the operation, maintenance, and development of the database on intellectual property right transaction prices in accordance with Article 9d of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. Develop and manage databases on intellectual property rights protection in accordance with Article 9e of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Article 134. Access to and use of information in industrial property databases All organizations and individuals shall have the right to access and use information in industrial property databases in two forms:
1. To search for information on databases publicly available at information lookup facilities open to the public or published on the Internet by the National Office of Intellectual Property.
2. Use information lookup and analysis services or information and document provision services provided by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, provided that fees are paid as prescribed.
Article 135. Information lookup and analysis services and information and document provision services The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall provide information lookup and analysis services and information and document provision services for organizations and individuals that have needs as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 134 of this Circular.
Article 136. Assurance of industrial property information in localities
1. Depending on their conditions and capabilities, industrial property authorities in localities may set up and manage industrial property databases to ensure information on industrial property for research, application, development of production, business activities and protection of industrial property rights in localities.
2. Industrial property right authorities in provinces and centrally run cities shall have the responsibility and authority to conduct activities to ensure industrial property information as prescribed in this Circular.
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for periodically (at least once every 6 months) making statistics on information in the database on industrial property to serve the state management of industrial property by provinces and centrally-affiliated cities.
Article 137. Issuance of copies of documents and confirmation of first application for priority right
1. All relevant organizations and individuals have the right to request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue copies of documents issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or copies from the master registers made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or copies of documents prepared by such organizations or individuals and submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam during the process of establishing industrial property rights. The requester for a copy shall pay the cost of document reproduction.
2. The applicant may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to provide a certified copy of the industrial property registration application submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in order to claim priority rights (including applications rejected as invalid or withdrawn by the applicant), provided that the applicant pays the document reproduction cost.
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall provide copy and confirmation service for organizations and individuals as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.
Chapter XIII
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 138. Implementation clauses
1. This Circular comes into force as of April 01, 2026.
2. The Circular No. 23/2023/TT-BKHCN dated November 30, 2023 of the Minister of Science and Technology of Vietnam on elaboration of the Law on Intellectual Property and measures for implementation of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP dated August 23, 2023 of the Government of Vietnam on elaboration of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights, and state management of intellectual property related to procedures for establishment of industrial property rights and assurance of industrial property information shall cease to be effective from the date this Circular comes into force.
Article 139. Transition clauses
Applications related to industrial property rights and complaints filed before the effective date of this Circular shall be processed in accordance with regulations of law effective at the time of filing, except for the following cases:
1. Clause 4 Article 14, Clause 4 Article 41, Clause 4 Article 48, Clause 3 Article 71 and Clause 4 Article 96 of this Circular on notification of format appraisal results shall be applied to applications for registration of inventions, layout designs, industrial designs, marks or geographical indications which have not been accepted as valid applications.

2. Regulations in Article 76 and Article 78 of this Circular shall apply to applications for registration of audio marks which have not yet been notified of content appraisal results./.
