                                                    DECREE
    ELABORATION AND GUIDANCE ON IMPLEMENTATION OF CERTAIN ARTICLES OF THE CIVIL CODE AND THE LAW ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY REGARDING COPYRIGHTS AND RELATED RIGHTS
                                                   GOV
   Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
   Pursuant to the Civil Code of June 14, 2005;
   Pursuant to the Intellectual Property Law dated November 29, 2005;
   At the proposal of the Minister of Culture and Information,

DECREES:
   Chapter 1.
                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS
   Article 1. Scope of regulation
   This Decree provides detailed regulations and guidelines for a number of articles of the Civil Code and the Intellectual Property Law regarding copyright and related rights.
   Article 2: Subjects of application
   This Decree applies to Vietnamese organizations and individuals; foreign organizations and individuals engaged in activities related to copyright and related rights.
   Article 3: Protection of copyright and related rights
   1. Copyright protection means the protection of authors' rights to literary, artistic and scientific works as defined in Article 738 of the Civil Code and Articles 18, 19 and 20 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Related rights protection means the protection of rights of performers to their performances; rights of producers of audio and video recordings to such recordings; rights of broadcasting organizations to broadcasts and encrypted program-carrying satellite signals prescribed in Articles 745, 746, 747 and 748 of the Civil Code and Articles 29, 30 and 31 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   Article 4.- Interpretation of terms
   In this Decree, the terms below are construed as follows:
   1. “posthumous work” means a work first published after the author’s death.
   2. Anonymous work means a work in which the author’s name (real name or pen name) is not written on the work when it is published.
   3. Original works of art are those existing in a material form, on which the creation of such works is first fixed.
   4. “copy of a work” means a direct or indirect copy, part or whole of a work. A photocopy of a work is also a copy of the work.
   5. “fixation” means the expression in written language, other characters, lines, three-dimensional figures, layout, colors, sounds, images or the reproduction of sounds and images in a certain material form so that they can be recognized, copied or communicated.
   6. Audio and video recording means fixation of sounds and images of a performance or other sounds and images, or reproduction of sounds and images not in the form of fixation associated with cinematographic works or other audio-visual works.

   7. “Copy of audio or video recording” means a direct or indirect copy of part or the whole of an audio or video recording.
   8. “Public disclosure of a fixation or audio and visual fixation” means the making available to the public of copies of a fixation or audio and visual fixation with the consent of the related rights holder.
   9. Re-broadcasting means simultaneous broadcasting of a broadcast by one broadcasting organization from another broadcasting organization's program. Re-broadcasting is also re-broadcasting.
   10. Encrypted program-carrying satellite signals means signals carrying programs transmitted by satellites in such a form that their sound or visual characteristics or both have been altered for the purpose of preventing illegal reception of the programs.
   Article 5. State policies on copyright and related rights The State policies on copyright and related rights specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of Article 8 of the Intellectual Property Law include:
   1. To provide financial support for purchasing copyrights of State agencies and organizations tasked with popularizing works, performances, audio and video recordings, broadcasts of ideological, scientific and artistic value in service of public interests, contributing to socio-economic development.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Planning and Investment and concerned agencies in, guiding the formulation of financial plans (sources and funds) and mechanisms for the implementation of the copyright purchase.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall approve the list of works eligible for purchase of copyrights by central authorities and organizations; the provincial People’s Committees shall approve the list of works eligible for purchase of copyrights by local authorities and organizations.
   2. To prioritize investment in, and training and fostering of officials and public employees engaged in management and enforcement of copyright and related rights protection from central to local levels.
   To focus on training and fostering a contingent of officials performing the task of self-protecting rights in collective representation of copyright and related rights.
   3. To prioritize scientific research topics related to law, mechanisms and policies, and apply scientific-technical and technological measures to protect copyright and related rights.
   4. To enhance the education of copyright and related rights knowledge in schools at all levels. The Ministry of Education and Training shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information in, including the contents of copyright and related rights teaching in the university, college and professional secondary school education programs.
   Article 6: Contents and responsibilities for state management of copyright and related rights
  1. The Government shall perform the unified state management of copyright and related rights.
   2. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall be responsible before the Government for performing the State management over copyright and related rights, having the following tasks and powers:
   a/ Formulating and directing the implementation of strategies, laws, mechanisms and policies to protect copyright and related rights;
   b/ To promulgate and organize and guide the implementation of legal documents on copyright and related rights in accordance with law;
   c/ To apply measures to protect the lawful rights of the State, organizations and individuals in the domain of copyright and related rights protection;
   d/ To manage copyright to works (including computer programs and data collections) and related rights to performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasts under the State's ownership in accordance with law;
   dd. To stipulate the provision, cooperation, ordering and assurance of copyright to works and related rights to performances, phonograms, video recordings and broadcasts;

   e/ To build and manage the copyright and related rights apparatus; to train and foster officials in copyright and related rights;
   g. To manage activities of organizations representing copyright and related rights collectives;
   h/ Granting, re-granting, changing and canceling copyright registration certificates, related rights registration certificates and other procedures related to copyright registration certificates and related rights registration certificates;
   i. Compiling and managing the National Register of Copyright and Related Rights;
   k. Publishing and distributing Official Gazette on copyrights and related rights;
   1. To organize and direct activities of education, propagation and dissemination of knowledge, laws, mechanisms and policies as well as information and statistics on copyright and related rights;
   m/ To organize and manage activities of assessment of copyright and related rights;
   n. Inspect and handle violations of copyright and related rights; settle complaints and denunciations about copyright and related rights;
   o. To conduct international cooperation on copyright and related rights.
   3. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall be responsible before the Government for assuming the prime responsibility for, and coordinating with ministries, ministerial-level agencies, government-attached agencies and provincial-level People's Committees in performing the State management over copyright and related rights.
   The Copyright Office of Vietnam is an agency of the Ministry of Culture and Information assisting the Minister of Culture and Information in performing state management of copyright and related rights. The Minister of Culture and Information shall specify the functions, tasks and powers of the Copyright Office of Vietnam in performing state management of copyright and related rights.
   The Ministry of Culture and Information shall coordinate with the Ministry of Science and Technology in formulating general policies and laws on intellectual property rights protection, consolidating general information on intellectual property, undertaking joint international cooperation projects on intellectual property, and performing other general tasks under the direction of the Government.
   4. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Government-attached agencies and provincial-level People’s Committees shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   Article 7.- The State management competence of the provincial People’s Committees
   1. The provincial People's Committees have the following tasks and powers:
   a/ To guide the implementation of law provisions on copyright and related rights in localities;
   b/ Promulgating documents according to their competence to guide and direct the implementation of law provisions and policies on copyright and related rights in conformity with local conditions;
   c/ Organizing local activities to protect copyright and related rights; taking measures to protect the legitimate rights and interests of the State, organizations and individuals in relation to copyright and related rights;
   d. Inspect and handle complaints, denunciations and violations of regulations on copyright and related rights within their jurisdiction in localities;
   dd. To guide and receive applications for registration of copyright and related rights within their competence and in accordance with law;
   e/ To coordinate with the Ministry of Culture and Information and concerned ministries, branches and provincial-level People’s Committees in copyright and related right protection activities.
   2. The provincial/municipal Culture and Information Services shall assist the provincial-level People’s Committees in performing the function of State management over copyright and related rights.
   The President of the provincial People’s Committee shall specify the functions, tasks and powers of the Department of Culture and Information and the People's Committees of districts and communes in performing the state management of copyright and related rights.
   Chapter II

                                                 COPYRIGHT
   Article 8.- Author
   1. Author means a person who personally creates part or the whole of a literary, artistic or scientific work, including:
   a/ Vietnamese individuals have works protected by copyright;
   b/ Foreign individuals have works created and expressed in a certain material form in Vietnam;
   c/ Foreign individuals having works first published in Vietnam;
   d/ Foreign individuals having works protected in Vietnam under international treaties on copyright to which Vietnam is a contracting party.
   2. Organizations and individuals who support, provide opinions or materials for others to create works shall not be recognized as authors.
   Article 9.- Works expressed in other characters
   "Work presented in other forms specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means a work presented in symbols instead of letters such as Braille for the blind, shorthand symbols and similar symbols which can be copied by different forms by users.
   Article 10. Lectures, speeches and other presentations
   Lectures, speeches and other presentations specified at Point b, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law are works expressed in spoken language and fixed in a certain material form.
   Article 11.- Press works
   Press works specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law include categories such as reportage, fast news, news report, interview, comment, investigation, editorial, special report, press comment and other categories for publication or broadcast on print, radio, television, online media or other means.
   Article 12.- Musical works
   Musical works specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in musical notes in musical pieces or other musical symbols with or without lyrics, regardless of whether they are performed or not.
   Article 13. Theatrical works
   Theatrical works specified at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works of various art forms of performance, including drama (spoken drama, musical and dance drama, opera, silent drama), circus, dance, pantomime and other types of theatrical works.
   Article 14: Cinematographic works
   " cinematographic works and works created by a process similar to that specified at Point f, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works composed of a series of consecutive images creating a moving effect accompanied or not accompanied by sound, expressed on a certain material and which can be distributed and communicated to the public by technical devices and technologies, including the following types: feature films, documentaries, scientific films, animated films and other similar types.
   Article 15.- Plastic art works and applied fine arts works
   1. Plastic works specified at Point g, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in lines, colors, three-dimensional forms and layouts such as paintings, graphics, sculptures, installation art and similar forms, existing in the form of original copies. Particularly for graphic works, they may be expressed up to the 50th version, which are numbered and signed by authors.
   2. Applied fine art works specified at Point g, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works expressed in lines, colors, three-dimensional forms and layouts with useful features which can be attached to useful objects and are mass-produced by hand or by machines, such as emblems; handicrafts; forms expressed on products or product packages.
   Article 16: Photographic works
   "Photographic works specified at Point h, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means works showing images of the objective world on photosensitive materials or on media from which such images are created or can be created by any technical method (chemical, electronic or other methods);
   Still images taken from a cinematographic work or similar to cinematography are not considered photographic works but are part of such cinematographic work.
   Article 17.- Architectural works
   Architectural works specified at Point i, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law means design drawings in any form expressing creative ideas on houses, construction works and space planning (construction planning) which have been or have not yet been built. Architectural works include design drawings on ground plans, top plans, cross-sectional views and perspective views expressing creative ideas on houses, works, architectural work complexes, space organization, landscape architecture of a region, an urban area, an urban system, an urban functional zone or a rural residential area.
   The model and plan for a house, construction work or space planning shall be considered as an independent architectural work.
   Article 18.- Schematics, plans, maps and drawings
   Sketches, plans, maps and drawings specified at Point k, Clause 1, Article 14 of the Intellectual Property Law include sketches, plans, maps and drawings related to topography, scientific works and architectural works.
   Article 19.- Copyright in cinematographic works and theatrical works
   1. Cinematographic and theatrical works shall be created by a collective of authors. Persons who participate in the creation of cinematographic and theatrical works specified in Clause 1, Article 21 of the Intellectual Property Law shall enjoy the moral rights to their creations according to Clauses 1, 2 and 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law. Producers, directors and screenwriters may reach agreement on the exercise of the right to title cinematographic works specified in Clause 1, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law and the revision of scripts of cinematographic works specified in Clause 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Organizations and individuals that invest in financial and material and technical facilities to produce cinematographic works or stage productions specified in Clause 2 Article 21 of the Law on Intellectual Property are owners of rights specified in Clause 3 Article 19 and Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Organizations and individuals investing in financial and material and technical foundations to produce cinematographic works or stage productions may reach agreements on the exercise of the rights stipulated in Clause 3, Article 19 and Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law and obligations stipulated in Clause 3, Article 21 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   Article 20.- Use of folk literature and art works
   1. Folk literary and artistic works specified at Points a, b and c, Clause 1, Article 23 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be protected regardless of their fixation.
   2. Use of folk literature and art works specified in Clause 2, Article 23 of the Intellectual Property Law means the study and collection of and introduction to the authentic value of such works.
   3. Users of folklore and folk art works defined in Clause 2 of this Article shall reach agreement on payment to the keeper of such works and enjoy copyright to their research and introduction.
   4. Reference to the origin of a type of folk literary or artistic work specified in Clause 2 Article 23 of the Intellectual Property Law means the indication of the place name of the community where such folk literary or artistic work is formed.
   Article 21.- Subjects not covered by the copyright protection scope

   1. News of the day, which are purely for information provision, as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 15 of the Intellectual Property Law, shall be daily news briefs which are merely for information provision and not creative.
   2. Administrative documents specified in Clause 2 Article 15 of the Law on Intellectual Property include documents of regulatory agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, economic organizations, people’s armed forces units and other organizations as prescribed by law.
   Article 22.- Personal rights
   1. The right to name works prescribed in Clause 1, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law does not apply to works translated from one language into another.
   2. The right to publish or authorize others to publish a work prescribed in Clause 3 Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law means the distribution of such work to the public in sufficient copies to meet reasonable public needs, depending on the nature of the work, by the author or copyright owner or another individual or organization with the consent of the author or copyright owner.
   Publication of works does not include performance of theatrical, cinematographic or musical works; reading of literary works before the public; broadcasting of literary and artistic works; display of plastic works; construction of works from architectural works.
   3. The right to protect the integrity of a work and prevent others from modifying or altering it as stipulated in Clause 4, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law means the act of preventing others from modifying or altering a work, unless otherwise agreed by the author.
   4. Authors of computer programs and investors in computer program production may agree on naming and development of computer programs.
   Article 23.- Economic rights
   1. The right to perform works in public as prescribed at Point b, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be exercised exclusively by copyright holders or permitted by such holders to directly perform or through audio and video recordings or any technical means accessible to the public.
   In this clause, performance of a work before the public includes performance of the work anywhere except at home.
   2. The reproduction right prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 20 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the right of copyright holders to exclusively exercise or authorize others to exercise the right to make copies of works by any means or form, including regular or temporary storage of works in electronic form.
   3. The right to distribute originals or reproductions of works specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law means the right of copyright holders to exercise or authorize others to exercise in any technical form or mode accessible to the public for sale, lease or other transfer of the original or reproduction of works.
   For plastic art works and photographic works, distribution rights also include public display and exhibition.
   4. The right to communicate a work to the public by wire, wireless means, electronic information networks or any other technical means specified at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law means the exclusive right of the copyright owner or his licensee to communicate the work or a copy of the work to the public which is accessible at a place and time selected by the owner or licensee.
   5. The right to lease originals or reproductions of cinematographic works or computer programs specified at Point f, Clause 1, Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be exercised exclusively by copyright holders or permitted by such holders for a definite term.

   The right to lease computer programs shall not apply where such programs are not the main objects of lease as computer programs associated with the normal operation of means of transport as well as other technical machines and equipment.
   Article 24.- Reasonable quoting and importation of copies of works
   1. The reproduction of a work in a reasonable manner without misrepresenting the author’s views for comments or illustrations in one's own work as prescribed at Point b, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law must satisfy the following conditions:
   a/ The quoted part is only used for introducing, commenting or clarifying matters mentioned in his/her work;
   b/ The quantity and actual nature of excerpts from works used for quoting which do not cause harm to copyrights of quoted works; and are suitable to the nature and characteristics of types of quoted works.
   2. Importing copies of others’ works for personal use specified at Point k, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law shall apply only to cases of importing not more than one copy.
   3. The use of works in the cases specified in Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law shall not apply to the reproduction of architectural works, plastic works and computer programs.
   Article 25.- Reproducing works
   1. Self-copying one copy specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law applies to cases of scientific research and teaching by individuals not for commercial purposes.
   2. Reproduction of works for archival in libraries for research purposes specified at Point dd, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law means reproduction of not more than one copy.
   3. The library must not reproduce and distribute copies of the work to the public, including digital copies.

Article 26. Term of copyright protection
   1. The term of protection of property rights and personal rights specified in Clause 3, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law for posthumous works shall be fifty years from the date such works are first published.
   2. The term of protection of property rights and personal rights specified in Clause 3, Article 19 of the Intellectual Property Law for photographic works and works of applied fine art specified at Point a, Clause 2, Article 27 of the Intellectual Property Law shall be fifty years from the first publication of such works. Within fifty years, if the works have not yet been published, the term of protection shall be fifty years from the date such works are fixed.
   3. From the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law on July 1, 2006, the term of protection for photographic works and works of applied fine art shall be calculated according to the provisions in Clause 2 of this Article.
   Article 27.- Copyright holders
   Copyright owners specified in Article 36 of the Intellectual Property Law include:
   1. Vietnamese organizations and individuals;
   2. Foreign organizations and individuals having works created and expressed in a certain material form in Vietnam;
   3. Foreign organizations and individuals having works first published in Vietnam;
   4. Foreign organizations and individuals having works protected in Vietnam under international treaties on copyright to which Vietnam is a signatory.
   Article 28.- Owners of copyright to anonymous works
   1. Anonymous works specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Intellectual Property Law are under state ownership.
   2. In case of anonymous works under management of organizations or individuals, such organizations or individuals shall have the rights of owners.

   3. When the actual owner of a work is identified, ownership shall belong to such owner from the date on which his/her identity is identified.
   Article 29.- Use of works under the State ownership
  1. Organizations and individuals using works under state ownership specified in Points a and b Clause 1 Article 42 of the Law on Intellectual Property, except for cases specified in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 28 of this Decree, must fulfill the following obligations:
   a/ Permission for use;
   b/ To pay royalties, remunerations and other material benefits;
   c/ To submit a copy of the work within thirty days after its dissemination and circulation.
   2. Organizations and individuals shall fulfill obligations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article at the Copyright Office of Vietnam.
   3. The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for receiving transfers of copyright in whatever form from organizations and individuals specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 42 of the Law on Intellectual Property as prescribed by law.
   4. The Ministry of Culture and Information and the Ministry of Finance shall stipulate the financial management regime prescribed at Point b, Clause 1 of this Article.
   Article 30.- Use of works belonging to the public
   1. Organizations and individuals using works which belong to the public as prescribed in Article 43 of the Law on Intellectual Property must respect the moral rights provided for in Clauses 1, 2 and 4, Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Owners of copyrights to works in the public domain shall not be entitled to the right to publication specified in Clause 3 Article 19 and economic rights specified in Article 20 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. When detecting acts of infringing upon personal rights specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 4, Article 19 of the Law on Intellectual Property, which are committed against works whose term of protection has expired, state agencies, organizations and individuals shall have the right to request persons committing such acts to stop their acts, publicly apologize, correct their mistakes and pay compensations for damage caused; to lodge complaints or denunciations and request competent state agencies to handle them. Depending on the nature and seriousness of the infringement, organizations and individuals committing acts of infringement may be handled according to administrative, civil or criminal law provisions.
   Social – political – professional organizations, social – professional organizations and collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights have the right to request competent state agencies to protect personal rights to works of members whose term of protection has expired.
   Chapter III
                                               RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 31.- Rights of performers
   1. “Direct reproduction of a performance fixed on audio or video recording according to Point b Clause 3 Article 29 of the Law on Intellectual Property” means the creation of other copies from such audio or video recording.
   2. Indirect reproduction of a performance fixed on audio or video recording according to Point b Clause 3 Article 29 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the creation of other copies not from such audio or video recording, including reproduction from electronic information networks, broadcasts, relevant telecommunications postal network services and similar forms.
   3. “transmission to the public of an unfixed performance prescribed at Point c, Clause 3, Article 29 of the Intellectual Property Law” means dissemination of an unfixed performance to the public by any technical means other than broadcasting.
   Article 32.- Use of related rights without having to ask for permission or pay royalties or remunerations For cases of use of related rights not for commercial purposes, there is no need to ask for permission or pay royalties or remunerations, including:

   1. Making one copy for personal scientific research purposes prescribed at Point a, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Making one copy for teaching purpose, except for published performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts for teaching purposes specified in Point b Clause 1 Article 32 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   Article 33.- Reasonable quotation
   Reasonable quotation for the purpose of providing information specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law means the use of quotations for the sole purpose of reporting news, which must satisfy the following conditions:
   1. Quotations are provided only for the purpose of introducing, commenting or clarifying issues in the provision of information;
   2. The quantity and authenticity of excerpts from performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts which are not prejudicial to the rights of performers, producers of phonograms or video recordings or broadcasting organizations to their performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts used for excerpting shall be appropriate to the nature and characteristics of such performances, phonograms, video recordings or broadcasts used for excerpting.
   Article 34. Temporary copies
   The provisional copy of a broadcasting organization entitled to the broadcasting right stipulated in Point d, Clause 1, Article 32 of the Intellectual Property Law means a fixed copy made by such organization with its own means and equipment for the purpose of serving the immediate subsequent broadcast of such organization. In special cases, such copy may be stored at an official archive center.
   Article 35.- Use of phonograms and video recordings
   1. Direct use of published audio and video recordings for commercial purposes in order to make broadcasts which are sponsored or advertised or collect money in whatever form specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Intellectual Property Law means use of such audio and video recordings by broadcasting organizations for broadcasting via wireless or wired means, including also transmission via satellites or digital environment. Indirect use of published audio and video recordings for commercial purposes in order to make broadcasts which are sponsored or advertised or collect money in whatever form specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Intellectual Property Law means rebroadcasting or relaying broadcasted programs; uploading programs in digital environment onto airwaves.
   2. Use of published audio and video recordings in business and commercial activities specified at Point b, Clause 1, Article 33 of the Intellectual Property Law means the direct or indirect use by organizations and individuals of such published audio and video recordings for use in restaurants, hotels, shops, supermarkets; karaoke service establishments, postal and telecommunications service providers, digital environment; tourism, aviation, public transport and other business and commercial activities.
   3. The performance remuneration of a performer in case the phonogram or video recording is used as prescribed in Article 33 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall depend on the agreement between the performer and the producer of such phonogram or video recording when performing the phonogram or video recording program.
   The ratio of royalty, remuneration and other material benefits shall be agreed upon by copyright holders or copyright and related rights collective rights organizations.
   Collective representative organizations may authorize another collective representative organization to collect and distribute royalties, remunerations and other material benefits. Authorized collective representative organizations shall be entitled to a fee as agreed upon.
   Article 36.- Owners of broadcasting programs
   Owners of broadcasting programs specified in Clause 3, Article 44 of the Intellectual Property Law means broadcasting organizations that invest their financial and material-technical facilities in broadcasting.

   When using works, phonograms or video recordings to produce broadcasts, broadcasting organizations shall fulfill obligations toward copyright owners and related rights owners in accordance with law.
   Chapter IV
                     CERTIFICATE OF COPYRIGHT REGISTRATION AND RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 37. Copyright and related rights registration

   1. Authors, copyright owners and related right holders as defined in Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property may submit applications for registration of copyright or related rights either directly or by authorizing other organizations or individuals to do so at the Copyright Office of Vietnam or the provincial-level Culture and Information Services of localities where they reside or are headquartered.
   2. Foreign individuals and juridical persons whose works, performance programs, audio and video recordings or broadcasts are protected by copyright and related rights as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 13 and Article 17 of the Law on Intellectual Property may directly file or authorize copyright and related right consultancy organizations to file applications for registration of copyright and related rights at the Copyright Office of Vietnam or the Culture and Information Services of the localities where the authors, copyright owners or related right owners reside or are headquartered.
   Article 38. Copies of registered copyright works, copies of fixed recordings of performances, audio and video recordings, broadcasts of related rights registration
   1. The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall keep a copy of the work registered for copyright and a copy of the fixed medium of the subject matter registered for related rights specified in Point b Clause 2 Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property after the grant of the copyright registration certificate or the certificate of related rights registration.
   2. Copies of works registered for copyright as prescribed in Point b Clause 2 Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property may be replaced by three-dimensional photos of such works if they have particular characteristics such as paintings, statues, monuments, bas-reliefs or grand murals associated with architectural works; or are too large and cumbersome.
   Article 39.- Competence to grant copyright registration certificates and related right registration certificates
   1. The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall be competent to grant, re-grant, renew and cancel validity of copyright registration certificates and related right registration certificates specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 51 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   a/ Authors, copyright owners and related right holders who wish to apply for re-grant or change of certificates of copyright registration or related right registration shall file applications clearly stating the reasons therefor and the dossiers specified in Article 50 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
   b/ The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall re-grant a copyright registration certificate or a related rights registration certificate in case such certificate is lost or damaged or in case the owner of copyright or related rights changes;
   c/ The Copyright Office of Vietnam shall cancel a copyright registration certificate or a related right registration certificate in case it is determined that the holder of such certificate is neither the author nor the owner and the work, performance, phonogram, video recording or broadcast has been registered for protection not as prescribed by law.
   2. The Department of Culture and Information shall, after receiving the application for registration, re-grant or change of a copyright registration certificate or related rights registration certificate in accordance with law, transfer it to the Copyright Office of Literature and Art for consideration and settlement according to its competence.
   The Culture and Information Service shall forward the application to the applicant immediately after receiving the results of the Copyright Office of Vietnam's consideration and settlement.

   3. Organizations and individuals shall pay fees and charges when carrying out procedures related to copyright and related rights to the Copyright Office of Vietnam as prescribed by law.
   4. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall issue forms of registration applications, certificates of copyright registration, certificates of related rights registration.
   Article 40.- Effect of copyright registration certificates and related rights registration certificates The copyright registration certificates issued by the Vietnam Copyright Protection Company, the Vietnam Copyright Protection Agency, the Copyright Department and the Literature and Art Copyright Department before the effect of the Civil Code and the Intellectual Property Law shall continue to be valid.
   Chapter 5.
      SPORT REPRESENTATION ORGANIZATIONS, COPYRIGHT AND CONTENT HOLDING SERVICE PROVIDERS
   Article 41. Copyright and related rights collective representative organization

   1. Collective representative organizations of copyright and related rights as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 56 of the Law on Intellectual Property must satisfy the following conditions when operating:
   a) The representative organization of the collective of copyright and related rights must be authorized by the author or owner of copyright and related rights;
   b) The collective representative organization is authorized to represent the author or owner of copyright or related rights in the management of a specific right or group of rights;
   c/ The collection and distribution of royalties, remunerations and other material benefits arising from the exploitation of rights or groups of rights specified in the operation charter of the collective representative organization.
   2. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall guide the distribution of royalties, remunerations and other material benefits in cases where authors or copyright holders or related rights holders have not yet authorized their representatives.
   3. In case a work, phonogram or video recording or broadcast is related to rights and interests of many organizations acting as collective representatives authorized to represent different rights or groups of rights, the parties may agree to let one organization negotiate on their behalf for use license, collect and divide money and report to the Ministry of Culture.
   - Information before implementation.
   4. Collective representative organizations must send reports and information to the Copyright Office of Vietnam on a six-monthly, annual or irregular basis about their activities.
   Article 42. Copyright and related rights consultancy service providers
   1. A copyright and related rights consultancy or service organization may be established under Clause 1 Article 57 of the Law on Intellectual Property when its head and individuals providing copyright and related rights consultancy and services fully satisfy the following conditions:
   a/ Being a Vietnamese citizen with full civil act capacity;
   b/ Having their permanent residence in Vietnam;
   c/ Having a university degree in law.
   2. To submit biannual, annual or irregular reports on copyright and related rights consultancy and services to the Copyright Office of Vietnam.
   Chapter 6.
                                 PROTECTION OF COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS
   Article 43.- Right to self-defense

   1. Application of technological measures to prevent acts of infringement of copyright and related rights specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 198 of the Intellectual Property Law means the right holders' putting management information on the original or copies of works, phonograms, videograms or broadcasts; putting management information on the works which are communicated to the public in order to identify the work, its author, the owner of rights, information on the term and conditions for use of the work and all data or codes or symbols expressing such information in order to protect copyright and related rights. At the same time, the right holders may apply technological measures to protect the management information on the works, prevent acts of accessing the works and illegally exploiting their ownership rights in accordance with law.
   2. Rights holders may apply other measures specified in Clause 1 Article 198 of the Law on Intellectual Property to protect their rights and legitimate interests.
   Article 44. Right to initiate civil lawsuits on copyright and related rights
   1. The following rights holders have the right to initiate lawsuits at competent Courts to protect their lawful rights and interests related to copyrights and related rights:
   a/ Author;
   b/ Owners of copyright and related rights;
   c/ The lawful heir of the author or the owner of copyright or related rights;
   d/ Individuals and organizations that are assigned the rights of copyright holders or related rights holders;
   dd. Individuals and organizations that use works under contracts;
   e/ Performers;
   g/ Producers of audio and video recordings:
   h/ Broadcasting organization;
   i. Authorized collective representative of copyright and related rights;
   k/ Other rights holders as prescribed by law.
   2. State agencies and relevant organizations, within the scope of their tasks and powers, may initiate civil lawsuits to request Courts to protect public interests and State interests in the field of copyright and related rights.
   Article 45.- Complaints and denunciations related to the registration of copyright and related rights
  1. The following persons may file complaints or denunciations:
   a) Authors, copyright owners, related right owners, and authorized organizations and individuals have the right to file complaints about the grant, re-grant, replacement, or cancellation of copyright registration certificates or related right registration certificates.
   b/ Any third party may lodge a denunciation of the grant of a copyright registration certificate or a related right registration certificate.
   2. Procedures for lodging complaints and denunciations shall be as follows:
   a/ Persons filing complaints about copyright and related rights registration must send written petitions to the Copyright Office of Vietnam. Such a petition must clearly state the date of complaint; name and address of the complainant; name and address of the complained agency/organization/individual; content and reason for the complaint and the complainant's request. The petition must bear the signature of the complainant. Enclosed with the petition are documents such as the copyright registration certificate, related rights registration certificate or decision on invalidation, written refusal to grant, re-grant or replacement of the copyright registration certificate, related rights registration certificate and other relevant documents and evidence.
   b/ The denouncer shall file a written denunciation to the competent State agency. In such a written denunciation, the denouncer shall clearly state the date of denunciation; the name and address of the denouncer; the name and address of the agency, organization or individual being denounced; the contents and reasons for denunciation and the denouncer's requests. The written denunciation must be signed by the denouncer. Enclosed with the written denunciation are relevant documents and evidence.

   3. Within the time limit prescribed by the Law on Complaints and Denunciations, the Copyright Office of Vietnam, the Ministry of Culture and Information or competent state agencies shall reply in writing to complainants or denouncers.
   4. If disagreeing with the reply of the Copyright Office of Vietnam, the Ministry of Culture and Information or a competent authority, the complainant or denouncer may lodge their complaints or denunciations to the competent authorities as prescribed by law.
   Chapter VII
                                                              IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
   Article 46.- Transition period
  1. Copyright and related rights shall be protected under legal documents which take effect before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law if they are still in the term of protection on the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law, they shall continue to be protected under the Intellectual Property Law.
   2. Applications for registration of copyright and related rights filed with competent authorities before the effective date of the Intellectual Property Law shall continue to be processed in accordance with the legal documents effective at the time of filing.
   3. Any act of infringement of copyright or related rights or breach of a contract made before the effective date of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be handled in accordance with the applicable law at the time such act is committed.
   Article 47.- Implementation effect
   This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO. This Decree replaces the Government's Decree No. 76/CP of November 29, 1996 guiding the implementation of a number of provisions on copyright in the Civil Code.
   Article 48.- Implementation responsibility
   1. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies, chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees and agencies, organizations and individuals having related rights and obligations shall implement this Decree.
   2. The Minister of Culture and Information shall guide the implementation of this Decree and organize its implementation./.
