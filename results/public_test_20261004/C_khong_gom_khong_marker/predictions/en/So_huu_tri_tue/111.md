                                            CIRCULAR
 GUIDING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE NO. 103/2006/ND-CP OF SEPTEMBER 22, 2006, DETAILING AND GUIDING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE LAW ON INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY
Pursuant to the Intellectual Property Law dated November 29, 2005;
Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP of September 22, 2006, detailing and guiding a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 54/2003/ND-CP of May 19, 2003, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Science and Technology, and the Government’s Decree No. 28/2004/ND-CP of January 16, 2004, amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 54/2003/ND-CP;
The Ministry of Science and Technology guides the implementation of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP dated September 22, 2006 of the Government as follows:
Chapter I
                 PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Section 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS ON PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
1. Grounds for establishment of industrial property rights
1.1 Industrial property rights arising or established on the grounds specified in Clause 3, Article 6 of the Intellectual Property Law dated November 29, 2005 (hereinafter referred to as the "Intellectual Property Law"), Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 6 of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP dated September 22, 2006 of the Government detailing and guiding a number of articles of the Intellectual Property Law on industrial property (hereinafter referred to as the "Decree on industrial property") and according to specific provisions at this Point.
1.2 Industrial property rights to inventions, layout designs of semiconductor integrated circuits (hereinafter referred to as “layout designs”), industrial designs and marks shall be established on the basis of decisions of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam on grant of protection titles to registrants of such objects. Persons who are granted protection titles by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam are owners and enjoy rights to industrial property objects within the scope of protection stated in the protection titles and during the term of validity of the protection titles. When disputes arise, owners of industrial property objects may use protection titles as grounds for proving their rights without any other evidence.
1.3 Industrial property rights to geographical indications shall be established on the basis of decisions of the National Office of Intellectual Property on the grant of certificates of registration of geographical indications to geographical indication management organizations.

1.4 Industrial property rights to internationally registered marks under the Madrid Agreement and Protocol (hereinafter referred to as “internationally registered marks”) shall be established on the basis of decisions to accept protection or certificates of protection of internationally registered marks in Vietnam granted by the National Office of Intellectual Property at the request of mark owners. Such decisions and certificates shall have the same validity as protection titles granted to mark registrants in Vietnam.
1.5 Industrial property rights to well-known marks established on the basis of extensive practical use which make such marks well known shall not require registration procedures at the National Office of Intellectual Property. When exercising and settling disputes over rights to well-known marks, owners of such marks must prove their rights with evidence in accordance with Article 75 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
1.6 Industrial property rights to a trade name shall be established on the basis of lawful use of such trade name without having to carry out registration procedures at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. When exercising and settling disputes over rights to a trade name, the owner of such trade name must prove his/her rights with evidence showing the time, territory and field in which the trade name has been used by him/her.
1.7 Industrial property rights to trade secrets shall be established on the basis of financial or intellectual investment activities or results of other lawful activities to discover, create or obtain information forming a trade secret and to keep such information confidential without having to carry out registration procedures at the National Office of Intellectual Property. When exercising and settling disputes over rights to trade secrets, the subject of trade secrets must prove their rights with evidence showing the activities in which the information forming the trade secret was created, discovered, obtained and the measures to keep such information confidential.
1.8 The right to combat unfair competition is established on the basis of the practical reality of competition activities without having to carry out registration procedures at the National Office of Intellectual Property. When using the right to combat unfair competition, the holder must prove his/her rights with evidence showing the object, field, territory and time of business related to the competition activity.
2. Owners of industrial property applications
2.1 Applicant for industrial property registration (hereinafter referred to as applicant) means an organization or individual filing an application for registration of an invention, layout design, industrial design, mark or geographical indication. When a title of protection of an invention, layout design, industrial design, mark or geographical indication is granted, the applicant shall be recognized as the owner of such title. When a title of protection of a geographical indication is granted, the applicant shall be recognized as the registrant of such geographical indication.
2.2 The applicant must satisfy the conditions on the right to register industrial property specified in Articles 86, 87 and 88 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Articles 7, 8 and 9 of the Decree on Industrial Property. If such conditions are not satisfied, the industrial property registration shall be considered invalid.
3. Representatives of applicants
3.1 The applicant may, by himself/herself or through a lawful representative in Vietnam, carry out procedures for registration of industrial property at the Department
Intellectual property as prescribed in this Point and Point 4 of this Circular.
3.2 The following organizations and individuals may represent the applicant:
a) For organizations and individuals specified in Clause 1 Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property:
(i) In case the applicant is an individual: the legal representative or authorized representative of the applicant, or the industrial property representation service provider authorized by the applicant;
(ii) In case the applicant is an organization: the legal representative of the applicant or a person authorized by the legal representative of the applicant; an industrial property representation service organization (authorized by the applicant); the head of a representative office or a branch in Vietnam (if the applicant is a foreign organization).
b) For organizations and individuals specified in Clause 2 Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property: industrial property representation service providers (under the authorization of the applicant).
3.3 When carrying out procedures for industrial property registration, the National Office of Industrial Property shall only be permitted to conduct transactions with the applicant or his/her lawful representative. Organizations and individuals not falling into the cases specified in Clause 3.2 of this Circular that act as representatives of applicants shall be regarded as unlawful representatives.
4. Authorization of representation to carry out industrial property registration procedures 4.1 The authorization of representation and the performance of authorization of representation to carry out industrial property registration procedures (hereinafter referred to as "authorization") must comply with the provisions of law on authorization in Part Three of the Civil Code and the provisions of this Circular.
4.2 The authorization must be made in writing (power of attorney) and contain the following main contents:
a) Full name and address of the authorizing party and authorized party;
b) Full name and address of the authorized representative or the re-authorized representative (if any);
c) Scope of authorization and authorized workload;
d) Authorization period (an indefinite letter of authorization shall cease to be effective when the authorizing party declares termination of authorization);
e) Date of signing of the power of attorney;
g) Signature (specify full name, position and seal, if any) of lawful representative of the authorizing party (and of the authorized replacement party, the re-authorized party, if any).
4.3 The time when a power of attorney is legally valid in transactions with the National Office of Intellectual Property shall be determined as follows:
a) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a valid authorization letter;
b) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam accepts the valid authorization or re-authorization;

c) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a notice of change to authorization scope, premature termination of authorization, or change to address of the authorized party.
4.4 In case of authorization substitution or re-authorization, the power of attorney shall be considered valid only if the authorized party or the re-authorized party commits to take responsibility for all issues arising from the previous transaction with the National Office of Intellectual Property conducted by the authorizing party.

4.5 If the power of attorney has a scope of authorization including several independent procedures and the original copy of the power of attorney has been submitted to the National Office of Intellectual Property, when carrying out subsequent procedures, the authorized party must submit copies of the power of attorney with precise instructions on the number of the application containing the original copy of such power of attorney.
5. Responsibilities of the applicant and the representative of the applicant
5.1 The applicant and his/her representative shall ensure the truthfulness of information and documents provided to the National Office of Industrial Property in the process of industrial property registration according to the following regulations:
a/ All transaction documents must be certified by the applicant’s signature or that of his/her representative and affixed with the organization’s seal (if any). In case a notarization or certification by another competent agency is required, such certification shall be made according to regulations;
b/ All Vietnamese translations of foreign-language documents must be certified by the applicant or his/her representative that they are made verbatim from the original texts.
5.2 The applicant shall be responsible for all consequences and obligations arising from the representative's performance in transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5.3 The representative of the applicant shall be responsible before the applicant for all consequences caused by the untruthful declaration or provision of information in transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam; if damage is caused, compensation must be made.
5.4 The applicant and his representative shall be collectively referred to as "applicant" below, except for special regulations.
6. Handling of opinions of third parties before the issuance of protection titles 6.1 From the date an industrial property registration application is published in the Industrial Property Gazette up to the date of issuance of a decision on grant of a protection title, any organization or individual may express written opinions on the registration right, priority right, conditions for protection and other matters related to the industrial property registration application according to Article 112 of the Law on Intellectual Property. Written opinions of third parties shall be regarded as information sources for the processing of industrial property registration applications.
6.2 Within 1 month from the date of receipt of written opinions from a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such opinions to the applicant and impose a maximum time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response. After receiving the feedback from the applicant, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the third party and impose a maximum time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the third party to provide a written response. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the feedback from the applicant and the third party based on evidence, arguments provided by the parties and documents included in the application.
6.3 In case opinions of a third party are considered groundless, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall not notify such opinions to the applicant but shall notify the third party of the refusal to consider such opinions, clearly stating the reasons.
6.4 In case opinions of a third party are related to registration rights and it is deemed impossible to determine whether such opinions are well-grounded or not, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the third party to file a lawsuit for settlement. Within 1 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification, if the third party fails to notify the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam that it has filed a lawsuit for settlement, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider that the third party withdraws its opinions. If the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives such notification within the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the application pending the dispute settlement results of the Court. After receiving the dispute settlement results of the Court, the application processing shall be carried out in accordance with such results.
6.5 The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize direct dialogues between the third party and the applicant to further clarify issues subject to objection if necessary and at the request of both parties.
6.6 The time limit for the applicant to respond to the objection of a third party shall not be included in the time limit for the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to carry out relevant procedures as prescribed.
7. General requirements for applications for registration of industrial property
7.1 Minimum documents

The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall accept an application for industrial property registration (hereinafter referred to as "application") if it contains at least the documents specified in Points a, b and e Clause 1 Article 100, Clause 1 Article 108 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following specific regulations:
a) Regarding applications for registration of inventions, layout designs, industrial designs, marks and geographical indications, the following documents are required to be submitted in order to receive the applications:
(i) Declaration for registration;
(ii) Documents, samples and information showing industrial property objects to be registered; Specifically: for an invention registration application, it is the invention description; for an industrial design registration application, it is a set of photos, a set of drawings and an industrial design description; for a trademark registration application, it is the trademark sample and the list of goods or services bearing the trademark; for a geographical indication registration application, it is a description of particular properties and quality of products bearing the geographical indication and a map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication;
(iii) Receipts of fees and charges.
If any of the above documents is missing, the National Office of Intellectual Property may refuse to receive the application.
b/ For applications for registration of collective marks or certification marks, apart from the documents prescribed at Point 7.1.a above, the application must also contain the following documents:
(i) Regulations on use of collective marks/ certification marks;
(ii) Presentation of characteristics and specific quality of products bearing marks (if the marks are registered as collective marks used for products with specific characteristics or as marks certifying quality of products or as marks certifying geographical origin);

(iii) Map determining territory (if the mark is registered as a mark certifying geographical origin of products).
7.2 Requirements for applications
a) The application must satisfy the general requirements specified in Articles 100 and 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the specific requirements applicable to each type of industrial property subject matter prescribed in Articles 102, 103, 104, 105 and 106 of the Law on Intellectual Property, which are guided in detail in Points 23, 28, 33, 37 and 43 of this Circular.
b/ In order to ensure technical requirements in the processing process, the application must also satisfy the following formal requirements:
(i) Each application may request the grant of only one title of protection and the type of title of protection requested shall be appropriate to the subject matter of industrial property stated in the application;
(ii) All documents of the application must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents which may be made in other languages as prescribed in Points 7.3 and 7.4 of this Circular;
(iii) All documents of the application shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one sheet of paper of A4 size (210 mm x 297 mm), with margins of 20 mm on each side, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
(iv) For documents to be made according to set forms, it is required to use such forms and fill in all information as requested in appropriate places;
(v) Each document, if comprising multiple pages, must have each page bearing the ordinal number in Arabic numerals;
(vi) Documents must be typed or printed with an ink type which is not easily erased, clearly and cleanly, and cannot be erased or modified; if it is found that there are minor spelling errors in documents already submitted to the National Office of Intellectual Property, the applicant may correct such errors but must append his/her signature (and seal, if any) at the correction place;
(vii) Terms used in the application must be common terms (not local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with Vietnam’s standards;
(viii) The application may be enclosed with supporting documents being objects carrying electronic data of part or all of the contents of the application.
c) The application must fully satisfy the requirements regarding the number of documents, samples, drawings, photos and specific requirements applicable to each type of application as prescribed in this Circular.

d) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure that all mandatory and consistent information is provided; the Vietnamese translation of the application documents must be consistent with the original copies; the power of attorney must cover the tasks within the scope of authorization.
e) The subjects stated in the application must be accurately grouped and classified as prescribed.
g/ For documents requiring certification by competent agencies (the first receiving agency, notary public, People's Committee, etc.), there must be their seals for certification. 7.3 The following documents may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese:
a) Power of attorney;
b) Documents certifying registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person (certificate of inheritance; certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application, including transfer of an already filed application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.);
c/ Documents evidencing the grounds for enjoying priority right (certification of the receiving agency for copies of the first application(s); list of goods or services included in the first mark registration application; certificate of assignment of priority right if such right is acquired from another person).
7.4 The following documents may be made in other languages besides Vietnamese, but if so requested by the National Office of Intellectual Property, they must be translated into Vietnamese:
a) The first copy of the application to prove the grounds for priority right;
b/ Other documents to support the application.
8. Fees and charges for industrial property registration
8.1 The applicant shall pay fees and charges according to regulations of the Ministry of Finance.
8.2 Fees and charges
a) Upon receiving the application or requesting to carry out any other procedures, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to pay fees and charges as prescribed and examine the fee and charge receipts enclosed with the application documents.
b) If the fee or charge has not been fully paid as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of collection and notify it to the applicant.
In case the applicant fully pays the fee and charge, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue 2 receipts specifying the paid fee and charge amounts and levels, one of which is enclosed with the application as a fee payment record.
8.3 Refund of fees and charges
a) The paid fees and charges shall be partially or fully refunded at the request of the applicant in the following cases:
(i) The paid fees and charges exceed the prescribed limits;
(ii) The fees and charges have been paid but the corresponding work has not been performed because no such situation arises.
b) In case of accepting the request for refund of fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refund of fees and charges, specifying the amount, method of refund, and send it to the applicant.
c) In case of refusal to accept the request for refund of fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant and clearly state the reasons.
9. Time limit
9.1 The time limits prescribed in the Intellectual Property Law, the Decree on Industrial Property and this Circular shall be calculated according to the provisions of Chapter VIII, Part One of the Civil Code.
9.2 The time limit for the applicant and the related party to submit, amend or supplement documents or opinions may be extended once for the exact duration already set, provided that the applicant for extension submits a written request before the deadline and pays the fee as prescribed.
9.3 The applicant may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to carry out the procedures ahead of schedule when there is a written request and payment as prescribed. In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam does not accept such request, it must notify the applicant and clearly state the reason.
10. Forms of documents and titles of protection

10.1 Forms of applications are provided in the annexes to this Circular. Applicants shall use the above forms to make documents of their applications when carrying out procedures for industrial property registration.
10.2 Forms of protection titles are prescribed in the Appendices to this Circular. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall keep the issued forms of protection titles for examination of the legality of used protection titles. The change of forms of protection titles shall be made only on the basis of decisions of the Minister of Science and Technology.
11. General procedures
All types of industrial property registration applications shall be processed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the following order: receipt of the application; formal examination of the application; publication of the valid application; substantive examination of the application (except for layout design registration applications, which are not subject to substantive examination); grant or refusal to grant a protection title; registration and publication of the decision on grant of a protection title.
12. Submit and receive applications
12.1 An application may be filed at the National Office of Industrial Property or other places designated by the National Office of Industrial Property. Applications may also be sent by post to the places designated by the National Office of Industrial Property.

12.2 Upon receipt of an application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall check the documents enclosed with the application and compare them with the list of documents stated in the application form in order to conclude whether the application is received or not.
a) In case the application contains all the minimum documents specified in Point 7.1 of this Circular, the applicant shall receive the application, affix a seal to certify the submission date and number of the application on the declarations;
b) In case an application is missing one of the minimum documents specified in Point 7.1 of this Circular, the officer receiving the application shall refuse to accept it or send a notice of refusal of acceptance of the application from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to the applicant (if the application is submitted by post). For rejected applications, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return documents of the application to the applicant but must refund the fees and charges already paid according to the procedures for refunding fees and charges specified in Point 8 of this Circular;
c/ In case of receipt of an application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall hand over (or send) one copy of the declaration to the applicant, which is stamped with the date of filing the application, the serial number of the application and the result of examination of the list of documents enclosed with the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official. The above-mentioned handed-over (sent) copy of the declaration may be used as a receipt for the application.
13. Formal examination of applications
13.1 Purposes and contents of the formal examination of applications
Formal examination of an application means an examination of compliance with regulations on form of the application in order to conclude whether the application is valid or not.
A valid application shall be considered further. An invalid application shall be rejected (not considered further).
An application shall be considered valid if it satisfies the provisions of Point 7 of this Circular and does not fall into one of the following cases:
a) The application is made in a language other than Vietnamese, except for the cases specified in Points 7.3 and 7.4 of this Circular;
b) The declaration does not contain sufficient information on the author (for an invention registration application, industrial design registration application or layout design registration application), on the applicant, on the representative, or does not contain the signature and/or seal of the applicant or the representative; the mark registration application lacks a list of goods and services; the geographical indication registration application fails to list products bearing the geographical indication;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights;
d) The application is filed in contravention of Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
e) The application has the deficiencies specified in Point 13.3 of this Circular which affect its validity and although it has been requested by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to be corrected, the applicant fails to correct them or the correction is unsatisfactory;
g) There are grounds to immediately affirm that the subject matters stated in the application are clearly not protected by the State according to the provisions of Articles 59, 64, 69, 73 and 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property. For an application with multiple subject matters, if it falls into the cases specified at Points 13.2.a and 13.3.b and c of this Circular and the deficiencies only concern one or several subject matters in the application, the application shall be considered partially invalid (corresponding to the deficient subject matters), while for the remaining subject matters, the application shall still be considered valid.
13.3 Handling of deficiencies in the application during the stage of formal examination

If the application has the following defects, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify such to the applicant and within one month after the date of notification, the applicant must correct such defects.
a) The application fails to meet the requirements regarding the format prescribed in Point 7.2 of this Circular (insufficient copies of one of the mandatory documents; inconsistent application; application does not satisfy the requirements regarding the presentation format; the trademark registration application does not clearly state the registered trademark, lacks a description of the trademark, the classification results are inaccurate, lacks a translation of the priority right document, if necessary; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or unverified as prescribed, etc.);
b) The fee for filing and publication of the application has not been fully paid;
c) Having no power of attorney or having an invalid power of attorney (if the application is submitted by a representative).
The submission date shall be determined as follows:
a) The filing date is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, as stated in the receipt seal on the declaration according to Point 12.2.a of this Circular;
b) For an international application with designation or/and selection of Vietnam, the submission date shall be the same as the submission date of the international application.

13.5 Priority date
a/ If an application does not request priority right or although it requests priority right, it is not approved by the National Office of Industrial Property, such application shall be regarded as having no priority date.
b/ If the application requests priority right, the priority date(s) are the dates stated in such application and approved by the National Office of Intellectual Property.
c) The priority date determined according to the request for priority right on the basis of the first application(s) submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c and dd, Clause 1, Article 10 of the Decree on Industrial Property.
13.6 Notification of the results of the formal examination and notification of the acceptance of the valid application
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Point 13.2 of this Circular or contains errors as prescribed in Point 13.3 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice to the applicant stating the intended refusal to accept the valid application. The notice must clearly state the name and address of the applicant; the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the application is submitted through such organization); the name of the subject matter stated in the application; the submission date and application number; reasons and errors that may lead to the rejection of the application, and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinion or correct errors.
b) In case of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of acceptance to the applicant, specifying the name and address of the applicant, the name of the authorized representative (if any), and information about the subject matter stated in the application, the submission date, and the priority date (in case the request for priority rights is not accepted, the reasons must be clearly stated).
13.7 Refusal to accept applications
In case the applicant has been notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam of the intention to reject the application as invalid according to Point 13.6.a of this Circular but fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction or does not have any objection or provides unjustifiable objection within the prescribed time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of rejection and refund fees and charges already paid for the post-formal examination work at the request of the applicant.
13.8 Time limit for formal examination of applications
a) The time limit for formal examination is 01 month from the date of submission of the application.
b) If during the process of formal examination of an application, the applicant takes initiative or at the request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to amend or supplement documents, the time limit for formal examination shall be extended by the duration for amendment or supplementation of documents.
c/ Before the date of expiry of the time limit prescribed at Point 13.8.a above, the National Office of Industrial Property shall complete the examination of the form of the application and send a notice of results to the applicant according to the provisions of Point 13.6 of this Circular.
14. Public disclosure of valid applications
14.1 All applications accepted as valid shall be published by the National Office of Industrial Property in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property. The applicant shall pay an application publication fee.
14.2 Time limit for publication of applications

a) Disclosure of the invention registration application:
(i) An invention registration application shall be published in the 19th month from the priority date or the filing date, if the application does not have a priority date, or within 2 months from the date of acceptance of the valid application, whichever is later;
(ii) An invention registration application under an Agreement on Cooperation in Inventions (hereinafter referred to as “PCT application”) shall be published within 2 months from the date of acceptance of the valid application after it has entered the national stage;

(iii) An invention registration application with early disclosure request shall be published within 2 months from the date the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives such request or from the date of acceptance of the valid application, whichever is later.
b) Announcement of other applications: layout design registration applications, industrial design registration applications, mark registration applications and geographical indication registration applications shall be announced within 2 months from the date on which the valid application is accepted.
14.3 Contents of the application disclosure
Information relating to a valid application, including also information relating to a split-off application, which is published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property, shall include information relating to the validity of the application in terms of form stated in the notice of acceptance of the valid application, information relating to the valid application (application transfer, application split-off, original number of the split-off application, etc.); an invention summary enclosed with drawings (if any); a set of photos or a drawing of the industrial design; samples of marks and a list of goods and services enclosed therewith; a summary of the particular characteristics of products bearing GIs and names of products bearing GIs.
14.4 Access to information about valid applications published
Everyone may access detailed information on the nature of subject matters stated in applications published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property or request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to provide such information and shall pay fees for information provision as prescribed.
15. Content appraisal of applications
15.1 Purposes and scope of application
a) The purpose of substantive examination is to assess the subject matter stated in the application according to the conditions for protection and determine the scope (volume) of protection corresponding to such subject matter.
b) Procedures for content appraisal shall not apply to layout design registration applications.
15.2 Use of information lookup results
a) During the process of content appraisal of an application for registration of a patent/industrial design with priority right, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may use information lookup results and appraisal results of the corresponding application filed overseas.
b) The applicant may (proactively or upon request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam) provide the following documents to serve the content appraisal of the application:
(i) Regarding an invention registration application or industrial design registration application: results of information lookup or appraisal of the overseas submitted application for the subject matter stated in the application; copies of protection titles issued based on the overseas submitted application forms; documents related to the technical status of the subject matter stated in the invention registration application provided by the overseas competent authority and other documents;
(ii) For a mark or geographical indication registration application: documents proving that the mark or geographical indication has been protected abroad, including documents describing the use of such mark or geographical indication and other documents.
15.3 Correction of errors, explanation of application contents and provision of information
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to explain the contents of the application and provide information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application; if it is necessary to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter for the purpose of appraising the contents of the application.
b/ All amendments or supplements to the application documents which contain errors shall be made by the applicant himself/herself. The National Office of Industrial Property may make such amendments or supplements only if so requested in writing by the applicant. The applicant shall pay an amendment or supplement fee according to regulations. Documents requesting amendments or supplements to the application shall be attached to the relevant application documents and shall be regarded as the official documents of the application.
15.4 Premature termination of content appraisal
a/ In the following cases, the content appraisal of an application shall terminate ahead of time:

(i) The application does not clearly indicate the nature of the subject matter: documents related to the nature of the subject matter, such as the description, the list of goods or services, etc., lack information to the extent that it is impossible to determine the content of the nature of the subject matter, or the information about the nature of the subject matter of the invention registration application is unclear or too brief or too general to the extent that it is impossible to determine the subject matter of the protection request;
(ii) The subject matter is not suitable for the type of protection title requested or the subject matter is not protected by the State as prescribed;
(iii) There are grounds to confirm that the subject does not satisfy one or more of certain protection conditions, therefore it is unnecessary to assess other conditions and still conclude that the subject fails to satisfy the protection conditions;
(iv) The applicant fails to comply with requests for correction of errors, explanation of application contents or provision of necessary information as requested by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as prescribed in Point 15.3 of this Circular;
(v) The applicant requests termination of the content appraisal or declares withdrawal or abandonment of the application.
b/ Except for cases specified at Point 15.4.a (v) above, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant in writing of the early termination of the content appraisal, clearly stating the reason and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to give its opinion.
15.5 Re-opening of the content appraisal of the application
a) In case the applicant submits a written objection to the notice of termination of content appraisal within the time limit specified in Point 15.4.b of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider the objection from the applicant.
b) If the objection is justifiable, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall resume the content appraisal and the time reserved for the applicant with an objection shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal. If the objection is unjustifiable, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall officially terminate the content appraisal and issue a notice of refusal to grant the protection title. The applicant may file a complaint about this notice according to the procedures specified in Point 22 of this Circular.
15.6 Appraisal contents
a) Content appraisal of the application includes the following contents:
(i) Assess the conformity of the subject matter stated in the application with the type of protection title requested to be granted; (ii) Assess the subject matter according to each protection condition;
(iii) Inspect the first-to-file principle.
b/ The assessment under protection conditions shall be carried out one by one for each subject (if an application covers many subjects, the consistency must still be ensured). For each subject, the assessment shall be carried out one by one under each protection condition;
(i) For an invention registration application, the assessment shall be carried out sequentially for each point within the scope of protection;
(ii) For an application for registration of an industrial design, the assessment shall be carried out separately for each product (if the application covers a set of products); in case the application covers several schemes, the assessment shall be carried out separately for each scheme, starting with the basic scheme (the first scheme stated in the application);
(iii) For a mark registration application, the assessment shall be carried out separately for each component of the mark for each good or service in the list of goods and services.
c/ The content appraisal of each subject matter stated at Points 15.6.b(i), (ii) and (iii) above shall be completed after the subject matter has been assessed with all conditions for protection and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter fails or meets the conditions for protection, specifically:
(i) There are grounds to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy one or more than one of the protection conditions; or (ii) There is no ground to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy at least one protection condition.
d) Before issuing the notice specified in Point 15.7.a of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall review the results of substantive examination on the basis of examining related applications with earlier priority dates received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam after the date of commencement of substantive examination. 15.7. Tasks ending substantive examination
a) Notify the applicant of the results of the content appraisal

No later than the deadline for content appraisal prescribed in Clause 15.8 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send one of the following notices to the applicant:
(i) If the subject matter stated in the application does not satisfy the protection conditions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to grant a protection title, clearly stating the reasons for refusal, and may provide guidance on amendments to the scope (volume) of protection and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide their opinion and meet the requirements. The applicant may request an extension of such time limit under Point 9.2 of this Circular;
(ii) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to grant a protection title, clearly stating the deficiencies in the application and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide explanations or correct the deficiencies. The applicant may request an extension of such time limit under Point 9.2 of this Circular;
(iii) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions or the applicant has corrected deficiencies and obtained satisfactory results or provided a legitimate explanation within the time limit prescribed at Points 15.7.a(i) and (ii) above, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of intended grant of a title of protection and fix a time-limit of one month from the date of issuance of such notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of a title of protection, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of a title of protection, the registration fee and the fee for maintenance of validity for the first year of an invention. The applicant may request an extension of the above-mentioned time-limit according to Point 9.2 of this Circular.
b/ If the applicant fails to correct errors or unsatisfactory corrections within the time limit prescribed at Points 15.7.a(i) and (ii) above, or makes no objection or unjustifiable objection, then within 15 days after the expiry of such time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to grant a protection title. The applicant may lodge a complaint about such notice according to Point 22 of this Circular.
c/ If the applicant fails to pay the fee for the grant of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of a protection title or the registration fee within the time limit prescribed at Point 15.7.a (iii) above, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to grant a protection title within 15 days after the expiry of the corresponding time limit. For an invention registration application, if the applicant has paid the fee for the grant of an invention patent, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of an invention patent or the registration fee but fails to pay the fee for maintenance of the first-year validity of the invention patent or utility solution patent, such patent shall still be granted but shall cease to have effect right after its grant.
15.8 Time limit
a) The time limit for content appraisal of an application shall be determined as follows:
(i) 12 months for an invention registration application from the date of receipt of a request for substantive examination (if such request is filed after the date of disclosure of the application) or from the date of disclosure of the application (if such request is filed before the date of disclosure of the application);
(ii) 06 months from the date of publication of the application for industrial design registration, mark registration or geographical indication registration.
b) If during the process of content appraisal, the applicant takes initiative or at the request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to amend the application, supplement documents or provide explanations, the time limit for content appraisal shall be extended accordingly with the time limit for the applicant to perform such tasks.
16. Re-examination of applications
16.1 Re-examination of applications after receiving objections to the issued titles, after having notified the intended issuance or refusal to issue protection titles
a) The re-examination of an application under Clause 4 Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be carried out in the following cases:
(i) Having a written opinion from the applicant filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam during the period from the date of issuance of the notice of intended grant/intended refusal to issue the title of protection until before the date of issuance of the decision on grant/notification of official refusal to issue the relevant title of protection; or having a written opinion from a third party with legitimate reasons for the reason that there is no reasonable condition or opportunity to express their opinions; (ii) The opinion mentioned in Point 16.1.a(i) above is based on sound grounds, accompanied by evidence or indications leading to reliable information sources;
(iii) The reasons and evidence proving the opinions stated in Point 16.1.a(i) above are different from the reasons and evidence (if any) presented in the previous stage, or although such reasons and evidence are not different but have not yet been answered by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam under Point 6.2 of this Circular.

b) The time limit for re-appraisal of an application shall be equal to two-thirds of the time limit for initial appraisal prescribed in Point 15.8 of this Circular; for complicated cases involving many facts requiring verification or expert opinions, this time limit may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial appraisal.
c) The contents and procedures for re-appraisal shall comply with the corresponding provisions at Points 15.6 and 15.7 of this Circular.
d) The re-appraisal of an application shall be carried out only once.
16.2 Re-examination of applications due to requests for narrowing of the scope of protection of protection title holders In case a protection title holder requests narrowing of the scope of protection of industrial property rights as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall re-examine the corresponding application according to the contents and procedures specified in Points 15.6 and 15.7 of this Circular, provided that the applicant pays fees and charges as prescribed.
17. Amendments to applications
17.1 Amendments to applications
a) Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of refusal to accept the valid application or a notice of refusal to grant a protection title or a decision on grant of a protection title, the applicant may, at their discretion or upon request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, amend and supplement documents included in the application.
b) In case of amendments to the following documents, applicants shall submit amended documents enclosed with detailed explanations of amendments made to the original documents submitted:
(i) Description and summary of the invention for an invention registration application;
(ii) Drawings, photos and descriptions of industrial design registration applications;
(iii) Mark sample, list of goods and services bearing the mark for a mark registration application;

(iv) Description of particular characteristics, map of geographical area corresponding to a GI for a GI registration application.
c/ The amendment or supplementation of an application must not expand the scope (volume) of protection beyond the contents already disclosed in the description for applications for registration of inventions or industrial designs, or in the list of goods or services for applications for registration of marks, and must not change the nature of the subject matter stated in the application. If the amendment expands the scope (volume) of protection or changes the nature of the subject matter, the applicant shall have to file a new application and carry out all procedures again from the beginning.
d) The applicant may request correction of errors in the name and address of the applicant or author.
e) All requests for amendments shall be made in writing according to Form No. 01-SDD specified in Appendix B of this Circular. Requests may be made for amendments to the same content related to many applications, provided that applicants pay fees corresponding to the number of applications.
g) In case the applicant voluntarily amends or supplements the application after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam has issued a notice of acceptance of the valid application, such amendment or supplementation shall comply with Points 13.2, 13.3 and 13.6 of this Circular. The contents of the amended or supplemented application shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette according to Point 14 of this Circular and the applicant shall pay an application publication fee as prescribed.
h) The amendment or supplementation of an application, including changes in the authorized person, shall be made by the applicant at their discretion or upon request by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and must be documented in writing, clearly stating the amended or supplemented contents, enclosed with the receipt for payment of fees for amendment or supplementation of the application. Documents used for amendment or supplementation of applications must comply with corresponding provisions specified in Points 7, 10 and 13 of this Circular.
17.2 Splitting of applications
a) The applicant may, on their own initiative or at the request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, separate an application (separate one or more technical solutions in an invention registration application, one or more industrial designs in an industrial design registration application, one or more components of a mark or part of a goods or service list in a trademark registration application into one or more new applications, called split applications).
b) The separate application shall bear a new serial number and shall be submitted on the same day as the initial application or on the priority date of the initial application (if any); it shall be published in accordance with Point 14 of this Circular after the notice of acceptance of the valid application is issued.
c/ For each separate application, the applicant shall pay an application fee and all charges and fees for procedures carried out independently from the initial application but is not required to pay a charge for requesting priority right. The separate application shall be appraised in terms of form and further processed according to the unfinished procedures for the initial application. The separate application must be re-announced and the applicant shall pay an announcement fee if the application is separated after the National Office of Industrial Property has issued a notice of acceptance of the valid initial application.
d) The initial application (after being split) shall continue to be processed according to common procedures and the applicant must pay an amendment fee.
17.3 Application conversion
a) Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of refusal to grant a protection title or a decision on grant of a protection title, an applicant may change the request for an invention patent into a request for a utility solution patent or vice versa in accordance with Point dd Clause 1 Article 115 of the Law on Intellectual Property, provided that the applicant pays the application conversion fee as prescribed.
b) After receiving a request for conversion of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue to carry out the conversion procedures in accordance with relevant regulations but not repeat the same procedures as those applied to the application before the conversion request is received.
c/ Requests for conversion of applications submitted beyond the time limit prescribed at Point 17.3.a above shall not be considered. Applicants may submit new applications but shall take the application submission date (priority date, if any) of their initial applications.
17.4 Application transfer
Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues any of the notices specified in Point 17.1.a of this Circular, applicants may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to record the transfer of their applications to other persons. The transfer recording request shall be made using Form No. 02-CGD prescribed in Appendix B of this Circular and corresponding regulations prescribed in Point 17.1 of this Circular. The transfer recording request must include documents proving that the transferee satisfies the registration requirements.
18. Refusal to grant, grant and re-grant of copies of protection titles
An application for a title of protection shall be rejected in one of the cases specified in clauses 1 and 2 of article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In these cases, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall carry out procedures for rejecting the grant of a title of protection according to the provisions of clauses 3 and 4 of article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
18.2 Granting protection titles
a/ Within 10 days after fully and punctually paying fees and charges, the National Office of Industrial Property shall carry out procedures for granting protection titles according to Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b/ After being granted a protection title, if the protection title holder finds any error in it, he/she may request the National Office of Industrial Property to correct such error under Point 20.2 of this Circular.
c/ From the date the National Office of Intellectual Property issues a decision to grant a protection title, the transfer of applications shall not be considered.
18.3 Issuing copies of protection titles and re-issuing protection titles
a/ In case industrial property rights are under joint ownership, protection titles shall be granted only to the first among the list of applicants. Other co-owners may request the National Office of Industrial Property to grant copies thereof, provided that they pay a fee for the copy grant.
b) In the following cases, industrial property owners who have been granted protection titles or protection title copies may request the National Office of Industrial Property to re-grant them a protection title or a protection title copy, provided that they pay the corresponding fee:
(i) The protection title/duplicate of the protection title is lost;
(ii) The protection title or a copy of the protection title is damaged, torn, dirty or faded beyond the point of being unusable.
c/ Request for issuance of duplicates/reissuance of protection titles
Except for requests for issuance of copies stated in declarations of registration of industrial property objects, requests for issuance of copies/reissuance of protection titles must be made in writing and include the following documents:
(i) The application form for issuance of a copy/reissue of a protection title shall be made using Form No. 03-PBVB specified in Appendix C of this Circular;
(ii) 01 sample of mark; 01 set of photos of industrial designs identical with the sample of mark, and a set of photos of industrial designs in the original protection title;

(iii) Power of attorney (if the application is submitted by a representative);
(iv) Documents on payment of fees for issuance of protection titles or fees for issuance of copies of protection titles.
d) Handle requests for issuance of copies, reissuance of protection titles
(i) Within one month after receiving an application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider granting a duplicate or re-granting a protection title. In case an application for duplication or re-granting of a protection title meets the above conditions, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to grant a duplicate of a protection title/re-granting a protection title and record it in the register section of the corresponding protection title in the National Register of Industrial Property;
(ii) The contents of the copy of the protection title shall fully display the information of the corresponding protection title. The contents of the re-issuance of the protection title/copy of the protection title shall fully display the information of the first issuance of the protection title/copy of the protection title and must be accompanied by the instructions "Re-issuance", "Copy" or "Re-issuance of Copy";
(iii) In case of a request for re-grant/re-grant of a copy of a protection title which fails to satisfy the provisions of Point 18.3.c above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to grant/re-grant of a copy of a protection title, clearly stating the reason therefor.
19. National Register of Industrial Property, publication of decisions on grant of protection titles
a/ National Register of Industrial Property is the official and public database of the State, fully expressing information on the legal status of established industrial property rights. The national register of industrial property comprises the following types:
(i) National invention register;
(ii) National register of utility solutions;
(iii) National register of industrial designs;
(iv) National register of layout designs of semiconductor integrated circuits;
(v) National register of marks;
(vi) National register of geographical indications;
(vii) National register of transfer of industrial property rights;
(viii) National register of industrial property representatives.
b) The National Register of Certificates mentioned in Points 19.1.a(i), (ii), (iii), (iv), (v) and (vi) above includes sections corresponding to each protection title, each section including:
(i) Information on protection title: number and date of issuance; name of protected subject matter, scope/volume of protection, validity period; name and address of owner of protection title/Geographical indication registrant, name and address of author of invention, layout design or industrial design; (ii) Information on application for protection title (number, submission date, priority date of application, name of industrial property representation service organization - if any);
(iii) All information on amendments to protection titles, validity of protection titles (validity maintenance, extension, termination or cancellation); transfer of ownership rights or use rights to industrial property objects; serial numbers, issue dates and names of persons issued with copies or re-issued with protection titles.
c/ The national registration book prescribed at Point 19.1.a (vii) above comprises sections corresponding to each registered industrial property right transfer contract, specifically:
(i) Information on the certificate of registration of industrial property right transfer contract (serial number and issue date);
(ii) Information about the registered contract (contract name, signing date, place of signing, names and addresses of the delivering party and receiving party, transfer object, transfer scope);
(iii) Information about changes related to the contract (amendments, additions, extensions, cancellations or termination of the contract);
(iv) Information on transfer and termination of transfer of invention use rights under compulsory decisions.
d) The National Register of Industrial Property Representatives specified at Point 19.1.a (viii) above includes the relevant sections for each industrial property representation service organization, specifically:

(i) Information on industrial property representation service organizations (full names, transaction names, addresses, name recording and deletion, and amendments to information on industrial property representation service organizations); (ii) Information on lists of industrial property representatives of organizations (full names, permanent addresses, and practice certificate numbers of each member in the list);
(iii) Information on changes to the list of industrial property representatives (issuance, re-issuance, revocation of practice certificates, etc.).
e) National registration books made and kept by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in paper or electronic form or other means. Any person may search the electronic registration book or request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue a copy or extract from the registration book, provided that the fee for issuance of copies is paid.
a/ All decisions on grant of protection titles and decisions on acceptance of international registration shall be published by the National Office of Industrial Property in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 2 months after their issuance after the applicants have paid the publication fee according to regulations.
b) Information disclosed under Point 19.2.a above includes information specified in the corresponding decision: summary of the invention; set of photos or drawings of the industrial design; sample of the mark and list of goods and services bearing the mark; geographical indication and products bearing the geographical indication.
20. To amend or change the title holders, narrow the scope of protection, maintain and extend the validity of the titles of protection
20.1 Recording changes in names and addresses of protection title holders, narrowing the scope of protection and amending descriptions of properties/quality/reputation of products bearing GIs or maps of geographical areas corresponding to GIs, regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks
a/ To request amendment or replacement of the title holder of the protection title
The owner of a protection title may request the National Office of Industrial Property to record changes in the protection title in the following cases:
(i) Changes to the name and address of the owner of the protection title;
(ii) Changing the owner of the protection title (transferring ownership due to inheritance, acquisition, merger, separation, joint venture, association, establishment of a new legal entity under the same ownership, business format conversion or decision of a competent regulatory authority).
The applicant for recording of changes in name and address or changes in protection title holder shall pay a fee for modification of the protection title and a fee for publication of the decision on recording of modification of the protection title.
b) Request for narrowing of protection scope and amendments to descriptions of properties/quality/reputation of products bearing GIs, maps of geographical areas corresponding to GIs, regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks
Requests for narrowing of the scope of protection as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property may include one of the following contents:
(i) Request amendments to certain details which do not significantly alter the mark sample stated in the mark registration certificate;
(ii) Request to reduce one or several goods or services or group of goods or services on the list of goods and services written in the certificate of mark registration without changing the mark pattern;
(iii) Request for reduction of one or several independent or dependent points within the scope of protection stated in the patent or utility solution patent;
(iv) Request the removal of one or more industrial design solutions, one or more products in a set of products stated in an industrial design patent; request the removal of one or more fundamental design features of an industrial design.
c/ Written request for amendment of the protection title
Depending on the contents of amendment and supplementation prescribed in Point 20.1.a and Point 20.1.b above and Point b, Clause 1, Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, a written request shall include the following documents: (i) Declaration requesting the amendment of a protection title, clearly stating the request for recording changes to the name or address of the owner of the protection title, changes to the owner of the protection title, requests for narrowing the scope of protection or amendments to the description of properties/quality/reputation of products bearing a geographical indication, map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication, regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks, made according to Form No. 01-SDB prescribed in Annex C of this Circular;
(ii) Original copies of protection titles;

(iii) Documents certifying the change of name and address (original or notarized copy of the decision on change of name and address; business registration permit recording the change of name and address; other legal documents proving the change of name and address) - if the content requested for modification is name and address; (iv) Documents proving the transfer of ownership as prescribed in point 20.1.a (ii) above, if the request for modification is the owner of the title of protection (documents proving inheritance, acquisition, merger, separation, joint venture, association, establishment of a new legal entity by the same owner, business form conversion or under the decision of the competent state agency);
(v) Detailed presentation documents on amendments;
(vi) 05 samples of modified marks (if requested to modify details of marks); 05 sets of photos or drawings of industrial designs (if requested to modify industrial designs); 02 descriptions of properties/quality/reputation of products bearing GIs, maps of geographical areas corresponding to modified GIs (if requested to modify GIs); 02 regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of modified certification marks (if requested to modify collective marks or certification marks);
(vii) Power of attorney (if submitting application through representative);
(viii) Documents on payment of fees for amendments to protection titles, fees for content appraisal requesting narrowing of the scope of protection, and fees for publication of decisions on amendments or registration according to regulations.
A request for amendment of a title of protection may relate to more than one title of protection if it contains the same amended contents, provided that the requester pays fees and charges for each title of protection.
d) Processing requests for amendments to protection titles
Within one month after receiving an application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider a request for amendment of a protection title. If the request is judged valid, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to amend the protection title and register such amendment in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property. In the reverse case, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send the applicant a notice of intended refusal to accept the amendment, clearly stating the reason therefor and fixing a time-limit of one month from the date of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or to express opposition. Past this time-limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or his/her corrections are unsatisfactory, or if he/she makes no opposition or his/her opposition is groundless, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice refusing the request for amendment of the protection title.
20.2 Correction of errors in protection titles
a) In case of detecting any error in a protection title, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall, on its own initiative or upon request of the person detecting the error, revoke the protection title containing such error and grant a new one.
b/ The protection title holder shall pay a fee for the amendment of the title according to Clause 1, Article 97 of the Intellectual Property Law if such defects are caused by his/her fault. If such defects are caused by the National Office of Intellectual Property, the protection title holder is not required to pay a fee for the correction.
20.3 Maintenance of validity of invention protection titles
20.4 Extension of validity of a patent protection title:

For the purpose of maintaining the validity of a patent protection title, the owner of such title shall pay an effective maintenance fee within six (6) months before the expiry date of the term of validity. The payment of the effective maintenance fee may be made after the above-said time limit but not more than six (6) months from the expiry date of the previous term of validity and the owner of the protection title shall pay an additional fee equal to ten (10) per cent of the fee for each month of late payment.
a) An invention patent, utility solution patent or certificate of registered layout design shall not be extended. An industrial design patent may be extended up to 2 times for 5 years each time. A certificate of registered mark may be extended multiple times for 10 years each time.
b) In order to have the validity of an industrial design patent or a certificate of registered mark extended, within 6 months before the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark expires, the owner of such industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark shall file an application for extension with the National Office of Intellectual Property.
An application for extension may be filed beyond the above-said time limit but not more than 6 months after the validity of the title of protection expires and the holder of the title of protection shall pay an extension fee plus 10% of the extension fee for each month of late payment.
c) Application for extension
An application for extension of validity of a protection title shall comprise the following documents:

(i) An application for extension of validity of a protection title, made according to Form No. 02-GHVB in Appendix C of this Circular;
(ii) Original copy of the protection title (in case of application for extension of protection title); (iii) Power of attorney (if application is filed by a representative);
(iv) Documents on payment of fee for extension, announcement of decision on extension and registration of decision on extension of protection title as prescribed.
d) Process the application for extension
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider the application for extension within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application. If the application is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on extension, record it in the protection title, register it and publish it in the Industrial Property Gazette.
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to extend the validity period, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide their opinions, if the application for extension falls under one of the following cases:
(i) The application for extension is invalid or submitted in contravention of regulations;
(ii) The applicant for extension is not the owner of the corresponding protection title.
If within the prescribed time limit, the requester fails to correct errors or fails to sufficiently correct errors, does not make any objection or makes unjustifiable objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal of extension.
21. Termination or invalidation of protection titles
21.1 Request for termination or invalidation of protection titles
Requests for termination or invalidation of protection titles shall be handled in accordance with Articles 95 and 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the provisions of this Point.
21.2 Written request for termination or invalidation of a protection title
a/ In one document, one or more protection titles may be requested to be terminated or invalidated if they are based on the same reason, provided that the requester pays a fee according to regulations for each protection title.
b) Written request for termination or invalidation of a protection title, comprising the following documents: (i) Declaration requesting termination or invalidation of a protection title, made using Form No. 04-CDHB under Appendix C hereof;
(ii) Evidence (if any);
(iii) Power of attorney (if submitting a written request through a representative);
(iv) Explanation of reasons for the request (specifying the number of the title, reasons, legal grounds, contents proposed to terminate, partially or entirely invalidate the validity of the protection title) and relevant documents as prescribed in Points 7.2, 22.2 and 22.3 of this Circular;
(v) Payment of fees and charges as prescribed.
21.3 Handling requests for termination or invalidation of protection titles
a) In case of request for termination or invalidation of a protection title by a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify in writing the opinion of the third party to the owner of the protection title within 2 months from the date of notification. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may organize direct exchange of opinions between the third party and the owner of the protection title.
b) Based on the parties' opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to terminate/partially or completely invalidate the validity of the protection title or issue a notice of refusal to terminate/partially or completely invalidate the validity of the protection title in accordance with Clause 4 Article 95 and Clause 4 Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
c) If disagreeing with the decision to terminate or invalidate a protection title issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the applicant or related party may file a complaint about such decision or notice according to the procedures specified in Point 22 of this Circular.
d) Decision on termination or invalidation of a protection title published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property and recorded in the National Register of Industrial Property within 2 months from the date of signing of the decision.
21.4 Termination or invalidation of international registration of marks

a) For a request for termination or invalidation of third parties in international mark registrations under the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the contents of such request to the mark owner via the International Office, setting a time limit of 3 months from the notification date for the mark owner's opinion.
b/ The decision to terminate or invalidate the international registration of a mark shall be sent to the international office for carrying out relevant procedures in accordance with the corresponding provisions of the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol and published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property.
22. Complaints and settlement of complaints related to procedures for establishment of industrial property rights

22.1 Persons with right to complain, objects and statute of limitations for filing complaints Persons with right to complain specified in Clause 1, Article 14, within the statute of limitations specified in Clause 4, Article 14 of the Decree on Industrial Property shall have the right to file complaints about official notices and decisions of the National Office of Intellectual Property related to procedures for establishment of industrial property rights.

22.2 Complaint applications
a/ Each complaint application mentions one complained decision or notice. One complaint application may also mention many decisions or notices if they have the same content and reason for complaint, provided that the complainant pays a complaint fee according to regulations for each complained decision or notice.
b/ The complaint must include the following documents:
(i) The written complaint made using Form No. 05-KN under Appendix C hereof; (ii) Written explanation of the complaint and evidence proving the grounds of the complaint (specify reasons, legal grounds, content of the complaint, list of accompanying evidence, if any);
(iii) Copies of decisions or notices subject to complaints of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
(iv) Copy of the decision on first complaint resolution (for second complaints); (v) Authorization letter (if submitting the application through a representative);
(vi) Documents on payment of fees and charges related to complaints as prescribed.
c/ Evidence means documents (evidences) or exhibits (material evidences) used to prove and clarify the grounds of the complaint. Evidence must meet the following requirements:
(i) Evidence may be documents in foreign languages, provided that their Vietnamese translations are enclosed in cases where the persons competent to resolve complaints so request;
(ii) In cases where the evidence is documents bearing the seals of individuals or organizations not having lawful seals, or bearing the seals of foreign individuals or organizations, the signatures must be certified by notary offices or competent authorities;
(iii) In cases where the evidence is objects carrying information ( publications, video tapes, etc.), it shall, on a case-by-case basis, specify the origin, time of issuance or publication of such documents, or specify the origin and time of publication of the information shown on such objects;
(iv) The exhibits must be accompanied by documents clearly describing characteristics directly related to the content of the complaint.
The complainant must ensure the truthfulness in providing evidence and take responsibility for the consequences of providing untruthful evidence.
22.4 Withdrawal of complaints
a/ At any time, the complainant may send a written notice of withdrawal of his/her complaint. If the withdrawal is made by an industrial property representation service organization, the authorization for withdrawal must be clearly stated in the power of attorney.
b/ The withdrawn application shall be considered as not submitted. The complainant shall not be refunded his/her complaint application and the paid complaint fee and charge.
22.5 Processing of complaints
a) Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the complaint, the person competent to resolve complaints must examine the application according to the requirements regarding form and notify in writing to the complainant whether or not the complaint is accepted, which shall record the date of acceptance or clearly state the reason for non-acceptance.
b) The complaint is not accepted if it falls into one of the following cases:
(i) The complainant has no right to lodge a complaint;

(ii) The complaint is filed beyond the prescribed statute of limitations;
(iii) The complaint form does not meet the requirements specified in Points 22.1 and 22.2 of this Circular.
a) For complaints already accepted, the person competent to resolve complaints shall notify in writing the content of the complaint to the person with direct related rights and interests (“related party”) and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of notification for such person to provide opinions.
b/ The concerned party may provide information and evidence to support its arguments within the time limit prescribed at Point 22.6.a above; the person competent to settle the complaint shall have to consider such information and evidence when settling the complaint.
c/ If the above-said time limit expires but the concerned party has no opinion, the complaint shall be settled on the basis of the complainant's opinions.
22.7 Decision on complaint settlement
a) Based on arguments and evidence of the complainant and relevant parties, the person competent to resolve complaints shall issue a decision to resolve the complaint within the time limit for resolving complaints as prescribed by law on complaints.
b) Before issuing a decision to resolve the complaint, the person competent to resolve the complaint shall notify the complainant and the related party of the arguments and evidence used by the other party to resolve the complaint as well as the conclusion on resolving the complaint.
c/ The complaint settlement decision must contain the contents prescribed by law on complaints. 22.8 The complaint settlement decision shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette within 2 months after its signing.
22.9 Effect of the complaint settlement decision
Any procedures for establishment of industrial property rights which depend on the outcome of the complaint settlement shall be carried out only on the basis that:
a/ The first complaint settlement decision, if after the expiration of the statute of limitations for filing a second complaint the complainant does not file a second complaint and after the expiration of the statute of limitations for initiating an administrative lawsuit the complainant does not initiate an administrative lawsuit; or

b/ The second complaint settlement decision and the complainant's failure to initiate an administrative lawsuit within the prescribed time limit or under the effective decision of the court, if the complainant initiates an administrative lawsuit.
Section 2. REGISTRATION OF INVENTIONS
23. Requirements on invention registration applications
23.1 An invention registration application (application) must satisfy the general requirements prescribed in Points 7 and 10.1 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this Point.
23.2 The application must specify that the subject matter to be protected is a product or process in accordance with Clause 12 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
23.3 The application shall ensure consistency with Clause 1 and Clause 2 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following guidelines.
An application shall be considered as ensuring uniformity if:
a/ Protection of a single subject; or b/ Protection of a group of subjects with technical connection, expressing a single common creative intention, falling into the following cases:
(i) One object used to create (manufacture, design, or prepare) another object;
(ii) One object used for the performance of the other;
(iii) One object is used for use of the other;
(iv) Objects of the same type and with the same function to ensure the same result. 23.4 In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of information in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to submit documents verifying such information within 01 month from the date of issuance of the notification, especially documents confirming the lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (certificate of inheritance rights, certificate or agreement on transfer of application submission rights; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.); documents showing drug test results on humans, animals or plants mentioned in the description (when the subject matters of protection requests are pharmaceuticals for humans, animals or plants).
23.5 Requirements for declarations
The applicant shall submit 02 declarations, made according to Form 01-SC in Appendix A of this Circular. In the section "International classification of inventions" in the declaration, the applicant shall specify the classification code of the technical solution to be protected according to the latest International Classification of Inventions (according to the Strasbourg Agreement) published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Gazette. If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify and the applicant shall pay a classification fee as prescribed.
23.6 Requirements on invention descriptions
The applicant shall submit 2 invention descriptions. An invention description must include an invention description and a protection scope.
a) The description section of an invention description must fully disclose the nature of the technical solution registered. The description must contain sufficient information to enable any person with average knowledge in the relevant technical field to implement such a solution; it must clarify the novelty, inventive step and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requires the issuance of an invention patent); and clarify the novelty and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requires the issuance of a utility solution patent).
Persons with average knowledge in a corresponding technical field means persons who have practical skills in common techniques and know general common knowledge in the corresponding technical field.
b) The description must include the following contents:
(i) Name of invention: briefly indicates subject matter or subjects registered (hereinafter referred to as "subject matters"); name of invention must be concise and not promotional or advertising nature;

(ii) Field of use of invention: field in which subject matters are used or related;

(iii) Technical status of field of use of invention: technical status in the above-mentioned field at the time of filing application (similar known subject matters, if any);
(iv) Technical nature of the invention: nature of subject matter, in which must clearly state signs (characteristics) creating subject matter and must indicate new signs (characteristics) compared to similar known technical solutions;
(v) Brief description of accompanying drawings (if any);
(vi) Detailed description of the invention implementation schemes;
(vii) Examples of implementation of inventions;
(viii) Benefits (efficiency) that may be achieved.
c) Scope of protection of an invention (hereinafter referred to as "scope of protection" or "protection request") The scope (request) of protection is used to determine the scope of industrial property rights to an invention. The scope (request) of protection must be presented in brief and clearly, in conformity with the description and drawings, which must clarify the new signs of the subject matter requested for protection (hereinafter referred to as "subject matter") and must comply with the following provisions:
d) The scope of protection must be adequately described by the illustration, including necessary and sufficient basic technical signs to identify the subject matter, achieve the set objectives and distinguish the subject matter from known ones.
e) Technical signs within the scope of protection must be clear, accurate and accepted in the corresponding technical field.
g) Scope of protection (requirements) must not be cited to description and drawings, except for cases where it is cited to parts that cannot be accurately described in words, such as the order of nucleotides and the order of amino acids, interference diagrams, state diagrams...
h/ If the application contains drawings illustrating the protection request, the signs mentioned in the scope of (requested) protection may be accompanied by indication numbers which must be enclosed in parentheses. Such indication numbers shall not be regarded as limiting the scope of (requested) protection.
i) The scope of protection (requested) shall be divided into two parts: "limited part" and "differential part". The "limited part" includes the name of the subject matter and signs of the subject matter which are identical to those of the nearest known subject matters, and is connected with the "differential part" by the phrase "differs in" or "is characterized by" or equivalent words; the "differential part" includes distinctive signs of the subject matter compared to the nearest known subject matters, and these signs combined with the signs of the "limited part" constitute the subject matter of the protection request.
k) The scope of protection may include one or more points. Where the scope of protection covers multiple points, it may be used to represent a single protected subject matter, with the first point (referred to as the independent point) and subsequent points (referred to as dependent points) used to specify the independent point; or to represent a group of protected subject matters, with several independent points, each representing a protected subject matter within the group, each of which may have dependent points.
l) Points of the protected scope (requirements) must be numbered in Arabic numerals followed by a period.
m) Scope of protection (requirements) for multiple points used to represent a group of subjects must satisfy the requirements: independent points, representing distinct subjects, not being cited to other points of the scope of protection (requirements), except where such citation allows avoiding the complete repetition of the content of other points; dependent points must be presented immediately after the independent points from which they depend.
23.7 Requirements on an invention summary
The applicant shall submit 2 inventions. The invention summary shall be used to briefly describe (not exceeding 150 words) the nature of the invention. The summary shall disclose principal contents of the technical solution for information purpose. The summary may contain drawings and specific formulas.
23.8 Additional provisions for invention registration applications related to biological technology
a) In addition to the general requirements for invention descriptions prescribed in Clause 23.6 of this Circular, for an application for registration of a genetic sequence or part of a genetic sequence, the description must contain a list of genetic sequences presented according to the WIPO ST.25 standard section 2 (ii) (Standard for presenting lists of nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences in invention registration applications).
b) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request applicants to submit electronic information-carrying objects (e.g., floppy disks, optical discs) readable by common electronic devices, which contain nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences identical to those in the sequence list specified in the description.
c) Regarding inventions relating to biological materials which cannot be described or adequately described for persons with average knowledge of biotechnology to implement, such inventions shall only be considered fully disclosed if the following conditions are met: (i) Biological material samples have been deposited at a competent depository in accordance with Point 23.9 of this Circular no later than the filing date;
(ii) The description must clearly state necessary information about characteristics of biological materials that the applicant may obtain;
(iii) The declaration must specify the biological material storage agency, the storage number of the biological material sample already stored by the storage agency, and documents confirming these information submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date or no later than the submission date of the earliest application (if any), whichever is earlier, except for the case specified in Point 23.9.d of this Circular.
d) In case the applicant is not the biological material depositor, the name and address of the depositor and documents certifying the lawful use of biological materials must be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date or no later than the submission date of the earliest application (if any), whichever comes first, except for the case specified in Point 23.9.d of this Circular.
23.9 Biological material specimen storage
a) The purpose of deposit of biological material samples is to serve the process of content appraisal of applications for registration of inventions related to biological materials.
b) Biological material samples must be submitted to the biological material storage agency no later than the submission date of the invention registration application related to such biological materials.
c) The competent authority in charge of biological material storage means an agency in Vietnam or a foreign country designated by the Ministry of Science and Technology or recognized for the function of storing biological materials.
d) The filing of biological material samples and certification documents for international applications for inventions shall comply with the provisions of the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT).

e) For biological material samples deposited at a foreign depository agency, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to deposit such samples at another competent depository agency in Vietnam if it deems necessary to clarify the nature of the subject matter requested for protection or to satisfy a third party's request for access to such subject matter.
23.10 Additional provisions for applications for registration of inventions related to pharmaceuticals In addition to the general requirements for invention descriptions prescribed in Clause 23.6 of this Circular, for applications for registration of inventions related to pharmaceuticals, the description must state the results of clinical tests and pharmacological effects of the pharmaceutical, at least including the following information:
a) Used materials/compound;
b) The method(s) used for testing;
c) Test results;
d) The relationship between the pharmacological effects obtained in tests and the practical application of pharmaceuticals in disease prevention, diagnosis and treatment.
23.11 Additional provisions for applications for registration of inventions related to genetic resources or traditional knowledge
Apart from the general requirements for invention registration applications prescribed in points 23.1 to 23.7 of this Circular, an application for registration of an invention related to a genetic resource or traditional knowledge must also contain a presentation on the origin of such genetic resource or traditional knowledge which has been accessed by the author of the invention or the applicant, if the invention is directly based on such genetic resource or traditional knowledge. If the author of the invention or the applicant cannot identify the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge, he/she/it shall clearly state this and take responsibility for the truthfulness of such identification.
24. Format appraisal and publication of invention registration applications
The procedures for formal examination and publication of invention registration applications shall comply with the general provisions prescribed in clauses 13 and 14 of this Circular.
25. Content appraisal of an invention registration application
25.1 Request for substantive examination of an invention registration application
a) The applicant or any third party has the right to request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to conduct a content appraisal of an invention registration application in accordance with Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property, and is specified as follows:
(i) The request for content appraisal of an invention registration application must be made in writing according to Form No. 03-YCTD specified in Appendix B of this Circular or stated in the invention registration application declaration (if the applicant is the applicant and such request is made immediately upon filing the application);

(ii) The request for content appraisal of an invention registration application must be filed within 42 months from the priority date if it requests the issuance of an invention patent, or within 36 months from the priority date if it requests the issuance of a utility solution patent; The time limit for filing a request for content appraisal may be extended but shall not exceed 6 months if there are legitimate reasons;
(iii) The applicant for content appraisal of an invention registration application shall pay a fee for searching and a content appraisal fee as prescribed; if the content appraisal request is submitted beyond the prescribed time limit, the applicant must pay an extension fee as prescribed in Point 20.4.b of this Circular; if the content appraisal fee is not paid, the content appraisal request shall be considered invalid and the National Office of Intellectual Property will not conduct content appraisal.
b/ Requesting content appraisal of an invention registration application filed after the date of its publication shall be published in the Industrial Property Gazette in the second month after receipt of such request; if such request is made by a third party, it shall be notified to the applicant.
An application for substantive examination of an invention registration application filed before the date of disclosure shall be disclosed together with the corresponding application.
c/ In case no request for content appraisal is filed within the time limit prescribed at Point 25.1.a above, the application shall be regarded as having been withdrawn at the time of expiry of such time limit.
25.2 Procedures for content appraisal
The content appraisal of an invention registration application shall be carried out according to the general procedures specified in Clause 15 of this Circular and the specific provisions of this Clause.
25.3 Assessment of compatibility between subject matters stated in the application and the type of invention protection title

a) Subject matters stated in an invention registration application shall be considered incompatible with the type of invention protection title requested by the applicant for issuance (invention patent/utility solution patent) if such subject matters are not technical solutions, specifically products or processes. The identification of technical solutions is prescribed in clause 25.3.b below.
b) Technical solutions - subject matters of inventions - means a necessary and sufficient combination of information on technical methods and/or technical means to solve a definite task (problem).
Technical solutions may be of one of the following forms:
(i) Physical products (instruments, machinery, equipment, components, electrical circuits, etc.) expressed as a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by signs (features) regarding structure, which has functions (utility) as a means to meet certain needs of humans; or physical products (materials, substances, foods, pharmaceuticals, etc.) expressed as a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by signs (features) regarding the presence, proportion and state of elements, which have functions (utility) as a means to meet certain needs of humans; or biological materials (genes, genetically modified plants/animals, etc.) expressed as a collection of information about a product containing genetic information altered under human influence, capable of self-reproduction;
(ii) Process (technological process; method of diagnosis, prediction, inspection, handling, etc.) expressed as a set of information defining the manner in which a specific process or task is carried out, characterized by signs (features) regarding the order, conditions, participants, measures and means to perform operations in order to achieve a certain purpose.
c) The subject matter stated in the application shall not be considered a technical solution in the following cases: (i) The subject matter stated in the application is merely an idea or intention, or merely raises a problem but does not constitute a way of solving the problem, and cannot answer the question "by what means" or "by what means"; (ii) The problem (task) raised for resolution is not a technical issue and cannot be resolved by technical means;
(iii) Natural products, not created by humans.
25.4 Assessment of industrial applicability according to Article 62 of the Law on Intellectual Property
a) The technical solutions stated in the application are considered "feasible" if:
(i) Information on the nature of the solution together with instructions on necessary technical conditions shall be presented clearly and sufficiently so that persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field may create, manufacture or use, exploit or implement such solution;
The concept of "person with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field" is understood as prescribed in Point 23.6.a of this Circular;
(ii) The creation, production, use, exploitation or implementation of the above solutions may be repeated with the same results and similar to the results stated in the invention description.
b) Technical solutions deemed incapable of industrial application in the following cases: (i) The nature of the subject matter or instructions aimed at implementing the subject matter contradicts fundamental principles of science (e.g., does not comply with the principle of energy conservation, etc.);
(ii) The subject matter includes elements and components that have no technical connection with each other or cannot be connected (joined, bound, dependent, etc.) with each other;
(iii) Subject to internal contradictions;
(iv) The subject matters of the instructions may be given only a limited number of times (cannot be repeated);
(v) In order to implement the solution, the person must have special skills which cannot be transferred or only given to others;
(vi) The results obtained from the implementation are not consistent;
(vii) The results obtained are different from those stated in the application;
(viii) There is no or insufficient most important instruction for solution; (ix) Other cases with other plausible reasons.
25.5 Assessment of novelty as prescribed in Article 60 of the Law on Intellectual Property

a) Minimum mandatory information sources
In order to assess the novelty of the technical solutions stated in the application, it is required to search information in the following compulsory sources (but not limited to the minimum number of searches in these sources): (i) All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam having the same classification index with the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal, up to the third sub-classification index and with an earlier publication date than the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal;
(ii) Applications for invention registration or invention protection titles published by other organizations or countries within 25 years before the filing date or priority date of the application being appraised (if the application is eligible for priority right) shall be kept in the database on inventions at the National Office of Intellectual Property and other information sources prescribed by the National Office of Intellectual Property, with the search scope prescribed at Point 25.5.a(i) above.
In necessary and possible cases, the search may be extended to scientific reports, reports on results of research programs and projects, and other documents in the same technical field, which are published and stored at the National Center for Science and Technology Information.
b) Search purpose
The purpose of information lookup is to find technical solutions with similar or identical nature to those stated in the application.
In this point:
(i) Two technical solutions shall be regarded as identical when they have all basic signs (characteristics) identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
(ii) Two technical solutions shall be considered similar when they have most of their basic signs (characteristics) identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
(iii) “Controlled technical solution” means a technical solution identical or most similar to the one stated in the application;
(iv) “Comparison documents” are documents describing the technical solution of comparison or evidence proving that the technical solution of comparison has been publicly disclosed.
c) Reference report
Search results must be presented in the search report, specifying the searched field, scope and results within such scope (statistics of found control technical solutions, specifying identical signs, names of control documents, page numbers, line numbers, origin of documents and date of publication of corresponding documents) and full name of the person making the report (searcher).
d) Methods for assessing the novelty of technical solutions
In order to evaluate the novelty of a technical solution stated in an application, it is required to compare fundamental signs (characteristics) of such technical solution with signs of control technical solutions found during the information lookup process; in which:
(i) Fundamental signs of a technical solution may be its functional, utility, structural, compositional and/or interconnection characteristics, together with other fundamental signs, which form a necessary and sufficient set for determining the nature (content) of the subject matter;
(ii) The fundamental signs of technical solutions stated in the application and protection titles, which are shown within the scope (requested) of invention protection;
(iii) Basic signs of technical solutions stated in other documents shall be presented and detected according to description documents or actual forms of such technical solutions.
e) Conclusion on the novelty of technical solutions
Corresponding to a point of the protection scope (requested), the technical solution stated in the application shall be considered new compared with the world's technical level if:
(i) No technical solution for comparison is found during the information lookup; or (ii) A technical solution for comparison is found but there is at least one fundamental difference between the technical solution stated in the application and the technical solution for comparison (and such difference is called a fundamental different sign).
25.6 Assessment of creativity level as prescribed in Article 61 of the Law on Intellectual Property
a) Minimum mandatory information sources

When assessing the creativity of technical solutions, it is required to at least search information in compulsory sources (but not limited to such a minimum source as prescribed in Point 25.5.a of this Circular).
b) Assess creativity level
The assessment of the level of creativity of technical solutions stated in the application shall be carried out by assessing fundamental distinctive signs (signs) stated within the scope (requirements) of protection in order to make a conclusion that: (i) Fundamental distinctive signs (signs) have been considered as having been disclosed in the mandatory minimum information source or not, and;
(ii) Collecting other basic distinctive signs which are considered obvious to persons with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field or not.
In response to a point within the scope of (requested) protection, a technical solution shall be considered creative if the addition of a distinctive basic sign to the collection of basic signs of the technical solution is the result of creative activity and not the obvious result of common knowledge in the relevant technical field.
c/ In the following cases (but not only in these cases), for a point within the protection scope, a technical solution shall be considered not being creative:
(i) A collection of other obviously distinctive fundamental signs (anyone with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field would know that in order to perform a given function or achieve a given purpose, it is necessary to use such a collection of signs and vice versa when using such a collection of signs, it is necessary to achieve the corresponding purpose or perform the corresponding function); (ii) A collection of distinct fundamental signs which have been presented in the form of uniformity or equivalence in one or several known technical solutions in the mandatory minimum information source; (iii) Technical solutions are simple combinations of known technical solutions with functions, purposes and effectiveness, which are also simple combinations of functions, purposes and effectiveness of each known technical solution.
d) In this point:
(i) Two signs are considered identical if they have the same nature;
(ii) Two signs shall be regarded as equivalent if they have similar natures, the same purpose and the same method of achieving the basic purpose.
25.7 Examination of the first-to-file principle as prescribed in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property Before issuing a decision to grant a protection title, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall examine the first-to-file principle for invention registration applications which have been substantively examined and concluded to satisfy the protection conditions under the following provisions:
a) Minimum mandatory information sources
In order to verify the first-to-file principle, at least the following mandatory sources of information must be consulted (but not limited to the minimum number of these sources):
All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (up to the time of examination) have the same classification index as the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal, up to the third classification index and the date of filing or priority date is earlier than the date of filing or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights), but has not been published or the publication date is later than the date of filing or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights).
b) The purpose of the search is to find (multiple) applications for registration of the same invention and determine the earliest filing date or priority date of an application;
c/ In case there are many applications for registration of the same invention, a protection title may be granted only to the valid application with the earliest priority or submission date among applications which satisfy all conditions for the grant of a protection title;
d/ In case there are many applications for registration of the same invention which meet all conditions for the grant of a protection title and are filed on the same priority day or the earliest day, the protection title may be granted to only one of such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; otherwise all applications shall be rejected from the grant of the protection title;
e) In case an applicant for registration of an invention requests priority right on the basis of the first application for registration of the same invention filed in Vietnam, the protection title may only be granted to the application for registration of the invention requesting priority right accepted and the first application filed in Vietnam shall be considered as withdrawn.

25.8 Notification of content appraisal results
Notification of content appraisal results of an invention registration application shall be carried out according to the general procedures specified in Point 15.7.a of this Circular.
26. Granting, registration and publication of invention patents and utility solution patents The procedures for issuing, registering and publishing invention patents and utility solution patents shall comply with the general provisions specified in Points 18 and 19 of this Circular.
27. Processing international applications for inventions
27.1 Receiving authority
The competent agency to receive international applications for inventions in Vietnam is the National Office of Intellectual Property. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall have the responsibility to:
a) Receive international applications originating in Vietnam;
b) Collecting fees for international application submission and notifying fees as prescribed to enable applicants to transfer them to International Office and International Search Agency in accordance with the Agreement on Cooperation in Patent Activities - PCT (hereinafter referred to as "the Agreement");
c/ To check whether the fees are paid on time;
d) Inspect and handle international applications originating in Vietnam according to the provisions of the Treaty;
e) Identify protected subject matters: if the protected subject matters of the application are classified as national secrets, further work shall not be carried out and fees shall be refunded to the applicant, except for international filing fees and international duplicate fee;
g/ To send one copy (application dossier) of the international application originating in Vietnam to the International Office and one copy (reference copy) to the international reference agency;
h/ To send and receive letters from applicants and international agencies.
27.2 Language
An international application originating in Vietnam shall be made in English. The application shall be made in 3 copies.
In case the number of copies prescribed is not enough, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall make additional copies to the required number and the applicant must pay an international copy fee.
27.3 International search agencies and international preliminary examination agencies For international applications of Vietnamese origin, the competent international search agencies and international preliminary examination agencies are the Inventions Agency, the Industrial Property Agency or the Intellectual Property Agency of Austria, Russia, Switzerland, South Korea and the European Inventions Agency.
a/ If an international application contains a designation of Vietnam, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall be designated agency. In this case, in order to enter the national stage, within 31 months after the priority date, the applicant shall submit it to the National Office of Intellectual Property:
(i) Declaration for invention registration application, made using Form 01-SC under Appendix A hereof; (ii) International application copy (if applicant requests to enter national phase before international publication);
(iii) Vietnamese translation of the international application: description, including description, protection request, annotations to drawings and summary (initially submitted original or published copy if the application has not yet been published and revised copies and explanatory notes on the revised parts, if the international application is amended under Article 19 of the Agreement); (iv) National fees and charges.
b/ An international application filed with the National Office of Industrial Property within six months after the expiry of the time limit prescribed at Point 27.4.a above may be accepted on condition that the applicant pays the prescribed fee and charge.
27.5 International applications with selection of Vietnam
a/ If Vietnam is selected in an international application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall be the selected agency. In this case, if the selection of Vietnam is effected within 19 months from the priority date so as to enter the national stage, within 31 months from the priority date, the applicant shall submit to the National Office of Industrial Property the following documents:
(i) An invention registration application form, made according to Form 01-SC in Appendix A of this Circular;

(ii) Vietnamese translation of the international application: description, including description, protection request, annotations to drawings and summary (published version or original submitted initially if the application has not yet been published and revised version and explanatory document on the revised version if the international application is amended under Article 19 and/or Article 34(2)(b) of the Agreement);
(iii) Vietnamese translations of the international preliminary examination reports (when there is a request for examination of the application contents);
(iv) National fees and charges.
b/ An international application filed with the National Office of Industrial Property within six months after the expiration of the time limit prescribed at Point 27.5.a above may be accepted, provided that the applicant pays the fee according to regulations.
27.6 Priority right request
In order to enjoy the priority right, the applicant must re-state such in the declaration, pay the fee for enjoying the priority right and, at the request of the National Office of Intellectual Property, submit the Vietnamese version of the documents already submitted to the International Bureau of the necessary documents according to Rule 17.1(a) of the Implementing Regulation.
For a PCT application, the handling of the request for priority right in accordance with the PCT Convention and the Regulations on implementation of the Convention.
27.7 Handling of international applications in the national stage
a) Amendments to documents during the national stage
In accordance with Rule 51bis of the Implementing Regulations of the Treaty, the applicant shall submit a power of attorney and a document on transfer of the right to file an application in the international phase (if any) within 34 months from the priority date.
In accordance with Article 28 and Article 41 of the Treaty and Rules 52.1(b) and 78.1(b) of the Regulation on implementation of the Treaty, the applicant may amend or supplement documents in the national stage. At the time of entering the national stage, the applicant may also amend or supplement the description. The above amendment or supplementation must comply with Point 17 of this Circular.
Additional and amended documents submitted by applicants to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese.
b) The starting time of the national stage
The starting time for processing an international application with designation or selection of Vietnam at the national stage shall be the first day of the thirty-second month from the priority date, if the applicant does not submit a written request to enter the national stage earlier than the above-mentioned deadlines.
c) Appraising international applications
After the international application has entered the national phase, it shall be subject to both formal and substantive examination according to the procedures applicable to ordinary invention applications. If the applicant files a written request for an early examination and pays the prescribed fee, the international application shall be subject to an early examination as stipulated in point 27.7.b above in accordance with Article 23(2) of the Agreement.
d) International applications are considered as withdrawn
Apart from cases which are considered as withdrawal under the provisions of the Treaty and the Regulation on implementation of the Treaty, in case the national fee is not paid to the National Office of Intellectual Property or there is no Vietnamese translation after the prescribed time limit, the international application designated or selected to be processed in Vietnam shall be considered as withdrawn.
27.8 International registration fees and charges
a) Applicants for international applications at the national stage shall pay fees and charges according to regulations applicable to invention registration applications submitted in Vietnam.
b/ International applicants of Vietnamese origin shall pay fees and charges according to the Regulation on implementation of the Treaty and the Circular guiding the collection, payment, management and use of industrial property fees and charges of the Ministry of Finance.
Section 3. REGISTRATION OF PLANNING
28. Requirements for layout design registration applications
28.1 An application for layout design registration shall satisfy the general requirements prescribed in Points 7 and 10 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this Point.

28.2 Documents, samples and information showing layout design specified in Point 7.1.a (ii) of this Circular include:
a) A set of photos or a set of layout design drawings, including 04 sets;
b) Form of integrated circuit manufactured by layout design, including 04 forms if the layout design has been commercially exploited;
c) Description of the production integrated circuit made under a layout design, including information to clarify the nature of the layout design.
28.3 The application must ensure consistency as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically requesting protection for a single layout design of a semiconductor integrated circuit. 28.4 Where there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to submit verification documents within one month, particularly documents confirming lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits submission rights from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate of transfer of application submission rights, agreement on assignment of work or employment contract, etc.); 28.5 Requirements for the Declaration
The applicant shall submit 02 declarations, made according to Form No. 02-TKBT in Appendix A of this Circular.
a) General requirements: The set of photos and drawings of layout design must fully show the spatial structure of components and their interconnections in semi-conducting integrated circuits so that based on such set of photos/drawings, a unique layout design can be identified. For the above purpose, the set of photos/drawings of layout design must satisfy the conditions specified at Points 28.6.b, c and d below.
b) Document type:
A set of photos or drawings of layout design must include at least one of the following documents: (i) Drawings made by a layout-designing machine for each layer of an integrated circuit;
(ii) Drawings or photos of photolithically carved masks used for producing each integrated circuit layer;
(iii) Photographs of each layer of layout design shown in the integrated circuit;
c) Document form: The set of photos/drawings of layout design must be submitted in paper form and may be accompanied by supplementary documents being objects carrying electronic data for part or all of such set of photos/drawings of layout design.
d) Form of documents
(i) The set of photos/drawings of layout design must include separate photos/drawings for each class of layout design, with symbols for each class, size of integrated circuit and magnification; (ii) All photos/drawings of layout design must be under the same scale. For paper documents: each photo/drawing of layout design must be a magnified copy at least 20 times the size of the integrated circuit so that the basic circuit can be seen by the naked eye;
(iii) Each photo or drawing of layout design may be presented on an A4 paper sheet or a larger paper sheet, provided that it is folded into an A4 size;
(iv) The layout photos/drawings must be clear and sharp.
28.7 Requirements for production integrated circuit boards made according to layout design
a/ The submitted form must be the whole or a part of an integrated circuit corresponding to the layout design stated in the application. If the integrated circuit produced according to the layout design is a part inseparable from another product, the product enclosed with the application must be accompanied by documents indicating accurately the integrated circuit produced according to such layout design.
b) If the layout design has been commercially exploited anywhere in the world before the filing date, the applicant shall submit the first commercial exploitation sample.
28.8 Requirements on descriptions of semi-conducting closed circuits produced by layout design The description must include the following detailed information about a semi-conducting closed circuit produced by a layout design which is required to be protected:
a) Name/code: is a collection of letters and/or numbers used to distinguish this integrated circuit from other integrated circuits when being put on the market;

b) Describe basic functions of the integrated circuit (e.g., memory function, logic function, or other functions);
c) Description of fundamental structure of integrated circuit (e.g., bipolar or MOS, BiMOS, optoelectronic or other structures);
d) Description of technology for manufacturing integrated circuits (e.g. TTL, DTL, ECL, ILT, CMOS, NMOS, PMOS or other technologies);
e) Describe main characteristics distinguishing from other integrated semiconductor circuits on the market at the time of filing or at the time of first commercial exploitation in the world, whichever is earlier.
29. Confidentiality of information in layout design registration applications
Applicants may request to keep confidential information submitted in their applications for layout design registration as follows:
29.1 The maximum level of confidentiality to be kept is permitted:
a) For layout designs not yet commercially exploited: 50% of the surface area of each layer;
b/ For a layout design which has been commercially exploited: 2 layers in each of 5 groups counted from the top downwards.

29.2 In order to have information kept confidential, the applicant shall make a request for information confidentiality keeping under the guidance of the National Office of Intellectual Property and indicate documents and materials containing secret information.

29.3 Documents containing secret information must be separated into a package of confidential documents which may be submitted in the following forms:
a) Microfilm or similar form of documents showing size of design on computer drawings;
b) Electronic data;
c) Drawings or photos of parts that are not visible, provided that the layout design’s characteristics are basically visible.
29.4 The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for maintaining confidentiality of information at the request of applicants in accordance with Point 29.1 of this Circular.
30. Appraisal of layout design registration applications
30.1 Procedures for formal examination of layout design registration applications shall comply with general provisions in Points 13.1, 13.2, 13.3, 13.4 and 13.8 of this Circular and special procedures applicable to layout design registration applications prescribed in this Point.
30.2 Notification of appraisal results
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Point 13.2 of this Circular or contains errors as prescribed in Point 13.3 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the procedures specified in Point 13.6.a of this Circular.
b) If the application is valid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of acceptance to the applicant, clearly stating the name and address of the applicant, the name of the representative (if any), and information about the subject matter stated in the application, the submission date, and also stating that the subject matter stated in the application may be eligible for a layout design registration certificate if, after 3 months from the date on which the layout design is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette, there is no legitimate objection from a third party against the registration of such layout design.
30.3 Refusal to accept applications
In case where the applicant has been notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam of the results of the formal examination, including deficiencies and the intended refusal to accept the application according to Point 13.6.a of this Circular, but the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to meet requirements for correction, does not have any objection or provides unjustifiable objections within the prescribed time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the refusal to accept the layout design registration application and refund the fees and charges already paid related to the post-formal examination work at the request of the applicant.
31. Publication of the layout design registration application
31.1 Design registration applications which have been accepted as valid shall be published according to the general procedures specified in Point 14 of this Circular and the provisions of this Point.
31.2 Access to detailed information about valid layout design registration applications

a) From the date on which the application is published in the Industrial Property Gazette, all persons may access detailed information about the nature of layout design stated in the published application, except for confidential information as prescribed in Point 29 of this Circular.
b) Only competent authorities may carry out procedures for invalidating protection titles or handling acts of infringement upon rights to new layout designs, and are permitted to access confidential information about such layout designs.
32. Grant, register and publish decisions on grant of certificates of registered layout design

32.1 Notice of intended grant of a certificate of registered layout design If, after 3 months from the date on which the layout design is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette, there is no third party’s objection to the registration of the layout design or, despite having an objection, the handling results prove that the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended grant of a certificate of registered layout design for the subject matter stated in the application; and the time limit for the applicant to pay the fee for publication of the title of protection, the registration fee and the title of protection.

The above time limit is 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice.
32.2 Issuance, registration and publication of decisions on issuance of certificates of registered layout design The procedures for issuance, registration and publication of certificates of registered layout design shall comply with the general procedures prescribed in Points 18.2 and 19 of this Circular.
32.3 Refusal to grant certificates of registered layout design
If within 3 months from the date on which the layout design is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette, there are third parties' opinions opposing the grant of a certificate of registration of the layout design and such opinions are proved to be justifiable, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures for refusal to grant a certificate of registration of the layout design according to general procedures specified in Point 18.1 of this Circular.
Section 4. PROCEDURES FOR REGISTRATION OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGN
33. Requirements on applications for registration of industrial designs
33.1 An industrial design registration application shall satisfy the general requirements prescribed in Points 7 and 10.1 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this Point.
33.2 An industrial design registration application shall ensure uniformity as prescribed in clauses 1 and 3 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following regulations.
An industrial design registration application shall be considered as ensuring uniformity if:
a/ Requesting protection of an industrial design of a product; or b/ Requesting protection of industrial designs of many products in a set of products, each of which has a corresponding industrial design; or c/ Requesting protection of an industrial design of a product accompanied by one or more variants of such industrial design.
33.3 Information provision request
a) In case of having grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of information in an industrial design registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to submit documents verifying such information within 1 month, especially documents certifying lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (certificate of inheritance rights, certificate or written agreement on transfer of application submission rights; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.);
b) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may also request applicants to submit documents certifying the ownership or lawful use right of trade indications (marks, geographical indications, trade names), industrial designs protected by others within one month if there are grounds to suspect that the industrial designs stated in the applications contain such objects.
33.4 Requirements for declarations
The applicant shall submit 02 declarations, made according to Form No. 03-KDCN in Appendix A of this Circular. In addition to other fields to be declared, the declaration must specify the international classification code of the industrial design requested for protection in accordance with the International Classification of Industrial Designs (according to the Locarno Agreement). If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify it and the applicant shall pay a classification fee as prescribed.
33.5 Requirements on descriptions of industrial designs
The applicant shall submit one industrial design description including the following contents:

a) Name of industrial design means the name of the product bearing such industrial design expressed in common words, not for advertising purposes, containing no signs, annotations or commercial indications;
b) Field of application of an industrial design means a specific field in which the product bearing such industrial design is used, clearly indicating the use purpose of such product;
c) The closest similar industrial design: clearly stating the most dissimilar industrial design to the industrial design of the same product type stated in the application, which is widely known before the filing date or the priority date (if the application requests priority rights), specifying the source of information disclosing such closest similar industrial design;
d) List of photos or drawings: list photograms, three-dimensional drawings, projections, cross-sections, etc. of industrial designs in the order of their serial numbers;
e) The description of industrial designs must comply with the following regulations:
(i) Fully disclose the nature of the industrial design to be protected, which must fully describe the features expressing the nature of the industrial design and also indicate new and distinctive features compared with the nearest similar industrial designs as mentioned in Point 33.5.c above, consistent with the features expressed in the set of photos or drawings;
(ii) The design features of the industrial design to be protected shall be presented in the following order: three-dimensional features, lines, correlation between three-dimensional features and/or lines, color features (if any);
(iii) For products with different use states (e.g., products with a lid or foldable cover), the industrial design of the product in different states must be described;

(iv) If the industrial design consists of multiple schemes, it is required to specify the distinctive features of the remaining schemes compared to the basic scheme (the first scheme stated in the application);

(v) If the industrial design is the design of a set of products, the design of each product in the set must be described.
g) Scope of protection of industrial designs (or "protection requests"): it is required to fully list features necessary and sufficient to determine the nature of the industrial design requested for protection and the scope of industrial property rights to such industrial design, which are shown in photos or drawings stated in the application, including new and distinctive features compared with similar known industrial designs.

33.6 Requirements on sets of photos or drawings of industrial designs The applicant shall submit 05 sets of photos or 05 sets of drawings of industrial designs. Sets of photos or drawings must fully show the design features of the industrial designs requested for protection to the extent that any person with average knowledge in the relevant field can identify such industrial designs according to the following guidelines:
a) The photos or drawings must be clear and sharp; the drawings must be drawn with continuous lines; the background of the photos or drawings must have a uniform color and contrast with the industrial design; on the photos or drawings, only products with the industrial design requested for protection are shown (not accompanied by other products).
b) The photos or drawings must show the industrial design in the same scale. The size of the industrial design in the photos or drawings must not be smaller than 90 mm x 120 mm and not larger than 190 mm x 277 mm.
c) The photos and drawings must show the industrial design in the same orientation and in the following order: three-dimensional perspective views of the industrial design, views from the front, back, right, left, top and bottom; these views must be frontal.
d) For a symmetrical industrial design, the photograph or drawing may not need to further show the symmetry, provided that this is clearly stated in the list of photographs and drawings in the description.
e) For industrial designs of products which can be developed (for example: containers, packages, etc.), images of such industrial designs may be replaced by photos or drawings of the industrial designs in the developed state.
g) Depending on the complexity of the industrial design, additional photos and perspective drawings from different angles, cross-sections, enlarged views of parts, separate details of the product, etc., which are sufficient to clearly show the new and distinctive features of the industrial design subject to protection may be required.
h) For products with different usage states (e.g., products with a lid or foldable), there must be photos and drawings of the industrial design of the product in different states.

i) For the industrial design of a part of a complete product, there must be additional photos and drawings illustrating the installation and use of such part on the complete product.
k) Each industrial design must be accompanied by a set of photos and drawings showing each option in accordance with this Point.
l) For a set of products, there must be a set of photos and drawings of each product in the set as prescribed at this Point.
33.7 Design features of industrial designs
a) Design characteristics of an industrial design means elements expressed in the form of lines, three-dimensional forms, colors, positional correlation or size correlation so that when combined with other characteristics (signatures), they form a necessary and sufficient set to create such industrial design.
b) The following elements shall not be considered as design features of an industrial design: (i) Shapes and lines determined by the technical function of the product (e.g., the flat shape of a data recording disc determined by the relative movement between the disc and the disc reader);
(ii) Elements whose presence in the collection of signs does not sufficiently create aesthetic impression (the impression of the product’s appearance remains unchanged when such elements are present or absent; for example: a change to a familiar shape or line but such change is insufficient to be recognized, therefore the changed shape or line is still only recognized as the old one); (iii) Materials used for manufacturing products;
(iv) Signs affixed or stuck on products are only for the purpose of providing information and instructions about the origin, characteristics, composition, uses, and methods of use of such products; e.g., terms on product labels;
(v) Size of products, except for changes to decorative sizes of fabric samples and similar materials.
c) Fundamental design features and non-fundamental design features Fundamental design features are those which can be easily recognized or remembered, and which are necessary and sufficient to determine an industrial design and distinguish one industrial design from another for products of the same type.
Design features which fail to satisfy the above conditions are called “non-substantive design features”.
34. Format appraisal and publication of industrial design registration applications The format appraisal and publication procedures for industrial design registration applications shall comply with the general procedures prescribed in clauses 13 and 14 of this Circular.
35. Content appraisal of applications for registration of industrial designs
35.1 Assessment of similarity of industrial designs:
a) Two industrial designs shall be regarded as identical when they are used for products of the same type and have the same collection of fundamental and non-fundamental design characteristics;
b) Two industrial designs shall be regarded as similar when they are used for products of the same type and have some basic design features which are identical;
c) Two industrial designs shall be considered most similar when they have more identical basic design characteristics than all other similar industrial designs.
35.2 Procedures for substantive examination of applications for registration of industrial designs The substantive examination of an application for registration of industrial design shall be carried out in accordance with the general procedures specified in Clause 15 of this Circular and the specific procedures prescribed in this Clause.
35.3 Assessment of compatibility between subject matters stated in the application and type of industrial design protection title
The subject matter stated in the application shall be considered incompatible with the type of industrial design protection title if:
a) The subject matter is not the appearance of the product;
b) The subject matters stated in the application are:
(i) The appearance of a product which is required by its technical characteristics; or (ii) The appearance of a civil or industrial construction work;

(iii) Internal appearance (the invisible part) during the use of the product (utilizing the product's functions in the ordinary way by any user, excluding maintenance, repair or repair work).
35.4 Information lookup
a) Information lookup purpose
The purpose of information lookup is to search among the mandatory information sources for industrial designs identical or similar to those stated in the application.
b) The mandatory minimum sources of information used in the process of content appraisal of an application include the following documents:
(i) Industrial design registration applications that have been received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and have an earlier publication date than the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights);
(ii) Applications for registration of industrial designs and protection titles of industrial designs filed by other organizations or countries within 25 years before the filing date or the priority date of the application which is being examined (if the application enjoys priority right) shall be kept in the existing database on industrial designs at the National Office of Intellectual Property;
(iii) Other information relating to industrial designs collected and stored by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
(iv) The industrial design registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam with submission dates or priority dates earlier than those of the applications currently under appraisal (used to check the first-to-file rule prescribed in Point 35.9 of this Circular).
c) In necessary and possible cases, the search may be expanded beyond the minimum compulsory sources of information.
35.5 Search report
Search results must be presented in search reports, specifying the searched field, scope of search and search results within such scope (statistics and identification of found control industrial designs, information sources, date of publication of corresponding information) and full names of report preparers (searchers).
In this point, “industrial design used for comparison” means an industrial design identical or similar to the industrial design stated in the application, which is compared with the industrial design stated in the application when assessing novelty and creativity.
35.6 Assessment of industrial applicability of industrial designs according to Article 67 of the Law on Intellectual Property
a) The industrial design stated in the application shall be considered industrially applicable if, based on the information about the industrial design presented in the application, a person with average knowledge in the relevant field can use such industrial design as a model to manufacture products with an appearance identical to that of the industrial design by industrial or handicraft methods.
The concept of "average person" in the relevant field shall be understood according to the provisions at Point 23.6.a of this Circular.
b/ In the following cases, the subject matter stated in the application shall be regarded as having no industrial applicability:
(i) The subject matter stated in the application is the form of a product with an unstable existence state (products in solid or liquid forms, etc.);
(ii) The product is only capable of having a shape like the object stated in the application with special skills or it is impossible to repeatedly create products with the shape like the object stated in the application;

(iii) Other cases with other legitimate reasons.
35.7 Assessment of novelty of industrial designs according to Article 65 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
a) Methods for assessing novelty of industrial designs
To evaluate the novelty of an industrial design stated in an application, it is required to compare a collection of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial design with a collection of fundamental design characteristics of the closest identical or similar industrial design used as a control industrial design found during the information lookup process.

b) Conclusion on the novelty of the industrial design
An industrial design stated in an application shall be regarded as new if:
(i) The industrial design is not found in the mandatory information sources; or (ii) Although the industrial design is found in the mandatory information sources, it has at least one basic design feature not present in (not belonging to) the set of basic design features of the industrial design mentioned in the application, or (iii) The industrial design mentioned in the application is the same as the industrial design disclosed in the case specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 Article 65 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
a) Methods for assessing the creativity of industrial designs
To evaluate the creativity of an industrial design mentioned in an application, it is required to compare a collection of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial design with a collection of fundamental design characteristics of each identical or similar control industrial design found during the information lookup process.
b) Conclusion on the creativity of the industrial design
In the following cases, industrial designs stated in applications shall be deemed not to be creative:
(i) Industrial design means a simple combination of known design features (publicly disclosed design features arranged or assembled in a simple manner such as replacement, change of position, increase or decrease in quantity, etc.);
(ii) Industrial design means a form which is a copy or imitation of part or the whole of the inherent natural forms of trees, fruits, animals, etc., or the geometric forms (circle, ellipse, triangle, square, rectangle, regular polygon, prism with cross-section being one of the above-mentioned forms) which are widely known;
(iii) Industrial design means a mere reproduction of the appearance of products or works which are well known or widely recognized in Vietnam or in the world;
(iv) Industrial designs which are a simulation of industrial designs in other fields if such simulation has been widely known in practice (e.g. toys simulating cars, motorcycles, etc.). If not falling into the above cases, industrial designs shall be regarded as being creative.

35.9 Examination of the principle of first filing according to Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property
a) To verify the first-to-file principle, it is required to search information in the mandatory sources specified in Point 35.4.b (iv) of this Circular.
b) The industrial design stated in the application shall be considered meeting the first-to-file rule if no identical or significantly different industrial designs are found in the applications that have satisfied the conditions for the issuance of an industrial design patent, which is discovered during the information search.
c) The industrial design of the product part stated in the application shall also be considered satisfying the first-to-file principle if no identical or significantly different industrial designs of the product parts and/or products stated in other applications that satisfy the conditions for the grant of an industrial design patent are found during the information search.
d) In case there are many different applications for registration of industrial designs which are identical or insignificantly different from each other and all satisfy the conditions for grant of an industrial design patent and have the earliest priority date or submission date, the industrial design stated in such application shall still be regarded as satisfying the first-to-file rule prescribed in Clause 2, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property if all applicants reach agreement on the filing of a single application bearing one industrial design patent.
35.10 Notification of results of content appraisal
Notification of results of content appraisal of industrial design registration applications shall comply with Point 15.7.a of this Circular.
36. Granting, registering and publishing industrial design patents

The procedures for issuing, registering and publishing decisions on the grant of industrial design patents shall comply with the general provisions prescribed in clauses 18 and 19 of this Circular.
Section 5. PROCEDURES FOR REGISTRATION OF MARKS
37. Requirements on mark registration applications
37.1 A mark registration application shall satisfy the general document requirements prescribed in clauses 7 and 10.1 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this clause.
37.2 An application must ensure consistency with provisions of clauses 1 and 4 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property. Each application may request registration of one mark for one or more goods or services.

37.3 In case of doubt about the authenticity of information stated in an application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to submit the following documents within 01 month for verification of such information:
a) Documents proving the applicant’s status:
(i) The business registration paper, contract or other document certifying the production of products or provision of services by the applicant as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property; (ii) Agreement or letter certifying that the manufacturer does not use the mark and does not object to the registration of the mark by the person conducting commercial activities of the manufacturer as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(iii) Decision or license to establish, charter of the organization certifying the functions and authority to manage collective marks, quality certification marks, geographical origin marking marks for goods or services as prescribed in clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

(iv) Agreement, business registration certificate, documents related to mark registration of co-owners as prescribed in clause 5 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(v) Documents certifying that the applicant has acquired the mark registration right from another person according to Clause 6 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(vi) The agreement or written approval of the mark owner certifying the registration right of the representative or agent as prescribed in Clause 7, Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 6 of the Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property.
b) Documents proving the representative status of the applicant: original power of attorney of the applicant; documents confirming that the representative of the applicant is the legal representative of the mark registration organization or the person authorized by such organization; documents confirming that the authorized person of the applicant meets the requirements to act as the representative of the applicant as prescribed in Point 3 of this Circular.
c) Documents proving the right to use/registration of marks containing special signs for: (i) Names, symbols, flags, armorial bearings of domestic and international agencies or organizations or certification seals, inspection seals, warranty seals of international organizations as prescribed in clauses 2 and 4 Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(ii) Names of characters and images of works under copyright protection that are widely known or trade names, commercial indications, origin indications, prizes, medals or distinctive symbols of certain products which may cause confusion as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(iii) Signs falling within the scope of protection of industrial designs of others as prescribed at Point n, Clause 2, Article 74 of the Intellectual Property Law;
d) Documents proving priority rights;
e) Necessary information to clarify or confirm contents stated in regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks or other application documents.
37.4 Requirements for declarations
The applicant shall submit 02 declarations, made according to Form No. 04-NH specified in Appendix A of this Circular, with the following notes:
a) The description of a mark in the declaration must specify the type of registered mark (common mark, collective mark, integrated mark or certification mark);
b/ For a mark registered as an integrated mark, the applicant shall specify the integrated elements of the mark or of goods or services in compliance with the following provisions:
(i) In case the linking element is a mark (similar to other marks of the same applicant used for the same goods or service or used for similar goods or services), it is required to specify whether among such linking marks, there is any basic mark or not, if so, which one is the basic mark; if one or several of such marks have been registered or mentioned in previous applications, it is required to specify the protection title number and the previous application number;

(ii) In case the linking element is a good or service (one mark used for similar or related goods or services), it is required to specify whether among such goods or services, there is any basic good or service or not, if so, which one is the basic good or service; if one of such goods or service has been registered or mentioned in previous application, it is required to specify the protection title number and the previous application number;
(iii) If the applicant fails to specify the mark or basic goods or service, all marks and all goods or services related to the mark stated in the application shall be considered independent of each other. The assessment of distinguishability of the mark stated in the application shall not be subject to the exception for integrated marks prescribed at Point f, Clause 2, Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property but must comply with the general provisions on assessment of distinguishability prescribed at Point 39 of this Circular.
c/ For certification marks, the applicant shall specify in the declaration the purpose, contents and mode of certification (certifying what, quality, origin, geographical origin or a combination of these purposes; certification contents: specific conditions on subjects, goods and services; certification mode: certification licensing order and procedures, methods of inspection and maintenance of certification purposes and contents).
d) The declaration must contain a sample of the mark and a description in words of such mark according to the following regulations:
(i) If a mark consists of multiple elements, it must specify these elements and their combination; if the mark contains a figurative element, its content and meaning must be clearly stated;
(ii) If a request for protection of a color trademark is filed, such request must be clearly indicated and the name of the color shown on the trademark must be stated.
(iii) If the mark contains words or phrases other than Vietnamese, the Vietnamese pronunciation must be clearly indicated and, if such words or phrases have a meaning, they must be translated into Vietnamese;

(iv) If the mark contains numerals other than Arabic numerals or Roman numerals, they must be translated into Arabic numerals.
e) The section "List of goods and services bearing marks" in the declaration must be grouped according to the International Classification of Goods and Services under the Nice Agreement, which is published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Gazette.
37.5 Requirements for mark samples
In addition to the sample mark affixed on the declaration, the application must be accompanied by 9 identical samples of the mark and must meet the following requirements:
a) The trademark sample must be clearly presented with the size of each component in the trademark not larger than 80 mm and not smaller than 8 mm, the overall trademark must be presented in the trademark mold with size of 80 mm x 80 mm printed on the declaration;
b) For a 3D mark, the mark sample must be accompanied by a photograph or drawing showing the perspective view and may also be accompanied by a descriptive model in the form of a projection;
c/ For a trademark with color protection requirements, its sample must be presented in the colors required for protection. If no color protection is required, the trademark sample must be presented in black and white.
37.6 Requirements on regulations on use of collective marks and certification marks Regulations on use of collective marks and certification marks must contain the contents corresponding to those prescribed in clauses 4 and 5 Article 105 of the Law on Intellectual Property and must clarify the following issues:
a) Summary information on marks, mark owners, goods and services bearing marks;
b) Conditions for the mark registrant to grant permission for use of the mark and conditions for termination of the right to use the mark;
c/ The obligations of mark users (to ensure the quality and particular characteristics of goods or services bearing marks, to submit to the control of mark registrants and pay mark management fees, etc.);

d) Rights of mark registrants (to control compliance with regulations on use of marks, collect fees for mark management, and terminate the use right of marks used by persons ineligible to use marks according to regulations on use of marks, etc.);
e) The mechanism for licensing, controlling and inspecting the use of marks and ensuring the quality and reputation of goods or services bearing such marks;
g) Dispute settlement mechanism.
37.7. Requirements on documents certifying the permission to register and use geographical origin marking trademarks
a/ For a geographical origin marking, in addition to the regulations on use of the mark and necessary documents evidencing the registration right, the application must be enclosed with a license from the concerned local authority permitting the applicant to register the geographical origin marking (geographical name, symbol or map of the area or locality) for goods or services bearing such mark.
b/ In case it is impossible to identify a region or locality bearing a geographical origin indication based on the administrative boundaries and license of the local authority specified at Point 37.7.a above, the National Office of Industrial Property shall request the applicant to provide a geographical map clearly indicating the scope of the region or locality bearing the geographical origin indication of goods or services with certification by the concerned local authority.
38. Formal examination and publication of mark registration applications
The procedures for formal examination and publication of mark registration applications shall comply with the general provisions prescribed in clauses 13 and 14 of this Circular.
39. Substantive examination of applications for registration of marks
39.1 The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for conducting content appraisal of valid applications in accordance with the general procedures specified in Point 15 of this Circular and the specific procedures prescribed in this Point.
39.2 Assessment of the compatibility between subject matters stated in applications for registration of marks and the request for issuance of certificates of mark registration
a) Under Clause 1 Article 72 of the Law on Intellectual Property, signs registered as marks must be visible signs in the form of letters, numbers, words, drawings, images, including three-dimensional images or combinations thereof, expressed in one or several certain colors.
b) The following signs shall not be protected as marks:
(i) Signs are color alone and not combined with signs in the form of letters or figures, or not presented as signs in the form of letters or figures;
(ii) Signs which are subject to protection refusal as marks according to Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(iii) Signs contrary to social order or harmful to national security.
39.3 Assessment of distinguishability of signs in the form of letters or numbers (hereinafter referred to as “letters”) according to Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Apart from the exceptions specified in Point 39.5 of this Circular, the following signs shall be considered indistinguishable:
a) Characters in languages which Vietnamese consumers have common knowledge of which they cannot recognize or remember (cannot read, understand or remember), such as characters not derived from Latin: Arabic, Slavic, Sanskrit, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, etc.; except for characters in the above-mentioned languages accompanied by other components forming a whole which can be distinguished or presented in graphic form or in other special forms
b/ Although they are Latin originals, signs may consist of a single letter or number or two letters which cannot be read as a word, even when accompanied by numbers; except for cases where such signs are presented in graphic form or in other special forms;
c/ A collection of too many letters or words which cannot be recognized and remembered as a series of characters not arranged in an orderly manner or a document or a passage of a document;
d) Although it is a Latin origin character, it is a word with meaning and its meaning has been widely used and common in Vietnam in the relevant field to the extent of being indistinguishable;
e) A single word or a collection of words used in Vietnam as common names for the goods or services concerned;

g) One or a set of words describing the main contents of goods or services bearing the mark as a sign indicating time, place and geographical origin (except for cases where the mark is registered as a mark certifying the geographical origin of goods or a collective mark), production method, type, quantity, quality, characteristics (except for cases where the mark is registered as a mark certifying the quality of goods or services), components, uses, value of goods or services;
h) One or a set of words with descriptive meaning of legal form and business sector of the mark owner;
i) Signs of identical or similar letters with one of the objects subject to protection of industrial property rights of others as prescribed in Points e, g, h, i, k, l and m, Clause 2, Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
k) Signs of misinterpretation or confusion about the origin, features, uses, quality, value or other characteristics such as composition, production process, materials, superiority of goods or services as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
l) Signs of identical or confusingly similar letters to real names, aliases, pseudonyms of leaders, national heroes and celebrities of Vietnam or foreign countries; identical or confusingly similar names to characters and images in works protected by copyright of others which are widely known, except for cases permitted by owners of such works.
39.4 Assessment of distinguishability of signs in the form of drawings or images (hereinafter referred to as “graphic signs”) according to Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property
Except for the exceptions specified in Point 39.5 of this Circular, a three-dimensional sign shall be considered indistinguishable if:
a) Geometric shapes such as circles, ellipses, triangles, quadrilaterals, etc., or simple drawings; drawings and images may only be used as backgrounds or decorative lines of products or product packages;
b/ Drawings or images which are too complicated for consumers to easily perceive and remember their features, including too many combined or overlapping images or lines;
c) Illustrations, images, symbols or signs that have been widely used;
d) Drawings and images depicting the main characteristics of goods or services bearing the mark; locations, methods of production, geographical origins, categories, quantity, quality, properties, components, uses, value or other characteristics of goods or services bearing the mark;
e) Signs which are identical or insignificantly different from the industrial designs of others;
g) Signs which are identical or confusingly similar to the image of a leader, national hero or famous person of Vietnam or foreign country; identical or confusingly similar to the image of characters and images in works protected by copyright of others who have been widely known, except for cases permitted by the owners of such works.
39.5 The following exceptions shall be applied when assessing the ability to distinguish between letters and figures:
a/ Signs falling into the cases mentioned at Points 39.3.a, b, c, g, h and Points 39.4.a, b, c, d, e of this Circular which have been used with the function of marks and are widely known by consumers, so that these marks have achieved the distinguishing capability for related goods or services.
b/ To be entitled to this exception, the applicant shall provide evidence of the extensive use of such mark (the starting time, scope and extent of current use, etc.) which is considered being used only when such use is made in lawful production, business, commercial or advertising activities, and evidence of the distinguishability of the mark for related goods or services of the mark owner. In this case, such mark shall be recognized as having a distinguishability only when it is shown in the form in which it has been continuously and widely used in practice.
39.6 Assessment of distinctiveness of signs combined between characters and images (hereinafter referred to as “combined signs”)

A combined sign is considered distinguishable when the sign of letters and signs of figures combine into a distinguishable whole, specifically:
a) The letter and figure signs are distinguishable and form an overall distinguishable sign;
b) The strong component of a mark (an element that strongly affects the consumer's perception, causing attention and impression about the mark upon observation) is a sign in the form of letters or figures that is distinguishable, even though the remaining components are indistinct or indistinctly distinguishable;
c) In case a combination of signs including both written and graphic signs is indistinguishable or has little distinguishable ability but the unique combination of these signs creates a distinct impression, such overall combination shall still be considered distinguishable;
d) The combination sign consists of letters and images that are indistinguishable or have little distinguishable but overall has achieved distinguishable capability through use according to Point 39.5 of this Circular.
39.7 Minimum information sources
a) In order to assess the ability to cause confusion of signs stated in the application, at least the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct a search in the following minimum sources of information:
(i) Mark registration applications already filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam with an earlier filing date or priority date than the filing date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal, and international mark registration applications with a designation of Vietnam to which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam has been notified by WIPO with an earlier filing date or priority date than the filing date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal for identical or similar goods or services;
(ii) Markes which have been registered for protection or recognized for protection and are still valid in Vietnam (including well-known marks) used for identical, similar or related goods or services; (iii) Markes which have been registered but have their validity terminated within less than 5 years, except for cases where the validity of a mark is terminated due to non-use as prescribed at Point d, Clause 1, Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property, used for identical or similar goods or services;
(iv) Geographical indications currently under protection in Vietnam;
(v) Geographical origins of goods and services; geographical names, quality marks, inspection marks; national flags and national emblems of countries; flags, names and symbols of agencies and organizations of Vietnam and the world; names and images of leaders, national heroes, names and images of Vietnamese and foreign celebrities, etc., which are collected and preserved by the National Office of Intellectual Property.
b/ In necessary cases, reference sources may be consulted in addition to the minimum sources mentioned at Point 39.7.a above, such as applications for registration of industrial designs, trade names, etc.; 39.8. Assessment of the similarity to the point of confusion between a registration request sign and another mark
a) In order to assess whether or not the sign of registration request stated in the application is identical or confusingly similar to another mark (hereinafter referred to as “comparison mark”), it is necessary to compare the structure, content and pronunciation (for signs in the form of letters), meaning and form of expression of the sign (for both signs in the form of letters and signs in the form of images), and at the same time compare goods or services bearing the sign with those bearing the comparison mark according to the provisions of this Point.
b) Sign identical to a control mark: A sign shall be considered identical to a control mark if it is identical to such mark in terms of structure, content, meaning and form of expression.
c) The sign is considered similar to the point of confusion with the control mark if:
(i) Such signs are similar to the control marks in terms of structure, content, pronunciation, meaning or form to the extent that consumers mistakenly believe that these two subjects are one or that this subject is a variant of that other subject or that these two subjects have the same origin;
(ii) Signs shall be interpreted as transcriptions or translations of the reference marks if such reference marks are well known.
39.9 Assessment of similarity of goods and services
a/ Two goods or two services are regarded as identical (of the same type) when they have the following characteristics:
(i) having the same nature (components, structure, etc.) and the same function and use purpose; or (ii) having nearly the same nature and the same function and use purpose;

b/ Two goods or two services are regarded as similar when they have the following characteristics:
(i) being similar in nature; or (ii) being similar in function and use purpose; and (iii) being put on the market via the same commercial channel (being distributed by the same method, being sold together or side-by-side, in the same type of shop, etc.);
c) A good and a service shall be regarded as similar if they fall into one of the following cases: (i) They are related to each other in terms of nature (the goods or service or parts thereof are made from the other goods or service); or (ii) They are related to each other in terms of function (to fulfill the function of this good or service, the other good or service must be used or they are often used together); or (iii) They are closely related to each other in terms of mode of performance (this good or service is the result of using or exploiting the other good or service, etc.).
39.10 Examination of the principle of first to file
Before issuing a decision to grant a certificate of registered mark, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file principle as prescribed in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property. 39.11 Conclusion on the likelihood of confusion between signs and control marks Signs are considered identical or confusingly similar to control marks used for identical, similar, or related goods in the following cases:
(i) Signs identical with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of the goods or services bearing the control mark;
(ii) Signs identical with the control mark and goods or services identical with those bearing the control mark of the same owner;
(iii) Signs are similar to the extent that they cause confusion with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of the control mark, except for exceptions where similarity between goods or services and similarity between signs is insufficient to create the possibility of confusion when using similar signs;
(iv) Signs identical or similar to a control mark being a well-known mark and goods or services bearing signs not identical or similar to those of goods or services bearing such mark, but the use of such signs as marks may mislead consumers into believing that there is a relationship between goods or services bearing such signs and owners of the well-known mark, which may actually reduce the distinguishability of the well-known mark or damage the reputation of the well-known mark.
39.12 Assessment of other capacity to cause confusion of signs
The appraisal of other possible confusion of signs shall comply with Article 73 and Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following specific regulations.
a/ Signs are considered misleading as to the origin of goods or services in the following cases:
(i) Signs identical or similar to the name or symbol of a country or territory (national flag, national emblem, national name, local name) or confusingly similar to the name or symbol of a country or territory leading to the misconception that goods or services bearing the mark originate from such country or territory but actually originate from another country or territory;

(ii) Signs identical or similar to protected geographical indications where the use of such signs may mislead consumers as to the geographical origin of goods; signs identical to or containing geographical indications or being translated or transcribed from protected geographical indications for wines or spirits where the signs requesting registration as marks for wines or spirits do not originate from the geographical areas bearing such geographical indications;
(iii) Sign means a word identical or similar to the trade name of another person which has been lawfully used for the same type of goods or services and which may cause consumers to mistakenly believe that the goods or services bearing such sign are produced or provided by the person with the said trade name; sign means an image identical or similar to the commercial symbol of another person which has been lawfully used for the same type of goods or services and which may cause consumers to mistakenly believe that the goods or services bearing such sign are produced or provided by the person with the said commercial symbol;

(iv) Signs identical or similar to real names, aliases, pen names and images of leaders, national heroes and famous personalities of Vietnam and foreign countries; signs identical or similar to names or images of characters or typical images of well-known works, if the use of such signs may cause consumers to mistakenly believe that goods or services bearing such signs are produced or performed by owners of such works;
(v) Signs identical with or insignificantly different from industrial designs of others protected on the basis of applications for registration of industrial designs with submission dates or priority dates earlier than those of applications for registration of marks.
b/ In the following cases, signs shall be considered likely to cause confusion or misunderstanding as to the nature and value of goods or services:
(i) Sign means words, drawings, images or symbols which create a misleading impression on the features and utility of goods or services, such as signs identical with or similar to marks or other signs widely used to the extent that they are considered associated with certain features and utilities of specific goods or services, causing consumers to mistakenly believe that goods or services bearing such signs also have such features and utilities;
(ii) Signs are words or images that create a misleading impression about the composition or structure of goods or services, such as describing other goods or services related to those with signs that create the misleading impression that the goods or services with signs are created from or have the same nature as the described goods or services.
40. Grant, register and publish mark registration certificates The procedures for issuing, registering and publishing mark registration certificates shall comply with general procedures prescribed in clauses 18 and 19 of this Circular.
41. Processing of applications for registration of international marks of Vietnamese origin and applications for registration of international marks designating Vietnam
41.1 Procedures for international registration of marks of Vietnamese origin The provisions on procedures for processing applications for mark registration prescribed in this Circular shall also apply to the processing of applications for international registration of marks at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
41.2 Right to international registration of marks based on the basic mark registration in Vietnam
a) A person who has been granted a certificate of registered mark in Vietnam may register internationally the corresponding mark under the Madrid Agreement;
b) Any person who has filed an application for registration of a mark in Vietnam and is granted a certificate of registration of the mark in Vietnam shall have the right to register such mark internationally under the Madrid Protocol. 41.3 Applications for international registration of marks of Vietnamese origin
a/ An application for international registration of a mark which designates the country where it is registered for protection as a member of the Madrid Agreement and does not designate any other country as a member of the Madrid Protocol must be made in French.
b/ An application for international registration of a mark shall designate at least one country being a contracting party to the Madrid Protocol, including concurrently designate a country being a contracting party to the Madrid Agreement, in English or French.
c) The applicant shall submit an application form for international registration of a mark made according to Form No. 06-DKQT in Annex C of this Circular and an international registration application for a mark made according to the form provided by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam free of charge. In the application form, specify the country being a member of the Madrid Agreement (and possibly a member of the Madrid Protocol) and the country being a member of the Madrid Protocol which the applicant wishes to register for protection of the mark. The international registration application for a mark must be made by accurately and fully filling in information in the fields reserved for applicants and must be enclosed with the mark samples as registered in Vietnam.
d/ The applicant shall preliminarily calculate the total fees and charges according to the fee schedule printed on the application form or may request the National Office of Industrial Property to notify the exact amount of fees and charges payable to the International Office. The applicant shall directly pay such fees and charges to the International Office and additionally pay relevant charges and fees to the National Office of Industrial Property.
e) The applicant shall ensure that the information declared in the international mark registration application is accurate, including the language and translation, and consistent with the information stated in the certificate of base mark registration or the corresponding base mark registration application. The applicant shall pay all fees related to amendments to the international mark registration application due to inaccurate or inconsistent declarations as notified by the International Office.

g) All letters and transactions related to the international registration application for a trademark shall be conducted through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall promptly notify the applicant's requests to the International Office and vice versa, and comply with relevant international treaties.
41.4 Agencies receiving applications for registration of international marks of Vietnamese origin
a) The international mark registration application shall be filed with an International Office via the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall transfer the international mark registration application to the International Office within 30 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application as prescribed.
b) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the international registration application for a mark shall be deemed as the filing date of the international registration application for the mark in case the International Office receives such application within 2 months from the date specified in the receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the applicant fails to complete and submit the application to the International Office within the said time limit, the date on which the application is received at the International Office shall be deemed as the filing date of the international registration application for the mark.
41.5 To supplement, modify or transfer applications for international registration of marks originating in Vietnam
a/ After an application is filed with the International Office, all transactions between the applicant and the International Office related to the modification of the name, address or limitation of the list of goods or services stated in the application for international registration of marks, for extension of validity of international mark registrations shall be made according to Form No. 08-SDQT in Annex C of this Circular and through the National Office of Intellectual Property. The applicant shall pay fees and charges for these transactions according to regulations.
b) Owners of marks must, through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (or directly request International Office for international mark registration in a country being a signatory to the Madrid Protocol), request International Office to record the transfer of ownership of marks according to international mark registration and comply with relevant international treaties and pay fees and charges as prescribed.
a) After receiving a notice of the International Office on an international mark registration application with designation of Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct a substantive examination of the application according to procedures applicable to applications for mark registration submitted directly to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. Within 12 months from the date of the International Office's notice, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conclude on the protectionability of the mark.
b/ For a mark which is eligible for protection under Vietnamese law, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to accept the protection of the internationally registered mark and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property and record it in the National Register of Marks within one month after the date of issuance of the decision. The scope (volume) of protection shall be certified according to the contents of the international registration application for the mark already recorded by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) and certified by the National Office of Industrial Property.
c/ For marks which are ineligible for protection or partially rejected, before the expiration of the above-mentioned 12-month time limit, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall send a written notice on the rejection of the application to the International Office for notification to the applicant, clearly stating the reason and contents of the rejection.
d) Within 3 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the decision to reject the application, the applicant may file a complaint about such decision. Procedures for filing and resolving complaints shall be the same as those applicable to mark registration applications submitted directly to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the results of the resolution of the complaint to the International Office and the applicant.
e) From the date of registration of international mark recognition, which takes effect in Vietnam, at the request of the mark owner, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall grant a certificate of protection of internationally registered mark in Vietnam, provided that the applicant pays the prescribed fee.
41.7 Extension of validity of international registration of a Vietnamese origin mark Within 6 months before the expiry date of validity of an international registration of a mark (20 years from the filing date of the international registration of a mark in respect of a country being a member of the Madrid Agreement, including a country concurrently being a member of the Madrid Protocol; or 10 years from the filing date of the international registration of a mark in respect of a country being a member of the Madrid Protocol but not concurrently being a member of the Madrid Agreement), the mark owner shall pay the fee for extension of validity of the international registration of the mark according to the notice of the International Office.

41.8 Application for conversion of a mark which has been invalidated by international registration of the mark
a) In case of international registration of a mark in Vietnam by its owner who is a country member of Madrid Protocol which has been invalidated according to Article 9 quinquies of Madrid Protocol, such owner may file an application for registration of a transformed mark using Form No. 07-DKCD specified in Appendix C of this Circular to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam for protection registration of such mark for part or all of goods or services included in the list of goods and services recorded in the international registration of the invalidated mark.

b) The application for registration of a transformed mark shall be accepted if it satisfies the following conditions: (i) It is filed within 03 months from the date on which the international registration of the corresponding mark becomes invalid;
(ii) Goods or services described in the mark registration application falling within the scope of the list of goods and services stated in the international registration for common marks;
(iii) The application for registration of a mark converted shall satisfy all requirements in terms of form and content as prescribed by Vietnamese law and the applicant fully pay fees and charges as prescribed.
c) The application for registration of a transformed mark shall be considered as having the priority date of the international application for registration of the mark or the priority date of the international application for registration of the mark (if the international application for registration of the mark is eligible for priority under an international treaty).
d) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the mark registration application for conversion as for ordinary marks.
42. Recording of well-known marks
42.1 Well-known marks shall be protected under Vietnamese law as prescribed in Article 75 of the Law on Intellectual Property and in accordance with Article 6bis of the Paris Convention for Industrial Property.

42.2 The right to a well-known mark shall be protected and belong to its owner without registration procedures. The mark owner may use the documents specified in Point 42.3 of this Circular to prove his ownership of the mark and to prove that the mark satisfies the conditions for being considered well-known.
42.3 Documents proving the ownership of a mark and its popularity include information on the scope, scale, level and continuity of use of the mark, including descriptions of the origin, history and period of continuous use of the mark; the number of countries where the mark has been registered or recognized as a popular mark; the list of goods and services bearing the mark; the territorial scope in which the mark is circulated, sales volume of products or services provided; the quantity of goods and services bearing the mark that have been produced and sold; the value of the asset of the mark, the price of transfer or licensing of the right to use, the investment capital contribution value of the mark; investments and costs for advertising and marketing of the mark, including participation in national and international exhibitions; infringement cases, disputes and decisions and judgments of courts or competent authorities; survey data on consumers' knowledge of the mark through purchase, use and advertising and marketing; rankings and assessments of the reputation of the mark by national and international organizations and mass media; prizes and medals obtained by the mark; assessment results of intellectual property assessment organizations.
42.4 In case a well-known mark is recognized according to civil proceedings or a decision of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, such well-known mark shall be recorded in the List of well-known marks kept at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 6. PROCEDURES FOR REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL GUIDELINES
43. Requirements on applications for registration of geographical indications
43.1 A geographical indication registration application shall satisfy the general requirements prescribed in Points 7 and 10.1 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this Point.
43.2 An application shall ensure consistency with Clause 1 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property and may register only one geographical indication for a product.
43.3 Application documents: Declaration form – made according to Form No. 05-CDDL specified in Appendix A of this Circular; Description of characteristics/quality/reputation of products; Map of geographical area corresponding to geographical indications (both must be made into 2 copies) and 10 samples showing the presentation of geographical indications, which shall be used with size not larger than 80mm x 80mm and not smaller than 20mm x 20mm (in case of geographical indications that are not words).
43.4 Requirements for product description of properties/quality/reputation
a) The description of properties/quality/reputation of a product must contain the following main information: (i) A list of particular properties/quality of the product bearing a geographical indication determined by geographical conditions, which is determined by perceptible, qualitative and quantitative physical, chemical or biological indicators which can be tested by technical means or by experts according to a testing method; and/or (ii) Reputation of the product bearing a geographical indication determined by geographical conditions, which is determined through widespread knowledge of such product among relevant consumers which can be verified; and (iii) Geographical conditions creating particular properties/quality and reputation of the product bearing a geographical indication, including unique elements of meteorology, hydrology, geology, topography, ecology and other natural conditions; unique elements of skills and techniques of producers, including traditional production processes of localities (which may include one, several or all stages from production of raw materials, processing of raw materials to creation of products and may also include the packaging stage if such stage affects properties/quality and reputation of the products), if such processes are factors creating and maintaining particular properties/quality and reputation of the product bearing a geographical indication, in which there are clear and detailed information which can be checked (if among the above-mentioned information there are secret information or technical secrets not yet disclosed or not widely known outside of localities, the applicant may refuse to provide details about such secrets or techniques if he/she does not commit to keep such information confidential at his/her request); and (iv) Organic relationship between particular properties/quality and reputation of the product bearing a geographical indication mentioned in Points 43.4.a(i) and (ii) with geographical conditions mentioned in Point 43.4.a(iii) above.
b) The description of properties/quality/reputation of the product must be accompanied by documents certifying that information about properties/quality/reputation is well-grounded and accurate (test results, research results, investigation results, etc.).
43.5 Requirements for maps of geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications Maps of geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications must fully present information to the extent where geographical areas meeting all natural conditions creating particular characteristics/quality and reputation of products can be accurately identified. Maps may be submitted together with documents describing geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications.
44. Format appraisal and announcement of geographical indication registration applications The format appraisal and announcement of geographical indication registration applications shall comply with the general procedures prescribed in Points 13 and 14 of this Circular.
45. Content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application
45.1 Procedures for content appraisal
The content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application shall be carried out according to the general procedures prescribed in Point 15 of this Circular and specific provisions in this Point.
45.2 Assessment of compatibility between subject matters stated in applications and geographical indication registration certificates Subject matters stated in applications for geographical indication registration shall be considered incompatible with geographical indication protection titles if such subject matters are not visible signs used to indicate products originating from specific regions, localities, territories or countries according to Clause 22 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
45.3 Assessment of geographical indications according to protection conditions
a) The subject matter stated in the application shall be accepted for registration and recorded in the National Register of Geographical Indications if fully satisfying the conditions specified in Article 79 of the Law on Intellectual Property and not falling into the cases specified in Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically, it must be fully demonstrated that all conditions are met;
(i) There is a geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication stated in the application;
(ii) Products originating from the above-mentioned geographical area;
(iii) Such products have particular characteristics/quality and/or reputation decided by geographical conditions of such geographical area according to Article 82 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) In the following cases, geographical indications mentioned in applications shall not be registered:
(i) Geographical indications which have become common names of goods in Vietnam;
(ii) A geographical indication of a foreign country which is not protected, has been terminated or no longer used in such country;
(iii) The geographical indication is identical or similar to a mark currently protected in Vietnam, where such use would cause confusion as to the origin of products;

(iv) The geographical indication misleads consumers as to the true geographical origin of products bearing such geographical indication.

The assessment of a geographical indication according to the protection conditions stated in Points 45.3.a and b above shall be carried out on the basis of information provided by the applicant and on the basis of the following minimum compulsory sources of information:
(i) Marks currently protected in Vietnam for products identical or similar to those bearing a geographical indication, with the protection starting date earlier than the date of filing the application for registration of such geographical indication, including marks protected under international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory;
(ii) Marks which are recognized by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as well-known marks.

The consideration of the mark owner's opinion shall comply with regulations on the consideration of opinions of third parties prescribed at Point 6 of this Circular. Section 1. PROCEDURES FOR REGISTRATION OF CONTRACTS FOR TRANSFER OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
47. Applications for registration of contracts on transfer of industrial property rights

47.1 An application for registration of a contract on transfer of industrial property rights must include the following documents:

c/ The written consent of co-owners to the transfer of the right to use industrial property objects, if the industrial property rights concerned are under joint ownership;
d) Power of attorney (if submitting application through representative);
e) Receipts of fee payment.

48. Procedures for processing registration applications
48.1 In case an application for registration of a contract on transfer of industrial property rights is free from the defects mentioned in Clause 3.3 of this Circular, the National Office of Industrial Property shall perform the following tasks:
a) Issue decisions on grant of certificates of registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights/transfer of the right to use industrial property objects;
b) (For an industrial property right assignment contract): To record in the title of protection for new owner; in case of partial assignment of the list of goods or services bearing protected marks, to grant a certificate of registration of new mark to the assignee and determine the scope of the list of goods/services in the original title of protection for the assigned part; or (for a contract on transfer of the right to use an industrial property object): to grant a certificate of registration of contract on transfer of the right to use the industrial property object to the applicant; to append the registration seal to 02 copies of the contract and hand over 01 copy to the applicant and keep 01 copy;
c) Record the transfer of industrial property rights in the National Register of Transfer of Industrial Property Rights;
d/ Publicizing the decision on grant of a certificate of registration of an industrial property right transfer contract in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within two months after its signing. 48.2 In case the dossier of registration of an industrial property right transfer contract contains errors as prescribed at Point 48.3 of this Circular, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Issue a notice of intended refusal to register the contract, clearly stating the deficiencies in the application and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of signing the notice for the applicant to correct the deficiencies or provide their opinion on the intended refusal to register the contract;
b) Issue a notice of refusal to register the contract if the applicant fails to correct errors or makes insufficient corrections, does not have any objection or provides unjustifiable objections regarding the intended refusal to register the contract within the prescribed time limit.
48.3 An application for registration of a contract on transfer of industrial property rights shall be deemed to contain errors in one of the following cases:
a) The declaration is invalid;
b) One of the documents in the list of required documents is missing;
c) The power of attorney is invalid;
d) The contract is not certified as valid;
e) The name and address of the transferring party in the contract that does not match the corresponding information in the protection title or in the contract is the basis for the emergence of the transfer right, power of attorney, declaration; the name and address of the transferred party in the contract that does not match the name and address stated in the power of attorney, declaration;
g) The contract is not signed by both parties (and bears their seals, if any);
h) The assignor is not the owner of the protection title;
i) The related industrial property subject matter is no longer within the term of protection or is in dispute;
k) The transfer contract does not contain all of the contents required in accordance with Article 140 or Clause 1 Article 144 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
l) The contract contains contents not in compliance with regulations on conditions restricting transfer of industrial property rights prescribed in Article 139 of the Law on Intellectual Property or contains clauses that unreasonably restrict the rights of the transferee of the right to use industrial property subject matter prescribed in Clause 2, Article 144 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
m/ There are grounds to affirm that the transfer of industrial property rights infringes upon the industrial property rights of a third party.
48.4 The time limit for processing an application for registration of a contract on transfer of industrial property rights shall be 2 months (excluding the time reserved for the applicant to correct errors).
49. Record the modification of contents, extension and premature termination of validity of contracts for transfer of the right to use industrial property objects
49.1 The modification of contents, extension or premature termination of validity of contracts for transfer of the right to use registered industrial property objects must be recorded at the National Office of Intellectual Property under the provisions of this Point.

49.2 Application for recording of amendments to contents, extension or termination of contracts
a/ A dossier requesting the recording of the modification of contents, extension or premature termination of a contract on transfer of the right to use an industrial property object must be made in writing and comprise the following documents:
(i) 02 copies of the written request for recording of amendments to contents, extension or termination of validity of contracts on transfer of the right to use industrial property objects, made according to Form No. 03-SDHQ mentioned in Appendix D of this Circular;
(ii) Original copies of certificates of registration of contracts for transfer of the right to use industrial property objects (in case of registration of amendments to contents or extension of validity of contracts);
(iii) Documents proving the modification of names and addresses of parties to the contract;
(iv) Agreements and documents recording specific articles to be amended or supplemented in contracts, including the premature extension or termination of contracts;
(v) Power of attorney (if submitting application through representative);
(vi) Documents proving payment of fees.
b) The application for contract extension must be submitted at least one month before the expiry date of the contract stated in the Certificate of registration of transfer of the right to use an industrial property object.
49.3 Within 01 month from the date of receipt of the application for recording of changes in content, extension or termination of a contract, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall review the application as follows:
a) In case of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to record amendments to contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract for transfer of rights to use industrial property subject matter; record amendments to contents and extension of validity of the contract for transfer of rights to use industrial property subject matter in the Certificate of registration of the contract for transfer of rights to use industrial property subject matter; record amendments to contents, extension of validity of the contract for transfer of rights to use industrial property subject matter in the National Register of Industrial Property Rights Transfer; and publish the decision to record amendments to contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract for transfer of rights to use industrial property subject matter in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 2 months from the date of signing the decision;
b) In case the application is flawed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize the modification of contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract for transfer of the right to use an industrial property object, clearly stating the flaws in the application and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice of intended refusal for the applicant to correct the flaws or provide opinions opposing the intended refusal to register the contract; or issue a notice of refusal to recognize the modification of contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract for transfer of the right to use an industrial property object in case the applicant fails to correct the flaws in the application, fails to adequately correct the flaws, does not provide opinions opposing the intended refusal to register the contract, or provides unjustified opinions opposing the intended refusal to register the contract within the prescribed time limit.
Section 2. PROCEDURES FOR RENUNCIATION OF LICENSING RIGHTS
50. Applications for issuance of decisions on compulsory licensing of inventions

50.1 Organizations and individuals capable, obliged or wishing to use inventions specified at Points a, b and c or subject to anti-competitive practices specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property may request competent persons specified in Clause 1, Article 147 of the Law on Intellectual Property to issue decisions on compulsory licensing of inventions under specific provisions in this Section.

50.2 An application for issuance of a decision on compulsory licensing of an invention must include the following documents:
a) 02 written requests for compulsory licensing of inventions, made according to Form No. 04-CGBB in Appendix D of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the request for issuance of a decision on compulsory licensing of an invention is well-grounded in accordance with law, specifically:
(i) In case of requesting issuance of a decision on compulsory licensing of an invention pursuant to Point a Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, documents proving that at the time of filing the application, the invention is actually used for public and non-commercial purposes, national defense and security, disease prevention and treatment, nutrition for the people or to meet other urgent social needs but the holder of the invention's exclusive right has not used the invention and such failure to use the invention will affect achievement of the above-mentioned objectives shall be included in the application.

 (ii) In case of requesting issuance of a decision to force the transfer of the invention use right on the basis of Point b, Clause 1, Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, documents proving that the holder of the invention's exclusive right has failed to perform the obligation to use the invention prescribed in Clause 1, Article 136 and Clause 5, Article 142 of the Law on Intellectual Property and at the time of filing the application, the time limit for filing an application for registration of the invention has expired 4 years after the date of filing the application for registration of the invention and the time limit for filing an application for registration of the invention has expired 3 years after the date of grant of the exclusive right to the invention;
(iii) In case of requesting issuance of a decision on compulsory licensing of an invention pursuant to Point c Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, documents proving that the person wishing to use the invention has failed to reach agreement with the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention on the conclusion of an invention license contract despite having made reasonable efforts to negotiate a satisfactory price and commercial terms, including the indication of the need to use the invention, the period during which negotiations were conducted, the specific price and commercial terms proposed by the person wishing to use the invention;
(iv) In case of requesting issuance of a decision on compulsory licensing of an invention pursuant to Point d Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, documents proving that the holder of the exclusive right to use such invention has committed acts deemed to be anti-competitive restrictions prohibited by the law on competition shall be included in the application.
(v) In case of requesting issuance of a decision on compulsory licensing of an invention in semiconductor technology, documents proving that such invention is used for public and non-commercial purposes must be included in the application, or documents proving that the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention has committed acts considered anti-competitive restrictions prohibited by the law on competition must be presented;
c) Power of attorney (if submitting application through representative);
d/ Documents proving payment of fees.
51. Procedures for processing applications for issuance of decisions on compulsory licensing of inventions

51.1 Applications shall be filed as follows:
a) Applications falling under Points b, c and d Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
b) Applications falling under the cases specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be submitted to ministries and ministerial agencies managing inventions.
51.2 Appraisal of dossiers
When there are dossiers falling into the cases specified in Point 51.1.b of this Circular, ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall designate an agency to receive and appraise such dossiers.
Within 2 months from the date of receipt of the application dossier, the National Office of Intellectual Property and the agencies appraising the dossiers of ministries and ministerial-level agencies (hereinafter referred to as the dossier-appraising agency) shall consider the dossier according to the following provisions:
a) In case of valid dossiers: Within 15 days after receiving a dossier, the dossier appraisal agency shall issue a notice requesting compulsory licensing of the invention to the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention and request such person to give his/her opinion within one month from the date of issuance of the notice; request related parties to re-negotiate in order to overcome their disagreement in order to sign an invention licensing contract if necessary; in case no agreement is reached among the parties and it is deemed unreasonable to refuse to sign the invention licensing contract, it shall report the results of the dossier examination and propose the Minister of Science and Technology or the minister or head of a ministerial-level agency to issue a decision on compulsory licensing of the invention.
If an application falls within the case specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 145 of the Intellectual Property Law and the invention is used for public and non-commercial purposes, the ministries or ministerial-level agencies may issue decisions to force the licensing of the invention without soliciting opinions from the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention and without soliciting negotiation among the parties.
b) In case of request for issuance of decision on transfer of invention use rights without justifiable grounds as prescribed in Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the agency appraising the application shall report on the results of examination of the application and propose the Minister of Science and Technology or the Minister or Head of a ministerial-level agency to issue a notice of intended refusal, clearly stating the reasons for refusal and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide its opinion on the intended refusal.
The time limit for applicants to correct errors in their applications or to provide opinions shall not be included in the time limit for application review.

c/ For dossiers falling into the cases specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the dossier-appraising agencies of the ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall send copies thereof for consultation to the Ministry of Science and Technology (via the National Office of Intellectual Property) before submitting them to the ministers or heads of the ministerial-level agencies for decision according to the provisions of Points a and b above. Within 15 days after receiving the dossiers, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall consider the dossiers and report thereon to the Minister of Science and Technology for sending written opinions requesting the ministers or heads of the ministerial-level agencies to issue decisions to force the transfer of the invention use rights or to issue notices of refusal.
51.3 Within 15 days after receiving the report on the results of examination of an application from the National Office of Intellectual Property, the Minister of Science and Technology shall consider issuing a decision to force the transfer of the right to use the invention or shall send the applicant a notice of refusal to force the transfer of the right to use the invention, clearly stating the reasons for refusal.
Within 15 days after receiving a written request from the Minister of Science and Technology, ministers or heads of ministerial-level agencies shall consider issuing a decision to force the licensing of an invention or shall send the applicant a notice of refusal to force the licensing of the invention, clearly stating the reasons therefor.
In case of disagreement with the proposal of the Minister of Science and Technology, Ministers or Heads of ministerial-level agencies shall issue written notices clearly stating the reasons therefor.
51.4 The decision on compulsory licensing of an invention shall be sent by the Minister or Head of a ministerial agency to the licensee, the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention and the National Office of Intellectual Property.
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall record the decision in the National Register of Transfers of Industrial Property rights within 01 month and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 02 months from the date of signing the decision.
52. Request for termination of the right to use an invention pursuant to a compulsory decision

52.1 The termination of the right to use an invention pursuant to a compulsory decision must be made by the Minister or Head of a ministerial-level agency who has issued the transfer decision.

52.2 A request for termination of the right to use an invention pursuant to a compulsory decision shall include the following documents:
a) Written request for termination of the invention use right under a compulsory decision;
b) Documents proving the grounds leading to the compulsory licensing of the invention no longer exist and are unlikely to reappear, and the termination of the use of the invention does not cause damage to the licensee under the compulsory licensing decision;
c) Power of attorney (if submitting application through representative);
d/ Documents proving payment of fees.
52.3 Procedures for receiving and processing requests for compulsory termination of invention use rights and issuing termination decisions shall be carried out in the same manner as procedures for receiving and processing applications for transfer of invention use rights under compulsory decisions prescribed in Clause 51 of this Circular.
Chapter III
                            INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REPRESENTATIVE
Section 1. ISSUANCE AND REVOCATION OF LICENSES FOR PROFESSIONAL REPRESENTATIVE SERVICES IN INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY
53. Issuing practicing certificates
53.1 Conditions for issuance of practicing certificates
Only persons who fully satisfy the conditions specified in Clause 2, Article 155 of the Intellectual Property Law may be granted industrial property representation service practice certificates. To be granted a certificate, such persons shall submit their application dossiers to the National Office of Intellectual Property under this Point.
53.2 Application for issuance of practicing certificates
An application for issuance of an industrial property representation service practice certificate shall include the following documents:
a) 02 copies of the application form for issuance of industrial property representation service practice certificate (practice certificate), made according to Form No. 01-CCHN specified in Appendix E of this Circular;
b) Copies of the notice of satisfactory performance in the industrial property representation examination organized by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) 02 3x4 photos;

d/ Documents proving payment of fees.
53.3 Processing of applications for issuance of practicing certificates:
In case of a valid application, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application, based on the review of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant the practicing certificate and issue the practicing certificate to the applicant; record the issuance of the practicing certificate in the National Register of Industrial Property Representatives; publish the issuance of the practicing certificate in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 2 months from the date of signing the decision; or notify deficiencies in the application and impose a 1-month time limit for the applicant to correct such deficiencies; or issue a notice of refusal to grant the practicing certificate, clearly stating the reasons for refusal, if the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or the application is invalid.
54. Revocation of practicing certificates
If the industrial property representative commits a violation and is sanctioned by revocation of his/her professional practice certificate, on the basis of the decision to revoke the industrial property representation service professional practice certificate issued by the competent state agency handling the administrative violation, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to revoke the professional practice certificate; delete the name of the industrial property representative from the list of industrial property representatives of the industrial property representation service organization; and publish the revocation of the professional practice certificate in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within 2 months after the date of signing the decision.
55. Re-grant of practicing certificates
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall reissue industrial property representation service practice certificates at the request of industrial property representatives in cases where the practice certificate is lost or damaged (torn, dirty, faded, etc.) to the extent that it cannot be used.
The procedures for re-granting practicing certificates shall be carried out similarly to the procedures for granting practicing certificates prescribed in Point 53.3 of this Circular.
Section 2. RECORDING, AMENDING AND REMOVING NAME OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REPRESENTATIVE SERVICES
56. Recognition of industrial property representation service organizations
56.1 In order to exercise the right of representation for applicants before the National Office of Intellectual Property, industrial property representation service providers must carry out procedures for recording in the National Register of Industrial Property Representatives as prescribed in this Point.
56.2 An application for registration of an industrial property representation service organization in the National Register of Industrial Property Representatives shall include the following documents:
a) 02 written requests for recognition of industrial property representation service organizations, made according to Form No. 03-YCGN in Appendix E of this Circular;
b) List of members who have obtained industrial property representation service practice certificates of their organizations, enclosed with copies of recruitment decisions/employment contracts for each member;
c) Copies of business registration certificates/operation registration certificates of the organization;
d) List of industrial property representation service charges of the organization;
e/ The written authorization of the head of the organization to represent one of the members in the list mentioned at Point 56.2.b above (if necessary);
g/ Documents on payment of fees.
56.3 Within 01 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall review the application for recognition of an industrial property representation service provider in a manner similar to the procedures for issuance of industrial property representation practice certificates prescribed in Point 53.3 of this Circular.
57. Recording of changes in information on industrial property representation service organizations 57.1 Industrial property representation service organizations may request the National Office of Intellectual Property to record changes related to their names and addresses in the lists of industrial property representatives.
57.2 An application for recording of changes in information on an industrial property representation service organization shall include the following documents:
a) 02 copies of the written request for recording of changes to information on industrial property representation service providers, made using Form No. 04-YCSĐ as prescribed in Appendix E of this Circular;
b) Copies of the amended business registration certificate/operation registration certificate of the industrial property representation service organization (in case of change of name or address of the organization);

c) Decision on employment or decision on termination of labor contract with industrial property representatives being members of organizations (in case of change of members in list of industrial property representatives of organizations);
d/ Documents proving payment of fees.
57.3 Procedures for processing applications for revision of information on industrial property representation service providers shall be carried out similarly to the procedures specified in Point 53.3 of this Circular.
58. Deletion of names of industrial property representation service organizations
In the cases specified in Clause 2 Article 156 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to delete the name of the industrial property representation service organization; record the deletion of the name of the industrial property representation service organization in the National Register of Industrial Property Representatives; notify the deletion of the name of the industrial property representation service organization to the agency competent to grant the certificate of business registration or certificate of operation registration of such organization; and publish the deletion in the Industrial Property Gazette within 02 months from the date of signing the decision.
Section 3. INSPECTION OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REPRESENTATION
59. Industrial property representation examination
The examination of industrial property representation practice (hereinafter referred to as "examination") under Article 28 of the Decree on Industrial Property shall be conducted in accordance with specific regulations below.
59.1 The Industrial property representation examination council:
The Industrial Property Representation Examination Council (hereinafter referred to as the “Examination Council”) is established by the Director of the National Office of Intellectual Property and has the task of issuing examination papers, organizing the examination and marking the examination papers according to the Regulation on examination of industrial property representation issued by the Director of the National Office of Intellectual Property (hereinafter referred to as the “Examination Regulation”).
The Examination Council shall be composed of the President of the Council, who is the Director General of the National Office of Intellectual Property, Vice President of the Council, Secretary and other members designated by the President from the list of experts who may participate in the Examination Council. The list of experts who may participate in the Examination Council shall include those who are knowledgeable about law and practical implementation of law on establishment of industrial property rights under the National Office of Intellectual Property and professional organizations on industrial property representation approved by the Minister of Science and Technology on the basis of proposals of such agencies and organizations. Decisions of the Council shall be passed on the principle of voting with two-thirds of the votes in favor of the members of the Council.
Remuneration for the work of members of the Council shall be paid from the revenue generated by fees for inspection of industrial property representation cases under the Inspection Regulation.
59.2 Registration for examination:
a) Only those who fully satisfy the conditions specified in Points from a to dd Clause 2 Article 155 of the Law on Intellectual Property may register for examination according to this Point.
b) The application for examination registration shall be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and includes the following documents: (i) 02 copies of the declaration for examination registration (made according to Form No. 05-KTNV specified in Appendix E of this Circular);
(ii) Copies of university degrees;
(iii) Documents proving that the applicant has received training in industrial property law or has experience in this field: A copy of the certificate of completion of a training course on industrial property law recognized by the Ministry of Science and Technology, or a written confirmation from the training institution where the applicant completed their undergraduate or postgraduate thesis on industrial property; or documents confirming that the applicant has been directly involved in the appraisal of industrial property registration applications at national or international industrial property offices for five consecutive years or more, or has been directly involved in the work of industrial property law as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 155 of the Law on Intellectual Property (including inspection, examination, supervision, adjudication, legal affairs, and legal consultancy on industrial property; scientific research with the title of researcher or lecturer on industrial property).
(iv) 02 3x4 photos;
(v) The receipt of payment for examination fee.
59.3 In case of a valid application dossier, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of his/her eligibility to take the examination and specify the time, location and schedule of the examination.

59.4 Examination contents and questions:

a) The inspection contents include:
(i) Vietnamese industrial property law and international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory; (ii) Practices of filing, submitting and pursuing applications for registration of industrial property;
(iii) Industrial property information search and exploitation;
(iv) Other contents if necessary.
b) The examination questions shall be enclosed with answers and score scales prepared by the Examination Council, approved by the President of the Examination Council and kept confidential until the start of the examination time.
59.5 Examination organization
a) The inspection shall be organized in sessions (not on a regular basis) when there are at least 5 persons registering for inspection.
b) The examination papers shall be marked by the Examination Council according to the approved answers and score scale.
c/ The examination results shall be notified by the National Office of Intellectual Property to examinees. Examinees may request the National Office of Intellectual Property to conduct a final review and the Examination Council shall conduct a final review under regulations of the director of the National Office of Intellectual Property.
The examination results shall be valid for 2 years for application for industrial property representation service practice certificates.
Chapter IV
                     PROTECTION OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY INFORMATION
60. Development and management of national database on industrial property

60.1 The National Office of Intellectual Property shall develop and manage a national database on industrial property in order to promptly, fully and accurately meet the needs of information on industrial property for research, application, development and other socio-economic activities.
60.2 National database on industrial property means information already published as mentioned below which is collected, selected and systematically compiled for search purposes:
a/ Applications for registration of industrial property;
b/ The granted protection titles and industrial property rights which have been recognized for protection in Vietnam;
c) Patents granted by countries/regions with the most advanced science and technology;
d/ Some other industrial property right protection titles depend on the use purpose of information.
61. Access to and exploitation of industrial property information in national databases 61.1. All organizations and individuals shall have the right to access and exploit information in national databases on industrial property after registering their use of such information with the National Office of Intellectual Property.
61.2 Persons wishing to access and exploit industrial property information ("information users") shall have to search for such information themselves in the national database.
Users may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to search information in the national database and shall pay search fees as prescribed.
62. Industrial property information lookup services
62.1 When requesting the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to look up or search for information on industrial property, users shall make an application form for lookup (using Form No. 01-YCTC, O2-YCTC and 03-YCTC in Annex F of this Circular) which must clearly state the purpose and scope of the lookup (field, type of documents containing information, time, country or region to be looked up, etc.).
62.2 Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request form for lookup, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall respond to the requester.
In case of a valid request for lookup (with a valid request form as prescribed in Point 62.1 of this Circular and payment of lookup fee), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall respond by sending a "lookup report" to the user, clearly stating the lookup or search results as requested by the user.

In case of an invalid request for lookup (invalid application form, unclear purpose or scope of lookup, failure to pay lookup fee, etc.), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify refusal and provide reasons.
62.3 A search report shall contain only found information and indications of sources of such information. If no information is found in the requested sources, this must be clearly stated in the search report.
A search report must not contain any comments or assessments about the found information. 62.4 The report on a search must clearly state the full name of the person who conducts the search and shall be responsible for the search results.
63. Assurance of industrial property information in localities 63.1. Depending on their conditions and capabilities, the State administrative bodies for industrial property (the provincial/municipal Services of Science and Technology) may set up and manage industrial property databases to ensure industrial property information for research, application, development of production, business activities and protection of industrial property rights in localities. 63.2. The State administrative bodies for industrial property in the provinces and centrally run cities shall have the responsibility and right to conduct activities to assure industrial property information according to this Circular.
64. Issuing copies of documents
64.1 All relevant organizations and individuals have the right to request the National Office of Intellectual Property to issue copies of documents issued or kept by the National Office of Intellectual Property, including certification of the copy from the original or the archive. Only the applicant has the right to request the issuance of copies of documents related to the application that has not yet been published. The requester for a copy must pay a fee as prescribed.
64.2 A request for issuance of copies of documents shall include:
a) Written request for issuance of copies of documents, including 2 copies;
b) Original copies of documents issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (in case documents are not stored at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam);
c/ Charge receipts.
64.3 Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of a request for issuance of copies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue copies or notify the refusal and provide an explanation.
Chapter V
                                   IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
65. Regulations on carrying out procedures for industrial property
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall promulgate regulations on procedures for industrial property in accordance with the Decree on Industrial Property and this Circular.
66. Transitional provisions
66.1 An invention registration application as stipulated in this Circular shall be understood as including both an application for issuance of an invention patent and an application for issuance of a utility solution patent in accordance with the previous regulations.
66.2 For internationally registered marks already recognized for protection in Vietnam under previous regulations, the WIPO Official Gazette or the Industrial Property Official Gazette of the National Office of Intellectual Property announcing such internationally registered marks shall be valid as basis for certification of rights to such marks.

66.3 Before new regulations on fees and charges are promulgated, the fee and charge rates prescribed in this Circular shall apply according to the Circular No. 132/2004/TT-BTC of December 30, 2004 of the Ministry of Finance guiding the collection, payment, management and use of industrial property fees and charges.
66.4 The documents mentioned above shall be used until the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam satisfies technical requirements and issues an official notice on the application of these documents as prescribed in this Circular.
67. Effect
67.1 This Circular replaces the following Circulars:
a) The Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment's Circular No. 3055/TT-SHCN of December 31, 1996 guiding the implementation of the provisions on procedures for establishing industrial property rights and a number of other procedures in the Government's Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996 detailing industrial property;

b) The Ministry of Science and Technology's Circular No. 29/2003/TT-BKHCN of November 5, 2003 guiding the procedures for establishing industrial property rights to industrial designs;
c) The Ministry of Science and Technology's Circular No. 30/2003/TT-BKHCN of November 5, 2003 guiding the procedures for establishing industrial property rights to inventions/utility solutions.
67.2 This Circular takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO.
