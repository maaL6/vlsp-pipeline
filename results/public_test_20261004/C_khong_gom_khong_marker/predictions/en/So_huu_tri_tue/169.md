                                                   CIRCULAR

   THE MINISTRY OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND ENVIRONMENT’S SERIES NO. 3055-TT/SHCN OF DECEMBER 31, 1996 ON GUIDING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROVISIONS ON PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS AND A NUMBER OF OTHER PROCEDURES IN THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 63-CP OF OCTOBER 24, 1996, DETAILING INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996 detailing industrial property; The Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment hereby promulgates this Circular in order to specify and guide the procedures for making, filing and examining applications for protection titles, the procedures for approving and registering industrial property right transfer contracts, the procedures for considering applications for non-voluntary licenses, the procedures for modifying and extending the validity of protection titles, the procedures for processing international applications for invention and utility solutions under the PCT Convention and international applications for trademarks under the Madrid Agreement and the procedures for granting industrial property representation permits.

  Chapter 1.

                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. Terms

  1.1. Terms used in this Circular are construed as follows:

  - "Decree" is used to refer to Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996 of the Government detailing industrial property;

  - "Application" is used to refer to an application for the title of protection;

  - "Invention application", "Utility solution application", "Industrial design application", "Trademark application", and "Origin appellation certificate application" shall be used respectively to refer to applications for the grant of an invention patent, utility solution patent, industrial design patent, trademark registration certificate, or origin appellation use right certificate;

  - "International application" means an international application for registration of an invention or utility solution filed under the PCT Convention;

  - "International registration application" means an application for international registration of a trademark filed under the Madrid Agreement on International Registration of Marks;

  - "Trademark" is used to refer to "Trade Mark" as defined in Article 2 of the Decree;

  - "Subject of title of document" means an individual, juridical person or organization that has made, promulgated or signed to recognize the validity of the document.

  1.2. Other terms shall be construed according to the Decree.

  2. Document certification

  2.1. Authentication of signatures

  In the course of carrying out procedures related to the establishment, maintenance, extension, enforcement and transfer of industrial property rights specified in this Circular, signatures of persons appearing on papers and documents related to transactions with competent agencies must be certified as being those of the persons appearing on such papers and documents, and where the signatories are representatives of the persons appearing on such papers and documents, it must be certified that the signatories are persons having the authority to represent the persons appearing on such papers and documents, as follows:

  (i) For a holder of a lawful seal, the authentication of signature shall be carried out by affixing the holder's seal on the signature;

  (ii) For Vietnamese entities having no lawful seal, the signature confirmation must be carried out at a State Notary Public or at the local authority where the entity resides or is headquartered;

  (iii) For foreign entities having no lawful seal, the signature confirmation must be carried out at a notary office or a competent authority.

  2.2. Certification of copies

  a/ All documents which are copies made by any means must be certified as being copied from the originals according to the provisions in Item b below before they can be used as official documents in the course of carrying out related procedures at the competent agencies.

  b/ Documents are recognized as copies from originals if they bear certification of copying by one of the following agencies: (i) Notary Public office, (ii) People's Committee or competent agency, (iii) State agency or social organization which made the original documents; and, if the copy has many pages, each page must be certified or the pages must be joined together.

  2.3. Translation certification

  a/ All Vietnamese translations of documents must be certified as being made verbatim from the original texts according to the following Clause b) before they can be used as official documents in the course of carrying out related industrial property procedures before competent agencies.

  b/ The certification of a translation may be effected in one of the following modes: (i) notarization; (ii) certification by the original document holder himself/herself; (iii) certification by all parties to the contract or agreement (if the original document is a contract or agreement); (iv) recognition by the agency competent to use such translation in the course of carrying out related procedures.

  3. Persons acting as owners shall carry out procedures related to industrial property

  3.1. Only persons defined in Points 3.2 and 3.3 below may act as applicants for protection titles, supplement or modify documents of applications; receive and answer opinions of the National Office of Industrial Property related to applications; decide to continue or terminate the process of protection request; receive protection titles; maintain, modify and extend validity of protection titles as well as other procedures on industrial property before the National Office of Industrial Property and competent agencies.

  The National Office of Industrial Property shall be permitted to conduct transactions with the above-mentioned persons and such transactions shall be considered official ones with the subjects. 3.2. For subjects who have the right to directly carry out the filing of Applications and related procedures prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 a/ Article 15 of the Decree, the following persons shall be permitted to act on behalf of the subjects to carry out the jobs mentioned in Point 3.1 above:

  (i) The individual or their legal representative (if the entity is an individual);

  (ii) The legal representative of the entity; an individual being a member of the entity authorized by the legal representative of the entity to represent; the head of the representative office or branch of the entity authorized by the legal representative of the entity to represent (if the entity is a juridical person or another entity);

  (iii) Heads of representative offices in Vietnam of foreign entities authorized by such entities; persons represented under a proxy agreement of enterprises established in Vietnam with 100% investment capital of foreign entities authorized by such entities;

  (iv) The person who satisfies one of the conditions stated in paragraphs (i), (ii) and (iii) above is one of the individuals or one of the juridical persons or other subjects - if the subject includes many individuals, juridical persons or other subjects and if such person is authorized by all individuals, juridical persons or other subjects to act as representative.

  3.3. For entities which are permitted to file applications and carry out related procedures through industrial property representation service organizations defined in Clause 3.b, Article 15 of the Decree as well as for all other entities carrying out the above-mentioned procedures through industrial property representation service organizations, only persons who have been granted industrial property representative cards by industrial property representation service organizations shall be permitted to carry out the jobs mentioned in Point 3.1 above.

  4. Authorization for carrying out procedures related to industrial property

  4.1. All authorization for industrial property procedures must be made in writing (power of attorney) and contain the following contents:

  (i) Full name and address of the Authorized Party; (ii) Full name and address of the Authorized Party; (iii) Scope of authorization (tasks performed by the Authorized Party on behalf of the Authorizing Party); (iv) Authorization period; (v) Place and date of making of the Authorization Letter; (vi) Signature of the Authorizing Party (certified in accordance with regulations on signature certification).

  4.2. The authorized party must be an individual or organization permitted to carry out procedures for industrial property as prescribed in Points 3.2 (ii), (iii), (iv) and 3.3 of this Circular.

  4.3. Any change in the scope of authorization and termination of authorization before the prescribed time must be notified in writing to the National Office of Industrial Property.

  4.4. If the scope of authorization of a power of attorney includes several independent procedures, the authorized party may submit copies of the power of attorney, provided that the original copy of the power of attorney has been submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property and the number and date of submission of the dossier containing the original copy of the power of attorney are indicated.

  Chapter II

                                                APPLICATIONS AND PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS

  5. General requirements for Applications

  5.1. The application must ensure the uniformity prescribed in Clause 2, Article 11 of the Decree and meet the general requirements on form prescribed at Point 5.2 below.

  5.2. The application must meet the general requirements in terms of form as follows:

  (i) Each Application may request the grant of only one Title of protection which must be appropriate to the subject matter of industrial property stated in the Application;

  (ii) All documents of the Application must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents which may be presented in other languages as prescribed in Point 5.3 below;

  (iii) All documents of the Application shall be presented in a vertical manner on one sheet of paper of size A4 (210mm x 297mm), with margins of 20mm on each side, except for documents added to the Application for necessary purposes of supplementation or illustration which are not intended to be included in the Application and may be presented differently;

  (iv) If documents are required to be made according to a set form, such forms must be used by filling in appropriate blanks;

  (v) Each document must be made in sufficient quantity as required; if a document consists of more than one page, the page number in Arabic numerals shall be written in the middle of each page.

  (vi) Documents must be typed or printed with an ink of which the letters are not erased, and which is clear, clean, and unaltered.

  5.3. The following documents may be made in other languages than Vietnamese but must be translated into Vietnamese:

  (i) Power of attorney (if any);

  (ii) Documents certifying the lawful right to file an application if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application, including transfer of the submitted application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.);

  (iii) The priority right assignment document (if the Application requests the priority right and such right is inherited from another person);

  (iv) Relevant documents proving the grounds for priority right (first submission, exhibition certificate, etc.);

  (v) Original documents or copies thereof, which the applicant has included in the Application to support the Application.

  6. Requirements for invention applications/utility solutions

  Apart from general requirements specified in Point 5 of this Circular, an invention/utility solution must satisfy requirements specified in this Point.

  6.1. An application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application form for issuance of an invention/utility solution patent, made according to a form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) Description of an invention/utility solution (below referred to as description), comprising 3 copies;

  (iii) The protection request, including 3 copies;

  (iv) Drawings, schematics, calculation sheets, etc. (if necessary) to further clarify the nature of the technical solutions stated in the Description, including 3 copies;

  (v) Summary of invention/utility solution, comprising 3 copies;

  (vi) Documents certifying the lawful right to file an application if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate or agreement on assignment of the right to file an application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.), 1 copy;

  (vii) A power of attorney (if necessary), one copy;

  (viii) A copy of the first application or document certifying the exhibition if the application requests priority rights under an international treaty, one copy;

  (ix) The fee and charge receipts for filing the application and the charge for announcement of the application, 1 copy each.

  6.2. The documents mentioned in Point 6.1 above must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of filing the Application:

  (i) Vietnamese versions of documents 6.1(ii), 6.1(iii) and 6.1(v) if such documents are available in English/French/Russian versions in the Application;

  (ii) Original copies of documents 6.1 (vii) if the Application has a copy;

  (iii) Document No. 6.1 (viii), including the Vietnamese version.

  6.3. The description must fully disclose the nature of the technical solution to be protected. The description must contain all information sufficient to enable any person with average qualifications in the relevant technical field to implement such solution.

  The description must clarify the novelty, creativity (if the subject matter is an invention) and applicability of the technical solution to be protected.

  The description must include the following contents:

  (i) International classification indicators (according to the Strasbourg Agreement), (ii) Name of technical solution, (iii) Field in which the technical solution is used or related, (iv) Technical status in the above-mentioned field at the time of application (known technical solutions), (v) Nature of the technical solution, (vi) Brief description of accompanying drawings (if any), (vii) Example of implementation of the technical solution, (viii) Possible benefits (effectiveness of the technical solution).

  6.4. Protection request for determination of scope (volume) of protection of an invention/utility solution. The protection request must be presented in brief and clearly in conformity with the description and drawings, which must clarify new signs of the technical solution to be protected.

  6.5. Summary of invention/utility solution for briefly disclosing nature of invention/utility solution. The summary must disclose principal contents of technical solutions for information purpose.

  6.6. Requirements on form and contents of Description, Drawings, Protection Request, Summary of Invention/Utility Solution and other documents of Application for invention/utility solution shall be stipulated by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  7. Requirements for applications for industrial designs

  Apart from general requirements prescribed in Point 5 of this Circular, an Industrial Design Application shall satisfy requirements prescribed in this Point.

  7.1. An application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application form for issuance of a patent for an industrial design, made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) Description of industrial designs, including 3 copies;

  (iii) The set of photos or drawings of industrial designs, including 6 sets; (iv) Documents certifying the lawful submission right if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance rights; certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application; assignment contract or labor contract), including 1 copy;

  (v) Documents certifying the ownership of marks if industrial designs contain marks, 1 copy;

  (vi) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  (vii) One copy of the first application or documents certifying the display at the exhibition if the application requests priority rights under an international treaty, including 1 copy;

  (viii) The fee and charge receipts for filing the application and announcement of the application, 1 copy each.

  7.2. The documents mentioned in Point 7.1 above must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of submission of the Application:

  (i) Vietnamese version of document 7.1(ii), if the application already contains an English/French/Russian version of such document;

  (ii) Document No. 7.1(v);

  (iii) The original of document 7.1 (vi), if a copy is available in the Application;

  (iv) Document No. 7.1 (vii), including its Vietnamese translation.

  7.3. The description of an industrial design must fully and clearly present the nature of such industrial design and must be consistent with the set of photos or drawings, including the following contents:

  (i) Name of industrial design, (ii) International classification index of industrial designs (according to the Locarno Agreement), (iii) Field of use of products bearing the industrial design, (iv) Known similar industrial designs, (v) List of photos or drawings, (vi) Nature of the industrial design, in which the basic design characteristics of the industrial design requested for protection must be clearly stated, which are different from known similar industrial designs.

  7.4. The set of photos or drawings must fully express the nature of the industrial design as described in order to determine the scope (volume) of protection of such industrial design.

  The photos or drawings must be clear and sharp, and must not mix with other products of the industrial design protected.

  All photos/drawings must be taken under the same scale. Size of each photo shall not be smaller than 90 mm x 120 mm and not larger than 210 mm x 297 mm.

  7.5. Requirements for the Description and the set of photos/drawings of industrial designs shall be prescribed by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  8. Requirements for mark applications

  Apart from the general requirements prescribed in Point 5 of this Circular, a mark application shall satisfy the requirements prescribed in this Point.

  8.1 An application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application form for grant of a certificate of registered mark enclosed with the sample mark made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) Regulations on use of a mark if the protected mark is a collective mark, including one copy;

  (iii) Mark sample, 15 samples;

  (iv) Copies of documents certifying lawful business rights (business licenses or certificates of business registration, etc.), including 1 copy;

  (v) Documents certifying the lawful right to file an application, if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application, including the submitted application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.), 1 copy;

  (vi) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  (vii) One copy of the first application or certificate of exhibition if the application requests priority right under an international treaty, including 1 copy;

  (viii) Documents certifying the origin, prizes or medals, if the mark contains such information, one copy;

  (ix) The license of a competent authority, if the mark has the symbols, name, etc. prescribed in point g clause 2 Article 6 of the Decree, shall be one copy;

  (x) The fee receipt for filing the application, 1 copy.

  8.2. The above documents must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of submission of the Application:

  (i) Original copy of document 8.1 (vi), if the Application has a copy;

  (ii) Document No. 8.1 (vii), including its Vietnamese translation.

  8.3. The description of a mark in an application form must clarify the distinguishability of such mark, specifying each constituent element of the mark and its general meaning. If the mark contains words other than Vietnamese, their pronunciation (transcribed into Vietnamese) must be clearly indicated; if such words have meanings, their Vietnamese meanings must be provided.

  If the words or phrases subject to protection are depicted in graphic form as distinctive elements of a mark, the graphic form of such words or phrases must be described.

  If a mark contains Arabic numerals other than Arabic numerals or Roman numerals, it must be translated into Arabic numerals.

  If a mark consists of several distinct parts used simultaneously on a product, the position of each part of such mark on the product or its packaging must be clearly indicated.

  8.4. The list of products and services bearing marks in the declaration must be appropriate or of the same type as those permitted for business as stated in the business license or certificate of registration of business and must be grouped according to the International Classification of Products and Services (according to the Nixon Agreement).

  8.5. The mark samples affixed in the declaration and other mark samples must be clearly presented with size not exceeding 80 mm x 80 mm and the distance between two nearest points not smaller than 15 mm.

  If a color protection request is made, the mark sample must be presented in the exact colors protected.

  If the protection of colors is not requested, all marks must be presented in black and white.

  9. Requirements for origin appellations.

  Apart from general requirements specified in Point 5 of this Circular, origin appellations must satisfy requirements prescribed in this Point.

  9.1. An application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application for a certificate of rights to use goods origin appellations, made according to a form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) Copies of documents certifying lawful business rights (business licenses, business registration certificates, etc.), including 1 copy;

  (iii) One set of presentation on quality characteristics of products bearing appellations of origin with certification by competent state agencies;

  (iv) The certification of a competent authority that the product manufactured or commercially traded by the applicant has particular characteristics and quality and is manufactured in the territory corresponding to the origin of such goods (consistent with the description in document (iii)), 1 copy;

  (v) A copy of the title of protection of appellations of origin of goods granted by the country of origin or documents of the country of origin certifying the rights of the applicant to use the appellation of origin of goods currently protected in the country of origin (if the appellation of origin of goods is of foreign origin), 1 copy;

  (vi) Description map of the territory corresponding to the origin of goods, including location of production and business of the applicant, 1 copy;

  (vii) A power of attorney (if any), 1 copy;

  (viii) The fee receipt for filing the application, 1 copy.

  If the applicant requests only the grant of a certificate of rights to use an appellation of origin of goods already registered, documents (iii) and (vi) are not required in the application. If the appellation of origin of goods is of foreign origin, documents (ii), (iii) and (vi) are not required in the application.

  9.2. Documents to be submitted simultaneously. The original of document 9.1 (vii) may be submitted within 3 months from the date of application if a copy is included in the Application.

  9.3. Competent agencies capable of certifying the particular characteristics of products bearing appellations of origin and certifying that products manufactured by applicants possess such particular characteristics are central or local agencies managing goods quality with regard to the goods appellations of origin.

  10. Submitting applications

  An application may be filed at the National Office of Industrial Property or at any other places designated by the National Office of Industrial Property to receive applications. Applications may also be sent by post under security to the above-mentioned places designated to receive applications.

  11. Receipt of Applications

  11.1. Upon receiving an Application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall have to perform the following tasks:

  (i) Re-examine the list of documents stated in the Declaration;

  (ii) Affixing the seal to certify the date of receipt of the Application and Declaration;

  (iii) To record differences between the list of documents stated in the Declaration and the number of actual documents in the Application;

  (iv) Preliminary examination of the Application for conclusion on whether or not the Application is received according to Point 11.2 below;

  (v) Send to the applicant a declaration sealed with the date of receipt of the application, the number of the application and stating the results of the examination of the list of documents, with the signature and seal of the official receiving the application; (the above-mentioned declaration replaces the receipt of the application).

  11.2. The National Office of Industrial Property shall not receive an Application if it finds that the Application contains one of the following defects:

  (i) The application lacks one of the following mandatory documents: Declaration, which must include the trademark sample and list of products or services (for mark applications), goods origin appellation and type (for origin appellation applications), Description of invention/utility solution, Protection request (for invention/utility solutions), Description of industrial design and set of photos and drawings of industrial designs (for industrial design applications), Authorization letter (for applications requiring an authorization letter), fee payment document;

  (ii) The form of protection (type of title of protection requested for grant) is not appropriate to the subject matter of industrial property stated in the Application;

  (iii) The declaration is not signed or has been erased or seriously altered.

  11.3. In case the Application is not received, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the Applicant of the reason for non-receipt. For an Application sent by post, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify in writing within 15 days after the date on which the Application arrives; The National Office of Industrial Property is not required to return to the Applicant documents of the Application but must refund the fee already paid according to the Application not received after having deducted expenses for sending and returning such money.

  12. Processing of received applications

  Upon receipt, the Application shall be processed as follows:

  A set of documents, including necessary documents, shall be separated to preserve the original status of the Application (referred to as the "Application dossier");

  The remaining documents shall be compiled into a set of documents for testing in accordance with this Circular.

  13. Examination of form

  13.1. After being processed according to Point 12 of this Circular, the Application shall be examined for form according to the provisions of this Point.

  13.2. An application shall be considered invalid if it contains one of the following defects:

  (i) The application is made in a language other than Vietnamese, except for the case specified in Point 5.3 of this Circular;

  (ii) The declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author (for inventions, utility solutions or industrial designs), the applicant, the applicant’s failure to sign or the signature is not certified, or the information about the representative has been erased.

  (iii) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the right to file the application;

  (iv) The application is submitted in contravention of Article 15 of the Decree;

  (v) Description, Summary, Request for protection of inventions, utility solutions, Description of industrial designs in English/French/Russian which the applicant fails to supplement with Vietnamese version within the time limit prescribed in Points 6.2 and 7.2 of this Circular;

  (vi) The power of attorney is only a copy without adding the original within the time limit specified in Points 6.2, 7.2, 8.2 and 9.2 of this Circular;

  (vii) The application has deficiencies specified in Point 13.3 below which affect its validity and although it has been requested to be corrected by the National Office of Industrial Property, the applicant fails to correct them or the correction is unsatisfactory;

  (viii) The subject matters stated in the application are those not protected by the State as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 4, Clause 3 Article 5, Clause 2 Article 6 and Clause 2 Article 7 of the Decree;

  13.3. Handling of errors in the Application during the stage of examination of form

  If an Application contains the following defects, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify such to the applicant and within two (2) months from the date of notification, the applicant must correct such defects.

  (i) Not having enough of one of the mandatory documents;

  (ii) The application does not meet the uniformity requirement;

  (iii) The application does not meet the requirements regarding the format of presentation;

  (iv) The mark application does not clearly state the type of mark registered, lacks a description of the mark, or contains a list of products that is not grouped or grouped incorrectly;

  (v) Information about the applicant in documents is inconsistent or erased;

  (vi) The fee for filing the application has not been fully paid.

  13.4. The applicant may take the initiative in modifying and supplementing documents in the Application but not to expand the scope (volume) of protection and not to change the nature of the industrial property object stated in the Application and must pay a fee according to regulations. If the modification expands the scope (volume) of protection or changes the nature of the object, the applicant shall have to file a new Application and all procedures shall be carried out again from the beginning.

  13.5. Determination of the date of filing a valid application

  The date of submission of a valid application shall be determined as follows:

  (i) For Applications without the faults prescribed in Point 13.2 above, the date of filing shall be the date on which the Application is delivered to the National Office of Industrial Property as indicated in the receipt seal on the Declaration;

  (ii) For applications with errors specified at Point 13.3 above and errors already corrected within the respective time limit, the date of filing is the date on which the application reaches the National Office of Industrial Property as indicated in the receipt seal. If the errors are corrected later than such time limit, the date of filing shall be the date on which the errors are completely corrected so that the application becomes valid.

  13.6. Determination of priority date

  The priority date of an Application shall be determined as follows:

  If the Application does not request priority right, the priority date is the date of filing a valid application;

  If the application requests priority right, the priority date is the date stated in such application and approved by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  13.7. Results of examination of the form of application shall be notified by the National Office of Industrial Property to the applicant as follows:

  (i) If the Application is considered valid, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send to the Applicant a Notice of acceptance of the Application, clearly stating the name and address of the Applicant; the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the Application is filed through such organization); the name of the subject matter stated in the Application, the date of filing the valid Application, the priority date; the serial number of the Application; the remaining deficiencies which the Applicant must continue to correct and the time limit for correcting such deficiencies. Past this time limit, if the Applicant fails to correct the deficiencies, the Application will not be further considered;

  (ii) If the Application is deemed invalid, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send to the Applicant a Notice of refusal to accept the Application, which must clearly state the name and address of the Applicant, the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the Application was filed through such organization); the date the Application was filed with the National Office of Industrial Property, the subject matter stated in the Application; and the reason for refusal to accept the Application (the reason why the Application was deemed invalid).

  (iii) If the Application still has defects as stated in Point 13.3 above, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send to the Applicant a Notice of results of examination of the form of the Application, clearly stating the name and address of the Applicant, the name of the industrial property representation organization (if the Application is filed through such organization), the date the Application was submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property; the subject matter stated in the Application, the defects to be corrected and a time limit for the Applicant to correct the defects.

  13.8. The time limit for examination of the form shall be 3 months from the date the Application is received by the National Office of Industrial Property as indicated on the receipt seal; for applications with late documents as stipulated in Points 6.2, 7.2, 8.2 and 9.2, the time limit for examination of the form shall be 3 months from the date the supplementary documents are completed. Before the end of the above-mentioned time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property must complete the examination of the form and notify the applicant according to the provisions of Point 13.7 above.

  14. Public disclosure of valid applications

  14.1. All inventions, utility solutions and industrial designs which have been accepted as valid shall be published by the National Office of Industrial Property in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within the following time limits:

  a/ An invention or utility solution shall be published in the 19th month from the date of priority, except for cases specified at Points b, c and d Below;

  b/ In case of early disclosure request, the Application shall be disclosed within one month from the date the National Office of Industrial Property receives such request or within a later time stated in the request;

  c/ For an invention or utility solution application, if there is a written request for examination of contents submitted before the date this application is accepted as valid, it shall be published within one month after the date it is accepted as valid;

  d/ For an invention or utility solution application, if there is a written request for content examination after the date of acceptance of such application as valid but before the expiration of the 18-month time limit from the priority date, the application shall be published within one month after the National Office of Industrial Property receives the request for content examination;

  e/ International Applications and Industrial Designs shall be published in the second month counting from the date of acceptance of the valid Application, except for cases specified at Point b above.

  14.2. Information related to a valid Application published in the Official Gazette includes: all information about the valid Application stated in the Notification of acceptance of the application except for information on errors that must be corrected; an abstract of the invention or utility solution enclosed with drawings if necessary; one or more drawings or photos of the industrial design.

  14.3. Everyone may have access to more detailed information on the nature of the subject matter stated in the Application published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property or request the National Office of Industrial Property to provide such information and shall pay a fee for providing information as prescribed.

  15. Request for examination of contents of inventions and utility solutions.

  15.1. Within 42 months from the priority date of an invention application or 36 months from the priority date of a utility solution application, the applicant or any third party may request the National Office of Industrial Property to conduct examination of the contents of such invention or utility solution.

  The applicant for examination of contents of an invention or utility solution shall pay a fee as prescribed.

  15.2. The applicant shall be requested to test the contents of the invention or utility solution after the Application is published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property within one month from the date of receipt of a written request for testing and shall be notified of such request to the applicant.

  Request for examination of contents of inventions and utility solutions filed before the disclosure of Applications shall be disclosed together with the corresponding Applications according to Point 14.1c) and d) of this Circular.

  15.3. Except for cases where the applicant requests content testing as stated in the declaration, a request for content testing of an invention or utility solution must be made in writing, clearly stating the name and address of the requester; the application number and submission date of the invention or utility solution requiring content testing; the name and address of the applicant, the name of the corresponding invention or utility solution, and must be accompanied by a receipt or document confirming the payment of the fee for requesting content testing.

  16. Examination of contents of the Application

  16.1. The contents of an Application shall be examined by the National Office of Industrial Property under the provisions of this Point for:

  (i) All applications for marks, industrial designs and appellations of origin which have been accepted as valid and the applicants have paid the fee for content examination according to regulations; and (ii) All international applications; and (iii) All inventions, utility solutions, including international applications, which have been accepted as valid and the content examination requests have been filed with the National Office of Industrial Property within the time limit specified in Clause 15 of this Circular.

  16.2. The purpose of examination of contents of an Application is to assess the protection capacity of the subject matter stated in the Application according to the protection criteria and determine the scope (volume) of protection corresponding thereto.

  16.3. Within the time limit for examination of contents of an Application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send a notice of results of examination of contents to the applicant and the person requesting examination of contents as follows:

  a/ If the industrial property object fails to meet the protection criteria, the notice of examination results must clearly state the reasons for the intended refusal to grant the title of protection and fix a time limit of two months from the date of notification for the applicant to give his/her opinion;

  b/ If the industrial property object satisfies the protection criteria but the scope (volume) of protection has to be narrowed or the Application contains deficiencies, the Notice on the results of the content examination must clearly state this and fix a time limit of two months from the date of notification for the applicant to give his/her opinions or correct the deficiencies;

  c/ If the subject matter satisfies the protection criteria, even in the case stated at Point b above, the notice of test results shall require the applicant to pay the fee for publication of the title of protection, the registration fee and the title of protection and the fee for maintenance of validity for the first year (for inventions and utility solutions).

  16.4. During the time limit for examination of contents, the applicant may take initiative in amending and supplementing documents in the Application and must pay a fee according to regulations.

  The National Office of Industrial Property may request the applicant within a given time limit to amend and supplement documents. If the applicant fails to make such amendment and supplementation at the request of the National Office of Industrial Property without plausible reason, the application shall be considered withdrawn.

  The amendments shall not alter the nature of the subject matter and shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection stated in the Application.

  16.5. Time limit for examination of contents of an Application is (i) 18 months for an invention application or 9 months for a utility solution application from the date of receipt of a request for examination of contents, if such request is filed after the date of publication of the Application or from the date of publication of the Application if the request for examination of contents is filed before the date of publication of the Application; (ii) 9 months for an industrial design application or a trademark application from the date of signing of a notice of acceptance of the valid application; (iii) 6 months for an application for appellation of origin from the date of signing of a notice of acceptance of the valid application.

  If during the process of examining the contents of the Application, the Applicant takes initiative or at the request of the National Office of Industrial Property to amend or supplement documents, the time limit for examination of contents may be extended by the duration reserved for the purpose of amending or supplementing documents.

  Before the deadline for content examination, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of results of content examination to the applicant and the person requesting examination under Point 16.3 above.

  Chapter III

                              TRANSFER OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

  17. Contracts for transfer of industrial property rights

  17.1. Industrial property ownership transfer contract means a contract on transfer of ownership of industrial property objects or a contract on transfer of the right to use industrial property objects.

  17.2. A contract on transfer of ownership of an invention, utility solution, industrial design or mark must contain the following principal details:

  - Full names and addresses of the Transmitting Party and Receiving Party;

  - Basis for transfer (certificate of protection granted to the Transferee or transferred to the Transferee);

  - The transferee (being the entire ownership right over the entire quantity of protection of industrial property object or over a part of the quantity of protection of mark - a part of the list of goods and services);

  - Transfer price;

  - Rights and obligations of each party, including corresponding obligations not contrary to Article 40 of the Decree;

  - Conditions for modifying, terminating or invalidating contracts;

  - How to settle complaints and disputes;

  - Date and place of signing;

  - The signatures of the Parties or of their authorized representatives, with their full names and positions, and the certification of the signatures.

  17.3. A contract for licensing of an invention, utility solution, industrial design or mark (hereinafter referred to as license contract) must contain the following contents:

  - Full names and addresses of the Transmitting party and Receiving party;

  - The basis for license transfer (the protection title has been granted to the Licensor; or the exclusive license contract);

  - Scope of license, including:

  . Type of license (exclusive/non-exclusive);

  . License object is determined by use right limit (belonging to protected use acts) and industrial property ownership object limit (belonging to protection volume of industrial property objects);

  . Territorial limits (belonging to Vietnam);

  . Time limit (within the time limit for protection of industrial property objects).

  For secondarily licensed shares, the scope of the license must fall within the scope of the License Contract for Secondarily Licensed Shares in question;

  - License price;

  - Rights and obligations of each Party, including corresponding obligations not contrary to Article 40 of the Decree;

  - Conditions for modifying, terminating or invalidating contracts;

  - How to settle complaints and disputes;

  - Date and place of signing;

  - The signatures of the parties or their authorized representatives, with their full names and positions, and the certification of the signatures.

  17.4. License contracts must not contain unreasonable restrictions on the licensee's rights, especially those not arising from the licensor's rights to the corresponding industrial property object or not aimed at protecting such rights, such as:

  - Provisions directly or indirectly restricting the export of products manufactured under licenses to territories other than those where the license transfer parties are holders of the corresponding industrial property rights or have exclusive right to import the corresponding industrial property objects;

  - The provisions forcing the licensee to buy all or a certain percentage of raw materials, components or equipment supplied by the licensor or by the person designated by the licensor for the purpose of ensuring the quality of goods produced by the licensee;

  - To prohibit licensees to improve industrial property objects (except marks) and force licensees to transfer free of charge improvements they have created or the right to file applications for protection of industrial property and industrial property rights to such improvements;

  - Provisions prohibiting licensees from filing lawsuits on the validity of industrial property rights or on the right to transfer licenses of licensors.

  17.5. If the transfer of industrial property rights is part of another Contract, the contents of the transfer of industrial property rights must be set up as a separate part from the remaining parts of the Contract and must comply with the provisions in this Point.

  18. Industrial property right transfer contracts shall be approved and registered.

  18.1. Under Clause 5, Article 38 and Clause 5, Article 62 of the Decree, all contracts for transfer of ownership or use right of industrial property subject matter in the following cases must be approved by the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment before carrying out the registration procedures for the contract according to Article 42 of the Decree and Point 20 of this Circular;

  (i) One of the parties is a State organization or a joint venture with State capital contribution, while the other party is an individual or organization not under the State;

  (ii) The transferor is an individual, juridical person or another entity of Vietnam and the transferee is a foreign individual or organization.

  18.2. All contracts for transfer of ownership or use right of industrial property objects, including those already carried out with approval procedures, must be registered under Article 42 of the Decree and Point 20 of this Circular.

  19. Procedures for approving industrial property right transfer contracts

  19.1. The dossier of approval for a contract (hereinafter referred to as the dossier of approval) shall comprise the following documents:

  (i) The written request for approval of the industrial property right transfer contract, made according to the form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, shall be made in 3 copies, in which the applicant for approval must be a State organization or an organization jointly invested by the State if the contract falls under case 18.1(i); or the Vietnamese party if the contract falls under case 18.1(ii);

  (ii) 2 originals or 2 copies of the Contract, including the Appendixes (if any); if the Contract is made in a language other than Vietnamese, it must be enclosed with a Vietnamese translation;

  (iii) The original of the title of protection (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects) or a copy of the corresponding title of protection (in case of transfer of the right to use industrial property objects), if the contract to be approved is a secondarily registered one, must be enclosed with a copy of the certificate of registration of the exclusive contract on secondarily registered object;

  (iv) The written consent of the co-owners to the transfer of rights, if the corresponding industrial property right is under joint ownership; or, in case of failure to reach such agreement, a written explanation of the reason for the dissent of the remaining co-owners must be provided.

  (v) The license to operate of the transferee in case of transfer of ownership or use right of a mark;

  (vi) Documents on payment of fees for approval of contracts;

  (vii) Power of attorney (if necessary).

  19.2. The dossier of approval shall be submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property as prescribed in the submission form (point 10 of this Circular).

  The time limit for submission of the application for contract approval is 60 days from the date of contract signing. This time limit may be extended if the applicant can prove that the delay is justifiable.

  19.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall receive the dossier of approval as prescribed in Point 11 of this Circular, with appropriate amendments, including the documents mentioned in Point 11.2(i): Declaration; Contract for transfer of industrial property rights; Fee payment document and Power of attorney.

  19.4. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the dossier of approval within 2 months from the date of receipt of the dossier as follows:

  a/ In case the dossier is valid and the contents of the contract are in compliance with regulations, the National Office of Industrial Property shall report the results of its examination of the dossier to the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment for issuance of a decision approving the contract within 15 days after receiving such report.

  b/ In case the dossier has errors which can be corrected (except for cases specified in Paragraph c below), the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant thereof and approve the correction of such errors within an appropriate time limit.

  The time limit for applicants to approve the correction of errors in their applications shall not be included in the time limit for application review.

  c/ In case the dossier is approved ineligibly for the following reasons, the National Office of Industrial Property shall propose the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to refuse to approve the industrial property right transfer contract:

  (i) The applicant for approval fails to correct errors within the time limit notified by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  (ii) The applicant for approval is not the person specified in Point 19.1(i) above;

  (iii) The transferee is neither the owner of the protection title (in case of transfer of ownership right to industrial property object); nor the owner of the protection title and also not the licensee of the exclusive right and the transferee of the right to sub-licensing for the corresponding industrial property object (in case of transfer of the right to use industrial property object).

  (iv) The recipient does not have a license for trading in goods/services appropriate to the corresponding certificate of registered mark (in case of transfer of industrial property rights to marks);

  (v) The corresponding industrial property rights no longer remain within the term of protection; or the subject matter of industrial property is in dispute;

  (vi) There are grounds to affirm that the transfer infringes upon industrial property rights of a third party;

  (vii) The contract has contents not in conformity with regulations on conditions restricting transfer or/and lacks mandatory contents as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree and Points 17.2, 17.3 and 17.4 of this Circular;

  (viii) The contract does not contain any clause on price or the transfer price is higher than the minimum and maximum limits as prescribed;

  (ix) The contract is not fully signed by both the Transmitting Party and Receiving Party and/or the signature is not legally certified;

  (x) The signatory is not authorized to sign the contract.

  19.5. Before proposing to refuse approval of a Contract, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the results of the examination of the dossier of application for approval, the intended refusal and reasons therefor and fix a proper time-limit for the applicant to express his/her opinion. If past such fixed time-limit the applicant for approval does not express any objection or his/her objection is groundless, the National Office of Industrial Property shall officially propose the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to refuse approval of the Contract.

  20. Procedures for registration of contracts on transfer of industrial property rights 20.1. A dossier of application for registration of a contract (below referred to as registration dossier) shall comprise the following documents:

  (i) An application form for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract, made according to a form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, which is made in 2 copies;

  (ii) 2 originals or 2 copies of the Transfer Contract, including the Appendix (if any); if the Contract is made in a language other than Vietnamese, it must be enclosed with the Vietnamese translation of the Contract;

  (iii) The original of the title of protection (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects); or a copy of the title of protection corresponding thereto (in case of transfer of the right to use industrial property objects), if the contract to be registered is a secondarily licensed contract, it must be enclosed with a certificate of registration of a secondarily licensed exclusive contract in corresponding area;

  (iv) The written consent of the co-owners to the transfer of rights, if the corresponding industrial property right is under joint ownership; or, in case of failure to reach such agreement, a written explanation of the reasons for the dissent of the remaining co-owners must be provided.

  (v) The license to operate of the transferee in case of transfer of ownership or use right of a mark;

  (vi) Decision on approval of the Contract of the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment (if the Contract is required to be approved);

  (vii) Documents on payment of fees for registration of contracts;

  (viii) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  In case both approval and registration procedures are required, documents submitted in the Approval application shall be considered as documents of the Registration application.

  20.2. Registration dossiers shall be submitted and received as prescribed as for approval dossiers (points 19.2 and 19.3 of this Circular).

  20.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider registration dossiers within 2 months from the date of receipt of such dossiers; for approved contracts, this time limit shall be 15 days.

  a/ If the dossier is valid and the contents of the contract are in compliance with regulations, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to grant a certificate of registration of a contract for transfer of ownership of an industrial property object or a protection title in case of transfer of a mark for part of the product/service list; or a certificate of registration of a license contract, and carry out the following procedures:

  (i) Recording in the National Register of Industrial Property and the Register of Contracts for Transfer of Ownership of Industrial Property Objects or the Register of License Contracts, respectively;

  (ii) Recording in the Title of protection (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects) and granting the Title of protection to the transferee of the right to use a part of the List of products or services;

  (iii) Affix registration seal to 2 copies of the Contract and give 1 copy to the applicant for registration and keep 1 copy;

  (iv) Granting to applicants for registration a copy of the Certificate of registration of contract on transfer of ownership of industrial property objects or the Certificate of registration of license contract;

  (v) Publicizing decisions on grant of registration certificates in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property.

  b/ In case the dossier contains errors which can be corrected (except for cases specified in Paragraph c below), the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify such to the applicant and request him/her to correct the errors within an appropriate time limit.

  The time limit for applicants to submit correction of errors in their applications shall not be included in the time limit for application review as prescribed.

  c/ In case the registration dossier is invalid for the following reasons, the National Office of Industrial Property shall refuse to register the contract:

  (i) The applicant fails to correct errors within the time limit set by the National Office of Industrial Property or fails to make satisfactory correction;

  (ii) The applicant is neither the Transmitting Party nor the Receiving Party of the Transfer Contract, and is not an authorized industrial property representative as prescribed;

  (iii) The transferee is neither the owner of the protection title (in case of transfer of ownership right to industrial property object); nor the owner of the protection title and also the licensee of the exclusive right and the transferee of the right to sub-license for the corresponding industrial property object (in case of transfer of license).

  (iv) The recipient does not have a license to trade in goods or services appropriate to the corresponding trademark registration certificate (in case of transfer of industrial property rights to trademarks);

  (v) The industrial property rights are no longer valid within the term of protection; or are being disputed;

  (vi) There are grounds to affirm that the transfer of industrial property rights infringes upon the industrial property rights of a third party;

  (vii) The contract has contents not in compliance with regulations on conditions restricting transfer as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree, or/and does not contain mandatory contents as prescribed in Points 17.2, 17.3 and 17.4 of this Circular;

  (viii) The contract is not fully signed by both the Transmitting Party and Receiving Party, or the signature is not legally certified;

  (ix) The signatory is not authorized to sign the contract;

  (x) There is no decision approving the contract (if the contract must be approved).

  d/ Before officially refusing to register a Contract, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant of the results of examination of the registration dossier, the intended refusal and reasons therefor, and fix a proper time-limit for the applicant to give its opinion. Past this fixed time-limit, if the applicant does not express any objection or gives unjustifiable opinions, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to register the Contract on transfer of industrial property rights, clearly stating the reasons therefor.

  21. Consider applications for non-voluntary license

  21.1. A dossier of application for issuance of non-voluntary licenses shall comprise the following documents:

  (i) Declaration requesting the grant of non-voluntary license, made according to a form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  (ii) Documents proving the special significance for security, national defense and public health protection or environmental protection of the related invention, utility solution or industrial design and documents proving that the related invention, utility solution or industrial design is not used by the industrial property owner (or the person who has been wholly licensed to use such subject matter) for plausible reasons or at a level which fails to meet the security, national defense and public health protection or environmental protection needs;

  (iii) Documents proving the applicant’s capability to use the invention, utility solution or industrial design and conditions which the applicant considers reasonable but have been presented without justification and rejected by the industrial property owner (or person wholly assigned the right to use the industrial property object);

  (iv) The documents on payment of fee for application for issuance of non-voluntary license.

  (v) Power of attorney (if necessary).

  21.2. The dossiers of application for non-voluntary licenses shall be submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property.

  21.3. After receiving the dossier of application for non-voluntary license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the dossier according to the provisions in Clause 5, Article 51 of the Decree. The procedures for considering the dossier of application for non-voluntary license are similar to those for approving contracts on transfer of industrial property rights (Point 19 of this Circular).

  Chapter 4:

      HANDLING OF INTERNATIONAL APPLICATIONS FOR INVENTIONS/PROVISIONS OF INTERNATIONAL REGISTRATION OF marks

  22. Procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property

  The provisions on filing of Applications and carrying out other relevant procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property specified in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 15 of the Decree and Point 3 of this Circular shall also apply to the carrying out of procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property for international Applications for inventions, utility solutions or marks mentioned in this Chapter.

  23. Processing of international applications for inventions/utility solutions under PCT treaties

  23.1 Receiving agencies

  The agency competent to receive international applications in Vietnam is the National Office of Industrial Property.

  The National Office of Industrial Property shall be responsible for:

  (i) Receive international applications originating in Vietnam;

  (ii) To collect and remit the corresponding charges to the International Office and the International Search Agency under the provisions of the Treaty;

  (iii) To check whether the prescribed fees are paid on time;

  (iv) Inspect and handle international applications originating in Vietnam according to the provisions of the Agreement;

  (v) Identification of subject matters of protection applications: If the subject matters of protection applications are classified as national secrets, the following procedures shall not be carried out and fees shall be refunded to applicants, excluding international application submission fees and international application copying fees;

  (vi) Send one copy (Application file) of the international application originating in Vietnam to the International Office and one copy (Reference sheet) to the international reference agency;

  (vii) To send and receive letters from applicants and international agencies.

  23.2. Language

  An international application originating in Vietnam shall be made in English or Russian. The application shall be made in 3 copies.

  In case of insufficient copies, the National Office of Industrial Property shall make additional copies to meet the required number and the applicant must pay an international application copying fee.

  23.3. International search agencies and international preliminary examination bodies.

  For Vietnamese origin international units, the competent international search offices and preliminary examination offices shall be the patent offices of Australia, Austria, the Russian Federation, Switzerland and the European Patent Office.

  23.4. International applications with designation of Vietnam

  If an international application contains a designation of Vietnam, the National Office of Industrial Property shall be the designated agency. In this case, in order to enter the national phase, within 21 months from the priority date, the applicant must submit it to the National Office of Industrial Property:

  (i) An application form for issuance of an invention/good utility solution patent, made according to a form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) Three copies of the international application (if the applicant requests to enter the national phase before the international publication date);

  (iii) Vietnamese translation of the International Application (including: Description, Protection Request (original submitted; amended and explained according to Article 19 of the PCT Treaty), Summary, and Illustrations with notes, including 3 copies;

  (iv) National fees.

  23.5. International applications with selection of Vietnam

  If Vietnam is selected in an international preliminary examination application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall be the selected agency. In this case, and if the selection of Vietnam is effected within 19 months from the priority date for entry into the national stage, within 31 months from the priority date, the applicant shall submit to the National Office of Industrial Property:

  (i) An application form for issuance of an invention/good utility solution patent, made according to a form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) Vietnamese translation of the International Application (including: Description, Protection Request (original submitted; amended and explained according to Article 19 of the PCT Treaty), Summary, and Illustrations with notes, including 3 copies;

  (iii) Vietnamese translations of the International Preliminary Examination Report Appendices, in 3 copies;

  (iv) National fees.

  23.6. Documents applying for priority right

  In order to enjoy the priority right, the international applicant shall have to submit to the International Office the necessary documents according to Rule 17.1(a) of the Implementing Regulation; and shall have to submit to the National Office of Industrial Property three Vietnamese versions of such documents within the time limits prescribed in Points 23.4 and 23.5 above.

  23.7. To amend and supplement documents in the national stage

  In accordance with Rule 51 bis of the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Convention, the applicant shall submit a Power of attorney and a Letter of assignment of the right to file an international application in the International Phase (if any) within 24 months from the priority date for an international application designating Vietnam and 34 months for an international application selecting Vietnam.

  In accordance with Article 28 and Article 41 of the PCT Agreement, during the national phase, the applicant may amend and supplement documents of the Application in accordance with Point 16.4 of this Circular.

  Additional and amended documents submitted by applicants to the National Office of Industrial Property must be made in Vietnamese and in three copies.

  23.8. The starting time of the national stage.

  The time limit for the start of processing of an international application with Vietnam designated or selected at the national stage shall be the first day of the 22nd month from the priority date, if Vietnam is designated, or the 32nd month from the priority date, if Vietnam is selected, and such selection has been made before the end of 19 months from the priority date, if the applicant does not request entry into the national stage earlier than the above-mentioned time limits.

  23.9. Testing of international applications

  An international application shall be examined for its form and contents according to procedures applicable to national applications.

  23.10. International applications shall be considered as withdrawn

  Apart from cases which are considered as withdrawn under the provisions of the PCT Treaty and the Implementing Regulations, in case the national fee is not paid to the National Office of Industrial Property or there is no Vietnamese translation after the time limit prescribed at Points 23.4 and 23.5 above, the international application with designation of Vietnam shall be considered as withdrawn.

  23.11. Charges

  International applicants of Vietnamese origin shall pay fees at the prescribed rates and according to the procedures set in the Regulation on implementation of the PCT Agreement and the Joint Circular of the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Science and Technology and the Ministry of Environment.

  24. To prepare and submit applications for registration of international marks of Vietnamese origin to foreign countries under the Madrid Agreement

  24.1. Any individual, juridical person or other entity may file an international registration application for a mark under the Madrid Agreement, provided that such mark has been registered in Vietnam.

  24.2. Registration application

  An international mark registration application must be made in French according to a form provided free of charge by the National Office of Industrial Property, and must be filled in the special sections reserved for applicants (except for the sections reserved for the National Office of Industrial Property and the International Office), and must be enclosed with the mark samples. The application must specify the countries of the Madrid Agreement of which an applicant wishes to have his/her mark protected. The applicant shall preliminarily calculate the total fee payable to the International Office according to the fee schedule on the application form. If the applicant is sure that the calculated fee is correct or after receiving the National Office of Industrial Property's notification of the exact fee payable, the applicant shall pay such fee to the International Office. Furthermore, the applicant shall pay an additional fee prescribed by law to the National Office of Industrial Property.

  24.3. The receiving agency

  An international mark registration application shall be filed with an International Office through the National Office of Industrial Property.

  The date on which the National Office of Industrial Property receives the Application shall be regarded as the date of receipt of the Application at the International Office if the International Office receives the Application within 2 months from such date.

  24.4. Sending the Application to the International Office

  After an application is filed with the International Office, all transactions between the applicant and the International Office shall be conducted through the National Office of Industrial Property, including amendments to documents, restriction of product lists, transfer of registered rights.

  25. Processing applications for international registration of marks with Vietnam designation

  25.1. After receiving a Notice of the International Office on an international mark registration application with designation of Vietnam, the National Office of Industrial Property shall examine the contents of such application as for marks submitted directly to the National Office of Industrial Property. Within 12 months after a mark is registered internationally, the National Office of Industrial Property shall make a conclusion on the possibility of protection of such mark. If the mark has no possible protection or is partially rejected, within the above-said time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify in writing the applicant through the International Office, clearly stating the reason for rejection.

  If within the above-said time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property does not issue a notice of refusal, the mark shall be accepted for protection in Vietnam.

  25.2. Within three months from the date of sending the Notice of refusal by the National Office of Industrial Property, the applicant may lodge a complaint about the Decision of the National Office of Industrial Property. The procedures for lodging and settling complaints are the same as those applicable to applications for marks filed directly with the National Office of Industrial Property. The results of settlement of complaints shall be notified by the National Office of Industrial Property to the applicant and to the International Office.

  25.3. Marks accepted for protection in Vietnam under the Madrid Agreement shall be published in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property. The scope (volume) of protection shall be confirmed according to the contents of such mark registration and recorded by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) and certified by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  Chapter 5.

   AMENDMENTS TO GUARANTEE DOCUMENTS; PRESERVATION OF EFFECTIVENESS OF INVENTION GUARANTEE DOCUMENTS, UTILITATIVE SOLUTIONS; EXTENSION OF EFFECTIVENESS OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGN GUARANTEE DOCUMENTS, MARKS AND APPellations OF GOODS.

  26. To amend the titles of protection

  26.1. The owner of a protection title shall have to notify in writing the National Office of Industrial Property of all changes in the name and address of the owner of the protection title.

  26.2. The owner of a protection title may request the National Office of Industrial Property to narrow the scope (volume) of protection of an industrial design or a mark by removing one or several schemes of the industrial design, modifying a number of details of the mark which, however, does not alter the fundamental nature of such mark; or by reducing a number of products on the list of products and services inscribed in the protection title.

  26.3. In order to modify the above-said contents, the owner of a protection title shall file an application for modification of the protection title to the National Office of Industrial Property. The application for modification shall be made according to a form set by the National Office of Industrial Property and must be enclosed with: (i) The original of the protection title; (ii) Documents certifying the modification of the name and address of the owner of the protection title; (iii) Two sets of photos or drawings of the industrial design to be deleted; (iv) Ten samples of the modified mark; (v) Receipt for payment of fee for modification of the protection title; (vi) A power of attorney, if necessary.

  26.4. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider an application for amendment of a title of protection within two months from the date of receipt of such application. If the application is found to be valid and the proposed amendment does not increase the scope (volume) of protection or does not alter the nature of the protected subject matter, the National Office of Industrial Property shall effect the amendment to the title of protection, register the change in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property. In the opposite case, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant of the refusal to effect the amendment, clearly stating the reasons therefor.

  27. Maintenance of validity

  In order to maintain the validity of an invention or utility solution title, its owner shall pay a validity maintenance fee within six (6) months before the expiry of the term of validity. The validity maintenance fee may be paid after the above-said time limit but not more than six (6) months from the expiry of the previous term of validity and the protection title holder shall pay an additional fee of ten (10) per cent for each month of late payment.

  28. Extension of validity

  28.1. In order to have the validity of a protection title extended, within six months before the expiry date of such title, the owner of the protection title shall file an application for extension with the National Office of Industrial Property.

  An application for extension may be filed after the above-said time limit but not more than six months from the date the title of protection expires and the applicant shall pay an extension fee plus 10% of the extension fee for each month of delay in payment.

  28.2. The dossier of application for extension of validity of a title of protection shall comprise the following documents:

  (i) The application form for extension of validity of a Certificate of protection, made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, which is made in 2 copies;

  (ii) Original copies of the Certificates of protection;

  (iii) The documents on payment of fee for extension;

  (iv) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  28.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider an application for extension within two months from the date of receipt of such application. The National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to extend the protection term, record it in the title of protection and registration certificate and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property if the application does not fall into the following cases:

  - The application for extension is invalid or is filed in contravention of the prescribed procedures;

  - There are grounds to believe that the owner of a mark registration certificate or the owner of a certificate of trademark use right has not used such mark or trademark or such subject matter for five consecutive years before the expiry date of the protection title without plausible reasons;

  - The applicant for extension is not the owner of the title of protection of the corresponding mark, appellation of origin or industrial design.

  If the application falls into one of the above cases, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to extend the validity of the application, clearly stating the reason for refusal.

  Chapter 6.

                                       PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE OF REPRESENTATION LICENSES

  29. Application for a representative license.

  29.1. The representative license shall be granted by the National Office of Industrial Property on the basis of examination of dossiers of application for the representative license as prescribed in Points 29.2 and 29.3 below. The applicant for a representative license shall pay a fee according to regulations.

  29.2. An application for a certificate of industrial property representation service organization shall include:

  - Application for a certificate of industrial property representation service organization, which includes a proposal on the list of industrial property representatives of the organization.

  - A copy of the operation charter and business registration certificate;

  - The copy of the decision on appointment to the leading position of the Organization or the written authorization for representation of the Organization, signed by the head of the Organization, by one of the members in the above-mentioned list of requesters;

  - The list of charges for industrial property representation services of the organization after having carried out registration procedures in accordance with regulations on charge and fee management;

  - The fee receipt for application for a representative permit.

  Dossiers of application for certificates of industrial property representation service organizations must be filed together with dossiers of application for industrial property representative cards of individuals on the above-said list.

  29.3. An application for grant of an industrial property representative card shall comprise:

  - Application for grant of industrial property representative card, which contains certification by the People's Committee of the ward, commune or township of the applicant's permanent residence address;

  - Copies of university degrees;

  - A copy of the certificate of graduation from a formal training course in industrial property; or a written certification of the duration and job performed as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 58 of the Decree;

  - Copies of certificates of passing the current examination on industrial property law of Vietnam issued by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  - A copy of the decision on recruitment issued by the industrial property representation service organization;

  - The fee receipt is the receipt of the application.

  30. Consider application for license to act as representative

  Within one month after receiving an application for a representative license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider issuing or refusing to issue such license. In case of refusal, the National Office of Industrial Property shall clearly state the reason therefor to the applicant. In case of acceptance and the applicant has paid the fee for the issuance of a representative license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall carry out the following procedures:

  - Granting representative permits;

  - Recording the grant of representative licenses in the industrial property representation book;

  - To publish the grant of representative office permits in the Official Gazette on industrial property.

  Chapter VII

                                         FINAL PROVISIONS

  31. Complaints

  If disagreeing with a decision related to the processing of an Application as well as the approval and registration of an industrial property right transfer contract, consideration of an application for issuance of non-voluntary license, extension of protection title or grant of a representation permit, the applicant may lodge a complaint or a lawsuit according to the order and procedures specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4, Article 27 of the Decree.

  32. To process applications filed from July 1, 1996 to the effective date of this Circular.

  Applications filed from July 1st, 1996 to the effective date of this Circular may use the forms prescribed in Circular No. 1134/SC of October 17th, 1991 of the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment, guiding the implementation of Decree No. 84/HDBT of March 20th, 1990 of the Council of Ministers, and shall be processed according to this Circular. The time limit for processing the above-mentioned applications shall be extended by the period from the date of filing of the applications to the effective date of this Circular.

  33. Document forms and Regulations on testing of applications

  The Director of the National Office of Industrial Property shall be responsible for promulgating necessary forms related to Applications and related to the approval and registration of industrial property right transfer contracts, and promulgating the Regulations on examination of Applications.

  34. Implementation

  This Circular replaces the following documents of the State Committee for Science and Technology, the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment:

  - Chapters II, III and IV of Circular No. 1134/SC of October 17, 1991 guiding the implementation of Decree No. 84/HDBT of March 20, 1990 of the Council of Ministers,

  - Circular No. 437/SC of March 19, 1993 providing guidance on the registration of trademarks,

  - Circular No. 163/TT-SHCN of April 15, 1994 guiding the implementation of the regulations on approval and registration of license contracts,

  - Circular No. 238-TT-SHCN of May 2, 1994 guiding the filing and processing of international applications for protection of inventions/utility solutions under the Patent Cooperation Treaty in Vietnam,

  - Decision No. 199/QD of December 21, 1992 promulgating regulations on industrial property representation.

  This Circular takes effect 15 days after its signing.
