                                                  RESOLUTION

     GUIDANCE ON APPLICATION OF CERTAIN PROVISIONS OF THE CRIMINAL CODE TO THE JUDGMENT OF COMMITTING AGENDA VIOLATIONS AND OTHER OFFENCES INVOLVING OFFENSES

                            JUDGES' COUNCIL OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of People's Courts dated November 24, 2014;

  To properly and uniformly apply a number of provisions of the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 amended by Law No. 12/2017/QH14 in the trial of corruption-related crimes and other position-related crimes;

  After obtaining opinions from the Prosecutor General of the Supreme People's Procuracy and the Minister of Justice.

                                                 DECISION:

  Article 1. Scope

  This Resolution provides guidance on application of some provisions of the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 amended by Law No. 12/2017/QH14 (hereinafter referred to as “the Criminal Code”) on corruption-related crimes and other crimes involving positions; determination of value of property appropriated, damage caused by corruption-related crimes and other crimes involving positions; handling of items and money directly related to crimes.

  Article 2. Definitions

  1. “Agencies and organizations” as prescribed in clause 1 Article 352 of the Criminal Code include state agencies, organizations, units and non-state enterprises and organizations.

  2. “State agencies, organizations and units” are those specified in clause 9 Article 3 of the Law on Anti-Corruption, including: state agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, people’s armed forces units, public service providers, state enterprises and other organizations or units established or invested in by the State with full or partial funding for their operations, directly managed or under the State's management, serving the general development needs and essential needs of the State and society.

  3. “Non-state enterprises and organizations” as prescribed in Articles 353, 354, 364 and 365 of the Criminal Code are enterprises and organizations not falling into the cases guided in clause 2 of this Article.

  4. “Persons holding positions” as prescribed in clause 2 Article 352 of the Criminal Code are those specified in clause 2 Article 3 of the Law on Anti-Corruption.

  5. “Carrying out a task through another means” as prescribed in clause 2 Article 352 of the Criminal Code is the case where a person is assigned to perform a task and has authority while performing such task, but is not appointed, elected, recruited or hired under a contract.

  Example: A person is assigned by a competent state agency to attend the checkpoint for Covid-19 prevention and control.

  6. “Voluntary declaration before detection” as prescribed in clause 7 Article 364 and clause 6 Article 365 of the Criminal Code is the case where the act of offering bribes or acting as bribery broker has not yet been detected or reported by anyone, but the person offering bribes or acting as bribery broker has voluntarily declared all acts of offering bribes or acting as bribery broker.

  7. “Voluntarily returning at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property” means cases where the offender has voluntarily returned at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property after committing the crime. It is also considered voluntary return of at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property in cases where the offender, after committing the crime, influences their parents, spouse, children, siblings, or other relatives to return or do not object to the return of at least three-quarters of the property they embezzled or were bribed.

  For cases within the same case where the person subject to criminal prosecution is liable for multiple crimes, including embezzlement, bribery, and other crimes, but has voluntarily returned property equivalent to at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property, it is also considered voluntary return of at least three-quarters of the embezzled or bribed property, except where there are clear grounds to determine that the property originated from another crime.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled VND 4.000.000.000 and abused his position and power to appropriate VND 2.000.000.000. After A was prosecuted, A’s wife transferred a piece of real estate as personal property to replace A in returning VND 3.000.000.000, which is considered voluntary return of at least three-quarters of the embezzled property.

  8. “Actively cooperating with competent authorities in detecting, investigating, and handling crimes” means that after committing corrupt or other crimes involving positions, the offender has voluntarily provided information, documents, and evidence that is significant for the detection, investigation, and handling of crimes related to the crimes for which they are charged (such as identifying the right place to hide important exhibits to assist competent authorities in recovering such exhibits; providing accurate information and identifying the right places where other accomplices are hiding; providing information about new crimes and offenders involved in the crimes for which they are charged...). In addition to the above cases, other cases may be determined as “actively cooperating with competent authorities in detecting, investigating, and handling crimes,” but the Court must clearly state this in the judgment.

  9. “Great meritorious contribution” means an offender has assisted the presiding agency in detecting, arresting, investigating or handling crimes unrelated to the crime for which he/she is charged; rescued other people in dangerous circumstances or rescued property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more of the State, of the collective or of individuals during natural disasters, epidemics, fires or other force majeure events; or made inventions, discoveries or innovations with significant value as certified by competent state agencies. Apart from the above cases, other cases may be determined as “great meritorious contributions” but the Courts must clearly state them in their judgments.

  Article 3. Regarding some circumstances being signs of determination of guilt

  1. “Having been disciplined for this act but committing violations again” prescribed at Point a, Clause 1, Articles 353, 354, 355 and 358 of the Criminal Code means that the offender has been previously disciplined for the corresponding act but the time limit for being considered not yet having been disciplined under law has not expired.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was given a warning for embezzlement of VND 500,000 but 6 months later, A committed embezzlement of VND 1,500,000.

  In case where the offender has been disciplined by a competent agency or organization and then examined for penal liability by the presiding agency for the same act, the previous disciplinary action shall not be considered “having been disciplined for this act but committing violations again”.

  Example: Nguyen Van A was disciplined by a competent agency for embezzlement of property and then examined for prosecution for the same act, the circumstance of “having been disciplined for this act but committing violations again” shall not apply to A.

  2. “Having been convicted of one of the crimes prescribed in Section 1 of this Chapter but not yet having had the criminal records remitted and committing violations again” prescribed in Clause 1, Articles 353, 354 and 355 of the Criminal Code means that the offender has been previously convicted once of one or more than one crime prescribed in Section 1, Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, has not yet had his/her criminal record remitted but has committed one of the acts specified in Clause 1 of one of Articles 353, 354 and 355 of the Criminal Code.

  In case the offender has other previous convictions besides the one mentioned above, such previous convictions shall be used to determine recidivism or dangerous recidivism, except for cases where all previous convictions have been used to determine the signs of commission of crimes in previous judgments.

  For example: Before committing the act of accepting a bribe amounting to VND 1.500.000, A had two previous convictions, in which the first conviction, A was convicted of embezzlement with an amount of VND 5.000.000; the second conviction, A was convicted of abuse of position and power to appropriate property with an amount of VND 1.000.000. In this case, the second previous conviction is used to determine the signs of commission of the crime of embezzlement. For the first previous conviction, since it has already been used to determine the signs of commission of the crime of abuse of position and power to appropriate property (with an amount of VND 1.000.000), it is not used further to determine recidivism.

  3. "Other material benefits" prescribed in Articles 354, 358, 364 and 366 of the Criminal Code means material benefits other than property specified in Article 105 of the Civil Code.

  For example: Bribery by way of financial support for studying abroad, traveling abroad, etc.

  4. "Non-material benefits" prescribed at Point b, Clause 1, Articles 354, 358, 364, 365 and 366 of the Criminal Code means non-material benefits.

  For example: Bribery by way of rewards, proposing rewards, titles, prizes; election, appointment, designation of positions; raising of test scores; promises of graduation, study abroad, participation in competitions, performance abroad; sexual bribery, etc.

  5. "Abuse of position and power" prescribed in Clause 1, Article 355 of the Criminal Code means the use beyond the assigned authority, responsibilities or duties, or the performance of tasks or powers not assigned or assigned in such domain.

  Example: Nguyen Van A is the Vice President of the People's Committee of a province. A is only assigned to manage social - cultural affairs but is not assigned to manage land and still issues a decision on land expropriation for Company X to hand over to Company Y (owned by A’s family). In this case, A’s act has exceeded his/her assigned duties and responsibilities.

  6. “Abusing position or power” as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 356 of the Criminal Code means relying on one’s assigned position or power to commit illegal acts, failing to commit or committing against the law.

  Example: Nguyen Van A, the Head of the Natural Resources and Environment Division of District B, committed an act of violation of official duties, and then submitted it to the leader of the People's Committee of District B to sign a decision to grant a certificate of land use rights for a plot of land that does not meet the conditions for issuance of a certificate of land use rights under the provisions of land law.

  7. “Gaining benefits” as prescribed in Clause 1 Articles 356, 357 and 359 of the Criminal Code means cases where offenders abuse their positions or powers or abuse their powers to gain material or non-material benefits for themselves or other agencies, organizations or individuals.

  8. “Other personal motives” as prescribed in Clause 1 Articles 356, 357 and 359 of the Criminal Code means cases where offenders abuse their positions or powers or abuse their powers to unjustifiably affirm, consolidate or enhance their own status, prestige or power.

  9. Damage caused by criminal acts as prescribed in Articles 353, 354, 355 and 358 of the Criminal Code means actual damage that occurs and is causally related to the criminal acts, excluding money, property and other material benefits that have been appropriated or will be appropriated by offenders.

  Example: Nguyen Van A, the warehouse keeper of Company B, embezzled a sum of 200,000,000 dong worth of epidemic prevention medicine. As a result, all poultry valued at 10,000,000,000 dong of Company B died. In this case, the amount embezzled by A is 200,000,000 dong and the actual damage caused by A’s criminal act is 10,000,000,000 dong.

  10. “Work-related secrets” as prescribed in Article 361 and Article 362 of the Criminal Code refer to work-related information presented in any form (e.g., documents, drafts, speeches, images, etc.) that agencies or organizations are not allowed to disclose to others and are not included in the list of state secrets.

  Article 4. Regarding some circumstances determining the penalty bracket

  1. The circumstance of “using deceitful tricks” or “using dangerous tricks” as prescribed in Clause 2 of Articles 353, 354, 355, 364 and 365 of the Criminal Code:

  a) “Using deceitful tricks” refers to cases where offenders use sophisticated deception techniques, employ high technology to commit criminal acts, conceal crimes, shift blame to others, or destroy evidence, thereby obstructing the detection, investigation, and handling of crimes.

  b) “Using dangerous tricks” refers to cases where offenders use methods that may endanger the lives or health of others or cause other serious consequences to appropriate property or conceal crimes.

  2. The circumstance of “committing the crime more than once” as prescribed in Clause 2 of Articles 353, 354, 355, 356, 357, 358, 361, 364, 365 and 366 of the Criminal Code refers to cases where an offender has committed one of the acts specified in these articles more than once, each constituting a separate crime, but has not yet been criminally prosecuted and the statute of limitations for criminal prosecution has not expired.

  Example: On August 15, 2018, Nguyen Van A stole 10,000,000 VND. On September 25, 2019, A stole another 20,000,000 VND. A’s criminal acts have not yet been criminally handled and the statute of limitations for criminal prosecution has not expired. In this case, A is criminally prosecuted for theft and shall be subject to the circumstance of “committing the crime more than once” as prescribed in Point c Clause 2 Article 353 of the Criminal Code.

  3. The circumstance of “causing adverse effects on the lives of officials, public employees, and employees in agencies or organizations” as prescribed in Point g Clause 2 Article 353 of the Criminal Code refers to one of the following cases:

  a) Loss or reduction of regular income, lawful increase in income of officials, public employees, and employees;

  b) Causes angry complaints and denunciations, causes loss of unity and trust within agencies and organizations, affecting the spirit of officials, public employees, and employees.

  4. The circumstance of "causing bad influence on social order, security, and safety" as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 353 and Article 355 of the Criminal Code is one of the following cases:

  a) Causes a large number of lawsuits, protests, causes disturbance for hostile forces to take advantage of, entice, incite against the government, distort the Party's line, policies, and laws of the State;

  b) Causes panic, fear, or anger among the people;

  c) Causes difficulties in implementing the Party's line, policies, and laws of the State.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled poverty alleviation funds for economic development, causing the People's Committee of commune B to face difficulties in implementing policies to eliminate hunger and reduce poverty in the area.

  Article 5. Principles for handling corruption crimes and other crimes related to positions

  1. The handling of corruption-related crimes and other crimes involving positions must be strict and in full compliance with the principles of criminal law and criminal procedure law.

  2. During the process of legal proceedings, if the offender has actively returned at least three-quarters of the property embezzled or bribed and cooperated actively with competent authorities in detecting, investigating, handling the crime or made meritorious contributions, the highest level of the penalty bracket applicable to the offender shall not apply.

  3. Consider applying special amnesty policies with exemption from penalties as prescribed in Article 59 of the Criminal Code for offenders who have at least two mitigating circumstances specified in Clause 1, Article 51 of the Criminal Code or first-time offenders who are accomplices but play insignificant roles in one of the following cases:

  a) The offender commits the crime not for self-seeking motives or other personal motives but for the purpose of innovation and daring to break through for the socio-economic development and national defense;

  b) The offender is a person with dependent relationships (such as subordinates to superiors, employees receiving salaries, or persons performing tasks under the direction of superiors), unaware of their criminal acts, not for self-seeking motives or other personal motives, and not benefiting from such acts; has actively declared before being detected, actively cooperated with competent authorities, and effectively contributed to the investigation and handling of the crime;

  c) The offender has actively declared before being detected, actively cooperated with competent authorities, contributed to minimizing damage, actively returned all embezzled property, fully remedied all consequences, and compensated for all damage caused;

  d) The offender, after being detected, has sincerely declared, shown repentance, and actively returned all embezzled property, fully remedied all consequences, and compensated for all damage caused.

  Article 6. Consideration of penal liability and decision on penalties in case of separation of cases

  In case a case is split into different stages for settlement (split into multiple cases), the consideration of penal liability and decision on penalties shall be carried out as follows:

  1. The fact that the offender has been convicted in a previous case shall not serve as a ground for determining that he/she has committed the crime more than once, except where the criminal acts examined and handled from different stages are independent of each other;

  2. The fact that the offender has been convicted in a previous case shall not serve as a ground for denying him/her the suspended sentence if he/she meets all other conditions for being granted the suspended sentence;

  3. The penalty decision in each judgment or decision must ensure that when aggregated, the total penalty does not exceed the highest level of the corresponding penalty bracket corresponding to the total value of property appropriated, bribes paid or damage caused by the crime.

  For example: Nguyen Van A committed an act of abuse of power while performing his duty. Only at the first stage could it be proved that A caused property damage valued at VND 100.000.000, so the presiding agency decided to split the case into two separate cases and handle A first for the act of causing property damage valued at VND 100.000.000. However, at the second stage, the presiding agency could prove that A's act also caused property damage valued at VND 350.000.000. At the first stage, the Court applied Clause 2 Article 357 of the Criminal Code to convict A of 5 years' imprisonment for abuse of power while performing his duty. When hearing the case at the second stage, A was still prosecuted under Clause 2 Article 357 of the Criminal Code, so in order to ensure that the total penalty of both judgments did not exceed the highest level of this clause, the Court could only decide a penalty of up to 5 years' imprisonment against A.

  Article 7. Criminal prosecution in case property users acquired through corruption are used for money laundering

  In case property users acquired through corruption are used for money laundering as prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code, apart from being examined for penal liability for the corresponding crimes prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, offenders shall also be examined for penal liability for money laundering as prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code.

  Example: Nguyen Van A embezzled 5.000.000.000 VND and used the money to invest in real estate business to conceal the illegal origin of the embezzled money. In this case, A is criminally prosecuted for the crime of embezzlement prescribed in Article 353 and money laundering prescribed in Article 324 of the Criminal Code.

  Article 8. Criminal prosecution in cases where a person commits the same act multiple times prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code

  1. If a person commits the same act multiple times prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code but the value of property embezzled or bribed or the damage caused by such act is below the minimum value prescribed for criminal prosecution and does not fall under other cases subject to criminal prosecution, and no disciplinary action has been taken and the statute of limitations for disciplinary action has not expired, if the total value of property embezzled or bribed or the damage caused by such act is equal to or higher than the minimum value prescribed for criminal prosecution as prescribed by the Criminal Code, then the person who commits the same act multiple times shall be criminally prosecuted for the corresponding crime based on the total value of property embezzled or bribed or the damage caused.

  Example: On August 15, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of 1.500.000 VND; on September 15, 2019, A continued to accept a bribe of 1.500.000 VND; on December 30, 2019, A again accepted a bribe of 1.500.000 VND, which was discovered by a competent authority. These acts of A have not yet been disciplined and the statute of limitations for disciplinary action has not expired. In this case, the total amount of bribes accepted by A is 4.500.000 VND, so A is criminally prosecuted according to Clause 1 Article 354 of the Criminal Code.

  2. In case an offender commits the same act in multiple times as prescribed in Chapter XXIII of the Criminal Code, each time constitutes a crime and total value of property embezzled or bribed or property damaged in each violation falls within the aggravated penalty bracket, if such offenses have not yet been examined for penal liability and the statute of limitations for examination for penal liability has not expired, apart from being subject to the corresponding penalty bracket applicable to the total value of property embezzled or bribed or damaged, he/she shall also be subject to the aggravating circumstance or aggravating circumstances as follows:

  a) If the total value of property embezzled or bribed or damaged falls within the range specified in clause 2 of the corresponding article, the offender shall be additionally subject to the aggravating circumstance of “committing the offense more than once”.

  Example: On August 15, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of VND 50.000.000; on June 30, 2020, A again accepted a bribe of VND 100.000.000 which was detected by competent authorities. In this case, A is subject to 2 aggravating circumstances: “The bribe is money, property or other material benefits valued at between VND 100.000.000 and under VND 500.000.000” and “Committing the offense more than once” as prescribed in points c and dd clause 2 Article 354 of the Criminal Code.

  b) If the total value of property embezzled or bribed or damaged falls within the other aggravated penalty bracket, the offender shall be additionally subject to the aggravating circumstance of “committing the offense more than once”.

  Example: On March 11, 2019, Nguyen Van A accepted a bribe of VND 400.000.000; on July 30, 2020, A again accepted a bribe of VND 200.000.000 which was detected by competent authorities. In this case, A is subject to the aggravating circumstance of “The bribe is money, property or other material benefits valued at between VND 500.000.000 and under VND 1.000.000.000” as prescribed in point a clause 3 Article 354 and the aggravating circumstance of “Committing the offense more than once” as prescribed in point g clause 1 Article 52 of the Criminal Code.

  Article 9. Criminal prosecution in case offenders appropriating property or accepting bribes while causing damage to property

  Where the offender appropriates property or receives a bribe and causes damage to property, the value of the appropriated property, the bribe, and the damaged property shall be subject to different penalty frameworks. The offender shall be criminally prosecuted according to the corresponding points of such penalty frameworks. Where the value of the appropriated property, the bribe, and the damaged property falls under different penalty frameworks, the offender shall be criminally prosecuted according to the higher penalty framework.

  Article 10. Determination of value of property expropriated and damage caused by corruption-related crimes and other position-related crimes

  1. The value of property appropriated as a basis for criminal handling shall be determined at the time of commission of the criminal act. In case the crime is committed continuously or over a long period, the value of property appropriated shall be determined at the time of completion of the criminal act. For cases where the criminal act is in progress but detected or prevented, the value of property appropriated shall be determined at the time when the criminal act is detected or prevented.

  2. Property damage used as a basis for criminal prosecution shall be determined on a case-by-case basis as follows:

  a) In case damage occurs at the time of commission of a crime, such damage shall be determined at this time; for a continuous and prolonged crime, the damage shall be determined at the time when the crime is detected or the crime ends.

  b) In case damage occurs or continues after the crime is committed or has ended, the damage shall be determined at the time when the damage is prevented.

  c) In case of failure to determine the damage as guided in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article, the damage shall be determined at the time of filing the lawsuit.

  3. The civil liability shall be examined in accordance with the civil law.

  Article 11. Handling of items and money directly related to crimes

  1. After accepting a case, the Court shall immediately consider applying the measure of distraint of property or freezing of accounts directly related to the criminal act if such property has not yet been subject to enforcement measures during the stage of investigation and prosecution.

  2. The Court shall consider and decide on confiscation for the state budget, destruction, return or compensation to lawful owners or managers in accordance with law for property directly related to corruption crimes and other position-related crimes, including:

  a) Instruments and means used for committing the crime;

  b) Money and property appropriated;

  c) Bribes;

  d) Damage caused by criminal acts;

  dd) Illegal gains from criminal acts;

  e) Profits, yields or profits arising from property acquired through commission of crimes;

  g) Other property as prescribed by law.

  3. If the property subject to confiscation into the state budget no longer exists at the time of settlement of the case, the Court shall decide on the confiscation of its value according to the valuation conclusion of a competent authority.

  4. In case the property originates from corruption crime or other position-related crimes and has been incorporated into the common property, the Court shall only confiscate or compel the return of the portion of property originating from the crime to its lawful owner or manager. The profits earned from this common property shall be divided in proportion to the portion confiscated or returned to the lawful owner or manager in accordance with law.

  Article 12. Entry into force

  1. This Resolution was adopted by the Council of Justices of the Supreme People's Court on December 30, 2020 and comes into force as of February 15, 2021.

  2. Where the offender was tried before the effective date of this Resolution in accordance with previous regulations and guidance and the judgment has taken legal effect, this Resolution shall not serve as a basis for lodging protests according to cassation or reopening procedures.
