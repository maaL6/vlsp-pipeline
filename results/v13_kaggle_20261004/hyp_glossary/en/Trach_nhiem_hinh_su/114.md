                                                  RESOLUTION

     ADDITIONAL GUIDANCE OF DECISION No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP OF OCTOBER 2, 2007 AND DECISION No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP OF OCTOBER 2, 2007 OF THE COUNCIL OF JUDGES OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT

                           JUDGES' COUNCIL OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of People's Courts;
  In order to properly and uniformly apply the provisions of the Penal Code and the Criminal Procedure Code;
  After reaching agreement with the Prosecutor General of the Supreme People's Procuracy and the Minister of Justice,

                                                  DECISION:

  Article 1. To supplement Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Judge's Council of the Supreme People's Court guiding the application of a number of provisions of the Criminal Code on statute of limitations for judgment execution, exemption from penalty execution and reduction of penalty execution duration

  1. To add guidance on the reduction of the term of imprisonment at Point b, Item 3.2, Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Council of Judges of the Supreme People's Court as follows:

  b/ A person sentenced to imprisonment for 30 years or less may have his/her sentence reduced from three months to three years each time. In case of a three-year sentence reduction, he/she must have made particularly outstanding achievements in labor, reform and strict observance of the internal rules and regulations on detention.

  In case a person who is serving a prison sentence and fully meets the conditions for consideration of reduction of the term of imprisonment and the remaining part of the sentence to be served is less than 3 months, the reduced term of imprisonment may equal the remaining term of imprisonment but he/she must serve half of the imposed sentence in accordance with Clause 3, Article 58 of the Penal Code.

  Example 1: Nguyen Van A was sentenced to 5 years' imprisonment, A has served 4 years and 10 months of his/her imprisonment sentence and fully meets the conditions for consideration of reduction of the term of imprisonment under the guidance in Item 3.1 Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP. In this case, A may have the remaining part of the sentence reduced (2 months).

  Example 2: Tran Van T was sentenced to 3 months' imprisonment, T has served one-third of the sentence (1 month) and fully meets the other conditions for consideration of reduction of the term of imprisonment under the guidance in Item 3.1 Section 3 of Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP. In this case, T may have the sentence reduced by up to 1 month and 15 days because according to Clause 3, Article 58 of the Penal Code, T must serve half of the imposed sentence (i.e., 1 month and 15 days).

  2. To add Section 1a providing guidance on the combination of life imprisonment or death penalty which has been imposed on a defendant by another legally effective judgment in Resolution No. 01/2007/NQ-HDTP dated October 2, 2007 of the Council of Judges of the Supreme People's Court as follows:

  In case a defendant is prosecuted for one or several crimes within the jurisdiction of a district-level People's Court but when the case is accepted, it is considered that the defendant has been subject to life imprisonment or death penalty under another judgment and such judgment has taken legal effect, the district-level People's Court shall report it to the provincial-level People's Court so that the provincial-level People's Court can reach agreement with the provincial-level People's Procuracy to withdraw the case file for prosecution and trial at the provincial level.

  Article 2. Supplementing Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP dated October 02, 2007 of the Council of Judges of the Supreme People's Court guiding a number of provisions in Part Five "Execution of Court's judgments and decisions" of the Criminal Procedure Code

  1. Adding Section 10 in Part IV of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP dated October 02, 2007 of the Council of Judges of the Supreme People's Court on decisions of the Council for consideration of remission as follows:

  10. Based on the review of the dossier and opinions of the representative of the Procuracy, the Council for consideration of remission has the right:

  a) To accept the whole proposal for remission of penalty execution term or shortening of probation period of suspended sentence;

  b) To accept part of the proposal for remission of penalty execution term or shortening of probation period of suspended sentence;

  c) To refuse the proposal for remission of penalty execution term or shortening of probation period of suspended sentence;

  d) For the person proposed for consideration of remission, if after the acceptance of the whole proposal for remission of penalty execution term for them, the remaining penalty execution term is less than 1 month, the Council for consideration of remission may decide to reduce the whole remaining penalty.

  2. Adding Section 13 in Part IV of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP dated October 02, 2007 of the Council of Judges of the Supreme People's Court on the effect of decisions on remission of penalty execution term or shortening of probation period of suspended sentence as follows:

  13. The court’s decision on exemption from or reduction of the term of imprisonment or shortening of the probation period of a suspended sentence may be protested against by the Procuracy of the same level or the immediate superior Procuracy according to appellate procedures. The time limit for protestation by the Procuracy of the same level is seven days and that by the immediate superior Procuracy is fifteen days as from the date the court issues the decision. The court’s decision on exemption from or reduction of the term of imprisonment or shortening of the probation period of a suspended sentence takes effect after the time limit for protestation according to appellate procedures expires. In case the convict is serving his/her imprisonment penalty but the reduced term is equal to the remaining term of imprisonment he/she has to serve, the court’s decision on reduction of the term of imprisonment shall be immediately executed even if it is protested against by the Procuracy according to appellate procedures.

  3. To add Section 3a providing guidance on the time of commencing the non-custodial reform period in Part III of Resolution No. 02/2007/NQ-HDTP of October 2, 2007 of the Council of Judges of the Supreme People's Court as follows:

  The time of commencing the non-custodial reform period is the date the agency or organization assigned to supervise and educate the convicted person receives the judgment execution decision and copy of the judgment. The time of commencing the non-custodial reform period must be declared in the part of the judgment decision.

  Ex: Nguyen Van A was sentenced to 1 year of non-custodial reform for theft of property under Clause 1, Article 138 of the Criminal Code. For this case, the part of the judgment decision should clearly state: “…sending Nguyen Van A to 1 year of non-custodial reform for theft of property; the non-custodial reform period is counted from the date the agency or organization assigned to supervise and educate the convicted person receives the judgment execution decision and copy of the judgment”.

  Article 3. Effect

  1. This Resolution was passed on September 23, 2010 by the Council of Judges of the Supreme People's Court and takes effect 45 days after its signing.

  2. Difficulties arising during the implementation of this Circular should be promptly reported to the Supreme People's Court for further clarification or guidance.
