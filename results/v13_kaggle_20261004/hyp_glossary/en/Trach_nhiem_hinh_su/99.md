                                             JOINT CIRCULAR

                               GUIDANCE ON IMPLEMENTATION OF DEPORTATION SENTENCE

  Pursuant to the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments dated June 17, 2010;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 77/2009/ND-CP of September 15, 2009, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 15/2008/ND-CP of February 4, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 104/2008/ND-CP of September 16, 2008, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of National Defense;

  In order to uniformly implement provisions of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments on execution of deportation sentences, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall jointly provide guidance as follows:

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidelines on procedures for enforcing deportation sentences; handling cases where persons subject to deportation sentences abscond, fall ill, or die during the period pending their exit; handling of receipt of corpses, remains, and ashes of persons subject to deportation sentences who die; funding for enforcement of deportation sentences.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to persons subject to deportation under court judgments or decisions (hereinafter referred to as “deported persons”) and agencies, organizations and individuals involved in enforcement of deportation sentences.

  Article 3. Principles of enforcement of the decision on deportation

  1. Ensure political, legal, foreign affairs and humanitarian policy requirements of the State.

  2. The enforcement of the deportation penalty shall ensure safety, the right persons, the right time, and the prescribed procedures and powers in accordance with Vietnamese law and international law and practices.

  3. Ensure the legitimate rights and interests of the expelled person; strictly prohibit all acts infringing upon the life, health, dignity, and lawful property of the expelled person.

  Article 4. Agencies enforcing deportation sentences

  1. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the People’s Court or Military Court issues the decision on execution of the deportation penalty shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in organizing the execution of the deportation penalty according to the court’s judgment execution decision.

  2. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level police authority where the prison affiliated to the Ministry of Public Security, the prison affiliated to the Ministry of National Defense or the military zone shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies in organizing the enforcement of the deportation sentence for persons who have completely served their imprisonment sentences and had the additional penalty of deportation.

  Article 5. Funds for judgment enforcement

  The funding for enforcement of the expulsion sentence shall be covered by the state budget in the annual budget estimates of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority and the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security. The cost of purchasing exit tickets for repatriation of the expelled person shall comply with Article 102 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  Chapter II

                          PROCEDURES FOR ENFORCEMENT OF DEPORTATION SENTENCE

  Article 6. Notice of enforcement of the decision on deportation

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the decision on execution of the expulsion sentence from the Court in the case specified in Clause 1 Article 96 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments or from the date of receipt of the notification from the Superintendent of the prison regarding the period during which the convict has completely served the additional penalty of expulsion, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority shall notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, diplomatic missions, consular offices, and other agencies authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen and headquartered in Vietnam, or the representative office of the international organization for which such person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed his/her entry into Vietnam.

  2. In cases where the convict has been granted special amnesty or exemption from serving the prison sentence as an additional penalty of expulsion, within 24 hours from the date of receipt of the decision on special amnesty or exemption from serving the prison sentence, the Superintendent of the prison shall notify in writing the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority where the prison is located to promptly notify the authorities specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 7. Documents for execution of expulsion sentence

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of the court’s judgment execution decision or from the date of receiving the person who has completely served the prison sentence, the person who has been granted special amnesty or exemption from serving the additional penalty of expulsion, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police authority shall prepare documents for execution of the expulsion sentence.

  2. The documents for execution of the expulsion sentence (hereinafter referred to as judgment execution documents) must include all the papers and documents prescribed in Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments. A certificate of completion of other penalties or other obligations prescribed in Point c Clause 2 Article 98 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments includes: a certificate of completion of the prison sentence, special amnesty, exemption from serving the prison sentence, exemption or reduction of fines, decisions on termination or suspension of judgment execution, or certificates or confirmations from competent judgment execution agencies regarding the results of judgment execution or the performance of other obligations.

  3. Upon handing over a person who has completely served their prison sentence, or a person who is granted amnesty or exemption from serving an additional penalty of deportation, to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the prison is located, the superintendent of the prison shall provide copies of the judgment and decision on execution of prison sentence, certificate of completion of prison sentence, certificate of amnesty or exemption from serving prison sentence, and documents proving the fulfillment of other obligations (if any), to prepare the record for deportation enforcement.

  Article 8. Designation of accommodation

  1. Within 03 working days from the date of preparation of the judgment enforcement record, the head of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the record is prepared shall issue a decision designating the place of accommodation for the person subject to deportation pending their exit. The designated accommodation must have a clear address and facilitate management and supervision. It is prohibited to use the detention room or custody cell of the detention center or custody house of the prison to manage the person subject to deportation pending their exit.

  2. In cases where the person subject to deportation falls under one of the circumstances specified in Clause 2 Article 99 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the record is prepared shall issue a decision and arrange for the person subject to deportation to be placed in the accommodation establishment of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as “the accommodation establishment”).

  3. In case the person subject to expulsion or his/her relatives or lawful representative request permission for him/her to stay in a lawful temporary residence in another province or city, a written request shall be sent to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared for consideration and decision. The written request must clearly state the full name and address of the requester, the relationship with the person subject to expulsion, the address of the place of stay, and the commitment to comply with all requirements of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority, and to abide by the law and regulations of the local authority of the place of stay. The written request must be translated into Vietnamese and certified by the diplomatic mission, consular office or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen, or by the representative office of the international organization where such person works, which has its headquarters in Vietnam, and by the commune-level People's Committee or agency or organization where the expelled person comes to stay.

  4. Within 02 working days from the date of issuance of the decision on designation of the place of stay, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall notify the court that issued the judgment enforcement decision, the same-level Procuracy and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.

  Article 9. Management of persons subject to expulsion

  1. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall take charge and cooperate with relevant agencies and local authorities of the province or city where the person subject to expulsion comes to stay in managing such person.

  2. Within 03 working days from the date of issuance of the decision on admission to the accommodation establishment or the decision on designation of the place of stay in another province or city, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall send a notice enclosed with a copy of the judgment enforcement decision to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the accommodation establishment is located or the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the provincial police authority where the person subject to expulsion comes to stay for cooperation in management and taking charge of carrying out procedures for settlement when the person subject to expulsion absconds or dies as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 99 and Article 100 of the Law on Criminal Judgment Enforcement.

  Article 10. Settlement of cases where persons subject to expulsion abscond

  1. During their stay pending exit, if the expelled person absconds, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment is located or the province where the place of accommodation is located shall immediately organize a pursuit and notify such to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared; in case of no result of pursuit, within 7 days from the date on which the expelled person absconds, a pursuit warrant shall be issued as prescribed by law.

  2. In case the absconding expelled person is recaptured or surrenders, the agency that captures or receives the surrender shall make a record and immediately notify such to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared for receiving and taking the person to the accommodation establishment and notify such to the agency that has issued the pursuit warrant for issuing a decision on termination of pursuit as prescribed.

  3. In case the expelled person absconds on escort to the accommodation establishment or the place of exit, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the escort is organized shall organize the pursuit, issue a pursuit warrant and terminate the pursuit.

  4. The criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall immediately notify such to the same-level Procuracy, the Court that issued the decision on execution of the expulsion sentence, the criminal judgment enforcement management agency of the Ministry of Public Security and the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular of the expulsion sentence server’s absconding, recapture or surrender.

  Article 11. Settlement of cases where the person serving the expulsion sentence falls ill or dies

  1. During their stay pending exit at the accommodation establishment, the expelled person may receive medical examination and treatment according to regulations of the Government for persons serving the expulsion sentence and foreigners who have completely served their imprisonment sentences and are staying at the accommodation establishment pending completion of exit procedures. In case the expelled person falls seriously ill and needs to be treated at a health facility of the State, the accommodation establishment or the place of accommodation designated shall immediately notify such to the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the accommodation establishment or the place of accommodation designated is located so that such is notified to the agencies specified in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.

  2. In case the deported person is undergoing emergency treatment or suffers a serious illness and cannot travel and has confirmation from a hospital of the province or higher level, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office managing the deported person at the accommodation place shall designate, and the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the accommodation establishment is located shall notify the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is prepared to issue a written notice to the court that has issued the judgment execution decision for consideration and issuance of a decision to extend the time limit for compulsion to leave the Vietnamese territory.

  3. During the stay period, if the deported person dies, the designated accommodation establishment or place shall immediately report it to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the accommodation establishment or place is located, the investigation agency and the competent Procuracy for determination of the cause of death. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours after the deported person dies, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the deported person dies shall notify the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is prepared to immediately issue a written or telegraphic notice to the court that has issued the judgment execution decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular. After completing the procedures for determining the cause of death and obtaining permission from the competent agency, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office where the accommodation establishment or place is located shall organize the burial.

  In case the deported person dies on the way to the accommodation establishment or the exit place, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office organizing the escort shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial police office, the investigation agency and the competent Procuracy where the deported person dies in, carrying out the settlement procedures as prescribed in Clause 4, Article 99 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments.

  4. Burial expenses for the deceased deported person:

  a/ Burial expenses for the deceased deported person at the accommodation establishment shall comply with the Government's regulations on regime of food, accommodation, clothing, daily-life activities, medical examination and treatment and burial expenses for persons serving the deportation penalty during their stay at the accommodation establishment;

  b/ Expenses for burial of an expelled person who dies during his/her stay at another accommodation establishment shall be applied similarly to those incurred by a deceased expelled person at the accommodation establishment, unless his/her relatives or lawful representative pays them.

  Article 12.- Handling of the receipt of corpses, remains and ashes of expelled persons

  1. In case a deceased expelled person’s relatives or lawful representative request the receipt of his/her corpse for burial, within 48 (forty eight) hours after such person dies, there must be a written request sent to the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department which has compiled the judgment execution dossier for consideration and decision.

  2. The written request must clearly state the full name and address of the corpse-receiving party, the relationship with the deceased expelled person, the place of burial, the commitment to ensure security, order and environmental sanitation and the payment of expenses. The request must be certified by the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which the deceased expelled person is a citizen or the representative office of the international organization where he/she works and must be translated into Vietnamese. If the requester is a Vietnamese citizen, it must be certified by the commune-level People's Committee of the locality where he/she resides. In case of receipt of a corpse for burial in the Vietnamese territory, it is required to obtain the consent of the commune-level People's Committee of the locality where the burial takes place.

  3. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours after receiving the written request, the head of the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department shall decide on the receipt of the corpse and notify in writing the requester, the court that has issued the judgment execution decision and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the receipt or non-receipt of the corpse, when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of the corpse affects security, order and environmental sanitation.

  4. Within 24 (twenty-four) hours after notifying the receipt of the corpse, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department and the requester shall hand over and receive the corpse, money and lawful property of the expelled person (if any) and make a record bearing the signatures of the deliverer, the receiver and the witness; past this time limit, if the requester fails to come and receive the corpse, the criminal judgment execution agency of the provincial-level Police Department shall organize the burial.

  5. The receipt of exhumed remains shall be granted only after 3 years from the date of burial. In case of cremation, the receipt of ashes may be granted after the completion of the cremation. The person requesting the receipt of remains or ashes must send a written request to the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security where the burial takes place for consideration and decision. The written request must contain all the contents specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Within 7 working days from the receipt of the written request, the head of the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security shall decide to grant the receipt of remains or ashes and notify in writing the applicant, the court that issued the judgment enforcement decision, and the agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the grant or refusal of the receipt of remains or ashes when there are grounds to believe that the receipt of remains or ashes affects security, order, and environmental hygiene.

  6. The preservation and transportation of the exhumed remains, corpse, or ashes of the expelled person shall be carried out by the applicant or the diplomatic mission or consular office of the country of which such person is a citizen, or the representative office of the international organization where such person works, and must comply with the provisions of law on medical treatment; environmental hygiene; prevention and control of infectious diseases and border quarantine, and other provisions of Vietnamese law.

  Article 13. Exit permit

  1. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level Public Security where the judgment enforcement dossier is prepared shall examine the passport or a passport substitute paper of the expelled person. In case the passport does not have a visa or the visa has expired, the immigration management agency shall issue an exit visa as prescribed. In case of emergency requiring visa issuance at the border checkpoint, the written request must be sent no later than 12 (twelve) hours before the expelled person arrives at the border checkpoint.

  2. In case the person subject to deportation does not have a passport or a document of substitute value for a passport, the criminal enforcement agency of provincial police authority shall notify in writing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security to request diplomatic missions, consular offices of the country of which the person is a citizen or representative office of international organization where he/she works to issue necessary documents for exit visa procedures.

  In case the requested agency or organization fails to settle or fails to meet requirements but there is a need to immediately carry out deportation due to emergency circumstances or the deadline for leaving the Vietnamese territory has expired as prescribed by law, the criminal enforcement agency of provincial police authority shall request the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security to issue exit documents and request the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to notify in writing the country of which the deportee is a citizen for settlement of entry procedures. In case the country of which the deportee is a citizen refuses to accept or allow entry, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall take charge and cooperate with the immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security and the criminal enforcement agency of provincial police authority in settling through diplomatic channels or carrying out deportation according to international practices.

  3. Where the person subject to expulsion is required to serve another sentence or has not yet served the fine or fulfilled other civil obligations under the case of exit suspension as prescribed by Vietnamese law, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level police authority where the expulsion judgment enforcement record is prepared shall immediately send a report to the Court that issued the judgment enforcement decision to request the extension of the period during which the person is out of Vietnamese territory; cooperate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in notifying and requesting the diplomatic mission, consular office, or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen and headquartered in Vietnam, or the representative office of the international organization for which the person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed the person’s entry into Vietnam to resolve the matter or contact the family of the expelled person or the employer of the expelled person to provide financial support for the execution of the fine or fulfillment of other civil obligations; and concurrently, notify the competent civil judgment enforcement agency to carry out judgment enforcement procedures or to request the competent Court to consider granting exemption from or reduction of the judgment enforcement obligation regarding amounts payable to the state budget in accordance with the Law on Enforcement of Civil Judgments. Upon receipt of a decision or document from the civil judgment enforcement agency on the completion of the judgment enforcement for the expelled person, the criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level police authority shall carry out procedures to compel the expelled person to leave Vietnamese territory.

  Article 14. Compulsory departure from Vietnamese territory

  1. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level police authority where the judgment enforcement record is prepared shall decide on the duration of exit, but it must not exceed the time limit specified in the Court’s judgment enforcement decision or the time limit prescribed by law.

  2. The criminal judgment enforcement agency of the province-level police authority where the judgment enforcement record is prepared shall purchase exit tickets for the expelled person if the expelled person bears the cost or the diplomatic mission, consular office, or other agency authorized to perform consular functions of the country of which the expelled person is a citizen or the representative office of the international organization for which the person works, or the agency or organization that has guaranteed the person’s entry into Vietnam, covers the costs of bringing the expelled person back to his/her country.

  In case of using budget to pay for air tickets, cars, trains or sea ferries for repatriation of persons expelled from Vietnam as prescribed in Article 102 of the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments, the criminal judgment execution management agency of the Ministry of Public Security shall decide to use budget and purchase tickets for means of transport to take repatriating persons out of the Vietnamese territory.

  3. At least 24 (twenty-four) hours before the date of exit, the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is made shall notify the repatriating person, the competent immigration management agency, the Border Guard Command’s Department of Checkpoints and agencies specified in Clause 1, Article 6 of this Circular of the time and place of exit for coordination in repatriation. In case of repatriation by land road to a country sharing a land border with Vietnam, the exit shall not be carried out at night, except for special cases.

  In case the repatriating person has money or legal property deposited at the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office or accommodation establishment, at least 24 (twenty-four) hours before the date of exit, the criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office or accommodation establishment shall return such money or property to him/her. The return of money or property must be recorded in writing, signed by the delivering and receiving parties and witnesses.

  4. The criminal judgment execution agency of provincial police office where the judgment execution dossier is made shall escort the repatriating person to the place of exit; coordinate with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, immigration management agency of the Ministry of Public Security, the Border Guard Command’s Department of Checkpoints in carrying out procedures to force the repatriating person to leave the Vietnamese territory. The execution of repatriation at the place of exit must be recorded in writing, signed by the repatriating person, representatives of agencies participating in the execution of repatriation, diplomatic missions, consular offices of the country of which the repatriating person holds nationality or representative offices of international organizations where he/she works.

  5. In case the person subject to deportation fails to voluntarily serve the deportation order or refuses to leave Vietnam, the criminal enforcement agency of the province where the judgment execution dossier is prepared shall escort him/her to the border gate of the country of which he/she is a citizen; in case of escorting by air, the criminal enforcement agency of the province shall notify and reach agreement with the air-freight company on security measures during the escorting as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 17 of Decree No. 81/2010/ND-CP of the Government on civil aviation security and comply with international agreements on civil aviation security to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  Chapter III

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 15. Effect

  This Circular takes effect on January 1, 2012.

  Article 16. Implementation

  Agencies of the Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of National Defense and Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, within their functions, tasks and powers, organize implementation, guide and inspect implementation of this Circular.

  During the implementation of this Circular, if any problems arise, agencies, units and localities should report them to the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense or the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for timely guidance.-
