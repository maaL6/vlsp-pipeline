                      AMENDMENTS TO CERTAIN ARTICLES OF THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE CODE

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, as amended by Resolution No. 203/2025/QH15;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on amendments to the Criminal Procedure Code No. 101/2015/QH13, as amended by Law No. 02/2021/QH15, Law No. 34/2024/QH15 and Law No. 59/2024/QH15.

  2. To amend and supplement Article 20 as follows:

  “Article 20. Responsibilities for exercise of prosecution rights and supervision of legal compliance in criminal proceedings The People’s Procuracy shall exercise prosecution rights and supervise legal compliance in criminal proceedings, decide on charges, detect violations of law in order to ensure that all criminal acts, offenders, juridical persons committing crimes, and violations of law are detected and handled promptly and strictly; that charges, investigations, prosecutions, adjudications, and judgments are imposed against the right persons, right crimes, and in accordance with law; that no crime or offender, juridical person committing a crime is left unpunished; and that no innocent person is wrongfully convicted.

  The Prosecutor General of the Supreme People's Procuracy shall provide for the jurisdiction of the People's Procuracies at each level over the exercise of prosecution rights and supervision of legal compliance in criminal proceedings in accordance with this Code.”.

  41. Amendments to Article 382:

  "Article 382. Jurisdiction over cassation procedure

  1. The Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court conducts the cassation procedure in the form of a Trial panel composed of five Judges for legally effective judgments/decisions of the Appellate Court of the Supreme People’s Court, provincial People’s Courts and Central Military Court which are appealed against.

  2. The General Assembly of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court conducts the cassation procedure for legally effective judgments/decisions prescribed in clause 1 of this Article which are complicated or judgments/decisions which have been conducted by the Council of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court through the cassation procedure in the form of a Trial panel composed of five Judges but they fail to reach an agreement when voting on the settlement of the case.

  When the General Assembly of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court conducts the cassation procedure, at least two-thirds of its members must attend the trial which is chaired by the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court. The decision of the General Assembly of Justices of the Supreme People’s Court must be approved by more than half of its members; otherwise, the trial shall be adjourned. Within 30 days from the day on which the decision to adjourn the trial is issued, the General Assembly of Justices must resume the trial.

  3. The Committee of Judges of the Central Military Court conducts the cassation procedure for legally effective judgments/decisions of military courts of military zones or regional military courts which are appealed against.
