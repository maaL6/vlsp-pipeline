                                                  CIRCULAR

  GUIDANCE ON ISSUANCE OF VISAS, PERMANENT RESIDENCE PERMITS AND PERMANENT RESIDENCE CARDS FOR FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM UNDER THE EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MINISTRY'S POWERS

  The Circular No. 04/2016/TT-BNG dated June 30, 2016 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs guiding procedures for issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, takes effect from September 1, 2016, and is amended by:

  Circular No. 04/2025/TT-BNG dated June 13, 2025 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs on amendments to Circular No. 04/2016/TT-BNG dated June 30, 2016 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs providing guidelines for procedures for issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Decision No. 2985/2007/QD-BNG dated November 29, 2007 of the Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgating the Regulation on organization and operation of the Fund for protection of overseas Vietnamese citizens and legal entities, which takes effect from July 13, 2025.

  Pursuant to the Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;

  Pursuant to the Law on promulgation of legislative documents dated June 3, 2008;

  Pursuant to Decree No. 58/2013/ND-CP dated June 11, 2013 of the Government defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP dated August 6, 2015 of the Government on mechanism for cooperation among ministries, ministerial agencies and People's Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities in management of entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam;

  The Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgates the Circular providing guidelines for procedures for issuance of visas, issuance of temporary residence cards, and extension of temporary residence for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs [1].

  Chapter I

                                             GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidance on issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs as prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of Law on Immigration of foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Immigration”).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to foreigners under the management of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs who enter and reside in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.

  Chapter II

              POWER AND PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE OF RESIDENCE PERMITS, PERMANENT RESIDENCE PERMITS, AND TEMPORARY RESIDENCE CARDS

  Article 3. Powers of relevant agencies in issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence and issuance of temporary residence cards

  1. The Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as “Consular Department”) shall receive and process applications for issuance of visas or extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration.

  2. The Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs Interpretation, Ministry of Foreign Affairs [3] shall receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration (excluding members of foreign consular missions based in Da Nang city southward and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants).

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City, Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as “the Department of Foreign Affairs of HCMC”):

  a) Receive and process applications/notification for issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration in provinces and cities from Da Nang city southward.

  b) Receive and process applications for issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners who are members of foreign consular missions in provinces and cities from Da Nang city southward and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants.

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions:

  a) Receive applications for visa issuance according to Clause 2 Article 4 of this Circular, passports or passport substitute documents (hereinafter referred to as “passport”), application forms for visa issuance (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 of the Ministry of Public Security on forms related to entry, exit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam - hereinafter referred to as “Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA”) of foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration to transfer information to the Department of Consular Affairs or the Department of Foreign Affairs of HCMC for processing within their jurisdiction.

  b) Issue visas for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration according to notification of the Department of Consular Affairs or the Department of Foreign Affairs of HCMC.

  Article 4. Procedures for processing applications/notification for visa issuance

  1. Agencies and organizations that invite or guarantee entry of foreigners into Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 15 of the Law on Immigration shall directly submit application for visa issuance to Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs
  TP. HCM.

  The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (or other agencies as prescribed by the sending country) of the country where the foreigner is located, who falls under the provisions of Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration, may send the application for visa issuance, along with the passport and the declaration form for visa issuance (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the relevant foreigner to the Vietnamese representative mission in the host country or the concurrent country.

  Vietnam’s overseas representative missions shall, within 1 working day from the date on which they receive all documents mentioned above, forward them to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for processing and issuing visas within their powers.

  2. The application for issuance of a visa includes:

  a) A written notice or diplomatic note requesting visa issuance, specifying full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and symbol, issuance date and expiry date, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa duration, visa type and visa-accepting country of the foreigner.

  b) In case the foreigner is a visitor to a member of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an additional copy of the identity card of the relevant member of the representative mission is required.

  c) In case the foreigner is a contractual employee working at a Vietnamese foreign representative office, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an additional copy of the valid employment contract is required.

  d) In case a foreigner is subject to an increase in the number of staff members of a Vietnamese representative mission, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an official note sent by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country notifying the increase in the number of staff members shall be added.

  3. Handling of application for issuance of visas:

  a) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as the Immigration Department) and the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the issuance of visa to the foreigner specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration within 01 working day from the date of receipt of a complete and valid dossier.

  b)[5] (annulled)

  c) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall send a written notice to the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security within 01 working day from the date of receipt of a complete and valid dossier requesting the issuance of visa to the foreigner specified in Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration.

  The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall reply the agency/organization requesting the issuance of visa and at the same time notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the issuance of visa to the foreigner within 02 working days from the date of notification to the Immigration Department if it does not have any opinion.

  d) The notice sent by the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City to the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the issuance of visa to the foreigner shall include the following information: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and code, date of issue and expiry of the passport, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa validity period, visa type, visa-accepting country of the foreigner and other information (if any).

  4. Issuance of visas:

  a) Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall issue visas to foreigners within 01 working day from receipt of notice specified in point d clause 3 of this Article, passport and visa application form (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the foreigners.

  b) In case a foreigner is approved for issuance of visa at border checkpoint, the receiving agency specified in clause 1 of this Article shall notify the agency or organization that notifies/recommended the issuance of visa of the foreigner’s acceptance of visa at border checkpoint.

  5. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize Director of Consular Department to decide on reciprocal treatment with foreign party regarding processing time as well as components of application for issuance of visa in accordance with Immigration Law.

  Article 5. Procedures for processing application for extension of temporary residence

  1. Foreigners who have been issued with visas marked NG1, NG2 and NG4, if they wish to extend their temporary residence after entering Vietnam, they need to send notice/application for extension of temporary residence to Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City through agencies or organizations that invite and guarantee them to enter Vietnam.

  2. Application for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Written document or diplomatic note of application for extension of temporary residence, which specifies full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, number and symbol of passport, date of issue - expiry date of passport, occupation, position, purpose of entry, duration of application for extension of temporary residence and need for issuance of new visa (if any) of the foreigners.

  b) Passport of the foreigners that is still valid for more than 30 days compared to the time of application for extension of temporary residence.

  c) The application form for issuance of a visa or extension of temporary residence (using Form NA5 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA), bearing confirmation and seal of the inviting or sponsoring agency or organization.

  d) For foreigners entering Vietnam to visit members of foreign representative missions in Vietnam, 1 copy of identity card issued by the Department of State Protocol and External Relations[6] or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City is required.

  e) For foreigners who are contract workers working at foreign representative missions in Vietnam, 1 copy of valid employment contract is required.

  3. Processing applications for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider extending the temporary residence and issue a new visa (if necessary) for the foreigner.

  b) The period of temporary residence to be extended and the period of time for which a new visa is issued (if any) shall be appropriate to the purpose of entry and the request of the inviting or sponsoring agency or organization, and must not exceed 12 months, and must be at least 30 days shorter than the passport validity period of the foreigner.

  c) For those mentioned in Point d Clause 2 of this Article, the period of temporary residence to be extended and the period of time for which a new visa is issued (if any) shall comply with Point b Clause 3 of this Article and the period of identity card of members of foreign representative missions in Vietnam issued by the Department of State Protocol and External Relations[7] or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  4. [8] Foreigners who have entered Vietnam under visa exemption category specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration shall be considered by the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City to extend their temporary residence and issue one or more visas for a period not exceeding the previously issued temporary residence period on the basis of the application submitted as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, unless otherwise provided by international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  5. Director General of Consular Department shall decide on extension of temporary residence and issuance of new visas (if necessary) for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of Law on Immigration who have entered Vietnam via SQ visas.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize Director General of Consular Department to decide on reciprocal agreements with foreign parties regarding extension of temporary residence for foreigners in accordance with Law on Immigration.

  Article 6. Procedures for processing application for temporary residence card

  1. Agencies that invite and guarantee foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of Law on Immigration shall submit application for temporary residence card to competent agencies specified in Article 3 hereof.

  2. Application for temporary residence card includes:

  a) Official note requesting issuance of temporary residence card of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam which specifies information: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, number and symbol of passport, occupation, position of the applicant (refer to Form NA6 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA);

  In case of replacement or addition of new staff of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, the official note requesting issuance of temporary residence card shall specify full name and title of the replaced or added staff;

  b) Declaration requesting issuance of temporary residence card (using Form NA8 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) bearing certification of competent persons and seal of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam;

  c) Valid passports of foreigners;

  d) In case a foreigner is subject to an increase in the staff of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, apart from the documents specified in Points a, b and c Clause 1 of this Article, it is required to supplement a diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country notifying the staff increase.

  3. Granting temporary residence cards:

  Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid dossier as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the State Protocol and External Liaison Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider granting a temporary residence card to the foreigner.

  4. [10] The temporary residence card has the code NG3, which is valid for no more than 05 years and shorter than the remaining duration of the passport by at least 30 days.

  The expired temporary residence card shall be considered for re-granting a new one at the request of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam.

  5. The State Protocol and External Liaison Department and the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall transfer the list of persons granted temporary residence cards to the immigration management agency according to the provisions of Clause 2, Article 7 of Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP of the Government on the mechanism for cooperation among ministries, ministerial-level agencies and provincial-level People's Committees in managing the entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director of the State Protocol and External Liaison Department to decide the reciprocal handling of dossiers and the composition of dossiers applying for temporary residence cards with the foreign party in accordance with the Law on Entry and Exit.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 7. Transitional provisions

  1. Visas, temporary residence certificates and temporary residence cards which are valid before the effective date of this Circular may be used until their expiry dates.

  2. Applications for visas, temporary residence cards or extension of temporary residence submitted before the effective date of this Circular which have not been processed shall be processed by competent agencies of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in accordance with this Circular.

  Article 8. Implementation organization

  1. The Consular Department shall:

  a/ Guide overseas Vietnamese representative missions and relevant agencies in granting visas and extending temporary residence in accordance with this Circular.

  b) Submit annual reports on issuance and extension of visas under this Circular to the Minister of Foreign Affairs.

  2. The Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs shall:

  a) Provide guidance for foreign representative missions in Vietnam and relevant agencies on procedures for issuance of temporary residence cards under this Circular.

  b) Submit annual reports on issuance of temporary residence cards under this Circular to the Minister of Foreign Affairs.

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall submit monthly, quarterly, and annual reports on issuance and extension of visas to the Consular Department; issuance of temporary residence cards to the Department of State Protocol and Foreign Affairs.

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall:

  a) Issue visas according to notices of the Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  b) Submit monthly, quarterly, and annual reports on issuance of visas to foreigners under management of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to the Consular Department.

  Article 9. Entry into force [15]

  This Circular comes into force from September 1, 2016.

  Article 10. Responsibility for implementation

  The Director of Consular Department, Director of State Protocol and Interpretation Department, Director of Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City and Heads of Vietnam representative missions in foreign countries and units affiliated to Ministry are responsible for implementing this Circular. Any problems arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (via Consular Department for visa application and extension procedures; via State Protocol and Interpretation Department for temporary residence card application procedures) for timely guidance./.
