                                                  DECISION

    PROMULGATING PROVISIONS ON THE PERMANENT REINTATEMENT OF FOREIGNERS’ CHILDREN WHO ARE VIETNAMESE ORphans, Orphaned Children or Abandoned Children at Child Care Facilities Managed by the Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs Sector

                                           THE MINISTER’S COUNCIL

  Pursuant to the Law on Organization of the Council of Ministers dated July 4, 1981;
  Pursuant to the December 29, 1986 Law on Marriage and Family;
  Pursuant to the Law on protection, care for and education of children of August 12, 1991;
  At the request of the Minister of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs,

                                                  DECISION

  Article 1.- To promulgate now provisional regulations on the adoption of Vietnamese orphans, abandoned children or disabled children by foreigners at the care establishments under the management of the labor, war invalids and social affairs service, together with this Decision.

  Article 2.- This Decision takes effect as from the date of its signing. All previous regulations contrary to this Decision are now annulled.

  Article 3.- The ministers, the heads of the State committees, the heads of the agencies attached to the Council of Ministers, the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall have to implement this Decision.


                                                                       Phan Văn Khải

                                                                            (Signed)



                                                    PROVISIONS

     STATUS OF PERMITTING FOREIGNERS TO RECEIVE ABANDONED OR ABDUCTED VIETNAMESE CHILDREN WHO ARE ABROAD FROM THEIR FAMILIES, OR WHO ARE ABROAD FROM THEIR FAMILIES AND ABROAD FROM THEIR FAMILIES IN THE PROVINCES OF NATIONALITY, VICTIMS OF WAR INVALIDS AND SOCIAL AFFAIRS
        (Promulgated together with Decision No. 145-HDBT of April 29, 1992 of the Council of Ministers)

  Chapter 1.

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- The adoption of Vietnamese orphans, abandoned children and disabled children by foreigners must be based on the affection between the adoptive parents and the adopted child in the relationship between father and daughter, ensuring that the child is properly nurtured, looked after and educated.

  Article 2.- A foreigner who adopts a child must be twenty years older than the adopted child.

  Article 3.- The adoptees mentioned in Article 1 of this Decree are persons living in the fostering establishments managed by the labor, war invalids and social affairs branch, including:

  - Children aged 15 years or younger, or up to 15 years and disabled.

  - The adopted child may be over 15 years old if the adopter is an old, weak and lonely person.

  Article 4.- The adoption shall be based on the voluntary principle of the adopter, his parents, tutor or director of the child-rearing establishment. If the child is 9 years old or older, his consent is also required.

  Article 5.- The People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government where there is the child-rearing establishment mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation shall consider and grant a certificate of permission for a foreigner to adopt a Vietnamese child within two months after receiving the complete dossier. In case of refusal, the applicant shall be informed of the reason.

  Chapter II

                           CONDITIONS FOR FOREIGNERS TO ADOPT CHILDREN

  Article 6.- Foreigners who apply for Vietnamese children mentioned in Article 1 of this Regulation shall send to the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs the following papers:

  1. The written permission for the adoption issued by the competent State agency of the country of which he/she is a national or of which he/she resides (the written permission must clearly state the legitimate purpose of the application for adoption);

  2. The certificate of the competent State agency of the foreign country certifying the health condition, mental and material capability to ensure the nurturing, education and healthy physical, intellectual and moral development of the adopted child;

  3. The application for adoption shall be sent to the People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government where there is the child-rearing establishment, clearly stating the reason for the application, the applicant's name, age and address (if the applicant has a spouse, both must sign) and the name, age and address of the child whom he/she wishes to adopt, and other requirements.

  In case the name, age and address of the child whom he/she wishes to adopt are not yet known, the foreign applicant may send an application expressing his/her aspiration for adoption and request the Vietnamese Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs to select and recommend him/her.

  The above papers written in foreign languages must be translated into Vietnamese and certified by a Vietnamese notary public.

  Article 7.- The papers prescribed at Points 1 and 2 of Article 6 shall remain valid for six months after they are certified by the competent State agencies of the host country.

  Article 8.- After being notified of the acceptance of the application for adoption or after being authorized by the adopter, the applicant shall have to come to Vietnam and carry out the following procedures with the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs:

  1. To produce the permit for the child to enter the country of the adopter;

  2. To make a commitment to record the child's name as adopted at the local social organization (non-governmental organization sponsored by the State) where the adopter resides;

  3. To make a commitment to annually notify the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs of the child's photograph enclosed with the notice of adoption until the adopter's child reaches full 18 years old according to the set form.

  4. To undertake to rear the adopted child in a proper manner; to assist in his/her health restoration or rehabilitation in order to improve the disabled condition if the adopted child is a disabled child.

  5. To undertake to provide the adopted child with education or vocational training.

  6. To bear all expenses related to the adoption procedures.

  Chapter III

                                            IMPLEMENTATION ORGANIZATION

  Article 9.- The adoption of children shall be effected when the following papers are available:

  1. The certificate of the People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government on the permission for a foreigner to adopt a Vietnamese child;

  2. The record on the hand-over of the adopted child to the foreigner.

  3. The commitments of Article 8 of this Regulation.

  4. The passport of the foreign adopter.

  5. In case the authorized person comes to receive the adopted child, he/she must have a lawful authorization from the adopter.

  Article 10.- The Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall set up a specialized working section with the task of monitoring Vietnamese children adopted by foreigners according to the Provisional Regulations of this Decision.

  Article 11.- The Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall coordinate with the Ministry for Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Justice in guiding the implementation of these Provisional Regulations.

  Article 12. These provisional regulations shall take effect from the date of their promulgation.
