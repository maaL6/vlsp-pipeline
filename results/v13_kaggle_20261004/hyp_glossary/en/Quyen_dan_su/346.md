                                                   DECREE

   THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 77/2001/ND-CP OF OCTOBER 22, 2001, PROVIDING DETAILED REGULATIONS ON MARRIAGE REGISTRATION UNDER THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY’S RESOLUTION NO. 35/2000/QH10 ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE LAW ON MARRIAGE AND FAMILY

                                                   GOV

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization of September 30, 1992;
  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family of June 9, 2000;
  Pursuant to the National Assembly's Resolution No. 35/2000/QH10 of June 9, 2000 on implementation of the Law on Marriage and Family;
  In order to strengthen the State management in the field of marriage and family, ensure the legitimate rights and interests of citizens, and move towards eliminating the non-registered marriage situation, and encourage the establishment of lawful marriage relations; At the proposal of the Minister of Justice,

                                                   DECREES:

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- Scope of regulation

  1. This Decree provides detailed regulations on marriage registration for:

  a/ Couples whose relationship is established before January 3, 1987, the effective date of the 1986 Law on Marriage and Family, but have not yet registered their marriage;

  b/ A man and a woman who cohabit as husband and wife from January 3, 1987, to January 1, 2001, the effective date of the 2000 Law on Marriage and Family, but have not yet registered their marriage.

  2. The provisions in Clause 1 of this Article shall also cover cases where one party is a Vietnamese citizen and the other party is a citizen of a neighbor country bordering Vietnam and has been living stably and for a long time in Vietnam; cases where one party is a Vietnamese citizen and the other party is a stateless person permanently residing in Vietnam or both parties are stateless persons permanently residing in Vietnam.

  Stateless persons permanently residing in Vietnam mentioned in this Clause means those who do not hold Vietnamese nationality and are not recognized by other countries as holding such nationality and are currently working and living stably and for a long time in Vietnam.

  Article 2.- Encouragement of marriage registration and marriage registration obligations

  1. Couples whose relationship is established before January 3, 1987, but have not yet registered their marriage shall be encouraged by the State and given favorable conditions for marriage registration. The time limit for marriage registration in these cases shall not be restricted.

  2. A man and a woman who cohabited as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001 and met all conditions for marriage under the 2000 Law on Marriage and Family shall have the obligation to register their marriage. After January 1, 2003, if they fail to register their marriage, the law will not recognize them as husband and wife.

  Article 3. Recognition of marriage valid day

  Marriage relationship of persons who register their marriage under this Decree shall be recognized from the date on which the partners establish the husband and wife relationship or live together as husband and wife in reality. The date of recognition of marriage shall be clearly written in the Marriage Register and Marriage Certificate under guidance of the Ministry of Justice.

  Article 4.- Exemption from marriage registration fee

  The marriage registration for cases specified in this Decree is exempted from fee.

  Chapter II

                                        MARRIAGE REGISTRATION PROCEDURES

  Article 5.- Competence and procedures for marriage registration

  1. The People's Committee of a commune, ward or township (below referred to as commune-level People's Committee) where either partner registers his/her permanent residence shall perform the marriage registration.

  In case both partners have no permanent residence but register for temporary residence with a definite term according to regulations of law on household registration, the commune-level People's Committee of the locality where either partner registers for temporary residence with a definite term shall perform the marriage registration.

  2. Upon marriage registration, the partners shall only have to fill in the marriage registration form and produce their identity cards or valid substitutes.

  In the marriage registration form, the partners shall write the date of establishment of their husband and wife relationship or cohabitation as husband and wife in reality. In case they cannot determine the date of establishment of their husband and wife relationship or cohabitation as husband and wife in reality, the date shall be calculated as follows:

  If the month is determined but the day is not, the first day of the next month shall be taken;

  If the year is determined but the day and month are not, January 1st of the next year shall be taken.

  Article 6.- Settlement of marriage registration for husband and wife relationship established before January 3, 1987.

  In case a couple resides or temporarily resides with a definite term at the place of their marriage registration, the People's Committee shall perform the marriage registration right after receiving the marriage registration form.

  In cases where one of the partners does not permanently or temporarily reside at the place of their marriage registration, but the People's Committee is aware of their marital status, the marriage registration shall also be processed immediately. When there are unclear circumstances as to whether the partners have committed a monogamous relationship, the People's Committee shall request them to make a written commitment with certification by at least two witnesses regarding the contents of such commitment. The witnesses shall be legally responsible for the accuracy of their testimonies.

  Article 7.- Settlement of marriage registration for cases where a man and a woman cohabit as husband and wife from January 3, 1987 to January 1, 2001

  In case either of the two partners does not have a permanent residence registration or has a temporary residence registered at the place of marriage registration, the application form for marriage registration shall be certified by the agency or unit where he/she is working (if he/she is an official or public employee or an armed forces serviceman) or the commune-level People's Committee of the place where he/she registers his/her permanent or temporary residence. If both partners permanently or temporarily reside at the place of marriage registration, the above certification is not required.

  After receiving a marriage registration declaration, the People's Committee shall check whether both partners are eligible for marriage as prescribed in Article 9 of the 2000 Law on Marriage and Family, and then register their marriage immediately. When there is an unclear circumstance that one or both partners are not eligible for marriage, the People's Committee shall request them to make a written commitment which is certified by at least two witnesses. The witnesses shall be responsible before law for the accuracy of their testimonies.

  Article 8. Places of marriage registration

  In order to create favorable conditions for the marriage partners, the marriage registration may be carried out at the office of the People's Committee or at a village, hamlet or residential quarter according to the plan of the commune-level People's Committee.

  Article 9. Recognition of common children of husband and wife

  Children born before the date of marriage registration in cases specified in Article 1 of this Decree and recognized by their parents are also common children of husband and wife.

  If the father's section in the child's birth register and birth certificate remains blank, the commune-level People's Committee which has registered the birth shall, based on the marriage certificate of the parents, add the father's section in the child's birth register and birth certificate and cross out the remark "child out of wedlock" in the birth register.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 10. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Justice The Ministry of Justice shall assist the Government in directing, guiding and inspecting the implementation of this Decree.

  Article 11. Responsibilities of People's Committees at all levels

  1. Provincial-level People's Committees and district-level People's Committees shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, guide, direct, inspect and urge commune-level People's Committees in implementing this Decree.

  2. Commune-level People's Committees shall:

  a/ Propagate, mobilize, review and make lists of cases subject to marriage registration under this Decree;

  b/ To organize timely, convenient and accurate marriage registration for cases specified in this Decree;

  c/ To accurately and promptly certify the marital status of the marriage partners, if so requested;

  d/ To ensure balance of funds from the State budget according to current regulations for marriage registration activities mentioned in this Decree.

  Article 12.- Implementation effect

  1. This Decree takes effect 15 days after its signing.

  2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of governmental agencies and chairpersons of provincial/municipal People’s Committees shall have to implement this Decree.
