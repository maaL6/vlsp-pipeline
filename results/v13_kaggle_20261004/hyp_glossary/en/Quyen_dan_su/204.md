                                                   DECREE

                                     PROVISIONS ON FAMILY WORK

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;

  Pursuant to the Law on Marriage and Family dated June 9, 2000;

  Pursuant to the Law on Gender Equality dated November 29, 2006;

  Pursuant to the Law on Prevention and Combat of Domestic Violence dated November 21, 2007;

  At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;

  The Government promulgates a Decree prescribing family affairs,

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Decree provides for family affairs; responsibilities of agencies and organizations in performing family affairs.

  Household work means activities of agencies and organizations aimed at building prosperous, equal, progressive, happy and sustainable families.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Decree applies to agencies and organizations in performing family work.

  Article 3. State management agencies in charge of family affairs

  1. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall be responsible before the Government for performing the state management of family affairs nationwide.

  2. The ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall, within the ambit of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in performing the state management of family work.

  3. People's Committees at all levels shall perform the state management of family affairs in their localities.

  Chapter II

                                      FAMILY WORK

  Article 4.- Formulation and organization of implementation of policies and laws on family work

  Formulating and organizing the implementation of policies and laws on families; equality in families; prevention and combat against domestic violence and social evils invading families.

  Article 5.- Planning, training and fostering the contingent of officials engaged in the family work

  1. Formulating and organizing the implementation of plans on the contingent of officials engaged in the family work at all levels.

  2. Training and fostering the contingent of officials engaged in the family work to raise their capacity; compiling documents on the family work.

  3. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Ministry of Home Affairs shall provide detailed regulations on Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 6.- Propagating, mobilizing and disseminating knowledge about the family work

  1. Propagating and disseminating the Party’s policies and the State’s policies and laws on families, equality in families, prevention and combat against domestic violence, and prevention and control of social evils invading families, and practice of family planning.

  2. To develop documents and popularize knowledge about families, mobilize people to get rid of backward customs and practices on marriage and family; inherit, preserve and promote good traditional cultural values of families and clans associated with building advanced values of families in the developing society.

  Article 7. Organizations and agencies providing public services in the field of families

  1. Public services in the field of families shall be provided by public service providers and enterprises.

  2. Conditions, procedures and competence for establishment, reorganization and dissolution of public service providers and enterprises providing public services in the field of families comply with the provisions of the Government’s Decree No. 55/2012/ND-CP of June 28, 2012, prescribing the establishment, reorganization and dissolution of public service providers, the Law on Enterprises and relevant laws.

  Article 8. Activities in support of family building

  1. Provide pre-marriage counseling and education; provide knowledge and skills for building and organizing family life; appropriately integrate contents of family life education into educational programs at all levels; support the maintenance and preservation of good traditional values of families.

  2. Settle family conflicts and disputes and protect legitimate rights and interests of family members.

  3. Develop and guide the replication of models of happy and sustainable families.

  4. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Education and Training, the Ministry of Justice and relevant ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall provide detailed regulations on Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Organization of Commemorating Day of Vietnamese Family

  Every year, the Vietnamese Family Day (June 28) is commemorated in order to promote the responsibility of leaders of all sectors, all levels, mass organizations, social organizations and families to pay attention to building prosperous, equal, progressive, happy and sustainable families; encourage and promptly commend individuals and families with outstanding achievements in family work.

  Article 10.- Inspection and handling of violations of the legislation on family work

  Organize inspection and handling of violations against regulations on family work as prescribed by law.

  Article 11. Scientific research and development of database on families and family affairs

  1. Organize investigation, survey and scientific research activities serving the formulation of policies and laws on family affairs.

  2. Develop a database on families and family affairs.

  3. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall detail Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. International cooperation

  Cooperate with other countries, international organizations, non-governmental organizations in formulating policies, management experience, scientific research, exchange of experts, technical and financial support in the family field as prescribed by law.

  Article 13. Preliminary review, final review, information and reporting regime

  1. People's Committees at all levels shall organize preliminary review, final review, assessment of the implementation of activities related to the family work in localities and report them to superior agencies.

  2. Annually, before December 15, relevant ministries and branches, People's Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall send reports on results of implementation of the family work to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for summarization and report to the Prime Minister.

  Chapter III

                                             IMPLEMENTATION ORGANIZATION

  Article 14. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism

  1. Develop and implement policies and laws on families.

  2. Provide guidance and organize training and advanced training for family affair officials.

  3. Provide guidance and propagate, mobilize, and disseminate policies, laws, and knowledge about family affairs.

  4. Develop and provide guidance on replicating family models.

  5. Perform state management of public service providers in the field of families.

  6. Provide guidance and organize the Commemoration of Vietnam Family Day.

  7. Conduct international cooperation and scientific research on families and family affairs as prescribed by law.

  8. Develop a database on families and family affairs.

  9. Inspect and handle violations against regulations on family affairs as prescribed by law.

  10. Conduct preliminary and final reviews and submit periodic reports on implementation of family affairs to the Prime Minister.

  Article 15. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Planning and Investment

  Taking charge and cooperating with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in guiding and integrating policies on family affairs into socio-economic development strategies, planning and plans.

  Article 16. Responsibilities of Ministry of Finance

  To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and concerned ministries and branches in, allocating funds for family work according to the current regulations on decentralization of the state budget; to guide and inspect concerned ministries, branches and localities in allocating and using funds for family work.

  Article 17. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Justice

  1. To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and relevant agencies in, formulating policies and laws on family work within their assigned functions and tasks.

  2. Guide the work of reconciliation of family conflicts and disputes in accordance with law.

  Article 18. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Education and Training

  To assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in, integrating contents on education about family into educational programs at all levels.

  Article 19. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Home Affairs

  To coordinate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in guiding the implementation of the planning and strengthening of the contingent of family affair officials at all levels.

  Article 20. Responsibilities of the Ministry of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs

  Take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, relevant ministries and agencies in developing policies and laws on protection, care for and education of children in families, care for and support of older persons in families, and prevention and control of social evils invading families within their assigned functions and tasks.

  Article 21. Responsibilities of ministries and ministerial-level agencies

  1. Take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism in implementing contents related to family affairs within their assigned scope, functions, tasks and powers.

  2. Promulgate and submit to competent authorities for promulgation policies, laws, programs and schemes related to family affairs; provide guidance, inspect, examine, make preliminary and final reviews of implementation of policies, laws, programs and schemes under their management related to family affairs.

  3. Annually review implementation of tasks related to family affairs under state management by ministries or sectors and send them to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for consolidation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  Article 22. Responsibilities of People's Committees at all levels

  1. Organize and inspect implementation of policies and laws on family affairs in localities.

  2. Develop and implement family affair programs and plans associated with local socio-economic development programs.

  3. Allocate budget for family affairs in accordance with regulations of law on state budget.

  Article 23. Participation in state management of family affairs at all levels The Women’s Union

  The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Governmental agencies and People's Committees at all levels shall ensure that the Women's Union at the same level participates in the state management of family affairs in accordance with law and Article 4, Article 6, Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of Article 8, Article 9 of this Decree.

  Article 24. Participation in family affairs

  Request the Central Committee of the Vietnam Fatherland Front and its member organizations to participate in propagating, disseminating and educating about policies and laws, inspecting and supervising the implementation of policies and laws on family affairs as prescribed by law.

  Article 25. Funding for family affairs

  1. Funds for family activities of ministries, sectors and people's committees at all levels shall be allocated from the state budget in accordance with law. The estimation and allocation of funds for family activities shall comply with the Law on State Budget and relevant documents.

  2. Funds mobilized by domestic and foreign organizations and individuals for family affairs shall be managed and used in accordance with law.

  Chapter IV

                                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 26. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on February 18, 2013.

  Article 27. Implementation responsibilities

  The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies, the heads of the Government-attached agencies, the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities and the concerned agencies and organizations shall have to implement this Decree.
