                                           CIRCULAR
   GUIDANCE ON ISSUANCE OF LAISSEZ-PASSER, PERMANENT RESIDENCE CARD, PERMITS FOR EXIT FROM VIETNAM AND RESIDENCE SOLUTION FOR FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM
Pursuant to the Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 106/2014/ND-CP dated November 17, 2014 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;
At the request of the Director General of General Department of Security, The Minister of Public Security promulgates a Circular providing guidelines for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits and permanent residence for foreigners in Vietnam.
Chapter I
                                       GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
This Circular provides guidance on issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits, and permanent residence for foreigners in Vietnam within the powers of the Ministry of Public Security.
Article 2. Regulated entities
This Circular applies to foreigners entering and residing in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Chapter II
 PROCEDURES AND POWERS TO RESOLVE APPLICATION FOR VISA, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE, PERMANENT RESIDENCE
Article 3. Procedures and authority for processing visa application
1. Agencies, organizations and individuals that guarantee entry of foreigners into Vietnam under Clause 1 Article 16 of Law on Entry, Exit, Transit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “Law”) shall submit application at Immigration Department.
2. The visa application form made by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 5, 2015, which specifies forms of papers related to entry, exit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam, includes:
a) Form NA2 used for agencies and organizations;
b) Form NA3 used for individuals.
3. Processing visa applications:
a) Immigration Management Department shall consider processing visa applications and reply them to agencies, organizations and individuals as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 16 of the Law;
b) In case of visa application at border checkpoint, Immigration Management Department shall consider processing visa applications as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 16 of the Law.
Article 4. Procedures and competence to process applications for temporary residence cards
1. Foreigners eligible for issuance of temporary residence cards as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 36 of the Law must apply through agencies, organizations or individuals that have directly invited or guaranteed them to carry out procedures at Immigration Management Department or Immigration Management Division of police authorities of provinces or centrally-run cities where agencies, organizations invite or guarantee their head offices to be located or where individuals reside.
2. Application for issuance of temporary residence card as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law. Written application for issuance of temporary residence card specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law using form promulgated by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 on forms related to entry, exit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam including:

                                                                                                    1

a) Forms NA6 and NA8 used for agencies and organizations;
b) Forms NA7 and NA8 are used for individuals.
3. Grant temporary residence cards
Within 5 working days after receiving a complete dossier, the Immigration Management Department or Immigration Management Division of the police office of the province or centrally-run city shall consider granting a temporary residence card.
4. Duration of temporary residence cards shall be issued in conformity with entry purposes and request of agencies, organizations and individuals. Duration of temporary residence cards is between 1 year and 5 years but shorter than duration of passports at least 30 days.
Article 5. Settlement of permanent residence
1. Procedures and powers for settlement of permanent residence shall comply with Articles 41 and 42 of the Law.
2. Determination of the period of temporary residence in Vietnam for foreigners applying for permanent residence:
a/ Foreigners who have temporarily resided in Vietnam for 3 years or more as prescribed in Clause 3, Article 40 of the Law shall be determined on the basis of the entry and exit check stamps issued at border gates, with a total temporary residence duration in Vietnam of 3 years or more in the last 4 years counting until the date of filing their dossiers of application for permanent residence.
b/ Papers evidencing the continuous temporary residence in Vietnam before 2000 as prescribed at Point b, Clause 1, Article 42 of the Law include one of the following papers: certificate of temporary residence; temporary residence register; written explanation of the period of temporary residence in Vietnam, certified by the police of the commune, ward or township where the non-national temporarily resides.
3. Places for submission of dossiers of application for permanent residence settlement:
a/ Persons applying for permanent residence as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 39 of the Law shall submit their dossiers to the Immigration Management Department.
b/ Persons applying for permanent residence as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4, Article 39 of the Law shall submit their dossiers to the Immigration Management Division of the police of the province or centrally-run city where they apply for permanent residence.
Chapter III
 PROCEDURES FOR GRANTING AND RE-GRANTING PERMITS OF EXIT FOR NON-NATIONAL RESIDERS IN VIETNAM
Article 6. Immigration permits
1. International travel documents specified in Point i Clause 1 Article 44 of the Law are immigration permits issued by Immigration Department to stateless persons residing in Vietnam who wish to leave Vietnam (using form NC13 enclosed with this Circular).
2. Immigration permit is valid for 3 years; in case of loss or damage, re-issuance of immigration permit may be considered.
Article 7. Procedures for issuance and re-issuance of immigration permits
1. Persons applying for issuance or re-issuance of immigration permits shall submit 1 set of dossier to Immigration Department or Immigration Management Division of police authority of province/centrally-run city where they reside. A dossier includes:
a) Declaration requesting issuance or re-issuance of immigration permit (using form NC14 enclosed with this Circular);
b) Photocopies of permanent residence cards enclosed with original copies for comparison with persons who have been issued permanent residence cards;
c) Written explanation about period of temporary residence in Vietnam, certified by police of commune, ward or township where stateless persons temporarily reside if they have not been permitted to permanently reside;
d) Issued entry and exit permits, in case of application for re-issuance due to damaged entry and exit permits;
dd) Report on lost entry and exit permit in case of application for re-issuance of lost entry and exit permit (no confirmation by competent authority where it is lost).
2. Issue and reissue entry and exit permits:
a) In case of submission of application at Immigration Department:


                                                                                                       2

Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of a complete application, the Immigration Department shall consider issuing or reissuing an entry and exit permit.
b/ For cases of submitting dossiers at immigration management sections of police offices of provinces or centrally-run cities:
Within 3 working days after receiving a complete dossier, the Immigration Management Division of the police office of the province or centrally-run city shall consider and transfer the dossier of application for issuance or reissuance of an entry and exit permit to the Entry and Exit Management Department. The Entry and Exit Management Department shall consider issuing or reissuing an entry and exit permit within 2 working days.
Chapter IV
                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 8. Transitional provisions
1. The valid entry and exit permits issued before the effective date of this Circular may be used until their expiry dates.
2. Applications for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards; issuance and reissuance of entry and exit permits; permanent residence settlement submitted before the effective date of this Circular which have not been settled shall be considered and settled by immigration management agencies according to regulations in this Circular.
Article 9. Effect
1. This Circular takes effect on August 20, 2015.
2. This Circular replaces the Circular No. 45/2011/TT-BCA dated June 29, 2011 of the Minister of Public Security guiding issuance of entry and exit permits for foreigners permanently residing in Vietnam who do not possess passports.
Article 10. Implementation responsibilities
1. The General Department of Security shall have to guide the police offices of units and localities as well as other concerned units in organizing the implementation of this Circular.
2. The directors general of the departments directly under the Ministry, the directors of the police offices and the fire prevention and fighting police offices of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall have to implement this Circular. Any problems arising in the course of implementation of this Circular should be reported by the police offices of units and localities to the Ministry (through the Immigration Management Department - the General Department of Security) for timely guidance.-
