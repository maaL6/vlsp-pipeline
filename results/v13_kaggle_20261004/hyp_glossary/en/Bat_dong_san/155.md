                                                               CIRCULAR

   REGULATIONS ON LAND FOR CONSTRUCTION OF CULTURAL FACILITIES, MEDICAL FACILITIES, EDUCATION AND TRAINING FACILITIES, SPORTS FACILITIES

  Pursuant to the Law on Land dated November 29, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 21/2013/ND-CP dated March 4, 2013, defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment;

  At the request of Director General of Department of Land Administration and Director of Department of Legal Affairs, Minister of Natural Resources and Environment promulgates Circular on norms for use of land for construction of cultural establishments, medical facilities, educational and training facilities, and sports facilities.

  Article 1. To promulgate together with this Circular norms for use of land for construction of cultural establishments, medical facilities, educational and training facilities, and sports facilities.

  Article 2. This Circular takes effect from March 30, 2017.

  Article 3. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Governmental agencies, People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities, heads of units under the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment and relevant organizations and individuals shall implement this Circular./.

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides for land use rates applicable to land for construction of cultural facilities, health facilities, education and training facilities, sports facilities and cultural works classified as community land (excluding cultural facilities, health facilities, education and training facilities, sports facilities managed by the Ministry of National Defense or the Ministry of Public Security).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This rate shall apply to regulatory agencies, specialized agencies in charge of natural resources and environment, relevant organizations and individuals involved in preparation, appraisal and approval of provincial and district-level land use planning and plans; appraisal of land use demand upon land allocation, land lease or land repurposing for construction or expansion of the scale of use of land for cultural, health, education and training, sports facilities and cultural works classified as community land.

  Article 3. Interpretation of terms

  1. Land use quota by works means the land area necessary for a work to fully utilize its functions and to use land effectively for a specific purpose.

  2. Land use quota per capita means the land area calculated in square meters for each person to fully utilize his/her capacity in one field and to use land effectively for a specific purpose. The land use quota per capita is prescribed according to the administrative units at commune, district or provincial level. The land use quota per capita is calculated according to the total population of each administrative unit.

  3. Communal cultural centers; village cultural houses; village meeting rooms, head offices and clubs are cultural works classified as community land specified in Circular No. 28/2014/TT-BTNMT dated June 2, 2014 of the Minister of Natural Resources and Environment on land statistics, land inventorying and land use mapping.

  Chapter III

                                          APPLICATION OF LAND USE QUOTAS

  Article 8. Land use quota for a work shall be determined by the minimum and maximum land area of that work corresponding to each administrative division; if there is a work of a higher level in the same administrative division, the land type corresponding to that work shall be added to the land area of that work.

  Article 9.- The land use quota per capita shall determine the minimum and maximum land area for each person in each specific region and corresponding to each administrative level; if there is a work of a higher level in such administrative level, the land type corresponding to such work shall be added to the land area of such higher-level work. The land use quota per capita shall serve to determine the total area of a land type according to each administrative unit.

  Article 10.- Depending on local conditions, one or more works may be built but the total land area of each work must not exceed the maximum area determined according to the population size of the locality.

  Article 11. In case a population is low but total area of a land type per capita of an administrative unit is too small, the minimum land area of the land use quota for the project may be applied.

  Article 12.- Difficulties that arise during the implementation of this Decree should be promptly reported to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment for consideration and settlement./.
