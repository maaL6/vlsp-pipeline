                                                    TELECOMMUNICATION

   ON IMPLEMENTATION OF DECISION NO. 25/2023/QD-TTG DATED OCTOBER 3, 2023 OF THE PRIME MINISTER ON REDUCTION IN LAND RENTS FOR 2023.

                                 THE DIRECTOR GENERAL OF THE ELECTRICITY TAX OFFICE

                        - Tax Departments of the provinces and centrally-run cities;
                        - Departments and Units under the General Department of Taxation.
  On October 03, 2023, the Prime Minister issued Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg on reduction of land rents for 2023. In order to promptly implement the contents specified in the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg, the General Tax Department requests the Directors of Tax Departments of provinces and centrally-run cities to direct Tax Departments and Sub-Departments to, based on the contents specified in the Prime Minister’s Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg, to promptly carry out the following tasks:

  1. Tax authorities at all levels shall widely and thoroughly disseminate the content of the policy on reduction of land rent as prescribed in Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg on reduction of land rents in 2023 to organizations, units, enterprises, households and individuals that are directly leased land by the State under land lease decisions or land lease contracts or certificates of land use rights and ownership of houses and other property on land issued by competent state agencies in form of annual land rent payments, specifically:

  - In each tax area, tax authorities shall proactively cooperate with press agencies, radio and television stations, branches/representative offices of Vietnams Customs, etc. in organizing communication and dissemination of the contents of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg.

  - Develop documents guiding implementation of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg, post these documents on the website of the Department of Taxation, and send them via email to organizations, units, enterprises, households, and individuals directly leasing land from the State under the Land Lease Decision or Land Lease Contract or Certificate of land use rights, ownership of houses and other property on land issued by competent state agencies in form of annual payment.

  - Information dissemination must be regular and continuous in various forms, ensuring taxpayers have access to and fully understand the scope, eligible taxpayers, deadline, procedures, and benefits when implementing the policy on land rent reduction so that taxpayers can comply with and promptly implement state support policies and resolve financial difficulties for taxpayers.

  2. Process applications for land rent reduction according to Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister.

  Based on the 2023 land rent application submitted by the land lessee according to Article 4 of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister, the tax authority shall determine the amount of land rent eligible for reduction and issue a decision on land rent reduction as prescribed by law on collection of land rents. At the same time, consolidate the situation of land rent reduction under Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg within provinces/cities, update information on land rent reduction of taxpayers, the amount of land rent payable in 2023, and adjust the corresponding late payment interest (if any) on the system. In case the land lessee has paid the land rent in 2023 but there is an excess amount after the competent authority determines and decides the land rent reduction, the excess amount shall be deducted from the land rent of the subsequent period or the next year according to the provisions of law on tax administration and other relevant laws. In case there is no subsequent period for land rent payment, make offsetting or refund the excess amount according to the provisions of law on tax administration and other relevant laws.

  3. The head of the tax authority shall assign relevant departments within the tax authority to update, search, review, and inspect the dossiers, and expedite implementation as prescribed by law. In case it is detected that the taxpayer does not meet the conditions for land rent reduction as prescribed, notify the taxpayer to pay according to the current regulations of law on land rent (not implemented according to Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister).

  4. The Tax Department shall consolidate and submit a report on the implementation of Decision No. 25/2023/QD-TTg of the Prime Minister (according to the appendix/form enclosed therewith), specifically: the implementation status, the amount of land rent reduction, the number of applications for reduction, the number of applications already reduced, the number of pending applications (if any) and the reasons for failure to reduce them, difficulties and proposals (if any) to the Department of Policy - General Tax Department before April 30, 2024 at the address of the General Tax Department, 123 Lò Đúc, Hai Bà Trưng, Hà Nội, and send the soft copy via email: nbngoc01@gmail.com.

  The General Tax Department requests the Directors of the Tax Departments to seriously implement this Decision. In case of any actual difficulty arising, promptly report it to the General Tax Department for guidance on resolution./.
