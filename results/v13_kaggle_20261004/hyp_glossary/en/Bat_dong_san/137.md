                                                          PLAN

     IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PRIME MINISTER’S DIRECTIVE NO. 11/CT-TTG DATED APRIL 23, 2019 ON A NUMBER OF MEASURES TO SUPPORT THE STABLE AND HEALTHY DEVELOPMENT OF THE REAL ESTATE MARKET
      (Enforced together with Decision No. 398/QD-BXD dated May 22, 2019 of the Minister of Construction)

  I. OBJECTIVES

  - Synchronously, promptly and effectively implement Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019 of the Prime Minister on solutions to promote the stable and healthy development of the real estate market (hereinafter referred to as Directive No. 11/CT-TTg).

  - Strengthening housing and real estate market management institutions in a timely manner and in line with practical situations; effectively implementing social housing programs: supporting housing for people with meritorious services to the revolution; supporting housing for poor households in rural areas, flood-prone areas of central Vietnam, regularly flooded areas of the Mekong Delta; housing for low-income people in urban areas, housing for workers in industrial parks, and housing for students, etc.; strengthening guidance on inspection and examination of housing and real estate market management.

  II. PLAN DETAILS

  1. Regarding the task of completing projects of the Law on Amendments to the Law on Construction, the Law on Housing and the Law on Real Estate Business:

  1.1. To study and finalize the draft Law Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Construction Law.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Construction Management;

  - Coordinating units: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Deadline for completion: Submitting to the Government in July 2019; finalizing and submitting to the Government for submission to the National Assembly in October 2019.

  1.2. Studying and finalizing the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Housing regarding support policies for housing for officials, public servants and employees facing housing difficulties according to Resolution No. 26-NQ/TW of the XIIth Central Committee;

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Deadline for completion: Expected in 2020 (after the Politburo approves the housing scheme for officials, public servants and employees in the political system and issues a resolution on this issue).

  1.3. Studying and finalizing the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Real Estate Business

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Completion time limit: According to the program for law development of the National Assembly.

  2. Research and promulgate amendments to construction regulations and standards applicable to apartment buildings, condominium buildings, resort villas, officetels, guest houses, and dormitories.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Science, Technology and Environment;

  - Executing unit: National Architecture Institute;

  - Completion time limit: Complete in 2019.

  3. Research and propose promulgation of legislative documents on regulations on management and operation of officetels.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Legal Department and related units;

  - Completion time: 3rd quarter of 2019.

  4. To urge localities to complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  4.1. To urge localities to complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: Information Center, Construction Economics Institute;

  - Completion time limit: Regular tasks.

  4.2. To coordinate with ministries and sectors in collecting relevant information for completing the housing and real estate market information system

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The General Department of Statistics - the Ministry of Planning and Investment;

  - Completion time limit: Regular tasks.

  5. Research, amend policies and laws, and expedite social housing development

  5.1. Research and amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing;

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Legal Department and related units;

  - Completion time limit: after the Law on amendments to the Law on Housing is approved by the National Assembly.

  5.2. Urging and guiding local governments to focus on promoting social housing development in accordance with the direction of the Prime Minister in Directive No. 03/CT-TTg dated January 25, 2017.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Local Departments of Construction and relevant units;

  - Time limit for completion: monthly regular work.

  5.3. Inspecting and reviewing planning and use of urban land reserved for social housing development in key localities, consolidating the situation and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Local Departments of Construction and relevant units;

  - Completion deadline: September 2019.

  6. Tasks of cooperating with ministries, central authorities and local governments in strengthening inspection and requiring project developers of urban area projects and housing development projects to synchronously build technical infrastructures, social infrastructures, fire prevention and firefighting systems in projects and connect with general infrastructure of the area before transferring houses to people as prescribed by construction laws, housing laws and relevant laws.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Cooperating units: Construction Inspectorate, Department of Urban Development and relevant units;

  - Completion deadline: 2019.

  7. Tasks of regularly monitoring and summarizing the situation periodically or irregularly for reporting to the Government and Prime Minister on developments of the real estate market in order to promptly take measures to stabilize the market when there are signs of abnormal or large fluctuations.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Cooperating units: Institute of Construction Economics and relevant units;

  - Time limit for completion: quarterly and irregularly at request.

  III. Implementation organization

  1. Task assignment:

  The Minister of Construction shall take charge, direct and manage the implementation of the Plan on a number of solutions to promote the stable and healthy development of the real estate market;

  The Ministers shall, according to their respective fields of work, assign and direct units to implement the contents in the Plan;

  The heads of units under the Ministry shall, based on the Ministry's implementation plan, organize the implementation of assigned tasks, ensuring timeliness and effectiveness; clearly assign tasks to leaders and specialists responsible for each specific task of the unit; focus on directing, strengthening inspection and supervision of progress and results of implementation of the Plan; report on the implementation of the Plan according to their assigned functions and tasks to the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management for consolidation and reporting to the Ministry's leadership.

  The Director of the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry Office in monitoring, consolidating and reporting on the results of implementation of the Plan; promptly report to the Ministry's leadership on handling difficulties arising during the implementation.

  2. Implementation funding

  Departments of Planning and Finance, Science, Technology and Environment shall balance and allocate funding for performing tasks according to the estimates of units assigned to perform tasks from annual sources of economic and scientific and technological services and other lawful funding sources as prescribed by law.

  Units assigned to perform tasks shall prepare estimates for performance and send them to the Departments of Planning and Finance, Science, Technology and Environment for reporting to the leadership of the Ministry for consideration and approval.

  During the implementation of the Plan, in case of amendments or supplements to specific tasks, units shall take the initiative in proposing and reporting to the Minister for consideration and decision./.
