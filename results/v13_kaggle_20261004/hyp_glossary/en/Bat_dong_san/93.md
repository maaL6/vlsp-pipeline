                                                      DIRECTIVE

     REGARDING A NUMBER OF SOLUTIONS TO SUPPORT THE REAL ESTATE MARKET FOR SAFE, HEALTHY AND STABLE DEVELOPMENT

  Over the past period, the world's political and economic situation has been characterized by complex, unstable and unpredictable changes, affecting Vietnam's economy with a very large opening. In this context, the Government has issued many directives aimed at stabilizing macroeconomics, controlling inflation, ensuring major balances, helping the economy to recover and achieve relatively high growth rates compared to other countries in the region and in the world.

  On October 11, 2021, the Government promulgated Resolution No. 128/NQ-CP on safe, flexible and effective control of COVID-19; Resolution No. 01/NQ-CP dated January 8, 2022 on major tasks and solutions for implementation of the socio-economic development plan and the state budget estimate for 2022; Resolution No. 02/NQ-CP dated January 10, 2022 on major tasks and solutions for improvement of business environment and enhancement of national competitiveness in 2022; Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP dated January 30, 2022 on Resolution on the socio-economic recovery and development program and implementation of Resolution No. 43/2022/QH15 of the National Assembly on fiscal and monetary policies supporting the Program. In particular, the 13th Central Committee of the Communist Party has promulgated Resolution No. 18-NQ-TW on further reform, improvement of institutions and policies, enhancement of the effectiveness and efficiency of land management and use, and promotion of Vietnam’s development into a high-income developed country.

  The active implementation by authorities, sectors, localities and enterprises of the resolutions of the Communist Party and the State, regulations of law, and the direction, management, solutions and policies of the Government on the real estate market have led to positive changes in socio-economic situations, ensured social security and macroeconomic stability.

  The real estate market plays a very important role in maintaining macroeconomic stability, controlling inflation, ensuring balance and promoting growth. However, despite the positive results, the development of the real estate sector has also revealed many problems, limitations and shortcomings, and is not truly sustainable, with many risks remaining, such as: the structure of real estate goods is unreasonable, real estate product prices, especially housing prices, are still quite high compared to people's incomes, the information database on the real estate market is not synchronized, complete, reliable or transparent...

  Some problems and shortcomings that need to be addressed include: The legal system related to the real estate market still has some contents that have not yet met practical needs and have not been promptly amended or supplemented; the development of real estate projects in some localities does not follow the planning and plans and is not suitable for market demand; the investment procedures are prescribed in different legal documents through multiple steps, resulting in a prolonged time for implementing investment procedures; regulations on mandatory real estate transactions via the platform have created the basis for the formation of "ghost projects" and customer fraud cases, while the State also faces many difficulties in managing information about the real estate market, leading to tax evasion and loss of revenue for the state budget; state management is not strict, timely, or satisfactory...

  In order to address the above-mentioned problems and shortcomings and continue to promote the safe, healthy, and sustainable development of the real estate market in the future, the Prime Minister requests:

  I. A NUMBER OF GENERAL CONCERNS AND SOLUTIONS

  1. Review laws to resolve immediate and long-term issues in the direction of developing sound, appropriate, and effective policy instruments to ensure interconnection, safety, and certainty between capital markets and the real estate market, and diversify capital sources for the real estate market; promote decentralization and delegation at all levels, conduct thorough research, and propose pilot policies to create new momentum and force for market development.

  2. Persistently, firmly, and decisively develop a safe, healthy, and sustainable real estate ecosystem; do not tighten credit unreasonably but strengthen inspection and supervision, and do not relax state management; do not criminalize economic - civil relationships; proactively detect, prevent, and strictly handle violations in accordance with the law, paying attention to acts of tax evasion in real estate business, and do not let any collapse occur, protecting those who act correctly, protecting and harmonizing the interests of the State, the people, and enterprises.

  3. Harmonize, reasonably and effectively combine fiscal policies and monetary policies, implement monetary policies in a flexible, safe and cautious manner, expand fiscal policies in a reasonable manner, focus on ensuring effectiveness, strictly manage prices and markets; do not implement policies abruptly, do not suddenly change from “loosening” to strict control or vice versa.

  4. Ministries, sectors and local governments shall focus on synchronously implementing solutions prescribed in Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on the socio-economic rehabilitation and development program.

  5. Focus on implementing solutions to attract investment, promote production and business activities, create more jobs in various fields and localities; stabilize macroeconomics, control inflation, ensure major balance of the economy.

  6. Specifically and accurately assess the situation and supply-demand relationship to develop the real estate market on the basis of respecting the market law and parallel competition law, promote the regulatory role of the State, policy tools to intervene and control when necessary in a proper, timely, effective manner, prevent manipulation of the market, hoarding of goods, price hikes, etc.

  7. Identify market segments and demands such as industrial real estate, worker housing, low-income housing to develop appropriate policies to promote the market, focus on social security, create opportunities for people with low incomes, “not sacrificing social progress and equality for mere economic growth”.

  8. Develop strategic infrastructure to create new development space, new development driving force, promote the formation of new industries, services and urban areas.

  9. Strengthen inspection, examination and audit to resolve difficulties and handle violations to protect the market, property, and people, do not legalize violations but objectively evaluate, understand causes, and have mechanisms to resolve practical difficulties.

  10. Do a good job in communication, adopt appropriate and effective forms, promote education and training in financial knowledge and legal knowledge for the people, in schools and other forms.

  II. SOME SPECIALIZED TASKS AND SOLUTIONS

  1. Ministry of Construction

  a) Expeditiously study and complete the legal system related to real estate to ensure consistency, resolve difficulties and obstacles, create conditions for the real estate market to develop stably and healthily, build a real estate market information system associated with land information, improve legal basis and increase implementation of non-cash payment in real estate transactions. Accordingly, propose the National Assembly to give opinions and approve the Law on Housing (amended) and the Law on Real Estate Business (amended) to create a legal framework for real estate business activities, enhance the effectiveness of real estate market management; expedit the amendment and supplementation of Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing, Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP dated April 01, 2021 of the Government on amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing in accordance with the Law on Housing.

  b) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries and central authorities in studying and proposing the promulgation of regulations guiding the implementation process of housing projects and urban areas.

  c) Regularly monitor the situation and development of the real estate market; submit quarterly reports on the real estate business and real estate market nationwide to the Prime Minister and propose solutions for the stable and healthy development of the real estate market.

  d) Inspect and examine the implementation of laws on real estate business, real estate transaction center operation, and real estate brokerage; cooperate with provincial People's Committees in organizing inspections and reviews of housing and real estate business projects to propose competent state agencies to revoke, suspend, temporarily stop, adjust, convert, or transfer real estate business projects as prescribed by law.

  dd) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in establishing working groups to direct, guide, and urge local governments to implement Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on the program for socio-economic rehabilitation and development regarding policies supporting social housing development, worker housing in industrial parks, and renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings.

  e) Direct, guide, and cooperate with local governments in reviewing and supplementing planning and allocating land fund for social housing and worker housing development in accordance with regulations of law, strictly complying with regulations on allocation of 20% land fund in housing projects and urban areas, ensuring demand for social housing and worker housing development in their areas.

  g) Direct, guide, and cooperate with local governments in reviewing, preparing, and announcing list of project developers of social housing, worker housing, and renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings eligible for loan packages with interest rates of 2% via commercial banking system.

  h) Take charge and cooperate with relevant ministries in researching and proposing policies to promote development of social housing and worker housing in industrial parks.

  i) Improve and implement public information systems regarding housing and real estate market.

  2. Ministry of Finance

  a) Urgently amend Decree No. 153/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020 of the Government on sale and transaction of private corporate bonds on domestic market and sale of corporate bonds on international market.

  b) Control capital mobilization activities of real estate enterprises on stock market to avoid speculation, manipulation, and price manipulation.

  c) Cooperate with the State Bank of Vietnam in inspecting and supervising compliance with laws on issuance, investment and provision of services regarding corporate bonds, especially issuance of separate bonds of real estate enterprises, credit institutions related to real estate enterprises, enterprises with large issuance volume, high interest rates, loss-making enterprises, issuing enterprises having no collateral.

  d) Direct and guide implementation and capital mobilization activities (including bond issuance) of real estate trading enterprises on the securities market in accordance with law; create conditions for, not obstruct enterprises (with sufficient capacity, good and healthy business results, etc.) to mobilize capital to support recovery and development.

  dd) Research and improve tax policies suitable to reality for real estate in order to encourage effective use of houses and land, contribute to limiting real estate speculation, ensure reasonable and stable revenues for the state budget, suitable to socio-economic conditions of Vietnam and international practices.

  3. Ministry of Planning and Investment

  a) Direct, urge and guide localities, propose solutions to difficulties and obstacles, resolutely reduce unnecessary administrative procedures in investment procedures, investment policy approval, investment decisions and issuance of certificates of investment registration of housing and real estate projects to support and increase supply for the market.

  b) Monitor and promote attraction of foreign investment in the field of real estate trading.

  4. Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment

  a) Cooperate with the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Justice in researching, reviewing and amending current regulations of law related to land use rights auction (law on auction, law on land, law on tax administration) to ensure consistency and conformity with actual situation in localities.

  b) Inspect and inspect, and direct local governments to prevent and strictly handle violations in land use rights auction activities in accordance with law.

  c) Inspect and review indicators of land use planning and plans to ensure appropriate land use planning for various industries, professions and fields in a harmonious and rational manner, avoiding overlapping and disputes.

  5. The Ministry of Public Security shall closely and effectively cooperate with the Ministry of Finance, State Bank of Vietnam and relevant agencies in enhancing macro-economic situation monitoring; take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Construction and the Ministry of Finance in strictly handling illegal real estate trading floors and real estate brokerage activities, tax evasion, providing inaccurate information, causing negative impacts on financial, credit and real estate market activities.

  6. The Ministry of Information and Communications shall direct news and press agencies to enhance the provision of timely, official, accurate and complete information on the real estate market, securities market and bond market; strictly handle acts of providing inaccurate information, causing negative impacts on the operation of financial markets, credit markets and real estate markets.

  7. State Bank of Vietnam

  Strictly monitor credit in real estate sector. Direct credit institutions to strictly control credit quality and grant loans in real estate sector in accordance with regulations of law; promptly consider prioritizing loans for social housing, worker housing, affordable commercial housing with high efficiency and capable of being repaid.

  8. The People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities

  a) Expeditiously organize the formulation, appraisal and approval of urban and rural construction planning, land use planning and plans; publicly disclose information on construction planning, lists and progress of infrastructure development projects and real estate projects, especially large-scale ones, as well as the merger, establishment or upgrading of local administrative units, thereby preventing rumors and speculation to push up prices for illegal profit-seeking purposes.

  b) Expeditiously develop housing development programs for the period from now until 2025 and 2030 and 5-year and annual housing development plans in accordance with the Law on Housing, which clearly define lists of housing projects serving as the basis for approving investment policies of housing projects in the area.

  c) Review and produce lists of local housing and real estate projects; specifically assess reasons and causes for projects approved for investment policies but not yet implemented or delayed implementation. On this basis, focus on resolving legal difficulties and procedures for preparing investment in housing projects to increase supply for the market.

  d) Organize public disclosure of lists of projects, land funds, and procedures for developing local housing and real estate to attract investment; facilitate domestic and foreign investors to easily access and participate in housing and real estate investment.

  dd) Focus on directing and organizing implementation of land allocation, selection of project developers for new real estate projects and housing projects to increase supply for the market.

  e) Promote implementation of land use right auctions; bidding for land-based projects to select investors to increase supply of commercial housing projects for the real estate market.

  g) Implement solutions to promote development of social housing and worker housing in the area:

  - Review and supplement planning and allocate land fund for social housing and worker housing development in accordance with the law, strictly implement regulations on land fund allocation of 20% in housing projects and urban areas, and ensure demand for social housing and worker housing development in the area.

  - Focus on implementing Resolution No. 11/NQ-CP of the Government on the program for socio-economic recovery and development, which urgently produces lists of projects, reviews land fund, completes legal procedures, selects project developers for implementation of social housing, worker housing in industrial parks, renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings in the area.

  - Review eligible entities and eligibility on which basis to produce and publish list of investors in social housing, worker housing, renovation and reconstruction of old apartment buildings eligible for loan package with 2% interest via commercial bank system according to Decree on support for interest rates from state budget for loans granted by enterprises, cooperatives, business households.

  - Cooperate with Vietnam Bank for Social Policies in reviewing and consolidating demand of individuals, households borrowing to purchase, lease-purchase social housing, housing for workers; build, renovate, repair houses according to Article 16 of Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP, Clause 10 Article 1 of Decree No. 49/2021/ND-CP and relevant documents, ensuring accuracy and reflecting actual demand of people.

  h) Expeditiously complete quality inspection and assessment of remaining old apartment buildings in the area to determine apartment buildings that must be deconstructed; promptly produce and approve plans, list of apartment building renovation and reconstruction projects according to Decree No. 69/2021/ND-CP.

  i) Control, detect, prevent and strictly handle violations in land use right auction activities in accordance with the law.

  k) Adopt measures to manage and prevent separation, “zoning division, land sale” in areas where investment is not permitted or lacks infrastructure; strengthen control of operation of real estate trading floors, organizations, individuals engaging in real estate brokerage; rectify handover and “pushing prices” causing disturbance to real estate market information.

  l) Review projects using land but delayed implementation, left to decay, used for wrong purposes, wasted land; on which basis cooperate with relevant agencies in considering, guiding handling within their competence and according to regulations of land laws, other relevant laws to effectively use land fund, develop in a manner suitable for actual demand of society.

  m) To closely monitor, promptly grasp information and developments of the market and take timely measures to stabilize the market and prevent price spikes and real estate bubbles in their localities.

  n) To intensify inspection and examination and strictly handle violations against laws on real estate business, land, construction and relevant laws (if any).

  9. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall, within the ambit of their assigned functions and tasks, thoroughly understand and strictly organize the implementation of this Directive; and at the same time send biannual and annual reports to the Ministry of Construction for summarization and reporting to the Prime Minister.

  10. The Ministry of Construction shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Government Office and concerned agencies in, guiding, urging and inspecting the implementation of this Directive, and summarize and report it to the Prime Minister.
