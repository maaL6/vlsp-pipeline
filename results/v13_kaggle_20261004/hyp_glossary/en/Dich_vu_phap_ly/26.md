                                            CIRCULAR
GUIDANCE ON CALCULATION OF TIME SPENT IN PRACTICAL WORK AND BUDGET FOR LEGAL AID
Pursuant to the Law on Legal Aid dated June 20, 2017;
Pursuant to Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 of the Government providing detailed regulations on the Law on Legal Aid;
Pursuant to Decree No. 96/2017/ND-CP dated August 16, 2017 of the Government defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Justice;
At the request of Director of Department of Legal Aid;
The Minister of Justice promulgates Circular guiding calculation of working hours and payment of legal aid expenses.
Chapter I
                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope and regulated entities
1. This Circular provides guidelines on calculation of working hours and payment of legal aid fees as a basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for participation in legal proceedings or extrajudicial representation.
2. This Circular applies to the following entities:
a) Legal aid assistants;
b) Lawyers providing legal aid services;
c) Organizations signing contracts for provision of legal aid services;
d) State legal aid centers (hereinafter referred to as “centers”) and their branches (hereinafter referred to as “branches”); Departments of Justice and other relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Article 2. Principles
1. Ensure compliance with regulations of law on remuneration, allowances and procedures for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling of legal aid-related cases by persons providing legal aid services or organizations signing contracts for provision of legal aid services.
2. Ensure legality and rationality in declaring time and work as basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling of legal aid-related cases.
3. When declaring time and work for handling of legal aid-related cases, declarers shall be responsible for authenticity of their declarations.
4. When verifying time and work of persons providing legal aid services, verifiers shall be responsible for authenticity of their verification.
Chapter II
               CALCULATION OF TIME IN PERIODS OF PRACTICAL WORK
Article 3. Time in periods of practical work
1. Time in periods of practical work (half a working day) shall apply to legal aid-related cases in the form of participation in proceedings or extra-procedural representation selected by persons providing legal aid services as basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling of cases.
2. A working session shall be counted on the basis of 4 working hours. In case the legal aid-providing person works less than 4 hours, it shall be counted as follows:
a) The remaining time shall be counted as half of a working day if the number of hours is not divisible by 3;
b) It is considered as 01 working day if the number of hours of work is odd and at least 03 hours.

3. When applying the method of calculating working hours, legal aid-providing persons shall record their tasks and time of performing such tasks in the Schedule of actual working hours (Form TP-TGPL-01) enclosed with this Circular.
Article 4. Time for criminal proceedings

1. The time for legal aid in criminal proceedings shall be determined as follows:
a) Participate in interrogation of suspects or take statements from accused persons, crime victims, witnesses and litigants in criminal cases;
b) Participate in confrontation, voice identification and recognition, scene examination, autopsy, inspection of traces on the body, experimental investigation, and other activities to participate in legal proceedings as required by the presiding authorities;
c) Working with proceeding officers and agencies;
d) Verify, collect and assess relevant documents, items and evidence according to stages of legal proceedings;
dd) Work with persons facing charges in detention cells of border guard stations, detention centers or prisons;
e) Meet with persons facing charges on bail or their relatives; crime victims or their relatives; witnesses, litigants in criminal cases and other participants in legal proceedings;
g) Study files, make copies and prepare documents at presiding authorities or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts for provision of legal aid;
h) Prepare defense arguments;
i) Attend court sessions. If a legal aid provider attends a court session but the trial is postponed not at the request of the legal aid provider and no notice is given, the time during which the legal aid provider works with presiding authorities or proceeding officers on matters related to the postponement of the court session shall be included in such time;
k) Perform tasks related to procedures for appeal against first-instance judgments, appellate judgments, cassation procedures or reopening procedures;
l) Perform other reasonable tasks as prescribed by law to serve the defense and protection.
2. Remuneration and allowances for legal aid-providing persons performing tasks specified in Points e, g, h, l Clause 1 of this Article shall not exceed the number of sessions of such tasks according to the corresponding case assignment levels in Appendix No. 01 enclosed with this Circular.
3. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours of activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or proceeding agencies;
b) Working hours of activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or proceeding agencies or persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
c) Working hours of activities specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where legal aid-providing persons are directly working;
d) Working hours of activities specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by accused persons or proceeding agencies or officers of border guard stations, custody centers, detention camps or prisons;
dd) Working hours of activities specified in Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
e) Working hours of activities specified in Point g Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by proceeding officers or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts on provision of legal aid services;
g) The time of performing activities specified in Points h, k and l Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the center or its branches or organizations signing contracts for legal aid services;
h) The time of performance of activities specified in Point i Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or Court clerk.
Article 5. Time limit for participation in civil procedures

1. The time limit for provision of legal aid in a civil case shall be determined on the basis of:
a) Work with legal aid beneficiaries and relevant persons to determine the legal relationship of the dispute; assess conditions for initiating lawsuits, counter-claims, independent claims; provide guidance on writing petitions for initiation of lawsuits, petitions for application of provisional urgent measures, counter-claims, independent claims, and evidence for the court; guide preparation of lawsuit files or other tasks during the stage of lawsuit initiation and acceptance of cases;
b) Work with legal aid beneficiaries, litigants and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals on the origin and content of disputes, evidences for proof or other tasks in the stage of trial preparation;
c) Attend meetings to inspect the submission, access, and disclosure of evidence; verify, collect, and assess necessary documents, items, and evidence;
d) Participate in taking statements from involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons; participate in activities of property appraisal and valuation and perform other procedural tasks at the request of the presiding agency;
dd) Participate in mediation as prescribed by law;
e) Working with proceeding officers and agencies in various stages of legal proceedings;
g) Meeting with litigants, witnesses, and other participants in legal proceedings regarding other relevant matters;
h) Studying documents, photocopying, and preparing materials at procedural agencies or centers, branches, or organizations signing contracts for legal aid services;
i) Preparing defense arguments;
k) Attending court sessions or meetings to resolve civil matters. In cases where legal aid-providing individuals attend court sessions but such sessions are postponed not at the request of the legal aid-providing individuals and without prior notice, the period during which the legal aid-providing individuals work with procedural agencies and proceeding officers on matters related to the postponement of court sessions shall be included;
l) Performing tasks related to procedures for appealing against first-instance judgments, appellate judgments, cassation procedures, or reopening procedures;
m) Performing other reasonable tasks as prescribed by law to serve the protection.
2. Remuneration and allowances for legal aid-providing individuals performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i, and m Clause 1 of this Article shall not exceed the number of sessions of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 02 enclosed with this Circular.
3. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours for activities specified in Points a and b Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts on provision of legal aid;
b) Working hours for participation in meetings for inspection of the handover, access to and disclosure of evidences specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons assigned to settle lawsuits; working hours for verification, collection and assessment of documents, objects and necessary evidences related to these activities specified in Point c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where legal aid-providing persons conduct verification or collection of evidences;
c) Working hours for taking statements of involved parties, witnesses and other relevant persons specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by these persons or presiding officers or procedural-conducting agencies; working hours for appraisal and valuation of assets and performance of other activities for participation in legal proceedings at the request of presiding agencies specified in Point d Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals where assets are appraised or valued or presiding officers or procedural-conducting persons;
d) Working hours for activities specified in Points dd and k Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by judges or court clerks or mediators’ meeting clerks;
dd) Working hours for activities specified in Point e Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by presiding agencies or presiding officers;
e) Working hours for activities specified in Point g Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons directly working with legal aid-providing persons;
g) Working hours for activities specified in Point h Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons assigned to settle lawsuits or centers, branches or organizations signing contracts on provision of legal aid.
h) The time for performing activities specified in Points i, l and m Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Center or its Branch or organization signing the contract for legal aid.
Article 6. Time for participating in administrative proceedings
1. The basis for calculating the time for providing legal aid for cases participating in administrative proceedings includes:

a) Participating in activities as prescribed in Points a, b, c, d, e, g, h, i, k, l and m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) Participating in dialogues.
2. The remuneration and allowances for persons providing legal aid when performing the tasks specified in Points g, h, i and m Clause 1 Article 5 of this Circular shall not exceed the number of sessions of performance of these tasks according to the corresponding case assignment rates in Appendix No. 03 enclosed with this Circular.
3. The working hours of persons providing legal aid must be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) The time for performing activities specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals specified in Clause 3 Article 5 of this Circular;
b) The time for performing activities specified in point b clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by the Judge or the Dialogue Secretary.
Article 7. Time for participation in extrajudicial representation
1. The basis for calculating the time for providing legal aid for extrajudicial representation cases includes:
a) Meeting and contacting the legally-aided person, their relatives, and witnesses;
b) Studying the dossier and preparing documents for the representation;
c) Verifying, collecting documents, items, evidence, and circumstances related to the representation;
d) Working with relevant agencies, organizations, and individuals;
dd) Participating in the representation before the competent agency, organization, or individual that resolves the case.
2. Working hours of legal aid-providing persons shall be certified by agencies, organizations or individuals as follows:
a) Working hours for activities specified in Points a, d and dd Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by persons working with legal aid-providing persons or relevant agencies, organizations or individuals or competent agencies, organizations or individuals handling the cases;
b) Working hours for activities specified in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article shall be certified by centers, branches or organizations signing contracts for providing legal aid.
Article 8. Determination of working hours in some special cases
1. In case two or more persons providing legal aid for one person receiving legal aid in the same case as prescribed by law, the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling the legal aid-related case is the actual working time of each legal aid-providing person but the total working time must not exceed 30 working sessions/case for cases involving participation in legal proceedings or 20 working sessions/case for cases involving extrajudicial representation.
2. In case the procedural agency suspends the case
a) The working hours as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling the legal aid-related case are the actual working time from the time of suspension of the case;
b) When the case is resumed, the working hours as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for handling the legal aid-related case are the actual working time of subsequent tasks from the time when the case resumes until the end of the case.
3. In case of replacement or change of legal aid-providing persons
a) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the replacement or changed person is the actual period during which they provided legal aid until the time of replacement or change;
b) The period serving as the basis for payment of remuneration and allowances for the person designated as a replacement is the actual period during which they performed subsequent tasks after being designated as a replacement. If the designated replacement chooses the method of incurring expenses for the case, the remuneration and allowances for both persons (the replacement and the replaced or changed person) shall not exceed the corresponding expenses prescribed in Annexes No. 01, No. 02 and No. 03 enclosed with this Circular.
4. In case of providing legal aid for persons denounced, persons against whom charges are proposed or persons facing criminal charges at the time of arrest or temporary detention of persons, victims according to the provisions of procedural law, the time and confirmation of time shall be as follows:
a) The time of verification, collection and assessment of necessary documents, items and evidence related by agencies, organizations and individuals where the legal aid-providing persons conduct verification and collection of evidence for confirmation;
b) The time of participating in taking statements from denounced persons, persons against whom charges are proposed or victims or other relevant persons; participating in activities of confrontation, identification and recognition of voices of denounced persons, persons against whom charges are proposed together with investigators or procurators as confirmed by investigators or procurators;
c) The time of working with persons facing criminal charges in detention cells of border guard stations, custody houses or detention camps as confirmed by persons facing criminal charges or officials of border guard stations, custody houses or detention camps or presiding authorities;
d) The time of meeting denounced persons, persons against whom charges are proposed or their relatives; victims or their relatives; other relevant persons as confirmed by these persons but shall not exceed the number of sessions of performing this work under the method of incurring expenses for the cases prescribed in Annex No. 01 enclosed with this Circular according to the level of less serious crimes.
Chapter III
                                      WORKING BLOCK
Article 9. Case-based payment

1. The case-based payment shall only be applied to legal aid cases in which the participants in the proceedings are selected by the legal aid-providing persons in writing immediately after being assigned and not changed during the process of handling the legal aid cases.
2. Complexity, procedural requirements and contents of each specific case shall be used to determine the level of reimbursement for case-related expenses as follows:
a) Apply regulations on classification of crimes in Clause 2 Article 1 of the Law on amendments to the Criminal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 dated June 20, 2017 to determine the level of expenditure on criminal proceedings.
The classification of crimes must be applied immediately upon selecting the form of charging and based on one of the documents issued by the presiding agency: the decision to charge the suspect, the investigation conclusion, the indictment, the forensic report, or the judgment. In cases where multiple documents contain different crime titles under different penalty brackets, the document with the higher penalty bracket shall prevail.
b) Apply Clause 3 Article 337 of the Civil Procedure Code and Article 5 of Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the level of case allocation for civil proceeding cases.
c) Apply Clause 12 Article 3 of the Law on Administrative Procedures and Article 6 of Circular No. 09/2018/TT-BTP dated June 21, 2018 of the Minister of Justice on criteria for determining complex and typical legal aid cases to determine the level of case allocation for cases involving participation in administrative procedures.
3. When incurring expenses for a case, the legal aid-providing person shall perform the following tasks:
a) For criminal proceedings: perform the tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 4 of this Circular.
Details of tasks to be performed and maximum amounts of expenses corresponding to such tasks (converted by working session) for each case are provided in Appendix No. 01 enclosed herewith. In case the number of converted working sessions for incurring expenses for the case prescribed in Appendix No. 01 is lower than 03 times the basic salary, the amount of expenses for the case shall be equal to 03 times the basic salary.
b) For civil proceedings or administrative procedures: perform the tasks specified in Clause 1 Article 5 and Clause 1 Article 6 of this Circular.
Details of tasks to be performed and maximum amounts of expenses corresponding to such tasks (converted by working session) for each case are provided in Appendix No. 02 and Appendix No. 03 enclosed herewith. In case the number of converted working sessions for incurring expenses for the case prescribed in Appendix No. 02 or Appendix No. 03 is lower than 03 times the basic salary, the amount of expenses for the case shall be equal to 03 times the basic salary.
4. When incurring expenses for a case, the legal aid-providing person shall record the tasks and confirm the performed tasks in the Work List (Form TP-TGPL-02) enclosed herewith.
5. In case a legal aid case is carried out through multiple stages of legal proceedings as prescribed in the Appendices enclosed herewith, the amount of expenses for the case shall be applied according to the stages but must not exceed 10 times the basic salary.
Article 10. Incurring expenses for cases in special cases
1. In case 2 persons providing legal aid for 1 legal aid recipient in the same case, depending on the actual tasks performed by each person, the maximum amount of case assignment shall be 130% of the corresponding amount specified in the Appendix enclosed with this Circular but not exceeding 10 times the basic salary.

For example 1: A is prosecuted for a crime with a penalty bracket of very serious crime, the Director of Legal Aid Center assigns 2 persons to provide legal aid for A from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual tasks performed by 2 persons providing legal aid, the maximum amount of case assignment shall be: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, only 10 times the basic salary can be assigned.
2. In case of providing legal aid for 1 legal aid recipient who is prosecuted for 2 or more crimes in the same case, the amount of case assignment to the legal aid provider shall be as follows:
a) If 2 crimes have different penalty brackets, based on the actual tasks performed, the maximum amount of case assignment shall be 130% of the corresponding amount specified in the Appendix enclosed with this Circular but not exceeding 10 times the basic salary.
For example 2: A is prosecuted for 2 crimes: 1 crime with a penalty bracket of serious crime and 1 crime with a penalty bracket of very serious crime. The legal aid provider provides legal aid for A from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual tasks performed, the maximum amount of case assignment shall be: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since this number exceeds 10 times the basic salary, only 10 times the basic salary can be assigned.
b) If two offences have the same penalty bracket, based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of budget for the case is 130% of the maximum amount of budget for such case as prescribed in Appendix No. 01 enclosed with this Circular but not exceeding 10 times the basic salary. Example 03: B is prosecuted for 02 offences with the same penalty bracket of very serious offence. The legal assistant provides legal aid for B from the stage of investigation to the stage of first-instance trial. Based on the actual work performed, the maximum amount of budget for the case is not exceeding: 130% x 23 sessions = 29.9 sessions. However, since the number of sessions exceeds 10 times the basic salary, only 10 times the basic salary can be allocated.
3. In case the presiding agency suspends the case
a) The budget for the case of the legal aid-providing person shall be allocated based on the actual work performed by the legal aid-providing person up to the time of suspension;
b) When the case continues to be resolved, the budget for the case of the legal aid-providing person shall be allocated based on the work performed by the legal aid-providing person from the time of resumption of resolution until the end of the case.
4. In case of replacement or change of legal aid-providing person
a) The budget for the case of the person replaced or changed shall be allocated based on the actual work performed by the person replaced or changed up to the time of replacement or change;
b) The budget for the case of the person appointed as a replacement shall be allocated based on the actual work performed by the person appointed as a replacement after being appointed as a replacement.
If the person appointed as a replacement chooses the working period by session, the remuneration and allowance for both persons (the replacement and the person replaced or changed) shall not exceed 30 working sessions/case as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 13 of the Government's Decree No. 144/2017/ND-CP dated December 15, 2017 providing detailed regulations on some articles of the Law on Legal Aid.
Chapter IV
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 11. Transitional provisions
For activities arising before the effective date of this Circular, regulations of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases shall continue to apply.
Article 12. Entry into force
1. This Circular comes into force from July 15, 2021.
2. This Circular replaces Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases and Circular No. 05/2017/TT-BTP dated April 20, 2017 of the Minister of Justice on amendments to some Articles of Circular No. 18/2013/TT-BTP dated November 20, 2013 of the Minister of Justice on guidelines for calculation of time and procedures for payment of expenses for legal aid-related cases.
