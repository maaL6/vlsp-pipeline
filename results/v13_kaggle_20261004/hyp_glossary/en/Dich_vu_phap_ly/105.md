                                                  RESOLUTION

                                      ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF LAW ON LAWYERS

                      THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY OF THE SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM

     Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10 of December 25, 2001 of the Xth National Assembly, the 10th session;

                                                  RESOLVES:

   1. The Law on Lawyers, passed by the XIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 9th session on June 29, 2006, takes effect as from January 1, 2007.

  2. The Government shall review the documents providing detailed regulations and guidance for implementation of the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers in order to annul them and promulgate new documents for implementation of this Law.

  3. From the effective date of the Law on Lawyers:

  a/ Persons who have been granted law practice certificates under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers may continue their law practice under this Law;

  b/ Lawyers being probationers under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers may continue their probation under this Law; the probation period is counted into the probation period of law practice under this Law and the probationer's cards are no longer valid;

  c/ Lawyer's cards issued by bar associations under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers shall remain valid until they are replaced; the national lawyers' organizations shall guide the replacement of lawyer's cards;

  d) Within six months, lawyer’s offices and partnership law firms granted with operation registration certificates under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers shall carry out procedures for conversion into lawyer’s offices or partnership law firms under this Law.

  The Justice Ministry shall guide the conversion of lawyer’s offices and partnership law firms granted with operation registration certificates under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers;

  dd) Within six months, individuals and organizations providing legal services under the 1999 Law on Enterprises who continue providing legal services must fully satisfy conditions for practicing law and convert their organizational forms of practice under this Law; otherwise they must terminate their operation.

  The Justice Ministry shall guide the conversion of individuals and organizations providing legal services under the 1999 Law on Enterprises;

  e) Foreign lawyers granted with practice permits in Vietnam; branches of foreign law-practicing organizations, foreign law firms, foreign partnership law firms and Vietnamese law firms granted with establishment permits under Decree No. 87/2003/ND-CP of July 22, 2003 of the Government on law practice by foreign law-practicing organizations and foreign lawyers in Vietnam may continue practicing under this Law;

  g) Bar associations established under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers are not required to carry out procedures for re-establishment;

  h) During the period when the National Lawyers’ Organization is not yet established, the examination of results of law-practicing probation shall be organized by the Justice Ministry and the grant of lawyer’s cards shall be organized by bar associations.

  4. The Government shall specify the support of funding from the State budget for provinces in mountainous areas with extremely difficult conditions for development of the contingent of lawyers and assurance of operation of bar associations.

  This Resolution was passed on June 29, 2006 by the XIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 9th session.
