                                                  DECREE

    AMENDMENTS TO THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 14/2013/ND-CP DATED FEBRUARY 5, 2013 AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 07/2007/ND-CP DATED JANUARY 12, 2007 ON ELABORATION AND GUIDANCE ON THE LAW ON LEGAL AID

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;

  Pursuant to the Law on Legal Aid dated June 29, 2006;

  At the request of the Minister of Justice, The Government promulgates a Decree amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 14/2013/ND-CP dated February 5, 2013 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 07/2007/ND-CP dated January 12, 2007 providing detailed regulations and guidance for implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid.

  Article 1. Amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 14/2013/ND-CP dated February 5, 2013 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Government’s Decree No. 07/2007/ND-CP dated January 12, 2007 providing detailed regulations and guidance for implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Legal Aid as follows:

  1. Clause 7 Article 1 is amended as follows:

  1. Persons who have law bachelor degrees, graduated from lawyer training courses and are working in state legal aid centers, and are appointed to attend legal aid professional training courses; if they pass the examination of legal aid professional training courses, they shall be granted certificates of completion of legal aid professional training courses by the Judicial Academy or the Ministry of Justice under law.

  Persons who used to be lawyers under the Law on Lawyers or were exempted from lawyer training courses under the Law on Lawyers, and are working in state legal aid centers, and are appointed to take the examination of legal aid professional training courses; if they pass the examination of legal aid professional training courses, they shall be granted certificates of completion of legal aid professional training courses by the Judicial Academy or the Ministry of Justice under law.

  2. The Minister of Justice shall stipulate programs and time for legal aid professional training courses for legal aid professionals; promulgate a Regulation on organization, examination and grant of certificates of completion of legal aid professional training courses."

  2. Clause 8 Article 1 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  Article 25. Professional titles of legal aid professionals

  1. Legal aid professionals shall have professional titles of public employees as prescribed by law.

  2. The Minister of Home Affairs shall promulgate a list of professional titles of legal aid professionals, their codes and classification.

  3. The Minister of Justice shall promulgate professional standards for professional titles of legal aid professionals after reaching agreement with the Minister of Home Affairs."

  3. Clause 9 Article 1 is amended as follows:

  1. Legal aid professionals shall be paid salaries according to the salary table of public officials as prescribed by law.

  2. Legal aid professionals shall receive a responsibility-based allowance equal to 25% of their current salary plus a seniority-based allowance and an over-the-limit allowance (if any). When participating in legal proceedings, legal aid professionals shall receive case handling allowances equal to 40% of the allowance applicable to lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State as prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article. When representing outside legal proceedings or conducting mediation, legal aid professionals shall receive case handling allowances equal to 20% of the allowance applicable to lawyers providing legal aid at the request of the State.”

  4. Clause 12 Article 1 is amended and supplemented as follows:

  2. A collaborator shall be entitled to allowances for each legal aid case he/she has handled based on the number of hours of legal consultancy and other forms of legal aid provided. The allowance for each working session (half a working day) shall be applied to legal aid cases handled by collaborators in the form of participation in proceedings or extra-procedural representation.

  The level of allowance for each legal aid case handled by a collaborator or lawyer providing legal aid at the request of the State shall be determined based on the quality of legal aid services provided, the duration of legal aid services provided, the complexity of the legal aid case, the form of presentation of legal aid results, and the form of legal aid provided.

  In the case of providing legal aid in the form of participation in proceedings, the level of allowance paid to the lawyer providing legal aid at the request of the State shall be VND 500,000 per working session or allocated as a lump sum for each case with a minimum of 3 months' basic salary and a maximum of 10 months' basic salary (depending on the nature and content of the case). The Ministry of Justice shall specify the nature and content of the case, the method of calculation of working sessions, and the time basis for allocating or paying the allowance for the lawyer per working session or working period.

  The time for providing legal aid includes the time for studying the case file and preparing documents for the provision of legal aid, the time for meeting and contacting the legal aid recipient or their relatives, the time for verifying the legal aid case, and the time spent working at agencies, organizations, or individuals related to the legal aid case. The basis for determining the working time of a collaborator or lawyer providing legal aid at the request of the State is the time confirmed by the relevant agency, organization, or individual."

  Article 2. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on November 10, 2015.

  Article 3. Implementation responsibilities

  The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies, the heads of the Government-attached agencies and the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall have to implement this Decree.-
