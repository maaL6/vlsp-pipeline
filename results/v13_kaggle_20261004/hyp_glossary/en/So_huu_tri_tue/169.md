                                                   CIRCULAR

   THE MINISTRY OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND ENVIRONMENT’S SERIES NO. 3055-TT/SHCN OF DECEMBER 31, 1996 ON GUIDING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROVISIONS ON PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS AND A NUMBER OF OTHER PROCEDURES IN THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 63-CP OF OCTOBER 24, 1996, PROVIDING DETAILED REGULATIONS ON INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996 detailing industrial property; The Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment hereby promulgates this Circular in order to specify and guide the procedures for making, filing and examining applications for protection titles, the procedures for approving and registering industrial property right transfer contracts, the procedures for considering applications for non-voluntary licenses, the procedures for modifying and extending the validity of protection titles, the procedures for processing international applications for invention or utility solutions under the PCT Convention and international applications for trademarks under the Madrid Agreement and the procedures for granting industrial property representation permits.

  Chapter I

                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. Terms

  1.1. Terms used in this Circular are construed as follows:

  - "Decree" is used to refer to the Government’s Decree No. 63/CP of October 24, 1996 detailing industrial property;

  - "Application" is used to refer to an application for a protection title;

  - "Invention application", "Utility solution application", "Industrial design application", "Trademark application", "Appellation origin registration application" respectively refer to an application for an invention patent, an application for a utility solution patent, an application for an industrial design patent, an application for a trademark registration certificate or an application for a certificate of registered appellation origin right;

  - "International application" means an international application for registration of an invention or utility solution filed under the PCT Convention;

  - "International application for registration" means an international application for registration of a trademark filed under the Madrid Agreement on International Registration of Marks;

  - "Mark" means "trade mark" as defined in Article 2 of the Decree;

  - "Document holder" means an individual, juridical person or organization that has created, issued or signed to recognize the validity of a document.

  1.2. Other terms shall be construed according to the provisions of the Decree.

  2. Document certification

  2.1. Signature certification

  In the course of carrying out procedures related to the establishment, maintenance, extension, enforcement and transfer of industrial property rights specified in this Circular, signatures of persons standing for documents and transactions with competent agencies must be certified as being those of the persons standing for such documents and, where the signatories are representatives of the document holders, must be certified as being those having the authority to represent the document holders according to the following regulations:

  (i) For a holder of a lawful seal, the authentication of signature shall be carried out by affixing the holder's seal on the signature;

  (ii) For Vietnamese entities having no lawful seal, the signature confirmation must be carried out at a State Notary Public or at the local authority where the entity resides or is headquartered;

  (iii) For a foreign entity having no lawful seal, the signature confirmation shall be carried out at a notary office or a competent authority.

  2.2. Certification of copies

  a/ All documents being copies made by any means must be certified as copies from originals according to the provisions in Item b below before they can be used as official documents in the course of carrying out related procedures at the competent agencies.

  b/ Documents are recognized as copies from originals if they bear certification of copying by one of the following agencies: (i) Notary Public office, (ii) People's Committees or competent agencies, (iii) State agencies or social organizations which have made the originals; and, if the copies consist of many pages, each page or the pages must be appended together.

  2.3. Translation certification

  a/ All Vietnamese versions of documents, which are certified as being translated from the original texts according to the following Clause b), may be used as official documents in the course of carrying out relevant industrial property procedures before competent agencies.

  b/ The certification of a translation may be effected in one of the following modes: (i) notarization; (ii) certification by the original document holder himself/herself; (iii) certification by all parties to the contract or agreement (if the original document is a contract or agreement); (iv) recognition by the agency competent to use such translation in the course of carrying out relevant procedures.

  3. Persons acting as owners shall carry out procedures related to industrial property

  3.1. Only persons defined in Points 3.2 and 3.3 below may act on behalf of the subject matters in submitting Applications, supplementing or modifying documents of the Application; receiving and responding to opinions of the National Office of Industrial Property related to the Application; deciding to continue or terminate the process of protection request; receiving Protection titles; maintaining, modifying and extending the validity of Protection titles as well as other procedures concerning industrial property before the National Office of Industrial Property and competent agencies.

  The National Office of Industrial Property shall be permitted to conduct transactions with the above-mentioned persons and such transactions shall be considered official ones with the subjects. 3.2. For subjects who have the right to directly carry out the filing of Applications and related procedures specified in Clauses 2 and 3 a/ Article 15 of the Decree, the following persons shall be permitted to act on behalf of the subjects to carry out the jobs mentioned in Point 3.1 above:

  (i) The individual or their legal representative (if the entity is an individual);

  (ii) The legal representative of the entity; an individual being a member of the entity authorized by the legal representative of the entity to represent; the head of the representative office or branch of the entity authorized by the legal representative of the entity to represent (if the entity is a juridical person or another entity);

  (iii) Heads of representative offices in Vietnam of foreign entities authorized by such entities; persons represented under a proxy agreement of enterprises established in Vietnam with 100% investment capital of foreign entities authorized by such entities;

  (iv) The person who satisfies one of the conditions stated in paragraphs (i), (ii) and (iii) above is one of the individuals or one of the juridical persons or other subjects - if the subject includes many individuals, juridical persons or other subjects and if such person is authorized by all individuals, juridical persons or other subjects to act as representative.

  3.3. For entities which are only permitted to file applications and carry out related procedures through industrial property representation service organizations defined in Clause 3.b, Article 15 of the Decree as well as for all other entities carrying out the above-mentioned procedures through industrial property representation service organizations, only persons who have been granted industrial property representative cards by industrial property representation service organizations may carry out the jobs mentioned in Point 3.1 above.

  4. Authorization for carrying out procedures related to industrial property

  4.1. All authorization for the performance of industrial property procedures must be made in writing (power of attorney) and must contain the following contents:

  (i) Full name and address of the Authorized Party; (ii) Full name and address of the Authorized Party; (iii) Scope of authorization (tasks performed by the Authorized Party on behalf of the Authorizing Party); (iv) Authorization period; (v) Place and date of making of the Authorization Letter; (vi) Signature of the Authorizing Party (certified in accordance with regulations on signature certification).

  4.2. The authorized party must be an individual or organization permitted to carry out procedures on industrial property as prescribed in Points 3.2 (ii), (iii), (iv) and 3.3 of this Circular.

  4.3. Any change in the scope of authorization and termination of authorization before the due date must be notified in writing to the National Office of Industrial Property.

  4.4. If the power of attorney covers a number of independent procedures, the authorized party may submit copies of the power of attorney, provided that the original copy has been submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property and the number and date of submission of the dossier containing the original copy of the power of attorney are indicated.

  Chapter II

                                                APPLICATIONS AND PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS

  5. General requirements for applications

  5.1. Applications shall ensure uniformity as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 11 of the Decree and meet general formal requirements as prescribed in Point 5.2 below.

  5.2. Applications shall satisfy general formal requirements as follows:

  (i) Each application may request the grant of only one title of protection which must be appropriate to the subject matter of industrial property stated in the application;

  (ii) All documents of the application must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents which may be presented in other languages according to the provisions of Point 5.3 below;

  (iii) All documents of the application must be presented in a vertical layout on a single sheet of paper of size A4 (210mm x 297mm), with margins of 20mm on each side, except for documents added to the application for necessary support or illustration purposes whose origin is not for inclusion in the application and may be presented differently;

  (iv) Where documents are required to be made according to a set form, such forms must be used by filling in appropriate designated places.

  (v) Each document must be made in sufficient copies as requested; if a document consists of multiple pages, the page number must be written in Arabic numerals between the first and last letter of each page.

  (vi) Documents must be typed or printed with an ink type that is not easily faded, clear, clean, and cannot be erased or modified.

  5.3. The following documents may be made in other languages besides Vietnamese but must be translated into Vietnamese:

  (i) Authorization letters (if any);

  (ii) Documents certifying the right to file applications lawfully if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate of transfer of the right to file an application, including the transfer of the submitted Application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.);

  (iii) Certificate of priority right transfer (if the Application requests for the priority right and such right is inherited from another person);

  (iv) Relevant documents proving the basis for the priority right (first application, certificate of display at an exhibition, etc.);

  (v) Original documents or copies thereof that the applicant has included in the Application to support the Application.

  6. Requirements for invention applications/utility solutions

  Apart from general requirements specified in Point 5 of this Circular, an invention/utility solution must satisfy requirements specified in this Point.

  6.1. An application must include the following documents:

  (i) An application form for grant of an invention patent/utility solution made according to a form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) Description of an invention/utility solution (below referred to as description), comprising 3 copies;

  (iii) The protection request, including 3 copies;

  (iv) Drawings, schematics, calculation sheets, etc. (if necessary) to further clarify the nature of the technical solutions stated in the Description, including 3 copies;

  (v) Summary of invention/utility solution, comprising 3 copies;

  (vi) Documents certifying the lawful right to file an application if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate or agreement on assignment of the right to file an application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.), 1 copy;

  (vii) A power of attorney (if necessary), 1 copy;

  (viii) A copy of the first application or documents certifying the exhibition if the application requests priority rights under an international treaty, 1 copy;

  (ix) Receipts for payment of fees for filing and publication of applications, 1 copy.

  6.2. The documents mentioned in Point 6.1 above must be filed simultaneously. The following documents may be filed within 3 months from the date of filing the Application:

  (i) Vietnamese versions of documents 6.1 (ii), 6.1 (iii) and 6.1 (v), if the Application already contains English/French/Russian versions of these documents;

  (ii) Original copies of document 6.1 (vii) if the Application already contains copies;

  (iii) Document 6.1 (viii), including its Vietnamese translation.

  6.3. The description must fully disclose the nature of the technical solution to be protected. The description must contain all information sufficient to enable any person with average qualifications in the relevant technical field to implement such solution.

  The description must clarify the novelty, creativity (if the subject matter is an invention) and applicability of the technical solution to be protected.

  The description must include the following contents:

  (i) International classification indicators (according to the Strasbourg Agreement), (ii) Name of technical solution, (iii) Field in which the technical solution is used or related, (iv) Technical status in the above-mentioned field at the time of application (known technical solutions), (v) Nature of the technical solution, (vi) Brief description of accompanying drawings (if any), (vii) Example of implementation of the technical solution, (viii) Possible benefits (effectiveness of the technical solution).

  6.4. Protection request for determination of scope (volume) of protection of an invention/utility solution. The protection request must be presented in brief and clearly in conformity with the description and drawings, which must clarify new signs of the technical solution to be protected.

  6.5. Summary of invention/utility solution for briefly disclosing nature of invention/utility solution. The summary must disclose principal contents of technical solutions for information purpose.

  6.6. Requirements on form and contents of Description, Drawings, Protection Request, Summary of Invention/Utility Solution and other documents of the Application for Invention/Utility Solution shall be stipulated by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  7. Requirements for applications for industrial designs

  Apart from general requirements prescribed in Point 5 of this Circular, an Industrial Design Application shall satisfy requirements prescribed in this Point.

  7.1. An application must include the following documents:

  (i) The application form for grant of an industrial design patent, made according to a form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, shall be made in 3 copies;

  (ii) Description of the industrial design, including 3 copies;

  (iii) The set of photos or drawings of the industrial designs, including 6 sets; (iv) Documents certifying the lawful submission right if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance rights; certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application; assignment contract or labor contract), including 1 copy;

  (v) Documents certifying the ownership of marks if the industrial design contains marks, 1 copy;

  (vi) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  (vii) One copy of the first application or documents certifying the display at the exhibition if the application requests priority rights under an international treaty, including 1 copy;

  (viii) The fee and charge receipts for filing the application and announcement of the application, 1 copy each.

  7.2. The documents mentioned in Point 7.1 above must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of submission of the Application:

  (i) Vietnamese version of document 7.1(ii), if the application already contains an English/French/Russian version of such document;

  (ii) Document No. 7.1(v);

  (iii) The original of document 7.1 (vi), if a copy is available in the Application;

  (iv) Document No. 7.1 (vii), including its Vietnamese translation.

  7.3. The description of an industrial design shall fully and clearly present the nature of such industrial design and shall be consistent with a set of photos or drawings, and shall include the following contents:

  (i) Name of the industrial design, (ii) International classification index of the industrial design (according to the Locarno Agreement), (iii) Field of use of the product with the industrial design, (iv) Known similar industrial designs, (v) List of photos or drawings, (vi) Nature of the industrial design, in which it is necessary to clearly state the basic shaping characteristics of the industrial design requested for protection to be different from known similar industrial designs.

  7.4. The set of photos or drawings shall fully express the nature of the industrial design as described in order to determine the scope (volume) of protection of such industrial design.

  The photos/drawings must be clear and sharp, and must not mix other products with the product bearing the industrial design requested for protection.

  All photos/drawings must be taken under the same scale. The size of each photo shall not be smaller than 90 mm x 120 mm and not larger than 210 mm x 297 mm.

  7.5. Requirements on the Description and set of photos/drawings of the industrial design shall be stipulated by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  8. Requirements on Applications for Marks

  Apart from the general requirements stipulated in Point 5 of this Circular, an application for a mark must satisfy the requirements stipulated in this Point.

  8.1. An application shall comprise the following documents:

  (i) Declaration requesting the grant of a certificate of registration of the mark, which bears the sample of the mark made according to the sample issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 3 copies;

  (ii) Regulations on use of marks if the protected mark is a collective mark, 1 copy;

  (iii) Mark samples, 15 copies;

  (iv) Copies of documents certifying lawful business rights (business licenses or business registration certificates, etc.), 1 copy;

  (v) Documents certifying the lawful right to file an application, if the applicant inherits the right to file an application from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application, including the filed application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.), 1 copy;

  (vi) Authorization letter (if necessary);

  (vii) Copy of the first application or Certificate of exhibition if the application requests priority rights under an international treaty, 1 copy;

  (viii) Documents certifying origin, prizes, medals, if the mark contains such information, 1 copy;

  (ix) License of a competent authority, if the mark uses symbols, proper name, etc., prescribed at Point g, Clause 2, Article 6 of the Decree, 1 copy;

  (x) The fee and application payment documents shall be submitted in 1 copy.

  8.2. The above documents must be submitted simultaneously. The following documents may be submitted within 3 months from the date of submission of the Application:

  (i) Original copy of document 8.1 (vi), if a copy is included in the Application;

  (ii) Document 8.1 (vii), including its Vietnamese translation.

  8.3. The description of the mark in the Declaration must clarify the distinguishability of the mark, specifying each constituent element of the mark and the overall meaning of the mark. If the mark contains words other than Vietnamese, their pronunciation (transcribed into Vietnamese) must be clearly written; if such words have meanings, their Vietnamese meanings must be provided.

  If the protected words or phrases are presented graphically as elements of the mark, the graphic form of such words or phrases must be described.

  If the mark contains Arabic numerals other than Arabic numerals, they must be transcribed into Arabic numerals.

  If the mark consists of several distinct parts used simultaneously on a product, the position of each part of the mark on the product or its packaging must be specified.

  8.4. The list of products and services bearing marks in the declaration must be appropriate or of the same type as those permitted for business activities as stated in the business license or certificate of business registration and must be grouped according to the International Classification of Products and Services (according to the Nixon Agreement).

  8.5. The mark samples affixed in the declaration as well as other mark samples must be presented clearly with size not exceeding 80mm x 80mm and the distance between two nearest points not smaller than 15mm.

  If a color protection is requested, the mark samples must be presented in the exact colors to be protected.

  If a color protection is not requested, all mark samples must be presented in black and white.

  9. Requirements for Origin Appellations.

  Apart from the general requirements specified at Point 5 of this Circular, an Origin Appellation must meet the requirements stated in this Point.

  9.1. An application must include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration requesting the grant of a certificate of rights to use an origin appellation of goods, made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 3 copies;

  (ii) Copies of documents certifying lawful business rights (business licenses, business registration certificates, etc.), 1 copy each;

  (iii) Presentation on quality characteristics of products bearing appellations of origin, with certification by competent State agencies, 1 copy each;

  (iv) Certification by competent agencies that products manufactured or commercially traded by applicants have particular properties and quality and are manufactured in territories corresponding to such appellations of origin (conforming to presentation in document (iii)), 1 copy each;

  (v) Copies of titles of protection of appellations of origin granted by the countries of origin or documents of the countries of origin certifying the right of applicants to use appellations of origin currently under protection in the countries of origin (if the appellations of origin originate from foreign countries), 1 copy each;

  (vi) Description map of the territory corresponding to the appellation of origin, with indication of location of production and/or trading of the applicant, 1 copy each;

  (vii) Authorization letter (if necessary), 1 copy each;

  (viii) Fee payment documents, 1 copy each.

  If the applicant only requests the grant of a certificate of the right to use an appellation of origin already registered, documents (iii) and (vi) are not required in the Application. If the appellation of origin originates from a foreign country, documents (ii), (iii) and (vi) are not required in the Application.

  9.2. Documents to be submitted simultaneously. The original of document 9.1 (vii) may be submitted within 3 months from the date of application if a copy is included in the Application.

  9.3. Competent agencies capable of certifying the particular characteristics of products bearing appellations of origin and certifying that products manufactured by applicants possess such particular characteristics are central or local agencies managing goods quality where goods bearing appellations of origin exist.

  10. Submitting applications

  An application may be filed at the National Office of Industrial Property or at any other places designated by the National Office of Industrial Property for receipt of applications. Applications may also be sent by post under security to the above-mentioned places designated for receipt of applications.

  11. Receipt of Applications

  11.1. Upon receiving an Application, the National Office of Industrial Property shall have to perform the following tasks:

  (i) Re-examine the list of documents stated in the Declaration;

  (ii) Affixing a seal to certify the date of receipt of the Application and Declaration;

  (iii) To record differences between the list of documents stated in the Declaration and the number of documents actually included in the Application;

  (iv) To conduct preliminary examination of the Application for conclusion on whether or not the Application is accepted according to Point 11.2 below;

  (v) To send to the Applicant a Declaration bearing the seal certifying the date of receipt of the Application, the serial number of the Application and stating the results of the examination of the list of documents, with the signature and name of the Applicant’s receiver; (the above-mentioned Declaration replaces the Receipt of Application).

  11.2. The National Office of Industrial Property shall not accept an Application if it finds that the Application has one of the following defects:

  (i) The Application lacks one of the following compulsory documents: the Declaration, which must contain the sample mark and the list of products or services (for marks Applications), the appellation of origin of goods and the type of goods (for appellation of origin Applications), the Description of invention/utility solution, the Protection request (for inventions/utility solutions), the Description of industrial design and the set of photos and drawings of the industrial design (for industrial design Applications), the Power of attorney (for applications requiring a Power of attorney), the fee payment document;

  (ii) The form of protection (type of the requested protection title to be granted) is not appropriate to the subject matter of industrial property stated in the Application;

  (iii) The Declaration is not signed or is seriously erased or altered.

  11.3. In case the Application is not accepted, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the Applicant of the reason for non-acceptance. For Applications submitted by post, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the Applicant in writing within 15 days after the Application is received; the National Office of Industrial Property is not required to return to the Applicant the documents of the Application but must refund the fee already paid according to the Application not accepted after subtracting the cost of sending and returning such money.

  12. Processing of received applications:

  After receiving an application, it shall be processed as follows:

  - A set of documents including necessary documents is separated to preserve the initial state of the application (referred to as the "application dossier" of the application);

  - The remaining documents are combined into a set of documents for examination in accordance with this Circular.

  13. Formal examination

  13.1. After being processed according to Point 12 of this Circular, the application shall be formally examined in accordance with the provisions of this Point.

  13.2. An application shall be considered invalid if it contains one of the following deficiencies:

  (i) The application is made in a language other than Vietnamese, except for the case specified in Point 5.3 of this Circular;

  (ii) The declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author (for inventions, utility solutions or industrial designs), about the applicant, the applicant does not sign or the signature is not certified, and the information about the representative has been erased.

  (iii) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the right to file the application;

  (iv) The application is filed in contravention of the provisions of Article 15 of the Decree;

  (v) The description, summary, protection request for invention, utility solution, description of industrial design made in English/French/Russian language but the applicant fails to supplement the Vietnamese version within the time limit prescribed in Points 6.2 and 7.2 of this Circular;

  (vi) The power of attorney is only a copy without the original supplemented within the time limit prescribed in Points 6.2, 7.2, 8.2 and 9.2 of this Circular;

  (vii) The application contains errors prescribed in Point 13.3 below which affect the validity of the application and although the Industrial Property Office has requested correction, the applicant still fails to correct them or the correction is unsatisfactory;

  (viii) The subject matter stated in the application is a subject matter not protected by the State as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 4, Clause 3 Article 5, Clause 2 Article 6 and Clause 2 Article 7 of the Decree;

  13.3. Handling of errors in the Application during the stage of examination of form

  If an Application contains the following defects, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify such to the Applicant and within two (2) months from the date of notification, the Applicant shall correct such defects.

  (i) Not having enough of one of the mandatory documents;

  (ii) The application does not meet the uniformity requirement;

  (iii) The application does not meet the requirements regarding the format of presentation;

  (iv) The mark application does not clearly state the type of mark registered, lacks a description of the mark, or contains a list of products that is not grouped or grouped incorrectly;

  (v) Information about the applicant in documents is inconsistent or erased;

  (vi) The fee for filing the application has not been fully paid.

  13.4. The applicant may take initiative in modifying and supplementing documents in the Application but not expanding the scope (volume) of protection and not changing the nature of the subject matter of industrial property stated in the Application and shall pay a fee according to regulations. If the modification expands the scope (volume) of protection or changes the nature of the subject matter, the applicant shall file a new Application and all procedures shall be carried out again from the beginning.

  13.5. Determination of the date of filing a valid application

  The date of submission of a valid application shall be determined as follows:

  (i) For an Application which has no defects specified at Point 13.2 above, the date of filing shall be the date on which the Application is received by the National Office of Industrial Property as indicated in the receipt seal on the Declaration;

  (ii) For applications with the deficiencies prescribed in Point 13.3 above and such deficiencies having been corrected within the respective time limit, the valid filing date shall be the date on which the application is received by the National Office of Industrial Property as indicated in the receipt seal. If the deficiencies are corrected after such time limit, the valid filing date shall be the date on which the deficiencies have been completely corrected so that the application becomes valid.

  13.6. Determination of priority date

  The priority date of an Application shall be determined as follows:

  If the Application does not request priority right, the priority date is the date of filing of the valid Application;

  If an application requests priority right, the priority date shall be the date stated in such application and approved by the National Office of Industrial Property.

  13.7. Results of examination of the form of an application shall be notified by the National Office of Industrial Property to the applicant as follows:

  (a) If the application is considered valid, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send the applicant a notice of acceptance of the application, clearly stating the name and address of the applicant; the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the application was filed through such organization); the subject matter stated in the application, the date of filing the application, the priority date; the serial number of the application; the remaining deficiencies that the applicant must still correct and the time limit for correcting these deficiencies. Past this time limit, if the applicant fails to correct the deficiencies, the application will not be further considered;

  (b) If the application is considered invalid, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send the applicant a notice of refusal to accept the application, clearly stating the name and address of the applicant, the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the application was filed through such organization); the date the application was filed with the National Office of Industrial Property, the subject matter stated in the application; the reason for refusal to accept the application (the reason why the application is considered invalid);

  (c) If the application still has deficiencies as stated in Point 13.3 above, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send the applicant a notice of results of examination of the form of the application, clearly stating the name and address of the applicant, the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the application was filed through such organization), the date the application was filed with the National Office of Industrial Property; the subject matter stated in the application, the deficiencies that need to be corrected and the time limit for the applicant to correct these deficiencies.

  13.8. The time limit for examination of the form of an application is 3 months from the date the application is filed with the National Office of Industrial Property, which is indicated on the receipt seal; for applications with late-filed documents as stipulated in Points 6.2, 7.2, 8.2 and 9.2, the time limit for examination of the form of the application is 3 months from the date the documents are supplemented. Before the end of the above-mentioned time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property must complete the examination of the form of the application and notify the applicant as stipulated in Point 13.7 above.

  14. Publication of valid applications

  14.1. All inventions, utility solutions and industrial designs which have been accepted as valid shall be published by the National Office of Industrial Property in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within the following time limits:

  a/ An invention or utility solution shall be published in the 19th month from the date of priority, except for cases specified at Points b, c and d Below;

  b/ In case of early publication request, an application shall be published within one month from the date the National Office of Industrial Property receives such request or within the later time limit stated in the request;

  c/ For an invention or utility solution, if there is a written request for content examination filed before the date the application is accepted as valid, it shall be published within one month after the application is accepted as valid;

  d/ For an invention or utility solution, if there is a written request for content examination filed after the application is accepted as valid but before the end of the 18-month time limit from the date of priority, it shall be published within one month after the National Office of Industrial Property receives such request;

  e/ International applications and industrial design applications shall be published in the second month from the date of acceptance of valid applications, except for cases specified at Point b Above.

  14.2. Information related to valid applications to be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette includes all information on valid applications recorded in the notices of acceptance of applications, except for information on defects which must be corrected; summaries of inventions and utility solutions enclosed with drawings if necessary; one or more drawings and photos of industrial designs.

  14.3. Everyone may access more detailed information on the nature of subject matters stated in applications published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette or request the National Office of Industrial Property to supply such information. Applicants who request information supply shall pay fees for information supply according to regulations.

  15. Request for examination of contents of inventions or utility solutions.

  15.1. Within 42 months from the priority date of an invention application or 36 months from the priority date of a utility solution application, the applicant or any third party may request the National Office of Industrial Property to examine the contents of such invention or utility solution.

  The person requesting examination of the contents of an invention or utility solution shall pay a fee as prescribed.

  15.2. Requests for examination of the contents of inventions or utility solutions after their publication shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within one month from the date of receipt of the written request for examination and shall be notified to the applicant.

  Requests for examination of the contents of inventions or utility solutions filed before the publication of the applications shall be published together with the corresponding applications according to Point 14.1c) and d) of this Circular.

  15.3. Except for cases where the examination of the contents is stated by the applicant in the declaration, requests for examination of the contents of inventions or utility solutions must be made in writing, clearly stating the name and address of the requester; the number and filing date of the invention or utility solution application subject to examination; the name and address of the applicant, the name of the invention or utility solution, and must be accompanied by receipts or vouchers confirming payment of the examination fee.

  16. Examination of contents of Applications

  16.1. The examination of contents of Applications shall be conducted by the National Office of Industrial Property under the provisions of this Point for:

  (i) All applications for marks, industrial designs and appellations of origin which have been accepted as valid and the applicants have paid the fee for content examination according to regulations; and (ii) All international applications; and (iii) All inventions, utility solutions, including international applications, which have been accepted as valid and have made a request for content examination filed with the National Office of Industrial Property within the time limit prescribed in Point 15 of this Circular.

  16.2. The purpose of content examination is to assess the protection capacity of the subject matter stated in the application according to the protection criteria, and determine the corresponding scope (volume) of protection.

  16.3. Within the time limit for content examination, the National Office of Industrial Property shall send a notice of the results of content examination to the applicant and the person requesting content examination according to the following provisions:

  (a) If the industrial property subject matter fails to satisfy the protection criteria, the notice of the results of content examination shall clearly state the reason for the intended refusal to grant the title of protection and fix a time-limit of two months from the date of notification for the applicant to give its opinion;

  (b) If the industrial property subject matter satisfies the protection criteria but the scope (volume) of protection must be narrowed or the application contains errors, the notice of the results of content examination shall clearly state such matters and fix a time-limit of two months from the date of notification for the applicant to give its opinion or to correct the errors;

  (c) If the subject matter satisfies the protection criteria, even in the case specified at Point b above, the notice of the results of content examination shall require the applicant to pay the fee for publication of the title of protection, the registration fee and the title of protection and the fee for maintenance of validity for the first year (for inventions and utility solutions).

  16.4. Within the time limit for content examination, the applicant may take the initiative in amending and supplementing documents in the application and shall pay the fee according to regulations.

  The National Office of Industrial Property may request the applicant within a given time limit to amend and supplement documents. If the applicant fails to make amendments and supplements at the request of the National Office of Industrial Property without a plausible reason, the application shall be considered withdrawn.

  Amendments or supplements shall not alter the nature of the subject matter and shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection stated in the Application.

  16.5. The time limit for examination of the contents of an Application is (i) 18 months for invention Applications and 9 months for utility solution Applications as from the date of receipt of a request for examination of contents, if such request is filed after the date of publication of the Application or as from the date of publication of the Application if the request for examination of contents is filed before the date of publication of the Application; (ii) 9 months for industrial design Applications and mark Applications as from the date of signing of the notice of acceptance of the valid Application; (iii) 6 months for appellations of origin Applications as from the date of signing of the notice of acceptance of the valid Application.

  If during the process of examination of contents of the Application, the applicant takes initiative or at the request of the National Office of Industrial Property to amend or supplement documents, the time limit for examination of contents may be extended by the duration reserved for the purpose of amending or supplementing documents.

  Before the expiry date of the time limit for examination of contents, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of results of examination of contents to the applicant and the person requesting examination under Point 16.3 above.

  Chapter III

                              TRANSFER OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

  17. Contracts for transfer of industrial property rights

  17.1. A contract for transfer of industrial property rights means a contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property objects or a contract for transfer of the right to use industrial property objects.

  17.2. A contract on transfer of ownership of an invention, utility solution, industrial design or mark shall contain the following principal contents:

  - Full names and addresses of the Transmitting Party and Receiving Party;

  - Basis for transfer (certificate of protection granted to the Transferee or transferred to the Transferee);

  - The transferee (who has the entire right to ownership over the entire quantity of the protected object of industrial property or over a part of the protected quantity of a mark - a part of the list of goods and services);

  - Transfer price;

  - Rights and obligations of each party, including corresponding obligations not contrary to Article 40 of the Decree;

  - Conditions for modifying, terminating or invalidating contracts;

  - Methods of settling complaints and disputes;

  - Date and place of signing;

  - Signatures of the Parties or of their authorized representatives, with their full names and positions, and signatures certified.

  17.3. A contract for licensing of an invention, utility solution, industrial design or mark ("licensing contract") shall contain the following contents:

  - Full name and address of the Licensor and the Licensee;

  - Grounds for licensing (the protection title granted to the Licensor; or the exclusive licensing contract);

  - Scope of licensing, including:

  - Form of licensing (exclusive/non-exclusive);

  - Subject matter of licensing, which is determined by the scope of use rights (protected acts of use) and the scope of industrial property ownership (protected volume of industrial property);

  . Territorial limit (belongs to Vietnamese territory);

  . Term (belonging to the term of protection of industrial property objects).

  For secondarily licensed shares, the scope of the license must fall within the scope of the License Contract for Secondarily Licensed Shares in question;

  - License price;

  - Rights and obligations of each Party, including corresponding obligations not contrary to Article 40 of the Decree;

  - Conditions for modifying, terminating or invalidating contracts;

  - Methods of settling complaints and disputes;

  - Date and place of signing;

  - The signatures of the parties or their authorized representatives, with their full names and positions, and the certification of the signatures.

  17.4. A license contract must not contain unreasonable clauses restricting the licensee's rights, especially those not arising from the licensor's rights to the corresponding industrial property object or not aimed at protecting such rights, such as:

  - Clauses directly or indirectly restricting the export of products produced under the license to territories where the licensor does not own the corresponding industrial property rights or has the exclusive right to import the corresponding industrial property object;

  - Clauses forcing the licensee to buy all or a certain percentage of raw materials, components or equipment from the licensor or his designated person, not for the purpose of ensuring the quality of goods produced by the licensee;

  - Clauses prohibiting the licensee to improve the industrial property object (except for marks), forcing the licensee to transfer free of charge improvements made by the licensee or the right to file an application for industrial property protection or industrial property rights to such improvements;

  - Clauses prohibiting the licensee to sue about the validity of industrial property rights or the licensing right of the licensor.

  17.5. If the transfer of industrial property rights is part of another Contract, the contents of the transfer of industrial property rights must be set up as a separate part from the remaining parts of the Contract and must comply with the provisions in this Point.

  18. Types of contracts on transfer of industrial property rights must be approved and registered.

  18.1. Under Clause 5, Article 38 and Clause 5, Article 62 of the Decree, all contracts for transfer of ownership or use right of industrial property subject matter in the following cases shall be approved by the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment before carrying out the registration procedures for the contract according to Article 42 of the Decree and Point 20 of this Circular:

  (i) One of the parties is a State organization or an organization with joint venture capital contributed by the State while the other party is an individual or organization not belonging to the State;

  (ii) The transferring party is an individual, juridical person or another entity of Vietnam while the receiving party is a foreign individual or organization.

  18.2. All contracts for transfer of ownership or use right of industrial property subject matter, including those already carried out the approval procedures, shall be registered according to Article 42 of the Decree and Point 20 of this Circular.

  19. Procedures for approving contracts for transfer of industrial property rights

  19.1. A dossier for approval of a contract (hereinafter referred to as approval dossier) shall include the following documents:

  (i) Declaration requesting approval of the contract for transfer of industrial property rights, made according to the form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 3 copies, in which the applicant for approval must be a State organization or an organization with joint venture capital contributed by the State if the contract falls under case 18.1(i); or a Vietnamese party if the contract falls under case 18.1(ii);

  (ii) 2 originals or 2 copies of the contract, including the Appendix (if any); if the contract is made in a language other than Vietnamese, it must be enclosed with a Vietnamese translation;

  (iii) The original of the Protection Title (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects) or a copy of the relevant Protection Title (in case of transfer of the right to use industrial property objects), if the Contract to be approved is a secondarily licensed one, must be enclosed with a copy of the Certificate of registration of the exclusive contract on secondarily licensed object;

  (iv) Written consent of co-owners regarding the transfer of rights, if the relevant industrial property rights are under joint ownership; or in case the above-mentioned agreement cannot be reached, there must be a written explanation for the reason for the dissent of the remaining co-owners;

  (v) Business license of the Receiving Party in case of transfer of ownership or transfer of the right to use a mark;

  (vi) Documents on payment of fees for approval of the Contract;

  (vii) Power of attorney (if necessary).

  19.2. The dossier of approval shall be submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property according to regulations on submission of Applications (point 10 of this Circular).

  The time limit for submitting the dossier of approval for the Contract is 60 days from the date of signing the Contract. This time limit may be extended if the applicant can prove that the delay is justifiable.

  19.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall receive the dossier of approval according to regulations on receipt of Applications at Point 11 of this Circular, with appropriate amendments, in which the documents mentioned at Point 11.2(i) are: Declaration; Contract for transfer of industrial property rights; Documents on payment of fees and Power of attorney.

  19.4. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the dossier of approval within two months from the date of receipt of the dossier as follows:

  a/ In case the dossier of approval is valid and the contents of the contract are consistent with regulations, the National Office of Industrial Property shall report on the results of its consideration of the dossier of approval and propose the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to issue a decision approving the contract within 15 days after receiving the report of the National Office of Industrial Property.

  b/ In case the dossier contains errors which can be corrected (except for cases specified in Point c Below), the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant of approval to correct such errors within an appropriate time limit.

  The time limit for the applicant of approval to correct errors in the dossier shall not be included in the time limit for considering the dossier.

  c/ In case the dossier of approval is invalid for the following reasons, the National Office of Industrial Property shall propose the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to refuse to approve the contract for transfer of industrial property rights:

  (i) The applicant of approval fails to correct errors within the time limit notified by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  (ii) The applicant of approval is not the person defined at Point 19.1(i) above;

  (iii) The transferee is not the owner of the protection title (for the case of transfer of the right to own industrial property objects); or is neither the owner of the protection title nor the licensee of the exclusive right and the transferee of the right to sub-license the corresponding industrial property object (for the case of transfer of the right to use industrial property objects).

  (iv) The receiving party does not have a license for trading in goods or services appropriate to the corresponding certificate of registered mark (in case of transfer of industrial property rights to marks);

  (v) The corresponding industrial property rights are no longer valid; or the subject matter of industrial property is being in dispute;

  (vi) There are grounds to confirm that the transfer infringes upon the industrial property rights of a third party;

  (vii) The contract contains contents which are not appropriate to the regulations on conditions restricting the transfer, or/and lacks mandatory contents as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree and Points 17.2, 17.3 and 17.4 of this Circular;

  (viii) The contract has no clause on price or the transferred price is outside the minimum and maximum limits prescribed;

  (ix) The contract has no full signature of the Transferor and Receiving party and/or the signature is not legally certified;

  (x) The person signing the Contract is not authorized to sign.

  19.5. Before requesting approval refusal of the Contract, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the results of the examination of the dossier of approval, the intended refusal, the reason for refusal and fix a proper time limit for the applicant to give his/her opinion. If past such fixed time limit the applicant for approval does not give any opposing opinion or his/her opposing opinion is groundless, the National Office of Industrial Property shall officially request the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment to refuse to approve the Contract.

  20. Procedures for registration of contracts on transfer of industrial property rights 20.1. A dossier of application for registration of a contract (below referred to as registration dossier) shall comprise the following documents:

  (i) Declaration for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract, made according to a form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 2 copies;

  (ii) 2 originals or 2 copies of the Transfer Contract, including the Appendix (if any); if the Contract is made in a language other than Vietnamese, it must be enclosed with the Vietnamese translation of the Contract;

  (iii) The original of the Title of protection (in case of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects); or a copy of the Title of protection (in case of transfer of the right to use industrial property objects), if the contract to be registered is a secondarily licensed contract, it must be enclosed with a certificate of registration of a secondarily licensed exclusive contract in the corresponding area;

  (iv) Written consent of co-owners on the transfer of rights if the corresponding industrial property rights are under joint ownership; or, if such an agreement cannot be reached, there must be a written explanation for the dissenting co-owners;

  (v) The license to operate of the transferee in case of transfer of ownership or use right of a mark;

  (vi) Decision on approval of the Contract of the Minister of Science, Technology and Environment (if the Contract is required to be approved);

  (vii) Documents on payment of fees for registration of contracts;

  (viii) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  In case both approval and registration procedures are required, documents submitted in the Approval dossier shall be regarded as documents of the Registration dossier.

  20.2. The Registration dossier shall be submitted and received according to regulations like the Approval dossier (points 19.2 and 19.3 of this Circular).

  20.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the Registration dossier within 2 months from the date of receipt of the dossier, except for the approved Contract, the above time limit is 15 days.

  a/ In case the Registration dossier is valid and the contents of the Contract conform with regulations, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a Decision on grant of Certificate of registration of Contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property object or Protection Title in case of transfer of a part of the List of products or services; or Certificate of registration of License Contract and carry out the following procedures:

  (i) Recording in the National Register of Industrial Property and Register of Contracts for Transfer of Ownership of Industrial Property Objects or Register of License Contracts for each respective case;

  (ii) Recording in the Protection Title (for cases of transfer of ownership of industrial property objects) and granting the Protection Title to the transferee of trademark ownership for a part of the List of products or services;

  (iii) Stamping the registration seal on 2 copies of the Contract and giving 1 copy to the applicant for registration and keeping 1 copy;

  (iv) Granting to the applicant for registration a copy of the Certificate of registration of contract for transfer of ownership of industrial property object or the Certificate of registration of license contract;

  (v) Publicizing the decision on grant of the Certificate of registration in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.

  b/ In case the application file contains errors which can be corrected (except for cases specified in Clause c below), the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify such to the applicant and request him/her to correct the errors within an appropriate time limit.

  The time limit for the applicant to correct errors in the application file shall not be included in the time limit for examination of the application file under regulations.

  c/ In case the application file is invalid for the following reasons, the National Office of Industrial Property shall refuse to register the contract:

  (i) The applicant fails to correct errors within the time limit set by the National Office of Industrial Property or fails to make satisfactory correction;

  (ii) The applicant is neither the Transmitting Party nor the Receiving Party of the Contract for Transfer of Ownership of Industrial Property Object nor an authorized industrial property representative as provided for;

  (iii) The Transmitting Party is not the owner of the protection title (for the case of transfer of ownership of industrial property object); or is not the owner of the protection title but also the transferee of the exclusive license and the licensee of the secondary license for the corresponding industrial property object (for the case of license transfer).

  (iv) The transferee does not have a license for trading in goods or services appropriate to the certificate of registered mark (in case of transfer of industrial property rights to marks);

  (v) Industrial property rights are expired or in dispute;

  (vi) There are grounds to confirm that the transfer of industrial property rights infringes upon industrial property rights of a third party;

  (vii) The contract contains contents which are not appropriate to regulations on conditions restricting the transfer as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree, or/or contains mandatory contents as prescribed in Points 17.2, 17.3 and 17.4 of this Circular;

  (viii) The contract is not fully signed by both parties and/or the signature is not legally certified;

  (ix) The signatory is not authorized to sign;

  (x) There is no decision approving the contract (if the contract is required to be approved).

  d) Before officially refusing to register the contract, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant of the results of the examination of the registration application, the intended refusal, the reason for refusal and set a reasonable time limit for the applicant to provide feedback. After such time limit, if the applicant does not provide any objection or an unjustifiable objection, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to register the contract for transfer of industrial property rights, clearly stating the reasons therefor.

  21. Consider applications for non-voluntary license

  21.1. A dossier of application for issuance of non-voluntary licenses shall comprise the following documents:

  (i) Declaration requesting the grant of non-voluntary license, made according to a form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  (ii) Documents proving the special significance of the invention, utility solution or industrial design for national defense and security, public health protection and environmental protection, and documents proving that the invention, utility solution or industrial design is not used by the industrial property owner (or the person who has been wholly licensed to use such subject matter) for any legitimate reason or at a level which does not meet the needs of national defense and security, public health protection and environmental protection;

  (iii) Documents proving the applicant’s ability to use the invention, utility solution or industrial design and conditions which the applicant considers reasonable but have been offered without a plausible reason and rejected by the industrial property owner (or person wholly assigned the right to use the industrial property object);

  (iv) The documents on payment of fee for application for issuance of non-voluntary license.

  (v) Power of attorney (if necessary).

  21.2. Applications for non-voluntary licenses shall be filed with the National Office of Industrial Property.

  21.3. After receiving the dossier of application for non-voluntary license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the dossier according to the provisions in Clause 5, Article 51 of the Decree. The procedures for considering the dossier of application for non-voluntary license are similar to those for approving the contract on transfer of industrial property rights (Point 19 of this Circular).

  Chapter IV

      HANDLING OF INTERNATIONAL APPLICATIONS FOR INVENTIONS/UTILITARIAN SOLUTIONS REGISTERED AS NATIONAL MARKS

  22. Procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property.

  The regulations on filing and carrying out other relevant procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property as stated in Clauses 2 and 3, Article 15 of the Decree and Point 3 of this Circular shall also apply to the procedures before the National Office of Industrial Property for international applications for inventions, utility solutions and marks mentioned in this Chapter.

  23. Handling of international applications for inventions/utility solutions under the PCT Convention

  23.1. Receiving agency.

  The agency competent to receive international applications in Vietnam is the National Office of Industrial Property.

  The National Office of Industrial Property shall have to:

  (a) Receive international applications of Vietnamese origin;

  (b) Collect fees and transfer them to the International Office and the International Searching Agency under the provisions of the Treaty;

  (c) Check whether the prescribed fees are paid on time;

  (d) Examine and process international applications of Vietnamese origin under the provisions of the Treaty;

  (e) Identify objects of protection application; if the objects of protection application of the application fall into the category of national secrets, the following procedures shall not be carried out and the fees shall be refunded to the applicant, excluding the fee for sending and copying of international applications;

  (f) Send one copy (dossier copy) of the international application of Vietnamese origin to the International Office and one copy (search copy) to the International Searching Agency;

  (g) Send and receive letters from the applicant and from international agencies.

  23.2. Language

  An international application of Vietnamese origin filed with the National Office of Industrial Property must be made in English or Russian. Each application shall be made in 3 copies.

  In case the number of copies prescribed is not enough, the National Office of Industrial Property shall make additional copies for the necessary number of copies and the applicant shall pay an international application copying fee.

  23.3. International search agencies and international preliminary examination bodies.

  For Vietnamese origin international units, the competent international search offices and preliminary examination offices shall be the patent offices of Australia, Austria, the Russian Federation, Switzerland and the European Patent Office.

  23.4. International applications with designation of Vietnam

  If an international application contains a designation of Vietnam, the National Office of Industrial Property shall be the designated agency. In this case, in order to enter the national phase, within 21 months from the priority date, the applicant shall submit it to the National Office of Industrial Property:

  (i) The application form for grant of an invention patent/utility solution, made according to a form promulgated by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 3 copies;

  (ii) Three copies of the international application (if the applicant requests the national stage before the international publication);

  (iii) Vietnamese translation of the international application (including: Description, Protection Request (original submitted; amended and explained according to Article 19 of the PCT Treaty), Summary, and Notes on drawings), in 3 copies;

  (iv) National fees.

  23.5. International applications selected for Vietnam

  If an international application for preliminary examination is selected for Vietnam, the National Office of Industrial Property shall be the selected agency. In this case, if the selection for Vietnam is made within 19 months from the priority date to enter the national stage, within 31 months from the priority date, the applicant must submit to the National Office of Industrial Property:

  (i) An application form for the grant of an invention patent/utility solution, made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, in 3 copies;

  (ii) Vietnamese translation of the international application (including: Description, Protection Request (original submitted; amended and explained according to Article 19 of the PCT Treaty), Summary, and Notes on drawings), in 3 copies;

  (iii) Vietnamese translation of the Appendixes to the Preliminary Examination Report, in 3 copies;

  (iv) National fees.

  23.6. Documents applying for priority right

  In order to enjoy the priority right, the international applicant shall have to submit to the International Office the necessary documents according to Rule 17.1(a) of the Implementing Regulation; and shall have to submit to the National Office of Industrial Property three Vietnamese versions of such documents within the time limits prescribed at Points 23.4 and 23.5 above.

  23.7. To amend and supplement documents in the national stage

  In accordance with Rule 51 bis of the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Convention, the applicant shall submit a Power of attorney and a Letter of assignment of the right to file an international application in the International Phase (if any) within 24 months from the priority date for an international application designating Vietnam and 34 months for an international application selecting Vietnam.

  In accordance with Article 28 and Article 41 of the PCT Agreement, during the national phase, the applicant may amend or supplement documents of the Application in accordance with Point 16.4 of this Circular.

  Additional and amended documents submitted by applicants to the National Office of Industrial Property must be made in Vietnamese and in three copies.

  23.8. The starting time of the national stage.

  The time limit for the start of processing of an international application with Vietnam designated or selected at the national stage shall be the first day of the 22nd month from the priority date, if Vietnam is designated, or the 32nd month from the priority date, if Vietnam is selected, and such selection has been made before the end of 19 months from the priority date, if the applicant does not request entry into the national stage earlier than the above-mentioned time limits.

  23.9. Testing of international applications

  An international application shall be examined for its form and contents according to procedures applicable to national applications.

  23.10. International applications shall be considered as withdrawn

  Apart from cases which are considered as withdrawn under the provisions of the PCT Treaty and the Implementing Regulations, in case the national fee is not paid to the National Office of Industrial Property or there is no Vietnamese translation after the time limit prescribed at Points 23.4 and 23.5 above, the international application with designation of Vietnam shall be considered as withdrawn.

  23.11. Charges

  International applicants of Vietnamese origin shall pay fees at the prescribed rates and according to the procedures defined in the Regulation on implementation of the PCT Agreement and the Joint Circular of the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment.

  24. To prepare and submit applications for registration of international marks of Vietnamese origin to foreign countries under the Madrid Agreement

  24.1. Any individual, juridical person or other entity may file an international registration application for a mark under the Madrid Agreement, provided that such mark has been registered in Vietnam.

  24.2. Registration application

  An international mark registration application shall be made in French according to a form provided free of charge by the National Office of Industrial Property, and shall be filled in the special sections reserved for applicants (except for the sections reserved for the National Office of Industrial Property and the International Office), and enclosed with sample marks. The application shall specify the countries of the Madrid Agreement of which an applicant wishes to have his/her mark protected. The applicant shall preliminarily calculate the total fee payable to the International Office according to the fee schedule on the application form. If the applicant is sure that the calculated fee is correct or after receiving the National Office of Industrial Property's notification of the exact fee payable, the applicant shall pay such fee to the International Office. Furthermore, the applicant shall also pay an additional fee as prescribed by regulations for the National Office of Industrial Property.

  24.3. The receiving agency

  An international mark registration application shall be filed with an International Office through the National Office of Industrial Property.

  The date on which the Industrial Property Department receives the Application shall be regarded as the date of receipt of the Application at the International Office if the International Office receives the Application within 2 months from such date.

  24.4. Sending the Application to the International Office

  After an Application is filed with the International Office, all transactions between the Applicant and the International Office shall be conducted through the National Office of Industrial Property, including the modification of documents, restriction of the product list, transfer of registered rights.

  25. Processing applications for international registration of marks with Vietnam designation

  25.1. After receiving a Notice of the International Office on an international mark registration application with designation of Vietnam, the National Office of Industrial Property shall examine the contents of such application as for marks submitted directly to the National Office of Industrial Property. Within 12 months after a mark is registered internationally, the National Office of Industrial Property shall make a conclusion on the possibility of protection of such mark. If the mark has no possibility of protection or is partially rejected, within the above-said time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify in writing the applicant through the International Office, clearly stating the reason for rejection.

  If within the above-said time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property does not issue a notice of refusal, the mark shall be accepted for protection in Vietnam.

  25.2. Within three months from the date on which the National Office of Industrial Property sends the Notice of refusal, the applicant may lodge a complaint about the Decision of the National Office of Industrial Property. The procedures for lodging and settling complaints shall be the same as those applicable to applications for marks filed directly with the National Office of Industrial Property. The results of the complaint settlement shall be notified by the National Office of Industrial Property to the applicant and to the International Office.

  25.3. Marks accepted for protection in Vietnam under the Madrid Agreement shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette. The scope (volume) of protection shall be certified according to the contents of such mark registration and certified by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) and the National Office of Industrial Property.

  Chapter 5.

   AMENDMENTS TO INVENTORY NOTICE; MAINTENANCE OF EFFECTIVENESS OF INVENTORY NOTICE ON INVENTIONS AND UTILITIES; EXTENSION OF EFFECTIVENESS OF INVENTORY NOTICE ON INDUSTRIAL DESIGNS, MARKS AND APPellations FOR GOODS.

  26. To amend the titles of protection

  26.1. The owner of a protection title shall have the obligation to notify in writing the National Office of Industrial Property of all changes in the name and address of the owner of the protection title.

  26.2. The owner of a protection title may request the National Office of Industrial Property to narrow the scope (volume) of protection of an industrial design or a mark by removing one or several schemes of the industrial design, modifying a number of details of the mark which, however, does not alter the essence of such mark; or by reducing a number of products on the list of products and services stated in the protection title.

  26.3. In order to modify the above-said contents, the owner of a protection title shall file an application for modification of the protection title to the National Office of Industrial Property. The application for modification shall be made according to a form set by the National Office of Industrial Property and enclosed with: (i) The original of the protection title; (ii) Documents certifying the modification of the name and address of the owner of the protection title; (iii) Two sets of photos or drawings of the plan showing the industrial design to be deleted; (iv) Ten samples of the modified mark; (v) Receipt for payment of fee for modification of the protection title; (vi) A power of attorney, if necessary.

  26.4. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider an application for amendment of a title of protection within two months from the date of receipt of such application. If the application is considered valid and the proposed amendment does not increase the scope (volume) of protection or does not change the nature of the protected subject matter, the National Office of Industrial Property shall carry out the amendment of the title of protection, register the new title and publish such change in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property. In the opposite case, the National Office of Industrial Property shall notify the applicant of the refusal to make the proposed amendment, clearly stating the reason therefor.

  27. Maintenance of validity

  In order to maintain the validity of an invention or utility solution title, its owner shall pay a validity maintenance fee within six (6) months before the expiry date of such title. The validity maintenance fee may be paid after the above-said time limit but not more than six (6) months from the expiry date of the previous valid term and the protection title holder shall pay an additional fee equal to ten (10) per cent of the fee for each month of late payment.

  28. Extension of validity

  28.1. In order to have the validity of a protection title extended, within six months before the expiry date of such title, the owner of the protection title shall file an application for extension with the National Office of Industrial Property.

  An application for extension may be filed after the above-said time limit but not more than six months from the date the title of protection expires and the applicant shall pay an extension fee plus 10% of the extension fee for each month of delay in payment.

  28.2. The dossier of application for extension of validity of a title of protection shall comprise the following documents:

  (i) The declaration for extension of validity of the Title of protection, made according to the form issued by the National Office of Industrial Property, comprising 2 copies;

  (ii) Original copies of the Certificates of protection;

  (iii) The documents on payment of fee for extension;

  (iv) Power of attorney (if necessary);

  28.3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall consider an application for extension within two months from the date of receipt of such application. The National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision on extension, record it in the title of protection and register it and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette if the application does not fall into the following cases:

  - The application for extension is invalid or is filed in contravention of the prescribed procedures;

  - There are grounds to believe that the owner of the mark registration certificate or the owner of the certificate of origin of goods name has not used such mark or origin of goods name or such subject matter continuously for the last five years before the expiration date of the protection title without a plausible reason;

  - The applicant for extension is not the owner of the protection title of the corresponding mark, origin of goods name or industrial design.

  If the application falls into one of the above cases, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice of refusal to extend the protection title, clearly stating the reason.

  Chapter VI

                                       PROCEDURES FOR GRANTING REPRESENTATION LICENSES

  29. Dossiers of application for representation licenses

  29.1. A representation license shall be granted by the National Office of Industrial Property on the basis of examination of the dossier of application for a representation license as stipulated in Points 29.2 and 29.3 below. The applicant for a representation license shall pay a fee according to regulations.

  29.2. An application file for the certificate of an industrial property representation service organization shall comprise:

  - An application for the certificate of an industrial property representation service organization, which includes a proposal on the list of industrial property representatives of the organization.

  - A copy of the operation charter and the business registration certificate;

  - A copy of the decision appointing the head of the organization or a written authorization for the organization's representation signed by the head of the organization and one of the members in the above-mentioned proposed list;

  - The charge table for industrial property representation services of the organization after the registration procedures have been completed according to regulations on the management of charges and fees;

  - The charge payment document for the grant of the representation license.

  The application file for the certificate of an industrial property representation service organization must be filed together with the application file for the card of an industrial property representative of an individual in the above-mentioned proposed list.

  29.3. An application file for the card of an industrial property representative shall comprise:

  - The application for grant of an industrial property representative card, which contains certification by the People's Committee of the ward, commune or township of the applicant's permanent residence address;

  - Copies of university degrees;

  - A copy of the certificate of graduation from a formal training course in industrial property; or a written certification of the duration and job performed as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 58 of the Decree;

  - A copy of the certificate of passing an examination on the current law on industrial property issued by the National Office of Industrial Property;

  - A copy of the decision on recruitment issued by the industrial property representation service organization;

  - The fee receipt is the receipt of the application.

  30. Consider application for license to act as representative

  Within one month after receiving an application for a representative license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider issuing or refusing to issue such license. In case of refusal, it shall clearly state the reason therefor to the applicant. In case of acceptance and the applicant has paid the fee for the issuance of a representative license, the National Office of Industrial Property shall carry out the following procedures:

  - Granting representative permits;

  - Recording the grant of a representation license in the industrial property representation book;

  - To publish the grant of representative agent certificates in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.

  Chapter VII

                                         FINAL PROVISIONS

  31. Complaints

  If disagreeing with a decision related to the processing of an Application as well as the approval and registration of an Industrial Property Right Transfer Contract, consideration of an Application for issuance of non-voluntary license, extension of a Protection Title or grant of a Representative License, the Applicant may lodge complaints or lawsuits according to the order and procedures specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4, Article 27 of the Decree.

  32. To process applications filed from July 1, 1996 to the effective date of this Circular.

  Applications filed from July 1st, 1996 to the effective date of this Circular may use the documents made according to the Circular No. 1134/SC of October 17th, 1991 of the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment guiding the implementation of Decree No. 84/HDBT of March 20th, 1990 of the Council of Ministers, and shall be processed according to this Circular. The time limit for processing the above-mentioned applications shall be extended by the period from the date of filing of the applications to the effective date of this Circular.

  33. Document forms and Regulations on testing of applications

  The Director of the National Office of Industrial Property shall be responsible for promulgating necessary forms related to Applications and related to the approval and registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights, and promulgating the Regulations on examination of Applications.

  34. Implementation

  This Circular replaces the following documents of the State Committee for Science and Technology, the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment:

  - Chapters II, III and IV of Circular No. 1134/SC of October 17, 1991 guiding the implementation of Decree No. 84/HDBT of March 20, 1990 of the Council of Ministers,

  - Circular No. 437/SC of March 19, 1993 providing guidance on the registration of trademarks,

  - Circular No. 163/TT-SHCN of April 15, 1994 guiding the implementation of the regulations on approval and registration of license contracts,

  - Circular No. 238-TT-SHCN of May 2, 1994 guiding the filing and processing of international applications for protection of inventions/utility solutions under the Patent Cooperation Treaty in Vietnam;

  - Decision No. 199-QD of December 21, 1992 promulgating regulations on industrial property representation.

  This Circular takes effect 15 days after its signing.
