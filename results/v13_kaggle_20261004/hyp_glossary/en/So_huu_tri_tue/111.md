                                            CIRCULAR
 GUIDANCE ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 103/2006/ND-CP OF SEPTEMBER 22, 2006, PROVIDING DETAILED REGULATIONS AND GUIDANCE ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE LAW ON INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY
Pursuant to the Law on Intellectual Property dated November 29, 2005;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP of September 22, 2006, providing detailed regulations and guidance for a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 54/2003/ND-CP of May 19, 2003, defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Science and Technology, and the Government’s Decree No. 28/2004/ND-CP of January 16, 2004, amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 54/2003/ND-CP;
The Ministry of Science and Technology guides the implementation of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP dated September 22, 2006 of the Government as follows:
Chapter I
                 PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Section 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS ON PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
1. Grounds for establishment of industrial property rights
1.1 Industrial property rights arising or established on the grounds specified in Clause 3, Article 6 of the Law on Intellectual Property dated November 29, 2005 (hereinafter referred to as "the Law on Intellectual Property"), Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 6 of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP dated September 22, 2006 of the Government detailing and guiding a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property (hereinafter referred to as "the Decree on Industrial Property") and specific provisions at this Point.
1.2 Industrial property rights to inventions, layout designs of semiconductor integrated circuits (hereinafter referred to as “layout designs”), industrial designs and marks shall be established on the basis of decisions of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam on grant of protection titles to registrants of such objects. Persons who are granted protection titles by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam are owners and enjoy rights to industrial property objects within the scope of protection stated in the protection titles and during the validity period of the protection titles. When disputes arise, owners of industrial property objects may use protection titles as grounds for proving their rights without any other evidence.
1.3 Industrial property rights to geographical indications shall be established on the basis of decisions of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam on the grant of certificates of registration of geographical indications to geographical indication managers.

1.4 Industrial property rights to internationally registered marks under the Madrid Agreement and Protocol (hereinafter referred to as “internationally registered marks”) shall be established on the basis of decisions on acceptance of protection or certificates of protection of internationally registered marks in Vietnam granted by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam at the request of mark owners. Such decisions and certificates shall have the same validity as protection titles granted to mark registrants in Vietnam.
1.5 Industrial property rights to well-known marks shall be established on the basis of extensive and practical use which makes such marks well known without having to carry out registration procedures at the National Office of Intellectual Property. When exercising and settling disputes over rights to well-known marks, owners of such marks must prove their rights with evidence in accordance with Article 75 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
1.6 Industrial property rights to trade names shall be established on the basis of lawful use of such trade names without having to carry out registration procedures at the National Office of Intellectual Property. When exercising and settling disputes over rights to trade names, entities with such trade names must prove their rights with evidence showing time, territory and field in which such trade names have been used.
1.7 Industrial property rights to business secrets shall be established on the basis of financial or intellectual investment activities or results of other lawful activities to discover, create or acquire information forming a business secret and to keep such information confidential without having to carry out registration procedures at the National Office of Intellectual Property. When exercising and settling disputes over rights to business secrets, entities with such business secrets must prove their rights with evidence showing activities in which information forming the business secrets is created, discovered, acquired and measures to keep such information confidential.
1.8 Right to combat unfair competition shall be established on the basis of practical competition activities without having to carry out registration procedures at the National Office of Intellectual Property. When exercising the right to combat unfair competition, entities must prove their rights with evidence showing objects, fields, territories and time of business related to competition activities.
2. Industrial property registration applicants
2.1 Industrial property registration application applicants (hereinafter referred to as “applicants”) are organizations and individuals filing applications for registration of inventions, layout designs, industrial designs, marks or geographical indications. When protection titles for inventions, layout designs, industrial designs, marks or geographical indications are granted, the applicants shall be recognized as holders of such protection titles. When protection titles for geographical indications are granted, the applicants shall be recognized as registrants of such geographical indications.
2.2 The applicant shall satisfy conditions on the right to register industrial property specified in Articles 86, 87 and 88 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Articles 7, 8 and 9 of the Decree on Industrial Property. If such conditions are not satisfied, the registration of industrial property shall be considered invalid.
3. Representatives of applicants
3.1 The applicant may, by himself/herself or through a lawful representative in Vietnam, carry out procedures for registration of industrial property at the Department
Intellectual property as prescribed in this Point and Point 4 of this Circular.
3.2 The following organizations and individuals may represent the applicant:
a) For organizations and individuals specified in Clause 1 Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property:
(i) In case the applicant is an individual: the legal representative or authorized representative of the applicant, or the industrial property representation service provider authorized by the applicant;
(ii) In case the applicant is an organization: the legal representative of the applicant or a person authorized by the legal representative of the applicant; an industrial property representation service organization (authorized by the applicant); the head of a representative office or a branch in Vietnam (if the applicant is a foreign organization).
b) For organizations and individuals specified in Clause 2, Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property: industrial property representation service organizations (authorized by the applicants).
3.3 When carrying out procedures for industrial property registration, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may only conduct transactions with the applicant or the lawful representative of the applicant. Organizations and individuals not falling into the cases specified in Point 3.2 of this Circular that act as representatives of the applicant shall be regarded as unlawful representatives.
4. Authorization for representation in carrying out procedures for industrial property registration 4.1 The authorization for representation and the performance of authorization for representation in carrying out procedures for industrial property registration (hereinafter referred to as "authorization") must comply with the provisions of law on authorization in Part Three of the Civil Code and the provisions of this Circular.
4.2 The authorization must be made in writing (power of attorney) and must contain the following main contents:
a/ Name (full name) and address of the authorizing party and the authorized party;
b/ Name (full name) and address of the authorized party or the re-authorized party (if any);
c/ Scope of authorization and volume of work authorized;
d) Authorization period (an indefinite letter of authorization shall cease to be effective when the authorizing party declares termination of authorization);
e) Date of signing of the power of attorney;
g) Signature (specify full name, position and seal, if any) of lawful representative of the authorizing party (and of the authorized replacement party, the re-authorized party, if any).
4.3 The time when a power of attorney is legally valid in transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be determined as follows:
a) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a valid authorization letter;
b) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam accepts the valid authorization or re-authorization;

c) Date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a notice of change to authorization scope, premature termination of authorization, or change to address of the authorized party.
4.4 In case of authorization substitution or re-authorization, the power of attorney shall be considered valid only if the authorized party or the re-authorized party commits to take responsibility for all issues arising from the previous transaction with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam conducted by the authorizing party.

4.5 If the power of attorney has a scope of authorization including multiple procedures independent of each other and the original copy of the power of attorney has been submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, when carrying out subsequent procedures, the authorized party must submit copies of the power of attorney with accurate instructions to the application number of the original copy of the power of attorney.
5. Responsibilities of the applicant and the representative of the applicant
5.1 The applicant and his representative shall ensure the truthfulness of information and documents provided to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam during the process of industrial property registration in accordance with the following regulations:
a/ All transaction documents must be certified by the applicant’s signature or that of his/her representative and affixed with the organization’s seal (if any). In case a notarization or certification by another competent agency is required, such certification shall be made according to regulations;
b/ All Vietnamese translations of foreign-language documents must be certified by the applicant or his/her representative that they are made verbatim from the original texts.
5.2 The applicant shall be responsible for all consequences and obligations arising from the representative's transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5.3 The representative of the applicant shall be responsible before the applicant for all consequences caused by the untruthful declaration or provision of information in transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam; if damage is caused, compensation must be made.
5.4 The applicant and his representative shall be collectively referred to as "applicant" below, except for special regulations.
6. Handling of opinions of third parties before the issuance of decisions on grant of protection titles 6.1 From the date an industrial property registration application is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette up until before the date of issuance of a decision on grant of a protection title, any organization or individual may express written opinions on the registration right, priority right, protection conditions and other matters related to the industrial property registration application according to Article 112 of the Law on Intellectual Property to the National Office of Intellectual Property. Written opinions of third parties shall be regarded as information sources for the processing of industrial property registration applications.
6.2 Within 1 month from the date of receipt of written opinions from a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such opinions to the applicant and impose a maximum time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response. After receiving the feedback from the applicant, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the third party and impose a maximum time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the third party to provide a written response. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the feedback from the applicant and the third party based on evidence, arguments provided by the parties and documents included in the application.
6.3 In case the opinion of a third party is considered groundless, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall not notify such opinion to the applicant but shall notify the third party of the refusal to consider such opinion, clearly stating the reasons.
6.4 In case opinions of a third party are related to registration rights and it is deemed impossible to determine whether such opinions are well-grounded or not, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the third party to file an application with a court for settlement. Within 1 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification, if the third party fails to notify the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam that it has filed an application with the court for settlement, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider that the third party withdraws its opinion. If the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives notification from the third party within the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the application pending the dispute settlement results of the court. After receiving the court's handling results, the application shall be processed in accordance with such results.
6.5 The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize direct dialogues between the third party and the applicant to further clarify issues subject to objection if necessary and at the request of both parties.
6.6 The time limit for the applicant to respond to the objection of a third party shall not be included in the time limit for the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to carry out relevant procedures as prescribed.
7. General requirements for applications for registration of industrial property
7.1 Minimum documents

The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall only accept applications for registration of industrial property (hereinafter referred to as "applications") if they contain at least the documents specified in Points a, b, and e Clause 1 Article 100, Clause 1 Article 108 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following specific regulations:
a) For applications for registration of inventions, layout designs, industrial designs, marks, and geographical indications, the mandatory documents required for acceptance of an application include:
(i) Declaration of registration;
(ii) Documents, samples, and information describing the subject matter of industrial property registered; Specifically: for an invention registration application, the invention description; for an industrial design registration application, a set of photos, a set of drawings, and a description of the industrial design; for a mark registration application, the mark samples and the list of goods and services bearing the mark; for a geographical indication registration application, a description of the characteristics and quality of products bearing the geographical indication and a map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication;
(iii) Receipts for payment of fees and charges.
If any of the above-mentioned documents is missing, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may refuse to accept the application.
b) For applications for registration of collective marks or certification marks, in addition to the documents specified in Point 7.1.a above, the application is also required to have the following additional documents:
(i) Regulations on use of the collective mark/certification mark;
(ii) Presentation of characteristics and specific quality of products bearing marks (if the marks are registered as collective marks used for products with specific characteristics or as marks certifying quality of products or as marks certifying geographical origin);

(iii) Map determining territory (if the mark is registered as a mark certifying geographical origin of products).
7.2 Requirements for applications
a) The application shall satisfy the general requirements specified in Articles 100 and 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the specific requirements applicable to each type of industrial property subject matter prescribed in Articles 102, 103, 104, 105 and 106 of the Law on Intellectual Property, which are guided in detail in Points 23, 28, 33, 37 and 43 of this Circular.
b/ In order to ensure technical requirements in the processing process, the application must also satisfy the following formal requirements:
(i) Each application may request the grant of only one title of protection and the type of title of protection requested shall be appropriate to the subject matter of industrial property stated in the application;
(ii) All documents of the application must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents which may be made in other languages as prescribed in Points 7.3 and 7.4 of this Circular;
(iii) All documents of the application shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one sheet of paper of A4 size (210 mm x 297 mm), with margins of 20 mm on each side, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
(iv) For documents to be made according to set forms, it is required to use such forms and fill in all information as requested in appropriate places;
(v) Each document, if comprising multiple pages, must have each page bearing the ordinal number in Arabic numerals;
(vi) Documents must be typed or printed with an ink of such a type that it is not erased, modified or altered; if there are minor errors in spelling of documents submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the applicant may correct them but must bear his/her signature (and seal, if any) at the correction place;
(vii) Terms used in the application must be common terms (not local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with Vietnam’s standards;
(viii) The application may be enclosed with supplementary documents being objects carrying electronic data of part or all of the contents of the application.
c) The application must fully satisfy the requirements regarding the number of documents, samples, drawings, photos and specific requirements applicable to each type of application as prescribed in this Circular.

d) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure that all mandatory and consistent information is provided; the Vietnamese translation of the application documents must be consistent with the original copies; the power of attorney must cover the tasks within the scope of authorization.
e) The subjects stated in the application must be accurately grouped and classified as prescribed.
g/ For documents requiring certification by competent agencies (the first receiving agency, notary public, people's committee, etc.), there must be their seals for certification. 7.3 The following documents may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese:
a/ Power of attorney;
b/ Documents certifying registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person (certificate of inheritance; certificate or agreement on transfer of the right to file an application, including transfer of an already filed application; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.);
c/ Documents evidencing the basis for enjoying priority rights (certificate of the receiving agency for copies of the first (first) application(s); list of goods and services in the first mark registration application; paper transferring the priority right if such right is inherited from another person).
7.4 The following documents may be made in other languages besides Vietnamese but must be translated into Vietnamese when requested by the National Office of Intellectual Property:
a/ Copies of the first application to prove the basis for enjoying priority rights;
b/ Other documents to support the application.
8. Industrial property registration fees and charges
8.1 Applicants shall pay fees and charges according to regulations of the Ministry of Finance.
8.2 Fees and charges
a) Upon receiving an application or requesting to carry out any other procedures, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to pay fees and charges as prescribed and examine documents on payment of fees and charges enclosed with the application.
b) If the fees and charges have not been fully paid as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of collection and notify it to the applicant.
In case the applicant fully pays the fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue 2 copies of receipts of payment of fees and charges, clearly stating the amounts and rates of fees and charges already paid, of which 1 copy shall be enclosed with the application as a document on payment of fees and charges.
8.3 Refund of fees and charges
a) The paid fees and charges shall be partially or fully refunded at the request of the applicant in the following cases:
(i) The paid fees and charges exceed the prescribed amount;
(ii) The corresponding part of the work is not carried out because the situation for which the work must be carried out does not occur.
b/ In case of acceptance of a request for refund of fees and charges, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall make a note of such refund, clearly stating the amount and method of refund, and send it to the applicant.
c/ In case of refusal of a request for refund of fees and charges, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall notify the applicant thereof, clearly stating the reason therefor.
9. Time limit
9.1 The time limits prescribed in the Law on Intellectual Property, the Decree on Industrial Property and this Circular shall be calculated according to the provisions of Chapter VIII, Part One of the Civil Code.
9.2 The time limit for an applicant or a related party to submit, amend or supplement documents or give opinions may be extended once for the full duration already set, provided that the applicant submits a written request for extension before the deadline and pays the fee as prescribed.
9.3 An applicant may request the National Office of Intellectual Property to carry out procedures ahead of time when so requested in writing and pay the fee as prescribed. In case the National Office of Intellectual Property refuses such request, it shall notify the applicant thereof, clearly stating the reason therefor.
10. Forms of documents and titles of protection

10.1 Forms of documents of applications are specified in the appendices to this Circular. Applicants shall use the above-mentioned forms to prepare documents of their applications when carrying out the procedures for registration of industrial property.
10.2 Forms of protection titles are specified in the annexes of this Circular. The National Office of Intellectual Property shall keep the issued forms of protection titles for examination of the legality of used protection titles. The change of forms of protection titles is only made on the basis of decisions of the Minister of Science and Technology.
11. General procedures All types of applications for registration of industrial property shall be processed by the National Office of Intellectual Property according to the following order: receiving the application; formal examination of the application; announcement of the valid application; substantive examination of the application (except for applications for registration of layout designs which are not subject to substantive examination); grant or refusal to grant a protection title; registration and announcement of the decision on grant of a protection title.
12. Submission and receipt of applications
12.1 Applications may be submitted at the National Office of Intellectual Property or other places designated by the National Office of Intellectual Property. Applications may also be sent by post to the above-mentioned places.
12.2 Upon receipt of an application, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall examine documents enclosed with the application and compare them with the list of documents stated in the declaration in order to conclude whether the application is received or not:
a/ In case the application contains all the minimum documents prescribed at Point 7.1 of this Circular, the applicant-receiving officer shall receive the application, affix the seal certifying the filing date and serial number of the application to the declaration;
b/ In case the application lacks one of the minimum documents prescribed at Point 7.1 of this Circular, the applicant-receiving officer shall refuse to receive the application or send a notice of refusal to receive the application from the National Office of Intellectual Property to the applicant (if the application is submitted by post). For applications refused to be received, the National Office of Intellectual Property is not required to return the application documents to the applicant but must refund the fees and charges already paid according to the fee and charge refunding procedure prescribed at Point 8 of this Circular;
c/ In case the application is received, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall hand over (send) a copy of the declaration to the applicant, which bears the seal certifying the filing date, serial number of the application and the results of examination of the list of documents enclosed with the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the applicant-receiving officer. The above-mentioned handed-over (sent) declaration is valid as a receipt for the application.
13. Formal examination of applications
13.1 Purposes and contents of the formal examination of applications
Formal examination of an application means an examination of compliance with regulations on form of the application in order to conclude whether the application is valid or not.
A valid application shall be considered further. An invalid application shall be rejected (not considered further).
An application shall be considered valid if it satisfies the provisions of Point 7 of this Circular and does not fall into one of the following cases:
a) The application is made in a language other than Vietnamese, except for the cases specified in Points 7.3 and 7.4 of this Circular;
b) The declaration does not contain sufficient information on the author (regarding an invention registration application, industrial design registration application or layout design registration application), on the applicant, on the representative, or does not contain the signature and/or seal of the applicant or the representative; the mark registration application does not contain a list of goods and services; the geographical indication registration application does not list products bearing such geographical indication;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights;
d) The application is filed in contravention of Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
e) The application has the deficiencies specified in Point 13.3 of this Circular which affect the validity of the application and although it has been requested by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to be corrected, the applicant fails to correct or fails to meet the requirements;
g) There are grounds to immediately confirm that the subject matters stated in the application are clearly not protected by the State according to the provisions of Articles 59, 64, 69, 73 and 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property. For an application with multiple subject matters, if it falls into the cases specified at Points 13.2.a and 13.3.b and c of this Circular and the deficiencies only concern one or several subject matters in the application, the application shall be considered partially invalid (corresponding to the deficient subject matters), while for the remaining subject matters, the application shall still be considered valid.
13.3 Handling of deficiencies in the application during the stage of formal examination

If the application has the following defects, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such to the applicant and within 1 month from the date of notification, the applicant must correct such defects;
a) The application fails to meet the formal requirements prescribed in Point 7.2 of this Circular (insufficient copies of one of the mandatory documents; inconsistent application; application does not satisfy the presentation requirements; the trademark registration application does not clearly state the registered trademark, lacks a description of the trademark, the classification results are inaccurate, the priority right document is not provided, if necessary; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or unverified as required, etc.);
b) The fee for filing and publication of the application has not been fully paid;
c) Having no power of attorney or having an invalid power of attorney (if the application is submitted by a representative).
The submission date shall be determined as follows:
a) The filing date is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, recorded in the receipt seal on the declaration as prescribed in Point 12.2.a of this Circular;
b) For an international application with designation or/and selection of Vietnam, the submission date shall be the same as the submission date of the international application.

13.5 Priority date
a/ If an application does not request priority right or requests priority right but is not approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, it shall be regarded as having no priority date.
b/ If the application requests priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in such application and approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
c) The priority date determined according to the request for priority right shall be based on the first application(s) submitted in Vietnam and comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c and dd, Clause 1, Article 10 of the Decree on Industrial Property.
13.6 Notification of the results of the formal examination and notification of the acceptance of the valid application
a/ If the application falls into one of the cases specified at Point 13.2 of this Circular or contains errors specified at Point 13.3 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice to the applicant of its intention to refuse to accept the valid application. Such notice must clearly state the name and address of the applicant; the name of the industrial property representation service organization (if the application is filed through such organization); the subject matter stated in the application; the filing date and serial number of the application; reasons and errors which may lead to the refusal to accept the application and fix a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuing the notice for the applicant to give its opinion or correct errors.
b) In case of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of acceptance of the valid application to the applicant, clearly stating the name and address of the applicant, the name of the authorized representative (if any), and information about the subject matter stated in the application, the submission date, and the priority date (in case the request for priority rights is not approved, the reasons must be clearly stated).
13.7 Refusal to accept applications
In case the applicant has been notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam of its intention to refuse to accept the valid application as prescribed in Point 13.6.a of this Circular but the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction or does not have any objection or provides an unjustifiable objection within the prescribed time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the refusal to accept the application and refund the fees and charges already paid for the post-formal examination work at the request of the applicant.
13.8 Time limit for formal examination of applications
a) The time limit for formal examination is 01 month from the date of submission of the application.
b) If during the process of formal examination of an application, the applicant takes initiative or at the request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to amend or supplement documents, the time limit for formal examination shall be extended by the duration for amendment or supplementation of documents.
c/ Before the date of expiry of the time limit prescribed at Point 13.8.a above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall complete the examination of the form of the application and send a notice of results to the applicant according to the provisions of Point 13.6 of this Circular.
14. Public disclosure of valid applications
14.1 All applications accepted as valid shall be published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Official Gazette. Applicants shall pay an application publication fee.
14.2 Time limit for publication of applications

a) Disclosure of the invention registration application:
(i) An invention registration application shall be published in the 19th month from the priority date or the filing date, if the application does not have a priority date, or within 2 months from the date of acceptance of the valid application, whichever is later;
(ii) An invention registration application under an Agreement on Cooperation in Inventions (hereinafter referred to as “PCT application”) shall be published within 2 months from the date of acceptance of the valid application after it has entered the national stage;

(iii) An invention registration application with early disclosure request shall be published within 2 months from the date the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives such request or from the date of acceptance of the valid application, whichever is later.
b) Disclosure of other applications: Layout design registration applications, industrial design registration applications, mark registration applications and geographical indication registration applications shall be disclosed within 2 months from the date on which the valid application is accepted.
14.3 Contents of the application disclosure
Information relating to a valid application, including also information relating to a split-off application published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette, shall include information relating to the validity of the application in terms of form stated in the notice of acceptance of the valid application, information relating to the valid application (application transfer, application split-off, original number of the split-off application, etc.); an invention summary enclosed with drawings (if any); a set of photos or a drawing of the industrial design; sample of the mark and list of goods and services enclosed therewith; summary of specific characteristics of products bearing the geographical indication and names of products bearing the geographical indication.
14.4 Access to information about valid applications published
Everyone may access detailed information on the nature of subject matters stated in applications published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette or request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to provide such information and shall pay fees for information provision as prescribed.
15. Content appraisal of applications
15.1 Purposes and scope of application
a) The purpose of substantive examination is to assess the subject matter stated in the application according to the conditions for protection and determine the scope (volume) of protection corresponding to such subject matter.
b) Procedures for content appraisal shall not apply to layout design registration applications.
15.2 Use of information lookup results
a) During the process of content appraisal of an invention registration application or industrial design registration application with priority right, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may use information lookup results and appraisal results of the corresponding application submitted overseas.
b) The applicant may (proactively or upon request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam) provide the following documents for the content appraisal of the application:
(i) For an invention registration application or industrial design registration application: results of information search or results of appraisal of the overseas application for the subject matter stated in the application; copies of protection titles already granted based on the identical overseas application; documents related to the technical status of the subject matter stated in the invention registration application provided by the applicant to a competent foreign authority and other documents;
(ii) For a mark registration application or a geographical indication registration application: documents proving that the mark or geographical indication has been protected overseas, including documents describing the process of using the mark or geographical indication and other documents.
15.3 Correction of errors, explanation of application contents, provision of information
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to explain the contents of the application and provide information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application if it is necessary to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter for the content appraisal of the application.
b) Any correction or addition of documents in the application containing errors shall be made by the applicant. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may only make such correction or addition if so requested in writing by the applicant. The applicant shall pay the fee for correction or addition of the application as prescribed. Documents requesting the correction or addition of the application shall be attached to the relevant application and shall be considered the official documents of the application.
15.4 Premature termination of content appraisal
a) In the following cases, the content appraisal of the application shall be terminated prematurely:

(i) The application does not clearly show the nature of the subject matter: documents related to the nature of the subject matter such as the description, list of goods and services... are insufficient information to determine the content of the nature of the subject matter or the information about the nature of the subject matter of the invention registration application is unclear or too brief, too general to determine the subject matter of protection request;
(ii) The subject matter is not suitable for the type of protection title requested to be issued or the subject matter is not protected by the State as prescribed;
(iii) There are reasons to confirm that the subject matter does not meet one or several certain protection conditions, therefore it is unnecessary to evaluate other conditions but still conclude that the subject matter does not meet the protection conditions;
(iv) The applicant fails to comply with the request for correction of errors, explanation of the application contents or provision of necessary information at the request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam specified in point 15.3 of this Circular;
(v) The applicant requests termination of the content appraisal of the application or declares withdrawal or abandonment of the application.
b) Except for the case specified in point 15.4.a (v) above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of premature termination of content appraisal to the applicant, clearly stating the reason and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide its opinion.
15.5 Restoring the content appraisal of the application
a) In case the applicant has a written objection against the notice of premature termination of content appraisal within the time limit specified in point 15.4.b of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider the objection of the applicant.
b) If the objection is justifiable, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall resume the content appraisal and the time reserved for the applicant with an objection shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal. If the objection is unjustifiable, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall officially terminate the content appraisal and issue a notice of refusal to grant the protection title. The applicant may lodge a complaint about this notice according to the procedures prescribed in Point 22 of this Circular.
15.6 Content of appraisal
a) Content appraisal of an application includes the following contents:
(i) Assessment of the conformity between the subject matter stated in the application and the type of protection title requested; (ii) Assessment of the subject matter according to each protection condition;
(iii) Examination of the first-to-file principle.
b) The assessment according to protection conditions shall be carried out one by one for each subject matter (if the application contains multiple subjects while ensuring consistency). For each subject matter, the assessment shall be carried out one by one according to each protection condition:
(i) For an invention registration application, the assessment shall be carried out one by one according to each point stated in the scope (request) of protection;
(ii) For an industrial design registration application, the assessment shall be carried out one by one according to the design of each product (if the application mentions a set of products); in case it mentions multiple solutions, each solution shall be assessed one by one, starting from the basic solution (the first solution stated in the application);
(iii) For a mark registration application, the assessment shall be carried out separately for each component of the mark for each good or service in the list of goods and services.
c/ The content appraisal of each subject matter stated at Points 15.6.b(i), (ii) and (iii) above shall be completed after the subject matter has been assessed with all conditions for protection and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter fails or meets the conditions for protection, specifically:
(i) There are grounds to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy one or more than one of the protection conditions; or (ii) There is no ground to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy at least one protection condition.
d) Before issuing the notice specified in Point 15.7.a of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall review the results of substantive examination on the basis of examining related applications with earlier priority dates received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam after the date of commencement of substantive examination. 15.7. Tasks ending substantive examination
a) Notify the applicant of the results of the content appraisal

No later than the deadline for content appraisal prescribed in Clause 15.8 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send one of the following notices to the applicant:
(i) If the subject matter stated in the application does not satisfy the protection conditions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to grant a protection title, clearly stating the reasons for refusal, and may provide guidance on amendments to the scope (volume) of protection and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide their opinion and meet the requirements. The applicant may request an extension of such time limit under Point 9.2 of this Circular;
(ii) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to grant a protection title, clearly stating the deficiencies in the application and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide explanations or correct the deficiencies. The applicant may request an extension of such time limit under Point 9.2 of this Circular;
(iii) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions or the applicant has corrected deficiencies satisfactorily or provided a legitimate explanation within the time limit prescribed at Points 15.7.a (i) and (ii) above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended grant of a protection title and fix a time-limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of such notice for the applicant to pay the fee for grant of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on grant of a protection title, the registration fee and the fee for maintenance of validity for the first year for an invention. The applicant may request an extension of the above-mentioned time-limit according to Point 9.2 of this Circular.
b) If the time-limit prescribed at Points 15.7.a (i) and (ii) above expires but the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or provides inadequate correction, no objection or inadequate objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to grant a protection title within 15 days from the date of expiration of the above-mentioned time-limit. The applicant may lodge a complaint about such notice according to Point 22 of this Circular.
c) If the applicant fails to pay the fee for grant of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on grant of a protection title, or the registration fee within the time limit prescribed at Point 15.7.a (iii) above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to grant a protection title within 15 days from the date of expiration of the corresponding time-limit. For an invention registration application, if the applicant has paid the fee for grant of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on grant of a protection title, and the registration fee but fails to pay the fee for maintenance of validity for the first year of an invention patent or utility solution patent within the time limit prescribed at Point 15.7.a (iii) above, such protection title shall still be granted but shall cease to have effect immediately after its grant.
15.8 Time-limit
a) The time-limit for substantive examination of an application is determined as follows:
(i) 12 months for an invention registration application from the date of receipt of a request for substantive examination (if such request is filed after the date of publication of the application) or from the date of publication of the application (if such request is filed before the date of publication of the application).
(ii) 06 months from the date of publication of the application for an industrial design registration application, a mark registration application or a geographical indication registration application.
b) If during the process of content appraisal, the applicant takes initiative or at the request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to amend the application, supplement documents or provide explanations, the time limit for content appraisal shall be extended accordingly with the time limit for the applicant to perform such tasks.
16. Re-examination of applications
16.1 Re-examination of applications after receiving objections to the issued titles, after having notified the intended issuance or refusal to issue protection titles
a) The re-examination of an application under Clause 4 Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be carried out in the following cases:
(i) Having a written opinion from the applicant filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam during the period from the date of issuance of the notice of intended grant/intended refusal to issue the title of protection until before the date of issuance of the decision on issuance/notification of official refusal to issue the relevant title of protection; or having a written opinion from a third party with plausible reasons for the lack of conditions or reasonable opportunities to express their opinions; (ii) The opinion prescribed in Point 16.1.a(i) above is well-grounded, accompanied by evidence or indications leading to reliable sources of information;
(iii) The reasons and evidence proving the opinions stated in Point 16.1.a(i) above are different from the reasons and evidence (if any) presented in the previous stage, or although such reasons and evidence are not different but have not yet been answered by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam under Point 6.2 of this Circular.

b) The time limit for re-appraisal of an application shall be equal to two-thirds of the time limit for initial appraisal prescribed in Point 15.8 of this Circular; for complicated cases involving many facts requiring verification or expert opinions, this time limit may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial appraisal.
c) The contents and procedures for re-examination shall comply with the corresponding provisions at Points 15.6 and 15.7 of this Circular.
d) The re-examination of an application may be carried out only once.
16.2 Re-examination of applications due to requests for narrowing of the scope of protection of protection title holders In case a protection title holder requests narrowing of the scope of protection of industrial property rights as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the re-examination of the corresponding application according to the contents and procedures specified in Points 15.6 and 15.7 of this Circular, provided that the applicant pays fees and charges as prescribed.
17. Amendment/supplementation/separation/conversion/transfer of applications
17.1 Amendment/supplementation of applications
a) Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of refusal to accept a valid application or a notice of refusal to grant a protection title or a decision to grant a protection title, the applicant may, on its own initiative or upon request of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, amend/supplement documents of the application.
b) For requests for amendment/supplementation of the following documents, the applicant must submit the amended documents enclosed with detailed explanations of the amendments compared to the initial documents submitted:
(i) Description and summary of invention for invention registration applications;
(ii) Drawings, photos and descriptions of applications for registration of industrial designs;
(iii) Sample mark, list of goods and services bearing the mark for mark registration application;

(iv) Description of particular characteristics, map of geographical area corresponding to geographical indication for geographical indication registration application.
c/ The amendment or supplementation of an application shall not expand the scope (volume) of protection beyond the contents already disclosed in the description for applications for registration of inventions or industrial designs, or the list of goods and services for applications for registration of marks, and shall not change the nature of the subject matter stated in the application. If the amendment expands the scope (volume) of protection or changes the nature of the subject matter, the applicant shall file a new application and carry out all procedures again from the beginning.
d) The applicant may request correction of errors in the name and address of the applicant or author.
e) All requests for amendments shall be made in writing according to Form No. 01-SDD specified in Appendix B of this Circular. Requests may be made for amendments to the same content related to many applications, provided that applicants pay fees corresponding to the number of applications.
g) In case the applicant takes initiative in modifying or supplementing the application documents after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam has issued a notice of acceptance of the valid application, such modification or supplementation shall comply with Points 13.2, 13.3 and 13.6 of this Circular. The contents of the modified or supplemented application shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette according to Point 14 of this Circular and the applicant shall pay an application publication fee as prescribed.
h) The amendment or supplementation of an application, including changes in the authorized person, shall be made by the applicant at their discretion or upon request by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and must be documented in writing, clearly stating the amended or supplemented contents, enclosed with the receipt for payment of fees for amendment or supplementation of the application. Documents on amendment or supplementation of applications must comply with corresponding provisions specified in Points 7, 10 and 13 of this Circular.
17.2 Splitting of applications
a/ The applicant may, on his/her own initiative or at the request of the National Office of Intellectual Property, separate an application (one or more technical solutions in an invention registration application, one or more industrial designs in an industrial design registration application, one or more components of a mark or part of a goods or service list in a trademark registration application into one or more new applications, called separated applications).
b/ A separated application shall bear a new application number and the filing date of the original application or (priority dates of the original application, if any); it shall be published under Point 14 of this Circular after the notice of acceptance of the valid application is received.
c/ For each separated application, the applicant shall pay the filing fee and all charges and fees for procedures carried out independently from the original application but not required to pay the charge for claiming priority right. A separated application shall be appraised for form and further processed according to the unfinished procedures for the original application. A separated application shall be re-published and the applicant shall pay the publication fee if the application is separated after the National Office of Intellectual Property has issued a notice of acceptance of the valid original application.
d/ The original application (after being separated) shall continue to be processed according to common procedures and the applicant shall pay the fee for amendment and supplementation of the application.
17.3 Application conversion
a/ Before the National Office of Intellectual Property issues a notice of refusal to grant a protection title or a decision to grant a protection title, the applicant may convert the application for an invention patent into an application for a utility solution patent or vice versa under Point e, Clause 1, Article 115 of the Law on Intellectual Property, provided that the applicant pays the fee for application conversion under regulations.
b/ After receiving a valid application for conversion, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall continue carrying out the procedures for processing the converted application under corresponding provisions but not carry out again the procedures already carried out for the application before the conversion request is made.
c/ Requests for conversion of applications filed after the time limit prescribed at Point 17.3.a above shall not be considered. The applicant may file a new application but shall take the filing date (priority date, if any) of the original application.
17.4 Application transfer
Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues any of the notices specified in Point 17.1.a of this Circular, the applicant may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to record the transfer of the application to another person. The request for recording of the transfer shall be made using Form No. 02-CGD as prescribed in Appendix B of this Circular and the corresponding provisions in Point 17.1 of this Circular. The request for recording of the transfer must include documents proving that the transferee meets the requirements regarding registration rights.
18. Refusal to grant, grant and re-grant of copies of protection titles
An application shall be rejected for grant of a protection title in one of the cases specified in clauses 1 and 2 of article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In these cases, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures for rejecting the grant of a protection title according to the provisions of clauses 3 and 4 of article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
18.2 Granting protection titles
a/ Within 10 days after fully and punctually paying fees and charges, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall carry out procedures for granting protection titles according to Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b/ After obtaining a protection title, if finding any error in it, the protection title holder may request the National Office of Intellectual Property to correct such error under Point 20.2 of this Circular.
c/ From the date the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision on grant of a protection title, the transfer of applications shall not be considered.
18.3 Issuance and re-issuance of protection titles
a) In case industrial property rights are under joint ownership, a protection title shall only be issued to the first person in the list of applicants. Other co-owners may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue a duplicate of the protection title, provided that they pay the fee for issuance of duplicates.
b) In the following cases, an industrial property owner who has been granted a protection title/replica of a protection title may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to re-grant a protection title/replica of a protection title, provided that he/she pays the corresponding fee:
(i) The protection title/replica of a protection title is lost;
(ii) The protection title/replica of a protection title is damaged, torn, dirty or faded beyond the point of being usable.
c) Requests for issuance of duplicates/re-grant of protection titles
Except for requests for issuance of duplicates stated in the declaration of registration of industrial property objects, requests for issuance of duplicates/re-grant of protection titles must be made in writing and include the following documents:
(i) Declaration requesting issuance of duplicates/re-grant of protection titles, made according to Form No. 03-PBVB specified in Appendix C of this Circular;
(ii) 01 sample of mark; 01 set of photos of drawings of industrial designs identical with the sample of mark, and a set of photos of drawings of industrial designs in the original protection title;

(iii) Power of attorney (if the application is filed by a representative);
(iv) Document on payment of fee for issuance of protection title or fee for issuance of a copy of a protection title.
d) Processing of requests for issuance of copies, reissuance of protection titles
(i) Within 01 month from the date of receipt of an application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider the request for issuance of a copy/reissue of a protection title. If the request for issuance of a copy/reissue of a protection title meets the above requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on issuance of a copy/reissue of a protection title and record it in the register section of the corresponding protection title in the National Register of Industrial Property;
(ii) The contents of the copy of a protection title shall fully display the information of the corresponding protection title. The contents of the reissue of a protection title/copy of a protection title shall fully display the information of the first-time issued protection title and must be accompanied by the instructions "Reissue", "Copy" or "Copy Reissue";
(iii) In case the request for reissuance/copy issuance of a protection title fails to meet the requirements specified in Point 18.3.c above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to issue a copy/reissue of a protection title, clearly stating the reason.
19. National Register of Industrial Property, announcement of decisions on issuance of protection titles 19.1 National Register of Industrial Property
a) National Register of Industrial Property means an official and public database of the State, which fully presents information on the legal status of established industrial property rights. The National Register of Industrial Property comprises the following types:
(i) National Register of Inventions;
(ii) National Register of Utility Solutions;
(iii) National Register of Industrial Designs;
(iv) National Register of Semiconductor Integrated Circuit Layout Designs;
(v) National Register of Marks;
(vi) National Register of Geographical Indications;
(vii) National Register of Transfer of Industrial Property Rights;
(viii) National Register of Industrial Property Representatives.
b) The National Register of Marks prescribed in Points 19.1.a(i), (ii), (iii), (iv), (v) and (vi) above includes sections corresponding to each protection title, each section including:
(i) Information on the protection title: number and issuance date of the protection title; name of the protected subject matter, scope/volume of protection, validity period; name and address of the owner of the protection title/geographical indication registrant, name and address of the author of the invention, layout design or industrial design; (ii) Information on the application for the protection title (number, submission date, priority date of the application, name of the industrial property representation service organization - if any);
(iii) All information on amendments to protection titles, validity of protection titles (validity maintenance, extension, termination or cancellation); transfer of ownership rights or use rights to industrial property objects; serial numbers, issue dates and names of persons to whom protection titles are issued as copies or re-issued.
c/ The National Register provided for at Point 19.1.a (vii) above comprises sections corresponding to each registered industrial property right transfer contract, specifically:
(i) Information on the certificate of registration of an industrial property right transfer contract (serial number and issue date);
(ii) Information about the registered contract (contract name, signing date, place of signing, names and addresses of the delivering party and receiving party, transfer object, transfer scope);
(iii) Information about changes related to contracts (amendments, additions, extensions, cancellations, or termination of contracts);
(iv) Information on transfer and termination of transfer of invention use rights under compulsory decisions.
d) The National Register prescribed in Point 19.1.a (viii) above includes sections corresponding to each industrial property representation service organization, specifically:

(i) Information on industrial property representation service organizations (full names, transaction names, addresses, name recording and deletion, and amendments to information on industrial property representation service organizations); (ii) Information on lists of industrial property representatives of organizations (full names, permanent addresses, and practicing certificate numbers of each member in the list);
(iii) Information on changes in the list of industrial property representatives (issuance, re-issuance, revocation of practicing certificates, etc.).
e) National registers shall be made and kept by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in paper or electronic form or other means. Any person may search the electronic register or request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue a copy or extract from the register, provided that the fee for issuing a copy is paid.
a/ All decisions on grant of protection titles and decisions on acceptance of international registration shall be published by the National Office of Intellectual Property in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 2 months after their issuance after the applicants have paid the publication fee according to regulations.
b/ Information disclosed under Point 19.2.a above includes information stated in the relevant decision: an invention summary; a set of photos or drawings of the industrial design; a sample of a mark and a list of goods and services bearing the mark; a geographical indication and products bearing such geographical indication.
20. To amend or change the title holders, narrow the scope of protection, maintain and extend the validity of the titles of protection
20.1 Recording of changes in names and addresses of protection title holders, narrowing of protection scope and amendments to descriptions of properties/quality/renown of products bearing geographical indications, maps of geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications, regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks
a) Request for amendment or change of protection title holder
A protection title holder may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to record changes in the protection title in the following cases:
(i) Changes in names and addresses of protection title holders;
(ii) Changes in protection title holders (transfer of ownership due to inheritance, acquisition, merger, separation, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entities by the same owner, business format conversion or decisions of competent regulatory authorities).
The requester for recording of changes in names and addresses, changes in protection title holders shall pay a fee for amendment of protection title and a fee for announcement of decision on recording of amendment of protection title.
b) Request for narrowing of protection scope and amendments to descriptions of properties/quality/renown of products bearing geographical indications, maps of geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications, regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks
Requests for narrowing of protection scope as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property may include one of the following contents:
(i) Request amendments to certain details which do not significantly alter the mark sample stated in the mark registration certificate;
(ii) Requesting the reduction of one or several goods or services or groups of goods or services on the list of goods and services stated in the trademark registration certificate without changing the trademark design;
(iii) Request for reduction of one or several independent or dependent points within the scope of protection stated in the invention patent or utility solution patent;
(iv) Request the removal of one or more than one industrial design scheme, one or more than one product in a set of products stated in an industrial design patent; request the removal of one or more than one basic design feature of an industrial design.
c/ Written request for amendment of the protection title
Depending on the contents of amendment and supplementation prescribed in Point 20.1.a and Point 20.1.b above and Point b, Clause 1, Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, a written request shall include the following documents: (i) Declaration requesting the amendment of a protection title, clearly stating the request for recording changes in the name or address of the protection title holder, changes in the protection title holder, requests for narrowing the scope of protection or amendments to the description of properties/quality/renown of products bearing a geographical indication, map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication, regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks, made according to Form No. 01-SDB prescribed in Annex C of this Circular;
(ii) Original copies of protection titles;

(iii) Documents certifying the change of name and address (original or notarized copies of decisions on change of name and address; business registration permits recording the change of name and address; other legal documents proving the change of name and address) - if the content of the request for amendment is name and address; (iv) Documents proving the transfer of ownership as prescribed in point 20.1.a (ii) above, if the request for amendment is the change of owner of a title of protection (documents proving inheritance, acquisition, merger, separation, joint venture, association, establishment of a new legal entity by the same owner, business form conversion or under the decision of a competent state agency);
(v) Detailed presentation documents on amendments;
(vi) 05 samples of the modified mark (if requested to modify details of the mark); 05 sets of photos or drawings of the industrial design (if requested to modify the industrial design); 02 descriptions of properties/quality/reputation of products bearing the geographical indication, maps of geographical areas corresponding to the geographical indication which has been modified (if requested to modify the geographical indication); 02 regulations on use of collective mark, regulations on use of modified certification mark (if requested to modify the collective mark or certification mark);
(vii) Power of attorney (if submitting application through representative);
(viii) Documents on payment of fees for amendments to protection titles, fees for content appraisal requesting narrowing of the scope of protection, and fees for publication of decisions on amendments or registration according to regulations.
A request for amendment of a title of protection may relate to more than one title of protection if it contains the same amended contents, provided that the requester pays fees and charges for each title of protection.
d) Processing requests for amendments to protection titles
Within one month after receiving an application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider a request for amendment of a protection title. If the request is considered valid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on amendment of the protection title and register such amendment in the Register of Protection Titles and publish such decision on the Industrial Property Official Gazette. In the opposite case, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send the applicant a notice of intended refusal to accept the amendment, clearly stating the reason therefor and fixing a time-limit of one (01) month from the date of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or to express its opposition. If within the prescribed time limit the applicant fails to correct errors or his/her correction of errors is unsatisfactory, he/she does not make any objection or his/her objection is groundless, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment of the protection title.
20.2 Correction of errors in protection titles
a) In case of detecting a flaw in a protection title, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall, at its discretion or upon request of the person detecting the flaw, revoke the protection title with such flaw and grant a new one.
b) The owner of a protection title shall pay a fee for modification of the protection title according to Clause 1 Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property if such flaw is caused by the fault of the owner of the protection title. If such flaw is caused by the fault of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the owner of the protection title is not required to pay a fee for modification.
20.3 Maintenance of validity of an invention protection title To maintain the validity of an invention protection title, the owner of the protection title shall pay a validity maintenance fee within 6 months before the expiry date of the term of validity. The validity maintenance fee may be paid later than the prescribed time limit mentioned above but must not exceed 6 months from the expiry date of the previous term of validity and the owner of the protection title shall pay an additional fee equal to 10% of the fee for each month of late payment.
20.4 Extension of validity of a protection title
a) An invention patent, utility solution patent or certificate of registered layout design shall not be extended. An industrial design patent may be extended up to twice for 5 years each time. A certificate of registered mark may be extended multiple times for 10 years each time.
b) Within 6 months before the expiry date of an industrial design patent or a certificate of registered mark, the owner of the industrial design patent or the owner of the certificate of registered mark shall file an application for extension with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
The application for extension may be filed later than the prescribed time limit mentioned above but must not exceed 6 months from the expiry date of the protection title and the owner of the protection title shall pay an extension fee plus 10% of the extension fee for each month of late payment.
c) Application for extension
An application for extension of validity of a protection title shall comprise the following documents:

(i) An application for extension of validity of a protection title, made according to Form No. 02-GHVB in Appendix C of this Circular;
(ii) Original copy of the protection title (in case of application for extension of protection title); (iii) Power of attorney (if application is filed by a representative);
(iv) Documents on payment of fee for extension, announcement of decision on extension and registration of decision on extension of protection title as prescribed.
d) Process the application for extension
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider the application for extension within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application. If the application is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on extension, record it in the protection title, register it and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to extend the validity period, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide their opinions, if the application for extension falls under one of the following cases:
(i) The application for extension is invalid or submitted in contravention of regulations;
(ii) The applicant for extension is not the owner of the corresponding protection title.
If within the prescribed time limit, the requester fails to correct errors or fails to correct errors to the satisfaction of requirements, does not make any objection or makes unjustifiable objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal of extension.
21. Termination or invalidation of protection titles
21.1 Request for termination or invalidation of protection titles
Requests for termination or invalidation of protection titles shall be handled in accordance with Articles 95 and 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the provisions of this Point.
21.2 Written request for termination or invalidation of a protection title
a/ In one document, one or more protection titles may be requested to be terminated or invalidated if they are based on the same reason, provided that the requester pays a fee according to regulations for each protection title.
b) Written request for termination or invalidation of a protection title, comprising the following documents: (i) Declaration requesting termination or invalidation of a protection title, made using Form No. 04-CDHB under Appendix C hereof;
(ii) Evidence (if any);
(iii) Power of attorney (if submitting a written request through a representative);
(iv) Explanation of reasons for the request (specifying the number of the title, reasons, legal grounds, contents proposed to terminate, partially or entirely invalidate the validity of the protection title) and relevant documents as prescribed in Points 7.2, 22.2 and 22.3 of this Circular;
(v) Payment of fees and charges as prescribed.
21.3 Handling requests for termination or invalidation of protection titles
a) In case of requesting termination or invalidation of a protection title implemented by a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify in writing the opinion of the third party to the owner of the protection title within 2 months from the date of notification for the owner to provide their opinion. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may organize direct exchange of opinions between the third party and the relevant owner of the protection title.
b) Based on the parties’ opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to terminate/partially or completely invalidate the validity of the protection title or issue a notice of refusal to terminate/partially or completely invalidate the validity of the protection title in accordance with Clause 4 Article 95 and Clause 4 Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
c/ If disagreeing with the content of the decision on handling of the request for termination or invalidation of a protection title issued by the National Office of Intellectual Property, the requester or related party may lodge complaints about such decision or notify the matter according to the procedures specified at Point 22 of this Circular.
d/ The decision to terminate or cancel the validity of a protection title, which is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette and recorded in the National Register of Industrial Property within two months after its signing.
21.4 Termination or invalidation of international registration of marks

a) For a request for termination or invalidation of a third party’s validity in an international mark registration under the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the contents of such request to the mark owner via the International Office, setting a time limit of 3 months from the date of notification for the mark owner's opinion.
b/ The decision to terminate or cancel the validity of an international registration of a mark shall be sent to the international office for carrying out relevant procedures in accordance with the corresponding provisions of the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol and published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.
22. Complaints and settlement of complaints related to procedures for establishment of industrial property rights 22.1 Persons with right to complain, objects and statute of limitations for filing complaints Persons with right to complain specified in Clause 1, Article 14, within the statute of limitations specified in Clause 4, Article 14 of the Decree on Industrial Property shall have the right to file complaints about official notices and decisions of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam related to procedures for establishment of industrial property rights. 22.2 Complaint applications
a/ Each complaint petition addresses one complained decision or notice. A complaint petition may also address many decisions or notices if they have the same content and reason for complaint, provided that the complainant pays a complaint fee according to regulations for each complained decision or notice.
b/ A written complaint shall include the following documents:
(i) The written complaint form, made according to Form No. 05-KN specified in Appendix C of this Circular; (ii) Written explanation of the complaint and evidence proving the grounds for the complaint (specifying reasons, legal grounds, content of the complaint, list of accompanying evidence, if any);
(iii) Copies of decisions or notices subject to complaints of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
(iv) Copy of the decision on first settlement of complaints (for second-time complaints); (v) Power of attorney (if the application is submitted by a representative);
(vi) Documents on payment of fees and charges related to the complaint as prescribed.
c/ Evidence means documents (evidences) or exhibits (material evidences) used to prove and clarify the grounds of the complaint. Evidence must meet the following requirements:
(i) Evidence may be documents in foreign languages, provided that they are accompanied by Vietnamese versions in cases where so requested by persons competent to settle complaints;
(ii) In cases where the evidence is documents bearing the seals of individuals or organizations not having lawful seals, or bearing the seals of foreign individuals or organizations, the signatures must be certified by notary offices or competent authorities;
(iii) In cases where the evidence is objects carrying information ( publications, video tapes, etc.), it shall, on a case-by-case basis, specify the origin, time of issuance or publication of such documents, or specify the origin and time of publication of the information shown on such objects;
(iv) The exhibits must be accompanied by documents clearly describing characteristics directly related to the content of the complaint.
The complainant shall ensure the truthfulness in providing evidence and take responsibility for the consequences of providing false evidence.
22.4 Withdrawal of complaints
a/ At any time, the complainant may send a written notice of withdrawal of his/her complaint. If the withdrawal is made by an industrial property representation service organization, the authorization for withdrawal must be clearly stated in the power of attorney.
b/ An withdrawn application shall be considered as not submitted. The complainant shall not be refunded his/her complaint application and paid complaint charge and fee.
22.5 Processing of written complaints
a) Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the complaint, the person competent to settle complaints shall examine the application according to the prescribed form and notify in writing to the complainant whether the complaint is accepted or not, specifying the date of acceptance or clearly stating the reason for non-acceptance.
b) The complaint is not accepted if it falls into one of the following cases:
(i) The complainant has no right to lodge complaints;

(ii) The complaint is filed beyond the prescribed statute of limitations;
(iii) The complaint form fails to satisfy the requirements prescribed in Points 22.1 and 22.2 of this Circular.
a) For the accepted complaints, the person competent to settle the complaints shall notify in writing the contents of the complaints to the persons with related rights and interests (“related parties”) and set a time limit of 01 month from the date of notification for such persons to give their opinions.
b/ The concerned party may provide information and evidence to support its arguments within the time limit prescribed at Point 22.6.a above; the person competent to settle complaints shall have to consider such information and evidence when settling the complaint.
c/ If the above-said time limit expires but the concerned party has no opinion, the complaint shall be settled on the basis of the complainant's opinions.
22.7 Decision on settlement of complaints
a) Based on arguments and evidence provided by the complainant and relevant parties, the person competent to resolve complaints shall issue a decision to resolve the complaint within the prescribed time limit for resolving complaints in accordance with the law on complaints.
b) Before issuing a decision on settlement of complaints, the person competent to settle complaints shall notify the complainants and related parties of the arguments and evidence used by the other party for settling the complaints as well as the conclusion on settlement of complaints.
c/ The complaint settlement decision must contain contents prescribed by law on complaints.
22.8 The complaint settlement decision shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 2 months after its signing.
22.9 Effect of the complaint settlement decision Any procedures for establishment of industrial property rights which depend on the results of complaint settlement shall only be carried out on the basis of:
a/ The first complaint settlement decision if after the expiration of the statute of limitations for filing a second complaint, the complainant does not file a second complaint and after the statute of limitations for initiating an administrative lawsuit, the complainant does not initiate an administrative lawsuit; or b/ The second complaint settlement decision and the complainant does not initiate an administrative lawsuit within the prescribed time limit or under the effective decision of the court if the complainant initiates an administrative lawsuit.
Section 2. PROCEDURES FOR REGISTRATION OF INVENTIONS
23. Requirements for invention registration applications
23.1 An invention registration application (application) must meet the general requirements specified at Points 7 and 10.1 of this Circular and specific requirements specified at this Point.
23.2 The application must clearly indicate that the subject matter to be protected is a product or a process in accordance with Clause 12, Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
23.3 The application shall ensure consistency as prescribed in clauses 1 and 2 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property and under the following guidelines.
An application is considered consistent if:
a) It requests protection for a single subject matter; or b) It requests protection for a group of subjects with technical connections, expressing a unique common creative intention, falling into the following cases:
(i) One subject is used to create (produce, manufacture, or prepare) the other;
(ii) One subject is used to perform the other;
(iii) One subject is used to utilize the other;
(iv) Subjects are of the same type and have the same function to ensure the same result. 23.4 In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of information in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to submit documents verifying such information within 01 month from the date of issuance of the notification requesting such verification, especially documents confirming lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits submission rights from another person (certificate of inheritance rights, certificate or agreement on transfer of application submission rights; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.); documents showing drug test results on humans, animals, or plants mentioned in the description (when the subject matters of the protection request are pharmaceuticals for humans, animals, or plants).
23.5 Requirements for the declaration
The applicant shall submit 02 declarations, made according to Form 01-SC in Annex A of this Circular. In the section "International classification of inventions" in the declaration, the applicant shall specify the classification code of the technical solution to be protected according to the latest International Classification of Inventions (according to the Strasbourg Agreement) published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Official Gazette. If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify and the applicant shall pay a classification fee as prescribed.
23.6 Requirements on invention descriptions
The applicant shall submit 2 invention descriptions. An invention description must include an invention description and a protection scope.
a) The description section of an invention description must fully disclose the nature of the registered technical solution. The description must contain sufficient information so that anyone with average knowledge in the relevant technical field can implement such solution; it must clarify the novelty, inventive step and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requires the issuance of an invention patent); and clarify the novelty and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requires the issuance of a utility solution patent).
Persons with average knowledge in a corresponding technical field means persons who have practical skills in common techniques and know general common knowledge in the corresponding technical field.
b) The description must include the following contents:
(i) Name of invention: briefly indicates subject matter or subjects registered (hereinafter referred to as "subject matters"); name of invention must be concise and not promotional or advertising nature;

(ii) Field of use of invention: field in which subject matters are used or related;

(iii) Technical status of field of use of invention: technical status in the above-mentioned field at the time of filing application (similar known subject matters, if any);
(iv) Technical nature of the invention: the nature of the subject matter, in which the signs (characteristics) creating the subject matter must be clearly stated and new signs (characteristics) compared with similar known technical solutions must be indicated;
(v) Brief description of accompanying drawings (if any);
(vi) Detailed description of the implementation schemes of the invention;
(vii) Example of implementation of the invention;
(viii) Benefits (efficiency) that may be achieved.
c) Scope of protection of the invention (hereinafter referred to as "scope of protection" or "protection request") The scope (request) of protection is used to determine the scope of industrial property rights to the invention. The scope (request) of protection must be presented briefly and clearly in accordance with the description and drawings, in which the new signs of the subject matter requested for protection (hereinafter referred to as "subject matter") must be clarified and must comply with the following provisions:
d) The scope (request) of protection must be fully illustrated by the description, including basic technical signs necessary and sufficient to identify the subject matter, achieve the proposed purpose and distinguish the subject matter from the known subject matter.
e) Technical signs within the scope (request) of protection must be clear, accurate and accepted in the corresponding technical field.
g) The scope (request) of protection must not be cited to the description and drawings, except for cases where it is cited to parts which cannot be accurately described in words, such as the nucleotide sequence and the amino acid sequence, the interference diagram, the state diagram, etc.
h) If the application contains drawings illustrating the subject matter of the request for protection, the signs mentioned in the scope (request) of protection may be accompanied by reference numbers, but must be enclosed in parentheses. These reference numbers shall not be considered as limiting the scope (request) of protection.
i) The scope (request) of protection should (but is not required) be divided into two parts: "the restricted part" and "the distinctive part", in which: "the restricted part" includes the name of the subject matter and signs of this subject matter identical with those of the nearest known subject matter and is connected to "the distinctive part" by the phrase "distinct in the place" or "characteristic in the place" or equivalent words; "the distinctive part" includes distinctive signs of the subject matter compared to the nearest known subject matter and these signs combined with the signs of the "restricted part" constitute the subject matter of the protection request.
k) The scope (request) of protection may include one or more points. In which case, the scope (request) of protection with multiple points may be used to express a subject matter that needs to be protected, with the first point (called independent point) and subsequent points (called dependent points) used to specify the independent point (called dependent point); or express a group of subject matters that need to be protected, with several independent points, each independent point expressing a subject matter that needs to be protected in that group, each independent point may have dependent points.
l) Points of the scope (request) of protection must be numbered consecutively with Arabic numerals followed by a dot.
m) The scope (request) of protection with multiple points used to express a group of subject matters must meet the requirements: independent points, expressing separate subjects, must not be cited to other points of the scope (request) of protection, except for cases where such citation allows for avoiding complete repetition of the content of other points; dependent points must be expressed immediately after the independent points from which they depend.
23.7 Requirements for an invention summary
The applicant shall submit 2 inventions. The invention summary shall be used to briefly describe (not exceeding 150 words) the nature of the invention. The summary shall disclose principal contents of the technical solution for information purpose. The summary may contain drawings and specific formulas.
23.8 Additional provisions for invention registration applications related to biological technology
a) In addition to the general requirements for invention descriptions prescribed in Clause 23.6 of this Circular, for an application for registration of a genetic sequence or part of a genetic sequence, the description must contain a list of genetic sequences presented according to the WIPO ST.25 standard section 2 (ii) (Standard for presenting lists of nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences in invention registration applications).
b) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request applicants to submit electronic information carriers (e.g., floppy disks, optical disks) readable by common electronic devices, which contain nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences identical to those in the sequence list specified in the description.
c) Regarding inventions relating to biological materials which cannot be described or adequately described for persons with average knowledge of biotechnology to implement, such inventions shall only be considered fully disclosed if the following conditions are satisfied: (i) Biological material samples have been submitted to a competent repository as prescribed in Point 23.9 of this Circular no later than the filing date;
(ii) The description must clearly state necessary information about characteristics of biological materials that the applicant may obtain;
(iii) The declaration must clearly state the biological material storage agency, the storage number of the biological material sample already submitted for storage issued by the storage agency, and the documents confirming these information must be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date or no later than the submission date of the earliest application (if any), whichever is earlier, except for the case specified in Point 23.9.d of this Circular.
d) In case the applicant is not the biological material depositor, the name and address of the depositor and documents certifying the lawful use of biological materials must be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date or no later than the submission date of the earliest application (if any), whichever comes first, except for the case specified in Point 23.9.d of this Circular.
23.9 Biological material specimen storage
a) The purpose of deposit of biological material samples is to serve the process of content appraisal of invention registration applications related to biological materials.
b) Biological material samples shall be submitted to the biological material storage agency no later than the filing date of the invention related to such biological materials.
c/ The competent biological material storage agency means a Vietnamese or foreign agency designated by the Ministry of Science and Technology or recognized for the function of storing biological materials.
d) The filing of biological material samples and certification documents for international applications for inventions shall comply with the provisions of the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT).

e) For biological material samples deposited at a foreign depository agency, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to deposit such samples at another competent depository agency in Vietnam if it deems necessary to clarify the nature of the subject matter requested for protection or to satisfy a third party's request for access to such subject matter.
23.10 Additional provisions for applications for registration of inventions related to pharmaceuticals In addition to the general requirements for invention descriptions prescribed in Clause 23.6 of this Circular, for applications for registration of inventions related to pharmaceuticals, the description must state the results of clinical tests and pharmacological effects of the pharmaceutical, at least including the following information:
a) Used materials/compound;
b) The method(s) used for testing;
c) Test results;
d) The relationship between the pharmacological effects obtained in tests and the practical application of pharmaceuticals in disease prevention, diagnosis and treatment.
23.11 Additional provisions for applications for registration of inventions related to genetic resources or traditional knowledge
Apart from the general requirements for invention registration applications prescribed in points 23.1 to 23.7 of this Circular, an application for registration of an invention related to a genetic resource or traditional knowledge must also contain a presentation on the origin of such genetic resource or traditional knowledge which has been accessed by the author of the invention or the applicant, if the invention is directly based on such genetic resource or traditional knowledge. If the author of the invention or the applicant cannot identify the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge, he/she/it shall clearly state this and take responsibility for the truthfulness of such identification.
24. Format appraisal and publication of invention registration applications
The procedures for formal examination and publication of invention registration applications shall comply with the general provisions prescribed in clauses 13 and 14 of this Circular.
25. Content appraisal of an invention registration application
25.1 Request for substantive examination of an invention registration application
a) The applicant or any third party has the right to request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to conduct content appraisal of an invention registration application according to Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property and is specified as follows:
(i) The request for content appraisal of an invention registration application shall be made in writing according to Form No. 03-YCTD specified in Appendix B of this Circular or stated in the invention registration application declaration (if the applicant is the applicant and such request is made immediately upon filing the application); (ii) The request for content appraisal of an invention registration application shall be filed within 42 months from the priority date if it requests the grant of an invention patent, or within 36 months from the priority date if it requests the grant of a utility solution patent; The time limit for filing a request for content appraisal may be extended but shall not exceed 6 months if there is a justifiable reason;
(iii) The applicant for content appraisal of an invention registration application shall pay a fee for searching and a content appraisal fee as prescribed; if the content appraisal request is submitted beyond the prescribed time limit, the applicant shall pay an extension fee as prescribed in Point 20.4.b of this Circular; if the content appraisal fee is not paid, the content appraisal request shall be considered invalid and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam will not conduct content appraisal.
b/ An application for content appraisal of an invention registration application filed after the date of its publication shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette in the second month after receipt of such request; if such request is made by a third party, it shall be notified to the applicant.
An application for substantive examination of an invention registration application filed before the date of disclosure shall be disclosed together with the corresponding application.
c/ In case no request for content appraisal is filed within the time limit prescribed at Point 25.1.a above, the application shall be regarded as having been withdrawn at the time of expiry of such time limit.
25.2 Procedures for content appraisal
The content appraisal of an invention registration application shall be carried out according to the general procedures specified in Clause 15 of this Circular and the specific provisions of this Clause.
25.3 Assessment of compatibility between subject matters stated in the application and the type of invention protection title

a) Subject matters stated in an invention registration application shall be considered incompatible with the type of invention protection title requested by the applicant for issuance (invention patent/utility solution patent) if such subject matters are not technical solutions, specifically products or processes. The identification of technical solutions is prescribed in Point 25.3.b below.
b) Technical solutions - subject matters of inventions - means a necessary and sufficient combination of information on technical methods and/or technical means to solve a definite task (problem).
Technical solutions may be of one of the following forms:
(i) Physical products (instruments, machinery, equipment, components, electrical circuits, etc.) expressed as a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by signs (features) regarding structure, which has functions (utility) as a means to meet certain needs of humans; or physical products (materials, substances, foods, pharmaceuticals, etc.) expressed as a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by signs (features) regarding the presence, proportion and state of elements, which have functions (utility) as a means to meet certain needs of humans; or biological materials (genes, genetically modified plants/animals, etc.). is expressed as a collection of information about a product containing genetically modified genetic information that is capable of self-reproduction;
(ii) Process (technological process; method of diagnosis, prediction, inspection, handling, etc.) expressed as a set of information defining the manner in which a specific process or task is carried out, characterized by signs (features) regarding the order, conditions, participants, measures and means to perform operations in order to achieve a certain purpose.
c) The subject matter stated in the application shall not be considered a technical solution in the following cases: (i) The subject matter stated in the application is merely an idea or intention, or merely raises a problem but does not constitute a way of solving the problem, and cannot answer the question "by what means" or "by what means"; (ii) The problem (task) raised for resolution is not a technical issue and cannot be resolved by technical means;
(iii) Natural products, not created by humans.
25.4 Assessment of industrial applicability according to Article 62 of the Law on Intellectual Property
a) The technical solutions stated in the application are considered "feasible" if:
(i) Information on the nature of the solution together with instructions on necessary technical conditions shall be presented clearly and sufficiently so that persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field may create, manufacture or use, exploit or implement such solution;
The concept of "person with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field" is understood as prescribed in Point 23.6.a of this Circular;
(ii) The creation, production, use, exploitation or implementation of the above solutions may be repeated with the same results and similar to the results stated in the invention description.
b) Technical solutions deemed incapable of industrial application in the following cases: (i) The nature of the subject matter or instructions aimed at implementing the subject matter contradicts fundamental principles of science (e.g., does not comply with the principle of energy conservation, etc.);
(ii) The subject matter includes elements and components that have no technical connection with each other or cannot be connected (joined, bound, dependent, etc.) with each other;
(iii) Subject to internal contradictions;
(iv) The subject matters of the instructions may be given only a limited number of times (cannot be repeated);
(v) In order to implement the solution, the person must have special skills which cannot be transferred or only given to others;
(vi) The results obtained from the implementation are not consistent;
(vii) The results obtained are different from those stated in the application;
(viii) There is no or insufficient most important instruction for solution; (ix) Other cases with other plausible reasons.
25.5 Assessment of novelty according to Article 60 of the Law on Intellectual Property

a) Minimum mandatory information sources
In order to assess the novelty of the technical solutions stated in the application, it is required to search information in the following compulsory sources (but not limited to the minimum number of searches in these sources): (i) All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam having the same classification index with the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal, up to the third sub-classification index and with an earlier publication date than the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal;
(ii) Applications for invention registration or invention protection titles published by other organizations or countries within 25 years before the filing date or priority date of the application being appraised (if the application is eligible for priority right) shall be kept in the invention database at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and other information sources prescribed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, with the search scope specified in Point 25.5.a(i) above.
In necessary and possible cases, the search may be extended to scientific reports, reports on results of research programs and projects, and other documents in the same technical field, which are published and stored at the National Center for Science and Technology Information.
b) Search purpose
The purpose of information lookup is to find technical solutions with similar or identical nature to those stated in the application.
In this point:
(i) Two technical solutions shall be regarded as identical when they have all basic signs (characteristics) identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
(ii) Two technical solutions shall be considered similar when they have most of their basic signs (characteristics) identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
(iii) “Controlled technical solution” means a technical solution identical or most similar to the one stated in the application;
(iv) “Comparison documents” are documents describing the technical solution of comparison or evidence proving that the technical solution of comparison has been publicly disclosed.
c) Reference report
Search results must be presented in the search report, specifying the searched field, scope and results within such scope (statistics of found control technical solutions, specifying identical signs, names of control documents, page numbers, line numbers, origin of documents and date of publication of corresponding documents) and full name of the person making the report (searcher).
d) Methods for assessing the novelty of technical solutions
In order to evaluate the novelty of a technical solution stated in an application, it is required to compare fundamental signs (characteristics) of such technical solution with signs of control technical solutions found during the information lookup process; in which:
(i) Fundamental signs of a technical solution may be its functional, utility, structural, compositional and/or interconnection characteristics, together with other fundamental signs, which form a necessary and sufficient set for determining the nature (content) of the subject matter;
(ii) The fundamental signs of technical solutions stated in the application and protection titles, which are shown within the scope (requested) of invention protection;
(iii) Basic signs of technical solutions stated in other documents shall be presented and detected according to description documents or actual forms of such technical solutions.
e) Conclusion on the novelty of technical solutions
Corresponding to a point of the protection scope (requested), the technical solution stated in the application shall be considered new compared with the world's technical level if:
(i) No technical solution for comparison is found during the information lookup; or (ii) A technical solution for comparison is found but there is at least one fundamental difference between the technical solution stated in the application and the technical solution for comparison (and such difference is called a fundamental different sign).
25.6 Assessment of creativity level according to Article 61 of the Law on Intellectual Property
a) Minimum mandatory information sources

When assessing the creativity of technical solutions, it is required to at least search information in compulsory sources (but not limited to such a minimum source as prescribed in Point 25.5.a of this Circular).
b) Assess creativity level
The assessment of the level of creativity of technical solutions stated in the application shall be carried out by assessing fundamental distinctive signs (signs) stated within the scope (requirements) of protection in order to make a conclusion that: (i) Fundamental distinctive signs (signs) have been considered as having been disclosed in the mandatory minimum information source or not, and;
(ii) Collecting other basic distinctive signs which are considered obvious to persons with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field or not.
In response to a point within the scope of (requested) protection, a technical solution shall be considered creative if the addition of a distinctive basic sign to the collection of basic signs of the technical solution is the result of creative activity and not the obvious result of common knowledge in the relevant technical field.
c/ In the following cases (but not only in these cases), for a point within the protection scope, a technical solution shall be considered not being creative:
(i) A collection of other basic distinctive signs of obvious nature (any person with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field would know that in order to perform a given function or achieve a given purpose, it is necessary to use such a collection of signs and vice versa when using such a collection of signs, it is necessary to achieve the corresponding purpose or perform the corresponding function); (ii) A collection of basic distinctive signs which have been presented in the form of uniformity or equivalence in one or several known technical solutions in the mandatory minimum information source; (iii) Technical solutions are simple combinations of known technical solutions with functions, purposes and effectiveness which are also simple combinations of functions, purposes and effectiveness of each known technical solution.
d) In this point:
(i) Two signs are considered identical if they have the same nature;
(ii) Two signs shall be regarded as equivalent if they have similar natures, the same purpose and the same method of achieving the basic purpose.
25.7 Examination of the first-to-file principle as prescribed in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property Before issuing a decision on grant of a protection title, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine the first-to-file principle for invention registration applications which have been substantively examined and concluded to satisfy the protection conditions under the following regulations:
a) Minimum mandatory information sources
In order to verify the first-to-file principle, at least the following mandatory sources of information must be consulted (but not limited to the minimum number of these sources):
All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (by the time of examination) having the same classification index as the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal, up to the third sub-classification index, and having an earlier submission date or priority date than the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights), have not yet been published or have a later publication date than the submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights).
b) The purpose of the search is to find (multiple) applications for registration of the same invention and determine the earliest filing date or priority date of an application;
c/ In case there are many applications for registration of the same invention, a protection title may be granted only to the valid application with the earliest priority or submission date among applications which satisfy all conditions for the grant of a protection title;
d/ In case there are many applications for registration of the same invention which meet all conditions for the grant of a protection title and are filed on the same priority day or the earliest day, the protection title may be granted to only one of such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; otherwise all applications shall be rejected from the grant of the protection title;
e) In case an applicant for registration of an invention requests priority right on the basis of the first application for registration of the same invention filed in Vietnam, the protection title may only be granted to the application for registration of the invention requesting priority right accepted and the first application filed in Vietnam shall be considered as withdrawn.

25.8 Notification of content appraisal results
Notification of content appraisal results of an invention registration application shall be carried out according to the general procedures specified in Point 15.7.a of this Circular.
26. Granting, registration and publication of invention patents and utility solutions The procedures for issuing, registering and publishing invention patents and utility solutions shall comply with the general procedures prescribed in clauses 18 and 19 of this Circular.
27. Processing international applications for inventions
27.1 Receiving authority
The agency competent to receive international applications for inventions in Vietnam is the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall:
a) Receive international applications originating in Vietnam;
b) Collecting fees for international application submission and notifying fees as prescribed to enable applicants to transfer them to International Office and International Search Agency in accordance with the Agreement on Cooperation in Patent Activities - PCT (hereinafter referred to as "the Agreement");
c/ To check whether the fees are paid on time;
d) Inspect and handle international applications originating in Vietnam according to the provisions of the Treaty;
e) Identify protected subject matters: if the protected subject matters of the application are classified as national secrets, further work shall not be carried out and fees shall be refunded to the applicant, except for international filing fees and international duplicate fee;
g/ To send one copy (application dossier) of the international application originating in Vietnam to the International Office and one copy (reference copy) to the international reference agency;
h/ To send and receive letters from applicants and international agencies.
27.2 Language
An international application originating in Vietnam shall be made in English. The application shall be made in 3 copies.
In case the number of copies prescribed is not enough, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall make additional copies to the required number and the applicant shall pay an international copy fee.
27.3 International search agencies and international preliminary examination agencies For international applications of Vietnamese origin, the competent international search agencies and international preliminary examination agencies are the Inventions Agency, the Industrial Property Agency or Intellectual Property Agency of Austria, Russia, Switzerland, South Korea and the European Inventions Agency.
a/ If an international application contains a designation of Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be designated agency. In this case, in order to enter the national stage, within 31 months after the priority date, the applicant shall submit it to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam:
(i) Declaration for invention registration application, made using Form 01-SC under Appendix A hereof; (ii) International application copy (if applicant requests to enter national phase before international publication);
(iii) Vietnamese translation of the international application: description, including description, protection request, annotations to drawings and summary (initially submitted original or published copy if the application has not yet been published and revised copies and explanatory notes on the revised parts, if the international application is amended under Article 19 of the Agreement); (iv) National fees and charges.
b/ An international application filed with the National Office of Intellectual Property within six months after the expiry of the time limit prescribed at Point 27.4.a above may be accepted on condition that the applicant pays the prescribed fee and charge.
27.5 International applications with selection of Vietnam
a/ If Vietnam is selected in an international application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be the selected agency. In this case, if Vietnam is selected within 19 months from the priority date so as to enter the national stage, within 31 months from the priority date, the applicant shall submit to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam the following documents:
(i) An invention registration application form, made according to Form 01-SC in Appendix A of this Circular;

(ii) Vietnamese translation of the international application: description, including description, protection request, annotations to drawings and summary (published version or original submitted initially if the application has not yet been published and revised version and explanatory document on the revised version if the international application is amended under Article 19 and/or Article 34(2)(b) of the Agreement);
(iii) Vietnamese translations of the international preliminary examination reports (when there is a request for examination of the application contents);
(iv) National fees and charges.
b/ An international application filed with the National Office of Intellectual Property within six months after the expiry of the time limit prescribed at Point 27.5.a above may be accepted, provided that the applicant pays the fee according to regulations.
27.6 Priority right request
In order to enjoy the priority right, the applicant shall have to re-state such in the declaration, pay the fee for enjoying the priority right and, at the request of the National Office of Intellectual Property, submit Vietnamese versions of documents already submitted to the International Bureau of necessary documents according to Rule 17.1(a) of the Implementing Regulation.
For a PCT application, the handling of the request for priority right in accordance with the PCT Convention and the Regulations on implementation of the Convention.
27.7 Handling of international applications in the national stage
a) Amendments to documents during the national stage
In accordance with Rule 51bis of the Implementing Regulations of the Treaty, the applicant shall submit a power of attorney and a document on transfer of the right to file an application in the international phase (if any) within 34 months from the priority date.
In accordance with Article 28 and Article 41 of the Treaty and Rules 52.1(b) and 78.1(b) of the Regulation on implementation of the Treaty, the applicant may amend or supplement documents in the national stage. At the time of entering the national stage, the applicant may also amend or supplement the description. The above amendment or supplementation must comply with Point 17 of this Circular.
Additional and amended documents submitted by applicants to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese.
b) The starting time of the national stage
The starting time for processing an international application with designation or selection of Vietnam at the national stage shall be the first day of the thirty-second month from the priority date, if the applicant does not submit a written request to enter the national stage earlier than the above-mentioned deadlines.
c) Appraising international applications
After the international application has entered the national phase, it shall be subject to both formal and substantive examination according to the procedures applicable to ordinary invention applications. If the applicant files a written request for an early examination and pays the prescribed fee, the international application shall be subject to an early examination as stipulated in point 27.7.b above in accordance with Article 23(2) of the Agreement.
d) International applications are considered as withdrawn
Apart from cases which are considered as withdrawal under the provisions of the Treaty and the Regulation on implementation of the Treaty, in case the national fee is not paid to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or there is no Vietnamese translation after the prescribed time limit, the international application designated or selected to be processed in Vietnam shall be considered as withdrawn.
27.8 International registration fees and charges
a) Applicants for international applications at the national stage shall pay fees and charges according to regulations applicable to invention registration applications submitted in Vietnam.
b/ International applicants of Vietnamese origin shall pay fees and charges according to the Regulation on implementation of the Treaty and the Circular guiding the collection, payment, management and use of industrial property fees and charges of the Ministry of Finance.
Section 3. REGISTRATION PROCEDURES FOR LAYOUT DESIGNS
28. Requirements for layout design registration applications
28.1 An application for registration of a layout design shall satisfy the general requirements prescribed in clauses 7 and 10 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this clause.

28.2 Documents, samples and information showing the layout design specified in Point 7.1.a(ii) of this Circular include:

b) Designs of layout-based integrated circuits, including 4 designs if the layout has been commercially exploited;
c) Description of the production integrated circuit according to the layout design, including information serving to clarify the nature of the layout design.
28.3 The application shall ensure uniformity as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically requesting protection for only one layout design of a semiconductor integrated circuit.

28.4 Where there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to submit documents verifying such information within 01 month, particularly documents confirming lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits submission rights from another person (certificate of inheritance, certificate of transfer of application submission rights, agreement on assignment of work or employment contract, etc.);

28.5 Requirements for the declaration
The applicant shall submit 02 declarations, made according to Form No. 02-TKBT in Appendix A of this Circular.
a) General requirements: The set of photos and drawings of layout design must fully express the spatial structure of components and their interconnections in semi-conducting integrated circuits so that based on such set of photos/drawings, only one unique layout design can be determined. For the above-mentioned purpose, the set of photos/drawings of layout design must satisfy the conditions specified at Points 28.6.b, c and d below.
b) Document type:
A set of photos or drawings of the layout design shall include at least one of the following documents: (i) Drawings made by a layout design machine for each layer of an integrated circuit;
(ii) Drawings or photos of photolithically carved masks used for producing each integrated circuit layer;
(iii) Photographs of each layer of layout design shown in the integrated circuit;
c) Document form: The set of photos/drawings of layout design shall be submitted in paper form and may be accompanied by supplementary documents being objects carrying electronic data for part or all of such set of photos/drawings of layout design.
d) Form of documents
(i) The set of photos/photos of layout design drawings must include separate photos/photos for each class of layout design, with symbols for each class, size of integrated circuit and magnification; (ii) All photos/photos of layout design drawings must be under the same scale. For paper documents: each photo/photo of layout design drawing must be a magnified copy at least 20 times the size of the integrated circuit so that the basic circuit can be seen by the naked eye;
(iii) Each photo or drawing of layout design may be presented on an A4 paper sheet or a larger paper sheet, provided that it is folded into an A4 size;
(iv) The photos or drawings of the layout design must be clear and sharp.
28.7 Requirements for samples of integrated circuits produced under a layout design
a) The submitted sample must be the whole or part of an integrated circuit corresponding to the layout design stated in the application. If the integrated circuit produced under a layout design is an integral part of another product, the product enclosed with the application must contain documents indicating the integrated circuit produced under such a layout design.
b) If the layout design has been commercially exploited anywhere in the world before the filing date, the applicant must submit the first commercially exploited sample.
28.8 Requirements for the description of semi-conducting integrated circuits produced under a layout design The description must include the following detailed information about the semi-conducting integrated circuit produced under a layout design requested for protection:
a) Name/symbol: a collection of letters and/or numbers used to distinguish this integrated circuit from other integrated circuits when put on the market;

b) Description of basic functions of the integrated circuit (e.g., memory function, logic function, or other functions);
c) Description of the basic structure of the integrated circuit (e.g., bipolar, MOS, BiMOS, optoelectronic, or other structures);
d) Description of technology for manufacturing integrated circuits (e.g. TTL, DTL, ECL, ILT, CMOS, NMOS, PMOS or other technologies);
e) Describe main characteristics distinguishing from other integrated semiconductor circuits on the market at the time of filing or at the time of first commercial exploitation in the world, whichever is earlier.
29. Confidentiality of information in layout design registration applications
Applicants may request to keep confidential information submitted in their applications for registration of layout designs as follows:
29.1 The maximum level of confidentiality to be kept is permitted:
a) For layout designs not yet commercially exploited: 50% of the surface area of each layer;
b/ For a layout design which has been commercially exploited: 2 layers in each group of 5 layers from top to bottom.

29.2 In order to have information kept confidential, the applicant shall make a request for information confidentiality keeping under the guidance of the National Office of Intellectual Property and indicate documents and materials containing secret information.

29.3 Documents containing secret information shall be separated into a package of confidential documents and may be submitted in the following forms:
a) Microfilm or similar form of documents showing size of design on computer drawings;
b) Electronic data;
c) Drawings or photos of parts invisible, provided that characteristics of the layout design are basically visible.
29.4 The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be obliged to keep confidential information at the request of applicants in accordance with Point 29.1 of this Circular.
30. Format appraisal of layout design registration applications
30.1 Procedures for format appraisal of layout design registration applications shall comply with general provisions in Points 13.1, 13.2, 13.3, 13.4 and 13.8 of this Circular and separate procedures applicable to layout design registration applications prescribed in this Point.
30.2 Notification of format appraisal results
a) If the application falls under one of the cases prescribed in Point 13.2 of this Circular or contains deficiencies prescribed in Point 13.3 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the procedures prescribed in Point 13.6.a of this Circular.
b) If the application is valid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of acceptance of the valid application to the applicant, clearly stating the name and address of the applicant, the name of the representative (if any), and information about the subject matter stated in the application, the submission date, and also clearly stating that the subject matter stated in the application may be granted a layout design registration certificate if, after 03 months from the date on which the layout design is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette, there is no legitimate objection from a third party against the registration of such layout design.
30.3 Refusal to accept applications
In case where the applicant has been notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam of the results of the formal examination which indicates deficiencies and the intended refusal to accept the application according to Point 13.6.a of this Circular but the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to meet requirements or does not have any objection or provides unjustifiable objections within the prescribed time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the refusal to accept the layout design registration application and refund the fees and charges already paid for the post-formal examination work at the request of the applicant.
31. Publication of applications for registration of layout designs
31.1 Layout designs of which applications have been accepted as valid shall be published according to general procedures prescribed in Point 14 of this Circular and this Point.
31.2 Access to detailed information about valid layout design registration applications

a) From the date on which the application is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette, all persons may access detailed information about the nature of the layout design stated in the published application, except for information that is kept confidential as prescribed in Point 29 of this Circular.
b) Only agencies with powers to carry out procedures for invalidating protection titles or carrying out procedures for handling acts of infringement upon rights to new layout designs may access information on layout designs kept confidential.
32. Granting, registration and publication of decisions on grant of certificates of registered layout designs 32.1. Notice of intended grant of a certificate of registered layout design If, after 3 months from the date on which a layout design is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette, there is no third party's objection to the registration of the layout design or, despite having an objection, the handling results prove that the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended grant of a certificate of registered layout design for the subject matter stated in the application; and the time limit for the applicant to pay the fee for publication of the title of protection, the registration fee and the fee for granting the title of protection. The above-said time limit is 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice.
32.2 Issuance, registration and publication of decisions on issuance of certificates of registered layout designs The procedures for issuance, registration and publication of certificates of registered layout designs shall comply with the general procedures prescribed in Points 18.2 and 19 of this Circular.
32.3 Refusal to grant a certificate of registered layout design
If within 3 months after a layout design is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette there are third parties' opinions opposing the grant of a certificate of registration of the layout design and such opinions are proved to be well-grounded, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures for refusal to grant a certificate of registration of the layout design according to the general procedures specified at Point 18.1 of this Circular.
Section 4. PROCEDURES FOR REGISTRATION OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGNS
33. Requirements on applications for registration of industrial designs
33.1 An industrial design registration application shall satisfy the general requirements prescribed in Points 7 and 10.1 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this Point.
33.2 Industrial design registration applications shall ensure uniformity as prescribed in clauses 1 and 3 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following regulations.
An application for registration of an industrial design shall be deemed to ensure uniformity if:
a/ Request for protection of an industrial design of a product; or b/ Request for protection of an industrial design of many products in a set of products, each of which has a corresponding industrial design; or c/ Request for protection of an industrial design of a product accompanied by one or more variants of such industrial design.
33.3 Information provision request
a) In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of information in an industrial design registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to submit documents verifying such information within 1 month, especially documents confirming lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (certificate of inheritance rights, certificate or written agreement on transfer of application submission rights; assignment contract or labor contract, etc.);
b) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may also request applicants to submit documents certifying the lawful ownership or use right of trade indications (marks, geographical indications, trade names) or industrial designs protected by others within one month if there are grounds to suspect that the industrial designs mentioned in the applications contain such objects.
33.4 Requirements for declarations
The applicant shall submit 02 declarations, made according to Form 03-KDCN specified in Appendix A of this Circular. In addition to other fields subject to declaration, the declaration must specify the international classification code of the industrial design requested for protection in accordance with the International Classification of Industrial Designs (according to the Locarno Agreement). If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify it and the applicant shall pay a classification fee as prescribed.
33.5 Requirements on descriptions of industrial designs
The applicant shall submit one description of the industrial design, including the following contents:

a) Name of an industrial design means the name of the product bearing such industrial design expressed in common words, not for advertising purposes, containing no signs, annotations or commercial indications;
b) Field of use of an industrial design means a specific field in which the product bearing such industrial design is used, clearly indicating the purpose of use of such product;
c) The closest similar industrial designs: clearly stating the industrial designs most dissimilar to the industrial designs of the same product type stated in the application, which are widely known before the filing date or the priority date (if the application requests priority rights), specifying the sources of information disclosing such closest similar industrial designs;
d) List of photos or drawings: list photograms, three-dimensional drawings, projections, cross-sections, etc. of the industrial design in the order of their numbers;
e) The description of an industrial design shall comply with the following regulations:
(i) Fully disclose the nature of the industrial design subject to protection, which must fully describe the design features expressing the nature of the industrial design and at the same time indicate new design features different from the nearest similar industrial designs mentioned in Point 33.5.c above, consistent with the design features shown in the set of photos or drawings;
(ii) Design features of the industrial designs subject to protection shall be presented in the following order: three-dimensional features, lines, correlation between three-dimensional features and/or lines, color features (if any);
(iii) For products with different usage states (e.g., products with a lid or foldable cover), the industrial designs of such products in different states must be described;

(iv) If the industrial design consists of multiple schemes, the distinctive features of the remaining schemes compared to the basic scheme (the first scheme stated in the application) must be specified;

(v) If the industrial design is the design of a set of products, the design of each product in the set must be described.
g) Scope of protection of the industrial design (or "protection request"): it is required to fully list features necessary and sufficient to determine the nature of the industrial design requested for protection and the scope of industrial property rights to such industrial design, which are shown in the photos and drawings stated in the application, including new and distinctive features compared with similar known industrial designs. 33.6 Requirements for sets of photos or drawings of industrial designs The applicant shall submit 05 sets of photos or 05 sets of drawings of industrial designs. The set of photos and drawings shall fully display the design features of the industrial design requested for protection so that anyone with average knowledge in the relevant field can identify such industrial design according to the following guidance:
a) The photos or drawings must be clear and sharp; the drawings must be drawn with continuous lines; the background of the photos or drawings must have a uniform color and contrast with the industrial design; on the photos or drawings, only products with the protected industrial design are shown (not accompanied by other products).
b) The photos or drawings must show the industrial designs in the same scale. The size of an industrial design shown in a photo or drawing must be between 90 mm x 120 mm and 190 mm x 277 mm.
c) The photos and drawings must show the industrial designs in the same orientation and in the following order: three-dimensional views of the industrial designs, projections of the industrial designs from the front, back, right, left, top and bottom; the projections must be frontal.
d) For a symmetrical industrial design, the photograph or drawing may not need to further show the symmetry, provided that this is clearly stated in the list of photographs and drawings included in the description.
e) For a product’s industrial design which can be developed (for example: containers, packages, etc.), the projections of such industrial design may be replaced by photos or drawings of the industrial design in the developed state.
g) Depending on the complexity of the industrial design, additional photos and perspective drawings from different angles, cross-sections, enlarged views of parts, separate details of the product, etc., may be required to clearly show the new and distinctive features of the industrial design requested for protection.
h) For products with different usage states (e.g., products with a lid or which can be folded), photos and drawings of the industrial design of the product in different states are required.

i) For the industrial design of a part of a complete product, additional photos and drawings illustrating the installation and use of the part on the complete product are required.
k) Each scheme of an industrial design must have a set of photos and drawings fully presenting each scheme as prescribed in this Point.
l) For a set of products, there must be a perspective drawing of the entire set of products and a set of photos and drawings of each product in the set as prescribed in this Point.
33.7 Design features of industrial designs
a) Design features of an industrial design means elements expressed in the form of lines, shapes, colors, position correlation or size correlation so that when combined with other characteristics (signatures), they constitute a necessary and sufficient collection to create such industrial design.
b) The following elements shall not be considered design features of an industrial design: (i) Shapes and lines determined by the technical function of the product (e.g., the flat shape of a data recording disk determined by the relative movement between the disk and the reader...);
(ii) Elements whose presence in the collection of signs does not sufficiently create aesthetic impression (the impression of the product’s appearance remains unchanged when such elements are present or absent; for example: a change to a familiar shape or line but such change is insufficient to be recognized, therefore the changed shape or line is still only recognized as the old one); (iii) Materials used for manufacturing products;
(iv) Signs affixed or stuck on products are only for the purpose of providing information and instructions about the origin, characteristics, structure, uses, and methods of use of such products; e.g., words on product labels;
(v) Size of products, except for changes to decorative sizes of fabric samples and similar materials.
c) Fundamental design features and non-fundamental design features Fundamental design features are those which can be easily recognized or remembered, and which are necessary and sufficient to identify an industrial design and distinguish it from other industrial designs of the same type used for products of the same type.
Design features which fail to satisfy the above conditions are called “non-basic design features”.
34. Format appraisal and publication of applications for registration of industrial designs The format appraisal and publication of applications for registration of industrial designs shall be carried out according to the general procedures specified in clauses 13 and 14 of this Circular.
35. Substantive examination of applications for registration of industrial designs
35.1 Assessment of similarity of industrial designs:
a) Two industrial designs shall be regarded as identical when they are used for products of the same type and have the same collection of fundamental and non-fundamental shaping characteristics;
b) Two industrial designs shall be regarded as being similar when they are used for products of the same type and have some basic shaping features which are identical;
c) Two industrial designs shall be regarded as the closest similar when they have more identical basic shaping characteristics than all other similar industrial designs.
35.2 Procedures for substantive examination of applications for registration of industrial designs The substantive examination of an application for registration of industrial design shall be carried out in accordance with the general procedures specified in Clause 15 of this Circular and the specific procedures prescribed in this Clause.
35.3 Assessment of compatibility between subject matters stated in applications and types of industrial design protection titles
The subject matter stated in the application shall be deemed incompatible with the type of title of protection of industrial designs if:
a) The subject matter is not the appearance of the product;
b) The subject matters stated in the application are:
(i) The appearance of a product which is required by its technical characteristics; or (ii) The appearance of a civil or industrial construction work;

(iii) Internal appearance (the invisible part) during the use of the product (utilizing the product's functions in the ordinary way by any user, excluding maintenance, repair or repair work).
35.4 Information lookup
a) Information lookup purpose
The purpose of information lookup is to search among the mandatory information sources for industrial designs identical or similar to those stated in the application.
b) The mandatory minimum sources of information used in the process of content appraisal of an application include the following documents:
(i) Industrial design registration applications already received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and having an application publication date earlier than the submission date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights);
(ii) Applications for registration of industrial designs and protection titles of industrial designs announced by other organizations or countries within 25 years before the filing date or the priority date of the application which is being examined (if the application enjoys priority right), shall be kept in the existing industrial design database at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
(iii) Other information relating to industrial designs collected and kept by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
(iv) The application for registration of an industrial design which has been received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and has a submission date or priority date earlier than the submission date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal (used to check the first-to-file rule prescribed in Point 35.9 of this Circular).
c) In necessary and possible cases, the search may be expanded beyond the minimum compulsory sources of information.
35.5 Search report
Search results shall be presented in search reports, specifying the searched field, scope of search and search results within such scope (statistics and identification of found control industrial designs, information sources, date of publication of corresponding information) and full name of persons making reports (searchers).
In this point, “industrial design for comparison” means an identical or similar industrial design with respect to the industrial design stated in the application, which is compared with the industrial design stated in the application when assessing the novelty and creativity.
35.6 Assessment of industrial applicability of industrial designs according to Article 67 of the Law on Intellectual Property
a) The industrial design stated in the application shall be considered industrially applicable if, based on the information about the industrial design presented in the application, a person with average knowledge in the relevant field can use such industrial design as a model to manufacture products with an appearance identical to that of the industrial design by industrial or handicraft methods.
The concept of "person with average knowledge" in the relevant field is understood according to the corresponding provisions at Point 23.6.a of this Circular.
b) In the following cases, the subject matter stated in the application shall be considered as having no industrial applicability:
(i) The subject matter stated in the application is the shape of a product with an unstable existence state (products in solid form, liquids, etc.);
(ii) It is only possible to create products with the shape of the subject matter stated in the application by using special skills or it is impossible to repeatedly create products with the shape of the subject matter stated in the application;
(iii) Other cases with legitimate reasons.
35.7 Assessment of the novelty of industrial designs according to Article 65 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
a) Method of assessing the novelty of industrial designs To assess the novelty of industrial designs stated in applications, it is necessary to compare the set of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial designs with the set of fundamental design characteristics of the closest identical/similar industrial designs used as control industrial designs found during the information lookup process.

b) Conclusion on the novelty of industrial designs
An industrial design stated in an application shall be regarded as new if:
(i) The industrial design is not found in the mandatory information sources; or (ii) Although the industrial design is found in the mandatory information sources, the industrial design mentioned in the application has at least one basic shaping feature not present in (not belonging to) the set of basic shaping features of the industrial design used as a reference, or (iii) The industrial design used as a reference is the industrial design mentioned in the application that is published/discovered and falls under the cases specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 65 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
a) Methods for assessing the creativity of industrial designs
To assess the creativity of an industrial design mentioned in an application, it is required to compare a collection of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial design with a collection of fundamental design characteristics of each control industrial design that are identical or similar found during the information lookup process.
b) Conclusion on the creativity of the industrial design
In the following cases, the industrial designs stated in the applications shall be regarded as not being creative:
(i) Industrial design means a simple combination of known design features (publicly disclosed design features arranged or simply assembled together such as replacement, change of position, increase or decrease in quantity, etc.);
(ii) Industrial designs means the shape of a natural form of trees, fruits, animals, etc., which is partially or entirely replicated or simulated, or the shapes of geometric figures (circle, ellipse, triangle, square, rectangle, regular polygon, prism with cross-section being one of the above-mentioned figures) which are widely known;
(iii) Industrial designs means a mere reproduction of the appearance of products or works which are well known or widely recognized in Vietnam or in the world;
(iv) Industrial designs which are imitations of industrial designs in other fields if such imitation has been widely known in practice (e.g. toys imitating cars, motorcycles, etc.). If not falling into the above cases, industrial designs shall be regarded as being creative.

35.9 Examination of the principle of first filing according to Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property
a) To verify the first-to-file principle, it is required to search information in the mandatory sources specified in Point 35.4.b (iv) of this Circular.
b) The industrial design stated in the application shall be considered meeting the first-to-file rule if no identical or significantly different industrial designs are found in the applications that have satisfied the conditions for the grant of an industrial design patent during the information search.
c) The industrial designs of product parts stated in the application shall also be considered satisfying the first-to-file rule if no identical or significantly different industrial designs of product parts and/or products stated in other applications which satisfy the conditions for grant of an industrial design patent are found during the information search.
d) In case there are many different applications for registration of industrial designs which are identical or insignificantly different from each other and all satisfy the conditions for grant of an industrial design patent and have the same earliest priority date or submission date, the industrial design stated in such application shall still be regarded as satisfying the first-to-file rule prescribed in Clause 2, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property if all applicants reach agreement on the filing of a single application bearing one industrial design patent.
35.10 Notification of results of content appraisal
Notification of results of content appraisal of applications for registration of industrial designs shall comply with Point 15.7.a of this Circular.
36. Granting, registering and publishing industrial design patents

The procedures for issuing, registering and publishing decisions on issuance of industrial design patents shall comply with the general provisions prescribed in clauses 18 and 19 of this Circular.
Section 5. PROCEDURES FOR REGISTRATION OF MARKS
37. Requirements on mark registration applications
37.1 A mark registration application shall satisfy the general document requirements prescribed in clauses 7 and 10.1 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this clause.
37.2 An application shall ensure consistency with provisions of clauses 1 and 4 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property. Each application may request registration of only one mark for one or more than one good or service.

37.3 In case of doubt about the authenticity of information stated in an application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to submit the following documents within 01 month for verification of such information:
a) Documents proving the applicant’s status:
(i) The business registration certificate, contract or other document certifying the production of products or provision of services by the applicant as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property; and (ii) Agreement or letter certifying that the manufacturer does not use the mark and does not object to the registration of the mark by the person conducting commercial activities of the manufacturer as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(iii) Decision or license to establish, charter of an organization certifying the function and powers to manage collective marks, quality certification marks, geographical origin marking marks for goods and services as prescribed in clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

(iv) Agreement, business registration paper, documents related to mark registration of co-owners as prescribed in clause 5 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(v) Documents certifying that the applicant has acquired the mark registration right from another person according to Clause 6 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(vi) The agreement or written approval of the mark owner certifying the right to register the mark of the representative or agent as prescribed in Clause 7 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 6septies of the Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property.
b) Documents proving the representative status of the applicant: original power of attorney of the applicant; documents confirming that the representative of the applicant is the legal representative of the mark registration organization or the person authorized by such organization; documents confirming that the authorized person of the applicant meets the requirements to act as the representative of the applicant as prescribed in Point 3 of this Circular.
c) Documents proving the right to use/registration of marks containing special signs for: (i) Names, symbols, flags, armorial bearings of domestic and international agencies or organizations or certification seals, inspection seals, warranty seals of international organizations as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 4 Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(ii) Names of characters or images of works subject to copyright protection that are widely known, or trade names, commercial indications, origin indications, prizes, medals or distinctive symbols of certain products which may cause confusion as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(iii) Signs falling within the scope of protection of an industrial design of another person as prescribed at Point n, Clause 2, Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) Documents proving priority rights;
e) Information necessary to clarify or confirm the contents stated in the regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks or other documents.
37.4 Requirements for declarations The applicant shall submit 02 declarations made according to Form No. 04-NH specified in Appendix A of this Circular with the following notes:
a) The description of the mark in the declaration must specify the type of registered mark (common mark, collective mark, integrated mark, certification mark);
b) For a mark registered as an integrated mark, the applicant must specify the integrated elements of the mark or of goods or services as follows:
(i) In case the integrated element is a mark (similar to another mark used by the same applicant for the same goods or service or for similar goods or services), it must be specified whether there is any integrated mark considered fundamental or not, if so, which one is it; if one or several of these marks have been registered or mentioned in previous applications, it must be specified the protection title number, the previous application number;
(ii) In case the integrated element is a good or service (one mark used for similar or related goods or services), it must be specified whether there is any good or service considered fundamental or not, if so, which one is it; if one of these goods or services has been registered or mentioned in previous applications, it must be specified the protection title number, the previous application number;
(iii) If the applicant does not specify the fundamental mark or fundamental goods or service, all marks and all goods or services related to the mark stated in the application of the applicant shall be considered independent of each other. The assessment of distinguishability of the mark stated in the application shall not apply the exception for integrated marks prescribed at Point e Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property but comply with the general provisions on assessment of distinguishability prescribed at Point 39 of this Circular.
c/ For certification marks, the applicant shall specify in the declaration the purpose, contents and mode of such certification (certifying what, quality, origin, geographical origin or a combination of these purposes; certification contents: specific conditions on subjects, goods and services; certification mode: certification licensing order and procedures, methods of inspection and maintenance of certification purposes and contents).
d) The declaration must contain a sample of the mark and a description in words of such mark according to the following regulations:
(i) If a mark consists of multiple elements, it must specify these elements and their combination; if the mark contains a figurative element, its content and meaning must be clearly stated;
(ii) If a request for protection of a color trademark is filed, such request must be clearly indicated and the name of the color shown on the trademark must be stated.
(iii) If the mark contains words or phrases other than Vietnamese, the Vietnamese pronunciation must be clearly indicated and, if such words or phrases have a meaning, they must be translated into Vietnamese;

(iv) If the mark contains numerals other than Arabic numerals or Roman numerals, they must be translated into Arabic numerals.
e) The section "List of goods and services bearing marks" in the declaration must be grouped according to the International Classification of Goods and Services under the Nice Agreement, which is published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.
37.5 Requirements for mark samples
In addition to the sample mark affixed on the declaration, the application must be accompanied by 9 identical samples of the mark and must meet the following requirements:
a) The mark sample must be clearly presented with the size of each component in the mark not larger than 80mm and not smaller than 8mm, the overall mark must be presented in the mark template with size of 80mm x 80mm printed on the declaration;
b) For a three-dimensional mark, the mark sample must be accompanied by a photograph or drawing showing the perspective view and may be accompanied by a descriptive sample in the form of a projection;
c) For a mark requiring color protection, the mark sample must be presented in the exact colors required for protection. If no color protection is required, the mark sample must be presented in black and white.
37.6 Requirements regarding regulations on use of collective marks and certification marks Regulations on use of collective marks and certification marks must contain the corresponding contents specified in Clauses 4 and 5 Article 105 of the Law on Intellectual Property and must clarify the following issues:
a) Summary information about the mark, mark owner, goods, and services bearing the mark;
b) Conditions for obtaining permission from the mark registrant to use the mark and conditions for terminating the right to use the mark;
c) Obligations of the mark user (ensuring quality and specific characteristics of goods and services bearing the mark, subject to the control of the mark registrant, paying mark management fees, etc.);

d) Rights of the mark registrant (controlling compliance with regulations on use of the mark, collecting mark management fees, terminating the right to use the mark of those who fail to meet the conditions prescribed in the regulations on use of the mark, etc.);
e) The mechanism for licensing, controlling and inspecting the use of marks and ensuring the quality and reputation of goods and services bearing marks;
g) Dispute settlement mechanism.
37.7. Requirements on documents certifying the permission to register and use geographical origin marking trademarks
a/ For a geographical origin marking, in addition to the regulations on use of the mark and necessary documents evidencing the registration right, the application must be enclosed with a license from the concerned local authority permitting the applicant to register the geographical origin marking (geographical name, symbol or map of the region or locality) for goods or services bearing such mark.
b/ In case it is impossible to identify a region or locality bearing a geographical origin indication based on the administrative boundaries and license of the local authority specified at Point 37.7.a above, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall request the applicant to provide a geographical map clearly indicating the scope of the region or locality bearing the geographical origin indication of the goods or services with certification by the concerned local authority.
38. Formal examination and publication of mark registration applications
The procedures for formal examination and publication of mark registration applications shall comply with the general provisions prescribed in clauses 13 and 14 of this Circular.
39. Substantive examination of applications for registration of marks
39.1 The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for conducting content appraisal of valid applications in accordance with the general procedures specified in Point 15 of this Circular and the specific procedures prescribed in this Point.
39.2 Assessment of the compatibility between subject matters stated in applications for registration of marks and the request for issuance of certificates of mark registration
a) As prescribed in Clause 1 Article 72 of the Law on Intellectual Property, signs registered as marks must be visible signs in the form of letters, numbers, words, drawings, images, including three-dimensional images or a combination thereof, expressed in one or several certain colors.
b) The following signs shall not be protected as marks:
(i) Signs are color alone and not combined with signs in the form of letters or figures, or not presented as signs in the form of letters or figures;
(ii) Signs which are subject to protection refusal as marks according to Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
(iii) Signs contrary to social order or harmful to national security.
39.3 Assessment of distinguishability of signs in the form of letters or numerals (hereinafter referred to as “letters”) according to Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Apart from the exceptions specified in Point 39.5 of this Circular, the following signs shall be considered indistinguishable:
a/ Characters belonging to languages which Vietnamese consumers have no common knowledge about and cannot read, understand or remember, such as characters not derived from Latin: Arabic, Slavic, Sanskrit, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, etc.; except when such characters are accompanied by other components forming a whole which can be distinguished or presented in graphic form or in other special forms;
b/ Although they are characters derived from Latin, signs consist of only one letter or only numbers, or although they contain two letters but cannot be read as a word, even when numbers are attached; except when such signs are presented in graphic form or in other special forms;
c/ A collection of too many letters or words which cannot be recognized and remembered, such as a string of too many characters not arranged in a definite order or rule, or a document or a paragraph of a document;
d/ Although they are characters derived from Latin, they are words with meanings which have been widely used and common in Vietnam in related fields so that the discrimination is lost;
e/ A word or a collection of words used in Vietnam as common names of relevant goods or services;

f/ A word or a collection of words with contents describing relevant goods or services bearing marks as signs indicating time, place, geographical origin (except for cases where marks are registered as marks certifying the geographical origin of goods or collective marks), production methods, categories, quantity, quality, properties (except for cases where marks are registered as marks certifying the quality of goods or services), components, uses, value of goods or services;
g/ A word or a collection of words with meanings describing legal forms or business lines of mark owners;
i) Signs of letters identical or similar to one of the objects within the scope of protection of industrial property rights of others as prescribed in Points e, g, h, i, k, l, m Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
k) Signs of letters misleading, confusing or deceiving consumers about origin, features, utility, quality, value or other characteristics such as composition, production process, raw materials, superiority of goods and services as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
l) Signs of letters identical or similar to the point of confusion with real names, aliases, pseudonyms of leaders, national heroes, famous personalities of Vietnam or foreign countries; identical or similar to the point of confusion with names of characters, images in works under the scope of copyright protection of others which have been widely known, except for cases permitted by owners of such works.
39.4 Assessment of distinguishability of signs of drawings and images (hereinafter referred to as “graphic signs”) according to Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property
Except for exceptions prescribed in Point 39.5 of this Circular, graphic signs shall be considered indistinguishable if:
a) Graphic signs are common geometric figures such as circles, ellipses, triangles, quadrilaterals, etc., or simple drawings; drawings and images are only used as backgrounds or decorative lines of products or product packages;
b) Drawings and images are too complicated for consumers to easily perceive and remember their characteristics such as having too many combined or overlapping images and lines;
c) Drawings, images, symbols and signs have been widely used;
d) Drawings or images mainly describing the goods or services bearing the mark; locations, methods of production, geographical origins, categories, quantity, quality, properties, components, uses, value or other characteristics of the goods or services bearing the mark;
e) Signs which are identical with or insignificantly different from the industrial designs of others under protection;
g) Signs which are identical with or confusingly similar to the images of leaders, national heroes or famous personalities of Vietnam or foreign countries; identical with or confusingly similar to the images of characters or figures in works protected by copyright of others, which have been widely known, except where permission is granted by the owners of such works.
39.5 The following exceptions shall apply when assessing the distinguishability of written and graphic signs:
a) Signs falling into the cases specified at Points 39.3.a, b, c, g, h and Points 39.4.a, b, c, d, e of this Circular which have been used with the function of marks and are widely known to consumers, thereby enabling the marks to achieve a degree of distinguishability for related goods or services.
b) In order to be entitled to this exception, the applicant must provide evidence of the widespread use of such mark (the starting time, scope and extent of current use, etc., provided that the mark is only considered "used" when such use is carried out in lawful production, business, commercial, advertising or marketing activities) and evidence of the distinguishability of the mark for related goods or services of the mark owner. In this case, the mark shall only be recognized as having distinguishability when it is presented in the form in which it has been continuously used and is widely known in practice.
39.6 Assessment of the distinguishability of combined signs of written and graphic signs (hereinafter referred to as "combined signs")

A combined sign shall be considered having distinguishability when the combination of the written and graphic signs forms an overall sign with distinguishability, specifically:
a) The letter and figure signs are distinguishable and form an overall distinguishable sign;
b) The strong component of a mark (an element that strongly affects the consumer's perception, causing attention and impression about the mark upon observation) is a sign in the form of letters or figures that is distinguishable, even though the remaining components are indistinct or indistinctly distinguishable;
c) In case a combination of signs including both written and graphic signs is indistinguishable or has little distinguishable ability but the unique combination of these signs creates a distinct impression, such overall combination shall still be considered distinguishable;
d) The combination sign consists of letters and images that are indistinguishable or have little distinguishable but overall has achieved distinguishable capability through use according to Point 39.5 of this Circular.
39.7 Minimum information sources
a) In order to assess the ability to cause confusion of signs stated in the application, at least the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct a search in the following minimum sources of information:
(i) Mark registration applications already filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam with an earlier filing date or priority date than the filing date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal, and international mark registration applications designating Vietnam which have been notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to WIPO with an earlier filing date or priority date than the filing date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal for identical or similar goods and services;
(ii) Markes which have been registered for protection or recognized for protection and are still valid in Vietnam (including well-known marks) used for identical, similar or related goods and services; (iii) Markes which have been registered but have their validity terminated within less than 5 years, except for cases where the validity of a mark is terminated due to non-use as prescribed at Point d, Clause 1, Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property, used for identical or similar goods and services;
(iv) Geographical indications currently under protection in Vietnam;
(v) Indications of geographical origin of goods or services; geographical names, quality marks, inspection marks; national flags and national emblems of countries; flags, names and symbols of agencies and organizations of Vietnam and the world; names and images of leaders, national heroes, names and images of Vietnamese and foreign celebrities, etc., which are collected and preserved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
b) Where necessary, reference sources may be consulted in addition to the minimum sources mentioned in Point 39.7.a above, such as applications for registration of industrial designs, trade names, etc.; 39.8 Assessment of the similarity to the point of confusion between a registration request sign and another mark
a) In order to assess whether a sign mentioned in an application is identical or similar to the point of confusion with another mark (hereinafter referred to as "control mark"), it is necessary to compare the structure, content, pronunciation (for signs in written form), meaning and form of expression (for both signs in written form and signs in graphic form), and at the same time compare goods or services bearing the sign with those bearing the control mark according to the provisions of this Point.
b) Sign identical to the control mark: A sign shall be considered identical to the control mark if it is identical to the control mark in terms of structure, content, meaning and form of expression.
c) A sign shall be considered similar to the point of confusion with the control mark if:
(i) The sign is similar to the control mark in terms of structure or content or pronunciation or meaning or form of expression to the point of confusion, making consumers mistakenly believe that these two subjects are one or that this subject is a variant of that other subject or that these two subjects have the same origin;
(ii) The sign is merely an transcription or translation from the control mark if the control mark is a well-known mark.
39.9 Assessment of similarity of goods and services
a/ Two goods or two services are regarded as identical (of the same type) when they have the following characteristics:
(i) having the same nature (components, structure, etc.) and the same functions and uses; or (ii) having nearly the same nature and the same functions and uses;

b/ Two goods or two services are regarded as similar when they have the following characteristics:
(i) being similar in nature; or (ii) being similar in functions and uses; and (iii) being put on the market through the same commercial channel (being distributed by the same mode, being sold together or side-by-side, in the same type of shop, etc.);
c) A good and a service shall be regarded as similar if they fall into one of the following cases: (i) They are related in terms of their nature (the one is made from the other); or (ii) They are related in terms of their function (the one is required for the performance of the function of the other or they are often used together); or (iii) They are closely related in terms of their mode of performance (the one is the result of the use of the other).
39.10 Examination of the principle of first to file
Before issuing a decision to grant a certificate of registered mark, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file principle as prescribed in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property. 39.11 Conclusion on the likelihood of confusion between signs and control marks Signs are considered identical or confusingly similar to control marks used for identical, similar, or related goods in the following cases:
(i) Signs identical with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of goods or services bearing the control mark;
(ii) Signs identical with the control mark and goods or services identical with those bearing the control mark of the same owner;
(iii) Signs are similar enough to be confused with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of the goods or services bearing the control mark, except for exceptions where similarity in goods or services and similarity in signs is insufficient to create the possibility of confusion when using similar signs;
(iv) Signs identical or similar to a control mark being a well-known mark and goods or services bearing signs not identical or similar to those of goods or services bearing such mark, but the use of such signs as marks may mislead consumers into believing that there is a relationship between goods or services bearing such signs and owners of the well-known mark, which may actually reduce the distinguishability of the well-known mark or damage the reputation of the well-known mark.
39.12 Assessment of other capacity to cause confusion of signs
The appraisal of other possible confusion of signs shall comply with Article 73 and Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following specific regulations.
a/ Signs are considered misleading as to the origin of goods or services in the following cases:
(i) Signs identical or similar to the name or symbol of a country or territory (national flag, national emblem, national name, local name) or similar to the extent that they cause confusion with the name or symbol of a country or territory leading to the mistaken belief that the goods or services bearing the mark originate from such country or territory but actually originate from another country or territory; (ii) Signs identical or similar to a protected geographical indication if such signs may mislead consumers as to the geographical origin of goods; signs identical to or containing or being translated or transcribed from protected geographical indications for wines and spirits if the signs requesting registration as marks for wines or spirits do not originate from geographical areas bearing such geographical indications;
(iii) Sign means a word identical or similar to the trade name of another person which has been lawfully used for the same type of goods or services and which may cause consumers to mistakenly believe that the goods or services bearing such sign are produced or performed by the person with such trade name; sign means an image identical or similar to the commercial symbol of another person which has been lawfully used for the same type of goods or services and which may cause consumers to mistakenly believe that the goods or services bearing such sign are produced or performed by the person with such commercial symbol;

(iv) Signs identical or similar to real names, aliases, pen names, images of leaders, national heroes, famous personalities of Vietnam and foreign countries; signs identical or similar to names or images of characters, typical images of widely known works, if the use of such signs may cause consumers to mistakenly believe that goods or services bearing such signs are produced or performed by owners of such works;
(v) Signs identical with or insignificantly different from protected industrial designs of others on the basis of applications for registration of industrial designs with submission dates or priority dates earlier than those of applications for registration of marks.
b/ In the following cases, signs shall be considered likely to cause confusion or misunderstanding as to the nature and value of goods or services:
(i) Sign means words, drawings, images or symbols which create a misleading impression on the features or utility of goods or services, such as signs identical with or similar to marks or other signs widely used to the extent that they are considered associated with certain features or utilities of specific goods or services, causing consumers to mistakenly believe that goods or services bearing such signs also have such features or utilities;
(ii) Signs are words or images that create a misleading impression about the composition or structure of goods or services, such as describing other goods or services related to those with signs that create the misleading impression that the goods or services with signs are created from or have the same nature as the described goods or services.
40. Grant, register and publish mark registration certificates The procedures for issuing, registering and publishing mark registration certificates shall comply with general procedures prescribed in clauses 18 and 19 of this Circular.
41. Processing of applications for registration of international marks of Vietnamese origin and applications for registration of international marks designating Vietnam
41.1 Procedures for international registration of marks of Vietnamese origin The provisions on procedures for processing mark registration applications prescribed in this Circular shall also apply to the processing of international mark registration applications at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
41.2 Right to international registration of marks based on the basic mark registration in Vietnam
a) A person who has been granted a certificate of registered mark in Vietnam may register internationally the corresponding mark under the Madrid Agreement;
b) Any person who has filed an application for registration of a mark in Vietnam and is granted a certificate of registration of the mark in Vietnam shall have the right to register such mark internationally under the Madrid Protocol. 41.3 Applications for international registration of marks of Vietnamese origin
a/ An application for international registration of a mark which designates a country being a member of the Madrid Agreement but not any other country being a member of the Madrid Protocol shall be made in French.
b/ An application for international registration of a mark which designate at least one country being a member of the Madrid Protocol, including concurrently designate a country being a member of the Madrid Agreement shall be made in English or French.
c/ The applicant shall submit an application form for international registration of a mark, made according to Form No. 06-DKQT in Annex C of this Circular, and an application for international registration of a mark, made according to a form provided free of charge by the National Office of Intellectual Property. In the application form, the applicant shall specify the countries being members of the Madrid Agreement (which may concurrently be members of the Madrid Protocol) and the country designated as a member of the Madrid Protocol for which he/she wishes to register the protection of the mark. The application for international registration of a mark shall be made by accurately and fully filling in information in the blanks reserved for the applicant and by enclosing the mark samples in conformity with the sample already registered in Vietnam.
d/ The applicant shall preliminarily calculate the total amount of fees and charges according to the fee schedule on the application form or request the National Office of Intellectual Property to notify the exact amount of fees and charges payable to the International Office. The applicant shall directly pay such fees and charges to the International Office and additionally pay relevant charges and fees to the National Office of Intellectual Property.
e/ The applicant shall ensure that the information (especially the name and address of the applicant, goods and services and classification of goods and services) declared in the application for international registration of a mark is accurate, including language, translation and consistency with the information stated in the certificate of base mark registration or the application for registration of the base mark. The applicant shall pay all charges arising from amendments or additions to the application for international registration of a mark due to inaccurate or inconsistent declaration of information as notified by the International Office.

g) All letters and transactions related to the international registration application for a trademark shall be conducted through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall promptly notify the requester of its request to the International Office and vice versa, in accordance with relevant international treaties.
41.4 Agency receiving an application for international registration of a Vietnamese origin trademark
a) An application for international registration of a trademark shall be filed with the International Office through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall transfer the application within 30 days from the date of receipt of all valid documents as prescribed.
b) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the application for international registration of a trademark shall be considered the filing date of the application if the International Office receives such application within 2 months from the date indicated on the receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the applicant fails to complete the application before the above-mentioned time limit, the date on which the application is received at the International Office shall be considered the filing date of the application.
41.5 Supplementation, amendment or assignment of an application for international registration of a Vietnamese origin trademark
a) After the application is filed with the International Office, all transactions between the applicant and the International Office related to the modification of the name, address, limitation of the list of goods and services stated in the application for international registration of a trademark, or extension of validity of the international registration of a trademark shall be made using Form No. 08-SDQT specified in Appendix C of this Circular and through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The applicant shall pay fees and charges for these transactions as prescribed.
b) The owner of a trademark shall, through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (or directly request the International Office for international registration of a trademark in a country that is a signatory to the Madrid Protocol), request the International Office to recognize the assignment of the trademark ownership registered under international registration of a trademark, in accordance with relevant international treaties, and pay fees and charges as prescribed.
a/ After receiving a notice from the International Office on an international mark registration application with designation of Vietnam, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall conduct an examination of the contents of the application according to the procedures applicable to applications for mark registration submitted directly to the National Office of Intellectual Property. Within 12 months after the International Office issues the notice, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall make a conclusion on the protectability of the mark.
b/ For marks which are likely to be protected under Vietnamese law, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall issue a decision to accept the protection of the internationally registered mark and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette and record it in the National Register of Marks within one month after issuing the decision. The scope (volume) of protection shall be confirmed according to the contents of the request for international registration of the mark recorded by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) and certified by the National Office of Intellectual Property.
c/ For marks which are ineligible for protection or partially rejected, before the expiration of the above-said 12-month time limit, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall send a written notice of rejection to the International Office so that the latter may notify the applicant thereof, clearly stating the reason and contents of the rejection.
d/ Within three months after the National Office of Intellectual Property sends the rejection notice, the applicant may lodge a complaint about such notice. The procedures for lodging and settling complaints shall be the same as those applicable to applications for mark registration submitted directly to the National Office of Intellectual Property. Results of the complaint settlement shall be notified by the National Office of Intellectual Property to the International Office and the applicant.
e/ From the date the internationally registered mark is recognized and takes effect in Vietnam, at the request of the mark owner, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall grant a certificate of internationally registered mark protected in Vietnam, provided that the requester pays the fee according to regulations.
41.7 Extension of validity of international registration of a Vietnamese origin mark Within 6 months before the expiry date of validity of an international registration of a mark (20 years from the filing date of the international registration of a mark in respect of a country being a member of the Madrid Agreement, including a country concurrently being a member of the Madrid Protocol; or 10 years from the filing date of the international registration of a mark in respect of a country being a member of the Madrid Protocol but not concurrently being a member of the Madrid Agreement), the mark owner shall pay the fee for extension of validity of the international registration of the mark according to the notice of the International Office.

41.8 Application for conversion of a mark which has been invalidated by international registration of the mark
a) In case of international registration of a mark in Vietnam by its owner who is a country member of Madrid Protocol which has been invalidated according to Article 9 quinquies of Madrid Protocol, such owner may file an application for registration of a transformed mark according to Form No. 07-DKCD specified in Appendix C of this Circular to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam for protection of such mark for part or all of goods or services included in the list of goods and services recorded in the international registration of the invalidated mark.

b) The application for registration of a transformed mark shall be accepted if it satisfies the following conditions: (i) It is filed within 03 months from the date on which the international registration of the corresponding mark becomes invalid;
(ii) Goods and services described in the trademark registration application for conversion are included in the list of goods and services specified in the international registration of common marks;
(iii) The application for registration of a mark converted shall satisfy all requirements in terms of form and content as prescribed by Vietnamese law and the applicant fully pay fees and charges as prescribed.
c) The application for registration of a transformed mark shall be considered as having the priority date of the international application for registration of the mark or the priority date of the international application for registration of the mark (if the international application for registration of the mark is eligible for priority under an international treaty).
d) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the trademark conversion registration application as for a regular trademark.
42. Recording of well-known marks
42.1 Well-known marks shall be protected under Vietnamese law as prescribed in Article 75 of the Law on Intellectual Property and in accordance with Article 6bis of the Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property.

42.2 The rights to well-known marks shall be protected and belong to their owners without registration procedures. Mark owners may use the documents specified in Point 42.3 of this Circular to prove their ownership of the marks and to prove that the marks meet the conditions for being considered well-known.
42.3 Documents proving the ownership of a mark and proving its popularity shall include information on the scope, scale, level and continuity of use of the mark, including descriptions of the origin, history and period of continuous use of the mark; the number of countries where the mark has been registered or recognized as a popular mark; the list of goods and services bearing the mark; the territorial scope in which the mark is circulated; sales volume of products or provision of services; the quantity of goods and services bearing the mark that have been produced and sold; the value of the asset of the mark, the price of transfer or licensing of the right to use, the investment capital contribution value of the mark; investment and advertising costs, mark advertising, including participation in national and international exhibitions; infringement cases, disputes, court decisions and judgments or competent authorities; survey data on consumers’ knowledge of marks through purchase, use, advertisement and marketing; ranking and assessment of the prestige of marks of national and international organizations and mass media; prizes and medals won by marks; results of assessment by intellectual property assessment organizations.
42.4 In case a well-known mark is recognized according to civil procedures or a decision of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, such well-known mark shall be recorded in the List of well-known marks kept at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 6. PROCEDURES FOR REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICES
43. Requirements on applications for registration of geographical indications
43.1 A geographical indication registration application shall satisfy the general requirements prescribed in Points 7 and 10.1 of this Circular and the specific requirements prescribed in this Point.
43.2 The application shall ensure consistency as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 101 of the Law on Intellectual Property, and each application may only register one geographical indication for a product.
43.3 Application documents: Declaration - made according to Form 05-CDDL prescribed in Appendix A of this Circular; Description of properties/quality/reputation of the product; Map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication (both must be made in 02 copies) and 10 samples showing the presentation of the geographical indication shall not be larger than 80mm x 80mm and not smaller than 20mm x 20mm (in case the geographical indication is not a word).
43.4 Requirements for the Description of properties/quality/reputation of the product
a) The description of properties/quality/reputation of a product must contain the following main information: (i) A list of particular properties/quality of the product bearing a geographical indication determined by geographical conditions, which is determined by perceptible, qualitative and quantitative physical, chemical or biological indicators which can be tested by technical means or by experts according to a testing method; and/or (ii) Reputation of the product bearing a geographical indication determined by geographical conditions, which is determined through widespread knowledge of such product among relevant consumers which can be verified; and (iii) Geographical conditions creating particular properties/quality and reputation of the product bearing a geographical indication, including unique elements of meteorology, hydrology, geology, topography, ecology and other natural conditions; unique elements of skills and techniques of producers, including traditional production processes of localities (which may include one, several or all stages from production of raw materials, processing of raw materials to creation of products and may also include packaging stages if such stages affect properties/quality and reputation of products), if such processes are factors creating and maintaining particular properties/quality and reputation of the product bearing a geographical indication, in which there are clear and detailed information which can be checked (if among the above-mentioned information there are secret information or technical secrets not yet disclosed or not widely known outside of localities, the applicant may refuse to provide details about such secrets or techniques if he/she does not commit to keep such information confidential at his/her request); and (iv) Organic relationship between particular properties/quality and reputation of the product bearing a geographical indication mentioned in Points 43.4.a(i) and (ii) with geographical conditions mentioned in Point 43.4.a(iii) above.
b) The description of properties/quality/reputation of a product must be enclosed with documents certifying that information on properties/quality/reputation is well-grounded and authentic (test results, research results, investigation results, etc.).
43.5 Requirements for maps of geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications Maps of geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications shall fully display information to the extent where geographical areas meeting natural conditions creating particular characteristics/quality and reputation of products can be accurately identified. Maps may be submitted together with documents describing geographical areas corresponding to geographical indications.
44. Format appraisal and publication of geographical indication registration applications The format appraisal and publication of geographical indication registration applications shall comply with the general procedures prescribed in Points 13 and 14 of this Circular.
45. Content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application
45.1 Procedures for content appraisal
The content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application shall be carried out according to the general procedures prescribed in Point 15 of this Circular and specific provisions in this Point.
45.2 Assessment of compatibility between subject matters stated in applications and geographical indication registration certificates Subject matters stated in applications for geographical indication registration shall be considered incompatible with geographical indication protection titles if such subject matters are not visible signs used to indicate products originating from specific regions, localities, territories or countries according to Clause 22 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
45.3 Assessment of geographical indications according to protection conditions
a) The subject matter stated in the application shall be accepted for registration and recorded in the National Register of Geographical Indications if fully satisfying the conditions specified in Article 79 of the Law on Intellectual Property and not falling into the cases specified in Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically, it is required to fully prove all conditions:
(i) There is a geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication stated in the application;
(ii) Products originating from the above-mentioned geographical area;
(iii) Such products have particular characteristics/quality and/or reputation decided by geographical conditions of such geographical area according to Article 82 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) In the following cases, the geographical indications stated in the application shall not be registered:
(i) Geographical indications which have become common names of goods in Vietnam;
(ii) Foreign geographical indications which are not protected, have been terminated or no longer used in their country;
(iii) The geographical indication is identical or similar to a mark currently protected in Vietnam, where such use would cause confusion as to the origin of products;

(iv) The geographical indication misleads consumers as to the true geographical origin of products bearing such geographical indication.
c) Methods of assessment of geographical indications according to protection conditions

The assessment of a geographical indication according to the protection conditions stated in Points 45.3.a and b above shall be carried out on the basis of information provided by the applicant and on the basis of the following minimum compulsory sources of information:
(i) Marks currently protected in Vietnam for products identical or similar to products bearing geographical indications, with the protection starting date earlier than the date of filing the application for registration of geographical indications, including marks protected under international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory;
(ii) Marks recognized by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as well-known marks.
In case of finding a mark identical or similar to a geographical indication, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the mark owner thereof for obtaining their opinions within 1 month from the date of signing the notice, which clearly states the right to object to the registration of the geographical indication if there are sufficient grounds to prove that the geographical indication falls under the case specified in Clause 3 Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property. The consideration of the mark owner's opinions shall comply with regulations on the consideration of third-party opinions prescribed in Point 6 of this Circular. Geographical indications cause consumers to misunderstand the true geographical origin of products bearing such geographical indications when they are regarded as identical or similar to the point of no return from which they cannot be distinguished from a sign known by Vietnamese consumers as the geographical origin indication of goods or services.
45.4 Notification of results of content appraisal
The results of content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application shall comply with Point 15.7 of this Circular.
46. Issuance, registration and publication of geographical indication registration certificates The procedures for issuing, registering and publishing geographical indication registration certificates shall comply with general provisions in Points 18 and 19 of this Circular.

48.3 An application for registration of a contract on transfer of industrial property rights shall be deemed flawed in one of the following cases:
a/ The declaration is invalid;
b/ One of the documents in the list of required documents is missing;
c/ The power of attorney is invalid;
d/ The contract copy is not certified as valid;
e/ The name and address of the assignor in the contract do not match the corresponding information in the protection title or in the contract which is the basis for the emergence of the right to assignment, power of attorney or declaration; the name and address of the assignee in the contract do not match the name and address stated in the power of attorney or declaration;
f/ The contract does not have full signatures (and seals, if any) of the assignor and the assignee;
g/ The assignor is not the owner of the protection title;
h/ The related industrial property object is no longer within the validity term of protection or is being disputed;
k) The contract for transfer lacks the prescribed contents according to Article 140 or Clause 1 Article 144 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
l) The contract has contents not in compliance with regulations on conditions restricting the transfer of industrial property rights specified in Article 139 of the Law on Intellectual Property or contains clauses that unreasonably restrict the rights of the transferee of the right to use industrial property subject matter specified in Clause 2 Article 144 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
m) There are grounds to confirm that the transfer of industrial property rights infringes upon the industrial property rights of a third party.
48.4 The time limit for processing applications for registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights is 02 months (excluding the time reserved for applicants to correct errors).
49. Recording of amendments to content, extension and premature termination of validity of contracts for transfer of the right to use industrial property subject matter
49.1 The amendment to content, extension and premature termination of validity of registered contracts for transfer of the right to use industrial property subject matter shall be recorded at the National Office of Intellectual Property as prescribed in this Point.

49.2 Application files requesting recording of amendments to content, extension and premature termination of validity of contracts
a) An application file requesting recording of amendments to content, extension and premature termination of validity of a contract for transfer of the right to use industrial property subject matter shall be made in writing and include the following documents:
(i) 02 copies of the written request for recording of the modification of contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract on transfer of the right to use industrial property object, made according to Form No. 03-SDHQ mentioned in Annex D of this Circular;
(ii) The original of the certificate of registration of the contract on transfer of the right to use industrial property object (in case of registration of modification of contents or extension of validity of the contract);
(iii) Documents proving the modification of names and addresses of parties to the contract;
(iv) Agreement and documents recording specific articles in the contract which need to be modified or supplemented, including the early extension or termination of validity of the contract;
(v) Power of attorney (if the request is filed through a representative);
(vi) Fee receipt.
b) The application for contract extension must be filed at least 01 month before the expiry date of the contract stated in the certificate of registration of the contract on transfer of the right to use industrial property object.
49.3 Within 01 month after receiving the application for recording of the modification of contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall consider the application as prescribed below:
a) In case of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to record the modification of contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract for transfer of the right to use an industrial property object; record the modified or extended contract for transfer of the right to use the industrial property object in the Certificate of registration of the contract for transfer of the right to use the industrial property object; record the modified or extended contract for transfer of the right to use the industrial property object in the National Register of Industrial Property Transfer; and publish the decision to record the modification of contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract for transfer of the right to use the industrial property object in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 02 months from the date of signing the decision;
b) In case the application contains errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to record the modification of contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract for transfer of the right to use the industrial property object, clearly stating the errors in the application and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuing the notice of intended refusal for the applicant to correct the errors or provide opinions opposing the intended refusal to register the contract; and issue a notice of refusal to record the modification of contents, extension or termination of validity of the contract for transfer of the right to use the industrial property object in case the applicant fails to correct errors in the application or fails to meet requirements after being given adequate time for correction of errors, or provides no opinion opposing or provides unjustifiable opinions opposing within the prescribed time limit.
Section 2. PROCEDURES FOR DECISION ON COMPULSORY TRANSFER OF RIGHT TO USE INVENTIONS
50. Applications for issuance of decisions on compulsory licensing of inventions 50.1 Organizations and individuals capable of, obliged or wishing to use inventions specified at Points a, b and c or subject to anti-competitive practices specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property may request competent persons specified in Clause 1, Article 147 of the Law on Intellectual Property to issue decisions on compulsory licensing of inventions under specific provisions in this Section. 50.2 An application for issuance of a decision on compulsory licensing of an invention shall include the following documents:
a) 02 written requests for compulsory licensing of inventions, made according to Form No. 04-CGBB in Appendix D of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the request for issuance of a decision on compulsory licensing of an invention is well-grounded in accordance with law, specifically:
(i) In case of requesting issuance of a decision to force the licensing of an invention pursuant to Point a Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, documents proving that at the time of filing the application, there is a need to use the invention for public and non-commercial purposes, national defense and security, disease prevention, treatment and nutrition of the people or to meet other urgent social needs but the holder of the invention’s exclusive right has not used the invention and such failure to use the invention will affect the achievement of the above-mentioned objectives;

 (ii) In case of requesting issuance of a decision to force the transfer of the invention use right on the basis of Point b, Clause 1, Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, documents proving that the holder of the invention's exclusive right has failed to perform the obligation to use the invention prescribed in Clause 1, Article 136 and Clause 5, Article 142 of the Law on Intellectual Property and at the time of filing the application, the time limit for filing an application for registration of the invention has expired 4 years after the date of filing the application for registration of the invention and the time limit for filing an application for registration of the invention has expired 3 years after the date of grant of the exclusive right to the invention;
(iii) In case of requesting issuance of decision on compulsory licensing of an invention pursuant to Point c Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, documents proving that the applicant for use of the invention has failed to reach agreement with the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention on entering into a contract on use of the invention despite having made reasonable efforts to negotiate a satisfactory price and commercial terms, including the need for use of the invention, period during which negotiations have been conducted, specific price and commercial terms proposed by the applicant;
(iv) In case of requesting issuance of decision on compulsory licensing of an invention pursuant to Point d Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, documents proving that the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention has committed acts considered to be anti-competitive restrictions prohibited under the law on competition;
(v) In case of requesting issuance of decision on compulsory licensing of an invention in semiconductor technology, documents proving that the invention is used only for public or non-commercial purposes or documents proving that the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention has committed acts considered to be anti-competitive restrictions prohibited under the law on competition;
c) Power of attorney (if the application is submitted through a representative);
d) Fee receipt.
51. Procedures for processing applications for issuance of decisions on compulsory licensing of inventions 51.1 Applications shall be filed as follows:
a) Applications falling within the cases specified at Points b, c and d Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
b) Applications falling within the case specified at Point a Clause 1 Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be filed with ministries or ministerial agencies managing inventions.
51.2 Appraisal of dossiers
When there are dossiers falling into the cases specified in Point 51.1.b of this Circular, ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall designate an agency to receive and appraise such dossiers.
Within 2 months from the date of receipt of the application dossier, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and the agency appraising the dossier of a Ministry or ministerial-level agency (hereinafter referred to as the "dossier appraisal agency") shall consider the dossier according to the following regulations:
a) In case of valid application: Within 15 days after receiving an application dossier, the dossier appraisal agency shall issue a notice requesting compulsory licensing of the invention to the holder of the exclusive right to use such invention and request such person to give his/her opinion within one month after the date of issuance of the notice; request the involved parties to re-negotiate in order to overcome their differences for signing a contract on licensing of the invention if necessary; in case no agreement is reached among the involved parties and it is deemed unreasonable to refuse to sign a contract on licensing of the invention by the rights holder, it shall report the results of the dossier examination and propose the Minister of Science and Technology or the Minister The heads of ministerial-level agencies shall issue decisions to force the licensing of inventions.
If an application falls within the case specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the invention is used for public and non-commercial purposes, ministries or ministerial-level agencies may issue decisions to force the licensing of the invention without seeking the opinion of the holder of the exclusive right to use the invention or without negotiating with the parties.
b) In case of request for issuance of decision on transfer of invention use rights without justifiable grounds as prescribed in Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the agency appraising the application shall report on the results of examination of the application and propose the Minister of Science and Technology or the Minister or Head of a ministerial-level agency to issue notice of intended refusal, clearly stating the reasons for refusal and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide its opinion on the intended refusal.
The time limit for applicants to correct errors in their applications or to provide opinions shall not be included in the time limit for application review.

c/ For dossiers falling into the cases specified at Point a, Clause 1, Article 145 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the dossier-appraising bodies of ministries or ministerial-level agencies shall send copies thereof for consultation to the Ministry of Science and Technology (via the National Office of Industrial Property) before submitting them to the ministers or heads of ministerial-level agencies for decision according to the provisions of Points a and b above. Within 15 days after receiving the dossiers, the National Office of Industrial Property shall examine the dossiers and report to the Minister of Science and Technology for sending written opinions requesting the ministers or heads of ministerial-level agencies to issue decisions to force the licensing of inventions or to issue notices of refusal.
51.3 Within 15 days after receiving reports on the results of examination of dossiers from the National Office of Industrial Property, the Minister of Science and Technology shall consider issuing decisions to force the licensing of inventions or shall send notices of refusal to force the licensing of inventions to the applicants therefor, clearly stating the reasons therefor.
Within 15 days after receiving written requests from the Minister of Science and Technology, the ministers or heads of ministerial-level agencies shall consider issuing decisions to force the licensing of inventions or shall send notices of refusal to force the licensing of inventions to the applicants therefor, clearly stating the reasons therefor.
In case of disagreement with the request of the Minister of Science and Technology, the ministers or heads of ministerial-level agencies shall issue written notices clearly stating the reasons therefor.
51.4 Decisions to force the licensing of inventions shall be sent by the ministers or heads of ministerial-level agencies to the licensees, the holders of the exclusive right to use inventions and the National Office of Industrial Property.
The National Office of Industrial Property shall record such decisions in the national register of industrial property licensing within one month and publish them in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within two months after the date of signing of the decisions.
52. Request for termination of the right to use an invention pursuant to a compulsory decision 52.1 The termination of the right to use an invention pursuant to a compulsory decision shall be made by the Minister or Head of a ministerial-level agency who has issued the compulsory decision. 52.2 A request for termination of the right to use an invention pursuant to a compulsory decision shall include the following documents:
a) Written request for termination of the invention use right under a compulsory decision;
b) Documents proving the grounds leading to the compulsory licensing of the invention no longer exist and are unlikely to reappear, and the termination of the use of the invention does not cause damage to the licensee under the compulsory licensing decision;
c) Power of attorney (if submitting application through representative);
d/ Documents proving payment of fees.
52.3 Procedures for receiving and processing requests for compulsory termination of invention use rights and issuing termination decisions shall be carried out in the same manner as procedures for receiving and processing applications for transfer of invention use rights under compulsory decisions prescribed in Clause 51 of this Circular.
Chapter III
                            INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REPRESENTATIVE
Section 1. ISSUANCE AND REVOCATION OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REPRESENTATION SERVICE PRACTICE CERTIFICATES
53. Issuance of practicing certificates
53.1 Conditions for issuance of practicing certificates
Only persons who fully satisfy the conditions specified in Clause 2, Article 155 of the Law on Intellectual Property may be granted industrial property representation service practicing certificates. To be granted a certificate, such persons shall submit their application dossiers to the National Office of Intellectual Property under this Point.
53.2 Application for issuance of practicing certificate
An application for issuance of an industrial property representation service practicing certificate shall include the following documents:
a) 02 copies of the application form for issuance of industrial property representation service practicing certificate (practicing certificate), made according to Form No. 01-CCHN specified in Appendix E of this Circular;
b) Copies of the notice of satisfactory completion of the examination on industrial property representation organized by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c/ 2 3x4 photos;

d/ Fee payment document.
53. Handling of dossiers of application for practicing certificates:
In case the dossier is valid, within 1 month after receiving the dossier, based on the dossier review, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to grant a practicing certificate and grant it to the dossier submitter; record the granting of the practicing certificate in the National Register of Industrial Property Representatives; publish the granting of the practicing certificate in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 2 months after signing the decision; or notify the deficiencies in the dossier and fix a time limit of 1 month for the dossier submitter to correct them; or issue a notice of refusal to grant the practicing certificate, clearly stating the reason therefor if the dossier submitter fails to correct the deficiencies or the dossier is invalid.
54. Revocation of practicing certificates:
If an industrial property representative commits a violation and is sanctioned by revocation of his/her practicing certificate, based on the decision to revoke the industrial property representation service practicing certificate issued by the competent State agency handling the administrative violation, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to revoke the practicing certificate; delete the name of the industrial property representative from the list of industrial property representatives of the industrial property representation service organization; publish the revocation of the practicing certificate in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 2 months after signing the decision.
55. Re-granting of practicing certificates:
The National Office of Industrial Property shall carry out procedures for re-granting the industrial property representation service practicing certificate at the written request made according to the form 02-CLCC specified in Annex E of this Circular of the industrial property representative in case the practicing certificate is lost or damaged (torn, dirty, faded, etc.) to the extent that it cannot be used.
The procedures for re-granting a practicing certificate shall be carried out similarly to those for granting a practicing certificate as prescribed in Clause 53.3 of this Circular.
Section 2. RECORDING, AMENDING AND DELETING NAMES OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REPRESENTATION SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
56. Recording of industrial property representation service organizations
56.1 In order to officially exercise the right to represent applicants before the National Office of Intellectual Property, an industrial property representation service organization must carry out the procedures for recording in the National Register of Industrial Property Representatives as prescribed in this Point.
56.2 A dossier requesting the recording of an industrial property representation service organization in the National Register of Industrial Property Representatives shall include the following documents:
a/ Two copies of the declaration requesting the recording of the industrial property representation service organization, made according to Form No. 03-YCGN in Appendix E of this Circular;
b/ The list of members who have the industrial property representation service practicing certificates of the organization, enclosed with copies of the decisions on recruitment or labor contracts applicable to each member;
c/ Copies of the business registration paper/operation registration paper of the organization;
d) The list of charges for industrial property representation services of the organization;
e) A written authorization for representation issued by the head of the organization to one of its members on the list mentioned in Point 56.2.b above (if necessary);
g) Charges paid.
56.3 Within 01 month after receiving the dossier, the National Office of Industrial Property shall consider the dossier requesting the recognition of an industrial property representation service provider similar to the procedures for granting an industrial property representation service practicing certificate prescribed at Point 53.3 of this Circular.
57. Recognition of changes in information about industrial property representation service providers 57.1 An industrial property representation service provider may request the National Office of Industrial Property to recognize changes related to the name and address of its organization or member on the list of industrial property representatives.
57.2 A dossier requesting recognition of changes in information about an industrial property representation service provider must include the following documents:
a) 02 copies of the declaration requesting recognition of changes in information about the industrial property representation service provider, made according to Form No. 04-YCSD specified in Annex E of this Circular;
b) A copy of the modified business registration certificate/operation registration certificate of the industrial property representation service provider (in case of change of name or address of the organization).

c) Decision on employment or decision on termination of labor contract with industrial property representatives being members of organizations (in case of change of members in list of industrial property representatives of organizations);
d/ Documents proving payment of fees.
57.3 Procedures for processing dossiers of request for amendment of information on industrial property representation service organizations shall be carried out similarly to the procedures specified in Clause 53.3 of this Circular.
58. To delete names of industrial property representation service organizations
In the cases specified in Clause 2, Article 156 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to delete the name of the industrial property representation service organization; record the deletion of the name of the industrial property representation service organization in the National Register of Industrial Property Representatives; notify the deletion of the name of the industrial property representation service organization to the agency competent to grant the certificate of business registration or certificate of operation registration of such organization; and publish the deletion in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 02 months from the date of signing the decision.
Section 3. INSPECTION OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REPRESENTATION ACTIVITIES
59. Industrial property representation inspection
The examination of industrial property representation practice (hereinafter referred to as "examination") under Article 28 of the Decree on Industrial Property shall be conducted in accordance with specific regulations below.
59.1 The Industrial Property Representation Examination Council:
The Industrial Property Representation Examination Council (hereinafter referred to as “the Examination Council”) is established by the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and has the tasks of issuing examination papers, organizing the examination and marking examination papers according to the Regulation on examination of industrial property representation issued by the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “the Examination Regulation”).
The Examination Council shall be composed of the President of the Council, who is the Director of the National Office of Intellectual Property, Vice President of the Council, Secretary and other members designated by the President from the list of experts who may participate in the Examination Council. The list of experts who may participate in the Examination Council shall include those who are knowledgeable about law and practical implementation of law on establishment of industrial property rights under the National Office of Intellectual Property and professional organizations on industrial property representation approved by the Minister of Science and Technology on the basis of proposals of such agencies and organizations. Decisions of the Council shall be passed on the principle of voting with two-thirds of the consent of the members of the Council.
Remuneration for the work of the members of the Council shall be paid from the revenue generated by examination fees for industrial property representation cases according to the Examination Regulation.
59.2 Registration for examination:
a) Only those who fully satisfy the conditions specified in Points from a to dd Clause 2 Article 155 of the Law on Intellectual Property may register for examination according to this Point.
b) The application for examination registration shall be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and includes the following documents: (i) 02 copies of the declaration for examination registration (made according to Form No. 05-KTNV specified in Appendix E of this Circular);
(ii) Copies of university degrees;
(iii) Documents proving that they have been trained in industrial property law or have experience in this activity: Copies of certificates of graduation from training courses in industrial property law recognized by the Ministry of Science and Technology or written certifications of training institutions where applicants have completed their undergraduate or postgraduate dissertations on industrial property; or documents certified by competent agencies that such persons have directly worked as appraisers of industrial property registration applications at national or international industrial property offices for 5 years or more, or have directly worked in the field of industrial property law specified at Point d, Clause 1, Article 155 of the Law on Intellectual Property (including inspection, examination, supervision, adjudication, legal affairs, and legal consultancy on industrial property); Scientific research with title of researcher or lecturer in industrial property;
(iv) 02 3x4 photos;
(v) The receipt of payment for examination fee.
59.3 In case of a valid application dossier, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of his/her eligibility for taking the examination and specify the time, place and schedule of the examination.

59.4 Examination contents and questions:

a) The inspection contents include:
(i) Vietnamese law on industrial property and international treaties to which Vietnam is a signatory; (ii) Practices of filing, submitting and pursuing applications for registration of industrial property;
(iii) Industrial property information lookup and exploitation activities;
(iv) Other contents if necessary.
b) The examination questions shall be enclosed with answers and score scales prepared by the Examination Council, approved by the President of the Examination Council and kept confidential until the start of the examination time.
59.5 Examination organization
a) The examination shall be organized in sessions (not on a regular basis) when there are at least 05 persons registering for examination.
b) The Examination Council shall mark the examination papers according to the approved answers and score scales.
c) The results of the examination shall be notified by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to examinees. Examinees may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to conduct a re-examination and the Examination Council shall conduct a re-examination according to regulations of the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
The examination results shall be valid within 02 years for application for industrial property representation service practicing certificates.
Chapter IV
                     INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY INFORMATION SECURITY
60. Development and management of national database on industrial property 60.1 The National Office of Intellectual Property shall develop and manage a national database on industrial property in order to promptly, fully and accurately meet the needs of information on industrial property for research, application, development and other socio-economic activities.
60.2 National database on industrial property means information already published as mentioned below, which is collected, selected and systematically compiled for search purposes:
a/ Industrial property registration applications;
b/ The granted protection titles and industrial property rights which have been recognized for protection in Vietnam;
c) Patents granted by countries/regions with the most advanced science and technology;
d/ Some other types of protection titles for industrial property rights depend on the use purpose of information.
61. Access to and exploitation of industrial property information in national databases 61.1. All organizations and individuals shall have the right to access and exploit information in national databases on industrial property after registering their use of such information with the National Office of Intellectual Property.
61.2 Persons wishing to access and exploit industrial property information ("information users") shall have to search for such information themselves in the national database.
Users may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to search for information in the national database and shall pay a search fee as prescribed.
62. Industrial property information lookup services
62.1. When requesting the National Office of Intellectual Property to look up and search for information on industrial property, the information user shall make an application form for lookup (according to the forms 01-YCTC, O2-YCTC and 03-YCTC specified in Annex F of this Circular), which clearly states the purpose and scope of the lookup request (field, type of documents containing information, time, country or region to be looked up...).
62.2 Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request form for lookup, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall respond to the user.
In case of a valid request for lookup (with a valid lookup request form as prescribed in Point 62.1 of this Circular and payment of lookup fee), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall respond by sending a "lookup report" to the user, clearly stating the lookup or search results as requested by the user.

In case of an invalid request for lookup (an invalid or unclearly defined request form, failure to pay lookup fee, etc.), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify refusal and provide reasons.
62.3 A search report shall contain only found information and indications of sources of such information. If no information is found in the requested sources, this must be clearly stated in the search report.
A search report must not contain any comments or assessments about the found information. 62.4 The report on a search must clearly state the full name of the person who conducts the search and shall be responsible for the search results.
63. Assurance of industrial property information in localities 63.1. Depending on their conditions and capabilities, the State management agencies in charge of industrial property (the provincial/municipal Services of Science and Technology) may set up and manage industrial property databases in order to assure industrial property information for local activities of research, application, development of production, business and protection of industrial property rights. 63.2. The State management agencies in charge of industrial property in the provinces and centrally-run cities shall have the responsibility and right to conduct activities to assure industrial property information according to the provisions of this Circular.
64. Issuing copies of documents
64.1 All concerned organizations and individuals may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue copies of documents issued or kept by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, including certification of the copy from the original or the archive. Only applicants are entitled to request the issuance of copies of documents related to unpublicized applications. Applicants shall pay fees as prescribed.
64.2 A request for issuance of copies of documents shall include:
a) Written request for issuance of copies of documents, including 2 copies;
b) Original copies of documents issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (in case documents are not stored at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam);
c/ Charge receipts.
64.3 Within 03 working days from the date of receipt of a request for issuance of copies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue copies or notify the refusal and provide an explanation.
Chapter V
                                   IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
65. Regulations on carrying out procedures for industrial property
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall promulgate regulations on procedures for industrial property in accordance with the Decree on Industrial Property and this Circular.
66. Transitional provisions
66.1 An invention registration application as stipulated in this Circular shall be understood as including both an application for grant of an invention patent and an application for grant of a utility solution patent in accordance with the previous regulations.
66.2 For internationally registered marks already recognized for protection in Vietnam under previous regulations, the WIPO Industrial Property Official Gazette or the National Office of Intellectual Property's Industrial Property Official Gazette announcing such internationally registered marks shall be valid as basis for certification of rights to such marks.

66.3 Before new regulations on fees and charges are promulgated, the fee and charge rates prescribed in this Circular shall comply with the Ministry of Finance's Circular No. 132/2004/TT-BTC of December 30, 2004 guiding the collection, payment, management and use of industrial property fees and charges.
66.4 The documents mentioned above shall be used until the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam completes technical requirements and issues an official notice on the application of these documents as prescribed in this Circular.
67. Effect
67.1 This Circular replaces the following Circulars:
a/ The Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment's Circular No. 3055-TT-SHCN of December 31, 1996 guiding the implementation of the provisions on procedures for establishing industrial property rights and a number of other procedures in the Government's Decree No. 63-CP of October 24, 1996 providing detailed regulations on industrial property;

b) The Ministry of Science and Technology's Circular No. 29/2003/TT-BKHCN of November 5, 2003 guiding the procedures for establishing industrial property rights to industrial designs;
c/ The Ministry of Science and Technology's Circular No. 30/2003/TT-BKHCN of November 5, 2003 guiding the procedures for establishing industrial property rights to inventions/utility solutions.
67.2 This Circular takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO.
