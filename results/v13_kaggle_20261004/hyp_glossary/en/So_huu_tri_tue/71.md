                                                  CIRCULAR

  AMENDMENTS TO A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH MINISTRY’S CIRCULAR NO. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN OF FEBRUARY 14, 2007 ON GUIDANCE FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF DECREE NO. 103/2006/ND-CP PROVIDING DETAILED REGULATIONS ON INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY, AMENDED BY THE SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH MINISTRY’S CIRCULAR NO. 13/2010/TT-BKHCN OF JULY 30, 2010 AND CIRCULAR NO. 18/2011/TT-BKHCN OF JULY 22, 2011

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 28/2008/ND-CP dated March 14, 2008 defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Science and Technology;

  Pursuant to the Law on Intellectual Property dated November 29, 2005 and Law No. 36/2009/QH12 dated June 19, 2009 amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property dated November 29, 2005 (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Intellectual Property”);

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP dated September 22, 2006 detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property on industrial property and Decree No. 122/2010/ND-CP dated December 31, 2010 amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP;

  The Minister of Science and Technology amends and supplements a number of provisions of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN dated February 14, 2007 guiding the implementation of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP detailing a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property on industrial property, which is amended and supplemented under the Circular No. 13/2010/TT-BKHCN dated July 30, 2010 and the Circular No. 18/2011/TT-BKHCN dated July 22, 2011 (hereinafter referred to as “Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN”):

  Article 1. Amending and supplementing a number of provisions of Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN

  1. To amend Point 1.1 of Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “1.1 Industrial property rights arising or established on the grounds specified in Clause 3, Article 6 of the Law on Intellectual Property, Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 6 of Decree No. 103/2006/ND-CP dated September 22, 2006 of the Government providing detailed regulations and guidance for implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, as amended by Decree No. 122/2010/ND-CP, and as specifically provided in this Point.”

  2. To amend Point 7.1.b (iii) and add Point 7.1.b (iv) of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  "(iii) The geographical area map (if the registered mark is a certification mark of geographical origin of products or a collective mark or certification mark containing a place name or another sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties);

  (iv) A document of the People's Committee of the province or centrally-run city permitting the registration of the mark as prescribed in Point 37.7.a of this Circular (if the registered mark is a collective mark or certification mark containing a place name or another sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties)."

  3. To amend and supplement point 13.8 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “13.8 Time limit for formal examination of applications

  a) The time limit for the formal examination of an application is 01 month from the date of submission of the application as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.

  b) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice according to Point 13.6.a of this Circular, the period for the applicant to respond to the notice shall not be included in the time limit for formal examination. This period is understood as follows:

  (i) The period from the date of issuance of the notice to the date on which the applicant responds to the notice; or (ii) The time limit specified in the notice (including the extension as prescribed), in case the applicant does not respond to the notice.

  c) In case the applicant proactively requests amendment or supplementation of the application or responds to a notice of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam prescribed in Point 13.6.a of this Circular, the time limit for formal examination shall be extended by 10 days in accordance with Clause 4 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.

  d/ Before the date of expiry of the time limits prescribed at Points 13.8.a, 13.8.b or 13.8.c above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall have to complete the examination of the form of the application and notify the result to the applicant according to Point 13.6 or Point 13.7 of this Circular."

  4. To amend Point 15.6.d, to add Points 15.6.dd and 15.6.e of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “d) Before issuing a notice of intended grant of a protection title as prescribed in point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall check the first-to-file rule as prescribed in points 25.7, 35.9 and 39.10 of this Circular.

  dd) The notice prescribed in point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular shall be issued for the following applications:

  (i) Applications not falling into the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

  (ii) Applications with the earliest filing date or priority date among invention registration applications falling into the case specified in clause 1 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

  (iii) Applications with the earliest filing date or priority date among industrial design registration applications falling into the case specified in clause 1 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

  (iv) Applications with the earliest filing date or priority date among mark registration applications falling into the case specified in clause 2 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

  (v) Applications under agreement prescribed in clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.

  e) Applications not falling into the cases specified in point 15.6.d of this Circular shall be handled as follows:

  (i) Being rejected to grant a title of protection because the application does not satisfy the rule of first filing, if the earliest date of filing or priority date is granted a title of protection; or (ii) Being considered as an application with the earliest date of filing or priority date and handled according to the provisions at Point 15.6 above, if all applications with the earlier date of filing or priority date are rejected to be granted a title of protection or withdrawn, being considered as withdrawn."

  5. To amend and supplement point 15.7.a (iii) of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  "(iii) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions or the applicant has corrected deficiencies and obtained satisfactory results or provided a legitimate explanation within the time limit prescribed at Points 15.7.a(i) and (ii) above, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of the content appraisal results, which shall include:

  - For applications falling into the cases specified at Point 15.6.d of this Circular: A notice of intended grant of the protection title and a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for announcement of the decision on the grant of the protection title, the registration fee and the fee for maintenance of validity for the first year for the invention. The applicant may request an extension of the above-mentioned time limit according to the provisions of Point 9.2 of this Circular.

  - For applications not falling into the cases specified at Point 15.6.d of this Circular: A notice of continued processing of the application according to Point 15.6.e of this Circular."

  6. To amend and supplement point 15.8 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “15.8 Time limit for substantive examination of applications

  a) The time limit for content appraisal of an application shall comply with Clause 2 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.

  b) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice according to Points 15.7.a(i) and (ii) of this Circular, the period for the applicant to respond to the notice shall not be included in the time limit for formal examination. This period is understood as follows:

  (i) The period from the date of issuance of the notice to the date on which the applicant responds to the notice; or (ii) The time limit specified in the notice (including the extension as prescribed), in case the applicant does not respond to the notice.

  c) In case the applicant proactively requests amendment or supplementation of the application, or responds to the notice of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam prescribed in Points 15.7.a(i) and (ii) of this Circular, the time limit for substantive examination shall be extended accordingly with the time limit for processing the request for amendment or supplementation of the application or explanation of the applicant as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:

  (i) For an invention, not more than 6 months;

  (ii) For marks, not more than 03 months;

  (iii) For an industrial design, not exceeding 02 months and 10 days;

  (iv) For a geographical indication, not more than 02 months."

  7. To amend and supplement point 25.7 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  “25.7 Examination of the principle of first-to-file for inventions

  For invention registration applications already concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing notices of intended issuance of invention patents or utility solution patents as prescribed in Point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examinations to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:

  a) In order to verify the first-to-file rule, at least the following mandatory sources of information must be searched (but not limited to these sources): All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (by the time of examination) with the same classification index as the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal - up to the third class index and with an application submission date or priority date earlier than the application submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights) but has not yet been published or with a later publication date than the application submission date or priority date (if the application is eligible for priority rights) of the application currently undergoing appraisal.

  b/ The lookup is to find out cases where there are many applications (including the one being appraised) for registration of inventions identical or similar to each other and determine the earliest date of filing or priority date of an application.

  c/ If there are many applications falling into the cases mentioned at Point 25.1.b above, the invention patent or utility solution patent may only be granted to inventions in valid applications with the earliest submission date or priority date among applications which satisfy all conditions for the grant of protection titles.

  d/ Among applications falling into the cases mentioned at Point 25.7.b above, if there are many applications with the same earliest filing date or priority date, the invention patent or utility solution patent may only be granted to an invention or a utility solution of one application out of such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all corresponding objects of such applications shall be rejected from the grant of protection titles."

  8. To amend and supplement point 35.9 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  35.9 Examination of the principle of first-to-file for industrial designs

  For industrial design registration applications which have been concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing a notice of intended issuance of an industrial design patent under Point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:

  a) To verify the first-to-file principle, it is required to search information in the mandatory sources specified in Point 35.4.b (iv) of this Circular.

  b) The lookup is to find cases where there are multiple applications (including those undergoing appraisal) for registration of industrial designs of product parts and/or products that are identical or insignificantly different from each other, and determine the application with the earliest submission date or priority date.

  c/ If there are many applications falling into the cases mentioned at Point 35.9.b above, an industrial design patent may only be granted to the industrial designs in valid applications with the earliest submission date or priority date among the applications which satisfy all conditions for the grant of protection titles.

  d/ Among applications falling into the cases specified at Point 35.9.b, if there are many applications with the same filing date or the earliest priority date, the industrial design patent may only be granted to the industrial design of a single application among them according to the agreement of all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all related objects of such applications shall be rejected from the grant of protection titles."

  9. To amend and supplement point 37.7 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  37.7. Requirements on documents permitting registration of collective marks or certification marks containing geographical names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam

  a/ Documents permitting registration of collective marks or certification marks containing geographical names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties, which are granted by the following competent agencies:

  (i) The People's Committee of the province or centrally-run city where the geographical area corresponds with another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of a local specialty (in case the geographical area belongs to a locality);

  (ii) All People's Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities where geographical areas are similar to geographical names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties (in case geographical areas belong to many localities).

  b/ The map of the geographical area corresponding to another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of a local specialty must fully show information enough to identify accurately such geographical area and must be certified by the competent agency mentioned in Point 37.7.a above."

  10. To add the following point 37.8 to the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN:

  “37.8 Criteria for determining place names and other signs indicating geographical origins of products

  a) Signs indicating the geographical origin of a product are signs used for local products and have the meaning to indicate the geographical origin of the product (indicating that the product originates from such locality).

  Signs indicating the geographical origin of a product are usually place names but can also be symbolic signs of a locality (images of typical things of a locality, such as symbols, maps, flags, badges, scenic views, special works of a locality, etc.) or any other signs.

  Place names may be current names or historical names, official names or folk names of a geographical area (determined according to administrative boundaries or geographical methods).

  b) A place name or symbolic sign of a locality used for ordinary products (not specialties) may or may not have the meaning to indicate the geographical origin of the product, depending on the product and the actual use of the place name or symbolic sign of the locality.

  c) The place name or symbolic sign of a locality has the meaning to indicate the geographical origin of the product in the following cases:

  (i) Used for local specialties (special products with reputation due to certain characteristics, produced in the locality);

  (ii) Used for plants, domestic animals and products processed from plants and domestic animals of the locality;

  (iii) Used for products extracted from natural resources (coal, iron, steel, aluminum, cement, stone, salt, wood, etc.) in the locality;

  (iv) Used for products of local developing industries;

  (v) Other cases determined according to products and actual use of local place names and symbolic signs for products.

  d) Local place names and symbolic signs have no meaning to indicate geographical origins of products in the following cases:

  (i) They have been used with common mark function and widely recognized, i.e., achieving the meaning to indicate commercial origin (distinguishability) and losing the meaning to describe geographical origin, e.g.: Hanoi beer, Saigon beer;

  (ii) The corresponding locality cannot be the place where the product is manufactured, e.g.: Arctic cigarettes...

  Local place names and symbolic signs which have no meaning to indicate geographical origins of products may be protected as common marks without permission of local authorities.

  dd) Local place names and symbolic signs which are part of common geographical knowledge and known by many people (e.g.: names of provinces, cities, scenic spots) used for local common products (including products with business advantages but not yet having reputation or quality characteristics) and used by many local business entities for their goods and services with the meaning to describe the place of manufacture (but not having enough grounds to be classified under categories (c) and (d) above) will be objects not eligible for protection.

  However, such local place names and/or signboards may still be used as an auxiliary element in forming the common marks of organizations or individuals in the respective localities, provided that such place names are excluded from the scope of protection (not protected separately) and no permission from the local authorities is required."

  11. To amend and supplement point 39.10 of the Circular No. 01/2007/TT-BKHCN as follows:

  39.10 Examination of the principle of first-to-file for marks

  For mark registration applications which have been concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing notices of intended grant of mark registration certificates under Point 15.7.a (iii) of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:

  a) In order to examine the first-to-file principle, it is required to search all trademark registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (by the time of examination) with an application submission date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority rights) earlier than the application submission date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority rights) of the application currently undergoing appraisal.

  b/ Searching means finding cases where there are many applications (including applications being appraised) of different persons registering marks which are identical or confusingly similar to each other for identical or similar products or services, or many applications of the same person registering identical marks for identical products or services; and determining the earliest filing date or priority date of an application.

  c/ If there are many applications falling into the cases mentioned at Point 39.10.b above, the certificate of registered mark shall be granted only to the mark in the valid application with the earliest filing date or priority date among the applications which satisfy all conditions for the grant of a protection title.

  d/ Among applications falling into the cases mentioned at Point 39.10.b above, if there are many applications with the same filing date or the earliest priority date, the certificate of mark registration shall be granted only to the mark of a single application among them according to the agreement of all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all relevant objects of such applications shall be refused the protection title."

  Article 2. Effect

  This Circular takes effect 45 days after its signing./.
