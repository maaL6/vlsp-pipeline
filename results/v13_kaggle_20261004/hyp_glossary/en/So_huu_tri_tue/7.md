                                            CIRCULAR
  ELABORATION OF A NUMBER OF ARTICLES AND MEASURES FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THE LAW ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, DECREE NO. 65/2023/ND-CP DATED AUGUST 23, 2023 OF THE GOVERNMENT ELABORATING A NUMBER OF ARTICLES AND MEASURES FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THE LAW ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY REGARDING INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY, PROTECTION OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS, RIGHTS TO PLANT VARIETIES AND STATE MANAGEMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ARE AMENDED BY DECREE No. 15/2026/ND-CP DATED JANUARY 14, 2026, DECREE No. 33/2026/ND-CP DATED JANUARY 21, 2026, AND DECREE No. 100/2026/ND-CP DATED MARCH 31, 2026
Pursuant to the Law on Intellectual Property No. 50/2005/QH11 amended by Law No. 36/2009/QH12, Law No. 42/2019/QH14, Law No. 07/2022/QH15, Law No. 93/2025/QH15 and Law No. 131/2025/QH15; Pursuant to Decree No. 55/2025/ND-CP of the Government on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Science and Technology;
Pursuant to Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP of the Government providing detailed regulations on and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP;
At the request of the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and the Director of the Legal Department;
The Minister of Science and Technology promulgates a Circular elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP dated August 23, 2023 of the Government elaborating certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights, and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP dated January 14, 2026, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP dated January 21, 2026, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP dated March 31, 2026.
Chapter I
                                    GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
1. This Circular elaborates Clause 2 Article 67, Clause 3 Article 74, Clause 4 Article 89, Clause 4 Article 94, Clause 8 Article 95, Clause 7 Article 96, Clause 4 Article 97, Clause 8 Article 100, Clause 1 Article 108, Clause 6 Article 109, Clause 3 Article 112a, Clause 4 Article 113, Clause 4 Article 114, Clause 5 Article 115, Clause 4 Article 116, Clause 7 Article 119a, Clause 3 Article 120, Clause 2 Article 150, Clause 3 Article 155 of the Law on Intellectual Property; Clause 5 Article 14a, Clause 2 Article 48, Points a and c Clause 4 Article 62, Points a and c Clause 4 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. Guidelines for implementation of certain contents of the Law on Intellectual Property and Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP:
a) Authorization for implementation of procedures related to industrial property rights in accordance with Clause 4 Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Industrial property official gazette, national register of industrial property, industrial property representatives;
c) Assurance of industrial property information.
Article 2. Regulated entities
Organizations and individuals implementing procedures related to industrial property rights, complaint procedures, and resolution of complaints related to industrial property procedures, organizations and individuals involved in assurance of industrial property information, organizations and individuals involved in training in industrial property laws, professional training in industrial property assessment, and other relevant organizations and individuals.
Article 3. Interpretation of terms
In this Circular, the terms below are construed as follows:
1. “Applicant” performing procedures related to industrial property rights (hereinafter referred to as “applicant”) means an organization or individual filing an application for registration of an invention, layout design, industrial design, mark, geographical indication, filing an international application, submitting a request for extension or maintenance of validity of a protection title, termination or invalidation of a protection title, amendment of a protection title, or submitting an application for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract.
2. “Procedures related to industrial property rights” in this Circular include procedures for registration of establishment of rights to inventions, industrial designs, layout designs, marks, geographical indications, procedures for maintenance and extension of validity of protection titles, procedures for termination and invalidation of protection titles, procedures for amendment of protection titles, and procedures for transfer of industrial property rights. Requests arising from procedures related to industrial property rights include opposition to applications, amendments, additions, recognition of changes in applicants, division, withdrawal, conversion of applications, and requests for rapid substantive appraisal, which are part of the procedures specified in this clause.
3. “Complainant” means an organization or individual making a complaint related to industrial property procedures prescribed in Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
4. “Law on Intellectual Property” means the Law on Intellectual Property No. 50/2005/QH11 amended by Law No. 36/2009/QH12, Law No. 42/2019/QH14, Law No. 07/2022/QH15, Law No. 93/2025/QH15, and Law No. 131/2025/QH15.
5. “Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP” means the Government’s Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP providing detailed regulations on and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, rights to plant varieties, and state management of intellectual property, amended by Decree No. 15/2026/ND-CP, Decree No. 33/2026/ND-CP, and Decree No. 100/2026/ND-CP.
6. "Paris Convention" means the Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property of 1883, amended in 1967 and 1979.
7. "PCT Treaty" means the Patent Cooperation Treaty of 1970, amended in 1979, 1984 and 2001.
8. "Budapest Treaty" means the Budapest Treaty on International Recognition of the Submission of Microbiological Residues for the Conduct of Patent Procedures of 1977, amended in 1980.
9. "Madrid Agreement" means the Madrid Agreement on International Registration of Marks of 1891, amended in 1979.
10. "Madrid Protocol" means the Protocol relating to the Madrid Agreement of 1989, amended in 2006 and 2007.
11. "Haag Convention" means the Hague Convention on International Registration of Industrial Designs of 1999.
12. "PCT Application" means an invention registration application filed under the PCT Treaty.
13. "PCT Application with designation or selection of Vietnam" means a PCT Application filed with any member of the PCT Treaty, including Vietnam, in which Vietnam is designated or selected.
14. “National stage PCT application” means a PCT application designated or selected for Vietnam filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
15. “PCT application originating from Vietnam” means a PCT application filed from Vietnam, which includes a protection request in any member of the PCT Agreement, including Vietnam.
16. “Madrid application” means an international mark registration application filed under the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol.
17. “Madrid application originating from Vietnam” means a Madrid application for protection of a mark filed from Vietnam in any other member of the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol.
18. “Madrid application designated for Vietnam” means a Madrid application for protection of a mark filed from Vietnam in any other member of the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol.
19. “La Hay application” means an international industrial design registration application filed under the Hague Agreement.
20. “La Hay application designated for Vietnam” means a La Hay application for protection of an industrial design filed from Vietnam in any member of the Hague Agreement, including Vietnam.
21. “La Hay application originating from Vietnam” means a La Hay application filed from Vietnam, which includes a protection request for an industrial design filed from Vietnam in any member of the Hague Agreement, including Vietnam.
22. “International Office” means an International Office of the World Intellectual Property Organization.
Article 4. Procedures for processing applications for registration of inventions, industrial designs, marks, geographical indications and layout designs
1. Applications for registration of inventions, industrial designs, marks, geographical indications and layout designs shall be received and processed in the following order, except for cases where there are separate regulations applicable to each type of application according to this Circular and relevant legal documents:
a) Receipt as prescribed in Articles 13, 47, 70 and 95 of this Circular;
b) Formal examination as prescribed in Articles 14, 48, 71 and 96 of this Circular;

c) Publication as prescribed in Articles 15, 49, 72 and 97 of this Circular, except for confidential invention registration applications which shall not be published according to Article 49 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
d) Content examination as prescribed in Articles 17, 50, 73 and 98 of this Circular. Content examination of invention registration applications shall be carried out upon request;
dd) In case a third party has an objection to an application, such objection shall be handled according to Articles 27, 59, 82 and 103 of this Circular;
e) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue decisions on grant or refusal to grant protection titles based on the results of the substantive examination of applications. Decisions on grant of protection titles shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette;
g) During the processing of applications, applicants have the right to amend, supplement, withdraw, split or convert applications as prescribed, provided that corresponding fees and charges are paid.
2. Layout design registration applications shall be received, formally examined and published according to Articles 40, 41 and 42 of this Circular. During the processing of applications, applicants have the right to amend, supplement or withdraw applications as prescribed, provided that corresponding fees and charges are paid. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue decisions on grant or refusal to grant protection titles based on the results of the formal examination of layout design registration applications. Decisions on grant of protection titles shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.
Article 5. Calculation of time limits in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaint procedures
1. Time limits in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaint procedures shall be calculated according to Article 15 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. The period or fixed time limit (including extended periods as prescribed) for applicants, complainants or related parties to respond to notifications, amend or supplement documents as prescribed shall not be included in the time limits for processing applications or requests as prescribed, including:
a) The period from the date of issuance of a notification until the date on which the applicant, complainant or related party responds to the notification; or the fixed time limit stated in the notification in cases where the applicant, complainant or related party does not respond to the notification;
b) The period from the date of filing the application until the date on which the applicant or complainant or related party supplements documents as prescribed in this Circular; or the period for the applicant, complainant or related party to submit supplementary documents as prescribed in this Circular in cases where the applicant, complainant or related party fails to submit supplementary documents.
Article 6. Methods of filing applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights and filing complaints
1. Applicants and complainants may file applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints by themselves or through their lawful representatives in Vietnam according to the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 Article 89, clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
2. Legal representatives of individuals as prescribed in clause 1 of this Article and Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property are determined as follows:
a) For Vietnamese individuals, foreign individuals permanently residing or having production and business establishments in Vietnam, legal representatives include: individuals acting as representatives at law or under authorization; industrial property representation service organizations acting under authorization;
b) For foreign individuals not permanently residing in Vietnam or not having production and business establishments in Vietnam, legal representative is an industrial property representation service organization acting under authorization.
3. Legal representatives of organizations as prescribed in clause 1 of this Article and Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property are determined as follows:
a) For Vietnamese organizations, legal representatives include: representative offices or branches in Vietnam; industrial property representation service organizations acting under authorization;
b) For foreign organizations having production and business establishments in Vietnam, legal representatives include: representative offices or branches in Vietnam or wholly foreign-invested organizations in Vietnam of such organizations established under investment laws; industrial property representation service organizations acting under authorization;
c) For foreign organizations that have no production and business establishments in Vietnam, their lawful representatives are industrial property representation service organizations under authorization.
4. Vietnamese individuals and foreign individuals permanently residing in Vietnam shall submit applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights by themselves and file complaints according to Clause 1 Article 89 and Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property by signing their personal names on the declarations.
5. Vietnamese organizations and foreign organizations having production and business establishments in Vietnam shall submit applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights by themselves and file complaints according to Clause 1 Article 89 and Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property through their legal representatives or persons belonging to such organizations authorized by their legal representatives.
6. When carrying out relevant procedures, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may only conduct transactions with applicants, complainants or lawful representatives of applicants or complainants as prescribed in Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article. The conduct of transactions with the above-mentioned subjects is collectively referred to as transactions with applicants and complainants.
Article 7. Application submission forms
1. Applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints shall be submitted in paper or electronic form via the electronic application submission system in accordance with Articles 89 and 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Circular.
2. Electronic applications specified in Clause 1 of this Article are data messages created or digitalized from paper documents, accurately and completely reflecting the contents and format of paper documents and certified electronically in accordance with the law on electronic transactions. Applications for carrying out procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints in electronic form shall be submitted via the electronic application submission system and shall have the same legal validity as those submitted in paper form.
3. Applications shall be received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam at the One-stop Division of the Ministry of Science and Technology.
Article 8. Authorization in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaint procedures
1. The authorization of representation, including re-authorization and performance of authorization of representation in procedures related to industrial property rights (hereinafter referred to as “authorization”) must comply with the provisions of the Law on Authorization of the Civil Code, Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property and guidance in this Circular.
The applicant or complainant may change the authorized representative. The change of the authorized representative terminates the authorization relationship between the applicant or complainant and the authorized person. The termination of authorization with the authorized person and the change of the new authorized representative must be declared in writing by the applicant or complainant (in the authorization document or a separate document).
The authorized person may re-authorize another person under the provisions of the Civil Code and the re-authorized person must meet the requirements for lawful representation under Article 6 of this Circular. When the re-authorized organization or individual conducts transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints shall submit the initial authorization document together with the re-authorization document, except for cases where such authorization document has already been submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. The time when the authorization document is recognized in the transaction with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints is the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints receives the valid authorization document. For cases of change of the authorized representative or re-authorization or amendment of information related to changes in the scope of authorization, premature termination of authorization, change of the address of the authorized party, this time is the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints receives the corresponding valid documents.
3. In case the written authorization is submitted after the deadline for submission but before the date on which the application is accepted or processed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints with the applicant or complainant (in the declaration or complaint) shall carry out the procedures for formal examination to conclude whether the application is valid or invalid, accepted or rejected, including conclusions on the legality of the representative status.
4. All transactions of any authorized party within the authorization scope at any time shall be considered as transactions in the name of the applicant or complainant, giving rise to rights and obligations of the applicant or complainant. In case of change of the authorized representative or re-authorization, the new authorized representative or the re-authorized person shall continue the representation and all issues arising from the previous authorization by the authorized person in the transaction with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints.
5. If the written authorization has a scope of authorization consisting of multiple independent procedures and the original copy of the written authorization has been submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or the administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints, when carrying out subsequent procedures, the authorized party must submit copies of the written authorization with accurate instructions on the number of the application containing the original copy of such written authorization in the declaration or documents of subsequent procedures.

6. In case the written authorization clearly states that the authorization only terminates when either party issues a written declaration on termination or when another written authorization replaces it, such written authorization shall be considered as having provisions on the term specified in point c clause 2 Article 107 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. In case of authorization to an organization or individual not permitted to represent or authorization to multiple organizations or individuals, including organizations or individuals not permitted to represent, the application shall be considered invalid.
8. The authorization regulations in this Article shall also be applied correspondingly to the implementation of administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP and the complaint about the results of settling such administrative procedures.
Article 9. Responsibilities of applicants, complainants and representatives
1. Applicants, complainants and representatives of applicants or complainants shall ensure the truthfulness of information and documents provided for the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or assigned administrative organizations to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints according to the following regulations:
a) All transaction documents must be self-certified by the applicant, complainant or representative of the applicant or complainant with their signatures and seals of the organization (if any). In cases where the law stipulates that a document must be notarized or authenticated, such must comply with such provisions.
b) All Vietnamese translations of documents made in languages other than Vietnamese must be certified by the applicant, complainant or representative of the applicant or complainant that they are the original translation from such documents, except for Vietnamese translations already notarized to confirm the translation;
c) In case the representative of the applicant or complainant is an industrial property representation service organization, the representative of such organization who signs transaction documents must have the certificate of practice of industrial property representation services.
2. The applicant or complainant shall take responsibility for all consequences and obligations arising due to the representative of the applicant's performance in transactions with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or assigned administrative organizations assisting the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints in accordance with the law.
3. The representative of the applicant or complainant shall be responsible before the applicant or complainant in accordance with the law.
Article 10. Industrial property fees and charges
1. Applicants shall carry out procedures related to industrial property rights and other relevant procedures as prescribed in this Circular and users of industrial property services shall pay fees and charges according to regulations of the Minister of Finance and other service charges as prescribed.
2. Fees and charges shall be collected as follows:
a) Applicants shall pay fees and charges to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as prescribed;
b) When collecting fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue receipts as documents for payment of fees and charges, clearly stating collected fees and charges, and keep them in the application dossier for examination of the application;
c) In case fees and charges are paid by post or directly transferred to the account of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the collection of fees and charges shall be determined through documents proving the payment of fees and charges in the application.
Chapter II
                               HANDLING OF INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Section 1. INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 11. Invention registration applications
1. An invention registration application shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration for invention registration, made according to Form No. 01 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Description of the invention;
c) Summary of the invention;
d) Authorization document (if the application is filed by a representative);
dd) Documents proving the registration right, if the applicant inherits such right from another person;
e) Documents proving priority rights if priority rights are claimed, including:

e1) A copy of the application or the first applications submitted by the receiving authority; e2) The document on transfer of priority rights if such rights are inherited from another person;
g) Presentation documents on the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource for inventions directly created from such genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource;
h) Other documents supporting the application (if any).
2. An application for registration of a confidential invention shall comprise the following documents:
a) Documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall bear a seal of confidentiality as prescribed by law on protection of state secrets;
b) Written confirmation that the subject matter of registration in the application is a state secret as prescribed by the law on protection of state secrets.
Article 12. Requirements on documents in an invention registration application
1. Documents of an invention registration application shall meet respective requirements prescribed in Clauses 2 through 12 of this Article and general requirements regarding format as follows:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (drawings, diagrams, and tables may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one A4 sheet of paper (210 mm x 297 mm) with four margins of 20 mm each, using Times New Roman font, font size not smaller than 13, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b) Documents consisting of multiple pages shall have page numbers written in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents shall be typed or printed using opaque ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents consist of at least 2 pages, signatures shall be affixed to each page or a seal affixed to every two adjacent pages by the applicant or the issuing authority (if any), except for digitally signed electronic documents. Before receiving the application, if insignificant spelling errors are detected, the applicant may correct them and shall affix signatures (and seals, if any) at the correction locations; the signatures and seals shall not obscure the contents of the documents;
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system shall be formatted as “.pdf”; the name of the electronic document shall correspond to the name of the document type as prescribed; individuals signing documents of the invention registration application may use digital signatures to directly sign on the electronic documents or directly sign on the physical documents and scan the physical documents in color mode in case of submission via the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application shall be consistent and common (not local languages, rare words, or self-created terms). Symbols, measurement units, electronic fonts, and spelling rules used in the application shall comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and transaction papers between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Points d, dd, e, and h Clause 1 Article 11 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but shall be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) Documents of the application must ensure that all mandatory information and consistent information are available.
2. Documents in the application must show that the subject matter of protection is a product or process in accordance with Clause 12 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The invention registration application must ensure consistency as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. A group of inventions has a close technical relationship for the sole common creative purpose as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property if it falls into the following cases:
a) One object is used to create (manufacture, manufacture, or prepare) the other object;
b) One object is used to perform the other object;
c) One object is used to use the other object;
d) Objects belong to the same type and have the same function to ensure the same result.
4. The invention registration declaration must be fully filled out with information as required in appropriate places in the Declaration, where in the section "International classification of inventions", the applicant must specify the classification index of the technical solution to be protected according to the latest International Classification of Inventions (according to the Strasbourg Agreement on International Classification of Inventions) published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette (complete classification, including parts, classes, sub-classes, groups (main groups or subgroups)). If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam will classify and the applicant must pay classification fees as prescribed.
5. An invention summary shall be used to briefly describe (not exceeding 150 words) the invention subject to protection requests. The summary shall disclose principal contents of the technical solution and shall only be used for information purpose. The summary may contain drawings or specific formulas. All drawings and specific formulas (if any) shall be presented on half a page of A4 paper and shall be clear. The summary shall be presented as a separate document.
6. An invention description shall include an invention description and protection request (protection scope) of the invention and shall comply with Clauses 7, 8, 9 and 10 of this Article. The invention description may contain drawings (if necessary) to illustrate the invention.
7. The description section of the invention description shall fully and clearly disclose the nature of the technical solution of the invention to the extent that it can be implemented by a person with average knowledge in the art; shall clarify the novelty, inventive step and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requested is the invention patent); and shall clarify the novelty and industrial applicability of the technical solution (if the protection title requested is the utility solution patent). If the application contains drawings, the description shall be consistent with such drawings.
The description shall include the following contents:
a) Name of the invention: briefly indicate one or more objects subject to protection requests (hereinafter referred to as “objects”); the name of the invention shall be concise, accurate and not promotional;
b) Field of use of the invention: the field in which the object is used or related to. If the invention is used or related to many fields, all such fields shall be indicated. Such fields shall be consistent with the results of classification of the invention;
c) Technical status of the invention: the technical status in the field of use of the invention at the time of filing the application (similar objects already known (if any)). If there is no information on the technical status of the invention, this shall be clearly stated;
d) Technical nature of the invention: the nature of the subject matter to be protected, clearly stating the following contents: technical issues to be solved (the purpose of the invention), specifying the purpose that the invention is intended to achieve or the task (issue) that the invention is intended to solve (for example, to overcome disadvantages and limitations of the technical solutions already indicated in the technical state of the invention). The purpose or task of the invention must be presented in an objective and specific manner, not for expansion or advertisement; technical signs (characteristics) that constitute the object requesting protection, namely technical signs (characteristics) that form a solution to achieve the purpose of the invention (called fundamental technical signs) and must indicate new technical signs (characteristics) compared to similar known technical solutions; benefits (effects) that may be achieved compared to the technical state (if any), this content can be described as a separate part, as prescribed at Point h of this Clause;
dd) Brief description of accompanying drawings (if any);
e) Detailed description of the implementation methods of the invention: a detailed description of one or more than one method of implementing the invention so that a person with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field can implement the invention. A person with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field means a person with common practical skills and knowledge about general and popular knowledge in the corresponding technical field;
g) Example of implementation of the invention (if any): indicating one or more than one specific method of implementing the invention. If the invention is characterized by quantitative signs, it is necessary to indicate the specific values of such signs; if it is not possible to determine the quantitative signs, it is necessary to indicate the definite state of such signs. In addition, there must be specific results related to the functions and purposes that the corresponding object allows to achieve;
h) Benefits (efficiency) that may be achieved (if any and not stated in the technical nature section of the invention): may be expressed as increasing productivity, quality, accuracy or efficiency; saving consumed energy and materials; simplifying or creating convenience when processing, operating, managing or using; overcoming environmental pollution, etc. If the benefits (efficiency) that may be achieved are derived from statistical results from experimental data, the applicant must provide necessary conditions and methods for such experiments;
i) Illustrations (if any) must be numbered sequentially and grouped together on separate pages dedicated to illustrations and placed at the end of the description. If there are reference signs on the illustrations to serve the description, these reference signs must be used consistently in all documents of the application.
8. The protection request must be presented briefly, clearly and in conformity with the description and illustrations on separate pages, including points of the protection request, which must clarify new signs of the subject matter of the protection request and comply with the following regulations:
a) The protection request must be fully illustrated by the description, including basic technical signs needed and sufficient to identify the subject matter, achieve the proposed purpose and distinguish the subject matter of the protection request from known subjects;
b) Technical signs in each point of the protection request must be clear, accurate and accepted in the corresponding technical field; terms used in the protection request must be clear and consistent with terms used in the description;
c) The protection request must not refer to the description and illustrations, except for cases where it refers to parts that cannot be accurately described in words, such as the nucleotide sequence and amino acid sequence, interference diagrams, state diagrams, etc.;
d) If the application has illustrative drawings, the signs mentioned in the protection request may be accompanied by reference numbers, but must be placed within brackets. These reference numbers shall not be considered as limiting the scope of protection;
dd) The protection request point should (but is not required) be divided into two parts: "Restricted part" and "Distinct part", in which: "Restricted part" includes the name of the subject and signs of such subject identical to those of the nearest known subject and connected with "Distinct part" by the phrase "distinct in the place" or "characteristic in the place" or equivalent words; "Distinct part" includes distinctive signs of the subject compared to the nearest known subject and these signs combined with signs of the "restricted part" constitute the protected subject;
e) A protection request may include one or more points. In which case, a multi-point protection request may be used to express an object to be protected, with the first point (called independent point) and subsequent points (subordinate points) used to specify the independent point (called dependent point); or express a group of protected objects, with several independent points, each independent point expressing an object to be protected in that group, each independent point may have dependent points. Each protection request point shall only be related to one protected object and must be expressed in a single sentence;
g) Points of the protection request must be numbered consecutively in Arabic numerals and end with a period;
h) Multi-point protection requests used to express a group of objects must meet the requirements: independent points, expressing distinct objects, not citing to other protection request points, except for cases where such citation allows avoiding complete repetition of content of other points; dependent points must be expressed after the independent points from which they depend.
9. For invention registration applications related to pharmaceuticals, in addition to the general requirements for invention descriptions specified in Clause 7 of this Article, the description must state the results of clinical tests and/or pharmacological effects of the pharmaceutical, at least including the following information:
a) Substances/mixes used;
b) Test methods (system) used;
c) Information on test results;
d) The relationship between the results of pharmacological effects obtained in tests and the practical application of pharmaceuticals in disease prevention, diagnosis and treatment.
10. Regarding an invention registration application related to biological technology, apart from meeting the general requirements for the invention description specified in Clause 7 of this Article, the application shall also meet the following requirements:
a) Regarding an invention registration application concerning a genomic sequence or a part of a genomic sequence, the description must include a list of genomic sequences presented according to the World Intellectual Property Organization's standards on presentation of lists of nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences in the invention registration application. The sequence list shall be presented separately at the end of the description;
b) Include electronic information carriers (e.g., floppy disks, optical disks, etc.) readable by common electronic devices, which contain nucleotide sequences and amino acid sequences identical to the sequence list stated in the description in case of requirement of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) Specifically regarding inventions concerning or relating to biological materials, if they cannot be described or fully described to the extent that persons with average knowledge of biological technology can implement them, the invention registration application shall meet the following conditions: c1) Biological material samples have been deposited at the biological material storage agency no later than the date of filing the application for registration of such biological materials; c2) The description clearly states necessary information about the properties of biological materials that the applicant may obtain; c3) The declaration clearly states the biological material storage agency, the storage number of the biological material sample deposited by the storage agency, and documents confirming these information are submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date, or no later than the date of filing the earliest request for disclosure (if any), whichever is earlier, except for cases where the deposit is carried out in accordance with the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Agreement;
d) In case the applicant is not the biological material depositor, the name and address of the depositor and documents certifying the lawful use of biological materials shall be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 16 months from the priority date or no later than the earliest submission date of the application for early disclosure (if any), whichever is earlier, except where the deposit is made in accordance with the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Agreement.
11. For inventions relating to biological materials falling under Point c Clause 10 of this Article, the submission of biological material samples to competent authorities as prescribed in Point c1 Clause 10 of this Article shall meet the following requirements:
a/ The biological material-keeping agency means a Vietnamese or foreign agency on the list of international biological material-keeping agencies under the Budapest Treaty or recognized by a competent Vietnamese agency for its function of keeping biological materials;
b) For biological material samples deposited at a foreign depository, if it is deemed necessary to clarify the nature of the subject matter requested for protection or to meet the requirements of a third party regarding access to such subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may take the following actions:

b1) Request the applicant to deposit additional biological material samples at a competent depositor in Vietnam in cases where the biological material samples have not been deposited at an international competent depositor under the Budapest Treaty;

b2) Request the biological material sample depositor to provide samples in cases where the biological material samples have been deposited at an international competent depositor under the Budapest Treaty.
12. Regarding an application for registration of an invention directly created from a genetic resource or traditional knowledge about a genetic resource, where the applicant fails to submit explanatory documents on the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource that the author of the invention or the applicant has accessed in accordance with Point g Clause 1 Article 11 of this Circular due to the inability to determine the origin of the genetic resource or traditional knowledge about the genetic resource, such fact must be clearly stated and the applicant shall take responsibility for the truthfulness of such statement.
Article 13. Receipt of applications for registration of inventions
1. An invention registration application shall be accepted when it satisfies the following conditions:
a) Having all minimum documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Description, including protection request;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. A secret invention registration application shall be accepted when it satisfies the following conditions:
a) Have sufficient documents as prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article and documents prescribed in Point b Clause 2 Article 11 of this Circular;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
3. In case an application meets all conditions specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and verify the submission date and application number of such application and return a receipt note to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number and results of verification of the list of documents, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For electronic applications, upon receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue an application receipt to the applicant, which includes information on the submission date, the application number, and the results of the inspection of the list of documents in the application, clearly indicating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application submission system.
4. In case an application fails to satisfy all conditions specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the Department of Intellectual Property shall reject it (if the application is submitted by post or via the electronic application submission system, or if the application is submitted by email, the Department of Intellectual Property shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reasons for rejection). For applications submitted by post that are rejected, the Department of Intellectual Property is not required to return the application documents other than the original submitted for comparison.
5. Regarding documents proving priority rights, applicants may submit additional documents within 3 months from the date of application submission as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
6. Regarding secret invention registration applications, the receipt of applications according to Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article must comply with the law on protection of state secrets.
Section 2. APPLICATION FORMS OF INVENTION REGISTRATION
Article 14. Format appraisal of invention registration applications
1. Format appraisal of an invention registration application according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means examination of compliance with regulations on format of the application, thereby concluding whether the application is considered valid.
2. An invention registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:

a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the registration right as prescribed in Article 86 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed against regulations in Article 89 and Article 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not eligible for protection according to Clause 1 Article 8 and Article 59 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) There are grounds to confirm that the author of the invention does not satisfy the requirements specified in Clause 1 Article 122 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) The applicant fails to pay fees and charges as prescribed in Article 10 of this Circular (including cases where the fees for filing the application, publication of the application, examination of the application, and information lookup for examination have not been fully paid, except for the information lookup fee for examination and the content examination fee if no request for content examination is made in the application);
e) The application does not meet the formal requirements specified in Article 12 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding presentation; failing to classify or incorrectly classifying the invention without paying classification fees; lacking a translation of documents proving priority rights (if necessary), a translation of documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent with each other or erased or not certified as prescribed, the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author, applicant, representative, does not have the signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; for a separate application, the explanatory text about the subject matter of protection in the separate application that is included in the original application; documents in the application for secret invention registration that have not yet been sealed as secret as prescribed, etc.;
g) There is no valid written authorization (if the application is filed through a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming the lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (documents proving inheritance rights according to civil laws, documents proving the transfer of application submission rights; assignment contracts or labor contracts, etc.); documents showing pharmaceutical test results on humans, animals or plants mentioned in the description (when the subject matters of protection requests are pharmaceuticals for humans, animals or plants); etc.
In case the above-mentioned fixed time limit expires but the applicant fails to submit additional documents satisfying requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application.
4. Notification of the results of the formal examination shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notification of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the protection title, reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application (including the priority right request) and fixing a time limit of 2 months from the notification date for the applicant to provide opinions or correct deficiencies.
b) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to meet requirements or does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection by the end of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the protection title and send it to the applicant within 15 working days from the end of the above-mentioned time limit.
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant has corrected deficiencies and met requirements or has provided justifiable explanations within the prescribed time limit, the application shall be determined to be valid and transferred for publication and substantive appraisal as prescribed (in case the application requests substantive appraisal). In case the priority right request is rejected, the application shall still be considered valid and transferred for publication as prescribed.
5. Every week, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of invention registration applications that are transferred for publication, clearly stating the application number, filing date, and date of transfer for each application.
6. The filing date of an invention registration application with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in accordance with Clause 3 Article 13 of this Circular.
In case the application is filed through the electronic filing system after the administrative hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the filing date as prescribed in this Clause shall be the next working day.
7. The priority date of an invention registration application shall be determined as follows:
a) If the application does not contain a request for priority right or although it contains a request for priority right, it is not approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the application shall be considered having no priority date;
b) If the application contains a request for priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) The determination of the priority date according to the request for priority right based on the first (first) application submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
8. In case the applicant has a request for priority right but at the time of filing the invention registration application there is no document proving the priority right, the formal examination of the application shall start from the time the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the supplementary documents or end at the expiration of the 3-month period prescribed in Clause 5 Article 13 of this Circular.
Article 15. Publication of invention registration applications
1. An invention registration application shall be published in accordance with Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Contents of an invention registration application published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette include the following information: applicant, representative (if any); application number, filing date, priority date (if any); name and nationality of the inventor; information about the origin of the application (application conversion, application splitting, initial application number of split/conversion application, etc.); summary of the invention; drawings enclosed with the summary (if any); content appraisal request date (if any); early disclosure request date (if any); international classification of the invention; and other information (if any).
3. The description of the invention shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 3. CONTENT APPRAISAL OF INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 16. Request for content appraisal of invention registration applications
1. Applicants may request content appraisal of invention registration applications in accordance with Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following regulations:
a) A written request for content appraisal of an invention registration application shall be made using Form No. 02 in Appendix I of this Circular or included in the invention registration application declaration (if such request is made at the time of filing the application) or Form No. 13 in Appendix I of this Circular in case of a fast content appraisal request;
b) The time limit for filing an application for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall comply with Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property; an application for expedited substantive examination of an invention registration application shall be filed immediately upon submission of the application in accordance with Clause 3 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) The applicant shall pay a lookup fee and a substantive examination fee as prescribed. In case the applicant fails to fully pay the fees and charges mentioned above, the request for substantive examination shall be considered invalid and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam will not conduct substantive examination of the application.
An application for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall be submitted after the date the application is published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette in the second month from the date of receipt of the request. An application for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall be submitted before the date of publication of the application.
2. A third party may request substantive examination of an invention registration application in accordance with Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following regulations:
a) The request for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall be made using Form No. 02 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) The time limit for filing an application for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall comply with Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) The applicant for substantive examination of an invention registration application shall pay a lookup fee and a substantive examination fee as prescribed;
d) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the request for substantive examination of the invention registration application within 02 months from the date of receipt of the request.
dd) In case of an invalid request for content appraisal, within 01 month from the date of receipt of the request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify and impose a 02-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to correct errors. If the applicant fails to correct errors within the imposed time limit or fails to meet the requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the refusal to conduct content appraisal;
e) In case of a valid request for content appraisal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct content appraisal of the application according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Circular and notify the results of content appraisal to the applicant.
Article 17. Content appraisal of invention registration applications
1. Content appraisal of an invention registration application as prescribed in Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the protection eligibility of the technical solution stated in the application according to the protection conditions and determination of the corresponding protection scope according to the provisions of this Article.
2. Assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles for inventions registration applications includes:
a) Assessment of the conformity of subject matters stated in the invention registration applications with the type of protection title according to Article 19 of this Circular;
b) Assessment of subject matters stated in the invention registration applications according to each protection condition according to Articles 20, 21 and 22 of this Circular. The assessment shall be carried out sequentially according to each point stated in the protection request;
c) Assessment of the first-to-file principle according to Article 23 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles under Point b Clause 2 of this Article shall be considered complete when the subject matter of the application has been assessed in all aspects and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter fails to satisfy or satisfies one or more than one protection condition, specifically:
a) There is a reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy one or more than one protection condition; or b) There is no reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Based on the results of the assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles mentioned in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding scope of protection.
5. Before issuing a notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of an invention patent/utility solution as prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 25 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall verify the first-to-file principle as prescribed in Article 23 of this Circular.
6. The notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of an invention patent/utility solution prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 25 of this Circular shall be issued for the following invention registration applications:
a) Applications not falling into the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Applications with the earliest submission date or priority date among invention registration applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 1 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Applications agreed upon under Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. An invention registration application not falling into the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article shall be handled as follows:
a) It shall be rejected from grant of an invention patent/utility solution because it fails to satisfy the first-to-file rule if the earliest filing date or priority date of such application is the one on which the invention patent/utility solution is granted; or b) It shall be regarded as having the earliest filing date or priority date and handled as the case specified in Clause 6 of this Article if all other invention registration applications with earlier filing dates or priority dates are rejected from grant of invention patents/utility solutions or withdrawn or regarded as withdrawn.
8. During the process of content appraisal of an application, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations for the contents of the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application in order to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to remedy the deficiencies.
9. During the process of content appraisal of an application, depending on the complexity of the assessment of protection conditions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may consult independent expert consultants or the Council for Consultation on Invention Appraisal.
The Council for Consultation on Invention Appraisal shall be established and operate on the basis of the principles prescribed in decisions of the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Independent expert consultants shall be selected from the List of Industrial Property Consultants compiled by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and other sources (in case no suitable consultant is available in such List). The applicant may request to participate in meetings of the Council for Consultation on Invention Appraisal or independent expert consultants to clarify information stated in the application for the appraisal by the Council or experts.
Article 18. Minimum information sources
1. To assess the novelty and creativity of technical solutions stated in an application, information must be searched in the following compulsory sources (but the search shall not be limited to these sources only):
a) All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall have the same classification index with the subject matter stated in the application being appraised, up to the third sub-classification index and have an earlier priority or filing date than the priority or filing date of the application being appraised, except for applications that will or will not be published (when assessing novelty) or applications that have not been published by the priority or filing date of the application being appraised (when assessing creativity);
b) Invention registration applications or invention protection titles published by other organizations or countries within 25 years until the filing date or priority date of the application being appraised (if the application is eligible for priority rights) shall be stored in the invention database at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and other information sources as prescribed by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, with the search scope specified in point a of this clause;
c) Confidential invention protection titles issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam until the filing date or priority date of the application being appraised.
In necessary and possible cases, the search may be extended to the national database of science and technology and other accessible information sources, including Internet sources.
2. The purpose of information search is to find technical solutions with similar or identical nature to the technical solutions stated in the application. In this clause:
a) Two technical solutions are considered identical when they have all basic signs (characteristics) that are identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
b) Two technical solutions are considered similar when they have most of their basic signs (characteristics) that are identical or equivalent (interchangeable);
c) "Control technical solution" means a technical solution that is identical or the closest similar to the technical solution stated in the application;
d) “Comparative documents” are documents describing the comparative technical solutions or evidence proving that the comparative technical solutions have been publicly disclosed.
3. The information search results must be presented in the search report, which must clearly state the field of search, the scope of search, and the search results within that scope (statistics on found comparative technical solutions, specifying identical signs, names of comparative documents, page numbers, line numbers, origin of documents, and publication dates of corresponding documents) and must state the name of the person preparing the report (the searcher).
Article 19. Assessment of the conformity of subject matters stated in invention registration applications
1. Subject matters stated in invention registration applications shall be considered unconformable with the type of invention protection title requested by the applicant for issuance (invention patent or utility solution patent) if such subject matters are not technical solutions, specifically not products or processes.
2. Technical solutions (subject matters protected as inventions) as stipulated in Clause 1 of this Article are a necessary and sufficient collection of information on technical methods and/or technical means (application of natural laws) aimed at solving a specific task (problem). Technical solutions may take one of the following forms:
a) Products in physical form, such as tools, machines, devices, components, electrical circuits, etc., expressed as a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by technical signs (features) regarding structure, the product has the function (utility) as a means to meet certain human needs; or products in the form of substances (including simple substances, compounds, and mixtures of substances), such as materials, materials, foods, pharmaceuticals, etc., expressed as a collection of information identifying a man-made product characterized by technical signs (features) regarding the presence, proportion, and state of elements, having the function (utility) as a means to meet certain human needs; or products in the form of biological materials, such as genes, genetically modified plants/animals, etc., expressed as a collection of information about a product containing genetic information altered under human influence, capable of self-reproduction;
b) Process (technological process; method of diagnosis, prediction, inspection, handling, etc.) expressed as a set of information defining the manner in which a specific process or task is carried out, characterized by signs (features) regarding the order, conditions, participants, measures and means for performing operations to achieve a certain purpose.
3. The subject matter stated in the application shall not be considered a technical solution in the following cases:
a) The subject matter stated in the application is merely an idea or intention, or merely raises a problem but does not constitute a way to solve the problem, or fails to answer the question "by what means" or "by what means";
b) The problem (task) raised for resolution is not a technical issue and cannot be resolved by technical means;
c) Natural products, not created by humans.
Article 20. Assessment of novelty

1. To assess the novelty of a technical solution stated in an application, it is necessary to compare the fundamental signs (characteristics) of such technical solution with those of the control technical solution found during the information lookup process according to the provisions of Article 18 of this Circular, in which:
a) Fundamental technical signs of a technical solution may be characteristics of the physical object's structure (details, detail cluster, connection, etc.) or the composition of substances (components (presence, proportion), state of elements, etc.), together with other fundamental technical signs, forming a sufficient and necessary set to determine the nature (content) of the subject matter.
The above fundamental technical signs may be expressed in the form of technical functions of an element in the structure or composition of a product (called functional signs), provided that such expression is sufficient for persons with average knowledge in the field to easily understand the technical means or method of performing such function under normal conditions without requiring creativity. The functions and utility of the subject matter of the protection request are not fundamental technical signs but may be the purpose or achieved result of such subject matter;
b) Fundamental technical signs of the technical solution stated in the application, in protection titles, shall be expressed in the invention protection request, description or drawings;
c) Fundamental technical signs of the technical solution stated in other documents shall be expressed and detected according to the description document or the practical form of such technical solution.
2. In order not to be considered losing novelty for inventions already published in the exceptions prescribed in clauses 3 and 4 Article 60 of the Law on Intellectual Property, applicants shall submit documents related to the publication to prove eligibility for the exception. The above-mentioned documents shall be submitted together with the application or as supplements according to regulations on amendment and supplementation of applications.
3. Corresponding to one point of the protection request, the technical solution stated in the application shall be considered novel compared to the world's technical state if:
a) No control technical solution is found during the information search; or b) A control technical solution is found but the technical solution stated in the application has at least one fundamental technical sign not present in the control technical solution (and such sign is called a different fundamental sign).
Article 21. Assessment of inventive step
1. To assess the inventive step of the technical solution stated in the application, it is required to conduct information search in compulsory sources (but not limit the search to such minimum source) according to clause 1 Article 18 of this Circular.
2. The assessment of the level of creativity of a technical solution stated in an application shall be carried out by assessing fundamental distinctive signs mentioned in the protection request in order to make the following conclusion:
a) Whether or not the fundamental distinctive signs have been considered to be disclosed in the mandatory minimum information source; and b) Whether or not the collection of fundamental distinctive technical signs has been considered obvious to persons with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field.
For a protection request point, a technical solution shall be considered creative if the inclusion of fundamental distinctive technical signs in the collection of fundamental technical signs of the technical solution is the result of creative activities and not the obvious result of common knowledge in the corresponding technical field.
3. In the following cases (but not limited to these cases), for a protection request point, a technical solution shall be considered not creative:
a) The collection of fundamentally distinctive technical signs is obviously obvious (any person with average knowledge in the corresponding technical field knows that in order to perform a given function or achieve a given purpose, it is necessary to use such a collection of signs, and conversely, when using such a collection of signs, it is necessary to achieve the corresponding purpose or perform the corresponding function);
b) The collection of fundamentally distinctive technical signs has been uniformly or similarly disclosed in one or several known technical solutions in the mandatory minimum information source. In which case, two signs are considered uniform if they have the same nature; two signs are considered similar if they have similar natures, the same purpose and the same way of achieving the basic purpose;
c) A technical solution is a simple combination of known technical solutions with functions, purposes and effects, which is also a simple combination of functions, purposes and effects of each known technical solution.
Article 22. Assessment of industrial applicability

1. Technical solutions stated in applications shall be considered “feasible” according to Article 62 of the Law on Intellectual Property if:
a) Information about nature of the solutions together with instructions about necessary technical conditions are presented clearly and sufficiently enough so that persons with average knowledge about corresponding technical fields can create, manufacture or use, exploit or implement such solutions;
b) The creation, manufacture, use, exploitation or implementation of the above solutions is repeated with identical results and similar to results stated in invention descriptions.
2. Technical solutions shall be considered incapable of industrial application in the following cases:
a) Nature of subject matters or instructions for subject matter implementation are contrary to fundamental principles of science (e.g., not conforming to the principle of energy conservation, etc.);
b) Subject matters include elements and components that have no technical connection or cannot be connected (joined, bound, dependent, etc.) to each other;
c) Subject matters contain internal contradictions;
d) Instructions about subject matters can only be implemented a limited number of times (cannot be repeated).
dd) To be able to implement the solution, the person must have special skills which cannot be transferred or only given to others;
e) The results obtained from the inconsistent implementation of the project are not consistent;
g) The results obtained are different from those stated in the description;
h) Having no or lacking the most important instructions for solution;
i) Other cases with legitimate reasons.
Article 23. Evaluation of the first-to-file principle
For invention registration applications already concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing content appraisal results notices with the intended issuance of invention patents or utility solution patents as prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 25 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct inspections to ensure the first-to-file rule prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property under the following regulations:
1. In order to assess the first-to-file rule, at least the following compulsory sources of information must be searched (but not limited to the minimum number of these sources): All invention registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (up to the time of examination) with the same classification index as the classification index of the subject matter stated in the application currently undergoing appraisal - up to the third class index and with an application submission date or priority date earlier than the application submission date or priority date of the application currently undergoing appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights) but has not yet been published or with a later publication date than the application submission date or priority date (if the application is eligible for priority rights) of the application currently undergoing appraisal.
2. The search is to find cases where there are many applications (including those being appraised) for registration of inventions identical or similar to each other and determine the earliest application submission date or priority date.
3. If there are many applications falling into the case specified in Clause 2 of this Article, an invention patent or utility solution patent may only be granted to an invention in a valid application with the earliest application submission date or priority date among applications satisfying the conditions for protection title issuance.
4. Among applications falling into the case specified in Clause 2 of this Article, if there are many applications with the same earliest application submission date or priority date, an invention patent or utility solution patent may only be granted to one application out of such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; otherwise, all such applications shall be rejected from the grant of protection titles.
Article 24. Use of information search results and content appraisal results of foreign invention agencies' invention registration applications
1. During the process of content appraisal of invention registration applications, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may refer to information search results and content appraisal results of corresponding applications filed overseas.
2. Results of search and appraisal mentioned in Clause 1 of this Article include one of the following documents:
a) Search report, appraisal report or notice of appraisal results;
b) Publication of invention patents or protection titles.

3. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of content appraisal results, an applicant may request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to use the content appraisal results of an invention application filed overseas for the purpose of assessing the protectionability if the following conditions are satisfied:
a) The content appraisal results specified in the documents mentioned in Clause 2 of this Article were issued by national or regional intellectual property offices selected as preliminary examination and reference agencies under the PCT Agreement;
b) At least one protection request point in the content appraisal results of the invention registration application filed overseas mentioned above is assessed as meeting the protection conditions;
c) Protection request points of the application filed in Vietnam initially or after amendments are made are identical to protection request points assessed as meeting the protection conditions in the content appraisal results of the invention registration application filed overseas mentioned above;
d) The applicant submits the following documents to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam: a request for use of foreign content appraisal results made using Form No. 12 in Appendix I of this Circular; copies of the appraisal results; Vietnamese translations of the appraisal results, protection request points assessed by the foreign invention agency as meeting the protection conditions, and Vietnamese translations and documents cited in the processing results of the foreign invention agency upon request; a description of amendments, a detailed presentation of amended and added contents compared to the initial description submitted (if any); and fees as prescribed.
4. If the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article are met, the request for use of foreign content appraisal results shall be accepted, and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results within 8 months from the date of receipt of the applicant's request. All procedures for amendment and addition to the invention registration application within the 8-month period mentioned above, if causing any of the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article no longer to be met, shall lead to the application being appraised according to ordinary procedures.
5. In cases where any of the conditions specified in Clause 3 of this Article is not met, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the rejection of the request for use of foreign content appraisal results and the invention registration application shall be appraised according to ordinary procedures.
Article 25. Grant and refusal to grant invention/utility solution patents
1. An invention registration application shall be refused to be granted an invention patent or utility solution patent in the cases specified at Points a, b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clauses 1a and 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. An invention registration application shall be granted an invention patent or utility solution patent when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For invention registration applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clauses 1a and 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of the protection title and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of the protection title, the fee for disclosure of the decision on the grant of the protection title, the registration fee for the decision on the grant of the protection title, the validity maintenance fee and the fee for use of the first-year protection title;
b) If the subject matter stated in the invention registration application does not satisfy the protection conditions or the application falls into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clauses 1a and 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions but the application has deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the protection title, all reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application, and may provide guidance on amendments to the scope (quantity) of protection and impose a time limit of 03 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide opinions opposing or correcting deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or does not provide opinions opposing within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the protection title;
d) In case the applicant rectifies deficiencies or provides objections within the time limit specified in point b of this clause, within 4 months from the date of receipt of the rectification document or objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the examination as follows:

d1) If the rectification is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;

d2) If the rectification is satisfactory or the objection is justifiable given the content examination results and/or the amended application is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content examination results. specifying the intended grant of a title of protection and fixing a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of a title of protection, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of a title of protection, the registration fee for the decision on the grant of a title of protection, the fee for maintenance of validity and the fee for use of the first-year title of protection;
dd) If the applicant fails to fully pay the fee for issuance of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on issuance of a protection title or the registration fee for issuance of a protection title within the time limit specified at Points a and d2 of this Clause, then within 15 working days from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the protection title;
e/ If the applicant fully and punctually pays all fees and charges within the time limit specified at Points a and d2 of this Clause, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall issue a decision on protection title issuance within one month after the payment of such fees and charges. If within the prescribed time limit the applicant has paid all fees for the issuance of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on protection title issuance, the registration fee for the decision on protection title issuance but fails to pay the fee for maintenance of validity and the fee for use of the first-year protection title, such protection title shall still be issued but its validity shall be terminated immediately after its issuance.
4. The decision on issuance of an invention patent or utility solution patent shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance. Published contents include information specified in the corresponding decision (including decision number and issuance date) and those prescribed in Clause 2 Article 15 of this Circular.
The full description of an invention patent or utility solution patent shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5. Inventions and utility solutions shall be issued as electronic copies and paper copies (if the applicant requests a paper copy) according to the forms specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP. In case industrial property rights to an invention are under joint ownership, the invention patent or utility solution patent shall only be issued to the first person in the list of co-applicants.
A copy of an invention patent made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests such copy (specified in the invention registration declaration) for other co-owners.
Section 4. PROCESSING OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO INVENTION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 26. Processing requests for rapid content appraisal of invention registration applications
1. Requests for rapid content appraisal of invention registration applications shall be received after the applicant has fully paid fees and charges as prescribed and has submitted the following documents:
a) Request form for rapid content appraisal, made according to Form No. 13 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the invention has been commercially exploited according to Point b Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP within 12 months prior to the filing date;
2. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process requests for rapid content appraisal as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article as follows:
a) If the request for rapid content appraisal fails to satisfy the requirements specified in Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the appraisal results, clearly stating the reasons for the intended refusal or deficiencies, and impose a time limit of 10 working days from the notification date for the applicant to provide feedback or correct errors;
b) Upon expiration of the time limit specified in Point a of this Clause, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate corrections, or does not provide any objection or provides an unjustifiable objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the refusal to accept the request for rapid content appraisal and continue processing the corresponding invention registration application in accordance with regulations;
c) In cases where the request for rapid content appraisal satisfies the requirements specified in Clause 1 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the request for rapid content appraisal shall be published simultaneously with the publication of the invention registration application and shall undergo content appraisal in accordance with this Circular within the time limit specified in Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, except where the invention registration application is determined to be invalid under Article 14 of this Circular.
Article 27. Handling of objections to invention registration applications
1. Within 6 months from the publication date of an invention registration application, or 3 months in the case of an application subject to rapid content appraisal as prescribed in Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, any third party shall have the right to object to the grant of protection titles for such application and must pay fees and charges.
Objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese accompanied by documents or sources of information to prove. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. In case an objection to an invention registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process such objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 1 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response;
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such feedback to the objector and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) On the basis of information, evidence and arguments provided by the parties, results of the dialogue (if any) as prescribed in Points a, b and c of this Clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the result of processing the objection together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application to the objector, except for the case specified at Point dd of this Clause.
dd) In case an invention registration application has been withdrawn or is considered as having been withdrawn according to Article 116 and Clause 3 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the suspension of processing of objection opinions within 01 month from the date of notification of acceptance of withdrawal of the application or from the date of expiry of the time limit for requesting content appraisal of the invention registration application specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 113 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. In case opinions of objectors are related to the right to register inventions, such objector’s opinions shall be handled as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the objector to file a lawsuit petition to a competent Court according to regulations of law on civil procedures and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the objector to send a copy of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit;
b) Upon expiration of the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if the objector fails to send a copy of the notice of acceptance of the lawsuit or his/her objection, such objector's objection shall be considered withdrawn and the invention registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection.
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the invention registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the Court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment/decision of the Court.
4. The time limit for the applicant to respond to the objection and the time limit for the objector to respond to the applicant's opinion as prescribed in points a and b clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal of the application.
Article 28. Amendment to invention registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention patent/utility solution, the applicant may take initiative or based on the notification of appraisal results to amend or supplement information and documents in the invention registration application.
2. A request for amendment to an invention registration application shall satisfy the following requirements:
a) In case of amendment to the application based on the notification of appraisal results of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the request for amendment shall be made in writing in response to the notification, clearly stating the contents of the amendment. In case the applicant takes initiative in amending the application, the request for amendment shall be made using Form No. 10 in Appendix I of this Circular, clearly stating the contents of the amendment.
b) In case of amendments related to the description or summary, the applicant shall submit part or all of the amended description or summary together with a detailed presentation of the amended content. The amendments to the description and summary of the invention registration application must not expand the scope (volume) already disclosed in the initial description and must not change the nature of the subject matter of the invention stated in the application;
c) In case of changes to the industrial property representative, the applicant shall submit a document declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representative and a new industrial property representative as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular;
d) In case of changes to the name, address, or country code of the applicant, the applicant shall submit certification documents (original copies or certified true copies) or legal documents (certified true copies) proving the changes (decision on name or address change; certificate of enterprise registration recording the name or address change, etc.), except where the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may proactively extract and use information already available in databases publicly announced by database management agencies. Where information cannot be extracted or is insufficiently or inaccurately extracted, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to individuals or organizations to supplement the application components and clearly state the reasons. The time required for requesting and supplementing the application shall not be included in the time limit for processing administrative procedures;
dd) In case of amendments to other information besides Points b, c, and d of this Clause, supporting documents proving the lawful amendments or the applicant’s commitment are required;
e) In case of amendments to information under Points c, d, and dd of this Clause, the applicant may request amendments with the same content related to multiple invention registration applications in one declaration of amendment requests;
g) The applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee for each content of amendment and each invention registration application and a fee for disclosure of information about amendments or supplements to the application if such amendments or supplements must be disclosed under Point a Clause 4 of this Article, except where such amendments or supplements are made to correct errors made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
3. In case an applicant proactively requests amendment of contents specified at Points b, c and d in Clause 2 of this Article before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant a protection title due to invalidation of the application, such request shall be processed during the process of application appraisal and the result of processing this request (acceptance or refusal to accept the request for amendment) shall be stated in the notice of application appraisal results or the result of processing the invention registration application.
4. Requests for amendments or additions not related to the description and summary submitted after the application has been published shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment or supplementation of an invention registration application satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:

a1) Issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendment or supplementation of the application;

a2) Publish the amended or supplemented content on the Industrial Property Official Gazette;
b) If the request for amendment or supplementation of the application fails to satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment or supplementation of the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide their opinions;
c/ If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to correct errors or his/her correction of errors is unsatisfactory, or does not have any objection or makes groundless objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment or supplementation of the application.
5. Requests for amendments to the description and summary submitted after the application has been published shall be processed as follows:
a) In case the applicant proactively amends the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice acknowledging the request for amendment and publish the amended content on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
b) The request for amendments shall be processed during the process of appraisal of the application and the result of processing this request (acceptance or rejection of the request for amendments) shall be stated in the notice of appraisal results or the results of processing the application for registration of such invention;
c) In case the request for amendments to the application relates to the nature of the invention stated in the description filed after the notice of intended issuance of the invention patent/utility solution is issued, the application for registration of the invention must be re-appraised within the time limit for re-appraisal prescribed in Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the applicant must pay a fee as prescribed.
Article 29. Recording changes of applicants of inventions
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or rejects the issuance of an invention patent/utility solution, the applicant may request the recording of changes of the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, or according to the decision of a competent authority.
2. A request for recording of changes of the applicant must meet the following requirements:
a) The request for recording of changes of the applicant shall comply with Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to transfer of the application, the applicant must submit documents showing the transfer of the application for registration of the invention (original or certified true copy), including the name and address of the transferring party and the transferee; the number of the transferred application or information sufficient to identify the application;
c) In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to inheritance, succession, or according to the decision of a competent authority, the applicant must submit documents proving the inheritance or succession of assets upon consolidation, merger, division, separation of legal entities, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entities under the same ownership, business format conversion, or according to the decision of the Court or other competent authorities, or inheritance due to abandonment of the right to register by another applicant;
d) The applicant shall pay a fee for examination of request for change of applicant and a fee for disclosure of information on change of applicant;
dd) A request for change of many applications filed by the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that an examination fee is paid according to regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for transfer.
3. A request for change of applicant shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for change of applicant satisfies the provisions of clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of change of applicant and publish it in the Industrial Property Gazette (except for cases where the amendment or supplementation of the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the protection title due to invalidation of the application);
b) If the request for change of applicant does not satisfy the provisions of clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to recognize the change of applicant and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection or unsatisfactory objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to recognize the change of applicant.
Article 30. Splitting of inventions registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention patent/utility solution, the applicant may split one or several technical solutions in the submitted invention registration application (initial application) into one or more new invention registration applications (split applications). The splitting of an application must comply with the following regulations:
a) The request for splitting of an application must be made in writing, clearly stating the number of the initial application and describing the subject matter of the protection request in the split application and the changes in the split application compared to the initial application;
b) The applicant must submit a new invention registration declaration for the separated technical solution and the description, summary and written authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative);
c) The applicant must pay the application fee and all fees and charges for procedures performed for the separated application outside the procedures already performed in the initial application without having to repeat them in the separated application and is not required to pay the charge for examination of requests for priority rights;
d) The subject matter of protection requested in the separated application must be the same as that in the initial application and must be different from the subject matter of protection requested in the initial application after being separated;
dd) The separated application must not expand the scope (volume) of protection beyond the content disclosed in the description and must not change the nature of the subject matter stated in the initial application.
3. The separated application shall bear a new application number and the filing date of the initial invention registration application and (priority dates) of the initial invention registration application (if any).
4. The separated application shall be examined for form and continue to be processed according to the unfinished procedures for the initial application. The separated application must be published as prescribed.
5. The initial application (after being separated) will continue to be processed according to the procedures being processed if there is no amendment to the initial application when submitting the separated application or will be processed according to the procedures for amendment of the application as prescribed in Article 28 of this Circular if there is an amendment to the initial application when submitting the separated application.
Article 31. Conversion of invention registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention patent or utility solution patent, the applicant may change the application for an invention patent into one for a utility solution patent or vice versa for the entire or part of the invention registration application.
2. The application for change of an invention registration application shall satisfy the following requirements:
a) The application for change (regarding the initial application) must be presented in writing, clearly stating the change request;
b) The applicant must submit a Declaration which specifies the number of the initial application, the filing fee for the change application as prescribed, and the fees and charges, and a written authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative);
c) In case of changing a part of an invention registration application, the applicant must carry out procedures for separating the application according to Article 30 of this Circular before requesting the change of the application.
3. After receiving a valid application for change, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the change application according to corresponding regulations but shall not re-carry out the procedures already carried out for the application before the change request.
The change application shall have a new application number and the submission date of the initial application or the priority date of the initial application.
4. In case the application for change from an application for a utility solution patent to an application for an invention patent is filed after the notice of content appraisal results regarding the intended grant of a protection title is issued, the application shall be re-appraised and the applicant must pay fees and charges as prescribed.
5. The initial application (after conversion) shall be considered as withdrawn at the time of filing the request for conversion of the application, except for cases where only a part of the registration application is converted in accordance with Point c Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 32. Withdrawal of invention registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an invention patent/utility solution, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the invention registration application.
2. The withdrawal of an invention registration application shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative through a written declaration. For applications filed through a representative, the authorization document must clearly state the authorization for application withdrawal or enclose a collection order specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 2 months from the date of receipt of a declaration of withdrawal of the invention registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application withdrawal request as follows:
a) Where the withdrawal request satisfies the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the application withdrawal request and terminate the processing of the corresponding application. The withdrawn invention registration application cannot be re-registered but can only be used as a basis for requesting priority rights.
b) Where the withdrawal request does not satisfy the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the application withdrawal request and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to correct errors or has corrected errors but fails to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the application withdrawal request.
Article 33. Re-examination of applications for registration of inventions
1. An application for registration of an invention shall be re-examined in the following cases:
a) There is a written opinion of a third party opposing the intended grant of an invention patent in the notice of content examination results, together with legitimate evidence proving that due to a force majeure event or objective obstacle, the opposition cannot be made within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The applicant requests amendments to the nature of the invention stated in the application after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam has notified the content examination results, which state the intended grant of a protection title according to the provisions of Point c Clause 5 Article 28 of this Circular;
c) The application for registration of the invention is requested to be converted in the case specified in Clause 4 Article 31 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of an application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of an application for registration of an invention shall comply with the provisions of Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complex cases involving many details requiring verification or expert opinions, the time limit for re-examination may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial examination prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The contents of the re-examination shall comply with the corresponding provisions in Section 3 Chapter II of this Circular;
c) For each case specified at Point a Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of an invention registration application may only be carried out once for the third person.
3. After obtaining the results of the re-examination, the invention registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For the cases specified at Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the results of the re-examination, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing according to the provisions of this Circular;
b) For the cases specified at Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article, the processing shall comply with the provisions of Article 25 of this Circular.
Section 5. PCT APPLICATIONS AND PROCESSING OF PCT APPLICATIONS
Article 34. PCT Applications

1. A PCT application includes a PCT application originating from Vietnam and a national-stage PCT application.
2. For a PCT application originating in Vietnam, the applicant may file it through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or directly with an International Office. The application filed directly with an International Office must be made in the language prescribed by the PCT Convention and must meet the requirements regarding form and content prescribed by the PCT Convention.
The application filed through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in English, each application shall be made in 1 copy and must meet the requirements regarding form and content prescribed by the PCT Convention and the applicant must pay an initial formal examination fee and fees and charges as prescribed by the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Convention and the law on fees and charges of the member countries designated in the PCT application.
3. For a PCT application designated or selected for Vietnam, to enter the national phase, the applicant must submit to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 31 months from the priority date (if the application requests the priority right) or from the date of filing the international application the following documents:
a) An invention registration declaration, made according to Form No. 01 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) A copy of the international application (in case the applicant requests entry into the national phase before the international application is published);
c) A Vietnamese translation of the description and summary in the international application (published version or original submitted initially if the application has not yet been published and the amended version and explanatory version of the amended part, if the international application is amended under Article 19 and/or Article 34.2(b) of the PCT Convention);
d) A written authorization (in case the application is filed through a representative).
4. The international application designated or selected for Vietnam shall have the submission date as the international application submission date.
Article 35. Processing of PCT applications originating from Vietnam filed through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam
1. After receiving a PCT application originating from Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Collect the fee for preliminary examination of the application form;
b) Examine the conformity of the filing of the application with security control requirements for inventions as prescribed in Article 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 14 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) Notify the fees as prescribed for the applicant to transfer them to the International Bureau and the international search agency under the provisions of the PCT Agreement;
d) Examine and process the application according to the provisions of the PCT Agreement;
dd) Transfer the application to the International Bureau and the international search agency in cases where the application meets the preliminary requirements regarding the form and fees as prescribed by national law, is filed within the prescribed time limit, and the subject matter of protection requested in the application is not a state secret;
e) Issue a notice of refusal to carry out further work in cases where the application does not meet the security control requirements for inventions as prescribed in Article 89a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 14 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. After the Vietnamese application originals have been transferred by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to the International Office, all transactions related to the application shall be directly conducted by the applicant with the International Office or the competent authority of a member country of the PCT Convention designated in the application under the provisions of the PCT Convention.
Article 36. Processing of applications for PCT filing in the national phase
The application for national stage processing shall be processed as follows:
1. Request for priority right in the PCT Application at the national stage shall be handled in accordance with the PCT Treaty and the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Treaty. In order to enjoy the priority right, the applicant must:
a) Reaffirm the priority right in the declaration;
b) Pay fees for appraisal of priority right requests;
c/ Submitting Vietnamese versions of documents already submitted to the International Office at the request of the Intellectual Property Office and necessary documents according to Rule 17.1(a) of the Regulation on implementation of the PCT Agreement.
2. The applicant may amend or supplement documents in the application. The amendment or supplementation of documents in the application must comply with the following regulations:
a) Article 28 and Article 41 of the PCT Treaty, Rules 52.1(b) and 78.1(b) of the Regulations on implementation of the PCT Treaty, Article 115 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 28 of this Circular;
b) The written authorization or transfer of the right to file an international application (if any) must be filed within 34 months from the priority date (if the application requests the priority right) or from the date of filing the international application;

c) Amendments and supplements submitted by applicants to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese.
3. The starting time for processing a PCT Application at the national stage is the first day of the 32nd month from the priority date (if the application requests the priority right) or from the date of filing the international application. If the applicant requests early processing of the PCT Application at the national stage, the PCT Application shall be processed ahead of schedule as prescribed in this Clause in accordance with Article 23.2 of the PCT Treaty.
4. A PCT Application at the national stage shall be subject to formal examination and substantive examination according to procedures applicable to invention registration applications filed under the national filing format and shall be published within 01 month from the date on which the application is considered valid, provided that such publication does not occur earlier than the date on which the application is published internationally under the PCT Treaty, except where the applicant requests early publication.
Chapter III
                         PROCESSING OF LAYOUT DESIGN REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Section 1. LAYOUT DESIGN REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 37. Layout design registration applications
An application for registration of a layout design shall include the following documents:
1. An application form for registration of a layout design shall be made using Form No. 6 under Appendix I attached hereto.
2. A set of photos or a set of layout design drawings (if the application is submitted in paper form, 4 sets are required).
3. Semiconductor integrated circuit designs shall be produced in a layout design and consist of 4 designs if the layout design has been commercially exploited.
4. Description of an integrated circuit manufactured under a layout design, including information on functions and structure of the semiconductor integrated circuit manufactured under the layout design in order to clarify nature of the layout design.
5. A letter of authorization (if the application is submitted by a representative).
6. Documentary evidence of registration rights, if the applicant inherits such rights from another person.
7. Other supporting documents (if any).
Article 38. Requirements for documents in an application for registration of layout design
1. Documents in an application for registration of a layout design shall meet specific requirements prescribed in clauses 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 of this Article and general requirements regarding format as follows:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (drawings, diagrams and tables may be presented horizontally) on one sheet of paper of A4 size (210 mm x 297 mm) with four margins, each margin being 20 mm wide, using the Times New Roman font, the font size shall not be smaller than 13, except for supporting documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b) Documents consisting of multiple pages shall have page numbers written in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents shall be typed or printed using an ink type that does not fade, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. Documents requiring signatures and seals, if containing at least 2 pages, shall bear signatures of each page or a seal on every two adjacent pages of the applicant/issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents bearing digital signatures. Before receiving the application, if detecting minor spelling errors, the applicant may correct them and shall append a signature (and affix a seal, if any) at the correction location. The signature and seal must not obscure the contents of the documents;
d) Documents in the application submitted electronically via the electronic filing system shall be in “.pdf” format; the name of the electronic document shall correspond to the name of the document type as prescribed; the individual signing documents in the application for registration of a layout design may use a digital signature to directly sign on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as color photos in the case of submission through the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application shall be consistent and common (not local languages, rare words, or self-created terms). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts, and spelling rules used in the application shall comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers used for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 5, 6, and 7 Article 37 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) Documents included in the application shall contain all mandatory information and ensure consistency.
2. The layout design registration application shall ensure consistency as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The declaration for layout design registration shall be completed with all required information in appropriate spaces. If the applicant requests confidentiality of certain information, such information shall be specified in the declaration.
4. The set of photos and drawings of layout designs shall fully represent the spatial structure of elements and their interconnections in semiconductor integrated circuits so that based on such set of photos and drawings, only one unique layout design can be identified. For the above-mentioned purposes, the set of photos and drawings of layout designs shall satisfy requirements prescribed in Clauses 5, 6, and 7 of this Article.
5. The set of photos and drawings of layout designs shall include at least one of the following documents:
a) Drawings created by a layout design machine for each layer of the integrated circuit;
b) Drawings or photos of photolithically patterned masks for manufacturing each layer of the integrated circuit;
c) Photographs of each layer of layout design shown in the integrated circuit.
6. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the applicant shall submit a set of photographs and a set of layout design drawings enclosed with supporting documents being objects carrying electronic data of part or all of such set of photographs and set of layout design drawings.
7. Form of set of photographs and set of layout design drawings:
a) The set of photographs and set of layout design drawings must include (a collection) of photographs and drawings separated for each layer of layout design, accompanied by symbols of each layer, size of the integrated circuit and magnification;
b) All photographs and layout design drawings must be on the same scale. For paper-based documents, each photograph and layout design drawing must be a magnified copy at least 20 times the size in the integrated circuit so that the eye can see the basic circuit design;
c) Each photograph and layout design drawing may be presented on an A4-sized paper sheet or a larger paper sheet, provided that it is folded into an A4-sized sheet;
d) Photographs and layout design drawings must be clear and sharp.
8. Samples of semi-conducting integrated circuits produced according to layout designs to be submitted must be the whole or part of the integrated circuits corresponding to the layout designs stated in the applications. If the integrated circuits produced according to layout designs are part inseparable from another product, the product enclosed with the application must be accompanied with documents indicating accurately the integrated circuit part produced according to such layout design. In case the layout design has been commercially exploited anywhere in the world before the application submission date, the applicant must submit samples of the first commercially exploited one.
9. Description of a semiconductor integrated circuit manufactured under a layout design shall include the following detailed information about such semiconductor integrated circuit which is required to be protected:
a) Name/symbol: a collection of letters and/or numbers used to distinguish this integrated circuit from other integrated circuits when being put on the market;
b) Description of basic functions of the integrated circuit (e.g., memory function or logic function or another function);
c) Description of basic structure of the integrated circuit (e.g., bipolar structure, MOS, BiMOS, optoelectronic structure or another structure);
d) Description of technology for manufacturing the integrated circuit (e.g., TTL technology, DTL technology, ECL technology, ITL technology, CMOS technology, NMOS technology, PMOS technology or another technology);
dd) Description of principal characteristics distinguishing it from other semiconductor integrated circuits on the market at the time of filing the application or the first commercial exploitation in the world, whichever comes earlier.
10. Documents requesting confidentiality protection shall be separated into a package of confidential documents and may be submitted in the following forms:
a) Microfilm or similar form for documents showing the size of the design on computer drawings;
b) Electronic data;
c) Drawings or photos of parts that are not visible, provided that the characteristics of the layout design are basically visible.
The maximum level of confidentiality for a layout design not yet commercially exploited is 50% of the surface area of each layer; for a layout design already commercially exploited, it is 2 layers out of 5 layers from top to bottom.
Article 39. Confidentiality of information in layout design registration applications The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and organizations and individuals involved in the process of receiving and processing layout design registration applications shall be obliged to keep confidential information in such applications that the applicant requests to be kept confidential in accordance with Clause 3 Article 38 of this Circular.

Article 40. Receipt of applications for registration of layout designs

For an electronic application, if the application is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall return a receipt note to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number, and results of verification of the list of documents of the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application submission system.

In case an application fails to meet all conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, it shall be rejected if it is submitted by post or electronically through the electronic application submission system, and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reasons for rejection). For an application rejected due to submission by post, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return documents of the application except for the original submitted for comparison.
Section 2. DETERMINATION OF THE FORMS OF REGISTERED LAYOUT DESIGN APPLICATIONS
Article 41. Formal examination of layout design registration applications
1. After being received, a layout design registration application shall be subject to formal examination as prescribed in Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property to assess the validity of the application and no substantive examination shall be conducted.
2. A layout design registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the registration right as prescribed in Article 86 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed against regulations in Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not eligible for protection as prescribed in Articles 8 and 69 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) There are grounds to confirm that the author of the layout design fails to satisfy the requirements specified in Clause 1, Article 122 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed in Article 10 of this Circular (including cases where the fees for filing the application, publication of the application, and appraising the application have not been fully paid);
e) The application does not meet the formal requirements prescribed in Article 38 of this Circular (the application contains errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding the presentation; translation of documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not certified as required, the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author, applicant, representative (if any), does not include the signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.
g) There is no valid authorization document (if the application is filed through a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 01 month from the date of notification by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming legal registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (documents proving inheritance rights, documents proving the transfer of application submission rights; assignment contracts or labor contracts), etc. After the above-mentioned time limit, if the applicant fails to submit additional documents to meet the requirements, the application will continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application.
4. Notification of results of appraising the layout design registration application shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notice of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the Certificate of registered layout design, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies in the application and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide their opinions or correct deficiencies;

b) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or provides unsatisfactory corrections within the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, or fails to provide any objection or provides an unjustifiable objection, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the Certificate of registered layout design and send it to the applicant;
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant has provided satisfactory corrections or justifiable explanations within the prescribed time limit, within 01 month from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant the Certificate of registered layout design.
5. The submission date of a layout design registration application is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam according to Clause 2 Article 40 of this Circular.
In case the application is submitted via the electronic application system after the administrative hour or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the submission date of the application according to this Clause is the next working day.
6. The Certificate of registered layout design made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued in both electronic and paper forms (in case the applicant requests a paper copy). In case the industrial property rights to a layout design are under joint ownership, the Certificate of registered layout design shall only be issued to the first person on the list of co-applicants.
A copy of the Certificate of registered layout design made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests a copy (specified in the declaration of layout design registration) for other co-owners.
Article 42. Publication of layout design registration applications
1. Layout design registration applications shall be published according to Clause 4 Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. From the date on which the applications are published, all persons may access detailed information about the nature of layout designs stated in the published applications, except for information that is kept confidential according to Clause 3 Article 38 and Article 39 of this Circular.
3. For confidential information about layout designs specified in Clause 3 Article 38 and Article 39 of this Circular, only agencies competent to carry out procedures for invalidating protection titles or carrying out procedures for handling acts of infringement upon rights to new layout designs are permitted to access such information.

a) If the request for amendment or addition to the application satisfies the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendment or addition to the application.
The contents of the amendment or addition to the application shall be published when the application for layout design registration is published according to the provisions of clause 4 Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 42 of this Circular.
b) If the request for amendment or addition to the application does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment or addition to the application, clearly stating the reasons therefor and setting a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opposing opinions. Upon expiration of the set time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, fails to provide opposing opinions or provides inadequate opposing opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment or addition to the application.
6. In case the applicant makes an amendment or addition to the application based on the notice of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam of appraisal results, the acceptance or refusal to accept the applicant's request for amendment or addition to the application shall be made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the notice of appraisal results or the corresponding decision during the processing of such application for layout design registration.
7. In case the request for amendment or addition to the application includes amendments or additions made by the applicant on his/her own initiative and amendments or additions to the application based on the notice of appraisal results, the request for amendment or addition shall be made into a Declaration according to the provisions of point a clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the request for amendment or addition in this case shall comply with the provisions of clause 5 of this Article.

a) The request for recording of changes in the applicant shall be made using Form No. 11 under Appendix I hereof;

d) The applicant must pay an appraisal fee for the request for recording of changes in the applicant and a fee for disclosure of information on the recording of changes in the applicant;
dd) Requests for recording of changes due to transfer of multiple applications of the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that the appraisal fee is paid according to regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for recording of transfer.

a) If the request for change of applicant satisfies the provisions of Clause 9 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of change of applicant and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette (except for cases where the amendment or supplementation of the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the Layout Design Registration Certificate due to invalidation of the application);
b) If the request for change of applicant does not satisfy the provisions of Clause 9 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the change of applicant and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection, or provides unjustified objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the change of applicant.

Article 44. Withdrawal of layout design registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a Layout Design Registration Certificate, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the layout design registration application.

For applications submitted through a representative, the authorization document must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or must be enclosed with a subpoena specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 02 months from the receipt of the declaration of withdrawal of the layout design registration application, the withdrawal request shall be processed as follows:
a) In case the withdrawal request satisfies the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the withdrawal request and terminate the processing of the corresponding application;
b) In case the withdrawal request does not satisfy the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are not satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the rejection of the application for withdrawal of the application.

Section 1. APPLICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGNS
Article 45. Applications for registration of industrial designs
An application for registration of an industrial design shall include the following documents:
1. Declaration for registration of industrial design, made according to Form No. 3 in Appendix I of this Circular.
2. Documents identifying the industrial design to be protected as a set of photos or drawings (four sets of photos or drawings shall be submitted if the application is filed in paper form).
3. Description of the industrial design.
4. Power of attorney (if the application is filed by a representative).
5. Documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits such rights from another person.
6. Documents proving priority rights if priority rights are claimed, including:
a) A copy of the first application or applications received by the receiving agency;
b) A document on transfer of priority rights if such rights are inherited from another person.
7. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 46. Requirements for documents in an industrial design registration application
1. Documents of an industrial design registration application shall meet respective specific requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article and general requirements on form as prescribed below:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm) with 20 mm margins on all sides, using the Times New Roman font size of 13 pt or larger, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b/ For each document other than a set of photos or drawings of industrial designs, if it comprises more than one page, each page must be inscribed with the Arabic ordinal number;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with fade-resistant ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents have 2 pages or more, each page must bear a signature or a seal affixed by the applicant or the issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if minor spelling errors are found, the applicant may correct them and must affix a signature (and seal, if applicable) at the correction location. The signature and seal must not obscure the content of the documents.
d) Documents of the application submitted electronically under the electronic filing system must be in the “.pdf” format, except for sets of photos, drawings of industrial designs, and documents that may be submitted in common image file formats (such as “.jpg” or “.png”, etc.); the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the paper document as prescribed; the individual signing the documents of the industrial design registration application may use a digital signature to sign directly on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as a color photo in the case of submission via the application.

dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not use local languages, rare words or made-up words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers used for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be made in Vietnamese, except for documents specified in Clauses 4, 5, 6, and 7 Article 45 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but shall be translated into Vietnamese upon request;
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure complete and consistent mandatory information.
2. An industrial design registration application shall ensure uniformity as prescribed in clauses 3 and 5 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In case an application for protection of an industrial design covers the whole product or part of a product accompanied by one or more variants of such industrial design, such variants must not be significantly different from the basic variant and each other.
In which, products are understood as follows:
a) Physical products are tangible objects such as articles, tools, equipment and means, which are produced by industrial or handicraft methods, have clear structures and functions;
b) “Non-physical product” means an object which does not exist in a tangible material form and is created by projecting or displaying a pattern on a surface or into the environment (including air), and has practical functions for operation, interaction, or performance of specific tasks;
c) Part of the product means the upper part of the product requested for protection by the applicant, including detachable and non-detachable parts;
d/ The detachable part of a product means a product component which can be independently circulated for assembly or combination into a product if it is registered independently and is also collectively called product, except for special regulations.
3. The declaration for registration of an industrial design shall be fully completed with information as required in appropriate places and meet the following requirements:
a) In the field "Name of the industrial design", the name of the product bearing the industrial design must be written in brief common words, not for advertising purposes, without commercial signs, annotations or instructions;
b) In the field "International classification of the industrial design", the international classification index of the industrial design for which protection is requested must comply with the International Classification of Industrial Designs (according to the Locarno Agreement on International Classification of Industrial Designs). If the applicant fails to classify or incorrectly classify, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall classify it and the applicant must pay a classification fee as prescribed;
c) In the field "Field of use of the industrial design", the specific field of use of the product bearing the industrial design must be clearly stated, including its purpose, utility and functions;
d) In the field "Type of industrial design", one of the two fields "Industrial design of the whole product" or "Industrial design of part of the product" must be selected. In case of industrial design of part of the product, the method of expression of the protection request shown on the set of photos/drawings must be clearly stated.
4. The set of photos/drawings of the industrial design must meet the following requirements:
a) The set of photos/drawings must fully express the design characteristics of the industrial design for which protection is requested to the extent that the person with average knowledge in the relevant field can determine such industrial design. The person with average knowledge in the relevant field as prescribed at this point is the person who has common practical skills and knows general and popular knowledge in the relevant field;
b) The set of photos/drawings must express the subject matter of the protection request in a consistent and accurate manner. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the set of photos/drawings must be printed or affixed on A4-sized paper without frames;
c) The photos or drawings must be clear and sharp; the background color of the photos or drawings must be consistent with and contrast with the product’s color bearing the industrial design; on the photos or drawings, only the product bearing the industrial design requested for protection shall be shown (not accompanied by other products), except for the cases specified in Points k and l of this Clause; they must not contain technical drawing instructions or explanations about the industrial design, except for brief and necessary instructions to indicate cross-sections or enlarged figures;
d) The photographing and drawing must clearly show the protected parts and unprotected parts of the product in one of the following ways:

d1) The protected area is represented by continuous lines while the unprotected area is represented by interrupted lines; d2) The protected area is represented by contrasting colors with the unprotected area;
dd) The photos and drawings must show the industrial designs in the same scale. In case the application is submitted in paper form, the product embodying the industrial design must have size of not more than 190 mm x 277 mm and its largest side length of not less than 120 mm;
e) The photos and drawings must show the industrial designs in the same orientation and be numbered sequentially as follows: three-dimensional views of the industrial designs, projections of the industrial designs from the front, back, right, left, top and bottom; the projections must be presented in the frontal view;
g) Photographs or projections identical or symmetrical with existing photographs or projections, photographs or projections of the bottom surface of products with large size and weight, photographs or projections of products with excessively thin thickness, photographs or projections of the surface with excessively thin thickness of industrial designs, photographs or projections of parts not visible in protection requests which are not required in the application, provided that such parts are clearly stated in the list of photographs and drawings included in the description;
h) For an industrial design of the whole product or part of a product which can be developed in the form of a plane (e.g. containers, packages, etc.), projections of such industrial design may be replaced by photos or drawings of the industrial design in the developed state;
i) Depending on the complexity of the industrial design, additional photos and perspective drawings from different angles, cross-sections, enlarged views, detached details or parts of the product, photos or drawings illustrating the installation and use of components on the complete product, which are intended to clarify the nature and design characteristics of the industrial design (which are not used for establishing industrial property rights to the industrial design of the component or part);
k) For the industrial design of the whole product, photos or drawings of each component or part of the product may be provided but only for illustrative purposes and not for establishing industrial property rights to the industrial design of such component or part;
l) The photos or drawings must show the industrial design in the selected state of use; photos or drawings showing other states may be provided to clarify the nature of the industrial design. In case the non-physical product's industrial design has a shape change or displacement, this effect can be shown by a series of sequential images, each image in the series showing a successive state of the change process;
m) For an application with multiple schemes, the basic scheme must be presented first. Each scheme of the industrial design must be presented with a complete set of photos and drawings as prescribed in this clause; the photos and drawings must be numbered to show the order of the corresponding scheme and the order of such photos and drawings in such scheme;
n) For a set of products, there must be a perspective drawing of the entire set of products and a set of photos and drawings of each product in the set according to the provisions of this clause.
5. The description of an industrial design must include the following contents:
a) List all basic design features expressing the nature of the industrial design and conforming to design features shown in the set of photos or drawings as follows:

a1) Design features of the industrial design eligible for protection shall be listed including: three-dimensional features, lines, correlation between three-dimensional features and/or lines, color features (if any), shape, layout, proportion, display order and visual effects of graphic elements on non-physical products;

a2) For products with different use states (e.g., products with a lid or can be folded, etc.), list basic design features of the products in different states; a3) If an industrial design comprises different schemes, it is required to specify distinctive features of each scheme in comparison with the basic scheme;

a4) If an industrial design is the set of products, it is required to list basic design features of each product in the set;
b) The closest similar industrial designs (if any): clearly stating whether or not there are similar industrial designs, and if so, specifying the industrial design most dissimilar to the industrial design of the same type of product stated in the application, which is widely known before the filing date or the priority date (if the application requests priority rights), indicating the source of information disclosing such the closest similar industrial design;
c) List of photos or drawings: list three-dimensional perspective photos, projections, cross-sections, etc. of the industrial design in the order of their numbers.

Section 2. APPLICATION REVIEW FOR INDUSTRIAL DESIGN REGISTRATION
Article 48. Application review for industrial design registration
1. The application review for industrial design registration according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the examination of compliance with regulations on form of the application, thereby making a conclusion on whether the application is considered valid.
2. An industrial design registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have registration rights as prescribed in Article 86 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed against regulations in Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not eligible for protection as prescribed in Articles 8 and 64 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) There are grounds to confirm that the author of the industrial design fails to satisfy the requirements prescribed in Clause 1, Article 122 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed in Article 10 of this Circular (including cases where the fees for filing the application, publication of the application, examination of the application, and information lookup for examination have not been fully paid);
e) The application fails to meet the formal requirements prescribed in Article 46 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding presentation; failing to classify or incorrectly classifying the industrial design without paying classification fees; lacking a translation of documents proving priority rights (if required), a translation of documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not properly certified, the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the author, the applicant, the representative, lacks signatures and/or seals (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.;
g) There is no valid authorization document (if the application is filed through a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification. The above-mentioned documents may be documents confirming the lawful registration rights if the applicant inherits the submission rights from another person (documents proving inheritance rights, documents proving the transfer of application submission rights; assignment contracts or labor contracts, etc.). After the above-mentioned time limit, if the applicant fails to submit additional documents satisfying requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents in the application.

4. Notification of the results of the formal examination shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notification of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant an industrial design patent, reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application (including the priority right request) and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to provide opinions or correct deficiencies;
b) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to meet requirements after the time limit prescribed at Point a of this Clause expires or does not have any objection or has an objection but it is unconvincing, within 15 working days from the expiration of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant an industrial design patent and send it to the applicant;
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article or the applicant corrects deficiencies and meets requirements or has a convincing objection within the prescribed time limit, the application shall be considered valid in terms of form and be transferred for publication and substantive appraisal according to regulations. In case the priority right request is not accepted, the application shall still be considered valid and be transferred for publication according to regulations.
5. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its web portal the list of industrial design registration applications transferred for publication, specifying the application number, filing date and date of publication of each application.
6. The filing date of an industrial design registration application is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam according to Clause 2 Article 47 hereof.
In case the application is filed via the electronic filing system after the administrative hours or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the filing date specified in this Clause shall be the next working day.
7. The priority date of an industrial design registration application shall be determined as follows:
a) If the application does not contain a request for priority right or although the application contains a request for priority right, it is not approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the application shall be considered having no priority date;
b) If the application contains a request for priority right, the priority date (or priority dates) is the date stated in the above-mentioned request and approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) The determination of the priority date according to the request for priority right based on the first application submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles specified in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
8. In case the applicant has a request for priority right but at the time of filing the industrial design registration application there is no document proving the priority right, the examination of the application form shall start from the time the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the supplementary documents or end when the 3-month period specified in Clause 4 Article 47 hereof expires.
Article 49. Publication of industrial design registration applications
1. A valid industrial design registration application shall be published as follows:
a) The application shall be published within 01 month from the date on which it is considered valid if the applicant does not request late publication or requests late publication but the application is considered valid after the time limit for requesting late publication;
b) In case the applicant requests late publication and the application is considered valid before the time limit for requesting late publication, the application shall be published in the following month of the end month of the time limit for requesting late publication.
2. Contents of an industrial design registration application published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette include the following information: Applicant, representative (if any); Application number, filing date, priority date (if any); name and nationality of the author of the industrial design; information about the origin of the application (split application, initial application number of split application, etc.); set of photos or drawings of the industrial design; number of schemes/products requested for protection; international classification of the industrial design; and other information (if any).
Section 3. CONTENT APPRAISAL OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGN REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 50. Content appraisal of industrial design registration applications
1. Content appraisal of industrial design registration applications according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application according to the protection conditions and determination of the corresponding protection scope according to this Article.
2. Assessment of the grant eligibility of industrial design registration applications includes:
a) Assess the conformity of subject matters stated in applications for registration of industrial designs with the requirements for grant of industrial design patents according to Article 52 of this Circular;
b) Assess subject matters stated in applications for registration of industrial designs according to each protection condition specified in Articles 54, 55 and 56 of this Circular. The assessment shall be carried out separately for each product type (if the application refers to a set of products); in case it refers to multiple solutions, each solution shall be assessed separately, starting from the basic solution (the first solution stated in the application);
c) Assess the first-to-file principle according to Article 57 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles according to Point b Clause 2 of this Article shall be completed after the industrial design subject matter stated in the application has been assessed with respect to all protection conditions and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter fails to satisfy or satisfies the protection conditions, specifically:
a) There is a reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy one or several or all protection conditions; or b) There is no reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Before issuing the notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of industrial design patents according to Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 58 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine the first-to-file principle according to Article 57 of this Circular.
5. Based on the results of the assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles prescribed in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding scope of protection.
6. The notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of industrial design patents prescribed in Points a and d2 Clause 3 Article 58 of this Circular shall be issued for the following applications for registration of industrial designs:
a) The application does not fall into the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application has the earliest submission date or priority date among all applications for registration of industrial designs falling into the case specified in Clause 1, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) The application is made under an agreement specified in Clause 3, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. An application for registration of industrial design which does not fall into the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article shall be handled as follows:
a) It shall be rejected from grant of an industrial design patent because it fails to satisfy the first-to-file rule if it has the earliest submission date or priority date among all applications for registration of industrial designs; or b) It shall be considered as having the earliest submission date or priority date and handled as the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article if all other applications for registration of industrial designs with earlier submission dates or priority dates are rejected from grant of industrial design patents, withdrawn or considered as withdrawn.
8. During the process of substantive examination of an application, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations about the contents of the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application in order to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to remedy the deficiencies.
Article 51. Minimum information sources
1. The purpose of information lookup is to search the mandatory minimum information sources for industrial designs identical or similar to those stated in the application. Mandatory minimum information sources used during the process of substantive examination of an application include the following documents:
a) Industrial design registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam with an application publication date earlier than the submission date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights);
b) Industrial design registration applications and protection titles of industrial designs published by other organizations or countries within 25 years before the application submission date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal (if the application is eligible for priority rights) and stored in the existing industrial design database at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) Other information related to industrial designs collected and stored by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
d) Industrial design registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, international industrial design registration applications designating Vietnam and having a submission date or priority date (if the application is eligible for priority rights) earlier than or on the same day as the submission date or priority date of the application currently under appraisal (used to verify the first-to-submit principle prescribed in Article 57 of this Circular).
2. Where necessary and feasible, the information may be searched further beyond the minimum compulsory information sources specified in Clause 1 of this Article, including national databases on inventions and marks and other accessible information sources.

3. The search results shall be presented in the search report, specifying the field searched, the scope of search, and the search results within that scope (including statistics and identification of the matching control industrial designs, the source of information, and the publication date of the corresponding information) and the full name of the person preparing the report (the searcher).
In this context, "control industrial design" refers to an industrial design identical or similar to the industrial design stated in the application, which is compared with the industrial design stated in the application when assessing novelty and creativity.
Article 52. Assessment of conformity of subject matters of industrial design registration applications
1. Subject matters stated in applications for registration of industrial designs shall be deemed incompatible with industrial design patents if such subject matters are not the external appearance of the whole product or part of the product.
External appearance of the whole product or part of the product means a collection of shaping features (shapes, lines, colors or combination thereof) of the whole product or part of the product which can be seen during the utilization of the product’s utility. In this context, utilization of the product’s utility means putting such products into use according to their properties and utilities, excluding maintenance, repair or servicing of the products. For an industrial design of a separate part registered independently, the visibility assessment shall consider the product assembled from such part.
2. Subject matters stated in applications under Clause 1 of this Article include:
a/ External appearance of the whole product or part of the product which is required by its technical characteristics;
b/ External appearance of civil or industrial construction works other than external appearance of separate modules or units which may be used independently or assembled together to form construction works such as shops, kiosks, mobile houses or similar products.
Article 53. Assessment of the degree of distinctness of industrial designs The assessment of the degree of distinctness between two industrial designs shall comply with the following provisions:
1. Two industrial designs shall be regarded as identical when they are used for products of the same type and have the same collection of fundamental and non-fundamental shaping features.
2. Two industrial designs shall be regarded as insignificantly different from each other when they are used for products of the same type and have the same collection of fundamental shaping features.
3. Two industrial designs shall be regarded as similar when they are used for products of the same type and have at least one fundamental shaping feature which is identical or insignificantly different from each other.
4. Two industrial designs among similar industrial designs shall be regarded as the closest to each other when they have the maximum number of fundamental shaping features which are identical or insignificantly different from each other compared with all other similar industrial designs.
5. Two industrial designs shall be regarded as significantly different from each other when they are used for products of different types or products of the same type but have at least one fundamentally different shaping feature.
6. Shaping features of an industrial design are construed as follows:
a) Shaping features of an industrial design are elements expressed in form of lines, three-dimensional figures, colors, position correlation or size correlation of the protection requirement on the whole product, shape, layout arrangement, proportion correlation, display order and visual effect of graphic elements on non-physical products so that when combined with other features (signs), they form such industrial design;
b) Fundamental shaping features are shaping features which can be easily recognized/remembered, necessary and sufficient to determine the nature of the industrial design and distinguish the industrial design from another industrial design used for products of the same type.
Products of the same type are products with identical or similar use purposes or functions. Products assembled from multiple parts and parts used for assembly into such products are products of different types.
c) The following elements shall not be considered fundamental design features of an industrial design:

c1) Shapes and lines determined by the technical functions of the product (e.g., the flat shape of a data recording disc determined by the relative movement between the disc and the disc reader, etc.);

c2) Elements whose presence in the set of signs is insufficient to cause aesthetic impression (the appearance of the product remains unchanged when such elements are present or absent; for example, a change in a familiar shape or line but such change is insufficient to be recognized, so the changed shape or line is still only recognized as the old one);

c3) Materials used for manufacturing the product; c4) Information displayed inside non-physical products (such as documents, data, etc.); signs affixed or stuck on products only for the purpose of providing information and instructions on origin, characteristics, composition, uses, usage methods, etc. of such products; for example, information on goods labels (such as manufacturers, commercial indications, origin, barcodes, etc.), trademarks, geographical indications, etc.; c5) Size of products, except for changes in decorative sizes of fabric samples and similar materials; c6) Invisible design features during the use of the products. For an industrial design of a detachable part registered independently, the identification of invisible shaping characteristics shall be considered on products assembled from such part; c) Other elements failing to satisfy the conditions specified at Point b of this Clause.
Article 54. Assessment of novelty
1. In order to evaluate the novelty of an industrial design stated in an application, it is required to compare a collection of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial design with a collection of fundamental design characteristics of each control industrial design found during the information search.
2. An industrial design stated in an application shall be regarded as new if:
a) The industrial design is not found in the mandatory information source; or b) Although the industrial design is found in the mandatory information source, it is considered significantly different from the industrial designs in the application; or c) The industrial design is the one mentioned in the application that has been disclosed or presented and falls under the cases specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 65 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 55. Assessment of creativity
1. To assess the creativity of an industrial design mentioned in an application, it is necessary to compare a collection of fundamental design characteristics of such industrial design with a collection of fundamental design characteristics of the industrial designs found during the information search.
2. An industrial design mentioned in an application shall be considered creative if it does not fall into the following cases:
a) It is merely a combination of known design characteristics (design characteristics that have been publicly disclosed and arranged or assembled in a simple manner such as replacement, change of position, increase or decrease of quantity, etc.);
b) It is a copy/mockery of part or the whole of the inherent natural appearance of trees, fruits, animals, etc., or the appearance of geometric shapes (circle, ellipse, triangle, square, rectangle, regular polygon, prism with cross-section being one of the above shapes, etc.);
c) It is merely a copy/mockery of part or the whole of the appearance of well-known products or works in Vietnam or around the world;
d) It is a mockery of an industrial design in another field if such mockery has been recognized in reality (for example: toy mocks of cars, motorcycles, etc.).
Article 56. Assessment of industrial application capability
1. An industrial design stated in an application shall be deemed to have industrial application capability if, based on information about such industrial design presented in the application, a person with average knowledge in the relevant field could use it as a model to create a batch of products with the external appearance or part of the external appearance being such industrial design, by manufacturing physical products by industrial or handicraft methods or by uniformly recreating non-physical products.
2. In the following cases, subject matters stated in applications shall be deemed not to have industrial application capability:
a) Subject matters stated in applications are the appearance of the whole product or part of the product existing in an undefined state (products in forms, liquids, interfaces with images displayed randomly, etc.);
b) The whole product or part of the product can only have the appearance like the subject matter stated in the application by using special skills or it is impossible to repeatedly manufacture the whole product or part of the product with the appearance like the subject matter stated in the application;
c) Other cases for valid reasons.
Article 57. Assessment of the first-to-file principle
For industrial designs whose registration applications have been concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing content appraisal results notice with the intention to grant an industrial design patent according to the provisions at Point a, Clause 2, Article 58 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine to ensure the first-to-file principle prescribed in Clauses 1 and 3, Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:
1. In order to check the first-to-file principle, information in compulsory sources prescribed at Point d, Clause 1, Article 51 of this Circular must be searched.
2. The search is to find industrial design registration applications for products of the same type identical or insignificantly different from each other or to find industrial design registration applications for products containing parts with industrial designs identical or insignificantly different from registered industrial designs or to find industrial design registration applications for products including parts with external appearance identical or insignificantly different from registered industrial designs and determine the application with the earliest filing date or priority date.
3. If there are many applications falling into the cases prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, an industrial design patent may only be granted to a valid application with the earliest filing date or priority date among applications satisfying conditions for industrial design patent grant.
4. Among applications falling into the cases prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article and registering for products of the same type, if there are many applications with the same earliest filing date or priority date, an industrial design patent may only be granted to one application out of such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all such applications shall be rejected from industrial design patent grant.
Article 58. Grant and rejection of industrial design patents
1. An industrial design registration application shall be rejected from grant of an industrial design patent if it falls into the cases prescribed at Points a, b, c, d and dd, Clause 1 and Clause 1b, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. An industrial design registration application shall be granted an industrial design patent when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For industrial design registration applications falling into the cases prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clause 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the protection title and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on issuance of the protection title, and the registration fee for the decision on issuance of the protection title;
b) If the subject matter stated in the industrial design registration application does not satisfy the protection conditions or the application falls into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd Clause 1, Clause 1b Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to issue the protection title, all reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application, and may provide guidance on amendments to the scope (quantity) of protection and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide objections or correct deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or does not provide objections within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the protection title;
d) In case the applicant rectifies deficiencies or provides objections within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the rectification document or the objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the examination as follows:

d1) If the rectification is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the protection title;

d2) If the rectification is satisfactory or the objection is justifiable given the content examination results and/or the amended application is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content examination results. specifying the intended grant of a title of protection and fixing a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to pay the fee for the grant of a title of protection, the fee for publication of the decision on the grant of a title of protection, and the registration fee for the decision on the grant of a title of protection;
dd) If the applicant fails to fully pay the fee for issuance of a protection title, the fee for publication of the decision on issuance of a protection title or the registration fee for issuance of a protection title within the time limit specified at Points a and d2 of this Clause, then within 15 working days from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to issue the protection title;
e) If the applicant fully and punctually pays all fees and charges within the time limit specified at Points a and d2 of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on grant of a protection title within 1 month from the date of full payment of fees and charges.
4. The decision on grant of an industrial design patent shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days after its issuance. Published contents shall include information stated in the corresponding decision (including decision number and date) and other contents specified in Clause 2, Article 49 of this Circular.
5. Industrial design patents made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued as electronic copies and paper copies (if the applicant requests a paper copy). In case industrial property rights to an industrial design are under joint ownership, the industrial design patent shall only be granted to the first person on the list of joint applicants.
A copy of an industrial design patent made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests a copy (specified in the declaration for registration of industrial design) for other co-owners.
Section 4. PROCESSING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO INDUSTRIAL DESIGN REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 59. Processing of objections to industrial design registration applications
1. Within 3 months from the date on which an industrial design registration application is published, any third party has the right to object to the grant of an industrial design patent for such industrial design registration application and shall pay fees and charges.
The objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese enclosed with documents or with sources of information for verification. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. In case the objection to the industrial design registration application satisfies the provisions of clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process the objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 1 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objection to the applicant and impose a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to respond in writing.
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of such feedback and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) Based on information, evidence, arguments provided by the parties, the dialogue results (if any) as prescribed in points a, b, and c of this clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the objector of the results of processing the objection together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application, except for the case specified in point dd of this clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of the suspension of processing the objection regarding the withdrawal of the industrial design registration application according to Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 1 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification of acceptance of application withdrawal.
3. In cases where the objection of the objector is related to the right to register industrial designs, the objection shall be processed as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector to file a lawsuit petition to a competent court in accordance with the law on civil procedures and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to submit a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit;
b) Upon expiration of the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if the objector fails to submit a copy of the notice of acceptance of the lawsuit, the objection shall be considered withdrawn, and the industrial design registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the industrial design registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the court.
4. The time limit for the applicant to provide feedback on the objection and the time limit for the objector to respond to the applicant's feedback as prescribed in points a and b clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal of the application.
Article 60. Amendment to industrial design registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may proactively amend or supplement documents in the industrial design registration application or based on the notification of appraisal results.
2. Requests for amendment to industrial design registration applications include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment to the application by the applicant, the request for amendment must be made using Form No. 10 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of amendment to the application based on the notification of appraisal results of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the amendment shall be made in a written response clearly stating the amended contents;
c) The applicant may request amendment with the same content related to multiple industrial design registration applications in one declaration or one written request for amendment;
d) In case of amendments related to the set of photos or drawings or description, the applicant shall submit the set of drawings or photos (4 sets for paper applications) or the amended description enclosed with detailed presentation of the amended content compared to the initial submitted documents;
dd) In case of amendments to name, address, or country code of the applicant, the applicant shall submit certification documents (original or certified true copies) or legal documents (certified true copies) proving the changes (decision on change of name, address; certificate of enterprise registration recording the changes in name, address, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for proactively extracting and using information available in databases publicly announced by database management agencies to replace components of the application for handling administrative procedures. In cases where information cannot be extracted or the extracted information is incomplete or inaccurate, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to individuals or organizations to supplement the application components and clearly state the reasons. The time for requesting and supplementing the application is not included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
e) In case of amendments to industrial property representatives, the applicant shall submit a document declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representative and change of the new industrial property representative as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular;
3. The applicant shall pay an appraisal fee for the request for amendments to each amendment and a fee for disclosure of information about amendments to the application if the amended content must be disclosed as prescribed in Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except for cases where the amendments are made to correct errors caused by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Amendments to industrial design registration applications must not expand the scope (volume) of protection already disclosed in the set of photos, drawings, and description stated in the application and must not alter the nature of the industrial design subject matter stated in the application.
5. In case of amendments to the application initiated by the applicant, the request for amendments shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment or supplementation of an application satisfies the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of such request (except where the amendment or supplementation is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant an industrial design patent due to the invalidity of the application in terms of form).
The contents of the amendment or supplementation shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette in case the request for amendment or supplementation of information related to the application that is valid in terms of form includes the name, nationality and address of the author of the industrial design, the set of photos or drawings of the industrial design;
b) If the request for amendment or supplementation of an application does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept such request, clearly stating the reasons therefor and setting a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opposing opinions. Upon expiration of the prescribed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, fails to provide opposing opinions or provides inadequate opposing opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment or supplementation of the application;
c) In case the request for amendment or supplementation of an application relates to the nature of the industrial design stated in the set of photos, drawings or description filed after receiving a notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of an industrial design patent, the application shall be re-appraised and the applicant shall pay a fee as prescribed.
6. In case the applicant makes amendments or supplements to the application based on the notice of appraisal results issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall accept or refuse to accept the request for amendment or supplementation of the application in the notice of appraisal results or the corresponding decision during the processing of the application for registration of such industrial design.
7. In case an application for amendment or supplementation includes amendments or supplements made by the applicant and amendments or supplements to the application based on notification of appraisal results, such application shall be made into an Application Form as prescribed in Point a Clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application for amendment or supplementation in this case shall comply with the provisions of Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 61. Recording of changes of applicants for registration of industrial designs
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may request recording of changes of the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, adoption or decision of a competent authority.
2. A request for recording of changes of the applicant shall meet the following requirements:
a) The request for recording of changes of the applicant shall be made using Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to transfer of the application, the applicant shall submit documents showing the transfer of the industrial design registration application (original or certified true copy), including the name and address of the transferor and transferee; number of transferred application or information sufficient to identify the application;
c) In case of recording of changes of the applicant due to inheritance, adoption or decision of a competent authority, the applicant shall submit documents proving the inheritance or adoption of assets upon consolidation, merger, division or separation of juridical persons, joint venture, association, establishment of new juridical person under the same ownership, business format conversion or decision of a court or other competent authority or inheritance due to abandonment of the right to register by another applicant;
d) The applicant shall pay an appraisal fee for the request for recording of changes of the applicant and a fee for disclosure of information on recording of changes of the applicant;
dd) The request for recording of changes due to transfer of many applications filed by the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that an appraisal fee is paid according to regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for recording of transfer.
3. Requests for recording of changes of applicants shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for recording of changes of the applicant satisfies the provisions of clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice on recording of changes of the applicant and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette (except for cases where the amendment or supplementation of the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant the industrial design patent because the application is invalid in terms of form);
b) If the request for recording of changes of the applicant does not satisfy the provisions of clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice intending to refuse to record the changes of the applicant and set a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the set time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements in correcting errors, does not provide objections or provides unjustified objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice refusing to record the changes of the applicant.
Article 62. Splitting of industrial design registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may take the initiative or based on the notification of the results of appraisal by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to split one or several industrial designs in the submitted industrial design registration application (initial application) into one or more new industrial design registration applications (split applications).
2. The applicant shall submit a new declaration for registration of the split industrial design. The split application shall bear a new application number and the submission date of the initial industrial design registration application or the priority dates of the initial industrial design registration application (if any).
The applicant shall pay an application fee and all charges and fees for procedures performed for the separate application outside the procedures already performed in the initial application without having to perform again in the separate application and is not required to pay a charge for examination of the request for priority right, except for cases where the application separation is required because the initial application does not meet the requirement on the uniformity of the application.
3. For the initial application, the applicant shall perform the amendment and supplementation of the application according to the provisions of Article 60 of this Circular.
4. Separate application and initial application shall be processed as follows:
a) The separate application shall be appraised in terms of form and continue to be processed according to the unfinished procedures for the initial application. The separate application shall be published according to regulations.

b) The initial application shall continue to be processed according to the provisions of Article 60 of this Circular.
Article 63. Withdrawal of applications for registration of industrial designs
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue an industrial design patent, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the application for registration of industrial design.
2. The withdrawal of the application for registration of industrial design shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative through a written declaration. For applications filed through representatives, the authorization document must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or must be enclosed with a command specifying the number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 2 months from the date of receipt of a declaration on withdrawal of an application for registration of an industrial design or a request for withdrawal of an application, the following shall be made:
a) In case of a request for withdrawal of an application satisfying the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of such withdrawal and terminate the processing of the application. The withdrawn industrial design registration application cannot be re-registered but may only be used as a basis for requesting priority rights.
b) In case the withdrawal request fails to satisfy the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedges deficiencies but still fails to meet requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal request rejection.
Article 64. Re-examination of applications for registration of industrial designs
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the re-examination of an application for registration of an industrial design in the following cases:
a/ Having a written opinion of a third party opposing the intended grant of an industrial design patent in the notice of content appraisal results, together with legitimate evidence proving that due to force majeure events or objective obstacles, the application cannot be opposed within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The applicant for amendment to the application after notification of the results of the content appraisal, which states the intended issuance of a protection title as prescribed in Point c Clause 5 Article 60 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of an application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of an industrial design registration application shall comply with Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complicated cases involving many details requiring verification or expert opinions, the time limit for re-examination may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial examination prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The contents of re-examination shall comply with corresponding provisions in Section 3 Chapter IV of this Circular;
c) For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of an industrial design registration application may only be carried out once by a third party.
3. After obtaining the results of re-examination, the industrial design registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For the case specified at Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-examination results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application in accordance with this Circular;
b) For the case specified at Point b Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application in accordance with Article 58 of this Circular.
Section 5. LAWSUITS AND PROCESSING OF LAWSUITS
Article 65. La Haye letters

1. A La Haye letter includes a La Haye letter addressed to Vietnam and a La Haye letter originating in Vietnam.
2. For a La Hay formula application of Vietnamese origin, the applicant may file it through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or directly to the International Bureau. An application filed with the International Bureau must be made in the language prescribed in the Hague Agreement and must satisfy the formal and substantive requirements prescribed in the Hague Agreement.
3. The Hague formula applications filed through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in English (each application shall be made into 02 copies for paper applications) and must satisfy the format and content requirements specified in the Hague formula. Applicants shall pay international application transfer fees, fees and charges according to the Hague formula and the laws on fees and charges of designated member countries.

4. For a Hague-Addis Ababa Convention application with Vietnam's designation, the filing date shall be the international registration date.
Article 66. Processing of applications Originates in Vietnam and filed through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam
1. In case of an application for registration of a La Hay domain name originating from Vietnam and filed through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Collect international application transfer fees;
b) Notify the applicant of fees payable directly to the International Office in accordance with the Hague Agreement within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the application;
c) Conduct preliminary examination of the form of the application within 12 working days from the date of receipt of the application;
d) In case the application contains errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of such errors and impose a time limit of 10 working days from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify them;
dd) Transfer the Hague Application originating from Vietnam to the International Office within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application.
2. The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the Hague Application originating from Vietnam shall be considered as the date of filing the international registration of industrial designs in case the International Office receives such application within 1 month from the date indicated on the seal of receipt of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
3. After the Hague Application originating from Vietnam has been filed with the International Office, all transactions related to the application shall be conducted directly by the applicant with the International Office or the competent authority of a member country of the Hague Agreement designated in the application in accordance with the Hague Agreement.
Article 67. Processing of Hague Applications with Vietnamese Designation
After receiving the notice from the International Office, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the Hague Application with Vietnamese designation in accordance with the following regulations:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct a substantive examination of an application like that for registration of an industrial design filed under the national filing format, except for the cases specified in clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 9 of this Article. Within 6 months from the date on which the application is published by the International Office, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conclude on the possibility of protection of the industrial design stated in the application.
2. In case the industrial design stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions prescribed by Vietnamese law and the application has no deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Before the expiration of the 6-month period specified in clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to accept the protection of the internationally registered industrial design stated in the application, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of Internationally Registered Industrial Designs) and send it to the International Office for announcement of acceptance of protection for the internationally registered industrial design according to the form of the International Office;
b) Publish the decision in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 1 month from the date of issuance of the decision.
3. In case the industrial design does not satisfy the protection conditions or the application has deficiencies (lack of photos/drawings making the set of photos/drawings fail to fully disclose the design features of the industrial design or the internationally registered industrial design fails to satisfy the declarations of Vietnam or there are information that need to be verified, etc.), before the expiration of the 6-month period specified in clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal according to the form of the International Office, clearly stating the reasons for refusal and send such notice to the International Office.
4. For some industrial designs that are registered but fail to satisfy the protection conditions or the application has deficiencies (lack of photos/drawings making the set of photos/drawings fail to fully disclose the design features of the industrial design or the internationally registered industrial design fails to satisfy the declarations of Vietnam or there are information that need to be verified, etc.), before the expiration of the 6-month period specified in clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Issue a notice of refusal for an industrial design which fails to satisfy the protection conditions or has errors according to the form of the International Office, clearly stating the reasons for refusal and send such notice to the International Office;
b) Issue a decision to accept protection for industrial designs which satisfy the protection conditions and have no errors, record in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part of Industrial Designs Registered Internally) and send to the International Office a Declaration of acceptance of protection for industrial designs registered internally according to the form of the International Office, clearly stating the industrial designs accepted for protection;
c) Publish the decision in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 01 month from the date of issuance of the decision.

5. Within 03 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office issues the notice of refusal as prescribed in clauses 3 and 4 of this Article, the applicant may correct errors or oppose the opinion of the Intellectual Property Office. The correction of errors or opposition of the refusal stated in the notice shall be carried out according to the procedures applicable to applications for registration of industrial designs filed under the national filing form, including regulations on the method of filing.
In case the Hague Application is intended to be refused due to failure to meet the requirements on the uniformity of the application as prescribed in clauses 3 and 5 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the applicant may remedy the above-mentioned errors by requesting the separation of one or several industrial designs in the international registration mentioned above into one or more new applications. The Intellectual Property Office shall separate the application and issue decisions and notices for the new application independently from the original application.
6. In case the applicant successfully corrects errors and/or has a justifiable opinion within the time limit prescribed in clause 5 of this Article, within 02 months, the Intellectual Property Office shall carry out the following procedures:
a) To issue a decision to accept protection of an internationally registered industrial design if it satisfies the protection conditions, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (Part on Internationally Registered Industrial Designs) and send it to the International Office for announcement of acceptance of protection of the internationally registered industrial design after refusal using the form of the International Office, clearly stating the industrial design accepted for protection;
b) To publish the decision in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 01 month from the date of issuance of the decision.
7. In case the time limit specified in Clause 5 of this Article expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements, has no objection or has an unjustifiable objection to the industrial designs notified of refusal, within 15 working days from the expiration of the above time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to protect such internationally registered industrial designs.
8. In case the time limit of 03 months from the date on which the Hague-Paris Office announces the application with Vietnamese designation expires but the applicant fails to submit documents proving priority rights or submits documents proving priority rights but they are not approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the application shall be considered as having no request for priority right.
9. The procedures for complaint and settlement of complaints against decisions specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article shall be carried out like those applicable to applications for registration of industrial designs filed under the national filing form. In case after receiving the results of complaint settlement, some or all industrial designs are refused in the decisions to refuse to accept protection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send them to the International Office for announcement of acceptance of protection after refusal using the form of the International Office, clearly stating the industrial designs accepted for protection.
10. In case a third party has opinions about the Hague-Paris application with Vietnamese designation before the date of issuance of the decision to accept protection, opinions of the third party shall be regarded as a reference source for the processing of the Hague-Paris application with Vietnamese designation.
11. The contents of disclosure prescribed at Point b Clause 2, Point c Clause 4 and Point b Clause 6 of this Article include basic information specified in the corresponding decision (including decision number and date) and other information as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 49 of this Circular.
Chapter V
                            HANDLING OF MARK REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Section 1. MARK REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 68. Mark registration applications
A mark registration application includes the following documents:
1. Declaration for mark registration, made according to Form No. 04 in Appendix I of this Circular.
2. Mark samples enclosed therewith (5 samples shall be enclosed for paper applications).
3. Regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks for marks registered as collective marks or certification marks.
4. Authorization document (if the application is filed by a representative).
5. Documents proving the mark registration right if the applicant inherits the registration right from another person.
6. Documents proving the permission for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing geographical locations or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam according to Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. Documents proving the priority right if the applicant requests the priority right, including:

a) A copy of the first application or applications filed by the receiving agency;
b) A document transferring the priority right if such right is inherited from another person.
8. Documents proving the right to use an individual's image for mark registration, including:
a) Copies of identity papers (bearing personal photos) in case the applicant uses his/her own photo;
b) Written consent to permit the use of a personal photo in accordance with civil law regulations in case the applicant uses another person’s photo and copies of identity papers (bearing personal photos) of such person;
c) The applicant is not required to submit copies of identity papers as prescribed in Points a and b of this Clause in case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam can access such information from the national population database.
9. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 69. Requirements for documents in a mark registration application
1. Documents in a mark registration application shall meet corresponding requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of this Article and general requirements regarding format as follows:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one side of an A4 paper sheet (210 mm x 297 mm); except for geographical map documents which may be presented on one side of an A3 paper sheet (420 mm x 297 mm) with four sides margins, each margin being 20 mm wide, using the Times New Roman font, letter size not smaller than 13, except for supporting documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b) For documents consisting of multiple pages, each page shall bear the Arabic numeral for its ordinal number;
c) Documents must be typed or printed with an ink type that is difficult to fade, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasures or corrections. For documents requiring signatures and seals, if the documents have 2 pages or more, each page must bear a signature or a seal affixed by the applicant or the issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents that are digitally signed. Before receiving the application, if minor spelling errors are detected, the applicant may correct them and must affix a signature (and seal, if applicable) at the correction location; the signature and seal must not obscure the content of the documents;
d) Documents included in the electronic application must be formatted as “.pdf”; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the paper document as per regulation; the individual signing the marks in the documents of the application may use a digital signature to directly sign on the electronic document or directly sign on the physical document and scan it as a color photo in the case of submission via the VNeID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not local languages, rare words, or self-created terms). Symbols, measurement units, electronic fonts, and spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and records related to transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents under Clauses 4, 5, 7, 8, and 9 Article 68 of this Circular, which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure complete and consistent mandatory information.
2. The mark registration application must ensure consistency as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 6 Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The mark registration declaration must be fully completed with all required information in appropriate spaces and meet the following requirements:
a) In the section "Non-traditional marks", if the applicant requests registration of a sound mark or a three-dimensional mark, the corresponding box must be marked;
b) In the section "Mark samples", there must be samples according to regulations on mark samples in Clause 4 of this Article;
c/ In the section "Description of mark", specify each constituent element of the mark and its general meaning (if any) and describe the following contents: c1/ If the mark contains words in a language other than Vietnamese, clearly write their Vietnamese pronunciation and if such words or phrases have meanings, they must be translated into Vietnamese; c2/ If the mark contains numerals other than Arabic numerals or Roman numerals, they must be translated into Arabic numerals; c3/ If the mark is a shape or contains a shape element, clearly state the content and meaning of this shape element; c4) If the mark shows colors and is subject to protection of color marks, such request shall be specified and name of colors shown on the mark shall be stated;

c5) If the mark is a sound, it must be described in full, detailed and complete about the sound sign which is requested to be registered as a mark (the sound of what musical instrument, with or without lyrics, etc.). This description can be presented in the attached documents;
d) In the “Exclusion elements” section: The applicant may exclude one or more elements of the mark sample which are not separately protected by the applicant;
dd) In the field "Applicant": If the applicant is a foreign individual or organization, the name and address must be written in Latin letters according to the international form of the original name, not translated or transcribed into Vietnamese;
e) In the Section "List and classification of goods and services bearing marks", the applicant shall classify them in accordance with the International Classification of Goods and Services under the Nice Agreement, which is published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Official Gazette. If the applicant fails to classify or classify incorrectly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall re-classify the goods and services, and the applicant shall pay a classification fee as prescribed.
4. A mark sample must satisfy the following requirements:
a) The mark sample must be clearly presented with the size of each component in the mark not larger than 80 mm and not smaller than 8 mm, the overall mark must be presented in the mark template with size of 80 mm x 80 mm printed on the declaration;
b) For a three-dimensional mark, the mark sample must be accompanied by a photograph or drawing showing the perspective view and may be accompanied by a descriptive sample in the form of a projection;
c) For a mark requiring color protection, the mark sample must be presented in the exact colors required for protection. If no color protection is required, the mark sample must be presented in black and white;
d) For a sound mark, the mark sample must be an MP3 audio file with a capacity of no more than 5 MB and a graphic representation of the sound, specifically represented by a 5-line musical staff.
The 5-line musical staff must be clear, accurate, complete, and consistent with the audio file, which may include notes, clefs, beats, rhythms, and musical notes, etc.
5. Regulations on use of collective marks shall include the following main contents:
a) Information about the mark and goods or services bearing the mark;
b) Name, address, grounds for establishment and operation of the collective organization being the owner of the mark when the protection title is granted;
c/ Criteria for becoming a member of a collective organization;
d/ List of members of a collective organization;
e/ Conditions for using marks. For collective marks containing geographical origin indications, conditions for using collective marks must include conditions on geographical origin and characteristics of goods or services;
f/ Measures to handle acts of violating the regulation on use of marks.
6. A regulation on use of a certification mark must contain the following principal contents:
a/ Information on the mark and goods or services certified by the mark;
b/ The organization being the owner of the mark when the protection title is granted;
c/ Conditions for using the mark. For certification marks containing geographical origin indications, conditions for using certification marks must include conditions on geographical origin and characteristics of goods or services;
d) Characteristics of goods or services certified by marks;
dd) Methods of assessing characteristics of goods and services and methods of controlling use of marks;
e) Expenses paid by mark users for certification and protection of marks, if any.
7. Documents proving the permission for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing place names or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property, including written permission for use in accordance with Point a and maps of the corresponding geographical area in accordance with Point b of this Clause.
a) Documents permitting the use of other place names or signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing such elements, issued by the following competent authorities:

a/ The People's Committee of the province or centrally-run city where the geographical area corresponds with another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of a local specialty (in case the geographical area belongs to one locality);

b/ All the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities where the geographical area corresponds with another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of a local specialty (in case the geographical area belongs to many localities).
b) The map of the geographical area corresponding to another place name or sign indicating the geographical origin of local specialties must fully present information sufficient to accurately identify such geographical area and must be certified by the competent authority specified in point a of this clause.
8. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date of notification. After the above time limit, if the applicant fails to submit supplementary documents satisfying requirements, the application shall continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application. Documents specified in this Clause may include the following documents:
a) Documents proving the applicant’s status:

a1) The Certificate of enterprise registration, contract or other documents certifying the production of products or provision of services by the applicant as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a2) Agreement or written confirmation that the manufacturer does not use marks and does not object to the registration of marks by the person conducting commercial activities of the manufacturer as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a3) Decision or establishment license, organizational charter which may contain information about the functions and powers to manage collective marks, quality certification marks, geographical origin marking marks of goods; d) Services specified in clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property; a4) Agreement, certificate of enterprise registration, documents related to mark registration of co-owners as prescribed in clause 5 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property; a5) Documents certifying that the applicant acquires the mark registration right from another person as prescribed in clause 6 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property; a6) Agreement or written consent of the mark owner certifying the mark registration right of the representative or agent as prescribed in clause 7 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property and Article 6septies of the Paris Convention. a7) Documents proving the applicant’s permanent residence in Vietnam that are still valid.
b) Documents proving the representative status of the applicant: the original letter of attorney of the applicant; documents confirming that the applicant is the legal representative of the mark registration organization or the person authorized by such organization; documents confirming that the authorized person of the applicant meets the requirements to act as the representative of the applicant as prescribed in Article 6 of this Circular;
c) Documents proving the right to use or registration of marks containing special signs as prescribed in Clauses 2, 4 and 7 Article 73, Point p Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property; documents proving that the use of commercial indications, origin indications, prizes, medals or distinctive symbols of a certain product does not mislead or deceive consumers or is deceptive in nature;
d) Documents proving priority rights;
dd) Documents proving the control and certification functions of the mark registration organization;
e) Necessary information to clarify or confirm contents stated in regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks or other application documents;
g) Other appropriate documents to clarify the authenticity of the information in the application.
Article 70. Receipt of applications for registration of marks
1. A mark registration application shall be accepted when it fully satisfies the following conditions:
a) Having all minimum documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Mark samples enclosed (enclosed with 05 samples of marks in case of paper application);

a3) Regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks for marks registered as collective marks or certification marks; a4) Documents proving the permission for registration of collective marks or certification marks containing geographical locations or other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. In case an application fully satisfies the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and verify the submission date and application number of such application and return a receipt note to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number, and results of the verification of the list of documents included in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For applications submitted via the electronic application system, upon receipt of an application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue an application receipt to the applicant, which includes information on the submission date, the application number, and the results of the inspection of the list of documents in the application, clearly indicating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application system.
3. In case an application fails to meet all conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Section, the Department of Intellectual Property shall reject it (if the application is submitted by post or via the electronic application submission system, the Department of Intellectual Property shall send a notice of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reasons for rejection). For applications rejected by post, the Department of Intellectual Property is not required to return documents of the application except for the original submitted.
4. Regarding documents proving eligibility for priority right, applicants may submit additional documents within 3 months from the date of application as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Section 2. REGULATION ON APPLICATION FORMS FOR REGISTRATION OF TRADE DESIGNS
Article 71. Formal examination of mark registration applications
1. The formal examination of a mark registration application according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the inspection of compliance with regulations on form of the application in order to conclude whether the application is valid or not.
The formal examination of a certification mark registration application or a collective mark registration application does not include the assessment of specific characteristics of goods or services certified by the mark, methods for assessing characteristics of goods or services, methods for controlling the use of the mark, costs incurred by the mark user for certification and protection of the mark (if any), and conditions for using the mark, and measures for handling violations against regulations on use of the mark prescribed in the regulations on use of such mark.
2. A mark registration application shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the registration right as prescribed in Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is filed in contravention of Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is not protected by the State as prescribed in Articles 8 and 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
d) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed;
dd) The application fails to meet the requirements regarding form prescribed in Article 69 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding the quantity of one of the mandatory documents; failing to meet the requirements regarding the presentation; the declaration does not clearly state the type of mark registered, lacking the description of the mark; failing to classify goods or services bearing the mark, or classifying incorrectly without paying classification fees; lacking the translation of documents proving priority rights (if necessary), the translation of documents proving registration rights if the applicant inherits registration rights from another person; information about the applicant in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not verified as prescribed, the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the applicant, the representative, signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.
e) No valid authorization document is provided (if the application is submitted by a representative).
3. Notification of the results of the formal examination shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the results of the formal examination, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the trademark registration certificate, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application (including priority right claims) and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to provide feedback or correct deficiencies.

b) Upon expiration of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or provides unsatisfactory corrections or does not provide any objection or provides an unsatisfactory objection, within 15 working days from the expiration of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the trademark registration certificate and send it to the applicant.
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article (or the applicant has provided satisfactory corrections or a satisfactory explanation), the application shall be determined to be valid and published and subject to substantive examination in accordance with regulations. In case the priority right claim is rejected, the application shall still be considered valid and be transferred for publication in accordance with regulations.
4. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of trademarks registered applications to be transferred for publication, clearly stating the application number, submission date and date of transfer for publication of each application.
5. The date of filing a trademark registration application is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in accordance with Clause 2 Article 70 of this Circular.
In case the application is filed through the electronic application system after the administrative hour or on a holiday, public holiday or Tet holiday as prescribed, the application submission date as prescribed in this Clause shall be the next working day.
6. The priority date of a mark registration application shall be determined as follows:
a) If the application does not request priority rights or, despite requesting priority rights, is not approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, it shall be considered having no priority date;
b) If the application requests priority rights, the priority date(s) shall be the date specified in such application and approved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
c) The determination of the priority date according to the request for priority rights based on the first application submitted in Vietnam shall comply with the principles prescribed in Article 91 of the Law on Intellectual Property and corresponding provisions at Points b, c, and dd Clause 1 Article 12 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
7. In case an applicant requests priority rights but, at the time of filing the mark registration application, there is no document proving the priority rights, the examination of the application form shall start from the time the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the supplementary documents or end after 3 months as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 70 of this Circular.
Article 72. Disclosure and publication of mark registration applications
1. Mark registration applications shall be disclosed immediately after they are received in accordance with Clause 2 Article 70 of this Circular.
2. Valid mark registration applications shall be published in accordance with Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
3. Contents of a disclosure of a mark registration application in the Industrial Property Official Gazette shall include information about the applicant, the representative (if any), the application number, the filing date, the priority date (if any); information about the mark sample and the list of goods and services bearing the mark; international classification of goods and services; information about the origin of the application (application conversion, application splitting, the original application number of the split application); and other information (if any).
Regulations on use of collective marks and regulations on use of certification marks shall be disclosed on the website of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 3. EVALUATION OF CONTENTS OF MARK REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 73. Evaluation of contents of mark registration applications
1. Evaluation of contents of a mark registration application according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application for protection eligibility and determination of the corresponding scope of protection according to this Article.
2. The evaluation of the eligibility for grant of protection titles for a mark registration application includes:
a) Evaluation of the conformity of the subject matter stated in the mark registration application with the requirements for grant of the certificate of mark registration according to Article 75 of this Circular;
b) Evaluation of the subject matter stated in the mark registration application according to the protection conditions specified in Articles 76, 77 and 78 of this Circular. The evaluation shall be carried out separately for each component of signs for each good or service listed in the list of goods and services;
c) Assessing the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 79 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles under Point b Clause 2 of this Article shall be completed after the subject matter stated in the application has been assessed with all protection conditions met and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter fails to satisfy or satisfies the protection conditions, specifically:
a) There is a reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy one or more than one protection condition; or

b) There is no reason to conclude that the subject matter fails to satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Before issuing a notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of a mark registration certificate as prescribed in Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall examine the first-to-file rule as prescribed in Article 79 of this Circular.
5. Based on the results of the assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles mentioned in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding scope of intended protection.
6. The notice of content appraisal results with the intended grant of a mark registration certificate as prescribed in Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular shall be issued for the following mark registration applications:
a) Applications not falling into the cases specified in Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application has the earliest submission date or priority date among trademark registration applications falling under the case specified in Clause 2 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) The application is submitted as agreed in Clause 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
7. A trademark registration application not falling under the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article shall be handled as follows:
a) It shall be rejected from the grant of a trademark registration certificate because it fails to satisfy the first-to-file rule if it has the earliest submission date or priority date for the grant of a trademark registration certificate; or b) It shall be considered having the earliest submission date or priority date and handled as the cases specified in Clause 6 of this Article if all other trademark registration applications with earlier submission dates or priority dates have their trademark registration certificates rejected, withdrawn or considered withdrawn.
8. During the process of substantive examination of an application, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations about the content of the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the applicant to remedy the deficiencies.
Article 74. Minimum information sources
1. In order to assess the likelihood of confusion of signs stated in a trademark registration application, at least the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct searches using the following minimum information sources:
a) Trademark registration applications filed with the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam with an earlier submission date or priority date, or with the same submission date or priority date as the application currently undergoing examination, and international trademark registration applications designating Vietnam which have been notified by the International Office of Vietnam of an earlier submission date or priority date, or with the same submission date or priority date as the application currently undergoing examination for identical or similar goods or services;
b) Markes which are being protected or recognized for protection and used for identical, similar or related goods or services;
c) Protected marks which have expired within 3 years, except for cases where marks are expired due to non-use as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property and used for identical or similar goods or services;
d) Geographical indications which are being protected in Vietnam;
dd) Well-known marks which have been recognized according to regulations;
e) Place names, symbols or other signs indicating geographical origins of goods or services; geographical names, quality marks, inspection marks, warranty marks of international organizations; national flags, national emblems, national anthems of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and of countries and international organizations; flags, names, symbols of state agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations of Vietnam and the world; names and images of leaders, national heroes, names and images of Vietnamese and foreign celebrities, etc. collected and preserved by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. In case of necessity, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may look up reference information outside the minimum information sources specified in Clause 1 of this Article, such as trade names, industrial designs, plant varieties which have been or are being protected in Vietnam; names and images of characters and figures in works under copyright protection, etc.
Article 75. Assessment of compatibility of subject matters stated in applications for mark registration The assessment of compatibility between subject matters stated in applications for mark registration and requirements for grant of a certificate of registered mark shall be carried out as follows:

1. As prescribed in Clause 1 Article 72 of the Law on Intellectual Property, signs registered as marks must be visible in the form of letters, numbers, words, drawings, images, including three-dimensional images or combinations thereof, expressed in one or several certain colors or sound signals expressed in graphics (specifically, 5-line music staves).
2. The following signs shall not be eligible for protection as marks:
a) Signs which are merely colors and not combined with signs in the form of letters (letters or numerals) or signs in the form of images or not presented in the form of signs in the form of letters or signs in the form of images; or sound signs which cannot be presented in the form of graphics (specifically, the form of a 5-line musical staff);
b) Signs which fall under the categories ineligible for protection as marks according to the provisions of Article 73 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) Signs contrary to social ethics, public order, harmful to national defense and security according to the provisions of Article 8 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 76. Assessment of distinguishability of signs stated in applications
1. The assess of the distinguishability of signs in the form of letters according to the provisions of Clause 2, Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be carried out as follows:
Except for the exceptions prescribed in Clause 5 of this Article, the following signs in the form of letters shall be considered indistinguishable:
a) Characters belonging to languages which Vietnamese consumers have common knowledge of which they cannot recognize or remember (cannot read, understand or remember), such as characters not derived from Latin: Arabic, Slavic, Sanskrit, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, etc.; unless the characters belong to the above-mentioned languages are accompanied by other components that constitute the whole and are distinguishable or presented in the form of graphics or other special forms;
b) Latin characters consisting of only one letter or number or not pronounceable as a syllable despite having two letters (in Vietnamese) or not forming a word in other common languages (English, French) - even if accompanied by numbers, except for cases where such signs are presented in graphic forms or other special forms;
c) A collection of too many letters or words making it impossible to recognize and remember as a sequence of too many characters not arranged in a definite order or rule or a document or a paragraph of a document;
d) Latin characters forming a word with meaning which has been widely used and is common in Vietnam in the relevant field to the extent of losing distinguishable ability;
dd) A word or a collection of words used in Vietnam as the common name of the related goods or service;
e) A word or a collection of words describing the main goods or service bearing a trademark as a sign indicating time, location, production method, type, quantity, quality, characteristics, components, utility, value of the goods or service;
g) A word or a collection of words describing the geographical origin of the goods or service;
h) A word or a collection of words with meanings describing the legal form and business sector of the trademark owner;
i) Signs of letters falling under the cases specified at Points e, g, h, i, k, l, m, o, p, Clause 2, Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Assessment of distinguishability of graphic signs (including visual signs, drawings, shapes, etc.) according to Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be carried out as follows: Except for exceptions prescribed in Clause 5 of this Article, graphic signs shall be considered indistinguishable if:
a) The graphic sign is a common shape such as circle, ellipse, triangle, quadrilateral, etc., or simple drawing; the drawing or image is only used as background or decorative lines of products or product packages;
b) The graphic sign is too complicated for consumers to easily perceive and remember its characteristics, including too many combined or overlapping images and lines;
c) The drawing, image, symbol, symbolic sign, or common shape of goods or part of goods, or common shape of packaging or goods container has been used regularly and widely recognized;
d) The graphic sign mainly describes the goods or service bearing the mark, such as location, production method, type, quantity, quality, properties, components, utility, value, or other characteristics of the goods or service bearing the mark, or the sign significantly increases the value of the goods;
dd) The drawing or image describes the geographical origin of the goods or service;
e) The graphic sign is identical or insignificantly different from the industrial design already or currently protected by another person;
g) The graphic sign falls under cases prescribed in Points e, g, h, i, l, m, and p Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. The ability of a sound signal to be distinguished shall be assessed as follows:
Except for the exceptions specified in Clause 5 of this Article, a sound signal shall be considered indistinguishable if:
a/ Popular and common sounds used for goods and services or in production and business domains related to goods and services and already familiar to concerned public;
b) The sound has too many melodies or tones or is too long to be recognized and remembered;
c/ The short and simple musical notes, including one or two musical notes;
d) Sounds containing descriptions of main goods or services bearing marks as indications of time, location, production method, type, quantity, quality, nature, composition, utility, value or other characteristics of goods or services bearing marks;
dd) Sounds containing descriptions or indications of geographical origins of goods or services;
e) The sound has a meaningful description of legal form and business sector;
4. In case a sound mark contains linguistic content, such linguistic content shall be evaluated for distinguishability similar to evaluation of distinguishability of a word mark according to Clause 1 of this Article.
5. Exceptions applicable to evaluation of distinguishability of word marks, graphic marks and sound marks:
a) Mark signs mentioned in Points a, b, c, e, g Clause 1, Points a, b, d, dd Clause 2, Points d, dd Clause 3 of this Article which have been used with the function of marks and are widely recognized by consumers as having achieved the distinguishability for related goods or services before the filing date. To apply exceptions specified in this Clause, applicants must provide evidence on the widespread use of such mark signs with the function of marks (number of relevant consumers, starting time of use, scope and level of use before the filing date, sales volume from sale of goods or provision of services, etc.) and thus the mark signs have the distinguishability for related goods or services of the applicants. In this case, the mark signs must be continuously used and widely spread in lawful production, business, commercial activities, advertising and marketing in the form shown in the application.
b) Mark signs mentioned in Point g Clause 1, Point dd Clause 2, Point dd Clause 3 of this Article which are registered as certification marks or collective marks containing signs indicating geographical origins of goods or services.
c) Mark signs mentioned in Point g Clause 1, Point dd Clause 2, Point dd Clause 3 of this Article which are registered as certification marks indicating geographical origins of goods or services or collective marks.
6. Evaluation of distinguishability of combined marks including both word marks and graphic marks (hereinafter referred to as “combined marks”) shall be carried out as follows:
A combined mark shall be considered having distinguishability when the combination of the word mark and graphic mark forms an overall mark having distinguishability, specifically:
a) Both the word mark and graphic mark have distinguishability and form an overall mark having distinguishability;
b) The strong component of a mark (an element that strongly affects the consumer's perception, causing attention and impression about the mark when heard or seen) is a sign in letters or figures that can be distinguished, even though the remaining components have little or no distinguishable ability;
c) In case a combination of signs including both written and graphic signs is indistinguishable or has little distinguishable ability but the unique combination of these signs creates a distinct impression, such overall combination shall still be considered distinguishable;
d) The combination sign consists of letters and images that are indistinguishable or have little distinguishable features but, overall, is distinguishable through use in accordance with Clause 5 of this Article.
Article 77. Criteria for determining place names and other signs indicating geographical origins of goods and services

The identification of geographical origins of goods or services by using other place names and signs as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 and Points dd and dd1 Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be carried out according to the following criteria:
1. Sign indicating geographical origin of goods or services means a sign used for local goods or services and serving as an indicator of the geographical origin of such goods or services (indicating that the goods or services originate from such locality).
Signs indicating the geographical origin of goods or services are local place names or distinctive signs (images of typical local objects such as symbols, maps, flags, badges, scenic views, special works of local areas, etc.) or any other signs. Place names may be current names, historical names, official names, or folk names of a geographical area used as a substitute for current place names or widely known place names (identified according to administrative boundaries or geographical methods).
2. A place name or a local emblem used for ordinary goods and services (not specialties) may or may not have a guiding meaning of geographical origin of goods and services, depending on goods and services and actual use of such place name or local emblem.
3. Place names and local emblematic signs have the meaning of indicating geographical origins of goods or services in the following cases:
a) Used for local specialties (special products with reputation due to certain characteristics, manufactured locally);
b) Used for characteristic plants and domestic animals of a locality and products processed from such plants or animals;
c) Used for products extracted from natural materials (coal, iron, steel, aluminum, cement, stone, salt, wood, etc.) of a locality;
d) Used for products of local developed industries;
dd) Used for specific services (services with reputation associated with certain characteristics of a locality);
e) Other cases determined according to products and actual use of place names and local emblematic signs for products.
4. Place names and local emblematic signs do not have the meaning of indicating geographical origins of goods or services in the following cases:
a) Having been used with the function of a common mark and widely recognized, i.e., achieving the meaning of commercial origin indication (distinguishing capability) and losing the descriptive meaning of geographical origin, e.g.: Hanoi beer, Saigon beer;
b) The corresponding locality cannot be the place where the goods are produced, e.g.: Arctic cigarettes, etc. Place names and distinctive signs of localities which have no meaning for indicating the geographical origin of goods or services may be protected as common marks without permission from local authorities.
5. Place names and distinctive signs of localities which belong to common geographical knowledge and are known by many people (e.g.: names of provinces, cities, scenic spots) used for local common goods and services (including products with business advantages but not yet having reputation or quality characteristics) used by many local business entities for their own goods and services with the meaning of describing the production location (but there is not enough ground to classify them into the cases prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article) are signs not protected as marks but may be used as an auxiliary element to form common marks of organizations and individuals in the corresponding localities, provided that such place names are excluded from the protection scope (not separately protected) and do not require permission from local authorities.
6. Signs indicating the geographical origin of Vietnam prescribed at Point dd1 Clause 1 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall be understood as signs consisting of letters with the meaning of indicating the name of Vietnam, including "Việt" or "Việt Nam", the corresponding English letters "Viet", "Vietnam", or "Viet Nam".
Article 78. Assessment of the ability to cause confusion of signs stated in applications
1. In case the signs stated in the application are letters or figures, the assessment of the similarity to the point of confusion with other marks requested for registration shall be carried out as follows:
a) In order to assess whether or not the sign requesting registration stated in the application is identical or confusingly similar to another mark (hereinafter referred to as “comparative mark”), it is necessary to compare the pronunciation and meaning (for signs consisting of letters) and the structure and method of presentation (for both signs consisting of letters and signs consisting of figures as prescribed in Points b and c of this Clause), and at the same time, to compare goods or services bearing the sign with those bearing the comparative mark according to the provisions of Clause 3 of this Article;
b) Sign identical to a control mark: a sign shall be considered identical to a control mark if it is identical to such mark in terms of its structure and manner of presentation;
c) A sign is considered similar to the point of confusion with a control mark if:

c1) The sign is similar to the control mark in terms of composition or /and pronunciation or /and meaning or /and form to the point of confusion that consumers may mistakenly believe that these two subjects are one or that this subject is a variant of that one or that these two subjects have the same origin in commerce;

c2) The sign is just an transcription or translation from the control mark if the control mark is a well-known mark.
2. In case the sign stated in the application is a sound sign, the similarity to the extent of confusion between the sign requesting registration and another mark shall be assessed as follows:
a) In order to assess whether the signs of registration request stated in the application are identical or similar to the point of confusion with the control mark, it is necessary to compare the sound elements (length, structure, composition of sounds; melody, rhythm, pattern, etc.) and language elements (if any) according to the provisions of Points b and c of this Clause, and at the same time, it is necessary to compare the goods or services bearing the signs with those bearing the control mark according to the provisions of Clause 3 of this Article;
b) Sign identical to a control mark: a sign shall be regarded as identical to a control mark if it is identical to such mark in terms of sound elements and linguistic elements;
c) A sign is considered similar to the point of confusion with a control mark if:

c1) The sign is similar to the control mark in terms of sound elements or language elements (pronunciation) or meaning to the point where consumers may mistakenly believe that these two subjects are one or that this subject is a variant of that other subject or that these two subjects have the same origin;

c2) The sign is just the pronunciation of the control mark if the control mark is a well-known mark.
3. The similarity of goods and services shall be assessed as follows:
a) Two goods or two services are considered identical (of the same type) when they have the following characteristics:

a1) They have the same nature (components, structure, etc.) and the same functions and uses; or

a2) They have nearly similar nature and the same functions and uses;
b) Two goods or two services are considered similar if they fall into one of the following cases:

b1) They are similar in nature and are marketed through the same commercial channel (distributed by the same method, sold together or next to each other, in the same type of shop, relevant public/potential consumers, etc.);

b2) They are similar in functions and use purposes and are marketed through the same commercial channel (distributed by the same method, sold together or next to each other, in the same type of shop, relevant public/potential consumers, etc.);
c) A good and a service shall be regarded as similar if they fall into one of the following cases: c1) There is a relationship between them in terms of nature (the good or service or parts thereof are made from the other good or service); or c2) There is a relationship between them in terms of function (the other good or service is required for the performance of the function of this good or service or they are often used together); or c3) There is a close relationship between them in terms of mode of implementation (this good or service is the result of the use or exploitation of the other good or service, etc.).
4. Signs shall be considered identical or confusingly similar to a control mark used for identical or similar goods in the following cases:
a) Signs identical with the control marks and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of goods or services bearing the control marks;
b) Signs are similar enough to cause confusion with the control mark and goods or services bearing signs identical or similar to those of the goods or services bearing the control mark;
c) Signs are identical or similar to the control mark which is a well-known mark and goods or services bearing signs that are neither identical nor similar to those of the goods or services bearing such mark but the use of such signs as marks may mislead consumers into believing that there exists a relationship between goods or services bearing such signs and the owner of the well-known mark, which may actually reduce the distinguishability of the well-known mark or damage the reputation of the well-known mark.
5. The appraisal of other confusability of signs shall comply with Article 73 and Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property and the following specific regulations:
a) Signs are considered causing confusion as to the origin of goods or services in the following cases:

a1) Signs are identical or confusingly similar to the name or symbol of a country or a territory (national flag, national emblem, national name, place name, local name, local symbol, etc.) which leads to the misconception that the goods or services bearing such signs originate from such country or territory but actually originate from another country or territory;

a2) Signs are identical or similar to protected geographical indications if the use of such signs may mislead consumers as to the geographical origin of goods; signs are identical to protected geographical indications or contain protected geographical indications or are translated or transcribed from protected geographical indications for wines or spirits if the signs requesting registration as marks for wines or spirits do not originate from the geographical area bearing such geographical indications;

a3) Signs are identical or similar to the trade names of other persons that have been lawfully used for the same type of goods or services and may lead to the misconception that the goods or services bearing such signs were produced or provided by the persons with such trade names;

a4) Signs are identical or similar to real names, aliases, pen names, images of leaders, national heroes or famous personalities of Vietnam or foreign countries; signs are identical or similar to names or images of characters or typical images of works that are widely known if the use of such signs may lead to the misconception that the goods or services bearing such signs were produced or provided by the owners of such works;

a5) Signs are identical or insignificantly different from industrial designs of other persons that have been and are being protected on the basis of applications for registration of industrial designs with submission dates or priority dates earlier than those of applications for registration of marks.
b) Signs are considered likely to cause confusion or misinterpretation of the nature or value of goods or services in the following cases:

b1) Signs that are words, drawings, images, symbols, sounds, etc., that create a misleading impression on the features or utility of goods or services, such as signs identical or similar to marks or other widely used signs to the extent that they are considered associated with a function or utility of a certain type of goods or service, causing consumers to mistakenly believe that goods or services bearing such signs also have such features or utilities;

b2) Signs that are words, images, sounds that create a misleading impression on the composition or structure of goods or services, such as descriptions of other related goods or services. Services bearing a sign which misleads as if they were created from or have the nature of goods or services described.
Article 79. Evaluation of first-to-file principle
For mark registration applications which have been concluded to satisfy the protection conditions, before issuing content appraisal results and intending to grant mark registration certificates under Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 Clause 3 Article 80 of this Circular, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct examination to ensure the first-to-file rule prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 90 of the Law on Intellectual Property as follows:
1. In order to examine the first-to-file principle, it is required to search all mark registration applications received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (by the time of examination) with an application submission date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority rights) earlier than or on the same day as the submission date or priority date (if the application enjoys priority rights) of the application currently undergoing appraisal.
2. The lookup is to find cases where there are many applications (including applications currently being appraised) of different persons registering marks which are identical or confusingly similar to each other for identical or similar goods and services, or where there are many applications of the same person registering identical marks for identical goods and services; and to determine the earliest application submission date or priority date.
3. If there are many applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the mark registration certificate shall only be granted to the mark in the valid application with the earliest submission date or priority date among the applications satisfying the conditions for grant of the mark registration certificate.
4. Among the applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, if there are many applications with the same earliest submission date or priority date, the mark registration certificate shall only be granted to the mark of a single application among such applications as agreed upon by all applicants; if no agreement is reached, all corresponding subjects of such applications shall be rejected from the grant of the mark registration certificate.
Article 80. Grant and refusal to grant mark registration certificates
1. Mark registration applications shall be refused to grant mark registration certificates in the cases specified at Points a, b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Mark registration applications shall be granted mark registration certificates when they satisfy the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. Mark registration applications falling into the cases specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article shall be subject to the following tasks by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of the mark registration certificate and fixing a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for grant of the mark registration certificate, the disclosure fee for the decision on grant, and the registration fee for the decision on grant of the mark registration certificate;
b) If the subject matters of the protection request stated in the application fail to satisfy the protection conditions or fall into the cases specified at Points b, c, d and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or satisfy the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant the trademark registration certificate, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide its opinion;
c/ In case the applicant fails to correct errors or does not make any objection within the time limit prescribed at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days after the expiry of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the grant of the mark registration certificate;
d) In case the applicant corrects deficiencies or objects within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the written correction of deficiencies or objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the content appraisal as follows:

d1) If the correction is unsatisfactory or the objection is unjustified, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the certificate of trademark registration;

d2) If the applicant has satisfactorily corrected deficiencies or justifiably objected to part or all of the content appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results. specifying the intended grant of a certificate of registered mark to the entire or part of the mark which satisfies the protection conditions and fixing a time limit of 2 months from the date of notification for the applicant to pay the fee for grant of the certificate of registered mark, the fee for publication of the decision on grant of the certificate of registered mark, and the registration fee for the decision on grant of the certificate of registered mark;
dd) If the subject matters of the application for protection include goods or services not eligible for protection or have elements in the mark sample not protected separately (and the applicant fails to exclude them by themselves or exclude them improperly), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of the Certificate of Mark Registration for the eligible parts, fees and charges for the grant of the Certificate of Mark Registration and other fees and charges as prescribed that the applicant must pay in case of agreement with all content appraisal results of the application, reasons for refusal to grant for the remaining parts, non-protected elements (if any), and at the same time impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide written opinions;
e) In case the applicant does not provide opinions or does not provide written consent and fully pays the fees and charges within the time limit specified at Point dd of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the Certificate of Mark Registration;
g) In case the applicant submits a written opinion within the time limit specified at Point dd of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the document, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall proceed as follows:

g1) If the applicant has a justifiable objection to the entire appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the trademark registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the trademark registration certificate, the disclosure fee and the record registration fee for the decision on issuance of the trademark registration certificate;

g2) If the applicant has an unjustifiable objection or provides a justifiable explanation for part of the appraisal results, The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, specifying the intended grant of a trademark registration certificate for the part satisfying the protection conditions, reasons for refusal to grant the certificate for the remaining part or non-protection elements (if any), and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for grant of the trademark registration certificate, the disclosure fee, and the record registration fee for the decision on grant of the trademark registration certificate;

h) If the applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge within the time limit specified at Points a, d2, g1 and g2 of this Clause, within 15 working days after the expiry of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant the certificate of registered mark;
i) If the applicant fully and on time pays all fees and charges prescribed in Points a, d2, dd, g1, g2 of this Clause and obtains written consent for all appraisal results in the case specified at Point dd of this Clause, within 01 month from the date of full payment of fees and charges, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant a certificate of registered mark with the part satisfying the protection conditions and the non-protection elements (if any), and refuse to grant the part failing to satisfy the protection conditions (if any), clearly stating the reasons for refusal (if any).
4. The decision on grant of a certificate of mark registration shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance. Published contents include information in the decision on grant of a certificate of mark registration (including the decision number and issuance date) and the contents specified in Clause 3 Article 72 of this Circular.
Regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks shall be published on the website of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5. A certificate of mark registration made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP may be issued in electronic form and paper form (if the applicant requests a paper copy). In case industrial property rights to a mark are under joint ownership, the certificate of mark registration may only be issued to the first person on the list of joint applicants.
A copy of the certificate of mark registration made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests a copy (specified in the mark registration declaration) for other co-owners.

Article 81. Processing of requests for fast-track content appraisal of mark registration applications
1. Requests for fast-track content appraisal of mark registration applications shall be accepted when the following documents are available and the applicant pays the fee and charge for fast-track content appraisal:
a) Application for fast-track content appraisal made according to Form No. 14 specified in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the registered mark falls under one of the cases specified in Point a Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
2. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process requests for fast content appraisal as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article according to the following regulations:
a) In case the request for fast content appraisal fails to meet the requirements specified in Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, within 15 working days from the date of receipt of the request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the refusal to accept the request for fast content appraisal and shall continue processing the related mark registration application as prescribed;
b) In case the request for fast content appraisal meets the requirements specified in Clause 2 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the request for fast content appraisal shall be published at the same time as the publication of the mark registration application and shall undergo content appraisal in accordance with this Circular within the time limit specified in Clause 2a Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property, except where the mark registration application is determined to be invalid under Article 71 of this Circular or falls under the cases specified in Points b and c Clause 4 Article 14a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Article 82. Handling of objections to mark registration applications
1. Within 3 months from the date of publication of a mark registration application, any third party has the right to object to the grant of a certificate of mark registration and must pay fees and charges. Such an objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese, enclosed with documents or citing sources of information to support it. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. Where an objection to a mark registration application satisfies the requirements specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and handle such objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in Clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 01 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 01 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response, except in the following cases where the objection may be handled during the content appraisal process:

a1) The mark and goods or services in the application are identical to the mark and goods or services provided by the objector or have sufficient grounds to conclude that the marked and goods or services are similar and misleading or not similar and misleading to the mark and goods or services provided by the objector;

a2) Other cases where there are sufficient grounds to conclude that the objection is unjustified;
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a clause 2 of this Article, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the feedback to the objector and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide written responses to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) On the basis of information, evidence and arguments provided by the parties, results of the dialogue (if any) as prescribed in Points a, b and c of this Clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the result of processing the objection together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application to the objector, except for the case specified at Point dd of this Clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the opposition of the withdrawal of the mark registration application as prescribed in Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 01 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of the application.
3. In case of objection related to registration rights, such objection shall be handled as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the objector to file a lawsuit petition to a competent Court in accordance with the law on civil procedures and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of notification for the objector to submit copies of the Court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit, excluding the following cases where the objection is handled during the content appraisal:

a1) There are clear grounds to determine that the applicant does not have the registration right as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 7 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;

a2) The objection concerns the right to file a trademark registration application for signs that are or contain geographical indications. Other signs indicating geographical origins of local specialties of Vietnam as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property; a3) Opposition opinions about the right to file registration applications without attached documents or sources of information for proof or with attached documents or sources of information but such documents or sources of information are not sufficient to determine that the applicants do not have the registration right;
b) Upon expiry of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if the objector fails to submit copies of the notice of acceptance of the case or his/her objection, such copies shall be regarded as withdrawn and the application for registration of mark shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the mark registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the Court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall resume immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the Court.
4. The time limit for applicants to respond to objections and the time limit for objectors to respond to applicants’ opinions as prescribed in Points a and b Clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal.
Article 83. Amendment to mark registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of trademark registration, the applicant may take initiative or based on the notice of appraisal results to amend or supplement documents in the application for trademark registration.
2. The request for amendment or supplementation of an application for trademark registration shall include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment or supplementation of the application by the applicant, the request for amendment or supplementation shall be made in a Declaration, made according to Form No. 10 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of amendment or supplementation of the application based on the notice of appraisal results from the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the amendment or supplementation shall be made in a written response clearly stating the amended or supplemented content;
c) The applicant may request amendment or supplementation with the same content related to multiple applications for trademark registration in one Declaration or one written request for amendment or supplementation;
d) In case of amendment or supplementation related to the trademark sample, the amended trademark samples (5 samples in case of paper application) or the list of goods and services shall be submitted, and the list of goods and services that have been amended or supplemented and attached with the detailed presentation of the amended or supplemented content compared to the initial submitted documents. In case the applicant takes initiative in amendment or supplementation without protecting the exclusive elements in the trademark sample, the applicant must clearly state it in the Declaration of amendment;

dd) In case of change to name, address or country code of the applicant, the applicant shall submit certification documents (original copies or certified true copies) or legal documents (certified true copies) proving such change (decision on change to name or address; certificate of enterprise registration which records the change to name or address, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for actively extracting and using information available in databases announced by database management agencies to replace components of application files for handling administrative procedures. In case where information cannot be extracted or extracted information is insufficient or inaccurate, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a written request to individuals or organizations to supplement application components and clearly state reasons. The time limit for requesting and supplementing application files shall not be included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
e) In case of change to industrial property representative, the applicant shall submit a document declaring termination of authorization for the old industrial property representative and change to the new industrial property representative as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. The applicant shall pay an appraisal fee for the request for amendment to each amended content and a fee for disclosure of information about amendments to the application if the amended content must be disclosed as prescribed in Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except for cases where the amendments are made to correct errors made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Requests for amendments to mark registration applications must not expand the scope (volume) of protection already disclosed in the mark samples and the list of goods and services stated in the applications, and must not change the nature of the subject matters of the registration requests stated in the applications.
5. In case of amendments to the application made by the applicant, the request for amendments shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendments to the application satisfies the provisions of Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the request for amendments to the application (except for cases where the amendments to the application are made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam announces the application or refuses to grant the mark registration certificate due to invalidation of the application).
a) The amended contents shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette if the request for amendment or supplementation of information related to the valid application in terms of form includes the sample mark and the list of goods and services enclosed therewith;
b) If the request for amendment or supplementation of the application does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment or supplementation of the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the set time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection or inappropriate objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment or supplementation of the application;
c) In case the request for amendment or supplementation of the application is related to the sample mark, the list of goods and services bearing the mark, changes in information of the applicant (in case of collective mark registration, certification mark registration), regulations on use of collective mark or regulations on use of certification mark which are submitted after the notice of intended grant of the certificate of mark registration is issued, the mark registration application shall be re-examined and the applicant shall pay fees as prescribed.
6. In case the applicant amends or supplements the application based on the notice of results of examination of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the acceptance or refusal to accept the applicant's request for amendment or supplementation of the application shall be made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the notice of results of examination or the corresponding decision during the processing of such mark registration application.
7. In case the request for amendment or supplementation of the application includes both the content of amendment or supplementation actively implemented by the applicant and the content of amendment or supplementation of the application based on the notice of results of content examination, the request for amendment or supplementation shall be made into an application form according to the provisions of point a clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the request for amendment or supplementation in this case shall comply with the provisions of clause 5 of this Article.
Article 84. Recording of changes of applicants for mark registration
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of mark registration, an applicant may request the recording of changes in the applicant on the basis of transfer, inheritance, or according to a decision of a competent authority.
2. A request for recording of changes in the mark registration applicant shall meet the following requirements:
a) The request for recording of changes in the applicant shall be made using Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of recording of changes in the applicant due to transfer of the application, the applicant must submit documents showing the transfer of the mark registration application (original or certified true copy), including the name and address of the transferring party and the receiving party; the number of the transferred application or information sufficient to identify the application. For documents with multiple pages, each page must bear the signatures of the parties or have a seal on every two adjacent pages.

The receiving party must meet the conditions applicable to persons having registration rights as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) In case of recording of changes in the applicant due to inheritance, succession, or according to a decision of a competent authority, the applicant must submit documents proving the inheritance or succession of assets upon consolidation, merger, division, separation of legal entities, joint venture, association, establishment of new legal entities under the same ownership, business model conversion, or according to a decision of a court or another competent authority, or inheritance due to abandonment of registration rights by another applicant;
d) The applicant must pay fees and charges as prescribed;
dd) Requests for recording of changes due to transfer of multiple applications from the same applicant may be made in the same declaration, provided that the appraisal fee is paid in accordance with regulations corresponding to the number of applications requested for recording of transfer.
3. Requests for change of applicants shall be processed as follows:
a) If a request for change of an applicant satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of such change and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette (except for requests for change of an applicant filed before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant a protection title due to invalidation of the application);
b) If a request for change of an applicant fails to satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept such change and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection is provided, or the objection provided is unconvincing, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept such change;
c) In case of a request for change due to transfer of a mark registration application filed after a notice of intended issuance of a certificate of registered mark is issued, the mark registration application must be re-examined and the transferred contents must be published. The applicant must pay the examination fee and publication fee as prescribed.
Article 85. Splitting of mark registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision on issuance or refusal to issue a certificate of registered mark, the applicant may take the initiative or based on the notice of examination results to split part of the list of goods and services in the previously filed mark registration application (initial application) into one or more new mark registration applications (split applications).
2. For a split-up application, the applicant shall submit a declaration of registration for a new mark to the list of goods and services split up. The split-up application shall bear a new serial number and the filing date of the original mark registration application or (priority dates) of the original mark registration application (if any). The applicant shall pay the filing fee and all charges and fees for procedures performed for the split-up application other than the procedures already performed in the original application without having to perform again in the split-up application and is not required to pay the charge for examination of the request for priority right.
3. For an original application, the applicant shall carry out procedures for amendment and supplementation of the application as prescribed in Article 83 of this Circular.
4. Split-up applications and original applications shall be processed as follows:
a/ Split-up applications shall be appraised for form and further processed according to the unfinished procedures for original applications. Split-up applications shall be published under regulations;
b/ Original applications shall be further processed under Article 83 of this Circular.
Article 86. Withdrawal of mark registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam decides to grant or refuses to grant a certificate of mark registration, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the mark registration application.
2. The withdrawal of a mark registration application shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative through a written declaration. For an application filed through a representative, the power of attorney must clearly state the authorization for application withdrawal or enclosed with a command specifying the serial number of the application to be withdrawn.
3. Within 02 months from the date of receipt of a declaration of withdrawal of a mark registration application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process such withdrawal as follows:
a) Where the withdrawal request satisfies the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the withdrawal request and concurrently terminate the processing of the application. The withdrawn mark registration application shall not be reinstated but may only serve as a basis for requesting priority rights;

b) Where the withdrawal request does not satisfy the provisions of clause 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or has failed to meet requirements after correction, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the withdrawal request within 15 working days from the expiration of the imposed time limit.
Article 87. Re-examination of mark registration applications
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct re-examination of mark registration applications in the following cases:
a) Re-examination of mark registration applications due to written opinions of a third party opposing the intended grant of a certificate of mark registration in the notice of content examination results, together with legitimate evidence proving that due to force majeure events or objective obstacles, the opposition could not be made within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Re-examination of applications due to requests for amendment or supplementation of applications, or recognition of changes to applicants after notification of content examination results, which indicate the intended grant of protection titles according to the provisions of point c clause 5 Article 83 and point c clause 3 Article 84 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of a mark registration application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of a mark registration application shall comply with Clause 3 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complicated cases involving many details requiring verification or expert opinions, the time limit for re-examination may be extended but must not exceed the time limit for initial examination prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The contents of re-examination shall comply with corresponding provisions in Section 3 Chapter V of this Circular;
c) For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of the mark registration application may only be carried out once by a third party.
3. After obtaining the results of re-examination, the mark registration application shall be processed as follows:
a) For the case specified at Point a Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-examination results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application according to the provisions of this Circular;
b) For the case specified at Point b Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application according to Article 80 of this Circular.
Article 88. Recording of well-known marks
1. Well-known marks shall be protected and belong to their owners without registration procedures. Mark owners may use documents specified in Clause 2 of this Article to prove their ownership over the marks and to prove that the marks meet the conditions for being considered well-known.
2. Documents proving the mark ownership and proving the popularity of a mark may include information on the scope, scale, level and continuity of the use of the mark, including descriptions of the origin, history and period of continuous use of the mark; the number of countries where the mark has been registered or recognized as a well-known mark; the list of goods and services bearing the mark; the territorial scope within which the mark is circulated, sales volume of products or provision of services; the quantity of goods and services bearing the mark that have been manufactured and sold; the value of assets of the mark, the price of transfer or licensing of the right to use, the investment capital contribution value of the mark; investments and costs for advertising and marketing of the mark, including participation in national and international exhibitions; infringement cases, disputes and decisions and judgments of courts or competent agencies; survey data on consumers’ knowledge of the mark through purchase, use and advertising and marketing; rankings and assessments of the reputation of the mark by national and international organizations and mass media; prizes and medals obtained by the mark; assessment results of intellectual property assessment organizations.
3. In case a well-known mark is recognized, leading to a decision to handle an infringement upon rights to such well-known mark according to Point d Clause 1 Article 129 of the Law on Intellectual Property or leading to a decision not to protect another mark according to Point i Clause 2 Article 74 of the Law on Intellectual Property, such well-known mark shall be recorded in the List of well-known marks kept at the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam for use as reference information in the work of establishment and protection of intellectual property rights.
Section 5. Madrid System Applications and Madrid System Processing
Article 89. Madrid applications

1. A Madrid application includes a Vietnamese origin Madrid application and a designated Vietnam Madrid application.
2. For a Vietnamese origin Madrid application, the applicant shall submit it through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and pay fees for carrying out procedures for international registration of marks of Vietnamese origin.
3. A Vietnamese origin Madrid application includes the following documents:
a) Declaration for international registration of a mark of Vietnamese origin, made according to Form No. 7 in Vietnamese in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) MM2 declaration, made according to the form of the International Bureau in English or French (2 declarations in paper form);
c) Mark samples identical to the marks in the application submitted in Vietnam (base application) or certificate of mark registration (base registration) (2 mark samples in paper form);
d) MM18 declaration in English if the application is designated by the United States (2 declarations in paper form).
dd) A written authorization in Vietnamese (if the application is submitted by a representative);
e) Other relevant documents (if any).
4. A Madrid application originating from Vietnam must satisfy both the form and content requirements as prescribed. The applicant must fully, accurately, and correctly fill in the information in the declaration, and agree with the information stated in the application for establishment or registration of the establishment.
5. For a Madrid application with Vietnam designated as the country of international registration or the later designation (in case of later designation), the date of filing in Vietnam and the priority date of the application (if any) shall be the later one.
Article 90. Processing of applications originating in Vietnam and related requests
1. After receiving a Madrid application originating from Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall appraise whether the application satisfies the requirements prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 89 of this Circular and carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the application contains errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant to correct such errors. If the applicant fails to correct the errors within 3 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification, the application shall be considered withdrawn;
b) In case the application is flawless or has flaws but the applicant has repaired them and satisfied requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify fees and charges that the applicant must pay directly to International Office, sign and send the application to International Office within 15 working days from the date of notification of fees and charges;
c) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the Madrid application originating from Vietnam shall be considered as the international registration date of such application in case the International Office receives the application within 02 months from the date specified on the receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the application is not complete for submission to the International Office within the above-mentioned time limit, the date on which the application is received at the International Office shall be considered as the international registration date.
2. After the Madrid application originating from Vietnam is submitted to the International Office, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant thereof and continue processing (in cooperation with the applicant if necessary) notifications and requests from the International Office or other matters related to the application (if any).
3. Requests for the following actions after the Madrid application originating from Vietnam is granted with an international registration number as prescribed below (expansion of protection territory), modification of name and address of the owner of the international registration, limitation of the list of goods and services, extension of validity of the international registration, designation of representative, change of representative, recognition of transfer of international registration, etc. may be directly made to the International Office or through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the application is submitted through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the applicant shall submit the following documents and pay the corresponding appraisal fee for the relevant request:
a) The written request form No. 08 in Vietnamese in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) The corresponding declaration form of the International Office (02 declarations in case the application is submitted in paper form);
c) The power of attorney in Vietnamese (in case the request is submitted through the representative);
d) Other relevant documents (if any).
4. After receiving the requests specified in Clause 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the application contains errors, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant to correct such errors. If the applicant fails to correct such errors within 03 months from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification, the application shall be considered as withdrawn;

b) In case the application contains no errors or errors have been corrected by the applicant and the application is satisfactory, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify fees to the applicant for direct payment to the International Bureau, sign the application and transfer it to the International Bureau within 10 working days from the date of issuance of the fee notification;
c) The date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the subsequent request for designation shall be considered as the subsequent date of international registration in case the International Bureau receives such request within 02 months from the date specified in the request receipt seal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. In case the request is not completed before being sent to the International Bureau within the above time limit, the date on which the request is received at the International Bureau shall be considered as the subsequent date of international registration.
5. In case of an application for extension of international registration submitted through the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the applicant shall submit it within 06 months before the expiration date of the international registration. In case of an application for extension of validity of international registration during the grace period, the application shall be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 01 month before the expiration date of the grace period.
Article 91. Processing of Madrid-based applications with designated Vietnam
1. After receiving a notification of the International Bureau of a Madrid-based application with designated Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conduct content appraisal of the application like that of a mark registration application filed under the national filing method, except for cases specified in clauses 3, 4 and 12 of this Article. Within 12 months from the date on which the International Bureau issues the notification, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall conclude the protectionability of the mark.
2. In case the mark satisfies the protection conditions prescribed by Vietnamese law, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Before the expiry of the 12-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to accept protection for an internationally registered mark in Vietnam, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (the Part of International Marks), and send it to the International Office for Protection Declaration;
b) The decision shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance.
The scope (volume) of protection shall be determined according to the contents requested in the international registration of the mark already recorded by the International Office and accepted by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
3. In cases where a mark fails to meet the protection conditions for part or all of goods or services, or where a protected mark has deficiencies in its international registration (lack of regulations on use of collective marks, certification marks, lack of photos or drawings showing three-dimensional perspective of the mark, application submission rights, list of vague goods and services, etc.), before the expiry of the 12-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a provisional refusal notice, clearly stating the contents (part or all of the list of goods and services subject to the refusal) and the reasons for the provisional refusal, and send such notice to the International Office.
4. In cases where a mark contains a non-competitive element that the applicant has not included in the application or has included but incorrectly, before the expiry of the 12-month period specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a provisional refusal notice and send such notice to the International Office.
5. Within 3 months from the date the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the provisional refusal notice specified in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article, the applicant may correct errors or oppose the intended refusal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
The correction of errors or opposition of the intended refusal shall be carried out according to the procedures applicable to mark registration applications filed under the national filing form, including regulations on the method of filing applications.
6. For a mark temporarily rejected under Clause 3 of this Article, upon expiration of the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article, if the applicant has corrected deficiencies and/or has a legitimate objection to such temporary rejection notice, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) Issue a decision to accept protection of the internationally registered mark in Vietnam with a protection scope (volume) corresponding to the goods or services meeting the protection conditions; record it in the National Register of Industrial Property (internally registered mark section); and send it to the International Office for protection declaration after the temporary rejection notice;
b) Publish the decision in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance.
7. For a mark temporarily rejected under Clause 3 of this Article, upon expiration of the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article, if the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies, does not have an objection or has an illegitimate objection to such temporary rejection notice, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the mark does not meet the protection conditions for a part of the list of goods or services, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article to the goods or services meeting the protection conditions (goods or services not mentioned in the temporary rejection notice);
b) In case the mark does not meet the protection conditions for the entire list of goods or services, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to reject acceptance of protection of the internationally registered mark in Vietnam and send it to the International Office for confirmation of rejection of the entire mark;
c) In case there are grounds to determine that the mark meets the protection conditions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article to the goods or services meeting the protection conditions.
8. For a mark temporarily rejected under Clause 4 of this Article, upon expiration of the 3-month period specified in Clause 5 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the following procedures:
a) In case the applicant does not have any objection or agrees with the contents stated in the notice of temporary refusal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article with the exclusion elements as stated in the notice of temporary refusal;
b) In case the applicant has a justifiable objection to the entire or part of the contents stated in the notice of temporary refusal, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Clause 6 of this Article with corresponding exclusion elements;
c) In case the applicant has an unjustifiable objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall apply similar procedures prescribed in Points a and b Clause 7 of this Article.
9. In case the international registration for a trademark is not extended or invalidated before the date of issuance of the decision on acceptance or refusal of protection in Vietnam, the international registration shall be considered as withdrawn.
10. Procedures for filing complaints and settling complaints against decisions prescribed in Point a Clause 2, Clauses 6, 7 and 8 of this Article shall be carried out similarly to those applicable to applications for registration of trademarks filed under the national procedure if there are grounds to believe that such decisions are issued inconsistently with the provisions of law on content and issuance order. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the results of complaint settlement. In case after obtaining the results of complaint settlement, part or all of the list of goods and services is rejected in the refusal decision and accepted for protection or there is a change in the exclusion elements (not protected separately), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send to the International Office the next decision affecting the protection of the trademark according to the form of the International Office regarding the corresponding contents.
11. From the date of international registration of a trademark accepted for protection in Vietnam, at the request of the trademark owner, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a certificate of protection of the trademark registered internationally in Vietnam, provided that the applicant pays fees and charges as prescribed.
12. From the date on which the Madrid application is published by the International Office in the Official Gazette before the date of issuance of the decision to grant protection or 12 months from the date on which the International Office notifies the application with designation of Vietnam, whichever comes first, if the third party has opinions about the Madrid application with designation of Vietnam, these opinions shall be regarded as reference information during the processing of the application.
13. The contents of publication specified at Point b Clause 2, Point b Clause 6 of this Article include basic information written in the corresponding decision (including decision number and date) and other information specified in Clause 3 Article 72 of this Circular.
Article 92. Conversion of international mark registration into applications filed under national procedures
1. In case of international registration of a mark in Vietnam of the mark owner being an individual or organization that is a member of the Madrid Protocol which is invalidated according to Article 6 of the Madrid Protocol, such person may file an application for conversion to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to register for protection for such mark for part or all of goods or services included in the list of goods and services already recorded in the international registration of the mark but is invalidated according to Article 9 quinquies of the Madrid Protocol. The application for conversion of mark registration shall be considered valid if it meets the following conditions:
a) The application is filed within 03 months from the date on which the corresponding international registration that is invalidated is recorded in the international register;
b) The list of goods and services included in the application for conversion of mark registration has never been subject of the decision to reject, terminate or cancel in Vietnam;
c) The application is made using Form No. 09 in Appendix I of this Circular (in which the list of goods and services in Vietnamese in the conversion application must be smaller than or equal to the list of goods and services that are invalidated in the corresponding international registration);
d) The application meets all other requirements regarding form for the mark registration application according to regulations of Vietnam;
dd) The applicant shall fully pay fees and charges as prescribed for a mark registration application filed under the national filing format; in the case specified at Point b, Clause 2 of this Article, the applicant is not required to pay the application publication fee or the lookup fee for application appraisal.
The date of filing of an application for conversion of a mark shall be the international registration date or the later designated date (in case of designation after Vietnam). In case of international registration enjoying priority rights under an international treaty, the application for conversion of a mark shall have the corresponding priority right recorded, unless there are grounds to reject it.
2. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall appraise applications for conversion of marks according to regulations on conversion conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article and the following principles:
a) For formal elements already accepted by the International Office in the corresponding international registration, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall not re-appraise them, except for cases where the application has deficiencies (lack of regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks, lack of photos or drawings showing the three-dimensional perspective of marks, etc.). The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant a mark registration certificate in case the application fails to meet the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article;
b) For applications for conversion of marks from international registrations already accepted for protection in Vietnam, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall not re-appraise their contents. In case the application meets the conversion conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures to decide to grant a protection title, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property, and publish the decision in the Industrial Property Official Gazette like for applications filed under the national filing format;
c) For valid applications for conversion of marks other than the cases specified at Point b of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out procedures to determine that the applications are valid, publish the applications, appraise their contents and carry out subsequent procedures like for applications for registration of marks filed under the national filing format.
Chapter VI
                          HANDLING OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Section 1. APPLICATION FOR REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATIONS
Article 93. Applications for registration of geographical indications
A geographical indication registration application includes the following documents:
1. Declaration for registration of a geographical indication, made according to Form No. 5 in Appendix I to this Circular.
2. Description of peculiar characteristics and quality, reputation of products bearing geographical indications and specific elements of natural conditions creating such peculiar characteristics and quality, reputation of those products (hereinafter referred to as description of peculiar characteristics).
3. Map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication.
4. Geographical indication forms in case of geographical indications which are not words (05 forms in case applications are submitted in paper form).
5. Documents proving that a geographical indication is being protected in the country of origin, if it is a foreign geographical indication.
6. Presentation documents on conditions for use and method of presentation of geographical indications to ensure the ability to distinguish between geographical indications in case registered geographical indications are homonym geographical indications.
7. Power of attorney (if application is submitted by representative).
8. Other documents supporting the application (if any).
Article 94. Requirements for applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Documents of an application for registration of a geographical indication shall meet corresponding specific requirements prescribed in clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of this article and general requirements regarding form as follows:
a) Documents shall be presented in vertical orientation (except drawings, diagrams and tables which may be presented in horizontal orientation) on one sheet of paper of A4 size (210 mm x 297 mm), except for documents being maps of geographical areas which may be presented on one sheet of paper of A3 size (420 mm x 297 mm) with margins of 20 mm on all sides, using Times New Roman font, font size not smaller than 13, except for supplementary documents whose origin is not intended to be included in the application;
b) Documents consisting of multiple pages shall have page numbers written in Arabic numerals;
c) Documents shall be typed or printed with opaque ink, presented clearly and cleanly, without erasing or modifying. Documents shall bear signatures and seals; if documents consist of at least 2 pages, signatures shall be appended to each page or every two adjacent pages shall bear the seal of the applicant/issuing authority (if any), except for electronic documents bearing digital signatures. Before receiving the application, if detecting insignificant spelling errors, the applicant may correct them and shall append signatures (and affix seals, if any) at the correction positions; signatures and seals shall not obscure the contents of documents.

d) Documents of the application submitted electronically under the electronic filing system must be in “.pdf” format; the name of the electronic document must correspond to the name of the type of paper as prescribed; the individual signing documents of the geographical indication registration application may use a digital signature to sign directly on the electronic document or directly sign on the paper document and scan it as color photos for the case of submission through the VneID application;
dd) Terms used in the application must be consistent and common (not use local language, rare words, or self-created words). Signs, measurement units, electronic fonts, spelling rules used in the application must comply with national standards of Vietnam;
e) Documents and papers for transactions between the applicant and the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam must be made in Vietnamese, except for documents under Clauses 7 and 8 Article 93 of this Circular which may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese when requested;
g) The declaration and other documents of the application must ensure full compliance with mandatory and consistent information.
2. The geographical indication registration application must ensure consistency as prescribed in Article 100 of the Law on Intellectual Property. Each application may register only one geographical indication for a product determined by its nature, characteristics, and relationship with the geographical area, regardless of its form of existence or processing level.
3. The declaration for geographical indication registration must be fully filled out with information as required at appropriate places in the Declaration.
4. The geographical indication sample (for cases of geographical indications that are not words) must be presented clearly with each sample size not larger than 80mm x 80mm and not smaller than 20mm x 20mm.
5. The description of peculiar characteristics must include the following contents:
a) The detailed content description, including the following main information:

a1) Information on geographical indications and products bearing such geographical indications;

a2) Characteristics and specific quality of products bearing geographical indications determined by geographical conditions - determined by perceptible, qualitative, physical, chemical and biological quantitative indicators. These indicators must be identifiable and verifiable by technical means or experts according to sampling standards and testing methods;

and/or

a3) Reputation of products bearing geographical indications determined by geographical conditions through widespread knowledge about such products and selection of such products among relevant consumers, which can be verified;

and

a4) Geographical area;

a5) Geographical conditions creating characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications, including climatic, hydrological, geological, topographical and ecological factors and other natural conditions; skills and techniques of producers, including traditional production processes of local areas (including one, several or all stages from raw material production and processing to product creation and may also include product packaging if such packaging affects the characteristics, quality and reputation of products), where such processes are elements creating and maintaining characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications, including clear and detailed information that can be verified (if there is any secret or technical secret not yet disclosed or widely known outside the locality, the applicant may refuse to provide detailed information on such secret or technique if he/she does not commit to keep such information confidential at his/her request);

and

a6) Organic relationship between characteristics, specific quality and reputation of products bearing geographical indications mentioned in points a2 and a3 of this clause with geographical conditions mentioned in point a5 of this clause;

a7) Information on the mechanism for self-inspection of characteristics and specific quality of products;
b) Summary section, including summary of specific characteristics, quality, reputation of products, geographical conditions contributing to specific characteristics, quality, and reputation of products, and organic relationship between specific characteristics, quality, and reputation of products bearing geographical indications and geographical conditions.
6. Description of specific characteristics according to Clause 5 of this Article must be accompanied by documents proving that information on characteristics, quality/reputation is well-grounded and accurate (test results, research, investigation, etc.). Characteristics and quality mentioned in the description must be distinctive characteristics and quality of products of all manufacturers in the corresponding geographical area (determined based on collection, statistics, analysis, and synthesis of relevant information and data from all manufacturers in the geographical area or by appropriate sampling methods to ensure representation of all manufacturers in the geographical area). The applicant shall be responsible before the law for the accuracy of information stated in the description of specific characteristics and attached documents.

The description of specific characteristics shall be considered well-grounded and accurate when physical, chemical, biological indicators of products bearing geographical indications are analyzed and tested at recognized testing organizations in accordance with laws on technical standards and regulations and other relevant laws related to the recognition scope, including corresponding tests.
In cases where physical, chemical, biological indicators of products cannot be carried out at recognized testing organizations due to unmet technical conditions (no standardized testing methods available, no recognized testing organizations suitable for the type or specific characteristics of the products, etc.), the applicant may use analysis and testing results from unrecognized organizations or other analysis methods but must provide documents proving sampling methods, analysis, testing, implementation conditions, scientific and practical basis of the methods, assessment of reliability of results, and other relevant documents to ensure scientific, objective, and honest results.
7. The map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication shall fully show information enough to determine accurately the geographical area which is suitable for natural conditions creating particular characteristics and quality and reputation of products. For a Vietnamese geographical indication, the map must be certified by the People's Committee of the province or centrally-run city where the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication exists and may be enclosed with a document describing such geographical area.
8. Presentation documents on conditions for use and presentation of geographical indications in order to ensure the ability to distinguish between geographical indications (in case of homonym geographical indications) shall specify the method of presenting and indicating information about geographical indications on products (product packaging, business facilities, transaction documents) in actual production and business activities of products bearing geographical indications in order to clearly determine the geographical origin of the real products and ensure consumers are not confused that products bearing such geographical indications originate from other geographical areas, for example, specifying the geographical origin as the name of a country/province or city next to the geographical indication or using other identification signs accompanied by the geographical indication such as images. logo/icon or other rules for labeling goods, etc.
Article 95. Receipt of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. A geographical indication registration application shall be accepted when it fully satisfies the following conditions:
a) Having all minimum documents, including: a1) Declaration for registration; a2) Description of peculiar characteristics;
b) The applicant has paid fees and charges as prescribed.
2. In case an application fully satisfies the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and verify the submission date and application number of such application and return a receipt note to the applicant, which contains information about the submission date, application number, and results of the verification of the list of documents included in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official.
For electronic applications, if received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall return an application receipt to the applicant, which includes information about the submission date, the application number, and the results of the inspection of the list of documents in the application, clearly stating the full name and signature of the receiving official through the electronic application submission system.
3. In cases where the application does not meet the conditions specified in Clause 1 of this Article, it will be rejected (if the application is submitted by post or as an electronic application through the electronic application submission system, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam will send a notification of rejection to the applicant, clearly stating the reasons for rejection). For applications rejected by post, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam is not required to return the application documents to the applicant, except for the original submitted for comparison.
Section 2. AUTHENTICATION OF REGISTRATION FORMS OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICES
Article 96. Format appraisal of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Format appraisal of an application for registration of a geographical indication according to Article 109 of the Law on Intellectual Property means the examination of compliance with regulations on the format of the application, thereby concluding whether the application is considered valid.
2. An application for registration of a geographical indication shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) There are grounds to confirm that the applicant does not have the registration right as prescribed in Article 88 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) The application is submitted in violation of the provisions of Article 89 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
c) There are grounds to confirm that the subject matter stated in the application is clearly not protected by the State as prescribed in Articles 8 and 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
d) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed (including cases where the application submission fee, the application disclosure fee, the application appraisal fee, and the information lookup fee for appraisal have not been fully paid);
dd) The application does not meet the requirements regarding form specified in Article 94 of this Circular (the application has errors), including: failing to meet the requirements regarding the quantity of one of the mandatory documents; failing to meet the requirements regarding the presentation form; the declaration does not clearly state the product bearing the geographical indication; information about the applicant or the organization managing the geographical indication in the documents is inconsistent or erased or not certified as prescribed; the declaration does not contain sufficient information about the applicant, the organization managing the geographical indication, the representative, or does not include the signature and/or seal (if any) of the applicant or the representative; etc.;
e) There is no valid written authorization (if the application is submitted through a representative).
3. In case there are grounds (information or evidence) to suspect the authenticity of the information stated in the application or the information stated in the application is unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request the applicant to submit documents to verify or clarify such information within 2 months from the date of notification issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. After the above time limit, if the applicant fails to submit additional documents to meet the requirements, the application will continue to be appraised based on the documents included in the application.
4. Notification of the results of the formal examination shall be carried out as follows:
a) If the application falls under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send a notification of the results of the formal examination to the applicant, clearly stating the intended refusal to issue the Certificate of geographical indication registration, the reason for refusal or errors in the application, and impose a time limit of 2 months from the notification date for the applicant to provide their opinion or correct errors;
b) If the applicant fails to correct errors or unsatisfactory corrections or does not have any objection or provides unsatisfactory objection by the end of the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant a certificate of registered geographical indication and send it to the applicant;
c) If the application does not fall under one of the cases specified in Clause 2 of this Article (or the applicant has made satisfactory corrections or provided a satisfactory explanation), the application shall be determined as valid and transferred for publication and content appraisal as prescribed.
5. Weekly, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall publish on its website a list of geographical indication registration applications that are transferred for publication, clearly stating the application number, submission date, and publication date of each application.
6. The date of submission of a geographical indication registration application is determined as follows:
a) The application submission date is the date on which the application is received by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam according to Clause 2 Article 95 of this Circular. In case the application is submitted through the electronic application system after the administrative hour or on a holiday, public holiday, or Tet holiday as prescribed, the application submission date according to this Point is the next working day;
b) For a geographical indication submitted under international requests, the application submission date is the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives an official written request from the party participating in the negotiation of the international treaty, including a brief description of the particular characteristics.
Article 97. Publication of geographical indication registration applications
1. A valid geographical indication registration application shall be published according to Article 110 of the Law on Intellectual Property and this Article.
2. Contents of a geographical indication registration application disclosed in the Industrial Property Official Gazette include information about the applicant, the geographical indication management organization, the representative (if any); the application number and submission date; information about the geographical indication, products bearing the geographical indication, geographical area, and summary specified at Point b, Clause 5, Article 94 of this Circular, and other information (if any).
3. The description of peculiar characteristics shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Section 3. CONTENT APPRAISAL OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION REGISTRATION APPLICATIONS
Article 98. Content appraisal of geographical indication registration applications
1. Content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application according to Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property means assessment of the subject matter stated in the application for protection eligibility and determination of the corresponding protection scope according to the provisions of this Article.
2. Assessment of the eligibility for grant of protection titles for a geographical indication registration application includes:
a) Assessment of the conformity of the subject matter stated in the geographical indication registration application with the requirements for grant of the Certificate of geographical indication registration as prescribed in Article 100 of this Circular;
b) Assessment of the subject matter stated in the geographical indication registration application according to the protection conditions specified in Article 101 of this Circular.
3. The assessment of the eligibility for a title of protection shall be completed after assessing the conformity of the subject matter stated in the application and the subject matter stated in the application with all the protection conditions and there are sufficient grounds to conclude that such subject matter is or is not conformable, does not satisfy or satisfies the protection conditions, specifically:

a) A reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy one or several or all of the protection conditions; or b) No reason is found to conclude that the subject does not satisfy at least one protection condition.
4. Based on the results of assessment of the eligibility for issuance of protection titles prescribed in clauses 2 and 3 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall determine the corresponding proposed protection scope.
5. During the process of content appraisal, if the application does not fully disclose the nature of the subject matter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may request the applicant to provide explanations for the application and information within the scope of the nature of the subject matter stated in the application to fully disclose the nature of the subject matter and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to rectify the deficiencies.
Article 99. Minimum information sources
The minimum sources of information for content appraisal of a geographical indication registration application include:
1. Marks currently protected in Vietnam for products identical or similar to products bearing a geographical indication, with the protection starting date earlier than the date of filing an application for registration of such geographical indication, including marks protected under international treaties to which Vietnam is a contracting party.
2. Marks in applications for registration of marks with an earlier submission date or priority date in Vietnam for products identical or similar to products bearing a geographical indication.
3. Marks shall be recognized by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam as well-known marks.
4. Geographical indications currently under protection in Vietnam for products identical with those bearing such geographical indications.
Article 100. Assessment of conformity of subject matters stated in applications for registration of geographical indications Subject matters stated in applications for registration of geographical indications shall be deemed unconformable with the type of geographical indication protection title if such subject matters are not visible signs used to indicate the geographical origin of products from specific regions, localities, territories or countries according to Clause 22 Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
Article 101. Assessment of conditions for protection of geographical indications
1. The assessment of geographical indications according to protection conditions shall comply with the following regulations:
a) Subject matters stated in applications shall satisfy the conditions specified in Article 79 of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically, they must fully prove the following conditions: a1) There exists a geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication stated in the application; a2) Products originate from the geographical area mentioned above; a3) Such products have particular characteristics, quality and/or reputation determined by the geographical conditions of the geographical area mentioned above according to Article 82 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Subject matters stated in applications do not fall into the cases specified in Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In particular, a geographical indication shall be deemed misleading consumers about the true geographical origin of products bearing such geographical indication according to Clause 4 Article 80 of the Law on Intellectual Property if such geographical indication is identical or similar to the point of confusion with a known sign being the geographical origin of goods or services, causing relevant consumers in the Vietnamese territory to misunderstand the actual origin of goods or services;
c) The assessment of conditions for protection of geographical indications according to Points a and b of this Clause shall be carried out based on information provided by applicants and information found from the minimum information sources specified in Article 99 of this Circular;
d) Depending on the complexity of assessing the protection conditions specified at Point a of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may consult independent consulting experts, the Geographical Indication Consultation Council or conduct additional surveys.
The Geographical Indication Consultation Council shall be established and operate on the basis of the principles prescribed in decisions of the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Independent consulting experts shall be selected from the List of Industrial Property Consulting Experts prepared by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and other sources (in case no suitable expert is available in such List).
2. During the process of assessing the protection conditions of a geographical indication, if a mark identical or similar to the geographical indication is found, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the owner of the mark for their opinion as prescribed in Article 112 or Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. During the process of assessing the protection conditions of a geographical indication, if there are grounds to determine that the geographical indication in the application for registration is homonymic with the protected geographical indication as prescribed in Clause 22a Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual Property or homonymic with the geographical indication in another received application for registration of a geographical indication, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant and request them to submit explanatory documents on the conditions for use and the method of presentation of the geographical indication to ensure the distinguishability between geographical indications. At the same time, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the owner of the protected geographical indication and request them to provide information on the method of presentation of the geographical indication to serve as a basis for consideration and assessment of the distinguishability among homonymic geographical indications. In case two or more geographical indications in the submitted applications are determined to be homonymic with each other, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall request all applicants to submit explanatory documents on the conditions for use and the method of presentation of the geographical indication for consideration and decision on protection.
In necessary cases, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall work with all applicants and owners of homonymic geographical indications to clarify the conditions for use and the method of presentation of the geographical indication before deciding to grant protection titles.
Article 102. Grant and refusal to grant certificates of registered geographical indications
1. An application for registration of a geographical indication shall be refused to grant a certificate of registered geographical indication in the cases specified at Points a, b and dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. An application for registration of a geographical indication shall be granted a certificate of registered geographical indication when it satisfies the provisions of Article 118 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
3. For an application for registration of a geographical indication falling into the cases specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) If the subject matter stated in the application satisfies the protection conditions and the application does not fall into the cases specified at Point b or dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended grant of a certificate of registered geographical indication and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for issuance of the certificate of registered geographical indication, the disclosure fee and the registration fee for the decision on issuance of the certificate of registered geographical indication;
b) If the subject matter stated in the application fails to satisfy the protection conditions or falls into the cases specified at Point b or dd, Clause 1, Article 117 of the Law on Intellectual Property or satisfies the protection conditions but the application contains deficiencies, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, clearly stating the intended refusal to grant a certificate of registered geographical indication, the reasons for refusal or deficiencies of the application and impose a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to provide opinions;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or does not have any objection within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 15 working days from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to grant a certificate of registered geographical indication.
d) In case the applicant corrects deficiencies or provides opinions within the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, within 50 days from the date of receipt of the correction document or opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall carry out the content appraisal as follows:

d1) If the applicant has corrected deficiencies satisfactorily or provided justifiable explanations for the content appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of content appraisal results, stating the intended issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to pay the fee for the geographical indication registration certificate. fees for publication and registration of decisions on issuance of geographical indication registration certificates; d/ If the applicant fails to meet the requirements or makes groundless objections, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the issuance of the geographical indication registration certificate;
dd) If the applicant submits all and on time fees and charges prescribed in point a, d1 of this clause upon expiry of the time limit specified in point a, d1 of this clause, within 01 month from the date of submission of all fees and charges as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to grant a geographical indication registration certificate;
e) If the applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges within the time limit specified at Points a and d1 of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on refusal to grant a certificate of registered geographical indication within 15 working days from the date of expiry of the corresponding time limit.
4. The decision on grant of a certificate of registration of a geographical indication shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days after its issuance. Published contents shall include information in the decision on grant of a certificate of registration of a geographical indication (including decision number and date) and contents specified in Clause 2, Article 97 of this Circular.
Description of peculiar characteristics shall be published on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
5. The geographical indication registration certificate made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued in both electronic and paper forms (if the applicant requests a paper version).
The copy of the geographical indication registration certificate made according to the form specified in Appendix I of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam if the applicant requests such copy, which is stated in the declaration for geographical indication registration.

Section 4. PROCESSING OF OTHER REQUESTS RELATED TO THE REGISTRATION FORGE OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICES
Article 103. Processing of objections to applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Within 03 months from the date on which an application for registration of a geographical indication is published, any third party has the right to object to the grant of a protection title for such application and must pay fees and charges.
An objection must be made in writing in Vietnamese, enclosed with documents or sources of information for verification. Documents or sources of information may be made in other languages but must be translated into Vietnamese upon request.
2. Where the objection to the application for registration of a geographical indication satisfies the provisions of clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall receive and process the objection in accordance with the following regulations, except for the case specified in clause 3 of this Article:
a) Within 01 month from the date on which the objection is received, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of the objection and impose a time limit of 01 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide a written response;
b) After receiving feedback from applicants within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of such feedback and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to provide a written response to such feedback;
c) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall organize a dialogue between the objector and the applicant to clarify issues subject to objection if necessary or at the request of both parties;
d) Based on information, evidence, arguments provided by the parties, the dialogue results (if any) as prescribed in points a, b, and c of this clause and documents included in the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the objection and notify the objector of the results of processing the objection together with the content appraisal results of the corresponding application, except for the case specified in point dd of this clause;
dd) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector of the suspension of processing the objection regarding the withdrawal of the geographical indication registration application according to Article 116 of the Law on Intellectual Property within 1 month from the date on which the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues the notification of acceptance of application withdrawal.
3. In cases where the objection of the objector is related to the right to register a geographical indication, the objection shall be processed as follows:
a) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the objector to file a lawsuit petition to a competent court in accordance with the law on civil procedures and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the objector to submit a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit;
b) Upon expiration of the time limit specified in point a of this clause, if the objector fails to submit a copy of the notice of acceptance of the lawsuit, the objection shall be considered withdrawn, and the geographical indication registration application shall continue to be processed as if there is no objection;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives a copy of the court's notice of acceptance of the lawsuit within the time limit specified in point a of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of the geographical indication registration application pending the dispute settlement results of the court, except for the case specified in point dd clause 2 of this Article. The processing of the application shall continue immediately after the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives the legally effective judgment or decision of the court.
4. The time limit for the applicant to provide opinions on objection and the time limit for the objector to respond to the applicant's opinions as prescribed in points a and b clause 2 of this Article shall not be included in the time limit for content appraisal of the application.
Article 104. Amendment to geographical indication registration applications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of geographical indication registration, the applicant may take the initiative or based on the notice of appraisal results to amend or supplement documents in the geographical indication registration application.
2. Requests for amendment to geographical indication registration applications include the following documents:
a) In case of amendment to the application by the applicant, the request for amendment must be made using Form No. 10 in Appendix I of this Circular;
b) In case of amendment to the application based on the notice of appraisal results of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the amendment shall be made in a written response clearly stating the amended contents;
c) The applicant may request amendment with the same content related to multiple geographical indication registration applications in one declaration or one written request for amendment;
d) In case of amendments related to description of peculiar characteristics or maps of geographical areas, applicants shall submit amended descriptions of peculiar characteristics and maps of geographical areas;

dd) In case of amendments to name or address of country code of applicants or organizations managing geographical indications, applicants shall submit certification documents (original copies or certified true copies) or legal documents (certified true copies) proving the changes (decisions on change of name or address; certificates of enterprise registration recording the changes in name or address, etc.) unless the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam can take advantage of information available in databases announced by database owners. In case information cannot be taken advantage of or is not fully and accurately taken advantage of, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall send documents requesting individuals and organizations to supplement application components and clearly state reasons. Time for requesting and supplementing applications shall not be included in the time limit for handling administrative procedures;
e) In case of change of industrial property representatives, applicants shall submit documents declaring termination of authorization for old industrial property representatives and change of new industrial property representatives as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. Applicants shall pay an appraisal fee for amendment requests for each amendment and a fee for disclosure of information about amendments to the application if amendments are required to be disclosed according to Point a Clause 5 of this Article, except for amendments to correct errors made by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
4. Amendments to geographical indication registration applications shall not expand protection scope (volume) stated in the applications and shall not change nature of geographical indication subject matters stated in the applications.
5. In case amendments to applications are made by applicants, amendment requests shall be processed as follows:
a) If the request for amendment or supplementation of an application satisfies the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of such request (except where the amendment or supplementation is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to grant a protection title due to the invalidity of the application in terms of form).
The amended or supplemented contents shall be published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette in case the request for amendment or supplementation of information related to the application that is valid in terms of form includes the description of specific characteristics and products bearing the geographical indication;
b) If the request for amendment or supplementation of an application does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept such request, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 1 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide opposing opinions. Upon expiration of the set time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no opposing opinions are provided, or provides inappropriate opposing opinions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the request for amendment or supplementation of the application;
c) In case the request for amendment or supplementation of an application relates to the description of specific characteristics or geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication filed after the notice of intended issuance of the certificate of registered geographical indication is issued, the application for registration of the geographical indication must be re-examined and the applicant must pay a fee as prescribed.
6. In case the applicant makes amendments or supplements to the application based on the notice of appraisal results of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, the acceptance or refusal to accept the applicant's request for amendment or supplementation shall be carried out by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the notice of appraisal results or the corresponding decision during the processing of the application for registration of the geographical indication.
7. In case the request for amendment or supplementation of the application includes both the content of amendment or supplementation made proactively by the applicant and the content of amendment or supplementation of the application based on the notice of appraisal results, the request for amendment or supplementation must be made into a Declaration according to the provisions of point a clause 2 of this Article. The handling of the request for amendment or supplementation in this case shall comply with the provisions of clause 5 of this Article.
Article 105. Recording of changes in applicants for geographical indication registration
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of geographical indication registration, an applicant may request recording of changes in the applicant on the basis of inheritance or according to a decision of a competent authority.
2. A request for recording of changes in an applicant for geographical indication registration shall satisfy the following requirements:
a) In case of recording of changes in the applicant due to inheritance or according to a decision of a competent authority, the applicant shall submit a declaration made according to Form No. 11 in Appendix I of this Circular and documents proving the inheritance, rights and obligations arising from consolidation, merger, division, separation or other changes in the organization of the applicant, or according to a decision of a court or another competent authority.
b) The applicant shall pay fees and charges as prescribed.
3. Requests for recording of changes in applicants shall be processed as follows:

a) If the request for recording of changes in the applicant satisfies the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of recording of changes in the applicant and publish it in the Official Gazette of Industrial Property (except for cases where the amendment or supplementation of the application is made before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam publishes the application or refuses to issue the protection title due to the invalidation of the application).
b) If the request for recording of changes in the applicant does not satisfy the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to record the changes in the applicant and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide objections. Upon expiration of the imposed time limit, if the applicant fails to correct errors or provides unsatisfactory corrections, no objection or unsatisfactory objection, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to record the changes in the applicant.
Article 106. Withdrawal of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues or refuses to issue a certificate of registration of a geographical indication, the applicant may declare withdrawal of the application for registration of the geographical indication.
2. The withdrawal of an application for registration of a geographical indication shall be made by the applicant or his/her authorized representative in writing. In case the application is filed through a representative, the letter of attorney must clearly state the authorization for withdrawal of the application or enclose a command specifying the number of the application subject to withdrawal.
3. Within 02 months from the date of receipt of a written declaration on withdrawal of the application for registration of a geographical indication, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal request as follows:
a) If the withdrawal request satisfies the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of the application and terminate the processing of the application and record the withdrawal in the application file. The withdrawn application for registration of the geographical indication cannot be re-registered.
b) If the withdrawal request does not satisfy the provisions of clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal request and impose a time limit of 01 month from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to remedy deficiencies;
If the prescribed time limit expires but the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are insufficient, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the withdrawal request.
Article 107. Re-examination of applications for registration of geographical indications
1. The re-examination of a geographical indication registration application shall be carried out by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the following cases:
a) Re-examination of a geographical indication registration application due to a written objection from a third party against the intended issuance of a certificate of geographical indication registration in the notice of content appraisal results, together with legitimate evidence proving that the objection is due to a force majeure event or objective obstacle, making it impossible to object within the time limit prescribed in Article 112a of the Law on Intellectual Property;
b) Re-examination of an application after a request for amendment or supplementation of the application is made, after notifying the content appraisal results which state the intended issuance of a certificate of geographical indication registration as prescribed at Point c, Clause 5, Article 104 of this Circular.
2. The re-examination of a geographical indication registration application shall be carried out as follows:
a) The time limit for re-examination of a geographical indication registration application shall comply with Clause 3, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
For complex cases involving multiple facts requiring verification or expert opinions, the re-examination time limit may be extended but shall not exceed the initial examination time limit prescribed in Clause 2, Article 119 of the Law on Intellectual Property.
b) The contents of re-examination shall comply with corresponding provisions of Articles 98 to 101 of this Circular;
c) For each case specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the re-examination of a geographical indication registration application may only be carried out once by a third party.
3. After the re-examination results are available, the application for registration of a geographical indication shall be processed as follows:
a) For the case specified at Point a, Clause 1 of this Article, based on the re-examination results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue processing the application in accordance with the provisions of this Circular;
b) For the case specified at Point b, Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the application in accordance with the provisions of Article 102 of this Circular.
Chapter VII
REQUESTS FOR REGISTRATION OF INTERNATIONAL PROTECTION TITLES AND PROCESSING OF REQUESTS FOR REGISTRATION OF INTERNATIONAL PROTECTION TITLES

Article 108. Maintenance of validity of invention patents/utility solutions
1. A dossier of request for maintenance of validity of an invention patent/utility solution includes the following documents:
a) Declaration, made according to Form No. 01 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b) The written authorization (in case the request is submitted through a representative);
2. The request for maintenance of validity of an invention patent/utility solution and the fee for examination of the request for maintenance of validity, the validity maintenance fee, the fee for use of the invention patent/utility solution, the registration fee and the publication fee shall be paid to the Intellectual Property Office before the expiry date. This request may be filed after the above-mentioned time limit but not more than 6 months from the expiry date of the previous validity period and the protection title holder shall pay a fee for each month of delay in accordance with the law on fees and charges.
3. A request for maintenance of validity of an invention patent/utility solution shall be considered invalid if it falls into one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all the documents specified in Clause 1 of this Article or the documents are invalid;
b) The applicant fails to pay all fees and charges as prescribed;
c) The request for maintenance of validity is filed beyond the time limit prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;
4. Applications for maintenance of validity shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of a valid application, within 1 month from the receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office shall issue a notice of maintenance of validity of the protection title, record it in the National Register of Industrial Property and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance of the notice;
b) In case of an unsatisfactory application, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to maintain the application, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide feedback;
c) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies within the time limit specified in point b of this clause, does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection, within one month from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to maintain validity.
d) In addition to the cases specified in point c of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to maintain validity in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired according to regulations at the time of submission of the application for validity maintenance; d2) The protection title was invalidated at the time of submission of the application or during the processing of the application.
5. In case the applicant submits a written request for withdrawal of the validity maintenance request, within 1 month from the receipt of such a document, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal of the validity maintenance request as follows:
a) In case of valid withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal of the request and terminate the processing of the request for maintenance of validity. The withdrawn request for maintenance of validity cannot be restored.
b) In case the written request for withdrawal of validity is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to remedy deficiencies or remedial measures are insufficient, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the withdrawal of the request for maintenance of validity.
Article 109. Extension of validity of industrial design patents and marks registration certificates
1. An industrial design patent may be extended for up to two consecutive times, each time for 5 years. In case an industrial design is protected by several schemes, the patent may be extended for all or some of them, including a basic scheme.
2. A certificate of registered mark may be extended for up to ten consecutive years for the whole or part of the list of goods and services.
3. An application may request extension of validity for one or more industrial designs; one or more certificates of registered marks.
An application for validity extension shall include the following documents:
a/ Declaration, made according to Form No. 2 in Annex II to this Circular;

b/ Original copy of the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark (if the protection title is issued in paper form and it is requested to record the extension in the protection title);
c/ Power of attorney (if the request is filed through a representative). For cases of extension only for some schemes or parts of the list of goods and services, there must be information on the authorization for extension with corresponding scope.
4. Applications for extension of validity of industrial design patents or certificates of registered marks and fees for examination of validity extension requests, validity extension charges for protection titles, use charges for protection titles, registration charges and publication charges for validity extension decisions shall be submitted to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 6 months counting to the date of expiry of the industrial design patents or certificates of registered marks. Requests for extension may be filed after the above-said time limit but not more than 6 months after the industrial design patents or certificates of registered marks expire, provided that the protection title holders pay charges for each month of delay under the law on charges and fees.
5. A dossier of request for extension of validity shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 3 of this Article or contains invalid documents;
b) The applicant fails to fully pay the fee or charge as prescribed;
c) Request for extension of validity of submission not within the prescribed time limit as specified in Clause 4 of this Article;
d) Lack of information confirming that the protection title holder authorizes only the extension for certain arrangements or parts of the list of goods and services (in case of submission by a representative).
6. Applications for extension of validity shall be processed as follows:
a) In case of a valid application, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on extension of validity, record it in the industrial design patent or trademark registration certificate (if requested), register it and publish the decision on extension of validity in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance;
b) In case of an unsatisfactory application, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to extend validity, clearly stating the reasons and setting a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide feedback;
c) If the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies within the time limit specified in point b of this clause, does not have any objection or has an unjustifiable objection, within one month from the end of such time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse extension of validity.
d) In addition to the cases specified in point c of this clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse extension in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired at the time of submission of the application for extension; d2) The protection title was cancelled at the time of submission of the application or during the processing of the application.
7. In case the applicant submits a written request for withdrawal of the validity extension, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of the request for withdrawal of maintenance of validity as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 108 of this Circular.
8. After completing the procedures for extension, if the owner of an industrial design patent or a certificate of registered mark requests the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to record the decision on validity extension in the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark, he/she shall carry out procedures for amendment of the industrial design patent or certificate of registered mark and pay fees and charges as prescribed.
Article 110. Amendment of titles of protection
1. Owners of protection titles, organizations and individuals exercising the right to register geographical indications, and geographical indication management organizations may request amendments to the following information in protection titles, provided that they pay a fee for appraisal of requests for amendments to protection titles, and registration fees and publication fees for decisions on recording of amendments to protection titles.
a) Changes in the name and address of the owner of the protection title; the name, address and nationality of the author of the invention, industrial design or layout design;
b) Changing the owner of a protection title (transferring ownership due to inheritance, acquisition, merger, division, separation, consolidation, joint venture, association, establishment of a new legal entity under the same ownership, changing business form or as decided by the Court or other competent authorities);
c) Amend descriptions of geographical indications or geographical areas corresponding to such geographical indications, regulations on use of collective marks or regulations on use of certification marks. In this case, the protection title holder shall pay an additional fee for content appraisal.
2. The protection title holder may request narrowing of the scope of protection according to Clause 3 Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, provided that he/she pays a fee for appraisal of the request for amendment to the protection title, a fee for appraisal of the request for narrowing of the scope of protection, and a registration fee and a fee for publication of the decision on amendment to the protection title in the following cases:
a) Requesting reduction of one or several independent or dependent points included in the protection request stated in the invention patent or utility solution patent;
b) Requesting removal of one or several industrial designs or products included in the set of products stated in the industrial design patent;
c) Requesting reduction of one or several goods, services or group of goods or services included in the list of goods or services stated in the certificate of mark registration or removing minor details being excluded elements (not separately protected) without affecting the distinguishability of the mark stated in the certificate of mark registration.
3. A dossier requesting amendment specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration requesting amendment, made according to Form No. 03 in Appendix II of this Circular, clearly stating the request for amendment. One declaration requesting amendment may request amendment to multiple protection titles if they have the same amended contents, provided that the applicant pays fees as prescribed for each protection title;
b) Original copy of the protection title in case the protection title is issued in paper form;
c) Documents certifying the change of name and address (original or certified true copy); decision on change of name and address; certificate of enterprise registration which records the change of name and address; other legal documents proving the change of name and address (original or certified true copy) if the content requested for modification is name and address;
d) Documents proving the transfer of ownership as prescribed in point b clause 1 of this Article (original or certified true copy), if the request for modification of the owner of the protection title is made;
dd) Photographs or drawings of the industrial design which have been modified if the request for modification of the industrial design protection title is made (5 sets if the application is submitted in paper form); description of the geographical indication, map of the geographical area corresponding to the geographical indication which has been modified if the request for modification of the protection title of the geographical indication is made (2 copies if the application is submitted in paper form); regulations on use of collective marks, regulations on use of certification marks which have been modified if the request for modification of the protection title of collective marks or certification marks is made (2 copies if the application is submitted in paper form); mark samples, if the request for modification of mark samples as prescribed in point c clause 2 of this Article is made (5 samples if the application is submitted in paper form);
e) Documentary explanation detailing the contents of the modification;
g) Written authorization (if the request is submitted through a representative). For cases of modification of the protection title as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, there must be information on the authorization for requesting narrowing of the scope of protection.
4. An application for modification of a protection title shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all the documents prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article or the documents are invalid;
b) The request for narrowing of the scope of protection does not fall within the case specified in Clause 2 of this Article;
c) The applicant fails to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed;
d) There is insufficient information to confirm that the protection title holder authorizes the request for narrowing the protection scope (in case the request is submitted through a representative).
5. A request for amendment of a protection title shall be processed as follows:
a) In case the request is valid, within 02 months from the date of receipt of the request or from the date of expiry of the re-examination period specified at Point d of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to amend the protection title, record it in the protection title, register it and publish such decision on the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance.
b) In case the request is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the request for amendment, clearly stating the reasons therefor and setting a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or provide an objection.
c) If the prescribed time limit specified at Point b of this Clause expires but the requester fails to correct errors or provides inadequate correction of errors, no objection or an inappropriate objection, within 01 month from the date of expiry of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the request for amendment of the protection title;

d) For requests for amendment specified at Point c, Clause 1, Clause 2 of this Article, procedures for re-examination of the corresponding application shall be carried out in accordance with Article 114 of the Law on Intellectual Property;
dd) In case the protection title has been terminated or expired at the time of submission of the request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the request for amendment of the protection title, except where the content of the request for amendment aims to record accurate information before the termination of the protection title. In case the protection title has been invalidated at the time of submission of the request or during the processing of the request, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse the request for amendment of the protection title;
e) The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the processing of requests for amendments to protection titles in cases where it receives written requests from competent authorities for suspension of processing to implement relevant decisions and procedures as prescribed by law or documents regarding the subject matter of industrial property rights under the protection title that is being used as collateral. Requests for amendments shall continue to be processed when the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam receives documents proving that the relevant decisions and procedures have been terminated.
6. In case the applicant submits a written request for withdrawal of the application for amendments to the protection title, within 01 month from the date of receipt of such a document, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal request as follows:
a) If the withdrawal request is valid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal and terminate the processing of the request for amendments to the protection title. The withdrawn request for amendments to the protection title cannot be re-issued.
b) If the withdrawal request is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the request and impose a 15-day time limit from the date of issuance of such a notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to correct errors or has failed to meet requirements after correction, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to withdraw the request.
7. In case it is discovered that there are errors in a protection title, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall, either by itself or upon request of the person who discovers the errors, revoke the protection title and re-issue the protection title with amended information. Within 02 months from the date of revocation of the original protection title, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall re-issue the protection title recording correct information and send it to the owner of the protection title.
The owner of the protection title shall pay a fee for appraisal of the request for amendments to the protection title according to Clause 1 Article 97 of the Law on Intellectual Property, registration fee and fee for disclosure of amended information in case the protection title has errors that have been disclosed if such errors are caused by the owner of the protection title, except for errors caused by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Article 111. Termination of validity of protection titles
1. Organizations and individuals requesting termination of validity of protection titles according to Clause 4 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall pay request fees, fees for appraisal of requests for termination of validity of protection titles, registration fees and fees for publication of decisions on termination of validity of protection titles.
2. A mark registration certificate shall be terminated due to the fact that the owner or the authorized person has not used the mark within five consecutive years before a request for termination of validity is made without legitimate reasons as prescribed in Point d Clause 1 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property. A mark shall not be considered used if the act of using it is only formal and does not reflect actual commercial exploitation of goods or services bearing such mark, as in the following cases:
a) The use of the mark is only for business preparation, research or testing of goods or services bearing the mark without giving rise to any commercial transaction or without clear evidence of actual implementation of bringing such goods or services to market;
b) The use of the mark is only conducted within the internal operation of the applicant and is not intended to supply goods or services bearing the mark to the market;
c) The use of the mark is only shown in documents, reports, designs, web pages (website pages), advertising media or other information media without being associated with goods or services bearing the mark and plans to bring them to market or have already brought them to market.
3. In a request for termination of validity, only one protection title may be requested for termination of its validity.
A request for termination of validity of a protection title shall include the following documents:
a) Declaration requesting termination of validity of a protection title, made using Form No. 4 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b) Evidence (if any);
c) Power of attorney (if the request is submitted by a representative);
d) Presentation documents on the reasons for the request (specifying the number of the protection title, reasons and legal grounds, and the proposed partial or full termination of the validity of the protection title), and relevant documents.
4. Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the application for termination of validity, if the application contains formal errors (e.g., an invalid power of attorney; failure to fully pay fees and charges as prescribed; lack of necessary information to determine the scope of the request, etc.), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to correct such errors.
If the applicant fails to submit a document correcting errors within the above-mentioned time limit, or submits a document correcting errors but fails to meet requirements within the aforementioned time limit, within 1 month from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to consider the request for termination of validity of the protection title.
5. In case a third party requests termination of validity of a protection title, the application for termination of validity of the protection title shall be processed within 5 months from the date of submission of the application; for complex cases or where the owner of the protection title has a different opinion from the requesting party, the processing period may be extended but shall not exceed 7 months from the date of receipt of the application for termination of validity of the protection title.
The application for termination of validity of a protection title shall be processed as follows:
a) Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request or from the date the request for termination of validity reaches completion, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify in writing the third party's opinion to the protection title holder, setting a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the protection title holder to provide its opinion;
b) After receiving the feedback of the protection title holder within the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if it is deemed necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant for termination of validity and set a time limit of 1 month from the notification date for the applicant to provide their written feedback on such feedback;
c) In cases where information, documents, and evidence provided by the third party or the protection title holder are unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the third party or the protection title holder to submit additional documents and information to verify or clarify such information within 1 month from the date of notification by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam;
d) Based on the review of information, evidence, arguments, and documents provided by the parties, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title or notify the refusal to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title;
dd) The decision to terminate the validity of the protection title shall be recorded in the National Register of Industrial Property and published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance.
6. In case the protection title holder declares relinquishment of industrial property rights as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 95 of the Law on Intellectual Property, within 1 month from the date of receipt of the declaration, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall handle as follows:
a) Issue a decision to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title or notify the intended refusal to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title, setting a time limit of 1 month for the protection title holder to provide feedback;
b) Upon expiration of the above-mentioned time limit, if the protection title holder does not provide any feedback or provides inadequate feedback, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to partially or entirely terminate the validity of the protection title.
7. In case the applicant for termination of validity submits a written withdrawal of the request for termination of validity of the protection title, within 01 month from the date of receipt of such a document, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the withdrawal as follows:
a) If the withdrawal is valid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of the withdrawal and terminate the processing of the request for termination of validity of the protection title. The withdrawn request for termination of validity of the protection title cannot be re-validated;
b) If the withdrawal is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to accept the withdrawal and impose a 15-day time limit from the date of issuance of such a notice for the applicant to correct errors.
If the prescribed time limit expires but the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements after being requested to do so, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to accept the withdrawal and continue considering the request for termination of validity of the protection title.
8. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall terminate the processing of the entire or partial request for termination of validity of a protection title in cases where such protection title is subject to a request for termination of validity of which the entire or part has been completely or partially invalidated under a decision of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (or under a legally effective judgment/decision of a court).
Article 112. Invalidation of protection titles

1. Organizations and individuals requesting invalidation of protection titles according to Clause 4 Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property shall pay a fee for requesting invalidation of protection titles, an appraisal fee for requesting invalidation of protection titles, a registration fee and a fee for publication of the decision invalidating the protection title.
2. The validity of an invention patent/utility solution shall be invalidated if the invention is granted a patent beyond the disclosure scope in the initial description of the invention registration application according to Point dd Clause 2 Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property in cases where, compared with the initial description and for a person with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field, the description of the invention contains changes in content and such changes lead to information not directly and clearly derived from the initial description of the application, specifically as follows:
a) During the amendment or supplementation of the application, the applicant inserts into the description technical signs or technical signs that cannot be directly and clearly determined from the initial description;
b) Information is added (including information on purpose, effectiveness, etc.) that cannot be directly and clearly determined from the initial description (including drawings) and/or the initial protection request to clearly disclose the invention or fully disclose the protection request;
c) The added content in the description is technical signs related to size parameters obtained by measuring size parameters on drawings;
d) Details or components are added to the description that were not mentioned in the initial description of the application, leading to special effects and/or benefits not present in the initial application;
dd) Effects and/or benefits (benefits) are added to the description that a person with average knowledge of the corresponding technical field cannot determine from the initial application;
e) Technical signs of the protection request are changed but such changed technical signs are not directly and clearly disclosed or determined from the initial description;
g) New contents are added by changing undefined contents into definite and specific contents;
h) Combining separate technical signs of the initial application into a new technical sign while the relationship between these technical signs is not disclosed in the initial application;
i) Changing one or more technical signs in the description to make the changed technical signs different from the technical signs stated in the initial description;
k) Removing a technical sign from the protection request point which is necessary for the subject matter of the protection request to achieve the proposed purpose and/or the removal of this technical sign changes another technical sign or (more than one) technical signs.
3. The validity of a mark registration certificate shall be cancelled by the mark registration applicant with malicious intent according to Point a Clause 1 Article 96 of the Law on Intellectual Property if falling into one of the following cases:
a) The mark registration applicant registers a large quantity of marks identical or similar to the point of indistinguishability from other marks currently used by others in Vietnam for identical or similar goods and services where such registration exceeds normal business capacity and there is no evidence showing the actual intention to use these marks in production and business activities; or b) At the time of filing the application, the registered mark is identical or similar to the point of indistinguishability from a mark recognized by relevant consumers in Vietnam as a sign indicating the commercial origin of identical or similar goods and services of others or a mark that is identical or similar to the point of indistinguishability from a well-known mark in other countries; and the registration aims to take advantage of the reputation of such mark to seek profits or is mainly aimed at reselling, licensing or transferring the registration right to the holder of marks specified in this clause or aims to prevent the market entry of the holder of marks specified in this clause to limit competition or acts contrary to other practices of fair trade.
4. In a request for invalidation, only one protection title may be requested for invalidation. A request for invalidation of a protection title includes the following documents:
a) Declaration requesting invalidation of the protection title, made according to Form No. 5 in Appendix II of this Circular;
b) Evidence (if any);
c) Authorization document (if the application is submitted by a representative);
d) Explanation of the reason for the request (specifying the number of the title, the reason, legal grounds, and the content of the request for partial or full cancellation of the validity of the protection title) and relevant documents;
5. Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request for invalidation, if the application has formal deficiencies (e.g., the authorization document is invalid; fees and charges are not fully paid as prescribed; necessary information is missing to determine the scope of the request, etc.), the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct such deficiencies.
If the applicant does not have a document correcting deficiencies or has a document correcting deficiencies but fails to meet the requirements within the above-mentioned time limit, within 1 month from the end of the corresponding time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to consider the request for invalidation of the protection title.
6. The request for invalidation of a protection title shall be processed within 12 months for requests for invalidation of invention patents/utility solutions and 5 months for requests for invalidation of industrial design patents, certificates of registered layout designs, certificates of registered marks, and certificates of registered geographical indications from the date of submission of the request for invalidation; for complex cases, this period may be extended by up to 3 more months. A request for invalidation of a protection title shall be processed as follows:
a) Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request or from the date the request for correction meets the requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify in writing the opinion of a third party to the owner of the protection title, setting a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the owner of the protection title to provide their opinion;
b) After receiving the feedback from the owner of the protection title within the time limit specified at Point a of this Clause, if it is deemed necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the feedback to the person requesting invalidation and impose a 2-month time limit from the date of issuance of the notice for the person requesting invalidation to provide their written feedback on such feedback.
c) In case information, documents and evidence provided by a third party or the owner of a protection title are considered unclear, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify such third party or owner of additional documents and information to verify or clarify the information within 01 month from the date of notification by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam. Where necessary, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam may organize direct consultation between the third party and the owner of the protection title to clarify related matters;
d) Based on the examination of information, evidence, arguments and documents provided by the parties, the results of direct consultation and the appraisal results, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to partially or entirely invalidate the protection title or issue a notice of refusal to partially or entirely invalidate the protection title.
dd) The decision to invalidate the protection title shall be recorded in the National Register of Industrial Property and published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision.
7. In case the applicant for invalidation withdraws the written request for invalidation of the protection title, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of withdrawal of requests for termination of validity as prescribed in Clause 7 Article 111 of this Circular.
8. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall terminate the processing of the entire or partial request for invalidation of the protection title if the protection title is subject of the request for invalidation and has been entirely or partially invalidated under the decision of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam (or according to legally effective judgment/decision of a competent court).
9. Regulations in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article shall also apply during the processing of invention registration applications and mark registration applications.
Article 113. Termination and invalidation of international registrations
1. Applications for termination and invalidation of international registrations of marks shall be processed as follows:
a) For an application for termination or invalidation of international registration of a mark under the Madrid Agreement or Madrid Protocol filed by a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the content of such application to the mark owner via the International Office, setting a time limit of 3 months from the date of notification for the mark owner to provide their opinion;
b) The international registration of a mark may be terminated or invalidated for part or all of the list of goods and services;
c) In case the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision on termination or invalidation of the international registration of a mark for part or all of the list of goods and services and such decision is no longer subject to administrative complaints or lawsuits, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of termination or invalidation of the international registration of the mark using the form of the International Office, clearly stating the list of goods and services subject to termination or invalidation, and send this notice to the International Office;
d) Other relevant provisions in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular regarding the processing of applications for termination or invalidation of protection titles on the basis of mark registrations filed under national procedures as prescribed in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular shall apply to the processing of applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of marks.
2. Applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of industrial designs shall be processed as follows:

a) For an application for termination or invalidation of international registration of an industrial design under the Hague Agreement filed by a third party, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the content of such application to the industrial design owner via the International Office, setting a time limit of 3 months from the date of notification for the industrial design owner to provide their opinion;
b) The international registration of an industrial design may be invalidated for part or all of the industrial designs in such registration;
c/ In case the National Office of Intellectual Property issues a decision to invalidate the international registration for some or all industrial designs and such decision is no longer subject to complaint or administrative lawsuit, the National Office of Intellectual Property shall issue a notice on invalidation of the international registration of industrial designs according to the form of the International Office, clearly stating the industrial designs which are invalidated, and send such notice to the International Office;
d/ Other relevant provisions in Articles 111 and 112 of this Circular on processing of requests for termination or invalidation of protection titles for industrial designs granted on the basis of applications for registration of industrial designs filed under the national filing form shall apply to requests for termination or invalidation of international registration of industrial designs.
Chapter VIII
   REGISTRATION OF CONTRACTS FOR TRANSFER OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Article 114. Dossiers of registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights
1. The owner of a protection title (the transferee) or the transferee of industrial property rights may file a dossier of registration of a contract for transfer of industrial property rights to request the National Office of Intellectual Property to record the transfer of industrial property rights, provided that it pays the fee for dossier appraisal, the lookup fee (in case of mark transfer), the fee for grant of certificate of mark registration (in case of partial transfer of the list of goods and services bearing marks), the registration fee and the fee for publication of the decision recording the transfer of industrial property rights.
2. A dossier of registration of a contract for transfer of industrial property rights comprises the following documents:
a/ Declaration for registration of a contract for transfer of industrial property rights, made according to Form No. 6 in Annex II of this Circular;
b/ The contract (original or certified copy as prescribed); if the contract is made in a language other than Vietnamese, it must be enclosed with a Vietnamese version; if the contract has many pages, each page must bear the signatures of the parties or be appended with a seal on every two adjacent pages;
c/ The original protection title in case the protection title is issued in paper form;
d/ Written consent of the co-owners about the transfer of industrial property rights, if the corresponding industrial property rights are under joint ownership; or the mortgagee in case the industrial property rights are being used as mortgaged assets;
e/ A written authorization (if the application is filed through a representative);
f/ For applications for registration of contracts on transfer of collective marks or certification marks, apart from the above-said documents, the following documents are also required: e1/ Regulations on use of collective marks or certification marks of the transferee according to Article 105 of the Law on Intellectual Property; e2/ Documents evidencing the right to file an application of the transferee for certification marks or collective marks according to Clauses 3 and 4, Article 87 of the Law on Intellectual Property. In this case, apart from fees and charges applicable to applications for registration of contracts on transfer of industrial property rights as prescribed, the applicant shall pay an application appraisal fee.
3. Each application for registration of a contract on transfer of industrial property rights shall be recorded only for one step of transfer. If the object of industrial property is transferred in more than one step, each step of transfer shall require a separate application for registration of a contract on transfer of industrial property rights.
Article 115. Procedures for processing applications for registration of contracts on transfer of industrial property rights
1. An application for registration of a contract on transfer of industrial property rights shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a) The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 2 Article 114 of this Circular or contains invalid documents, including: The contract is not signed by both parties (and seal, if any); name and address of the transferor in the contract are not consistent with corresponding information in the protection title, authorization document, and declaration; name and address of the transferee in the contract are not consistent with name and address stated in the authorization document and declaration;

b) The copy of the contract is not certified as valid; the contract for transfer lacks required contents or has contents inconsistent with regulations on conditions restricting transfer of industrial property rights as prescribed in Articles 139 and 140 of the Law on Intellectual Property, etc.;
b) The applicant fails to pay sufficient fees and charges as prescribed;
c) The transferor is not the owner of the protection title;
d) There are grounds to confirm that the transfer of industrial property rights infringes upon the industrial property rights of a third party.
2. In case the application for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract is valid, within 02 months from the date of receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall perform the following tasks:
a) Issue a decision to recognize the transfer of industrial property rights;
b) Record the new owner in the protection title; in case of partial transfer of the list of goods and services bearing protected marks, issue a certificate of mark registration to the transferee and determine the scope of the transferred part in the original protection title;
c) Recording the transfer of industrial property rights in the National Register of Industrial Property;
d) Publishing the decision on recording the transfer of industrial property rights in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the date of issuance of such decision.
3. In case the application for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract is invalid, within 2 months from the receipt of the application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to register the contract, clearly stating the deficiencies in the application, and impose a time limit of 2 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct the deficiencies or provide opinions opposing the intended refusal to register the contract.
4. In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or provides inadequate corrections, no opinions opposing or provides inaccurate opinions opposing the intended refusal to register the contract within the time limit specified in Clause 3 of this Article, within 2 months from the expiration of the above-mentioned time limit, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision refusing to register the contract.
5. In case after receiving the application for registration of an industrial property right transfer contract, the parties have disputes related to the contract or there is a written request from a competent agency or organization for the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to suspend the processing of the application for the purpose of implementing relevant decisions and procedures as prescribed by law or documents regarding the subject matter of industrial property rights being used as collateral, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall suspend the consideration of the application until it receives documents proving that the dispute has been resolved or documents proving that the relevant decisions and procedures have been terminated; thereafter, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall continue to consider the application according to regulations.
6. In case the protection title is subject to an industrial property right transfer contract that has been terminated, invalidated, or expired as prescribed, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision refusing to register the industrial property right transfer contract.
7. Before the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam issues a decision to record the transfer or refuses to register the contract for transfer of industrial property rights, if either of the parties signing the contract wishes to withdraw the application for registration of the contract for transfer of industrial property rights, it is required to obtain the consent of the other party regarding the withdrawal of the submitted application for registration of the contract for transfer of industrial property rights, except for cases where the application is withdrawn due to irreparable deficiencies as requested by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Within 1 month from the date of receipt of the request for withdrawal of the application for registration of the contract for transfer of industrial property rights, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process such request as follows:
a) In case the request for withdrawal of the application is valid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of acceptance of withdrawal and terminate the processing of the application for registration of the contract for transfer of industrial property rights. The withdrawn application for registration of the contract for transfer of industrial property rights cannot be re-registered;
b) In case the request for withdrawal of the application is invalid, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of intended refusal to withdraw the request and impose a time limit of 15 working days from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct the deficiencies.
If the prescribed time limit expires and the applicant fails to correct the deficiencies or has corrected the deficiencies but fails to meet the requirements, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a notice of refusal to withdraw the request.
Chapter IX
 COMPLAINTS AND SETTLEMENT OF COMPLAINTS RELATED TO INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY PROCEDURES
Article 116. Persons having the right to file complaints, subjects subject to complaints, statute of limitations for filing complaints and persons settling complaints
1. Persons entitled to lodging complaints are organizations and individuals as prescribed in Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property.
2. Decisions and notices subject to complaint as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property are official decisions and notices issued by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam or organizations authorized or delegated by People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities to carry out procedures related to industrial property according to corresponding provisions herein and Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, including the following decisions and notices:
a) Notices of rejection of receipt of applications;
b) Notices of acceptance or rejection of requests for amendments to applications, conversion of applications, change of applicants, withdrawal of applications;
c) Decisions on rejection of grant of protection titles; Decisions on grant of protection titles;
d) Decisions on rejection of protection of internationally registered industrial designs; Decisions on rejection of protection of internationally registered marks; Decisions on acceptance of protection of internationally registered industrial designs; Decisions on acceptance of protection of internationally registered marks;
dd) Notices of maintenance of validity of protection titles; Decisions on rejection of maintenance of validity of protection titles;
e) Decisions on extension of validity of protection titles; Decisions on rejection of extension of validity of protection titles;
g) Decision on amendments to protection titles; decision rejecting requests for amendments to protection titles;
h) Decision on partial or total termination or invalidation of protection titles; notice of rejection of termination or invalidation of protection titles;
i) Decision and notice on handling of applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of marks; decision and notice on handling of applications for termination or invalidation of international registration of industrial designs;
k) Decision on recording of transfer of industrial property rights, decision rejecting registration of contracts for transfer of industrial property rights; decision on recording of transfer of rights to use subject matters of industrial property, decision rejecting registration of contracts for transfer of rights to use subject matters of industrial property;
l) Decisions, notices and other acts which satisfy the conditions that they are objects complained about under the law on complaints.
Notifications of information and requests for completion of dossiers which are not administrative decisions shall not be subject of complaint, such as notifications of appraisal results, notices of deficiencies, requests for amendment or supplementation of documents, intended refusals, notices of provisional refusal to protect internationally registered marks, notices of refusal to protect internationally registered industrial designs.
3. The following contents shall not be accepted in the course of complaint settlement:
a) Requests for amendments to applications for establishment of industrial property rights which are subject of complained decisions or notices (such as requests for amendments to invention descriptions (including protection requests); requests for amendments to sets of photos or sets of drawings and descriptions of industrial designs; requests for amendments to mark samples and lists of goods or services bearing marks; requests for amendments to product names bearing GIs and descriptions of specific properties of products bearing GIs and maps of geographical areas corresponding to GIs for applications for registration of GIs), except where otherwise provided for by international treaties to which Vietnam is a contracting party;
b/ An existing circumstance in the process of examination of an industrial property registration application which, for objective reasons, the National Office of Intellectual Property and concerned organizations or individuals can only know after a decision on grant or refusal of a protection title is issued (hereinafter referred to as new circumstance), except for cases where such circumstance is raised by a third party under Point b. Clause 6, Article 119 of this Circular.
4. The complaint must be filed within the statute of limitations prescribed in Articles 9 and 33 of the Law on Complaints.
5. The person competent to settle first-time complaints is the Director of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam; the person competent to settle second-time complaints is the Minister of Science and Technology (hereinafter referred to as “complaint solver”) for procedures related to industrial property rights prescribed in this Circular. For procedures related to industrial property rights prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, the persons competent to settle first-time and second-time complaints shall be determined in accordance with the law on complaints.
Article 117. Complaint letters
1. The complaint shall be made in accordance with Clause 1 Article 3 of Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP dated October 19, 2020 providing detailed regulations and measures for implementation of the Law on Complaints (hereinafter referred to as “Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP”), which specifies all information specified in Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and documents related to the content of the complaint as prescribed in Clause 2.

Article 8 of the Law on Complaints, specifically as follows:
a/ Copies of the decision or notice complained about and copies of the industrial property registration application are subject matter of such decision or notice (in case the applicant files a second complaint); or documents guiding information on the above-said documents;
b) Copy of the decision on first-time complaint settlement (for second-time complaints);
c) Evidence (evidence or material evidence) used to prove and clarify the complaint argument. Evidence may be submitted additionally within 01 month from the date of filing the complaint.
2. In case the complaint is filed through a lawful representative in Vietnam as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property, the application must be enclosed with a written authorization or a copy of the written authorization if it falls under the case specified in Clause 5 Article 8 of this Circular.
3. A complaint can only be filed against one official decision or notice related to industrial property procedures.
Article 118. Withdrawal of complaint and suspension of complaint settlement
1. The complainant may withdraw the complaint according to regulations in Article 10 of the Law on Complaints. If the withdrawal of the complaint is done under the authorization of the complainant, the authorization for withdrawal of the complaint must be clearly stated in the written authorization. The withdrawn application shall be considered not submitted. The complainant shall not be refunded the complaint and the fees paid for complaint settlement services, except for cases where the complaint is withdrawn before the notification of acceptance or rejection of the application is issued.
2. The person settling the complaint shall issue a decision to suspend the complaint settlement in the following cases:
a) The complainant withdraws the complaint;
b) The protection title, international registration of marks, international registration of industrial designs in Vietnam has been invalidated or the validity of the international registration of marks has expired according to relevant international treaties.
Article 119. Order and procedures for settlement of complaints
1. The acceptance of a complaint shall comply with Article 27 of the Law on Complaints and Clause 1 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property, specifically as follows:
a) Within 10 working days from the date of receipt of the complaint petition, the complaint settler shall:

a1) Issue a notice of non-acceptance of complaint settlement if the petition falls under one of the cases specified at Point b of this Clause, clearly stating the reason for refusal;

or a2) Issue a notice of acceptance of complaint settlement if the petition does not fall under the cases specified at Point b of this Clause, specifying the date of acceptance and determining the lookup fee and/or appraisal fee in case of re-appraisal for the purpose of settling the complaint corresponding to the content of the complaint (if any), and impose a time limit of 01 month for the complainant to pay the fee;
b) The complaint shall not be accepted for settlement in one of the following cases: b1) The subject matter of the complaint is not an official decision or notice prescribed in Clause 2 Article 116 of this Circular; b2) The decision, notice, or act complained about is not directly related to the lawful rights and interests of the complainant; b3) The complaint is not submitted in accordance with Clause 2 Article 117 of this Circular; b4) The complaint is submitted beyond the prescribed statute of limitations, except for the cases specified in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 15 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP; b5) The complaint has already been settled by a second complaint settlement decision; b6) The complaint has been accepted by the Court or has been settled by a judgment. - Decisions of Courts, except for cases where there are decisions to terminate the resolution of administrative lawsuits by Courts; b7) The complainants continue their complaints after the 30-day time limit from the date of issuance of decisions to terminate the complaint settlement as prescribed in Article 10 and Clause 8, Article 11 of the Law on Complaints; b8) Complaint petitions which do not indicate illegal elements of decisions, notices or acts complained about and only request amendments to such decisions or notices; b9) Complaint petitions against notices, administrative decisions or acts related to industrial property objects falling within the scope of state secrets.
c) If the complainant fails to pay the appraisal fee for the case subject to re-appraisal in service of the complaint settlement according to the notice of acceptance of complaint settlement specified at Point a2 of this Clause, the complaint shall be settled based on documents included in the application.

2. The time limit for complaint settlement shall comply with Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property and Articles 28 and 37 of the Law on Complaints.
3. To verify the content of the complaint, the complaint solver shall seek opinions from persons with rights and obligations related to the accepted complaint according to Article 21 of Decree No. 124/2020/ND-CP (hereinafter referred to as “relevant parties”) as follows:
a) The complaint solver shall notify in writing the content of the complaint to the relevant party and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for such party to provide its opinion (if any);
b) The relevant party may provide information or evidence to support its argument within the time limit prescribed at Point a of this Clause, and the complaint solver shall be responsible for reviewing such information and evidence when settling the complaint;
c) The complaint solver shall notify in writing the content of the opinion of the relevant party and impose a 2-month time limit from the notification date for the complainant to provide feedback on the opinion of the relevant party;
d) If the prescribed time limit expires but one party does not provide an opinion, the complaint shall be settled based on documents included in the application, including documents expressing the opinion of the other party.
4. During the first-time complaint settlement for complicated complaint cases, the first-time complaint solver may proactively or at the request of the complainant carry out re-appraisal according to Clause 4 Article 119a of the Law on Intellectual Property. During the re-appraisal, the first-time complaint solver may consult an independent expert or the Advisory Council in accordance with the following regulations:
a) The first complaint settler may consult an independent consulting expert or a Consulting Council during the re-appraisal process depending on the complexity of the contents to be re-appraised. A Consulting Council shall be composed of a president and members. An independent consulting expert or a Consulting Council shall advise the complaint settler on technical issues, legal issues of the contents to be re-appraised and on the settlement plan.
Independent consulting experts, the chairman and members of the Consulting Council are persons with appropriate professional qualifications selected from the List of industrial property consulting experts and other sources (in case no appropriate expert is available in such list).
List of industrial property counseling experts compiled by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in cooperation with an administrative organization assigned to assist the Minister of Science and Technology in settling complaints and published in the Industrial Property Official Gazette.
b/ The Advisory Council shall be organized and operate according to the following principles: b1/ The Advisory Council is set up under the decision of the first complaint settler; b2/ The Advisory Council works in the form of meetings, collective discussions and voting by majority; b3/ Involved parties in the complaint case and parties with related rights and obligations may be invited to attend the meeting of the Advisory Council to clarify the case details.
c/ The following persons shall not participate in the Advisory Council and not act as independent consultants in the complaint case: c1/ The complained person (the person who issued the complained decision or notice); c2/ The person who appraised the application for registration of industrial property rights related to the complained decision or notice; c3/ The person whose rights and interests are directly related to the complaint case; c4/ The person who may not be objective in the complaint case, if there are grounds to determine such. The persons mentioned at this point shall explain, give explanations and information related to their work involved in the complaint.
d) Opinions of independent consulting experts, opinions of the chairman and members of the Consulting Council and working results of the Consulting Council must be presented in writing.
5. The person who settles the first complaint shall organize a dialogue session as prescribed in Article 30 of the Law on Complaints. Independent consultants and members of the Advisory Council for Re-valuation during the settlement of the first complaint (if any) may be invited to attend the dialogue session.
6. The person competent to settle the first complaint shall issue a decision to settle the complaint as follows:
a) Based on the results of re-examination of the decision or notice complained about, the person who settles the first complaint shall issue a decision to settle the complaint, which must contain the contents specified in Clause 2, Article 31 of the Law on Complaints;

b) In cases where the complainant is not the applicant or the requester for establishment of industrial property rights or registration of an industrial property right transfer contract subject to the decision or notice complained about provides new details that may affect the conclusion on the complaint settlement, the re-valuation of the content related to such new details shall be carried out. Based on the results of this re-valuation, the person competent to settle the first complaint shall issue a decision to settle the complaint as prescribed at Point a of this Clause.
7. The provisions on settlement of first-time complaints in this Circular shall also apply to the settlement of second-time complaints, except for the provisions related to consultation with experts in the revaluation of application contents prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article. For complex complaint cases, if necessary, the person settling the second complaint shall establish an Advisory Council under the guidance prescribed in Clause 4 of this Article to consult opinions during the complaint settlement process.
8. In cases where the complaint settlement results lead to amendments, supplements, partial or complete cancellation of administrative decisions, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall resume the process of appraising relevant applications to carry out the remaining procedures in accordance with the complaint settlement results.
Article 120. Publication of complaint settlement decisions
A complaint settlement decision shall be published on the web portal of the complaint-settling agency within 15 working days and in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within one month from the date of issuance of the decision.
Article 121. Effect of decisions and notices complained about and decisions on complaint settlement
1. Decisions and notices complained about shall remain effective during the complaint settlement period, except for cases where they are suspended according to written decisions of complaint settlers as prescribed in Article 35 of the Law on Complaints or at the request of Courts according to the law on proceedings.
2. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and organizations authorized by People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall immediately implement legally effective complaint settlement decisions as prescribed in Article 44 of the Law on Complaints, specifically as follows:
a) First-time complaint settlement decisions of Directors of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and heads of organizations authorized by People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall take effect after 30 days from the date of their signing if the complainant does not file a second complaint; for remote areas where travel is difficult, this period may be extended but shall not exceed 45 days;
b) Second-time complaint settlement decisions of the Minister of Science and Technology or Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall take effect after 30 days from the date of their signing; for remote areas where travel is difficult, this period may be extended but shall not exceed 45 days.
3. The acceptance of a second complaint shall be notified to the first complaint settler within 10 working days from the date of acceptance. In this case, the first complaint settlement decision shall not take legal effect. The complained decisions and notices shall continue to be effective until the second complaint settlement decision takes legal effect.
4. The second complainant may immediately notify the first complaint settler of the filing of such complaint to ensure his/her rights and interests.
Article 122. Measures to limit arising complaints
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam and organizations authorized or delegated by People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities to carry out administrative procedures prescribed in Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall be responsible for reviewing decisions and notices already issued; if detecting signs of violation, they shall promptly correct and remedy such issues to avoid arising complaints, except where such decisions or notices are currently subject to complaints.
2. During the resolution of complaints, the person in charge of complaint resolution shall encourage and facilitate disputing parties to reach agreement through mediation as prescribed by law.
Chapter X
   INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY OFFICIAL GAZETTE, NATIONAL REGISTRY OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY
Article 123. Industrial property official gazette
1. Industrial property official gazette means an official publication published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, providing public information on the establishment, change, termination or invalidation of industrial property rights and other relevant information required to be disclosed in accordance with the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP and this Circular.

2. Industrial property official gazette shall be published in print form or online.
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for:
a) Retain 1 copy of each issue of the Official Gazette for the printed version. The retention period shall be permanent;
b) Manage the electronic Gazette database, including the electronic versions of issues of the Official Gazette and information and attributes of documents published in the Official Gazette.
4. Contents of the Industrial Property Official Gazette include information that must be published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam when organizations and individuals carry out procedures related to industrial property rights prescribed in the Law on Intellectual Property, Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, and this Circular.
5. Geographical indications accepted for protection under international treaties shall be published by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days from the effective date of relevant international treaties. Necessary information related to the above-mentioned geographical indications shall be published in accordance with Clause 2 Article 11 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP, including: name of the geographical indication; name of the product bearing the geographical indication; summary of the specific characteristics of the product bearing the geographical indication; protection area of the geographical indication, and other information (if any).
Article 124. National Register of Industrial Property and Industrial Property Representatives
1. The National Register of Industrial Property is the official and public database of the State, fully reflecting information on the legal status of established industrial property rights. The National Register of Industrial Property includes the following types:
a) National Register of Inventions;
b) National Register of Utility Solutions;
c) National Register of Industrial Designs;
d) National Register of Semiconductor Integrated Circuit Layout Designs;
dd) National Register of Marks;
e) National Register of Geographical Indications.
2. For subject matters of industrial property registered under national procedures, the National Register specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall include sections corresponding to each protection title, and each section shall comprise:
a) Information on the protection title: number, issuance date; name of protected subject matter, scope (volume) of protection, validity period; name and address of the owner of the protection title/geographical indication registrant, geographical indication management organization, name and nationality of the author of the invention, layout design or industrial design;
b) Information on the application for the protection title (number, submission date, priority date of the application, name of the industrial property representation service provider (if any));
c) Any amendments related to information on amendments to the protection title, validity status of the protection title (validity maintenance, extension, termination, or cancellation); transfer of ownership or use rights of subject matters of industrial property; number of re-issuance times, re-issuance date, issuance of copies, number of copies (for any co-owner), copy issuance date, changes to the industrial property representation service provider (if any), etc.
3. For marks and industrial designs accepted for protection under international registration procedures, in the Parts of International Mark Registration and International Industrial Design Registration in the respective National Registers prescribed at Points c and dd, Clause 1 of this Article, the following sections shall be included:
a) Information on the protection status: decision number, decision issuance date or protection acceptance date; name of protected subject matter, protection scope, validity period; name and address of the owner of the protection title, name and nationality of the author of the industrial design;
b) Information on the application for the protection title (application number, submission date, priority date of the application, name of the industrial property representation service organization (if any));
4. For geographical indications protected under international treaties, the Part of International Geographical Indications in the National Register prescribed at Point e, Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following sections:
a) Information on the protection status: name of the international treaty, effective date of the international treaty or date of acceptance of protection of the geographical indication under the international treaty; protected geographical indication, protection scope; name and address of the organization managing the geographical indication;
b) Information on the dossiers and documents related to the geographical indication requesting protection (international treaty, international agreement on protection, database related to the geographical indication requesting protection (if any));
c) Any amendment related to the legal status of the protected geographical indication: validity status, protection scope, transfer of management rights, etc.
5. The National Register of Industrial Property Representatives shall include sections corresponding to each industrial property representation service organization, specifically:
a) Information on industrial property representation service organizations (full names, transaction names, addresses, name recording and deletion, and amendments to information on industrial property representation service organizations);
b) Information on lists of industrial property representatives of organizations (full names, permanent addresses, and practicing certificates of each member in the list);
c) Information on changes to lists of industrial property representatives (issuance, re-issuance, revocation of practicing certificates, deletion, etc.).
6. National registers prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 of this Article shall be established and stored by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam in paper or electronic form. Any person may search the electronic register (if available) or request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to issue copies or excerpts from the register, provided that they pay fees for the issuance of copies or excerpts from the register.
Article 125. Changes to information in the National Register of Industrial Property
1. Owners of protection titles, organizations and individuals permitted by the State to exercise the right to register geographical indications, and organizations managing geographical indications shall have the right to request the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam to record changes to industrial property representation service organizations of protection title holders, provided that they pay the fee for examination of the request for recording, the registration fee, and the fee for publication of the decision to record changes to information on industrial property representation service organizations as prescribed.
2. A dossier requesting changes to information on industrial property representation service organizations in the National Register of Industrial Property includes the following documents:
a) A declaration made using Form No. 03 in Appendix II of this Circular, clearly stating the request for recording of changes. One declaration may request recording of changes to information on industrial property representation service organizations of multiple protection titles if the same information is changed, provided that the requester pays the fee as prescribed for each protection title;
b) Authorization document.
3. An application for change of information on an industrial property representation service organization in the National Register shall be considered invalid in one of the following cases:
a/ The application does not contain all documents specified in Clause 2 of this Article or contains invalid documents;
b/ The applicant fails to pay all fees and charges as prescribed.
4. Applications for recording of changes in information on industrial property representation service organizations in the National Register shall be processed as follows:
a/ In case the application is valid, within 02 months after receiving it, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to record the change in the National Register of Industrial Property and Registration Book and publish it in the Industrial Property Official Gazette within 30 days after issuing the decision;
b/ In case the application is invalid, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a notice stating the intended refusal to record the change, clearly stating the reason therefor and fixing a time limit of 02 months from the date of issuance of the notice for the applicant to correct errors or to express its opposition;
c/ Past the fixed time limit prescribed at Point b of this Clause but the applicant fails to correct errors or fails to meet requirements in correcting errors, makes no opposition or makes unjustifiable opposition, within 02 months after the expiration of the above-mentioned time limit, the National Office of Industrial Property shall issue a decision to refuse to record the change in the industrial property representation service organization.
d) In addition to the cases specified at Point c of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse to record changes in industrial property representation service providers in the following cases: d1) The protection title has been terminated or expired according to regulations at the time of application submission; d2) The protection title was invalidated at the time of application submission or during the processing of the application.
5. Where the applicant requests withdrawal of the application for recording changes in information on the industrial property representation service provider in the National Register, the handling of such withdrawal shall be carried out similarly to the handling of withdrawal of applications for amendments to protection titles according to Clause 6 Article 110 of this Circular.
Chapter XI
  TRAINING IN INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY LAW AND INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY ASSESSMENT PROFESSIONAL TRAINING

Article 126. Training program in industrial property law
1. Duration and time: at least 400 hours of lectures on legal knowledge and skills regarding industrial property (excluding basic law lectures for learners who do not have bachelor’s or master’s degrees in Law) and not exceeding 12 months.
2. Contents of the training program and guidelines for implementation are provided in Appendix III of this Circular.
Article 127. Recognition of institutions providing training in industrial property law The recognition of institutions providing training in industrial property law shall be carried out as follows:
1. Training institutions wishing to be recognized as eligible to organize training courses on industrial property law in accordance with Clause 4 Article 62 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall submit applications for recognition to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. An application for recognition as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a) Written request for recognition of the training institution as prescribed using Form No. 01 under Appendix III hereof;
b) Documents proving that the organization meets the criteria and conditions for recognition (conditions regarding facilities, equipment; information about officials in the payroll who have taught at the undergraduate level in intellectual property for 5 years or more; list of information about instructors participating in the training program; approved standard documents suitable for the contents specified in the structure of the training program on industrial property law).
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process an application for recognition of a training institution within 15 working days from the date of receipt in the following order:
a) In case of valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to recognize the training institution, record it in the List of institutions eligible to provide training courses on industrial property law, and publish it on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision;
b) In case of invalid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of deficiencies in the application and impose a 1-month time limit from the date of notification for the applicant to correct deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to meet requirements after the deadline specified in Point b of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to refuse recognition of the training institution, clearly stating the reasons for refusal.
4. List of recognized training institutions under this Article shall be posted and updated on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Article 128. Deletion of names of training institutions providing legal education on industrial property The deletion of names of training institutions providing legal education on industrial property from the List of eligible training institutions shall be carried out as follows:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall delete names of training institutions no longer satisfying eligibility for providing legal education on industrial property in the following cases:
a) The training institution is temporarily suspended, shut down, or dissolved;
b) The training institution no longer satisfies the criteria and conditions specified in Clause 2 Article 62 of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) The training institution fails to organize training programs according to the prescribed industrial property law training program specified in Article 126 of this Circular.
2. For the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to delete the name from the List of training institutions providing legal education on industrial property and publish information about the deletion on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision.
Article 129. Industrial property assessment training programs
1. Duration and time of training: at least 400 hours of lectures on legal knowledge, professional skills, and industrial property assessment methods shall be provided and not exceed 12 months.
2. Contents of the training program and guidelines for implementation are specified in Appendix III of this Circular.
Article 130. Recognition of industrial property assessment training institutions The recognition of an industrial property assessment training institution shall be carried out as follows:
1. The training institution wishing to be recognized as eligible to organize industrial property assessment training courses under Clause 2 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP shall submit a request for recognition to the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
2. A request for recognition as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall include the following documents:
a) Written request for recognition of the industrial property assessment training institution, made using Form No. 02 in Appendix III of this Circular;
b) Documents proving that the organization meets the criteria and conditions for recognition (material facilities and equipment; information on officials in the payroll who have participated in industrial property assessment; list of information about instructors participating in the training program; approved standard documents for use, consistent with the contents specified in the structure of the industrial property assessment training program).
3. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall process the request for recognition of the industrial property assessment training institution within 15 working days from the date of receipt, in the following order:
a) In case of a valid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision on recognition of the training institution as an industrial property assessment training institution, record it in the List of institutions eligible for industrial property assessment training and publish it on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision;
b) In case of an invalid application, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall notify the applicant of deficiencies in the application and impose a 1-month time limit from the notification date for the applicant to correct such deficiencies;
c) In case the applicant fails to correct deficiencies or fails to sufficiently correct deficiencies, within 15 working days from the end of the time limit specified at Point b of this Clause, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision rejecting the recognition of the training institution as an industrial property assessment training institution, clearly stating the reasons for rejection.
4. The list of recognized training institutions under this Article shall be published and updated on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam.
Article 131. Deletion of names of industrial property assessment training institutions The deletion of names of industrial property assessment training institutions from the List of institutions eligible for industrial property assessment training shall be carried out as follows:
1. The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall delete names of training institutions no longer satisfying the conditions for industrial property assessment training in the following cases:
a) The training institution is temporarily suspended, shut down, or ceases to exist as a juridical person;
b) The training institution no longer satisfies the criteria and conditions specified in Clause 2 Article 108a of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP;
c) The training institution fails to organize the training according to the industrial property assessment training program as prescribed in Article 129 of this Circular.
2. For the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall issue a decision to remove the name from the list of industrial property assessment training institutions and publish information about the removal on the web portal of the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam within 7 working days from the date of issuance of the decision.
Chapter XII
                    ASSURANCE OF INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY INFORMATION
Article 132. Measures to assure information on industrial property rights The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall take the following measures to assure information on industrial property rights to serve the exploitation of industrial property information and the exploitation of industrial property rights:
1. Maintain the information technology infrastructure to serve the assurance of information on industrial property rights.
2. Develop and manage the database on industrial property and the information system for management of geographical indications as prescribed in Articles 9b and 9c of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
3. Carry out activities to disseminate information on industrial property rights, including providing information on industrial property rights according to the titles issued, disseminating laws on the scope of rights of protection title holders, providing training and refresher courses on necessary knowledge and skills for organizations and individuals on the establishment, exploitation and management of industrial property rights and respect for industrial property rights.
Article 133. Development and management of databases on transactions of intellectual property rights and databases on protection of intellectual property rights
The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall be responsible for:

1. Develop, manage operation, maintain and develop database on transaction prices of intellectual property rights in accordance with Article 9d of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
2. Develop and manage databases on intellectual property right protection in accordance with Article 9e of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP.
Article 134. Access and use of information in the industrial property database All organizations and individuals shall have the right to access and use information in the industrial property database in two forms:
1. To search for information on databases publicly available at information lookup facilities open to the public or published on the Internet, by themselves.
2. Use information lookup and analysis services or information and document supply services provided by the Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam, on payment of fees as prescribed.
Article 135. Information lookup and analysis services and information and document provision services The Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam shall provide information lookup and analysis services and information and document provision services for organizations and individuals that have needs as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 134 of this Circular.
Article 136. Assurance of industrial property information in localities
1. Depending on their conditions and capabilities, the State administrative body for industrial property in a locality may set up and manage an industrial property database to ensure industrial property information for research, application, development of production, business activities and protection of industrial property rights in the locality.
2. The State administrative body for industrial property in a province or centrally-run city shall have the responsibility and authority to conduct activities to assure industrial property information as prescribed in this Circular.
3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall make statistics of information in the industrial property database for the purpose of performing the State management of industrial property in provinces and centrally-run cities on a periodical basis (at least once every six months).
Article 137. Grant of copies of documents and certification of first applications for priority right
1. Any organization or individual concerned shall be entitled to request the National Office of Industrial Property to grant copies of documents issued by the National Office of Industrial Property or copies of master registers made by the National Office of Industrial Property or copies of documents prepared by such organization or individual and submitted to the National Office of Industrial Property in the course of establishment of industrial property rights. The requester for copies shall pay the cost of document copying.
2. An applicant shall be entitled to request the National Office of Industrial Property to supply certified copies of the industrial property registration application filed with the National Office of Industrial Property to apply for priority right (even if the application is rejected as invalid or withdrawn by the applicant), provided that the applicant pays the cost of document copying.
3. The National Office of Industrial Property shall provide copies of and certification of applications for organizations and individuals that wish to use them as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.
Chapter XIII
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 138. Implementation provisions
1. This Circular comes into force as of April 01, 2026.
2. Circular No. 23/2023/TT-BKHCN dated November 30, 2023 of the Minister of Science and Technology detailing certain articles of the Law on Intellectual Property and measures for implementation of Decree No. 65/2023/ND-CP dated August 23, 2023 of the Government detailing certain articles and measures for implementation of the Law on Intellectual Property regarding industrial property, protection of industrial property rights, plant variety rights and state management of intellectual property related to procedures for establishment of industrial property rights and assurance of industrial property information shall cease to be effective from the date this Circular comes into force.
Article 139. Transitional provisions
Applications in procedures related to industrial property rights and complaints filed before the effective date of this Circular shall be processed according to regulations of law effective at the time of filing, except for the following cases:
1. Regulations in Clause 4 Article 14, Clause 4 Article 41, Clause 4 Article 48, Clause 3 Article 71 and Clause 4 Article 96 of this Circular on notification of results of the formal examination shall apply to applications for registration of inventions, layout designs, industrial designs, marks and geographical indications which have not been accepted as valid applications.

2. Regulations in Article 76 and Article 78 of this Circular shall apply to applications for registration of audio marks which have not yet been notified of content appraisal results./.
