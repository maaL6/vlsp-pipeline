                                                   ORDINANCE

                                         PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING VESSELS

  Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10;
  Pursuant to Resolution No. 11/2007/QH12 on the program for elaboration of laws and ordinances in 2008;
  The National Assembly Standing Committee promulgates the Ordinance on Procedures for Arrest of Seagoing Ships.

  Chapter 1.

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Ordinance provides for competence and procedures for arresting seagoing ships in order to ensure settlement of maritime complaints, application of provisional urgent measures, enforcement of civil judgments, provision of mutual legal assistance and competence and procedures for releasing arrested seagoing ships.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Ordinance applies to Vietnamese agencies, organizations and individuals and foreign agencies, organizations and individuals related to settlement of maritime complaints, application of provisional urgent measures, civil judgment enforcement and judicial assistance giving rise to the right to arrest seagoing ships.

  Article 3. Authority to decide arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The People’s Court of a province or centrally-run city (below referred to as provincial-level People’s Court) in which there is a seaport or inland waterway port (below referred to as port) where the seagoing vessel subject to arrest request is operating shall have the power to decide on the arrest of such vessel in order to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints, civil judgment enforcement and judicial delegation by foreign Courts.

  In case a port has several wharves in different provinces or centrally-run cities, the provincial-level People’s Court of the locality where the wharf where the seagoing vessel requested for arrest is operating shall have the power to decide the arrest of such seagoing vessel.

  2. The People’s Court which is settling the civil lawsuit or the provincial-level People’s Court of the place where the arbitration council accepts the dispute shall have the power to decide on the application of provisional urgent measures of arrest of seagoing ships.

  3. The Chief Justice of the Supreme People's Court shall consider deciding on the competent court to decide the arrest of seagoing ships in case there is a dispute over jurisdiction among provincial-level People's Courts.

  Article 4. Responsibilities for improper requests for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Persons making requests for arrest of seagoing ships shall be responsible before law for their requests. In case the requests for arrest of seagoing ships are improper and cause damage, the persons making such requests shall pay damages.

  2. All damage caused as a consequence of improper requests for arrest of seagoing ships shall be settled by the involved parties themselves. In case they fail to reach agreement and there is a dispute, they may request the court or arbitrator to settle it according to law provisions.

  3. If the court issues a decision to arrest a seagoing ship not in accordance with the reason for the request for arrest of seagoing ship or the seagoing ship has made a request for arrest but causes damage, the court shall pay damages according to law provisions.

  Article 5. Financial security measures for requests for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Persons making requests for arrest of seagoing ships shall apply financial security measures, except for cases specified at Points c and d, Clause 1, Article 44 of this Ordinance, in either or both of the following forms:

  a/ To submit to the court guarantee documents in form of assets of banks or other credit institutions or of other individuals, agencies or organizations;

  b/ To remit a sum of money, precious metal, gemstones or valuable papers according to the court's decision on forcible application of financial security measures into a safe account at the bank where the competent court is headquartered within forty eight hours after receiving such decision.

  In case of application of financial security measures for the purpose of arresting a seagoing ship on the last weekend or public holiday, the security assets shall be temporarily kept at the court; the court shall receive only the sum of money or valuable papers and seal and preserve them. On the following working day, the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship shall immediately remit such assets into a bank under the court's supervision.

  2. The value of financial security determined by the court is equivalent to the loss or damage which may arise as a consequence of an improper request for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. When deciding to release a seagoing ship which is being arrested, the judge shall consider the application of financial security measures for the purpose of arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clause 1 of this Article, unless otherwise agreed upon by the parties. On a case-by-case basis, the judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To maintain the application of financial security measures for the purpose of arrest of the seagoing ship if deeming that the request for arrest of the seagoing ship is improper and the value of financial security is sufficient or insufficient to compensate for the damage;

  b/ To return part of the value of financial security for the purpose of arrest of the seagoing ship if deeming that the request for arrest of the seagoing ship is improper and the value of financial security exceeds the liability to compensate for the damage;

  c/ To return the whole value of financial security for the purpose of arrest of the seagoing ship if deeming that the request for arrest of the seagoing ship is proper.

  Article 6. Fees for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Persons requesting arrest of seagoing ships shall pay charges according to law provisions

  2. Fees for arresting seagoing ships to secure the settlement of maritime complaints, application of provisional urgent measures, enforcement of civil judgments and performance of judicial entrustment by foreign courts shall be paid to Courts competent to decide on arrest of seagoing ships specified in Article 3 of this Ordinance within forty eight hours after the Courts request payment of fees.

  Article 7.- Supervising the observance of law in the arrest of seagoing ships.

  The People’s Procuracy shall supervise the compliance with laws on arrest of seagoing ships; exercise the right to make recommendations as prescribed by law in order to ensure the timely and lawful arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 8. Documents and evidence accompanying a request for arrest of a seagoing ship or a written request for release of a seagoing ship under arrest

  1. The request for arrest of a seagoing ship or the written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested must be accompanied by documents and evidence proving that the request for arrest of the seagoing ship or release of the seagoing ship being arrested is well-grounded and lawful.

  2. In case documents and evidence proving the request for arrest or release of a seagoing ship being arrested are in foreign languages, they must be enclosed with their Vietnamese versions which have been lawfully authenticated under Vietnamese law. Documents and papers made, issued or certified by foreign competent authorities under foreign laws must be consular legalized, except for cases of exemption under international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party.

  Article 9. Execution of decisions on arrest and release of seagoing ships

  1. Immediately after issuing a decision to arrest or release a seagoing ship under arrest, the court’s chief justice shall assign a court official to execute the delivery of the decision as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  2. Within twelve hours from the time of assignment, the court official shall arrive at the port and deliver two copies of the decision to the director of the maritime port office or the director of the inland waterway port (hereinafter referred to as the port authority) where the seagoing ship is operating. The director of the port authority shall execute the decision to arrest or release the seagoing ship under arrest in accordance with law and hand over one copy to the captain for execution.

  In case the court official cannot reach the port within the above-mentioned time limit, the decision may be sent by fax or email in accordance with law.

  3. Maritime, security, quarantine, customs, tax, culture and information, fire prevention and control, environmental protection and other state management agencies shall coordinate in executing the decision to arrest the seagoing ship upon request of the director of the port authority and submit to the direction of the director of the port authority in coordinating the execution of the decision to arrest the seagoing ship.

  The Vietnam Coast Guard shall coordinate in executing the decision to arrest the seagoing ship upon request of the director of the port authority.

  4. During the period in which the seagoing ship is arrested, the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator shall maintain the operation of the seagoing ship.

  5. The Government shall stipulate the execution of the court’s decision specified in Clause 2 of this Article and the handling of seagoing ships in cases where the ship owner abandons or puts it up for auction while being arrested.

  Article 10. Notification of the execution of decisions to arrest or release seagoing ships under arrest

  1. Directors of port authorities shall notify in writing the Courts, Vietnam Maritime Administration and relevant state management agencies at ports of the execution of decisions on arrest or release of seagoing ships being arrested; in case of arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement, they shall immediately notify such to civil judgment enforcement agencies so that these agencies can enforce civil judgments.

  2. Captains shall notify ship owners, ship lessees, ship operators and persons with related interests of the arrest or release of seagoing ships.

  Chapter II

    PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS, RELEASE OF SEAGOING SHIPS BEING ARRESTED AND ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS AGAIN TO ENSURE SETTLEMENT OF MARITIME COMPLAINTS

  Article 11. Maritime complaints giving rise to the right to request arrest of seagoing ships

  Maritime complaints giving rise to the right to request arrest of seagoing ships are complaints about:

  1. Salaries, repatriation expenses, social insurance premiums and other amounts payable to crew members, officers and other crew members in the crew of seagoing ships;

  2. Compensation for loss of life, injury and other health damage directly related to the operation of seagoing ships;

  3. Payload charges, maritime security charges, pilotage charges, port charges and other port charges;

  4. Shipwreck salvage charges;

  5. Losses and damage to property outside of the contract directly related to the operation of the ship;

  6. Damage or threat of damage caused by the ship to the environment, shore or related interests; measures applied to prevent, limit or remove this damage; compensation for such damage; costs of reasonable practical measures already applied or to be applied to restore the environment; loss incurred or likely to be incurred by a third party in connection with such damage; damage, costs or losses similar to those specified in this clause;

  7. Costs related to the lifting, moving, salvaging, destroying or rendering harmless the wrecked, stranded or abandoned ship, including any items on board and costs or expenses related to the preservation of the abandoned ship and costs for crew members of the ship;

  8. Agreements related to the use or hiring of ships, even if they are stipulated in the ship hire contract or in other forms;

  9. Agreements related to the transport of goods or passengers on board the ship, even if they are stipulated in the ship hire contract or in other forms;

  10. Losses or damage related to goods, including luggage transported on board the ship;

  11. General damage;

  12. Steering of seagoing ships;

  13. Use of maritime pilots;

  14. Goods, materials, foodstuffs, fuels and equipment (including civil engineering works) supplied or services provided for the operation, management, preservation and maintenance of seagoing ships;

  15. Building, modification, repair or provision of equipment for seagoing ships;

  16. Payment made on behalf of ship owners;

  17. Insurance premiums paid by ship owners or persons acting on behalf of ship owners or persons hiring bare-handed ships;

  18. Commissions and/or brokerage or agency fees related to seagoing ships which must be paid by ship owners, persons hiring bare-handed ships or authorized persons;

  19. Disputes over the ownership of seagoing ships;

  20. Disputes among co-owners of seagoing ships over the use of their seagoing ships or revenues generated from their seagoing ships;

  21. Mortgaging seagoing ships;

  22. Disputes arising from contracts for sale of seagoing ships.

  Article 12. Right to request arrest of seagoing ships for the purpose of ensuring settlement of maritime complaints

  Those who file maritime complaints specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance may request competent courts defined in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide to arrest seagoing ships to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints.

  Article 13. Conditions for arresting seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime complaints

  1. When there is a request for arrest of seagoing ships in order to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints specified in Article 12 of this Ordinance, the Court shall decide to arrest seagoing ships in the following cases:

  a) The ship owner is responsible for maritime claims at the time when such claims arise and remains the ship owner at the time of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  b) The bareboat hirer is responsible for maritime claims at the time when such claims arise and remains the bareboat hirer or the ship owner at the time of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  c) Such maritime claim is made on the basis of mortgage of the seagoing ship;

  d) Such maritime claim is related to the ownership or possession of the seagoing ship;

  dd) Such maritime claim is secured by a maritime lien related to the seagoing ship.

  2. The arrest of a seagoing ship shall also be carried out against one or more seagoing ships under the ownership of persons who are responsible for maritime claims and at the time when such claims arise, they are:

  a) Owners of the seagoing ships related to the arising of maritime claims;

  b) Bareboat hirers, time charterers or voyage charterers of the seagoing ships related to the arising of maritime claims.

  3. The provisions in Clause 2 of this Article shall not apply to maritime claims related to the ownership of seagoing ships.

  Article 14. Time limit for seagoing ship arrest to ensure settlement of maritime claims

  1. The time limit for seagoing ship arrest to ensure settlement of maritime claims is thirty days from the date of arrest of the seagoing ship.

  2. Within the time limit for seagoing ship arrest to ensure settlement of maritime claims, if the person requesting seagoing ship arrest initiates a lawsuit at court or requests arbitration to settle the dispute and continues to request seagoing ship arrest, the time limit for seagoing ship arrest to ensure settlement of maritime claims shall end when the court issues a decision on application or non-application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship.

  Article 15. Application for seagoing ship arrest to ensure settlement of maritime claims

  1. The person requesting seagoing ship arrest to ensure settlement of maritime claims shall make an application.

  2. An application for seagoing ship arrest to ensure settlement of maritime claims shall contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of making the application;

  b) Name of the Court receiving the request;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting arrest of the seagoing ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or ship operator in case the hirer or operator is required to pay a fee;

  h) Specific maritime claims giving rise to the right to request arrest of the seagoing ship and the maximum value of such maritime claims;

  i) Expected loss or damage that may arise due to the request for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. In case the person requesting arrest of a seagoing ship fails to fully and accurately know the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write down what he/she knows related to such matters.

  Article 16.- Sending written requests for arrest of seagoing ships in order to secure the settlement of maritime complaints and enclosed documents and evidence The person requesting the arrest of a seagoing ship in order to secure the settlement of a maritime complaint shall have to send such request and enclosed documents and evidence to the competent Court which decides on the arrest of the seagoing ship as prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 17. Receipt of written requests for arrest of seagoing ships in order to ensure settlement of maritime complaints

  Right after receiving a written request for arrest of a seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints and enclosed documents and evidence, the competent court shall decide to arrest the seagoing ship specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance and record it in the book of receipt of requests. The court's chief justice shall immediately assign a judge to settle the request.

  Article 18. Consideration of requests for arrest of seagoing ships in order to ensure settlement of maritime complaints

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving a written request for arrest of a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider the request and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Accept a written request for arrest of a seagoing ship in order to secure settlement of maritime complaints if it finds that all conditions for issuing a decision on arrest of the seagoing ship are met, request the requester to apply financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship and the seagoing ship arrest charge;

  b) Return the request for arrest of seagoing ships in order to secure the settlement of maritime complaints if deeming that conditions for issuing a decision on arrest of seagoing ships are not fully met or the handling of such request does not fall under the jurisdiction of such Court.

  2. In case of decision on return of application for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime complaints, the Court shall issue or immediately send such decision together with the application and enclosed documents and evidence to the requester.

  Article 19. Complaints and settlement of complaints against decisions on return of applications for arrest of seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime complaints

  1. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a decision on return of an application for arrest of seagoing ship to ensure settlement of maritime complaints, the requester may lodge a written complaint with the Court’s Chief Justice about such decision.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a written complaint about a decision on return of an application for arrest of seagoing ship to ensure settlement of maritime complaints and enclosed documents and evidence, the Court’s Chief Justice shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision on return of the application for arrest of seagoing ship to ensure settlement of maritime complaints;

  b/ To cancel the decision on return of the application for arrest of seagoing ship to ensure settlement of maritime complaints and receive back the application and enclosed documents and evidence for handling the application for arrest of seagoing ship.

  3. The complaint-settling decision of the Court’s Chief Justice is final.

  Article 20. Decisions on arrest of seagoing ships to ensure settlement of maritime complaints

  1. The judge shall immediately issue a decision to arrest a seagoing ship to secure the settlement of maritime complaints when the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship produces invoices and documents proving that he/she has taken financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, and has paid the seagoing ship arrest charge specified in Article 6 of this Ordinance.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing ship to secure the settlement of maritime complaints shall contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance;

  b/ Name of the issuing court;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the person requesting the arrest of the seagoing ship;

  d/ Maritime complaint giving rise to the right to request the court to arrest the seagoing ship;

  e/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested to be arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating on maritime waters;

  f/ Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  g/ Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  h/ Name, address and nationality of the hirer or operator of the bareboat;

  i/ Judgments of the Court and legal grounds for acceptance of the application;

  j/ Decisions of the Court.

  3. The decision on arrest of a sea-going ship to secure the settlement of a maritime complaint shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaints or petitions.

  4. The Court shall hand over two copies of the decision on arrest of a sea-going ship to secure the settlement of a maritime complaint to the port authority director for enforcement according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or send it to the person requesting the arrest of the sea-going ship; and send it to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the arrested sea-going ship involves foreign elements.

  Article 21. Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints, petitions about decisions on arrest of sea-going ships to secure the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. The captain, ship owner, hirer or operator of a bareboat may lodge a written complaint with the Court’s chief justice about the decision on arrest of a sea-going ship to secure the settlement of a maritime complaint. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty eight hours from the moment the captain receives the court’s decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level may make written recommendations to the Chief Justice of the Court on the decision to arrest a seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints. The time limit for making recommendations is forty eight hours from the time the Procuracy of the same level receives the decision of the Court.

  2. Within forty eight hours after receiving the written complaint or recommendation about the decision to arrest a seagoing ship, the Chief Justice of the Court shall consider and settle it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision to arrest the seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints;

  b/ To cancel the decision to arrest the seagoing ship in order to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints.

  3. The decision to settle the complaint or recommendation by the Chief Justice of the Court is final.

  Article 22. Grounds for releasing a seagoing ship being arrested in order to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. A seagoing ship being arrested in order to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints shall be immediately released when there is one of the following grounds:

  a/ After the ship owner, the ship hirer or the ship operator has taken measures to secure the replacement or fully pay the debt;

  b/ The property obligation of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator who has been guaranteed by another person to perform on their behalf or who has a written commitment from a reputable insurance organization. The Ministry of Finance shall notify the list of reputable insurance organizations;

  c/ At the request of the person who has requested the arrest of the sea-going ship;

  d/ The decision on arrest of the sea-going ship has been annulled;

  e/ The time limit for arrest of the sea-going ship under the court's decision has expired.

  2. The security measure to be substituted shall be agreed upon by the parties. In case the parties fail to reach agreement on the level and form of the security measure to be substituted, the court shall decide on such level and form but it must not exceed the value of the arrested sea-going ship or the property obligation which is the basis for the arrest of the sea-going ship in case the property obligation is smaller than the value of the sea-going ship.

  Article 23. Request for release of an arrested sea-going ship to secure the settlement of maritime complaints

  1. When there exists one of the grounds specified at Points a, b and c, Clause 1, Article 22 of this Ordinance, the ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator, the captain, the person who has requested the arrest of the sea-going ship and other related persons may request the release of the arrested sea-going ship. Such a request must be made in writing.

  2. A written request for release of an arrested sea-going ship must contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of making of the written request;

  b) Name of the requesting Court;

  c) Name and address of the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating on maritime waters;

  dd) Number and date of the decision arresting the seagoing ship and the issuing Court;

  e) Reason for requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  g) Guarantee of the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested.

  Article 24. Sending written requests for release of seagoing ships being arrested to secure settlement of maritime complaints

  The applicant for release of a seagoing ship being arrested to secure settlement of a maritime complaint shall send the written request enclosed with accompanying documents and evidence to the Court that has issued the decision on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  Article 25. Decision on release of a seagoing ship being arrested to secure settlement of a maritime complaint

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested to secure settlement of a maritime complaint, the Chief Justice of the Court shall assign a Judge to settle the release of the seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving the written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested to secure settlement of a maritime complaint and accompanying documents and evidence, if finding there are grounds, the Judge assigned to settle the release of the seagoing ship shall consider issuing a decision on release of the seagoing ship. In case of refusal without grounds, the Judge shall notify in writing the applicant for release of the seagoing ship thereof, clearly stating the reasons for refusal.

  3. A decision on release of a seagoing ship being arrested to secure settlement of a maritime complaint shall contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance;

  b/ Name of the issuing Court;

  c/ Legal grounds for the Court to issue the decision on release of the seagoing ship being arrested.

  d/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested, port where the seagoing ship is arrested and operating on maritime waters;

  dd/ Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e/ Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  g/ Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h/ Reason for release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i/ Decisions of the court.

  4. The decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints shall take effect immediately.

  5. The court shall hand over two copies of the decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints to the port authority director for implementation according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision immediately to the procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision immediately to the requester for the release of the seagoing ship being arrested; send such decision immediately to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship being arrested is released with foreign elements.

  Article 26.- Retention of seagoing ships for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of maritime claims

  1. Seagoing ships which have been seized for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of maritime claims already released or having been secured by substitute measures shall not be seized again on the basis of the same maritime claim, except for the following cases:

  a/ The total value of the substitute security already paid is still not enough to fulfill the property obligation if the total value of such security is smaller than the value of the ship to be released;

  b/ The guarantor fulfills the property obligation on behalf of the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator but fails to perform or cannot perform part or the whole of the guaranteed property obligation;

  c/ The release of the ship or the cancellation of the substitute security has been effected at the request of the requester for seizure of the seagoing ship on plausible grounds;

  d/ The requester for seizure of the seagoing ship cannot prevent the release of the ship or the cancellation of the security measure despite the application of necessary measures.

  2. A seagoing ship shall not be considered as being released if its release is not decided by a competent court or it escapes from the place of arrest, except for the cases specified at Points d and e, Clause 1, Article 22 of this Ordinance.

  3. The procedures for seizing a seagoing ship for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of maritime claims shall comply with the procedures for seizing a seagoing ship for the purpose of ensuring the settlement of maritime claims prescribed in this Chapter.

  Chapter III

              PROCEDURES FOR APPLICATION AND CANCELLATION OF PROVISIONAL URGENT MEASURES TO ARREST SEAGOING VESSELS

  Article 27. Provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing vessels

  Provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing vessels are applied in the course of settlement of civil lawsuits as prescribed in Clause 13, Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Code.

  Article 28. Right to request application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing vessels

  In the course of settlement of civil lawsuits, involved parties and their lawful representatives may request the courts which are settling such lawsuits to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing vessels to temporarily settle their urgent requests, protect evidences, preserve the existing conditions so as to avoid irrecoverable damage or to ensure judgment enforcement in one of the cases specified in Article 29 of this Ordinance.

  Article 29. Conditions for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing vessels

  When there is a request for arrest of seagoing vessels as provided for in Article 28 of this Ordinance, the court shall decide to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing vessels in the following cases:

  1. The seagoing ship is being arrested to secure the settlement of a maritime complaint while the person requesting the arrest has initiated a civil lawsuit at court;

  2. The ship owner is a person with property obligations in the case which is being settled and is still the ship owner at the time of application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  3. The bareboat hirer, the scheduled boat hirer, the cargo boat hirer or the ship operator is a person with property obligations in the civil case arising from the maritime complaint specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance and is still the bareboat hirer, the scheduled boat hirer, the cargo boat hirer or the ship operator or the ship owner at the time of application of the provisional urgent measure of arrest of the seagoing ship;

  4. The dispute is being settled in the case arising on the basis of the mortgage of such seagoing ship;

  5. The dispute is being settled in the case relating to the ownership or possession right of such seagoing ship.

  Article 30. Application for application of provisional urgent measures of arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The applicant for application of provisional urgent measures of arrest of seagoing ship shall make an application.

  2. An application for application of provisional urgent measures of arrest of seagoing ship must contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of filing of the application;

  b) Name of the Court receiving the application;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the person requesting arrest of the seagoing ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested to be arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating on sea;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the hirer or operator in case the hirer or operator demands payment;

  h) Specific disputes being settled in the lawsuit and their maximum value;

  i) Reasons for requesting the application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  k) Expected loss or damage that may arise due to improper request for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  l) Commitment of the requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  m) Number, date and contents of the decision of the Court to arrest the seagoing ship to ensure the settlement of maritime complaints (if any).

  3. In case the requester for application of provisional urgent measures does not know accurately and fully the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write down what he/she knows related to such matters.

  Article 31. Submission of applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence The requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall submit an application and enclosed documents and evidence to the Court which is handling the case.

  Article 32. Receipt of applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Immediately after receiving an application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the Court which is handling the case shall record it in the register of receipt of applications.

  2. In case a court is settling a case and has competence to decide on arrest of a seagoing ship as provided in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance, the application and enclosed documents and evidence shall be handled as follows:

  a/ Immediately handed over to the judge assigned to settle the case if the court receives the application and enclosed documents and evidence before opening a court hearing;

  b/ Immediately handed over to the trial panel which is hearing the case if the court receives the application and enclosed documents and evidence during the time of hearing the case.

  Article 33. Consideration of applications for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Within three days after receiving an application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider the application and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To accept the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship if seeing that all conditions for issuing a decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship are met, and at the same time request the applicant to provide financial security for the application and pay the seagoing ship arrest charge within the time limit set by the court without applying the time limits prescribed in Articles 5 and 6 of this Ordinance. When the requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship produces invoices and vouchers evidencing that he/she has provided financial security and paid the seagoing ship arrest charge, the judge shall immediately issue a decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  b/ To return the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship if deeming that all conditions for issuing a decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship are not met.

  2. After receiving the application for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the trial panel shall immediately consider it and issue one of the decisions specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. In case of decision on return of application for provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the Court shall issue or immediately send such decision together with the application and enclosed documents and evidence to the applicant; the Trial panel shall publicly notify the decision on return of the application for provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship at the hearing and record such notification in the minutes of the hearing.

  Article 34. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints, recommendations about decisions on return of applications for provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. Within three working days after receiving the decision on return of the application for provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the applicant may lodge a written complaint with the Chief Justice of the Court against such decision.

  2. Within three working days after receiving the written complaint about the decision on return of the application for provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the Chief Justice of the Court shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision on return of the application for provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  b/ To cancel the decision on return of the application for provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and receive back the application and enclosed documents and evidence for handling the request for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. After receiving the public notice of the decision on return of the application for provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship at the hearing, the applicant may lodge a complaint, the Procuracy may make a recommendation to the Trial panel about such decision and record it in the minutes of the hearing. The Trial panel shall promptly settle the complaint or recommendation and issue one of the decisions specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The decision on settlement of the complaint by the Chief Justice or the decision on settlement of the complaint or recommendation by the Trial panel is final.

  Article 35. Decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. A decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship must contain the following principal details:

  a/ Date of issuance;

  b/ Name of the issuing court;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  d/ Reason for requesting application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  dd/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  e/ Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  g/ The ship owner’s name, address and nationality;

  h/ Name, address and nationality of the hirer or operator of the bareboat;

  i/ Judgments of the Court and legal grounds for acceptance of the application;

  j/ Decisions of the Court.

  2. Decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaint or recommendation.

  3. The Court shall hand over two copies of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships to the port authority director for execution according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or send it to the requester for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships; and send it to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the arrested seagoing ship involves foreign elements.

  Article 36. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints and recommendations about decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. The captain, ship owner, hirer or operator of a seagoing ship may lodge a written complaint with the Court’s chief justice about the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship. The time limit for lodging a complaint is three working days after the captain receives the Court’s decision.

  The Procuracy of the same level shall be entitled to make written proposal to the Chief Justice of the Court on the decision to apply provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship. The time limit for making such proposal is 3 working days after the Procuracy of the same level receives the decision from the Court.

  2. Within three working days after receiving a written complaint or petition against a decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, the court’s chief justice shall consider settling it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To uphold the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  b) Cancel decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships.

  3. At the hearing, the settlement of complaints and petitions shall fall under the jurisdiction of the Trial Panel. The Trial Panel shall consider settling them and issue one of the decisions specified in clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The decision on settlement of complaints or petitions by the Chief Judge or the Trial Panel shall be final.

  Article 37. Grounds for annulment of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  Decision on application of provisional urgent measures for arrest of seagoing ships shall be annulled when conditions for application of provisional urgent measures no longer exist or at the request of persons requesting application of provisional urgent measures for arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 38. Request for cancellation of decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  1. The following persons have the right to make a written request for cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships:

  a/ Persons requesting the application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships;

  b/ Owners, ship lessees, ship operators, masters and other persons concerned when conditions for application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships no longer exist.

  2. A written request for cancellation of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships must contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of making the request;

  b/ Name of the court receiving the request;

  c/ Name and address of the person requesting cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  dd) Number and date of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e) Reasons for requesting cancellation of decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship.

  Article 39. Sending written requests for cancellation of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  The requester for cancellation of a decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall send such written request and enclosed documents and evidence to the court that has issued the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest such seagoing ship.

  Article 40. Handling written requests for cancellation of decisions on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships

  In case the Court is handling a case and receives a written request for cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship, such request shall be handled as follows:

  1. To immediately hand over the case to the Judge assigned to settle it if the Court receives a written request before opening the hearing;

  2. The Court shall immediately hand over the request to the Trial Panel in charge of the case.

  Article 41. Decision on cancellation of decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and enclosed documents and evidence, the assigned judge or the Trial Panel in charge of the case shall consider issuing a decision on cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship if there are sufficient grounds. In case of refusal due to lack of grounds, the judge or the Trial Panel must notify the requester in writing of the reason for refusal.

  2. A decision on cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall contain the following main contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision on cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship is operating.

  d/ Serial number and date of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  e/ Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g/ Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  h/ Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  i/ Reason for cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship;

  k/ Decisions of the court.

  3. The decision on cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship shall take effect immediately.

  4. The court shall deliver two copies of the decision on cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest a seagoing ship to the port authority director for enforcement according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision to the procuracy of the same level; issue or send it to the requester for cancellation of the decision on application of provisional urgent measures to arrest the seagoing ship; and send it to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the arrested seagoing ship is released involving foreign elements.

  Article 42. Application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships in case arbitrators settle disputes

  During the process of dispute settlement by arbitrators, involved parties and their lawful representatives may request competent courts defined in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide on the application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships. The procedures for deciding on the application of provisional urgent measures to arrest seagoing ships shall comply with the provisions of this Chapter.

  Chapter IV

              PROCEDURES FOR ARREST AND RELEASE OF SEAGOING VESSELS INVOLVED IN CRIME

  Article 43. Right to request arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  Through competent civil judgment enforcement agencies, judgment creditors under Vietnamese law on enforcement of civil judgments shall be entitled to request competent Courts specified in Clause 1 Article 3 of this Ordinance to decide to arrest seagoing ships for judgment enforcement.

  Article 44. Conditions for arresting seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. When there is a request for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment execution as prescribed in Article 43 of this Ordinance, the court shall decide to arrest seagoing ships in the following cases:

  a/ The ship owner being the judgment debtor for assets and still being the ship owner at the time of arrest of the sea-going ship;

  b/ The hirer of bare-boat, the hirer of scheduled ship, the hirer of cargo ship or the ship operator being the judgment debtor for assets in a civil case arising from maritime complaints specified in Article 11 of this Ordinance and still being the hirer of bare-boat, the hirer of scheduled ship, the hirer of cargo ship, the ship operator or the ship owner at the time of arrest of the sea-going ship;

  c/ The judgment execution obligation secured by the mortgage of such sea-going ship;

  d/ The judgment execution obligation being the return of such sea-going ship to the judgment creditor.

  2. The court shall decide to arrest a sea-going ship for judgment execution only when the civil judgment enforcement agency cannot apply the measure of distraint of assets or other coercive measures for judgment execution, except for cases specified at Points c and d, Clause 1 of this Article or the judgment debtor is overseas and has no other assets in Vietnam.

  Article 45. Applications for arrest of sea-going ships for judgment execution

  1. An applicant for arrest of a sea-going ship for judgment execution shall make an application enclosed with a copy of the court’s judgment or decision or a copy of the arbitral award.

  2. An application for arrest of a sea-going ship for judgment execution must contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of making the request;

  b) Name of the civil judgment enforcement agency receiving the request;

  c) Name, address and nationality of the seagoing ship arrest requester;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest and the port where it is operating on sea;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the hirer or operator in case the hirer or operator demands payment;

  h) Obligations regarding assets to be enforced according to the court’s judgment or decision or the arbitral award;

  i) Reason for request for arrest of seagoing ships;

  k) Expected loss or damage that may arise due to improper request for arrest of seagoing ships.

  3. In case the requester for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement does not know accurately and fully the contents specified at Points dd, e and g, Clause 2 of this Article, he/she shall write what he/she knows related to such issues.

  Article 46. Sending applications for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement and enclosed documents and evidences The requester for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement shall send the application and enclosed documents and evidences to the competent civil judgment enforcement agency for forwarding them to the competent Court prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 47. Receipt of applications for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. Immediately after receiving the application for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement and enclosed documents and evidences, the civil judgment enforcement agency shall record it in the book of receipt of application and issue a written transfer of the application and enclosed documents and evidences to the competent Court for decision on arrest of seagoing ships prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance. The written transfer of the application should clearly state the reason for failure to apply property distraint or other coercive measures for judgment enforcement.

  2. Immediately after receiving the written transfer of the application from the civil judgment enforcement agency together with the application for arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidences, the competent Court for decision on arrest of seagoing ships prescribed in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record it in the book of receipt of application. The Chief Justice of the Court shall immediately assign a Judge to settle the application.

  Article 48. Consideration of applications for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving the documents specified in Clause 2, Article 47 of this Ordinance, the Judge shall consider the application and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To accept the application for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement if he/she finds that all conditions for issuing a decision on arrest of the seagoing ship are met, request the applicant to take financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship and pay the seagoing ship arrest charge, except for cases where the application is not subject to financial security measures;

  b/ To return the application for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement if he/she finds that all conditions for issuing a decision on arrest of the seagoing ship are not met or the handling of the application does not fall under the jurisdiction of such Court.

  2. In case of deciding to return the application for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement, the Court shall send the decision to the competent civil judgment enforcement agency; and shall immediately issue or send such decision together with the application and enclosed documents and evidence to the requester.

  Article 49. Complaints, recommendations and settlement of complaints, recommendations about decisions to return applications for arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. Within twenty four hours after receiving the decision to return the application for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement, the requester may lodge a complaint, the competent civil judgment enforcement agency may make a recommendation to the Court’s Chief Justice about such decision. Complaints and recommendations must be made in writing.

  2. Within twenty four hours after receiving the written complaint or recommendation about the decision to return the application for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement and enclosed documents and evidence, the Court’s Chief Justice shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision to return the application for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  b/ To cancel the decision to return the written request for arrest of a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement and receive back the request together with enclosed documents and evidence for handling the arrest of the seagoing ship.

  3. The Chief Justice's decision on settlement of complaints or recommendations shall be final.

  Article 50. Decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement

  1. The judge shall immediately issue a decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement when the requester produces vouchers and/or documents evidencing his/her application of financial security measures for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, except for cases where financial security measures are not required and the seagoing ship arrest fee has been paid as prescribed in Article 6 of this Ordinance.

  2. A decision to arrest a seagoing ship for judgment enforcement shall contain the following principal details:

  a/ Date of issuance;

  b/ Name of the issuing court;

  c/ Name, address and nationality of the requester for arrest of the seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  d) Reasons for requesting arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement;

  dd) Name of the civil judgment enforcement agency with jurisdiction;

  e) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be arrested, port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  i) Name, address and nationality of the hirer and operator;

  k) The Court’s findings and legal grounds for accepting the petition;

  l) Decisions of Courts.

  3. The decision on arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement shall take effect immediately, even in case of complaint or proposal.

  4. The Court shall hand over two copies of the decision on arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement to the port authority director for enforcement under Article 9 of this Ordinance; send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level immediately; issue or send such decision to the person requesting the arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement and competent civil judgment enforcement agency; send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam immediately in case the seagoing ship is arrested involving foreign elements.

  Article 51. Complaints, proposals and settlement of complaints, proposals on decisions on arrest of seagoing ships for judgment enforcement

  1. The ship owner, the ship lessee, the ship operator and the captain have the right to lodge a written complaint with the Court’s chief justice about the decision on arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement. The time limit for lodging a complaint is forty eight hours from the time the captain receives the decision of the Court.

  The Procuracy of the same level has the right to make a written proposal to the Court’s chief justice about the decision on arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement. The time limit for making a proposal is forty eight hours from the time the Procuracy of the same level receives the decision of the Court.

  2. Within forty eight hours after receiving the written complaint or proposal about the decision on arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement, the Court’s chief justice shall consider settling it and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision on arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement;

  b/ To cancel the decision on arrest of seagoing ship for judgment enforcement.

  3. The decision on settlement of complaints or petitions by the Chief Justice of the Court is final.

  Article 52. Grounds for release of seagoing ships being arrested for judgment enforcement

  1. A seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall be immediately released when there is one of the following grounds:

  a/ After its owner, hirer or operator has taken measures to secure the replacement or fulfilled his/her obligation to enforce the judgment;

  b/ The property obligation of the ship owner, hirer or operator has been guaranteed by another person for fulfillment, or a written commitment of a reputable insurance organization is made;

  c/ At the request of the person who has requested the arrest of the seagoing ship.

  2. The measure of security for replacement shall be agreed upon by the parties. In case the parties fail to reach agreement on the level and form of security for replacement, the Court shall decide on such level and form but it must not exceed the value of the arrested seagoing ship or the property obligation which serves as the ground for arrest of the seagoing ship in case the property obligation is smaller than the value of the seagoing ship.

  Article 53. Request for release of seagoing ships being arrested for judgment enforcement

  1. When there is one of the grounds prescribed in Clause 1, Article 52 of this Ordinance, the ship owner, the ship lessee or the ship operator, the captain and other persons concerned shall have to request in writing the release of the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment execution.

  2. A written request for the release of a seagoing ship being arrested must contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of making the request;

  b/ Name of the court receiving the request;

  c/ Name and address of the person requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship being arrested operates on maritime waters;

  dd/ Serial number and date of the decision arresting the seagoing ship and the court that has issued such decision;

  e/ Reason for requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested.

  Article 54. Sending written requests for release of seagoing ships being arrested for judgment enforcement

  The petitioner for release of a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall send documents and accompanying evidence to the court that has issued the decision on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  Article 55. Decision to release seagoing ships being arrested for enforcement of judgments

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement, the court’s chief justice shall assign a judge to settle the release of such seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving a written request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement and enclosed documents and evidence, the judge shall consider and issue a decision to release the seagoing ship if finding there are grounds. In case of refusal without grounds, the judge shall notify in writing the requester of such refusal, clearly stating the reason for the refusal.

  3. A decision to release a seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall contain the following principal contents:

  a) Date of issuance of the decision;

  b) Name of the Court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested to be released; port where the seagoing ship is arrested to be released;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or ship operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for releasing the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i) Decisions of the Court.

  4. The decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement shall take effect immediately.

  5. The Court shall hand over two copies of the decision on release of the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement to the Director of the Port Department for enforcement under Article 9 of this Ordinance; immediately send such decision to the Procuracy of the same level; issue or send such decision to the requester for release of the seagoing ship being arrested for judgment enforcement and competent civil judgment enforcement agency; immediately send such decision to the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam in case the seagoing ship is released with foreign elements.

  Chapter V

   PROCEDURES FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS AND RELEASE OF SEAGOING SHIPS BEING ARRESTED FOR JUDGMENT ENFORCEMENT

  Section 1. JUDGMENT ENTREPRENEURSHIP TO FOREIGN COURTS FOR ARREST OF SEAGOING SHIPS

  Article 56. Execution of judicial entrustment to foreign Courts for arrest of seagoing ships

  1. In the course of settlement of a lawsuit at a Court or settlement of a dispute at an Arbitrator, if there is a request for seagoing ship custody, the competent Vietnamese Court specified in Clause 2, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall execute judicial entrustment to a competent foreign Court for arrest of seagoing ship.

  2. Vietnamese Courts shall execute judicial entrustment to foreign Courts for arrest of seagoing ships under Vietnamese law and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party or on the principle of reciprocity.

  Article 57. Judicial entrustment documents Judicial entrustment documents on arrest of seagoing ships shall contain the following principal contents:

  1. Date and place of making the written request for judicial assistance;

  2. Name and address of the Vietnamese Court requesting judicial assistance;

  3. Name and address of the foreign Court providing judicial assistance;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing vessel requested to be seized; port where the seagoing vessel is operating;

  5. Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  6. Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  7. Reason for the request for judicial assistance in seagoing vessel seizure;

  8. Time limit for seagoing vessel seizure;

  9. Persons shall be responsible for damage or loss caused by improper requests for arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 58. Procedures for judicial entrustment of arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Vietnamese competent courts executing judicial entrustment to foreign competent courts for arrest of seagoing ships shall make and send entrustment dossiers to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam. An entrustment dossier must contain the following documents:

  a/ A written request of a Vietnamese competent court for mutual legal assistance in arrest of seagoing ships;

  b/ A written judicial entrustment of arrest of seagoing ships;

  c/ Other papers at the request of the competent court of the requested State.

  2. Within ten working days after receiving an entrustment dossier of arrest of seagoing ships, the Ministry of Justice shall record in the judicial entrustment book, check the validity of the dossier and transfer it to the competent court of the foreign State under the provisions of an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and such foreign State are members or through the diplomatic channel. In case the dossier is invalid, the Ministry of Justice shall return it to the court that has made the dossier and clearly state the reason therefor.

  3. Within five working days after receiving a written notice of results of execution of judicial entrustment from the competent court of the foreign State, the Ministry of Justice shall transfer such notice to the competent court of Vietnam that has sent the entrustment dossier of arrest of seagoing ships.

  Section 2. EXECUTION OF MUTUAL LEGAL ASSISTANCE IN CAPTURE OF SEAGOING VESSELS

  Article 59. Principles of mutual legal assistance in capture of seagoing vessels

  1. Mutual legal assistance in capture of seagoing vessels between Vietnamese Courts and foreign Courts shall be executed on the principles of respect for independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of each other’s countries, non-interference in internal affairs of each other, equality and mutual benefit, compliance with international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory, and compliance with Vietnamese law.

  2. In case the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the foreign country have not yet signed or acceded to an international treaty providing for mutual legal assistance in capture of seagoing vessels, mutual legal assistance in capture of seagoing vessels may be accepted by Vietnamese Courts on the principle of reciprocity, but must not contravene Vietnamese law, international law and international practice.

  Article 60. Principles of execution of judicial requests for capture of seagoing vessels

  1. Vietnamese Courts shall execute judicial requests for capture of seagoing vessels from foreign Courts according to provisions of international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory or on the principle of reciprocity.

  2. Vietnamese Courts shall not accept execution of judicial requests for capture of seagoing vessels from foreign Courts in the following cases:

  a/ Execution of judicial requests for capture of seagoing vessels infringes upon the sovereignty of Vietnam or threatens Vietnam’s security;

  b/ The performance of judicial mandates on arrest of seagoing ships falls beyond the jurisdiction of Vietnamese courts.

  Article 61.- Procedures for judicial mandates on arrest of seagoing ships

  1. The foreign court’s judicial mandate to the Vietnamese court on arrest of seagoing ships must be made in writing and sent to the Vietnamese Ministry of Justice under the provisions of the international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party or Vietnamese law.

  2. Upon receiving the written judicial mandate on arrest of seagoing ships, the Vietnamese Ministry of Justice shall immediately transfer it to the Vietnamese court with competence to decide on arrest of seagoing ships defined in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  Article 62.- Written judicial mandates on arrest of seagoing ships

  A written judicial mandate on arrest of seagoing ships must contain the following principal contents:

  1. Date, month, year and place of making the written judicial mandate;

  2. Name and address of the foreign court issuing the judicial mandate;

  3. Name and address of the Vietnamese Court executing the judicial request;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the seagoing ship is operating;

  5. Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  6. Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  7. Reason for the judicial request for arrest of the seagoing ship;

  8. Time limit for arrest of the seagoing ship;

  9. Person responsible for damage caused by improper request for arrest of the seagoing ship.

  Article 63. Receipt of written request for arrest of seagoing ship

  1. Upon receipt of a written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence, the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam shall record it in the register and send a written notification of such request together with enclosed documents and evidence to the competent Court which shall decide on arrest of seagoing ships specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance.

  2. Upon receipt of a written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence, the competent Court which shall decide on arrest of seagoing ships specified in Clause 1, Article 3 of this Ordinance shall record it in the register. The Chief Justice of the Court shall immediately assign a Judge to settle the request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships.

  Article 64. Consideration of requests for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships

  1. Within forty eight hours after receiving a request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships and enclosed documents and evidence, the Judge shall consider and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To accept the request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships if he/she deems that such acceptance ensures the principles of mutual legal assistance and execution of judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships;

  b/ To return the request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships if he/she deems that such return violates the principles of mutual legal assistance or execution of judicial requests for arrest of seagoing ships or the handling of the request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships does not fall under the jurisdiction of such Court.

  2. In case of issuing a decision to return the request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships, the Court shall immediately send such decision together with the enclosed documents and evidence to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam for notification to the foreign Court.

  Article 65. Decisions on arrest of seagoing ships pursuant to judicial requests of foreign Courts

  1. The judge shall issue a decision on arrest of a seagoing ship under judicial mandate of a foreign Court immediately after the seagoing ship arrest requester has produced a bill or document evidencing his/her application for financial security for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship specified in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5, and has paid the seagoing ship arrest charge specified in Article 6 of this Ordinance in Vietnam, except otherwise provided for by an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a contracting party.

  2. A decision on arrest of a seagoing ship under judicial mandate of a foreign Court must contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance;

  b/ Name of the Vietnamese Court issuing the decision;

  c/ Name of the foreign Court providing judicial mandate;

  d/ Name, address and nationality of the seagoing ship arrest requester under judicial mandate of the foreign Court;

  dd/ Reason for the request for arrest of the seagoing ship;

  e/ Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship requested for arrest; port where the ship is operating;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  i) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or operator;

  k) Judgments of the Court and legal grounds for accepting the written request for judicial assistance in arrest of seagoing ships;

  l) Decisions of the Court.

  3. The decision on arrest of seagoing ships under judicial requests of foreign Courts shall take effect immediately even in case of complaints or petitions.

  4. The Court shall send two copies of the decision on arrest of seagoing ships under judicial requests of foreign Courts to the port authority director for execution according to Article 9 of this Ordinance; and send such decision to the Justice Ministry and the Procuracy of the same level.

  Article 66.- Complaints, petitions and settlement of complaints, petitions about decisions on arrest of seagoing ships under judicial requests of foreign Courts

  1. The captain, owner, hirer or operator of a seagoing ship may lodge complaints in writing to the chief justice of the court against the decision on arrest of the seagoing ship under judicial mandate of a foreign court. The time limit for lodging complaints is forty eight hours from the moment the captain receives the court's decision.

  The procuracy of the same level may make written recommendations to the chief justice of the court against the decision on arrest of the seagoing ship under judicial mandate of a foreign court. The time limit for making recommendations is forty eight hours from the moment the procuracy of the same level receives the court's decision.

  2. Within forty eight hours after receiving the written complaints or recommendations specified in Clause 1 of this Article, the chief justice of the court shall consider and settle them and issue one of the following decisions:

  a/ To uphold the decision on arrest of the seagoing ship under judicial mandate of a foreign court;

  b/ To cancel the decision on arrest of the seagoing ship under judicial mandate of a foreign court.

  3. The decision on settlement of complaints or recommendations by the chief justice of the court is final.

  Article 67. Grounds for release of seagoing ships arrested under judicial mandate A seagoing ship arrested under judicial mandate shall be immediately released when there appears one of the following grounds:

  1. The decision on arrest of the seagoing ship is cancelled.

  2. The time limit for arrest of seagoing ships under the court's decision has expired;

  3. At the request of the foreign court, which has entrusted the arrest of seagoing ships;

  Article 68. Request for release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial entrustment In cases specified in Clause 3, Article 67 of this Ordinance, the foreign court shall make a written request for release of the seagoing ship being arrested under judicial entrustment. A written request for release of a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial entrustment must contain the following principal contents:

  1. Date and place of making the written request;

  2. Name and address of the foreign court requesting the release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  3. Name of the Vietnamese court receiving the written request;

  4. Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship being arrested; port where the seagoing ship being arrested is operating on maritime waters;

  5. Serial number and date of the decision to arrest the seagoing ship and the court issuing such decision;

  6. Reasons for requesting the release of a seagoing ship being arrested.

  Article 69. Sending written requests for the release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandate Foreign courts shall send written requests for the release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandate and enclosed documents and evidence to the Ministry of Justice of Vietnam for the latter to forward them to the court that has issued the decision on arrest of such seagoing ship.

  Article 70. Decisions on the release of seagoing ships being arrested under judicial mandate

  1. Immediately after receiving a written request for the release of a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate, the chief justice of the court shall assign a judge to settle the matter of the release of such seagoing ship.

  2. Within twenty-four hours after receiving the written request for the release of a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate and enclosed documents and evidence, the assigned judge shall consider issuing a decision on the release of such seagoing ship if he/she finds there are grounds for doing so. In case of refusal for no ground, the judge shall notify in writing the requester of the refusal, clearly stating the reason(s) for the refusal.

  3. A decision on the release of a seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate must contain the following principal contents:

  a/ Date of issuance;

  b/ Name of the Vietnamese court issuing the decision;

  c) Legal grounds for the Court to issue a decision on release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  d) Name, nationality, IMO number, tonnage and other characteristics of the seagoing ship to be released; port where the seagoing ship is arrested to be released;

  dd) Name, address and nationality of the ship owner;

  e) Name, address and nationality of the ship hirer or ship operator;

  g) Name, address and nationality of the captain;

  h) Reason for release of the seagoing ship being arrested;

  i) Decisions of the Court.

  4. The decision on release of the seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate of a foreign Court shall take effect immediately.

  5. The Court shall deliver two copies of the decision to release the seagoing ship being arrested under judicial mandate of a foreign Court to the port authority director for execution according to Article 9 of this Ordinance, and send such decision to the Justice Ministry and the Procuracy of the same level.

  Chapter 6.

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 71. Effect

  This Ordinance takes effect on July 1, 2009.

  Article 72. Implementation guidelines

  The Government, the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuracy shall, within the ambit of their respective functions and tasks, guide the implementation of this Ordinance.
