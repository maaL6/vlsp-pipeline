              Law No. 13/2012/QH13                           Hanoi, June 20, 2012



                                                       LAW

                                              LEGAL ASSESSMENT

  Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on judicial expertise.

  Chapter I

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Law provides for judicial experts; judicial assessment organizations; subject-matter judicial experts and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations; judicial assessment activities; judicial assessment costs, benefits and policies in judicial assessment activities and responsibilities of regulatory agencies for judicial assessment organizations and activities.

  Article 2. Interpretation of terms

  For the purposes of this Law, the terms below shall be construed as follows:

  1. Judicial assessment means a judicial expert’s use of knowledge, means, scientific, technical and professional methods to make professional conclusions on matters related to investigation, prosecution, adjudication and enforcement of criminal judgments, settlement of civil cases and administrative cases at the request of presiding authorities or persons or at the request of assessment requesters according to regulations of this Law.

  2. Assessment requester means presiding authorities or persons.

  3. Assessment petitioner means a person who has the right to request an assessment himself/herself after requesting a presiding authority or person to request an assessment but his/her request is rejected. Persons who have the right to request an assessment themselves include involved parties in civil cases and administrative cases, civil plaintiffs, civil defendants, persons with relevant rights and obligations in criminal cases or their lawful representatives, except for cases where the request for assessment is related to determination of criminal liability of suspects or defendants.

  4. Judicial assessment organizations and individuals include judicial experts, subject-matter judicial experts, public judicial assessment organizations, non-public judicial assessment organizations and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations.

  5. Judicial assessors include public judicial assessors and subject-matter judicial assessors.

  6. Public judicial assessor is a person who satisfies the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law and is appointed by a competent authority to conduct judicial assessment.

  7. Subject-matter judicial assessor is a person who satisfies the criteria specified in Clause 1 or Clause 2 Article 18 and Article 20 of this Law and is solicited or requested for judicial assessment.

  8. Subject-matter judicial assessment organization is an agency or organization that satisfies the criteria specified in Article 19 and Article 20 of this Law and is solicited or requested for judicial assessment.

  Article 3. Principles of conducting judicial assessment

  1. Comply with laws and professional standards.

  2. Be honest, accurate, objective, impartial, and timely.

  3. Conclude only on professional matters within the scope of the request.

  4. Be responsible to the law for the judicial assessment conclusion.

  Article 4. Responsibilities of individuals and organizations for judicial assessment activities

  1. Individuals and organizations solicited or requested to conduct judicial assessment shall be responsible for receiving and conducting judicial assessment in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  2. Other individuals and organizations shall be responsible for enabling judicial assessors to conduct judicial assessment in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws.

  Article 5. State policies on judicial assessment activities

  1. The State shall invest in and develop public judicial assessment system in fields where there is a large and regular demand for judicial assessment to meet requirements of legal proceedings; adopt preferential policies to enable non-public judicial assessment organizations to develop.

  2. The State shall adopt policies to prioritize training and professional development for judicial assessors.

  Article 6. Prohibited acts

  1. Refusing to make judicial assessment conclusions without a legitimate reason.

  2. Intentionally making false judicial assessment conclusions.

  3. Intentionally prolonging the time for conducting judicial assessment.

  4. Abusing the conduct of judicial assessment for personal gain.

  5. Disclosing information secrets known when conducting judicial assessment.

  6. Inciting or forcing judicial assessors to make false judicial assessment conclusions.

  7. Interfering with or obstructing judicial assessors in conducting judicial assessment.

  Chapter II

                                           LEGAL ASSESSOR

  Article 7. Criteria for appointment of judicial assessors

  1. Vietnamese citizens permanently residing in Vietnam who fully meet the following criteria may be considered and appointed as judicial experts:

  a) Have good health and moral qualities;

  b) Hold a university degree or higher and have at least 5 years of professional experience in the training field.

  In case the person proposed for appointment as forensic examiner, forensic psychiatric examiner or criminal technique examiner has directly assisted in assessment activities at a forensic examination organization, forensic psychiatric examination organization or criminal technique examination organization, the period of actual professional experience shall be three years or more;

  c) Persons proposed for appointment as judicial experts in forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry and criminal techniques must possess certificates of training or refresher training in expertise skills.

  2. A person who falls into one of the following cases shall not be appointed as a judicial assessor:

  a) Lacking civil act capacity or having limited civil act capacity;

  b) Being examined for penal liability; having been convicted but not yet granted a remission of criminal record for unintentional crimes or less serious intentional crimes; having been convicted of serious crimes, very serious crimes or particularly serious crimes committed intentionally;

  c) Being subject to administrative handling measures such as education at commune, ward or township level, placement in compulsory detoxification centers or compulsory education institutions.

  3. Ministers and Heads of ministerial-level agencies shall elaborate on clause 1 of this Article for judicial experts working in fields under their management after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  Article 8. Dossiers of request for appointment of judicial experts

  1. Written request for appointment of judicial expert.

  2. Copy of university degree or higher in a major relevant to the proposed area of expertise.

  3. Curriculum vitae and judicial record card.

  4. Written certification of the actual period of professional operation of the agency or organization where the person proposed for appointment works.

  5. Certificate of training or refresher course in judicial assessment skills for persons proposed for appointment as judicial assessors in forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry and criminal techniques.

  6. Other papers proving that the proposed appointees meet the criteria prescribed by the Ministers and Heads of ministerial-level agencies competent to manage the assessment domain.

  Article 9. Competence and order of, procedures for appointment of judicial assessors

  1. The Minister of Health shall appoint forensic examiners and forensic psychiatrists working at central agencies.

  The Minister of Public Security shall appoint criminal technique assessors working at central agencies.

  Ministers and heads of ministerial-level agencies shall appoint judicial assessors working in other domains at central agencies under their management.

  Presidents of People's Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities (hereinafter referred collectively to as provincial level) shall appoint local judicial assessors.

  2. The Ministry of National Defense and the Ministry of Public Security shall select persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law and propose the Minister of Health to appoint forensic examiners under their management.

  The Ministry of National Defense shall select persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law and propose the Minister of Public Security to appoint criminal technical experts under their management.

  Heads of units affiliated to ministries and ministerial-level agencies assigned to manage judicial assessment activities shall select persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law and propose ministers or heads of ministerial-level agencies to appoint judicial experts in the field of assessment under their management.

  Heads of specialized agencies affiliated to provincial People’s Committees managing the field of judicial assessment shall take charge and cooperate with directors of Departments of Justice in selecting persons who fully meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law, receive applications from persons applying for appointment as judicial experts specified in Article 8 of this Law, and propose Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees to appoint judicial experts in their provinces.

  Within 20 days from the date on which the valid application is received, ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies and Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees shall decide to appoint judicial experts. In case of refusal, they must notify such in writing to the applicant and provide explanation.

  3. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies and provincial People’s Committees shall prepare and publish lists of judicial experts on their web portals and at the same time send them to the Ministry of Justice for compilation of a general list of judicial experts.

  Article 10. Removal of judicial experts

  1. Cases subject to removal of judicial experts:

  a) Failing to meet the criteria specified in Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law;

  b) Falling into one of the cases specified in Clause 2 Article 7 of this Law;

  c) Being subject to a disciplinary measure at the level of reprimand or more severe, or administrative penalties for intentional violations of laws on judicial assessment;

  d) Committing one of the acts specified in Article 6 of this Law;

  dd) At the request of the judicial assessor who is a civil servant, public employee, army officer, people’s police officer, professional serviceman, or national defense worker and has decided to resign for retirement or discharge.

  2. The application for dismissal of a judicial assessor includes:

  a) A written request for dismissal of the judicial assessor from the agency or organization that proposed the appointment;

  b) Documents and papers proving that the judicial assessor falls into one of the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. The Minister of Public Security and the Minister of National Defense shall consider proposing the Minister of Health to dismiss forensic examiners under their management.

  The Minister of National Defense shall consider proposing the Minister of Public Security to dismiss criminal technical experts under their management.

  Ministers and Heads of ministerial-level agencies shall dismiss judicial experts working in central authorities within their management according to requests of Heads of units affiliated to ministries or ministerial-level agencies assigned to manage judicial expertise activities.

  Presidents of provincial People’s Committees shall dismiss local judicial experts at the request of heads of specialized agencies affiliated to People’s Committees after heads of specialized agencies reach agreement with directors of Departments of Justice.

  4. Within 10 days from the date on which competent persons receive valid applications, ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies and chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees shall consider deciding to dismiss judicial experts and adjust lists of judicial experts on web portals of ministries, ministerial-level agencies and provincial People’s Committees, and concurrently send them to the Ministry of Justice for adjustment to the general list of judicial experts.

  Article 11. Rights and obligations of judicial experts

  1. Conduct judicial expertise as per orders or requests of ordering parties or requesting parties or assignments by agencies or organizations that are ordered or requested to conduct judicial expertise.

  2. Refuse to conduct judicial expertise if contents to be expertised exceed their professional competence; subjects of judicial expertise or relevant documents provided are inadequate or invalid for making expert conclusions; or time is not sufficient for conducting judicial expertise or there are other legitimate reasons. In case of refusal to conduct judicial expertise, within 5 working days from the date on which decisions on ordering or requesting judicial expertise are received, notify ordering parties or requesting parties in writing and clearly state reasons.

  3. Participate in training courses on judicial expertise skills and legal knowledge.

  4. Establish a judicial expertise office when fully satisfying the conditions specified in Article 15 of this Law.

  5. Establish and join a private judicial expert association in accordance with the law on associations.

  6. Enjoy benefits and policies prescribed by this Law and other relevant laws.

  7. The rights and obligations prescribed in Article 23 and Clause 1 Article 34 of this Law.

  Chapter III

                                       JUDICIAL EXPERTISE ORGANIZATIONS

  Section 1. PUBLIC JUDICIAL EXPERTISE ORGANIZATIONS

  Article 12. Public judicial assessment organizations

  1. Public judicial assessment organizations shall be established by competent state authorities in the fields of forensic medicine, forensic psychiatry and criminal techniques.

  Where necessary, Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies and Chairpersons of provincial People's Committees shall consider deciding on establishment or propose establishment of public judicial assessment organizations in other fields after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  2. Public judicial assessment organizations in the field of forensic medicine include:

  a) National Forensic Institute affiliated to the Ministry of Health;

  b) Provincial Forensic Centers;

  c) Military Forensic Institute affiliated to the Ministry of National Defense;

  d) Forensic Assessment Center affiliated to Institute of Criminal Science, the Ministry of Public Security.

  3. Public forensic psychiatric assessment organizations include:

  a) Central Forensic Psychiatry Institute affiliated to the Ministry of Health;

  b) Regional Forensic Psychiatry Centers affiliated to the Ministry of Health.

  Pursuant to the request for forensic psychiatric assessment of procedural activities and actual conditions of regions and zones nationwide, the Minister of Health shall consider and decide on the establishment of regional forensic psychiatry centers after reaching agreement with the Minister of Justice.

  4. Public forensic criminalistics assessment organizations include:

  a) Institute of Criminal Science affiliated to the Ministry of Public Security;

  b) Department of Forensic Criminalistics affiliated to provincial police authorities;

  c) Department of Forensic Criminalistics Assessment affiliated to the Ministry of National Defense.

  5. Based on local demand and practical conditions, the Department of Criminal Techniques affiliated to the provincial police authority shall have forensic examiner(s) perform forensic examination of death.

  6. Public judicial assessment organizations shall have their own seals and accounts as prescribed by law.

  7. The Government shall provide detailed regulations on functions, tasks, organizational structure, working regime of public judicial assessment organizations specified in this Article.

  Article 13. Assurance of material facilities for public judicial assessment organizations

  1. Public judicial assessment organizations shall be assured by the State of material facilities, funding, equipment, means and other necessary conditions for performance of judicial assessment.

  2. Funding for operation of public judicial assessment organizations shall be ensured from the state budget and other sources as prescribed by law.

  3. The Ministry of Health shall provide regulations on material facility, equipment and means requirements for public judicial assessment organizations in the fields of forensic medicine and forensic psychiatry.

  The Ministry of Public Security shall provide regulations on material facility, equipment and means requirements for public judicial assessment organizations in the field of criminal techniques.

  Section 2. ORGANIZATION OF PUBLIC JUDICIAL EXPERTISE FIELD

  Article 14. Judicial expertise office

  1. A judicial expertise office is a public non-business judicial expertise organization established in the fields of finance, banking, construction, antiques, relics and copyright.

  2. A judicial expertise office established by 01 judicial expert shall be organized and operate as a sole proprietorship. A judicial expertise office established by 02 or more judicial experts shall be organized and operate as a partnership.

  The legal representative of a judicial expertise office is its head. The head of a judicial expertise office must be a judicial expert.

  Article 15. Conditions for establishment of a judicial expertise office

  1. A judicial expert may establish a judicial expertise office when fully satisfying the following conditions:

  a) Having worked as a judicial expert for at least 05 years in the field proposed for establishment of the office;

  b) The establishment plan is made in accordance with Point d Clause 2 Article 16 hereof.

  2. Officials, public officials, public employees, army officers, people’s police officers, professional servicemen, and national defense workers are not allowed to establish judicial assessment offices.

  Article 16. Licensing establishment of judicial assessment offices

  1. Chairpersons of provincial People's Committees where headquarters of judicial assessment offices are located shall consider licensing establishment of judicial assessment offices at request of directors of Departments of Justice.

  2. Judicial assessors applying for establishment of judicial assessment offices shall submit application for establishment to Departments of Justice. Application consists of:

  a) Application for establishment;

  b) Copies of decision on appointment as judicial assessors;

  c) Draft regulations on organization and operation of judicial assessment offices;

  d) The plan for establishment of the judicial assessment office shall clearly state its establishment purpose; expected name, personnel and location; conditions regarding facilities, equipment and means for judicial assessment in accordance with regulations of ministries or ministerial-level agencies specialized in judicial assessment and implementation plan.

  3. Within 30 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application dossier, the director of the provincial-level Justice Department shall consider and appraise the application for establishment of the judicial assessment office, reach agreement with the head of the specialized agency of the provincial-level People's Committee in charge of judicial assessment, and submit it to the president of the provincial-level People's Committee for consideration and decision.

  Within 15 days after the provincial-level Justice Department submits the dossier, the president of the provincial-level People's Committee shall consider and decide to permit the establishment of the judicial assessment office. In case of refusal, he/she shall issue a written notice clearly stating the reason therefor. The applicant may lodge complaints or initiate lawsuits according to law.

  Article 17. Operation registration of judicial assessment offices

  1. Within one year after the president of the provincial-level People's Committee issues a decision permitting the establishment of a judicial assessment office, such office shall register its operation at the provincial-level Justice Department.

  Past this time limit, if the judicial assessment office fails to register its operation within one year after the president of the provincial-level People's Committee issues a decision permitting the establishment of the office, the decision permitting the establishment of the judicial assessment office shall cease to be effective.

  2. A judicial assessment office shall send an application dossier for operation registration to the provincial-level Justice Department. Such a dossier comprises:

  a/ An application for operation registration;

  b) Regulations on organization and operation of the judicial assessment office;

  c) Documents proving eligibility for operation of the judicial assessment office according to the establishment scheme specified in Point d Clause 2 Article 16 of this Law;

  d) Copies of decisions permitting establishment of the judicial assessment office.

  3. Within 30 days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application, the Department of Justice shall take charge and cooperate with the specialized agency of the provincial-level People's Committee in charge of judicial assessment in inspecting the satisfaction of conditions specified in Point d Clause 2 Article 16 of this Law and issue an operation registration certificate; in case of refusal, it shall notify in writing and clearly state the reason, and concurrently report to the President of the provincial-level People's Committee for consideration and decision on revocation of the decision permitting establishment. The applicant may file complaints or lawsuits according to law.

  4. A judicial assessment office may commence operation from the date of issuance of its operation registration certificate.

  Chapter IV

   AD HOC JUDICIAL ASSESSORS AND AD HOC JUDICIAL ASSESSMENT ORGANIZATIONS

  Article 18. Ad hoc judicial assessors

  1. A Vietnamese citizen residing in Vietnam who satisfies the following criteria may be selected as a subject-matter judicial assessor:

  a) Having good health and moral qualities;

  b) Holding a university degree or higher and having at least five (05) years of practical experience in the field of training.

  2. In cases where a person does not hold a university degree but has in-depth knowledge and extensive practical experience in the field requiring assessment, he/she may be selected as a subject-matter judicial assessor.

  3. Subject-matter judicial assessors shall conduct assessments upon request or order in accordance with this Law. Subject-matter judicial assessors have the rights and obligations prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7 Article 11 of this Law.

  Article 19. Organization of subject-matter judicial assessment

  1. An organization conducting subject-matter judicial assessment must satisfy the following conditions:

  a) Having legal entity status;

  b) Have professional activities suitable with contents solicited or requested for judicial assessment;

  c) Have conditions regarding professional officers and physical foundations to ensure the performance of judicial assessment.

  2. The case-specific judicial assessment organization shall conduct judicial assessment at the request as prescribed in this Law. The head of the organization shall be responsible for receiving and assigning persons to perform judicial assessment.

  3. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies, specialized agencies of provincial People’s Committees shall conduct judicial assessment only at the request of the ordering party.

  Article 20. Preparation and announcement of lists of case-specific judicial assessors and case-specific judicial assessment organizations

  1. The Ministry of Construction, the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Information and Communications, the Ministry of Planning and Investment, the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment, the Ministry of Transport, the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, Vietnam State Bank, other ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Committees shall select, prepare and annually announce lists of case-specific judicial assessors and case-specific judicial assessment organizations in fields under their management to meet requirements for judicial assessment of proceedings.

  The list enclosed with information on judicial assessment specialization, experience and capacity of case-specific judicial assessors and case-specific judicial assessment organizations shall be posted on web portals of ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Committees and concurrently sent to the Ministry of Justice for compilation of general list.

  2. In special cases, the ordering party may request individuals or specialized organizations eligible not included in the announced list to conduct judicial assessment but must clearly state reasons therefor.

  At the request of the presiding authorities, presiding officers, ministries, ministerial agencies and specialized agencies of provincial-level People's Committees managing the field of assessment shall introduce individuals or organizations eligible for conducting assessment outside the published list.

  Chapter V

                                     JUDICIAL ASSESSMENT ACTIVITIES

  Article 21. Rights and obligations of ordering parties

  1. An ordering party has the right to:

  a/ Order an individual or organization specified in Clause 4, Article 2 of this Law to conduct assessment;

  b/ Request the individual or organization specified at Point a, Clause 1 of this Article to provide an assessment conclusion in accordance with the contents and within the prescribed time limit;

  c/ Request the individual or organization that has conducted judicial assessment to explain the assessment conclusion.

  2. The ordering party shall have the following obligations:

  a) Select an organization or individual to conduct judicial assessment in conformity with the nature and requirements of the case subject to judicial assessment;

  b) Issue a decision on ordering judicial assessment in writing;

  c) Provide information and documents related to the subject matter of judicial assessment at the request of the organization or individual conducting judicial assessment;

  d) Advance judicial assessment costs when ordering judicial assessment; pay promptly and fully judicial assessment costs to the organization or individual conducting judicial assessment upon receipt of the judicial assessment conclusion;

  dd) Ensure the safety of the judicial assessor during the process of conducting judicial assessment or when participating in proceedings as a judicial assessor.

  Article 22. Rights and obligations of requesting parties

  1. A requesting party may send a written request to the presiding agency or presiding person to order judicial assessment. If the presiding agency or presiding person does not accept the request, it must notify the requesting party in writing within 7 days. Upon the expiration of such time limit or from the date of receipt of the notice of refusal to order judicial assessment, the requesting party may independently request judicial assessment.

  2. The requesting party shall have the rights to:

  a) Request the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider to provide the judicial assessment conclusion within the time limit agreed upon and in accordance with the contents requested;

  b) Request the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider to explain the judicial assessment conclusion;

  c) Request the Court to summon the judicial assessor who has conducted the judicial assessment to participate in the hearing to explain and present the judicial assessment conclusion;

  d) Request the presiding agency or presiding officer to re-request the judicial assessment; request supplementary assessment as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 29 of this Law.

  3. The requesting party shall have the obligations to:

  a) Provide information and documents related to the subject matter of judicial assessment at the request of the judicial assessor and take responsibility for the accuracy of the information and documents provided;

  b) Pay an advance on judicial assessment costs when requesting the judicial assessment; pay promptly and fully the judicial assessment costs to the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider when receiving the judicial assessment conclusion.

  4. The person requesting judicial assessment may only exercise the right to request judicial assessment before the Court issues a decision to bring the case to first-instance trial.

  Article 23. Rights and obligations of judicial assessors when conducting judicial assessment

  1. A judicial assessor has the rights to:

  a) Select necessary and appropriate methods for conducting judicial assessment according to the contents requested in the judicial assessment;

  b) Use results of additional experiments or tests or professional conclusions conducted by other individuals or organizations to serve the judicial assessment;

  c) Independently make judicial assessment conclusions.

  2. A judicial assessor has the obligations to:

  a) Comply with the principles of conducting judicial assessment;

  b) Conduct assessment in accordance with the contents of the request for assessment;

  c) Conduct and provide assessment conclusions within the prescribed time limit; in cases where additional time is necessary for conducting assessment, promptly notify the ordering party or requesting party;

  d) Prepare an assessment file;

  dd) Preserve the specimens of assessment and documents related to the assessment case;

  e) Not to notify the results of assessment to others, unless it is agreed in writing by the ordering party or requesting party;

  g) Take personal responsibility for the assessment conclusions issued. In cases where a false conclusion is intentionally made, causing damage to individuals or organizations, compensation or reimbursement must be provided in accordance with the law.

  3. In addition to the rights and obligations specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, judicial assessors have other rights and obligations as prescribed by the law on proceedings.

  Article 24. Rights and obligations of organizations solicited or requested for judicial assessment

  1. Organizations solicited or requested for judicial assessment shall have the rights to:

  a) Request persons soliciting or requesting judicial assessment to provide necessary information and documents for the judicial assessment;

  b) Refuse to conduct judicial assessment if conditions necessary for the judicial assessment are not fully satisfied;

  c) Receive advances on judicial assessment expenses upon receiving solicitation or request for judicial assessment; receive timely and full payment of judicial assessment expenses when returning judicial assessment results.

  2. Organizations solicited or requested for judicial assessment shall have the obligations to:

  a) Receive and assign persons with professional qualifications appropriate to the contents of the solicitation or request for judicial assessment within their organizations to conduct judicial assessment and take responsibility for such persons’ professional qualifications; assign persons responsible for coordinating the conduct of judicial assessment in cases where multiple persons are required to conduct a judicial assessment case;

  b) Ensure equipment, facilities, and other necessary conditions for conducting judicial assessment;

  c) Pay compensation for damage caused by judicial assessors assigned by such organizations who intentionally make incorrect judicial assessment conclusions, causing damage to individuals or organizations.

  d) Notify the ordering party or requesting party in writing within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the decision on ordering or requesting judicial assessment and clearly state the reason in case of refusal to accept the order or request for judicial assessment.

  Article 25. Ordering of judicial assessment

  1. The ordering party shall decide to order a judicial assessment in writing and send the decision together with the subject matter of assessment and relevant documents and objects (if any) related to the individual or organization conducting the assessment.

  2. A decision on ordering a judicial assessment must contain the following contents:

  a) Name of the ordering agency; full name of the person competent to order the judicial assessment;

  b) Name of the organization; full name of the person requested to conduct the judicial assessment;

  c) Name and characteristics of the subject matter of assessment;

  d) Name of relevant documents or comparison samples enclosed (if any);

  dd) Contents of the request for judicial assessment;

  e) Date of the request for judicial assessment and time limit for issuance of the judicial assessment conclusion.

  3. In case of a request for additional or repeated judicial assessment, the decision on the request for judicial assessment must clearly state that it is an additional or repeated request for judicial assessment.

  Article 26. Request for judicial assessment in civil cases, administrative cases and criminal cases

  1. The requesting party shall send a written request for judicial assessment enclosed with the subject matter of assessment, relevant documents and objects (if any), and copies of documents proving their status as involved parties in civil cases or administrative cases, civil plaintiffs, civil defendants, persons with related rights and obligations in criminal cases, or their lawful representatives to the organization or individual conducting the judicial assessment.

  2. A written request for judicial assessment must contain the following contents:

  a) Name of the organization or full name of the requesting party;

  b) Contents of the request for judicial assessment;

  c) Name and characteristics of the object of assessment;

  d) Name of relevant documents or comparison samples enclosed (if any);

  dd) Date of request for assessment and time limit for issuance of assessment conclusions;

  e) Signature, full name of the person requesting the assessment.

  Article 27. Delivery and receipt of dossiers and objects solicited or requested for assessment

  1. Dossiers and objects solicited or requested for assessment shall be delivered and received directly or sent to individuals or organizations conducting the assessment by post.

  2. The direct delivery and receipt of dossiers and objects solicited or requested for assessment must be recorded in writing. A record of delivery and receipt must contain the following contents:

  a) Time and place of delivery and receipt of the assessment dossier;

  b) Full name of representative of the delivering party and the receiving party;

  c) Decision on solicitation or written request for assessment; subject matter to be assessed; relevant documents and items;

  d) Method of preservation of the subject matter to be assessed, relevant documents and items upon delivery and receipt;

  dd) Condition of the subject matter to be assessed, relevant documents and items upon delivery and receipt;

  e) Signature of representative of the delivering party and the receiving party.

  3. The sending of dossiers, subject matters solicited or requested for assessment by post must be carried out in the form of service with serial number. Individuals and organizations receiving the dossiers sent by service with serial number shall be responsible for preserving them and, upon breaking the seal, shall make a record as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. For the delivery and receipt of forensic assessment subjects, including forensic psychiatric assessment subjects being human subjects, the person soliciting or requesting the assessment shall take charge and cooperate with the individual or organization solicited for assessment in managing the subject matter during the process of conducting the assessment.

  5. Upon completion of the assessment, the individual or organization conducting the assessment shall return the subject matter to the person soliciting or requesting the assessment, unless otherwise provided by law.

  The person soliciting or requesting assessment shall be responsible for returning the subject matter of assessment in accordance with law.

  The handover and receipt of the subject matter of assessment after the assessment has been completed shall comply with Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 28. Individual assessment, collective assessment

  1. Individual assessment means an assessment conducted by a single person. Collective assessment means an assessment conducted by at least two persons.

  2. In the case of individual assessment, the assessor shall conduct the assessment, sign the assessment conclusion, and bear personal responsibility for such conclusion.

  3. In the case of collective assessment in one professional field, the assessors shall jointly conduct the assessment, sign the joint assessment conclusion, and bear joint responsibility for such conclusion; if having different opinions, the assessors shall record their opinions in the assessment conclusion and take responsibility for such opinions.

  In the case of collective assessment in multiple different professional fields, each assessor shall conduct the assessment within their professional field and bear personal responsibility for such assessment conclusion.

  Article 29. Additional assessment, re-assessment

  1. Additional assessment shall be conducted in case the contents of the initial assessment conclusion are unclear or incomplete, or when new issues arise related to the facts of the case or matter previously concluded by assessment. The solicitation or request for additional assessment shall be carried out as for the first-time assessment.

  2. Re-assessment shall be conducted in cases where there are grounds to believe that the initial assessment conclusion is inaccurate, or in the cases specified in Clause 2 Article 30 of this Law.

  3. The ordering party shall decide on the re-assessment either on its own initiative or upon the request of the requesting party. If the ordering party does not accept the request for re-assessment, it must notify the requesting party in writing, clearly stating the reasons.

  Article 30. Assessment councils

  1. In cases where there is a difference between the initial assessment conclusion and the re-assessment conclusion regarding the same assessment content, the re-assessment shall be decided by the ordering party. The second re-assessment must be conducted by an assessment council.

  The Minister or Head of the ministerial-level agency managing the field subject to assessment shall decide on the establishment of a council for conducting the second re-assessment. The assessment council shall consist of at least three members who have high expertise and prestige in the field subject to assessment. The assessment council shall operate under the collective assessment mechanism prescribed in Clause 3 Article 28 of this Law.

  2. In special cases, the Prosecutor General of the Supreme People's Procuracy or the Chief Justice of the Supreme People's Court shall decide on the re-assessment after obtaining the conclusion from the assessment council.

  Article 31. Written records of the process of judicial assessment

  1. The judicial assessor shall promptly, fully and truthfully document the entire process and results of the judicial assessment in writing.

  2. The written record of the judicial assessment process must be kept in the judicial assessment file.

  Article 32. Judicial assessment conclusions

  1. Judicial assessment conclusions are written comments or evaluations by judicial assessors on the subject matter of assessment according to the contents of the order for or request for judicial assessment. A judicial assessment conclusion must contain the following contents:

  a) Full name of the judicial assessor; organization conducting the judicial assessment;

  b) Name of the procedure-conducting agency; full name of the procedure-conducting person issuing the order for judicial assessment; number of the written order for judicial assessment or full name of the person requesting judicial assessment;

  c) Information identifying the subject matter of assessment;

  d) Time of receipt of the written order for or request for judicial assessment;

  dd) Contents of the request for expertise;

  e) Method of conducting the expertise;

  g) Conclusion on the object of expertise;

  h) Time and place of conducting and completing the expertise.

  2. In case an individual is requested to conduct the expertise, his/her signature must be authenticated according to regulations of law on authentication.

  In case an organization is requested to conduct the expertise, its head must sign and affix a seal on the conclusion of the expertise and such organization shall take responsibility for the conclusion of the expertise.

  In case the Expertise Council specified in Clause 1 Article 30 of this Law conducts the expertise, the person who decides the establishment of the Council must sign and affix a seal on the conclusion of the expertise and take responsibility for the legal status of the Expertise Council.

  3. In case the expertise is conducted before the decision to institute criminal proceedings is issued, in accordance with the order and procedures prescribed by this Law, the procedural authority may use such conclusion as judicial expertise conclusion.

  Article 33. Judicial assessment file

  1. A judicial assessment file prepared by a judicial assessor includes:

  a) Decision on ordering or requesting a judicial assessment and attached documents (if any);

  b) Record of handover and receipt of the file and subject matter of the order or request for judicial assessment;

  c) Written record of the process of conducting the judicial assessment;

  d) Judicial assessment photos (if any);

  dd) Previous judicial assessment conclusions or results of tests or experiments conducted by other persons (if any);

  e) Other documents related to the judicial assessment (if any);

  g) Conclude judicial assessment.

  2. Judicial assessment files shall be made according to a uniform form. The Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of Health and ministries and ministerial-level agencies shall, within the scope of their tasks and powers, coordinate with the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuracy in uniformly prescribing forms of judicial assessment files.

  3. Individuals and organizations conducting judicial assessment shall be responsible for preserving and keeping judicial assessment files they have conducted in accordance with the law on archives.

  4. Judicial assessment files shall be presented upon request by presiding authorities or presiding officers competent to resolve criminal, administrative cases or civil matters.

  Article 34. Cases where judicial assessment is not allowed

  1. A person shall not conduct judicial assessment in one of the following cases:

  a) He/she falls into one of the cases where participation in legal proceedings must be refused or changed as prescribed by procedural laws;

  b) He/she is solicited to conduct re-assessment of the same content in a case or matter for which he/she has conducted judicial assessment, unless otherwise provided by law.

  2. An organization shall not conduct judicial assessment in any of the following cases:

  a) It has interests and obligations related to the case or matter in accordance with procedural law; or

  b) There are clear grounds to believe that such organization may be subjective or biased during the judicial assessment.

  Article 35. Mutual legal assistance in judicial assessment

  1. A request for judicial assessment by a foreign individual or organization shall only be made if the subject matter of the judicial assessment is located overseas or if the professional capacity, equipment, and facilities of the domestic judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider are insufficient to meet the requirements of the judicial assessment.

  2. The judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider shall be responsible for receiving and conducting the judicial assessment at the request of the competent authority of the foreign proceeding state.

  3. Procedures and costs for mutual legal assistance in judicial assessment between Vietnam and foreign countries shall comply with the laws on mutual legal assistance.

  Chapter VI

     COSTS OF LEGAL ASSISTANCE, POLICIES AND REGULATIONS IN LEGAL ASSISTANCE

  Article 36. Judicial assessment expense The ordering party or requesting party shall pay judicial assessment expense to the judicial assessor or judicial assessment service provider in accordance with law on judicial assessment expense.

  Article 37. Benefits of judicial assessors and participants in judicial assessment

  1. A judicial assessor, a subject-matter judicial assessor, an assistant of a judicial assessor who is receiving salary from the state budget, or a person assigned by a competent authority to perform tasks shall receive remuneration for the performance of such tasks during the period in which they are conducting an assessment, in cases involving autopsy, post-mortem examination, or exhumation.

  2. In addition to case-specific allowance for judicial expertise as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article, full-time public expert officers under public expert bodies shall be entitled to professional allowances and other allowances.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 38. Policies applicable to judicial assessment activities

  1. Private judicial assessment organizations shall be eligible for preferential treatment policies as prescribed by the Government.

  2. Judicial experts, judicial expertise service providers and subject-matter judicial expertise service providers who make active contributions to judicial expertise activities shall be honored and rewarded in accordance with the law on emulation and commendation.

  3. Ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial People’s Councils shall, based on their actual capabilities and conditions as well as competence, prescribe other regimes and policies to attract experts and organizations capable of participating in judicial expertise activities.

  Chapter VII

     RESPONSIBILITIES OF STATE AGENCIES FOR JUDICIAL EXPERTISE SERVICES AND ACTIVITIES

  Article 39. State management agencies in charge of judicial expertise

  1. The Government shall perform the unified state management of judicial expertise.

  2. The Ministry of Justice shall assist the Government in performing the unified state management of judicial expertise.

  3. The Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of National Defense, the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Construction, the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment, the Ministry of Transport, the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, the State Bank of Vietnam and other ministries, ministerial agencies shall perform the state management and take responsibility before the Government for organizing and conducting judicial expertise in the fields under their management; shall cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in performing the unified state management of judicial expertise.

  4. Provincial People’s Committees shall, within their tasks and powers, perform state management of judicial assessment in their localities.

  Article 40. Tasks and powers of the Ministry of Justice

  1. Promulgate or request competent regulatory authorities to promulgate legislative documents on judicial assessment and provide guidance on the implementation thereof.

  Take charge of developing and submitting to the Prime Minister comprehensive strategies, planning, and plans for development of judicial assessment; cooperate with ministries and ministerial agencies in developing strategies, planning, and plans for development of each field of judicial assessment.

  2. Provide written opinions on establishment of public judicial assessment organizations under the decision-making authority of ministries and ministerial agencies managing professional fields and provincial People's Committees; where necessary, propose ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People's Committees to consider and establish public judicial assessment organizations to meet the requirements of judicial assessment in legal proceedings.

  3. Develop programs for training in legal knowledge for judicial assessors; cooperate with ministries and ministerial agencies in organizing professional training and legal knowledge training for judicial assessors.

  4. Collect, compile, and publish a general list of individuals and organizations engaged in judicial assessment on the web portal of the Ministry of Justice.

  5. Encourage the performance of state management of judicial assessment by ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People's Committees; request ministries, ministerial agencies, and provincial People's Committees to report on the organization and operation of judicial assessment; and submit reports to the Government on the organization and operation of judicial assessment nationwide.

  6. Take charge or request relevant ministries, ministerial agencies and provincial-level People's Committees to organize inspections of judicial assessment organization and activities.

  7. Perform state management of international cooperation on judicial assessment.

  Article 41. Tasks and powers of ministries and ministerial agencies in charge of professional management of the field of judicial assessment

  1. Promulgate or submit to competent regulatory agencies for promulgation legal normative documents on judicial assessment in the field under their management and provide guidance on implementation thereof.

  2. Promulgate judicial assessment regulations or guide the application of professional regulations to judicial assessment activities at the request and specific characteristics of the field under their management.

  3. Take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in deciding the establishment, consolidation and strengthening of public judicial assessment organizations under their management in accordance with this Law.

  4. Appoint and dismiss judicial assessors within their competence; make and publish lists of subject-matter judicial assessors and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 20 of this Law.

  5. Ensure funding, assessment equipment and facilities and other necessary material conditions for public judicial assessment organizations under their management to meet assigned tasks and responsibilities.

  6. Annually, assess the quality of operation of judicial assessment organizations and subject-matter judicial assessment organizations and judicial assessors in the fields under their management.

  7. To stipulate conditions on material foundations, assessment equipment and means of judicial assessment offices in domains under their management.

  8. Develop and organize implementation of programs and plans for training in judicial expertise and legal knowledge for a contingent of public expert officers in the fields under their management.

  9. Inspect and settle complaints and denunciations about organization and operation of judicial expertise in fields under their management; cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in inspection and examination of organization and operation of judicial expertise as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 40 hereof.

  10. Conduct international cooperation in judicial assessment in the fields under their management.

  11. Annually, make a review of organization and operation of judicial expertise in fields under their management and send report to the Ministry of Justice for consolidation and reporting to the Government.

  Article 42. Tasks and powers of the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of National Defense

  In addition to the tasks and powers specified in Article 41 of this Law, the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of National Defense have the following tasks and powers:

  1. The Ministry of Health shall have the following tasks and powers:

  a) To perform state management of forensic examination and forensic psychiatric examination;

  b) To promulgate professional standards in the fields of forensic examination and forensic psychiatric examination;

  c) To specify standards for forensic examiners and forensic psychiatric examiners;

  d) To provide training and refresher courses and issue certificates of competency in forensic examination and forensic psychiatric examination as prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall have the following tasks and powers:

  a) To perform state management of criminal technical examination;

  b) To promulgate professional standards in the field of criminal technical examination;

  c) To specify criteria for criminal forensic examiner;

  d) To provide training and refresher courses and issue certificates of competency in forensic examination as prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 7 of this Law;

  dd) To conduct annual statistics on solicitation and assessment of forensic examination and use of forensic examination conclusions in investigation authorities under its management;

  e) To guide investigation authorities under its management to apply regulations of law on solicitation of forensic examination and assessment and use of forensic examination conclusions;

  g) To ensure funding and guide payment of forensic examination costs in investigation authorities under its management;

  h) To annually review and send reports on solicitation and assessment of forensic examination and use of forensic examination conclusions in investigation authorities under its management to the Ministry of Justice.

  3. The Ministry of National Defense has the tasks and powers specified in Points dd, e, g and h Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 43. Tasks and powers of provincial-level People's Committees

  1. Provincial People’s Committees have the following tasks and powers:

  a) Establish public judicial assessment organizations; decide to permit the establishment of judicial assessment offices; prepare and publish lists of local judicial assessment organizations and ad hoc judicial assessment organizations;

  b) Appoint and dismiss judicial assessors within their competence; prepare and publish lists of local judicial assessors;

  c) Ensure funding, operational facilities, material bases, and other necessary conditions for local public judicial assessment organizations;

  d) Organize professional training and legal knowledge education for local judicial assessors;

  dd) Annually assess the organization and quality of judicial assessment in their localities; ensure the quantity and quality of judicial assessors and ad hoc judicial assessors to promptly and adequately meet the requirements for judicial assessment in local procedural activities;

  e) Conduct inspections and settle complaints and denunciations about judicial assessment within their competence; cooperate with the Ministry of Justice in inspecting the organization and operation of judicial assessment as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 40 of this Law;

  g) Report to the Ministry of Justice on the organization and operation of judicial assessment in their localities, and concurrently send reports to relevant ministries and ministerial-level agencies to perform the state management tasks prescribed in Articles 40, 41, and 42 of this Law.

  2. Departments of Justice shall be responsible for assisting provincial People's Committees in performing state management of judicial assessment in their provinces and cities; taking charge and cooperating with specialized agencies in assisting provincial People's Committees in managing operation of judicial assessment offices.

  Specialized agencies of provincial People's Committees shall assist provincial People's Committees in performing state management of specialized fields of judicial assessment, take responsibility before provincial People's Committees for organization and operation of judicial assessment under their management; cooperate with Departments of Justice in assisting provincial People's Committees in performing state management of local judicial assessment.

  Article 44. Responsibilities of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuracy

  1. Guide application of regulations of law on solicitation of judicial assessment and evaluation and use of judicial assessment conclusions in system of People's Courts and People's Procuracies.

  2. Implement regulations on statistics on solicitation and evaluation of judicial assessment and use of judicial assessment conclusions in system of People's Courts and People's Procuracies and report to the National Assembly in annual reports.

  3. Cooperate with Ministry of Justice in implementing regulations on statistics and reporting on solicitation and evaluation of judicial assessment and use of judicial assessment conclusions.

  4. Ensure funding and guide payment of judicial assessment costs in system of People's Courts and People's Procuracies.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 45. Effect

  1. This Law takes effect on January 1, 2013.

  2. The Judicial Assessment Ordinance No. 24/2004/PL-UBTVQH11 ceases to be effective as from the effective date of this Law.

  3. From the effective date of this Law, regulations of the Criminal Procedure Code, Civil Procedure Code and Administrative Procedure Law on judicial assessment which are different from those in this Law shall be applied.

  Article 46. Detailed regulations and guidance for implementation

  The Government, the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuracy shall provide detailed regulations and guidance on implementation of articles and clauses as assigned in the Law.
