                                                     DECREE

     ELABORATION ON THE ORDER AND PROCEDURES FOR PREPARING AND PAYING PROCEDURAL EXPENSES

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated February 18, 2025;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on amendments to the Law on Securities, the Law on Accounting, the Law on Independent Audit, the Law on State Budget, the Law on Management and Use of Public Property, the Law on Tax Administration, the Law on Personal Income Tax, the Law on National Reserves, and the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated November 29, 2024;

  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Court Fees dated December 11, 2024;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government hereby promulgates a Decree elaborating on the procedures for preparing estimates and paying court fee expenses.

  Chapter I

                                                 GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Decree provides for procedures for preparing and paying procedural expenses.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Decree applies to competent procedural authorities and agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the estimation and payment of procedural expenses.

  Article 3. Estimation, allocation and settlement of funding for payment of procedural expenses

  The estimation, allocation and settlement of procedural expenses shall comply with regulations of law on state budget, accounting laws and relevant laws. This Decree provides the following guidelines:

  1. Make a cost estimate

  Annually, at the time of preparing the state budget estimate, based on the actual payment of procedural expenses incurred in the previous year and the estimated implementation of the current year, the competent procedural authority and relevant agencies shall prepare an estimate of procedural expenses in accordance with the Ordinance on Procedural Expenses, consolidate it into their own estimates, and submit it to the superior management agency for review and consolidation into the unit's first-level estimate, which is then sent to the finance authority at the same level for submission to the competent authority in accordance with the law on the state budget.

  2. Allocation of estimates

  After being assigned by the Prime Minister and People's Committees, the central and local units in charge of budget estimation shall allocate and assign the budget estimates to their affiliated budget-using units and subordinate budget-using units in accordance with the law on the state budget. In particular, for the allocation and assignment of recurrent expenditure estimates for payment of procedural costs, the allocated amounts shall be included in the funding not subject to the autonomy regime of agencies and units.

  3. Settlement

  Agencies and units shall be responsible for finalizing expenditures on payment of procedural costs in accordance with this Decree and consolidating them in their annual budget finalization reports in accordance with laws on finalization of recurrent state budget expenditures, accounting laws, guiding documents, and other relevant laws.

  Chapter II

    PROVISIONS ON PROCEDURES FOR ADJUSTMENT AND PAYMENT OF PROCEDURAL COSTS BETWEEN AGENCIES WITH PROCEDURAL POWERS AND AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND INDIVIDUALS EXECUTING PROCEDURAL ACTIVITIES

  Article 4. Procedures for payment of on-site inspection and appraisal expenses; on-site inspection expenses

  1. After completing on-site inspection and appraisal or on-site review, the person invited to participate in on-site inspection and appraisal, the person invited to participate in on-site review, or the person conducting the on-site review shall submit a payment request to the competent procedural authority by post, in person, or online if infrastructure and technical conditions are met.

  2. Application for payment of expenses for persons invited to participate in on-site assessment, persons invited to participate in on-site review, and persons conducting on-site review shall include:

  a) Payment request form, made according to Form No. 01 enclosed herewith.

  b) Documents attached to the payment request form are the original invoices and legal documents related thereto to prove necessary and reasonable expenses incurred when being invited to participate in on-site assessment, on-site review or conducting on-site review.

  3. In case the application for payment is incomplete as prescribed, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority shall notify the payer of such and clearly state the reasons for supplementation of the application as prescribed. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the complete and valid application, the competent procedural authority shall pay the expenses.

  4. The competent procedural authority shall pay service fees and other expenses (if any) under contracts, invoices, and service charge receipts in accordance with law.

  Article 5. Procedures for advance payment and payment of property valuation expenses

  1. In case of need to advance property valuation expenses, within 05 working days from the date of receipt of the request for property valuation from the competent procedural authority, the Property Valuation Council shall send an application for advance payment to the competent procedural authority by one of the methods via postal services, in person or online if meeting infrastructure and technical conditions for advance payment procedures.

  2. The application for advance payment of property valuation expenses shall include:

  a) The written request for advance on property valuation expenses made using Form No. 02 enclosed herewith.

  b) The table for calculating total property valuation expenses and the basis for calculating property valuation expenses.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete application for advance from the Property Valuation Council, the competent procedural authority shall make the advance. The maximum advance shall not exceed the property valuation expense calculated by the Property Valuation Council and included in the application for advance as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Within 10 days from the date of obtaining the valuation results, the Property Valuation Council shall submit an application for payment of valuation expenses to the competent procedural authority by one of the following methods: by post, in person or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technical requirements for payment procedures are met.

  5. The application for payment of valuation expenses includes:

  a) The written request for payment made using Form No. 01 enclosed herewith.

  b) Documents attached to the written request for payment are the written request for valuation; the original copies of relevant invoices and vouchers to prove necessary expenses and expenses determined under Articles 21, 22, 23 and 24 of the Ordinance on Expenses for Procedural Activities arising during the process of valuation.

  6. In case the application for payment of valuation expenses is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the application, the competent procedural authority shall notify the Property Valuation Council and provide an explanation for supplementation of the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the date of receipt of the application for payment of full and valid valuation expense, the competent procedural authority shall pay the valuation expense to the Property Valuation Council in accordance with regulations.

  If the paid advance on valuation expense is insufficient, the competent procedural authority shall pay the deficit.

  In case the advance payment exceeds the valuation cost, the property valuation council shall refund the difference within 2 working days from the date of submission of the application for payment of valuation costs.

  Article 6. Procedures for advance payment and payment of assessment expense

  1. Procedures for advance payment of assessment expense

  a) In civil procedures and administrative procedures

  The procedures for advance payment of assessment expense shall comply with Article 38 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  b/ In criminal proceedings, procedures for advance payment of assessment expense shall comply with Article 44 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  Within 5 working days from the date on which decision on request for expertise issued by competent procedural authorities is received, entities conducting expertise shall submit 1 set of application for advance payment of expertise costs to competent procedural authorities via one of methods below: by post, in person or online if infrastructure and technical requirements for advance payment are met.

  2. Application for advance payment of expertise costs consists of:

  a) Written request for advance payment using Form No. 2 attached hereto.

  b) Table calculating total costs of expertise and basis for calculation of costs of expertise.

  3. Deadline for payment and amount of advance payment of expertise costs

  a) In civil and administrative procedures: Deadline for advance payment of expertise costs shall conform to Article 38 of Ordinance on Procedure Costs.

  b) In criminal procedures: Deadline for advance payment of expertise costs shall conform to notice of request for advance payment of entities conducting expertise under Clause 2 Article 44 of Ordinance on Procedure Costs.

  c) Maximum advance payment shall not exceed expertise costs calculated and submitted by entities conducting expertise under Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Within 10 days from the date of obtaining the appraisal results, the appraiser shall submit a payment request to the competent procedural authority by post or in person or online if the conditions for infrastructure and technical requirements are met.

  5. The payment request includes:

  a) A written request for payment made using Form No. 1 enclosed with this Decree.

  b) Documents attached to the payment request are the original invoices and relevant legal documents to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred during the appraisal process.

  6. If the payment request is incomplete or invalid, within 3 days from the receipt of the request, the competent procedural authority must notify the requesting organization or individual and provide an explanation for supplementation as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the receipt of the complete and valid payment request, the competent procedural authority shall pay the expenses to the appraiser as prescribed.

  If the paid advance is insufficient, the competent procedural authority shall pay the remaining amount.

  If the advance exceeds the appraisal costs, the appraiser must refund the difference within 2 working days from the date of submission of the payment request.

  Article 7. Procedures for payment of expenses for lay assessors

  1. Within 10 days from the end of a trial, the lay assessor shall submit an application for payment of expenses for the lay assessor to the Court by one of the methods: post service, in person or online if the conditions regarding infrastructure and technical requirements for payment procedures are met.

  2. The application for payment of expenses for the lay assessor includes:

  a) An application form for payment, made according to Form No. 01 enclosed with this Decree.

  b) Documents attached to the application form for payment include the schedule of time of participating in the trial, the time of study of the dossier of the lay assessor certified by the presiding judge; the original invoices and legal documents related to prove the necessary and reasonable expenses that have arisen as prescribed in Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 Article 46 of the Ordinance on Procedural Expenses.

  3. In case the application for payment of expenses for the lay assessor is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the receipt of the application, the Court must notify the lay assessor and clearly state the reason for supplementing the application as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the receipt of the complete and valid application for payment of expenses for the lay assessor, the Court shall pay the expenses for the lay assessor as prescribed.

  Article 8. Payment of expenses for lawyers, legal assistants and people’s defense counsels in cases where defense counsels are appointed in criminal proceedings

  1. Procedures for paying expenses for lawyers and legal assistants in case of designation of defense counsels shall comply with regulations of law on lawyers and legal assistance.

  2. Procedures for paying expenses for people’s advocates in case of designation of defense counsels shall be carried out as follows:

  a) A dossier requesting payment of expenses for people’s advocates includes:

  A written request for payment made using Form No. 01 enclosed herewith.

  The documents attached to the written request for payment are a schedule of working time of the people’s advocate certified by the competent procedural authority or proceeding officer; originals of relevant invoices and vouchers to prove necessary and reasonable expenses incurred as prescribed in Points b, c, d and dd Clause 2 Article 49 of the Ordinance on Procedure Expenses.

  b) After finishing each stage in the procedure (investigation, prosecution and trial), the people’s advocate shall send a dossier requesting payment to the competent procedural authority to request designation of a defense counsel being a people’s advocate by one of the methods via postal service, submission in person or online on the electronic environment if meeting infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures.

  c) In case the dossier requesting payment of expenses for people’s advocates is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the receipt of the dossier, the competent procedural authority shall notify the people’s advocate and clearly state the reason for supplementing the dossier as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier requesting payment of expenses for people’s advocates, the competent procedural authority shall pay expenses for people’s advocates as prescribed.

  Article 9. Payment of expenses for witnesses, interpreters and translators

  1. After completing their tasks, witnesses, interpreters and translators shall submit payment requests to competent procedural authorities by post or in person or online if infrastructure and technical conditions are satisfied.

  2. A payment request includes:

  a) A written request for payment of expenses for witnesses, interpreters and translators made using Form No. 01 hereto appended.

  b) Documents attached to the written request are relevant invoices and vouchers to prove necessary and reasonable expenses paid by witnesses, interpreters and translators when performing their tasks.

  3. If a payment request is inadequate or invalid, competent procedural authorities shall inform witnesses, interpreters and translators within 03 days from the date on which they receive the request and provide justification for supplementation as prescribed.

  Competent procedural authorities shall pay expenses for witnesses, interpreters and translators within 10 days from the date on which they receive adequate and valid payment requests.

  Article 10. Payment of overseas judicial entrustment expenses

  1. Procedures for payment of costs of judicial entrustment overseas in civil procedures, administrative procedures and criminal procedures shall comply with regulations of law on mutual legal assistance.

  2. Procedures for payment of costs of service and notification of procedural documents overseas in civil procedures and administrative procedures through overseas representative missions of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall comply with procedures stipulated by the Supreme People's Court in cooperation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  Article 11. Payment of costs of participation in court sessions or meetings for settlement of cases or lawsuits

  1. After the end of a court session or meeting for settlement of a case or lawsuit, persons specified in Clause 1 Article 69 of the Ordinance on Procedural Costs shall send payment request dossiers to competent procedural authorities in one of the following forms: by post, in person or online if infrastructure and technical conditions for payment procedures are satisfied.

  2. A payment request dossier includes:

  a) A written request for payment of costs of participation in a court session or meeting, made using Form No. 01 enclosed herewith;

  b) Documents attached to the payment request dossier are original copies of relevant invoices and vouchers to prove necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by participants in court sessions or meetings when attending such sessions or meetings.

  3. In case the payment request dossier is incomplete or invalid, within 03 days from the date of receipt of the dossier, the competent procedural authority shall notify the participant in court session or meeting and provide an explanation for supplementation of the dossier as prescribed.

  Within 10 days from the receipt of a complete and valid payment request, the competent procedural authority shall pay the court fee or meeting fee to the participant in the hearing as prescribed.

  Chapter III

                                            IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 12. Effect

  1. This Decree comes into force from the date of its signing.

  2. In case documents referred to in this Decree are amended or replaced, such amendments or replacements shall prevail.

  3. The Government’s Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP dated August 14, 2014 on providing detailed regulations on the Ordinance on Assessment and Valuation Expenses; Expenses for Witnesses and Interpreters in Legal Proceedings (Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP) ceases to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  4. For cases where payment requests for assessment expenses, valuation expenses, expenses for witnesses and interpreters in legal proceedings are being processed according to the provisions of Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP before the effective date of this Decree, such payment requests shall continue to comply with the provisions of Decree No. 81/2014/ND-CP. Competent procedural authorities, agencies, organizations and individuals performing procedural activities shall take full responsibility for the completeness and validity of payment requests and procedures for payment of expenses as prescribed in this Clause, ensuring compliance with law.

  5. The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies, the heads of the Government-attached agencies and the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall have to implement this Decree.
