                                                   DECREE

       THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE NO. 49/2005/ND-CP OF APRIL 11, 2005, ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF EDUCATION

                                                  GOV

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
  Pursuant to the December 2, 1998 Law on Education;
  Pursuant to the July 2, 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;
  At the proposal of the Minister of Education and Training,

                                                  DECREES:

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- Scope of regulation

  1. This Decree prescribes acts of administrative violation in the field of education (the vocational training field not falling under the scope of regulation of this Decree), sanctioning forms and levels, sanctioning competence, sanctioning procedures and remedial measures.

  2. Administrative violations in the field of education are acts of violating the State management regulations in the field of education committed by individuals or organizations either intentionally or unintentionally but not constituting crimes and, as prescribed by the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and this Decree, shall be administratively sanctioned.

  3. Acts of administrative violation in the field of education prescribed in other Government’s decrees (hereinafter referred to as relevant decrees) shall be governed by the provisions of such decrees.

  Article 2.- Subjects of application

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations committing acts of administrative violation in the field of education shall all be sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree and relevant decrees.

  2. Foreign individuals and organizations committing acts of administrative violation in the field of education in Vietnam shall all be sanctioned like Vietnamese individuals and organizations according to the provisions of this Decree and relevant decrees, except otherwise provided for by international agreements which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam has signed or acceded to.

  3. Individuals being minors who commit acts of administrative violation in the field of education shall be handled according to the provisions of Clause 1 and Clause 3, Article 7 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 3.- Sanctioning principles

  1. All acts of administrative violation in the field of education must be detected in time and stopped immediately.

  The handling of administrative violations in the field of education must be conducted quickly, fairly and thoroughly; all consequences caused by administrative violations must be remedied according to law provisions.

  2. Individuals or organizations shall be sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education only when they commit acts of violation prescribed in this Decree and other relevant Government’s decrees prescribing sanctioning of administrative violations.

  3. An act of administrative violation in the field of education shall be sanctioned only once.

  One person or organization that commits many acts of administrative violation in the field of education shall be sanctioned for each of his/her/its acts of violation.

  Many persons or organizations that jointly commit one act of administrative violation in the field of education shall be sanctioned for each of their acts of violation.

  4. The sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of education must base itself on the nature and seriousness of the violations, the personal records of the violators and extenuating and/or aggravating circumstances in order to decide on appropriate handling forms and measures.

  5. Administrative violations in the field of education shall not be sanctioned in cases of emergency, legitimate self-defense, unexpected events or administrative violations committed by individuals suffering from mental illness or other diseases that have caused them to lose the ability to perceive or control their acts.

  Article 4. Mitigating and aggravating circumstances

  The mitigating and aggravating circumstances applicable to the sanctioning of administrative violations prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree shall comply with the provisions of Articles 8 and 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 5. Prescriptive periods for sanctioning administrative violations

  1. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education is one year from the date the administrative violation is committed; for administrative violations in the field of education related to finance, intellectual property, construction, environment, housing, land, publication, export, import, exit, entry or administrative violations being acts of smuggling, production and/or trading of fake goods, the statute of limitations shall be two years; past the above time limit, no sanction shall be imposed but the remedial measures prescribed in Clause 3, Article 7 of this Decree shall still apply.

  2. For individuals who are prosecuted or brought to trial according to criminal procedures but later have their investigations or cases terminated and their violations show signs of administrative violations in the field of education, they shall be administratively sanctioned; within three days from the date of issuing the decision on investigation termination or case termination, the person who has issued such decision shall send it to the person with sanctioning authority; in this case, the statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations is three months from the date the competent person receives the decision on termination and the violation dossier.

  3. Within the time limit specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, if individuals or organizations commit new administrative violations in the field of education or deliberately evade or obstruct the sanctioning, the statute of limitations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply. The statute of limitations for sanctioning administrative violations shall be recalculated from the time of committing new administrative violations or from the time of terminating acts of deliberately evading or obstructing the sanctioning.

  Article 6. Time limit for being considered as not yet sanctioned for administrative violations

  Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education shall be considered as having never been sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education if they do not relapse within one year from the date of completely serving the sanctioning decisions or the date of expiry of the sanctioning decisions.

  Article 7. Penalties and remedial measures

  1. For each administrative violation in the field of education, the violating individual or organization shall be subject to one of the following main forms of sanction:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fine.

  When applying a fine, the specific fine level for an administrative violation is the average level of the fine bracket prescribed for such act. If the violation involves aggravating circumstances as prescribed in Article 8 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, the fine level may be lowered below the average level but not lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation involves extenuating circumstances as prescribed in Article 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, the fine level may be raised above the average level but not higher than the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  2. Depending on the nature and severity of their violations, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education may be subject to one or more of the following additional sanctions:

  a) Depriving the right to use decisions on establishment, licenses or practicing certificates for a definite or indefinite period, applicable to individuals or organizations that commit serious violations of regulations on the use of decisions, licenses or practicing certificates;

  b) Confiscating material evidence and means used for committing administrative violations;

  c) Expelling violators who are foreigners.

  3. Apart from the sanctions prescribed in clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory restoration of the initial state altered due to administrative violations at the request of competent authorities;

  b) Compulsory suspension or termination of teaching or educational activities;

  c) Compulsory cancellation of examination results or re-marking of examination papers;

  d) Compulsory implementation of teaching, practice and internship with adequate number of periods, contents and programs as prescribed; provision of documents and equipment according to approved plans;

  dd) Compulsory cancellation of decisions which are wrong or contrary to regulations of competent authorities;

  e) Compulsory restoration of learners’ right to study, legitimate interests and correct assessment results, ensuring rights of learners who are holders of diplomas/certificates;

  g) Compulsory refund of collected amounts to learners and payment of all expenses for refund due to administrative violations;

  h) Compulsory revocation of illegally issued diplomas/certificates;

  i) Compulsory bringing out of the Vietnamese territory or compulsory re-export of textbooks, documents and educational equipment illegally imported due to administrative violations.

  Chapter 2:

    ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF EDUCATION, FORMS OF SANCTION AND LEVELS OF SANCTION

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on establishment and operation of educational institutions

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to report the loss of a decision on establishment, license or practicing certificate to a competent authority.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for erasing, altering, modifying, buying, selling, transferring, leasing, borrowing or lending a decision on establishment, license or practicing certificate in violation of regulations of law.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal establishment of an educational institution:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the educational institution is a preschool education institution or general education institution;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the educational institution is a vocational secondary education institution, continuing education center, foreign language center or computer center;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed if the educational institution is a college;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 60.000.000 to VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed if the educational institution is a university or postgraduate institution.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for opening independent classes for teaching preschool education programs, general education programs, professional secondary education programs, undergraduate preparatory courses, university entrance examination preparation courses, college and postgraduate training courses without permission from competent authorities or imposing permission on violations beyond their competence:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if such violation involves preschool education classes;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if such violation involves general education classes;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed if such violation involves professional secondary education training courses, undergraduate preparatory courses, or university entrance examination preparation courses;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if such violation involves college or postgraduate training courses.

  6. Additional sanctions and remedial measures applicable to the violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscation of exhibits, for the violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory return of collected amounts to learners and payment of all return fees incurred by the entity committing the violations prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on operation of educational institutions

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for failing to comply with a competent authority’s decision on suspension or dissolution of an educational institution; operating beyond the time limit specified in the license; suspending operation without permission from a competent authority, causing damage to learners:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation involves an early childhood education institution or general education institution;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation involves a vocational secondary education institution, continuing education center, foreign language center or computer center;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation involves a college;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation involves a university or postgraduate institution.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for providing training and issuing certificates against the law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Training and issuing degrees of national education system beyond the assigned authority;

  b) Training in fields and specializations beyond the assigned authority.

  4. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Enforced return of collected amounts to learners and incurrence of all return fees for violations specified in Clause 1 and revocation of illegally issued degrees and certificates for violations specified in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of lost learning rights of learners due to violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article according to decisions of competent authorities.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on programs, contents and plans for education

  1. A warning or fine shall be imposed for failing to teach enough periods or knowledge content (calculated by period) of a subject as prescribed by competent authorities in general education, vocational education and training, college, university and postgraduate education, with the following fines:

  a) A warning shall be imposed for committing less than 5 periods of a class per year;

  b) A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for violations of from 5 to 10 class periods in a school year;

  c) A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for violations of from 11 to 15 class periods per class in a school year;

  d) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for violations of from 16 to 20 class periods per class in a school year;

  dd) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for violations of 21 class periods or more per class in a school year.

  2. To impose a fine on acts of teaching or disseminating contents not in the prescribed programs, textbooks, teaching materials in order to distort educational contents (but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability) according to the following fine levels:

  a) Between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for first violations;

  b) Between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for second violations or more.

  3. Additional sanctions for violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscate exhibits for violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Deportation if the violating individual is a foreigner for the acts specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Suspend the license or practicing certificate for a fixed period in case of first-time violation or permanently in case of repeated violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Remedial measures for violations under this Article:

  a) Enforced suspension of teaching and educational activities of violating individuals in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced addition of teaching details and missing contents in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on eligible candidates, eligibility criteria, enrollment targets and powers

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 200,000 and VND 300,000 for acts of making false declaration or abetting the making of false declaration in enrolment dossiers in order to be enrolled.

  2. To impose a fine on act of enrolling students at different levels and grades in general education not in conformity with the prescribed subject and criteria by competent agencies, the following fines shall be imposed:

  a) A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for violation of enrolling less than 3 students;

  b) A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for violation of enrolling from 3 to 5 students;

  c) A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 for violation of enrolling from 6 to 10 students;

  d) A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for violation of enrolling from 11 to 15 students;

  dd) A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for violation of enrolling 16 students or more.

  3. To impose a warning or a fine on act of enrolling students for training in vocational secondary education in excess of the target number, wrong subjects or criteria as prescribed by competent agencies, the following fines shall be imposed:

  a) A warning shall be imposed on violation of enrolling students in excess of less than 5% of the target enrollment;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by more than 5% but less than 10%, or enrolling fewer than 10 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by more than 10% but less than 15%, or enrolling 10 - 20 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by more than 15% but less than 20%, or enrolling 21 - 30 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 6.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners who exceed the target enrollment volume by more than 20%, or enrolling 31 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for enrolling learners for college, university or postgraduate training programs in excess of the prescribed target enrollment volume, or learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements as regulated by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if learners exceed the target enrollment volume by less than 5%;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if learners exceed the target enrollment volume by more than 5% but less than 10%, or enroll 10 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if learners exceed the target enrollment volume by more than 10% but less than 15%, or enroll 10 - 20 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements.

  d) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners whose number exceeds the target enrollment volume by 15% - 20%, or enrolling 21 - 30 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed for enrolling learners whose number exceeds the target enrollment volume by more than 20%, or enrolling 31 learners who fail to meet eligibility requirements.

  5. The following fines shall be imposed for enrolling learners for training and issuance of diplomas of national education system beyond the assigned authority:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for announcing enrolment (in any form) before obtaining permission from a competent authority;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for accepting applications, organizing entrance exams or consults, or summoning examinees before obtaining permission from a competent authority.

  6. The following fines shall be imposed for violations against regulations of the State on enrolment for overseas training or training at educational institutions with foreign elements operating in the Vietnamese territory according to general education, vocational education and training, college, university, postgraduate programs:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if 1 - 5 learners are enrolled improperly;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if 6 - 10 learners are enrolled improperly.

  c) A fine of from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 for the violation involving the wrong enrollment of 11 - 15 learners;

  d) A fine of from VND 30,000,000 to VND 40,000,000 for the violation involving the wrong enrollment of 16 - 20 learners;

  dd) A fine of from VND 40,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 for the violation involving the wrong enrollment of 21 - 25 learners;

  e) A fine of from VND 50,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 for the violation involving the wrong enrollment of 26 learners or more.

  7. Additional sanctions against violations specified in this Article:

  a) Confiscation of exhibits, for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Suspension of practicing certificates and licenses if committed for the first time and indefinite suspension if repeated, for violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 6 of this Article.

  8. Remedial measures applicable to violations specified in this Article:

  a) Enforced cancellation of false decisions due to acts specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of collected amounts to learners and payment of all return fees for acts specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on entrance examinations to educational institutions and graduation examinations for issuance of diplomas of national education system

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for disturbing or threatening to use force to obstruct examiners, invigilators, marking officers and service providers from performing their tasks.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for using force to obstruct examiners, invigilators, marking officers and service providers from performing their tasks.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 4.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Taking an exam instead of another person or taking an exam with the purpose of assisting another person;

  b) Illegally transferring documents, information or equipment into the examination room for examinees.

  c) Disclosing secrets of test papers.

  4. A fine of between VND 6,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of swapping test papers or abetting others to swap test papers of examinees in contravention of regulations of competent agencies.

  5. A fine of between VND 1,500,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations of examination regulations:

  a) Issuing decisions on establishment of apparatus, assigning tasks to officials in contravention of regulations applicable to such examination;

  b) Losing or damaging test papers to the extent that such test papers cannot be graded;

  c) Grading entrance exams or graduation exams not in accordance with answers or score range;

  d) Making wrong grade sheets with grading results of test papers.

  6. A fine of between VND 4,000,000 and 6,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of writing additionally or modifying contents of test papers of examinees in contravention of regulations.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for disclosing or losing examination papers (but not liable to criminal prosecution).

  8. Additional sanctions against violations specified in this Article:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit violations specified in Clauses 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures against violations specified in this Article:

  a) Enforced restoration of the initial state altered by the violations specified in Clause 4, Point d Clause 5 and Clause 6 of this Article;

  b) Enforced cancellation of examination results of violators (if they are examinees) for violations specified in Clauses 1 and 2 and of examinees entitled to benefits for violations specified in Clauses 3, 4, 6 and 7 of this Article;

  c) Enforced re-marking of examination papers for violations specified in Point c Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on assessment of learners’ performance at general education schools, vocational secondary schools and higher education institutions

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent authorities on marking, assessment and grading of learners’ performance which affect learners’ performance in a subject, term or school year as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed if the violation involves general education;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if the violation involves vocational education;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 600.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation involves higher education.

  2. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced restoration of learners’ actual performance in assessment and grading if the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article is committed.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on management, issuance and use of diplomas/certificates of national education system

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to fulfill their responsibility to issue or certify copies of or to certify the legitimacy of issued diplomas/certificates;

  b) Issuing diplomas/certificates with false contents thus causing obstacles to their users.

  2. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for act of using illegally issued diplomas/certificates.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to make or making incomplete dossiers as basis for issuance and management of diplomas/certificates;

  b) Making untruthful certification or falsifying dossiers leading to the issuance of diplomas/certificates to ineligible persons.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Issuing decisions or requesting competent authorities to issue certificates illegally;

  b) Destroy or falsify documents used as grounds for issuance or certification of validity of certificates (but not liable to criminal prosecution).

  5. A fine of from VND 4,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Issuing decisions or requesting competent authorities to issue decisions on illegal issuance of diplomas;

  b) Destroying or falsifying documents used as grounds for issuance or certification of validity of diplomas (but not liable to criminal prosecution).

  6. Additional sanctions against violations specified in this Article:

  Depriving the right to use licenses and practicing certificates for a definite period if the violation is committed for the second time and indefinitely if the violation is repeated for the third time or more.

  7. Remedial measures against violations specified in this Article:

  a) Revoking diplomas and certificates, applicable to violations specified in Point b Clause 1, Clause 2, Point b Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 and Point a Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Forcible restoration of legitimate rights and interests of holders of diplomas/certificates for acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 15. Violations of regulations on using teachers

  1. To impose a fine on act of using teachers who fail to meet prescribed criteria as follows:

  a) To serve a warning on act of using preschool teachers who fail to meet criteria;

  b) To impose a fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for act of using general education teachers who fail to meet criteria;

  c) To impose a fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 4,000,000 for act of using teachers of professional secondary schools, continuing education centers, foreign language centers or computer centers who fail to meet criteria;

  d) To impose a fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for act of using college lecturers who fail to meet criteria;

  dd) To impose a fine of between VND 11,000,000 and 15,000,000 for act of using university or postgraduate lecturers who fail to meet criteria.

  2. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced suspension of teaching and education activities for teachers who fail to meet the prescribed standards while working as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 16. Violations against regulations on teacher and lecturer ratio in educational institutions

  Fines for violations against regulations of competent authorities on ensuring the ratio of full-time teachers and lecturers to total number of teachers and lecturers in educational institutions shall be imposed at the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations in formal education levels or grades;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing the violation in a vocational secondary education institution, continuing education center, foreign language center or computer center;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for committing the violation at a college;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for committing the violation at a university or postgraduate training institution.

  Article 17. Violations against regulations on management of learners’ dossiers

  1. A caution or fine shall be imposed for irresponsibility in management leading to the alteration of school books, mark sheets or other documents related to assessment of learners’ performance against regulations, causing the following levels of penalties:

  a) A caution or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed if 1 - 2 learners are involved in the violation;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if 3 - 5 learners are involved in the violation;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 600.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if 6 learners or more are involved in the violation.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to prepare or incompletely preparing learner management dossiers as prescribed in the school charter.

  3. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  Enforced restoration of the original state altered by the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on use of textbooks, educational materials and provision and use of educational equipment

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for issuing a decision against regulations on purchase, receipt of gifts, textbooks, educational materials and educational equipment that fail to meet quality requirements or cause adverse impacts on teaching and learning activities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for transporting and distributing textbooks, educational materials, or educational equipment against the plan for provision prescribed by a competent authority.

  3. Remedial measures for violations under this Article:

  a) Enforced bringing out of the Vietnamese territory or re-export of textbooks, documents and educational equipment illegally imported due to the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original plan altered due to the violations prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on tuition fees, charges and other amounts collected from learners

  To apply provisions in Chapter II of the Government's Decree No. 106/2003/ND-CP of September 23, 2003, prescribing the handling of administrative violations in the domain of fees and charges.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on forms of disciplinary actions for compulsory withdrawal from learning

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for the act of making a disciplinary decision to compel learners to withdraw from learning in violation of regulations, with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if 1 - 2 learners are involved in the violation;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if 3 - 5 learners are involved in the violation;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed if 6 - 10 learners are involved in the violation;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed if 11 learners or more are involved in the violation.

  2. Remedial measures for violations specified in this Article:

  The violating entity is compelled to cancel the illegal decision and restore learners’ right to learn if the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article is committed.

  Article 21.- Violations of the regulations on education popularization

  1. A caution or a fine of between VND 50,000 and 100,000 shall be imposed on acts of obstructing the attendance of students at all levels and in all educational levels.

  2. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed on acts of inciting students to not attend school or drop out of school at all levels and in all educational levels.

  Chapter III

                                     COMPETENCE AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTIONING

  Article 22.- Competence of the People's Committee presidents at all levels to sanction administrative violations in the field of education

  1. The People's Committee presidents of communes, wards or district townships may sanction according to the provisions of Article 28 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their respective localities and under their management.

  2. The People's Committee presidents of rural districts, urban districts, provincial capitals or townships may sanction according to the provisions of Article 29 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their respective localities and under their management.

  3. The presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall have the right to sanction according to the provisions in Article 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of administrative violation in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree within their respective localities and under their management.

  Article 23. Authority to sanction administrative violations of inspectors of education

  1. Inspectors on duty in the field of education have the right to impose penalties according to the provisions of Clause 1, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for administrative violations in the field of education specified in Chapter II of this Decree.

  2. The Chief Inspectors of the provincial/municipal Services of Education and Training shall have the right to sanction according to the provisions in Clause 2, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of administrative violation in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  3. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Education and Training has the right to sanction according to the provisions in Clause 3, Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  Article 24. Authorization for imposition of penalties for administrative violations in the field of education

  In case where the persons competent to sanction administrative violations in the field of education specified in Article 22 and Clauses 2 and 3, Article 23 of this Decree are absent, the authorization shall comply with the provisions of Article 14 of the Government's Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003, detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 25. Principles for determining the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on education

  1. The chairmen of the People's Committees at all levels shall have the right to handle administrative violations in the field of education in their respective localities according to competence prescribed in Articles 28, 29 and 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The education inspectors shall have the right to sanction administrative violations in the field of education according to the provisions of Article 38 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  In case where the acts of violation fall beyond the competence of the Chief Inspector of the provincial-level Education and Training Department, the dossiers on violations shall be transferred to the chairmen of the provincial-level People's Committees for handling according to competence.

  3. In case an individual or organization simultaneously commits many acts of administrative violation, including violations in the field of education, the sanctioning competence shall be determined according to the principle prescribed in Clause 3, Article 42 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  4. The competence to expel individuals being foreigners who commit administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with the provisions of Clause 1, Article 17 of Decree No. 21/2001/ND-CP of May 28, 2001 of the Government detailing the implementation of the Ordinance on Entry, Exit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam.

  5. In case where it is deemed that a violation against the law on education shows signs of a crime, the dossier shall be immediately transferred to the competent agency for settlement according to the provisions of Article 62 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 26.- Procedures for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education

  1. The procedures for making records and decisions on sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Articles 19, 20 and 21 of Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 of the Government detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The fine and payment thereof must comply with the procedures prescribed in Articles 24 and 25 of the Government’s Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. The procedures for stripping off the right to use decisions on establishment, licenses or practicing certificates shall comply with the provisions of Article 59 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 11 of the Government’s Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  4. The procedures for confiscating and handling material evidences and means used in the commission of administrative violations in the field of education shall comply with the provisions of Articles 60 and 61 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 31 of the Government’s Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 27.- Execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education shall have to execute the sanctioning decisions within 10 days after being handed over the sanctioning decisions, except for cases already provided for in Article 65 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. The time limit is calculated according to the provisions of Article 9 of the Government’s Decree No. 134/2003/ND-CP of November 14, 2003 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of education but failing to voluntarily execute the sanctioning decisions shall be forced to do so. Individuals and organizations subject to coercive execution shall bear all expenses for the organization of the coercive measures. The coercive execution procedures are prescribed in Articles 66 and 67 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                                 COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 28. Complaints and denunciations and settlement thereof

  1. Individuals, organizations or their lawful representatives may lodge complaints about sanctioning decisions of competent persons specified in Articles 22, 23 and 24 of this Decree. The procedures for lodging complaints and settling complaints shall comply with Article 118 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. The competence and time limit for lodging complaints and settling complaints shall comply with the Law on Complaints and Denunciations. The lodging of complaints about decisions on sanctioning administrative violations does not suspend the execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of education.

  2. Individuals have the right to denounce illegal acts in handling administrative violations in the field of education by competent sanctioning authorities to competent state agencies. The procedures for denunciation and settlement of denunciations and complaints shall comply with Article 118 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 29. Handling of violations committed by persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of education

  Persons who have the power to sanction administrative violations in the field of education but harass, tolerate, cover up, fail to handle or handle not in time, improperly, handle beyond prescribed authority shall be handled according to the provisions of Article 121 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 30. Handling of violations committed by persons subject to penalties for administrative violations in the field of education

  Persons sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of education who commit acts of opposing officials on duty, delaying or shirking the execution or committing other violations shall be handled according to the provisions of Article 122 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter 5.

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 31. Effect

  This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO.

  To annul the regulations on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of education prescribed in Decree No. 18/2001/ND-CP of May 4, 2001, and other regulations previously in contravention of this Decree.

  Article 32. Responsibility for guidance and implementation

  The Minister of Education and Training shall, within the ambit of his/her functions, tasks and powers, guide and organize the implementation of this Decree.

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of governmental agencies and presidents of provincial/municipal People's Committees shall have to implement this Decree.
