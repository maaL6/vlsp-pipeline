                                                  DECREE

     PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY; THRIFT AND WASTEFREEING PRACTICES; NATIONAL RESERVES; STATE TREASURY

  The Government’s Decree No. 63/2019/ND-CP dated July 11, 2019 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift and wastefreeing practices; national reserves; state treasury shall take effect from September 1, 2019 and be amended by:

  Decree No. 102/2021/ND-CP dated November 16, 2021 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decrees on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on tax and invoices; customs; insurance business and lottery business; management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury; independent accounting and audit; comes into force from January 01, 2022.

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Law on Management and Use of Public Property dated June 21, 2017;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Thrift Practice and Wastefulness Combat dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Law on National Reserve dated November 20, 2012;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury.[2]

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, remedial measures, power to record administrative violations, and power to impose penalties for the following sectors:

  a) Management and use of public property at agencies, organizations, units; projects funded by the State; property under public ownership and infrastructure invested in and managed by the State;

  b) Thrift practice and wastefulness combat;

  c) National reserves;

  d) State Treasury.

  2. Administrative violations related to management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserves; state treasury in terms of accounting, construction, investment planning, valuation, auction, and other regulations not specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be sanctioned according to relevant laws.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrift practice against wastefulness; national reserve; state treasury.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies;

  b) Units of the People's Armed Forces;

  c) Public non-business units;

  d) Agencies of the Communist Party of Vietnam;

  dd) Socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, and other organizations established under the law on associations;

  e) Other relevant enterprises, organizations, and individuals.

  3. Persons having power to impose penalties and organizations and individuals involved in imposition of administrative penalties according to this Decree.

  4. In case officials, public employees commit violations while performing their official duties or tasks and such violations are included in their assigned official duties or tasks prescribed in legislative documents or administrative documents issued by competent authorities or persons, they shall not be administratively sanctioned as prescribed in this Decree but handled in accordance with regulations of law on officials, public employees.

  In case regulatory agencies commit violations within their assigned state management tasks, they shall not be administratively sanctioned as prescribed in this Decree but handled in accordance with relevant laws.

  Article 3. Prescriptive periods for imposition of administrative penalties [3]

  1. The prescriptive period for imposition of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury prescribed in this Decree is 01 year. Regarding administrative violations involving public property being houses, land and infrastructure assets invested and managed by the State, the prescriptive period is 02 years.

  2. Time limit for calculation of prescriptive period for imposition of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property:

  a) For completed administrative violations, the prescriptive period shall start from the time when the violation ends;

  b) For ongoing administrative violations, the prescriptive period shall start from the time when the violation is detected;

  c) Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property under Article 6 hereof shall be considered completed if investment and procurement of property have been finished and property has been handed over to agencies, organizations, or units for management and use; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  d) Administrative violations against regulations on leasing property under Article 7 hereof shall be considered completed if property has been leased out and brought into use; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  dd) Administrative violations against regulations on encroaching on working offices or public service facilities under Article 11 hereof shall be considered as follows:

  - If an organization or individual that is using a land adjacent to another land parcel changes the boundary of the land parcel to expand the land area or builds an encroaching work within the land area of the working office or public service facility of an agency, organization, or unit, it shall be considered that such violation has been completed if the land area has been expanded or the encroaching work has been built out; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases.

  - If an organization or individual uses a house or land belonging to a working office or public service facility of an agency, organization, or unit without permission, it shall be considered that such violation is ongoing.

  e) Administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property that are being committed include those prescribed in Articles 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, and 26 hereof.

  3. Determination of ongoing administrative violations and completed administrative violations for calculation of prescriptive periods for imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness control:

  a) Administrative violations specified in Clause 2 Article 31 hereof shall be considered completed if property procurement and handover to agencies, organizations, or units for management and use have been finished; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  b) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered completed if the fund is fully used for purposes other than those intended; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered completed if the fund is not used in accordance with standards, regulations, or norms; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  c) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered completed if the fund is not used in accordance with standards, regulations, or norms; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  d) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 33 of this Decree shall be considered completed if the illegal obstruction of research, application of science and technology, and implementation of projects using recycled resources has ended; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 33 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  dd) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 33 of this Decree shall be considered completed if the exploitation and use of resources have not complied with the planning, plan, or process has ended; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 33 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  e) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 35 of this Decree shall be considered completed if the wastefulness in management of working offices and public service facilities has ended; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 35 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  g) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2, Clause 3 Article 36 of this Decree regarding procurement of assets and supplies in a single-member limited liability company funded by the State shall be considered completed if the procurement has been completed; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 36 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  h) Administrative violations in the field of savings being committed are violations specified in Clause 1 Article 31, Clauses 1 and 4 Article 36 of this Decree; violations regarding management and use of capital in single-member limited liability companies with state capital specified in Clause 2 Article 36 of this Decree; violations in management and use of assets and supplies specified in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 36 of this Decree.

  4. Determination of current administrative violations and completed administrative violations for calculation of prescriptive periods for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of national reserves:

  a) Administrative violations determined to be being committed are administrative violations specified in Articles 41, 42, 47, Points b and c Clause 3 Article 48, Article 49 of this Decree;

  b) Except for administrative violations specified in Point a of this Clause, remaining administrative violations are determined to be completed.

  5. Time of completion of administrative violations in the field of State Treasury is determined as follows:

  a) For administrative violations specified in Articles 54, 57, 58, 59 and 60 of this Decree: time of completion is the time of submission of documents and vouchers at the State Treasury;

  b) For administrative violations specified in Articles 55, 56 and 61 of this Decree: time of completion is the time when the State Treasury receives the conclusion of the criminal proceeding agency on the violation case which has no sign of crime.

  Article 4. Forms of sanctioning administrative violations and remedial measures

  1. Principal sanctions:

  a) The principal sanctions prescribed in this Decree include warnings and fines;

  b) The maximum fine for a violation against regulations on management and use of public property, national reserves or State treasury shall be VND 50,000,000 if the violation is committed by an individual or VND 100,000,000 if the violation is committed by an organization; the maximum fine for a violation against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat shall be VND 100,000,000 if the violation is committed by an individual or VND 200,000,000 if the violation is committed by an organization.

  2. Additional sanctions:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation, the violating entity may also be subject to additional sanctions such as confiscation of exhibits and/or means used to commit the violation.

  3. Remedial measures for violations against regulations on management and use of public property:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation, the violating entity may also be subject to one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Coercive payment of an amount equivalent to the value of the property;

  b) Enforced payment of amounts incurred from committing administrative violations;

  c) Enforced return of property; in case where the property has been altered due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original condition; in case where the original condition cannot be restored, it must be replaced with money or other property with similar functionality and use value;

  d) Enforced demolition of construction works built on encroached areas of working offices or public service facilities;

  dd) Enforced cancellation of false or fraudulent declarations in case of violations against regulations on access to and use of data on public property;

  e) Enforced adjustment, supplementation of data, information, and additional declarations on public property.

  4. Remedial measures for practice of thrift and prevention of wastefulness:

  Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating organization or individual may also be subject to one or several remedial measures, including:

  a) Revocation and transfer to the state budget of funds used for purposes other than those prescribed by the state budget for acts of using funds originating from the state budget for purposes or guidelines other than those issued by competent authorities;

  b) Forcible remittance into the State budget of illicit earnings from the use of funds originating from the State budget which fail to comply with operation regulations and financial mechanisms of the fund promulgated by competent authorities.

  5. Remedial measures in the field of national reserves:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of violations, violating organizations and individuals may be subject to one or several remedial measures as follows:

  a) Forcible remittance of illicit earnings from acts of violation of regulations on purchase, sale, preservation, distribution and assistance; violation of regulations on import and export of national reserves; violation of regulations on management of capital and charges in national reserve activities; violation of regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease and exploitation of assets under national reserves;

  b) Forcible restoration of the original state of establishments, material-technical facilities and storage of national reserves;

  c) Forcible refund of damaged national reserves caused by acts of infringement or destruction; violation of regulations on distribution and assistance of national reserves;

  d) Forcible recall of national reserves issued or distributed in contravention of regulations;

  dd) Forcible restoration of the original state of assets for acts of illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease and exploitation of assets being storage of national reserves.

  6. Remedial measures in the field of State treasury:

  Depending on the nature and severity of violations, violating organizations may be subject to one or several remedial measures as follows:

  a) Forcible revocation of amounts already paid for the unfinished work; unfinished work not eligible for payment and the paid amount exceeding the contract value or appendix;

  b) Forcible revocation of amounts already paid for the improper documents and improper payments made from improper documents compared with the original documents at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Forcible revocation of all amounts already paid for the use of fake documents to pay the State budget but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Forcible completion of documents according to regulations for acts of violating regulations on payment of the State budget;

  dd) Forcible completion of procedures for commitment before requesting the State Treasury to pay or remit the State budget or forcible completion of procedures for commitment for acts of violating procedures for control of commitment;

  e) Forcible completion of procedures for advance payment for acts of violating procedures and time limit for advance payment of the State budget;

  g) Forcible freezing or closing of accounts in case of violations against regulations on registration and use of accounts at State Treasuries; forcible freezing of accounts in case of making false documents to register use of accounts at State Treasuries but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  7.[4] Organizations and individuals sanctioned for administrative violations under this Decree are not allowed to use state budget money or money originating from state budget to pay fines and remedial measures for consequences caused by their violations, except for cases specified in Clause 8 Article 37 of Government’s Decree No. 10/2021/ND-CP dated February 9, 2021 on management of construction investment costs.

  Article 5. Application of fine levels [5]

  1. The fine levels prescribed in Section 1, Section 2, Section 3 and Section 4 Chapter II, Section 1 Chapter III, Section 1 Chapter IV, Section 1 and Section 2 Chapter V of this Decree are the fine levels prescribed for administrative violations committed by organizations; the fine level imposed on an individual is half of that imposed on an organization for the same administrative violation (except for the provisions of Articles 17, 23 and 27 of this Decree).

  2. When imposing a fine, the specific fine level for an administrative violation shall be the average level of the fine bracket prescribed for such violation. If there are mitigating circumstances, each mitigating circumstance may reduce the average fine level of the fine bracket by 10%, but the fine level imposed on such violation must not be lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if there are aggravating circumstances, each aggravating circumstance may increase the average fine level of the fine bracket by 10%, but the fine level imposed on such violation must not exceed the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  3. When determining the fine level for an administrative violation which has both aggravating and mitigating circumstances, the aggravating circumstances may be reduced according to the principle that one mitigating circumstance may be reduced by one aggravating circumstance.

  Chapter II

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS AND FINES IN MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY AT AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND EMPLOYEES

  Article 6. Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets without a decision of a competent authority or person:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if investing in or purchasing assets being machinery, equipment, or other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a total purchase value of less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 in case of investing in or purchasing assets being machinery, equipment and other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a total purchase value of VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) VND 20,000,000 - VND 50,000,000 in case of investing or purchasing assets being working offices, public service facilities, and cars.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to conduct centralized procurement of assets on the list of assets subject to centralized procurement in accordance with laws on management and use of public property.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets beyond the limits prescribed by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds the limit by less than 50.000.000 per unit of asset;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds the limit by 50.000.000 - 100.000.000 per unit of asset;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds the limit by 100.000.000 per unit of asset or more.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced payment of an amount equal to the value of assets invested or purchased beyond the limits prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the excess value as a basis for imposing penalties:

  a) In case of investing in or purchasing assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment, or other assets in excess of the limits in terms of quantity: The excess value per unit of asset is determined based on the unit price of such assets according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or the asset purchase contract.

  b) In case of investing in or purchasing assets being cars, machinery, equipment, or other assets in quantity but exceeding the price compared to standards and norms: The excess value is determined by subtracting (-) the value of the asset according to standards and norms prescribed by competent state agencies from the value recorded on the asset purchase invoice or asset procurement contract;

  c) In case of investing in or purchasing working offices or public service facilities in excess of standards and norms: The excess value is determined by subtracting (-) the area used according to standards and norms multiplied by (x) the average unit price recorded on the invoice (or asset procurement contract);

  d) Persons having sanctioning competence prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as a basis for sanctioning in accordance with laws on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 7. Administrative violations against regulations on leasing assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on leasing assets if the value of the asset lease contract is less than VND 100.000.000 in the following cases:

  a) Committing the act of leasing assets without a decision on asset lease issued by a competent agency or person;

  b) Committing the act of leasing assets in excess of standards and norms prescribed by competent state agencies;

  c) Committing the act of selecting asset lease service providers in a manner or order not in compliance with laws on management and use of public property and relevant laws.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on leasing of property as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article if the value of the property lease contract is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of the amount of rent in excess of the prescribed limits and norms in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The value of the property lease contract serving as the basis for imposing penalties shall be determined as follows:

  a) In case the value of the property lease contract is specified in the contract, the value used as the basis for imposing penalties shall be the value specified in the contract;

  b) In case the property is leased out without being written into a contract or with insufficient information to determine the value as prescribed in Clause a of this Point, the value used as the basis for imposing penalties shall be the market price of similar property with equivalent standards and technical specifications at the time when the violation occurs multiplied by (x) the period during which the property is leased out from the beginning of leasing until the date of issuance of the decision on imposition of administrative penalties.

  The person having the power to impose penalties prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the value used as the basis for imposing penalties in accordance with laws on handling of administrative violations and shall take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 8. Administrative violations against regulations on allocation and use of public property, destruction or intentional damage to public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of allocating public property in excess of the standards and norms issued by competent authorities or persons (incorrectly allocated, exceeding the area, quantity, or price) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed where the property is allocated or used with a value of under VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed where the property is allocated or used with a value of from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed where the property is allocated or used with a value of VND 100.000.000 or more.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of allocating or using public property for wrongful purposes (using public property for purposes or uses other than those intended; using working offices or public service facilities as residential houses or for personal use; using cars to transport from residence to workplace for unqualified positions; using cars for business trips for unqualified positions without approval by competent authorities; allocating or using cars for personal use; using machinery, equipment, or other property for personal use) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed where the property allocated or used is machinery, equipment, or other property (excluding working offices, public service facilities, and cars) with an original cost recorded in the accounting book of less than VND 100.000.000 per unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property valued at less than VND 100.000.000);

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed where the property allocated or used is machinery, equipment, or other property (excluding working offices, public service facilities, and cars) with an original cost recorded in the accounting book of VND 100.000.000 or more per unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property valued at VND 100.000.000 or more);

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed where the property allocated or used is working offices, public service facilities, or cars.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for destroying or intentionally damaging public property if not liable to criminal prosecution:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging machinery, equipment and other property whose book value is less than VND 100.000.000 per unit of property;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging machinery, equipment and other property whose book value is VND 100.000.000 or more per unit of property;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory payment of an amount equivalent to the value of property exceeding the prescribed limits and norms in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of property altered by the administrative violation; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, the offender shall pay another amount of money or return property with similar functions and use value to the original one in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the value exceeding the prescribed limits and norms shall be carried out as follows:

  a) In case of delivery and use of assets being cars, machinery, equipment or other assets in excess of the standard or limit: The excess value is determined by multiplying the number of excess assets by the original cost of assets recorded in accounting books; in case the original cost of assets is not recorded in accounting books, it shall be determined according to the purchase value of new assets of the same type or those having equivalent technical standards and uses on the market at the time of commission of violations;

  b) In case of delivery and use of assets being cars, machinery, equipment or other assets with correct quantity but exceeding the price level compared to the standard or limit: The excess value is determined by multiplying the number of excess assets by (the difference between the original cost of assets recorded in accounting books minus (-) the value of assets according to the standard or limit prescribed by a competent authority);

  c) In case of assets being working offices or public service facilities: The excess value is determined by multiplying the difference between the actual area and (-) the area used according to the standard or limit by the average unit price recorded in accounting books; in case the unit price is not recorded in accounting books, it shall be determined according to the investment rate of houses or works having equivalent technical standards issued by the Ministry of Construction applicable at the time of commission of violations;

  d) Persons having sanctioning competence specified in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as the basis for sanctioning according to the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 9. Administrative violations against regulations on lending of public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for lending or using public property in contravention of regulations (lending the public property to another organization or individual regardless of whether there is a loan contract or not, regardless of the loan term) with the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the lent public property is valued at less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the lent public property is valued at VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed in case of lending working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  2. Remedial measures:

  The organization committing the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also take the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of lent property. In case where the property has been changed due to the administrative violation, it is required to restore the original condition of the property; in case where the original condition of the property cannot be restored, it is required to return the money or property with similar functions and use value to the initial property;

  b) Enforced payment to the state budget of an amount corresponding to the rent of property during the lending period. The determination of the rent of property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 10. Administrative violations against regulations on exchange and gifting of public property inconsistently with regulations

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of exchanging public property inconsistently with regulations (using public property of one organization to exchange for property of another organization or individual without permission of competent agencies or persons) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case where the property used for exchange is valued at less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if the property put up for exchange is assessed at ≥ VND 100,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed if the property put up for exchange is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for gifting public property against regulations (using public property as gifts in violation of Regulations on gift-giving promulgated by competent authorities).

  3. Remedial measures:

  The violating entity is compelled to return the property or pay an amount of money equivalent to the value of the property put up for exchange or gifted in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 11. Administrative violations against regulations on encroaching on working offices or public service facilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for encroaching on a working office or public service facility (an organization/individual that is using adjacent land and relocates the boundary of a land parcel to expand the land area or build an encroaching work into the land area of the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit; or uses the house/land belonging to the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit without permission).

  2. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Organizations and individuals committing violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original conditions of working offices or public service facilities and return of the encroached working offices or public service facilities;

  b) Enforced demolition of works built on encroached areas;

  c) Enforced payment to the State budget of an amount corresponding to the rent of property during the period of encroachment. The determination of the rent of property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4, Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 12. Acts of appropriating public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed on acts of appropriating public property which are not serious enough to be examined for penal liability (holding or using public property without permission of competent State agencies) according to the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of public property is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of public property is VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of expropriating working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  2. Additional sanctions: Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures: The violator that commits any of the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also take the following remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of the property altered by the administrative violation in order to return it to its original state to the organization; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and use value to the original property shall be provided;

  b) Mandatory payment of an amount equivalent to the rent of the property during the period of expropriation to the state budget. Determination of the amount of rent as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with regulations in Clause 4 Article 7 hereof.

  Article 13. Administrative violations against regulations on using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes without a decision on approval of the Scheme issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with the law on management and use of public property at the time of commission of the violation:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on determination of value of property used for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for establishing a Council for valuation or a Council for appraisal of property with improper composition;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for selecting an appraisal organization to determine the value of property used for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes against the prescribed procedures.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes against the approved Scheme issued by a competent authority or person, thereby affecting the performance of assigned functions and tasks by the State:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is a car or the property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is an office building, a public service facility or a car.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) The violator is compelled to restore the original condition of the property which has been changed as a result of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, the violating entity is liable to have such property replaced with another one of equal value or with another one having similar functions and use value;

  b) The violating entity is compelled to return the illegal profit earned from committing the violations specified in Clauses 1 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Administrative violations against regulations on maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law

  1. If an organization is assigned by a competent authority to prepare an estimate for maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law but fails to carry out the maintenance and repair of the property, resulting in damage to the property, it shall incur the following penalties:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the damaged property is assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the damaged property is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed if the damaged property is assessed at VND 100.000.000 or more and is a working office, public service facility or car but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Determination of the amount of money to be returned corresponding to the value of the damaged property:

  a) If the damage is irreparable, the amount of money to be returned shall be determined by multiplying the purchase price of a new property of the same type or standard with the remaining quality percentage at the time of valuation;

  b) If the damage is repairable, the amount of money to be returned shall be the cost of repairing the property;

  c) The head of the organization shall assign the financial agency under his/her management to act as the focal point for determining the amount of money to be returned; on the basis of which, the head of the organization shall decide the amount of money to be returned and take responsibility for his/her decision.

  Article 15. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare plans for disposition of public property as prescribed by law;

  b) Declaring and preparing plans for disposition of public property beyond the prescribed time limit;

  c) Failing to submit reports on the repossession, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of property to competent state authorities when conditions are met as prescribed by law (except where public property has reached the end of its useful life in accordance with law but remains usable and is still needed by agencies, organizations, units or enterprises, in which case heads of such agencies, organizations, units or enterprises shall decide appropriate disposal methods).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on an organization for damaging or losing property while awaiting disposal.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for failure to obtain a decision from a competent authority or person before disposing of property:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of property sold, transferred, liquidated or destroyed is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of property sold, transferred, liquidated or destroyed is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of selling, transferring or liquidating assets that are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for providing false information about the list of assets and their current conditions (leading to improper decisions on asset disposal):

  a) A warning shall be imposed if the declared assets are valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the declared assets are valued at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the declared assets are valued at VND 100.000.000 or more;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the declared assets are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) The violating entity is compelled to restore the original condition of the assets that have been changed due to the violations prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the assets, the violating entity is required to refund the money or return the assets with similar functions and use value to the original ones;

  b) Enforced return of an amount equivalent to the value of lost or damaged property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  6. Determination of the amount to be returned as compensation for the loss or damage of property specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article shall be carried out as follows:

  a) In case the lost or damaged property cannot be repaired, the amount to be returned shall be determined based on the price of a new property of the same type or with similar standards and use in the market at the time when the violation is committed.

  The market price may be based on the quoted prices of suppliers on the market that have been posted or announced on the market or from official information disclosed by suppliers via the Internet.

  b) In case the damaged property can be repaired, the amount to be returned shall be the repair cost.

  Article 16. Administrative violations against regulations on organization of handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to implement the disposal plan approved by a competent authority or person within the prescribed time limit.

  a) Exceeding the time limit as determined by the handling time limit specified in the decision on handling of property. In case the decision on handling of property does not specify a specific time limit, it shall comply with relevant laws.

  In case the plan for extending the handling of property is approved by a competent authority, the prescribed time limit shall be determined according to the extended time limit.

  b) Failing to transfer property under a decision on transfer or recovery to the receiving agency within the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Establishing the valuation council ultra vires in accordance with law;

  b) Establishing the valuation council with an improper composition in accordance with law;

  c) Selecting a valuation organization or professional auction organization to handle property in a manner that does not comply with the prescribed form and procedures in accordance with law;

  d) Failing to fully disclose information about the public auction of property in accordance with the law on management and use of public property (failing to post up information about the public auction of property at the prescribed locations; failing to publicly disclose information on the public auction of property on the mass media; failing to provide adequate information as required by law on the public auction of property; failing to fully disclose information as required by law on the public auction of property).

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to establish a valuation council as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to publicly notify the sale of public property according to Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for failing to conduct valuation as the basis for determining reserve prices when handling property that is office buildings, public service facilities or other property on land in form of sale; failing to hold property auctions in cases where such auction is required by law:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of the property is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of the property is VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 if the property is an office building, public service facility or car.

  Article 17. Administrative violations against regulations on entry and use of data on public property

  Fines imposed on organizations and individuals committing violations against regulations on public property data entry and use shall be as follows:

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing a declaration report on assets for data entry into the National Public Property Database against the legal documents or in a manner inconsistent with the current status of assets of the unit;

  b) Implementing data entry decentralization to subordinate units without a written agreement from the Ministry of Finance;

  c) Entering or approving public property data inconsistently with the declaration report of the organization assigned to manage and use the property.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Erasing, deleting, or altering the declaration report on assets to falsify the data on public property compared to the actual status of the property;

  b) Accessing, intruding, or attempting to intrude, destroy, or alter data in the National Public Property Database or the software program structure;

  c) Exploiting information on public property in the national database on public property of agencies, organizations and units not under their management;

  d) Using data on public property in the national database on public property for personal purposes without permission from the agency competent to manage such database (using information stored in the database for purposes other than those prescribed by laws on management and exploitation of the Public Property Management Software without permission from the agency competent to manage such database).

  3. Remedial measures

  a) Enforced cancellation of false or fraudulent declaration reports;

  b) Enforced correction of data to match the actual condition of property of the unit;

  c) Enforced preparation of additional declaration reports when there is a change in property operations;

  d) Enforced correction of false or misleading information;

  dd) Enforced transfer of illegal profits earned from committing administrative violations.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF ASSETS OF STATE-Funded PROJECTS

  Article 18. Administrative violations against regulations on procurement of assets of state-funded projects

  1. Sanction an organization for committing the act of asset procurement without a decision issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with Clause 1 Article 6 hereof.

  2. Sanction an organization for committing the act of failure to conduct centralized procurement of assets on the list of assets subject to centralized procurement as prescribed by law in accordance with Clause 2 Article 6 hereof.

  3. Sanction an organization for committing the act of procurement of assets exceeding the prescribed standards and limits in accordance with Clause 3 Article 6 hereof.

  The value of assets exceeding the prescribed standards and limits shall be determined in accordance with Clause 5 Article 6 hereof.

  4. Sanction an organization for committing the act of violating regulations on leasing assets for management purposes of state-funded projects in accordance with Article 7 hereof.

  Article 19. Administrative violations against regulations on allocation and use of assets of state-funded projects

  1. Sanction an organization for the act of assigning or using assets of a state-funded project in excess of the prescribed standards and limits or for purposes other than those prescribed in Article 8 of this Decree.

  2. Sanction an organization for the act of lending assets of a state-funded project in contravention of regulations prescribed in Article 9 of this Decree.

  3. Sanction an organization for the act of exchanging, gifting or presenting assets of a state-funded project in contravention of regulations prescribed in Article 10 of this Decree.

  4. Sanction an organization or individual for the act of encroaching upon working offices or public service facilities of the State-funded Project Management Board prescribed in Article 11 of this Decree.

  5. Sanction an organization or individual for the act of appropriating assets of a state-funded project prescribed in Article 12 of this Decree.

  6. Sanction an organization for the act of violating regulations on use of assets of a state-funded project for production, business, service provision, lease, joint venture or association activities prescribed in Article 13 of this Decree.

  7. Sanction an organization for the act of failing to maintain or repair assets of a state-funded project in accordance with law prescribed in Article 14 of this Decree.

  Article 20. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of assets of projects at the end of their implementation or when they are no longer needed

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to inventory and report to a competent authority for handling of assets within the prescribed time limit;

  b) Failing to report to a competent authority for establishing public ownership of assets transferred by ODA experts, consulting contractors or supervision entities to the Government of Vietnam as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Letting assets deteriorate or go missing during the waiting period for handling;

  b) Removing or changing the structure, parts or components of assets.

  3. The violating organization shall be liable to penalties for selling, transferring, liquidating or destroying assets without decision of a competent authority or person as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 15 hereof.

  4. The violating organization shall be liable to penalties for incorrect declaration of the list of assets to be handled and their current conditions as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 15 hereof.

  5. Sanctioning organizations for committing acts of violating regulations on organization of handling of assets approved by competent agencies or persons shall comply with the provisions of Article 16 of this Decree.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Organizations that commit acts of violating the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a/ Forced to restore the original state of assets which have been changed due to administrative violations; in case of failure to restore the original state of assets, they shall have to return money or assets with functions and use value equivalent to the original assets;

  b/ Forced to pay an amount corresponding to the value of assets lost or damaged. The determination of the amount payable corresponding to the value of assets lost or damaged shall comply with the provisions of Clause 6, Article 15 of this Decree.

  Section 3. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS, SANCTIONING LEVELS IN MANAGEMENT AND HANDLING OF ASSETS WITH FULL PUBLIC PROPERTY RIGHTS ESTABLISHED, SANCTIONING FORMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES

  Article 21. Acts of violating regulations on establishment of full public property rights over assets, preservation and transfer of assets with full public property rights established

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to report to competent agencies or persons for establishment of public ownership of property as prescribed;

  b) Transferring property to competent authorities for handling beyond the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Letting property deteriorate or go missing during the waiting period for handling;

  b) Removing or changing the structure, parts, or components of the property.

  3. Remedial measures:

  The organization committing the violation in Clause 2 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original condition of the property altered due to the administrative violation; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, it is required to return money or property with similar functions and use value to the original property;

  b) Enforced payment of an amount equivalent to the value of the lost or damaged property. The determination of the amount payable equivalent to the value of the lost or damaged property shall comply with Clause 6 Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on disposal of property under public ownership

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit reports to competent authorities or persons responsible for preparing plans for disposal of property under public ownership;

  b) Submitting reports on preparation of plans for disposal of property under public ownership by the prescribed deadline.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to prepare plans for disposal of property under public ownership;

  b) Preparing plans for disposal of property under public ownership beyond the prescribed deadline.

  3. Sanctioning organizations or individuals committing acts of violating regulations on organization of handling of property under establishment of public ownership approved by competent agencies or persons shall comply with the provisions of Article 16 of this Decree.

  Article 23. Violations of regulations on logging in and using data on property under establishment of public ownership
  Sanctioning organizations or individuals committing violations in logging in and using data on property under establishment of public ownership shall comply with the provisions of Article 17 of this Decree.

  Section 4. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS AND SANCTION LEVELS IN MANAGEMENT AND USE OF INFRASTRUCTURAL PROPERTY

  Article 24. Violations in exploitation of infrastructure property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for leasing out the right to exploit or transferring the right to exploit infrastructure property for a definite term without obtaining a decision approving the Scheme issued by a competent agency or person as prescribed by law on management and use of public property.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on determination of lease prices of the right to exploit or transfer the right to exploit infrastructure property according to the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for establishing a valuation council with improper composition or authority;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for selecting an appraisal organization to determine the lease price of a definite right to exploit or assign a definite right to exploit a physical infrastructure asset in a manner that does not comply with regulations of law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for leasing out a definite right to exploit or assign a definite right to exploit a physical infrastructure asset for purposes other than those specified in the scheme approved by a competent authority or person.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of the proceeds obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 25. Violations against regulations on disposal of physical infrastructure assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare a plan for disposal of assets as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to report to a competent authority for revocation, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of assets when fully satisfying eligibility requirements as prescribed by law (except where public assets have expired according to regulations of law but agencies, organizations, units or enterprises still require them for use, in which case heads of such agencies, organizations, units or enterprises shall decide on appropriate disposal methods).

  c) Failing to report to competent state agencies for handling of assets in case of loss or destruction.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Causing damage or loss of assets while awaiting disposal;

  b) Declaring incorrect information about the list and current conditions of assets proposed for disposal, resulting in a decision on asset disposal that is contrary to regulations.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for disposing of assets without a decision issued by a competent agency or person.

  4. Remedial measures:

  The violator is compelled to return an amount equivalent to the value of assets that are lost or damaged, applicable to the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article. The determination of the amount to be returned equivalent to the value of assets that are lost or damaged shall comply with Clause 6 Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 26. Violations against regulations on organization of disposal of assets of infrastructure facilities

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Past the prescribed time limit but still fail to implement the plan for handling approved by competent agencies or persons;

  b) Failing to hand over property under transfer decision or recovery decision to the receiving agency within the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Establishing not in accordance with competence, not in accordance with composition of the valuation council, destruction council as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to fully perform the notification of public auction of public property according to provisions of law on property auction (failing to post up the information about property auction at the prescribed places; failing to make public announcement on mass media or making public announcement but not enough time for property auction according to provisions of law on property auction; contents of posted up, public announcement are not full information as prescribed by law on property auction).

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to establish the valuation council, destruction council as prescribed by law;

  b) Selecting a valuation organization or a professional auction organization to handle assets in an improper form or order as prescribed by law;

  c) Failing to publicly notify the sale of public property according to Point b Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out valuation as a basis for determining the reserve price when selling infrastructure assets.

  Article 27. Violations against regulations on access to and use of data on infrastructure assets

  Sanctioning of organizations and individuals committing violations against regulations on access to and use of data on infrastructure assets shall comply with Article 17 of this Decree.

  Section 5. POWERS TO MAKE RECORDS AND IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

  b) Officials assigned to inspect compliance with laws on management and use of public property.

  2. Persons having authority to issue offense notices as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article are responsible for issuing penalty decisions within their jurisdiction or transferring the case to competent authorities as prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 hereof for issuance of penalty decisions.

  1. Chairpersons of district-level People’s Committees have the power to:

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations[6];

  d) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  1. Inspectors and persons assigned to perform specialized inspections in the field of management and use of public property while on duty shall have the power to:

  d) Take remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chief Inspectors of provincial Departments, heads of specialized inspectorate teams at provincial level have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 50,000,000;

  c) [8] Confiscate exhibits and instrument of administrative violation whose value does not exceed VND 100,000,000;

  d) Remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of ministerial-level specialized inspectorates shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  c) [9] Confiscate exhibits and instrument of administrative violation whose value does not exceed VND 140.000.000;

  d) Remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. Chief Inspector of Ministry shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) A fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter III

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR THESE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF SAVIOR PRACTICE AND WASTE PREVENTION

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON SAVIOR PRACTICE AND WASTE PREVENTION

  Article 31. Violations against regulations on saving practice and waste prevention in use of communication means, electricity, water, gasoline, oil, books, newspapers, stationery, organization of conferences or seminars, expenses for reception and business trips at home and abroad with the State budget funds; procurement of equipment serving the operation of health facilities funded by the State budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for using communication means, electricity, water, gasoline, oil, books, newspapers, stationery, organization of conferences or seminars, expenses for reception and business trips at home and abroad with the State budget funds in excess of the standards and norms issued by competent authorities.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for failure to purchase equipment and devices serving medical examination and treatment establishments provided by the State budget not on the list approved by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the total value of equipment and devices purchased is less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the total value of equipment and devices purchased is from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the total value of equipment and devices purchased is from VND 100.000.000 to under VND 200.000.000.

  3. Medical examination and treatment establishments that purchase equipment and devices serving their operations provided by the State budget in excess of the prescribed standards and norms shall incur penalties according to Article 6 hereof.

  Article 32. Violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat in management and use of non-state budget financial funds [10]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Using non-state budget financial funds [11] for purposes or objectives other than those set forth in the fund regulations issued by competent authorities;

  b) Using state budget funds not in accordance with regulations of competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed for wasting investment capital in contravention of standards, regulations, norms, and unit prices as prescribed by law.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) The entity committing the violation in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to return the funds that were used for improper purposes;

  b) The violating entity is compelled to transfer illegal profits obtained from administrative violations to state budget funds not in accordance with regulations of competent authorities in case of commission of the violation in Point b Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 33. Violations against laws on thrift practice and waste combat in exploitation and use of natural resources but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for illegally obstructing research, application of science and technology, or implementation of projects using recycled natural resources.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for exploiting and using natural resources against planning, plans, and procedures approved by competent authorities.

  Article 34. Violations against laws on thrift and wastefulness in investment projects using state budget, public money and assets

  Violations against laws on thrift and wastefulness in investment projects using state budget shall be subject to penalties prescribed in Decree No. 139/2017/ND-CP dated November 27, 2017 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on construction investment; mineral exploitation, processing and trading as building materials, production and trading of building materials; management of technical infrastructure works; real estate business, housing development, management and use of houses and offices; relevant documents and amendments (if any).

  Article 35. Violations against laws on thrift and wastefulness in management and use of working offices and public service facilities of organizations using state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on management of working offices and public service facilities of organizations using state budget and causing wastefulness.

  2. The penalties for violations against regulations on standards and norms for use of working offices and public service facilities of organizations using state budget and causing wastefulness shall be subject to Article 8 hereof.

  Article 36. Violations against laws on thrift and wastefulness in management and use of capital and public property in a single-member limited liability company funded by state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on establishment, management and use of funds in a single-member limited liability company funded by state budget in a manner that fails to comply with purposes, exceeds limits, standards and regimes issued by competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for wasting capital in a one-state-owned limited liability company or purchasing, managing and using assets and supplies in a one-state-owned limited liability company against regulations on management of public finance, public assets and construction investment.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed for purchasing, managing and using fixed assets, supplies and other assets in a one-state-owned limited liability company in excess of the limits, norms and regimes promulgated by competent authorities.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 70.000.000 to VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to comply with the limits, unit prices, norms and regimes prescribed by competent authorities when managing and using expenses in a one-state-owned limited liability company.

  Section 2. POWERS TO MAKE ADMINISTRATIVE OFFENSE NOTICES AND IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF SAVIOR PRACTICE AND WASTE PREVENTION

  Article 37. Powers to make administrative offense notices and impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of thrift practice and waste prevention

  1. Persons having the power to make administrative offense notices in the field of thrift practice and waste prevention include:

  a) Persons having the power to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of thrift practice and waste prevention as prescribed in Articles 38 and 39 of this Decree;

  b) Officials assigned to inspect compliance with laws on thrift practice and waste prevention.

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 50,000,000;

  b) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chairpersons of provincial-level People's Committees have the power to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 200,000,000;

  b) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 39. Power to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Chief Inspectors of Departments and holders of equivalent titles assigned by the Government to perform specialized inspection functions, heads of specialized inspection teams at Departmental level shall have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Heads of specialized inspection teams at ministerial level shall have the power to:

  Chapter IV

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON NATIONAL RESERVES

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON NATIONAL RESERVES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES

  Article 40. Violations against regulations on purchase and sale of national reserves

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to notify or publicly post information about entities engaging in purchase and sale; methods of purchase and sale; purchase and sale prices; time limit for purchase and sale; quantity, categories, specifications, standards and quality of national reserves according to regulations of competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the following violations:

  Failing to comply with regulations issued by competent regulatory authorities on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to purchase national reserve goods when all conditions have been met under decisions of competent state agencies;

  b) Trading in national reserve goods without written consent of competent state agencies;

  c) To buy or sell national reserve goods when the time limit for buying or selling national reserve goods has expired;

  d) Trading in national reserve goods not in conformity with quantity, category, specifications, standards and quality approved by competent authorities;

  dd) Failing to comply with regulations issued by competent state authorities on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for arbitrarily changing the prices of national reserve goods for self-seeking purposes.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of profits earned from illegal activities to government budget in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 41. Violations against regulations on management of national reserve

  1. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts of violation:

  a) Failing to open books for monitoring the quality, quantity and categories of national reserve goods during preservation;

  b) Failing to submit regular or irregular reports on preservation of national reserve goods.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for violations against regulations on transfer of national reserve goods:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent regulatory authorities on transfer of national reserve goods;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 7.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent regulatory authorities on transfer of national reserve goods.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recall of national reserve goods that have been improperly transported in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 42. Violations against regulations on preservation of national reserve goods

  1. To impose a fine on act of failing to implement or implementing not in compliance with national technical regulations or temporary technical requirements for preservation of national reserve goods issued by competent state agencies;

  Failing to comply with regulations on the storage duration of national reserve goods; storing national reserve goods at a location other than that prescribed by competent authorities, penalties shall be imposed as follows:

  a) VND 300,000 - VND 500,000 in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of the warehousing, preservation and circulation of national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for hiring an organization/individual ineligible to preserve national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100.000.000.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 through 3 of this Article to state budget.

  Article 43. Acts of infringing upon or destroying material-technical facilities and/or storage of national reserves which are not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for illegally infringing upon material-technical facilities and/or storage of national reserves.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for destroying material-technical facilities and/or storage of national reserves.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for acts of infringing upon or destroying national reserves:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused to national reserves;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if less than VND 50,000,000 is damaged to national reserves;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000 is damaged to national reserves.

  4. Additional sanctions

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory restoration of the original state of material and technical facilities and storage of national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed;

  b) Compulsory return of damaged national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article is committed.

  Article 44. Violations against regulations on provision and assistance of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of provision or assistance of national reserve goods or performance of other tasks related to national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000.

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out procedures for distribution and provision of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 within the prescribed time limit.

  3. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for using national reserve goods for wrong purposes; distributing or providing national reserve goods to wrong entities according to decisions of competent authorities; failing to ensure the type, quantity, quality, standards and specifications of national reserve goods in accordance with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for loss of national reserve goods used for distribution, provision or performance of other tasks:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the lost national reserve goods are valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the value of the national reserve goods is from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for distributing national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100,000,000 to ineligible entities according to a competent authority’s decision.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for delayed or untimely provision of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 in accordance with regulations of competent authorities.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for delaying or procrastinating the distribution of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 under decisions of competent authorities, thus causing damage to national reserve goods.

  8. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clauses 3, 5 and 6 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of lost national reserve goods, or goods used for wrong purposes or issued to wrong entities, in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  c) Enforced return of damaged, inferior or devalued national reserve goods, or goods with insufficient quantity, in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 45. Violations against regulations on importation and exportation of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of importation, exportation or transportation of national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused but the value of the damage is less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused but the value of the damage is from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to implement or improperly implementing procedures for import and export of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 as prescribed;

  b) Delivery of goods not in conformity with quantity, type, specifications, standards and quality of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 according to decisions of competent authorities.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 not in conformity with the type, specifications, quantity, quality, time and place specified in the decision issued by a competent authority.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 without a decision issued by a competent authority.

  5. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing administrative violations prescribed in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;

  b) Enforced recall of national reserve goods that have been issued against regulations for violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 46. Violations against regulations on receipt of national reserve goods but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Receiving national reserve goods not at the time and place specified in the decision of a competent authority;

  b) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on procedures and order for receiving national reserve goods.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to receive national reserve goods (national reserve goods issued for aid or other tasks of competent authorities) when all conditions prescribed by the competent authority are satisfied.

  Article 47. Illegal intervention in national reserve activities

  1. Warnings or fines shall be imposed for illegal intervention in national reserve activities as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000.

  2. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 48. Violations against regulations on management of capital and charges in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for failure to report the use of capital from national reserve funds for purchase of national reserve goods as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Payment for goods in stock without a sale contract;

  b) Payment for goods in stock not in quantity or with insufficient invoices and documents;

  c) Payment for goods in stock without a record of contract liquidation or a list of documents on receipt of goods in stock bearing the signature of the unit’s head for cases of direct purchase without bidding;

  d) Payment for goods in stock without approval by the head of the national reserve unit;

  dd) Using fees for receipt, dispatch and preservation of goods in stock inconsistently with the prescribed contents or exceeding the prescribed limits.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Using national reserve funds for purposes other than those prescribed;

  b) Failing to comply with regulations on management of funds derived from national reserve funds or improperly complying with such regulations.

  c) Failure to remit the remaining amount after completing the purchase of national reserve goods in accordance with regulations of competent state agencies.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remittance of illegal profits earned from committing the violations specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 49. Violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets included in national reserves

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets included in national reserves (except for storage of national reserve goods):

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of the assets is less than VND 70.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of the assets is from VND 70.000.000 to less than VND 150.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 if the value of the assets is from VND 150.000.000 to less than VND 300.000.000;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth from VND 300.000.000 to under VND 500.000.000;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth VND 500.000.000 or more.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for trading, pledging, mortgaging, leasing or illegally exploiting the national reserve storehouse:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the national reserve storehouse is less than VND 500.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the national reserve storehouse is VND 500.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory restoration of the original condition of the property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory disgorgement of illegal gains from commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Chapter V

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF STATE TREASURY

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE BUDGET SPENDING THAT MUST BE INCLUDED IN THE STATE BUDGET ESTIMATES APPROVED BY COMPETENT AUTHORITIES

  Article 54. Violations against regulations on state budget spending that must be included in the state budget estimates approved by competent authorities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for activities or tasks not included in the estimates approved by competent authorities;

  b) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for expenditures exceeding the annual funding estimates or plans assigned by competent authorities or exceeding the approved estimates (for activities not carried out under contracts);

  c) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for expenditures not in accordance with the estimates assigned by competent authorities (expenditures made from sources other than those specified in the estimates; expenditures made for purposes or beneficiaries other than those specified in the estimates; expenditures made from sources other than those specified in investment capital; expenditures made for projects other than those specified in the lists approved by competent authorities).

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 6,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Making dossiers and documents to send to the State Treasury for payment for the workload not yet performed;

  b) Making dossiers and documents to send to the State Treasury for payment for the workload being performed incompletely or not eligible for payment;

  c) Making dossiers and documents to send to the State Treasury for payment in excess of contract value or contract appendix.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forced revocation of amounts already paid for the workload not yet performed; the workload being performed incompletely or not eligible for payment and the part already paid in excess of contract value or contract appendix as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 55. Acts of making dossiers and documents improperly with regulations and acts of making dossiers and documents improperly compared with the original dossiers and documents at unit but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of signing contracts before having capital plans approved by competent authorities (except for construction works under emergency orders).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses not in compliance with regulations on title holders or beneficiaries;

  b) Preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses exceeding the prescribed limits in terms of quantity or value;

  c) Preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses against regulations (payment of expenses not meeting eligibility requirements or principles of payment regimes).

  In case an organization purchases or leases state assets in excess of the limits prescribed by competent authorities, it shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of Article 5 and Article 6 of this Decree.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury which are incorrect with the contents stated in the original documents and invoices at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of expenses incurred from preparation of documents and records against regulations and expenses paid for preparation of documents and records which are incorrect with the contents stated in the original documents and invoices at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 56. Acts of making fake documents and records for spending on state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for making fake documents and records sent to the State Treasury for payment of regular and professional expenses, regular programs, or programs using professional funding (excluding expenses for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrade, expansion of facilities from regular state budget expenditures and retained fees according to regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of at least VND 500.000.000).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for making fake documents and records sent to the State Treasury for payment of investment capital from state budget and investment capital from state budget for implementation of target programs or expenses for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrade, expansion of facilities from regular state budget expenditures and retained fees according to regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of at least VND 500.000.000.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of all amounts spent on using fake documents and records for spending on state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 57. Violations against regulations on payment of state budget expenditures

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for making documents and records requesting the State Treasury to transfer payments to wrong names or account numbers of beneficiary units specified in contracts or contract appendices.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for making documents and records requesting payment of state budget expenditures inconsistently with payment terms specified in contracts or contract appendices between the estimated unit or investor and suppliers of goods or services, including one of the following acts:

  a) Incorrect value of contract;

  b) Incorrect payment term;

  c) Incorrect method of payment;

  d) Incorrect payment rate (including advance payment);

  dd) Incorrect other payment terms specified in the contract and appendixes.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced completion of documents and invoices as prescribed for the acts specified in clause 1 and clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 58. Violations against procedures for control of expenditure commitments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit expenditure commitments to the State Treasury for expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  b) Submitting requests for expenditure commitments to the State Treasury beyond the prescribed deadline for expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  c) Submitting requests for expenditure commitments in excess of the annual investment capital plan, the balance of the approved annual estimate or the value of contracts for which commitments are permitted.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) The entity committing the violation in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to carry out procedures for expenditure commitment before requesting payment from the State Treasury;

  b) The entity committing the violation in Point c Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to carry out procedures for expenditure commitment again.

  Article 59. Violations against regulations on procedures and time limit for advance payment of state budget

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to carry out procedures for making advance payment after the deadline for making advance payment for regular expenditures without contracts on sale of goods or services or expenditures not required to send contracts on sale of goods or services to the State Treasury as prescribed;

  b) Failing to carry out procedures for making advance payment for investment capital expenditures from the sources of the State budget and investment capital from the State budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities from the regular budget of the State budget and retained charges under the prescribed regime for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500,000,000 through several payments of completed volume under contracts.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 4,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to carry out procedures for making full advance payment for regular expenditures with contracts on sale of goods or services under the prescribed regime in the final payment of the contract;

  b) Failing to carry out procedures for making full advance payment for investment capital expenditures from the sources of the State budget and investment capital from the State budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities from the regular budget of the State budget and retained charges under the prescribed regime for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500,000,000 when the value of payment (including advance payment and payment of completed volume) reaches 80% of the contract value;

  c) Failing to carry out or carrying out procedures for making advance payment for expenditures on compensation, support and resettlement after the prescribed time limit for making advance payment.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Compulsory advance payment shall be required for the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 60. Violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury

  Account freezing or account closing shall be required for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for making false documents to carry out procedures for registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury.

  2. Remedial measures:

  The violator is compelled to freeze the account in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  b/ Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 6, Article 4 of this Decree.

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 64. Power to impose penalties of Director General of State Treasury; Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and centrally-run cities

  1. Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and centrally-run cities have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Director General of State Treasury has the power to:

  a) A fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  b) Remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter VI

                                          IMPLEMENTATION CLAUSES [24]

  Article 65. Entry into force

  1. This Decree comes into force from September 01, 2019.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury and the Government’s Decree No. 58/2015/ND-CP dated June 16, 2015 on amendments to the Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  Article 66. Transition clauses

  For administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury committed before the effective date hereof but detected afterwards or being considered for handling, regulations on penalties hereof shall be applied if this Decree does not prescribe legal liability or impose lighter legal liability on violating individuals and organizations.

  Article 67. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Ministry of Finance shall provide guidelines for implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Governmental agencies, People’s Committees at all levels shall be responsible for providing guidelines for implementation and inspecting the imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury as prescribed herein.

  3. When detecting administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury, competent authorities of the State, organizations and individuals shall transfer documents (if any) or notify competent persons entitled to impose penalties for administrative violations as prescribed herein for consideration and handling.

  In case where administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property are subject to recovery of public property according to regulations of law on management and use of public property, competent persons entitled to impose penalties for administrative violations as prescribed herein shall notify the competent authority entitled to recover public property in accordance with regulations of law on management and use of public property after issuing decisions on imposition of penalties for administrative violations. The handling of recovered property shall comply with regulations of law on management and use of public property.

  4. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels and relevant organizations and individuals shall implement this Decree./.
