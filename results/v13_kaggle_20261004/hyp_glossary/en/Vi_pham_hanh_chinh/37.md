                                                  DECREE

     AMENDMENTS TO A NUMBER OF ARTICLES OF THE GOVERNMENT’S DECREE NO. 106/2025/ND-CP DATED MAY 15, 2025 ON SANCTIONS AGAINST ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELDS OF FIRE PREVENTION, FIRE FIGHTING AND INJURY TREATMENT AND WIDE RECOVERY

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization No. 63/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 18/2017/QH14, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 09/2022/QH15, Law No. 11/2022/QH15, Law No. 56/2024/QH15, and Law No. 88/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Fire Prevention, Fighting and Rescue No. 55/2024/QH15;

  At the request of the Minister of Public Security;

  The Government hereby promulgates a Decree amending and supplementing several articles of the Government’s Decree No. 106/2025/ND-CP dated May 15, 2025, prescribing penalties for administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention, fighting and rescue.

  Article 1. Amending and supplementing Point i Clause 2 Article 2 as follows:

  i) Organizations assigned to directly manage facilities and organizations operating within a facility in accordance with fire prevention, firefighting and rescue laws;".

  Article 2. Addition of Clauses 6, 7 and 8 after Clause 5 Article 3 as follows:

  1. Addition of Clause 6

  “6. Organizations and individuals committing multiple administrative violations of the same violation in one facility subject to fire prevention and firefighting management shall not be sanctioned for each administrative violation but only be sanctioned for one administrative violation with the aggravating circumstance applied.”.

  2. Addition of Clause 7

  “7. Organizations operating within a facility, organizations and individuals renting, borrowing or staying in houses shall be sanctioned for administrative violations prescribed herein corresponding to responsibilities of organizations operating within facilities, organizations and individuals renting, borrowing or staying in houses according to fire prevention, firefighting and rescue laws.”.

  3. Addition of Clause 8

  “8. The handling of administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting and rescue under this Decree in the electronic environment shall comply with Article 28a and Article 28b of the Government’s Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP dated December 23, 2021 providing detailed regulations on and measures for implementation of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations (amended by the Government’s Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP dated July 1, 2025).”.

  Article 3. Amendments to Clause 4 Article 5 as follows:

  “4. The time of termination of violations is determined as follows:

  a) The time of termination of violations for calculation of the prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations prescribed in Clause 3 and Clause 4 Article 6 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which competent authorities issue written notices of training and refresher training in fire prevention, firefighting, rescue and salvation as prescribed;

  b) The time of termination of violations for calculation of the prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 Article 18 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which specialized authorities issue written appraisal of fire prevention and firefighting design as prescribed;

  c) The time of termination of violations for calculation of the prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations prescribed in Clause 3 and Clause 4 Article 18 of this Decree shall be calculated from the date on which specialized authorities issue written approval of fire prevention and firefighting commissioning results as prescribed or terminate operation;

  d) The time of termination of violations for calculation of the prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations prescribed in Point a and Point b Clause 9 Article 20 of this Decree for works not subject to appraisal of fire prevention and firefighting design shall be calculated from the date on which project owners sign records of completion commissioning of work items or construction works for use as prescribed.”.

  Article 23. Implementation clauses

  1. This Decree comes into force as of April 20, 2026.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and cities, and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals shall implement this Decree.

  Article 24. Transitional provisions

  1. For administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention, firefighting and rescue committed before and completed before the effective date of this Decree but detected and the prescriptive period has not expired or are being considered and handled after the effective date of this Decree, the Government’s Decree on penalties for administrative violations effective at the time of commission of such violations shall apply.

  For administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention, firefighting and rescue committed before the effective date of this Decree but are still ongoing after the effective date of this Decree, this Decree shall apply.

  2. For decisions on imposition of administrative penalties already issued or completely executed before the effective date of this Decree, if the penalized organizations or individuals still lodge complaints, regulations on imposition of administrative penalties effective at the time of issuance of the decision on imposition of administrative penalties shall apply.
