                                                    DECREE

    REGULATIONS ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN SOCIAL SUPPORT AND CHILD CARE FIELD

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization No. 63/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 56/2024/QH15 and Law No. 88/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Children No. 102/2016/QH13 amended by Law No. 28/2018/QH14, Law No. 59/2024/QH15 and Law No. 81/2025/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Elderly Persons No. 39/2009/QH12 amended by Law No. 41/2024/QH15;

  Pursuant to the Law on Persons with Disabilities No. 51/2010/QH12 amended by Law No. 32/2013/QH13;

  Pursuant to the Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking No. 53/2024/QH15;

  At the request of the Minister of Health;

  The Government promulgates a Decree prescribing penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children.

  Chapter I

                                                GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations; penalties, fines and remedial measures applicable to each administrative violation; entities subject to penalties; authority to impose penalties, specific fines imposed by title and authority to make records of administrative violations; implementation of administrative penalties and remedial measures in social protection and children affairs.

  2. Administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children affairs not specified in this Decree shall be handled according to regulations on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children affairs.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations; foreign individuals and organizations committing administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children affairs within the territory, territorial waters, contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam; on aircraft bearing Vietnamese nationality or seagoing ships flying the flag of Vietnam, unless otherwise provided by international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory, including:

  a) Individuals committing administrative violations;

  b) Organizations committing administrative violations that are juridical persons as prescribed by civil law or other organizations established under law, including: types of social support facilities, facilities for care of the elderly, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, victim support facilities, child protection service providers; religious facilities; policy payment service providers; socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations; enterprises, cooperatives, production and business facilities; cultural, entertainment and sports facilities; medical examination and treatment facilities; education facilities; vocational education and training facilities and other organizations.

  2. Persons having authority to make records of administrative violations.

  3. Persons having authority to impose penalties for administrative violations.

  4. Agencies, organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of penalties for administrative violations as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 3. Prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations

  1. The prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children shall be 01 year.

  2. Determination of whether an administrative violation has ended or is being committed for calculation of the prescriptive period for imposing penalties for administrative violations shall comply with Clause 1 Article 8 of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP dated December 23, 2021 of the Government providing detailed regulations on and measures for implementation of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  Article 4. Enforcement of penalties for administrative violations

  1. The enforcement of decisions on imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children shall comply with the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations No. 15/2012/QH13 amended by Law No. 54/2014/QH13, Law No. 67/2020/QH14, Law No. 56/2024/QH15 and Law No. 88/2025/QH15 (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Handling of Administrative Violations”) and guiding documents.

  2. The imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children online shall comply with Chapter IIIa of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  3. During the process of considering and handling violations, during the implementation of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations for violations prescribed in clause 2 Article 8, Article 9, Article 11, clause 1 Article 12, point a clause 2 Article 19, clause 2 Article 20, clause 2 Article 22, clause 1 Article 23, clause 2 Article 24, clause 1 Article 25, clause 1 Article 29, clause 1 Article 33, clause 1 Article 36, points a and b clause 1 Article 37 of this Decree, if detecting signs of crimes in the violations, competent persons who are handling the cases or issuing decisions on sanctioning administrative violations shall immediately transfer the violation files to competent authorities conducting criminal proceedings for handling according to Article 62 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations. In case competent authorities conducting criminal proceedings do not conduct criminal prosecution, transfer the violation files to persons with power to impose administrative penalties according to clause 3 Article 62 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations for imposing administrative penalties according to regulations of this Decree.

  Article 5. Sanctioning forms, remedial measures; implementation of sanctioning forms and remedial measures

  1. Principal sanctioning forms:

  a) Warnings;

  b) Fines.

  2. Additional sanctioning forms:

  a) Confiscation of exhibits of administrative violations;

  b) Suspension of part or all of business activities related to the violations for 1 to 3 months;

  c) Suspend operation for a fixed period of 03 – 06 months.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) The remedial measure prescribed in Points a, dd, e and i Clause 1 Article 28 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  b) Mandatory cessation of care;

  c) Mandatory revocation, deletion or removal of information, images and documents of human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  d) Mandatory apology upon request;

  dd) Mandatory return of illegally collected amounts;

  e) Mandatory payment of all medical examination and treatment costs;

  g) Enforced repair, supplementation, and assurance of facilities and personnel as per regulation;

  h) Enforced payment of benefits and policies as per regulation;

  i) Enforced return of money or aid items obtained from committing violations;

  k) Enforced submission of confirmation of disability;

  l) Enforced fulfillment of responsibilities of councils or enforced implementation of methods for determining and re-determining the level of disability;

  m) Enforced determination and re-determination of the level of disability;

  n) Enforced payment of all costs of purchasing books, notebooks, and learning supplies for children;

  o) Enforced supplementation of adequate programs, textbooks, and teachers in conformity with vocational education for persons with disabilities;

  p) Enforced issuance of diplomas/certificates;

  q) Enforced recall and destruction of products, publications, toys, newspapers, and other printed materials;

  r) Enforced removal of news articles from online newspapers, magazines, radio, television, and electronic publications;

  s) Enforced termination of substitute care or transition to appropriate substitute care;

  t) Enforced relocation of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods or goods with fire/explosion risks, child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and entertainment venues for children;

  u) Enforced dismantling of structures and equipment;

  v) Enforced timely implementation of safety measures;

  x) Enforced removal of information and services harmful to children; removal of services and applications that are counterfeit, distorted, or infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of children on the internet;

  y) Enforced revocation, deletion or removal of information about private lives and personal secrets of children.

  4. The implementation of penalties and remedial measures prescribed in this Decree shall comply with Section 2 and Section 3 Chapter III of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations, Articles 17a, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23 and Article 41 of Decree No. 118/2021/ND-CP amended by Decree No. 68/2025/ND-CP and Decree No. 190/2025/ND-CP.

  Article 6. Fines and authority to impose fines

  1. The maximum fine for a violation against regulations on social protection and children for an individual is VND 50.000.000, and for an organization is VND 100.000.000.

  2. The fines prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree are imposed on administrative violations committed by individuals, except for Articles 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 34, Clause 1, Clause 2 Article 35 and Clause 2 Article 37 of this Decree. For the same administrative violation, the fine imposed on an organization shall be twice as much as the fine imposed on an individual.

  3. The authority to impose administrative penalties of a title prescribed in Articles 39, 40, 41, 42, 43, 44 and 45 of this Decree is the authority to impose penalties for administrative violations committed by individuals; in case of fines, the authority to impose administrative penalties on organizations shall be twice as much as the authority to impose penalties on individuals committing the same violation.

  Chapter II

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN SOCIAL SUPPORT AND ASSISTANCE FIELD

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on declaration of eligibility for social assistance, pension, and human trafficking victim support

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed upon an individual for committing fraudulent acts in order to receive emergency social assistance but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed upon an individual for committing fraudulent acts in order to receive one of the following support policies: education, vocational training, or initial hardship allowance but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Making false declarations in order to receive monthly social allowances but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Making false declarations in order to receive monthly pension but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Making false declarations in order to receive monthly allowance for care and nurture but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Making false declarations in order to be admitted for care and nurture at social assistance establishments, child protection service providers, elderly care facilities, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, or victim support establishments.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory return of profits illegally obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1, Clause 2, Points a, b, and c Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory cessation of care and nurture at social assistance establishments, child protection service providers, elderly care facilities, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, or victim support establishments, applicable to the violations prescribed in Point d Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on support for human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Obstructing the reception of human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  b) Obstructing the provision of support for human trafficking victims or persons undergoing victim identification;

  c) Insulting, stigmatizing, or discriminating against victims of human trafficking or persons undergoing victim identification.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for threatening victims of human trafficking, persons undergoing victim identification, their relatives, witnesses, informants, reporters, denouncers, or individuals preventing violations as prescribed in Article 3 of the Law on Prevention and Combat of Human Trafficking, if not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for disclosing information about the name, age, phone number, social media account, address, place of residence, workplace, native place, form of human trafficking, health condition, image, and personal documents of a victim of human trafficking or a person undergoing victim identification without their consent, if not liable to criminal prosecution, except where such disclosure is necessary to protect the life or health of the disclosed individual or where otherwise provided by law.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory revocation, deletion, or removal of information, images, and documents related to victims of human trafficking or persons undergoing victim identification, applicable to the violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory apology for the acts mentioned in Clauses 1, 2, and 3 of this Article, upon request, by the person responsible for caring for or raising the social protection beneficiary, elderly person, person with disability, or child living in the community.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on care and nurturing responsibilities of persons providing care for social protection beneficiaries, elderly persons, persons with disabilities, and children living in the community

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on a person providing care or nurturing for a social protection beneficiary who is living in the community but is not liable to criminal prosecution.

  a) Forcing hunger or thirst; forcible abstention from eating or drinking; forcible restriction of personal hygiene;

  b) Forcible living in a hazardous and dangerous environment;

  c) Insulting, mistreating, hurting, persecuting, discriminating against social protection beneficiaries, elderly persons, persons with disabilities and children.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on an individual who takes care of or nurtures another person but commits one of the following acts which, however, is not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) Taking advantage of the act of taking care of or nurturing another person for self-seeking purposes;

  b) Forcing the person taken care of or nurtured to work arduously, in a hazardous or dangerous environment.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed on an individual who takes care of or nurtures another person but commits one of the following acts which, however, is not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) Enticing or inciting the person taken care of or nurtured to commit illegal acts;

  b) Forcing recipients of care and nurture to commit violations.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Forced to return illegal benefits obtained from committing the violations specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Forced to pay all costs of medical examination and treatment for persons whose health is affected by the violations specified in Clause 1, Point b Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on operation of social assistance establishments, facilities for care of the elderly, facilities for care of persons with disabilities, victim support facilities, child protection service providers (hereinafter referred to as “Facilities”)

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed on Facilities failing to adequately store documents of persons receiving care and nurture as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed on Facilities committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit periodic reports on operation of the Facilities as prescribed by law;

  b) Impose a fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 for one of the following violations: failing to provide adequate or quality items for personal daily life including blankets, mosquito nets, mats, summer clothes, winter clothes, undergarments, towels, sandals, toothbrushes, common medicines; monthly hygiene items for female customers, books and study supplies for students and other items as prescribed by law.

  3. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed upon the establishment that commits one of the following violations:

  a) Using funding and facilities for wrong purposes;

  b) Collecting service charges against regulations of law;

  c) Failing to satisfy environmental, health, facility and personnel requirements;

  d) Failing to comply with procedures for provision of services issued by competent authorities.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing the violation in Point a Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of illegal collected amounts to beneficiaries for committing the violation in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;

  c) Enforced repair, supplementation, and assurance of physical and human resources as per the law for committing the violation in Point c Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on responsibilities of service providers

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Making payments of monthly social allowances, monthly care and nurturing expenses, monthly social pension, and funeral expenses (hereinafter referred to as “policy benefits”) which are insufficient for eligible beneficiaries as prescribed by law but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Making payments of policy benefits beyond the deadline for eligible beneficiaries as prescribed by law but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to make payments of policy benefits for eligible beneficiaries but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Payment of policies to wrong entities but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory payment of policies to the right entities for violations under Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on management of aid money and goods

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations but not to a degree that is subject to criminal prosecution:

  a) Damaging or losing aid money or goods, except force majeure events such as natural disasters or fires;

  b) Using or distributing aid money or goods for wrong purposes or to wrong entities;

  c) Swapping aid goods.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of the amount or compensation for the value of aid products that are damaged or lost due to the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced transfer of the illegal profit earned from committing the violations prescribed in Points b and c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by beneficiaries whose health is affected as a result of the violation prescribed in Point a or c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on prohibited acts against persons with disabilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Making a discriminatory remark or act against persons with disabilities;

  b) Obstructing the marriage or lawful child adoption rights of persons with disabilities;

  c) Obstructing persons with disabilities from living independently and integrating into the community;

  d) Obstructing persons with disabilities from participating equally in social activities;

  dd) Obstructing persons with disabilities from exercising their right to access information technology.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to fulfill responsibilities to nurture and care for persons with disabilities as prescribed by law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of the image, personal information, condition of persons with disabilities, organizations of persons with disabilities, or organizations for persons with disabilities for self-seeking purposes, or committing violations of the law but not to the extent of being liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for enticing, persuading, or forcing persons with disabilities to commit violations of the law but not to the extent of being liable to criminal prosecution.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory return of illegal profits obtained from the commission of the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced fulfillment of responsibilities to nurture and care for persons with disabilities in accordance with the law.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on health care responsibilities for persons with disabilities of medical examination and treatment establishments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to provide counseling on measures to prevent and detect disabilities early.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to identify congenital disabilities in newborns so as to promptly take appropriate treatment and rehabilitation measures.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to renovate or upgrade facilities serving medical examination and treatment to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities when renovating or repairing medical examination and treatment establishments.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on education responsibilities for persons with disabilities of education establishments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to ensure teaching and learning conditions suitable for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  b) Failing to provide learning support means and materials specifically for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  c) Failing to allow persons with disabilities to be exempted from or reduced the number of subjects or contents and educational activities that they cannot handle;

  d) Refusing to admit persons with disabilities at an older age than prescribed by law;

  dd) Imposing enrolment requirements that exclude persons with disabilities, except for certain cases as prescribed by law;

  e) Failing to give priority to enrolment for persons with disabilities in accordance with law;

  g) Obstructing the right to learn of persons with disabilities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed on an entity committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to provide exemption or reduction of tuition fees, training costs and other contributions as prescribed by law on education;

  b) Failing to renovate and upgrade teaching and learning facilities to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities when renovating and repairing educational institutions.

  Article 16. Violations against regulations on vocational education and training activities of vocational education and training institutions for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to provide employment counseling for persons with disabilities in accordance with law.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to maintain conditions for vocational education and training activities for persons with disabilities after organizing training activities for 6 months or more;

  b) Failing to provide adequate programs, textbooks or qualified teachers suitable for vocational education and training activities for persons with disabilities;

  c) Failing to ensure appropriate forms and duration of training suitable for vocational education and training activities for persons with disabilities;

  d) Failing to issue diplomas or certificates when persons with disabilities are eligible for issuance of diplomas or certificates as prescribed by law.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced provision of adequate vocational education and training programs, syllabi, and qualified teachers for persons with disabilities in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced issuance of degrees or certificates in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 17. Violations against regulations on traffic participation for persons with disabilities and older persons

  A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  1. The sale of tickets to persons with disabilities and the elderly shall not be prioritized.

  2. Persons who are responsible as per the law but fail to assist or arrange seats suitable for persons with disabilities and the elderly.

  3. Refusal to transport persons with disabilities or refuse to transport appropriate assistive devices and equipment for persons with disabilities by public means.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on information and communications for persons with disabilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed upon an enterprise manufacturing or distributing information and communication products, including hardware, software, and digital content, that violates one of the regulations on mandatory application of standards and technologies to support persons with disabilities in accessing and using information and communication products.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to apply for eligible beneficiaries of preferential policies on loans at preferential interest rates and other support for research, development, production, and provision of services and assistive devices for persons with disabilities to access information technology and communications.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the illegal profit earned from committing the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on determination of disability level

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to fully exercise the responsibilities of the Council for determination of disability level;

  b) Failing to fully apply methods for determining and re-determining the disability level.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Taking advantage of the determination of disability level for self-seeking purposes, if not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Refusing to determine or re-determine the disability level, issue a written confirmation of disability or reissue a written confirmation of disability;

  c) Committing dishonesty in determining or re-determining the disability level, issuing a written confirmation of disability or reissuing a written confirmation of disability.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory fulfillment of responsibilities of the Council for the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced full implementation of methods for determining and re-determining the level of disability in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Enforced transfer of illegal benefits obtained from the violation specified in Point a or Point c Clause 2 of this Article;

  d) Enforced determination and re-determination of the level of disability in case of commission of the violation specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article;

  dd) Enforced return of the certificate of disability in case of commission of the violation specified in Point c Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on rights and obligations of older persons

  1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Obstructing an older person who lives with their children or grandchildren or lives alone;

  b) Obstructing an older person from exercising their right to own property, participate in cultural, educational, physical training, sports, entertainment, tourism, rest activities, or other lawful rights;

  c) Failing to exempt contributions for social activities for older persons;

  d) Failing to prioritize older persons receiving money, aid, healthcare and accommodation in order to overcome initial difficulties when older persons face difficulties due to natural disasters or other force majeure events;

  dd) Failing to pay support amounts provided by the State for older persons as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations: Forcing older persons to abstain from eating or drinking; preventing or restricting personal hygiene; forcing older persons to live in a hazardous or dangerous environment or otherwise mistreating older persons, if not liable to criminal prosecution.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory apology upon request, applicable to the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory return of illegally collected amounts of money to older persons, applicable to the violation prescribed in Point c Clause 1 of this Article;

  c) Mandatory payment of policy benefits in accordance with regulations, applicable to the violation prescribed in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by older persons whose health is affected as a result of commission of the violation in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on care for older persons

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to perform the obligation to care for older persons as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to fully perform the commitment under the service contract signed with the elderly care provider to provide care for older persons as prescribed by law;

  c) Taking advantage of caring for older persons for personal gain but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Point c Clause 1 of this Article to state budget.

  Section 2. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON CHILDREN

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on care for children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) The parent or caregiver fails to fulfill their obligations and responsibilities in caring for the child, except where the child is placed under temporary separation or adopted as prescribed by law;

  b) The parent or caregiver fails to care for the child or fulfill their obligations and responsibilities in caring for the child or abandons the child to fend for themselves, breaks the emotional and material bond with the child, or forces the child not to live with the family, except where the child is placed under temporary separation or adopted as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed on a parent or caregiver who intentionally abandons a child but not to the extent of criminal liability.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on prohibition of violence against children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations but not to the extent of criminal liability:

  a) Forcing children to abstain from eating or drinking; preventing or restricting personal hygiene;

  b) Forcing children to live in a toxic or dangerous environment or subjected to other forms of ill-treatment;

  c) Causing mental damage, infringing upon dignity, honor, insulting, scolding, threatening, isolating children and affecting their development;

  d) Isolating, repelling or using punitive measures to teach children causing physical and mental damage to children;

  dd) Regularly threatening children with images, sounds, animals or objects, causing fear and mental damage to children.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Forced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article that cause injury or health damage to children but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Forced destruction of articles harmful to children’s health in case of violations specified in Point dd Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 24. Violations against regulations on child exploitation

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for forcing children to do housework beyond their capacity, beyond the prescribed time limit, affecting their study, entertainment and recreation or adversely affecting their development.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations which, however, are not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) Forcing children to beg;

  b) Leasing out, lending out or using children to beg;

  c) Leading, inciting, enticing, persuading, inducing, abusing or forcing children to act as intermediaries in child exploitation activities;

  d) Leading, inciting, enticing, persuading, inducing, abusing or forcing children to work against the law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of children’s personal images and information to create content that negatively affects their physical and mental development or for the purpose of making profits from such content but not seriously enough for criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of medical examination and treatment costs for children in case of committing violations prescribed in Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article that cause injury or damage to children’s health but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 25. Violations against regulations on prohibiting using, inciting, persuading, encouraging, abusing, enticing, forcing children to commit violations of law, hurting honor and dignity of other people

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations which is not liable to criminal prosecution:

  a) Using children to commit violations of law, hurting honor and dignity of other people;

  b) Inciting children to commit violations of law, hurting honor and dignity of other people;

  c) Encouraging or instigating children to commit violations of law, hurting honor and dignity of other people;

  d) Taking advantage of, enticing or deceiving children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of other persons;

  dd) Forcing children to commit acts of law violation, hurting the dignity and honor of other persons.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Forced payment of all expenses for medical examination and treatment for children in case of violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article which cause injury or damage to their health but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 26. Violations against regulations on right to health care of children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to ensure appropriate nutrition suitable for physical and mental development of children at each age group;

  b) Failing to perform or partially performing the initial health care, disease prevention, counseling and support for reproductive and sexual health of children.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to provide or obstructing pregnant women from accessing medical services in order to be counseled on screening and prevention of congenital diseases for children;

  b/ Failing to abide by decisions, measures and regulations of competent agencies or individuals to ensure the life safety and health of children;

  c/ Applying customs and practices which are harmful to children’s health;

  d/ Failing to prioritize medical examination and treatment for children.

  Article 27.- Violations of the regulation on the right to education of children

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Destroying books, notebooks and school supplies of children;

  b) Intentionally failing to fulfill obligations to contribute to children’s learning as prescribed by law;

  c) Failing to ensure time and conditions for learning for children.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for obstructing children’s attendance at school.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for inciting, enticing or forcing children to drop out of school.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory payment of all expenses for books, notebooks and learning materials for children in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 28. Violations against regulations on children’s right to access information and participate in social activities; right to express opinions; right to preserve and promote their identity; right to play and entertain themselves; prohibition of stigmatization and discrimination against children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Obstructing children from participating in social activities suitable for their age, maturity level, needs and capacity, except where such obstruction is in the best interests of the children;

  b) Obstructing children from expressing opinions, aspirations and participating in matters concerning children;

  c) Obstructing the right to preserve and promote identity;

  d) Obstructing the right to enjoy, relax, participate in cultural, artistic, physical training, sports and tourism activities as prescribed by law.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Forcing or repressing children when they express opinions, aspirations and participate in matters concerning children;

  b) Failing to respect, listen to, consider, respond to or explain opinions and aspirations of children;

  c) Failing to receive, process or respond to opinions, recommendations and aspirations of children, or failing to establish a representative body for the voices and aspirations of children as prescribed by law;

  d) Failing to disclose or to accurately disclose information on study and training plans, care regimes and contributions as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for stigmatizing or discriminating against children due to their personal characteristics, family circumstances, gender, ethnicity, nationality, belief or religion.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology when requested for violations prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced correction of false or misleading information in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 29. Violations against regulations on support and intervention for children in special circumstances, children who are victims of abuse or children at risk of exploitation, violence, abandonment, and other forms of abuse

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for any one of the following acts, which shall not be examined for penal liability:

  a) Failing to notify or provide information about children at risk of exploitation, violence and other forms of abuse to competent authorities or individuals;

  b) Obstructing the provision of information on children at risk of exploitation, violence and other forms of abuse to competent authorities or individuals.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to provide or concealing information on a child victim of abuse for competent authorities or individuals without facing criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to notify the place where information on child abuse is received;

  c) Obstructing the provision of information on a child victim of abuse to competent authorities or individuals;

  d) Failing to provide information and cooperate in verifying the authenticity of the abuse, the state of insecurity, and the extent of the risk of harm to the child when requested by competent authorities or individuals;

  dd) Failing to maintain confidentiality of information, or failing to report or denounce acts of child abuse.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to adequately and promptly provide support or intervention in cases where children are harmed or at risk of violence, exploitation, abandonment, or have special circumstances;

  b) Refusing or failing to provide support, intervention, or substitute care in cases where children are harmed or at risk of violence, exploitation, abandonment, or have special circumstances.

  Article 30. Violations against regulations on prohibiting sale to children or allowing children to use addictive substances, other stimulants, unsafe foods harmful to children; violations against regulations on providing safe and child-friendly products and services without criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Providing unsafe products or services that cause harm to children;

  b) Selling addictive substances, other stimulants, or unsafe foods harmful to children;

  c) Allowing children to use addictive substances, other stimulants, or unsafe foods harmful to children.

  2. Additional penalties:

  a) Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Suspend part or all of business activities related to the violations for 1 to 3 months for violations specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced destruction of goods and items for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs for children for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article that cause injury or damage to their health but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced return of an amount equal to the value of exhibits of administrative violations that have been sold, dispersed or destroyed against the law for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 31. Violations against regulations on provision of cultural products, information and communication services that affect healthy development of children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Providing internet services and other services with contents affecting healthy development of children;

  b) Failing to note that children are not allowed to use on publications, products, toys if publications, products, toys have contents inappropriate for children; failing to issue warnings about contents inappropriate for children in digital products on cyberspace;

  c) Failing to inform about age of children who are not allowed to watch, listen to radio programs, television programs, art, cinema and performances if there are contents inappropriate for children;

  d) Failing to comply with requirements for recording information on publications for children.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for one of the acts of producing, publishing, reproducing, circulating, operating, distributing, owning, transporting, storing, trading in publications, toys, games, digital contents on cyberspace and other products serving children but having contents affecting healthy development of children, by one of the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed when the violation involves 01 - 10 products;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed when the violation involves 10 - 50 products;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed when the violation involves 50 - 100 products;

  d) A fine of from VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 for committing the violation with between 100 and under 200 products;

  dd) A fine of from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 for committing the violation with between 200 and under 500 products;

  e) A fine of from VND 30,000,000 to VND 40,000,000 for committing the violation with between 500 and under 1,000 products;

  g) A fine of from VND 40,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 for committing the violation with ≥ 1,000 products.

  3. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations on warning contents inappropriate for children on programs of radio channels, television channels, press and publications.

  4. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits of administrative violations for violations specified in Points b and d Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced removal of news articles from online newspapers, magazines, radio and television, and electronic publications, for violations specified in Points b and d Clause 1 and Clause 3 of this Article;

  b) Enforced recall and destruction of products, publications, toys, printed newspapers, and other electronic publications, for violations specified in Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Enforced apology upon request, for violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  d) Enforced payment of an amount equal to the value of exhibits of administrative violations that have been unlawfully sold, liquidated, or destroyed, for violations specified in Points b and d Clause 1, Clause 2, and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 32. Violations against regulations on prohibiting disclosure of information about private life and personal secrets of children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for disclosing or publicizing any of the following information without consent of parents, caregivers, guardians, and children aged 7 years or older, including: name, age; personal identification characteristics; health conditions and private life recorded in medical records; personal images; family members, caregivers; personal property; phone number; personal postal address; address, information about place of residence, hometown; address, information about school, class, academic performance, and friends; information about services provided to children.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced apology upon request, for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced removal, deletion or removal of information on private life and personal secrets of children in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 33. Violations against regulations on prevention of abuse of child adoption for the purpose of harming children; abuse of benefits and policies provided by the State and assistance from organizations and individuals for the purpose of self-seeking

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations which, however, is not serious enough for criminal prosecution:

  a) Taking advantage of child adoption to cause physical, emotional, psychological, honorable or dignity-related harms to children in the forms of violence, exploitation, abandonment, neglect and other forms of harm;

  b) Taking advantage of benefits and policies provided by the State and assistance from organizations and individuals for the purpose of self-seeking.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) The entity committing the violation in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to pay all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by children if such violation causes bodily injury or health damage to children but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  b) The violating entity is compelled to return illegal gains obtained from the violation in Point b Clause 1 of this Article.

  c) Enforced termination of substitute care or change to appropriate substitute care for the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 34. Violations against regulations on construction of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods with fire and explosion risks near child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, entertainment venues of children or vice versa

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Building works or installing equipment of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods with fire and explosion risks near child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, entertainment venues of children;

  b) Building works or installing equipment of child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, entertainment venues of children near service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods with fire and explosion risks.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Building service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods with fire and explosion risks near child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, entertainment venues of children;

  b) Building child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, entertainment venues of children near service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods and goods with fire and explosion risks.

  3. Additional sanctions:

  Suspend operation for a fixed period of 03 to 06 months in case of commission of any violation specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of works and equipment in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced relocation of service facilities, manufacturing facilities, storage of hazardous goods or goods with fire and explosion risks, child protection service providers, educational institutions, medical facilities, cultural facilities, and entertainment venues due to incorrect placement as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 35. Violations against regulations on child protection service providers and management and use of infrastructure for children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on an infant care service provider operating beyond the registered scope of operation.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on an infant care service provider that operates before obtaining registration or fails to register for operation.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Illegally occupying infrastructure facilities for learning, entertainment, recreation and child protection services;

  b) Using infrastructure facilities for learning, entertainment, recreation and child protection services for wrongful purposes or against the law.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of works and equipment if the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 3 of this Article is committed;

  b) Enforced restoration of original condition if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 3 of this Article is committed.

  Article 36. Violations against regulations on failure to ensure safety leading to accidents or injuries to children

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to inform or warn children of dangerous, toxic, flammable, explosive, electric shock, fall, and other risks leading to accidents or injuries, but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to fully and promptly comply with safety measures for manufacturing, trading, and transporting means of transport leading to accidents or injuries to children, but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory prompt implementation of safety measures in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by children in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article that causes injury or damage to their health, but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 37. Violations against regulations on responsibility for protecting children online

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations while conducting business or providing services online:

  a) Providing or sharing information or services harmful to children, or information or services that are fake, toxic, pornographic, violent, suicidal, distorted, threatening, or infringe upon honor, dignity, or legitimate rights and interests of children, if not liable to criminal prosecution;

  b) Failing to issue warnings or remove information or services harmful to children, or information or services that are fake, toxic, pornographic, violent, suicidal incitement, distorted, threatening, or infringing upon the honor, dignity, or legitimate rights and interests of children, but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Failing to apply measures and tools to ensure the safety of information on private life secrets of children; failing to send warning messages about risks when children provide or change their private life secrets;

  d) Failing to comply with requests for removal or deletion of information on private life secrets of children when such requests are made by parents, caregivers, guardians, or competent agencies, organizations, or individuals responsible for child protection;

  dd) Failing to organize the receipt of information, assessment, and classification according to the safety level for children sent by agencies, organizations, individuals, and children;

  e) Failing to publish a list of online information networks, services, and products classified by safety level for children;

  g) Failing to detect and remove images, documents, or information inappropriate for children or affecting their healthy development;

  h) Failing to provide guidance on the use of information technology services and devices to protect children;

  i) Failing to implement measures to protect children who access information or use services.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on online video game service providers that fail to install tools for controlling time and protecting children from excessive or addictive use of video games. Online video game service providers that fail to apply measures to limit the playing time of G1 games for children shall face administrative penalties in accordance with the Government’s Decree on administrative penalties for violations against regulations on postal services, telecommunications, radio frequencies, information technology and electronic transactions.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced payment of all medical examination and treatment costs incurred by children in case of committing the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article that causes injury or damage to their health but not to the extent of criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced removal of information and services harmful to children; removal of counterfeit services and applications that infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of children on the internet (including data deletion, application removal from servers or application stores; domain name closure or other technical measures to prevent users from accessing them) in case of committing the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) Enforced deletion or removal of information on private lives of children in case of committing the violation prescribed in Point d Clause 1 of this Article.

  Chapter IV

                                                               IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 47. Entry into force

  1. This Decree comes into force as of May 16, 2026.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 130/2021/ND-CP dated December 30, 2021 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on social protection and children affairs shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  Article 48. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Minister of Health shall guide, inspect, expedite, and organize the implementation of this Decree.

  2. The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies, the heads of the Government-attached agencies, the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities as well as the concerned organizations and individuals shall have to implement this Decree.
