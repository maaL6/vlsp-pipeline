                                                   DECREE

     PROVISIONS ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY; IMPLEMENTATION OF EXPENDITURES AND ANTI-SUBSIDIES; NATIONAL RESERVES; STATE TREASURY

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015; and the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Law on Management and Use of Public Property dated June 21, 2017;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Thrift Practice and Anti-Wasteness dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Law on National Reserves dated November 20, 2012;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and anti-wasteness; national reserves; state treasury.

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, remedial measures, power to record administrative violations, and power to impose penalties for the following sectors:

  a) Management and use of public property at agencies, organizations, units; state capital projects; property under public ownership and infrastructure invested in and managed by the State;

  b) Thrift practice and wastefulness combat;

  c) National reserve;

  d) State treasury.

  2. Administrative violations related to management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury in terms of accounting, construction, investment planning, valuation, auction, and other regulations not specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be sanctioned according to relevant laws.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrift practice against wastefulness; national reserve; state treasury.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies;

  b) Units of the People's Armed Forces;

  c) Public non-business units;

  d) Agencies of the Communist Party of Vietnam;

  dd) Socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, and other organizations established under the law on associations;

  e) Other relevant enterprises, organizations, and individuals.

  3. Persons having power to impose penalties and organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree.

  4. If an official or public employee commits a violation while performing his/her official duties and such duty is included in his/her assigned official duties as prescribed in a legislative document or an administrative document issued by a competent authority or person, he/she shall not be subject to administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with regulations of law on officials, public employees.

  State agencies committing violations within their state management tasks shall not be subject to administrative penalties under this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws.

  Article 3. Prescriptive periods for sanctioning administrative violations

  The prescriptive period for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury prescribed in this Decree is 01 year. For administrative violations involving public property being houses, land and infrastructure assets invested and managed by the State, the prescriptive period shall be 02 years.

  Article 4. Forms of administrative penalties and remedial measures

  1. Main forms of sanction:

  a) The main forms of sanction prescribed in this Decree include: Warning and fine;

  b) The maximum fine for violations against regulations on management and use of public property, national reserves or state treasury shall be VND 50,000,000 if the violation is committed by an individual or VND 100,000,000 if the violation is committed by an organization; the maximum fine for violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat shall be VND 100,000,000 if the violation is committed by an individual or VND 200,000,000 if the violation is committed by an organization.

  2. Additional sanctions:

  Depending on nature and severity of violations, violating organizations and individuals may also be subject to additional sanctions such as confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit administrative violations.

  3. Remedial measures in the field of management and use of public property:

  Depending on the acts, nature and seriousness of violations, violating organizations or individuals shall be also applied one or several remedial measures such as:

  a) Enforced return of an amount equivalent to the value of the property;

  b) Enforced payment of amounts incurred from committing administrative violations;

  c) Compulsory return of property; in case the property has been changed due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original state; in case it cannot be restored to its original state, it must be compensated in cash or with other property having similar functions and use value to the original property;

  d) Enforced demolition of construction works built on encroached working offices or public service facilities;

  dd) Enforced cancellation of false or fraudulent declaration reports in case of violations against regulations on entry and use of data on public property;

  e) Enforced adjustment, supplementation of data, information, and additional declaration reports on public property.

  4. Remedial measures for violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness control:

  Depending on nature and severity of violations, violating organizations and individuals may be subject to one or several remedial measures, including:

  a) Revocation and transfer to state budget of funds used for purposes other than those prescribed if funds originated from state budget are used for purposes or guidelines other than those prescribed by competent authorities;

  b) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by using funds originating from state budget to state budget if funds originated from state budget are used inconsistently with operating regulations or financial mechanisms of funds issued by competent authorities.

  5. Remedial measures for violations against regulations on national reserves:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of violations, violating organizations or individuals may be subject to one or several remedial measures, such as:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal profits earned from committing violations against regulations on purchase, sale, preservation, distribution, and assistance; violations against regulations on import and export of national reserve goods; violations against regulations on management of capital and charges in national reserve activities; violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, and exploitation of assets included in national reserves;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state of facilities, physical and technical equipment, and storage of national reserve goods;

  c) Enforced return of damaged national reserve goods caused by acts of infringement or destruction; violations against regulations on distribution and assistance of national reserve goods;

  d) Enforced recall of national reserve goods issued or distributed inconsistently with regulations;

  dd) Enforced restoration of the original state of assets if the violation involves illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, or exploitation of assets that are storage facilities for national reserve goods.

  6. Remedial measures in the field of state treasury:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of violations, violating organizations may be subject to one or several remedial measures, such as:

  a) Enforced revocation of amounts paid for the unfinished work; unfinished work not eligible for payment and overpaid amounts in excess of the contract value or contract appendix;

  b) Enforced revocation of amounts paid for improper records and documents and improper payments made from improper records and documents compared to the original records and documents at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Enforced revocation of all amounts paid for the use of fake records and documents for state budget payment but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Enforced completion of records and documents as per the law in case of violations against regulations on payment of state budget expenditures;

  dd) Enforced commitment to advance payment before requesting the State Treasury to pay the state budget or enforced re-commitment to advance payment in case of violations against procedures for controlling commitment payment;

  e) Enforced procedures for advance payment in case of violations against procedures and time limit for advance payment of state budget;

  g) Enforced freezing or closing accounts in case of violations against procedures for registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury; enforced freezing of accounts in case of making fake records and documents for procedures for registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  7. Organizations and individuals subject to administrative penalties under this Decree are not allowed to use state budget money or money originating from the state budget to pay fines and remedial measures for their violations. In case of imposing penalties on organizations, after complying with penalty decisions, the sanctioned organizations shall determine individuals at fault for administrative violations to determine legal responsibilities, including the return of fines and remedial measures for consequences caused by their violations corresponding to the degree of violation of such individuals according to the provisions of law and Regulations on management and use of public property of the organizations.

  Article 5. Application of fines

  The fine levels prescribed in Items 1, 2, 3 and 4 of Chapter II, Item 1 of Chapter III, Item 1 of Chapter IV, Items 1 and 2 of Chapter V of this Decree are the fine levels prescribed for administrative violations committed by organizations; for the same administrative violation, the fine level imposed on an individual shall be half of that imposed on an organization (except for the provisions of Articles 17, 23 and 27 of this Decree).

  Chapter II

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS AND FINES IN MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY AT AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND EMPLOYEES

  Article 6. Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets without a decision of a competent authority or person:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if investing in or purchasing assets being machinery, equipment, or other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a total purchase value of less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing in or purchasing assets that are machinery, equipment, or other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and automobiles) with a total purchase value of ≥ VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed in case of investing in or purchasing assets that are office buildings, public service facilities, or automobiles.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to implement centralized procurement of assets on the list of assets subject to centralized procurement as prescribed by laws on management and use of public property.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets beyond the limits prescribed by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets exceeds by less than 50.000.000 for each asset unit;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets exceeds by from 50.000.000 to less than 100.000.000 for each asset unit;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets exceeds by from 100.000.000 for each asset unit or more.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced payment of an amount equivalent to the value of assets invested or purchased in excess of the prescribed standards and norms for the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the value exceeding the prescribed standards and norms as a basis for sanctioning is prescribed as follows:

  a) In case of investing or purchasing assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment and other assets in excess of the prescribed quantity: The excess value per unit of asset is determined by the unit price of such asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or contract for asset purchase;

  b) In case of investing or purchasing assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment and other assets in excess of the prescribed quantity but at a price higher than the prescribed price: The excess value is determined by the value of the asset according to the purchase price stated on the invoice or contract for asset purchase minus (-) the value of the asset according to the prescribed standards and norms stipulated by competent state agencies;

  c) In case of investing or purchasing working offices or public service facilities in excess of the prescribed standards and norms: The excess value is determined by the difference between the actual area minus (-) the area used according to the prescribed standards and norms multiplied by (x) the average purchase unit price according to the invoice (or contract for asset purchase);

  d) Persons with sanctioning competence specified in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as a basis for sanctioning according to the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 7. Administrative violations against regulations on leasing assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on leasing assets if the value of the asset lease contract is less than VND 100.000.000, for the following acts:

  a) The act of leasing property without a decision on property lease issued by a competent authority or person;

  b) The act of leasing property in excess of the standards and norms prescribed by a competent state authority;

  c) The act of selecting property lease service providers in a manner that does not comply with the procedures prescribed by laws on management and use of public property and relevant laws.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on property lease as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article if the value of the property lease contract is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory return of the amount of rent in excess of the standards and norms prescribed in the case specified at Point b Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The value of the property lease contract serving as the basis for imposing penalties shall be determined as follows:

  a) In cases where the value of the property lease contract is specified in the contract, the basis for imposing penalties shall be the value specified in the contract;

  b) In case of leasing property without entering into a contract or with insufficient information for determining value as prescribed in Clause a of this Point, the value used as the basis for imposing penalties shall be determined by the lease price of property of the same type or with equivalent standards and technical specifications on the market at the time of committing the violation multiplied by (x) the duration of leasing property from the beginning of leasing to the time of issuing the decision on administrative penalties.

  The person having the power to impose penalties specified in Article 29 and Article 30 of this Decree shall determine the value used as the basis for imposing penalties according to regulations of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 8. Administrative violations against regulations on allocation and use of public property, destruction or intentional damage to public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of allocating public property beyond the standards and norms issued by competent agencies or persons (incorrect subjects, area, quantity, or price), subject to the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the value of allocated and used property is less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the value of allocated and used property is from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case the value of allocated and used property is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for the act of allocating and using public property for wrongful purposes (using public property for wrongful purposes or not in accordance with the intended uses of invested, equipped, or purchased property; using working offices or public service facilities as residential houses or for personal purposes; using cars to transport from residence to workplace for unqualified positions; using cars for business trips for unqualified positions without approval by competent authorities; allocating and using cars for personal purposes; using machinery, equipment, or other property for personal purposes), subject to the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of assignment or use of assets being machinery, equipment and other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles) with a book value of less than VND 100.000.000 per asset unit (hereinafter referred to as “assets valued at less than VND 100.000.000”);

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of assignment or use of assets being machinery, equipment and other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles) with a book value of VND 100.000.000 or more per asset unit (hereinafter referred to as “assets valued at VND 100.000.000 or more”);

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of assignment or use of assets being office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for destroying or intentionally damaging public property if not liable to criminal prosecution:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging assets being machinery, equipment and other assets with a book value of less than VND 100.000.000 per asset unit;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging assets being machinery, equipment and other assets with a book value of VND 100.000.000 or more per asset unit;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging assets being office buildings, public service facilities and automobiles.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of an amount equivalent to the value of assets in excess of the prescribed norms and limits for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the initial state of assets altered due to administrative violations; in case of failure to restore the initial state of assets, they must be returned in cash or assets with similar functions and use value to the initial ones for the acts specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. The determination of the value exceeding the prescribed norms and limits is as follows:

  a) In case of delivery and use of assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment, and other assets in excess of the prescribed norms and limits: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the number of excess assets by the original cost of assets recorded in the accounting books; in case the original cost of assets is not recorded in the accounting books, it shall be determined according to the purchase price of new assets of the same type or those meeting technical standards and having similar uses on the market at the time of violation;

  b) In case of delivery and use of assets such as automobiles, machinery, equipment, and other assets in excess of the prescribed norms and limits in terms of quantity but exceeding the prescribed prices: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the number of excess assets by (the difference between the original cost of assets recorded in the accounting books minus (-) the value of assets according to the prescribed norms and limits prescribed by competent regulatory authorities);

  c) In case of assets being working offices or public service facilities: The excess value shall be determined by subtracting (-) the area used according to the prescribed norms and limits from the actual area recorded in the accounting books; in case the area is not recorded in the accounting books, the unit price shall be calculated according to the investment rates of houses and works with similar technical standards issued by the Ministry of Construction applicable at the time of violation;

  d) Persons having sanctioning competence prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as a basis for sanctioning in accordance with the law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 9. Administrative violations against regulations on lending of public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for lending or using public property in contravention of regulations (lending public property to other organizations or individuals regardless of whether there is a loan contract or not, regardless of the loan term):

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the lent property is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the lent property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed if the lent property is a working office, public service facility, or car.

  2. Remedial measures:

  The organization committing the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of lent property. In case the lent property has been changed due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original condition; in case it cannot be restored to its original condition, it must be returned in cash or property with similar functions and use value to the original property;

  b) Enforced transfer of an amount equivalent to the rent of property during the loan period to the state budget. The determination of the rent of property as a basis for penalty imposition shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 10. Administrative violations against regulations on public property exchange and gifting

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of exchanging public property in contravention of regulations (using public property of an organization to exchange for property of another organization or individual without permission from a competent authority or person) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed where the property used for exchange is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed where the property used for exchange is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed where the property used for exchange is a working office, public service facility, or car.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of gifting public property in contravention of regulations (using public property as gifts in violation of Regulations on gift gifting promulgated by a competent authority).

  3. Remedial measures:

  Mandatory return of property or monetary compensation corresponding to the value of property exchanged or gifted in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 11. Administrative violations against regulations on encroaching on working offices or public service facilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for encroaching on a working office or public service facility (an organization/individual that is using adjacent land and relocates the boundary of the land parcel to expand the land area or builds an encroaching work within the land area of the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit; or uses the house or land belonging to the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit without permission).

  2. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  The violator that commits the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also take the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original condition of the working office or public service facility and return of the encroached working office or public service facility;

  b) Enforced demolition of the work built on the encroached area;

  c) Compulsory payment to the state budget of an amount equivalent to the rent for property during the period of encroachment. The determination of the rent for property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4, Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 12. Acts of appropriating public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed on acts of appropriating public property which are not serious enough to be examined for penal liability (possession or use of public property without permission of competent state agencies) according to the following fine levels:

  a/ Between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 for public property valued at less than VND 100,000,000;

  b/ Between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for public property valued at VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c/ Between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 in cases of appropriation of working offices, non-business operation establishments or automobiles.

  2. Additional sanctions: Confiscation of exhibits and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures: Organizations and individuals that commit violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original condition of property altered due to administrative violations in order to return it to its original state; in case of failure to restore the original condition of property, monetary compensation or property with similar functions and utility to the original property shall be provided;

  b) Enforced transfer of an amount equivalent to the rent of property during the period of expropriation to the state budget. The determination of the amount of rent as a basis for imposing penalties shall comply with Clause 4 Article 7 hereof.

  Article 13. Administrative violations against regulations on using public property for business, lease, joint venture, or association purposes

  1. A fine shall be imposed for using public property for business, lease, joint venture, or association purposes without a decision approving the Scheme issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with laws on management and use of public property at the time of commission of the violation, and imposed in the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of the property is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of the property is VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 if the property is a working office, public service facility, or car.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on determining the value of property used for business, lease, joint venture, or association purposes, and imposed in the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for establishing a valuation council or property valuation council with improper composition;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for selecting a property valuation organization to determine the value of property used for business, lease, joint venture or association purposes in contravention of the prescribed procedures.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business, lease, joint venture or association purposes in a manner inconsistent with the scheme approved by a competent authority or person, thereby affecting the performance of functions and tasks assigned by the State:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the property is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the property is a car or the property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 if the property is an office building, a public service facility or a car.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of the property altered by the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, the offender shall pay an amount of money or return property with similar functions and use value to the original property;

  b) Enforced return of illegal benefits obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Administrative violations against regulations on failure to maintain or repair property as prescribed by law

  1. Organizations assigned by competent authorities with estimates for maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law but failing to maintain or repair property resulting in damage shall be sanctioned as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of damaged property is less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of damaged property is between VND 50.000.000 and under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of damaged property is VND 100.000.000 or more and the property is a working office, public service facility or car and not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Determination of the amount of money to be returned corresponding to the value of damaged property:

  a) For irreparable damage, the amount of money to be returned shall be determined by multiplying the purchase price of a new property of the same type or standard and with similar use by the remaining quality percentage at the time of valuation;

  b) For damaged assets that can be repaired, the amount to be returned is the repair cost;

  c) The head of the organization assigns the financial agency under their management to act as the focal point for determining the amount to be returned; based on this determination, the head of the organization decides the amount to be returned and shall be responsible for such decision.

  Article 15. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare or prepare a plan for disposition of public property in accordance with law;

  b) Declaring or preparing a plan for disposition of public property beyond the prescribed time limit;

  c) Failing to report to a competent authority for revocation, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of property when conditions are met in accordance with law (except where public property has expired in accordance with law but remains usable and an agency, organization, unit or enterprise still has a need to use it, in which case the head of the agency, organization, unit shall decide on an appropriate disposal method).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on an organization for damaging or losing property while awaiting disposal.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for handling assets without a decision from a competent authority or person:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the assets are sold, transferred, liquidated or destroyed and are worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the assets are sold, transferred, liquidated or destroyed and are worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the assets are sold, transferred, liquidated or destroyed and are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. The following penalties shall be imposed for providing false information about the list of assets to be handled or their current status (leading to improper decisions on asset disposal):

  a) A warning shall be imposed if the assets are worth less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the assets are worth VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the assets are worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for declaring as assets working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) The violator is compelled to restore the original condition of the asset altered by the violation prescribed in Clause 2 or Clause 3 of this Article; in case of impossibility of restoring the original condition of the asset, the violator is liable to return money or an asset with similar functions and use value to the initial one;

  b) Enforced payment of an amount corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged asset in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  6. Determination of the amount payable corresponding to the value of the lost or damaged asset specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article:

  a) In case the lost or damaged asset cannot be repaired, the amount payable shall be determined according to the purchase price of a new asset of the same type or standard with similar use function on the market at the time when the violation is committed.

  The market price may be based on the quoted prices of suppliers on the market which have been posted or announced on the market or from the official information disclosed by suppliers available via the Internet.

  b) In case the damaged asset can be repaired, the amount payable shall be the repair cost of such asset.

  Article 16. Administrative violations against regulations on organization of handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to implement the disposal plan approved by a competent authority or person after the prescribed time limit.

  The time limit for determining the expiration of the implementation of the disposal plan is the disposal time specified in the decision on disposal of property. If the disposal time is not specified in the decision on disposal of property, the relevant provisions of law shall apply. In case the disposal plan is approved by a competent authority to extend the disposal time, the prescribed time limit shall be determined according to the extended time limit.

  b) Failure to transfer property subject to a decision on transfer or recovery to the receiving agency within the prescribed time limit.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to establish a valuation council in accordance with regulations of law;

  b) Failing to make public announcements of the auction of public property in accordance with Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. A fine shall be imposed for failing to carry out price appraisal as a basis for determining the reserve price when handling property that is a working office, a public service facility, or other property affixed to land through sale; failing to conduct an auction of property in cases where such auction is required by law:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of the property is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of the property is VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) VND 10,000,000 - VND 20,000,000 if the assets are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  Article 17. Administrative violations against regulations on public property data entry and use

  Fines imposed on organizations and individuals committing violations against regulations on public property data entry and use shall be as follows:

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Prepare declaration reports on assets for uploading data to the National Public Asset Database in contravention of legal documents and inconsistent with the current status of assets of the unit;

  b) Implement data input decentralization for subordinate units without written agreement from the Ministry of Finance;

  c) Input and approve data on public property inconsistently with declared reports of organizations assigned to manage and use the property.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Erasing, deleting or modifying property declaration reports in a manner that falsifies data on public property in comparison with the actual state of property;

  c) Exploiting information on public property in the National database for public property of agencies, organizations and units not under their management;

  d) Using data on public property in the National database for public property for personal purposes without permission from the agency competent to manage such database (using information stored in the database for purposes other than those prescribed by laws on management and exploitation of the software for management of registration of public property without permission from the agency competent to manage such database).

  3. Remedial measures

  a) Enforced cancellation of false or fraudulent declaration reports;

  b) Enforced correction of data to match the actual state of property of the unit;

  c) Enforced preparation of additional declaration reports that have not been prepared when there is a change in property operations;

  d) Enforced correction of false or misleading information;

  dd) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing administrative violations.

  Mandatory return of the proceeds obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  b) Using funds originating from the state budget inconsistently with operating regulations and financial mechanisms of the fund promulgated by competent authorities.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with limits, unit prices, standards and regimes promulgated by competent authorities when procuring, managing and using fixed assets, supplies and other assets in a single-member limited liability company (SMLLC).

  4. A fine ranging from VND 70.000.000 to VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with limits, unit prices, standards and regimes promulgated by competent authorities when managing and using expenses in a single-member limited liability company (SMLLC).

  Chapter IV

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINE RATES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON NATIONAL RESERVES

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON NATIONAL RESERVES, FINE RATES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON NATIONAL RESERVES

  Article 40. Violations against regulations on purchase and sale of national reserves

A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to notify or publicly post information about entities engaging in purchase and sale activities; methods of purchase and sale; purchase and sale prices; purchase and sale duration; quantity, categories, specifications, standards and quality of national reserves in accordance with regulations of competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the following violations:

  Failing to comply with regulations issued by competent authorities on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods.

  a) Failing to purchase national reserve goods when all conditions have been met under decisions of competent state agencies;

  b) Trading in national reserve goods without written consent of competent state agencies;

  c) To buy or sell national reserve goods when the time limit for buying or selling national reserve goods has expired;

  d) Trading in national reserve goods not in conformity with quantity, category, specifications, standards and quality approved by competent authorities;

  dd) Failing to comply with regulations issued by competent authorities on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for arbitrarily changing the prices of national reserve goods for self-seeking purposes.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article to state budget.

  Article 41. Violations against regulations on management of national reserve goods

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to open books for monitoring quality, quantity and categories of national reserve goods during storage period;

  b) Failing to submit periodic or ad hoc reports on storage of national reserve goods.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for the violation involving the transfer of national reserve goods:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent authorities on transfer of national reserve goods;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent authorities on transfer of national reserve goods.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recall of transferred national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  Article 42. Violations against regulations on preservation of national reserve goods

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for failure to comply with national technical regulations or provisional technical requirements issued by competent authorities on preservation of national reserve goods; failure to comply with regulations on time limit for preservation of national reserve goods; or failure to preserve national reserve goods at locations specified by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 if damage is caused and the value of the damaged national reserve goods is less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of the warehousing, preservation and circulation of national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for hiring an organization/individual ineligible to preserve national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100.000.000.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 through 3 of this Article to state budget.

  Article 43. Acts of infringing upon or destroying material-technical facilities and/or storage of national reserves which are not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for illegally infringing upon material-technical facilities and/or storage of national reserves.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for destroying material-technical facilities and/or storage of national reserves.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for acts of infringing upon or destroying national reserves:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused to national reserves;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if less than VND 50,000,000 is damaged to national reserves;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000 is damaged to national reserves.

  4. Additional sanctions

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory restoration of the original state of material and technical facilities and storage of national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed;

  b) Compulsory return of damaged national reserve goods if the violation prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article is committed.

  Article 44. Violations against regulations on provision and assistance of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of provision or assistance of national reserve goods or performance of other tasks related to national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000.

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out procedures for distribution and provision of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 within the prescribed time limit.

  3. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for using national reserve goods for wrong purposes; distributing or providing national reserve goods to wrong entities according to decisions of competent authorities; failing to ensure the type, quantity, quality, standards and specifications of national reserve goods in accordance with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for loss of national reserve goods used for distribution, provision or performance of other tasks:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the lost national reserve goods are valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 if the value of the national reserve goods is from VND 50,000,000 to under VND 100,000,000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for distributing national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 to ineligible entities according to a competent authority’s decision.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for delayed or untimely provision of national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100.000.000 in accordance with regulations of competent authorities.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for delaying or procrastinating the distribution of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 under decisions of competent authorities, thus causing damage to national reserve goods.

  8. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clauses 3, 5 and 6 of this Article;

  b) Enforced return of lost national reserve goods, or goods used for wrong purposes or issued to wrong entities, in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  c) Enforced return of damaged, inferior or devalued national reserve goods, or goods with insufficient quantity, in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 45. Violations against regulations on importation and exportation of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of importation, exportation or transportation of national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused but the value of the damage is less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused but the value of the damage is from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to implement or improperly implementing procedures for import and export of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 as prescribed;

  b) Delivery of goods not in conformity with quantity, type, specifications, standards and quality of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 according to decisions of competent authorities.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 not in conformity with the type, specifications, quantity, quality, time and place specified in the decision of a competent authority.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 without a decision of a competent authority.

  5. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing administrative violations prescribed in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;

  b) Enforced recall of national reserve goods that have been issued out against regulations for violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 46. Violations against regulations on receipt of national reserve goods but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Receiving national reserve goods not at the time and place specified in the decision of competent authority;

  b) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on procedures and order for receiving national reserve goods.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to receive national reserve goods (national reserve goods issued for aid or other tasks of competent authorities) when all conditions prescribed by competent authorities are satisfied.

  Article 47. Illegal intervention in national reserve activities

  1. Warnings or fines shall be imposed for illegal intervention in national reserve activities as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000.

  2. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 48. Violations against regulations on management of capital and charges in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for failure to report the use of capital from national reserve funds for purchase of national reserve goods as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Payment for goods in stock without a sale contract;

  b) Payment for goods in stock not in quantity or with insufficient invoices and documents;

  c) Payment for goods in stock without a record of contract liquidation or a list of documents on receipt of goods in stock bearing the signature of the unit’s head for cases of direct purchase without bidding;

  d) Payment for goods in stock without approval by the head of the national reserve unit;

  dd) Using fees for receipt, dispatch and preservation of goods in stock inconsistently with the prescribed contents or exceeding the prescribed limits.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Using national reserve funds for purposes other than those prescribed;

  b) Failing to comply with regulations on management of funds derived from national reserve funds or improperly complying with such regulations.

  c) Failure to remit the remaining amount after completing the purchase of national reserve goods in accordance with regulations of competent state agencies.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remittance of illegal profits earned from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 49. Violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets included in national reserves

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets included in national reserves (except for storage of national reserve goods):

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of the assets is less than VND 70.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of the assets is from VND 70.000.000 to less than VND 150.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 if the value of the assets is from VND 150.000.000 to less than VND 300.000.000;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth from VND 300.000.000 to under VND 500.000.000;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth VND 500.000.000 or more.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for trading, pledging, mortgaging, leasing or illegally exploiting the national reserve storehouse:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the national reserve storehouse is less than VND 500.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the national reserve storehouse is VND 500.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory restoration of the original condition of the property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory disgorgement of illegal gains from commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. POWERS TO MAKE RECORDS AND IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF NATIONAL RESERVATION

  Article 50. Authority to make records of administrative violations against regulations on national reserves

  1. Persons having power to make records of administrative violations in the field of national reserves include:

  a) Persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on national reserves prescribed in Articles 51, 52 and 53 hereof;

  b) Officials assigned to inspect compliance with laws on national reserves.

  2. Persons having the power to issue offense notice specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be responsible for issuing sanction decisions within their competence or transferring to competent authorities specified in Articles 51, 52 and 53 of this Decree to issue sanction decisions.

  Article 51. Power to impose penalties of Chairpersons of People's Committees

  1. Chairpersons of district-level People's Committees have the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 50.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrument of administrative violation whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b this Clause;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chairpersons of provincial People's Committees have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrument of administrative violations;

  d) Remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 52. Power to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Inspectors and persons assigned to conduct specialized inspections of national reserves while on duty have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause.

  2. Chief Inspectors of provincial Departments, heads of provincial inspectorate teams performing specialized inspections of national reserves, heads of specialized inspection teams established by the Director General of State Reserves shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 50.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Enforce the remedial measures mentioned in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of ministerial-level specialized inspectorates performing the function of specialized inspection in the field of national reserves shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed the fine specified in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Enforce the remedial measures mentioned in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. Chief Inspector of a Ministry has the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Enforce the remedial measures specified in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 53. Power to impose penalties of Directors of Regional State Reserves Department, Director General of State Reserves Administration

  1. Directors of Regional State Reserves Departments have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrument of administrative violation whose value does not exceed the fine specified in point b this clause;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Director General of State Reserves shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrument of administrative violation;

  d) Take remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter V

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF STATE BUDGET

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE BUDGET SPENDING THAT MUST BE INCLUDED IN THE STATE BUDGET ESTIMATES APPROVED BY COMPETENT AUTHORITIES

  Article 54. Violations against regulations on state budget spending that must be included in the state budget estimates approved by competent authorities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for contents or tasks not included in the estimates approved by competent authorities;

  b) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for expenditures exceeding the annual funding estimates or plans assigned by competent authorities or exceeding the estimated expenditures approved (for tasks not carried out under contracts);

  c) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment for expenditures not in accordance with the estimates assigned by competent authorities (expenditures not in accordance with the estimated funding sources; expenditures not for the purposes or beneficiaries or contents as defined in the estimates); expenditures not in accordance with investment funding sources, expenditures not in accordance with the list of projects approved by competent authorities.

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 6,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Making dossiers and documents to send to the State Treasury for payment for the workload not yet performed;

  b) Making dossiers and documents to send to the State Treasury for payment for the workload being performed incompletely or not eligible for payment;

  c) Making dossiers and documents to send to the State Treasury for payment in excess of contract value or contract appendix.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Forced revocation of amounts already paid for the workload not yet performed; the workload being performed incompletely or not eligible for payment and the part already paid in excess of contract value or contract appendix as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 55. Acts of making dossiers and documents improperly with regulations and acts of making dossiers and documents improperly compared with the original dossiers and documents at unit but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of signing contracts before having capital plans approved by competent authorities (except for construction works under emergency orders).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses not in compliance with regulations on title holders or beneficiaries;

  b) Preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses exceeding the prescribed limits in terms of quantity or value;

  c) Preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury for payment of expenses against regulations (payment of expenses not meeting eligibility requirements or principles of payment regimes).

  In case an organization purchases or leases state assets in excess of the limits prescribed by competent authorities, it shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of Article 5 and Article 6 of this Decree.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records for submission to the State Treasury which are incorrect with the contents stated in the original documents and invoices at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of expenses incurred from preparation of documents and records against regulations and expenses paid for preparation of documents and records which are incorrect with the contents stated in the original documents and invoices at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 56. Acts of making fake dossiers or documents for spending the state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for making fake dossiers or documents and sending them to the State Treasury for payment of regular expenditures, recurrent expenditures, expenditures on national target programs or programmed target programs using recurrent expenditures (excluding expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities funded by recurrent expenditures of the state budget and retained fees according to regulations for recurrent expenditures with total investment of over VND 500.000.000).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for making fake dossiers or documents and sending them to the State Treasury for payment of investment capital funded by the state budget and investment capital funded by the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities funded by recurrent expenditures of the state budget and retained fees according to regulations for recurrent expenditures with total investment of over VND 500.000.000.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recall of all expenditures incurred from the use of fake dossiers or documents for spending the state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 57. Violations against regulations on payment of state budget expenditures

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for making dossiers or documents requesting the State Treasury to transfer payments to wrong names or account numbers of beneficiary units specified in contracts or contract appendices.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for making dossiers or documents requesting payment of state budget expenditures inconsistently with payment terms specified in contracts or contract appendices between the unit in charge of cost estimation or investor and suppliers of goods or services, including one of the following acts:

  a) Incorrect value of contract;

  b) Incorrect payment term;

  c) Incorrect method of payment;

  d) Incorrect payment rate (including advance payment);

  dd) Incorrect other payment terms specified in the contract and appendixes.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced completion of documents and invoices as prescribed for the acts specified in clause 1 and clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 58. Violations against procedures for control of expenditure commitments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit commitment of state budget expenditures to the State Treasury in respect of expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  b) Submitting request for commitment of state budget expenditures to the State Treasury beyond the prescribed deadline in respect of expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  c) Submitting request for commitment of expenditures exceeding the annual investment capital plan, the balance of the approved annual estimate or the value of contracts on which commitments are allowed.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory submission of procedures for commitment of expenditures before requesting payment by the State Treasury in respect of the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory re-filling of procedures for commitment of expenditures in respect of the violation prescribed in Point c Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 59. Violations against procedures and time limit for advance payment of state budget

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to carry out procedures for making advance payment after the deadline for making advance payment on regular expenditures without a contract for sale of goods or services or expenditures not required to submit contracts for sale of goods or services to the State Treasury as prescribed;

  b) Failing to carry out procedures for making advance payment on investment capital expenditures funded by the state budget and investment capital expenditures funded by the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities using regular expenditures from the state budget and retained fees in accordance with regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500.000.000 through multiple payments of completed volume under contracts.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to carry out procedures for making full advance payment on regular expenditures with contracts for sale of goods or services in accordance with regulations in the final payment of the contract;

  b) Failing to carry out procedures for making full advance payment on investment capital expenditures funded by the state budget and investment capital expenditures funded by the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities using regular expenditures from the state budget and retained fees in accordance with regulations for regular expenditures with total investment of over VND 500.000.000 when the value of the payment (including advance payment and payment of completed volume) reaches 80% of the contract value;

  c) Failing to carry out or carrying out procedures for making advance payment on expenditures on compensation, support and resettlement after the deadline for making advance payment.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Compulsory advance payment shall be required for the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON REGISTRATION AND USE OF ACCOUNTS AT THE STATE TREASURY

  Article 60. Violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury.

  2. Remedial measures:

  Account freezing or account closing shall be required for the acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 61. Acts of making false documents to carry out procedures for registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for making false documents to carry out procedures for registration and use of accounts at the State Treasury.

  2. Remedial measures:

  The violator is compelled to freeze the account in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 3. AUTHORITY TO IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE SECURITY DEPOSIT

  Article 62. Authority to make records of administrative violations against regulations on state security deposit

  1. Persons competent to make records of administrative violations against regulations on state security deposit include:

  a) Persons competent to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on control of state budget expenditures through state treasuries prescribed in Articles 63 and 64 of this Decree;

  b) Officials assigned to control state budget expenditures through state treasuries.

  2. Persons competent to make records prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall issue decisions to impose penalties within their competence or transfer them to competent authorities prescribed in Articles 63 and 64 of this Decree to issue decisions to impose penalties.

  Article 63. Power to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Chief Inspectors of Departments of Finance, heads of specialized inspectorates of Departments of Finance, heads of specialized inspectorates of State Treasuries established by Directors of provincial State Treasuries and heads of specialized inspectorates of State Treasuries established by General Directors of State Treasuries have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 50.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chief Inspectors of the Ministry of Finance have the power to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspectorates of the Ministry of Finance have the power to:

  a/ A fine of up to VND 250,000,000;

  b/ Application of remedial measures specified in Clause 6, Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 64.- Sanctioning competence of the General Directors of the State Treasuries and Directors of the State Treasuries of provinces and centrally-run cities

  1. Directors of the State Treasuries of provinces and centrally-run cities shall have the right:

  a/ To impose fines of up to VND 70,000,000;

  b/ To apply remedial measures specified in Clause 6, Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. The General Director of the State Treasury shall have the right:

  a/ To impose fines of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b) Remedial measures prescribed in Clause 6 Article 4 hereof.

  Chapter VI

                                          IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 65. Entry into force

  1. This Decree comes into force from September 01, 2019.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury and the Government’s Decree No. 58/2015/ND-CP dated June 16, 2015 on amendments to the Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury shall cease to be effective from the effective date of this Decree.

  Article 66. Transitional provisions

  Regulations on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury which are committed before the effective date of this Decree but detected afterwards or are being considered shall be applied if this Decree does not prescribe legal responsibilities or prescribe lighter legal responsibilities for violating individuals and organizations.

  Article 67. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Ministry of Finance shall provide guidelines for this Decree.

  2. Ministries, ministerial-level agencies, Governmental agencies, People’s Committees at all levels shall be responsible for providing guidelines for implementation and inspecting the imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury as prescribed in this Decree.

  3. When detecting administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury, competent authorities of the State, organizations and individuals shall transfer dossiers (if any) or notify them to persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations specified in this Decree for consideration and handling.

  In case where administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property are subject to recovery of public property according to regulations of law on management and use of public property, persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations specified in this Decree shall notify them to competent authorities according to regulations of law on management and use of public property for issuing decisions on recovery of property as prescribed. The handling of recovered property shall comply with regulations of law on management and use of public property.

  4. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels and relevant organizations and individuals shall implement this Decree./.
