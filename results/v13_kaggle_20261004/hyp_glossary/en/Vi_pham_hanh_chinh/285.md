                                                   DECREE

     REGULATIONS ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF FIRE PREVENTION AND FIRE FIGHTING

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Organization of the Government;

  Pursuant to the Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting dated June 29, 2001;

  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002 and the Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated April 2, 2008;

  At the request of the Minister of Public Security;

  The Government promulgates a Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting,

  Chapter 1.

                                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, principles of sanctioning, forms of sanctioning, levels of sanction, remedial measures, competence to sanction, procedures for sanctioning, complaints, denunciations and settlement of complaints, denunciations about sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting.

  2. Administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting or related to fire prevention and firefighting not prescribed in this Decree but prescribed in other Government’s decrees on handling of administrative violations shall be handled according to those decrees.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting within the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall be sanctioned in accordance with this Decree and other relevant laws on handling of administrative violations.

  Article 3. Principles for handling of administrative violations

  The principles for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting shall comply with Article 3 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 4. Forms of sanctioning of administrative violations

  1. For each act of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting prescribed in this Decree, violating individuals or organizations shall be subject to one of the following main forms of sanction:

  a) Caution;

  b) Fine.

  2. Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, individuals and organizations committing acts of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting may also be subject to one or more of the following additional sanctions:

  a) Deprivation of the right to use licenses, practicing certificates;

  b) Confiscation of material evidences and means used for committing administrative violations.

  3. In addition to the main forms of sanction, the additional sanctions prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:

  a) Forcible application of measures to remedy environmental pollution caused by administrative violations;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation or enforced dismantlement of illegally constructed works or structures;

  c) Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by the administrative violation;

  d) Enforced reduction in quantity of hazardous substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards according to prescribed norms;

  dd) Enforced relocation of hazardous substances with fire and explosion hazards caused by the administrative violation to storage facilities or designated locations as prescribed;

  e) Other remedial measures prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  4. Persons who commit acts of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting causing damage to organizations and individuals shall, in addition to being subject to administrative penalties as prescribed in Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article, be liable for compensation for damage in accordance with civil law.

  5. Foreigners who commit acts of administrative violation in the field of fire prevention and firefighting within the territory of Vietnam may be expelled from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Deportation may be applied as a principal or additional sanction. The competence, order and procedures for application of the sanction of deportation shall comply with current law on deportation under administrative procedures.

  Chapter 2.

                      ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES

  Article 5. Violations against regulations on promulgation, dissemination and implementation of fire prevention and firefighting regulations and internal rules

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to comply with fire prevention and firefighting regulations and internal rules;

  b) Damaging or making fire prevention and firefighting regulations, internal rules, signs, warning signs or instructions unclear or illegible.

  c) Failing to provide fire prevention and firefighting regulations, internal rules, signs, warning signs or instructions in conformity with regulations.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to provide fire prevention and firefighting regulations, internal rules, signs, warning signs or instructions as prescribed.

  b) Failing to comply with fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations;

  c) Failing to disseminate fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations to persons under their management;

  d) Promulgating fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations which do not contain sufficient contents or are not suitable for the nature of fire and explosion hazards of facilities.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to arrange or post fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to promulgate or promulgating fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations in contravention of legal normative documents of the State.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original state altered due to the administrative violation specified in Point b Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory promulgation, dissemination and posting of fire prevention and firefighting rules and regulations in accordance with regulations applicable to the violations specified in Points a, c and d Clause 2 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 6. Violations against regulations on fire safety inspection

  1. A fine of from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to present documents serving fire safety inspection.

  2. A fine of from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to comply with or to complete recommendations on fire prevention and firefighting at the request of competent authorities;

  b) Failure to appoint responsible persons to join the fire safety inspection team as prescribed;

  c) Failure to organize fire safety inspection as prescribed.

  3. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to comply with written instructions on fire prevention and firefighting issued by competent authorities;

  b) Failing to implement fire prevention and firefighting recommendations issued in writing by competent authorities;

  c) Obstructing inspection and examination of fire prevention and firefighting activities by competent authorities.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to issue a written notification and commitment on satisfaction of requirements and maintenance of conditions for ensuring fire safety during use of houses, structures, and production and business activities after a facility is approved and commissioned.

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on records for management of fire prevention and firefighting safety

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to make statistics and reports on fire prevention and firefighting activities as prescribed;

  b) Failing to keep adequate documents on fire prevention and firefighting activities;

  c) Failing to update information on changes related to fire prevention and firefighting activities in records for management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting activities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to prepare documents on management and monitoring of fire prevention and firefighting.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in management, preservation and use of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failing to keep records and documents on monitoring and management of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for storing, arranging or organizing substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards in excess of the prescribed quantity or weight, or failing to maintain the prescribed distance or group of substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using equipment and devices containing or holding substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards without inspection certificates or failing to meet safety requirements regarding fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for illegally storing substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for illegally using substances and/or goods with fire and explosion hazards.

  6. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate substances and fire/explosion hazards for violations specified in Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced development and establishment of documents and records for monitoring and managing fire/explosion hazards as prescribed for violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Enforced preservation, arrangement, reduction of quantity and volume of fire/explosion hazards as prescribed for violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  c) Enforced transfer of fire/explosion hazards to warehouses or locations as prescribed for violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 9. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting during production and trading of substances and fire/explosion hazards

  1. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to adopt measures and equipment for preventing electrostatic accumulation as prescribed;

  b) Failing to install natural ventilation or forced ventilation equipment as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to install equipment to detect and treat leaks of substances and inflammable and explosive goods in the environment as prescribed;

  b) Having no plan or equipment to deal with incidents of tank breakage, equipment, pipelines containing, carrying and transporting petroleum, petroleum products and other flammable liquids;

  c) Charging, filling inflammable and explosive goods at improper places or charging, filling inflammable and explosive goods into equipment of improper types or incompatible with inflammable and explosive goods.

  3. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for acts of manufacturing, trading, charging, filling inflammable and explosive goods without licenses as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 25,000,000 for acts of manufacturing, trading in substances and inflammable and explosive goods on the list of goods banned from trading.

  5. Additional sanctions:

  Confiscate substances and goods dangerous to fire and explosion for acts specified in Clause 3 and Clause 4 of this Article.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remediation of environmental pollution in accordance with regulations for violations specified in Point a and Point b Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on transportation of flammable and explosive substances

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for any of the following acts:

  a) Damaging or disintegrating the license for transport of substances and goods with fire and explosion hazards;

  b) Failing to carry the transport permit when transporting inflammable and explosive substances or goods as prescribed;

  c) Do not remove or detach fire and explosion hazard symbols on transport means when the hazardous materials or goods have been removed from the transport means;

  d) Losing licenses for transport of substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards but having timely reported to competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from 1.000.000 VND to 2.000.000 VND shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Arranging substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards on transport vehicles against regulations;

  b) Stopping or parking transport vehicles carrying substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards at places where such transport is not permitted as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from 2.000.000 VND to 5.000.000 VND shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to maintain adequate conditions for fire prevention and firefighting safety when using motorized transport vehicles to transport substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards during the permitted transport period as prescribed;

  b) Transporting other goods together with substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards on the same transport vehicle without permission as prescribed;

  c) Carrying unauthorized persons on transport vehicles carrying substances or goods with fire and explosion hazards.

  4. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Transporting hazardous materials or goods in quantities, volumes or types exceeding those specified in the license;

  b) Failing to post the symbol of transport of hazardous materials or goods on the means of transport as prescribed;

  c) Failing to comply with fire safety requirements or instructions of competent persons when loading, unloading, pumping or transferring hazardous materials or goods out of the means of transport as prescribed;

  d) Loading, unloading, pumping or transferring hazardous materials or goods at a location where fire safety requirements are not satisfied as prescribed;

  dd) Loading, unloading, pumping or transferring hazardous materials or goods while they are being transported to another vehicle without permission of a competent authority;

  e) Erasing or altering the license for transport of hazardous materials or goods;

  g) Losing the license for transport of hazardous materials or goods without reporting it to a competent authority.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Transporting inflammable and/or explosive substances or goods without licenses for transport of inflammable and/or explosive substances or goods as prescribed;

  b/ Faking or using fake licenses for transport of inflammable and/or explosive substances or goods.

  6. Additional sanctions:

  a/ Depriving the right to use licenses for transport of inflammable and/or explosive substances or goods, for acts specified at Point f, Clause 4 of this Article;

  b/ Confiscating inflammable and/or explosive substances or goods, for acts specified at Point b, Clause 1, Points a and b, Clause 5 of this Article.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a/ Forcible re-arrangement of inflammable and/or explosive substances or goods under regulations, for acts specified at Point a, Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Enforced reduction in quantity, weight or type of flammable and explosive substances or goods as prescribed in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Enforced transfer of flammable and explosive substances or goods to storage facilities or locations as prescribed for the acts specified in Points a and b Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting during use of fire sources, heat sources, devices and instruments that generate fire or heat

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to arrange cooking or worship places in a manner that ensures fire safety and firefighting requirements as prescribed;

  b) Bringing matches, lighters, mobile phones, electronic devices or devices and instruments that generate fire or heat to prohibited areas.

  2. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to maintain a safe distance from fire sources, heat sources, devices and instruments that generate fire or heat as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Using fire sources, mobile phones, electronic devices or other fire and heat generating devices at prohibited places;

  b) Welding or cutting metals without taking measures to ensure fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in design, installation, management and use of electricity

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to provide regulations on fire safety during the use of electricity at the establishment.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for using electrical equipment not in compliance with instructions of the manufacturer.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to maintain regular operation of the emergency lighting system;

  b) Changing the design or changing the structure or main specifications of the electrical system or electrical equipment without approval of a competent person or authority;

  c) Installing and using electrical conductors, power cables or switching, protection or consumption equipment not ensuring fire safety;

  d) Using electrical equipment in excess of the design capacity;

  dd) Using electrical equipment at prohibited areas.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Using electrical equipment not meeting fire safety requirements in a fire and explosion hazard environment;

  b) Failing to have a backup power source as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to design and install electrical systems serving fire prevention, firefighting and rescue purposes as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Forced to remedy unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations of regulations on safety of fire prevention and fighting in design, construction, inspection and maintenance of lightning protection system

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for acts of failing to have dossiers for monitoring of lightning protection system under regulation.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for acts of failing to carry out periodic inspection of lightning protection system under regulation.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for acts of failing to remedy mistakes and damage causing loss of effectiveness of lightning protection system.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for acts of failing to install lightning protection system for houses, works subject to installation of lightning protection system under regulation.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Forced to remedy unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting in investment and construction

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to take measures and means to ensure safety in fire prevention and fighting during construction of works.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to design fire prevention and fighting system for works subject to having design on fire prevention and fighting.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to satisfy one of fire safety requirements when putting a house or work into use if such house or work is not required to undergo fire safety approval.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Construction and installation not in accordance with fire prevention and firefighting design approved by competent authorities;

  b) Failing to submit applications for re-examination when renovating, expanding or changing the use of houses and works during construction and use as prescribed.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Organizing construction of works subject to fire prevention and firefighting approval without obtaining the Certificate of Fire Prevention and Fighting Approval as prescribed;

  b) Manufacturing or modifying motor vehicles with special requirements for fire safety assurance without obtaining approval from competent authorities.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for putting houses or works into operation or use without organizing fire prevention and firefighting commissioning as prescribed.

  7. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory remedial measures for ensuring fire safety in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory construction or re-installation of fire protection works according to the approved design in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;

  c) Mandatory remedial measures for eliminating unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations or mandatorily organizing fire prevention and firefighting commissioning by regulatory authorities in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 6 of this Article.

  Article 15. Violations against regulations on fire safety distance

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Arranging and arranging supplies and goods not keeping safe fire and flame prevention and fighting distance as prescribed;

  b/ Failing to organize industrial hygiene which leads to the possibility of creating an environment with fire and explosion danger.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for act of building fire walls, fire partitions or fire doors and other fire prevention measures not satisfying requirements as prescribed.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Making ceilings, floors, partition walls, roofs or gathering flammable materials at places where such activities are not permitted as prescribed;

  b/ Building houses in forests or near forests not ensuring the safety against spread of fire as prescribed.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to clean flammable substances located within the safety corridor of oil and gas pipelines and petroleum products;

  b) Building structures in violation of fire separation distance.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to build fire walls, fire partitions, fire doors and fire separation solutions as prescribed;

  b) Causing the loss of fire separation effect of fire walls, fire partitions, fire doors and fire separation solutions.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original condition which has been altered by the administrative violations specified in Point a Clause 1, Point b Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory dismantling of illegally constructed works if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 3 or Point b Clause 4 of this Article is committed;

  c) Forcible overcoming of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Point a Clause 3, Point a Clause 4, Point a Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 16.- Sanctions against violations of regulations on rescue in fire prevention and fighting

  1. Caution or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for acts of designing exit doors not opening in the direction of escape, installing mirrors in the escape staircases.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Arranging or arranging supplies, goods, means of transport and other utensils which obstruct the escape;

  b/ Removing, dismantling or damaging the emergency lighting equipment, signboards and/or guides on the escape routes;

  c/ Failing to install signboards and/or escape guides on the escape routes.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to provide adequate rescue equipment and devices as prescribed;

  b) Failing to provide smoke extraction and ventilation equipment for the emergency exit as prescribed;

  c) Failing to provide emergency lighting on the emergency exit or providing insufficient lighting as prescribed or ineffective lighting;

  d) Designing and building the number of, area, width of, or design of the emergency exits, staircases or ramps not in compliance with regulations.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for closing, squeezing or blocking the emergency exits.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 15,000,000 to VND 25,000,000 shall be imposed for damaging the effectiveness of the emergency exits.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced rectification of unsafe conditions caused by the administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of this Article.

  Article 17. Violations against fire prevention and firefighting plan of facilities

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100,000 to VND 200,000 shall be imposed for failure to manage fire prevention and firefighting plan as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Preparing fire prevention and firefighting plan which fails to meet requirements;

  b) Failure to disseminate fire prevention and firefighting plan as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failure to submit fire prevention and firefighting plan for approval as prescribed;

  b) Failure to participate in practice of fire prevention and firefighting plan as prescribed.

  c) Failing to supplement and adjust fire prevention plans as prescribed;

  d) Failing to practice all fire scenarios in approved fire prevention plans.

  4. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to develop fire prevention plans as prescribed;

  b) Failing to organize practice of fire prevention plans as prescribed.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 18. Violations against regulations on fire alarm information

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of leaving means, information devices of fire alarm to be broken or ineffective.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Having no command, information devices of fire alarm as prescribed;

  b) Slowly or untimely fire alarm; fire alarm not full.

  3. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Fake fire alarm;

  b) Failing to give fire alarm or obstructing, causing obstruction to fire alarm;

  c) The information service provider fails to promptly remedy the breakage of fire alarm receiving equipment of the Fire and Rescue Police when there is a written request.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of initial changes caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 19. Violations against regulations on fire and explosion prevention and control equipment, storage and use

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Move or change the installation position of fire and rescue equipment according to the design approved by a competent authority;

  b) Blocking fire and fighting equipment.

  2. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to carry out periodic inspection and maintenance of fire prevention and firefighting systems and equipment as prescribed;

  b) Failing to provide adequate or synchronized fire and fighting equipment as prescribed;

  c) Failing to provide common fire and fighting equipment for motor vehicles with 4 seats or more, or specialized motor vehicles used for transporting flammable and explosive goods as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Providing, installing or using fire and fighting equipment that has not been inspected for quality, type or design as prescribed;

  b) Providing fire and fighting equipment that is inappropriate for the fire and explosion hazards of the establishment as prescribed;

  c) Using fire and fighting equipment at the fire station for other purposes;

  d) Failing to store sufficient water for firefighting as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to provide common fire extinguishers for houses and works as prescribed;

  b) Losing, damaging or rendering ineffective fire prevention and fighting equipment.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of failing to provide or install fire alarm and firefighting system as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  a) Forced to overcome unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Point a Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Forced to overcome initial changes due to administrative violations specified at point b clause 4 of this Article.

  Article 20. Violations against regulations on fire prevention and fighting

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 100,000 and 200,000 shall be imposed for acts of entering the fire prevention and fighting area without permission of competent persons.

  2. A fine of from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to respond in time to rescue people and property or fire.

  3. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to prepare adequate forces, equipment, water sources and other conditions for firefighting as prescribed;

  b) Failure to arrange traffic routes, access positions of buildings, works and other empty spaces for fire trucks and other motorized fire fighting vehicles to operate as prescribed.

  4. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to comply with orders of the fire commander;

  b) Failure to comply with the order for mobilization to participate in firefighting issued by a competent person;

  c) Obstructing the operation of fire prevention and firefighting forces and equipment;

  d) Do not participate in remedying consequences of the fire at the request of competent persons;

  dd) Failing to protect the scene of the fire as prescribed.

  5. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to organize rescue, firefighting or fire prevention;

  b) Taking advantage of fire prevention and firefighting to harm the health or property of citizens and state property.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 21. Violations against regulations on dissemination of fire prevention and firefighting laws, knowledge, learning, training and education

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for damaging fire prevention and firefighting banners, posters, propaganda pictures or photos.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to organize dissemination of fire prevention and firefighting laws and knowledge as prescribed;

  b) Employing an individual who is not trained in fire prevention and firefighting but is a grassroots firefighting worker or a driver of a four-seat motor vehicle or higher, or a driver of a motor vehicle transporting flammable and explosive goods who has not completed training courses in fire prevention and firefighting or has not been issued with a certificate of completion of training courses in fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to provide training courses in fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced restoration of the original condition which has been altered by the administrative violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on establishment, organization and management of grassroots fire prevention and firefighting forces and specialized fire prevention and firefighting forces

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 100.000 to VND 200.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to ensure the required number of personnel and the required duration of on-call duty or emergency response duty in fire prevention and firefighting;

  b) Failure of grassroots fire department to proficiently use fire-fighting equipment installed at the grassroots level.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 200.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for failure to organize shift rotation at grassroots level as prescribed.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Establishing a grassroots or specialized fire department but failing to meet requirements as prescribed;

  b) Failure to manage or maintain the operation of the grassroots or specialized fire department as prescribed.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to participate in fire prevention and firefighting activities when requested by competent persons.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to establish grassroots or specialized fire prevention and firefighting teams as prescribed.

  6. Remedial measures:

  The violator is compelled to remedy unsafe conditions caused by the administrative violations specified in Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3 and Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 23. Violations against regulations on production and trading of means and equipment for fire prevention and firefighting and fire prevention and firefighting design

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed upon the fire prevention and firefighting design unit that fails to satisfy eligibility requirements regarding legal entity status and professional technical capacity as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed upon the individual or organization that fails to meet eligibility requirements regarding facilities, professional technical capacity, training or refresher training in fire prevention and firefighting as prescribed when trading means and equipment for fire prevention and firefighting.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for modifying a fire truck or dedicated fire vessel without permission of a competent authority.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed upon the unit that fails to satisfy eligibility requirements regarding legal entity status and professional technical capacity when installing fire prevention and firefighting works.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remedy of unsafe conditions caused by administrative violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 24. Violations against regulations on compulsory fire insurance

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to take measures to prevent or limit loss of members of compulsory fire insurance policy as prescribed.

  2. A fine of between VND 20,000,000 and 30,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) The entity is required to purchase compulsory fire insurance but fails to do so as prescribed;

  b) Buying compulsory fire insurance against regulations or the premium for compulsory fire insurance issued by the Ministry of Finance;

  c/ The establishments subject to compulsory purchase of fire insurance shall not separate the compulsory fire insurance contract in their comprehensive insurance contracts as prescribed;

  d) Failing to deduct compulsory fire insurance premiums for contributions to firefighting funding as prescribed.

  Article 25. Acts leading to house fires

  1. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire not causing damage or causing damage assessed at < VND 25,000,000.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire causing damage assessed at from VND 25,000,000 to VND 50,000,000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire causing damage assessed at > VND 50,000,000 but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  Article 26. Penalties imposed upon individuals and organizations for house fires

  1. A fine ranging from VND 200,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire causing damage assessed at < VND 2,000,000.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations leading to a house fire causing damage assessed at from VND 2,000,000 to VND 10,000,000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally violating fire safety regulations which leads to a fire or explosion causing damage assessed at from VND 10,000,000 to under VND 25,000,000.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally causing a fire or explosion causing damage assessed at from VND 25,000,000 to under VND 50,000,000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for irresponsibly causing a fire or explosion but not seriously enough to be examined for penal liability.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for unintentionally causing a fire or explosion causing damage assessed at over VND 50,000,000 without being examined for penal liability.

  Article 27. Expulsion

  Foreigners who commit acts of administrative violation prescribed in Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 18, 20 and 21 of this Decree may, depending on the seriousness of their violations, be subject to the penalty of expulsion from the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  Chapter III

                             POWER TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES

  Article 28.- The power to sanction administrative violations of the People's Police Force

  1. Soldiers of the People's Police Force on duty shall have the power to:

  a/ Impose caution;

  b/ Impose fines of up to VND 200,000.

  2. Station Heads and Team Leaders of the persons with competence defined in Clause 1 of this Article shall have the power to:

  a/ Impose caution;

  b/ Impose fines of up to VND 500,000.

  3. Chiefs of the police offices of communes, wards or district townships (hereinafter referred collectively to as commune-level police offices) shall have the power to:

  a/ Warning;

  b/ Fines of up to VND 2,000,000;

  c/ Confiscation of exhibits and means used in the commission of administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;

  d/ Forcible restoration of the initial state altered due to the commission of administrative violations.

  4. Chiefs of police offices of urban districts, rural districts, towns or provincial cities (hereinafter referred collectively to as district-level police offices) shall have the power to:

  a/ Impose warnings;

  b/ Impose fines of up to VND 10,000,000;

  c/ Deprive of the right to use licenses or practicing certificates under their jurisdiction;

  d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for committing administrative violations;

  dd) Apply remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  5. Heads of Fire and Rescue Police Divisions, Heads of Police Divisions for Administrative Management of Social Order, Heads of Road and Rail Traffic Police Divisions, Heads of Waterway Police Divisions, Heads of Police Divisions for Prevention and Control of Environmental Crimes, Heads of Police Divisions for Investigation into Social Order-related Crimes, provincial-level Police Departments and Heads of Fire and Rescue Police Divisions of district level under the Fire and Rescue Police Department shall, within the ambit of their functions, tasks and powers, have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 10.000.000;

  c) Deprive of the right to use licenses or practicing certificates falling under their jurisdiction;

  d) Confiscate material evidence and means used for committing administrative violations;

  dd) Apply remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  6. Directors of Police Departments of provinces or centrally-run cities (below referred collectively to as provincial-level) and directors of provincial-level Fire Prevention and Fighting Police Departments shall have the right:

  a/ To serve a warning;

  b/ To fine up to VND 30,000,000;

  c/ To strip off the right to use licenses, professional practice certificates under their jurisdiction;

  d/ To confiscate material evidence and means used in administrative violations;

  e/ To apply remedial measures specified in this Decree;

  f/ Directors of provincial-level Police Departments may decide on the application of the sanctioning form of expulsion according to the decentralization of the Minister of Public Security.

  7. The director of the Police Department for Fire Prevention, Fighting and Rescue, the director of the Police Department for Administrative Management of Social Order, the director of the Road-Railway Traffic Police Department, the director of the Waterway Police Department, the director of the Police Department for Prevention and Combat of Environmental Crimes, the director of the Investigation Police Department for Social Order Crimes, shall, within the ambit of their respective functions, tasks and powers, have the right:

  a/ Warning;

  b/ Fines of up to the maximum level prescribed at Point a, Clause 2, Article 14 of the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  c/ Deprivation of the right to use licenses or professional practice certificates under their jurisdiction;

  d/ Confiscation of goods or means used in the commission of administrative violations;

  e/ Application of remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  8. The Minister of Public Security shall decide on the application of the sanctioning form of expulsion.

  Article 29. Competence of the Presidents of the People's Committees at all levels to sanction administrative violations

  1. The Presidents of the commune-level People's Committees have the right:

  a/ Caution;

  b/ Fines of up to VND 2,000,000;

  c/ Confiscation of exhibits and means used in the commission of administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;

  d/ Compulsory restoration of the initial state altered due to the commission of administrative violations.

  2. Chairmen of district-level People's Committees have the right:

  a/ To issue warnings;

  b/ To impose fines of up to VND 30,000,000;

  c/ To confiscate exhibits and means used in the commission of administrative violations;

  d) Revoke licenses or practicing certificates within their powers;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  3. Chairpersons of provincial-level People's Committees have the right:

  a) Warning;

  b/ Fines of up to the maximum level prescribed at Point a, Clause 2, Article 14 of the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  c/ Confiscating goods and means used in the commission of administrative violations;

  d) Revoke licenses or practicing certificates within their powers;

  dd) Applying remedial measures specified in this Decree.

  Article 30. Authority to impose administrative penalties of other agencies

  Apart from persons with sanctioning competence prescribed in Articles 28 and 29 of this Decree, persons with sanctioning competence as prescribed in the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, when performing their functions and tasks within the areas under their management, if detecting acts of administrative violation specified in this Decree, shall have the right to impose sanctions according to law provisions.

  Article 31. Authorization for imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting

  Persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations specified in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 Article 28; Clauses 1, 2 and 3 Article 29 hereof may authorize their deputies to exercise their authority to impose penalties for administrative violations. Authorization shall be made in writing. Authorized deputies shall be responsible for their decisions on imposition of penalties for administrative violations before their heads and before law.

  Article 32. Principles for determining the power to impose penalties for administrative violations

  1. The authority to impose penalties for administrative violations prescribed in this Decree is the authority applicable to each administrative violation. In case of a fine, the authority to impose the penalty shall be determined based on the maximum level of the fine bracket prescribed for each specific violation. If an administrative violation falls under the authority to impose penalties of multiple persons, the penalty shall be imposed by the first person to receive and handle the violation.

  2. In case of sanctioning a person for committing many administrative violations, the sanctioning competence shall be determined according to the following principle:

  a) If the sanctioning forms and levels prescribed for each act are all under the authority of the sanctioning person, the sanctioning authority shall still belong to such person;

  b) If the form and level of sanction prescribed for one of acts of violation fall beyond the competence of the sanctioning person, he/she shall have to transfer the case to the competent sanctioning level.

  3. In cases where the persons with sanctioning competence of the People's Public Security are handling individuals or organizations committing violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting under this Decree but detect that such individuals or organizations also commit acts of administrative violation prescribed in other Government's Decrees, they may sanction such acts administratively.

  Chapter IV

                         ORDER OF AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 33. Order of and procedures for sanctioning administrative violations

  1. Upon detecting administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting, the persons with sanctioning competence shall have to issue orders to stop immediately the acts of violation.

  2. The order of and procedures for sanctioning administrative violations are as follows:

  a/ For administrative violations which are sanctioned by warning or a fine of up to VND 200,000, the persons with sanctioning competence shall issue decisions to sanction them on the spot according to simple procedures prescribed in Article 54 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations;

  b) For administrative violations subject to a fine exceeding VND 200,000, the person with sanctioning competence shall make a record on such administrative violation under Article 55 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and issue a sanctioning decision under Article 56 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations; if the violation falls beyond the sanctioning competence of the record maker, he/she shall send the record to the person with sanctioning competence for issuing a sanctioning decision in accordance with regulations.

  c) The forms for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting are applied uniformly according to the forms prescribed in the field of security and social order and safety.

  3. When applying the form of fine, the specific fine level for an act of administrative violation is the average level of the fine bracket for such act prescribed in this Decree; in case of an offense involving extenuating circumstances, the fine level may be reduced but not reduced below the minimum level of the fine bracket; in case of an offense involving aggravating circumstances, the fine level may be increased but not increased above the maximum level of the fine bracket already prescribed. When imposing fines, the fine bracket and specific fine levels must be announced to the sanctioned persons.

  4. Minors aged between full 16 years and under 18 years who commit acts of administrative violation and are sanctioned for administrative violations in the form of fines, the fine levels must not exceed half of the fine levels applicable to adults; in cases where they have no money to pay fines, their parents or guardians shall pay them instead.

  Article 34. Application of measures to prevent administrative violations and ensure sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting

  In cases where it is necessary to promptly prevent acts of administrative violation or to ensure the sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting, measures of temporary detention of persons; temporary seizure of material evidences and means used in the commission of administrative violations; body search; search of transport means and objects; search of places where material evidences and means used in the commission of administrative violations are hidden shall be applied in accordance with the provisions of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 35.- Collection and payment of fines for administrative violations

  1. Individuals and organizations fined for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting shall have to pay fines within the time limit prescribed in the State Treasury inscribed in the sanctioning decisions, except for cases where they have already paid fines on the spot as provided for in Article 54 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and are entitled to receive fine receipts. The collection and receipt of fines must use receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance in strict compliance with regulations.

  2. In remote and isolated areas, on rivers or seas, areas where travel is difficult or outside working hours, sanctioned individuals and organizations may pay fines to persons with sanctioning competence. Persons with sanctioning competence shall have to collect fines on the spot and remit them into the State Treasury in accordance with the provisions of Clause 3, Article 58 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations. Sanctioned persons may not yet pay fines if they do not have fine receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance in strict compliance with regulations.

  Article 36.- Execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting shall have to execute the sanctioning decisions within 10 days after being handed over the sanctioning decisions. Past this time limit, if sanctioned individuals and organizations fail to voluntarily execute the sanctioning decisions, they shall be forced to execute them according to law provisions.

  2. Individuals who are fined VND 500,000 or more may postpone the execution of fine decisions according to the provisions of Article 65 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. When it is deemed necessary, the person having sanctioning competence or the agency conducting sanctioning of individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting may publicly notify the violation or sanction decision to the agency, organization or local authority where the violating individual works or resides, and to the directly superior agency managing or the local authority where the violating organization registers its operation.

  Article 37. Revocation of the right to use licenses and practicing certificates

  The revocation of the right to use licenses and practicing certificates shall be effected only for violations prescribed in this Decree and other Government's decrees on sanctioning of administrative violations related to fire prevention and fighting and must comply with the provisions of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the 2008 Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 38.- Transferring dossiers on violation cases with criminal signs for penal liability examination In case of considering a violation case for deciding on sanctioning, if deeming that the act of violation shows signs of a crime or in case where a sanctioning decision has been issued but later detecting that the act of violation shows signs of a crime and the statute of limitations for penal liability examination has not yet expired, the dossier on the violation case with criminal signs shall be transferred to the competent criminal proceedings-conducting agency according to the provisions of Article 62 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 39. Complaints and denunciations

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and firefighting or their lawful representatives have the right to lodge complaints about decisions on sanctioning administrative violations and decisions on application of measures to prevent and ensure the handling of administrative violations of competent persons.

  All individuals have the right to lodge their denunciations with competent state agencies about illegal acts in handling administrative violations against regulations on fire prevention and firefighting.

  2. Complaints and denunciations shall be made and settled in accordance with regulations of law on complaints and denunciations.

  3. The lawsuit against decisions on imposition of administrative penalties and decisions on application of preventive measures for ensuring the imposition of administrative penalties in the field of fire prevention and firefighting shall comply with regulations of law on administrative procedures.

  Chapter 5.

                                            IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 40. Effect

  This Decree takes effect on August 5, 2012 and replaces Decree No. 123/2005/ND-CP of October 5, 2005 prescribing the handling of administrative violations in the field of fire prevention and fighting.

  Article 41. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Minister of Public Security shall organize implementation, provide guidance, inspect and urge implementation of this Decree.

  2. The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies, the heads of the Government-attached agencies, the presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and centrally-run cities and the concerned organizations and individuals shall have to implement this Decree.-
