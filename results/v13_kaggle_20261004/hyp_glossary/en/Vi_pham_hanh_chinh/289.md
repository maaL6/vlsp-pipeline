                                             DECREE
           REGULATIONS ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF TOURISM
Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
Pursuant to the Law on Tourism dated June 14, 2005;
Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002, the Ordinance amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated April 2, 2008;
At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
The Government promulgates the Decree prescribing the sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of tourism, Chapter 1.
                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope of regulation
1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, forms of sanction, levels of sanction, competence to sanction, procedures for sanction and remedial measures in the field of tourism.
2. Administrative violations in the field of tourism are acts of violating regulations of law on state management in the field of tourism committed by individuals or organizations intentionally or unintentionally but not being crimes and must be administratively sanctioned as prescribed by law.
3. Administrative violations in the field of tourism specified in this Decree include:
a) Violations of regulations on tour business, tourist guide business, car business to transport tourists;
b) Violations of regulations on tourist accommodation business;
c) Violations of regulations on tourism promotion;
d) Violations of other regulations on tourism activities.
4. Other administrative violations in the fields of tourism and tourist service provision not prescribed in this Decree shall be handled according to other Decrees of the Government on handling of administrative violations in relevant state management fields.
Article 2. Regulated entities
Vietnamese organizations and individuals, and foreign organizations and individuals (hereinafter referred to as “individuals and organizations”) committing administrative violations in the field of tourism within the Vietnamese territory.
Article 3. Penalties and remedial measures
1. For each administrative violation, the violating individual or organization shall be subject to one of the following penalties:
a) Warning;
b) Fine.
2. Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, the violating individuals or organizations may also be subject to one or several of the following additional sanctions:
a) Depriving the right to use international tour operator licenses, tour guide badges, or narrator certificates for a definite period of time;
b) Confiscating exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations.
3. In addition to the main forms of sanction and additional sanctions prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individual or organization may also be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:
a) Compulsory provision of adequate interior furnishings, amenities, means, equipment, and safety devices to ensure the health, life safety, and property of tourists;
b) Compulsory compliance with regulations on preparation and storage of documents as prescribed by law;

c) Compulsory return to tourists or confiscation to the state budget of illegally collected property of tourists;
d) Compulsory payment of the deposit as prescribed;
dd) Compulsory compliance with reporting regulations as prescribed by law;
e) Enforced installation of signboards on cars meeting standards for tourist service as prescribed by law;
g) Enforced provision of adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages of managers and staff corresponding to standards of each type and class of accommodation establishment as prescribed by law;
h) Enforced installation of signboards of tourist accommodation establishments in accordance with classification decisions issued by competent authorities;
i) Enforced removal and destruction of publications, advertising boards, signboards, items and equipment violating regulations of law;
k) Enforced dismantling and destruction of tourist propaganda and advertising items, termination of advertising programs, applicable to some violations against regulations on tourism promotion activities;
l) Enforced application of measures to remedy environmental pollution caused by violations against regulations on environmental protection;
m) Enforced implementation of decisions of competent agencies and persons, applicable to acts of obstructing inspection and examination and imposing penalties for administrative violations.
Chapter 2.
         ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES AND FINES
Section 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON TRAVEL BUSINESS, TOURISM GUIDANCE AND PASSENGER TRANSPORT BUSINESS
Article 4. Violations against regulations on notification of travel business operation A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
1. Failure to notify in writing the time of commencing travel business operation to the provincial/municipal Services of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the head office of the enterprise is located within 15 days after the enterprise officially commences its business.
2. Failure to notify in writing the change of the legal representative of the enterprise, change of the type of enterprise, change of the name of the enterprise or change of the location of the head office of the travel business enterprise to the provincial/municipal Service of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the head office of the enterprise is located within 15 days after the change.
3. Failure to notify in writing the change of the operator of international travel business operation to the provincial/municipal Service of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 30 days after the enterprise changes the operator of international travel business operation.
4. Failure to notify in writing the establishment and commencement date of the branch/representative office of the travel business enterprise to the provincial/municipal Service of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the head office of the enterprise is located and the provincial/municipal Service of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the branch/representative office is located within 45 days after the branch/representative office is established and officially commences its operation.
5. Failure to notify in writing the change of the head, name or location of the branch/representative office of the travel business enterprise to the provincial/municipal Service of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the head office of the enterprise is located and the provincial/municipal Service of Culture, Sports and Tourism where the branch/representative office is located within 10 days after the change.
6. Failing to notify in writing the General Department of Tourism within 7 days from the date of losing international tour business license.
7. Failing to promptly notify the Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism of accidents or risks or incidents that may occur to tourists.
Article 5. Violations against regulations on tour business
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:
a) Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for at least 3 years to operate domestic tour business;
b) Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for at least 4 years to operate international tour business;
c) Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for at least 3 years to operate domestic tour business;

d) Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for at least 4 years to operate international tour business;
dd) The tour contract is incomplete as prescribed by law.
2. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to provide written tour program for tourists or representatives of tourist groups;
b) Failure to have a tour contract with tourists or representatives of tourists as prescribed;
c) Failure to settle legitimate requests and recommendations of tourists as prescribed by law;
d) Using tour guides to perform tour program without having a written contract with tour guides or a contract which does not contain all basic contents as prescribed;
dd) Failure to guide or provide information related to tour program when the tour operator requests;
e) Failure to clearly, publicly and truthfully inform tourists about the number and prices of tourism services;
3. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) The tour operator uses false documents to operate tour business;
b) Failure to comply with regulations on making and keeping records as prescribed by law;
c) Using expired tour guide’s card to guide tourists.
4. A fine of from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using vehicles, equipment which fail to ensure health, safety and property of tourists as prescribed by law, except for cases specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 hereof;
b) Failure to comply with regulations on reporting to Vietnam National Administration of Tourism, Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism as prescribed;
c) Using persons who do not have tour guide’s cards to guide tourists.
5. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Providing international tour services without at least 3 international tour guides;
b) Failing to purchase insurance for Vietnamese tourists who travel abroad during the period of tour program implementation as prescribed;
c) Changing tour programs, standards or services without the consent of tourists or their representatives.
6. A fine of from VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to ensure sufficient deposit for international tour business activities as prescribed;
b) Erasing or modifying contents in license for international tour business;
c) Collecting money outside of contract or other acts of collecting illicit profits from tourists;
d) Failing to manage operation and business of branches, representative offices and affiliates in accordance with law;
dd) Failing to manage tourists under signed contracts or tour programs;
e) Declaring contents in application for issuance or replacement of international tour operator license dishonestly;
g) Failing to follow procedures for replacement of international tour operator license as prescribed by law;
h) Using enterprise name, transaction name or abbreviated name inconsistently with name registered with competent authority for business activities;
7. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Employing foreigners to act as tour guides in Vietnam;
b) Engaging in tour operator business after having issued a notice of suspension, termination or being suspended by a competent authority.

8. A fine of from VND 25,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with international tour operator license.
9. A fine of from VND 30,000,000 to VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Allowing other organizations and individuals to use international tour operator license to operate and provide tour services;
b) Providing international tour services without international tour operator license.
10. Additional sanctions:
a) Depriving the right to use international tour operator license for up to 12 months if one of the violations prescribed in Points a and c Clause 5; Points a, d and dd Clause 6 and Point a Clause 7 of this Article is committed;
b) Depriving the right to use international tour operator license for between 12 and 24 months if one of the violations prescribed in Clause 8 and Point a Clause 9 of this Article is committed;
c) Confiscating exhibits of administrative violations if one of the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 3 and Point e Clause 6 of this Article is committed.
11. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate equipment and facilities for health, life safety and property protection of tourists if any of the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 4 of this Article is committed;
b) Enforced compliance with regulations on preparation and retention of documents if any of the violations prescribed in Point b Clause 3 of this Article is committed;
c) Enforced submission of reports as prescribed by law if any of the violations prescribed in Point b Clause 4 of this Article is committed;
d) Enforced payment of deposit if any of the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 6 of this Article is committed;
dd) Enforced return of illegal property collected from tourists or transfer of such property to state budget if any of the violations prescribed in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 6 of this Article is committed.
Article 6. Violations against regulations on tour operator business
1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a/ Failing to notify in writing the provincial/municipal Culture, Sports and Tourism Services of the date of commencing tour operator business within 15 days after such tour operator starts its business;
b/ Failing to notify in writing the provincial/municipal Culture, Sports and Tourism Services of changes of legal representatives or names or addresses of tour operators within 15 days after such changes;
c/ Failing to clearly and publicly disclose information on the number and prices of tourist services provided by tour operator contractors to tourists;
d/ Operating at a place other than the registered place with competent state agencies.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a/ Erasing, modifying or falsifying contents of tour programs of tour operator contractors;
b/ Selling tour programs at prices higher than those charged by tour operator contractors;
c/ Operating as tour operator without tour operator business registration.
d) Travel agency business without a written travel agency contract with the travel agency business.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Acting as an agent for tour operator companies that do not have licenses to operate international tour business to sell tour programs to international tourists and Vietnamese tourists traveling abroad;
b) Continue their tour operator business after being revoked by competent authorities.
4. Remedial measures:
Enforced refund to tourists or transfer to state budget of revenues collected in contravention of regulations for violations under Point b Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 7. Violations against regulations on tourist attractions
1. A warning or a fine of from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed on tour guides and docents who fail to wear cards or certificates while working.

2. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Providing inaccurate or inadequate information about the points of interest or tourist attractions;
b) Failing to carry tour program and documents issued by tour operator while providing tour services;
c) Failing to instruct tourists to comply with Vietnam’s laws, internal rules and regulations of the places where they visit, and respect local customs and practices;
d) Failing to provide adequate information about tour itinerary and tour program for representatives of tourist groups and tourists.
3. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to enter into employment contracts with tour operators;
b) Failing to provide tourists with instructions on measures to ensure their life safety, health and property during tour programs;
c) Letting others use one’s tourist guide card or tour guide certificate to practice;
d) Using another person’s tourist guide card or tour guide certificate to practice;
dd) Using an expired tourist guide card;
e) Declaring untruthful information in documents and materials included in the application for issuance, replacement or re-issuance of a tourist guide card or tour guide certificate.
4. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Erasing or altering contents of the tourist guide card or tour guide certificate to practice;
b) Using a domestic tourist guide card to provide tours for foreign tourists;
c) Giving tours at tourist attractions or tourist sites without holding a tour guide certificate.
5. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Providing tour guide services without a tour guide badge as prescribed;
b) Using fake tour guide badges or certificates to provide tour guide services;
c) Arbitrarily changing tour programs, reducing standards and services provided to tourists;
d) Giving false information about cultural, historical, national and people’s values, causing negative impacts on image, traditions, ethics and fine customs of Vietnam;
dd) Taking advantage of tour guide services to give presentations or provide information that affects social security, order and safety;
e) Bringing tourists to prohibited areas;
g) Collecting money outside the contract or other illegal benefits from tourists.
6. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed upon a foreigner who provides tour guiding services in Vietnam.
7. Additional sanctions:
a) Suspension of tour guide’s badge for 12 months if any of the violations mentioned in Point d Clause 2; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article is committed;
b) Suspension of tour guide’s badge for 12 - 24 months if any of the violations mentioned in Point c Clause 3; Points a and b Clause 4 or the violations mentioned in Points a, b and c Clause 2; Points a and b Clause 3; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article is committed;
c) Suspension of tour guide’s certificate for 12 - 24 months if any of the violations mentioned in Point c Clause 3; Point a Clause 4 of this Article is committed;
d) Confiscation of exhibits involved in administrative violations if any of the violations mentioned in Points d, dd and e Clause 3; Point b Clause 5 of this Article is committed.
8. Remedial measures:
Mandatory return of illegally collected property to tourists or confiscation of such property to the state budget if the violation prescribed in Point c or g Clause 5 of this Article is committed.
Article 8. Violations against regulations on tourist transport vehicles

1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with fewer than 9 seats that fails to meet prescribed interior, amenities and equipment requirements to carry tourists.
2. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with 9 - 24 seats that fails to meet prescribed interior, amenities and equipment requirements to carry tourists.
3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with 24 seats or more that fails to meet prescribed interior, amenities and equipment requirements to carry tourists.
4. A fine ranging from VND 4,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using a caravan or a two-storey car that fails to meet prescribed interior, amenities and equipment requirements to carry tourists.
5. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:
a) Changing the tour itinerary without the consent of tourists;
b) Failing to install a signboard indicating that the vehicle meets standards for serving tourists as prescribed by law.
c) Using drivers of tourist transport vehicles who have no certificate of training in tourism services;
d) Using attendants on tourist transport vehicles who have no certificate of training in tourism services.
6. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to comply with regulations on reporting to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism or the Department of Transport as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to carry out procedures for reissuing the badge of a standard tourist vehicle as prescribed by law;
c) Failing to purchase passenger insurance for tourists on the transport vehicle.
7. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 7.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using fake badges of standard tourist vehicles for business purposes;
b) Falsely identifying tourist transport vehicles.
8. Additional sanctions:
Confiscate the badge of the fake standard tourist transport vehicle for violations specified in point a clause 7 of this Article.
9. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate interior, amenities and equipment of tourist transport vehicles to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists for violations specified in clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced installation of badge of standard tourist transport vehicle as prescribed by law for violations specified in point c clause 5 of this Article;
c) Enforced reporting as prescribed for violations specified in point a clause 6 of this Article.
Article 9. Violations against regulations on establishment and operation of branches and representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam
Acts of violating regulations on establishment and operation of branches or representative offices of foreign tourist enterprises in Vietnam shall be sanctioned according to Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 13; Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 14 of the Government's Decree No. 06/2008/ND-CP of January 16, 2008 prescribing sanctions against administrative violations in the field of trade.
Section 2. ACTS OF VIOLATING REGULATIONS ON TOURIST ACCOMMODATION BUSINESS
Article 10. Violations of regulations on tourist accommodation business
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to notify in writing the time of commencing operation of tourist accommodation establishments to the provincial-level Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism where such establishments are located within 15 days after such establishments officially commence operation;
b) Failing to notify in writing the change of heads of tourist accommodation establishments or names of tourist accommodation establishments to the provincial-level Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 15 days after such changes;

c) Failing to notify in writing the application of telecommunication charges as surcharges to classified tourist accommodation establishments to the provincial-level Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism or the provincial-level Departments of Information and Communications within 15 days after such application.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to comply with regulations on the form of name boards and classification of tourist accommodation establishments;
b) Failing to put up name boards after being classified by the General Department of Tourism, the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
c) Failing to promulgate and post up internal rules and regulations of tourist accommodation establishments;
d) Failing to clearly and publicly inform tourists about the quantity, quality and price of services and goods provided for them;
dd) Failing to put up a decision to recognize the classification of tourist accommodation establishment at a visible position at the reception desk.
3. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on making and keeping records of business activities of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to have sufficient number of sleeping rooms and area in accordance with standards corresponding to each type and class of tourist accommodation establishment as prescribed by law;
c) Failure to meet standards of parking and internal traffic, reception hall area as prescribed by law;
d) Failure to meet number and standards of restaurants, bars as prescribed by law;
dd) Failure to meet standards of kitchen area as prescribed by law;
e) Failure to meet number and standards of conference rooms, seminar rooms, meeting rooms as prescribed by law;
g) Failure to meet standards of equipment and other amenities as prescribed by law;
h) Failure to meet standards of services as prescribed by law;
i) Failure to meet standards of professional qualifications, foreign languages of managers and staff as prescribed by law.
4. A fine ranging from VND 7.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using means and equipment which fail to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to properly report to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism as prescribed by law;
c) Failing to provide additional equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages for managers and staff in accordance with the classification standards at the request of the tourism authority.
5. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to register with the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism for appraisal and classification of tourist accommodation establishments after 03 months from the date on which such establishments officially commence business activities;
b) Failing to register with the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism for appraisal and reclassification of tourist accommodation establishments before 03 months from the date on which the classification of tourist accommodation establishments expires;
c) Using images of stars affixed to tourist accommodation establishments or on publications, billboards, signboards, items and equipment used at tourist accommodation establishments which have not been recognized or are not appropriate to the recognized classification of tourist accommodation establishments.
6. A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using names of tourist accommodation establishments, transaction names or abbreviated names not conformable with names of tourist accommodation establishments registered with competent authorities for business purposes;
b) Collecting money outside the contract or other acts of illegally profiteering from tourists;
c) Collecting service charges in contravention of regulations.
7. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed for engaging in tourist accommodation business after having the operation suspended or terminated by competent authorities or after having the operation of tourist accommodation establishment suspended by competent authorities.
8. Regulations from Clauses 1 through 6 of this Article shall also apply to guest houses and hostels of ministries, central departments and local governments engaged in tourist accommodation business.

9. Additional sanctions:
Confiscating exhibits of administrative violations for violations prescribed in Point a Clause 4 and Clause 8 of this Article.
10. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign language skills of managers and attendants corresponding to standards applicable to each category and class of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law, for violations specified in Points b, c, d, dd, e, g, h and i Clause 3; Point c Clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced provision of adequate means and equipment to ensure health, life safety, and property of tourists in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
c) Enforced return of illegally collected amounts to tourists or transfer them to state budget in case of commission of the violation in Point b or Point c Clause 6 of this Article;
d) Enforced destruction of publications, billboards, signboards, equipment and devices in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 2 or Point c Clause 5 of this Article;
dd) Enforced installation of classification signs of tourist accommodation establishments in accordance with the decision on classification issued by competent authorities, for violations specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 11. Violations against regulations on sale of goods and provision of conditional services in tourist accommodation establishments
A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to register in writing with competent authorities when providing conditional goods or services at a rated tourist accommodation establishment.
Section 3. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON TRAVEL ADVISORSHIP
Article 12. Violations against regulations on tourism promotion
1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) The contents of tourist product are not consistent with the announced national tourism database;
b) Failure to implement or improper implementation of the tourism promotion program;
c) Promotion of a tourist product whose contents and quality are not satisfactory;
d) Use of tourist product without or with incorrect title and logo as prescribed by the General Department of Tourism.
2. A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of tourism promotion activities to harm legitimate rights and interests of other organizations and individuals.
3. Remedial measures:
Forcible dismantling and destruction of tourist propaganda and promotion articles, termination of tourism promotion programs, for violations specified at Points a, c and d, Clause 1 of this Article.
Section 4. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON OTHER TOURIST ACTIVITIES
Article 13. Violations of regulations on tourist resources, tourism development planning A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for acts of exploiting tourist resources in contravention of law provisions in tourist areas, tourist attractions or areas which have been planned for tourism development.
Article 14. Violations of regulations on protection of environment of tourism
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for acts of failing to post up the internal regulations on environmental protection as prescribed at places easily visible in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, means of transporting tourists.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to have public sanitation works meeting requirements for environmental sanitation maintenance in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, means of transporting tourists as prescribed;
b) Failing to classify garbage as prescribed in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, means of transporting tourists;
c) Failing to assign officials to monitor and manage environmental protection work in accordance with regulations applicable to each type or class of tourist accommodation establishment.
3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

a) Failing to collect and treat waste in accordance with regulations at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions and tourist transport means;
b) Causing damage to the living environment of wild animals in areas where tourism resources exist;
c) Cutting branches or committing other acts that cause damage to trees or vegetation in areas where tourism resources exist.
4. A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to submit annual reports on environmental protection work in accordance with regulations;
b) Failing to arrange collection and treatment equipment meeting environmental sanitation requirements at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist transport means and tourist areas or tourist attractions.
5. Additional sanctions:
Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in case of commission of the violations specified in Points b and c Clause 3 of this Article.
6. Remedial measures:
Forcible application of measures to overcome environmental pollution caused by violations at Points a and b, Clause 2; Point a, Clause 3 and Point b, Clause 4 of this Article.
Article 15. Violations against regulations on management and business activities in tourist areas and tourist attractions A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
1. Do not promulgate regulations on management of tourist areas and tourist attractions;
2. Promulgate rules and regulations of tourist areas or tourist attractions in contravention of law;
3. Violations of regulations on tourist areas or tourist attractions.
Article 16. Penalties for obstruction of inspection and examination and imposition of penalties for administrative violations
1. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for refusing to accept inspection decisions or enforcement decisions on penalties for administrative violations.
2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 7.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Failing to declare or declaring not truthfully or within the prescribed time limit at the request of law enforcement officers or competent authorities;
b) Failing to provide or providing insufficient information or documents related to the inspection, examination and imposition of penalties for administrative violations by law enforcement officers or competent authorities;
c) Obstructing the work of inspection teams or persons assigned to carry out inspection tasks;
d) Using words or actions that threaten, insult or hurt the honor of inspectors or persons carrying out inspection and handling violations.
3. A fine ranging from VND 7.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Delaying or avoiding the execution of administrative decisions and inspection decisions of competent persons or agencies;
b) Arbitrarily breaking seals on exhibits and means of violation or arbitrarily altering the scene of administrative violation;
c) Dispersing, altering or swapping exhibits under inspection, examination or temporary seizure;
d) Attacking people on duty.
4. Additional sanctions:
Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in respect of violations prescribed in Points b and c Clause 3 of this Article.
5. Remedial measures:
Enforced implementation of decisions by competent authorities and persons regarding violations prescribed in Clause 1; Points a and b Clause 2 and Point a Clause 3 of this Article.
Chapter III
                     POWER TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES
Article 17. Power to impose administrative penalties of Chairpersons of People's Committees at all levels

Chairpersons of People's Committees at all levels shall have the power to impose penalties within their competence prescribed in Clauses 4 and 5 Article 1 of the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, Article 30 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, which was amended by the 2007 Ordinance Amending a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations, for administrative violations specified in this Decree falling within local management scope.
Article 18. Power to impose administrative penalties of inspectors of Culture, Sports and Tourism
1. Inspectors of Culture, Sports and Tourism who are on duty shall have the power to:
a/ Impose warning;
b/ Impose fines of up to VND 500,000;
c) Confiscate material evidence or means used for committing administrative violations with a value of up to VND 2,000,000;
d) Apply remedial measures prescribed in Points a, b, d, dd, e, g, h, i, k, l and m Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
dd) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
2. Chief Inspector of Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism has the power to:
a) Issue warnings;
b) Impose a fine up to VND 30,000,000;
c) Suspend licenses or practicing certificates within his/her jurisdiction;
d) Confiscate material evidence or means used for committing administrative violations;
dd) Applying remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Performing other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
3. Chief Inspector of Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism has the authority to:
a) Issue warnings;
b) Impose fines up to maximum limits applicable to tourism sector;
c) Revoke licenses or practicing certificates within their jurisdiction;
d) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;
dd) Apply remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
Article 19. Power to impose penalties for administrative violations of People’s Public Security forces, Border Guard forces and Coast Guard forces
The People’s Public Security, Border Guard and Coast Guard have the power to impose penalties within their jurisdiction prescribed in Clauses 6, 7 and 8 Article 1 of the 2008 Ordinance Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and Article 37 of the 2002 Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for administrative violations against regulations on tourism directly related to their management specified in this Decree.
Article 20. Power to impose penalties of inspectors of other sectors
Within the scope of state management as prescribed by the Government, inspectors and Chief Inspectors of other inspection agencies shall have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations in the field of tourism prescribed in this Decree under their respective state management.
Article 21. Issuance of record forms and decision forms for use in handling of administrative violations The record forms and decision forms for use in handling of administrative violations against regulations on tourism are issued together with this Decree.
Chapter IV
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 22. Effect
1. This Decree takes effect on April 30, 2012.
2. This Decree replaces the Government’s Decree No. 149/2007/ND-CP of October 9, 2007 on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of tourism.
Article 23. Implementation responsibility
1. The Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall implement this Decree.

2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Presidents of People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-run cities shall implement this Decree.
