                                                  RESOLUTION

                                      ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF LAW ON LAWYERS

                      THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY OF THE SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM

     Pursuant to the 1992 Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, which was amended and supplemented under Resolution No. 51/2001/QH10 of December 25, 2001 of the Xth National Assembly, the 10th session;

                                                  RESOLVES:

   1. The Law on Lawyers, passed by the XIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 9th session on June 29, 2006, takes effect as from January 1, 2007.

  2. The Government shall organize the review of documents detailing and guiding the implementation of the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers in order to annul them and promulgate new documents for the implementation of this Law.

  3. From the effective date of the Law on Lawyers:

  a/ Persons who have been granted law practice certificates under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers may continue practicing law according to the provisions of this Law;

  b/ Lawyers being probationers under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers may continue their probation under the provisions of this Law; the probation period is counted into the probation period of law practice under the provisions of this Law and the probationer's cards are no longer valid;

  c/ Lawyer's cards issued by bar associations under the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers shall remain valid until they are replaced; the national lawyers' organizations shall guide the replacement of lawyer's cards;

  d) Within six months, lawyer’s offices and law partnership companies granted with operation registration papers under the provisions of the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers shall carry out procedures for conversion into lawyer’s offices or law partnership companies under the provisions of this Law.

  The Ministry of Justice shall guide the conversion for lawyer’s offices and law partnership companies granted with operation registration papers under the provisions of the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers;

  dd) Within six months, individuals and organizations engaged in legal services under the provisions of the 1999 Law on Enterprises which continue to provide legal services must fully satisfy conditions for practicing law and convert their organization forms of practice under the provisions of this Law; if they fail to make the conversion, they must terminate their operation.

  The Ministry of Justice shall guide the conversion for individuals and organizations engaged in legal services under the provisions of the 1999 Law on Enterprises;

  e) Foreign lawyers who have been granted with practice permits in Vietnam; branches of foreign law-practicing organizations, foreign law firms, foreign law partnership companies and Vietnam-based law partnerships granted with establishment permits under the provisions of the Government’s Decree No. 87/2003/ND-CP of July 22, 2003 on law practice by foreign law-practicing organizations and foreign lawyers in Vietnam may continue their practice under the provisions of this Law;

  g) Bar associations established under the provisions of the 2001 Ordinance on Lawyers are not required to carry out procedures for re-establishment;

  h) During the period when the National Lawyers’ Organization has not yet been established, the Ministry of Justice shall organize the examination of results of law-practice probation and the grant of lawyer’s cards by bar associations.

  4. The Government shall specify the support of funding from the State budget for provinces in mountainous areas with extremely difficult conditions for development of the contingent of lawyers and assurance of operation of bar associations.

  This Resolution was adopted on June 29, 2006 by the XIth National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam at its 9th session.
