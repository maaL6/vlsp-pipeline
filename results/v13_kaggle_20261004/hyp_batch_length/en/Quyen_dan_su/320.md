                                                    DECREE

    THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE No. 88/2003/ND-CP OF JULY 30, 2003, PROVIDING FOR THE ORGANIZATION, OPERATION AND MANAGEMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

                                                    GOV

  Pursuant to the Ordinance No. 102/SL-L004 of May 20, 1957 promulgating the Law on the Right to Establish Associations; and the Civil Code of October 28, 1995;
  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
  At the proposal of the Minister of Home Affairs,

                                                    DECREE

  Chapter 1.

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope of regulation

  1. This Decree provides for organization, operation and state management of associations.

  2. This Decree does not apply to organizations:

  a/ Vietnam Fatherland Front, Vietnam General Confederation of Labor, Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, Vietnam Peasants' Association, Vietnam War Veterans' Association, Vietnam Women's Union;

  b/ Religious organizations.

  Article 2. Associations

  1. Associations defined in this Decree are understood as voluntary organizations of Vietnamese citizens or organizations in the same industry or occupation, with the same interests or in the same gender, having the common goal of gathering and uniting members, carrying out regular activities for non-profit purposes, aiming to protect lawful rights and interests of members; assisting each other in effective operation, contributing to the national socio-economic development, and organized and operating under this Decree and other relevant legal documents.

  2. Associations have various names: unions, general associations, federations, societies, clubs with legal person status and other names prescribed by law (hereinafter referred collectively to as associations).

  3. Scope of operation of associations (territorially) includes:

  a/ Associations operating nationwide or inter-provincially;

  b/ Associations operating within provinces or centrally run cities (below referred to as provinces);

  c/ Associations operating within rural districts, urban districts, provincial capitals or towns (below referred to as districts);

  d/ Associations operating within communes, wards or district towns (below referred to as communes).

  Article 3. Establishment of associations and principles for their organization and operation

  1. The establishment of an association must be permitted by a competent state agency defined in Article 15 of this Decree.

  2. An association may be organized and operate under its charter approved by a competent state agency and not contrary to law; it shall be organized and operate on the principles of voluntariness, self-governance, self-financing and take responsibility before law.

  Article 4.- Responsibilities of State agencies toward associations

  1. State agencies shall, within the ambit of their respective functions and tasks, create favorable conditions for associations to operate according to their charters in an effective manner.

  2. Assigned associations recognized as socio-political organizations or socio-political-professional organizations, associations engaged in activities associated with the State’s tasks shall be supported by the State budget according to the Prime Minister’s regulations.

  Article 5.- Legal entity status, seals, names and symbols of associations

  1. Associations have legal entity status, seals and separate accounts.

  2. Associations may select their names and symbols according to law provisions.

  Chapter II

                                    CONDITIONS AND PROCEDURES FOR ESTABLISHMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 6.- Conditions for association establishment

  1. Having operation purposes not contrary to law; having names and main operation domains not identical with those of associations already established legally in the same territory.

  2. Having a charter.

  3. Having a head office.

  4. Having enough registered members.

  The Minister of the Interior shall stipulate the number of registered members of an association.

  Article 7.- Association establishment campaigning board

  1. To establish an association, its founders must set up an association establishment campaigning board, which is recognized by the State management agency in charge of the sector or domain in which the association is expected to operate.

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall prescribe the number of members of the Campaign Board.

  2. After being recognized, the Campaign Board shall perform the following tasks:

  a/ To mobilize citizens and organizations to join the association and complete dossiers of application for establishment;

  b/ The head of the Campaign Board shall send dossiers of application for establishment to competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 8. Dossiers of application for establishment

  1. An application for establishment.

  2. Draft Charter.

  3. Proposed operation orientation.

  4. List of members of the Campaign Board recognized by competent state agencies.

  5. Curriculum vitae of the head of the Campaign Board certified by competent agencies.

  6. Documents determining the headquarters and assets of the association.

  Article 9. Main contents of the association charter

  1. Name of the association.

  2. Objectives, scope and domain of operation of the association.

  3. Tasks and powers of the association.

  4. Organization and operation principles.

  5. Formalities for joining and leaving the association.

  6. Criteria for membership.

  7. Rights and obligations of members.

  8. Structure, organization, formalities for election and dismissal; tasks and powers of the leadership board, the inspection board and other leading posts of the association.

  9. Assets, finance and methods of managing assets and finance of the association.

  10. Conditions for dissolution and final settlement of assets and finance.

  11. Commendation and handling of violations.

  12. Formalities for amending and supplementing the Association Charter.

  13. Effect.

  Article 10. Responsibilities of competent state agencies permitting association establishment upon receiving dossiers of application for association establishment

  Competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall issue receipts when receiving dossiers of application for association establishment. If the dossiers are complete and lawful, within 60 days after receiving such complete dossiers, the competent state agencies shall reply to the association establishment campaigning boards; in case of refusal, they must issue written replies clearly stating the reasons therefor.

  Article 11. Time limit for holding association establishment congresses

  1. Within 90 days after a decision permitting association establishment takes effect, the association establishment campaigning board shall hold a congress.

  2. Past the above time limit, if the congress is not held, the association establishment campaigning board shall send a written request for extension to the state agency that has decided on the association establishment permission. The extended time limit must not exceed 30 days. Past the extended time limit, if the congress is not held, the decision permitting association establishment shall cease to be effective.

  Article 12. Principal contents of association establishment congresses

  1. Announcement of decisions permitting association establishment.

  2. Discuss and vote on the Charter.

  3. To elect the leadership and inspection boards in accordance with the association charter.

  4. To adopt the association’s operation program.

  5. To adopt the congress’s resolution.

  Article 13. Report on congress results

  Within 30 days after a congress, the leadership board of a society shall send the congress documents to the state agency which has decided to permit the establishment of the society, including:

  1. The society charter and minutes of the society charter approval;

  2. The minutes of the election of the leadership and inspection boards (enclosed with the list) and the personal background of the head of the association;

  3. Operating programs of associations;

  4. Resolutions of congresses.

  Article 14. Approval of associations charters and effect of associations charters

  1. Competent state agencies defined in Article 15 of this Decree shall decide to approve associations charters after they are passed by congresses. In case where provisions of associations charters are contrary to law, competent state agencies shall refuse to approve them and request associations to amend them.

  2. Associations charters shall take effect on the date competent state agencies decide to approve them.

  Article 15. State agencies competent to permit establishment; division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution and approval of associations charters

  1. The Minister of Home Affairs shall permit establishment; division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution and approval of charters for associations operating nationwide or inter-provincially.

  2. Chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall permit establishment; division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution and approval of charters for associations operating within provinces.

  Chapter III

                                                    MEMBER

  Article 16. Conditions for membership

  Vietnamese citizens and organizations who fully meet the membership criteria prescribed by the association charter and voluntarily apply to join the association may become its members.

  The competence and procedures for admitting members shall be prescribed in the association charter.

  Article 17. Rights and obligations of members

  Rights and obligations of members are prescribed in the society charter.

  Article 18. Associate members and honorary members

  1. Joint-venture enterprises and wholly foreign-owned enterprises (hereinafter referred to as foreign-invested enterprises) operating in Vietnam and contributing to the development of the association and agreeing with its Charter shall be considered for recognition as associate members by the association of economic organizations.

  Associate members may participate in activities and attend the congresses of the association. Associate members shall not participate in election and nomination of the leadership board of the association nor vote on matters of the association.

  The procedures for admission of associate members shall be prescribed in the association charter.

  2. Vietnamese citizens and organizations that are unable to become members of an association but contribute to the association may be recognized as associate members or honorary members by the association. The rights and obligations of associate members and honorary members shall be prescribed in the association charter.

  Chapter IV

                          ORGANIZATION, ACTIVITIES, RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE ASSOCIATION

  Article 19. Term and extraordinary congresses

  1. The highest leading body of an association is its general meeting or congress of delegates.

  2. The term of a congress shall be prescribed in the society charter but must not exceed five years.

  3. An extraordinary congress may be convened when at least two-thirds (two-thirds) of the total number of members of the Executive Committee or at least half (one-half) of the total number of official members request it.

  Article 20. Principal matters to be decided at a congress

  1. Operation orientation of the society.

  2. Election of the leadership and inspection boards.

  3. Renaming of the society and amendment of the society charter (if any).

  4. Joining of unions operating in the same field.

  5. Division, separation, merger, consolidation and dissolution of the society.

  6. Finance of associations.

  Article 21. Voting rules at congresses

  1. A congress may vote by hand or secret ballot. The congress shall decide on the form of voting.

  2. The approval of a congress decision must be voted for by more than half of the present official delegates.

  Article 22. Rights of associations

  1. To propagate their objectives.

  2. To represent members in internal and external relations related to their functions and tasks.

  3. To protect the legitimate rights and interests of the association and its members.

  4. To organize and coordinate activities among its members for the common interests of the association; to reconcile disputes within the association.

  5. To disseminate and train knowledge for its members; to provide necessary information for its members in accordance with law.

  6. To advise and critically examine issues falling within the scope of operation of the association at the request of organizations and individuals.

  7. To contribute opinions on legal documents related to the contents of its operation in accordance with law. To propose to competent state agencies matters related to the development of the association and the fields in which it operates.

  8. To coordinate with relevant agencies and organizations in performing the tasks of the association.

  9. To raise funds for the association on the basis of membership fees of its members and revenues from business and service activities in accordance with law to cover its operating expenses.

  10. To receive lawful financial supports from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals in accordance with law.

  11. An association with a nationwide or inter-provincial scope of operation may join as a member in international and regional associations in accordance with Decree No. 20/2002/ND-CP of February 20, 2002 of the Government on conclusion and implementation of international agreements by provinces and centrally-run cities, socio-political organizations, social organizations and socio-professional organizations of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  Article 23. Obligations of associations

  1. Activities of an association must comply with its Charter.

  2. An association operating in a sector shall be subject to state management by the state management agency in charge of such sector.

  3. Thirty days before holding its term congress, the leadership board of an association shall submit a written report thereon to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree and the ministry managing the sector in which the association operates.

  4. The establishment of a representative office of an association in another locality requires permission from the provincial-level People’s Committee of the locality where such representative office is located and a written report thereon must be submitted to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  5. When changing the association president, vice president or secretary general, or when changing its head office or amending or supplementing its Charter, the association shall submit a report thereon to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  6. The establishment of legal entities under an association must comply with law and must be reported to the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree.

  7. Annually, an association shall report on its organization and operation to the competent state agency and the state management agency in charge of the sector in which it operates, no later than December 1 every year.

  8. To abide by the guidance, inspection and examination of competent state agencies on law observance.

  9. The list of members, branches, affiliated units, financial documents of the association, minutes of meetings of the leadership board shall be made in dossiers and kept at the association head office.

  10. The funds collected as prescribed in Clauses 9 and 10, Article 22 of this Decree must be used for association activities according to the association charter and not distributed to members.

  11. The use of association funds must comply with law provisions. Every year, the association must report its financial settlement according to State regulations and send it to finance agencies of the same level.

  Chapter 5

                             DIVISION, SEPARATION; MERGER; MERGE AND DISOLUTION OF ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 24. Division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution

  1. Depending on the requirements and operation capability of the association, the leadership board shall propose the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree to permit the division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution of the association. The division, separation; merger; consolidation; dissolution of the association shall comply with law provisions.

  2. The association shall be dissolved in the following cases:

  a) Self-dissolution;

  b) Request competent state agencies specified in Article 15 of this Decree to decide on dissolution.

  Article 25. Self-dissolution of associations

  The association shall dissolve in the following cases:

  1. Upon expiry of operation duration;

  2. At the request of more than half of the total number of official members;

  3. Completed objectives.

  Article 26.- Responsibilities of the leadership board when the society dissolves itself

  1. To send to the competent State agency defined in Article 15 of this Decree the following documents:

  a/ The written request for dissolution of the society;

  b/ The resolution on dissolution of the society;

  c/ The statement on assets and finance;

  d/ The proposed mode of disposal of assets and finance and the time limit for paying debts.

  2. To notify the time limit for paying debts (if any) to concerned organizations and individuals under law on five consecutive issues of a central newspaper for a society operating nationwide or inter-provincially; or local newspapers for a society operating within a province.

  Article 27.- Decision on dissolution of societies

  The competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall decide on the dissolution of the association after 15 days from the date of expiration of the debt payment and liquidation period stated in the association's application for dissolution without any complaint.

  The association shall terminate its operation as from the effective date of the decision of the competent state agency on its dissolution.

  Article 28.- Dissolved associations

  The association is dissolved under the decision of a competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree in the following cases:

  1. The association fails to operate for 12 consecutive months;

  2. When a congress resolution on dissolution is passed but the association leadership board fails to comply with it;

  3. The association’s activities seriously violate law.

  Article 29. Responsibilities of competent state agencies upon association dissolution

  When a society is dissolved, the competent state agency specified in Article 15 of this Decree shall:

  a/ Issue a decision on dissolution of the society;

  b/ Notify the decision on dissolution of the society on the mass media.

  Article 30. Settlement of assets and finance upon dissolution, consolidation, merger or division or separation of a society

  1. Upon dissolution or liquidation of a society, its assets shall be settled as follows:

  a/ Assets and finance donated by domestic and foreign organizations; assets and finance supported by the State which have been fully used for performance of obligations regarding assets and payment of debts, the remaining assets and financial balance shall be decided by the competent state agency;

  b/ For self-owned assets and finance of the society which have been fully used for performance of obligations regarding assets and payment of debts after the society is dissolved, the remaining assets and financial balance shall be decided by the society under its Charter.

  2. Upon consolidation of a society, its assets and finance shall be settled as follows:

  a/ After the merger of associations into new associations, the old associations cease to exist and the new associations enjoy all lawful rights and interests and are responsible for unpaid debts and service contracts which the old associations are currently performing;

  b/ The merged associations' assets and finance shall not be divided or transferred but shall be entirely transferred to the new associations.

  3. Settlement of assets and finance of associations upon their merger:

  a/ When an association merges with another association, its assets and finance shall be transferred to the merging association;

  b/ The merging association enjoys all lawful rights and interests in its existing assets and finance and is responsible for unpaid debts and service contracts it is currently performing.

  4. Settlement of assets and finance of associations upon their division or separation:

  a/ After a society divides itself, the divided society ceases to operate and its assets and finance rights and obligations are transferred to the new society under the society division decision;

  b/ After a society separates itself, its members shall perform their assets and finance rights and obligations in accordance with the purpose of operation of such society.

  Article 31. Right to complain

  In case a society is dissolved without its consent, it may lodge a complaint according to law. While waiting for the settlement of the complaint, the society shall not operate.

  Chapter 6.

                                      STATE MANAGEMENT OF ASSOCIATIONS

  Article 32. State management of associations

  1. To formulate or promulgate legal documents on associations.

  2. To guide ministries, sectors, localities, associations and citizens in implementing the law on associations.

  3. To permit the establishment, division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the society charter as prescribed in Article 15 of this Decree.

  4. To provide professional guidance for officials and public employees engaged in the management of associations.

  5. To disseminate the association law.

  6. To inspect and examine the observance of the association law; to examine the implementation of the association charters by associations.

  7. To manage the conclusion of international cooperation on associations under law.

  8. To settle complaints and denunciations and handle violations of the association law.

  9. To summarize and report on the organization, operation and management of associations.

  The Ministry of Home Affairs shall assist the Government in performing the unified state management over associations nationwide.

  Article 33.- State management tasks of ministries and ministerial-level agencies regarding associations operating in domains under their management nationwide

  1. To sign with competent state agencies in accordance with Article 15 of this Decree on the permission for establishment, division, separation, merger, consolidation, dissolution and approval of the society charter, recognition of the society establishment campaigning board.

  2. To guide and create conditions for the society to participate in activities under the management of the Ministry as prescribed by law; to organize collection of opinions from the society to complete regulations on state management over the sector or domain.

  3. To inspect the implementation of regulations on state management over the sector or domain for the society, to handle or propose competent state agencies to handle violations in accordance with law.

  Article 34. State management tasks of provincial-level People’s Committees for societies operating within provinces

  1. To exercise their competence as prescribed in Clause 2, Article 15 of this Decree and perform state management over the organization and operation of the society.

  2. To inspect and examine the observance of the law on society.

  3. To settle complaints and denunciations and handle violations of the law on society.

  4. To consider providing support for societies operating within localities.

  5. To consider and permit associations operating within localities to receive donations from domestic and overseas organizations and individuals in accordance with law.

  6. To direct provincial/municipal departments, sectors, People’s Committees of districts and communes in managing associations.

  7. Annually to review and report to the Ministry of Home Affairs on the organization, operation and management of associations in localities.

  Chapter VII

                                    COMMENDATION AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 35.- Commendation

  1. Associations that make great contributions to socio-economic development shall be commended and/or rewarded according to the State’s regulations.

  2. Members who record many achievements shall be commended and/or rewarded according to the association’s and State’s regulations.

  Article 36.- Handling of violations

  1. Those who violate the right to establish associations or abuse the association name to conduct illegal activities shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, be disciplined, administratively sanctioned or examined for penal liability according to law provisions; in case of causing material damage, they must pay compensations therefor according to law provisions.

  2. Those who abuse their positions and powers to permit the establishment of associations in contravention of this Decree shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, be disciplined, administratively sanctioned or examined for penal liability according to law provisions; in case of causing material damage, they must pay compensations therefor according to law provisions.

  3. The association leaderships or representatives who deliberately prolong the term of the congresses under the association charters or fail to observe the regulations on the obligations of associations shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, be handled according to law provisions.

  Chapter VIII

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 37.- Implementation effect

  1. This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

  2. This Decree replaces Decree No. 258-TTg of June 14, 1957 of the Prime Minister detailing the implementation of Law No. 102-SL-004 of May 20, 1957 on the right to establish associations.

  3. Associations established under the previous permission of the Ministry of Home Affairs and the provincial executive committees according to the Ordinance No. 102-SL-004 of May 20, 1957 and permitted by the Chairman of the Council of Ministers (now the Prime Minister) or the Minister or Head of the Government Organization and Personnel Commission (now the Minister of Home Affairs), the President of the People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government according to Directive No. 01/CT of January 5, 1989 on the management, organization and operation of mass associations shall not have to apply for re-establishment.

  4. The association of economic organizations established under Decision No. 38-HDBT of April 10, 1989 of the Council of Ministers (now the Government) on economic association in production, circulation and services shall not have to apply for re-establishment.

  Article 38. Implementation organization

  The Minister of Home Affairs shall guide the implementation of this Decree.

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and presidents of the People's Committees of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government shall implement this Decree.
