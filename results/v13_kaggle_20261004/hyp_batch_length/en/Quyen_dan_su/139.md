                                                   CIRCULAR

  GUIDANCE ON ISSUANCE OF VISAS, PERMANENT RESIDENCE PERMITS AND PERMANENT RESIDENCE CARDS TO FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM UNDER THE EXPANSION OF THE MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS

  Pursuant to the Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;

  Pursuant to the Law on promulgation of legal documents dated June 3, 2008;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 58/2013/ND-CP dated June 11, 2013 defining functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

  Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP dated August 6, 2015 defining the mechanism for cooperation among ministries, ministerial-level agencies and People’s Committees of provinces and centrally-affiliated cities in management of entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam;

  The Minister of Foreign Affairs promulgates the Circular guiding procedures for issuance of visas, issuance of temporary residence cards and extension of temporary residence for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  Chapter I

                                              GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Circular provides guidelines on issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards for foreigners in Vietnam under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs as prescribed in Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, Clause 4 Article 8 of Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “Law on Entry and Exit”).

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Circular applies to foreigners under management of Ministry of Foreign Affairs entering and residing in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.

  Chapter II

              AUTHORITY AND PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE OF VISA, EXTENSION OF TEMPORARY RESIDENCE, ISSUANCE OF TEMPORARY RESIDENCE CARDS

  Article 3. Authority of relevant agencies in issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence, and issuance of temporary residence cards

  1. Consular Department, Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as “Consular Department”) shall receive and process application/notification for issuance of visas, extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3, Clause 4 Article 8 of Law on Entry and Exit.

  2. The State Protocol Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the State Protocol Department) shall receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration (except for members of foreign consular missions in Ho Chi Minh City and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants).

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City):

  a) Receive and process applications for visas and extension of temporary residence for foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration in provinces and cities from Quang Nam and Da Nang southward.

  b) Receive and process applications for temporary residence cards for foreigners who are members of foreign consular missions in Ho Chi Minh City and their spouses, children under 18 years old, and accompanying servants.

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative missions:

  a) Receive applications for visa issuance according to Clause 2 Article 4 of this Circular, passports or passport substitute documents (hereinafter referred to as passports), and application forms for visa issuance (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 5, 2015 of the Ministry of Public Security on forms related to entry, exit, and residence of foreigners in Vietnam - hereinafter referred to as Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration to transfer information to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for processing within their jurisdiction.

  b) Issue visas to foreigners specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration according to notices of the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  Article 4. Procedures for processing visa issuance applications

  1. Agencies and organizations that invite or guarantee entry of foreigners into Vietnam as prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 Article 15 of Law on Immigration shall directly submit notice/dossier of application for visa issuance to Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs
  TP. HCM.

  The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (or other agencies as prescribed by the sending country) of the country where the foreigner is located, who falls under the provisions of Clauses 1, 2, 3, and 4, Article 8 of the Law on Immigration, may send the application for visa issuance, along with the passport and the declaration form for visa issuance (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the relevant foreigner to the Vietnamese representative mission in the host country or the concurrent country.

  Vietnam’s overseas representative missions shall, within 1 working day from the date on which they receive all documents mentioned above, forward them to the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City for processing and issuing visas within their jurisdiction.

  2. The application for issuance of a visa includes:

  a) A written notice or diplomatic note requesting visa issuance, specifying full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and symbol, issuance date and expiry date, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa duration, visa type and visa-accepting country of the foreigner.

  b) In case a foreigner visits a member of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an additional copy of the identity card of the relevant member of the representative mission is required.

  c) In case the foreigner is a contractual employee working at a Vietnamese foreign representative office, in addition to the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, an additional copy of the valid employment contract is required.

  d) In case a foreigner is subject to the increase in the staff of a Vietnamese representative mission, apart from the documents specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article, it is required to supplement a diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country notifying the increase in the staff.

  3. Settlement of visa application:

  a) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security (hereinafter referred to as the Immigration Department) and the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit within 01 working day from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier.

  b) The Consular Department shall send a written request for visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit to the Immigration Department, the State Protocol Department and relevant units within 01 working day from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier. After receiving a complete written reply from the State Protocol Department and relevant units, if the Immigration Department does not have any opinion, the Consular Department shall reply to the visa-issuing agency or organization and at the same time notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to the foreigners.

  c) The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall notify in writing the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Public Security within 01 working day from the receipt of a complete and valid dossier requesting visa issuance to foreigners specified in Clause 4 Article 8 of the Law on Entry and Exit.

  The Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall reply to the visa-issuing agency or organization and at the same time notify the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to the foreigners after 02 working days from the date of notification to the Immigration Department if this agency does not have any opinion.

  d) The notification of the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City to the overseas Vietnamese representative mission of the visa issuance to the foreigners shall include the following information: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, number and symbol of passport, date of issue - expiry date of passport, occupation, position, entry purpose, visa duration, visa type, place of visa acceptance of the foreigners and other information (if any).

  4. Issuance of visas:

  a) Overseas Vietnamese representative missions shall issue visas to foreigners within 01 working day from receipt of notice specified in Point d Clause 3 of this Article, passport and visa application form (using Form NA1 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA) of the foreigners.

  b) In case a foreigner is approved for issuance of visa at border checkpoint, the receiving agency specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall notify the agency or organization that notifies/presents the visa application about the foreigner’s acceptance of visa at border checkpoint.

  5. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize Director of Consular Department to decide reciprocal treatment with foreign parties regarding processing time as well as components of visa application in accordance with Immigration Law.

  Article 5. Procedures for processing application for extension of temporary residence

  1. Foreigners who have been issued with visas marked NG1, NG2 and NG4, if they wish to extend their temporary residence after entering Vietnam, must send notice/application for extension of temporary residence to Consular Department or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City through agencies or organizations that invite and guarantee foreigners to enter Vietnam.

  2. Application for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Written document or diplomatic note on application for extension of temporary residence, which specifies: full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, number and symbol of passport, date of issue - expiry date of passport, occupation, position, purpose of entry, duration of application for extension of temporary residence and need for issuance of new visa (if any) of the foreigners.

  b) The passport of the foreigner is still valid for more than 30 days compared to the duration of application for extension of temporary residence.

  c) The application form for issuance of visa or extension of temporary residence (using Form NA5 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA), containing certification and seal of the agency or organization that invites or guarantees the foreigner.

  d) For a foreigner who visits a member of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, an copy of identity card of this member issued by the State Protocol Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City is required.

  e) For a foreigner who is a contractual employee working at a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, a copy of the valid employment contract is required.

  3. Processing applications for extension of temporary residence:

  a) Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider extending the temporary residence and issue a new visa (if necessary) for the foreigner.

  b) The duration of temporary residence that is extended and the duration of the new visa that is issued (if any) must be appropriate to the entry purposes and request of the agency or organization that invites or guarantees the foreigner and must not exceed 12 months, which is at least 30 days shorter than the duration of the passport of the foreigner.

  c) For those specified in Point d Clause 2 of this Article, the duration of temporary residence that is extended and the new visa that is issued (if any) must comply with the provisions of Point b Clause 3 of this Article and must be appropriate to the duration of identity card of the member of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam issued by the State Protocol Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  4. Foreigners who have been granted visa-free entry to Vietnam and fall under the cases specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration may be considered for one or more extensions of temporary residence by the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City on the basis of the application submitted according to Clause 2 of this Article, unless otherwise provided by an international agreement to which Vietnam is a signatory.

  5. The Director General of the Consular Department shall decide the extension of temporary residence and grant new visas (if necessary) for foreigners falling under the cases specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration who have entered Vietnam with SQ visas.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director General of the Consular Department to decide the reciprocal extension of temporary residence for foreigners in accordance with the Law on Immigration.

  Article 6. Procedures for processing applications for temporary residence cards

  1. Agencies that invite and guarantee foreigners specified in Clause 3 Article 8 of the Law on Immigration shall submit applications for temporary residence cards to competent agencies specified in Article 3 of this Circular.

  2. An application for a temporary residence card includes:

  a) A diplomatic note requesting the issuance of a temporary residence card from a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, specifying the applicant’s full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number and issue date, occupation and position (refer to Form NA6 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA);

  In case of replacement or addition of staff of a foreign representative mission in Vietnam, the diplomatic note requesting the issuance of a temporary residence card must specify the full name and title of the person replaced or added;

  b) The application form for issuance of temporary residence card (using Form NA8 enclosed with Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA), bearing certification of competent persons and seal of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam;

  c) Valid passport of the foreigner;

  d) In case the foreigner is subject to increase in headcount of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam, apart from documents specified in Points a, b, and c Clause 1 of this Article, the diplomatic note of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the sending country must be added to inform the increase in headcount.

  3. Issuance of temporary residence cards:

  Within 05 working days from the date on which the satisfactory application is received as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article, the Department of State Protocol or Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall consider issuing temporary residence cards to foreigners.

  4. Temporary residence cards shall bear the code NG3, have a term of not exceeding 05 years and be shorter than the term of the passport by at least 30 days.

  When the validity of the temporary residence card expires, it shall be considered for issuance of a new one at the request of the foreign representative mission in Vietnam.

  5. The Department of State Protocol and Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall transfer the list of persons issued with temporary residence cards to immigration authorities according to regulations in Clause 2 Article 7 of Decree No. 64/2015/ND-CP of the Government on cooperation between ministries, ministerial agencies, People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities in management of entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam.

  6. The Minister of Foreign Affairs shall decide or authorize the Director of the State Protocol Department to decide on reciprocal treatment with foreign parties regarding the time limit for processing dossiers as well as the contents of dossiers applying for issuance of temporary residence cards in accordance with the Law on Entry and Exit.

  Chapter III

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 7. Transitional provisions

  1. Visas, certificates of temporary residence and temporary residence cards which are valid before the effective date of this Circular may continue to be used until their expiry dates.

  2. Dossiers applying for issuance of visas, certificates of temporary residence and temporary residence cards or extension of temporary residence duration which have been submitted before the effective date of this Circular but have not yet been processed shall be processed by competent agencies of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in accordance with this Circular.

  Article 8. Implementation organization

  1. The Consular Department shall take responsibility:

  a) Guide Vietnamese representative offices in foreign countries and relevant agencies to grant visas and extend temporary residence according to this Circular.

  b) Annually report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the situation of visa granting and extension of temporary residence according to this Circular.

  2. The State Protocol Department shall be responsible for:

  a) Guiding foreign representative offices in Vietnam and relevant agencies on procedures for granting temporary residence cards according to this Circular.

  b) Annually report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs on the situation of granting temporary residence cards according to this Circular.

  3. The Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City shall be responsible for monthly, quarterly and annual reports on visa granting and extension of temporary residence for the Consular Department; and on the granting of temporary residence cards for the State Protocol Department.

  4. Overseas Vietnamese representative offices shall be responsible for:

  a) Granting visas according to notices of the Consular Department or the Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City.

  b/ To send monthly, quarterly and annual reports on visa granting to foreigners under the management of the Ministry for Foreign Affairs to the Consular Department.

  Article 9. Effect

  This Circular takes effect on September 1, 2016.

  Article 10. Implementation responsibilities

  The Director of Consular Department, Director of State Protocol Department, Director of Department of Foreign Affairs of Ho Chi Minh City and Heads of Vietnam representative offices located overseas and units affiliated to the Ministry are responsible for implementation of this Circular. Any problems arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (via Consular Department in case of procedures for issuance of visas and extension of temporary residence; via State Protocol Department in case of procedures for issuance of temporary residence cards) for timely guidance./.
