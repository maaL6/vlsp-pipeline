                                               JOINT CIRCULAR

     BY THE MINISTRY OF JUSTICE AND MINISTRY OF PUBLIC SECURITY

NO. 07/1999/TTLT-BTP-BCA OF FEBRUARY 8, 1999

PROVIDING FOR ISSUANCE OF LEGAL NOTARIZATION CERTIFICATES

  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 38-CP of June 4, 1993 on the functions, tasks, powers and organizational apparatus of the Ministry of Justice;
  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 37/1998/ND-CP of June 9, 1998 on the functions, tasks, powers and organizational apparatus of the Ministry of Public Security;
  In order to meet citizens' requirements for issuance of criminal record certificates;
  The Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Public Security shall stipulate the issuance of criminal record certificates as follows:

  I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

  1. Criminal record certificate A criminal record certificate is a document issued by a competent state agency specified in Point 5 Section I of this Joint Circular to a requester for confirmation of whether or not he/she has a previous conviction.

  The Judicial record form is Form No. 01/TP-LLTP promulgated together with this Joint Circular.

  2. The principle of confirmation of presence or absence of previous convictions

  A person shall be considered having a criminal record only when he/she has been sentenced by a court and his/her criminal record has not yet been expunged under law.

  3. Right to request issuance of judicial record cards Vietnamese citizens may request competent State agencies to issue judicial record cards for use in necessary cases according to law provisions.

  Foreigners may also request competent State agencies of Vietnam specified at Point 5, Section I of this Joint Circular to issue judicial record cards to certify whether or not they have any previous criminal conviction during their stay in Vietnam.

  Requesters for issuance of judicial record cards may authorize other persons to carry out procedures for requesting issuance of judicial record cards on their behalf.

  4. Non-issuance of judicial record cards The following cases shall not be issued with judicial record cards:

  a/ Applications for issuance of judicial record cards are filed by others without lawful authorization;

  b/ Persons who are currently accused or defendants in criminal cases.

  5. Competence to issue judicial record cards The issuance of judicial record cards shall fall under the jurisdiction of provincial/municipal Justice Services (below referred to as provincial/municipal Justice Services).

  Provincial/municipal Justice Services shall coordinate with provincial-level Police Departments (below referred to as provincial Police Departments) and, when necessary, with courts in verifying the judicial records of involved parties.

  6. Fees

  An applicant for a judicial record card shall pay a fee as prescribed by law.

  II. PROCEDURES AND ORDER FOR ISSUANCE OF LEGAL RECORD

  1. Procedures for submission and receipt of applications for issuance of criminal record certificates

  a) Application for issuance of criminal record certificates The applicant for issuance of a criminal record certificate shall file an application according to Form No. 02/TP-LLTTP promulgated together with this Joint Circular; the application for issuance of a criminal record certificate must be fully, clearly and accurately declared.

  Enclosed with the application shall be copies of identity cards and household registration books or other valid documents evidencing the places of residence of the applicants; for foreigners, copies of their passports and certificates of permanent or temporary residence in Vietnam.

  In case of authorization for carrying out procedures to request issuance of judicial record cards, there must also be a written authorization certified by the People's Committee of the commune, ward or township where the authorizing person or authorized person resides; if the authorizing person is a Vietnamese citizen residing abroad, the written authorization must be certified by the diplomatic mission or consular office of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam in such country; if the authorizing person is a foreigner who has left Vietnam, the written authorization must be certified by the competent authority of the country of which he/she is a citizen or a country of permanent residence and must be legalized by consular procedures in accordance with Vietnamese law.

  The application for issuance of a criminal record certificate shall be made in 2 sets.

  b/ Submission of dossiers requesting the grant of criminal record certificates The applicant for a criminal record certificate who is a Vietnamese citizen residing in the country shall submit his/her dossier to the provincial-level Justice Department of the locality where he/she resides; if he/she resides abroad, his/her dossier shall be submitted to the provincial-level Justice Department of the last locality where he/she resided before leaving the country.

  An applicant for a judicial record card who is a foreigner residing in Vietnam shall submit his/her dossier to the provincial-level Justice Department of the locality where he/she resides; if he/she has left Vietnam, his/her dossier shall be submitted to the provincial-level Justice Department of the last locality where he/she resided before leaving Vietnam.

  c/ Receipt of dossiers

  Upon receiving the application, the Department of Justice shall verify the contents stated in the application and the validity of accompanying documents; if any details are insufficient or unclear, the applicant is requested to provide additional information or clarify such details.

  If the application is considered satisfactory, the Department of Justice shall receive it, collect fees and issue a notice of appointment date to the applicant.

  In case where the matter is considered not falling under their jurisdiction, the Department of Justice shall immediately guide the involved parties to submit the dossiers according to the correct address.

  The receipt of the application shall be recorded in the Judicial record card issuing register.

  2. Procedures for searching and verifying criminal records

  a) Searching police records Within 3 days from the date on which the complete and valid application is received, the Department of Justice shall send a judicial record verification form using Form No. 03/TP-LLTP enclosed with this Joint Circular and 1 set of application to the provincial police authority for searching.

  Within 10 days after receiving a judicial record verification note, the provincial-level police office shall search and verify the involved party's judicial record and send to the provincial-level Justice Department a notice of results of the verification (on the back of the judicial record verification note). For complicated cases requiring search of records at the Police Records Department under the Ministry of Public Security, this time limit may be extended but by not more than 10 days; the provincial-level police office shall contact the Police Records Department.

  b/ Searching for the Court’s dossiers

  In case the results of the search of the police record show that there is a suspicious circumstance that the involved party has a previous conviction but there are not enough grounds to make a conclusion or the part about the previous conviction of the involved party is unclear and incomplete, the Department of Justice shall contact the court that has tried the case related to the involved party to search the judgment record in order to clarify whether the involved party has a previous conviction or not.

  The search of the Court's file (if any) must be conducted immediately after the receipt of the notice on the results of verification of the judicial record by the provincial-level police office; the time limit for searching the file is seven days.

  3. Compiling and issuing criminal record certificates Within 3 days after obtaining the search results of the police office or court (if any), the provincial-level Justice Department shall compile and issue a criminal record certificate to the concerned party.

  a/ How to make judicial record cards An involved party having no previous criminal conviction (having not yet been sentenced or having a criminal conviction but his/her conviction has been expunged) shall be clearly inscribed with the word "No previous criminal conviction" in the line "previous criminal conviction status" and crossed the columns (1), (2), (3), (4) and (5) of the judicial record card; if he/she has a previous criminal conviction, the crime, applicable legal provisions, principal and additional penalties (if any) and the date the judgment takes effect shall be clearly inscribed; if he/she has many previous criminal convictions, these convictions shall be listed in the order of time.

  In case an involved party has a criminal conviction but is eligible for automatic expungement of the conviction or for conditional expungement of the conviction as prescribed by the penal legislation, the provincial/municipal Justice Service shall guide him/her in carrying out procedures to request the competent People's Court to expunge the conviction according to the provisions of the criminal procedure legislation.

  b/ Issuance of judicial record cards A judicial record card is only one original and its duplication is not permitted. Depending on the purpose of requesting issuance of a judicial record card, the provincial/municipal Justice Service shall issue the necessary number of cards to the involved parties.

  4. Archiving of judicial record dossiers Judicial record dossiers include applications for issuance of judicial record cards, the involved parties' enclosed papers and judicial record cards showing the results of dossier search by the police office or the court (if any).

  Judicial record dossiers must be archived and preserved at the provincial/municipal Justice Services according to the law provisions on archives.

  III. ORGANIZATION OF IMPLEMENTATION

  1. The Minister of Justice and the Minister of Public Security shall have to direct and guide their respective branches in implementing this Joint Circular.

  2. Provincial/municipal Justice Services and Public Security Services shall appoint professional officers to do the judicial record work; provinces and cities with many requests for issuance of judicial record cards shall appoint officers specialized in this work.

  3. Every six months and annually, the Departments of Justice shall report to the Ministry of Justice; the provincial police departments shall report to the Ministry of Public Security on the implementation of this Joint Circular.

  4. This Joint Circular takes effect 15 days after its signing.

  Any problems arising in the course of implementation should be reported to the Ministry of Justice or the Ministry of Public Security for consideration and settlement.
