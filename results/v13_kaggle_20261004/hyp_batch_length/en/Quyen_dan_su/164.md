                                           CIRCULAR
   GUIDANCE ON PROVISION OF VISAS, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE PERMITS, PERMITS FOR EXIT FROM VIETNAM AND RESIDENCE SOLUTIONS FOR FOREIGNERS IN VIETNAM
Pursuant to the Law on entry, exit, transit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam dated June 16, 2014;
Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 106/2014/ND-CP dated November 17, 2014 on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Security;
At the request of the Director General of General Department of Security, The Minister of Public Security promulgates a Circular providing guidelines for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits and permanent residence for foreigners in Vietnam.
Chapter I
                                       GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
This Circular provides guidance on issuance of visas, temporary residence cards, entry and exit permits, and permanent residence for foreigners in Vietnam under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Public Security.
Article 2. Regulated entities
This Circular applies to foreigners entering and residing in Vietnam and relevant agencies, organizations and individuals.
Chapter II
 PROCEDURES AND AUTHORITY TO HANDLE APPLICATION FOR VISA, TEMPORARY RESIDENCE CARD, PERMANENT RESIDENCE
Article 3. Procedures and competence in processing visa application
1. Agencies, organizations and individuals that guarantee entry of foreigners into Vietnam according to Clause 1 Article 16 of Law on Entry, Exit, Transit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as “Law”) shall submit application at Immigration Department.
2. The visa application form made by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 shall be used to apply for visas to Vietnam:
a) Form NA2 is used for agencies and organizations;
b) Form NA3 is used for individuals.
3. Visa application processing:
a) Immigration Department shall consider and process visa applications as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 16 of the Law;
b) Immigration Department shall consider and process visa applications at border checkpoints as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 16 of the Law.
Article 4. Procedures and authority for processing applications for temporary residence cards
1. Foreigners eligible for temporary residence cards as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 36 of the Law must apply through agencies, organizations or individuals that have directly invited or guaranteed them to carry out procedures at Immigration Department or Immigration Division of police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities where agencies and organizations invite or guarantee their headquarters to be located or where individuals invite or guarantee them to reside.
2. Application for issuance of temporary residence card as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law. Written application for issuance of temporary residence card specified in Point a Clause 1 Article 37 of the Law using form promulgated by the Ministry of Public Security in Circular No. 04/2015/TT-BCA dated January 05, 2015 on forms related to entry, exit and residence of foreigners in Vietnam including:

                                                                                                    1

a) Forms NA6 and NA8 used for agencies and organizations;
b) Forms NA7 and NA8 are used for individuals.
3. Grant temporary residence cards
Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of a complete application, the Immigration Department or Immigration Division of the police authority of the province/centrally affiliated city shall consider issuing a temporary residence card.
4. Duration of temporary residence cards shall be issued in conformity with entry purposes and request of agencies, organizations and individuals. Duration of temporary residence cards is between 1 year and 5 years but shorter than duration of passports at least 30 days.
Article 5. Settlement of permanent residence
1. Procedures and competence for settlement of permanent residence shall comply with Articles 41 and 42 of the Law.
2. Determination of period of temporary residence in Vietnam for foreigners applying for permanent residence:
a) Foreigners who have been temporarily residing in Vietnam for 3 years or more as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 40 of the Law shall be determined on the basis of the entry and exit check stamps issued at border gates with a total temporary residence period in Vietnam of 3 years or more in the 4 years prior to the date of application submission.
b) Documents proving continuous temporary residence in Vietnam before 2000 as prescribed in Point b Clause 1 Article 42 of the Law include one of the following documents: Certificate of temporary residence; temporary residence registration book; written explanation of the period of temporary residence in Vietnam certified by the police of the commune, ward, or town where the stateless person temporarily resides.
3. Location for submission of applications for permanent residence:
a) Applicants for permanent residence as prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 Article 39 of the Law shall submit their applications at Immigration Department.
b) Applicants for permanent residence as prescribed in Clauses 3 and 4 Article 39 of the Law shall submit their applications at Immigration Division of Police of province or central-affiliated city where they apply for permanent residence.
Chapter III
 PROCEDURES FOR ISSUANCE AND REISSUANCE OF PERMITS FOR NON-NATIONAL RESIDERS IN VIETNAM
Article 6. Entry and exit permits
1. International travel documents specified in Point i Clause 1 Article 44 of the Law are entry and exit permits issued by Immigration Department to stateless persons residing in Vietnam who wish to leave Vietnam (using form NC13 enclosed with this Circular).
2. Entry and exit permits shall be valid for 3 years; entry and exit permits that are lost or damaged may be re-issued.
Article 7. Procedures for issuance and reissuance of entry and exit permits
1. Applicants for issuance or reissuance of entry and exit permits shall submit 1 application at Immigration Department or Immigration Division of police authority of province or central-affiliated city where they reside. Application consists of:
a) Declaration for issuance or reissuance of entry and exit permit (using form NC14 enclosed with this Circular);
b) Photocopies of permanent residence cards enclosed with original copies for verification if holders have been issued with permanent residence cards;
c) Written explanation regarding temporary residence in Vietnam certified by police of commune, ward or town where stateless persons temporarily reside if they have not been permitted to permanently reside;
d) Issued entry and exit permits, in case of application for re-issuance due to damaged entry and exit permits;
dd) Report on lost entry and exit permits, in case of application for re-issuance due to lost entry and exit permits (no need for certification by competent authorities where the permits are lost).
2. Handling issuance and re-issuance of entry and exit permits:
a) In case of submission of dossiers at Immigration Department:


                                                                                                       2

Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of complete dossiers, Immigration Department shall consider issuing or re-issuing entry and exit permits.
b) In case of submission of dossiers at Immigration Sub-departments of police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities:
Within 3 working days from the date of receipt of complete dossiers, Immigration Sub-departments of police authorities of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall consider and transfer dossiers requesting issuance or re-issuance of entry and exit permits to Immigration Department. Immigration Department shall consider issuing or re-issuing entry and exit permits within 2 working days.
Chapter IV
                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 8. Transitional provisions
1. The entry and exit permits which are still valid before the effective date of this Circular may be used until their expiry dates.
2. Applications for issuance of visas, temporary residence cards; issuance and reissuance of entry and exit permits; settlement of permanent residence applications submitted before the effective date of this Circular but not yet processed shall be considered and settled by immigration authorities according to regulations in this Circular.
Article 9. Effect
1. This Circular takes effect on August 20, 2015.
2. This Circular replaces the Circular No. 45/2011/TT-BCA dated June 29, 2011 of the Minister of Public Security guiding the issuance of entry and exit permits for foreigners permanently residing in Vietnam who do not have passports.
Article 10. Implementation responsibilities
1. The General Department of Security shall take charge of guiding police units, local authorities and other relevant units to implement this Circular.
2. Directors General of General Departments, Heads of units under the Ministry, Directors of Police Departments, Fire and Rescue Police Departments of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall be responsible for implementing this Circular. Any problems arising during the implementation of this Circular should be reported to the Ministry (via the Immigration Department - General Department of Security) for timely guidance./.
