                                                  DECREE

     PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY; THRIFT AND WASTEFREEING PRACTICES; NATIONAL RESERVES; STATE BANKING

  The Government’s Decree No. 63/2019/ND-CP dated July 11, 2019 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift and wastefreeing practices; national reserves; state banking shall take effect from September 1, 2019 and be amended by:

  Decree No. 102/2021/ND-CP dated November 16, 2021 of the Government on amendments to some Articles of Decrees on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on tax and invoices; customs; insurance business and lottery business; management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury; independent accounting and audit; comes into force from January 01, 2022.

  Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Actions against administrative violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Law on Management and Use of Public Property dated June 21, 2017;

  Pursuant to the Law on State Budget dated June 25, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on Thrift Practice and Wastefulness Combat dated November 26, 2013;

  Pursuant to the Law on National Reserve dated November 20, 2012;

  At the request of the Minister of Finance;

  The Government promulgates Decrees on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury.[2]

  Chapter I

                                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, remedial measures, power to record administrative violations and power to impose penalties for administrative violations in the following fields:

  a) Management and use of public property at agencies, organizations and units; property of projects funded by state budget; property under public ownership and property of infrastructure invested and managed by the State;

  b) Thrift practice and wastefulness combat;

  c) National reserve;

  d) State Treasury.

  2. Administrative violations related to management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury in terms of accounting, construction, investment planning, valuation, auction and other regulations not specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall be sanctioned according to relevant laws.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property; thrift practice against wastefulness; national reserve; state treasury.

  2. Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) State agencies;

  b) Units of the People’s Armed Forces;

  c) Public service providers;

  d) Agencies of the Communist Party of Vietnam;

  dd) Socio-political organizations, socio-political-professional organizations, social organizations, socio-professional organizations, and other organizations established under the law on associations;

  e) Other relevant enterprises, organizations, and individuals.

  3. Persons having power to impose penalties and organizations and individuals involved in the imposition of administrative penalties as prescribed in this Decree.

  4. In case officials or public employees commit violations while performing their official duties, if such violations fall within their assigned official duties prescribed in legislative documents or administrative documents issued by competent authorities or persons, they shall not be administratively sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with the law on officials, public employees.

  If a regulatory authority commits a violation falling within its assigned state management tasks, it shall not be administratively sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree but shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws.

  Article 3. Prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties [3]

  1. The prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; State treasury prescribed in this Decree is 01 year. For administrative violations involving public property being houses, land and infrastructure assets invested and managed by the State, the prescriptive period is 02 years.

  2. The time for calculation of the prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property is as follows:

  a) For completed administrative violations, the prescriptive period shall be calculated from the time the violation ends;

  b) For ongoing administrative violations, the prescriptive period shall be calculated from the time the violation is detected;

  c) Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property under Article 6 hereof shall be considered completed if investment and procurement of property have been finished and property has been handed over to agencies, organizations, or units for management and use; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  d) Administrative violations against regulations on leasing property under Article 7 hereof shall be considered completed if property has been leased out and brought into use; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  dd) Administrative violations against regulations on encroaching on working offices or public service facilities under Article 11 hereof shall be considered as follows:

  - The act of an organization or individual that is using a contiguous land parcel to relocate the boundary of a land parcel to expand the land area or build an encroaching work into the space of the land area of the working office or public service facility of an agency, organization, or unit shall be considered an administrative violation that has been completed if the land area has been expanded or the encroaching work has been built out; or an ongoing administrative violation in the remaining cases.

  - The act of an organization or individual that uses a house or land belonging to the working office or public service facility of an agency, organization, or unit without permission shall be considered an ongoing administrative violation.

  e) Administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property that are being committed include those prescribed in Articles 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, and 26 hereof.

  3. Determination of ongoing administrative violations and completed administrative violations for calculation of prescriptive periods for imposition of penalties for administrative violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness control:

  a) Administrative violations specified in Clause 2 Article 31 hereof shall be considered completed if property procurement and handover to agencies, organizations, or units for management and use have been finished; or ongoing administrative violations in the remaining cases;

  b) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered completed if funds have been used for purposes other than those intended by the fund; administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered completed if funds have been used for purposes other than those intended by the fund or exceed the limits; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  c) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered completed if funds have been used for purposes other than those intended by the fund or exceed the limits; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 32 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  d) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 33 of this Decree shall be considered completed if illegal obstruction of research, application of science and technology, implementation of projects using recycled resources has ended; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 33 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  dd) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 33 of this Decree shall be considered completed if exploitation and use of resources have not complied with planning, plans, and procedures have ended; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 33 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  e) Administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 35 of this Decree shall be considered completed if wastefulness in management of office buildings and public service facilities has ended; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 Article 35 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  g) Administrative violations against regulations on procurement of assets and supplies in a single-member limited liability company funded by the State prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 36 of this Decree shall be considered completed if procurement of assets has been completed; and administrative violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 36 of this Decree shall be considered ongoing in the remaining cases;

  h) Administrative violations in the field of savings being committed are violations specified in Clause 1 Article 31, Clauses 1 and 4 Article 36 of this Decree; administrative violations regarding management and use of capital in one-state-owned limited liability companies specified in Clause 2 Article 36 of this Decree; administrative violations in management and use of assets and supplies specified in Clauses 2 and 3 Article 36 of this Decree.

  4. Determination of administrative violations being committed or completed for calculation of prescriptive periods for administrative penalties in the field of national reserves:

  a) Administrative violations being committed are those specified in Articles 41, 42, 47, Points b and c Clause 3 Article 48, Article 49 of this Decree;

  b) Except for administrative violations specified in Point a of this Clause, remaining administrative violations shall be determined as completed ones.

  5. Time of completion of administrative violations in the field of State Treasury is determined as follows:

  a) For administrative violations specified in Articles 54, 57, 58, 59 and 60 of this Decree, time of completion is the time of submission of documents and vouchers at the State Treasury;

  b) For administrative violations specified in Articles 55, 56 and 61 of this Decree, time of completion is the time when the State Treasury receives the conclusion of the criminal proceeding agency on the violation case which shows no sign of crime.

  Article 4. Penalties and remedial measures

  1. Principal forms of sanction:

  a/ Principal forms of sanction prescribed in this Decree include warning and fine;

  b/ The maximum fine for a violation in the field of management and use of public property, national reserves or State treasury shall be VND 50,000,000 for an individual and VND 100,000,000 for an organization; the maximum fine for a violation in the field of thrift practice and waste combat shall be VND 100,000,000 for an individual and VND 200,000,000 for an organization.

  2. Additional forms of sanction:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation, the violator may also be subject to the additional form of sanction of confiscation of material evidences and means used to commit the administrative violation.

  3. Remedies in the field of management and use of public property:

  Depending on the act, nature and seriousness of the violation, the violator may also be subject to one or several remedies as follows:

  a/ Forcible payment of an amount corresponding to the value of the property;

  b) Enforced payment of amounts incurred from administrative violations;

  c) Enforced return of property; in case where property has been altered due to administrative violations, it must be restored to its original state; in case where it cannot be restored to its original state, it must be replaced by money or other property with similar functions and use value;

  d) Enforced demolition of construction works built on encroached areas of working offices or public service facilities;

  dd) Enforced cancellation of false declarations or fraudulent declarations regarding violations in the registration and use of data on public property;

  e) Enforced adjustment, supplementation of data, information, and additional declarations on public property.

  4. Remedial measures in the field of thrift practice and wastefulness combat:

  Depending on the nature and severity of violations, violating organizations and individuals may also be subject to one or several remedial measures, including:

  a) Revocation and transfer to state budget of funds used for purposes other than those prescribed by the state budget for acts of using funds originating from the state budget for purposes or guidelines other than those issued by competent authorities;

  b) Compulsory disgorgement of illicit gains from the use of funds originating from the State budget for acts of using funds originating from the State budget in contravention of the operational regulations and financial mechanisms of the fund promulgated by competent authorities.

  5. Remedial measures in the field of national reserves:

  Depending on the nature and severity of violations, violating organizations and individuals may also be subject to one or several remedial measures as follows:

  a) Compulsory disgorgement of illicit gains from acts of violation of regulations on purchase, sale, preservation, distribution, and assistance; violations of regulations on import and export of national reserve goods; violations of regulations on capital management and charges in national reserve activities; violations of regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, and exploitation of assets under national reserve;

  b) Compulsory restoration of the original state of establishments, physical and technical facilities, and storage of national reserve goods;

  c) Compulsory refund of damaged national reserve goods due to acts of infringement or destruction; violations of regulations on distribution and assistance of national reserve goods;

  d) Compulsory recall of national reserve goods issued or distributed in contravention of regulations;

  dd) Compulsory restoration of the original state of assets for acts of illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease, and exploitation of assets being storage of national reserve goods.

  6. Remedial measures in the field of State treasury:

  Depending on the nature and seriousness of violations, the violating organization may be subject to one or several remedial measures as follows:

  a) Forcible revocation of amounts already paid for the unfinished work; unfinished work not yet eligible for payment and the paid amount exceeding the contract value or appendix;

  b) Forcible revocation of amounts already paid for the improper documents and improper payments made from improper documents as prescribed but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  c) Forcible revocation of all amounts already paid for the use of fake documents to pay the State budget but not liable to criminal prosecution;

  d) Forcible completion of documents according to regulations for acts of violating regulations on payment of state budget expenditures;

  dd) Forcible completion of procedures for commitment before requesting the State Treasury to pay or remit the state budget or forcible completion of procedures for commitment for acts of violating procedures for control of commitment;

  e) Forcible completion of procedures for advance payment for acts of violating procedures and time limit for advance payment of the state budget;

  g) Mandatory freezing or closing of accounts in case of violations against regulations on registration and use of accounts at State Treasuries; mandatory freezing of accounts in case of making false documents to register for use of accounts at State Treasuries but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  7.[4] Organizations and individuals sanctioned for administrative violations under this Decree are not allowed to use state budget money or money originating from the state budget to pay fines and remedial measures for consequences caused by their violations, except for cases specified in Clause 8 Article 37 of Decree No. 10/2021/ND-CP dated February 9, 2021 of the Government on management of construction investment costs.

  Article 5. Application of fine rates [5]

  1. The fine rates prescribed in Section 1, Section 2, Section 3 and Section 4 Chapter II, Section 1 Chapter III, Section 1 Chapter IV, Section 1 and Section 2 Chapter V of this Decree are the fine rates applicable to administrative violations committed by organizations; the fine rate imposed on an individual is half of that imposed on an organization for the same administrative violation (except for the provisions of Articles 17, 23 and 27 of this Decree).

  2. When imposing a fine, the specific fine rate for an administrative violation shall be the average of the fine bracket prescribed for such violation. If there are extenuating circumstances, each extenuating circumstance may reduce the fine rate by 10% of the average fine rate of the fine bracket, but the fine rate imposed on such violation must not be lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if there are aggravating circumstances, each aggravating circumstance may increase the fine rate by 10% of the average fine rate of the fine bracket, but the fine rate imposed on such violation must not exceed the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  3. When determining the fine rate for an administrative violation which has both aggravating and extenuating circumstances, the aggravating circumstances may be offset by the extenuating circumstances according to the principle that one extenuating circumstance may offset one aggravating circumstance.

  Chapter II

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT AND USE

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS AND FINES FOR ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC PROPERTY AT AGENCIES, ORGANIZATIONS AND EMPLOYEES

  Article 6. Administrative violations against regulations on investment and procurement of public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets without a decision of a competent authority or person:

  a) VND 1,000,000 - VND 5,000,000 if investing in or purchasing assets being machinery, equipment, or other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a total purchase value of less than VND 100,000,000;

  b) VND 5,000,000 - VND 10,000,000 in case of investing in or purchasing assets being machinery, equipment and other assets (except for office buildings, public service facilities, and cars) with a total purchase value of VND 100,000,000 or more;

  c) VND 20,000,000 - VND 50,000,000 in case of investing or purchasing assets being working offices, public service facilities, and cars.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to conduct centralized procurement of assets on the list of assets subject to centralized procurement in accordance with laws on management and use of public assets.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for investing in or purchasing assets beyond the limits prescribed by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds the limit by less than 50.000.000 per unit of asset;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds the limit by 50.000.000 - 100.000.000 per unit of asset;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of assets invested or purchased exceeds the limit by 100.000.000 per unit of asset or more.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced payment of an amount equal to the value of assets invested or purchased beyond the limits prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the excess value as a basis for imposing penalties:

  a) In case of investing in or purchasing assets such as cars, machinery, equipment, and other assets in excess of the limits in terms of quantity: The excess value per unit of asset is determined according to the unit price of such assets stated on the invoice or contract for asset purchase;

  b) In case of investing or purchasing assets being cars, machinery, equipment and other assets in excess of the prescribed quantity but exceeding the prescribed price: The excess value is determined by subtracting (-) the value of the asset according to the standard or quota from the value recorded on the asset purchase invoice or contract;

  c) In case of investing or purchasing working offices or public service facilities in excess of the prescribed standard or quota: The excess value is determined by subtracting (-) the area used according to the standard or quota from the actual area multiplied by (x) the average unit price recorded on the invoice (or the asset purchase contract);

  d) Persons having sanctioning competence specified in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as a basis for sanctioning in accordance with the law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 7. Administrative violations against regulations on leasing assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for committing an administrative violation against regulations on leasing assets if the value of the asset lease contract is less than VND 100.000.000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Committing an act of leasing assets without a decision on asset lease issued by a competent authority or person;

  b) Committing an act of leasing assets in excess of the prescribed standard or quota;

  c) Committing an act of selecting a property leasing service provider in a manner or order not in compliance with the law on management and use of public property and relevant laws.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on leasing of property as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article if the value of the property lease contract is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of the amount of rent in excess of the prescribed limits and norms if the violation prescribed in Point b Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  4. The value of the property lease contract used as the basis for imposing penalties shall be determined as follows:

  a) If the value of the contract is specified in the contract, the value used as the basis for imposing penalties shall be the value specified in the contract;

  b) If the property lease contract is not made into a formal contract or contains insufficient information to determine the value as prescribed in Clause a of this Point, the value used as the basis for imposing penalties shall be the market price of similar property with equivalent standards and technical specifications at the time when the violation is committed multiplied by (x) the period of property lease calculated from the start date of property lease until the date of issuance of the decision on administrative penalty.

  The person having the power to impose penalties prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the value used as the basis for imposing penalties in accordance with laws on handling of administrative violations and shall be responsible for such determination.

  Article 8. Administrative violations against regulations on allocation and use of public property, destruction or intentional damage to public property

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of allocating public property in excess of the standards and norms issued by competent authorities or persons (incorrectly allocated, exceeding the area, quantity, or price) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed where the property is allocated or used with a value of less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed where the property is allocated or used with a value of from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed where the property is allocated or used with a value of VND 100.000.000 or more.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for the act of allocating or using public property for wrongful purposes (using public property for purposes or uses other than those intended; using working offices or public service facilities as residential houses or for personal use; using cars to transport from residence to workplace for unqualified positions; using cars for business trips for unqualified positions without approval by competent authorities; allocating or using cars for personal use; using machinery, equipment, or other property for personal use) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed where the property allocated or used is machinery, equipment, or other property (excluding working offices, public service facilities, and cars) with an original cost recorded in the accounting book of less than VND 100.000.000 per unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property valued at less than VND 100.000.000);

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed where the property allocated or used is machinery, equipment, or other property (excluding working offices, public service facilities, and cars) with an original cost recorded in the accounting book of VND 100.000.000 or more per unit of property (hereinafter referred to as property valued at VND 100.000.000 or more);

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed where the property allocated or used is working offices, public service facilities, or cars.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for destruction or intentional damage to public property if not liable to criminal prosecution:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging machinery, equipment and other property whose book value is less than VND 100.000.000 per unit of property;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging machinery, equipment and other property whose book value is VND 100.000.000 or more per unit of property;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of intentionally damaging working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory payment of an amount equivalent to the value of property in excess of the prescribed norms and limits in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of property altered by the administrative violation; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, the offender shall pay the corresponding amount in cash or provide property with similar functions and use value as the original property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  5. Determination of the value in excess of the prescribed norms and limits shall be carried out as follows:

  a) In case of delivery and use of assets being automobiles, machinery, equipment or other assets in excess of the prescribed quantity: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the excess quantity by the original cost of the asset recorded in the accounting book; in case the original cost of the asset is not recorded in the accounting book, it shall be determined according to the purchase price of a new asset of the same type or an asset meeting technical standards and having similar use function on the market at the time of commission of the violation;

  b) In case of delivery and use of assets being automobiles, machinery, equipment or other assets with correct quantity but exceeding the prescribed price: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the excess quantity by (the difference between the original cost of the asset recorded in the accounting book minus (-) the value of the asset according to technical standards and norms prescribed by competent state agencies);

  c) In case of assets being working offices or public service facilities: The excess value shall be determined by multiplying the difference between the actual area and (-) the area used according to technical standards and norms by the average unit price recorded in the accounting book; in case the unit price is not recorded in the accounting book, it shall be determined according to the investment rate of a house or work with equivalent technical standards issued by the Ministry of Construction applicable at the time of commission of the violation;

  d) Persons having sanctioning competence specified in Articles 29 and 30 of this Decree shall determine the excess value as the basis for sanctioning according to the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations and take responsibility for such determination.

  Article 9. Administrative violations against regulations on lending of public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed for lending or using public property in contravention of regulations (lending the public property to another organization or individual regardless of whether there is a loan contract or not, regardless of the loan term) in the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the lent public property is valued at less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the lent public property is valued at VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed in case of lending assets being working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  2. Remedial measures:

  The organization committing the violation prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article shall also take the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced return of borrowed assets. In case where the asset has been changed due to the administrative violation, it must be restored to its original state; in case where the original state cannot be restored, it must be returned in cash or an asset with similar functions and use value to the original asset;

  b) Enforced payment to the State budget of an amount corresponding to the asset rental during the lending period. The determination of the asset rental as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 10. Administrative violations against regulations on exchange and gifting of public property inconsistently with regulations

  1. A fine shall be imposed for the act of exchanging public property inconsistently with regulations (using public property of one organization to exchange for property of another organization or individual without permission of a competent agency or person) as follows:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case where the property used for exchange is valued at less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if the property put up for exchange is assessed at ≥ VND 100,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed if the property put up for exchange is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for gifting public property against regulations (using public property as gifts in violation of Regulations on gift-giving promulgated by competent authorities).

  3. Remedial measures:

  The violator is compelled to return the property or pay an amount of money equivalent to the value of the property put up for exchange or gifted if the violation prescribed in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  Article 11. Administrative violations against regulations on encroaching on working offices and public service facilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for encroaching on a working office or public service facility (an organization/individual that is using adjacent land and relocates the boundary of the land parcel to expand the land area or build an encroaching work into the space of the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit; or uses the house/land belonging to the working office or public service facility of an agency/organization/unit without permission).

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Organizations and individuals committing violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original state of working offices or public service facilities and return of the encroached working offices or public service facilities;

  b) Enforced demolition of constructions built on encroached areas;

  c) Enforced payment to the State budget of an amount corresponding to the rent for property during the period of encroachment. The determination of the rent for property as a basis for sanctioning shall comply with the provisions of Clause 4, Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 12. Acts of appropriating public property

  1. A fine shall be imposed on acts of appropriating public property which are not serious enough to be examined for penal liability (holding or using public property without permission of competent State agencies) according to the following fines:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of public property is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of public property is VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of expropriating head offices, public service facilities and cars.

  2. Additional penalties: Confiscate the exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  3. Remedial measures: The violator that commits any of the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall also take the remedial measures as follows:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original condition of the property altered by the administrative violation to return it to its original state to the organization; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, the violator must pay compensation in cash or property with similar functions and use value to the original property;

  b) Enforced transfer of an amount of money equivalent to the rent of property during the period of expropriation to the state budget. The determination of the amount of rent of property to serve as a basis for imposing penalties shall comply with regulations in Clause 4 Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 13. Administrative violations against regulations on using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes without a decision on approval of the Scheme issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with the law on management and use of public property at the time of commission of the violation:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the property is a working office, public service facility or car.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on determination of value of property used for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for establishing a Council for valuation or a Council for appraisal of property with improper composition;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for selecting an appraisal organization to determine the value of property used for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes against the prescribed procedures.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for using public property for business purposes, leasing, joint venture or association purposes against the approved Scheme issued by a competent authority or person, thereby affecting the performance of functions and tasks assigned by the State:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is worth less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is an automobile or the property is worth VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is a working office, public service facility or automobile.

  4. Remedial measures:

  a) The violator is compelled to restore the original condition of the property which has been changed as a result of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the property, the violating entity is liable to have such property replaced with another one of equal value or with another one having similar functions and use value;

  b) The violating entity is compelled to return the illegal profit earned from committing any of the violations specified in Clauses 1 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 14. Administrative violations against regulations on maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law

  1. If an organization is assigned by a competent authority to prepare a budget for maintenance and repair of property as prescribed by law but fails to carry out such maintenance and repair, resulting in damage to the property, it shall incur the following penalties:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the damaged property is assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the damaged property is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed if the damaged property is assessed at VND 100.000.000 or more and is a working office, public service facility or car but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. Determination of the amount of money to be returned corresponding to the value of the damaged property:

  a) If the damage is irreparable, the amount of money to be returned shall be determined by multiplying the purchase price of a new property of the same type or standard with the remaining quality percentage at the time of valuation;

  b) If the damage is repairable, the amount of money to be returned shall be the cost of repairing the property;

  c) The head of the organization shall assign the financial agency under his/her management to act as the focal point for determining the amount of money to be returned; on the basis of which, the head of the organization shall decide the amount of money to be returned and take responsibility for his/her decision.

  Article 15. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare plans for disposition of public property as prescribed by law;

  b) Declaring and preparing plans for disposition of public property beyond the prescribed time limit;

  c) Failing to submit reports on the repossession, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of property to competent state authorities when conditions are met as prescribed by law (except where public property has reached the end of its useful life in accordance with law but remains usable and is still needed by agencies, organizations, units or enterprises, in which case heads of such agencies, organizations, units or enterprises shall decide appropriate disposal methods).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on an organization for damaging or losing property while awaiting disposal.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for failure to obtain a decision from a competent authority or person before disposing of property:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of property sold, transferred, liquidated or destroyed is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of property sold, transferred, liquidated or destroyed is VND 100.000.000 or more.

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in case of selling, transferring or liquidating assets that are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for providing false information about the list of assets and their current conditions (leading to improper decisions on asset disposal):

  a) A warning shall be imposed if the declared assets are valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed if the declared assets are valued at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the declared assets are valued at VND 100.000.000 or more;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the declared assets are working offices, public service facilities or cars.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) The violating entity is compelled to restore the original condition of the assets that have been changed due to the violations prescribed in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article; in case of failure to restore the original condition of the assets, the violating entity must refund the money or return the assets with similar functions and use value to the original ones;

  b) Enforced return of an amount equivalent to the value of lost or damaged property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  6. Determination of the amount to be returned as compensation for the loss or damage of property specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article shall be carried out as follows:

  a) In case the lost or damaged property cannot be repaired, the amount to be returned shall be determined based on the price of a new property of the same type or with similar standards and use of the same type on the market at the time when the violation is committed.

  The price on the market may be based on the quoted prices of suppliers on the market that have been posted or announced on the market or from official information disclosed by suppliers via the Internet.

  b) In case the damaged property can be repaired, the amount to be returned shall be the repair cost.

  Article 16. Administrative violations against regulations on organization of handling of public property

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to implement the approved plan for handling public property within the prescribed time limit.

  The time limit for a violation is determined by the handling time limit specified in the decision on handling of assets. If the decision on handling of assets does not specify a specific time limit, relevant laws shall apply.

  In case the plan for extending the disposal of assets is approved by a competent authority, the time limit shall be determined according to the extended time limit.

  b) Failing to hand over assets subject to transfer or recovery decisions to receiving agencies within the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Establishing the Pricing Council ultra vires as prescribed by law;

  b) Establishing the Pricing Council with improper composition as prescribed by law;

  c) Selecting a valuation organization or a professional auction organization to handle assets in a manner that does not comply with the prescribed form and procedures;

  d) Failing to fully notify the public of the auction of public property in accordance with the law on management and use of public property (failing to post up information about the auction of public property at prescribed locations; failing to make public announcements on mass media or making public announcements but not for a sufficient period of time as required by the law on property auction; failing to adequately disclose information as required by the law on property auction).

  3. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to establish a valuation council as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to publicly notify the sale of public property according to Point d Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for failing to conduct valuation as the basis for determining reserve prices when handling property that is office buildings, public service facilities or other property on land in form of sale; failing to hold property auction in cases where such auction must be held as prescribed by law:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of the property is less than VND 100.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of the property is VND 100.000.000 or more;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 if the property is an office building, public service facility or car.

  Article 17. Administrative violations against regulations on entry and use of data on public property

  Fines imposed on organizations and individuals committing violations against regulations on public property data entry and use shall be as follows:

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing a declaration report on assets for data entry into the National Public Property Database inconsistently with legal documents or in conformity with the current status of assets of the unit;

  b) Implementing data entry decentralization to subordinate units without a written agreement from the Ministry of Finance;

  c) Entering or approving public property data inconsistently with the declaration report of the organization assigned to manage and use assets.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Erasing, deleting, or altering the declaration report on assets to falsify public property data in comparison with the current status of assets;

  b) Accessing, intruding, or attempting to intrude, destroy, or alter data in the National Public Property Database or the software program structure;

  c) Exploiting information on public property in the national database on public property of agencies, organizations and units not under their management;

  d) Using data on public property in the national database on public property for personal purposes without permission from the agency competent to manage such database (using information stored in the database for purposes other than those prescribed by laws on management and exploitation of the Public Property Management Software without permission from the agency competent to manage such database).

  3. Remedial measures

  a) Enforced cancellation of false or fraudulent declaration reports;

  b) Enforced correction of data to match the actual condition of property of the unit;

  c) Enforced preparation of additional declaration reports when there is a change in property operations;

  d) Enforced correction of false or misleading information;

  dd) Enforced transfer of illegal revenues generated by committing administrative violations.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF ASSETS OF STATE-FINANCED PROJECTS

  Article 18. Administrative violations against regulations on procurement of assets of state-funded projects

  1. Sanction an organization for committing the act of asset procurement without a decision issued by a competent authority or person as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 6 of this Decree.

  2. Sanction an organization for committing the act of failure to conduct centralized procurement of assets on the list of centrally procured assets as prescribed by law as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 6 of this Decree.

  3. Sanction an organization for committing the act of procurement of assets exceeding the prescribed standards and limits as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 6 of this Decree.

  The value of assets exceeding the prescribed standards and limits shall be determined as prescribed in Clause 5 Article 6 of this Decree.

  4. Sanction an organization for committing the act of violating regulations on leasing assets for management purposes of state-funded projects as prescribed in Article 7 of this Decree.

  Article 19. Administrative violations against regulations on allocation and use of assets of state-funded projects

  1. Sanction an organization for the act of assigning or using assets of a state-funded project in excess of the prescribed standards and limits or for purposes other than those prescribed in Article 8 of this Decree.

  2. Sanction an organization for the act of lending assets of a state-funded project in contravention of regulations prescribed in Article 9 of this Decree.

  3. Sanction an organization for the act of exchanging, gifting or presenting assets of a state-funded project in contravention of regulations prescribed in Article 10 of this Decree.

  4. Sanction an organization or individual for the act of encroaching on working offices or public service facilities of the State-funded Project Management Board prescribed in Article 11 of this Decree.

  5. Sanction an organization or individual for the act of appropriating assets of a state-funded project prescribed in Article 12 of this Decree.

  6. Sanction an organization for the act of violating regulations on use of assets of a state-funded project for production, business, service provision, lease, joint venture or association activities prescribed in Article 13 of this Decree.

  7. Sanction an organization for the act of failing to maintain or repair assets of a state-funded project in accordance with law prescribed in Article 14 of this Decree.

  Article 20. Administrative violations against regulations on handling of assets of projects upon their completion or when they are no longer needed

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to inventory and report to competent authorities for handling of assets within the prescribed time limit;

  b) Failing to report to competent authorities for establishing public ownership of assets transferred by ODA experts, consulting contractors or supervision entities to the Government of Vietnam as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Letting assets deteriorate or go missing during the waiting period for handling;

  b) Removing, changing structures, parts or components of assets.

  3. The violating organization shall be liable to penalties for selling, transferring, liquidating or destroying assets without a decision issued by a competent authority or person in accordance with Clause 3 Article 15 hereof.

  4. The violating organization shall be liable to penalties for providing incorrect information about the list of assets to be handled and their current conditions in accordance with Clause 4 Article 15 hereof.

  5. Sanctioning organizations for committing acts of violating regulations on organization of handling of assets approved by competent agencies or persons shall comply with the provisions of Article 16 of this Decree.

  6. Remedial measures:

  Organizations that commit acts of violating the provisions of Clause 2 of this Article shall also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a/ Forced to restore the original state of assets which have been changed due to administrative violations; in case of failure to restore the original state of assets, they must return money or assets with functions and use value equivalent to the original assets;

  b/ Forced to pay an amount corresponding to the value of assets lost or damaged. The determination of the amount payable corresponding to the value of assets lost or damaged shall comply with the provisions of Clause 6, Article 15 of this Decree.

  Section 3. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS, SANCTIONING LEVELS IN MANAGEMENT AND HANDLING OF ASSETS WITH DETERMINED PUBLIC PROPERTY RIGHTS, SANCTIONING FORMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES

  Article 21. Violations of regulations on establishment of public property rights, preservation and transfer of assets with established public property rights

  1. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to report to competent agencies or persons for establishment of public ownership of assets as prescribed;

  b) Transferring assets to competent authorities for handling beyond the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Letting assets deteriorate or go missing during the waiting period for handling;

  b) Removing or changing the structure, parts, or components of assets.

  3. Remedial measures:

  The organization committing the violation in Clause 2 of this Article shall also be subject to the remedial measures as follows:

  a) Enforced restoration of the original condition of assets that have been changed due to the administrative violation; in case of failure to restore the original condition of assets, the violating entity must refund money or return assets with similar functions and use value to the original ones;

  b) Enforced payment of an amount equivalent to the value of the lost or damaged property. The determination of the amount payable equivalent to the value of the lost or damaged property shall comply with Clause 6 Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 22. Violations against regulations on disposal of property under public ownership

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit reports to competent authorities or persons responsible for preparing plans for disposal of property under public ownership;

  b) Submitting reports on preparation of plans for disposal of property under public ownership by the prescribed deadline.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to prepare plans for disposal of property under public ownership;

  b) Preparing plans for disposal of property under public ownership beyond the prescribed deadline.

  3. Sanctioning organizations and individuals committing acts of violating regulations on organization of handling of assets under establishment of public ownership approved by competent agencies or persons shall comply with the provisions in Article 16 of this Decree.

  Article 23. Violations of regulations on logging in and using data on assets under establishment of public ownership
  Sanctioning organizations and individuals committing violations in logging in and using data on assets under establishment of public ownership shall comply with the provisions in Article 17 of this Decree.

  Section 4. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS AND SANCTION LEVELS IN MANAGEMENT AND USE OF INFRASTRUCTURAL ASSETS

  Article 24. Violations in exploitation of infrastructure assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for leasing out the right to exploit or transferring the right to exploit infrastructure assets for a definite term without obtaining a decision approving the Scheme issued by a competent agency or person as prescribed by law on management and use of public assets.

  2. A fine shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on determination of lease prices of the right to exploit or transfer the right to exploit infrastructure assets according to the following levels:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for establishing a valuation council with improper composition or authority;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for selecting an appraisal organization to determine the lease price of the right to extract or assign the right to extract a piece of infrastructure in a manner or order contrary to regulations of law.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for leasing out the right to extract or assigning the right to extract a piece of infrastructure for purposes other than those specified in the scheme approved by a competent authority or person.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced return of the proceeds obtained from committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 25. Violations against regulations on disposal of infrastructure assets

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to declare and prepare a plan for disposal of assets as prescribed by law;

  b) Failing to report to a competent authority for repossession, transfer, sale, liquidation or destruction of assets when fully satisfying eligibility requirements as prescribed by law (except where public assets have expired according to regulations of law but agencies, organizations, units or enterprises still require them for use, in which case heads of such agencies, organizations, units or enterprises shall decide on appropriate disposal methods).

  c) Failing to report to competent state agencies for handling of assets in case of loss or destruction.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Causing damage or loss of assets while awaiting disposal;

  b) Declaring incorrect information about the list and current conditions of assets proposed for disposal, resulting in a decision on asset disposal that is contrary to regulations.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for disposing of assets without a decision issued by a competent agency or person.

  4. Remedial measures:

  The violator is compelled to return an amount equivalent to the value of assets that are lost or damaged, applicable to the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article. The determination of the amount to be returned equivalent to the value of assets that are lost or damaged shall comply with Clause 6 Article 15 of this Decree.

  Article 26. Violations against regulations on organization of disposal of assets of infrastructure facilities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500.000 to VND 1.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to implement the disposal plan approved by a competent authority or person within the prescribed time limit;

  b) Failure to hand over property subject to a decision on transfer or recovery to the receiving agency within the prescribed time limit.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Establishing the Council for valuation or the Council for destruction of property against regulations of law;

  b) Failure to adequately publish information about public property auction in accordance with regulations of law on property auction (failure to post up information about property auction at the prescribed locations; failure to publicly announce such information on mass media or failure to provide adequate information about property auction in accordance with regulations of law on property auction; failure to adequately disclose information about property auction in accordance with regulations of law on property auction).

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to establish the Council for valuation or the Council for destruction of property in accordance with regulations of law;

  b) Selecting a valuation organization or a professional auction organization to handle assets in an improper form or order as prescribed by law;

  c) Failing to publicly notify the sale of public property as prescribed in Point b Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out valuation as a basis for determining the reserve price when selling infrastructure assets.

  Article 27. Violations against regulations on access to and use of data on infrastructure assets

  Sanctioning of organizations and individuals committing violations against regulations on access to and use of data on infrastructure assets shall comply with Article 17 of this Decree.

  Section 5. AUTHORITY TO MAKE RECORDS AND IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MANAGEMENT AND USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

  2. Persons having authority to issue offense notices as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article are responsible for issuing penalty decisions within their jurisdiction or transferring them to competent authorities as prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 hereof to issue penalty decisions.

  1. Chairpersons of district-level People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 50.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations[6];

  d) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 30. Power to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Inspectors and persons assigned to perform specialized inspections in the field of management and use of public property while on duty shall have the power to:

  2. Chief Inspectors of provincial Departments, heads of specialized inspectorate teams at provincial level have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 50,000,000;

  d) Impose the remedial measures specified in clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of ministerial-level specialized inspectorates shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 70.000.000;

  c) [9] Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations whose value does not exceed VND 140.000.000;

  d) Impose the remedial measures specified in clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  4. Chief Inspector of the Ministry shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warning;

  b) A fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  c) Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;

  d) Remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter III

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR CONSEQUENCES IN THE FIELD OF SAVIOR PRACTICE AND WASTE PREVENTION

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON SAVIOR PRACTICE AND WASTE PREVENTION

  Article 31. Violations against regulations on saving practice and waste prevention in use of communication means, electricity, water, gasoline, oil, books, newspapers, stationery, organization of conferences, seminars, expenses for reception and business trips at home or abroad with state budget funds; procurement of equipment serving operation of medical facilities funded by state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for using communication means, electricity, water, gasoline, oil, books, newspapers, stationery, organization of conferences, seminars, expenses for reception and business trips at home or abroad with state budget funds exceeding the standards and norms issued by competent authorities.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for failure to purchase equipment and devices serving medical facilities funded by state budget not on the list approved by competent authorities:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the total value of equipment and devices purchased is less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the total value of equipment and devices purchased is from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed if the total value of equipment and devices purchased is from VND 100.000.000 or more.

  3. Medical facilities that purchase equipment and devices serving their operations funded by state budget in excess of the prescribed standards and limits shall incur penalties according to Article 6 hereof.

  Article 32. Violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat in management and use of state budget funds[10]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Using state budget funds[11] for purposes other than those prescribed by competent authorities;

  b) Using state budget funds not allocated to the fund[12] against operating regulations and financial mechanisms of the fund issued by competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed for wasting investment capital against standards, regulations, norms, and unit prices as prescribed by law.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) The entity committing the violation in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to return the funds used for wrongful purposes to the state budget fund[13];

  b) The violating entity is compelled to transfer the illegal profits earned from administrative violations to the state budget fund[14].

  Article 33. Violations against laws on thrift practice and waste combat in exploitation and use of natural resources but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for illegally obstructing research, application of science and technology, or implementation of projects using recycled resources.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for exploiting and using natural resources against planning, plans, and procedures approved by competent authorities.

  Article 34. Violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat in investment projects using state budget, public money and assets

  Violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat in investment projects using state budget shall be subject to penalties prescribed in the Government’s Decree No. 139/2017/ND-CP dated November 27, 2017 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on construction investment; mineral exploitation, processing and trading as building materials, production and trading of building materials; management of technical infrastructure works; real estate business, housing development, management and use of houses and offices; relevant documents and amendments thereto (if any).

  Article 35. Violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat in management and use of working offices and non-business operation facilities of organizations using state budget but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on management of working offices and non-business operation facilities of organizations using state budget and causing wastefulness.

  2. The penalties for violations against regulations on standards and norms for use of working offices and non-business operation facilities of organizations using state budget and causing wastefulness shall be subject to Article 8 hereof.

  Article 36. Violations against regulations on thrift practice and wastefulness combat in management and use of capital and public property in a one-state-owned single-member limited liability company but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations against regulations on establishment, management and use of funds in a one-state-owned single-member limited liability company for purposes other than those prescribed or exceeding limits, standards and regimes issued by competent authorities.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for wasting state-owned capital in a single-member limited liability company or for purchasing, managing and using assets and supplies in a single-member limited liability company against regulations of law on public financial management and asset management and construction investment.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed for purchasing, managing and using fixed assets, supplies and other assets in a single-member limited liability company beyond the limits, norms and regimes issued by competent authorities.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 70.000.000 to VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed for managing and using expenses in a single-member limited liability company against norms, unit prices, norms and regimes issued by competent authorities.

  Section 2. AUTHORITY TO MAKE ADMINISTRATIVE OFFENCES RECORDS AND IMPOSE PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF SAVIOR PRACTICE AND WASTE PREVENTION

  Article 37. Authority to make administrative offence records in the field of thrift practice and waste prevention

  2. Persons competent to make records specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall issue sanctioning decisions according to their competence or transfer to competent authorities specified in Articles 38 and 39 of this Decree for issuing sanctioning decisions.

  Article 38. Sanctioning competence of Chairpersons of People’s Committees

  1. Chairpersons of district-level People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 50,000,000;

  b) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 200,000,000;

  b) Apply remedial measures specified in Clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 39. Authority to impose penalties of inspectors

  1. Chief Inspectors of Departments and holders of equivalent titles assigned by the Government to perform specialized inspection functions, heads of specialized inspection teams at Departmental level shall have the authority to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Heads of specialized inspection teams at ministerial level shall have the authority to:

  a) Impose a fine up to VND 140.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Chief Inspector of Ministry shall have the authority to:

  a) A fine of up to VND 200,000,000;

  b) Application of remedies specified in Clause 4 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter IV

     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES IN RESERVES

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON PROVISION OF NATIONAL RESERVES

  Article 40. Violations against regulations on provision of national reserves

  1. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to notify or publicly post information about participants in purchase and sale; methods of purchase and sale; price of purchase and sale; time limit for purchase and sale; quantity, type, specifications, standards and quality of national reserves as prescribed by competent authorities.

  2. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for the following violations:

  Failing to comply with regulations issued by competent regulatory authorities on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to purchase national reserve goods when all conditions have been met under decisions of competent state agencies;

  b) Trading in national reserve goods without written consent of competent state agencies;

  c) To buy or sell national reserve goods when the time limit for buying or selling national reserve goods has expired;

  d) Trading national reserve goods in quantities, categories, specifications, standards or quality not approved by competent authorities;

  dd) Failing to comply with regulations issued by competent state agencies on methods of purchase and sale of each type of national reserve goods.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for arbitrarily changing the prices of national reserve goods for self-seeking purposes.

  5. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of profits earned from illegal activities to government budget in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 1, Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 41. Violations against regulations on management of national reserve

  1. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts of violation:

  a) Failing to open books for monitoring the quality, quantity and categories of national reserve goods during preservation;

  b) Failing to submit regular or irregular reports on preservation of national reserve goods.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for violations against regulations on transfer of national reserve goods:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent regulatory authorities on transfer of national reserve goods;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 7.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with regulations of competent regulatory authorities on transfer of national reserve goods.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recall of national reserve goods that have been improperly transported in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 42. Violations against regulations on preservation of national reserve goods

  1. To impose a fine on act of failing to implement or implementing not in compliance with national technical regulations or temporary technical requirements for preservation of national reserve goods issued by competent state agencies;

  Failing to comply with regulations on the storage duration of national reserve goods; storing national reserve goods at a location other than that prescribed by competent authorities, penalties shall be imposed as follows:

  a) VND 300,000 - VND 500,000 in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of the warehousing, preservation and circulation of national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the violation causes damage to national reserve goods assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for hiring an organization/individual ineligible to preserve national reserve goods assessed at less than VND 100.000.000.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced transfer of the profits earned from administrative violations prescribed in Clause 1 through 3 of this Article to state budget.

  Article 43. Acts of infringing upon or destroying material and technical facilities, storage of national reserve goods but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for illegally infringing upon material and technical facilities, storage of national reserve goods.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for destroying material and technical facilities, storage of national reserve goods.

  3. The following fines shall be imposed for acts of infringing upon or destroying national reserve goods:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused to national reserve goods;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if less than VND 50,000,000 in value of national reserve goods is damaged;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000 in value of national reserve goods is damaged.

  4. Additional penalties

  Confiscate material evidence and means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  5. Remedial measures:

  a) Compulsory restoration of the original state of material and technical facilities and storage of national reserve goods for violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Compulsory return of damaged national reserve goods due to acts of infringing upon or destroying national reserve goods specified in Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 44. Violations against regulations on provision and assistance of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of provision or assistance of national reserve goods or performance of other tasks related to national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000.

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  2. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed for failure to carry out procedures for distribution and provision of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 within the prescribed time limit.

  3. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for using national reserve goods for wrong purposes; distributing or providing national reserve goods to wrong entities according to decisions of competent authorities; failing to ensure the type, quantity, quality, standards and specifications of national reserve goods in accordance with the following fines:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300.000 to VND 500.000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the loss of national reserve goods is assessed at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  4. The following fines shall be imposed for loss of national reserve goods used for distribution, provision or performance of other tasks:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed if the lost national reserve goods are valued at less than VND 50.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed if the national reserve goods are valued at from VND 50.000.000 to under VND 100.000.000.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for distributing national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 to wrong entities according to a competent authority’s decision.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for delaying or procrastinating in the distribution of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 as prescribed by a competent authority.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for delaying or procrastinating in the distribution of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100.000.000 as prescribed by a competent authority, thereby causing damage to national reserve goods.

  8. Additional penalties:

  Confiscate the exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing the violations specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  9. Remedial measures:

  a) Enforced transfer of the illegal profit earned from committing the violations specified in Clauses 3, 5 and 6 of this Article to state budget;

  b) Enforced return of lost national reserve goods, or goods used for wrong purposes or issued to wrong entities, in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clauses 3, 4 and 5 of this Article;

  c) Enforced return of damaged, inferior or devalued national reserve goods, or goods with insufficient quantity, in case of commission of any of the violations specified in Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 45. Violations against regulations on importation and exportation of national reserve goods

  1. A warning or a fine shall be imposed for obstruction of importation, exportation or transportation of national reserve goods as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed if no damage is caused;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused but the value of the damage is less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed if damage is caused but the value of the damage is from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Failing to follow or improperly following procedures for import and export of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 as prescribed;

  b) Failing to deliver goods in the right quantity, category, specifications, standards and quality of national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 under decisions of competent state agencies.

  3. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 in the wrong category, specifications, quantity, quality, time and place under decisions of competent state agencies.

  4. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of importing or exporting national reserve goods valued at less than VND 100,000,000 without decisions of competent state agencies.

  5. Additional forms of sanctions:

  Confiscating material evidences and/or means used to commit acts specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  6. Remedies:

  a) Forcible payment of illicit earnings from committing administrative violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article;

  b) Enforced recall of national reserve goods that have been issued out against regulations for violations specified in Clauses 2, 3 and 4 of this Article.

  Article 46. Violations against regulations on receipt of national reserve goods but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Receiving national reserve goods not at the time and place specified in the decision of competent authority;

  b) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations on procedures and order for receiving national reserve goods.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for failing to receive national reserve goods (national reserve goods issued for aid or other tasks of competent authorities) when all conditions prescribed by competent authority are satisfied.

  Article 47. Illegal intervention in national reserve activities

  1. Warnings or fines shall be imposed for illegal intervention in national reserve activities as follows:

  a) A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed in case of no damage;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at less than VND 50,000,000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed in case of damage to national reserve goods valued at from VND 50,000,000 to less than VND 100,000,000.

  2. Additional penalties:

  Confiscation of exhibits and means used for committing the violations prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 48. Violations against regulations on management of capital and charges in national reserve activities

  1. A warning or a fine ranging from VND 300,000 to VND 500,000 shall be imposed for failure to report the use of capital from national reserve funds for purchase of national reserve goods as prescribed.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Payment for goods in stock without a sale contract;

  b) Payment for goods in stock not in quantity or with insufficient invoices and documents;

  c) Payment for goods in stock without a record of contract liquidation or a list of documents on receipt of goods in stock bearing the signature of the unit’s head for cases of direct purchase without bidding;

  d) Payment for goods in stock without approval by the head of the national reserve unit;

  dd) Using fees for receipt, delivery and preservation of goods in stock inconsistently with the prescribed contents or exceeding the prescribed limits.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

  a) Using national reserve funds for purposes other than those prescribed;

  b) Failing to comply with regulations on management of funds derived from national reserve funds or improperly complying with such regulations.

  c) Failure to remit the remaining amount after completing the purchase of national reserve goods in accordance with regulations of competent state agencies.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced remittance of illegal profits earned from committing the violations specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 49. Violations against regulations on trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets included in national reserves

  1. The following fines shall be imposed for illegal trading, pledge, mortgage, lease or exploitation of assets included in national reserves (except for storage of national reserve goods):

  a) A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 if the value of the asset is less than VND 70.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 if the value of the asset is from VND 70.000.000 to less than VND 150.000.000;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 if the value of the asset is from VND 150.000.000 to less than VND 300.000.000;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at from VND 300.000.000 to under VND 500.000.000;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed in case the property is valued at ≥ VND 500.000.000.

  2. The following fines shall be imposed for trading, pledging, mortgaging, leasing or illegally exploiting the national reserve storehouse:

  a) A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the national reserve storehouse is less than VND 500.000.000;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed if the value of the national reserve storehouse is VND 500.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory restoration of the original condition of the property in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory return of illegal profits obtained from the violations specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article.

  Chapter V

  ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES, FINES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES FOR VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE EXPENDITURES

  Section 1. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE EXPENDITURES IN THE STATE BUDGET ESTIMATES

  Article 54. Violations against regulations on state expenditures in the State budget estimates approved by competent authorities

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records for payment to the State Treasury for expenditures not included in the State budget estimates approved by competent authorities;

  b) Preparing documents and records for payment to the State Treasury for expenditures exceeding the annual funding estimates or plans assigned by competent authorities or exceeding the approved expenditure estimates (for non-contractual work);

  c) Preparing documents and records for payment to the State Treasury for expenditures not in accordance with the estimates assigned by competent authorities (expenditures made from sources other than those specified in the estimates; expenditures made for purposes or for beneficiaries other than those specified in the estimates; expenditures made from sources other than those specified in investment capital; expenditures made for projects other than those specified in the lists approved by competent authorities).

  2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 6,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Making dossiers and documents to send to State Treasury for payment for the workload not yet performed;

  b) Making dossiers and documents to send to State Treasury for payment for the workload being performed incompletely or not eligible for payment;

  c) Making dossiers and documents to send to State Treasury for payment in excess of contract value or contract appendix.

  3. Remedies:

  Forced revocation of amounts already paid for the workload not yet performed; workload being performed incompletely or not eligible for payment and the paid amount in excess of contract value or contract appendix as prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 55. Acts of making dossiers and documents at variance with regulations and acts of making dossiers and documents at variance with original dossiers and documents at the unit but not serious enough to be examined for penal liability

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of signing contracts before having capital plans approved by competent authorities (except for construction works under emergency orders).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 6.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenses not in compliance with regulations on title and eligible beneficiaries;

  b) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenses exceeding the prescribed limits in terms of quantity or value;

  c) Preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury for payment of expenses against regulations (payment of expenses not meeting eligibility requirements or principles of payment regimes).

  In case an organization commits the act of purchasing or leasing state assets beyond the limits prescribed by competent authorities, it shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of Article 5 and Article 6 of this Decree.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for preparing documents and records to be sent to the State Treasury which are incorrect with contents of the original documents, invoices and records at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  4. Remedial measures:

  Enforced revocation of expenses paid due to preparation of documents and records against regulations and expenses paid due to preparation of documents and records which are incorrect with contents of the original documents, invoices and records at the unit but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clauses 2 and 3 of this Article.

  Article 56. Acts of making fake documents and records for state budget expenditure but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for making fake documents and records and sending them to the State Treasury for payment of regular expenditures, recurrent expenditures, expenditures on national target programs or recurrent expenditures on target programs using recurrent expenditures (excluding expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of facilities from recurrent expenditures of the State budget and retained fees according to regulations for recurrent expenditures with total investment of VND 500.000.000 or more).

  2. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for making fake documents and records and sending them to the State Treasury for payment of investment capital from the state budget and investment capital from the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenditures on repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading, expansion of facilities from recurrent expenditures of the State budget and retained fees according to regulations for recurrent expenditures with total investment of VND 500.000.000 or more.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced recovery of all expenditures incurred from the use of fake documents and records for state budget expenditure but not liable to criminal prosecution as prescribed in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 57. Violations against regulations on payment of state budget expenditures

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for making documents and records requesting the State Treasury to transfer payments to wrong names or account numbers of beneficiary units specified in contracts or contract appendices.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 2.000.000 to VND 4.000.000 shall be imposed for making documents and records requesting payment of state budget expenditures inconsistently with payment terms specified in contracts or contract appendices between the estimated unit or investor and goods or service providers, including one of the following acts:

  a) Incorrect contract value;

  b) Incorrect payment term;

  c) Incorrect payment method;

  d) Incorrect payment rate (including advance payment);

  dd) Incorrect other payment terms specified in the contract and its appendices.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Enforced completion of documents and vouchers as prescribed for the acts specified in Clause 1 and Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 58. Violations against procedures for control of expenditure commitments

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to submit expenditure commitments to the State Treasury for expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  b) Submitting requests for expenditure commitments to the State Treasury beyond the prescribed deadline for expenditures subject to commitment as prescribed;

  c) Submitting requests for expenditure commitments in excess of the annual investment capital plan, the balance of the approved annual budget estimate, or the value of contracts for which commitments are permitted.

  2. Remedial measures:

  a) The entity committing the violation in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to carry out expenditure commitment procedures before requesting the State Treasury to make payment or disburse funds for state budget expenditures;

  b) The entity committing the violation in Point c Clause 1 of this Article is compelled to carry out expenditure commitment procedures again.

  Article 59. Violations against regulations on procedures and time limit for making advance payments of state budget

  1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Carrying out procedures for payment of advances after the final deadline for advance payment for regular expenses without contracts on sale of goods or services or expenses not required to send contracts on sale of goods or services to the State Treasury as prescribed;

  b) Failing to carry out procedures for payment of advances for investment capital from the state budget and investment capital from the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenses for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities from the regular budget of the State and retained fees under regulations for regular expenses with total investment of over VND 500,000,000 through several payments of completed volume of contracts.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 4,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to carry out procedures for full payment of advances for regular expenses with contracts on sale of goods or services under regulations in the final payment of contracts;

  b) Failing to carry out procedures for full payment of advances for investment capital from the state budget and investment capital from the state budget for implementation of target programs or expenses for repair, maintenance, renovation, upgrading and expansion of material facilities from the regular budget of the State and retained fees under regulations for regular expenses with total investment of over VND 500,000,000 when the value of payment (including advances and payment of completed volume) reaches 80% of contract value;

  c) Failing to carry out or carrying out procedures for advance payment for compensation, support and resettlement after the prescribed time limit for advance payment.

  3. Remedial measures:

  Compulsory advance payment shall be made for the acts specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article.

  Section 2. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON REGISTRATION AND USE OF ACCOUNTS AT THE STATE BANK

  Article 60. Violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Bank

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 2.000.000 shall be imposed for committing violations of regulations on registration and use of accounts at the State Bank.

  2. Remedial measures:

  The account must be frozen or closed in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 61. Acts of making false documents to register accounts at the State Bank but not liable to criminal prosecution

  1. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for making false documents to register accounts at the State Bank.

  2. Remedial measures:

  The violator is compelled to freeze the account in case of commission of the violation specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Section 3. AUTHORITY TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES FOR OFFENCES AGAINST REGULATIONS ON STATE SECURITY

  Article 62. Authority to make records of administrative offences against regulations on state security

  1. Persons having authority to make records of administrative offences against regulations on state security include:

  a) Persons having authority to impose penalties for administrative offences against regulations on control of state budget expenditures through state treasuries as prescribed in Articles 63 and 64 hereof;

  b) Officials assigned to control state budget expenditures through state treasuries.

  c) Inspectors and persons assigned to perform specialized inspection tasks who are on duty.

  2. Persons competent to make records specified in Clause 1 of this Article shall issue sanctioning decisions according to their competence or transfer to competent authorities specified in Articles 63 and 64 of this Decree for issuing sanctioning decisions.

  Article 63. Sanctioning competence of inspectors

  1. Chief Inspectors of provincial-level Finance Departments, heads of specialized inspectorates of provincial-level Finance Departments, heads of specialized inspectorates of State Treasuries established by directors of provincial-level State Treasuries and heads of specialized inspectorates of State Treasuries established by the General Director of State Treasuries shall have the right to:

  a/ Impose fines of up to VND 50,000,000;

  b/ Apply remedies specified in Clause 6, Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Chief Inspectors of the Ministry of Finance shall have the right to:

  a/ Impose fines of up to VND 100,000,000;

  b/ Apply remedies specified in Clause 6, Article 4 of this Decree.

  3. Heads of specialized inspection teams established by the Ministry of Finance shall have the power to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 70.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Article 64. Power to impose penalties of Director General of State Treasury; Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and central-affiliated cities

  1. Directors of State Treasuries of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall have the power to:

  a) Impose fines of up to VND 70.000.000;

  b) Enforce the remedial measures specified in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  2. Director General of State Treasury shall have the power to:

  a) A fine up to VND 100.000.000;

  b) Remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 6 Article 4 of this Decree.

  Chapter VI

                                          IMPLEMENTATION CLAUSES [24]

  Article 65. Entry into force

  1. This Decree comes into force from September 01, 2019.

  2. The Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserves; state treasury and the Government’s Decree No. 58/2015/ND-CP dated June 16, 2015 on amendments to the Government’s Decree No. 192/2013/ND-CP dated November 21, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on management and use of state assets; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserves; state treasury shall cease to be effective from the date this Decree comes into force.

  Article 66. Transition clauses

  Regarding administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury committed before the effective date hereof but discovered afterwards or being considered and handled, regulations on penalties hereof shall be applied if this Decree does not prescribe legal liabilities or impose lighter legal liabilities on violating individuals and organizations.

  Article 67. Implementation responsibilities

  1. The Ministry of Finance shall provide guidance on implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministries, ministerial agencies, Governmental agencies, and People’s Committees at all levels shall be responsible for providing guidelines on and inspecting the imposition of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; and state treasury as prescribed in this Decree.

  3. When detecting administrative violations against regulations on management and use of public property; thrift practice and wastefulness combat; national reserve; state treasury, competent authorities shall transfer dossiers (if any) or notify them to persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations specified herein for consideration and handling.

  In case of administrative violations in the field of management and use of public property which are subject to recovery of public property as prescribed by law on management and use of public property, the person competent to impose administrative penalties specified in this Decree shall, after issuing a decision on imposition of administrative penalties, notify the competent authority in accordance with law on management and use of public property to issue a decision on recovery of property as prescribed. The handling of recovered property shall comply with the provisions of law on management and use of public property.

  4. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels and relevant organizations and individuals shall be responsible for implementing this Decree./.
