                                                   DECREE

     THE GOVERNMENT'S DECREE No. 74/2003/ND-CP OF JUNE 26, 2003, ON SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ELECTRONICS

                                                   GOV

  Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
  Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002;
  At the proposal of the Minister of Industry,

                                                  DECREE

  Chapter 1.

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  Administrative violations in the field of electricity means intentional or unintentional violations of law regulations on state management in the field of electricity operation and use which are not crimes and must be administratively sanctioned as prescribed by law, including:

  1. Violations of regulations on electricity operation permit.

  2. Violations of regulations on construction, production, transmission and distribution of electricity.

  3. Violations of regulations on power supply.

  4. Violations of regulations on electricity use.

  5. Violations of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grid.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Individuals and organizations committing acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree.

  2. Foreign individuals and organizations committing acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity within the territory, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, except otherwise provided for by international agreements which the State of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam has signed or acceded to.

  3. Minors committing acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned under the provisions of Article 7 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 3. Principles for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. The sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity must be carried out by competent persons prescribed in Chapter III of this Decree in strict compliance with law provisions.

  2. All administrative violations in the field of electricity must be detected in time and stopped immediately. The sanctioning must be conducted quickly, fairly and thoroughly; all consequences caused by administrative violations in the field of electricity must be remedied in strict compliance with law provisions.

  3. An act of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned only once. A person who commits many acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall be sanctioned for each act of violation. Many persons jointly committing an act of administrative violation in the field of electricity shall each be sanctioned.

  4. The sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity must be based on the nature and seriousness of the violations, personal records and extenuating and aggravating circumstances in order to decide appropriate sanctioning forms and measures. Extenuating and aggravating circumstances shall apply according to the provisions of Articles 8 and 9 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  5. No administrative penalties shall be imposed in the field of electricity in cases of emergency, legitimate self-defense, unexpected events or administrative violations committed while suffering from mental illness or other diseases that deprive them of the ability to perceive or control their acts.

  Article 4. Statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity and period during which no administrative penalty is imposed

  1. The statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity shall be one year from the date on which the administrative violation is committed; if the above-mentioned statute of limitations has expired, no administrative penalty shall be imposed but the remedial measures prescribed in Clause 3 Article 5 of this Decree shall still be applied to remedy the consequences caused by the administrative violation against regulations on electricity.

  2. For individuals who are prosecuted or brought to trial according to criminal procedures but later decided to suspend investigation or suspend the case by competent authorities, if their acts show signs of administrative violations against regulations on electricity, they shall be administratively sanctioned; in this case, the statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity shall be three months from the date on which the person competent to impose penalties receives the suspension decision and the dossier of the violation.

  3. Within the time limit specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, if the violating individual or organization commits a new administrative violation against regulations on electricity or deliberately evades or obstructs the imposition of penalties, the statute of limitations prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply. The statute of limitations for imposing administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity shall be recalculated from the time of committing the new administrative violation or the time of terminating the act of evading or obstructing the imposition of penalties.

  4. Individuals and organizations that are administratively sanctioned for violations against regulations on electricity, if they do not commit new violations within one year from the date on which they completely serve the sanctioning decisions or from the date on which the statute of limitations for executing the sanctioning decisions expires, they shall be considered as having not been administratively sanctioned for violations against regulations on electricity.

  Article 5. Forms of administrative penalties for violations against regulations on electricity

  1. Principal forms of sanctions: For each administrative violation against regulations on electricity, the violating individual or organization must bear one of the following principal forms of sanctions:

  a/ Warning shall apply to individuals or organizations committing minor and first-time administrative violations with extenuating factors or to all administrative violations committed by minors aged between full 14 years and under 16 years. Warning shall be decided in writing;

  b/ Fine shall be decided on the basis of the nature and seriousness of violations according to the fine bracket prescribed in this Decree for each violation. The specific fine level for an administrative violation in the electricity field is the average level of the fine bracket for such act; if the violation involves extenuating factors, the fine level may be reduced but not lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation involves aggravating factors, the fine level may be increased but not higher than the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  2. Additional sanctioning forms: Depending on the nature and seriousness of violations, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the electricity field may also be subject to one or more of the following additional sanctioning forms:

  a/ Deprivation of the right to use electricity operation permits for a definite term;

  b/ Confiscation of material evidences and means used for committing administrative violations in the electricity field.

  3. Apart from the principal sanctioning forms and additional sanctions prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals or organizations committing administrative violations in the electricity field may also be forced to restore the initial state altered due to their administrative violations or to dismantle illegally constructed works.
  Chapter II
     ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE ELECTRICITY FIELD, SANCTIONING FORMS AND LEVELS

  Article 6. Violations of regulations on electricity operation permits

  1. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using an expired electricity operation permit.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Leasing or lending out the electricity operation permit;

  b) Altering the contents of the electricity operation permit by oneself.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Carrying out electricity activities without obtaining a valid electricity operation permit;

  b) Carrying out electricity activities after being suspended or deprived of the right to use the electricity operation permit by a competent authority.

  4. Depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, violating individuals or organizations may also have their right to use electricity operation permits stripped from six months to one year in case of committing the violations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 7. Violations of regulations on power construction, production, transmission and distribution

  1. A fine of between 300,000 VND and 700,000 VND shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Cutting trees, pruning trees or dismantling works not in compliance with law regulations when building, operating and repairing power grid;

  b) Failing to restore the original state of relevant works which have been changed due to the construction and repair of electrical works.

  2. A fine of between 1,000,000 VND and 3,000,000 VND shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Violating regulations on safety assurance for people and property during renovation, upgrading, repair and construction of electrical works;

  b) Violating procedures and regulations in power production, transmission and distribution causing unsafe for people or damage to property.

  3. A fine of between 3,000,000 dong and 7,000,000 dong shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily installing power lines or substations serving the power distribution outside the scope permitted by competent authorities;

  b) Violating the provisions of law on electricity investment and development;

  c) Obstructing organizations and individuals from performing the task of repairing and building electrical works.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 shall be imposed for the act of manufacturing, transmitting and distributing electricity without being inspected and certified by competent authorities as eligible to manufacture, transmit and distribute electricity.

  5. In addition to the main forms of sanction against violations specified in this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be forced to restore the initial state which has been changed due to violations specified at Point b, Clause 1 and Point a, Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 8. Violations of regulations on power supply

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Inspecting organizations and individuals using electricity without professional cards or without inspection decisions of the grid management agency or unit;

  b/ Carrying out electricity connection or disconnection not in compliance with the notified contents.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to make records on acceptance of handover to the electricity user under regulations after installing, replacing, repairing or adjusting the electricity metering system;

  b/ Failing to appoint a person to conduct inspection of the meter within five days after the electricity seller receives the request from the electricity buyer for the inspection of the meter;

  c/ Failing to notify and post up at public places of electricity trading the provisions of law on rights and obligations of electricity buyers and sellers, conditions for signing electricity trading contracts, electricity prices and order and procedures for stopping power supply;

  d/ Failing to sign electricity trading contracts within ten days after the domestic electricity buyer has ensured all conditions prescribed by law and agreed terms in the draft contract.

  3. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to have working tickets or duty assignment tickets and professional cards but arbitrarily carrying out one of the following jobs: repairing or connecting equipment, lines or power stations of the electricity sector or of organizations or individuals using electricity; removing, installing or adjusting meters on the power grid;

  b/ Failing to handle incidents within two hours after receiving notices of grid incidents from electricity sellers;

  c/ Obstructing people in the performance of official duties from inspecting and supervising the electricity sale and purchase.

  4. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Arbitrarily using electricity works of buyers to supply electricity to other organizations or individuals using electricity;

  b/ Cutting off the power supply to organizations or individuals not required to be restricted in use in case of power shortage, except for cases where there is a risk of causing serious and unsafe incidents to people and equipment.

  5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to sign economic contracts on electricity sale and purchase after ten days as from the date the electricity buyer has ensured all conditions prescribed and agreed upon in the draft contract;

  b) Cutting off power without notice in accordance with the procedures for stopping power supply.

  6. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Delaying the repair of the power grid after an incident occurs without a plausible reason;

  b) Using electricity metering equipment which has not been inspected and sealed by competent authorities as prescribed or deliberately using electricity metering systems with error exceeding the prescribed value.

  7. A fine of between VND 15,000,000 and 30,000,000 shall be imposed for acts of abusing professional positions to harass organizations and individuals using electricity for self-seeking purposes.

  Article 9. Violations of regulations on electricity use

  1. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for acts of obstructing competent persons to inspect the use of electricity.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 700,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily opening, cutting, repairing or replacing electrical equipment and works of the electricity seller;

  b) Arbitrarily moving or falsifying the electricity metering system (including sealing and wiring diagrams), electrical equipment and works of the electricity seller;

  c) Unintentionally causing faults in the electricity system of the electricity seller;

  d) Using interfering devices to damage or falsify data transmission, measurement and protection equipment of the electricity system.

  3. To impose a fine on acts of stealing electricity in any form for daily life or consumption:

  a) A fine of between 200,000 VND and 400,000 VND shall be imposed on acts of stealing electricity with quantity of under 100 kWh;

  b) A fine of between 300,000 VND and 700,000 VND shall be imposed on acts of stealing electricity with quantity of from 100 kWh to less than 200 kWh;

  c) A fine of between 1,000,000 VND and 2,000,000 VND shall be imposed on acts of stealing electricity with quantity of from 200 kWh to less than 300 kWh;

  d) A fine of between 2,000,000 dong and 3,000,000 dong shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with a quantity of between 300 kWh and under 400 kWh;

  dd) A fine of between 3,000,000 dong and 5,000,000 dong shall be imposed for acts of electricity theft with a quantity of between 400 kWh and under 500 kWh.

  4. A fine of between 3,000,000 dong and 5,000,000 dong shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Arbitrarily turning on the electricity supply when the electricity project of the electricity purchaser has not been tested and handed over or when being suspended from power supply due to violations of regulations on electricity use;

  b) Using other power sources without permission of the electricity seller when the power sources permitted for use have been used in case of using electricity for production, business and service provision;

  c) Arbitrarily selling electricity to other organizations and individuals without consent of the electricity seller;

  d) Using electricity for fishing, trapping rats, preventing theft, protecting assets, crops or other acts violating regulations on safety in electricity use.

  5. To impose a fine on acts of electricity theft in any form to serve purposes other than those specified in Clause 3 of this Article;

  a) A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft of < 100 kWh;

  b) A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft of from 100 kWh to under 200 kWh;

  c) A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft of from 200 kWh to under 300 kWh;

  d) A fine ranging from VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft of from 300 kWh to under 400 kWh;

  dd) A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for electricity theft of from 400 kWh to under 500 kWh.

  6. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, the violating individual or organization may also have the exhibits and means used for committing the violation in Clause 2, Clause 3 and Clause 5 of this Article confiscated.

  7. In addition to the main forms of sanction and additional sanctions against violations specified in this Article, the violating individual or organization may also be compelled to restore the initial state altered due to the violation specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Clause 4 and Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grid

  1. A warning or a fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 shall be imposed for acts of climbing on power poles or entering power stations without permission.

  2. A fine of between VND 100,000 and 300,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Flying kites, balloons, remote-controlled toy planes or other flying objects within the protection perimeter of electrical works or safety corridor of power lines; tying cattle, vehicles or tools to power poles or walls of power stations;

  b) Installing antennas, drying ropes, box signs, advertising lights or other objects within the protection perimeter of electrical works, safety corridor of power lines or at positions where they may cause unsafe breakdown of lines or other parts of electrical works or threaten human lives;

  c) Cutting trees, planting trees, pruning branches not in compliance with regulations within the protection perimeter of electrical works or safety corridor of power lines;

  d) Cutting trees, pruning branches outside the protection perimeter of electrical works or safety corridor of power lines without taking measures to prevent them from hitting the lines or other parts of electrical works.

  3. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 700,000 shall be imposed for acts of violating safety of foundations, walls and fences of power stations, power poles or other structures and parts of electrical works.

  4. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Failing to place warning signs, signal lights and danger indication at the location of the electrical works as prescribed or failing to place landmarks or signs along the underground cable;

  b) Placing or storing flammable and/or explosive substances outside the protection perimeter of the electrical works or outside the safety corridor of the power grid in violation of regulations on fire and explosion safety, thus affecting the safety of the electrical works;

  c) Setting up booths, shops or other structures which affect water drainage, block the air vents or obstruct the access to the substations.

  5. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 4,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Placing or storing flammable and/or explosive substances within the protection perimeter of the electrical works or within the safety corridor of the power grid;

  b) Firing forests, fields or other substances in violation of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety corridor of the power grid;

  c) Taking advantage of the electrical works’ components and structures for other purposes without permission of the competent authority;

  d) Using tools and equipment with height exceeding the safe distance as prescribed by law on safety protection of high-voltage power grid;

  dd) Removing, erasing or covering the power lines, safety signs and signals on poles within the protection perimeter of the electrical works or the safety protection corridor of the power grid, substations and power plants;

  e) Damaging power poles, power lines, insulation materials, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, power cables and other parts and structures of the electrical works with voltage of under 1 kV.

  6. A fine of between 3,000,000 dong and 6,000,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of damaging power poles, power lines, insulation materials, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, power cables and other parts and structures of the electrical works with voltage of between 1 kV and 110 kV.

  7. A fine of between 4,000,000 dong and 8,000,000 dong shall be imposed for the act of damaging power poles, power lines, insulation materials, lightning protection wires, earthing wires, power cables and other parts and structures of the electrical works with voltage of over 110 kV.

  8. A fine of between 5,000,000 dong and 10,000,000 dong shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a) Building new houses, upgrading or renovating houses, setting up booths, shops or other works in violation of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of high-voltage power grids;

  b) Failing to ensure safety conditions for houses and lawful construction works left in the safety protection corridor of high-voltage power grids as prescribed;

  c) Executing works in the ground or dredging the river or lake within the safety protection corridor of underground power cable when there is no agreement with the electricity work management agency;

  d) Discharge waste and wastewater containing corrosive substances in the safety protection corridor of underground power cables;

  dd) Mining in violation of regulations on safety protection of electrical works and safety protection corridors of power grid;

  e) Failing to maintain a safe distance as prescribed by law when designing, building, or repairing electrical works;

  g) Using electricity as a means of direct protection against unauthorized use as permitted by competent authorities.

  9. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for acts of digging holes, setting piles, anchoring ships or other acts of violating protection corridors of underground power cables which have been put up with signboards or signals.

  10. A fine ranging from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for building a wharf, constructing a building or other works within the safety perimeter of a power plant or the cable protection corridor where warning signs have been installed or are expected to be installed.

  11. Depending on the nature and severity of their violations, violating individuals or organizations may also have the exhibits and means used for committing violations confiscated, applicable to the acts specified in Clause 2, Clause 3, Point b Clause 4, Points a, d and e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7, Point c Clause 8, Clause 9 and Clause 10 of this Article.

  12. In addition to the main forms of sanction and additional sanctions for violations prescribed in this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the field of electricity may also be subject to the following remedial measures:

  a) Enforced dismantling of illegally constructed works in case of administrative violations against regulations on electricity specified in Point b Clause 2, Point c Clause 4, Point c Clause 5, Point a and Point e Clause 8 and Clause 10 of this Article;

  b) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been changed due to violations specified in Point c Clause 2, Clause 3, Point c Clause 4, Point c, Point dd and Point e Clause 5, Clause 6, Clause 7 and Clause 10 of this Article.

  Chapter 3:

    POWER TO AND PROCEDURES FOR SANCTIONING ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON ELECTRICITY

  Article 11. Power to sanction of Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

  Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels have power to sanction administrative violations against regulations on electricity occurring in localities under their management, specifically as follows:

  1. Chairpersons of People’s Committees of communes shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 500,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the electricity sector with a value of up to VND 500,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state altered due to the administrative violation in the electricity sector.

  2. Chairpersons of People’s Committees of districts have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 20,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the electricity sector;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state altered due to the administrative violation in the electricity sector;

  dd) Enforced dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity.

  3. Chairpersons of provincial People’s Committees have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 30.000.000;

  c) Suspend the license for electricity operation issued by the People's Committee of the province or city directly under the Central Government;

  d) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations against regulations on electricity;

  dd) Enforce restoration of the original condition altered by the administrative violation against regulations on electricity;

  e) Enforce dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity.

  Article 12. Power inspection authority

  Electricity inspectors of the Ministry of Industry have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity across the country. Electricity inspectors of provincial Departments of Industry have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity occurring within their provinces and cities. The power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity of electricity inspectors is specified as follows:

  1. The electricity inspector on duty has the power to:

  a) Warning;

  b) A fine of up to VND 200,000;

  c) Confiscating exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations in the electricity sector with a value of up to VND 2,000,000;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation in the field of electricity.

  2. Chief Inspector of Electricity Department of the Provincial Department of Industry has the right to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 20,000,000;

  c) Confiscate exhibits and equipment used for committing administrative violations against regulations on electricity;

  d) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered by the administrative violation against regulations on electricity;

  dd) Enforced dismantling of illegally or improperly constructed works in violation of laws on electricity.

  3. Chief Inspector of Electricity Inspectorate of Ministry of Industry shall have the power to:

  a) Issue warnings;

  b) Impose a fine up to VND 30,000,000;

  c) Revoke the right to use electricity operation license issued by the Ministry of Industry and People’s Committees of provinces or central-affiliated cities;

  d) Confiscate material evidences and means used for administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  dd) Compel the restoration of the initial state altered due to administrative violations in the field of electricity;

  e) Compel the dismantlement of illegally or improperly constructed works violating regulations of law in the field of electricity operation.

  Article 13. Sanctioning competence of other forces

  Apart from the persons specified in Article 11 and Article 12 of this Decree, the following competent persons when detecting acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity prescribed in this Decree under their respective management fields and areas shall have the sanctioning competence, specifically as follows:

  1. People’s police officers on duty shall have the sanctioning competence for administrative violations in the field of electricity prescribed in:

  a) Point a Clause 1 Article 7;

  b) Point c Clause 3 Article 8;

  c) Clauses 1, 3 and 5 Article 9;

  d) Clauses 1, 2, 3, Points b and c Clause 4, Points a, b, c, dd and e Clause 5, Clauses 6, 7, Points dd and g Clause 8, Clauses 9 and 10 Article 10.

  2. Construction inspectors have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity as prescribed in:

  a) Point a Clause 2 Article 7;

  b) Clauses 3, Points a and c Clause 8 and Clause 10 Article 10.

  3. Environmental inspectors have the power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity as prescribed in Point d Clause 8 Article 10.

  Article 14. Delegation of power and principles for determination of power to impose penalties for administrative violations against regulations on electricity

  1. The authorization for sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity shall comply with the provisions of Article 41 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. The principles for determining the competence to sanction administrative violations in the field of electricity shall comply with Article 42 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 15.- Procedures for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. The procedures and order for sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity shall comply with the provisions of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Documents related to the sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity must be fully kept at the sanctioning agencies. The records of administrative violations shall be made according to the form prescribed in this Decree, except for cases where simple procedures are applied as prescribed in Article 54 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  3. Individuals or organizations that are fined must pay fines within the time limit and at the places stated in the sanctioning decisions, except for cases where fines have already been paid on the spot as prescribed in Articles 54 and 58 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and they are entitled to receive fine receipts. The collection of fines must use receipts issued by the Ministry of Finance under current regulations.

  The Ministry of Finance shall assume the prime responsibility for, and coordinate with the Ministry of Industry in, guiding the collection and use of fines for acts of administrative violation in the field of electricity.

  4. The procedures for stripping off the right to use the electricity operation permits must comply with the provisions of Article 59 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  When applying the form of confiscating material evidences and means of violation, the persons with sanctioning competence must strictly comply with the provisions in Article 60 and Article 61 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Article 16.- Execution of decisions on sanctioning and coercive execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity under this Decree shall have to strictly execute the sanctioning decisions within ten days after being handed over the sanctioning decisions, except otherwise provided for by law. If sanctioned individuals or organizations fail to voluntarily execute the sanctioning decisions, they shall be coerced to execute them according to Article 66 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. When applying measures to coerce the execution of decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity, competent agencies and persons must abide by the coercion order and procedures prescribed in Articles 66 and 67 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                          SETTLEMENT OF COMPLAINTS, DENUNCIATIONS AND HANDLING OF VIOLATIONS

  Article 17.- Complaints, denunciations and settlement of complaints and denunciations against decisions on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. Individuals and organizations sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity or their lawful representatives may lodge complaints or lawsuits about the sanctioning of them according to law provisions.

  2. All citizens have the right to denounce with competent state agencies on administrative violations in the field of electricity of individuals and organizations and denounce acts of violation of persons with competence to sanction administrative violations in the field of electricity.

  3. Competence, procedures, order and time limit for settlement of complaints, denunciations, lawsuits against administrative violations shall comply with provisions of the Law on Complaints and Denunciations and Ordinance on Procedures for Settlement of Administrative Cases (amended) of January 5, 1999.

  Article 18.- Handling of violations committed by persons with sanctioning competence and sanctioned persons for administrative violations in the field of electricity

  1. Persons with competence to sanction administrative violations in the field of electricity who commit acts of harassment, tolerance or covering up of violations, failure to handle or handling not in time, improperly, handling beyond prescribed competence, appropriation or illegal use of money, material evidences and means used in violations shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, be disciplined or examined for penal liability; if causing material damage, they must pay compensations therefor according to law provisions.

  2. Persons sanctioned for administrative violations in the field of electricity who commit acts of opposing officials on duty, delaying or shirking the execution of sanctioning decisions or other acts of violation shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, be administratively sanctioned according to the Government’s regulations on sanctioning of administrative violations in the domain of security and order or examined for penal liability; if causing material damage, they must pay compensations therefor according to law provisions.

  Chapter V

                                                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 19.- Implementation effect

  This Decree takes effect 15 days after its publication in CONG BAO. All previous regulations on sanctioning of administrative violations in the field of electricity which are contrary to this Decree are now annulled.

  Article 20. Implementation

  The ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.
