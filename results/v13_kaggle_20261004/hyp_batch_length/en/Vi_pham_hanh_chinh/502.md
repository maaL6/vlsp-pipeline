                                                    DECREES:

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1.- Administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of a number of social evils.

  Administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of a number of social evils prescribed in this Decree include administrative violations in circulation and trading of films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs; selling and leasing publications; cultural activities and cultural services at public places; advertising and writing, putting up signboards; prevention of prostitution, drug abuse and gambling.

  Article 2.- Combating administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of a number of social evils.

  All acts of violation in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of social evils must be detected in time and stopped immediately. The sanctioning must be conducted quickly and fairly; all consequences caused by administrative violations must be remedied according to law.

  Article 3.- Subjects to be sanctioned for administrative violations.

  Individuals and organizations that commit acts of administrative violation in cultural activities, cultural services and prevention of a number of social evils shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in this Decree.

  The heads of State agencies, organizations, State officials and employees who commit acts of violation prescribed in this Decree shall, apart from being administratively sanctioned, also be disciplined according to law provisions.

  Article 4.- Application of sanctioning forms and measures.

  1. When sanctioning in the form of fines, the specific fine level for an act of administrative violation is the average level of the fine bracket prescribed for such act; if the violation involves extenuating circumstances, the fine level may be reduced but not lower than the minimum level of the fine bracket; if the violation involves aggravating circumstances, the fine level may be increased but not higher than the maximum level of the fine bracket.

  Extenuating and aggravating circumstances shall be applied according to the provisions of Articles 7 and 8 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  2. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures must be applied together with the main sanctioning form if this Decree provides for additional sanctioning and application of other measures to acts of administrative violation in order to thoroughly handle violations, eliminate the causes and conditions for continued violations and overcome all consequences caused by administrative violations.

  All films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, books, newspapers, pictures, calendars and other cultural products with indecent or pornographic contents or those that incite violence must be confiscated and destroyed.

  Article 5.- Handling of violations committed by persons competent to sanction administrative violations.

  + If a person who has no authority over the case commits an act of violation beyond his/her competence, he/she shall, depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation, be disciplined or examined for penal liability; if causing material damage, he/she shall pay compensation therefor according to law provisions. The right to sanction administrative violations but harassing people, tolerating or covering up acts of administrative violation committed by individuals or organizations, failing to sanction or sanctioning not in time, shall be exercised by the competent person.

  Section 3: ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS OF SOCIAL ECONOMICS, FORMS AND LEVELS OF SANCTION

  Article 18.- Sanctioning acts of buying sex, selling sex, brothel keeping and prostitution brokerage.

  1. Persons who commit acts of buying sex, selling sex, brothel keeping and prostitution brokerage shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Article 4 of the Government's Decree No. 53-CP of June 28, 1994.

  1. For persons who commit acts of buying and/or selling sex but not on a regular basis, the commune-level People's Committee presidents of the localities where such persons reside shall consider deciding to apply the measure of education at the commune, ward or township.

  2. For persons who commit acts of regular prostitution and have been repeatedly educated by the local authorities and people but still refuse to reform themselves, the commune-level People's Committee presidents of the localities where such persons reside shall compile dossiers for the district-level People's Committee presidents to propose the provincial-level People's Committee presidents to send them to medical treatment establishments; if they are minors, they shall be sent to reformatories.

  3. The procedures for applying the measure of education at the commune, ward or township, sending to reformatories or medical treatment establishments shall comply with the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations.

  4. A fine of between VND 500,000 and 1,500,000 for acts of brothel dealing but not on a regular basis which, however, is not serious enough to warrant examination for penal liability.

  5. A fine of between VND 1,500,000 and 5,000,000 for acts of regular prostitution which, however, is not serious enough to warrant examination for penal liability.

  6. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for acts of prostitution which, however, is not serious enough to warrant examination for penal liability.

  Article 19.- Sanctioning acts of using, producing, storing, trading in and transporting narcotics

  1. Persons who commit acts of using narcotics in all forms shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels of sanction prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2, Article 5 of Decree No.53-CP of June 28, 1994 of the Government.

  1. Persons who have committed acts of drug use but not yet reached the level of addiction shall be considered and decided by the presidents of the commune-level People's Committees of the localities where such persons reside to apply measures of education at communes, wards or district towns.

  2. For drug addicts who have been educated many times by the local administration and people but still refuse to reform themselves, the presidents of the commune-level People's Committees of the localities where such persons reside shall compile dossiers for the presidents of the district-level People's Committees to propose the provincial-level People's Committees to send them to medical treatment establishments; if they are minors, they shall be sent to reformatories.

  3. The procedures for applying the measure of education at communes, wards or district towns, sending to reformatories or medical treatment establishments shall comply with the provisions of law on handling of administrative violations.

  4. Persons who illegally produce, store, trade in or transport narcotics but not yet liable to criminal prosecution shall be sanctioned according to the forms and levels prescribed in Clause 3, Article 5 of the Government's Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  5. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  Confiscating material evidences and means used in the commission of violations specified in Clauses 1, 2 and 4.

  7. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to the violations mentioned in this Article:

  a/ Confiscation of money gained from the violation, material evidences and means used in the violation, for the violations specified in Clause 2; Point a, Clause 3; Points c and d, Clause 4; Clause 5;

  b/ Confiscation and destruction of sheets prescribed at Point c, Clause 3.

  Article 21.- Violations of regulations on the prevention and combat of social evils in the massage service.

  1. A fine of 200,000 to 500,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a/ Leasing or lending certificates and professional technical diplomas in massage or physiotherapy;

  b/ Practicing massage without certificates or professional technical diplomas in massage or physiotherapy or using certificates or diplomas of other persons.

  2. A fine of 500,000 to 1,000,000 VND for acts of employing persons who have no certificates or professional technical diplomas in massage or physiotherapy to practice massage.

  3. A fine of 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 VND for acts of transferring certificates of qualification and conditions for massage practice to other establishments or using certificates of other establishments to provide massage services.

  4. A fine of 5,000,000 to 10,000,000 VND for acts of providing massage services without certificates of qualification and conditions for massage practice.

  5. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations specified in this Article:

  Depriving the right to use certificates, professional technical diplomas in massage or physiotherapy, certificates of qualification and conditions for massage practice, for violations specified in Clauses 1 and 3.

  Article 22.- Violations of regulations on prevention and combat of social evils in operation of accommodation establishments and restaurants.

  1. A fine of between VND 200,000 and 500,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to number rooms of accommodation establishments or restaurants in conformity with the registered plans;

  b/ Hanging up or displaying pictures or photos with a pornographic or provocative nature at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  c/ Failing to register accommodation for their guests;

  d/ Failing to declare temporary residence as prescribed for guests staying at accommodation establishments;

  e/ Locking or closing the inside doors of dining rooms or refreshment rooms of accommodation establishments or restaurants when they are occupied by guests;

  f/ Using dim lights or lights which fail to ensure sufficient light as prescribed in dining rooms, refreshment rooms or entertainment and recreation rooms of accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  g/ Using sexual provocation service methods at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  h. Failing to register the list of waiters and staff members with competent police offices.

  2. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 for acts of employing waiters and staff members working in accommodation establishments serving meals without concluding labor contracts.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Circulating music tapes and discs which have been banned from circulation or withdrawn under decisions issued at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  b/ Circulating music tapes and discs with pornographic or violent contents at accommodation establishments or restaurants.

  4. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Circulating films, video tapes and discs, performing theatrical works, musical works or dance works which have been banned from circulation or withdrawn under decisions issued at accommodation establishments or restaurants;

  b/ Circulating films, video tapes and discs, performing theatrical works, musical works or dance works with pornographic or violent contents at accommodation establishments or restaurants.

  5. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations of this Article:

  a/ Confiscation of films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, for violations specified at Point a, Clause 3; Point a, Clause 4;

  b/ Confiscation and destruction of pictures, photos, films, video tapes and discs, audio tapes and discs, for violations specified at Point b, Clause 1; Point b, Clause 3; Point b, Clause 4;

  c/ Deprivation of the right to use business licenses, for violations specified in Clauses 3 and 4.

  Deprivation of the right to use professional practice permits for up to six months, for performers who commit violations specified in Clause 4.

  6. For state accommodation establishments or restaurants, apart from being fined and having their evidences confiscated, the persons in charge of such establishments or restaurants shall also be disciplined according to law provisions.

  Article 23.- Tolerance, cover-up, enticing and harboring of social evils.

  1. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for directors of hotels, owners of hotels, guest houses, restaurants, inns, motels, massage establishments who tolerate or, due to irresponsibility, let acts of prostitution, drug abuse, gambling occur in establishments under their management.

  Apart from the above-mentioned fines, the violating establishment may have its business license withdrawn; if the violator is a State official, he/she shall also be disciplined.

  2. The head of the person who commits acts of buying and/or selling sex, using narcotics or gambling but covers up or fails to promptly discipline the violator; or the person who is tasked with directly combating social evils but tolerates, covers up or fails to promptly handle these acts in the area under his/her management shall be disciplined according to the provisions of Clauses 1 and 2, Article 8 of the Government’s Decree No. 53/CP of June 28, 1994.

  3. The director of hotel, owner of hotel, guest house, restaurant, inn, massage establishment or any other establishment that organizes gambling, facilitates gambling, or provides accommodation for, entices or leads prostitutes or organizations using narcotics in whatever form, but does not fall into the cases prescribed in Clauses 4, 5 and 6, Article 18; Clause 4, Article 19; Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5, Article 20 of this Decree, shall be examined for penal liability according to Articles 200, 202 and 203 of the Penal Code; in case of enticing or accommodating minors to engage in prostitution, use narcotics or gamble, they shall be examined for penal liability according to Article 148 of the Penal Code.

  Section 4. ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF ADVERTISING AND PUBLICATION OF SIGNATURES, FORMS AND LEVELS OF SANCTION

  Article 24.- Violations of the regulations on procedures for asking for permission for advertising.

  1. A caution or a fine of up to 20,000 VND shall be imposed on each advertisement in posters, loose-leaf publications or folded leaflets without permit.

  2. A fine of between 100,000 VND and 300,000 VND shall be imposed on one of the following acts:

  a) Advertising without permit;

  b/ Receiving and displaying advertisement boards or billboards without obtaining the license from advertisers.

  3. A fine of 500,000 to 1,500,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to write on advertisement boards or billboards the number of the license, the time limit for advertisement or name of the owner of the advertisement service;

  b/ Advertising products or goods or introducing activities of establishments without certification, registration or approval by specialized State management agencies as prescribed.

  4. A fine of 1,500,000 to 5,000,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising medicinal materials, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, medical instruments and medical activities without approval by competent health agencies;

  b/ Advertising with advertisement boards or billboards beyond the time limit prescribed in the license;

  c/ Advertising on publications or means of transport without license.

  5. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for act of transferring license of business in advertising service, license of conducting advertisement to other organizations or individuals or using license of other organizations or individuals to conduct advertising service.

  6. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Conducting advertising service without license;

  b/ Conducting advertising cooperation service with foreign individuals or organizations without business cooperation contract;

  c/ Conducting advertisement without license at places of cultural and artistic activities;

  d/ Hanging up, erecting, placing boards, signboards for advertisement without license.

  7. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 shall be imposed on foreign individuals or organizations that conduct advertising service in Vietnam or conduct advertisement without signing contracts with Vietnamese advertisers.

  8. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  a/ Stripping of the right to use permits in cases of violation specified in Clause 5;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of barricades, tape, loose sheets, folded sheets or advertising boards and signs in cases of violation specified in Clause 1; Points a and c, Clause 2; Points b and c, Clause 4; Point d, Clause 6; Clause 7.

  Article 25.- Violations of regulations on forms and methods of advertising:

  1. Caution or a fine of up to 50,000 VND for acts of using sound with the prescribed volume level for advertising from 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.

  2. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 1,000,000 for acts of advertising on advertising boards and signs with shapes, colors and forms similar to traffic signals and public signboards.

  3. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 2,000,000 for each square meter of area exceeded once, one day, one percent or more for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising on advertising boards and signs with an area exceeding the prescribed area (m2) in the permit;

  b/ Advertising on radio and television stations with the number of times exceeding the permitted number for a day or the permitted time (%)

  c/ Advertising on printed newspapers, radio and television stations for more days than allowed in a campaign;

  d/ Advertising on printed newspapers for more area (%) than allowed.

  4. A fine of 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a/ Modifying the form of advertising boards or billboards compared with the set form prescribed in the license;

  b/ Advertising goods, products or activities not clearly named but only general brand names or company names;

  c/ Advertising on printed newspapers, radio and television stations in violation of the minimum distance (5 days) between two advertising campaigns.

  5. A fine of 5,000,000 to 10,000,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising on the front page of daily, weekly or monthly newspapers or on the front cover of magazines, special issues or supplements;

  b/ Trading in goods or products banned from advertisement.

  c/ Inserting advertisement in the news, articles or programs on radio and television, except for live broadcast of foreign programs.

  6. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for act of producing goods or products banned from advertisement.

  7. A fine of between VND 40,000,000 and 50,000,000 for act of using national flags, national anthems, national emblems, portraits of leaders, Party flags or international anthem as background for advertisement presentation.

  8. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to acts of violation specified in this Article:

  a/ Confiscation of goods, for violations specified at Point b, Clause 5; Clause 6;

  b/ Forcible dismantlement of advertisement boards and signs, for violations specified in Clause 2; Point a, Clause 3; Points a and b, Clause 4 and Clause 7.

  Article 26.- Violations of regulations on places, positions and scope of advertisement.

  1. A warning or a fine of up to VND 10,000 shall be imposed for each advertisement on posters, loose-leaf or folded leaflets not placed at the prescribed places in the license.

  2. A fine of between VND 50,000 and 150,000 shall be imposed for advertisement on tape not placed at the prescribed places in the license.

  3. A fine of between VND 300,000 and 1,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following acts:

  a/ Placing advertisement boards or signs not at the places prescribed in the license;

  b/ Placing advertisement boards or signs within the protection perimeter of traffic works, thus affecting traffic order and safety.

  4. A fine of between VND 2,000,000 and 5,000,000 shall be imposed for advertisement at places banned from advertisement.

  5. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 shall be imposed for providing advertisement services beyond the scope of practice or area of operation prescribed in the license.

  6. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations specified in this Article:

  a/ Stripping of the right to use licenses for advertising services in case of recidivism of violation of Clause 5;

  b/ Forcible dismantling of signboards, posters, loose sheets, folded sheets or boards and screens for advertising in case of violations specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4.

  Article 27.- Violations of regulations on contents of advertisements:

  1. A fine of 200,000 to 500,000 VND for acts of using pictures, photos, posters, loose sheets, folded sheets, umbrellas, trolleys or boxes for advertising goods or products banned from advertisement.

  2. A fine of 500,000 to 1,500,000 VND for one of the following acts:

  a/ Offering goods or products banned from advertisement;

  b/ Advertising goods or products banned from advertisement on signboards;

  c/ Advertising contents for press, publications or artistic works which have not yet been granted permission for publication, distribution, dissemination or performance.

  3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for act of modifying in a wrongful manner contents of approved advertisement boards and signs in licenses.

  4. A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 20,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Making untruthful or unquality advertisement on registered goods;

  b/ Making advertisement with contents which reduce prestige and quality of goods of other organizations and individuals.

  5. A fine of between VND 30,000,000 and 50,000,000 for one of the following acts:

  a/ Advertising goods and products which are banned from advertisement on advertisement boards and signs, means of transport or at places of public cultural activities;

  b/ Advertising goods and products which are banned from advertisement on radio and television, publications, video tapes and disks, music tapes and disks.

  6. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations in this Article:

  a/ Confiscating goods or products in case of violations specified in Clause 1 and Point a, Clause 2;

  b/ Forcing the dismantlement of advertising boards and signs in case of violations specified at Point b, Clause 2; Clause 3; Clause 4; Point a, Clause 5;

  c/ Forcing the compensation for actual damage not exceeding VND 1,000,000 in case of violations specified at Point b, Clause 4; if the damage exceeds VND 1,000,000, the compensation must be settled according to civil proceedings.

  Article 28.- Violations of regulations on writing and putting up signboards

  1. A fine of between VND 50,000 and VND 150,000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts:

  a/ Putting up signboards not attached to head offices of agencies, organizations, hotels, restaurants, shops or stalls with signboards;

  b/ Failing to write down or to write in Vietnamese the full name on the signboard.

  2. A fine of between VND 150,000 and VND 500,000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts:

  a/ Failing to adequately display the contents prescribed on the signboards;

  b/ Using names and/or scripts in Vietnamese on the signboards of agencies, organizations, enterprises or companies of Vietnam while using names and/or scripts in foreign languages;

  c/ Displaying names and/or scripts in foreign languages on the signboards with more prominent colors and/or lights than the names and/or scripts in Vietnamese;

  d/ Signboards of economic organizations bearing abbreviated names, international transaction names or names in foreign languages which are written in Vietnamese above or bigger than the Vietnamese ones;

  e/ Signboards bearing advertisement contents.

  3. Additional sanctioning forms and other measures applicable to violations specified in this Article:

  Forcible dismantlement of signboards, for violations specified at Point a, Clause 1 and Clause 2.

  Chapter III

                       POWER AND PROCEDURES FOR IMPOSING ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES

  Article 29.- The competence of the People’s Committees at all levels to sanction administrative violations.

  The presidents of the People’s Committees at all levels shall have the right to sanction according to their competence prescribed in Articles 26, 27 and 28 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations within the localities under their management for violations prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 30.- The competence of specialized cultural and information inspection agencies to sanction administrative violations.

  The specialized inspection force of Culture and Information shall have the right to sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for violations in cultural activities and cultural services prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 31.- The competence of the police to sanction administrative violations.

  The People's Police Force shall have the right to impose penalties within its jurisdiction as prescribed in Article 29 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for social evils and violations in cultural activities and services related to social order and safety under its jurisdiction as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 32.- The competence of the market management agencies to sanction administrative violations.

  The market management force may sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 33 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in the business of films, video tapes and disks, music tapes and disks, books, newspapers, pictures, photos, calendars and business activities of accommodation establishments and restaurants prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 33.- The border guard and customs offices' competence to sanction administrative violations.

  The border guards and customs offices shall have the right to sanction according to their competence prescribed in Articles 29 and 30 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in the management of the export and import of films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, books, newspapers, pictures, photos and calendars prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 34.- The competence of the specialized medical inspectorate to sanction administrative violations.

  The specialized medical inspectorate has the right to sanction according to its competence prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations acts of violation in massage service, advertisement of pharmaceutical products, medicinal materials, cosmetics, medical instruments and medical activities not yet approved by competent medical agencies prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 35.- The competence of the specialized labor inspection agency to sanction administrative violations.

  Specialized labor inspectorates shall have the right to impose penalties within their competence as prescribed in Article 34 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations for acts of using labor without signing labor contracts as prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 36.- Collection and payment of fines.

  Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services or social evil prevention and combat shall have to pay fines at prescribed places; if failing to do so within the prescribed time limit, they shall be forced to pay fines.

  Sanctioning officials are strictly forbidden to collect fines on the spot.

  Article 37.- Confiscation and handling of material evidences and means used in commission of administrative violations.

  When applying the sanctioning form of confiscating material evidences and means used in commission of administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services or social evil prevention and combat, the persons with sanctioning competence must strictly observe the procedures prescribed in Articles 51 and 52 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations and the following provisions:

  a/ For material evidences of administrative violations being films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications containing no violation of contents and means used in commission of violations, the persons who issue the confiscation decisions must transfer them to finance agencies of the same level for organizing valuation councils (with representatives of the Culture and Information Service as compulsory members) and auction.

  b/ For material evidences of administrative violations being unlabeled video tapes or discs or tapes and discs with false labels, films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications not yet permitted for circulation, the persons who issue the confiscation decisions must transfer them to the Culture and Information Services of the same level for conducting the expertise; where films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications, after the expertise, are concluded not to contain any violation of contents and may be circulated, they shall be handed over to the Finance Agency of the same level for organizing valuation councils and auction according to the provisions at Point a of this Article; where films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications, after the expertise, are concluded to contain indecent, pornographic contents or incite violence, the Culture and Information Services shall conduct the destruction thereof according to the provisions in Article 38 of this Decree.

  c/ For films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications, which have been subject to circulation ban decisions or revocation decisions, the persons who issue the confiscation decisions must transfer them to the Culture and Information Services for handling according to their competence.

  d/ All cases of transfer and handling of material evidences and means used in commission of administrative violations prescribed at Points a, b and c of this Article must be recorded in minutes, which bear the signatures of the concerned parties.

  Article 38.- Destruction of material evidences of administrative violations.

  1. For material evidences being films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications with clearly indecent, pornographic or violent contents, the persons who issue sanctioning decisions shall decide on their destruction according to competence or transfer them to competent agencies for destruction.

  2. For material evidences being films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications with signs of indecency, pornography or violence, the persons with sanctioning competence must transfer them to the competent cultural and information agencies for assessment; if after assessment, the conclusion is that the material evidences have indecent, pornographic or violent contents, the cultural and information agencies shall organize the burning of such material evidences.

  In case the films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications, after assessment, are concluded not to have indecent, pornographic or violent contents, they must be transferred to the finance agency of the same level for organization of the valuation council and auction as prescribed in Article 37 of this Decree.

  3. When destroying films, video tapes and discs, music tapes and discs, publications with indecent, pornographic or violent contents, the agencies applying this measure must set up a handling council composed of representatives of the cultural and information agencies, the police office, the finance agency and the People's Procuracy of the same level; in case of necessity, representatives of concerned agencies and organizations may be invited to witness the destruction.

  The destruction must be recorded in writing with the certification of the members of the handling council.

  4. The Ministry of Culture and Information shall guide in detail the destruction procedures prescribed in this Article.

  Article 39.- Complaints and denunciations.

  1. Citizens may denounce to competent State agencies administrative violations committed by organizations and individuals in cultural activities, cultural services and social evils prevention and combat prescribed in this Decree or denounce violations committed by persons with competence to sanction administrative violations prescribed in this Decree.

  2. Organizations and individuals sanctioned for administrative violations or their lawful representatives may lodge complaints to persons who have issued sanctioning decisions within 10 days after receiving such decisions.

  3. The order of and procedures for lodging complaints and denunciations and competence to settle complaints and denunciations about administrative violations in cultural activities, cultural services and social evils prevention and combat shall comply with the provisions of Articles 87, 88 and 90 of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  Chapter IV

                                           IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 40.- This Decree takes effect as from February 1st, 1996.

  The previous provisions which are contrary to this Decree are now annulled.

  Article 41.- The Minister of Culture and Information, the Minister of Labor, War Invalids and Social Affairs shall, within the ambit of their respective functions and tasks, have to guide the implementation of this Decree.

  Article 42.- The ministers, the heads of the ministerial-level agencies and agencies attached to the Government, the presidents of the People’s Committees of the provinces and cities directly under the Central Government shall have to perform this task.
