                                             DECREE
           REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN THE FIELD OF TOURISM
Pursuant to the December 25, 2001 Law on Government Organization;
Pursuant to the Law on Tourism dated June 14, 2005;
Pursuant to the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated July 2, 2002, and the Ordinance on Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Ordinance on Handling of Administrative Violations dated April 2, 2008;
At the request of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
The Government promulgates a Decree prescribing penalties for administrative violations in the field of tourism, Chapter 1.
                                           GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1. Scope
1. This Decree provides for administrative violations, penalties, fines, authority to impose penalties, procedures for imposing penalties and remedial measures in tourism.
2. Administrative violations in tourism are violations against regulations of law on state management in tourism committed by individuals or organizations intentionally or unintentionally and not considered crimes and subject to administrative penalties as per the law.
3. Administrative violations in tourism prescribed in this Decree include:
a) Violations against regulations on tour operator business, tour guide business, car business;
b) Violations against regulations on tourist accommodation business;
c) Violations against regulations on tourism promotion;
d) Violations against other regulations on tourism.
4. Other administrative violations in the field of tourism and tourist service provision not prescribed in this Decree shall be handled according to other Government’s Decrees on handling of administrative violations in related state management fields.
Article 2. Subjects of application
Vietnamese organizations and individuals and foreign organizations and individuals (hereinafter referred to as individuals and organizations) committing administrative violations in the field of tourism in the territory of Vietnam.
Article 3. Forms of sanction and remedies
1. For each administrative violation, violating individuals or organizations must take one of the following forms of sanction:
a) Warning;
b) Fine.
2. Depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation, violating individuals or organizations may also be subject to one or several of the following additional forms of sanction:
a) Depriving the right to use international tour operator licenses, tour guide badges and/or interpreters’ certificates for a definite period of time;
b) Confiscating material evidences and means used for committing administrative violations.
3. In addition to the main forms of sanction and additional sanctions prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, depending on the nature and seriousness of their violations, the violators may be subject to one or more of the following remedial measures:
a) Compulsory provision of adequate interior furnishings, amenities, means, equipment and devices to ensure the health, safety and life of tourists and their property;
b) Compulsory compliance with regulations on making and keeping records and documents as prescribed by law;

c) Compulsory return to tourists or confiscation to the state budget of illegally collected property of tourists;
d) Compulsory payment of the deposit as prescribed;
dd) Compulsory compliance with reporting regulations as prescribed by law;
e) Enforced installation of signboards on cars meeting standards for tourist service as prescribed by law;
g) Enforced provision of adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages of managers and staff corresponding to standards of each type and class of accommodation establishment as prescribed by law;
h) Enforced installation of signboards of tourist accommodation establishments in accordance with classification decisions issued by competent authorities;
i) Enforced removal and destruction of publications, advertising boards, signboards, items and equipment violating regulations of law;
k) Enforced dismantling and destruction of objects used for tourism promotion and advertisement, termination of promotion programs, applicable to some violations against regulations on tourism promotion activities;
l) Enforced application of measures to remedy environmental pollution caused by violations against regulations on environmental protection;
m) Enforced implementation of decisions of competent agencies and persons, applicable to acts of obstructing inspection and examination and imposing administrative penalties.
Chapter 2.
         ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES AND FINES
Section 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON TRAVEL BUSINESS, GUIDED TOURISM, AND TOUR BUSINESS
Article 4. Violations against regulations on notification of travel business operation A fine ranging from VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
1. Failure to notify in writing the time of commencing travel business operations to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments of the localities where the enterprises are headquartered within 15 days after such enterprises officially commence their business operations.
2. Failure to notify in writing the changes of the legal representatives of enterprises, changes of enterprise types, changes of enterprise names or changes of enterprise head-office locations to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments of the localities where the enterprises are headquartered within 15 days after such changes.
3. Failure to notify in writing the changes of international travel business operators to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments within 30 days after such enterprises change their international travel business operators.
4. Failure to notify in writing the establishment and commencement dates of branches or representative offices of travel businesses to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments of the localities where the enterprises are headquartered or where their branches or representative offices are located within 45 days after such branches or representative offices are established or officially commence their operations.
5. Failure to notify in writing the changes of heads, names or locations of branches or representative offices of travel businesses to the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments of the localities where the enterprises are headquartered or where their branches or representative offices are located within 10 days after such changes.
6. Failing to notify in writing the General Department of Tourism within 7 days from the date of losing the license for international tour business.
7. Failing to promptly notify the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments of accidents or risks or incidents that may occur to tourists.
Article 5. Violations of regulations on tour business
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a/ Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for three years as managers of domestic tour business;
b/ Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for four years as managers of international tour business;
c/ Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for three years as managers of domestic tour business;

d/ Employing persons who have not worked in the field of tour business for four years as managers of international tour business;
dd) The tour contract is incomplete as prescribed by law.
2. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failure to provide written tour program for tourists or representatives of tourist groups;
b) Failure to have a tour contract with tourists or representatives of tourists as prescribed;
c) Failure to settle legitimate requests and recommendations of tourists as prescribed by law;
d) Using tour guides to perform tour program without having a written contract with tour guides or a contract which does not contain all basic contents as prescribed;
dd) Failure to guide or provide information related to tour program when the tour operator requests;
e) Failure to clearly, publicly and truthfully inform tourists about the number and prices of tourism services;
3. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) The tour operator uses false documents to operate tour business;
b) Failure to comply with regulations on making and keeping records as prescribed by law;
c) Using expired tour guide’s badge to guide tourists.
4. A fine of from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using vehicles, equipment or devices that fail to ensure health, safety and property of tourists as prescribed by law, except for cases specified in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 Article 8 hereof;
b) Failure to comply with regulations on reporting to Vietnam National Administration of Tourism, Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism;
c) Using persons who do not have tour guide’s badges to guide tourists.
5. A fine of from VND 10,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Providing international tour services without at least 3 international tour guides;
b) Failing to purchase insurance for Vietnamese tourists who travel abroad during the period of tour program implementation as prescribed;
c) Changing tour programs, standards or services without the consent of tourists or their representatives.
6. A fine of from VND 15,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to deposit sufficient amounts of security for providing international tour services as prescribed;
b) Erasing or modifying contents in license for providing international tour services;
c) Collecting money outside of contract or other acts of collecting illicit profits from tourists;
d) Failing to manage operation and business of branches, representative offices and affiliates in accordance with law;
dd) Failing to manage tourists under signed travel contracts or programs;
e) Declaring contents in application for issuance or replacement of international tour operator license dishonestly;
g) Failing to follow procedures for replacement of international tour operator license as prescribed by law;
h) Using enterprise name, transaction name or abbreviated name inconsistently with name registered with competent authority for business activities;
7. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Employing foreigners to act as tour guides in Vietnam;
b) Engaging in tour operator business after having issued notice of suspension, termination or being suspended by competent authority.

8. A fine of from VND 25,000,000 to VND 30,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to comply with international tour operator license.
9. A fine of from VND 30,000,000 to VND 40,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Allowing other organizations and individuals to use their international tour operator licenses to operate and provide tour services;
b) Providing international tour services without an international tour operator license.
10. Additional penalties:
a) Suspend the international tour operator license for 12 months if one of the violations prescribed in Points a and c Clause 5; Points a, d and dd Clause 6 and Point a Clause 7 of this Article is committed;
b) Suspend the international tour operator license for 12 - 24 months if one of the violations prescribed in Clause 8 and Point a Clause 9 of this Article is committed;
c) Confiscate the exhibits and instrumentalities of administrative violations if any of the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 3 and Point e Clause 6 of this Article is committed.
11. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate equipment and facilities to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists if they commit the violation in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced compliance with regulations on preparation and storage of documents if they commit the violation in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;
c) Enforced submission of reports as prescribed by law if they commit the violation in Point b Clause 4 of this Article;
d) Enforced payment of deposit if they commit the violation in Point a Clause 6 of this Article;
dd) Enforced return of illegal revenues collected from tourists or transfer them to state budget if they commit the violation in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 6 of this Article.
Article 6. Violations against regulations on tour operator business
1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a/ Failing to notify in writing the provincial/municipal Culture, Sports and Tourism Services of the date of commencing tour operator business within 15 days after such tour operator starts its business;
b/ Failing to notify in writing the provincial/municipal Culture, Sports and Tourism Services of changes of legal representatives or names and addresses of tour operators within 15 days after such changes;
c/ Failing to clearly and publicly disclose information on the number and prices of tourist services provided by tour operator contractors to tourists;
d/ Operating at a place other than the registered place with competent state agencies.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a/ Erasing, modifying or falsifying contents of tourist programs of tour operator contractors;
b/ Selling tourist programs at prices higher than those charged by tour operator contractors;
c/ Operating as tour operator without registration of tour operator business.
d) Travel agency business without a written travel agency contract with the travel agency business.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Acting as an agent for tour operator companies that do not have licenses to operate international tour business to sell tour programs to international tourists and Vietnamese tourists traveling abroad;
b) Continue to operate as travel agency after being revoked by competent authority.
4. Remedial measures:
Enforced refund to tourists or transfer to state budget of revenues collected in contravention of regulations for violations under Point b Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 7. Violations against regulations on tourist attractions
1. A warning or a fine of from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed on tour guides and docents who fail to wear cards or certificates while working.

2. A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Providing inaccurate or inadequate information about the points of interest or tourist attractions;
b) Failing to carry tour program and documents issued by tour operator while providing tour services;
c) Failing to instruct tourists to comply with Vietnam’s laws, internal rules and regulations of the places where they visit, and respect local customs and practices;
d) Failing to provide adequate information about tour itinerary and tour program for representatives of tourist groups and tourists.
3. A fine of from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to enter into employment contracts with tour operators;
b) Failing to provide tourists with instructions on measures to ensure their life safety, health and property during tour programs;
c) Letting others use one’s tourist guide card or tour guide certificate to practice;
d) Using another person’s tourist guide card or tour guide certificate to practice;
dd) Using an expired tourist guide card;
e) Declaring untruthful information in documents and materials included in the application for issuance, replacement or re-issuance of a tourist guide card or tour guide certificate.
4. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) Erasing or altering contents of the tourist guide card or tour guide certificate to practice;
b) Using a domestic tourist guide card to provide tours for foreign tourists;
c) Giving tours at tourist attractions or tourist sites without holding a tour guide certificate.
5. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Providing tour guide services without a tour guide badge as prescribed;
b) Using fake tour guide badges or certificates to provide tour guide services;
c) Arbitrarily changing tour programs, reducing standards and services provided to tourists;
d) Giving false information about cultural, historical, national and people’s values, causing negative impacts on image, traditions, ethics and fine customs of Vietnam;
dd) Taking advantage of tour guide services to give presentations or provide information that affects social security, order and safety;
e) Bringing tourists to prohibited areas;
g) Collecting money outside the contract or other illegal benefits from tourists.
6. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of foreigner providing tour guiding services in Vietnam.
7. Additional penalties:
a) Suspend the tour guide’s badge for 12 months if the violation prescribed in Point d Clause 2; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article is committed;
b) Suspend the tour guide’s badge for 12 - 24 months if the violation prescribed in Point c Clause 3; Points a and b Clause 4 or the violation prescribed in Points a, b and c Clause 2; Points a and b Clause 3; Points c, d, dd, e and g Clause 5 of this Article is committed;
c) Suspend the speaker’s certificate for 12 - 24 months if the violation prescribed in Point c Clause 3; Point a Clause 4 of this Article is committed;
d) Confiscate exhibits used for committing administrative violations in case of commission of the violation specified in Points d, dd and e Clause 3; Point b Clause 5 of this Article.
8. Remedial measures:
Mandatory return of illegally collected amounts to tourists or confiscation of such amounts to the state budget if the violation prescribed in Point c or Point g Clause 5 of this Article is committed.
Article 8. Violations against regulations on tourist transport vehicles

1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with fewer than 9 seats that fails to meet prescribed interior, amenities and equipment requirements to carry tourists.
2. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with 9 - 24 seats that fails to meet prescribed interior, amenities and equipment requirements to carry tourists.
3. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 4,000,000 shall be imposed for using a car with 24 seats or more that fails to meet prescribed interior, amenities and equipment requirements to carry tourists.
4. A fine ranging from VND 4,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for using a caravan or a two-storey car that fails to meet prescribed interior, amenities and equipment requirements to carry tourists.
5. A fine ranging from VND 2,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:
a) Changing the tour itinerary without the consent of tourists;
b) Failing to install a signboard indicating that the vehicle meets standards for serving tourists as prescribed by law.
c) Using drivers of tourist transport vehicles without certificates of training in tourism services;
d) Using attendants on tourist transport vehicles without certificates of training in tourism services.
6. A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to comply with regulations on reporting to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism or the Department of Transport as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to carry out procedures for reissuing the signboard of a standard tourist vehicle as prescribed by law;
c) Failing to purchase passenger insurance for tourists on the transport vehicle.
7. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 7.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using fake signboards of standard tourist vehicles for business purposes;
b) Falsely identifying tourist transport vehicles.
8. Additional penalties:
Confiscating the badge of the fake vehicle used for tourist transport if any of the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 7 of this Article is committed.
9. Remedial measures:
a) Mandatory provision of adequate interior, amenities and equipment of tourist transport vehicles to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists if any of the violations prescribed in Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4 of this Article is committed;
b) Mandatory installation of badge of the vehicle used for tourist transport as prescribed by law if any of the violations prescribed in Point c Clause 5 of this Article is committed;
c) Mandatory submission of reports as prescribed if any of the violations prescribed in Point a Clause 6 of this Article is committed.
Article 9. Violations against regulations on establishment and operation of branches and representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam
Acts of violating regulations on establishment and operation of branches or representative offices of foreign tourism enterprises in Vietnam shall be sanctioned according to Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 13; Clauses 1, 2, 3 and 4, Article 14 of the Government's Decree No. 06/2008/ND-CP of January 16, 2008 prescribing sanctions against administrative violations in the field of trade.
Section 2. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON TOURIST ACCOMMODATION BUSINESS
Article 10. Violations of regulations on tourist accommodation business
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to notify in writing the time of commencing operation of tourist accommodation establishments to the provincial-level Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism where such establishments are located within 15 days after such establishments officially commence operation;
b) Failing to notify in writing the change of heads of tourist accommodation establishments or names of tourist accommodation establishments to the provincial-level Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism within 15 days after such changes;

c) Failing to notify in writing the application of telecommunication charges as surcharges in ranked tourist accommodation establishments to the provincial-level Departments of Culture, Sports and Tourism or the provincial-level Departments of Information and Communications within 15 days after such application.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to comply with regulations on name boards and classification of tourist accommodation establishments;
b) Failing to put up name boards after being classified by the General Department of Tourism, the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Departments;
c) Failing to promulgate and post up internal rules and regulations of tourist accommodation establishments;
d) Failing to clearly and publicly inform tourists about the quantity, quality and price of services and goods provided for them;
dd) Failing to put up decisions on recognition of tourist accommodation establishment classification at easy-to-see positions at reception desks.
3. A fine of between VND 5,000,000 and 7,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to implement or improperly implementing the regime of making and keeping records and documents on business activities of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
b) Having insufficient number of sleeping rooms and area in corresponding standard with each type and class of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law;
c) Failure to meet standards of parking and internal traffic, reception hall area as prescribed by law;
d) Failure to meet number and standards of restaurants, bars as prescribed by law;
dd) Failure to meet standards of kitchen area as prescribed by law;
e) Failure to meet number and standards of conference rooms, seminar rooms, meeting rooms as prescribed by law;
g) Failure to meet standards of equipment and other amenities as prescribed by law;
h) Failure to meet standards of services as prescribed by law;
i) Failure to meet standards of professional qualifications, foreign languages of managers and staff as prescribed by law.
4. A fine ranging from VND 7.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using means and equipment which fail to ensure health, life safety and property of tourists as prescribed by law;
b) Failing to properly report to the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism as prescribed by law;
c) Failing to provide additional equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign languages for managers and staff in accordance with the classification standards at the request of the tourism authority.
5. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to register with the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism for appraisal and classification of tourist accommodation establishments after 03 months from the date on which such establishments officially commence business activities;
b) Failing to register with the Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism for appraisal and reclassification of tourist accommodation establishments before 03 months from the date on which the classification of tourist accommodation establishments expires;
c) Using star images affixed to tourist accommodation establishments or on publications, billboards, signboards, items and equipment used at tourist accommodation establishments which have not been recognized or are not appropriate to the recognized classification of tourist accommodation establishments.
6. A fine ranging from VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Using names of tourist accommodation establishments, transaction names or abbreviated names not conformable with names of tourist accommodation establishments registered with competent authorities for business purposes;
b) Collecting money outside contracts or committing other acts of illicit gain from tourists;
c) Collecting service charges in contravention of regulations.
7. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 25.000.000 shall be imposed for engaging in tourist accommodation business after having issued a notice of temporary suspension, termination or being suspended by competent authority.
8. Regulations from Clauses 1 through 6 of this Article shall also apply to guest houses and hostels of ministries, central departments and local governments engaged in tourist accommodation business.

9. Additional penalties:
Confiscating exhibits of administrative violations if the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 4 or Clause 8 of this Article is committed.
10. Remedial measures:
a) Enforced provision of adequate equipment, facilities, services, professional qualifications and foreign language skills of managers and attendants corresponding to standards applicable to each category and class of tourist accommodation establishments as prescribed by law, for violations specified in Points b, c, d, dd, e, g, h and i Clause 3; Point c Clause 4 of this Article;
b) Enforced provision of adequate means and equipment to ensure health, life safety, and property of tourists in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 4 of this Article;
c) Enforced return of illegally collected amounts to tourists or transfer them to state budget in case of commission of the violation in Point b or Point c Clause 6 of this Article;
d) Enforced destruction of publications, billboards, signboards, equipment and devices in case of commission of the violation specified in Point a Clause 2 or Point c Clause 5 of this Article;
dd) Enforced installation of the classification signboard in accordance with the decision on classification issued by a competent authority, for violations specified in Point b Clause 2 of this Article.
Article 11. Violations against regulations on sale of goods and provision of conditional services in tourist accommodation establishments
A fine ranging from VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to register in writing with competent authorities when providing conditional services or goods at a rated tourist accommodation establishment.
Section 3. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON TRAVEL ADVISORSHIP
Article 12. Violations against regulations on tourism promotion
1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:
a) The contents of tourist product are not consistent with the announced national tourism database;
b) Failure to implement or improper implementation of the tourism promotion program;
c) Promotion of a tourist product whose contents and quality are not satisfactory;
d) Use of tourist product without or with incorrect title and logo as prescribed by the General Department of Tourism.
2. A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for taking advantage of tourism promotion activities to harm legitimate rights and interests of other organizations and individuals.
3. Remedial measures:
Forcible dismantling and destruction of tourist propaganda and promotion articles, termination of tourism promotion programs, for violations specified at Points a, c and d, Clause 1 of this Article.
Section 4. VIOLATIONS OF REGULATIONS ON OTHER TOURIST ACTIVITIES
Article 13. Violations of regulations on tourist resources, tourism development planning A fine of between VND 10,000,000 and 15,000,000 for acts of exploiting tourist resources in contravention of law provisions in tourist areas, tourist attractions or areas which have been planned for tourism development.
Article 14. Violations of regulations on protection of environment of tourism
1. A fine of between VND 1,000,000 and 3,000,000 for acts of failing to post up the internal regulations on environmental protection as prescribed at places easily visible in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, means of transporting tourists.
2. A fine of between VND 3,000,000 and 5,000,000 for one of the following acts:
a) Failing to have public sanitation works meeting requirements for environmental sanitation maintenance in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, means of transporting tourists as prescribed;
b) Failing to classify garbage as prescribed in tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions, means of transporting tourists;
c) Failing to assign officials to monitor and manage environmental protection work in accordance with regulations applicable to each type or class of tourist accommodation establishment.
3. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

a) Failing to collect and treat waste in accordance with regulations at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist areas, tourist attractions and tourist transport means;
b) Causing damage to the living environment of wild animals in areas where tourism resources exist;
c) Cutting branches or committing other acts that cause damage to trees or vegetation in areas where tourism resources exist.
4. A fine ranging from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to submit annual reports on environmental protection work in accordance with regulations;
b) Failing to arrange collection and treatment equipment meeting environmental sanitation requirements at tourist accommodation establishments, tourist transport means and tourist areas or tourist attractions.
5. Additional penalties:
Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations in case of commission of the violations specified in Points b and c Clause 3 of this Article.
6. Remedial measures:
Forcible application of measures to overcome environmental pollution caused by violations at Points a and b, Clause 2; Point a, Clause 3 and Point b, Clause 4 of this Article.
Article 15. Violations against regulations on management and business activities in tourist areas and tourist attractions A fine of from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
1. Do not promulgate regulations on management of tourist areas and tourist attractions;
2. Promulgate rules and regulations of tourist areas or tourist attractions in contravention of law;
3. Violations of regulations on tourist areas or tourist attractions.
Article 16. Penalties for obstruction of inspection and administrative penalties
1. A fine of from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for refusing to accept inspection decisions or decisions on enforcement of decisions on administrative penalties.
2. A fine of from VND 5,000,000 to VND 7,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Failing to declare or declaring not truthfully or within the time limit as requested by law enforcers or competent authorities;
b) Failing to provide or providing insufficient information and documents related to the inspection and handling of administrative violations by law enforcers or competent authorities;
c) Obstructing the work of inspection teams or persons assigned to conduct inspections;
d) Using words or actions that threaten, insult or hurt honor of inspectors in charge of inspection and handling of violations.
3. A fine of from VND 7,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:
a) Delaying or avoiding the execution of administrative decisions and inspection decisions of competent persons or agencies;
b) Arbitrarily removing seals on exhibits and means used in commission of violations or arbitrarily altering scenes of administrative violations;
c) Dispersing, altering or swapping exhibits under examination, inspection or temporary seizure;
d) Attacking people on duty.
4. Additional penalties:
Confiscating exhibits and means used for committing administrative violations, applicable to the violations prescribed in Points b and c Clause 3 of this Article.
5. Remedial measures:
Enforced execution of decisions by competent agencies or persons, applicable to the violations specified in Clause 1; Points a and b Clause 2 and Point a Clause 3 of this Article.
Chapter 3.
                     POWER TO IMPOSE ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES
Article 17. Power to impose administrative penalties of Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels

Chairpersons of People’s Committees at all levels shall have the power to impose penalties within their competence prescribed in Clauses 4 and 5 Article 1 of the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2008, Article 30 of the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2002 amended by the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2007 for administrative violations specified in this Decree within their management scope.
Article 18. Power to impose administrative penalties of inspectors of Culture, Sports and Tourism
1. Inspectors of Culture, Sports and Tourism who are on duty shall have the power to:
a) Issue warning;
b) Impose a fine up to VND 500,000;
c/ Confiscating material evidence and means used in the administrative violations valued at up to VND 2,000,000;
d/ Applying remedies specified at Points a, b, d, e, f, g, h, i, j, k, l and m, Clause 3, Article 3 of this Decree;
e/ Performing other rights according to the law provisions on handling of administrative violations.
2. The Chief Inspector of the provincial-level Culture, Sports and Tourism Service has the competence to:
a/ Impose caution;
b/ Impose fines of up to VND 30,000,000;
c/ Deprive of the right to use licenses or professional practice certificates under his/her jurisdiction;
d/ Confiscate material evidence and means used in the administrative violations;
dd) Applying remedies as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Implementing other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
3. The Chief Inspector of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism has the authority to:
a) Issue warnings;
b) Impose fines up to maximum limits applicable to tourism sector;
c) Revoke licenses or practicing certificates within their jurisdiction;
d) Confiscate exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations;
dd) Apply remedies as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 3 of this Decree;
e) Exercise other rights as prescribed by law on handling of administrative violations.
Article 19. Power to impose penalties of People’s Public Security forces, Border Guard forces and Coast Guard forces
The People's Public Security, Border Guard and Coast Guard have the right to impose penalties within their competence prescribed in Clauses 6, 7 and 8 Article 1 of the Ordinance on amendments to the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2008 and Article 37 of the Ordinance on Handling Administrative Violations 2002 for administrative violations in the field of tourism directly related to their management as prescribed in this Decree.
Article 20.- Sanctioning competence of inspectors of other branches
Within the scope of state management prescribed by the Government, inspectors and chief inspectors of other inspection agencies shall have the authority to impose penalties for administrative violations in the tourism sector prescribed in this Decree under their respective state management.
Article 21. Issuance of record forms and decision forms for use in administrative penalties Issued together with this Decree are record forms and decision forms for use in administrative penalties for violations against regulations on tourism.
Chapter IV
                                    IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
Article 22. Effect
1. This Decree takes effect on April 30, 2012.
2. This Decree replaces the Government’s Decree No. 149/2007/ND-CP of October 9, 2007 on sanctioning administrative violations in the field of tourism.
Article 23. Implementation responsibility
1. The Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism shall implement this Decree.

2. Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, heads of government-attached agencies and chairpersons of provincial-level People’s Committees shall implement this Decree.-
