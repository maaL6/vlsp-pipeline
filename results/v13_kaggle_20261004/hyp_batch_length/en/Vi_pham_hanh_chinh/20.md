                                                  DECREE

                 ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON MARITIME

  The Government’s Decree No. 142/2017/ND-CP dated December 1, 2017 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime, which takes effect from February 1, 2018, is amended and supplemented by:

  1. Decree No. 123/2021/ND-CP dated December 28, 2021 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decrees on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime; road traffic and railway transport, comes into force from January 01, 2022.

  2. Decree No. 80/2026/ND-CP dated March 19, 2026 of the Government amending and supplementing a number of articles of Decree No. 142/2017/ND-CP dated December 11, 2017 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime amended by Decree No. 123/2021/ND-CP dated December 28, 2021 of the Government and Decree No. 139/2021/ND-CP dated December 31, 2021 of the Government on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on inland waterway transport, comes into force from May 15, 2026.

  Pursuant to the Law on Government organization dated June 19, 2015;

  Pursuant to the Law on actions against administrative violations dated June 20, 2012;

  Pursuant to the Vietnam Maritime Code dated November 25, 2015;

  At the request of the Minister of Transport;

  The Government promulgates Decree on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime [1].

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  1. [2] This Decree provides for administrative violations; completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations; penalties, fines, remedial measures applicable to each administrative violation; power to impose penalties, specific fines imposed by title holders, and power to issue notices of administrative violations against regulations on maritime; implementation of penalties and remedial measures.

  2. [3] Administrative violations against regulations on maritime prescribed in this Decree include:

  a) Violations against regulations on construction, management and operation of maritime infrastructure;

  b) Violations against regulations on construction, management and operation of ICDs;

  c) Violations against regulations on ship operation;

  d) Violations against regulations on registration, deregistration of ships, and crew assignment; use of certificates of competency and crew member books;

  dd) Violations against regulations on maritime pilots;

  e) Violations against regulations on operation of maritime transport, multimodal transport, maritime transport support services and provision of maritime safety services;

  g) Violations against regulations on eligibility requirements for establishment of ship building, modification and repair facilities and ship dismantling facilities;

  h) Violations against regulations on CTN safety;

  i) Violations against regulations on search and rescue operations; salvage of sunken property and maritime signals;

  k) Violations against regulations on training of seafarers;

  l) Violations against regulations on prevention of marine pollution;

  m) Violations against regulations on maritime labor; reporting of maritime occupational accidents;

  n) Violations against regulations on prevention and control of natural disasters in maritime sector;

  o) Violations against regulations on prevention and control of infectious diseases in the maritime sector.

  p) Violations against regulations on state management, inspection, examination, and imposition of penalties for administrative violations in the maritime sector.

  q) Violations against regulations on posting information about prices and surcharges in the maritime sector.

  3. [6] Violations specified in Points c, d, dd, e, i, and l Clause 2 of this Article if they occur outside the port waters but are not prescribed in the Decree on penalties for administrative violations in territorial waters, islands, and continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall also be sanctioned according to the provisions of this Decree.

  4. Other administrative violations related to the maritime sector that are not prescribed in this Decree shall be sanctioned according to the provisions of Decrees on penalties for administrative violations in relevant sectors. Persons having power to impose penalties specified in Articles 60, 61, 62, and 63 of this Decree when detecting administrative violations specified in this Clause shall impose penalties within their competence.

  5. The handling of administrative violations in the maritime sector shall comply with the provisions of this Decree and relevant regulations of law on handling of administrative violations.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  1. Vietnamese individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the maritime sector; persons having power to make records and persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations in the maritime sector.

  2. Foreign organizations and individuals committing administrative violations within the territory, contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone or continental shelf of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or on board a Vietnamese-flagged ship shall be administratively sanctioned in accordance with this Decree and relevant provisions of Vietnamese law on handling of administrative violations, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  2a. [8] Organizations specified in Clause 1 of this Article include:

  a) Economic organizations established under law;

  b) Social organizations, socio-political organizations, socio-professional organizations, socio-professional-political organizations;

  c) Public service providers and other organizations as prescribed by law.

  2b. [9] Household businesses must register their business activities in accordance with law, and households committing administrative violations prescribed in this Decree shall be sanctioned for administrative violations like individuals.

  3. Based on the nature and severity of the violations prescribed in Clause 2 Article 1 of this Decree, competent persons shall determine specific penalties for organizations and individuals to impose administrative sanctions as per the law.

  Article 3. Prescriptive period for imposing administrative penalties [10]

  The prescriptive period for administrative penalties in the maritime sector is 01 year; however, the prescriptive period for administrative penalties for violations against regulations on construction of seaports, ICDs, maritime works, environmental protection, price management and overseas labor management shall be 02 years.

  Article 3a. Completed administrative violations and ongoing administrative violations [11]

  1. Completed administrative violations include the following:

  a) Violations against regulations on loading cargoes at Point a Clause 1, Clause 2, Clause 3 Article 16 hereof. The time of termination of the violation shall be calculated from the time when the violation of overloading cargoes beyond the permissible payload of the vehicle is detected;

  b) Violations detected through professional technical equipment and devices. The time of termination of the violation shall be calculated from the time when the professional technical equipment and devices record the violation;

  c) Administrative violations prescribed herein that do not fall under the cases specified in Points a and b of this Clause but have been completely committed before the competent person detects the violation. In case there are no documents or evidence to accurately determine the time of termination of the violation, these violations shall still be considered within the prescriptive period for administrative penalties.

  2. Ongoing administrative violations prescribed herein that do not fall under the cases specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 4. Penalties and remedial measures

  1. Individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the maritime sector shall be subject to one of the following main penalties for each violation:

  a) Warning;

  b) Fines.

  2. [12] Depending on the nature and severity of their violations, violating individuals and organizations may also be subject to one or more of the following additional penalties:

  a) Suspension of practicing licenses/certificates for a fixed period;

  b) Suspension of operations for a fixed period;

  c) [13] Confiscation of exhibits and instrumentalities used for committing administrative violations, including: certificates, written certifications, licenses, written permission documents, certificates, and papers not issued by competent authorities, and vehicles used for committing administrative violations.

  3. [14] In addition to the penalties prescribed in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, individuals and organizations committing administrative violations in the maritime sector must also implement remedial measures as prescribed in Chapter II of this Decree, including:

  a) Enforced renaming of ports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves, quays, floating terminals, zones or waters in accordance with naming principles;

  Enforced adequate allocation of port security officers as per regulation;

  Enforced implementation of approved port security plans as per regulation;

  Enforced inspection of quality of port facilities within prescribed time limit or after accidents or collisions affecting maritime structures;

  b) Enforced arrangement and installation of life-saving equipment on board ships as per regulation;

  Enforced adequate installation of aids to navigation as per regulation or appropriate ones as per regulation;

  Enforced relocation of fishing nets, cages or other means of commercial fishing or aquaculture within the port waters outside of prescribed positions;

  Enforced demolition of illegal works or parts thereof;

  c) Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered;

  d) Enforced rectification of dredging monitoring system on vehicles involved in the transport and discharge of dredged materials during construction to ensure readiness and continuity;

  dd) Enforced installation of dredging monitoring system on vehicles involved in the transport and discharge of dredged materials during construction;

  Enforced adoption or implementation of or strict compliance with maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities;

  e) Enforced unloading of goods stacked beyond the permissible payload of the vehicle if the vehicle has not left the loading area;

  g) Enforced development of a marine structure protection plan or enforced implementation of the approved one;

  Enforced development of a natural disaster response plan as prescribed;

  h) Enforced change or correction of the name of the dock according to the name announced by a competent authority;

  Compulsory additional and correction of information;

  i) Enforced payment of maritime charges and fees and other relevant amounts as prescribed;

  Enforced return of expenses for salvage of sunken property as per regulation;

  k) Enforced relocation of obstacles on the port waterway to the prescribed locations;

  Forced to relocate to areas of operation appropriate to ship classes;

  l) Forced to disembark passengers or unload goods carried on board the ship in excess of the prescribed limit;

  Forcing ships to leave their positions;

  Enforced restoration of the original state of the signal position and structure of the work if the position is moved or the structure of the signal work is affected;

  m) Enforced implementation of safety measures for transport of oversized and overweight goods;

  Enforced completion of procedures for registration under Vietnamese flag as per regulation;

  Enforced completion of procedures for deregistration of ships as per regulation;

  n) Enforced provision of adequate and accurate information on embarkation and disembarkation of passengers and assignment of crew members via electronic means to the Crew Management Database of Vietnam Maritime and Inland Waterway Administration as per regulation;

  o) Enforced assignment of maritime pilots in accordance with certificates of competency of maritime pilots or certificates of operation zones of maritime pilots;

  Enforced provision of adequate and timely pilotage services within mandatory maritime pilotage zones or on assigned pilotage routes;

  Enforced use or arrangement of adequate pilotage vehicles as per regulation or use of pilotage vehicles meeting safety requirements;

  Enforced arrangement of adequate number of pilots or minimum number of pilotage vehicles as per regulation;

  p) Enforced removal from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam of imported marine flares;

  q) Enforced arrangement of rest periods for seafarers as prescribed;

  Enforced provision of essential supplies, food and water for seafarers working on board;

  Enforced provision of funds for repatriation of seafarers;

  Enforced fulfillment of the shipowner’s responsibilities for seafarers suffering occupational accidents or occupational diseases;

  Enforced purchase of compulsory insurance for seafarers working on board;

  Enforced arrangement for repatriation of seafarers within the prescribed time limit;

  r) Enforced payment of costs of solicitation for assessment, inspection, measurement and analysis of environmental samples in case of violations leading to oil spills or environmental pollution exceeding applicable norms and unit prices;

  To force the implementation of measures to overcome the environmental pollution and report on the results of overcoming consequences of violations;

  Compulsory compensation for damage caused by oil pollution in accordance with regulations within the time limit set by the person competent to impose penalties in the decision on administrative penalties;

  s) Enforced posting or proper posting of the list of organizations and individuals that have signed contracts for waste collection and treatment services from ships at seaports and the prices of waste collection and treatment services as prescribed;

  Enforced reporting on the management of waste collection and treatment from ships at seaports as prescribed;

  Enforced provision of accurate or correct declarations of waste by the port authority as per regulation;

  Enforced implementation of measures to control environmental pollution caused by spilled waste, dispersion and leakage during the collection of waste from ships;

  Forcing the implementation of measures to overcome the provisions preventing pollution caused by ships;

  Forced to implement measures to overcome environmental pollution and violations of regulations on management and control of bilge water and bilge sludge of ships;

  t) Enforced re-signing of crew member employment contracts in accordance with employment license;

  Forcing seafarers to leave the ship;

  Enforced correction of information on the location and condition of operating ships in case of natural disasters;

  Enforced medical examination and treatment;

  Enforced return to the ship or medical quarantine area;

  Enforced medical isolation, enforced medical isolation, and medical treatment for persons, ships, and goods carrying infectious agents.

  u) Enforced return of certificates, written confirmation, licenses, written permission, certificates and papers which are erased or falsified by the competent authorities or persons who have issued them.

  4. For administrative violations, the fines shall be imposed according to the ship’s capacity or total capacity (GT) which is the capacity measured in accordance with the International Convention on Measurement of Ship’s Capacity 1969 and specified in the certificate issued by the registry.

  In case the certificate of the ship does not specify the CT, the total capacity of the ship shall be converted as follows:

  a) For self-propelled seagoing ships and inland waterway vehicles: 1.5 tons of gross tonnage shall be equal to 01 GT;

  b) For barges: 01 tonne of gross tonnage shall be equal to 01 GT;

  c) For towing ships, pushing ships, passenger ships (including aircrafts) and floating cranes: 01 horsepower (HP, CV) shall be equal to 0.5 GT; 01 kW shall be equal to 0.7 GT; 01 tonne of lifting capacity of the crane on board the ship shall be equal to 06 GT;

  d) For passenger ships without specifying engine capacity: 01 passenger seat shall be equal to 0.67 GT; 01 bed shall be equal to 04 GT;

  dd) In case the ship is a towboat, pushing boat or boarding boat: The total capacity of the whole fleet including barges, towing units or pushing units shall be used for calculation;

  e) The conversion specified in Points a, b, c, d and dd Clause 4 of this Article shall be made by selecting the method with the largest total capacity;

  g) Unit of engine capacity: The main engine capacity of the ship shall be calculated according to HP, CV or KW; the fractional part below 01 HP or 01 CV or 01 kW shall be rounded up to 01 HP, 01 CV or 01 KW.

  5. [15] For administrative violations related to ships and vessels without information for calculating total ship capacity specified in Clause 4 of this Article, the method for determining gross tonnage and ship capacity for imposing penalties is as follows:

  a) Gross tonnage of a ship or vessel is symbolized by T (tonnes) and calculated according to the formula T = A x K, where:

  - A is the value of the measurement of the main deck length from bow to stern of the ship or vessel multiplied by the measurement of the middle of the ship’s side margin multiplied by the measurement of the height of the ship’s side, from the bottom to the top of the middle of the ship, calculated according to the formula A = L x B x D, where:

  L (m): Length of the main deck from bow to stern of the ship or vessel;

  B (m): Width of the middle of the ship’s side margin;

  D (m): Height of the ship’s side from the bottom to the top of the middle of the ship;

  - K is the coefficient corresponding to the value of A and applied as follows:

  The value of A from 4.55 m3 to 18.76 m3, the coefficient K = 0.26;

  a/ For the value of A from over 18.76 m3 to 49.80 m3, coefficient K = 0.29;

  b/ For the value of A from over 49.80 m3 to 387.20 m3, coefficient K = 0.35;

  c/ For the value of A from over 387.20 m3 to 1.119.80 m3, coefficient K = 0.51;

  d/ For the value of A from over 1.119.80 m3, coefficient K = 0.57.

  b/ If the ship is not a cargo or passenger transport ship, the form and level of sanction shall be applied based on the total main engine capacity installed on the ship. In case there is no basis for determining the main engine capacity installed on the ship or the ship has no name tag, the sanction level shall be applied according to the engine capacity of 50 horsepower;

  c/ In case the total tonnage cannot be determined as prescribed at Point a, Clause 5 of this Article, the competent person shall request the registry to determine the total tonnage of the ship as a basis for issuing a decision on sanctioning of administrative violation;

  d/ For ships such as floating terminals or other floating structures where dredging and pumping equipment is installed for dredging channels, mineral exploitation and cargo handling if there is not enough basis for determining the total tonnage or the ship's capacity, the determination shall be made as follows:

  The ship with the maximum length of up to 10 meters or the maximum width of up to 4 meters shall be determined as the ship with the total tonnage of between 5 tons and 15 tons;

  A ship of more than 10 m in length or more than 4 m in width shall be considered as having a gross tonnage of more than 15 metric tons;

  Length of a ship means the distance from the outer edge of its bow to the outer edge of its stern or from two outer edges of the ship where the largest cross-section on the deck is located;

  The width of a ship is measured from one side to the other at its widest point.

  Article 4a. Enforcement of penalties and remedial measures [16]

  1. The implementation of penalties and remedial measures prescribed in this Decree shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations and guiding documents.

  2. Violating individuals and organizations shall cooperate with relevant organizations and individuals (if any) in implementing remedial measures specified in the decision on administrative penalties as prescribed by law and bear all costs for the implementation of such remedial measures.

  3. In case of enforced return of a certificate, written confirmation, license, written permission, or document that is erased, altered, or falsified in content, the violating individual or organization shall be responsible for implementing the enforced return of such certificate, written confirmation, license, written permission, or document as prescribed in Article 85 of the Law on Handling of Administrative Violations.

  The competent person shall issue a decision to enforce the remedial measure of transferring the certificate, confirmation, license, written permission, certificate, or document that has been erased, altered, or falsified in content to the agency or person that issued such certificate, confirmation, license, certificate, or document.

  4. For the remedial measure of forcible payment of maritime charges and fees and other relevant amounts as prescribed:

  a) Persons having authority to impose administrative penalties and violating individuals or organizations shall contact the state management agency in charge of inland waterway shipping where the ship departs from to determine maritime charges, fees and other relevant amounts payable as prescribed.

  b) Violating individuals and organizations shall notify the results of implementation of remedial measures to persons having authority to sanction administrative violations.

  Article 5. Principles for determining fines

  The fine for each administrative violation prescribed in this Decree shall be imposed on an individual; in case of the same administrative violation, the fine imposed on an organization shall be twice as much as that imposed on an individual.

  Article 6.[17] (annulled)

  Chapter II

      ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, PENALTIES AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
                                         QUERY

  Section 1. VIOLATIONS AGAINST REGULATIONS ON CONSTRUCTION, MANAGEMENT AND OPERATION OF MARITIME INFRASTRUCTURES

  Article 7. Violations against regulations on declaration of opening of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves and quays [18]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for providing false information in the application for declaration of opening of a seaport, offshore oil and gas port, wharf or quay.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for using a certificate, written confirmation, license, written permission, certificate or paper that is erased, altered or falsified or not issued by a competent authority but not liable to criminal prosecution in the application for declaration of opening of a seaport, offshore oil and gas port, wharf or quay.

  3. Additional penalties: Confiscation of certificates, written confirmations, licenses, written permissions, certificates or papers that are not issued by competent authorities if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  4. Remedial measures: Enforced return of erased, altered or falsified certificates, written confirmations, licenses, written permissions, certificates or papers to issuing authorities or persons thereof if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  Article 8. Violations against regulations on naming and renaming of seaports, offshore oil and gas ports, wharves, quays, floating terminals, waters, sea areas [19]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 30,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 shall be imposed for naming or renaming a port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or sea area inconsistently with the principles of naming as prescribed, or failing to notify in writing the maritime authorities of the naming or renaming of the port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or sea area.

  2. Remedial measures: Enforced change or correction of the name of the port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or sea area to conform with the principles of naming as prescribed if the act of naming or renaming the port, offshore oil and gas port, wharf, jetty, floating terminal, port waters or sea area inconsistently with the principles of naming as prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article is committed.

  Article 9.[20] (annulled)

  Article 10. Violations against regulations on safety assurance, maritime security and order during operation of ports

  1. A warning shall be imposed for failure to comply with instructions of competent authorities or persons when entering or leaving the port land area or boarding a ship.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 500,000 to VND 1,000,000 shall be imposed for failure to obtain permission from competent authorities or persons when entering or leaving the port land area or boarding a ship.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Committing violations against regulations on warning signs for safe landing of ships as prescribed;

  b) Failing to assign personnel to tie or untie mooring lines as prescribed;

  c) Failing to notify the maritime port authority of the plan for the vessel’s arrival and departure from the port as prescribed;

  d) Placing objects on the wharf or occupying the space above the wharf, thereby obstructing the vessel's approach or departure from the wharf or hindering other activities at the port.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 3,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Employing employees who do not have practicing licenses or certificates as prescribed;

  b) Failing to promptly notify the maritime port authority of incidents or accidents related to maritime safety, maritime security and environmental pollution at the port as prescribed;

  c) Failing to regularly provide the maritime port authority with data on the depth of the waters in front of the wharf as prescribed;

  d) Failing to carry out periodic surveys to publish notices on the depth of the waters in front of the wharf and other waters as prescribed;

  dd) The lighting equipment at the wharf is not working or does not work in accordance with technical regulations as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Allowing ships to enter the port or anchor at the port waters without obtaining permission from the port authority;

  b) Deliberately unloading cargoes when the ship has not yet completed the procedures for entering the port as prescribed;

  c) The anti-collision cushion system and mooring fittings of the wharf are inadequate or unsafe for the ship to anchor;

  d) Failing to obtain or failing to carry out annual procedures for certification of the port’s certificate of compliance as prescribed or failing to fully implement the approved port security plan as prescribed;

  dd) Failing to provide or providing maritime security information to competent authorities or failing to conduct exercises or drills for maritime security information connection as prescribed;

  e) Arranging port security officers against regulations;

  g) Failure to arrange wharves or floating terminals for ships and vessels to enter or leave in a timely manner according to the plan for ship mobilization of the port authority or failure to meet requirements as prescribed;

  h) Failure to install lighting equipment at wharves as prescribed.

  i) Failure to adequately store documents serving the management, operation, and maintenance of maritime works;

  k) Failure to carry out inspection or operate specialized equipment, devices, vehicles, or machinery used within the port facility without undergoing inspection as prescribed.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 60.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Operating a port against its declared functions;

  b) Allowing ships and vessels to dock at the wharf before it is permitted to be put into operation;

  c) Failure to have approved port security plans as prescribed;

  d) Failure to carry out inspection of quality of port infrastructure within the prescribed time limit or after an incident involving collision or impact that affects the structure of maritime works;

  dd) Failure to comply with relevant technical regulations on inspection of quality of port infrastructure.

  7. [25] Additional penalties:

  a) Suspension of receiving ships operating on international routes into the port for a period of 01 – 03 months imposed for the violation prescribed in Points d and e Clause 5 and Point c Clause 6 of this Article;

  b) Suspension of receiving ships into the port for a period of 01 – 03 months imposed for the violation prescribed in Point d Clause 6 of this Article.

  8. [26] Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory assignment of sufficient port security officers as prescribed imposed for the violation prescribed in Point e Clause 5 of this Article;

  b) Mandatory preparation of approved port security plans as prescribed imposed for the violation prescribed in Point c Clause 6 of this Article;

  c) Enforced inspection of quality of port infrastructure within the prescribed time limit or after accidents and collisions affecting maritime structures in case of commission of the violation specified in Point d Clause 6 of this Article.

  Article 11. Violations against regulations on loading, unloading, storage and handling of goods

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for loading and unloading or storing goods inconsistently with applicable regulations.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for loading cargoes on a wharf beyond the permissible weight.

  3. Remedial measures: Enforced removal of cargoes exceeding the permissible weight if the violation prescribed in Clause 2 of this Article is committed.

  Article 12. Violations against regulations on safety assurance of maritime transport and environmental protection during construction of new ports, renovation and upgrading of ports, or construction and installation of other works and equipment affecting maritime safety [27]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5,000,000 to VND 10,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to provide life-saving equipment or provision of inadequate life-saving equipment as prescribed;

  b) Failure to inform the port authority of construction of other works in the port waters;

  c) Failure to install adequate or appropriate aids to navigation as prescribed or failure to operate or mislead navigation aids within the area where the work is being constructed;

  d) Construction ships and vessels are anchored outside the permitted limits, causing obstruction to maritime traffic on navigational channels;

  dd) Failure to record the results of monitoring the dredging process and transportation and discharge of dredged materials or failure to keep a construction logbook as prescribed; failure to submit periodic reports to the Vietnam Maritime and Inland Waterway Administration on the progress and results of dredging navigational channels, waters, and areas within the port waters for full utilization of products as prescribed;

  e) [29] Conducting aquaculture in port waters without obtaining permission from a competent authority or at a position or time not permitted by law.

  g) Carrying out dredging operations within the port waters or navigational channels.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 20,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Carrying out construction work at a position other than the licensed one;

  b) Executing construction beyond the time limit specified in the construction permit of the competent authority as per regulation;

  c) Using specialized equipment and devices for surveying, dredging channels, establishing navigational aids, and conducting other activities within the port waters without the approval of the port authority as per regulation;

  d) Failing to remove or discharge obstacles arising during the construction process after the project is completed;

  dd) Failing to fully implement the maritime safety plan approved by the competent authority;

  e) Creating obstacles within the port waters and territorial waters of Vietnam that affect maritime operations;

  g) Installing a dredging monitoring system on the vehicle used for transporting and discharging dredged materials in a manner that does not comply with regulations; the dredging monitoring system fails to meet minimum technical specifications, is not in a ready state, operates intermittently or stably as prescribed;

  h) Failing to organize supervision of construction and discharge of dredged materials as per regulation.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to install navigational aids as prescribed;

  b) Failure to install dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during dredging or maintenance of navigational channels, waters, or areas;

  c) Failure to implement or improper implementation of maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for organizing construction of works not falling under the cases specified in Point a Clause 5 of this Article without obtaining a license or written approval from a competent authority as prescribed.

  5. A fine ranging from VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Organizing construction of works subject to preparation of economic-technical reports on construction or investment projects for construction of works without obtaining a license or written approval from a competent authority as prescribed;

  b) Constructing works against the appraised and approved design;

  c) Failing to prepare maritime safety plans as prescribed.

  6. A fine ranging from VND 50,000,000 to VND 60,000,000 shall be imposed for constructing works against the approved planning.

  7. A fine ranging from VND 80,000,000 to VND 100,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Executing construction works without permission of a competent authority and causing an accident;

  b) Executing construction works at a location other than the permitted one and causing an accident;

  c) Failing to adopt or improperly adopting the maritime safety plan approved by a competent authority, resulting in an accident.

  8. Remedial measures:

  a) Mandatory provision of life-saving equipment or appropriate life-saving equipment as prescribed if the violation prescribed in Point a Clause 1 of this Article is committed;

  b) Mandatory installation of adequate aids to navigation as prescribed or installation of appropriate aids to navigation as prescribed if the violation prescribed in Point c Clause 1 of this Article is committed;

  c) [35] Enforced relocation of docks, floating rafts or fishing and aquaculture facilities to the wrong locations in the port waters in case of commission of the violation in Point e or Point g Clause 1 of this Article;

  d) [36] Enforced restoration of the original state which has been altered in case of commission of the violation in Point e Clause 2 of this Article;

  dd) Enforced demolition of illegally constructed works or parts thereof in case of commission of the violation in Point a Clause 2, Clause 4, Clause 5, Clause 6, Point a, and Point b Clause 7 of this Article;

  e) Enforced improvement of dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during construction in order to ensure readiness and continuity in case of commission of the violation in Point g Clause 2 of this Article;

  g) Enforced installation of dredging monitoring systems on vehicles used for transporting and discharging dredged materials during construction in case of commission of the violation in Point b Clause 3 of this Article;

  h) Enforced implementation of maritime safety plans approved by competent authorities in case of commission of the violation in Point c Clause 5 and Point c Clause 7; enforced implementation of maritime safety plans and traffic safety plans approved by competent authorities in case of commission of the violation in Point c Clause 3 and Point c Clause 7 of this Article.

  Article 13. Violations against regulations on prevention and control of fire and explosion at seaports

  1. A fine ranging from VND 1,000,000 to VND 2,000,000 shall be imposed for one of the following violations:

  a) Failing to have internal rules, warning signs or notices at fire and explosion-prone places as prescribed;

  b) Using specialized firefighting equipment and devices for wrong purposes.

  c) Enforced enforced medical quarantine, medical treatment for persons, ships, vessels, goods carrying infectious agents specified in Point b Clause 5 of this Article.

  Article 58m. Violations against regulations during inspection, examination, and imposition of administrative penalties [103]

  1. A fine ranging from VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for threatening, insulting, or hurting the honor of an official on duty but not liable to criminal prosecution.

  2. A fine ranging from VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for commission of one of the following violations:

  a) Failure to report, declare, or provide untruthful or untimely reports or declarations as required by an official on duty or competent authority;

  b) Delaying, avoiding, failing to provide, or providing inadequate or untimely information or documents related to the inspection, examination, and imposition of administrative penalties by an official on duty or competent authority;

  c) Obstructing or hindering the work of inspection teams, inspectorates, or persons assigned to conduct inspection and examination in the maritime sector;

  d) Failing to appoint a competent representative as prescribed by law to participate in the announcement of inspection or examination decisions, or failing to appoint a competent representative to work with the inspection team.

  3. A fine ranging from VND 20.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for arbitrarily removing seals on documents, exhibits, means, warehouses, equipment, or facilities involved in violations that are being sealed; temporarily seizing or dispersing documents or exhibits involved in violations; or arbitrarily altering the scene of administrative violation in the maritime sector.

  4. A fine ranging from VND 50.000.000 to VND 80.000.000 shall be imposed for committing one of the following violations:

  a) Delaying or avoiding the execution of inspection decisions in the maritime sector issued by competent authorities or persons;

  b) Failing to comply with or fully complying with the conclusions of inspection or examination in the maritime sector issued by competent authorities.

  Article 58n. Online inspection and handling of administrative violations [104]

  1. When information about documents, including the original copy of the ship registration certificate or a certified copy of the ship registration certificate and a valid confirmation issued by a credit institution (in cases where the ship is mortgaged), certificates, and other documents related to the ship as prescribed by law, and professional certificates and qualifications already integrated and updated in the electronic identification card or the electronic identification account on the National Identification Application, or in databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security or the Ministry of Construction, the inspection shall be carried out through the information available in such electronic identification card, electronic identification account, or database. The inspection of information contained in the electronic identification card, electronic identification account, or database shall have the same legal validity as the inspection of the original copies of such documents.

  2. Temporary seizure or suspension of rights to use documents

  a) When temporarily seizing or suspending the right to use documents, competent persons shall update information on such temporary seizure or suspension on the database system for handling administrative violations and synchronize it with the National Identification Application and databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction so that violators and vehicle owners (regarding documents belonging to vehicles) are informed and comply with regulations of law on maritime order and safety and serve competent authorities’ inspection and handling of violations;

  b) Documents whose information on temporary seizure or suspension of rights to use has been updated on the National Identification Application and databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction shall be returned to sanctioned violators by competent persons as per regulation; the database system for handling administrative violations shall synchronize information with the National Identification Application and databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Construction to remove information on temporary seizure or suspension of rights to use such documents;

  c) Persons having power to impose penalties shall access electronic receipts for payment of fines for administrative violations sent by the public service portal system for printing and storing records of administrative penalties and returning documents subject to temporary seizure or suspension of rights to use, removing information on temporary seizure or suspension of rights to use on the National Identification Application and databases managed by the Ministry of Public Security as prescribed in Point b of this Clause;

  d) Records and decisions issued by competent persons regarding temporary seizure or suspension of rights to use and return of documents shall be made and sent electronically via the National Identification Application, other applications, or electronic information systems when meeting technical requirements.

  3. Organizations and individuals having power to inspect and handle administrative violations shall be responsible for ensuring confidentiality of personal information and data of violators as per the law.

  4. Online handling of administrative violations prescribed in this Decree shall be carried out when infrastructure, technical and information requirements are satisfied in accordance with regulations of law on handling of administrative violations.

  Section 1. POWER TO RECORD ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS

  Article 59. Power to record administrative violations [105]

  Persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations and officials, public employees, persons of the People’s Army or the People’s Public Security forces who are performing official duties under the management of persons having power to impose penalties for administrative violations prescribed in Articles 60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 65 and 66 of this Decree and detect administrative violations against regulations on maritime must promptly prevent such violations and record them in writing as per regulation.   Chapter IV

                                        IMPLEMENTATION CLAUSES [115]

  Article 67. Guidelines for collection, transfer, management and use of fines paid by organizations and individuals

  The Ministry of Finance shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry of Transport in providing specific guidelines on collection, transfer, and management of the remittance of amounts to be returned to the state by organizations and individuals involved in administrative violations prescribed in this Decree.

  Article 68. Transition clauses

  For administrative violations in maritime sector committed before the effective date of this Decree but detected and handled after the effective date of this Decree, regulations on penalties shall be applied in a manner beneficial to violating organizations and individuals.

  Article 69. Effect

  This Decree comes into force as of February 1, 2018 and replaces the Government’s Decree No. 93/2013/ND-CP dated August 20, 2013 on penalties for administrative violations against regulations on maritime transport and inland waterway transport.

  Article 70. Implementation

  1. The Minister of Transport shall be responsible for organizing and guiding the implementation of this Decree.

  2. Ministers, Heads of ministerial agencies, Heads of Governmental agencies, Chairpersons of People’s Committees of provinces and central-affiliated cities shall be responsible for implementing this Decree./.
