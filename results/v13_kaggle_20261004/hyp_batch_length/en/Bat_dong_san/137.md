                                                         DECISION

    PROMULGATING THE PLAN FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GOVERNMENT PRIME MINISTER’S DECREE NO. 11/CT-TTG DATED APRIL 23, 2019 ON A NUMBER OF MEASURES TO SUPPORT THE REGIONAL UNIFIED AND HEALTHY DEVELOPMENT OF THE CONSTRUCTION SECTOR

                                                   THE MINISTER OF CONSTRUCTION

  Pursuant to Decree No. 81/2017/ND-CP dated July 17, 2017 of the Government on functions, tasks, powers and organizational structure of the Ministry of Construction;

  Pursuant to Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019 of the Prime Minister on solutions for promoting the development of the real estate market in a stable and healthy manner;

  At the request of Director of Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management,

                                                         HEREBY DECIDES:

  Article 1. To promulgate together with this Decision the Plan for implementation of Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019 of the Prime Minister on solutions for promoting the development of the real estate market in a stable and healthy manner.

  Article 2. This Decision takes effect from the date of its signing.

  Article 3. The Chief of Office of the Ministry of Construction, Director of Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management, heads of agencies and units assigned to perform tasks shall implement this Decision./.

Phạm Hồng Hà



                                                          PLAN

     IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GOVERNMENT PRIME MINISTER’S DIRECTIVE No. 11/CT-TTG DATED APRIL 23, 2019 ON A NUMBER OF MEASURES TO SUPPORT THE NON-INDUSTRIAL SCHOOLS IN THE REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND REHABILITATION
      (Enforced together with Decision No. 398/QD-BXD dated May 22, 2019 of the Minister of Construction)

  I. OBJECTIVES

  - Synchronous, timely and effective implementation of Directive No. 11/CT-TTg dated April 23, 2019 of the Prime Minister on solutions to promote the development of the real estate market in a stable and healthy manner (hereinafter referred to as Directive No. 11/CT-TTg).

  - Intensify the improvement of housing and real estate market management institutions in a timely manner and in conformity with practical situations; effectively implement social housing programs: support for housing for people with meritorious services to the revolution; support for housing for poor households in rural areas, flood-prone areas in central Vietnam, regularly flooded areas of the Mekong Delta; housing for low-income people in urban areas, housing for workers in industrial parks, and housing for students, etc.; intensify the guidance on inspection and examination of housing and real estate market management.

  II. PLAN DETAILS

  1. Regarding the task of completing projects of the Law on Amendments to the Law on Construction, the Law on Housing and the Law on Real Estate Business:

  1.1. To study and finalize the draft Law Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Construction Law.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Construction Management;

  - Coordinating units: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Deadline for completion: Submit to the Government in July 2019; finalize and submit to the National Assembly in October 2019.

  1.2. Research and finalize the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Housing regarding support policies for housing for officials, public servants and employees facing housing difficulties according to Resolution No. 26-NQ/TW of the XIIth Central Committee;

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Deadline for completion: Expected in 2020 (after the Politburo approves the housing scheme for officials, public servants and employees in the political system and issues a resolution on this issue).

  1.3. Research and finalize the draft Law on amendments to the Law on Real Estate Business

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Department of Legal Affairs and relevant departments;

  - Completion time limit: According to the program for law development of the National Assembly.

  2. Research and promulgation of amendments to construction regulations and standards applicable to apartment buildings, condominium buildings, resort villas, officetels, guest houses, and dormitories.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Science, Technology and Environment;

  - Executing unit: National Architecture Institute;

  - Completion time limit: Complete in 2019.

  3. Research and promulgation of legislative documents on regulations on management and operation of officetels.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Legal Department and relevant units;

  - Completion time limit: 3rd quarter of 2019.

  4. For the task of urging local governments to complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  4.1. Urging local governments to quickly complete and put into operation the housing and real estate market information system under Decree No. 117/2015/ND-CP dated November 12, 2015 of the Government

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Information Center, Construction Economics Institute;

  - Time limit for completion: Regular tasks.

  4.2. Cooperate with ministries and central authorities in collecting relevant information to improve housing and real estate market information system

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The General Department of Statistics - the Ministry of Planning and Investment;

  - Completion time limit: Regular tasks.

  5. Research and amend policies and laws, and expedite social housing development

  5.1. Research and amendments to Decree No. 100/2015/ND-CP dated October 20, 2015 of the Government on development and management of social housing;

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating unit: The Legal Department and related units;

  - Completion time limit: after the Law on amendments to the Law on Housing is approved by the National Assembly.

  5.2. Urge and guide local governments to promote social housing development in accordance with the Prime Minister’s Directive No. 03/CT-TTg dated January 25, 2017

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Local Departments of Construction and relevant units;

  - Time limit for completion: monthly regular tasks.

  5.3. Inspect and review planning and use of urban land reserved for social housing development in key localities, consolidate situation and report to the Prime Minister

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Coordinating units: Local Departments of Construction and relevant units;

  - Completion deadline: September 2019.

  6. Tasks of cooperating with ministries, central authorities and local governments in enhancing inspection and requiring project developers of urban area projects and housing development projects to synchronously build technical infrastructures, social infrastructures, fire prevention and firefighting systems in projects and connect with general infrastructure of the area before transferring houses to people as prescribed by construction laws, housing laws and relevant laws.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Cooperating units: Construction Inspectorate, Department of Urban Development and relevant units;

  - Completion deadline: 2019.

  7. Tasks of regularly monitoring and summarizing situation on a periodic or irregular basis for reporting to the Government and Prime Minister on developments of real estate market in order to promptly take measures to stabilize the market when there are signs of abnormal or large fluctuations.

  - Presiding unit: Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management;

  - Cooperating units: Institute of Construction Economics and relevant units;

  - Time limit for completion: quarterly and irregular as requested.

  III. Implementation organization

  1. Task assignment:

  The Minister of Construction shall take charge, direct and manage the implementation of the Plan on a number of solutions to promote the development of the real estate market in a stable and healthy manner;

  The Ministers shall, according to their respective fields of work, assign and direct units to implement the contents of the Plan;

  The heads of units under the Ministry shall, based on the implementation plan of the Ministry, organize the implementation of assigned tasks in a timely and effective manner; clearly assign tasks to leaders and specialists responsible for each specific task of the unit; focus on directing, strengthening the inspection and supervision of progress and results of implementation of the Plan; report on the implementation of the Plan according to their assigned functions and tasks to the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management for consolidation and reporting to the Ministry's leadership.

  The Director of the Department of Housing and Real Estate Market Management shall take charge and cooperate with the Ministry Office in monitoring, consolidating and reporting on the results of implementation of the Plan; promptly report to the Ministry's leadership on handling difficulties arising during the implementation.

  2. Implementation funding

  Departments of Planning and Finance, Science, Technology and Environment shall balance and allocate funding for performing tasks according to the estimates of units assigned to perform tasks from annual sources of economic and scientific and technological services and other lawful funding sources as prescribed by law.

  Units assigned to perform tasks shall prepare estimates for performance and send them to the Departments of Planning and Finance, Science, Technology and Environment for reporting to the leadership of the Ministry for consideration and approval.

  During the implementation of the Plan, in case of amendments or supplements to specific tasks, units shall take the initiative in proposing and reporting to the Minister for consideration and decision./.
