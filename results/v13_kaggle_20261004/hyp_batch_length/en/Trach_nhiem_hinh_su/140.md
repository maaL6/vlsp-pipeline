                                                 DECISION

    THE MINISTER OF PUBLIC SECURITY

CIRCULAR No. 315/2003/QD-BCA (V19) OF MAY 21, 2003 ON THE PUBLICATION OF THE LIST OF REGULATIONS AND LAWSUITS TO BE ANNULLED

                                         MINISTER OF PUBLIC SECURITY

  Pursuant to the Law on Promulgation of Legal Documents of November 12, 1996 and the Law on Amendments and Supplements to a Number of Articles of the Law on Promulgation of Legal Documents of December 16, 2002;
  Pursuant to the Government's Decree No. 101/CP of October 23, 1997 detailing the implementation of a number of articles of the Law on Promulgation of Legal Documents, the Prime Minister's Decision No. 355/TTg of May 28, 1997 establishing the Government Steering Committee for the review and systematization of legal documents, and the Decree No. 37/1998/ND-CP of June 9, 1998 defining the functions, tasks, powers and organizational apparatus of the Ministry of Public Security;
  At the proposal of the Director of the Legal Department,

                                                 DECIDES:

  Article 1.- To announce the list of legal documents promulgated by the Ministry of Public Security in the domain of security and order, which are to be annulled by December 31, 2002, including 15 documents (enclosed with the list).

  Article 2.- This Decision takes effect 15 days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

  Article 3.- The directors general and heads of the units under the ministers, the directors of the police offices of the provinces and centrally-run cities shall have to implement this Decision.
