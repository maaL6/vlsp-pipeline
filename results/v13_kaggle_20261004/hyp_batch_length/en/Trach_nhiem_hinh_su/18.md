                Law No. 101/2025/QH15                                      Hanoi, November 26, 2025


                                                      LAW

                             TRANSFER OF PERSONS SERVING IMPRISONMENT SENTENCES

  Pursuant to the Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, as amended by Resolution No. 203/2025/QH15;

  The National Assembly promulgates the Law on Transfer of Persons Serving Prison Sentences.

  Chapter I

                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

  Article 1. Scope

  This Law provides for principles, competence, conditions, procedures for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences between Vietnam and foreign countries; responsibilities of Vietnamese state agencies in transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  Article 2. Regulated entities

  This Law applies to Vietnamese agencies, organizations and individuals, and foreign agencies, organizations and individuals related to transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences between Vietnam and foreign countries.

  Article 3. Interpretation of terms

  For the purposes of this Law, the terms below shall be construed as follows:

  1. Transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences means the act whereby Vietnam and foreign countries transfer each other persons serving imprisonment sentences who wish to be transferred back to their home countries and agree to receive them for further serving their imprisonment sentences on the basis of domestic laws, international treaties or the reciprocity principle.

  2. Persons serving imprisonment sentences mean those who are sentenced to term imprisonment or life imprisonment and are serving their sentences at detention centers.

  3. The transferring country is a country that may transfer or has transferred its current imprisonment server to the receiving country.

  4. The receiving country is a country that may receive or has received the current imprisonment server for further execution of the judgment.

  5. Transfer of imprisonment penalty means a case where the competent People’s Court of Vietnam decides to transfer the imprisonment penalty in the judgment/decision of the competent foreign court which has been pronounced against the current imprisonment server to Vietnam in accordance with the provisions of the Penal Code and other provisions of Vietnamese law.

  Article 4. Application of laws and principles for transfer of current imprisonment servers

  1. The transfer of current imprisonment servers shall comply with this Law; in case this Law does not provide for such transfer, the provisions of criminal procedure law, criminal judgment enforcement law and other relevant provisions of Vietnamese law shall apply.

  2. The transfer of current imprisonment servers shall be carried out on the basis of the following principles:

  a) Respecting independence, sovereignty, territorial integrity, non-interference in each other’s internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit; conforming to the Constitution, Vietnamese law and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory;

  b) In case Vietnam and the foreign country are not signatories to the same international treaty on transfer of current imprisonment servers, the transfer of current imprisonment servers shall comply with the principle of reciprocity but not contrary to Vietnamese law, international law and international practice;

  c) Respect and protect lawful rights and interests of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  Article 5. Central authority of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. The Ministry of Public Security is the central authority of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  2. The central authority of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall act as the focal point for exchange of information and documents related to transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences; prepare, send and receive requests for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences; take charge and cooperate with ministries, sectors and local governments in considering and handling requests for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences; monitor and expedite the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences; perform other tasks and powers as prescribed by law on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences and international treaties to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a signatory.

  Article 6. Application of reciprocity principle in transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. The application of reciprocity principle in transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall be implemented when the following conditions are fully satisfied:

  a) Compliance with the principles specified in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  b) Competent authorities of foreign countries make written commitments on implementation of requests of Vietnam in similar cases. In case Vietnam is the country requesting the transfer, the Ministry of Public Security makes written commitment on application of reciprocity principle;

  c) In conformity with practical situations and cooperation demands for transfer of current sentenced persons between Vietnam and foreign countries.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall, pursuant to Clause 1 of this Article, decide on the application of the reciprocity principle in transfer of current sentenced persons. Where necessary, the Ministry of Public Security shall seek opinions from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and relevant agencies before making a decision.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 7. Time limit for withdrawal of request for transfer

  1. Current sentenced persons or their lawful representatives shall only have the right to withdraw their requests for transfer before decisions on admission of current sentenced persons or decisions on transfer of current sentenced persons of competent People’s Courts of Vietnam take effect.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 8. Language in transfer of current sentenced persons

  1. Where Vietnam and foreign countries are co-signatories to international treaties on transfer of current sentenced persons, the language used in transfer of current sentenced persons shall be the language prescribed in such international treaties.

  2. In case Vietnam and a foreign country are not parties to the same international treaty on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences, the application and documents requesting the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences must be enclosed with translations into the language of the receiving country or another language accepted by the receiving country.

  3. The agency preparing the application for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall translate the application and documents into the language prescribed in Clause 1 or Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 9. Exemption from consular legalization An application for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall be exempt from consular legalization if it is signed and sealed by a competent person or authority of a foreign country, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty to which the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is a party.

  Article 10. Costs of transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. In case Vietnam is the transferring country, Vietnam shall pay costs arising before the handover of persons serving imprisonment sentences, unless otherwise agreed upon.

  2. In case Vietnam is the receiving country, Vietnam shall pay costs arising after the receipt of persons serving imprisonment sentences, unless otherwise agreed upon.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 11. Funding for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. Funding for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall be ensured by state budget in accordance with regulations of law on state budget and other relevant laws.

  Persons serving imprisonment sentences, their lawful representatives, other agencies, organizations and individuals may voluntarily contribute or support all or part of living expenses, travel expenses and other expenses of persons serving imprisonment sentences who are transferred in accordance with law.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 12. Transit of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. Transit of persons serving imprisonment sentences through Vietnam’s territory must be notified in writing to the Ministry of Public Security for handling. Approval or refusal of transit of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall comply with regulations of law.

  2. In case of permission for transit, the country requesting transit of persons serving imprisonment sentences shall manage such persons during their transit through Vietnam and bear all transit expenses in accordance with Vietnamese law, unless otherwise agreed upon.

  3. In case of transportation by air and unexpected landing on Vietnam’s territory, persons serving imprisonment sentences may be managed in accordance with Vietnamese law. Management of persons serving imprisonment sentences and related expenses shall comply with Clause 2 of this Article.

  4. In case Vietnam requests a foreign country to permit transit for a person serving an imprisonment sentence, the Ministry of Public Security shall contact the foreign country to carry out procedures for requesting transit permission.

  5. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 13. Responsibilities of state agencies for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. The Government shall:

  a) Exercise unified state management over the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences;

  b) Report to the National Assembly on the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences in their annual reports on judgment enforcement.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall be held accountable to the Government for exercising state management over the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  The Minister of Public Security shall take charge and cooperate with the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court and the Procurator General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy in providing regulations on application of science, technology, and digital transformation in the transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  3. The Supreme People’s Court and the Supreme People’s Procuracy shall cooperate with the Ministry of Public Security in performing state management of transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  People’s Courts at all levels and People’s Procuracies at all levels shall perform tasks related to transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences according to this Law and other relevant laws.

  4. Ministries, ministerial agencies and other relevant agencies shall, within their functions and tasks, perform tasks related to transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences according to this Law and other relevant laws; and cooperate with the Ministry of Public Security in performing state management of transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences.

  Chapter II

              RECEPTION OF PERSONS SERVING IMPRISONMENT SENTENCES IN FOREIGN COUNTRIES TO VIETNAM

  Article 14. Authority to decide reception of persons serving imprisonment sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam

  Regional People’s Courts of last permanent residence in Vietnam of persons serving imprisonment sentences in foreign countries may request transfer and have authority to decide reception of persons serving imprisonment sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam. If it is impossible to identify the last permanent residence in Vietnam of the person requested for transfer or the person requested for transfer does not permanently reside in Vietnam, the Regional People’s Court of Hanoi has authority to decide reception of persons serving imprisonment sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam.

  Article 15. Conditions for reception of persons serving imprisonment sentences in foreign countries to Vietnam

  1. A person serving a sentence in a foreign country may be transferred to Vietnam for continued execution of his/her sentence when all of the following conditions are met:

  a) He/she is a Vietnamese citizen;

  b) The criminal act for which he/she was convicted in the transferring country also constitutes a crime under the criminal law of Vietnam at the time of making the request for transfer of the sentenced person;

  c) At the time of receiving the request for transfer, the period of unserved sentence must be at least one year; in special cases, this period may be less than one year;

  d) The judgment or decision against such person has taken legal effect and there is no pending procedural matter;

  dd) There is consent from Vietnam in case the competent authority of the transferring country makes the request for transfer or consent from the transferring country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes the request for transfer;

  e) There is consent from the sentenced person; in case the sentenced person is a minor or incapable of expressing consent due to age, health condition, or mental state, the consent must be given by a lawful representative.

  2. The Government shall elaborate special cases specified in point c clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 16. Cases of refusal to receive persons serving their sentences in foreign countries back to Vietnam

  1. The competent People’s Court shall decide to refuse to receive a person serving his/her sentence in a foreign country back to Vietnam if he/she falls into one of the following cases:

  a) He/she does not conform to the principles prescribed in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  b) He/she fails to satisfy one of the conditions prescribed in Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law;

  c) He/she may be detrimental to national sovereignty, defense, security, social order and safety, community health and the interests of the State, legitimate rights and interests of agencies, organizations or individuals in Vietnam;

  d) He/she exceeds the capacity to meet necessary requirements for further enforcement of the judgment in Vietnam of the sentenced person.

  2. The Government shall elaborate on Point d Clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 17. Application files for transfer of persons serving their sentences in foreign countries back to Vietnam

  1. A dossier of request for transfer of a person serving an overseas imprisonment sentence to Vietnam shall include the following documents:

  a) The written request for transfer from the Ministry of Public Security or the competent authority of the transferring country as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 18 of this Law;

  b) Accompanying documents as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 18 of this Law.

  2. A dossier of request for transfer of a person serving an overseas imprisonment sentence to Vietnam shall be prepared in 3 sets, unless otherwise agreed. Language used for preparation of the dossier shall comply with Article 8 of this Law.

  Article 18. Written request for transfer of a person serving an overseas imprisonment sentence to Vietnam and accompanying documents

  1. A written request for transfer of a person serving an overseas imprisonment sentence to Vietnam shall contain the following contents:

  a) Date and place of making the request;

  b) Grounds for requesting the transfer of the person serving the imprisonment sentence;

  c) Name and address of the competent authority of Vietnam and the transferring country;

  d) Information to identify personal identification, nationality, last place of residence in Vietnam (if any), and current place of serving sentence in the transferring country, based on legal grounds for the person serving the sentence to be eligible for transfer;

  dd) Other necessary information (if any).

  2. The written request for transfer of the person serving the sentence shall include the following documents:

  a) Application for transfer or document expressing the desire for transfer or agreement on transfer by the person serving the sentence or their lawful representative;

  b) Documents proving Vietnamese nationality of the person serving the sentence proposed for transfer in accordance with Vietnamese law;

  c) Copies of the court’s judgments or decisions of the transferring country regarding the person serving the sentence proposed for transfer;

  d) Written documentation on the execution of additional penalties (if any);

  dd) The law of the transferring country used to determine the constituent elements of the crime and the crime name, regulations on penalties, statute of limitations for execution of penalties for such crime of the judgment;

  e) Documents expressing the consent of the competent authority of the transferring country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a request for transfer or documents expressing the consent to consider the request for transfer of the Ministry of Public Security in case the competent authority of the transferring country makes a request for transfer;

  g) Documents describing the identity traits, fingerprints and facial photos of the person serving the imprisonment sentence proposed for transfer;

  h) Documents and materials certifying the period during which the person serving the imprisonment sentence proposed for transfer has served the sentence, the general amnesty, special amnesty, exemption from or reduction of sentence in the transferring country and the remaining time to serve the sentence;

  i) Documents related to health conditions, mental status, medical records of the person serving the imprisonment sentence proposed for transfer and recommendations (if any);

  k) International treaties on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences between Vietnam and the transferring countries (if any);

  l) Other necessary documents (if any).

  3. The Government shall elaborate clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 19. Receipt of transfer requests from persons serving their sentences in foreign countries

  1. Vietnamese citizens serving their sentences in foreign countries or their lawful representatives may submit transfer requests to Vietnam for continued execution of their sentences to one of the following agencies:

  a) Competent authorities of the transferring country;

  b) Vietnamese representative missions in the transferring country;

  c) Ministry of Public Security.

  2. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a transfer request, the Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country shall forward the request to the Ministry of Public Security. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the Vietnamese representative mission in the transferring country must forward the request to the Ministry of Public Security within 5 days from the date of receipt of the transfer request. Based on the transfer request, the Ministry of Public Security shall consider and prepare a transfer request as prescribed in Article 20 of this Law.

  The forwarding of transfer requests from Vietnamese representative missions in the transferring countries to the Ministry of Public Security may be carried out electronically in accordance with the law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 20. Preparation of request for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences overseas to Vietnam

  1. The Ministry of Public Security or a competent authority of the transferring country shall prepare a request for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences overseas to Vietnam.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the written request for transfer, and the information and documents provided by the competent authority of the transferring country, the Ministry of Public Security shall consider deciding on the preparation of a request for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences overseas to Vietnam and send it directly or via diplomatic channels to the competent authority of the transferring country.

  In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Vietnamese representative office in the transferring country receives a request for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences overseas from the Ministry of Public Security, it shall immediately forward it to the competent authority of the transferring country and notify the Ministry of Public Security in writing.

  3. In case the competent authority of the transferring country prepares a request for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences overseas, it shall be sent directly or via diplomatic channels to the Ministry of Public Security. In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Vietnamese representative office in the transferring country receives a request for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences overseas from the competent authority of the transferring country, it shall immediately forward it to the Ministry of Public Security for handling in accordance with this Law.

  4. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 21. Receipt, examination, and transfer of requests for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences overseas

  1. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the request for transfer of persons serving their imprisonment sentences and accompanying documents from the competent authority of the transferring country, or from the date of receipt of the documents specified in Clause 2, Article 18 of this Law from the competent authority of the transferring country, the Ministry of Public Security shall examine the dossiers in accordance with Articles 17 and 18 of this Law. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for examining the dossiers is 7 days.

  The Ministry of Public Security may request the competent authority of the transferring country to provide additional information and documents. If no additional information or documents are provided within 60 days from the date of sending the written request for additional information and documents, the Ministry of Public Security shall return the dossier to the competent authority of the transferring country and clearly state the reason. In special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for providing additional information and documents is 20 days.

  2. In case the transfer request dossier meets the requirements specified in Articles 17 and 18 of this Law, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately transfer 01 set of the dossier to the competent regional People’s Court for consideration and decision, and also immediately transfer 01 copy of the dossier to the competent regional People’s Procuracy.

  The transfer of documents and dossiers may be carried out electronically in accordance with law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 22. Acceptance of transfer requests for persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam

  1. Within 10 days from the receipt of a complete transfer request dossier for a person serving a prison sentence abroad to Vietnam, the competent regional People’s Court shall accept the dossier and notify the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy at the same level in writing. In special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for accepting the dossier is 05 days.

  Within the preparation period for considering the transfer request, if the transfer request dossier does not contain sufficient information and documents or contains unclear points or contradictory points, the competent regional People’s Court shall request the Ministry of Public Security to request the competent authority of the transferring country to provide additional information and documents. The time limit for providing additional information and documents is 20 days from the date on which the Ministry of Public Security issues the written request.

  2. Within 30 days from the date of acceptance, the competent regional People’s Court shall, on a case-by-case basis, issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision on consideration of request for transfer of current imprisonment servers when conditions prescribed by this Law are fully met. This decision must be sent to the person proposed for transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any);

  b) Decision on termination of consideration of request for transfer of current imprisonment servers as prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article and return of dossiers to the Ministry of Public Security.

  If falling into special cases prescribed at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for issuing one of decisions prescribed in this Clause is 10 days.

  The decisions prescribed in this Clause must be immediately transferred to the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy of the same level.

  3. Regional People’s Courts shall decide to terminate consideration of requests for transfer of current imprisonment servers in one of the following cases:

  a/ The cases do not fall under their jurisdiction;

  b/ Competent agencies of the transferring country withdraw their transfer requests;

  c/ Current imprisonment servers or their lawful representatives withdraw their transfer requests;

  d) The competent authority of the transferring country does not provide additional information or documents within the time limit specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  dd) Other reasons that prevent the consideration of the transfer request.

  4. Within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision to consider the transfer request, the competent regional People’s Court shall hold a first-instance meeting to review the transfer request. In special cases as prescribed in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for opening the meeting to review the transfer request is 10 days.

  Article 23. First-instance meetings to review requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences abroad to Vietnam

  1. A first-instance meeting to review a transfer request shall be conducted by a Judge, with the participation of 1 procurator of the People’s Procuracy at the same level and shall follow the following procedures:

  a) The Judge presents the contents of the transfer request and states his/her opinions on the legal grounds of the transfer;

  b) The procurator states the viewpoints of the People’s Procuracy on the transfer;

  c) The lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer presents their opinions (if any).

  2. On the basis of the results of the first-instance meeting, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision to receive the sentenced person and clearly state the crime and the period during which the recipient must continue serving the sentence in Vietnam. In this case, the Judge shall consider and decide on the conversion of the prison sentence (if any); the conversion of the prison sentence shall comply with Article 25 of this Law;

  b) Decision to refuse to receive the sentenced person and clearly state the reasons.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of issuance of the decision to receive or the decision to refuse to receive the sentenced person, the competent regional People’s Court shall send the decision to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the provincial People’s Procuracy, the Ministry of Public Security, the person proposed for transfer, and the lawyer or lawful representative of such person (if any). In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for the competent regional People’s Court to send the decision to receive or the decision to refuse to receive shall be 5 days.

  Article 24. Appellate meetings for consideration of decisions to receive or refuse to receive

  1. The person proposed for transfer or his/her lawful representative has the right to appeal within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision to receive or the decision to refuse to receive issued by the competent regional People’s Court. The People’s Procuracy at the same level has the right to appeal within 15 days, and the provincial People’s Procuracy has the right to appeal within 30 days from the date the competent regional People’s Court issues the decision to receive or the decision to refuse to receive. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for appeal is 7 days for the People’s Procuracy at the same level and 15 days for the provincial People’s Procuracy.

  The appeal must be notified in writing by the competent regional People’s Court to the People’s Procuracy at the same level and persons related to the appeal within 5 days from the expiration of the appeal time limit.

  The People’s Procuracy shall send the decision to appeal to the first-instance Court, the person proposed for transfer or his/her lawful representative within 02 days from the date of issuing the decision to appeal.

  The competent regional People's Court shall send the dossier and the appeal to the competent provincial People's Court within 07 days from the date of expiry of the time limit for appeal.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the dossier and the appeal, the provincial People's Court shall hold an appellate meeting to consider the decision to accept or refuse to accept the first-instance Court's decision appealed against. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for the provincial People's Court to hold the appellate meeting is 07 days.

  3. The appellate meeting shall be conducted by 01 Judge with the participation of 01 procurator of the People's Procuracy of the same level and shall be carried out in the following order:

  a) The Judge presents the brief contents of the dossier requesting the transfer of the person serving the sentence in a foreign country back to Vietnam, the decision to accept or refuse to accept, and the contents of the appeal;

  b) The Judge asks whether the lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer has made any changes, additions or withdrawn the appeal; if so, the Judge requests the procurator to present opinions on such changes, additions or withdrawal of the appeal.

  The Judge asks the procurator whether the appeal has been changed, added or withdrawn; if so, the Judge requests the lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer to present opinions on such changes, additions or withdrawal of the appeal;

  c) The procurator, lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer presents opinions on the contents of the appeal; the procurator presents the viewpoint of the People's Procuracy on the resolution of the appeal.

  4. On the basis of the results of the appellate meeting, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To reject the appeal and uphold the first-instance decision;

  b) To modify the first-instance decision;

  c) To cancel the first-instance decision and transfer the case to a competent regional People’s Court for re-consideration;

  d) To cancel the first-instance decision and terminate the consideration of the transfer request as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 22 of this Law;

  dd) To terminate the appellate consideration.

  5. Legally effective decisions on acceptance or refusal of acceptance include:

  a) Decisions of the first-instance Courts which are not appealed against shall take effect from the date on which the time limit for appeal expires;

  b) The decision of the appellate Court shall take effect from the date of issuance and is final.

  The decision of the appellate Court must be immediately sent to the first-instance Court, the first-instance Procuracy, the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the person requested for transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any).

  Article 25. Conversion of imprisonment penalty

  1. In case the imprisonment penalty in the judgment/decision pronounced by the Court of the transferring country against the convict who is serving the imprisonment penalty is not conformable with regulations of the Penal Code and other regulations of Vietnamese law, it must be converted accordingly.

  2. The conversion of the imprisonment penalty must be based on the circumstances of the case stated in the judgment/decision already pronounced by the Court of the transferring country.

  3. The converted imprisonment penalty must not be more severe than the imprisonment penalty pronounced by the Court of the transferring country regarding its nature and duration. The time served in the transferring country shall be deducted from the time served in Vietnam.

  4. The convict who is received back to Vietnam shall not be convicted twice for the crime already pronounced in the judgment/decision of the Court of the transferring country.

  5. The Minister of Public Security shall preside over and cooperate with the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court and the Procurator General of the Supreme People’s Procuracy in elaborating this Article.

  Article 26. Execution of decisions on receipt of persons serving prison sentences

  1. Within 10 days from the effective date of the decision on receipt of a person serving a prison sentence issued by the People’s Court, the Chief Judge of the regional People’s Court that issued the first-instance decision shall issue a decision to execute such decision. In special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for issuing a decision to execute the decision on receipt shall be 5 days. The decision to execute the decision on receipt takes effect from the date of its signing.

  2. The decision to execute the decision on receipt must be sent to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the transferring country and the transferee or their lawful representative.

  3. The Ministry of Public Security shall notify in writing the competent authority of the transferring country and the organization responsible for execution of the receipt. The receipt of a person serving a prison sentence must be carried out within 45 days from the effective date of the decision to execute the decision on receipt, unless otherwise agreed upon. In special cases specified in Point c Clause 1 Article 15 of this Law, the time limit for receipt of a person serving a prison sentence shall be 15 days.

  4. The Government shall elaborate Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 27. Cancellation of decisions on receipt of persons serving prison sentences and decisions on execution of decisions on receipt of persons serving prison sentences

  1. A decision on receipt of a person serving a prison sentence that has not yet taken legal effect shall be cancelled in the following cases:

  a) The person serving the prison sentence or their lawful representative withdraws their application for transfer or expresses their written wish not to be transferred;

  b) The transferring country withdraws its request for transfer;

  c) The sentenced person dies or absconds from the transferring country;

  d) Other cases where the reception of the sentenced person cannot be carried out.

  2. A decision to receive a sentenced person which takes legal effect shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) Cases specified at Points b, c and d Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) The decision to execute the decision to receive the sentenced person is annulled.

  3. A decision to execute the decision to receive the sentenced person shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) Cases specified at Point a Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The person handed over by the competent authority of the transferring country is not included in the decision on reception;

  c) The competent authority of the transferring country fails to hand over the transferred person at the agreed time and place without a legitimate reason or the reception is not carried out within the time limit specified in Clause 3 Article 26 of this Law.

  4. Within 05 working days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Ministry of Public Security, the Chief Justice of the competent People’s Court shall cancel the decision on reception of the sentenced person specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article; cancel the decision on execution of the decision on reception of the sentenced person specified in Clause 3 of this Article. These decisions take effect from the date of their signing and must be immediately sent to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the transferring country, the sentenced person or his/her lawful representative.

  5. The consideration of requests for transfer of sentenced persons in the cases specified in Points a and b Clause 1, Point c Clause 3 of this Article shall only be carried out after at least 03 years from the date the decision on cancellation of the decision on reception of the sentenced person, the decision on execution of the decision on reception of the sentenced person takes legal effect.

  Article 28. Reception of transferred persons

  1. The Ministry of Public Security shall organize the reception of transferred persons at the time and place agreed in writing with the competent authority of the transferring country.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 29. Continued serving of imprisonment sentences in Vietnam

  1. The continued serving of the sentence in Vietnam by the transferred person shall comply with Vietnamese law.

  2. Upon receiving a notification of the decision on special amnesty, general amnesty or exemption from or reduction of penalties, parole or other judicial decisions of the transferring country for a person currently serving imprisonment sentence who is received back to Vietnam, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately send such notification to the competent authority for consideration and decision in accordance with relevant laws.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 30. Notices of compliance with sentences of persons serving imprisonment sentences who are received back to Vietnam

  The Ministry of Public Security shall immediately notify in writing the competent authorities of the transferring country of the execution of the sentenced person who is received back to Vietnam in one of the following cases:

  1. Persons serving imprisonment sentences shall be granted suspension of sentence enforcement, exemption from or reduction of the length of time of serving their imprisonment sentences, amnesty, special amnesty, parole or conditional early release, or criminal prosecution for another crime;

  2. Persons who are serving imprisonment sentences and have completely served their sentences;

  3. The convicts who are on their prison sentences escape from the detention centers;

  4. The convict dies before completing his/her sentence;

  5. The competent authority of the transferring country requests information on the execution of the sentence by the person received to Vietnam.

  Chapter III

     TRANSFER OF PERSONS SERVING SENTENCES IN VIETNAM TO FOREIGN COUNTRIES

  Article 31. Authority to decide transfer of persons serving sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  The regional People’s Court where the foreigner is serving his/her sentence has the authority to decide the transfer of the person serving his/her sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country.

  Article 32. Conditions for transfer of persons serving sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. A person serving a sentence in Vietnam may be transferred to a foreign country for further execution of his/her sentence when all of the following conditions are met:

  a) Being a citizen of the receiving country or a person permitted to reside indefinitely in the receiving country or to be received by the receiving country;

  b) The criminal act for which the person is sentenced in Vietnam also constitutes a crime under the laws of the receiving country at the time of requesting the transfer of the person serving the sentence;

  c) At the time of receiving the request for transfer, the period of not having served the sentence must be at least one year; in special cases, this period may be less than one year;

  d) Meeting all the conditions specified in points d and e clause 1 Article 15 of this Law;

  dd) Having fulfilled civil liabilities in the additional judgment or penalty being a fine, property confiscation, and other legal responsibilities as prescribed by law, or having been supported by the receiving country, other agencies, organizations, or individuals in performing these legal responsibilities, or having been assured by the receiving country that the person serving the sentence will fulfill these legal responsibilities after being transferred back to the receiving country;

  e) With the consent of Vietnam in the case where the competent authority of the receiving country makes the request for transfer, or with the consent of the receiving country in the case where the Ministry of Public Security makes the request for transfer.

  2. The Government shall elaborate special cases specified in point c clause 1 of this Article.

  Article 33. Cases of refusal to transfer persons currently serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  The competent Vietnamese People’s Court shall decide to refuse the transfer of a person serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country if one of the following cases exists:

  1. It is not consistent with the principles prescribed in Clause 2 Article 4 of this Law;

  2. It fails to satisfy one of the conditions prescribed in Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law;

  3. It may be detrimental to national sovereignty, defense, security, social order and safety, community health and the interests of the State, lawful rights and interests of agencies, organizations or individuals in Vietnam;

  4. There are grounds to believe that the transferee is at risk of torture, revenge, coercion, ill-treatment, discrimination, cruel and inhumane punishment or humiliation in the receiving country.

  Article 34. Application files for transfer of persons serving sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. An application file for transfer of a person serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country shall include the following documents:

  a) A written request for transfer from the Ministry of Public Security or a competent authority of the receiving country as prescribed in Clause 1 Article 35 of this Law;

  b) Documents specified in Clause 2 Article 35 of this Law.

  2. The application for transfer of a person serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country shall be made in 3 sets, unless otherwise agreed. Language used for making the application shall comply with Article 8 of this Law.

  Article 35. Written request for transfer of a person serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country and enclosed documents

  1. A written request for transfer of a person serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country must contain the following contents:

  a) Date and place of making the document;

  b) Grounds for requesting the transfer of the person serving the sentence;

  c) Name and address of the competent authority of Vietnam and the receiving country;

  d) Information to identify personal identification, nationality, place of permanent residence in the receiving country (if any), and place of serving the sentence in Vietnam, legal grounds for determining that the person serving the sentence is eligible for transfer;

  dd) Other necessary information (if any).

  2. The written request for transfer of the sentenced person shall be enclosed with the following documents:

  a) The application for transfer or documents expressing the desire for transfer or consent to the transfer by the sentenced person or his/her lawful representative;

  b) Documents proving the nationality of the receiving country or being permitted to reside indefinitely in the receiving country (if any);

  c) A copy of the judgment or decision of the Vietnamese People’s Court against the sentenced person proposed for transfer;

  d) Written document on the execution of additional penalties (if any);

  dd) The law of the receiving country applied to determine the constituent elements of the crime and the crime, the provisions on penalties, the statute of limitations for execution of penalties for such crime of the judgment;

  e) Written document expressing the consent of the competent authority of the receiving country in case the Ministry of Public Security makes a request for transfer or written document expressing the consent to consider the transfer request of the Ministry of Public Security in case the competent authority of the receiving country makes a request for transfer;

  g) Documents specified in points g, h, i, k and l clause 2 Article 18 of this Law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate clause 2 of this Article.

  Article 36. Receipt of transfer requests of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam

  1. Foreigners serving prison sentences in Vietnam or their lawful representatives may submit transfer requests to foreign countries for continued serving of prison sentences to one of the following agencies:

  a) Ministry of Public Security;

  b) Competent agencies of the receiving country;

  c) Representative missions of the receiving country in Vietnam.

  2. Upon receipt of transfer requests or documents expressing the desire for transfer of persons serving prison sentences or their lawful representatives, the Ministry of Public Security shall receive and consider making a transfer request as prescribed in Article 37 of this Law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 37. Request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. The Ministry of Public Security or competent authorities of the receiving country shall request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries.

  2. Within 20 days from the date on which the request for transfer is received and information and documents provided by competent authorities of the receiving country, the Ministry of Public Security shall consider deciding to make a request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries and send it directly or via diplomatic channels to competent authorities of the receiving country.

  In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or Vietnamese representative missions in the receiving country receive the request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries from the Ministry of Public Security, they shall immediately forward it to competent authorities of the receiving country and inform the Ministry of Public Security in writing.

  3. In case competent authorities of the receiving country make a request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries, they shall send it directly or via diplomatic channels to the Ministry of Public Security. In case the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or Vietnamese representative missions in the receiving country receive the request for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam from competent authorities of the receiving country, they shall immediately forward it to the Ministry of Public Security for handling according to this Law.

  4. In case foreigners are serving prison sentences at detention facilities under the Ministry of National Defense, they must be transferred to detention facilities under the Ministry of Public Security to serve their sentences before carrying out procedures for transfer of persons serving prison sentences.

  5. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 38. Receipt, examination and transfer of requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of a request for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence and enclosed documents from the competent authority of the receiving country or from the date of receipt of documents specified in Clause 2 Article 35 of this Law from the competent authority of the receiving country, the Ministry of Public Security shall examine the application as prescribed in Articles 34 and 35 of this Law. In special cases specified at Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for examining the application is 7 days.

  The Ministry of Public Security may request the competent authority of the receiving country to provide additional information and documents. After 60 days from the date of sending a written request for additional information and documents, if no additional information and documents are received, the Ministry of Public Security shall return the application to the competent authority of the receiving country and clearly state the reason. In special cases specified at Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for providing additional information and documents is 20 days.

  2. If the application meets the requirements specified in Articles 34 and 35 of this Law, the Ministry of Public Security shall immediately transfer 1 set of the application to the competent regional People’s Court for consideration and decision, and also immediately transfer 1 copy of the application to the competent regional People’s Procuracy.

  The transfer of documents and applications may be carried out electronically in accordance with law.

  3. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 39. Acceptance of applications for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. Within 10 days from the date of receipt of a complete application for transfer of a person serving a prison sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country, the competent regional People’s Court shall accept it and notify the Ministry of Public Security and the People’s Procuracy at the same level in writing. In special cases specified at Point c Clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for acceptance is 5 days.

  Within the time limit for consideration of a request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence, if the request dossier is incomplete in terms of information and documents or contains unclear points or contradictions, the competent regional People's Court shall request the Ministry of Public Security to request the competent authority of the receiving country to supplement information and documents. The time limit for supplementation of information and documents is 20 days from the date on which the Ministry of Public Security issues the written request.

  2. Within 30 days from the date of acceptance, the competent regional People's Court shall, on a case-by-case basis, issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision on consideration of a request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence when all conditions prescribed by this Law are met. This decision must be sent to the person proposed for transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any);

  b) Decision on termination of consideration of a request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence as prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article and return of the dossier to the Ministry of Public Security.

  In special cases prescribed at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for issuance of one of the decisions prescribed in this Clause is 10 days.

  The decisions prescribed in this Clause must be immediately forwarded to the Ministry of Public Security and the People's Procuracy at the same level.

  3. The regional People's Court shall decide to terminate the consideration of a request for transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence in one of the following cases:

  a) It does not fall within its jurisdiction;

  b) The competent authority of the receiving country revokes the request for transfer;

  c) The person serving time or their lawful representative revokes the request for transfer;

  d) The competent authority of the receiving country does not supplement information and documents within the time limit specified in Clause 1 of this Article;

  dd) Other reasons that prevent the consideration of the transfer request.

  4. Within 30 days from the date of issuance of the decision to consider the transfer request, the competent regional People’s Court shall hold a first-instance meeting to consider the transfer request. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for holding a meeting to consider the transfer request is 10 days.

  Article 40. First-instance meetings to consider requests for transfer of persons currently serving time in Vietnam to foreign countries

  1. A first-instance meeting to consider a transfer request shall be conducted by a Judge, with the participation of 1 procurator of the People’s Procuracy at the same level, and shall follow the following procedures:

  a) The Judge presents the contents of the transfer request and states opinions on the legal grounds of the transfer;

  b) Procurators express opinions of the People’s Procuracy on the transfer;

  c) The person proposed for transfer presents his/her opinions (if any);

  d) The lawyer or lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer presents his/her opinions (if any).

  2. Based on the results of the first-instance meeting, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) Decision to transfer the sentenced person;

  b) Decision to refuse the transfer of the sentenced person and clearly state the reasons therefor.

  3. Within 10 days from the date of issuance of the decision to transfer or refusal to transfer the sentenced person, the competent regional People’s Court shall send such decision to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the provincial People’s Procuracy, the Ministry of Public Security, the person proposed for transfer, the lawyer or lawful representative of such person (if any). In special cases specified in point c clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for the competent regional People’s Court to send the decision to transfer or refusal to transfer is 05 days.

  Article 41. Appellate meetings for consideration of decisions to transfer or refusal to transfer

  1. The person proposed for transfer or his/her lawful representative may appeal within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision on transfer or decision on refusal of transfer issued by the competent regional People’s Court. The People’s Procuracy at the same level may appeal within 15 days and the provincial People’s Procuracy may appeal within 30 days from the date the competent regional People’s Court issues the decision on transfer or decision on refusal of transfer. If it falls under the special case specified in point c clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for appeal, the time limit for the People’s Procuracy at the same level to appeal is 07 days, the time limit for the provincial People’s Procuracy to appeal is 15 days.

  The appeal must be notified in writing by the competent regional People’s Court to the People’s Procuracy at the same level and persons related to the appeal within 05 days from the date of expiration of the time limit for appeal.

  The People’s Procuracy must send the decision on appeal to the first-instance Court, the person proposed for transfer or his/her lawful representative within 02 days from the date of issuance of the decision on appeal.

  The competent regional People’s Court must send the dossier and the appeal to the competent provincial People’s Court within 07 days from the date of expiration of the time limit for appeal.

  2. Within 20 days from the date of receipt of the dossier and the appeal, the provincial People’s Court shall hold an appellate meeting to review the decision on transfer or decision on refusal of transfer of the first-instance Court which is appealed against. If it falls under the special case specified in point c clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for the provincial People’s Court to hold the appellate meeting is 07 days.

  3. The appellate meeting shall be conducted by 01 Judge with the participation of 01 procurator of the People’s Procuracy at the same level and shall be carried out in the following order:

  a) The Judge presents the summary of the contents of the dossier requesting the transfer of the person currently serving a sentence in Vietnam to a foreign country, the decision on transfer or decision on refusal of transfer, the contents of the appeal;

  b) The Judge shall ask the person proposed for transfer or the lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer whether to change, supplement or withdraw the appeal; if so, the Judge shall request the Procurator to present opinions on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the appeal.

  The Judge shall ask the Procurator whether to change, supplement or withdraw the appeal; if so, the Judge shall request the person proposed for transfer, the lawyer and the lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer to present opinions on the change, supplementation or withdrawal of the appeal;

  c) The Procurator, the person proposed for transfer, the lawyer and the lawful representative of the person proposed for transfer shall express opinions on the contents of the appeal; the Procurator shall express the People’s Procuracy’s viewpoints on the resolution of the appeal.

  4. On the basis of the results of the appellate meeting, the Judge shall issue one of the following decisions:

  a) To reject the appeal and uphold the first-instance decision;

  b) To modify the first-instance decision;

  c) To cancel the first-instance decision and transfer the case file to the competent regional People's Court for re-consideration;

  d) To cancel the first-instance decision and terminate the consideration of the transfer request as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 39 of this Law;

  dd) Termination of appellate review.

  5. A decision to transfer or a decision to refuse transfer shall take legal effect as follows:

  a) The decision of the first-instance Court, which is not appealed against, shall take effect from the date on which the time limit for appeal expires;

  b) The decision of the appellate Court shall take effect from the date of issuance and is final.

  The decision of the appellate Court must be immediately sent to the first-instance Court, the first-instance Procuracy, the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the person requested for transfer, his/her lawyer or lawful representative (if any).

  Article 42. Execution of decisions to transfer persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. Within 10 days after the decision to transfer persons serving imprisonment sentences of the People’s Court takes legal effect, the Chief Justice of the regional People’s Court that has issued the first-instance decision shall issue a decision to execute the transfer decision. In special cases specified in point c clause 1 Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for issuing the decision to execute the transfer decision shall be 05 days. The decision to execute the transfer decision shall take effect from the date of its signing.

  2. The decision to execute the transfer decision must be sent to the People’s Procuracy at the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, competent authorities of the receiving country and the transferee or his/her lawful representative.

  3. The Ministry of Public Security shall notify in writing the competent authorities of the receiving country and organize the transfer. The transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence must be carried out within 45 days from the date the decision on execution of the transfer decision takes effect, unless otherwise agreed upon. In special cases specified at Point c, Clause 1, Article 32 of this Law, the time limit for transferring a person serving an imprisonment sentence is 15 days.

  4. The Government shall elaborate Clause 3 of this Article.

  Article 43. Cancellation of decisions on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences and decisions on execution of decisions on transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences

  1. A decision on transfer of a person serving an imprisonment sentence that has not yet taken legal effect shall be cancelled in the following cases:

  a) The person serving the imprisonment sentence or their lawful representative withdraws their application for transfer or expresses a written desire not to be transferred;

  b) The receiving country withdraws its request for transfer;

  c) The person serving the imprisonment sentence dies or absconds from Vietnam;

  d) Other cases where the handover of the person serving the imprisonment sentence cannot be carried out.

  2. A decision on transfer of a sentenced person who has entered into a legally effective sentence shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) Cases specified in Points b, c and d Clause 1 of this Article;

  b) The decision on execution of the decision on transfer of the sentenced person is annulled.

  3. A decision on execution of the decision on transfer of the sentenced person shall be annulled in the following cases:

  a) Cases specified in Point a Clause 2 of this Article;

  b) The transferee is not included in the decision on transfer of the sentenced person;

  c) The competent authority of the receiving country fails to receive the transferee at the agreed time and place without a legitimate reason or the transfer is not carried out within the time limit specified in Clause 3 Article 42 of this Law.

  4. Within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Ministry of Public Security, the Chief Justice of the competent People’s Court shall annul the decision on transfer of the sentenced person specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article; annul the decision on execution of the decision on transfer of the sentenced person specified in Clause 3 of this Article. These decisions shall take effect from the date of their signing and must be immediately sent to the People’s Procuracy of the same level, the Ministry of Public Security, the competent authority of the receiving country, the sentenced person or his/her lawful representative.

  5. The consideration of requests for transfer of persons serving prison sentences in the cases specified at Points a and b, Clause 1, and Point c, Clause 3 of this Article shall be carried out after at least 03 years from the date on which decisions to cancel decisions on transfer of persons serving prison sentences or decisions on execution of decisions on transfer of persons serving prison sentences take legal effect.

  Article 44. Handover of transferred persons

  1. The Ministry of Public Security shall organize the handover of transferred persons at the time and place agreed in writing with competent authorities of the receiving country.

  2. The Government shall elaborate this Article.

  Article 45. Re-consideration of judgments pronounced against transferred persons

  Judgments already pronounced against persons serving prison sentences in Vietnam and transferred to foreign countries shall only be re-considered by Vietnamese People’s Courts according to cassation or reopening procedures as prescribed by laws on criminal procedure.

  Article 46. Exchange of information on judgment compliance of transferred persons

  1. After persons serving prison sentences are transferred back to the receiving country, the Ministry of Public Security shall request competent authorities of the receiving country to provide information on judgment compliance of transferred persons at the request of state management.

  2. The Ministry of Public Security shall immediately notify in writing the competent authority of the receiving country when:

  a) The person serving the imprisonment sentence is granted exemption or reduction of the term of imprisonment, amnesty, special amnesty, parole, or is prosecuted for another crime;

  b) The People’s Court of Vietnam reviews the judgment against the person transferred under cassation or reopening procedures and the legal consequences of such review.

  Chapter IV

                                             IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

  Article 47. Entry into force

  1. This Law comes into force as of July 01, 2026.

  2. The Law on Mutual Legal Assistance No. 08/2007/QH12, amended by Law No. 81/2025/QH15, ceases to be effective from the effective date of this Law, except for transitional provisions specified in Article 48 of this Law, Article 45 of the Law on Extradition, Clause 1 Article 42 of the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters, and Clause 1 Article 38 of the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance in Civil Matters.

  Article 48. Transition clauses

  Requests for transfer of persons serving imprisonment sentences received by the Ministry of Public Security before the effective date of this Law shall continue to be considered and resolved in accordance with the Law on Mutual Legal Assistance No. 08/2007/QH12, as amended by Law No. 81/2025/QH15.
